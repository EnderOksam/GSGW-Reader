# Chapter 176 - The American Dream, II

First of all, one thing to point out, just because I had the characteristic 'Words of Persuasion', it didn’t mean I could persuade anyone about anything.

Let's look at the description of the characteristic again.

It caused 'emotional agitation,' but that didn't mean they would be convinced of whatever I was saying.

In other words, it was a supporting tool for various types of situations, ranging from the stage to conversation.

I had to find solid evidence this America-obsessed director would accept.

Then where should I prod him?

I had to stimulate this guy's inner desire that wanted to go to America.

Namely, the 'image'.

"If it goes well in the U.S., that would be great, but... How long are you planning on doing this?"

The director was outspoken in his answer, as if he had already made all the plans.

"Two years if it's short and about three years if it takes a long time. It's a fully feasible plan."

That would fill the entire remainder of his contract.

*That shouldn't happen.*

I asked right away.

"But if we work in the US for that long, won't all the Korean fans leave?"

"Hmm... Then I think they'll hate it even more."

"No. When we go to America, we have to start it all from scratch without having any recognition, but I think they'll hate it if they have to see it all."

I deliberately paused and then added.

"They think we're a great team, but wouldn't they think differently if we start from the bottom in America?"

"...!"

"If in Korea they don't see things going well for two to three years, I don't think our image will recover."

"That's right~ We're doing so well in Korea right now, and that's why other countries are interested! As the director said, everyone knows what's happening around the world in real time these days~"

Lee Sejin interrupted with a smile. I continued right away.

"The response in Japan you mentioned earlier, I think they got immersed in the program because they know that we succeeded in Korea after ⟨Idol Inc.⟩."

"...Mmm."

A strange look passed over the director's face, along with the displeasure at being talked back to by young idiots who hadn't even graduated from college.

Originally, he could have said something like, "If it goes according to my master plan, we'll succeed in America, so it doesn't matter!" but here, the characteristic popped out, and his agitation intensified.

*Now all I have to do is give the director an easy way to back down.*

So that he could rationalize it as 'being considerate of the singers' while quietly withdrawing.

"...It's because we're anxious."

"We're still growing in Korea. We want to see how much further we can go," Raebin added.

The members also fired their support shots.

"It's good to film a reality show in the US, but having America as the target is a bit..."

"It's strange. It's like a movie awkwardly copying Hollywood just to draw people in," Bae Sejin had said.

"I think it's a perfect metaphor, hyung!"

"W-Whatever...!"

This was exactly the position we'd agreed to take if the director pushed an America-first plan.

Here, Ryu Chungwoo carefully added the last words.

"Until our position is solid enough to receive the Grand Prize in Korea... Can't you think about it some more? Please."

"..."

The director twitched his eyebrows for a moment, then shrugged as if he was the bigger man.

"Well, it can't be helped. Since it's an entertainment business, the thoughts of the parties involved are important. I think your opinion should be respected."

"Thank you!"

Now, we just had to leave before he could change his mind.

"Phew."

When I left the director's office, sighs of relief were heard here and there.

"Who'd have thought he really wanted to send us away?"

"I-I was surprised."

*At least I’d bought us time until the end of the year.*

If we happened to win the Grand Prize this time... then. Well, at that time, our right to speak would be stronger, so negotiations would be easier.

Ryu Chungwoo stepped forward and patted both me and Lee Sejin on the back.

"Good job."

"What's with you?"

"*Sigh*."

"Are we still going to America? Are we going to my house?"

"Yes, we'll go there."

There wasn’t much I could do about the reality show.

"Wow!"

Gazes that said "I am glad you're happy" secretly fell on Cha Eugene and then disappeared.

*Still, it's going well.*

I had met with the director and was convinced.

He was the type to get overconfident in his work, but not difficult to deal with.

*He just needed to feel like he was the one making the decision.*

Above all, it was better to want to grow TeSTAR itself rather than thinking about using us for T1.

*I just need to change the flow.*

I stretched myself. There seemed to be a strange noise coming from my shoulders.

"*Sigh*, do you want me to give you a massage?"

"It's fine."

From 'Present' to the current repackage 'Picnic', I felt a little sluggish after rushing non-stop. *Still, the results are great.*
