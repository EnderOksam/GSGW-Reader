# Chapter 237 - Concert for a Cause, I

Cheongryeo picked up after only a few rings.

But instead of a normal greeting, he said:

– So you're still alive.

*I thought as much.*

"Yes, it certainly seems that way."

– Not that I wanted you to die, of course.

"It doesn't sound like you wanted me alive, either."

*Wouldn't he benefit if a competing *daesang* contender were out of the way?*

But the voice over the phone didn't laugh.

– You said you couldn't restart.

"......"

– Then you definitely shouldn't die. What an odd thing to say.

*What?*

Of all people, this lunatic was lecturing me about the value of life.

But I wouldn't deny that there were some pleasant aspects.

*Maybe time really was medicine, if the man from my coma was now preaching the value of life.*

Of course, the reset button was taken away from him now.

"Well, thanks for your concern. I'm fine."

– All right. Why did you call?

"I have something to ask you."

After doing a quick calculation, I asked,

"Are you free the day after tomorrow, or the day after that?"

– Why are you asking?

It was simple.

"I'm allowed visitors now."

Given my condition, he'd have to be the one to come to me.

It was only then that I heard laughter over the phone.

---

After a little coordination, Cheongryeo agreed to visit three days later, in the morning over the weekend.

The others who usually visited almost every day were planning to go home to their families for the weekend.

All except one.

"When can we eat melon?"

"When the hospital says we can."

"I want to ask them!"

"Be patient."

Eugene, who would otherwise spend the weekend alone at the dorm, stayed in my hospital room looking sorry for himself.

His parents had flown to Korea when they heard about his injury, but they'd gone home the previous week.

Few office workers could take more than two weeks off to stay in another country, even if they were from the United States.

I gave Eugene several enormous tangerines Ahyeon had brought instead of melons.

Ahyeon must have chosen fruit that would keep until I was cleared to eat it, though I doubted it'd last that long around Eugene.

"Here, eat."

"Are these the sweetest kind?"

*Did you learn that from Lee Sejin?*

"Yeah."

"Wow!"

I watched as Cha Eugene ate the tangerines.

In fact, this was a kind of bribe.

"Someone's coming in about thirty minutes. Don't tell the other members."

"A guest?"

"Something like that."

"Who?"

"VTIC's Cheongryeo."

"Oh."

Eugene swallowed his tangerine and shrugged.

"Okay~"

That was it.

For once, his individualism made him easy to deal with.

"But why's he coming?"

*He still asked too many questions.*

"I need advice about our next promotions."

"You can ask me!"

"I need someone with a lot of experience."

"Too bad."

Fortunately, the barrage of questions ended there, but it was obvious that a similar situation would repeat when Cheongryeo arrived.

And thirty minutes later...

"I want to listen too!"

*I knew it.*

"Go and buy yourself some snacks."

"Boo..."

He obeyed, though reluctantly.

Eugene grumbled as he left but didn't forget to greet Cheongryeo politely.

"Hello, Cheongryeo-sunbaenim!"

*Tattang!*

He bowed energetically and cheerfully shut the hospital-room door behind him.

Cheongryeo stared at the guy leaving and smiled.

"He's tricky to handle. You must have good group dynamics if you can manage him."

"......"

"I dealt with someone like him once. He refused to show up for group schedules only sixteen months after debut. Was that my sixth round? Haha."

He really knew how to kill the mood.

I crossed my arms and got down to business.

"I invited you today because I'm dealing with an emergency."

"Yes."

"I think I'm about to fail my mission."

"......"

There was no reason to hide the danger. I could use any help I could get, and since he could no longer restart, Cheongryeo might actually be useful.

"What is this mission?"

"Gather an audience of 400,000."

"......"

Cheongryeo was silent for a moment as if he was lost in thought.

And he tilted his head to the side.

"That's extremely specific."

"......"

"How can you be so sure? Interesting."

As expected.

*That got his attention.*

I'd kept the status window to myself until now, for safety.

But I couldn't avoid discussing some details of the status conditions if I wanted his advice.

*The more information we shared, the more precise our predictions could be.*

With the tour canceled, the risk of my dying suddenly was rising.

He might resent that I could see mission details he couldn't, so I needed to steer his thoughts away from that unfairness.

*Better not let him dwell on that.*

I shrugged.

"It's good that I know the details. Mission aside, I nearly went to Hwangcheon because of that accident."

"...Originally, this job has a lot of variables."

"I've almost died twice already in the two years I've been an idol."

"Twice?"

"And the first time was because of you."

He laughed, then gave a slightly apologetic nod. Maybe he'd come to his senses after his obsession with resetting.

Contacting him after receiving nearly twenty dog photos had paid off.

*He still seemed to feel a little guilty, and perhaps even a kind of camaraderie.*

Sending the photos after learning I'd woken up was probably his way of showing it.

Still, this bastard wasn't normal, so I had to move on quickly before he thought about anything else.

"I still need at least 300,000 more people to see me perform."

"Hmm, it must be difficult to set up a new dome tour at the end of the year."

"Yes."

The year-end and New Year holidays were peak season, and most venues large enough for TeSTAR were already booked.

Besides, if the lawsuit went forward, TeSTAR might be unable to perform for the rest of the year.

And Cheongryeo pushed forward the most common sense and possible alternatives to consider.

"What about an online concert?"

"I'm not sure the system would count it."

I pressed my forehead.

"The counting criteria are unclear. Only people physically at the fanmeeting were counted, not those streaming it remotely."

"......"

The fan meeting I had right before I got into an accident.

The performance was simultaneously broadcast online, but both real-time paid and illegal visitors were not counted.

Only the on-site audience was counted as the actual 'audience'.

Looking this far, it seemed that non-face-to-face performances were useless, right?

However, some performances actually counted on a non-face-to-face basis.

*Some paid W Live comeback shows had counted.*

The audience's comments and reactions had been displayed on a big screen.

Taking everything together, I could think of only one tentative rule.

"I think the system counts them when I recognize them as an audience during the performance."

Not simply when I saw the total number on a screen.

That passion and energy.

"Ah."

Cheongryeo narrowed his eyes.

"An audience in the theatrical sense?"

"Theatrical?"

"Audience reaction is part of the performance. A bit old-fashioned, perhaps."

"......"

*He must have read plenty about this in one of his lives.*

Cheongryeo stroked his chin.

"Anyway... Then it must be a bit tricky."

"That's right."

I clicked my tongue.

"I need around 300,000 people I can interact with live, but that won't be easy."

It wasn't enough for them to buy a VOD; I'd need people there and reacting as I performed.

*Even adding more shows would have diminishing returns.*

The audience would likely dwindle once I'd already performed the same sort of concert once.

That was how scarcity and diminishing marginal utility worked.

Busy people might pay for a performance and never watch it.

And some might tell themselves, *I paid for one stream, so it's fine to watch the rest illegally for free.*

Seeing my anguish, Cheongryeo smiled.

"Hm. Then why not guest on a VTIC tour? For a month or two..."

"Are you crazy?"

"Haha!"

He knew that it was nonsense, but he had the shamelessness to ask.

A guest appearance at a VTIC concert?

*It would be hell.*

The VTIC and TeSTAR fandoms would explode if I guested at someone else's concert as soon as I recovered.

However, Cheongryeo was calm.

"It's better than dying. Think about it—it might be your only chance."

"......"

Of course, VTIC would have more excuses than TeSTAR, so it was understandable why he was saying this.

*Still, a loss is a loss.*

*Why would a man so obsessed with being the flawless top idol make an offer like that?*

I remembered his offer to recruit me as VTIC's main vocalist when I was in my coma, and answered cautiously.

"I don't understand why you'd offer that. There's nothing in it for you."

"Maybe because I feel bad?"

"......"

I studied his face, looking for any sign that he was joking.

But he appeared perfectly calm.

"I've interfered pretty seriously a couple of times. Let's say I'm repaying a debt. What do you say?"

"...Hmm."

He seemed to have reflected on his past after watching me nearly die in the accident.

*It seems that raising a dog really helped him mentally.*

However, I had no intention of investing in such a scheme. I reviewed the discussion so far.

*The resources I can use...*

My abilities, connections, time, money...

*...Money?*

"...!"

I opened my eyes.

I found it.

"No, I won't use that method."

"Then?"

"I'll hold performances online—completely free."

I said it firmly.

"No admission charge."

"...!"

I didn't know if it would overcome all the other restrictions, but at least money wouldn't be a barrier.

*I could afford to pay for the concert myself.*

I still had money saved; I didn't need to accept someone else's support.

"Oh, you want me to compensate you with money?"

So, this was a suggestion.

I chuckled.

"No. Something else."

The biggest stumbling block to free performances wasn't money.

This was an existing contract.

*TeSTAR had signed a three-year exclusivity agreement with W App for group performances.*

The agreement didn't allow the group to hold free concerts there.

To do this, I needed a workaround.

"Let me use your platform."

LeTi operated a separate exhibition-and-performance platform with an overseas funding company.

*But the agreement didn't cover solo performances by individual members.*

A solo-hosted event would be much easier to arrange, and I could negotiate the remaining contract and public-reaction risks through Cheongryeo's company.

*I think this is the best winning combination right now.*

Sure enough, Cheongryeo also smiled and nodded.

"Good idea."

It was better than his suggestion. I responded calmly, holding back the thought of almost clicking my tongue.

"It'll work better than joining VTIC's tour."

"What? Haha, of course. Why would we do that? I was just saying I'd go that far if I had to. Admit it sounded tempting."

"......"

"I meant that you could even do it. I guess it sounded pretty plausible."

*He'd been playing me.*

*The Cheongryeo I met in my coma would've used me and cast me aside, too.*

---

I was irritated at first, but soon regained my composure and worked out the details with Cheongryeo.

I contacted the agency right away and discussed use of the new platform.

*That was surprisingly easy.*

The agency, eager to divert the backlash, gave us a quick informal yes.

Formal approval would come through early Monday morning, after the weekend.

"Yes, thank you."

It was a ridiculous miscalculation on their part, but Moondae was happy to take advantage of it.

*I hope it goes smoothly like this.*

I still needed the members' agreement, so I arranged for everyone to visit on Monday afternoon.

*Better to explain it to everyone together.*

So after a few days...

The members were already telling me to sit down and reflect on my choices.

"Park Moondae, sit down and listen."

Apparently they'd all heard about the plan somehow.

Cha Eugene didn't hold back.

"I asked the agency what was going on!"

*This guy.*

Of course, it was my karma.
