# Chapter 352 - The Weight of Secrets, III

The festival was over.

*They want more?*

I couldn't guarantee that, since I wasn't even sure if tomorrow's schedule would go ahead.

Scrolling down the screen, I found a video of me singing the fight song on the fly, handheld microphone in hand.

★$
– This video screams "This is what it means to be a true idol."

– He was so on top of it.
$★

*On top of that?*

If doing what I already knew was having 'quick wits', then a dog could also be called a tiger. I'd only managed it because I already knew the song. I didn't deserve any credit for that.

Park Moondae was the sort of person who utilized information for his own gain, and never spoke a peep about it afterward. It wasn't that I excelled at reacting swiftly under pressure.

If I had really been that sort of guy, I would have said something different in response to Seon Ahyeon's breakdown.

Instead, when I was speaking to Seon Ahyeon...

– *...*
– *I-I'm sorry...*
– *No. No.*

I just repeated the same word over and over again, like I couldn't even speak my mother tongue properly.

I stood there stupidly until the others dragged him and me from the bathroom.

*If I'd acted differently at that moment...*

*Stop.*

*I need to stop with the useless what-ifs.*

I would move on to the next situation and continue thinking on my feet.

I... barely remembered getting back to my room. Had Seon Ahyeon gone back to his?

*Probably.*

They hadn't taken him outside. That had to mean he was still in the dorm, and that he'd managed to calm down.

I pressed both hands against my face. My brain felt like it had frozen up entirely, and it refused to function.

*Think. It's the only thing you know how to do. Don't shirk your duty.*

*Find out why things happened the way they did—but no. That's not the right way to put it.*

*Why the fuck did you cause such an unpleasant situation?*

I gritted my teeth.

If I were to ask myself where my stupidity had begun, I had to go back to the beginning, of course.

*I shouldn't have told him in the first place.*

*How could I have thought that was a sane thing to do?*

If I'd realized that telling him would make him think I was out of my mind, I should've kept my mouth shut. What was I thinking, saying all those unrealistic things with this idiotic brain of mine?

*Because the others believed me? You generalized it from one case, you fucking bastard.*

With Chungwoo and Keun Sejin, I'd been found out and had no choice but to explain. I should have been relieved that it worked out and simply moved on.

*Why did I explain everything to someone who had no idea what was going on?*

Objectively speaking, was there any good reason for Ahyeon to accept all that nonsense? Was the story I'd told him something that could simply be shared because I felt like it?

"It was completely unnecessary..."

It had been utter foolishness, and now the situation we were in was horrible.

*Seon Ahyeon's status abnormality...*

It was activated once more, and now what was I going to do?

"......"

*Is knowing the reason all there is to it? Do you think that knowing what you fucking did will help you find some kind of fundamental solution?*

*Shut up.*

I clamped a hand over my mouth, allowing my teeth to bite into my hand.

I needed to stop with the useless time-wasting and face reality.

*What do I need to do?*

*...Whatever it takes... I need to deactivate his status abnormality again.*

Ahyeon had been able to keep it deactivated until now because of his trait.

*Fortitude.*

★-
\
#><✦ Trait: Fortitude (A)><#

Master your own mind. Gather your focus and repress negative thoughts.

Counteracts one status abnormality (one with the highest priority) when active.
-★

However, this trait had been deactivated by the nonsense I'd told him, and now the status abnormality was back.

I clenched my fists.

*Before...*

In the past, I had used a trait of my own to convince him.

– *Just think to yourself, "I'm going to do this." Nothing else.*

He had accepted my reasoning and decided to focus only on the task ahead of him.

Now, however, nothing I could say would convince him.

I was the very person who had confused him, making him incapable of focusing on his work.

He thought that I was out of my mind. There was no way it would work.

"......"

My brain was blank.

*What can I do...?*

*I need to persuade him somehow...*

*Like before...*

I reflexively brought up my status window. A pop-up appeared as if it'd been waiting for me the entire time.

$p
**[Gunwoo...]**
p$

It was Keundal. He didn't say anything else.

I got the impression that the window was shaking, but perhaps that was only because I was completely out of it in that moment.

*But what does it matter?*

I muttered in a hushed voice.

"Is it possible... to get a trait back?"

*That trait that allows me to persuade anyone.*

"......"

There was no movement from the status window.

Only after a while did tiny letters appear in front of me.

$p
**[No.]**
p$

*Oh.*

*Yeah, I thought as much.*

$p
**[Sorry...]**
p$

"You have nothing to apologize for."

Why did everyone apologize when I said something stupid?

*Are they all following some kind of manual? Even with something like this, I...*

$p
**[This isn't your fault. It really isn't.]**
p$

"It is."

Nobody had forced me to say anything. This disaster had happened because there was something wrong with my mind.

*I've lost my ability to judge the situation accurately.*

This was the only possible conclusion.

*Knock, knock.*

"Moondae."

*Who was it?*

When I turned my head, I saw Ryu Chungwoo's face as he cautiously entered the room.

"Ahyeon calmed down. He's okay."

"......"

"I know you said you wanted to be alone, but... I'm sorry, I wanted to come in."

He smiled, seeming slightly embarrassed, then he slowly sat down on his own bed.

*I don't want to hear any more apologies from anybody.*

"This is your room, too. You should do what you want," I said quietly.

"...Hm, okay."

Then there was a short period of silence. Ryu Chungwoo opened his mouth again after that.

"Moondae-yah. It's natural for people to want to share about their life experiences."

*Yeah. But actually going through with it like a fool was another matter.*

"This right now is just a matter of timing. Don't worry about it too much. Okay?"

"......"

I had been the one who had chosen the timing.

But I didn't need to say anything further.

*Knock, knock.*

Someone else entered the room.

*Keun Sejin.*

He closed the door behind him and spoke with a tense expression on his face as soon as he took stock of the situation.

"Park Moondae. It's my fault. I'm sorry."

"......"

*I felt like throwing up.*

"How could this be your fault?"

"I encouraged you."

He said it without batting an eye.

*What was this guy saying right now?*

"I thought that he would believe you. That was my mistake."

"......"

"You seemed really nervous when you told me. So I thought it would be better if you relaxed this time around. The fault is mine. All you did was do as you were told."

"No."

I massaged my temples.

"I was the one who said it. Stop it."

"As I said..."

Keun Sejin opened his mouth as if he wanted to say something more, but soon closed it.

I was thankful for that. My head felt like it was about to split open.

"This is nobody's fault. Things simply happened this way. So both of you stop," Chungwoo said, intervening. I didn't agree with him, but what did it matter?

But I couldn't ignore what he said next.

"But Moondae... Why don't we take a short break?"

"......"

*What did you say?*

"We've been going non-stop for over two weeks now. I think we can take a few days off."

"We could inform the agency that we've caught the flu, or that we are suffering from fatigue."

A soft, cautious voice followed.

"Why don't we use that time to recenter?"

*Recenter. Stop our promotions.*

"......"

If I had any conscience at all, and took into account Ahyeon's condition, I had to agree.

Considering the incredible difficulty of our current title track, a drop in Ahyeon's stats would immediately be noticeable.

It was obvious how people would respond to that. People would curse at us, and there would be no end to the controversies.

*It's right to rest.*

If I were in my right mind, I shouldn't be insisting that we keep going right now.

*But...*

I turned my head and looked at the pop-up in the status window that I hadn't turned off yet.

*If I take a break... He will...*

$p
**[Gunwoo, get some rest. You need to let yourself rest... Please.]**
p$

We didn't have much time left. If I didn't manage to set a new record during our current promotions, he would die. He would go back to being a status window.

The little bastard was telling me to rest, even though he knew.

"You..."

My brain was about to burst.

*Fuck, why is there so much to think about, so much to deal with... What the hell was this... This just isn't...*

"You..."

The word left my mouth.

"Later."

*Stop. Stop being absurd.*

*There is no "later."*

We had a shoot in the morning, and it was less than four hours away. Even now, I had to make a decision.

I needed to make the right call before it was too late. *I need to be efficient...*

"Moondae."

Someone grabbed my shoulder.

*Ryu Chungwoo.*

"Then let's empty our schedule for three days, at least. Three days won't cause any problems. We've made plenty of content already. Okay, Park Moondae?"

"......"

I nodded.

I wasn't sure what we could do with what we had filmed so far, but I would let him have his way.

At the same time, however, in the living room...

Kim Raebin was lost in thought while holding a cup of the hot tea Seon Ahyeon often drank.

"The only conclusion seems to be that something has happened between them..."

Cha Eugene shook his head. His dear friend was an extremely talented artist, but he was no good at matters like this one.

So he was frank.

"Everyone knows that, Kim Raebin."

"......"

Rather than getting upset, Kim Raebin nodded with a slightly gloomy look on his face.

The situation was rather serious.

*It's such a mess.*

Ahyeon was acting like a sleepwalker with self-destructive tendencies, and Moondae seemed to be out of it and in a state of shock. He had not even been able to walk back to his bed.

Cha Eugene honestly couldn't understand why the situation had come to this.

They had both apologized to each other, which had to mean that there were no hard feelings.

Didn't that mean they didn't have bad feelings toward each other?

At the same time, he was able to look at the situation rationally.

*Well, none of us seemed to have had many friends growing up, before we joined TeSTAR.*

He'd often seen relationships grow so important to the people involved that they became a problem.

These people could be so clumsy at times, even though their genuineness was their charm most of the time.

*Hmm... That hyung is like that too.*

He was looking at Bae Sejin, who tottered into the living room and sat down with the look of someone who had lost a battle.

"Why am I so bad at speaking?"

It seemed that he had tried to have a gentle conversation with Ahyeon and failed.

Feeling bad for him, Eugene nodded.

"Hey, I'm good at it. Let me go and speak to him."

"W-wait."

He had intended to go to Moondae first, to help cheer him up, but a conversation involving both consolation and advice was more of a challenge.

*That's what I should be doing.*

Cha Eugene shrugged his shoulders and confidently entered Kim Raebin's room, which was also Seon Ahyeon's room, after knocking.

Nobody stopped him, which suggested that he had made the right decision.

"Hey, I'm coming in."

"......"

*Creak.*

When he opened the door and entered, Seon Ahyeon was sitting on the edge of his bed beside a window through which moonlight was pouring.

He looked stiff and pale, like he was in shock. He seemed completely disoriented—partly because of the sedatives he'd taken.

"Hey, you're not feeling sick, right?"

"......"

Seon Ahyeon nodded very slowly. His eyes seemed unfocused, perhaps because of the medication.

"How are you feeling mentally?"

Silence.

Cha Eugene sat down next to him and got straight to the point.

"You can't tell me anything, right? Because it's Moondae's secret?"

"...!"

Ahyeon turned around, finally looking a little bit more alive. Cha Eugene shrugged his shoulders.

"Everybody has secrets. I don't need to hear it!"

Well, wasn't it rather strange to have no secrets in life?

And whether or not to share those secrets was entirely up to the person they belonged to.

Sharing more secrets didn't make a friendship closer or more genuine. Knowing secrets did not mean you knew the other person better.

This was what Eugene believed.

*If it doesn't happen naturally, secrets shared are only a burden.*

Perhaps the current situation had come about from something like that. He didn't know.

"Whatever it is, you heard that secret, and it caused a problem," Eugene said in English.

Seon Ahyeon remained motionless and silent, then muttered in a low voice.

"I-I... I ruined everything."

"Then fix it."

"...!"

"Don't think I'm just saying this lightly. Um, I don't think you're upset with Moondae-hyung," Eugene continued in English.

Cha Eugene stared at Seon Ahyeon.

"So get some sleep for now and talk to him when your mind's clearer. It'll make this much simpler."

He was advising Ahyeon to relax and leave his panicked state of mind behind.

And the answer came back.

"...I-I know."

Seon Ahyeon murmured in a low, hoarse voice.

"Th-that's... what is wrong with me."

"...!"

"Ev-every time. I know, I know, but I do nothing...!"

Eugene could see emotions in his eyes that were deep-rooted.

*That's...* It was self-hatred and rage.

Seon Ahyeon knew it too. Why wouldn't he know?

There was nothing he could do at this point, so keeping his mouth shut and going to sleep was the only way he could help.

But he couldn't do it. He was anxious, and the fear that he'd ruined everything had taken hold of his mind.

In much the same way, he had realized something as he heard Moondae's "secret."

*No...*

Moondae's past actions, his memory loss, nightmares, his attempt to take his own life... All of these things pointed to a very serious problem.

– *I'm not Park Moondae.*

The signs were there. The wounds on his heart had turned into a very serious identity crisis.

And he knew that he was the most suitable person to bring up the situation to Moondae.

*I need to be the one to do it.*

Ryu Chungwoo and Lee Sejin had overcome their own mental hardships with little to no medical help.

They were healthy, and if they believed Park Moondae completely, that left only him.

*It was just me...!*

He was the only one who could properly tell him about the danger, and that was the only way he could help Park Moondae.

He had told himself so many times that he would be a good friend and companion, and yet he had failed.

*No. I didn't fail.*

*I simply chose to do nothing.*

The precious relationships they'd built on trust and intimacy could come to an end with just one word if he spoke up.

That possibility alone was too frightening.

*Only me... What if he hates me because I'm the only one who doesn't believe him?*

*I'd basically be saying there's something wrong with Moondae. I-I can't do that...*

*H-he'll be offended...*

But as long as he kept his mouth shut, then they could keep getting along.

Seon Ahyeon clenched his fists until his fingers turned white.

*I didn't care about what he was going through. What mattered to me more was maintaining our friendship.*

His negligence had made Moondae's symptoms worse.

If Moondae really suffered while continuing his activities, to the point that irreparable damage was done...

*It would be my fault.*

As he went through many sleepless nights, trying to collect information without giving away that he had his doubts, he knew his own state was growing worse.

*Can't I just believe him?*

He did not believe Moondae, but he was so eager to justify his own actions.

He knew the truth, yet he'd done nothing and ruined things.

*It's because I am a useless human being.*

There was no way around that fact.

He was always thinking about what was best and what was right, but in the end, he couldn't do anything about the things that were most precious to him, the things that scared him the most.

And, unable to keep hold of his weak mind, he had spoken to Moondae in the worst way possible, at the worst time possible.

*I wasn't even helping. All I did was hurt him.*

He had hurt Moondae again. He felt like crying.

*You have no right to feel pain.* Self-pity was a luxury.

Ahyeon repressed the urge to scream. Apologizing while in tears did not mean he deserved forgiveness.

"......"

Eugene bit his lip, which was not like him at all.

*He looks like a monk from the Middle Ages, going through some kind of painful penance,* Eugene thought.

He didn't know all the details, but the mood was enough to tell him that Ahyeon was in pain.

Still, Cha Eugene decided he needed to say what he thought was necessary. Everyone needed to escape the pain they were going through using their own two feet.

But there was nothing wrong with giving him a hand in taking the first step.

"Hmm. Okay. But this is slightly different," Cha Eugene said in English, raising his hand.

"This time, if hyung doesn't do anything, Moondae-hyung will."

"...!"

"You know how he is. If something happens to this group, he will try to handle it himself."

Seon Ahyeon lifted his head.

"That's the kind of person Moondae-hyung is. You already knew that, right?"

Of course, he knew.

"So this isn't simply a matter of whether you choose to do something or not."

"......"

"Either you act, or he will. It's one or the other."

Seon Ahyeon gritted his teeth.

*In that case... I'll...*
