# Chapter 373 - The Wise Survival, I

A short-term project group focused solely on achieving maximum efficiency so we could escape this place.

For Cheongryeo to accept this, he ultimately had to acknowledge that a separate reality existed and that we could return to it.

*I doubt this bastard can reach that point.*

I kept my mouth shut. And Cheongryeo slowly opened his mouth.

"Alright."

It worked.

*There we go.*

I clenched my fists and suppressed a sigh. There was no need to show that I was relieved.

"Then the survival show—"

I was about to tell him to cancel it, but he spoke first.

"Hmm? Ah, that's already been put into motion."

"...!!"

*Did this bastard not hear a word I just said...?*

"Cancel it."

"Hmm? It's not like I own the broadcasting station... That's not possible now. Haha."

I dearly wished I could punch him in the face.

As if he had sensed it, he finally came up with a useful reason.

"There's nothing like a survival show for generating early buzz, is there?"

"......"

I rubbed my chin.

"I guess you're right."

Wasn't I living proof of that?

If we could appeal to the audience, there was no better way to build popularity in a short period of time than through a survival show.

The problem was that he was the one holding the sword hilt.

"As long as you don't do anything stupid."

"What do you mean by stupid?"

*For instance, trashing Cha Eugene on broadcast just because you're pissed at him. Bastard.*

I didn't say it out loud, but Cheongryeo seemed to guess what I meant from my tone. He shrugged his shoulders.

"Well, I think there was a misunderstanding here."

He snickered again.

That bastard seemed to be looking down on me with pity, as if I were a pathetic rookie. It put me in a foul mood.

"Do you really think that Hubaenim's group members will be able to debut as long as there's no unfair interference?"

"Yes."

*What is this guy even saying? He saw all the craziness that went down on ⟨Idol Inc.⟩.*

*How could anyone who debuted through that mess fail to survive a 2010s survival show?*

*As long as they get proper coaching, pushing through the rest of the contestants shouldn't be a problem.* I didn't back down.

"Hmm..."

Cheongryeo rested his chin in his hand.

"Then shall we make a bet?"

"What?"

"Let's bet on whether more VTIC members or TeSTAR members end up debuting from the show."

*Bullshit.*

"Is this really something we should bet on? And I've only gathered two so far."

"I have two right now as well."

"......"

In the first place, didn't VTIC have half as many members as TeSTAR?

"Hmm, losing confidence all of a sudden?"

*This guy was really...*

I gestured with my chin.

"Let's go by proportion instead. To make it fair."

"All right. You must feel confident."

The guy chuckled.

"Alright. Hubaenim, then... Let's make a plan."

And then he held out his hand.

"......"

I shook the hand he held out.

It was a temporary alliance.

His grip was very firm.

"However, if we get to the end and there's nothing there for us..."

The guy's expression disappeared.

"If Hubaenim's reasoning is wrong..."

"...!"

Cheongryeo narrowed his eyes and laughed.

"From then on, you'll follow *my* lead."

"......"

*So he's laying the groundwork for that, huh?*

*That won't happen.*

I nodded silently. And I also tightened my grip.

"Haha, if this were real, it would be a real mess."

"It'd be a nightmare."

If this were happening in the real world, this kind of mix-and-match survival show would've thrown the entire fandom into chaos.

And after a few days of preliminary work at the agency...

Articles began to appear in late February, and a trailer was uploaded on WeTube.

The seven members of the debut group and seven other trainees.

*There are many faces I saw back then.*

I realized that many of the people the director had summoned on my first day here had been selected.

*Had he been told to find contestants for the show?*

*I can see Sino and Judan as well.*

I didn't know whether the VTIC members from the real world had always been in the running or whether Cheongryeo had put them in, but they were here anyway.

The remaining TeSTAR members... That is.

"......"

*I'll have another chance later.*

I mentally went over the structure of the show that I'd heard about from Cheongryeo.

– *From the outside, it'll look like the final group is selected entirely by the company president.*

Soon the main cameras started rolling, and the idol trainees stepped onto the stage under the lights.

Fourteen of us entered side by side.

A snide-looking man was sitting in the judge's seat right in front of the stage. Apparently, he was the president.

He'd been the most prominent member of a popular singer-songwriter group in the 90s.

"From now on, I'll address you formally as fellow singers rather than as trainees. Understood?"

"Yes."

"Great. Then, I will explain the rules of this program to you, the rising stars of LeTi."

After intentionally pausing, the explanation continued.

"This is a survival show."

"...!"

"What?"

Everyone had heard the rumors going around the company, but they acted as though this was the first they'd heard of it. Their acting was pretty convincing. There were 14 people to begin with, so it was natural to expect that there would be some who were eliminated.

I just stood there with a stiff expression on my face.

"Who will be eliminated, how many people will be eliminated, who will debut, how many will debut... It's all unknown."

*You do realize you're repeating yourself, right?*

Anyway, everyone swallowed nervously.

"You run towards your dream, but to achieve that dream you have to overcome a lot of competition."

"......"

"So from now on, whoever wins the competition will survive."

– *Honors class, inferior class. At first, they'll divide us up like that,* Cheongryeo had said.

It was literally just like I was told.

Task number 1, of which everyone was notified in advance.

"You have to do well, but star quality is just as important as doing well. I'll also judge the qualities and charms that can't be expressed in words or numbers."

In other words, even if his judgment was bullshit, we'd have to accept that we simply weren't charming enough.

"Yes!"

After responding energetically in unison, they stepped forward one by one and performed dances, songs, or personal talents.

*They interpreted the meaning of performance broadly.*

Though it was unlikely to happen, I kept my expression neutral as I watched, not wanting to cause any unnecessary controversy.

The quality... They were very good for trainees. But—

*They're not as good as the debut group.*

I stifled a laugh.

*They told us to prepare something late on purpose.*

In episode 1, the other seven trainees had been added as fodder to make the seven debut-team members look better.

In these rookie days, there was a huge difference between practicing for a week and practicing for a month.

They probably wouldn't have warned anyone directly. I assumed they must have spread vague rumors among the debut group to give them an advantage.

Like this.

The debut group was the honors class. The rest formed the inferior class. I watched Sino and Judan go to the inferior class. Cheongryeo's expression didn't even change.

*Hmm.*

*Was he planning to have them dramatically move up later?*

It was then.

"Next performance... Ryu Gunwoo."

"Yes."

It was my turn. I got up and moved.

I was the 9th person to perform. It was neither a particularly good nor bad position.

It was only my first stage on the show.

*Actually... It's not that important.*

In a large-scale survival show like ⟨Idol Inc.⟩, where half the contestants could be eliminated in an instant, making a strong impression from the start was important, but I had to look at the bigger picture here.

First of all, all performances would be broadcast.

*For now, I'll just focus on establishing a stable reputation.*

I put on the head microphone and waited quietly for my cue.

The music soon began.

What interested him was the company's growth and greater success—and the fame and wealth that came with it.

*That's business.*

Having already gone more than a decade without releasing an album of his own, the former singer and current businessman intertwined his fingers.

Still, he had a good eye for these things. After all, he was the one who had carefully selected these promising trainees.
