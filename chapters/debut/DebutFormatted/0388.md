# Chapter 388 - Guerrilla Concert

Another day of filming for ⟨Wise⟩ arrived.

This time, without a scene of the president lecturing us on stage, filming of the main content began as soon as the high-tier and low-tier teams were announced at the dorm.

*We're not filming the stage performance that eliminates people just yet, but the idol event that comes before it.*

Previously, there'd been a public service announcement and a photo shoot.

*Next on the list is...*

"This event is a street guerrilla concert[1]{T/N: Guerilla concert: A concert that is announced on short notice and most likely free.}."

"Wow!"

"Oh my..."

I had a feeling there'd been some kind of deal with the local government, but in any case, we'd be performing on a makeshift stage in the middle of Myeong-dong.

Overtime was pretty much guaranteed this time as well.

Even if those of us who had our memories, including me, could work more quickly, it wasn't like we could just go to bed when we were done and leave the others behind. We had to coordinate our schedules and practice as a group.

*Of course, there are people who'll work harder than me.*

Case in point: the guy who was talking to me as I unpacked my things on the bed. He was the one leading the choreography practice in this agency's debut group.

"Welcome, hyung. I knew you'd work your way back up quickly, really."

"Yes, thank you."

Shin Jaehyeon warmly welcomed me back to the high-tier team, where I'd been moved as of today, and extended his hand without turning his back on the camera.

*As expected, he never misses an opportunity.*

I shook hands with Cheongryeo, who'd been assigned the bed next to mine and had never once been booted out of the high-tier team or had to move his luggage out of the room. I knew this scene would make it into the episode.

*Does this guy plan to continue with this image?*

A perfect leader who'd never lost his spot in the high-tier team.

In fact, if he was that consistent, it would strengthen his narrative and solidify his reputation.

"The classes assigned by the program are temporary. Regardless of that, let's think of it as all of us debuting together and work hard."

"Yes...!"

I wondered how many times that touching line had been used so far.

He was really laying it on thick, going on to say kind words to another trainee as if he meant them. I spent two days watching him push other trainees to their limits while pretending to take care of them.

And then the day of the performance arrived...

*That's because they call it a guerrilla concert, but they announce it 3 hours ahead of time.*

It seemed that viewers came running as soon as it spread on the Internet.

*Even if that wasn't the case, there are plenty of tourists here.*

Since it was in Myeong-dong, there were a lot of foreigners interested in K-pop.

It was a time when people still loved to see foreigners cheering in the crowd. Maybe that was part of what the production team was aiming for.

In any case, whether this was their first time or not, the participants were a bit excited in front of the open outdoor stage.

"Let's do our chant and go up."

"Yes, I am! ⟨Wise⟩, fighting!"

And then we went up to the small stage where a university's violin concert had been held just before.

"Wow!"

Raw shouts. A cluttered but dynamic mix of cheers and screams.

The sun, the wind, the smells.

My first performance in an open outdoor space in a long time felt quite different.

*It feels like we're at one of TeSTAR's events.*

Of course, it was a strange feeling that the setlist was filled entirely with other people's songs rather than familiar ones.

And it seemed like I wasn't the only one who felt that way.

We were backstage, just out of the cameras' view...

"I kind of miss how we did things."

Ryu Chungwoo, an idol with five years of experience who'd managed to master the theme song and fill in the team performance's vacancies in just two days, muttered softly.

Cha Eugene grumbled.

"I want to do our own song."

It was impressive how both of them avoided mentioning TeSTAR in case the microphones caught what they were saying. I nodded slowly.

"Gunwoo-hyung!"

"Hmm?"

Kim Raebin, who was warming up next to me, whispered to me with a serious face.

*Wait a second. This guy doesn't have his memories back, so maybe he's grown suspicious...*

"Was the theme song suddenly removed from the setlist?"

"...?"

"If the 'our song' Cha Eugene referred to was a song everyone has to perform together, it would have to be the theme song. And since he said he wants to perform it, I assumed that meant we're no longer able to."

"......"

I stared at him.

"So my conclusion is that it was excluded from the setlist—"

I barely opened my mouth.

"It probably just means he wants to get to it quickly. The theme song always comes last, right?"

"...! Okay! Thank you for your kind explanation."

*He was truly one of a kind.* It was a shame that the camera probably didn't catch that.

*If he had his memories back, he'd be tormented by his conscience over how unfair it was to the other contestants, and everyone would notice something strange was going on.*

Even if Kim Raebin appeared in the companion draw right away, I realized I should probably wait to awaken him until after this program ended.

The mini-concert lasted a full 50 minutes.

The death match performance with Jin Chaeyul, where we'd worn sailor outfits, was also recreated quite late in the concert.

Of course, we swapped the sailor outfits for baseball jackets, but in the end, it seemed the CEO had been forced to compromise with public opinion and couldn't remove it from the setlist altogether.

This was indeed a capitalist decision worthy of the head of an entertainment company.

Chaeyul. This guy seemed a little excited, perhaps simply because he felt recognized.

"I think President Kim Taein also appreciated our performance because he saw it several times! He might like this performance as soon as he sees it this time..."

"That's right!" Sino replied.

The way he and Sino were laughing was very similar to when they were in VTIC.

This guy seemed to gain fans every time his cheerful, guileless side was revealed on TV. Apparently, he came across as very amiable.

Even when he was in VTIC, I remembered that fancams capturing this side of him had sold for more... Well, anyway, he was definitely in high demand.

*I think he'll pass without any trouble.*

Having reached that conclusion, I came off the stage, waving to the people.

And as the performance was nearing its end...

"Thank you!"

Kyaaaaaak! Aaaaahhhh!

After the theme song performance was over, there were so many people that we were blocking foot traffic. It was a crowd mentality where seeing a crowd made outsiders want to join in themselves.

*Maybe they just think it's fun to cheer together...*

"Gunwoo! Wowww, you look handsome!"

"Yes... Thank you."

Anyway, I reacted whenever my name was called. *Being praised didn't feel bad.*

After the cameras had captured the crowd's excitement...

Finally, a surprise event that hadn't been announced to the participants in advance began.

"We will welcome the passionate viewers who came to the guerrilla concert today up onto the stage~"

"Oh my gosh!"

"Right now?"

It was a surprise fan meeting.

*I thought it would be like this.*

Before going on stage, I'd noticed that they seemed to be selecting a few people from the crowd and sending them to the front.

"Hmm."

"Welcome... Oh my god. What do I say?"

I shook my head inwardly as I looked at the faces of the participants, who were starting to get more excited and nervous than the fans.

*It's been a while.*

After getting hit with a plastic bottle during my debut showcase, we'd almost never held impromptu events with large crowds for fear of another accident.

Soon, people began coming up in groups of two or three. Each person went towards the participant they were rooting for.

"Wow! Oh my god, what should I do?!"

"You can come here and say hello."

"Ahh!"

A few people came up to me, so I shook their hands and gave them a quick hug that was more air than contact.

Most of them were trembling, and I wasn't sure if they were excited or nervous. I guess it was because it was a public place. This... It wasn't much different from when I was Park Moondae.

*But there was definitely a difference.*

"Ahhhhhh, you're so handsome..."

"Oh, I love it..."

And... They didn't even ask me to make cute poses, like poking my cheek.

*...I guess it's because this wasn't a planned event.*

Or maybe it was because it would be filmed and the show had a more general audience, rather than just a bunch of long-time idol fans. I didn't know, but it felt like they were being more cautious.

In other words, less comfortable.

*...Is it because Ryu Gunwoo looks less approachable?*

*No, it's not like I'm disappointed or anything.*

In any case, the production team seemed to have chosen well, since the fans were distributed fairly evenly and no participant was left without one.

Even Ryu Chungwoo, who'd only appeared in the previous episode, had a fan or two.
