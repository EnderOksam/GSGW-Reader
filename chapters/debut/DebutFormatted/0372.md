# Chapter 372 - Reassembling TeSTAR, II

"As you said, a survival show has too many variables. I don't think there's any need to go through all that."

"Really? Hubaenim also debuted through a survival show, so I thought you would prefer it."

*This won't do.*

"Wait a second."

"I know you're upset, but let's cool down and talk again."

"Well, I wouldn't say I'm angry. It's true, isn't it?"

*Don't act like you're not upset when you're really upset.*

"Why are you overreacting? I said I was willing to give everyone equal opportunities."

"I like that— Mmf!"

*You stay quiet.*

"Okay, let's meet again tomorrow and talk."

I was about to hurry away with Cha Eugene, but I looked back at Cheongryeo first.

*Oh, whatever. I'll say it.*

"...Thank you for coming to the airport today. See you tomorrow."

"......"

Cheongryeo seemed a little shaken, but now the priority was to catch this bastard that was running wild like a colt.

"And you, watch your mouth."

As soon as we left the game room, I slapped Cha Eugene on the back.

"Ah, ah! Moondae-hyung, it hurts!"

"The situation is a mess right now. Even if you're right, you have to think about how you say things."

"That sunbaenim wasn't being considerate either! Why should I be the only one who has to?"

"......"

*That... Anyway, this guy had a point.*

*Cheongryeo was more openly aggressive than I thought.*

It was unexpected. I thought he would just gently coax him into staying behind as a trainee at his agency and say things like eliminating competition.

Instead of calculating how he could make use of Eugene, he'd focused on establishing his dominance...

I clicked my tongue. It left a bitter taste in my mouth.

*...That bastard must be struggling to keep himself together too.*

After struggling so hard and adjusting to the idea that there was no going back, to his new reality of raising a dog, he suddenly found himself starting over again?

It was a wonder he hadn't completely lost his mind already.

I took my hand away from Cha Eugene and held back a sigh.

"...Okay. You didn't do anything wrong. But he also has his own circumstances."

Cha Eugene shrugged.

[We all have our own circumstances, but that doesn't mean we can act like assholes.]

"......"

*Well...* He kept hitting the nail on the head, leaving me with little to say in reply.

"Alright. Anyway, you've got some jet lag to work through, so get some sleep first. Have you found a place to stay?"

"No!"

"......"

"The plane ticket was expensive. Should I get a motel room?"

He said it so cheerfully.

"Just... Follow me."

I was already at a loss as to what I should say to Ryu Chungwoo, but I couldn't leave a guy who'd spent all his money getting to Korea to sleep on the street.

*If only this bastard wasn't 17 years old...*

With Cha Eugene in tow, I returned home to the officetel where I myself hadn't spent many nights.

%^*Ding-dong.*^%

As a courtesy, I rang the doorbell once.

"Gunwoo-hyung?"

And when Cha Eugene saw Ryu Chungwoo coming to the door, he was delighted.

"Chungwoo-hyung is here? Did he come first?"

"As I told you, we're relatives, and we're living together."

"OK..."

Looking a little disappointed, Cha Eugene spoke to Ryu Chungwoo.

"Do you know me?"

"What?"

Ryu Chungwoo seemed to think for a moment about what the situation was, but then burst into laughter.

Maybe because Eugene looked so young, Chungwoo didn't seem suspicious.

"Hyung, did you tell him about me?"

"Yes. This is Cha Eugene."

I explained that he was a pen pal through a college program and had come to Korea all of a sudden on a whim.

Ryu Chungwoo was impressed.

"He's pretty brave."

"Brave? Hardly."

If this were a real situation, he would've been forced straight back home by his parents with hardly a hello.

In fact, it seemed like his family had complained quite a bit, but they were pretty liberal overall, so he got permission after coming to Korea.

I rubbed my eyes.

*...I'm sure they'll at least send him some money to cover his living expenses.*

At least it wasn't the worst. He had guardians who would look out for him.

"Can he just stay for a few days until he finds somewhere else to stay?"

"Of course. I've had friends stay here before too."

"Thank you! I'll be in your care!"

Cha Eugene immediately took over the guest room that doubled as the utility room, and I went into my room and started thinking in earnest.

How on earth should I deal with this situation?

Just in case, I checked the status window... *What's the point of thinking about the status window, though?*

Composing music.

17-year-old Kim Raebin, who was about to start another year at high school, was working hard to prepare for his future during winter vacation.

*I passed...!*

He was aiming for a career as a composer.

He'd just been hired for his first job after exchanging emails with an agency in Seoul.

Considering his young age and lack of personal connections, it was a remarkable achievement, but unfortunately, neither he nor his guardians had any specialized knowledge.

As a result, Kim Raebin was planning to get fitted for a uniform at a nearby high school next week.

Instead of putting everything on the line to demonstrate every last bit of his amazing talent, he continued his peaceful daily life.

"Raebin, you should be heading back now before the sun sets~"

"It's already about that time! Then, I will get going."

As sunset drew near, he was about to return home after helping out at his grandparents' strawberry greenhouse.

Kim Raebin came out of the greenhouse and was about to lock the door when he heard someone shouting.

"Kim Raebin!"

"Yes!"

Everyone around here was older than him. He replied politely and turned toward the voice that had addressed him so casually.[1]{T/N: It is rude to address someone by their full name without any honorifics unless they are older than you or are a friend.}

"...??"

*Those people... Who were they?*

"That's right, it's Kim Raebin!"

"Yes."

The person wearing the jacket in a wild and haphazard manner shouted out excitedly, while the person in the long padded jacket calmly replied.

Both of them... For some reason, they didn't seem younger than him.

*Then...!*

*Is this the high-school bullying I've heard about...?*

During school violence prevention education, he'd heard about bullies visiting students who hadn't even entered high school yet to "teach them a lesson"...!

He had never been bullied by anyone in his life, but he might be getting bullied now!

Kim Raebin, whose fierce appearance had intimidated everyone his age, froze as he considered this absurd possibility.

"Wait a minute."

But the two who came close... They just looked happy to see him.

Their first meeting was intense.

"...!"

One of them had the brightest eyes of anyone he had ever met in his life.

With his eyes shining as if they glowed yellow, he held out a hand... And he pointed at Raebin.

"I'm the one who found Kim Raebin!"

"...?"

*What... Does he want something from me? No, it's rude to point a finger at someone's face like that!*

However, before Kim Raebin could ask a question, the other person spoke first.

"Yes, you found him."

And he turned to look at Kim Raebin.

"It's nice to meet you, Raebin-ah."

A clear and firm voice that was pleasant to listen to.

He looked cold and delicate, but his eyes were warm. Kim Raebin felt something hot rise in his throat.

For some reason, his heart ached. Where... He felt as though he knew the two from somewhere.

*...?*

*Why?*

Kim Raebin thought carefully, and then, like a lightning bolt, he came up with the most logical conclusion.

It was a very likely guess.

Kim Raebin opened his mouth with a very sorry expression.

"Sorry! I don't remember clearly, but I feel like I know the two of you!"

"...!"

The surprise on the two people's faces... Kim Raebin failed to notice the mysterious emotion that passed over them.

So he asked brightly.

"Are you relatives on my mother's side?"

Were they relatives he met when he was a preschooler and didn't remember?

"......"

"......"

There was silence for a moment.

"No."

"Then..."

"We came because we have a suggestion."

A person with a delicate expression smiled faintly.

"We're here to scout you."

"S-Scout??"

The strong-looking one shouted like a cheerleader.

**"Become an idol with us!"**

"What...?"

Kim Raebin was confused. The person with the delicate look took out a smartphone and turned the screen without hesitation.

It was his account.

"This is your account, right?"

"...!"

Before he had time to think, the delicate-looking person continued calmly.

It was as if the other person was already certain Kim Raebin would agree.

"You must have thought about wanting to personally perform this song on stage."

As if mesmerized, Kim Raebin gaped, forgetting to shake his head...

And five hours later, as if he had been scammed, he had an appointment to meet them in Seoul.

*...??*

Kim Raebin's modest dream of becoming a producer had been massively sidetracked.
