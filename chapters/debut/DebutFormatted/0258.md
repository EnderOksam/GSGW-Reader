"Thank you."

I looked at Youngrin.

"But my goal is to make no mistakes. I will do my best."

Youngrin smiled slightly. There was no team leader who was prouder of the new employees.

"Yes."

"Wow! Idols, fighting!"

It was a little funny to see the three of us cheering together with our hands holding the script, but I had to be content with the fact that no one wanted to skip it.

"How was the National Gugak Band's special performance to introduce the musicians of the year?"

"It was really nice!"

"I really enjoyed watching it because the flamboyant movements seemed to show a bright new year."

And the three of us did not make a single mistake throughout the first part. Including me, of course.

*Good.*
