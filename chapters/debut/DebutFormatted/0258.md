# The Youngest MC

**"Hello everyone! How is your December going? I hope it's going great."**

**"The musicians who got your hearts pumping this year have prepared amazing performances for you once again."**

The two idols next to me did quite well.

*The years of experience under their belts aren't for nothing.*

I also took the mic.

**"Then, shall we begin with the first performance of ⟨Gayo Daejeon⟩, featuring the artists whose activities have stood out the most this year?"**

I was the only one with particularly long sentences, but I don't think I was mistaken. *Am I getting the long lines because I have less experience?*

*And who the hell is the guy who made me poke myself in the cheek?*

I couldn't refuse because of my position, but they'd explicitly requested it in the script. I wasn't going to forget this.

**"⟨SBC Gayo Daejeon⟩ starts now!"**

Anyway, the cameras were rolling, so the three MCs flashed their corporate smiles at them. We were being paid to do so, after all.

And the moment the camera indicator light went out, we relaxed and took a moment to compose ourselves.

"Whew."

"Good!"

The VTIC guy on the other side of me reached over and patted my back.

*I don't need you playing the senior, even if you are one.*

"Moondae-ssi, you did a great job!"

"Thank you."

Youngrin, who was checking the script again, also smiled slightly and added her words.

"You're working hard, as always."

"Thank you."

I kept bowing my head. With such a big gap in our years of experience, it was better to continuously be a 'thank you' robot throughout the filming.

There were fans holding light sticks right under the podium where we were.

Of course, a few camera lenses were also visible.

*I wonder how they managed to sneak those in?*

I smiled slightly at the cameras held by some familiar people before looking away. It was a live broadcast anyway, so anything they filmed wouldn't even count as a leak. Preventing leaks was the reason studios usually banned cameras in the audience in the first place.

*When this stage is over, we'll introduce the theme of the show...*

I mentally ran through the stupidly long line again, starting with 'The festivity in all our hearts.' The script would be on the teleprompter, but I didn't want to stumble.

*However... Isn't three idols a bit unreasonable?*

Honestly, with this lineup, it was sure to be a hot topic, but for the sake of keeping the show running smoothly, it'd be better to have a professional anchor there to hold things together.

It was not an award ceremony like the acting or entertainment awards, so people probably wouldn't be too hard on us. Still, I didn't want to get criticized.

And they all seemed to have the same thoughts.

"Wow, I'm sweating! I've only hosted my own group's content and things like that. I never thought I'd suddenly be doing something like this at the end of the year! I'll do my best, everyone."

First of all, the guy with the longest career was promising to do his best like a rookie.

"Moondae-ssi is still a rookie, so don't be too nervous. If you mess up a line, just finish the sentence. I'll take over from there."

She sounded almost like a professional MC. She'd hosted all sorts of shows, and it showed.

"Thank you."

I looked at Youngrin.

"But my goal is to make no mistakes. I will do my best."

Youngrin smiled slightly. She looked just like a team leader proud of a new recruit.

"Sounds good."

"Wow! Idols, fighting!"

It was a little funny to see the three of us huddling up with our script-holding hands stacked together, but I had to be content with the fact that none of the MCs were just trying to do the bare minimum.

**"How was the National Gugak Band's special performance introducing the musicians of the year? Did you enjoy it?"**

**"It was really nice!"**

**"I really enjoyed watching it. Their impressive performance seems like a good sign for the bright new year ahead."**

And the three of us did not make a single mistake throughout the first part. Including me, of course.

*Good.*

At the end of Part 1, I noticed that an intermission advertisement had started and drank some water. I saw familiar faces waving at me from the artists' seats, placed front and center so the audience could see their reactions.

"Hyung, you were great! Keep it up!"

*I can't hear anything, you guys.*

The sounds and cheers were so loud. Why were they talking so hard?

Anyway, I was grateful for their support, so I waved back and flipped through my cue cards again.

*As long as nothing goes wrong, I'll come out of this looking like I did a fairly good job.*

In the middle of Part 1, I'd even covered for the VTIC guy when he was clearly embarrassed by the self-congratulatory script, so at least I wouldn't come off as an incompetent parachute rookie idol.

*I hope the articles are kind.*

I shrugged and put the water down.

And after the first performance of Part 2, we started speaking again.

**"Moondae-ssi!"**

Suddenly, something out of the blue that wasn't even in the script came out.

VTIC's Chaeyul shoved a large cupcake right in front of my face with a lit sparkler candle on top.

"...??"

*What the hell?*

**"I heard that it was Moondae-ssi's birthday a few days ago!"**

**"Happy birthday, Moondae-ssi."**

And the production team passed up a cone-shaped birthday hat with puppy ears from behind the cameras.

"......"

I took it and put it on, looking utterly shocked.

*Assholes, you should've told me in advance! This is a live show...*

*Are you serious?*

I smiled broadly and blew out the sparkler candle, then tugged on one of my puppy ears.

*Wow. I'm wearing a suit, hosting a show the whole country watches at the end of the year, and you're making me act cute like this, really...*

**"Thank you."**

I could see certain members rolling around laughing in the artists' seats. *I'll deal with you when we get back to the dorm.*

*...Wait a moment.*

*Come to think of it, the opening script for the second part...*

**"The next performance has to do with a beautiful birthday!"**

**"It was a really big hit this summer."**

I barely kept my composure and continued with my last words.

**"Let's welcome a special live performance of 'Birthday Boy' by Gray Cable, a band that's been around for 17 years."**

*So all that was a setup for these lines?*

*Are idol MCs supposed to sacrifice their dignity just to introduce the next song?*

I felt dizzy realizing that the demands of being an idol carried over even to a year-end program that wasn't a regular music show.

...And this kept going until the very end.

* * *

*Park Moondae is so cute!!*

Park Moondae's fansite master, who was holding the camera under the stage, almost cried with joy.

She'd heard a rumor from someone connected to the industry that Moondae would be an MC at a year-end show, so she'd secured this seat. It had been totally worth it!

*This is it! This is it!*

It had been a long time since she had seen Park Moondae in real life. She loved it...

Park Moondae was wearing a black shirt under a neat navy blue suit, and he was dressed like an MC with his hair neatly slicked back.

Presumably because he was the youngest MC, he wore a white ribbon tied in a bow instead of a tie, and that was really... It was so cute.

*Especially when he took the cupcake!*

Apparently, he hadn't been told about it in advance, since he was clearly taken aback. That made Park Moondae even cuter.

*He got cream on his nose...*

As soon as he found out, Park Moondae wiped it off, and a staff member even fixed his makeup, but Park Moondae's fan had already caught it on camera.

*Hahaha...! Hahaha!*

Excited about both online and offline shoots after so long, especially being back at an in-person event, she laughed to herself like a lunatic and snapped photos like crazy for her fan page.

*This one's going to be my first preview image!*

All the emotional burnout she'd built up after the accident, through those depressing times, the crazy roller coaster of emotions, and the worry she'd felt when they resumed activities, had been swept away.

She felt refreshed and jubilant.

*I'm really looking forward to this activity.*

It felt really good to see them come back safely. The fan smiled and fixed her mask.

*Moondae seems to be in good condition too!*

Whenever the members in the artists' seats caught Park Moondae's eye, they waved to him, and when the cameras weren't rolling, it was nice to see him wave back surreptitiously.

In addition, it was clear that Park Moondae found the fans' camera lenses in the middle of it. He even made eye contact with this camera!

*Let's be an idol forever...*

Park Moondae was a blessing from heaven and clearly the endgame of her K-pop life. If she believed it, then it was true.

*Transferring to like someone else? There is no such thing.*

The fansite master's bitter memory of drinking away her sorrows and vowing never to trust idols again, after her former bias made headlines for all the wrong reasons, had been shattered by everything she'd been through with Moondae since.

And after a while, part 3 started.

*They must be doing it soon! The cue sheet was right.*

Park Moondae jumped up and disappeared behind the MC seat. She also saw TeSTAR getting up from the artists' seats.

*Because year-end programs don't give official fancams...!*

Park Moondae's fan held the camera tight with a sense of mission.

She felt prouder than ever as she captured everything on camera.

*That felt... so good.*

And on the way home... Satisfied, she came out of the SBC Sangam Hall.

She didn't feel tired in the least. No, it was because the adrenaline was rushing like crazy that she couldn't feel it; she'd probably start to feel it when she got home.

Anyway, Park Moondae's fan was very happy.

"They're so good at this."

TeSTAR's performance skills hadn't diminished at all.

Not even a single choreography had the difficulty adjusted or was gotten rid of.

*No, rather, it felt more proficient!*

There were no signs of the traffic accident at all.

Immediately after being discharged from the hospital, Park Moondae, who briefly stumbled at a group concert, was flying around today.

His readiness and roaring enthusiasm could be felt both on set and through the camera lens.

Of course, the composition of the stage itself was also good. The performance of 'Spring Out', with its shooting effects, was spectacular and suitably grand for a year-end stage.

It must have been a hot topic on the Internet, the fan guessed.

*And now... this part!*

Excitedly, she got into a taxi and checked some of the videos she had taken by connecting the camera to her laptop. It was because she really wanted to revisit some of the scenes that left a deep impression on her!

Just as she was about to speed up the footage, she noticed something.

*Uh?*

After finishing the stage, Park Moondae went up to the MC seat.

Under the black hair of Park Moondae... A bright strand was visible.

A thrill ran down her spine.

"A wig...!"

The black hair was fake anyway, whether it was spray-on color or a wig.

Park Moondae... he escaped black hair again!

*Crazy! He dyed his hair for this album!*

This was a separate matter from her preference for black-haired Park Moondae!

*What color is it? What color is it? If I correct the lighting, the color will come out, right?*

The fan couldn't have been more excited. It had been so long since she'd last felt this kind of thrill.

*Haa...*

The fan pondered for a while whether she'd be able to keep herself from posting spoilers until January, and came up with pleasant hypotheses related to their activities.

In the meantime, the copied video started playing from the beginning.

Park Moondae successfully returned to the MC seat while checking the cue sheet.

But when she looked at it again... It was a little strange.

"Huh?"

In an instant, her excitement suddenly dampened.

The fan immediately enlarged Park Moondae's upper body.

"......"

From the angle, Moondae seemed to be looking at TeSTAR as they returned to the artists' seats, but his expression... It looked vicious.

His eyebrows were twitching.

*What?*

The fan replayed the footage a few more times, feeling a chill, but because only Park Moondae was being captured in the video, she couldn't tell exactly what Park Moondae was looking at.

And he was able to manage his facial expressions again rather quickly.

"Mmm."

*It must be an illusion.*

It was borderline obsessive to read this much into an expression that had lasted only a few seconds. The fan shrugged and closed the video.

But it was not an illusion.

* * *

"You worked hard~"

"MC Moondae was really good."

"Thank you."

"Hyung, a birthday present with dog ears... Ouch!"

"Stop."

The long live broadcast was over.

As soon as I joined them, we climbed into the van. We were going straight to the practice room for a couple more hours of practice before returning to the dorm.

*Being an MC was fine.*

The performance had gone well, matching the effort we'd put into it, so I had no complaints there.

Except for one thing I didn't expect.

"I-is your throat okay?"

"...It's fine."

"Thank god...!"

That guy, Seon Ahyeon.

When I was returning from the stage earlier, I looked at the singer's seats, and apparently, a few idols were bowing to Seon Ahyeon with excessive politeness.

*They're probably the ones working with him on the collaboration stage.*

It was fine until then.

The problem was that after that, Seon Ahyeon averted his gaze and lowered his head. As if he was intimidated.

"......"

It didn't feel good.

When we reached the practice room, I grabbed one of the guys by the back of his shirt as he got out of the car.

"Hey."

"Huh? Moondae, why?"

It was Lee Sejin.

I'd seen this guy subtly cut off the idols who'd been bowing to Seon Ahyeon earlier.

*I think this guy noticed it too.*

I lowered my voice a bit so the members walking ahead of us couldn't hear.

And got to the main point right away.

"In the singer's seat... You saw it. Seon Ahyeon."

...Hmm.

Lee Sejin seemed to understand immediately what I meant.

"That."

"Yes, did you notice anything? They looked like collaborators."

The guy shrugged.

"It's obvious. Looks like they're harassing Ahyeon."

"...!"

"Ahyeon's a good kid, you know~ He's not the cunning type, so they're messing with him."

Lee Sejin raised his eyebrows.

"There is such a thing. They were probably like, 'Hey~ Ahyeon gets a lot of cameras. I'm envious~ I don't think you need to practice much, right?' They'd say stuff like that and laugh about it a bit~"

"......"

His impression was so realistic that I wanted to punch him in the mouth.

However, Lee Sejin's tone quickly became serious.

"In addition, there must have been one or two people who tried to act like they knew him. Since Ahyeon's shy, they probably talked behind his back, saying he only talks to people who are popular."

"...Hoo."

"You understand, right?"

"Yes."

This kind of social bullying was ridiculous. Maybe it was because they were in the same age range. These bastards were no different from the ones in ⟨Idol Inc.⟩.

*I thought they'd fawn over him because we're so popular.*

I'd forgotten what people were like: if someone more successful looked like a pushover, they'd treat him even worse.

Besides, since only members from relatively popular groups had been gathered for the stage, they were even more competitive.

*I should hear Ahyeon's side of the story.*

Before entering the practice room, I decided to check Seon Ahyeon's condition.

And it was the moment I was about to move my feet.

"Wait a minute."

"...!"

I stopped.

I turned my head to Lee Sejin, who was holding my shoulder, and he shook his head.

"Achacha, Park Moondae, you're not going to talk about this to Ahyeon, right? No way~"

"Why?"

"Hey, people have pride."

Lee Sejin smiled wryly.

"How would you feel if a friend your own age realized you were being bullied? Wouldn't you hate it even more because a close friend was pointing it out?"

"...!"

"Let Ahyeon take care of it. That's right."
