# Chapter 279 - ⟨TeSTAR Roommate⟩ Season 4, II

Five minutes had passed since a zombie apocalypse broke out in the middle of our personality test...

"Watch your head."

"Hoo."

We were crawling through the vent shaft.

Seeing the pre-installed cameras here and there, I figured we were on the right route.

*The route is fine.*

The remaining crew had deliberately stood in front of the vent to make it look well hidden.

But in an emergency, it was only natural to seek out the production team, so the transition made sense.

*The atmosphere is fine.*

It was a cliché, but the dark and eerie ventilation shaft made it easy to immerse ourselves in the scenario.

*Keuuuuuuu—*

*Keureureuk! Geeeek!*

We could hear creaking and zombie noises like these through the walls.

"Oh my."

"Wow, the sounds."

"I'm sure those are zombies!"

*Looks like everyone is getting immersed.*

After checking the nervous guys, I nodded inwardly and continued crawling.

And after some time...

After turning a corner, we reached a fork in the shaft. Ryu Chungwoo, who was leading the way, stopped short.

"Should we split up—"

"Shh."

He pressed himself close to the wall, his ear against it, and had the others follow behind him.

"Heob."

Taking that unusual gesture as a sign he'd seen something, the other guys quickly pressed themselves against the wall.

That moment.

"Keeeeeeeeeeeeeeeak!"

"Uh-hyeop." Keun Sejin let out a shocked noise.

Kim Raebin hurriedly covered Keun Sejin's mouth.

A zombie was screaming from inside another vent on the opposite side, clinging to it.

The suit was covered in fake blood and wounds and looked pretty frightening.

*Wow. He's really giving it his all.*

It seemed the production team had invested a fair amount in hiring actors. It felt quite real... *Wait a second.*

An ominous sound was heard.

*Tuduk.*

"...!"

And the ventilation fan attached to the vent where the zombie was... fell off and bounced away.

*Ting, ting, Tididididing...*

Before the fan could land at our feet...

Cha Eugene shouted in English, "To the right!" and then in Korean, "Go right! Go right!"

"Whoa!"

Cha Eugene darted down the other passage like lightning, pulling the members along by their hands and hair.

*Is he herding sheep?*

But it worked. In an instant, we raced down the side passage on all fours and reached the vent at the other end.

But at that very moment, the zombie behind us started trying to smash through the vent, shaking the shaft noisily.

*Kwagwagwang!!*

"Aaaagh!"

"I-I'll open it right away!"

Beside the screaming Kim Raebin, Seon Ahyeon reached out and pulled a lever next to the vent.

It opened easily. Everything ahead was dark; the vent led into an unlit space.

I immediately opened my mouth.

"I can't hear any zombies in there."

Ryu Chungwoo immediately responded.

"Let's go inside, single file. Be careful!"

"Yes!"

One by one, we climbed out of the vent.

Relying on the dim light from the vent, I checked that the members who'd gone down first were helping the others climb down, then got out almost at the same time as Ryu Chungwoo.

Then I closed the vent behind us.

*Bang!*

The zombie was still trying to break through. Keun Sejin called out calmly.

"We can't lock the cover in place! See if there's anything we can use to block it!"

"Wait, wait! If we push this up against it..."

Bae Sejin pointed to something dark around where he was standing. Kim Raebin ran and shouted.

"It's a cabinet!"

"Move!"

Just in case, three of us held the vent closed while the other four went to move the cabinet.

But there was no time to spare.

*Thud.*

...I felt the vibrations through my hands as I held the vent closed.

"He got it open."

The zombie had smashed through the vent cover and entered the shaft. I saw it around the corner.

"Right there..."

"Hey, it's coming!"

The situation had grown urgent enough that we were all starting to sweat.

*It's going to slow down a bit, right?*

The lab had even included a promotional ad, so surely they wouldn't deny us the minimum amount of time to find a solution.

However, the zombie approaching from the end of the shaft, twisting its whole body with its bones creaking, was quite... No, it was fucking fast.

*Damn it.*

It had nearly reached us already.

Just in case, I pressed harder against the vent with both hands.

I was grateful for the enthusiastic performance during filming, but they should tone it down a little...

"Get out of the way!!"

I reflexively twisted aside, and Ryu Chungwoo did the same. A piece of metal furniture was shoved into the space we'd cleared.

*Bang!*

It was the cabinet. The huge piece of furniture covered more than half the vent and was pressed against the wall.

And at that moment, the zombie slammed into the grating.

"Kyaaaagh!"

*Thud!*

The cabinet, which had taken four grown men to move, seemed to contain some heavy items, and the zombie couldn't budge it.

"Ah!"

However, Cha Eugene, who was closest to the cabinet, had almost come within swiping distance of the zombie in the vent because of how hard he'd been pushing.

"...!"

"Kyaaak!"

Ryu Chungwoo and I reflexively pulled Cha Eugene back. The guy jumped and backed away.

"Are you okay?"

"No problem," Cha Eugene said in English, then added in Korean, "Let's go!"

Cha Eugene smiled, looking perfectly fine. None of the fake blood seemed to have gotten on him.

"Phew, that's a relief. I thought we might have to tie Eugene up~"

"Haha!"

Only then did a few people laugh at Keun Sejin's joke. The situation had been more serious than expected, but the initial panic was finally starting to wear off.

And naturally, as soon as the members came to their senses, complaints came pouring out.

It was even worse than when they'd said they'd let us travel but then made us work part-time at a hotteok business. I understood their reaction.

"Wow, but really... I never thought this would happen! The producers, really!"

"I really didn't expect..."

"...No wonder we signed a consent form when we came in. I thought it was because of the tests."

"Shh!"

Meanwhile, Cha Eugene pressed a finger to his lips, deadly serious.

"There are more zombies out there! Let's focus on the situation first!"

*He's so excited.*

It was obvious from the twinkle in his eyes that he found this amusing.

"Yes, Eugene is right. Let's start by checking where this is. It's dark, so let's make groups of two... I will go alone."

"All right, Leader!"

Remembering that the cameras were still running, we quickly resumed our search.

*They're going to edit it anyway, so it would have been fun to talk about it a bit more.*

I shrugged and started looking around, relying on the dim light coming from behind the zombie in the vent.

"Hyung, I can't see very well in the dark because of my eyesight, but I can move however you direct me, so just tell me..."

"Okay. Don't worry, I'll tell you if there's a problem."

"Yes!"

*No zombies have shown up despite all the noise we've made, so this room must be sealed...*

I quickly moved toward the wall, with Kim Raebin, who'd been standing closest to me, following behind. And the moment my hand touched it...

"...!"

It was a unique texture.

...Wood.

I stopped my hand.

*A dark, sealed space with unfamiliar wooden walls...*

I had a bad feeling about this.

"Hyung? Did you find some suspicious liquid on the wall over there...?"

I moved my hand again.

"No, it's clean. I was just being careful."

"Okay! You are smart. We never know where the zombies will appear!"

*Okay.* I was overreacting.

*We're just filming on a set.*

The zombie virus setting alone made it easy to remember that this was just a variety show.

It was obvious that they were all dressed up anyway, so it didn't matter if it was sealed or not...

"Wait a second."

"Yes?"

My hand, mechanically touching the wall, found something colder than wood.

"There's... some kind of metal box on the wall. It's pretty big."

"...!"

It was a suspicious box.

"Moondae, did you find something?" Keun Sejin asked.

"Oh, hold on."

"I will check this way!"

Kim Raebin moved past me and felt along the other side of the same metal box, then shouted.

"There's some kind of latch on the other side! Shall we open it?"

"Uh? Wait a minute! Guys, wait!"

Not long after, Keun Sejin and Seon Ahyeon approached with flashlights. I wasn't sure where they got them.

"Is this okay?"

"Excellent!"

And the moment we undid the latch using the flashlights to see what we were doing...

*Gying!*

The box opened by itself.

And a blue laser beam shot out of the box.

"What?"

"Avoid it!"

Even Bae Sejin and Cha Eugene, who'd been trying to look out through something resembling a window, shouted.

"Get down!"

I grabbed the guy next to me and ducked.

But right after...

*Click.*

Our surroundings were flooded with light.

"...??"

As I raised my head, I felt a light sting my eyes. A light came on in the room.

*Oh, come on.*

"It's fine now."

"Wh-what?"

I gave the guy I'd pushed down a wry pat on the back and helped him get up.

Then, I checked the box we'd just opened.

It was a huge maroon display cupboard. Beyond it was a neatly painted wall.

*Was it furniture?*

What I touched was not the wall, but the wooden furniture.

...This wasn't a wooden cabin. It was just an office.

"Ha."

An incredulous laugh escaped me.

And the other guys were laughing, too, though for a different reason.

"So the lights can come on?"

"We must have been so funny?"

"T-that was pretty tense."

"...How does this work?"

Bae Sejin touched the box. Keun Sejin laughed out loud and rubbed his hands.

"Ah, I guess the lights are activated by a laser— Aaackk!"

"What?"

I turned my gaze toward what Keun Sejin was staring at... In the corner lay what appeared to be a human corpse.

*What the fuck was this!*

"Oh my gosh!"

"Uhhh!"

"Guys, it's a mannequin."

...Correction. It was a mannequin that *looked* like a corpse.

"What is this?"

The mannequin was dressed in a uniform that appeared to be that of a security officer, and in his hand was a strange object that looked like a combination of a gun and a stun gun.

And the gun was pointed at his head.

*So I guess that's supposed to be a gun, just like the mannequin is supposed to be a corpse.*

I carefully twisted it out of the mannequin's hand and took a look.

*Hmm.*

The muzzle was blocked with glass. This...

"It looks like a laser gun."

"Yes?"

I remembered the metal box from which the blue laser had just come out.

"The tip looks similar to the one I saw on the box earlier, and the pattern is similar too."

"Oh, you're smart, Moondae~" Keun Sejin said.

"Wait a minute. S-sorry..."

"...Ahyeon's brave~" Keun Sejin said.

In the meantime, Seon Ahyeon turned over the mannequin and found a box and instruction manual underneath it.

What was in the box was a gun identical to the one I was carrying, and... Four purple cylinders that looked like they might contain live ammunition.

"Ah, here's a little explanation that seems to have been written by this mannequin at the bottom of the manual."

Keun Sejin quickly read it and summarized it for us.

A zombie virus had mistakenly been brought into this laboratory and leaked out, and the only thing that worked against zombies was this weapon that had come along with the virus.

And since the security guard had already been infected with the virus and couldn't escape, he'd used the laser gun... That was the gist of it.

"Hey, it says you can kill zombies with this?"

"Oh."

Keun Sejin pointed at the purple cylinders.

"Hey, those are rechargeable batteries... Each one holds enough charge for three shots!"

"Hmm. Is that so?"

Ryu Chungwoo picked up a laser gun and aimed it at the actor in the zombie suit clinging to the vent.

To be precise, at one of the arms of the zombie that was seen next to the cabinet.

He fired quickly.

*Gying!*

"Keyyak!"

When the laser was fired, one arm of the zombie stiffened as if paralyzed and stopped moving.

"Oh, oh!"

"Mhm, it's good."

Ryu Chungwoo shrugged and waved the gun as if to cool it off.

And spoke calmly.

"Excuse me, could you pull the cabinet away a little bit? I'll shoot it in the head."

"...Yes."

From now on, it seemed that the genre would be changed to zombie shooters rather than zombie escape.

***

The prediction was only half right.

*Giying— Bang!*

I saw a zombie sprinting down the hallway hit by a laser gun and falling over.

That was Ryu Chungwoo's handiwork. This was the seventh time I'd seen something like this.

"Wow."

"Hyung is awesome!"

"Haha."

That was the eighth time the members had marveled at his shooting. I'd bet my dyed hair that all those shots would be edited into a montage.

However, Ryu Chungwoo looked at the cylinders and shrugged.

"Now there are only two rounds left... Well, I hope we can escape soon."

"Ugh, you're right."

He'd been conserving his shots, too.

Surprisingly, he only fired the laser gun when he was sure of a one-shot kill. But we'd only had 10 shots to begin with, so we couldn't afford to waste them.

*Maybe we shouldn't have shot that zombie in the vent earlier.*

Still, what was done was done. I massaged my forehead.

*In the first place, the game probably included the guns to create potential conflict between the members.*

This was because the gameplay mainly revolved around sneaking around and cooperating.

On the contrary, it took more time to shoot and kill, so there shouldn't be a big problem with two shots left.

*They did a good job planning this out.* The way out was exciting.

From the bathrooms with a zombie in each stall to the private counseling rooms, the playrooms with creepy BGM, and the corridors... It was a fairly well-made course, but there was one concern.

"Phew!"

"Wow, two hours is a pretty tight limit, huh?"

By the time we neared the end, we realized we were running out of time.

According to the map we'd picked up at the desk earlier, the back door was at the end of this hallway, and I had a feeling it was our exit.

*And there must be a trick there too.*

However, checking Bae Sejin's watch, the remaining time was less than 10 minutes.

*We have to escape to create a good image.*

It would be a perfect finish if we escaped just in time.

"Let's hurry."

"Keep your eyes forward. If you see a zombie, just tap my shoulder."

"Yes!"

We cautiously made our way through the dark, flickering hallway to the back door, but... nothing happened.

"Oh, oh."

"I guess this is the end."

"Great! We made it!"

*This is boring.*

I quietly clicked my tongue, but I didn't stop Cha Eugene from trying to pull the back door open.

But... The door did not open.

*Beep.*

Instead, a red light flashed on the small screen next to the door.

+=
#><#r[Please scan your pass card.]r#><#
=+

We stared.

"Oh, my."

The final trick had been revealed.

"A pass card?"

The members looked at the back door with bewildered expressions for a moment, but a quick discussion immediately followed.

"Did the staff say anything about this?"

"No, they never gave us anything like that, and we never submitted anything either."

"Wow."

"Should we search the front desk?"

With less than 10 minutes remaining and such vague instructions, the members were doing their best to think things through.

It was no wonder they were nervous. I kept quiet and looked up at the ceiling.

*...There must be a hint.*

Since this was the final puzzle, there had to be a decisive hint hidden somewhere in what we'd experienced on our way here.

That would make for a neat setup and a refreshing escape.

*Whatever it is, it'll take some thinking...*

*– You're always coming up with unconventional ideas, making you an indispensable source of ideas for the group.*

"...!"

*Wait a minute.*

I lowered my head as my thoughts snapped into place.

*...I get it.*

And I started running back the way we'd come.

"M-Moondae?"

"Wait!"

There was no time to explain.

*Nine minutes.*

It was close.

I ran and calculated in my head.

Taking a straight-line route made the journey shorter than I'd expected.

*There are no zombies on the route to that room, so if I keep running...*

*We can escape!*

I ran faster through the hallway.

It was then.

*Ududuk.*

A strange noise came from across the corridor.

The sound of something collapsing.

*Peeeeek.*

And dark smoke began rising from cracks and openings in the walls.

The flashing lights in the hallway went out one by one as smoke poured from the walls. The path ahead went dark.

*The lights went out...*

I ran and reached the end of the hallway.

The smoke billowed into my face.

*The smell of something burning...*

I was standing alone in a dark, smoky, confined space.

"......"

I stopped.

Cold sweat ran down my neck.
