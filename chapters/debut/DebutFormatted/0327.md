# Chapter 327 - Independent Label, II

★$
– **[Idol of the Year “TeSTAR” wins Artist of the Year Award at ToneA]**

– **[TeSTAR wins “202X ToneA” *Daesang*]**

– **[Excited winning speech at ToneA… TeSTAR is “creating a label!”]**
$★

At first, the news about the grand prize, our acceptance speeches, and TeSTAR's prospects were widely covered.

I could only assume there were many people who had almost finished writing their articles and were preparing to press enter the moment we won the award.

If the content wasn't anything unique, the article that went up first was more likely to get more views.

For the next few hours, more articles poured in, with little more than what we'd said in our speeches tacked on.

However, the real fun started only after the awards ceremony was over.

★$
– **[TeSTAR is creating a new independent label… Is this T1’s new strategy for K-pop growth?]**

– **[T1 to create new K-pop artist label… starting with TeSTAR]**
$★

They were reporting on the surprising statement I'd made at the awards ceremony.

*Why are they creating a new label?*

In fact, it wasn't that rare anymore for an independent label to be created for a specific artist.

However, there was a difference in how it had been made public.

It hadn't been announced by the company as part of its business strategy. Instead, the artist had suddenly brought it up in his acceptance speech.

And it was at an awards ceremony hosted by the head office, no less.

*T1 is doubling down.*

*Looks like they've got something big planned.*

People who were interested in how the entertainment business worked inevitably came to that conclusion as soon as they clicked on the article.

In addition, the reaction of the fans also played a role.

★$
– Finally, finally, finally

– So the days of leaving our children with shitty subsidiaries are over? I'm really proud of you guys, really T\_T

– It's the first time I've seen Moondae so excited. He's been like this since the first grand prize. It's so cute I want to cry

– Am I dreaming?

– Independent label even before contract renewal... Give them proper support and put in a lot of manpower
$★

The fans knew how much we'd suffered because of the company, and now we'd won several grand prizes and announced our own independent label. Their reaction was explosive—in a good way, of course.

As enthusiasm and excitement filled the comments and posts, even members of the public who weren't fans started to take an interest.

They were thinking, 'Huh? Is this something amazing?'

That led to a secondary effect.

On the stock market, to be precise.

★$
[What does everyone think of the new label TeSTAR is creating?]

It's good news for T1 Entertainment's stock price, right?

– Spacer, the male idols from the new season of ⟨Idol Inc.⟩, could have overlapping positions with TeSTAR, so TeSTAR's being allowed to create an independent label. Looks like we'll see some new plans unfold next year.

– ㅋ Looking at T1 Stars' sales this year, you can see why they did it. Yeah, this is good news

└ You are correct. TeSTAR is top-class in Korea, but they're a bit weak overseas. Once they're popular domestically, popularity overseas usually follows. ^^ I'm sure they'll get bigger overseas next year, and their name value and sales will increase severalfold.

– T1 owns a 100% stake in the TeSTAR Label, right?

– Entertainment stocks are never a wise purchase... but this time I'm tempted.
$★

As the rumor that 'T1 has a big plan' spread, their stock price even went up.

Then posts analyzing the situation began to spread, until the rumor was practically accepted as fact.

★$
– **[What’s the reason T1 Entertainment’s stocks went up?]**

– **[What are the advantages of an independent label? T1’s new plan]**
$★

Now that it had reached this point, T1, the parent company, probably wouldn't be inclined to tell the public they'd never heard of any plans for TeSTAR's independent label.

If they did, it could easily be interpreted as bad news and end up hurting the company.

And even if we became independent, T1 had nothing to lose. After all, our label would still be one of their subsidiaries.

In the end, the agency lost its biggest card to use when trying to persuade or threaten us.

*The subtle threat that our relationship with T1 might suffer won't work anymore.*

And that was how it turned out.

Three days after the grand prize, the agency got in touch with us...

"The company is asking if we want to put out an article saying 'no details are available yet'."

"Oh my gosh."

*I knew it.*

Instead of taking a tough stance, the company was playing friendly and asking for our opinion.

*There we go.*

Now they were ready to voluntarily sit down for negotiations.

I nodded in satisfaction at the news Ryu Chungwoo brought us.

Most of the members seemed to find the situation a bit absurd, but despite their confusion about how it had happened, they knew it was a good sign that these bastards were willing to communicate.

Even Bae Sejin gave me a thumbs-up and mouthed the words.

*Good job...!*

"......"

I'd expect no less from him, since he'd been the first to hear about and agree to this plan. I gave him a wry nod.

However, one of the members turned red with anger. 

"I-I think they've been a little too much...!"

"...?"

...It was Seon Ahyeon. *Suddenly?*

He clenched his fists and began to explain why he was angry.

"Th-they were so mean to Moondae...!"

*Ah.* On the day of the awards ceremony.

Right. They were beside themselves that day and had even called to tell us off.

*They said something like, 'You were out of line. We've lost our trust in you,' and so on.*

I knew it would happen, but even if it did, it was just words, so I just listened to them and let them slide. 

Still, I'd apologized, saying, 'You must have been surprised. I'll discuss it with you beforehand next time,' so it wouldn't become an issue in future negotiations.

None of it had surprised me, but Seon Ahyeon must have been quite upset about it.

Seon Ahyeon clenched his fists and spoke.

"Moo-Moondae apologized... But they didn't even apologize and tried to make it seem like Moo-Moondae lied... That isn't right."

"I think so too. The company is mean. They must taste justice!"

Cha Eugene agreed. He must have learned a new idiom from the dubbed superhero movie he watched a while ago.

Ryu Chungwoo seemed to be thinking deeply, then asked softly.

"Then what do you want to do?"

"I want to protest. C-can't we put it to a majority vote...?"

"Sure, I'm fine with that. Then, let's vote."

"...???"

*Aren't you guys going to ask me for my opinion?*

They held a majority vote without asking the person who had the most right to feel insulted.

They reached a neat consensus.

Seon Ahyeon spoke firmly.

"We'll lodge a complaint...!"

"......"

I nodded uncertainly.

"Okay... I guess I'm grateful."

"N-no...! Umm, what do you want to say, Moondae?"

*Say what?*

Looking around, I realized that everyone was ready to follow my lead, staring back at me without a word.

*Well, all right, then.*

I shrugged.

"Then let's just tell them that."

"What?"

"What you just said."

There was nothing more to add. I wrote an honest reply that reflected Seon Ahyeon's position.

To put it simply, here was what it said:

★:
– We were very shocked by the company's request. We have a contract, so why do you want to say 'no details are available yet'? Were you not planning to fulfill your end of the agreement?

– We apologized because we thought the company might have been flustered by the press's reaction to what we said at the awards, but that also makes us uneasy.

– We naturally believed the company would fulfill the contract, so we mentioned it in our acceptance speech. We hope you weren't angry with us because you had no intention of doing so.
:★

To sum it up, it was 'Don't be silly, give us a plan right away, bastards'.

"Th-then I'll send it like this...!"

"Okay~~"

"It seems to have captured why we're upset very well! You are amazing!" Kim Raebin said.

Amid cheers from the members, Seon Ahyeon sent the message to the company's management.

And Keun Sejin leaned toward me and whispered.

"It won't work, will it?"

"It won't work."

The company wouldn't read that message and respond first thing tomorrow with something like, 'I'm sorry! We'll get your independent label up and running as soon as possible!'

It would just upset them and make them even more nervous.

*They'll get angry because it isn't going the way they want it to.*

In the meantime, they'd have to soothe us, so they couldn't openly play games with us. That would make them even angrier.

They'd suddenly become the disadvantaged party in every way.

A dead end, unable to move one way or another.

I chuckled.

"By the way, mistakes usually happen in situations like this."

"......"

Keun Sejin looked at me without saying a word, and then patted me on the back.

"Moondae, let's keep TeSTAR going for a long time. No, we're going to be TeSTAR for the rest of our lives. You know what I mean, right?"

"Sure."

I ended up laughing.

It felt very good to hear him say that out loud. And it was quite pleasant to be able to comfortably say yes to that statement.

And if things went according to plan, they would only get better.

A few days later, as expected, the agency made a huge mistake.

It was a fight with T1 Entertainment, its direct parent company. 

***

The agency, which had failed to persuade both the parent company and TeSTAR, could no longer release a statement as it pleased.

However, they couldn't just sit around without announcing anything. The fans were growing impatient.

★$
– Why isn't the agency saying anything?

– The articles are saying they haven't made an announcement yet. I don't like it

– Even if the label is only starting after awards season is over, they should at least make an announcement. The members wouldn't have mentioned something that hadn't even been discussed in advance
$★

It seemed like the fans would send a whole fleet of protest trucks to the agency if they delayed any longer.

Well, looking at it at this time, it seemed that my announcement also played a part in raising the amount of buzz among fans.

It seemed the shady people online who loved their conspiracy theories had really gone to town over the question, 'Why did Park Moondae say that?'

★$
– Judging by the way that glum dog grabbed the mic, I bet he invested a ton of money in the label ㅋㅋㅋ

– Is he the CEO?

– That's Moondog for you. He won't let the leader take anything from him
$★

These words were buried under the dominant public opinion, which dismissed them as malignant antis who didn't like how excited Moondae had been. But controversy of any kind only increased the number of mentions.

Moreover, since even these negative comments were based on the premise that 'TeSTAR's label will be amazing', everyone who heard the news naturally came to think that way. 

*It's the sort of business buzz that money can't buy.*

So, in the end, T1 Entertainment itself took the bait.

★$
[T1’s new vision for K-pop starts with “an artist-specialized label”]
$★

They posted the article first.

In fact, it was a declaration that T1 had no intention of helping T1 Stars stall any longer.

And did the executives of our agency give up after seeing this?

No. Hearing the rumors, it seemed that they were furious, and they sent a rather tough position statement to the parent company. 

*It was a stupid decision.*

They weren't idiots and normally wouldn't have done such a thing, but they'd been worked up over this contract issue for several days and made a mistake in their judgment.

The statement, which was not properly reviewed, was sent to the parent company... 

The agency's executives ended up being marked as insolent by T1's high-ranking officials.

The result?

"...So, at the earliest, we'll be able to go independent early next year."

What was it? A declaration of surrender. 

*– Oh my god!!*

The guy on my smartphone's video call screen looked excited.

Keundal had been curious about the situation ever since my acceptance speech at the last awards ceremony, so I'd called to tell him what happened.

Originally, I was going to do it over the phone, but I decided to make a video call to check the punk's condition. 

*Fortunately, he seems to be doing all right so far.*

His training was going well, and he seemed healthy.

*– Hyung, isn't it too cold out there? You're on the veranda, right?*

I chuckled. Come to think of it, every time I had to do something out of sight of the members, I seemed to end up out here.

I could have made a video call like this, with a screen the size of my palm, in my room, but it had simply become a habit.

"I'm okay. The apartment is expensive, so you can hardly feel a draft out here."

*– Wow... What a relief. That's really nice!*

"Yes."

I shrugged.

*– And really, congratulations on the label. As a fan, I'm really looking forward to the next album...*

Keundal, who was using the body of 'Ryu Gunwoo,' began rambling about how excited he was, and I let him continue without interrupting.

It was then.

There was a noise from the side.

*Dururuk.*

"Park Moondae, the company just got in touch—"

"Yes?"

When I turned my head, I could see Bae Sejin, holding a smartphone in his hand, hurriedly opening the veranda door and coming in.

*Huh?*

*The company?*

I tried to ask him about it after removing one of my earphones, but Bae Sejin reacted first.

He suddenly pointed at my smartphone.

"...?"

"Uh... uh, huh??"

It was as if he'd recognized someone. I stared.

"......"

*– Hyung? Hyung?*

*Hey, Bae Sejin, no way...*
