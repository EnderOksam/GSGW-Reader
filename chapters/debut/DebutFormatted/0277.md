# Chapter 277 - Another Piece of Bait

It was 4:00 in the morning, and I was running down the apartment walkway with a dog panting hard next to me.

"*Woof woof!*"

I had no idea how I'd ended up jogging with someone else's dog. Especially the dog of a bastard I could hardly stand the sight of, even though I hadn't seen him in almost half a year.

I held back a sigh and looked to my side.

"Kongi is running well. Good boy."

The guy heaping praise on his dog for doing what dogs normally do was... Cheongryeo, who'd emerged from the fog.

*Damn it.*

I recalled how things had come to this.

The guy who'd appeared was dressed in light sportswear and trying to get his dog under control.

@r@– I always take him for a walk when there are no people around, so I guess he got excited when he heard someone running nearby. I'm sorry if he startled you.@r@

He was far too calm as he said it. So I replied.

– I think you knew that it was me.

The answer to this doubt was as follows.

@r@– A pink-haired guy jogging around this apartment complex at dawn... Doesn't that narrow down the pool of candidates considerably?@r@

– ...

@r@– I can see the color even in the fog.@r@

It was an explanation I couldn't refute. I stifled a groan, realizing that the cap I was wearing hardly concealed my hair color.

Of course, since it was 4 in the morning, I thought no one would be there.

He even had an explanation for why he was taking a walk in this part of the apartment complex.

@r@– I have something to do during our break from touring, so... I'm living in the dorm again for a little while. And there aren't many people in this part of the complex at dawn.@r@

It was true.

@r@– I think you're here for the same reason, right?@r@

– ...

Okay.

To sum it up, it was a coincidence brought about by our similar jobs and circumstances.

*Hoo.*

I accepted the situation and tried to leave quickly.

– Okay. Then, I'll just—

@r@– We're going in the same direction anyway, so why don't you slow down a bit? I think Kongi would start barking if you ran off.@r@

– ...

@r@– It'd be a nuisance to hear that at this early hour. Isn't that right?@r@

I glanced at the yellow lump of fur wriggling in his arms, its little paws waving about. I reluctantly turned around...

*"Woof!"*

I... had no idea how a bastard like him had managed to raise such a cheerful, innocent dog.

*There are security cameras installed, so I don't think he'll do anything.*

I held back a sigh and started jogging down the trail at the same pace as Cheongryeo.

The dog and its owner ran alongside me at about the same speed. We didn't talk as we jogged.

However, the problem began after that.

"Drinks?"

"I already have some."

At the end of the path, I drank some water from the bottle I'd brought with me. Cheongryeo fed his dog a snack.

"You should wear a beanie next time. It'll cover up your hair color better."

I had no intention of coming out here to jog at this hour ever again.

"......"

Instead of answering that, I just nodded. Cheongryeo shrugged his shoulders as he stroked the dog eating snacks.

As he bent over the dog, I caught a glimpse of his hair under the hood... It wasn't black.

*Wait a moment.*

I inadvertently opened my mouth.

"You dyed your hair?"

"Ah, yes."

Cheongryeo tucked his hair back under his hood.

The dark green strands vanished beneath the cloth.

*Or was it gray?* I couldn't see very well because of the fog... Actually, it wasn't the color that mattered.

"You've never dyed your hair since your debut, have you?"

I had never seen VTIC's Cheongryeo dye his hair once when I was a data collector.

I didn't know what was going on, but there seemed to be a drastic change in their concept. I needed to get a hint of what VTIC was planning for their next comeback, which would be a repackage album.

I looked at the biggest obstacle to this year's grand prize.

*I have to admit it.*

These guys still had incredible power.

VTIC had been going just as strong since their comeback in February.

*I thought it was about time their popularity started to decline.*

They were at a point in their careers where there was scarcely any room left to grow, yet VTIC had somehow managed to extend their prime even further.

★$
– VTIC's first-week sales are in: 1.92 million~~~~

└ Are they crazy?

└ Fuck, are they going to keep this form up until they have to go to the military? ㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋ They've been around this long and they're still doing so well. I'm so proud of them and our country.

– VTIC's world tour resale tickets are also sold out. They're not slowing down anytime soon

– I wonder if they will be a first-rate group until they retire
$★

It was hard to believe that a group that had already renewed their contract was still in this kind of form.

Their February comeback had interpreted the feel of Broadway in the 50s in a very sophisticated and dark way, and the fans had been delighted with it.

I checked it out too, and nearly cracked open a beer.

*The quality continues to exceed expectations.*

This was unavoidable.

I hoped this would be their only comeback this year, but I wasn't counting on it. They'd probably go all out in the few years they had left before their oldest member had to enlist.

*He dyed his hair, so it's confirmed.*

They were preparing something. It'd be a dramatic change, and they were clearly putting a lot of effort into it.

"I wonder what kind of surprise you have in store, if you're going so far as to dye your hair for the first time."

As I waited for his reply, I closed the water bottle and crossed my arms.

He... just grinned at me.

"Well, I think it would be less surprising than pink."

*Fuck.*

"Fans like it."

"Is that so? Congratulations."

He'd changed the subject without giving me a single clue. I nodded inwardly.

*He's deliberately avoiding saying anything.*

He was wary of TeSTAR, too.

I thought back to the messages and calls we'd sometimes get from the VTIC guys.

It felt like they were monitoring us.

This was the text I'd received near the end of TeSTAR's January promotions.

★:
-Everyone, I watched the variety show ⟨Dance Over Flowers⟩ you guys appeared in. We're going to be on it soon, too. Haha
:★

This was a message from the concert.

★:
-Wasn't combining the two songs an amazing idea? Raebin-ssi... Any chance you'd give some of your songs to another group?? (_Face with tears of joy emoticon_)

-^^b
:★

★:
-The documentary was so touching ㅠㅠ
:★

Well, as for that one... They'd done a quick about-face after Cha Eugene's incident came to a head.

Anyway, they were keeping close enough tabs on us to consistently check all the content TeSTAR had put out this year.

They might have told themselves they were cheering us on, but subconsciously, there was an urge to keep an eye on the growth of the second-place group coming up behind them.

*...This year must be really intense.*

I nodded inwardly.

However, the topic that Cheongryeo brought up next was unexpected.

"No, I haven't dyed my hair this time around."

The guy tilted his head to the side.

"However... I didn't know that you would know that."

"......"

"Have you been doing a lot of research on me? By any chance, when you were in your original body, were you our fan instead of a Marshmallow fan? Ah, there are people who like all the artists under the same agency. But men like that are... rare, I suppose."

*What a creepy guess.* I was getting goosebumps.

*Damn it.*

I held back a sigh and put the water bottle in my backpack. I quickly ran the calculations in my head.

*Good.*

The situation gave me a chance to tell him the truth.

"I wasn't a fan of anyone."

"Then what were you?"

I said it intentionally.

As if I were distracted by the dog running around my feet and had let my guard down, I answered casually...

Like this.

"To be honest, I wasn't an idol fan, but I sold idol footage to fansites when I was in college. Naturally, I saw plenty of idols whose footage was worth money, including you guys. That's how I know."

"Ah."

I deliberately revealed information about 'Ryu Gunwoo'.

The fact that I was a data collector when I was in college.

It was an action with a clear purpose.

*Track me down.*

I wanted him to find out more about 'Ryu Gunwoo' in this world.

"That's all."

He'd said it himself, after all.

I remembered it from when he told me about Youngrin's surprise release at the end of last year.

*– You didn't know. You don't have anyone bringing you information, do you? Hubaenim, that's... going to be your biggest problem from now on.*

This meant that he had enough sources of information.

In fact, his knowledge of Youngrin's upcoming release, something only those closest to her had known about, was proof of that.

*He probably doesn't limit his information gathering to the entertainment industry.*

To control all external factors, he needed to know what was going on with anything connected to people in the entertainment industry.

Then, of the cards I could play... This guy was my best bet for finding out more about Ryu Gunwoo without getting Park Moondae into trouble for ‘illegally tracking a civilian’.

*You're right that I don't have an informant. So I'll use you, who does have one, to get information for me.*

The video at Ryu Chungwoo's house had confirmed that Ryu Gunwoo had definitely existed in this world, so I might be able to use this guy to find out whether he was still alive.

*It doesn't matter if he actually finds him.*

After all, if this bastard tried to tell anyone what he knew, it'd sound like nonsensical bullshit about me having switched bodies.

And realistically, 'Ryu Gunwoo' could not be my weakness.

*He has nothing of value, after all.*

Around the time I graduated college, all I had was money to last for three years.

But this guy didn't know that.

To keep TeSTAR, their toughest rival, in check and control any possible variables, he would definitely look into 'Park Moondae's original body'.

Until now, that must have been impossible with the limited information he had.

*His only clues were my original name and approximate age.*

So, let's see... I hoped to get a reaction out of him by adding ‘data collector’ to ‘Ryu Gunwoo’.

*Because there was a lot I could deduce from his reaction.*

If my guess was right, this guy was busy doing the math himself right now.

I ended my speech casually.

"As for Marshmallow... I wasn't exactly a fan, but I did like them casually."

"......"

I petted the dog and smiled. Holding the dog's leash, Cheongryeo looked at me with a strange expression.

"More than I thought... You were something of an industry insider."

*He's calling a guy who sold idol footage an industry insider?*

"It was just a side gig to make some pocket money."

"Haha, judging from the way you operate, I doubt it was just a simple side gig. Well, I guess that experience gave you a good eye for what was worth trying and what wasn't. Interesting."

He muttered to himself, putting his free hand to his chin.

It was foggy, so I couldn't see his expression clearly... But he seemed to be lost in thought.

*Okay.*

*Take the bait.*

Soon, a soft voice was heard.

"Still, a guy in his 20s with a camera... That's pretty unusual. Tell me what you looked like. You filmed VTIC too, right? I think I might remember you."

*There.*

*He's trying to get more information out of me.*

I rubbed behind the ear of the dog, who was now lying on his back.

*Here... I can't let him realize I'm giving him information on purpose.*

Because if this bastard knew I was after information myself, he'd demand an outrageous deal or try to go behind my back. *Or both.*

Answering too readily would look suspicious, so I shook my head instead.

"I just went around wearing glasses and a hat. There were plenty of people who looked like me."

"Hmm."

That should give him the impression that I was being careful.

However, the actual information was sufficient.

*A man in his early 20s, glasses, a hat, and a camera.*

Surely, if he looked through the right years of event footage stored by the agency, he'd be able to pick out faces that might belong to 'Ryu Gunwoo', even if the footage was low quality.

Data collectors like me could usually be found in one of the front rows, holding large cameras that stood out from the rest of the audience.

*And if I'd made enough money selling data, he'd guess that I'd attended university in Seoul.*

Main broadcasting stations and performance facilities were mainly concentrated in Seoul.

*Then, if you search by matching the year, university in Seoul, age group, name, and face...*

"Hmm."

If he was as skilled as I thought, he might be able to find out what 'Ryu Gunwoo' had been up to lately in no time.

*Done.*

I shrugged and got up.

There was no need to say something like ‘I've said too much’ here. That would only make things more awkward.

The most natural thing to do now was to leave.

"*Woof!*"

The dog at my feet let out a disappointed whine and stomped back and forth.

"I'm going in now."

"Well, yes. Goodbye."

Cheongryeo picked up his dog and spoke casually.

"I'm going to be walking around here for another 15 days or so, so if we happen to meet again, say hello to Kongi."

"...We'll see."

"Haha..."

Holding back a sigh, I left him behind and made my way out of the fog on the path.

"...Hoo."

I thought I handled it well, but somehow I got tired when I met that bastard.

***

A few minutes later, I entered the dorm building, finally feeling the unpleasantness fade.

*Is the fog some kind of debuff or something? Ha!*

Well, I just had to wait for the results.

If Cheongryeo wanted to make a deal with me, then as soon as he found 'Ryu Gunwoo', he'd have no choice but to tell me at least something about 'Ryu Gunwoo's current condition'.

*Good.*

I'd done a clean job of luring him in.

I shrugged and got on the elevator. My mind was working quickly again.

*Is it because I went to bed early yesterday?*

We had an active schedule this afternoon for the first time in a while, and I'd slept a little longer than usual.

As for the schedule...

"Now, let's start assigning rooms~"

"Wow!"

And just like that, the roommate assignment content was back to fill the ambiguous gap in our latest hiatus.
