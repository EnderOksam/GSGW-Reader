# Chapter 149 - ⟨Bespoke Singer⟩, II

The week before the semifinals and finals of *Bespoke Singer* were due to air.

The impressive hologram character, unlike anything the show had featured in a while, caused a little buzz online.

★$
– They seem to have run out of fresh ideas. All they've done lately is remake sports mascots. It's nice to see something new.

– The profile and concept art is coming out this week, right? I'm looking forward to it. It's been so long since my heart raced when watching TV. I'm flustered...

– I was surprised that his voice was so good, but he completely ripped apart the next stage. The bouquet head made my heart pound

– From the first stage that seems to symbolize a groom on a spring day to the next stage where the beauty of tragedy is alive. Everything was good. (#b_link_b#)

– He's really good at singing, it's his definite victory ㅋㅋㅋㅋ
$★

Of course, because it was of high quality and didn’t lack anything else, he naturally got popular.

And, of course, most of Park Moondae's fans had guessed.

That the Groom in May would be Park Moondae.

★$
– Honestly, it's obvious

– It's so obvious. If you can't tell, you don't have eardrums

– No, his skills got better. His voice seemed to have improved a lot since the American program, but how is this possible

└The consensus is that it's because he's a genius

– Just look at his avatar. It's obviously something Moondae would chooseㅋㅋㅋㅋ

└ ㅇㄱㄹㅇ The guy who made Magical Boys hasn't lost his touchㅋㅋㅋ
$★

But it never reached the wider public.

After months of hostile reactions, TeSTAR's fans were reluctant to sound too certain when their opinion differed from the majority.

They kept the speculation low-key, even among themselves, so trolls couldn't take screenshots to ridicule them later.

The long-awaited *Bespoke Singer* episode finally aired.

Even people who rarely watched live tuned in to see 'Groom in May', including these siblings.

Among them were this brother and sister.

"Look. It's Park Moondae."

"Yeah, you're delusional~"

It was a college student who was a Kim Raebin fan, and her younger brother.

The younger brother was prepared for his older sister’s curses to come flying as soon as he babbled, but it didn't happen.

"...*Huft.*"

The college student put up with it and just laughed it off.

It was the confidence of those who knew the outcome.

"What's with you?"

"Fine. Just watch the broadcast."

The pissed-off high school student huffed and turned to the TV.

Still, as soon as the broadcast started, the siblings forgot the fight.

Because 'Groom in May' appeared in the first match of the semifinals between the remaining three cast members and one winner of the last episode.

**[The audience is buzzing]**

**[Hey~ I'm looking forward to it]**

**[I don't know what kind of performance he'll show us!]**

It was a reaction full of high anticipation from the audience and judges who had grasped the situation through the last two stages.

With captions and comments intersecting, the 'Groom in May' finally appeared on the stage.

"Heol."

"He's here."

The college student noticed that the butterflies, veil and glove patterns now looked more natural and intricate.

It seemed they slightly reinforced the hologram.

*I guess they poured some money because of the reaction?*

The main vocalist of the first-tier idol group came out, so the college student snorted, saying that it was only natural.

"Hey, here it is! Here it is!"

"Be quiet!"

On the dark stage, only the hologram shone alone.

And the accompaniment played out.

The sweet and clear sounds of violins and oboes filled the space.

The stage was filled with bright, glaring lights. With the flowers swaying, the groom raised his right hand and gently placed it on the microphone.

$p
♪ *My world*\
*It's a place without sadness*\
*I live where there's only joy*\
*And I'll share this gladness with you* ♫
p$

"Huh? That..."

"Shh!"

After the sweet line, a lower and more desperate line followed.

$p
♪ *My world*\
*It hasn't a single dark place*\
*The starlight*\
*It'll shine upon your face* ♫
p$

"Wow...."

This time, the siblings opened their mouths and looked at the screen, forgetting to prevent each other from getting in the way.

'Groom in May' was singing a duet song that a man and a woman sing together.

It was a very romantic song, famous for its fairy tale lyrics and lyrical melodies.

And another characteristic of this song was that it was often used as a wedding song.

$p
♪ *Sometimes we'll have*\
*Sad days and bad days*\
*But stay in this world for me* ♫

♪ *Let my love*\
*Wrap its arms*\
*Around you*\
*And protect you with its warmth* ♫
p$

In the original song, the lyric was composed and solidified by two people exchanging melodies with each other.

However, sung alone by 'Groom in May', the wedding song had changed its nuance and completely turned into a song of perfect courtship.

Unlike the graceful marriage proposal song in the preliminaries stage, the composition that crossed the heights of men and women, like the tail feathers of courting peacocks, captivated the ears.

*It really sounds nice.*

*It's such an old-fashioned song, so why does it sound this good?*

The brother and sister were completely immersed in the song and listened to the rest of the song in silence.

The subtitles were frantic too.

**[※Groom in May expresses his love※]**

**[※That sweet, captivating voice...※]**

The faces of the judges with closed eyes and the judges with their eyes wide open looking at the stage crossed each other.

"He's going to the final round, right?"

"Definitely."

Watching ‘Groom in May’ bow once again after finishing the song and then slowly disappear, the siblings expressed the same opinion for the first time in a long time.

And, just as they predicted, 'The Groom in May' advanced to the finals.

**[The 2nd finalist is... 'Groom in May']**

"Yeeeess!"

“It would be a farce if the show didn't put him through.”

As if watching a sports game, the siblings swung the remote control at the TV excitedly.

And the profiles of the semi-finalists were introduced at length.

This content was to fill the time, but there were cases where a well-made character profile caught people’s interest.

The Groom in May was like that.

★$
– Crazy... He can only sing love songs until he meets his bride;; What's with the concept

– So he could only sing 'Winter Night' in the main round because he met his bride and lost her?

└Heol

└ I love stories like this

– The bouquet head only has flowers that bloom in May... The Groom in May... If he gets married in another month, the flowers change

└Please brainwash him until he becomes the Groom in June. It's June right now

└ㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋ

– When he gets married, the head of the flower changes to the bride's ideal type. This setup is too much but it's great
$★

As soon as the profile appeared, the college student browsed through SNS and felt proud.

*Park Moondae is really good at things like this!*

And she was sure once again. This must be Park Moondae!

"What would you do if he is really Park Moondae?"

"I think you'll get humiliated if you keep being like this."

"Hey, do you want to bet 100,000 won?"

"Wow, thank you for the allowance."

Right before the final stage, the siblings even decided to make a high-stakes bet.

The two people that couldn’t back out anymore stared at the TV with serious faces.

“He'll still win even if he messes up badly in the last round, right?”

"Saying that from my point of view, yes."

The two skimmed the first final performance. A whole fried chicken decorated with foil ribbons sang an upbeat samba, but it wasn't especially impressive.

And finally... It was finally his turn.

The Groom in May reappeared on stage.

**[Wooow!]**

Red butterflies fluttered around the flowers behind the white veil.

'Groom in May' held his standing microphone with both of his hands covered in white gloves.

And the stage went dark.

*Clang.*

Several beams of light shot down from all directions toward the hologram.

It was dark red.

"...!"

The lights shook distortedly.

Then, an ominous sound began to ring out.

*Shhhh-shhhh-*

The sound of the sogeum[1]{T/N: The sogeum is a small bamboo transverse flute used in traditional Korean music.} leaking like the wind was mixed with the frantic sound of a flute going back and forth between notes, creating a strange atmosphere.

A slow drum beat rang out.

*Boom... Boom... Boom...*

It was chilly.

In that eerie atmosphere, the Groom in May began to sing.

The white suit was stained with mottled red light.

$p
♪ *It's the final day of April*\
*No moon's in the sky*\
*My love's nowhere to be seen*\
*And love rots, left to die* ♫
p$

A slightly hoarse, scraping sound echoed across the stage.

Perhaps because of the shaking of the lights, the flowers of 'The Groom in May' seemed to be swaying.

As if he would soon wither.

$p
♪ *I've yearned for the longest time*\
*But life is never so simple*\
*I open the window*\
*And the road is nothing but a faded symbol* ♫
p$

The lyrics spilled out over anxious keys as the pitch climbed ever higher, as if about to leap from a great height.

Then the notes plunged down low.

$p
♪ *But I'm still here* ♫
p$

In line with the low note, the accompaniment exploded.

"...!!"

Amid the rushing sound of traditional Korean instruments, 'The Groom in May' bowed his head, burying the flowers in the stand microphone.

$p
♪ *And I'll stay until my mind rots*\
*And my love evaporates*\
*Until I'm buried under soil*\
*And my memories deflate*\
*And even then, I'll wait* ♫
p$

The melancholy melody began with a very low tone and rose frantically.

In the repeated lyrics, the countless metaphors all ended with one word.

*Waiting.*

The screen didn't even show the audience's reaction. Only the 'Groom in May' was illuminated on the red-colored stage.

There seemed to be an invisible gaze from the veil and beyond the bouquet.

Even beyond the screen, they could feel the desperate feeling as if being overwhelmed.

And at last, it arrived.

A high-pitched voice that sounded like a cry raised the final chorus lyrics.

$p
♪ *And until she finally comes*\
*And finds me, tells me I'm beautiful* ♫
p$

The final note followed without him seeming to take a breath.

Chasing after it, the gloomy accompaniment ran frantically... Then, it stopped.

$p
♪ *I'll wait* ♫
p$

"..."

A faint sound of the sogeum finished the song.

In the place where fear and madness left, a painful and sad aftertaste remained.

With the bouquet on the standing microphone, the frozen 'Groom in May' didn't move.

And the moment the accompaniment was completely cut off.

He disappeared from the stage as if blown away.

**[Oooooh!]**

**[Wow...!]**

Only then did the audience seats fill with applause and admiration.

The camera finally showed the judges’ stunned expressions and the audience covering their mouths finally appeared.

**[※The audience is spellbound※]**

**[※They can't seem to take their hands from their mouths※]**

And the siblings who were frozen in front of the TV also came to their senses.

"...It's really crazy."

"I'm getting goosebumps."

"It's Moondae's win."

"You're crossing the line again."

The performance told of a groom driven mad waiting for a bride who never came, leaving a tremendous impression.

He could have gone overboard, but his chillingly perfect vocals made the performance work.

*I'll watch live the week after next, when he defends his title.*

Convinced of the victory of 'Groom in May', the high school student made a decision in his heart.

He had to watch.

He even thought about buying and listening to his album once his identity was revealed.

*He's definitely a band vocalist. Maybe from some obscure indie band.*

So when the 'Groom in May' revealed his identity shortly after, he was completely frozen in shock.

**['Groom in May', the identity of the person who created this hologram character... Revealed now!]**

Just before announcing the winners, the identities of the cast members were revealed in advance to make up the picture of them waiting for the announcement with their holograms on stage.

The hologram of 'Groom in May', whose motion link was released, stood calmly on the stage. He looked somewhat quiet for someone who had sung such a miserable song just now.

He could hear the MC's loud voice.

**[Come on in!]**

At that moment, the bottom of the stage opened and the person began to appear.

And people started making strange exclamations.

"...!?"

Because what came up was... the 'Groom in May'.

"What?"

The actual 'Groom in May', who had come up all the way, tilted the bouquet.

He looked just like the hologram, giving everyone goosebumps.

"*Gasp*."

Until now, animals and food had been playfully reproduced using doll clothes, but it had never looked this similar before.

Most contestants had chosen holograms that couldn't realistically be recreated as costumes.

*What is this bastard doing?*

Among the buzzing audience and the judges, only the MC shouted cheerfully.

**[This person is...!]**

The newly come-up 'Groom in May' ripped off the bouquet from his head with his right hand.

"...!"

A thick mesh mask covered with flowers flowed down to his neck, revealing a sweaty face and hair.

**[The popular idol, TeSTAR's Park Moondae!]**

**[!!!!]**

The screaming audience and the judges who deliberately pretended to be more surprised were captured and replayed continuously on the screen.

And the high school student was stunned, too.

"..."

"Hey, hey."

*Tuk-tuk*, the college student tapped her brother with her foot.

"Give me 100,000 won."

By the way, a similar situation was happening on the internet in real time.
