# Chapter 172 - Roommate Roulette, II

Let me explain what the hell this was about.

This time our roommate assignments were decided with a land-expansion board game that used cards.

"Now, Yeouido belongs to my Beet Coin Faction! Everyone hand over a coin!"

"...H-Huh??"

"Already? Sejin is very good."

"Haha, thank you!"

For reference, this unfamiliar game had complicated rules, a long playtime, and, crucially, had little to do with roommate assignments.

It turned out to be a new board game that used the IP of ⟨127 Section⟩ that we had collaborated with.

*It must be PPL from T1.*

I was used to it now.

Anyway, the production team somehow managed to fit this into the roommates' rearrangement, allowing us to choose roommates in the order of the top players in the game.

And here was the result.

"And Eugene's Flame Bike Gang owns thirty-six lots, putting him in first place!"

"Yeah!"

"Oh~ That's too bad!"

Cha Eugene made a comeback victory and won at the last minute.

*I can't believe he rolled six three times in a row.*

Thanks to his incredible luck, Cha Eugene triumphed, and by then everyone was screaming like gamblers, not even caring who won.

And Cha Eugene... He pointed me as his roommate without hesitation.

"Moondae-hyung, I choose you! Let's have fun!"

*Just why.*

When did we get close?

Even the option to distribute the responsibility by inserting another guy was blocked.

"Hmmm, then room choice of the first-place winner Eugene..."

"Number 1!"

It was a room for two people.

"Oh~ Moondae is returning to room number 1? Congratulations!"

"...Yeah."

It didn't make me happy at all.

Of course, I had no intention of doing anything crazy like expressing this openly.

I couldn’t say anything positive, but I still gave Cha Eugene positive signals, such as high-fives and shoulder taps.

*I shouldn't provoke Cha Eugene's fans.*

Those people wouldn't pass this off as a gag, they'd squeeze me out and turn me into the maknae's bully.

So calm down, let's share a room with this guy and think of the benefits...

...Is there any?

"Then, what is the choice of the second-place winner Sejin?"

"Yes, my choice is... Da-dum-da-dum. It's our cool Raebin!"

"Ah, thank you!"

"Haha! I think it would be nice if we could have the three-person room."

"Well, the third-place winner is Moondae, so let's skip him... Coincidentally, it's Raebin's turn to pick roommates since he won fourth place. What do you think, Raebin?"

"I want Seon Ahyeon-hyung to join as the last roommate!"

"...! M-Me?"

"Yes! I'd like to ask you a few questions since we were roommates last time..."

I thought about it as the others were picking roommates one after another, but I couldn't think of any good points.

But I knew one thing.

"Let's have some gummies!"

"Okay."

It would be difficult to go to bed early for a while.

And this judgment was not wrong.

– Stop.

If it had gone on any longer, I would’ve smacked the back of his head and forced him to sleep.

*I'm going crazy.*

Of course, it wasn't always like this.

There were times when Cha Eugene went to bed early, but the problem was that it was all up to him.

Still, looking at his attitude, it seemed he was trying to keep his word, but the problem was that he was the kind of person who wouldn't store things in his head if he thought it wasn't a big deal by his standards, so he always forgot.

*Should I scare him?*

Of course, it was a joke. The moment Cha Eugene showed a timid look in front of the camera, there would be rumors of discord.

I could usually ignore it, but I'd already left evidence of my uncooperative attitude toward Ryu Chungwoo in several videos from a few months ago.

The summer package.

*That's not good.*

So for now... I would put up with it.

However, judging by the trend so far, these roommate assignments would last for more than half a year, and I wasn’t sure I could avoid showing even the slightest sign of discomfort around this bastard.

*How did Ryu Chungwoo endure being his roommate in the first place?*

Well, come to think of it, Lee Sejin was there, so Cha Eugene's aggro must have jumped at him.

"Eat a lot."

"Yes!"

*It's like my thoughts disappeared.*

If I just let it flow naturally, it often felt lacking.

As easy as it was, I had to pay more attention so that it didn't look plain.
