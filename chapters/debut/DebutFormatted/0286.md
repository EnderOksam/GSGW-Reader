# Chapter 286 - A Battle for the Throne, II

"Shall we talk it through first?"

"Okay."

"Yes!"

Keun Sejin started writing down the topic on the whiteboard to one side of the conference room.

It was an emergency meeting that started in a hurry after midnight.

<br>
#*#><$$TeSTAR's comeback date$$><#*#
<br>

Our comeback date was about to overlap almost perfectly with VTIC's.

*Originally, the agency decides these things on its own, and idols with only a few years of experience like us aren't supposed to have any say...*

But after everything we'd been through, we'd ended up involving ourselves in just about every part of the process.

Well, that was what I intended anyway. It was more comfortable to do this. If someone else decided and our album failed, I'd go crazy.

"First of all... Right now, our comeback date is here."

Keun Sejin drew a long timeline and marked the beginning of July.

Monday, July 5th. This was the day originally set as the comeback date.

"It was a choice that gave us a week's worth of sales on music shows, a full month's worth on the July charts, and all the sales from July, August, and September for the third quarter! Hey~ Whoever picked it sure knew what they were doing, right?"

It was a date unanimously agreed upon by the company and the members. I nodded.

"And VTIC probably had the same idea."

"...!"

Of course, they did. If they were coming back in July, they would've gone with the most efficient choice.

Seon Ahyeon cautiously opened his mouth.

"Th-then... Would they choose the same date...?"

"They'll time their album release for Billboard, so it won't be exactly the same."

*The day that'd give them full representation on the Billboard charts would be...* Keun Sejin was already writing on the whiteboard.

<br>
#><$$[VTIC sunbaenim's comeback date]$$><#

#><$$[Billboard is Friday!]$$><#
<br>

"Friday! At this rate, it must be Friday, July 2nd~"

"Hmm."

It was only 3 days from our comeback.

July 2nd and July 5th.

The two marks on Keun Sejin's timeline were far too close for comfort. The other guys stared gravely at the whiteboard.

Except for one guy, of course.

"Is there a problem? Do we have to avoid VTIC sunbaenim?"

It was Cha Eugene.

Of course, at the very next moment, the guy who had Cha Eugene marked one-on-one shot him down.

"Cha Eugene, it's not a personal matter! We're talking about both teams losing out in many of the evaluation categories based on relative market share!"

Kim Raebin proudly explained the calculation method he'd only learned while preparing for this comeback.

*He’s making good use of something he’s only known about for less than a month.*

To put it simply, no matter how many albums either group sold, we'd be sharing the same pie percentage-wise because we'd released them in the same timeframe.

*Both groups will lose impact and their short-term results will suffer.*

However, Cha Eugene didn't seem impressed.

"No! We got first place last time! There's no problem. We can just focus on doing our own stage!"

"Last time?"

"Our debut album!" Cha Eugene said in English.

*Ah, the debut album we dragged things out for weeks to get #1 in digital track sales?*

*That was only possible because VTIC was away on tour.*

In fact, the timing worked out amazingly well for us. It usually didn't go that way.

The others seemed to be thinking the same thing as they chimed in.

"That was just luck."

"Ah, that's right. We didn't win first place right after it came out, so to be honest, it's a bit different~" Keun Sejin said.

In addition, Ryu Chungwoo gently soothed Cha Eugene.

"What Raebin means is that we don't have to take such a hard road if we can avoid it. It'll make things harder for us and for VTIC."

I didn't need to open my mouth for this.

"Boo..."

Cha Eugene reluctantly gave in to the majority opinion.

*Then everyone except Cha Eugene wants to change the date...?*

I looked around and organized my thoughts.

What options did we have?

"First of all, we can't move the comeback any earlier. There's the music video schedule to consider, too."

"That's right, we'll also release our teasers separately. No matter how much we move it up, a week seems to be the limit."

And moving it up by a week wouldn't change anything. After our week on the music shows, VTIC would come in and wipe us out.

"Okay. So we should probably consider postponing it..."

Ryu Chungwoo carefully added his words.

"The company suggested August or September."

"Mmm."

We'd lose at least a month of potential sales and streams before the end of the year.

*That seems dangerous. It's still difficult to beat VTIC in terms of performance as it is.*

Still, it was a relatively safe choice in terms of avoiding a greater risk. At least we could stay at the top of the music show rankings for about 2-3 weeks.

But that didn't suit me.

*I should avoid making choices that could cost us the grand prize.*

I wanted to choose a date that would improve our chances of winning a grand prize rather than help us win first place on music shows.

But this... I could speak only when there was a clear alternative.

*Usually, I'd just choose August and end it here.*

I shook my head, trying to think of something to say before everyone settled on that direction.

It was then.

"But I don't want to put it off that long."

"...!"

Ryu Chungwoo spoke first.

"What? Really?"

"Wh-why not?"

"We could easily win first place on music shows right now, but in the long run, I think we should stick to our original plan."

The guy grinned.

"We should aim for the grand prize this year."

*No way...*

*Is he trying to...?*

"Ohhh, hyung!" Keun Sejin said.

"It is a wonderful aspiration," Kim Raebin said.

"I like that too!" Cha Eugene said.

Everyone reacted strongly because he rarely said things like that.

And I was convinced after our eyes met.

*He's doing everything he can to help me.*

He'd taken me seriously when I'd told him we needed to win the grand prize.

*Good.*

That made it much easier for me to speak up.

"Then, if we try to preserve our original schedule as much as possible... Let's analyze the expected performance."

I took a pen and paper instead of using the whiteboard Keun Sejin had occupied.

"First of all, VTIC sunbaenim will have a shorter sales timeframe than us when it comes to physical albums, and it's a repackage album, so I don't think their numbers will be dramatic."

I didn't have to mention that the group was getting on in years, too.

"Then let's estimate 1.93 million sales based on the previous album."

A terrifying number.

"Yes. And we..."

If TeSTAR did well in our first week, we could potentially aim for 1.3 million.

Honestly, that number was pretty crazy, too.

*A million is no easy feat.*

Still, there was a gap we couldn't overcome.

"...If we stop at around 1.3 million, we won't be able to catch up in album sales."

"Hmm."

But there was more than just physical album sales to consider.

"However, we tend to do better than them in digital track sales."

"Oh, right."

TeSTAR had an advantage over other male idols in that regard. Mass appeal.

This was thanks to the fact that while adhering to our concepts, we released easy-listening songs with every other release and often secured exclusive appearances on popular variety shows.

*Of course, I can't deny that debuting through Idol Inc. gave us a huge advantage.*

Anyway, that was why we could count on long-term digital track sales.

"That's why, if no other major group decides to compete with us and we release at the same time as VTIC sunbaenim, the second week will be..."

*Uh, wait.*

*At this rate...*

"...!"

"What is it, Moondae?"

I looked over what I'd written down. And I realized something.

*I see.*

I'd been so caught up in how much we suffered from releasing at the same time as VTIC during our debut that I'd overlooked something.

We weren't debuting anymore.

I spat out the answer almost unconsciously.

"Let's let VTIC go first."

"Huh? Wh-what?"

"We only need to postpone it for one week. Then..."

Bae Sejin intervened.

"Wait, then it'd be the same as when we debuted."

*That was the point.*

"No. The timing might be the same, but the circumstances are different."

I turned the paper towards Bae Sejin.

"Once VTIC's first week is over, they'll be in their second week while we'll just be starting our first. There's no contest there. We'll win that week."

"...!"

"And the digital charts... Once we get in and things stabilize, our sales will start to grow along a similar curve. Maybe we'd even be a little higher."

I wrote the comparison down on paper.

!-
\
**Albums**

VTIC (Week 2) < TeSTAR (Week 1)

**Digital tracks**

VTIC (Week 2) ? TeSTAR (Week 1) (Graph suggests we'll be able to hold our own)
-!

Just looking at it, it was clear we'd win on music shows.

*This can't be overturned even with broadcast scores.*

We'd make a comeback on Monday and our music would hit the radio right away!

The number of social media mentions and music video views wouldn't fall behind VTIC's, since we'd be in our first week.

Now, we were a top-tier group ourselves.

"The situation from our debut would repeat itself, but this time we'd definitely take the win in our first week."

This was a huge boost to our reputation.

"And it doesn't matter if things even out by the third week. No, as long as we aren't completely crushed by them, we stand to benefit no matter what."

Keun Sejin nodded.

"Mm~ We've grown so much that now it'll look like we can hold our own even against VTIC sunbaenim, right?"

"Yes."

Following the simultaneous release with Youngrin in January, we'd be cementing our position.

It would show that we could release at the same time as a competitor who usually won a grand prize and still hold our own.

*And comparing this to our debut would make for a great story. People would definitely talk about it.*

There would be social media posts and articles pouring in that it was a signal of a generational change.

That was the best angle we could hope for.

*It's almost impossible to beat VTIC in physical album sales this year anyway. And in digital track sales, we have Youngrin to contend with.*

Since our strength lay in doing well in both categories, there was only one grand prize we could realistically aim for this year.

***[Artist of the Year]***

As the most popular group this year, we could make it seem like the awards ceremony was giving us the grand prize because the public thought we deserved it—a 'public opinion award', so to speak.

*Our music and album performance will be top-notch, so no one will have grounds to complain.*

It was a gamble, but it was worth a try.

"But if we do this, VTIC fans will really hate it, you know?" Keun Sejin said.

"I-is that so?"

"Yeah. They'll feel like their group got the short end of the stick because of the timing!"

That was the part I had to accept.

A confrontation that generated this much buzz would always come with backlash, drama, and fatigue.

"Yeah, I think the f-fans will have a hard time too..." Seon Ahyeon said.

"Hmm."

In fact, it wouldn't matter if we did as well as I'd expected. The thrill of winning would outweigh the fatigue of fighting VTIC's fans online.

*Wouldn't it help with fandom solidarity, too?*

The new season of ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ was about to end, and internal strife was bound to start up again. I thought designating VTIC as a common enemy, so to speak, would be a good plan.

And just as my thoughts reached that point, I received some unexpected support.

"If we do well, it's OK! No need to worry. Hyung, let's do our best! The fans love us!"

It was Cha Eugene.

It seemed like this guy was just shouting 'OK' because we wouldn't have to delay our promotional schedule...

"How is it? Hyung, we're good, right?"

"Y-yes. W-we're good...!" Seon Ahyeon said.

"That's what I say!" Cha Eugene exclaimed in English.

There was no one like him when it came to stubbornly pushing ahead. I opened my mouth, watching how Cha Eugene had gotten Seon Ahyeon on board before Ahyeon even realized it himself.

*That might be useful, but this is something all of us need to think through carefully before making a decision.*

After thinking it all through, we had to agree. We couldn't leave room for disagreement after making a comeback.

"I just said my opinion, but I'm sure some of you don't agree. Why don't we think it over for a while and then raise our hands to vote?"

"Not a bad idea."

I proposed a majority vote, and the members readily agreed.

And after thinking it over for a while, we voted.

"......"

"Oh, it's unanimous~" Keun Sejin remarked.

Surprisingly, not a single member voted against the idea.

*I didn't expect that.*

I thought they might need some persuasion.

And those I'd thought would oppose the idea began explaining their reasons one by one.

"I-I don't think there's any need to be afraid already... For the fans, I think all we need to do is give it our all...!" Seon Ahyeon said.

"I've concluded that, given the concept, if we make our comeback at the end of summer, we won't be able to fully showcase the song's appeal. Moondae-hyung, you made an excellent call," Kim Raebin said.

The last was Bae Sejin.

"...Come to think of it, if we postponed our schedule just because another group was coming back too, it'd feel like we'd already lost without a fight."

"......"

*Uh... is that right?*

Bae Sejin slightly averted his eyes. Ryu Chungwoo laughed and explained.

"He knows listening to you has never led us astray."

"......"

*Well, right. Haha.* I rubbed the back of my neck and nodded.

"Then, let's go with this date?"

"Yes!"

And so we agreed on our comeback date.

However, I still had a question in my mind about Cheongryeo's dyed hair and secrecy.

*What the hell is VTIC planning?*

And I would soon find out the answer, even if I didn't want to.

Because just two weeks later, VTIC released the first teaser.

***

★$
[Shocking changes coming to the music industry in July… Huge boy idol group is after the throne!]
$★

In mid-June, when even TeSTAR revealed the news of a comeback in July, social media turned upside down.

The office worker who was a fansite master for both Park Moondae and Lee Sejin was keeping track of how things were going behind enemy lines in her spare time.

★$
-Kkkuyeokkkuyeok, they're really forcing their way into the same month ㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋ

-They'll be a dead group after five years. I guess they're just jealous that our boys renewed their contracts and kept going
$★

*Same old nonsense.*

The office worker, having logged into VTIC's anonymous fansite, clicked her tongue. It was going to be a tiring month.

*Why are they both so eager to make a comeback in July?*

Did they have some amazing summer song ready?

*They're catering to their fans anyway. Why obsess over seasonal songs?*

The office worker did the math and decided to film VTIC whenever their schedules overlapped. At least she'd make some extra money selling VTIC fancams.

*This company and that company seem to have lost their senses. No, T1 originally had no sense.*

Later that day, VTIC's teaser was released.

★$

👍 👎  ㅤㅤ ㅤ➦    ㅤㅤㅤ⫶☰  ㅤ ㅤ ㅤ•••
$★

"...!!"

The office worker was stunned, her mind filled with exclamation marks.

*Wait...*

It was definitely a summer concept.

However, the key point was a bit... unconventional.
