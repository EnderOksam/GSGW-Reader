# Chapter 65 - The First Album, I

It was common for a group debuting in a survival program to film a reality show.

This was because they could carry over the buzz and popularity gained from the audition program.

*...Still, I didn’t expect to go straight into filming right away after one meeting like this.*

Even during the meeting, I didn’t hear much about it.

It was just an interview about what the members wanted to do.

*It’s a relief that the dorm is good at least.*

Maybe they were conscious of the filming, but the dorm was more splendid than I thought. It was the first time I stayed in a large new apartment with such good security.

Of course, there were cameras on every corner.

“Ah, camera!”

“Hello!”

The guys who took off their shoes at the entrance and ran to the living room nodded at the camera. It was an action that had been requested in advance.

*They said it’s unnatural to act like we haven’t noticed the cameras.*

In the first place, with this many of them around, it would be difficult not to notice them.

Even in the living room, there were several cameramen and production crew waiting in the blind spot of the unmanned camera.

From what I heard, they would stay briefly today to shoot footage for the reality show’s early episodes, then withdraw, leaving only the unmanned cameras behind.

“First of all... Congratulations on passing, everyone~!”

“Wow~!”

“Our proof shot!”

The passing participants sitting around the living room clapped and took a picture together before starting the reality show.

“Wow, it feels like a new team match has begun.”

“I think you’re right, except that the team match will last five years.”

“Oh~ Raebin’s good at this!”

Fortunately, everyone looked natural.

*This is how you benefit from living with the camera all the time in ⟨Idol Inc.⟩.*

In addition, the atmosphere was friendly with the joy of passing.

They were at least three times less sensitive and much more tolerant than they were at the time of the audition, exchanging good wishes and jokes and laughing together.

“You all did a great job.”

“That’s right. I feel like I can enjoy anything now!”

“L-Let’s have a good time...!”

And at the right time, the crew sent a robot vacuum cleaner out from their hiding place in the cameras’ blind spot.

It was the mascot of this reality show and the main PPL.

“Huh?”

“Hey, it’s got something!”

A card covered with glitter dangled near the vacuum’s head.

Quick-handed Cha Eugene lifted the vacuum cleaner and pulled out the card.

“Is there a message on it?”

“Yes!”

Cha Eugene excitedly read the contents of the card while still holding the robot vacuum cleaner under his arm.

“Hm... ‘You guys... From now on, you’ll receive missions through cards! Clear the missions to strengthen your teamwork!’”

“Ah!”

“M-Mission?”

The atmosphere darkened slightly. They were still a little traumatized by missions.

Ryu Chungwoo laughed as if he had given up.

“Haha, we passed the audition, but the missions still don’t end, huh?”

I thought bitterly.

*...To succeed as a group, I think our agency needs to get its act together.*

I thought of Tnet, which had notified us of our new agency assignment three times in a single day, only three days ago.

I had a bad feeling.

*It seems the success went to their heads.*

Because the program was so successful this time, T1 clearly wanted to manage TeSTAR by creating a new agency of its own.

However, since there were already contracts with Tnet, a T1 affiliate, taking the group away was probably difficult.

So, because they were busy fighting each other, the group was in a state of neglect.

We only learned from an article this morning that we would be releasing a debut song within a month. But we didn’t even have a manager yet.

*It’s driving me crazy.*

I held back my sigh.

At that time, Cha Eugene raised his hand.

“Oh! Oh! There’s more! ‘Today’s mission is... Free time!’”

“Ooh!!”

In an instant, the atmosphere turned relaxed.

I also showed how happy I was about the break, exchanging high fives with the guys around me.

*Let’s not think about it.*

It was almost confirmed that we would join T1’s new agency anyway.

Rookies had no authority. I just had to accept it.

*...Please don’t put your relatives in charge of the music and concepts.*

I hoped the high-ranking people would take care of the sales, accounting, and management and leave the creative work alone. Please use people with experience on the job.

“Let’s look at the rooms! Let’s decide who gets which one!”

The passing participants stood up with the ‘free time’ card and started touring the house.

Three rooms and three bathrooms. That was plenty for all of us.

And there was a number sign in front of each bedroom.

**“TeSTAR’s first~ mini-game! The subject is choosing a room!”**

“You sounded the same.”

“R-Really.”

Kim Raebin volunteered to imitate the MC of ⟨Idol Inc.⟩, but he sounded so similar that it triggered our PTSD and made us solemn again. Still, the game went on without a problem.

We picked notes from a box in the order that was determined through several board games with so many random elements.

“This also has a strange deja vu...”

“I know right...”

Every time it overlapped with ⟨Idol Inc.⟩, the kids got sentimental.

Anyway, I pulled out the second note after Cha Eugene and unfolded it.

It was number one.

*I avoided number three.*

Bedroom no. 3, the main bedroom, was shared by three people. Naturally, two people were much better than three.

*I wish I could share a room with a quiet guy.*

For now, I had dodged Cha Eugene.

*I’ll just have to dodge Keun Sejin.*

As soon as I thought so, Lee Sejin, who was next in order, pulled out number 1.

“...”

Right before an awkward silence could fall, Keun Sejin and Ryu Chungwoo jumped in.

“Oh~ Moondae and Sejin-hyung are in the same room~!”

“Both of them are decisive types, so I think they’ll get along well.”

“Right?”

It was fortunate that there were quick-witted guys.

“Take good care of me.”

I quickly reached out my hand, and Lee Sejin slowly held out his hand and shook mine.

*I’m a bit worried about this filming.*

I liked that he seemed to be the quiet type.

However, the camera would be running for at least 2-3 weeks, so the key was whether or not this guy would pretend to be friendly during that time.

*The production team will take care of it.*

It was not an audition, but a reality show, so I figured the editing would go well.

It wasn’t good for the production team if there was a controversy.

“Let’s quickly pick one too~!”

The remaining rooms ended up being shared by people with opposite personalities.

Kim Raebin and Seon Ahyeon were in the same room.

“P-Please take care of me.”

“I look forward to your kind cooperation, and please take care of me as well.”

Looking at the two guys who weren’t close to each other having a greeting battle, I thought it would be uncomfortable over there, too.

Of course, they were still much better off than me.

First of all, neither of them had to deal with a personality like Lee Sejin’s.

“Oh~ Our room looks really fun!”

“I like the big room!”

“Okay! Let’s get along.”

And room number 3 for 3 people... Keun Sejin, Cha Eugene, and Ryu Chungwoo shared the room together.

It was already noisy. However, Ryu Chungwoo looked somewhat satisfied.

*Come to think of it, Ryu Chungwoo’s position is a little awkward.*

Except for Cha Eugene, Ryu Chungwoo didn’t know any of the winning participants very well.

It would be better to get into a crowded room and settle in quickly.

The fact that there was also Keun Sejin, who was friendly and polite to his seniors, must be the icing on the cake.

Following the crew’s signal, we moved to our bedrooms so they could film us unpacking and resting.

“Which bed would you like to use?”

“It doesn’t matter to me.”

“Really?”

In that case.

I went straight to the bed on the left and unpacked. Our room was opposite the living room, so there would probably be less noise coming through the wall.

By the way, Lee Sejin was also looking at this bed, but he didn’t say anything.

“...”

It’s not a survival show, so why should I please you?

I planned to do whatever I wanted as long as it wouldn’t draw criticism.

Lee Sejin looked this way without saying anything, then went to the other side and unpacked.

With a calm mind, I started unpacking my things. Actually, I didn’t have much, so I didn’t have anything to organize.

Oh, I bought some clothes because I didn’t have a lot of them. It was with the prize money that my team won by a narrow margin in the final.

I heard that the production team had only announced the result on the Internet after the program ended because they couldn’t finish counting the votes in time. They got criticized a bit, but well, it was fine because I still got the money.

By the way, the kimchi refrigerator... I hadn’t received it yet. There were no other swindlers like them.

*I’m getting a little pissed off.*

By the time I folded the last of my clothes, holding back a sigh, the quiet room was filled with the sound of human voices.

“Ta-da!”

“As expected, room number 1 is the best. Is it because this is where first place is staying?”

It was the guys from room three.

Ryu Chungwoo, a former national athlete, had taught the others how to unpack, so they finished organizing in an instant. Cha Eugene and Keun Sejin were now happily wandering around the apartment.

“Is there anything you want to eat for dinner?”

Ryu Chungwoo smiled and sat down on the edge of the bed.

“How about galbi?”

“Jokbal!”

Cha Eugene was understandable, but it seemed Keun Sejin was being extra on purpose to get more screen time.

...Suddenly, I had a foreboding feeling that I wouldn’t be able to escape this ruckus while the reality show was in progress.

* * *

We had to cook and eat dinner together. Thanks to this, I cooked for more than 2 servings for the first time in a long time.

*Cooking is better than going to the grocery store.*

Once I went out, I had to talk more. Because there would be fewer people with me.

In addition, it took quite a bit of courage to enter the large mart with three cameras.

Anyway, the cooking was quite successful. Surprisingly, the others were a big help.

Except for Seon Ahyeon.

“S-S-Sorry...”

“You have nothing to apologize for.”

I think you should apologize to your own hands instead of me.

I kicked Seon Ahyeon out of the kitchen after he almost cut off his own finger for the third time.

Seon Ahyeon, who was anxious about having nothing to do, was relieved when I gave him the job of setting the table instead.

*...That’s why the old lady came to his house.*

I was wondering why a guy who was good at keeping things tidy needed a housekeeper. It was because he was terrible at cooking.

Anyway, the jjimdak was well done. It was Ryu Chungwoo who picked this dish after winning rock-paper-scissors.

*I tried to cook it on my birthday because eating out was a waste of money, but it ended up being useful like this.*

I shrugged my shoulders as I recalled my birthday in the first year of earning money from selling data.

“It looks delicious.”

“Right.”

“...”

Lee Sejin did not respond in particular to Kim Raebin’s words, but he was surprisingly cooperative in cooking.

*Is it because I am holding a knife?*

Realizing I was still holding the kitchen knife, I put it away. In the meantime, the jjimdak was taken to the table in the hands of the grocery shopping group.

“Oh~ The jjimdak is really good!”

“The chicken has soaked up the soy sauce well, and it’s tender.”

The meal was a success.

“Should I learn how to cook, too?”

“I’m good at cooking.”

“Oh? Then why didn’t you cook?”

“But grocery shopping is better.”

With Cha Eugene’s bright remark, the reality show’s first shoot was wrapped up.

“Thank you for your hard work~!”

As the filming team withdrew, they installed a claw machine in the living room.

All the dolls inside were placed with the back of their heads facing up.

*It would be creepy to see it at night.*

Anyway, they asked us to act surprised when we saw it in the living room tomorrow morning, then try to win one of the dolls.

*More content for the show.*

I heard that we would meet the manager tomorrow and finally start discussing the album with the agency. That probably meant we’d be getting to bed late tomorrow.

I quickly entered the room with the determination to sleep a lot today.

Lee Sejin was already in bed and looking at his smartphone.

“Good night.”

“...You too.”

Is it because he ate well? He seemed a little more relaxed than in the morning.

I immediately put in earplugs and went to bed. The last thing I saw was the camera running alone in the corner.

*I hope the people we meet tomorrow don’t make any absurd demands.*

And this wish was only half fulfilled.

* * *

The manager was a decent guy.

“If you need anything, just tell me!”

He looked gentle and patient. He seemed like the kind of person who wouldn’t lose his temper or treat us rudely.

*Indeed, we’re already popular enough that he probably has to treat us with some respect.*

“Once your album is out and things get busier, there will be another manager to help, so don’t worry!”

“Yes!”

The first manager would probably set the tone for later hires, so I decided not to worry about that for now.

And the meeting with the agency.

This is...

“Our talented TeSTAR can handle making the song, right?”

“...? Yes?”

To sum it up, we were fucked.
