# Chapter 366 - Ending the System, II

Kwon Heeseung had just seen shocking proof of the supernatural with his own eyes—his own 'mission' displayed through a hologram!

It took Kwon Heeseung several moments to come to his senses.

"H-h-h-h-how uh, does this... How does this work??"

*Correction. I thought he'd come to his senses, but his mouth is still moving faster than his brain,* Park Moondae thought.

*Ahhh!*

*What kind of sci-fi movie is this? A Limestone movie?*

Despite Heeseung's panic, Park Moondae's voice over the phone was calm.

*– I don't know the exact principle, but... It's like hacking.*

"Hacking...? But this isn't a computer!" Heeseung half-shouted.

*– ...*

He didn't say anything. Park Moondae seemed to fall silent for a moment, considering what Heeseung had said.

And he answered, sounding mildly amused.

*– You might be on to something.*

*Onto what?!*

*What is this, the metaverse? VR? AI?*

Having used up all the IT terms he knew, Kwon Heeseung had somewhat recovered from the shock and was about to ask in more detail, 'How on earth did he do this?'

Park Moondae struck first.

*– Anyway, you saw something earlier, right? The specifics of your mission.*

"Hmm."

Although the rather unpleasant phrase 'status abnormality' bothered him, perhaps what this sunbaenim was asking was not that, but the content underneath it.

Kwon Heeseung opened his mouth reflexively.

"Well... Something about releasing a collaborative work, I think?"

*– Okay.*

"It's amazing. So it's really a self-produced album..."

Kwon Heeseung felt an electric thrill course through him.

He felt like the protagonist of a movie. He wasn't sure if that was good or bad, though.

But Park Moondae was cool-headed.

*– No, it's different.*

*What?*

*– The standard isn't a self-produced album, but rather, a collaborative work.*

"Yes... But isn't that what it is?"

*– No. A collaborative work doesn't necessarily have to be an entire album.*

"Ah."

Park Moondae smoothly applied his extensive experience to the problem.

*– It's much easier to get the public to recognize something as a 'collaborative work'.*

"Wow."

He sounded like a real secret organization agent.

Kwon Heeseung, who became a little more serious, politely held the smartphone with both hands and asked a question.

"Then... I can probably succeed with this album, right? The odds are good?"

*– Yes.*

*Oh, oh!*

Kwon Heeseung was briefly filled with admiration, but stopped to consider things a little more.

He wasn't stupid either. He considered the possibility that Park Moondae was plotting something or lying to him.

*Hmm...*

However, no matter how much he thought about it, he couldn't see why Moondae would need to.

*If he wanted to screw me over, he's already had plenty of chances.*

Park Moondae had been taking good care of him so far, so there wasn't a need for him to suddenly go behind his back and reveal things like... these supernatural phenomena just to manipulate him.

*It's not like he's some psychotic supervillain. If he were planning something like that, I'd already be screwed.*

Kwon Heeseung decided to accept Park Moondae's favor this time too!

Just then, Park Moondae spoke in a cautious voice.

*– Want me to help you?*

"Oh my gosh!"

Kwon Heeseung accepted right away.

"I'm so grateful, hyung! But does this mean I'm going to owe you one—"

*– I'd tell you beforehand if that was what I wanted.*

He sounded rather nonplussed.

Kwon Heeseung scratched his head. "Sorry. That's usually how it goes in movies, you know? Are you still going to help me? Hehe..."

Park Moondae chuckled.

*– Sure.*

And Park Moondae really began to thoroughly help Kwon Heeseung.

***

The first step was the production process.

Park Moondae skillfully picked out an aspect of Spacer's upcoming album where they could earn some easy extra credit.

*– The lyrics are your best bet. They don't get much scrutiny, but unlike a photobook that's mainly for fans, they're still an important part of the album.*

"Oh, oh."

*– Even if an amateur contributes, it's unlikely to ruin the result. And since the lyrics aren't revealed in advance, they're comparatively easy to change.*

"You're right... You're totally right!"

Kwon Heeseung thought he should tell this to the member who doubted his own creativity and had refused to participate in the production of the album so far.

However, the very next day, he saw that the member had offered his opinion on the lyric-book design.

"Oh, what's this?" Heeseung asked.

"I don't know. The company suggested I give my opinion."

The revised lyric-book design was approved within two days.

*...This fast?*

He hadn't known his company could make decisions this quickly.

Kwon Heeseung was a little confused, but he didn't have time to dwell on it as work continued.

The physical albums were manufactured, and the promotional material was revised and sent out.

★$
[Leaps and bounds toward the dream!]

Every Spacer member was involved in the production of ⟨Fighting Spirit⟩, from the tracks to the album inclusions. Coming your way soon! 💥
$★

It wasn't the main focus of the publicity. It was just an additional introductory phrase that let fans know about it and made them think, 'That's right.'

It fit perfectly with the album's concept, which centered on their dreams and aspirations as idols, so nothing felt out of place.

A finishing touch that neatly added the essential information without damaging Spacer's original plans.

*Wow. I didn't think this would actually be possible.*

Kwon Heeseung was impressed.

Even though Park Moondae had lived longer than he did before returning to the past, wasn't this level of skill—and, dare he say, *authority*—another matter entirely?

*There are rumors that Moondae-hyung single-handedly set up the entire label. Don't tell me they're true?*

He'd believed the rumors going around the company were false, since he'd asked Moondae about them before and Moondae had denied them. Apparently he'd just been modest.

Kwon Heeseung nodded, but at the same time felt a little creeped out.

*If Spacer grows big enough to become a competitor to TeSTAR someday...*

*...TeSTAR sunbaenim will have gone independent by then!*

Yes, he heard that next year was contract renewal season. Of course, they would run away from this crappy company. *Haha!*

Kwon Heeseung decided to move on for now. What was the use of dwelling on it any longer? *Let's focus on the here and now!*

So he immediately thanked his benefactor.

"It's all thanks to you! Oh, thank you so much! I will repay you!"

*– Alright.*

Park Moondae's answer was short, but not unfriendly. On the contrary, his voice sounded gentle.

Excitedly thinking that he should treat Park Moondae to lobster once he got paid, Kwon Heeseung happily shouted.

"I think I'm in the clear. I am, right?"

*Now I'm free!*

But it was too early to celebrate. Park Moondae gave an ambiguous answer.

*– It's true that you met the conditions.*

"...Uh, what do you mean?"

A calm voice answered.

*– You'll see soon.*

*Wait, what?*

"What?"

There was a burst of static.

"Wow!"

A glitching hologram appeared before Kwon Heeseung's eyes again, followed by another strange message.

★-
\
#><[A Successful Release!]><#

You successfully released a collaborative work!

!Time cap: Satisfied (Successful)

!Status Abnormality: **'Make It Work'** removed!

**{[: Reveal the *^@5#&{** ☜ Click!
-★

"...!"

The word 'success' appeared multiple times, and things looked much better than the last time Park Moondae had shown it to him.

The button glittered brightly, making it hard to ignore.

*This... Can I just press it?*

Kwon Heeseung looked at the 'Click!' button and raised his hand as if possessed...

Park Moondae's voice sounded like cold water being poured on him.

*– I mean, you said you'd repay me, right? About that.*

"...?"

Kwon Heeseung, who was reaching out for the hologram, paused for a moment.

That moment...

*– Don't press the button.*

"Wh-what?"

*– Waiting a few days before pressing it is how you can repay me.*

*No, how did he know?* Kwon Heeseung jerked his hand away as if he'd touched something scalding hot.

"Wh-why?"

*– Hmm.*

After being silent for a moment, Park Moondae spoke in a voice with a hint of laughter.

*– Are you interested in doing something useful with a single day of your vacation?*

"...??"

***

Beneath the stage of a large concert hall in Seoul, the audience's cheers still rang in our ears. The members around me laughed.

"Oh my, everyone worked hard~" Keun Sejin said.

"It was fun!" Eugene said.

It was the end of a joint concert hosted by a credit card company.

"Moondaemoondae, are you going to sit in the back?" Keun Sejin asked.

"Yes."

The guys were in a great mood on the way home from work after a successful performance without a single mistake. I sat in the back seat.

And I looked back at the conversation I had with Kwon Heeseung yesterday.

*Here's the premise of my plan.*

I could control when the status abnormality cleared by choosing when to click 'Reveal the Truth.'

And that was entirely thanks to the status window.

*So I thought about applying that concept in reverse.*

Anyone could do the same thing, as long as they had access to a status window.

*– If you can give someone with a status abnormality access to the status window, wouldn't you be able to control when it gets cleared?*

So, Keundal accessed Kwon Heeseung's system and opened a status window.

$p
**[It's a bit uncomfortable... Ughhh]**
p$

Of course, he said it was more inconvenient than using my own status window, but this guy succeeded without any negative side effects.

Since this access came from legitimately clearing a mission rather than trying to cheat the system, Keundal seemed to have even more control.

*Good.*

It was inspired by Keundal changing the state of my status window, so I was glad that it worked without any problems.

And Kwon Heeseung quickly accepted my suggestion that he lend us some support before we destroyed the system.

*– Of course, I'm all for it! Doesn't it feel like we're on a secret mission?*

*...It would have been better if he hadn't talked as if we were at a VR arcade.* Anyway, I was glad we were helping each other.

*Good.*

I nodded and adjusted my neck pillow.

The way I'd arranged the timing gave me some wiggle room too.

There was a slight lull in my schedule after promotions ended and before preparations for the tour began.

*I guess I can take a day off.*

If all went according to plan, one day would be more than enough to see the results. I nodded to myself as we arrived back at the dorm.

*I planned to spend the rest of today reviewing my plans—*

"Moondae-hyung, could we please talk for a moment..." Raebin said.

"Mhm, sure."

*It'll have to wait.* It looked like he had something to talk about. I followed Kim Raebin.

I didn't know why, but all the members were gathered at the kitchen table.

"...?"

*What are you all up to?*

"Moondae," Chungwoo said.

"Yes."

"We have some time before the tour."

*Yeah,* I was thinking that too.

*What about it?*

Ryu Chungwoo smiled and turned the screen of the laptop on the table to me.

"Shall we go here then?"

"What?"

What was on the screen... It was a page for a camping resort.

★$
[Relaxing Glamping]
$★

Beautifully composed photos slid across the screen automatically.

*Woodland walks, hot springs, countryside, animals...*

*Familiar keywords.*

The word 'relaxing' reminded me of ⟨The House Next Door's Grandson⟩, while the other keywords were things the production team had promised for our next shoot.

*What?*

Keun Sejin grabbed my shoulder.

"Ahyeon said he went there on a family trip! He said it's really good too!"

*Okay. The facilities and appearance looked good. If a family as wealthy as his had gone there, I was sure it was luxurious.*

*But why suddenly?*

I looked at Seon Ahyeon. Ahyeon flinched.

"M-Moondae, I heard you liked the town where we filmed the variety show... I didn't want that sort of place to carry bad memories for you."

"...!"

"That's why I l-looked into it."

I was speechless.

"D-do you dislike the idea?"

"...It's not that."

I answered.

"And... I didn't feel that bad."

It had been a little embarrassing, but I'd enjoyed our stay—at least until I learned what the production team was really up to. That only made the whole thing more embarrassing afterward.

"But they didn't give you anything delicious—" Eugene began.

"Cha Eugene, be quiet!" Raebin said.

*Being annoyed and being angry are two different things, you bastard.*

But I had no intention of saying that now to those people who were watching me nervously.

Bae Sejin quickly added in a word.

"Okay. Well, anyway, there's no need to go to the hot springs with the variety-show team. Let's go by ourselves!"

"That's right. Then how about we just think of this as a holiday trip and go have fun?"

Ryu Chungwoo finished speaking.

I chuckled.

"Sounds good."

"Oh~" Keun Sejin said.

"Then it's decided!" Chungwoo said.

"OK!" Eugene said.

The guys relaxed and started talking amongst themselves.

"Should we all go on a cruise on our next vacation? If we go to Europe or somewhere like that, no one will recognize us," Keun Sejin said.

"Oh, that's good too," Chungwoo said.

"What is a cruise ship?" Eugene asked.

"Cruise ship."[1]{T/N: Cha Eugene didn't know what cruise ship was in Korean, so Moondae says it in English here for him!}

"Oh..."

"You don't like them, Eugene?" Chungwoo asked.

"My grandpa loves cruise ships. But I'm still young," Eugene replied.

*You really never change, do you?*

*Indeed.*

The next morning, for the first time in a while, I spent the morning cooking a variety of dishes for no particular reason. I didn't have to worry about leftovers, since the members ate everything without a word of complaint.

It was funny, but our big meal also felt like a kind of ceremony before a big day.

*A trip after we're done, huh?*

I chuckled. It wasn't intentional, but it all felt almost domestic.

And in the afternoon...

I called the last person whose cooperation I needed.

The call went through almost immediately, and I heard a voice answer with a dog barking in the background.

*– It's been a while, Hubaenim. What do you need?*

"Let me borrow your private jet."

I heard laughter.

*– For what purpose?*

I cracked my neck.

"To make sure nobody else ends up in our shoes."

It was time to put an end to the system.
