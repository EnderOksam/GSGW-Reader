# Chapter 334 - The Aftermath

Keun Sejin believed me.

*How did this happen?*

I had no idea. I even opened the status window last night to see if something had been affecting the situation in my favor, but nothing seemed out of the ordinary.

Keun Sejin had been completely himself, and he'd accepted my explanation.

*This... is reality?*

I still couldn't believe it. But... Of course, it wasn't that I didn't like the outcome.

It felt a bit odd. I'd never been this honest with anyone before.

I hadn't even expected his attitude to remain unchanged after hearing everything.

Thanks to that, we seemed to have gone a little... overboard at today's concert.

*I feel like my lungs are about to burst.*

"Here you go!"

"Th-thank you."

I gasped as I took the towel and smartphone a staff member handed me.

*He didn't even need this.*

In fact, this was the fallback I'd prepared on my phone for the revelation last night.

*Keun Sejin is a practical person.*

And I thought I'd need real evidence to convince a practical bastard like him of bullshit that would normally only come out of a web novel.

But could there be such a thing?

There was nothing but circumstantial evidence.

*Testimonies of those involved.*

The only cards I had to play were people's statements, so I tried to secure at least a variety of them.

First of all, Ryu Chungwoo.

*– Of course. If I can help, I'll do it.*

He'd told me to try talking to Keun Sejin first.

Next was Keundal.

*– Uh... You want me to have a video call with Lee Sejin?*

This guy welcomed the idea. Nothing more to say.

I even arranged a meeting for Keundal and Keun Sejin at a later date.

The problem was...

"......"

Wouldn't there be some suspicion if I only asked people who liked me or whom he didn't personally know?

From Keun Sejin's perspective, the picture would only be convincing if someone who was actually at odds with me was involved.

Cheongryeo.

*– Really?*

He seemed to wonder, 'Why are you doing this?', but he didn't refuse.

He just said this.

*– I don't mind, but... I wouldn't recommend it.*

*– He won't believe you.*

*Yeah, I fucking know.* Actually, I'd thought so, too.

But he believed me.

*And all it took was an explanation.*

I wasn't going to question my good fortune. Maybe I was just out of my mind from lack of sleep, but when it was all over and I went back to my room late at night, I felt a thrill running through my head.

Thanks to that, I did something last night that would've caused a massive shitstorm of controversy if it had gotten out on the Internet.

In the early morning before going to bed, I texted an idol who was very much my senior.

★:
He believed me.-
:★

And this was the reply I received this morning.

★:
-Is that so?

-Bring him to see me sometime ^^
:★

*Are you crazy?*

*Why is he inviting us over like it's nothing?*

I didn't know what he was planning. I took my eyes off my smartphone uneasily.

It was then.

"...What are you doing?"

"I was checking if anyone had texted me."

"I see."

Bae Sejin spoke. He hesitated for a bit before continuing with an expectant look on his face.

"You guys... made up, right?"

I laughed. I hadn't had time to tell him because we were too busy preparing for the concert, and now he was asking as soon as it was over.

"Yes."

"Whew..."

Bae Sejin let out such a long sigh of relief that he looked as if he might sink into the ground.

*That's one hell of a sigh.* I looked at the guy again in surprise.

We'd gathered later than usual that morning thanks to this guy.

"Thank you."

"Wh-what?"

"For delaying the meeting time. And for telling Lee Sejin to talk to me."

"...! D-did you hear?"

"Yes. Not on purpose, but I overheard it. Thanks to that, I found the courage to apologize."

Bae Sejin's face reddened. I chuckled.

"But you didn't even have to apologize to him."

"I know! I was just trying to start a conversation...!"

"......"

*Really?*

"Things seemed so bad between you two..."

"......"

*Was it that obvious?*

Bae Sejin looked so relieved that his soul might fly out of his mouth.

"I-I've never been in a situation like this before... I don't know if I did the right thing."

"You did."

*That's what I've been telling you. Are you even listening?*

Still, I understood what Bae Sejin meant. Had that guy ever tried to mediate between two of his peers before?

*Even when he was in school, he seemed to have gone out of his way to avoid other people.*

I guess he didn't know that his first attempt would work so well.

"Yeah, anyway... I'm glad you guys made up..."

Bae Sejin looked burned out, as if the new experience had drained all his energy, but he was very proud of himself.

*Should I make him a late-night snack?*

Just as I was about to speak, I saw Keun Sejin stop talking to Ryu Chungwoo and start approaching us.

*Don't tell me he and Ryu Chungwoo planned to talk to us separately?*

I'd never been treated like this before. Keun Sejin approached, gave me a few glances, then started talking to Bae Sejin.

"Uh, hyung."

"Huh?"

"Thank you. I heard from Chungwoo-hyung that you arranged for us to meet later."

It looked like Bae Sejin was thinking, 'Did he tell him that too?', but pride soon seemed to win.

"Well, we're a group. It's best to take these things into account for each other's sake. ... And! I wasn't the only one who thought that way... Everyone else agreed too."

"......"

"Well, um, you might be wondering why I interfered with work, but this is also..."

"I don't think so."

"...!"

Keun Sejin rubbed his neck. *This bastard's embarrassed, isn't he?*

"I didn't think hyung would pay so much attention to this. No, I'm not being sarcastic. I really mean it."

"......"

I was going to leave them and slip away, but I decided to stay and watch in case there was another dogfight.

Bae Sejin didn't seem angry, but his expression grew subdued.

"Why would you say that...? You thought I wouldn't care?"

"I thought you'd avoid getting involved even if you were worried about the other members. This sort of thing isn't very pleasant."

"...!"

Now I was the one who was surprised.

*Would you look at that? This bastard's actually saying exactly what he thinks for once.*

Even Bae Sejin, who'd been filtering his words so carefully, began to speak more freely in response.

"No, it's not exactly pleasant."

"Yes."

"But we had the concert today, and I got involved in your fight. ... I did what I could."

"......"

"I know you would've done the same."

"...!"

Keun Sejin looked as if he'd been slapped in the face. Bae Sejin, on the other hand, looked pretty confident.

"Kuhm, isn't that right?"

"Well..."

At that moment, Keun Sejin relaxed his expression and smiled.

"Of course, I would. Hyung, anyway, it was all a huge misunderstanding. Sorry."

"......"

"And thank you. Really."

"Yes. Well, I'm sure it wasn't easy, but it looks like things turned out well."

The atmosphere lightened up a lot. I shrugged.

"...Park Moondae! You did a great job too!"

He dragged me in because he was embarrassed.

"Don't mention it. Let's go down."

"Oh, yeah."

Only then did the three of us start walking briskly down the hallway. All the others had already gone to the waiting room.

In the meantime, Keun Sejin suddenly laughed and even made a joke.

"Is that why you were out in the living room when we were roommates? Did you do what you could?"

"No... I was just uncomfortable."

"......"

"......"

"Well, all right."

"Ahem, if we become roommates again... Let's try talking more."

Keun Sejin took on another challenge.

"Hyung, even when I spoke to you, you didn't even answer properly!"

"What! I really just had nothing to say!"

*I knew this would happen.*

But honestly, it was a huge improvement that they were telling each other what they didn't like.

*They must have known in their heads that there was no malice between them, but I guess it hadn't really sunk in.*

It seemed to have sunk in now. I shrugged.

"You know I really don't understand hyung sometimes, right?"

"I know. I don't understand you either."

It seemed this was about as far as Lee Sejin and Bae Sejin's arguments would go now.

It was a big step forward.

*Things won't explode even if they're left alone together.*

I was quite happy about this unexpected bonus.

* * *

When I entered the waiting room and got ready to leave, I went through a similar process.

*– Hyungs! Welcome!* Kim Raebin said.

*– Hyung, this is delicious. Eat it!* Cha Eugene said.

They tried to hide it in their own ways, but it was obvious.

*'I know that you guys fought and reconciled, and I'm proud to have contributed in some way'*... That was the atmosphere.

Even the staff smiled as we passed by, as if rumors had spread. *Damn it.*

*– Ahaha, thank you~*

Keun Sejin couldn't stop laughing. He was probably deeply embarrassed that everyone knew. *I know because I felt the same way.*

Even so, he quietly said something to me.

*– Park Moondae, if you run into any problems later, tell me anytime. And I'd like to meet that person you said you'd introduce me to.*

*Right. If he wants to see it with his own eyes, I don't think it's a bad idea.*

*And it seems like he and Ryu Chungwoo have already talked on their own.*

...I felt a little more light-hearted. I straightened my shoulders. Then another guy caught my eye.

Seon Ahyeon.

He was sitting alone in a corner looking at something, and he was startled when he noticed me looking at him.

"...? Wh-what...?"

"It's nothing."

Come to think of it, this guy must have felt pretty uncomfortable, with the two other members his age having just fought and made up.

*I didn't expect so many people to find out we'd fought.*

Besides, there would be more situations in the future when I'd have to leave this guy out of certain conversations.

I decided to put a clean end to this whole thing.

"I just wanted to say I'm sorry if we stressed you out."

"N-no...! I was worried, but... I-I knew you would make up."

Seon Ahyeon smiled faintly, then added in a small voice.

"Excuse me... Moo-Moondae, i-if you have any troubles, y-you can tell me too."

"......"

*Don't tell me he knows something...?*

"Thank you."

"D-don't mention it."

Seon Ahyeon smiled and started packing his things. I found myself wondering.

*If this guy ever finds out, how will he react?*

I didn't know. In the first place, I hadn't even thought Keun Sejin would believe me.

Well, I hadn't originally intended to bring Keun Sejin into this game as much as I had, so I didn't think Seon Ahyeon would feel too left out.

I stood beside him and picked up my belongings as well.

Then I found something of Bae Sejin's... Hm, was it an e-book reader?

"This is yours, right, hyung?"

"Huh? Yeah."

I'd picked it up, so I handed it to him, and the device's screen lit up in the process.

It was locked, but the title of the book was visible.

!pb
$lat@c@**⟨It's Okay to Fight Today⟩**@c@lat$
pb!

"......"

I stared.

"I-I just read it as a reference!"

"Yes."

It was a self-help book.

*Really... He took this seriously, didn't he?*

*It'd be funny if I didn't praise him here.*

I opened my mouth right away.

"Hyung... You're really determined, and you know how to get results."

Surprisingly, Bae Sejin didn't seem taken aback by these words. Instead, he murmured lightly, sounding a little excited.

"I know. It's the same for producing, too. I-I thought I'd be bad at it, but it seems some things just take the effort to try... It surprised me."

"......"

"From now on... I want to be a little more proactive."

"Good idea."

"Yes."

Bae Sejin seemed to have made a new resolution.

And the opportunity to act on that resolution came soon.

A few days later, we received word.

"I got an answer from the film company."

"Ohhhh."

Our request to participate in the OST for a movie based on the world of Ruins Factory's game seemed to have been safely delivered via T1.

However, Ryu Chungwoo, who'd been our contact point, smiled with a troubled expression.

*...Troubled?*

"They like the idea."

"Oh my god!"

"Wooooow!"

Cha Eugene, who'd been looking forward to it the most, whistled. However, Ryu Chungwoo still looked troubled.

"However... The deadline is very tight."

"What?"

*Don't tell me...*

"Looks like they already have something in the works, and we asked a little late. So, if we really want to participate..."

"Yes?"

"We need to give them a demo in four days."

"...!"

It was an absurd deadline.

Everyone reflexively looked at Kim Raebin. This was because the situation required an expert opinion.

But I already knew the answer.

*I know exactly how he's going to react.*

As expected, Kim Raebin... His eyes were gleaming.

"As expected of Moondae-hyung! You saw this coming, and that's why you insisted we work together for three days a week!"

"......"

*Well, he can think of it that way if he wants.*

The K-Pop Hell Camp... was about to resume.
