# Chapter 278 - ⟨TeSTAR Roommate⟩ Season 4, I

TeSTAR were reallocating roommates.

*Is this the 4th time?*

At this point, fans had come to see it as a long-standing tradition.

*Until now, we've mainly been playing board games.*

So this time, we switched things up a bit, figuring another similar game would be boring.

"Hello~ TeSTAR teachers.[1]{T/N: TeSTAR are being referred to as "teachers" by their guest, and will continue to be referred to as teachers for the rest of the chapter.

Editor's Note: I believe they're using 선생님 (seonsaengnim)? In this context, it's more of a polite way to address clients/strangers.}"

"Hello!"

This time, we'd hired an expert.

The seven of us were sitting around a table in the office of a laboratory run by communication professionals.

The table was arranged like one in a typical soap opera, with no seats on the camera's side. The cameras and staff were in front of us.

But maybe it was because we had done this kind of thing a lot, so it felt natural.

I glanced at the other members, who were looking expectantly at the expert.

The expert had a gentle smile.

"I understand you've asked us to help you decide on your new roommates today. Is that correct?"

"Yes!"

Bae Sejin replied cheerfully, his face full of energy.

It was probably the most energetic he'd sounded in the past month. I guessed he was hoping to get a room to himself.

*I thought he said sleeping in the living room wasn't so bad.*

Choosing to stay in the living room because he liked it was different from avoiding his bedroom because he didn't like the person he shared it with.

Keun Sejin shot Bae Sejin a wry glance, but since we were filming, the expression vanished in an instant.

The head of the lab stepped out from behind the expert, looked around at the smiling members, and spoke.

"At our Communications and Relations Lab, we analyze the personalities, tendencies, and lifestyles of group members and feed that information into an algorithm..."

Skipping the promotional introduction, this was the conclusion.

"Through behavioral analysis and interviews, we'll find the most suitable roommates for each of you and match you up."

"Wow!"

Simply put, 'Roommates are science.'

*Sounds pretty convincing.*

To be honest, it sounded like a venture company repackaging an existing business model, but it sounded pretty impressive anyway.

*I think the fans will enjoy it.*

The analyses here, combined with clips of TeSTAR's usual habits and daily lives, would make it easier to highlight each of our characters.

It worked on the same principle as an MBTI test.

A sense of kinship that came from the same characteristics.

More distinct characterizations from differing traits.

Keun Sejin made a fuss once more, perhaps trying to liven up the atmosphere.

"Wow, I'm so excited right now!"

"......"

*I get it, so stop shaking my shoulder.*

"Then let's start with group activities!"

"Oh, oh!"

Soon, the director disappeared, and only the expert remained for the group interview.

*We already did the individual interviews before filming the opening.*

This was the main attraction... Hmm. I looked at the first question on the screen.

+=
[There is a farmer raising ducks and cows. But one winter, this farmer decided to keep only one kind of animal.]

[What kind of animal did he choose?]
=+

"Please feel free to answer."

"...?"

It looked like one of those quizzes that floated around the Internet under the label of an IQ test.

At best, it was the sort of question that tested creative thinking in a job interview.

*Professionalism... Where did it go?*

The individual interviews had felt professional, but... this seemed to be more for the cameras than anything else.

*Well, it doesn't matter.*

Because the content mattered here.

I waited for my turn as I listened to the other guys talk.

"I think it might be ducks? Aren't ducks easier to care for? It's winter, so you can bring them indoors if necessary~" Keun Sejin said.

"Yes. But wouldn't they be hard to manage if there were too many of them?"

"I know! You can make more dishes with beef! It's cows. Go, cows!" Cha Eugene said.

Everyone seemed pretty eager to answer.

I raised my hand and looked at the expert.

"Is there a set answer to this question?"

"No. You teachers are free to use your imagination and thinking skills."

"Oh~"

As if the expert's words had given them the cue, the members started talking even more.

"Yeah, it's winter... ah! Maybe he's trying to make a blanket out of duck down...?"

"Yes! Ah~ You're smart, Ahyeon-nim~" Keun Sejin said.

"That's a pretty good idea, Ahyeon."

...No, if you looked closely, half of them were really excited, and half were trying to save the content.

They seemed to have noticed the drawback of this format.

*It's pretty dull.*

Would the content be appealing if there were no best answers and we were just brainstorming randomly among ourselves?

"A chicken."

"What??"

"It never says you have to choose between ducks and cows, so I chose the most economically viable animal."

"Oh my gosh."

"Hey, look at Moondae's brain."

We were all reacting appropriately, but I didn't think the flow was very interesting.

The expert closed the question like this.

"I've listened to what you all said. Thank you for your opinions. They were all good."

"Hehe."

"Aigoo, we thank you~"

There was no conclusion.

If they were just going to ask simple, non-professional questions, it would've been more entertaining to distinguish our personality types based on our answers.

Well, these days, there are a lot of different personality tests. They compare everything from drama characters to trees.

*[You are the 'unchanging evergreen' type!]*

Something like this.

*There's not even a time limit.*

There was nothing to keep viewers engaged, so the content was dull.

I glanced at the crew. I could see some of the people we'd worked with on the variety show where we made hotteok in a foreign country.

*Are they falling into a creative rut?*

*I think they brought in the wrong people.*

"I will ask you the next question."

Fortunately, after a few more similar questions and answers, they gave us an interim review.

"Now, I've taken a look at the way you teachers answered these questions."

The expert picked up a piece of paper and began to speak eloquently.

"First of all, Teacher Seon Ahyeon! You think carefully before making decisions, so it takes you a little longer than the others to come up with an answer."

"Yes..."

"That's not a weakness. I think it's because you're considerate of the people around you while forming your own opinions. Wouldn't you make a wise leader in the future?"

"Um, yes...! Thank you..."

"Oh~"

Seon Ahyeon's face reddened, and he lowered his head. The other guys clapped.

"And teacher Bae Sejin."

"...! Yes!!"

Bae Sejin, who was smiling at Seon Ahyeon, was startled.

"You're very clear about what you like and dislike, and I can see your determination to do your best even in unfamiliar or uncomfortable environments."

"That..."

This time, Bae Sejin's face turned red.

"You were especially eager to answer the question of 'who has the key'..."

In this way, all seven of us received very positive reviews of our behavior.

After the two youngest and Keun Sejin, who was sitting next to me, got their evaluations...

"Our teacher Ryu Chungwoo is the type to step back and support other people's opinions. Maybe that's because you're the leader."

"Hmm. I think you might be right."

Ryu Chungwoo smiled and responded politely. I'd expected as much, since he wasn't really interested in this sort of thing.

And the last was me.

The expert made eye contact with me and said.

"Teacher Park Moondae has a very broad perspective. People often tell you that you see the big picture, don't they?"

"Hahaha! That's right! You hit the nail on the head~" Keun Sejin said.

"Moondae-hyung is indeed such a person!" Kim Raebin said.

A few of them enthusiastically responded as if they were hooked by some kind of talkative swindler.

One guy in particular seemed to have really come to trust this expert.

*Kim Raebin... If you ever decide to go into business, I'll have to talk you out of it.*

Anyway, the expert ended the talk in a good way again.

"You're always coming up with unconventional ideas, making you an indispensable source of ideas for the group."

"Thank you."

He was pretty persuasive. He spoke gently without unnecessarily stirring up our emotions, which was nice.

I had a feeling he'd based his analyses on the data he'd received in advance rather than just our answers.

*Still, it's bland.*

There was no professional terminology or real analysis. It was just talk, with nothing particularly interesting to it.

*I was half joking earlier, but using actual internet personality tests might have been more fun.*

At least that would've felt silly but fun.

I looked at the expert with some disappointment.

"Then, on to the next question— Ah, let's take a short break first."

"Yes~"

The expert who received the signal from the production team wrapped up the segment at the right time.

People relaxed all at once.

I did the same as well.

"Hoo."

*It's a shame.*

It was weak.

I tried to predict how well this content would do, then shrugged.

*Well, what's done is done.*

We'd already started filming, so worrying about it now would accomplish nothing. For now, I'd just focus on doing my best.

"That was an amazing experience. I feel like I've gotten to know myself better!" Kim Raebin said.

"I need an explanation for this! 'Strong-hearted'? What does that mean?" Cha Eugene asked.

...Two of the guys were genuinely excited, so at least the fans would enjoy seeing that.

Their enthusiasm was welcome as far as fan service was concerned.

"Wait a minute..."

At that time, the crew secretly got up during the break and moved, leaving only the cameraman and a small number of people.

"What?"

"They said they were going to see the director."

Ryu Chungwoo, who was sitting closest to the production team, explained succinctly.

*I see. The production team must have realized this was shaping up to be pretty lackluster, too.*

And the expert standing in front of us also smiled, got up, and headed down the corridor.

"Teachers, I need to go to the bathroom for a moment."

"Yes, yes!"

The room's population density suddenly dropped.

"Yes, then..."

That was the moment.

"Ack!"

The expert who opened the back door and stepped into the hallway... He grabbed the back of his head and staggered backward.

And fell.

*Thud.*

*...Geeeek.*

The expert's white coat was caught halfway in the door.

"...??"

"Sir, sir?"

"What?"

A heart attack?

I tried to rush over to him with the others.

However...

Suddenly, an iron door slammed down right above the back door.

*Tutung!*

"Oh my gosh?"

Suddenly, the path was blocked.

*What the fuck is this?*

It wasn't just there.

An iron door also came down from above the big door on the side where the production team went out.

***Bang!***

***Kwak Kwa Kwak!***

"What."

A fire? A terrorist attack?

I immediately tried to check the windows, but... Iron plates had already come down over them.

And the lights changed.

%^***Whi-ing-! Whi-ing~!***^%

The red and green lights in every corner of the space flickered madly, and sirens sounded.

"Guys, this way!"

At the sound of Ryu Chungwoo's voice, the members instantly moved away from the walls and gathered together.

*Emergency?*

*I need to get my smartphone—*

"Hey! Over there!"

Hearing Bae Sejin's voice, I turned toward him.

The huge screen where the questions popped up earlier...

It... had changed.

"Crazy."

+=
#><#r[!AREA CLEARED!]r#><#

#><[QUARANTINE IN EFFECT]><#
=+

Behind the red letters, what looked like CCTV footage appeared on an even number of smaller split screens.

There were videos of people collapsing and doors slamming shut all over the building.

And... the people who had fallen were slowly getting to their feet and starting to stagger around.

+=
#><#r[There has been a zombie virus leak in the lab]r#><#

[Detonation in 02:00:00]
=+

"......"

What?

Wait a minute. We stared at the screen in silence, at a loss for words.

It didn't last long.

+=
#><[01:59:57]><#
=+

...The countdown started.

"......"

The members exchanged uncertain, wavering glances, looking as though they were all about to demand to know what was going on.

*What is this!*

And after a few seconds...

We spotted a few people hiding in the corner.

...It was the production crew who were trying not to laugh.

"Hey."

"No way."

"Where's the camera director? What is this?"

However, the two or three crew members refused to leave their spot in the corner or explain anything.

But still, their faces were full of smiles.

Only then did the members begin to realize what was really going on.

"Aaaaaaa!"

"Again! We were tricked!"

This was a staged situation.

"Mmmm."

Meanwhile, the production team gritted their teeth, determined not to ruin the audio with their laughter.

Bae Sejin vigorously pointed his finger at the screen.

"Zombies?"

*I get it.*

I grabbed my head and smiled wryly.

*I guess they wanted to figure out our personalities by seeing how we reacted to unexpected situations.*

*I was wondering why the research institute was so grandiose.*

By mixing in nonsense and unrealistic elements like 'zombies', they cleverly avoided potentially triggering PTSD associated with a real-life emergency.

It was made for fun at the same time.

*They're like cockroaches, fuck. This is something new.*

The bland group discussion beforehand had all been a setup for this reveal.

*I really didn't see it coming.*

I took back what I'd said about the content being boring.

Now... I could understand why the production team had hired these people.

It felt like blood was rushing to my head.

"Wait, wait!! There's a door behind here!"

"What??"

"Is it a door?"

Just in time, Cha Eugene hurriedly exclaimed, pointing to the back of the production team as if he had found something.

"Wait a minute~ Ah, honestly, if you have a conscience, please step out of the way, noona!"

Keun Sejin skillfully coaxed the writer into stepping aside, then touched one corner of the wall.

"Oh my~"

With his right hand, he pried away a rectangular metal cover. Beneath it, a large vent was revealed, lit by a bright red light.

"Wow."

"Just what you'd expect."

"Do you think we should go through here?"

"I guess so."

Those who were already immersed quickly began exchanging opinions.

*Hmm.*

The concept actually seemed fun. But they'd given us limited time to react, since they were trying to see how we dealt with a 'disastrous situation'.

It had already been 4 minutes.

But it would be pretty disappointing if TeSTAR couldn't escape from here.

*It would be best to escape cleanly without forcing them to loosen the restrictions for us.*

Thinking this far, I suddenly remembered what the expert had just said.

*– People often tell you that you see the big picture, don't they?*

*Yes, that's right. I'm a bit obsessed with the big picture.*

I grinned and shook out my hands.

"Let's go."

*Let's make this look good for the cameras.*

I thought this roommate content would be something worth remembering.
