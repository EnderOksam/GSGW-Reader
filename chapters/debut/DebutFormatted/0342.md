# Chapter 342 - An Unexpected Visit

A guy I'd never given my address to and who hadn't arranged to meet me in advance was standing at our dorm door.

He hadn't even rung the doorbell, and instead, I had a handful of missed calls out of the blue.

And on top of that, this bastard already had a history of unhinged behavior.

*Fuck.*

I glanced at Cheongryeo's face again through the gap in the door.

It was still shockingly expressionless.

"Why did you come?"

"I didn't seek you out. I'm just dropping by on my way back to my dorm."

"I never gave you my address."

"You told me last time."

*Never.*

"You told me which floor you lived on."

"......"

Yes, when I'd run into this guy with his dog early in the morning, I *had* mentioned it in passing to ease the tension before bringing up 'Ryu Gunwoo'.

And if he'd already known which building I lived in, it would've been easy enough to figure out where I lived...

*You just put two and two together, you bastard.*

It was the sort of habit someone who'd had so many second chances thanks to his resets would develop.

Even this bastard wasn't so unhinged that he couldn't handle basic social decisions.

However, Cheongryeo's words did not end there.

"I have some news to share with you."

"What..."

Then he told me something I wasn't expecting to hear.

"Kongi is sick."

"...!"

*What?*

"I just left him at the vet."

It was only then that I understood what this bastard's strange behavior meant.

He was... confused.

*...Fuck.*

I suppressed a sigh, for a different reason this time.

"Wait a moment."

*Click.*

I undid the chain and opened the door again, stepping outside. Our dorm was the only residence on this floor, so it shouldn't matter.

"...What's wrong with him, exactly?"

"Acute renal failure."

Cheongryeo frowned slightly.

"Or something very close to that, anyway. One of the employees... brought over some grapes and didn't put them out of Kongi's reach."

"......"

"This sort of thing is completely new to me."

His voice was so still and calm that it made me wonder if he'd taken a sledgehammer to that employee's head.

I rubbed my brow.

"Did they apologize?"

"Does that matter? An apology won't make them suffer in place of my dog."

It was exactly the sort of thing I'd expect from someone who'd made enough money to last him a lifetime.

*So he ran off to take care of his dog first.*

And after taking care of that... his head must've grown crowded with thoughts on his way home.

*Is that why he called me so many times? To tell me this?*

I guessed the reason for the missed calls. And Cheongryeo quietly continued to speak.

"So I won't have any photos of Kongi to share with you for a little while. I thought I'd explain in person."

"......"

I stared at him in silence.

"Hubaenim, you like Kongi."

*Unbelievable.*

"You could have done it via text."

"Ah."

*Did he only just realize that?*

But he quickly changed the subject.

"Hmm... This way I can tell you right away, face-to-face."

"Or you could've told me over the phone."

"I was calling just to give you a heads-up. It would've been rude to come without saying anything. There are others living in the dorm with you, so it's only right to be careful."

"...How did you guess I would be at the dorm?"

"There was no need to guess. If you weren't here, I would've just left."

He was talking utter nonsense while somehow managing to sound perfectly reasonable. He was out of his mind.

I rubbed my eyes.

I guessed that this bastard was deeply on edge and needed someone to talk to, but he didn't seem to realize what a sorry state he was in.

"...Your dog will be fine."

Cheongryeo tilted his head to the side.

"Is that so? I don't know how you can be sure."

"You left him with a professional vet, right? If they let you leave right away, he should be fine. The vet explained his condition to you, right?"

"......"

After staring into space for quite some time, he spoke again.

"Yes, you're right."

"......"

"I took action quickly enough to keep his kidneys from being seriously damaged. They inserted an IV without any problems, too. It's unlikely, but the vet said they'd contact me if Kongi's condition suddenly worsened."

"Okay."

"Anyway, I have schedules starting tomorrow, so I won't be able to take care of him at home."

"Yes."

I nodded.

"Leaving him at the vet was the right decision, so what's wrong?"

"Well. I was just thinking that unexpected things happen so often in life."

"...Hmm."

The guy's eyes were dark.

I hoped he was only thinking about his dog. *Don't tell me he wants to do something to the person who left the grapes behind?*

*Actually, neither is a very good sign.*

It must be that the dog occupied a very large part of his life.

Wasn't this the first time since VTIC's main vocalist went down the wrong path that I'd seen this bastard so out of sorts?

*...It's a similar situation.*

Something important in his life seemed about to be ruined by factors beyond his control.

*I just hope this doesn't make him obsessed with resetting again.*

*At this rate, I wouldn't be surprised if he said, "Let's kill Goldie #2 and see what happens."*

*No. I can't let that happen.*

After a brief silence, I opened my mouth.

No matter what he put at the center of his life, this bastard needed to find a little more peace of mind somehow.

"No matter how you live, accidents happen. It is impossible for anyone to prepare for all of them."

"......"

"That's why what matters is how you deal with what life throws at you. In that sense, I think you did pretty well today."

"...!"

"You acted quickly and kept your dog from suffering too much. Thanks to you, he'll recover soon, and I'm glad to hear it."

I summed up what I was trying to say.

"Good job."

"......"

Cheongryeo stood blankly for a moment, then nodded slowly.

"...Right. Yes."

"So if your dog..."

"That's right. If Kongi recovers well, there is no problem."

He still wasn't listening. *Just as I thought.*

Still, his tone seemed a little calmer, as if he'd regained his composure. His expression was back to normal, too.

"Come see Kongi once he's out of the vet's."

"We'll see."

I nodded, and only then did the smile return to his face.

*Okay.*

I had no idea why I was doing this for the bastard who'd tried to kill me, but before I knew it, here I was, volunteering my time for no pay.

And I had other work to do, at that.

*The composition camp break time...*

Then I looked down at the smartphone in my hand and realized there was quite a gap between the missed calls and my call back.

*Almost 30 minutes.*

It seemed that Cheongryeo had been standing outside the front door for longer than expected.

*It's an unreasonable waste of time by his standards.*

I guess it was because he was frustrated since I was the only one who knew why he had adopted the dog.

I debated for a moment, then spoke.

"Do the people in your group know?"

Cheongryeo answered readily.

"No. They're not at the dorm right now. I sent them all to the agency. You know, because of that incident."

"...?"

*That incident?*

"Ah."

Cheongryeo cocked his head to the side.

"Hubaenim, you didn't check the internet today, did you?"

"......"

Cheongryeo stood there staring blankly for a moment.

"I see. You didn't ignore my calls on purpose, then. You're not looking at your smartphone right now, are you? You're either preparing for a new album or taking a break."

*Why did this bastard keep trying to deduce what I'd been doing?*

"What happened? Something serious?"

*Don't tell me it's another article in the general news section?*

I hadn't expected any other VTIC member to make the news like that, but that sort of assumption wasn't always right.

But Cheongryeo was indifferent.

"I wouldn't say anything serious happened. Let's just say we all knew this was coming."

"What was coming?"

"Hmm? We all have to serve in the military, you know."

"...!!"

Suddenly, a bolt of lightning struck my brain.

*Is it that time already...?* It was good for us, but it wasn't time for them to enlist yet.

"Isn't there still time left? I thought you could postpone your enlistment because of a medal you received."

"Hmm... We'd already agreed to enlist together."

Cheongryeo smiled faintly and leaned against the wall.

"We agreed that that would be the best way to extend the life of the group."

"...Ah."

*No way.*

"We all enlist on the same date and get discharged on the same date."

"......"

*I was right. No way he actually convinced all of them.*

The entire group was enlisting and getting discharged together. It was unheard of.

It allowed them to return as a full group as quickly as possible, but it was an extreme strategy that completely ruled out individual activities in the meantime.

I'd never imagined an idol group would actually do something like this.

"So it's as I said. This year's *daesang* will be a bit easier."

"......"

I nodded slowly. I understood now.

"Then you're enlisting too."

So the anxiety of being about to enlist must've played a part in why he'd lost his composure...

"Hmm? No."

*Hey.*

"I only have to serve for six months. Haha, so I'll enlist next year. Wouldn't it naturally make a much bigger impact if we all got discharged together?"

He never failed to get on my nerves with that mouth of his.

"So I'll be the only one in my group attending the year-end award ceremonies. The press reported it today."

"Is that right?"

"Yes."

In any case, Cheongryeo spoke as if there was nothing new. He looked a bit subdued, but nothing unusual.

*It must be a mess on the internet right now.*

I tried to picture the situation online, then quickly gave up.

"Doesn't that make you feel better? A competitor you've had trouble beating is about to disappear."

"We can beat you even if you don't go."

"Oh. Right."

*So this fucker doesn't believe me, huh?*

"How are you feeling?"

"Well. Not good. It's difficult managing a hiatus like this."

It was a more businesslike response than I expected.

Even though the members of his group were all leaving, this guy didn't seem particularly perturbed on a personal level.

*Hmm.*

"It's a bit intriguing, though. I've never gotten this far before."

"......"

I had nothing to say to that.

"I will use the dorm alone..."

The guy speaking stopped for a moment. At that moment, a noise started coming from the front door.

*Tidik.*

The sound of a door opening.

"...!"

And someone suddenly stuck their head out.

It was Keun Sejin.

"Moondaemoondae, why did you go outside..."

It seemed that the break was over, and he'd come to find me because I hadn't returned.

However, he stopped talking when he saw Cheongryeo standing nearby. *Damn.*

"Lee Sejin."

"Wait a minute."

And the guy switched tack in an instant.

"Ah, sunbaenim. Hello!"

"Hello."

"I've heard a lot about you. What brings you here..."

"You've heard a lot about me?"

"......"

Keun Sejin stopped talking for a moment, but then replied in the same tone.

"Yes, a lot."

In the meantime, I heard Kim Raebin shouting from behind the front door.

"Hyung, who's here?"

"Hm~ That's-"

"Wait."

This wouldn't do. I grabbed Keun Sejin and pushed him back into the dorm, shutting the door behind us.

*Thud.*

As soon as the door closed, Keun Sejin's face hardened.

"Park Moondae, why is that person here?"

"His dog's sick... No, that's got nothing to do with us anyway. I heard VTIC is enlisting."

"What??"

"Hey, is VTIC sunbaenim going to the army already?"

"......"

"Oh my god. Did he come all the way here to tell you that personally?"

Seeing Kim Raebin rush into the entryway, I immediately realized things were getting more out of hand than I'd thought.

The people gathered in the living room had heard Kim Raebin shout.

"The army??"

"...That bastard's out there right now?"

"Oh, did Moondae-hyung invite sunbaenim over?" Eugene said.

"...! Did you invite him over so we could ask his advice on our album?"

"Wait a second."

I put a halt to the conversation for now. I was breaking out in a cold sweat.

"First of all, I didn't call him here."

"Then who did?"

I briefly explained the guy's circumstances, making an excuse along the lines of, 'His dog had an accident, but none of his friends were available to talk, so he came to see me.'

*...No, come to think of it, it's not an excuse. It's a fact.*

*I wasn't expecting that.*

"Ooh, poor puppy..." Eugene said.

"I see."

The members who were under the impression I'd only gotten into a nasty fight with Cheongryeo in the past seemed to accept that explanation.

Bae Sejin looked ready to throw open the door and tell Cheongryeo to get lost, but Ryu Chungwoo was doing his best to calm him down, so it should've been okay.

I immediately put an end to the conversation.

"I'll see him off and come back."

"Ah, I should see sunbaenim off too~" Keun Sejin said.

"......"

*Sure, do as you please.*

I didn't bother telling Keun Sejin to stay inside and opened the front door again.

Cheongryeo was still leaning against the wall. *Why don't you just go home already?*

"So all of you are home, I see. Hmm. I'm sorry if I made you uncomfortable."

"Forget it," I said.

I had a headache.

"As an apology, I have something to tell you."

"What?"

Cheongryeo shrugged, took out his smartphone, and sent me a file.

"...?"

"...It's a music file."

Lee Sejin was right. But it wasn't a song.
