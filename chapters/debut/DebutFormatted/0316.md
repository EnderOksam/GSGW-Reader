# Chapter 316 - Conversation, IV

What had happened over the past year for a signed poster from a rival in the same industry to end up in this apartment?

And of all people, why Cheongryeo?

*Isn't this around the time he was busy going behind my back?*

What had prompted him to get an autograph from that Reset Syndrome bastard?

He didn't seem like the sort to spend money on a fansign just because he'd won the lottery.

Sure enough, there was another reason.

[Ah! I won a fansign ticket after buying some clothes!]

His life had been completely reset after winning the lottery, and even though he wasn't particularly interested in clothes, it seemed he'd bought a lot at once when he realized he could afford them.

Especially winter clothes... It was easy to freeze to death without a coat, so he'd bought several coats and padded jackets at once during a sale.

[I don't know much about clothes... I just bought everything from one store, based on what the employee recommended...]

It just so happened that the store was an outdoor brand VTIC modeled for.

He'd entered the lucky draw for fun with the ticket he received and won admission to the models' fansign.

*I'd have preferred it if he'd gotten into a TeSTAR fansign... No, that would've made things weird in its own way.*

*Why did he enter a draw for a male idol group's fansign in the first place...?*

[The concert we went to see last time was amazing... I became more interested in idols after that. A member of that group was on a variety show with you, too, hyung...]

"......"

*So it was my fault. Got it.*

I looked away from the signed poster, frowning. He seemed to be studying properly, so there probably wasn't much to worry about.

"When did you go?"

[Yesterday!]

It seemed he'd gone to the fansign to celebrate finishing the first round of tests, just like last time.

He'd be given another year's reprieve anyway, and I'd told him to focus on the first round without worrying about the second yet. It made sense for him to take some time off and have fun.

And, to be honest, I'd expected another status abnormality like the first one, where all he had to do was avoid failing any individual subject.

★!
@c@Status Abnormality:@c@
#><["Pass or Die!" (3)]><#

Avoid failing any subject in the second-round written exam of the “Grade Seven Open Competitive Civil Servant Exam” or die.
!★

*Yeah, I thought as much.*

So next year, all he had to do was pass the first round and avoid failing any of the subjects in the second. I knew the questions that would be on the exams up until next year, so there wouldn't be any problems.

I tried to explain it, but this guy said something unexpected.

[I... Actually, I've already taken the second round, and judging from my own marking... I don't think I failed any of the subjects!]

"...!"

[I got really lucky with some of my guesses! I don't think I'll pass, though...]

Still, it was an incredible achievement.

"That's enough. Good job."

[...! Yes!! Thank you!]

That meant we could clear the new status abnormality within a month without any extra effort. I nodded, working out our next steps.

"Still, don't clear it right away. Wait until just before next year's second-round results are out, then clear it. Buy yourself some extra time."

[Yes!]

So that meant... I'd probably come back in September next year, too.

I checked the date on my smartphone. It was indeed September. I'd returned to this body after a full year.

[You told me to wait this time too, so I put it off for as long as I could...!]

"Good job again."

The voice echoed in my head as though he were laughing. He was probably smiling.

*Then I don't have anything special to do today.*

I figured I could spend my time finding out whether anything unusual had happened over the past year, other than that autograph.

I accessed the stock app and continued talking with 'Park Moondae' while choosing which ones to sell.

"How's the boarding academy?"

[Great! Everyone's friendly... And the teachers there are good, so I've been doing really well. The food's delicious, too...!]

*I see.* After selling and buying a few stocks, I closed the app.

*That should be enough to keep him from having money problems even if he does nothing but study for the next few years.*

While I listened to him tell me about his daily life over the past year, I stepped out into the living room and was greeted by quite a sight.

"......"

A man with flowers in place of his head.

To be precise, it was a doll of the 'May Bridegroom', the hologram character I'd created for ⟨Bespoke Singer⟩, sitting right in the center of the sofa.

*What.*

Apparently... He'd been quite pleased with the achievements of his own body.

"You bought this too?"

[Hyung, you sang really well... I thought it wouldn't hurt to buy one as a keepsake...]

I was the one who'd set aside money each month for him to blow off some steam, so it didn't matter as long as he was enjoying himself...

Still feeling a little uncomfortable, I pushed the doll aside and sat down on the sofa.

The voice cautiously brought up the subject he'd apparently been working toward.

[Well, once the second-round results are out... Would it be okay if I went to see you at TeSTAR's year-end concert? I couldn't go earlier this year because I was studying...]

It seemed like a phrase he had prepared since yesterday.

I chuckled.

"Why do you need my permission? If you don't think it'll hurt anything, then go."

[...! Yes! Thank you!]

There was nothing to be thankful for. Of course, going to a male idol concert in my body would be a bit conspicuous... *But wait.*

"The fan signing event."

[...??]

"...You said you went yesterday."

[Yes!]

I looked again at the poster with the VTIC signature on it.

It wasn't an album fansign, but a fansign for the brand's advertising models, so plenty of non-fans would have attended... Even so, he probably stood out.

[He was very kind.]

I didn't like the sound of that at all.

Just to be sure, I accessed WeTube and entered a search query.

Using today's date, the name of VTIC's current album, and the clothing brand as search terms... Sure enough, I found a fancam of the fansign.

As I scrolled through the comments, I found exactly what I'd feared.

★$

"......"

When I clicked on the timestamp, the back of 'Ryu Gunwoo' receiving an autograph appeared on the screen.

The resonance echoed like a scream.

[Uhhhhh! I-I didn't know videos like this would be uploaded...]

I took a screenshot and adjusted the colors. 

[Hyung?]

He was wearing a hat, and probably glasses too...

Didn't he match the description of Ryu Gunwoo I'd given Cheongryeo perfectly?

*Damn it.*

Just in case, I checked the signed poster once again.

What name had he given?

*Don't tell me it was Ryu Gunwoo...*

– $$***To. Keundal***$$[1]{T/N: Keundal/큰달: Directly translates to "Big Moon".}

"......"

*Keundal?*[2]{Translator’s and Editor’s Note: To avoid confusion between the protagonist, Park Moondae (originally Ryu Gunwoo), and the original Park Moondae, both Korean and international fans commonly refer to the latter by his nickname, Keundal (큰달), which literally means “Big Moon” and is rendered as such in the official English translation. If you’re looking for fanart, discussions, or other content featuring him, you can use 큰달 as a search term!}

[That's a handle I used to use... For some reason, it didn't feel right to receive it under hyung's name.]

That had been excellent judgment on his part.

*Hoo.*

I quickly closed the video of Cheongryeo smiling and signing autographs.

"Avoid this guy from now on."

[Ch-Cheongryeo?]

"Yes. Because he's crazy."

[Uh, oh!]

I'd given Cheongryeo a hint so he'd try to track down 'Ryu Gunwoo' for me, but I now knew Gunwoo's exact location, phone number, and even his address.

There was no need to introduce another variable by meeting that unpredictable, dangerous bastard.

*Come to think of it, was it so hard to find him because I'd sent him to a boarding academy?*

Now it made sense. I rubbed my chin.

*Then, once I finish this and go back, I can contact him and...*

No. I was getting ahead of myself.

*...Who knows if that'll even be possible?*

I didn't know what would happen when his status abnormalities came to an end.

In the worst case, he might simply disappear because my reward was complete.

And even if he remained in this body... Wouldn't he want to return to his own?

He'd even gotten the autograph using his old nickname.

"......"

[I thought you were close to Cheongryeo-ssi, but I didn't know he was such a strange person... Uh, hyung?]

"I'm not close with him. It just looks that way on camera."

[Ah...]

This was something I'd have to think about for a while.

It was fortunate I had some free time today. I could think about it to my heart's content.

After coming up with a few theories and backup plans, I started preparing a meal.

After that, there was nothing particularly urgent that needed to be done. 

[Hyung, you're good at cooking too...]

"Oh, thank you."

Since we shared a body, it was nice to only have to cook one serving.

During prime time that evening, a variety show featuring Seon Ahyeon and me as guests was on TV. It was the one where we'd ended up in the Joseon era and solved puzzles.

*Was this around the time I rooted out the industrial spy within our agency?*

I'd been pretty busy. I watched myself showing off my smarts on screen without much interest.

[I like the variety shows, but I think you're even cooler on stage. 'Present' was really cool...! I hope you guys have a comeback soon...]

I'd raised my stats without holding back, but could he really say that while looking at his own face...?

Still, a compliment was a compliment, and it felt nice. I answered casually.

"The comeback's on the 14th of next month."

[Oh my gosh...!]

"Don't go around talking about it."

[Yes! I don't have anyone to tell anyway!! Nobody talks about idols at the boarding academy!]

*That figures.*

The voice hummed excitedly, making incomprehensible little noises, but he soon calmed down and began speaking more gently.

Even a little cautiously.

[Um, hyung. You've been helping me every time you wake up... Is there anything I can do to help you? Well, like when you called the police last time. If there's anything you need me to do, I'll do it!]

*Something I need him to do?*

Come to think of it, Cheongryeo would try to kill me in two months.

I pondered for a moment, but then shook my head.

I'd handled that well enough on my own. He needed to focus on his status abnormality, and I couldn't ask him to do anything more.

"There's nothing else. Just focus on your studies."

[Yes...]

He sounded disappointed now. *Oh, my.*

And the closer it got to midnight, the worse it got. The voice sounded almost anxious.

*Even if he doesn't say it, spending a whole year alone must have been hard.*

He had no one he could openly talk to about any of this, so it was understandable. Maybe he needed something other than studying to do or think about.

So I brought up a new topic by the time I went to bed.

"Is there anything else you want to ask me or tell me?"

[Me?]

*Yes.*

The voice seemed hesitant, but he soon spoke softly again.

[Then, hyung, by any chance... What did you think of my nickname?]

"Nickname?"

Keundal. It probably came from his real name, Moondae, which sounded like 'big moon' in Korean. It wasn't a particularly unique nickname, but it did seem familiar...

[That... You know that account hyung used to run? I often left comments on your videos.]

*Ah.*

"Was that you?"

[...! Do you remember?]

"Yes. You left a comment on every upload."

[Yes...!!]

Come to think of it, it was the nickname I'd seen the first time I clicked on 'Reveal the Truth'.

I hadn't paid much attention to the comments, but I remembered them now. He'd left a comment almost as soon as I uploaded a video.

That was why I remembered him.

*So he'd already been interested in idols back then?*

It was a strange realization, learning he'd been the one behind those comments. I shrugged.

"If you're interested in that sort of thing, why don't you buy a camera and try making your own fancams?"

[...Sh-should I?]

"After the exam is over, of course."

[Okay! Still... Wow.]

He seemed to feel a little better. The voice sounded more relaxed.

And just like that, the time had come.

"Okay. Then think about it. See you next year."

[Yes...]

Sleep came slowly. It was surprisingly peaceful. 

***

[Hyung? ...Hyung?]

I opened my eyes.

It was morning, just like every other time I'd woken up. The temperature seemed similar too, so maybe it was September of the following year...

[Gunwoo-hyung, right?? Hyung!!]

"Yes."

He seemed to be panicking. Had he summoned me because something had gone wrong?

I sat up immediately, but the voice was already echoing wildly in my head.

...It sounded like he was sobbing.

[Hyung, hyung, you... The car accident...]

*Ah.*

*Fuck.*

I immediately opened my smartphone.
