# Chapter 265 - The Tour Documentary, II

As expected, the encore itself went smoothly.

**[See ya!!]**

Cha Eugene ran across the stage looking for the cameras and the audience, as if he'd never been upset in the first place.

And he maintained that outwardly good mood until we went backstage.

*Good.*

I tried to calm myself, with the emotions from the concert still lingering.

I'd go down and talk to Cha Eugene, then take what I'd learned to the company and the production team.

I understood they were trying to create an interesting narrative, but they were taking things too far.

However, before I could do that, the cameramen came out from backstage to film us as we came down from the stage.

*These bastards really don't know when to quit.*

I had a strong feeling that if we got angry at them, they'd actually be pleased that we were showing more of our true selves.

"......"

Cha Eugene glanced at the documentary crew, but took a drink of water without saying anything.

*He seems to have cooled down a bit.*

It seemed like performing had helped with that. I mentally went over the situation as I wiped my neck with a towel.

It was then.

The crew quickly approached me, a camera crowding in close.

"Eugene has recovered very quickly... Tell me, was there anything in the accident before—"

"Enough," Cha Eugene said.

"...!"

Cha Eugene suddenly interrupted and pushed the camera away with his hand.

It was completely unexpected.

"Whoa!"

"I told you already, didn't I? Why aren't you listening?" Cha Eugene said.

"Wait a minute."

*What's the matter with this kid all of a sudden?*

*This will make it look like we were the ones who crossed the line first.*

The members, who'd still been trying to catch their breath, were shocked by his sudden action and rushed over.

However, Cha Eugene stubbornly kept his hand on the camera he'd pushed down for quite a while, staring at the production crew.

"Eugene, let go of the camera."

"What are you doing!?"

"Wait, wait a second. Director, I'm sorry, but could we have a moment to ourselves? Please. Thank you."

Lee Sejin managed to put the brakes on the situation and politely sent the production team away. Then he headed straight to our waiting room.

*Tak.*

"Let's sit down," Lee Sejin said.

"......"

Cha Eugene sat on the sofa without saying a word. Ryu Chungwoo began to speak.

"Eugene-ah, I understand that having the production crew around all the time might make you uncomfortable, but... you can't resort to using force like that. We need to use our words."

"They didn't listen to me," Cha Eugene said.

"Wait a minute, hyung. Cha Eugene, listen."

Lee Sejin intervened.

"During ⟨Idol Incorporated⟩, you cried, but you didn't complain when they filmed it. When we were filming our reality show at the beginning of our debut, even when the dorm was full of cameras, you didn't say anything, right?"

Perhaps because of how urgent and serious the situation was, Lee Sejin didn't sound as soothing as usual.

"If you act like this now, do you think people will care whether you're in the right or wrong? They'll just think success changed you."

"That is different."

"It could be different. But if the viewers can't see that for themselves, what's the use?"

"......"

Cha Eugene remained stubbornly silent.

"Eugene, not everything can be easy and right when it comes to work. If that were possible, we'd all be gods. First of all, you should apologize for touching the camera, and—"

However, someone interrupted him before he could finish.

"...No, they're the ones in the wrong in the first place. They were rude."

"...!"

It was Bae Sejin.

"That's the truth of the matter. It's strange to say we should put up with this now just because we did when we debuted."

"Se-Sejin-hyung."

Seon Ahyeon called out nervously, but Bae Sejin didn't back down.

Instead, a brief flash of annoyance crossed Lee Sejin's face.

"Hyung. This is no time for... No, I'm not saying those people *deserve* an apology, am I?"

"Yeah, I know you're trying to keep this from getting out of hand. But is that really necessary?"

"Damn it... I mean... Hoo." Lee Sejin sighed.

"Enough," I said.

I raised my hand and pressed my temples.

"We don't have to discuss this at length among ourselves. We can just talk to the production team and have that footage deleted."

We had differing opinions on this, but there was no need to see the discussion through to the end. We just had to be careful for now.

I concluded the matter with a single sentence.

"Things like this can happen when you work."

"......"

The two of them kept their mouths shut. They'd tried so hard not to clash, but it was clear they still weren't compatible.

*That was a close call.*

I held back a sigh and turned to Cha Eugene, who still had his head down.

"...Take some time to cool off."

"......"

There was no answer.

After confirming that Kim Raebin had hesitated but remained in the room, I placed some water in front of the three of them before leaving.

I was going to talk to the production team with Ryu Chungwoo, but another camera made a beeline for us... *Fuck, they had to stop doing this.*

I chimed in only when needed as Ryu Chungwoo politely asked after the cameraman whose camera Cha Eugene had pushed down, then brought up the main topic.

"I think the members need some rest today. How about meeting tomorrow and talking again?" I said.

"Yes, yes. I'm really sorry, but could we have a short interview right now? You'll be taking a break tomorrow, right? We'll keep it really short."

"...Wait a moment."

*They refuse to listen.*

*Is this why Cha Eugene was mad?*

It wasn't like they were using unmanned cameras. There were actual people constantly shoving cameras in our faces. From the perspective of someone like Eugene, who'd lived in America with its countless paparazzi, it might be particularly unpleasant.

*And the production team doesn't want to miss a chance like this.*

It was easier to get honest answers when people were tired or emotionally shaken.

Seeing how gently we'd guided Cha Eugene away, they'd probably guessed we weren't the type to throw an angry fit and say, 'Hey, this is just a fucking documentary, so stop it!'

Fortunately, the company staff rushed in like bullets, spoke to the production crew, and sent them away.

"I'm sorry, we can't do it right now! We ask for your understanding."

Then they turned to us anxiously.

"By any chance, was there a... conflict between Eugene-ssi and the documentary crew?"

"I think there is a bit of a problem, yes," Ryu Chungwoo said.

He glanced back at the room, and when our eyes met, he sighed briefly.

"Let's leave him alone for today. Let him cool down," I said.

"Yes. That sounds like the right thing to do."

Anyway, we really didn't have anything scheduled for tomorrow. Exhausted after the concert, we'd probably just stay in our hotel rooms and do a few interviews for the documentary.

The staff looked nervous, but eventually agreed and decided to wait for Cha Eugene to come out of the room.

"I think it'd be better not to try talking to him right now," I said.

"Yes, yes. We will be sure to pay attention."

We generally handled problems on our own if possible, or glossed them over without comment, so the staff seemed quite unfamiliar with this sort of situation and alarmed by it.

Still, they carried out their jobs properly.

"Everyone, please go and get some rest. We'll wait for the last two members to come out."

"......"

*Right.* I'd be seeing them tomorrow morning anyway, so I'd just leave for now. It'd do us no good to keep the guys with differing opinions together and risk another pointless fight.

*It'll be okay since Kim Raebin is with him.*

In a situation like this, it would be more comfortable for him to confide in someone his own age rather than someone older.

"Let's go back to the hotel," I said.

"...Okay."

The members looked a bit uncertain, but eventually agreed and went to the van.

"Sh-should I send him a text, at least...? In English, I mean," Seon Ahyeon said.

"Well, I think it'd be better to talk face-to-face over breakfast tomorrow."

"R-right..."

Trying to comfort him was nice, but he'd also need some time to think.

I returned to my hotel room, thoroughly exhausted. I showered and went straight to bed.

"...Hoo."

Tomorrow morning, I'd have to work on improving Cha Eugene's mood.

And I'd ask him in detail what had happened between him and the production team.

But the next morning...

While the others showed up at the usual breakfast gathering time, Cha Eugene did not appear.

"......"

"Is it past 9:30 now?"

"Yes."

I opened my mouth.

"Raebin."

"Yes!"

"How was Cha Eugene yesterday?"

"He was quiet and looked a bit put out, but he didn't express any further anger!"

"...Is that so?"

"Yes. I think he might be sleeping in or staging some kind of protest!"

*I see.*

I fixed my hat.

"I'll go get him. You come with me," I said to Raebin.

"Me...? Oh, all right, then!"

With Kim Raebin in tow, I left for Cha Eugene's room as the others waved us off with odd looks on their faces.

And immediately rang the doorbell.

*Ding dong.*

"......"

There was no response.

"Cha Eugene. Open the door."

*Still nothing.* Even though we'd reserved the entire corridor, I couldn't raise my voice any further.

I was sure he would have heard this much.

*Is this really his way of protesting?*

He was going too far. I sighed, took out my smartphone, and called Cha Eugene.

But the phone... it didn't go through.

"......"

*I've got a bad feeling about this.*

"Hyung?" Kim Raebin asked.

I tried calling one more time, then was forced to come to terms with the situation.

"Raebin."

"Yes?"

"Call the manager hyung."

I looked at Cha Eugene's door.

"It seems that Cha Eugene isn't in the room."

"...!!"

After a while...

With the help of the hotel staff, we got Cha Eugene's room open... It was empty.

"You're right."

*Damn it.*

I hadn't seen this coming.

*Was it a mistake?*

Rather than leaving this guy overnight to cool down, should I have sat down with him over a drink and calmed him down right away?

*He isn't a ninja, so why would he leave his hotel room like this?*

I nearly bit through my tongue, but managed to ask the manager a question.

"Did he text you or contact you at all last night?"

"No! Eugene never contacted me...!?"

People at the company were in a panic. It was understandable, since none of us had ever done anything like this since debuting.

At least he hadn't vanished without a trace.

Fortunately, he'd left behind a memo, written in large, blocky Korean letters.

"Eu-Eugene must've been really upset..." Kim Raebin said.

"But it's strange that he'd just disappear like this— Wait, this isn't a kidnapping, is it?"

"Then I think the room would've been in worse shape than this. This idiot left of his own accord!" Kim Raebin said.

"I guess you're right."

Kim Raebin had a point.

The room was far too pristine for this to be a kidnapping, and the hotel had good security.

After thinking it through calmly, though, I began to see what might've happened.

"...Hmm."

I looked around the room once and nodded.

"He didn't go far."

"Y-you think so...?" Seon Ahyeon asked.

"Yes. He left all his luggage here. He took only his wallet and phone... I wonder if leaving was a spur-of-the-moment decision?"

I guessed he'd decided to go out after waking up and realizing he was still pissed off.

At that moment, Kim Raebin raised his hand.

"Excuse me, I'm sorry for interrupting, but may I offer my opinion?"

"...? Okay."

He swallowed, then spoke with some hesitation.

"I just... think he might've gone out to have a good time by himself, since we don't have anything scheduled today."

"......"

"......"

*Wait a minute.*

"I suppose... You might be right."

"Yes!"

Bae Sejin hurriedly intervened.

"But! His smartphone is off...!"

"I think he just turned it off because he didn't want to be disturbed."

"......"

Yes. Since it was our day off, there was a good chance he thought he didn't need to ask permission from the company...

The members were perplexed.

"Th-that's right. That could be it."

"...That's pretty wild..."

Ryu Chungwoo cleared the air.

"Um, but that might not be it either, so let's look into things a little more."

"Yes. Okay~ Shall we check the CCTV footage, then?"

"I'll check right away, so everyone, please wait here. Someone else will come for you!" the manager said.

The manager immediately rushed off with the hotel staff. It was nice that he was working so hard, and I understood that this must've felt like a bomb had dropped for him.

However, I didn't think we really needed to worry about the search itself.

"I think I can find him with this."

I took out my smartphone.

Cha Eugene kicked at the sand with his bare feet, feeling the sea breeze on his face.

It was the first time in a long while he'd been able to spend a sunny morning of his day off outside, and it felt quite relaxing.

*The weather is nice.*

But he wasn't in a particularly good mood. He put his hands behind his neck and leaned back a little, trying to shake off his gloom.

They'd told him to 'cool down', so he'd come out here to do just that. But he'd concluded he still wasn't sure he'd done anything wrong.

*Well, I won't apologize.*

Instead, he decided he needed to come up with a good explanation for his teammates as to why he wasn't going to apologize to the stupid people with cameras.

But it left a bitter taste in his mouth.

*No one cares about how I feel.*

He grumbled that it was unfair that nobody had sent him even a single text message all night.

Was there really no one who understood how he felt...?

"Cha Eugene."

"...!"

Cha Eugene nearly jumped out of his chair at the familiar but unexpected voice.

"Hyung??"

"Yes. It's me."

It was Park Moondae.

*Oh my gosh.*

His teammate had run down the beach to find him.

It was a welcome sight, but it was also a surprise. What kind of magic did he do?

"How did you find me? Were you looking for me?"

"Why does that matter?" Park Moondae asked.

Park Moondae was dressed comfortably. His pink hair fluttered in the sea breeze, disheveled as though he'd been searching quite diligently for him.

But instead of getting angry, he grinned and held out a paper bag.

Inside was ice cream from a nearby shop.

"It was obvious where you'd try to go. I looked for places near the beach that sold sweets, and this cafe was on a route close to the hotel."

"......"

"I thought you would definitely be around here."

It was an accurate deduction. Cha Eugene was more impressed than bitter or spooked by it.

"Hyung is amazing," Cha Eugene said.

"Yeah. I know, so eat that ice cream first. This cafe allows outside food, right?"

[If the tip is big enough. This isn't Disneyland, after all.]

It was a pretty good joke, but Park Moondae didn't seem to get it. Cha Eugene was used to this kind of situation, so he just let it go.

If he was going to nitpick about cultural differences, he'd have to start all the way back with their fancy hair dye. Those pretty colors weren't common for American guys his age.

However, Park Moondae only brought up the main subject after Cha Eugene had eaten more than half of the ice cream.

"Then, tell me your side while you eat. Why did you lash out at the production team like that?"

[This sounds like an interrogation.]

"It's not. I'm asking because I think you had your reasons."

Park Moondae crossed his arms.

"You are a smart guy."

Cha Eugene was a little moved to hear that from his older teammate.

So he shrugged his shoulders and started explaining.
