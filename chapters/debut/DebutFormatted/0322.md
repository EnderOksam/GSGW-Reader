# Chapter 322 - An Unexpected Lead

A few days later.

TeSTAR's performance clip was uploaded to the official WeTube channel of the company that hosted this year's first awards ceremony.

It was the performance we'd given right after winning our first grand prize of the year.

***[Whee-ik!]***

Water and light spurted from some makeshift devices adapted from our concert stage, followed by an explosion of complex choreography.

We'd deliberately put the performance together like that so fans who'd seen our concert would tell people, 'Isn't that cool? The effects at their own concert were even better than this.'

Of course, it became a trending video.

Of course, VTIC's performance was trending too, but what did it matter whether they waved their capes around or rode a boat?

Our first performance in a while had been so complex it ate up all my brain capacity. I couldn't think about anything else.

I held back a sigh.

*I'm glad I didn't mess up.*

My body had barely remembered the moves, so I'd just managed to avoid disaster. I'd almost made mistakes in the choreography at our first performance after winning the grand prize.

*I'd never have heard the end of it.*

Strangely, the reaction to my acceptance speech was ridiculously better than expected...

If I'd actually messed up on stage, I wouldn't have been able to get away with an excuse like 'He did it because he was moved.'

People would've started debating whether I lacked professionalism.

But that didn't happen, and instead all that remained was... A few unnecessarily intense close-ups of me.

"What the...! I'm still watching!" Cha Eugene protested.

*I know you just popped out of the bathroom while brushing your teeth.*

I changed the screen to our 'Magical Girls' cover stage, and Cha Eugene ran away.

"Hyung, that's not fair!"

If he felt wronged, he could just grab the remote first next time.

Seeing that Cha Eugene had retreated to the bathroom, I was about to turn off the TV when I noticed the T-Holic guys standing in the background of the 'Magical Girls' performance video.

Their eyes were out of focus. The overwhelmed looks on their faces had contributed greatly to creating the cheesy mood.

*Come to think of it, I think those guys also sent a congratulatory message about the grand prize.*

I opened my smartphone and looked at the contents.

I hadn't been nearly as busy as Keun Sejin, whose phone had run out of battery by the time we got home on the day of the award, but I'd received quite a few messages and calls myself. 

After winning the grand prize, everyone from Youngrin to entertainers I'd only met once on a variety show had gotten in touch with me.

I stared. Did these bastards even open a new group chat just to send this?

There was even a personal message asking me specifically if it was okay to upload a screenshot of the messages to their variety show's social media account.

*Don't they ever take a break?* There was a reason they were still going strong on their variety show long after their heyday.

I told them it was fine to upload it, but any controversy was their responsibility—worded politely, of course.

As for the other messages, I could just respond with a quick thanks.

It was then.

The member who was quickest to notice things like this, Keun Sejin, shoved his face close to my smartphone.

"Wait, why is Moondaemoondae watching the penalty stage alone... Oh, T-Holic sunbaenim~!" he cried dramatically.

*This bastard seems to care less and less about my privacy.*

But I didn't bother covering the screen. The T-Holic thing was just work.

But I guess that wasn't what he was interested in. Lee Sejin grinned and sat down on the sofa.

"As expected of T-Holic sunbaenim~ Even their KakaoTalk messages are peak comedy! Hm, but what did the other sunbaenim say?"

There was no need to ask who it was. He was talking about the seniors who narrowly missed out on the first Artist of the Year award.

VTIC.

Of course this guy was curious.

*They've sent us messages after all kinds of awards and appearances. It'd look silly if they kept their mouths shut now.*

Of course, VTIC had shown they were far from finished by winning 'Album of the Year' at the finale.

And they would probably manage to take the Artist of the Year award instead of us at about half of the remaining award ceremonies.

*Still, they must feel like shit to suddenly have it snatched away after winning it for so long.*

They might stop sending friendly messages from now on.

"What did they send you?"

"They just congratulated me. But I know how to read between the lines~ I just wanted to know what they sent to the other members!"

*Uh-huh.*

He was curious whether they'd sounded overbearing or wary in any of their messages. I grinned and scrolled down through the messages I hadn't opened yet as I worked through my replies.

And I quickly found a message from a VTIC member.

"...!"

However... It wasn't a congratulatory message.

★:
-VTIC Shin Cheongryeo Sunbaenim: Do you have something to talk about now?
:★

*Ah, this.*

*He's figured it out.*

He'd been dropping hints since November, and apparently he'd caught on that something had changed after seeing my acceptance speech.

*This bastard, he's been waiting.*

Then, I heard a quiet muttering next to me.

"...Something to talk about?"

*Right.* To someone who knew just how bad my relationship with Cheongryeo was, this looked practically like an insult.

*He must think Cheongryeo's trying to pick a fight.*

Of course, it was true that the relationship was messy. So it was easy to make excuses. 

"Ah, we almost got into another fight over the grand prize."

"What??"

"It's nothing much, just a little quarrel. I'm sure I'll come out on top after I give him a call in a little while."

I waved my hand.

"This bastard can't do anything to me. I still have a recording."

"...Moondae, you're pretty impressive. Amazing."

Keun Sejin shook his head as if impressed. I was just about to change the topic...

"Give it here. I'll listen and tell you who I think won."

*Would you look at that?*

"Don't worry, I'm polite when I'm on the phone. There won't be a repeat of last time."

When I remembered Chae Seodam's recording situation, it was not unreasonable for this guy to come out like this.

However, Lee Sejin frowned.

"That's not what I'm worried about. No, I'm worried about that too, but it's not the main thing... Moondae, you've been acting weird these days."

"......"

I stared at him.

*Hmm.*

"You seem to forget your moves, and when you sing your parts, it feels like you've been off doing something else and are struggling to get back into the groove..."

*Wait, don't tell me...*

While I was silent, Lee Sejin finished the sentence.

"So you really have been on some secret NIS mission, as I suspected?"

"......"

*Look at his imagination.*

I stared at him, flabbergasted, but Lee Sejin just grinned.

"Our days are packed with practice and other schedules, so I don't have time for that."

"You could go out at dawn? Uh, wait. Chungwoo-hyung is a morning person too, so that might actually work."

"Stop talking nonsense."

"Moondae also caught Raebin composing early in the morning. Maybe~? Did you see him on your way out of the dorm?"

*Was this guy serious?*

But I could tell from his expression that he knew he was talking bullshit.

He knew something was going on, but he was letting me off by making jokes.

"......"

I held back a sigh and spoke.

"I told you I'd tell you about it when I could."

"...!"

"It's nothing serious, and everything is working out."

I spoke slowly.

"Of course, it has nothing to do with the NIS either. It's just something personal. It won't affect TeSTAR's activities."

"...I know."

Keun Sejin sighed, then smiled wryly.

"Sorry. We've been through a lot. I was just wondering if you were in trouble and couldn't talk about it."

It sounded like he was speaking sincerely.

I pondered briefly, but soon answered honestly.

"I'll tell you if I need help."

"You mean that, right?"

*Yeah.*

When I nodded, Lee Sejin's bitter smile disappeared. Then he patted me on the shoulder and got up from the couch.

"Okay. I'll trust you, then, Moondaemoondae~"

He dragged Cha Eugene, who had just arrived, to the kitchen.

"Hoo."

I leaned back on the sofa.

*I don't know how long that excuse is going to last.*

Even so, it would be crazy to tell them about this absurd situation on a whim and ask them to just believe me. There was a reason I'd kept my mouth shut so far.

*So this sort of thing happens when you've been in a group long enough.*

It was hard to hide things, especially from the more perceptive guys.

In fact, I was even starting to feel like I ought to tell them the truth to some extent...

*Is there any other option?*

I'd come up with a plan for that later, but right now, I had something I needed to do.

I went out onto the veranda and made a phone call.

"Did I wake you? Sorry."

– I'm fine.

His decades of experience were really showing. It seemed he'd completely adapted to an irregular schedule.

"I have something to talk about, like you asked in your message."

"I'm fine. There were no problems at all, and I got the grand prize as well."

He was insinuating I wouldn't have many more moments like this to enjoy.

I considered snapping back with something like 'You should be the one enjoying the moment, sunbaenim,' but thought better of it. 

It was not the time. Instead, I went straight to the point.

"Thank you. May I ask how that call I asked you to make went?"
