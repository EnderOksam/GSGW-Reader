# Chapter 301 - ⟨T-Holic's Show Business⟩, I

⟨T-Holic's Show Business⟩ was the kind of studio variety show that had largely been replaced by observational variety shows.

Unlike those shows, where interesting situations were created through editing, this format meant that only the really funny ones survived.

"Ahahaha!! That's hilarious."

"Why, why?"

"No... Do you know what Wonseok hyung just said to me? 'Do all idols these days look like that?'"

The impersonation was so convincing that even the staff burst out laughing. The member he'd just named replied awkwardly.

"No, I'm not the only one who's surprised."

"What? But you're right."

"You didn't know? If we had debuted now, we would have been doomed, guys. Don't forget that and be humble."

"No, don't we deserve some credit for being born in the right generation?"

"Stop it! You're making me sad!"

The jokes started pouring in as soon as the cameras rolled.

They came across as seasoned variety show professionals, and perhaps because they were famous as idols, that in itself had become part of their on-screen characters.

Whether that was prejudice or generosity.

*That's why it's hard for a guest who comes alone to shine here.*

They'd created the show themselves and hogged the spotlight, so most guests had little choice but to rely on them.

These guys had debuted a long time ago, too. Most guests were in a position of speaking to their industry seniors.

"Wow, I didn't even know there was another generation after us millennials!"

"You guys are so cute."

Of course, T-Holic would do what they could to keep the mood up, naturally setting their Hubaenims up to shine and drawing reactions like these online.

★$
- Wow, T-Holic really makes things easy for the guests ㅋㅋ

- I've never seen them talk so comfortably on a show before ㅠㅠ Thank you, T-Holic! ♡

- Even groups that are shy on other shows could do well on this one
$★

The moment T-Holic took that momentum for themselves, the guests lost the spotlight and were reduced to mere props in the show.

But if a guest didn't care much about who was the senior, things would be a little different.

"I'm not that cute! I'm cool!"

"Huh?"

Cha Eugene raised his hand and shouted.

The T-Holic members' mouths fell open, perhaps because they'd never received such an energetic (?) rebuttal from the very start.

*I knew it would be like this.*

Cha Eugene didn't know T-Holic very well.

No matter how much more experience they had, he spoke as freely as he usually did.

And it didn't come across as rude, because he was a foreigner.

"I-is that so?"

"Yes! I'm a tiger!"

"I see. Our friend Cha Eugene is a tiger."

"That's right! T-t-tiger!" Cha Eugene switched to English.

"T-t-tiger!" one of the T-Holic members repeated with a nod.

"Hyung, don't copy the kids just to look cool."

Most people would let him get away with it. Cha Eugene was already famous for his American-style behavior.

*And these guys will take care of the rest.*

The T-Holic guys were so used to keeping the mood lively that they couldn't very well act serious or awkward around Cha Eugene.

*Besides, we've got someone here who can tone him down if necessary.*

"Cha Eugene! They were complimenting you! Just thank them and be done with it!"

It was Kim Raebin.

He'd tried to whisper, but it was useless since he was wearing a microphone.

"I accepted it! I didn't refuse!"

"You said you weren't that cute! It could sound rude if you're not careful!"

"I just meant I'm more cool than cute! Thank you for calling me cute."

"Oh... Right."

"No, we got it! We understood what you meant!"

"Thank you so much for your understanding! We'll do our best today."

"You're really serious about this show, aren't you?"

Watching Cha Eugene and Kim Raebin bow, only for the T-Holic members to quickly bow back, was pretty funny.

*They're making a good impact.*

I noticed a few of the T-Holic members who'd stepped back smiling and exchanging glances.

They seemed to have decided it'd be funny to make it look like they were struggling right from the start.

"All right, then! Cute, cool, all-around amazing TeSTAR!"

"Hehe."

"Hey, is this a Gen Z thing? I think we're getting old."

"Hyung, we're all idols in our 30s. In this industry, we've got one foot in the coffin already."

T-Holic was painting themselves as a group struggling to keep up with the new generation but still trying to adapt.

They were taking full advantage of Cha Eugene's unstoppable momentum.

*The focus is still T-Holic.*

TeSTAR was a very popular idol group right now, and T-Holic was being portrayed as our polar opposite.

And I was faithfully fulfilling the role of a quiet person with common sense.

"Then, Moondae-ssi is the main vocalist, right?"

"Yes. That's right."

"Ah, my song! I loved the way you sang 'Party in Me'."

"Don't lie, you were in the army!"

"No! I heard it while I was on leave!"

"Ah, what an honor. Thank you."

"Yeah~ Moondae-hyung sings really well!"

They asked me to sing a little of the song, so I did, and they duly applauded.

In other words, I did the usual things an idol promoting something would do on a show like this.

T-Holic was probably starting to get a feel for us by now.

*They're probably thinking I'm pretty normal, so they can keep treating me like this and get their laughs out of those two.*

Since they were familiar with this kind of show, they probably figured us out quickly.

*I doubt these guys looked into my past variety show appearances before inviting us here.*

At most, they'd probably checked our recent promotions and results. The more convinced they were that I was a calm sort of guy, the better.

"Now then! That does it for the introductions!"

Figuring they'd gotten enough laughs out of Cha Eugene and Kim Raebin's banter, they moved on to the next segment.

For all intents and purposes, this was the main attraction.

It was a particularly funny segment, usually involving games.

There'd been games like basketball without a ball, chairs that sent people flying into a ball pit, and guessing who someone had drawn a portrait of with their feet.

"Now, the second track of ⟨Show Business⟩! It's time for a game where we reveal our true feelings!"

Since it was the main segment, I'd made a few guesses about what kind of game they'd play. *The most likely one is...*

"The game we are going to play with TeSTAR this time is~ **'Words of Persuasion'**!"

"Ahem."

*Right. That's the one.*

I'd nearly choked hearing the name. I couldn't help it, since it was a phrase I'd heard before.

*Good thing I did my research.*

The first time I saw that on WeTube, I almost spat out the water I was drinking.

"Hyung, hydration is important."

*Yeah, thanks.* I drank the water Kim Raebin handed me and listened as T-Holic got on with what they did best.

*This is the segment I was expecting.*

It had been appearing once every 3 episodes or so lately, and the audience loved it. I figured they'd bring it out for a top-tier idol group like us.

#*[Words of Persuasion!]*#

The title suggested persuading the opponent, much like the trait I'd had before, but this was a little different.

The maknae of T-Holic held a voice-changing microphone and adopted a rather serious tone.

"Each team chooses a statement and tries to persuade the other team. The game ends when one team is persuaded by the other's logic. A terrifying penalty awaits the losing team."

"The winner of the mini-game gets to pick a statement first. Now, good luck."

The game sounded normal enough, didn't it? But these guys hid a trap in every game.

It was revealed along with the statements after the mini-game.

"Voila!"

**@cc@[Your team's goal for next year]@cc@**

**@cc@[Receive one billion won VS be stranded in a remote location]@cc@**

"...???"

"Ahahahaha!! You must be crazy, really!!"

"Guys, welcome to ⟨Show Business⟩!"

The two options weren't remotely balanced.

After winning the mini-game, T-Holic happily chose the former.

"One billion! One billion!"

"You're stranded, Generation Z! Time to deal with it~"

At first glance, it might seem like the game would be boring, with the guests getting beaten one-sidedly.

But then the second trap was revealed.

"Everyone, I told you that you had to be persuaded by logic, right?"

"Hey, no way?"

"That's right! The team that gets persuaded by the most believable logic wins!"

In a sense, it was a battle of sophistry.

The goal was to graciously yield to the opponent's argument.

In other words, we had to defend our own side as absurdly as possible while accepting what the other team was saying.

In other words, for T-Holic to win, they'd have to be persuaded that it was better to be stranded in a remote location than to receive 1 billion won.

On the other hand, TeSTAR just had to accept the obvious proposition that taking 1 billion won was better than being stranded.

"Isn't that unfair??"

"Why not?"

*Whoever came up with this did a good job.*

The setup allowed them to entertain the audience by making fools of themselves while giving the guests an easy win.

*As expected, they're taking the top WeTube comments into account.*

★$
–-#just now#- I think it'd be fun if the team that *gets* persuaded wins haha [👍 👎]

–-#just now#- This game's legendary... Please keep bringing it back like beef bone soup! ㅋㅋㅋ [👍 👎]
$★

Like a startup that had just launched, the production crew here seemed to pay excessive attention to communicating with viewers.

It was common to see top comments from a couple of episodes earlier reflected in the show's content.

So I'd looked through the previous videos to anticipate what curveballs might be in store, and I'd hit the nail on the head.

It was obvious how things would go from here.

"Uh? Well... You know how suddenly receiving a lot of money can have a negative effect on people. But if I wanted to retire now, I could avoid that. I think taking 1 billion won is the way to go!"

"Yes, yes!"

"I don't want to retire! I want to do this for a long time!"

"Okay, then what will you do? Oh, would you look at that! The other option fits perfectly!"

"We'll take 1 billion won and not retire! Money won't ruin me. Our members are strong!"

"Wow, the way he talks... He's so full of energy..."

"But money can be a scary thing, you see~ No, we're not saying you shouldn't take 1 billion won, but..."

They'd act flustered while making absurd arguments and poking fun at the guests, who were supposed to respond with common sense.

Once they'd gotten enough funny reactions from the guests, they'd cave and graciously let them win.

*But that'd be exactly what they want.*

*I didn't come here just to let them use us as pawns...*

I grinned.

*I know the best way to play this game.*

Simple. We just needed to support the opponent's logic. That was how the production team expected us to play it.

But following the reasonable path was exactly what T-Holic wanted.

So... we needed to go the other way.

I put my hands on the table and interlocked them.

"Sunbaenim."

"Yes, Moondae-ssi, go ahead~"

"I've done my best to understand and accept the other side's argument so we can win, but..."

I solemnly declared.

"I'm afraid the statement we received is the right one. There's no getting around it."

"...??"

"I think our team would choose to be stranded over receiving 1 billion won."

"Wait, what??"

"W-what are you talking about?"

I answered seriously.

"Because we can learn things more valuable than money by being stranded."

"What?"

"What lessons?"

I answered Cha Eugene clearly when he quietly asked with a tap on my shoulder.

"Realistically speaking, we're likely to be stranded somewhere we'd be rescued from within a few days at most. In the meantime, we'd get to have new experiences."

"Aha! That's right."

Cha Eugene immediately nodded.

"Then I'm fine with being stranded! I like the idea!"

"...??"

T-Holic's maknae, who served as the MC, intervened.

"Wait! You're going to lose the game at this rate!"

"We can't give up our values over one game."

"You have... Values about being stranded??"

"Guys, why... I'm scared."

As the T-Holic maknae grew increasingly intimidated by this turn of events, even Kim Raebin nodded earnestly.

"I think so too."

"What?"

"Yes."

"Yes. We've actually been stranded before while filming a variety show."

Kim Raebin said with his eyes sparkling.

"And we learned a lot from that experience."

"Wait a minute."

"I made the title track of our last album while we were stranded. I'm afraid choosing the money over being stranded would be tantamount to abandoning musical achievements in pursuit of money!"

"Wait, that's not how this works!"

Kim Raebin had momentarily forgotten we were on a variety show and was earnestly expressing his opinion.

I'd known this would happen once he got immersed in the logic of it.

"No... Think about it, guys. Does this make sense? I mean, being *stranded*??"

"How much do you get paid to refuse 1 billion won?!"

I raised my hand.

“Come to think of it, the title track Raebin made while we were stranded increased our payouts. You could even say that being stranded might be more profitable...”

"Oh~"

"I see!"

"Please don't react like you've been persuaded, uhhhhhh."

And the set descended into chaos.

Just as I'd planned.
