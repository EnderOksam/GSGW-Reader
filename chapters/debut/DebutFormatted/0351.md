# Chapter 351 - The Weight of Secrets, II

Wow!

The music and cheering were so loud that they made our ears ring.

Cha Eugene peeked outside and whistled.

"Wow, there are so many people here!"

"Oh~ That's right. This is really exciting!" Keun Sejin said.

It wasn't the first time we'd seen a large crowd, but it was still spectacular to see people around our age wearing the same-colored clothes, singing and jumping in unison.

The thought that we'd be performing in front of them soon, perhaps within thirty minutes, made my hair stand on end.

"There are three more teams ahead of us. That's enough time for you guys to stretch, right?" the manager asked.

"Yes!"

"It's such a relief that we arrived on time," Kim Raebin said.

"That's right. Phew, you've worked hard, manager hyung~" Keun Sejin said.

The manager sighed in relief and started running around talking to various staff members.

In the meantime, we sat in the space set aside as a waiting room and warmed up our bodies and voices.

*I wasn't aware of this place.*

Maybe it was because I hadn't been interested in festivals when I was in college, but I hadn't even known there was a place like this on campus. No, even if I'd known, I'd never have come here.

*What matters is that we perform well today.*

College festivals were just like any other event, but a lot of people really looked forward to them.

*Maybe it's because they're a good way to measure general popularity.*

Fancams were also a great way to gain attention.

In fact, I'd been impressed that the festival lineup had remained a secret until this morning.

*I don't know who arranged it this time, but they sure were determined to prevent scalping.*

Of course, the moment we got here, people spotted our van and recognized us as we got out.

Meanwhile, some people were asking the university to lower tuition if it had enough money to afford TeSTAR for its festival, but that wasn't our problem. I wasn't even a graduate of this university anymore. It was none of my business.

In any case, we had to make the money they spent on us worthwhile.

"Moondae-hyung, do you need some water before you stretch?" Kim Raebin asked.

"I'm okay," I said.

"I want some!" Cha Eugene said.

"Cha Eugene, you're sitting closer to the water than I am!"

*They're the same as always.*

I watched the two guys bicker as usual. It was practically their warm-up routine by now. I kept stretching.

Ahyeon was next to me, faithfully loosening his muscles in descending order of size.

He did this every time we performed.

*It looks completely natural for him.*

It wasn't surprising, since he'd probably been doing it since he was little.

*Hmm, maybe I should've learned how to stretch from him?*

"Ahyeon-ah."

"Yes?"

"Could you teach me how you do those stretches in that order, just once?"

"O-of course...!"

Seon Ahyeon's face brightened in an instant.

*...Are you really that happy?*

Somehow, it reminded me of the days when I learned the choreography from him during ⟨Idol Inc.⟩.

I followed along, copying his movements. In his enthusiasm, he was being a little excessive.

*I think this guy could have made a living as a teacher.*

"And finally... back to the whole body."

"Mhm."

I finished the last stretch neatly.

*Hey, this isn't bad at all. I should start doing this from now on.*

It seemed I should've learned his technique sooner.

"Thanks. This is nice."

"......"

However, Seon Ahyeon stayed silent for a long time, then suddenly opened his mouth.

"Um... Moo-Moondae."

"What is it?"

"...N-nothing."

Seon Ahyeon smiled faintly.

"I... I just wanted to say... Let's have a good one."

"...Okay."

*I thought you had something to say.*

I pressed my lips together.

*Does he feel awkward around me, just like I expected?*

I was sure it had to be uncomfortable finding out that I was actually older than him.

*Maybe I shouldn't have—*

*No. I need to stop thinking about this.*

Work was our priority right now, so I'd focus on that. I put aside the pointless guessing and got up.

"Let's get ready!"

Moments later...

We ran out onto the outdoor stage, where the atmosphere was already on fire from the crowd's enthusiasm.

People's voices were already hoarse as they started screaming again, while excited audience members pressed toward the stage.

The huge crowd seemed to sway in waves that washed out from the stage.

The excitement was almost frenzied.

*Does it always feel this way?*

It was a completely different feeling to see such a large crowd screaming passionately when I knew they weren't all fans.

*Actually, there are some fans.*

"Park Moondae!!"

I grinned when I saw a few brave people holding lightsticks.

As soon as we reached the front of the stage, we started with last year's *daesang*-winning song, 'Promise,' and the atmosphere was incredible.

Events were a lot easier when you had popular songs like this.

*And it's nice hearing them sing along, too.*

One of the members even performed with an in-ear removed so he could listen to the crowd.

When the last notes of the accompaniment for 'Promise' faded, we stood up straight as screams tore through the air.

We were handed handheld microphones.

**"Take your star! Hello! We are TeSTAR!"** we called in unison.

The crowd roared. After catching his breath, Ryu Chungwoo smoothly kicked off a relaxed talk segment.

"We are so happy to meet all the wonderful students of Yeonhee University..."

But then Ryu Chungwoo suddenly looked my way and grinned.

*Huh?*

He smoothly held the mic up to my face.

"...??"

"How do you feel?"

*Hey... Even this bastard's making fun of me.*

I realized he wanted me to comment on returning to my old school. I held back a sigh and opened my mouth.

"The enthusiasm is amazing. Blue always beats red."

I gave the crowd a thumbs up.

*This is the smart way to go.*

*Mention the university's color and say it's better than its rival's.*

Everyone knew that if we went and performed at their rival university, we'd turn around and say red was the best. The crowd loved it anyway.

Aaaaaagh! Gyaaak!

*Not a bad response.*

Ryu Chungwoo smiled and took the microphone back.

"And so, we prepared a song about blue beating red."

"Please pay attention to our movements and those of the dancers as you enjoy the next song. Here we go!"

Keun Sejin waved his hand enthusiastically and took his position. The intro to our latest title track began to play.

Of course, I came in right on cue for my intro part.

Aagh!

The crowd screamed, loving the high notes. I ran across the stage, making exaggerated gestures with my arms.

The energy didn't die down as we moved on to the next song and then the one after that.

Neither ours nor the audience's.

"Phew."

"Thank you!"

We'd agreed to perform three songs, but since we had time left over, we decided to do an encore.

It was nice not having to worry about singing too many songs like we would at a full concert.

"One more?"

"Let's do it! We can do it!" Cha Eugene said.

The encore song was ⟨Black Hole⟩, the song from the movie soundtrack.

We could sing it while walking around, which made it easier to manage our stamina.

It was already well-known, so the audience responded well, too.

*A success,* I thought, my instincts kicking in.

This college festival appearance would be great for our reputation.

Everything seemed to be going so well lately that for once, I wasn't worried about anything.

It seemed the other excited members felt the same way, since they began another song. Apparently, they'd forgotten we had an early shoot the next morning.

"Phew! Why don't we do another one? Is there something you want to hear?" Cha Eugene asked.

Picnic! Picnic!!

Magical Boys!

The crowd shouted the names of our most popular songs. We grinned as we listened.

*We have all of these ready.*

We planned to keep the crowd on the edge of their seats for a little while before choosing the song they were shouting for the loudest.

But suddenly, Kim Raebin's eyes sparkled.

"I... Would it be all right if I said something?"

"Hm? Yes!"

"This is a college, after all. Everyone here is preparing to enter society for the first time. I think our debut song would be perfect!"

Wow!!

The crowd seemed to love the idea.

*Wow, I didn't know he could be such a smooth talker.*

"...I agree!" Bae Sejin shouted.

Had Bae Sejin always been this eager?

Everyone seemed to be excited today. Anyway, we'd decided on our next song.

"Let's go with that, then!"

The crowd roared, "Yes!!"

"All right! Please play 'Magical Boys'," Ryu Chungwoo called confidently.

The staff below the stage exchanged signals.

I waited for the intro to 'Magical Boys' to begin.

– Oh oh oh oh oh oh

"...!"

"Huh?"

At that time, an energetic mixed-gender chorus came blasting from the speakers.

It was a somewhat low-quality band sound, with a melody taken from a famous classical piece.

This was definitely not the music-box intro of 'Magical Boys'.

I got it right away.

*They played the wrong song.*

They had played the cheering song that was supposed to come on after our performance.

"...Mmm."

In other words, we had a mistake on our hands.

*Things like this happen.*

It was the sort of mistake that could easily happen at an event.

The problem was that it had been about seven seconds now, and the sound still hadn't turned off.

*There must be some chaos going on down there.*

If we all froze up in confusion, the mood would be ruined. There were too many phones pointed at us to let that happen.

*The least I can do is wave my hands.*

*We should pretend we're enjoying this.*

I watched the others smile and nod their heads to the beat, then raised both hands and moved along with them.

Then I realized something.

*Wait, I can actually just...*

*I can do this instead.*

I stepped forward and picked up the handheld microphone I had set down on the floor.

"...?"

And I began to sing along to the chorus.

Ohhh!

*And the lyrics are actually from a poem.*

The lyrics didn't have to be perfect, as long as I could get the crowd enjoying themselves.

And just after we'd passed the chorus of the first verse, the music finally turned off.

Wow!!

"Thank you," I said.

It was good timing for me. I didn't know the second verse. Laughter and applause poured in. The funny thing was, the members were laughing and applauding, too.

"Thanks for the encore fight song!" Keun Sejin said.

"Awesome!"

*Well, that wasn't half bad, if I do say so myself.*
