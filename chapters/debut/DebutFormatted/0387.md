*With this guy's personality, he must have run simulations of exposing the president himself several times...*

Seeing how profusely the president had apologized, it seemed all those meetings with lawyers hadn't been in vain.

"Of course, morally speaking, it would be better to report him to the police and follow the proper procedures, but... I wouldn't be able to do any activities for a while if I did that."

"Ah."

Bae Sejin fiddled with his water glass.

"Well... I still need to help you win the *daesang*."

"For that to happen, it's better for me to continue to be active. But still! I think I've made it so that the president won't dare do something like that again."

"Thank you."

*It's good.*

"Because I always play roles like that... Um, yeah."

"Hey, you probably feel that way because you do well every time!"

"That's not it... Anyway, thank you."

And it seemed that Lee Sejin immediately discarded all career-related small talk topics after that.

However, even after that, all of Lee Sejin's personal questions bounced off the stone wall that was Bae Sejin.

"Ah, that coat looks good on you! Do you wear it often?"

"Hmm... The manager gave it to me."

"The manager has a good eye."

So Bae Sejin, hearing praise for the manager he'd fired that very morning, responded like this.

"...It's hard to say."

"......"

Since he wasn't good at ignoring the truth and responding with a lie, he kept falling silent...

These guys just... didn't fit together.

"Let's talk more while we eat. You must be hungry."

*Ah~ This isn't going to work.*

Lee Sejin was eating and internally evaluating the situation.

It would be difficult to form a connection with the actor sitting across from him.

*Did he not want to come today? Why is he like that?*

Actually, he thought that they could be friends somehow if they kept talking to each other persistently... But for some reason, he didn't even want to do that.

*It's not necessary to have connections to actors.*

Lee Sejin had to wait at least a few more years before he could pursue his own solo career in acting. So, there was no need for him to be in a hurry... *Maybe.*

*...Or am I being too complacent?*

Looking at how his group was doing, it seemed there was no chance of renewing the contract. The thought of having to strike out on his own in a few years was somehow bittersweet.

*But that's a problem for later.*

It was silly to be taking this so seriously. After all, his group was doing well right now...

"The ribs here are delicious."

"...! Oh, that's right~ I didn't know about this delicious restaurant!"

"I heard that fans often post hashtags to recommend places like this to you."

"That's right, that's right, I hope they keep uploading a lot."

When Ryu Gunwoo saw that the conversation between the two Sejins wasn't going well, he gently took the lead himself.

He seemed unusually proactive.

*...Unusually proactive?*

Lee Sejin wasn't sure why he'd thought that when this was only their second meeting.

*Well, just by looking at him, he doesn't seem like an extrovert.* Lee Sejin moved on.

*Anyway, why did he suggest bringing someone like that along when we could've met alone...?*

*...No, then maybe I wouldn't have come.*

Right. He'd probably only agreed because Gunwoo said he'd introduce him to someone new... Was that it?

*He didn't know.* Strangely, the flow of his thoughts felt jumbled.

Lee Sejin didn't show it, but he was a little confused.

*It's strange.*

Still, the conversation continued. Oddly, talking to Gunwoo was fun and... comfortable.

"How was the tour?"

And when they moved on to idols, promotions, and survival programs, the conversation began to flow more naturally.

"The tour was fun. Hyung will get to do one soon too. You're so good! ⟨Wise⟩ is very popular."

"But if I actually debut, the state of the industry matters, along with the song and concept. I can't necessarily say it will work out well..."

Ryu Gunwoo's pessimistic way of speaking was similar to that actor's, but he ended his speech with a smile.

"Still, I hope you get to tour. I think you'll find it very rewarding."

"...Hmm, yes. Performing on stage is hard, but the work itself is really fun."

"Yes."

Although his questions were typical of a survival contestant with an uncertain future, there was nothing desperate or insistent about him.

What else could he say? The guy was pretty laid-back.

"...Hyung's agency is famous for arranging tours often. It'll be fine."

Perhaps that was why Lee Sejin found himself giving slightly more honest answers instead of his usual calculated responses meant to maintain relationships.

Naturally, the topic became more serious.

And then came this question.

"Now that I think about it... I wanted to ask you how you felt when you debuted. How was it?"

*Debut?*

"It felt surreal, but it was also very hectic, you know? I was so busy that I didn't have time to reflect much on it. You'll probably be the same."

It was true.

But he went on to say more than was necessary.

"...Well, it might not feel like you'd think, though."

If he was expecting burning passion, the fun of taking on new challenges, and the stability of a well-earned position, Lee Sejin would advise him to give it up.

Ryu Gunwoo asked quietly.

"Do you have any regrets?"

*Regrets?*

"Still, I don't think I have any particular regrets. Of course... Unexpected difficulties also arise."

*Like your group members, whom you can't do anything about since you've already debuted together.*

"Like what?"

"Oh, well..."

Lee Sejin opened his mouth without thinking, and only then did he realize.

He was being unnecessarily honest.

*Why am I saying this...*

And he forced himself to change the subject naturally.

"...There's no easy way to make money~ Something like that!"

"...Yeah. I guess so."

"......"

Ryu Gunwoo didn't ask any more questions. He'd definitely sensed something strange about Lee Sejin's reaction, and it felt like he was being considerate by not pursuing the topic.

*Ah.*

Lee Sejin was a little disappointed but relieved.

*...?? What am I disappointed about?*

Then the actor, who'd been eating with gusto, suddenly spoke up.

"That's right. There is nothing easy about making money."

"...?"

"People have to earn their keep."

*Suddenly?*

Lee Sejin didn't know why the actor had said that, but since the man was clearly older than him, he answered gently.

"That... That's right. Yes, yes."

The actor gripped his chopsticks so hard that the veins on his hands stood out.

"Some people just cling to their positions and act lazy, doing nothing to improve, and they end up doing things they shouldn't... People like that don't deserve to make money."

"Oh, they shouldn't do that. Really."

Lee Sejin found himself agreeing wholeheartedly with the actor without even thinking.

The examples close to home that immediately came to mind made him angry just thinking about them.

Lee Sejin spoke coldly, recalling the face of a bastard in his group who'd gotten drunk the day before their tour, failed to manage his condition, and couldn't perform at his best.

"There's a certain level of professionalism required of people. And if someone in a group fails to show that professionalism, the entire group suffers as a consequence."

"That's right! No matter how skilled they are, I'd rather have a good person who works hard, even if they're less skilled!"

However, despite shouting out energetically, the actor suddenly flinched and added on more words.

"Well... Maybe you prefer someone who's good at their job even if they're immoral or lazy, over someone who's less skilled but works hard."

And he quickly added,

"Most people are like that!"

"......"

Lee Sejin thought about answering, 'Yes, most people are like that,' but thought better of it.

For some reason, he was a little upset.

*No, I agreed with what you were saying... Don't assume I'm as pessimistic as you are. Do you really think I'm that kind of person?*

"No, I think I'd also prefer someone who works hard, even if they're not the best at what they do."

"...Really?"

"That's right. If we're going to keep working together in the long run, someone who works hard will eventually get good at their job, won't they?"

"...!"

Lee Sejin came to a conclusion without realizing it.

"If you're going to work together with someone, people like that are best."

It felt satisfying to say it out loud.

"......"

It looked like the actor was about to drop his chopsticks the moment he heard that.

*Why was he like that?*

But he quickly adjusted his grip and muttered in a much brighter tone.

"...That's right. You're right."

"I know, right~?"

For the first time today, they agreed on something.

And Lee Sejin slightly changed his mind.

Well... His personality seemed a bit eccentric, but he had a solid work ethic.

*He's... not bad.*

And Ryu Gunwoo, who had been eating without interfering in the conversation between the two, only then started talking again.

"Speaking of work, how much practice do you think is appropriate? Though I suppose being an actor is quite different from being an idol."

"Well, isn't consistency important?"

"...Right. I guess habits become ingrained in you."

And naturally, the conversation progressed with all three people participating...

"...!"

*Wait a minute.*

*...So, did he intentionally bring the conversation this way?*

Because that actor only opened his mouth during serious conversations. Lee Sejin himself had gotten so annoyed that he'd given up on small talk, but he didn't mind serious conversations...

Maybe Gunwoo was making sure things between him and the actor didn't get any more awkward.

"......"

Lee Sejin felt embarrassed for some reason and lowered his gaze to the bowl.

Although he was younger than Gunwoo, he was the sunbaenim with more work experience, so it felt a little strange to be on the receiving end of Gunwoo's consideration.

But at the same time... he felt a little at ease. Should he say that it felt reassuring?

*Wow, what's gotten into me today?*

He eventually gave up trying to figure it out.

It seemed he simply wanted to make friends with this Ryu Gunwoo.

*...Having an idol friend sounds nice.*

Lee Sejin held back a sigh and ate some of his soybean paste noodles.

It was delicious.
