# Chapter 253 - Memories

On the first day of the holiday season, Park Moondae came to the front door without warning at 9:00 p.m.

It was a situation that Ryu Chungwoo had never imagined.

*I posted in the group chat saying that it's okay to come over, but...*

It was mostly a joke he'd made while bragging about how delicious the food was.

However, the person who'd firmly rejected his invitation to spend the holiday together suddenly showing up this late at night, without any prior notice... It was very bizarre.

*Is this really Park Moondae?*

Wasn't he cool-headed, a person with good situational judgment, and a very reasonable dongsaeng?

But now, the Park Moondae, standing outside in the dark...

"Hyung."

"...Oh, sorry. Okay."

Ryu Chungwoo paused for a moment, then stepped aside to allow Park Moondae to enter the house.

In any case, he couldn't let him stand outside tonight.

However, an unexplained tension crept over him.

*What?*

A calm voice was heard.

"Where can I wash my hands?"

"...It's over there."

Ryu Chungwoo pointed toward the bathroom. He decided they'd have a calm conversation once Park Moondae had washed his hands.

*Let's tell my parents in advance.*

By now, they must have guessed from the voices that the visitor was his acquaintance.

And Ryu Chungwoo tried to close the door, but...

He came face to face with an awkward-looking Bae Sejin, who'd been standing behind Park Moondae.

"...??"

Why was he out there?

"Uh, hello."

"That, yes. Hi."

Bae Sejin quietly held out a box of fruit.

"These... They're apples."

"Ah, thank you."

Ryu Chungwoo hurriedly accepted the fruit. His strange tension was gone.

*Hmm.*

He then asked a question.

"It's nice of you to come over. But the two of you... What brings you guys here?"

"...I just followed Park Moondae."

Bae Sejin glanced toward the bathroom where Park Moondae entered.

"He wanted to go to your house, but it felt a little... I was curious."

*It felt strange, so he followed him.*

Ryu Chungwoo asked another question while reading Bae Sejin's worries.

"Do you know why he wanted to come here?"

"I don't know. Um, but..."

Bae Sejin whispered to Ryu Chungwoo.

"I think he remembered something."

"...!"

"You know how it always happens with him. Whenever he remembers something in his sleep."

Ryu Chungwoo immediately remembered what had happened in the United States last summer.

*– A person, in front of fear and pain... they can get a little angry and blame others, so that's okay.*

That was what he said to Park Moondae, who finally confessed his true feelings after drinking.

Later, when he noticed Park Moondae's alcoholic tendencies, Ryu Chungwoo privately regretted the approach he'd taken.

*Was there no other way?*

Anyway, it had been quite a shocking experience in its own right.

Wouldn't anyone have felt the same if their dongsaeng, who had been calm and smart, suddenly seemed painfully uncomfortable around them?

Especially when they knew that a previously hazy memory had suddenly come back to him in full force.

*It's a similar... feeling?*

Ryu Chungwoo felt a sense of déjà vu from the strange tension he felt from Park Moondae, whom he had seen earlier.

So he steeled himself for what was coming, only to find his worries unfounded when he saw Park Moondae coming out of the bathroom.

"Hyung, I have a question for you."

Park Moondae's face showed no reluctance towards Ryu Chungwoo.

Just... The look in his eyes made it seem like he'd completely lost it.

*Oh my.*

Ryu Chungwoo quickly exchanged glances with Bae Sejin.

"Well, first... Should we say hello to my parents?"

"Yes."

Bae Sejin hurriedly washed his hands and came out and joined Park Moondae. And Ryu Chungwoo introduced the two to his parents.

It felt like he was breaking into a cold sweat.

"Here... These are my dongsaengs, and they stopped by for a bit and brought some fruit."

"Oh!"

"Welcome~"

His parents seemed a bit surprised, but they welcomed them both happily.

"Hello."

"...Hello!"

The two greeted them politely, but Ryu Chungwoo checked Park Moondae's eyes just in case.

And he found a blank, uninterested look in Park Moondae's eyes.

*Why... Is he like that?*

So his parents weren't the reason he'd come.

Ryu Chungwoo was more confused, but anyway, he sat Park Moondae and Bae Sejin down in the living room.

Kkami ran up and sat on Park Moondae's lap, and Park Moondae skillfully scratched the black dog's ears and spoke.

"Hyung."

"Yeah, yes."

Park Moondae's eyes shone brightly again.

"You said you'd repay me."

"...I did."

That... Wasn't quite how he'd put it, but anyway, Ryu Chungwoo answered honestly. Beside him, Bae Sejin's pupils were shaking.

"I have a favor to ask."

Park Moondae spoke quickly without much expression.

"Hyung, I want to see some pictures of you when you were young."

"...??"

It wasn't what he'd been expecting to hear.

"No, preferably with a video. Are there any records that show your extended family together?"

Ryu Chungwoo asked the obvious question.

"Why?"

"Just because."

It was an invincible answer.

Bae Sejin silently mouthed from behind Moondae.

*Memories from when he was young!*

"...!"

Okay. It may be that he caught clues related to his memories when he was young and wanted to jog his memory by watching similar scenes.

*Is my place the closest to Sejin's house?*

Ryu Chungwoo thought vaguely, smiling in relief that he'd managed to guess what was going on.

"Okay then, shall I bring something out?"

"Yes. Please."

Ryu Chungwoo quickly went to the master bedroom and brought out some photo albums and videos.

And what he saw on the way back was Park Moondae, who was mechanically but skillfully stroking Kkami, and Bae Sejin, who was nervous.

"......"

Ryu Chungwoo pondered for a while, but decided to take into account that although Park Moondae may have lost his senses, he was not as shocked as he was last time.

"Here, would you like to see the album first?"

"I want to watch the video together while looking at the album. And if possible, I would like to see hyung in the lower grades of elementary school. Would that be okay?"

Seeing that the request was specific and the age range was accurate, Ryu Chungwoo also thought that Bae Sejin's guess might be correct.

"Haha, yes."

Ryu Chungwoo relaxed a little and played the video. Footage of him as a child played on the TV.

His shy younger self filled the screen. At the same time, Ryu Chungwoo himself was a little embarrassed.

It was probably Chuseok at that time too, and he remembered that a lot of his relatives had rented cars and gone on a family trip together.

"...What grade were you in? You were tall."

"Probably in the second grade."

A look of defeat appeared on Bae Sejin's face, but Ryu Chungwoo did not notice it.

Park Moondae was scratching the dog's chin.

"As expected."

"...?"

Anyway, the three of them sat there talking about various things while watching the album and video.

His parents smiled proudly whenever they walked past, but they also seemed a little burdened, looking like they were wondering, 'Why are you showing them?'

Perhaps it was because after retiring due to an injury, he'd been hounded by reporters and become defensive around other people.

Ryu Chungwoo smiled bitterly at the faint feeling of guilt.

*Still, I have to say that Moondae saved my life.*

If Park Moondae came over to his house during the holidays, he was originally going to tell them.

Ryu Chungwoo thought so and continued to watch the video. The atmosphere wasn't bad.

They'd moved on to another video.

The puppy had climbed onto Bae Sejin's lap, and he was at a loss for what to do.

*It wasn't a big deal.*

They'd probably sit like this and chat for a while before heading back.

Late-night snacks were not good for the body, but since it was a holiday, Ryu Chungwoo thought that they should eat something healthy.

It was then.

Park Moondae shot to his feet from the seat next to him.

"Wait a minute."

Park Moondae picked up the remote control and paused the video.

And walked towards the TV screen.

With the look of someone who'd found what he was searching for.

***

*It's me.*

I knew it as soon as I saw it.

On the screen, the boy with a bored expression sitting in the corner of the lodging sofa holding a book... It was Ryu Gunwoo.

"......"

Somehow, the tension melted away.

It felt like blood was circulating in my brain again. A suffocating weight seemed to lift.

*So there'd been footage of me at Chungwoo's house all along.*

It was still early in the video. I didn't know if I'd be able to see my parents if I fast-forwarded it a little more... Let's be patient.

I picked up my smartphone and took a picture.

*Click.*

At that point, Bae Sejin asked with a puzzled face.

"That... Is that someone you know?"

"......"

Right.

I was in a hurry right now, so I forgot to come up with an excuse.

*Is my brain rotting?*

Suppressing the urge to click my tongue, I replied.

"I know him, and I've been indebted to him."

"Ah..."

The longer I took to come up with an excuse, the less believable it would sound. As I answered quickly, I remembered the scene where I bought food for 'Park Moondae,' so these words came out naturally.

Bae Sejin was pleased.

"Then, did you come here because you remembered this person?"

"What? ... Yes."

He was creating a story all on his own. Thanks.

I added a little more to the story so I could dig for information.

"His name is Ryu Gunwoo, so I was wondering if he was related to Chungwoo. There aren't many people with Ryu as their surname."

"I see... Hmm. Looking at it this way, it seems that they look a little alike."

Is that so?

Anyway, Ryu Chungwoo nodded readily. My flimsy excuse about the similar surnames seemed to have worked, perhaps thanks to the resemblance.

And he spoke kindly.

"Should I ask my parents? See whether they remember him as a relative?"

"...Thank you."

To be honest, since I was such a distant relative, I had only seen them once every few years, so I don't think they'd know, but it wouldn't hurt to try.

*But what if they told me that I'd died in an accident in middle school...?*

I was a little dizzy at what misunderstanding I might be creating, but I decided to push on. There was nothing else I could do.

And the expected answer came from the parents.

It meant that they said, 'I don't remember'.

Even though it was my problem, these two guys were deep in thought.

It was because my 'amnesia' had made such a deep impression on them.

Even such a proposal came out.

"I can ask around in my clan. I think I can find him because I know his age and name."

"...! Then... Thank you."

"No need to thank me."

Ryu Chungwoo smiled and made a call, and soon after a few words of conversation, he gave me quite positive news.

"They said they'd find out quickly and tell me."

"It's a holiday, so thank you."

"Haha, I'll tell them that too."

Thus, 'tracing Ryu Gunwoo' ended quite successfully.

And the only conversation topic left was the puppy.

Bae Sejin asked quietly.

"...Is that the end?"

"Yes."

Bae Sejin must be worried that his mother was at home alone.

I didn't even ask for it, but it was just amazing that he followed me. Of course, I didn't even pay attention to whether he came or not.

"Don't worry, I'll call you when I hear something."

Ryu Chungwoo also euphemistically expressed his intention, saying, 'You can go back.'

After saying goodbye to Ryu Chungwoo's parents once more, I went back to Bae Sejin's house.

Of course, I didn't forget to say thank you.

"Thank you for the fruit."

"I-I was going to buy fruit anyway, so...!"

It sounded like he was pretty proud to say that.

Anyway, since he'd helped keep my strange behavior from seeming completely random and rude, I was thinking of making some holiday food tomorrow.

"Hoo."

So, I changed back into my pajamas and lay down on the bed.

However, I couldn't sleep well because of the sight I saw through 'Reveal the Truth'.

"......"

Fuck it, let's knock it off. Anyway, I didn't remember anything like that, so what was the point of thinking about it now? There was no answer.

I brushed off my thoughts and turned my attention to something else.

"Status window."

Well, it seemed that it was still in the middle of its 'balancing in progress'...

***Papapapang!***

"...!!"

Suddenly, confetti burst in front of my eyes.

The holographic confetti popped out of the pop-up and scattered beautifully onto the bed.

And the status window ecstatically flashed with a rainbow of light.
