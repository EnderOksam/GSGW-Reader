# Chapter 238 - Concert for a Cause, II

I was a bit taken aback by the members' reactions.

"Park Moondae, did you arrange a concert on your own because the tour was canceled? You're in the hospital. You know why it was canceled," Chungwoo said.

"You need to reconsider your work-life balance," Raebin added.

"Didn't you think to discuss it with us first?"

*They hadn't even let me explain.*

I held back a sigh, considering how things had gotten this way.

*-... Hey, Moondae, Eugene heard something from the company...*

Someone had clearly heard about the idea before I could discuss it with everyone.

*I was going to let them say their piece and then explain once they'd calmed down.*

The longer I listened to their complaints, the more I felt as though they thought I never listened to them.

*...I understood why they were upset.*

Yes, I'd admit that.

Their activities had stopped because I'd been in a coma, yet here I was, proposing a concert by myself without discussing it with them.

And beyond their frustration, they were scared for me.

"I-it won't be too late to think about it once you're completely recovered...!"

"Yes!"

"......"

They'd spent eighteen days fearing I might never wake up, so I listened to them with patience.

*About thirty minutes have passed.*

By then they were starting to repeat themselves. I sighed and finally began to explain.

"First of all, I'd planned to discuss it with everyone today."

"After you've already made all the arrangements?"

Lee Sejin, this bastard was acting like this again.

"Nothing's decided. I only mentioned the idea to the agency."

I pressed my temples.

"I wasn't proposing to perform right away. I thought it could mark our return once TeSTAR officially resumed activities."

"......"

At that, everyone fell silent to consider the possibility.

After a moment, I added,

"I understand why it looked like I was arranging a solo performance behind your backs. I'm sorry I gave you that impression."

"...!"

I was about to explain further when Chungwoo spoke.

However, Ryu Chungwoo spoke first.

"Moondae... You want to get back on stage soon."

"......"

Yes. If I didn't, I'd die...

Chungwoo nodded slowly.

"If you want to do a solo concert, that's fine. And if you don't want to sue the agency, we don't have to."

"...!"

"You have nothing to apologize for. But you do have to take care of yourself."

Bae Sejin's face turned red, but surprisingly, he silently nodded his head without objecting to Ryu Chungwoo.

"But you should take care of your health. That's what we've been talking about so far."

"......"

"You're pushing yourself too hard. Please understand why we keep saying that."

Chungwoo looked pale.

Watching that piece of metal enter my chest must have traumatized him.

*Hoo.*

*We were back at square one.* I clasped my hands together.

I was in perfect health, and a full recovery was just around the corner.

How could I convince them of this mysterious resilience...

"I'm following all the medical staff's instructions, and they keep saying I'm recovering remarkably fast."

"So you want to return as soon as you're better?"

"I'll consider it if the doctor says it's all right."

Lee Sejin laughed.

"The fans are going to love that, aren't they?"

"...!"

"You think it'll go over well, but the fans know you're human too. They might send protest trucks if the agency puts someone who was in a coma straight back to work."

"......"

"Unless this is part of some grand plan for the lawsuit. In that case, Moondae, you're a genius~"

This bastard was really...

I gritted my teeth.

"Do you really have to say it that way?"

"...!"

"Don't be sarcastic. I am speaking honestly."

*I could fucking die, and he was mocking me.*

Lee Sejin opened his mouth, then closed it without saying a word. I focused on calming down.

The other Sejin suddenly jumped in.

"H-he's just saying that because he's worried!"

Bae Sejin stepped forward, his fists clenched.

"Y-you know that's how he always talks!"

"......"

"Th-that's right... Se-Sejin wants to apologize too, r-right...?"

Ahyeon backed him up.

Lee Sejin stared at the floor without smiling.

"...Sorry."

"......"

I nodded.

*Maybe I'd been a little too sensitive.*

I was impatient because my status abnormality was still hanging over me.

*It probably wasn't his intention to get into a fight.*

*He hadn't meant to start a fight. Perhaps sarcasm was just his clumsy way of getting through to me.*

*...I can't help it.*

Yes, it wasn't just Lee Sejin but the other members as well.

After the accident, of course the others couldn't help worrying about my health.

We'd shared our lives for two years, after all.

*We simply didn't have the same information.*

*They don't know I'll die unless I reach an audience of 400,000.*

Our priorities weren't the same.

*...I didn't mean to go this far.*

I finally opened my mouth.

"...The truth is, I missed the fans."

"......"

"When I woke up, it was already a new month. With the internet going wild, I thought doing something might reassure everyone. That was why I mentioned a concert."

"...Okay."

"M-Moondae..."

It was a modest explanation, but the mood softened.

*...It's not wrong.*

Apart from the status abnormality driving my urgency, I genuinely felt that way.

I continued talking with a feeling of giving up a little.

"I won't stay up all night working like before."

*I couldn't, without Bacchus.*

"Still, I want to resume activities as soon as possible without overdoing it... That's my opinion."

This time, "No" didn't come out.

I hoped this was it.

"I wasn't planning anything grand."

"What?"

"Just a simple talk concert to show I'd recovered. Barely any dancing."

"But why a solo concert...?"

"Because of our W App contract. We can't hold a free concert under the group's name."

"Oh..."

They finally seemed to be starting to understand.

I explained the arrangement I'd discussed with Cheongryeo.

Then I took a break and spoke.

"The solo part is really just a technicality."

"...?"

I chuckled.

"I'd invite all the TeSTAR members as guests. Wouldn't that work?"

"...!!"

Of course, it was an obvious workaround.

It would look odd if anyone declined, but I was confident I could persuade them.

One member flushed red and interrupted.

"I-isn't that a breach of contract?"

"No. I'd technically be the host."

"B-but that's basically a lie!"

"It's a clever use of the contract's wording."

"......"

Bae Sejin sank into a chair.

He muttered that the other party might curse us for it, but he didn't object.

*I must have looked quite desperate.*

Since I had been bombarded with words already, he seemed to be trying to restrain himself.

Or maybe Bae Sejin was trying to organize things in his own way so that the language was less aggressive.

Of course, that statement itself made sense.

*Word of this could spread throughout the industry.*

W App belonged to a company behind a major search engine, so a backlash was possible.

*We have to get the presentation right.*

I'd already thought about how to frame the performance.

It would be difficult for them to attack an event that barely looked like a concert at all.

I spoke with a smile.

"One more thing."

"...?"

"I have a separate goal for this event."

"A purpose?"

I explained my idea.

"Oh."

"M-Moondae, you always have such good ideas..."

"He does!"

The response was encouraging.

Bae Sejin, who'd slumped in his chair, seemed to regain some color.

"...That's fine!"

"Yes."

*That was half the job done.*

If we kept negotiating this way, we could finalize matters by the time I was discharged.

I shrugged.

"N-now, Moondae, get some rest...!"

"No, I've already rested plenty..."

"R-rest!"

"......"

I gave up arguing, and our conversation returned to ordinary hospital-room chatter.

"The A&R Team made some relaxing music to help you sleep... but a program error deleted it before they could save it," Raebin said.

"What?!"

"Thank them anyway."

*I didn't think I'd ever need or want it.*

Time passed, and soon it was time for the hospital meal.

"You should go and eat something."

"Oh... Is it dinner already?"

"I ate early, so I'll stay and keep you company!"

"Me too! Eat fruits!"

No, they didn't have to... Well, it was okay.

I shrugged.

But Lee Sejin, who'd been about to leave, hesitated without meeting my eyes.

He'd been acting like that since the argument.

"...See you later~"

"Right."

His voice still sounded cheerful.

*We'd be all right.*

And the next guy.

"Hyung! Eat well..."

"Get over here."

"Ow!"

Eugene, who'd eaten all those tangerines and blurted out my plans, tried to slip away.

I flicked Eugene on the forehead and let him go.

"Ahhhh!"

I handed Raebin a large peach right in front of Eugene.

Funny, since Raebin had brought the peaches for me, but I'd eaten some as well.

"This tastes great!"

"I know."

Cha Eugene slumped.

"Hyung, you're too much!"

"What thing?"

"I didn't mention that other thing!"

"What?"

Eugene silently mouthed the words.

*VTIC senior!*

"...!!"

After a brief pause...

"Eat."

"Wow!"

I gave him a piece of peach.

It was a bribe to keep his mouth shut.
