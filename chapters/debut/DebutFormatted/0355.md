Seon Ahyeon took a deep breath.

His roommate, Kim Raebin, told him to rest easy, packed up his electronic devices, and went to Bae Sejin and Cha Eugene's room.

Ahyeon couldn't get his thoughts to slow down. His mind kept repeating the same resolve.

*This time, I need to react properly.*

*Yes.* Maybe this was an opportunity. A chance to alleviate the shock Moondae had received because of how badly he'd reacted last time.

"Whew."

*First of all, I'll thank him for speaking to me so seriously...*

After going over how he'd react several times in his head, Seon Ahyeon planned to visit Park Moondae's room at the agreed-upon time.

He didn't get the chance.

*Knock, knock.*

"I'm coming in."

"...!"

Moondae came to see him before that time even arrived.

And he went straight to the point.

"I've come early because I need to set something up. Let me use Raebin's computer for a moment."

*His computer?*

"Let's begin. Ah, Kim Raebin asked me to deliver this."

"Oh. Okay."

"Listen while drinking."

After coming through the door, Moondae held out the tea he'd been carrying, then placed a notebook and pen in front of him.

*Notebook...?*

What followed was a flood of new information.

"Let me start with the witnesses."

"W-witnesses...?"

Moondae paused for a moment, then spoke in a firmer tone than usual.

"...I didn't say anything before, fearing that you would be more confused, but in fact, I'm not the only person who has returned to the past."

"...??"

The statement caught Ahyeon completely by surprise.

Ahyeon froze with the tea in his hand.

However, Park Moondae immediately began to bring out the people he'd gotten involved.

*I need to push my advantage here.*

His chosen method was video calls.

"I needed to use an application that wouldn't allow voice or video recording. And it'll probably be easier for you to accept if you get to see their faces directly."

Moondae installed an application from the app store as Ahyeon watched. Not long after that, someone's face appeared on the screen of Raebin's computer.

"...?!"

Ahyeon almost shot to his feet. He could never have imagined it.

*– Hmm.*

VTIC's Cheongryeo. The great sunbaenim he'd just met wore an openly carefree smile.

*– Couldn't we have done this earlier, when we met? It would have been much easier.*

"You're right. Things have changed."

*– Well, that was quick.*

Cheongryeo exchanged a few words with Park Moondae. It was unclear whether he was amused or being sarcastic, but he soon admitted it.

*– Okay. I was the person who traveled to the past before Moondae. Moondae—that is, Gunwoo—came after me.*

"......"

Ahyeon stared.

Was this really the truth?

*– He was technically born before me, and he doesn't use formal speech with me. Perhaps you didn't notice earlier?*

Ahyeon had assumed it was because they were close, and he had envied that relationship a little.

*– It's hilarious that he's sticking to formal speech now, even though he said he wasn't going to record this.*

"Shut up," Park Moondae said.

*– Haha!*

Cheongryeo finished delivering his testimony with a nonchalant look on his face.

*– It looks like you have more to repay me now, Moondae.*

"Take care. You can leave now."

*– Oh, my. You aren't denying it.*

Cheongryeo vanished from the screen.

"Hoo."

Park Moondae took a deep breath as if holding back his irritation.

"He's annoying, but anyway... For that reason, I meet him from time to time."

"N-no, that's not necessary."

Seon Ahyeon responded impulsively.

His head felt like it was full of exclamation marks and question marks.

"Moving on, then. The next person is the one who came back to the past after me."

And when another familiar face appeared on the screen, he had to repress a scream.

Heeseung bitterly explained that he'd tried telling his parents the truth, only for them to slap him on the back and tell him to come to his senses. After a little more chatter, he ended the call with a sigh.

"So I arranged for you to meet yet another person who is closely involved."

*There was another person?*

And he asked himself.

Could someone really justify his own delusions with the help of so many people?

*It was impossible...*

*Even if I believe him... Moondae said he'd see a therapist no matter what.*

*It doesn't matter what I think.*

The predetermined conclusion had served its intended purpose.

Besides, all of this was something... Something Seon Ahyeon never could have predicted or prepared for.
