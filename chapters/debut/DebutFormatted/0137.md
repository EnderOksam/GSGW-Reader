# Chapter 137 - ⟨Copy Cat⟩, II

VTIC had stolen the screen time we would have gotten from the penalty.

“Then, the two of you, please come up to the stage!”

“Oh, too bad!”

“It’s a bit unfair~!”

The VTIC member who had stepped on the line to disqualify himself instead of hitting me took the water balloon penalty alongside his fellow member, both giving unremarkable reactions.

If they hadn’t been in a group that sold 1.8 million albums in its first week, their unremarkable reactions would have earned them only three seconds on screen. As Keun Sejin said, they really didn’t seem to have much passion for variety shows.

*...It meant they didn’t get eliminated on purpose to hog the screen time.*

Then, does that proud face really come from a ‘caring heart for new idols’?

*Isn’t it more likely he stepped on the line by mistake and was just embarrassed?*

My eyes met Keun Sejin’s. Smiling like a painting, he discreetly pointed a finger at himself.

Frustrating as it was, it seemed to mean that he would actively try to get a penalty next time.

“Now, for this team match~ Avoid the special ssanghwacha[1]{T/N: Ssanghwacha is a traditional herbal medicine that has been widely used for thousands of years, especially during the Joseon Dynasty, to treat infirmity, relieve fatigue, and facilitate recovery after an illness.}!”

However, in the next team match, this strange and ridiculous situation continued.

“Don’t pick the cup without an egg yolk! The first person to pick that cup of ssanghwacha makes their whole team lose~! Now, let’s dance to the music and choose!”

In short, it was a game with an ambiguous 90s vibe that combined musical chairs and roulette.

*It’s all about luck, isn’t it?*

It didn’t seem worth trying to lose this one on purpose, so I chose without thinking much about it. With the way things were going, it would be funnier if I got caught.

Of course, it was much more likely that I got the one with the egg yolk.

“Ah, Moondae-ssi! Pass!”

And it came true. The ssanghwacha I chose had yolk in it.

Then the VTIC guys clapped and started to make a fuss.

“Oh, that’s amazing!”

“Wow, how did you get it right so easily?”

I stared at them.

What’s wrong with you, bastards?

I gave them a polite answer instead of ignoring them, but their awkwardly warm reactions weren’t making things any funnier.

In the end, the MC, who couldn’t watch it, tried to make it funny by pointing it out.

“No, why have the two of you been giving such big reactions only to Moondae this whole time?!”

“Ah, hyung-nim, don’t you see~? The popular ones are close to each other~!”

“Is that so~?”

The VTIC guys waved their hands.

“No, no~! He’s just good at this sort of thing!”

“It was cool that he got it right without thinking much about it.”

Rather than playing along, they just denied it, so things became awkward again. I guessed it was true that those two were bad at variety shows.

But why are you trolling me with that?

*Are you trying to screw with me?*

I took a look at them just in case, but I didn’t feel any signs of provocation.

*It’s driving me crazy.*

And this time, the punishment was taken by the Marshmallow member.

“Look closely! It’s not an egg yolk... It’s a canned peach!”

“Argh!”

The penalty was gosamcha[2]{T/N: Gosamcha is a type of Korean tea that apparently tastes very bitter.}. Youngrin, who carried out the punishment together as the doppelganger, did a one-shot without changing her expression, eliciting reactions even from the staff.

*I’m jealous.*

It would have been nice to drink the gosamcha, even if I ended up spitting it out.

At this rate, we wouldn’t get a penalty or a memorable moment and would end up merely reacting in the background and providing side footage for VTIC.

*Should we try another way to get attention?*

While I was thinking about changing the route, the filming stopped for a while.

“Cut! We’ll continue after a short break~!”

“Let’s check our microphones for a moment.”

“Yes!”

It was time for a short maintenance break.

“I’m going to the bathroom.”

“Yeah.”

Keun Sejin followed the MC out, and I deliberately sipped my drink as I waited for the right moment.

Then the opponents moved first.

“Hello, Hubae-nim~!”

“We were in such a hurry that we couldn’t even say hello properly, right?”

The VTIC guys smiled and talked to me.

Although we’d never spoken before, I couldn’t ignore them; that wouldn’t be polite.

“...Yes, it’s been a while, sunbae-nims.”

“Ah~ Sunbae-nim sounds so awkward. Just call us hyung!”

“That’s right. Don’t call us that, it’s like we’re too old.”

Rather than dwelling on how shameless that sounded from idols eight years into their careers, I was struck again by something else.

*Hmm?*

You guys... Why are you guys so awkward?

*They look like they’re breaking into cold sweats.*

It seemed they tried to talk to me warmly and kindly in their own way, but there were limits.

They didn’t have the practiced ease I was used to seeing from charismatic guys like Keun Sejin or Ryu Chungwoo.

They didn’t seem used to being sociable seniors in the industry.

*What’s with them?*

Let’s give them a proper answer first.

“It’s not a matter of age. You’re great seniors, so how could I address you so casually...”

“Great seniors? Oh, come on~!”

“Just be casual with us!”

“...”

Wow, these guys are working hard.

This was more confusing than when the penalty was stolen earlier.

I was speechless. Perhaps thinking it was an implicit agreement, the two men grinned and exchanged glances.

Then, one of them opened his mouth with a dry cough.

“Hmm, variety shows are very difficult, right?”

“Yes? Yes...”

Without the two of you, the level of difficulty would have dropped by two levels.

But both of them had proud faces.

“Oh, don’t overdo it while filming... If you’re having a hard time, send us an SOS. We’ll help you.”

“...??”

“Ah, don’t feel pressured! If you get a junior later, treat them well. Don’t mind us.”

And these two guys were exchanging looks as if to say, ‘Oh, you’re quite the smooth talker!’

It was amazing.

“...”

I put up with the headache and asked openly.

“...Then, when you stepped on the line earlier...”

“Haha! No~ Was it that obvious?”

“No, I think this Hubae just has a good sense.”

“Right? Anyway... Don’t worry too much. It’s all in a day’s work~!”

...Now, I knew for sure.

*These guys, they did it completely out of goodwill.*

They said the road to hell is paved with good intentions, and it was exactly what happened.

In addition, their idea of helping was one-dimensional.

Even considering that they didn’t have much experience with variety shows, they didn’t seem to know how to use their brains.

*I think they’ll fall for a scam within five years.*

No matter how smoothly VTIC grew, considering that they had been in this hellish K-POP society for eight years, they were pretty dull.

*...Are they the replacements Cheongryeo brought into the group?*

If he looked for people who wouldn’t cause trouble and were easy to deal with, it could be these kinds of people.

Anyway, they didn’t seem to have any complicated agenda, so I could gently turn down their help.

“Thank you very much. But I’m going to try on my own. You don’t have to be considerate like you were before.”

“Huh?”

The slightly flustered guys hesitated, then one of them opened his mouth again, looking embarrassed.

“Um, actually, I heard something from Cheongryeo hyung.”

“...??”

What is this again?

*He didn’t say anything weird, did he?*

Just as my blood started to run cold, the guy in front of me smiled.

“No, Moondae-ssi is really hardworking and a good junior, so please treat him well, he said something like that!”

“...!!”

“That’s right! But he’s the kind of person who doesn’t really say things like that.”

“Yeah. He doesn’t seem to have many friends... It was amazing that he got close to Hubae-nim.”

We’re not close.

“He may seem a little cold, but... Still, Jaehyeon, I mean, Cheongryeo hyung is a lot nicer than people think.”

“That’s right. He donates half his pay to animal rescue centers.”

“Heol! He donates half of it?”

“Yeah, I was completely freaked out when I saw hyung using his banking app.”

“He lives like a mountain spirit, so I guess it’s no surprise he likes animals.”

The two of them talked and then got lost in a conversation among themselves. When they came to their senses, they awkwardly returned to our conversation.

“No, why did the topic go this way? Anyway, since we’ve crossed paths, tell us if you have any difficulties during today’s filming~ You know what I mean?”

“Are you having a hard time now?”

“...”

I heard so much nonsense that I was struggling to process it all.

I mentally punched myself in the head.

*Who the fuck cares? Get a grip.*

Whether Cheongryeo was the guardian of rescue animals or not, it was his business.

What mattered now was my goal. The current shooting.

I chose my words carefully and opened my mouth.

“Actually...”

After explaining the situation I was in, the two were horrified when I indirectly informed them that I was actually aiming for a penalty.

“Heol! Really?”

“Hey, I’m sorry!”

“Should we send all the penalties your way from now on?”

“Shall we get the penalty train rolling?”

“...It’s fine.”

Instead, I made a light request, and the VTIC members readily agreed.

“Hey, Moondae~!”

Right after that, Keun Sejin returned from the bathroom.

“Did you have a good conversation with them?”

“So so. You?”

“Hey, I went to the bathroom, so who could I have been talking to~?”

Seeing him smile while answering, it seemed he had achieved something, but his mouth said something else.

Sure enough, the loot soon popped out.

“Just... Somehow our paths happened to cross on the way out, so I had a conversation with MC Choi Hosu. And we exchanged numbers.”

As expected.

“Looks like you had a fruitful conversation.”

“Well, we talked about our goals for today’s show.”

Keun Sejin grinned.

“I said the two of us would never get a penalty.”

“...!”

“So now he’ll try to get reactions by making sure we get a penalty somehow.”

It was great.

* * *

The second half of the filming ended well with the cooperation of several cast members.

*Looks like it came out better than planned.*

Now it was a matter of editing, but honestly, I wasn’t too worried.

I wouldn’t cut this out if I were the PD.

“Thank you for your hard work~!”

“Good job!”

“Yeah, it was great today!”

Anyway, we left the set after bowing our heads just like when we first arrived.

And in the car on my way to the next schedule.

After the filming ended well, an unwelcome message suddenly arrived in the quiet and peaceful atmosphere.

★:
-VTIC Cheongryeo sunbae-nim: I heard you met the kids at the filming today.

-How was it? They were probably helpful, right? \^\^
:★

“*...Sigh.*”

Since the moment I met the VTIC guys, I’d suspected Cheongryeo might be behind their interference.

It was ridiculous, but if you looked at it like this, the content of the message itself was somehow normal.

*The problem with meeting him is that he’s crazy.*

After pressing my eyelids a few times, I replied.

★:
Yes, the sunbae-nims helped me. Thank you.-
:★

And surprisingly, it took a while for him to reply.

★:
-I’m glad they helped. Don’t you want to start over too, this time with better team members?
:★

*Fuck.*

Whatever I do, are you going to tell me to die so you can start all over again?

VTIC guys’ nonsense echoed in my mind.

*—Jaehyeon, I mean, Cheongryeo hyung is a lot nicer than people think. ...A lot nicer... A lot nicer...*

Like hell he was.

Calling him decent would leave all the truly kind people out in the cold.

But the VTIC guys didn’t seem to lie about it. In other words, the example itself... seemed to be the truth.

*...Just try it once.*

With superhuman patience, I tried to reply by assuming that the other person was normal for once.

★:
I don’t want to. And just hearing that kind of thing over and over again is very stressful. I’m at my limit just thinking about the mission all the time.-

You’re making things harder for me, so please stop.-
:★

And there was no reply for a long time.

*...Did I step on a landmine?*

However, even if the countdown to my burial in some remote mountain had begun, I would never die alone.

*I need to bury this bastard’s career first.*

Leaning on my neck pillow, I began to polish the plan I had thought of before.

Somehow... It was like a repetitive labor to calm the mind.

“Hmm.”

Then, again, a reply came back all of a sudden.

★:
-Got it.
:★

“...!”

Almost thirty seconds passed.

After that, the next text arrived.

★:
-I’m sorry.
:★

“...”

The text ended with that.

“...Phew.”

I locked my phone screen and closed my eyes again.

People were so mysterious.
