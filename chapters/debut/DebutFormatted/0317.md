# Chapter 317 - Conversation, V

Four days after the accident, the media would have started reporting that I was showing no signs of waking up.

I opened my mouth right away.

"It's okay. I'll recover completely."

[B-but the news... They said you were going to die!]

"I won't die."

I didn't know which lunatic had described my coma like that, but I'd like to sue them.

[Hyung, you're not really dead, are you...? Is that why you're here helping me?]

"I told you, I'm not. I was up on stage receiving the grand prize. What are you talking about?"

*He was the one who disappeared, not me.*

Hearing that from a guy whose fate I didn't even know once this supposed reward was over... It felt strange.

I held back a sigh and spoke.

"I'm sorry. I should've told you beforehand, but I didn't think of it. I woke up fine and got back to work without any problems."

[...]

I'd managed to smash through every obstacle that came my way, so I didn't think there was anything in particular I needed to warn him about. Things would work out even if he didn't do anything.

But for someone who didn't know how it would all turn out, it was certainly worth worrying about.

Especially... if he'd lost someone close to him in an accident.

*I was careless.*

I sat on the edge of the bed and spoke calmly.

"You wanted to check that I'd show up after you got the new status abnormality, right?"

It was still June. He was supposed to stall a bit longer before getting the new status abnormality, but he'd triggered it early to make sure I'd still return.

*Honestly, it's impressive he held out for four days at all.*

The news kept saying I was going to die, so I could imagine how anxious he must have been.

The voice seemed to calm down, then grew upset again, trembling.

[S-sorry, sorry. I was supposed to press it in September, but I...]

"No, you did the right thing."

Even from a purely practical standpoint, it was worth checking. As far as he knew, his guide was on the verge of disappearing.

*It's much better than taking a test in this state and then failing.*

I sighed in relief and got up.

"Don't worry about it. You handled it well. The exam's still a month away, so I can point out the right questions for you one more time. This actually works in your favor."

[Yes...]

The voice grew softer, as if in great relief. I was right—he'd been panicking.

"Hey, you must've had a hard time these past four days."

I could hear the voice sniffling.

I checked my body just in case, and realized my stomach was sore.

"Don't tell me you haven't been eating?"

[...I did eat a little, but people kept talking about hyung's accident while I was trying to eat...]

Even the people at his boarding academy who weren't interested in idols talked about them when they had accidents. People have always been drawn to other people's tragedies.

It must've been painful to hear that someone he knew was dying while he was trying to eat. I clicked my tongue inwardly.

"Even so, make sure you eat proper meals no matter what."

[Yes...] he agreed slowly.

The voice faded. It seemed the tension draining out of him had left him unable to hold a proper conversation, so I'd figure out the rest of the situation myself.

*First... Today is Monday.*

With this year's exam just around the corner, I'd expected him to be at the boarding academy from the weekend onwards, but I was at the officetel.

*He must've excused himself to come back here in time for my return.*

Judging from the schedule on his smartphone screen, he'd been attending the boarding academy diligently.

Next, I got on the internet.

★$
[TeSTAR's Park Moondae unconscious for four days... Agency claims “he is recovering,” but what does that really mean?]
$★

It was an article in the general news section that was getting torn apart for its provocative title, with comments like 'Why is the title like this?'. I read through the comments.

After four days, people were beginning to realize that it had become 'really serious'. A lot of the trolls had gone quiet, and most of the comments were from worried fans.

Worry, panic, despair, anxiety...

"......"

Back when I was in a coma, I'd only seen what had happened after I woke up. It was my first time seeing all that emotion so raw, and it wasn't a particularly good feeling. I was going to be unconscious for another 18 days, after all.

But on one hand... I was a little happy, silly as that was.

*...Enough useless thoughts.*

I sighed and scrolled down through the news. Everyone else must have come to by now and been receiving treatment in the hospital.

*I heard that most of them ended up with minor injuries, so there were no major problems...*

When I'd actually woken up, only Cha Eugene was still recovering from a major injury. The rest were, at most, wearing guards.

*Hmm, then it would make sense for the company to plan to cut me loose and come up with a new plan.*

Looking at it now, I was a little puzzled.

Once I'd been unconscious for two weeks, the company was sure to have taken drastic action.

Even if the agency, as a subsidiary largely responsible for the manager problem, held back, T1, the parent company, wouldn't have shown such restraint.

*...Come to think of it.*

Suddenly, I remembered what Seon Ahyeon had said before.

*– A-after the accident, Sejin really tried his best...*

*– Speaking to the company... He kept coming to the h-hospital to ask about your surgery... H-he did! He couldn't even eat properly because he was so worried...*

"......"

After thinking about it for a while, I created a new anonymous email address and started composing an email to the agency.

However, I didn't use the main contact address.

I entered the email address of the head of the Management Office directly.

And I titled it.

★$
--#Subject:#- A plan to force TeSTAR to start fourth quarter promotions about to be announced
$★

I wrote it like an anonymous tip-off about the public backlash there'd be if the group was forced to promote with only six members.

*This is bound to work since he'll be busy fighting the director at this point.*

Even if the director discussed this plan with the parent company, the head of the Management Office could respond immediately, since I'd already made him aware of its weaknesses. Then it wouldn't proceed properly with the two of them at each other's throats...

*...The members will have less to worry about.*

Keun Sejin.

At this point, he was the only one in the group who could properly communicate with the company. Just imagine the company coming to him with such a shitty proposal.

Who knew what all that stress would do to this guy?

*Still, he seemed to have kept it together pretty well until I woke up.*

Well, it would be nice if this little bit of effort had an impact. I clicked the send mail button.

I felt a little lighter.

Only then did the voice speak up again faintly.

[Hey hyung, may I ask where you are sending it...?]

"Ah, the company..."

*Wait a minute.*

Talking to this guy reminded me of something else.

*– Actually, the article was about to go out last year, but the company blocked it.*

*This guy's parents' plane crash.*

I'd assumed the company had blocked the article around the time I was discharged from the hospital, but I wasn't so sure those bastards had been capable of that much foresight amid all this confusion.

"...Park Moondae."

[Yes!]

"Just one thing... I want to ask for your understanding."

I explained the situation to this guy.

The media might latch onto his family situation soon.

It was probably annoying enough for him that it was already public knowledge, so I couldn't let that happen.

I planned to alert the agency in advance by sending an email posing as a reporter who wanted an exclusive.

...It wasn't a very desirable method, but given the limitations of time and space, it was the most effective one.

"I have to write an email about it, so I'm sorry if it makes you uncomfortable."

[No... Uh, I'm really fine with it.]

"...? Is that so?"

[Yes.]

The voice echoed calmly.

[Actually, it doesn't really feel like me anymore.... The Moondae in TeSTAR looks so different too... I don't know how you'll take this, but it's like watching you, hyung, on TV instead of me.]

"...Okay, I got it."

[Maybe it's just me, but I think it makes me feel closer to you...]

"Thank you for that."

The voice echoed as if he were laughing in spite of himself. It was a good thing he felt that way, since it meant I could do as I pleased... But for some reason, I wasn't entirely comfortable.

*Did I feel guilty?*

I'd think about it later. I frowned, created another anonymous email address, and finished what I'd set out to do.

I spent the rest of the day giving him spoilers about the activities I'd be doing after I woke up, and telling him when I'd wake up. It seemed to help, since he calmed down quite a bit.

"I'll wake up on July 6th, so don't watch the news until then."

[Yes!]

"Enjoy the charity concert after the second round's over."

[Yes...]

And I made sure to emphasize one more thing.

If he passed the second round, only the third stage—the interview—would remain. The next status abnormality was sure to revolve around the third round.

So he needed to prepare for it.

"First, go through the interview, and only trigger the next status abnormality when you're certain you've qualified."

If he received the status abnormality too early, he'd only have one chance to clear it.

He'd never done anything like an interview in his life, so there was a chance he'd fail.

[I will definitely do that!]

However, I did think this guy might pass on his first attempt.

And when I woke up in the morning again...

[...Hyung.]

"Yes."

I realized right away that it was only spring of the following year.

This guy had failed his interview.

***

'Park Moondae' had failed the third round.

Of course, it wasn't because he'd done particularly badly in the interview itself.

[Sorry, my scores put me outside the number of openings, and I was so nervous during the interview...]

The cutoff for the 1.5× selection fluctuated, and his score hadn't been high enough to make the shortlist.

"Good job. If you passed the second round, you're almost there. Just one more year of hard work and you'll get it."

All he needed to do was pass this year. But the voice trembled uneasily.

[I... What if I can't make it this year?]

After failing once, he seemed scared.

But he didn't have to be.

"No, you can do it. I'm not trying to encourage you. I'm serious."

In my case, I'd been stumped by the questions and failed because I'd uselessly tried to talk my way around the interviewer. This guy just needed to raise his scores a little.

And he'd be able to do it.

"You raised your scores enough to nearly get accepted in two years, which is honestly ridiculous."

[Ughhhhh.]

"And with this grade of exam, if you work hard for the first three years, your scores will inevitably rise to some extent."

I spoke without hesitation.

"So, if you're already this close to qualifying, a few more months of hard work should be enough to get you accepted."

[...Yes!]

Strength returned to his voice as he responded.

*Good.*

His motivation was back. I nodded and started my day.

Now that he'd have to study like his life depended on it for his last chance at the exam, I spent this time helping him get as much rest as possible.

I let him do what he wanted and answered his questions.

For example...

[Hyung, by any chance, the next comeback...]

"July 12th. Don't look it up anymore. Just listen to the song when it comes out that day."

[Yes!]

As I answered, I realized that the song for the July 12th comeback was our latest one, 'Promise'.

In other words, I'd already caught up to the year I'd left.

*I've caught up pretty quickly.*

And I still hadn't figured out anything in particular about the system.

*This guy himself doesn't seem to know anything about it.*

I crossed my arms.

Still, I'd had plenty of conversations with Park Moondae. I think I'd gotten a little closer to this guy than I'd expected.

Besides, it seemed like 'Park Moondae' would clear his status abnormalities more successfully than I'd initially thought.

*It's strangely rewarding.*

Well, I was seeing some gains too.

[Hyung, what is that?]

"I have something to deal with."

I'd remembered a 'well-timed' exposure post I could write to take down the guy who was annoying me the most at this point.

Chae Seodam's secret dating account.

Of course, I didn't do much. I was supposed to be studying for the civil service exam, and I couldn't risk getting Park Moondae in my body sued.

I just... posted this on an anonymous site.

★$
[Chae Seodam's antifans seem to be working on generating a new rumor]

The username they chose is very similar to one of Chae Seodam's old accounts, but the account was created recently.

I wonder how much they must hate him to go this far
$★

I'd deliberately described Chae Seodam's private dating account as an account created by a hater.

That would draw in people who wanted to claim it was real... And as I'd seen before, there might even be bastards among them who could hack the account.

It was only a possibility. Well... I'd already seen the outcome, though.

*If it really wasn't his, he could've used this as a card to silence his antifans.*

Unfortunately, it really was Chae Seodam's account, and he'd paid for his carelessness.

I watched the comments pour in, then deleted the post after five minutes.

*Neat and clean.*

[Hyung... Did you just help that person because he was being falsely accused?]

"No. The opposite."

[...??]

I didn't bother explaining any further to the confused voice. *You just focus on studying.*

After that, I spent time just resting as planned, and the day passed in an instant.

The voice spoke gloomily.

[It's already 11 o'clock...]

"Yes."

In other words, it was time for the decisive battle.

If my hypothesis and calculations were correct, he'd soon have to deal with his fifth and last status abnormality.

I hadn't explained much in detail, but this guy seemed to believe me when I told him that passing the exam would probably be the end of the status abnormalities.

But he was curious about what would happen after that.

Just before the day ended, he asked me.

[Hyung, when this is all over... What will happen?]

It was something I'd been thinking about too, but for now, I answered casually. There was no need to make him anxious.

"For me, nothing special happened. The status abnormalities disappeared, and that was it."

[Is that so?]

"Yes. And well... Even if your case is different, you won't suddenly be threatened with death again."

The resonance trembled like laughter.

[I guess so!]

*Yes.*

And even if I went back to Park Moondae's body at the time I was about to receive the grand prize, I'd prepared an alternative.

"Well then, do your best. See you in half a year this time."

[Yes!!]

"If I don't come back, use the number I gave you and call me in December."

The resonance wavered with tension and anticipation. It seemed like an affirmation.

*We can control it either way.*

*As long as this guy passes.*

So, I fell asleep considering the possible outcomes.

***

When I opened my eyes again, I realized that I was lying on the bed in the officetel this time too.

"......"

I was in Ryu Gunwoo's body again. Nothing in particular seemed to have changed.

And judging by the season outside the window... It was late autumn.

The time when the Grade Seven Civil Servant Exam results would be announced. I got it right away.

*He passed.*

That was the only reason I could've come back at this time.

I smiled and sat up. And I called out.

"Park Moondae."

*Well done.*

But no answer came back.

"...Ryu Gunwoo?"

There was no voice to be heard.

No trace of the strange vibrations that had resonated somewhere inside my head.

"......"

*What... What was going on?*

I waited a little longer just in case this bastard was playing a prank now that he'd passed the exam.

But five minutes went by, and there was nothing but silence.

I was the only one in the officetel.

I swallowed nervously.

"...Status window."

★-
\
#><[Currently receiving reward]><#
-★

It meant that it wasn't over yet. I was still in the middle of receiving my reward, 'A conversation with Park Moondae'.

Therefore... This wasn't the worst-case scenario I'd been considering.

*But why did my conversation partner disappear?*

*What in the world was going on?*

I held back the urge to swear and got out of bed immediately. First, I'd gather clues from my surroundings and try to figure things out.

It was then.

*Jiying.*

The smartphone by my pillow was vibrating with an incoming call.

★:
#><#*Unknown Caller*#><#
📞 Incoming call...
:★

*An unsaved number.*

I picked up right away, desperate to get any information I could.

Then I heard a voice over the phone.

It was a voice I recognized immediately.

– I found you.

...Cheongryeo.
