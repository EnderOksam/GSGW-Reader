# The OST Counterattack

Everyone agreed that since it was an OST this time, we needed a different approach than the album songs we had been making before.

"It's not a collaboration like a game, so we just have to make it fit for the movie."

"At the same time, it's important to show TeSTAR's color, right~"

The focus had to be on the movie, while retaining enough of TeSTAR's color for the song to be recognizable as ours.

There was just a problem.

"Hmm... I-it would be better if we could incorporate a main theme like in the game."

"...They're not giving us anything else, are they?"

The film company hadn't given us much to work with.

Citing security concerns, they had provided only a few keywords and a summary of the scenario, both poorer than expected.

Let's start with keywords.

**[Space, Cyberpunk, Superhuman, Battle, Focus]**

The plot was rather bland, typical of a sci-fi superhero movie. There was almost no explanation of its universe.

*It's too much.*

I frowned. They could have at least made us sign a nondisclosure agreement. They probably gave even the actors auditioning for roles more information than they'd given us.

"First of all, since this is a movie OST, I will try composing with the fact that it will serve as background music in mind..."

So, even if Kim Raebin spoke with grim resolve and started working, the rest of the members had little to do.

*We don't know how movie soundtracks are supposed to be made, so what can we do when we're starting completely from scratch?*

There were four days left, but it would be hard for any of us to learn what we needed in that time.

*At most, we'll have to fiddle with the melody...*

There was nothing to brainstorm about. As it stood, the rest of us were surplus manpower who could only give feedback on what Kim Raebin created.

*We started this camp to avoid this exact situation.*

Being forced to follow our old way of doing things left a bit of a bitter taste in my mouth. I stretched my neck and got up from my seat.

"Where is Moondae-hyung going?"

"Getting some water."

"I'm coming too!"

Cha Eugene followed. Somehow, he seemed to follow me whenever I went to the kitchen... I didn't think it was an illusion.

"Can I eat this?"

"Okay."

*We'd be splitting the cost anyway, so he'd be paying for half of what he ate.* I didn't bother to tell him that. This guy had enough money.

Cha Eugene, who was finding snacks in the hotel refrigerator and popping them into his mouth, suddenly spoke.

[You look worried.]

"...What."

"It's tasty! Take it!"

*I didn't need it.*

I took some of the nuts he offered me as he chewed and muttered under his breath.

"Do you have any worries?"

*Well, wasn't it obvious?*

"It's because coming up with an OST in four days is harder than I thought. The company hasn't given us much to work with."

He wasn't one to be swayed by realistic words, so it was okay to say this much.

As expected, Cha Eugene looked fine, but shrugged.

And he made a bombshell statement.

"That company is doing it on purpose!"

"...!"

Cha Eugene crumpled the snack bag and threw it into the trash.

*Bang.*

"On purpose?"

[Yes. This is perfectly clear. Um, this company doesn't seem to want to give us the job, right?]

"......"

*Keep talking.*

I leaned against the wall. Cha Eugene also leaned against the wall.

[They're upset because we suddenly stepped in. Well, maybe they're a little bit prejudiced.]

*Ah.*

*Is this discrimination? Maybe racial?*

He'd lived in America, so he probably knew a lot more about it than I did.

"Are you talking about it from experience?"

"It's not like I haven't! But I don't care."

Now he was using double negatives.[1]{Editor's Note: In Cha Eugene's phrase above, "It's not like I haven't!" he uses the words 없지 않아요 (eobsji anayo) in Korean, which are two words that are grammatical negations. This is where the term "double negatives" comes from, and having double negatives oftentimes makes a phrase turn out to be positive or have a different meaning.}

His Korean was getting better. Language skills aside, judging from how dismissive he was being, it seemed nothing serious had happened to him in that regard.

"So I know this too. If we do well, they can't complain."

*Oh.*

"And we don't even have to do it. We're cool even without an OST!"

"You wanted to go to America."

[Well, there are always other options!]

He hadn't changed a bit. He was saying that there was nothing to lose if we simply declined.

*It's been a long time since this guy showed how smart he is.*

It was kind of interesting. Shall I encourage him?

"Then what if we don't want to quit, but we want to get back at them?"

[Then we should make ourselves heard and demand a 100% proper request. Stars are like that.] "And I want to do it! Let's send an e-mail through our company."

*Hmm.*

It was definitely a direct way of expressing ourselves, but... It was true that we'd used T1 to squeeze our way in, so there was no need to give the other party room to argue.

I grinned.

"What if there is a quieter and even more satisfying way?"

"...! Let me know!"

*Okay.*

"Hmm... If we look at the fact that these guys are coming out unfairly, pretending to give us work..."

It was a rather good situation.

I smiled and opened the plastic bottle.

"Notice that they're not being obvious about it. That's something to note."

"Is that important?"

"Yes."

I drank water.

"It's just that they can't refuse us outright."

That was why they were making unreasonable demands without making it obvious.

"Oh..."

Cha Eugene nodded, unimpressed.

"But why is that a good thing?"

"It means our company has some clout in this situation."

"Is our company stronger than Limestone?"

*Not even T1 is stronger than that crazy IP-holding conglomerate...*

"It's not like that. It's because of their close relationship. T1 Entertainment distributes Limestone movies in Korea."

"Oh."

There was definitely a reason the film studio couldn't turn us down. I didn't know what it was, but T1 had offered them a deal they couldn't refuse.

Perhaps T1 saw our proposal as an attempt to get along with them.

*Did they think we bowed down on our own just when they were about to get upset over the independent label matter?*

They completely misunderstood, but it was good to make use of them. 

Anyway, T1 would also want to expand the overseas reach of its *daesang*-winning artists, so they were probably excited and offered the film company some kind of big deal.

However, considering its relationship with T1, the film company had agreed for now, but the working-level staff got annoyed when non-American K-pop idols suddenly intervened.

I touched my chin.

*Someone in the middle is pulling strings.*

They probably thought there wouldn't be any problems with letting some foreign guys make a single OST.

"Someone high up among Limestone's ground staff seems to dislike us."

"Okay! But when will you tell me how?"

"Right now."

*Impatient, are we?* I grinned.

"Okay. The meaning of the explanation so far is..."

"Please tell me!"

He's so straightforward.

"It means that once we meet the absurd conditions these guys set, they'll have no choice but to say okay."

"Hmm?"

"The higher-ups have already agreed. They can't refuse us if we have them on our side, though I suppose they could request revisions."

That meant we could go ahead with making the music.

"So how... First of all, we'll make a good song and send it."

"Boooooo. That's losing."

*This guy... That's not it.*

"It's not just about making it well. We're going to go above and beyond the keywords they gave us."

I drank water.

"We'll add keywords from the film company's existing superhero movies and the game company's universe, and build something elaborate on top of that."

"Why do we need to do that?"

"To make them ashamed."

"Hmm?"

I crumpled an empty plastic bottle.

"We'll add a frank note when we send it. Those guys sent us so little that we had to collect the data ourselves."

"Uuuuuh!"

"And the next words are important."

"What?"

I laughed.

"We'll kindly explain, since the person in charge doesn't seem to be aware, that more information and a looser deadline are customary when requesting an OST demo."

"...!"

I shrugged.

"And we'll be generous enough to tell them what they need to prepare before a meeting, just like teaching a kindergartener. Then it's over."

Even if it was only one OST, they'd have to report to their superiors about what went down while working with us, an external group.

Then, whoever was behind this little stunt would have nowhere to hide. They would have to explain to both companies what happened.

"WOW."

Cha Eugene seemed convinced.

"Hyung, that's totally Badass!"

"Do you like it?"

"I like it!"

*I was glad he liked it.*

Cha Eugene asked for a high-five, then ran to the hotel living room and started blurting out the details without being asked.

"Let's make our cool OST and embarrass them!"

"What?"

I knew it would be like this.

I completed the explanation by supplementing Cha Eugene's words. The members looked bewildered, but soon became serious.

"It's a classy way."

"Not bad~ Even if things go wrong, we'll have done our best." Keun Sejin said.

Even if the deal fell through, public opinion would be on our side. *Yes, I considered that too.*

"Shall we try a majority vote?"

"Okay."

As soon as everyone raised their hand in agreement, Cha Eugene blew a whistle.

"Our members are really cool!"

And Kim Raebin started to get excited again.

"Then we must get to work immediately."

"Okay. We'll quickly bring up movie and game references~ Our Raebin, fighting!"

"Yes!"

That was how the surplus manpower, excluding Kim Raebin, also had something to do.

"Well, then, should I tell the company about the memo we're going to send?"

"Well, I think it would be more effective if we wrote it ourselves."

It was unlikely that an interpreter who could convey all the subtle nuances would be available on such short notice. It was better for us, the senders, to put in a little extra effort.

*There's just the right person.*

"Should I do it?"

"No. Gather the references since you know American culture well."

"Okay."

Cha Eugene wasn't who I had in mind. He was eager, but to be honest, I didn't think he could word an official document very well.

I turned my head.

"Ahyeon-ah."

"Yes...?"

"Can you write the memo we're attaching to the music in English?"

Seon Ahyeon's face brightened.

"Y-yes...!"

He'd studied abroad at a prestigious private university and had an advanced English vocabulary, so he was probably the better choice. Before sending it, we could have it reviewed by an expert.

Besides, Seon Ahyeon wasn't very good at music production, so it seemed he was glad that he had something he could do.

"I-I will work hard...!"

"Good."

It seemed this would take some pressure off Seon Ahyeon's mind.

Keun Sejin smiled.

"Ah~ I think everything has been sorted out. Shall we get started?"

"OK!"

The air became lively again.

And in that atmosphere, Kim Raebin muttered as if he had now realized.

"Then, considering the memo and its translation... We have half a day less to work on the music."

"......"

"......"

Kim Raebin raised his thumb.

"The scheduled camp date was originally three days, so it seems to fit perfectly!"

*Yeah, if he was okay, we were all okay.*

* * *

"Aah... I am tired."

The employee on night duty at T1 Entertainment's Foreign Cooperation Department sighed.

Contacts from abroad came late at night in Korean time, so the worst part was having to be on emergency standby one night a week.

He could take naps and watch NetFlux, but it was still boring.

*In the first place, I don't have permission to open most of what comes in at this time.*

When something urgent came from a few designated clients, there was nothing to do other than quickly pass the messages along. The worker navigated the company's ERP system with a sigh.

Then he saw a document.

!pb
**테스타 (TeSTAR) Film Cooperation Case**
pb!

Because they were such popular idols, he had heard rumors within the department.

*They were the ones who contacted Limestone about working together.*

And he thought he had heard that they sent an actual demo the day before yesterday.

He didn't have permission to access it, so he'd only listened over the shoulder of someone who did, but he could tell that a lot of effort had gone into it, including the explanatory memo attached as an appendix.

So he felt uncomfortable.

*They're going this far...?*

To be honest, it seemed a bit servile.

*Limestone seemed to be snubbing them, but they were idols who'd won a daesang in Korea...*

Could it be that the company had put them up to this?

Somehow, his pride was hurt on their behalf, so the worker took the opportunity to curse his own company.

Based on his experience, he could predict what would happen next.

*If Limestone makes a generous show of accepting, this damn company will just put out press releases about it...*

It was then.

New mail had arrived.
★$
[Email] #f#[Inbox ×]#f#

–-#from: [Limestone] ⏷#-

– **[Fw: In response to your request...]**
$★

The sender immediately caught his eye.

*Limestone!*

He wanted to click on it... but he didn't have access. Disappointed, the worker tried to find out what he could, and one thing caught his eye.

The attachment size.

*Uh...*

What had arrived was a bundle of files just as large as the one TeSTAR had sent...!

*What the hell did they send?*

If it was a simple OK sign, there would be no need for so many files, and the same went for anything contract-related.

*Damn!*

He was about to lose his mind with frustration because he didn't have permission to read it...! The worker sighed.

And the next day, the worker found out what the e-mail had been.

It was a very roundabout gesture of apology and voluntary disclosure.

*Wow!*

It felt so satisfying to hear that.

*What did they send? No, how good was the song they made to get a response like this?*

* * *

"A proper request has arrived."

"Oh."

It worked.

Of course, I didn't know what they were going to say if we actually went to the meeting.

*No, at this rate, we might be able to handle everything by email without a meeting.*

Still, I didn't bother to stop the members from laughing and high-fiving.

*One win is one win.*

Ryu Chungwoo laughed and continued the briefing.

"There are almost no revision requests, only a few arrangement requests regarding the structure of the song."

"It shouldn't be difficult!"

Kim Raebin was almost flying. He showed unbelievable eagerness for a guy who had been running on nothing but caffeinated drinks for three days.

*Looks like he's having fun.*

It was then. Ryu Chungwoo showed a puzzled expression.

"And... They say they want to add a cameo appearance to the contract?"

"...?"

"Really? A movie cameo?"

"Mhm."

I was amazed.

*These guys...*

These guys... Just because things had turned out like this, they really wanted to take advantage of our popularity in Korea.

"You mean for this movie, right?"

"Yes. We'll just appear in the background as if passing by."

"At this rate..."

"Seems okay, I guess."

And naturally, we all looked at one guy.

"Why, why...?"

"We'll trust you."

"...!?"

Bae Sejin was a genius former child actor, after all. It was time to try his luck in Hollywood.

"Don't trust me!"
