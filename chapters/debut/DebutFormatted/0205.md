# Chapter 205

*I believe that you can do well on your own.*

At first glance, this sounded like a nice statement expressing trust and faith, but when it came from your superior, it meant one of two things:

'Figure it out yourself', or 'If it fails, it's your fault'.

And this guy, the management department's chief, was unwittingly making remarks that contained both of these meanings.

*If TeStar doesn’t achieve global results during these promotions, it would immediately give the director justification to barge in*

It was obvious. The director would be saying something like, 'As expected, you should do what I say to make it work in the global market. '

*Naturally, TeStar's right to speak will weaken compared to now.*

We barely managed to build up our authority to this point, so this kind of situation in this shitty, clueless company was no different from a landmine.

However, it would also be a problem to withdraw from this and show that TeStar was not confident in making a global hit with this album.

Because the director would also use that as a reason to intervene.

In short, we were stuck in a dilemma.

*This director bastard played us.*

It wasn't like I expected anything less from a parachute[1]{T/N: parachute = a person who got hired in a high position through connections} bastard, but it still pissed me off.

"Of course, we will do our best to produce good results, but we can't guarantee to hit the mark every time..."

"What? No, you just have to do what you have been doing so far. Don't worry!"

The section chief was only concerned about his own standing in the company, so of course, he confidently concluded that TeStar would continue its usual upward trend.

'You can just do what you've always done~', he said, without ever considering the burden on the singer.

It was very parachute-like to not know what it was like to suffer on the front line.

"...Mm, yes."

In the end, Ryu Chungwoo also shut his mouth.

He must have realized that seriously refusing at this point would just make the group look pathetic.

*Damn it.*

For this single, we had already minimized the arrangement and the choreography to make it lighter and more appealing. Because we wanted to accommodate the domestic recognition gained through the reality show.

*We even made the choreography for the chorus points easier to follow.*

With a few weeks left until the comeback, we had to redo all our objectives.

Once again, we were facing the same hellfire as during our debut.

"......"

Was there no way out of this?

I quickly shook my head.

*TeStar is also slowly getting out of the rookie phase, so if we could just squeeze something in...*

At that moment, Kim Raebin raised his hand and threw the fastball[1]{T/N: Threw the fastball = Gave a straightforward statement that could be considered rude}.

"Excuse me, the single for the promotion this time was produced with a focus on domestic popularity, so we did not aim for it to be a global hit."

"...!"

*Hey.*

I considered covering his mouth for a second but then realized it when I saw the flustered look on the section chief's face.

*This might actually be more effective.*

Kim Raebin had a pure look of doubt on his face without any intent behind his eyes.

Ryu Chungwoo met my eyes and nodded slightly.

"......"

Fine, let's see how it goes.

"Oh, if it does well domestically, it will naturally get good responses overseas."

"That isn't necessarily the case. The gap between K-pop's domestic and global hits has consistently widened over the past few years, resulting in various examples..."

Kim Raebin diligently explained songs that became hits in Korea but didn’t gain much traction in the global market, as well as the opposite cases.

And he finished his speech with a proud expression, having covered everything in his explanation.

"Due to that, we all the more cannot guarantee global success this time!"

"......"

The section chief seemed a little overwhelmed by this innocent bombardment of background knowledge.

However, perhaps he understood that Kim Raebin did not intend to be confrontational, so instead of looking offended, he began to speak in a disinclined tone.

"Since you still have a few weeks until the comeback, why don't you change up the direction a bit? Currently, the director has been very self-righteous... You all know that too, right?"

That's it. I found the opening.

I laced my fingers together.

"Then, you are saying that we need to reorganize the whole direction of our group promotions according to Section Chief's words right away."

"...!"

It wasn't like we have been bringing in bad results, so speaking harshly to this extent to this parachute was fine.

"Of course, I know that you are doing this for our sake, and I also know we are going to have to work hard..."

Still, I should put down a layer of cushioning.

"But I'm a little worried that the director will say this isn't artist-centered enough since it's the opposite route to what we had intended at first."

"......"

Wasn’t the conflict between ‘Let’s respect the artist’s wishes’ and ‘Let’s focus on global performance regardless of the artist’s plans’?

A look of perplexity flashed across the section chief's face.

My words must have sounded reasonable to you, did it not?

At best, he would be winning against the director, but he would also be under continuous stress about the director catching on that he had changed his words.

"Right~ It's because we originally had a direction we wanted to work on! So, I think something like that could happen."

"Since we don't have high aspirations towards America yet, we wanted to continue preparing for our comeback by working hard on our domestic activities first."

The support fires were raining in.

Meaning, we will continue with our current preparations, so you clean up the mess you made.

Unsurprisingly, the section chief's complexion soured.

"Hmm, then it would make it a little harder for me to support you in the future..."

Humiliatingly, he did not want to say any apologetic words while retracting what he said just before.

*Well, if we push any further, he might start babbling something about getting his feelings hurt.*

The section chief's arrows should be pointed at the director and not at TeStar.

Still, there were many things to be gained for pushing him to the corner like this.

*Extorting is going to be easy.*

Then, we'll just... Ah, right.

*...I'll go ahead with it after the members have consented first.*

I nodded and made eye contact with the other members, roughly signaling with them.

*Let's just do as he says, or the bomb might explode if things go wrong.*

*Sure, sure~*

*Either way, I will do my best...!*

*I'm hungry!*

The last signal seemed a little strange, but let's ignore it.

In any case, none of the guys seemed to shake their heads, nor did they look like they were dead set against it. So, I slowly responded to the section chief in a serious tone.

"Um... Then, how about we do it like this?"

"What, like what...?"

"Since the company saw how well the reality show was doing and had heavily invested in it, we could scale up our production plans before the comeback."

Invest a lot of capital in us.

"So, I think the context would seem more natural, but what do you think, section chief?"

The strategy was to make up for the shortage by pouring in as much money as possible since time was running out.

*This guy is a parachute, so he'll be able to raise the funds from the headquarters.*

Sure enough, the section chief was pleased.

"Seems fine to me."

Done.

Since it wasn't TeStar who asked for more money in this scenario, even if the success ends up being average, it would be less talked about.

Now, let's spend this huge amount of capital that poured in to revise the song arrangement, the choreography, and the music video... within four weeks.

*Fuck.*

All at once, our schedule got packed.

***

That day, we postponed our recording schedule and locked ourselves in the conference room.

"A global hit..."

"Hmm."

Surprisingly, Bae Sejin was the first to speak.

"...How about doing it in English?"

It was a fairly reasonable remark.

However, if you look at the global ecosystem of K-pop, it was too soon to carry out that suggestion.

"By using English, we would be targeting the English-speaking demographic... but for that to work, wouldn't they have to know about us first?"

"Ah."

Right. First, you would have to have a definite fan base to use that strategy.

If things went wrong, it could have a negative label such as, 'They're being such try-hards for America.'

So, what should be done to solidify the fan base of people who were brought in by the American memes?

"It would be good to look at some of the concepts that international K-pop fans have liked in the past."

"Ah, that's for sure~"

At Ryu Chungwoo's muttering, Lee Sejin quickly raised his hand.

"The choreography is great, and the beat is also good~ So, wouldn't it be good if we came up with a cool music video?"

"W-wouldn't people also like something cool like that in Korea...?"

"Well, you're right. But how should I say it? In Korea, I don't think many people listen to male idols with dance songs that have a lot of emphasis on the beat."

That's right.

That was why, out of the two debut title songs, we also pushed for 'Magical Boy' more since the song was easier to the ears without strong beats.

*That one ranked higher on music charts and had a longer run than 'Hi-five'.*

In the same sense, extremely conceptual and intense songs like 'Present' had a slightly weaker music chart ranking than the previous title songs.

And this was despite the fact that we reached our career high during that period of activity and entered the top-tier level as the fandom also grew with a greatly noticeable influx of new fans.

Still, it was as Lee Sejin said.

*Although it's a waste for the rapidly rising domestic popularity, if we need international attention, that would be the way to go.*

Of course, even if we had the direction set, it was not easy to revise everything overnight.

"We can just adjust the choreography and stuff like that so it's not a big prob... Sejin-ah, are you okay?"

"...Of course."

Bae Sejin adamantly replied to Ryu Chungwoo.

Ryu Chungwoo continued to talk with a slightly apologetic face.

"Okay. Well, so I think we can revise the rest step by step, taking a cooler direction... Although I'm just worried that the song arrangement will be difficult."

At those words, everyone turned to Kim Raebin.

"...?"

Kim Raebin looked around with a puzzled expression on his face, then he opened his mouth with realization.

"Once we decide on the concept, I will come up with arrangement drafts that will suit the image! Please allow me four days!"

It was an excited remark.

"W-will you be all right...? Isn't it going to be too much work...?"

"Even so, the title song this time has a good melodic top note that can be mixed in various ways, so I am quite excited to be able to try something new!"

"......"

Kim Raebin... seemed rather excited to try something he couldn't do before because he had to conform to the mainstream popularity formula.

*Right. Fortunately, everyone is content with this.*

I opened my mouth.

"Okay, Raebin-ah. Then, let's get the concept quickly out of the way so that Raebin can get to work."

"Yeah."

"O-okay!"

"Do you have something in mind, Moondae? Since you're the idea bank~"

Originally, we planned to release a moderately fresh, cheerful seasonal song suitable for spring with no particular fixed image.

Now, we needed to change this into a concept that would make the music video 'cool'...

At the same time, it had to appeal to English-speaking audiences so the director wouldn’t start flipping ou.

He seemed to be greedy, so I just hope he won't give up on domestic popularity.

Since this was a digital single, the music charts are important because there won't be album sales.

*...Hmm.*

A great choreography, a catchy beat, and something good for fans to consume?

*...Ah.*

I opened my mouth.

"We should use this opportunity to expand our world vision."

"Our world vision? Like what?"

"Like 'Present'."

A short while later, after some intense brainstorming, the revision was passed unanimously, down to the song arrangement.

*But in a way that would make it as listenable as possible!*

And three tumultuous weeks passed.
