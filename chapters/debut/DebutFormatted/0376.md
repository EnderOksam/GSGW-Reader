# Chapter 376 - The Wise Survival, IV

Two people went up onstage.

To be exact, there were three people, but one trainee was so overwhelmed that he was almost invisible.

Actually, it wasn't the trainee's fault. The problem was that the other two were about as experienced as one could get, having already reached the pinnacle of their craft and won *daesangs*.

Cha Eugene and Shin Jaehyeon.

*Hmm.*

Even the president, who was unaware of this fact, could see the stark contrast.

Although the two wore the same black school uniforms, the difference was obvious in everything from how they wore them to the way they stood.

They looked perfectly natural onstage, without a trace of nerves.

*Would you look at that?*

The president's lips twitched.

In fact, most of the supplementary team were supposed to be eliminated, and even if all of them were eliminated in the end, he wouldn't mind.

It was a good way to appeal to the audience by saying, 'Look, our trainees are so good.'

In other words, even if he saw potential in the supplementary team standing over there, he would eliminate them in most cases.

And he had his own personal reasons for wanting this particular trainee eliminated.

*He looks too much like a stereotypical entertainer.*

The president, who disliked the public's prejudiced views, grumbled but did not object to Cha Eugene's placement in the supplementary team.

His skills and appearance made it hard to imagine why he hadn't become a trainee earlier. It was only when he learned the boy was American that he nodded in understanding.

*A couple of them have to be good, at least, to achieve balance.*

He had no qualms about including the handsome boy who had rapped amazingly earlier. He intended to eliminate him in the end anyway and set him aside for later use.

It was at this point that he reeled in his wandering thoughts.

Now on stage, the lights were off.

"......"

*Hok.*

Plumes of dry ice rose from the stage, and red and blue lights shone down.

A humming that resembled the sheer drop from a cliff slowly began.

$p
♪ *Um, um-um-um, uum, um* ♫
p$

It was a pop song by a solo female singer famous for her gloomy, cold tone and bass lines.

The instrumental began.

*Thud.*

With a monotonous minor guitar riff, the three knelt to the beat of the drums.

And then the choreography began.

$p
♪ *His growling, howling,*\
*It raises me up* ♫
p$

Their opening movements almost seemed excessive, using their arms and legs to the fullest. Then they stood upright as if someone were pulling them up.

Shin Jaehyeon, at the center, looked at the camera and spun his hand.

The movement that started with his hand extended through the rest of his body, intriguingly using his entire upper body and lifting his head.

The lights flashed around him as he wore a gloomy smile.

$p
♪ *To the darkness, absence...*\
*No* ♫
p$

The song abruptly stopped, followed by silence.

The three performers fanned out.

They tilted their upper bodies back, bounced their hips, then flipped backward in a graceful acrobatic movement with their hands on the floor.

The music exploded.

$p
♪ *Tonight*\
*He's gonna find your room*\
*And*\
*Take you to the woods* ♫
p$

Even amid such intense movements, they skillfully kept their singing at just the right degree of scratchiness.

The bizarre, intense notes and movements, along with the school uniforms, created a very strong atmosphere.

And the refrain began.

$p
♪ *Watch out!* ♫
p$

Cha Eugene came forward into the center as if he had plunged down from above.

In an instant, all eyes were drawn to him.

$p
♪ *Watch out*\
*for the wolves!* ♫
p$

With intricate footwork, Cha Eugene came forward, deliberately reaching out to grab the camera before letting go in one swift movement.

His eyes were shining.

"...!"

Their movements left a huge impact while blending seamlessly into the performance.

That didn't make them any less effective.

The performance was gripping.

$p
♪ *Wo, wo-wo-woof*\
*Wolf* ♫
p$

Then came a stationary move isolating their shoulder and neck movements.

And a dance break began. Using the person standing in the middle as a kind of buoy, more difficult performances unfolded on both sides.

The choreography was executed precisely.

However, the feeling of their movements was very different.

One side was powerful and menacing enough to devour you, while the other was intense yet exquisite.

$p
♪ *Your guest who-o-o*\
*comes in the dead*\
*of the night* ♫
p$

*Thud.*

The song ended with only one dissonant piano chord.

The participants had dropped to the floor, their gazes fixed on the ceiling.

"......"

For a long moment, only the faint sound of their breathing spread through the venue.

Then, a moment later...

*Clap, clap clap clap...!*

Uncertain applause filled the venue from various directions.

There was no actual audience at this shoot, but the production crew and other contestants applauded to create an atmosphere, uncertain as they did so.

The president was a little flustered.

*What?*

This was because the performance was not of the quality that he expected from trainees. It was more like the year-end stage of a broadcasting station.

Two of them had completely eclipsed the third person, but no one could even bring themselves to be bothered by it.

They had completely dominated the stage.

*Don't tell me they planned it out to that extent, too...?*

*...No, if that were the case, they'd be running the company, not me!*

The president threw away his distracting thoughts and looked at the two people on stage with admiring eyes.

He was sincerely impressed. He didn't expect them to do this well.

*It's probably a matter of luck and synergy.*

Anyway, he was very satisfied that at least one performance had turned out so fantastic.

!-
\
– Passable practice atmosphere

– Brief friction over song selection
-!

After reading the summary of the practice process handed over by the production team, the president came to a conclusion.

And after briefly but effusively praising each person, he picked up the microphone again to ask some questions.

"Who chose the song?"

"We all did it together."

Shin Jaehyeon, who got up and quickly straightened his clothes, answered smoothly.

"Since this is a pop song, it might have given Cha Eugene an advantage."

Shin Jaehyeon only smiled faintly in understanding and did not reply.

There was nothing he could have said that would've really helped.

*Smart guy.*

The president felt briefly proud of the young man as he continued speaking.

His sentiments were sincere.

"However... Why does it seem to me that this song was made for Jaehyeon?"

"...!"

Yes.

At first glance, this intense stage appeared to be exactly the type Cha Eugene would prefer.

Colorful, complex and challenging.

But in fact, the message of this song was more multi-layered.

*Depression.*

Shin Jaehyeon thought simply.

The song spoke of the fear and resignation the lyricist felt about their deep depression.

And Shin Jaehyeon was able to express it like he was breathing.

It wasn't because he had performed the same song multiple times. In this strange world where time repeated itself, the songs he chose had often changed.

Only...

Even if it wasn't the same song, he had done so many similar things.

This dark concept had been a steady seller for VTIC ever since their fourth year. They'd released a song with a similar concept every single year since then.

*And crucially...*

*The president of LeTi has a personal preference for this sort of music.*

He didn't even bother to turn his head to check his competitor's face.

Cha Eugene.

Overwhelming energy, dazzling charm and talent that made him seem capable of devouring the song.

However, those were not the only things being evaluated.

"Why do you think that is, Jaehyeon?"

Shin Jaehyeon picked up the microphone.

"If you really want to put it into words... Isn't it just a matter of atmosphere? I've practiced songs like this for a long time."

*A textbook answer.*

"That's right."

The president smiled.

"I think what Jaehyeon showed on stage today was the perfect LeTi performance."

"Thank you."

Shin Jaehyeon deliberately lowered his head slightly to show that he was moved by the president's words.

And the president slightly turned his head.

Cha Eugene just stood still. Nevertheless, an afterimage of the contrasting feelings he saw on stage remained.

*Hmm.*

He changed his mind slightly.

*I'm seeing a picture here.*

"Here's my assessment."

The stage lights went out and the backdrop lit up.

And the results appeared smoothly.

"Congratulations."

It was Shin Jaehyeon's victory.

However...

The president picked up the microphone again.

"However, the production team has given me a special privilege that I can use only once."

*Even if I disqualify him in the end, I'll have him stick around a bit longer first to build more contrast!*

"During this program, I can save only one trainee from disqualification, regardless of the situation."

"...!"

The president decided.

*Let's keep him around first and see if he can change.*

If the president let him stay, he would surely be thrilled that his efforts had been recognized, and it would motivate him to improve and change to suit the tastes of the person who'd given him another opportunity.

What if that didn't work?

*Then I'll simply keep him from ever entering the industry.*

All he'd need to do was send the boy off to a small agency that would never get anywhere. The president carefully intertwined his fingers, his calculations complete.

"Cha Eugene lost, but I see potential in him—potential to become a great artist at LeTi Entertainment."

"......"

"So, under my authority, I am accepting Cha Eugene-ssi as an additional participant outside the quota."

*Bang.*

Text appeared on the screen amid the loud sound effect.

+=
#><#y[Boss Pass!]y#><#

#g[⟨Supplementary team⟩ Cha Eugene - Contestant status confirmed]g#
=+

"Can you make sure I don't regret my choice?"

"Of course! Thank you!"

Contrary to the president's expectations, Cha Eugene simply smiled and spoke plainly, not looking moved at all.

It was almost as if he knew he'd pass, or simply enjoyed the idea of being able to continue. It was refreshing.

There was no sense of gratitude or intense emotion.

*...Wait a second.*

The president tried to keep the frown from his face as he watched Cha Eugene shake hands with his team members, who then patted him on the back.

*Anyway, what did I expect from someone who grew up eating American food?*

The company staff would have a hard time teaching him proper etiquette, but he couldn't withdraw the pass he'd already used, so the president just nodded coolly.

*He'll help draw more attention to Shin Jaehyeon.*

There had to be some kind of synergy here. How else could such a high-quality stage have been possible, even with luck? That was what he thought.

The stage was soon cleared, and by the time he'd finished his train of thought, the next team had come up.

The contestants' names appeared on the screen.

+=
#><[High-tier team — Ryu Gunwoo]><#
=+

*Hmm.*

This time the atmosphere was quite different from before. The three wearing white jackets seemed nervous, but it was obvious they weren't trying to outdo one another.

Rather, they kept looking at the trainee in the middle and adjusted their movements and positions according to his hand gestures.

*Ohh.*

*The guy who sang that atmospheric jazz song is taking the lead here, too?*

The president recalled the summary of each team's practice routine that he had received in advance and looked at their outfits.

This was the outfit that idol groups wore at least once after their debut.

It was a marine look combining white jackets and pants with blue sailor collars.

One guy even wore shorts. The president almost burst out laughing.

"Looking at the outfits, it seems like a cute concept, right?"

"We wanted to try something new for us."

Ryu Gunwoo, who naturally took the microphone, glanced at his competitors, then smiled slightly and continued talking.

"That includes being cute."

Then the other two snickered to themselves. It was so heartwarming that anyone could believe it was a team game rather than a death match.

*Hmm.*

!-
\
– No potential for conflict

– Ryu Gunwoo acted as the leader of the group
-!

After skimming through the summary, the president nodded.

"Let's get started."

He'd have to see their performance before he could come up with a suitable storyline.

"Yes!"

Soon, the trainees nodded and got into formation around the sofa placed in the center of the stage.

The contestant from the supplementary team sat down, the one from the low-tier team stood, and Ryu Gunwoo simply leaned against the side.

[Hmm!]

The moment the accompaniment came out...

The trainees around the sofa bounced like squishy cushions to the refreshing beat.

$p
♪ *Oh, oh-oh, icy eyes*\
*They're cold, but I like them nevertheless!* ♫
p$

They'd subtly changed the rhythm and adjusted the pitch of the "oh-oh."

Thanks to this, the pronunciation of the lyrics had a bouncy staccato feel instead of the slightly over-the-top feel of the original.

$p
♪ *Your tone, like refreshing soda*\
*Your eyes, hard as ice* ♫
p$

They sat on the sofa in unison, got up, jumped over it as if they were flying, and came together to perform a group choreography.

$p
♪ *Oh, so pretty!* ♫
p$

Unlike the original song, the high notes exploded, but their jumps remained high.

The light, refreshing rhythm continued, and the choreography flowed without a pause.

$p
♪ *I see the butterflies*\
*The bees buzzing in my chest*\
*The complicated feelings* ♫
p$

*Oh.*

Chaeyul was there at every killing part that involved a gesture and a camera close-up.

And he performed each of those parts so perfectly that it was surprising.

$p
♪ *It's all right*\
*But it's too cold. I need you to melt* ♫
p$

Shockingly, these lyrics didn't come off as cringy.

*What is going on?*

And the refrain came back.

$p
♪ *Oh, oh-oh, icy eyes*\
*They're cold, but I like them nevertheless!* ♫
p$

Surprisingly, Ryu Gunwoo made a cute face while singing the chorus in an incredibly natural way.

The round hat on his head suited him strangely well.

$p
♪ *Oh, oh-oh icy ice*\
*Your eyes are so cold* ♫
p$

The high notes were clean, as expected, but the cute, refreshing vibe that should've been awkward on him didn't seem awkward at all.

*...??*

Only the president, who didn't know that Gunwoo had survived in the idol industry for 5 years with the nickname "Moon Puppy," was taken aback.

$p
♪ *When I come up to you and hold out my hand*\
*Let us look at each other with warmer eyes* ♫
p$

The tempo accelerated, and they performed more complex choreography at the front of the stage, leaving the sofa behind.

The center position for the dance break was given to the other trainee.

And the next refrain was sung by all three of them in harmony.

*Hmm.*

The president nodded inwardly.

And lastly...

$p
♪ *So cool!* ♫
p$

At the part Park Moondae had deliberately lied about by calling it 'intense,' their bright energy covered the stage like fireworks.

$p
♪ *Oh, oh-oh, icy eyes* ♫
p$

The jumps, high notes and mid-to-high original notes were balanced perfectly with the chords. There wasn't a hint of dissonance.

It was a refreshing stage that made whoever watched it feel good.

It was exactly what one would expect from two trainees who hadn't been with the agency for long and a non-trainee.

*It doesn't feel like LeTi.*

To be honest, it wasn't the LeTi president's taste, but it wasn't bad.

This was because a good stage was good regardless of taste.

$p
♪ *I still like it!* ♫
p$

Even the final movement matched the complicated rhythm neatly, and the stage ended with bright smiles on their faces.

*Oh.*

*Clap clap clap!*

This time, cheery applause erupted from all over the place.

It wasn't as overwhelming as the previous stage. However, it was pleasant and appealing, with both impressive quality and density.

The president willingly picked up the microphone.

"That was very pleasant to watch. The three of you worked really well together... Lovely."

He gave a few words of praise to each individual, especially the member of the supplementary team, and then got to the point.

"How was practice?"

The trainees' faces brightened and they answered quickly.

"We really had fun and worked hard!"

"Yes. Gunwoo-hyung guided us well... We all had fun practicing."

Ryu Gunwoo looked a little embarrassed, but did not deny it and nodded. The president laughed.

"I thought so."

The other two trainees' faces brightened.

*This was the moment.*

"But it's ironic... That's also the problem, right?"

"...!"

The president looked back at Ryu Gunwoo.

"Why can't I see any desperation from you?"

"...!"

"Gunwoo, I don't think this is the sort of thing you're best at... This is your second performance ever, right?"

"...Yes."

"Then isn't now the time to put your best foot forward and show what you're best at? Even then, there's no guarantee you'll succeed. You gave up all the important parts to the other team members, didn't you?"

The president made serious eye contact and spoke.

"Instead of unilaterally giving in to what your dongsaengs want, you should've been a little greedier and shown us more of yourself. This was a death match."

The other two participants beside him looked frightened and worried, but Ryu Gunwoo only swallowed once.

The president liked his courage, too!

"This isn't something you can just do for fun. You need to be earnest."

Like a lion throwing its cub off a cliff to toughen it up, he planned to make Gunwoo strong.

"You did well, but nothing more. This is a compliment given to a student, not a professional."

Ryu Gunwoo had an expressionless face, but his shoulders tensed noticeably.

*Yeah, that's it.*

Gunwoo needed to be stimulated.

And so did the viewers!

The president suppressed a victorious smile and pressed the button, keeping a solemn, conflicted expression.

"Announcing the winner."

Chaeyul had won.

"Congratulations. You're a formal contestant now."

"...! Th-thank you..."

As he said thank you, he staggered around, looking shocked.

"And Ryu Gunwoo is at risk of being disqualified."

The president smiled, pleased.

At that moment, however, the president had to pause.

*Huh?*

It was very brief, but he got the feeling that Ryu Gunwoo had smiled.

*...Was that a bittersweet smile or...?*

The president stared at him for a long moment, but decided he must have been mistaken. He shrugged his shoulders and then spoke gravely into his microphone again.

"Thank you. You may leave the stage."

That was how the evaluation ended.

And backstage...

Cha Eugene found Park Moondae after Jin Chaeyul had burst into tears. Moondae had comforted Chaeyul for the sake of the cameras, then sent him on his way when Eugene arrived.

With the cameras now gone, Eugene went over and tapped Moondae.

"Hyung!"

"Yes."

Park Moondae looked nonchalant. Even though his features had changed, that strangely peaceful expression was still the same.

And so was his subtle kindness.

"Are you sad you lost?" Moondae asked.

"No," Eugene said.

Cha Eugene shrugged his shoulders. And as he looked up at the stage, he grinned and spoke in English.

[It's not that old man with the microphone who really decides victory or defeat.]

"...!"

His hyung seemed taken aback for a moment, but he'd soon understand the situation.

*This was the result he wanted, wasn't it?*

Park Moondae thought.

And that was indeed the correct path forward!

[It'll soon become clear that I'm the one who won.]

"Is that so?"

Cha Eugene deliberately thumped Moondae on the shoulder. As usual, his hyung smoothly moved away.

"Are you disappointed, hyung?" he asked, switching back to Korean.

"No way."

*Of course not.*

Park Moondae smiled slightly.

"I'm thinking the same thing as you."

That was how the filming ended that day.

And not long after, LeTi's in-house survival show ⟨Wise⟩ finally went on air.
