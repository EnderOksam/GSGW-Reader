# Chapter 394 - Where We Belong, II

Lee Sejin knew that he was living a successful life.

He beat odds of hundreds to one to become a trainee at a large agency, survived the fierce competition there, and ultimately debuted.

Among the dozens of idol groups that debuted that year, his group received the best reviews and even won the Rookie of the Year Award. Everything was on track.

*Objectively... it's a success.*

Of course, he thought he would be happy, enjoying the most accomplished period of his career.

But as someone had once said, the same scenery could look different depending on where you stood.

*That's funny.*

It was amazing how he could feel anxious and frustrated at the same time every day.

His teammates had settled into a rut, the company's new plan was mostly hot air, and his opinions kept getting blocked and disappearing.

Being unable to do anything even when he saw something that needed fixing made him feel helpless.

– *Hey, Lee Sejin, cut it out. Can't you read the room right now? I'm so fucking tired.*

– *...Oh, that's right, I'm really tired as well... But if we do it now, tomorrow will be easier. Let's do it just once~* he would say.

He knew that reality and dreams were different. The anger that had once boiled inside him had cooled down.

Still, there were times when he felt indignant and hurt for no particular reason.

When moving between schedules, preparing an album, and getting a quick nap in the early morning...

*...*

The feeling of being deceived would suddenly hit him, leaving him sitting there in frustration.

That might have been why the words of this survival show contestant, whom he had just met, pierced his heart.

– *You might not have realized it, but your efforts have produced valuable results elsewhere.*

*Effort and achievement. Getting back into that cycle.* Ryu Gunwoo might not have meant exactly that, but that was how his words had sounded.

*What am I expecting to see?*

It could have just been a trivial psychological test floating around somewhere on social media. No, it probably was.

Lee Sejin guessed that Gunwoo had brought it up because the atmosphere suddenly became strange while they were watching the concert.

Nevertheless, he couldn't ignore it. He seized the offer with both hands, completely serious.

– *Do it.*

As if some button inside him had been pressed.

*What am I doing, really?*

Still, he had no intention of taking back his words. Lee Sejin pressed his lips together and looked at Ryu Gunwoo in front of him.

"......"

Ryu Gunwoo didn't smile awkwardly at the sudden silence or say anything in response.

Instead, he stretched out his hand.

*...?*

Then he made a strange movement in the air, as if pressing a button...

– *...!!*

"Ah."

At that moment, something exploded like a firecracker in his head.

It felt like a dam had burst.

Memories and knowledge that should have been there from the start were rushing back into place.

– *Thank you again for helping me stay.*

– *Yeah, baby! Our group's so good!*

– *Let's get all of TeSTAR's contracts renewed. I believe in you guys~!*

Memories and emotions filled his head. He'd taken those emotions for granted back then—all those moments of pain, excitement, inferiority, accomplishment, and victory at turning points in his life.

*Accomplishment.*

*The place where I belong.*

– *I wish the Seoul Concert could last just one more day.*

– *That's right! Let's do it!*

– *Stop talking about things that are realistically impossible and making everyone feel burdened. Get the luggage—*

– *Y-yes... You're d-disappointed too, aren't you, Moondae?*

– *...I'd be lying if I said no.*

– *I knew everyone felt the same way! The company is at fault~ It was too short~*

At that moment, Keun Sejin realized the cause of the helplessness he felt.

*I've already lived my dream.*

Yes.

He had already lived a life where his efforts were rewarded with achievement.

He'd learned that even if he hit rock bottom, he could recover and try again, knowing that even his last attempt might be the one that succeeded...

A life where he knew what it felt like to live with trustworthy, respectable people and give his all to his work.

"......"

The dizzying flood of memories finally ended. Lee Sejin raised his head. He could see his friend quietly studying his face, as if worried about him.

A real teammate he hadn't seen in a long time.

*He told me the results of my efforts were elsewhere.*

It had sounded like such an odd thing to say, as if he was subtly trying to lure him into something.

"You're right."

It was as he said.

The things Lee Sejin had worked so hard for were elsewhere.

"They're... somewhere else."

And it had been like that from the beginning.

"Ah, Moondaemoondae, how do you always say the right things?"

"...!"

Lee Sejin laughed out loud.

Ryu Gunwoo's—or rather, Park Moondae's—eyes widened, as if surprised that Lee Sejin had recognized him. Nothing could have felt better than seeing that.

Despite the confusion and questions crowding his mind about this incomprehensible, surreal situation, the first thing he felt was...

A sweet sense of relief.
