# Chapter 360 - A Carrot Revolution, II

An hour had passed since we learned that all our carrot coins had been stolen.

"Aaaahhh."

The once-comfortable house was now filled with chaos.

Kim Raebin, who had drawn the note, was huddled in a corner, drenched in cold sweat.

"Carrots... Carrots..." he kept muttering.

It seemed the shock had been too much for him.

"Then..."

Ryu Chungwoo calmly opened his mouth.

"That means we don't have any money at all right now."

*Dong!*

When they edited this, an exclamation mark would definitely appear above our heads.

*These guys are genuinely shocked.*

I crossed my arms and spoke quietly.

"Yes. In other words, we're beggars."

"B-beggars..." Ahyeon said.

"How could something like this happen overnight?"

All we had left to our name was one puppy.

A little bundle of fur jumped around the devastated members as if to cheer them up.

*Maaaang! Woof!*

The members' expressions brightened a little.

"Well, I'm glad they didn't steal Mungaemungae, right?"

"Y-yeah!" Ahyeon said.

"...It's still a pup, so there was a real risk. I'm glad they didn't take it away. We should be thankful it isn't even hurt."

*Were they serious?*

To be blunt, I wondered if this much shock was really necessary just because some fake money had disappeared, but then I realized the production team had gotten us to relax completely over the past two days...

*It's just a side effect—*

"...!"

*Actually, no. We got tricked.*

These guys had planned everything and executed it perfectly. They'd deliberately been so nice to us to lull us into a false sense of security.

To shock us like this when we'd completely let our guard down...

*They'd done it brilliantly.*

– *Huh? Dear me! Why did you pick this? The other penalty slip probably doesn't say anything like this!*

– *Then show us, so we can check!*

– *Oh, we can't do that! You might have to draw another one next time!*

– *Ugh! What? Darn it!*

*Both penalty slips probably said we'd lost all our coins anyway.*

I refrained from cursing as I remembered the face of the PD who was babbling so shamelessly.

*You called it healing, you fucker!*

*No... Let's calm down.*

This was the part that would finally get interesting for the show.

*The relaxation... Yes, two days of healing have already been more than enough.*

I had to think like that. It was the only way I could think about it. *Don't get upset. If I let them get to me here, they win.*

Meanwhile, Seon Ahyeon, who'd kept his mouth shut, suddenly raised his hand as if he'd made up his mind.

"Yes, Ahyeon?" Chungwoo said.

"W-we have some yarn... Would you like me to make something...? Maybe we could sell it."

"Wow."

"Hmm. I didn't think of that. I guess someone with good dexterity could come up with something like this. You can make things with yarn, after all," Chungwoo said.

It was a good idea. Seeing top-tier idols desperately sitting together and doing handicrafts to make ends meet would make for a unique and funny scene.

However, the production team's intentions were important here.

"...Well, it's true that it's a great idea... But shouldn't we find out who did this first? The production team also said so," Bae Sejin said.

*Yeah, that was probably the direction this program preferred.*

– *Who on earth?? Who stole it?*

– *Well, about that! This unidentified night guest is someone currently living in this town!*

Bae Sejin gulped.

"They're telling us to catch the culprit ourselves."

"Hmm."

The members fell into thought. A moment later, a pale-faced Kim Raebin muttered seriously.

"What if the thief tries to hurt us...?"

"Oh, that won't work."

"Y-yes. It could be dangerous..." Ahyeon said.

*No, you guys are taking this way too seriously.* This wasn't that kind of show, and the production team's top priority was making people laugh, so there was no way they'd actually put us in danger.

Keun Sejin nodded gravely.

"Yes. It could be dangerous."

*And you've gotten caught up in the excitement too. Finally, some hardship and adversity has come our way, giving us something to work with... Is that why you're so happy?*

Ryu Chungwoo proposed a compromise.

"But right now, we need food... For now, let's help out and earn some coins."

"Yes!"

"In the meantime, let's look into who the thief might be. There must be some kind of clue."

"Th-that's a wise decision," Raebin said.

I thought it was a good decision. It showed how immersed we were in the setting, and compared to the carefree volunteer work we'd done the day before, this was actually pretty funny.

"......"

*No, I said I wouldn't get upset,* I reminded myself, realizing my temper was rising again.

Anyway, we set off to meet the residents, cameras in tow.

"Father~ Is it okay if we continue cleaning the yard where we left off yesterday? If you give us some coins, we'll make it look like new!" Keun Sejin said.

"Huh? There's no need."

However, surprisingly, people gave us hardly any work.

"...?"

"How many houses have turned us down now?"

"Five houses."

It was an obviously manufactured situation.

The grandmother we met at the sixth house even clicked her tongue and said:

"Oh my... Those coins? I don't have any left either. Can't I just give you something to eat as payment before you go?"

The production team answered brightly.

"Sorry, that's not going to work~"

"Why are you answering for us?!"

"It's a rule! You know how the system works!"

*These bastards were really...*

It seemed that the rural hospitality the production crew had been boasting about had suddenly disappeared overnight.

At this point, Ryu Chungwoo smiled incredulously and asked again. Even the saintly Chungwoo seemed to be having his patience tested.

"Then what should we do?"

"So! We have prepared something just in case."

*Of course, you did.*

*They're definitely going to make us do some ridiculous amount of work—*

"It's a carrot farm!"

"...!"

*This is way bigger than I imagined.*

We were immediately loaded onto a bus, and after a short ride, we arrived at a green farm spread across a vast flat field.

"Wow..."

"It's so spacious."

*How on earth did they arrange this?* Then I realized they'd probably chosen the farm first, then decided on the accommodation and coin system.

The PD opened his mouth with a bright smile on his face.

"Did I tell you that each Carrot Coin is worth one actual carrot?"

"Ah, I remember!" Keun Sejin said.

"That's right."

As the members gaped, Kim Raebin spoke up with a relieved look on his face.

*Uh-oh.*

"So, can we exchange the carrots we harvest directly for coins?"

"Oh no~ Everyone, these carrots were planted and grown by someone else, so you can't just get coins for harvesting them~"

"......"

*I thought so.*

Then the PD made an absurdly unfair suggestion.

"First of all, if you harvest everything from here to there today, it'll be five coins per person!"

"Hey!"

"I'm not doing this!!"

"I'm leaving."

The production crew stifled their laughter as they watched our horrified reactions.

*What's so funny?*

"Everyone, you need to eat~"

"......"

Having quickly grown accustomed to the show's absurdity, the members overcame their shock and got to work.

"Shall we start from there?"

"Okay."

"I don't like carrots," Eugene said.

Even though they complained, they ended up doing their best.

I grabbed a tool and dug into the soil, pulling the carrots out by their leafy green tops, one bright orange root after another.

*At least this will make for good footage on the show.*

We would come across as sincere, diligent young people who hadn't forgotten their roots or lost their humility despite their success.

*Or just gullible bastards who never learn.*

*Fuck you.*

Either way, it was beneficial... but I still felt the sting of betrayal.

*This is like the inland version of the fishing boat I imagined before.*

"Let's put it here!"

"Okay."

We continued our hard labor, filling the huge basket with the carrots we'd harvested.

The PD grabbed the megaphone.

"Remember, this is worth five coins! And don't straighten your backs until you've pulled out all the carrots."

"Ugh! Will you take responsibility if our backs hurt?"

"Huh? I think your daily wages might be too high."

"Sorry."

Honestly, it wasn't *that* hard on the body. Compared to our intense practice during promotions, it was just a little tiring.

"Work, work!" the PD called.

"......"

*Still, the atmosphere was intense.*

Being treated like medieval servants was infuriating... Of course, it would become more interesting after editing. This must be it.

*I understand.*

Sometimes I paused to glance at the production crew with a hurt, resentful look as I continued pulling out carrots. It was bearable up to this point.

The problem arose from the following.

"Here's the daily wage!" the PD said.

"Wow!"

"We won't eat carrots for a while. I won't even eat carrot cake."

"Yes, Moondae."

We went to the supermarket to buy dinner with our hard-earned coins... only to be faced with exorbitant prices.

"That... It'll be ten coins!"

"...!?"

Suddenly, the price of *dalgona* had increased tenfold.

"Twenty coins for 400 grams?"

"Are you trying to starve us to death?"

The price of meat skyrocketed as well.

Of course, the members were indignant and ran over to the production crew to complain.

"I thought you said there was no manipulation!"

"We didn't tamper with the prices in any way~ Absolutely not! Please don't misunderstand!"

Kim Raebin asked with an almost delirious expression.

"Then what is this situation..."

"How are we supposed to know what the residents are thinking? They can sell things at whatever prices they want."

"......"

I clenched my fists. *I don't know who came up with this idea, but I sincerely want to punch them in the face just once.*

In the end, we returned home with a meager haul compared to what we'd bought over the previous two days.

I declared calmly.

"Let's just make some sujebi and eat."

"Ah, ah..."

"Sujebi... Okay. It's delicious."

They still remembered to react for the cameras, which I found impressive.

"Sorry... I'm the cause of all of this... It was my negligence..."

"What are you talking about! Of course, this is all the PD's fault!" Bae Sejin protested.

Kim Raebin, who had drawn the note, looked completely lost, and Bae Sejin and the dog were there to comfort him.

"......"

*This won't work.*

I tapped Keun Sejin, who was putting our groceries down in the kitchen.

"We have some flour and butter left, right?"

"Hm? Yeah. There's some left behind... Why?"

"Give me a second."

I checked the butter I'd splurged on the first day and headed to the kitchen.

The production staff had given us free time so we could badmouth them. I could use it however I wanted.

---

Park Moondae was making something.

*Mmm.*

As soon as Ryu Chungwoo came out of the shower, he caught a delicious scent. He saw Park Moondae from behind, bustling about in the kitchen.

*...Is he making Western food? I thought he said he was making sujebi.*

As always, he was conscious of the camera angles and took care to capture the cooking process properly.

*He's so calm.*

It was definitely a situation worth getting upset about, but Moondae was silently working toward practical solutions. It was moments like this that reminded Chungwoo that Moondae was actually older than him—that he really was the hyung here.

Ryu Chungwoo carefully entered the kitchen.

"Do you need anything?" Chungwoo asked.

"I'm almost done, so it's okay. The other guys helped me."

Park Moondae opened the air fryer.

A fragrant smell wafted out, revealing a batch of yellow cookies.

"Would you like to taste one?" Moondae asked.

"Ah."

*They're cookies.*

At that moment, Cha Eugene appeared out of nowhere.

"I want to eat!"

"It's hot so be careful."

"Hehe!"

Seeing Cha Eugene pop a cookie into his mouth, blow on it as he ate, and give a thumbs-up with shining eyes, Ryu Chungwoo realized something.

*I guess he's thinking of selling the cookies.*

It was a really good idea. He thought the members who didn't want to return to the carrot field would react well to the idea too.

"...! It's delicious," Chungwoo said.

"That's a relief."

Ryu Chungwoo was impressed by both the taste and the idea.

*I'll watch how he makes them so I can help when he's cooking in large quantities.*

But that idea didn't see fruition.

As soon as the cookies cooled down a little, Park Moondae put more than half of them into a container and said something else.

"The other guys are going to make sujebi... Hyung, let's go together."

"...Me?"

*Where are we going?*

And the place Park Moondae took him... was the village hall, where many of the residents had just finished dinner.

*...??*

*What on earth is he thinking?*

"Hello, everyone."

"Ah! Aren't you the stars who came with the cameras from Seoul?"

"The cameras are with us today too. And I made something. They're soft and perfect to eat now, since they're fresh."

"Oh my, what's this! You shouldn't have!"

The elderly residents, delighted to have something interesting happen for once, enthusiastically welcomed Park Moondae as he handed out the cookies generously.

Ryu Chungwoo played along, not sure what was going on.

Park Moondae went one step further and started talking about Ryu Chungwoo.

"This guy won a gold medal in archery at the Olympics. Do you remember him?"

"Oh my!"

"Yes, I think I remember. Yah~"

"Hahaha."

Ryu Chungwoo laughed, eagerly shook their hands, and massaged some of the elderly residents' shoulders. However, he still didn't know why Park Moondae was acting like this.

"Why don't I sing a song for you?"

"Okay, okay~"

Park Moondae even sang his own trot song and danced to 'POP★CON' like an entertainer working for hire, showing off all kinds of jokes.

"...??"

Ryu Chungwoo became even more confused, but he played along, singing a verse himself and eagerly answering any and all of the seniors' questions.

*I'm sure he has a reason for doing this.*

Once the atmosphere had warmed up and the elders had lowered their guard...

Park Moondae got straight to the point.

"By any chance, have you heard anything about this bottle cap-looking coin? Did the people from Seoul say anything about this today?"

"Ah, that!"

The camera director looked taken aback, but Park Moondae finally uncovered the truth.

"That is..."

---

I spoke up.

"So, I heard you promised a state-of-the-art massage chair to the ten residents with the most Carrot Coins."

After explaining the full story, I gathered the members on the veranda to confront the production team.

The PD avoided my gaze for a moment before shamelessly responding.

"Wait, how did you hear about that?"

"Aaaack!"

"So it's true!"

"How could you do that to us, really!"

The members immediately began protesting.

Even Seon Ahyeon turned red.

"Y-you said it would only be a healing show or else we could sue you...!"

"Huh?"

The PD casually accepted the challenge.

"Do it~ Sue me!"

"...!!"

"They're just Carrot Coins~ Are they even real currency? They're just bottle caps~ I don't know if you could actually sue us!"

"Aaaah!"

*Shake, shake.*

Bae Sejin shuddered with rage and muttered through gritted teeth.

"We won't ever forget this..."

"Producer~ One day, this will all come back as karma!"

The PD seemed a little stung and uncomfortable, so he hurriedly tried to explain.

"No, everyone... You shouldn't blame me. There's a culprit who stole your coins! There definitely is!"

"...!"

"If you just find the culprit! You'll get all your coins back... We'll also give you a bonus! 100 coins!"

"...! R-really?"

"Really~ What makes you think we'd lie? Are you still hung up on the betrayal?"

*Because you already lied to us. You cheated first.*

"......"

I took a deep breath.

*Okay. This is going to make it more fun for the audience.*

After being scammed by this production team so many times, TeSTAR had grown accustomed to their antics.

*So, this was the right way to make the show more stimulating and interesting.*

*Maybe it was a good idea. We'd have clips of us desperately searching for the culprit, along with us acting cute in front of the elders and production crew, doing our best to please the residents.*

"......"

*However... Why am I so upset? Were they trying to create some unexpected image? I don't like being outmaneuvered. Was I supposed to be funny? Me? This isn't entertaining for me at all.*

"Moo-Moondae? Are you okay?" Ahyeon asked.

"......Yeah."

I made up my mind.

The PD's voice rang out.

"Please find the culprit~"

It was late that night, well past midnight, when the production crew retired, leaving only the unmanned cameras behind.

*Dururuk.*

I quietly opened the front door and came out.

Of course, I wasn't alone.

"Uh... Moondae-hyung."

Kim Raebin, dressed in training clothes, hesitantly followed along with a confused look on his face.

"May I ask why you wanted me to come with you?"

"Get a shovel and follow me."

"...??"

"We're going to harvest carrots now."

*Just you wait, production team. I won't let you off that easily.*
