# Chapter 151 - ⟨Bespoke Singer⟩, IV

I scrolled down.

*It's already here.*

The trolling I'd expected ever since the first episode aired was already spreading online.

*They usually start by feeling around the atmosphere first.*

Disguising their words as compliments.

*Found it.*

I clicked on the text.

★$
[What do you think about Park Moondae coming out solo?]

He won ⟨Idol Inc.⟩, has plenty of fans, is a main vocalist, and can pull off any concept. That's rare for a male solo artist. ㅋㅋㅋ
$★

This kind of obvious bait was usually quickly dispelled by fans, but now things had turned a little differently.

TeSTAR was at the peak of its popularity thanks to the show, and viewers unfamiliar with idols were taking the bait sincerely.

★$
– I watched *Bespoke Singer*. I think he'd do great

– Does he want to go solo later?? I'm looking forward to it ㅋㅋ

– I want to listen to a lot of songs, that concept? Using that concept is cool too ㅠㅠ

– Honestly, I don't think he will fail
$★

It looked like good news, but people were much more eager for a solo debut than usual. That was the problem.

*It must have looked nice as firewood.*

Then, they would post something like this afterward.

★$
[There's one member of TeSTAR who obviously wants to go solo]

It's Park Moondae.

A month away from group activities due to 'health problems' before his solo variety appearance, and hardly any enthusiasm for group promotions

(_Notice of absence from W Live_)

(_Summer package video GIF_)

And yet he works so hard whenever he's filming aloneㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋ

(_Interview video_)

Fans had been uneasy for a month, and after his *Bespoke Singer* appearance, everyone was convinced he wanted to go soloㅋㅋ

It's obvious he only seems to give his all when he's promoting on his own.

His solo fans can't hide that they want the same thing.

(_screenshot_)

His solo fans are pretending to be non-fans and begging him to go solo. Please stop—we can tell it's you 🙏
$★

In that kind of post, the reaction screenshot of the well-knitted 'Park Moondae solo' post would be uploaded together.

And the main issue would be the attitude controversy.

*...I still feel uncomfortable.*

I knew that the content of when I was in a bad condition could become prey someday. This article was written well as if following a standard procedure.

But in fact, they weren't aiming at the general public opinion here.

*It doesn't matter if I go solo or not.*

It's not as if I'd already gone solo and flopped, giving them a reason to mock me. People were more likely to defend me right now.

It would be like this.

★$
– ??? Can't Park Moondae work solo? ㅋㅋ He's talented too, he's a real star ㅋㅋㅋㅋ

– Don't take out your inferiority complex on someone who's been sick. Is this how solo fans behave?

– Moondae's doing great. Why tear him down? Idols rest for a month or so to recover all the time.

– I would've rather gone solo rather than stay in a group that has such bad akgaes[1]{T/N: Akgae is a K-pop fandom term for a fan who likes only one member of a music group and hates the other members. Basically a solo fan.}. ㅅㅂㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋ

– TeSTAR is tired of every post that comes up. Please don't display it and keep the fight among yourselves.
$★

After the fight, the comments become longer and might be classified as popular posts, but public opinion didn't change.

So the target here was... a discord within TeSTAR fandom.

It was to stiffen the atmosphere of the fandom that liked the group itself, and to create an atmosphere that made it possible for members' malicious anti-fans to keep each other in check and disturb each other.

So another argument was brought up here.

The second episode of *Bespoke Singer* featuring Moondae aired on TeSTAR's first anniversary.

★$
– It's our 1st anniversary, but only posts about Park Moondae come up, it's really too much

– I'm not interested in whether you're the groom or the bride in May. Just don't harm the group's content, Gommeo

– Moondae looked so upset during our summer group activities. Why was he so cold to Chungwoo? Does he hate the leader?

– Couldn't you have set up the schedule and avoided it? I didn't know you would do this without thinking ㅋㅋㅋㅋ
$★

If I had known that it would have such a good response, I would have done so.

*...I was aiming to get a moderate amount of fun for the fans.*

I thought my show appearance would attract about as much attention as our anniversary at most. How could I know it would overshadow it?

Anyway, after a little more time passed by, the controversy would flow more comprehensively.

It was to encourage skepticism about the group's activities themselves.

★$
– Moondae, do you want to go solo? ㅋㅋㅋㅋ Where did your initial commitment go? It's an artist disease, it's a good thing I recognize it, please wake up

– Recently, Seon Ahyeon also shot a solo commercial and everyone's personal schedules increased, but it was Park Moondae who set the mood

– Let's go separately for things like this, make a separate SNS, really... I'm tired of hearing news from guys I don't care about
$★

If they really cared about our anniversary, they wouldn't be posting calls to split the group the very next day.

*So the ones who posted this were people who are not fans of the group in the first place.*

TeSTAR's image as a survival-show group built on individual voting had faded somewhat, but it hadn't vanished.

This meant that our identity as a group had been solidified through the album's worldview and various contents.

However, we still had a lot of solo stans, and if people stirred things up this way, there would be a lot of people who joined.

...That being said, it was a fact known to everyone who was interested.

Therefore, of course, it was a basic attitude of a member of society to prepare countermeasures.

I changed my smartphone screen to a phone call.

"Yes, hello. ...Yes. Thank you. It's nothing, but I have something to ask."

After a brief conversation with the company, I talked to the person sitting next to me.

"Raebin."

"Yes?"

Kim Raebin, who was busy listening to his own song, looked back in surprise.

"They said the interview will be released in the evening."

It was as scheduled.

"Ah!"

At that moment, Kim Raebin's face brightened. Um, he still looked like a gangster, though.

"It was a very meaningful interview, so I can't wait!"

"Yeah."

I leaned my back against the backrest.

Something like this wasn’t enough to make me anxious.

In the evening, when all kinds of aggro were running wild inside the fandom while on the outside, Park Moondae's reputation was still on the rise due to 'Groom in May', an interview was uploaded to WeTube.

★$

*Kim Raebin?*

*Why is Kim Raebin here?*

[※Titi: Wow! So the song in the finals was your work, Raebin-ssi?※]

[※※There's no end in sight!※※]

[※Raebin-ssi... is a master of TMI※]

[※Titi: Oooh!!※]

[※Titi: Wow! Then, when the album comes out, we will be able to listen to a slightly different recorded version of the song on the music site?※]

[※Moondae: That's correct.※]

[※Raebin: Objectively, Moondae-hyung has the best singing ability, so if you just compare simple songs, he has the upper hand.※]

[※Moondae: (hurriedly) Yes. Please look forward to the other songs in the upcoming album.※]

[※ㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋ※]

[※Titi: Ah~ You two are so funny! I laughed out loud!※]

[※Raebin: Thank you! (brightly)※]

[※Moondae: Thank you. (Tibetan fox)※]

[※Titi: I heard you were quite ill before appearing on Bespoke Singer...?※]

[※Moondae: Yes... I was unwell for about a month while we were working in the US. The members took great care of me, and I felt sorry for worrying them. That's why appearing on Bespoke Singer meant a lot. I finally had a chance to help the group...※]

[※Raebin: Hyung...※]

[※Titi: Raebin certainly seems touched! You're all right now, Moondae-ssi?※]

[※Moondae: Couldn't be healthier.※]

[※Strong Moon Puppy※]

I hadn't intended for that part of the interview to turn out this way.

I'd answered the included question sincerely for fans who'd worried during my absence. Somehow it came across as even more amusing.

Anyway, casually brushing off the topic, the cast and the interviewer continued to exchange questions and answers and ended the short 12-minute interview video.

[※Moondae: We'll return to this show with all of TeSTAR.※]

[※Raebin: I'm already looking forward to it. I think it'll be very harmonious.※]
