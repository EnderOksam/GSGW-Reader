# Chapter 131 - First Concert, III

'Loving the Wait', performed by the participants of ⟨Idol Inc.⟩, was a stage that had been steadily gaining views to this day with its unique oriental and sentimental atmosphere.

Of course, besides this song, TeSTAR had quite a few successful team-match stages.

Naturally, throughout the preparation of the concert, opinions on the stage they wanted to try again kept coming out.

– *I think the hero concept is the most suitable for raising the scale of the concert!* — Kim Raebin suggested.

– *T-To a New World... I-I think we could do it even better if we tried it again...!* — Seon Ahyeon suggested.

– *Vampire! I want to do it!* — Cha Eugene exclaimed.

– *Guys, do you want to try a VTIC sunbae-nim song? I think we can pull it off now...* — Ryu Chungwoo suggested.

– *...Calm down, hyung.*

However, most of them were eliminated at the discussion stage. Because the number of people had changed too much.

In the worst case, we could be accused of stealing the eliminated contestants' songs.

However, this song, which was actively pushed by Lee Sejin, didn't have such a concerning factor.

– *Haha! All our team members are here!*

Except for Gold 1, all the members of Moon Rabbit successfully debuted as TeSTAR.

In addition, Gold 1, who would debut soon, willingly gave permission via text message on the condition that it would be used in his own concert as well.

So TeSTAR was able to prepare the stage with excitement.

And now.

Seven figures wearing durumagis appeared at the front of the stage.

The sound of daegeum rang out.


An elegant and melancholy sound.

It was a sound that all the audience here had heard once.

At that moment, a figure gracefully turned back. Then, with the fluttering hem of the durumagi, he launched himself upside down into the air.

The figure leaped even higher, then tumbled forward onto the stage.

A splendid golden gongryongpo[1]{T/N: Gongryongpo = the clothes worn by the Kings in the Joseon Dynasty that have a dragon as their main decoration.} pattern shone on the fluttering navy blue durumagi.

"...!"

The audience didn't understand the situation for a while, but they soon started screaming when the detail of the rabbit mask was caught on the screen.

"Argh!"

"Crazy!"

After the cute unit stage, the long-missed rabbits had returned.

And with luxurious decorations too.

$p
♪ *At every turn of the road*\
*you'll find me standing, waiting for you* ♫
p$

Under the rabbit masks that the members flipped over, fancy jade norigaes[2]{T/N: Norigae is a typical, traditional Korean accessory used in Hanbok.} swayed.

The details of the costume, which were more dazzling than in Idol Inc, made beautiful circles and glittered whenever the tall figures moved.

TeSTAR preserved the strange atmosphere and slightly inhuman group choreography from back then.

No, rather, it became more intense.

$p
♪ *I'm loving the wait*\
*It's always long*\
*and it's always great!* ♫
p$

Thanks to the increase in the number of people, the choreography of the chorus became even more grandiose both in formation and overall movement.

$p
♪ *When this wait is over*\
*I know we'll meet again*

*#^#My heart is already racing#^#* ♫
p$

Park Moondae played the high notes in the chorus with a clear voice. On that face which was captured on the screen, the glitter from the unit stage was still slightly visible.

And the second verse, which was cut in ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ due to the performance time issue, continued without being cut this time around.

The new members of Moon Rabbit sang the new verses.

$p
♪ *Every moment that goes by again\
My focus is on you* ♫
p$

Cha Eugene grinned and executed fancy footwork, even the hem of his durumagi moving perfectly in time.

$p
♪ *I don't care if it's brief*\
*I already know it, I do* ♫
p$

The Moon Rabbits danced with infectious excitement, and the audience caught the same energy.

The concert hall was heated with joy.

And in the bridge part.

$p
♪ *You've been waiting for me, too*\
*So this clear sense of déjà vu tells me, ooh* ♫
p$

The moon rabbits ran to the extended stage and performed the verse, which had a sweet sensuality.

With a fan pulled out from the inside of the robe—

"Aaaah!!!"

"Ugh!"

The screams heard from the surrounding stands were terrifying.

They removed their durumagis, sweeping the fabric across the floor to reveal shirts beneath.

Harnesses decorated with glittering ornaments tied on by thread crossed their white shirts.

And finally, at the end of the last chorus.

As if waiting with excitement again for someone, the choreography with the sound of humming and the moving feet continued happily.

$p
♪ *Hum hu hu hum- huhu\
DDu-ru Du-ru Du Du* ♫
p$

The audience responded excitedly by humming along.

%^*Ddu-du-ru-du-du-du-du-du~*^%

It was a chorus of honest pronunciation without a single mistake.

In the unexpected cute situation, the moon rabbits ended the stage with bigger smiles than they had planned.

$p
♪ *Wait for me.* ♫
p$

Even Lee Sejin's last verse was sung in unison.

Lee Sejin smiled, crossed his arms, and leaned his head on the trunk of a huge tree that stuck out on the extended stage.

Even after the stage went dark, the cheers continued.

*Waaah!!!*

A prerecorded VCR should have played while they prepared for the next stage, so the audience waited expectantly.

However, even after a few seconds, the stage was still dark.

Some of the cheering spectators soon began to voice their doubts.

"Hmm?"

"Huh??"

"What?"

Right before people started making a fuss, wondering if it was a stage accident.

Suddenly, the stage was lit up.

*Thud.*

There was only one huge spotlight.

The spotlight was on the extended stage... It was illuminating a giant stump!

"...!!"

It was the spot where the tree used to be. But as if the body had been torn off, the whole thing disappeared, and only the stump remained.

And on top of that stump... two dark-haired people were sitting with their heads lowered.

"...?!"

A slow solo string instrument played out. The two slowly raised their heads.

Their faces were completely covered by rabbit masks.

However, the head part of the mask was partially damaged, and paint trickled down from that part.

"..."

In such an unusual atmosphere, the song began in front of a large audience who became quiet.

$p
♪ *Je te donne des fleurs que....* ♫[3]{T/N: (French)  I'm giving you flowers that...}
p$

With the accompaniment of the chanson that had become somewhat bizarre due to the extreme adjustment of pitch.

The third unit stage started without notice.

---

"Hey, Chungwoo-hyung and Raebin are really amazing, aren't they?"

Coming back down under the stage.

With people's help, Lee Sejin talked to me while changing his clothes in a hurry.

"Why all of a sudden?"

"What do you mean 'why?' Right now, without any choreography, they're just playing the song with their own arrangement and voice. Look at their guts. So cool~"

It was a compliment to those who were suddenly absent while he was just busy breathing.

*What are you thinking?*

But when I turned my head, I saw a camera for the behind-the-scenes of the concert.

*As expected.*

I see. What a consistent fellow. I replied with admiration.

"Well, yes."

Chungwoo must have considered all the risks of performing this song at an idol concert.

However, Kim Raebin probably didn't need to have the guts.

*The arrangement comes out well, and the live performance is good, so he must have thought it isn't a problem.*

And the arrangement really did come out well.

The decades-old chanson had been dismantled and rebuilt in an indie style that was sure to be a chart hit.

If the concert album came out, it would probably be the best song.

Lee Sejin grinned at my agreement.

"Right? Ah, of course, Moondae's guts were amazing too. The ribbon headband was amazing~"

"...Uh, yes."

There was a camera next to us. Let's not forget.

"Moondaemoondae, don't you have anything to say to me? Did you see the cowboy?"

"Yeah, that was cool."

"Please say it again more sincerely~"

I ignored the nonsense and listened to the sounds coming from the stage.

...A bright riff melody that was pleasant to the ears was placed on top of a gloomy beat.

$p
♪ *...Wounds that do not heal\
Tears harden like scabs\
Leaving traces down there...* ♫
p$

Then I heard a rap.

Apart from the iconic introduction and chorus, the entire song had been rearranged as rap.

That made the familiar tune even more addictive to listeners.

However, I wondered if the magic of the song, which only pushed ahead with rap, vocals, and atmosphere, could work at the concert.

*Though I think the atmosphere also played a part*

They decided to give it a strong narrative by using the set and lighting on the stage like a play.

Translations of the original chanson's lyrics appeared on the screen like ink splatters.

$p
♪ *To you dear*\
*I'm giving you flowers*\
*Today is a special day*\
*The streets are full of laughter* ♫

♪ *The trivial moments*\
*Piled up like dust.*\
*Shining like a jewel*\
*I still feel your breath* ♫
p$

The lyrics would have been fresh in the original song, but it met the gloomy beat and rap and came out with a different feeling.

*It will leave a strong impression.*

And in fact, thanks to those lyrics, in the first draft of the setlist we made, the next song was decided unanimously.

It was decided to take over the title track of the new album.

*The lyrics are similar.*

It was a good composition for the build-up.

However, the company rejected it. It was a matter of stamina and time.

– *Isn't Raebin-ssi and Chungwoo-ssi going to collapse at this rate?*

– *If we do this in a row, I think a mistake will happen while changing the outfit. It could be fatal for the performance of the new song, so shouldn't we avoid it?*

Each one of them was true, so I kept my mouth shut and agreed.

We'd added a short talk segment, but would talking really fit after a song with that kind of mood?

*I think it would be appropriate to have a talk after an exciting or lyrical song.*

It couldn't be helped because we only had a small number of songs to choose from, but it was a bit disappointing.

I clicked my tongue and finished my costume and hair maintenance, and then moved down the hallway. And soon after, those who finished their third unit stage joined.

"Whoo..."

"How was it?"

"Great, much better than I expected," Chungwoo said.

"I think we showed them everything we wanted to!" Raebin added.

The faces that answered were bright. It seemed the responses were good.

*The cheers were long.*

Without much worry, the concert was going smoothly.

"Go up! Be careful where you sit!"

"Yes!"

We were seated in seven chairs side by side and raised to the center stage via a lift.

In front of my eyes, countless lights that filled the huge space were shimmering and making waves.

*Aaaaaahhh!!!*

The cheers were like the sound of light.

*...This is no joke.*

It had been easier when I was singing and dancing; sitting here sober-minded made me feel dizzy.

If I think about the fact that over 10,000 people are sitting here, each holding a light... No, let's stop.

*It is difficult to even proceed with the script.*

I confirmed that Ryu Chungwoo, who would start the first talk, lifted the microphone.

"Nice to see you again, everyone! Are you enjoying the concert? Did you like our unit performances?"

"Ooh~ hyung, can you hear that? Everyone is enjoying it, they said it was so good!"

"So good!"

I put my mouth on the microphone, too.

"Which performance did you like the most?"

All kinds of words rushed in here and there like a tide.

"All of them!"

"I like them all!"

"Rabbit!"

"Wire!"

"Moondae! Moondae!"

Those expressions without any sharp words rang in my ears.

"..."

For a moment, I unintentionally shut up. ...My prepared answer came a beat late.

"...I like everything, too."

"Ah, Moondae is completely touched~"

"I'm touched, too!"

"This is rarer than you think, everyone."

After a brief moment of light teasing, the proceeding continued.

"Now then... It's time for the 'Things We Want to Say to TeSTAR' corner!"

And when I was about to enter the full-fledged content with the questions I had prepared.

Suddenly, the lights around the stage went dark.

"...?"

This wasn't planned.

I made eye contact with the slightly surprised members.

*What is this?*

*Is it an accident?*

At that moment, the light suddenly poured from behind.

It was light coming from the screen.

As I reflexively turned my head, an unexpected video was playing on the screen.

...It was TeSTAR's debut showcase.
