# Chapter 350 - The Weight of Secrets, I

During the flight home, I suddenly realized the problem we were facing.

*When should I tell him?*

We had no time.

We weren't in the middle of a tour, which meant I couldn't just make time whenever I wanted.

We were just starting promotions, and we'd be busy, with our schedule packed down to the minute.

*We can't even go out easily right now.*

With everything going on, would I even be able to find time to talk to Seon Ahyeon alone?

But the answer was easy to find.

"You can just use my room, right?" Keun Sejin said.

"...!"

"I have a single room~ You can talk to him at night after schedules!"

*Right.*

As it happened, the one person in the dorm who had a room to himself also knew everything about the situation. That meant we wouldn't have to leave the dorm to talk privately.

"I need to sleep too, of course, so if the conversation takes too long, I'm just going to use your room— Moondae? Are you listening?"

"Oh, thank you."

After adjusting a few details in our schedule on the plane, I decided to ask Ahyeon to talk as soon as we got back to the dorm...

"I'll stay in the room with you at first. No matter how much he likes you, we need to set the right mood if you want him to believe you~" Keun Sejin said.

He had a point.

Ahyeon seemed concerned that Keun Sejin and I shared some secret that we hadn't told him about.

It would be a good idea for Keun Sejin to be in the room, too.

We were taken straight to an ad shoot after arriving at the airport, and I sought out Ahyeon between takes.

"Ahyeon-ah."

"Yes?"

"After this is over and we go back to the dorm, would you be willing to talk with me and Sejin?"

"Yes! O-of course!"

"Uh, okay."

He seemed very eager. Thanks to that, we arranged a time to talk in an instant.

In the end, the three members who were the same age, at least physically, found themselves sitting together on the sofa in Keun Sejin's room.

"Come on, everyone, sit comfortably~" Keun Sejin said.

"Y-yes...!"

"......"

I stared at Keun Sejin's bed, where ten dolls designed to look like him were lined up neatly. How did he sleep with all those dolls staring at him?

Anyway, I turned my head and looked at Seon Ahyeon with the plushie heads behind me.

He seemed nervous, but also expectant.

*His eyes are glowing.*

Well, my secret was huge enough to warrant that kind of reaction.

Yes. Just last year, I never imagined I'd ever tell him this openly.

"Hmm."

I cleared my throat and opened my mouth.

"First of all... I'm sorry if you've been feeling a little left out lately."

"N-no! Don't say that! Of course, um, you don't *have* to tell me everything... Everyone's been working hard..."

"...?"

*Wait a minute.*

I turned to look at Keun Sejin. He seemed just as taken aback.

In the meantime, Seon Ahyeon blushed and continued to speak.

"I-I haven't exactly been impressive, you know, work-wise, or particularly charismatic... So I guess it wouldn't have been easy to share with me..."

"......"

*Hey. This guy...*

Apparently, Ahyeon thought Keun Sejin and I were working on some secret, complicated, and important work-related matter.

He seemed to think we hadn't told him, despite it being related to the group, because we didn't consider him trustworthy enough.

For a moment, I thought back on Seon Ahyeon's recent behavior and suppressed a groan.

*So is that why he was so eager to have his voice heard recently?*

Now the pieces fit together perfectly.

*Damn it.*

This wasn't good.

I resisted the urge to massage my forehead, forcing my hand to stay at my side.

*This is my fault.*

Chungwoo and Keun Sejin had both gotten caught up in this secret, so I'd fixated on the idea that Ahyeon must also be asking about it.

I'd assumed that if he wanted to hear anything from me, it had to be about my secret.

"......"

*Right...* In other words, there was no need to tell Seon Ahyeon about my identity.

*He'll never guess.*

At this point, I could just make something up about the label or the press and move on.

*Seon Ahyeon won't ask any questions.*

*...But is that okay?*

I clenched my fists.

Whenever I had to deal with system-related matters in the future, I'd have to leave him out again. Wouldn't that make him feel even more deceived?

In that case, I'd have been better off never bringing it up in the first place.

*At that point, I'd have no more excuses, even if he told me to stop playing games with him.*

The right thing to do was to explain that this wasn't work-related, and that I couldn't tell him because it was personal. There'd been a misunderstanding.

Seon Ahyeon might be a bit disappointed, but at least that would settle things.

*The problem is... I want to tell him the truth.*

*Funny, really.*

"......"

I opened my mouth almost impulsively.

"What's been going on... isn't actually work-related. I've never thought you were incompetent, either."

"What?"

"It's just my own personal problem."

I swallowed nervously. Oddly enough, I felt a bit of a rush.

"I'm not actually Park Moondae, but I've been living as him."

"...??"

"I couldn't go around telling people about it, so I kept it a secret."

"...What?"

Just as I'd told Keun Sejin, I began explaining everything to Seon Ahyeon.

"When I woke up a few years ago, I was in someone else's body."

Apparently, having told someone already meant I could explain everything much more succinctly this time.

*...This is really something I shouldn't be saying while being sober.*

"So, I can see these things that are kind of like holograms..."

"Pffftt!"

"Keun Sejin, if you laugh one more time, I'll send you out."

"Huh? It's my room, but Moondae wants to send me out... Sorry. I got that plushie from a fan, Moondae-nim. Be careful. Don't throw it at me."

There were a few points in the story when I desperately wanted a stiff drink, but I managed to get all the important details out in the end.

*I'll tell him about Cheongryeo and Goldie #2 later, if I have to.*

I didn't need to get into all the complicated details when I was talking to someone who had no idea what was going on.

Keun Sejin occasionally added a few details, and I finally finished explaining.

"...So now I'm doing fine."

"......"

I'd finished my story, but there was no reaction.

Feeling rather flustered, I spoke again.

"I know it's hard to believe. That's why it wasn't easy for me to come out and tell you. As for him," I said, pointing at Keun Sejin, "he sort of just found out."

"Yeah. You know how perceptive I am. Moondae tried to keep it from me, but I figured most of it out anyway," Keun Sejin said.

"......"

And when I raised my head, I saw the dazed look on Seon Ahyeon's face.

The guy looked back and forth between me and Keun Sejin a few times, then lowered his gaze to his hands.

"...I-I see."

"Yes," I said.

And there was silence.

"......"

"......"

The silence stretched on for much longer than I'd expected.

*Why...*

*Why isn't he asking me any questions?*

By the time a cold sweat formed on the back of my neck...

Seon Ahyeon opened his mouth again.

But he wasn't talking to me.

He was talking to Keun Sejin.

"...D-did he tell you the same thing, Sejin?"

"That's right. It hasn't been that long... A few months at most," Keun Sejin said.

"Wh-what did you think... when you first heard it?"

"Hmm?"

Keun Sejin shrugged.

"Is there any reason for Moondae to lie about something like this?"

"...!"

"I've known Moondae long enough to trust him... So I just accepted what he told me."

"......"

At Keun Sejin's words, Seon Ahyeon lowered his gaze to his hands again.

*What is he thinking?*

*Why isn't he asking me any questions?* No, of course it was pretty shocking... *But even so...*

I bit my tongue lightly.

*Don't jump to any extreme conclusions.*

It was natural that he'd want to know what someone who'd heard the secret first thought of it.

*Still...*

I clenched and unclenched my fists a few times, then decided to ask him outright.

"If you find this hard to believe—"

But I couldn't finish the sentence.

It was because Seon Ahyeon interrupted me.

"N-no. I believe you."

"...!"

He raised his head and smiled softly at me, looking a little shell-shocked.

"Yes... Th-that's right. Moondae is not the kind of person to lie about something like this... Of course, I know."

"I-it must have been tough for you... Moondae. All this time... you must have been through a lot. I can see that."

Somehow, I was speechless.

After clearing my throat a few times, I was able to answer calmly.

"Well, I made plenty of money and all that. We're successful. That's what matters."

"Yes..."

Seon Ahyeon answered quietly.

"Still... If you're having trouble, please tell me."

"Moondae, you must be feeling pretty reassured right now! You've got two people backing you up~ Oh, Chungwoo knows quite a bit, too."

"...Ch-Chungwoo-hyung too...?"

"Ah, that hyung had some family circumstances..." Keun Sejin explained.

I watched as Seon Ahyeon listened intently to Keun Sejin's explanation.

Concentration returned to his face.

"......"

*So... I guess that turned out okay?*

Keun Sejin chattered on as he quickly wrapped up the explanation.

"We were special from the start, being the same age and all. In the end, the two of us are the only ones who really know how Moondae feels~ You'd better appreciate us, Moondae."

I ended up laughing, too. After thinking for a moment, I held out my hand to Seon Ahyeon.

"Yeah. Thanks for listening, Ahyeon."

His hand trembled slightly as he took mine. It was a little cold, but his grip was firm.

"...Yes!"

I could see the joy in his eyes as he answered me. That was enough.

Ahyeon's attitude toward me hadn't changed the next day, and I think I was relieved by that.

---

A few days later, a somewhat unusual event came up.

"Well, tomorrow is the day we go to Yeonhee University."

"Oh!"

We would be performing at a college festival, something we hadn't done for nearly two years.

Although the fees we charged for these performances had risen sharply last year, we'd been attending college festivals regularly since our debut.

Our appearance on ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ and our successful debut track meant that even ordinary college students knew enough about us to enjoy our music.

And college festival performances were fun events, so the audience's reactions were always fun, too.

"Oh~ It's been a while. This sounds fun!" Keun Sejin said.

"I know. We're performing outdoors, right?"

That got the conversation going, and before long we were talking about university itself. Well, that was how small talk in the van usually went.

"...If I get a chance later, I would like to attend a lecture."

"I see. I don't regret taking online university classes to keep up with my work, but I'm still curious about attending classes in person," Kim Raebin said.

And Cha Eugene shrugged.

"I was offered a college scholarship, but I came to Korea anyway. I'm pretty amazing, right?"

"Wow."

"That's really great."

No one would be able to refute it. Kim Raebin was also nodding his head.

"Why did you do that?"

"Um, because dancing was more fun, I guess?" Cha Eugene said. "You know, I just wanted to," he added in English.

Given how successful things had turned out, no one could really criticize him for that decision.

Even I, who had supported myself by selling fancams while trying to keep my scholarship, had ended up with a job completely unrelated to my major.

"Hyung! Did you ever want to go to college?" Cha Eugene asked.

"Not at all."

I had no interest in attending college twice.

That reminded me of something else I'd be forced to go through twice—military service, perhaps even on active duty this time. But I put that thought aside for now.

*Phew...*

*I envy the gold medalist and the foreigner.*

"Okay~ Let's just look around the campus! We can do it secretly!"

"Well... If we have time," I said.

I looked at the festival listed on the schedule in my hand with a strange feeling as I listened to Eugene ramble.

There was only one reason.

"Moondaemoondae, is this the university you graduated from?"

"...Yes."

When Cha Eugene stopped slapping me on the shoulder from the back seat, Keun Sejin whispered to me.

This was the university I attended.

*I guess this is my first time going there in this body.*

I mean, there'd been no reason to go there before.

Our agency simply chose college festivals based on how much they offered and how close they were to our other scheduled events.

This time, it seemed that this college had offered the largest sum.

*It's one way to spend the students' tuition money, I guess.*

This college had plenty of money, but they still raised tuition every year.

Raising an eyebrow, I flipped through the schedule. I was squished between Keun Sejin, who was peering over my shoulder, and Seon Ahyeon.

Keun Sejin whispered to Ahyeon, "Hey, Ahyeon~ Moondae used to attend this university."

"...I-I see."

Seon Ahyeon asked me cautiously.

"How... does it feel?"

"...Well, I don't feel much of anything, to be honest."

I smiled at him.

"It's been a long time since I graduated, and the campus has been renovated since then. To be honest, it doesn't feel like a big deal."

Keun Sejin smiled.

"Got it, Hyung."

"......"

*That's enough, you little bastard.*

"Hehehehehe..."

I listened to Keun Sejin stifle his laughter, his shoulders shaking, and felt the urge to sigh. Seon Ahyeon smiled awkwardly.

*Well, what does it matter as long as we perform well?*

This would be our first event in Korea in a while. I cracked my neck.

We would be the closing act.

If time allowed, we would be able to perform at least three or four encores.

*Though I don't think we'll have enough time for that many...*

But, as often happened, things didn't go as expected.
