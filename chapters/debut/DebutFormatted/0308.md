# Chapter 308 - TeSTAR Songwriting Camp, III

The second day of the songwriting camp.

Arrangements were carried out amidst laughter.

"Hahahaha!"

"That sounds so stupid!"

Combining children's songs and idol songs turned out to be even sillier than I'd expected, resulting in all sorts of bizarre arrangements.

Even the songs were chosen through a lottery.

*We'll never disclose them.*

The moment people found out we'd made these, there'd be unnecessary controversy over comments like, "Are you mocking your juniors?"

I played my arrangement combining 'Dreaming Bear' and 'Hacker' for the others and turned it off without regret.

$p
♪ *Teddy bear~*\
*Go-go-go bear!* ♫
p$

It was the strangest piece of trash I'd heard in a while.

However, Kim Raebin produced quite interesting results here as well.

"Oh~ Raebin, is this using the beat from that Golden Age song?"

"That's right. I borrowed the beat from the verse to give it a more sophisticated feel!"

"...So, this is what 'Butterfly' sounds like when it's remixed?" Bae Sejin asked in puzzlement.

Kim Raebin's mash-up was unique and wonderful enough to warrant Bae Sejin's reaction.

Who would've thought an ordinary children's song like 'Butterfly' could sound so trendy?

*I'd intended for all of us to make a mess of it and take some pressure off Raebin.*

Our failures only seemed to get Kim Raebin more excited... Well, that was rather good.

I looked around the songwriting camp, which had turned into a comedy workshop, and got up from my seat.

"Moondaemoondae, where are you going?"

"To check on the chicken."

I was going to check on the whole chicken soup we were making in the pressure rice cooker for lunch.

But an unexpected guy caught up with me.

"I'm coming too!"

It was Cha Eugene.

*I thought he liked Baeksuk[1]{T/N: Baeksuk/백숙: dishes made by boiling or steaming meat or fish to be cooked thoroughly without seasonings.} much less than fried chicken.*

He must have been excited to be on a trip. I shrugged my shoulders and headed for the stove, with Eugene trailing behind me.

The seemingly untouched ginseng liquor on display on the nearby veranda caught my eye, reminding me of my drink with Raebin at dawn.

*Who would've thought I'd use breath freshener spray for something like this?*

We'd changed our clothes, brushed our teeth, and even used breath spray, so no one would know we'd been drinking. There was no way that little bottle would get anyone drunk.

*...Wait a minute.*

That was true for me, but what about the others?

"The more I listen, the more I think Sejin-hyung made an excellent choice using only the most addictive part of the children's song's melody!"

"Hahaha! Really? Raebin just wants to keep listening~?"

"Yes, very interesting!"

"......"

Could Kim Raebin have become so talkative because he was a little tipsy?

*Well... If he's relaxing, that's a good thing.*

After all, no one knew why he was so relaxed. I walked past the ginseng liquor on the other side of the glass.

Just then, Cha Eugene whispered.

"Hyung, did you drink that?"

"...!!"

*How did that brat know...? No, I had to deny it for now.*

"I don't know what you mean."

"You're lying! I don't believe you."

Cha Eugene smiled and pointed at the refrigerator.

[In the morning, I saw an empty bottle in the refrigerator. Isn't that the strange liquor over there?]

"......"

[I found it while looking for Coke.]

*So that's how he found out.*

It wasn't surprising, since he was probably the most gluttonous of us all.

When I looked at him, wondering if he was about to ask for another chicken leg, Cha Eugene unexpectedly shrugged his shoulders.

And he glanced at Kim Raebin.

[Did you drink while talking to him?]

"Yes."

[I knew it! No wonder he seemed more comfortable today. I heard alcohol makes people more talkative.]

"Who said that?"

[Hm, my grandmother?]

I was beginning to see why that guy and Kim Raebin had gotten along so well since their former agency. I briefly remembered what Kim Raebin had told me about his grandmother praising ginseng wine, then dismissed the thought.

Cha Eugene lowered his voice and spoke.

"I like seeing how Kim Raebin is so comfortable."

I felt rather proud of him for saying that.

"You must've been really worried. Why not tell him yourself?"

Cha Eugene shrugged.

[We are the same age. It's a matter of pride.]

That's quite a thing to say for someone who went crying to Kim Raebin during ⟨Idol Inc.⟩.

*Or is that a different matter entirely?*

Anyway, I just nodded and checked the pressure cooker with the chicken.

Cha Eugene caught up with me again and this time raised his thumb.

"Hyung is great."

*The Baeksuk?*

[Hyung's been helping Kim Raebin compose more music for a long time now.]

"That was all him."

Honestly, a guy who could turn nursery rhymes into pop songs would've done just fine wherever he went.

However, Cha Eugene crossed his arms.

[No. Our old company wasn't very smart! Kim Raebin definitely had talent, but they rarely let him compose.]

"Hmm."

[But hyung trusted him from the beginning, and Kim Raebin often said how grateful he was for that.]

I'd thought they were always yelling at each other, even when I wasn't around. I didn't know they had conversations like that.

It was so heartwarming that I felt embarrassed that I'd trusted him largely because I'd seen his Maestro trait in the status window.

However, Cha Eugene's story did not end there.

[Athletes and artists are bound to go through slumps at some point. So, it's not that Kim Raebin fell into a slump because hyung put pressure on him.]

"......"

I stared at him.

[I'm sure Kim Raebin would want you to know that, so I'll say it for him. Since we're BFFs!]

...I hadn't expected him to follow me into the kitchen to encourage me.

He patted me on the back, though I wasn't sure what he meant by that gesture.

[And look at that. Even hyung's magical little drink worked. Honestly, I'm impressed.]

*Huh.*

I looked at the smiling guy, glanced at Kim Raebin once again, and answered briefly.

"Thanks."

"Hehe."

[But don't tell anyone else about the alcohol.]

[Alright! I'll keep a secret for my friends.]

Cha Eugene spoke with his eyes shining.

"But I want to drink with you too! That's the condition."

*The ginseng wine?*

I didn't think he'd like it very much. Maybe the color made him think it would taste like some kind of sweet dessert wine.

"When?"

"NOW! No, how about tonight?"

*Come on.*

I smiled and answered.

"Okay. Let's drink."

Cha Eugene smiled and held out his fist.

Then, a gloomy voice interrupted.

"Drink what?"

"...!"

*Why was he popping out of there again?*

It was Bae Sejin.

He looked at Cha Eugene's raised fist with a frown, and then his face went slack with shock.

"You...! You drank ginseng wine!"

*Damn.*

"No, that..."

"Kim Raebin! Did you drink that with Park Moondae?"

"Ah yes! Together in the morning... ah."

Kim Raebin replied with an embarrassed face, probably remembering what he'd said about keeping it a secret so the other members wouldn't feel left out.

"Sorry. I should have restrained myself until evening... I'll make sure to set some out for dinner!"

"That...!!"

Bae Sejin's face turned red, but he held back whatever he was about to say.

*Oh. I'm impressed.*

"...Okay."

"Alright!"

"What's this~ What's going on?" Keun Sejin asked.

"We talked about drinking ginseng wine in the evening!" Raebin said.

"G-ginseng...?"

"Ah, that's what you guys were talking about."

The members had odd looks on their faces, but they didn't bother to argue.

I'd been off alcohol for a while, and they probably didn't want to come down on Kim Raebin right now.

Would they just let it slide like this?

Well, Bae Sejin seemed to have guessed there was more to our morning drinking. And I didn't feel very excited about drinking anymore, so it was okay.

*Then I'd naturally get to have another drink that evening—*

At that time, Bae Sejin grabbed my shoulder and spoke.

"You don't get any more."

"......"

*Hey!*

"Look, hyung! He's fine now. A little wine is no problem!"

"That's not wine, it's hard liquor!"

In the end, Cha Eugene made a sad face and whispered.

"Hyung, I'm sorry."

*Whatever...*

I didn't get another drop of alcohol until the moment we left the cabin.

Still... Well, overall it was a good camp.

When we left the cabin, Kim Raebin's laptop was back in his hands, not in my bag.

* * *

After returning from the songwriting camp where we were cut off from the outside world for 3 days and 2 nights, we focused entirely on our schedule to prepare for the concert.

Of course, that didn't mean there wasn't any free time.

I occasionally checked WeTube for reactions to the *127 Section* arrangements that Keun Sejin had uploaded anonymously, background images and all.

In conclusion, the response was better than expected.

Perhaps because the series had so many dedicated fans worldwide, people looking for covers kept finding and watching the videos. It seemed to have quietly spread through word of mouth.

*I'd expected as much, given the quality.* We didn't bury it on purpose, and we'd included strong keywords in the description to make the videos easy to find in searches.

And, of course, the theme song arranged by Kim Raebin received the best response.

Kim Raebin had polished ours to the point of practically remaking them, but the track he'd made himself was of much better quality.

And the reaction... People mainly talked about the arranger being a genius.

I deliberately had Kim Raebin read those comments. 

In fact, we read some of them together.

"That's what they're saying."

"Yes, uh, it's a bit embarrassing, but I'm thankful...!"

I hoped reading the comments would help Kim Raebin feel certain that he'd escaped his slump.

At first glance, these comments resembled the public's praise that had *caused* Kim Raebin's slump in the first place, but there was one crucial difference.

*They don't know who he is.*

There were no lofty expectations or background knowledge influencing their reactions.

It was just pure, unbiased praise for the arrangement, so it could serve as motivation instead.

And this time, even if we got cursed at, Raebin didn't have to fear the repercussions. It was anonymous anyway.

Besides, Raebin had room to shrug off criticism because he'd made the arrangements with us beginners.

*Whether it fails or does well, nothing more will come of it.*

In addition, it helped rekindle Raebin's interest in composing and kept the other guys interested, too.

A few times, I had seen Bae Sejin looking for feedback on his songs.

"...What, what!"

"It's nothing."

Well, I also looked for reactions to my own arrangements, so I wasn't one to talk.

Anyway, as we prepared for the concert, we could keep an eye on feedback without feeling pressured to make anything of it.

Maybe thanks to that, even though it was the first concert he didn't participate in arranging, Kim Raebin seemed to concentrate well without unnecessary thoughts.

Some people even took advantage of the opportunity.

"Moondaemoondae."

"...?"

Just after choreography practice ended, as we were about to head back to the dorm, Keun Sejin came over holding a tablet PC.

"Hmm... We haven't touched the music for this concert because of Raebin and all that, right?"

"Yes."

"So we'll get the arrangements sooner... Why don't we put more energy into other aspects with the extra time we have?"

*That sounds good.*

"In what way?"

"In this way~"

Lee Sejin turned on the screen and showed the setlist.

It wasn't the existing one, but a version he'd revised himself.

"...!"

"Not bad, huh?"

He was right.

I answered without hesitation.

"It's good. Shall we bring it up at the meeting tomorrow?"

"Okay~"

Keun Sejin smiled.

*He's productive.*

He shared the file with me while I drank some water.

But that wasn't the end of the day's events.

"Um... we got a comment."

On the way back to the dorm from practice, Bae Sejin showed me something on his phone.

It was the comment section on our anonymous arrangement channel, which was becoming a familiar sight.

It received so many likes and rose to the top...

It was a formal invitation.

"...Is that the person who contacted us for our game collaboration?"

Without even intending to, we'd set a huge ruse in motion.
