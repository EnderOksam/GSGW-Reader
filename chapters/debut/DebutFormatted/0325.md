# A New Record

K-pop. A newly coined word that mainly refers to Korean idol music.

In fact, it wasn't a term Koreans had coined themselves.

*I think foreigners started using it when the Korean Wave began to spread.*

So, if the new mission was to 'Set A New K-pop Record', there was a good chance only records that were recognized overseas as well would count.

To confirm this with the status window himself, I asked him by text.

★:
I've never thought of it that way, but I think you're right. Wow-
:★

I stared at the screen.

*Don't tell me any stupid record would've counted, but the requirements were updated because I asked?* I turned off my smartphone screen, disgusted by the possibility.

After returning to the dorm, I was sitting on my own bed.

Was I going to sleep? No. As soon as the choreography for one of our next special stages was ready, we'd have to go to the practice room. The end of the year wasn't the season for slacking off.

My roommate, who was also sitting on his bed waiting, spoke up. 

"If we have to set a new record, we'll have to do better than everyone who came before us."

"Yes."

He'd said, 'I'll think about it,' in the car, and apparently he'd really been doing so. What a diligent guy.

His next suggestion was very much in character.

"Why don't we aim for a category that's only recently been created? It'll be easier to set a new record that way."

It was a practical suggestion that spoke to his experience as an athlete. I cupped my chin in one hand.

"Let's see. First of all, in Asia, most records are held by..."

*VTIC owned it all. Fuck.*

"Haha. Our seniors have been working hard, haven't they?"

"Yes."

They really had. They'd held first place for dozens of weeks, sold over a million albums, and done a dome tour. It seemed every conceivable record had already been set by them.

While TeSTAR focused on securing a domestic presence, VTIC had gone ahead and solidified its presence overseas... They were practically running out of new records to set.

"The same goes for South America."

"Yes."

In regions where the K-pop market was strong, they already held impressive records. Trying to beat one of them before next summer would be a reckless gamble.

And the records of our predecessors weren't the only obstacles.

"And there are a few groups going strong, with good results."

In terms of global performance, quite a few groups other than VTIC were doing better than TeSTAR.

Ryu Chungwoo nodded as well, probably remembering what he heard in the company briefing.

"Well, it's difficult. Even if we do our best to put on the best stage yet, there's no guarantee the results will reflect that."

*That's just how it is in the entertainment industry.*

"It's a big question of timing and luck."

"Yes. There are a lot of similarities, but I think that's where it differs from sports."

Ryu Chungwoo smiled wryly.

"I tried to set records as an athlete too, but entertainers don't win or lose based on their own performance alone... You have to win people's hearts. That's what makes it complicated."

"That's right."

I nodded, and Ryu Chungwoo seemed lost in thought.

He was taking the conversation more seriously than I'd expected. It was probably best to ease the tension a little.

I shifted the topic slightly.

"Speaking of which, what records did you try to set?"

"Hm? Haha. Nothing much. It's the kind of thing every archer tries for. The Asian Games, the World Championships, and three Olympic gold medals. Being the youngest to achieve all of that is usually the goal."

"......"

*Three gold medals?*

What kind of industry was Korean archery?

*Why is he talking about gold medals as if it's like winning a local league?*

That ecosystem really was beyond me.

"That's amazing."

"Not at all. I failed. I wasn't selected for the World Championships."

Ryu Chungwoo grinned as if a bit embarrassed.

"Still, I did manage to set a record for being the youngest to win Olympic gold."

"That's still amazing."

"Haha, it'll be broken soon enough. A lot of young athletes have made it to the final selection..."

"...!"

*Wait a minute.* Something about his words had sparked an idea.

*The youngest, huh?*

Suddenly, a new path opened up in my mind.

*...Yes. That kind of approach is possible.*

Rather than simply aiming for the best results overall, we could aim for a more specific record category.

One that was widely recognized enough for people to take it seriously.

*Okay, I've got it.*

That was the direction I needed to take. I nodded.

"Anyway, I'll think about it some more. Sorry I can't give you any useful ideas right now."

"Not at all. Thank you. I think you just gave me an idea."

"...? Really? If so, that's good."

He looked puzzled over what sort of idea he'd given me, but Ryu Chungwoo simply laughed it off.

I added a word just in case.

"Don't worry. I won't try anything that could hurt TeSTAR's future as a group."

"What makes you think I'd worry about that...? Alright."

Ryu Chungwoo accepted my reassurance.

It was the same calm reaction he'd had when I'd said, 'We have to win the grand prize,' last time. 

"......"

Come to think of it, he'd never asked why I wanted to win the grand prize so desperately. He must have guessed that asking would make me uncomfortable.

*What a guy.*

It was the same as in the car earlier. Uncharacteristically, I'd been a little moved by the situation, so I'd simply been grateful for his acceptance and moved on...

Looking back, I wondered how the hell this guy understood the situation.

*He seemed like the type who didn't believe in the supernatural at all.*

He didn't even believe in ghosts.

Even if I'd laid some groundwork by implying before that something strange was going on that I couldn't talk about, wasn't this too unrealistic to chalk up to trust in Park Moondae alone? 

*It doesn't look like he has any ulterior motives.*

I rubbed the back of my neck and sighed.

*Whatever. I'll just ask him.*

"Hyung."

"Hm?"

"About what hyung said in the car earlier... I'm curious why you're so calm about my situation."

"......"

"Honestly, it probably seems completely absurd."

I was referring to the idea that Ryu Gunwoo and Park Moondae had switched bodies.

Ryu Chungwoo smiled awkwardly.

"Well, I did try to come up with other explanations... None of them seemed to fit."

"......"

"At first, I wondered if you two had influenced each other so much that you shared a lot of things. I thought I should take that into account."

I remembered what he had suggested to me: 'You can speak comfortably'.

*Is that why?*

But the explanation did not end there.

"Then, I met 'Ryu Gunwoo' today... And realized there was more to it."

Ryu Chungwoo concluded simply.

"You two were very different from each other... As if you'd swapped bodies."

"......"

"Then I remembered what you said the last time we climbed the mountain, like lightning. Remember?"

*– I was just... doing what I could.*

Ryu Chungwoo laughed.

"If I take that at face value, it answers all my questions. Everything fits perfectly."

"......"

"What you know, how you deal with problems, your trauma... And the reactions of both you and Ryu Gunwoo during our meal."

Ryu Chungwoo smiled wryly.

"And why you got angry with me and avoided me before... That too."

"......"

"It made so much sense."

*The summer package.*

Had he already thought about why I'd lost it when he spoke about his car accident?

I pressed my lips together, and Ryu Chungwoo continued quietly.

"Back then, it seemed like I was the one being understanding of you, but... In fact, you were being generous with me too. Right? I'm sorry."

"I... No."

I barely managed to speak.

"I'm the one who should apologize for this," Ryu Chungwoo said.

Ryu Chungwoo laughed.

"Stubbornness seems to run in the family."

"......"

I stared at him.

"Anyway, that's how I figured it out. Of course, I still don't know how it works... But I don't need to know. That's not important."

After all, this guy... Had followed his instincts and used logical thinking.

On top of that, he wasn't easily unsettled, so it seemed like everything had fallen into place smoothly, layer by layer.

*Besides, from what he's saying, he had a lot of information to work with.*

In the end, I laughed too.

"It's embarrassing that you're always explaining yourself when I can't do the same in return. Thank you."

"Hey, I told you. It's okay."

Ryu Chungwoo grinned, but soon his expression changed as if he remembered something.

"Well, come to think of it, there was more to it than that."

"What?"

*What else was there?*

"Actually... I ended up overhearing something without meaning to."

Ryu Chungwoo looked a little embarrassed.

"Earlier, when I left the room at the restaurant to make a call, I stood outside the door for a moment to make sure I wouldn't interrupt anything when I went back inside..."

*Wait a minute.*

"...So you mean..."

"Yes. He called you hyung very naturally."

So had that been what confirmed it?

After all, my ominous hunch hadn't been wrong. I saw it now.

The situation I'd feared was exactly what had happened. I felt a headache coming on, but soon got over it.

*Anyway, it worked out well.*

It was a blessing in disguise. I looked at Ryu Chungwoo, impressed.

And I spoke abruptly.

"I look forward to working with you."

"Haha, same here."

And just like that, I had a partner in crime I didn't have to hide my identity from.

Not long after that, our bedroom door opened, and the other guys all poked their heads inside.

"Everyone~ The choreography is here~"

It was practice time again.

"Yeah? One second."

Ryu Chungwoo stood up immediately and added something to me.

"Let's come back and continue talking about that K-pop record."

"Yes."

I could probably figure it out alone, but I needed to keep discussing it with him unless I intended to do everything by myself.

So I nodded without complaint, but someone else spoke up after Ryu Chungwoo left.

It was Keun Sejin.

"Moondaemoondae, what's the K-pop record?"

"We were debating what our next goal would be."

It was a perfectly natural and constructive topic to discuss with the leader, wasn't it?

"Oh~ You seem to have become much more comfortable with Chungwoo-hyung after you became roommates, Moondae?"

Lee Sejin grinned, then pretended to cry.

"Heukheuk, only the two of you went out earlier, so Sejin feels so left out..."

*How did this quick-witted bastard know we'd snuck out?*

"But you were on the phone with the variety show MC at the time, weren't you?"

"Ah, if our main vocalist and best friend Moondae had asked, I would've hung up immediately and come running~"

He didn't even pause and spoke naturally. 

I was about to hit him on the back, but hesitated at a thought that came to mind.

*...Come to think of it, I told him I'd explain my situation to him when I could.*

...Ryu Chungwoo had already found out before I could do that.

"......"

"Moondae?"

Was it only right to explain it to this guy without being asked?

But this... He hadn't found out anything on his own, so I didn't know if it was a good idea to tell him outright. Wasn't the risk too great?

If I told him myself and things didn't pan out well, it wasn't like I could just start over from scratch.

*Besides, once he sinks his teeth into something, he's the sort of guy who needs to know every detail.*

Just thinking about explaining everything to a punk who didn't even know what a status window was made me cringe with embarrassment. Whether he'd believe me or not was a separate matter.

I was silent for a moment, but then I held back a sigh and opened my mouth.

"...Then, if you have some time later, let's go out together, just the two of us."

Still, if I got a chance, I'd have to give it a shot. If Ryu Chungwoo remained the only one in the loop, things could get awkward quickly the moment someone let something slip by accident.

"...! Moondae finally wants to get out of the dorm! Good, good~ I'll look into it!"

*No, punk.*

Lee Sejin chuckled and thumped me on the back, and I groaned as I went out the front door with the members.

*I have more work to do...*

How was I going to explain this utterly surreal situation to that guy?

*I'll keep an eye on him and slowly decide how much to tell him.*

I put that thought aside and returned to my original worry.

*So, if I want to make setting a new K-pop record for this mission easier...*

1. Pioneering new markets

2. Subdividing an existing record category to create a new one

Those were the two options.

*Even after subdividing the category, it would have to be impressive to the public, have influence in a large market, and still leave enough of the pie for us to take...*

I unconsciously muttered one possible answer.

"The US."

"Are we going to the US?" Cha Eugene asked.

"No."

It was just one of the candidates.

"Boo..."

Cha Eugene had reacted in a flash, but now his shoulders slumped as he walked past me and got into the car. It couldn't be helped.

*There are plenty of cases of idols trying to break into the US and failing.*

Something else needed to come first.

*I'll start with an analysis.*

I'd gather data on which markets TeSTAR had a chance in, establish a method, then target the most promising one. 

We were never going to rush blindly into the US.

And to do that... We needed to have complete control over planning our own activities.

I cracked my neck.

*It's about time.*

And I remembered the contents of the contract I had renewed with the general manager.

!pb
If TeSTAR wins the grand prize at one of the annual awards ceremonies within the next year, you will have to fully support TeSTAR's establishment of a new independent label.
pb!

We'd already won the grand prize. The groundwork had been laid.

So the time had come for TeSTAR to establish its independent label.
