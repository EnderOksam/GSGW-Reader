# Chapter 138 - ⟨Copy Cat⟩, III

*Wow, it’s 22 million!*

It was 20 million when she saw it a while ago, but even though it had only been a week since its release, the number of views was accumulating quite fast.

The time it took to reach 10 million was also a new record for the group.

*I’m watching it again, aren’t I?*

The graduate student shrugged, thinking that it was understandable.

Because TeSTAR’s music video this time was a bit different from the previous ones.

“Ah, here we go!”

At that moment, the commercial ended and the music video started.

An antique gramophone covered in dust and cobwebs was captured on the screen.

A white hand came out of a corner and set the phonograph needle on the record.

The cobweb snapped, and a slow, crackling melody began to play out.

It was lo-fi jazz with its sound quality lowered on purpose.

A romantic and sad piano rang out.

Before she knew it, the screen changed to the entrance of a mansion.

Although it was old, the structure of the mansion had a trace of luxury and displayed tremendous visual beauty in the captured frame.

And in the middle.

Under the dusty, glowing chandelier, the focus was on the figure of seven people sitting in formation.

The song began.

♪ *Time slowly sinks*\
*Into the deep night*\
*I don’t want to escape* ♫

♪ *It’s quiet now*\
*No songs or dreams*\
*I want you to forget everything*\
*Your Midnight* ♫

The choreography unfolded.

The tone of modern dance and waltz stood out. It was an elegant and complex choreography with a lot of movements using the whole body to support each other.

The long sleeves swayed.

There were no repetitive movements that were easy to follow or memorize. It was a move solely aimed at making people watch with their mouths open.

The structured movements continued for a while under the chandelier lights.

♪ *Even if the clock ticks and calls me*\
*It can never pull me away*\
*I’m here, your midnight*\
*Your Midnight* ♫

The video changed the background from an old and luxurious entrance hall to a neat study flooding with rain, a huge dining table lined with rusty bird cages, and a bathroom full of red candles.

Each time, their hair and clothes changed shape and color to blend perfectly with the background.

The visuals were crazy.

And the background suddenly changed to a bedroom where a golden-blue butterfly fluttered around.

The seemingly endless change of clothes, which was based on colorful suits, also came to an end.

They were now wearing classic lightweight shirts.

Between their shoulder blades, she could see dark brown cranks protruding.

♪ *Spreading Midnight*\
*The distorted focus...*\
*Shaking, squeezing time with dread* ♫

The choreography of the last chorus fully bloomed.

Butterflies scattered and fluttered each time the formation changed. Once the boys held their positions, the butterflies settled on the cranks like birds perched on spinning wheels.

The shadows cast by the butterfly wings stretched across the floor.

♪ *Overflowing Midnight*\
*Its distorted shape*\
*Torn and a new day comes* ♫

There were no significant cutscenes, acting, or overarching narrative.

Consisting solely of choreography and close-up shots of the member singing, the video swallowed up time in an instant.

It was a music video with gorgeous, mesmerizing visuals and a song that fit them perfectly.

♪ *When I met you*\
*Midnight* ♫

The lyrics ended like that. All sounds stopped for a moment.

The last accompaniment resumed faintly.

While the piano and contrabass were playing, a white hand appeared just like in the first scene. However, this time, the camera followed the arm up to reveal his face.

The owner of the white hand was Seon Ahyeon.

Getting out of the choreography formation, Seon Ahyeon headed to the longcase clock on the other side of the living room.

Then he tore off the lower right wing of the preserved butterfly hanging above the clock.

In the glass beneath the wing, a horizontal crack was revealed.

Above the longcase clock that was captured from the front, an elegant cursive caption appeared, and the video ended.

*Whoo!*

It was great to see it again!

The graduate student couldn’t control the corners of her mouth and turned to the last verse where a close-up of Moondae was captured.

The flushed face of Park Moondae staring slightly upward with a blue butterfly fluttering near his temple was the best.

And there was the finishing touch.

*Choker on his neck!*

Although the song and atmosphere were completely different, this was better because it reminded her of the second team match of ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ when she first saw Park Moondae.

The graduate student already did it, but she screenshotted the scene one more time.

“Ah, this music video is really good! The song is just nice to listen to!”

It was her taste to enjoy enchanting visual beauty and easy-to-listen songs for 4 minutes without question marks popping up in her head.

And that point was revealed right away from the thumbnail.

Originally, TeSTAR used epic and meaningful thumbnails such as hands, landscapes, and props since ‘Magical Boys’.

However, this time, they openly put a close-up of a member’s upper body on the thumbnail.

It was Kim Raebin.

“Mmm.”

It was a pity that it wasn’t Moondae. Since Kim Raebin hadn’t made a good first impression on the fans, the grad student also found the choice of him for the thumbnail a little strange.

But the cut itself was an art.

*...Raebin is handsome.*

The cut of slightly sweaty Kim Raebin with a corset choker on his neck and a blue butterfly under his chin was great.

And he was a perfect match for this song.

It was a very languid and extravagant concept, so Kim Raebin, who had a slightly decadent impression, suited it well.

The music video also set the worldview aside and focused on a bombardment of sight and hearing.

*Ah, it changed over.*

While she was lost in her thoughts for a moment, the video that played again ended, and a related video was played next through automatic playback.

It was the first stage of ‘Midnight, and After’ at the concert.


[Aaaagh!]

[Uaagh!]


She couldn’t hear the song well because of the scream.

*Ah, right! All the videos were like this!*

She chuckled as she recalled the situation at the time.

The fancam her fansite master friend had filmed and sent her had neatly edited sound, so the graduate student had forgotten how loud the crowd had actually been.

According to her friend, the concept photos released right before the concert hadn’t given a single hint about the new song, which made the crowd even more excited.

*Hm, now that I think about it, they were just pretty pictures, nothing more.*

Right.

For a more dramatic first-stage impact, TeSTAR and the company accepted Park Moondae’s opinion and shot slightly boring concept photos and teasers.

They simply took good-looking shots in nice suits in front of the mansion, then picked the best ones for the concept photos.

The graduate student didn’t know, but plenty of people were making harsh comments like these under the surface.

But the moment they performed the song at the concert, everything turned upside down.

TeSTAR had even made the concert outfits a bit dramatic, with real cranks mounted on their backs and butterfly props trailing behind them like tails.

Because they poured in their capital, the costume didn’t look awkward even if it was that excessive.

After that, special photos with the concept of the music video intact were added exclusively for the album, and some of them were uploaded on TeSTAR’s SNS, making it perfect.

However, without knowing such elaborate calculations, the grad student was just happy.

*Okay, let’s watch Moondae’s fancam, too!*

The grad student excitedly put in the search term on WeTube and found Park Moondae’s new music show fancam.

The hairstyle with only half the forehead exposed and the loose stage suit looked really good on him.

*Blonde is the best...!*

While watching the video happily, she suddenly opened the comment window.

Of course, she knew from experience that the top comments were plastered in English, but it was nice to find one or two comments in Korean.

When she saw the silly comments, she felt like adding something.

“Hmhmm~”

The grad student happily scrolled down the comment window and finally found a comment in Korean.

However... It was not the kind of comment she was thinking of.

★$
– Park Moondae, fighting~! I like how strong you’re staying

– Good luck, Moondae! I hope you don’t have a hard timeㅠㅠ
$★

“...?”

She felt a sense of incongruity.

The concept this time was a little sexy and based on coolness and elegance... It was as though each element paid attention to the artistry.

And Park Moondae was a genius idol who performed the concept properly, neither lacking nor being too much!

*But instead of praising the performance, why are they saying this...?*

Of course, these were words with good intentions, so she didn’t feel bad.

But... just a little, her excitement cooled down.

“Mmm.”

The grad student just skipped the comment and shrugged it off. Fortunately, she soon found a silly comment that suited her and clicked like.

★$
– Every time Moondae blinks, the world flickers. This is a Moondae eclipse!
$★

“This is it!”

The grad student smiled and felt entertained.

The discomfort was quickly forgotten, but now she felt like she had a sneak peek of what her friend was worried about.

*They are more interested in how nice Moondae is than in Moondae himself...*

Of course, she wasn’t saying that the majority of people were like that, but she wasn’t happy that such an atmosphere had occurred.

*I want to hurry up and watch Moondae in the variety show.*

While waiting for the variety show, the grad student watched the video of the unit stage preparation from the last concert and the fancam of the unit stage during her breaks.

And the day the variety show aired.

The moment Park Moondae and Lee Sejin appeared, she nearly choked.

“*Gasp*!”

Park Moondae and Lee Sejin... They were wearing pink fairy wings for children.

“...?!”
