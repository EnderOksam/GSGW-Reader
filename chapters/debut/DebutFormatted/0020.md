# Chapter 20 - PR Live, I

Each of the two gold balls obtained through the treasure hunt contained a note.

!-
\[Guitar]
-!
!-
\[Flower Crown]

“...Ah.”

This is hopeless.

Guitar? I can’t play it.

Flower crown? Only someone with an A in physical attractiveness could pull that off.

No, even if I put all my points into leveling up that stat, the flower crown wasn’t a good option.

Because it wouldn’t help me seem more approachable to the audience.

Both items are objectively pretty good, but to me, they are landmines.

*I’m going crazy.*

I should trade with Keun Sejin or Gold #1 for an item they weren’t going to use. If not, I’d have to try the other participants.

The moment I was about to turn around, the participants poured into the meeting place.

**– There are 20 seconds left~~**

“Whoaaa!”

“Hey, wait! Wait!”

When they heard the announcement, it looked like they ran quickly to the meeting place so they wouldn’t lose the ball.

Could I get through that crowd and arrange a trade?

As I wondered, a familiar face caught my eye.

It was Seon Ahyeon, whose face was pale.

He had a bronze ball in his hand.

Finding even a bronze ball was impressive, given his timid personality.

But judging by the look on his face, he didn’t seem to like what he’d found inside.

At that moment, I saw the note inside the bronze ball that had already been opened.

!-
\[Frozen food]
-!

Jackpot.

I quickly got through the crowd and approached Seon Ahyeon.

**– 5 seconds left!**

Seon Ahyeon had a perplexed look.

**– 5!**

“I-I don’t...”

“Do you know how to play the guitar?”

**– 4!**

“Uh, y-yeah, I, I can play—”

“Do you like flowers?”

**– 3!**

“Y, yes.”

“Then, do you want to trade with me?”

**– 2!**

“H-huh? T-trade?”

I’ll take it as a positive answer.

**– 1!**

I handed over two gold balls in my hand and took the bronze ball.

#*– Ding! The treasure hunt is over!*#

Seon Ahyeon had a bewildered expression on his face.

As cheers and sighs echoed around me, I joyfully looked down at the bronze ball in my hand.

It was a great trade.

I’d given him two gold balls for his single bronze one. There’s no way I’d get criticized for that, right?
