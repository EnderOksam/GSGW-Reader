# Chapter 356

"They... Aren't they going to eat?"

"I'm hungry. We should eat chicken."

It had already been over an hour since Park Moondae and Seon Ahyeon disappeared into the latter's room.

Bae Sejin, who had willingly given up his desk to Kim Raebin, who was now playing with his keyboard, grew impatient.

*No... Don't tell me they're drinking.*

Bae Sejin checked the refrigerator just in case, but there was no sign of alcohol. Since no one had ever bought it in the first place, he knew that it was speculation.

*I thought mediation wasn't necessary.*

Although Seon Ahyeon had looked a bit dazed that morning, the atmosphere between them seemed to have lightened considerably.

Bae Sejin felt a bit relieved.

"I hope their conversation is going well."

A knife-like answer came back.

"That's right. I had no idea why he borrowed my laptop, but I hope it helped the two of them to have a deep conversation!"

"...Hmm, yes."

Bae Sejin decided to dispel his suspicions. It was because he was a bit embarrassed because he felt like he was the only one who was so anxious about this in the first place.

*Still, I'd like someone to confirm it.*

Should he text him?

However, not long after, Bae Sejin thought so hard that Park Moondae called Ryu Chungwoo and Lee Sejin, and entered the room for a while.

It was easy to see why they had been the ones called in.

Bae Sejin felt a little hurt as the oldest member, but he soon understood why they had been chosen.

No, he was relieved.

*I'm glad it wasn't me who called...*

And like a lie, the idea became reality.

The two members who had been called emerged two hours later, looking exhausted.

Even Lee Sejin waved his hand and disappeared into the kitchen to drink water without saying a word.

*...?*

Bae Sejin was taken aback.

"What's the matter?"

"Hmm."

Ryu Chungwoo smiled bitterly and shook his head.

"Moondae is certainly something."

"...What?"

"He was very logical."

They weren't supposed to be having some kind of debate, so what was he talking about?

Bae Sejin asked involuntarily.

"What?"

"Well, maybe this is his way of making up?"

"...??"

Was he really trying to make up with him through logical persuasion?

Something like that... Was it possible?

However, Ryu Chungwoo added a word with an airy smile.

"It went well."

"...??"

Bae Sejin was even more confused.

However, it was true that something similar was happening in the room.

*Good.*

I nodded.

The two guys who entered the room were put to good use in every form of proof.

Keundal and the three of us talked by writing, answering the questions the three of us made in random order, drawing random pictures, and describing what Seon Ahyeon was holding...

As the number of people increased, the process became more and more astronomically complex, and there were fewer and fewer holes to suspect deception.

Seon Ahyeon no longer had a need to come up with methods on his own.

*There's no need to have a fight.*

All the proofs went smoothly. Thanks to Keundal, there was no corner that got in the way.

Although Ryu Chungwoo looked at me with a little meaning.

– You could have done something like this.

"......"

It was a kind of telepathic twin gaze.

Should I have only invited Lee Sejin?

Anyway, both guys did a good job. In particular, as soon as Seon Ahyeon's barrier was lowered, Lee Sejin hit my contradiction very well.

– Moondae has a different curriculum from ours. Isn't he much more knowledgeable about every school subject than we are? You thought you heard wrong?

– He knows how to drive too. Last time, he unknowingly taught Manager Hyung. Ahyeon hasn't seen Park Moondae studying for a driver's license, right?

I wanted to shut him up a few times, but the effect of improving the situation was outstanding, so I passed it.

And as time passed, Seon Ahyeon began to actively come up with test methods.

By that point, I was starting to feel a bit nervous and exhausted.

– Th-then... I-I will ask any question, and y-you will answer it.

– Okay.

And now after countless resets and iterations...

"......"

"......"

Seon Ahyeon was looking at the empty space with a blank face. He seemed to have completely abandoned his obsession with notes.

*He was holding onto it like a rope, and he finally let it go.*

Questions and keywords were scattered across the notes.

– Magic

– No electronics...

– Other people

Of course, everything was lined up.

Seon Ahyeon's doubts were being cleared up one by one.

At this point, he had no choice but to admit that I wasn't a madman, but that there was something concrete behind my claims.

"Any other questions?"

"......"

The place where the two of us, invited as guests, went out was quiet. After tidying up Kim Raebin's computer, I sat across from Seon Ahyeon.

I heard the sound of Seon Ahyeon swallowing.

And I raised my head.

"...N-no."

Confidence returned to the eyes of the guy who answered.

"I don't have any questions."

He answered resolutely, as if every last doubt had been brushed aside.

*Yes.*

It was worth squeezing out everything I had for four hours.

I nearly clenched my fists in triumph.

"...Okay."

It wasn't over yet. If I asked him, 'Do you believe me?' there would be many different outcomes.

If I wrap things up like this, he's bound to come up with other doubts later.

That was why I had to speak first.

"Of course, just because this nonsense is possible doesn't mean that what I said is true."

"...! U-um."

Possibility did not automatically mean truth. Just because some crazy guy did magic didn't mean that everything he said was true.

If that were all I was trying to prove, I'd simply be a fraud.

So I must say this.

I needed to shut down every possible source of doubt.

"But there's no benefit to me if I lied about this."

"......"

"You might already know this, but frankly, we're currently in a position where we have everything we need."

Money, fame, and social status.

We were at the peak of our careers in an industry with a short lifespan, so there was no reason to worry about these things unless our popularity started declining in a few years.

Objectively speaking, idol 'Park Moondae' was satisfied both materially and in terms of recognition.

"It means that I have no reason to make up stories using these abilities."

And surprisingly, Seon Ahyeon replied immediately.

"I-I never thought... that you were lying to me."

"...!"

"Moo-Moondae... I never thought that you were lying."

"...Okay."

I didn't expect to hear this.

But it wasn't a bad feeling.

– Th-that's right. Moondae is not the kind of person to lie about something like this... Of course, I know.

*At that time... Did he mean what he said?*

The short flashback was over.

...Thanks to that, I could move on to the next part without delay.

After a short pause, I spoke.

"Then let's look at the hypothesis that I mistook myself for someone else."

I had to explain why this wasn't valid.

Why did 'Park Moondae' mistake himself for 'Ryu Gunwoo'? It was an awkward proposition.

*Let's prove that...*

I smiled bitterly.

"'Ryu Gunwoo'... As you know now, I didn't live a life that 'Park Moondae' would have envied."

"...!"

Both of us were orphans, and we struggled just to make ends meet.

It was easy to tell that they were similar, but if his mental state were to change... Wouldn't it be more understandable to be envious or imagine someone better than yourself?

"Even if Park Moondae's brain completely flips over and he mistakes himself for someone else, it wouldn't be him."

Perhaps it would have made sense if it were Ryu Chungwoo, but Gunwoo's life simply wasn't good enough.

"So that hypothesis simply doesn't hold up."

And unlike that hypothesis, so far I had spent hours proving to support my claim.

Seon Ahyeon looked at me with trembling eyes.

I unlaced my hands.

"This is all I prepared."

"......"

"I think I did everything I could. Honestly, at this point, I have no further proof."

I spoke frankly.

I was fully qualified to say this after I had been talking for such a long time without leaving a single gap between the words.

"I called in every witness I could, and told you everything I could."

Seon Ahyeon nodded slowly.

The mood was right for him to say, "That's right, Moondae. I believe you now."

I chuckled.

"So... I won't ask what you think."

"Wh-what?"

He had begged me to see a therapist, so I knew what would happen if he had to admit there was nothing wrong with me after all.

There was no reason to put him through that. So... I needed to ease things a little.

"I don't expect this to convince you all at once. Take your time, and doubt me as much as you want."

"C-continue...?"

"Yes. In the future, if you have any doubts, you can ask me anything, and I will continue to answer."

"......"

Seon Ahyeon opened his mouth.

This guy was not the type who made noise with malice, so it was okay to do something like this.

Well, I had gone this far now, so it was questionable whether there would be people who didn't believe it, but honestly, wasn't it normal to believe me at this point?

Still, I had to try putting it on full charge.

*I'm trying to make things okay.*

I looked at him with near certainty.

"Th-thank you... Moondae."

However, Seon Ahyeon didn't nod as I had expected.

"But, e-even if you don't do that, i-it's okay."

"...!"

"I-I... I showed you a lot of unreliable sides."

Seon Ahyeon squeezed both of his hands tightly.

"B-but Moondae has been working so hard for so long, t-to the extent that you couldn't admit it yourself. A-and if I have any doubts..."

Seon Ahyeon took a deep breath.

"F-first... I'll think it through and organize my thoughts carefully... A-and if I still have doubts after that... Only then will I ask you."

"......"

"Really... I believe you, Moo-Moondae."

Oh.

"Okay."

I held out my hand to Seon Ahyeon. Seon Ahyeon reached out and took it. It wasn't that cold this time.

"I'm grateful to have you in our group."

"Yes."

We hadn't shaken hands in a few days.

"Hmm... Are you worried that you could have done a little better?"

Yes.

*... I wasn't thinking rationally.*

The moment he first heard the story of Park Moondae, he was terrified, and thanks to that, he was buried in it.

So, step by step, without asking Lee Sejin or Ryu Chungwoo, who already knew about the situation, he crazily developed anxiety inside.

The incident had been a result of his own weakness.

He wanted to get this off his chest.

Seon Ahyeon opened his mouth.

"Yes, a-a little bit. I guess so. N-next time I have to do better."

However, what came out of his mouth was only a light statement.

"Okay, Sejin will do the same~"

"F-fighting."

Seon Ahyeon thought about the conclusion over and over again and decided not to repeat his thoughts and apologize to those around him.

*That... It's because I want their hearts to be at ease.*

Even if he tried, only Park Moondae would be more burdened, and it would only cause pain to the group.

Through all these events, Seon Ahyeon came to realize one thing.

*If he wants to share his baggage, I have to carry mine properly first.*

He had to show that he could handle it so that they could share the burden.

Rather than trying to prove that he was qualified, he first had to become the kind of person he wanted to be.

*My share is something I have to handle on my own.*

However, Seon Ahyeon would use his failure to trust Park Moondae as a means of self-reflection and self-discipline.

Seon Ahyeon decided to do so without borrowing anyone's judgment.

He had to handle it on his own.

So, in a surprisingly light tone, he ended his remarks.

"Hmm, sh-shall we go eat chicken?"

"Oh~ Sounds good."

The two of them, who were standing on the veranda, left and went back to the living room. A casual ease lingered between them as they returned to the living room.

But the conclusion Ahyeon had reached was anything but casual.
