# Chapter 356 - The Weight of Secrets, VII

"Aren't they... going to come out and eat?"

"I'm hungry," Cha Eugene said. "We should eat chicken."

It had already been over an hour since Park Moondae and Seon Ahyeon disappeared into the latter's room.

Bae Sejin, who had willingly given up his desk to Kim Raebin, now fiddling with his keyboard on the bed, grew nervous.

*They're not even making a sound... Don't tell me they're drinking or something.*

Bae Sejin checked the refrigerator just in case, but there was no sign of alcohol. Since no one had brought any into the dorm in the first place, he knew it was highly unlikely.

*I didn't think it was absolutely necessary for someone to... mediate.*

Although Seon Ahyeon had looked a bit dazed that morning, the atmosphere between them seemed to have lightened considerably.

Bae Sejin wished them the best of luck.

"I hope their conversation is going well."

An answer came back right away.

"Me too. I had no idea why they borrowed my laptop, but I hope it helps the two of them have a deep conversation!" Raebin said.

"...Hmm, yes."

Bae Sejin decided to stop worrying about it. It seemed he was the only one in the dorm who was so anxious, which made him feel a little embarrassed.

*Still, I wish someone would check in on them.*

Should he text them to remind them to eat?

Not long after Bae Sejin had thought this, however, Park Moondae briefly poked his head out and called Ryu Chungwoo and Lee Sejin into the room.

*Chungwoo is his roommate, and Lee Sejin is the same age as them.*

It was easy to see why they had been the ones called in.

Bae Sejin felt a little hurt as the oldest member, but he soon accepted it, recalling how poor his speaking skills could be.

No, he was relieved.

*I'm glad he didn't call me in...*

And as it turned out, Bae Sejin had no idea how right he was.

The two members who had been called in emerged two hours later, looking pale and exhausted.

Even Lee Sejin waved his hand and disappeared into the kitchen to drink water without saying a word.

*...?*

Bae Sejin was taken aback.

"What's the matter?"

"Hmm."

Ryu Chungwoo smiled wryly and shook his head.

"Moondae is certainly something."

"...What?"

"He was very logical."

They weren't supposed to be having some kind of debate, so what was he talking about?

Bae Sejin asked involuntarily.

"About what?"

"Uh, why it was necessary to make up with him?"

"...??"

*Make up on the basis of logic?*

*Is that even possible?*

However, Ryu Chungwoo smiled weakly and added,

"He was very convincing."

"...??"

*That sort of thing... works?*

Bae Sejin was even more confused. However, it was true that this was exactly what was happening in the room.

***

*Good.*

I nodded.

The two guys I'd invited into the room were put to good use in presenting all the proof I'd prepared.

I told them what the three of them and Keundal had discussed in writing, answered the questions the three of them made in random order, had them each draw a picture and mix them up, and described what Seon Ahyeon was holding in his hand...

As the number of people increased, the process grew astronomically more complex, leaving fewer and fewer openings for suspicion.

Finally, I had Seon Ahyeon come up with his own ways to test my claims.

*What a strange contest of wits.*

All the proofs went smoothly. Keundal had even taken the day off to help me, so there was nothing in the way.

Ryu Chungwoo did give me a meaningful look, though.

– *You can do something like this?*

"......"

It was the kind of look you'd give twins who could communicate telepathically.

*Should I have only invited Keun Sejin?*

Anyway, both guys did a good job. In particular, as soon as Seon Ahyeon's guard was lowered, Keun Sejin brought up the contradictions he'd noticed about me.

– *Moondae was educated differently from us. Have you ever heard him talk about obsolete school subjects? Gosh, I almost thought I'd misheard.*

– *He knows how to drive too. I've caught him nitpicking Manager Hyung's driving plenty of times. Ahyeon hasn't seen Park Moondae studying for a driver's license, right?*

I wanted to shut him up a few times, but he was definitely improving the situation, so I let him be.

And as time passed, Seon Ahyeon began to actively come up with test methods.

By that point, Seon Ahyeon seemed too exhausted to be nervous anymore.

– *Th-then... I-I'll ask a random question, and h-he can answer me.*

– *Okay.*

And now, after countless repeated questions and resets...

"......"

"......"

Seon Ahyeon was staring into space with a blank face. He seemed to have completely forgotten about his obsession with the notebook.

*He was holding onto it like a lifeline earlier, and he's finally let it go.*

Questions and keywords were scattered messily across the notebook.

!-
\
@c@$$~~– Magic~~$$@c@

@c@$$~~– No electronics...~~$$@c@

@c@$$~~– Another person?~~$$@c@
-!

Of course, everything was crossed out.

I'd made sure to ask Seon Ahyeon about every one of his doubts and clear them up one by one.

At this point, he had no choice but to admit that I wasn't a madman, but that there was something concrete behind my claims.

"Any other questions?"

"......"

The room was quiet now that my two guests had left. After putting away Kim Raebin's computer, I sat across from Seon Ahyeon.

I heard Seon Ahyeon swallow thickly.

And then he raised his head.

"...N-no."

Confidence returned to the eyes of the guy who answered.

"I don't have any questions."

He answered resolutely, as if every last doubt had been brushed aside.

*There we go.*

It was worth squeezing out everything I had for four hours.

I nearly clenched my fists in triumph.

"...Okay."

It wasn't over yet. All my hard work would be undone if I asked him whether he believed me now.

*If I wrap things up like this, he's bound to come up with other doubts later.*

That was why I had to speak first.

"Of course, just because this nonsense is possible doesn't mean that what I said is true."

"Th-that's not..."

Possibility did not automatically mean truth. Just because some crazy guy did magic didn't mean that everything he said was true.

*If that were all I was trying to prove, I'd simply be a fraud.*

I had to say this.

I needed to shut down every possible source of doubt.

"But there's no benefit to me if I lied about this."

"......"

"You might already know this, but frankly, we're currently in a position where we have everything we need."

Money, fame, and social status.

We were at the peak of our careers in an industry with a short lifespan. Things might change once our popularity started declining in a few years, but for now, there was no reason to worry about these things. We already had them.

Objectively speaking, idol 'Park Moondae' was satisfied both materially and in terms of recognition.

"It means that I have no reason to make up stories using these abilities."

And surprisingly, Seon Ahyeon replied immediately.

"I-I never thought that."

"...!"

"Moo-Moondae... I-I never thought that you were lying to me."

"...Okay."

*I didn't expect to hear this.*

It felt good.

– *Th-that's right. Moondae is not the kind of person to lie about something like this... Of course, I know.*

*I guess he meant what he said back then.*

I remembered what he'd said when I'd first told him the truth.

...Thanks to that, I could move on to the next part without delay.

After a short pause, I spoke.

"Then let's look at the hypothesis that I mistook myself for someone else."

It was a reasonable theory.

It wasn't all that far-fetched to wonder if 'Park Moondae' had simply mistaken himself for 'Ryu Gunwoo'.

*Let's prove that...*

I smiled bitterly.

"'Ryu Gunwoo'... As you know now, I didn't live a life that 'Park Moondae' would have envied."

"...!"

Both of us were orphans, and we struggled just to make ends meet.

It was easy to sympathize with someone in such a similar situation, but for him to delude himself into thinking he was someone else, there had to be something about that person's life worth envying.

"Even if Park Moondae's brain completely flips over and he mistakes himself for someone else, it wouldn't be Ryu Gunwoo."

Perhaps it would have made sense if it were Ryu Chungwoo, but Gunwoo's life simply wasn't good enough.

"So that hypothesis simply doesn't hold up."

And the hours I'd spent proving my claims so far only made it clearer that this hypothesis didn't hold up.

Seon Ahyeon looked at me with uncertainty in his eyes.

I opened my palms.

"This is all I prepared."

"......"

"I think I did everything I could. Honestly, at this point, I have no further proof."

I spoke frankly.

I had every right to say this after spending so long providing proof.

"I called in every witness I could, and told you everything I could."

Seon Ahyeon nodded slowly.

The mood was right for him to say, "That's right, Moondae. I believe you now."

I chuckled.

"So... I won't ask what you think."

"Wh-what?"

I knew him too well. He had begged me to see a therapist, so I knew what would happen if he had to admit there was nothing wrong with me after all.

*He'll just get caught in a vicious cycle of self-blame again.*

There was no reason to put him through that. *So... let's ease things a little.*

"I don't expect this to convince you all at once. Take your time, and doubt me as much as you want. Keep doubting my story."

"K-keep doubting...?"

"Yes. In the future, if you have any doubts, you can ask me anything, and I will continue to answer."

"......"

Seon Ahyeon's mouth fell open.

This guy wasn't the type to go around spreading malicious rumors, so it was okay to offer him this.

Well, I had gone this far now, so I doubted anyone could still disbelieve me.

*Honestly, isn't it only natural to believe me at this point?*

Still, I had to make sure he had a soft landing.

*He's going to agree for sure.*

I looked at him with near certainty.

"Th-thank you... Moondae."

However, Seon Ahyeon didn't nod reluctantly as I'd expected.

"But, th-that won't be n-necessary."

"...!"

"I-I... I haven't exactly been d-dependable until now."

Seon Ahyeon clenched both hands into fists.

"B-but Moondae has spent so many hours explaining this to me... A-and I can admit when I'm wrong. A-and if I have any doubts again..."

Seon Ahyeon took a deep breath.

"F-first... I'll think it through, organize my thoughts carefully and l-look into it... A-and if I still can't figure it out myself... Only then will I ask you."

"......"

"Really... I believe you, Moo-Moondae."

*Oh.*

"Okay."

I held out my hand to Seon Ahyeon. Seon Ahyeon reached out and took it. It wasn't that cold this time.

"I'm grateful to have you in our group."

"Thanks..."

We hadn't shaken hands in a few days.

---

Finally, Park Moondae and Seon Ahyeon came out to the living room.

And all the TeSTAR members who saw the atmosphere between the two realized that the conflict was well resolved.

"Well done! Whoever won can eat chicken!" Cha Eugene said.

"Cha Eugene! That's so rude..." Kim Raebin said.

"Okay. You buy the chicken," Moondae said.

"...! If Moondae-hyung says so..." Kim Raebin began.

"No, I wasn't talking to you."

The usual playful banter passed between them.

It was night by the time the leader smiled and called the company about their future schedules...

"Ahyeon-ah~"

"Yes."

When Seon Ahyeon, who was standing on the indoor balcony, turned his head, Lee Sejin smiled and stood beside him.

"Why are you here alone? Aren't you eating chicken?"

"I-it's okay."

Seon Ahyeon smiled faintly.

"I guess Ahyeon has a lot on his mind."

Lee Sejin bumped shoulders with him and laughed. The mood softened.

However, Seon Ahyeon didn't wave his hand or otherwise try to reassure him.

"...Yes. A little."

He just answered plainly. However, Lee Sejin hit the nail on the head.

"Hmm... Are you worried that you could have done a little better?"

"...!"

Yes.

After the conflict was resolved, Seon Ahyeon could look back at the incident without anything bothering him and reach a logical conclusion.

*...I wasn't thinking rationally.*

The moment he first heard Park Moondae's story, he was terrified, and as a result, he hadn't responded logically.

So, little by little, without asking Lee Sejin or Ryu Chungwoo, who already knew the situation, for clarification, he'd allowed his anxiety to grow.

The incident had been a result of his own weakness.

He wanted to get this off his chest.

Seon Ahyeon opened his mouth.

"Yes, a-a little bit. I guess so. N-next time I have to do better."

However, what came out of his mouth was only a light statement.

"Okay, Sejin will do the same~"

"F-fighting. We can both do it."

Seon Ahyeon was determined not to dwell on this conclusion, avoiding his usual habit of talking about it and apologizing to those around him over and over.

*That... It's because I only want to make myself feel better.*

Even if he tried, only Park Moondae would be more burdened, and it would only cause pain to the group.

Through all these events, Seon Ahyeon came to realize one thing.

*If I want the others to help me carry my baggage, I have to carry my own weight properly first.*

One could share a burden only with someone who seemed capable and ready to shoulder it.

Rather than trying to prove that he was qualified, he first had to become the kind of person he wanted to be.

*My share is something I have to handle on my own.*

However, Seon Ahyeon would use his failure to trust Park Moondae as a means of self-reflection and self-discipline.

Seon Ahyeon decided to do so without borrowing anyone's judgment.

He had to handle it on his own.

So, in a surprisingly light tone, he ended his remarks.

"Hmm, sh-shall we go eat chicken?"

"Oh~ Sounds good."

The two of them left the indoor balcony and returned to the living room, a casual ease lingering between them.

But the conclusion Ahyeon had reached was anything but casual.

And the next morning, Seon Ahyeon went to see Park Moondae...

"He-hello, Moondae."

"Yes..."

Park Moondae, who came out the door and met Seon Ahyeon, noticed something he hadn't expected.

Seon Ahyeon's status abnormality had returned to its inactive state.

Without any help.

*What?*

Park Moondae had been planning to do all he could to reactivate the trait quickly today, based on the trust he'd managed to build during yesterday's crazy five-hour proof relay.

*Did I see it wrong?*

Park Moondae almost squinted at Seon Ahyeon.

But first, he saw something else.

★-
\
#><✦ Trait: Fortitude (S)[1]{T/N: In case you didn't notice, Seon Ahyeon's special trait upgraded from A to S!}><#

Master your own mind. You know you can handle it.

Counteracts psychological status abnormalities (can affect multiple) when active.
-★

"Moo-Moondae...?"

Park Moondae reread the sentence that had caught his eye.

**[Master your own mind.]**

Yes.

He didn't know what kind of change of heart there was, but last night, Seon Ahyeon broke his slump and reactivated his trait.

Maybe Park Moondae would never know the full details of what had happened, but...

*But even so... that's okay.*

Seon Ahyeon overcame it on his own. He was likely aware of what had helped him overcome it.

Moondae looked at Ahyeon with appreciation, impressed that he'd overcome the status abnormality on his own, even though he'd once thought of the trait as unfair.

...Of course, if a problem did arise, he'd dig down to its very roots and look for a way to deal with it.

That didn't seem necessary today.

"Let's eat," Moondae said.

"Yes...!"

The next day, TeSTAR announced as planned that they had recovered and resumed their promotions.

And around this time, the first Billboard rankings went live.

It was the beginning of a real rollercoaster ride for TeSTAR.
