# Chapter 202 - Glitch, I

The first day of the encore concert ended successfully as planned.

The response to the unit stage was also good.

★$
- Please release the soundtrack ㅠㅠㅠㅠㅠㅠ

- The only good point about Idol Inc. is that we can see the two on the same team. Now, just abolish that show.

- Park Moondae's vocals and Cha Eugene's dancing, this combination should be put in the K-pop Hall of Fame (I know I'm greedy. Please, just pass by.)

- Cha Kitty has always been talking about doing a unit with his Moondae-hyung on W App. I can understand why. My kid is a genius.
$★

Occasionally, aggro comments wanting to compare it with other unit stages popped up, but it didn't seem to work very well.

Even though I was saying this myself... I think it was thanks to the fact that the unit consisted of the first and second-place members.

Since the member combination was how it was, the fans of the other units didn't seem to want to make a big deal out of it.

*It's hard to mess with the side that outnumbers you.*

In the first place, the unit performances were overall of high quality, so narratives such as 'TeStar does well with any combination', were drawn together.

So, the fan community was harmonious as each unit stage was appreciated by itself.

★$
- It's been so good today

- For real, the moment the opening VCR came out, I was so emotional I shed tears

- I'll go to TeStar's dinner show, I promise.
$★

The atmosphere between Park Moondae’s and Cha Eugene’s fans was the best it had been since the group debuted. It was just like a festival.

This meant I was the only one among the concerned parties who was feeling tense.

"Hyung!! I'll shake the chandelier tomorrow!"

"Sure. Good night."

I gave an okay to Cha Eugene's ad lib suggestion I had heard about five times now and left the bedroom.

"Where are you going??"

"To bathe."

Then I passed through the living room and closed the bathroom door behind me.

It was the only place I could be alone for an hour or two without being questioned.

*Clack.*

"Huu."

I sat down in the bathtub.

...In fact, I could already see the pop-up appearing the moment the concert ended.

It’s just that I put off checking it until bedtime so that it wouldn't have disrupted me during the simple late evening schedules such as interviews for the behind-the-scenes cuts.

*Of course, there's no need for me to check the pop-up right at this moment either.*

There was still plenty of time left for me to wait it out and see.

Still, since this could be the last time, it was hard for me to put it off any longer.

Let's see.

*Status window, notifications.*

As I recited the words, the pop-up that I had only glanced at earlier appeared again.

★-
\
#><[Successful encounter!]><#

You have successfully met 200,000 audiences!

!Time Limit: Requirement fulfilled (Success)

!Status Abnormality: Remove #*'Meet Audiences or Die'*#!

**{[!]Check 'Options'{** ☜ Click!
-★

First of all... the success has been confirmed.

"Haa..."

Somehow, my tension loosened up. I pressed my temples, feeling strangely enervated.

All that was left was to check that damn truth... Wait a minute.

"...!!"

★-
\
#><{[!]Check 'Options'{><# ☜ Click!
-★

The keyword wasn't 'truth'.

*Options?*

As soon as I saw those words, there was something I obviously associated it with.

*...It's the option to choose between my original body and the body of 'Park Moondae'.*

Wasn't that the most plausible and appropriate reward for clearing the last status abnormality?

"......"

However, it was a little too good to be true.

My head cooled down.

*Isn't it too late to present it at this point?*

This bizarre system has faithfully maintained its game style ever since I first entered Park Moondae's body.

However, it would be strange for it to offer me such a carrot as a surprise reward after never suggesting it to the end.

In normal games, they would have definitely thrown something like this in as bait right at the starting point before you were hit with the status abnormality.

*No, even if it's not about the games... in terms of structure and efficiency, I should be right.*

Wouldn't it make more sense to torture you from the start with the hopes of having a clear reward at the end than threatening you with death while flogging you?

*In that case, what are the other hypotheses?*

I tried to think about what else could be plausible if it was not about choosing the body, but I couldn't come up with any theories that would have me think 'This is it!'.

"...Huu."

I contemplated actually taking a bath. My head was throbbing.

*Tomorrow. Whatever it is, let's think about it tomorrow.*

The last concert had to finish well first. I should clear my head of anything that could affect my condition.

*Let's go to sleep.*

As I was about to return to the bedroom, someone waved at me from the kitchen.

"Hmh? Moondae, aren't you sleeping?"

It was Keun Sejin.

"I was bathing."

"Ah~ That's nice."

He smiled and poured out another glass of cold barley tea from the bottle he was holding.

"Want one? My mom gave it to me yesterday."

The color reminded me of beer.

I accepted the glass without a word. Keun Sejin continued talking while smiling.

"Oh, right. Your unit stage was pretty amazing, Moondae. You did it well even with Eugenie~ As expected of Park Moondae!"

However, his words had other meanings.

*He's subtly implying something.*

Was he reflecting on his own unit stage experience with Cha Eugene?

I thought back to that time.

*- Just, choose the performance well. I don't feel like comparing you and Cha Eugene as to who is better.*

I think that was the kind of advice I gave to him at that time. However, I ended up doing a head-to-head battle-style performance with Cha Eugene, so he must have been concerned about it.

However, this was just a matter of demonstration.

"...I just chose the best points to emphasize."

As I sipped on my cold tea, I calmly muttered.

"It wouldn't have worked out if it weren't for the musical. No matter how well I would sing."

"......"

It meant that, in the end, it was no different from when he was on stage with his unit and that it was just a matter of formatting it well.

*Of course, there are also the attributes, but I can't explain that much.*

Instead, let's just give this guy a chance to wrap up this conversation.

"Your unit stage was also good. The shadow performance went perfectly, and it was cool."

"...!"

A slightly embarrassed wry smile flashed across Keun Sejin's face.

He also probably understood in his head that I just said it passingly, so let's leave it at that.

The guy shrugged at the idle talk and quickly accepted the chance.

"...Haha. We worked hard. It's nice that the actual performance came out well."

"I know, right?"

Bae Sejin practiced with his soul ripped out of his body, so if the performance had gone wrong, he might even try to kill Keun Sejin.

*Although things seemed to have gone well with Kim Raebin who's tactless but hard-working.*

Keun Sejin smiled brightly.

"Next time, let's do it together, Moondae! We've never done a unit together after we debuted~"

Why would we if we don't have any overlapping positions?

"Moondae-moondae, you just thought that there's no need for us to do it since we have different positions, didn't you!"

"...!"

"Haha, I guess I was right~"

"...No."

How did this punk figure it out?

Keun Sejin was laughing out loud, but soon fixed his expression and spoke.

"Hum hum, but if the opportunity comes, we'll do well, right?"

"I guess so."

"Good~ That's the attitude!"

Keun Sejin put the barley tea into the refrigerator. Then, after a little hesitation, he continued.

"...There are many things I'm grateful to you for. Take care of me in the future, too."

"...It was nothing."

"You say that every time I compliment you!"

Keun Sejin laughed and patted my shoulder. I guess it's funny to him.

It seems that this guy also became more emotional since it's been a long time since we've had a concert back in Korea.

*...I guess it's a bit better, anyway.*

This guy probably didn't have any intentions, but I felt more at ease after talking about the stage with him since I didn't have to think about anything useless.

"Then, I'll go in."

"Yeah. Let's do well tomorrow, too~"

And I went to bed like that.

There had been no variables. So far.

And on the next day.

*Whaaaah!!*

"Thank you!"

The encore concert was completely over.

"Agh! The clothes are stuck!"

"First of all, guys, pack your things..."

"W-weren't we supposed to take a group photo...?"

Once the event was over, the remaining small tasks were quickly wrapped up.

The after-party was also finished with simply eating delivery food with the members at the dormitory. It was because after returning to Korea from the tour, we just wanted to stay home for once.

"No alcohol!"

"...I haven't even touched it."

I was assigned a non-alcoholic beer. It was quite a strange feeling to have my alcohol taken away by a drunk guy.

And after surveying my surroundings, I stepped out to the veranda, acting like I needed to get some fresh air.

"...Huu. Status window notifications."

*Pop.* The pop-up I saw yesterday was back once again.

Now, it was about time.

From today until our comeback, the group had almost no official activities.

Even though it was only for about a month, it still meant that this time gap was the most suitable if I wanted to take a look at something. Since I could afford to.

And there was one thing that I thought of.

*I'm sure clicking on this 'Options' entry will bring up another pop-up that introduces different options.*

Based on the user interface so far, this was a pretty compelling theory.

*Then it's better for me to know about it sooner to contemplate it.*

I swallowed, raised my hand, and pressed 'Options'. My finger cut through the air without feeling anything.

And as I expected... A new pop-up appeared on top of it.

★-
\
#><[_Options_]><#

**{[!]Check 'Truth'{** ☜ Click!

**{Obtain #y'Coin'y#{** ☜ Click!

*※ Double selection is not allowed.*
-★

"...!"

As I thought, it was not the option to choose the body. And one of the two options... was a familiar sight.

'Truth'.

That must be those shitty notices of the past that I've been shown so far.

If so, the meaning of these options was the new choice given below.

'Coin'.

What in the world did it mean?

I was racking my brains at the totally unintuitive word.

The very first thing that came to my mind was... money.

*Would a shop open up?*

It was the kind of setting I have seen many times in web novels. A character who was the only one with a status window shop.

It was plausible but too abstract. Other than that, only images of cryptocurrency came to mind.

"...Damn it."

It was acting vague to the end. I glared at the selection window.

But... the decision was pretty much a given.

I moved my finger straight to the option without mulling it over.

★-
\#><{Obtain #y'Coin'y#!{><# ☜ Click!
-★

It was a logical choice.

*The truth is... too risky.*

Because I couldn't know what it might show me.

Just based on the description of the pop-up, the ‘Truth’ was a confirmation, and the ‘Coin’ was an acquisition.

Since coins are something you own, wouldn't my current circumstances be maintained even if the choice turns out to be a flop?

There was a common-sense expectation that an explanation as to what to do with the coin would now appear.

However, the pop-up that appeared... contrary to my expectations, was a sight I was already familiar with.

★!
#><⚠︎ @_@%~[Unforeseen!]~%@_@><#

Status Abnormality: #*'Be a Star or Die!'*# has occurred!
!★

"What?"

The shitty status abnormality notification had popped up again.

*This damn bastard...!*

I was about to lose it. What fucking 'last' one! Just how long do I have to keep up with this shit?

...But on the other hand, a peculiar feeling came over me.

I was relieved.

This too... could be seen as maintaining my current circumstances.

At least for another year, I just had to live like I have been living until now.

I knew that I would think of my thoughts as crazy when tomorrow came, but still... there was a strange sense of comfort this setback had given.

It was the fact that I could continue to live like this.

"No."

I gritted my teeth to resist those sentiments and glared at the pop-up.

*What the hell is its objective?*

It was then.

Suddenly... the pop-up began to glitch.

"...!!"

★!
#f#~~Status Abnormality: 'Be a Star or Die' has occurred!~~#f#
!★

Then the contents were crossed out with a line and disappeared.

"What the?"

It wasn't the end.

*Diring!*

★!
Status Abnormality: #*'Win 1st Place or Die'*# has occurred!
!★

Another pop-up appeared. The contents of the status abnormality were something I had encountered before.

"What-"

And that wasn't the end either.

★!
#f#~~'Win 1st Place or Die' has occurred!~~#f#

#f#~~'Win an Award or Die' has occurred!~~#f#

#f#~~'Have a Concert or Die' has occurred!~~#f#

#f#~~'Be the Best or Die' has occurred!~~#f#

#f#~~'Debut or Die' has occurred!~~#f#
!★

*What the fuck?*

The status abnormalities were endlessly crossed out and deleted.

And as if someone was hacking it, the status abnormalities were updated with new names while flickering countless times.

How many times has it repeated this?

%%*Diriririring!*%%

★!
#><⚠︎ @_@%~[Unforeseen!]~%@_@><#

Status Abnormality: #*'Meet Audiences or Die'*# has occurred!
!★

The pop-up finally stopped updating.

With the same name as the previous status abnormality.

However, the description of the contents was different.

★!
@c@Status Abnormality:@c@
#><@_@['Meet Audiences or Die']@_@><#

Failure to meet 400,000 or more audience members within a set period of time will result in the death of 'Park Moondae'.

The Number of People Reached: #*0 / 400,000*#
!★

It was not 200,000 people but 400,000.

The number of people has doubled.

And there was a point added to the description. A specific name was added in association with death.

*...It's not just 'death' but 'the death of Park Moondae'.*

...What was I supposed to make of this?

From this damn coin thing to the buggy status window, there was no part in this where I could figure out the alternate situation.

There was no point in racking my brains. Because there were no clues in the first place.

"......"

I sat down on one side of the veranda to cool my head. I was craving cigarettes for quite a bit, but it was bearable.

"Huu."

And as my body cooled down, I could sort out the situation.

*First of all, two things are certain.*

The first thing was to fill 400,000 spectators within a year.

That one was rather good. I managed to fill 200,000 people on a two-month tour.

The scale of our tour for the second half of the year has increased, so based on the schedule, achieving 400,000 people was possible.

*It will be even more sufficient if I include all the events.*

So, let's move on from that for now.

The second thing was... that there was one guy who could be asked about this situation.

There were even more reasons for me to question him since what that bastard had told me turned out to be wrong.

"...Fuck."

I really wasn't tempted to do it, but since I had no other options, I had to do it.

I pulled my smartphone out from the pockets of my pants.

Then I found the text message with a picture of a puppy attached and called it.

The beep was cut short.

*—Yes.*

As you might have guessed, it was Cheongryeo.
