# Chapter 365 - Ending the System, I

First of all, Miry-nay's comeback was very successful—in terms of attracting trolls, that is.

So, in terms of aggro...

As expected, public opinion heated up because they were being compared to each other.

Originally, it was more fun for the public when there were two similar groups to compare.

Moreover, although the groups had similar positions in the industry, their strengths were different, so everyone's opinion was bound to be different.

In conclusion, there was a lot to talk about.

*What a mess.*

What was certain was that the news of Miry-nay's comeback had clearly caught the public's attention, reigniting interest in them.

*They really made good use of the buzz.*

Thanks to this, Miry-nay was rapidly climbing the digital music charts for the first time in a while.

Although it was no different from their previous comebacks in that it was aimed at the US, it seemed to have been received like a pop song because of their global-friendly image, similar to Powty, which was a hit overseas.

*The song itself is good, too.*

I shrugged after watching people argue over the two groups for a while.

*TeSTAR is completely absent from the conversation this time.*

Since Miry-nay came from the same survival show as us but was the same gender as Powty, naturally there was no room for us to be mentioned.

And that damned show, ⟨The House Next Door's Grandson⟩... Anyway, thanks to the variety show becoming a solid success, TeSTAR was being talked about for other reasons.

...The process was a little different from what I'd expected, but we'd gotten close to the goal anyway. *That was what I tried to believe.*

Moreover, the response from overseas was surprisingly positive.

This was because we'd taken the global audience into account and discussed our strategy from the beginning, so T1 handed the variety show over to NetFlux rather than its own OTT service.

And it was doing well. The production team even dared to text us directly, which said a lot.

*Hot springs? What a load of crap. I can't believe we were made fools of on that show and the global audience enjoyed every second of it...* Anyway, things were going according to plan, just as I'd imagined.

*And we'd already started the press campaign.*

Even before the final episode aired...

We contacted the agency to finish adjusting the timing of the press releases.

★$
– **[TeSTAR enters the Billboard charts and produces a hit variety show... What makes them such a global success?]**

– **[From Dotify to Billboard... How did the two songs from TeSTAR, the *daesang* idols, become successful?]**
$★

Our promotional period was now nearing its end.

With the Billboard rankings falling slowly, these articles would naturally be accepted without causing any backlash.

*The fans will be happy, too.*

Thanks to the favorable image we'd gained through the hugely popular variety show, now was the best time to spread the news gently.

*Good.*

And a few days later...

The status window appeared.

★-

I grinned. It was always nice when things turned out as I'd expected.

This was how things should have been from the start. *That damn variety show...*

I sighed briefly and checked my status window again.

The words I'd been expecting appeared.

"Because it'll come in handy."

In order for this guy to avoid reverting completely into the status window, I'd eventually have to get rid of the system. I called Kwon Heeseung, Goldie #2.

He was the person currently holding the system.

"Wow, my *bones* ache."

"Do we really have to do W Live today? Can't we do it tomorrow?"

Spacer, the group Kwon Heeseung belonged to, was near the end of their first-ever Japanese tour and was suffering from the aftereffects.

Of course, the one person among them who believed he'd been given another chance at life was still alert.

"Hey! That kind of laziness is what leads to a decline in popularity!" Kwon Heeseung said.

"Oh god..."

The Spacer members groaned at Kwon Heeseung's words, but they understood and got moving.

*There are no more people with terrible personalities on our team.*

Kwon Heeseung repressed the urge to shake his head.

*Though some kids don't get along very well!*

Spacer had more people than TeSTAR. It was the same even after the withdrawal of Chae Seodam, who was a nightmare for Seon Ahyeon during their school days.

And having so many people on the team meant conflicts could increase exponentially unless they were properly managed.

Really... It was hard.

*Still, we came this far!*

Although Spacer was less popular than TeSTAR, who had debuted from the hit Relisted season of ⟨Idol Inc.⟩, their fandom was solid. This was to be expected from a survival show.

And Kwon Heeseung was confident that he was doing an excellent job as the group's self-proclaimed spiritual leader!

"Come on, hyungs and dongsaengs, let's show them what we've got today too!"

"Okay..."

Everyone was exhausted because their comeback was just around the corner, but they completed the livestream successfully thanks to their professionalism.

"See you soon~"

"We'll be back next time!"

After a brief mukbang, the members all dispersed to their respective hotel rooms.

"Oh my."

Kwon Heeseung congratulated himself on a successful day, patted himself on the shoulder, and was about to sink into some long-overdue sleep.

But then his phone rang.

★:
#><#*[TeSTAR Moondae-hyung👍]*#><#
📞 Incoming call…

"No way?"

This was rather sudden.

Of course, depending on the person, some people preferred spontaneous calls, but he knew that this hyung wasn't that sort of person.

*He's very calm, collected, and deliberate.*

This wasn't to say that Kwon Heeseung disliked Park Moondae.

He could be a little scary, but he was a good sunbaenim.

*– It's better to back away when things get that bad, instead of fighting.*

*– Don't say something like that, even if it's by mistake.*

*– I think we can just have them stay in separate rooms.*

Moondae sometimes asked about his concerns or current situation without being prompted, and whenever Heeseung asked for advice, Moondae never turned him away and answered quite sincerely.

He seemed to be a very kind person to those he considered his own. Kwon Heeseung rubbed his nose.

*Well, that's been true since the* Idol Inc. *days.*

He clicked his tongue, recalling how Park Moondae, the only non-trainee at the time, calmly ignored Choi Wongil's shenanigans while taking care of those around him. It never ceased to amaze him.

Recently, Choi Wongil had even apologized to him through Park Moondae. It really showed how generous Park Moondae was.

*...Even when he found out that I nearly said something ridiculous on the radio, he was quite forgiving about it.*

*– If something happens, all we have to do is deal with it.*

*Though the problem is, sometimes dealing with it isn't possible.*

...The truth of that sent cold sweat down his spine.

*I never expected him to tell anyone about his situation...*

*Wow, did Park Moondae reveal his secret to his members because of me? Since I nearly exposed our secret organization(?) publicly on the radio!*

Or maybe he was trying to set an example and show, 'This is how you should reveal the secret.'

The timing was perfect.

*Wow, he's amazing.*

Kwon Heeseung, blinded by admiration for the top-tier idol who had come from the future, ended up making an absurd deduction.

Park Moondae was now practically his role model!

It wasn't simply because they were both idols who knew some of the future.

*He's also someone worthy of respect as a true idol.*

That was evident from the way he'd taken on the least desirable role on their new variety show despite his wonderful personality. It wouldn't have been easy for such a cool-headed person to do that.
