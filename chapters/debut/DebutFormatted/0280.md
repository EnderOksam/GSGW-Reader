# Chapter 280 - ⟨TeSTAR Roommate⟩ Season 4, III

As I stood there alone, my vision and sense of smell shut down...

Sudden thoughts crowded my mind.

*Alone.*

Living alone meant having to know everything and take responsibility for everything myself.

All the actions society required of me, and any mistakes I made, were my responsibility: contracts, reports, payments, overdue payments...

The risks and pain brought on by my decisions and actions were mine alone.

I couldn't share them with anyone. I had no other choice.

*It's okay.*

I'd get used to it after a few years.

I wasn't the only one who lived like this. I could still manage to live even if something shitty or difficult happened.

*I could handle it.*

But what about situations I couldn't handle? Couldn't something like that happen at some point in my life?

*I can't guarantee that they won't.*

Just like what happened to my parents... Sudden disasters were always a possibility.

What if there was a fire?

What if I was sleeping, injured, or sick at the time...? What if I was helpless and couldn't do anything?

*There are too many holes in my plan.*

If I let my guard down, I'd die. 

*Dduk.*

A drop of sweat fell onto my shoe... No, it felt strange that a single drop of liquid could feel so heavy.

*Is this real?*

The air was acrid and smoky.

But no matter how hard I strained my eyes, I couldn't see anything...

"Park Moondae!"

Suddenly, a voice broke through the silence.

And something touched my shoulder... It felt like a hand.

It was someone else's hand, not mine.

"Are you okay? Stop for a second."

Was I walking?

"Yes."

The answer came out reflexively.

I took a deep breath and stopped.

And I said something he hadn't even asked me about.

"I thought there was a real fire..."

"Yes, I think they added smoke because the escape time limit was approaching."

*Added smoke.*

*Right.*

To make the ending feel more real, they could create effects like a building collapsing. I looked up.

Light was leaking from around the corner of the hall in front of me. It was the green light of the emergency exit.

"Ah."

I'd already walked past the darkest, unlit section.

I just didn't know.

Only then did my hearing return, and I could hear loud sirens, distant zombies growling, and the fake sounds of the building collapsing.

"You must have been scared going by yourself. I'm glad I followed you."

And the hand belonged to Ryu Chungwoo, who was smiling awkwardly. He'd come after me immediately, holding the laser gun.

I grinned.

"Yes. It's nice to have backup."

The useless thoughts faded into the background.

*What kind of stupidity is this during filming?*

None of what I'd been worrying about had happened.

And even if it happened, I could deal with it. Because I wasn't alone.

"Okay."

Ryu Chungwoo glanced at my face, then lightly patted me on the back and nodded.

"If you're going to go somewhere, it's better to have a weapon. I'll go with you."

"Yes. Thank you."

We really didn't have time anymore.

I suppressed the urge to click my tongue, thinking of the time I'd wasted getting lost in those poisonous thoughts.

I started running again, and Ryu Chungwoo immediately followed.

"Where are you going?"

"The room with the vent."

"Ah, the room where we found this laser gun. Are you going to look there again?"

"No."

I answered quickly.

"We're going to use it to get back to the room where we started."

"...Why that room?"

"There wasn't anything tricky about the first room, so I think we might have missed something."

All we'd had to do was find the vent and go through it.

Even then, the entrance had only been clumsily hidden by the production team. This wasn't the institute's usual curriculum, either.

*There must be a puzzle in that room for the setup to make sense.*

Ryu Chungwoo and I passed the actor who was perfectly playing a dead zombie and went up the stairs through the emergency exit.

That room was right above us. Conscious of the cameras, I spoke quickly.

"When I saw the request for a pass card at the back door, the first thing I thought of was the staff's ID cards."

"ID cards?"

"Yes. ID cards are common at research institutes like this. However, none of the people we'd met were wearing one."

I remembered the time of the group interview.

The expert conducting the interview hadn't worn an ID card around his neck or on his chest.

"In that case, wouldn't they have kept it in their pockets?"

"...!"

And there was one particular piece of clothing that had made a big impression on us.

I opened the door, passed the mannequin, and climbed into the vent.

The cabinets had already been moved away, so I just had to carefully climb over the actors pretending to be dead.

And I remembered...

The first sign that this content had shifted from a personality test to a zombie escape.

*– Aaackk!*

"At that time, the counselor fell into the corridor, and his white coat got caught in the door."

And of course, we'd been too shocked to take our eyes off it.

"There was no need for the gown to get caught in the door, but they deliberately drew our attention to it... Isn't that because the most important clue is there?"

Almost certain I was right, I reached the end of the vent and quickly scrambled out.

*I can see it.*

Directly across from me was the gown, trapped beneath the first iron door that had come down.

I ran right over, crouched down, and searched his white coat.

I felt a rectangular piece of plastic in the front pocket.

*There we go.*

I smiled.

"The pass card was here all along."

Then I took out an ID card bearing the person's name, face, and title.

On the back, in unmistakable letters, it even said, ‘Doubles as a pass card’.

"That's our Moondae, smart as always."

Ryu Chungwoo sounded impressed.

I put the ID card in my pocket and turned around.

"It's really not that hard—"

*Click.*

"...?"

The barrel of the laser gun was pointed at my face.

It was the laser gun Ryu Chungwoo had been holding—no, was still holding.

*What?*

I only moved my eyes and looked up at Ryu Chungwoo.

He spoke to me with a slightly troubled expression.

"I need you to give me that ID card, Moondae."

"......"

I paused for a moment to gather my thoughts, then spoke.

"It doesn't matter whether I hold onto it or you do, hyung. We'll still use it to open the back door."

"No. Our goals don't align."

Ryu Chungwoo kindly added an explanation.

"You're trying to escape. I'm trying to stop you guys."

"...!!"

"Hmm... In other words, I'm the villain. That's what happened."

*No, what bullshit was this?*

*But you helped the most with the escape so far!*

The benefits we gained from that marksmanship... *Wait a minute.*

In an instant, Ryu Chungwoo's past actions flashed before my eyes.

*– Excuse me, could you pull the cabinet away a little bit? I'll shoot it in the head.*

That bastard wasted two shots of the laser gun's battery on a zombie in the vent.

He'd taken the initiative and shot the zombie's arm without asking what anyone else thought.

And he'd always asked the others whether he should shoot, even when it wasn't necessary to shoot at all.

*So, he'd wasted time even in the sections where shooting wasn't necessary...*

In the first place, the escape route through this laboratory was designed so we could sneak through without using a weapon...

In other words, the only benefit of this bastard using the gun was peace of mind. In reality, he'd wasted our time.

*Wow...*

We'd been completely tricked.

I stuck out my tongue in disbelief at the production team's handiwork and looked at Ryu Chungwoo.

*Do I have time to ask why he was selected and get his backstory?*

There wasn't. I'd have them put that in an interview.

I got straight to the point.

"That only works for zombies."

Ryu Chungwoo calmly replied.

"No. It says in the instruction manual that it works on humans, too."

"Ah."

Whether it was true or not didn't matter. The possibility alone made the gun threatening enough.

If I got hit, then I would be out, and then there would be no chance of escaping.

Ryu Chungwoo could simply disappear with the ID card.

*It's tough.*

*How many minutes did we have left?*

Coincidentally, the countdown was being displayed on the huge screen in this room.

+=
#><[00:05:19]><#
=+

...They'd planned the route to make the ending as immersive as possible.

*5 minutes.*

It was close.

I sighed and raised my hands.

"...Wait a minute. Let me get the card out."

"Okay."

I put my hand into the biggest pocket of my coat.

And as soon as my hand closed around the object, I quickly pulled it out and aimed it at Ryu Chungwoo.

"...!"

"Good thing it works on people, too."

It was a laser gun.

It was the first laser gun we'd found, the one I'd taken from the hands of the security officer who'd died because of the zombie.

Ryu Chungwoo opened his mouth with a mixture of surprise and doubt.

"But the battery—"

"Oh, it's got juice."

Everyone had quickly forgotten about the second gun because Ryu Chungwoo was carrying one with twelve shots.

No.

I checked.

"This has a single shot left."

The energy meter showed there was only enough charge left for one shot.

Although I'd completed my military service as a public service worker, I'd still had a month of military training, so I'd kept it with me.

Now that I was holding it, I realized how different it was from the real thing. My experience probably wouldn't be much use.

*We didn't need two guns to escape.*

Ryu Chungwoo's gun had been enough, so no one had paid attention to the other one.

"Mmm."

Ryu Chungwoo smiled wryly as if he could feel a headache coming on. He looked defeated.

But soon, as if realizing something, he nodded his head.

"Moondae. But as long as we're confronting each other like this, I can keep you from using the ID card. You won't be able to get it to the back door."

"...!"

"Then, what's the point of all this?"

That was right.

*I should have shot him as soon as I took it out.*

If he'd been lying, we'd have ended up in a scuffle that wouldn't suit an idol group's own content. But Ryu Chungwoo wasn't the kind of person to lie about something like this.

*Then, should I shoot now?*

I didn't know if I could shoot earlier than that bastard... And that guy had two rounds left.

Just as I was pondering this...

*Teodeodeong!*

"Moondae-hyung! Chungwoo-hyung!"

"...!"

The vent opposite me swung open with a loud noise.

And cheerful voices were heard.

"I was right! Here you are!"

"Wow! You're right!"

"W-we're here..."

The guys, who'd been crawling out of the vent and waving cheerfully, froze when they saw us.

"Uh, uh, uh."

We were aiming laser guns at each other, after all.

"What's happening!"

I opened my mouth unconsciously.

"What are you guys doing here...?"

*I thought you guys were waiting at the door.*

"Well, you ran off by yourself, so of course we came after you!"

"That's right!"

"Yes, we were worried..."

I stared at them, taken aback.

*I didn't have time... I ran alone because I couldn't explain.*

Then... If I'd just told them to come with me without explaining, would they have followed me?

*Yes. And that would've made for better footage, too.*

My mind answered, and I laughed involuntarily.

*Looks like I've still got some old habits to unlearn.*

One of the guys was completely stricken, though. Kim Raebin looked at me and Ryu Chungwoo, his eyes wavering.

"Our team, which should have stuck together as one until we escaped, has split internally..."

"......"

I needed to clear up the misunderstanding.

I opened my mouth right away.

"I just found the pass card in this gown, but Chungwoo-hyung tried to take it from me. It seems he's a spy."

Then I took the ID card out of my pocket and waved it.

"Oh, my God!"

"Huh! Chungwoo-hyung!"

"Wow! I knew it was in the gown... No, then we have to subdue him now!" Bae Sejin said.

In an instant, the mood changed again.

And since it was now six against one, we'd practically won.

*Four minutes. That's more than enough time to escape.*

But at that moment, I felt a sudden chill.

Why had these guys just taken my word for it? Both of us were pointing guns at each other.

Wasn't it even more suspicious that I was holding a gun when I hadn't used one this entire time?

*If Ryu Chungwoo claimed that I was the spy and he was trying to stop me...*

"Haha."

...But that didn't seem likely to happen. Ryu Chungwoo wore a resigned expression, as if saying, ‘I got caught’.

Well, he didn't seem like the sort of guy to resort to that kind of trickery, anyway.

"Well, this won't work. I will surrender."

Ryu Chungwoo put down the laser gun and raised his hands, smiling as though he was proud of me.

"Whoa!"

The members rushed over and took Ryu Chungwoo's laser gun, exclaiming and screaming in excitement. The final twist seemed to have been a lot of fun for them.

"We're escaping!"

"No, we have to run first!"

"That's right!"

Then, playfully threatening Ryu Chungwoo, we made him run with us to the back door.

We held the ID card up to the door lock, and...

*Tirik.*

"It's opening!"

"Good...!"

It worked. We went out into the open air, our arms around each other's shoulders.

The sun was setting.

A pleasant breeze blew against my face.

"Successfully escaped with 29 seconds left!"

"We did amazing!"

I went along with their celebration. Honestly, I felt pretty satisfied with the outcome.

After talking for a while about what happened behind the scenes and what each of us had experienced...

"Ah~ everyone worked hard!"

"Oh, come on!"

The production crew came back to us, grinning.

Besides, the cameras were still running, probably trying to capture our annoyed reactions. *The audacity, huh?*

"Who made Chungwoo-hyung betray us? Isn't it too much for our leader hyung~?"

"Chungwoo-ssi seemed to like it, though!"

"Haha, that's not true."

"Chungwoo-ssi, that's not what you said when we suggested it!"

After a few minutes of playful banter, we finally got to the main point.

"So, who are our roommates?"

"Mhm, that's right. I'm curious how they'll be decided."

Right, deciding on our new roommates had been the original purpose of this whole uproar. We looked at the crew, waiting for a proper answer.

"That..."

The PD smiled broadly.

"You'll find out when you watch episode 1~"

"Oh, really!!"

That was how TeSTAR were tricked by the production crew until the very end.

...It had been fun, so I decided to forgive them.
