# Chapter 333 - A Confession, II

Lee Sejin also speculated about the 'secret' of Park Moondae in his own way.

Some of them were unlikely, but he thought they actually made a lot of sense.

*Is he the real Park Moondae's older cousin?*

Perhaps the Park Moondae he knew was the older cousin and was using his dongsaeng's identity for some reason.

Of course, trying to think of a reason for it led him down such an absurd rabbit hole that he soon gave up.

*He said he'd tell me.*

After hearing those words, he'd carefully put together everything that had seemed odd about Park Moondae and gotten him to acknowledge it.

Still, he'd entertained some pretty wild guesses for a while. At one point, he'd even wondered if Moondae was a North Korean spy.

Amnesia, working hard to see results, and even manipulating public opinion all fit perfectly.

Of course, he immediately laughed the idea off.

*What is this, a drama? He's no spy.*

But now, Park Moondae said something more unrealistic than that.

*– I am not Park Moondae. I've returned to the past.*

It was the sort of thing a cult leader would say.

He broke out in a cold sweat.

*What?*

A dream... Weren't dreams usually like this?

Without thinking, Lee Sejin reached behind him and pinched his thigh hard.

*Ah, damn it.*

...It hurt.

Meanwhile, Park Moondae continued to speak.

"...I was preparing for the civil service exam, and my name was Ryu Gunwoo."

"......"

"The video call you saw yesterday was with my original body, and the real Park Moondae is in there now."

Lee Sejin tried to answer calmly.

"Ah... Really?"

*Really, my fucking ass.*

He had to think properly before reacting, but the words were coming out of his mouth without passing through his brain.

Lee Sejin stood frozen, trying to figure out what Park Moondae was getting at...

"Yes. I can make a video call again right now if you want. You can see for yourself."

*This is insane.*

"...Hmm."

Lee Sejin was confused. Now he was speechless.

Noticing Lee Sejin's dazed expression, Park Moondae smiled wryly.

"You don't believe me, do you?"

"...!"

"Well... I thought so."

Park Moondae said as if it was not a big deal.

"That's why I've been putting off telling you until now. I don't really have any tangible evidence. Nothing but testimonies."

But the body language of the other person said otherwise.

His friend was in a cold sweat.

*Ah...*

Lee Sejin felt a headache coming on. Oddly enough, his mind simultaneously snapped to attention as if he'd received an electric shock or put ice in his mouth.

Was the Park Moondae he knew the sort of person to make up a fairy tale at a time like this?

*No.*

Absolutely not.

There was no way the guy who'd carried on with a concert despite a cracked rib and never missed a schedule no matter how little he'd slept, even when his dreams disturbed him, would lie about something like this.

He'd never seen anyone work as fiercely as Park Moondae, and that was still true.

And if he wasn't mistaken, his friend was sincere now.

"I knew you'd suspect me of being mentally unstable or something. What I mean is..."

"No."

"......"

Lee Sejin moved toward him.

Then, with the table in between, he sat across from Park Moondae.

"I'll listen, so take your time explaining."

"...!"

A brief flash of surprise passed over Park Moondae's face, followed by another faint expression.

He smiled.

He looked relieved, just for a moment.

"All right, then."

And Park Moondae seemed to relax a little.

"Then... To start from the beginning, I woke up in Park Moondae's body a few months before ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ started."

From then on, events Lee Sejin knew well unfolded from an unexpected perspective.

Park Moondae told him how he'd participated in ⟨Relisted! Idol Incorporated⟩ and achieved one success after another.

Of course, some stories were hard to understand.

"I was told I'd die if I didn't become an idol."

"Mmm."

He said that some kind of instruction appeared in the air, bringing to mind symptoms of actual mental illness. Lee Sejin tensed up and kept his mouth shut.

*That is... Is it schizophrenia?*

Of course, Lee Sejin wasn't particularly interested in this field and hadn't had time to look into it, so he couldn't be sure. Fortunately, he was soon able to relax his tense expression.

This was because Park Moondae clearly wasn't suffering from delusions of persecution or fluctuations in his mental state.

More than anything, he didn't want to suspect Park Moondae of something like that right now. It felt wrong.

So instead, he just listened.

With little sounds of acknowledgment here and there.

"Okay."

"Hm, that's right."

He even made a joke like this.

"Then, is that why you became close with me and Ahyeon? Because you knew we'd do well in the future?"

Of course, he wasn't entirely joking.

Park Moondae looked a little more at ease and grinned back.

"Don't say shit like that. I had no intention of becoming friends, but you roped me in with the others."

Park Moondae said that, from what he remembered of his life as Ryu Gunwoo, neither Lee Sejin nor Seon Ahyeon had debuted in the group.

"Ah."

It wasn't a thought that fit the situation, but he felt a little pleased.

In the end, whatever the truth, Park Moondae had dealt with the photoshopped cigarette picture out of pure kindness.

*It's funny, really.*

Even while listening to a story so unrealistic it sounded like fiction, he was getting hung up on the little things.

In the meantime, Park Moondae moved quickly past their debut and into their activities as TeSTAR.

And then something came up that was so unbelievable it seemed to fit perfectly into a missing spot in the puzzle.

"He's the guy who came back to the past before me."

"...!"

VTIC's Cheongryeo.

"...Cheongryeo."

"You don't sound very respectful when you say sunbaenim's name," Lee Sejin commented.

"Why should I call him sunbaenim? Damn it..."

After all, he was a real madman.

Park Moondae didn't go into much detail, simply telling him that Cheongryeo had also returned to the past and had threatened him to find out about the future. But Lee Sejin read between the lines.

Just how unreasonable Cheongryeo had been.

*...Isn't he a psychopath?*

He'd really come close to killing Park Moondae out of anger because he wouldn't give him proper information about the future.

Goosebumps ran up his spine.

However, Park Moondae seemed strangely forgiving.

"Don't worry, he's managed to get his act together."

It wasn't simply a case of, 'If we report him to the police, TeSTAR will lose out too, so let's move on.'

Park Moondae had a generous side that came out with people he was close to.

*...So you shared the same experiences, is that it?*

Lee Sejin cut the thought short and suppressed the brief surge of displeasure.

His capacity for accepting all this had long since reached its limit, so it was time to move on instead of picking faults.

Besides, the next bomb was waiting.

"And speaking of Chungwoo-hyung... He is my relative."

"...!"

"We're in the same Pungsan Ryu clan. We're more closely related than I thought."

Another piece of the puzzle fell into place, and the situation became clearer.

That was why Park Moondae had avoided Ryu Chungwoo at first, then quickly grown close to him after they became roommates recently.

*And when the two of them went out together...*

"...You went to meet Ryu Gunwoo together," Lee Sejin said.

"......"

Park Moondae didn't bother to mention the accident involving his parents and Ryu Chungwoo, but Lee Sejin had already heard enough to put much of it together.

*So, Chungwoo-hyung...*

Come to think of it, his attitude toward Park Moondae had changed a little recently.

It all fit. In the end, Lee Sejin understood what Park Moondae had meant by saying he'd been 'found out'.

In addition, Park Moondae didn't neglect to explain how the other member had gotten involved.

"Bae Sejin-hyung only found out about Ryu Gunwoo because it was Chuseok. I was at hyung's house. He doesn't know the details at all."

"......"

By this point, Park Moondae even began to feel a slight sense of shame.

*It wasn't even anything special, and I made such a fuss...*

Park Moondae's explanation so far was unrealistic enough to make him doubt his ears, but there was a neatness to it that came from careful thought.

Lee Sejin could easily picture how nervous he must have been to tell him this nonsense.

*I shouldn't have gotten mad.*

He murmured frankly.

"...I'm sorry. I pressured you."

Park Moondae laughed.

"You had every right. I brought it up with you a long time ago."

Park Moondae continued to recount his experiences.

In fact, Park Moondae left out the daydream he'd had during his coma and some of the Reveal the Truth moments that didn't need to be mentioned, but the content was still extensive.

Park Moondae remained serious throughout, and it was clear that he was trying to answer Lee Sejin's questions truthfully without being evasive.

There was no fixation on useless details or repetition of the same words, as was often seen when someone was bluffing or lying.

"......"

Thanks to this, Lee Sejin found himself completely immersed in this unrealistic story.

And the story flowed to the most recent incident.

Park Moondae, who briefly returned to Ryu Gunwoo's body.

"...So, I came back to the point where we won the **daesang** and gave my acceptance speech. That's why I suddenly forgot the choreography."

Park Moondae wrapped up his story, looking satisfied and relieved.

"And that's my 'situation'."

"......"

Lee Sejin looked at Park Moondae.

Then he got up and brought him water. Park Moondae did not hesitate and took the plastic bottle and put it to his mouth.

As Park Moondae emptied half the bottle, Lee Sejin silently gathered his thoughts.

*Well? Well...*

*I'm dying of confusion.* He couldn't quite understand it... It was probably a more unrealistic explanation for all those oddities than anyone could've imagined.

*Not even a drama would have a plot this absurd.*

He was immersed in the story because of how seriously it was told, but his brain refused to make sense of most of it.

As Park Moondae said, there was no visible evidence.

*But even so...*

"Now I see. You put it off because you thought I wouldn't believe it."

"......"

"Certainly, Moondae knows me well. But... there's something else you should know about me."

Lee Sejin smiled.

"I really believe in you, Moondae~"

"...!"

After all, how realistic was it that Lee Sejin had debuted successfully, become a popular first-tier idol, and was touring and performing concerts in front of tens of thousands of people?

If he'd heard what his future held half a year before their season of ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ aired, he'd have laughed at the person who told him and gone back to stewing over his hopeless reality.

But he'd done it.

*I did it with him.*

Perhaps that was more unrealistic for Lee Sejin.

So he stopped laughing and answered seriously.

"I've always done that, and I'm going to do it this time, too. I believe in you, Park Moondae."

"......"

Lee Sejin added a joke.

"Again, whenever I believe in you, the results are always good. All the members think so, don't they? I mean, Park Moondae's just too good at this."

At that moment, Park Moondae laughed.

"...Thanks."

Park Moondae emptied the rest of the water.

Lee Sejin pretended not to know that the hand holding the plastic bottle was shaking slightly.

Instead, he realized again how absurd the explanation he'd just accepted as truth was.

*Well, I'm not getting any sleep anytime soon.*

His head would be full of all sorts of thoughts and guesses all night, but Lee Sejin didn't show it.

Then, Park Moondae seemed to open up and say all sorts of things that had been on his mind.

"I'm sure you realized it while listening to my story."

His friend, who had crumpled a plastic bottle, muttered hesitantly.

"I didn't originally intend to become an idol. You may be disappointed."

*What the hell is he talking about?*

"Park Moondae, are you an idiot?"

"What?"

"You love being an idol so much, but you're saying something like, 'I didn't mean to do it originally~'. What are you even saying?"

"...!"

"There's no one here who *intended* to become an idol from the very beginning... If you did it just to survive, that's fine. In fact, that makes you better than most of us."

Park Moondae looked uncharacteristically bewildered.

"Anyway, what should I call you? Should I call you Gunwoo? Gunwoogunwoo?"

Lee Sejin deliberately teased him, and Park Moondae shuddered a little, just as he'd expected.

The atmosphere lightened a bit.

"Forget it. I've lived as Park Moondae for years now."

"I know. We're all from a survival show, but everyone uses stage names these days. Park Moondae is your name, too."

"......"

"Isn't that right, Moondaemoondae?"

"...Yes."

Lee Sejin smiled.

In fact, he suddenly had the urge to ask if he really hadn't heard anything about him before returning to the past, but he held back.

*It couldn't have been anything good.*

If it hadn't been for Park Moondae, he would've voluntarily dropped out of the show due to the school bullying controversy... Even if Park Moondae knew about him, it wouldn't have been good news.

So instead, he said this.

"Thank you. It must have been hard to tell, but you still told me."

"......"

"Honestly, it was embarrassing to say something like 'There are words floating in the air that only I can see', right? I could see how embarrassed you were... Ow!"

"Shut it."

"Haha!"

Keun Sejin laughed as he dodged Park Moondae's hand.

That night, Park Moondae and Lee Sejin talked at length before they had to go to bed to get in shape for the next day's concert.

"Then how old are you, really?"

"I was 29 years old right before I... returned."

"Oh, how should I address you? Hyung?"

"No, it's okay."

"Wow... I expected as much. After all, you're a professional idol. It's better to be young than to be treated like an elder... Wow! You didn't get me this time!"

"Tsk." Moondae had tried to sock him in the arm.

Most of their conversation was silly, but it was a strange feeling to hear personal details he'd never heard before.

"...You're from a very prestigious university, Moondaemoondae! Isn't it a waste?"

"It's not a waste. What, do you think I could be earning more money?"

Park Moondae seemed comfortable.

So Lee Sejin could finally stop regretting getting angry with his friend over nothing.

There wasn't a drop of alcohol, but the conversation flowed naturally and only came to an end after they'd laid a solid foundation.

It was a new layer of trust.

And the next day...

Unlike yesterday, Lee Sejin found his usual joy returning as he went up onstage without any negative emotions weighing him down.

*Good, good!*

With Cha Eugene and Seon Ahyeon on either side of him, he showed off some freestyle choreography, then came down humming after a splendid encore.

And the moment he tried to find Park Moondae...

"Looks like you guys did a good job reconciling."

"...!"

Lee Sejin looked back at the group's leader, who'd lightly tapped him on the shoulder.

Ryu Chungwoo smiled lightly.

*So it showed.*

Lee Sejin immediately lowered his head.

"Hyung, I'm so sorry! I've been a little... lax these past few days."

"No, you were fine. It's just that I saw what happened... I decided to meddle a little in case things were difficult for the two of you."

"......"

*Meddle?*

Come to think of it, last night their meeting time for this morning had suddenly been pushed back by about three hours.

He thought it was because of the schedule adjustment.

"Don't tell me you delayed the meeting time so..."

"Yeah, well... We all started later so we could recover our stamina."

It was a thoughtful gesture that allowed the two of them to talk comfortably on their own.

"...Thank you."

"We're in the same group. We have to be understanding of each other about things like this if we're going to last a long time. Everyone agreed right away."

Ryu Chungwoo laughed. Lee Sejin was speechless for a moment.

It was because he'd thought no one was as serious as he was about keeping the group together for as long as possible.

*...Even so, I'd been worried that I'd lose all of them to individual activities once they were more experienced.*

Thinking he was the most invested... That may have been a bit conceited, Lee Sejin admitted to himself.

In the meantime, Ryu Chungwoo's words continued.

"Sejin—Bae Sejin—was the first to suggest it. He was very worried about you two."

"I'm glad it worked out well. Oh, there he is."

Following Ryu Chungwoo's gesture with his chin, he turned his head and saw Bae Sejin talking with Park Moondae, who was standing next to the steel structure with a towel in his hand.
