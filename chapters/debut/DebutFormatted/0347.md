"All the time?"

*I see.*

So, it seemed that whenever I opened the status window, this guy was alerted and given new information.

*I think I could use this in an emergency.*

The possibility was extremely slim, but I could end up stranded on another uninhabited island within the next few months, for instance. I nodded.

"Okay. I suppose it's nice to have one more way to contact each other."

*Or it* would *be nice if you weren't running short on time.*

$p
**[I know, right?]**

Then the window grew a little smaller, and a new message slowly appeared, as if he were hesitating.

$p

"Of course that's fine."

$p
**[Thank you!]**

The window grew again. It certainly seemed to convey his emotions quite well.

And he started pouring out his feelings in earnest.

$p

"Do my stats look good to you?"

$p
**[Yes! I'm so excited that my heart's about to jump out of my chest!]**

Watching TeSTAR had been his sole source of joy during his entrance-exam days, and now he sounded exactly like one of the fans, too.

I opened my mouth to respond, but another message appeared first.

$p
**[I'm really rooting for you all! Fighting! Oh, I need to get back to work now! Lunch break is oveeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeer.]**
p$

"......"

The pop-ups glitched and vanished in an instant.

*He's really using it like KakaoTalk.*

...I guess it would take some time to get used to. I rubbed my shoulders and closed the status window.

It was time to move on and return to reality.

What mattered now was our upcoming first performance.

*The preparations are done.*

We'd practiced endlessly, gained plenty of practical experience, and even prepared a trump card.

...Somehow, it sounded less like a live performance and more like we were entering the World's Best Martial Arts Competition.

The thought gave me pause. I shook my head and grinned.

*Well, what's so different about it?*

I wouldn't be wielding a sword or anything, but wasn't it the same in that we couldn't afford to embarrass ourselves?

*Let's go all in on this fight.*

Since we'd been called in as last-minute replacements, we couldn't add any special effects or devices. The only thing we had was a firework originally installed on the stage.

That was even better. If people were impressed by this performance, all the credit would go to TeSTAR alone.

"Moondae, we have to go to the practice room...!" Seon Ahyeon said.

"Okay."

I left the room.

Our first performance was right around the corner.

"Tsk."

After all, she'd been to the pre-recording herself.

It was really unlike her to go directly to a music-show pre-recording, which, unlike official events or concerts, was notorious for treating fans like rubbish.

*Was it worth enduring yet another broadcast station's neglect and waiting around in a huge crowd all day, starting early in the morning?*

It certainly had been.

No, it was to the point where she felt she should've paid for the pleasure of being there.

*It's even better that the audio was recorded completely live.*

It was better that there hadn't been any audio enhancements. The guys she knew would never neglect keeping their throats, ankles, and everything in between in top condition.

The office worker nodded to herself and calmly finished editing the photo she'd been working on.

However, her hands moved almost as if they were dancing to a rhythm, betraying her excitement.
