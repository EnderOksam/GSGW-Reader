# Chapter 299 - The Future People, II

Normally, I wouldn't be surprised to hear my roommate's voice in the middle of the night, but this was different.

*I'm going crazy.*

Earlier that day, Ryu Chungwoo had gone as a guest on a two-day, one-night camping variety show.

So I was supposed to have the room to myself, and I'd started the video call without a worry.

[Well...]

"Wait a moment."

*Tak.*

I closed my laptop, got up, and headed for the door.

"Hyung, just a second."

And I opened the door.

Ryu Chungwoo, casually dressed, stood with a somewhat puzzled expression. 

I was just as puzzled.

*Why was he here?*

"Um, I thought you were filming."

"Ah, one of the cast members had some kind of problem, so I'll be joining them again tomorrow evening."

Ryu Chungwoo touched the back of his neck a few times and spoke in a slightly embarrassed tone.

"But the door was locked... If you were sleeping, I was just going to sleep in the living room, but I heard noises from inside."

He wouldn't be so calm if he heard what I was saying.

*The room's pretty soundproof, too.*

I held back a sigh and stepped aside.

"The guys were making a lot of noise outside earlier, so I locked the door. I guess I forgot to unlock it. Sorry."

"There is nothing to be sorry about."

He seemed to mean it, which made it even funnier that he'd accepted my excuse. I silently thanked Cha Eugene and sat down on my bed.

I was going to wait a little bit, and when Ryu Chungwoo went to wash up, I'd take the laptop and go out...

"Moondae, your laptop's still on."

"I just played a game."

Ryu Chungwoo seemed a little shocked.

"This late?"

"Yes, well. I figured one night of gaming wouldn't hurt."

This stupid excuse came to mind because I'd just been thinking about Cha Eugene. I felt my dignity crumbling.

He answered uncertainly.

"Hmm... Staying up too late playing isn't good for you. Why don't you leave it for when you have some time off?"

"Right..."

I answered reluctantly.

"I'll just turn it off now."

"Okay. Our next schedule's in the afternoon, but it's already 3 in the morning."

Ryu Chungwoo smiled faintly and began to take out his pajamas.

I held back my desire to punch the bed and made a rational decision.

*He'll go in to wash up in 30 seconds.*

I'd pretend to turn off the laptop, then take it out with me—

"Um, Moondae. Since now seems as good a time as any... Is it okay if I ask you something?"

"Sure."

Ryu Chungwoo, clothes in hand, spoke very cautiously.

I should've taken that as a warning.

"About that VTIC sunbaenim."

*Oh, damn.*

I stifled a groan at the mention of the bastard currently on the call.

*The lid's closed, so he probably can't hear anything.*

"From what Sejin said, it sounds like something happened. Did he do something to you?"

"Hmm..."

I quickly calculated how much of the truth I was willing to tell him.

I decided to downplay it.

First of all, I'd make it clear that it was in the past so he wouldn't get worked up.

"Something did happen, but it's in the past."

I focused on explaining that it wasn't my fault rather than going into the incident itself.

"He picked a fight, so we fought. I didn't hit him first; I hit him back."

"He picked a fight?"

"Yes. So, we... We had a fistfight."

"......"

He was clearly taken aback.

*Good.* I figured this would go much like my conversation with the two Sejins.

"That... It sounds like he tried to hit his Hubaenim first, and you fought back."

*Exactly.*

"Yes. He's older and has more experience... He shouldn't have picked a fight with me. I was right to fight back."

"Right."

*Was this the end?*

It was about time for some comforting words or advice to wrap up the conversation...

"But did you become close enough to play games with that sunbaenim?"

I froze.

*Shit.*

The surprises just kept coming tonight.

However, Ryu Chungwoo didn't seem to be prying. He was just confused.

"When I came in, I heard his voice through your earphones. I wasn't trying to eavesdrop."

It must've taken the laptop a few seconds to enter sleep mode. *Why does he have such sharp hearing, anyway?*

"Is he bothering you in any way?"

...He seemed a bit worried too.

Perhaps, piecing together everything he'd seen of my interactions with Cheongryeo, including that recording of me speaking informally to him, he suspected that I was being bullied.

Did he think Cheongryeo was using me as cannon fodder in the 'game' I'd said I'd been playing until dawn?

*He used to be an athlete, so he must've seen plenty of that sort of thing.*

I shrugged.

"It's nothing. I'm comfortable around him now that we've gotten it all out of our systems. He apologized to me, too."

"Hmm."

"I'm using him well, so you don't have to worry."

"...Using him?"

"Well... He's easy to push around now that we've settled things. I just get him to help me out from time to time. Don't worry."

"Push around..."

Ryu Chungwoo seemed to understand something, but soon a worry flashed across his face.

And he asked hastily.

"Moondae, you're not bullying him back, are you?"

*I'm the one who gets pissed off every time because of that bastard.*

"Of course not."

I took some time to reassure Ryu Chungwoo, who had started worrying about the opposite situation. 

"Hyung, do I seem like that kind of person?"

"No. Of course not. But, um, since you're so smart... If you ever want to get back at him, remember you don't have to be like him—"

"Don't say anything so obvious and go wash up."

Only then did Ryu Chungwoo retreat to the bathroom with an awkward smile. I told him I'd explain what was going on to the person I was playing with and end the game, then carried my laptop out to the living room.

I opened it with my back to the veranda. I'd planned to close the call app and shut it down, but surprisingly, Cheongryeo's face was still on the screen.

[Ah.]

It seemed that he'd been writing something down, perhaps working on a song.

But he didn't bother hanging up this video call?

[Done talking?]

"I didn't know you were still connected."

[I wanted to hear the answer to the question.]

*Oh, so we're back to that.*

*Why is everyone questioning me today?*

I rubbed my throbbing forehead and replied quietly.

"I can't say it out loud, so let's use the chat."

[All right.]

@ll@– CHR: Like this?@ll@

@rr@– Apple: Yes@rr@

@ll@– CHR: ^^@ll@

@ll@– CHR: So why chase information you can't even use?@ll@

*Why the hell do I have to tell you that?*

I hesitated for a moment, but remembering his contribution to the conversation, I quickly typed out a reply.

@rr@– Apple: There's something I want to figure out by analyzing the pattern.@rr@

@ll@– CHR: What is it?@ll@

@rr@– Apple: I don't know why I'm here@rr@

@rr@– Apple: My memory back then is hazy@rr@

I heard a low grunt through my earphones.

I thought he was lost in thought, but his next response left me dumbfounded.

@ll@– CHR: I thought you wished to become an idol as someone else.@ll@

@rr@– Apple: I told you I was preparing for the civil service exam.@rr@

@rr@– Apple: My only connection to the industry was taking and selling photos.@rr@

@ll@– CHR: Could it be that you secretly developed a longing for idols?@ll@

*This bastard, seriously...*

I kept my expression neutral and calmly typed back.

@rr@– Apple: Judging by your case and Kwon Heeseung's, both of you had very specific wishes.@rr@

@rr@– Apple: Do you think I had one?@rr@

Laughter came from the earphones. I was about to mute him but stopped at the last moment.

@ll@– CHR: Alright.@ll@

Okay.

@ll@– CHR: But I don't think that's the only thing.@ll@

*What?*

@ll@– CHR: You're not the sort of person who shows his hand for so little payoff.@ll@

@ll@– CHR: Any other reasons?@ll@

*Fine.* I had the status window and missions at my disposal, so I could afford to tell him a little more.

There was a way to explain it without bringing those up. I paused for a moment, then slowly typed a reply.

@rr@– Apple: I don't want this insanity to continue.@rr@

In the end, it all came back to this.

Wish or no wish, it was insane to deal with 'status abnormalities' without a status window.

Even with one, how many times had I almost died?

...And I didn't even know how many times the guy on the other side had died.

*We're given neither a choice nor any knowledge of what's going on. It's madness.*

That was definitely part of why I'd acted on the clues I had instead of waiting for a chance to talk to 'Park Moondae'.

Cheongryeo didn't seem entirely convinced, though. He glanced at the monitor.

@ll@– CHR: So you mean you'd rather just die?@ll@

@rr@– Apple: Of course not.@rr@

*Jumping to conclusions again, are we?*

@rr@– Apple: There are other ways to keep people from dying.@rr@

@rr@– Apple: Donations, campaigns, things like that.@rr@

@rr@– Apple: Sending people back to the past without their consent and killing them if they can't clear some arbitrary mission isn't it.@rr@

A short answer came back.

@ll@– CHR: Are you unhappy now?@ll@

He probably found it ridiculous coming from me, considering how much I'd benefited from it.

I typed again.

@rr@– Apple: We are successful cases@rr@

@rr@– Apple: Who knows what happened to the ones who failed?@rr@

Kwon Heeseung's status abnormality.

It only mentioned 'failure'; it didn't specify the consequences.

*Cheongryeo's explicitly stated that he'd have to restart.*

If so, Heeseung might simply die if he failed, with no further chances, just like with my status abnormality.

What would happen after that... I didn't like the thought of it.

@rr@– Apple: It won't be easy to find the next target.@rr@

@rr@– Apple: But if we analyze the pattern, we might come up with a way to stop it.@rr@

@rr@– Apple: Isn't it worth a try?@rr@

Time and space were factors we could control.

[...]

He didn't respond for a few seconds, only moving his hand over the mouse a few times.

Just as I realized he was petting his dog, he answered.

[Perhaps you're right.]

I nodded slowly.

And the guy on the screen suddenly broke into a grin.

*What?*

@ll@– CHR: And...@ll@

@ll@– CHR: You want to prevent any unknown variables from threatening your career? People with knowledge of the future, for instance?@ll@

"......"

*Fine.*

@rr@– Apple: Why state the obvious?@rr@

[Hahaha!!]

His laughter sounded like it might carry beyond the earphones. Disgusted, I finally muted him.

@ll@– CHR: Kicking down the ladder you climbed up. I approve.@ll@

@ll@– CHR: Alright. I'll keep cooperating, Hubaenim :)@ll@

"This bastard—"

"Are you still playing the game?"

At that moment, I realized again that I was sitting in the living room of the dorm.

"No. Wait a minute."

I did a good job of putting it in silent mode.

@rr@– Apple: I'm logging off.@rr@

This time I closed the app and turned off the laptop completely.

Ryu Chungwoo was sticking out his upper body from the room with a subtle smile.

"It looks like the game was a lot of fun."

"I will delete it."

"There's no need to go that far—"

"No. I'm doing this for myself."

After silently enduring Ryu Chungwoo's encouragement, I lay down on the bed.

"I will close the door."

"Yes."

*Let's sleep properly.*

I told Ryu Chungwoo I was setting my alarm for 10 in the morning, then put my smartphone down.

I'd talked more than I normally did, and I was more tired than I'd expected.

*...I think I've gone through all the information I have right now.*

The system transferred to candidates close in both time and space.

Figuring out that pattern was a huge achievement. But the more I learned, the more chilling it became.

*How does the system know who'll try to take their own life in the future?*

No, the very act of sending someone back to the past after they'd tried to take their own life should've been impossible in the first place.

I glanced at my status window.

★-
\
[#*Name: Park Moondae (Ryu Gunwoo)*#]
-★

The rest of my details were listed below.

*Why do I still have this even though the system's moved on?*

The more I analyzed the pattern, the more I seemed to be an exception, which left me with a strange feeling.

In the end, though, I still felt that I'd made the right choice.

*I really need to talk to 'Park Moondae'.*

I couldn't deny it. There was a good chance that conversation would give me the missing piece.

*And at this rate, at least one grand prize shouldn't be too hard to win...*

When I thought that far, I fell asleep.

For the first time in a while, I slept deeply and without dreams.

And the next day...

*Geeeeeeeing-!*

"......"

*I slept well.*

I turned off my 10 o'clock alarm and cleared the notifications and messages on the screen. Among them was one from an entertainment newsletter app I'd recently subscribed to, though I didn't pay much attention to it.

★$
[“We hardly feel the army hiatus” The six-member group T-Holic makes a comeback]
$★

As it turned out later, I'd practically swiped away a disaster warning.
