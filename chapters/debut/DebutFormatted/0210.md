# Chapter 210 - Back on Track

Ryu Chungwoo continued his speech in a not particularly heavy tone.

"When I first found out about the after-effects, I thought it would be manageable. Everyone told me I would be fine with rehabilitation."

"......"

"The doctor also said that it seemed possible, so I kept at it... but it didn't work."

Out of habit, Ryu Chungwoo clenched his hand into a fist and released it.

"Potential was just that—potential. In reality, I couldn't recover."

"......"

"That's why I got so angry at the agency. Someone could have gotten hurt. But then I wondered if I was wasting my time again."

Was that the cause of his lethargy?

It seemed like the thought 'What am I doing right now?' must have overlapped with his fruitless rehabilitation days.

*...It's understandable.*

He must have felt even more depressed since he was so overworked, too.

I felt a little conflicted but responded after emptying my drink.

"You've never done anything useless, hyung."

"You think so?"

"Yes. Everyone received a lot of help from you... It's the same for me too."

*Actually, you have an amazing personality simply because you didn't get mad when I behaved like a total jerk.*

If things had gotten worse for me back then... I would probably be dead by now due to the status abnormality.

"Ah, you mean back then? Hmm, I don't think so. You were good considering the situation. I told you back then. I was also like that when it was confirmed that I couldn't recover."

Ryu Chungwoo chuckled.

"I'm really not kidding. I got angry at the doctor... and it was even on the day of the team trials. I'm embarrassed even thinking about it."

I wasn't really curious about it, but anyone would know this was the point where you're supposed to ask, so I did.

"What did you do?"

"I broke my bow."

"...!!"

*Wow, this bastard... that wasn't some normal temper.*

A professional archery bow was not something that just broke, so he must have really gone nuts then.

Of course, it would be stupid of me to answer with my exact thoughts.

"Well, you didn't hit anyone, so that's good."

"Is that so? Haha! Yeah, well, I did break the one meant for practice."

Ryu Chungwoo smiled with a much more refreshed face.

I wasn't sure if it was okay to get cooled off with something like that... let's leave it alone.

Still, as he seemed to have come to his senses, he added socializing remarks.

"Ah, I'm sorry. It might have been a bit uncomfortable for you to hear that..."

It sounded like he meant that I would be reminded of the time I went nuts at Ryu Chungwoo.

"I'm okay. It doesn't really bother me anymore. I'm the one who should apologize."

"I already said it's okay."

One way to defeat a worry was to think about a bigger one.

After I saw the status window go completely haywire, the truth I'd learned in the past didn't matter much anymore.

However, Ryu Chungwoo fiddled with his plastic bottle and blurted out words that seemed to come out unconsciously.

"Um, Moondae-ya."

"Yes."

"Actually, there's something that has been bothering me for a while now..."

"...? Yes."

Ryu Chungwoo frowned.

"How is your memory?"

"...!!"

"I thought it might be a good idea to see an expert on that matter."

An unthinkable topic came out.

To be honest, I've almost forgotten that I was using the 'I can't remember my past well, but it's slowly coming back to me' setting.

I mean, there have been so many incidents.

*...Looking at it like this, it's no different from a soap opera set-up.*

In any case, I didn't expect the excuse I had spat out before to suddenly come back to bite me in the butt.

And Ryu Chungwoo spat out a series of spine-chilling words.

"You've never gone to see your parents or seen pictures of them... I don't think you've ever brought them up either."

"......"

"We've been living together for over a year and a half, but it's strange that you never mention your parents, even in passing."

"...Mm."

I quickly racked my brains.

"I have very few memories of my family. I'm not really in a hurry to find out about it either."

"I see."

"Yes. I'm just satisfied with the present."

"Mm, okay."

Ryu Chungwoo crumpled and folded the plastic bottle, then took a deep breath.

"You're right. I also like the present."

I asked him, mentally picturing him refuting his own words.

"Even when exhausted?"

"I don't think there's a job that never makes you exhausted. It's just a matter of managing it well."

Yeah. His words were right.

"Honestly, I was skeptical when we left ⟨Idol Inc.⟩, but I'm grateful things worked out much better than I imagined."

"It sure did."

Apart from the stress, TeSTAR's steps were startlingly successful.

Although I did know that it would do well.

"I'm going to do my best never to take our success for granted."

*Wow. That was a healthy mindset.*

I smirked.

"Still, you should only do as much as an idol needs to do, like you have these past few days. You don't have to do the agency's work, either."

"Haha, well... I was going to work hard since I'm the leader after all."

At that moment, I heard Cha Eugene shouting from the other side of the room, having finally reached a new highest score.

"WOOOOOW!!"

"Be quiet, you idiot! It's already close to midnight, so the noise pollution..."

"I'm not noisy! There's only us here!"

"There are residents in nearby buildings..."

"There aren't!"

"There are!"

I muttered with full sincerity.

"...To be honest, I think you're doing well enough as a leader just from dealing with them."

"Mmm."

Seems like he couldn't deny that.

"I think doing that much is enough. At this rate, the manager hyung will forget why he's getting his salary."

Ryu Chungwoo burst into laughter as I added the words that sounded like a celebrity abusing their power.

"Haha! Okay, I got it."

Then he slightly stuck out his fist to the side.

"Let's keep doing well."

"Yes."

It felt a little cheesy, but it did leave a better feeling.

"We are done cleaning up~"
