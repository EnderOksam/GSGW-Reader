# Chapter 222 - An Overnight Getaway

TeSTAR's three-day vacation was something I had heard about a long time ago.

Surprisingly, the general manager had kept his promise that artists needed to be healthy and happy to perform at their best.

*Even though he doesn't invest in the management office for things like that.*

In fact, rather than caring about the company's singers, he seems to have more of an obsession with his management philosophy... Anyway, it was our benefit, so I had no intention of objecting.

Thanks to him, I'd be able to spend three quiet days alone.

Besides, there were some expectations.

*I can do some monitoring then.*

The only thing I've seen on the screen lately was furry four-legged beasts, so I was bitter.

"...Hmm."

Although I said I would control my usage, I didn't mean I wouldn't monitor anything during our break time.

At this level, negligence of duty was not honey, but high-fructose corn syrup.

So, I was going to take advantage of the time when they went back home and do my job.

And rest alone quietly.

...Of course, I've never really enjoyed a vacation thinking like this until now.

*Because I've never been on vacation that many times in the first place.*

This time, after blocking all variables, I would definitely be alone. I had such an idea...

But then an unexpected incident occurred.

I mean... maybe in a good way.

"Hyungs, what do you think about MT...!"

It's because Kim Raebin said this right before vacation.

"MT?"

"It's an abbreviation of membership training; it means a short trip to strengthen harmony and teamwork among colleagues."

"Hey, Raebin, we know that~ I asked if you meant that you wanted to do MT!"

"Ah, yes!"

Kim Raebin nodded enthusiastically at Keun Sejin's question.

The funny thing was that Cha Eugene was doing the same thing next to me.

*Did the two of them work together?*

I thought that my guess was right when I heard the following words.

"I hope to make up for the unfortunate point of not being able to share topics other than work while digesting a busy schedule!"

"Let's go play, I want to go to the sea in Korea!"

Cha Eugene answered coldly.

*Hmm, I guess I know roughly what the situation is.*

Cha Eugene couldn't go to the United States just for three days. And it would be ambiguous for his parents to come to Korea because it was not the holiday season.

*He'll be bored since he can't go home, so he'll have to hang out with the members.*

I was pretty sure that was why.

I shut my mouth, but suddenly I had a flash of realization.

*...If this fails, will Cha Eugene stay at the dorm for the rest of the vacation?*

Hey, no way.

"Oh, I like it? How about the rest of you?"

"I-I think I'll be fine too...!"

"...A day would be fine."

Votes were pouring in from everywhere. It seemed that Kim Raebin's opinion of taking a short trip while preserving the vacation as it is was quite plausible.

"What do you think, Moondae-hyung?"

I answered quickly.

"It's good. Let's go."

"...! Yes!"

I waited until the end to give my approval, hoping no one would propose anything else.

I had an idea.

*Someone has to make Cha Eugene go to their house afterward.*

Maybe during the MT, I would manage to avoid the situation, and some other members would invite Cha Eugene to their house for vacation.

"Oh- Moondae is active!"

"Yeah, it's good to go once. Is there a place Raebin has in mind?"

"I've got one! Let's go to Gang Island!"

"It's pronounced Ganghwa Island! ... And yes, hyung, I've seen a few places I'd like to stay at."

I contemplated when I touched my chin.

*Hm, are Ryu Chungwoo, Keun Sejin, and Kim Raebin candidates?*

The other two weren't in sync with Cha Eugene or felt burdened, so the possibility was extremely low.

I came up with a plan of my own while watching the guys talk about their destination.

And after a while...

"Then, considering the location and interior, we'll make a reservation here on Ganghwa Island. Are you all okay with that?"

"Yes, yes~."

"I'll talk to the company."

"Then, thank you for that."

I was ready to ask for the company's understanding and establish an emergency contact network.

The trip was only a few days away, so I wasn't worried about the destination leaking.

One of the managers would rent the car and register only Ryu Chungwoo as a driver, making it unlikely we'd be followed.

*The manager said he'd stay on standby in case we needed him.*

The manager had recently become noticeably cooperative. It seemed that the plan to form a dedicated team was in full swing, so he had a sense of crisis.

Anyway, finally, the reservation for the lodging was easily completed. It seemed that the price was high even in the off-season because of the security and facilities.

"Okay, the reservation is complete. Let's go and have fun!"

"Woooow!"

"Rae-Raebin, thanks for the good suggestion..."

"No need to thank me. Thank you for agreeing!"

Everyone was excited.

I leaned back on the sofa, resting my hands behind my head.

*It's our first trip like this that isn't for making content.*

Traveling for leisure instead of work felt refreshing.

In fact, it was more like a workshop from a co-worker's perspective... But no one was particularly uncomfortable, so I didn't have to say anything.

Even Bae Sejin seemed to be looking forward to it.

"...It's the first time I've done something like this."

Seriously, this guy even said something like this in the rental car that day.

"Really?"

"Have you ever participated in various events such as school trips?"

"I see! Certainly, traveling with dozens of people is often inconvenient and limited in choice."

Kim Raebin nodded happily as if he had accepted it as an honest expression of likes and dislikes, but everyone else seemed to notice the subtle nuance.

*It seems that he didn't mix well with the school group life.*

It wasn't particularly surprising.
