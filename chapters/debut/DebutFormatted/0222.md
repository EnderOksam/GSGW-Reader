# Chapter 222

*Hm, are Ryu Chungwoo, Lee Sejin, and Kim Raebin candidates?*

"Then, considering the location and interior, we'll make a reservation here on Ganghwa Island. Are you all okay with that?"

"I'll talk to the company."

*And he said that he would wait for the manager to come if it was necessary.*

"That's too much. Thank you for your willingness to agree!"

Everyone was excited.

In fact, it was more like a workshop from a co-worker's perspective... But no one was particularly uncomfortable, so I didn't have to say anything.

Even Bae Sejin seemed to be looking forward to it.
