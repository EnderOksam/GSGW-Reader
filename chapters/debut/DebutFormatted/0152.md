# Chapter 152 - Dog Commercial

As soon as I entered the commercial set, I saw an unwelcome face.

"How have you been? You seem to have a lot on your mind."

It was VTIC Cheongryeo.

*It's driving me crazy.*

Why is he here again?

The agency had rushed me here saying a great commercial opportunity had come up, and all I'd seen beforehand was the script.

If I am going to work with someone I know, shouldn't they at least tell me?

*I want to quit.*

I really didn't feel like working.

But if I made it obvious, it would be a rumor generator. Let's just nod.

"Yes. Thanks to your advice, it worked out well."

"Oh, really? That's great."

Cheongryeo smiled and held out his hand.

"Please take good care of me today."

"I look forward to your kind cooperation."

I didn't do anything childish like putting in strength, so the handshake was over in an instant.

"Then I'll see you in a little while."

"Yes, sunbae-nim."

I didn't want to see him.

I changed my costume and prepared for the shoot, feeling strange when I thought of the script.

The outfit was a training suit that followed the recent trend. However, it had the company logo on it.

*I already know why I'm wearing these clothes.*

First of all, the filming location was a grass field, and there was a lot of exercise during filming.

It was like this.

"Then, here we go!"

At the director's words, a group of four-legged animals rushed to me and Cheongryeo, who were standing in the grass.

It was a pile of three-month-old golden retriever puppies.

"*Woof!*"

I petted each of them as the script directed, and one even jumped up and headbutted my nose.

*They're excited.*

Three or four of them jumped in one after another. Hey, don't lick me.

I picked up the bulky lump with both hands.

...It looked cute.

"*Achoo!*"

One of them sneezed right in my face.

I nearly fell trying to adjust my grip.

With my face covered in saliva and four or five puppies in my arms, I tried not to look stiff.

"Cut! Oh, that was good~"

"...Thank you."

The staff ran to fix my messed-up face. We both had a hard time.

"Pick up the puppies with more confidence! And be sure to smile!"

Right. 'Feel secure' was important.

What I was filming was an insurance company advertisement.

To be precise, it was an advertisement for a corporate brand promoting the support of abandoned dogs and training of guide dogs at a large insurance company.

*Usually, they use people who are well-known to the public for this kind of thing.*

However, an actor who originally got hired for my position was caught in a sexual harassment scandal and they used me instead.

My new popularity with older viewers after *Bespoke Singer* might have had something to do with it.

"Good boy."

I didn't like the thought that my public acquaintance with Cheongryeo had probably influenced the casting.

Even with filming stopped, Cheongryeo deftly kept the puppies in check as some rolled onto their backs, begging for belly rubs.

*They're so photogenic.*

*I want to take a picture of it.*

But going off to fetch my smartphone now would hardly look professional.

At that moment, Cheongryeo opened his mouth.

"Do you like dogs?"

"...I don't dislike them."

Coincidentally, the staff had stepped away. We didn't have microphones because they only selected the scene and inserted the voice into the narration.

That meant it was the perfect time for this bastard to talk nonsense.

But my prediction was wrong.

"Isn't that right? They're really good kids. Good boy."

"..."

"They're also easy to deal with. Compliment them when they do good things and scold them when they do bad things."

Cheongryeo just petted the dogs while talking about them.

Information reflexively flashed through my head.

*– That hyung donated half of his settlement to the stray dog center.*

No way, was it real?

No, it didn't matter.

"I guess you really like dogs, sunbae-nim."

I was going to buy time until the shooting resumed by responding appropriately.

But he tilted his head.

For normal people, usually... that gesture meant they were puzzled.

"Do I like them? I don't know. It's easier to not raise one anyway."

Up to this point, I thought he meant it was troublesome to take care of a dog.

"I used to keep a dog every time I started over, but after a while... people began accusing me of chronically abandoning dogs."

"...!"

"I would’ve suspected myself too. I checked, and apparently, I mentioned a different breed every time I spoke. Hmm, when they proved it wasn't true, there were talks of schizophrenia... Hahaha, it was a total disaster at that time!"

Cheongryeo laughed out loud. I felt a chill run down my spine.

"...You didn't have the same puppy?"

"What? Haha, I didn't want to raise the same dog every time. Doing the same things over and over was bad enough."

"..."

Cheongryeo stopped laughing.

"I tried, but it wasn't a good idea. If something goes wrong, you might not even be able to adopt the same dog again."

I was at a loss for words.

This bastard... The fact that I could understand why he turned insane was a problem.

I answered as calmly as possible.

"...You don't have to start all over again, so I think you can try raising another one."

In other words, just give up.

"You're full of confidence. Perhaps... Is this the last mission? Is it because you calculated it as I told you?"

"..."

It was best not to react.

But this bastard interpreted my silence as an affirmation.

"Congratulations. I didn't think you'd believe me, though."

"...!"

"Haha, just kidding. I'm probably right. I wouldn't lie about something like this."

The thought of smashing the Grand Prize trophy into his face was oddly comforting.

"...Hmm, then really... Is it going to be over?"

The expression on his face disappeared in an instant, and he removed his hand from the dog's belly.

*...Wait.*

Surely, he wouldn't talk nonsense like 'Try it once before it's over', or 'there's no chance once it's over, the timing is now'.

Let's start with the bait.

"If VTIC sunbae-nims maintain the status quo, there won’t be any groups that can compete with you. Isn't the current situation the best?"

"Well... It's all right."

Cheongryeo was indifferent.

Cheongryeo picked up one of the puppies by the scruff of its neck.

"It's not like I completely hate it... Hmm, well... That's true. I have to get used to it."

"...!"

It was a euphemistic declaration of surrender.

*This shit is over.*

I felt like the span of my life was getting shorter. I wondered if cold sweat was running down my forehead.

However, Cheongryeo's words didn't end there.

"But Hubae-nim, I think it's better not to let your guard down too much."

Please, I don't want to hear any more nonsense.

I slowly petted the dog unwittingly. The dense fur was comforting.

And fortunately, Cheongryeo's next words were not nonsense.

It was the voice of cold logic.

"In this profession, 'popularity' is a limited resource. In other words, if another team gains good opportunities, yours shrink. It's always been like that. Especially if it is limited to Korea."

"..."

No way, is it a comparison of VTIC and TeSTAR?

However, an unexpected name popped up instead.

"The group from Traveler that debuted this year seems to be doing well. Pay attention to that."

Traveler, Goldie #1's agency.

In other words, he was advising me to keep Goldie #1's group, a decent rookie, in check.

*...Do I have to?*

They weren't at the level that required me to keep them in check yet, and if I did this wrong, I might just end up giving them a narrative.

Besides, they debuted during a period Cheongryeo didn’t know about. That meant Cheongryeo hadn't confirmed these guys' future.

But there was no need to aggravate this bastard, so I just nodded.

"I'll keep that in mind."

"Don't just say that. Their songs are great, but the people who write them aren't employed by the agency."

Cheongryeo laughed.

"Buying out the songwriters would be a good option."

"...!"

"It's easy. All they have to do is give that group a few bad songs and they're finished."

"I'll pretend I didn't hear it."

"You're like a knife. All right. But don't forget it."

*Why is this bastard so talkative?*

He blurted it all out as if he was receiving animal therapy with a bunch of puppies.

"Preparation for the next scene is almost done~ 3-minute standby!"

Fortunately, the sound of the filming resuming soon rang out everywhere. People were running around busily.

*This is the end.*

The next cut was the last time I would film with this bastard. I held back my sigh.

And right before filming resumed, I heard a small voice.

It sounded a little hesitant.

"...The problem you told me about, did you solve it?"

"..."

I opened my mouth slowly.

"So-so."

"Is that so? Well... If you change your mind or have any concerns, contact me."

"...All right."

Well, he was less creepy than before.

"*Woof! Woof!*"

The shooting went off without a hitch. Cheongryeo finished filming first due to schedule problems and left.

I was given the dog-hair-covered sweatsuit from the shoot and a few extra sets of the same outfit before returning to the dorm.

Perhaps everyone was away due to additional choreography practice, only one person who finished early was in the dorm.

It was Cha Eugene.

"Here," I said, holding out a sweatsuit.

"Gift?"

"Yeah. Take it."

"Wow!"

I suffered from a strange sense of déjà vu when I saw Cha Eugene happily accepting the gift.

*...Isn't he more puppy-like than I am?*

Although I actively adopted it, it was a world of mysterious nicknames.
