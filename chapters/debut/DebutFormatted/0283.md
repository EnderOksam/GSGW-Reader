# Chapter 283 - A Familiar Path

Things went as expected, but it wasn't as bad as I'd thought it would be.

I'd expected a hellish climbing session or something, since it was a place a former national athlete said he often went to.

"Isn't it nice here?"

"Yes."

I looked around as I walked.

This was a hiking trail on the outskirts of Gyeonggi-do, where we'd arrived after about an hour by car.

It didn't feel particularly well-maintained, but the inclines weren't steep and were pretty walkable.

*...Is he going easy on me?*

I remembered what this guy said the last time we were hiking on the uninhabited island.

*– You... You're already suffering from the aftereffects of that incident. You don't have as much stamina as you used to. In the past, a short hike like this wouldn't have made you so breathless.*

It was a flawed assumption in several ways, but I decided to accept the situation without protest.

*Work-life balance comes before pride.*

But there was one thing that puzzled me.

"But there really aren't many people."

There was nobody in sight except for a few older hikers.

Even in the middle of the day on a weekday, we should've seen quite a few hikers by now.

"Oh, there's construction going on nearby. See?"

Ryu Chungwoo pointed to the small stream on the left.

Nets and construction materials were scattered along the path.

"So most people seem to be taking the opposite path. It's better maintained over there, too."

"Ah."

"Well, it's just a neighborhood hill in the first place, so not many people come. There are no mineral springs or big rocks."

"You seem pretty familiar with this place."

"This is where I lived when I was young."

Ryu Chungwoo smiled awkwardly.

"The archery range I used to practice at is nearby, so I've kept coming here even after moving away."

"......"

The guy couldn't even shoot properly due to the aftereffects of the accident, yet he'd chosen a place near his old archery range...

*Oh, forget it. It's not like he needs my pity right now.*

I just nodded.

"The facilities must have been nice."

"More than that, it was nostalgia that brought me back."

Ryu Chungwoo was silent for a long moment before speaking again.

"I think we should bring the other guys next time. The owner is kind."

I'd have to throw my social skills out the window to refuse him outright.

"Why not?"

"Good."

Ryu Chungwoo smiled.

"Since the tour's over, we don't get many chances to hang out anywhere. This will be fun. When you guys went out a few days ago, I had something on my schedule."

*Ah. That was it.*

I remembered that some of the members had gone out for Seon Ahyeon's birthday.

At that time, Ryu Chungwoo and a few others had separate schedules, so they couldn't go.

"We didn't do much. There were too many people."

"Haha, but I saw your proof shot!"

My eyebrows twitched as I recalled the Polaroid Keun Sejin had taken after the escape room.

I'd be lying if I said I had no idea it would end up on the Internet so quickly, but it was okay because the fans liked it.

"After taking the photo, we returned to the dorm right away. Too many people came."

"I see. This job is rewarding, that's for sure, but I think that's the hard part. You can't go out whenever you want."

"Yes."

I jogged slowly along the path and shrugged.

"But even if you hadn't become an idol, I think it would've been the same for you, hyung. You were a national athlete."

"What? Haha!"

It took a while for Ryu Chungwoo's laughter to subside.

"That's right. That kind of thing was never my cup of tea, though. I guess it can't be helped if you're a celebrity of any kind."

Ryu Chungwoo sounded matter-of-fact, not like he was lamenting or grumbling. Well, that sort of difficulty was proof of success, I supposed.

*It's a worry only winners can afford to talk about.*

Ryu Chungwoo and I continued running along the secluded hiking trail we'd sought out because of the side effects of success.

And it was only after a while that I realized something was wrong.

"Phew."

The slope got steeper as we went up. I took a few deep breaths and realized that my stamina bar, which I'd worked hard to build up, was already at least half depleted.

*I should be at least average for an adult male by now.*

Besides, looking around, it seemed that not many people came this far up the trail.

...Moreover, we were the only ones running up.

And then I realized it.

*I was tricked.*

The path had started out flat, then gradually grown steeper, coaxing us into continuing the climb like a frog slowly boiling...

The flat stretch of the hiking trail had been bait, not consideration. I held back a groan.

Ryu Chungwoo was not an idiot, and he saw me working out like that, so of course he would know that my stamina had increased.

*Well, you got me...*

It seemed like it would take quite a while to reach the top.

However, complaining would only hurt my pride, and I wouldn't be able to turn back without reaching the top, so I decided to just shut up and keep climbing.

It wasn't as if I couldn't manage it.

*At least I'm getting some exercise in.*

I silently looked up at the sky as we kept jogging.

How much time had passed?

I felt a hand tap on my shoulder.

"Moondae, over there."

"Hoo, what..."

We were still a long way from the peak.

I raised my head and looked where he was pointing.

"......"

A beautiful view of the midday city spread out before me.

There were winding mountains, fortress walls with streams below them, and densely packed building complexes.

It was a colorful spring city.

*Swoosh.*

A breeze blew past, and I breathed in the mountain air.

It was a lovely view.

"Isn't it nice?"

Ryu Chungwoo spoke calmly.

"There's no need to go any farther. The view is best right here... After quitting archery, I climbed every day while preparing for this job, and that's when I discovered this spot."

"......"

I leaned against the railing, looking at the scenery.

*So he never meant to go all the way to the top?*

*Seriously.*

"How is it? Do you feel motivated? Coming up here is more than just exercise. It's always been that way for me."

"Yes."

I gave a definite answer. A set route, predictable difficulties, and a guaranteed reward.

It wasn't my taste, but hiking might not be such a bad hobby.

"Well, I'm glad to hear that."

I leaned against the railing with him, looking down the mountain in no hurry to leave.

Only after quite a while had passed did Ryu Chungwoo speak.

"Shall we go down?"

"Okay."

I felt very refreshed.

I was about to stretch when I recalled what I'd been thinking about from the moment I saw the hiking trail.

"Hyung."

"Huh?"

"This is good and all, but I probably need anaerobic exercise, specifically weight training."

"Ah."

I hadn't said anything right away when I saw the hiking trail because I figured complaining then would turn the atmosphere into shit.

Ryu Chungwoo replied with a clumsy smile.

"Because the whole trail was uphill... You probably used some muscles."

*What a professional-sounding excuse.* Now I understood.

I didn't feel particularly grumpy on the way down, and agreed when Ryu Chungwoo suggested we come for a run sometimes.

*The intensity of the exercise isn't bad, and we're roommates now. It wouldn't hurt to come here every so often.*

***

Two weeks after we'd changed rooms, all the members had more or less adapted to their new arrangements in their own way.

"Do you want some?"

"Oh, yes."

For example, surprisingly, Bae Sejin spent less time in the living room. He only came out during the day to get away from Cha Eugene.

"It's a bit noisy at night, but with earplugs, it's no problem!"

Bae Sejin had cheerfully told me that once. Unexpectedly, Cha Eugene respected his roommate's independent ways.

*Is it because he's an American?*

Of course, that also had its limits.

"If I play games with him every now and then or watch something together, he'll leave me alone when I go to bed—"

I calmly told the truth.

"That won't last long."

"What?"

"If you don't listen to him, he'll start shaking you by the shoulder."

"......"

Bae Sejin stared in horror.

"Congratulations on becoming a night owl."

Bae Sejin stared at his room in a daze.

As for the guy who'd ended up alone in a single room... He went from room to room and had a great time.

"Hey, you're practically best friends with Chungwoo-hyung now, Moondae~ Are you two going to exercise together again tomorrow?"

I guessed he'd chosen our room today. I answered wryly to Keun Sejin, who was lying on Ryu Chungwoo's bed playing with his smartphone.

"Well... We're roommates, so we went together. You can come too, if you want."

"Ah~ I've got more tact than that! Have a good workout~"

"......"

Keun Sejin didn't bother adding anything else, but it wasn't hard to figure out what he meant.

*He's suggesting I use this opportunity to turn things around.*

Some people were still dwelling on how shitty my attitude toward Ryu Chungwoo had been when we filmed our summer package.

Those same people were trying to stir up trouble over the roommate assignment, but there was no need to leave any sparks smoldering when we'd finally managed to unite the fandom.

*We need to look very comfortable around each other.*

I'd already decided to be friendly with him, but those people online were giving me even more motivation.

I waved to Keun Sejin and went to shower.

And the next day, I was back on the hiking trail.

"Why don't we climb to the top today? Just as a test."

Ryu Chungwoo made a new suggestion. He'd been quiet as we climbed, so apparently this was what he'd been thinking about.

*Well, it can't hurt to climb all the way up just once.*

We were slowly running out of days when we could take our time coming all the way out here to exercise.

We'd have to really start focusing on preparing for our comeback soon.

After gauging my remaining stamina, I nodded.

"Alright."

"Good."

It was 4 PM, neither early nor late, as we started walking toward the top.

The slope grew steeper, but we didn't run, which made it manageable.

We'd been walking for about forty minutes when...

The summit was visible.

"Hoo."

There wasn't much up there, and there was no one else around. Ryu Chungwoo and I picked up the pace a little more and reached the sign at the summit without stopping for a break.

"Whoo. I haven't been here in a while."

Ryu Chungwoo clutched the wooden post holding the sign, and I stood beside him, looking down.

And I could see that what Ryu Chungwoo had said on our first day here was right.

"The other spot really does have a better view."

The view from the top wasn't as impressive as the one halfway up the mountain, though the height did make it feel somewhat overwhelming.

"Yes."

Ryu Chungwoo nodded, looking at the scenery. Then he took a bottle of water from his backpack and drank.

I took out my own bottle of water and drank, and there was a short, peaceful silence.

*Hmm. It definitely feels more comfortable than before.*

"......"

"Moondae."

"Yes?"

"Is it okay if I ask you something?"

"Sure. What is it?"

I answered without much hesitation.

Ryu Chungwoo kept his gaze fixed on the scenery and slowly opened his mouth.

"Actually... You know that person you asked about after watching the family video during Chuseok last year? I asked about him a few more times within the clan."

"...!"

"Ryu Gunwoo-ssi, was it?"

I almost choked while drinking the water.

I quickly lowered the bottle and tried to figure out what he was getting at.

*Has he found him?*

"Do you know where he—"

"Um, no. Sorry. I don't know where he is. I did learn a little about his past, though."

"......"

"His entire family, except for him, was involved in an accident on a trip involving his father's family. Apparently, there was a car accident and a fire."

Ryu Chungwoo sighed, then continued talking so calmly it sounded artificial.

"And that car accident... is probably the same one whose aftereffects left me unable to shoot properly."

*Fuck.*

This wasn't a pleasant topic, so I gulped down some more water.

*If those people in the clan don't know where he is, they should just say they don't know and leave it at that.*

Why did they have to tell him that...? I was angry.

However, Ryu Chungwoo wasn't done.

"And Moondae, you know what?"

*What?*

"...Your parents didn't die in a car accident. They died in a plane crash."

"......"

*Wait, what?*

"Actually, an article was going to be published last year, but the company stopped it. They only discussed it briefly with me because it could be a shock to you. You hadn't been out of the hospital for long."

I looked up.

"But you... You have trauma related to car accidents and fires. Not planes."

Ryu Chungwoo had taken his eyes off the scenery and was looking at me now.

"The one who should have those traumas is Ryu Gunwoo, the person you've been looking for."

"......"

"Moondae. Who exactly are you looking for?"
