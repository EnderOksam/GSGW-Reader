# Chapter 19 - EP 1 Broadcast, II

To be honest, I was quite nervous when I opened my smartphone to check the reaction to the first episode.

*I thought I might become too much of a laughingstock—*

However, surprisingly, viewers reacted more favorably to me than mockingly.

By the time the chorus of ⟨POP☆CON⟩ came out, public opinion had completely turned around.

In my favor.

★$

– He’s cute ㅠㅠ I’m mesmerized.

– Just be honest, you came to the audition to do this ㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋ

– Nothing wrong with working hard. It’s good to see that attitude.

– No, I mean, he looks like a different person from the interview ㅋㅋㅋ

– So cute.

*This is cute?*

It was true that I prepared the song selection strategically.

However, I thought people would just consider me passable because I’d worked hard, so their enthusiasm was an unexpected gain.

Don’t audition program viewers usually sneer at people who aren’t skilled enough?

I was a little confused, but soon I understood the situation.

*It’s because I sang well just before.*

Since I’d already met their basic expectation that a singer should sing well, they could generously overlook my dancing as long as it wasn’t embarrassingly bad.

Making them laugh seemed to have endeared me to them even more.

Being seen as a bit of a clown was—a bit embarrassing, though.

*I chose a general answer on purpose, but it came out like this.*

Basically, I hadn’t wanted to give them anything to find fault with. In addition to that, in order to emphasize the main vocalist position, I had kept all my responses neutral and subdued[1]{T/N: 꼬투리를 잡히다 means giving someone a pretext to find fault with you.}.

In the interview, I didn’t mention any personal opinions and only said objective words that wouldn’t go against the social consensus.

*But—I had no idea they would portray that as a lack of enthusiasm.*

Anyway, even if I take a roundabout route, it doesn’t matter as long as I reach Seoul. I’d planned to take the KTX, but I don’t mind taking the express bus[2]{T/N: 모로 가도 서울만 가면 된다 means that reaching the goal matters more than the route taken.}.

‘I got through the first step well.’

It was clear that I had secured early recognition. This is not just a hunch but a real result.

This is because the status window popped up while I watched the broadcast.

*This feels a little unreal.*

I decided to look at the rewards that appeared in the pop-up later and instead opened the search box and entered keywords.

The results came right away.

★$
[Average ratings: 3.7%, Highest ratings in one moment: 6.8%]

Oh my God.

*...Isn’t this the first episode of a cable variety show that was supposed to fail?*

I guess the show succeeded in drawing attention[3]{T/N: In this context, aggro refers to attracting attention or provoking a reaction.}. Of course, objectively speaking, it wasn’t a crazy high rating.

Regardless of whether people liked me or not, the fact that 50,000 of them had taken notice of Park Moondae meant that I’d made quite an impression.

Now, let’s take a closer look at the ‘likes and dislikes’ section.

After checking the overall flow of public opinion in the first episode, I immediately checked my evaluation video clip.

★$
[An amateur participant’s shocking turnaround! Park Moondae]
$★

I cringed so hard I thought my neck muscles would cramp up.

‘...Let’s get it out of my head quickly.’

Then I checked the best comments first.

★$
– Huh? POP☆CON is here? ㅋㅋ (👍7326 / 👎181 )

– This woke me up. Moondae-ya, Noona will keep buying your stock until you become famous. (👍4522 / 👎294 )

– Yep, the main vocalist this time is Park Moondae (👍2061 / 👎372 )
$★

“Hmm.”

First of all—it was a good sign that such embarrassingly enthusiastic comments were at the top. It was a bit strange—but thank you.

However, the critical factor was a fairly high rate of ‘Dislike’.

Compared with other contestants who had been favorably edited—for example, Cha Eugene—I seemed to have roughly 1.5 times as many downvotes.

It meant that there were quite a few people who didn’t like the ‘Park Moondae’ who appeared on the broadcast. So maybe my lack of an outgoing personality was the problem.

So, what is the most effective appeal I can make at this point?

I couldn’t do much about the negative image that had already formed, but there were many idols who turned criticism into a gimmick to make people laugh.

Just thinking about it right now—hmm, there was probably at least one in every popular idol group.

So there’s only one thing I have to do in the next shoot.

*I have to look friendly.*

I immediately came up with the next search word.

★$
[Relisted! Idol Incorporated PR videos]
$★

The next shoot would be for our PR videos.

I still had three days left, so I’d look for the best examples from previous seasons before then.

***

The PR video shooting for ⟨Idol Incorporated⟩ was done live.

However, they recorded the preparations for the PR videos like a variety show. The footage would be used as filler during the ranking announcement.

“Everyone! Are you ready?”

“Yes!”

The participants answered the MC’s standard scripted lines with great enthusiasm.

*I think he says the same thing every single time we film something. Or am I mistaken?*

Anyway, the important thing was that the participants became more desperate.

“I’m glad that everyone’s doing well! Okay, do you know what we are preparing for today?”

“PR video!”

“Self-introductions!”

“Correct!”

Because I’m sure everyone has seen the reaction to the first episode.

Everyone must have realized. No matter what they do in front of the camera, it’s like nothing happens if it doesn’t air.

*So no matter what they do, they can’t help but be desperate for a PR video that’s being broadcast live.*

In fact, it was never a fair competition.

With the first episode already having created gaps in the participants’ popularity, the live broadcast competition’s outcome was already halfway decided.

However, every season there was someone who got their lucky break through a PR video, so everyone seemed to be eagerly preparing without complaint.

It was a live broadcast of 77 amateurs, so of course, all kinds of controversies followed, but—it seemed like no one cared about that.

The participants will think it’s fine as long as it’s not them, and the production team will like the rising buzz.

“You’ll each be doing your own live broadcast on TNET’s global WeTube channel at 8pm tonight!”

“I’m really nervous.”

Keun Sejin, who had ended up standing nearby again, whispered.

He didn’t look nervous at all—what a liar.

“Ah, wait a minute. I think I said something wrong!”

“?!?”

“The live broadcast is not just for you!”

As if the MC was enjoying the reaction, he encouraged the response with his arms. The panicked participants shouted.

“What do you think is your dream as a celebrity?”

“Dream—?”

“It’s an advertisement, everyone!”

“....!”

The MC spread his arms wide.

“You should each choose a product to advertise and promote both yourself and that product!”

“Yes?!”

These bastards are determined to get something for nothing[5]{T/N: 날로 먹다 means getting something easily without working for it.}. These so-called collaboration items sounded plausible, but I’d bet my left hand that they were products distributed by T1, TNET’s parent company.

If even one or two products went viral, they’d profit, and if none did, they had nothing to lose.

It was capitalistic enough to be counted among the crazy tactics of ⟨Idol Incorporated⟩.

*Did someone higher up want a slice of the pie?*

The series they’d written off as a failure was suddenly getting a response, so they wanted to put their spoon in[6]{T/N: 숟가락을 얹다 means taking a share of the benefits or credit from someone else’s work.}. Anyway, the MC’s words were plausible.

“This item may feel like a burden to promote, but please remember that it can also be used as a prop to show off your charm!”

“Ah~”

The participants seemed to be convinced. It was a wise attitude because it wasn’t as if they could refuse anyway.

“Then how will you choose these products?”

“Running?”

“Rock, paper, scissors?”

“Karaoke?”

Methods from previous seasons and similar programs were suggested here and there. The MC shook his head with a falsely sad expression and shouted the correct answer.

“It’s... a treasure hunt!”

“Heol?”

“The production crew hid treasure balls containing product names throughout the dorm. You have the next 15 minutes! All you have to do is find a treasure ball!”

Keun Sejin nodded, and his face showed a little bit of admiration.

“I think this will be fun?”

“...Yeah.”

It would be interesting for the viewers, all right. For the participants, it was the perfect setup for a fight.

With 77 people going through one building, someone was bound to get upset.

Of course, this extra footage was meant to lighten the mood during the ranking announcement. It didn’t seem like things would get overly serious.

However, there were plenty of ways to edit the footage to hint otherwise.

*I can already see all kinds of speculation and rumors coming up on the internet.*

“Ah! Since this is an audition program, the treasure balls also have grades. Please keep that in mind and check the balls carefully!”

It must have been painted in gold, silver, and bronze colors.

“All right, get ready—”

Everyone is ready to run.

**“Start!”**

“Whoa!!!”

With screams, 77 people rushed into the dormitory. I sneaked out and took a step back.

*Let’s go through the back door.*

No matter how much I thought about it, it was likely that I wouldn’t be able to get any of the treasure if I went with them through the main door.

If I went around to a back entrance, I would have less competition with the other participants.

Then Keun Sejin and Gold #1 followed me.

They were grinning smugly, without a hint of shame.

“Thank you, Moondae-ya. It’s nice to be carried.”

“Hyung-nim is smart.”

“....”

It doesn’t matter if there are two of them— but I’m pissed off about how it’s going.

Gold #2 and Seon Ahyeon, who were standing a little further apart, seemed to have been swept away by the crowd and entered the front door.

As expected, life is all about timing. What a pity.

“Oh, Moondae~ You found it right away.”

As soon as I entered through the back door, I immediately found a ball on the threshold.

It was silver.

*As expected.*

Keun Sejin also nodded, looking at the opaque silver ball.

“Looks like they split the balls into three grades, right?”

“Hey, let’s be accurate! Bronze, silver, and gold! Doesn’t that sound better!”

“Kk~ you’re a stickler for details.”

If they kept talking, I’d just leave them behind.

“...Why don’t we speed up the search? The others will be here soon.”

“Yes~”

“Since you’ve got your wits about you, Moondae, your hyung feels reassured.”

I moved my feet quickly. Surprisingly, the two followed without any more chatter.

First, the storage room.

“It’s easy if we think like the production crew. Where would they hide a ball—hmm, here?”

Keun Sejin smiled and took out a bronze-colored ball from a chalk case in front of the camera.

“....!”

“Ah~ bronze. Is this real?”

“Ah, damn it. I never have any luck with this[7]{T/N: 똥손 refers to someone who is clumsy or unlucky at things like picking prizes.}.”

Surprisingly—they were useful.

These guys were quick. Thanks to them, we divided the storage room into zones without saying much and searched it in an instant.

“Ah, I found another silver! Shall we move now?”

“I agree.”

“I agree.”

They were also quick to judge the situation. We moved quickly to the next area without waiting.

“Where?”

“Let’s go to the cafeteria first. There are cameras there.”

“Moondae has good eyes.”

“As expected, he’s the man who found the back door.”

“....”

It—would have been better if they hadn’t said anything.

We wasted no time in the cafeteria, either.

All three of us silently started searching around the areas where the cameras were located.

I opened a spoon holder.

“I found it.”

“Heol!”

“It’s gold.”

“We’re the ones in gold grade, so why was Moondae the first to find a gold ball?”

“It can’t be helped, hyung-nim. I think it’s because Moondae has a golden hand[8]{T/N: 금손, literally ‘golden hand,’ can describe someone skilled or lucky at things like picking prizes.}.”

“The other kids are coming.”

“Ah.”

They finally know how to shut up. All I have to do is give them something to do.

Anyway, when I heard rushing footsteps, we left the cafeteria quickly.

“Go upstairs?”

“Yes.”

“Hey~ It’s good that the three of us came!”

“That’s right! Honestly, you agree with this, right, Moondae?”

“Yeah.”

“Oh~”

I’ll be honest.

I’ve benefited from these two guys. It was efficient when three people searched together.

Overall, they had good eyesight and movement, so it was easy to search and move quickly. Their judgement was also good.

They weren’t stubborn, either, so we split the balls without any trouble.

“Then we— did we find 4 gold, 2 silver, and 4 bronze?”

Keun Sejin sorted the palm-sized plastic balls.

Gold #1 said carefully.

“Yeah. Well—let’s each take one of each color, and since Moondae brought us along, let’s give him the extra gold. What do you think?”

“I like it~”

It’s good that it ended neatly, but I shouldn’t leave any room for evil editing.

After experiencing the power of editing firsthand, I couldn’t help feeling anxious.

“Oh, then I won’t take the bronze one. Take two each.”

“Oh~ Thank you!”

“Okay!”

As we each took our own balls, an announcement came out at the dormitory.

**– There are 200 seconds left~**

For reference, we’d returned to a storage room next to the back door to avoid the crowds of participants.

It would be awful to have our balls stolen here.

We quickly exited the back door and headed for the meeting point.

“Ah~ farming nice~.”

“After all, our teamwork is the best. We freakin’ rock.”

These brats have all relaxed, and they’re all talking again.

Participants who had already found one or two balls opened them on their own at the meeting point.

So far, no one has tried to steal it by force.

*They’ll notice that there are cameras all over the place.*

They seemed to be psychologically hesitant to steal the opponent’s ball openly.

“Wow, shall we open ours too?”

“Okay~”

“Yeah.”

We started to open up our balls when we returned to our spots.

Keun Sejin and Gold #1 had a lot of balls in their hands, so there were a lot of jealous eyes.

Still, two higher-grade balls would be better. Having a bunch of mediocre options would only make it harder to choose one.
