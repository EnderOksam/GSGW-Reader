# Chapter 255 - The Algorithm Swamp

After accepting the crazy deal that would make the status window disappear if I wasn't able to win a grand prize within 500 days...

The rest of the holiday vacation went by peacefully.

Actually, I'd wanted to go back to the dorm a few days early to analyze the global and domestic trends in pop music in depth for the next album...

When I came to my senses, I was making more holiday food.

"...?"

"Oh my, Moondae makes songpyeon[1]{T/N: Songpyeon/송편: traditional Korean food made of rice powder. It is a type of rice cake in which a variety of fillings are used.} so beautifully~ Our Sejin's look like dumplings!"

"Mom!"

"...Thank you."

It seemed I'd worn myself out thinking about the 'Reveal the Truth' feature and the mission. All I wanted to do was something that didn't require much thought.

So I spent my time watching ⟨TeSTAR's Island Life⟩—or rather, ⟨TeSTAR's Stranded Life⟩—and monitoring the reactions online.

"They edited it so spectacularly."

"...I know."

Watching us dress in fancy hiking gear, climb the hill, and receive supplies by helicopter somehow reminded me of a passionate sports match or an expedition.

And we got to feast, as though it were a reward for surviving.

+=
**[Yummy yum yum!]**

**[Enjoy your food, TeSTAR. You deserve it! ☆]**
=+

It was strangely satisfying watching us feast, instead of simply watching a healing show. Was it because of the contrast with all the hardships we'd faced getting those supplies?

*Having watched it myself, I figured people must've enjoyed the show right to the end.*

And the reaction on the internet that I sneakily checked was really like that.

The fact that it had escalated into arguments like this meant it had become really popular.

*The haters can't control the public opinion that was saying the show was funny, so they're pushing the opinion that it's not because of TeSTAR.*

It was a good thing.

However, what was unexpected was that this ripple effect did not end in Korea.

*Even the hotteok show hadn't been this popular.*

It wasn't like the other variety show, where the producer's vision of targeting the United States had been shoehorned in. I hadn't expected a simple domestic pilot program like this to become so popular.

★$
**[TeSTAR on a deserted island leading to DISASTER? 😱]**
$★

The compilation of clips uploaded by the broadcasting station racked up views incredibly quickly, and within a few days, its title and captions had been translated into English.

Distress and luxury vacation.

Maybe getting stranded on a luxurious vacation had a basic appeal that transcended language. Overseas fans seemed to be actively recommending the compilation to WeTube reaction channels.

★$
**[Americans floored by TeSTAR's new variety show about being marooned on an island!]**

$★

★$

-#: ------- views#-
$★

Naturally, channels devoted to Korea-related content and translation picked up on it and made new videos, creating a virtuous cycle.

...It was getting a little cringeworthy watching it go this viral.

+=
**[WeTuber: Oh my fucking god, are they trapped in a hurricane right now?? On an uninhabited island??]**

**[WeTuber: No, I thought it would be sweet and cute, since it was supposed to be a K-pop idol variety show...]**

**[WeTuber: Oh my God! Look at how surprised they are, haha]**

**[(They're falling for TeSTAR as they watch. Welcome to the club.)]**

Adding subtitles like this would be one of the selling points, but I couldn't help feeling embarrassed myself.

"Hmm."

"...What are you looking at?"

"Ah, the overseas reaction to our variety show."

"That...! Did you see it too?"

Bae Sejin's eyes flashed. Having spent so much time monitoring his own appearances since the days he had two left feet, he seemed well-versed in this sort of thing.

"Oh, there's this other guy who puts the clips together really well... Wait a moment."

Bae Sejin turned on WeTube on TV and opened one of the channels he was subscribed to. It seemed that he was enjoying his WeTube life in earnest more than I thought.

And the video played by Bae Sejin was quite good.

+=
@cc@[Hello, this is K-pop Guru.]@cc@
=+

In fact, there was no distortion or exaggeration, and the channel also gave concise background on the WeTubers it was translating. There were no forced, overenthusiastic subtitles trying to hype things up.

"This guy's good."

"...Ahem! Yes."

I wondered how he'd managed to find something this good among all those similarly cringeworthy titles and channel names. Those keywords were flooded with videos like that.

I watched the video on that channel for a long time after that. It was good to watch without thinking.

However, in the process, a strange pattern was discovered.

*There is a flow.*

For example, if there was a foreigner who was new to TeSTAR through this variety show, they'd usually ask for music recommendations in the comments, since TeSTAR's main job was singing.

+=
**[If there are any of their videos I should watch, please recommend them in the comments. Don't forget to subscribe too!]**
=+

Then K-pop fans would rush over and flood the comments with recommendations, mostly for 'Spring Out'.

-Their true talent is in their performances, not variety shows???????????? Please watch this music video first! (#b_link_b#)

-'Spring Out' is a must-watch. I can't wait for his reaction. 🥳

It was lavishly funded Joseon steampunk. The blockbuster-movie feel seemed to work well, since it appealed to most viewers.

*We were aiming for that in the first place.*

It was rewarding to see overseas viewers responding to precisely the things we'd meant to appeal to them.

And if the response to this recommendation was good, the next song the fans recommended was 'Present'.

★$
-The two songs are connected in a kind of series! I bet it will definitely be to your taste ☺
$★

They were also careful to recommend the teaser with every member's solo song before recommending the main video.

*And if he asks them to recommend something similar again, they'll tell him the ⟨127 Section⟩ game collaboration trailer video...*

Next came 'Better Me', from the same universe, before they eventually watched 'Magical Boys'.

It was a magical rabbit hole—by the end, everyone was calling themselves a 'Loviewer'.

+=
**[Hello subscribers! As you know... I'm completely into TeSTAR, the big K-pop stars these days! I'm just, I'm just a Loviewer!]**

**[Today, we're going to react to their newest song, 'Nightmare'. Ready? Here we go. Three, two, one, play!]**

K-pop reactions were a lucrative business that got a lot of views, so many WeTubers might exaggerate their reactions, but it was a great thing anyway.

*Most of the famous groups went through this route as well.*

For example, VTIC also showed such a route. It usually started with ⟨Sangun⟩.

Anyway, more than I expected, TeSTAR was exerting a significant influence on the global WeTube trend.

And that wasn't the end.

★$
**[TeSTAR overcomes and triumphs]**
$★

There were promotional videos about TeSTAR with English titles along these lines, racking up a considerable number of views.

"......"

I checked out the one with the most views.

+=
**[Ryu Chungwoo, one of the oldest members and the leader, was originally a South Korean national archer and a gold medalist in the Olympics' men's team archery event.]**

**[However, due to severe injuries from a car accident when he was younger, he was forced to retire from the national team in the year he became an adult.]**
=+

This video summarized TeSTAR's backstories, how we debuted through a brutal audition program, and how we achieved great results thereafter.

It was also a video talking about how we recovered from a recent horrific car accident and held a donation concert.

In short, it meant that it was a dramatic success story.

*It's an American taste...*

Our story was full of hard-earned successes and intense twists and turns.

+=

**[He played a traditional Korean child shaman and won an award at a film festival with his tremendous acting skills...]**

"Huh..."

Beside me, Bae Sejin saw footage of his younger self performing a shaman ceremony and covered his face.

"Aren't you proud?"

"Are you kidding me?? My acting was terrible back then."

It seemed that his standards had gone up as he'd grown, and now he regarded his child-acting days as a dark chapter of his past.

In any case, even though it was somewhat exaggerated, it was clear that it was a well-made video.

In addition, they'd edited in news footage of the car accident to recreate what the fandom had gone through at the time.

"Hmm."

"...Everyone was worried."

+=
**[And finally, the moment their most severely injured member was well enough to leave the hospital, they came up with an amazing plan.]**

**[It was an online donation concert for children hospitalized for long periods.]**
=+

And the donation concert... Well, actually, from these people's point of view, since TeSTAR were foreigners, they would have a mental distance, so it was probably a good thing that the video focused on our 'good influence'.

For entertainers from other countries, even if something embarrassing happened, wasn't the rationalization of 'cultural differences' a barrier?

*Still, I should find out what people really think of us.*

I checked the comments on my phone, just in case there was anything cruel that Bae Sejin shouldn't see.

★$
–-#2 mins ago#- A group of people who want to execute the ex-manager of this video [👍 👎]

–-#1 hour ago#- He must rot in prison for the rest of his life. [👍 👎]

–-#30 mins ago#- Oh my God, in the middle of the night, on the road, stopped the car and made threats? He's an evil seed that shouldn't exist in the world [👍 👎]
$★

"......"

The comments didn't have much to say about the concert itself. Instead, they wanted to burn the former manager at the stake. Even though they were in another country's language, I could feel the ferocity.

*As expected, anger is a universal sentiment.*

It wasn't a bad thing for us, anyway. People were criticizing a violent criminal for his own misdeeds.

He didn't have a particularly heartbreaking story, and he had nothing to sympathize with.

*Of course, he'd probably have caught some flak even without this incident.*

He had a bleak future ahead of him.

I heard that the former manager, who faced the wrath of public opinion, was charged with gross negligence and attempted murder.

As expected, someone who'd attracted that much public ire and antagonized a large company, without anyone to back him up, had fared very poorly at his criminal trial.

"Come to think of it, our former manager got 17 years, right?"

He'd been sentenced to the full extent of the law. He'd appealed, but the result of the second trial would probably be the same.

Bae Sejin nodded glumly.

"Yes. Actually, that kind of guy should live and die there for the rest of his life, not 17 years."

"......"

No... Even if he got out of prison, he wouldn't be able to live properly.

Thanks to the fact that while working as a manager, all sorts of fans took pictures of him, his face would come up just from searching the Internet.

Even if he'd only been sentenced to three or four years in prison, as was common in such cases, he'd have had to give up any hope of landing a respectable job in Korea.

Besides, now that I saw it, it seemed difficult to live abroad.

The promotional video about TeSTAR, in which the former manager's face was openly exposed, had over 8 million views.

*The more popular TeSTAR gets, the harder life will be for him after he's released from prison.*

And we were definitely going to get more popular.

So that was that.

I shrugged.

"I don't think we'll ever see him in the future, so we don't need to worry about him."

"...Okay. It's a holiday."

Bae Sejin nodded firmly.

Beside him, Bae Sejin's mother smiled proudly at us.

Yes, it was a nice sight.

*I should rest a lot before I have to work again.*

As soon as the vacation ended, I planned to properly prepare for this album.

I thought for a moment.

What would the fans want, now that they were being swept into TeSTAR's Algorithm Swamp by all these videos?

*A good song.*

Okay. The deep concept was good, and the easy listening and musicality were also good, but we needed an approach that went back to the basics.

Tight and cool choreography, an intense music video with stunning cinematography, and an exciting and expensive sound.

Just fun things to watch and listen to without thinking about anything else.

There was no better remedy than that to solidify the global momentum spreading like a virus.

*In a word, money should be poured like water.*

That was just about the only thing the higher-ups at this company were good at, so there would be no problem.

However, I was no longer willing to humor the people who kept trying to take credit for our work and interfere with the big picture.

*It's a waste of time.*

I had to keep my promise to Bae Sejin, who handed over the data collected for the lawsuit.

*– Actually, I've been thinking we might achieve the same result without filing a lawsuit.*

The company had tried to slip away by making the former manager its scapegoat, only to get caught and nearly get fucked.

*I need to see him.*

I spent the rest of the holiday planning a pleasant meeting with the general manager.

And on the first day after the holiday, I arranged a meeting with him.
