# Chapter 251 - A Holiday Visit

It was a good thing that the Chuseok holiday was guaranteed after the variety show that left us stranded on an island was over. I already had plans for how to use that time...

*...I was going to press 'Reveal the Truth' then.*

Even if what I learned left me in a bad way, I'd have the holidays to recover, and it was very good that they'd all go to their parents' homes because it was a holiday.

*I'll be able to sort through my feelings by myself.*

But now, all of a sudden, what kind of suggestion was this?

*Inviting a co-worker to spend the holidays at one of the members' houses?*

Was he trying to make things awkward between me and one of the members?

In particular, Ryu Chungwoo, like me when I was young, still had a chance of going down to his parents' house every holiday...

"......"

*Forget it.*

That's enough... Let's not speculate unnecessarily. It was meaningless.

*Tabak.*

I was slowly walking along the mountain path, and I just asked him openly.

"Wouldn't it be too much for me to come during the holidays?"

"It's okay."

Ryu Chungwoo answered easily.

"Nothing different from usual. I've already asked that we not meet up with any of our relatives this time. At most, we'll make some holiday food."

"......"

Ryu Chungwoo smiled bitterly, evidently drawing his own conclusions from my silence.

"Actually... It's a little uncomfortable to see my relatives."

"...!"

"I was recruited into ⟨Idol Incorporated⟩ that way."

Ryu Chungwoo spoke calmly.

"There was even an article about my family ties... Since then, my parents have refused to spend the holidays with our relatives."

"......"

Okay. The ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ writer had called him in, using her family connection when they were desperate for recruits.

Ryu Chungwoo's parents also seemed to refrain from interacting with relatives, fearing that being seen with them would attract reporters.

I chose my words.

"That's wise."

"Haha, is that so? Anyway, so it's just my parents at home and me. My sister is going on a trip with her friends. Oh, and our dog is there too."

"......"

"Think about it. You probably won't get to stay alone in the dorm this time."

"What?"

The bitter smile disappeared from Ryu Chungwoo's face, and he showed a bit of mischief.

"You didn't know? The members and the company said that you shouldn't be left alone because strange things happen every time you go on vacation."

"......"

That... I couldn't exactly blame them.

I was at a loss for words as I recalled what had happened during my previous vacations.

*Because they'd found out about all of it, too.*

If I were in their shoes, I would also monitor a team member who got sick or ended up embroiled in some ridiculous conflict every time they were on vacation.

I'd have to try to find a way to get out of this, but if it wasn't possible...

*Then... Maybe Ryu Chungwoo's idea isn't so bad?*

I wasn't that close to this guy, so if I got my own room, I'd be able to keep some distance and spend the time comfortably.

It wouldn't be a very pleasant time to see Ryu Chungwoo's parents, but it would be bearable.

*And...*

I inhaled.

There might be pictures of my parents.

So, when they went on a big family trip or something... Wouldn't there be someone who took a picture or video?

*If only I could look at a group photo.*

How could I put this... Even if 'Ryu Gunwoo' didn't exist here, I could ask a few questions and confirm whether my parents existed here...

*That's nonsense.*

I brushed off my thoughts.

Here... Even if there were records that my parents were there, seeing them now wouldn't help at all. It would only shake me up.

*Don't fall for the bullshit in my head.*

I cut off the urge and gritted my teeth. My teeth ground audibly together.

The problem was, the guy next to me heard it too.

"Is the idea of coming to my house that uncomfortable?"

"...!!"

"No... Things like that happen, too. It can be uncomfortable to spend time with adults you don't know."

That's not what he really meant...

I understood what he meant the first time.

*He was asking if I found him uncomfortable.*

Ryu Chungwoo looked a bit bitter, but soon relaxed his expression and spoke lightly.

"Well, think about it."

"......"

It might be right to move on here.

But I opened my mouth.

"It's not that hyung makes me uncomfortable. It's not that I don't want to come, either."

"...Hmm."

Ryu Chungwoo seemed to stare in my direction as he walked down, then answered easily.

"Okay."

"No, I'm not lying... It's true."

"I know. Thank you."

I guess he didn't understand anything.

I kicked a stone with my foot.

"So I hope you won't feel some strange sense of obligation toward me."

"...!"

Wasn't it a hard decision to invite a co-worker over for the holidays?

Maybe if it were the usual Ryu Chungwoo, he would have recommended that I go to the house of someone the same age as me that I was close with.

*Instead of rambling on about his house like this.*

But the reason why he did this... It meant that this guy felt obligated to be nice to Park Moondae.

And there was one reason for that.

"I was not injured in the accident instead of hyung. It was just bad luck."

In the car, instead of this guy, I was hit in the chest with a steel rod.

Ryu Chungwoo was silent for a moment, but then answered firmly.

"No."

"But that's right. In terms of seat position..."

"Moondae, I have eyes too. I saw you pushing me... You almost died from it. And we don't know what the aftereffects will be like."

Ryu Chungwoo continued without hesitation.

"I had to give up my career because of the aftereffects of a car accident. It wasn't like I was about to die."

"......"

Yeah. I saw that too.

I reflexively recalled the scene of the accident I saw when I used 'Reveal the Truth'.

The guy who broke his arm and cried.

*...Maybe it also affected me.*

That much was definitely true.

Pushing the memory of Ryu Chungwoo's accident aside, I quietly listened as he got it off his chest.

"And you... you're already in a state of low stamina due to the aftereffects. In the past, hiking like this would not have made you so breathless."

"......"

"You were seriously injured, and lying down for too long must have made your condition like this."

It wasn't like that.

Hey, that's... It was because there was no Bacchus.

*I'm going to get it back during the next drawing, you bastard.*

And there wouldn't be any other side effects because I'd used Nectar. I'd recovered at a terrifying speed, so what was he talking about?

It was a problem that would end once I bulked up.

*This conversation is making me break out in a cold sweat.*

However, Ryu Chungwoo, a civilian who did not know this web novel-like setting, was rubbing his forehead.

"It's like that happened to you instead of me. So how could I not be sorry?"

"......"

Okay. Anyway, because Ryu Chungwoo gave up archery due to the aftereffects of a car accident and had a hard time, he must have felt deeply indebted to me, who'd been injured in his place.

"Whew."

Well, I didn't think it was going to work anymore if we kept going back and forth like this.

After thinking for a while, I opened my mouth.

"Is that so? Well, let's say I saved your life."

"...!"

"You can continue to be very grateful. If I need help, I'll ask you without hesitation, so be sure to help me out then."

I thought it would be more inconvenient to tell him not to thank me, so let's just use it well until he felt less sorry.

"Oh, and I'll be okay because I'll build my stamina back up with exercise."

"......"

Ryu Chungwoo stopped walking with a bewildered face for a moment. I guess he didn't know that such a change of attitude would happen in an instant.

But in the end, he burst out laughing.

"...Haha!"

Don't laugh. I don't want to get attached to you.

Ryu Chungwoo spoke as though a weight had lifted off him.

"Okay, I'll repay you."

"Yes."

The air got a little lighter. I shrugged.

And as we continued to descend, I added something.

"Still, I won't go to hyung's house for the holidays. I'm going to the house where they'll pay me the least attention."

"Yeah, do that. Get some rest," Ryu Chungwoo answered readily.

I shrugged my shoulders.

Okay. If I must go to someone else's house, I must go to the quietest place.

Of course, the outside world was in an uproar following the airing of this variety show—the opposite of the quiet holiday I was hoping for.

* * *

*"Really good!! TeSTAR is the best!"*

"...Thank you."

I received a phone call from the production crew making a fuss.

And I totally understood why they were making a fuss.

*The program went better than we thought.*

The ratings for episode 3 reached 6.1%.

*These ratings are unheard of for an idol variety show on cable.*

It was as high as CVN's most popular long-running variety show.

We even landed a PPL deal afterward for the frozen chicken we'd eaten on the island, and it was obvious how good the response was because sales were soaring.

We'd not only saved the show from flopping, but turned it into a huge success. The voices of the production crew were bright.

*"We really want to do Season 2!"*

"Good."

Of course, the people at the top of the broadcasting station would also be giggling.

I heard there was even talk of replacing the pilot program that was supposed to air after us and airing a behind-the-scenes special episode of our show instead.

*Well, coincidence overlapped with coincidence, so the luck of filming was a big factor.*

It was probably successful because it was exciting and unpredictable. Honestly, it would be difficult to revive that feeling, so the next season... I didn't know.

*Still, should I say something good?*

The production crew kept their promise.

As soon as we were no longer stranded, they used all the remaining production budget and made it a luxurious vacation.

We must've eaten at least three snow crabs each. Well, Cha Eugene had five.

"We also want to appear again if the schedule is possible. I really enjoyed it."

*"Ah~ You're too kind. Thank you, Moondae-ssi!"*

That should be enough. I ended the call with the excited PD, who was calling each of the members in turn.

"Is it the PD?"

"Yes."

"He keeps calling."

Bae Sejin grumbled. I shrugged.

"Looks like he's in a good mood because the program did well."

"That... yes."

Bae Sejin muttered darkly.

"...Everyone saw us lose our minds."

"......"

Okay. We really were... the butt of the joke.

Bae Sejin and I were silent in the car for a while, remembering that the stupid things we did in episodes 2 and 3 were emphasized one by one and got on the broadcast.

*I'm afraid of the next round of fansigns...*

After a while, the silence was broken by a voice from behind the wheel.

"Sejin, is it here?"

"Ah, yes! This apartment."

Bae Sejin glanced at the navigation screen and instantly regained his composure.

*Is this the power of an apartment without any loans?*

Probably.

"It's the top floor over there!"

Bae Sejin spoke with a slightly flushed face.

"My mother said she'd be home."

"Yes."

That was right.

I decided to spend this holiday at Bae Sejin's house.

I heard that he was not going anywhere and planned to spend quiet time with his mother.

There was a reasonable excuse too.

"Y-you could think of it as a housewarming party!"

"Okay, let's do that."

"...?!"

"Thank you for the invitation."

And just like that, I accepted Bae Sejin's offer.

*I'd feel bad if he'd only invited me out of politeness.*

Just in case, I bought expensive fruit and meat.

If I had to, I was planning to stay for a day or two just to be polite, so it'd be okay.

"Welcome~ You must be Moondae!"

"Hello."

"Come in, come in~"

Bae Sejin's mother was a bright person with a soft impression.

"I just saw your entertainment show again. Ugh, our babies have been through a lot..."

Okay. Bae Sejin must be an only child, so it must've been not very pleasant to see her only child going to the island and going through all kinds of hardships...

"Still, it was so much fun!"

"...Um, yes."

Well, I was glad we gave her satisfaction.

Anyway, as expected, Bae Sejin was quite harmonious with his mother.

"Moondae is really handsome in person. My kid has talked a lot about Moondae since he was on that audition show."

"Wh-when did I!"

"What, you told me you have a dongsaeng who can sing very well~"

"Ugh!"

Bae Sejin sank in embarrassment. Bae Sejin's mother smiled and hugged her son tightly.

"......"

Well, it was a nice mother-son relationship.

After a while, Bae Sejin, recovering from the shock, stood up after being patted on the back by his mother.

"...I'll show you the room."

"Yes. Thank you."

Let's not make fun of him.

I quietly followed him to the room I'd be staying in, looking around the house on the way.

"I chose the wallpaper. Sky blue."

"It looks good. It's neat."

"...Great. Oh, this closet is like this too!"

"Oh."

Bae Sejin seemed very proud of the home he'd paid for himself, and gradually came back to life completely.

And after spending some time eating the fruit I bought, we had dinner.

"Thank you for the food."

"There's not much, but you guys should eat a lot~"

Dinner was mung bean pancakes and bulgogi japchae.

Bae Sejin, who was worried that his mother would prepare it by herself, had ordered the food in advance from a famous restaurant.

*It feels like a holiday.*

Anyway, I couldn't deny that it was a great time.

"No, I'll do them—"

"Forget it. Give me the dishes."

I cleaned my bowl and wrested the dishwashing duty away from Bae Sejin.

And after a while, I returned to my room.

I closed the door.

*Tak.*

"Hoo."

Lying on the bed, I crossed my arms.

I told them I'd stay alone until night because I had places to contact, so I had plenty of time.

"Status window."

It was time.

The right time to press 'Reveal the Truth'.
