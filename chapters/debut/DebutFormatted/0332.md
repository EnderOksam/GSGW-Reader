# Chapter 332 - A Confession, I

What kind of situation was this?

I was lying in bed, with no intention of sleeping. I had no idea what to make of what had just happened, so I went over it in my head.

Just before this, I'd gone after Keun Sejin, who'd stormed out of the hotel room we'd set aside for the retreat...

*– Beep.*

I pressed the doorbell of Lee Sejin's hotel room, but there was no response.

– ...

I wondered if he wasn't in the room. But was Lee Sejin the kind of person who would leave while upset and give people something to gossip about?

He was obviously inside.

*– Lee Sejin.*

I waited a moment, then rang the doorbell again.

Then came the response.

*Ding.*

Not at the door, which hadn't budged, but on my smartphone.

★:
-Let's talk next time, Moondae. I'm a bit tired right now
:★

That was it.

"......"

I took my hands off the pillow, glanced at the text notification, and swiped it away.

*– Hyung, are you okay?*

"Yes."

Then, the face of Ryu Gunwoo appeared on the screen. I was in the process of returning the call I had hung up earlier.

*...Work. I had work to do.*

"What were you going to tell me?"

*– ...Wait a minute.*

The guy on the screen moved. Then, he raised both hands and pointed to the air. 

*– Do you see this?*

"See what?"

The guy on the screen carefully lowered his hands.

*– You can't see it...*

"......"

*– Well, I've done a little research since I tried to get rid of the 'receiving reward' pop-up last time.*

*Was that so?*

*– A few days ago, I was able to call up your status window and see it on my end. So I wondered if you could see it on the screen...*

"Okay."

It was something worth making a video call about. I understood his intentions.

And when I said nothing more, the room became quiet again.

"......"

A cautious voice was heard.

*– Um, hyung. Something happened earlier, didn't it?*

"It's nothing."

Couldn't he stop asking me about that?

I almost snapped at him, but I realized there was no need to and suppressed the urge.

...From his point of view, the call had suddenly ended and he had no idea why, so of course he'd ask.

"I hung up because someone who didn't know the situation showed up, and there was a bit of a misunderstanding."

*– Ah...*

After a brief exclamation, he went quiet again. He seemed to have roughly understood the situation.

*I guess that's it, then.*

He spoke at the moment when I wondered if it would be okay to end the conversation and hang up.

*– I... Then wouldn't it be okay if you told him the circumstances?*

"What?"

*– Lee Sejin, right? He's hyung's close friend. Then...*

"Just tell him that I returned to the past in someone else's body to become an idol? That I leveled up and came this far because I'd die if I didn't?"

*– ...*

"Does that make any sense at all...? No, sorry."

At that moment, I realized why Keun Sejin had stopped the conversation and left. He'd been trying not to shout needlessly.

I shook my head and continued.

"I'll think over and analyze the status window problem you just mentioned, so you should get some rest today..."

*– No, hyung.*

The guy cut me off.

*– I think this is the first time hyung hasn't been able to focus when we talk about the system. Normally, you'd have asked a lot of questions.*

"...!"

*– That's how much this situation is bothering you.*

"......"

And a small voice was heard.

*– Then why not just tell him... so you'll feel better?*

I involuntarily clenched my fists.

That was the moment.

I suddenly thought back on something those words had brought to mind.

The mistakes this guy had made whenever he'd met other people while I was there.

*– Hyung!*

*– Gunwoo-hyung, uh, uhm...*

Mistakes in what he called me or how he addressed me.

I'd assumed it was because he was so bad at deceiving others.

But now that I thought about it, how was it that the guy who lived in the body of Ryu Gunwoo for several years made such a mistake every time...

Wasn't it unnatural?

"Don't tell me... All those times you called me the wrong thing when I was with the members..."

*– ...*

"Is it because you wanted me to be found out?"

After a moment of silence...

A voice came from the screen.

*– Maybe...*

"...!"

*– I wasn't aware of it at the time, but I think I acted on that wish subconsciously... I thought it would probably help hyung.*

"Help me?"

*– You've been close to the TeSTAR members for years and have lived with them all that time. I wanted hyung to feel at ease after revealing his secret...*

It was an action based on goodwill.

However, the fact that he hadn't considered my intentions or feelings at all reminded me of something.

And he seemed to realize it himself.

*– But that's what the system does.*

*Yes.*

*– Ignoring hyung's thoughts and feelings and doing whatever it wants.*

It was an overly mechanical and inhuman impulse for a softie like him.

I opened my mouth slowly.

"...So, that's why you assumed you were being affected by the system?"

I remembered how especially afraid of the system he'd seemed the last time we met.

*– I feel that the part of me that's become linked to the system is getting stronger. Even though I've moved into another body, it seems to still affect me somehow.*

And a bitter, rather discouraged voice came from the screen.

*– Yes. So, ever since I noticed, I've really been trying not to make mistakes...*

"......"

*– It really wasn't intentional. It must have made things uncomfortable for you, and I'm really sorry. I'll do my best not to let it control me in the future...*

"...Okay."

A sigh of relief was heard from the screen.

*He must have had a hard time.*

Being influenced by something he didn't understand or have any control over must have been quite unpleasant.

*I'm going to have to pay close attention to this guy's condition from now on.*

As if someone had thrown cold water in my face, I came to my senses a little and looked at Keundal.

But we soon returned to the original topic.

*– Yes, b-but hyung, this is really coming from me. I have a feeling your friend will believe you.*

*I was going crazy.*

I spoke while holding back a groan.

"If he were the sort of person to believe such science-fiction stories, I would have told him already."

However, Lee Sejin was one of the most pragmatic among us.

I had to admit that.

*So... that's why I'd been putting it off.*

Because we had to work, and because the timing wasn't right.

With excuses like that, I was justifying keeping my mouth shut and putting it off.

In the heat of the moment, I'd said, 'I'll tell him when the time is right,' so I'd have to get around to it eventually... No matter how I explained it, the chances that he wouldn't believe me seemed too high.

I sighed.

Then, a voice that sounded a little flabbergasted came from the screen... *Wait, flabbergasted?*

*– Hyung, I'm telling you in case you've forgotten... I was your status window, remember??*

I knew that.

*– I know how Lee Sejin-nim and hyung have been with each other!*

"...!!"

*– I know about the fights and everything, and I don't want hyung to get into fights... Anyway, I'm telling you this as a third party who knows all the information. Please trust me...!*

It was quite embarrassing to think he'd seen all of that.

But putting my embarrassment aside, Keundal was practically pleading with me now.

*– As practical as that friend is, he knows you well...! And you trust each other a lot.*

Funny enough, I *was* listening.

*– If hyung explains it seriously and honestly, he'll believe it.*

"......"

I lay back with my head resting on one arm.

*– Hyung...*

I didn't know what to do.

"I don't even know where to start after what just happened."

*– Ah...*

"First... let me see how things play out."

I couldn't even think about telling the truth right now, not knowing how this guy would react tomorrow.

There was a chance that it wasn't a big deal and his emotions were just running high. If he cooled down, I could explain the misunderstanding, be a little embarrassed, and that would be the end of it.

*– Hyung, so...*

The guy on the screen seemed to be trying to encourage me in his own way, but it didn't really help much.

"Okay."

I soon ended the call, and after debating for a bit, replied to Keun Sejin's text message.

★:
Sorry. Rest well-
:★

And oddly enough, it really did seem to be no big deal.

The very next day, the overnight work camp for the album went smoothly.

* * *

"I added an explosive sound to the beat. How does it feel?" Kim Raebin asked.

"It sounds good. But how about using it only in the chorus and replacing it with a different sound in the verses? People's ears might get tired quickly."

"Okay!"

Keun Sejin gave feedback and kept things running smoothly, just like the previous day. He supported good ideas and rejected inappropriate ones.

"How about making a song based on demo number 4?"

"...That's fine. But I think number 3 is better."

"Okay."

It wasn't like before, when I'd been angry and things had gotten awkward between us, and he'd just smiled and pretended to be nice.

It was just... The sense of distance had deepened.

*Damn it.*

He wasn't giving me a chance to have a private conversation.

Well, with things like this, even apologizing was difficult. There was no way for an 'I'm sorry' and 'I'm fine' conversation to lead to a proper resolution.

"Let's get some rest."

"Yes."

It was break time. I followed with my eyes the guys who were scattered around the suite more freely than yesterday. I stood up.

*Let's splash some cold water on my face.*

If I couldn't concentrate because I was frustrated, it would be stupid. Even if I had to use cold water, I'd get myself back in the zone and focus on the album.

I was on my way back after using the washstand by the front door when I heard a voice.

"...Lee Sejin."

"Yes?"

A voice came from the kitchen next to the hallway.

Bae Sejin called Keun Sejin.

It was rare. I immediately remembered the conversation I'd had with Bae Sejin just after Keun Sejin left yesterday.

*– I... Was it something I said?*

*– ...No. There was a bit of a misunderstanding between me and him. Sorry for getting you into trouble.*

*– No, no, don't mention it.*

It seemed obvious that this was about to come up.

The voices of two people with the same name continued to be heard from across the corner of the hallway.

"Excuse me... Yesterday, if I made a mistake, I'm sorry."

"What? No. It's not like that. I made a fuss by myself."

"...But I thought there was a problem?"

"Oh, if there's a problem, it's mine. Did I say something rude then? I'm sorry, hyung."

"No, it's fine..."

After a moment of silence.

"Um, hyung. I have something else to do, so is it okay if we stop here?"

"...Wait a minute."

I heard Bae Sejin swallow, as if trying to hold back a groan.

"...Well, yesterday, the person Park Moondae was looking for... It's not like Park Moondae told me about him. I only knew because I happened to be there."

"......"

"He probably wouldn't have told me if..."

"Hyung."

Keun Sejin cut him off.

"I think I should hear this from Moondae himself."

"...!"

"I really don't think it's your fault, so you don't have to worry. Rather, thanks to you, something's been cleared up."

I heard Bae Sejin groan. He seemed to want to explain what was going on, but he also felt that saying more would be meddling.

And he finally spoke.

"Okay. That... I hope you have a good talk with Park Moondae."

"......"

The guy who would normally have said 'yes' and finished instead spat out in a tired and sharp tone.

"...I don't know. I don't know if I will."

"...!"

"Anyway... yes. Thanks."

I knew then.

Clearly, this guy was taking the current situation more personally than it warranted.

And even though he was so pissed off right now, he still wanted to hear more.

*In that case.*

I suddenly remembered what Keundal had said at the end of our conversation yesterday, when I'd told him I didn't know what would happen.

Words that hadn't made much of an impression on me at the time.

*– Now that the situation is like this, wouldn't he believe it more if you told him? You're not the type to make up some ridiculous story just to appease an angry person.*

*– ...!*

*– Since he knows you, the stranger it sounds, the less likely he is to think it's an excuse or a prank!*

"......"

I thought about it.

*Fuck. Okay.*

I picked up the phone and texted the manager.

There was no other way. I had to do it.

* * *

Lee Sejin liked concerts.

If you knew him, it would be easy to assume that someone like Lee Sejin would prefer variety shows or appearances on famous WeTube channels.

But what people thought with their heads and what they felt with their hearts were different.

The concert was a way of proving oneself as an idol.

The embodiment of their dreams and ambitions.

*That's why I like them...*

But today, he couldn't fully enjoy that good concert.

Normally, at a concert, the members would gather on the center stage around the encore and enjoy themselves together.

Even if that didn't happen, moments of fantastic teamwork between the members onstage also deepened his immersion and sense of belonging.

But that was impossible today.

*Hoo.*

Ever since the uproar a few days ago, he hadn't had a proper conversation with Park Moondae, and now here they were onstage together.

It kept bothering him like a stone in his shoe, bringing his mood down every time.

*Why...*

In fact, he already knew that Park Moondae wasn't as lenient with him as he was with the other members.

Actually, he welcomed it.

It felt like the right kind of relationship for them.

*We were equals. Friends.*

He was even a little proud. Even if the group disappeared, he was confident that he and Park Moondae would continue to work together and remain friends, even if that wasn't the case with the others.

That reassurance.

But when he realized that his certainty about their friendship had been an illusion, that reassurance turned into a sense of betrayal.

*It's really funny.*

No, Park Moondae hadn't even said he trusted him the most, so what right did he have to feel betrayed?

Lee Sejin laughed in disbelief.

He knew very well how easily people could play nice depending on their circumstances. And there was nothing particularly sad about it.

*Especially in this industry.*

So he couldn't understand at all why he was so disappointed with Park Moondae, as if they had become great friends.

But thinking about it again, wasn't it only natural to feel that way?

*It's not like we were just any other group.*

They'd spent a lot of time talking about all sorts of troubles, been through accidents together, and shared their personal histories.

*And yet...*

Lee Sejin clenched his fists.

*All he did was apologize over text.*

It meant that Park Moondae didn't see the need to do anything more right now.

What was even funnier was that Lee Sejin was thinking about whether he should approach him first and talk things out.

*Wow!*

Watching the shower of confetti burst from the side of the stage, Lee Sejin took a deep breath.

He wasn't just out of breath.

*It's annoying.*

On this good day, it was absurd that he was in such a bad state because he had a fight with one of the members in front of a large number of spectators. 

He'd always despised idiots who couldn't keep personal affairs separate from work, and now he felt like one himself.

That was why he'd always paid extra attention to performances and anything done live. If he couldn't immerse himself, he'd at least make his performance precise.

"Thank you for your hard work~"

"You worked hard!"

So, when he came down after the concert, he felt hollow rather than filled with the usual joy.

The energy he'd at least been able to show onstage was gone.

"......"

"Uh, where's Moondae?"

"He left quickly! He said he had something to do!"

In the meantime, the fact that the other party had apparently left without being bothered at all somehow made him even more pissed off.

*Why am I the only one getting worked up?*

Torn between anger and fatigue, Lee Sejin held back a sigh and eventually left.

He planned to take the van back to the hotel and go to sleep.

*Tirik.*

However, when he opened the door to his hotel room and went in...

"You're back."

"...?!"

Someone was already sitting in the room.

"I got the key from the manager."

Someone with his hands on the table... It was Park Moondae.

Lee Sejin couldn't believe his eyes for a moment.

*This...*

*What was going on?*

Why the hell was the guy who left first here?

At that moment, a flash of hope passed through his head.

*No way?*

*Was he going to apologize properly...?*

"Sorry I came in without asking."

"Ah, are you sorry about that?"

*Fuck, fuck.*

Keun Sejin felt the urge to slap a hand over his own mouth. He didn't even know why he bothered to say that.

In the first place, it seemed like showing displeasure was losing.

And... And he was afraid of what it might do to their relationship.

"I'm sorry about that, and I'm sorry about the other things, too."

However, Park Moondae simply answered calmly, without snapping back.

Somehow, his calmness only made Lee Sejin more pissed off.

"What other things?"

*Is he really this calm...?*

He almost snapped, but then he noticed Park Moondae's expression and posture...

His expression was still serious, but he was fidgeting with his hands and shifting his shoulders awkwardly.

"...!"

Park Moondae... He was very nervous.

"There's something I couldn't properly explain to you yesterday."

"......"

"The hyungs, Chungwoo and Bae Sejin, don't know everything about my situation. They only found out about part of it by chance."

"Found out?"

"Yes. So this is the first time I'm telling anyone of my own accord."

"......"

For a moment, Lee Sejin forgot all the complexities and irritations and focused on the conversation.

What the hell was he going to say?

"First... I think it would be appropriate to start by saying this."

Park Moondae's eyes lit up.

"I am not Park Moondae."

"....?"

"And I've traveled back in time."

Lee Sejin began to suspect he'd actually fallen asleep in the van, and that all this was a dream.
