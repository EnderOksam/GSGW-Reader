# Chapter 345 - OVER the Masquerade

TeSTAR's album teaser was released in mid-May.

Around that time, the movie in which they'd made cameo appearances, ⟨Cosmic Gunner⟩, had become a global box-office success, drawing 8 million viewers in Korea alone, and was finally starting to leave theaters.

Some people were grumbling in response to the articles, and the comeback rumors had only started circulating three days before the teaser was released.

Park Moondae's first fansite master was trying desperately not to bite her nails.

*The general manager looks like an idiot who's obsessed with America...! Don't tell me he's gotten to TeSTAR too!*

The general manager had been fixated on Miry-nay until now, allowing TeSTAR to avoid his clutches. She feared he'd finally sunk his claws into them with all his hot air.

The thought crossed her mind. Of course, only a small portion of the public was worried, and most of those who heard the news of TeSTAR's comeback were riding high on the success of ⟨Cosmic Gunner⟩.

That was actually a little worrying.

★$
– No way, no way, TeSTAR's coming back! My god T_T That came out of nowhere

– They'll rank on the Billboard charts again. Wow, I'm already excited
$★

*...People's expectations are sky-high.*

Moondae had never let her down, but the success never hinged on him alone.

*Even if Moondae and the kids do well, it's useless if the company does stupid things again...!*

Even the quote from a company official at the end of the article, saying this album would delight a wide variety of global fandoms, made her uneasy.

★$
– Heart pounding! (#b_link_b#)
$★

Still, she shared TeSTAR's comeback article and commented on it, but it was for the benefit of anyone who came across her post while searching for TeSTAR.

*People will see my reactions... I need to be as cautious as possible...*

She suddenly missed Moondae, who'd put an apple bun sticker she'd made on his cheek at the fansigning event.

...At least, if they made a comeback, she'd see it again. They would do another fan-signing event.

*Okay. I'll trust them and wait.*

In fact, wasn't this the season when it wouldn't be strange for them to make a comeback, judging by the timing alone?

She decided to leave it at that, approaching it from the perspective of a genuine fan.

And three days later...

★$
– It's up! Guys, it's up!
$★

The teaser, which she clicked with equal parts nervousness and excitement, started with dark smoke curling upward.

What unfolded was a dark, cozy, modern banquet hall.

In the slightly ominous yet elegant hall, cast in thick shadows, figures in colorful, elegant attire danced in masks.

The silhouettes overlapped and blended together.

For a few seconds, it looked like a scene from a 20th-century art film...

The sound suddenly changed.

– *Rumble!*

Thunder.

"...!"

Amid flashes of light, the scene changed to the outdoors surrounding the dark and pale banquet hall.

Rain poured down from the nighttime sky above the castle gate, and light from the banquet hall leaked through its windows.

[...]

*Splash.*

And a pair of bare feet came to a stop at the rain-soaked gate, splashing water from the puddles around them.

Their skin was coated in red, the color dripping off into the water.

The water was becoming contaminated.

"...!"

There was something sinister about the camera work and editing.

And slowly, the camera tilted upward.

What was revealed... A face hidden deep in the shadows of a red hood.

A nearly featureless mask.

*Goooooh.* An ominous noise sounded in the background.

Just as the viewers took in the chilling sight, the screen turned black again.

Darkness.

A riff pierced the darkness.

– *Mmmmm.*

The graceful, slow, low-pitched violin played ominously, and the laughter of the people in the banquet hall faded.

The album title popped up.

Elegant and ominous, dark yet luxurious.

But she, too, was a bit uneasy.

*This concept isn't at all similar to their cameo.*

Wasn't it a bit too heavy-handed? It wasn't like the usual TeSTAR...

"......"

Anyway, despite her worries, the teaser's quality was incredible, so she concluded that they'd be fine as long as they could endure the aggro that would likely accompany its release.

*Let's go for a career-high this time too!*

But in fact, the group itself had already considered her concerns.

They'd thought long and hard about this: what was at the core of TeSTAR's identity?

Where did their uniqueness come from?

– *I think it all comes back to one thing.*

After Seon Ahyeon's analysis, Park Moondae came to this conclusion.

– *Fun.*

It was just fun.

And with that fun came another key element...

Immersion.

– *That's probably why the first keyword Ahyeon came up with after analyzing all this was immersion.*

They never made obscure symbols and allusions their main value.

Those were just secondary elements that people could talk about.

The main value was one thing.

*Fun to see and hear.*

The most traditional and fundamental value of the stage and music.

A pleasant experience that could be enjoyed without discomfort or an unpleasant, lingering aftertaste.

TeSTAR's core had never changed. And that would be the case with this album as well.

– *Let's do this.*

A week after the teaser was released, the music video was uploaded.

*Oh... my... god!*

The fansite master clicked on the video right away, even as her hands trembled.

The music video began with the gate from the teaser, which had been partly torn off its hinges.

It was a cut following the teaser, but there was a clear difference.

*...There is no light.*

The faint light that had been leaking out of the banquet hall was gone. The rain had stopped, too.

Outside, it was the middle of the night, and everything seemed frozen in time.

*Goooooh.*

The rumbling sound was chilling, but moments later...

The camera seemed to be sucked through the castle gate, passing down an unlit corridor and into the dark banquet hall.

Broken masks and splashes of red paint lay on the floor.

In the hall, already in ruins, the inhuman figure in the red robe from the teaser stood in the center of the disorder. Anyone could see that it was the cause of all this destruction.

*Hmm... Is that supposed to represent the plague?*

Was it one of the members giving off that creepy feeling?

But the camera didn't get any closer.

Instead, the scene changed in a swish.

There was the gate again.

– *Whoa.*

The sound of a short intake of breath. The camera captured someone facing the huge, damaged castle gate, his back to the camera.

Instead of silence, there were the sounds of the forest at night and chirping insects.

A tall man was wearing a navy jacket.

At that moment, the music returned.

*Whoa-!*

The sounds of drums and trombones came together in a quick, magnificent passage, and the person standing there slowly looked up at the sky.

*Kwakwang!*

Thunder and lightning struck.

It was starting to rain.

But maybe it was because of the music. Unlike the gloom of the teaser, there was something satisfying and almost emotional about the scene.

*Huh?*

The moment the person turned around and took off the mask...

A close-up shot caught the face.

It was Park Moondae.

"...!"

His neck, shoulders, and ears, which were in shadow, were adorned with techwear-style accessories that looked like sci-fi equipment.

But the viewers only had a moment to take it all in.

In the very next scene, Park Moondae threw back the navy hood that had been pulled over his face.

White hair, with the faintest hint of blue, fluttered.

*Chichik!*

Then something glitched in the air behind his head, and blue light rose behind his shoulders and rippled around him like a mane.

"...!?"

Park Moondae grinned.

[*Ha!*]

With that short shout, the gloomy scene instantly became a stage filled with sci-fi-like neon lights!
