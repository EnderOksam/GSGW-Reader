# Chapter 319 - Conversation, VII

Leave 'Park Moondae' be?

That would mean... Whether or not he disappeared, I'd just go back to the point where I won the grand prize and continue living as a TeSTAR member.

There was no longer any need to talk further with Park Moondae to understand how the system worked. He didn't seem to know anything about it.

I'd done more than was required of me, so I could put it out of my mind and claim the idol life I'd been building for years.

I couldn't deny it.

As ruthless as it was, it sounded reasonable. It would mean I could finally acknowledge that the life I'd lived in that body had been my own.

*But...*

I took a deep breath and relaxed my fists.

"No."

"......"

Cheongryeo stopped tapping his fingers on the table.

"Of course, the experiences I had in Park Moondae's body were my own. I'm the one who went through them."

"Yes. So—"

"But the body is something else."

I leaned back.

"Just because I've been using it for a few years doesn't make it mine. All the years before that, it was his."

"......"

"And as it turned out, those years helped me quite a bit."

If 'Park Moondae' hadn't lived his life as a good person, it would've been difficult to win such a dramatic first place. Even if I'd managed to debut, my past would've brought me down. 

Cheongryeo stared at me.

"Has it ever occurred to you that you might've succeeded faster if you'd been in your original body?"

"I haven't tried it, so how can I be sure? Besides, this has little to do with achievements in the first place."

I grinned.

"Park Moondae has the right to live in his own body. Don't twist things."

"......"

He continued to stare at me without saying a word, then slowly opened his mouth.

"So, Hubaenim. If he wants it back, you're going to give up your career?"

"I never said that."

"Then what?"

I crossed my arms.

"We have to reach an agreement."

Now that his status abnormalities were over, I was going to listen to what he wanted and work things out from there. I was good at negotiating.

"I'm just saying I'm not going to stab him in the back and cheat him out of his own body."

"I don't understand. You have no reason to be so kind to him."

I shrugged.

"Well... I guess I've grown attached to him."

After seeing how 'Park Moondae' had lived over the past few years in the span of just a few days, I couldn't help it. I was only human, after all.

*It's kind of funny how I've decided to respect his wishes instead of making this other bastard give up his body no matter what it takes.*

At that moment, the guy sitting across from me took his hands off the table, his expression blank.

"Attached?"

"Yes."

"I didn't expect your attachment to someone to carry the same weight as your achievements."

I put my glasses back on.

"You keep trying to weigh things that might not even be comparable... Try choosing between your dog and your career."

"......"

"Isn't it better to live with both than to give one up for the other?"

Just in time, the dog under the table barked. Cheongryeo reflexively lowered his hand.

I watched the dog rub its nose against his hand.

"I'm going to try to find a way to have both too, if possible."

"......"

Cheongryeo fell silent, an odd expression on his face. He seemed to be calculating something in his head—or perhaps just thinking.

*It's surprising.*

I'd expected this bastard to encourage me so he'd have one less competitor.

*His way of thinking is twisted and ruthless, but perhaps he was giving me honest advice this time.*

I ended up adding it briefly.

"Anyway... Thank you for recognizing TeSTAR's Park Moondae as me."

"......"

It felt good that his first reaction after hearing the truth had been to acknowledge me. 

He paused for a moment, then smiled wryly.

"Fine. If you say so."

*I take that back.* He sounded like he was giving me permission or something, and that pissed me off.

Cheongryeo removed his hand from his dog.

"That's too bad. I was willing to help if things came to that."

*Don't tell me this bastard was offering to help get rid of the real Park Moondae?* 

*Just how much does he think the career of a top-tier idol is worth?*

I changed the topic before it turned into some kind of obsession.

"Then give this body a call tomorrow."

"Tomorrow?"

"I need to check who's in this body after today."

"Hmm."

"Don't do anything stupid."

Just in case, I planned to leave 'Park Moondae' a note telling him to remember the voice on this phone and ignore any future calls from the same person.

Cheongryeo shrugged.

"Fine. I'll contact the other body too, so wait."

"You don't need to—"

*Wait a minute.*

I remembered the phone call I received from this guy out of the blue one day in November.

*– Hmm... I wonder if you have anything to say to me.*

And the next words.

*– So not yet, then?*

"......"

Looking back and comparing the dates, I realized today was the day before that call.

*I see.*

Now I understood. This bastard contacted me knowing the situation at that time.

*But he didn't mention it even once until I won the grand prize.*

Was he actually trying to help or not?

He was an unpredictable bastard. I clicked my tongue and got up.

"Are you going?"

"Yes."

The conversation was over, and since I hadn't gotten much out of it... I thought I'd look for more traces of Park Moondae, even physical ones.

"Well, see you. I'll be in touch tomorrow."

It felt a bit odd that I had already received that call.

"Yes."

I ended the conversation with Cheongryeo and returned to the officetel I'd woken up in.

I began searching as soon as I arrived.

*Is there really nothing?*

I didn't find anything useful.

The officetel didn't look particularly lived in, perhaps because he'd spent most of his time at the boarding academy. Only the bedroom was filled with hobby-related items. 

TeSTAR's albums and MDs.

They were neatly stored in various drawers that I hadn't even had time to open because I'd been chatting with him every time.

*Did he go to another concert?*

I even found MDs from the world tour we'd done earlier this year.

"Looks like he made good use of his time."

I smiled and put them back. It felt good to have been able to help him relieve some stress while he was studying for the exam.

But the situation was still the same.

'Park Moondae' was still nowhere to be found, and the only thing I could find was a memo in his smartphone's calendar app.

★:
#><#r📅 Calendarr#><#

** #cy●cy# The day I meet hyung!**
:★

"......"

Other than that, there was nothing else on the calendar, and he wasn't logged into any social media accounts. Whenever he wasn't looking up TeSTAR, it seemed he did nothing but study.

I tried a few times to see if I could call up his status window, but nothing popped up. All I got was my own 'Currently receiving reward' notification.

"Hoo."

I supposed that was as much as I could do.

It was almost midnight, so I sat near the sofa and started scribbling a note.

"......"

I'd set calls from Cheongryeo to be recorded, so I told him to memorize the voice, not meet him or give him any additional information, and explained once more how to contact me in December.

Then soon... It was midnight.

*Dang–*

I was lying on the sofa.

A small bell rang softly in the corner.

*He got a new clock.*

*I think that was a concert MD, too.* With that brief thought, sleep washed over me again.

My mind sank into darkness, and my thoughts vanished. Just like every night over the last few days.

*Dang–*

But the bell rang again.

*Wait a minute.*

I realized I could still think and hear.

*I'm coming back.*

I didn't know what it was, but something seemed to have interrupted me just as I was about to fall asleep...

"Hyung!"

"...!"

I sat up.

I was still in the officetel, but there was something here that hadn't been there before.

"It's so great to see you again, hyung...!"

An oddly familiar-looking boy was sitting on the floor in front of the sofa where I'd been lying.

He resembled me strangely, but he wasn't exactly the same...

"Park Moondae."

He smiled awkwardly when I called his name.

"Yes... Though I'm not sure if I can go by that name anymore."

I could guess why he said that. He looked like a mix of Park Moondae and Ryu Gunwoo, but seemed to be in his early twenties.

*Don't tell me that's his new body.*

Perhaps I was still too sleepy to make sense of it, but I felt a headache coming on. I pressed my temples and rose from the sofa.

"That's your name, though."

"That... Hyung uses that name too. And I used hyung's name as well, so... Ah, please call me 'Keundal' for now!"

It sounded like a nickname someone would use at a gaming meet-up, but he could do as he liked. I nodded and went straight to the point.

"What's going on here?"

It was an unexpected situation, but he seemed strangely calm.

The guy in front of me looked toward the blinds on the veranda, looking a little nervous.

Then he pushed a button.

"Ah, first of all... Please look at this."

*Wiing.*

The motorized blinds rose, and what I saw...

"...!"

The sun was setting outside the window.

But the colors were anything but ordinary. Golden, purple, and red sunlight seemed to collide, and I could hear the faintest tinkling of a music box somewhere.

It was a view I recognized.

"It's a scene from the ⟨Magical Boys⟩ teaser. It must've left a deep impression on both hyung and me, since it manifested here like this."

"...Manifested?"

"Yes."

The guy smiled awkwardly.

"I... I've been told this place is imaginary. A mindscape, I guess."

"...!"

"So, it's not real, it's all in our heads, you know..."

"Yeah. I get it."

"Yes."

It was probably similar to the daydream I'd had during that coma.

And I realized why he looked the way he did. Living as 'Ryu Gunwoo' for so long must've mixed up his sense of self.

*What a strange experience.*

I held back a sigh.

The guy smiled awkwardly.

"Hyung always seems to know everything..."

I had no interest in hearing that when I knew so little I was about to go crazy.

"Not at all. Rather, you seem used to this situation."

"......"

"Don't tell me you were here all day today... I mean, yesterday?"

The original Park Moondae, 'Keundal', sat carefully on the floor and answered gravely.

"Yes."

*As expected.*

"And it wasn't just yesterday."

"What?"

"Hyung, I... I've been pressing 'Accept Synchronization' every time I clear a status abnormality."

I remembered. It had bothered me that every time, all it said was *'Accumulation complete.'*

"It turned out that the synchronization was applying to the moments hyung and I spent together."

"With me?"

"Yes."

"I don't recall us ever spending any time together."

"I wasn't in a form hyung would recognize."

Keundal rubbed his palms on his thighs with a nervous expression. Then he spoke quietly.

He suddenly changed the subject.	

"Hyung, that... I want to apologize first."

"For what?"

"Everything hyung suffered through... Actually, it's my fault."

"......"

I watched him in silence.

"That... When hyung was in a coma, I had a strange thought."

He slowly began to explain the situation as he'd experienced it in the four days following my accident.

"I thought hyung might never wake up again. So I... I went through your old things while cleaning your house. That's when I knew."

As he spoke, the thoughts he'd had back then began to float through my head, like an echo from the past.

*– This...*

*This is my old camera.*

And I hadn't bothered to delete the media on it.

Among the files was a video I'd uploaded to my 'gun1234' account.

This guy, who was tidying up the house, seemed to recognize it at once.

It had been quite a shock to him.

*– Wh-what should I do...?*

"At that time... I guess I was really shaken up when I realized hyung was the one behind all those fancams. You'd been helping me all along, but there was nothing I could do for you, and I was so sure hyung was going to die soon..."

"......"

"So after organizing your things, I was on my way home carrying some trash, and I leaned against the railing of the outdoor stairwell without thinking..."

It was such a cheap one-room building that I'd often thought the railing looked dangerous whenever I went out to smoke.

"But the railing couldn't support my weight and gave way..."

And of course, it had given way precisely when this guy was at his most shaken.

*– Ping!*

At the worst possible timing.

"Until now, I thought I'd probably just broken my leg or something... I didn't tell you because I didn't want you to worry. But maybe... Maybe I was already dead after I fell."

I almost bit my tongue. But his story wasn't over.

"So I think the system made a mistake."

"A mistake?"

"This system gives you what you want right before you die."

Keundal swallowed nervously.

"But... I wanted hyung to live a long, happy life. And at that time, you were practically dead too, since you were in a coma... An error occurred. It got the target of my wish wrong."

"...!"

"Hyung went back to the past and lived in my body instead of me."

*– Ugh...*

I remembered waking up in that dirty motel room.

But I was looking at it through my own eyes.

"I think I accidentally fused with the system in the process... But apparently, some of my consciousness remained. A sort of desire to correct the situation."

And I realized.

This guy had been using the word 'system' so naturally for some time.

...Even though I'd never called it that when talking to him. It was a word I'd defined for myself alone.
