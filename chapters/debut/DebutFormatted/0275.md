# Chapter 275 - Facing the Past

In the hallway of the broadcasting station, we ran into contestants from ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ even though we weren't appearing on the same program.

It was obvious.

*It's on purpose.*

The staff led them here to film a scene for the broadcast.

⟨Idol Incorporated⟩ was more than capable of pulling this off. They could make up for TeSTAR not appearing on the show with this trick.

And if the contestants said hello here and we flatly refused to be filmed, we'd be the ones who looked bad.

As far as anyone could tell, they were just filming for their appearance on ⟨Music Bomb⟩, so wouldn't it seem like an overreaction to ask them to blur our faces when we'd just happened to run into each other?

Ultimately, the problem with this situation was this.

*There's a camera.*

Cameras were all over the place.

Seon Ahyeon was meeting Chae Seodam for the first time in years, in full view of the cameras.

*Damn it.*

I winked at the other guys. Taking the hint, the more sociable members stepped forward and greeted them first.

"Oh~ hello!"

"We're cheering for you. Go for it!"

That was enough.

There were seven of us, so Seon Ahyeon could just nod.

I bowed and greeted the participants, conscious of two or three cameras turning toward us.

I was thinking of ending it like this.

But I guess the contestants didn't get the message.

"I'm a real fan of TeSTAR sunbaenim!"

"Oh my god... Wow, I can't believe it."

"Sunbaenim, I'm really, really sorry, but can I get your autograph...?"

Perhaps because of TeSTAR's reputation, about half of the contestants slowly approached us.

I didn't think the production team had told them to do this. It just seemed like they were excited to see a genuinely popular idol group.

And the cameras followed them.

"......"

*This is going to take a while. Not good.*

I checked Seon Ahyeon's face.

Fortunately, he wasn't visibly agitated. He was smiling faintly as usual, and although he looked a bit stiff, it wasn't particularly noticeable.

It seemed like nothing out of the ordinary was going to happen.

Chae Seodam, who was on the other side, hadn't said much either, quietly staying behind the others and blending into the crowd.

*...As expected, he's pretty smart.*

It seemed he knew that letting on he knew Seon Ahyeon here and showing off their acquaintance wouldn't benefit him; it would only increase the risk.

*He didn't say a word about Seon Ahyeon even while appearing in ⟨Idol Inc.⟩.*

He must have been trying his best not to provoke Seon Ahyeon, keeping his mouth shut because saying anything would only hurt him.

However, that was what *he* wanted.

The producers had other ideas.

"Come to think of it, aren't you two from the same school?"

I knew the broadcasting station guys wouldn't let this prey slip away.

Friendships with famous idols always made for good content.

"Ah."

Crafty as ever, Chae Seodam jumped in first with a calm and measured answer.

"Yes. We say hello sometimes... Long time no see, sunbaenim!"

He sounded a bit awkward, as if greeting someone he knew but wasn't particularly close to.

*This bastard is quite repulsive.*

Did he guess that if Seon Ahyeon frowned here, the production team would try to portray him as someone who'd changed after becoming famous?

Now that the production team had backed them into a corner, he must have decided to get footage of himself smiling and greeting Seon Ahyeon, showing that they knew each other.

Unfortunately, the industry wasn't that fair.

I looked at him carefully.

*If we have to, we can make a deal with the production team and get the footage removed.*

Because TeSTAR weren't contestants on ⟨Idol Inc.⟩, but idols who had already signed a contract with the company. They were also under T1's umbrella.

If that didn't work, we could openly say something that would force them to cut the footage entirely. Any damage would be manageable.

*– Excuse me. Why don't you return all the things you stole from him? I’ve lost count of how many there were.*

Something like that. It might start some rumors, but we could deal with a little drama just this once.

That would be better than letting this bastard use Seon Ahyeon to get ahead while Seon Ahyeon went through a mental breakdown.

*Should I do it?*

It was a moment of contemplation.

"...Yes. Hello."

"...!"

Seon Ahyeon calmly responded.

A small, victorious smile flitted across Chae Seodam's face.

And the production team quickly intervened to draw out the conversation.

"Can I ask for a word of encouragement?"

"Ah.... That."

Seon Ahyeon seemed to be taking a deep breath. Chae Seodam waited with a confident smile.

"That..."

"Wait a minute."

I saw Ryu Chungwoo restraining an angry Bae Sejin behind him, quietly whispering for him to stop.

And Seon Ahyeon, without any interruption, spoke slowly while looking at Chae Seodam.

"No matter what you do... I hope you'll believe in yourself more."

Seon Ahyeon smiled faintly.

"Now that you've... come this far, I think that's the most important thing. Instead of focusing on what others are doing, focus on yourself... Trust yourself."

"......"

A flash of surprise crossed Chae Seodam's face.

Seon Ahyeon took a step forward. He made eye contact with Chae Seodam openly.

"We... weren't very close. But I hope you can make it through the rest of the show with a kind heart."

"......"

"I will pray for that."

It was a pep talk.

However, depending on how they were interpreted, those words could mean very different things.

And it probably sounded quite different to Chae Seodam.

Something like this, perhaps.

*– If you have time to attack others because of your inferiority complex, focus on yourself and try to be a good person.*

*He's tense all of a sudden.*

I saw a look of bewilderment and tension in his eyes. It was quite pleasant.

Seon Ahyeon looked around and called out, a little shyly.

"And the others too. Go for it...!"

"Yes! Thank you!"

As the other contestants responded, Seon Ahyeon raised his fist encouragingly.

He looked comfortable.

Chae Seodam seemed to have managed a smile, but he didn't look very comfortable.

Still, he had no choice but to hold out his hand for a handshake.

"...Thank you for your kind words."

"Yes."

Seon Ahyeon shook his hand casually, then gave a wave.

"Oh, shall we leave a message too? It's not often we get to meet like this~"

"We are so grateful!"

Keun Sejin was quick to jump in when he saw an opening, steering the conversation away.

And with the enthusiastic support of the contestants and the production team, we filmed a few words of encouragement.

Taking advantage of that opening, Seon Ahyeon stepped back.

"Are you okay?"

"...Yes."

Seon Ahyeon answered quietly and calmly. His voice sounded somewhat relieved.

"Our manager said we really have to move now."

"Yes, thank you."

"Ayy, don't mention it. Then, we'll see you on stage next time~"

The other members filmed a few short words of support for the contestants before moving on.

And the moment we parted in the hallway, I saw Chae Seodam turn his head.

"...!"

Perhaps he was trying to glare at Seon Ahyeon, but when our eyes met, he flinched.

*That gave him a scare, huh?*

As the first-place contestant this season, he must have convinced himself he could take on Seon Ahyeon.

*The program feels like everything when you're a contestant.*

*Does he have a sense of superiority because he ranked higher in the program than Seon Ahyeon did?*

He'd expected Seon Ahyeon to be intimidated by him, so it was probably driving him crazy to find himself on the receiving end of a threat.

I smiled at the guy.

And I mouthed the words.

*Lucky bastard.*

"...!"

There was no need to go out of my way to make enemies, but he was more emotional than I'd expected, so I didn't think he'd last long.

It was nice to know I wouldn't have to worry about him for much longer.

I rubbed the back of my neck and turned away. I wondered how long that bastard could keep it together in front of the cameras.

Not long after, TeSTAR walked through the corridors and arrived at the waiting room.

"I'll go check on our lunch," our manager for the day said.

"Okay, manager~"

*Click.*

After the door closed like that, only the members remained.

"Phew...!"

As if the tension had finally eased, Seon Ahyeon sighed and sank into the sofa.

The other members crowded around him, and congratulations poured in.

"Hey, Ahyeon~"

"WOW!!"

"You did well to stay calm."

Seon Ahyeon seemed taken aback, but he was clearly happy with our reaction.

Even Bae Sejin spoke with an excited face.

"...You taught him a pretty good lesson."

"Yes? I didn't mean to do that, but... D-did it sound like that?"

Seon Ahyeon lowered his head as his face flushed as if it were about to explode. Bae Sejin was a little taken aback.

"Uh, really?"

"Yes...! It was just what I honestly thought... Still, I... I didn't know I was going to say that until I said it..."

Seon Ahyeon let out a small sigh with a relaxed face.

"...It's amazing. I-I thought I'd be scared."

I asked.

"How was it?"

"Better than I thought... It was fine. Yes."

Seon Ahyeon held his hands together and murmured.

"Now that I've actually met him, I realize... He's nobody special."

"......"

Contrary to my concerns, this meeting seemed to have been a kind of awakening for Seon Ahyeon.

He'd experienced firsthand that the guy no longer had any power over him.

"I was... I was okay."

Seon Ahyeon finished speaking as if he were sure.

"...Okay."

"That's it!"

Keun Sejin tapped him on the shoulder. The other members also surrounded Seon Ahyeon, smiling proudly.

Then, Ryu Chungwoo spoke with a smile.

"Come to think of it, didn't Ahyeon barely stutter when he was talking just now? Even without trying?"

"...!"

"I know!"

Had overcoming some of his trauma helped him?

Seon Ahyeon's face lit up.

"R-really? I-I think you might be right... Oh..."

...As soon as he became conscious of it, he started stuttering again.

Then, the members who were looking at each other started shouting in a hurry.

"No, Ahyeon-ah, what's the big deal~ Anyone can stutter when they're relaxed!"

"It doesn't matter at all when it's just us, so relax."

"You're right. Cha Eugene doesn't choose his words very carefully, but he gets along just fine," Kim Raebin said.

"I speak very well!"

"Yes, yes."

Although the conversation had taken a strange turn, Seon Ahyeon bowed his head as if our intentions had gotten through anyway.

"...Yes."

He sounded moved. But right after that, he raised his head and spoke firmly.

"Still... I'll practice more! S-so that I can speak better even without trying!"

"Okay. We'll cheer you on."

The atmosphere in the waiting room couldn't have been warmer.

*That's good.*

I patted Seon Ahyeon on the back. He'd really made us proud.

"Th-thank you, thank you...!"

Seon Ahyeon smiled, looking much more relaxed.

***

The surprising thing was, after that, Seon Ahyeon really started to speak more comfortably and without stuttering at public events.

It seemed that feeling less pressure and letting go of some of his past burdens had helped.

"Th-the therapist said my progress would be like climbing stairs, but I feel like I've gone up a step...!"

"Yes, congratulations."

And everyone watched this week's episode of ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ together with an eager Seon Ahyeon. It was the ranking announcement ceremony.

+=
[And in first place is... Chae Seodam!]
=+

"Oh my."

*What's there to be surprised about? I saw this coming.*

I looked at the screen, unimpressed.

Thanks to the footage of 'Seodam suffering because of his teammates' in the last episode, he'd only gain momentum, not lose it.

+=
[Chae Seodam: Thank you... Thank you so much. I'll work even harder...!]
=+

However, the atmosphere in the living room was not so bad.

"D-do you want some, Moondae?"

"Yes."

"Thank you, Ahyeon~"

Seon Ahyeon looked unconcerned as he handed out dried apple chips to the members.

It probably wouldn't bother him even if Chae Seodam took first place at the end of the show and went on to succeed as an idol.

*Well, it's a good thing.*

I shrugged.

However, I didn't want to move on like this.

And it was that night that the pre-laid bait caught a fish.

– Hello, sunbaenim!

I was referring to Miry-nay, of course.

I entered my room and got straight to the point.

"Hello. Did you call me about what I asked you last time?"

– Yep! That... We were at the team battle filming today and just got home!

Since the ranking announcement was airing now, I'd guessed they'd be busy filming for a few days around this time.

And this phone call... It must mean that there was something on her mind.

The fact that she'd called without hesitation that very day suggested it was serious.

"Did you see something?"

Rank 2 on the other end of the phone... So, Park Minha sighed.

– Well... Hoo. You see...

"Yes. Go on."

– That, the one we talked about last time. I think he was subtly trying to get our phone numbers.

"...!"

*Did I just hit the jackpot?*
