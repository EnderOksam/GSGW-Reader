They hadn't expected him to be this good.

Everyone was amazed by how he had transformed an old children's anime theme song all by himself, especially a magical girl theme song that could easily have become ridiculous.

*Most of his tracks had been co-produced, so people hadn't really focused on just how good Kim Raebin was until now.*

"Raebin, look at this~ They're calling you a genius!" Keun Sejin said.

"I really appreciate the generous praise, but I will keep working harder!"

Kim Raebin answered instantly, like a machine running on autopilot.

Judging by the grave expression on his face, this guy had also seen the reactions on the internet, and it seemed to have made him even more uptight than usual.

*Well, he's always done a good job, that's true.*

Actually, these reactions were well-deserved. Kim Raebin's abilities were practically cheat codes in this industry.

His composing abilities in particular, which weren't displayed as stats, were outstanding. He even had the trait 'Maestro (S)', which made him very fast when hard at work.

He could follow any instructions he was given.

*Isn't he the perfect employee in the eyes of modern society?*

He could be a bit tactless... But even that could be an advantage, since he was diligent and honest.

*Come to think of it, I was pretty lucky, wasn't I?*

I hadn't evaluated each member's long-term potential because I'd been in a hurry to debut back when I was competing on ⟨Idol Inc.⟩, and I had no idea about the crazy future full of consecutive 'status abnormalities' awaiting me.

But after debuting, I was starting to realize how miraculous it was to have found such a talented lineup with such complementary skills on a survival show.

*I thought at least one or two of them would turn out to be duds.*

I shrugged.

Anyway, we'd gone up against T-Holic directly and managed to hold our own, so we'd secured our popularity for the second half of the year.

We just had to maintain that until the year-end awards season.

Ryu Chungwoo smiled and said kindly.

"Come to think of it, our promotions will be over soon. Good work, everyone."

"I know, right?"

Our domestic schedule was nearing its end, with no new ads or events on the horizon.

All that remained were the concert and preparations for the next album. The promotions had been a complete success.

"Now, our Raebin will do a great job arranging our concert tracks~" Keun Sejin said.

"I will do my best to meet your expectations...!"

The mood was good. There were no pressing issues, and everyone was riding the waves of our success after all the great reviews we'd gotten for our TV appearance.

I nodded and flicked through the channels.

*It's just the right time to relax and enjoy.*

All the big things had passed, so I thought it would be okay.

However, one of us wasn't nearly as relaxed or at peace as the rest.

From his point of view, there was still something big looming on the horizon.

Not long after that, we took a few days off after our last domestic event.

"Moondae. By any chance, did you see Raebin...?"

"Hmm."

Seon Ahyeon, who'd come in after knocking softly, asked cautiously.

*But the question was odd.*

He was asking about Kim Raebin's whereabouts. I tore my eyes away from the proposal I was drafting and answered.

"No. Why?"

"Oh, that... H-he hasn't been coming back to our room much lately."

"......"

Even after hearing the explanation, I was still puzzled.

Seon Ahyeon and Kim Raebin were roommates. Both were the type to stay in their room, so they naturally saw each other all the time. It was strange that Raebin hadn't been coming back much.

He wasn't the type to go out often.

"I haven't seen him today either. Why don't we ask him?"

"Ah, I-I think we should. Wait a moment...!"

"No, wait."

*I've already got my phone in my hand. Why go back?*

*What's going on?*

There was a chance he'd left his phone somewhere and was on the dorm's indoor balcony or something... But if he wasn't in the dorm, he'd probably gone out.

*Has anyone ever seen Kim Raebin go out by himself?*

I immediately went out to the living room and asked the others if they had seen Kim Raebin.

Cha Eugene and Keun Sejin weren't in. The only ones left were Ryu Chungwoo and Bae Sejin.

"Raebin? Come to think of it, I don't think I've seen him today."

"......"

He often worked on music at the dorm, too. A bit of tinkering with a track wouldn't have left him looking like this...

*No way.*

The guilt was obvious from the look on his face.

Kim Raebin hesitated but soon answered cautiously.

"I was so engrossed that I didn't realize how much time had passed. That's why I didn't tell anyone where I was. Sorry."

He'd never been good at lying, so he told the truth right away.

Pulling all-nighters wasn't unusual in our line of work, and I figured he'd be able to handle it. What bothered me was that there was no need to do it right now.

"What were you working on?"

"Tracks for the concert and our next album!"

I decided to sound him out.

"You don't need to work so hard right now. We've got time."

There was quite a bit of time until the concert. The time until the next album would be even longer.

"You work fast, so there's no need to stay up all night to—"

"I can’t rely on something like that!"

"...!"

Kim Raebin seemed shocked at himself for cutting me off.

"I-I'm sorry! Well, I might not be as fast this time, so I want to be prepared..."

So he hadn't been driven by some sudden burst of inspiration after all. He felt pressured.

"All right, then. Just don't push yourself too hard."

"Yes..."

Kim Raebin nodded and walked slowly back to his room.

Seon Ahyeon stayed where he was, watching him go.

Worry was written all over his face.

"Does it bother you?"

"Yes."

Seon Ahyeon seemed to have come to a similar conclusion.

"H-h-he seems to feel a lot of pressure... I think I've been like that before."

"Hmm. Right."

Everyone on the internet was calling him a genius, so it was only natural that he'd worry even more about his next projects.

Wasn't he always anxious and on edge right before every album release?

*Still, he's been doing well so far.*

This would pass. As time went by, he'd see the results and regain his confidence.

*Telling him not to worry right now would only give him more reasons to worry.*

For now, I decided to let him do as he liked without interfering too much.

But things were slightly different this time.

A few days later, in a blind test for the concert arrangements... Kim Raebin's submission was disqualified.
