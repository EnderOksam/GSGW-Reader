# Chapter 268 - The Leaked Footage, II

I quickly pieced together a hypothesis.

The production crew behind this TeSTAR documentary were the ones who'd tried to clean up their reputation after the collapse of ⟨Idol Incorporated⟩ Season 2.

They'd clipped the most crucial moments from footage they'd shot in breach of contract, then tried to smuggle it away, causing it to leak in the end.

The more I thought about it, the more indignant I felt.

*I need someone to talk this through with.*

My anger was clouding my mind and affecting my judgment, so I needed to stay objective.

I told the most level-headed member I could think of at the moment.

"...No wonder."

After hearing my reasoning, the amiable look vanished from Lee Sejin's face. He finally seemed to understand what was going on.

"They were so hell-bent on crafting a story. Strange for a documentary."

"Yes."

There had been actual documentary specialists on the team, so we'd mistaken their fervor for passion for making documentaries. That sort of energy belonged in variety shows.

Thanks to that, the TeSTAR documentary was interesting, but after the mess they'd made, they needed to be held responsible.

*We should destroy their careers for this.*

I had no intention of ever seeing these bastards make another variety show, or even an informational show with a variety show twist.

What should I do?

First of all, there was the most convenient and simple way.

"How about telling T1 about all of this?" I said.

"Hmm, didn't the company already know that the production team was from ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ Season 2? They probably entrusted our documentary to them precisely because they worked for T1."

I shook my head.

"That's not what I'm talking about."

"Then?"

"I want to sow doubt about what happened."

A slight change in the company's perspective on the incident would be enough to put them on their guard.

*I'll tell them that the crew tried to sneak away sensitive footage of their artists, possibly to sell it to another company or strike some kind of deal.*

It would raise suspicions of a serious betrayal.

The suspicion that it hadn't been a mistake, but a deliberate attempt to profit.

As evidenced by the industrial espionage case at Gold 1's agency, the larger the company, the angrier it tended to get when it thought someone had made a fool of it.

*Then all I need to do is give them that impression.*

After quickly explaining the matter, I added something else.

"I think they'll get even angrier because they'll feel they've been stabbed in the back by the people they helped."

We had to gently steer T1 into thinking that the people they'd saved after the fiasco of ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ Season 2 had grown greedy and forgotten the favor.

*Well, it wouldn't be a complete lie.*

Then the head office would take care of it, making sure the production crew could never get mainstream work again.

Even if the accusations were false, T1 probably wouldn't care. The doubt alone would be enough to upset and annoy them. It wasn't uncommon for a small studio to simply disappear.

It would be a clean and effective way to ruin the careers of the crew that had betrayed us.

"......"

Lee Sejin rubbed his chin, then smiled wryly.

"To be honest... I think it'll work. Moondae, you're pretty persuasive."

"And? You've got more to say, right?"

"Yes. Well... would that really benefit us right now? It's just revenge."

"......"

Lee Sejin shrugged.

"Well, I think someone like Bae Sejin would be somewhat satisfied, but... If we did this, it'd be up to T1 to take action, and that'd be that."

"...Yes."

I didn't know if Bae Sejin would like it, but the second half was right anyway.

There'd be backlash if we used this method.

*It would end there.*

We wouldn't be able to bring these people up again in an attempt to clear Cha Eugene's name.

The pressure from T1 would of course be seen as unreasonable abuse by the public.

And that overlapped with Cha Eugene's controversy.

Only by handling it quietly behind the scenes could we end their careers without anyone coming to their defense.

And that would be the wise decision.

"......"

*But damn, why did I dislike this method so much?*

Lee Sejin was right.

I slammed my fist against the armrest of the sofa.

"I don't like that," I said.

"Yeah, I knew you wouldn't."

It annoyed me to think those bastards would just tell themselves they'd succumbed to unfair pressure from a large corporation.

Besides, even if I didn't bring it up, T1 probably already suspected the production team.

If they had any brains, wouldn't they have come to the same conclusion already?

*I'd just be stirring the pot, nothing more.*

And... It was true that even if we did all this shit, there'd be no practical benefit to Cha Eugene.

*Damn it.*

I tilted my head back against the sofa. Fuck, I needed to do something to ruin those bastards' lives. I couldn't let it go.

"Whoa, Moondae, do you want me to bring you some ice water?"

"No," I said.

*I need to think.*

Was there any way to help Cha Eugene while completely destroying these bastards?

And I found myself saying something.

"...Can't we frame them?"

Was there any way to make these bastards the internet’s scapegoats instead of Cha Eugene?

Lee Sejin's voice was unreadable.

"Whew, that would be great, but... hmm."

He thought it was unrealistic bullshit.

Right. Revealing that they'd worked on ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ Season 2 would end with a 'so what?' People would just say we were trying too hard to sway public opinion.

I took a deep breath to calm myself.

*Right. I had to put aside wishful thinking and be practical.*

I could ruin the production team's lives whenever I wanted. Now that I knew it was possible, I'd keep that option in reserve.

*And the only thing that would be of some use to Cha Eugene is...*

...I'd thought about it many times, but there was no such option right now.

So I had to think in the longer term.

By the time people got fed up with the controversy over Cha Eugene's abuse of power, we'd need to release something that would reassure his fans and encourage them to stay.

*We need someone who can talk about Cha Eugene's usual behavior.*

All I needed... was a witness.

"We'll have to gather testimonies."

"Yes. We could use them later, if we time it correctly."

"...Okay. For now, that seems to be the best decision."

It was obvious, but if we got the timing right and released the testimonies quickly, there was at least a chance of improving things in the long term.

Lee Sejin smiled wryly. He didn't look entirely happy.

*Maybe I'm just trying to convince myself I've figured this out...*

I clicked my tongue and tossed my smartphone onto the sofa. Lee Sejin patted me on the back.

"Moondaemoondae, you've worked hard. Don't worry too much."

"Okay."

...I needed some beer.
