# Chapter 357 - Muddying the Waters, I

Asking to be put back on a schedule that had already been canceled?

Of course, that wouldn't work.

Not only would it inconvenience a lot of people, but in most cases, circumstances wouldn't allow it anyway.

So, our schedule was light on the day we resumed our activities. A handful of articles came out saying things like 'Managing recovery', reporting that TeSTAR had resumed activities after only two days, and that was all.

The members only had to attend the activities the company had arranged directly for that day, so we had plenty of free time.

"Hm~ I'm sorry and thankful to the company staff," Keun Sejin said.

"Y-yes. Really, you're right..." Seon Ahyeon said.

*Yes.* Even if we paid the cancellation fees and everything else, a problem suddenly cropping up on the artist's side would've been a headache. The company had really helped this time. I was grateful to the staff.

Still, there was something I was anticipating.

*This whole thing probably caused a lot of complaints.*

Keun Sejin had quickly posted on social media, but it was obvious what the people who were already riled up over the 'All-Star Music' incident would say.

*I'll have to prioritize dealing with that.*

As the one who caused the situation, it was essential that I helped deal with it.

I brainstormed a few plans and opened my internet browser.

*Hmm...?*

But to my surprise, it didn't turn out the way I expected.

Instead, the Billboard charts were getting more attention alongside the news of our hiatus.

"Wow... I forgot about this."

"That's right. I think they must have confused the previous movie OST with our current title track," Kim Raebin said.

*Gosh. They're doing their best to be positive about this.*

They could've just said I'd been distracted by the chaos with Seon Ahyeon.

I shook my head and minimized the window.

*First, the album chart, Billboard 200.*

#><8. ⟨WEAPON for you⟩ #gNew!g#><#
$★

8th place.

"Ohhhh."

"S-single digits...?"

The members had learned our ranking belatedly because of the mess and seemed happy, but in fact, it was a fairly predictable result.

Nowadays, it wasn't uncommon for a K-pop boy group that was somewhat well-known in the US to reach fifth place on the Billboard album chart.

*Still, it's a result I really like.*

It was a sign that our chronic lackluster popularity overseas had been dealt with.

But there was something else to consider...

The song chart, the so-called Hot 100.

★$
#><15. Savior #gNew!g#><#
$★

"...?"

"Whoa!"

Our song had landed in 15th place.

*What?*

It might seem strange that I was so surprised when our ranking was lower than on the album chart. But it was several times more difficult for a foreign-language song to break through the ranks on this chart than it was for an English song. It required enough popularity to offset the disadvantage of being in a foreign language, or a ridiculously huge fandom to overcome it.

*So this... is kind of weird.*

*I expected it to land somewhere in the thirties at best...?*

In fact, even the thirties would've been beyond our reach if it hadn't been for the movie OST we released.

*This is on par with our ranking on the Korean weekly music charts when we released a performance-oriented song a few years ago.*

I wasn't sure what to think.

And thanks to this, the Internet's reaction to TeSTAR's hiatus had taken an unexpected turn.

I mean... like this.

★$
– They were about to ride the wave, but their surfboard broke!

– If I were a Shamstar, I'd be so upset right now.

– They're suspending their activities during this time? They must be really sick ㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋ

– Is this some kind of ruse so they can rush back to America?
$★

There was no way anyone sane would suddenly take a break in this situation, so people were convinced we really were sick.

In addition, conspiracy theories sprang up suggesting we wanted to return to promoting in the US as quickly as possible, and all the other nonsensical complaints and opinions were buried.

"......"

The result was better than expected, but I still didn't know what had caused this reaction.

Bae Sejin asked the American among us with a skeptical look, apparently after checking WeTube.

"This... Is it supposed to be this easy?"

However, the local couldn't answer.

"I don't know! I don't look at Billboard often."

"What do you follow?"

"Umm, Dotify~"

"Dotify?"

It was a global music streaming site that was popular in the US.

*Streaming, huh?*

I realized at that moment.

"Ah."

*Yes. I should've checked how the points are distributed first.*

I immediately checked the breakdown of Billboard points.

*Digital music sales, streaming indicators, radio airplay...*

"Hmm."

*We've gotten more radio airplay than I thought.*

Foreign-language songs weren't likely to be played much on American radio, so considering that, we'd gotten quite a few plays. The company seemed to have spent its money well.

Of course, digital music sales, driven by our fandom, were our strongest category, but...

"...!"

*The streaming results aren't bad, either.*

Yes. Our song had received almost as much streaming traffic as the other songs with similar rankings.

*Also, this means...*

"Cha Eugene, do you have a Dotify account?"

"Yes!"

"Let me see it."

I accessed Cha Eugene's smartphone and checked the rankings.

*26th on the global chart.*

Considering that it had been about two weeks since its release, that kind of staying power was impressive.

And if I checked the US charts, not the global...

★$
#><25. Savior by TeSTAR><#
$★

"Oh~ 25th place is good!" Cha Eugene said.

"...!"

*It's higher than on the global chart?*

This wasn't normal, either. Usually, K-pop songs performed better on the global chart than on the US chart...

"Hyung, how much longer are you going to use my phone?"

"Wait a second."

I checked the fan accounts on my smartphone, and kept checking Dotify's streaming rankings on Cha Eugene's phone.

And I realized...

"It's a promotion algorithm."

"...A promotion algorithm? So it's like an ad?"

"Yes. It seems that people who listened to our movie OST were also recommended this song."

The latest TeSTAR title track had been recommended to listeners of 'Black Hole' from ⟨Cosmic Gunner⟩. It seemed that the two songs had been linked through an advertising campaign using the algorithm.

*...It's really effective.*

The songs were composed by the same person and sung by the same group, and even their samples had a similar feel, so of course the second song fit the tastes of people who liked the first. This naturally boosted the promotional effect.

In addition, the image we'd established through our movie cameo and our appearance on the American variety show created a synergistic effect.

*...They're enjoying our alien image: unpredictable, easygoing and completely random.*

So it seemed that even the incomprehensible lyrics became a unique charm of their own, overcoming the reluctance people might usually feel toward a foreign-language song.

*So, all in all...*

"It looks like the company did a good job this time."

"Yes."

Ryu Chungwoo's words couldn't be denied.

I could sense a familiar obsession with getting results in the US market...

There was only one guy who could do this.

"Here. Thanks."

"Yes!"

I returned Cha Eugene's smartphone.

I smacked my lips, tasting something bitter.

*...The general manager actually did a good job?*

It seemed he wasn't obsessed with the US market for nothing. Or had he learned something through trial and error with Miry-nay?

It was a good thing, but it felt strange. Was it because I'd always assumed he was completely useless?

"Moondae, aren't you getting out of the van?" Keun Sejin said.

"Hmm."

I got out of the van at the location for our tour concept photo shoot, setting my thoughts aside.

First of all... I'd concentrate on work. Later, I should set up a Dotify account for monitoring.

Ignoring Cha Eugene's rambling, I made an American account that day.

Our schedule had quickly gotten busy, and we'd just returned to the dorm past midnight, completely exhausted.

Depending on how much the public acknowledged it, it could become a meaningful record or end up as nothing more than lame media play.

In that sense... Seeing the same singer's name twice in the top twenty would be quite an achievement.
