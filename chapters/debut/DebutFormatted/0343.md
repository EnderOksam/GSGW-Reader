# Chapter 343 - Believing and Understanding

Gold 2, Kwon Heeseung.

I liked to keep track of the activities of the group he belonged to, 'Spacer'.

Of course. They were newer on the scene, but they had a similar background and were signed to the same agency as us. It was wise to keep an eye out for any unexpected developments.

Besides, wasn't he someone who knew a little about the future? I had no way of knowing how he might be benefiting from that knowledge.

Of course, that was not the main purpose of keeping an eye on this guy.

*He's a card I could potentially play later.*

I recalled running into him at the agency a few months ago, when he was preparing for his first year-end awards ceremony.

*– Hello, sunbaenim!*

To be precise, I'd checked his status window.

Judging by the timing, his status abnormality should have been close to wearing off, but it still hadn't.

However, the content was different.

★!
@c@Status Abnormality:@c@
#><[Make It Work]><#

Perhaps there is nothing like teamwork for an idol, after all?

Failure guaranteed unless the team releases a collaborative work within the given timeframe.
!★

Instead of the old one, a new status abnormality took its place.

*– After Chae Seodam quit on his own, the atmosphere in the team really improved! At this rate, if we all get along well, we'll succeed at the mission—wait, what? Did we already succeed?*

*– Aaacckkk! There's another one? What else do I have to do? What??*

In other words, he'd already cleared the first status abnormality, but a second had taken its place.

It seemed the system had counted 11 months as a year, which was why he'd been given a second status abnormality.

*...Have the conditions become harsher?*

I wasn't sure about this, so I put it aside.

Anyway, Gold 2 was still holding onto the system, so I didn't have to look for the next person who had it. That wasn't a bad situation for me. It meant less work.

*Here, 'collaborative work' must mean the group has to produce its own music.*

We were in the same agency, so if I got a little involved, clearing it within a year wouldn't be too difficult. Managing him would be easy. That's why I'd kept in touch with him from time to time and kept a close eye on him.

...Or so I thought, until the VTIC leader bastard in front of me sent me the audio file.

"What's wrong? Aren't you going to check?"

"......"

If it wasn't for his dog, I would've made that bastard pay for running his mouth, but I held back for now.

I looked down again at the audio file from the radio show Gold 2 had appeared on.

*This radio program.*

I remembered the show 'Mirae's Future Radio'. It had quite a long tradition, and we'd been on it once when we were rookies.

It was hosted by a radio personality with the stage name 'Mirae', and was famous for its playful skits guessing what would happen 10 years in the future.[1]{T/N: Mirae/미래 also means Future.}

*– Kim Raebin will give me 100 songs!*

*– Cha Eugene, even if 10 years have passed, it would be difficult to give 100 songs to the same person...*

*– Give me 200!*

*– I'm not doing that!*

*– Kids... No, you're 30-year-olds. Calm down already.*

I briefly recalled the hilarious, completely random skit Kim Raebin and Cha Eugene had delivered, then returned to the matter at hand.

I looked down at my smartphone with suspicion.

*Don't tell me this guy also...*

I played the file at the lowest possible volume. I heard the presenter's voice.

@r@**[...Your special friendship has become a hot topic. We hear that Kwon Heeseung-ssi is still friends with VTIC's Cheongryeo and TeSTAR's Park Moondae.]**@r@

*Damn.*

@r@**[We hear you recently went on a trip to celebrate 10 years of friendship. How did you get to know each other?]**@r@

$p
[Yes... It was because we all knew the future.]
p$

@r@**[I'm sorry?]**@r@

$p
[...! Oh, I meant this show, Mirae—Future Radio~ That was what we had in common!]
p$

@r@**[Aha~ Heeseung-ssi, your first appearance on our show 10 years ago must have been very memorable.]**@r@

$p
[Y-yes! Hahaha!]
p$

"......"

It seemed he'd been trying to make a joke about the program's name, playing on the host's name in the moment.

Then he'd realized, 'Hey, come to think of it, that's actually true,' and stiffened in shock.

*This bastard should know better...*

Shouldn't I have taught this bastard a lesson when he called us 'close friends' in that earlier interview?

I held back a sigh.

*...It's no big deal.*

With the crazy busy schedule of a rookie idol, if he'd barely slept, I could perfectly understand him making a mistake like that.

Kwon Heeseung's fans had probably been angry that, on his first radio show, he'd been asked about his friendship with other groups before the interview simply moved on.

However, I could understand why Cheongryeo bothered to tell me this.

I tightened my grip on my smartphone.

*Is this a warning?*

Put nicely, it was a silent word of advice.

A warning about the dangers of sharing my secret with other people.

"I feel like this needs to be taken care of. I could do it myself, but... I don't think Hubaenim would like my methods very much."

The guy smiled.

"He may be in the same industry, but he doesn't seem like the careful sort. Hm... I thought I'd give you a heads-up. What do you think?"

"......"

"People are always the hardest to manage."

That didn't just mean Gold 2.

It must have included the fact that I'd confided my secret to the other members of my group, too.

"Park Moondae."

Just then, Keun Sejin spoke up. He seemed to have guessed what was going on and muttered with a rigid expression.

"Hey... Is Kwon Heeseung also in on the secret?"

"Hmm?"

But before I could react, someone else did.

Cheongryeo.

He looked back and forth between me and Keun Sejin with a slightly puzzled expression, then nodded as if he'd understood something.

"Oh, so he didn't know. Sorry."

"......"

"I thought you'd told them everything because you said you'd talked about me too. I guess you didn't go that far."

Then he turned away, looking somewhat impressed.

"You know where to draw the line, Hubaenim."

*This bastard.*

I refrained from swearing and responded calmly.

"If you were so worried that I'd bring disaster down on our heads, why didn't you tell me sooner?"

It seemed he was dissatisfied that I'd explained this absurd, completely unrealistic situation to Keun Sejin.

However, Cheongryeo simply tilted his head to the side.

"Hmm... There seems to be a misunderstanding."

*A misunderstanding?*

"Hubaenim, I have nothing to lose from you doing this."

"...!"

"I've got more than a few solid excuses I can use if I get dragged into controversies like this."

Cheongryeo smiled brightly.

"I've already tested exactly what wording and tone are convincing. I'm past the point where somebody else's mistake can cause me any damage."

"......"

"You didn't really think this was the first time I'd experienced something like this, did you? You're smarter than that."

I looked at him.

"So you've told somebody else about this too?"

"Of course. I thought it might be useful. But it turned out not to be useful enough, so I never did it again."

Cheongryeo seemed lost in reminiscence for a moment, staring into space, but his eyes soon came back into focus.

"So I'm saying this for your sake, Hubaenim, not mine. I told you, didn't I? I mean it as an apology."

*Fuck.*

I rubbed my brow.

What pissed me off the most was that he seemed to be serious.

"Is that so? Then listen. First of all, Gold 2... I'll meet with him soon and get him back in line."

I looked at Keun Sejin, who'd tactfully been listening in silence, and spoke.

"You don't need to worry about him. He's not the sort of person who'll talk about this."

"...Of course not."

Keun Sejin held out his fist.

*Does he really have to do this in this situation?*

Still, it would've been even sillier not to respond, so I bumped my fist against his.

However, Cheongryeo didn't seem impressed.

"Yes. Well... Hubaenim, he probably told you he'd believe you. They usually do."

*What?*

"But there seems to be some misunderstanding here as well... 'Believe' does not mean 'understand.'"

"...!"

Cheongryeo spoke without hesitation, as if quoting someone else's words directly.

"Because you're close, because you've known each other for a long time, because you're in the same group... They'll cite all sorts of reasons for 'believing' you. It's understandable. If they don't believe you, it means one of their members is mentally ill. They don't want to believe that."

"......"

I listened.

"But that's about it. They won't be cooperative after that, so... I gave up trying. It became more of a bother than anything else."

"......"

The guy continued smoothly.

"Unless you've had the same experience, you can't expect people to empathize with a situation as unrealistic as ours. So, Hubaenim, don't expect too much."

"......"

*Is this why he's always acted like that?*

I'd gotten the impression that this guy didn't feel any personal connection even with the members he'd known for so long. Maybe this was part of the reason.

*It's almost like... he's not entirely human.*

It was a rather chilling way of thinking, even more so with Keun Sejin standing right in front of him, listening.

"Hmm."

I crossed my arms and made to speak.

It was then.

A honeyed voice cut in from beside me.

"But, sunbaenim, I am *very* cooperative with Moondae."

"...!"

It was Keun Sejin. What was with this guy now?

"Aha, is that so?"

"Sure. Ayyy~ People can't all share the same experiences. We're friends. Our similarities and differences make our friendship richer."

"If you say so."

"Yes. It can't be helped if you don't really understand what I mean! Everyone's situation is different. I really don't mind~"

It was amazing how he could say, in such a roundabout way and with a straight face, 'You might not know because you don't have friends.'

The problem was that he was saying it to Cheongryeo, who had a tendency to revert to his unhinged past actions.

*I hope this doesn't push him over the edge.*

I watched Cheongryeo stare at Keun Sejin without expression.

"......"

"......"

*No, Keun Sejin, why are you smiling and glaring at him at the same time?* I sighed and decided to end the conversation.

"Okay. Well... Everyone's entitled to their own opinion. Anyway, thanks for the audio file, and your dog... I hope it gets better."

"All right. If you ever have something to talk about, contact me anytime, Hubaenim."

"Yes~"

Keun Sejin smoothly answered in my place. *What are you, the right-hand man of some Mafia boss?*

*The tension in the air is no joke right now.*

At that moment, the front door opened at just the right time.

*Ddeering.*

To be honest, I welcomed the interruption.

And the person responsible for it... was Seon Ahyeon, looking slightly nervous.

"Th-there... I-it's already past the start time for our camp meeting."

"...!"

"Oh, sorry. So you were working."

Cheongryeo smiled and nodded his head slightly.

"Then, I'll be on my way."

"Okay."

"Yes, yes!"

It seemed the conversation was finally coming to an end. *Go home already.*

However, instead of turning his body right away, Cheongryeo hesitated a little before adding his words.

"I'll call you when Kongi comes back."

"...Okay."

I nodded as he put on his mask. Despite everything, he just wanted his dog to be safe.

Cheongryeo turned away, got into the elevator, and disappeared.

"Let's go in."

"Yes."

We finally went back into the dorm.

Keun Sejin took the lead toward the living room, checking whether Seon Ahyeon was listening. He whispered to me quietly so Ahyeon wouldn't hear.

"Wow, that sunbaenim really has an irritating way of talking. Hoo, Moondaemoondae. How have you put up with him all this time?"

"......"

It was embarrassing that I'd almost shouted 'That's right!' in agreement.

"Well, I managed."

"Hm~ yes. Park Moondae was pretty sharp with him at times, so I guess that helped relieve some stress?"

"......"

Come to think of it, I'd slipped into the habit of not being particularly polite to him without even realizing it.

The guy chuckled and slapped me on the back, so I turned to him and opened my mouth.

First, I brought up what he must have been curious about after that conversation.

"About Kwon Heeseung..."

"Ah~ I know. You couldn't tell me because it wasn't your secret to tell. I understood that right away~"

From the context, he'd already understood that Kwon Heeseung was my successor, and Lee Sejin didn't seem particularly dissatisfied that I'd kept it a secret.

"But since I know now, should I warn Heeseung for you? I'm good at things like this~"

"Let's wait first."

I hadn't wanted to involve him at all, but at this rate, I'd even end up sharing my goal of destroying the system with him. I sighed and returned to the living room.

"Has that bastard left?"

"Yes. He left."

Keun Sejin answered cheerily.

"Ugh~ It was disgusting how he pretended to be such a close friend of Moondae's~"

*He's a natural liar.*

"...! And you just let him?" Bae Sejin said.

"Of course not. I was quarreling with him the entire time."

"...Okay. Then next time, I can help..."

"Hyung, no. If you do it, it'll turn into a real fight."

"Hey!"

I returned to my seat, leaving the two Sejins to duke it out in comfort.

Then Seon Ahyeon, who was sitting next to my seat, spoke up.

"Moo-Moondae. By any chance, did something happen...? The conversation seems to have taken longer than I thought..."

"...Hmm."

I debated for a moment, but decided to tell him the truth, for the most part.

"It's no big deal. We just had some differences to sort out."

"Ah..."

Seon Ahyeon hesitated, then visibly forced himself to speak again.

"Wh-what differences...?"

"......"

I was silent for a long moment.

"A-actually, never mind. Sorry...!"

It was nothing to be sorry about, but his habit of apologizing hadn't abated. I held back a sigh.

*...Is this the second time?*

The second time Seon Ahyeon had inadvertently asked me to tell him what was going on, that is.

He wasn't asking me outright if I had a secret, but it still made me uncomfortable. He seemed to have guessed that Keun Sejin had already heard about it.

I shut my mouth instead of saying, 'No need to be sorry. It was a bit personal.' Somehow, I thought that would backfire.

*Seon Ahyeon doesn't look upset right now, so maybe I'll wait this one out.*

It seemed the situation was over for the moment.

But that evening...

Seon Ahyeon's attitude toward participating in the camp suddenly changed.

Was it on the bad side? No.

He became far more eager than I'd ever seen him be.
