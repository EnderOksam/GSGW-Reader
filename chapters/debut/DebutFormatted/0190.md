# Chapter 190 - ⟨TeSTAR's Working Holiday⟩, I

Of course, I had guessed that the new idol group selected by season 4 of ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ would come to the agency.

It was just that things were getting a little more interesting now.

"Naturally, I thought they would hire a new person in charge for that part."

TeSTAR was only in its second year, yet the agency was already trying to take staff away from our team.

For the sake of a group that hadn’t even been properly evaluated yet.

"Oh~ We're getting juniors, too! Ah, but instead of hiring new ones, our employees are going there? Aren't you getting too much work?"

"They'll hire new ones, too! But it seems they're trying to distribute some of the experienced people. Don't worry, hyung isn't going~ They'll send off the second manager!"

"Haha~"

Keun Sejin laughed brightly at the manager's joking remarks, but he must have roughly guessed the atmosphere since he had experience with big and small agencies.

*The director had switched his backing to the junior group.*

To be exact, he was fed up with TeSTAR's fans.

With two predecessors replaced so quickly, he probably assumed TeSTAR's fans were far too sensitive.

In fact, TeSTAR's fans were already watching the agency closely.

*And the members even oppose his vision.*

That American disease.

The new group had also been through that crazy ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ survival show and had formed enough fandom, so it wasn't strange that he turned the central manpower to that side and dreamed of a new vision.

"Our agency's doing well lately! The new hires will be great. Don't worry too much!"

"Yes, yes!"

What do I think?

*It's a great profit.*

It was great.

The company wouldn't want to pick a fight with the fans again, so they would take care of it properly.

In that case, this situation where the director could only pull capital investment without interfering was the best.

After all, we could plan on our own.

*So the approval bot is basically back.*

However, there was one thing that bothered me.

They might stop investing properly in our new albums and make us focus only on touring.

*He might start treating us like cash cows.*

There was a good chance that the director would go crazy when he saw the sales volume once he checked the profits after this tour ended.

With another group to develop, he might exploit TeSTAR as an easy source of cash. I couldn't let that happen.

So what are the countermeasures?

It's simple.

*We just need to make sure that they can't give up their capital investment in TeSTAR.*

Three and a half years until the end of the contract.

In the meantime, we had to steadily keep growing.

It checked both boxes: the financial incentive (“because they’re bigger, we can increase the scale of their tour”) and the prestige (“the artist under me has become so successful”).

*Looking at the personality of the director this time, he won't be able to give it up.*

Therefore, TeSTAR's external atmosphere was important during this tour.

It was the first long hiatus. We should manage our popularity beyond showing our faces at the awards ceremonies.

However, with the already tight schedule, we couldn't put in additional activities in Korea.

Then there was only one answer.

*We'll have to present the reality show better than planned.*

We'd have to get more content out of the schedules we already had.

*I'll have to be proactive.*

It was the first time I made this resolution since ⟨Idol Inc.⟩, so let's grab the content and the personalities in a more provocative way.

"Will we get to meet the new idols?"

"It will be difficult right now."

I could hear Ryu Chungwoo answering Cha Eugene's words nearby.

And he was right.

The departure for the tour was tomorrow.
