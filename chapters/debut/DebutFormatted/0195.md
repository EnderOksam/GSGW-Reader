# Chapter 195 - From Japan to San Diego

When someone said something like 'I don't know' in today's Grand Prize award speech, of course, the concert hall froze.

It wasn’t even a tone that came from emotion or embarrassment; it was just a soft and mysterious tone.

*This bastard can't be going crazy and talking nonsense, right?*

A chill ran down my spine. I was sure he knew a recording proving he'd tried to kill me would appear online if he even mentioned me.

Cheongryeo continued.

**[I really don't. At one point I was so desperate to reach this place that I cried every night, unable to sleep.]**

Even if the members next to him were frozen, Cheongryeo's slow words continued.

**[And after the time that felt like it would never come, I finally reached this far.]**

Cheongryeo tilted his head.

**[I still don't know what that desperation taught me. But I feel somehow different inside now.]**

The audience held its breath. Cheongryeo smiled.

**[Thank you. For not letting me give up.]**

That was how the speech ended.

Belatedly, normal applause started pouring out from the people who realized that it had a proper heartwarming and emotional ending.

Fans had been cheering enthusiastically from the beginning.

They probably linked his speech to the incident involving the member they'd expelled.

Probably... That feeling actually came from other experiences.

"..."

I sat down quietly, following the others.

The VTIC members wiped their eyes and awkwardly patted Cheongryeo's back. They'd probably never had to comfort him before.

Still, with the title song of the Grand Prize album playing, VTIC stood on stage until the song was over.

Then, after the camera light was turned off for a while, they slowly came down from the stage.
