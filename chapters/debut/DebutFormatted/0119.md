# Chapter 119 - KBC Song Festival, I

I couldn't figure out what the hell that crazy guy was thinking.

He posted shit on SNS, and only a few days later, this nonsense happened again.

*He's calling me while I'm working?*

I was having my makeup done at the salon for a schedule later that evening.

I saw Kim Raebin, who was getting his hair dried next to me, widen his eyes. It was clear that he had read through the shape of the manager's mouth.

It was a place full of staff, so I felt even more reluctant.

*How do I know what this bastard is going to say?*

The hairstylist who was working on my hair casually spoke up.

"Take it, take it~ It's okay. I'm almost done."

Maybe they thought I was hesitating because I was afraid it would get in the way of getting my hair done

*I don't want to take it, but...*

I held back my sigh. Then I told the manager.

"...Can you tell him I'll contact him later?"

"Uh, he said you don't know his number? He changed his number and called you this way. Moondae, you don't answer numbers you don't know."

"..."

It was obvious why he had to call through the company when he could just text.

*He knows I blocked him.*

There was nothing I could do if he went this far.

It was also unpleasant to keep asking the manager to do something.

Let's finish it quickly.

"Yes, okay."

I got the smartphone from the manager and put it to my ear.

"Hello. This is Park Moondae."

"Ah, finally."

I heard a voice filled with laughter.

Do I have to listen to that thing's laugh?

"I heard you've changed your number. I'll save this number."

"Yes. Go ahead and do that. Congratulations on the Rookie Award."

My hand holding the handle of the chair automatically tightened.

"...The awards ceremony is not over yet, so we don't know. Then..."

As soon as I was about to finish my words, Cheongryeo replied without giving me a break.

"It's practically confirmed. Well, aren't you wondering what's going to happen next?"

I'm not curious, you bastard.

I didn't think I would get proper information even if I interacted with this guy anyway.

He backstabbed me, and now he was trying to hook me in.

"Not really. Anyway, I'll save the number. I hope you safely finish your year-end activities, sunbae-nim."

It meant that I would hang up now.

"...Ah, it turns out like this."

Then, suddenly, his voice changed to a slightly awkward tone.

"I think there's been a misunderstanding. I don't just go around hurting my juniors. There are lines I won't cross."

*Lines you won't cross, my ass.*

"Is that so? I understand."

"We'll continue to meet during the year-end activities... Hmm, maybe I should explain it at that time. It's kind of hard to do it over the phone."

"That sounds great. Sorry, but I have something to attend to, so I'll hang up now."

Cheongryeo replied jokingly.

"Okay. Don't accidentally block it this time."

"Yes. Well then."

I quickly hung up the phone. If it was at the dorm, I would have said something, but I held back because I was at the salon.

Then I returned the phone to the manager.

"Here, hyung. Thank you."

"Okay~ Ah, I'll text you this person's number."

"..."

I couldn't curse out the kindness of the manager who didn't know the whole story. I held back my sigh and closed my eyes as I leaned against the chair.

*It's troublesome.*

At first, I wondered if I could take some kind of benefit, but this bastard was the worst kind of troll.

Besides, he seemed like a real crazy guy, I couldn't predict his sudden change of attitude. *As expected, stop loss[1]{T/N: A stop loss is an action taken to limit investment losses.} is the answer.*

Finish it quietly without provoking him.

After two years, the future I knew was over anyway. Then I would be of no use to him, so he would naturally drift away.

Until then, it was enough that I didn't get involved and avoided the situation.

If he valued his reputation, he wouldn't be able to approach me so openly again once TeSTAR became a top-tier group.

Aside from being insane, he seemed quite obsessed with his status as an idol.

*Then we just need to stop building bad feelings like wanting to kill each other.*

I'd wanted to make him pay when I was furious, but once things settled down, it became clear how risky that would be.

*I don't have to risk my life just to kill a troll.*

I kept this as a backup plan just in case. I cleared my thoughts and opened my eyes.

And Kim Raebin, whose eyes were shining next to me, spoke up.

"...What about the program he uses for arranging music?"

"It's confidential."

"I see..."

I'm sorry, but I don't think the day will come when I ask that.
