# Chapter 232 - Daydream, IV

The crazy plan to bring in a new member to VTIC started as fresh bullshit.

"First, Gunwoo, you'll do a solo pre-debut[1]{T/N: pre-debut: the period when a trainee releases a public song/promotes it for the first time.}."

"......"

Why was he suddenly talking about going solo?

"If nothing unusual happens this year, you'll easily sweep the Rookie of the Year awards for male solo artists. You won't have much competition."

*Easy enough for you to say.*

"Didn't this agency say that it would not support solo activities?"

"I think a pre-debut is worth pushing for."

Cheongryeo calmly gave a positive answer.

*Even if it's only a pre-debut, how can he be so sure?*

As expected, the fact that this agency excluded solo artists to an extreme level was due to this guy's influence.

I crossed my arms.

"Even if I debuted like that, where is the guarantee that I'd be successful as a male soloist?"

It had been a long time since a male idol without a group of his own performed as a soloist and achieved good results.

But Cheongryeo laughed.

"Ah, not a performance-oriented idol, but a vocal-oriented singer."

"...!"

"You're LeTi's first new artist since VTIC. People will notice you. Since you won't be debuting strictly as an idol, you can avoid some of the scrutiny idols face."

That was what he said.

Besides being a dance-type solo singer like an idol, a traditional male soloist aiming for the music itself, a ballad or an indie song... The batting average was fine.

But that was only if the song was good.

"And early next year, you will release a collaboration song with the VTIC unit. It would be nice if it was the kind that showed some kind of performance."

"......"

Appealing to the performance aspect was possible, but it also weaved me together with VTIC.

"There are many cases of collaboration songs within the same agency, so even if there is a backlash, it would be at a level that would pass by. If the results are good and your role is made clear, the fans' disgust will soften."

At this point, I knew what he was trying to say.

I opened my mouth.

"...And when your main vocalist gets exposed... you'll replace him with me?"

"You are smart. That's right."

It meant that Ryu Gunwoo would be put in as a substitute for VTIC's main vocalist in the most natural way possible.

*It sounds crazy.*

It was natural.

The reason I told him about the main vocal case was that it was the most valuable information he was thirsting for.

*He'll be exposed in a year or two, but he's already acting up.*

It would be easy enough to check what he was doing now and use that to prove my knowledge of the future.

But this bastard had no intention of preventing the scandal. He was already thinking of replacing the member with me.

I furrowed my eyebrows.

"I don't know why you're suggesting that when you can prevent it from happening."

The guy's expression disappeared.

"People can't be changed."

"......"

"Remember that. It'll come in handy."

*He treated a member of his own group like a disposable part. He really was out of his mind...*

Let's move on.

In other words, even if the group suffered a blow, he would not leave any seeds of conflict that he couldn't control.

And instead, he said he would add a guy who listened well and was desperate, and a guy who needed to be monitored because he was useful.

"So, are you going to try it?"

Me?

*Who would ever have guessed it?*

*I might actually end up in the same group as this bastard.*

I opened my mouth.

"No. I will decline."

"......"

I couldn't shake the feeling that I'd end up dead if I went through with this plan.

And wouldn't it be like completely handing over the hilt to him?

*He can promise me whatever he wants now, then leave me stuck as a solo artist if things change.*

Besides, if it was just a dream, all my actions right now were just trying to find a hint to wake up from the coma. I didn't need to be adventurous.

*It's as similar to reality as possible.*

...Bae Sejin, who couldn't be persuaded, would have another chance to be recruited if the ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ program collapsed.

I clasped my hands.

However, there was no need to fight with this guy, so I started with a very plausible excuse.

"I'm not sure if the solo rookie award part will fit into the mission. If they give you the idol group rookie award, I would die just like that."

"Aha."

I thought this would work. Cheongryeo crossed his arms and smiled cheerfully.

"Haha, then next time we can try another method. You still lack a sense of challenge."

"......"

With this bastard... there was no use talking for a long time.

Afterward, Cheongryeo did not immediately withdraw the proposal. He left the room after saying, 'I will give you a month.'

*It sounds like he's pretty sure I'll give up within a month.*

He was an awful bastard. I would rather be a guy in real life who sent pictures of dogs when he was bored.

I held my breath and got up.

*Let's do the next thing.*

Now that I had laid out the groundwork, I would have to bring out the reclusive guy.

...Seon Ahyeon of this dream.

* * *

The hospital room was quiet.

Only the medical machines and the white noise from people moving across the corridor gently enveloped the single-person ward.

He'd been given a private room solely because he was a public figure.

He still hadn't regained consciousness.

"......"

Seon Ahyeon quietly sat in the caregiver's chair.

Park Moondae had no relatives. Even after TeSTAR became very successful, no one claimed to be a distant relative.

Apart from the assigned caregivers, only those he'd met since becoming an idol came to visit him.

That included the members who had recovered from the shock and an occasional staff member from the agency.

However, numerous gifts from fans were still undergoing scrutiny. Thanks to that, the hospital room was empty.

"......"

Seon Ahyeon put a fluffy doll crocheted on the table. He knitted it himself.

The hand that let go of the doll trembled.

*Let's calm down...*

Seon Ahyeon held his breath.

He was able to visit this room the most often among the other members and take care of him.

Because Seon Ahyeon... Because he was barely hurt.

Unlike the other members who couldn't be there as often for reasons such as fractures or members who were busy fighting with the company, he was able to move from the beginning.

And even though he didn't have to, he felt a burning guilt over it.

*I couldn't say a word...*

When the other members somehow persuaded or reported the former manager, he couldn't bear to say anything.

It wasn't that he was speechless with fear.

It was because he was worried that he, who couldn't speak properly, would get in the way.

"......"

Seon Ahyeon quietly gathered his hands together and placed them on his lap, and sat still in the chair.

He didn't expect the younger members to have intervened, since they were younger than him.

But two members his own age had tried to persuade the manager, while he'd done nothing.

It was the same after the accident.

No matter how brilliant or brave an idea was, lack of speech meant that he needed someone else to deal with public opinion and the company.

*Even if I get angry with the company, it doesn't seem to work properly...*

There was nothing else.

However, it was not that he suddenly had medical knowledge and could help with the treatment of Park Moondae.

All he'd done was guide the members toward the ambulance after the 119 emergency call, since he was uninjured.

However, he was half out of his mind.

Seon Ahyeon clenched his fists.

*I feel so stupid.*

So he was really useless. He felt that way, at least.

However, because of the worries of a bigger reality, his self-loathing was pushed out of priority.

Park Moondae... He had been unconscious for five days.

Even though he heard that the operation was quite successful, it seemed that it was because the shock was so great and there was a lot of blood loss.

*I should have sat in the back seat then...*

That would have been a much better result. Then Moondae would've been less injured...

"......"

Seon Ahyeon wiped his face.

He didn't think he had the right to cry.

If he had time for tears, he should pray instead.

*Healthily... I hope nothing happens, and that there are no aftereffects.*

He wished that he would come back soon.

* * *

"Wow, seeing you again like this~ Nice to meet you!"

"H-he-hello..."

"......"

Lee Sejin was so friendly it almost seemed false, while Seon Ahyeon couldn't even raise his head.

I closed my mouth and watched them.

I had a headache.

*They're still so formal with each other.*

They'd both earned gold grade, yet Sejin clearly hadn't tried to get close to Ahyeon on the show.

*Ahyeon was good-looking enough that someone should have tried being friendly with him.*

It also meant that there was something wrong with Seon Ahyeon's performance quality and broadcast editing.

Actually, I saw the broadcast, so I knew what happened.

*...He was kept in check too much.*

The other contestants had forced him into a part that didn't suit him in the first team battle and tormented him.

The editing only made it worse.

Seon Ahyeon's team members also received all kinds of curses, but the public opinion that they were 'worthy of being beaten' was formidable.

Thanks to that, comments like "A kid like that shouldn't be on a survival show" got the most likes and came up. It made me sick.

And Seon Ahyeon here... He was directly affected by the negative votes and was eliminated at the last minute.

I pressed my temples.

"......"

*Since he's out, I...*

I already knew this guy's house, so it wasn't hard to go and talk to him.

I visited while he was packing to move out, brought him with me, and persuaded his parents by introducing myself as a LeTi trainee.

They were so worried about Ahyeon that they trusted the agency's reputation for caring for its artists.

Of course, persuasion was attached as a condition.

@r@He wouldn't debut right away. I thought he could join me as a trainee.@r@

– Thank you for saying that. But we won't force Ahyeon if he doesn't want to.

@r@May I talk with him alone for a moment?@r@

I was immediately allowed to speak with Ahyeon privately in his room.

@r@At the agency... They said that you have to be included in the group for us to debut.@r@

"..."

@r@I was wondering if you had any plans to come out for practice. I think you will do well.@r@

Seon Ahyeon did not respond and kept his head down, but in the end he nodded.

He looked very unstable, but... If he ate well and slept well, he'd be much better.

*If he can debut properly through practice, his mentality will be fine.*

This was because Seon Ahyeon was a person with good resilience even though his mentality looked broken at times like this.

*...I've seen it many times.*

*All he needed was a little encouragement. That was my objective assessment, anyway.*

And after a while...

"Ahyeon, it'll only take a moment."

"......"

Seon Ahyeon left with his parents to meet the agency staff, his head bowed.

Lee Sejin turned to me and asked quietly.

"Is that friend also included in this group?"

"I guess so."

"Oh~ that's right. I heard earlier that you persuaded him yourself. Is that true?"

"......"

"Hey, you're good at it~"

Lee Sejin sat down next to me. I held back a snort.

*It looks like Sejin has recovered a little, at least.*

His manner was almost the same as when we'd first met during ⟨Idol Inc.⟩.

"......"

Lee Sejin, who was sitting next to me, spoke in a sociable tone.

"Hmm... Do you have any other friends in mind to work with us?"

"...Why is that?"

I wondered if he was curious about the other members. However, Lee Sejin shook his hand and replied lightly.

"Uh? Well, that new friend might leave~ Just in case!"

"...!"

He was sure that Seon Ahyeon wouldn't join us... I felt a strange nuance.

I immediately asked him.

"Why do you think so?"

"Hm? He came here because he was afraid of you."

"...!!"

"He pretended, but... Oh, wasn't it meant to scare and motivate him a bit? Haha, sorry~"

"......"

I... did it look like that?

*It can't be.*

I persuaded in the most moderate way possible. He wasn't being forced.

*Just saying that I need Seon Ahyeon...*

At that moment, Seon Ahyeon's condition came to mind.

A state in which his self-esteem hit the floor and his mentality was smashed.

And the words I used.

– At the agency... They said that you have to be included in the group for us to debut.

...If we couldn't make our debut because he didn't come, it might have sounded like it'd be his fault...

That was why Seon Ahyeon didn't respond and came with me.

"Well, anyway, you don't know who's coming, do you? Okay~"

"......"

Lee Sejin finished the conversation appropriately and walked out of the practice room while fiddling with his smartphone.

I thought about it.

Was the Seon Ahyeon I knew like that?

Was the Lee Sejin I knew the one who said these things?

*...No.*

They weren't.

All at once, my train of thought began to fracture.

Standing alone, I looked back on the course of action I had taken.

*It isn't adding up.*

Why did I start doing all of this just to debut as if it were reality?

Because I was looking for a clue to wake up from the coma?

Where was the guarantee that I would find one? There was no definite reason why it would go the same as in reality.

*It was just my guess.*

Then, the reason why I tried to make this assumption somehow with certainty...

*Fuck. I'd only done it because I wanted to.*

I wanted to relive the life I'd had as Park Moondae.

"Hoo."

My breathing quickened, and I sank to the floor of the practice room.

Then I remembered the message on the status window.

*- Enjoy your daydream :)*

*Is this why it was a daydream?*

Daydreaming meant empty dreaming.

...So, this was my desire.

It seemed that I wanted to take the life I lived as Park Moondae. And make it mine.

But I already knew the answer.

This was what I said to Cheongryeo directly, with my own mouth.

*– Even if I started over and built the same team, it would never be the same as now. Because the events and stories shared would be different.*

This was impossible.

It could never be the same...

So, why did I justify this shit?

"......"

I put my head on the giant mirror and thought to myself.

And finally admitted.

*It's probably because the past two and a half years have been my life as Park Moondae.*

This was because all choices, thoughts, and experiences were mine, even though they were experienced through the body of Park Moondae.

Because my mind experienced it firsthand. That couldn't be changed.

Even if all this shit was over and I went back to my original body, Ryu Gunwoo... This was all my experience, and it wouldn't change.

Now I understood.

"Hoo."

I lifted my head against the mirror.

The mirror was fogged up, and I couldn't see my face.

"......"

Even though what I should have seen was the face of 'Ryu Gunwoo', this dream was not my life.

*Enough.*

I had been here too long. Now, I wanted to go back to reality.

To 'my reality' in which I lived.

That was the moment.

*Bing!*

"...!!"
