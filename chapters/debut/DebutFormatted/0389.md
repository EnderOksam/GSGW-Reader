# Chapter 389 - A Familiar Stage

I already knew how Seon Ahyeon lived here.

*I'd seen it. There was plenty of information.*

Articles.

Interviews, magazines, WeTube...

Just entering 'Seon' in the search box brought up Seon Ahyeon's name in the autocomplete suggestions, no different from reality.

However, the related search keywords were different.

...Because Seon Ahyeon here didn't quit ballet.

★$
– **[Seon Ahyeon wins the Prix de Lausanne... The future is bright for Korean ballet]**

– **[A K-syndrome for ballet, too? “I did my best,” Seon Ahyeon says]**
$★

After winning a prize at an international competition that only teenagers participated in, he distinguished himself in various competitions and joined an overseas ballet company.

Because he was so good-looking, he became famous in Korea as soon as the WeTube algorithm picked him up, and he even filmed a few commercials after appearing on a talk show.

So, I thought there would be no chance of meeting him until after I debuted as Ryu Gunwoo.

The person in question suddenly jumped out and started talking.

Without stuttering even once, his tone was much more refined than that of the guy I knew.

"Would you like some coffee instead?"

"...No."

It felt like I'd been slapped in the face. *Fuck.*

I finished the drink and mumbled.

"Thank you. It was a good drink."

*So please go away.*

“Okay...”

However, Seon Ahyeon did not leave and sat quietly next to me.

*What is he doing?*

I reflexively glanced at him. He was wearing a casual black polo, and his hair hadn't been styled.

If he'd come to the filming set as an idol, he wouldn't have looked this unpolished.

"......"

I had wondered why Seon Ahyeon wasn't an idol here.

I knew one major difference between his life here and in reality.

#*⌕ Sehwa Arts Middle School*#

#b**_Graduation List_**b#

*There was no Chae Seodam among his classmates.*

Seon Ahyeon here hadn't met Chae Seodam in middle school because he was a different age.

So he graduated without any trouble, continued to study ballet, and eventually made it his career.

There had been nothing to derail him from that path.

– *A-actually... I couldn't continue ballet... My body wasn't suited for it.*

This guy had previously said he quit because of issues with his feet and pelvis, but it seemed that the real reason had been entirely psychological.

Even though his physique hadn't changed, I saw that he was still doing ballet right now.

*...That's why I didn't even bother to contact him.*

In fact, there was no way to meet him without doing something extremely unnatural, and even if I did, what could I say?

He'd already established a career in a completely different field at the same age he was in reality.

...Even if I awakened him, all I'd do was confuse him.

*Just... I was planning on letting him live his own life and dealing with this myself.*

I folded the now-empty paper cup and put it down without saying a word.

Then, suddenly a voice interrupted me.

“I'm sorry if I interrupted your break.”

“You didn't.”

I needed an excuse.

"I was a little surprised. I didn't expect to meet someone like you in a place like this, Seon Ahyeon... sunsoonim[1]{T/N: Sunsoonim translates to athlete and is a respectful way to address athletes! It is similar to sunbaenim.}."

I'd nearly addressed him too casually, but caught myself at the last moment. He wasn't even an athlete, but I'd addressed him as though he were on a national team. It didn't matter.

"Thank you for your concern."

"Ah... Yes."

He smiled faintly and opened his mouth as if to say something, but his smartphone vibrated in his pocket.

*Bzz.*

Then his expression changed, and he looked a little troubled.

"If it's okay, can you keep the fact that you met me here a secret? I made a mistake."

"Sure."

"Thank you. Ah—"

For some reason, Seon Ahyeon picked up the paper cup I'd placed on the floor and quickly ran down the hallway while answering the phone.

Even then, he looked back and nodded to me. It seemed he was still the same nice guy on the inside.

**[Why is Seon Ahyeon here...]**

I held back a sigh.

*It's obvious.*

We met in the middle of the hallway on the set of a program being prepared, and he asked me not to tell anyone I'd seen him?

It meant he would be appearing on the program.

Not as a private consultant giving choreography advice, but as an actual special guest.

*For example... a judge.*

"The theme of this next performance is 'art.'"

Filming started again.

On stage, the president was excitedly holding the microphone and speaking.

He gave a long explanation about how singers could give special performances through various collaborations and commemorative stages, and how they needed to express themselves through performances with deeper meaning.

– *We've recruited special guests from the arts! Make use of them!*

*I guess Seon Ahyeon is included there.*

And as expected, before even announcing the randomly formed teams, the president introduced them with the name 'special judges.'

Artists ranging from a pop art master to an orchestra conductor came in.

"This is the ballerino, Seon Ahyeon."

"Wow!"

I wasn't sure if it was a good idea to cast someone like him on a show meant to create a male idol group, since his profession invited comparisons, but the president seemed excited anyway.

Cha Eugene slapped me on the back.

"Hyung, did you see him??"

"Yes."

*I had eyes, didn't I?*

Fortunately, Cha Eugene's fuss didn't seem to draw any attention. Anyway, the lineup was too impressive for an in-house agency survival show.

*Did the show attract more investors because it was doing well?*

There was only one person who knew.

I looked back at Cheongryeo, but of course, he was wearing his usual mask of calmness for the cameras.

And when our eyes met, he grinned as if he were excited about the special judges.

*Is he even making sure they get a reaction shot?*

He really was impressive in more ways than one.

*Of course, I can read him in other ways, too.*

I opened the status window and brought up the Companions List.

*Well, if I looked at this, I could get a rough idea of what that guy was thinking...*

*Oh, yeah.*

I smacked my lips in disappointment and closed the status window.

Separately, I felt that Keundal was intentionally making more of a fuss than usual with the pop-up because of how I'd acted in the hallway before filming.

*It's okay now.*

Okay. When I was filming, I could separate my private self from my work persona, so it was nice to be able to shut out as many distracting thoughts as possible.

With work drowning everything else out, I only had to focus on my goals.

Thanks to this, my head was less noisy.

*Useless thoughts are prohibited from now on.*

It was nice to see Seon Ahyeon again after so long. My first fansite master, too... Well, she seemed to be enjoying her life, so that was fine.

*That was all there was to it.*

I just waited for the boss to make the announcement about the teams.

"The judges will also give you feedback as mentors, so we will form a team under each judge."

*We get it, you bastard.*

Then, the participants were called one by one.

They were organized into four teams of three contestants, each with a mentor, but there was nothing special about the first teams other than Ryu Chungwoo and Cheongryeo ending up on the same team.

Until I was called.

"Ryu Gunwoo, Jung Woodan and... Jin Chaeyul."

"...!"

"The mentor artist will be ballerino Seon Ahyeon."

"......"

I'd gotten both Seon Ahyeon and Jin Chaeyul.

*Just when I thought I'd be fine because I could focus on filming, they had to crank up the difficulty.*

This team match looked like it would test my patience.

***

"Hyung, we're finally on the same team!"

"Yeah. It'll be nice working with you."

"Yes! Let's do our best!"

Jin Chaeyul seemed to be in a great mood. He even explained why he was so excited.

“Really... I guess the president's finally becoming more flexible. The guerrilla stage, and letting us perform together again!”

*He's free to think whatever he wants. If it puts him in a better mood, all the better.*

Of course, it wasn't as if the president had gotten over his strong preferences and fallen in love with the marine look.

I checked my team members and mentor again and came to a conclusion.

*He set it up like this on purpose to destroy the marine look image.*

Since we were paired with a ballerino, we couldn't perform a stage with as light a concept as before, and he'd even included Woodan to set the mood.

In other words, I think he was planning to erase the impression that Jin Chaeyul and I had left by choosing something more impressive than our previous marine look performance.

*He's dreaming big too.*

If anyone in the world was benefiting from Cheongryeo's interference, it was this guy. How had he managed to make the agency successful while being so stubborn about his own tastes?

*Anyway... Why am I the leader again?*

Since the other two people were members of Cheongryeo's VTIC, it couldn't be helped. Both were probably yes-men with no opinions of their own.

It was about time I took the lead again and won high praise from the president, anyway. It would make for a good picture on screen.

"Then, let's do our best."

"Yes, yes!"

"Yes."

I smoothly gathered our practice sheets and started planning.

First of all, we'd choose a song from among LeTi's artists, and for the collaboration... probably classical dance.

...I assumed this would happen because our mentor was Seon Ahyeon. Fortunately, the agency's experts and Seon Ahyeon were in charge of the choreography and specific arrangements, and all we had to do was come up with the concept.

I asked the others out of courtesy.

"Is there a concept you would like to do that fits the keyword 'art'?"

I didn't expect an answer, but to my surprise, Chaeyul raised his hand with a very determined expression.

"Oh, I... I have something...!"

"...!"

*That VTIC guy... had an opinion?*

*Let's hear it first.*

"Speak comfortably."

"Yes! I... You know the concept that hyung used during our last photo shoot! That vampire concept."

“Uh, yeah...”

*The problem was, it was so cringe it practically made my toes curl permanently.*

I bit back a groan as I remembered the photo shoot that had surprisingly received a good response on the Internet.

“How about doing it that way? When I think of vampires, I feel a certain elegance... Classical dance is elegant, too...!”

“It sounds okay to me,” Woodan said.

"...Yeah, it sounds great."

No, actually it wasn't okay.

*Why does this bother me?*

I found the answer in a strange sense of déjà vu.

*Classical dance and vampires.*

*A LeTi song.*

*Ah.*

This... It overlapped a lot with what I did in the first team match during ⟨Idol Inc.⟩.

Now that there were guidelines on what we could and couldn't do, they were finally starting to speak up.

"Have you thought of a song?"

"I don't know... Oh, should we find a song from our agency that goes well with ballet?"

“Are there songs suitable for ballet? In that case, we should first write down the songs that used classical dance in their choreography.”

"Okay! Hyung, is this... How does it feel? Is it okay?"

I'd need perfect timing and good reasons to dissuade them.

The problem was that, objectively, I knew there was no need to stop them.

*They don't know.*

None of them knew anyway.

This wasn't the world where Park Moondae had competed in ⟨Idol Inc.⟩.

I already knew. That stage didn't exist in this world. There was no problem with using it. But...

*Shut up.*

I didn't know why I was whining so much. The answer was simple enough; I just had to accept it.

For now, my silence could pass for careful consideration, but if I waited any longer, the editors might portray me strangely.

I opened my mouth.

"It seems like a good approach. I'll look for a song too."

"Yes!!"

Jin Chaeyul's face became brighter.

He said, ‘I wanted to be helpful this time,’ since I'd done most of the work last time. He then excitedly searched WeTube for songs to use.

However, even after searching through all of LeTi's songs, there was no song that used classical dance in its choreography as much as ⟨To a New World⟩.

Of course. It was the only song with enough gentle lines to lend itself to soft classical dance.

"......"

*But... I guess I could do this.*

“I'll also look for songs that I think would go well with classical dance once arranged. It might give them a unique charm compared to the original, so we can consider them as candidates.”

"Wow... Yes, yes!"

I finished the selection process by finding four or five more candidate songs and listing them.

And the next time I saw Seon Ahyeon was two days later, on the day of the interim evaluation.
