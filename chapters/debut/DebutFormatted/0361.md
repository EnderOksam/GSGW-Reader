# Chapter 361 - A Carrot Revolution, III

Walking through rural rice fields in the middle of the night could be quite creepy because there weren't many lights like in the city. There were only the occasional flickering streetlights.

Still, it didn't seem to have much of an effect on someone who was used to this sort of thing.

I turned to the guy trudging along behind me with a shovel.

"Aren't you scared?"

"...! Is there any reason I should be afraid?"

*Never mind. Let's just keep going.* I raised my smartphone and turned on the camera.

"Is that the camera?" Kim Raebin asked.

"Yeah. We should probably film what we're doing."

I aimed the back camera at myself and waved. Kim Raebin also instinctively bowed.

"Hello, everyone. It is now 3:30 in the morning. We have just left our accommodations."

"Th-that's right," Raebin said.

"I'll explain why we came out."

I declared calmly.

"We don't wish to be scammed anymore in this carrot economy... We have decided that a revolution is in order."

"What??" Raebin said.

"A revolution," I repeated.

Kim Raebin's gaze began to waver.

"I... hyung. I know that getting carrot coins is important, but wouldn't it be rude to carelessly dig up a field that belongs to someone else...?"

"Of course, that's rude behavior."

"What?"

I stopped where the rice paddies ended and the outskirts of the village began.

*This should be far enough.*

"Let's leave the shovel here."

"The shovel...?"

Kim Raebin looked dazed for a moment but then regained his wits.

"Well, if we're going to pull carrots, wouldn't it be easier to take the shovel with us?"

I chuckled and grinned at him.

"Our destination is not the carrot field."

"...!"

"We only brought the shovel to make other people think we were going to the carrot field."

There were hidden cameras all over the house. Someone was bound to be watching the feeds, even at this hour.

Our unexpected actions would surely have been reported by now. The production team probably thought we were headed to the carrot field.

*In fact, they probably predicted we would do so from the start.*

Carrots were worth one coin each. It wasn't hard to predict that some of us might try to steal actual carrots in the night.

Just look at Kim Raebin right now. Even he had assumed that was what we were doing.

"Were you worried that we were going to steal crops from someone else's carrot field?"

"I did not doubt your morals in the least! This is a variety show, so I assumed that it might be allowed. That is all!" Raebin said.

"Okay. You must have had a hard time thinking about it. Don't worry anymore."

This confusion was precisely what I had in mind.

*You can sit around watching that carrot field all night.*

Certain that they wouldn't realize what we were really up to, I was doing them a favor by recording it.

I put a flashlight and a hoe on top of the shovel. And then I started walking again.

"A-are we abandoning these?"

"No, we'll leave them here for a little while and come back to get them. We don't want to walk into a store carrying shovels, do we?"

"That's true, but... What kind of store is open this early in the morning?"

"Yes."

I raised my finger and pointed to a faintly twinkling sign in the distance.

"They're open twenty-four seven and will travel to your location."

"...?"

Kim Raebin turned his gaze to follow my finger. I filmed him as he did so.

His puzzled face filled the screen.

"What... are we doing here?"

"I thought you said you'd been to a store like this before?"

It was when I heard him talking about his childhood last time.

"Yes. That's true, but..."

"Okay, I'll trust you to handle it."

"...?? Oh, I understand!"

Kim Raebin looked even more confused but immediately went along with my request. Bringing him was convenient, since he wasn't one to complain.

"Good. Let's go in."

We entered a store in an old building that was still lit.

Some illegally posted flyers on the street were fluttering in the wind, their red letters showing.

!pb
**[Woori General Hardware Store]**

#r[Available for emergency work on-site / Open 24/7]r#
pb!

---

Morning dawned.

"Good morning, everyone," I said.

"Good morning..." Raebin echoed.

"...Why are you guys coming in through the front door instead of your rooms?"

By the time we returned to our accommodations, it was after seven in the morning. We weren't carrying anything other than the tools we'd taken with us.

The PD, who'd been camping out in the yard, asked us a question in a puzzled voice.

"Where have you two been?"

I'd sent a text message to the production crew last night to let them know we were safe, saying we were on an adventure so they wouldn't report us as missing or anything.

I left the shovel where it belonged in the yard and said, "We wanted to go to the carrot field, but we got lost."

"Hahaha!"

*Are you happy? I want you to laugh more. It'll be more interesting that way.*

And after the production crew's interrogation, questions came pouring in from the members.

"What did you do, hyung? Did you eat something delicious with Kim Raebin? Did you do something fun?" Eugene asked.

"No. We starved."

"Oh... alright. Cheer up."

*That takes care of Eugene.* Cha Eugene passed by.

I passed the other members who were still sleeping or had just gotten up. One of them threw his arms around my neck in a headlock.

Of course, it was Keun Sejin.

"No~ I'm disappointed. How could you leave Sejin behind like this and film something at night? Heok heok."

*Let go, you bastard.*

"It wasn't proper filming anyway. I didn't even have a camera, so I used a smartphone."

"With Park Moondae's filming skills, you're practically a professional. It must have been an important scene, so I'm even more disappointed~"

*Oh, come on.*

"I did it because I thought we'd get caught if too many of us left together, okay? Raebin had a job to do, so I brought him along."

"Oh..."

Keun Sejin stopped his mock crying and grinned.

"I'm becoming more and more curious?"

"I'll tell you all before breakfast, so gather the other guys."

"Hmm?"

I chuckled.

"We've prepared something good. It's time to share."

And after a while...

The production team was informed of our sudden plans to leave the accommodations.

"Everyone, you only have 4 coins. Are you really going to go shopping?"

"Yes. We're still going."

"Oh, that's good for us~ We're not complaining. Whatever it is, it's up to TeSTAR!"

*Smooth talkers, all of them.* They seemed excited at the prospect of watching us struggle to buy something to bring home.

Anyway, despite the early hour, we went to the supermarket again, accompanied by the production crew.

As soon as we entered, Ryu Chungwoo spoke in a friendly voice.

"Guys, why don't we pick out everything we want to eat?"

"Yeaaah! Great!" Eugene exclaimed.

"W-well, then... I'll bring some fruit...!" Ahyeon said.

The production team seemed confused as the members scattered in every direction.

"Huh?"

"What?"

And the PD spoke to me in a subdued voice.

"You know that harvesting carrots without permission is theft, right? We will deduct twice as much as the penalty."

"We didn't steal any carrots."

He seemed to think that we'd dug up carrots from some field without their supervision and hidden them.

*Unfortunately for you, that's not the case.*

"Okay~ So is this all?" Keun Sejin said.

"Wait a moment."

"Wow, Moondae! You're really going all out. One duck per person?"

After we filled our cart with various groceries, we headed to the counter, where the store manager welcomed us once again.

However, what he said was different.

"Hmm, coins... Just give me one for all of this today."

"...!!"

"Thank you~" Keun Sejin said.

As Keun Sejin quickly tried to complete the transaction, the production crew shouted in confusion.

"O-one?"

"Wait."

"This can't be happening...?"

"Hm? What can't be happening~ Didn't you hear the manager? Chungwoo-hyung, give us a coin!"

"Haha, yes."

Ryu Chungwoo handed over a single coin and paid for the groceries.

*Click!*

Judging from the price on the screen, we'd probably racked up a 300,000-won grocery bill.

*Nice.*

The PD managed to ask.

"...What did you do?"

"Do what?"

"No, something's going on here! What did you do??"

"Why do you keep asking? This whole system is about the residents setting whatever prices they want, right?"

"......"

Other staff members could be heard holding back laughter around the PD, whose mouth was wide open.

I glanced at Raebin to warn him not to say anything and nodded.

"I'm glad they gave us such a cheap price."

"I know."

Step one was complete.

After we'd had a generous breakfast, we took the cart out again to help the elderly.

"Hey!" Eugene cried.

"Cha Eugene, slow down! I said slow down!" Raebin cried after him.

Once again, we were enthusiastically rewarded with a generous amount of coins.

"Take this~ It's for sweeping the yard yesterday~"

"Th-thank you...!" Ahyeon said.

"Oh my, you young people are doing a lot of good work~ Here! I've included five coins for you."

"Wow! Father, I'm really touched... Well, I'd expect no less from someone as handsome as you, right?" Keun Sejin said.

We made thirty coins in only three hours. It was more than we'd made on the first day.

The members exchanged proud looks.

"Oh, we earned as much as we did working an entire day on the carrot farm," Keun Sejin said.

"That's it. Let's go," the director said.

The director was at the end of his rope.

"The massage chair for seniors... You haven't forgotten about it, have you?"

"Of course not~ It would be great to have one~"

"Ugh!"

The director was clearly in the midst of a minor meltdown.

*To translate, I guess he wanted to say something like,* "You cheaters!"

*You flatter us.*

I low-key high-fived the members.

*Step two was also successful.*

By the time we'd sat down for a short snack...

I prepared the final exposé before things got old.

"I'm done washing dishes~" Keun Sejin said.

"Let's get some rest," Chungwoo said.

As if he were going to lie down comfortably with the guys on the veranda, Ryu Chungwoo quietly set his lucky bag full of coins beside him.

And the production team, who had been going around the village asking what was going on in their confusion, immediately fell for the bait.

"What on earth..."

One of the writers checked the pouch just in case and soon discovered something.

"Huh?"

"What?"

The writer urgently called the director over and held out a coin.

"Th-this coin is different!"

"...!!"

The production crew began to murmur quietly. And for a while, they were running around and making phone calls like crazy.

Just then, the truth emerged.

"Someone went around in the morning and gave dozens of carrot coins to every house! They said it was a special gift..."

"...!"

*Ah, they found us out. But it's too late.*

"...TeSTAR, grandchildren."

"Yes?"

"Now... We've just been informed that over a thousand carrot coins were handed out in this town, and not by us?"

"Is that so?"

*Yes.*

Once the villagers believed they had enough coins to secure a massage chair, it was only natural for their generosity to return.

"Even at the supermarket... Apparently there are another five hundred of them?"

"Right."

*Okay. I'd negotiated there as well.*

– *From now on, please let us buy everything we need for a single coin when we show up with cameras.*

They had nothing to lose, since the production team would be paying for whatever we bought anyway.

"Everyone, how did this happen??"

"We don't know anything about this. Right?"

"Of course we don't," Keun Sejin said.

The PD called out.

"Moondae-ssi! Raebin-ssi! What did you do when you left at dawn?"

*Well, we went carrot mining.*

We hadn't gone to any of the fields, but we had mined coins nonetheless.

*The method was rather unorthodox, of course. It's your fault for making the coins like a kids' plaything.* They were easy to reproduce.

"No, this...! These! They're fake."

"Is there a problem?"

I tilted my head. The production team was in shock, holding the identical yellow carrot-patterned coins we'd had printed at the hardware store.

"I don't see the problem here... They look identical."

"Oh, it's exactly the same," Keun Sejin said.

"...I can't tell the difference either," Bae Sejin agreed casually.

The production team suppressed frustrated screams as the two Sejins calmly agreed with me.

*Okay.*

It was true that variety shows in which successful people were forced to endure unexpected tribulations were fun.

But that didn't mean *we* had to suffer.

I had instead decided to turn the tables on the production team that had experienced massive success with every one of their shows by tricking the guests in each one.

*These people right here.*

"Anyway! Moondae, Raebin, we know that you ordered something at the hardware store!"

*So you figured that out, did you?*

"Yes."

"No, TeSTAR can only use carrot coins to purchase items. What did you do?"

The PD shouted agitatedly, thinking he'd finally found a clue.

I shrugged.

"Oh, they didn't charge us because they said they were fans of ours."

"...!!"

"Who works for free out of fan spirit?! You gave him money, didn't you?!"

*Yes. Of course, I was going to pay. The order was on credit. It was one of the advantages of being a celebrity whose face was well-known.*

*It was a few times more expensive because we'd put in an urgent order in the middle of the night, but it was worth every penny.*

But I answered without so much as batting an eye.

"Do you have any evidence?"

"Ugh!"

*Whoa, calm down. I don't want you passing out.*

And soon the PD changed his target.

"Raebin-ssi!"

"Y-yes?"

"Raebin-ssi, you are a good, upright person, aren't you? Are you going to go along with this farce?"

"W-well, that's..."

*Oh. That was a good approach.*

*But I also expected him to do that.*

"It's not a farce. It's not a lie."

"What...?"

Kim Raebin clenched both hands into fists.

"Besides, it's only right that people get more carrot coins."

"...!!"

"If there are any problems, I will buy them the massage chairs with my own money!"

"What??"

I had already brainwashed—that is, *explained*—the situation to him, though it had taken two hours to do so.

"......"

The production team was at a loss.

"Moondaemoondae, let's buy everything from the store. We only have to pay one coin," Keun Sejin said.

"Wait a minute."

"Why? You said the residents are setting these prices of their own free will."

"I'm sorry! I'm sorry! Wait!"

Even the writers were joining in now. They quickly held up a sketchbook with an urgently scrawled message.

!$
\
$$[We can't go over the budget! We have product placement deals to carry out! Please go easy on us! Help!]$$
$!

*They're surrendering, clearly.*

"You want relaxing? We'll give you relaxing!"

"Just this time?"

"Next time too! Next time, we'll take you on a full luxury course to... I know! The hot springs, for our Very Healing Full Course!"

And so the shenanigans planned with the coins were stymied by our indiscriminate production of replicas.

This was around the time when the trailer for the first episode dropped.
