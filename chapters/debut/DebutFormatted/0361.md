# Chapter 361

Walking through rural rice fields at night could be quite creepy because there weren't many lights like in the city. There were street lights that flickered occasionally.

Still, it didn't seem to have much of an effect on someone who was used to this sort of thing.

I asked the guy trudging along with a shovel.

"Aren't you scared?"

"...! Is there any reason I should be afraid?"

*Okay then.* Let's just keep going. I raised my smartphone and turned on the camera.

"A camera?"

"We should probably film what we're doing."

I aimed the back camera at myself and waved. Kim Raebin also instinctively lowered his head.

"Hello everyone. The time right now is 3:30 in the morning. We have just left our accommodations."

"Yes, that's right."

"I'll explain why we came out."

I declared calmly.

"We don't wish to be scammed anymore in this carrot economy... We have decided that a revolution is in order."

"Yes??"

"A revolution."

Kim Raebin's pupils began to tremble.

"I... hyung. I know that getting carrot coins is important, but wouldn't it be rude to carelessly dig up a field that belongs to someone else..."

"Of course, that's rude behavior."

"What?"

I stopped where the rice paddies ended and the outskirts of the village began.

This should be far enough.

"Let's leave the shovel here."

"The shovel...?"

Kim Raebin looked dazed for a moment but then regained his wits.

"Well, if we're pulling carrots, wouldn't it be easier to use a shovel?"

I chuckled.

"Our destination is not the carrot field."

"...!"

"We only brought the shovel to make other people think we were going to the carrot field."

There were hidden cameras all over the house. Someone was bound to be watching the feeds, even at this hour.

They probably thought we were headed to the carrot field.

No doubt they predicted we would do so from the start.

Carrots were worth one coin each. It wasn't hard to predict that some of us might try to steal actual carrots in the night.

Just look at Kim Raebin right now.

"Were you worried that we were going to steal crops from someone else's carrot field?"

"I did not doubt your morals in the least! This is a variety show, so I assumed that it might be allowed. That is all!"

"Okay. You must have had a hard time thinking about it. Don't worry anymore."

This confusion was precisely what I had in mind.

They could sit around watching us in that carrot field all night.

I was doing them a favor by recording what we were actually up to.

I put a flashlight and a hoe on top of the shovel. And then I took another step.

"Are we abandoning these?"

"No, I'll leave it for a moment and come back to get it. We don't want to walk into a store carrying shovels, do we?"

"That's true, but... Are there any stores open this early in the morning?"

"Yes."

I raised my finger and pointed to a faintly twinkling sign in the distance.

"They're open twenty-four seven and will travel to your location."

"...?"

Kim Raebin turned his gaze to follow my finger. I took a picture of him with my camera.

A puzzled face appeared on the screen.

"Why... There?"

"I thought you said you'd been to a store like this before."

It was when I heard him talking about his childhood last time.

"Yes. That's true, but..."

"Okay, trust me."

"...?? Oh, I understand!"

Kim Raebin looked even more confused but quickly answered. Bringing him was convenient, since he wasn't one to complain.

"Good. Let's go in."

We entered a store in an old building that was still lit.

Some illegally posted flyers were fluttering in the wind, their red letters showing.

!pb
**[Woori General Hardware Store]**

#rEmergency on-site construction available – open 24 hoursr#
pb!

---

Morning dawned.

"Has everyone woken up?"

"Good morning..."

"...Why are you guys coming out of the entrance instead of your rooms?"

By the time we returned to our accommodations, it was after seven in the morning. We weren't carrying anything other than the tools we'd taken with us.

The PD camped out in the yard and asked us a question in a puzzled voice.

"Where have you two been?"

Still, I sent a text message to the production crew last night saying we were on an adventure so they wouldn't report us as missing or anything.

I left the shovel where it belonged in the yard and said, "We wanted to go to the carrot field, but we got lost."

"Hahaha!"

Are you happy? I want you to laugh more. It'll be more interesting that way.

And after the production crew's interrogation, questions came pouring in from the members.

"What did you do, hyung? Did you eat something delicious with Kim Raebin? Did you do something fun?"

"No. We starved."

"Oh... alright. Cheer up."

Cha Eugene passed by.

I passed the other members who were still sleeping or had just gotten up. One of them threw his arms around my neck in a headlock.

Of course, it was Lee Sejin.

"No~ I'm disappointed. How could you leave Sejin behind like this at night? Heok heok."

*Let go, you bastard.*

"It was nothing like that. I didn't even have a camera, so I used a smartphone."

"With Park Moondae's filming skills, didn't you film it like a professional? It must have been an important scene, so I'm even more disappointed~"

*Indeed.*

"I did it because I thought I would get caught if there were a lot of people, okay? Raebin had a job to do, so I brought him along."

"Oh..."

Lee Sejin stopped crying and grinned.

"I'm becoming more and more curious?"

"I'll tell you before breakfast, so gather the other guys."

"Hmm?"

I chuckled.

"We've prepared something good. It's time to share."

And after a while...

The production team was informed of our sudden plans to leave the accommodations.

"Everyone, you only have 4 coins. Are you really going to go shopping?"

"Yes. I still want to go."

"Oh, that's good for us~ Whatever it is, it's up to TeSTAR!"

Smooth talkers, all of them. They seemed excited at the prospect of watching us struggle to buy something to bring home.

Anyway, we went to the supermarket again in the morning, accompanied by the production crew.

As soon as we entered, Ryu Chungwoo explained in a friendly voice.

"Why don't we pick out everything we want to eat?"

"Yeaaah! Great!"

"Well, then... I'll bring some fruit...!"

The production team seemed confused as the members scattered in every direction.

"Huh?"

"What?"

And the PD spoke to me in a subdued voice.

"You know that if you steal from the carrot field, it's theft, right? We will deduct twice as much as the penalty."

"I said I didn't do it."

He seemed to think that we'd dug up carrots from some field without their supervision and hidden them.

Unfortunately, that was not the case.

"Okay~ So is this all?"

"Wait a moment."

"Wow, Moondae is going all out. One duck per person?"

After we filled our cart with various groceries, the store manager welcomed me once again.

However, what he said was different.

"Hmm, coins... Please pay me just one today."

"...!!"

"Thank you~"

As Lee Sejin quickly tried to finish the calculation, the production crew shouted in confusion.

"O-one?"

"Wait."

"This can't be happening...?"

"Hm? What can't be happening~ Chungwoo-hyung, please calculate the bill!"

"Haha, yes."

Ryu Chungwoo handed out the coin and finished the calculation.

*Click!*

Judging from the price on the screen, we'd probably racked up a 300,000-won grocery bill. Nice.

The PD managed to ask.

"...What did you do?"

"What?"

"No, what did you do??"

"Why do you keep asking? This whole system is about the residents setting whatever prices they want, right?"

"......"

Other staff members could be heard holding back laughter around the PD, whose mouth was wide open.

I glanced at Raebin to warn him not to say anything and nodded.

"I'm glad he gave it to us cheaply."

"I know."

Step one was complete.

After we'd had a generous breakfast, we drove the cart again to help the elderly.

"Hey!"

"Cha Eugene, slow down! Your speed!"

Once again, we were awarded an enthusiastic and generous amount of coins.

"Take this~ It's a service for sweeping the yard yesterday~"

"Thank you...!"

"Oh my, the young people are having a hard time~ Here! You can have five coins."

"Wow! Father, really... Well, good-looking people think differently, right?"

We made thirty coins in only three hours. It was more than we'd made on the first day.

The members shrugged their shoulders.

"Oh, we earned as much as our daily wage on our carrot farm."

"That's it. Let's go."

The director was at the end of his rope.

"The massage chair for seniors... Don't you need it?"

"I don't need it~ But it would be great to have that~"

"Ugh!"

The director clearly had a minor meltdown.

To translate, I guess he wanted to say something like, "You cheaters!"

I low-key high-fived the members.

Step two was also successful.

By the time we'd sat down for a short snack...

I prepared the final exposé before things got old.

"I'm done washing dishes~"

"Let's get some rest."

As if to lie down comfortably among the guys lying on the veranda, Ryu Chungwoo quietly unpacked his lucky bag and put it in front of him.

And the production team, who had been going around the village asking what was going on, suddenly asked the question.

"What on earth..."

One of the writers checked the pouch just in case and soon discovered something.

"Huh?"

"What?"

The writer urgently called the director over and held out a coin.

"Hey, isn't this different?"

"...!!"

The production crew began to murmur. And for a while, they were running around and making phone calls like crazy.

Just then, the right news arrived.

"Someone went around in the morning and gave dozens of carrot coins to every house!"

"...!"

Ah, they found us out. But it's too late.

"...TeSTAR, grandchildren."

"Yes?"

"Now... I heard there are over a thousand carrot coins loose in this town?"

"Is that so?"

*Okay.*

Once the villagers believed they had enough coins to secure a massage chair, wouldn't it be natural for their generosity to return?

"Even at the supermarket... There are about five hundred of them?"

"What?"

*Okay. We negotiated there as well.*

– From now on, please let us buy everything we need for a single coin when we show up with cameras.

They had nothing to lose, since the production team would be paying for whatever we bought anyway.

"Everyone, how did this happen??"

"We don't know either. Right?"

"Okay then."

The PD called out.

"Moondae-ssi! Raebin-ssi! What did you do when you left at dawn?"

*Well, we dug up carrots.*

We hadn't gone to any of the fields, but we had mined coins nonetheless. It's your fault for making the coins like a kids' plaything. They were easy to reproduce.

"No, this...! This! It's fake."

"Is there a problem?"

I tilted my head after seeing the production team in shock, holding the same yellow carrot-patterned coin printed at a hardware store.

"I don't see the problem here... They look identical."

"Oh, it's exactly the same?"

"...I can't tell the difference either."

The production team suppressed frustrated screams as the two Sejins calmly agreed with me.

*Okay.*

It was true that variety shows in which successful people were forced to endure unexpected tribulations were fun.

But that didn't mean we had to suffer.

I had instead decided to turn the tables on the production team that had experienced massive success with every one of their shows by tricking the guests in each one.

"Anyway! Moondae, Raebin, we know that you ordered something at the hardware store!"

So you figured that out, did you?

"Yes."

"No, TeSTAR can only use carrot coins to purchase items. What did you do?"

The PD shouted excitedly at the theory he had finally caught.

I shrugged.

"Oh, he gave it to me for free because he was a fan of ours."

"...!!"

"Who works with fan spirit?! You gave him money, didn't you?!"

*Yes. Of course, I paid. It was comfortable because I was a celebrity whose face was well-known.*

*It was a few times more expensive because it was handled by real-time express at night, but it was worth every penny.*

But I answered without changing my expression.

"Do you have any evidence?"

"Ugh!"

And soon the PD changed his target.

"Raebin-ssi!"

"Yes, yes?"

"Raebin-ssi, you are a good person, aren't you? Or are you condoning lies like this?"

"Well, that's..."

*Oh. That was a good approach.*

*But I also expected him to do that.*

"It's not a lie."

"What...?"

Kim Raebin spoke with clenched fists.

"Besides, it's only right that people get more carrot coins."

"...!!"

"If there are any problems, I will buy them the massage chairs with my own money!"

"What??"

I had already brainwashed—that is, explained—the situation to him, though it had taken two hours to do so.

"......"

The production team was at a loss.

"Moondaemoondae, let's buy everything from the store. We only have to pay one coin."

"Wait a minute"

"Why? You said the prices were up to the residents' voluntary pricing."

"I'm sorry! I'm sorry!"

Even the writers were joining in now. They quickly held up a sketchbook with an urgently scrawled message.

!$
\
$$We have a rabbit-like production cost and product placement! Help!$$
$!

They are surrendering, clearly.

"You want relaxing? We'll give you relaxing!"

"Just this time?"

"Next time too! Next time, we'll take you to the hot springs over there for our Very Healing Full Course, hot springs!"

And so the shenanigans planned with the coins were stymied by our indiscriminate production of the same coins.

This was around the time when the trailer for the first episode dropped.
