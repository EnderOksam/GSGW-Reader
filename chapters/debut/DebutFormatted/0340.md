# The Soundtrack Controversy, II

Let's take a look at the public reaction to the plagiarism controversy involving 'All-Star Music' and TeSTAR's Kim Raebin.

No, to be precise, I categorized the guys who were excited because they thought they'd finally found an excuse to curse TeSTAR.

Number one.

It was called, 'I didn't expect that.'

★$
– So many people insisted Kim Raebin was a genius, but look how that turned out... Disgusting.

– It's so shocking. I didn't know a *daesang*-winning idol could stoop so low T_T

– TeSTAR is really disappointing. Wouldn't Korea also be ridiculed?
$★

They were all emphasizing that they'd never thought badly of TeSTAR before, but were deeply disappointed by this incident.

Of course, doing the exact opposite was just as satisfying for them.

Number two, which was called 'I knew it.'

★$
– Face-reading is a science. Just look at his face. He looks shady too.

– I didn't think it was all malicious editing during ⟨Idol Inc.⟩. No wonder the fans were so eager to make him look innocent.

– This kind of nonsense usually ends with the person doing drugs... The way Kim Raebin rambles... I don't know, guys, but this looks suspicious.
$★

It was fun for them to get away with all sorts of speculative accusations and rumors that would normally have been shot down immediately.

And the most important, number three.

The people who liked to praise All-Star Music.

The more they worshiped the victim, the better it felt to attack the alleged wrongdoer.

★$
– The songs on the All-Star Music channel are really beautiful. No wonder TeSTAR copied them.

– ᄏᄏᄏ I'm a music major. I can tell All-Star Music came up with the original music. Kim Raebin's been using their music as a reference for some time, and it seems he's only recently taken it a little too far.

– So he was cosplaying as a genius, but All-Star Music is the real genius ᄏᄏ
$★

"So, if we were to predict the reaction of Kim Raebin, the core member behind All-Star Music, who's currently being praised as the Mozart of our generation and a genius..."

"...He'll be grateful?"

"Oh, that's it."

At Keun Sejin's words, the members either sighed or burst into incredulous laughter.

We'd stopped Kim Raebin, who was about to post "TeSTAR is All-Star Music" on social media, and gathered in the dorm in the early morning as soon as we got back to Korea.

"What about Raebin?"

"H-he's sleeping just fine."

"Okay."

"But why stop Kim Raebin?" Cha Eugene asked. "Let's tell them it's us, quickly! Give them a taste of justice!"

"Hmm~ Eugene, it's better to prepare a little before talking about something like this."

Keun Sejin smiled wryly.

"They're the ones who misunderstood, but some of them will think we deceived them and get angry."

"Ooo... That's ridiculous."

"Yeah, it is."

The guy was right.

Of course, the moment it was revealed that All-Star Music was TeSTAR's account centered on Kim Raebin, mainstream public opinion would turn around.

However, depending on how we revealed it, there could be some unnecessary side effects.

I crossed my arms and opened my mouth.

"They might even think we did it on purpose for publicity."

"Yeah, that's another possibility."

Some people might claim that we'd created the WeTube account as soon as we signed the OST contract, and only started uploading arrangements related to ⟨127 Section⟩ after that.

★$
– To be honest, they responded way too quickly for it to seem like a coincidence.

– Hmm... So they signed a contract to do the OST for a movie based on Ruins Factory's universe, then made a channel and started uploading arrangements of Ruins Factory's songs?

– Hey, this stinks of lies. Those T1 guys ᄏᄏᄏᄏ
$★

There was no reason to leave them such easy ammunition.

Ryu Chungwoo spoke with a serious face.

"But we can't wait long. Raebin is being attacked too much."

"Yes. It is a serious misunderstanding."

If we took too long trying to work out a solution, the backlash would only grow stronger.

*They won't want to apologize.*

Most of the people who'd hopped on the plagiarism bandwagon wouldn't want to admit that they were wrong.

Then they'd try to find any reason they could to blame us instead of themselves.

Seon Ahyeon looked sad.

"E-even if it's a misunderstanding... Th-this isn't fair. They didn't even wait for Raebin to explain before going this far..."

"...Yes. It's too harsh."

I nodded.

"It's a side effect of the song doing too well."

Things might not have gone this way if it had been one of our normal releases, but this OST had the whole country feeling proud to be Korean.

*Their vicarious satisfaction has been shattered and turned into vicarious shame.*

They were even more enraged because they feared the entire country would be ridiculed abroad because of this.

It was the backlash of all that national pride. Even if one of our title tracks had been embroiled in a similar controversy, the Internet wouldn't have been in such an uproar.

"Hoo."

I laughed incredulously at the overheated atmosphere as I watched the number of subscribers on the 'All-Star Music' WeTube channel keep skyrocketing. Keun Sejin leaned in close to check and looked shocked.

"Wow, over 100,000 subscribers?"

"I know."

In the meantime, it had turned into noise marketing. Should we register the account for ad revenue and collect the money minus the copyright fees?

★$
–-#10 mins ago#- Go for it! [👍 👎]

–-#8 mins ago#- I just subscribed. Your songs are really good. [👍 👎]

–-#5 mins ago#- ☆★☆★☆Limestone, look here, he's the real creator of the songs. Use this genius☆★☆★☆ [👍 👎]

–-#2 mins ago#- It's really amazing. I heard you're a college student. Age doesn't seem to matter when it comes to talent. [👍 👎]
$★

Bae Sejin, who was reading the comments on the channel, muttered with a wry expression.

"...Kim Raebin is really being recognized just as much as he's being criticized..."

"Th-that's right..."

It was an absurd situation that left us wondering whether we should be happy or sad. Cha Eugene stretched.

"Whatever, if we do well on the next one, they won't be able to say anything! Let's just tell them the truth!"

He never changed.

And surprisingly, Ryu Chungwoo opened his mouth seriously.

"Actually, I feel the same way."

"What?"

"Maybe I'm being a bit optimistic, but... I don't think there'll be any more trouble if we're just honest about it."

*Hmm.*

"Be honest?"

"Yes. We can explain why we created the account in the first place. It's actually a good story."

"Mmm."

*The standard approach, huh?*

It was admittedly the most correct way to make a statement. Honestly explaining the situation as it was.

"So why don't we make an official statement through the company?"

"......"

Then I heard a low voice from behind me.

"Uh... I agree."

"Ahh!" Bae Sejin exclaimed in surprise.

"Sorry!"

When I turned my head, I saw Kim Raebin standing hesitantly behind Bae Sejin, who was frightened.

"I-I thought you were sleeping!"

"I woke up a few minutes ago. I came out for some water, but you seemed to be having a chat, so I tried to make as little noise as possible and ended up overhearing you!"

"Uh..."

We were the ones who should feel guilty for talking about him behind his back, so why was he making excuses?

Anyway, now that he was up, we decided to hear what he had to say.

"So... Raebin, is this what you want to do? Be honest about everything?"

"Yes."

Kim Raebin sat down among us and nodded.

"In fact, we started that account because the hyungs wanted to help me overcome my slump, so I really want to tell people that."

"R-Raebin..."

"Of course, we won't be able to keep running the account as a hobby anymore... But I'll find another healthy hobby!"

Kim Raebin clenched his fists.

*Oh.*

I smiled.

"Okay. Let's just be honest."

"...!!"

I heard murmuring beside me.

"Moondaemoondae?"

"Has he been drinking?"

I ignored them.

"Raebin is right. Truth always prevails. I think it's best to be honest about it."

"Thank you!"

"...Wow~"

Keun Sejin looked at me as though he didn't believe a word I was saying. *Look at this bastard.*

Of course, I still had more to say.

"By the way, why don't you make the statement yourself instead of going through the company?"

"Ah, you mean on TeSTAR's social media...?"

"No, not there."

"...?"

"Then where?"

I smiled and held my smartphone up in the air.

We had to maximize the impact. *Let's go.*
