# Chapter 203 - Glitch, II

As soon as I saw that Cheongryeo had picked up the phone, I went straight to the point.

"Can you talk?"

*—Yes. Having trouble with your mission?*

"......"

*—I feel like that's the only reason why you would contact me.*

Was the timing of my call too obvious, coming right after the encore concert ended?

I got hit in the back of my head by so many unexpected circumstances that I was lacking in judgment.

*Still, I can't give the impression that I'm begging for clues.*

I had to start by asking questions to set the tone. I cracked my neck.

"There were no problems with the mission itself."

*—I see.*

"But another mission appeared, though?"

*—...*

"It wasn't the last one. Did you lie to me on purpose?"

*—No.*

The answer across the phone sounded compliant. There was no laughter in his tone.

I could hear a tiny sound of a dog whining for a moment, but soon he continued his words quietly.

*—I think I can explain... If you don't mind, why don't we meet when you have time? Someone could hear us talking on the phone.*

"It would be better to do this over the phone."

I did not actually mean that talking about these details over the phone was better, but that I would rather someone overhear us than have my skull cracked.

*—If you're uncomfortable, I'm fine with meeting you at Testar's dorm. I don't have any intentions... I know it's impossible anyway.*

Well, I had planned to pry it out from him by threatening him with the recording if he began to spew nonsense or left something out, but it seems like that's not necessary.

Of course, I couldn't be sure when this guy would go insane again, so I was obviously going to make a safety plan.

"Then let's do it like this."

After setting the time and place, I went back from the veranda.

Kim Raebin, who happened to be nearby, was startled.

"Hyung! You were away for 35 minutes so I was worried that you might have sneaked off to drink, but you were actually on the veranda??"

"Yeah."

This guy also seemed to be quite drunk.

I sat down next to him before the other guys would notice and begin talking to me.

"Raebin-ah."

"Yes."

"I have a favor to ask."

"I will be all ears!"

As if mistaking that I was going to point out something about him, Kim Raebin psyched himself up.

*No, that wasn't what I meant.*

I waved my hand to calm him down and continued my explanation.

"The thing is..."

And things played out as expected.

* * *

A few days later.

"Seems fine."

I arrived first at an outdoor café on a downtown corner that I had suggested and that he had rented out.

The place was hard to observe from the outside, but if you heard a scream, someone nearby would likely report it immediately. There were also plenty of CCTVs.

"You got here early."

And a few minutes later, Cheongryeo appeared in front of the entrance fence... with a panting dog wearing a snug chest collar next to him.

"*Woof!*"

"......"

I thought of the possibility but didn't expect it to really pop out here.

"I thought bringing him would be good for assurance. It's fine, right?"

"...It doesn't matter."

"Haha, it's okay to pet him. He likes people."

Cheongryeo smiled as he picked up the dog and moved while embracing it. The dog was vigorously wagging its tail.

*The dog seemed to be in good shape.*

It looked chubby and well-fed.

"The café owner said it's okay to eat whatever food you want inside, so feel free to go in to get some food."

There was no way I could eat anything in this situation.

I sat down at the table and answered calmly.

"I'm not going to. Let's start with the conversation."

"Is that so? All right. Then, should we start with the fact that the mission isn't over?"

"Before that, I need you to answer something first."

"Hmh?"

I threw out the question I had been meaning to ask but had put on hold because I didn't think he would answer it.

"How did you determine the number of missions?"

"......"

This guy told me about the number of status abnormalities with full confidence right from the start.

*—I calculated it... and it seems like the number of tasks is given by the one time you went back and adding the number of years that you returned.*

"I need to know where you derived the formula of returning years plus one from. Because it was wrong."

"Ah, about that."

Cheongryeo compliantly nodded while petting his dog.

And then he threw out a bombshell of a statement.

"I collected cases involving people like us."

"...!"

"Ah, it wasn't right now. Since they're dead."

The guy had a dull, unimpressed look on his face.

"What was the illness again…? Anyway."

I clenched my hands together. My mind went blank.

There was someone else?

"How did you meet them?"

"Ah, it's a bit of an embarrassing story... I think it was my fifth time? At one time I went around saying useless things in an attempt to debut as quickly as possible."

Cheongryeo smiled brightly and continued his words.

"I talked about knowing the future all over on TV."

"...!"

"But maybe it was because I got the line of field wrong, I couldn't clear the mission... Well, I managed to do it properly the next time. It was pretty funny now that I think about it."

This bastard's tone was changing.

I shouldn't give the lunatic time to unnecessarily wallow in his past. I cut off the mood that was about to take a strange turn.

"Anyway, so what?"

"So... an old man had approached me, saying that he had also come from the future."

Cheongryeo let go of his dog.

"I did a little bit of checking to make sure it was real... and after that, I figured it out."

"What?"

"The ending point of the future he knew of coincided right at my restart point."

"...!"

"Therefore... it seems like there can only be one person in the same time frame who knows the future. It was a few years for that person, then a few years for me after that."

"......"

"And now it's you, Moondae-ssi."

I finally understood what he had meant when he previously talked to me.

*—It's perfect for you right now. It must feel like you're living as the main character.*

Was it because only one person occupied the time period of knowing the future?

*...Then, what are the criteria?*

What did he and I have in common? And was the dead old man also involved in the entertainment industry?

My head was cluttered with all sorts of questions, but the situation that buried all those questions was abstruse.

It was because Cheongryeo still kept muttering to himself even in the midst of this.

"Come to think of it... I don't think I ever restarted before that person died."

"......"

"It would've been nice if I restarted at least once while that person was still alive to see if he returned to the past as well, right? Then, we wouldn't have had to suffer for nothing. Haha!"

"No."

I deliberately shut him off.

"No?"

"Yeah. It's better to just get beaten up than to die to get that answer confirmed."

"...Mm. Well, I guess."

Cheongryeo lost his enthusiasm.

*This is crazy.*

As I wiped my hand, which the dog had started licking, on its fur, I suddenly realized something else.

"...Then, you noticed right away that I had returned to the past."

"That's right. Because it was just at the right time."

Cheongryeo laughed.

"I wondered... maybe there was someone else who knew of the future now, and all of a sudden, a person of common background appeared in the audition show and won first place."

"...!"

"In a program that everyone had expected to fail but became immensely successful instead... and at a very critical time too, the person won first place with his family history getting unraveled."

"......"

It was the combined result of coincidence and the effects of the status window. However, Cheongryeo smiled, seeming slightly embarrassed.

"How many times do you think I've returned? It was obvious."

"...Hmm."

The reasoning was plausible enough to investigate further.

"But I couldn't have predicted you to be another person... Well, I think the reason for another mission might be because of that."

In that instant, a theory flashed through my mind.

"...It's because I'm not 'Park Moondae'."

"Yes."

This would mean that the fact that I entered someone else's body gave me additional penalties compared to the cases where you just simply returned to the past.

*It's really driving me crazy when I didn't even ask for this.*

I pressed down on my forehead.

...If this theory was correct, the fact that the death condition specifically named ‘Park Moondae’ in this status abnormality seemed to considerably correlate.

Was this not basically a sign that from now on, if I'm not able to overcome the status abnormality, it would make sure I wasn't able to use Park Moondae's body?

*Fuck.*

...Would it be over with this one?

I didn't know anymore. Even in addition to that, there were many other irregularities from the status window's error to the coins.

*The coins can't even be checked.*

There was no news about it after the pop-up saying that I received it appeared.

I even shamefully shouted 'Inventory' out loud as a command, but there was no change.

Cha Eugene, who happened to pick it up even with earphones on, only responded with 'Are there albums left in stock?'

*Damn it.*

My face was getting hot just thinking about it. I roughly pet the dog as it rubbed its head against me.

*Is there no one else with a status window?*

It made me want to tell the bastard sitting in front of me to try shouting, 'Status window'.

Of course, instead of acting on that urge, I just got up from my seat.

*It would be a problem if a status window actually appeared, and it would be a problem even if it didn't.*

Let's not provoke more of this. Since I achieved my purpose here, I should keep anything provocative low and dig up more next time.

But suddenly, the dog began to whine.

"*...Kyung!*"

Did he not want me to get up?

"Looks like Kongi wanted to play more."

"...Kongi?"

"Yes."

It was a more native name than I thought. I was good with dogs, but the fact that that guy named him felt a bit gross.

At that moment, Cheongryeo spoke up as if to emphasize it.

"It was his original name."

"......"

I got what he meant.

"Okay."

I patted the dog on the head, which had most likely been called Kongi for quite some time, and then stood up.

"Let's play some more."

"Forget it."

I felt drained. There was no need to talk to this guy any more than this.

I headed towards the entrance fence.

The guy following me with the dog in his arms suddenly asked me a question at the entrance.

"Hmm... Come to think of it, you must have an original name too, Moondae-ssi."

"...!"

"Or could it have been the same name as now? Haha."

I answered rather nonchalantly.

...It didn't really matter. After all, my records couldn't be found anyway.

"Ryu Gunwoo."

"Hmm."

The guy made an odd expression.

"It's better than Park Moondae."

"...I guess so."

I couldn't deny it.

Still, Park Moondae also was, in its own way... a pretty good name when you hear it.

*In the end, the fans also seem to like it because it's unique.*

Anyway, I walked past the fence of the outdoor café and started to head home.

"*Awoooong-!*"

I could hear the dog howling rather mournfully until I turned around the corner.

I rubbed the back of my neck.

*It wasn’t a complete waste.*

At least I had a theory as to why the status abnormality appeared again.

I hate to admit it... but it was definitely easier to get results with an extra head. That guy wasn’t making empty claims anymore, either.

However, it was more disturbing to hear that this bizarre phenomenon had happened to other people one after another.

*Why did the status window only appear to me?*

It really felt like I was the main character of some web novel.
