# Chapter 326 - Independent Label, I

When I made a deal with the general manager for the establishment of an independent label upon winning the grand prize, I deliberately set strict criteria for the award ceremony.

It was to lower the company's guard. If the goal seemed too difficult to achieve within the timeframe, they would be more likely to sign the contract.

Let's look at the grand prize we won.

It was received at an awards ceremony hosted by a music platform, where that year's sales accounted for 75% of the criteria.

*It's flawless.*

We'd met the requirement beyond any dispute.

And there was a guy who said this before me.

"We... We can be independent now!"

As soon as the manager disappeared from the practice room today, Bae Sejin shouted as if he had been waiting for it.

"I-independence...?"

"Are you moving out of the dorm? Is that what you mean by independence?"

Of course, the others gave him strange looks, and Bae Sejin's face turned red, but he stood firm.

"No, not that... Do you guys remember? Park Moondae had the company sign a contract. If we win a grand prize, we'll be able to set up a label!"

Well done. Another guy had brought it up first, so it was easier to talk about. I nodded to those who looked back at me.

"That's right. I asked them to turn our dedicated management team into an independent label and affiliate company."

"Ohhhh."

"We've been through so much lately that I forgot all about it..." Kim Raebin said.

Most of them nodded, seemingly receptive to the idea.

It had been quite a while since the damn car accident, and our resentment toward the company had softened a lot, but that didn't mean we'd come to think any better of them.

"Okay. Sejin, thanks for bringing it up. We need to talk to the company," Ryu Chungwoo said.

"Ahem, that's right. So, let's talk right away when the manager comes back."

However, not everyone agreed with this statement.

Keun Sejin glanced between Bae Sejin and the practice room mirror several times, then spoke in a deliberately friendly tone.

"Oh, that sounds really nice, but once awards season is over, why don't we talk about it then? We have a lot to prepare now..."

"What are you talking about? We have to bring it up right away so they can't back out...!"

"......"

Keun Sejin's lips twitched for a moment, but then he spoke softly again.

"Think about it, hyung. Don't you think we'll win a few more grand prizes this year?"

*Hmm. He has a point.*

"There are many award ceremonies at the beginning of next year, and if we win a few more grand prizes, get everything in order, and then talk to the company, they really won't be able to back out. It'll be a done deal!"

"Oooh."

Keun Sejin gave Cha Eugene a thumbs-up in response, then drove his point home.

"So, right now, I think it'd be better for us to focus on preparing for the upcoming awards ceremonies. After all, the stage is the most important~ Of course, this is just my opinion."

"......"

And, as if these words had struck a nerve, Bae Sejin fell silent for a moment before opening his mouth.

"...It doesn't mean I'll neglect to prepare for the stage. I'll do my best."

"That's not what I meant... No, that's not the point."

Two people who simply didn't get along were trying their best to be polite to each other. I ended the conversation at that point.

"It's just a question of whether we bring it up now or a little later. Let's not make a big deal out of it."

Wasn't there only one answer anyway? I crossed my arms.

"Because no matter what we decide, we're setting up an independent label."

"......"

"Th-that's right."

"Oh, we should."

*What? What's the matter?*

"Hmm... So, shall we do it by majority vote?"

As suggested by Ryu Chungwoo, who was trying to smooth things over, the small dispute was neatly settled with a vote.

The result was a close call.

"It's 4:3."

Bae Sejin, Seon Ahyeon, and Ryu Chungwoo voted for talking to the company right away.

And... One additional vote decided the winning side.

"Moondae, you want to talk to them right away too, right?"

"Yes."

It was me.

"...!"

Ryu Chungwoo immediately declared.

"More of us want to bring it up right away. Then, let's arrange a meeting with the company."

"Sejin-hyung sounded cool! The vote was weird—"

"Hey, what's wrong with you, Eugene! We have to accept the results fair and square!"

Keun Sejin smiled and raised both hands.

"All right. If that's the team's opinion, I'll go along with it. But everyone will practice as hard as usual, right?"

"Of course!" Kim Raebin agreed.

"That's a given."

Practice resumed in such a warm atmosphere.

But after a while...

I went to the bathroom during practice and got caught in a headlock by Keun Sejin, who followed me.

"Moondaemoondae, this is betrayal...! We have to take things slowly and do them properly. Why are you being so impatient? Have you already talked to the company without us knowing?"

As expected, he hadn't accepted the result at all. This guy had pretended to be fine with it, but he was pissed.

I barely managed to slip out of his hold and spoke.

"It's not like that."

"Then?"

I rubbed the back of my neck and murmured.

"I just want these bastards to get our label up and running as soon as possible..."

"......"

No, even if it wasn't necessarily because of the mission, it was annoying to see the way the company was being run.

Crossing the line once or twice was one thing. Doing it every time was also a talent.

Keun Sejin let out a sigh.

"Yeah, Moondae, I suppose it's understandable that you feel that way..."

*Why? Oh, so I get a pass because I was the one most badly injured in the car accident?*

*It means he doesn't understand the guy who brought this up in the first place.*

Bae Sejin.

I'd been thinking that their relationship was starting to fray again, but especially during promotions or the year-end and New Year season, when they were emotionally and physically exhausted, their patience wore thin.

The tacit hierarchy within the group had gradually broken down over the years. Keun Sejin no longer had the upper hand as he once did, so new friction arose precisely because they were equals.

*Hmm.*

I was about to defend Bae Sejin myself, but instead of ruining the atmosphere, I spoke in a roundabout way.

"Don't worry, I'll make sure nothing gets in the way of practice."

"I know... But it's just one more thing to worry about, isn't it?"

Keun Sejin sighed as he walked alongside me.

"All in all, everyone is so impatient. Label independence is no joke, and the company won't let us go easily... Why are we giving them time to deal with it in advance... Hoo."

Yes, he had a point. We couldn't just trust the contract. We needed to have everything in order first.

I honestly admitted.

"In fact, if we're thinking rationally, you're right."

"Hey, you should have said that earlier!"

*Then I would've influenced the vote, bastard.*

I knew how he felt about that, though, so I let him in on what I was thinking.

"The problem is that the company has probably had the same rational thought."

"......"

Keun Sejin kept his mouth shut for a moment, but soon opened his mouth with a face as if he had been hit in the back of the head.

"They already know."

"Yes."

How could the company forget that we'd brought up the idea of leaving when we were such a major source of income for them?

There must have been a related meeting the moment we won the grand prize.

"That's why I think bringing it up sooner rather than later is the way to go."

"...Hoo."

Lee Sejin smiled bitterly.

"They're going to do everything they can to stall. It'll be annoying."

And just like that, things unfolded.

On the way back from practice, we brought up 'label establishment' through the manager, and the company's response came late the next day.

"Since it is the end of the year and the beginning of the next year, there are a lot of decisions to be made within the agency, so it seems difficult to set up a meeting right away."

*Bullsh\*t.*

I snorted. They were already starting with their tricks.

That was their first tactic. They were buying time.

First of all, they'd try to put off fulfilling their promise for as long as possible. In the meantime, they'd consult a lawyer.

While making offers such as, 'We'll give you your own label when your contract ends'.

*We didn't specify a deadline for their end of the deal in the contract.*

The moment I wrote that down, the company would've probably made a huge fuss, so I left it out.

We could put pressure on them by citing the principle of good faith and sincerity, but even if we established a label, it'd still be under T1, so we had to press them slowly and tactfully here.

*We can't look like we want to leave T1 entirely.*

As artists, we had to give the impression that we simply wanted the freedom to demonstrate our full potential.

"Hmm..."

I went back and forth in the hallway of the practice room, briefly organizing my thoughts. I was thinking of going in before the break was over.

It was then.

*Click.*

"Excuse me."

The guy who opened the practice room door and came out spoke to me. It was Bae Sejin.

*What.*

He closed the door, looked around, walked towards me, and muttered softly.

"...Thank you."

"...?"

"That, thank you for agreeing with me!!"

*Oh, yesterday.*

Bae Sejin exhaled, trying to calm his agitation, then continued speaking quite seriously.

"I actually thought it wouldn't work. Some of the members seem to think that I lack experience and can't grasp the situation..."

*Right.*

Just like when he'd pushed for us to sue the company, Bae Sejin had seen his opinions turned down by the team a few too many times.

"No. You and I had the same thoughts. Thank you for speaking up first."

"...Yes."

Only then did Bae Sejin's expression relax. He seemed quietly pleased.

*He didn't have to come all the way out here just to thank me...*

Indeed, he was rash, sensitive, and direct, with a soft spot for anyone who supported him.

Despite how unwavering he seemed, he was the type who could easily be fooled and taken advantage of.

"...Hmm."

*Wait a moment.*

"Why, why?!"

No, he reminded me of something.

*...I think I did something similar while I was in Ryu Gunwoo's body.*

When we met in front of the bathroom sink.

*– I really enjoyed your performance earlier. You always have such a great smile on your face.*

I'd encouraged Bae Sejin at a time when he wasn't getting much recognition.

*Surely he's not so easily won over by acknowledgment or compliments that he still remembers what I said back then.*

I doubted myself when I thought of the guy who looked up things like 'Bae Sejin's stage history' on WeTube, but I soon dismissed the thought.

*No way.*

No matter how much criticism he'd faced, he had been popular enough to become a TeSTAR member. There should've been more than one or two occasions when people acknowledged him like that.

I continued with what we were talking about.

"Just, judging by the way the company is acting, they don't seem keen on letting us go."

"Right... Right? They're doing this on purpose! Should we, uh, be a little more forceful...?"

"Wait a minute."

I stopped the bastard.

"We don't have to be more heavy-handed. Just... We have to move first."

"...?"

Actually, I'd just come up with an idea. There was a reason I hadn't specified a deadline in the contract.

*The power dynamic between us and the company had broken down long ago.*

It was something I had thought about before, but we shouldn't create tension with T1 while setting up our label.

However, what about 'T1 Stars', a subsidiary that was hastily put together to manage TeSTAR and would lose more than half its revenue if we became independent?

*As long as we had the justification, we could walk all over them.*

I smiled and opened my mouth.

"Hyung, I have an idea."

* * *

A few days later, at ToneA, the awards ceremony hosted by T1.

After performing in matching modern suits on a stage set featuring a very antique fountain, we received the award we'd expected.

Artist of the Year Award.

"Congratulations, TeSTAR!"

Flower petals fluttered down amid cheers. This was our second time receiving a grand prize, and the guys, a little more used to it now, went up onto the stage smiling brightly.

*Honestly, it'd be a lie to say we hadn't expected this.*

"Thank you. It is a great honor to receive such a big award in front of ToneA's K-pop listeners around the world. I've been told this is being broadcast live in 32 countries, and above all, I want to dedicate this win to Loviewers..."

I listened as Kim Raebin took a breath and poured out his thoughts, then the other members who hadn't gotten a chance to speak last time gave their speeches.

And at the end, when the teleprompter was telling us to wrap it up.

When everyone thought that our speeches were over.

"Thank you~"

"Thank you!"

I hurriedly grabbed the microphone with a bright expression, as if I had just remembered something. 

And I went for it.

"We are launching a label to commemorate our grand prize!"

"...!"

*Sorry, but you didn't include a confidentiality clause in our additional contract.*

*What are you going to do about it now?*

It was enough to say that the terms of the contract had already been fulfilled, that we'd told the company we expected them to follow through, and that we mentioned it in the heat of the moment.

"Wow!!"

"Please look forward to it~"

The members were laughing happily and adding words one by one, as if they saw nothing wrong with this remark at all. We'd pulled it off.

*Good.*

Moreover, this public declaration did more than just head off their objections—it was something the company could never take back.

*I'm already looking forward to the reaction.*

"I would like to thank the company once again for supporting us in every way possible. We'll be back with even better music. Thank you!"

I smiled and put the microphone down.

And from that very evening, articles started going up at an incredible pace.
