# Chapter 321

The buzz of people under the stage, the noise of the equipment, and the flickering lights of the cheering rods stimulated all five senses.

I was back.

"......"

Someone grabbed my shoulder. When I turned my head, I saw Lee Sejin and the faces around me.

This guy was laughing, but his grip was getting stronger. It seemed that this was the guy who called me earlier. 

*I must have stumbled.*

When I debuted after ⟨Idol Inc.⟩, I remembered that I almost collapsed after watching the first truth check.

I didn't know if it was a similar situation, but the difference in the time I felt was so big.

Suddenly, I quickly scanned the faces of the guys standing behind me.

*It's been a long time.*

From Ryu Chungwoo to Bae Sejin, who stood on the outskirts. In fact, I met Bae Sejin a few days ago, but it was surprising that he had a different impression from the guy at the beginning of his debut.

*I've seen it for a long time.*

It had been 4 years.

As a student, it was no different than going to college together after graduating from the same high school.

I came this far by forming a team with six strangers my age. It was a rare experience.

However, I had an irrational thought that I could not have made a better team than this even if I had chosen them with my own eyes with a status window.

It was a rare thing.

I laughed in my heart when I saw Kim Raebin with his pupils shaking.

*It's as he said.*

– Although we did not meet for the purpose of friendship, I believe that a true sense of solidarity can grow within!

There was more to this than simply wanting to keep doing it because of the good results.

And the same went for the lightsticks shaking in front of me.

I nodded slightly to the guys standing behind me, then turned to the front again.

People who supported TeStar.

And, as TeStar's Park Moondae said, the one in front of me was me.

The conclusion I reached after going through all those situations was still the same. 

"......"

I couldn't do this. Perhaps because of the words I heard in the imaginary world just before, my head struggled with my own emotions.

*It's embarrassing.*

It was then.

All of a sudden, a text popped up on the prompter in front of me.

+=
Please express your feelings quickly.
=+

"...!"

Yes, my feelings.

I came to my senses. The fact that I had something to do right now raised my head to reality.

I was frozen for about 3 or 4 seconds. It had already been a long time since I'd been in the middle of an awards ceremony, but I was going to take my time. 

*Still, it's our first grand prize after having such good results, so I hope they understand.*

I smiled and grabbed the mic.

"Did I take too long? Sorry. I'm nervous."

Small laughter erupted from the audience.

I didn't think it was a funny remark... Well, they accepted it well, so I moved on.

"When I look back at the moments I was active in TeStar, it seems like it was a very long time, and it seems like I debuted just yesterday."

I took a deep breath, then spoke again.

"It was like that moment when I decided to become an idol."

It was a journey that started because of an absurd threat of sudden death.

"Honestly, as an outsider, I wasn't sure or expecting how far I could go. Just... I thought I could do it, so I appeared on the program."

I wouldn't have thought of doing it without the sudden death threat. But...

Right before I came to my senses, I remembered what I had said to Keundal.

– I didn't know that I wanted to be an idol either, but I guess I did.

I could imagine in a very specific way the perspective of the person taking pictures of me from under this podium, avoiding security.

Because I had done it a lot.

And at that time, I did not bother to trace the cause of my satisfaction. Because I couldn't imagine the sight of looking down directly from the stage beyond that point of view.

"But after meeting these members and meeting the fans, TeStar came all the way here."

But what if standing on the stage in person was more genuine and stimulating than watching the stage from the audience?

That... What I did as a data collector was exactly the vicarious satisfaction.

And if I thought that finding this real pleasure was actually thanks to my 'infinite potential'... It was an illusion.

*It's too much.*

Numerous variables came into play. And I wasn't the only one who moved that variable.

Because idols are a group.

"Regardless of this award, I think it was a good thing that I tried to become an idol at that time... I'm very happy."

I didn't expect such embarrassing words to come out of my mouth, but maybe everyone would understand since it was a grand prize, after all. 

"I believe the members would think so too."

I put my arm around Seon Ahyeon, who was standing next to me.

"...!"

*...Why does he look frightened?*

I thought he would take it naturally because the atmosphere was burning, but he almost looked embarrassed.

But it wasn't a hateful look, so I looked around and said what I had to say.

"Thank you."

"......"

Then, strange expressions, as if they were about to cry, began to appear on their faces, and naturally, something like a group ceremony began to happen.

Encouragement and a sense of achievement for having worked hard.

*Oh my god.*

In the audience seats, the lightsticks shook like crazy.

I found myself among the seven members hugging each other. 

And I was thinking of another person.

*Did he go in safely?*

I finished my last words between their arms, thinking of the guy who was probably watching us on TV.

"Thank you so much for this award."

It was a general thank you.

* * *

A fanfare burst out, signaling the end of our speeches and telling us to vacate our seats, and we went backstage holding the trophy.

As soon as the award ceremony was over, our stage continued after a brief preparation time.

That was probably what the first grand prize placement was for.

I thought silently as I moved with the trophy still in my hand.

*We must give up the next award ceremony unconditionally.*

It was because when reason came back, I realized that I had been overexcited.

*Next time, I'll make those who haven't been able to give a speech prepare separately...*

But I couldn't keep calculating. This was because an urgent question came before I even arrived at the place of change.

First of all, Lee Sejin.

"Park Moondae, are you okay?"

"...?"

"You just almost fell. If you feel unwell, tell me. Let's get painkillers and leave."

That was why he grabbed my shoulder. I understood his behavior.

*I'll start by grasping the situation.*

"Did I almost fall?"

"I-I thought you were about to collapse..."

Seon Ahyeon muttered with a blue face. I seemed to have staggered more than expected.

*Oh, my.*

I'd have to see if this excuse worked. I sighed first and rubbed the back of my neck.

"Sorry. Since this is our first grand prize... I couldn't even remember what to say, so I think it was because I was nervous."

"...You said it was because you were nervous?"

"You...?"

Why were these bastards so suspicious?

I answered as calmly as possible.

"Yes."

"Wait a minute."

"Manager, please give me a thermometer!"

Questions rained down like bombs. I think I knew roughly what my impression was during that time.

I raised both hands.

"I'm fine. Just... I really like it."

I looked around at the faces of those guys who seemed to be a little shocked, and smiled.

"Because it's the first grand prize. I know how to be moved."

"Park Moondae..."

Astonishment disappeared, and warmth permeated. I thought this would work because this was also everyone else's first grand prize, too.

"...Okay. Things like that happen, too."

"It's a little late to say this, but I think it was a wonderful acceptance speech!"

Good. All the easily persuaded people had been convinced. 

*...Well, it wasn't a lie.*

It was true that I felt good. I handed over the trophy to those who were intoxicated with the grand prize atmosphere.

"Let's fight next year too."

"Yes, yes!"

"Good!"

Ryu Chungwoo seemed to be enjoying the atmosphere, his face showing how happy he was with the teamwork, and calmly ended his remarks. 

"I'll take your temperature anyway."

"......"

He could do whatever he wanted.

I changed into my outfit and had my temperature checked. It was normal. It was absurd, but it seemed like I had overcome some kind of hurdle.

"13 minutes left~"

But at the same time, I realized the real problem.

*Damn it.*

I used my head and finally stabbed Seon Ahyeon.

"Ahyeon-ah."

"Uh, huh?"

And I spoke quietly.

"Can we quickly check the revised choreography right now..."

"Moondaemoondae, why? Can't you remember the choreography?"

He was a quick-witted guy.

"...After the speech, my tension was relieved, so I want to double-check."

"Ah, that's right."

It wasn't a lie.

*Rather than dancing for a few days, I haven't sung any songs.*

It was a revised year-end choreography that I had practiced for just one week right before I entered Ryu Gunwoo's body, and I was going to use it now.

I was breaking out in a cold sweat because I was afraid I wouldn't be able to remember it properly on stage. 

"Anyway, let's do it."

"Yes! L-let's do it right away...!"

Fortunately, there was no time, so instead of other conversations, the choreography lesson started right away.

Once the two dance-line members started helping me, I quickly got the movements down. 

"Here you kick, spin... Hit your toe with a point and go over..."

"Moondae was half a beat fast, be careful!"

"Yes."

"I know that choreography best. Hyung, I'll show you!"

"Eugene, would you like to go get some drinks?"

So, with the help of the others, I completely relearned the choreography in three minutes. 

It was comfortable to live alone, but the feeling of being with many people was not bad.

* * *

That evening, the prestigious music awards ceremony was being broadcast for the first time at the end of the year.

They seemed thrilled. Look at them running like a pack of dogs.

Looking at the real-time comments where expectations for humanity disappeared, the office worker who took pictures of Lee Sejin and Park Moondae, the twin fansite master, clicked her tongue.

But this was a normal thing, and something else bothered her more.

*I can't believe he's feeling that emotional.*

Park Moondae had handled everything with the cold precision of a knife until now, but only this time he acted as if he couldn't stand the overwhelming feeling.

Before the acceptance speech, he couldn't think of what to say, stumbled, looked back at the members, and even his speech was unusually long... He did everything he wouldn't normally do.

She didn't know if there was a story or if the object was so meaningful...

*It wasn't bad.*

The twin fansite master nodded, saying that she could understand why Lee Sejin had to concede to Park Moondae.

Since Park Moondae had always presented a calm image to the public, it seemed like he had a fourth dimension now, so even shaking as this would come as a charm rather than an annoyance.

It was no different from showing the public a scene with an effect similar to crying at a concert.

In fact, by the time Park Moondae's speech was over, the reaction to the comments had changed quite a bit.

★$

Furthermore, even the last act that seemed to confirm the members' affection for their group...

There seemed to be something about that series of events that tugged at people's heartstrings. 

*This kind of thing works well.*

Compilation videos of singers reacting emotionally to getting their first No. 1 on the music charts after debut weren't circulating on WeTube for nothing. 

People liked the fall of a star, but they also liked when stars couldn't beat their emotions.

To suit the tastes of such people, TeStar's emotions were laid bare. 

The twin fansite master nodded while being quite satisfied.

*Good job.*

Park Moondae didn't really calculate that, and he was expecting a very bad reaction.

In any case, contrary to his expectations, TeStar's grand prize acceptance speech was successfully received as a touching moment.

The awards season was only just beginning.

And this season, TeStar's impact was going to be huge.
