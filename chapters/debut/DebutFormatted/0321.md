# Chapter 321 - A First Grand Prize

All five senses slowly readjusted as I took in the buzz of people in front of the stage, the noise of the equipment, and the swaying lightsticks.

I was back.

"......"

Someone grabbed my shoulder. When I turned my head, I saw Keun Sejin and the other members looking at me.

This guy was smiling, but his grip on my shoulder was tight. It seemed he'd been the one to call out to me. 

*I must have stumbled.*

I remembered how I'd nearly passed out at our ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ debut after seeing my first flashback when I revealed the truth.

Maybe the situation was similar, but the difference in how much time I'd experienced was huge.

I glanced over at the other members' faces.

*It's been a while for me.*

Everyone was here, from Ryu Chungwoo standing at the edge of the group to Bae Sejin. I'd actually seen Bae Sejin just a few days ago, but the difference between his expression now and back at the start of our career was jarring.

*I've known him for a long time now.*

We'd been a group for four years.

As a student, it was no different than going to college together after graduating from the same high school.

Seven complete strangers, none of whom had chosen the others, had formed a team and come this far. It was a rare experience.

I suddenly had the emotional thought that I couldn't have put together a better team than this, even if I'd chosen the members myself using the status window and their stats.

It was a strange feeling.

I stifled a laugh when I saw Kim Raebin's unsteady gaze.

*It's as you said.*

*– Even though we didn't meet for the sake of friendship, I believe we can still become very good friends!*

There was something between us that went beyond simply wanting to stay a group because we were doing well.

The same went for the people waving their lightsticks in front of us.

I nodded slightly to the members, then turned back around.

These people were all rooting for TeSTAR.

And I was standing before them as TeSTAR's Park Moondae.

After everything I'd been through, that was still the bottom line. 

"......"

This was bad. Perhaps because of the words I'd heard in the mindscape only moments earlier, I was getting really emotional.

*Not good.*

It was then.

All of a sudden, some words appeared on the teleprompter in front of me.

"...!"

*Right.*

I came to my senses. Having something I needed to do right away pulled me back into reality.

I'd stood frozen in place for about three or four seconds—as if people didn't already complain at every awards ceremony that the speeches were too long! 

*Still, this is the first grand prize we've earned fair and square based on our results, so I hope they understand.*

I grinned and grabbed the mic.

"I paused a bit too long there, didn't I? Sorry about that. I guess I'm nervous."

Small laughter erupted from the audience.

I didn't think I'd said anything particularly funny... Well, I had nothing to complain about if they'd taken it well.

"It feels like we've been TeSTAR for a very long time, but at the same time, it seems so short."

I paused to take a breath.

"The moment I decided to try to become an idol feels like just yesterday."

It was a journey that started because of an absurd threat of sudden death.

"Honestly, as an outsider who knew so little about the industry, I wasn't confident I'd make it very far. But I was given the chance, so I appeared on the program."

I wouldn't have even considered trying without the sudden death threat. *But then again...*

I remembered what I'd said to Keundal right before I came to my senses.

*– I didn't know I wanted to be an idol either, but I guess I did.*

I could picture exactly how the people with hidden cameras in front of the stage were filming me at this very moment, avoiding security.

Because I'd done it so many times myself.

Back then, I hadn't bothered to examine what made it so satisfying. I'd never imagined I might one day be looking down from the stage at people filming just as I had.

"But after meeting these members and our fans, we became TeSTAR, and look how far we've come already."

Standing on stage myself turned out to be far more genuinely exciting than watching from the audience.

That meant what I'd done as a data collector really was a way of enjoying it vicariously.

Discovering that joy for myself thanks to my 'Infinite Potential' trait might have made the taste of victory even sweeter... But that wasn't the case.

*Not at all.*

So many variables had come together just right, and I wasn't the only one responsible.

We were a group, after all.

"Regardless of this award, I think I made the right decision back then... I'm very happy."

I hadn't expected such embarrassing words to come out of my mouth, but we were receiving a grand prize, after all. Everyone would understand. And even if they didn't, it didn't matter. 

"I believe the other members feel the same way."

I put my arm around Seon Ahyeon, who was standing next to me.

"...!"

*...He looks taken aback.*

I'd expected him to take it naturally because of the atmosphere, then realized I'd nearly embarrassed us both.

But he didn't seem unhappy about my arm around him, so I looked around at the others and finished my speech.

"Thanks, guys."

"......"

Then strangely moved expressions began to appear on their faces, as if they were about to cry, and the crowd suddenly broke into applause all at once.

Encouragement for our hard work, and a celebration of what we'd accomplished.

*Oh my god.*

In the audience, the lightsticks shook like crazy amid loud cheering.

I accepted that I was one of the seven professional idols up on stage, hugging each other. 

And I was thinking of another person.

*Did he make it back safely?*

With my arms still around the others, I finished my speech, thinking of the guy who was probably watching us on TV.

"Thank you so much."

It was a thank-you meant for a great many people.

***

A fanfare burst from the speakers, signaling us to finish our speech and get off the stage as soon as possible. We went backstage carrying the trophy.

After a brief break, we'd be performing.

That was probably why the first grand prize presentation had been placed there in the schedule.

I thought silently as I walked, the trophy still in my hand.

*I have to make sure the other members get a turn to speak at the next grand prize, no matter what.*

Now that reason was returning, I realized I'd gotten a little carried away.

*People are probably criticizing me online for that right now. Next time, I'll have the ones who haven't given speeches before prepare in advance...*

But my thoughts were interrupted by a barrage of questions before I'd even reached the changing room.

First of all, Keun Sejin.

"Park Moondae, are you okay?"

"...?"

"You nearly fell over just now. Tell us if you're not feeling well. They can give you a painkiller before we perform."

That was why he grabbed my shoulder. I understood his behavior.

*I need to figure out how bad it was.*

"Did I almost fall?"

"I-I-I thought you were about to collapse..."

Seon Ahyeon muttered, pale. It seemed I'd staggered more than I'd thought.

*Uh oh.*

I'd have to come up with an excuse. I sighed and rubbed the back of my neck.

"Sorry. Since this is our first grand prize... I couldn't think of what to say. I guess I was nervous."

"You were... nervous?"

"You...?"

*Why were these bastards so suspicious?*

I answered as calmly as possible.

"Of course."

"Stop right there."

"Manager, can you get us a thermometer, please?"

Their skepticism was getting out of hand. I think I had a rough idea of what kind of impression they had of me.

I raised both hands.

"I'm fine. Just... It felt really great."

I looked around at the faces of those guys, who seemed a little shocked, and grinned.

"It's our first grand prize. Hey, I can get emotional too."

"Park Moondae..."

Their confusion disappeared, replaced by warmth. I'd known this would work because everyone was excited about our first grand prize.

"Yeah... It makes sense that you feel that way," Ryu Chungwoo said.

"It's a little late to say this, but I think it was a wonderful acceptance speech!"

*Good. The more soft-hearted ones are already convinced.* 

*...Well, it wasn't a lie.*

It was true that I felt good. I handed over the trophy to the members high on dopamine from winning the grand prize.

"Let's keep it up next year too."

"Y-y-yes!"

"Very good!" Cha Eugene said.

Ryu Chungwoo smiled proudly at the display of teamwork, then concluded gently. 

"I'm still going to take your temperature, Moondae."

"......"

*As you please.*

I changed into my outfit and had my temperature checked. It was normal. It was absurd, but it seemed like I had overcome some kind of hurdle.

"13 minutes left~" someone shouted.

But at the same time, I realized the real problem.

*Damn it.*

I racked my brain, but in the end, I had to approach Seon Ahyeon.

"Ahyeon-ah."

"Y-yeah?"

And I spoke quickly.

"Can you give me a quick run-through of the revised choreography?"

"Moondaemoondae, why? Can't you remember the choreography?" Keun Sejin asked.

*You're too perceptive for your own good.*

"...I think winning and giving the speech got me a little too worked up, so I want to double-check."

"Ah, that's right."

*It wasn't a lie.*

*I hadn't sung or danced for days.*

It was a revised choreography we'd only be performing at this particular year-end awards show, and I'd practiced it for just one week right before I entered Ryu Gunwoo's body.

I was breaking out in a cold sweat because I was afraid I wouldn't be able to remember it properly on stage. 

"Anyway, give me a refresher, please."

"Yes! L-let's do it right away...!" Seon Ahyeon said.

Fortunately, perhaps because there wasn't much time left, we got right to the choreography without further ado.

With two dance-line members helping me, I quickly got the movements down. 

"Here you kick, spin... Use the tip of your foot here, then move on..."

"Moondae, you went half a beat too fast! Be careful!"

"Yes."

"I know that choreography better than anyone else. Hyung, I'll teach you!" Cha Eugene said.

"Eugene, would you like to go get some drinks?"

So, with the help of the others, I memorized the choreography again in three minutes. 

I was the sort of person who preferred to get things done on my own, but it felt nice having so many people ready to help me.

*I can't believe he's feeling that emotional.*

Park Moondae had always handled everything with the cold precision of a knife, but strangely, he looked deeply moved today.

He'd paused too long before his acceptance speech, nearly lost his footing, looked back at the members, and even gone off on an unusually long tangent... He'd done everything he wouldn't normally do.

She didn't know if there was some reason for his behavior or if the grand prize really meant that much to him...

*But his speech wasn't half bad...*

The twin fansite master nodded to herself. She could understand why Lee Sejin had let Park Moondae give the speech.

Since the public saw Park Moondae as almost inhumanly logical, seeing him get so emotional this once would come across as charming rather than annoying.

It had pretty much the same effect as bursting into tears in front of the public.

In fact, by the time Park Moondae's speech was over, the reaction to the comments had changed quite a bit.

*This kind of thing works well.*

Compilation videos of singers reacting emotionally to their first win on a music show after debut weren't circulating on WeTube for nothing. 

People liked watching the downfall of a star, but they also liked watching stars get overwhelmed by a great accomplishment.

And TeSTAR's emotions had felt genuine, laid bare in a way that perfectly suited their tastes.
