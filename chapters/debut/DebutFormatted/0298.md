# Chapter 298 - The Future People, I

Idols were extremely busy before and after their promotional cycles.

When they hadn't made a name for themselves, they were busy trying to do so, and once they had, they were busy capitalizing on it.

In that sense, getting a rookie on the verge of debut, a fourth-year idol with several hit songs, and a tenth-year idol in the middle of a stadium tour together in one place at short notice was nearly impossible.

[That's why we're doing a video call? Amazing.]

"Yes."

I massaged my forehead as I stared at the split screen on my laptop.

The left pane was empty, and on the right, Gold 2 sat wearing a headset, looking excited.

It was 2:00 AM now.

This was the only time when all three of us were available.

"You're somewhere soundproof, right?"

[Yes. I'm in the dorm's dressing room right now, and the door is locked! But even if someone overhears, wouldn't they think we're talking about a drama?]

It took a lot of self-control to say only this.

"Still, be careful."

[Yes!]

This guy was too carefree, perhaps because he'd only gone back 11 months and had done well on the ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ stock exchange.

He seemed to have his wits about him, but his attitude... Well, it was his life, so he'd take care of it.

[Our group finally has a name. Did you hear? Spacer!]

"Yes, congratulations."

After chatting about his personal affairs for a while, Gold 2, Kwon Heeseung, took a deep breath and got down to business.

[I'm a little excited. A meeting of people from the future... This should definitely be a drama on NetFlux!]

*Is that right?*

[And seriously... Wow, I didn't think that sunbaenim would actually come.]

*How did I end up in this situation?*

I checked the username marked 'online' in the friends list, belonging to the third person we were waiting for.

@ll@[CHR]@ll@

Anyone could see it, but this guy was Cheongryeo. 

Yes. Instead of keeping these bastards apart and in the dark while I collected information, I'd told them from the beginning and arranged a three-way meeting.

There was only one reason.

[Come to think of it, I've done some research into my situation on my own.]

Gold 2, this bastard... If I hadn't figured out what was going on, he would've been found out within a few months at most.

*Look at this bullshit.*

[There was this online forum called 'People from the Future,' right? But it turned out they were just role-playing... What kind of concept is that? Wow, I was really disappointed.]

*Cheongryeo would've gotten to him by the end of the year.*

I could guarantee it.

And could I have dealt with Cheongryeo when he eventually realized that I'd known all along and used him to learn more about our situation from Gold 2?

I thought for a moment, then nodded.

*Yes. I could have.*

*There was no reason I couldn't have.*

However, the outcome was so predictable and troublesome. There was no need to bring his ire down on myself. It was better to reveal the truth on a playing field I could control.

I clicked to add 'CHR', Cheongryeo, to the video call.

Soon, his face appeared on the screen, seemingly in a hotel room.

I'd heard he was in Argentina, and it seemed to be daytime there. Cheongryeo squinted at his screen for a moment before opening his mouth.

[Hello.]

[Heok, hello, sunbaenim...! It's really nice to meet you like this!]

[Yes. It's nice to meet you.]

With a smile on his face, he glanced at the corner of his screen, presumably where Gold 2's face was.

It looked like he was appraising him.

*I hope he doesn't get any stupid ideas.*

I recalled when I'd first relayed that information to him.

– *So you mean... the Rank 1 contestant from the latest season of ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ is another person who came back from the future?*

He hadn't gotten excited or shown undue interest. He'd just sounded bored as he said something absurd.

– *Interesting. Is this some kind of tradition now, starting with Hubaenim?*

*"I don't know if that's meant to be a joke, but quit it."*

– *And 11 months... Hmm. I guess I pity him.*

*"You pity him?"*

– *Haha. You'd need at least two years to really gain an advantage, wouldn't you? Well, I doubt that group will do very well. He probably doesn't have much useful information to offer... How are you going to use him?*

It seemed he'd concluded that Gold 2 wasn't a real competitor and didn't have much to offer as a trading partner.

Anyway, I honestly stated my purpose.

*"I don't need information about the future."*

– *Then what do you need?*

*"I'm trying to come up with a hypothesis as to why this is happening."*

The guy on the other end was silent for quite some time before he spoke again.

– *All right.*

And now he was participating in this video call.

"Since we've all gotten acquainted, let's get started."

[Yes, yes!]

[Haha, speak comfortably.]

Already tired, I opened my mouth, trying my best not to lose my patience with them.

"First... We need to get our timelines straight."

[..? Timelines?]

[Oh, the order.]

Cheongryeo put his hand on his chin and muttered.

[There should be no problem with the order. Let's see... First, Hubaenim, you were transported back four years ago, in December.]

Right. The day I woke up with Park Moondae's wallet in that old motel.

I nodded.

[And you?] Cheongryeo asked Gold 2.

[Yes, when I woke up, it was the end of October last year!]

[So you say. Then it still holds that only one person knows anything about the future at any given time.]

I froze for a moment as I drew a line in my spreadsheet.

"And what about you, sunbaenim?"

I was referring to the time he'd stood on the rooftop, staring at a sign as he smoked that winter night.

He smiled faintly.

[A few days before you returned.]

"All right..."

The pattern was strange.

Based on my memories, my knowledge of the future extended to July of last year.

*The day I found out I'd failed the civil service exam again, got drunk, and fell asleep.*

However, Gold 2 had come back from the future and woken up in October last year.

*July and October.*

*That's a three-month difference.*

This was different from Cheongryeo and Park Moondae's timelines, which had followed almost immediately one after the other.

But if that strange memory I'd seen the last time I used 'Reveal the Truth' was real... The timelines actually overlapped.

The process of giving up on the civil service exam and getting a job at a company had taken at least a year.

*Then, Gold 2 shouldn't have appeared already, claiming to know the future.*

Neither scenario made sense.

*If this was all going to fit together...*

"...!"

*Wait a second.* I had to change my way of thinking completely.

Okay. What if the point in the future that my knowledge extended to wasn't the criterion for this strange baton-passing?

There was another criterion that could just as plausibly explain the pattern.

"...Sunbaenim."

[Yes?]

I asked quietly.

"...When did you realize you'd cleared your final mission?"

[...]

I was referring to the date he'd cleared his status abnormality.

[Mission? We have missions?]

Ignoring Gold 2's question, Cheongryeo answered briefly.

[November 30.]

*Right.*

*And for me...*

It had been during the Chuseok holiday last year, in October. I still remembered the confetti erupting in the hologram.

★-
\
#><[Massive Success!]><#

User: Park Moondae (Ryu Gunwoo) has successfully removed all status abnormalities!
-★

I grinned. This was starting to make sense.

"So that's how it is."

[Any new ideas?] Cheongryeo asked.

"It seems like a new person appears when the existing person clears all the missions."

I erased the line, drew another, and shared my screen.

+$
**4 years ago**

$lat *Cheongryeo, end of November (missions cleared) - Park Moondae, mid-December (started missions)* lat$

**1 year ago**

$lat *Park Moondae, mid-October (missions cleared) - Kwon Heeseung, end of October (started missions)* lat$
$+

It fitted neatly.

[...]

Cheongryeo stared at the screen without a word, as though processing the information.

And soon he nodded.

[That's one way to look at it. But why does this matter to you?]

"Let's suppose... there's something here."

There was an element to all this that only I'd experienced.

I had access to a status window, and I'd seen with my own eyes how status abnormalities, which this guy called 'missions', appeared and disappeared. I'd also seen everyone's various special abilities.

"There's something that allows people to wake up in the past according to their wishes and gives them customized missions."

The operating system that had left me the status window and disappeared.

*The system.*

"What if there's only one of whatever that is, and the moment all the missions are cleared, it moves on to someone else?"

[Hmm.]

So, perhaps this chain of people returning to the past was based on who the system was with at any given time.

What if the system that left me after I'd cleared all my status abnormalities during last year's October holiday had entered Gold 2's body?

It had granted him his wish and given him a corresponding 'status abnormality.'

[Excuse me... Excuse me, could you tell me what these missions are...? I'm getting a little scared!]

Gold 2 whined.

"They're missions you have to complete in exchange for coming back to the past."

[What??]

Cheongryeo spoke in a flat tone, as though reading from a flyer.

[Think about what you feel you need to do right now.]

[Uh, um... Making a debut without any problems?]

[Not what you're planning, but what you think you need to achieve.]

[Wh-what we need to achieve? Uh... All our members getting along and being successful!]

[Hmm, really? I suppose that's better than having to get Rank 1 in this situation. Cheer up.]

[What?]

[Hmm? That was encouragement, wasn't it?]

*This crazy guy.*

I gave the frightened Gold 2 a quick explanation and got the conversation back on track.

Fortunately, he understood rather quickly. I guessed I'd been right about his wits.

[Uh uh, then... I don't know exactly what's going on, but something that sends people back to the past has been going in and out of our bodies?]

"Yes."

If I had to put it into words... It seemed like it was always in need of a host.

This mental parasite, which I called 'the system,' switched bodies while granting wishes.

But I still had no idea how it moved from one person to another.

"There must be a reason we were selected."

Gold 2 had a slightly expectant look on his face.

[Ah, maybe we all saved someone and nearly lost our lives doing something honorable... Could it be something like that? Maybe you have to deserve it?]

Cheongryeo smiled brightly.

[An interesting theory.]

*One that's obviously wrong.*

In my opinion... It had to be someone who was willing to die and had something they wanted to achieve by going back to the past.

*I'm sure there are plenty of people like that.*

Frankly, if it put its mind to it, it could find hundreds or thousands of people to switch to much sooner. There was no shortage of them.

There seemed to be some bias in terms of occupation or age, too... but it was hard to say for sure.

Was that enough?

After a few pointless musings and ideas flitted through my mind...

Cheongryeo opened his mouth again.

[Let's see... We've considered timing. Why don't we look at it from another angle?]

"What do you mean?"

[I mean location.]

"...!"

[You seem to like that idea.]

Location wasn't the first thing that came to mind with a supernatural phenomenon like this, but the idea could be useful if we refined it a little.

*If we considered that the system might be able to 'move'...*

"Distance."

I spoke quietly.

We'd focus on the dates when the system moved on.

"On the 11th, where were you, sunbaenim, when you realized you'd cleared your missions?"

[In the van. Probably... the Seobu Urban Expressway.]

I immediately opened the Maps app.

"...!"

[Where did Hubaenim wake up with knowledge of the future?] Cheongryeo asked.

"...Guro-dong."

On the map app, I could see the Seobu Urban Expressway passing right by Guro-dong.

"It's nearby."

I felt a brief flash of excitement.

Laughter came from the speakers.

[Ah, this is interesting. And where did you wake up?] Cheongryeo asked Gold 2.

[Oh, that Hangang Park! At Gwangnaru!]

Gold 2 had gained the system's help at Hangang Park in Gwangnaru.

And the place where I'd officially cleared my status abnormalities and the system had disappeared was Bae Sejin's house.

"...Cheonho-dong. Again, nearby."

[No way!!]

Bae Sejin's apartment was in Cheonho-dong.

With a view of the Han River.

*Fuck.*

It fit perfectly. 

*Time and distance.*

In the end, the system chose the candidate who was closest and whom it could reach in the shortest amount of time.

A voice mixed with laughter asked.

[So, what do you think? Have we deduced something here?]

I declared calmly.

"Yes, we have."

***

Thus, the three-way meeting ended with something concrete to show for it.

*Now we just have to figure out how the system selects its candidates, and we'll have a clearer picture of what's going on.*

A little more thought would make things clearer, but honestly, I hadn't expected us to make even this much progress.

[It's already this late? Sorry, but I've got to go!]

Gold 2, who'd been immersed in the discussion, checked the time and panicked at the thought of his early morning schedule. He ended the call first.

I was about to end the call too, since I needed at least a few hours of sleep.

"I guess we'll call it a day."

However, instead of getting ready to turn off the screen, Cheongryeo tapped on it.

*What, it's daytime over there, so you're not sleepy?*

[I don't understand.]

"You don't get what?"

He spoke in a monotone, tapping on the desk.

*Tuk-tuk.*

[Why the hurry to have this conversation? This sort of information isn't really all that useful to us.]

I was silent.

[Hubaenim would already know, of course. Analyzing this pattern doesn't mean we can figure out who's next. And it's not as though we can predict where that person will die in the future.]

Cheongryeo smiled.

[Or... Is there a concrete plan you're hoping to carry out?]

*Damn.*

I repressed a sigh.

"Well—"

Then, a knock was heard outside the door.

– *Knock knock.*

"...!"

"Moondae?"

It was the voice of my roommate.
