# Chapter 285 - A Battle for the Throne, I

It was the last thing I'd expected to hear... No, it was the exact opposite of what I'd expected.

*Do I need help?*

So, he wasn't pressing me for an explanation of why my statements didn't add up. He was asking out of concern for me...

*Is he just setting the mood?*

Was it just bait to get a more specific explanation out of me? I looked up to read Ryu Chungwoo's expression.

I immediately dismissed the thought. Ryu Chungwoo's expression was open and sincere.

Yeah, he wasn't that kind of guy.

He'd said that because he really wanted to help.

"...Hoo."

...I was bewildered.

*I never expected him to ask me that in this situation.*

It seemed that Ryu Chungwoo still felt deeply indebted to me after our car accident.

*I didn't consider that part in particular.*

Come to think of it, I'd even told him he could be grateful if he wanted to.

I remembered what I'd said to Ryu Chungwoo, who seemed weighed down by a sense of debt when we filmed that variety show on the deserted island.

*– Is that so? Well, let's say I saved hyung's life.*

*– ...!*

*– You can continue to be very grateful. If I need help, I'll ask you without hesitation, so be sure to help me out then.*

Ryu Chungwoo was simply living up to his word.

*...We were hiking then, too.*

It was kind of funny to hear my own words handed back to me in a similar setting.

"Hmm."

I rested my arms on the railing and smiled wryly.

Once I'd sorted through my thoughts... I felt even stranger. He was offering me a helping hand instead of doubting me.

*How odd.*

I was shaken, unsure what to say. But it didn't feel bad.

I answered slowly.

"First... thank you for saying that."

"......"

"You could've been angry with me."

"Not at all."

Ryu Chungwoo replied firmly, leaning comfortably against the railing himself.

"You didn't lie to me, and I wasn't harmed, so why would I be angry?"

What surprised me was that he believed I hadn't lied.

I was about to lose myself in a strange train of thought, but I caught myself.

I still had something I wanted to say.

"And as for my situation... It's difficult even for me to understand or explain everything. But things aren't so bad that I need help."

I pondered over my situation.

I had succeeded in this job, the status abnormalities had disappeared... and the guys I lived with were all good.

Life was going pretty well.

*No, it's better than I deserve.*

Risk management came with this job. And unlike suddenly dying because of a status abnormality, at least those risks had some context.

"All the difficult things are behind me. What I'm doing now is... just making things harder for myself. I'm trying to find out why the contradictions you mentioned came about."

I just didn't know why I'd ended up in this body, doing what I was doing, so I had to know the truth.

*If I don't know the cause, who knows when things will suddenly go wrong again?*

Ryu Chungwoo nodded.

"That's why you need information on 'Ryu Gunwoo'."

*He catches on quickly when it comes to things like this.*

"Yes. So you're already helping me, hyung."

Ryu Chungwoo gave a short laugh.

"Okay. Don't worry about that part. I'll do everything I can. Anything else?"

"That's all—"

*Oh, there is one thing.*

"I really need us to win the grand prize this year, so please cooperate with me."

That was the only way I could claim my mission reward, 'A conversation with Park Moondae'—a chance to speak with the real Park Moondae.

"What?"

Ryu Chungwoo finally looked a little shocked, but soon relaxed and smiled.

"That's what our team should be aiming for anyway, right?"

"I guess so."

He hadn't asked why I suddenly needed to win so badly... He probably thought I was playfully changing the subject.

*And I don't really have anything to say to correct that impression.*

But what should I say... There was a strange sense of liberation from just saying it so openly.

The tension left my shoulders.

"All right. I'll work even harder."

"Thank you."

We grinned at each other and looked out at the scenery again. Halfway up the mountain, it was quiet, with nobody around.

"It's a bit hot, but the weather is nice."

"Yes."

It was too hot to be truly pleasant, but I didn't bother pointing it out.

Even after that, Ryu Chungwoo didn't ask for any more details or explanations.

He did check one thing on the way down, though.

"Your circumstances match Ryu Gunwoo's... That's not a coincidence, is it?"

"No..."

"Alright."

And that was it. He dropped the subject entirely.

*Doesn't he feel at least a little frustrated?*

If it were me, I'd have demanded he tell me everything and let me decide for myself whether I understood it or not. His composure was amazing.

Instead, Ryu Chungwoo concluded the conversation with, 'You said no now, but if you need help in the future, feel free to tell me.'

Even that wasn't the end.

"Moondae, why don't we speak more casually?"

Ryu Chungwoo brought this up while driving us back to the dorm after we'd descended.

"Come to think of it, I'm not an athlete anymore. Maybe I've been making you treat me like I still am."

"Not at all. It's fine as it is."

*Don't be absurd.*

"Oh, if I spoke too casually, it wouldn't look good to people. It could've caused controversy for no reason."

If I'd done that right after debut... Hmm, Park Moondae would've been brutally criticized online for acting arrogant toward the leader just because he'd placed first on the audition show.

However, Ryu Chungwoo did not give up.

"Um, I see. Then how about this? Let's just be a *little* more casual. You can be polite without being too formal. I think you can just add '-Yo' to the end of your sentences."

*Do I really need to?*

Still, I thought I could guess why he was saying that.

I finally smiled and answered.

"Sure, hyung. If you want."

"Haha."

We didn't have any music playing in the car on the way back, but I didn't mind the silence.

I realized my worries had eased.

*...I guess there won't be a repeat of the summer package controversy?*

That was how I grew more comfortable with my new roommate.

But when one problem was solved, another problem arose.

***

After my conversation with Ryu Chungwoo, we were back to our insanely tight comeback preparation schedule. Early one morning...

★:
-VTIC Shin Cheongryeo Sunbaenim: Aren't you going for a run?
:★

An unexpected text came in.

*Why is this guy uselessly texting...*

★:
-(_Photo of a dog_)
:★

I looked at a picture of a dog happily walking along a familiar apartment path.

I recognized the dog, too. It was the one I'd met while jogging in the fog at dawn.

*Come to think of it, I never went for a walk there after that.*

I couldn't believe it, but with all the hiking, I'd actually forgotten about it.

I typed a quick reply with the hand that wasn't adorned.

★:
I've been exercising in other ways.-

-I see. I thought you might be busy since I hadn't seen you around, so I texted. Today's probably my last day out here, too.
:★

That meant I could resume my early morning walks without worrying about meeting this bastard. It was good news, but I was busy preparing for a comeback, so I couldn't go out anyway...

*Wait, this bastard can't go out there anymore?*

*That meant...*

I threw the most nerve-wracking possibility straight at him.

★:
Are you preparing for a comeback?-
:★

He threw me a curveball in return.

★:
-I'm always preparing for our next album^^
:★

*Fuck.*

These bastards were making a comeback now. It was obvious from how evasive he was being.

*How long has it been since their last comeback?*

I shook my head and thought frantically.

We made a comeback this year in January, and these guys in February.

It was the end of May now... Judging by this bastard's tone, VTIC would be making a comeback around June.

*Then it's a four-month interval... It's perfect for a repackage album given their popularity.*

Usually, when a repackage was released, sales tended to drop compared to the previous album.

A repackage only added one or two songs to the previous full album, so there wasn't much incentive to buy it. And with so little time since the last album, fans who'd already spent their money would have trouble spending much more.

But 4 months was enough.

*They'll barely lose any sales.*

It was about the usual comeback interval for a rookie group, and with VTIC so well-known for the quality of their albums, anyone who wanted one would buy it.

And looking at the timing they'd chosen...

*They were doing their best to get good year-end results.*

A comeback at the beginning of the third quarter was probably the latest they could manage without hurting those results, so they'd likely planned it carefully since the beginning of the year.

*They want at least half a year's worth of sales to count toward their results.*

At the same time, they were confident the repackage would do well.

"......"

*This won't be easy.* I smiled bitterly.

*They have the same plan as us.*

We were aiming for a comeback this summer for exactly the same reason.

"Moondae-ssi, it's your turn in front of the camera now!"

"Yes."

I put down my smartphone and followed the staff. But my head was spinning.

"Tilt your head forward a little... Yes, that's it."

As I posed for our album concept photos, I had one single wish.

*Please don't let it be in the same month as us, at least.*

July was as late as we could go, just like them. Hopefully they'd make their comeback as scheduled in June so there'd be at least a month between ours.

Not long after, however, the urgent news that came through the company's emergency contact network was the exact opposite of my hopes.

"...It's July."

*– Yes... Darn it...*

I'd taken Ryu Chungwoo's phone from him, and I could hear a Planning Team employee trying his best not to swear in frustration. I held back a sigh.

Two days after I'd texted Cheongryeo, articles spread the news of VTIC's surprise comeback in July.

★$
[The “Kings of K-pop” VTIC to be back in July… Countdown for album preorders begins]
$★

There hadn't been a hint of the news before it exploded in our faces.

"......"

Okay. I had to think this through a little more carefully... Looking on the bright side, they'd announced it roughly a month and a half early.

They were probably warning potential competitors to reschedule if they didn't want to be overshadowed.

*That stings.*

Our company was terrible at keeping information under wraps, so our comeback schedule had probably already leaked through T1.

*Does that mean they're willing to go head-to-head with us unless we postpone until September?*

Would Cheongryeo really want to do something so dangerous?

"They knew we were making a comeback in July, too, right?"

*– ...No. They probably thought it was June. That's what our initial articles said...*

"......"

Right.

Apparently, when T1 released presentations and articles for stockholders and mentioned TeSTAR's upcoming activities, they'd said we were making a comeback in June, even though we hadn't yet settled on June or July.

*June was in the second quarter, so I bet that made a difference in how they put together the PPT.*

It was a common trick.

And if VTIC's agency, LeTi, had scheduled their comeback thinking ours was in June, then VTIC would probably be making theirs near the end of July.

Even worse. Our comebacks could really end up being nearly simultaneous.

"All right. Thanks for letting us know."

I ended the call calmly and returned the phone to Ryu Chungwoo. Anyway, we were on our way to another schedule, so I couldn't talk anymore.

"Hyung, here."

"Okay."

Of course, the members had all been listening to the conversation.

"Are we overlapping with VTIC sunbaenim again?"

"Yes."

"Hoo." I let out a deep sigh.

Keun Sejin sighed and smiled bitterly. The other guys were also nervous.

And it was understandable. This was a repeat of the nightmare of VTIC making a comeback at the same time as our debut album.

Back then, we'd somehow managed to take first place, but the hardships we'd gone through weren't easily forgotten.

"Umm~ OK!" Cha Eugene said in English.

*Oh, except for that guy. He's not nervous.*

And Bae Sejin spoke up in a frustrated tone.

"If it's that burdensome, can't we postpone it a bit? The end of August is fine, too."

"If we push it back that far... It won't be good for our year-end results."

If we released our album after the beginning of the third quarter, we'd lose too much ground in our year-end digital track performance.

Then we could focus solely on the physical album grand prize, but that was... VTIC already had us beat there.

We needed to make a decision.
