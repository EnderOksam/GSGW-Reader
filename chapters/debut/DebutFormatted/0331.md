# Chapter 331 - K-pop Hell Camp, II

At the mountain lodge songwriting camp meant to encourage Kim Raebin, only praise and encouragement were allowed.

So even if one of the beginners came up with something awful, everyone laughed it off. Because we were just there to play.

But when the camp was for 'work', things changed.

Let's take a look at Bae Sejin's carefully put-forward opinion.

"...Well, anyway, it was the song 'Promise' that won the *daesang*. Then, how about using it as the basis for a series?"

"A common-sense suggestion!" Kim Raebin said.

It seemed that the feedback would end warmly like this, but that wasn't the case.

"Yes. It's good to base our next song on the one with the best results. However, even though that song won the *daesang*, the overseas reaction didn't change much."

"I-is that so?"

"Yes. Overseas, I think they'd prefer something strong like 'Drill'~" Keun Sejin said.

"That's right. I am against it!" Cha Eugene said.

In an instant, responses were relentless.

"...Okay. We don't have to go with my idea!"

"I like it, though. Sejin-ah, I'm sorry," Ryu Chungwoo said.

"Hmm. Okay. Well, then what do you guys think?"

There was no particular criticism. However, if an idea was rejected by the majority, we quickly moved on.

"Is there anything else fresh?"

"I came up with a unique idea! A robotic concept using mechanical sounds from MIDI and electric noise..." Kim Raebin began.

"Boo~ Cheesy~~" Cha Eugene said.

No, at least one of us was blatantly booing.

"Cha Eugene, you're only criticizing without coming up with your own ideas! That's a very inappropriate attitude!" Kim Raebin said.

"Okay! I'll say something, guys. I like transforming heroes!"

"We did it for ⟨Idol Inc.⟩, and when it comes to OSTs, it overlaps too much. Dismissed."

"Oh..."

*Boo yourself, why don't you?*

Anyway, most of the members made sensible remarks in this way. We quickly moved past any ideas that didn't fit the current situation.

As a result, we brainstormed at an unprecedented speed.

"We're coming up with too many ideas at once, so let's put the data into a table. We can broadly summarize the concepts that received the best response overseas and pinpoint the core elements of a superhero movie OST."

"Okay~ Moondae, you're going to make it, right?"

*Pushing the work onto me, huh? Smooth.*

"Sorry, Moondae. I need to learn how to do these things too," Ryu Chungwoo said.

"It's okay. You already have a lot to do."

In the end, it took just half a day to pick out the concept traits we should be aiming for.

+.
@l@ 1. Intense (superhero movie-style) @l@

@l@ 2. Unique (TeSTAR) @l@

@l@ 3. Familiar (American) @l@
.+

I rattled off the details without even pausing.

"A concept based on a powerful action beat that's familiar to American audiences yet has the uniqueness of TeSTAR."

"......"

"......"

"Wow."

If such a request came from the company, there would be a proposition to beat the boss to death.

But we had to. That was how the entertainment industry worked.

"From now on, let's use these points as our framework... and talk about the concepts and songs we want to try."

"Yes..."

The bloody meeting, which had been going back and forth like crazy for hours, went into a lull for a while.

"My throat hurts..."

"Eugene, from now on, let's whisper instead of shouting. You can't perform with a hoarse voice at a concert, can you?"

Hearing Ryu Chungwoo hit Cha Eugene with that fact in a quiet but firm voice, I checked the others.

*First of all, Kim Raebin is doing well.*

He was practically the elite member among us when it came to this sort of work. Pass.

*Keun Sejin has good sense too.*

On the contrary, he was more competent here than at the creative camp, which was just for a hobby. He was highly skilled at gathering good ideas and guiding the discussion.

*Cha Eugene keeps the meeting lively.*

He opposed ideas without hesitation, but without insulting anyone, which kept the meeting going.

*...It's a good group.*

Rather than just coming up with good ideas, they offered useful input without getting discouraged, even when constrained by a rigid framework.

And when we entered the composing part, even one unexpected guy came up with an excellent idea.

It was Bae Sejin.

"...Since it's American, how about taking some metaphors from American literature?"

It was an approach no one had ever thought of.

"American literature?"

*It sounds good.*

I asked right away.

"Have you been thinking about anything specific?"

"I was... thinking of Edgar Allan Poe's works among the classics."

Edgar Allan Poe.

He was a pioneer of American mystery and horror literature.

"Well, his works have something grotesque about them. They're very famous and distinctive..."

"Ohhhh."

Perhaps thanks to the response, Bae Sejin spoke quickly with a slightly flushed face.

"You know ⟨The Black Cat⟩ written by that writer, right? It's creepy and there's a twist... Isn't that the kind of uniqueness TeSTAR is trying to achieve?"

"...!"

"I-I think you're right...!"

Oh.

As a voracious reader, he had a good grasp of symbolism.

*Is it classic horror literature?*

It was okay.

Most importantly, Kim Raebin's eyes were already glittering.

"If the concept works, I think we could use it to write some really cool rap lyrics. I'll write this down."

"...Okay."

It was almost the first time Bae Sejin himself came out and gave his opinion on producing.

*Surprisingly, he has talent.*

Bae Sejin's face showed bewildered pride, probably not knowing that it would be accepted so well.

Ryu Chungwoo smiled and summed it up.

"Then how about giving it the feel of a Hollywood movie based on classic American literature while also making it fit our universe?"

"For now, let's go with that direction."

I rubbed my chin.

To be honest, it was a bit... lacking.

*It just lacks a little... oomph.*

For now, I decided to focus on the fact that we'd at least settled on a direction.

*We can work more on the key points while working on the movie OST.*

In fact, pushing this camp was not intended to produce high-quality results right away.

Rather, it was about checking each member's producing ability and encouraging everyone to get involved.

*I was just trying to see if the label would work.*

Because now the group was really halfway on the road to standing alone.

*When we become an independent label, we'll have more decision-making authority, but there are definitely guys who need a period of adaptation.*

And from what I had seen so far, most of the members had been doing well. Everyone was chatty and cooperative.

*Ryu Chungwoo has a talent for mediating, so that's fine.*

I gave almost everyone a passing grade.

But after looking around like that, only one member stood out as different.

"......"

"Seon Ahyeon."

"Yes...!"

The guy who had mostly just reacted to other people's opinions all day raised his head.

"I was curious what you thought about what we've come up with so far."

"Yes... It s-sounds like a good idea...!"

"Which part?"

Seon Ahyeon flinched, but answered slowly.

"Ho-Hollywood movies... have a lot of mass appeal. I-I think it's good to add something like classic literature... It adds depth."

"...Alright."

Yes. If I asked him directly about individual topics, one by one, he would give pretty good feedback.

The problem was... He couldn't keep up with the others when giving feedback in real time, which was arguably the most important part.

*He's bad at expressing his own opinions.*

Seon Ahyeon was amazing when it came to expressing a given task well, but he was a bit weak when it came to novel ideas.

Moreover, unless it was a moral issue, he wouldn't even try to argue when someone opposed him.

Out of consideration, he tried to take a step back and listen to others' opinions, so he couldn't find the right moment to intervene. 

In short, in this kind of thing, he just added his vote to the majority without contributing anything useful.

He knew that himself, too.

– Hyung! Any comments?

– I-I... Not yet.

– Hm~ Okay.

– ...

After this conversation with Cha Eugene, Seon Ahyeon lowered his head.

I sat next to him.

"It's okay if you take it slow."

"...Yes."

Seon Ahyeon didn't quite agree, but he nodded his head.

That was rather good.

*He has his own pride.*

I guess he felt like he was being outdone by the others, at least a little. There was room for improvement...

"What, why's the atmosphere like this?"

"...!"

Keun Sejin laughed and intervened.

"Ah~ Did Moondae tell you off, Ahyeon, because you didn't have enough ideas?"

"......"

"N-no...!"

Seon Ahyeon reacted a beat late. He must have felt that way...

"Ah, why do you care about that? There are a lot of friends who are good at producing like this. Let's just enjoy it, Ahyeon-ah!"

Of course, these words did not work for Seon Ahyeon.

"Th-there will be a time when I-I have to do it myself..."

Keun Sejin smiled.

"At that time, Moondae can do it for you!"

"...!"

"Moondae, won't you produce for Ahyeon later, when he does a solo album?"

"My, my solo??"

"Yes! We're going to do a unit or solo at least once in the future~"

He skillfully avoided the depressing wording of 'when the other members go to the army'.

Anyway, I laughed.

"Of course, if that's what he wants."

"...!"

"See? You can relax now."

"Yes."

Seon Ahyeon gave a faint smile and stood up with a much more comfortable face.

"Th-thank you."

"Don't mention it."

Still, looking into his eyes, he hadn't lost his desire to try. He seemed to feel a little better about himself, at least.

"I-I'm going back to my room."

"Yes."

"See you tomorrow, Ahyeon~"

Today's camp was almost over, so some of them had already left.

With a thoughtful face, Seon Ahyeon followed them out of the living room of the suite.

"......"

Keun Sejin kept his mouth shut until Seon Ahyeon disappeared.

And suddenly he spoke out.

"You take care of Ahyeon a lot, every time."

"Because he's one of our members."

"Hm~ Really?"

Keun Sejin added in a comfortable tone.

"Moondae, you do realize Ahyeon is really good once the producing part is done? He always delivers."

I knew. Still, I wanted him to grow a little bit.

"Instead of worrying about friends our age, let's focus on doing better ourselves~"

"Okay."

No, in fact, you guys are all younger than me. I endured Keun Sejin giggling as he patted me on the back.

*This guy is still fine.*

It seemed that he had some stamina left, so if I spoke bullshit to him now, I'd probably regret it.

I got up from my seat, thinking that tomorrow I'd have to find the right time to tell him some of the truth about my 'circumstances'.

"Where are you going?"

"Restroom."

Just then, an unexpected text message came to my smartphone.

★:
-Keundal: The concert was so cool. It was the best. ㅠㅠ
:★

"......"

I stared at my phone.

It was Keundal.

★:
Did you follow us on tour?-

-Yes! Since it's the country next door, I made time over the weekend. I don't regret it at all. I think you did really well. This is healing...
:★

Yeah, he had enough money to fly first class every time.

I shrugged after estimating his wealth. As long as he didn't get scammed, it didn't matter how he used it.

However, a few more text messages arrived in succession.

★:
-Can we video call? There is something I want to check.
:★

Hmm.

I guess something's going on.

★:
Okay. Wait a minute.-

-Yes!!
:★

I went into the small room I was sharing with Ryu Chungwoo, where he was typing on his laptop.

"I'll have a video call with Ryu Gunwoo for a moment."

"Hm, yes."

I even thought about getting a new room and talking on the phone, but I gave up because I thought it would lead to more suspicious misunderstandings. This suite was my room.

Since this guy was with me, he'd probably keep the other members from coming in even if they were curious.

*...It's reassuring to have someone who knows what's going on.*

"Can I say hello too?"

"You can do as you please."

I made a video call to Keundal, and he answered immediately.

*– Hello!*

"Hyung, you keep talking to me as if I'm Ryu Gunwoo, instead of the other way around."

*– Ah, sorry... Are you outside the dorm?*

"Yes."

This time, I wasn't going to let the last accident repeat itself.

*– Have you been well?*

"Yes."

Keundal immediately switched to a less formal tone, and only then did I ask him how he was doing and get right to the point.

"Why did you want to call..."

At that time, Ryu Chungwoo called from behind.

"Moondae-yah?"

I said he could say hello. Was he asking for permission again?

"No, not that... Sejin said he wants to say hello too."

"...!"

"Ah, I meant Bae Sejin, not dongsaeng Sejin."

When I turned my head, I saw Bae Sejin, who looked a bit nervous, standing outside the door.

Ryu Chungwoo looked a bit flustered.

"He says he knows the guy... Is that right?"

I was going crazy.

I looked at Bae Sejin, and Bae Sejin cleared his throat.

"That... I guess you make video calls like this often. I thought I'd say hello too..."

"...Um, yes."

If I said okay here without thinking, I'd have to come up with more useless lies. I racked my brain for a way out of this crazy situation.

It was then.

"What are you all doing there... hmm?"

Keun Sejin, who saw the three of us gathered in front of the door, approached.

I immediately hit the end call button.

*Diring.*

"Park Moondae, your call...!"

"Ah."

Bae Sejin gasped, as if he thought I'd hung up by mistake. Keun Sejin burst out laughing in disbelief.

"Were you on the phone? You said you were going to the bathroom, but you were here?"

"I got a call from someone I know."

"Really?"

Keun Sejin looked back at Ryu Chungwoo and Bae Sejin and shrugged.

"...Do you all know this person?"

"Ah... He's the one who helped Moondae when he was young," Ryu Chungwoo said.

"Ah, I see."

"This is the person Park Moondae has been looking for since last year. That... Ah."

Bae Sejin realized he'd probably misspoken and belatedly shut his mouth.

*Damn it.*

He must have assumed Keun Sejin already knew, only to realize he probably didn't. It didn't help me at all.

"Anyway, I happen to know the guy a little too..." Bae Sejin said.

"Ah... yes."

"......"

Keun Sejin kept his mouth shut. An awkward silence I couldn't quite explain settled between us.

"...First of all, shall we go get some rest too? This is Moondae's room, after all," Bae Sejin said.

"W-well, yes."

Bae Sejin looked back at me with an expression that showed how he knew he had made a mistake, but when I shook my head, he quietly kept his mouth shut and followed Ryu Chungwoo out of the room.

"Sejin-ah, let's go in too," Ryu Chungwoo said to Keun Sejin.

"......"

Instead of responding to Ryu Chungwoo's call, Keun Sejin rubbed his shoulder.

And after a while, he seemed about to turn away, only to stop and speak up.

"This is a little... unfair, isn't it?"

"...!"

His voice sounded upset.

"It's not even the first time... Everyone seems to know something I don't."

Fuck.

It wasn't surprising he'd misunderstood.

"No, not everyone knows. It's not that kind of thing, but the situation I told you about before..."

"Then why do they know everything if you're saying that it's hard to explain? Even hyung..."

Keun Sejin seemed to be referring to Bae Sejin, but he held back his next words and shut his mouth.

"......"

*Say something.*

I thought quickly and finally managed to think of something to say. I opened my mouth as calmly as possible.

"I mean..."

"Park Moondae, you look flustered. Aren't you even a little sorry for me?"

"...!"

Words came out reflexively.

"It's not like that."

"Really? Then why..."

Lee Sejin opened his mouth as if he was about to lash out, but... he took a deep breath instead, visibly calming himself.

"...No, I... Now isn't a good time. Let's talk later."

"Wait a minute. You..."

"Later."

Lee Sejin muttered in a voice that sounded less angry, turned, and immediately left the room.

*Bang.*

A moment later, I heard the front door slam shut.

Bae Sejin muttered in the hallway.

"...Sorry."

"......"

I fucked up.
