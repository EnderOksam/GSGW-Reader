# Chapter 309 - A Surprise Offer

T1 Plays, the company that took over the development team behind 127 Section, left a comment on the theme song Kim Raebin had arranged alone.

And less than a day after it was posted, the comment was flooded with excited replies from people making pilgrimages to the "holy site" after noticing it was from the official T1 Plays account.

★$
└-#1 hour ago#- Gaahhhh [👍 👎]

└-#45 mins ago#- Ah, ㅋㅋ they're doing their jobs well ㅋㅋ [👍 👎]

└-#30 mins ago#- We've been waiting for this. [👍 👎]

└-#5 mins ago#- Get this comment to the top with likes! [👍 👎]
$★

The atmosphere made it seem like this arrangement would be officially adopted for something like a cinematic trailer.

There were even crowds of foreign fans posting foreign memes plastered with emoticons.

"Uh, uh..."

Kim Raebin was stunned, his brain overloaded by the unexpected situation.

It was funny, but I felt the same way.

*How did it get to this point?*

I knew the reaction would be good, but becoming such a hot topic was another matter. 

How could a game parody video uploaded by a new account become such a big deal in just one week?

I'd let it be because it was an anonymous account, but we couldn't just leave things like this.

I unlocked my smartphone to find the cause of this uproar.

*Is it an algorithmic pick again?*

It wasn't. It was more artificial than that.

It was true that *127 Section* had passed its heyday in Korea, leaving only its die-hard fans behind and losing popularity and sales.

However, perhaps because something about the game appealed more to foreign fans, it had become a steady seller overseas, backed by a strong fan base.

Thanks to that, plenty of overseas gaming WeTubers were still devoted to this game series, and one of them seemed to have seen our video during a livestream.

*And his subscriber count was...*

★$
@cc@25.92 million subscribers@cc@
$★

*Of course our video got a lot of views.* Thanks to that, it came to the attention of someone perceptive at T1 Plays, and here we were.

So what were we going to do about it?

"What should we do?"

"I have no idea."

For the past few minutes, we'd been saying "I have no idea" over and over.

That's how absurd the situation was.

Was he about to start a second career as a professional composer? Was that even possible?

Someone in the industry had taken the bait all on their own, even though that hadn't been our intention when we uploaded the video.

At that moment, Cha Eugene's hand shot into the air like he was in school.

"Let's do it! It's fun!"

*I thought you'd say that.*

"Wait, wait!"

"Let's think about it, Eugene!"

Watching Ryu Chungwoo and Lee Sejin hurry to stop Cha Eugene, who was trying to settle it with a majority vote, I opened my mouth.

"First... If you really accept this offer, you'll have to give them your real name and bank account number when you sign the contract."

"Ah, so we'll get caught right away, right?"

"Yes."

I could already see them using TeSTAR's story to promote the game if that happened. Since the two companies were affiliates, headquarters would push it without a second thought.

It was a fun little story that both TeSTAR and the game could benefit from.

*And everyone would suspect it was some kind of ruse...*

Of course, such a coincidence could look like an obvious marketing deception.

★$
– Using TeSTAR is disgusting.

– Look at them trying to attract idol fans to breathe life into a dead game.

– This is driving me nuts. What's wrong with the T1 marketing team? T_T
$★

It would be stupid to invite that kind of criticism when we were in the running for the grand prize. The public had already accused TeSTAR of not deserving the Rookie of the Year awards we'd received.

It was important to actually receive the grand prize, but whether the public thought we'd deserved it would definitely affect the success of the mission.

*Even if we try to stop it, the moment someone higher up at headquarters decides to do something else, we're screwed.*

I couldn't let that happen.

Then, an unexpected person opened his mouth with a serious face.

"H-how about doing it in the name of a family member...?"

It was Seon Ahyeon. It seemed that he suggested it because he didn't want Kim Raebin's achievement to go to waste.

"Oh, that sounds pretty good?"

"I like it!"

I answered gravely.

"No. The moment we get caught, they'll accuse us of tax evasion."

They definitely would. It was a sensitive issue these days, so we had to be careful.

Bae Sejin, who once went through an uproar over his comprehensive income tax return in the first year of our debut, swallowed nervously.

"Then... Should we ignore it?"

"That's not a good idea either."

Since the account had already attracted attention once, it seemed we'd keep receiving offers if we continued posting arrangements on it.

*Given Kim Raebin's talent, that's understandable.*

Besides, people were already having fun in the comments. If we didn't respond, that alone could stand out and attract even more attention.

Then there'd be people trying to make the song and the whole situation go viral.

*...Should we just get rid of the account?*

Well, I thought this account had a symbolic meaning for Kim Raebin overcoming the slump, so I was going to leave it as long as possible.

To avoid that... In the end, we had to handle things the proper way.

"If we're going to refuse, I think we should explain why."

"Yes."

It would be perfect if we had a reasonable excuse that everyone could understand and move on from. 

If we kept focusing on uploading arrangements, people's interest would eventually fade, and he could go back to composing without difficulty.

"Well, make up something plausible, since you are good at such things!"

"What? Me?"

Bae Sejin flinched when Lee Sejin laughed in disbelief. They had been behaving politely for about a year now, so it seemed their patience with each other was wearing thin.

*Still, at least they're being open about how they feel.*

Anyway, Lee Sejin gave his opinion instead of fighting uselessly with Bae Sejin.

"As expected, the person who contributed the most should get to decide~ What does Raebin think?"

"What?"

Kim Raebin, who seemed to be deep in thought with his mouth open, suddenly came to his senses at Lee Sejin calling his name.

And he answered carefully.

"I... I don't mind refusing the offer!"

"You're not disappointed?"

"No! It was originally made at the songwriting camp as a hobby, not for commercial purposes, so I'm grateful for the response, which is more than I deserve, and I'm enjoying reading the comments!"

"Okay, if Raebin thinks so."

"Okay~"

The conversation ended with us agreeing on an appropriate refusal message.

"We should say that we're busy studying, right?"

"Yes. Let's make it sound a little smoother."

So this was the comment we put on the video.

★$
–-#Just now#- I really appreciate the contract offer, but I don't have time because I'm busy with my studies T_T. I'm happy just being able to share my work on WeTube. [👍 👎]
$★

It was a reasonable enough excuse that no one could nitpick it, and common enough not to be suspected.

*That should be the end of it.*

Everyone probably had similar thoughts, but things didn't go that way.

And as if they actually liked this cheesy gag, people kept replying with even more excitement.

*...It's starting to get attention on community sites.*

★$
– ㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋ

With the current atmosphere, we'd look like party-poopers if we refused again.

Of course, there would be people who would curse the person in charge and say, 'Why are you bothering them when they said they didn't want to do it?'

*Then it would really be shitty...* There was no topic that spread as easily as a controversial one.

If it came out amid all this that TeSTAR was behind the music, a really unpleasant situation would break out.

*This could turn into a huge controversy if we don't handle it right.*

Moreover, elements other than the arrangement were becoming a hot topic, so more and more people were writing bad reviews or compliments in the comments with ulterior motives.

The original reason we'd opened the account was already being forgotten.

*Fuck.*

It really bothered me.

We were busy enough preparing for the concert. What was all this fuss?

This seemed to be weighing on Kim Raebin, too.

"I'm really sorry for bothering you about trivial matters at such an important time..."

I smiled.

"What, are you apologizing for your talent?"

"N-no, of course I didn't mean it that way...!"

Kim Raebin was stunned, but he couldn't think of what else to say, so I asked another question to steer the conversation back on track.

"You agreed to turn it down because that seemed to be the cleanest solution given the circumstances, not because the offer was overwhelming. Right?"

"Yes? Yes. That's right, but..."

"That's all I need to know. It's okay to ignore all of this. The concert comes first."

"...Yes. Alright!"

Kim Raebin seemed relieved to hear such a definite answer. Keun Sejin grinned and put his arm around Raebin's shoulders.

"Yes~ We've got to show everyone what we've got at this concert~ We've been working so hard to prepare!"

That was true too.

In fact, we didn't really have the time or energy to pay much attention to this game company's shit.

The more we immersed ourselves in Keun Sejin's revised setlist, the more details needed our attention.

*He's pretty useful.*

I looked at Keun Sejin again.

If there was something to gain, he was pretty good at finding a realistic way to get it.

*That's why he's the one who played the biggest role in my plan.*

Yes. The curriculum I had set up when planning the trip to the mountain cabin was still unfinished.

There was one final step left.

*Stage 3 of composition camp.*

Since he'd regained his confidence by the second step, this time we'd work on keeping his morale from collapsing even during a slump.

*In other words...*

We'd make him feel a sense of accomplishment and reward for his efforts, even while keeping him from taking part in any aspect of the production process.

That was what this concert was.

* * *

"I feel like my heart will explode."

"Me too."

The graduate student was freaking out with her friend, who was a Park Moondae fansite master sitting diagonally in front of her.

And unlike the excited pair, a third person who wasn't at all excited to be there was also sitting nearby.

*Why am I here...*

It was their friend. She'd been recruited as a ticketing mercenary, but surprisingly, all three of them had succeeded, leaving them with an extra ticket.

Her friends' persuasion and her own curiosity had brought her here... and she was already regretting it.

*I should have just scalped the ticket online.*

She didn't want to get caught and have trouble getting a job, but sitting here in an uncomfortable chair, she was already regretting her decision.

*I could've bought a hundred auto-draw gachas with that money!*

Yes. She was a modern mobile gamer who wasn't afraid to spend money on money-hungry gacha games.

*I only agreed to come because these idols are so famous... phew.*

She looked at her friends who were already talking excitedly, then shook her head.

*This is also a bit embarrassing.*

The lightstick was so dazzling that she wanted to hide it away.

However, everyone at the concert seemed to share the same excitement, so she had no choice but to keep holding the lightstick.

*Ugh, really.*

Just when she was beginning to resent her friends...

*Geeeeeng!*

An announcement in a soft voice began to play over the speakers.

**[Hello, everyone.]**

*Aaaaaa!!*

"Oooh."

Was it that exciting at the start? She was startled by the cheers that erupted around her.

**[We have an announcement for those of you attending TeSTAR's ⟨Wave for Me⟩ concert today.]**

It was nice to listen to the man's voice as he quietly went over the required concert etiquette, but somehow it felt a little out of place.

The wording sounded more suited to a theater than a concert, but she didn't think much more of it.

**[Then, we hope you enjoy the concert.]**

At that time, the graduate student grinned widely and mouthed the name of the person speaking to her friend.

*That's Moondae!*

*What?*

At that moment, the lights went black.

**[Now, we'll go into the waves...]**

That was the voice's final announcement before the concert began.

And in the venue that suddenly became quiet, a sound slowly began to be heard.

It was the sound of waves.

Then orchestral and electronic notes slowly joined in.

Soft, clear notes.

It was a song she had heard.

*Is it TeSTAR's debut song?*

However, the very next moment, another powerful melody joined in.

This was a song she knew. But again, in a matter of seconds, the next accompaniment appeared...

*What?*

It was an overture.

An opening style used in operas and musicals.

The difference was that this was not meant to introduce the songs.

Because everyone at the scene already knew the songs.

It was meant to immerse the audience.

*...This is pretty atmospheric.*

And the lights came back on in the arena.

It was an intense blue light that covered all the seats. But it wasn't static.

"...!"

Light and dark blue rippled through the indoor performance hall.

The air moved.

*Cheeeeek.*

Dry ice was releasing smoke from below. It billowed softly.

"Wow."

The lighting, the waves, the cool smell, and the accompaniment echoing gently in her ears...

All her senses worked together to make her believe in the illusion.

The concert hall was underwater!
