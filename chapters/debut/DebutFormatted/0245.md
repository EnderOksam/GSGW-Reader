# Chapter 245 - A Healing Retreat, I

It had been a while since I'd last had a serious conversation with Ryu Chungwoo.

*Is this the first time since I woke up?*

Actually, no. Before the coma, I'd been keeping my distance from him because of that damned “reveal the truth” feature. It had been longer than I'd thought.

*...I'll have to let the system reveal some other truth before long.*

I briefly recalled the pop-up and then erased it from my mind. There was still time.

Anyway, after I woke up, he hadn't seemed to be doing well, so I deliberately left him alone. Experience told me that badgering someone in that state would only cause trouble.

So, I hadn't expected Ryu Chungwoo to prepare something like this first, as soon as the concert was over.

"Would you like something non-alcoholic?"

"...Um, yes."

I accepted the non-alcoholic canned beer he gave me.

*Click. Gulp.*

The sound of opening a can and the tingling sensation of swallowing it were refreshing.

"...You really enjoy beer, huh?"

"It's kind of like that."

It's not that I wanted to drink like crazy, but I couldn't say that I didn't think about drinking from time to time.

I couldn't tell whether Chungwoo was trying to put me at ease or just being polite, but he opened a nonalcoholic beer for himself, too.

The conversation started in a rather relaxed atmosphere, and it was not difficult to get to the point.

"Have you ever considered a lawsuit?"

That's right, the sudden concert ended, so it was time for this topic to come back. I answered honestly.

"I've thought about it... But I don't think our popularity can hold for more than six months if we stop now. We'll lose our momentum."

"That makes sense too."

Ryu Chungwoo quietly drank his beer.

"Losing a year now... isn't the same as losing a year ten years from now."

Yes.

It was a concern for professions whose prime years ended early.

*Is this worth giving up a year at the peak of our careers?*

Ryu Chungwoo was originally an athlete, so this kind of judgment would be more familiar.

The guy smiled bitterly.

"Sejin doesn't seem to have given up yet. He's been looking into various issues, even consulting a lawyer."

"...A lawyer."

"Yes. It's like consulting via text."

When did he have time to do that while practicing?

I was surprised, and at the same time, it left a bitter taste in my mouth.

He must have wanted to prepare for a lawsuit rather than a concert, so he must have been frustrated because he suddenly had a donation concert.

He must have been very patient with me because everyone was granting the wish of someone who had nearly died.

"..."

Ryu Chungwoo and I just drank beer without saying a word. And I was the first to speak.

"First... Shall I call Sejin-hyung?"

"Okay"

After all, the story would proceed only when there was an opinion actively in favor of the lawsuit.

I texted Bae Sejin.

★:
Hyung, would you like to come to Chungwoo-hyung's room? We're talking right now.-
:★

He read the message without replying. I heard hurried footsteps approaching, followed by a cautious knock.

"..."

He was a very easy-to-understand guy.

"Come on in."

"Yes, yes."

I opened the door for him, and the three of us settled in to talk.

"We were talking about the lawsuit."

"...! That, yes."

"So, you still think we should sue the agency, right, hyung?"

"...Yes."

Bae Sejin's face was tense, but the answer was quite calm. There would be no more yelling at me and making his own face red...

"The Human Rights Commission warned them, but they just shifted the blame! They haven't even replaced the director. At this rate, nothing will change!"

"..."

"They're just waiting for the commotion to die down!!"

Never mind. He was still plenty upset.

Well... The company was now keeping its head down and thinking about it, but Bae Sejin was right. The general manager hadn't changed either.

However, it was better not to change.

"Hyung, but I think it would be more comfortable for the general manager to stay."

"What?? Why!"

I spoke boldly.

"Because he would have been scared."

"...??"

"He can't act against us now. He'll have to watch our reactions and tread carefully. Things have gotten too big for him not to."

In other words, the power dynamic had shifted.

An artist under contract had almost died because of the agency, and now we had clear grounds to sue. Those were the facts.

He must have gotten into a lot of trouble in the head office, so in order to preserve his position, he would have to go through hardships in the future.

I lightly crushed the beer can for recycling.

"So even if the agency still seems scummy, we'll have plenty of leeway from now on."

"Uh, calling it crap..."

"Hmm. Then let's call them less than conscientious."

"..."

Bae Sejin was silent for a moment. I didn't know if he was speechless or stunned.

However, he soon answered in a broken voice.

"Okay... If everyone thinks so."

It looked as though Bae Sejin would yield to the majority again, just as he'd done when we postponed deciding on the lawsuit in my hospital room.

Bae Sejin seemed to hesitate for a moment, but then continued.

"Actually, I kind of thought of that. With this case, if we win by filing a lawsuit, it will set a good precedent..."

"..."

Did he think that far?

"But you're right. I should spend my time doing my part for the team instead of dwelling on this."

Bae Sejin lowered his head.

"I will give up the lawsuit."

"No..."

I didn't mean to put so much pressure on him.

I just tried to convince him in a good way, or even if we filed a lawsuit, we could try to set it in a way that would end in the shortest possible time.

I pondered over the words for a while. However, Ryu Chungwoo was the first to speak calmly.

"Not everyone thinks like that, Sejin."

"What?"

"I like the idea of a lawsuit."

"...!"

What?

Bae Sejin raised his head.

"...R-really??"

"Yes."

Didn't you, bastard, agree with me when I said that lawsuits were burdensome?

...Wait a moment.

I remembered the sentence Ryu Chungwoo agreed with.

*– You make a good point.*

...It was 'that too'.

I swallowed a groan. He'd stayed neutral all along.

*Come to think of it, he was like that in the hospital room too.*

Ryu Chungwoo continued with a calm tone.

"And Sejin, I think you're doing a great job as one of the older members of our team."

"..."

This... I had to add more words.

"I agree. Don't worry about the lawsuit. I'm only hesitant because I'm not sure it will work out for us."

"You guys..."

Bae Sejin seemed to be moved for a moment, but soon came to his senses and cleared his throat.

"Hmm, then... S-should I ask the other kids? Maybe they haven't changed their minds!"

"Yes. I see."

"Good."

And after a while...

"A lawsuit? Uh... Didn't we decide not to? Haha."

"So, if we file a lawsuit, will the next album be postponed indefinitely...?"

"I want to keep promoting! I like performing!"

"..."

It was a KO.

Even Cha Eugene, who wanted to file a lawsuit at first, turned around, perhaps because the feeling of the concert was good.

Bae Sejin sat on the floor with a bitter expression, dwelling on his defeat. I felt a little sorry for him.

Seon Ahyeon, who remained neutral at least, was restless, but soon raised one hand and spoke cautiously.

"I... I-if we file a lawsuit, will we take a break...?"

"That's right, Ahyeon-ah."

Ryu Chungwoo answered instead. Then, Seon Ahyeon hesitated, and he gave a small answer.

"Well, then, if it's not too long... I like the idea."

"...!!"

What was he talking about?

I asked right away.

"Seon Ahyeon, did you want to rest?"

"Yes? Yes..."

Seon Ahyeon avoided my gaze. This guy...?

"Wait a minute, Ahyeon wants to take a break, so you want to file a lawsuit?"

"Yeah, the concert ended well, and the reaction was good. T-taking a break... I thought it would be nice to take care of our bodies...!"

Lee Sejin quickly jumped in.

"Then we can rest~ Let's take two or three weeks off before preparing the next album, and go somewhere to recuperate!"

"Oh~"

"What would we do during the break?"

"It means to eat something delicious and play~"

"Great!"

Everyone seemed in favor. I could see Bae Sejin trying not to glare at Lee Sejin. Even if Ahyeon supported a lawsuit, they wouldn't win a majority.

It was my turn to answer next.

After thinking briefly, I took a sip of beer from a new can and answered.

"Healing is good."

"Oh!"

"R-really...?"

"Yes."

Anyway, the big hurdle was over.

There was still a grace period until I pressed the truth check, so I could spend it a little leisurely in the meantime.

Because it was a big accident.

*...They've had a hard time because of me.*

Not just me, but these guys.

As soon as I was discharged from the hospital, I pushed ahead with the plan of a donation concert, and of having the best senior group in this edition as guests.

*I was desperate to survive, but to them it must have looked like I was pushing everyone to work hard for no good reason.*

Still, the logic I used when persuading worked.

I briefly recalled the conversation at the time.

*– We need a talking point.*

*– A hot topic?*

*– Yes. People should be talking about us as idols, not because of the accident or the agency. And some rivalry would make that conversation even more intense.*

*– Hmm.*

This method rarely worked as well as rival positioning.

And this seemed to have motivated the members. Fortunately, the results were good.

*After the concert, the follow-up went well.*

Public opinion would be fine for a while since we used the donations and photos to smooth things over.

VTIC seemed to have benefited a bit, so I was hesitant, but the champion-and-challenger narrative seemed to have settled nicely within the acceptable range of the public and the parties involved.

*We were bound to face VTIC when TeSTAR got bigger. I'd just made it happen early.*

We'd gotten everything we could out of the concert, so my conscience told me I ought to give the others some room now.

Even if we couldn't file a lawsuit, let's stop trying to interrupt when they wanted to rest.

*As for the lawsuit... I think we might still get Bae Sejin what he wants without one.*

I thought it over slowly. Ryu Chungwoo across from me smiled.

"I like it too. We should have some recovery time. What about Sejin?"

"...That's okay."

"Okay, everyone agrees."

The mood became harmonious. Lee Sejin snatched a beer from in front of me and opened it. *Be patient.*

"Ah~ Chuseok[1]{T/N: Chuseok/추석: a major mid-autumn harvest festival and a three-day holiday in South Korea celebrated on the 15th day of the 8th month of the lunar calendar on the full moon.} is just around the corner, so shall we get together and rest well before going to our parents' house then?"

"O-okay...!"

Among the guys who were excitedly talking about where to go, this time, Kim Raebin's hand sneaked up.

"Uh, what about Raebin?"

"Should we tell the agency we're putting all our schedules and preparations on hold?"

That was fine.

"Anyway, our schedule is almost done for a while. We only have to shoot a few videos to show our fans."

"Okay! Then, only the video for fans to see is canceled... Hmm."

Yes, making it like that made the meaning of the sentence mysterious.

Fortunately, a plausible opinion emerged.

"Why don't we show the fans how we spend our break? Let's keep it relaxed and just have some unmanned cameras installed!"

"...I don't know if they'd like to see something like that."

"Well, it may be a bit boring, but... who cares! Healing travel is all entertainment. Well, I think it will be okay since we set up a donation concert as well."

That was right. Not bad for break content.

Similar opinions came from all over the place.

We just needed to change the theme a bit. I emptied the can and opened my mouth.

"But isn't a travel concept a little repetitive? We traveled in our previous reality show already."

"Ah, yes."

"That's a good point."

Idol Working Holiday. Everyone looked oddly reflective as they remembered making and selling hotteok.

I crossed my arms.

*Other than that, isn't there a typical healing entertainment? Something that fits the idea of recuperation.*

Not traveling, something else... Oh, that.

Chicken feet.

"How about setting a theme for an eating show?"

"An eating show?"

Wasn't that proven content? I remembered the days of ⟨Idol Inc.⟩.

And I wasn't the only one who remembered. Lee Sejin burst out laughing.

"That's our chicken-feet-loving Tibetan fox for you~ Wait, Tibetan? Oh, let's mix in animals, too! What other keywords do we have?"

"N-natural scenery...?"

"...That's good."

In the end, they combined every healing keyword they could think of in such a haphazard way, and a hastily prepared variety show was organized.
