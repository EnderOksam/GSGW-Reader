# Chapter 330 - K-pop Hell Camp, I

TeSTAR's global tour.

Although it was not possible to recreate the concert we had in Seoul, which incorporated elements of musicals, exactly as it was, the important parts and stage equipment were brought over.

*Certainly, the Planning and A&R staff do a good job.*

Now that we'd established our own label, the staff had become even more capable. They seemed to relish their newfound freedom.

For reference, the head of the Planning Team took over as the label representative. He didn't do anything unnecessary, and he was experienced and had good instincts, making him just the right person.

*He's not a celebrity, so even if a problem arises, he won't be singled out for criticism.*

I'd heard some ridiculous suggestions along the way.

– How about Moondae as the representative?

– What? Does *hyung* want to do it?

– Haha, sorry.

One of the guys had come up with an idea that might only be feasible if we'd been around for at least ten more years.

*An idol directly representing the label?*

That would give people endless things to criticize us for. Why not just tell me to set up my own company and be the boss? I held back a sigh.

Anyway... The first performance of the tour was very successful.

**[You make me breathe!]**

Even after the last encore song ended and we came down backstage, soaked in sweat and water, the cheering continued unabated.

*Wow!*

*Thud!*

Cha Eugene, who excitedly jumped down the iron stairs, laughed out loud.

"It's so much fun! Concerts are the best!"

"Th-that's right...!" Seon Ahyeon said.

"There are so many pretty lights sparkling onstage, too. We have to do this more often!"

It must have been really fun wielding a lightsaber. I just laughed and brushed the water off my hair.

Meanwhile, Bae Sejin, who was wiping his stomach with a towel, awkwardly opened his mouth.

"Surely... Concerts have their charm."

"Hyung!"

Before his debut, the guy who was always on the verge of death whenever he performed because of his lack of stamina and nervousness was now saying things like that.

*Was it influenced by the two of them being roommates?*

Cha Eugene exclaimed more excitedly after putting his arm around Bae Sejin's shoulders.

"Let's not take a week off between concerts. It's okay! Let's do it again! Let's have lots of rehearsals, too!"

*Yeah?*

"We're not taking a week off."

"Ah??"

I slung the towel over my shoulder.

"We have the work camp, remember?"

"......"

"......"

"Yes! I've prepared everything so we can have a productive time!" Kim Raebin said.

Only the workaholic was excited.

No, wait. Didn't they all like to work?

"Don't you want to do more work?"

"...! N-no...!"

Then what was it?

At that time, following Seon Ahyeon's words, Bae Sejin turned his head and muttered.

"When you say things like that... Unexpected things usually happen."

"......"

I had nothing to say to that.

"Yes, because Park Moondae made it sound so ominous, everyone is scared~ Moondaemoondae is to blame... Ouch!"

*Shut it.*

I slapped Keun Sejin's back, then immediately regretted it when my hand came away wet. Ryu Chungwoo, who was watching us with a proud look in his eyes, opened his mouth.

"Hm, then, will there be a camp instead of an after-party tomorrow after the concert in Osaka?"

"Ah, ah..."

The lively atmosphere suddenly deflated.

However, no one insisted on having an after-party instead of the camp.

...Were they really scared?

*Was I too forceful?*

Come to think of it, I hadn't actually asked for their opinions. I rubbed the back of my neck and shook my head.

"The after-party... We have to do it."

"...!"

I relaxed and laughed.

"It's been a while since our last concerts, so we should celebrate. Even after receiving the *daesang*, we were too busy to do it properly."

"Park Moondae..."

"Yes, th-that's right...!"

Keeping them motivated and maintaining their stamina was important if I was going to work them... No, work with them for a long time.

Satisfied with the members' reactions, I nodded.

"Come on, let's move."

"Yeah~"

Soon after, TeSTAR started running backstage, following the staff and leader's instructions as the crowd continued to cheer.

I let the others go first and slowly made my way out. Then, Keun Sejin fell into step beside me.

"Moondaemoondae, you know I was joking, right? Everyone follows your plans because listening to you always gets good results."

"......"

"It's not because you're forcing it on us, okay?"

"Okay."

"That's right~"

I looked at Keun Sejin, who had a big smile. Surely... He was even more cunning than he was perceptive...

That was why I was more sure.

*I'm sure he's waiting.*

That was, waiting for me to come clean about my situation. I was sure he'd done his own share of analyzing the oddities he'd noticed so far.

*I should find the right timing during camp, just as I'd planned.*

We'd focus on work first, and by the time this guy was exhausted and his critical thinking was at its lowest, I'd use several movies and dramas as examples to explain things to him...

"Let's put together a great album this time, too."

Yes. I knew that would be a priority for him, too.

*Let's do a good job for now.*

The next day's concert also ended well, without a single minor injury, and everyone was in surprisingly good condition. 

And we had a pleasant, reasonably low-key after-party.

It was a good starting point.

Thanks to that, everyone attended the camp the next day with a smile on their face.

"Shall we order room service?"

"The manager brought laptops for our composing camp!" Kim Raebin said.

Everyone had happy faces after having a good time. They seemed to have plenty of energy, too.

I didn't stop these guys as they excitedly set up their workstations and arranged the snacks.

On the contrary, I helped where I could, and when they were at their most relaxed, I started.

In the living room of the suite, I turned on the projector in front of the guys sitting side by side on the sofa.

+.
#*[TeSTAR's Future Direction]*#
.+

"Ahyeonahyeon, look at that. He prepared presentation materials. He's completely serious," Keun Sejin said.

"Y-yes..."

*This is work. Of course I'm serious.*

"First, I'll give you an analysis of our current situation."

Cha Eugene quietly put down the popcorn he was eating.

"As you all know, TeSTAR won five daesangs at awards ceremonies this year."

"Oh, oh."

"But that's not what we should be focusing on right now."

"What?"

I switched to the next slide.

+.
#*[Distribution of Loviewers]*#

[⇾ ~~*Daesang*~~ Most Popular Artist award voting pool]
.+

"This is the result of the Most Popular Artist award vote."

I gestured at the screen.

"As you can see, we won the Most Popular Artist award at three award ceremonies. What do they have in common?"

"We don't know! Please tell us!" Cha Eugene said.

He wasn't the least bit embarrassed.

"These are the award ceremonies that didn't allow overseas voting. In terms of domestic fans' firepower alone, TeSTAR is one of the top two artists who made a comeback this year."

"Wow."

"Moondae, where in the world did you get this?" Ryu Chungwoo asked.

"The Internet."

I spoke again to the open-mouthed bastards.

"However, the moment overseas K-pop fans participate in the vote, the results change."

"...VTIC sunbaenim would win no matter what."

"Yes."

I switched to a graph showing the voting rankings. There were even a few screenshots of the results where TeSTAR was ranked 3rd or 4th.

"Sometimes we're even beaten by idols from other big companies."

"Hmm."

"We are still definitely weak overseas."

I concluded.

This was what I'd been building up to.

"So, I think we should aim for a global audience with the next album and promote it accordingly. What do you all think?"

"......"

The members exchanged glances, then looked at the presentation screen.

There was no enthusiastic agreement. Even Cha Eugene, who was from the United States, was the same.

I held back a smile.

*That's how it should be.*

No one should fall for such naive words.

Instead, a rebuttal came.

"If we do well in Korea, won't overseas people recognize us? We've seen a lot of cases where idols tried to force their way overseas and things didn't go well."

Right. That was precisely why I hadn't focused on overseas promotions until now.

"Since we've already won several daesangs, I think those overseas who pay attention to what's happening in Korea already know about us. Isn't it time to increase our overseas exposure a little more?"

"Hmm... Okay."

Ryu Chungwoo seemed convinced by my reasoning. But another member chimed in.

"But on the other hand, I don't think our domestic fans will like it very much, so I'm not sure~"

It was Keun Sejin.

"Of course, we do a lot of W App streams and put out a lot of our own content, but honestly, it'll make us feel more distant~ Doing it right after winning so many daesangs might seem like we're betraying their trust!"

I crossed my arms and laughed.

"Yes, if the most popular idols in Korea suddenly change course to cater to foreign fans, it'll look like they're pandering to them."

"Ah, well, I wouldn't go that far, but we need to understand how disappointed they'd feel and take that into account! That's what I mean. Moondae knows everything~"

That was an accurate point.

"That's right."

I nodded.

"So, it'd be best if we were invited to go overseas."

"Invitation?"

"Yes. If we're invited from America, we can test the waters while increasing our exposure."

"T-test the waters...?"

We shouldn't look desperate to make it in the US. Instead, we'd go there by invitation and casually do some activities while we were there.

Instead of giving them the answer, I laid out the conditions.

"I hope it's not openly K-pop-related. VTIC sunbaenim has already conquered most of that playing field."

"At the same time, I hope the invitation doesn't seem too contrived."

"It also needs to be talked about enough to be useful to us."

The members' faces gradually went blank as they listened to me. Yes, it was tricky, so they should think slowly.

But the moment I shut my mouth, there was a guy who raised his hand right away.

*Oh.*

It was Kim Raebin.

"If you'll allow me to say my opinion..."

"Of course. Say it."

"Yes! I'd like to point out that there are people we're connected to who are highly recognized overseas, including in the United States!"

I held back a grin. He was getting close to the answer.

"Who?"

"The people from 'Ruins Factory', the company that made ⟨127 Section⟩."

"...!"

"Their newly released game is also a big hit in the US, and a movie spin-off is in the works. I think it would be good for us to try to make a song for that movie's OST!"

"M-movie??"

Of course, opposition poured in here and there.

"Hmm~ Should we really work with them again? We didn't create a separate 'All-Star Music' account for nothing."

Of course, getting involved with the same game company again would seem needlessly repetitive.

Surprisingly, Bae Sejin sided with Keun Sejin.

"Yes! And that company is also owned by T1. It would seem too much like an inside arrangement...!"

Actually, an inside arrangement was probably the only way we could contact them first. But our public image mattered, too.

*If people think we had our parent company arrange it just to fool the public, we'll become a laughingstock.*

However, Kim Raebin did not withdraw his opinion.

Instead, he offered more information.

"...! It could be like that. However, from what I have confirmed, the film is being produced in a place other than T1."

"Who's making it?"

"It's a Hollywood production."

"...!"

"I saw the news in the comments while running the All-Star Music account. All the gamers were excited because 'Limestone' will be producing the movie."

"Limestone??"

Cha Eugene whistled.

"They specialize in superhero movies!" he added, then said in English, "And they're insanely famous!"

Yes. It was the superhero movie division under an animation company notorious for its IP business.

An entirely American company.

I opened my mouth.

"So... Through our parent company, T1, we can make contact quickly, and make it look like the American company reached out to us first."

Also, since the film production company was another large corporation with famous IPs, it wouldn't look like we were repeatedly tying ourselves to the same game company and boring our audience.

"Yes! What do you think?"

What did I think?

It was the best.

*He found the top candidate right away.*

As expected, this guy had a good sense for this sort of thing, even if he lacked tact. He was good at choosing what he thought would be fun.

"I like it."

"...!"

"I wonder what everyone else thinks."

"I like it!"

"Of course, I do too!"

Quite a few of them were in favor.

It seemed that many of them were interested because it was a fresh challenge that addressed several of their biggest concerns.

Finally, Keun Sejin also smiled and opened his mouth.

"Okay, let's give it a go!"

"Well, then we've reached a consensus."

We decided to aim to participate in the OST of a Hollywood superhero movie, practically the essence of subculture and sure to draw plenty of devoted fans.

Sporadic applause came from here and there.

"We-we'll contact the person who'll be the label representative... And tell him about this, right?" Seon Ahyeon asked.

"That's right."

"Wow."

"I liked that game. The movie could be amazing."

Bae Sejin muttered bitterly.

"But if they don't accept it...?"

"So what? It's Limestone's loss."

"...Is that so?"

Cha Eugene shrugged, asking why he was so unsure when he was a *daesang*-winning artist. That lightened the mood considerably.

*Good.*

His timing was perfect. I opened my mouth.

"Then, I hope our title track will appeal to people who discover us through a superhero movie based on a game."

"Yes."

"I think it will be a lot of fun," Kim Raebin said.

"Mm, it's good."

I laughed.

"Then, from now on, let's brainstorm concepts that work for that vibe without being too obvious about it, while still expressing TeSTAR's colors. Let's go!"

"Go..."

That was how the brainstorming K-pop hell camp began.

There was just a problem.

Unlike the hobby songwriting camp, the skill gap between the honor students and the less talented students was huge.
