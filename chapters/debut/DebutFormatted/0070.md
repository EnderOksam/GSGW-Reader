# Chapter 70 - The Handwritten Letter

When I opened the locked door and walked into the room, Lee Sejin, who was sitting gloomily on his bed, freaked out.

"Urgh...!"

"Calm down."

I sat on the opposite bed. Ryu Chungwoo and Keun Sejin, who I thought would follow immediately, were nowhere to be seen.

*...I guess they courteously left us alone.*

*That's just like them.*

Well, it was fine. Anyway, since Lee Sejin was like this, I couldn't sleep right away, so let's hear what he thinks.

*I wish we had some beer.*

There was no way that there would be such a thing in the dorm of an idol group that was about to be launched. Thinking it was such a pity, I just spoke out.

"Hyung, you know the agency will want to talk to you tomorrow morning, sooner or later."

"...!"

"If that's the case, wouldn't it be better to just relax and organize your thoughts here? It is not a situation that can be avoided by avoiding it."

As I wondered whether the characteristic 'I heard it's true' didn't pop out this time, Lee Sejin spoke only after a while.

"...I don't know."

"What is it that you don't know?"

"What I want to do..."

"...?"

Isn't it too late to worry about your career now?

"Do you want to go back to your former agency and continue acting?"

"...I don't want to go back."

"So you're saying you want to act?"

"...Isn't that obvious?"

Lee Sejin buried his face in his palms.

"I never thought about becoming an idol in the first place..."

"..."

*This reminds me of when I first discovered my status abnormality.*

I sucked on my bottom lip.

*I guess you didn't participate in the audition out of your own will.*

"I guess you participated in ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ because of your former agency."

"...That's right. I'd been on hiatus too long... They said I needed to do something if I wanted to work again."

"Um."

I said nonchalantly.

"Even so, you worked really hard on the show."

"What else was I supposed to do? I don't have much talent."

Lee Sejin flared up a little.

*Hmm, it felt different from that.*

I remembered what he said to me in the final.

*– "You're debuting, so it's not over for you, is that what you mean? ...It must be nice"*

Doesn't that mean you want to continue? In addition, the change in Lee Sejin's status window was also noticeable.

*There is no F anymore.*

Lee Sejin's dance stat, which was F at the beginning of filming, had now risen to D+.

It was never a growth that could be achieved if it had been done without any special interest.

So I got a rough idea.

"You thought it was fun, didn't you?"

"...!"

"It was fun, so you worked harder without realizing it."

Lee Sejin kept his mouth shut. He couldn't deny it.

"That's right... It felt good being on stage. I thought I would like to continue. But acting, too... I want to continue it."

"...? Then do it."

"What?"

I don't know why you are worried about this.

"In a couple of years, the agency will probably start offering you acting roles. After the five-year contract expires, you can focus on acting instead of renewing if you want."

Lee Sejin, who had been listening with his mouth open for a while, soon lowered his head with a bitter smile.

"...I can't."

"Yes?"

"You don't know what my former agency is like."

"..."

Well, I didn't know. Learning that kind of stuff wasn't my thing.

Lee Sejin seemed close to giving up as he began explaining the situation.

"Dream K, over there... It's not a decent agency. If I stay here like this, I don't know what will explode out of their resentment."

"Do they have any blackmail?"

If drugs came out here, I would go ahead and recommend withdrawal.

Fortunately, Lee Sejin frowned.

"I didn't lead that sort of careless life. ...I mean they will blow it up in the form of plausible tabloid gossip."

He said the agency had spread rumors about several actors who refused to renew their contracts.

Whenever contract renewals came around, they spread rumors to drive down the actors' value.

*Isn't that just like the perfect picture of a bad company?*

"Um, is there a line-up of rumors that you can predict?"

"Mine is obvious... First of all, the purpose of joining the audition was to make a comeback as an actor, and they'll move on to saying that I didn't even want to be an idol."

"Hmm."

"And then they'll spread rumors that I had an affair with a married woman... or some other scandal that isn't technically a crime and is hard to disprove."

"Well, T1's backing us. I doubt your former agency would dare to go up against a company that big."

"...Their plan is to make me look so bad that T1 will decide to cut me loose first."

Lee Sejin murmured bitterly.

"At any rate... I joined last minute."

"..."

"Public opinion about me on the internet is not good right now. There are a lot of people asking to leave... because I'm no good."

"...Um."

He must have looked it all up.

Certainly, that kind of talk had been circulating.

In other words, the group would be much better if we left out the person who was an additional passing participant, a former actor, and had the lowest skill.

I shrugged.

"That sort of talk will fade when we debut."

"...I know saying that won't change anything, but it does make me feel a little better."

"I wasn't trying to console you."

"...What?"

"We are releasing an album together with VTIC. It means that the fans are uniting to keep the common enemy in check right now."

"...!!"

When a powerful enemy is formed outside, the inside is bound to unite.

"Besides, if hyung leaves, someone else will be the target. That's how it is. So, I don't think you should think about it."

Wanting to get rid of someone who looked like the weakest is common behavior in any group. This meant that it didn't happen particularly because it was Lee Sejin.

Lee Sejin seemed a little teary-eyed, but he soon recovered and shook his head.

"Still... There's no way to stop my former agency right now. It's not like just talking to DreamK will stop them from spreading rumors."

"I guess you should do it yourself then."

"What?"

I crossed my arms.

"Hyung, you can make a statement before they do."

"...Beforehand?"

"You were forced to participate by your agency, and you post an article saying that you don't want to go back because you've found your calling. Make sure to emphasize 'force, forced' by your former agency, and thanks to the audition, you escaped."

"...!"

"Then even if gossip comes out, it won't work well."

'My former agency is spreading rumors', in other words, creating an atmosphere that will allow the fans to defend him.

Once public opinion was turned around, the former agency would feel burdened to drag out the lawsuit, and then T1, who didn't want to give shares, would properly end it.

Lee Sejin was silent for a moment with his mouth open.

"...What exactly did you do after dropping out of high school?"

"I joined the audition."

I'd been getting questions like that a lot lately. At least he hadn't asked if I worked for the NIS this time[1]{Editor's Note: NIS = National Intelligence Service. People keep asking why Moondae knows so much. In this part, he's saying it's fortunate Sejin didn't ask if he was a secret agent or something like that.}.

"Then discuss all of that with our agency, okay?"

"...Okay."

Anyway, the situation was sorted out. Ryu Chungwoo would take care of the rest. I held back my sigh.

"...Should I post it on our SNS account?"

"Tell them you'll write the statement yourself, by hand."
