# Chapter 269 - The Leaked Footage, III

Without any new fuel, the fun of criticizing someone over a scandal was bound to wear off with time.

In particular, people who'd already grown tired of feeding on the Cha Eugene controversy for five days were excited by this new turn of events.

They hadn't lost a target for their criticism. They'd just been given a new one.

★$
-The production crew did that? They must have gone crazy

-Where the hell do these people come from? Who do they think they are, picking on the staff? They pop up every time.

-So this is the legacy of that Idol Inc. fiasco, fuck. ㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋ

-What is this, a mafia game? I wasn't ready for that twist

-The staff must have been really scared, so I'm glad they recorded it ㅠㅠㅠ
$★

Even though the production team weren't celebrities like Cha Eugene, everyone was talking about them.

⟨Idol Incorporated⟩ was a very well-known series, especially because season 2 had been the most spectacular flop of all time.

People were thrilled to learn about the production team's past, as if some juicy secret had been exposed.

And to make it easier to criticize their new target, they made Cha Eugene the victim overnight.

Just as they'd put Park Moondae on a pedestal when they were criticizing Cha Eugene.

★$
-Cha Eugene got all that hate for nothing because of these crazy bastards. If it were me, I'd have lost my mind.

-I feel so bad for him I can't even speak.

-I have a feeling the production team leaked that video too. Maybe they hated Cha Eugene because of what happened in that recording?

└ Wow

└This would really give me goosebumps if it were true...
$★

Everything went smoothly as public opinion shifted, gradually painting the production crew as far worse villains than Cha Eugene had been just days ago.

And seeing public opinion reverse so suddenly, quite a few fans and people following the situation felt utterly exhausted.

★$
-They started cursing him without even checking if it was true ㅋㅋㅋ And now this? I'm seriously disappointed.

-I asked everyone to stay neutral, but they said the video was undeniable evidence. Clearly it wasn't. Couldn't they have waited a little longer...? Just a little.

└Honestly, wasn't the situation too clear-cut at the time? We can't blame the people who criticized him.

└ You seem to be a fan. Just be glad it turned out this way. That's probably for the best. These guys will never change...
$★

Even if they found the situation hard to accept, there was nothing they could do. It was too risky to pour cold water on this reversal in public opinion that had just saved Cha Eugene.

Many people wanted to justify their criticism of Cha Eugene somehow.

So even the fans who'd stood by him kept quiet and accepted that people were criticizing the production team and restoring Cha Eugene's image.

And Cha Eugene's recovery gained momentum.

This was only accelerated when the agency released a brief statement.

★$
["He did not want to harm any third parties"... T1 Stars reveals statement regarding TeSTAR Cha Eugene's stance]
: Official Notice

-He didn't know the recording existed, so he couldn't explain what had happened right away because he was afraid the staff member would get criticized

└ㅠㅠㅠㅠ Ah, I really have tears in my eyes

-Eugene, stay strong. You're so amazing, and I don't know why you have to go through so many unfair things. I hope only happy things await you...

-He's really amazing. If it were me, I couldn't have kept quiet like that.

-I'm glad the staff member reported it. This is why you should be nice to the people around you.
$★

Of course, some people read between the lines and noticed that something didn't quite add up.

Even if their suspicions stemmed from resentment at the recovery of his reputation.

★$
-But why did Cha Eugene push the camera down? Are we supposed to believe the production team was stupid enough to point the camera at the staff while doing that? Hmm...

└ It must have been during the documentary filming. They probably didn't care who they pointed the camera at since they were going to edit it out anyway

└ Wow, you really just want to criticize him, don't you? Didn't you see the transcript?

└ Did the Idol Inc. studio hire you part-time to defend them?

-If they had the transcript, what were they doing for the other 5 days? They handed it over to the press, right?

└ You're going to get sued by T1 Stars.
$★

Of course, these people were ruthlessly driven out by Cha Eugene's fans, who sprang into action.

Fans of Cha Eugene spread the news everywhere as if making up for their helplessness over the past five days, putting all their energy into making sure he wouldn't be criticized again.

★$
-Idol Inc. 2 producers... They still haven't kicked the habit of ruining people's lives

-Cha Eugene stopped them from abusing someone, only to take the blame himself.

-No wonder nobody explained anything. Was it because the studio behind Idol Inc., part of the T1 family, would go to shit?
$★

When the fandom, which had always been good at handling things like this, actively joined in, it had a huge effect on public opinion.

And whenever there was a backlash against how quickly things were moving, another article came out with more information.

★$
– **[Idol Inc. 2 production team has provided a statement]**

– **[The staff was mistaken.jpg (feat. Statement)]**

– **[T1 seems to have finally cut ties with the power-abuse studio]**
$★

After this happened three or four more times, this controversy slowly cooled down and gradually faded away.

That was how Cha Eugene finally shed the label and emerged free of blame.

***

I checked the latest article.

I looked at the comments on a new article related to the documentary controversy.

★$
[The "Gapjil Studio" set to close? Film J shuts down website -#(21)#-]
$★

The discussion had dwindled to just a few dozen comments, most of them brief emotional outbursts that didn't amount to much.

"Mhm."

I took my eyes off the screen and looked up.

"I think it's safe to say it's over now."

"Hoo!"

"Woooo!"

"Thank goodness, Eugene..."

The members groaned and dramatically sank to the floor.

It was because we'd mostly stayed off the internet to keep ourselves in good condition for our performances, hoping things would work out.

That was how we usually dealt with controversies.

*This wasn't even about them, and they're making such a fuss.*

I stared at Cha Eugene, who was sitting on the sofa with an open can of beer.

*Tak.*

Cha Eugene put down the can and opened his mouth seriously.

"Hyung is a genius."

*He sure speaks well.*

Apparently, my manipulation of the evidence had left quite an impression on him.

"Give your thanks to the masseuse. All I did was hand her recording over to the company."

[Of course, I'm thankful to her too! I'd love to give her a gift or something...]

"No way. Don't you dare. Just be thankful."

If people found out he gave her a present, they'd start making up all sorts of stories.

T1 would take care of rewarding the staff member, so it was best for both of us to stay out of it and just be thankful.

"Umm, okay."

Cha Eugene nodded.

And quite politely, he handed me the can of beer he'd opened.

"...?"

"It has no alcohol! Enjoy it, hyung! Thank you very much."

"...Okay."

Looking at it again, it really did say ‘0.0% alcohol’ on the label.

Now even this guy was giving me non-alcoholic beer. Feeling slightly bitter, I lifted the can.

"Moondaemoondae! Cheers first!" Lee Sejin said.

"W-wait a minute...! I haven't opened mine yet..." Seon Ahyeon said.

"......"

*Huh.*

After waiting a little, I clinked cans with the guys who seemed to think it was so important.

Only then did all their fatigue seem to melt away, their faces breaking into grins.

"It's good~ The food tastes even better now that we can relax. Right?" Lee Sejin said.

"Yes! The room service at this hotel is really good," Kim Raebin said.

Yes. Now we were all in a hotel room together.

Because today was the one day a week when the members camped together in the same room.

It was Ryu Chungwoo's suggestion for building team cohesion.

Since the tour in Japan was over, it was a little wrap-up party and a celebration of Cha Eugene making it through the controversy.

I shrugged my shoulders as I looked at the spread of food and drinks.

*Actually, I think the original plan was to do more than this.*

The company seemed to have planned an encore at the Tokyo Dome, but it was canceled because of the controversy about Cha Eugene and the caution we'd exercised afterward.

So tomorrow, we would be going home.

Of course, that didn't mean we'd be getting any rest.

"Let's put on a great encore concert in Korea, too," Ryu Chungwoo said.

"Sounds good~" Lee Sejin said.

An encore concert would be held in Korea soon. It was a good thing for the fans back home who'd grown tired of the drama of the past few weeks.

*When I think about it... It was pretty unlucky that the dome tour and the controversy overlapped, wasn't it?*

Footage of performances in Japan rarely circulated, apart from fan accounts and blurry photos. At least Cha Eugene would have been able to perform at the Tokyo Dome without much worry.

It was fortunate that the articles had come out before our next performance.

I mentally went over the performances we'd given on this tour.

*Well, the tour itself was great, as usual.*

Performing on our home turf felt the best, but being overseas had its own charm.

*We always had at least one song translated into the local language or performed a famous local song. It was fresh and fun.*

For the first time in a long time, I could leisurely think back on our performances and enjoy a beer... Although it was non-alcoholic.

And I just sat back and listened to the other guys talking.

"Eugene, it must have been hard for you, but you pulled off all the performances. Good job. I'm sure the audience enjoyed them."

"...Good job."

Even Bae Sejin awkwardly patted Cha Eugene on the back. Was it the power of alcohol?

I watched them without thinking, and when they noticed me looking at them, they suddenly started complimenting me, too.

"You did a great job too, Moondae."

"...That's right. You too... You were great."

"...??"

Lee Sejin, beside me, burst into laughter again.

"Hey, what's with that look on your face? You made the biggest fuss!"

"A fuss?"

"Yes! You threw your smartphone, punched the sofa, and made quite a spectacle~ Don't you remember?"

"......"

I briefly thought back on my behavior.

*Right.*

And I groaned.

"...I was just annoyed."

"And why were you so annoyed? Hm? Wasn't it because you were so worried about our Eugene?"

No. I was upset because no matter what I did, I couldn't see a way to fight those unfair accusations.

However, Lee Sejin, with an arm around Cha Eugene's shoulders, was grinning as if his mouth was about to tear.

"Ah~ Our team has a really good atmosphere~"

"......"

*Well... Yeah.*

When he put it that way... Maybe he wasn't entirely wrong.

I never expected that I would feel this sense of belonging in this group.

And you could never discount the impact of emotions.

I finally laughed and opened my mouth.

"Yes. I was worried. Of course I was."

"...!"

Then I looked at Cha Eugene and spoke.

"You held up well. Good job."

If this guy had panicked or given up, things would've been many times more difficult, but he'd stayed calm, so we'd been able to cover for him.

"......"

Cha Eugene kept his mouth shut and didn't answer.

*Come to think of it, I don't think he responded to what the other guys said earlier either—*

Even before I could finish the thought, Cha Eugene started sniffling loudly.

"...!"

"Eu-Eugene?"

Instead of answering again, Cha Eugene grabbed the person next to him by the hair.

Kim Raebin dropped his glass of champagne, shocked to find Cha Eugene gripping his hair.

"Hey, Cha Eugene!"

"Heok!!"

Cha Eugene burst into tears. However, even Seon Ahyeon didn't stop him.

...It was the first time any of us had seen Cha Eugene cry over this.

"...Hmm."

Kim Raebin seemed to accept his fate pretty quickly, too.

"Hng..."

Cha Eugene cried for quite a long time while clinging to his friend's head.

It was a cathartic night.

***

On our way back to Korea the next day...

Kim Raebin said that he would generously forgive his friend for pulling his hair, considering the circumstances.

"Cha Eugene could have been forced to leave the group altogether, so I understand how worried and anxious he must have been."

That was right.

So, T1 must have completely cut ties with the studio. It was an incident that could have badly damaged the group's reputation.

It meant that it was more beneficial to preserve an influential idol group generating astronomical profits than a minor studio whose performance was declining.

Of course, even after all this, the criticism of Cha Eugene would eventually return to some extent, though it would be much weaker.

-Still, I don't like that he got physical like that

-It's hard to say Cha Eugene did the right thing. He seems to have an impulsive personality.

Maybe... Comments like these would come up. Controversies like this didn't disappear completely once they'd started.

It meant that Cha Eugene would have to be much more careful not to cause similar controversies in the future.

*It would be hard for him.*

Besides, the biggest problem was... The unity within the fandom had been shattered.

Most of the fans who supported the group itself had criticized Cha Eugene and were now apologetic, relieved, or weary.

It meant that the tension could still be eased with some good content.

The problem was on the individual fandom side.

*This... I don't think they'll recover easily.*

First of all, it seemed that Cha Eugene's fans were greatly shocked.

It was because quite a few of the other members' personal fans had immediately called for Cha Eugene to be kicked out as soon as the incident broke.

★$
-Even after watching his personality and behavior for several years, they tried to kick him out as soon as this happened. I'm shaking with anger. Was it that hard to wait for a statement before criticizing him?

-You guys want Cha Eugene to go solo? I'd rather he went solo than had to deal with these disgusting fans in this cesspool

-This just feels so wrong... We're not asking you to take his side, are we? The people who actively criticized him were obviously in the wrong, weren't they?

-He's hardly ever caused any trouble, so how much must they have hated him to come out of the woodwork and immediately say he should be kicked out? ㅋㅋ
$★

To be honest, I didn't think I could blame only one side, because it happened as various unfortunate factors came together. But for the same reason, the wounds between the fans ran deep.

*This... I'll have to do something about this, too.*

...I thought I'd have to put the upcoming Korean encore concert to good use. In many ways...
