# Walking Off the Set

The film production company called TeSTAR to the United States just ten days after we accepted the offer.

It seemed that filming was already in full swing.

*I guess we did ask to join when things had already progressed quite a bit.*

But they even offered a cameo?

I thought this was a gesture of reconciliation, but I figured I should leave room for other possibilities.

*Maybe they're trying to sucker us.*

I was about to warn the others to be wary, but it turned out I didn't have to.

"We don't know what order they'll film things in, so we might have to wait for a few hours even if it's just a passing scene. I'm sure they didn't set aside the whole day for no reason."

"Oh, oh."

"Of course, Hollywood may have a different system, but keep in mind that we probably won't be high on anyone's list of priorities..."

This was because Bae Sejin was going on and on about the problems we'd run into on set.

Gone was the idea that this movie cameo would be a refreshing little break in the middle of the tour. Everyone was treating it as a new kind of work that we were complete rookies at.

Bae Sejin was now trying to make up for his overly cautious warnings, but it was probably too late.

"...But it could be nice! Acting in a movie definitely has its charms!"

The effect was minimal.

"I guess so."

"Whether we can actually do a good job is a different matter."

"...I became an idol! You guys can do it, too!"

"It's hard to generalize like that when we're not all the same person..."

"That's right~ Let's do our best!"

"....?"

Keun Sejin clamped a hand over Kim Raebin's mouth and tried to brighten the mood.

*Good save.*

Bae Sejin seemed to want to persuade us some more, but he'd said enough.

I spent some time on the plane looking through the OST materials they sent us.

Of course, it was in English.

*I knew they wouldn't translate it for us.* There was too little time.

The agency provided a translated summary, but the quality was not very good. As soon as the label was fully independent, I would start by finding a full-time professional interpreter.

Fortunately, Cha Eugene and Seon Ahyeon gave a briefing, so everyone was familiar with the contents of the movie.

A brief synopsis of the movie would go like this.

**[After the world nearly comes to an end, a warp gate appears out of nowhere, leading a courier on a dangerous and exciting adventure in outer space!]**

It borrowed the sci-fi format, but the actual style seemed to be a mixture of old westerns and superhero movies.

*And the apocalyptic storyline, coupled with the outer space theme, was Ruins Factory's way of worldbuilding.*

While looking over it, I recognized some of the words I'd seen while keeping an eye on ⟨127 Section⟩, as well as names that had appeared in their new game.

*I'm a little confused because it's in English...*

"I-i-if you need an explanation of the vocabulary, I can help..."

"Then, thank you."

"Yes!"

I listened to Seon Ahyeon's explanation as he sat beside me, organizing my thoughts as he spoke.

I also confirmed once more what role we would play in the cameo.

*Hmm... They want us to pay homage to the starting characters from the game we collaborated with before.*

It seemed we would appear briefly in a fight scene.

We hadn't been asked to prepare anything in advance. We had only one line of dialogue.

I knew it would turn out like this because the film company had bought the rights to the universe, so there was no copyright problem. Since I'd expected this, I could easily sort out the pros and cons in my head.

"...Hmm."

And I made a prediction.

*There's a good chance we'll end up looking like idiots.*

Wasn't the part we'd been assigned overly one-dimensional? There was another thing that worried me, too.

*There are no East Asians in the main cast.*

If the seven of us appeared together, it would be difficult to make a strong impact, and we'd seem out of place.

"Hmm."

"Moo-Moondae?"

"Oh, I just want to take another look at the scenario."

I put our cameo documents aside and pulled out the screenplay summary again.

My brain went into overdrive.

It was 12 hours before arriving in the United States.

* * *

And we finally arrived in the United States.

We quietly entered and traveled in private. Fortunately, it seemed that T1 hadn't alerted the press about our arrival in their excitement, so nothing special happened.

After that, we were taken directly... to the movie set.

*Interesting.*

"Uh... Aren't we going to start with the OST meeting?"

"It seems they can't change the filming schedule. Everything is set in hourly slots."

The scale of the set aside, everything was running quietly and smoothly, with everyone taking care of their own jobs.

"......"

"...I thought it would be noisy."

Even Bae Sejin seemed a little flustered. The person assigned to guide us for a while laughed briefly and spoke in Korean.

"There's a very professional division of labor. It's one of the good things about Hollywood."

"Ah, yes."

And after hearing a few words of explanation and saying hello to the assistant director... We were left to wait in the trailer.

"......"

"Mmm."

At this point, everyone seemed to have noticed the strange atmosphere and smiled wryly at one another.

"So they're just leaving us here."

"I know."

They took care of blankets and food pretty well, but we hadn't even started on our makeup yet.

It seemed that only our manager and relevant staff were hard at work.

*It's going as I expected.*

It looked like they hadn't made proper preparations for us. We hadn't been treated like a fifth wheel in Korea since we became a top-tier group. This was interesting too.

"The manager must be having a hard time~"

"It seems there's been a breakdown in communication. Shall I go out and see what I can do?"

I was making my own plans while listening to the other guys talking when...

Cha Eugene, sitting next to me, poked me in the side.

"...?"

*What?*

When I looked at him, he muttered glumly.

"This is not normal!"

"Then what's going on?"

"This is bad."

Cha Eugene continued to speak only by mouthing the words.

*They're purposely ignoring us.*

"...!"

[It's a pretty classic method.]

*Oh... So that's what he means.*

*They're ignoring our staff while making it look unintentional?*

This was no longer amusing.

I crossed my arms.

"Why do you think that?"

[In Hollywood, actors are basically kings. But look at how they're treating us. They're treating us entirely as extras or props, not as actors.]

"......"

That made sense.

I'd seen earlier how much they cared about their schedule. Maybe they thought it was fine to neglect us a little, since our contract had no special clauses and we had plenty of time.

"Why, what's wrong, Eugene?"

"Hmm."

Cha Eugene shrugged and looked at me. *What, are you asking me to translate this?*

I briefly explained the situation.

"He says they're probably going to deliberately delay things until the very last minute before letting us shoot."

"...Ah."

"Ugh."

Everyone breathed a sigh, as if they guessed it based on the atmosphere.

Ryu Chungwoo smiled bitterly.

"I guess Bae Sejin was right."

"...No. I said this while thinking about the filming in Korea, but this place... Things look different."

Bae Sejin continued with a frown.

"If they're doing this on purpose... It's bullying."

And Kim Raebin was astonished.

"...! Is this racism?"

*Yes. That was it.*

"Well, we did join on pretty short notice... We're foreigners, and our race and reputation here probably played a part."

We'd appeared out of nowhere when the schedule had already been set, and how we were treated depended on how much clout we had.

And in their perception, we didn't seem to have much power.

"......"

I cracked my neck and checked the others.

None of them seemed all that angry.

Hell, before we sold a million albums, it wasn't like we'd never been treated like this.

"It reminds me of ⟨Idol Inc.⟩."

"Wow, that's right."

Crucially, most experiences weren't as bad as what we'd gone through on our debut program.

We were calm because we'd been through chaos even before our debut. I shrugged.

"There's a meeting scheduled for the evening anyway, so why don't we tell them we can have the meeting first and discuss everything else afterward?"

I asked Cha Eugene, who had spoken up.

"What do you think?"

[If we leave now, they'll say we refused to film as scheduled and breached the contract.]

"I-I see."

"Yes!"

Seon Ahyeon immediately agreed with him. The two guys who had lived abroad had reached the same conclusion.

"Hmm."

"Ah, what a pain."

As pissed off as they were, the members all enjoyed their work, and like most people who did, they were allergic to breaking from the schedule.

"What do you think, Moondaemoondae?"

*What else?*

I crossed my arms.

"I... I don't think it matters if we break the contract."

"...!"

"Well, it's not like we're desperate to do a cameo."

I turned to Bae Sejin, whose expression was stiff.

"Hyung, I'm sure you'd be upset to miss your chance at Hollywood, but..."

"Hey!"

Bae Sejin shouted. The atmosphere lightened for a moment, but he soon calmed down.

"I don't want to film in this situation, either. It's not just my own name on the line. It's the group's name."

"Hyung..."

The members, who knew how excited he'd been over the past few days despite his blunt advice, immediately reacted to his words.

"That's right. It's a role too small for Sejin to appear."

"That's not what I meant!"

I chuckled.

"It was a joke. In fact, I don't think we'll be accused of breaking the contract."

"...?"

"Why, why?"

"These guys probably won't be able to give up on the OST."

"They won't want us to call off the OST too, so I think they'll be quite amicable at the meeting this evening."

"...!"

If they heard the finished version and still said that they didn't want to use it, they would be shitty bastards.

Even if the people working on set and the people on the music side had completely separate roles, and even if we were treated like this here, the OST managers would have to take us seriously.

They'd already learned their lesson...

"Kim Raebin did such a great job with the track."

No matter what kind of politics I tried to play with the memo, it only worked because of how good the finished track was.

"Oh, my..."

"As expected, Raebin is a genius."

"This is an undeserved compliment. I will continue to do my best."

*Yes. Try slowing down a bit when you speak.* That settled things. In fact, we knew exactly what to do next.

Keun Sejin shrugged.

"Then... Are we leaving?"

The members looked back at each other a few times, and soon even Seon Ahyeon nodded.

"Let's go."

"OK~"

At that moment, as if he'd been waiting for this, Cha Eugene burst out of the trailer.

"...!?"

The staff on set were looking around, wondering what was going on, but we ignored them.

"Let's go find the manager."

"Yes."

And we simply said we'd discuss the cameo again after the meeting, then took our company staff and left the set.

The staff barely tried to stop us and just followed us out.

*They must have had a hard time dealing with all this, too.*

"If it comes to it, we'll also say we're going to claim damages for psychological distress and the impact on our tour schedule."

"Good."

By then, everyone was in a fighting mood.

Even Bae Sejin was furious as he entered the hotel.

"We don't... We don't have to put up with such rudeness. Do we?"

"Of course not."

And that wasn't the end of it.

In fact, from the moment they first guided us onto the filming set, I'd been planning to persuade the others to hold off on filming until after the meeting.

*This is exactly what I wanted.*

I shrugged.

I didn't even have to execute the plan, but it was going smoothly on its own.

*Thanks for being so unfriendly.*

That was how we left the set on the day we were supposed to film our cameo.

It was the first time TeSTAR had missed a prearranged schedule.

* * *

And the meeting place that night...

Surprisingly, the head producer of the film was there.

*It's rare.*

I hadn't expected someone that high up among the working-level staff to show up for an OST meeting.

"Shouldn't the director... be here instead?"

"I heard directors don't have that much clout in Hollywood. You saw how everything was divided, right?"

"I see."

It was a relief that the interpreter didn't translate our whispers.

Afterwards, we exchanged the usual greetings and small talk with the staff dispatched from T1 joining us.

The interpreter carefully conveyed the producer's words.

"He's asking if there was a problem on set. Why didn't you film?"

*Oh, did the producer come out because of this?*

T1 had been informed after we left the filming set, and it seemed there had been some communication between the higher-ups.

I nodded.

"We were told to wait without hearing any explanation. The trailer was cold, and we couldn't risk damaging our throats. It would have interfered with our tour schedule."

If I used English here, my vocabulary would be limited, and it would feel like I was the one adjusting to them. *I had no intention of doing so.*

As the interpreter translated these words, the producer nodded as if he understood.

And this was what came out.

"Hmm... He says he's very disappointed that things have turned out this way, but it's almost impossible to reschedule filming, so he wishes you'd show a little more respect for their busy schedules..."

"...!"

*What?*

Bae Sejin got angry first.

"No, that's not what we..."

"Hyung, hyung, wait."

Okay. It seemed that he misunderstood that we were taking our anger out on the cameo shoot because we were pissed off at being fooled with a few keywords during the request.

The atmosphere quickly cooled.

Suddenly, someone spoke in polite English.

[It's absolutely not normal to be told to wait without any further instructions. Also, our staff's messages weren't conveyed to the assistant director. Shouldn't respect go both ways?]

"...!"

Seon Ahyeon had spoken up in English.

His face was pale, but his gaze was determined.

And he hadn't discussed this with any of us beforehand.

Keun Sejin was amazed.

"Ahyeon must have been really pissed off," he whispered.

"I know."

I said no more. The producer seemed a little taken aback, but he didn't seem angry at Seon Ahyeon's words.

On the contrary, it seemed that only then did he begin to grasp the situation properly.

And after a while, the interpreter opened his mouth again.

"He says there seems to have been a mutual misunderstanding. He says he'll make sure your time is respected more on set."

"...Yes."

Seon Ahyeon kept his mouth shut. He didn't look very satisfied, but I gave him a quick pat on the back.

*Good job.*

He'd done more than enough to set the mood we needed. Seon Ahyeon smiled faintly.

And I opened my mouth again.

"Then, let's do the cameo again on another day and in another scene."

"...!"

*Does it look like we're giving in to your wishes? Far from it. We're going to squeeze you dry like a lemon.*
