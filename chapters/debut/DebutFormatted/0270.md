# Chapter 270 - The Encore Concert, I

The mood within TeSTAR's individual fandoms was at an all-time low.

To be honest, I didn't think there was an elegant solution that would magically solve this.

Expecting the idea of ‘The members are close, so let's all get along’ to actually work? That was wishful thinking.

Liking the same group was a good start, but wasn't personal experience what mattered in the end?

In that sense, the Cha Eugene controversy had all but destroyed the fragile trust between them that they'd barely managed to build after ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ ended.

*We can't openly praise Cha Eugene just to make up for all the unfair criticism, either.*

That would make Cha Eugene's individual fans feel a little better, but those who'd been attacked by his vicious individual fans early on would explode.

★$
-Honestly, Cha Eugene didn't do anything praiseworthy, so why are you feeding his akgaes[1]{T/N: Akgae/악개: When a fan likes one individual member of a group and hates the others. It is the shortened version of the word akseong gaeinpaen (악성개인팬), which means malicious individual fan.}?
$★

★$
-I'm tired... I'm really tired. I'm going crazy.
$★

Comments like these would pour out.

In the end, we couldn't let either side come away feeling that they'd won.

"...It's difficult."

Several ideas came to mind, but it was hard to see which would restore balance between the fans.

*Let's gather some more information.*

I was about to go online, but I stopped.

*...Wouldn't it be better to ask the parties involved?*

Even if I came up with a solution, I'd need the group's consent before putting it into practice. It would be easier to proceed if I took their thoughts into account.

I decided to ask the members about the situation.

If I explained the situation in detail, they might fall into the same wishful thinking—‘Our fans will eventually get along!’ So I used an analogy instead.

"So, how do the members of a group who are completely at odds with each other get along?"

"Alright."

Lee Sejin shrugged his shoulders.

"They're just people you work with~ Isn't it best to think of each other as business partners and leave it at that? You only need to get along at work."

That was the accepted way of handling it.

The answer was to stay focused on the goal, no matter how unpleasant things got. But being a fan wasn't a job, so there was no pressure to make that solution effective.

The atmosphere in our fandom right now was just like that... I was trying to settle the tension between the fandoms, and some people were probably already considering leaving for good.

*Maintaining the status quo isn't going to cut it.*

"But why are you suddenly asking this, Moondaemoondae? Is there a group this has happened to?"

"No, I was just asking."

Anyway, if I explained it to him in detail, it'd only stress him out, so I'd fill him in after I got things sorted.

I asked the same question to another guy.

And the results weren't much better.

"I-I'm not sure. Uh... Listening to each other and respecting each other's opinions..."

I stared at Kim Raebin.

*Next.*

"...How to get along?" Bae Sejin asked.

"Yes."

Bae Sejin looked at me suspiciously.

"...Um, you're really asking me?"

"......"

He wasn't confident in his social skills. I skipped over this guy too.

Ryu Chungwoo, who happened to be sitting next to him, answered as if it were obvious.

"If we all work hard together, we'll become close quickly."

"...Hmmm."

*Yeah, but...*

*They’ve already suffered enough.*

Of course, Ryu Chungwoo didn't know who I was talking about.

A little embarrassed, he added an explanation.

"Ah, I'm not saying hard work is everything... If there's a reward, and we all overcome the same obstacles to reach it, we'll become close. That's what I meant."

"Ah."

It sounded like advice drawn from his experience as a professional athlete.

*That's true, I guess.*

*Rewards and obstacles, huh?*

I decided to remember this and moved on.

The next answer was similar to Ryu Chungwoo's, but it sounded more appealing to me.

"Me! I know! They should do something fun together! Like sports!" Cha Eugene said.

"Sports?"

"Yes! Have them run around together. And if they win together, they'll become friends!"

Now that was a useful idea.

*Right... Isn't being in a fandom a bit like playing a sport?*

There were rules, wins and losses, and a sense of emotional accomplishment.

The only difference was that it was less immersive than actually playing a sport.

Still, it seemed like a useful perspective, so I asked again.

"What if they play together but end up fighting more because they have bad teamwork? What if one of them selfishly tries to score or won't pass the ball, taking all the credit?"

[Hmm... They'll need something to encourage them to behave!]

Cha Eugene answered without hesitation in English.

[You know, to make them ashamed of what they did. After that, if they focus on the game and win, they'll regret how they behaved and grow closer!]

"Hmm. Okay."

That was what it meant after all.

If they couldn't solve it by themselves, an authoritative third party would have to intervene.

But TeSTAR couldn't do that.

*We can't lecture the fans on right and wrong.*

It would be a strange dynamic, like a company trying to educate its customers. We'd rub them the wrong way. I could already imagine the backlash.

*Then all that was left... was to earn their sympathy?*

*Should we do something to soften them up so they'll want to listen to us?*

A solution was starting to take shape, but I frowned.

...It didn't feel right. It was too simplistic.

"Moo-Moondae?"

"Ah."

When I turned my head, Seon Ahyeon was hesitating, unable to enter the kitchen because I was blocking his way in the hallway.

*Good timing.*

"Is it okay if I ask you something?"

"S-sure!"

I got out of the way and asked Seon Ahyeon the same question one more time.

"What do you think we can do to help a group whose members are constantly upset with each other?"

"Huh??"

Seon Ahyeon was taken aback.

Cha Eugene looked at him with curious eyes.

"Um..."

And Seon Ahyeon thought about it quite deeply before giving an unexpected answer.

"I-I think... Wouldn't that be a pretty difficult situation?"

"...!"

He was saying it might be impossible.

"Because their relationship is already so bad... I think there must be a reason for it. So for them to suddenly start getting along... I-I think that would be difficult."

It was the most realistic, level-headed answer I'd heard yet.

"O-of course someone else might come up with a good solution, but... That's just what I think."

"...Okay."

I nodded.

Seon Ahyeon smiled awkwardly, and Cha Eugene firmly raised his hand.

"But I think my way's good too!"

"Ah, w-what way...?" Seon Ahyeon asked.

I let the two of them talk, lost in thought.

*I've heard everyone's opinions. Now, let's see...*

I'd reached a conclusion.

*We can't defuse the whole situation immediately by ourselves.*

Seon Ahyeon was right.

This wasn't something TeSTAR could resolve in an instant just by doing something.

So I'd put aside vague concepts like reconciliation and repairing relationships, and go back to my usual way of doing things.

What motivated people?

*Benefits.*

Victory, rewards, achievement, joy. Once people had these, they naturally tended to justify the process.

I remembered Ryu Chungwoo's words.

– *If there's a reward, and we all overcome the same obstacles to reach it, we'll become close.*

In the end, we had to make the fans recognize that every member had been necessary to achieve those results.

So we had to make our plans around that principle.

*We have to show them what they gain from each member of TeSTAR.*

We had to make them feel that when all seven of us were together, we were at our best—the most picturesque, popular, first-rate team.

So they'd know on a visceral level that keeping every member in the group was in everyone's best interests.

The enjoyment they got from supporting TeSTAR as a whole had to outweigh the stress of dealing with other individual fandoms they didn't like.

*And we'll give them an excuse for the way they're acting now.*

The emotional part would help this process go more smoothly.

*We'll show them how close the kids are and how hard they work together... So they'll come to see all seven members of TeSTAR being together as something precious.*

It was about giving the fans emotional, more *human* reasons to stay, so they wouldn't have to justify openly weighing up the gains and losses.

Without making it feel like anyone had won, they could tell themselves it was the warm and passionate atmosphere that drew them in, and the mood among the fandoms would settle.

*– They'll need something to encourage them!*

In a sense, this was what Cha Eugene said.

But since we couldn't admonish the fans, we had to go in a different direction.

*So, what I have to do next is...*

Beyond working hard at our jobs as idols, we had to show that we valued TeSTAR itself and that it meant a great deal to us personally.

Also, thinking about it now, a direct appeal would probably have the opposite effect.

*We shouldn't say it directly, but we should make them feel it deeply.*

That would lay the groundwork for a healthy sense of pride in TeSTAR as a group.

"Hmm."

[...A team match like that would solve everything! Why do you think there are so many sports movies?]

"I-I suppose so...!"

I laughed and left the kitchen, watching Cha Eugene eagerly explain his logic and Seon Ahyeon gradually come around to it.

"Where are you going!?" Cha Eugene called after me in Korean.

"To take a shower."

I had to organize my thoughts.

There was a lot to discuss when planning out the content, but there were priorities.

*I should ask them to contact W Live.*

First of all, I needed to get the encore concert set list sorted out.

She had a reason for saying that. She'd proudly succeeded in getting a standing ticket for the concert!

That was why she was at the concert venue now, leisurely browsing social media posts.

*Well, the atmosphere is nice.*

She put down her smartphone and glanced around.

For a while, the mood among the individual fandoms had been like walking on thin ice, but at an offline event like this, everyone seemed happy.

*I guess the online world really is a world of its own.*

Amid all the people full of anticipation, she clicked her tongue, then nodded to herself when she saw a social media post from someone else at the concert.

*So they're just hiding it, then.*

It was shit. Kim Raebin's fan closed her social media app and decided to focus on the concert. Otherwise, she felt like she'd jump in and say something too.

*Let's take a good look at Kim Raebin and Park Moondae.*

It was better than getting into arguments online.

She waited for the concert in the left block, which she'd carefully chosen based on her previous experiences.

Whatever the mood among the fans, her heart was pounding at the prospect of seeing her idols in person again.

And after a while, she heard a voice...

**[Hello, Seoul~!]**

*Wow!!*

The performance began with an explosion of energy.

The concert opened with their newest song, ‘Drill,’ bringing back the atmosphere of the previous concert in Seoul and drawing the audience in immediately.

*‘Present’ next!!*

Because it was an encore concert, the composition hadn't changed much from the last concert in Seoul, but knowing the set list in advance was an advantage.

It was satisfying to know what was coming next.

Above all, TeSTAR's concerts had a force you could only feel in person. Watching through a screen just wasn't the same.

TeSTAR had her screaming until her voice grew hoarse.

The concert flowed by at a skillfully controlled tempo, and before she knew it, the ending had come.

**[Bye-bye~]**

The lighting on the stage dimmed and returned to its default state.

"Phew!"

Of course, it wasn't really over. She already knew they'd do at least three or four more songs as an encore.

Kim Raebin's fan wiped the sweat from her forehead.

She'd shouted about how handsome they were so many times she felt like she was about to descend into another dimension!

So she tried to calm down a little by thinking about the next song.

*Was the first song of the encore a medley?*

During the tour, this was the part where they'd perform a local song, but originally it had been a short medley of the songs they'd performed in their ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ days.

*There's no reason they'd change it.*

Kim Raebin's fan looked around at the people singing the fan song while TeSTAR changed outfits backstage.

Given the atmosphere these days, if songs from ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ came on, they'd probably remind her of the bitter rivalry between the fandoms back then rather than bring back pleasant memories.

*Oh, whatever. We'll just have to live with hating each other.*

What did it matter as long as the group was doing well? She grumbled and pushed the thought away.

It was a common custom at this point in the concert, but judging by the SNS posts earlier, a quarter of them probably hated each other. It felt a little strange to stand here singing together.

Everyone seemed happy, though. At least that much was clear.

*I guess that's the effect a concert has on everyone.*

Even if only for a moment, as she sang along, she got a little taste of what it felt like to be a fan of the whole group.

*Come out, boys!*

It was the melody of the fan song everyone had been singing just moments ago, ‘You Are the Magic’.

And playing that melody on the screen with a guitar... It was Kim Raebin.

"Wow...?"

However, Kim Raebin's outfit was unusual.

Pajama pants and a T-shirt.

He was wearing pajamas that looked very comfortable, and he'd even tied his hair in a low ponytail.

"Eh."

Looking at it again, the video didn't seem to have been taken with a professional camera. It looked like phone footage with a filter.

It felt like a very private video.

**[Mm, mmm~]**

Next to Kim Raebin was Cha Eugene, who was lying down and humming, and Bae Sejin, who was sitting on the sofa and choosing food with a serious expression.

Behind them was Park Moondae bringing out drinks, and Lee Sejin, who was looking for a towel after showering. Lee Sejin spotted the camera and waved.

**[It's just something to remember the tour by.]**
