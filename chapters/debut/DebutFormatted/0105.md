*...Was that why he’d become especially sensitive around the third team battle?*

His parents had divorced because of his father’s gambling and violent behavior. Even afterward, his father kept threatening his mother for money.

“...Then why are you still in contact with him...?”

“...I-If I acted rashly, it could cause problems for my mom... or the group again. I already... had a problem with my old agency last time.”

“I’ve decided not to run away, so I’ll do whatever I can to handle it myself... That’s what I thought. ...But it didn’t work out.”
