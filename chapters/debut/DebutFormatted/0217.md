# Chapter 217

The cover stage.

It refers to a stage where performers perform songs by other groups or artists, not their own.

And naturally, even that had an implicit boundary.

*Do not cover a junior group's songs unless absolutely necessary.*

Because if you were not as good as your juniors, the picture would become extremely awkward. Moreover, even if you performed well, people would commonly say things like, 'They lack business ethics.'

In the usual situation where a cover stage had to carry out someone else's songs in just a few days of practice, the risk was quite fatal.

Moreover, many of the idol groups who appeared as mentors in this variety show produced by the production team of ⟨Idol Inc.⟩, were already successful groups.

*I mean, there are so many guys there who aren't at a level to only do a cover stage at the end-of-the-year concerts!*

It means that no one expected to see them doing something unexpected like covering a rookie group's hit song.

*Heol.*

For the record, related social media platforms around the same time zones were also flooded with '??'.

However, Cheongryeo's fan immediately had an epiphany.

*Though, wouldn't the number of options decrease too much if Jaehyun were to choose a song from his senior groups?*

This was because there were only a handful of idols who were seniors to VTIC and had a hit song within the last five years.

Moreover, it was not very entertaining to always cover old songs because that kind of content has been reused many times and was predictable.

*A song from a rookie girl group... would be fun for sure!*

Come to think of it, it was actually the first time since his debut year that she got to see Cheongryeo perform a female idol song!

The fan's heart began to race at the thought of a rare treat that suddenly emerged.

Reflexively, the thought that he might not perform well did not even cross her mind.

And by the time all of these thoughts flew by in the span of two or three seconds...

♪ *WE GONNA FLY HIGH* ♫

The screen stopped at the close-up, and the stage began.

*Boom boom boom boom*

Over the bass and the drums, the oud string instrument's strumming distorted like a synthesizer's electronic sound.

Then, the idols dressed in tight-fitting leather harness outfits took their positions in the formation and began the choreography on the purple-lit stage.

The sense of disharmony from being from different groups smoothly melted away.

Their years of experience and polished skills played a significant role, but ultimately... it was because the center stood out.

Naturally, it was Cheongryeo.

♪ *YOU'RE SHOUTING, HIGH UP\
FLY UP OVER THERE\
FROM THE RESTRAINTS, SHOOT UP\
BREAK FREE, PARADOX* ♫

*Aah!*

The strong chorus abruptly hit right from the get-go. Cheongryeo's fan automatically fell silent.

"Haa, so good..."

On the screen, Cheongryeo seamlessly connected several seconds of choreography like flowing water, effortlessly finishing the introduction as if it were a single fluid motion.

Without even having a chance to recall the original song, it felt like it passed by instantly.

It was an incredible display of skill.

*Kyaaah!*

Without the unintelligible screams and shouting of the members' names, similar posts also sporadically appeared in fan communities.

It wasn't only the key that was changed.

The nuance was different.

Miry-nay's debut song, 'High Up,' was a metaphorical song that depicted a caterpillar trapped in a spider's web, struggling and eventually breaking free to transform into a butterfly.

It had a touch of nostalgic and wistful flavor in some parts, yet it was sophisticated and rhythmically pleasing, perfectly matching the recent trend, making it a hit song.

It was an exceptional debut song that showcased the raw talent of a rookie, featuring deliberate sentiments scattered throughout the song... It left no room for criticism, but it also made the boldness that much more striking.

In the first place, the metaphor itself was about shaking off the controversy and noise surrounding season 4 of ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ and signifying the determination to rise above that.

And the already seasoned idols who had already experienced such acts did not get caught in this snare.

It meant that they did not try to clumsily replicate the original unique sentiments for this particular cover stage.

♪ *TEAR OUT YOUR SKIN, THROW IT AWAY\
TAKE OFF, TAKE OFF, TAKE OFF\
I'M TAKING OFF RIGHT NOW* ♫

They simply emphasized their energy and presence.

"Wow, damn."

There was no inherent stiffness that often accompanies rookie idols' debut songs. Instead, their expressive power was maximized through a sense of ease.

Furthermore, the choreography elements set by Cheongryeo prevented this sense of ease from appearing self-indulgent.

As a result, it became a stage that impactfully consumed the audience, delivering a unique feeling different from the original song.

Simply put, it became a great representation of a 'concept overflowing with great talent' that drives the global K-POP fandom into a frenzy.

Moreover, due to the fact that they were not from the same groups, the atmosphere of not wanting to concede to anyone intensified it.

Especially during the chorus.

♪ *YOU'RE SHOUTING, HIGH UP\
(HIGH-UP, UP, UP, UP)\
FURTHER AWAY, HIGHER UP* ♫

"Whoa!"

They went all out.

The formation led by Cheongryeo was fluid with exquisite control of dynamics, capturing everyone's attention.

The choreography rule that when the person in the center was maintaining control effectively, the overall quality would improve was faithfully adhered to.

Perhaps, as soon as the third episode was released, some people who skimmed through it quickly and selectively extracted performance scenes may already be tirelessly creating an endless stream of GIF files.

*Ah, seems like talks about business ethics are surely going to emerge!*

Even though Cheongryeo's fan envisioned an aggro-infested future, a smile never left her lips. It was thanks to the magic of the stage.

As the lyrics go, 'I will soar', it was overflowing with such immense energy that it felt like everything would be broken down and used as a jumping foundation.

♪ *HIGH UP!* ♫

Then came the second verse.

It was a part where a suddenly dramatic melody came in, emphasizing the vocal abilities.

Of course, Park Moondae took charge.

And this was the part that was originally assigned to Miry-nay's center, the first-place winner.

*He also dances pretty well.*

Cheongryeo's fan recalled the incredible all-rounder personality who ranked first in Season 4, but upon seeing Park Moondae on the screen, she reflexively felt a strange sense of unease.

Despite Park Moondae now being quite skilled in dancing, many non-fans still had doubts about his dancing abilities.

...It was because his first appearance in ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ dancing to 'POP☆CON' had left a very strong impression.

"Hmm."

However, those concerns ended up being groundless speculations.

♪ *EVEN IF IT HURTS, EVEN IF IT SCARS\
I CAN NEVER STOP\
CAN'T LET US DOWN* ♫

"Oh."

It was because Park Moondae's dance was seamless and entrancing.

The sensations felt when he bent his body were far from ordinary.

*He's pretty good.*

Since there were rarely any instances where the center was involved in such elaborate choreography in TeSTAR's usual title songs, the novelty was even greater.

Cheongryeo's fan nodded her head, thinking that perhaps it was a pity to be in a group with so many members specialized in dance.

*But his dancing style is somehow...*

It kind of reminded her of her bias.

However, before Cheongryeo's fan could fully grasp that strange sense of déjà vu, the song captured her ears first.

♪ *WE GONNA FLY HIGH!\
RISE AGAINST THESE CHAINS\
SAY GOODBYE* ♫

Certainly, there must have been some post-production involved, but it had a more dramatic development than the original version.

"Whoa."

The difference between the balance and vocal-specific members was significant.

*It's really good.*

Cheongryeo's fan, while watching the screen with renewed interest, suddenly felt a strange fondness when she caught a glimpse of Park Moondae's youthful face.

And at the same time, her anger surged up.

It was because she was reminded of a certain nasty guy.

*Just thinking about how the poor kids had to suffer because of the incompetent bastard loitering on his butt...*

That bastard didn't have any talent to speak of, so he must have been busy finding a place to hide!

*Beat it with being an all-fan! We should have cursed him out a long time ago to fix that thickheadedness!*

Thinking of the overseas fans who were still posting comments such as 'VTIC is 5 membersㅠ' made Cheongryeo's fan grind her teeth...

Anyway, her anger quickly subsided because the stage she was watching was so captivating and enjoyable that it felt bewitching.

"*Phew*."

She was happy seeing Cheongryeo doing this typical conceptual rookie idol song after such a long time.

♪ *WE GONNA FIND* ♫

After her idol practically flew around the stage, the song ended with Park Moondae briefly taking center stage during the final chorus.

"Hmm."

Although she thought that the part should have been given to Cheongryeo in terms of the distribution of abilities, Park Moondae was still pretty impressive, so the fan decided not to complain.

Of course, that kind of sentiment was possible only because Cheongryeo already had plenty of screen time.

"It's fun..."

Lying on her bed, Cheongryeo's fan felt satisfied as she saved the uploaded photos and GIFs of Cheongryeo. It has been a while since she experienced such fan service without a sense of distance.

And she also secretly saved some of the things about Park Moondae.

*Well, he's cute.*

The fan ended her day satisfied, speculating, 'Maybe he would sign a contract with LeTi after TeSTAR since he seemed close with the kids.'

And when the behind-the-scenes video was uploaded a few days later, she confirmed that Cheongryeo had instructed Park Moondae about the dance movements. She couldn't help but feel proud, thinking, 'As expected, my eyes didn't deceive me.'
