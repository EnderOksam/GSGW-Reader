# Chapter 248 - A Healing Retreat, IV

The villa had electricity again.

After washing up with hot water and drying our laundry in the dryer, we sat around and ate baeksuk[1]{T/N: Baeksuk/백숙: dishes made by boiling or steaming meat or fish to be cooked thoroughly without seasonings.}.

And boiled a pot of porridge.

"Ah, this really hits the spot..."

"I-it was delicious!"

"Hyung's cooking is always delicious!"

"Yes, thank you."

To be honest, since there were almost no vegetables, I didn't expect it to taste as good as restaurant food. Given the circumstances, though, they devoured both chickens in no time.

*It's fine.*

I began planning our meals for the days ahead.

If we had a cellar of that size, there would probably be other ingredients that were good for storage as well as meat.

As the situation was, we'd probably be forgiven for taking one or two more things, as long as we didn't go overboard.

...I was embarrassed and ran out quickly, so I couldn't look for it properly.

*Damn it.*

*– Ahhhh!!*

*– I told you it was weird!! Ahh!*

The dizzying stupidity we'd displayed came back to me, making my head spin.

Let's forget about it. It wouldn't do my mental health any good.

"Um, you still can't get in touch with the production staff?"

"That's right. Shall we go out and try it again?"

The others were trying to contact them with fully charged smartphones.

*Well, electricity has been restored, and we have something to eat, so there's nothing to be in a hurry about.*

The revised weather forecast wasn't very reliable, but things would probably be sorted out within the next two or three days.

Now, I was going to focus on being as relaxed as I could here regardless of the broadcast. Honestly, we were past the point of worrying about that.

I put down my spoon and made a suggestion.

"Shall we check if there are any vegetables in the basement warehouse? It's an emergency, so I think we can use it to some extent."

"Maybe we should."

"I agree~ Oh, and let's clean up the blood! The production crew will come here and be scared like us~"

"I-I'll find a mop...!"

So, as soon as the meal was finished, we went down to the basement again with the cleaning tools.

We didn't forget to bring a flashlight in case there was another power outage.

*There should be things that keep well... At least some dried spices or pickled olives to have with drinks.*

And, as expected, I found several more things we could eat.

"Oh, wine~"

"Could there be a possibility that this cheese is so expensive or limited that if we eat it, we won't be able to give enough compensation to the owner?"

"...Let's just eat something else."

But that wasn't the only thing I found in the basement.

"Hey, look at this."

"...!"

While cleaning the freezer blood stains...

I found another door that was skillfully hidden in the shadow of the gigantic machine.

Even this one was quite small. At my height, I had to bend slightly and move.

Ryu Chungwoo carefully inspected the door.

"Um, this..."

"...Isn't this the boiler room of this building?"

That seemed quite likely.

"Open it!!"

"We've already had a blackout. We can't afford to lose the gas too~ Let's check it out, right?"

"Yes, we should."

"Oh!"

"Then, let's open it."

It seemed that everyone agreed. I propped my mop against the wall, then turned the metal doorknob and pulled it.

*Durureuk.*

The door opened gently... It revealed something unexpected inside.

"...A passage?"

"Flashlight!"

When Bae Sejin shined a flashlight inside, a long concrete passageway came into view, curving to one side in the distance.

It wasn't the kind of structure you'd expect beneath a house.

*Hok.*

A dank breeze blew out from within, brushing past my nose.

"Oh my."

"Wow, what is this??"

"I-is it okay if we go inside...?"

The people who were so excited about the unknown area of exploration started whispering as if they had completely forgotten how frightened they were because of the bloodstains.

I understood them. Since we were stuck indoors doing nothing, this must have been interesting.

Ryu Chungwoo slightly narrowed his eyes and pointed inward with his finger.

"It looks like an emergency evacuation route... Look over there. Isn't that an emergency exit sign?"

"Oh, oh."

Indeed, a faint green light was emanating from the inside.

*Did they make it in preparation for a disaster?*

Then I think they should have said something to the guys filming here, but it was up to the owner.

Ryu Chungwoo shrugged.

"Seeing as we are in distress... I guess it wouldn't hurt to check it once."

"Let's go! Let's go!"

"...It will be fine."

So, with the entrance door propped open with a mop, we entered the passage.

Of course, those who had learned a lesson from the last incident turned on all their flashlights and walked comfortably down the path.

However, this path was considerably longer than expected.

Eventually, when we passed three or four emergency exit signs...

"This looks like we came out of the villa quite a bit, doesn't it?"

"...I know."

Since we didn't know what kind of passage this was and there was no end in sight, some people started to get nervous.

I spoke with a sigh.

"Wait here. A few people should quickly check the end of this path and..."

"NONONO!"

"What are you talking about? Let's all go together!"

"......"

What a mess.

Anyway, so we ended up going around the corner a few more times.

"Th-there, it's like a door...!"

Finally, we reached the end of the passage, where another door stood.

The door had an oddly well-designed lock, but it didn't seem to be working.

"Oh, the owner must have good taste."

"I know."

I waited until the others finished talking, then put my hand on the doorknob again.

"Then I will open..."

"Uh~ Moondaemoondae, wait! Shouldn't we all do this together?"

"That's right! I want to do it too!"

"...Is that so?"

Okay. Do whatever you want.

Thanks to the guys who were very active in proving our friendship without a camera, we ended up with the strange sight of seven people holding onto a single door.

They even shouted a chant. It was like a school retreat... Well, they were having fun, so there was no need to spoil it.

I saw Bae Sejin reach out a hand, and I slightly moved my hand away from the handle.

"Come on, let's open it together."

"Three, two... one!"

*Click.*

The first thing I felt was a strange sense of resistance.

However, when all seven young men pulled together, the door swung wide open with little resistance.

And...

*Udang-tang-tang!*

"...!"

"What, what??"

What appeared before my eyes was a dark indoor space, but that wasn't what caught my attention.

"Aaaagh!!"

"Wow!"

A scream very similar to the one I had heard in the cellar a few hours earlier rang out, making me want to leave this room.

"...??"

"Uh huh?!"

Outside the door... there were people.

They were even familiar faces.

They were hesitantly holding up camera tripods as if they would hit us with them at any moment... It was the production crew.

What?

*Why are they here?*

"No, why are you guys there!! Where the hell!!"

"Mom!"

Apparently, the other people thought the same thing as well.

The crew let out miserable groans and collapsed forward in relief.

"Ughhhhh!"

The two groups shouted at each other in complete bewilderment, only to calm down after a while.

"So... you're the emergency backup team."

"Yes... That's right."

It seemed that the production crew with haggard complexions hadn't even washed properly for several days already.

"Who would have known that the weather would suddenly turn like this and we would be locked up?"

When I heard that story, I immediately thought of it.

*That text.*

★:

Well, come to think of it, no matter how natural an image they wanted to draw, it was crazy to leave the cast alone on the island for any length of time without any production crew. Who knew what kind of accidents might happen?

However, it seems that such a natural disaster was unexpected.

One of the production crew members shuddered.

"At most, there was only a forecast for showers... Ugh."

That was why the production crew was isolated here without any preparation. They seemed to have suffered quite a bit from a lack of water and food.

And, here came the obvious question.

"But here...?"

"Ah, this is the entertainment show set! The container!"

"...!"

I remembered what I checked outside of the villa on the first day.

"The container with that logo?"

"Yes, yes."

It was said that the production crew used the container for the escape entertainment show that was not demolished as a preparation base for filming.

"We were going to use it as tourism content for everyone when the weather got bad... Yes, it's ruined."

"Hahahahaha..."

In any case, thanks to this, it was said that the container was habitable enough for people to spend several days in.

So, the production crew was using it as a temporary residence, but we suddenly burst through the wallpaper and scared the life out of them.

"W-wallpaper?"

"Yes. No, you don't even know where it is, so how did you get here?"

And the production crew was stunned when they saw the path that we came through.

"Wow, there was something like this?"

"I really won't leave Director Park alone..."

As a secret story that I learned later, it was said that it was actually a passage prepared for the previous escape variety show that its cast had never discovered.

The main PD and others were aware of it, but the youngest duty officers who remained in the emergency standby team were unaware.

Anyway, there was one reason why they hadn't contacted us and had stayed cooped up here.

"Ah, you don't have much to eat, and you must be anxious... We were afraid that knowing we were stranded here too would only burden you further."

They brought us here saying they would let us enjoy the healing content to our hearts' content, but they were ashamed now.

I read the context.

*They must have thought that TeSTAR would be mad...*

In fact, from the perspective of the production team, they wouldn't have had anything to say in their defense even if we'd gotten upset.

However, there was an unexpected little backstory.

"It looks like the clips will be ruined..."

"...??"

"Clips from the shoot... What?"

I was puzzled. Surely all the cameras must have run out of battery soon after the storm began.

"Ah, that... Maybe some of the fixed cameras are still alive?"

"What??"

"It is a piece of new equipment. The infrared camera is probably still alive."

"Huh, that's right~"

"......"

It'd be okay. After all, there was no way that there'd be a camera in the basement. At least our horrified reactions down there wouldn't make it into the show.

Anyway, after that, a perfectly reasonable suggestion followed.

"There are quite a few ingredients in the villa! Don't worry, let's go together. Aigoo, you worked so hard..."

"Yes. Let's talk after you've washed up and eaten."

"A-are you feeling okay...?"

"I'm okay... Heokheok."

"Thank you."

The production crew moved to the villa, thankful that there was an indoor passage instead of the dangerous unpaved road with wind and rain.

And as soon as they came through the door, they cried out.

"Ah, it leads here!"

That... What was that familiar response?

"Writer Choi said we'd originally planned to hold some kind of treasure hunt here for today's content."

"There's no one in charge of the installation here, is there? That's right. Anyway, so it's our first time coming in... Oh, there were a lot of ingredients here. That's a relief."

The producers shouted brightly.

...And immediately, they scattered all over the place, and they found hidden cameras somewhere and checked them.

"Hey, I'm glad. We can save these cuts!"

"......"

Was this real?

I opened my mouth unconsciously.

"...Story-wise, wouldn't it be a little disconnected? There are no cameras in the passageways."

"Just in case, we kept our cameras running in the container to record everything. Your entrance should've been captured on camera!"

"Of course, the editing will go well, so don't worry about that."

Okay, let's give up on this part. No, it would be nice to have content. My worries were soothed.

*As long as there's nothing from the basement during the power outage.*
