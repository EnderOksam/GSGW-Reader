# Chapter 284 - Do You Need Help?

A cold sweat was dripping down my neck.

Or maybe it was just sweat from hiking that felt cold because of the situation.

Because I was in a completely shitty situation.

*A plane crash?*

It was the first time I had heard of it.

*Organize... I need to organize my thoughts first.*

There was no way for me to find out exactly how Park Moondae's parents died.

...It was because by the time I ended up in his body, Park Moondae had already gotten rid of everything except what was in his single-room apartment.

All I could confirm were his parents' names and ages. Just in case, I'd searched several times for articles using their region of residence and names, but nothing about an accident came up.

*Then it must've been something common enough not to make the news...*

It seemed natural to assume it had been a traffic accident, so I'd let everyone else think the same thing, too.

For example, Ryu Chungwoo.

*– Mentioning my parents' traffic accident... must have made me sound like I was making it up.*

I'd thought a traffic accident was likely, and even if it wasn't true, there was no one who could prove otherwise.

But an entirely different accident had just overturned that assumption.

And I... Fuck, I rode airplanes fine and even went skydiving.

"......"

*Damn.*

Realizing that there was still water in my mouth, I swallowed.

The water bottle was almost empty. I could almost hear the digital watch on my wrist.

It felt as if it were counting down the seconds I'd delayed my answer.

*– Tick.*

*Excuses... I can't think of a reasonable one.*

I was in quite a bind.

The car accident, the fire, and my overreaction to Ryu Chungwoo's accident all fit together so perfectly with the story of 'Ryu Gunwoo'.

And whatever excuse I came up with, denying something that had already been confirmed as fact wouldn't work.

*He's almost certain now.*

He'd figured out that my words and actions didn't match Park Moondae's past at all, then thought it over several times before bringing it up with me.

I slowly screwed the lid back on my canteen and tried to think quickly.

Ryu Gunwoo and Park Moondae.

*– Tick.*

"......"

*Okay.* The most realistic hypothesis I could come up with was probably my only way out.

*All I have to do is add a psychological condition to my amnesia.*

The psychological explanation would be that Park Moondae had mistaken Ryu Gunwoo's situation for his own.

I could say that 'Ryu Gunwoo' had helped me a great deal, so I'd formed a strong emotional attachment to him and become confused while recovering my memories.

*But the moment I use this excuse... I'll get sent to see a psychiatrist.*

I'd probably have to go on a long hiatus. This... Fuck, it would be better to talk about the status window.

*No, am I going to explain that there's a status window only I can see? What bullshit.*

I'd be lucky if he just thought I had delusions of grandeur and left it at that.

*Either way, I can only see myself ending up in a psychiatric hospital.*

None of those excuses would work.

*– Tick.*

In the meantime, time was running out.

Ryu Chungwoo was obviously still looking at me. I didn't even have to check. His gaze felt like a countdown.

*This bastard brought me all the way to the peak on purpose.*

I'd run out of stamina, none of the other guys were there to back me up, and he'd left me with nothing I could use to ease the situation.

There was a limit to how long I could stall, but I couldn't think of any brilliant countermeasure.

*Fuck, I can't think of anything.*

*I had no options left.*

*– Tick.*

This wouldn't do. I wanted to scream, but I decided to go for it.

I opened my mouth, ready to say, 'I don't remember the plane crash—only the explosion, the fire, and that I was on my way somewhere, so I assumed it was a traffic accident...' But the other guy spoke first.

"If you don't feel like you can tell me the truth, it can't be helped."

"...!"

"Tell me when you feel ready."

Ryu Chungwoo said this casually, then put his canteen in his bag.

Then he casually turned away.

"Then, shall we go down?"

That was it. Ryu Chungwoo moved slowly.

I shut my mouth and followed him to the base of the mountain.

But the tension remained. I knew he'd only given me more time to answer.

Besides, now I had to come up with a reason why I couldn't answer right away.

*This is driving me crazy.*

That was how life with my roommate became fucking uncomfortable.

***

And after a few days...

"What... what are you doing?"

It was 2 AM. Bae Sejin, who came out to drink water, saw me sitting on the sofa in the living room and almost fell over.

"I can't sleep."

"Then... you should at least go in and lie down."

"I don't want to."

Bae Sejin looked at me with his mouth wide open, and I turned away from his gaze.

It must've seemed ridiculous, as though Chungwoo and I had passed a baton between us.

But in this situation, who could be thick-skinned enough to go into that room and sleep comfortably?

My roommate had pointed out the contradiction in my story and given me time to think before I had to answer.

And then he hadn't brought it up for days, leaving us with an ominous sort of peace.

*What is this, the eye of a typhoon?*

I'd rather he threw a fit and accused me of lying every time I opened my mouth. What was he thinking?

*Damn it.*

I leaned back on the sofa, trying not to sigh.

Still, falling asleep in the living room would be too obvious, so I planned to go back in once my head cooled down and I felt a little better.

*It usually works if I sit out here until I'm too exhausted not to lie down.*

But Bae Sejin seemed to have a different idea.

He gave me an odd look, then seemed to realize something.

Then he asked with a slightly expectant look.

"Park Moondae, you and me... would you like to switch rooms?"

*Hey!*

"I-I'm not saying I don't like my room. You just don't look well!"

*I can read you like a book.*

I thought about it for a while, then held back a sigh and shook my head.

"No. I don't want to switch."

"...Is that so?"

"Yeah. I'll probably feel better soon enough."

I needed to.

If this kept up until our comeback and we had to film more content, everything would fall apart again. The summer package nightmare would return.

*So I have to find a solution somehow.*

But there was no solution—no explanation Ryu Chungwoo would accept.

I leaned my head back against the sofa, a bitter taste in my mouth.

Bae Sejin drank a glass of water as he'd intended, then quietly came over to me.

"Ahem... Ryu Chungwoo... He's a good guy."

"...Yes. I know."

"Yeah! You too! You're a good guy, too."

"......"

*You're going to wake someone up at this rate.*

His ears red, Bae Sejin lowered his voice. After clearing his throat a few times, he spoke more quietly.

"There are people who just aren't meant to be around each other... but I don't think that's the case with you two."

"......"

"Just tell him honestly if you feel uncomfortable with him. Don't stay out in the living room."

I paused for a moment, let out a small sigh, then grinned.

"It sounds like advice from experience."

"Yes."

I'd expected him to recoil in surprise, but Bae Sejin unexpectedly nodded seriously.

"I prefer the living room. I find it comfortable, but you don't seem to feel the same way."

"......"

"You want to get along with Chungwoo, right? He's... not the sensitive type, so he'll listen if you tell him."

Sorry, but it was the opposite situation. He was the one who'd be uncomfortable around me.

I shook my head. Even if I explained, I'd just be giving him information he couldn't make sense of.

I gave him the answer he wanted to hear to end the conversation and get him to leave.

"I'll give it a try. Thanks for your help."

"Ahem. Well, I'm glad if it helped. Good night."

"Yeah. Good night to you, too."

Bae Sejin walked into the room with his shoulders straight. He must have thought he had solved one thing.

*So he was trying to act like a hyung, huh?*

Seeing that, strangely, my heart felt a little lighter. I laughed and suddenly remembered what Bae Sejin said.

*Ryu Chungwoo will just listen to what I say... huh?*

"......"

I lay down in the living room and sorted through my thoughts.

This time, I didn't focus on finding an explanation that wouldn't sound strange.

Instead, the focus was on 'what Ryu Chungwoo wanted to hear'.

I arranged everything he must have thought through before asking me that question at the peak in chronological order and tried to see the bigger picture.

"......"

And a few hours later...

"Hoo."

I'd reached a conclusion.

It was a little funny, but Bae Sejin's attempt to act like a hyung had actually worked.

I made a suggestion to Ryu Chungwoo in the van on the way back from that day's recording.

***

My suggestion was simple.

*– How about exercising on Thursday morning?*

*– In the morning?*

*– Yes, I think that's the only free time we have.*

Now that we were focusing on preparing for our comeback, we were back to strict dieting and fasting, along with short, intense calorie-burning workout routines.

As expected, we had no time to exercise at our leisure. My suggestion was that we spend some of the little time we had for meals and rest that week on exercise instead.

Ryu Chungwoo looked a bit puzzled, but soon nodded.

*– Yeah, sure.*

When the agreed-upon time came and we set out to exercise, he seemed a little surprised.

"Are you sure about this?"

"Yes."

It was the same hiking trail we'd gone to so often over the past few weeks that I was getting tired of it.

"Let's go."

We had no time to talk any more, so I immediately broke into a light jog.

"Heok."

It was getting close to early summer, and the mountain was growing hotter.

There were bound to be people hiking in the morning, but thanks to the weather, there were fewer people and it was still as quiet as before.

So it was rather good.

*Nobody will overhear us.*

I stopped midway up the mountain, where there were almost no people around.

Of course, that wasn't the only reason I'd stopped.

"Hmm?"

"As you said, I don't think we need to go all the way to the top. I like the view from here better."

"Oh, yeah?"

Ryu Chungwoo looked a little taken aback, but soon nodded and turned to look at the scenery.

"It's gotten greener."

"I know, right?"

I stood beside him for a moment, admiring the view, then caught my breath.

And counted down.

*He would have guessed by now.*

I'd followed the same route he'd taken before questioning me, giving myself time to prepare.

No, he'd probably guessed the moment I suggested we exercise together.

*He'll be expecting an answer to the contradiction he pointed out.*

Sure enough, Ryu Chungwoo spoke cautiously.

"...Um, do you have something you want to say?"

I shook my head.

I hadn't decided to speak to him because I'd come up with a perfect excuse that could turn the situation around.

What I thought of after hearing Bae Sejin's words last night was something else.

"There's nothing I want to say... but there is something I *have* to say."

It was something I had to do.

Ryu Chungwoo had the right to receive an answer to this question.

*Back then, this guy believed I was giving him shit because he'd reminded me of my parents' accident, and he let it go.*

Even after that, when I vaguely told him I'd suddenly remembered something, he helped me look for 'Ryu Gunwoo' without objection. I'd only asked for his help because they were relatives, so he might know him.

But it turned out the reasons and explanations I'd given for my actions didn't add up.

*I'm surprised he's not angry at me.*

This guy hadn't been prying into anything. He'd just gotten caught up in it and noticed a contradiction that didn't fit.

*Honestly, even if I found an appropriate excuse here, it's over as soon as another contradiction comes out...*

At that point, I couldn't blame him if he lost trust in me and cut ties with me altogether in a fit of anger, our group activities be damned.

What made it worse was that even after he'd figured out the situation, he'd waited quite a while without saying anything, looking for the right time.

Probably until I'd recovered my physical strength and mental energy after the accident.

*He probably brought it up at the peak so I could clear my head after some exercise and we could talk in private...*

There was no malice or intent to meddle.

So I'd concluded that the least I could do was tell him the truth, leaving out unrealistic things like the reset and the status window.

*Not that I expect him to accept what he hears.*

Either way, there was no convincing explanation. If I had a fucking conscience, this was the least I could do for him.

I sighed and spoke slowly.

"Hyung."

"Mhm."

"I know... that some things about my story might not seem to add up."

I reflexively put my hand in my pocket, then removed it when I realized I didn't smoke anymore.

And I spoke slowly.

"But I've never lied to you."

"......"

"Well... I could try to explain, but I'm having trouble understanding it myself... Oh, it's not a psychological problem. It's not like I'm mistaking myself for someone else."

This bastard, with the best intentions, would send me to a mental hospital if I messed up. I held back a sigh and brushed the back of my neck.

"I was just... doing what I could."

Ryu Chungwoo did not refute. I continued talking.

"It's true that traffic accidents and fires make me uncomfortable because of my parents' accident. Of course, hyung might think I freaked out at you during the summer package and lied about why, but..."

"Wait a minute."

Ryu Chungwoo cut me off, looking a little taken aback.

Then he smiled wryly.

"Moondae. I think you're misunderstanding something... I'm not saying I distrust you or trying to accuse you of lying."

*What?*

"During the summer package? Everyone who saw you then would never say that your pain was made up. I don't think you lied to me."

I stared at him blankly.

"And... You saved my life when I almost died, at the risk of dying yourself. That's a fact that'll never change, isn't it?"

*That...*

"How could I get angry at the person who saved my life just because something they said doesn't add up? Don't worry about that."

Ryu Chungwoo slowly added:

"What I want to ask is this, Moondae."

"......"

I listened.

"Do you need help right now?"
