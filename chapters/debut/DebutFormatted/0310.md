# Chapter 310 - ⟨Wave for Me⟩, I

TeSTAR's concert opened by recreating the feeling of being underwater in the venue.

And what unfolded next was unlike anything the gamer who'd tagged along with her friends had expected.

*Huh.*

Usually, whether it was a singer or an idol, the word 'concert' brought a certain format to mind.

A performance in which the performers took center stage, showcasing everything they'd prepared.

The hit songs and the latest tracks were mixed in a carefully curated order so the performance never grew stale.

And in fact, TeSTAR had been doing such concerts up until now, so her friends gave her a similar explanation.

★:
-You'll have fun because you'll recognize a lot of the songs haha

-That's right, and all the kids are really handsome and good at what they do. You won't be wasting your money! ㅠㅠ Don't cancel!
:★

But not now. Instead, this concert had a story.

$p
♪ *The night falls slowly*\
*I don't want to*\
*Escape the night* ♫
p$

Through the dry ice, elegant choreography inspired by modern dance unfolded, fabric fluttering around the members as if they were floating in the sea.

The first song was ⟨Midnight, and Next⟩, arranged perfectly to suit an orchestra.

It was a very unique choice.

This was because idols with years of hit songs behind them usually performed only a medley of their early post-debut songs, at most.

But TeSTAR had gone so far as to arrange this song specifically for the concert.

Because this was the starting point of the narrative that would be shown in this performance.

$p
♪ *Forget the songs, the dreams*\
*This is midnight*\
*Your midnight* ♫
p$

The boys, lost in the sea, seemed to drift into the past, taking their dreams with them.

The clock ticked and gears turned, and the members sank into the sea, re-experiencing the past.

As the clock tolled, announcing the end of ⟨Midnight, and Next⟩, the members lay still in the depths of the sea.

+=
[Dang-]
=+

And the stage background naturally became a classroom.

As if in a dream, the lighting changed and became clearer. 

The song that followed naturally was ⟨Hi-five⟩. A bright and cheerful rock track about being young and alive!

The members stood up, looking a little dazed as the intro played, then grinned at each other and stamped their feet as they ran around the stage.

$p
♪ *My feet are moving*\
*Faster! Farther!* ♫
p$

The boys, seemingly sent back to the past, pursued their dreams, fell in love, met enemies, and grew strong enough to overcome them.

A TeSTAR song was woven into each part of the story.

Of course, TeSTAR were idols, and the story was not as explicit as a real musical or opera.

However, the audience was able to fully understand the context through the lyrics, the atmosphere, and the VCR that appeared in between.

Especially if they didn't have a lot of background knowledge about TeSTAR!

*Were the original songs made with this purpose in mind?*

The gamer was baffled.

For fans familiar with the songs, it was easier to follow, but the members had even included expressive narrative elements between each song to enhance the story.

And the gamer took everything in with all her senses, rather than just listening to the music.

Perplexity, joy, tension, vigilance...

With TeSTAR's incredible playfulness and expressive power, the gamer quickly immersed herself in songs unfamiliar to her.

The members, portraying the boy who'd grown through understanding love, put on an intense performance as if showing off his newfound strength.

$p
♪ *Tuck in, tear it up, enjoy it*\
*Now, spring out!* ♫
p$

The narrative gave the performance more depth.

By the time the sound of gunfire rang out and the powerful concert-specific performance with dancers burst onto the stage, she was almost unconsciously clapping and cheering!

"Oh my gosh."

She wasn't even accustomed to idol concerts, yet she was reacting as though on instinct?

She was baffled by her own reaction—she hadn't come to see a musical, after all—but there was no need to feel strange about it.

TeSTAR was taking it even more seriously than she was.

The high notes, their rich voices, and the extreme choreography combined into the gripping ⟨Spring Out⟩ ending. Then the lights flashed.

Shouts filled the air, and her heart raced as the story reached its climax.

But in an instant, everything came to a halt as if washed away.

"...!"

After the song ended with a splendid climax... It was time for a break.

#><*[※Intermission~※]*><#

They'd even included an intermission in the setlist.

It was as though they were giving the audience time to think over what they'd just seen and grow even more excited for what came next.

+=
[Hurray!]
=+

The gamer opened her mouth as she watched the VCR playing, with the members running around in animal pajamas, like some kind of public service announcement.

It wasn't just her.

As if it were a real intermission, the lights came on and the atmosphere relaxed. Given the tacit permission to express themselves during the break, the fans started going wild.

"Oh my god. Oh—my—god."

"What is this?"

Numerous people reached for their smartphones and frantically typed away.

And the gamer also started getting messages.

★:
-Great genius, great genius, great genius

-😵‍💫
:★

They were her two friends.

When she looked over at where the two were sitting, they seemed to have sent their KakaoTalk messages without taking their eyes off the VCR.

*Huh.*

It was kind of scary.

But at the same time, she understood them a little better now.

*TeSTAR made the concert fun even for people like me who don't know anything about them.*

A concert like this was worth seeing at least once. She finally understood why her friends had insisted so strongly that she not sell her ticket, she admitted inwardly.

Unaware that this was TeSTAR's first concert of this kind and that social media was going wild over it, she waited for the second act with far more excitement than before.

*Will they start fighting in the second act?*

She was familiar with that kind of classic hero's journey from games! With the members right there singing and dancing brilliantly, how could it not be fun?

Soon, the performance resumed with lively announcements.

**[Hey, everyone. You're probably wondering, what on earth are these guys thinking? Aren't you curious?]**

It was Lee Sejin.

The audience burst out laughing at his mischievous comment.

**[You'll find out soon what happens to TeSTAR. Here comes Act 2.]**

The lights went out.

As light orchestral music began at a carefully chosen tempo, the audience's hearts filled with anticipation.

The gamer suddenly realized the lightstick in her hand could be controlled remotely and stared at it with wide eyes.

$p
♪ *What makes people live?* ♫
p$

The opening performance of the second act was about chasing their enemy. A streak of light shot out from the stage and rippled through the lightsticks along a long trajectory across the audience seats. They'd incorporated the lightsticks to enhance the mood and the story unfolding onstage.

*Oh my god!*

The gamer was really getting into it now.

*They'd made it feel as though the audience was taking part in the performance!*

This almost obsessive attention to detail struck a chord with the hardcore gamer in her!

*They're so serious about this!*

Yes.

TeSTAR had carefully planned this concert and poured all their skills into making it immersive and full of storytelling.

However, no matter how carefully the songs were put together, there were unavoidable gaps in the story.

TeSTAR's songs weren't composed for a single story, and they didn't have the variety of songs a musical would.

So the cover stages filled that void.

They included both solo and unit performances.

The second act was filled with recent popular songs as highlights and cover songs that filled gaps in the story.

So even backstage, TeSTAR's movements had to be carefully calculated.

* * *

"Heok."

"Three minutes!"

Groans, curt answers, and shouts filled the backstage area.

The noises were grating, but I continued doing my mental calculations.

*Time's tight.*

Connecting each stage to the next and maintaining the atmosphere mattered even more, so there was less time than usual to catch my breath. There were no talk segments or breaks between the songs.

– Wow!

I pressed my tongue against the roof of my mouth to avoid getting swept up in the cheers I'd just heard.

And I thought soberly.

*As expected, some people will love it, and some won't.*

Now, the audience loved it because it was fresh and fun, but the moment the excitement cooled, the complaints would start.

Some fans would miss the usual interaction between idols and fans at a concert.

Planning a W Live stream after the concert to make up for that had been the right call.

*Good.*

There was no time to savor that, though. The guy next to me, who was going up for the next stage, looked like he was dying.

I asked Kim Raebin, who was as pale as a sheet.

"Are you worried?"

"What? N-no. I will do my best to show a stage that will satisfy the fans...!"

Kim Raebin answered quickly, but he was opening and closing his hands.

It meant he was more nervous than usual.

*It's no wonder.*

I crossed my arms.

We lacked experience with this.

We had to understand and perform songs someone else had created for us without touching the music.

Since debuting, we'd had very little experience fitting ourselves into a completed work in accordance with its composer's intent.

*We'd always participated in songwriting and concept planning.*

It was as if we'd skipped the basic training rookies normally undergo for four or five years.

We couldn't touch the music, so we had to learn to make the most of everything else... Especially ourselves.

*We'll all learn from this opportunity.*

That was what we had to master one by one and show properly today, through our solo and unit performances.

Why did Keun Sejin revise the setlist in such a way that the story stood out in the first place?

It was simple.

*"We're not supposed to touch the songs themselves~ Shouldn't we use that extra time here?"*

Instead of spending time arranging music, we could use that time to hone our craft further.

We could add another element to what the performers considered and expressed onstage: context.

I patted Kim Raebin on the back.

"You did great in practice, so relax and have fun."

I heard him swallow nervously.

"...Yes."

Kim Raebin nodded firmly.

Well, actually, he didn't need to be so nervous.

"Moo-Moondae, Raebin and I have to g-get moving..."

The guy who'd finished changing his outfit hurried over to Kim Raebin.

Seon Ahyeon.

He was the member who would do a unit performance with Kim Raebin on the next stage. I smiled and opened my mouth.

"Seon Ahyeon."

"Yes?"

"Kim Raebin will do great, don't you think?"

Seon Ahyeon answered without blinking an eye.

"Yes."

"...!"

There wasn't a trace of hesitation in his firm answer. It was rare for him to sound so self-assured.

"Thank you...!"

Kim Raebin seemed touched by that. The two shook hands, ready to go onstage.

"L-let's do well and come back!"

"Yep!"

*Good thing we'd paired these two together.*

There was a reason for Seon Ahyeon's confidence. He was the only one of us for whom this wasn't entirely new.

He'd already trained for this sort of thing.

Seon Ahyeon had devoted his entire childhood to interpreting and expressing the intent of a given work as perfectly as possible.

He had been a top ballet student.

"W-we're going now...!"

"Yes."

I watched Kim Raebin and Seon Ahyeon break into a trot.

The two made quite a picture as they went.

The performance right before this was ⟨Nightmare⟩, expressing a young man's struggle to resist temptation.

Even if Park Moondae's melancholic solo part added to the 'Nightmare' performance was so incredibly sexy that she wanted to stay in that moment forever, she had to get ready for the next stage!

Calming herself, she fixed her gaze on the screen again.

"Start!"

And he focused on the screen.

The stage scene was being broadcast on a large monitor installed by their dedicated management team.

The VCR ended and the music began. This unit stage was the final step in the build-up to the climax.

*Rrrrrrrrrrr!*

*This is a good rendition.*

At this point in the story, the boys had been defeated at a decisive moment in a confrontation with the enemy.

If ⟨Nightmare⟩ had been about the temptation of greater power, this performance was about inner conflict.

The thundering electric guitar that raged like a fierce temptation soon died down, replaced by the graceful, nimble sound that had appeared in the first act.

A violin solo accompanied by quiet vocals.
