# Chapter 234 - Daydream, VI

Bae Sejin stared down at his phone with haggard eyes.

The agency claimed it was removing articles that deliberately stoked anxiety.

But Bae Sejin didn't believe them.

*Isn't this just because the reporters have run out of things to write about?*

The agency had cut off access to information about the accident, leaving the press with no more facts to report.

Yes. The company was desperately blocking stories related to the former manager from the press.

In the end, the press described it as 'neglect behind the wheel by a former manager who was soon to resign.'

*This is nonsense.*

Bae Sejin clasped his hand tightly.

Some of the members had questioned the staff persistently and discovered how the former manager had managed to gain access.

@r@*– The venue staff weren't told about the change of managers... They let him in because they thought he'd returned late after running an errand during the day.*@r@

-...!!

In other words, the agency had failed to tell the relevant staff that the former manager had been fired. That was partly why the accident had happened.

Bae suspected the agency knew this and was desperate to keep it out of the press, even asking headquarters to help cover it up.

The agency was already under fire for failing to protect its artists.

If the full truth emerged, the damage would be devastating, so it was stalling for time.

*If they have time to cover their own asses, they should be more worried about Moondae's recovery.*

Of course, he had no expectations.

If several members hadn't lost their tempers with the agency, it would already be drawing up plans to continue as a six-member group.

*They're disgusting...*

Bae Sejin covered his mouth.

He was sick of the agency's ruthless capitalism, which reduced the group to a source of revenue.

It didn't seem to care whether its artists were safe.

He had seen it so many times since he was young that he was tired of it.

*Accidents like this are so common in this industry!*

Why did the same things keep happening?

The agency was no different from his previous one, apart from its reputation as a 'legitimate' operation.

Bae Sejin gave so much strength that the hand that covered his mouth became white.

*I need to find a better agency.*

There had to be agencies run by people who valued basic human decency.

There had to be.

*Just three more years, and...*

Bae Sejin almost fell into an endless stream of consciousness. But he barely managed to get out.

Because he thought of Park Moondae.

...He still hadn't woken up.

"......"

Bae Sejin weakly dropped the hand holding the smartphone.

No matter what plans he made, he knew that in this kind of work, they would only work properly with Park Moondae’s help.

*He's so good at handling things like this... It's almost like he's been doing it his entire life.*

Bae Sejin had never met anyone his age with such seemingly superhuman abilities.

Moondae had talent, confidence, and courage, which made him an ideal entertainer; he was also remarkably good at dealing with people.

Bae had felt inferior during the audition show, but not after debuting.

There was no point in comparing himself to Moondae anymore.

*So...I also received a lot of help.*

He'd once promised to repay Moondae someday, though he'd never been sure how.

*I always doubted Moondae would ever need my help.*

In fact, Bae Sejin coldly evaluated that he was too clumsy to help.

But this time, Bae Sejin really had to move. Something had to be done.

Park Moondae couldn't wake up, and the other members were injured or physically and mentally exhausted, so they couldn't show their usual skills.

*I'm used to being tired and worn down.*

He'd spent years exhausted and touchy, so he knew how to function even when things were difficult.

That was why he needed to be the one to step up now.

"......"

Bae Sejin removed the hand that was covering his mouth. His eyes were shining.

*Let's do it.*

Bae Sejin took out his phone and found the email address he'd been looking for.

He took a deep breath and started drafting his message.

......

His hands were shaking.

*Still, I'm the only one who can do it!*

Bae Sejin gritted his teeth and pressed the send button.

He didn't know if this would be an effective option. But in his opinion, it was the right thing to do.

"...Whoa."

Bae Sejin sat back in his seat.

And he tried to stay calm while fiddling with the hamster-patterned case a fan gave him.

It was then.

He got a call.

Bae Sejin hurriedly checked the smartphone screen.

"Uh. what..."

Several excited voices spilled through the phone.

Bae listened as patiently as he could, then froze when the meaning of their words sank in.

"......"

"W-wait, wait! What did you just say...?"

Bae Sejin stuttered and ran out of the meeting room before he could finish his own sentence.

His heart raced.
