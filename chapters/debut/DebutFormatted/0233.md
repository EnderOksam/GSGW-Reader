# Chapter 233 - Daydream, V

Kim Raebin adjusted his grip on the box as he walked down the hospital corridor.

His other arm was still in a soft cast, restricting his movement, so he had to be careful.

He pressed his lips tightly together.

*It'd be a disaster if I dropped the box!*

The journalists who had crowded the hospital after the accident had largely dispersed, letting him come and go more easily.

Quite some time had passed since news of the terrible car accident first broke.

After more than ten days of hearing that Moondae was in a coma recovering from surgery, most of them had moved on to other stories.

That was how things went in the entertainment industry, where something happened practically every day.

However, the internet was still in chaos with all sorts of pessimistic speculation articles, columns, fan concerns, and anger swirling around.

★$
– **[TeSTAR Park Moondae… Possible risk of being brain dead?]**

– **[The accident that befell the “May Bridegroom” ⟨Reconstitute the Day⟩]**

– **[Prayers for Park Moondae’s recovery, seven days after the operation]**
$★

Raebin only occasionally saw such articles on the search engine's main page; he had neither the time nor the energy to read them in detail.

His understanding of the reactions was limited to fan comments under posts on TeSTAR's official account.

*Everyone is paying great attention to whether Moondae-hyung wakes up.*

That was basically correct.

It was then.

"Kim Raebin!"

"...! It's a hospital, be quiet!"

Eugene rushed out of the next hospital room and chased after Raebin at surprising speed, deftly handling his crutches.

His injury was more severe than Raebin's, so he was still hospitalized.

"Are you going to see Moondae-hyung?"

"...Yes."

"I want to go too."

Cha Eugene answered firmly and walked along with Kim Raebin.

It was hard to believe this was the same Eugene who'd burst into tears upon hearing about the operation, muttering prayers in Spanish too quickly for anyone to understand.

*It must be nice to be stupid, like Eugene.*

Raebin felt a brief pang of envy, then quietly continued on.

Park Moondae's hospital room was right next door.

"Hyung."

"We are here."

"R-right. H-hey, guys..."

Seon Ahyeon, who had been sitting quietly like a stone statue in the hospital room, quickly got up and gave a small answer.

"Moondae-hyung, is he comfortable?"

"...Y-yes. Th-they said he shouldn't feel any discomfort."

"All right!"

Cha Eugene bravely sat down next to the bed and began to diligently explain his current situation in a mixture of Korean and English to Moondae, who was unconscious.

"The fans are sending paper birds? Or something like that. [*It's interesting, you know. I guess cultures can overlap regardless of country. In any case, they're hoping you'll get better soon. That makes you feel better, doesn't it?*]"[1]{T/N: Any words in brackets by Eugene are said in English.}

Eugene believed Moondae might be able to hear them, and the others didn't stop him because his hopeful words were comforting.

"It seems that the other hyungs have already come and gone."

"...E-earlier, they went to the agency."

"Alright."

Kim Raebin nodded and waited for Cha Eugene's turn to end.

He had important news to share, and he couldn't let Eugene drown him out.

Watching Eugene talk on and on, he suppressed the urge to shout, *Don't waste our visiting time repeating yourself!*

*We have to be quiet in the hospital room!*

Raebin wanted to behave properly in front of the Moondae-hyung he admired.

So he waited his turn without arguing with Eugene.

"Hey, Moondae-hyung..."

Of course, there was no answer from Park Moondae, who was lying down.

For some reason, Kim Raebin felt like he was going to cry. It might have been because it reminded him of his grandmother's time, but he held back anyway.

"I-I have something prepared for you."

Trying to stay calm, he lifted the box and opened it.

The inside of the box was full.

Several sheets of paper with all sorts of lines and musical notes drawn on them, iconic props, and... a laptop.

Seon Ahyeon, who had been standing quietly, widened his eyes.

"This, this..."

"...These are the concept materials for our next album."

Kim Raebin answered courageously.

"We're working hard, so don't worry about work. Just wake up whenever you feel better!"

It was something he did right after he was discharged.

"......"

Even Cha Eugene was speechless and looked at Kim Raebin.

In fact, this was a very unreasonable act.

Even if Park Moondae woke up quickly, no one knew if rehabilitation and return would be possible right away.

Moondae had been unconscious longer than anyone expected.

The medical staff were even beginning to discuss whether his condition needed to be reassessed.

Nevertheless, Kim Raebin had been specifically 'preparing' for the next activity as if it were possible right away.

It was to report to Park Moondae, who couldn't even see it because he was asleep.

*I know already.*

Kim Raebin thought to himself.

He knew perfectly well that he might be spending his time on something that would never happen.

*But... I've experienced it.*

His grandma had woken up too, so he was sure that Moondae-hyung would wake up soon too.

"While preparing... I'll wait!"

Recalling his illogical conviction, Kim Raebin took the box and sat down on the chair next to the bed.

Then, a loud noise erupted from the side.

"That's right!"

"...!"

"When you wake up, let's do another concert! Lots of them!"

"Y-yes! Moondae, we'll be ready!"

Encouraged by Eugene, even the downcast Ahyeon clutched his crocheted yarn and called out.

Anyone else might have clicked their tongue or looked at the three of them with pity.

Still, they persevered.

"......"

Of course, there was still no answer from Park Moondae.

But this was reality, not a webtoon. Hearing his companions' voices wouldn't magically wake a person from a coma.

Still, the three of them chose to hold on.

Instead of planning for a future without Park Moondae, they refused to give up on him.

And a choice was now awaiting Park Moondae as he slept.

My body finally relaxed as I sat on the floor.

My limbs trembled as the tension drained away, and my head spun with unanswered questions.

But I did know one thing.

*I can go back.*

The moment I activated it, my real body should recover completely and I would wake up.

The possibility that it would only heal Ryu Gunwoo's dream body crossed my mind, but I pushed it away.

*The status window hadn't even worked properly in this dream until now.*

Besides, the new trait had fused with something I'd possessed in reality as Park Moondae.

*It has to apply to my real body.*

My brain was thrilled.

And at the same time... I was nervous.

*I have to do it quickly before the status window becomes inactive again.*

I didn't know what else would happen in this damn dream. I immediately tried to activate the ability 'Nectar (S)' in the pop-up.

And I murmured involuntarily.

"Let's go back."

"Already?"

...When I turned my head, the guy who went back, saying he had a schedule, pushed his face into the practice room.

It was Cheongryeo.

I instinctively set myself up for a crisis. There were many excuses.

"Practicing any more today wouldn't accomplish much. That's what I meant."

"You're lying."

He strode into the practice room.

*Press it right now...*

Wait, could this crazy guy attack me if he felt that something was out of place with me randomly pressing the air?

While I was quickly weighing the profit and loss, Cheongryeo stood across from me.

And... He opened his mouth with a sullen face.

"Hmm. You're not as patient as I thought you were."

"What?"

"You said it was your third time? I thought it would be a bit hasty to try to restart with just this at this time."

Oh, was that the story?

Well, this bastard had no way of knowing that this was a dream I had in a coma.

It seemed that he understood it in the way of 'going back to the beginning.'

I tried to clear up his misunderstanding.

"I'm not restarting. I was just thinking about something, and I guess I sounded serious. I meant I'm going back to the dorm."

"Is that so? Then, listen."

It didn't even work.

He continued talking nonchalantly, with a face that showed how he was convinced that I had made up my mind to restart.

"First of all, the two people you collected... It's a bit messy. They're not always cooperative. You saw it, right?"

"......"

"From now on, it would be better to wait a month or so before deciding on people to join. I think you were a little hasty this time. Were they pretty good before?"

"Yeah," I said with a smile.

"I chose them because I wanted to do this with them."

An odd look appeared on Cheongryeo's face.

"...!"

For a moment, he looked as though some distant memory had resurfaced.

It seemed that the distant past had popped out of nowhere.

And he muttered.

"I suppose restarting a couple of times might not matter, since you're only at the beginning..."

"......"

I didn't bother answering.

Instead, after a while, I deliberately threw his words lightly.

"Even if I do restart, let those two debut. I'll give you plenty of good information."

"Those two?"

"You said they weren't very good. They'd hardly threaten VTIC."

Cheongryeo shrugged.

It seemed that he didn't get it, but he agreed. In this case, if there were no problems, there was a considerable probability that he would do it if he was offered something.

"Uh, thank you, but I don't know what you're talking about. Why don't you calm down and then talk to me later? No, why is sunbaenim a crazy person..."

*Maybe he'd have opened up to me a little more, if we'd had time.*

Somehow, it was kind of fun.
