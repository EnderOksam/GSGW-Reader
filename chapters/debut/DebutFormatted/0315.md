# Chapter 315 - Conversation, III

When I woke up, several months had passed again, and suddenly I found myself headed to one of my own past performances.

And the cause of this situation... It was surprisingly simple.

I massaged the space between my eyes and summed it up.

"So... You looked up my activities in your spare time between studying, and at some point you got into it?"

[Yes!]

He answered without hesitation. *He sounded so cheerful.*

[Magical Boys... Oh, the performance in the baseball uniforms was great too! And that variety show you appeared on by yourself was really fun... I didn't get to watch it live because I was studying, but still...]

He seemed to have kept up with all our best-known public activities. I wondered how he'd managed to avoid failing any of the subjects and dying as a result.

*...No, maybe this was the only thing he'd been doing for fun.*

*Was he relieving his stress by watching idols?*

If so, I couldn't really criticize him.

[And then, when I marked my answers provisionally, I was sure I wouldn't fail any of the subjects... I thought it might be okay to take a day off and go see them, so I bought a ticket...]

"......"

The voice trailed off.

*Oh, come on.*

I held back a sigh.

"...Where is it?"

[...!]

Yes. I understood how he felt.

Being cooped up in a room studying all day could drive anyone crazy. In the long run, taking a day off wasn't a bad idea.

The resonance buzzed with excitement. 

[It's not far from here! It's a Gyeonggi-do festival...]

"Wait."

I changed my clothes.

There would be thousands of people there, so I doubted anything bad would happen. I just had to endure the ordeal of cheering for my own performance.

[Hyung, thank you so much!]

I must have heard him say “thank you” at least three times just while squeezing toothpaste onto my toothbrush.

"No need. It's not that difficult a request. You need to have some fun in your life."

[What?]

I chuckled.

"You're going to start studying at a boarding academy tomorrow."

[...What! Aww...]

I had no intention of denying the student his final taste of freedom.

***

An hour later.

I bought wireless earbuds at a convenience store so people wouldn't think I was some lunatic talking to myself. That would have to do as far as preparations went.

Of course, there was something I needed to do on the way.

"First, tell me everything that's happened."

[Yes!]

First, I checked his new status abnormality.

★!
@c@Status Abnormality:@c@
#><["Pass or Die!" (2)]><#

Pass the first-round written exam of the “Grade Seven Open Competitive Civil Servant Exam” or die.
!★

*Just as I thought.*

The deadline was one year, just as it had been before. *Hmm.*

*I'll have to tell him to wait longer before confirming he cleared the status abnormality next time.*

Otherwise, the deadline might expire just before he took the second or third round of the exam.

It wouldn't make much difference since the exam ran in yearly cycles, but we might be able to manipulate the time limits to our advantage.

The moment I thought of that, I remembered the way I'd confirmed clearing my own status abnormalities.

*Reveal the Truth.*

Perhaps he'd also gotten some trashy pop-up like 'Reveal the Truth ☜ Click!', so I'd have to check.

"Judging from the dates, the status abnormality didn't change as soon as you checked your scores."

[Uh... no! Actually, something strange came up...]

The resonance trembled faintly. And as soon as he explained, I knew why.

An option had appeared at the bottom of the success pop-up as soon as he'd checked his scores for the first round.

★=
{Accept Synchronization{ ☜ Click!
=★

He'd found the idea of clicking it rather creepy, so he'd hesitated for weeks before finally taking the plunge in September.

[I was going to wait a while and ask when hyung comes back...]

But I hadn't shown any signs of coming back, and in the end, he'd clicked that button yesterday.

And the result...

★-
\
#><[Accumulation complete.]><#
-★

He said this window popped up.

*...Talk about cryptic.*

[Hyung. Maybe I did something wrong and the punishment is accumulating...?]

"No, I doubt it."

If I had to guess, Park Moondae's existence was being established in the timeline in the process of my own reward being delivered, but I couldn't be sure.

*What kind of mechanism is it?*

There was more information to analyze, but I decided to leave it for now.

We'd already reached our destination.

I got off the bus and looked up at the stadium in front of me.

"Here."

[Wow!]

I remembered.

*I think it was some corporate festival.*

Unusually enough, tickets had gone on sale for this event, and somehow he'd managed to get one.

*As for the lineup... We were the best group performing at this event.*

I went inside, reviewing the details.

The air inside the venue felt stuffy. It was still hot near the end of September, as one might expect in this era of global warming.

*No wonder he'd slept in a sleeveless shirt.*

Sitting in an aisle seat off to the side, I felt a strange sense of déjà vu. *Was I remembering my days as a data collector?*

[Hyung. Do you remember this performance?]

"Yes."

[Then...! By any chance, when will TeSTAR perform?]

I deliberately pressed my Bluetooth earbud to avoid looking strange and answered.

"Third to last."

[Wow...]

I wondered what part of that conversation had impressed him. The practicality of picking a slot that let us go home early when everyone else wanted the closing stage?

*I should tell him a few stories about my life as an idol if I get the time.*

I almost felt like a content curator. I stopped talking. The performance was starting onstage.

"......"

The performances at the beginning weren't particularly exciting. I'd seen those groups often because our schedules overlapped.

But watching from the audience seats again gave me a sense of déjà vu, as if I'd gone back to my days as a data collector.

[Hyung, are you not having fun...?]

"No, I'm fine. It probably just looks that way because I'm used to it."

It was so loud that I could speak out loud without the people around me hearing. Park Moondae could understand me just fine, though, perhaps because it was his body too.

[Since you were active as an idol, of course you are!]

"By the way, I went to shoot something like this when I was in college."

[...!]

*Maybe I shouldn't have told him that.*

But instead of asking if I'd interned as a reporter or something, the voice enthusiastically agreed.

[Yes! Hyung also filmed that Youngrin fancam...!]

"...??"

*How did he know that?*

"Did I even tell you that?"

[That... no. Somehow I just heard about it!]

Well, it wasn't particularly a secret, so I might have mentioned it in passing while drinking or something.

I nodded.

Since the guy sharing my view was so excited, I kept my eyes on the stage and watched the rest of the performances.

And after a long time, the long-awaited guys finally came up on stage.

*Wow!!!*

The crowd screamed about three times louder than they had for the previous performer.

Seven figures came up and quickly got into formation.

TeSTAR.

$p
♪ *I met you tomorrow*\
*But I've been thinking of you all day* ♫
p$

Wearing a navy blue stage costume and holding a microphone... It was me.

$p
♪ *My dreams are as blue as the sky* ♫
p$

"......"

[Wow...!]

It was a strange feeling, watching like this.

I'd monitored my own performances countless times. I needed to know what I was doing to perfect it.

But seeing an actual performance from a real audience member's perspective... It was almost shocking.

Even if I was only the size of a lighter from where I sat.

*Was that how I looked when I performed?*

I could see our faces on the huge screen and the matchstick-sized figures running around onstage.

$p
♪ *Cast a spell* ♫
p$

Only then did it really sink in that I was up there, singing and dancing in the same kind of performance I'd once filmed as data.

The Park Moondae onstage... I looked like the perfect idol.

I sat quietly in my seat among the cheering people until TeSTAR's performance was over.

It was only when the rapper came on—someone ‘Park Moondae’ wasn’t interested in—that I got up to leave. 

[Hyung?]

"Bathroom."

I wanted to splash some cold water on my face. I couldn't believe how impressed I was by my own performance.

...I missed performing.

*Oh my god.*

I headed naturally down the hallway, and the moment I entered the bathroom, I realized something.

Just now... I'd walked right into the staff-only area as if I belonged there.

"......"

I guess they thought I was a staff member because I didn't look like one of the fans. *Security was pretty lax for an event like this.*

*Well, it's because I don't even have a camera.*

I shrugged and turned on the water at the sink.

Then I heard a sound.

*Bang!*

Someone kicked a stall door open so hard it sounded like it might shatter.

"...?"

*What's going on?*

Then I saw the person's face in the mirror in front of me.

But... I knew exactly who it was.

"...!"

[Uh, uh, uh! It's him!]

It was Bae Sejin.

At that moment, I grasped the situation.

*He'd locked himself in the stall and cried, hadn't he?*

It was still the early days after debut. Come to think of it, I'd encountered something similar during the daydream.

"......"

Back then, Bae Sejin had been trying to be kinder, but still had a foul temper because of his ongoing family issues.

I watched him freeze before he reached the sink. His eyes were wary.

*Hmm.*

I felt strange. A little... Should I say I was glad to see him?

I didn't want to come across as a weirdo, so I figured I should make sure I didn't.

I spoke briefly as I washed my hands.

"Lee Sejin, right? Sorry. I'm a fan, and I was just surprised to see you here."

"...!"

"I really enjoyed your performance earlier. You always have such a great smile on your face."

Bae Sejin, no, Lee Sejin, who had not yet changed his last name, had a blank face.

*Ah, he was getting a lot of criticism around this time, too.*

He'd been added to the group at the last minute, and his lack of stats had made him the target of criticism online. My words might just make him more suspicious.

But Bae Sejin didn't react that way.

"...Thank you."

Instead, he seemed a bit embarrassed, an awkward expression passing over his face.

"No, thank *you*. I'll be cheering you on."

"...Yes."

Only then did Bae Sejin come to the sink and start washing his hands.

*There. That went well.*

I was about to shake the water off my hands and head for the door when I heard a voice behind me.

"That...! I can give you an autograph, if you want one."

"Ah."

I'd noticed it before, but this guy was pretty good at fan service. I pretended to rummage through my pockets, then bowed.

"Sorry. I don't have a pen. I'd love to get one next time."

"...Yes."

I ended my short conversation with Bae Sejin from the past, and carefully left the staff area without being noticed by anyone.

I couldn't deny it had been a novel experience.

[Wow... Maybe he recognized you?]

"I don't think so."

We hadn't even really talked to each other at this point in the timeline.

*If it was September of the year I debuted...*

Come to think of it, he'd soon report his father for fraudulent gambling to the police and change his surname. Was that early October?

*Hmm.*

If I remembered correctly, his father's sentence had been made much heavier because he'd been found guilty of distributing drugs as well as gambling.

*And that's how I'd figured out the cause of the drug scandal.*

I stopped.

*...Wait a second.*

"Do you mind if I stop by somewhere for a moment?"

[What? No!]

I found the nearest public phone.

[...?]

And I called the public service center of the Gyeonggi Bukbu Provincial Police Agency.

[Hyung??]

Someone picked up.

"There are drugs in the basement of Building XX in XX City, where there's an illegal gambling den."

And hung up.

[Uh... Uhh...]

They might think it was a prank call now, but once Bae Sejin made his anonymous report, the police would remember it.

Two reports concerning the same place would come in around the same time, but with completely different vibes.

*That should at least get them to search for drugs once.*

I shrugged and walked out of the phone booth.

"It wasn't a prank call, so don't worry. The police won't come after us."

[Wh-what was it, then...?]

I slowly returned to the officetel, telling him about a few things that had happened early on after our debut.

And after dinner and washing up, it was already nearing the end of the day.

*Going out definitely eats up time.*

I first told him what he needed to know about the status abnormalities, then quickly pointed out the types of questions that would appear in next year's first-round exam. He'd gotten faster at writing things down, perhaps because he'd already been studying for months.

After that, there were only 30 minutes left for today.

Park Moondae sounded a little put out, but we spent the remaining time chatting.

[Hyung, maybe... Does hyung come back every time I get rid of this status abnormality?]

"Maybe."

There had only been one instance of that so far, but it was a possibility. The voice hummed gloomily.

[Then... Maybe I'll see you in a year or so next time.]

I chuckled.

"But think of it the other way around. We'll see each other again as soon as you pass the first round."

[That... You're right!]

"Yes. Work hard."

[...Yes!!]

He'd die if he didn't work hard, so there was no real need to tell him. But it was nice to have a guide appear once a year to help him along, like an annual subscription to a fairy godmother.

Before falling asleep, I gave him one last instruction on how to sell the stocks I'd bought, just in case he needed money.

It was midnight.

***

And morning came again...

I opened my eyes to the sound of the alarm.

Sunlight was pouring in through the window.

*Let's see if Park Moondae's hypothesis was right...*

[Hyung! It's you, right?]

The familiar voice seemed to confirm it.

"Yes."

I grinned at how excited he sounded.

"Did you do well on the test?"

[Yes! Yes!! I passed...! The first round!]

*Excellent.*

Even if he'd had to enroll in a boarding academy... *Wait, but this was still the officetel.*

I got out of bed and looked around. There was something on the table.

"Didn't you enroll in a boarding academy?"

[I did! Oh, it's the weekend, and I had something to do yesterday, so I came back to the officetel for a while... And I thought you'd be more comfortable here when you came back.]

"Alright."

I appreciated his forethought. I nodded and headed for the table. Some kind of poster was lying on it. It looked like an ad.

*Wait a minute.*

!-
\
$$[Best wishes ^^ - VTIC Cheongryeo]$$
-!

I stared at it silently. What was that bastard's autograph doing here?
