# Chapter 231 - Daydream, III

The ER was busy as usual. The reporters hadn't caught wind of the accident yet.

Lee Sejin looked at the floor and thought silently.

*We contacted the agency. A lot more people will be here soon.*

I'd yelled into the phone before I could calm down, so the agency was probably drawing all kinds of conclusions from the little I'd managed to say.

Even so, it was understandable that he hadn't been able to stay calm.

With Ryu Chungwoo unconscious, he had been the only one capable of contacting the agency.

"...Hueup."

Lee Sejin grabbed his smartphone and caught his breath.

His hands trembled.

Most of the members had suffered nothing worse than sprains or fractures.

They would have to wait to know the full extent of the injuries, but Lee Sejin himself had only suffered a few scrapes.

The person who caused all of this was thrown from the vehicle and passed out because he was not wearing a seatbelt. But even that was merciful compared to someone else.

*That bastard should be dead.*

But his anger at their former manager was drowned out by something even stronger.

Fear.

– Is everybody okay...!

When he checked the conditions of the members right after the accident...

What he'd seen in the back row, next to Ryu Chungwoo...

*...No. It'll be fine.*

Maybe the shock had made the injury look worse than it really was.

All of them were only lightly injured, but Ryu Chungwoo, who was closest to the place where the car hit the back, only cracked his leg, so only Park Moondae... It couldn't be true.

*Maybe it had only looked that bad because of all the blood. He'd received a transfusion and was in surgery. He'd be all right.*

Lee Sejin tried to calm his head, making guesses about a field he didn't even know well.

It was because he had almost signed the consent form for Park Moondae's surgery right before.

– The patient... critical condition... life support right away...

He tried to listen as much as he could, but he couldn't concentrate.

There was no sense of reality.

*We... Until just now, our 2nd anniversary performance had just ended.*

Accidents came without warning, but they'd been completely blindsided.

There had been nothing they could do.

Yes, this was nonsense.

*Everything was going well.*

Wasn't this too unfair? Park Moondae did nothing wrong...

Talented, sincere, and hard-working people were not so common in the industry, so why did it have to be Park Moondae?

The guy whose life wasn't easy.

@r@– We need a guardian's signature.@r@

– A guardian...

There was no guardian. Lee Sejin felt his blood run cold.

– C-couldn't I just sign instead? Please?

@r@– One moment...@r@

The medical staff exchanged quick looks, checked Moondae again, and immediately took him into surgery without asking for Lee Sejin's signature.

It was possible because it was an emergency, but anyway, Lee Sejin sat there stunned and waited for the results.

"......"

And he thought.

Now, the other members who were already in a state of panic would have received their treatments already, so they would have known the situation.

Lee Sejin could immediately imagine the scenes of crying and fuss.

*Soon, sightings will come out... When an article is published, rumors...*

No, what was the use of all that? First of all, all the members should be fine.

Lee Sejin gritted his teeth and clasped his hands.

*It'll be fine.*

Still, having the surgery meant that there'd be room for recovery.

So, if Park Moondae came to his senses soon...

– If you keep that up, they'll find you dead in your bed by your thirties!

*Fuck, I said that for no reason...*

Lee Sejin slammed a fist into his knee. He didn't know what to think.

*He'll wake up.*

He had to.

* * *

"...I am Ryu Gunwoo."

"Ah, yes. Nice to meet you. I hope we get along well! I am Lee Sejin."

I felt strangely uneasy as I watched the man greet me.

He was just as smooth as ever, but something was different.

*His eyes...*

His eyes were full of suspicion and wariness.

He smiled pleasantly, trying to seize his chance, but he couldn't hide how guarded he was.

*...Was it a fatal blow because he had to leave due to his rumors?*

He couldn't understand why LeTi had approached him after the school-bullying rumors ended his career, much less how they could refute the allegations with such detailed information.

"How old are you? I'm 21 years old~"

"I am also 21 years old."

"Oh, we are the same age."

Even though we were the same age, he still treated me like his senior and wouldn't speak informally.

The rumors had clearly undermined the confidence he'd built during ⟨Idol Inc.⟩.

I spoke with a sigh.

"Shall we speak informally?"

"Oh, shall we? Good~"

Lee Sejin made casual conversation to learn more about me, then went off to meet an agency staff member.

"......"

Was this the right thing to do?

I tried to bring in the people I could scrape together before ⟨Idol Inc.⟩, but I was strangely bitter.

Besides, Seon Ahyeon's side... The agency had not yet responded.

...After watching the broadcast of ⟨Idol Inc.⟩, I could understand why.

*Fuck.*

*My head was starting to hurt.*

*Maybe I should have forced my way onto ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ somehow.*

In fact, with LeTi, in such a ruined program, kicking out one participant and inserting one's own trainee wouldn't be a chore.

The problem was that the agency strongly refused.

– Idol Incorporated? Why there...

*...They were saying how there was nothing to gain.*

Cheongryeo probably had enough influence to get two or three trainees onto the program.

But LeTi refused to attach its name to an untested trainee on a program expected to flop.

At this time, everyone thought that ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ Season 3 would fail spectacularly.

*The persuasion didn't work.*

I thought I could encourage Cheongryeo a bit more with the bait of future knowledge, but it was impossible due to time constraints, as Idol Inc.'s first filming was right around the corner.

*It's like shit.*

The positive thing was that this agency treated me very well.

– Gunwoo, have you really never taken private lessons? Really?

I had been a trainee for the past two or three months already, and after about 15 days in the beginning, they figured out my skills and their attitude toward me became extremely friendly.

I'd been performing professionally as a main vocalist for three years.

*Looks like they're already making plans for me to join their new debut group.*

But the debut group they were considering me for would spend three years without getting anywhere. I wanted to warn them not to bother.

"...Hoo."

I sat looking at the mirror in the practice room. My familiar face I had seen for 29 years was reflected in the mirror.

...To be honest, things were progressing much more easily.

My abilities were already almost complete, the company was smart, and the treatment was good. Besides, I already knew what song and concept I should debut with.

If members with verified abilities were gathered, we would surely achieve success after our debut.

Since I couldn't check people's stats anymore, it made sense to recruit members whose abilities I'd already seen.

*...I think it's only a matter of time before Seon Ahyeon gets in touch.*

Once we'd recruited the others during the Casting Calls, we could debut. Failing that, we could add a current trainee.

So even though it was much easier than the reality where I'd been through everything with ⟨Idol Inc.⟩...

"......"

Oddly enough, I didn't like it.

"...This isn't real."

I finished my thoughts, saying that it was unavoidable because the purpose of this was to wake up.

"What do you mean this isn't real?"

"...!"

When I turned my head, I saw Cheongryeo opening the door at the back of the practice room and walking in.

*That bastard didn't even learn to knock.*

I gave a stern answer.

"I was just talking to myself."

"Well, it's about time for you to think about things like that."

"......"

"But it's real, so don't think like that. Because it's a waste of time."

It wasn't real. It was a dream, bastard.

I just nodded without saying anything. Cheongryeo smiled and continued.

"Oh, I heard you're coming along for the Casting Calls. They're filming in three days."

"You can't actually appear on TV. You'll only be accompanying us."

"That's enough."

I didn't want to be on the show.

Anyway, after my future knowledge was verified several times, the guy who became horribly kind was willing to accept the manipulation of ⟨Idol Inc.⟩.

"Hmm... You mean you want me to ask these questions?"

"Yes."

It was a reverse use of the method of 'receiving a transfer offer from the agency if you had the most similar answers to the given questions.'

Kim Raebin knew that he would be invited by LeTi even if he went anyway.

And Cha Eugene and Ryu Chungwoo... No matter what I did, I knew that these guys weren't the kind to be swayed.

So, I planned to catch the one left.

"He'll probably do exactly what we tell him."

Bae Sejin.

If done right, he was the easiest fish to catch at this point.

* * *

And a few days later, at the filming site of ⟨Idol Inc.⟩.

"Move this first!"

I checked the situation while running errands for LeTi among the staff wearing similar outfits.

*...The odds are high.*

The agency confirmed it had spoken with those two contestants before the Casting Calls.

*Kim Raebin and Bae Sejin.*

It was through the advice of Cheongryeo, who had been instructed by me.

With ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ already wildly popular, the agency agreed readily to make such offers.

Both were offered deals that would tempt them the most.

*For Kim Raebin, it was centered on work support.*

– Among national entertainment companies, we invest the most in production-related facilities.

This agency's AR work was very systematic. Besides, Kim Raebin was curious about the recording programs used by VTIC, so there was quite a possibility.

Given Raebin's principles, we hadn't told him the questions had been leaked.

We just emphasized that all of this was the 'official way'.

– It would be a great pleasure if we could meet through the official transfer method in the program.

We also said he could bring Cha Eugene with him if he wanted.

*Cha Eugene is a guy who pays attention when necessary.*

If he received this proposal, he would notice what was going on and would imagine the answer Kim Raebin would give. Although the odds were extremely low.

*And for Bae Sejin... safety.*

– Our legal team is fully staffed, and we are actively working with large law firms to protect our artists.

With the subtle nuance of being strong against agency disputes and more, we openly contacted him with answers to questions.

He was likely to be tempted by that legal support even without the leaked questions.

Throughout all this, Cheongryeo had been surprisingly diligent about speaking up for me within the agency.

*Good thing I offered him a composer in return.*

So now, it was time to see the results.

I continued to pretend to be a staff member and checked the set.

First... Kim Raebin.

[That... Um, sorry. Since I appeared already, I want to do my best in my work.]

He thought about it more than in real life, but in the end, he refused.

*I thought so.*

But the important point was after that.

[Here you go.]

[Ah, yes! Thank you.]

He got a business card.

If the show lost popularity or Raebin had to leave it, he could contact LeTi.

I didn't know how this world would turn out.

*This is enough.*

I knew the production team well enough to think of three or four ways to ruin the show's chances.

In addition, after the filming was over, even Ryu Chungwoo took advantage of the confusion and took the business card from a LeTi official who sneaked up on him.

The performance was better than expected.

*What should I do with Cha Eugene... I think I could just gather all of them and debut.*

Suddenly, I felt a thrill in my head.

I swallowed.

However, not everything went as expected.

[...No. It's okay.]

Bae Sejin... refused.

He didn't even take the business card.

*Is he crazy?*

Why was he refusing the lifeline thrown to him?

[Yes. Good luck.]

However, the results did not change.

Bae Sejin had been confirmed to remain in ⟨Idol Inc.⟩.

* * *

"......"

I walked away with my mouth shut.

*It feels like I've been slapped on the cheek.*

I was going to skip the ones that didn't work out anyway, but I didn't know why.

*Click.*

Thinking of splashing cold water on myself, I rushed into the bathroom for workers.

"...!!"

"...!"

And Bae Sejin's eyes met mine.

He looked as though he'd been crying while the tap ran.

*Why refuse the offer if he was upset enough to cry over it?*

I couldn't hold back my irritation and spoke impulsively.

"Why don't you accept it while you still have the chance?"

"What?"

Bae Sejin blankly replied.

"LeTi is a decent agency."

Bae Sejin's face turned blue, then red again, and finally went white.

*Did I stab him too directly?*

"What... You, LeTi..."

"Yes. I'm a staff member there."

Bae Sejin looked at me with a bewildered face, then suddenly became extremely defensive.

And he responded sharply.

"Are you seriously going this far just to poach talent?"

"...!"

"You contacted me beforehand... You stole the questions."

*Fuck.*

The situation got twisted around.

This guy was terrified.

We sent him questions and even brought up the story of the legal team in advance.

It must have felt similar to what Bae Sejin originally did at his agency.

"......"

*Bang.*

Bae Sejin left the bathroom as if he were running away.

And I had no choice but to admit my mistake.

* * *

In the car going back...

I crossed my arms, trying to clear my mind.

*Let's forget about it.*

Still, I felt dirty.

Cheongryeo, on the other side, checked that the member next to him was sleeping soundly, and then opened his mouth.

"Gunwoo?"

"Yes."

"I don't understand. I'm asking since he didn't join us anyway," Cheongryeo continued.

"The former actor was not even a good contestant, so why did you bother to take him? If it were me, I would have cut it at this opportunity."

"...What?"

"He wasn't cooperative, and he doesn't seem very useful."

"...!!"

I felt like I had been hit on the head.

Why did I want to bring him here?

*That...*

I did it unconsciously.

*If I debuted without him, Bae Sejin's career would end because of those drug rumors.*

*His life... fuck.*

I gritted my teeth.

Actually, I knew already.

This wasn't even real, so what did it matter if Bae Sejin had drug rumors or died?

I didn't know why I even bothered with all this shit, damn it.

"Don't worry about your past relationships. It's a loss."

"......"

"The number of people hasn't been finalized since everyone only received business cards anyway, so how about something like this instead?"

"What?"

Cheongryeo smiled.

"Um, joining VTIC?"

"...!"

What kind of crazy shit was this?

*Is this a trap?*

Joining a top-tier group like VTIC after its members had already renewed their contracts? It sounded like a different way of telling me to kill myself.

"No, listen closely."

And the guy, surprisingly, started talking about a fairly plausible route.
