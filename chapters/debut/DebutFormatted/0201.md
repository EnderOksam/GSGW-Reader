# Chapter 201

On the day of TeStar's encore concert in Seoul.

The graduate student entering Gocheok Sky Dome had a faint smile on her face.

*I can finally… be at the first concert too...!*

Thanks to the fact that the first Seoul encore concert started on Saturday, she could barely make time for it.

It was finally time for her to enjoy the concert without spoilers!

Although the graduate student was standing at the crossroads of becoming a doctor or getting a master's degree, she had only one concern today.

*Will I be able to see Moondae well? This is the best spot, right?*

That's right. The graduate student succeeded in securing a great seat in front of the extended stage.

It was the same area that had caused controversy at the last concert when Season 4 of ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ occupied the place.

Her photographer friend, who took pictures of Park Moondae, had congratulated her with tears in her eyes from envy.

★:
I just got in. I think my heart is about to jump out of my chest. ㅠㅠㅠ-
:★

The graduate student got to her seat and sat down, replying to her friend's message that she received a few minutes ago.

It really did feel like her heart was about to jump out!

Encore concerts usually had similar setlists as well as venues, so she had already made an estimate.

*I'll see at least four songs on the extended stage!*

In anticipation of seeing Park Moondae performing right in front of her nose, she held onto her light stick and waited for the concert to begin.

And after a while.

"Uaaaah!!"

"Aaagh!!"

The graduate student was also cheering among the crowd that was bursting with adrenaline.

*Ah, I love it so much!!*

Watching a concert from the center with a good view was virtually an artistic experience.

"Park Moondae!! Moondae-ya!!"

Park Moondae's solo performance, which had just ended, was an example of that.

As Park Moondae removed himself from the chair he had been using throughout the tour and floated in the air with his body alone, the intent of the performance could be felt even more clearly when viewed from the center front row.

A dizzying tangle of colorful fabrics dancing like a whirlwind of emotions.

In the center, under the lights, a transient love floats in the air, the falling fabrics.

The graduate student silently nodded her head and recited a phrase.

*Park Moondae... is a genius.*

And it wasn't just because of the good seat that she could feel all those emotions so clearly.

*Moondae is really good.*

It was even more spine-chilling than last time.

The acoustics were the same since it was held in the same venue, but there was something strangely raw about his voice that touched a person's emotions.

If the graduate student had accessed social media, she could have seen the sentimental comments from the people peeping at the concert through live streams.

The change of level in immersion made a difference in the quality of emotional performance on stage.

The effect of 'Getting Lost in Time (S)' was more pronounced than Park Moondae had expected.

And Park Moondae himself, who had just removed the stage equipment backstage, felt it too.

"...Huu."

His hands were trembling.

It was not because of pain or cramps, but because of the lingering sensations.

The feelings of emptiness that seemed to submerge his whole being, and the euphoria, were so overwhelming, it was as if he had been drowned and then lifted out of the water.

*Nevertheless... It was a better choice than I expected.*

It was because he too was satisfied with his own performance just now.

The stage immersion of jumping right in without taking any conscious steps elevated the artist's performance.

In particular, it was even more so since he carried out such a bleak, mournful song by himself.

*It's more powerful than usual.*

To regain his composure, Park Moondae drank an ionized drink to clear his mind.

"M-Moondae-ya! Do you feel uncomfortable anywhere...?"

"Yeah, I'm good."

"Is, is that so? I'm glad... B-but still, you have to say it if you feel uncomfortable!"

"I will."

"Uhhuh! ...Ah! The Flower of Eve just now, you were so cool...!"

"Yeah, thanks. You were also cool."

The time was almost up as he listened to Seon Ahyun, who was changing outfits, trying to disguise his worries as small talk during the interlude.

*Next is a group song, then the impromptu stage, followed by a long VCR...*

"Seven minutes to go!"

*Then the unit stage right before the encore.*

Park Moondae thought about how time flies faster than expected during the concert.

Which is why he had to keep himself together all the more.

*Let's do it right.*

Having his heart set, Park Moondae moved down the hallway to wait behind the stage again.

Then he walked out as the final chorus of Ryu Chungwoo's solo stage was ending and began to immerse himself in the stage again.

*Whaaaa!!*

In fact, it was hard not to get immersed in such an environment.

* * *

As the title song performance came to an end, which by now had been done numerous times, and the cute impromptu stage passed by, the audience got to thoroughly enjoy their offline experience after a long time.

And yet, everyone knew.

Based on the past shows reviewed through the VOD, the concert was now definitely in its second half.

Therefore, the fact that the 'unit stage', which was promoted at the end of TeStar's greatly successful reality show, would soon be performed, seemed to be true.

*There wasn't much change in the setlist!*

*As expected, they must have put a lot of effort into the unit stages instead...*

In particular, as the VCR similar to the last concert came out, even in the midst of all the excitement, the calculations began to rotate in people's heads as if this was a break time.

And without disappointing those anticipations, the unit stage came on just at the right time.

♪ *Falling- Like the falling autumn stars, fall, fall, fallen on you* ♫

The first ones were Seon Ahyun and Ryu Chungwoo.

The audience was intrigued to see the fresh combination of two people they hadn’t seen much before.

The two performed a refreshing rendition of the debut song of a famous male idol senior, T-Holic.

*Oh~ That's nice!*

That opinion came from a graduate student who was a fan of Park Moondae.

Next up was Bae Sejin, Lee Sejin, and Kim Raebin's stage.

♪ *I'm aiming for you, my target, I won't miss it, my enemy* ♫

They chose a female idol song with a strong concept called Hitman and distributed the parts appropriately.

*They're pretty cool?*

As expected, even if Park Moondae was excluded, the graduate student with the sensibility of any ordinary person appropriately gave a favorable review.

Both unit stages had a much bigger scale and were more grandiose than during the first concert.

Thanks to that fact, she naturally pulled herself up.

*Then now there's only Moondae left...!*

As much as the previous two stages were great, expectations were high on what kind of stage Park Moondae and Cha Eugene, who were in charge of the finale, would have prepared.

*Even everyone on social media has been looking forward to this!*

If you dug a little deeper under the surface, there were plenty of concerning comments that would make you bite on your nails, but the graduate student was not very Internet savvy yet.

Therefore, she only approached the last unit stage with 100% anticipation.

[...]

As the previous performance ended, a short video was played on the display board above the darkened stage.

*Clang-*

The bell of the clock rang, and the light suddenly returned.

A tall male figure with eight other dancers in an angled choreography formation was seen on the black-and-white stage.

It was Cha Eugene.

*And Moondae...?*

Before the graduate student's thoughts could even finish forming a sentence, the music started blasting.

It was the melody of a pop song she had heard somewhere at least once before.

%~*Babambambambam!*~%

♪ *LISTEN!*

*NO MATTER HOW HARD*

*I THINK ABOUT IT,*

*I CAN NOT HOLD IT* ♫

Leading the dancers over an upbeat and quick electric swing soundtrack, Cha Eugene began to move.

Out of everyone else, he was the only one dressed in black, wearing everything from a harness to jewelry chains, giving him the appearance of a solo singer.

♪ *WHY WHY~*

*ARE YOU NOT A MONSTER?* ♫

Cha Eugene looked splendid.

The use of twinkling lights, cheerful voices, and even glitter effects bursting in the background as part of the choreography was jaw-dropping.

Cha Eugene was enjoying his performance as if he was possessed.

♪ *UMM UMM UMM~* ♫

The screaming and cheers never stopped.

So, it wasn't until the moment he stepped out of the center and strode to the left that the graduate student came to her senses.

*No, so what about Moondae??*

However, Cha Eugene unyieldingly entered the chorus.

Thanks to the song's hooking melody, it was an iconic part that was quickly repeated four times.

♪ *MONSTER, MONSTER*

*YOU GOT ME SO*

*I GRABBED YOU*

*BEBE, BABE* ♫

Among the dancers, Cha Eugene single-handedly made the most complex choreography movements look light and airy.

It was as if he was absorbing your attention.

♪ *BEBE, BABE!* ♫

After a quick rise, Cha Eugene smiled as he drew his index finger across his mouth.

"Whoa..."

Even Park Moondae's fan reflexively expressed her admiration.

However, the moment the memorable first chorus ended.

The accompaniment suddenly changed.

*Whooong-!*

*Huh?*

The refined arrangement using extremely modern electronic instruments disappeared as if it had been swallowed.

And instead, an aggressive band orchestra stormed in its place.

"...?!"

"...??"

Before there were any gasps of bewilderment coming from the audience.

The background set opened with a radiant light flashing at the center of the stage.

"...!!"

Then, like a throne on a giant blue carpet structure, a golden chair began to emerge.

The man nonchalantly resting on top of that magnificent chair was... Park Moondae wearing glasses and dressed in a conspicuous knitted school uniform.

*What is this??*

Park Moondae began singing with a naturally pained expression.

♪ *RIGHT,*

*JUST THINKING ABOUT IT*

*I CAN'T STAND YOU*

*ANY LONGER* ♫

It was the lyrics of the pop song adapted into Korean.

Park Moondae led the song with a clear and distinct voice. Somehow, it sounded a little more exaggerated and emotional than usual...

*No, wait a minute.*

After almost falling in love, the graduate student noticed something recognizable about Park Moondae's arrangement.

This wasn't just an adaptation.

*...It's a musical!*

That's right.

This pop song, 'Monster Baby', was also one of the famous numbers in a musical that was created with the original singer's collection of songs.

♪ *YOU,*

*YOU DON'T EVEN LOOK HUMAN, YOU!* ♫

Park Moondae, dressed in the tidy school uniform, loosely leaned back in his chair as he continued to sing.

It was because he was embodying the role of a modern-day student who got stranded in a strange 17th-century castle.

And it didn't end there.

♪ *Wo, woo, wooo~* ♫

Surrounding Park Moondae, figures in gaudy modern clothes appeared and began to dance and sing to the chorus.

It was a musical ensemble no matter who watched this.

This allowed the audience, regardless of whether they knew about the context or not, to make a general guess.

He was recreating a musical scene.

*Heol.*

It was a performance of the same song in two opposite styles, but because of the greatly impactful energy, the audience forgot about their bewilderment and became engrossed in it.

♪ *MONSTER, MONSTER,*

*SNATCHED ME AWAY,*

*DRAGGED ME IN,*

*YES, JUST LIKE THAT!* ♫

While Park Moondae danced to the choreography on top of the giant golden chair, the structure itself was moved around in the hands of the ensemble cast to match the lyrics and the storytelling.

♪ *ALL THE WAY, JUST LIKE THAT!* ♫

Amid the intersecting colorful lights, feathers, and flashy pieces of elements from period dramas, Park Moondae's vocals split the whole venue.

This was the taste of an explosive and dramatic musical.

"Waaah!"

First, the audience reflexively applauded again; the quality of the performance was goosebumps-inducing.

However, it couldn't be called a unit stage.

*Isn't this practically just a... showdown of solo stages?*

*This isn't a unit, though.*

Some people could already predict what kind of comments would come up the moment the high from the concert subsides.

*Ziiing!*

The instrumentals shook, and figures dressed in stylish black-and-white outfits rushed into the center from the dimmed left side of the stage.

"Oh?"

It was Cha Eugene and the dancers.

And they pushed Park Moondae and the ensembles singing on top of the chair and the carpet structure all the way to the right!

"...??"

The musical set was quickly pushed aside as if toppling it over...

And then.

*Babambambam- bababam!*

In line with the synthesizer and the brass that came back in, this time, Cha Eugene and the dancers pulled out sticks.

The sticks were assembled and disassembled according to the dance movements, adding dynamics and extravagance to the choreography.

"Whoa."

The moment the stick flashed a luminous color, countless lights gathered in the air to assist Cha Eugene's movements.

It was a glow-in-the-dark drone performance.

Cha Eugene smiled smugly and leisurely handled the drones as if they were alive.

♪ *BEBE, BABE!* ♫

And as the chorus once again ended grandiosely.

This time, without time to spare, Park Moondae was instantly pushed into the center of the stage.

Standing on the golden chair with his arms crossed, Park Moondae had a huge chandelier hanging on the structure as if he couldn't be defeated.

And as if declaring war, a high-pitched harmony with the ensemble was added in.

♪ *I THOUGHT ABOUT IT TODAY, TOO*

*I CAN'T STAND IT ANY LONGER!* ♫

The lyrics were rather exquisite. The chandelier was blasting out light, torturing the dancers' eyes.

*This is crazy!*

Now the audience began to laugh.

But rather than laughing at this unexpectedness, there were feelings of relief and anticipation to enjoy the finished product of this performance.

It was because this somewhat meta-humorous, genre-transcending performance was shamelessly taking place onstage.

Cha Eugene and Park Moondae themselves were such natural performers that the stage felt very enjoyable rather than awkward.

*Drrrr-!*

The two, who had been quarreling like that, eventually rushed out to the extended stage and began to perform while having a silent fight with each other in the last chorus.

With the merging of synth and orchestra, the song had become loud and grand enough to be used for festivals and carnivals.

♪ *MONSTER, MONSTER*

*BEBE, BABE!*

*SNATCHED AND DRAGGED ME*

*YES, JUST LIKE THAT!* ♫

The original and the adapted lyrics were mixed. The dancers stormed in between the ensembles.

Cha Eugene climbed on top of the golden chair, changing his singing style, while Park Moondae began to dance with the drones.

A sense of frenzy was felt on the stage as they invaded each other's territory without hesitation.

♪ *MONSTER, JUST LIKE THAT!* ♫

*Wheeez!*

Fireworks of all colors exploded, bringing the vocal harmony and the choreography to a climax.

Making the mutual sense of destruction even more radical, the two disparate stage elements met their ending.

*Whoaaah!!*

Beyond the sounds of the fireworks and the electronic instrumentals, people's screams shook the scene as if they were part of the performance.

"Eyaaah!!"

Even the graduate student found herself clapping her hands while holding the light stick.

Little did she know that one hour later, as she was heading home, she would be shocked to realize that this stage was only 4 minutes and 20 seconds long.
