# Chapter 259

The night practice ended quickly as planned. After returning to the dorm, while washing up, I recalled the conversation I had with Lee Sejin.

After all, that was what he told me.

*Hurting Seon Ahyeon's pride...*

It was because he didn't want to show that he was being humiliated to his close peers.

"Hmm."

I wouldn't deny it. It was a point of view that had never been considered.

Maybe it was because he was the guy who had been following me around since I first met him on ⟨Idol Inc.⟩, so it didn't feel like we were the same age.

*Honestly, the only thing this body reflects is my physical age.*

Since I had a conscience, it was kind of hard to say that I was the same age as him... Right? I didn't know about being an office worker, but I had a clear memory of being 29 years old.

In the first place, it was ambiguous to say that we were of the same age.

*I don't know if Seon Ahyeon is the kind of person who cares about that.*

I didn't know about the other guys, but Seon Ahyeon was just a nice guy who seemed innocent, so I thought he'd just accept it.

However, I had to admit one thing.

*I can't solve things every time.*

No matter how smooth the activities were, I instructed him a lot whenever he had problems.

Was it time for Seon Ahyeon to try it himself?

"Because he's a guy with a backbone."

I shrugged.

Except for that brief moment at the awards ceremony, he didn't look particularly dark, so let's leave it at that.

I just needed to keep an eye on him to make sure he wasn't overdoing it.

*Click.*

I got out of the shower and headed to the refrigerator. I was trying to lose weight, so I thought about drinking some iced water or milk that night.

Seon Ahyeon was not in the room.

*He must be in the living room.*

And before I turned the corner in the hallway, I heard a voice.

"I-I mean... I-if you feel like someone's complimenting you because they don't like you... what would you do?"

"They don't like me, so they're complimenting me?"

"Y-yeah. It's similar."

It was a conversation between Cha Eugene and Seon Ahyeon.

The sound of Cha Eugene answering without hesitation followed.

"Why would they compliment me when they don't like me?"

"I-I don't know... Is it because you can't get angry?"

"OK, ОК."

The guy answered cheerfully.

"Then, I'd say 'I know~'! I know what I'm good at!"

"I-is that so...?"

"Yes. I like compliments!"

It was a mind-boggling way of claiming a mental victory that could help someone accept compliments no matter what the opponent was thinking.

However, I didn't think that Seon Ahyeon would be able to use it comfortably.

*Why did he even bother asking Cha Eugene for advice...*

It was a strange idea.

I thought about waiting for the conversation to end for a moment, then realized that it'd be more interesting to walk out.

When our eyes met, Seon Ahyeon jumped up.

"Moo-Moondae. Do you want water?"

"Yes."

"Here...!"

A glass of water was handed to me.

"...Thanks."

*Would it hurt his pride to get help from me?*

I still couldn't get rid of the thought that this kind of thing felt completely out of character... But I was thankful for the drink anyway.

I drank some water and waited for him to resume his conversation with Cha Eugene.

But the conversation did not follow. Seon Ahyeon was flustered and questioned me.

"Why, why...?"

Ah. Did I look at the two of them too much?

"Um, no. I was wondering if tomorrow's practice is at 7 o'clock."

"Ah, yes. 7 o'clock is right...!"

"Yes, thank you."

I ended my speech in a hesitant manner.

There was no particular abnormal behavior. He seemed a little cheered up, but...

"Good~"

At that time, Lee Sejin walked out of the living room and smiled, and patted me on the back.

...Somehow, it felt like that bastard was making fun of me.

*I don't know, fuck.*

Everyone could take care of things on their own.

There were so many things to do until the comeback. Let's get back to my own job.

"Seven o'clock is too early..."

"Go to sleep."

I went into the room, checked the stage composition once again, and immediately lay down on the bed.

Seon Ahyeon came in quite late and went to bed. His attempts to be quiet were pointless.

*He was almost crawling.*

I was going to tell him that I wasn't sleeping, but I just let it go because I thought it would freak him out.

And the next day...

After morning practice, around lunchtime, Seon Ahyeon went alone to the collaboration practice.

The schedule would run on a weekly basis, with shorter practices every two or three days.

"Will you be okay? I think it would be better for you to eat first and then go."

"I-I can eat once I come back...! I will be back soon."

"Well, if you say so."

"Wait a minute."

I threw a glucose candy to Seon Ahyeon. This was an emergency dose under the agreement with the trainer, so we could have some.

"Thank you..."

"Have a good trip."

"Ahyeon, fighting~"

"I hope you have a rewarding practice time!"

Seon Ahyeon hurriedly grabbed the candy and walked out the door, nodding his head.

That... it wasn't like he was dragging his feet on the way there.

"Hyung! Give me candy too!"

"Here."

"Hurray!"

I dusted off the remaining candy and crossed my arms.

*As expected, I'd like to find out...*

At that time, Lee Sejin suddenly started fanning uselessly with his hands.

"Ah~ It's hot since we're practicing. Shall we eat some ice cream? They were sold at the healthy food store down there!"

"I like it!!"

"Oh, that place. It's light, so that's fine."

"Okay~ Then I'll go around and buy it with Moondae!"

"...?"

Why did I have to go too?

First of all, I wanted to know what the plan was, so I followed him.

And Lee Sejin burst into laughter as soon as we walked out the door.

"Hey, Park Moondae! Does it bother you that much?"

"What are you talking about?"

"Don't you know that Ahyeon monitors our schedule times already? It would be more natural to talk to him!"

"......"

No, I was just trying to check his face before and after his collaboration practice, you punk.

However, Lee Sejin laughed.

"Ah~ What should I do with Moondaemoondae, really? You have to know everything in the world and fix it to relieve your anger, right?"

"Be quiet."

"You just need to use that wonderful ability only for the album, talented Moondae-ssi~"

"That's right, if you use it to pick out ice cream... Ouch!"

"Where?"

I sprinted to catch the fugitive, while the others received a mysterious delivery of ice cream bullets.

And I sat on the floor of the practice room and pondered briefly.

Was I always trying to take control?

*No, I'm trying to live.*

It was about my survival right now, so I was concerned about the possibility of problems in the surrounding group. Wasn't that some kind of biological instinct?

*And now it's not even that much.*

It wasn't like I was going to die with the status window being deleted, so the grand prize was just a high-risk target.

*I don't waste time on unnecessary things.*

I ate the ice cream and focused on my afternoon practice.

But this concentration was soon disrupted.

It was because Seon Ahyeon, who had returned, had a lot of worry on his face.

"I-I'm back."

"......"

That... I saw that it didn't go well.

* * *

Seon Ahyeon, who returned, quickly recovered his composure and joined the practice, but now the other guys had noticed the atmosphere.

"Did something happen while preparing for the collaboration?"

"The preparation seems to be more complicated than I thought."

However, there were no signs that Seon Ahyeon was overdoing it. It meant that it wasn't a big deal.

Thanks to this, Seon Ahyeon was quickly given ice cream, and this topic died down.

Usually, the right timing was to ask when he was resting.

And usually, it was up to the roommate or the leader... After all, I was his roommate.

*Do I have to ask on purpose?*

But there was nothing in particular for me to say.

After practicing, I was sitting in my room watching the video of my choreography practice that I had taken earlier.

There, Seon Ahyeon spoke first.

"Excuse me... Moo-Moondae. Can I ask just one question?"

"Okay. What is it?"

No way?

And the guess was right.

"By chance, Moondae... If the people you have to work with for a while are a bit difficult to deal with... Wh-what would you do?"

"......"

It was a direct request for advice.

"It depends on what situation you are in. What do you mean by being difficult to deal with? Is it a superior?"

"N-no, that's not it. U-uncomfortable... It feels like they're deliberately saying things they don't mean."

As expected.

"Well, then, it's okay to ignore it as long as it's not sarcastic or disrespectful. If you perform better, they'll eventually come crawling back."

"Th-that's right."

And at that moment, I realized something.

*Fuck, it's refreshing.*

I thought in the past that I needed to know everything and be able to control things to live comfortably. Damn it.

I hated having to admit Lee Sejin was right, but I continued to listen to what Seon Ahyeon said. To understand the situation in more detail.

But Seon Ahyeon... He seemed a bit embarrassed.

No, a lot.

"I-I'm sorry. I can't do well alone... I-it seems like I'm always getting help."

"...?"

"Everyone, the other members, are all good... I-I must be having a problem with something. Even after counseling and practicing, it doesn't work out..."

Why was the story like that?

"There's nothing wrong with you. What's the matter?"

"It's like this every time- every time I meet people my age... It was like that from the beginning..."

"......"

As if embarrassed, Seon Ahyeon forgot to ask for advice, like a third-party problem.

"When was the first time?"

"When I was in middle school... E-even when I was in ballet."

Seon Ahyeon hesitated, then spoke briefly. His voice trembled.

"My ankle... It hurt, but everyone loved it..."

Goosebumps ran down my spine.

"Th-they weren't mean kids, but from then on, I guess I had a bad feeling about going to a group of my age..."

"......"

I got up.

"First of all, I haven't seen a member who said he didn't like it when he was with you."

"Everyone- because everyone is nice..."

"You are the best person in that group. And in middle school as well."

"Huh? Yes."

"You are at the top."

"That, a-a few times..."

When Seon Ahyeon said that, it meant that he knew he performed well.

I crossed my arms.

Seon Ahyeon's parents, who seemed cultured, would not have his professional counselor talk to him in this way, so I'd just have to do it myself.

"Listen. Everything you've been through so far was because of bastards who couldn't stand seeing someone better than them."

"Huh?"

"They did it because they were pissed that a handsome guy was good at dancing too. Got it?"

"B-but... I-I understand."

It seemed that Seon Ahyeon nodded, being pushed by the force. Great.

"So, don't mind those idiots. You have to look in the mirror every day and shout 'I'm a really good guy' ten times and then turn a blind eye to the envy and jealousy. Got it?"

"That, that, I only did it at the beginning of the consultation..."

"Do it again."

It was clear on Seon Ahyeon's face that he was embarrassed in a different way now, but in the end, he nodded his head with difficulty.

And suddenly, I remembered some of his words.

"Wait, then what did you do before..."

Was bullying the reason why he switched from ballet to modern dance?

However, Seon Ahyeon hurriedly shook his head.

"N-no...! The... ballet. They said my body wasn't suited for it."

"Body shape?"

"Yes, ballet is- well... It is better if the top of the foot is round. I-I'm not like that, so... My pelvis is also small. So, they recommended that I go down a path where I can do better."

I understood it roughly.

Seon Ahyeon had a talent for dancing, but due to his body shape, modern dance was more advantageous than ballet.

So he quickly changed it before preparing for the college entrance exam in earnest... I guess that was it.

After all, this guy came to the idol industry, not the dance industry, but it was good to make money while doing well.

"And now that you're using what you learned in modern dance, so it's okay."

"R-right...?"

Seon Ahyeon smiled. But he soon lowered his head with a slightly darkened face.

"Yes, actually, today... The topic of modern dance came up."

"In the collaboration practice?"

"Yes."

Seon Ahyeon nodded. He spoke in a voice mixed with a little resentment.

"Uh, they said, 'The choreography is so difficult that none of us can pull it off except Ahyeon. I'm jealous. Congrats.'"

As befitting of people in a capitalist society, these guys had a knack for running their mouths.

"...So, why didn't you answer?"

"N-no. I answered..."

Seon Ahyeon blushed.

"Well, I don't know. Just... I said, 'Thank you for saying that, I'll do my best'..."

"......"

"Eugene said it was okay to just take compliments as compliments. I'm sorry, did I do okay? Everyone didn't answer..."

I barely answered.

"Yes. Good job."

Seon Ahyeon was surprisingly... He was able to be a good person without any malice.

Lee Sejin was right once again that I didn't have to worry.

...Still, the conversation we had earlier was worth trying at least once.

"Just do it. You're doing a good job."

"Yes."

Seon Ahyeon's face brightened.

And I readily admitted defeat.

*...Let's prepare the album well.*

And two weeks later, on December 31st...

The airing of MBS' music program, which marked the last day of the year, was just around the corner.

And the stage where the TeSTAR members first appeared was a collaboration stage with Seon Ahyeon participating.
