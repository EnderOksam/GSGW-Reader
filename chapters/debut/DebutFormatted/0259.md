# Year-End Festivals, III

The night practice ended quickly as planned. After returning to the dorm, while washing up, I recalled the conversation I had with Lee Sejin.

His point had been simple.

*Hurting Seon Ahyeon's pride...*

It was because he wouldn't want a close friend to notice that he was being bullied.

"Hmm."

I wouldn't deny it. It was a perspective I hadn't considered.

Maybe it was because he'd been pretty compliant, following me around ever since I first met him on ⟨Idol Incorporated⟩, so it didn't feel like we were the same age.

*Honestly, we're only the same age physically.*

Since I had a conscience, it was kind of hard to claim we were the same age... Right? I wasn't sure if I'd ever worked in an office, but I remembered all 29 years of my life.

And even if we were the same age, this problem wasn't quite so clear-cut.

*I don't know if Seon Ahyeon is the kind of person who cares about that.*

Unlike the other guys, Seon Ahyeon was so nice he sometimes came off as a little naive. I figured he'd be grateful for the concern.

However, I had to admit one thing.

*I can't solve all his problems for him every time.*

Even if it was for the good of the group, I'd told him what to do far too often whenever he had problems.

Was it time for Seon Ahyeon to try it himself?

"He's got a backbone, if nothing else."

I shrugged.

Except for that brief moment at the awards ceremony, he hadn't seemed particularly gloomy, so I'd leave him be for now.

I just needed to keep an eye on him to make sure he wasn't struggling.

*Click.*

After showering and drying off, I headed to the refrigerator. Milk probably wasn't the best choice at night when I was trying to lose weight, so I wanted some iced water.

Seon Ahyeon was not in our room.

*He must be in the living room.*

And before I turned the corner in the hallway, I heard a voice.

"I-I mean... I-if you feel like someone's complimenting you because they *don't* like you... what would you do?"

"They don't like me, so they're complimenting me?"

"Y-yeah. Something like that."

It was a conversation between Cha Eugene and Seon Ahyeon.

The sound of Cha Eugene answering without hesitation followed.

"Why would they compliment me when they don't like me?"

"I-I don't know... Maybe it's because they can't be openly mean?"

"OK, OK."

The guy answered cheerfully.

"Then, I'd say 'I know~'! I know I'm good!"

"I-is that so...?"

"Yes. I like compliments!"

It was a mind-boggling way of claiming a mental victory: taking a compliment as a compliment no matter what the other person actually meant.

However, I didn't think that Seon Ahyeon would be able to use it comfortably.

*Why did he even bother asking Cha Eugene for advice...*

It was a strange idea.

I thought about waiting for the conversation to end for a moment, then realized that waiting around would be silly, so I walked into the kitchen.

When our eyes met, Seon Ahyeon jumped up.

"Moo-Moondae. Do you want water?"

"Yes."

"Here...!"

A glass of water was handed to me.

"...Thanks."

*Would it hurt his pride to get help from me?*

I still couldn't shake the feeling that he was the easy-going type who'd take it well... *But thanks for the water anyway.*

I drank some water and waited for him to resume his conversation with Cha Eugene.

But the conversation did not follow. Seon Ahyeon was flustered and questioned me.

"Why, why...?"

*Ah. Was I staring too obviously?*

"Um, no. I was wondering if tomorrow's practice is at 7 o'clock."

"Ah, yes. 7 o'clock is right...!"

"Yes, thank you."

I answered wryly.

There was no particular abnormal behavior. He seemed a little more worked up than usual, but...

"Good~"

At that time, Lee Sejin walked out of the living room and smiled, and patted me on the back.

...Somehow, it felt like that bastard was making fun of me.

*Ah, fuck it. Whatever.*

*Everyone can take care of their own problems.*

There were so many things to do until the comeback. Let's get back to my own job.

"Seven o'clock is too early..." Eugene said.

"Go to sleep."

I went into the room, checked what people were saying about us online and watched how our performance had come out on the broadcast, then lay down on the bed.

Seon Ahyeon came in quite late and went to bed. He was going to a ridiculous amount of trouble to keep quiet.

*He was almost crawling.*

I was going to tell him that I wasn't sleeping, but I just let it go because I thought it would freak him out.

And the next day...

After morning practice, around lunchtime, Seon Ahyeon went alone to the collaboration practice.

They'd be meeting more frequently over the next couple of weeks, with short rehearsals every two or three days.

"Will you be okay? I think it would be better for you to eat first and then go."

"I-I can eat once I come back...! I will be back soon."

"Well, if you say so."

"Wait a minute."

I threw a glucose candy to Seon Ahyeon. I'd checked with our trainer, who said we could have these in emergencies, so it should be fine to share them.

"Th-thank you..."

"Have a good one."

"Ahyeon, fighting~" Lee Sejin said.

"I hope you have a great practice session!" Raebin said.

Seon Ahyeon hurriedly pocketed the candy, bowed, and walked out the door.

*I hope he doesn't come back crying or anything.*

"Hyung! Give me candy too!" Eugene said.

"Here."

"Hurray!"

I handed out the remaining candy and crossed my arms.

*Maybe I really should look into it myself...*

At that time, Lee Sejin suddenly started fanning uselessly with his hands.

"Ah~ It's hot since we're practicing. Shall we eat some ice cream? They have some at the healthy food store down there!"

"I like it!!"

"Oh, that place. Their ice cream isn't too high in calories, so that should be fine."

"Okay~ Then I'll go buy some with Moondae after we do one more run-through!"

"...?"

*Why me?*

First of all, I wanted to know what the plan was, so I followed him.

And Lee Sejin burst into laughter as soon as we walked out the door.

"Hey, Park Moondae! Does it bother you that much?"

"What are you talking about?"

"Do you know you've been watching Ahyeon all day? It'd look more natural if you just talked to him!"

"......"

*No, I was just checking his face so I could compare it before and after collaboration practice, you punk.*

However, Lee Sejin laughed.

"Ah~ What should I do with Moondaemoondae, really? You're not satisfied unless you have every variable under control, are you?"

"Be quiet."

"You just need to use that wonderful ability only for the album, talented Moondae-ssi~"

"Oh, and you can pick out the ice cream for me too, since you like meddling so much... Ouch!"

"Where do you think you're going?"

I sprinted to catch the fugitive, while the others wondered how we'd delivered the ice cream at bullet speed.

And I sat on the floor of the practice room and pondered briefly.

*Am I really that addicted to control?*

*No. This is about survival.*

I'd started keeping an eye on problems in the group to stop them from becoming problems for me, since they directly affected my chances of survival. Wasn't that some kind of biological instinct?

*And it's not even as bad as it used to be.*

It wasn't like I'd die if I lost the status window, so the grand prize was just a high-risk goal.

*I'm not going to waste my time on unnecessary things.*

I ate the ice cream and focused on my afternoon practice.

But this concentration was soon disrupted.

It was because Seon Ahyeon, who had returned, had a lot of worry on his face.

"I-I'm back."

"......"

*That... doesn't look like it went well.*

* * *

Seon Ahyeon, who returned, quickly recovered his composure and joined the practice, but now the other guys had noticed the atmosphere.

"Did something happen while preparing for the collaboration?"

"The preparation seems to be more complicated than I thought," Raebin suggested.

However, there were no signs that Seon Ahyeon was struggling. It meant that it wasn't a big deal.

Thanks to this, Seon Ahyeon was quickly given ice cream, and this topic died down.

Usually, it was enough to ask once in passing during a break.

And usually, it was up to the roommate or the leader... After all, I was his roommate.

*I guess I should ask him, just to be nice.*

But as it turned out, I didn't need to say anything at all.

After practicing, I was sitting in my room watching the video of my choreography practice that I had taken earlier.

There, Seon Ahyeon spoke first.

"Excuse me... Moo-Moondae. Can I ask just one question?"

"Okay. What is it?"

*Could it be?*

And the guess was right.

"M-Moondae... I-if the people you have to work with for a while are a bit difficult to deal with... Wh-what would you do?"

"......"

It was a direct request for advice.

"It depends on what situation you are in. What do you mean by being difficult to deal with? Are they your superiors?"

"N-no, that's not it. Th-they... They're deliberately making it obvious that they're saying things they don't mean."

*As expected.*

"Well, then, you can be sarcastic right back or ignore them altogether, as long as you remain polite. If you perform better, they'll eventually come crawling back."

"I-I see."

And at that moment, I realized something.

*Fuck, that felt good.*

It seemed I really was a control freak. *Damn it.*

I hated having to admit Lee Sejin was right, but I continued to listen to what Seon Ahyeon said. To understand the situation in more detail.

But Seon Ahyeon... He seemed a bit embarrassed.

*No, a lot.*

"I-I'm sorry. I can't do well alone... I-it seems like I'm always getting help."

"...?"

"Th-the other members are doing fine... Th-there must be something wrong with me. Even after counseling and practicing on my own, it doesn't work out..."

*Why is he taking it in that direction?*

"There's nothing wrong with you. Why are you saying that?"

"I-i-it's like this every time I work with people my own age... I've always been like this..."

"......"

Seon Ahyeon looked confused and forgot he'd been asking as if it were someone else's problem.

"When was the first time?"

"When I was in middle school... Wh-when I was doing ballet."

Seon Ahyeon hesitated, then spoke briefly. His voice trembled.

"I... injured my ankle. E-everyone seemed so happy..."

Goosebumps rose on my arms.

"Th-they weren't mean kids... I-I guess kids my age h-hated me even then..."

"......"

I got up.

"First of all, I've never seen any of the members say anything like that about you."

"Th-they're all nice guys..."

"You're the nicest guy among us. And you went to an arts middle school, right?"

"Huh? Y-yeah."

"You had top grades, didn't you?"

"That, a-a few times..."

When Seon Ahyeon said that, it meant he'd *always* had top grades.

I crossed my arms.

Seon Ahyeon's parents seemed rather cultured, and a professional counselor probably wouldn't say what I was about to. So I decided to go for it myself.

"Listen. Everything you've been through so far was because of bastards who couldn't stand seeing someone better than them."

"H-huh?"

"They did it because they were pissed that a handsome guy was good at dancing too. Got it?"

"B-but... I-I understand."

Seon Ahyeon seemed to be nodding simply because he didn't know what to say. *Great.*

"So, don't mind those idiots. You have to look in the mirror every day and shout 'I'm a badass' ten times and then turn a blind eye to the envy and jealousy. Got it?"

"I-I only did that at the beginning of counseling..."

"Do it again."

It was clear on Seon Ahyeon's face that he was embarrassed in a different way now, but in the end, he nodded his head with difficulty.

Then something he'd said caught my attention.

"Wait, then the reason you changed majors..."

*Was bullying the reason why he switched from ballet to modern dance?*

However, Seon Ahyeon hurriedly shook his head.

"N-no...! They said m-my physique wasn't suited to ballet."

"Body shape?"

"Yes. I-it's better if the tops of your feet are round for ballet. M-my feet aren't like that, so... My hips are also small. So, they recommended that I go down a path where I can do better."

I understood it roughly.

Seon Ahyeon had a talent for dancing, but due to his body shape, modern dance offered him a better chance of becoming the best in his field than ballet did.

So he quickly changed it before preparing for the college entrance exam in earnest... I guess that was it.

After all, this guy came to the idol industry, not the dance industry, but he was popular and making good money. It had turned out well.

"And now you're making good use of what you learned in modern dance, so that's perfect."

"R-right...?"

Seon Ahyeon smiled. But he soon lowered his head with a slightly darkened face.

"A-actually, today... The topic of modern dance came up."

"In the collaboration practice?"

"Yes."

Seon Ahyeon nodded. He spoke in a voice mixed with a little resentment.

"Th-they said, 'The choreography's so difficult that none of us can pull it off except Ahyeon. He's going to be the only one on camera. I'm jealous. Congrats.'"

As befitting veterans of capitalist society, these guys knew how to make insults sound harmless.

"...So, you didn't know what to say?"

"N-no. I answered..."

Seon Ahyeon blushed.

"I-I'm not sure if I said the right thing. Just... I said, 'Thank you for saying that, I'll do my best'..."

"......"

"E-Eugene said it was okay to just take compliments as compliments. I'm sorry, d-do you think I did okay? Nobody answered..."

I struggled not to show my amusement as I answered.

"Yes. Good job."

Seon Ahyeon was surprisingly... the type who could innocently return an insult without even realizing it.

Lee Sejin was right once again that I didn't have to worry.

*...Still, the conversation we'd had earlier had been worth having.*

"Keep doing what you did. You're doing a good job."

"Y-yeah!"

Seon Ahyeon's face brightened.

And I had no choice but to admit defeat.

*...I should just focus on the album.*

And two weeks later, on December 31st...

The airing of MBS' music program, which marked the last day of the year, was just around the corner.

And the first performance featuring a TeSTAR member was Seon Ahyeon's collaboration stage.
