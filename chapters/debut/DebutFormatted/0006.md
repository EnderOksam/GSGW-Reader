# Chapter 6 - ⟨Shining Star⟩, I

I'm tired.

My eyes sting.

Those shameless producers kept everyone on set until all seventy-seven contestants had finished performing.

Even during breaks, they only allowed contestants to go to the restroom in groups.

It was probably difficult to manage so many energetic teenagers, but my patience was running out.

Just by looking at their status windows, I could tell what kind of competition I was up against, and every performance I’d seen so far had been pretty bland.

I'd filmed countless performances by professional idols while working as a fancam photographer, so the trainees' performances weren't particularly impressive.

Of course, there were two or three times when I was impressed.

I glanced up at the two people sitting on the plush-looking sofa.

They were currently ranked first and second. Both were from the same famous agency, and their skills were undeniable. Their status windows were impressive, too.

I knew that both of them had successfully debuted in my previous life, with one of them even taking first place.

Getting close to those guys might improve my chances of debuting, but the thought of forcing myself to get along with teenagers ten years younger than me made my skin crawl.

Let's think about it again when the team battle comes out.

Fortunately, the evaluation is coming to an end.

"Participant Ryu Chungwoo...9th place!"

The final contestant had squeezed into the already crowded top ranks. That pushed me down to twenty-second place.

Not bad. Not bad at all.

In fact, I'd ranked higher than at least seven contestants whose stats were better than mine.

I'd have to keep performing well so no one realized I'd been overestimated.

"Thank you!"

The former representative of the national archery team, who left a soft impression, smiled as he walked off the stage.

I'd heard he'd won an Olympic gold medal in the team event, but that was about all I knew of him. I recall him only as a part of the group's debut lineup.

In any case, his exemption from military service would make him an appealing prospect for an agency.

Wait.

Come to think of it... Will I have to do my military service all over again?

In my previous body, I'd received Grade IV on my physical exam and had been assigned to non-active duty at City Hall.

Filming finally ended just as my mind wandered to a scary topic that I had never thought of before.

"Cut! Thank you for your hard work! Participants, let's move right away."

"Yes~"

Applause and responses rang out from throughout the place.

My head was spinning as I followed the staff's guidance.

I'd have to check whether Park Moondae had already completed his military physical exam.

"Get your rank ticket~ You can take it on board!"

The accommodation was a 10-minute walk from the set. After the huge success of the first season, it was a newly built dormitory on a mountain behind the broadcasting station.

The production staff insisted on driving us there anyway, and the reason was obvious.

They wanted more footage for the broadcast.

That means they had varied modes of transportation based on their rank.

"Participants! If you were in the Top 10, please come this way~."

"Wow, limousine!"

"Wow~ I really like it!"

"This is amazing."

Behind the top-ranked participants in the black limousine, who gave the camera a thumbs-up, the lower-ranked participants could be seen.

Contestants ranked fifty-first through seventy-seventh had to walk.

They did their best to act happy and humorous, but I could see that they were holding back tears all over their faces.

There were always cameras following the participants.

The cameramen seemed especially good at finding contestants whose misery would make entertaining footage.

To be honest, it's a bit gross.

"Oh, it's good?"

From the 11th to the 25th, premium bus vehicles were waiting.

Lee Sejin smiled broadly as he got into the bus first.

I waited for a while to ensure we wouldn't end up sitting next to each other by accident, then entered the bus slowly.

Someone quickly followed and got on the bus from behind, making a thumping sound.

Then he hesitated and sat quietly next to me.

It was Seon Ahyeon.

"....?"

Why are you...?

Well, never mind. It was nice to be quiet.

***

We arrived at the dormitories in no time. We also used different floors for each rank, just like last season.

The caste system will stay the same until the team battle mission comes out.

A spacious five-person room was arranged from the 11th to the 25th ranked. We were assigned rooms in ranking order, so I ended up in a different room from Lee Sejin, who was ranked twentieth.

I was lucky.

"Wow~ Me! I'm going to use this bed!"

As soon as I entered the bedroom consisting of two bunk beds and one single bed, a man randomly jumped into the single bed.

I guess he wanted to look fun and cheerful for the camera.

I chose one of the bottom bunks.

Then, once again, Seon Ahyeon placed his luggage on the bed above me.

"...."

When I looked at him, he lowered his gaze and avoided my eyes. *It's like I've suddenly acquired a second shadow...*

He continued following me even after we changed into the training clothes we'd been given and went to assemble.

It occurred to me that he probably found those bossy aspiring celebrities intimidating and felt more comfortable around someone relatively ordinary and quiet like me.

I left him alone because I didn't think he would be a problem.

Still, I didn't want to get too close or end up on the same team. If someone took issue with Ahyeon, his timid personality could end up causing problems for me too.

"You're going to start training in earnest from now! It's called~ Customized training classes!"

The MC introduced a system that everyone already knew with an exaggerated tone.

"Everyone, your scores for vocal and dance have been decided based on the grades you got from the judges!"

"Yes!"

"There are upper, middle, and lower groups in each class; each level aims to improve their skills through specialized training. "

Until now, it was similar to other auditions. The next thing was the problem.

"And you can be moved to another class at any time, without notice!"

"....!"

"It all depends on your hard work and dedication!"

For example, if you absorb the tutor's lessons in "Intermediate Vocal" like a sponge and become good at it quickly, you will be moved up to "Advanced Vocal" the next time you train.

Of course, it also meant that if you didn't do well, your class would drop right away.

After ten days of this, the final grade is determined based on an individual evaluation of the theme song.

In other words, the producers planned to work these kids to the bone to get as much footage as possible.

"This badge... Participant Park Moondae!"

"Yes."

As expected, I received 'Advanced Vocal' and 'Beginner Dance'.

It was a little embarrassing to have a gold vocal badge and a dull bronze dance badge on my shoulder.

The badges were so exaggerated that they looked cheap.

For reference, there were cases when they didn't provide badges at all.

Online, they were referred to as the 'trash grade.'

"If you don't have a badge... It means you're not a part of any grade. You will take a beginner's class together, but you also have to take extra special training at night."

To be honest, for a determined contestant, being in that position meant more screen time and a chance to create a dramatic comeback storyline.

However, if you are the person involved, you will think differently.

A former child actor with trembling hands over there was a good example.

It was said that he was in a thriller movie that 10 million people watched 10 years ago, but during the auditions, he'd explained that unfortunate circumstances had put his acting career on hold.

He hadn't received a dance grade, and his vocals were only at the beginner level. His status window showed an F in Dance and a D in Vocal.

He was probably hoping to revive his acting career rather than seriously pursue becoming an idol.

What was a little surprising was that the name over there was also 'Lee Sejin'.

Though I didn't know that there were two people with the same name, it was obvious that the other Lee Sejin was the one who eventually debuted.

It was obvious he'd be used to stir up drama in the early episodes, then discarded.

More importantly, he couldn't hide how upset he was about his situation.

*The PD will like it.*

Eventually, the huge screen lit up when all the badges were distributed.

"Now, the song that you will be practicing will be revealed. For the next ten days! You will do your best to master this song and present it to the shareholders of ⟨Idol Joint-Stock Company⟩!"

It was a song that would be played everywhere until everyone was sick of hearing it.

Even though I was studying for a civil service exam and had already stopped using social media, I still remembered the chorus's moves.

It wasn't as easy as ⟨POP☆CON⟩, but it was still simple enough to pick up quickly.

"The song for this season is... ⟨Shining Star⟩!"

An addictive beat began to play, accompanied by emotional but undeniably cringeworthy lyrics.

A dancer appeared on stage and demonstrated the choreography.

There were a few exclamations and a mysterious groan here and there during the performance.

And I realized it, too.

The chorus's relatively simple choreography was a trap.

It had been made easy to follow for the general public, while the intro was packed with complicated movements.

By the time the first verse ended, I'd already forgotten everything I'd just seen. And then the chorus started.

Even the seemingly easy chorus had complicated footwork that looked like it would trip me up.

I made up my mind right away.

"Status window."

Let's just... Let's not sleep.

"...Activate 'Sleep is for the Dead'."

***

Calories. I had to calculate the calories well.

I should've realized sooner that practicing all night without sleep would burn through far more calories than usual.

I was so hungry on the first night that I almost got angry.

"T, t, this... Would you like some?"

And Seon Ahyeon gave me a chocolate bar. Out of gratitude, I ended up chatting with him for a while. Since he was the same age as Park Moondae, we decided to drop formalities.

Speaking casually with someone so young was a sharp reminder of just how young I was now.

From the next day on, I tried to eat and drink three times a day as much as I could. The food tasted like jjambap[1]{T/N: 짬밥 (jjambap) refers to military meals.}, but at least there was no limit on how much we could eat.

On the ninth day.

I memorized all the choreography. From beginning to end.

Being able to spend all night without fatigue certainly worked.

In addition, I was able to level up multiple times.

Fortunately, I received a reward roughly every hundred attempts, up to five hundred attempts. But I hadn't allocated my points yet. Spending them now wouldn't have much impact.

*Until today... 5 people.*

Five people have already been moved from a beginner level to an intermediate level.

Very few people have dramatically improved their skills after only a few days of training, so most of them had probably messed up their initial evaluations and were only now showing their true skill levels.

If I moved up alongside them, I'd just be lumped together with the others and overlooked.

Then it was pointless to be promoted. I already had plenty of footage they could use. Now I needed to show a dramatic improvement to make the most of it.

After all, the choreographer had been on my case since the very first day.

– Again!

– Foot, bend your left foot!

– Why are you swinging your arms? Hold it up!

The choreographer had been yelling at the contestants from the very first day, and the mood had only grown more depressing as the class went on.

As class drew to a close, the choreographer singled me out.

– Moondae-ya.

– Yes.

– If you keep dancing like this, you'll fail the next evaluation.

– ...

I wasn't exceptionally bad at this class.

It was the beginner class, after all, and I was probably somewhere around average. I wasn't a particularly bad dancer, just an ordinary person trying his best.

But I'd received one of the highest initial rankings among the beginners, so expectations for me were higher.

It's not surprising that these are the same lines as last season.

– You know what I'm thinking? Maybe I gave you the wrong ranking. Maybe you never deserved to rank that high in the first place.

He was right, of course. He probably knew it too, and was only giving me a hard time for the cameras.

– Let's do it right, okay? Get a hold of yourself.

– Yes.

Unless someone was exceptionally talented, they couldn't be expected to improve dramatically after just a few days of training.

But I can, so I just nod my head as if I had made up my mind.

The criticism and advice meant nothing to me personally.

But it needed to seem to hurt my feelings in other people's eyes.

I had to make the most of this footage, even if it meant putting up with the criticism for now.

"Thank you for your hard work!"

"Yeah. Don't give up."

The choreographer nodded at the greetings of the participants, who were drenched in sweat.

After checking that the cameras had been turned off, he looked at his phone and left the room.

This is the end of the morning dance class.

After eating, it's time for vocal class.

For lunch, there was Bulgogi and Seaweed Soup.

I ate as much as I could, then took a quick shower before heading to the advanced vocal class alone.

Oh, come to think of it, I have a confession to make.

"Then why don't you show us how it's done, Moondae~"

When it came to vocals, I was basically coasting.
