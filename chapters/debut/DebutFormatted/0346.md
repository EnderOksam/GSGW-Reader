# Chapter 346 - A Message from Keundal

"...It seems like it's a massive success?"

"You're right."

We watched silently as the comments and social media posts poured in about the music video that had just been released.

No... The reception we were getting was much better than expected.

In fact, the day of a comeback usually brought all sorts of soul-crushing criticism and even curses, so we'd been planning to avoid monitoring as much as possible...

*But it looks like there's no need for that.*

★$
– GodStar, God Concept, God Music Video, Just God. This music video is the stuff of heaven

– TeSTAR!! TeSTAR!! TeSTAR!! TeSTAR!! TeSTAR!! TeSTAR!! TeSTAR!!

– Ahhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhh TeSTAR is serious about Korea~~~~ Oh, did you see those *haetae* heroes? Amazing stuff!
$★

It had reached the point where Korean comments, which should have been hard to spot amid all the English ones, were sprouting like bamboo shoots throughout the music video comment section.

These weren't the usual cheering comments from fans, but comments from netizens in the general public, as though our music video were some high-profile sporting event.

"Uh... We really made something great, huh?"

"You're right."

To put it bluntly, Ryu Chungwoo had become a reaction robot, unable to say anything but 'You're right' for a while now.

"This hasn't happened before, has it...? Everyone seems to like it!"

"You're right."

That was me who said it. I'd spoken up to spare Ryu Chungwoo from repeating himself again. Bae Sejin, meanwhile, was almost sticking his nose into his smartphone, refusing to look up.

I also swiped my smartphone screen.

I checked tentatively, and even the sites that were usually the most vicious and unkind were giving us surprisingly good reviews.

★$
– Holy crap, this is good. I lost sleep worrying that the *daesangs* would go to their heads and mess up their music. What a waste of sleep

– Shamstar still have the bodies of teenagers. Look at how they perform. You can't do that with old joints
└ ㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋ

– They really know how to make use of the attention from the movie ㅋㅋ This is why I can't let you guys go. Keep up that insane work ethic, TeSTAR!
$★

I stared. "......"

Well... This particular community was practically made up of antis, so I hadn't expected such blatant praise...

Anyway, they preferred performances where you could tell how much physical effort had gone into them, the kind you grind your bones away for. But since we'd won a *daesang* with our last title track, which had targeted popular tastes, they'd been ready to criticize us as soon as our next album dropped.

*Why are they wasting their energy on that, anyway?*

However, the new release had a performance as intense as 'Drill', and they could even feel the nostalgia of 'Present', their favorite of our releases, so I guess they started applauding instead.

In other words, there was no reason for unnecessary turmoil within the fandom.

"Hmm," I said.

"No way, Moondaemoondae~ You said you wouldn't look at that site specifically! But here you are again, peeking at weird websites..." Keun Sejin said.

"Yeah. I'm closing it now."

He'd searched for such malicious fan communities early in his career himself, and had nearly lost all his morale for it.

*Listen to you.* Anyway, we weren't on ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ anymore, so I closed the page for the sake of my sanity.

I was still a bit dazed.

*Honestly, I thought at least some people really wouldn't like it.*

Even with excellent CG and superb editing, this was an idol music video that included science fiction, modern ballrooms, *haetae*, and even the plague.

We dared to do it even though we'd thought most of the general public might have trouble wrapping their heads around it.

However, as the saying went, quality ranked above preference, and reputation above quality.

★$
– **[TeSTAR's *haetae* take down America's plague! The globe is taken by storm!]**

– **[Nothing like TeSTAR to come through with that good old Korean flavor! The shocking identity of the new TeSTAR release “Savior!”]**
$★

Looking at the excited video titles from the WeTube channels that had been eagerly covering us since our cameo appearance...

I understood why.

*It's gukppong. The concept and quality of the music video have made people proud to be Korean.*

As expected, the Gukppong Meta was highly effective as long as it wasn't taken too far. It was definitely worth the money we spent.

*Good thing I suggested it, even if it was toward the end.*

– *If we include too many American elements, Korean fans might be upset and think we're catering too much to American audiences.*

– *'Spring Out' received a good response even overseas, so let's sprinkle in a little of our steampunk Joseon worldview.*

Of course, the more often we reused this idea, the harder it became to keep it from getting stale the next time. It was the sophisticated references and structure that made it possible.

*Seon Ahyeon had pulled at least triple his own weight.*

No wonder that guy had been monitoring reactions to the music video far more than usual. He was proud of what we'd accomplished, too. This must be it.

*This probably isn't a good time to say anything to him.*

It was like he was about to be sucked into the music video.

Of course, every member was excited right now.

"The music video views are skyrocketing to unbelievable numbers. The cameo in the movie was a smart choice!" Kim Raebin said.

"You're right... Hm, and Sejin and Ahyeon, great job thinking of Edgar Allan Poe. Moondae's *haetae* concept was an amazing addition as well," Ryu Chungwoo said.

"Yeah!" Cha Eugene agreed enthusiastically.

Ryu Chungwoo seemed to have come to his senses somewhat, and Bae Sejin calmly opened his mouth.

"...All I did was make a suggestion! It was Seon Ahyeon who did the rest."

"......"

Seon Ahyeon still hadn't looked up, lost in the comments on our music video. *That says it all.*

Unfortunately, Bae Sejin turned red and tried again.

"Uh... Seon Ahyeon did the—"

"Hyung, forget about trying to get a reaction from other people. Let's just pretend nothing happened and move on," Keun Sejin said.

"Hey!" Bae Sejin said.

"What about me? My acting was amazing!" Cha Eugene said.

"Ah~ Eugene, you sure showed off your charisma!" Keun Sejin said.

"Ahem. Yes, that's for sure... It was good. You're good at using your facial muscles to express depth of feeling," Bae Sejin said.

"Hehe," Cha Eugene replied.

It was like a drinking party at this point, the way they were tossing praise around. It wouldn't have been strange if someone had brought out some alcohol and proposed a toast.

Next came compliments for Kim Raebin, who'd made a new arrangement in just three days, and the main dancers who'd come up with the rough draft of the choreography. It was going to be my turn soon.

*Hmm.*

*Should I bring these guys back down to earth?*

"And Moondaemoondae~ It was Moondae's idea to have this camp in the first place. If anyone deserves credit for this album, it's him~" Keun Sejin said.

"Yes. In fact, it was Moondae, not me, who served as the leader at the camp. Good work," Ryu Chungwoo said.

"Hyung was really cool! I'll acknowledge it!" Cha Eugene said.

"Th-that's right...!" Seon Ahyeon said.

*So you finally snapped out of it, huh?*

Anyway, I grinned.

"Thank you. However, public opinion can change quickly, so people might start saying strange things soon. But don't worry, that'll all fade with time."

Then they grinned at me. *Uh, guys?*

"Moondae, you're feeling shy, aren't you?" Keun Sejin asked.

"No," I replied.

"Okay, okay, let's say you aren't, then~"

I felt the urge to sock this guy in the head.

However, he tactfully changed the subject. *Quick-witted bastard.*

"Public opinion is definitely like that. I don't know if it's because of all the work we put in, but I want to perform this on stage soon!" he said.

"I heard we're doing a pre-recording the day after tomorrow. I'm so excited!" Kim Raebin said.

These guys were full of confidence after all the suffering they'd gone through preparing for the stage. Well, we really had put ourselves through a forced march.

– *Hey! The formation's off right there~ Again! Try not to be so out of breath!*

– *Could we take a 5-minute—*

– *That just means we'll end 10 minutes later~*

"......"

We'd squeezed out every last drop of stamina... Well, if I thought about it any more, it would probably ruin my current mood. I quickly shook off those thoughts and returned to thinking about our upcoming schedule.

*We'll do one music show in Korea, then head to America.*

It would be a gamble to make it look like we were deliberately going all-in on America. Who knew what might happen in the future?

So, the original plan was to do a few things in Korea first, then appear on a few American talk shows, but...

That evening, our plans changed abruptly.

"They want us on their show?"

"The company says so," Ryu Chungwoo said.

Suddenly, we received an offer from a prime-time variety show on a major American channel.

"If we accept..." I said.

"The performance will be live... and it'll be next Tuesday."

"......"

And everyone figured out the situation.

"We're filling in for someone else."

"Y-yeah," Seon Ahyeon said.

If they'd wanted us on the show from the start, there's no way they'd have called us in such a hurry. Well, it looked like somebody who was originally scheduled had dropped out at the last minute.

Keun Sejin shrugged.

"Hm~ Well, an appearance is an appearance."

Yes. Even though it wouldn't be a talk segment, and all we'd be doing was perform.

There was no doubt that it was a major broadcast show with better ratings than most of the talk shows we were invited to.

"America is huge. I'm sure there were plenty of artists they could've asked to appear. The fact that they contacted us right away means we were next on their list," Ryu Chungwoo said.

"......"

Even if the attention from our cameo in the movie was probably short-lived, the appearance itself had definitely been worthwhile.

"Then, shall we?"

"I don't think there's any reason to refuse, as long as we can make time for it."

Everyone quietly agreed with Ryu Chungwoo.

Fortunately, we did have time in our schedule.

As soon as we expressed our interest, the company quickly rearranged our schedule to accommodate the appearance.

"Okay. It's been arranged."

"Oh~"

In fact, the outcome wasn't surprising.

*It was fortunate that our first domestic comeback stage was on TNet.*

It was supposed to be TeSTAR's first live stage after the album dropped, but instead, we'd just pre-record the performance.

TNet had agreed to it, probably because it was part of T1 as well.

*In the first place, TNet got our exclusive comeback show for the same reason, so it makes sense they're accommodating us.*

Being in the same T1 gang was a massive advantage. If it weren't for that, we would've lost out on the offer entirely.

"Hmm."

Anyway, it wasn't a bad situation for us. We'd return to Korea right after this one performance, so not too many fans would be upset.

*Even so, the gap between the music video's release and our first performance was already too long.*

We'd released our album on Friday to aim for Billboard, which meant the first domestic music show wouldn't be until the following Thursday.

*That's almost a week of downtime.*

That was too long to keep the momentum going.

And anyone interested in this industry would know that.

*They'll be aware of this and try to use it against us.*

As expected, after about two days, the excitement over TeSTAR meeting such high expectations subsided, and negative comments quietly began appearing online.

★$
[My personal take on TeSTAR's new song]

The song is great, but to be honest, I think it's a bit excessive. Listening to it, I wonder how the hell they'll translate this into a live performance...

Same goes for the performance in the music video. Are they going to do both at the same time? Hmm...

I assume they'll pre-record this stuff, but I'm a little bummed out about that. TeSTAR's biggest strength is performing live, but it feels like they kicked that down the drain themselves.

I know they're aiming for the US, but I hope they don't lose touch with their original audience either. It leaves a bit of a bitter taste

+ I'm neither an anti nor a fan, just a passing listener. Here's some proof (_screen capture of music streaming site play history_)

– As expected, I wasn't the only one who thought this way! To be honest, the mood seemed to practically forbid criticism, if that's the right word. It's not like worrying about the live performance means you're an anti...

– TeSTAR is a group whose strength is performing live, so I get the point. If they mess it up, they'll be ridiculed all the more

– Well, I suppose they'll tone down the choreography as needed. Why all this shit over it? ㅋㅋ
$★

These were the top three comments.

"Hmm."

I'd expected some complaints, but it seemed they were only worried about the live performances.

*This is perfect.*

*Looks like there's not much to complain about.*

I figured the negative commenters had wanted to stress us out more than they could by simply saying, 'It's too noisy, it's not my taste'... I actually liked this angle very much.

In the meantime, the English-speaking crowd, including idiots who'd picked up some K-pop knowledge somewhere, were going wild too.

★$
– They really did those high notes while doing this dance? *Insanity*
└ To tell you the truth, most groups use pre-recorded audio and dance along to it :( Don't expect too much
└ Shut up, K-pop hater! They've sung live for most of their performances. Wait a little and you'll see something amazing!
$★

*What a ruckus.*

People drawn in by ⟨Cosmic Gunner⟩, existing fans, and people who liked ⟨Cosmic Gunner⟩ but didn't like K-pop were all mixing together, creating chaos.

The general consensus was, *the music video is amazing, but can they really pull this off live?*

I liked that this was the question people were asking, too.

*The buzz is sweet.*

Wouldn't this kind of buzz turn into a great talking point after just one performance?

*Assuming we do well, of course.*

From the members' reactions when we talked about the performances, I thought I already knew whether we'd do well or not.

I smirked and cracked my neck.

*Time to look at some objective indicators.*

*Status window!*

I summoned the hologram for the first time in a while.

It had been almost half a year since I checked my stats properly.

*It's not like I can level up or anything anymore, so I don't reach for it as often.*

Still, it wasn't like I'd been living so carelessly that the stats I'd worked on would have deteriorated.

*They'll be the same.*

Recalling the moments when I'd benefited from raising my stats appropriately, I checked the window again.

*Let's see—*

And I was shocked by what I saw.

★-
[Name: Park Moondae (Ryu Gunwoo)]

Level: (-)

Title: The Successful (Idol)

Vocal: S

Dance: B+

Visual: A

Talent: S-

Traits: Infinite Potential, Time of Indulgence (S), Missions Are My Thing (S), Catch-All Ears (A)

[Remaining points: 1]
-★

*Why are there two S ranks?*

No, I hadn't raised those stats using points. I had no idea how they'd grown like this.

*And why is Talent of all things at S?* I hadn't done anything to warrant such a stat. Was it my experience on stage that had raised my level?

In addition, my appearance had gone up a grade.

Now, it wasn't an A-, which meant it barely qualified as an A, but a true A.

*What was this...? Was it the power of exercise and hair dye?*

*My natural gains have been pretty amazing.*

I ran a hand through my white hair and crossed my arms. It seemed I'd been working harder than I'd thought.

"Hmm."

It made me feel pretty good about myself.

*I doubt I could fail a performance with these stats...*

*Hmm. Shall I shock them a little?*

I spent my last point without hesitation.

"Let's give it a try," I said.

I smiled and crossed my arms. Thinking about what the internet would say in a few days...
