# Chapter 354 - The Weight of Secrets, V

I considered the situation we were in. A member had left the dorm without their smartphone early in the morning during promotions and remained unreachable for several hours.

Worse still, I was the reason the group's schedule had been put on hold for three whole days.

*I must be out of my mind.*

There was no excuse for what I'd done. It was complete lunacy. I gritted my teeth.

*At least Cha Eugene left a note.*

There was obviously an emergency going on at the dorm.

At worst, even the agency might have heard about it by now.

*...Damn it.*

*I'm the problem now?*

It was a bomb waiting to explode.

I could already imagine the look on Seon Ahyeon's face.

I gritted my teeth and looked at the intercom screen again.

*Ding-dong.*

Seon Ahyeon was standing right in front of the door, not merely at the entrance on the first floor. I didn't even know how this was possible.

So it was even more confusing...

"It's one of Hubaenim's members. Shall I open the door for him?" Cheongryeo asked.

"...Wait a minute."

I stared at the front door and slowly took a deep breath.

"I want to open it myself."

"Hmm? Is that so?"

I headed for the front door, leaving him behind with an expression that openly said, 'Why?'

And I pulled the door open.

The same person I had seen on the intercom screen appeared through the doorway.

"...!"

Seon Ahyeon's face was pale despite the mask covering it. However, the moment the door opened, his expression changed. I could see his relief. He hung his head.

"S-so you were here..."

*This is driving me crazy.*

I managed to speak with some difficulty.

"I didn't realize I'd left my phone behind this morning... I'm sorry I made you worry so early."

"...! N-no, don't mention it. I..."

Seon Ahyeon took a deep breath and spoke without raising his head.

"I'm the one who's sorry..."

"......"

Judging from his appearance, it seemed like he'd spent the morning running around looking for me. What was he apologizing for?

"I-I caused a commotion... Moo-Moondae, m-m-maybe you needed some time alone, but... I-I didn't realize..."

"No."

I wanted to stop hearing these fucking apologies.

I tried my best to respond calmly, pushing those thoughts aside.

I failed completely.

"Cut it out. It's my fault for disappearing on you without a word."

"......"

"......"

Seon Ahyeon immediately shut his mouth. I wanted to bite my tongue clean off.

*You fucking idiot.*

*Why did you answer him like that?*

*You sound unstable and confrontational!*

I found the silence unbearable.

At that moment, a nonchalant voice intervened.

"Kongi is likely to think we're taking him out on another walk. Please come in, or leave—whichever you wish."

*Woof!*

The dog's barking echoed through the hallway. Seon Ahyeon's head shot up.

"...! S-so sorry! Then... Moo-Moondae."

His eyes did not meet mine. Something like a smile appeared on his face despite the mask covering it.

"I-i-if you start feeling better... come back w-whenever... Here..."

"......"

He carefully held out my smartphone, as if encouraging me to take it.

"Y-you'll probably need your phone..."

I looked at the distorted apple pattern and clenched my fists.

And I accepted the smartphone.

"I was going to leave anyway. I'll come with you."

"......"

"Let's go."

"Y-yes...!"

I took my phone and went out the door. Seon Ahyeon hurried to press the elevator button.

"Woof!"

The dog barked excitedly and ran out with a harness in its mouth.

*What are you doing out here?*

"It looks like he already misunderstood. And thanks to you, today's walk did end rather early, Moondae," Cheongryeo said.

"......"

I stared.

"Kongi, wait a minute. Let's put this on."

I barely opened my mouth.

"I'm sorry."

"Yes."

*No, I was talking to your dog.*

The dog was the only one excited as the three of us entered the elevator and rode down in silence.

I opened my mouth first.

"How did you know... I was here?"

Seon Ahyeon flinched slightly.

"Th-the security footage."

"...!"

*CCTV?*

That was an extreme method I'd never considered. The natural thing would have been to call the police or perhaps tell the agency. Seon Ahyeon hadn't mentioned either, however.

*Don't tell me...*

"I-I asked urgently... saying it was an emergency... So I filled out an agreement, and they showed me..."

He was speaking as if he'd acted alone. Had this guy gone to the apartment's security office alone and asked for something like that?

I looked down. He wasn't wearing socks, and his feet had been crammed into his shoes haphazardly.

"......"

This situation wasn't particularly pleasant, but even so, there was an odd feeling stirring in me.

*I guess it's not completely over for me yet, then.*

The alarm that had been blaring in my mind grew a little quieter.

I answered quietly.

"So that's how you... knew I'd followed this sunbaenim here?"

"...Y-yes."

Seon Ahyeon nodded vigorously.

The atmosphere seemed to soften between us...

"Strange... I've never told either of you which unit I live in," Cheongryeo said, pointing out the obvious.

"...!"

"I don't think I've ever shown you the interior of the building, either... That's hardly enough information, is it?"

He'd cut right into the conversation.

However, Seon Ahyeon didn't seem flustered by his questions.

"N-no. I asked one of the VTIC members over the phone..."

"......"

"Who?" Cheongryeo asked.

"Chae-Chaeyul sunbaenim. I-I was really thankful because he let me know right away..."

"Ah."

I was witnessing a disaster rapidly unfolding for the guy who was about to enlist.

*That's too bad.*

Misfortune always came in batches. *Sucks to be you.*

After that conversation, we split up from Cheongryeo on the first floor.

I knew that no matter how annoyed he was, he'd take care of his dog, so he'd probably still go for a walk.

Even so, he didn't leave without a few muttered parting words.

"Hmm. I see why you were worried. He's the sort that can be emotionally unstable..."

"...!"

"Haha. So this affects your group's activities, then. No wonder you were worried."

*This bastard...*

He had immediately recognized that Ahyeon's stats were in jeopardy.

*...It's his trait, after all.*

I considered Cheongryeo's trait once again.
★-
\
#><✦ Trait: Appraisal (A)><#

That which is valuable is rare, and there's garbage everywhere you look.

– Human resource appraisal ability +150%
-★

*...I'll have to be careful around him.*

In the meantime, his physical attractiveness had even gone up. It seemed that he wasn't anywhere close to running out of steam anytime soon.

Cheongryeo raised his voice back to a normal speaking volume.

"My advice still stands."

"Yes."

*Whatever.*

"See you," he said.

I put a hand to my face, then finally said, "Thanks for the... tea."

"Haha."

Cheongryeo walked in the opposite direction with the dog.

And then silence came again.

Seon Ahyeon did not ask why I'd gone to Cheongryeo, nor why I'd suddenly left the dorm so early in the morning.

We kept our mouths shut and quietly walked along the promenade in the opposite direction.

Surprisingly, Seon Ahyeon spoke first, bringing up a completely different topic.

"...I-i-is it okay if I tell the others that... I-I found you?"

"...If we go into the dorm right now, won't everyone be there?"

Had a few of them gone to the police station? I felt a chill go down my spine, but thankfully there was no mention of the police.

"P-probably... I-I came out alone, so I'm j-just worried..."

"......"

It seemed that he'd also left the dorm without thinking it through fully or telling any of the members. And only now did he seem to have come to his senses. Seon Ahyeon lowered his head, apparently embarrassed.

After some hesitation, I spoke.

"Thanks for worrying about me."

"...! N-no..."

Seon Ahyeon suddenly stopped walking.

There was a muffled quality to his voice... Don't tell me he was crying.

"Seon Ahyeon."

But he didn't cry.

Instead, he continued speaking in a trembling voice.

"Moo-Moondae. I-I-I'm sure you're sick of hearing this, but... C-can I... say one thing?"

"......"

I silently nodded.

The words followed right away, as if he'd gone over them again and again in his head.

"You're... an amazing person, and y-you've always been great at everything you do. Always. You've never failed. I mean that..."

*Ah.*

I remembered the words he'd shouted at me yesterday.

– *Y-you shouldn't be performing right now... I-i-it's getting worse...*

He was trying to counteract all the negative meanings I could have inferred from those words.

"I-I... I used the wrong words... Moondae, you've always done so well..."

I stopped walking on the street in front of the dorm where no one was.

I blankly listened to him. Seon Ahyeon's words did not end.

"I-I'm the problem."

"...!"

"I-I know... I'm always shaky, and I'm bad at managing my emotions. I-I've always been that way..."

*What?*

It was almost as if he'd overheard what Cheongryeo had said.

His words were objective to the point of cruelty.

"Th-this happened before..."

As if he'd already felt himself slipping into the same mistake again, he looked down at his feet.

"I m-m-might seem a lot more awkward than usual at the moment... And that could be a bad thing for the team..."

Seon Ahyeon ended his speech with a calm tone typical of a person who had already reached a conclusion.

"S-so we can simply say that I'm ill."

"...!"

"Th-this is my own problem. We d-don't need to say anything else..."

Seon Ahyeon swallowed.

"And as for the schedules I'll miss... I-I'll practice. I'll practice hard..."

His voice became increasingly desperate.

"Our performances... sh-shouldn't be affected if I do that... There will be no problem in the t-team... You don't have to do anything about my problem. It's fine..."

"......"

"I'm the one who needs to see a doctor, not you... S-so please don't worry about what I told you yesterday... Please forget about it..."

"Wait—"

*Don't say it like that.*

However, before I had time to say anything, Seon Ahyeon hurriedly continued talking.

"B-b-but, Moo-Moondae. I know this might sound offensive to you, but... J-just once, please listen to me."

He asked in a trembling voice.

"Could you please speak to a therapist just once?"

I turned around.

Seon Ahyeon flinched.

"I-I'm sorry for bringing this up again... I'm the one who needs to see a doctor, not you..."

"......"

"N-next time, I'll listen properly. I promise. I really will. I'll listen to everything you say..."

Seon Ahyeon never cried or screamed, even though his shoulders trembled.

He only stuttered, but it was clear that he'd already made up his mind.

He'd decided that all this conflict was due to his own problems and that he should take care of them himself.

So, he was telling me not to worry about it, and to look into my own condition at least once.

"......"

I tried several times to choose the right words to say, but stopped short.

The calculations were a mess.

But I knew what conclusion I wanted to reach.

"You say you're ready to listen to whatever I have to say?"

Seon Ahyeon nodded quickly.

And again, he spoke in a low voice.

"I-I asked you to share, but I failed to—"

"No, that was my fault."

I spoke confidently, without hesitation.

*You have reached your conclusion, and now I have reached mine.*

"It's just not something anybody could be asked to easily believe. In hindsight, I realize that it just sounded like the ravings of a lunatic."

"Moo-Moondae-yah..."

Seon Ahyeon seemed to hold back his tears.

I nodded seriously, my thoughts falling into place.

*Okay.*

The source of my mistake was there.

"I should have told you in such a way that you had no choice but to believe me."

"...Wh-what?"

*Let's think about it.*

Ahyeon was not the sort of person who came up with sudden, intuitive ideas or jumped to logical conclusions based on evidence.

He was the sort of person who filtered through all the inconsistencies that needed explaining and questions that needed answering until the most suitable answer remained. Then he accepted that answer.

*That's how he contributed to our recent album.*

I knew this about him.

Then, I should have explained it in a way that suited him from the beginning.

Things were clearer now.

*This is not a matter of trust or closeness, but of timing.*

Normally, I would have taken all of those things into consideration and kept control of the situation, but I'd mistakenly thought he believed me from the start, and that had caused this fiasco.

"You said you would listen. All right. I'm going to explain to you one more time, so listen."

"...!! Th-that..."

This time, I would pack my explanation with witnesses and evidence so that he would have no way to refute what I was saying.

No matter what came of this, by doing this, I would come to a result that I would have no regrets over.
