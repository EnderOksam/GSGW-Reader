# Chapter 235 - The Human Rights Gambit

*The Human... Rights Commission?*

I'd expected the room to erupt in confusion, but everyone except me seemed calm. Apparently they had all agreed on this already.

*At least he told the others what he planned to do before he filed the report.*

I was about to press my forehead, but when I realized that I still had an IV attached, I stopped.

"You mean... the National Human Rights Commission, right?"

"Yes!"

*As far as I knew, it had no actual judicial power.*

At most, it could reprimand the agency.

I doubted they'd even take an anonymous report about an idol's human rights seriously.

Bae Sejin didn't seem fazed by my reaction.

"I know. They can't solve all of this."

"......"

"But the press will still cover it! And the agency will get a warning to correct its conduct!"

"...?"

Bae Sejin still clenched his fists and eagerly explained the principles of his actions.

So, to sum up, this guy's judgment was like this.

"So because the agency is blocking the entertainment press... you're trying to get the general news to cover the story?"

"Yes."

In short, it was a diversion.

Bae Sejin... Did he have such an ambition?

*Hoo...*

*Whew... I could really use a smoke—no, forget it.*

I'd nearly bitten my tongue, so I pulled myself together and gathered my thoughts.

I quickly checked in my head what would happen.

*If the complaint was anonymous, the impact Bae was hoping for might never materialize.*

An entertainment company trying to hide an accident involving its artists?

The chance of the Human Rights Commission going out of its way to identify an anonymous agency seemed slim.

Even if reporters stirred up a few rumors, it might only anger the company.

Then again, if the company came to fear investigation more than whoever had reported it, that could make negotiations easier.

"When I said anonymous, I didn't mean I hid the group's name."

"Then what did you mean?"

"I only kept the informant's identity secret. I reported it as a friend of my mom's..."

Bae Sejin swallowed.

"That way, they won't think one of us did it. They might suspect a competing agency or someone who left the company."

As it turned out, he had compiled a list of the labor and employment violations at the agency.

These included unpaid overtime and illegal inspection of personal information linked to the corporate spy.

*How big is he planning to make this?*

In fact, I doubted this was reality.

"I-I'm not trying to brag! But we needed to do at least this much..."

I held back a bitter laugh.

"No. I think you did well."

"...!"

Bae Sejin's eyes widened.

"R-really??"

"Yes."

*Well, we'd addressed the most pressing concerns for now.*

*At least T1, the parent company, won't direct all its anger at TeSTAR.*

It was a shame I couldn't use the accident solely to my own advantage.

But I hadn't been the only one involved, and there was no rule saying things always had to go the way I wanted.

The other members had grievances of their own.

They had every right to make decisions themselves.

*This might get out of hand in an unexpected way.*

If it became a broader issue about workplace mistreatment, senior executives and the parent company would have to deal with it.

From our perspective, it was enough for the staff who handled our actual work to keep working.

As long as the group could continue functioning, that was what mattered.

*If we're lucky, the director will go back to signing things without getting involved.*

Just imagining it was comfortable.

I nodded my head, feeling more comfortable.

"Yes. I'm looking forward to the results."

"All right!"

Bae Sejin brightened. The other members nodded without showing any unease.

*It seems like they fought a lot with the agency after the accident.*

I clicked my tongue in my head and asked the unanswered question again.

"So, what day is it today?"

"......"

Then the guys exchanged glances.

*What?*

Ryu Chungwoo, who had been quiet until now, slowly opened his mouth.

"...Moondae."

"Yes."

"Today is July 6th."

"...!"

It was already a different month.

*Wait. I lost consciousness on June 18, our debut anniversary...*

That meant I'd been in a coma for eighteen days.

"......"

The rest of June had just vanished.

While I was stunned for a moment, Ryu Chungwoo spoke in a small voice.

"So, don't think about anything else and rest. Think about work or activities later."

"......"

"From now on... Think of your body first."

Ah.

I looked up at Ryu Chungwoo.

He had an unusually dark complexion.

*A metal fragment would have struck Chungwoo in the head, so I'd taken the hit to my chest instead.*

Honestly, it was a better trade-off than losing him.

I wasn't a psychopath who could sit there and watch someone next to me die.

I shot him a wry look, though I understood how he felt.

*Does he think I fell into a coma instead of him?*

It might have been the system's doing, so he didn't need to feel indebted. Still, I hoped he'd understand that eventually.

I shrugged and got up.

"I will take care of it. In fact, I think my body is fine even now... Could it be that I just couldn't wake up because I was tired?"

"Y-you shouldn't get up...!"

"Park Moondae, sit down!"

"I think you'd better get tested again."

"......"

That's right... They were relentless.

And after a while...

The medics returned to the hospital room sooner than planned because of their fervent requests.

The medical staff were shocked that someone who'd been in a coma for over fifteen days had eaten melon almost immediately after waking.

"You ate what? Please tell me how your stomach feels right now!"

"...!!"

"I am fine."

"Doctor! The patient here..."

And all sorts of calls followed.

*...Yes.*

*Right. I'd been unconscious for eighteen days and then eaten an extremely sweet melon. No wonder they were alarmed.*

Big Sejin, who'd suggested it, turned pale. Fortunately, the test results showed nothing wrong.

If I'd been an ordinary patient, my stomach might have cramped the moment I swallowed the first bite.

"I should have eaten it!"

I ignored Eugene's disappointment at not having eaten the melon himself.

*This must be thanks to Nectar.*

Its complete vitality recovery effect had probably restored my digestive system, too.

*Let's check it out while they're talking.*

Once things had quieted down, I opened the traits section of my status window.

★-
\
Traits: Infinite Potential, Time of Indulgence (S), Nectar (S) #r[Activated]r#, Catch-All Ears (A)
-★

It was also marked as activated.

The trait seemed to be speeding up my recovery and would vanish once the wound in my chest was fully healed.

*So Bacchus is gone?*

I couldn't help feeling disappointed. It had been so useful during our promotions.

Maybe I'd draw it again next time, but I couldn't count on that.

I swallowed my regret and turned off the status window.

*So... eighteen days.*

It was a waste, but it was fortunate that there wouldn't be any major problems with the tour schedule.

*A month should be more than enough time for me to recover.*

The other members who were still in casts expected to be better within three weeks.

With another month to prepare, we could hold the tour more or less as planned—perhaps missing a few concerts scheduled in Japan.

*That's enough.*

I nodded inwardly.

And after all the medical staff had left, the members sighed in relief and came to their senses.

"Moondae... Really, I'm sorry."

"No, it was delicious."

"Let's get permission from now on..."

And after a while...

It seemed that at last, it had entered their minds that I might as well get some rest.

"We'll be back tomorrow!" Raebin said.

"I-if there's a problem, call me anytime! I-I live nearby!" Ahyeon added.

"I'm not leaving the hospital."

"Ah, Cha Eugene is such a mess, let's get out!"

"Hing."

"Get some rest, Moondae. We're so glad you're back."

"Thank you."

Chungwoo's small smile faded almost immediately.

*He looks really upset.*

It must have been harder on him than I'd realized.

The moment I tried to catch him and dig it up...

"...Park Moondae."

"...? Yes."

Just before Bae Sejin left, he spoke with a serious face.

"If the commission starts an investigation... I want to look into suing T1 Stars based on the results."

"...What?"

"And if we succeed, we'll find ourselves a new agency!"

*What the hell was he talking about?*

He wasn't just trying to attract press coverage. He wanted to use the commission's investigation to take the agency to court.

Would it even work? More importantly, why pick a fight with T1, a giant corporation that owned its own broadcasting station and studios?

*Were they planning to throw away all those resources?*

I stared at the one member I couldn't believe had agreed to this. Big Sejin carefully avoided my eyes.

"Hmm. Well, T1 will probably try to make changes at the agency if that happens," Big Sejin said. "I bet we could get a better deal if we discussed litigation."

"Even if we don't leave for a new agency, getting better terms would be good for us."

*So that's why he'd agreed...*

"It won't happen right away. We can discuss it carefully later. Let Moondae rest a little longer," Chungwoo said.

"Sure~"

"...Good."

I'd need time to think about this on my own.

I waved the others off. They smiled and waved back as they left the room, closing the door.

*Click.*

I sighed in the hospital room, which became quiet in an instant.

"Whew."

A lawsuit.

*I could roll with most things, but a lawsuit was a bad idea if we wanted a long career in this industry.*

I'd hardly ever seen an artist sue and then go on to have a smooth career.

Win or lose, it would stay on our record in the industry.

*If we wanted to keep the public on our side, staying on good terms was the practical approach.*

Even if we won, litigation could take ages, and our activities might be put on hold until then...

*Wait a minute.*

Suddenly, goosebumps ran up my spine.

*What about the tour?*

We might not be able to hold it at all.

*What were these guys thinking?!*

If we couldn't tour, I could die.

The road to hell was paved with good intentions, and the danger suddenly felt very real.
