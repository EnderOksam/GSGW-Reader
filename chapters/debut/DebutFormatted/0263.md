# Chapter 263 - The Reversal Challenge

Surprisingly, the promotional idea Cha Eugene came up with wasn't all that outlandish.

"Let's make a challenge!" Cha Eugene said.

"Challenge?"

"A dance challenge!"

In other words, 'please film yourselves doing our choreography.'

It was a kind of short-form content that had been popular for several years now. To be honest, I hadn't seen a group make a comeback recently without trying it.

*Goldie #1's group also got a lot of attention with this.*

But that was because the company had done a good job putting together the challenge. Most groups were satisfied just having tried it. People didn't dance along just because the song and choreography were good.

I wasn't sure what the magic formula was either, but it seemed that people filming these challenges were looking for a particular kind of enjoyment.

However, Cha Eugene had a surprisingly good grasp of it.

[We put a sky filter here... Then it suddenly shifts to a Halloween party, and the song changes too!]

"Wow."

[First, there's Wheel's pre-chorus, and at the end of it...]

Cha Eugene adjusted the music, combining the refreshing, emotional climax of Wheel with the powerful, dark whistling chorus of Drill.

[There's the whistle! And then that bounce with the drop from Drill, and we're done. How is it?]

*That's actually pretty good?*

It was a bit childish, but the atmosphere, song, and choreography all changed at the whistle, so it had a taste that drew attention in an instant.

Even Seon Ahyeon, who was not interested in this, nodded.

"Th-that's cool...!"

"I know!" Cha Eugene said, switching back to Korean.

It didn't take much thought to see the appeal. Even without any context, you could tell it was similar to the kinds of videos that went viral.

*If this works as intended, we'll get something out of it.*

"What do you all think—"

I was about to put it to a show of hands, but noticed that several members looked unsure whether they'd understood Cha Eugene's English.

*I guess I should translate what he said first.*

"We can connect the two title tracks to make a dance challenge. He thinks it'll make for a fun contrast."

"Ah~ I get it!"

And then opinions came out.

Most were in favor.

"Then both songs will be able to receive publicity."

"The story behind it will be similar to our promotional concept, right? That twist! Oh, Cha Eugene, that's a great idea~"

"Hehe."

It wasn't a risky gamble, and honestly, it was a tried-and-tested method these days, so both the group and the company approved it quickly.

"They say they'll make arrangements through a platform partner."

"Yeah~"

"Then, we should shoot a video for the announcement of the challenge. One per person."

Bae Sejin swallowed.

"So we... film it alone?"

"Yes."

Given the nature of these videos, there could be at most two people in each one if we wanted to make a strong impression.

Bae Sejin suddenly looked deeply worried.

"Me! Me! Me! I'll go first!"

Of course, Cha Eugene filmed his video with great excitement.

To be honest, I thought his came out the best. He'd probably suggested it because he'd already pictured how it would look.

The way he tilted his head and added the catchy hand movement from Drill's chorus was perfect.

*Is it because he's young?*

He had an instinctive grasp of these things.

However, there was also a guy the same age as Cha Eugene who'd redone his filming about ten times.

"H-how about this...?"

"Hahaha! Kim Raebin, you're terrible at this!"

"...! It's my first time trying this, but if I can just figure out how it works, I'll do even better than you!"

"No, you idiot!"

Kim Raebin barely managed to succeed, somewhat scarred by Cha Eugene's ridicule.

"Th-there..."

Cha Eugene was relishing his victory.

"Everyone did great. It's because Kim Raebin made such good songs!"

*You've got some nerve giving him medicine after making him sick.*[1]{Editor's Note: The expression 'give someone the disease and then the medicine' describes causing someone trouble and then pretending to help them. Here, Eugene teases Raebin before praising him.}

*Wait a minute.* Come to think of it, Kim Raebin was technically the youngest, since his birthday came after Eugene's... Well, he didn't really keep up with the latest trends like this.

Anyway, the videos we uploaded worked nicely with our promotional strategy and got plenty of attention online.

The challenge took on a life of its own beyond Cha Eugene's original plan, and users began adapting it into their own challenges.

★$
[The TeSTAR twist challenge!]

"So they wear a doll mask and run around among butterflies... Then they throw the mask aside and pretend to be villains?"

"...Yes."

"...Isn't it so different from what we did?!"

Bae Sejin's comment was a little harsh, but it was true.

The Drill stage, which we'd first performed at the awards ceremony, had become quite popular, so that probably had something to do with it.

*This is a good thing.*

It was a great success, considering how extensively the content was being reproduced. The number of videos using our song would only keep growing.

*Youngrin can never do something like this with her new song.*

It was a song that focused only on the 'pleasure of listening'.

As planned, we'd expanded the playing field to include more than just music streaming.

The company was excited and even suggested partnering with other companies to make 'Drill-exclusive camera filters', but Cha Eugene was adamantly opposed.

"There are lots of people who think these challenges are cheesy. Our music videos are so cool, so we can't make silly filters out of them!"

"Um, yes."

He meant we should lower the barrier to enjoying the songs by producing viral content, without taking it too far.

It also matched what I was trying to say.

*He's useful.*

He was a smart guy. The problem was that he only used his brains when he wanted to.

With Cha Eugene's promotional idea having a huge impact, our activities went ahead as planned without a hitch, as if the emergency right before them had never happened.

*We're about 1,000 points below Youngrin when it comes to streaming platforms.*

But that was fine. Most people thought we deserved first place, unless they were obsessed with streaming numbers or were antis.

Our strategy of making the win feel deserved had worked.

I nodded my head in a rather thrilling sense of satisfaction.

"Yes, your hyung is crying loudly~"

*Looks like we've established ourselves across Korea with this.*

We spent about two weeks doing promotions through variety shows.

*I'll have to check up on other news as well.*

Gradually, I began to look at news from abroad. It was after confirming that the scale of the tour had increased tremendously.

*In the first place, Drill was made with a global audience in mind.*

It was uncomplicated, exciting, and easy to listen to. The music video was beautiful, what with all the money we'd spent on it, and the choreography was amazing... That sort of thing had universal appeal.

*Is that why there was so much more demand for this album?*

The global market. This was where Cha Eugene's PR strategy really began to show its true worth.
