# A Secret Reunion

*Park Moondae, going to meet Ryu Gunwoo with Ryu Chungwoo...*

"Moondae, did that give you a shock? Are you okay?"

"I'm okay."

What the hell had happened so suddenly? I closed my eyes to keep myself from trembling.

So... Ryu Chungwoo had found Ryu Gunwoo's whereabouts.

*Let's start with that.*

"I guess I'm just surprised. I wonder how they got in touch with him all of a sudden."

"Right? So did I."

*No, I doubt we feel the same way.* Anyway, Ryu Chungwoo grinned and continued his explanation.

"One of the eldest son's grandsons met him at the civil service training center in Seoul and got in touch with the clan."

"Apparently, a lot of people remembered him."

*I doubt it was simply that a lot of people remembered him.*

A former national athlete and gold medalist, now a grand prize-winning idol... Ryu Chungwoo was sure to have an effect on the elders in the clan. They'd probably searched high and low, asking everyone they could think of.

*It's no wonder.*

Just one of those titles would have been enough to make them proud of him forever, but having two was practically overwhelming. No wonder they'd gone all out to help him. 

So, as soon as Ryu Gunwoo reemerged into society, he was found.

Whatever I thought of the situation, I understood the logic behind it and nodded silently.

*But how do I turn this car around?*

If that guy noticed anything strange about Park Moondae in Ryu Gunwoo's body and me, I'd probably end up being sent for a psychiatric evaluation. I racked my brain.

At the very least, I had to make Ryu Chungwoo drop me off and turn around and leave...

"Sorry. I guess I got excited and wanted to surprise you. You probably needed some time to prepare yourself for something like this."

*He was making excuses for me without even being asked, huh?*

I tried not to smile as I answered seriously.

"Well, I'm grateful, but I'm a little nervous and taken aback. I'm worried I won't be able to talk properly. How about we go home for today?"

It would be stupid to suddenly say, 'I've changed my mind. I don't want to meet him,' after making such a fuss about asking him to find Ryu Gunwoo in return for saving his life.

*I'll stall and gently persuade him to turn around.*

However, Ryu Chungwoo opened his mouth with a worried expression.

"Yeah? It'll be hard to find another time to meet him for a while... We have a tour after the awards ceremonies."

*Damn.*

"Hmm... I'll have to make time for it somehow."

Ryu Chungwoo smiled.

"Don't worry too much. I have a driver's license... So it won't be a problem for me to drive you out to see him without telling anyone."

"......"

"You don't have a driver's license."

"...Yes. Thank you."

*I was going crazy.*

*Hoo...*

Now that this guy knew about it, it would be difficult for me to just go meet Ryu Gunwoo alone.

But if I refused to meet him now and demanded his contact information instead, it could turn into an emotional mess.

*He'll think I changed my mind after he went to all the trouble of asking the clan to find him.*

I was even starting to think it might be best to meet him today, now that we'd already gotten in the car. I considered all sorts of possibilities. 

And I concluded.

"If you're too nervous, we can go back today, and I'll talk to the company about arranging another time..."

"No. I'll try to calm down while we go."

"Hm, yes."

*I don't want to make this any bigger than it has to be.*

The moment he contacted the company, word might reach some staff member or reporter, and then things would really go to shit.

*Many people are already interested in Park Moondae's background.*

Ryu Gunwoo was a civilian, but he was related to Ryu Chungwoo. There was no guarantee that nobody would dig into his finances or family background. 

"......"

*Fine, fuck it.* I'd decided that I was going now.

Given the situation, I tried to smile. It'd look even more suspicious if my expression looked like trash.

"Anyway... Thank you so much. I'm really surprised. I think my heart's racing."

*Though not for the reason you think.*

"Haha. Yes. We have some time, so I'll go slowly."

Before meeting Ryu Gunwoo, I tried one last time to send him back to the dorm without me by saying, 'It's okay to drop me off and go,' but...

"Um, how would you get back?"

I just kept my mouth shut.

Since I didn't want the company to know, I couldn't even call a manager. If I took a taxi, there'd be eyewitness accounts.

*Either way, Ryu Chungwoo is better for security than that...*

I gave up and accepted my fate. I felt so much better it was almost unbelievable.

*I'll just focus on what I can do.* I erased the text I'd been writing to the two or three possible numbers for 'Keundal'. I couldn't let Ryu Chungwoo see any message records between us when we exchanged numbers.

*We'll just exchange numbers and part ways quickly.*

I ran through a few scenarios in my head, but the strange headache I was getting felt like a bad sign.

And little by little, some practical questions came to mind.

"But... Did he agree to meet us?"

"What?"

Ryu Chungwoo shrugged.

"I just got a message. A relative who went to the training center with him told him, 'There's someone who wants to meet you. Can they see you for a moment?' He said it was okay."

*What?*

***

We soon arrived at a training center on the outskirts of Seoul.

People who had just finished the day's training poured out of the doors.

Apparently, some issues with appointments meant that only Seoul was holding urgent training sessions. Nobody was staying at the center, so everyone was commuting daily... Not that I cared about the details, since I'd never even passed the exam myself.

The important thing was that 'Ryu Gunwoo' really was sitting in a private room at the restaurant next door, waiting for us.

*This bastard.*

I was shocked. I'd never imagined he'd fall for such a flimsy excuse as 'there's someone who wants to meet you'.

But he'd fallen for it.

"Uh, um... Hello."

That reaction told me it really was 'Keundal' in there. Seeing him on the verge of breaking out in a cold sweat was enough to convince me.

It was frustrating, but it was also true that it was reassuring.

Ever since I'd seen him in the mindscape, I'd expected to sense that same strange disharmony when I met him in person, but apparently, that wasn't the case.

On the contrary, since this was my first time seeing him moving around in Ryu Gunwoo's body in person... I was pretty intrigued.

Perhaps this was how I'd feel if I had a younger brother. 

"You came to see me...?"

*Yes, you punk. You look perfectly fine, so why did you ignore my messages but agree to such a dubious meeting?*

...I couldn't say that out loud, so I calmly bowed my head.

"Hello. Hyung."

"Wh-what?"

"Do you remember me? I'm Park Moondae. Hyung helped me before."

My words were true. For the most part. *Please act natural.*

Keundal quickly looked between Ryu Chungwoo and me, then answered as if an exclamation mark had popped up above his head.

"Ah, ah~ Moondae...! Of course!"

"...Yes."

*This is hopeless.* This guy had no talent for acting.

Ryu Chungwoo looked a bit puzzled, but perhaps thinking the other man was just taken aback, he smiled and greeted him.

"I was surprised to hear that a relative of mine had helped Moondae out. Thank you for agreeing to meet with us."

Considering the various troublesome situations that could arise because of our jobs, Ryu Chungwoo was likely going to stay for the whole conversation.

After all, he was a relative of Ryu Gunwoo and Park Moondae's roommate, so it was only natural.

*On the contrary, he might feel hurt if I sent him away.*

He'd probably excuse himself once he thought the atmosphere had loosened up enough. Until then, I had no choice but to wait. I watched wryly.

"No need to thank me...! I hardly did anything. In fact, just call me Gunwoo... Ahem."

*I knew you'd be like this.* I immediately changed the topic.

"Let's start by eating."

"Sure. And what would you like to eat, Gunwoo-hyung? Oh, is it okay if I call you that?"

"...Yes."

I was doomed. I'd been hoping to get out of here quickly after drinking tea, but now we were ordering food.

My stomach hurt watching Ryu Chungwoo order a full-course meal. I had a long way to go and would have to cover for Keundal more than once.

*Damn it.*

...And an hour later.

By the time we'd eaten everything and were digging into the main course, Peking Duck, I'd already had to cover for more than five of Keundal's mistakes. 

"......"

It was an astonishing record.

*Though I'd expected this.*

At this rate, he'd build up momentum and make even more mistakes. In fact, Keundal wasn't even being that careless. While Ryu Chungwoo kept talking to lighten the mood, Keundal inadvertently used the wrong names every now and then.

For example, when he explained how Park Moondae and Ryu Gunwoo had met, he mixed up who had helped whom.

*– Ah, really, back then we ate together, and he gave a lot of advice to someone he'd only just met...*

*–* Hyung *did that. I still remember it well.*

Most of his slip-ups were like this. They cut it close.

Keundal tried to stay conscious of our swapped bodies as much as possible, but it wasn't easy for a guy who'd done nothing but study for four years to navigate the conversation.

A particularly close call had come when we were eating Dongpa-yuk just before this.

*– Back then, Gunwoo-hyung... Uh, I mean...*

He'd called me Gunwoo.

I wouldn't lie. I broke into a cold sweat here too.

*– ...Yes. I said, 'Gunwoo-hyung, you're such a great help, thank you.'*

– ...

I honestly thought I was screwed.

However, Ryu Chungwoo answered softly.

*– Oh, I see.*

A little while later, he lowered his voice and whispered to me.

*"It must be a bit difficult for him to hold a conversation after studying for so long. I guess I should talk less."*

*...*

Just like in the car, Ryu Chungwoo had made another excuse for me without being asked. He was a thoughtful guy who tried to understand other people.

*"You're right. Let's finish this quickly."*

I didn't miss my chance. 

And just before another main dish was served...

"I'll be back after I take this call."

"Yes!"

Ryu Chungwoo left.

The moment the door closed behind him, I saw the guy on the other side let out a heavy sigh.

"Phew..."

I knew it couldn't have been easy for him.

I crossed my arms and looked at the guy in front of me. He kept his eyes on the floor, perhaps conscious of his mistakes.

"......"

"......"

The silence between us lengthened.

*Good.* I couldn't hear anyone lingering outside our room.

I uncrossed my arms and got to the point.

"Why didn't you contact me?"

He looked perfectly fine. My thoughts were getting tangled as I wondered if some other problem had occurred. 

But the guy on the other side was startled.

"I did...! I really did!"

*What?*

"I didn't hear from you at all, so I was going to contact you separately, but what are you talking about?"

"Oh, but I thought this was... I-I mean, first of all..."

Keundal sounded deeply wronged.

"I texted you right away when I saw you'd won the grand prize..."

"......"

*Wait, what?* I had to confirm what he'd just said.

"Right after watching the live broadcast?"

"Yes... There was no reply, so I waited because I thought you were busy."

*Hey!*

"I... I told you to contact me after the awards ceremony was over."

"What??"

He looked dazed, as if he didn't understand the difference. I massaged my temples.

"Numbers that aren't in my contacts list are all blocked, and I can't change the settings because I don't have time to look at my smartphone while I'm at an awards ceremony."

"...! Whoops."

His text had been filtered out along with all the other messages sent to me during the awards ceremony. Keundal bowed his head deeply.

"Sorry...!"

"What's done is done."

It was my fault for not explaining the details. I held back a sigh.

"And if someone you don't know asks to meet like this, ask them for more detail."

I didn't know how he had the guts to accept such a dubious offer. 

The guy on the other side muttered in a dejected voice.

"No... I thought this was something like a signal from hyung to me."

"A signal?"

"Yes, like in a spy movie. I wondered if this was your way of getting in touch because we share the same 'Ryu' surname... And Ryu Chungwoo is one of your members..."

"......"

He certainly had quite an imagination. But he was unexpectedly close to the truth, which was funny. 

I grinned.

"Okay. You were right this time. But be careful in the future."

"Yes!"

It felt strange seeing that proud expression on Ryu Gunwoo's face. He felt even less like me than I'd expected.

*Can someone's appearance change this much depending on who's inside?*

I accepted that it apparently could and changed the subject.

It was the most urgent subject.

"And right now, you need to be especially careful about the mistakes you keep making. You don't want to get caught, do you?"

"Oh! Sorry..."

He bowed his head as if he was frustrated.

"Actually, this situation is partly my fault, since I asked Ryu Chungwoo to find my original body. So don't worry too much. I'll continue to cover for you."

I explained that he should avoid giving long replies and keep his answers short, using 'I'm tired from training' as an excuse.

The guy nodded eagerly. He seemed to understand.

I also gave myself a task.

"I'll steer the conversation so our past doesn't come up."

The eyes of the guy across from me lit up.

"Ah, then should we talk about TeSTAR?"

"That's not a bad idea."

"Hmmmm, I could talk about you guys all day, from the very beginning of your debut."

*Oh, my.*

Perhaps a little relaxed, he let his shoulders hang down a bit. I gave him a few pieces of the leftover Peking Duck as the atmosphere in the room relaxed. 

"Take some time to think up an answer while you eat."

"Yes!"

As the atmosphere calmed down, talking about our debut reminded me of something.

That experience in the mindscape, where the scenery from the teaser for our debut song, 'Magical Boys', appeared outside the veranda window.

Something this guy had said there had been bothering me.

"......"

I opened my mouth slowly.

"About you..."

"Yes!"

"...The other day, you said you'd use that body 'for a while'. Are you planning to leave it soon?"

"......"

"There's no need for that," I added.

His face, which had so easily shown his bewilderment and joy until now, suddenly stiffened. A strange expression appeared on it, one that almost didn't look human.

It reminded me of how he'd looked in the mindscape. I remained silent.

His answer came very firmly.

"It's necessary, actually."

*What?*

"Not that I'm the one who decides, hyung."

"...What do you mean?"

"I... There's a set date after which I won't be alive as a human anymore."

The day this guy fell off the railing and made a wish to the system. 

"My time runs out next summer."

"......"

I didn't know what to say.
