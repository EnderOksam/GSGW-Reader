# Chapter 293 - Turning the Tables, I

The debut group from Season 5 of ⟨Idol Inc.⟩, whose name had yet to be decided, had quite a packed schedule.

While preparing for their debut, they also had to fulfill the usual obligations of audition program winners, such as advertisements and interviews.

Although they weren't getting nearly as much buzz as TeSTAR, the previous male group to debut from the show, it was still enough to excite the members.

And one of them felt a sense of victory for another reason.

*Doesn't he even have the guts to contact me? This is fucking funny.*

Chae Seodam glanced at a saved contact on his smartphone. The owner of the number had yet to get in touch with him.

★:
#*[Moondae Sunbaenim]*#
:★

It was a number he'd obtained by coaxing a former TeSTAR manager. A little 'Hyung-nim, hyung-nim' was all it took to make that nerdy guy feel important and wheedle the number out of him.

*That's the kind of person he is, no matter how much he struts around.*

Chae Seodam assumed that Park Moondae was cut from similar cloth.

The way that guy, who could hardly talk back to anyone at school, now acted so tough because he'd become a popular idol was embarrassing to watch.

*He's just a lucky bastard. Who does he think he is?*

He'd sounded as though he was doing Seodam a favor, but it had all been hot air.

Even now, Chae Seodam subtly mentioned Park Moondae whenever he had the chance during interviews or filming, but the guy hadn't reacted at all.

He was obviously terrified.

*I thought he'd get angry and do something, but look at him. He's too scared to do even that.*

It was obvious he was painting himself as the hero of some comic book in his head. There were one or two losers like that in every class.

*He owes everything he has to luck. You idiot, you didn't realize you were describing yourself, did you?*

It was just funny how a guy who'd succeeded thanks to good timing and perhaps some talent in singing could go on a power trip against a top-ranked student like him, who'd always fought so fiercely.

Chae Seodam felt proud of himself for practicing until his feet felt like they'd fall off, and for cleverly dealing with every obstacle he faced while covering his tracks.

He'd let his guard down for a moment and slipped, but that wouldn't happen again.

*I won't let it happen again.*

After he'd taken advantage of Park Moondae's fame and reputation as much as possible, he had an even more thrilling plan in store.

The moment he encountered Park Moondae again, he'd provoke him until he couldn't stand it anymore and exploded. Getting a rise out of people was his specialty.

*It would be even better if he went running to the company for help.*

If the company tried to cover it up, all the better. He'd gather evidence of the cover-up and use it against Park Moondae too.

*If necessary, I can use it as proof that I was bullied. It'll be my way out.*

That way, he could improve his reputation into the bargain, killing two birds with one stone.

*An established idol continues to bully a rookie over a recording that was deleted on the spot!*

"Ah, I love it."

The mere thought of turning the tables filled Chae Seodam with satisfaction.

He also thought about Cheongryeo, the other person present when he'd played the recording. He'd been too excited at the time to think much about him.

Of course, now he was rationalizing that *not* thinking about him had all been part of his plan.

He was sure Cheongryeo wouldn't make any moves.

*Everyone's the same.*

Chae Seodam was sure that since his plan wouldn't hurt Cheongryeo, the guy would welcome Park Moondae's predicament. Haha, no matter how well they seemed to get along, they were still competitors.

He'd looked into Cheongryeo and seen that he'd been successful ever since his debut, so he could easily imagine how childish his personality would be.

*Rather, he might contact me to make a deal.*

He himself had been polite enough during their encounter, so Cheongryeo had no reason to be offended.

Perhaps Cheongryeo had even been waiting for Park Moondae to show a weakness.

"Yes~" he hummed.

Chae Seodam let his expectations snowball, shaking off the nervousness he'd felt over the past few days.

*I'm doing everything just right. Bold moves.*

The arrogance that had constantly tripped him up in life was returning.

And his complacency was shattered a few days later when he encountered someone in the company hallway.

Seon Ahyeon, who now shared his agency and had become his sunbaenim.

"Hello, Seon Ahyeon sunbaenim!"

"Hello...!"

Unlike when they were in school, Seon Ahyeon was walking toward him with his head held high, looking straight at him. *To think I have to treat a guy like that as my sunbaenim!*

Chae Seodam suddenly felt offended.

*If ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ hadn't worked out for him, he wouldn't even have landed a proper job.*

Seeing someone like that act as though he were more successful always made him feel how unfair life was.

*But what are you going to do about it?*

Even if he had seniority, what could he do besides throw it around? He probably cared about maintaining the little social status he'd managed to scrape together.

And Chae Seodam wasn't impressed by Seon Ahyeon's high-handedness in the least.

"Uh, I have something to say to Seodam-ssi, if that's okay?"

"Ah, yes! Seodam hyung, go ahead!" one of his group members said.

Seeing him try so hard not to stutter disgusted Chae Seodam, but he suppressed the urge to scoff when Seon Ahyeon singled him out.

The recorder was still running.

*Looks like I'll have another weakness to play around with.*

However, as soon as they entered a meeting room tucked away in the corner, Chae Seodam was caught off guard by Seon Ahyeon.

"I've been thinking a lot... about whether to tell the truth about you bullying me."

"...!"

*Tell the truth?*

For a moment, Chae Seodam took stock of his surroundings, wondering if this was really happening.

However, Seon Ahyeon wasn't done.

"Seodam, I have a recording."

"...!"

"Because I was so... so scared of you... I wondered if I was doing something wrong, so I recorded our conversations to listen to them again and try to understand, you know? I did that for a while."

"......"

"And I still have them."

Chae Seodam felt a chill run down his spine.

His survival instincts were screaming that he was in danger.

*N-no. This can't be happening.*

Chae Seodam denied reality with great effort, trying to convince himself that he was being rational.

It was what he'd always done, only more stubbornly than ever.

*He might threaten me, but he wouldn't dare actually release it.*

Wouldn't he stand to lose a lot himself if he released it? He was such a popular idol right now.

All he'd done was mess up Ahyeon's practical tests a little because he'd been in a bad mood. That was hardly school bullying. At least, that was how he saw it.

*And what recording?*

As far as he was concerned, he'd never been verbally abusive or hit the guy in a way that could come back to bite him.

Ahyeon was just trying to scare him.

*But...*

There was always the slim chance that he actually had something. *Fine. I'm doing this just in case.*

After justifying his behavior to himself, Chae Seodam adopted a pitiful tone.

"Ahyeon-ah... Do you hate me that much?"

"......"

"There was some friction when we were in school, but... It wasn't bad enough to warrant this. Is this because I was named the top student and you were only in second place? Gosh, I don't know what to say."

If Seon Ahyeon had expected him to get flustered and start making excuses, he was sorely mistaken.

"You know, I'm starting to think you're amused by how my reputation has taken a hit, and you're just trying to make fun of me..."

Hyper-aware of his recorder, he was doing his best to fabricate evidence...

"Seodam-ah."

However, Seon Ahyeon didn't seem shaken in the least.

"It doesn't work like that anymore."

"...!!"

"I'm really, really considering making it public. And telling you beforehand... is the last step toward freeing myself. So I thought I'd let you know."

"......"

"Then, I'll be leaving now."

Seon Ahyeon gave him a slight nod and left the conference room with a firm expression.

And Chae Seodam's head went blank.

***

*There's no way he'll actually do it.*

Chae Seodam lay on the bed in the dorm and repeated this thought.

The guy was just getting carried away by his popularity and trying to intimidate him.

*That stupid fucking bastard wants to feel like he's somebody, huh?*

...But on the other hand, he couldn't stop imagining what might happen if Seon Ahyeon really released the recordings.

Eventually, as time went by, he started to think like this.

*...Should I apologize now?*

*No, an apology would be admitting it!* He could never apologize... In the first place, had he done anything wrong enough to deserve an apology?

Ahyeon had been mentally weak, and all he'd done was push him a little further toward a breakdown. That was all. He was sick of the guy's victim mentality.

"Fuck..."

He couldn't predict how things would turn out. But the moment he did something, it would seem as though he was guilty.

*Should I just ignore him?*

But that didn't seem like a good idea either. His mind kept going in circles...

When those anxious, insanely long days had passed...

Finally, the other shoe dropped.

"Seodam, um... The head of the Management Office at the company wants to see you."

"...Yes."

*Fuck! Fuck!*

Chae Seodam felt it in his gut. Judging from the manager's expression, this couldn't be good.

Seon Ahyeon, that bastard, had gone through with it.

"...!"

*Right!*

At that moment, an incredible idea occurred to Chae Seodam.

If he could get evidence that Park Moondae had been harassing him before Seon Ahyeon made the recordings public, couldn't he use it to make a deal with the company even if things came to the worst?

As he followed the manager to the Management Office, he kept making calculation after calculation.

*I could even text him now and provoke him...*

*Click.*

However, after he'd been checked for recording devices, he was allowed into a meeting room used by the Management Office for consultations.

"You're here."

Sitting there was someone he hadn't expected at all.

Park Moondae.

"...!"

*What on earth?*

Park Moondae, who was sitting where the head of the office should have been, casually glanced at Chae Seodam and gestured toward the chair with his chin.

"Sit."

"......"

"Ah, I'm standing in for the head of the Management Office, by the way."

*Tuk.*

Park Moondae made the absurd claim casually and glanced at the door the manager had just closed.

And he grinned.

"Why don't we have a little chat?"

...Only then did Chae Seodam recall the initial suspicions he'd forgotten as he underestimated Park Moondae.

Why had he tried to find something incriminating on Park Moondae in the first place?

Because he suspected Park Moondae was out for revenge.

*– Lucky bastard.*

He'd wondered if all the incidents and revelations that had happened to him since Park Moondae mouthed those words might have been arranged by Park Moondae himself...

***

I lifted my head and looked at the guy who'd just opened the door and entered.

Chae Seodam looked as though he'd seen a ghost.

*That's good.*

The more disoriented he was, the easier he'd be to fool. He was already easy to manipulate, so this would make it even less of a challenge.

I spoke to the guy who was still standing.

"Aren't you going to sit down?"

"......"

"Well, I guess it doesn't matter... Should I start talking, then?"

I continued nonchalantly.

"I've gotten a report about school bullying–"

"It was all your doing, wasn't it?"

"What do you mean?"

"......"

But instead of answering, this bastard looked like he'd just had a revelation.

Then he finally pulled out a chair and sat down.

*Oh.*

The guy spoke, his eyes flashing.

"I think you're mistaken... I don't care if a controversy erupts and I have to quit, sunbaenim. I've already been criticized enough... It's not like being an idol is the only way I can make a living, right?"

I stared at him.

"......"

"That's right. I'm just going to quit being an idol, so I'll say everything I have to say before I leave. Yeah? I'll tell everyone that TeSTAR conspired against me, with the company in tow, to stir up rumors about me."

*Hmm. Typical reaction.*

*He's cornered, so he doesn't want to go down alone. He's threatening to take us down with him.*

*He's probably thinking he can go back to the field he majored in if it comes to the worst.*

This bastard had a brain, so he could probably surmise that whether it was TeSTAR or Seon Ahyeon who released something incriminating, it wouldn't be good for us either.

*That's why I was always careful to avoid that.*

He must have thought that talking like this would dissuade me from any further threats and get me to start negotiating.

"Is that right?"

*Too bad. You're wasting your time.*

*I'm not negotiating anything with him.*

"But if you quit being an idol, are you thinking of studying abroad?"

"What is that supposed to–"

"I don't think you'll find any opportunities in Korea for the time being."

I cupped my chin and spoke without even looking at Chae Seodam.

"I'll do everything in my power to expose you. I'll spread the truth so widely that nobody in Korea will think twice about turning you away."

"...!"

"I don't care either if any of this goes public. That doesn't mean TeSTAR is ruined."

I shrugged.

"From the moment you started talking bullshit about that recording, I'd already discussed everything with the people involved, you idiot."

How do you drive a bastard like him to the brink of ruin, where he feels he has nowhere to turn?

By bluffing, with a generous helping of genuine hatred.
