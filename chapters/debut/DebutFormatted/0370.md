# Chapter 370 - A Five-Star Surprise

Things were hectic backstage at the awards ceremony, which was about to go live.

"Unnie, you forgot this!"

"Sohyeon's here!"

This year too, Marshmallow was crazy busy. It was common for members to arrive late and hastily prepare due to personal schedules.

One of the staff members preparing for the performance nodded to herself.

*The girls are all really pretty.*

Although they looked tired, as expected from an idol group that had already reached the top, Marshmallow had the distinctive look of a group whose appearance and health were carefully maintained.

*Maybe they picked pretty girls in the first place.*

One of the staff member's reasons for thinking this way was right in front of her.

"Do it and do it properly."

"Yes!"

This was because the trainee dancers who came with them were also gorgeous.

*I heard LeTi was betting everything on this new boy group.*

It wasn't wise to believe every bit of entertainment industry gossip that went around, but about half of it usually turned out to be true.

There appeared to be about seven of them, and they were all handsome.

*Hmm.*

Taking advantage of a moment when her hands were free, the staff member took a quick look at their faces. She might see them on a music program sometime next year.

Being a little nosy and speculating like this was fun!

Then her eyes landed on a boy who looked particularly calm.

*...! He's a real LeTi trainee.*

A boy in black slacks and a button-up shirt with a slightly gloomy air about him. His neat, refined good looks had a calm quality to them.

And there was something delicate and light about him that made him seem a little cold.

*Oh.*

The staff member was a little stunned. Suddenly, the trainee turned toward her as if he had something to say.

"...!"

And then their eyes met.

*Aigoo.*

The moment she hurriedly tried to look away as if she hadn't been staring...

*Oh, he's smiling.*

The trainee gently lowered his head and then lifted it.

His smile was so natural that he seemed to have done this at least a hundred times before.

There was nothing forced or fake about it that would make an onlooker uncomfortable.

The staff member quickly bowed in return but was secretly impressed.

*Wow... It looks like he's had a lot of idol training.*

That wasn't necessarily the case.

Ryu Gunwoo, who had once been a member of TeSTAR, a *daesang*-winning idol group well into their five-year career, and was now a LeTi trainee, was sighing in relief.

*I almost asked for my microphone.*

He was so familiar with this atmosphere that he'd relaxed and felt at home without realizing it.

In addition, the thin glasses on his face felt a little out of place, which bothered him more.

*I put on contact lenses, but there are fake glasses on top...*

He couldn't see what selling point they were going for. He'd gain nothing by standing out more than the actual idols as a trainee dancer.

*Wasn't that sort of thing criticized even back then?*

The criticism was likely to have been even harsher at this point in time, certainly not less. However, the moment he saw Marshmallow's outfits, that thought went out the window.

*...This much?*

Layers of chiffon and sparkling artificial crystals greeted him. It looked like they were dressing up for the red carpet at a film festival.

It was to the point where he wondered if they could dance wearing those elaborate outfits, but anyway, Ryu Gunwoo looked down at his own costume and put two and two together.

*I guess it's roughly the concept of a butler or servant.*

It was cringeworthy, but this was a time when excessive concepts were rampant, so he took it in stride.

*It's enough as long as I make myself known.*

The part he played was just a short dance in the intro.

"Thank you for having us~"

"Thank you, Marshmallow sunbaenim."

After bowing to Sohyeon, with whom he'd danced only once during an in-house rehearsal, he walked out with the other trainees.

Nearby, he heard whispering in a lowered voice.

"Oh, she's so fucking pretty."

"Right. But her personality is a bit..."

*Wow.*

Ryu Gunwoo was suddenly forced to accept that Cheongryeo's careful selection of VTIC members had been necessary.

It wasn't for nothing that Sino and Judan, despite their relatively low rankings among the trainees, were brought in.

*It's full of unexploded bombs.*

Maybe the agency had lost its edge and was only collecting people based on looks and charm, but some of them seemed to lack even basic sense.

"Hmm?"

"It's nothing."

Cheongryeo, who was standing next to him, noticed Ryu Gunwoo's gaze and even explained further.

"Ah, that guy. I even tried putting him in the group... Three years after our debut, his ex-girlfriend made a post exposing all the details of his nasty personal life."

"......"

"Haha, people really don't change, right?"

Ryu Gunwoo wouldn't have been surprised to hear that the guy had been buried in cement and thrown into the Han River in a past life.

Anyway, he set that train of thought aside.

"I hope you aren't planning to overdo it just because it's your first stage..."

"Nope. I've got it."

The trainees went onstage with Marshmallow, each wrapped up in their own thoughts.

Since they'd only appeared as backup dancers, only idol fans who were very interested in LeTi paid them any attention. However, for Ryu Gunwoo himself, it was quite a new feeling.

*It's been a while since I've had to look up people's reactions online like this.*

Ryu Gunwoo lowered his smartphone.

It was fortunate that he'd controlled his expression and kept himself from standing out too much.

Still, becoming known all over again strangely reminded him of the day ⟨Idol Inc.⟩'s first performance was revealed.

"......"

Back then, seeing just a few supportive comments had strangely been enough to make him feel better.

A strange feeling of nostalgia came over him.

Ryu Chungwoo, who was making a PowerPoint on his laptop in the living room, glanced up casually and was quite surprised.

*Wow.*

He'd decided not to tease his hyung regardless of how well or badly he danced, but there turned out to be no need. There was nothing to make fun of.

Ryu Gunwoo, who was occasionally seen at the edges of the screen, looked surprisingly natural onstage.

Ryu Chungwoo gaped in amazement.

"Hyung, no wonder you wanted to become an idol. You're good at this."
