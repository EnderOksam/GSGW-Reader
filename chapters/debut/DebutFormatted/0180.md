"Most of these injuries came from fighting back, not being helpless when he hit me. If articles start coming out about this, it'll be more trouble than it's worth."

I guess not. Our well-built trust really shone here.

"Your condition is very serious. You had a high fever, and you have traces of being beaten and tied up..."

I could just make up a sketch and talk about it.

Perhaps my subconscious had slipped the person who had helped me when I was sick back into my mind.

*I guess there's nothing like being rescued to warm you up to someone.*

As he helped with my health, my mind returned a bit.

Let's flatter him a little bit.
