# Chapter 215

Bae Sejin calmly spoke.

"Next up... it's your turn."

"Ye- yes!"

Seon Ahyeon swallowed with a nervous face and cautiously called out.

"I- I'll pick Sejinie...!"

"Whaaat? Ahyeon, aren't you just picking me because I suspected you earlier? Personal feelings shouldn't come into play~"

"N-no, I wasn't!"

I've never seen these two guys argue like this before.

What was even funnier was that the people around were watching this confrontation with sweat on their palms.

*How did it come to this?*

As I looked down at the remaining card in my hand, I fell into deep contemplation.

When we first entered the new accommodation, the atmosphere couldn't have been more harmonious.

"It's so spacious! I love it!"

"There are still 3 bathrooms, right?"

"They added another small bathroom by remodeling and adding plumbing on the inside. So, there are four."

"Oh."

Since someone from a cleaning company will come anyway, the increase in the number of bathrooms was very welcome.

...Although I had some concerns about the dorms' location.

*In the end, we ended up in SV Village...*

It was the neighborhood where VTIC's dormitory was located.

There was no choice but to come here since the fact that we were going to go to E-Palace was leaked. We had to prioritize security, which narrowed down the options and led us here.

However, there was a difference in floor space, and even within the complex, the distance between units was quite far, so there shouldn't be any unnecessary encounters.

*Let's check the bedrooms.*

I pushed away my pointless unease and began to look around the accommodation.

"Wow! So many rooms! Do I get one all to myself??"

"There are four bedrooms. There's only one person who can use an individual room!"

"...!"

Bae Sejin, who suddenly got surprised, perhaps from listening to the two talking, muttered in a serious voice.

"...You could use one by yourself?"

"Yes! Alternatively, we could have the large rooms for three people, and one remaining person can enjoy the benefits of a private room..."

That won't do.

*It would make a bad picture.*

In truth, we could have rented two separate accommodations, and each had a private room. However, continuously using one shared accommodation had the purpose of preventing any further speculations about our relationship.

It hasn't even been our second anniversary yet, so it would be easy for people to assume we were not getting along because we were already using individual rooms.

*We'll have to wait at least another year.*

But to needlessly add a private room while there were rooms of three people?

I could already see some bastards out there creating a weird image about the guy using the private room.

I interjected at that point.

"There's no need to make it so cramped for no reason. As you said, three rooms for two and one single room would be better."

"Yes!"

Kim Raebin agreed with a slightly proud face, but I felt a bitter taste in my mouth after saying it.

*I want to have it to myself.*

It's a shame I had to kick away a chance to double my odds.

"Oh~ This room looks great!"

"Th-the beds are much bigger."

Anyway, with the dorm becoming more spacious and the rooms getting bigger, it seemed like we would have a bit more comfort in our daily lives.

"Ah, right."

And here came the decisive suggestion.

Ryu Chungwoo smirked and jokingly spoke.

"Should we give the largest room to the one who gets to use the room alone?"

"...!"

And surprisingly, this opinion was strongly supported and agreed upon by the majority.

"Oh! That sounds good~"

"Mm, I think the fans will find the rewards appealing when they watch roommate-related content!"

"Haha, is that so?"

Ryu Chungwoo himself seemed a little surprised, but it looked like everyone had already pictured themselves using their own room.

So, as a result, the room assignment game became ridiculously heated.

"Even when I rationally think about it, Cha Eugene's remarks over the past two turns have been contradictory, so the imposter is Cha Eugene!"

"No, I'm not! Think again!"

"I'm right!"

"Okay, okay. So, Raebin, you think Eugenie is lying, right?"

"That's right! I will 'verify' it with the proposition that Cha Eugene was unable to take a bribe in the previous turn!"

"I don't want to!"

As soon as Cha Eugene was accused, he began to make a fuss as if he was forced to drink poison.

*The problem is that guy isn't the only one.*

Everyone has been acting like that throughout the entire turn. I rested my chin, giving up.

In fact, this was a simple board game that combined Mafia and a card game.

There were predetermined actions that could be taken based on the profession cards that were secretly drawn at the beginning, and the player who collects the most money wins.

However, there is one distinguishing factor.

*No matter what action I take, I'm in the clear if I don't get caught.*

Even actions that are not allowed by your profession could be freely committed by bluffing your way through.

In other words, inevitably, there was a lot of lying and deception going on.

*...I chose it because I thought it would be funny.*

The problem was that in the first round, Keun Sejin won with a perfect bluff.

"Se-Sejinie... isn't a member of the National Assembly...?"

"...A flower shop owner, you said?"

"That's right~ Ah, 'How could you fall for that~' should be the right thing to say in this situation, right? Hahahaha!"

"Uuuugh!!"

Perhaps Keun Sejin quoting his line from the music video left quite an impact as from the next round, lying became the dominant strategy.

All of us, without exception, began to indiscriminately lie to collect money, and it eventually turned into a mess by the third round.

"Then, Eugene, let's 'verify'. Show me your card."

By the way, when someone's profession was 'verified' by another person, regardless of the truth, they couldn't collect money for a while.

Therefore, once the individual was targeted for 'verification', they all vehemently defended themselves...

Well, as I mentioned before, it was a mess.

"Hmm... Eugene's occupation was 'police'! Eugene is eliminated since you took bribes in the previous turn."

"Euuh..."

"I knew it!"

Now, one by one, everyone was getting caught during 'verification.'

*It's ruined.*

We have been filming for over two hours now, and looking at it, it seems like it would be condensed into about thirty minutes in the final edit.

The fans would be sad, asking for the full version, but if we actually showed the full version, the atmosphere would just be awkward... It was just an uninteresting brainless situation.

*Now that we've come to this, the guy who hasn't been lying should win and break free from this brainless situation.*

This wasn't good.

*For the next round... it would be better to target Seon Ahyeon.*

Wasn't he the one who was the worst at lying?

So, in the fourth round, I gave Seon Ahyeon a slight advantage to make progress.

"I-I'll buy this...!"

"Hmm, it seems like the property acquisition side gives more money."

"I-is that so? Then, I'll go with that...!"

And Seon Ahyeon steadily accumulated money, surprisingly not falling behind in verbal fights with Keun Sejin, and successfully managed to point him out during the 'verification' time.

"I r-really thought about this c-carefully... and I request Sejinie's verification...! It's about the building construction in the last turn!"

"Okay, Ahyeon-ah."

Ryu Chungwoo, who voluntarily took on the role of the game host as he didn't find the private room appealing, immediately checked Keun Sejin's card.

I folded my arms.

*Will this guy be eliminated?*

Of course, as I had that thought, the words that came out of Ryu Chungwoo's mouth were unexpected.

"...! Sejin... wasn't lying!"

"...!!"

"R-really?"

"Yeah. It's a job that allows building construction."

This bastard just pulled off a double bluff.

Thanks to this, Seon Ahyeon, who failed the 'verification', was penalized for three turns.

"Ahyeon is prohibited from acting in this turn as well."

"Ye-yes..."

"Sejin, you won't be able to earn money for another turn."

"Understood~"

This resulted in an unexpected winner.

"The winner of this round is... Moondae!"

It was me.

"Yes, thank you."

For the record, this was all pointless.

Because in the previous rounds, the difference in the total amount of money was due to the heavy targeting I received from that Keun Sejin guy.

*Fuck.*

Maybe I should have just had fun while not giving a damn and taken all the money.

Anyway, so the final winner was...

"The overall first-place winner, who gets to choose the room, is Lee Sejin!"

"Hahaha!"

In the end, that bastard won.

This might be entertaining for the viewers to watch... but somehow it left me feeling irritated.

*Should I mess with him?*

I smiled and opened my mouth.

"Congratulations."

"Oh~ Moondae-moondae thank you!"

"It must be comfortable to use a room all by yourself."

"...??"

"This was about choosing the person to use the single room."

"That's right, I'm so jealous!"

"I hope you have a great solitary life in that spacious room."

At the prospect that using the single room was a foregone conclusion, Keun Sejin's expression momentarily twisted but quickly returned to normal.

"Haha, no! Um, it doesn't necessarily have to be like that~"

Oh, he's trying to avoid it.

He played an active role in this content to secure screen time, but it seems that he didn't really want to use the single room after all.

It must be because if he used it alone, there was a high chance of a slight disadvantage in terms of screen time in our own content.

*Is he going to naturally yield it and make it look like a voluntary gesture?*

In that case, well, there would be only one person to yield it to.

"Eyy, I'd like to use it too... but well, I want to give the private room to Chungwoo-hyung! Our leader hyung-nim has truly supported this team since our debut!"

"Oh, me?"

"That's right, hyung-nim~ Isn't this something that the eldest, the leader, should use?"

"Oooh!"

"It is certainly an excellent choice!"

That was how Ryu Chungwoo easily secured the private room without a drop of sweat on his hands.

*I'm envious.*

As I internally clicked my tongue, I heard a small voice beside me.

"...I'm the oldest..."

As he didn't intend to speak out loud, Bae Sejin hastily closed his mouth when our eyes met.

Come to think of it, Bae Sejin has the earliest birthday. I guess this is why impressions are important.

*...Of course, technically speaking, I'm the oldest.*

It was disappointing seeing the opportunity for a single room flying away.

Well, I didn't have a problem with a double room either. My roommate happened to be a quiet guy too.

"Then, Moondae and Ahyeon will share a room."

"L-let's get along well...!"

"Yeah. It's been a while."

It was because I ended up sharing a room with Seon Ahyeon. As soon as the room arrangement was completed with this lineup, Seon Ahyeon's face lit up.

*Could it be that instead of going for the private room, this guy was also trying so hard so he could avoid sharing a room with Cha Eugene?*

A compelling hypothesis passed through my mind, but it wasn't important, so let's move on.

For the record, this Keun Sejin guy managed to properly cover up his karma of being blinded by screen time and stirring the game's dynamics.

The issue is that an innocent guy got caught up in it too.

"Oh, then... Both Sejin-hyungnims will be in the same room!"

"......"

"......"

It was because Cha Eugene and Kim Raebin both picked the same room while yelling at each other to get out.

As a result, Keun Sejin, who had relinquished his right to choose by giving the choice to Ryu Chungwoo... ended up automatically joining Bae Sejin's room.

"Hyung, please take care of me!"

"Y-yeah."

The two exchanged slightly delayed friendly greetings. They better be thankful this wasn't a live broadcast.

*They probably won't fight.*

After that big fight, they never interfered in each other's business except for necessary conversations.

And even though they were now in the same room, it was the size of the previous dorm's living room. It should be fine as long as they don't clash.

*Well, if there's any trouble, we can change it.*

As for the public explanation... it would suffice to jokingly say that their bedtimes were so different that they both were suffering from it.

"Okay then, let's go unpack in our respective rooms and see you again with another roommate story!"

"See you again, Loviewers!"

*The quality is definitely better.*

After undergoing extensive remodeling, it was evident that the room had improved.

"M-Moondae-ya, let's have fun and take care of each other...!"

"Yeah, that'd be nice."

I was already looking forward to a peaceful bedtime without noise pollution.

"I want to go and play! Let's go a lot!"

"I'll just accept your thoughts."

Although this conversation seemed a bit ominous, I couldn't help but feel relieved to have escaped from being roommates with Cha Eugene.

The only sound I could hear was Seon Ahyeon organizing his French embroidery and his crocheted scrub pads.
