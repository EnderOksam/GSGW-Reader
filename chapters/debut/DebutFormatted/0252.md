# Chapter 252 - Meeting

***

In fact, there was still more time before I had to press 'Reveal the Truth' because the status abnormality countdown wasn't over yet.

However, the problem was that there was no time to review this leisurely after the Chuseok holiday.

*We're preparing for the end of the year shows right after our new album comes out.*

In order to show the group's ability to put out albums, I couldn't waste the opportunities at the end of the year. I wouldn't have time to deal with the aftermath of pressing 'Reveal the Truth'.

*I don't know what will happen... I can't pass up the time I have now.*

I made the most reasonable judgment on the premise that I could continue this activity.

...If I went back to my original body, I might regret not waiting a few more months, but first... Let's not think about that.

I'd decided to prioritize the future in which I could continue working as an idol.

I exhaled.

*Here goes.*

"You're twenty years old?"

"...Yes, yes."

I was sitting in a familiar restaurant.

This soup-and-rice restaurant had become my regular spot after I started studying for the civil service exam. The food was at a good price and it was convenient to come and eat alone.

But right now, I wasn't alone.

Right now, across from me, a glum-looking guy was sitting and eating soup.

In front of the motel next door, he was lying face down without a coat, looking deeply distressed, so I was going to report him, but I came to feed him.

*Uselessly nosy.*

I had enough on my plate just taking care of my own life, but it was too late to turn back now.

I sighed. The man sitting in front of me winced, and he spoke in a tiny voice.

"I... I have no money."

"......"

Did he think I'd ask for money from someone who looked like that?

"I brought you here because I was going to eat anyway, so finish your food."

"...Thank you."

He was acting like a sinner for a meal that cost less than 10,000 won. Was it because I didn't look like someone who was going to pay?

I briefly checked myself in the mirror next to the cash register.

I was a typical civil service exam student wearing a thin jacket, a hat, and glasses. Still, I didn't look that down on my luck.

I certainly looked better off than the guy in front of me.

*Hmm.*

I looked over at the guy who was picking up diced radish kimchi.

Since he was 20 years old, it wasn't as if he had run away from home... He was probably in a similar situation to mine, except he hadn't made it into college.

"Do you have someone to contact?"

"......"

"What about a part-time job?"

"...I was fired."

I could roughly guess what had happened. I scooped up a spoonful of soup and drank it.

And I spoke.

"Go to the community service center first."

"What...?"

"The Administrative Welfare Center. From there, check whether you qualify for basic livelihood assistance. That way you can get money every month. Even if you don't qualify, there's probably some other assistance you can receive, so ask about everything you can."

I went on giving him advice as I ate. The man on the other side seemed perplexed and did not object.

*He must feel uncomfortable.*

It was understandable at that age. I quickly drank the remaining broth and stood up.

"Then, I'll take my leave now. You should eat and go."

I'd said my piece and began to leave. It was up to him whether he'd take my advice.

"W-wait a minute!"

However, while putting on my padded jacket, a panicked call was heard.

And a trembling voice spoke.

"Excuse me... Thank you, and I'm sorry."

"......"

I paused for a moment, then blurted out the words involuntarily.

"Have you ever worked part-time at a barbecue restaurant?"

"What? I've never done it..."

Well, it was hard for minors to work part-time at a barbecue restaurant. They usually didn't hire minors unless they were related to the owner.

I crossed the first option off my list and moved on to the next.

"I'll introduce you to a part-time job at a convenience store, so contact them. Say that their former part-timer told you about it. I'll give you my number."

Although the barbecue restaurant owner was the biggest soft touch for a sad story, the convenience store owner was also a decent person who treated people fairly.

But the other side was more bewildered.

"I-I don't have a phone..."

"......"

Holding back a sigh, I searched my smartphone and wrote down my number and the number of the convenience store owner on a piece of tissue paper.

"You should ask for an advance and get a phone that comes free with a plan. You can't work without it."

"......"

"Take it."

The guy received the tissue paper with a blank face.

And he muttered without raising his head.

"Thank you... Thank you so much."

"......"

He was probably crying.

I just turned my head. And that was the moment I was about to leave.

"Your name..."

Yes, since I had done all this already, it would be right to say my whole name.

"...Ryu Gunwoo."

Yeah. Well done, Ryu Gunwoo.

Looking at how I was wasting my time and thoughts by projecting my situation onto someone I had never seen before.

"What is your name?"

The young guy on the other side raised his head and answered with more energy than before.

"I am Park Moondae."

It was an odd name.

I nodded and finally left the restaurant. To escape from this odd situation.

Even after that, I had occasional contact with this 'Park Moondae'.

★:
-I don't know if you remember me, but this is Park Moondae, who got a meal from you. Thank you for introducing me to the convenience store.

I got a text message saying that he'd received his first month's pay from the convenience store job and wanted to treat me to a meal.

But we hadn't met much. There was no reason to meet, and I already had enough to do taking care of my own life.

This guy said he would pay me back, but I didn't have any expectations. I didn't do anything for him other than buy a meal for him.

It was okay to just meet up and have a meal once in a while. I did nothing but study without seeing anyone, and I felt like my social skills were deteriorating, so this level was fine.

But apart from that, things weren't going well for me.

"Are they crazy?"

I never thought I would fail the interview, which was supposed to be much easier than the exam itself. Objectively speaking, I even thought I'd done well.

*So much for a blind interview. Bastards.*

I could only guess that my family background had counted against me.

*I'd even put the best possible spin on my family history.*

It had even worked in an interview with a professor back in college.

Normally, it would've been a plus, but it seemed that I'd had bad luck with my interviewers this year.

"...Hoo."

I lay down on the mattress. It felt like my brain was broken.

*Shall I have a drink?*

Other than that, there was nothing special to do.

After three years of studying for the civil service exam, after failing that damn interview, I drank alone...

The next morning...

I gave up.

"Let's give up, fuck."

The reason was simple. All the money I earned was gone.

This was as far as I'd planned to go from the start. One attempt in the second year and another in the third.

I might scrape by if I stepped up my work selling idol photos and videos to rebuild my savings... But at my age, it was safer to get a job.

*My goal in the first place was stable employment. I can't let the means become the end.*

I wanted to have a job where I wouldn't starve to death in the worst case and would give me a salary no matter what terrible accident happened.

"......"

I also thought about taking a lower-level civil service exam for a while, but I put it on hold because it also cost new money.

*Surely I can find a job at 29. I'm not too old yet.*

That year, I got a job at a suitable mid-sized company.

And I thought I was adapting quite well... I don't know.

"Finish it by tomorrow."

"Yes."

Maybe I'd used up all my mental strength after only 29 years of living, or maybe I just wasn't suited to office life.

I often thought about it on the way home from working overtime while on the bus.

"......"

Life just didn't feel worth living.

Nothing felt good.

Life itself was exhausting.

Nothing particularly bad, but nothing good either.

It was not too difficult to survive in the company itself, but internal politics were also boring. Promotion was also not very exciting.

*I don't think this is a typical case.*

What was this helplessness that seemed to have accumulated throughout this life?

I thought about consulting a psychiatrist for a while but I decided against it. I didn't want to risk getting fired.

I thought about changing jobs, but I wasn't very motivated. First of all, this company didn't give me time to prepare for a job change.

"......"

No matter how much I thought about it, no answer came.

Besides having a drink once in a while, was there anything else I could do that interested me?

There was something that flashed through my mind.

Pictures?

*That's a pipe dream.*

It was a very expensive hobby. I had neither the time nor the money for that.

However, making money by selling idol photos and videos wasn't a sustainable job, and it couldn't replace my current job. There were numerous cases where the investment did not earn as much.

"Hmm."

I ordered a rope and painkillers online to be sent to my studio apartment. It was interesting for a while.

Still, I thought about it for a few weeks. It was not an easy decision to make.

However, even after going through that process, everything came to a head on the night of my last weekend.

Immediately after the implementation, I had a thought of kicking the chair and brushing it short.

*Was this depression?*

However, that thought did not last long.

I ran out of breath...

***

"Heok."

I got up as if jumping out of bed. I pressed a hand over my heart, which was pounding rapidly.

*Breathe...*

It was fine.

"Hoo."

I leaned against the headboard. My pajamas were soaked with sweat.

What I just experienced... No, is this what I had already been through?

Until now, what I'd seen had been other people's memories, but this...

*That's me?*

It felt as though I was 'recalling' my own memory.

But the problem was, I had absolutely no recollection of doing anything like that!

My last memory was of failing the civil service exam, getting drunk, and falling asleep. I had no memory of the subsequent job or the frantic suicide attempt.

Besides, first of all...

*I've never met 'Park Moondae'.*

Even the first scene didn't match my memory.

If I'd done something so foolish that gained me nothing, I wouldn't be able to forget it.

The first time I'd ever seen Park Moondae was after entering his body.

Also, the timing was odd.

"Park Moondae wasn't dead."

Obviously, in the first 'Reveal the Truth' vision, Park Moondae made an extreme attempt at the motel with sleeping pills...

At that moment, a strange realization came to my mind like a flash.

An attempt was just an attempt.

*Could Park Moondae have survived?*

Let's think about it.

Park Moondae used non-prescription sleeping pills...

So, he just bought a sleep-inducer at the pharmacy.

*Can a person die from over-the-counter medicine?*

I was certain when I started to doubt it. When I searched for it on my smartphone, I was right that no matter how much someone consumed, at most, they couldn't die from side effects.

Then, if I combined the season and date seen in this 'Reveal the Truth' vision...

*I met Park Moondae right after his suicide attempt.*

The guy who was lying lifeless in front of the motel. And when I woke up in his body, he'd been lying blankly on a motel bed.

It was... It made sense.

"......"

And if this guess was correct, it would also mean that I entered the body of Park Moondae, who was not dead.

"Then, where did he go?"

But there was no answer. There had never been one, and there was no way there would be one now.

I called the status window and asked.

*Are you Park Moondae?*

However, there was no response on the status window either. Instead, only strange words appeared.

I didn't know what kind of fucking bullshit that was, but anyway, there was no sign of letting me know the whereabouts of Park Moondae.

No, it was not only the whereabouts of Park Moondae that were important. What was the meaning of the madness I had just witnessed?

Did I really do that?

*I've never thought of myself as such a crazy bastard.*

If I did, why the hell did that memory disappear? Was 29 the upper age limit for being an idol?

"This is driving me crazy."

I couldn't help but laugh.

Unlike Park Moondae, 'Ryu Gunwoo' didn't exist here in the first place, so there was nothing I could investigate.

As soon as I first entered Park Moondae's body, I looked for my cell phone number, social media, and even my university, but I didn't even have an account...

*...Wait a moment.*

The idol photos and videos I'd taken were still there.

I ran a hand through my hair.

Then... What if the proposition was changed to 'whether Ryu Gunwoo existed here in the past'?

How to find out any traces of it... There was a way.

*– Yes, Moondae. If it's okay... During this Chuseok holiday, how about spending it at one of the members' homes rather than the dorm? Oh, my house is fine too.*

Ryu Chungwoo's house.

Whether it was a family trip album or a video, there was a possibility that traces of me and my parents would remain.

"......"

*Knock knock.*

Then, a knock was heard outside the door.

"Park Moondae? Are you okay??"

It was Bae Sejin. Did he hear me talking to myself?

"...Yes. I have a cramp, wait..."

"Uh, well, do you want me to get you a massager?"

"It's okay. It's gone. Wait a minute."

I got up and changed my clothes.

When I opened the door, Bae Sejin, who was sitting at the kitchen table, brightened, then froze.

"You, your clothes..."

"Yes."

Dressed in street clothes, I nodded.

"I'll be out for a while. Because I have someone to meet."

I needed to know the truth.

It was a particularly peaceful first day of the holiday.

"Kkami, come here."

"*Woof!*"

The black toy poodle jumped onto the sofa and then onto Ryu Chungwoo's lap. The smell of savory pancakes and the sound of holiday specials on the TV lifted the mood.

Since it was not his crowded grandfather's house, the atmosphere was relatively peaceful.

"Oppa, I'm going!!"

"Okay, have a good trip."

"Aish, you should've come home a little earlier!"

"Haha, okay."

His younger sister left the house grumbling that she hadn't been able to get a fancy meal out of her older brother, who had come home after a long time. Ryu Chungwoo smiled and saw off his sister.

It was a family of five, including a dog, but it was not empty. Warm words were exchanged.

"So, how are your dongsaengs?"

"They are still good kids. They're nice and..."

Ryu Chungwoo, who inadvertently thought of including Bae Sejin as his younger brother, hurriedly put Bae Sejin in a position of the same age.

Then he thought of a member who was quite mature even though he was a dongsaeng.

"Yes, since they are good kids, my son was going to invite them over for the holidays~"

"Hahaha."

Just then, his parents brought up the story.

The most mature dongsaeng. Ryu Chungwoo had wanted to invite Park Moondae to his house for the holidays.

Naturally, his parents hadn't objected to the idea, but they hadn't welcomed it eagerly, either.

Even for Ryu Chungwoo's close dongsaeng, it was not comfortable to have strangers at home during the holidays.

*Hmm, I think it was better that he went home with Sejin.*

Ryu Chungwoo reflected that Park Moondae was quick-witted so that he might have been rather uncomfortable.

*I hope he's doing well.*

Ryu Chungwoo posted a picture of his dog in TeSTAR's group chat and saw photos of all kinds of holiday foods come in response.

"Everyone is doing well."

It seemed like such a peaceful day had passed by.

But that evening. The time period just before going into the night...

*Ding Dong-*

Someone rang the doorbell. The person outside the door didn't leave, so it couldn't have been a delivery person.

"I will see who it is."

"Okay, son-"

Ryu Chungwoo got up from the sofa.

*Is it a stalker?*

Ryu Chungwoo had a rather negative premonition and immediately checked the intercom to see who was standing there.

"...!"

An unexpected person appeared on the intercom screen.

Ryu Chungwoo immediately opened the door.

"...Moondae?"

"Yes, hyung."

As he said, Park Moondae was standing in front of the gate.

Without any notice. However, not with calm eyes like usual, but with sharp eyes that gleamed with resolve.

"I came over, just like you said. Can I come in?"

Ryu Chungwoo somehow felt chills.
