# Chapter 359 - A Carrot Revolution, I

*Rattle.*

"...The scenery is nice."

"Th-there are cows over there...!" Ahyeon said.

An idyllic rural scene passed by outside the windows as all the members pressed themselves against them, commenting on the scenery.

Yes. We boarded a typical tourist bus and headed for the countryside.

Of course, there were cameras all over the bus. The ride itself would be featured in the show, so we had to make the most of it.

"Wow, there's a karaoke machine here too?" Keun Sejin said.

"Ah, it's been a while since I've seen that," Chungwoo said.

"I want to sing! Give me the microphone!" Eugene said.

"Okay! Shall we play a game to see who gets the highest score?"

Keun Sejin smiled and lifted the karaoke microphone to liven up the atmosphere. He seemed almost desperate to create some variety-show energy.

I understood him.

He probably couldn't stand this peaceful atmosphere any longer, with not a single usable clip in sight.

"Come on~ Our main vocalist has to sing just one song here!"

*You're working really hard today, huh?*

"Give it to me," I said.

"Oooh!"

I chose a popular song from about ten years ago and sang it with as much excitement as I could muster, trying to get a good score while wondering what we could possibly do about this situation.

I was just as desperate as Keun Sejin.

*There's a possibility that we'll arrive and find that we're supposed to get on a fishing boat...*

*Production Team, please just go back to your old ways.*

♪ *That blue sea~ Cool breeze~* ♫
p$

I sang.

"Oh, oh! Oh, oh!"

*Please, just keep messing with us like you've always done before!*

But when we finally arrived, we were greeted by a cozy, warm-looking rural farmhouse.

"......"

The picturesque country house had a blue roof, a veranda, and plenty of sunlight.

The members were excited.

"Wow!" Eugene said.

"It's a wonderful home with a very cozy feel!" Raebin said.

"......"

*No... Looks can be deceiving. Yes, it's what's inside that matters.*

But after entering...

"Here... The floor is warm," Bae Sejin said.

"It feels much more heartwarming because it looks so lived in," Raebin said.

"Th-that's right...!" Ahyeon said.

The neatly organized rooms had heated floors and an old-fashioned charm, with mother-of-pearl cabinets, white porcelain, colorful vintage candy containers, and uniquely shaped stones.

"Have you seen the refrigerator?" Chungwoo said.

"...Yes, it's pretty full," I replied.

The kitchen was stocked with various sauces, rice, and side dishes.

With sunlight shining through the window, even the air felt warm and refreshing. I swallowed.

*...It's even better inside.*

The place seemed like the perfect embodiment of the romance and longing of rural life—the ideal retirement home in the countryside, with everything one could possibly want.

*What on earth did the art director get up to?*

Why did they give the show a title like ⟨The House Next Door's Grandson⟩ and make the accommodation so fancy?

*How dare they not pull any tricks on the cast? This was treachery of the highest degree.*

"Wow~ It's really amazing! How did they do this?"

Keun Sejin read the atmosphere and quickly switched to reacting as if he were attending a housewarming party.

*He's certainly trying hard.*

In the meantime, the director also quietly added a word.

"Haha, everyone, these things belong to the owners of the house. I borrowed them from a resident, so I have to return them when the period is over. Please keep everything in pristine condition~"

"Yes!"

*Why do you look so proud of yourself?*

Wondering if there was something I'd missed, I opened my mouth just in case.

"Is there anything we have to pay off, or...?"

"Oh, of course not~ We paid the rent for everything! You won't have to pay a single won!"

*Yes, we're very grateful.*

*Things really are messed up, aren't they?*

After a while, we were asked to gather on the veranda, so we finished up and headed out.

"TeSTAR, or grandchildren as we'll call you on this show, please sit here~"

"Yes, yes~"

Then a short briefing on the content followed.

It wasn't much different from what we heard at the meeting. Living in the countryside, helping the elderly with their work and cooking meals for ourselves...

*No fun...*

I would have preferred some intense volunteer work, but instead, we were about to be treated like royalty. The show would be boring.

And the production team's explanation did not end there.

"And here's how you'll be compensated~"

Before I knew it, there was a goodie bag on the table on the veranda. Ryu Chungwoo picked it up and opened it.

"What? Wow~" Keun Sejin said.

"...That's interesting. Are these bottle caps?" Chungwoo said.

Inside, there were handfuls of glittering circular objects with engraved patterns.

"You can only use this currency here, and you can earn more by completing various jobs!"

The director pointed to the goodie bag.

"That's right~ These are what we call Carrot Coins!"

"Oh, oh!"

*The name has some rather dubious cryptocurrency connotations.*

However, the members' eyes gleamed at the toy-like coins made from pressed bottle caps.

"Why is it called Carrot?" Eugene asked.

"That's because each coin is worth one real carrot~"

"Hahaha!"

It was an utterly absurd concept, but we all reacted as if it were a great joke.

"I understand~ Carrot, carrot!" Keun Sejin said.

"That's interesting, isn't it?"

"We'll have to manage our money together," Chungwoo said.

And then I nodded.

*Yes.*

This production team was way too unhinged to let us simply enjoy a relaxing time on the farm without a catch, with everything we could possibly need.

*They must have something planned involving these coins.*

They would probably put us into debt that would snowball, or create a situation where we'd run out of coins very quickly.

*Then, for maximum impact...* we needed to start by spending as many coins as possible.

My brain was finally working again. I made my suggestion with enthusiasm.

"For now, shall we start by shopping for groceries for dinner?"

"Sounds good," Chungwoo said.

"I'll make a list of things to buy!"

First, I needed us to be shocked by the prices of everything.

As we entered the grocery store, I held up a coin I had received from Ryu Chungwoo and asked the production crew gravely.

"Are you lending us these?"

However, the production team responded firmly.

"No. Those are yours~ They're your starting funds!"

"...Yes."

They didn't seem to be lying.

*Hmm. I suppose it might really be true.*

Perhaps they would make the prices ridiculously high so we'd run out of coins, even though we'd started with free ones.

So, we started shopping.

"How much is this?"

Towels were a necessity. *Usually, items like these would be marked up ridiculously—*

"Ah, you must be the young people who... Anyway, these are worth two Carrot Coins! That's all!"

"Thank you. We'll take some."

"...?"

*Wait a minute.*

"Please buy me that!" Eugene said.

"Eugene, do you want to eat *dalgona*? Okay. Auntie, how much is this?" Keun Sejin asked.

"Wow, you're so handsome. Hm? Very handsome~"

"Aigoo, thank you! Auntie is so beautiful too~" Keun Sejin said.

"Really? Then, just one Carrot Coin is enough for 7 of these!"

"...Wow~ Awesome! Thank you!!"

"...?"

*Everything was ridiculously cheap.*

*They were basically giving everything away for free.*

*What was this?*

I almost dropped the *dalgona* Keun Sejin handed me, then immediately turned back to the production crew to ask them about it.

"...It's very cheap."

"All the residents are kind-hearted."

*You mean the residents are deciding their own prices.*

*Of course they'd be generous when the cameras were rolling, since this wasn't even real money!*

*Had they completely lost their minds?*

*What were they thinking, coming up with such a meaningless system?*

"Then, shall we go in and cook quickly?" Keun Sejin said.

"Moo-Moondae. Would you like me to help this time...? I can at least help grill the meat..." Ahyeon said.

"...Sounds good," I said.

*The prices were practically free, the work was easy, the house was nice, and there were no problems to solve.*

I made a comprehensive judgment.

*This show was a dud.*

*Who the hell would want to watch already-successful idols simply relax on a farm?*

No, of course, fans might want to see it... But the least the production team could do was set up some kind of hook that would keep things interesting.

*There were no ups and downs.*

This bland structure couldn't evoke any strong emotions, laughter, or excitement.

In short, it was about as boring as a TV show could get.

It would be better to go somewhere amazing and flex our wealth or do something exciting, because the show was going nowhere like this.

*This is driving me crazy.*

I stuffed the *dalgona* into my mouth and suppressed the urge to groan.

Of course, I had already tried several times to change the direction of the program before we left.

Anything from a difficult farming boot camp to a horror special would have been fine. If only we could make it even a little more exciting to watch.

But surprisingly, it didn't work.

– *Oh, I don't think... we can do that.*

– *Yes, I don't think that will work.*

*They cut everything off.*

The production team smoothly rejected every attempt, saying they had already discussed everything with the agency.

*They're certainly good at talking their way out of things. Why were they so determined to stick with this boring healing content when they'd been so good at pulling tricks before?*

*Why were they doing this to us when their previous show had been a quiz show about pulling pranks on actors?*

I smacked my lips bitterly.

*Are we too popular?*

Maybe they were afraid that, since TeSTAR was so successful, making us do anything unpleasant would anger the fans.

I could understand if that was the case, which just left me with a bitter taste in my mouth. *Who knew dalgona could taste bitter?*

"Wow~ Look at this, Moondae! There's eel here too!" Keun Sejin said.

"Wait."

I decided to find myself some work to do for now.

In this situation, the least I could do was build my image as a peaceful workaholic.

*If there's nothing to do, I just have to make something to do.*

Thanks to this, I incorporated all sorts of unnecessary steps into making sweet-and-sour pork for dinner, and it turned out amazingly well.

It'd be nice if this could at least count as a *mukbang*.

*Please.*

***

My futile attempts to generate interesting content continued, but no matter how much I thought about it, nothing seemed to work.

It was amazingly peaceful.

*This show is a dud. I'm tired.*

Still, we did get something a little fun after dinner on the first day.

The guys who'd visited a nearby house brought something back with them.

"Voila!"

"Wow..."

A smiling Ryu Chungwoo placed a dog with a black nose and white fur on the veranda.

The dog scampered about energetically at my feet.

"Woof!"

"......"

I almost reached out my hand without thinking, but then took it back.

It looked like a ball of hair small enough to be picked up and lifted.

"We met the camera director's grandmother, and she said this was one of the pups her dog gave birth to."

"He kept following us, and she said it was okay to bring him back!" Eugene said.

"I-its name is Mungae."

"Woof!"

The dog squirmed its way between me and Bae Sejin. Bae Sejin's face turned red.

"Do... Do you mind if I hold it?"

"Here, you can lift him by holding him on his sides, like this."

I looked at the dog's rather stupid-looking face. Its tail was wagging wildly.

*This... It might be okay.*

*Because people like dogs.*

Moreover, since it didn't have an easily identifiable breed, there was no need for unnecessary controversy.

*Hmm. Good.*

Feeling glad to have the puppy here, I nodded internally.

"Since this puppy came to TeSTAR, I think it should get a stage name~ How about Moon Puppy?" Keun Sejin said.

"Hey."

And, deliberately overreacting to Keun Sejin's bullshit, I raced across the yard after him, with the dog hot on our heels.

"Hahaha!"

I wondered if the threshold for laughter had been set too low, but the members and staff laughed anyway.

*...Maybe it wasn't that bad.* From that day on, the dog slept with us in the middle of the heated room.

After that, small tasks continued.

We dug potatoes, watered crops, fed ducks, and gave massages to villagers.

And what we were doing right now was delivering groceries.

"Ugh!"

"We're almost done."

We were making deliveries all over, including farms that were a little farther away.

Walking through the countryside with a puppy loping around underfoot was the perfect mindless activity.

And on the way back...

"Hm, shall we take a rest over there?" Chungwoo said.

"Good idea!" Raebin said.

There were no restrictions, so we could use our time however we wanted.

So, just before sunset, we parked the bicycle cart and sat down in the pavilion.

"I want to lie down~" Keun Sejin said.

"Me too!" Eugene said.

We could simply lie around if we wanted to.

*Of all the things...*

I lay down with the others, wondering how they would even use footage of all of us simply lying there.

At that time, Seon Ahyeon, who was lying next to me, muttered briefly.

"Th-this isn't so bad."

"......"

I listened.

"Without having any worries, just like right now... It's been a while since we had time like this."

"...You're right."

We'd been running hard without stopping for the past few years. In this line of work, if you slowed down, you'd fall behind.

*We even plan our breaks so that we can rest efficiently.*

*It had been a long time since we'd simply relaxed without having a goal in mind.*

Time passed by aimlessly.

"It's one per person. Don't take any more," Bae Sejin said.

"Boo..." Eugene said.

Bae Sejin had just returned from the supermarket with a bag of popsicles.

They tasted fruity.

*Sururu-sururuk.*

The grass swayed in the wind.

"It smells good."

"Yes."

It was quiet.

I lay down in the pavilion and realized that I was quite relaxed.

"......"

*Maybe this wasn't so bad after all.*

*Is this why healing entertainment shows keep getting made?* Looking back, the steady supply probably meant they were secretly quite popular.

*Maybe people liked the feeling of getting away from unnecessary problems.*

*It's quite a far cry from our intense struggle to get the best record possible.* I tilted my head slightly and had a thought.

*Maybe I'll just let things be this time.*

Even if I just enjoyed the entertainment show without thinking about anything else, it didn't seem like there would be much of a problem in completing the mission.

Just this time, maybe I could trust the production team's editing skills and let them handle the content.

*It could be that since I took my first step into entertainment with* Idol Inc., *I've only ever sought out overly sensational content.*

Maybe I'd been missing out.

I slowly enjoyed the sweetness of the ice cream and placed my head on the floor.

It was nice to simply relax together, all crammed onto the floor of the small pavilion.

Two peaceful days passed.

Around the time when the members and I were finally getting used to this uneventful life...

"Hello, everyone!"

"Hello!"

The production team, who dutifully appeared every morning, called us over again.

*I guess they'll give us some small tasks to do again, as usual.*

However, they were doing something different today before the daily briefing.

"Everyone, shall we play a mini-game for a moment?"

"A mini-game?"

"Yes. There was a small incident this morning, so let's play a mini-game to decide what happened!"

"Oooh."

The mini-game was a simple game played against the production team.

We'd already played a few. When we won, we'd received snacks, fireworks, and other rewards.

So everyone naturally agreed and participated.

"Wow, it seems like our Mungae is always with Moondae," Keun Sejin said.

"Haha, I guess he feels a connection to Moon Puppy," Chungwoo said.

"...Chungwoo-hyung."

*Not you too, Ryu Chungwoo.*

I played without much tension, more focused on petting the puppy, and lost.

"Ah."

"Well, it happens," Chungwoo said.

"It's okay! Let's do more work!" Eugene said.

No one was very upset. Everyone was still smiling and encouraging each other.

"Now then, let's find out what penalty losing will incur!"

"Yes~"

Until now, the penalty notes had words like '1 volunteer activity today' and 'buying corn'.

And all that was left were two notes.

"Raebin, why don't you pick one?" Chungwoo said.

"Alright!"

Then his expression froze.
