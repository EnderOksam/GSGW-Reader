The usual response was, 'But they earn so much money at such a young age,' and the outrage would die down.

But if the conclusion became 'This huge corporation is shit,' that was another story.

With a common enemy and a justifiable cause, people had much more reason to rally together.

People relieved the stress of July's late-arriving heatwaves, caused by the delayed rainy season, by lambasting T1.

The parent company T1 was far better known than the relatively new T1 Stars, so it took the worst of the backlash.

A national petition had even appeared.

*Even though they aren't famous for good employee welfare.*

Honestly, they brought it on themselves.

I was sorry for the people in charge, but it turned out to be really fun.

As Bae Sejin had expected, the company seemed to suspect an internal whistle-blower or deliberate sabotage by a competitor.

The TeSTAR incident had begun to look like the start of a calculated smear campaign.

*...That's good.*

*Keep digging. You're not going to find anything.*

The more time they wasted, the less the company would accomplish and the more influence TeSTAR would gain.

I smiled and switched the screen of my smartphone.

This time, the fan community.

Most related posts and articles had this tone, yet the overall mood wasn't especially gloomy.

People seemed overjoyed that I'd finally woken up.

Since public opinion favored TeSTAR rather than condemning us, the fans weren't too distressed.

Judging by all the 'I knew it' reactions, many fans had suspected there was more to the story.

*...I'm glad.*

I wiped my phone screen with my shirt and locked it.

I wanted to post something soon, but decided to discuss it with the others first, just in case.

*Even a quick post letting people know I'm back would be fine.*

...And, I had to make a very important suggestion.

*We need to go on that tour, no matter what.*

This had been planned in its own way.

I crossed my arms and decided to calmly wait for them to come.

And at 2 PM...

Bae Sejin, who burst into the hospital room, was red with excitement.

"It went well, don't you think?"

"Yes. The reception online has been pretty good."

"...!"

It was the moment when I was about to get to the point, leaving the man with both hands clasped and shuddering at the taste of success.

When the National Human Rights Commission releases its findings, let's sue the agency!

...!!

If they find the evidence, we can win! I-I'll pay for the lawyer and everything!

Bae Sejin strongly insisted on stepping on the accelerator once more.

*No.*

Was this guy too drunk on success?

He'd gotten drunk on success. I understood, but a lawsuit was a drastic last resort in this industry.

Before I could even open my mouth, Lee Sejin stepped in.

“I guess you haven't discussed this part of the plan with us yet.”

He seemed to have finally grasped what Bae Sejin was suggesting.

"Bae Sejin, this is part of your plan for us to move to another agency, right?"

"Yes..."

"But then we wouldn't be allowed on broadcast stations or shows T1 invests in. Isn't that a big risk?"

"...There are many other broadcasts too. And WeTube or... There are many other ways to be active."

Bae Sejin swallowed his saliva.

"If we stay, we've got three more years with them. Who knows what they'll do to try to make us renew our contracts after that?"

"......"

"So what I'm doing now... I, I think it's right to do it now."

Lee Sejin's eyebrows twitched, but he settled on a cheerful voice.

"It would be nice if the lawsuit went well~ But if we can't be active for more than a year now, our careers will die."

"...!"

"And... Even if we did win, I don't think any agency would dare to sign with us."

Lee Sejin finished his speech softly.

We'd need to be so popular that another agency would risk defying T1 just to sign us, and we're not there yet~

"......"

Why not use this chance to strengthen our dedicated team and negotiate better terms with the agency? What do you all think?

Bae Sejin seemed to have a lot to say, but after recalling various expected answers, his complexion worsened as if he had been hit in advance.

*It turned out like this.*

I shook my head and spoke.

"First... Then let's have a show of hands. Who wants to start a lawsuit right now?"

Bae Sejin slightly raised his hand.

Cha Eugene's hand shot up.

"......"

"We will win!"

*Americans. Why did he sound so excited about hiring a lawyer?*

"Hey, Cha Eugene. You want to take a year off?"

"Even if we take a year off, we will do well. It's okay! We can do it again!"

It was a very positive, confident, yet unrealistic answer. Thanks.

"Then, who wants to stay at the agency and improve the environment?"

Lee Sejin raised his hand, as expected, and to my surprise, Kim Raebin followed.

There are a lot of good people at the agency, too... I'd like us to improve things together.

"......"

*That was optimistic in the opposite direction. Thanks to you, too.*

Then, the only ones left were Ryu Chungwoo and Seon Ahyeon.

"What about you, Ahyeon?"

"I, I... E-either way, as long as everyone is comfortable and safe, I'm fine with anything...!"

"What do you think, Moondae?"

Me? Simple.

"...Wouldn't it be difficult to file a lawsuit right now?"

Even if it wasn't about my tour problem, the lawsuit was the opposite.

*It's gotten too big.*

Once this became a question of workers' rights rather than just an idol-group dispute, the corporation wouldn't want to risk losing in court.

A defeat against TeSTAR would create a legal precedent.

*If the labor union filed a lawsuit after that, they'd go crazy.*

It would be better to use the company's present wariness to negotiate concessions without completely ruining our relationship.

*We needed the company to look as though it regretted what it had done to TeSTAR.*

Even if they were not actually sorry, they could make the most of the moment if they pretended to be like that.

But because of Bae Sejin's contribution, it was hard to say this openly... I turned it around.

"We don't know how public opinion will change tomorrow. Why don't we observe more and collect data so that we can file a lawsuit if the circumstances are right?"

"Yes."

Why don't we watch how things develop, then carry out the schedules we already have?

There are cancellation penalties, and showing everyone that the group is still active could lift people's spirits.

What tour? Moondaemoondae, you'd better stay in bed.

Hyung, you need to live a long life!

These crazy guys.
