# Chapter 369 - The Perfect Illusion

After doing something he called a 'reboot', Keundal started talking eagerly again.

$p
**[Hyung, I think I'm feeling better!]**
p$

Certainly, the pop-ups he displayed were no longer unstable and had calmed down.

But my head was a complete mess. It felt like it was about to explode.

*What's a new game system doing popping up here?*

I was silent for a moment and then slowly opened my mouth.

"So this means... this game status window was created by the system itself, not you?"

$p
**[Yes... I just modified it a bit.]**
p$

It seemed that during the mixing process, the system could interfere with the status window, just as Keundal could interfere with the system.

*Or is this a completely new way of doing things?*

Either way, it was not a good thing. ... However, there was something puzzling.

I rested my chin in my hand.

"Why does this remind me of ⟨127 Section⟩?"

Yes. I knew I'd seen this whole 'collect companions' thing somewhere, and everything from the interface to the elements on the windows strangely resembled the game we'd collaborated with.

$p
**[That's because of me! I wanted the game format to at least be familiar to you... So I tried mimicking it.]**
p$

"Really? ... Thanks."

If so, it meant the system had intended this structure and content from the start.

*I don't know what to think about that.*

With a frown, I flipped through the holograms one by one and looked at the contents.

First of all, the status window itself looked much like it had before.

★-
[Player: Park Moondae (Ryu Gunwoo)]

Vocal: A

Dance: B

Visual: A

Talent: A-

Traits: Infinite potential

Points until next awakening opportunity / 1000 EXP
-★

Even though my traits were all gone and all my stats had dropped, they were still impressive considering my age.

*An all-rounder who's good at pretty much everything.*

Were these my original stats, without the bonuses I'd received from the system so far? The situation was too much of a mess to feel proud of it, so I moved on.

The problem started after that.

★-
\
#><[Collected companions: Shin Jaehyeon]><#

Points until next collection opportunity / 1000 EXP
-★

*Why was this bastard listed here?*

*Before that, what did it mean to collect companions? What was awakening?*

I asked Keundal a few more questions, but he seemed just as confused as I was.

*I guess it can't be helped.*

It was impossible to even guess what was happening to him in reality right now. I couldn't expect any more help from this guy.

*I'll have to figure the rest out myself.*

I lay down on the strangely comfortable bed and lost myself in thought.

First of all, I'd put together all the clues I had so far.

*...I need to think in terms of* ⟨127 Section⟩.

There, leveling up was called awakening.

★-
\
**Points until next awakening opportunity / 1000 EXP**
-★

The line displayed beneath my status window was probably referring to that.

I'd gain random stats or skills each time I awakened. In the game, the outcome was random, influenced by the choices I'd made so far.

If Keundal had properly implemented that mechanism, it would be similar here. And here, the experience points used for awakening... Reputation EXP, or reputation points.

*It's obvious.*

If I became famous, won awards, and got good results after a release, I'd earn those points. It was no different from modifying the system I'd had as Park Moondae to make it easier to understand.

In short, I could see what the system wanted from me.

*It's telling me to debut.*

Anyone could see that what this system wanted was my debut in this world.

This meant I should debut, build up my reputation, and achieve success more easily than last time.

*It's like telling me to just settle down and live here.*

It was an unpleasantly blatant suggestion.

*Do you think I've lost my mind?*

*I don't know what you're trying to achieve here. Why would I decide to just live here like this?* *And...*

*...I have so much to lose in the real world.*

*I couldn't throw it all away. Never.*

I closed my eyes.

I was actually glad my head was full of plans for what to do starting tomorrow. I wouldn't have to waste energy on useless worries and thoughts.

***

The next morning...

I ate breakfast with a younger Ryu Chungwoo.

"......"

No... This was something we usually did at our dorm, but eating alone with him like this made it really feel like home.

Ryu Chungwoo looked relaxed, even a little bored, as if he was used to the silence.

*Keundal said he was resting.*

It seemed like he was sleeping, in a sense, because he'd used too much power. Thanks to this, things were even quieter.

"I'm going now."

"Have a nice day, hyung. I'll see you in the evening."

Instead of persistently questioning him for information, I left the house. This was more efficient.

I was heading to the service center to get my smartphone unlocked.

*There must be information in it.*

Data wouldn't lie.

And the service center unlocked my phone in just 15 minutes. *Neat.*

*This was the correct answer.*

If this guy were actually me, I would have enabled the security option that erased data after too many consecutive failed attempts at unlocking it, so I'd been careful not to try too many times.

I opened my smartphone, pleased with myself.

"Hmm."

First of all, there was nothing special in the KakaoTalk history. There were mainly group assignments and group chats with classmates.

*...Cheongryeo is the most recent contact?*

*What happened to this guy's social life?* It seemed I'd even withdrawn from the photography club.

★:
-Lee Yujin: Ryu Gunwoo-ssi?? Did you quit the photography club? Why haven't you been coming to meetings?

I found something I want to do.-

-Lee Yujin: ??? Okay. Congratulations, congratulations.
:★

Judging from my conversation with the guy who introduced me to the data-selling job, I guess what I wanted to do here was become an idol.

And maybe... Apparently, LeTi's talent scouts had approached me while I was filming a proxy fancam.

★:
-LeTi 16th Shin Jaehyeon: Hello, this is Shin Jaehyeon. I was at the Dream Concert with Mr. Lee. Do you remember me? Please take care of me in the future ^^
:★

This was the first message I'd received from Cheongryeo. This was probably when it all started.

*...It was only three months ago.*

So, it meant I'd been put into the debut group almost as soon as I was scouted, which was understandable given my stats.

*It's difficult to find a good vocalist.*

Didn't Cheongryeo once get into trouble because he'd lowered his strict standards in his search for a main vocalist?

*I roughly understand how that came about. Next...*

...Ryu Chungwoo.

I looked through the history of messages exchanged with him.

★:
-Ryu Chungwoo: Engineering building soft tofu stew?

Yep-
:★

*Ryu Gunwoo here was 21, not 20.*

*I guess so.*

There were traces of Ryu Gunwoo having been in college for a year, and the same was true of Ryu Chungwoo, who was the same age.

And... Just like in reality, Ryu Chungwoo here had also quit archery.

However, there were no aftereffects from his injury, and he'd simply chosen to go to college of his own free will.

*He'd still won the gold medal, though.*

★:
-Ryu Chungwoo: I thought it would be fun to take a more academic route.
:★

Ryu Chungwoo simply chose what he wanted to do among the open options.

*Then...*

*...Wasn't this world a happier one for him?*

I held my smartphone and stood still for a moment.

I was confused. Everything had turned out much better for everyone.

*No.*

*This is just a dream.*

Every time I opened the status window, the English sentence at the top said it was real, but I couldn't believe it.

*But it's better than being in a crappy situation.*

In my first daydream, every single person I'd met had been in some kind of crisis. I set those thoughts aside and looked at KakaoTalk again.

And while scrolling down.

I saw it.

★:
#><[Our Family]><#
:★

"Ah..."

...Family group chat room.

I couldn't delete my parents' contact information from my phone until I graduated from college.

I later deleted it after confirming that the numbers had been passed on to someone else.

And at this point... I was probably still doing pointless things like making chat rooms and sending messages I'd never get a reply to.

*Fuck.*

However, a thought whispered to me from somewhere in the back of my mind.

*If this is a world where everything turns out well, then maybe...*

"......"

I stared at the screen, then opened the chat. The last message just said 'Okay'.

And I saw it.

★:
-Mom: Don't skip meals and make sure you eat, okay?
:★

A message from just a month ago.

"...!"

I hastily clicked on the profile. 'Replies can be slow as I'm overseas at the moment,' the introduction said... I saw a picture of flowers.

And the number that appeared below it.

I pressed the call button.

*Tiririk.*

An unfamiliar tone played, and I became completely incapable of logical thought.

And the moment the connection sound was cut off...

*– Gunwoo?*

I nearly choked.

A familiar, gentle voice came through the phone—a voice I'd somehow become unable to remember.

*– Gunwoo, what's going on?*

"I..."

I swallowed.

The smartphone trembled in my hand as I pressed it hard against my ear.

"I just wanted to hear your voice."

*– Did you just say what I think you said?*

My mother's voice rose in pitch, as if she were delighted.

And then I remembered. She had always spoken like this.

*– It's a good thing you called when you did. We've been communicating only by radio here in Antarctica, but your timing was perfect.*

This time, her voice softened again.

*– I'm sorry. Your dad and I miss you so much too, Gunwoo.*

*Damn it. Damn it!*

"Yes, me too."

*What was I even talking about?*

*– Oh my, what is really going on? You've finally grown out of puberty, my son! Mom has to go now.*

My eyes stung.

*– Then, I'll definitely see you at Christmas this year, all right?*

"Yes..."

The call ended like a mirage. However, the call record remained as proof.

★:
#><[01:04]><#
:★

It was telling me that this was real.

"......"

My hands were shaking.

*My thoughts raced wildly.*

*Where did she say she was? Antarctica?*

I went back to the chat. My parents had been sent to an Antarctic base for two years as part of a research team—we'd been in intermittent contact for a year...

And they'd even told me that since they'd been assigned to the winter team this year, they wouldn't be able to contact me often.

"......"

*Antarctica.*

An idea lodged itself in my brain like an awl.

*Wouldn't it be okay to go?*

There was no longer anything like a status abnormality forcing me down a certain path. I didn't have to debut here.

If I made up an excuse, took some time off, and traveled somewhere near them, wouldn't they come see me? No one here would recognize my face anyway.

"......"

I turned off my smartphone.

And took a deep breath.

*No.*

I felt like my brain was going to explode...

*But I had to think rationally. This was a trap.*

Even if they were researchers, why would my parents suddenly go to Antarctica? It was all made up.

"It's a dream. It's a dream..."

*But if it's a dream, doesn't that mean I can do whatever I want? I can drop everything if I want...*

"Shut up."

*Both my mouth and brain need to shut up.*

*I have to go back.*

I stumbled, feeling as though I was about to collapse.

*This can't be happening.* I had to get out of here as quickly as possible.

*I can't stand this for much longer.*

I put my smartphone in my pocket and started walking again.

LeTi, Ryu Gunwoo's agency.

"......"

My hands were damp with sweat.

***

"You don't look good."

"I'm fine."

Practice at LeTi was difficult, as one might expect of such a successful agency. But there was almost no training scheduled for today, and everyone was practicing on their own, apparently without any rigid schedule.

"We came back during a loose point in the schedule. That's perfect. We're not practicing as a team."

"...Yes."

We needed to have a productive conversation.

This guy was probably completely unhinged at the moment... Even so, his composure in this absurd situation had an oddly calming effect.

*Let's work.*

I only needed to think about work. Nothing else was allowed.

I did my best to remain calm.

"There was nothing unusual when I returned home. What about you, Hubaenim?"

"A new status window appeared."

"Hmm."

I explained the current situation in as much detail as possible. We weren't going to go behind each other's backs.

And I even tried to give him an example to help him understand.

"Have you ever played ⟨127 Section⟩?"

"I'm not that interested in games."

*I thought so.*

Anyway, as if he understood the context, Cheongryeo nodded quite obediently.

"In the end, you need reputation to fully recover your Vocal stat. Hmm."

"......"

*So that's what matters to you most? Fine. I get it.*

Then he suddenly threw out a question.

"Do you want to debut right away?"

*Do you own this agency or something? You're just a trainee, or have you forgotten?*

*Is it because he's so used to being in charge?*

If we both had the same goal, we'd probably make the most efficient team possible.

However, I had no intention of doing something careless by going straight down the route that this system wanted.

"Wait."

I brought up the Quest tab.

★-
\
#><[Quest: Awaken companions 1/?]><#

Collect companions and awaken them

Companions who can be awakened: ?

Needed reputation: 1000 exp
-★

My quest was to awaken companions.

And I reminisced about the conversation I had with Keundal yesterday just before I went to sleep.

*– What do you think it means by 'companions that can be awakened?'*

*– I don't know either... Like hyung guessed, isn't it related to stats? Because it was like that in* ⟨127 Section⟩*...*

*– I guess so.*

I thought this meant my companions would get to level up too, making it easier for me to adapt to this world.

And this time, there was nothing forcing me into it.

*Then, isn't there some room to maneuver?*

As generous as it was, there had to be some opening I could exploit.

*– Then perhaps we could modify this a little? Just add one line of explanation that fits the context.*

*– ...Maybe just a little?*

So I added this line.

★-
\
**[Awakened companions will grow stronger, recalling the past timeline.]**
-★

This was a feature in ⟨127 Section⟩.

In that game, every time a companion was awakened, they regained the stats they'd accumulated in a previous playthrough and became stronger... There was a setting like that.

"......"

I clenched my fists.

*I have to try it.*

I just had to increase my reputation a little and check if the new condition worked properly.

I explained the contents to Cheongryeo.

And his reaction was like this.

"Oh? All right."

"......"

*Wait, that's it?*

"Hmm. What's with that reaction? Was that a joke?"

"Of course not."

I'd get the most out of this guy for as long as he cooperated. I crossed my arms.

"First... I understand that in large entertainment companies, information about some of the top-tier trainees gets leaked before debut."

"Yes. Sometimes it's leaked on purpose."

I'd done a little research before coming here. For example, Shin Jaehyeon's name was already somewhat well-known among fans of LeTi idols.

I'd start by gaining a reputation that way.

"I want some information about me leaked the same way."

"Oh, are you going to post something on the Internet yourself?"

I looked at him in bewilderment.

"Of course I'm not going to do that."

"Haha!"

He knew perfectly well I wasn't going to settle for something that crude. Attracting attention through Internet posts required some controversy, and it would be difficult to control precisely.

The official route was better than that.

"This company also gives work to trainees, right?"

Large agencies often put trainees who hadn't debuted yet to work in various ways.

They made them backup dancers for debuted senior idols so they could gain stage experience, or had them appear in music videos to build their reputation.

Usually, they worked with seniors of the opposite sex so the fandoms wouldn't overlap.

And as far as I knew, LeTi had done this often before VTIC debuted with a more mysterious approach; afterward, the agency changed its policy.

*It's awards season.*

*And I knew exactly which of LeTi's female idol groups was the most popular at this point.*

*Marshmallow.*

The group behind the hit song ⟨POP☆CON⟩.

So Ryu Gunwoo, currently on the debut team, could work as a backup dancer for Marshmallow.

"I need you to help me make that happen."

I looked at Shin Jaehyeon, who undoubtedly knew the ins and outs of this agency.

The guy nodded calmly in a way that inspired confidence.

"Hmm. A main vocalist probably doesn't need that kind of experience... but if you're that desperate to try, why not?"

"......"

*Damn it. This bastard and I really don't mesh.*
