# Chapter 260 - Year-End Festivals, IV

TeSTAR was busy preparing for a live broadcast even on the last day of the year.

"They started early."

The office worker who ran a fansite for both Park Moondae and Lee Sejin clicked her tongue when she noticed that the second part of the ⟨MBS Song Festival⟩, in which TeSTAR was scheduled to appear, was almost over.

It was funny that this was the first time she'd been able to rest in front of the TV, even though it was already the last day of the year, thanks to all the overtime she'd been working. It was also amusing that a program set to run past midnight had started before nine.

And in fact, she didn't really need to watch it at the moment.

Because TeSTAR would come out after a while.

*I've already hired someone to film fancams because I had overtime work. I'll use those for uploads later.*

So, she was just watching it out of mild curiosity.

*I'm a bit curious about what the collaboration with Seon Ahyeon will be like.*

Of course, it wasn't a question of skill.

In fact, there'd been a bit of talk in a group chat containing a few of Park Moondae's fansite masters.

When Park Moondae was MCing the SBC show, he'd briefly had a vicious look on his face.

*It rarely happens.*

Park Moondae was surprisingly the type to keep himself in check in front of the camera.

Of course, he wasn't quite as good at it as Lee Sejin, since his expression had grown cold during things like the summer package, but... That was probably the only time he'd failed to rein in his emotions.

This time, too, it was so brief that there was no controversy, but the fansite masters sensed something was off, analyzed the angle of his gaze, and even figured out who he'd been looking at.

And they confirmed it.

★$
– It was our kid greeting the other collaboration artists...

– ㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋ Uh...

– I think something's up from the angle, but don't say anything.
$★

So they'd already arrived at a conclusion that was very close to the truth: 'Ahyeon seems to have had a hard time preparing for the collaboration stage.'

Of course, aware of the risks of screenshots and the matter going public, they'd avoided discussing the details and deleted the messages. But the office worker had been in that chat, too.

*Don't tell me Seon Ahyeon's feeling down about it...*

Now that she'd guessed what had happened behind the scenes, she wanted to see how the performance turned out. She was curious whether she'd be able to spot the problem.

She refreshed her social media feed and watched TV indifferently.

The special stage was scheduled near the end of Part 2.

It was the typical setup where junior idols were tasked with a hastily put-together performance of a song by legendary first-generation K-pop seniors.

*It's still tacky.*

Half of those first-generation seniors' careers had long since been buried because of drunk driving, so she watched the TV quite cynically.

She was starting to regret tuning in to watch people she wasn't interested in perform songs she didn't like.

Soon, an accompaniment mixed with synthetic sounds began to play. It had been arranged for a dance performance, without lyrics.

$p
♪ *Wee Oo Wee Oo* ♫

They must have spent some money, so the LED signboard and floor with water puddle-like LED effects were quite good.

*Of course, that impression would disappear the moment they started dancing.*

She expected the group shot to be a mess, since busy idols rarely had time to rehearse together.

But the moment the choreography started...

$p
♪ *Oo-u Oo Oo-u Oo* ♫
p$

The quality was decent.

"Hmm?"

Her many years of K-pop experience told her the reason right away.

*It's because the person in the center did well.*

And the opening center was Seon Ahyeon.

"Oh."

His acrobatic movements made full use of his long limbs, setting him apart from the start.

The cameras were doing a terrible job, but Seon Ahyeon's movements were so stunning that they made the camerawork look good.

The group dance didn't even need to be perfectly in sync. The performer in the center was overwhelmingly good, so it looked as though everyone was doing well.

*No, wait.*

"Never mind."

But four seconds later, she clicked her tongue at the mess in the corner where the idols got up from the floor out of sync or nearly fell over.

*It's ruined.*

Year-end collaborations that gathered busy people for a one-off stage often ended up like this, but somehow it felt even clumsier this time.

It was because of the stark contrast to when Seon Ahyeon was in the center.

"I guess Seon Ahyeon's the only one who's going to come out of this looking good."

Wondering if the difference was purely a matter of skill, the office worker watched with interest.

It seemed that Seon Ahyeon was the only one who benefited from the choreography being closer to modern dance than to choreography designed for TV.

Apparently, he'd majored in ballet or modern dance, and he'd probably studied and practiced longer than the industry average.

Just in time, a close-up of Seon Ahyeon caught on camera filled the screen.

As he put one hand on the floor, the expression in the shining eyes on his beautiful face was unexpectedly powerful.

She knew he had a pretty face, but this was unexpected.

*I thought he'd be too intimidated to perform to his full potential.*

She corrected her judgment.

She'd assumed that Seon Ahyeon had been invited to the collaboration because Lee Sejin and Park Moondae were in the same group, but it seemed he had the spunk to shine on his own stage.

Without taking her eyes off the TV, she watched Seon Ahyeon's dance performance until it reached the end.

Her reaction was fairly representative of the viewers', only a little less dramatic.

In fact, he said the other guys did well during the practice. However terrible their personalities were, they were still experienced professionals.

The problem came from the stage.

"Th-the floor was a bit slippery..." Seon Ahyeon said.

Because it was a live broadcast, there'd been a timing mistake, and the water sprayed for visual effects during the previous stage hadn't dried yet.

However, since only Seon Ahyeon had managed to move normally, using that excuse would just make the others look bad.

"But you didn't slip!" Cha Eugene said.

"Th-that's because I've practiced it before...!"

"As expected, your past experiences have led to your current level of skill! I respect you!" Kim Raebin said.

"Uh... Th-thank you..."

*Just like that.*

*Tough luck.*

Normally, the other artists would've caught some flak, followed by criticism of the broadcast station's poor preparations once the facts came out.

But now, it looked like Seon Ahyeon would be the only one to come out of it unscathed, getting all the praise.

*Good.*

It felt refreshing. Everything was going smoothly.

Except for the fact that this waiting room was not private.

"Aha, that's right! Anyway, we did something similar back then, so it reminded us of that time."

"......"

VTIC shared this waiting room with us.

And they were trying to get involved in TeSTAR's conversation.

*Why did it end up like this?*

In fact, I could roughly understand why we shared a room.

Since VTIC was currently at the very top of the industry, the broadcasting station didn't want to inconvenience them by having them share a room with an older group.

And it wouldn't do to put them in with a group too far below them either, so TeSTAR, who seemed to know them well, had finally been selected to share their room.

They could've gone to an American New Year's show, so of course the station had to be careful. I had no idea how SBC had managed to get them to come.

*Damn it.*

I understood that, but these bastards seemed not to have developed any social tact during their nine years of activity. Why did they keep butting into the kids' conversation?

*Stop.*

However, they were also dragging the other guy into the conversation.

"Do you remember, Cheongryeo hyung? The year-end special stages!"

"Yes."

Cheongryeo, who was getting his hair done by a stylist, raised the corners of his mouth.

"We did a lot."

"......"

To think that I'd have to celebrate the New Year with this bastard. I'd rather be exiled to an outdoor stage.

"Guys, Sejin is coming up."

"Oh!!"

At this time last year, I'd thought it might be my last.
