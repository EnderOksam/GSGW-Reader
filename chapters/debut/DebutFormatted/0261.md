# Chapter 261 - The Element of Surprise

As soon as the live broadcast was over, I called the agency and spoke with the Management Office employee on duty.

I told them that our song was going to be released at the same time as Youngrin's.

And from what I heard, the company was turned upside down. It was hardly surprising, given Youngrin's recent performance...

*Ranked 3rd on the largest music site in Korea.*

For comparison, TeSTAR's latest song, 'Nightmare', had ranked 37th on the yearly music chart.

Besides, Youngrin had no fewer than four songs in the TOP50 on this yearly chart. Two title songs, one OST, and one variety collaboration song.

It was a staggering number.

*We'll never win.*

She wasn't just popular with the public, but had a solid, dedicated fandom, so there was no opening we could take advantage of.

In a word, the simultaneous release of both of our songs... It was like losing before we'd even begun.

Of course, it was not just the company that had turned upside down.

"With Youngrin sunbaenim?"

"Yes."

"Ha..."

Even within the group itself, the first meeting of the new year was convened.

Our year was starting off with a pile of work, but that hardly mattered in the face of our current situation.

I stayed calm and carefully considered our situation.

*He deliberately waited until now to tell me.*

There were four days left until the comeback, but it was impossible to push back the date.

Cheongryeo knew there was no significant way I could respond, but he told me regardless so I could at least steel myself.

It could be seen as a favor, so there was no point wasting time getting angry at him. We'd always been competitors, and the situation pissed me off, but it was better than knowing nothing.

*Regardless... I was still upset.*

"Moondae."

"What?"

"Are you sure about that information? Where did you hear that?"

"From VTIC."

Lee Sejin gave me a subtle look that roughly translated to, 'Are you sure he didn't just tell you that to fuck you over?'

Yes. Just in case, I checked that too.

I tossed my smartphone into the air and caught it as I spoke.

"I asked Youngrin directly, just in case. They were right."

"Phew."

With the release only four days away and our comebacks overlapping, Youngrin hadn't seen any need to lie.

She had seemed curious, though.

-It hasn't been long since I decided, so I'm surprised Moondae-ssi knows about it.

I told her I'd overheard staff talking about it and asked her just in case.

The crew she worked with would be reminded to keep things secret, but given our own desperate situation, I had no time to worry about them.

*This is driving me crazy.*

I pressed my temples. Then, Cha Eugene suddenly raised his hand.

"Hey! I have a question!"

"Yes, tell us, Eugene."

"Why is releasing at the same time as Youngrin sunbaenim such a big deal?"

Ryu Chungwoo replied.

"...Youngrin sunbaenim has a strong reputation with the public. She's not someone you compete against. We'll be pushed down on the music charts."

"We don't know what will happen before we even try it! We have to try it!"

Yes, his confidence was great, but... It was a matter of morale.

How much would morale drop if we couldn't even reach first place on the real-time music charts with a full-length album?

And Youngrin would only attract more listeners as time went on, so our song being scheduled for release one day after hers was the worst possible timing.

*Releasing a new song when Youngrin's song is at its peak?*

*We can never be number one unless it's on some obscure site nobody uses.*

Our streak of full albums hitting first place was about to come to an end.

"......"

Cha Eugene shrugged when he saw the members were still lost in their thoughts and worries.

"Okay. It's okay if we don't win first place! We're good. We can do it well on stage!"

"...!"

"Let's get first place for our performance instead of the music!"

It was a simple statement, but it did dispel some of our gloom.

"Yes, it's been a while since we've released an album, so it's important to show our best work and see it through safely."

"Y-yeah, that's right," Seon Ahyeon agreed. "W-we prepared hard... If we focus on doing our best, I'm sure w-we'll have fun."

Yes, such positive thoughts were good too.

But... I'd still like to win.

*Let's think.*

I tapped the living room floor with my fingers.

*How can we beat Youngrin on the music charts?*

First of all, it was clear that Youngrin wasn't going to promote this song at all, and there were hardly any rumors going around the industry.

That was what I'd gathered from Youngrin's reaction as well as our company's.

*This is T1 we're talking about. They usually know when most major singers are making their comebacks.*

Youngrin herself said she'd only confirmed the New Year release a few days ago, so Cheongryeo must have gotten hold of the information unusually quickly. Most people would still be in the dark.

*Then, if TeSTAR takes that into account and goes all-in on promotion through variety shows...*

*No, that won't work.* All the year-end promotions had already taken place, and we had plenty of variety show appearances lined up for the start of the year. Adding more would only risk overexposure.

*Hmm.*

As I was rubbing the back of my neck, my eyes met Kim Raebin's. He wore a dark expression.

"...Are you that worried?" I asked.

"What? N-no! Not that much!"

*It makes sense that you're more worried than the others. You helped produce at least half of this album.*

I'd honestly been taken aback when I found out he'd been working on music with the last of his laptop's battery even when we were stranded on the deserted island.

"Just... I'm somewhat worried that Youngrin sunbaenim's song might satisfy people's desire for new music, so many listeners won't even give our song a chance."

If he had that much to say just because I'd poked him once, he really was worried.

And his concerns were warranted. He was focusing on the right things.

"That's right. If someone with strong public appeal takes all the attention, it usually becomes a contest between fandoms, and the stronger one crowds out the weaker one."

It was inevitable.

"......"

After thinking about it some more, I finally opened my mouth.

"So, for now, I think we have to assume that we can't beat her on the charts no matter what we do," I said.

"...I guess so."

"Hmm~"

"But I think we'll win first place on music shows."

Bae Sejin, who had a serious face, was a little surprised.

"...How?"

"That's right, Youngrin sunbaenim is releasing a digital single, and we're releasing an album, right? There are album points."

"Ah."

Yes. We were releasing a full-length album after a while, and Youngrin was just releasing a surprise single to celebrate the new year.

*Our fans will try to get us first place by raising our scores in the other categories[1]{Editor's Note: Typically there are separate categories to make up an album's total score. For example, there's live voting, online streams, album sales, etc.} somehow.*

Because trophies were important.

I was grateful to the fans and felt a little bad, but the public's reaction was not difficult to predict.

This was our first full-length album since the car accident, and it was time to get the group's story rolling again.

A hollow victory would kill that excitement at a time when we needed everyone following TeSTAR to feel satisfied.

So, in the end, we couldn't try to have everything.

*Even if we give up first place on the charts, we need to get people talking about us more than her.*

The perception that 'they deserve to be number one' and our public influence had to be maintained. That was as much as I was willing to concede to Youngrin.

"If we're going to lose on the charts anyway, I suggest we aim for average chart performance and go all-in on promotions so people talk about us..."

"...!"

"The promotions, publicity, mentions, the order of the reveals... Everything."

We'd need to make some drastic choices and focus.

I spoke, and each member seemed to be lost in thought.

And Lee Sejin was the first to raise his hand.

"I agree. Isn't this the most reasonable way? There is no other option."

"Okay. It's a time when strategy is important," Ryu Chungwoo said.

Kim Raebin also nodded.

"I believe that if we can get people talking about our promotions, more people will give our songs a chance, even if it's later than we'd like."

He was a pretty tenacious guy.

I smiled and then nodded.

Bae Sejin opened his mouth with a nervous face.

"So, do you have any ideas for how we can get people talking about us?"

"We need to discuss the specifics a bit more, but... The element of shock has always worked well, hasn't it?"

This was the right thing to do if we wanted to steal some of the attention Youngrin would get from her surprise announcement.

"Let's catch people completely off guard."

"...??"

Unexpectedness. We'd hit them with a surprising plot twist.
