# Chapter 390 - A Different Answer

With all the elements and concepts overlapping, our performance naturally headed in a similar direction to the first time I'd performed this song in a team match.

It might have been natural since Seon Ahyeon did the choreography and I took on the role of leader.

This performance was becoming more and more similar to the stage of the first team match of ⟨Idol Inc.⟩.

"Thank you for your hard work."

"Hyung, here!"

"Thanks."

I wiped the sweat dripping from my chin with a towel.

"Hyung, I guess you're good at this type of dance choreography! You're really fast at learning it, and your moves already look polished. You're amazing..."

"I guess so. I got lucky."

*I already knew the choreography.*

I didn't need to memorize the revised chorus choreography we were currently practicing. It was almost identical to what I remembered.

That wasn't all.

A similar phenomenon occurred with the composition of the arrangement.

*...I didn't expect to end up proving how well we'd done it back then.*

The experts in this survival show made changes similar to the ones I'd made during my first team match in ⟨Idol Inc.⟩.

As the 'art' collaboration had to be completed in just one week, more of the agency's in-house experts than ever before were lending their skills to ensure stage quality.

And this was the feedback we received right before the interim evaluation.

"I think it would be nice to emphasize the bridge a bit more..."

"...Yes."

"Since your other two teammates have a lot of dance parts at the beginning of the song, and this is a collaboration stage, you should do this part solo," one of the experts said to me.

*Me? A solo?*

I did the same thing in ⟨Idol Inc.⟩. To show off my vocal stat, I'd arranged it in a high register.

In fact, it wasn't an exaggeration to say the arrangement would be almost identical to the one I'd already done.

"How is it?"

"I completely agree...!" one of my teammates said.

But no one protested.

"......"

I relaxed my hands and opened my mouth.

"Alright."

However, the arranger massaged his own shoulder with a somewhat dissatisfied expression and blurted out something.

"Are you sick, Gunwoo? You look really out of it today."

"...!"

*This wouldn't work.*

In a survival show, appearing lackluster was a landmine. I was sure to be accused of having an attitude.

It was best to clear up the misunderstanding quickly.

I immediately raised my voice.

"No! I just have some body aches... I'm sorry if my poor condition was obvious."

"No, there's nothing to be sorry about... You need to take good care of yourself. Debut is just around the corner, you know?"

"Yes."

And the guys on either side of me quickly began whispering to me.

"I'm sorry, hyung. I didn't know you were sick..."

"Please at least drink some water."

"Thanks. I'm the one who didn't tell you, though."

I got some water from Judan and let the situation go. The arranger asked again.

"Then, how about we arrange the bridge like that? To emphasize Gunwoo's vocals?"

"Yes."

I couldn't afford to protest. From now on, I had to look more dedicated than ever on camera so nothing went wrong.

*This is right.*

But I was mentally resisting the song and concept for no good reason, and that was the problem. Throughout practice, I had the unpleasant feeling that I wasn't singing to my full potential.

*Damn it.*

"Let's take a good rest and come back tomorrow..."

"If you work even harder now, you'll sleep better tonight," I said.

"...Yes."

I talked my teammates, who were concerned about my condition, out of their crazy suggestions to 'work hard, finish early and rest', and finished practice that afternoon.

I couldn't help but sigh.

*Am I crazy?*

With the cameras rolling, my strange mood was actually affecting my performance. Wasn't that enough to make me unworthy of being on the show?

*Aren't you going to do a proper job?* Maybe I should put the reputation points I'd earned toward upgrading my own stats, putting companion recruitment aside for now.

"Hyung, will you eat dinner...?"

"I'll leave after I finish this. You can eat first."

I headed to the cafeteria after stretching for quite some time.

After taking a shower and getting my interim evaluation, I planned to come back to the practice room at night and practice some more. I felt like shit.

*Dururuk.*

I pulled out a chair.

Perhaps because it was past dinner time, there was no one in the cafeteria. Except for one guy who was using it like a study cafe.

"Hello, hyung. Sit here."

Cheongryeo.

I immediately checked the corners of the room and all the dining tables. The guy grinned.

"There are no cameras. So we can still talk, right?"

"......"

I put some food on my tray and sat down across from him. The guy had been checking a notebook of choreography sequences when he asked me a question.

"You're not sick, are you?"

Since we were sharing a practice room, I guess he'd overheard a few things.

"Why are you asking when you already know?"

"Hmm? I'm asking because I already know."

He closed the notebook and asked in a light tone.

"Isn't this your first time? A performance of yours was well received, but now it's as if it never happened, and you're being forced to use it again in a different situation."

"......"

I stared across the table.

"I guess it's bothering you quite a bit."

*What the hell am I supposed to do?*

The words rose up to my throat, but of course, I didn't say them. *Fuck, he probably did it because he had no other choice.*

"Are you thinking about that stage? The stage you performed during the first team match of ⟨Idol Inc.⟩?"

"Yes."

"The first stages are always memorable. I still remember mine too."

*This guy's first stage?*

"Come to think of it... My first stage was also on a survival show. This survival show."

"......"

"We have quite a few things in common. Interesting, isn't it?"

The guy was silent for a moment, staring into space, but then smiled.

"It's okay. Once you've been through it once, you realize it's nothing. Weren't you saying that this isn't reality anyway? You'll feel disappointed, and that'll be it."

"...Maybe."

I carelessly lowered my chopsticks from the tray onto the table.

*I couldn't believe I was saying this to this guy.*

"But the problem is that the repulsion I feel is affecting my performance."

"Oh my."

The guy finished writing in the notebook and closed it. And he spoke quietly.

"Don't practice anymore today and go to bed early. And eat well."

"......"

"Your thoughts are more influenced by your physical condition than you might think. You *are* going to take care of yourself, right?"

I nodded my head slowly. The guy shrugged his shoulders.

"Okay... If you really feel like you can't do it, think of an alternative."

"Why bother doing that?"

*Clink!*

I slammed my glass of water down on the table.

"I know what I should do, and I know I can do it."

"So you *do* know."

The guy smiled and got up from his seat. And he pointed to my tray with his chin.

"Eat all of that and sleep."

"......"

I nodded. The guy went out.

"Hoo."

I hadn't had an appetite to begin with, but now I felt even less like eating.

Here... He was probably talking about ballet intermissions.

"......"

"Actually, when I saw you in the hallway... You looked familiar, so I thought I'd seen you somewhere before."

*What?*

"But when I got closer, I realized it wasn't the right time to ask that. You looked like you were in pain."

"Pain?"

Seon Ahyeon flinched slightly but nodded.

"Yes. You looked like you were in pain. Even now."

"......"

Seriously... I must have been wearing my emotions on my sleeve, for anyone and everyone to read.

*On camera too?*

No, I'd made the excuse that I wasn't feeling well, so it would be okay. I'd done my best in practice without missing any, and I'd never said anything mean to the other contestants, so that was about it...

"You have no obligation to confide in me, but... Still, I hope you find someone to talk to. This is my counselor's number..."

"I'm all right."

*Stop it.*

"...Yes, I'm sorry. I was too presumptuous."

Seon Ahyeon looked uncertain. I held back a sigh. And as I was about to exhale, I blurted out an answer without realizing it.

"It's not presumptuous."

"Yes."

"It's just... I've already done a stage with the same song and the same concept."

"...This stage?"

"That's right."

I lowered my gaze.

"...I'd done this as my first performance with some close friends who I can't see right now, so it doesn't feel right for the same performance to be shown to the world for the first time on this show."

Even as I said it, I knew how absurd I sounded.

Seon Ahyeon looked puzzled. He seemed to be thinking the same thing.

"Then... If you're having such a hard time, why didn't you tell me?"

*Why else?*

"Because if I don't say anything, the results will be better."

"...!"

"The response to that stage was really good. I'm not stupid enough to not use something that's been proven."

He might misunderstand and assume I was talking about a month-end evaluation at another agency. I guess it didn't matter.

"......"

Seon Ahyeon looked at the table for a long moment and said nothing.

But he soon spoke in a firm voice.

"Good results are not necessarily the answer. Choosing not to inflict pain on yourself is also a valid choice."

"...!"

It wasn't a remark I'd have expected from a professional. However, Seon Ahyeon didn't back down.

"Forcing yourself to face something you don't want to do isn't the only way to succeed."

He hesitated, then added in a faint voice.

"I... I've also been in pain because I disregarded my own feelings too much."

"......"

"And Gunwoo, I'm sure you can put on a good performance even if you change your approach. There's no single right answer when it comes to staging and interpretation."

He concluded gently.

"So you don't have to insist on suffering on your own just to succeed. An alternative can also be the right answer."

I didn't know what to say.

I wouldn't deny that his words had shaken me and that, for a moment, I was tempted to take his advice.

But there was something else weighing on me.

*...I thought he was living well without any difficulties.*

A refined speaking style without stuttering, proactive communication in unfamiliar situations, and considerable success.

He was the one who already had everything that Seon Ahyeon was working toward in the real world.

Wouldn't it look to anyone like this version of him was doing better?

So why was he talking like that... No, why was he talking about his pain to a guy he'd only just met?
