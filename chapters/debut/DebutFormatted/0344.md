# Chapter 344 - Seon Ahyeon's Resolve

Seon Ahyeon's attitude changed.

It started with a camp schedule meeting.

"Then it's okay not to collect more image references for the concept, right? Shall we go with what we have?"

"I think that should be fine!" Kim Raebin said.

"Okay. So, if anyone has a different opinion—"

Everyone assumed no one did.

"I-I do!"

"...!"

The guy who had never interrupted anyone raised his hand before the sentence was even finished.

Ryu Chungwoo seemed a bit flustered, but kindly asked him.

"Alright, what do you think, Ahyeon?"

"I-it would be nice if we could broaden the scope and collect a little more data. We still have time, so..."

"Hmm..."

And Seon Ahyeon even expressed the opposite opinion directly.

*What's wrong with him?*

*Should we end the meeting now and check on him?*

I could tell from their eyes that the members were thinking along those lines, but Seon Ahyeon wasn't finished.

"Two... just two more days! Couldn't we take two more days? The project team member said the deadline was the afternoon of the day after tomorrow... If that's the schedule, I-it won't go any faster even if we finish early...!"

Even though he looked like he was about to break into a sweat, he went on to explain his reasoning.

And it was quite convincing.

"Ah, I see."

"Wow~ Ahyeon, you were able to remember that? It was 3 months ago..."

"Y-yes... I wrote it down..."

He wasn't particularly good at coming up with original ideas, but he made good judgments in a given situation and was persistent in making his case.

It was in line with what Seon Ahyeon usually showed.

"S-so...! Let's think for two more days...!"

"Well~ Of course, I agree! Don't we all?" Keun Sejin said.

"Mmm, yes."

However, his voice had about ten times its usual energy, and it was impossible to ignore. Everyone was taken aback and unsure how to react.

*What's happening?*

I wondered whether this was a good thing, but somehow it made me uneasy. What had come over him to make him change so much?

"Moondae, what do you think!?" Seon Ahyeon asked.

*Geez, you're making me nervous.*

"...I agree."

"Yes!"

He was even asking me to answer out loud. It was an initiative I had never seen from him before.

*What's going on?*

And he kept up that same energy at the evening composition meeting.

"I-I... I wish the song had a bit more string accompaniment!"

"Oh, oh."

"Which string instrument?"

"Something with a sharp, upbeat, dramatic sound... Um, a fiddle, for example! I think it'd suit the concept better...!"

"Fiddle?"

And Seon Ahyeon began to explain what a fiddle was in even more detail and with greater fervor.

It was the least classical of the bowed string instruments, so it had been given a separate name, and it had a country-like sound, and blah blah...

"Oh, oh."

It was like he was giving a presentation. Cha Eugene, who was listening with interest, was almost ready to clap.

"That's a good idea! Hyung is cool!"

"Yes!"

And even though Seon Ahyeon's face looked like it was about to explode at those words, he didn't turn down the compliment.

*What in the world?*

I really didn't know what the situation was.

*Well, even if it's a bit much for him, the other guys speak with this much enthusiasm when they feel strongly about something...*

Yes, it would have been a good thing if he had a change of heart and wanted to actively participate.

In fact, Seon Ahyeon had always had trouble joining conversations, and compared with how passionately the other members spoke their minds, this wasn't all that unusual.

"Then, as Ahyeon-hyung said, let's add the violin, a string instrument that has both sharpness and softness and fits well with the current arrangement—" Kim Raebin began.

"N-no! Not just a violin, but a fiddle! Fiddle! That's what we need!"

"R-right!"

"......"

No, maybe this *was* a little too much?

*This guy's acting like it's a matter of life and death.*

"I will try my best!" Kim Raebin said.

"Th-thank you...!"

With even Bae Sejin looking cautious, the meeting continued under Seon Ahyeon's lead until it finally ended.

That night...

Keun Sejin, who was sitting in the kitchen drinking sparkling water, asked seriously.

"Moondaemoondae, there isn't some sad backstory I don't know about, right? If we don't make a good album, is someone going to go out of business or something?"

"......"

"Heok, or could it be that Moondae bullied Ahyeon for not pulling his weight— Ow!"

How could that be?

I held back a sigh as I looked at the guy pretending the slap I'd given him on the back actually hurt.

"...There must be another reason. I don't think it's that."

"Yeah? Hmm... Well..."

As if he'd come to the same conclusion as me that it wasn't a bad situation, Keun Sejin shrugged and moved on.

"You don't have to know everything about people to be friends, right?"

"......"

It was kind of pissing me off to hear that from the guy who'd demanded every little detail about my situation.

In the meantime, Seon Ahyeon ran to his room as soon as the night camp was over and didn't come out.

*What is he doing?*

I swallowed a groan and opened my mouth.

"...I wonder if Seon Ahyeon feels left out."

"Hm?"

"You and I have been talking a lot lately, and we haven't involved him in any of those conversations."

In fact, the three of us were the same age, had the same experience, and so on.

Maybe he was angry about this unfair treatment and was relieving his stress in his own way, albeit a positive one.

Keun Sejin nodded, thinking it over.

"Well, maybe that could be the case. Ah, but Ahyeon isn't the type to be like that... Ah."

He groaned.

"Ahyeon didn't really have many good friends in school, did he?"

"...No."

"Well, um... I guess he doesn't feel very good about this whole situation, then."

Lee Sejin glanced back at me and asked quite seriously.

"What are you going to do?"

"......"

*How would I know?*

Of course, he wasn't really asking me to come up with a countermeasure. He was probably wondering if I'd share my secret with Ahyeon, too.

That is, if the cause of Seon Ahyeon's abnormal behavior was that I hadn't told him about the supernatural phenomena yet...

"Hm... Of course, I can't tell you what to do, but I don't think Ahyeon's attitude toward you will change much just because you tell him."

"......"

That... I didn't think so either.

*He's such a nice guy.*

I let out a short sigh.

"Hey, it's not like Ahyeon will start avoiding you just because you're actually older than us— Ow! Hey, that one actually hurt!"

*Yes, I'm sure it did. That's the strength of a man in his twenties, you bastard.*

...No, actually, the most embarrassing thing was that I'd probably lashed out because I felt guilty.

I sighed at the giggling Keun Sejin.

He stopped laughing before changing the topic.

"Oh, and speaking of which... What about Heeseung? Should I tell him I know his secret and give him a little scolding?"

The guy grinned.

"Or should I say that I came from the future too? Wow, that would be hilarious."

"Stop."

That would be in poor taste, and the timing would throw the whole story into disarray.

"That doesn't make sense. It seems to work like a relay race."

"Oh~ Moondae's really analyzed the situation. As expected of Moondaemoondae. So that's why you were so nice to Heeseung. To learn more from him."

*Quick-witted bastard.*

I clasped my fingers.

"Yes, and we have a use for Gold 2."

"......"

However, Keun Sejin did not answer for a moment.

*What.*

Then, suddenly, as if in doubt, he opened his mouth again with a serious face.

"But Moondaemoondae."

*What?*

"I wanted to ask you earlier... That Gold 2. Are you talking about Heeseung?"

"...!!"

*How did you know—*

"......"

*Wait, have I been saying that out loud all this time?*

"So I was right! Is that what you call Heeseung? Why?"

"...That-"

"Wait, wait. Let me guess. No way... Is it because he was gold rank in ⟨Idol Inc.⟩? Did you attach a number to his rank?"

"......"

"I got that right too?? Oh, this is great! Wow~ Look at how heartless Park Moondae is! ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ ended ages ago and you still call him that!"

*Fuck.*

I gnashed my teeth, watching the guy who burst out laughing.

"I can call him whatever the hell I want in my head. So what?"

"You should really be embarrassed... No, no, sorry, did I laugh too much? Hey, I'm really sorry~"

This bastard was trying to gloss over his laughter with an apology he didn't mean.

However, Keun Sejin soon calmed down, his eyes shining.

"Moondaemoondae, I'm asking because I'm really curious... Did you call Sejin that too? Oh, did you classify all the guys like that when we were just in ⟨Idol Inc.⟩? Am I Gold 1?"

*Dream on.*

"...It was just Keun Sejin."

"Oh~ So at least I got a nickname. Good, good."

I felt the urge to sigh as I watched Lee Sejin nod to himself. *This was driving me crazy.*

"Then how long did you call me like that?"

"......"

"Moondaemoondae?"

*I still call you that, you bastard.*

I left the whiner behind and went back to my room to go to bed.

***

The album production camp continued the following day.

And the momentum of Seon Ahyeon continued.

"If we keep making no progress like this, we'll have to work until night. None of you like working overtime. So—" I said.

"W-why not work overtime...! Let's do it!" Seon Ahyeon said.

"Huh? ...Okay."

Was this really necessary?

This was completely out of character. I shut my mouth when I saw Seon Ahyeon burning with the same passion Cha Eugene had when he got to work with special devices.

"Hyung, I hate working overtime!" Cha Eugene said.

"W-we still have to do it if it's necessary...!" Seon Ahyeon said.

*Would you look at that?* Now he was even being a little harsh with Cha Eugene.

Of course, it wasn't a bad thing. On the contrary, it was good for efficiency, but... I mean, he seemed to be pushing himself too hard.

I made my decision after seeing Seon Ahyeon secretly wiping the cold sweat from the back of his neck.

*This won't do.*

I'd have to at least try talking to him.

"You worked hard~"

"Well, let's clean this up."

After dinner, as soon as the meeting was temporarily over, I went to Seon Ahyeon's room. He'd retreated there again as soon as we were done.

*Knock, knock.*

"...!"

"It's me."

*Thud.*

When I opened the door and entered, I saw Seon Ahyeon hunched over his desk as if trying to cover something.

*What was he doing, exactly?*

"I-is there something you're looking for...?"

"I just wanted to talk—"

As I got closer, I realized what this guy was covering.

Articles, reviews, ratings.

It was a scrapbook of sorts, full of printed materials related to our previous albums.

!-
\
$$[The most important part?]$$

$$[The problem is expressiveness]$$
-!

And there were comments in neat handwriting here and there, presumably written by this guy himself.

Not only that, but something was displayed on the e-reader that this guy reflexively covered.

★:
#*#><$NE📖NE$ <br>
[The Collected Works of Edgar Allan Poe, Volume 5]><#*#
:★

*...No way.*

*Did he collect all this on his own?*

Anyone could see that everything was preparation material for TeSTAR's new album.

But I asked one more time.

"What is this all about?"

"...! Ah, yes, this..."

Seon Ahyeon seemed to hesitate several times, but soon answered in a rather confident voice.

"Among the works of Edgar Allan Poe that S-Sejin-hyung told us about... I-I think I found a subject that suits us better."

"...!"

"Well, the b-black cat was nice too, but... I feel like it overlaps a little with ‘Nightmare’. I wanted to include something that suits TeSTAR better."

Seon Ahyeon seemed nervous, but smiled faintly.

And I was already pretty convinced by what this guy was saying.

"......"

"H-here."

I accepted the notebook that had been lying under the e-reader as this guy held it out with a slightly trembling hand.

I opened it. It contained the name of the album and the existing TeSTAR concepts he wanted to connect it to.

I quickly read a few highlighted words in the notebook.

!-
\
$$[Burst of energy]$$

$$[Sense of immersion, sense of speed]$$
-!

Then I read the whole notebook without a word.

"......"

"I-I haven't organized it so it's easier to read yet... That's why I couldn't tell anyone. Y-you need to have supporting facts... to make an argument."

Seon Ahyeon lowered his head as if somewhat embarrassed.

"I... I'm not very good at speaking... So I thought at least the material itself should be thorough..."

*Really...*

"Seeing what you've already done today, I don't think you should have worried about that."

Seon Ahyeon's face reddened.

"Th-thank you. Still, adding the finishing touches..."

"Ah, so you were working on this on the side!" Kim Raebin said.

*Whoa.*

When I turned my head, Kim Raebin, who'd been working at the opposite desk with headphones on, took them off and turned to face us.

*Since when has he been here?*

I hadn't noticed him because he'd been working without making a sound.

"I didn't realize that what Ahyeon-hyung's been reading every day for the past few days was album reviews."

"Yes..."

Seon Ahyeon's face turned red as he affirmed, and Kim Raebin nodded as if he knew.

And his eyes lit up.

"I thought it was a new hobby you'd developed during the tour, but I never thought you'd put so much time and effort into such a large-scale analysis. You're diligent and talented, just as I expected."

"Y-y-yes..."

His face said he desperately wanted to decline the compliment, but he was doing his best not to.

I chuckled.

"That's right. It's great."

"...!"

"The content is good. You could have suggested it right away. It's enough like this."

"R-really...?!"

*Would I lie in this situation?*

"Yes."

To put it succinctly, his idea was so enticing that it would've drawn us in even if someone had simply pitched the rough draft.

*The reasoning that led to that conclusion is impeccable.*

Of course, there were people like Kim Raebin who needed only a little inspiration to create something anyone would love listening to.

However, sometimes people reached a single conclusion by putting many pieces together like a puzzle and carefully filtering out the other possible answers.

And that was exactly what this guy had done.

The conclusion this guy came to after looking through and analyzing all the existing TeSTAR album data, which was extensive even at a glance... It was very compelling.

*He must have worked hard.*

I thought of the guy who had been depressed because he couldn't participate properly in the camp, and I was deeply impressed.

This was beyond my expectations.

"I want to try this right now."

"...!"

Seon Ahyeon's eyes shone brightly. He seemed almost moved.

"Moo-Moondae. Really... Thank you."

"You're the one who deserves thanks for coming up with such a good idea."

However, Seon Ahyeon's words did not end.

"Well, then... You know..."

*Hmm?*

"C-could you... maybe... t-tell me about..."

At that moment, I realized what this guy meant by 'what's going on'.

*Wait... Is he asking me to tell him my secret?*

I guess I misunderstood something.

This guy's eagerness didn't come from rage.

The way this guy was watching my reaction... It was like he was trying to prove himself worthy of joining a secret society.

"N-no! Never mind... I-I'll organize this information a little more..."

Despite saying no, with a face full of anticipation, Seon Ahyeon crept towards the desk.

"Do you mind if I take a look, too?" Kim Raebin asked.

"Sure!"

"......"

...Apparently, Seon Ahyeon had channeled his emotions in a more productive way than I predicted.

Two things: the desire to contribute to the album camp and the desire to be recognized by the members his own age.

*At this rate, I'll be in deep shit if I don't tell him.*

"......"

*Fuck. I don't know what to do anymore.*

*Set a date or something.*

At this rate, I was going to tell all the tight-lipped guys anyway. I rubbed my face.

Anyway, on this night, Seon Ahyeon, who eventually called the meeting, made a presentation.

The members all had good judgment, and their reaction was much like mine.

They loved his suggestions.

"...I like this one more, too," Bae Sejin said.

"Then we'll shift our direction this way. Nice one, Ahyeon," Keun Sejin said.

"Y-yes, th-thank you... Thank you!"

Seeing that everyone, from Seon Ahyeon himself to the more practical members, seemed to like the idea, he bowed his head.

The atmosphere was very cheerful, as if applauding Seon Ahyeon.

Until the next words came out.

"Well, then we should recheck the schedule."

"...?"

Ryu Chungwoo smiled.

"That means... It means we have to revise every single plan we've already finalized."

"......"

"......"

Seon Ahyeon stared at the floor.

"S-sorry."

"What are you talking about? The album concept is getting better, which is a good thing."

It was a reply that carried the sadness of an office worker.

*K-pop Overtime Hell Camp... It's back in business.*

Fortunately, our overtime camp was remarkably effective at getting things done.

"I'll just add this one," I said.

"Mmm."

And since it had turned out this way, I added what I had wanted to include but had excluded for balance reasons.

*So this is the final plan.*

By the time it was warm outside, we had finished preparing for the release of our new album.
