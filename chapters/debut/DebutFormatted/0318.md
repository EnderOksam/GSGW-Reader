# Chapter 318 - Conversation, VI

His tone of voice over the phone changed, and he began to speak softly.

– Hello. We've met before, right?

It was definitely Cheongryeo's voice. It gave me goosebumps.

*Why is this bastard calling me?*

*Even the phone number was different. What the hell was going on? And what did he mean, we'd met before?*

– I'm the guy who moved into your old dorm room. We've met before. You're Gunwoo, right?

"...?"

– I found something you left in your room. I'll send it by courier. Can you give me your address? The boarding academy only gave me your phone number. They said your address was private information.

"......"

*Wait, was he voice phishing?*

I was dumbfounded for a moment and kept my mouth shut, then considered the possibility that it really was someone else.

And I came to the correct conclusion.

"No need to send it. I'll stop by today and pick it up. Thank you."

– Yes. All right.

He agreed surprisingly easily. No, could it really be someone else with the same voice...?

– It's a weekday, so I have to listen to lectures during the day... Shall we meet in front of the boarding academy at 9 pm?

*It couldn't be.*

I couldn't stand it and opened my mouth.

"Stop it."

– What are you talking about?

"Stop it, Shin Jaehyeon."

– ...!

I'd avoided calling him by his stage name just in case, but his reaction confirmed I was right.

There was a brief silence over the phone, but then a voice mingled with laughter was heard.

– So it really is you, Hubaenim.

"...!"

– Hmm... Judging by your reaction, it seems you know me. How are you managing to occupy both bodies?

The quicker he caught on, the more annoying he was. How had he managed to deduce this absurd situation so quickly...? I decided to question him first.

"How did you get this number?"

– You came to the VTIC fansign.

"You've probably had autograph signings thousands of times, so don't make ridiculous excuses."

It could hardly have been his first fansign for a brand he modeled for. How had he managed to identify a guy who'd gotten an autograph without even giving his name, more than a year ago at that?

But the voice was calm.

– Well, you made quite an impression. Besides, it's not common for someone to change their name twice and end up asking for a nickname.

"What?"

He remembered the situation in detail and explained it.

I could practically hear Park Moondae stammering in my head.

*Park Moon... No, Ryu Gunwoo... Oh, sorry. Please just write Keundal!*

– He told me both of your names, Hubaenim.

– Wouldn't it have been weird if I didn't know? I remembered him immediately when I heard your description.

"......"

*Wait...*

– I thought you told me about him on purpose... Wasn't that it?

*Damn it...*

I bit back a curse and suppressed my anger. 'Park Moondae' didn't know anything, and cursing at him wouldn't change the past.

He wasn't even here anymore, and I was left circling back to the same fundamental question.

*Where did this guy go?*

I had to say one thing, though.

"That was not me."

– Even the nickname was Keundal. That's a pretty funny excuse.

"It isn't an excuse. That wasn't me."

I pressed my throbbing head.

"That was the real Park Moondae. Not me."

Cheongryeo was silent for a while over the phone, then spoke.

– I think we need to talk.

***

After an hour and a half...

I came face-to-face with him and his dog at a cafe converted from a house on the outskirts of Seoul.

It was the same place we'd met last time, and he had the same little dog with him. It felt like déjà vu.

*Is he testing me one more time to see if I'm the right person?*

*His brain never stops, does it?* I sat across from him and spoke.

"I see you come here often."

"Sometimes, when I have time."

Cheongryeo shrugged as he stroked the dog, who was chewing on a ball. He didn't look suspicious.

I held back a sigh.

I felt a bit uneasy about being alone with this bastard again, but he'd been through the same strange situation I had. Talking to him would be better than nothing.

In the meantime, he examined my appearance and nodded his head.

"What should I say... It's a look that matches your personality."

It sounded like an insult, but I didn't have the time or resources to rise to the bait as he wanted, so I let it pass.

I nodded, and he changed the question.

"And it's quite interesting. Do you go through life changing bodies? But whenever you were promoting in Park Moondae's body, you always seemed to be you, Hubaenim."

"I'd like to ask you when you picked up voice phishing."

"That's not fair. Voice phishing? I never lied."

Cheongryeo put something on the table.

"You left this behind."

"...!"

That... It was a compact camera. Brand new, unopened packaging.

And the advice I gave to 'Park Moondae' came to mind.

*– If you're interested in that sort of thing, why don't you buy a camera and try making your own fancams?*

*– After the exam is over, of course.*

*Did he buy it for motivation?*

I sighed, feeling an indescribably complex emotion. Cheongryeo smiled.

"I tracked down the sightings and the boarding academy came up, so I hired someone to look into it further. There's no scam."

"And why did you make the call yourself?"

"Of course I had to. You shouldn't leave the most important part to someone else."

He was a meticulous bastard.

Even after thinking through the plan he'd carried out to get me here, my assessment wasn't likely to change. 

Looking at what he said as soon as we agreed to meet...

*– Great. I'll send a car over to the officetel. How's that?*

I almost agreed involuntarily, then realized what that meant.

*– ...You already know the address.*

*– Haha. Procedures are important in human relationships.*

He knew how to play his cards well, befitting a guy who'd repeated the same life several times.

That was why I wasn't particularly happy about revealing my hand to him... But under the circumstances, I stood to gain more by doing so.

I decided to give him a little more credit for the cooperation he'd shown during the Gold 2 incident. 

"Listen."

I leaned back.

"I can *read* my missions in text form."

"For me, they've always appeared directly before my eyes as status abnormalities. Like messages in a game window."

A heavy silence followed.

Cheongryeo tilted his head and replied casually.

"Is that so?"

He didn't seem too surprised. I clicked my tongue.

"Was it obvious?"

"A little bit. But considering the balance, it's not hard to understand."

"Balance?"

"Look. In return for getting to restart, you get a mission... A status abnormality. If you gain one thing, another follows as a penalty."

Cheongryeo slowly drew a line with his finger across the table.

"Hubaenim, both your body and your job changed, right? That's a big penalty. It wouldn't be strange if that was why you were given extra help."

"......"

*I could see it from that angle too.*

Cheongryeo continued asking as if he was really curious.

"Hmm... You graduated from a prestigious university. Wasn't it a waste to give up that background?"

"I was preparing for the civil servant exam."

"Haha."

Cheongryeo's laugh was so gentle it sounded ominous.

"Were those messages also what told you that if you died, there'd be no second chance, Hubaenim?"

"...Yes."

"Hmm, maybe that could also be considered part of your penalty."

At this rate, he'd figure out my level-up system too. I held back a sigh.

Now that I'd told him this much, there was something I'd wanted to try with him before getting into the main topic.

"First... Repeat after me."

"Hmm?"

"...Say 'status window'."

He stared at me blankly, an expression I rarely saw on his face.

And burst into laughter.

"Hahahahaha!"

*Don't laugh, for fuck's sake.*

Saying it out loud in front of someone made me want to sink into the ground. I took off my glasses and pressed the space between my eyebrows.

"Hubaenim, you must have a rich imagination."

"Shut up."

"Actually, I've already tried it too."

Cheongryeo leaned forward.

"I tried all kinds of catchphrases and superstitions. Saying 'log out', wondering if I was in a coma, and so on... Hm, was that the ninth or tenth time? I don't remember."

"......"

I grimaced.

"How many times do you think I've done that? Nothing works."

Cheongryeo smiled.

"Nothing."

"......"

"So... Well, shall I say 'status window' anyway?"

"Forget it."

I rolled my eyes. After hesitating, I finally spat out the next words.

"Good job."

"......"

I didn't know why I'd said something so useless.

*...Had helping 'Park Moondae' study turned it into a habit?*

This bastard would probably say something like 'Will you give me a song instead of words?' instead of thanking me...

"...Thank you."

"......"

I didn't know what to say to that.

"So, can I hear about your current situation, Hubaenim?"

"Yes."

I ignored the dog sitting at my feet and finally got to the point.

I gave him a quick summary of how my status abnormalities had ended, how I'd received a mission I'd created myself, and how things had progressed up to now.

His eyes narrowed slightly at the part about me receiving a mission I'd created myself. He made the occasional comment throughout my explanation.

None of them were very useful.

"It is strange."

"What?"

*It wasn't just one or two strange things. Could he be a little more specific?*

Cheongryeo tapped his fingers on the table.

"Look. Whether it was you or me, Hubaenim, whenever either of us restarted, the previous timeline was erased."

"Yes."

"But this time, the two of you exist together on the same timeline and influence each other. Something completely different is happening."

"What do you mean?"

"I wonder if there is another force at work. It seems that a time paradox also occurred in the process, and hm, that's interesting."

Whether this bastard found it interesting or not wasn't going to help me. What mattered was what would come of this situation.

"Anyway, after the status abnormality that required passing the exam was fulfilled, I woke up in this body, and 'Park Moondae' was gone."

"Oh, the guy who was sharing this body with you?"

"Yes."

I had to scrape together any hint of what the hell was going on. 

"I'd like to ask if anyone ever suddenly disappeared during one of your restarts."

"No. It was always the same."

He replied immediately, without having to think about it.

Then he added with a puzzled expression.

"However... Is it that important?"

*What?*

"From the sound of things, you'll probably return to your original body after today, Hubaenim. Wouldn't he come back to this body on his own after that?"

"That might not be the case."

"Even if that isn't the case, there's nothing to complain about. Are you worried that your original body will disappear?"

"Wait..."

What was this madman talking about?

"I'm just trying to save you the time you'd waste on ethical concerns. Think carefully. Hubaenim, that... What if 'Keundal' asks you to give his original body back?"

"......"

I swallowed a mouthful of the coffee, which had gone cold, and set the cup on the table.

"If he wants that, I'll have to think of something."

"There's nothing to think about."

"I have something in mind."

I had no intention of telling this guy, but I had something in mind.

*Another mission.*

I could get one more mission and request a 'body change' as the reward. 

"Park Moondae's body belongs to him, so he has the right to demand it back."

Regardless of what I decided to do, didn't he have a right to his own body? 

But the guy sitting across the table... He looked unimpressed.

"I think you're misunderstanding something."

"...!"

"Everything Park Moondae has was achieved by you, Hubaenim. It doesn't matter whose body it was. It was your mind that made everything possible."

"......"

"But why would you even consider giving that to him? The right thing to do is to avoid getting involved with him any further..."

"I'm not making plans. I'm just leaving my options open..."

"Why are you keeping that option open then?"

"......"

I stared across the table at him.

"Listen closely. Let me describe everything you've achieved so far, Hubaenim."

His fingers started tapping the table again.

"You saved someone who didn't exist before using your own body, helped him study, and made a plan so that he could survive until now. Well, morally speaking, I think you've done more than enough."

"......"

"I'm not telling you to kill him. Just... What's wrong with leaving him as he is?"

Cheongryeo met my eyes.

"Don't you think so?"

I clenched my fists.
