# Chapter 328

Certainly, I had met Bae Sejin in the body of Ryu Gunwoo.

*All I did was cheer him on in front of the sink to avoid the situation.*

There were times when I doubted whether Bae Sejin remembered the experience vividly.

However, I never suspected that he would remember what Ryu Gunwoo looked like. 

There must be tens of thousands of fans who had seen this guy's face, but he remembered the face of a fan he'd only seen once? The one who even wore a hat?

However, if he pointed with his finger and exclaimed, it meant that he was saying, 'I know him'. There was no doubt about it.

*Hoo.*

Still, I guess he couldn't hear the bizarre situation of 'Ryu Gunwoo' calling me hyung because I was wearing earphones. Good.

"...!"

*Ah. Was it like that?*

Right. I remembered now. Bae Sejin accompanied me when I checked Ryu Chungwoo's video.

He must have remembered after watching it carefully several times back then. 

...Or he remembered doing crazy things like breaking into a co-worker's home on a holiday night and rummaging through their family videos.

*Even so, he has good eyesight.*

The video was filmed when I was quite young, and the content inside had changed, so the impression would be different, but he still managed to find out.

***– Excuse me... Shall I say hello?***

No, just stay still.

"Because there is someone in Chungwoo-hyung's family whom he contacted."

That... It looked like he didn't even know that Ryu Chungwoo and I had gone out that day. 

*Well, he's not Lee Sejin.*

Even though Cha Eugene was his roommate, he was the one who didn't come out of the room often.

In any case, the situation seemed to be entering a calming phase. I nodded and opened my mouth.

"I was going to tell you the news because hyung went with me to check Chungwoo-hyung's video. Thank you."

"That... No. What did I do? Anyway, that's good, uh... Tell me if there's anything I can help with..."

Bae Sejin spoke gibberish, but soon became more sociable. 

He said hello as soon as his eyes met Ryu Gunwoo on the screen.

"So this is you... Kuhm, hello."

***– Yes? Yes...!***

Even if he answered, I could only hear it in my earphones, so why was he saying hello?

***– Ah, even if I say this, he can't hear me over there, right?***

I guess he knew now.

"...What is he saying?"

"Wait a minute."

This was what happened anyway. It would be better if they exchanged greetings briefly and then hung up. 

I deliberately looked at the screen and spoke clearly.

"Hyung. We couldn't get the conversation across... If you don't mind, I'll unplug the earphones now."

It was meant to prepare.

***– ... I got it!***

His tone changed, probably because he thought he wouldn't mess up this time. And his expression changed... Was he copying me?

The guy composed himself and spoke in a firm voice. 

***– Hello. I am Ryu Gunwoo.***

It didn't suit him.

"Yes, um, I am Bae Sejin, who is in the same group as Moondae."

As soon as the conversation started, Bae Sejin returned to his true self, as if the first greeting had been a lie. He was quite shy.

Still, it was welcome that the atmosphere had calmed down. It was because the guy on the screen also greeted him almost excessively politely. 

It was a good sense of distance. They should keep it that way. 

***– Yes. I'm cheering for TeSTAR a lot. Congratulations on the grand prize.***

"...Thank you."

However, Bae Sejin answered a beat late, and with a strange expression, he moved away from the smartphone camera and whispered to me again. 

"...Does that person work in broadcasting?"

"...?"

What kind of nonsense was this?

"No. Why?"

"Just... Somehow, the face looks familiar..."

Bae Sejin stopped talking and stared blankly at the guy who was still imitating me on the screen. 

"......"

It was a very telling sign. 

I opened my mouth right away.

"Hyung, then I'll see you next time... "

"Wait a moment."

Bae Sejin raised his hand and brought up the words with an air of uncertainty.

"Have we met a few years ago? ... It was at a performance hall, and we talked about getting an autograph in front of the sink..."

"...!"

No, that was no surprise. I needed to calm down.

*He's not sure.*

I had to check if the details matched and whether he had figured it out. 

But the guy on the screen didn't do that at all. He could have answered 'I don't know what you're talking about' without much conscience...

***– Oh...! In the bathroom...***

"......"

Why was he so excited about that? 

*Is this because he experienced it while he was still the resonance?*

I watched the two excited guys with cold eyes.

On the screen, 'Ryu Gunwoo' reacted excessively, and thanks to this, Bae Sejin was immediately convinced.

"..!! I knew it!"

***– Wow, I was wondering if you would remember...***

"...Of course, I remember. I was really grateful for that."

Bae Sejin seemed to be a bit conflicted, but eventually added a word.

"A lot... It gave me a lot of strength. Thanks to that, I had a lot of good conversations."

***– Hmmmm, that's right.***

What was he proudly squinting his eyes at?

It was absurd, but he continued with a slightly excited tone.

***– I thought you would have been very happy to hear it...!***

"......"

Ryu Gunwoo was like that at the time? 

Don't tell me he was playing along with him... 

*Did he hear?*

I was speechless and looked at the screen.

Keundal was twitching the corners of his mouth with a happy face, perhaps forgetting to manage his expression. Bae Sejin seemed to be embarrassed and cleared his throat. 

"Hmm, yes. I am also happy to greet you like this. I couldn't even give you an autograph back then..."

"......"

Yes, well... It was good that this little thing had helped Bae Sejin this much. 

It wasn't a big deal, and it was a small joke, so it would be better to wrap it up here and move on rather than have him realize it later and make a fuss. 

I smiled.

"We will give you a signed album when the next album comes out."

"Oh, hmm, that will work."

***– I'm really looking forward to it...***

"That is an honor."

The atmosphere slightly relaxed. To be precise, Bae Sejin alone began to let go of his shyness.

*It looks like they're chatting like this.*

There might be loopholes in this conversation if they continued talking, so I ended the conversation this time.

"Hyung, I heard that we also have training tomorrow. I guess we wasted too much time."

***– ...! That's right. Sorry, I'll hang up now.***

"Ah, yes. Well... Yes. Thank you."

*Tiriling.*

"...He was a real fan of our group."

"I guess so. We don't remember the faces of every person we meet."

"Still... It's interesting. You and I both got help from the same person... I didn't know that we could have something in common like this. I also think that human connection is real."

Because it would be an affiliate label.

It was a literal transfer.
