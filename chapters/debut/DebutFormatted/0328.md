# Chapter 328 - Independent Label, III

I'd met Bae Sejin once before while I was in Ryu Gunwoo's body.

*All I did was cheer him on in front of the sink.*

I'd suspected that the experience might have left a deep impression on Bae Sejin.

However, I'd never considered that he might remember what Ryu Gunwoo looked like.

He must've seen tens of thousands of fans by now, but he remembered the face of a fan he'd only met once? One who'd even pulled his hat down over part of his face?

But the way he pointed and exclaimed made it obvious that he remembered. There was no doubt about it.

*Hoo.*

Still, since I was wearing earphones, he wouldn't have heard 'Ryu Gunwoo' calling me hyung, at least. *Good.*

As soon as I figured out what was going on, I immediately opened my mouth. Naturally.

I spoke to the guy in my smartphone.

"Hyung, I'll call you back."

***– Uh...? Oh, okay...***

I couldn't give Bae Sejin time to gather himself. I'd nip any weird ideas in the bud and try to salvage the situation.

However, Bae Sejin moved first. He grabbed my arm and whispered.

"You... You found him!"

"...!"

"That... Is that the guy from the video?"

At that moment, I understood the situation. 

*Ah. So that's how he knows.*

Right. I remembered now. Bae Sejin had been there when I watched Ryu Chungwoo's home videos.

He must have remembered Ryu Gunwoo's face after watching them carefully several times back then.

...Or maybe he remembered how I'd done something crazy like showing up unannounced at a co-worker's home on a holiday night and rummaging through their family videos.

I barely answered.

"...Yes. That's him."

"I knew it!"

Bae Sejin looked at 'Ryu Gunwoo' on the screen with recognition.

*Even so, he has a good eye.*

The video was filmed when I was quite young, and the person inhabiting the body had changed, so his appearance would be slightly different. Yet he'd still managed to recognize him.

***– Excuse me... Shall I say hello?***

*No, just stay still.*

I glanced at the screen as Bae Sejin asked me a question.

"How did you find him?"

"A member of Chungwoo-hyung's family got in touch with him."

I offered a few more details to avoid raising suspicion that something strange was going on.

*I'll leave it here.*

"You remember when Chungwoo-hyung and I went out a few days ago, right? That's when I met him and exchanged contact information."

"Hmm, yes."

That... It looked like he didn't even know that Ryu Chungwoo and I had gone out that day. 

*Well, he's not Keun Sejin.*

Even though Cha Eugene was his roommate, he was the one who didn't come out of the room often.

In any case, the situation seemed to have settled down. I nodded and opened my mouth.

"I was going to tell you the news because hyung went with me to check the home videos at Chungwoo-hyung's place. Thank you."

"That... No. What did I do? Anyway, that's good, uh... Tell me if there's anything I can help with..."

Bae Sejin murmured, but then he did something unexpectedly sociable.

He said hello as soon as his eyes met Ryu Gunwoo on the screen.

"So this is your... Kuhm, hello."

***– Huh? Oh, hello.***

Why was Keundal greeting Bae Sejin back? He knew I was wearing earphones.

***– Ah, even if I say this, he can't hear me over there, right?***

*So you finally figured it out?*

"...What is he saying?"

"Wait a minute."

Since we'd come this far, I figured it'd only be natural to let them exchange greetings briefly.

I deliberately looked at the screen and spoke clearly.

"Hyung. He can't hear you... If you don't mind, I'll put you on speaker now."

It was a heads-up for him.

***– ...Sure!***

His tone changed immediately, probably because he didn't want to mess up this time. His expression changed, too.

*Wait, is he copying me?*

The guy composed himself and spoke in a firm voice. 

***– Hello. I am Ryu Gunwoo.***

It didn't suit him.

"Yes... Um, I'm Bae Sejin. I'm in the same group as Moondae."

As soon as the conversation started, Bae Sejin reverted to his introverted self, as if the first greeting had been a lie.

Still, I was relieved. The guy on the screen was also being almost excessively polite.

*Good. They should keep that distance.*

***– Yes. I'm a big fan of TeSTAR, by the way. Congratulations on the grand prize!***

"...Thank you."

However, Bae Sejin answered a beat late, and with a strange expression, he moved away from the smartphone camera and whispered to me again. 

"...Does he work in entertainment broadcasting?"

"...?"

Where did that come from?

"No. Why?"

"Just... I feel like I've seen him somewhere before..."

Bae Sejin stopped talking and stared blankly at the guy who was still imitating me on the screen. 

"......"

*That can't be a good sign.*

I opened my mouth right away.

"Hyung, then I'll talk to you next time—"

"Wait a moment."

Bae Sejin raised his hand and spoke uncertainly.

"Have we met before, a few years ago? ...It was at a concert hall, and we talked about getting an autograph in front of the bathroom sink..."

"...!"

*There's nothing to be too worried about. Calm down.*

*He's not sure.*

The situation must've reminded him of that encounter, and he was just trying to confirm whether he was right.

And it wasn't the guy on the screen who'd met him, anyway. He could've answered 'I don't know what you're talking about' without any trouble...

***– Oh...! You mean in that bathroom at...***

"......"

*Hey, you weren't supposed to act like you remember.*

*Do you remember it too because you were still tagging along as the resonance back then?*

I watched the two excited guys with cold eyes.

On the screen, 'Ryu Gunwoo' reacted far too warmly, and thanks to this, Bae Sejin was immediately convinced.

"..!! I knew it!"

***– Wow, I didn't think you'd remember...***

"...Of course, I remember. I was really grateful for what you said back then."

Bae Sejin seemed to be a bit conflicted, but eventually added a word.

"A lot... It gave me a lot of strength. Thanks to that, I had a lot of good conversations."

***– Ahem, I see.***

*What are you looking so proud of yourself for?*

I was dumbfounded, but he continued in a slightly excited tone.

***– I think past me would be very happy to hear that...!***

"......"

*Past me?*

*Don't tell me he steered the conversation this way on purpose...*

*Did he want me to hear that?*

I was speechless and looked at the screen.

Keundal smiled proudly, obviously forgetting that he was trying to act like me. Bae Sejin seemed embarrassed and cleared his throat.

"Hmm, yes. I'm glad I got the chance to talk to you like this. I couldn't even give you an autograph back then..."

"......"

Yes, well... It was good that this little thing had helped Bae Sejin this much. 

It wasn't a big deal, just a small incident, so it would be better to wrap it up here while they were talking than have him realize it later and make a fuss.

I smiled.

"I said I'd give him a signed album when our next album comes out."

"Oh, hmm, that's a good idea."

***– I'm really looking forward to it...***

"That is an honor."

The atmosphere slightly relaxed. To be precise, Bae Sejin alone began to let go of his shyness.

*Looks like they could start making small talk at this rate.*

If they kept talking, something might slip out, so I finally brought the conversation to an end.

"Hyung, your training continues tomorrow, right? I must've taken up too much of your time."

***– ...! R-right! Sorry, I'll hang up now.***

"Ah, yes. Well... Yes. Thank you."

Keundal quickly recognized the signal this time, and the call ended like that.

*Tiriling.*

Staring at the dark call screen, Bae Sejin muttered with a strange expression.

"...So he really was a fan of our group."

"......"

Wouldn't it be weird if I didn't ask? 

"Do you two know each other?"

"It's not like we know each other... He said a few encouraging words to me as a fan... No, I only saw him for a few seconds, so I had no idea he was the person you were looking for! It's not that I kept him from you even though I knew you were looking for him..."

"I guess so. We don't remember the faces of every person we meet."

"R-right."

Bae Sejin sighed with a slightly relieved expression.

"Still... It's interesting. You and I both got help from the same person... I didn't expect us to have something like that in common. I guess human connections really are something."

"That's right."

"Ahem... It's a bit strange to hear that the person who said such a good thing to me in the beginning is still cheering for me..."

"It must be because hyung has been doing well since back then."

"Is, is that so? Hmmm, it's not to that extent... Thank you."

"Don't mention it."

Bae Sejin seemed to be getting excited now. 

"W-wouldn't it be nice to talk about this on variety shows? Right?"

"......"

*Let's stop there.*

"Or we could just keep it as a nice memory between us. More importantly, hyung, you said there was something urgent..."

"Ah, ah!"

Only then did the guy remember what he'd come to tell me, and his expression changed.

"That's right, the company got in touch with us...!"

*Hmm.*

*What kind of news would make Bae Sejin come running to find me?*

It wasn't like they were trying to reverse the declaration of surrender. I crossed my arms.

"The agency?"

"It's not our company... It's the company above it!"

Bae Sejin shouted with a slightly excited look. 

"T1 Entertainment got in touch with us directly!"

"...!!"

The parent company had intervened.

I followed Bae Sejin into his room. A few of the others were already there, as if Cha Eugene had been told to let them know, too.

"I came home!! As you warned, I got out quietly without being noticed by anyone on the A&R Team!"

"Well, yes."

When Kim Raebin, who'd been at the studio, was the last to join us, the briefing finally began.

"We got an offer."

"An offer?"

Ryu Chungwoo explained briefly.

T1 Entertainment sent an email directly without going through our agency. The opinions of the board of directors were sent directly through the person in charge who had discussed the dedicated team issue last time... 

I rubbed my chin as I listened, then summed it up in a single sentence.

"So... They want us to form a label that's directly affiliated with them."

"Yes."

Not under their subsidiary, T1 Stars, but as a direct affiliate of T1 Entertainment itself. 

*They're crazy.*

It was an incredibly generous offer. They'd even sent us a proposed change to the payment structure, along with an explanation of how much more we'd stand to gain.

"Then, what about our current company?"

"There was no mention of it."

*They'd be screwed, plain and simple. I suppose that's why they didn't mention the agency.*

Originally, even if we set up a label, only planning and activities would be carried out independently, while accounting, including sales processing, would still fall under T1 Stars.

*It meant that our sales would continue to be counted under this company.*
*Because we'd be a subsidiary label.*

However, if this happened, the relationship with the agency would come to an end.

It was a literal transfer.

We'd even be taking our entire dedicated management team with us, so the agency would take a massive hit to its business.

However, there was no need to worry about retaliation, as the parent company was backing us.

"If it goes like this... We can leave that company!"

"Oooh."

It was the perfect escape opportunity, something we could only have dreamed of.

It was great news, and some of the members were already clapping their hands...

*Hmm. It's not good.*

I raised an eyebrow.

This was extraordinarily generous treatment by anyone's standards. It was such a significant change in affiliation that anyone even slightly interested in the entertainment industry would conclude that TeSTAR was T1's most valuable asset.

And that was exactly the problem.

*We can't let our image become too closely tied to T1's.*

T1 was a large corporation that had spread its tentacles into all sorts of businesses, damaging its own image.

Even in the entertainment business, its aggressive K-pop clickbait strategy, represented by the TNet channel, had drawn a lot of criticism for 'ruining the industry's ecosystem'.

But giving people the impression that T1 was seriously backing us?

It'd be the worst if that image stuck to us.

*It's not just about benefiting from large companies. It's about becoming the main player in T1's entertainment business.*

Even at the Rookie of the Year award, there'd been a fuss about us abusing our connections to a major broadcasting company. There was a good chance we'd be lumped in with T1.

Whenever something happened from then on, we'd be painted as the powerful party or the perpetrators.

*That's not all.*

There was an even more troubling reason.

*...We shouldn't give people the impression that the company fully supports every decision we make.*

In business, things don't always go according to plan. Obviously, problems would arise.

It was the same with the idol business.

Then, when a problem arose, someone would have to take responsibility... If we started out with the company feeding us well and giving everyone the impression that it respected all our decisions, we'd end up taking all the aggro.

– T1 is T1

– Ah fuck, let my kids go

Instead of getting comments like these,

★$
– ㅋㅋㅋㅋ Guys, please do it properly.

– Whichever member planned these promotions needs to realize they don't have a brain and keep their hands off the planning next time, okay?
$★

The number of people saying this would increase. Absolutely not.

*It needs to look like T1's and TeSTAR's interests don't always align.*

The moment that distinction broke down, we could end up with an absurd situation where fans defended T1's mistakes because they associated the company with their idols, or blamed their idols right alongside T1.

It would be a disadvantage in the long run.

"......"

"What do you all think? I... I think it's okay!"

"M-me too!"

The problem was, I didn't know if I could convince these guys, who were already excited, just by explaining my reasoning. 

*Honestly, the reason is a bit dirty.*

Wasn't it a bit much to say that we needed to leave room for people to blame the company for things like that?

I could think of at least three guys who wouldn't be persuaded by those words.

*...It would be perfect if I had an emotional appeal.*

At that time, Kim Raebin quietly muttered in a glum voice next to me.

"Then, we'll have to part ways with some of the members of the A&R Team..."

"...!"

*This bastard... He knew just what to say.*

*This was it.*

"Um, can't we just bring them all?"

"That will be difficult. I don't think the agency will let them go so easily."

I answered as deliberately and firmly as I could.

"Losing our dedicated management team alone would be a huge blow to the agency, so it would be difficult to get anyone else out."

"Umm..."

"W-well, then... What about those who can't go with us?" Kim Raebin asked.

"They will be working in a more difficult environment than before."

Kim Raebin's face turned pale. I crossed my arms.

"But we don't have to be forced to stay for them. Because everyone lives their own lives."

"Yes..."

It was then. Seon Ahyeon carefully raised his hand.

"Th-then..."

"Yes?"

"Then why don't we just stay here and drive out all the bad guys? I-is that possible...?"

I answered calmly.

"Yes, it would be."

"Y-yeah. I suppose..."

It was then. Ryu Chungwoo opened his mouth.

"By the way, we'll be able to stop them from doing bad things in the future, right?"

"...!"

"Right?"

To my surprise, this guy had backed me up, too. I nodded.

"That works."

"Oh~ I like that. Let's fight!"

Cha Eugene immediately changed his stance. That was just like him. And Bae Sejin mumbled a few words.

"Not bad..."

*Yes. I thought he'd like that, too.*

"Then, shall we consider this option as well?"

I got a pretty good response.

*Okay.*

I intertwined my fingers.

"Then, until everyone's convinced one way or the other... Let's wait and see."

"Wait?"

"Since the negotiations have already come this far, I think we can afford to wait."

I turned to the guy sitting next to me.

Keun Sejin.

"As he said last time, once we win a few more grand prizes, we'll have more say."

"...!"

In other words, until those guys were at their most restless.

Keun Sejin opened his eyes wide, then smiled.

I laughed along too.

"Let's wait until then," Keun Sejin said.

After that, TeSTAR received another 32 award trophies during the awards season at the beginning of the year.

And five of them were grand prize trophies.

Only then did we set the deal we wanted in motion.
