"You eat them."

"N-no! I'm okay."

Seon Ahyeon put the box of cherry tomatoes back into the refrigerator.

I looked at him without much thought and checked the current time on the screen on the refrigerator door.

"I think you should go within an hour."

"H-huh?... Yeah."

Yes. Seon Ahyeon had a solo practice schedule these days.

This was because there would be a special collaboration stage at the MBS Song Festival on December 31st.

It was obvious, but it was a stage where the broadcasting company pressed the agencies and somehow picked out members of popular groups one by one and put them together.

The participants all had extremely busy schedules, so they rarely saw each other. They'd already been assigned their individual parts and sent footage to learn them from, though.

*It only took him three hours.*

Guys who danced well had the advantage. To be honest, it was true that learning choreography took more energy than learning songs.

*He has talent.*

Even the style of the movements was not the style of modern dance, which was his major.

*...This quarter, our payouts will be cut in half at least.*
