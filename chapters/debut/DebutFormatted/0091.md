# Chapter 91 - ⟨Meet and Greet⟩, I

⟨Meet and Greet⟩’s preview video began by showing past guests.

After showing the appearances of huge celebrities, it showed the new guest at the climax.

+=
[This guest is...]
=+
+=
**[VTIC!]**
=+

Behind the caption, VTIC’s numerous records and activities passed. And one of the members was zoomed in. It was a famous scene of them receiving an award and giving their thanks at an international award ceremony.

+=
[VTIC’s leader,\
Cheongryeo]

[#yWho does he want to be friends with?y#]
=+

Soon after, a new screen appeared.

+=
[One day in July 202X]
=+

[Cheongryeo: Hello, I’m VTIC’s Cheongryeo.]

It was a pre-meeting video featuring Cheongryeo in plain clothes.

[Q: What are the criteria for selecting the person you want to be friends with?]

[Cheongryeo: I guess... A fellow singer? (*Laughs*)]

[Cheongryeo: Actually, I’ve been focusing on my activities, so I’ve rarely met fellow singers.]

A small caption saying ‘A Workaholic’s Lamentation’ appeared and disappeared.

[Q: If you can only ask for one person?]

[Cheongryeo: It’s difficult. (*Laughs*)]

[Cheongryeo: Actually, there’s someone I’ve been hoping to talk to lately.]

[Cheongryeo: I think he’s kind of similar to me.]

Cheongryeo’s voice that came out next was covered with a *‘beep-’* sound.

The shape of his mouth was also obscured, so no clues could be gathered from it.

But the next moment, almost everyone could guess who Cheongryeo was choosing.

It was because only some part of ⟨Idol Inc.⟩’s ‘Shining Star’ performance was zoomed in and broadcast in blurry quality.

It meant that since it all was leaked in the article anyway, they were openly giving a hint.

+=
[Who is the ‘similar fellow singer’ pointed out by Cheongryeo?]

[It will be revealed #ynext weeky# in **#y⟨Meet and Greet⟩y#**!]
=+

And the comments were already occupied with spoilers.

The best comments were basically like these.

It was another story if you arranged it by the latest order or went to SNS, but at any rate, the expectations of the general viewers were high.

It was rare for male idols to have this kind of public appeal. These two, neither of whom had appeared alone on a variety show before, were about to appear together on a public TV show.

And for one of the two, it was his first appearance on a public TV variety show, so the fans were completely excited.

There were definitely lingering conflicts caused by VTIC’s overlapping activities and dissatisfaction from fans of TeSTAR’s other members.

But they were a minority. It was because even TeSTAR’s individual fans wanted someone to break into public TV variety shows.

It was the same even for the most malicious solo fans.

★$
– He was picked out and not randomly put in by the agency, so I am not going to curse... In the first place, it’s uncomfortable to see Gommeo winning first place thanks to his tragic backstory and still benefiting from itㅎ

– I’m not interested in Gommeo’s fucking entertainment, so I’ll mute it but they only have a group account, so I have to keep watching

$★

Negative public opinion subsided quietly after they only said this much under the surface.

★$
– Ah~ I hope it’s next Friday soonㅠㅠ
$★

Overall, this was the prevailing opinion across celebrity fan communities.

And now, the subject of this excitement was entering the filming set, filled with distrust.

***

*What is that bastard up to?*

Suddenly, without discussing it beforehand, he pointed out Park Moondae as a guest on a variety show?

He kept sending me overbearing messages, but he hadn’t said a word about this variety show. It meant that he hadn’t intended to tell me personally in the first place.

*He’s trying to screw me over, isn’t he?*

However, there was no way the company would be okay with canceling my appearance on a Friday night public TV variety show that had good ratings.

Thanks to this, I was currently approaching the filming location after saying hello to the staff.

“...”

I saw a huge wooden building in front of me.

*It brings back bad memories.*

Why did it have to be wood? I held back my sigh and knocked on the door.

“Is there anyone inside?”

*#^#Thump#^# #^#thump.#^#*

When I knocked hard on the door, the door opened slightly.

“It must be cold, come in quickly.”

“...Thank you.”

Because of this shit midwinter setting, I almost died from the heat trying to wear a padded jacket in the summer.

*Since the originally invited guest gets to choose, this bastard probably decided the setting.*

It didn’t matter to him because he was indoors from the beginning.

I swallowed the curse that rose up to my throat and entered the building.

Inside... It was a typical mountain cabin. It wasn’t like a pension, but it looked like a simple facility for hikers to stay for a while.

*...Is it time to say the lines?*

I muttered as I set my bag down in the corner.

“I was surprised by the sudden snowstorm, but it’s fortunate that there is a mountain cabin.”

“I see. I also quickly came in after seeing the weather.”

Yes. Currently, the default setting was ‘stranded in a mountain cabin during a snowstorm’.

The reason for this absurd setting was obvious.

It was a feature of this program.

⟨Meet and Greet⟩ was a program that cast a celebrity and let them spend time with the celebrity they pointed out.

However, since healing content like this was common, they added fresh elements.

An extreme situation.

The default was always a setting where the two guests were isolated in an extreme situation and had to survive on their own.

Of course, it wasn’t real; it was just some kind of skit. To the point that it went back and forth between gag and immersion.

For reference, the pilot set in an apartment building during a zombie apocalypse was a big hit.

Youngrin picked out an actor from Marshmallow, and the two of them appeared together; it was quite interesting when I looked at some clips.

*The problem is that I’m on the show.*

With an idol group leader I couldn’t figure out, whose group had sold 1.8 million copies in its first week.

*Let’s not show weakness.*

The goal was to finish filming safely and leave, as if dealing with a bad-tempered senior.

“It’s rare to go hiking in this weather, so an encounter like this is amazing. Ah, would you like some coffee?”

Cheongryeo smiled and lifted a thermos bottle. His other hand held an old and battered coffee cup, perhaps one he was meant to have found in the cabin.

“Thank you.”

I accepted the coffee cup offered by Cheongryeo with both hands. It held a generous serving of hot Americano.

I was about to die from the heat, and now I had a hot Americano in my hands...?

“...”

I pretended to drink it and put it down.

Fortunately, the air conditioning was cranked up inside, so it was gradually bearable even with winter clothes.

*It’s my turn to offer something.*

I opened my mouth.

“I’ve received the coffee, so if you don’t mind, can I treat you to dinner? I brought some non-perishable food.”

“That’s great. Um... The snowstorm is still strong. It looks like we will have to wait a long time, so we should eat well.”

I wondered what this was all about as I glanced out the window that was blocked by the green screen.

*...What can I say?*

I did this job for the money. Let’s not think about useless things.

I approached the backpack I had left in the corner and took out the non-perishable food.

I didn’t pack this up; it was prepared by the production team...

The label on one of the bags caught my eye.

@ll@[Ready-made #rBoneless Chicken Feetr# _Rice Bowl_]@ll@

I froze.

Let’s pick another one. As for the chicken feet... This gag was overused.

“Thank you for the food. It was great.”

“Don’t mention it.”

The meal passed with a light skit about why we had come hiking today.

“I’ll clean it up.”

“...No! We have to do this together.”

Are you trying to make your Hubae look bad for letting his senior clean up after him?

As we cleared away the remaining plastic packets and containers, we heard a loud rumble outside.

*#^#Rumble!!#^#*

They were actually playing the sound outside? I was honestly surprised.

“Whoa!”

“Is it thunder?”

Cheongryeo frowned slightly and went to the cabin door to try to open it.

But the door didn’t open.

“...I guess that sound earlier was a landslide. It looks like there’s snow blocking the door.”

“I’ll call the police for now.”

I picked up an old flip phone. And I pretended to be ‘the person who called the police, but heard that they couldn’t come today because the snow was piled too high’.

“Well, that’s not good. Then let’s hold out in this cabin for today.”

“Yes. I guess I’m lucky I’m not alone...”

“Likewise, I feel lucky to have company.”

With this, the scripted setting was over.

The production team had explained that from here, we could talk freely as long as we kept a few points about the setting and our roles in mind.

So we pretended to look for the heater and lock the windows, feeling silly, then sat down in the living room again.

“The heater doesn’t seem to be working. We don’t know what might happen, so we’ll have to stay up all night.”

The production team wanted an all-night conversation to build the atmosphere for the show.

*...What should I do?*

Given the format of this program, I supposed it was time for the kind of honest chat that wouldn’t feel odd between ‘strangers.’

*If I start by asking for advice as a rookie, will that work for the show?*

After pondering about it, I tried my luck.

“Come to think of it, how did you end up hiking today?”

“I wanted to organize my thoughts, but I only had the time today, so it ended up like this. *Haha.* Um, actually, I am a singer.”

“Yes?”

As if embarrassed, Cheongryeo cleared his throat and continued.

“I am from an idol group called VTIC. I don’t know if you’ve heard of it.”

Look at this hideous setting.

I gave a reaction like, ‘Of course, I’ve heard of you. I’m a fan,’ and told my story.

“Me too... I debuted recently.”

“Really? So you’re my junior. What a coincidence.”

“I think so too.”

It was a setup with barely a pinhole’s worth of realism.

I was about to start a serious conversation, but Cheongryeo, who was rubbing his chin, suddenly said:

“Um... We happen to have the same job, and we’re not supposed to sleep... It reminds me of a story.”

“Yes?”

“Would you like to hear it?”

Could I say no in this situation?

“Yes, I will listen.”

“Haha, it’s nothing serious... It’s just small talk.”

Cheongryeo lightly tapped the wooden floor with a finger.

“Do you know of the idol ghost story going around? I heard it recently.”

“...A ghost story? An Idol ghost story?”

“Yes.”

...I didn’t know what kind of skit he was going for, but let’s just accept it.

“What is it about?”

“Um, as for what it is about...”

Cheongryeo grinned.

“There was an idol who suddenly found himself back in the past.”

I froze.

“The idol was surprised, but this time he tried hard to fix his past mistakes and become successful without any trouble... But what’s this? He kept dying in accidents and going back to the past!”

“...”

“So, the idol trapped forever in the past had become a ghost. He haunted recording booths, inserting his voice into the tracks of idols who seemed likely to succeed. He couldn’t let go of his own lost chance at success.”

Cheongryeo crossed his arms.

“What do you think?”
