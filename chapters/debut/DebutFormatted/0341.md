"Oh~ This one's fun. Why doesn't Raebin post something where he's copying this meme?"

*It'd be nice if we could wrap it up at just the right point.*

I shrugged.

If possible, I was thinking of winning over the Korean public this way and turning it into an amusing anecdote when we went to America.

Wasn't it perfect to talk about on a talk show? It was also related to the movie.

Some foreign online personalities got to it before we could use it ourselves.

*Not bad for an opening in the US market.*

If people kept insisting that we were an exception, it'd invite hostility from other K-pop fans, but it hadn't reached that point yet, so I let it pass.

If it'd ended here, on such a pleasant note, I could've shared the whole thing as a fun little anecdote on a talk show.

People who'd been curious about the amazing band in the movie, but hadn't bothered to find out more, were now very impressed.

The algorithm led them to TeSTAR's performances, videos about the plagiarism incident, and our game collaboration trailers.

*That's not really us.*

But I intended to use it well. I nodded.

9th place.

Of course, I'd heard that a movie OST topped the Billboard charts every few years, but I never expected us to rank in the single digits.

*I didn't expect that.*

"Hyung! Kim Raebin's about to pass out!" Eugene said.

"No, I'm not... However, I'm not sure a song that was produced in four days without much deliberation, and for which I even made the mistake of reusing a sample, deserves this ranking..."

"Okay, never mind. Kim Raebin's fine."

*What a ruckus.* And it was an achievement worthy of that reaction.

Amid the members' screams, as if they'd all gone insane, I lowered my voice and asked Ryu Chungwoo.

"This wasn't even released on Friday, was it?"

"No, it wasn't."

It was unbelievable that we'd climbed this high even without a full week's worth of streaming.

I checked the analytics.

*The on-air score is fine too.*

It was a song by a group few people even knew, but it seemed a wide variety of radio stations had played it. *Long live big movies, I guess.*

The U.S. market, now that its barriers to entry had been lowered, was a gold mine.

The operation was a success.

"Good."

"Sh-should we post a thank-you note...?"

"Let's do it~! Share the Billboard posts!"

I nodded as I watched the excited members.

*Before this energy and motivation dies down...*

"And we have to work on our next album."

"Ah."

With the tide coming in like this, it'd be a waste not to get the boat moving.

*We'll have to cement our reputation with the next album before the Limestone hype wears off.*

I cracked my neck. Beside me, Ryu Chungwoo spoke gently.

"Okay. These are even better results than what we'd hoped for when we discussed the plan at the last camp. Good work, everyone... Let's make another great album."

The guys nodded eagerly. I guess seeing the actual results was a great motivator.

"Yes, yes...!"

"We'll do our best. We'll make songs that both those newly interested in the movie OST and our existing fans can enjoy!" Kim Raebin said.

"Good! Let's go, TeSTAR~" Keun Sejin said.

"Well, shall we start right after eating?"

Riding the high of their accomplishment, these guys planned an intense camp schedule on their own, like students preparing for an exam. It was a good attitude.

And... I was thankful.

I raised my eyebrows, recalling the situation that applied only to me.

Achieving the mission of *'Set A New K-pop Record'*.

*...The time I have left is disappearing faster than I thought.*

According to Keundal himself, he could only stay in Ryu Gunwoo's body until August.

That left me only four months. I had to complete the mission before then.

If everything went according to plan, there were plenty of possibilities...

[Hyung, I've been loving Black Hole[1]{T/N: Reference to Cha Eugene's character trait, "Black Hole".}. Really ᅲᅲ But did you make it with Cha Eugene in mind? The phrasing reminded me of him!]

...In the meantime, Keundal wasn't pressing me or even asking any questions, which left a bitter taste in my mouth.

Did he not have any hope at all? I clicked my tongue.

*I'll make sure you live longer, no matter what I have to do.*

"Come on, let's eat and get our laptops out right away~" Keun Sejin said.

So we decided to spend all seven days before the US tour working on the title track and concept.

...The problem, however, was that even the best-laid plans could go awry.
