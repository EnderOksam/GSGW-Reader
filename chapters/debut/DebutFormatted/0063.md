# Chapter 63 - Finals Announcement, III

“...I will recruit a new team member.”

“Ah! Participant Park Moondae has made his choice!”

At the MC’s shout, the prize money disappeared from the electronic display and the words ⟨Recruit a new member!⟩ expanded to fill it.

The audience cheering and the judges clapping in joy were shown on the other display screens.

I almost clenched my teeth.

*Thirty-nine million won...*

It flew away into the air.

Still, it was an inevitable choice in the long run.

Having one more participant meant that the number of fans who liked the group would also increase. And it was usually multiplied, not just added. The amount of money we could make would also change.

It was an investment that could earn me far more than 50,000 won in the long run.

The problem was what happened next.

“Then who is the last member chosen by participant Park Moondae?”

The rest of the participants were nervous again on the screen.

“The participant I choose is...”

No matter who I chose, it was the perfect chance to arouse resentment from the fans of the eliminated participants.

So I avoided it.

“The participant ranked 7th by the shareholders’ votes.”

“Ah!”

The murmurs grew louder. Most seemed pleased or relieved.

*...Even the production team is delighted.*

It was a little annoying.

“Participant Park Moondae left the choice to the shareholders!”

The MC kept glancing at the prompter as he eagerly continued the proceedings, and soon the participant in 7th place appeared on the display.

And the guy who made it...

“Congratulations, Lee Sejin!”

I almost choked on my own spit.

*How could he suddenly rise like that?*

Perhaps because he was well known to the public from his child actor days, he had received a lot of text votes during the final episodes.

*...It can’t be helped.*

The milk was already spilled. Trying to hide my shock, I made way for Lee Sejin as he climbed up to the podium.

“...Thank you.”

Lee Sejin barely managed a few more words of thanks before bowing his head. His fans could defend him by saying he had been too shocked by the unexpected situation to speak.

The MC guided Lee Sejin to join the seats for those who had passed. Lee Sejin brushed past me and murmured softly.

“...Thank you.”

I just nodded. You must have wanted to join.

*I want to go there, too.*

It felt like my legs were going to cramp.

How long are you going to keep me standing?

However, the MC continued the process with me standing next to him.

“Now there’s only one thing left! The group name of the ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ participants who will debut!”

It was followed by the usual explanation that they had taken the shareholders’ online suggestions into account.

Then a dazzling logo appeared on the electronic display.

An upper case ‘T’ and a triangle were skillfully combined, and brilliant glitter effects burst from the sides and the top before settling into small diamond marks.

+=
Your investment verified.

Your idol, your star
=+
+=
TEST + STAR
=+

“The group name selected by the shareholders is... TeSTAR!”

“...”

The name had changed.

As far as I knew, the boy group that had debuted through this program in my previous life was called ‘STier’.

Looking back, I thought it would be that way again because it was listed as a candidate with an explanation attached as ‘Star + Tier’ on the homepage.

Maybe a different name came out because the members had changed quite a bit, including me.

It seemed the previous name had been decided at the last minute.

*...Isn’t STier better?*

I honestly didn’t expect a group name that would have star occupying more than half of the name.

I was a little confused, but... Well, as long as the group name wasn’t too weird.

“The seven members of TeSTAR will receive a trophy with the group logo engraved on it!”

Then Youngrin came up and handed me the trophy. There was only one trophy in the shape of a graph soaring into the sky.

*You set me up here to do this.*

I guess it would be over if I carried that and passed it on to the kids.

“Congratulations.”

“Thank you.”

After lightly shaking hands with Youngrin, I was handed the trophy and the cushion it rested on.

Youngrin looked at me with calm eyes and smiled.

“I hope you will do well.”

“Yes, thank you.”

I murmured a series of thanks.

Then, just like that, I walked toward the place where the winning participants were standing.

The winning participants had already stood up from their seats, probably drunk on the atmosphere.

“Wow!”

“They give us a trophy, too?”

I handed the trophy to the first guy who approached me without hesitation.

Then we gathered around the trophy, put our arms around each other’s shoulders, and started making a fuss.

*My shoulders hurt.*

My back ached where Cha Eugene and Keun Sejin had enthusiastically thumped me.

I felt sore all over, from head to toe, but thanks to the adrenaline, my brain was still functioning.

I heard the MC’s voice nearby.

“TeSTAR, congratulations on your debut!”

The stage fireworks effects exploded spectacularly. I listened to the cheers of the people as I stood among the winning participants gathered around the trophy.

And at that moment.

A status window popped up.

★-

You made your debut by winning a famous audition program!

!Time limit: Satisfactory (Perfect)

!Status Abnormality: #*‘Debut or Die’*# removed!

**} [!] Reveal the Truth}** ☜ Click!

*...Really?*

I turned on the status window. The status abnormality was gone. It was over.

Somehow it didn’t feel real.

Not knowing the purpose of the status window, I had been haunted by the ominous fear that the status abnormality might somehow be prolonged for some absurd reason.

*I never thought it would disappear so neatly.*

I blankly closed the status window. Then, the text at the bottom of the remaining pop-up caught my eye.

It was originally the location where the reward was supposed to be.

*...Wait.*

What’s this?

★-

}[!]Reveal the Truth} ☜ Click!
-★

The truth?

I reflexively clicked on the phrase.

“Moondae?”

For a moment, my vision was distorted.

And I blacked out.

* * *

A skinny high school student was lying on a mattress holding an old smartphone.

The mattress springs creaked every time he moved.

A WeTube video was playing on the smartphone’s cracked screen. The image quality was not good because it was barely connected to public Wi-Fi.

However, thanks to the filming skill of the cameraman, the charm of the singer on the screen still stood out.

*It was fun.*

The high school student, Park Moondae, looked wistfully at the screen after the video ended.

The latest fancam posted on this account was the ‘2X0412 Lilodawn Han River Water Festival’ video he had just watched.

This account, ‘gun1234’, was an account that mainly uploaded fancams of singers and idols.

In particular, it mainly uploaded videos of little-known performers, but even that seemed cool to him.

*It feels like this person recognizes their potential.*

He somehow felt good because it felt like the uploader was cheering for people who rarely got any recognition.

*I hope it uploads more often.*

There were few things he could do to enjoy himself without spending money.

It was the same on WeTube, where he could watch just by watching advertisements.

Few videos made his heart pound enough to keep him watching through the low quality and constant buffering on unreliable Wi-Fi.

He’d heard that there was no profit from fancams because all of the advertising revenue went to the singers, but he was grateful that they continued to upload.

After thinking it over, Park Moondae scrolled down and wrote a comment.

He knew that this account had never given any particular feedback to viewers until now.

Even when Youngrin’s fancam was a hit, they never left any comment.

But just in case they might see it, Park Moondae checked for spelling errors and pressed the post button.

└ This person is always present ㅋㅋ

└└ I guess they’re a big fan, huh?

He was a little embarrassed because there were a few reply comments.

He thought about replying, but suddenly didn’t feel like it, so he stopped.

*It’s not like I have anything to say...*

When he tried to talk about himself, he became speechless.

The house, no, the tiny room that could hardly be called home, was lonely.

There was nothing to say about his daily life.

When he recalled the last time he was motivated to do something, he remembered a time when everything was normal.

When his parents were still there.

It felt like it was just yesterday, but in fact, it had been years.

And for years, he had felt lost, wandering through a dark tunnel. Or maybe he was actually dreaming.

Maybe that’s why I am so focused on the videos uploaded by that account, the boy thought.

It was comforting to watch people singing and dancing passionately; he could feel a little satisfaction through them, despite his own hopeless situation.

*...Let’s sleep.*

Tomorrow was probably Monday.

Park Moondae slowly relaxed into the mattress and closed his eyes.

And time passed again. Life flowed past like a river, while Park Moondae alone remained still as a rock.

A terrible thing happened at school. He quit just like that. Quitting didn’t change anything.

Well, wouldn’t giving up on life be just as easy as quitting school?

*Maybe so.*

But he didn’t really want to die.

Park Moondae found part-time work, but it soon dried up.

Also, ‘gun1234’ stopped uploading videos.

*Why...?*

Park Moondae thought about it and eventually sent a note to the account. It was the day when he failed to find a new part-time job. Of course, he didn’t get a reply.

And soon after, all the videos in the account disappeared.

Only a short post remained on the empty page.

★$
[gun1234] -#@gun1234#-
:Subscribers 98k
I quit to play games ㅅㄱ See ya -#⟩#-

Game.

Park Moondae installed a mobile game. He had no money to buy a desktop or a game console.

However, his old smartphone couldn’t run mobile games properly. It overheated until it was too hot to hold and broke down.

For some reason, Park Moondae felt very tired.

He hadn’t done anything that day, so there should have been nothing to tire him out.

*I’m tired.*

He was tired of living.

Park Moondae disconnected his phone number. And he didn’t extend the lease on the old one-room apartment either.

He went out for a proper meal for the first time in ages. After that, the money he had left was just enough.

He could afford a motel room and sleeping pills, and then it would all be over.

*I heard a lot of people die in motels.*

It was too cold to try drowning or jumping, and he felt like he might give up halfway.

If he died in his room, he would be left to rot for at least a month.

*I’m sorry.*

Park Moondae took a room and inwardly apologized to the curt owner.

Then, after going into the room, he lay down on the bed and swallowed sleeping pills.

Death came in the guise of sleep.

Moments later.

The boy groaned and opened his eyes, gasping for breath on that dirty motel bed...

But it was me inside his body.

* * *

“Gasp!”

I let out a breath.

“A-Are you okay?”

Someone next to me tapped me on the back. When I turned my head, I saw Seon Ahyeon’s worried face looking down at me.

“...Sorry, I’m a bit tired.”

I barely managed to speak and straightened up.

Almost no time seemed to have passed. Thanks to that, I just looked like I had staggered for a moment.

Because I was surrounded by the winning participants, the others didn’t notice at all; the atmosphere was still the same.

I wiped the sweat from my forehead. My mind was a mess.

The owner of Park Moondae’s favorite video account, ‘gun1234’.

‘It’s me’.

It was the account that I deleted after I began studying for the civil service exam.
