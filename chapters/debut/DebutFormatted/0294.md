# Chapter 294 - Turning the Tables, II

This was the most important part.

*I need to hit him fast and give him no time to think.*

I took my smartphone out of my pocket and placed it in plain sight on the desk.

And just like this bastard did to me last time, I played the recording.

+$
– *Are you aiming for the grand prize?*

– *Aren't we all?*
$+

The audio was much clearer than Chae Seodam's recording.

"...!"

I'd recorded it myself, after all.

"You're not the only one who can make a recording."

I paused the recording and grinned at him.

"Did you think I'd get on my knees and beg you not to reveal it just because I spoke a little informally to my sunbaenim?"

"......"

"I could go on a variety show tomorrow and prove how close I am to that sunbaenim, and that would be that."

Chae Seodam seemed to be trying to understand what the hell was going on.

It wouldn't help his situation, but he instinctively searched for something to say to deny what was happening.

*Maybe I should wait for him to finish thinking.*

A few seconds later, words came tumbling out of his mouth.

"Your new song! How it got popular! You said it was all planned–"

*Oh, that.*

"Well, did I? I was just trying to move on from the subject... It's all a matter of context and how you explain it."

I picked up my smartphone again without another word.

I went to my contacts list and made a call.

★:
#><#*[VTIC Shin Cheongryeo sunbaenim]*#><#
📞 Calling...
:★

Tiriring, an acoustic version of VTIC's new song played for a while... and then it stopped.

*Click.*

– *Hello, Hubaenim.*

"Hello, sunbaenim. About what we talked about in the waiting room last time, would it be okay if I mentioned it on a variety show?"

Hearing his laughter over the phone annoyed me a little, but Cheongryeo answered just as we'd agreed.

– *Of course. Go ahead. I was the one who told you to speak informally anyway.*

"Thank you."

– *Don't mention it.*

I hung up.

"You heard him."

"......"

*Now, both points he thought were my weaknesses are gone.*

The idiot in front of me had suddenly grown docile, the malicious look in his eyes entirely gone.

*I guess his temper tantrums don't last very long.*

He had very strong self-preservation instincts.

I knew it was time to take the pressure up a notch.

So I quickly changed the subject.

"That should answer your question... Now, about the report of school bullying that I originally wanted to talk about."

*Now that I've gotten rid of my weaknesses, let's talk about yours.*

I said casually.

"Wouldn't it be quick and clean to just let the victim talk to a reporter?"

"...!!"

"Anyway, making it public would send a positive message, socially speaking. And since there's plenty of evidence, I think people will believe what they hear."

*Evidence.*

I was willing to bet this bastard was going over the conversation he'd had with Seon Ahyeon in his head right now.

I reached my conclusion.

"Even now, you're trying to record my voice. That shows you're still looking for other people to threaten with their weaknesses. That'll be the icing on the cake. I think we'll go ahead with it. That'll be all."

I picked up my smartphone and stood to leave. It was a gesture that made it clear I was done with the conversation.

"Uh, wait!"

Chae Seodam jerked to his feet in surprise as though someone had prodded him with a needle.

"W-wait. Why... why would you..."

*Okay, so you're asking why I'm telling you all these details? Well, thanks for asking.*

I answered with a faint smile.

"I'm telling you to give up."

"......"

"We're going to release everything. I thought the least I could do was notify you beforehand. Now all you have to do is wait."

I was simply notifying him of what was to come.

"......"

Chae Seodam froze in place.

It was a normal reaction. At this point, the average person would be overwhelmed.

His opponent had no intention of negotiating, his preparations had been proven useless, and now he'd been told his own weakness would be attacked without mercy.

Right now, he would feel cornered, with no way out.

Regret and distrust would fill his mind.

And this ego-bloated bastard would come to a conclusion different from the one most people would reach.

"Why are you doing this to me?"

He was shifting the blame onto others.

"What did I ever do to you? So I didn't get along with Seon Ahyeon when we were in school... And because of that, you're doing this to me? I don't... why would you?"

Chae Seodam started to ramble.

"You can't do this to me just based on Seon Ahyeon's words! You're trying to bury me unfairly before my career even begins..."

"Is that so?"

I spoke nonchalantly.

"So you're saying what you did to Seon Ahyeon was something anyone would have done under the circumstances, and yet you can't stand being on the receiving end?"

"......"

"You knew what you were doing to Seon Ahyeon. Just as you knew what you were doing when you recorded my conversation."

Chae Seodam looked as though I'd slapped him.

Bastards like him only began to regret their actions when the consequences caught up with them.

"If it were me, I'd be too embarrassed to say something like that. Wouldn't it be better to just accept what's happening?"

Of course, I said that knowing he wasn't the sort of person who'd accept it.

"But if this gets out, Seon Ahyeon will also lose out–"

Yeah, if he was the sort to feel remorse so quickly, we wouldn't have come this far.

I shrugged.

"It's what he wants to do. It's not my place to worry about that. Who cares?"

"...!!"

"Hey, it's not my reputation that'll be affected."

Chae Seodam closed his mouth.

At this point, he wouldn't be able to think anything else.

*This bastard is completely insane.*

*This guy's willing to do anything to crush me, even if it takes the whole group down with him.*

...That realization would really sink in.

What's more, his imagination would run wild about how far I was willing to go. He'd already be picturing hell.

He was probably thinking, *That bastard's preparing for my social death! I'll never recover from this!*

*Hmm.*

Of course, it was bullshit.

I wasn't an idiot. I'd have to be one to actually make all this public.

*It's just a threat.*

"You're the one who should take responsibility for your own actions, aren't you? He has proof, as you heard."

"B-but..."

The same went for this.

During our discussions, Seon Ahyeon had revealed that he'd recorded their conversations. But the audio quality was so low that any claim we made that the recordings contained Chae Seodam's voice wouldn't hold up.

The only thing left was the counseling records Seon Ahyeon had first mentioned, but making those public would likely start a messy fight as all kinds of public opinion latched onto them.

Well, that aside, this group's career had been eventful enough without dragging another private matter unrelated to our activities into it.

But Chae Seodam didn't know that.

"......"

He'd fallen for this massive bluff, hook, line and sinker.

He'd lost the will to fight and was panicking so badly that he couldn't even cry or get angry.

*I'd like to leave him like this for a week or two, but...*

But that could lead to unforeseen circumstances, so it was time to give him some hope to hang on to.

I tapped my smartphone once under the table.

It was time for the last player in this game to appear.

A few seconds later...

*Knock knock!*

At the same time as the sudden knock, the door burst open.

"Hy-hyungs."

Gold 2.

He looked like he had something urgent to talk about.

"...Kwon Heeseung."

Despite everything, Chae Seodam spun around and stared at him. Apparently, his brain was still churning through all the different possibilities.

But the moment Gold 2's next words came out, the atmosphere changed.

"I heard... at a meeting. Moondae-hyung, could you please reconsider making an official statement?"

"...Hmm."

I intentionally said no more, making it seem like I was conflicted.

"...!"

Then, a flash of light returned to Chae Seodam's eyes.

It was hope.

"Seodam hyung must be very sorry for what he's done. I-isn't that right, hyung?"

Chae Seodam nodded as if he were sincere. And he hurriedly opened his mouth.

"I-I'm sorry."

*He's realized he might have a chance.*

"Uh-huh."

"For recording your voice... and trying to do something with it. I was wrong. I misunderstood, you see..."

"Hmm..." I waited a while before answering.

"I don't think you would've apologized if things weren't like this. But that's beside the point."

"The situation has made Seodam-hyung realize what he did wrong. S-Seodam hyung, tell him what you've realized."

I could practically see Chae Seodam's brain going into overdrive at Gold 2's words.

In the end, he more or less repeated what I'd told him earlier.

"I... realized I shouldn't try to succeed by hurting other people... That it's wrong... Yes."

*Good.*

He'd finally said the words himself.

I didn't know how sincere he was, since he'd spent most of his life lying, but what mattered was that he'd said it with his own lips while in a state of shock.

Once he'd calmed down, he'd have to take that admission into account when he tried to justify his own actions to himself.

As the silence stretched on, Gold 2 fanned the flames again.

"Th-think about it, Moondae-hyung! If Seodam hyung is forced out of the group like this... Things might get out of hand... And our activities for this year might be ruined..."

"......"

I stared at Gold 2 in silence. He looked like he was about to break out into a cold sweat, and he was gasping for air.

*Gulp.*

I finally shrugged.

"Well... I don't want to do any harm to the company."

"...!"

As if holding back a sigh, I glanced at Chae Seodam and then nodded.

"But I'm only letting it go this once."

***

After that, it was simple.

Relieved, Chae Seodam immediately signed a contract and left the team voluntarily, with the encouragement of Gold 2.

*The nuance of the contract was also important.*

It wasn't Chae Seodam who was keeping our conversation a secret. We made it clear that we were the ones covering up his weaknesses.

In return for us burying Chae Seodam's attempt to spread rumors and his history of school bullying, he would leave quietly and do the team no further harm. That was the contract.

And the articles and announcements were released quickly.

★$
[Chae Seodam, #10 on the new season of ⟨Idol Inc.⟩, won’t be debuting… “Health issues” cited]
$★

The debut team's fandom hadn't liked him much to begin with, and since the reason given was 'health issues', the incident passed without any major problems or controversy.

And Gold 2, who'd come in at the last moment and played the good cop, was grinning from ear to ear.

It might've been an obvious ploy, but Lee Sejin had recruited him to be the one offering Chae Seodam a carrot when we'd made the plan.

*– Okay, Moondae will play the bad cop~! Let's have Heeseung play the good cop at the end and give him an offer he can't refuse!*

I couldn't deny it. It was good casting.

"Your acting was on point."

"Me? I wasn't acting. I was completely sincere."

Chae Seodam moved out immediately that day, and Gold 2 let out a long, deep sigh of relief.

"Hoo... I mean, how did that hyung even get so close to debuting again– Oh, I don't know! Anyway, I'm really relieved we got him to leave quietly before our debut!"

I understood how he felt. Nobody wanted a guy like a ticking time bomb in their group. I just laughed.

"Thank you again for being so generous, hyung!"

"No need to thank me."

"Ah, don't say that, hyung! You're amazing! I'll always think of you as my savior!"

Gold 2 started texting me more often after that.

And after a while, another type of text message came in through the manager of the new debut group of ⟨Idol Inc.⟩.

The sender was Chae Seodam.

It was a long message, but interestingly, it was addressed to Seon Ahyeon.

★:
- I saw everything as a competition, so I guess I hurt you more than I intended, and I'm sorry. I really learned my lesson this time. I can see now that there might be something wrong with the way I approach things...
:★

To summarize the very long message, he was saying he'd been too hard on Seon Ahyeon during their school years.

Of course, more than half of it was him trying to justify his actions.

"...'More than intended'? Is that what he calls an apology? That son of a– He still can't come to his senses...!"

"I-it's okay...!"

Bae Sejin was angry on his behalf, but Seon Ahyeon quickly reassured him.

"I didn't expect an apology from him... I told myself I didn't need one, but... I guess I wanted one after all."

"Ahyeon-ah..."

"Th-thank you."

Seon Ahyeon bowed to the others and met my eyes.

"A-and thank you, Moondae... I-I try to help, but it seems like I'm the one getting help every time."

"...?"

*What is he talking about? He's the one who laid the foundations.*

Of course, if I said that out loud, we'd be going back and forth complimenting each other forever, so I answered briefly.

"I was just cooperating. Thank you too."

"Yes...!"

Seon Ahyeon smiled brightly and closed the screenshot of Chae Seodam's text.

"Yeah, yeah!"

The members applauded as if it was some kind of ceremony. Seon Ahyeon seemed a bit shy.

"Hmm."

I thought to myself as I watched him flick the screenshot away.

*That bastard will forget this happened soon enough.*

However, it was a plan that had been designed with that in mind.

*He's good at avoiding anything that could harm him. He'll keep his mouth shut.*

And after coming this close to being crushed, people generally felt accepting and respectful rather than vengeful.

*I handled the situation well and got out of it.*

That's what he'd tell himself.

So this guy would never talk about anything related to this incident again. And Seon Ahyeon would simply forget about him.

*I guess we won't be seeing him again anytime soon.*

We'd taken him off the playing board.

"...Hmm."

After thinking for a while, I sent a text that night.

★:
It turned out well. Thanks-
:★

Well, since he'd helped me with this, I supposed some thanks were in order.

*Ding!*

Soon after, a picture of a familiar dog holding up a paw arrived.

*Is that supposed to be a greeting?*

"...Hmm."

I rubbed my chin.

Surprisingly, he wasn't being sarcastic.

*Maybe that bastard is finally coming to his senses–*

★:
-VTIC Shin Cheongryeo Sunbaenim: Is that right? Then I'd like a song in return.
:★

"......"

*Has he lost his mind?*

★:
- I was kidding ^^
:★

I locked my phone without replying.

And that was the end of the aftermath of sending Chae Seodam away.

And the very next day, it was revealed at the meeting that if we hadn't sent Chae Seodam away, we'd have been forced to work with him.

"We have some more specific dates for the company's concert..."

The meeting was about holding the agency's hallmark event—a joint concert featuring all its artists, a symbol of success for any agency worth its salt.
