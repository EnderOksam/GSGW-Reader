# Chapter 292 - Threat in the Background

When Chae Seodam brought up the story of a 'recording', the atmosphere changed abruptly.

I mean, it was funny.

*What is this bastard saying?*

No matter what he'd recorded, if he was smart and wanted to use it, wouldn't he keep his mouth shut and wait for the right moment?

Why was he giving us time to prepare by telling us himself?

*Hmm. Is he trying to take the initiative by intimidating us?*

Did he expect me to throw a fit and demand that he hand over the recording?

Well, I was curious how he'd react if I said he'd recorded something he shouldn't have, or even if I said he hadn't.

I immediately opened my mouth.

"You do realize it's illegal to record someone without their knowledge, Chae Seodam-ssi, right?"

"What? Ah... I was just trying to record my daily interactions, and I asked because I wouldn't want any sensitive conversations between sunbaenims to end up in the background."

Chae Seodam bowed his head.

"I'm so sorry if I overheard something I shouldn't have!"

*Well, well.*

If I admitted it was sensitive, it'd expose a weakness. If I said it wasn't, I'd have no basis for criticizing him.

I crossed my arms and laughed.

I was right. He was trying to make me angry.

"Hmm... I get the feeling you do this sort of thing regularly. It's an invasion of privacy, and clearly illegal."

*Didn't you hear me? What you're doing is illegal.*

However, this bastard's reaction was rather peculiar.

"Yes! I'm being careful so that doesn't happen. For now, I'll delete the file that might contain your conversation, sunbaenim."

Chae Seodam took out a button-shaped recorder from his back pocket.

*A Bluetooth device?*

Then he took out his smartphone and played the recording in front of me.

+$
– *Hmm. Where am I?*

– *My legs hurt...*

I couldn't be sure, but it seemed he'd pushed the recorder close to the crack in the door, allowing it to pick up sounds from inside the waiting room.

+$
– *You're after the grand prize...*

*Oh, that's when he started recording?*

It was when we'd started talking about viral marketing.

I listened to Cheongryeo's faint voice.

And Chae Seodam immediately clicked on the recording file and deleted it from his phone.

"There. It's deleted."

"Ah, yes."

"......"

He seemed taken aback.

*Was I calmer than he'd expected?*

Did he think that playing the recording for me would make me nervous? *Nice try.*

"Anything else to say?"

"No. Then, I'll be going now. I'm sorry for bothering you, sunbaenims."

The guy bowed his head and turned around.

I glanced at the other 'Sunbaenim' Chae Seodam had addressed.

Cheongryeo.

"......"

The guy was silently watching the bastard as he turned away.

*Wait a second...*

At that time, Chae Seodam suddenly looked back and spoke.

"But you know... Sunbaenim, do you trust me?"

"...!"

*What kind of bullshit was this?*

"I'm really sorry, but I don't know much about devices like this. Maybe the file's still there somewhere, and there could be a big problem if someone hacked my phone, for instance..."

I stared.

"I'm referring to the recording."

Was he trying to make me nervous by suggesting that although he'd put on a show of deleting the file, he might still have it intact?

*I can already see how this guy operates.*

He kept trying to scare me, watching my reaction closely each time. He seemed to have a talent for nitpicking and jumping to conclusions.

It was such a stupid move considering how much more popular I was than him... Oh, was it a calculation he'd made because I had more to lose?

"Hmm. That wouldn't be good for you, Seodam-ssi."

"...Me?"

"Yes. Think about it. An idol who goes around secretly recording other people's conversations... I wonder what people would say to that."

I leaned back against the doorway.

"I'd be careful if I were you."

It meant that the moment he released the recording, I'd take him down.

Chae Seodam raised his head defiantly for a moment, but quickly bowed again.

"...Thanks for the advice."

Then he walked quickly and disappeared across the hallway.

*Funny bastard.*

I didn't know why he was so eager to wreck his career after barely managing to debut... But if that's what he wanted, what could I do?

I shrugged and lifted my head from where it had been leaning against the wall.

It depended on how much of our conversation that bastard had actually recorded... I decided to add up my losses in the worst-case scenario, if I did nothing to respond.

*There are two possible issues: my informal tone and what I said about viral marketing.*

Neither was a pleasant prospect. It could turn into an 'attitude controversy' and accusations of 'public deception'.

However, I couldn't say for sure whether they'd be fatal.

*It's never good to be on the receiving end of criticism.*

However, if I had enough time to prepare, I could set up a defense. After all, it was a matter of context.

In particular, if I coordinated what to say with Cheongryeo here, it could all end in a storm in a teacup...

"Interesting."

...This bastard was extremely calm. Maybe it was because I'd be the only one with much to lose if the recording went public.

No, from this other bastard's point of view, wouldn't it actually be a benefit if the recording got out?

In this head-to-head situation, the more TeSTAR's reputation suffered, the more VTIC stood to gain.

*No wonder he didn't open his mouth.*

I massaged the back of my neck and went back into the waiting room. I figured I'd get a few things out of the way before the others arrived.

However, he asked me a question.

"I'm curious what you plan to do."

"Why are you curious about that, sunbaenim?"

"Since I was the one who told you to speak informally, I feel partly responsible. Um... Are you enemies with him or something?"

"Enemies?"

Was there any need to go that far?

I honestly didn't understand Chae Seodam. In the first place, our only point of contact was when I'd insulted him. Was that really enough to warrant this whole recording thing?

*Or maybe he's the type who thinks, "If I'm miserable, everyone else should be too."*

That was a mentality comparable to that of a terrorist lashing out at random. Don't ask me to understand someone like that.

However, Cheongryeo pointed something out clearly.

"He didn't seem to have any desire to speak with me... I thought that was pretty odd."

"......"

*Hmm.*

*Yep, he did zero in on me specifically...*

"Then I would like you to tell me what you are going to do."

"Well... It depends."

I finished the text I'd been typing and sent it. Since Chae Seodam had started out with a threat, it had probably occurred to him that unless I was an idiot, I'd be prepared if he decided to release the recording.

If so, he'd probably lay some groundwork instead of releasing it immediately.

*Head off every risk.*

It was true that any controversy would be a loss for me, so first I'd observe quietly.

Until I caught him off guard and his goal and weaknesses were revealed.
