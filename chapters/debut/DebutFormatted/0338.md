# The Cameo, II

It was April.

Kim Raebin's fan came to the cinema after a long time.

*I'm crazy!*

With graduation just around the corner and midterms underway, the college student knew how crazy she was being, applying for every screening event she could until she finally won a ticket.

And all because of a gossip article she wasn't even sure she could trust.

Rumor had it that a K-pop idol group would make a cameo in this Limestone movie.

And, of course, it was the very sci-fi movie based on the universe created by Ruins Factory, the company behind ⟨127 Section⟩. No self-respecting Korean could resist the urge to watch it.

Of course, speculation ran wild about which group might be involved, but her favorites were the most likely candidates.

Because everything made sense.

★$
– Think about it. They'll definitely use the same group they'd collaborated with before.

– No way, is it TeSTAR?

– I'm so excited, what's going on?
$★

On the surface, most people were excited, though reactions ranged from enthusiasm to anxiety.

Of course, the game community was cursing the game company, saying it had lost touch in its pursuit of the American market.

If the rumor about slotting in K-pop idols was true, the general opinion was that the game company had cheapened its universe for the sake of over-the-top patriotic marketing.

And interestingly, similar reactions were coming from lesser-known idol fan communities.

★$
– As soon as TeSTAR won a *daesang* in Korea, they were sent straight to America ㅋ That's so like T1. I'm at a loss for words.

– I don't like this.

– Now Korea's a fish they've already caught. They expect us to live off translated gossip about the group from now on.

– An idol cameo ㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋ Are they crazy? I'm cringing so hard ㅋㅋ
$★

Kim Raebin's fan felt much the same way.

*Wait, do they plan to base the independent label in the US too?!*

Fans who had already suspected the agency wouldn't actually let them form an independent label were gearing up to come down on the company.

At the same time, they were keeping any negative reactions toward TeSTAR quiet in public.

*Is the company out of its mind, making this much trouble while the group's on hiatus?*

However, despite this heated response, T1 Stars did not make any official statement.

Normally, the company would have been quick to deny the rumors or put something out to the press. Its silence only made fans more anxious.

It made them suspicious too.

*Is the cameo so bad they can't even defend it...?*

*Wouldn't they have bundled up the kids and sold them with some crap deal?*

*Why are they keeping so quiet about a short cameo appearance? Could there be more to it?*

Of course, TeSTAR was on tour, so there wasn't any other content to distract the fans with.

Questions flooded the comments on W Live, but TeSTAR wasn't answering them either. A lot of people now believed TeSTAR really was in the movie and had been asked to keep it a secret.

And the anxiety was heightened.

*Come out... No, please don't be in the movie!*

That was what the college student was thinking while frantically trying to get a ticket to the screening.

But time passed steadily.

Yesterday had been the first day of screenings.

And everything turned upside down.

★$
– Crazy

– Limestone is always right

– Oh my god. Just come and watch, everyone.

– Hahahah, anyone worried about excessive patriotic marketing doesn't need to worry about this one ㅋㅋㅋㅋ~~
$★

The film was excellent, with an average critic rating of four out of five stars. The game's universe had also been adapted successfully, giving patriotic WeTubers plenty of good material.

Besides, TeSTAR's appearance...

★$
– I was so shocked. Our members were...
$★

She stopped reading there and logged out.

*How can this crazy bastard post spoilers so that they can be searched for!*

She didn't want anything to ruin the experience. She didn't go on social media again until she came to today's screening.

She didn't even check TeSTAR's official social media accounts.

*I'll go in completely blind and judge it for myself.*

Rigged comments to inflate movie ratings were nothing new, nor were fans trying to make the first day's reviews look favorable.

She remained wary.

*The only certain thing is that TeSTAR will appear!*

The college student sat down in the movie theater, her mind cool and clear.

"Hey, I want some popcorn."

"Shut up."

She snapped at her younger brother, whom she'd brought along because there was nobody else who would go with her. Before long, the emergency exits were shown on the screen, and the logo appeared.

Limestone's distinctive English logo.

And the title.

★$
@c@#*[Cosmic Gunner]*#@c@
$★

It was a typical title for a movie like this. As one might expect at a preview screening, short cheers rang out here and there.

But the grand story that followed... As cinematic as it was, it didn't particularly impress her.

She wasn't interested in science fiction, had a hard time following what they were talking about, and didn't find the game particularly interesting either.

*I knew it would be like this.*

The college student chewed on her caramel popcorn indifferently.

*When will TeSTAR come out?*

But it was definitely fun to watch as a movie.

The dark humor when explaining how Earth had met its demise, the wonderful scene transitions, and the easy-to-follow storyline.

Overall, it was a movie worth seeing even for those who knew little about science fiction or superheroes and weren't particularly interested in either.

*It's fine.*

It was a classic Limestone movie. She relaxed for a moment and stared at the screen in a daze.

She assumed TeSTAR, like most Hollywood cameos, would only appear after the midpoint.

Around that time, the protagonist on-screen arrived at a space station and was staring at its decadent, colorful neon signs.

[Wow, that's quite something.]

*– (Seriously, I had it totally wrong.)*

People in the theater burst into laughter at the narration accompanying the protagonist's words.

In the meantime, the movie's protagonist noticed a sign for a pub and quietly walked inside.

The camera closed in on the band's powerful, profound performance for a moment, then the protagonist chose a table and sat down, their music continuing to play as BGM.

Then he exchanged some comical banter with a supporting character who was a trusted ally.

[So you're from Earth. What's it like there?]

[Well, the bar music is just as noisy.]

At that moment, the outlandish band in the background began shouting in wild English slang.

[What did you fucking say?]

[I love this song, my friend! Hey! Best song I've ever heard!]

In the distance, one of the band members, blurred and out of focus, glared at the protagonist, then nodded and looked away. Once again, the audience burst into laughter.

The protagonist spoke again with a groan.

[They're just teenagers. Impertinent little brats staring at me.]

[Uh-huh, watch out! Newbie friend, what passes for common sense on your planet might not hold up here.]

Then the small alien ally, sitting across from the main character, lowered their voice and began introducing him to...

The world he was about to discover.

[Over 1,200 planets are tied to this station!]

Elaborate, grand shots of outer space were spliced together, the dialogue playing over them.

*Wow.*

The visual effects were stunning. The college student let out a small sound of admiration.

In the meantime, the alien's narration continued.

About the peculiarities and treasures of the various races that used the space station, their power dynamics, and their armed groups.

Quite a few things went against common sense on Earth.

Even what was considered dangerous.

[The smaller ones all carry force shooters because of the Protection Act. You see them over there?]

[Like you?]

[Yes, like me.]

The camera returned to a wide shot of the bar. The supporting character chuckled, briefly revealing a force shooter tucked away in their chest pocket before hiding it again. The protagonist squinted at it.

[And finally... Stay clear of the crazies who don't bother hiding that they're crazy.]

[Crazies?]

[Ah, you know, the guys who obviously stand out from everyone else!]

After the supporting actor's words, the camera panned again.

This time, it focused on the seemingly “teenage” band on the bar's tiny stage that had spoken to the protagonist earlier.

As they climbed down after finishing their performance, soldiers in intimidating combat uniforms immediately picked a fight with them.

Shown only from behind and in profile, they were dressed in colorful, avant-garde fashion, like a cross between game characters and rock bands from the 70s, but they were nowhere near as big as the guys in combat uniforms.

The atmosphere seemed like the band would be beaten up by the soldiers or run away at any moment.

[Oh.]

But when the camera showed the band from the soldiers' point of view...

Those faces looked completely unimpressed.

And those faces were also very familiar to most of the audience at the screening.

"...!"

"Wow!"

TeSTAR!

Excited shouts burst from all around the theater at their unexpected appearance...

[They don't bother hiding it because they don't have to!]

The supporting actor's voice continued over the scene.

"...!"

*Whiik!*

In a quick, well-choreographed fight, the soldiers were subdued with extreme ease by the band's bizarre space instruments.

*Tarak!*

A member of the band blasted the last one over a table with a laser.

Then, naturally, he brushed the dust off his crumpled doctor's coat, carefully and protectively wiping at a green stain.

With just that momentary movement, the audience could recognize that the stain on the white gown was deliberately styled.

He looked up, revealing a young man wearing glasses and thick makeup. He cracked his neck expressionlessly.

*Wow.*

The college student realized.

It was a costume based on the 'Doctor' in ⟨127 Section⟩!

And it was only a beat later that she realized that the person was Bae Sejin.

"...!"

Even without saying a word here, he looked like a completely different person from the usual idol Bae Sejin. When he'd shouted in English earlier, she hadn't even recognized his voice.

She knew TeSTAR so well, and yet that only made him seem all the more unfamiliar.

In that short time on-screen, Bae Sejin had been completely immersed in his character.

*What in the world?*

Kim Raebin's fan swallowed hard. She'd once thought of Bae Sejin as deadweight, and now viewed him more as the weakest member who didn't quite fit in with the others. But when it came to acting, he was clearly a professional.

And the scene changed again.

There was a brief glimpse of the seven band members leisurely passing the table of the protagonist, ignoring the fallen soldiers.

Bae Sejin spoke without looking back.

[Come and listen to us again sometime.]

[Well, um, it's an honor–]

However, the band disappeared off-screen before the main character could finish, leaving only the sci-fi sound of the door opening and closing.

*Shuuk!*

[...]

The two main characters stared wide-eyed at the door, then quickly huddled together and whispered.

[See that? That's the sort of place this is.]

[Wow...]

The protagonist blinked.

[So just now, I...]

[Yes, you could've died. But listen, I have a good plan...]

The cinema was filled with laughter again. Only those who knew the group making the cameo could fully appreciate it.

And Kim Raebin's fan sat there in shock, holding her popcorn tub.

The scene, which ended on a laugh, was clearly meant to introduce the movie's universe to the protagonist and the audience alike.

Humor, twists, and coolness.

A cameo appearance with all three virtues.

*Wow, fuck.*

T1 couldn't have arranged something this good... No, it had to be the members' doing. It definitely wasn't those bastards.

"Bae Sejin..."

Kim Raebin's fan muttered the name of the member who'd carried the scene particularly well. She didn't usually spare him much thought. Then a realization struck her like lightning.

*Come to think of it, what song were they playing?*

And when the band's music from the bar returned with vocals in the final battle scene and again during the ending credits, she realized she hadn't been wrong.

♪ *Exploding far away* ♫
p$

Those high notes were perfect, even though it was a pop song.

And who the hell else could've created this insanely good OST?

*Kim Raebin! Park Moondae!*

She'd definitely picked the best members!!

And as soon as the screening was over, she went back online to find that the short cameo and OST were already making waves.
