# Chapter 296 - The Joint Stage

Even on the day the agency announced the concert and ticketing dates, we still had joint practice scheduled.

Thanks to that, I got to check the online reactions during the latter half of practice.

To summarize... It was as expected.

A whirlpool of anticipation, apprehension, annoyance, and interest.

It couldn't be helped. An agency concert being broadcast on TV was unusual, and there were things to be both worried and excited about.

Moreover, since all three groups came from a popular audition show, it was easy to draw public attention.

Thanks to the concert airing on a TNet channel, it was easily accessible. Anyone interested could simply tune in.
It was rare to find someone who liked all three groups from this agency.

*So there's only one certain thing.*

Of the three teams from ⟨Idol Inc.⟩, the one that fell furthest behind could be crushed by ridicule from all directions.

Fans had no attachment or loyalty to this agency, not even a love-hate relationship, so they wouldn't stick up for their favorite group's labelmates.

*The fandoms would grow hostile toward one another and end up exhausted by all the fighting.*

There was no need to add fuel to the fire, so we had to avoid stirring up trouble during our joint stage with Miry-nay.

There was more to consider than just the fact that it was a mixed-gender performance.

*We have to avoid a stage that invites direct comparisons.*

And the stage was already set like that.

...I didn't know it would be this hard.

– *Here, here! Wouldn't it be so cool if we put this step in when we land?*

– *If you don't mind, I was thinking we could hold the freeze at the end of our first chorus a little longer...!*

– *Ah~ Shall we all try this part again? We're doing really well already, but wouldn't it look ten times cooler if we hit the bridge in sync? Nice and neat~*

The other side was just as eager to put on a good stage, so the difficulty kept climbing as neither of us wanted to be outdone.

In the end, the amount of stamina we had to pour into this stage skyrocketed.

*Is it time to invest in my Dance stat?*

I considered using my one remaining point, but held back, knowing that I could no longer level up through the system.

I knew for certain that there would come a time when my stats would rise naturally. And the stats I had now were more than enough.

"Hoo, shall we go one more time?"

"Let's do it...!"

So in the end, we were working ridiculously hard on a special stage no one was expecting much from.

Looking at the others, I came to a realization.

*At this rate, it might be a good idea to release the behind-the-scenes practice footage.*

Even though this was a mixed-gender stage, with backup dancers helping us and everyone going all out, practice was practically a boot camp. I wiped the sweat from my temples.

*At least it'll look good onstage.*

"Oh, we've got this part down!"

"Wow, I'm really proud!"

I just hoped the agency wouldn't get any unrealistic expectations after watching this stage.

"Shall we finish it today?"

"Yes!"

I shrugged my shoulders and resumed practice without further comment.

And the remaining time until the concert flew by in an instant.

*We have to nail this.*

"Here. My mic's come undone."

*How does he know so much about everything...?*

*How on earth did he grow up to become like this?*

Park Minha remembered thinking Park Moondae was cute while watching the Relisted season of ⟨Idol Inc.⟩. Just thinking about it now made her want to bang her head against the wall.

Park Minha staggered backstage in a daze.

Somehow, pushing herself to the point of nearly throwing up onstage felt exhilarating. She'd sensed how well it was going even while performing, and her heart still felt like it might explode.

*Besides, using props let us make up for the differences in our proportions that would've made uniform choreography difficult...*

Park Moondae's idea was great.
