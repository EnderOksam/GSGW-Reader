# Chapter 157 - Two Weeks on the Sidelines

It was in the second recording after the stage rehearsal that Kim Raebin got injured.

During a difficult group move in Seon Ahyeon's part, Raebin stretched out a leg to rise from the floor, accidentally knocking against a large piece of stage equipment that toppled onto his foot.

This happened because the stage was narrower than expected.

*...It was smaller than what they notified us.*

The stage set they prepared for the English-speaking talk show was too small for a lot of people to dance together, so we had to reduce the choreography movement accordingly.

*It's not that Kim Raebin's movement stuck out.*

Their incorrect stage measurements had left Raebin's foot badly injured.

*Fuck.*

No, let's not assume that it was broken yet.

The bruise was bleeding, but the wound might be smaller than I thought.

"Raebin-ssi, do you think you can move your foot? Don't actually move it—just tell me how it feels!"

"...I can, but it hurts, it hurts..."

"I'll stop the bleeding first!"

After receiving first aid, Kim Raebin was put in an ambulance and went to the hospital. And the rest of the members were able to simply organize the situation and then move to the hospital.

There was no way we could record the performance again, so they'd have to make do with the footage from the first take.

And immediately, the conclusion was briefed.

"Two weeks?"

"At least. He still won't be able to dance, and he'll need a foot guard even just to stand."

"..."

The company staff who came out of Kim Raebin's hospital room explained through an interpreter.

The diagnosis was ligament damage. And cracked bones and torn skin.

*...We avoided the worst.*

If the fracture had been more serious, or if he'd ruptured a tendon, he might have been unable to perform for months.

Of course, this situation still wasn't good.

"Doing something as intense as the current choreography, I think we should wait for a month before talking about it again."

Raebin wouldn't be able to dance for the rest of this promotional cycle.

For a song that put the most emphasis on performance, it was perfectly bad news.

*What's wrong with damn America?*

It wasn't funny that we had to take turns going to the hospital since last time.

I kept my mouth shut, and Ryu Chungwoo carefully asked the person from the company.

"There aren't any aftereffects?"

"Yes. He didn't get hurt that bad."

"...*Huft*."

I could see Ryu Chungwoo sighing in relief. The other guys barely looked relieved. Even Cha Eugene was almost crying.

"W-Would it be okay to go inside and see Raebin now...?"

"Yes, yes. The doctor is out now."

We'd come all the way to the hospital; we couldn't leave without seeing Raebin.

We opened the door to the hospital room and entered.

"...Raebin?"

Kim Raebin was sitting on the hospital bed with a slightly dazed expression. His injured foot was fixed and lifted up.

As soon as Kim Raebin turned his face this way, Keun Sejin quickly opened his mouth.

"Raebin, is your foot okay?"

"Yes, but the stage..."

"It's fine, you don't have to worry. We just talked about it, and they said you can perform if you endure it for two weeks. It's not a big deal~"

However, despite what Keun Sejin said, Kim Raebin seemed to have noticed the solemnity of the situation.

Just because this guy had no sense of interpersonal relationships, that didn't mean he didn't know what was going on.

But I didn't know he'd be like this.

Kim Raebin cried and started to shed large drops of tears.

"I'm sorry... T-two weeks, that's just..."

"Hey, what are you talking about? Two weeks will pass by soon! We already had our comeback performance. What are you worried about?"

"Sejin is right, Raebin. It's not even your fault in the first place. Just focus on recovering, okay?"

"...Yes. *Sniff.*"

Kim Raebin sniffed and tried to hold back his tears. I held back my sigh and pulled out the tissue from the table and handed it over.

I didn't feel good.

"Don't think about anything else and take a rest!"

"Take a rest, you idiot!"

Kim Raebin decided to stay at the hospital for about two days so that he could receive care, rather than staying at the hotel alone during his U.S. schedule.

The members shouted loudly and closed the door to Kim Raebin's hospital room and came out.

Then the topic returned to reality.

"We should split Raebin's part, right?"

"We should."

Ryu Chungwoo crossed his arms and smiled bitterly.

"We'll have to redo the choreography for the entire performance," Chungwoo said.

"Jet lag's going to keep us awake anyway, so let's give it a try~"

"..."

Bae Sejin's face seemed to turn white, but he didn't say anything.

He must have felt sorry for complaining about the increased choreography for the guy that injured his foot. Compared to the time in ⟨Idol Inc.⟩, it was a great development.

*We're all tired.*

Kim Raebin was mentally tired, and the rest looked physically tired.

To be honest, I wanted to tell the media, whether local or Korean, that this accident had happened because the talk show's staff gave us the wrong stage measurements.

It was disgusting.

*But I can't do that carelessly.*

This was the first time we appeared on an English-speaking talk show without it being part of the game collaboration. Avoiding bad rumors was the correct choice.

Still, I couldn't help but get angry.

What kind of nonsense is this?

*Maybe we'd have to get more famous first.*

I had no idea how popular we'd need to be before boycotting foreign talk shows would make any difference.

I sighed and cleared my thoughts.

*Let's just practice.*

I came all the way to a foreign country and fell into practice hell again.

"Congratulations on being able to release the cast~"

"Raebin, you've had a hard time."

"No, I'm sorry for the inconvenience."

Kim Raebin, wearing a lighter foot brace than before, spoke bravely and bowed his head. He managed to get permission from the doctor to return to the non-dancing stage.

"Ah, it's not an inconvenience~ I'm relieved to see you're back!"

"T-That's right!"

"Thank you."

The members comforted Kim Raebin in moderation and dispersed to their hotel rooms. Because they had to sleep quickly and wake up early to revise the stage that included Kim Raebin's movement.

I nodded my head.

"You're in this room."

"Yes!"

We'd assigned the rooms while Raebin was seeing the doctor.

*We don't stay for long anyway.*

We didn't have a long schedule in Japan. I held the door until Kim Raebin came in, then I went inside to wash up.

"I'll take a quick shower first."

"Yes!"

He'd been resting the past few days, so he'd become more disciplined. I shook my head and came out of the shower.

Then, I made eye contact with Kim Raebin, who was very nervous.

"...?"

He was on the verge of making some kind of bombshell remark.

*Could it be that the injury has gotten worse?*

I also kept quiet as I didn't want to hear that his break from the choreography would be longer, and Kim Raebin finally opened his mouth with difficulty.

"That, hyung."

"What is it?"

"Hyung... do you think Golden Age's new song appeals to the general public more than the ones I've composed?"

"...??"

What kind of nonsense is this all of a sudden?

I looked at him dumbfounded, then Kim Raebin closed his eyes and recited his words.

"I can hardly contribute to the performance right now, but if my music is holding us back, I'll take a break from composing and arranging for a while..."

"Wait, wait. What are you talking about?"

I cut him off because it was going in an absurd direction.

*What kind of nonsense are you talking about when you've released such a good album?*

Of course, self-producing members were a double-edged sword. Because if you did well, it was synergistic, but if you didn't, it became the subject of internal conflict.

It was easy to create a frame like "The reason why the music score this time is bad is because of the producing member's stubbornness and incompetence."

*But Kim Raebin has never failed.*

Golden Age hadn’t become a contender for first place, and Kim Raebin's form hadn't fallen either, so this was really out of the blue.

I asked as calmly as I could.

"Why did you suddenly think like that?"

"That... While monitoring the reaction to the music, I accidentally watched a video of Golden Age members' new song due to the influence of the algorithm, but there were a lot of top comments..."

"Give it to me. Let me see."

"Yes?"

I got the video address from Kim Raebin and checked it.

"...So I'd like to hear your honest, objective evaluation of my music."

Right.

Kim Raebin was not the kind of person who could lose confidence in his ability.

Perhaps being unable to join the full choreography had made him feel he had to prove his worth through music.

That's why he kept looking for the gossip that compared the songs of similar groups with the title track he made, and eventually, he would have that kind of thought.
