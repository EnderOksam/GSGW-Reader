# Chapter 254 - Removal

Glitter and confetti were still falling from the pop-up in front of me.

But no matter how surreal the scene was, the content inside the pop-up was the problem. *Was this the end?*

*What's this?*

It felt like I got hit in the back of the head.

Of course, I wanted to put an end to this nonsense of being constantly threatened with sudden death if I failed to become an idol within a year.

*But this way, this timing... Are you kidding me right now?*

"What the fuck is this?"

It had shown me my meeting with 'Park Moondae' and my life after giving up on the civil service exam, neither of which I remembered, right up to the crazy things I'd done at the end.

And now it was all over without any explanation, as though the system just wanted to be rid of me.

"Then why did you show me all that?"

If it wasn't for that fucking 'Reveal the Truth' feature, wouldn't I have somehow accepted my success and continued living?

Did my former life not matter because 'Ryu Gunwoo' had already given up on it once?

Did it just want me to live well as Park Moondae?

"Then where did the original Park Moondae go?"

Originally, where did Park Moondae go? And where is the 'Ryu Gunwoo' in Ryu Chungwoo's video that I checked?

'Reveal the Truth' hadn't cleared up a single thing. I didn't understand anything.

However, there was no change in the pop-up.

"...Hoo."

*Okay.*

I shouldn't stupidly scream at the hologram. I should start by figuring out the situation.

I took a deep breath and took a closer look at the contents of the pop-up.

First, the title—the first one I'd ever been given.

★-
\
#><$a[Title: The Successful (Idol)]a$><#

You have succeeded.

Status abnormalities permanently disabled
-★

It meant that another 'status abnormality' wouldn't appear out of nowhere to drive me crazy.

However, even the one who guaranteed this couldn't be trusted very much, given how many curveballs the system had thrown at me before. Pass.

And... there was one other thing other than the title in the Rewards tab.

★-
\
**Permanent status window**
-★

In the future, it meant that I could probably keep checking my own stats and other people's by calling up their status windows.

"...Hmm."

I crossed my arms.

Seeing all that helped me cool my head again.

Because it was weird.

*Isn't it too advantageous?*

There was nothing bad about it. It was one of the most useful abilities I could have in this industry. With it, I could probably set up an agency of my own and be practically guaranteed success.

This... It left me a supernatural reward and ended it there.

Without any prior notice?

I felt as though the system was getting rid of me, but this... It seemed more like the system considered its services complete.

The system had just packed up and left because I'd cleared all the status abnormalities assigned to me.

It had no reason to explain the things I was curious about or the cause of this situation.

"...Damn it."

I had no idea what was going on.

I looked at the pop-up despondently, then just lay down.

That's right, fuck, anyway, the status abnormalities were gone. Even if I lived freely, there was no need to die suddenly, so wasn't that good?

No matter how much I tried to think of it, the bitter aftertaste wouldn't go away.

I was able to fall asleep after quite some time.

The damn celebration pop-up stayed up until morning.

***

Since my head was a mess, it was better to use my hands to forget about it.

"I told you it's fine, you don't have to do this!"

"I'm fine with cooking, don't worry about it."

I made seafood and green onion pancakes and battered pan-fried meatballs. I even made seasoned ribs, which hadn't been part of the plan. Among the meat I bought, there were raw ribs.

Both mother and son seemed uneasy about their guest taking over the kitchen, but they relaxed once they'd tasted the food.

"Does it taste good?"

"It's good..."

"It's delicious, dear, but you're still our guest..."

*A guest.*

Come to think of it, unlike Bae Sejin, I still hadn't bought a place of my own.

The other members had their parents' homes to go back to, so they didn't need to buy a place of their own, but I... Maybe I should start looking for an apartment. It seemed I'd have to keep living in this body.

However, even though I judged it to be a reasonable plan, I was not very inclined.

*This is driving me crazy.*

What made it even more unpleasant was knowing how ungrateful I probably sounded, given my circumstances.

"Thank you, I ate well."

"Don't think about washing the dishes, hurry up and watch TV~"

The meal was fine, though. It was also suitable for a holiday meal.

And around the time I was blankly watching TV, my smartphone vibrated.

★:
#><#*[Ryu Chungwoo-hyung]*#><#
📞 Incoming call…
:★
"...!"

He'd said he would ask his clan about 'Ryu Gunwoo'.

*He's fast.*

I answered the call right away.

"Hyung."

– Ah, Moondae. You picked up quickly.

Behind Ryu Chungwoo's voice, I heard the faint barking of a dog.

Of course, that didn't matter. The important thing was the main point of the phone call.

– About that 'Ryu Gunwoo' person you saw in the video.

"Yes."

– Well, he hasn't really been in touch with anyone since college.

"......"

Oh.

Ryu Chungwoo said he was sorry that he couldn't even give me his contact information, but it was already a big enough clue.

*This means that 'Ryu Gunwoo' went to college at least.*

I crossed my arms.

This... His actions matched mine up to that point.

After spending my high school years living with a few relatives, I'd stopped keeping in touch with the clan altogether. Whenever I contacted them, it only stirred up troubling thoughts.

"Okay. Thank you for telling me."

– It's a bit disappointing we couldn't find him, isn't it?

"Not at all. At least I know the person I remember is real. That's a relief. Thanks for looking into it."

– It's just a phone call.

Ryu Chungwoo added in a slightly joking tone.

– I have to do better to repay you for saving my life.

"......"

Sure. Well... If he felt comfortable thinking like that.

I gave up on correcting him and just hung up after talking about the holidays.

And I immediately decided what to do next.

"What are you doing?"

"Mind if I use your laptop?"

Now, I'd have to take a different approach.

As soon as I entered this body, I had already tried to look for myself, 'Ryu Gunwoo'.

It meant that I'd called my old phone number and tried logging into the web portal accounts and university account I'd used.

So this time, I'd go backwards.

*Are there any signs of a dead account, not an active one?*

It was to look for traces of accounts I'd already deleted or that had gone dormant.

*The videos from the fancam account I'd deleted were still floating around online.*

Videos from 'gun1234'.

Things like Youngrin's Legendary fancam on a rainy day.

That was where the idea came from.

I immediately turned on the search engine's advanced search function and adjusted the search date range.

To the past.

"......"

After two or three hours of searching and browsing like that...

I came to a conclusion.

*There's something.*

The accounts that had once belonged to 'Ryu Gunwoo' and records of their activity still existed.

For example, things like questions I left in photo-related cafes when I was a newbie.

It took me a while to find them because I disliked leaving traces online and usually deleted my posts before too long. But knowing where to look, I'd eventually found them.

The only things missing were my internet accounts, phone number, and other still-active traces.

So, 'Ryu Gunwoo' is... In all places, including the Internet and the real world, he disappeared from the moment I entered the body of 'Park Moondae'.

*I evaporated.*

Yes. It was as though I'd vanished into thin air by supernatural means.

"...Hoo."

I closed my laptop.

I went over what I'd learned so far.

Actually, from the first time I came into this body, I guessed the following.

This was not the world I used to live in, and there was no Ryu Gunwoo.

But no.

*This is the past of the same world I used to live in.*

The 'Ryu Gunwoo' I'd seen in Ryu Chungwoo's video and found traces of online was me.

Therefore... While returning to the past, I became Park Moondae instead of Ryu Gunwoo due to an unknown event, and Ryu Gunwoo disappeared.

*Why did that happen...*

I didn't know. I didn't know the mechanism for why people come back to the past in the first place.

Even Cheongryeo, who returned to the past before me, didn't know why.

"......"

*Is there really no way to learn more?*

Was I supposed to stay in the dark and just go house-hunting so I could settle down?

It was when I was glaring down at the closed laptop with a strange sense of exhaustion...

*Ding-*

My smartphone rang. Without thinking, I picked it up and checked it.

Dozens of messages floated there.

*That's quite a lot.*

I hadn't seen it for about three hours. I checked the newer messages first, leaving the group chat, which had become quite active over the holidays, for later.

Then a notification came. It was Seon Ahyeon.

★:
-Moondae, how are you doing? My parents were going to text you, but they thought you might find it uncomfortable, so I decided to text you instead.
:★

Seon Ahyeon's long message started by describing how his holiday was going and eventually wound its way around to the group's future activities.

It was just like Seon Ahyeon.

★:
-I think Moondae is a really great person. If we keep doing things the way we have been, I'm sure we'll make another great album...
:★

This part bothered me a little, though.

"The way we have been..."

I knew it was just a kind turn of phrase, but for some reason I read it again.

Like a clue to something...

"...!!"

*Okay. I got it.*

★:
Thank you for your consideration. Have a good Chuseok and see you at the dorm.-
:★

After double-checking my response, I immediately locked my smartphone screen.

Then I sat on the bed, looked at the wall, and thought.

*The way I've always done things.*

I threw away all other thoughts.

I had to stay calm. Let's combine only the knowledge I gained from experiencing this damn status window.

The first was the daydream from the coma.

*The system and the status window didn't function properly during that dream.*

***#f#Enjoy your daydream :)#f#***

Only that one message popped up, and after that it was pure shit. The achievements didn't work at all.

*Nevertheless... I could draw in the daydream.*

It must have been because it was a pop-up that I obtained in advance and did not delete.

And now, as in that daydream, the system was unresponsive.

The trait draw pop-up I'd saved for emergencies still hadn't disappeared.

★=
{Draw Legendary Characteristics{ ☜ Click!
=★

This.

It was the draw I'd received for completing a performance achievement after the online donation concert.

There was only one.

And since I was able to draw in my daydream, I knew it would probably work now, too.

*The second is...*

This status window, which only appeared to me, was favorable to my success.

Cheongryeo did not have a status window. Perhaps the old man whom Cheongryeo met, who said he came from the future, was the same way.

It seemed that I was the only one with this. I didn't know why.

*And... In particular, this point is revealed through the draw.*

I didn't know if the probabilities were being manipulated, but I always seemed to draw what I needed.

However, there were cases where it didn't.

*Time of Indulgence and Bacchus were different.*

They were picked after I figured out this 'necessity' mechanism, and with a strong desire in front of the draw.

In other words, if I wished specifically and intensely, there was a chance I'd receive what I wanted.

I took note of this.

*Pingururururu-*

The illustration of the roulette machine began spinning.

I hoped.

*What I want...*

There was one thing.

*An effect similar to the rewards from status abnormalities!*

Fanfare erupted from the roulette, along with confetti.

It was probably the last roulette effect I could see.

It landed on a compartment shining with rainbow light.

★-
\
#><✦ Characteristic: Acquired 'Missions Are My Thing (S)'!><#
-★

★-
\
#><✦ Characteristic: Missions Are My Thing (S)><#

An exchange of equivalents, perfect for someone who enjoys a challenge.

You now have access to missions.
-★

*There we go!*

I knocked my head against the wall in relief.

And in front of my eyes, a new pop-up was emerging.

★!
#><[New mission!]><#

Goal: Daesang

Mission deadline: D-\_\_\_

Reward: \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\
Penalty: \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
!★

I looked up and saw a hologram.

Perhaps because it was a 'characteristic', it had a high degree of freedom that was incomparable to a 'status abnormality'.

"Good."

I entered the deadline. 500 days.

★!
#><⚠︎ [Exceeding D-365 will enhance the penalty.]><#
!★

Are you kidding me? We're talking about a grand prize, and there are only 3 months left in the year. Next.

*The important thing is the reward.*

It could not include anything not specific, such as 'truth' or 'reason'. I had to remember all the times the system had let me down.

However, I couldn't put anything specific like an 'explanation of why and how I came into this body'. I ran out of space before I could type it all out.

*That's funny.*

I smiled and crossed my arms.

If that were the case, wouldn't there be no choice but to approach it in another direction?

Someone who could help answer all my questions.

A man whose whereabouts were unknown.

"A conversation with... Park Moondae."

What do you say to that?

★!
Reward: A conversation with Park Moondae
!★

It was entered normally.

And the penalty was automatically filled.

★!
Penalty: Deletion of status window
!★

"Oh."

*So I'm betting the status window as collateral?*

I deleted the contents of the reward tab for a while and put something like 'dance EX' in it.

The penalty became a decrease in my Dance stat.

*As expected, it demands something of equivalent value.*

If so, it meant that a 'conversation with Park Moondae' was a valuable enough reward that I'd lose the status window forever if I failed.

"All right."

The way forward couldn't be clearer.

I returned the reward to 'A conversation with Park Moondae'.

When I clicked OK, an unfamiliar pop-up appeared.

★-
\
#><[Confirm?]><#

[Y / N]
-★

"Yes."

I was going to secure that reward.
