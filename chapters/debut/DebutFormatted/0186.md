# Chapter 186 - Aftershocks, I

I crossed my arms.

"So... You're saying that it would be better to revise my solo stage."

"Yes."

Ryu Chungwoo nodded with a firm face.

We'd ordered good food and were eating at the dorm to celebrate the first concert day, but I hadn't expected this conversation.

*Is this what he meant when he said we would talk later?*

I have to replace the person in charge of the device, why should I give up on the performance?

It was my solo stage, so it's not like I was hurting anyone else, but it was funny to hear people telling me what to do.

I answered bitterly.

"We can just find the person who made the mistake, change them, and keep going. I've never felt any particular pressure on that stage."

"You might not feel the pressure, but your wrists and ribs probably do."

I held back my sigh.

"...It's all right. I wouldn't have done it if I couldn't stand it in the first place."

Keun Sejin asked me a question.

Yes, not only Ryu Chungwoo, but the other guys were also listening intently as if we were having a meeting.

"Park Moondae. We have over three months of touring ahead of us—twelve performances, not including Seoul."

I know that.

"Can you go up on the wires twelve more times, in a new venue each time? Can you guarantee there won't be a single mistake?"

"That's why, it's fine to make a mistake..."

"I-It's not fine."

"...!"

...It was Seon Ahyeon.

"Moondae, you passed out... I-If you do something wrong, you'll be in big trouble. Y-Your wrist still hurts..."

"...."

My head was pounding. I rubbed my shoulder.

"...I have time until we go abroad anyway. I can fully recover if I rest."

"Moondae."

Ryu Chungwoo shook his head.

"Are you a doctor?"

"...!"

"Whatever the injury... once a bone has been damaged, there's no guarantee there won't be long-term side effects."

Ryu Chungwoo smiled bitterly.

"I'm telling you from firsthand experience."

"..."

This bastard was using a cheat key.

I kept my mouth shut at the words of the former national team member who retired before he got into his prime due to the aftereffects of injury.

Of course, my silence was not a declaration of surrender.

*Should I set up the mood first?*

As long as sensitive keywords such as 'injury' were included, persuading them only with logic was not a good match.

*If I sound convincing enough, my 'Words of Persuasion' characteristic might activate.*

The probability was more than half.

I came up with a suitable structure.

And I said hesitantly.

To appeal in this way.

"But... I've practiced for a long time to show you."

"...That."

"I put a lot of thought into the sequence too. And when I performed it, the response was really good..."

I finished my speech with a serious tone.

"It's such a waste to quit after just one day. I want to do as much as possible. If the recovery from the injury is too slow, I'll ask for your understanding and change it."

Ryu Chungwoo's face darkened slightly.

*Did that work?*

However, Keun Sejin interjected, looking flabbergasted.

"You've only been on the device for less than 2 weeks, what do you mean you've practiced for a long time?"

This bastard.

"...I mean, I've thought about it for a long time."

"Ah, really? You picked this to make an excuse for your injury, right?"

"...!"

"The excuse would have worked for one day, so let's go for an alternative now. You can refrain from doing it and recover completely first. We will continue to have concerts in the future."

Keun Sejin continued to speak with a serious look.

"Look at it from the long-term perspective. Are you going to retire after this tour?"

"...!"

"You're not, are you?"

That... I don't know.

I might not be able to continue.

Then, I realized as if I had been hit in the head.

*So that was what I'd been worried about.*

If all the status abnormalities were over with this tour, I didn't know if I would continue to stay as Park Moondae.

*So this time, I really wanted to do it while I still could...?*

It was too sentimental... I wasn't a kid.

I erased the sentence that crossed my mind at the moment and thought of a nuance that made more sense.

*Yeah. I guess I want to do well while I am still performing.*

The stage was more fun than I thought, and it was rewarding work. In addition, the pay was good, so there was no area to blame.

After being immersed, I wasn't certain this would be long-term, so my standards might have gotten a bit blurred.

While I was lost in thought for a while, Keun Sejin spoke as if driving a wedge.

"Rather than hindering your performance with your injury, just recover first."

"..."

Yeah, fuck.

He was right.

*I shouldn't push this body too hard—it isn't even mine.*

Maybe I could continue to live as Park Moondae.

I sighed.

"All right."

"...!"

"I already have an alternative, so I'll look at the condition tomorrow morning and think about it again."

Bae Sejin, who had been listening quietly, suddenly shouted.

"D-Don't look at it by yourself! Go to the hospital!"

"...Um, yes."

It was my solo stage anyway, so I could ignore them and push ahead, but let's accept it this time.

"Well thought out."

"Make sure you go to the hospital with the manager tomorrow."

"I will."

*It would be funny if the doctor said I am fine.*

I drew an unanswerable hopeful prediction and postponed the decision until tomorrow morning.

By the way, it was a meaningless move.

Because early the next morning, an argument broke out among the people who had concert tickets.

A pretty big one, too.

I calmly added to the members who responded.

"Yeah. But only after we're done in Seoul."

To put it bluntly, all gazes were pouring out to me, asking what kind of nonsense it was.

It didn't matter. I said it firmly.

"I came up with a good way to explain it to the audience. This is the best."

"Moondae."

"Once Seoul is over, I'll stop doing this until I'm fully recovered."

It was funny that I persuaded them to do my own stage, but anyway, these guys reluctantly agreed.

They would get criticized a little, but if I had to revise the performance anyway, it would be better to focus on 'prevention' rather than highlighting my injury.

*Either way, it's another device error, they'll get criticized for their poor handling.*

It was none of my business. Anyway, the person in charge had been changed to someone more professional, so there wouldn't be any accidents anymore.
