# Chapter 207 - Behind the Screen

It was a tumultuous month.

*– Excuse me, can we get this choreography draft approved?*

*– I'm sorry, but many parts of the lyrics won't match the rhythm if they're revised like this...*

*– The outfits for the concept photos are ready.*

The deadline for the promotion was counting down while there was an infinite number of revisions to be made, so later on, we all, including the staff, had to work like crazy.

And of course, the pinnacle of it was the music video.

We concluded that no matter how much or by what means we worked on it, the editing couldn’t be finished by the deadline.

*It was gruesome.*

Therefore, the schedule was revised to release the song first on Tuesday, the day of the comeback, and then the music video at midnight on Thursday.

At the time, I was disappointed because the schedule seemed less than ideal... but unexpectedly, it had a positive side effect.

*Thanks to the fact that the song was released first, we made it to the charts without any bias.*

Steampunk Joseon as a theme in the music video was an entertaining element, but not exactly an approachable one.

If this unique feature affected the song's impression, it was highly likely that there would be an unnecessary fault in the popularity.

However, after drawing attention with an interesting teaser and getting people to listen to the song separately, the song’s appeal smoothly took off.

Thanks to this, the song was fluctuating between fourth and fifth place on the country's biggest music chart.

Frankly, I'd expected the song to sit at tenth place or lower, but it was doing surprisingly well.

*Will streaming numbers increase further once we perform and make the rounds on variety shows?*

So, this time, we decided to go all out and set up quite a few schedules for major domestic variety shows as a team. Also, to make use of the buzz created by the reality show.

*It will be worth looking forward to.*

Of course, for us to have a smooth future in the company... the most important thing was the global response.

For the record, I didn’t even expect to appear on the Billboard main chart.

*We don't even have that kind of international recognition yet.*

In the first place, if we were to go after Billboard, we should have released the song simultaneously in America on a Friday to match the chart and fully reflect the first week's scores after the comeback.

It should be easy enough to understand... that such infrastructure couldn't be built in three weeks.

*Damn it.*

Besides, we would have to aim for the album chart to even have a chance. The song chart wouldn't even be an option unless your international fanbase was at VTIC's level.

Hence, this time around, TeSTAR's global growth couldn't be measured by entering the Billboard charts, as we were releasing a single song for the promotions.

*We'll focus on proving that TeSTAR's global visibility has increased.*

This can be measured by the number of searches on TeSTAR in global search engines, the number of views per country on platforms, and the degree of increase in the size of the tour.

And the music video reaction we were doing right now will make for great WeTube content to drive that.

"Oh, it's starting."

"Woohoo!"

It showed a great attitude for the members to talk enthusiastically even with bleary eyes.

*Is it because of the paycheck?*

Bae Sejin had announced that he had paid off all his real estate mortgage loans.

Anyway, I also clapped my hands as sincerely as I could and looked at the monitor.
