# Chapter 224 - Back to Work

It was after vacation.

"You're here?"

"Yes."

As soon as we returned to the dorm, we shared the details we'd worked out regarding Kim Raebin's remote arrangement.

We'd told Raebin it would be hard on him, but preparing two versions of everything would be difficult for the group too, since we still didn't know whether he could participate.

*Actually, it's heavy labor.*

We were doing all this on the assumption that our comeback schedule would stay the same.

If the others objected to the decision the three of us had made without them, we couldn't exactly blame them.

Of course, I didn't think these guys would protest.

"...So, we finished talking in the direction of preparing the album remotely while Raebin worked on the song at home."

"Wow."

"Nice job, hoo."

After gathering in the living room and giving a briefing, sighs of relief came out from here and there.

During the vacation, they'd all been texting to ask how Raebin was doing. It seemed each of them had been worrying quite a bit.

"Well, Raebin will be by his grandma's side... He's able to stay."

"I know. It's fortunate~ Then we'll give feedback on Raebin's work at nine o'clock on Thursdays?"

"That's right. And we'll keep other communication as brief as possible."

It was a compromise that would satisfy people who were focused on Kim Raebin's peace of mind and the people who were concerned about Kim Raebin's absence.

"It's just a different way of working; nothing has changed. Let's all make this album well together."

"Fighting~"

"F-fighting...!"

We were used to taking on extra work, so that hadn't been a major problem.

I hadn't even needed to think of how to convince the members.

*The agency staff would probably react much the same way...*

That only left one thing to check.

...I'd have to check whether the company was paying us overtime.

However, it was not without exception.

Because one member had gone pale.

"...That's great, Kim Raebin."

It was natural, but it was Bae Sejin. Even after all the topics had passed, his response came a moment later.

*Though the words themselves sound sincere.*

It was clear that his eyes went dark when he imagined a future where he had to learn two choreography sequences separately.

Still, his recovery was surprisingly quick.

Let's see what Bae Sejin said right after the choreography formation discussion.

"Then, shall we talk about the choreography first?" Chungwoo said.

"Oh~ Since the number of people is different, why don't we ask for separate formations?" Keun Sejin asked.

"Hmm... Wouldn't it be better to keep the changes to a minimum for the vocalists?" Chungwoo asked.

Ryu Chungwoo made a rough drawing, but everyone knew who he was trying to be considerate of.

And the reaction of the other party was this.

"...No, that won't be necessary. You don't need to worry about me."

"Is that so?"

"...Yes!"

"Oh! Cool!"

"K, hm."

Bae Sejin seemed to be secretly happy with Cha Eugene's response.

*...I get it roughly.*

There was a reason why he had a small amount of confidence.

Maybe it was because of his latest reputation.

I recalled that right before the vacation, he was secretly watching a WeTube video titled 'No Longer the Group's Weak Spot: Bae Sejin' in the corner of the living room.

★$
– **[Despite the controversy surrounding him during ⟨Idol Inc.⟩, Bae Sejin has made amazing progress since his debut.]**

– **[Beyond simply not standing out in the group dance, he surprised fans by not missing the details of the choreography from start to finish in the fancam.]**

– **[Isn't this a sample of what you need to be at this level to attach a 'growth type'?]**
$★

Even with Bluetooth earphones on, the subtitles of such videos were so large that I could still watch them.

Most of the comments seemed to be surprisingly favorable.

★$
–-#2 hours ago#- Bae Sejin really surprised me. Seeing variety shows, he was cute and worked hard, so I wanted to support him ㅋㅋㅋ [ 👍 👎 ]
$★

Hmm, I also thought of Bae Sejin's face, who couldn't control his lips as he secretly clicked 'Like' on a comment like this.

Anyway, since his debut, his public reputation had been on the rise after going through the hotteok-making reality show, which meant that he himself seemed to have been positively motivated.

*Still, we're in trouble if we overdo it unnecessarily.*

He shouldn't burn out during our promotional period and interfere with the tour, so he needed to let his shoulders loose.

"Then, we won't make any special requests of the choreographer—"

"Actually, I think that's a good idea."

"Huh?"

"Minimizing changes. That would help, I think."

I deliberately stared at Bae Sejin and Ryu Chungwoo in turn.

Why? Wasn't I a vocal member too?

Ryu Chungwoo put on a smile on his face and lowered his head, pretending to tidy up.

"Hmm... okay. Then, Sejin, would it be okay if we asked for the choreography to stay similar in both versions? Moondae would prefer that, too."

"...O-okay."

"Yes, yes, then let's decide it like that~"

When I met Keun Sejin's eyes, he raised and lowered his eyebrows.

It seemed like he was asking why I was taking care of it. He was quite a consistent guy.

"Have you contacted the company?"

"Yes. I asked for a double shooting schedule..."

I rubbed my chin.

"Now... We just need to proceed quickly, starting with the concept of the album."

"...How much time do we have?"

"Probably a week at most."

"Okay."

A heavy, determined mood settled over the living room.

We suddenly felt Raebin's absence—he'd gotten so good at organizing things in spreadsheets.

Our next concept also needed to stand apart from everything we'd done before.

That meant we had to organize our ideas clearly.

"Today, let's quickly check the contact with the people involved, and start in earnest tomorrow."

"Great."

"I-I'll do my best...!"

And so work on our album began.

***

Scheduling a busy freelancer was more cumbersome than one may think, and there were many things to consider.

It was because the freelancer himself shouldn't be too overworked, but the schedules of the other people involved needed to be properly arranged as well.

Even if that freelancer was in an idol group?

The things that needed to be considered were multiplied.

That was why a Management Office was essential...

*I've never been happy with the way they work, though.*

It was more reassuring to keep an eye on things myself.

For example, contact with Kim Raebin.

★:
-KRB: Kim Raebin: 
-KRB: #b[_Title track—Verse 2—Lyrics (1)_]b# 
-KRB: #b[_Title track—Verse 2—Lyrics (2)_]b#

-KRB: I worked on the rap for the second verse today. Please listen and vote for whichever version you prefer.

-KRB: Thank you as always.
:★

Raebin sent his music right on schedule. I sent a reply.

★:
PMD: Good job. I'll send you feedback quickly.-
:★

Then, after receiving this, the opinions of the members were collected and reviewed by the A&R Team, usually by the next morning.

And if I sent it back to Kim Raebin in the evening, another work would come on the next contact date.

Originally, Kim Raebin would have communicated directly with the A&R Team, but... Because the current situation was like this...

I added a text while thinking about it.

★:
PMD:Tell me if there's anything particularly difficult or worrying you while you're there-
:★

Because of this problem, we needed some flexibility outside of work.

Fortunately, the answer came quickly without any hesitation.

★:
-KRB: Nothing out of the ordinary has happened.
:★

His grandmother's condition hadn't changed for better or worse since the operation.

*...Still, this period shouldn't be too long.*

As I was contemplating the answer, messages arrived in quick succession.

★:
-KRB: Thank you for always worrying!

-KRB: (_Image_)
:★

The attached image was... The phrase 'Here's a courageous smile to power your day!' was flashing in rainbow colors on the rose picture.

★:
-KRB: My grandfather sent it, but I really like the phrase, so I'm sharing it. I hope you have a great day.
:★

I chuckled.

*The day's almost over, you know.*

It was already 10 p.m.

Even so, I understood that he wanted me to have a good day even for the remaining two hours.

★:
PMD: Okay. Sleep well too.-
:★

After checking Kim Raebin's polite reply, I turned off my smartphone screen.

In front of me, I heard the sound of shoe soles sliding.

"Let's go again."

"Yes!"

This was the reason why no one except me could reply in real time even though Kim Raebin uploaded the file in the group message room.

It was because we were in the process of learning the choreography of 6 people, excluding Kim Raebin.

No one else could reply, so I handled the responses for the time being.

"Moondaemoondae, did you check Raebin's song well~ Which part?"

"The start of the second verse."

"Okay, is it over?"

It meant rejoining practice soon. I stood next to him before entering the chorus loop.

"Let's go again, Moondae."

"Yes."

The entire composition of the song had been released, and arrangements and some recording-related work were in progress, so a tentatively edited demo song was coming out of the speaker.

For reference, I was the one who recorded it.

When I recorded the different parts, I mimicked each member's singing style. The others laughed quite a lot when they first heard the demo.

"Hahahaha! Is that supposed to be me? Not bad, Moondae!"

"Try me again! Me!"

"Moondae, you should do more impersonations."

I had a feeling that the next time we went on a variety show, it'd be a short story they'd tell.

...Yes, I was pissed off again. I had to stop thinking about it now.

I focused on the choreography moves.

"One, two..."

The choreographer watched my movements and nodded.

"You're doing really well. Just remember to keep your head raised."

"Thank you."

"And by the way, I like the song, too. It came out well."

It was a compliment that any idol who was about to release an album would be happy with.

Bright answers came from here and there.

"Thank you!"

*Interesting.*

For reference, this choreographer was the same choreography trainer I met during ⟨Idol Inc.⟩.

He pretended to be natural, but his attitude of spitting out vicious words had gone, and he had become much more cautious and kind.

Even when Bae Sejin was slow to react or made a mistake, the choreographer no longer dared to lash out or look down on him.

*This is society.*

In particular, he was almost polite to Seon Ahyeon, whom he had uttered abusive words to during the audition.

I couldn't say this was a very good thing, but no etiquette injector worked as well as social position when working.

"...Ahyeon, can you do a little spin right here?"

"Yes, yes!"

*It seems that Seon Ahyeon himself doesn't even care.*

Well, choreographers had come out with more pressure because of show business, so there was no need to treat them as people with particularly bad personalities.

Besides, the most important choreography came out well.

There was a reason why this choreographer's draft was chosen as the main one.

*It's almost the first time I've seen this style.*

"End of verse 1."

"You worked hard~"

"Thank you!"

The guys who fell here and there seemed to be having a hard time, but they didn't look too anxious.

Apart from being busy, it was thanks to the sense of security that things were going well step by step.

*It's good.*

"Moo-Moondae. Here is some water."

"Thank you."

I drank water while imagining the rest of my schedule in my head.

Either way, there were no problems.

*Still, I'd like to go with the best scenario with Kim Raebin joining us.*

Because the image of this album suited Kim Raebin so well.

I came to that conclusion and took a short break to the fullest.

"Ten minutes is over."

"Yeah~"

However, there was one point that could not be taken into account during this process.

That I hadn't been monitoring the internet deeply for weeks.

And under the water, the posts of people who knew were already spreading.

★$
[Kim Raebin's probably not going to participate in the upcoming promotional cycle]
$★

I realized what was happening too late, and I regretted my inattention.

"Ahyeon-ah."

"Yes?"

I managed to convince my roommate that day.

Therefore... It meant that the season of only watching animal videos on WeTube was over.
