# Chapter 77 - First Fansign, I

After practicing the choreography again on Sunday evening, we collapsed, and when we woke up, it was already the next week.

This meant it was time to check the first week’s album sales.

The result was... Terrifying.

“...670,000 copies?”

“That’s right!”

I asked the manager one more time.

“The physical sales volume, not the retail pre-order volume?”

“...I didn’t think you’d know about that. Anyway, it really is the sales volume!”

“...”

For reference, pre-order volume referred to the number of pre-orders from retail stores in anticipation of the album being sold.

*Retailers order more than they’ll sell in the first week. I thought that was what the number meant.*

670,000 copies were sold.

*I can’t believe it.*

No matter how high the recent album sales inflation was, it was a sales volume that really made you feel paralyzed.

It was even more amazing when considering that TeSTAR’s fanbase was formed mainly in Korea from the former audition program.

It was even bigger than it was originally.

*Wasn’t it originally 600,000?*

Certainly, the group that originally debuted through ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ had sold 600,000 copies in its first week.

I couldn’t figure out where the hell the 70,000 came from...

“...We’ve set a new record for male idol debut album sales.”

“U-Uuuh...”

“Holy moly.”

The other guys were overwhelmed rather than happy.

If it had been 300,000 copies, they would have happily rolled on the floor, but seeing twice that figure scared them a little.

Of course, most of the guys soon came to their senses. This meant that they were starting to make realistic judgments.

“...Can we win first place next week?”

“...!!”

In an instant, the tension rose.

Ryu Chungwoo opened his mouth.

“How many albums did VTIC sunbae-nim sell this week?”

“Let me check.”

Kim Raebin pulled out his phone and immediately started searching.

Weekly sales could be checked immediately on the album chart.

★$
[LONG NIGHT / VTIC 312,608]
$★

“...!”

“Wow.”

It was an unbelievable number considering it was the second week.

*Even if this includes the last day of their first week... That’s still ridiculous.*

It was at the level of total sales that most successful boy idol groups managed.

Now that it came to this, I got curious about this album’s first-week sales.

“...Wait a moment.”

I tapped Kim Raebin’s smartphone screen several times to check VTIC’s album sales in the first seven days.

★$
[1,872,863]
$★

“...”

Ah, it was hopeless.

“Wow~!”

“Haha.”

“If we work hard, the day will come when we win first place!”

The tension instantly disappeared. Members began to laugh and cheer each other up.

We already expected it, but with such a gap between us, the desire to win also disappeared.

Our album sales were at least half of theirs, so we still had a chance, but we were so far behind on the music charts that catching up looked difficult.

*After the first week, there’s no chance of an album sales spike... It’s probably going to be tough to take first place even in the second week.*

Although the music ranking continued to rise slowly, it was also uncertain whether we could push out VTIC, which was stuck in first place.

I shrugged.

“Shall we get ready to go out?”

“That would be nice.”

“Today too, fighting~!”

Members dispersed to change out of their pajamas into outdoor clothes.

The manager murmured, ‘This isn’t how it was supposed to go...’ but soon sighed deeply as if he had accepted reality.

*I guess there was something he didn’t get to say?*

Well, he would take care of it.

It was right after getting in the car that the manager started again.

“Your fan signing events start two days from now, do you know that?”

“Oh!”

“Already?”

The members were nervous. We heard about it when we debuted, but we forgot the date because of our busy schedule. The manager nodded.

“Yeah, they bought so many albums and cheered for you passionately. Do your best~! Treat them well with sincerity~!”

“Ah, of course!”

Perhaps the reason why he talked about the album sales first was to lead into this topic. He seemed relieved to be able to say it now.

*A fan-signing event, huh?*

If we didn’t want to make a mistake while talking with over a hundred people in a row, we needed to be mentally prepared.

However, the advice seemed to have the opposite effect on one member. Seon Ahyeon’s face turned white at once.

“I-I-I don’t think I can.”

“Why’s that?”

“Huh? I-I’m not good at talking...?”

“...? Hyung is good at speaking Korean right now.”

Seon Ahyeon, who was caught up in Cha Eugene’s nonsense, started to get more confused.

I sighed and interrupted.

“You’re cautious, so you’re less likely to make a mistake. Don’t worry too much.”

“...T-Thank you.”

Seon Ahyeon replied with a faint smile, but it wasn’t an expression of someone who wasn’t worried.

*It’s understandable.*

We hadn’t even talked much to fans in the first place.

We sometimes saw fans up close as we went back and forth during schedules, but usually, we only had time for a quick wave or smile as fanservice before getting in the car.

Sometimes, the people who followed us near the dorm or at the convenience store were sasaengs rather than fans, so those encounters didn’t count.

*Any members would be nervous to have a one-on-one conversation...*

“I hope time flies~ I can’t wait to talk to our fans face-to-face!”

“...”

Except for him.

Keun Sejin in the front seat was humming and rummaging through his smartphone.

*He’s confident, is he?*

Were these two the perfect examples of opposite ends of the idol spectrum ahead of a fan-signing event?

...What about me?

*I don’t know.*

I had been paid a few times to take pictures as a substitute at public fan signing events for famous idols.

But I’d never heard what the idols and fans were talking about during the signing.

This was because the place itself was noisier than expected, so most sounds were muffled.

*Well... I’ve seen a lot of headbands.*

It was kind of embarrassing, but doable, so let’s move on.

I clicked my tongue as I searched my memory; only a vague impression remained. Nothing useful.

*Should I find out what kind of conversation they usually have?*

We were on the road, but there was no reality show camera in the car. I immediately logged in to SNS on my smartphone and started searching.

However, before I could move to the popular posts tab, a recent post that just popped up caught my eye.

“...!”

Wait.

The image attached to the post was a screenshot showing that the uploader had bought 100 copies but still failed to get into the fan signing event.

“...”

*120?*

To be honest, I thought of this as soon as I saw it.

*...How much is this?*

The regular price of TeSTAR’s debut mini-album was 15,000 won. Even if the discount rate was raised through group purchases, etc... about 11,000 won would be the lowest price.[1]{Around 10 USD and 7 USD.}

*Even at that price...*

It was 1.32 million won.[2]{Around 880 USD.}

“...Huh.”

My brain spun.

*Even if the conversation is long, it would be a minute or two.*

Since there were seven TeSTAR members, each of us had to make the interaction worth about 180,000 won for every fan.[3]{Around 120 USD.}

*Isn’t the efficiency too low?*

Can I make 1 minute of conversation worth 180,000 won? Of course, I’m not confident. -# Around 120 USD #-

*Should I just... do whatever they ask me to do?*

Other than that, I couldn’t think of any special measures. I put down my smartphone and frowned.

Even though I had taken pictures at fan signs with a cut-off of more than 100 albums, I never thought I’d end up on the idol’s side.

I broke out in a cold sweat.

The small stage and audience seats could accommodate 300 people, so there was plenty of room for the 100 people waiting in line.

And the staff distributed random numbers to decide the order.

*Hooray, a single-digit number!*

Happy that she would be able to talk before Moondae got tired, the fansite master quickly set up her camera.

And when it was just past 7:30 pm.

TeSTAR entered the stage.

In an instant, deafening cheers filled the small art hall. Usually, camera clicks would have been the loudest sound, but this was their first fansign, and excitement won out.

TeSTAR, who waved their hands and entered with slight embarrassment, bowed in front of the long autograph tables set on the stage.

**“Take your STAR! Hello, we’re TeSTAR!”**

“Wooow!”

It was an official greeting befitting of a rookie who was not used to the fan signing event yet. The fans cheered enthusiastically, but on the inside, they were chanting the names of the members they were rooting for.

The same was true of Park Moondae’s fansite master.

*Moondae, black hair! Ah, I love it!!*

She’d been to the pre-recordings several times, so it wasn’t her first time seeing Park Moondae’s black hair, but she still liked it every time.

It might be because Park Moondae had black hair when she first became a fan.

*Blonde hair is good too, but as expected, it has to be black.*

She briefly thought of her friend who was a stubborn blonde enthusiast, but soon forgot about it and quickly focused on the camera.

She had to take a good picture and share that face with the world.

*I think he gets more handsome every time I see him.*

Park Moondae had a strangely refreshing air with his black hair and white summer stage uniform, probably from the album photoshoot.

*This isn’t just my opinion as a fan. It’s the truth...!*

While she was muttering like that, the greeting ended, and TeSTAR slipped back behind the tables and sat down.

*Moondae is at the very end.*

She congratulated herself on bringing gifts for the other members, just in case. It would be kind of weird if she walked with her hands full of gifts but didn’t give any away until the last seat, where Moondae was.

“We’ll start soon~ Please check your numbers!” a staff member called.

Soon, the fan signing event began in earnest.

Because she got a single-digit number, she could start getting autographs without waiting long. When she was called up, Moondae was about to meet the first fan in line.

The first in line was Ryu Chungwoo.

“Hello!”

“H-Hello.”

Seeing Ryu Chungwoo greeting first, she stuttered a little.

*He’s handsome, too.*

She had seen him being called nicknames like the hot Confucian man on SNS, but seeing him up close, she could see why.

*You look like an athletic college student who will appear as the main character in a webtoon...*

They exchanged a little small talk, and after a high five, she handed over a small red ginseng box.

“Wow, thank you!”

“Eyy~, no, it’s fine~!”

The first signing ended warmly. And next.

“Where can I sign for you?”

It was child actor Lee Sejin. Lee Sejin, who gave a gentle, courteous impression, was not particularly enthusiastic, but he wasn’t insincere either.

“Who are you here to see?”

Unexpectedly, he even asked a question first.

“I came to see all of TeSTAR~!”

“Is that so? Thank you.”

Lee Sejin answered calmly without showing any attitude and finished the autograph neatly.

*He’s more refreshing than I thought.*

Somehow, her impression of the members improved with each autograph.

It was the same for the members who signed next.

“Um, then I will try to find a blank space where I can sign without covering any of our faces...”

She also liked Kim Raebin, who signed shyly with a tense look, contrary to his sharp impression.

“Which album cover do you like best? I love violet the best!”

She found it cute that Cha Eugene was more excited than the fans receiving his autograph; his signature was a jumble of scribbles, and he bombarded them with questions.

*At the beginning of Idol Inc, I really hated him.*

She praised herself, relieved that she hadn’t posted anything negative about him at the time.

And then Keun Sejin appeared.

“Welcome~ How’s your first fan signing event today? I was really nervous!”

“I was nervous, too!”

“Eyy, you don’t look nervous~!”

With a friendly tone, Keun Sejin asked her what she did during the day, how she was feeling, how she was doing now, and even her worries.

In addition, he offered her a high-five without her even asking.

“See you next time~!”

“Yes...!”

*...I think we’ve gotten closer?*

She felt like she had gotten close to a handsome college student representative. She could guess he was this friendly to everyone he met, but that didn’t stop her from developing a crush.

While she was impressed by the popular guy vibe she felt from a conversation that was only two-and-a-half minutes long, she grew tense again at the thought of Moondae’s turn coming soon.

*Just two more...!*

Filled with expectations, she was thinking of passing Seon Ahyeon quickly.

*...He’s too handsome to just hurry past...*

“N-Nice to meet you...!”

Seon Ahyeon smiled shyly and listened carefully to her words. Thanks to this, she got an autograph almost as if she had been possessed.

Seon Ahyeon even put a sincere ‘P.S.’ underneath it.

*...He really is a national treasure.*

That face and that personality. There was no way to fake both.

...And now, what was left was the long-awaited favorite!

She gulped and turned to Moondae.
