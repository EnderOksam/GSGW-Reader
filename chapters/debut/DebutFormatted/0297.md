# Chapter 297 - Another Time Traveler

"Hyungs are bad. You tricked me."

"Ah~ Don't say that, Eugene!" Lee Sejin said.

Cha Eugene had seemed genuinely shocked by the lasers going off during rehearsal, and after watching the actual joint stage, he declared that he was boycotting all conversation with us.

The reason was, 'How could you do something that cool without me?'

*Well, it only lasted 30 minutes.*

After TeSTAR's stage ended, he started talking to us again, probably feeling better.

I spoke, pulling the towel off my head.

"You can have a solo stage with lasers next time and swing them around like dual lightsabers."

"Oh!! ... Umm, it would've been better if I could've done that today!"

"Today's just a one-off. If you do it at our own concert, you can do it for months. Wouldn't that be better?"

"That's right~ It's better if you can do it several times!" Lee Sejin said.

"That's right!" Eugene agreed.

"Then it's better to wait."

"OK, got it!" Cha Eugene said in English, then switched back to Korean.

"So you guys didn't trick me."

By now, steering Cha Eugene's mood was ridiculously easy.

After chatting with Cha Eugene a little longer, I continued walking.

Compared to the end of a normal concert, my body was much lighter.

*Doing five songs isn't that hard for me anymore.*

Compared to a full solo concert, this was practically nothing. The other groups involved probably felt the same way.

I opened the door to the waiting room and entered.

"Hello, TeSTAR sunbaenim!"

I looked at the cameras waiting for us in the waiting room, along with the rookie group.

*The same went for these guys, who had quietly played their cards well.*

"It was really cool. Thank you so much for teaching us and helping us!"

"We're the ones who are thankful~ Fighting on your debut!" Lee Sejin said.

And just like that, we said our official goodbyes to the debut group from ⟨Idol Inc.⟩, which would no doubt be included in the behind-the-scenes footage. It'd probably be released later on TNet's WeTube channel.

Watching the members bow to us, I thought to myself.

*They went with the safe choice.*

Picking a famous song to tide them over wasn't some incredible strategy that could turn the tables. An obviously safe choice like that was bound to draw some criticism.

But by avoiding direct comparisons and ridicule, they'd at least shown that they knew where they stood.

*Or maybe... They were being extremely cautious because they already knew what would happen if they weren't.*

As soon as the cameras were turned off and the set cleared, I called one of them over.

"Heeseung-ah."

Gold 2 came over.

"Yes, yes?"

"Shall we talk for a minute?"

"Yes!"

The rookies made idle remarks among themselves, along the lines of 'They must be really close!'

Gold 2 happily followed me out of the room.

Lee Sejin asked quietly.

"Heeseung didn't do anything wrong, did he?"

"No."

I immediately denied it. Lee Sejin shrugged and waved us off. He probably assumed we were going to talk about the performance or something.

However, I brought Gold 2 all the way to the van parked in the parking lot.

"...?"

"Get in."

At this point, Gold 2 seemed to realize that this wasn't going to be a polite little conversation and immediately bowed his head.

'Did I do something wrong!?' was clearly visible on his face.

*Could I really call it a mistake...?*

I closed the car door and tossed him a canned drink.

"Oh, thank you."

Gold 2 quickly caught it and opened the can. I waited until he'd taken a few sips.

And when the timing was right...

*...I didn't know I'd be the one saying this line this time.*

I wasn't that crazy guy from VTIC, after all. I suppressed the urge to sigh and opened my mouth.

"So... What year did you come back from?"

"...!! Ack."

Gold 2 choked, but thankfully didn't spit out his drink.

*Too bad for him, but that reaction alone was as good as an answer.*

"Uh, uh... I don't..."

I took out another canned drink, opening and closing my hand around it as I spoke.

"You were way too obvious."

His situation had been unique from the very beginning.

*It's rare for a contestant from a previous season to take Rank 1 on the same show.*

Especially if that audition show was successful.

Because the production team and viewers preferred fresh faces unique to that season.

*But this guy still managed to debut at Rank 1.*

What was more, he'd been way too calm when Chae Seodam, the previous Rank 1, was cut from the debut group.

– *Ah... No wonder it felt kind of strange.*

That was the extent of his reaction, as though he trusted our decision completely.

*The response was too skillful for his age.*

So I checked his status window the next time we met.

And this was at the very bottom of his stats.

★!
@c@Status Abnormality:@c@
#><[Bounce Back]><#

I'm going to be an idol no matter what it takes!

Failure guaranteed unless the team harmonizes within a set period.
!★

The wording alone was all the hint I needed.

It was a status abnormality much like Cheongryeo's 'Revision' or my 'Debut or Die'.

And there was what this guy said when we'd successfully gotten rid of Chae Seodam.

– *Hoo... I mean, how did that hyung even get so close to debuting again– Oh, I don't know!*

He'd probably been about to ask how that hyung had gotten so close to debuting again, only to stop himself. That was all the circumstantial evidence I needed.

Gold 2, Kwon Heeseung, had come back from the future.

Maybe the one who came after me.

"You talked as though Chae Seodam had debuted before, and your actions in general were way too suspicious."

"......"

Of course, Kwon Heeseung didn't seem to have any intention of admitting it. It was understandable. The biggest problem was trust. From his point of view, he had no reason to tell me that he came from the future.

*I've got to give him some bait of my own, too.*

I had information I needed to get out of him. I'd have to be willing to admit a little about myself.

*I'll start by giving this much.*

*Click.*

I muttered as I opened the can of drink.

"I'm from the future too."

"Heok."

Where had he been storing all that air?

Gold 2 clamped his hands over his mouth and stared at me.

"......"

"......"

After a fairly long silence, he nodded and murmured.

"...No wonder! I thought you were way too good at this."

*Did it work?*

"Uh, yes."

"Wow, I thought I was the only one. I was even wondering whether the past I remembered was just some sort of dream or vision I'd had."

Words poured out of Gold 2 as though he'd had a lot of pent-up frustration.

"So other people came back too, right? It's not just me. Usually it's only one person when you watch dramas."

There seemed to be a misunderstanding, but I put it aside and encouraged him to keep talking.

I needed to understand the situation.

"Okay. Then... Did you come back because something didn't work out for you?"

However, Kwon Heeseung answered awkwardly, looking like he was about to break into a sweat.

"Uh, I, I... I wasn't an idol."

*...What?*

***

"...So you were a model?"

"Yes."

We returned to the agency because it looked like the conversation would take a while.

Gold 2 said, 'I thought my heart was going to stop,' and started guzzling green tea.

"And... A lot of things happened while I was modeling. I really started to think I'd chosen the wrong career path."

"Hmm."

"So I quit modeling altogether and chose to appear on ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ again this time."

Gold 2 said he'd gained some confidence from watching this season so avidly, looking rather proud of himself.

"Then, Chae Seodam..."

"Ah! That hyung goes down anyway because of dating rumors and controversy about his personality as soon as he debuts. So I had no qualms about letting him go early!"

*I knew it.*

I was about to ask about my future, but I let him talk a little more.

"No wonder hyung was so on top of everything, like a ghost! I'm really... so relieved. I liked what was happening, but I couldn't make sense of it."

Gold 2 looked as though a weight had been lifted off his shoulders.

"Usually people go back to the past because the world's about to end or something, right? Do you think this could be something like that?"

One thing was certain: this guy was under the impression that I knew the same things about the future as he did.

And this was the first time he had come back to the past.

Judging from the description in his status window, this might be his first and only chance, just like mine.

*But his case seems a little different.*

I'd have to find out a bit more.

"Let's confirm one thing first. How many years did you go back?"

"Uh... I came back from the end of this year, so... I think about 11 months?"

"......"

*One year?*

"Isn't it amazing? A whole year! I have no idea what to make of it."

I was speechless for a moment and looked at him.

*That's... hardly time travel at all, is it?*

"I bought some stocks. Really... What did you buy, hyung? A lottery ticket, maybe? Oh, you wouldn't need it."

"Hmm."

That meant he had no useful information about the future for me. I smiled wryly and returned to my original objective.

I supposed I could tell him now.

"I came back a while ago. The future I came back from has already passed."

"Whoa."

The last time I used 'Reveal the Truth,' I found out some of my memories were missing... But for now, I set that aside and tried to connect the dots.

"Oh my god. So, did you find out anything about what was going on at that time?"

I intentionally rubbed my chin.

"I'm still trying to figure it out... If you don't mind, may I ask what you were doing right before you returned to the past?"

Based on the cases so far... It was likely a sensitive question, so I had no intention of pressing him if he hesitated to answer.

However, this guy's reaction was a bit different.

"What was I doing? Oh, that... It might sound a little silly."

Gold 2 smiled in embarrassment, breaking into a cold sweat.

"What was it?"

"I accidentally fell into the river and hit the bridge's foundations headfirst... You could say I was really unlucky..."

"......"

I stared at him.

"N-no! I was trying to help someone else! I wasn't doing anything stupid or anything!! A sacrifice, you know! There was an accident during filming. Wow, the safety standards were terrible..."

It started as an excuse, but Gold 2 soon launched into an indignant rant.

It sounded like he'd been through a lot while working as a model. Whenever he had time off, he'd hole up at home and try to forget the world existed.

Gold 2 sighed.

"...Anyway, as I fell, I thought things like 'I'll never come back to this filthy industry' or 'The answer was to become an idol instead'... I think that's what it was? And then I really became an idol–"

*Wait a minute.*

"Then, now... It turned out the way you wanted it to."

"Well, I guess so?"

Gold 2 nodded. I glanced over at him.

*The guy who came back to the past just once.*

*And what he wanted... 'To quit modeling and become an idol.'*

...I was starting to see a pattern.

I brought up the part that was bothering me.

"If you'd come back earlier, you could've debuted with me in the previous season, couldn't you?"

"Uh, uh..."

Gold 2 rubbed his head with a shy expression.

"Um, hyung. I'm sorry, but I don't think..."

"...??"

"That season was such a damn mess. I don't think I'd want to go through it again..."

"......"

"Ah! And to be honest, I don't even know if I'm good enough to debut in that lineup! You were all just ridiculously good."

...I had nothing to refute.

*Anyway, he hated his job.*

I tapped the desk with my finger and asked the question I'd finally arrived at.

"Then, the time you came back to... Would you say it was the perfect time to achieve what you wanted right before you returned?"

"What? Uh..."

Gold 2 thought about it for a moment, perhaps never having seen it that way before, but soon nodded.

"That's right! I was able to redo only the parts I wanted to."

"...!"

I was sure. I intertwined my fingers.

*He went back to the point he most wanted to return to.*

I recalled the vision of Cheongryeo I'd seen the last time I used 'Reveal the Truth.'

That bastard's monologue.

– *If it were me, I wouldn't have done it like that.*

...He wanted to start over from the very beginning to become an idol again.

Perhaps... 'Keep repeating it until you succeed.'

And he'd been sent back to a time before his debut.

*I get it.*

Everything was starting to make sense.

The system. That bastard wasn't just sending people back to the past.

*It's granting their wishes.*

Desires, regrets, whatever you called them... In the end, their wishes were being answered.

Following that line of reasoning, the status abnormalities might work along the same lines. The penalties were designed to spur the person toward fulfilling their wish.

*It fits perfectly.*

There was just a problem.

...It was me.

*I don't remember wishing for anything like this.*

Compared to the cases of Cheongryeo and Gold 2, there was a clear disconnect in mine.

No matter how drunk or depressed I'd been, it was hard to imagine that I would've wanted to become an idol in someone else's body out of the blue.

Then... Was this the wish of 'Park Moondae'?

Why?

"......"

First of all, I needed to look into the details further to figure out how the mechanism worked.

And to do that, I'd have to compare cases.

"Hyung?"

"...Hmm. Wait a minute."

After thinking for a moment, I pulled out my smartphone.

★:
Are you on tour?-
:★

A moment later, I got a reply...

★:
- VTIC Shin Cheongryeo Sunbaenim: On the move

- What's going on?
:★

Ultimately, there was only one thing left to do.

Cross-examine.
