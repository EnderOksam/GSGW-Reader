# Chapter 281 - ⟨TeSTAR Roommate⟩ Season 4, IV

TeSTAR's roommate content usually consisted of short video series that ended after one or two episodes.

But this time, the video had a season number, of all things, and there were zombies too?

Even if you couldn't analyze exactly why, you knew instinctively.

*This... It'll be fun!*

Expectations were bound to rise for content that already looked fun based on so little information.

The thumbnail and title had the magic to make even those who would think they'd 'watch it on the weekend' and then forget about it click on the video right away.

It was the same for the graduate student.

She'd intended to go home, shower, and get ready for bed before watching it with a reverent heart, but she ended up playing the video on the subway.

*Thank you, Bluetooth earphones!*

The first thing she heard was elegant classical music played on a stringed instrument.
