Even though I received a platinum grade on my first stage, I received so little attention in the beginning that all I got was a few comments.

"Introducing your dorms!"

Typical of a survival show, there was a deliberately cruel distinction between the two groups.

What could it be? The high-tier team got the good rooms, and the low-tier team got the not-so-good ones.

"Ah, this is the high-tier team..."

"But they also have the names of the guys in the low-tier team on them?"

"They must mean they can only use these rooms if they join the high-tier team!"

The dorm was split into two areas by the living room in the center, with all 14 people's name tags posted on both sides.

*Well, the low-tier team's rooms aren't that bad.*

They were better than the single-room apartment I'd lived in during college. They were basically motel-level rooms. The problem was that the high-tier team rooms looked like expensive hotel rooms by comparison.

"......"

Still, it was typical of a broadcasting station to show the low-tier team the high-tier team's dorm rooms, where they'd be able to sleep if they were promoted, not let them unpack, and then make them return to the lesser-quality rooms.

It wasn't as though the high-tier team members could look happy while the others walked away with gloomy expressions.

*That bastard will do whatever he can to send them away.*
