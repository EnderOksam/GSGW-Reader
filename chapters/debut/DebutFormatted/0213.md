# Chapter 213

The stage set was styled after an open, circular staircase amphitheater that you might find in an American park.

In short, it was a 360-degree view, which meant that the audience would be watching from all directions.

*That must be a new experience.*

I sat in the waiting room, camera in hand, as the invited audience members arrived and were screened one by one, and I was handed an envelope.

The glittery envelope was flashy, matching the American sentiment.

Along with the English explanation of the artificial intelligence MC, an interpreter could be heard through the in-ear.

**[What's in this envelope is the signature song, chosen by the participants!]**

**[Our KPOP mentor is going to learn all three of these songs in one hour.]**

**[Let's see what happens!]**

There was no need to look.

The producers had already told us which songs were going to be nominated.

*Of course, it wasn't just these three songs.*

About a dozen songs were submitted and it was promised that the songs within that would come out. Since they were pop songs, there might be some you didn't know at all.

And the three songs were probably chosen to have the contestants run around or pick a fight.

Mm, I think I could already see a contestant on the broadcast shouting, 'What I wanted is not this damn song!'

**[Are you nervous?]**

**[Well, a little bit.]**

After giving an obvious answer to an obvious question, I opened the envelope.

"Hmm."

Well, it was exactly as expected.

*I don't even need an hour.*

Let's relieve my fatigue from the airplane flight.

* * *

In the crowded set.

With wine and cosmetic bags in one hand (all of which were sponsored by T1), the audience crossed their arms and waited for the stage without much anticipation.

It was not a big-name show, and nothing was known about it other than it was a Netplus production.

**[I heard they'll be singing.]**

**[I'd rather they did a magic show!]**

The father and daughter shrugged and chatted.

Backstage, cameras were also rolling to capture the contestants listening to the buzzing of the audience.

At this 'Intro Stage' a total of eight people chose 'singing' as their talent to become a celebrity.

And the audience was given a button to press.

**[When the audience presses the button, the lights flash on the spot!]**

**[They will press the button if they feel 'good'.]**

**[And... Warning! They think everyone who comes out is a contestant, and they do not know the existence of our secret K-Pop mentor!]**

**[And now, all the participants of the 'K-pop' camp, let's go on stage!]**

The audience reflexively cheered as nine people appeared on the stage in a lift.

**[Whoa!]**

Participants reflexively waved or jumped around. There was no telling what kind of arrogance or immature behavior would be edited into the broadcast.

And in the corner was a young Asian man wearing a large hoodie.

However, unlike the other noisy participants, he didn't move much and was barely noticeable.

Of course, it was Park Moondae.

**[Now, the first song is... oh! It's Phil Garcia's 'Drive It'! Shall we go for a drive!?]**

It's a catchy song from a few years ago that skillfully blends R&B and pop, and it's a staple of audition shows for men in this country.

**[Ooh~]**

The audience reacted with a moderately polite level of appreciation to the standard set of songs.

However, when the following rules were explained, this appreciation began to mix with sincerity.

**[Contestants on stage will begin singing when they are randomly selected.]**

**[If you like them, feel free to press the button! The more lights are lit up, the longer you will get to hear the contestant sing!]**

**[And if there aren't many lights... well, next one!]**

Laughter erupted from the audience at the machine voice's cheeky remarks.

**[Hahaha!]**

If the response was good, you were allowed to sing more, and if the response was bad, that was it.

It was an interesting rule because it easily stood out and was cruelly provocative.

**[Then, starting with number one... Let's get started!]**

Music blared as participants took deep breaths and pretended not to be nervous.

Then, contestant Number 1 picked up the microphone with wavering eyes.

And the trembling voice produced a fairly decent tone.

♪ *Breezing with my car~ I've got a big engine and blinding head- headlights~* ♫

**[Oh...]**

Those who had been pressing the button consistently stopped as soon as the participant stuttered.

**[Okay, next!]**

As the flames waned, the AI bluntly pointed to number five.

♪ *Though, I'm no gentleman, I am wild, and that's my charm...* ♫

And so, the song began to circle the room.

Most people barely made it through one verse, and those who went a little further only made it through two or three.

It was unavoidable.

When you're being suddenly pointed out by an AI, you'll make mistakes that you wouldn't otherwise make because you have a visual reminder in front of you that 'if you make a mistake, you're done'.

♪ *My eyes that see, see... Shit!* ♫

**[Next!]**

The contestant who couldn't finish his sentence was booed by the now-energized audience.

The moment when the audience became more ruthless as they got accustomed to it.

**[Number 7!]**

Number seven, who was standing blankly in the corner, got called for the first time on the electronic display.

**[...]**

With a quick glance at the flashing lights, the young Asian man in a hoodie immediately opened his mouth as he picked up the microphone.

And he began to sing exactly when the song was supposed to enter.

As naturally as if it had always been his part.

♪ *SUCH SIGHTS MAKE ME LIVE*

*TODAY, TOO, THE CITY LIGHTS*

*RUN, RUN*

*BETWEEN THE STARS AND THE LIGHTS* ♫

**[Ooh!!]**

A soft voice echoed from the speaker with clarity.

The difference in tone and volume was clear as if he was using equipment of different quality alone.

*Tadadak! Tak! Tarararak!*

In an instant, lights of all colors began to flash with the sound of the audience hitting the buttons.

The song went on.

♪ *RUN, RUN*

*BETWEEN THE STARS AND THE LIGHTS*

*TONIGHT.* ♫

Even when he hit the high notes of the chorus, his voice didn't falter.

No, on the contrary, it was the melodic parts that really set the flavor of the song.

**[Wow!]**

The continuous mashing of the button did not slow down. The lights continued to twinkle, and Number 7 kept going.

But the problem was... that it was now entering the bridge.

When the song stops, the lights will inevitably die down. And that was when it was time to move on to the next person.

**[Mmm!]**

When the lyrics disappeared from the electronic display, some of the audience who quickly noticed the situation even expressed their disappointment.

♪ *DDU DDU DDURURU*

*DDU DDU DDU* ♫

**[...!]**

Number 7 began to hum the bridge.

It was not just humming, it was almost like a concert ad-lib with a huge range and technique.

**[Whoa!!]**

The buttons were being repeatedly hit again with dexterity, extending the life span of Number 7.

*Papapapak! Papapak!*

Lights were flashing from all directions.

At this point, some of the other contestants had their mouths open as they watched Number 7.

However, Park Moondae did not continue.

Instead, he stopped ad-libbing a syllable or two into the second verse and politely lowered the microphone.

**[Then, next!]**

The audience was left disappointed to hear the artificial intelligence MC shout, but there was a reason.

*This is the timing to step away.*

Because Number 7, Park Moondae, knew the flaw in this rule.

*People get used to stimuli.*

If you kept going, it was important to sing even better than the previous verse rather than just keep singing well.

People's reactions would fade after the climax if you just kept singing without thinking.

It wouldn't be better than the last climactic part they heard, so they wouldn't press the button.

So, whether it was a high note or an ad-lib, you had to vary it and keep the elements fresh and surprising.

This was also the reason why even those who sang it well earlier could not go over two or three bars.

That was why Park Moondae skipped the introduction of the second verse, where the tempo of the song was adjusted again.

*Well, I also need to include a picture that gives the contestants a chance.*

As long as you are a mentor, you need to give a picture of the concession to the participant so that there would be no bullshit on the domestic Internet saying, 'Am I the only one who feels it was cheap'.

And Park Moondae had already done the math.

*Given the number of participants... they'll probably give me one more at the end anyway.*

Based on the timing of the rotation, it seemed like they would go around the participants once and then he would have his turn again.

**[That's too bad, next!]**

Because Park Moondae had done such a good job earlier, the audience's judgment became unfortunately harsh, and most of the overwhelmed contestants dropped out after one verse.

And just as Park Moondae had planned, he was nominated again for the pre-chorus just before the final chorus.

**[Oooh!]**

Amid the anticipation and preemptive cheers of the already biased audience, Park Moondae did not let go of the song from the first verse.

♪ *RUN, RUN*

*HIT THE HORN AND CRUSH THE LIGHT*

*TONIGHT* ♫

Park Moondae's song occupancy time was 1 minute and 3 seconds in the end.

And just like that, the shockingly overwhelming first 'Intro Stage' that wasn't even a game was over.

**[The winner is... Number 7!]**

**[Whaaa!!]**

*I've paid my dues.*

Instead of bowing his head, Park Moondae smiled, waving his hand to suit the audience's sentiment.

He could already hear the production team drooling on the side of the stage, beyond the camera.

*If I perform well, they're going to use it on the trailer.*

And that prediction was correct.
