# Chapter 305 -  Composing Slump

Even in the conference room that day, Kim Raebin was clearly nervous.

– It's okay, Raebin, you've done well so far.

– Of course~ Even if it doesn't get picked, that just means we don't have ears! But there's no way that'll happen, right?

– That's right, I like Kim Raebin's song!

– ...Thank you.

At least after listening to the encouragement from the others, his complexion improved a little.

But when the results came in...

– Then, we're going to go with number 2, 4, 5, like this? Okay. Then our Raebin's number is...

– ...Number 3.

– ...!

His arrangement hadn't even made it into the list of candidates.

Literally, it was eliminated.

It was funny to put it so matter-of-factly, but it was something no one could have guessed.

*Kim Raebin's arrangement was eliminated...*

This was because up until now, this guy's arrangements had been put into the final drafts to the point where it was okay to call them a 'must'.

Of course, it hadn't always been chosen unanimously. However, his work had always played a crucial part in the process, and it was always reflected in the final draft.

So the members talked about his music being on the next album as if it were a given.

– Now, our Raebin will do a great job arranging music for concerts~

However, this was the first failure since debut.

*That was also after he had gone several days without sleep while pushing himself.*

"......"

I swiped through the material for the 'Concert Arrangement Plan Blind Voting Results', which also arrived via e-mail, and turned off the screen.

Then I folded my arms and leaned back.

*There must be several reasons.*

Maybe the direction was different, maybe the A&R Team had submitted a bunch of good drafts because the style suited them, or maybe they'd had plenty of time to work.

But why did it have to be now, of all times?

*It's no different from pushing Kim Raebin into a slump at the most crucial moment.*

However, it was foolish to overturn the results of the vote right away.

Knowing his personality, he wouldn't be happy about that, and we couldn't lower the quality just to spare his feelings.

*It's just aggravating the situation without any benefit.*

But it was foolish to leave him as he was.

"......"

I got up from my seat.

After the morning concert meeting, I went looking for him, figuring he'd quietly holed himself up in his room.

But I didn't have to go far. Kim Raebin was sitting on the sofa in the living room.

Next to him was Cha Eugene, who was quite resolute, Seon Ahyeon, who had a worried expression, and Keun Sejin, who looked troubled.

Cha Eugene held out something to Kim Raebin.

"Kim Raebin, eat this."

Kim Raebin was wearing a coat and socks. They seemed to have caught the guy trying to get out of the dorm.

"I'm not going to eat. I'm going to the studio for a little bit..."

"Why the studio?"

"...!"

They turned to me. Kim Raebin gulped and muttered in a low voice.

"If I don't work on correcting my shortcomings, I won't be able to produce results that people will be satisfied with in my next attempt..."

"You'll do better next time, Kim Raebin! It's okay if it doesn't work out once. There's no problem, it's okay!"

However, Kim Raebin seemed neither annoyed nor convinced by Cha Eugene's words.

"I'm not sure if it'll be okay or not."

He just muttered quietly.

"But since I failed this time, I can say that the probability of failing again has increased. So, in order to lower that probability, I need to prepare more..."

"......"

After a short silence passed...

"Raebin, why don't you take a break?"

"Th-that's right. We still have a lot of time left until the concert... You need to conserve your stamina...!"

Words poured out in an instant.

They must have thought that something bad would happen if he went to the studio in that state.

*I think so too.*

It would've been one thing if he'd been angry or frustrated, but this was bordering on obsession. Keun Sejin sat down next to him and patted him on the shoulder.

"Don't monkeys sometimes fall from trees?[1]{T/N: "Even monkeys fall from trees" Is a Korean proverb that describes how, just like a monkey who spends its whole life climbing trees, can fall off trees once in a while, even someone skilled in their profession or very good at something can make mistakes as well.} Let's take a break for the sake of our performances~ Rest is important too, you know?"

This... I'd have to help.

I opened my mouth.

"You've been running without resting so far, so you should rest your head. You know how muscles grow when you rest? It's not about muscles here, but the same principle applies. You haven't been getting enough rest."

"......"

"Go get some rest."

In the end, Kim Raebin reluctantly nodded. I held back a sigh.

The timing was bad.

***

A few days passed.

Kim Raebin did not go to the studio as promised, but his complexion was still not good. He still seemed to be reeling from his first real failure.

Still, after a busy period filming promotional videos for the tour, we had some downtime again because the drafts for some of the performances weren't ready yet.

*It's quiet.*

Maybe my body hadn't adapted to the changed schedule, but I woke up way too early in the morning.

*Should I drink some water?*

I left the room quietly so as not to wake my roommate.

And I found something unexpected. Light was coming from the indoor balcony adjoining the living room.

"...!"

The light was too close to be coming from outside the building. It looked like light from an electronic device...

*A break-in?*

There was no way some crazy bastard would be climbing down a rope from the rooftop... Well, there could be.

I approached the glass cautiously without dismissing the possibility. I had the emergency number up on my screen, ready to call if necessary.

But what was out there... There was a person, but not a stranger.

"Kim Raebin."

"...!"

Kim Raebin, who was sitting on the tiles, practically jumped out of his skin and looked around.

"Hy-hyung."

He was wearing headphones, and there was a program on his laptop screen... Anyone could tell he was working.

This kid secretly came out to the balcony at dawn and was squatting and working.

"What are you doing?"

"That is..."

"You're working on music, aren't you?"

Was this why he looked so tired during the day?

No wonder he couldn't recover. *This stupid guy...*

I opened the balcony door, strode in, and stood in front of him.

"I..."

Kim Raebin hurriedly tried to pick up his laptop, but accidentally hit some keys.

And the laptop switched between windows.

"...!"

Kim Raebin was... He was reading feedback while working.

★$
[Email] #f#[Inbox ×]#f#
--#from:#- xxxxx.email.com
--#to: me ⏷#-
– [Results of blind voting for the concert arrangement plan]
$★

A group email with the same subject as the one I saw a few days ago.

*This...*

Judging by how Kim Raebin had been acting recently, I doubted he was rereading it for anything constructive, like motivation or inspiration.

He must have been shocked enough.

And... That email wasn't just about the voting results.

To work out a reasonable combination, people had anonymously explained why they'd selected certain numbers and not others.

But from the tone, it was easy to guess who'd written each comment.

★$
--#from:#- xxxxx.email.com
--#to: me ⏷#-

-$lat Number 4 is the most fun lat$

-$lat Number 2 and 5 are smooth and good to listen to~ Go for it!^^ lat$

-$lat All are good, but number 5 is the most natural. I didn't choose the 1st and 3rd because I thought they'd be a matter of taste. lat$
$★

"......"

That was why we couldn't tell Kim Raebin that he'd simply been unlucky and that his song was actually the best. The members had voted too.

...And I also put one comment for each number there. Of course, even under Kim Raebin's candidate song.

★$
-$lat The sound is too much. lat$
$★

"......"

For the past few days, Kim Raebin had kept his peers' evaluations open, reading them over and over in silence.

And there wasn't a single comment that said anything good about Kim Raebin's song.

*Fuck... really.*

Could it be that he was trying to incorporate this feedback into his arrangement, at this hour?

"Listen."

I sat in front of him and made eye contact.

"The majority isn't always right. Actually, the public might like your arrangement more."

He stared up at me in silence.

"You don't have to worry too much about this result. It's just a concert arrangement."

Kim Raebin suddenly replied.

"However, hyung also judged that my arrangement was of poor quality."

"......"

"The feedback that says the sound is 'too much'... Isn't Moondae-hyung the one who wrote that?"

"I'm not a composer, so you don't have to believe my words unconditionally. Your own sense of music matters more than anyone else's words..."

"No!"

Kim Raebin lowered his head.

"Hyung's judgment of songs has always been objective and reliable. You've never been wrong before, but this time I didn't arrange it properly..."

"......"

It felt like getting hit in the head.

This had shocked him even more than I'd expected.

*He'd noticed that my judgment on songs was particularly good.*

It was thanks to the trait, 'Catch-All Ears (A)'. He probably didn't realize it was something unusual, like a skill, but he had such a good ear for music that he'd noticed it right away.

*– Moondae-hyung's choice is always great.*

He thought he'd been harshly criticized by the person he trusted most to judge songs objectively.

*Hoo...*

It left a bitter taste in my mouth.

I wasn't going to lie to myself. In fact, even now, I didn't think Kim Raebin's arrangement was very good.

*It was too bland for something Kim Raebin had made.*

I'd thought that the A&R Team was the one who created it.

And since the trait was what I got from the status window, it was definitely guaranteed that...

*Wait, status window?*

At that moment, suddenly, a few cases I had experienced passed through my head.

*...*

I immediately called up Kim Raebin's status window.

I hoped it wasn't the case, but there was a word I hadn't seen there that was added at the very end.

★!
@c@Status Abnormality:@c@
#><[Repeat Sign]><#
!★

Damn.

★!
#><[Repeat Sign]><#

You've lost your way, and it's on repeat.

A repercussion of 'Trait: Maestro (S)'. Your sensibilities are diminished.
!★

"B-but... By it being too much, do you mean there's too much packed into the music? So, if I take a little out here... Can you tell me what this sounds like? Is it better than before? Is it better?"

"......"

This... this wouldn't work.

It was clear that this guy was completely demoralized.

I get it now. The reason Kim Raebin seemed solid against criticism was that he was able to come up with an alternative right away.

But now that ability seemed to have completely deserted him. He'd been relying on it.

*Fuck...*

I received the headphones he gave me, but I didn't wear them. I asked him a question instead.

"...How does it sound to you?"

I heard Kim Raebin sniffle quietly.

"I don't know... I tried to incorporate as much of what people liked before as possible, hoping they'd like it again... I don't know if I did it right or not."

"Do what you want to do."

"I... I can't figure out what I want to do, either..."

I knew what this meant.

*The balance is broken.*

I knew that this guy had a humble side that sincerely tried to accept other people's opinions.

But at the same time, he had faith in his abilities.

An instinctive conviction that he could tell what was good and what wasn't.

So he would take in feedback based on his own judgment, accepting some of it and filtering out the rest.

He only did what made sense to him.

*That's what he's like at heart.*

It was like how he interpreted T-Holic's remarks as being similar to a family on his own.

But this time, things were a little different.

Far more people had praised him than he'd expected, and he was overwhelmed by the barrage of praise.

*It made him lose his confidence.*

The thought that he shouldn't disappoint anyone and the pressure to produce perfect results destroyed the joy.

In trying to please everyone, he lost the individuality that had made his music good.

However, when he received harsh criticism, he was completely confused and took the feedback too seriously. It was the first time his abilities had ever failed him.

*It's a vicious cycle.*

I held back the swearing.

And after calming the guy who was about to burst into tears, I confiscated the laptop.

"Stop this for now. You've got plenty to do even without this, and you're a talented guy. Let it go for now."

"......"

"I'm not saying this because I don't think you can do it, but if you do this and get insomnia, bigger troubles will happen."

"Once I finish one..."

"No."

I refused to give him back his laptop. After a moment of hesitation, I spoke.

"...If you're doing it for fun, it's not my business to interfere, but right now it's ruining your mental health. You can't even sleep."

"......"

Kim Raebin lowered his head.

I patted him on the shoulder, only to realize how terrible I was at this sort of thing.

*Fuck.*

A countermeasure was needed.

***

Even after that dawn, Kim Raebin faithfully participated in the schedule.

But his former liveliness was gone.

And one of the guys who noticed it found the reason in my room.

"Park Moondae, is that his laptop?"

Keun Sejin spoke seriously.

"Just let him work on his music. Not being able to is killing him."

"......"

After tugging roughly at my hair, I answered a beat late.

A solution I'd been mulling over for days.

"Then, rent us a mountain cabin."

"...? What?"

I wasn't kidding.

I remembered Kim Raebin's status abnormality.

A repercussion of the Maestro trait, which had increased his songwriting speed to almost industrial levels.

Kim Raebin's exceptional senses were deteriorating.

*In the end, he was obsessed with results and efficiency, so it backfired.*

Then it was simple.

We had to do something creative without a productive goal that had nothing to do with TeSTAR's music.

So, I started planning a healing course with terrible efficiency that I had never considered in my life.

It was a training camp for self-development.

"Let's just... Go to the mountains."

To the mountains, not toward some specific goal.
