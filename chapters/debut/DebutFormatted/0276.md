# Chapter 276 - Self-Destruction

To be honest, dating within the industry was something that had to be kept quiet, but people who wanted to date usually did anyway.

However, this was slightly different.

A bastard who hadn't even debuted through the audition yet wanted to collect the phone numbers of female idols.

*Winning first place twice must have really gone to his head.*

It seemed that he was getting carried away, but I wanted to look into this new information more closely first.

"Did he give any particular reason for asking for your numbers?"

– That is... Um, when our members gave them feedback on their stage, we told them it was okay to ask if they had any more questions.

The voice over the phone sounded wry.

– Then, he asked if he could follow us on InHeart, saying, ‘Could you give me some more feedback on my interpretation of the song?’

I chuckled. I had a pretty good idea why he'd acted that way.

"By the way, according to company policy, we can't have personal accounts."

– Yes. Since we don't have personal accounts for him to follow... As the conversation unfolded, it seems he wanted the members' personal numbers instead.

*He maneuvered that pretty skillfully.*

"So, did he get your numbers?"

– No! I cut in and gave him the manager's number!

There was a slight hint of pride in Park Minha's voice. I thought about whether or not to compliment her on her common sense, but I gave up because it seemed odd.

– But now that I think about it, I think he steered the conversation that way on purpose, knowing we couldn't have private InHeart accounts.

"Hmm. Okay."

It was perceptive of her to have noticed that, too.

*It must have rubbed her the wrong way, so she reported it to me right away.*

It would be annoying for a group on the rise to get bogged down because some bratty bastard of a trainee who hadn't even debuted was trying to get their numbers.

But this... I didn't think Chae Seodam was particularly interested in any member of Miry-nay.

*He didn't have anyone in particular in mind; he was just trying to get someone's contact information.*

His intentions weren't romantic. He just wanted to show off.

He must have wanted proof that 'I'm successful enough to be involved with female idols of this caliber'.

His actions reeked of a strange kind of contempt.

For the idol profession.

*Is this a job that doesn't mean much to a guy like him?*

Trying to start a romantic relationship like this just to show off. Anyone who'd been a trainee for more than a few days would have shelved the idea.

But this guy seemed to think, ‘I can get away with it’.

*It's strange, considering he's clearly a guy who knows how to calculate realistically.*

Chae Seodam deleted his social media presence more than half a year ago. And judging from the actions he was taking and how he considered the way footage would be edited, he was definitely a crafty guy.

Nevertheless, acting like this... It meant he looked down on his own fans and on the idols who could one day become his competitors.

*He thinks he'll succeed no matter how he acts behind the scenes.*

And that trend seemed to have intensified with recent events.

*Winning first place made him feel like everything was in his hands, but Seon Ahyeon brought him back down to earth. Then he won first place again...*

Anxiety and arrogance seemed to have created a wonderful collaboration.

*Hmm.*

I considered a few possible outcomes, then shrugged and opened my mouth.

"Leave him be."

– Yes?

I laughed.

"Just leave him be. I don't think you'll have to worry about him for much longer."

Looking online, I saw the other guys were coming up fast, so I was really looking forward to seeing how they'd keep the guy who was drunk on winning first place twice in check.

I decided to follow Seon Ahyeon's example and refrain from targeting this bastard directly.

*Let's see how he brings himself down.*

"Oh."

To make a private account for dating in the middle of an audition program?

It was exactly what I'd expected him to do.

"Looks like he let his guard down."

And the fact that the other person was a model confirmed my guess that he just wanted to show off.

*Intriguing.* But it was the timing that really caught my interest.

First place was within reach.

If Goldie #2's fans saw an opportunity to bring down the current Rank 1, it was obvious what they'd do.

Calls to ‘wait and see’ didn't work any better than usual. Neither did arguments like ‘Dating isn't a sin’.

And most fans were taken aback and confused. It must have been a mess within the fandom.

*They're losing energy. Feels good to be right.*

After spending money and time buying his stock on the show, the fans found out he was secretly dating someone.

If it had been a controversy over ‘careless words and actions’, the fans might have rallied around him, but this was the sort of thing that cooled their enthusiasm.

*With just this much, he won't take first place this time.*

People who were more clear-headed would stop supporting him, convinced that he wasn't taking the audition seriously.

It meant that the momentum was broken.

I tossed my smartphone aside.

It was easy to guess Chae Seodam's current feelings.

*It must feel like he was struck by lightning.*

The best thing to do was to delete the account right now and put out a statement claiming that the hacked post was fabricated...

His only hope now was that the editors would take pity on him and give him footage that would move the viewers in the second half of the show...

"Good job."

That was how he was finally relieved of a burden he'd never needed to carry.

Even though we had schedules the next day, we talked about our experiences on ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ over a few beers late into the night.

And after that, we decided to stop paying attention to what happened to Chae Seodam.

The bait we'd laid had worked better than we'd hoped, and there was little else to do.

*We won't have to worry about him anymore.*

Chae Seodam no longer had any power over Seon Ahyeon.
