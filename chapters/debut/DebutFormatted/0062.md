# Chapter 62 - Finals Announcement, II

Only the announcement for first and second place was left.

I didn't worry whether 'Park Moondae' would be eliminated like this.

*There are no other candidates.*

None of the remaining contestants could possibly have placed first or second.

Ah, the guy who won third place at the last ranking announcement was spotted with his girlfriend, so public sentiment plummeted.

I feel a little bad, but... Isn't it his own fault for meeting his girlfriend every time he took a break from filming while he was still participating in an idol audition?

**"We're revealing the first-place candidates now!"**

The MC continued dropping hints into the microphone, his voice growing hoarse as the lengthy live broadcast neared its end.

And after a little bit of delay, just like the previous rankings, the candidates were announced.

**"Cha Eugene!"**

Cha Eugene smiled and came forward. The people below cheered as if it was natural.

**"And the other candidate..."**

Let's get ready to go.

**"Participant Park Moondae!"**

As expected.

I came forward while receiving formal congratulations from some of the participants I wasn't close with.

"Hyung, congratulations."

At least Gold 2 seemed sincere, but I could see that he was forcibly pretending to be strong, perhaps realizing that he was about to be eliminated.

I patted him on the back a few times, then headed to the front of the stage.

Cha Eugene was already standing there. He held out his hand.

"Let's shake hands!"

He looked very excited. I hoped he could keep this momentum and take it to first place.

I didn't say much and accepted the handshake. The atmosphere of the concert hall temporarily became friendly.

*At least my debut is guaranteed now.*

I checked to see if there was a change in the status window, but there was only the 'Successful stage' pop-up that appeared earlier.

"How do you two feel now that you're competing for first place?"

Cha Eugene immediately picked up the microphone.

"It's really cool!"

"Haha! That's a very clear answer~"

The MC laughed and asked fishing questions to create tension.

"Do you think you'll win first place today?"

"I don't know. But getting first place would be nice!"

Cha Eugene raised his thumb.

He sounded at ease enough to make the viewers feel fond of him but not to the extent that would make people resent him for joking around in the final.

"I see~ Now, let's hear from participant Park Moondae. How do you feel when you see him?"

I picked up the microphone.

"He's so talented... I can't believe I'm standing up here beside someone like him."

"It was a very humble statement~ But, the first place... How about it, do you see a possibility?"

I knew you'd ask me this question somehow.

I took a break from answering as if I were thinking about it. Then I shook my head and replied.

"...It seems difficult... Um, to be honest, second place is also higher than my goal, so I'm just grateful."

I didn't know why, but when I answered 'It seems difficult' a small laugh broke out among the audience below the stage.

*Did I sound too sincere?*

"Ah, it's not too bad to come out in 2nd place? Won't you regret it?"

"...Yes, of course, I'd appreciate it if I win."

"Ah, I see! Well, you've heard from Park Moondae, everyone."

*You're persistent, aren't you?*

The MC finally abandoned that line of questioning and began stalling again.

The MC went on about ⟨Idol Inc.⟩'s popularity, reactions from different demographics, and the supposedly amazing rewards awaiting us after debut.

And after a long wait, right before the announcement.

**"The long-awaited first place is...... It will be revealed after the commercial!"**

*I knew this would happen.*

Other finalists might have collapsed from nerves or reacted dramatically, but since it was me and Cha Eugene, all we did was laugh and applaud. The audience, on the other hand, let out groans and curses.

*I think they just threw out curses.*

I was almost certain that I just heard swear words popping out from behind the slogan 'Moon Puppy'.

"Hyung! Debut! Do a lot of fun things with me!"

"...Great."

As soon as the sign of the commercial break appeared, Cha Eugene started talking to me. He was completely excited, anyone who saw it would have thought he had already debuted and entered the dorm.

*Are you really not interested in winning first place?*

The first-place reward was still a mystery. The MC had only described it as something incredible and amazing.

If you didn't have a desire for honor, it wouldn't be weird if you were like, 'I don't know~ I just debut~'

However, if you knew what the first-place reward was, you would be scared.

As I said before, the reward was actually a penalty.

Cha Eugene had consistently ranked first, including at every previous ranking announcement, so he was the more likely winner.

But don't people have this thing called premonition?

Looking at the Internet response I saw yesterday, I couldn't help but feel anxious that 'Park Moondae' would win first place.

*...I don't know. Then I'll just get cursed at.*

I gave up thinking. It was good enough that I could avoid sudden death.

After chatting with Cha Eugene for a while, the live broadcast resumed.

**"Welcome back to ⟨Relisted! Idol Incorporated⟩! The long-awaited first-place announcement is just around the corner!"**

The MC repeated what he had said earlier one more time. I could almost memorize it by heart.

*Get on with it already.*

Seriously, why drag this out when we both already know we're debuting?

*A lot of people have probably changed the channel by now.*

Fortunately, the MC finally said the name.

The long-awaited first place is...

**"...Park Moondae!"**

Although it wasn't the name I had expected.

*...It ends up like this.*

I got first place.

I lowered my head to hide my face in case it would show, but I realized an unexpected fact. It felt... good.

The first-place penalty aside, hearing that I'd won sent a strange rush of adrenaline through me.

The taste of achievement was thrilling.

I almost laughed out loud.

*This is stronger than when I got into college.*

Thanks to that, I wouldn't have to fake my emotions during my speech.

"Congratulations~!"

Cha Eugene hugged me enthusiastically, looking completely sincere.

*It's American style.*

I gave back a short hug and picked up the microphone.

Looking down at the audience from the MC's position, I noticed a few production staff watching me expectantly, as though hoping I'd cry.

*Not today, you bastards.*

I just smiled.

Well, to be honest, half of it was thanks to luck and the status window... Anyway, I had parts in it too.

"Participant Park Moondae, your feelings! Please tell us how you feel."

"Yes."

I paused for a while, then I just spoke honestly.

Maybe it was the adrenaline, but for once, I wasn't calculating anything. The words seemed to come straight from my heart.

"Honestly, my goal was to debut. I really wanted to debut, and I had to... So I didn't even think about winning first place. I didn't even expect it. I think my debut was already too good for me."

I caught my breath.

"But... it feels nice to win."

Cheers and light laughter broke out everywhere.

It was to the point that my next words, 'Honestly, I didn't know this would happen' were difficult to hear. The MC received a signal from the production team that 'It's still taking time' and asked me again.

"Is there anyone you want to thank through this event?"

I was going to say it anyway.

"Well... I can't think of anyone in particular, but I'd like to thank everyone who invested in me."

Screams erupted from the audience, so I paused before continuing.

"I wish I could repay you somehow... Um, no matter how much you've spent on me, I'll do my best to deliver performances worth your investment, within my price range, so to speak. Thank you."

I bowed my head at the end of my speech. Amidst cheers and applause, I could hear some sniffles.

The person holding the Moon Puppy banner was sobbing so hard that they'd crumpled it in their hands, leaving the words barely legible.

*Just because of these words?*

I was a little embarrassed, but it was a relief that they liked it.

It moved me a little to see people who didn't even know me personally celebrating my success.

*Not bad.*

At this rate, it didn't matter if I got the first-place penalty.

As I made up my mind, the MC was conducting an interview with Cha Eugene, who came out in second place.

Cha Eugene laughed and expressed his feelings to his mother in English, then waved eagerly to the audience.

He was clearly enjoying himself, and I could see why people were drawn to him. He was the type that didn't give people unnecessary stress or emotional exhaustion.

"Thank you~"

Cha Eugene cheerfully finished his speech. The production team sent him a sign to join the debut group next to the podium.

After Cha Eugene left running excitedly, the MC looked back at me.

The background music that created tension started playing.

"Now it's time to make a choice."

It had come.

I stood upright and put my hands together.

"A huge reward for the winner of first place, here it is! Let me show you. Participant Park Moondae!"

"Yes."

"What kind of reward do you want?"

"...Well."

I wanted to get 100 million won.

"I don't know.... A quick debut?"

"Ah~ that's important!"

The MC spread his hands.

"Now, show us the reward!"

An elaborate animation appeared on the huge screen above the stage and the two smaller screens on either side.

+=
[Prize money 50 million won[1]{T/N: Around 33,000 USD.}!]
=+

"...!?"

Why is the prize money showing up there?

"Yes! Participant Park Moondae immediately receives 50 million won as the first-place reward! You can claim it as soon as this live broadcast is over!"

This couldn't be happening.

Even in the midst of confusion, my head diligently drew out the actual amount. 39 million won[2]{T/N: Around 26,000 USD.}.

Of course... It was good to receive it.

Of course, that much money would be nice to have. But as expected, there had to be more to it.

"Or! There is another option too!"

Following the MC's words, a new message appeared on the electronic display.

+=
[Recruit a new member!]
=+

"With the authority of the first-place winner, Park Moondae can nominate one of the 14 participants who came up to the final ranking announcement as a team member to debut with!"

*Here it is.*

This damn option finally popped up.

The first place's ultimate reward to select one of the finalists to join the debut group!

Penalty disguised as a reward!

It had become even more vicious.

*You're asking me to choose between the prize money and the second option...!*

How can I pick the prize in this situation?!

If I chose the prize money here, I would be turning the remaining 14 people into enemies, so of course, I had no choice but to choose the new recruitment option!

I could already tell how I would get cursed at.

★$
– Fifty million won is a lot, but it's worth less than another person's career, isn't it?

– You'll make plenty of money after debuting anyway ㅋㅋ You must really love money~ I can already see you leaving for a better-paying agency ㅎ

– I can't believe he'd choose the money after everything they've been through together... That would really ruin his image
$★

Perhaps no one would have chosen the prize money, no matter who it was.

I was sure the production crew knew that and did this.

Putting some drama in the last episode of the program to torture the winner really sounded just like those guys who put negative votes as an official rule.

*It's pissing me off.*

Should I grab the prize money and get cursed at?

"Participant Park Moondae?"

"...Yes."

I nearly answered too late.

All kinds of deafening screams were ringing out from the audience seats.

People closest to the stage were screaming different names, and I couldn't get my thoughts in order.

"Have you made up your mind?"

"...Yes."

It was no good dragging it out too long. I opened my mouth.
