# Chapter 273 - An Old Wound

*I had to sort things out.*

After watching a video of the current Rank 1 from the new season of ⟨Idol Incorporated⟩, Seon Ahyeon showed a very bad reaction.

He froze and broke out in a cold sweat.

*– Are you okay?*

*– I-I'm okay.*

He was almost just repeating my words back to me.

"......"

Afterward, he made excuses about having a stomachache, but of course, I couldn't believe him.

*How can a guy who was fine suddenly get worse after watching a ballet video?*

However, I didn't ask why on the spot, because I could already guess what was going on.

And I wasn't the only one who noticed something was wrong with Seon Ahyeon.

"Park Moondae, what were you two watching?"

"What?"

"In the car earlier."

"......"

After we finished our schedule that day and returned to the dorm, Lee Sejin immediately asked what had happened.

*Come to think of it, he sat right behind Seon Ahyeon.*

It seemed he'd overheard the conversation.

I spoke as I sat down on the edge of Bae Sejin's bed, which the owner rarely used.

"A video of the first-place contestant on this season's ⟨Idol Inc.⟩."

"Ah, that contestant..."

Keun Sejin seemed to recognize him, perhaps because he'd already watched this season, but his face soon hardened.

He probably made the same guess as me.

"...Did he do ballet?"

"Yes."

This season's Rank 1 and Seon Ahyeon's backgrounds were quite similar.

And in fact, I had already pretty much confirmed my guess.

– Seodam went to an arts middle school while doing ballet. ㅜㅜ Can you believe he was a ballerino?

– Chae Seodam went to Sehwa Arts Middle School? I think he might know S. A. H. There are few students there.

Both of them were from the same middle school.

And when I saw Seon Ahyeon's reaction... It seemed that this kid contributed considerably to Seon Ahyeon's trauma.

"Ah... shit."

Keun Sejin had already searched on his own, and he was frowning.

"No... Is this bastard crazy? How did he have the guts to come to the audition?"

"Maybe he has a different way of thinking."

*Or maybe... He was smart.*

I clasped my fingers together.

"Perhaps there is no evidence."

"But there's a witness."

Lee Sejin countered right away.

"People even photoshopped pictures of me, but you're saying this guy has nothing? Not even one thing? And he's in first place."

"......"

"There would be accusations floating around everywhere."

*No.*

"Then it would have come out by now."

I told him what I'd found.

"This bastard started catching people's attention from the first stage of the theme song. He's almost in the center position, and he's currently in first place. In addition, he received a lot of screen time on the show."

"...If accusations were going to come out, they'd have come out already?"

"Yes."

I threw my phone on the bed.

"But no. No rumors are going around."

I had looked all over, from anonymous sites to private accounts, but there were no rumors there.

It was clean.

It meant that he did not make any enemies other than Seon Ahyeon.

"Ha..."

Keun Sejin let out a sigh.

And after being silent for a while, he spoke with difficulty.

"...This isn't going to be easy."

"Yes."

If we wanted to do something, we should've done it before the first broadcast.

Now, this bastard already had a pretty big fandom. It was also an aggressive fandom unique to audition programs.

What if, in this situation, his bullying of Seon Ahyeon during middle school was exposed and this guy was kicked out?

Most of this guy's fans would pour their anger on Seon Ahyeon.

I could already picture the comments.

★$
– Because of that stuttering bastard, my favorite rising star fell off the charts. Things have turned to shit. I'm so frustrated. This is why you stay away from people with a victim complex and a weak mind
$★

Like this.

And there were definitely those who would doubt or suspect him. In the eyes of the public, Seon Ahyeon, who was in a first-tier group, held the advantage.

Something like ‘taking revenge for his unhappy school days’ would come out.

★$
– Honestly, if Chae Seodam was really abusive, would he have come to the same audition as Seon Ahyeon?

– Everyone else is praising Chae Seodam, and suddenly someone's claiming he's a bully; am I the only one who feels weird about this?

– Seon Ahyeon knows he can ruin Chae Seodam's life with all the fans he has, so he's doing this. But there's no evidence... Hmm.
$★

And in fact, it was very difficult to tell right from wrong in this kind of interpersonal controversy, and a single piece of circumstantial evidence could sway public opinion.

I ended up coming to an unpleasant conclusion.

"If we expose him now, Seon Ahyeon will suffer for it."

"......"

Keun Sejin opened his mouth to say something, but bit his lip instead. And he smiled bitterly.

"But don't you think Ahyeon will have a hard time if it's left alone?"

"......"

"That's what you're worried about, Park Moondae, right?"

He was right.

If things went on like this, it seemed like someone would ask Seon Ahyeon during an interview or show at least once.

They would ask, "Do you know Chae Seodam?"

*...That's not good.*

It was really bad. It was not a matter of whether the bastard did well or not. It was a matter of whether Seon Ahyeon could endure being around him without showing how upset he was.

Could Ahyeon say hello to him, talk with him, and laugh along at music shows, variety shows, and year-end programs?

It was a red flag for his future activities.

I pressed my temples.

"This isn't easy, is it?"

It was then.

*Click.*

"...What are you doing?"

"Ah."

Bae Sejin entered cautiously, luggage in hand, then hesitated when he saw the atmosphere in the room.

"No. We had something to discuss."

"Yes, yes. It's nothing, hyung~"

"...Is that so."

Bae Sejin looked unconvinced, but without saying anything else, he switched blankets, gathered some books, and left the room without hesitation.

"Hmm."

Keun Sejin had noticed it for himself, so there was nothing I could do about that, but telling the others before Seon Ahyeon brought it up himself would only upset him even more.

Keun Sejin dusted off his hands and stood up.

"Moondaemoondae, let's think about this a little more."

"Okay."

We tacitly agreed.

But it quickly became clear there hadn't been any need for secrecy. It wasn't long before they noticed everything.

"Guys, by any chance, regarding ⟨Idol Inc.⟩..."

"...Did Ahyeon ever say anything to you?" Ryu Chungwoo asked.

"What?"

"He seemed so nervous while watching TV."

It was because T1 advertised the new season of ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ on all the programs they invested in.

The two youngest sat next to the gloomy Seon Ahyeon, looking worriedly between him and the TV.

"His expression is so bad! I think he's ill!" Cha Eugene said.

"This provocative season is obviously bringing back the pain from when he participated in the audition program... Yes! Shall we report this season to the Korea Communications Commission?"

"Let's not go that far. Calm down," I said.

"Yes, you fool! It's not the broadcast, it's a person!" Cha Eugene said.

"Person?"

"Yes!"

Lastly, Bae Sejin spoke.

"What you guys were talking about last time, it was about ⟨Idol Inc.⟩, right? That person! You said he did ballet, so don't tell me Seon Ahyeon..."

"Yes. You're probably right," I said.

"Y-yeah."

In that way, the six members besides Seon Ahyeon reached the same conclusion and started thinking about how to handle the situation.

And on the day Seon Ahyeon went out for a solo photoshoot, we gathered in the living room and started a discussion.

"First, reporting it anonymously... No, that won't work. Because Ahyeon's not involved with the other person in any way right now."

Ryu Chungwoo, who spoke out, nodded. The rest of them nodded along with serious faces.

However, other opinions did not come easily. It was understandable.

*There's no one with a similar experience.*

Most of them had never been involved in school bullying on either side, so I wasn't sure what to think.

Even Bae Sejin, who was less sociable, seemed to have shut out all the other kids in his grade during his school years, rather than the other way around.

"Umm, then shall we spread rumors first?"

"On the contrary, I think his fans will unite. Wouldn't that be giving them ammunition to refute the formal accusation later?"

"Wow~ That's convincing."

Keun Sejin spoke as if resentful, then tilted his head back.

In the meantime, Bae Sejin opened his mouth with a nervous face.

"...Are there any social media records?"

"Ah, are you referring to that person's personal social media?" Kim Raebin asked.

"Yes. Those kinds of guys really want attention, so they blab all over social media."

It was a good approach, but... I shook my head.

"All his social media accounts are gone."

"E-everything?"

"Yes."

All his accounts had been deleted, and six months ago, at that.

*He must have cleaned everything up as soon as the audition announcement for the new season of ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ came out.*

He was smart, at least in that respect.

"Then... There's very little we can do in this situation. There isn't any evidence."

"Yes."

Just as gloom settled over us, Cha Eugene raised his hand.

"Let's listen to Ahyeon-hyung's opinion!"

"...Ahyeon?"

"Yes! Ask if there is anything he wants to do. His thoughts are the most important!"

"Mmm."

We'd avoided it because we thought it would be burdensome for him, but in fact, that was the best approach.

It would be silly not to talk to him when the other members had already noticed.

*And if he wants to do anything, it's right to do it right away.*

The more time passed, the more famous this bastard would become.

This was because even in the midst of this, the new season of ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ was growing more popular as the controversy drew more attention.

As expected, the talent pool was not as good as in the previous seasons, but the production team had put together a strong narrative by distinguishing between the contestants to eliminate and those to keep.

*Ryu Seorin.*

It seemed that the writer definitely had the ability. Her experience with ⟨K-NOW!⟩ had sharpened her instincts.

The popularity of the program was better than the previous season that produced Miry-nay.

*I have to do something.*

It also had to be done quickly. If it went until the next ranking announcement, it would be out of control.

*...After all, is the only way to ask Seon Ahyeon?*

I nodded.

"I'll try asking him a little. Since I'm his roommate."

"Well, yes."

"Also, Ahyeon might be scared if it's just Moondae, so I'll be there too~"

"......"

*What?*

Surprisingly, however, Lee Sejin's words were passed without any resistance. I began to wonder what my image was among these guys.

Anyway, I got to work that night as soon as Seon Ahyeon returned from filming and came into our room to go to bed.

"H-huh? Sejin?"

"Yeah~ Ahyeon-ah, come and sit down!"

Seon Ahyeon had a puzzled face, but he approached us without question and sat down on his bed.

"Did the photoshoot go well?"

"Yes. I think it was fine...!"

Then, after a bit of small talk to soften the atmosphere, we finally got to the main topic.

"Ahyeon-ah. Do you know ⟨Idol Inc.⟩'s current Rank 1?" I asked.

"...C-could you tell?"

"A little."

To be honest, it was obvious, but I softened the blow.

Seon Ahyeon, who was stiff, seemed not to know what to do, but nodded slowly.

"Yes, w-we were in the same middle school."

I threw a fastball.

"He was the one who liked it when you got hurt, right?"

"...!"

"Th-that's right."

*As expected.*

"But I... I'm not sure."

"Uh, maybe... Maybe it's because I'm too sensitive. Th-there are bound to be people who don't like me in life, right? But I-I can't handle that kind of thing..."

"Wait a second."

I cut him off.

"If he didn't like you, he could have just stayed away from you. He didn't have to pick on you."

"I-I'm not even sure if... he really picked on me. He wasn't hitting me or anything like that..."

"What did he do?"

"H-he was always smiling at me... It didn't seem like he meant it, but strange things happened often..."

"...How about an example?"

"I-I was in trouble because my shoes were gone... A few days later, he returned them to me and said, 'Thank you for lending them to me'... B-but I didn't remember lending them to him, and he smiled as he gave them back."

"Okay."

"H-he laughed when I failed a technique, but when I turned to look, it seemed like he was just laughing while talking to someone, so I think I was being sensitive..."

*Oh.*

*What a disgusting bastard.*

*He only picked on Seon Ahyeon and bullied him.*

Was there a need to hate one person so much and be so subtle about it?

It would have been easier to deal with him if he'd just beaten and teased him like an ordinary bully.

Keun Sejin, who'd been listening beside me with an odd expression, asked quietly.

"Ahyeon~ By any chance, were you at the top, and was he always second?"

"What? N-not always... Maybe a few times?"

"Hmm. He was sometimes at the top, too, right?"

"M-maybe?"

"Okay~ Oh, it's true that he bullied you. Ahyeon-ah, you're worrying about the wrong thing~ That guy was wrong! It's because he was jealous of you!"

"A-and yes, the counselor also said that it wasn't strange that I was hurt, even if it wasn't bullying..."

Chae Seodam.
