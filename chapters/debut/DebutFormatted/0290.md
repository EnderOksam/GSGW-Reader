# Chapter 290 - A Battle for the Throne, VI

The format of this week's special music show was something we'd experienced a few times before.

*Group K-pop concert.*

TNet held a global concert called 'TaKon' every half year.

Well, it was the usual broadcasting station nonsense: they were broadcasting live to dozens of countries without the proper infrastructure to support it, but at least it looked impressive on the outside.

With VTIC appearing this time, they'd gone all out, arranging two parts across two days.

And as for TeSTAR... Wasn't it obvious? We were TNet's cherished sons, so we had to perform on both days.

However, now there was not one son of this broadcasting station, but two.

"Hello, sunbaenim!"

Day 1. The group we met at the rehearsal venue was the debut team from this season of ⟨Idol Inc.⟩. I'd heard they were going to perform a cover of one of our songs.

I spotted Gold 2 grinning among the guys who greeted us enthusiastically.

He'd narrowly secured first place amidst much drama. No wonder he was grinning from ear to ear.

"Sunbaenim, we look forward to learning from you~" Gold 2 said.

"Aigoo, we look forward to working with you too~" Lee Sejin replied.

It seemed he still got along well with Lee Sejin.

Of course, Gold 2 wasn't the one I needed to pay attention to.

One of the guys had quietly joined the others in greeting us.

Chae Seodam was in the debut group.

"......"

The guy who bullied Seon Ahyeon when he was in school. He still couldn't get rid of that habit in ⟨Idol Inc.⟩, and he'd been punished for it.

*So he's still alive and kicking.*

Surprisingly, this bastard had clawed his way back to Rank 10 after the latter half of ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ edited him to emphasize his “desperate determination to survive.”

It seemed that only then had he gotten his story straight with the others, so the explanation that 'everything was a misunderstanding' came out at the last minute and won back some fans...

Well, for this reason, it seemed that he was taking a lot of heat from fans of the debut group.

*Still, it's incredible he managed to debut at all.*

If he'd been eliminated due to controversy, he wouldn't have been able to come back from it.

I didn't know if that was a coincidence or if he still had some grit left in him, but either way, it didn't matter to me.

Seon Ahyeon didn't care much anymore.

"Uh, fighting today...! We'll be... cheering for you!" Seon Ahyeon said.

"Thank you!"

He was cheering the other guys on.

Since Ahyeon was the one directly involved, we'd decided to simply act like this bastard didn't exist and leave it at that.

Moreover, Chae Seodam's fall from first place had already dealt a blow to this season's momentum. So this debut group... Hmm, they'd need some excellent producing to go anywhere.

*And I doubt the director will provide that.*

I wasn't too nervous.

"Don't be too nervous, and try to give your best performance."

"Yes!"

So all I was considering now was the gain we could get from this not-so-welcome encounter.

*What matters is whether I can use it somehow.*

I was thinking of ways to bolster our popularity.

"See you next time!"

The moment we finished exchanging greetings and parted ways, Lee Sejin quietly spoke.

"We switched stages with them, right?"

"Yes."

I'd already taken the first steps toward seizing this opportunity.

"TeSTAR is next!"

"Okay!"

When we got on the rehearsal stage, I breathed in the morning air through my nose.

We were on an outdoor stage.

The new debut group had originally been scheduled to perform on this outdoor stage, but we'd offered to switch with them on the pretext that they reminded us of ourselves in the past.

*Thank you so much.*

Thanks to them, our performance would make for a good story.

I remembered the weather forecast I'd been watching closely for the past few days.

The probability of precipitation on the day of the performance was 70%.

Kim Raebin's fan ignored her brother's words and shot to her feet, slamming her fist against the keyboard as she did so.

*They're only still going because of their overseas fans, and look how full of themselves they are!!*

She'd been belligerent since the donation concert, and her tendency to fight online was only getting worse. But it was time for her to calm down a little now.

"Hey, you want to watch this, right?"

Without even bothering to correct his rude way of speaking, she shoved him aside and took his seat on the couch.

And indeed, the TeSTAR VCR intro flowed on the screen.

"Yes! Wait, what...??"

However, she was speechless when she saw the camera start zooming in on the stage from afar.

It was because it was absurd.

"...What is this?"

"What?"

*What do you think it is?*

*Why are these guys standing on an outdoor stage?*

Since TaKon was broadcast live, the feed alternated between two stages to save time.

And this time, because of the venue's layout, one stage was indoors and the other outdoors.

Then how did it usually work?

More experienced groups usually performed on the indoor stage, along with very popular groups regardless of how long they'd been around.

So, of course, she thought TeSTAR would be indoors too...

These guys were standing on the outdoor stage.

Even on an evening when rain was pouring down!

*What the fuck is TNet thinking!* She'd already been grumbling about how they'd had the audacity to call TeSTAR in two days in a row because of their affiliation, and this only made her angrier.

By then, social media was overflowing with furious fans who'd already seen the new ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ debut group performing indoors.

It was actually for the best that she'd been too busy fighting VTIC's fans to notice before.

Because she was already angry enough!

...Still, the moment the camera zoomed in on TeSTAR, Kim Raebin's fan forgot her anger for a moment.

*These idols look amazing.*

TeSTAR looked flawless, standing ready onstage.

*Is Kim Raebin crazy? That silver robe looks stunning.*

They seemed to be wearing outfits inspired by the music video, and the colorful robe cardigans over their white shirts were damp from the rain...

"...?"

*Water was... falling on his head?*

*Where the hell is the roof?*

Even an outdoor stage usually had a roof to keep the rain off.

No, since the weather forecast said it would rain, all the performances so far had been covered.

But suddenly, the roof was gone.

*No wonder it looks so wet...!*

"These madmen...!"

She almost started cursing again, but the performance began in earnest, so she reflexively focused on it.

The song began with a rhythmic, emotional opening, TeSTAR moving their legs expressively.

TeSTAR didn't seem affected at all by getting drenched.

No, rather, the falling raindrops gave the performance a whole new flavor.

*Uh?*

As the lights grew brighter in the evening, the water droplets on their robes and the accessories covering their arms and legs sparkled.

*Whiik.*

Maybe it was because the choreography involved so many flicks of the arms and legs as they spun around, but even the splashing water seemed to be part of the performance.

And with every step they took, the water splashed as though it were part of the choreography.

And most striking of all... they were barefoot.

*...!*

*Such long legs... No, more importantly...*

*Isn't water pooling on the stage??*

The roof must have been pulled away after the previous indoor performance, and quite a lot of water had pooled on the floor.

*Too dangerous...*

However, perhaps thanks to their bare feet, TeSTAR did not slip.

Instead, the performance was just as clean and fun to watch as when they danced on dry land.

No, it seemed even better.

*What...*

In particular, when they entered the chorus, it was beautiful how the water splashed up in time with the steps of the point choreography.

It was eye-catching.

The stage took on an elegant quality, departing from its original refreshing summer atmosphere.

Doing it all with clothes and hair that looked even better wet, it was practically a meticulously planned water show.

*Ah.*

And then it hit her like lightning. The colors of the decorations on the robes and the gemstones in the accessories on their arms and legs... They matched the colors of their tails in the music video.

A performance in the rain, wearing symbols of mermaids.

*Did they do this on purpose?*

Had they planned to run around barefoot in the rain for their concept? Even taken the roof off?

Surely not? However, TeSTAR's performance on TV was so impressive that she couldn't be sure, her mind flooded with question marks and exclamation marks.

Seon Ahyeon’s acrobatic move sent a clean arc of water into the air, and the ending approached.

The members were behind him, ready to catch him if he slipped, so the sight was more impressive than concerning. It was choreography that caught the eye and held it there.

And instead of sitting down as they usually did while humming at the end, they put their arms around each other's shoulders and turned their backs to the audience.

$p
♪ *Mmm* ♫

So the stage was over.

Kim Raebin's fan fell silent.

No, saying they were good simply wasn't enough...

How amazing was this?

"Oh, really!"

Kim Raebin's fan immediately grabbed her phone and checked social media.

She had to figure out what the hell was going on!
