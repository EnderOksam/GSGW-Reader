# A Way Forward

I clenched my fists.

I'd guessed that he'd meant he wouldn't use Ryu Gunwoo's body for very long when he said 'for a while'. 

But I'd assumed he'd said that out of guilt about using his hyung's body, not because he was going to die as a human next summer. 

And why would he die next summer and return to the past?

He wasn't the only one who'd gone back in time.

"There are people who died at a certain point and lived just fine past that time. What are you talking about?"

"Hyung, it doesn't mean that I will die."

The guy waved his hand. Then he answered rather gloomily.

"It's just... Once that date passes, I won't be able to remain human."

I understood the words in an instant.

*So you mean...*

"You're going back to being the status window?"

"Yes..."

The guy on the other side lowered his head. I held back a groan and asked.

"Why will that happen?"

"It's because I, as the status window, gave you missions and rewards instead of the system."

*Right.* This guy had been brought back into being because I'd received 'A conversation with Park Moondae' as a reward using my new trait, 'Missions Are My Thing.'

"What about it?"

"You saw it. Even if you return to the past, nothing about your previous life changes. And when the trip to the past is over, you go back to the life you were living before... That's how it works."

"......"

I stared at him.

"I guess that's how it works for me too. So when this trip to the past, where I'm living as a human, is over, I'll go back to the life I was living before."

The guy swallowed.

"I'll go back to being the status window."

"Wait a moment."

I raised my hand.

"Then does that mean the status window won't appear if I call it up now? Because you're here."

"Oh, no. I'm here as a human, but the part that's influenced by the system is still there. It should still appear."

"So you've been separated?"

"Yes..."

I called the status window as a test.

He was right.

*Come to think of it, even after I returned to my original body, I never saw a pop-up saying I'd finished receiving my reward.*

Then I... Was still in the process of receiving my reward, 'A conversation with Park Moondae'.

This guy was extending that period as much as possible, but the limit was next year.

*Hoo.*

I held back a sigh and organized what we'd discussed in my head.

Then I considered the first solution that came to mind. It was similar to an idea I'd had before.

"I can do another mission and ask for a similar reward, can't I?"

The 'Missions Are My Thing' trait couldn't have suddenly disappeared.

Then wouldn't it be enough if I requested a mission with a reward that let this guy use Ryu Gunwoo's body permanently? 

However, Keundal shook his head.

"That is... I think once I go back to being the status window, it won't be possible for me to separate the human part of myself from the system again and enter a body."

"Why?"

"I can feel my connection with the system getting stronger. It seems to affect me even when I'm in another body."

Keundal swallowed.

"Maybe once I go back, I'll be completely fused with it."

"......"

I pressed my lips together. The guy on the other side grinned.

"But hyung, that's really okay! I still have several months left. And even if I become the status window, I'll try to talk to hyung..."

"Wait a moment. Be quiet."

"What?"

I rubbed my chin and quickly reached a conclusion. It was clear and simple.

"Let's get rid of the system."

"What? Wait."

"If the system disappears, I think part of the system that assimilated with you will also disappear..."

"It could be, but... That's just a guess..."

"I was going to get rid of it anyway. The system creates too many unpredictable variables. There's already so much to consider in this industry, and it's getting in the way of my work."

"You can't just get rid of the system because it gets in the way of your work!?"

I came up with the most reasonable conclusion I could, but why was this guy freaking out? 

He thumped the table as if he'd suddenly remembered something.

"Oh, come to think of it, hyung talked about that! With those regressors!"

"Regressors?"

"People who have returned to the past!"

He was throwing the term around so casually by now. Anyway, I nodded.

"That's right. I've been tracking the system for a while. You should've seen me do it when you were the status window."

"Well... About that!"

The guy on the other side jumped to his feet and muttered with a slightly uneasy expression.

"Hyung... No, I've really wanted to tell you this ever since I was the status window..."

"What?"

"Don't mess with the system if you can help it, hyung..."

Keundal looked at me grimly. For a moment, I wondered if he'd grown attached to the system somehow, but I recognized a familiar emotion in his eyes.

It was fear.

"That... It's really weird. This isn't some kind of fantasy or sci-fi movie, after all. It's like some kind of AI, or maybe a ghost..."

"You said you were influenced by it, and that you influenced it in turn."

"That's why I know what I'm talking about!" he shouted.

I'd been about to point out that he was saying those things about himself, but I decided to keep quiet. 

"Actually, even if I did influence it, I don't know exactly how... You called the system a mental parasite. I really think you're right... Like some kind of super-parasite that's also a supercomputer..."

"......"

"Just... Hyung, can we not do that? I'm kind of scared."

After contemplating, I nodded.

"Well, I'll consider it."

"You mean you're going to ignore me?!"

He hadn't lost his touch from his days as the status window. I ignored him, just as he'd expected. He looked despondent.

"It's... It's really dangerous. You really shouldn't... I'm serious."

"Let me ask one more question."

"Yes?"

I asked calmly.

"Is it possible to use the original status window's functions instead of this '[Currently receiving reward]' window? Like checking someone else's stats or my traits?"

The guy on the other side blinked.

"Well... I think it should be possible."

"Really?"

"Yes. I've tried messing with it from this body before. If I 'connect' to the status window and manipulate it in a certain way..."

He forgot his frustration for a moment, his expression going blank as he concentrated.

It seemed like he was trying to hack something.

Just in case, I closed the status window and waited for him.

A little while later, the guy's expression brightened again, and he opened his mouth. 

"Hyung, I think I...!"

That was the moment.

*Knock knock.*

A knock on the door.

"...!"

The door to the private room opened gently, and Keundal sank into his seat.

"Oh, you two seem to have been having a nice chat."

Ryu Chungwoo came back in.

I glanced at his face. He seemed calm.

*Did he overhear us?*

'Ryu Gunwoo' had just called 'Park Moondae' hyung.

As long as Keundal didn't say anything too damning, I could handle the situation...

"Y-yes! There you are."

*Please don't look so fierce when you speak. Or smile awkwardly, for that matter.*

"It's been a while since I've met someone I used to know... It was a bit hard to keep up the conversation. Haha..."

*Never mind.* He really did look like someone who'd spent several years shut away studying for exams.

I was relieved. And Ryu Chungwoo simply smiled back awkwardly.

"A test is a battle with oneself, isn't it? I've had similar experiences myself... Ah, hyung, you're doing just fine at holding a conversation, by the way."

*Was that pity I detected in his tone?*

"M-me? Thank you. Actually, it's a wonder I passed the interview at all... Hahaha."

"Hyung, please sit down."

"Ah, yes."

I cut into their small talk. *Just shut up and eat.*

*Let me organize my thoughts.*

***

After that, without any problems, we each ate the noodles we ordered and talked about TeSTAR.

Ryu Chungwoo seemed a little taken aback by how much the guy knew about TeSTAR, from our albums to our concerts.

*– You know so much about us. Don't tell me we got in the way of your studies...*

*– Not at all. In fact, watching you guys helped me a lot...*

It was certainly heartwarming.

Seeing how the guy was no longer awkward, Ryu Chungwoo naturally concluded, 'He must be good at talking when it comes to things he's interested in.'

"Thank you for today."

"No, thank *you*...!"

Anyway, after successfully exchanging numbers and finishing our meal, we got up to leave. I made eye contact with him as we parted ways. 

*Details on the phone.*

*I'll definitely go to the concert!*

I didn't think I'd gotten my meaning across, but now there was no problem texting him, so it was fine. 

Moreover, since he'd even asked for our autographs as we were about to part ways, it seemed Ryu Chungwoo had settled on his impression of him.

It was because he got into the car, started the engine, and said this.

"We should've brought autographs from the other kids or something."

"That's true."

*Good.* It seemed whatever doubts he might've had would be put to rest by what he'd seen today. I relaxed my shoulders and leaned against the backrest.

Now I just had to figure out a way to keep Keundal in Ryu Gunwoo's body... 

"Ryu Gunwoo-hyung seems to have changed quite a bit."

I stopped thinking for a moment.

"...Changed?"

Did he remember meeting me when I was young? I fell silent and searched my memories, but he continued.

"Like when we saw that video of him at my house. You saw it too."

"...Yes."

*Oh, that.* He meant the video we'd seen at Ryu Chungwoo's house, the one with Ryu Gunwoo in it.

I leaned back against the backrest again.

"People adapt to their environment. Of course he's changed."

"I guess so?"

Ryu Chungwoo answered comfortably.

"The thing is... I think he used to resemble you, actually."

"...!"

"At least, the person in the video did."

Ryu Chungwoo glanced at me, then looked straight ahead again.

"I don't mean his appearance, but his facial expressions and movements."

"......"

"Things like that don't change easily unless you make a conscious effort to change them. I know because I've had to correct my posture and gait after being scolded while doing archery."

Ryu Chungwoo didn't say anything else on the subject. From his attitude, I could tell that he was mulling something over.

And I'd missed my chance to make a natural excuse.

He wasn't asking me for a convincing explanation. He was just trying to tell me that he'd noticed the two of us had swapped bodies.

He wasn't going to say any more than that.

And that... Was far more understanding than I'd expected... No, it was ridiculously understanding of him. 

*Does this... make sense?*

He was responding with tacit understanding instead of telling me I needed to go to a mental hospital?

I glanced at the guy driving.

Beneath his apparent calm, I could see an odd tension, but there was something steady underneath it.

In other words, trust.

"......"

"Actually, I have something you could help me with now."
