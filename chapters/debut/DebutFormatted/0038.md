# Chapter 38

“For now, I think ⟨Loving the Wait⟩ would be a good choice.”

“...!!”

Seon Ahyeon looked visibly touched.

His reaction showed that he hadn’t expected me to agree with his song selection.

“R-R-Really?”

“Yes.”

It was true that it was a good song, and the fact that it was a song from 5 years ago added an extra point.

⟨Hone⟩, which Kim Raebin mentioned, was a song from two years ago.

I thought this during the first team match, but VTIC’s recent songs are difficult to pull off.

Kim Raebin himself seemed confident, but we didn’t have to take risks.

However, I had to incorporate Kim Raebin’s opinion, since he would lead the arrangement.

To take advantage of his characteristic, ⟨Maestro⟩, I had to give him a keyword that suited his taste.

In contrast to Seon Ahyeon, who looked touched, Kim Raebin looked a little gloomy. I guess he already expected that his opinion wouldn’t be accepted.

“But ‘self-production’ is the theme of this team match, so we can’t perform the original song as it is.”

“Ah...”

“So I’d like an unconventional arrangement. Personally, of Raebin’s earlier suggestions, ‘oriental style’ seems best.”

“Well...”

Kim Raebin looked lost in thought, then immediately brightened.

“Certainly, the lyrics aren’t awkward either... With the right instruments, I can come up with a very interesting arrangement. If we modify the choreography according to the arrangement, we could create a very memorable performance.”

“That’s a relief.”

That satisfied Raebin for now.

Then all that’s left is Keun Sejin and Gold 1.

I turned my head and scanned the two.

As soon as our eyes met, Keun Sejin smiled calmly.

“What about arranging it in a sexy oriental style?”

“...!”

“Even if we go with the oriental style, we still have to decide what kind of atmosphere we want. If we’re doing this, let’s make it sexy and impactful, yeah?”

His words weren’t wrong.

*You must have thought of this since the beginning...*

None of the ideas so far ruled out the ‘sexy concept,’ so he probably intended to work it in regardless of my answer.

Funnily enough, it’s hard to refute him.

Sure enough, Gold 1 sighed playfully.

“You’re such a great guy, really...”

In fact, he meant it. From the other two’s expressions, I could tell they’d already let it pass.

“Haha! Am I that persuasive?”

Keun Sejin shrugged. With this, the opinions of the four team members were reflected in the song selection.

*Since it’s like this, let’s take the remaining one.*

There were few viewers who hated a warm and democratic team atmosphere. I opened my mouth again calmly.

“Shall we use the rabbit, too?”

“What?”

Gold 1 waved his hands emphatically.

“Oh, what I said? It’s all right. I was just saying it.”

“No, I think it would suit our theme well.”

“...The rabbit?”

“Yes. Rabbits appear in lots of old Korean stories, right?”

The other team members seemed to have come up with their own ideas.

“B-Byeoljubujeon[1]{T/N: Byeoljubujeon or ‘The Story of the Rabbit’ is one of Korean classic novels from the Joseon Dynasty.}?”

“That... Is ‘The Tortoise and the Hare’ a Korean story?”

“It’s an Aesop fable.”

Keun Sejin was the only one grinning among the team members. A quick-witted guy.

“Moon Rabbit?”

“Moon Rabbit. That’s right.”

It was the rabbit in the old story of pounding rice cakes on the moon. It was also known as the Jade Rabbit.

It was also famous from the nursery rhyme about the rabbit under the katsura trees.

“...How do you make it sexy?”

Gold 1 murmured in dismay, worried that it might destroy childhood innocence. It must have reminded him of a rabbit outfit from Playboy.

But it seemed Kim Raebin liked this keyword. His face cleared up and his eyes shone. It was honestly convenient that I didn’t have to open my mouth.

“I think we can use it as a motif instead of basing the whole performance on it. It’s an attractive concept if we can make good use of its image as a fantasy animal.”

“Oh, is that so? Well, it’s fine if you include my idea.”

Gold 1 nodded reluctantly. Keun Sejin immediately moved to wrap up the discussion.

“Hmm~ I like it, too! Shall we decide by raising our hands?”

A few nearby teams had already decided on a song and were informing the production team.

Due to the time limit, it was time for our team to conclude the song selection and move on to the details.

“Raise your hand if you agree with reinterpreting ⟨Loving the Wait⟩ with oriental sounds, a sexy concept, and the Moon Rabbit!”

Immediately, everyone’s hands came up. As soon as he finished speaking, Keun Sejin also raised his hand and laughed.

“Wow, that was really democratic~ All of our opinions have been taken into account. Let’s work hard!”

“Yes!”

“Yay!”

The discussion ended with friendly applause just like when it began.

*It was then.*

“I said no!”

Someone got up screaming from the other team over there.

I happened to catch his face. It was Lee Sejin.

“What’s going on?”

“Uh...”

“Are they fighting?”

The participants around quietly whispered.

But everyone seemed to be reticent. Because saying useless words was like sending a signal that you wanted to be a victim of editing.

Meanwhile, Lee Sejin was glaring at the participants on the opposite side.

I could see Choi Wongil stiffen, while Cha Eugene wore a dead expression.

But the standoff did not last long.

Lee Sejin turned around and ran out of the set with his teeth clenched.

“...!!”

“Heol. They’re...”

Fortunately, Ryu Chungwoo reacted quickly and followed Lee Sejin.

“You guys wait a minute. ...Sejin? Lee Sejin!”

A camera hurriedly followed Ryu Chungwoo.

“.......”

*Wow, that team is really... screwed.*

The atmosphere must have turned really sour if even the easygoing Cha Eugene couldn’t keep a fight from breaking out.

“Aigoo...”

Gold 1 sighed briefly.

Kim Raebin’s face darkened as if he was recalling the trauma of the last team match.

However, no one was tactless enough to approach that team and ask questions or offer comfort.

It had been several weeks since filming began, so there was no participant who still couldn’t understand the situation.

If you’re meddling, you’ll just become the broadcast’s scapegoat.

So, our team quickly finished reporting our song choice to the production team and moved to the practice room.

It was a wise decision.

***

It was long past midnight when our team returned to the dorm.

Everyone looked like a withered bean sprout.

“Ah, the bed is nice...”

Keun Sejin made a dying sound and fell on the bed.

He couldn’t stand the crazy schedule of arranging the song, revising the choreography, and developing the stage concept, all within 10 days, so he was K.O.’d.

“I-I’m going to wash up...”

Seon Ahyeon said those words but couldn’t get out of bed. Instead, he fell fast asleep.

I stared at him.

He’d been in a state of extreme tension as he kept revising the choreography to match the arrangement, and in the end, it seemed to have completely drained him.

Gold 1 trudged to another room, saying he needed to eat something. I think he was going to swipe some of Gold 2’s cup noodles, but he’d take care of it.

“...May I use the shower first...?”

“Yes.”

Kim Raebin called Seon Ahyeon a few times, and when there was no answer, he asked if I wanted to use the shower before going into the bathroom.

So I’m the only one still alert in this room.

Ah, why am I relatively fine?

Of course, it’s the status window. I thought of the new characteristic that floated at the bottom of the status window.

★-

#><✦ Characteristics: Bacchus 500 (B)><#

Clear mind and healthy body!

Fatigue accumulation rate -50%
-★

It was a characteristic I’d drawn as a reward for reaching the next stage of ‘Fame Achievement’ after the last team match aired.

★=
#><The Ripening of Fame!><#

500,000 people remembered your existence!

{Draw Rare Characteristics{ ☜ Click!
=★

Quite frankly, it was an unrealistic number.

500,000 people?

How popular had this show become for more than 500,000 people to know one participant? It was a surprising number even considering how highly I ranked.

Considering the speed, it was a little terrifying to think that the number of people would surpass one million soon.

One million people... No, isn’t it ‘Park Moondae’ whom they remember?

At times like this, I thought it was rather fortunate that it was someone else’s body.

In any case, I’d hit the jackpot with the fame achievement draw for reaching 500,000 people. It was the most useful thing I’d gotten so far for this survival show.

There was no immediate increase in lung capacity or muscle strength, but busy and physically exhausting days had certainly become easier to bear.

Even after running so hard, I still have room to organize my thoughts.

I got up from my seat and stretched briefly. Anyway, I was tired and drowsy.

I should wash up and go to bed as soon as Raebin comes out.

Then, there was a sound from the door.

*Knock. Knock.*

“...?”

Gold 1? But if it was him, he wouldn’t have bothered knocking.

If it were a staff member, they would have already said who they were.

I couldn’t guess who it was.

#^#*Knock. Knock. Knock!*#^#

But the knock continued. However, the team members that might get a visitor were passed out.

...It’s bothering me.

I sighed, then opened the door.

“...!”

Cha Eugene was standing outside.

It was surprising, but it was also understandable. He might have come to meet Kim Raebin, since they’d been at the same agency in the past.

The problem was that he was crying.

Cha Eugene was sniffling, tears and snot running down his face.

I stared at him in silence.

...Why are you crying here?

It was very disconcerting.

Fixed cameras were also installed in the hallway and rooms of the accommodation. That meant he had already been caught coming here and crying, but I had no idea how it would turn out on the show.

No, more than that, why did you have to crawl in here?

Isn’t it common sense to consult with Ryu Chungwoo?

Considering Ryu Chungwoo’s personality, he would have been happy to listen. Why come to another team and risk causing trouble?

Come to think of it, I don’t think he’s smart in that area...

In a moment of silence, I thought of all sorts of things and finally opened my mouth.

“...Did you come to see Raebin?”

“I came here to meet hyung and Raebin.”

Cha Eugene replied indistinctly.

Why me?

I didn’t think we’d made that much of a connection. Well, I guess he grew fond of me during the last team match.

The results were good, and the process (from Cha Eugene’s point of view) might have been fun and easy.

After tasting the hell of this team match, you must have missed it again.

Anyway, if I sent him back, I didn’t think I’d get a positive edit, no matter what scene ended up on the show.

I held back a sigh and answered.

“Come on in. Raebin is washing up.”

“Yes...”

Cha Eugene kept sniffling and entered the room.

Other team members were sleeping soundly in bed. I didn’t bother to lower the volume because I was certain that they wouldn’t wake up with this much noise.

For now, I had him sit down at a nearby table.

Should I give him something?

I looked at Cha Eugene’s upset face, then pulled out a few chocolate bars from my bag and handed them to him.

You’d feel better with something sweet.

“Thank you...”

Cha Eugene mumbled his thanks, unwrapped a chocolate bar, and shoved it into his mouth.

“...”

Only the sound of chewing chocolate echoed through the room. I was uneasy.

...What am I supposed to say to this bastard?

There was no worse situation than asking the wrong question and getting caught up in another team’s problems.

However, it was also a problem if I kept my mouth shut and let Kim Raebin talk.

Kim Raebin isn’t the social type either.
I had no idea what kind of situation this would turn into. I would be in trouble if the arrows came my way when I was only a bystander.

In the end, feeling like I was stepping into a minefield, I opened my mouth again.
