# Chapter 307 - TeSTAR Songwriting Camp, II

The songwriting camp started right away.

The task was to arrange the boss theme BGM from the sequel to ⟨127 Section⟩ into a track no longer than 60 seconds.

"Wow, why are game songs so addictive?"

"Maybe it's because it loops."

It wasn't something to risk our lives for, so the members turned on the composition program while chatting casually to lighten the mood.

Everyone knew the basics because the agency had given us a cursory introduction to composition software as a formality when we debuted as self-producing idols.

The problem was that even that knowledge had grown rusty after three years.

"Mmm."

"Why is this step here? Teach me!"

"F-first, I'll take the top note..."

*It's messy.*

I looked down at my laptop screen.

I knew I had to copy the original notes first, but... I didn't really know what I was doing, either.

"Moondae, do you know how to load a track?"

"...Why don't we start by watching the basic tutorial?"

Yes. To put it bluntly, we were shit at it.

Everyone knew it, but we'd deliberately avoided studying more than necessary.

*That doesn't fit the purpose.*

Because this was a hobby.

"I'm sorry, but this..."

"It's a drum machine group!"

"...Thank you."

I saw Kim Raebin working away quickly on his laptop, answering the questions around him wholeheartedly without batting an eye.

Even then, he got sucked into the laptop again.

The hectic atmosphere left him no room to dwell on his worries while composing.

*That's good.*

I also moved the mouse and started building the base appropriately.

And after some time...

"Three hours are up! Now, let's hear each other's creations~"

"Wow!!"

With the exception of Kim Raebin, anyway, all of us were amateurs, and since it was a three-hour work, we took turns playing the songs without any burden.

And as expected, the overall quality was a mess and... there were some unexpected twists among these pieces of shit.

"Hahahaha!"

"This is great!"

"Yeah, cool!"

Raw, unprocessed sounds blasted through the Bluetooth speaker.

It was fortunate that it was a mountain cabin. If it were a resort, we'd probably have been politely shown the door.

I shook my head as I listened to Cha Eugene's crazy arrangement.

"How is it, hyung?"

"It's like thunder."

"Hehe."

It wasn't a compliment, but at least he liked it. When I turned my head, I saw Kim Raebin's pupils shaking. He looked flustered.

It was easy to give feedback, though. 

It was because we could give advice and talk to our heart's content, instead of worrying that the other person was better than us and that we'd be overstepping.

As Seon Ahyeon's track played next, Kim Raebin, who was seated next to him, finally spoke up.

"W-what do you think...?"

"That..."

Besides, we could only say good things.

The pressure to be completely honest eased once again. 

Kim Raebin played the sound a few times earnestly, and seemed to be doing everything in his power to squeeze out compliments.

"The harmony is thicker than the original song, so it sounds soft and beautiful...!"

I guess so. Because the original song was 8-bit.

"I see... Th-thank you."

Still, Seon Ahyeon's face brightened because it was a compliment from an expert.

"Right, it feels more like a main character's theme song than a boss' theme song, right? As expected from Ahyeon~"

"...It's easy to listen to."

It really felt like a friendly after-school club. Kim Raebin quietly added his words.

"That is correct. The guitar sounds good, so I think it'd help to turn up the volume a little."

Oh, my. It seemed that he wanted to polish it up a bit more.

It seemed certain that he was freed from the evaluation pressure. If he praised the arrangements of these beginners, he would gain confidence.

And not long after, the time to play Kim Raebin's song came.

I deliberately did not put it first or last, and put it in the middle.

"Oh~ Raebin~"

"Yes. I will play it."

Kim Raebin operated the mouse with a slightly excited face.

And the sound, tightly woven like a spider's web, began to flow.

"...!"

It wasn't in a mainstream pop song format.

A sleek structure close to New Age.

The short sections from the original BGM were well placed, staying true to its purpose as background and theme music.

However, the way the song came together was different.

*...I can't believe it can sound like this.*

It felt like he'd drawn out the essence of the song that stayed in people's heads after listening and brought it to life in the most ideal way.

And after the first 30 seconds, the song grew in power as a new element was mixed in.

"...!"

"This is our song! Right?"

He'd mixed in the signature part of his own rap from our collaboration OST, 'Bonus Book'.

The melancholy minor keys fell like dewdrops on the intricate, spiderweb-like structure.

The sound matched the character perfectly, and the way the song developed was exhilarating.

"Yeah~"

"Raebin, this is really good?"

Alright. Good.

It wasn't the sort of song likely to catch on with the public, but it was good enough that "spider web" was the best description I could come up with.

And even though we'd agreed to give only positive feedback, their gestures and voices were sincere.

"I love hearing this!"

Kim Raebin seemed a little taken aback by the flood of praise, but also seemed a little teary-eyed.

I gave a short evaluation.

No other words were needed.

"It's more like the original than the original itself."

"...!!"

Kim Raebin's head shot up. I nodded.

"...Thank you."

I could hear his voice shaking, but I didn't bother pointing it out.

Kim Raebin looked at the laptop screen and touched the touchpad even after the one-minute arrangement had finished playing.

We left him alone for a while.

And by the time Kim Raebin came to his senses, the next guy came, which was like a public execution.

"Yes~ Next is Moondae!"

It was me.

I sighed inwardly, but it was I who brought myself to this hell.

*Keep it short and sweet.*

And hit play.

*Ugh.*

Well, this character was known for his drones, so I turned up the buzzing electronic sounds to make them sound ominous.

And that fuzzy... whatever that instrumental effect was called, I added that too.

The people around me nodded.

"It's pretty good?"

*Yeah? I wasn't asking for sympathy votes.*

Listening to this after that perfect professional track felt like taking several steps backward.

It sounded like my 'Catch-All Ears' were screaming at me to stop.

I crossed my arms nonchalantly, but Kim Raebin across from me raised his hand.

"There are some fun elements, and the way you modified the melody is really clever...!"

"I know, right? Moondae must have a sense."

Even Kim Raebin was just flattering me... *Wait a moment.*

I saw his face.

Kim Raebin was focused on the song, just like when he'd watched the theme song play on TV earlier.

"...?"

*Was he serious?*

He even came to my laptop and checked the notes I used.

And he clenched his fist.

Like he wanted to hold a mouse.

"Hyung! If you don't mind, may I edit this song a little..."

"Okay."

Kim Raebin looked up at me with a bewildered expression.

"I don't mind at all."

A smile grew on his face as if he couldn't contain it.

His face was bright despite his harsh features, nothing like before.

---

On the first day, the content of the songwriting camp was changed to Kim Raebin's arrangement lecture.

"There are a lot of rough and strong sounds in the VST you used, so if you adjust the level here, Cha Eugene..."

"WOW!"

Kim Raebin excitedly gave advice to the members to make the most of their arrangements, and the members quite happily corrected their own songs.

And my song... Well, Kim Raebin practically rewrote it from scratch.

The strange thing was that it still retained its original feel. I had no idea how he'd managed it.

"The idea you used for the arrangement was great, and the direction was clear, so it was easy to modify!"

In other words, didn't that mean there were imbalances he could fix easily that I hadn't even noticed?

...Well, anyway, if he was satisfied with it, it was fine.

Kim Raebin changed all 7 theme songs to his own version, then ate dinner with gusto and called it a day.

And now it was early the next morning.

"Hmm."

I woke up alone before the alarm went off, so I thought I'd check today's schedule in advance.

I stepped over the blankets and sat down at the kitchen table, careful not to step on the guys sleeping on the floor.

And tousled my hair.

*Today...*

I was thinking we'd each pick a children's song and a popular idol title track and combine them.

Of course, we'd choose hugely popular junior idol groups to make sure we could never officially use the results.

*And when time permits, Ryu Chungwoo even brought hiking boots...*

"Moondae?"

At that moment, the guy who'd been sleeping closest to the kitchen opened his eyes.

It was Kim Raebin.

"Go back to sleep."

"No, it's fine..."

And then he staggered up and sat across from me.

"I really want to say thank you."

*Now?*

"I may be overestimating my own importance, but I feel that you've been very considerate of me so that I can recover my ability to compose music..."

"Not exactly."

I tilted my head back.

And the words I should have said when I confiscated his laptop a few days ago came out.

"We have to gradually get familiar with composing. There are seven members, so it's not right to put the whole burden of producing our music on one person."

"......"

"So it's okay if you don't want to compose. Nobody else was doing it, either."

However, Kim Raebin struggled to open his mouth.

"However, many people are looking forward to my music. The company's employees and the A&R Team also said they were waiting for me to make music..."

"Are you afraid people will be disappointed if you don't compose anything?"

Kim Raebin hesitated. It meant that he agreed.

I held back a sigh.

"Raebin. You're good at being on stage and rapping, so why do you act like composing is the only thing you're good at?"

"...!"

"It's good if you write songs because it's fun, but if it isn't, that's fine too. It won't kill the group. We can buy other songs."

Kim Raebin raised his head.

"Of course, you indeed make ridiculously good songs, and thanks to you, we're getting a lot of help. We should be grateful for that."

I spoke calmly.

"But there's nothing wrong with not wanting to continue, and it won't hurt us in any way. Let's always remember that there are other options."

"Yes."

Kim Raebin answered while clenching his fists and lowering his head.

"I will not forget."

His voice sounded teary, but there was certainly a sense of relief.

*I was right that there was a lot of pressure.*

As long as he kept writing good songs, he never really felt the pressure, but once he slipped, it really sank in.

Worrying that his reputation would be ruined and that he'd cause a lot of damage to the team.

*Come to think of it, it was like that when he was maliciously edited in* Idol Inc.

During the 2nd team battle, I remembered that this guy was so intimidated that he blindly accepted the feedback.

*...Could that have been the cause?*

Well, it wasn't something I could determine by guesswork, so I moved on.

But anyway, after talking it through and making good music again, he'd shaken off some of that helplessness, so it would be a little easier to breathe.

I waited for Kim Raebin to calm down. 

After briefly wiping his face with his shirt, he opened his mouth again in a quiet but resolute voice.

"I... However, may I tell you one thing?"

"Okay."

*Let's see.*

I even expected him to say, "With the current members' composing skills, you'd feel my absence right away."

However, Kim Raebin spoke seriously.

"Since all the members are involved in the production of our albums and activities, it seems like an exaggeration to say I'm the one helping everyone...! We're all helping each other!"

He was a really consistent guy.

"Yes."

I smiled and loosened my arms.

"Thank you."

"Not at all."

Kim Raebin answered steadfastly.

Beyond that, I could see the veranda where the sun rose, and the light came in. It seemed to push the darkness away.

*Looks like this went well.*

And a bottle shining with golden light...

*Hmm.*

I spoke nonchalantly.

"Shall we have a drink?"

Kim Raebin followed my gaze and checked the ginseng liquor. And shouted with a bright expression.

"Yes! Let me pour you a glass."

"...?"

I thought he would say no.

However, Kim Raebin had a puzzled face.

"What? I heard ginseng wine is good for the mind and body..."

"...Your grandma?"

"Yes!"

*She was absolutely right.* I drank ginseng wine with Kim Raebin at 6:30 AM when everyone else was asleep.

It was my first drink in a long time, and though it wasn't as thrilling as I remembered, it tasted good.

---

"I-is something bothering you?"

"No, something... Something about the bottles has changed."

I ignored Bae Sejin's words after he woke up. We'd left no evidence.

We'd all had a modest breakfast of rice balls and were about to move on to the next activity...

One of the guys, happily replaying yesterday's songs, opened his mouth.

"Hmm... Raebin. Rather than not revealing this at all, shall we put it on the Internet? It's a shame that only we get to hear it."

"...! You mean on TeSTAR's social media account?"

I did all this in a way to prevent this very thing from happening.

*...Originally, I was going to do it on the last day after camp.*

Now that someone had brought it up, I figured we might as well do it now.

I naturally moved to the next level.

"Let's just make a new WeTube account and upload it. We don't have to use our names."

"Ah, yes."

"Yes, that will work!"

Bae Sejin shouted out excitedly and quickly created an account. Everything was going according to plan.

I reviewed the order.

Camp level 1.

Keep making songs as a hobby without negative feedback.

And the next step.

Anonymous validation.

"Uh huh, wait~ How about choosing the images for the videos first?"

...However, what was meant to be another small step in my plan quickly got out of hand.
