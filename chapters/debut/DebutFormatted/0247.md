# Chapter 247 - A Healing Retreat, III

It was the first day of our isolation on the island.

***Kwagwawang!!***

Outside the villa, it was still raining like crazy.

*It's fortunate that the guy who built this had a very thorough mind for his own safety.*

The villa was in good shape, so it was fortunate.

I suppressed a sigh and lay down in the living room.

Cha Eugene lay down next to me in the same way.

[It's good to think about it like this... there's a zombie apocalypse outside! That's why we have to live in this comfortable shelter by ourselves.][1]{T/N: Just as a reminder, if Eugene has his words in brackets, he's speaking in English.}

"Yeah. What a consolation."

"Hehe!"

Don't laugh, you punk.

Eugene laughed, and a smirk tugged at my lips, though I was still deeply worried.

*What about the shooting?*

If a few days of shooting flew by like this, it'd really be ruined...

[Hyung, we can't shoot right now anyway. Relax~ and have a good time.]

"...!"

[There is no need to worry about things that humans cannot do.]

*What a simple way to look at life...*

I kept my mouth shut for a while, then looked outside at the rain.

*What will we do with the weather?*

This was only a replacement slot anyway. Even if this variety show flopped, it wasn't the end of the world. We could do better on the next one.

"Okay. You are right."

"That's right!"

I lay in the living room watching the rain and passed the time without thinking until mealtime.

During lunchtime...

All seven of us were eating the same preserved food, and it really felt like we were in some kind of disaster movie.

"Hm, why don't we check the chicken coop for eggs?"

"Sejin-ah, you'll be swept away."

"Yes."

After a couple of dull meals, time seemed to slow to a crawl.

Even the phone signal, already poor, was slowly disappearing altogether.

*I feel so isolated...*

Thanks to that, there was really nothing to do.

I, who was in charge of preparing meals today and was exempted from cleaning up, sat in the living room with the best view of the weather situation and was lost in thought.

We had run out of board games, and we couldn't go outside to get food...

*Jing. Ttuk.*

At that time...

Suddenly, the surroundings became dark.

"Huh??"

"Is everyone okay?"

It was a blackout.

There was only one house on the island, so there were no lights visible outside. It was completely dark.

I immediately raised my upper body.

At that moment, someone grabbed my shoulder.

"...!"

"Hyung!"

"Yes."

...It was Cha Eugene. He was bringing laundry into the living room.

After checking the voice, I recognized the voices of the others.

"Guys, don't move for a moment. Let's adapt to the dark."

That was Ryu Chungwoo.

"Yes, yes...!"

"Wow. Five seconds earlier, and I'd still have been doing the dishes."

Those were Seon Ahyeon and Lee Sejin in the kitchen.

Cha Eugene was by my side and Kim Raebin was on the sofa.

All that remained...

*Bae Sejin?*

At that moment, the sound of holding back a scream was heard along with the sound of a fall from below.

Ugh!

"...!"

"Hyung!"

Fortunately, the voice that came back was fine.

"...I'm okay!"

It was just shaking a bit. I shouted in the direction of the sound.

"What's going on?"

"I saw something strange... No, don't come here! I'll come to you."

"No. Stay."

Don't move.

I turned on the flashlight function on my smartphone and got up.

"Let's go together!"

It was okay to go together, but you shouldn't strangle others.

With Cha Eugene's arm around my neck, we joined the others who followed after seeing the light and moved quickly.

"Sejin-hyung, are you there?"

Bae Sejin lay face down on the floor in an odd pose. He visibly perked up when he saw the flashlight.

"Are your legs okay?"

"...They're okay. No problem."

Bae Sejin, as he spoke, grabbed Ryu Chungwoo's hand and got up from his seat, but he looked a little nervous.

"Excuse me... I think I pushed something as I fell."

Bae Sejin swallowed.

"Look... over there."

I immediately moved the light from my smartphone in the direction he was pointing.

A half-pushed cabinet.

And after that... a door?

What was revealed in the light was a dark-colored fire door.

"Oh my."

"What's this?"

It seemed that the reason Bae Sejin was able to find it was because of the soft luminous light on the floor. It seemed that some kind of glow-in-the-dark paint was used.

"Wow, what kind of secret room is this?"

"I think they use it for evacuation!"

"It's plausible."

Those who were whispering soon made a very common-sense judgment that 'it's private property, so it's better not to go into it.'

*...It seems to lead underground.*

Structurally, it seemed that the area beyond that door was a wildflower path.

Lee Sejin murmured in a spooky voice behind me.

"Doesn't it remind you of that? Isolated in a snowy mountain cabin, and an incident occurs at a hidden door... Oh my gosh!"[2]{Editor's Note: Lee Sejin is talking about the entertainment show that Moondae was on with Cheongryeo in chapter 91-93.}

"No."

Horrible... No, stop talking nonsense.

I slapped Lee Sejin on the back and went back to the living room.

The other guys also followed along, stopping at each room and looking for a smartphone.

"Phew."

"Shall we use this to find a flashlight?"

"There's one in the cabinet beside the entrance. I'll get it."

"Oh~"

Thanks to Chungwoo, we got ourselves a flashlight, and things became a little easier.

The problem was that there were no means to recharge the smartphones at this time.

*I should save it.*

The electricity was out, so there was nothing to do. I clicked my tongue.

"Well, it could be dangerous, so shall we sleep together?"

"I agree with you~"

"I'll tell you a scary story!"

That day, we gathered in the living room and went to bed.

Eugene wanted me to interpret a horror story he would tell in English. It took considerable effort to put a stop to that plan.

And the next morning...

The situation took a more frenzied turn.

"Th-the chicken coop...!"

"Oh my gosh! They're floating away!"

It had rained for two days and the water had filled the front yard.

Strong winds had forced the chicken coop doors open, and the chicks were slowly being swept away through the flooded yard.

On top of the flowing muddy water, yellow lumps gradually moved away...

*Gogogogogok!*

The chickens crowed pitifully.

"No!!"

"We have to save them...!"

In our panic, we all dashed to the chicken coop in our pajamas, without umbrellas or raincoats.

*I'm going crazy.*

"Catch! Catch!"

"Careful not to slip! Keep your center of gravity low!"

Fortunately, all that exercise paid off, and nobody slipped into the muddy water.

And the chicks were all rescued after twists and turns.

Eugene and Chungwoo were at the center of Operation Chicken Rescue.

"Thank god!"

"Whoa..."

With soggy yellow chicks in our hands, we waddled back to the coop and tried to put everything right.

"Whew, I'm glad."

"We'll also bring eggs with us!"

I doubted wringing out our clothes did much good while we were still standing in the rain, but everyone seemed proud of doing it anyway.

And... eggs.

*Hmm.*

I shook the water out of my hair before speaking.

"Why don't we kill one of them?"

"What?"

"Uh huh??"

"Since there are five hens, I thought we could catch one."

"...!!"

There were seven adults here. Surely we could manage to butcher a single chicken.

Kim Raebin nodded.

"That's a good idea. Excessive consumption of preserved foods isn't good for the health..."

"No!! No! No!!"

"Oh my God, how could you say that about these little chicks we just rescued!!"

"They don't understand what we're saying. And aren't they being raised as food anyway?"

And when it came to catching chickens, it was funny how the guys who would love it the most opposed it the most.

Cha Eugene was the most fiercely opposed.

"They're not food! They're our pets!"

This guy passionately explained the fable of 'The Little Prince and the Rose' while mixing in English.

[These chickens are special to us, just like the rose of the little prince is special?!]

Come on, I got it.

"Haha, yes. Let's get some eggs."

After Ryu Chungwoo tidied up, Kim Raebin secretly whispered to me.

"I strongly suggest not distributing the chickens to Cha Eugene when they are killed."

"......"

I tactfully said nothing. After the uproar in the rain, we brought the eggs back inside.

But another ambush awaited.

"...The water was cold."

"I know, right."

There was no hot water, probably due to the power cut, so we had to shower in cold water. Bae Sejin was particularly horrified; he even used hot water in summer.

*We can't go on like this.*

At least the kitchen ran on natural gas rather than electricity, so we could eat warm food. But there were limits to that too.

After we ate white rice, eggs over easy, and canned spicy tuna, I spoke up.

"First of all, we should do something with the electricity."

"...Yes."

"You are right, but... we have no special expertise."

The seven of us had spent our lives becoming entertainers, and not one of us knew much about electrical work. An awkward silence followed.

At that time, Bae Sejin spoke with a worried face.

"Wouldn't it be nice to find something like a fuse box?"

"Oh."

"Oh, maybe!"

Yes. There was a possibility that the switchboard breaker was down.

'We had been supplying electricity with solar power, but the last few days the sun hadn't come out and even the electricity had been cut off...' I stopped thinking in the middle of making quite pessimistic reasoning.

*Then, it might be easier than I thought.*

Ryu Chungwoo crossed his arms.

"Hmm, where could it be? I didn't see anything by the front entrance or on the outside walls."

"Hey, let me ask...?"

I tried to text the PD as I said, but it kept failing to send. It seemed that the signal was not being picked up well.

"Maybe the transmitters were damaged by the wind?"

"Maybe it's because it's such a remote place. It hadn't worked a few times already."

"Mmm."

I thought about it for a while, but soon concluded.

"We have nothing to do anyway, so let's look for a fuse box."

"Let's do that~"

So, roughly divided into groups of two or three, the guys brought a flashlight one by one and started exploring every nook and cranny of the house.

Well, we'd find it before the sun went down.

---

"Um, nothing?"

"......"

Why couldn't we find it?

– There's a high chance we'll find it, right?

– Yes.

I'd been searching the first floor with Lee Sejin and Kim Raebin.

And fuse boxes were usually in the corner of the first floor.

But even after wandering around for quite a long time, we couldn't find anything.

"Is there a possibility that it's on another floor?"

"It could be, but..."

Isn't a fuse box usually on the first floor in a house like this?

At that time, Kim Raebin raised his hand.

"Could it be inside the secret door that Bae Sejin discovered last time?"

"...!"

"Oh, it could be."

I thought of a dark fire door hidden behind a cabinet.

*Surely... There is a possibility.*

It seemed to lead underground, so a boiler room and a distribution board may have been installed there.

"Shall we go?"

"...Okay."

Together with them, I headed to the corner of the hallway where the cabinet was.

The further we went in that direction, the darker it was because there were no windows. Even in the daytime, I had to turn on the flashlight.

"Good thing you brought it~ It almost scared me!"

"Visibility is important."

We reached the partially shifted cabinet in no time and pushed it fully aside, revealing a large fire door.

*...It's a bit excessive to say it's the entrance to the boiler room.*

"Do you want me to open it?"

"...It's alright."

It was better for the person holding the flashlight to open it.

I grabbed the handle of the fire door and turned it.

The door was not locked

*Geeeeeek.*

The door opened with a reluctant sound, as if it hadn't been opened often.

Inside, it was dark and quiet.

When I shined inside with a flashlight, I could see the stairs going down in the dark.

"......"

"......"

".. Isn't it a little scary?"

*It's just dark.*

"Let's go."

"Wait a minute!"

"Everyone, how about joining the other people who are searching elsewhere and moving together?"

"Moondae, you're scared too~ We can see it all on your face, right?"

It was entirely because the two scaredy-cats actively insisted that we wait for a while and joined the search team on the second floor.

"Ah, here."

"WOW, it's dark!"

Bae Sejin and Cha Eugene.

I was doubtful that that combination had completed the search on the second floor, but... Anyway, they joined us, so we went in.

The five of us propped the fire door open, then carefully descended using our flashlights.

The temperature was low, probably because it was underground, and there was a rusty, fishy smell somewhere.

*Ddubeok.*

"I wonder what this room is for?"

"The owner probably uses it for storage or something."

"That's true"

*Ddubeok.*

"...Here's what I've been thinking."

"Yes."

Bae Sejin stopped.

"Why did this person bother to build such a villa on an uninhabited island...?"

"......"

The air suddenly seemed to turn colder.

"...Well, maybe they preferred a quiet atmosphere~"

"But they provided it for entertainment purposes."

Bae Sejin spoke with an expression as if he was breaking out in a cold sweat.

"If they liked the quiet, they wouldn't do that."

"......"

Everyone was quiet again, but soon desperate defenses came out here and there

"They're a person who likes entertainment!"

"They may have a very close acquaintance at the station!"

"...Yes. Sorry."

Bae Sejin quietly kept his mouth shut.

I turned my slightly stiff neck and pointed forward.

"Looks like we've come all the way down."

"Ah."

At last, the stairs ended, opening into a huge room below.

A huge nook... It looked like wooden boxes were piled up all over the place.

"Oh, is it a warehouse?"

"It seems so."

The voices of the others brightened as if they were a little relieved.

*Distribution board...*

I swept my flashlight along the walls, looking for the fuse box, when something unexpected caught my eye.

"...!"

"Huh?"

It was another door. Even double doors.

There was nothing special about it. However, what leaked under that door...

Lee Sejin asked in a trembling voice.

"...Isn't that blood?"

Blood?

A thick, sticky dark red liquid was slowly flowing out from under the doors.

"......"

After recognizing it, I realized what I had thought was a rusty smell until now.

*That metallic smell...*

Blood.

"...!!"

"Aaaagh!!"

"Ahhh!!"

Frightened screams filled the nook.

I staggered back.

*Report...*

Where the hell would I report this? What was this?

[My God, what the hell is this shit! It's blood!!]

"I told you it was weird!! Ahh!!"

"Go up, go up now...!"

At that time...

*Click.*

Suddenly, the light returned.

In an instant, my vision became clear.

"...??"

"Uh?"

A cozy lamp had switched on. The atmosphere in the basement changed at once.

The supposed gloomy warehouse was actually a neatly decorated cellar.

" ...?!"

The wooden boxes I saw earlier also seemed to have been stacked for a pure atmosphere.

A storage room with a nice interior.

And the doors...

"...It's a freezer."

Yes.

What I mistook for a door... It was a modern gray, glossy freezer.

"......"

I strode over and opened the freezer door.

Cold air wafted out, suggesting the freezer had retained some of its chill.

The sausages and other meat had thawed, blood dripping down and pooling at the bottom.

It was the source of the fishy smell.

"......"

"......"

A long silence passed.

"Oh, you guys were here."

"Moo-Moondae. We found the fuse box...!"

" ... Guys?"

"U-um...?"

Until the guys from the 3rd-floor exploration team who found the power distribution board came down here, the rest of them stood in vain and chewed on their foolishness.

I wouldn't deny the fact that I was among the rest...

---

"Still, there is a lot of meat."

"Yeah, it was a problem because we mistook it for human meat."

Be quiet.

After coming to my senses, I took out some edible meat from the freezer that had not yet melted.

I'd have to ask the owner of this villa for his understanding later, but since it was a natural disaster, he should be able to understand us to some extent.

"See? It's a good thing we didn't kill that chicken!"

"...I know."

Eugene lifted the frozen chicken over his head as I nodded quietly.

"The refrigerator above is running again, so let's move the food."

"Yeah~"

*Still, it's a relief that no one has to see that idiotic side of me...*

I was relieved internally, but soon realized it was in vain.

...Cameras these days, the batteries on them lasted longer than I thought.
