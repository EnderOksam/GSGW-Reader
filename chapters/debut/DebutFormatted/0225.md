# Chapter 225 - The Morning Call

Convincing my roommate wasn't as difficult as I thought.

"It's been a while since I stopped checking the internet, and I haven't gone back on my word or made things difficult during that time, have I?"

"Yes. I-it's fine, Moondae...!"

"So, I think it's true that I monitored it for stress relief, not for addiction."

"Uh, uh uh?"

"As you said, if I go too far, it might be stressful, but... I took a break and got my senses back."

Seon Ahyeon couldn't keep his mouth shut as if he didn't know that the words would connect like this.

"I think I’ll be able to control myself and not get stressed."

"O-of course, Moondae would do well, but..."

And this would be the part where I'd turn the conversation around.

"It was thanks to you. Thank you."

"...!! N-no, Moondae handled it well, but, what...!"

"Okay. In the future, I will adjust well and monitor in a stress-free way."

It was over.

Afterward, Seon Ahyeon was dragged around the schedule in a daze, feeling like he had been scammed, but it must have been okay because he soon recovered.

In this way, I succeeded in freely resuming monitoring using the break time.

"The staff will start setting up for the concept photo shoot at seven tomorrow, so let's not stay up too late."

"Yes!"

And the night when today's work was over...

*It's been a while.*

*I won this time.*

...It seemed that Seon Ahyeon's advice really worked well at the right time.

I turned my eyes to the bed next to me. Just in time, our eyes met, and it saved me the trouble of calling for him.

"Is there anything you want to eat?"

"What?"

"For a late-night snack."

Seon Ahyeon seemed to think about what this meant for a moment, then raised his upper body with a '!!' expression written on his face.

"Ah, Moo-Moondae, do you want to have a late-night snack?"

"......"

He must be blaming himself for not noticing that I was hungry.

I decided to make him something tomorrow morning in return. I calmed Ahyeon down and put an end to his rambling before returning to the forums.

Let's see, there hasn't been a big topic for a while since then.

Since we were inactive, the main focus was on the fans' reactions to roommates and moving content.

But when it came to recent times, the atmosphere behind the scenes changed completely.

It started with one eyewitness post.

★$
[I saw Shamstar at the hospital. Nice!]

I went to the emergency room because I had a stomachache, and on the way back, I saw several long-legged men getting out of a van in a parking lot.

From the height and the number of people, I figured they were idols. When I got closer, I realized they were Shamstar—Kim Raebin wasn't wearing a mask. He seemed pretty clumsy, haha.

But he didn't look happy. Could someone in his family be ill? That was my guess.

(_shaky picture_)

$★

Since the post was in a community that treated celebrities' private lives as public property, it drew no criticism—only nosy responses from other users.

I held back a sigh.

*...We hadn't been careful enough.*

But none of us had the presence of mind to think about that while we faced the possibility of losing one of Raebin's family members.

"......"

I searched for posts using 'Raebin' and 'hospital' and related terms.

★$

Most fans had guessed correctly that one of Raebin's grandparents was ill.

Still, in most fan communities, it was forbidden to mention it openly.

*It spread faster than I thought.*

That first eyewitness post must have spread widely before anyone managed to get it deleted.

*The company's response was a bit slow.*

The company had clearly missed this because its staff were busy reorganizing our album schedule and preparing press releases.

So, under the water, it was perfect for stalker-style crazies to rampage.

They seemed to feel a strange sense of superiority as if they owned information that many people were curious about.

Reading all that hateful bile made me feel sick.

*...No one's done anything to stop them, either.*

It only got this bad because I hadn't been paying attention.

"......"

Fuck, stop. I don't have time to waste on self-reproach right now.

*Shut up and use your brain.*

A few patterns that could be used immediately came to mind.

*First of all, it would be best to release the message room screenshots first.*

We needed to post the words shared about Kim Raebin's work on social media as a kind of comeback notice.

Then, we would be able to put an end to speculation about Kim Raebin's current condition or anxiety related to participation...

Even in the worst case, it would work.

Therefore... Even if Kim Raebin ended up unable to participate in the promotions at all...

There would be less needless cursing.

Still, there would be pups that would try to bite, but this time I was ready to handle it neatly.

*We need to warn them that we'll sue if they keep this up.*

That'll be the first task for our dedicated management team, and I'll make sure it's ready before our promotions end—even if I have to go without sleep.

*Before that, let's start with what we can do right now.*

I immediately selected screenshots of the most suitable conversations about Kim Raebin's work.

I immediately asked the group chat for permission to upload the screenshots to social media.

Kim Raebin was nocturnal, so he'd probably be awake even now.

Maybe it was a sign that he slipped while trying to write 'I think it's going to be okay.'

*...Did he make a mistake because he was sleepy?*

I crossed my arms. And I waited for a reply with a cautious attitude.

However... Several minutes passed, and no corrected reply came.

"......"

So, this meant that Kim Raebin did not correct the typo and lost contact.

It wasn't something to laugh at.

*What is to be expected...*

It was like shit, but then an intense assumption hit me on the head.

I tapped my arm with my finger.

*...Was it an obituary[2]{T/N: Obituary: An announcement of death.}? Had his grandmother passed away?*

If so, I shouldn't call him now. I'd only be getting in the way.

I barely came to a reasonable conclusion.

*...I'll check around tomorrow morning.*

By then, we'd know the outcome—good or bad.

★:
-LSJ:Keun Sejin: Raebin?
:★

In the message room, messages from other guys calling Kim Raebin appeared a few times, but they soon went away.

Everyone seemed to be grasping the atmosphere.

About what might have happened.

"......"

I wanted to respond as rationally as possible... To derive it, I kept turning my brain.

*If the timing of the obituary and social media upload overlap, it wouldn't be possible.*

I decided to put the upload on hold.

I put my phone down and lay on the bed.

"M-Moondae, are you going to sleep?"

"Yes."

*Let's go to bed without unnecessary thoughts.*

There was no time or space for this.

I wasn't particularly sleepy, but fatigue finally won out, and I fell asleep a little past midnight.

However, even after the day was bright, Kim Raebin did not contact us.

Damn it.

My head wasn't working.

*Is it because I couldn't sleep?*

This speculation was also an excuse. My Bacchus 500 trait was running well. It wasn't about fatigue, it was about my mental state.

I didn't know if it was because of Raebin, the call, the awful limbo we'd been through, or the status window... Just, fuck, that was it.
