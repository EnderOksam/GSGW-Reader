# Chapter 221 - GED, II

Even while the bell rang and the test papers were being distributed, three or four sentences popped up in the student's head at the same time and then disappeared.

*Come to think of it, Moondae must have silver hair now, black hair? What? Is it a wig? Isn't that also Moondae?*

*Even if it's not really Moondae, I'm still lucky to see someone who looks like him.*

*Wait a minute! Can't I see his face when they're checking his identity!?*

*Oh! He'll look around when the papers get to him—no, wait. The supervisors are handing them out.*

Some were correct, some were incorrect assumptions.

For example, Park Moondae used color spray to cover his silver hair, and when checking his identity, he slightly lowered his mask and showed his face to the supervisor.

However, it was not properly visible even to the examinee sitting in the back, and since the supervisor was an elderly man, he did not immediately recognize Park Moondae, so he remained calm.

*Ugh!*

So, the examinee in the back seat could only scream internally, suffering from frustration and curiosity.

However, an unexpected opportunity came soon after.

"Now, please pass the tests back."

"...!"

This was because the test papers were handed out by the person in front of you!

And the student... finally saw him.

*Oh my god.*

The man in the front seat turned around only slightly, handed over the test paper in an instant, and straightened his posture.

In addition, he even wore a mask, but the test taker was sure.

Those horizontally large, clean-lined eyes!

*Park Moondae!*

It was really Park Moondae!

The examinee received the test paper while feeling like she was about to faint.

Her heart was pounding so much that she couldn't tell whether she was getting an album signed at a fan signing event or taking her GED.

No, she'd never been to a fan signing event!

"You can start after the bell rings."

*Save me.*

She screamed internally, but after so many mock tests, she picked up her pencil and began working without hesitation.

But the shock left her head white, so she couldn't see anything.

After a while, though...

*...The sooner I solve it, the sooner I can see Moondae's back!*

A strange idea gave a realization to the examinee who was now unable to raise her head and stuck her nose in the test paper.

So, the student began to solve the problem vigorously. It was the moment when failing the GED was one step further away.

*Ah!*

And to the surprise of only Park Moondae, it wasn't just the examinee in the back seat who was conscious of him in this classroom.

*It's fine.*

I finished the last elective, Ethics, and put down my pencil.

The test went smoothly without any problems.

Lunchtime had been risky, but I found a secluded corridor and ate by the window.

*It was easy to eat because it was porridge in the first place.*

Most importantly, this year's exam felt easier than most of the sample tests I'd taken.

I was worried that the social sciences and sciences would be written only in the forgotten parts, but that wasn't the case.

*I think I did pretty well.*

I stretched a little, almost certain that I would pass.

"Hoo."

Someone behind me gasped. I lowered my arms so I wouldn't distract them.

*The remaining time... 10 minutes.*

And I quietly waited until the exam was over.

On the way back, I had already called a taxi on time, so I just had to get down and get on.

*It's done.*

However, the reality that I met 10 minutes later was not very expected.

It was because someone in the same classroom started talking to me while I was waiting for everyone to leave after opening my smartphone.

"Hey, student!"

"..."

"Student... Aren't you Moondae? Moondae who made hotteok?"

I pondered briefly here.

Should I shout 'You're looking at the wrong person' and run away?

However, I stopped because there seemed to be more than one or two witnesses.

Besides this... Seeing that the lady also spoke in small whispers in her own way, I thought I could finish it quickly.

"Yes. I'm here for the test."

"Oh my! I see. That's lovely... May I have your autograph? My daughter is such a fan!"

"Sure."

After briefly signing the admission ticket, I was about to leave.

But it was already too late.

"*Ahem*, if you don't mind, me too..."

"Ah, yes."

"Excuse me! I'm really sorry, but can I have it too?"

The few people who were still in the classroom immediately began to line up. It was also orderly.

None of them made a fuss.

A strong hypothesis came to mind.

*Could all these people have been suspicious before?*

Somehow, six or seven of them didn't go out of class and wandered around. It could have been because of me.

It seemed that I was too complacent about the popularity of TeSTAR.

*I'm breaking out in a cold sweat.*

Above all, it was most surprising that so many people did not make any movements during the test.

If even one person had made a fuss and talked about it, there would have been a high probability that it would turn into a nuisance controversy.

Then I would have already escaped at lunchtime.

*They must have been considerate of me during the test.*

Thanks to their discretion, I could finish undisturbed. They'd had my back, so I kept signing without complaint.

"Thank you!"

"Wow, thank you..."

I was about to leave after signing autographs for a few people who had gathered there.

*Let's get out of here quickly before photo requests come in.*

However, when I picked up the bag, I saw that the person in the back was still sitting down.

"...?"

Moreover, without a slight movement, she stared at me with her eyes shining brightly, then opened her mouth when our eyes met.

That was the expression I saw a lot at fan signing events.

"Do you need an autograph?"

"...?! What? Yes, yes!"

It wasn't self-consciousness. It was relief.

"I-I love you."

"Thank you."

I quickly signed the young woman's test ID slip and left.

"Goodbye!"

"Good luck on your activities!"

It was only fortunate that the people at the other exam halls didn't see this because this class was at the end of the exam hall.

I hurried down the nearly empty corridor.

*Are they going to post about it?*

There wouldn't be any big problems.

They would either sympathize with me or provoke me.

After shrugging my shoulders, I went outside and immediately got into a taxi.

It was as soon as I came back to the dorm.

"Welcome~"

"D-did everything go well?"

"Yes."

"How was the porridge for lunch?"

"I ate it well. Thanks."

And under the strong insistence of Cha Eugene, who used my test as an excuse, we ordered and ate pork feet.

Thanks to that, I did another subtle thing of listening to a toast without being able to drink alcohol.

"You did a good job taking the exam today... Let's recover well until our next activity and show a cool image again."

"Sure~"

"Yeah!"

"I want to spend a fulfilling preparation period."

"W-wait, Moondae, put your phone down!" Bae Sejin said.

"...It's just a video of a dog eating meat."

It was another ordinary day of our hiatus.

Dinner too... It was delicious.
