# Chapter 271 - The Encore Concert, II

Were they making their own documentary?

*Oh, no.*

After everything they'd been through, Kim Raebin’s fan felt sick at the mere mention of the word ‘documentary.’ A chill ran down her spine.

*Hey, is this really a good idea...?*

*Wouldn't it be better to let sleeping dogs lie?*

However, that thought soon disappeared entirely.

It was because TeSTAR, who'd reappeared on the screen, looked so warm and approachable.

It seemed to be morning, judging by how the person holding the shaky camera was poking at the members sprawled on the floor.

**[Let's eat.]**

**[Wow... I'm so sleepy.]**

**[...I wish I could eat meat.]**

"...??"

Rather than their simply looking close... What stood out was how casual and genuine their actions were, without any obvious purpose or exaggeration for the camera.

Many TeSTAR videos had been polished through the skillful planning and editing of production teams.

The members' personalities shone through even in those videos, but this was the first time she'd seen them looking as though they were completely unaware of their viewers.

♪ *Diririring-* ♫

The slow accompaniment was... An acoustic version of Kim Raebin's ‘You Are the Magic,’ which they'd performed just before.

The raw, almost unmixed audio resonated throughout the venue, accompanying the footage nicely.

**[Hmm.]**

**[You can't really tell, can you?]**

**[I guess not.]**

Between cuts of them practicing the concert choreography, they gathered together to watch their dance practice videos and monitor their performance.

They sat around eating ice cream in a hotel room while blankly watching some foreign TV show.

In a hotel in another country, they played board games, sometimes throwing cards at each other.

**[Haha!]**

The laughter of the person holding the camera echoed over the guitar. The screen shook.

Short videos followed.

"......"

Kim Raebin's fan found herself mesmerized by the video, unable to think about anything else.

And she didn't even realize how absorbed she was.

The screen jumped between shots of them dancing, throwing objects back and forth, checking the dye in each other's hair, and coming off the stage laughing and running.

And when the accompaniment passed the bridge and entered the climax...

Whoa...

The photos they'd taken of the concert preparations and wrap-up passed through the video like a panorama.

A picture of everyone holding firecrackers at the beach, Park Moondae grilling meat at an after-party, and Lee Sejin shaking a drink.

Cha Eugene smiled as he climbed onto a piece of stage equipment during rehearsal...

They were just countless moments from TeSTAR's lives, and it was hard to tell when or where they'd been filmed.

It was all pouring onto a single screen like a huge meteor shower, showing how much time the members of TeSTAR spent together outside their promotions...

"......"

The audience fell into deafening silence as the intermittent screams faded.

And, it was only then that Kim Raebin's fan was able to let out an internal scream.

*This... You despicable bastards!!*

The fandom was already soaked in melancholy.

Fans of the group who'd refused to watch the other documentary in solidarity with Cha Eugene were probably shedding tears as they watched the livestream at home.

And somehow, her eyes were stinging and she felt her nose starting to run too.

Given the atmosphere in the concert venue, it would've been strange if she hadn't been affected.

"Ha..."

Finding her sudden surge of emotions unfamiliar, she was forced to admit something.

*Okay, this will help for now...*

It wouldn't last long, but combined with the magic of the concert, a video like this was bound to have an effect.

*The atmosphere will improve for a while after the concert ends...*

However, the preparations of the members did not end there.

+=
[Get ready for the encore]
=+

Under the captions in the video, a silent scene of the members ordering and eating fried chicken in the studio while talking played out.

Then they were shown entering the recording booth one by one, dressed comfortably.

!-
\
@c@$$[In other countries, we sang songs that were famous there. So... We thought we'd do something similar in Korea.]$$@c@
-!

After showing the short handwritten memo, the screen went black again.

The soft guitar accompaniment passed the climax and slowly faded into silence.

And there were subtitles.

+=
[In celebration of TeSTAR]
=+

+=
[200X]

[The song the most people listened to in the year all seven members were first in Korea]
=+

The large screen on the stage parted, and fog from the dry ice slowly rolled out, filling the air with anticipation.

And instead of the medley from ⟨Idol Incorporated⟩, a melodic, emotional accompaniment began to play.

"...!"

It was a familiar and good melody.

It was a carol-sampling song released at the end of the year when Cha Eugene and Kim Raebin, the youngest members of TeSTAR, were born.

+=
[It's Getting Better]
=+

Arranged as a Korean-style ballad, the carol beautifully rang through the venue.

The stage floor split open, and the lift slowly rose through the dry ice fog.

The members, dressed in neat winter casual clothes, were standing on top of it.

Bae Sejin, wearing a coat, took up the microphone first.

$p
♪ *When small mistakes hurt you*\
*When small things hurt more than they should* ♫
p$

His voice wasn't particularly elaborate, which made it sound all the more sincere.

$p
♪ *I just sit by the window*\
*And think of myself next year* ♫
p$

Seon Ahyeon's clear, low bass voice rang out.

And Lee Sejin came in to harmonize on his second line.

$p
♪ *In the same season, on the same day*\
*When I look back on my memories* ♫
p$

An orchestra began to play. The live band, which had been playing powerfully throughout the concert, set aside the electronic sounds and layered soft strings and keyboards.

TeSTAR's clear, powerful voices rose in pitch alongside the music.

$p
♪ *This gloomy day*\
*Becomes nourishment for my soul*\
*Helping it grow; the sharp pain*\
*Anchors me firmly* ♫
p$

It was Park Moondae's voice. His pink hair looked soft above his gray leather jacket, but his voice rang out powerfully.

A beautiful harmony layered over his voice, supported by Ryu Chungwoo's baritone.

$p
♪ *On the day I head down there*\
*And find my true self* ♫
p$

Cha Eugene sang the last verse with a smile on his face.

$p
♪ *I won't feel a thing* ♫
p$

It was a voice that sounded somewhat relieved.

"...Ah."

The lyrics and mood of the song, the video of the documentary.

Everything came together and created a crazy synergy.

*These children... They've worked really hard.*

Here and there in the audience, she could hear the muffled sounds of people trying not to cry.

Kim Raebin's fan tried hard to stay cool-headed and logical, wondering if this wasn't an overly blatant attempt to manipulate the fans' emotions.

It was self-torture. She couldn't believe she was acting just like the Internet meme of someone pretending not to be moved while fighting back tears.

$p
♪ *You won't feel a thing* ♫
p$

They sang the song sincerely and affectionately, without excessive embellishment.

Fans slowly waved their light sticks back and forth, the lights twinkling in unison.

TeSTAR's voices hummed the melody of the sampled carol.

$p
♪ *Du- durururudu-* ♫
p$

The audience was immersed in the harmony of sound and light, as though sinking into the depths of a warm sea.

$p
♪ *Happy New Year to you*\
*I know you'll be okay.* ♫
p$

The song ended on a fairly low note.

"......"

*Wow... This is a scam.*

Kim Raebin's fan thought as she watched the close-up on the giant screen of the members lowering their microphones and smiling.

*Just... They're doing everything they can to make the most of this concert...*

Maybe it was because she was a personal fan herself, but she couldn't shake the feeling she'd been tricked too.

It felt like all the emotions were sucked out of her body.

*Does TeSTAR mean that much to you guys...? Raebin...*

Seeing Kim Raebin, who was wearing a cute white padded jacket, holding hands with the other members and bowing, she felt a complex sense of defeat...

There were plenty of fans who could resist most things their idols threw at them, but right now, she didn't feel like one of them.

However, Kim Raebin's fan eventually pulled herself together again.

*But it's only temporary! By next week, everyone will have forgotten what happened at the concert, and then...*

**[Thank you for listening.]**

**[How did you like the song we prepared?]**

"It was great!!"

"Wow!!"

The cheers of approval were automatic. The audience couldn't help it.

The members smiled in anticipation, stepped off the lift and got into formation, turning to face the back of the stage.

**[Let's move on to the next encore song.]**

*With those clothes??*

Kim Raebin's fan paused in confusion as she saw the members tossing aside their winter outerwear.

They were wearing their usual stage outfits underneath: leather pants and white linen shirts.

"Oooo!!"

It was a typical look, but it worked.

While screams poured out at the guys who took off their gloves and hats and threw them away, the lights went out.

And then began a ringing, sweet intro.

It was 'Wheel (Day)'.

$p
♪ *Give me back the wheel*\
*Let me fly far*\
*The melody rings. It's beautiful*\
*Let it pop. It touches my heart* ♫
p$

*What?*

That song... They did it earlier.

Well, it was a stage she could watch over and over (maybe because a certain someone had made the song), but she was a little surprised.

$p
♪ *Give me back the wheel*\
*Let me find my way to your dreams*\
*Stay unchanged today and tomorrow*\
*It doesn't end here, let me in* ♫
p$

And the outfits were a little too spicy to be a good match for ‘Wheel.’

They didn't quite suit the song.

*Mmm.*

*Did they not have time to prepare anything else?*

Kim Raebin's fan was starting to feel a little disappointed...

$p
♪ *And as it flies*\
*Whi-whee-whee-whee!* ♫
p$

"...!!"

Suddenly, a familiar whistle cut into the song.

And just like that, a powerful bass and beat flooded the airwaves...

The drop came out of nowhere.

$p
♪ *Jump off*\
*No parachute is necessary*\
*On the contrary* ♫
p$

*It's Drill!!*

Yes. The chorus of their second title track, ‘Drill (Night),’ took over in an instant.

The stage lighting changed, leaving behind the wistful, refreshing feel of ‘Wheel’ in favor of powerful pinpoint lights.

The luminous paint on their black leather pants, invisible before, now flickered into view under the lights.

And the point choreography of 'Drill' began without mercy.

"What!!"

And only then did everyone understand the situation.

$p
♪ *Just run. I'm coming in, coming in*\
*(Let me in)* ♫
p$

These guys had merged their two title tracks into a single remix!!

And subtitles appeared for those who were watching this scene through the broadcast.

+=
[TeSTAR ‘Daybreak’ (Wheel + Drill)]
=+

It was the title of a new song.

★$
-@ll@What the? I never expected that challenge to be a teaser for a new song@ll@

-@ll@Genius idols. They're crazy@ll@

-@ll@Are they crazy? Are they crazy?@ll@

-@ll@The song is so good. I love it@ll@
$★

Even as the viewers filled the live chat with comments, the song raced on like crazy.

And the chorus mixed the two songs together perfectly with entirely new lines.

$p
♪ *Like a drill*\
*Digging in, taking you in*\
*Yes, it's beautiful*\
*Let it pop. It touches my heart* ♫
p$

She didn't know how they'd done it, but the two songs blended wonderfully without losing either of their distinct moods.

The structure of ‘Wheel,’ which was nice to listen to but somewhat boring, and 'Drill', which was intense but somewhat excessive, fit together like a puzzle.

On top of that, the members maintained that delicate balance with unbelievable stability.

The performance was powerful and refreshing like a carbonated drink, while at the same time having a playful, silly quality to it.

$p
♪ *Turn my ferris wheel around*\
*Lights that explode like stars* ♫
p$

It was satisfying to see the two choreographies refined into a collection of their best parts, the ones that had received the strongest reactions.

"Aaaagh!!"

Kim Raebin's fan screamed, but her voice couldn't be heard.

Because her surroundings were already full of screams.

A rush of adrenaline came from the pleasant surprise and satisfaction, and the tension of seeing something so different from the previous stage had the audience riveted.

$p
♪ *Enjoy the moment*\
*That's my thrill. Ha!* ♫
p$

As the group performed the choreography with knife-like precision, Cha Eugene delivered his line while tumbling across the stage.

*Aaaagh!*

"Uhhhh..."

Kim Raebin’s fan groaned as if she were about to pass out, but she was forced to admit something.

People were going to be talking about this concert for a very long time.

The voices of the fans who supported the whole group were louder than ever.

*People tend to become more relaxed when they're happy.*

I shrugged and put down my phone.

"Did everything turn out the way you wanted it to?" Lee Sejin asked.

"Yes."

"Sit down and eat some meat, Park Moondae!" Bae Sejin said.

Alcohol seemed to make Bae Sejin more confident. I sat down as he suggested and ate my late dinner.

And I thought about what would help us sustain this victory after the lingering emotions from the concert wore off.

*We did everything we had to do.*

We'd eased the fans' wariness, giving them both satisfaction and justification for supporting us.

So what was the next step? To unite the fans in a battle against a common enemy.

And as it happened, a suitable one was approaching at just the right time.

A comparison target that would make fans say things like 'We're still better than them' and 'Looking back, we were fairies.'

And at the same time... It would be an enemy outside the fandom.

I remembered the article I checked just before.

★$
[#f#First stage of season three of ⟨Relisted! Idol Incorporated⟩ revealed#f#]
$★

It was the new season of ⟨Idol Incorporated⟩.

This season would have male contestants, not female ones. The resulting group would be TeSTAR’s first male juniors from the show.

*As long as we can keep our fans from jumping ship, there are few external targets as suitable as this.*

I shrugged.

*Let's see how this unfolds.*
