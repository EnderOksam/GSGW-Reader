# Chapter 295 - Setting the Stage

An agency group concert.

Usually, it was an event held only by companies with at least one famous idol group, both male and female groups, and some promising rookies.

In that sense, it might seem that our agency, which had three groups from ⟨Idol Inc.⟩, met the minimum requirements... but that was an illusion.

On my way back to the dorm, I frowned as I read over the proposal I'd brought with me.

*I don't think anyone will like it.*

This hastily assembled agency had no distinct identity, no matter how hard you looked.

It was missing what people commonly referred to as the 'feeling unique to that agency'.

*For the audience to be satisfied, there has to be something in common between the groups.*

Our agency had simply gathered up every group that had won on the audition program. It was pretty bold of them to plan their own concert using those groups and the money they'd made from them.

Of course, I could roughly guess what they hoped to get out of it.

"Hm~ I guess they're trying to drum up some attention for the group that's about to debut?"

Lee Sejin shrugged and spoke.

"Look, they've even given them a few of our songs to cover! And songs from our season of ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ are in the draft setlist too~"

"Mmm."

And Bae Sejin, who was drinking water in the kitchen, spoke with a slightly nervous look on his face.

"...Isn't this an attempt to replace us?"

"Replace us?"

"Well, uh, we don't have much time left on our contracts... They might be trying to get the public to move on to the next group!"

"What...! Do they really think this group has already exhausted its potential? They'd be so wrong!"

Kim Raebin balked as if the theory sounded plausible. Bae Sejin nodded gravely.

"...I'm just saying it's possible."

*Come on.*

*Did he see someone saying something like that on WeTube?*

He'd been watching a lot of K-pop-related channels recently, so it seemed he'd picked up some half-baked ideas here and there.

I smiled and shook my head.

"I don't think so. It seems like they're just trying to bring some buzz to the new group."

"Y-yeah?"

"Yes. The recent season didn't do as well as ours, so I think they're trying to piggyback off us. Don't worry too much."

They'd only just managed to build up a friendly relationship with us, so it would be a waste for the company to give up on TeSTAR now.

*They probably think we're going to renew our contracts for sure.*

At most, they'd try to take advantage of TeSTAR's status in any way they could.

*There's no benefit in killing the goose that lays the golden eggs.*

Lee Sejin readily agreed.

"I agree! Still, it's good to be wary~ Hmm, I think what hyung said is worth thinking about too."

"...Yes."

"Excellent assessment."

Bae Sejin and Kim Raebin quickly discarded the conspiracy theory. And Cha Eugene intervened without hesitation.

"So when are we doing it? Is it happening soon?!"

"Let's see... In twenty-five days."

Ryu Chungwoo pointed to the *Venue rented for August* part of the proposal. I was more interested in a different part.

*It's smaller than I thought.*

Jamsil Indoor Stadium, only about 11,000 seats.

At least they hadn't made the same stupid calculation based on fan counts: 'TeSTAR + Miry-nay + Rookie? Let's go to the stadium!'

That wasn't the kind of industry insight I'd come to expect from the boss of our agency.

*Headquarters must have stepped in.*

It looked like T1 had gotten involved.

Since the concert itself was going to air on TNet's channel, it seemed T1 had pushed them to hurry things along.

*TaKon was so well received that they probably want a repeat of it.*

The fans probably wouldn't be too happy about it, and the agency might take some flak... Well, TeSTAR had little to lose.

*If any of the videos taken at the concert go viral, that'll be free publicity.*

We were approaching the end of our contracts, so now was the time to avoid friction with T1 if possible. I figured we could go along with this much without a boycott.

"We only need to prepare five songs in total."

I chuckled and tapped the plan.

*Let them get some buzz, then.*

But as for what kind of attention they got... That was up to them to deal with.

Two male idol groups of a similar age from the same audition series... Even singing the same song?

People would compare them like crazy.

And those comparisons wouldn't necessarily come out in the other group's favor.

"Um, three and a half weeks. That's tight," Ryu Chungwoo said.

"It's similar to the theme of the last concert, so I think we'll be fine~" Lee Sejin said.

"We'll need to ask about the particulars, like whether any special arrangements are necessary," Kim Raebin said.

I called up the status window of the member who was talking so eagerly right now.

★-
[Name: Kim Raebin]

Rap: A+ (S+)

Vocal: C+

Dance: B (A+)

Appearance: A- (A+)

Talent: A (S)

Characteristic: Maestro (S)
-★

Even if I picked anyone at random in this group, their stats came out roughly like this.

Compared to the ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ days, a lot of the guys had gained at least three stat points.

*And Kim Raebin's characteristic even went up a rank.*

Maybe that was only natural, considering he willingly threw himself into intense short-term bursts of production for every album.

Bae Sejin was relatively the weakest, but even he seemed to have learned a strange trick recently.

– *Wow! I can just memorize the entire movement along with the lyrics...!*

– *...??*

This guy had grown accustomed to the basic dance routines and could now focus on reproducing the movements rather than just dancing to the rhythm. It made his dancing within the group far more appealing.

*Isn't that more difficult?*

Well, it didn't matter, since he seemed to find it more intuitive.

Anyway, the important thing was that there were now very few people online criticizing TeSTAR's skills onstage.

*That makes us a very difficult group to cover.*

What had happened with Onyx when we'd switched songs for the Rookie of the Year award had already cemented that impression.

The internet was convinced that covering TeSTAR was impossible.

Besides, this time, the other group would be covering us while we did nothing.

Given the difference in basic stats and the amount of practice, the result was practically already decided.

So it'd be strange to worry about losing fans over something like this.

*All we have to do is be careful to treat our juniors with respect.*

What worried me instead was this joint stage that seemed to have been added as filler. As it happened, Ryu Chungwoo was pointing at it now.

!$
#***Joint stage (Undecided) / Two members per group***#
$!

"And there is one joint stage among the five."

"Yes."

It was a joint stage where a couple of our members would perform with members from the other two groups.

Aside from the tacky ending where everyone would sing a popular song that wasn't TeSTAR's, this would be the first and last time our juniors performed with us.

*No matter who gets picked, there's going to be some noise over it.*

Then, Cha Eugene shot his hand up again.

"I want to do it! I want to do the joint stage!"

"Haha."

*Just as I thought.*

None of the members answered Cha Eugene's eager request. If any of us agreed now, he'd really end up doing it.

*It would be a bit cruel to send Cha Eugene out there.*

We didn't care, of course, but that was how it would look to the public.

It was like sending a strategic weapon to a friendly charity match.

It was something worth thinking about a little more, so I decided to put it on hold for now.

And before long, at the first group meeting and practice session, it became clear that this had been worth worrying about.

"This is the new setlist."

We arrived a little ahead of schedule and received a piece of paper. We immediately noticed that the joint stage had been revised.

!$
#***Joint stage (Undecided) / Two members from TeSTAR, two from Miry-nay***#
$!

*Those bastards tapped out.*

The debut team from the recent season of ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ had dropped out of the joint stage.

Their excuse was that preparing for their debut kept them too busy, and they already had too many performances to practice for.

"If you don't mind, I'd like the end of this performance to lead smoothly into the next one through the storytelling. That'll be how we bring the rookies in."

The director's explanation sounded reasonable, but the decision was a little too strategic to have been thought up by the rookie group alone.

It was a good excuse, but... The timing was odd.

"Hmm."

*...This is pretty interesting.*

I'd set it aside for now and deal with our more pressing priorities.

While the director was called away elsewhere, I started revising our plan for the joint stage.

It wasn't long before Miry-nay arrived, out of breath.

*Bang!*

The door flew open.

"Hello!"

They weren't late, but it looked like they were also busy preparing for a comeback. After we exchanged greetings, with some preparations being made and staff moving back and forth, Ryu Chungwoo kindly explained.

"There's been a bit of a change to the proposal. It'll be just our two groups in the joint stage."

"Ah, yes."

Rank 2, so... Park Minha spoke with a somewhat resigned look on her face.

"The proposal...? We didn't get anything like that, sunbaenim..."

None of us said anything. It seemed Miry-nay was still too new a group to warrant the courtesy of a heads-up, and they'd only been given a one-sided notice.

*Then again, maybe TeSTAR is the special case.*

A series of incidents had given us a measure of control, but most groups would still be pretty docile when it came to their agency even in their fourth year.

*Hmm.*

Lee Sejin quickly stepped in to keep things from getting awkward.

"Ah, it's only the draft. It's hardly important! What matters is putting on a great performance! Please take good care of us~"

"Yep. You're absolutely right. We'll do our best no matter what. I look forward to a great concert!"

Cha Eugene cheerfully held out his hand for a handshake.

"Yeees! Let's have a great performance together–"

"Wait a minute."

Without changing my expression, I calmly grabbed Cha Eugene's shoulder.

"Eugene, what do you mean, 'together'?"

"...??"

"Oh, right... I won't be doing that stage..."

Cha Eugene looked a little put out and pulled his hand back.

Yes. We'd just excluded Cha Eugene from the joint stage in an emergency meeting.

– *What? But I'll do a great job!*

– *Yes, we know you're good at it. That's why you should do something even bigger at the concert.*

– *What would that be?*

– *Uh, well...*

...Let me just say that we used a lot of carrots. And the reason we'd gone out of our way to exclude him wasn't actually my initial concern that he'd outshine the others onstage.

*This guy is just too friendly.*

At the same time, he seldom showed restraint. He wouldn't hesitate to do anything that suited the stage, and could easily cross a line in the fans' eyes.

*There'll be two female idols and two male idols on this stage. There are too many things to consider, so we can't send him out there.*

In a situation like this, it was safest to send only those who could keep a cool head and knew how to use their brains.

So there was only one answer.

"I... see!"

"All right~!"

Lee Sejin smiled and put his arm around my shoulder.

"It'll be me and Moondae joining you for the joint stage~! I think it'll be great. Please take good care of us!"

And that settled it.

If we did this, the worst criticism we'd get would be people laughing at how out of place we seemed in our roles.

"Oh, really! Is it okay if I come up with ideas and stuff? I hope we can do something really cool!"

"Yulki- ackkkkk."

"Huh? Why? Shouldn't I have said that?"

At Miry-nay's Rank 1's remarks, the other members groaned, lowering their heads and clinging to her arms.

Rank 2 nodded quietly with a resigned look on her face.

"Yes. It will be Yulki unnie and me."

Rank 1 and Rank 2 from both audition shows, side by side.

They didn't want to lose... It was probably a lineup Rank 2 had come up with.

*Did they guess that TeSTAR would try to win even against female idols?*

Was it because we were all from an audition program? This was also a unique situation.

At this rate, we'd end up trying to outdo each other like in a team battle on the audition program. And that would mean a one hundred percent chance of unpleasant rumors spreading.

"Wow, that's great news~! Let's put on a fantastic performance!" Lee Sejin said.

Besides, Lee Sejin, who'd normally say things like 'Let's make a cheer, let's create a group chat for the four of us,' was focusing solely on work. That gave me a rough idea of how things would go.

*And if we're going to put on a high-quality performance...*

There was only one option.

I mentally set aside all the joint stage concepts I'd come up with except for one.

Then I ran through the conversation a few times in my head, working out how to steer it as naturally as possible in the direction I needed.

***

After the director returned, a few important announcements were made and different topics discussed...

Those of us taking part in the joint stage held a separate meeting.

We started with what would be difficult to change later... stage equipment.

*As expected.*

And because our joint stage was wedged between the existing stages, we wouldn't be able to use equipment reserved for the performances before and after ours.

"Director, you're saying it's best if the equipment usage doesn't overlap, right?" Lee Sejin asked the camera director.

"Yes, yes."

*Hmm.*

*Then wire rigging is out. No confetti or lifts either, since those will be used in the following stage.*

"Water might be difficult too. Our title track comes two performances later, so we don't want people drawing direct comparisons, right?"

"You're right."

Lee Sejin smoothly crossed out the equipment TeSTAR planned to use for our own stages. *He's doing great.*

However, Miry-nay's Rank 1 seemed a little frustrated.

"Why don't we start by choosing a song like we usually do? Right now we're only talking about what we can't do. That's no fun!"

"H-hey, unnie!" Minha cried.

"You've got a point," I said.

*Nice timing.*

"Is there something you have in mind?"

"Ah! Well, we think hip-hop might be good..."

I let her list as many songs as Miry-nay wanted to do.

I waited until she grew excited and all kinds of possibilities came to mind.

*Judging by her personality, she's the type who wants to try a lot of different things.*

And while she was talking, a few of the songs I'd been considering were sure to come up.

"In that sense, I think 'Attack' might be pretty good too..."

*There it is.*

*Now it'll feel like you chose the song, not us.*

After waiting a little longer, I signaled Lee Sejin and intervened.

"Come to think of it, you said earlier that you wanted to do something cool."

"Yes, yes!"

"Then why don't we do the song you just mentioned... with a concept like this?"

I wrote lines and letters on the paper.

Concept, expected effect, the direction of arrangement...

I pretended to brainstorm on the spot to make it seem like I was coming up with everything on the fly.

"Like this."

I actually found the process pretty fun, which made the way I was steering the conversation seem a little more natural and spontaneous.

I thumped my hand down on the paper and looked up.

Miry-nay's Rank 1's eyes were shining.

*She took the bait.*

"I love it!"

"Me too... No, yes. I think you've already given us a pretty solid option!"

*Good.* They were being polite, perhaps, but they seemed sincere too.

It was time for the next step.

"Ah~ So both of you like this sort of thing. Then choosing the stage equipment will be easy, right? I think this would be perfect!"

Following up immediately, Lee Sejin pointed to one of the remaining pieces of stage equipment.

– Lasers

And so our plan was nearing completion without wasting any time on unnecessary rivalries.
