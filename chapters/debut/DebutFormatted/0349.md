# Chapter 349 - Turning the Tables

*So... Eugene's supposed to be on this show, right?*

Hannah Hamilton, who attended the same middle school as Cha Eugene, sat on her couch, watching TV late at night for the first time in a while.

She was using the fact that she knew Cha Eugene from school as an excuse to watch the program.

*Anyway, we met once a few years ago.*

She'd run into him at a restaurant where he was filming a TV show and selling a Korean fusion snack—*hotteok*.

– *Um... have we met before? Middle school? Elementary school?*

– *Ah... I'm Hannah Hamilton. We were in the same class in middle school.*

– *Ah~ Hannah! Nice to see you. How have you been?*

Cha Eugene still had that popular-guy vibe, but surprisingly, he'd chosen the niche genre of K-pop as his career.

After learning about his group (she knew to call it a group, not a band now!), she'd kept up with their activities from time to time.

She found it strangely charming that a boy her age with so much potential had fallen in love with such a niche, just as she had.

And despite being startled by some cultural differences, she had kept following them this far.

*A heterosexual man in his twenties with pink-dyed hair?*

*...Cha Eugene does this sort of thing too?? Anime magical girl stuff?*

*They're such a good group that really cares about each other...*

She'd even become emotionally invested in them enough to watch this American variety show as it aired.

[MESS~ MEEEESSS~]

Brightly colored, facetious text appeared on the screen. Hannah watched with a wry look on her face.

*This show's funny and all, but I don't know why you're choosing to appear on it.*

★$
– TeSTAR shouldn't be on this show!

– This is basically a third-rate reality show that tortures people desperate for fame. It's sadistic, noxious crap.

K-pop fans were loudly complaining about it on social media. She honestly sympathized with them to some extent.

**[Today's challenge is... Let's go! A dark, abandoned house... Can the contestants hold out for 30 minutes in a red coffin in an abandoned house without electricity?]**

**[And that's not all! The moment they start cowering, these adorable stuffed unicorns will fall on their heads, screaming that they're losers!]**

Though presented as a challenge, it was really just an elaborate way of mocking the contestants.

It cleverly avoided ethical concerns by filming contestants arrogantly claiming they could handle the challenges, then making fun of them when they failed.

**[Boooo! He's garbage!]**

**[Didn't he just blink like he was really scared? Yes. That's a failure, and you're a failure, too!]**

Sometimes there was no precise standard for punishment. The production team and MC simply handed out penalties whenever they thought it would be funniest.

Sometimes, even the MC himself became the target of the punishment.

That only made the show even funnier.

Even Hannah, who only occasionally watched short clips on WeTube, could clearly remember the MC's cackling voice.

*That's why the show's lasted as long as it has.*

In any case, it wasn't suitable for anyone who wanted to maintain a certain degree of dignity and grace.

That was why TeSTAR's fans in the US were so unhappy, and the production team was trying to make fun of their complaints.

★$
– We're honored. Their agency came to us *first* to tell us they wanted to be on the show. 🙄
$★

Later, even the MC joined them.

★$
– Fresh-faced, sweet little Asian boys... Hang in there 😎
$★

It was shit.

They'd very nearly started an online war.

The stubborn, conservative, and somewhat vulgar MC got into a fierce online standoff with the K-pop fandom.

However, the scary thing was that once filming started, the production team went completely silent about TeSTAR.

*TeSTAR must've done all right. Or maybe... they were completely humiliated.*

...If it was the latter, that would be really messed up.

That nasty MC must've gone quiet because things had gotten so out of hand that it couldn't be considered a joke anymore.

"Hmm..."

Hannah swallowed nervously and turned her attention back to the TV.

The contestant who burst out of the coffin was filmed sprinting as hard as he could, with glittering confetti and unicorns trailing behind him.

**[Loser~~]**

**[Actor Antonio Soltre is a cute little scaredy-cat—or should I say scaredy-bear?]**

She hoped TeSTAR wouldn't have to go through something like that.

Still, the segment TeSTAR was appearing in was fortunately meant for singers.

**[And next up... My Own Street Karaoke!]**

The 'Street Karaoke' challenge corner.

**[The crew has carefully selected all kinds of famous songs, rearranged in various styles!]**

**[Can the artists taking on this challenge really do justice to these songs?]**

At first glance, it might have seemed more like a professional segment than an absurd challenge, but...

Not at all.

Viewers would soon realize that the arrangements were part of a downright ridiculous prank.

Besides, everything was done on the fly, and naturally, it was up to the production team to decide whether a song had been sung properly.

*Ugh.*

Because the contestants were professional singers, the viewers would find it even more entertaining, while the contestants themselves would find it even more nerve-racking.

Besides, the penalty this time was terrible.

**[Today's penalty is... a Food Cannon!]**

**[We've carefully prepared only the hottest foods from various social media platforms! This isn't really even a penalty, is it?]**

*Puck!*

"Yuck!"

She watched a ground-up hamburger explode like a bomb against a dazed contestant's face.

She immediately felt bad for almost laughing.

*That's... That's so humiliating!*

Wasn't this just like watching someone get bullied at school?

Nonetheless, the challenge started on a cheerful note.

**[The first song is ~ 'Counting Ma Green!']**

It was supposed to be a strong, dark hip-hop song.

However, it was arranged as an amusement park march with exaggerated pronunciation that was difficult to understand.

*Puck.*

*Pupupupuck.*

It was a spectacle.

"Pooh!" Hannah stifled the urge to laugh.

One contestant stood dazed as the instrumental break passed and got hit, while another tried to start a verse only to miss the beat entirely.

Others sang a few lines properly and got their hopes up, only to abruptly have food flung into their faces.

[Cast: What is this shit—]

*Puck!* Food flew through the air again.

**[I don't like the way you're moving your hand!]**

"Phehehehe!"

She laughed through pursed lips. It was a primal sort of comedy that she couldn't help but laugh at.

"Oh, please! No! This is evil!"

The problem was that TeSTAR still hadn't appeared even after two or three songs had passed.

She knew she'd have recognized them even if they'd appeared for just one second. How could she miss seeing food thrown in their faces?

*Don't tell me they've been edited out?*

Fortunately—or unfortunately—that wasn't the case.

TeSTAR finally appeared after ten minutes and a mid-show ad break.

Seven young men stood on the street, just as the other contestants had.

A faint spotlight shone down on them.

**[That's right. We're giving them preferential treatment because they were in Cosmic Gunner. I know the director, you know?]**

With the crazy narration of the MC, the first song, a sunny march, appeared again.

"Oh..."

Now TeSTAR were going to be hit with all kinds of food, too.

Hannah cringed, curling up as she watched TV.

Cha Eugene, who had been staring sullenly at the microphone fixed in front of the camera with his hands in his jumper pockets, suddenly smiled.

He immediately launched into the intro of the marching song.

"Oh my god! Ha ha ha!"

**[Well... All right. Got lucky this time, eh?]**

The MC sounded put out because he'd failed to hand out any penalties this time. The sound of the passersby in the audience laughing hysterically behind his voice made his reaction even funnier.

However, TeSTAR wasn't done yet.

**[Next song!]**

Next, a gentle, trendy R&B band song played...

Instead, it had been rearranged as extremely high-pitched heavy metal.

"Yikes!"

How many people had been so shocked by the raised key that they'd ended up with food in their faces before they could even attempt to sing?

But this time, Park Moondae, the white-haired member, casually stepped up to the microphone in front of the camera.

And he opened his mouth.

*[Cast: YEAAAAAAAAAAAAH!]*

His neck was red and the veins were protruding, but his expressionless face and the impact of those extremely high notes made it hard to notice.

The other members then started headbanging behind him.

*Oh oh oh!*

They waved their jackets about in an exaggerated way, making the performance even more visually entertaining. The audience's cheers and screams rang out from the screen.

**[...Again?]**

**[Yeah, well, you're pretty amazing. I'll clap along, too. Clap clap clap. You heard me, see? I clapped.]**

The audience laughed again.

But the MC seemed to recover from his disappointment quickly.

**[But we still have our next song... As you all know, it's the mess-ups toward the end that are the funniest!]**

"Oh no!"

And a song that hadn't appeared before began to play.

A recent disco song with a catchy beat that was currently high on the Billboard charts...

"Pop!"

**[Haha! Ever heard of Tchaikovsky's 'The Nutcracker', you suckers?]**

*Classical music has no lyrics! This has no singing part at all!*

It was already ridiculously funny, but it was funnier precisely because there was no way for TeSTAR to escape this one!

**[Haha!]**

A pretty young man with black hair styled back off his forehead. He stood naturally with his legs crossed, then gracefully kicked one leg into the air and spun.

The segment had been around for more than a hundred episodes, so there were plenty of references for us to study, and it was easy to guess which songs they'd use.

We also had statistics to help us predict the arrangements.

1. Types of arrangements used on the program over the past three years, ranked by frequency.

2. Give more weight to arrangements newly introduced in the past year.

3. Exclude arrangements used in the latest three episodes.
