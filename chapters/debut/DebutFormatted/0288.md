# Chapter 288 - A Battle for the Throne, IV

TeSTAR's music video started with a blue sea just like the thumbnail.

However, the point of view was different.

It wasn't outside the sea, but *within* it.

As if continuing where the teaser left off, the view rose from the depths, and the water was bright blue, probably close to the surface.

*Ah, that's why the teaser...?*

People were beginning to realize it.

Then the camera broke through the glittering surface of the water.

[Ahyeon-ah!]

Someone was calling out. When the camera turned, a person was running toward it from the sandy beach where waves were crashing against the shore.

It was Lee Sejin in a T-shirt and shorts, smiling.

[Here!]

Soon, Lee Sejin was waist-deep in the sea, holding out his hand.

At first, the story followed the members, each shown in a different cut, as they planned to gather together and head to the sea.

It was a classic summer song concept.

*It's typical, but there's a reason it works.*

The office worker, who was a fansite master for both Lee Sejin and Park Moondae, had been anxiously waiting for the video. She was a little disappointed by the cliché, but also relieved.

And the guys who arrived at the beach ran out of the bus and met Seon Ahyeon, and the chorus came in again.

The signature choreography was shown off on the beach, where they danced barefoot in white T-shirts and jeans. It was a cool, refreshing, nimble dance incorporating popping and locking.

It was comfortable to listen to and fun to look at.

*It's a real summer song.*

Everyone who watched it nodded along to the beat.

The members on the screen had the same hair colors as at the time of their debut.

The choice evoked a strange nostalgia and a wistful appreciation for summer.

Besides, the light clothes and the beach backdrop seemed to satisfy those who wanted something sexy.

*A healthy, refreshing kind of sexy, I guess.*

It felt like an advertisement for a sports drink, and this time the office worker nodded her head in satisfaction.

And on the screen, the sun was already setting.

Shots of the members dancing were intercut with shots of them sitting around a campfire on the beach.

As the catchy rap section ended on an offbeat, the instrumentals fell silent.

And the sounds accompanying the footage, rather than the music, echoed through the speakers.

[...]

The sound of the fire crackling and sparks popping. The sound of the sea. The sound of cloth rustling.

TeSTAR, sitting around the campfire, smiled and exchanged glances, then looked around to make sure no one was watching.

And...

[Yaaaa~!]

***[Wheeik!]***

They jumped into the sea.

"...?"

It was rather sudden, and they seemed particularly eager.

*Plop.*

Laughter filled the screen with the sound of water splashing.

*What?*

The office worker was about to be disappointed, wondering if this awkward scene had been shoehorned in to force a touching moment.

Just as Seon Ahyeon, smiling as he swam, lay back as if doing the backstroke, something light purple beneath the surface sent bubbles rising to the top.

It split into two tips...

"...!!"

The live chat was in an uproar, and the office worker glanced at it before looking back at the screen.

A shape of another color appeared beneath the surface for a moment, then vanished...

*Mermaids?*

"Ah."

In the darkening sea, the scales glistened and reflected the sunset light.

The members slowly swam farther out into deeper water. Their arms splashed water on each other, and their tails splashed on the surface.

*[Haha!]*

The colors and camera work made it feel more like an independent art film than a Hollywood children's movie, giving viewers the impression they were watching a kind of human documentary.

And the moment they suddenly dived into the sea...

*[Ohhh~]*

There were strange audio vibrations as the video briefly dipped into fantasy.

Quick cuts of shimmering tails flashed across the screen alongside their hair, which changed color the moment they entered the water.

Schools of small fish and seaweed drifted by in the clear sea.

It was a mesmerizing sight.

*Splash!*

And out of the water again.

Returning to reality, the dark beach came back into view, and the soaked members laughed and pointed to the sky.

*Ping!*

Fireworks exploded.

Yellow fireworks burst into the deep purple sky, the sun all but set.

The members cheered as they floated in the sea.

It was at that time that the song returned.

They were dressed more like they were about to perform on stage than before.

The members filled the final chorus with a splendid group dance on the beach.

The cloth accessories on their robes and their bracelets reflected the lights from the string of mini-bulbs on the beach.

It was a gorgeous ending.

[...]

The music video ended with Seon Ahyeon placing a group photo into a suitcase after the summer had ended, then leaving the sea with a smile.

However, the office worker remained frozen in her seat.

"......"

The office worker first tried to predict the results—comparing VTIC and performance trends, imagining fans' reactions to the performances, and more—but instinct prevailed.

She replayed the video, feeling a little dazed.

*What... It seems there was a lot of foreshadowing.*

She put her worries aside and simply watched the video again, almost by reflex.

In the meantime, her social media timeline was abuzz.
