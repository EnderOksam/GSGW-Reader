# Chapter 256 - Negotiating Independence

On the evening of the last day of our Chuseok holiday, back at the dorm...

The members started to return one by one.

Actually, I thought Bae Sejin and I would be the first to arrive, but there was someone who came first.

"Uh?"

"Oh, you're here! Welcome, Welcome! Sejin-hyung too~"

Lee Sejin, whose parents' home was close to the dorm, had returned early and was lying on the sofa in the living room. He was watching the commentary version of the special episode of ⟨TeSTAR's Island Life⟩.

From what I had heard, it seemed that he enjoyed a top-tier idol's holiday by giving autographs to his excited cousins.

It wouldn't be long before eyewitness accounts and photos of his autographs posted by his relatives would flood social media.

"How's my cool-headed Moondaemoondae doing? My mom was so disappointed she didn't get to see you in person!"

Big talk from the guy who went as far as visiting his grandfather's brother this holiday.

"Okay. I'll have to give her a video call sometime."

"Haha!"

Lee Sejin chuckled.

I didn't know if it was because he'd had a good rest or because seeing how popular we were in real life had put him in a good mood, but it was nice that he seemed more relaxed.

It meant he'd have more energy to spare when we needed to squeeze out every last drop working on the album.

"Ah, many of you have already come."

"H-hey everyone...!"

Ryu Chungwoo and Seon Ahyeon arrived almost at the same time. Seon Ahyeon said that he went on a family trip again, and he even brought souvenirs from a small island in Namhae.

I accepted the seagull-shaped bread that Seon Ahyeon offered. Inside was green tea cream.

"I-it was near the island we went to! M-my parents said they wanted to go there..."

*After seeing all the trouble we went through...?*

I didn't know what to make of that. Apparently, they'd had fun, though, so I decided not to comment.

"Did you spend your holiday comfortably?"

And Kim Raebin had come back with a bundle of things... He looked a little chubbier.

From his grandmother's hospitalization to his own car accident, he'd been through a lot this year. She seemed to have fed her grandson plenty to make up for it.

"Hyungs?"

"...Well, welcome."

"Yes!"

Well, it would be fine because we were inactive. It wasn't a health problem... If we prepared an album, he would automatically have no choice but to return to how he was originally.

Besides, it turned out in the early hours of the morning that this was the least of our worries.

This was because the last member to return had undergone the most shocking transformation.

"Hurray! [Everybody say hello~ like] Hello! How's it going?"

"Cha Eugene!!"

It was Cha Eugene, who'd arrived early in the morning on a late-night flight from Los Angeles.

This guy... His skin was mottled.

In a good way, he looked healthy, and in a bad way, he clearly hadn't worn enough sunscreen.

It must have been an amazing holiday at the beach.

*The stylists will be in a mess...*

Wouldn't they lock him indoors and make him take vitamin D pills instead?

"Hey! You! What's up with your face!"

First of all, Kim Raebin was startled and slapped Cha Eugene on the back.

"Ouch! There's nothing wrong with my face! I'm fit and healthy!"

"No, you're not! This isn't a healthy tan, it's an uneven tan from not taking care of your skin!"

"Kim Raebin didn't exercise either! A failure!"

"...! N-no, I'll soon recover my muscle loss through exercise..."

Anyway, after the fuss was over, I was able to get to the point.

It was 2:00 in the morning.

"Tomorrow... No, I'm thinking of visiting the agency around lunchtime today."

"Huh?"

"Why, why...?"

"Moondae, you are too much of a workaholic. Get some rest. You worked at Sejin-hyung's house too, right?"

"No, just listen, please."

If I let them interrupt, I'd lose my train of thought.

I pressed my forehead and spoke out.

"I've been thinking that I don't want the material we collected for our lawsuit—especially the stuff Bae Sejin-hyung put together—to go to waste..."

I put into words the plans I had leisurely laid out during the holidays.

"Hmm."

Bae Sejin, who had already heard it before we'd returned to the dorm, nodded without any reaction, while the others looked surprised, then conflicted.

"I-is that possible...?"

"...It's possible. I asked the lawyer."

Following Bae Sejin's answer, Kim Raebin raised his hand.

"Is it illegal?"

"No, it's not."

After that, a fairly productive discussion and Q&A ensued among the seven of us.

In the end, Lee Sejin shrugged.

"Well, anyway, we've got everything we wanted covered, right? It looks like it will work."

"Okay. Then, let's all go together tomorrow and talk about it."

I shook my head.

"No, I think it would be better for me to go alone."

"What?"

A man like him would find a secretive one-on-one meeting more intimidating. If a group of young people stormed his office, he'd just think his underlings were rebelling.

One representative was adequate.

After explaining that part, I added a comment.

"I think if Chungwoo-hyung went, he'd mistake it for an official deal since he's the leader. I think it's better to catch him off guard."

"Um..."

Bae Sejin frowned and opened his mouth.

"Still, considering retaliation or threats..."

"It's okay. I have no intention of using such extreme measures."

"...?"

*What was with the looks on all their faces?*

I was taken aback for a moment, then burst out laughing.

"Ah, you meant the general manager threatening me?"

"......"

"He can't do that. He wouldn't dare."

And after a while, it was decided I'd be the one to speak to the general manager.

Things were going according to plan.

***

The third general manager of T1 Stars had a decent holiday break.

Although the fired employee's actions had given him a massive headache, the agency was already well on its way to recovering as the holiday approached.

Still, his head was pounding when he thought of the damage to the image of the company, interference from the head office, and the loss caused by the cancellation of the tour.

*It was fortunate that the kids had recovered.*

Fortunately, the member of TeSTAR who was in a coma did not die, but the way he'd acted after waking up hadn't been all that welcome.

*A donation concert after an accident like that?*

It had even been held on another agency's platform.

He was very suspicious.

*They're not trying to transfer there, are they?*

He couldn't stop it due to public opinion and the atmosphere, but the general manager had no previous experience in the entertainment industry and thought of an idol agency like a soccer player's club. The idea left a bitter taste in his mouth.

He thought TeSTAR was testing the waters before jumping ship.

However, there was no sign that TeSTAR was contacting the other agency again, so he put his doubts aside.

*I'll have to sweet-talk them into going on tour as soon as the agency isn't under so much scrutiny.*

At the same time, he planned to have them prepare their next album by the end of the tour, with a global launch in mind.

In the meantime, it was stressful to have to take the constantly complaining idols' opinions into account.

*None of them have even graduated from college. Why can't they just be obedient and do their job as the faces of our company?*

In the early days of Hollywood, didn't the industry come to this point because of star marketing made to sell tickets?

Anyway, he clicked his tongue, saying that the entertainment business had no structure or dignity.

He'd thrown himself into the job with ambition, but he was growing disenchanted with it.

*I'll spend a few more years adding this to my portfolio, then launch a business of my own elsewhere.*

Then, during lunchtime, he came to an appointment that a TeSTAR member had made in advance.

"Hello."

"Yes, welcome."

Actually, this was a bit odd. As the general manager, was he really obliged to make time for a meeting just because one of his celebrities requested it?

Still, it might give him a useful opportunity, so he was willing to hear the member out.

It was true that the company was in trouble because of the car accident.

*Let's see.*

He made an assumption by looking at Park Moondae, a member of TeSTAR who came out alone.

*He must want something from me.*

He was also informed that it was not the leader who contacted the head office last time, but it was actually him.

However, he didn't even think that the dedicated TeSTA&R Team was created because of that.

It was because it had never occurred to him that a tantara[1]{T/N: Tantara/ 딴따라: A derogatory term used to belittle celebrities/idols/entertainers.} who hadn't even graduated from high school would have the brains for that.

Still, he had an idea of how he could take advantage of Moondae.

*He's the type who can't overcome his own greed if he's encouraged.*

It was because he seemed like a guy who had the right to speak on the team and was greedy for success in his own way.

So, even after recovering from a coma, he'd come to see the general manager!

However, as soon as he sat down in the sushi restaurant room where the course meal was served, Park Moondae took out the paperwork.

"These are documents for a lawsuit."

"*Kheup!*"

The general manager choked on his sip of water.

However, the idol in front of him just stared at him indifferently.

The idol watched him with a calm, observant expression.

"......"

The general manager felt a rush of anger for a moment, but he put practical considerations first.

He examined the documents.

And he was astonished.

"...!"

"This is evidence of the agency's negligence regarding the former manager."

The paperwork was legally meticulous.

In addition, there were parts where specific details were deliberately redacted, so even if they checked this paper, it was difficult for the company to create refutation materials.

Anyone could tell that an expert was used.

*Did they hire a lawyer...?*

The general manager took a deep breath, making sure the idol couldn't see his unease, then put down the papers.

"So why are you showing this to me?"

"......"

"Looking at it, I don't think you're going to sue right away. Is this because you want something?"

Park Moondae acknowledged it.

*He's got his wits about him.*

Indeed, the general manager was neither a nepotism hire nor a relative of an executive. He was just an ego-bloated senior.

And that made things easier for Moondae.

Park Moondae remembered the conversation he had with Bae Sejin the night before they returned to the dorm.

*– ...Park Moondae, you talked about taxes when we got our first payout. You said a lot of it would be deducted in taxes later.*

*– Yes.*

*– Yes, you were right. Celebrities are basically freelancers.*

When signing his own contract, Park Moondae had already known this based on the legal knowledge he had gained through college and civil service exams.

Celebrities did not sign an employment contract with a company.

Therefore, they were classified as individual entrepreneurs rather than workers, and in many cases, the Labor Standards Act or the Occupational Safety and Health Act were not properly applied.

*That gives the agency far too much power.*

It wasn't something he could change right now, so he had to move on.

This was what Park Moondae thought.

*On the contrary, doesn't this mean that I can write the contract more freely?*

Wasn't this a contract between business owners?

So, he made this suggestion.

"We want to add some clauses to our existing contracts. Penalty and guarantee clauses."

"Guarantees, you say?"

"Yes, it's nothing serious, I'm putting it as a kind of bet. The contents are..."

Park Moondae laughed.

"If TeSTAR wins the grand prize at one of the annual awards ceremonies within the next year, you will have to fully support TeSTAR's establishment of a new independent label."

"...!!"

"The timeframe is two years... Oh, of course, the label will remain under T1. We're not looking to breach our contracts. We'll have to stay under T1."

The paper that Park Moondae gave out was full of details.

!pb
@ll@– 'Annual awards ceremonies' refers to awards ceremonies hosted by an album or digital music distribution platform, at which that year's album sales account for 70% or more of the criteria for the daesang.@ll@

@ll@– ... During this period of verification, A will guarantee B's independent and safe activity. In this provision, 'independent' refers to...@ll@
pb!

In a word, the group would handle its own activities without interference from the agency. The agency's only role would be to take proper care of them.

And Park Moondae knew.

*This bastard has no connections in the entertainment industry.*

What he had was a personal connection with the board of directors at T1 Headquarters, enough to fill the position here. But he wouldn't be able to talk about this to his superiors because of reputation issues.

*Then he'll just have to handle it himself.*

No, actually, it didn't matter if he talked.

*We have no intention of antagonizing T1. He'd just be reaffirming that we want to distance ourselves from this agency where he's the general manager.*

It was a logic that was already used when requesting a dedicated team. Even if the deal was discovered, there was no reason for the headquarters to be hostile to TeSTAR.

At that time, the general manager finished his own calculations similarly.

Knowing that if he shouted or came out forcefully, the bomb would explode.

So, he clasped his hands together and spoke more seriously.

"I understand the artist's opinion, but... It's not something I can easily say 'OK' to, right? I'm just the person in charge... I have to tell the board of directors and the CEO."

It was a lie. In fact, the CEO position only existed to give a fancy business card to a relative or acquaintance of someone important at T1.

In fact, the general manager was the one in charge of discussing contract revisions.

"Oh, I don't know much about that."

Park Moondae did not blink an eye.

"I just need you to convince them."

"...! What..."

"Well... I don't know how the agency works internally. If this doesn't work out the way we want, I think it'd be easier for us to just sue. Our lawyer will take care of that."

*Was he crazy?*

The general manager was speechless and looked at Park Moondae, but there was no agitation on his smooth face.

So he knew too.

This guy was really going to file a lawsuit without batting an eye.

And this crazy guy couldn't be persuaded.

"In a little while, you will do another new season of ⟨Idol Inc.⟩, and the people who will debut there will also belong here. As for us, our contract period is going to run out soon, so... A label would be fine. Right?"

"......"

"All you have to do is set up a label for us if we win the grand prize. It doesn't seem difficult."

And at sunset that day...

Park Moondae headed back to the dorm with all 7 modified, signed and stamped contracts.

*That went pretty well.*

He knew it would work if he backed the general manager into a corner without giving him time to think.

The general manager would've done something troublesome if he'd given him another day or two to think it over.

*The correct answer is to deal with it quickly.*

And regardless of the additional clauses, TeSTAR's performance as a group was tied to the general manager's own career. He wouldn't be able to sabotage them and stop them from winning the grand prize.

That general manager was a guy who was incredibly greedy for glory.

In addition, he'd also included two compensation clauses as a precaution in case of a breach of contract.

Park Moondae shrugged.

*I couldn't set a legal precedent the way Bae Sejin wanted... I hope he'll be satisfied with this.*

Anyway, they'd be free to do as they pleased until the end of next year—and perhaps even afterward, if they won the grand prize.

What if they couldn't win the grand prize?

*It doesn't matter because the contract period isn't long anyway.*

He shrugged.

Either way, there was nothing to lose.

"Time to start preparing our next album."

He was ready to devote every bit of his time to it.

*We're going to go all out this time.*

...That was how the extremely high-intensity album preparation camp began.

For reference, the first comeback stage planned by the agency was the year-end awards ceremony hosted by T1.
