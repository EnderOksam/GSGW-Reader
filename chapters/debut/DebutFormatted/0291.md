I felt the rush myself.

*Nothing gets your blood pumping like seeing an investment pay off in a big way.*

The song above ours had been made popular by a drama, and the top song was an indie track that had been popular for a long time.

In fact, it meant we had the most successful easy-listening song aimed at summer.

*We did a great job with the build-up.*

The song had been completed two months before the comeback, and it had been almost blissful watching every piece fall into place like a jigsaw puzzle since then.

Buzz. And more buzz.

I looked back on the past week.

The heavy rain stage, which drew half controversy and half admiration, had swept social media, and thanks to the just-in-time explanation, it had taken on a positive image without losing any momentum.

*People become more enthusiastic when a restriction is put in place and then lifted than when they're simply free to enjoy something from the start.*

After that, as expected, people who'd seen this rain performance immediately started listening to the song on streaming platforms.

Once again, the virtuous cycle between the music and the stage was complete, and it was much greater this time.

The song itself and the stage itself had become a trend on social media.

*It's a kind of flow.*

Our promotions had been cut short by the car accident, but it was similar to the trend we'd noticed during the 'Nightmare' promotions.

Its influence extended beyond TeSTAR's top-tier reputation.

To the point where people on the internet who said 'I can't feel it' were being mocked to no end.

"It's all thanks to you, Raebin. You made such a good song," Ryu Chungwoo said.

"You're too kind! It was a collaborative effort, and the song wouldn't have been possible without the hyungs' feedback and Moondae-hyung's ideas, so I'll remember that and stay humble!"

Kim Raebin barely paused for breath. Anyone who saw him would think he was receiving an award.

Meanwhile, Cha Eugene spoke gravely.

"Kim Raebin made the music, but Sejin-hyung and I made the cool choreo. Kim Raebin didn't do it alone!"

"That's what I just said, fool!"

"Me too, idiot!"

*Look at these two.* I said a word to the Sejin they'd just mentioned.

"Must be nice."

"Huh? ...Of course it's nice~ People really like the song."

Surprisingly, Lee Sejin seemed a little embarrassed.

"How should I put it... It's kind of hard to believe it was such a success."

"......"

*No, maybe he was just moved.*

He was a guy who had almost no music-related solo activities. It must have been quite something for him to see the choreography he'd personally chosen and arranged become so popular.

Lee Sejin looked at the ceiling once, took a deep breath, and raised both arms.

"Ah~ I don't know! It's nice! Thanks! Thank you, our members~"

"Yeah!!"

Our success was having a positive effect on the general mood. Even Bae Sejin was red in the face and applauding Lee Sejin for our new music chart ranking, which was fun to watch.

"Ah, looks like TeSTAR showed everyone what we're made of this time too~" Lee Sejin said.

Even though it was 1:00 in the morning and the next schedule was at 4:00, no one seemed all that worried.

Judging by experience, it would be fine. They weren't the type to slack off at work...

*We can't let our guard down just yet.*

It was difficult to say we'd surpassed VTIC in terms of name value alone. Besides, the gap in first-week sales wasn't going anywhere.

Of course, this time we... We'd sold exceptionally well, though.

We'd sold 1.55 million copies in the first week.

To be honest, my head was a mess because I hadn't expected it to go this well.

Just looking at it, it was a staggering number. Excluding VTIC, there were only about three active idol groups that had ever sold this many copies.

*And we're still going strong.*

I checked how many albums had been sold since the first week ended and shrugged.

*This momentum should last us at least a month.*

I was almost certain that this momentum would continue.

It didn't seem like other groups aiming for first place on music shows would enter until mid-August.

*I'd do the same in their shoes.*

In a situation where the two top groups were splitting the audience share as if their lives depended on it, what would anyone else gain by stepping in now?

TeSTAR was no longer struggling to hold on; we'd really found our footing. It would take quite a while for both of us to slow down.

The VTIC fans seemed to know that too, since they'd changed their stance a bit.

It was a gesture of truce.

The moment TeSTAR's song established itself as a trend, it seemed they'd decided that fighting us would do them no good.

Instead, they wanted to make sure VTIC's top-tier position remained unchanged and avoid criticism that would only stress them out.

*They've obviously been at this for a long time.*

Of course, behind the scenes, they'd grind their teeth and try to take TeSTAR down by spreading malicious rumors... But that was what I'd expected.

Since the song had done so well, the fans... Our fans would be having much more fun than stress right about now.

As the group challenging the top and climbing our way up, we'd essentially won.

*We did it.*

I locked my smartphone.

...Maybe it was time to savor the success of our adventurous challenge a little more.

"Come here, Moondae! You should join in too!"

*Haha.*

I got up from my seat. It was funny watching the excited guys loudly encourage each other.

I went over and joined in for a bit, and we wrapped our arms around each other's shoulders. It was a pretty satisfying moment.

*They'd obviously been given the most convenient time slot.*

But... I didn't see why they'd made us share the same waiting room, even if it was the largest and best-equipped one.
