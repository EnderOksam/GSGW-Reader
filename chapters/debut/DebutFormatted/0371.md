# Chapter 371 - Reassembling TeSTAR, I

Cha Eugene.

A center with 'Trait: Black Hole (S)'.

With a crazy combination of conditions optimized for idols, from skills to mentality, he had Triple A stats at a young age. He wouldn't even go to the military, so there'd be no break in his career.[1]{T/N: Since Cha Eugene is an American citizen, he doesn't have to serve in the military.}

No wonder the system gave this guy 5 stars.

But... there was something else bothering me about drawing him.

"Yeah, he doesn't know."

This guy... He didn't know a thing about how I was living as another person, how I'd returned to the past, or the status window. He knew nothing.

And he was the hardest person to explain all of this to.

– *OK. So, we used to know each other, but you supposedly went back in time and became a different person?*

– *Should I call 911?*

*I'm doomed.*

I groaned and rubbed my face.

[Cha Eugene: Sleeping soundly (―p―)zZ]

I wasn't sure what to make of it yet, but it might be useful for gathering information. It was a bit shabby, though.

...It was nice to see Cha Eugene's name. It had been a while since I'd seen him. Anyway, it seemed like everyone else was in this world too.

"Hoo."

I sighed and checked my remaining experience points. *2000, huh?*

*Don't tell me there's a chance I'll fail at leveling up too?*

Not even ⟨Section 127⟩ had gone that far. Though honestly, I'd already lost hope when the star-ranking system appeared.

*If it's an enhancement feature, there's a chance it can fail.*

*And if I fail, the points will just evaporate.*

"Is there something like a probability table here?"

So I could see the chances of failure, the chances of drawing a five-star, and other possibilities.

Shouldn't I at least know the expected value and calculate it?

$p
**[Wait a minute!]**
p$

Keundal's pop-up disappeared in a hurry, then slowly reappeared as if reluctant to come back.

$p
**[That kind of thing... There isn't anything like that.]**
p$

"......"

*It didn't even meet my lowest expectations...*

Well, in the first place, the Companion Recruitment banner hadn't provided any information earlier.

I wasn't even angry anymore. I'd be the idiot for expecting any decency from this crazy system. I looked at the ceiling of the room and held back a sigh.

Then I noticed a mail icon in the top corner of the status window.

*Wait. Are those announcements?*

*Click.*

\

I stared.

There was no gold currency in this status window.

In other words, it was completely fake. I figured it had been created when the system copied the look of ⟨Section 127⟩.

*You bastard... Some consolation this is.*

I looked away because I felt like my blood vessels would burst if I continued. I kept thinking and got back to business.

*Awaken companion!*

★-
\
[Awaken the companion Cha Eugene?]

-#Uses 1000 EXP#-

Then, a glow appeared around Cha Eugene's pixel-art image.

★-

Unlocking potential...
-★

Fireworks exploded around Cha Eugene's profile.

★-

#><#y [First awakening successful!] y#><#

I clenched my fists. However, it was not because of the thrill of success.

*So what happens now?*

I needed to check how effective the condition I'd added to the system really was. There was no indication of anything, however, other than the emoticons beside their names.

*So what am I supposed to do with the companions?*

It was then.

Another pop-up quietly appeared.

★-

*Wait, that's a real phone number.*

No, if I looked closely... This even had an area code in the United States.

San Diego, California, USA.

*Damn it.*

I punched the pillow on my bed.

*Cha Eugene, are you in the US?*

*Is that why your status said you were sleeping?!*

***

Cha Eugene had a dream.

In the dream, he left his hometown and took a job in his ancestral homeland.

– *Take your STAR. Hello, we are TeSTAR!*

He was a K-pop idol!

He'd never imagined that his life would take this direction.

Everything had been decided in an instant after he impulsively accepted the scouting offer that suddenly came in after the semester ended.

There was one reason he agreed to go.

*It sounded fun!*

And after all, he debuted in a good group...

– Eugene is great at this sort of thing.

They were good members.

Not everything that happened was good, but things tended to end well.

It was a thrilling, dynamic life!

Then, he suddenly realized something.

*This isn't a dream.*

Cha Eugene opened his eyes.

And at that moment, he realized he'd remembered the past, not just had a dream.

"Phew."

It was 6 am.

He stretched out his arm and silenced the alarm that had not yet gone off.

Then he walked out to the kitchen, drank some water, and reflected on what had happened.

*Ah.*

Having undyed hair felt somehow awkward.

*So... who am I?*

*I'm Cha Eugene, 23, a member of the K-pop group TeSTAR.*

However, Cha Eugene's current body was living a completely different life. He went to the local high school, where he was on the football team...

He didn't know much about K-pop. Until just yesterday.

*Wait a minute, was that really yesterday?*

He couldn't figure out what was going on. If he remembered correctly, he'd gone over to a friend's house during his first break in a long time...

*Am I dreaming right now?*

He punched himself in the face without hesitation.

*Slap.*

"Ow."

It hurt so bad.

He scolded himself for testing reality that way, then realized why he was doing so.

He was worried he'd injure his face, which couldn't happen if he was going to perform onstage!

"Hmm."

He put down his glass of water and shrugged.

*I'm an idol.*

He knew at least that much was true.

All the knowledge, information, and experience he remembered couldn't be fake. They were an elaborate, realistic set of memories that no lunatic could simply make up.

*If anything, the life I'm living now is more suspicious.*

He rubbed his chin. Compared to his actual life, wasn't life at this local high school too dull?

It was no different from middle school.

*Well, anyway.*

First, he'd try contacting the other TeSTAR members by phone...

*...I don't know their numbers?*

Well, who in this world memorized other people's numbers these days?

He shrugged his shoulders and tried to think of another way to contact them. But before that, a notification appeared on his smartphone.

*Ding dong.*

It was a message from the Facelook account.

And it was all in Korean.

★:
-Apple: Hello.

-I'm Park Moondae. If you remember me, please reply.
:★

The message had a familiar tone.

"Oh my god."

Cha Eugene laughed.

How did Moondae always show up at the perfect time?

He tapped the screen with one hand.

★:
I'm in America lol-

I'm going to Korea tomorrow!-
:★

It seemed that using up his savings on airfare was a constant in both worlds. Cha Eugene giggled.

***

"...So, we're waiting for a member of your former group who's coming from the United States?"

"That's right."

I nodded.

I wasn't very happy about him calling TeSTAR my former group, but there was no need to be rude to the guy who followed me to the airport.

"It's an unusual variable that past memories can come back at random times. I'm not so sure you need it, though."

Cheongryeo was doing something on his smartphone with a bored expression on his face.

That bastard still seemed to be acting under the complete assumption that this was reality.

*...I understand.*

He'd probably been through this too many times to count.

I'd give this guy a moment... I was thinking of bringing up the story about his dog, but I stopped.

*I have to tell him about it only when I've found a way back.*

If I brought it up now, wouldn't it just push him further over the edge? I held back a sigh and held up a placard.

$p
#><$tf [Welcome, TeSTAR's Cha Eugene] tf$><#
p$

...It was embarrassing, but there was nothing I could do because my face was different from before.

*It was fortunate that I was able to find his Facelook account using the phone number.*

And fortunately, before long, one guy passed the gate and ran out excitedly.

A guy wearing a padded jacket that looked like a ski uniform and swinging a suitcase with one hand.

"Hyung!"

Cha Eugene, who looked like an ordinary person.

I was at a loss for a moment, frozen and staring at him... *Wait, how old was that guy?*

The pale, young-looking Cha Eugene stopped short and frowned.

"Moondae-hyung?"

"Yes. It's me."

*I had to come to my senses.*

No matter how old this bastard was, I'd first have to explain why I was in a different body before we could proceed.

But before I could open my mouth, that guy opened it first.

"You look like Moondae-hyung, but you're a different person. Did you get plastic surgery?"

"...Something like that."

"Oh..."

"Ahem."

I heard the sound of that bastard Cheongryeo snickering. I massaged my brow and muttered.

"Anyway... Test me first. I can give you our dorm key code if necessary."

"There is no need for that. I know it's hyung!"

"...Really?"

"Yes." Suddenly switching to English, he added, [Only Moondae-hyung would say something like that.]

Cha Eugene smiled.

"I know it's Moondae-hyung. So please explain what's going on!"

"...Okay."

Perhaps because he was younger now, he'd reverted to some of the simpler Korean expressions he'd used during ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ We left the airport together.

And in a private game room in Seoul where a group could play console games together...

"...That's how it happened."

I somehow managed to get through the necessary but absurd explanation in just one hour.

I was breaking into a cold sweat.

*Just summarizing all this is mortifying...*

I gulped down a few mouthfuls of coffee to stop my mouth from twitching. I had no idea what Cha Eugene would say.

But the guy nodded obediently.

[So, this is a kind of parallel dimension, a multiverse. Yes?] he asked in English.

"...??"

*Cha Eugene... Did he understand this?*

*And he was using those terms?*

The guy shrugged his shoulders.

[Plus, there's an alien mind parasite involved... Limestone movies love that kind of thing. I know all about this stuff.]

I got the sense that he wasn't particularly into those kinds of stories himself, but I was just grateful he'd accepted my explanation. *Who knew Limestone would be so helpful?*

Of course, this was possible because he could see the evidence with his own eyes.

Because his life had changed.

[Anyway, I didn't think this place was real in the first place.] he continued in English.

Cha Eugene, who'd told me that he'd recovered all his memories, shook his head as he gulped down the last of his hot chocolate.

[I'm so bored. What can I say... It's like watching a rerun of a show I've already seen.]

*Really?*

[And there's no way I could be a quarterback candidate this easily. It's a world for those 6'3" and above.]

Cha Eugene grumbled, saying that for someone only 6 feet tall like him, there should have been more adversity and mountains to jump over.

I agreed.

"It feels like everything was put together too neatly to fit the system's conditions. Even our ages don't match."

"Right. Cheongryeo-sunbaenim used to be much older."

Cha Eugene pointed to Cheongryeo quite politely.

"......"

*That guy... He's lived much longer than Cha Eugene thinks.*

Anyway, Cheongryeo seemed unconcerned as he did something on his smartphone.

"Are you busy right now?"

"A little. We need to start adjusting our debut schedule little by little."

*You can do that?*

At that time, Cha Eugene suddenly intervened in Korean.

"Debut?"

"It looks like I have to become famous to give the other members their memories back. Everything here requires experience points."

"Uhm..."

A look of confusion passed over Cha Eugene's face for a moment, but he soon understood.

"OK, I got it."

*Got what?*

"I'm debuting too!"

Cha Eugene grinned.

"If I'm there, the group will become famous faster."

"...!"

It was an arrogantly confident statement, but I couldn't refute it.

"And then we can become TeSTAR again faster! Let's gather everyone and do something!"

"...Do what?"

Cha Eugene grinned.

[Anything! We have to find a way back. Back to being TeSTAR!] he said in English.

*This bastard...*

He was supposed to be 17, but right now he felt quite trustworthy.

But it seemed like the other guy didn't feel the same way.

"Hmm."

Cheongryeo looked Cha Eugene up and down as if assessing livestock, then came to a short conclusion.

"I don't think it will work."

"...!"

However, Cha Eugene didn't seem fazed at all.

"Everyone has the opportunity to challenge themselves. The group hasn't debuted yet. So it's not your group, sunbaenim," he said in Korean.

He was spitting facts now.

However, Cheongryeo wasn't impressed.

"There seems to be a misunderstanding."

Cheongryeo grinned.

"It's not me, but the company that won't accept you, Eugene."

"...!?"

And it was said like a death sentence.

"Because you don't have a look that LeTi prefers."

"......"

"......"

I thought about the current debut group, Sino, Judan, and Cheongryeo. Then I thought about my current appearance.

...Didn't we all look somewhat gloomy and cold?

Then I glanced at the 17-year-old Cha Eugene sitting in front of me.

His eyes were bright and radiant.

*Hmm.*

*Sure enough... he'd stick out like a sore thumb here.*

"But I'm really good at it. Doesn't that help?"

"Well. Even if you ask me... I'm not the owner of the company."

Cheongryeo grinned.

"Just like you said."

*Ah, this guy's pissed.*

To be honest, with Cha Eugene's abilities, even if they didn't debut him right away, any agency with some sense would find a way to keep him around for something else.

*LeTi will do the same.*

He had to know that too. Judging by the way he was talking, he seemed to be trying to get rid of Eugene personally.

Cha Eugene stared at Cheongryeo, then nodded without looking the least bit disappointed.

"Okay. Then Moondae-hyung, you should leave. Let's find a new agency together!"

"Wait a minute."

It wasn't like agencies debuted new groups every quarter, and if I did that, there was no telling when I'd be able to debut again.

*This is driving me crazy.*

I massaged my brow. Cheongryeo spoke without once losing his smile.

"So he says."

Cha Eugene looked at Cheongryeo in silence for a moment, then spoke quite seriously in English.

[I don't know why you're being so hostile, but don't you want to go back? You already have your own group in the real world. Don't you miss them?]

"......"

*Should I have stopped these two from talking to each other?*

[We can find a way to go back together, so I don't understand why you'd refuse.]

Cheongryeo looked at Cha Eugene without an expression. I tensed, ready to move if necessary.

*Now... Isn't it like picking up an unexploded bomb with an iron skewer?*

But after a moment, Cheongryeo answered calmly.

"Is that so?"

"...!"

"I suppose you're right. Thinking about it... maybe I've been too complacent."

Cheongryeo's lips twisted into a smirk.

"It's not like I have a mission to accomplish or anything! Haha!"

*Oh, fuck...*

Just as I was about to get up, Cheongryeo stopped laughing.

"So let's go with the logic of survival of the fittest."

*What?*

Cheongryeo laughed.

"Come to think of it, I don't think I've ever mentioned this before... Do you know how many people my group originally had?"

*Five people.*

But that didn't seem to be what this guy meant.

This guy meant... the original VTIC, before he'd changed its lineup through his many resets.

"Nine people."

"...!"

"Quite a lot, right? Haha. The current debut group has seven members, and two more were added... Where do you think those two people suddenly appeared from?"

"......"

He was asking why there had suddenly been a change in the team at the last minute...

*Let's consider the timing.*

I answered reflexively.

"The survival program."

"That's right."

Cheongryeo picked up his smartphone again.

"LeTi partnered with Tnet... The agency originally had an in-house survival program."

Although survival shows helped form a fandom in the beginning, the trend had lost a lot of momentum by the time TeSTAR was active, with more engaging idol survival shows being produced left and right.

But at this point, they were still popular.

"But people's minds are very difficult to control. Right? The more people there are, the more variables you have to deal with."

Cheongryeo's eyes narrowed.

"So after a certain point, I started getting the show cancelled altogether..."

The guy put down his smartphone and spoke lightly.

"I'll just leave it alone this time."

"...!"

"Whoever it is, only those who survive the show will debut."

"But..."

"It's reasonable, right? It's efficient."

I swallowed. *Shit.*

At first glance, Cheongryeo's words made it sound as if he were giving up all influence over the group. As if he were offering to debut with whoever survived the show.

But actually, no.

*The influence this guy has on his agency is ridiculous.*

I didn't know whether he had some kind of leverage over the agency, remembered and exploited every butterfly effect, or both. Still, one thing was certain: he had a lot of influence.

There was no way this guy would let the survival show run its course without interfering.

*That's crazy to begin with.*

I was sure this guy had already seen many times over what would happen if he left that survival show alone.

*The only logical thing to do would be to select people in advance.*

And this time, he'd be the one selecting every single member. He already had the best of the best from VTIC in mind... as well as me, to replace the main vocalist.

In short... That was just a declaration saying, 'I'll make sure you're torn to pieces before you ever make it to debut, Eugene.'

*Fuck.*

I hastily opened my mouth.
