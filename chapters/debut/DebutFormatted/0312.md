# Chapter 312 - Grand Prize

The two-day concert ended in great success.

★$
– Please, encore

– No, how could you make something like this and finish it in two days? You crazy bastards (affectionate, but not entirely)

– Be sure to buy the VOD when it comes out. Just buy it. Trust me.
$★

The overwhelming majority of fans who attended the concert responded favorably. First of all, it was fun and fresh.

A theatrical concert with a flowing story.

*And being good performers probably helped us a lot in pulling it off.*

I had the status window to reassure me, so there'd been no need to worry on that front. Our performances were great.

Of course, some people were disappointed by the lack of perceived intimacy and communication, but the complaints died down dramatically after the W Live after-party went ahead as scheduled.

!$
$$[(Congratulations!) TeSTAR karaoke now ☞ **OPEN** ☜]$$
$!

We didn't just talk about what went on behind the scenes at the concert. We took song requests from the audience and sang them together like we were at karaoke.

Thanks to that, our throats were a bit strained, but... It was worth it.

*Good.*

I gulped down the rest of the throat-soothing pear and bellflower juice I'd been drinking.

A cold wind was blowing outside. It looked like the guys who went out would have a hard time.

*– Are you really not going, Moondaemoondae?*

Keun Sejin, who kept asking until the others left, eventually went out too, determined to enjoy his single day off. I didn't blame him.

It was early November, the time to polish our performances for the year-end shows.

That meant we shouldn't expect another day off until well into next year.

*No, until about February.*

All that hard work would culminate in a series of awards ceremonies and a tour.

Today, there was no special contact from the staff. They'd be filthy busy too.

They said the format of this concert was so unique that it was difficult to pull off in the venues where we usually performed.

So they were probably diligently modifying the plans to suit each venue, substituting standard concert elements where the more complex ones weren't feasible.

*It's a busy season.*

Today would be my last break for a while, so it was better to clear my head and rest...

*Ding!*

My smartphone vibrated.

★:
-VTIC Shin Cheongryeo Sunbaenim: (_Image_)

- How are you?
:★

"......"

I stared at the screen.

*I'm pretty sure we're not friends.* Whenever I had a day off, this bastard would contact me, and nothing good ever came of it, so ignoring him was my best bet.

However, when I saw the attached image, I changed my mind.

I thought it was another picture of his dog, but it was a screenshot of an internet article.

★$
[“The Magical Boys who fight villains with music!” TeSTAR appears on ⟨T-Holic's Show Business⟩]
$★

*So he's trying to provoke me, huh?*

I replied to the guy.

★:
Yes, sunbaenim. I've been working hard. So many people enjoyed the photo you sent that I've come to just accept it.-
:★

The reaction to the show had been overwhelmingly positive, and our appearance had been a rousing success. What did it matter if they called us magical girls or boys?

I wasn't just trying to justify things to myself, either. People were still talking about our episode online.

Both in name and reality, we were the hottest male idol group in Korea this year.

*We hadn't made a single mistake this year. Everything had gone perfectly.*

We hadn't taken any hits in terms of performance, popularity, or buzz. At the very least, one of the awards ceremonies affiliated with T1 was almost certain to give us a grand prize.

*Is this bastard doing this because he's pissed off?*

But the reply came without a delay.

★:
-I see. I was going to say that it suits your group perfectly ^^
:★

*Was this bastard trying to insult us?*

*So you think you made up for everything you did by cooperating with Gold 2, is that it?*

I decided I preferred the dog photos. I briefly contemplated whether to just turn off my smartphone.

But before I'd even replied, a call came through out of nowhere.

★:
#><#*[VTIC Shin Cheongryeo Sunbaenim]*#><#

📞 Incoming call...
:★

"...?"

*What does he want?*

There might be something useful hidden among the nonsense, so I answered it for now.

"What is it?"

Then a calm voice was heard.

– Hmm... I wonder if you have anything to say to me.

"What?"

*What kind of bullshit was he spewing?*

Then there was a brief silence over the phone, followed by his slightly disappointed voice.

– So not yet, then?

"Not yet?"

– Oh, it's nothing. I was just talking to myself. I called because I was curious how you were doing.

*I don't think that's what's going on here.*

"As I said, I am doing well."

Surely this didn't mean he'd set a trap for me and was waiting for it to explode in my face?

I asked outright.

"Are you expecting something to happen?"

– Hmm?

"You said 'not yet?'"

Then, the sound of laughter came over the phone.

– Oh, it's really nothing. I was just wondering if Hubaenim had returned to Korea yet.

TeSTAR had a brief overseas schedule last week, that much was true.

*But it sounds like he's trying to distract me.*

There was no sign he was up to anything, so I decided to let it go for now.

"We're back in the country, you see."

*Is that so?*

"If you have time while you're here, you should go on T-Holic sunbaenim's variety show. I recommend it."

I wondered what they'd do with a group as boring as VTIC. The guy answered in a drawl.

– Oh, those... sunbaenims.

If it wasn't for the phone call, his expression would probably have made his lack of respect obvious, despite calling them sunbaenims.

– They're real survivors. I've often admired how they keep bouncing back, even after something bad happens...

He'd done everything he could to bury them before, but he'd failed in the end.

I summed it up tersely.

"Sunbaenim is like that too."

– Hm, is that so? Has it turned out that way?

I didn't know why, but it pissed me off because he seemed to be having fun.

– Anyway, I have no intention of going there, but thanks for the recommendation.

"You're welcome."

He was calm.

*...He doesn't seem worried, even if we take one of the grand prizes.*

Well, that was a good thing. It meant that the probability that this bastard would hit me in the back of the head had decreased.

Cheongryeo ended the call with laughter in his voice.

– Then, see you at the awards ceremonies.

"Yes."

And it actually happened.

The next time I saw Cheongryeo was at an awards ceremony hosted by a major music chart organization—the last one held that month.

***

"Ah, TeSTAR, let's show them what we've got... We can, right?"

"Of course!"

"Haha!"

Before the awards ceremony began, the members who'd finished all preparations in the dressing room looked half-excited and half-nervous.

To be honest, if anyone didn't expect us to win an award in this situation, they'd be the weird one.

...I didn't expect that they had already taken out the champagne.

"Come on, shall we have a toast?"

[Yeah, cheeeeers!] Cha Eugene shouted in English.

The guys had opened the non-alcoholic champagne provided by the catering service. They looked like students eating lucky rice cakes before their college entrance exams.

*What... The atmosphere is good.*

Standing amid the cheery members, Ryu Chungwoo pointed at one of them with a smile.

"Sejin seems to have something to say. Can you give us a toast?"

"M-me?"

"Yes."

"Oooh~" Keun Sejin exclaimed.

Bae Sejin's face reddened, but he wasn't angry.

"Ahem, in that case..."

He cleared his throat a few times, then spoke in a serious voice.

"Good work this year, too... and no matter what the results! Let's not doubt how hard we worked and how well we did!"

It was a surprisingly good toast. Ryu Chungwoo must have picked him for a reason.

*Looks like Bae Sejin prepared for this.*

"Hyung..."

"I-I think so too...!"

"I'm touched."

The atmosphere was even better.

Keun Sejin didn't complain either, holding out his hand with a smile.

"I wish us all the best next year! Ah, we still have to do the official chant properly one more time~"

"...Okay!"

"Great!"

And with a chorus of "Fighting!", the members formed a circle, leaning forward, ready to do their chant.

A huddle like the kind you'd see just before a sporting event.

Maybe the analogy wasn't so far off. We'd soon find out who'd won, and the winner would receive a reward.

"Oh, Moondae, your hand!"

I reached out my hand and heard the familiar chant ring out energetically.

"Ah, TeSTAR, let's show everyone something today!"

"Wow!!"

Adrenaline was pumping through our veins.

I finished the non-alcoholic champagne in one go, and set the empty glass on the table.

"Then let's go out."

That was how our first awards ceremony began.

The first part of the Grape Music Awards went by uneventfully.

A meaningful performance by a reasonably popular rookie group that unfortunately hadn't done so well, a performance by rookies from a large agency...

It was all pretty standard and pointless. The group with Gold 2 showed up briefly.

Of course, I stayed alert and focused, not wanting anyone to think I was bored and start a controversy over my attitude.

Only after the long first part ended and the second part began did the long-awaited announcement arrive.

After stage after stage passed... 

The artists appearing now were more popular, and the visual effects and preludes before their performances grew longer and more elaborate.

**[The next award goes to the artist who touched the hearts of the most people this year.]**

*Here we go.*

**[The Artist of the Year!]**

It was the first grand prize. And it was the prize we were aiming for.

The cameras panned over to the grand prize candidates, showing them on the big screen.

One camera appeared right in front of our seats, focusing on the members in preparation.

"Oh..."

"Shh!"

Keun Sejin immediately shushed Cha Eugene, keeping him under control. Perfect.

The presenter paused for dramatic effect.

**[The Artist of the Year Award goes to…]**

A drumroll.

The artists exchanged awkward smiles.

People were swinging their lightsticks.

And finally, a name rang out through the venue.

#*[...TeSTAR!]*#

The indicator lights on the cameras aimed at us came on.

**[Congratulations!]**

*Wow!!*

Confetti burst, the crowd roared, and music played.

And the familiar faces on the big screen. They all looked shocked and overjoyed, as though their expressions had been copied and pasted.

The funny thing was, I was no different.

"Ah."

I was expecting to receive it.

There was a reason our award was presented first. The senior idols would receive theirs at the end of the show.

But... Yeah, I'd be honest.

It felt like someone had hit a gong inside my head. The vibrations ran right through my skull, sending goosebumps down my spine.

*The* grand prize.

Had we really won it this year?

Keun Sejin staggered to his feet, and I did the same, shoving my chair back.

It felt like the people around me were clapping, but I wasn't sure. Cha Eugene was patting my shoulder, and I tugged him along as we went up to the podium.

My shoulder was pressed against Seon Ahyeon's.

We stood onstage as someone handed us bouquets and the trophy.

"Thank you."

As if momentarily choked up, Ryu Chungwoo turned his face away from the microphone, then continued calmly.

"We... We are truly honored to receive this award. It's more than we deserve. Thank you to the members who've been working so hard, not just this year but every day since our debut..."

Ryu Chungwoo thanked our fans, staff, and even his parents. I'd heard him practicing in the dorm without him noticing, but I listened carefully anyway.

He was the one who deserved it.

I wanted to hear him speak.

"...Thank you again."

But just as he was finishing, someone prodded me in the back.

The first time I thought it was an accidental poke, but nobody poked someone twice by mistake.

"...?"

*What do you want?*

*Moondae!*

It was Keun Sejin, mouthing my name.

He pushed me toward the microphone. The other guys moved aside, making room in the middle.

*What?*

That was how I ended up being pushed to the center. The guys thumped me on the shoulder as I passed them. A piece of confetti briefly stuck to my nose, then fell away.

There was a microphone in front of me.

*I mean, come on.*

*Are they pushing me to go now so they can have the final word?* Of course not. I chuckled and grabbed the mic, facing the audience.

*...This is rather exciting.*

*I should say something since I'm here anyway.*

That was when a notification appeared.

★=
#><[Mission cleared!]><#
=★

"...!"

A hologram appeared.

It had never appeared without my summoning it since I'd cleared my status abnormalities.

It took me a moment to understand what was happening.

*Ah.*

*Right.*

As I'd expected, public opinion seemed to matter a lot.

This... This was a reputable grand prize, given to TeSTAR thanks to the public consensus that we deserved it.

*Still, to think I'd clear the mission at the very first awards ceremony.*

It was a development I liked, so I decided to welcome it.

But the hologram didn't disappear. It was displaying messages I hadn't requested.

★=
#><[Calculating rewards…]><#
=★

"...!"

*Right now?*

The timing was odd.

*Aren't I supposed to accept the reward first? Oh, fuck!*

I realized that I was mistaken about something.

This wasn't a status abnormality given to me by the system.

Therefore... There was no guarantee things would unfold the same way they had when I'd been clearing my status abnormalities.

The same was true of checking my rewards.

*If I'm not supposed to push a button to receive the reward...*

A cold sweat ran down my neck.

The hologram that appeared in front of me glitched and changed.

★=
#><[Reward complete!]><#

**Reward: A conversation with Park Moondae**
=★

*Complete?*

At that moment, my vision faded.

*– Park Moondae!*

Just like when I'd cleared my first status abnormality upon debuting after ⟨Idol Inc.⟩.

I was abruptly plunged into darkness.

***

When I thought about it, it was really strange.

That I, who'd never even danced in public before, now danced and sang in front of others for a living.

*The original me... Why had I filmed idols?*

*I could say it was for the money, but why had I bothered filming even the ones that weren't profitable?*

*The joy I'd found there...*

*Had I been vicariously living through them?*

"Heok."

I opened my eyes.

Inside a dark room.

I recognized the ceiling. But it'd been so long since I'd seen it that it didn't feel particularly familiar or comforting... It was the ceiling of my old studio apartment.

"...!!"

I sat up and ran a hand over my face. I was wearing glasses.

Without running to the bathroom to look in the mirror, this hair, hands, glasses...

My original body.

Ryu Gunwoo, not Park Moondae.

"...!"

I balled my hands into fists and bowed my head.

*Calm down.*

*And this situation...*

*This.*

This... I'd experienced something similar before. 

*The daydream.*

Was this like the time I'd been in a coma? *Was this just a dream too—*

It was then.

[E-excuse me!]

"...!"

*What?*

A voice was ringing in my head... No, it was a strange resonance that didn't even feel like something I was hearing in the usual sense.

[I... hyung, why am I here...]

I spat out.

"Who are you?"

[Wh-what?]

It was a tearful sound.

Then the voice echoed again.

[I'm Pa-Park Moondae.]

"......"

*This... What the fuck was this?*
