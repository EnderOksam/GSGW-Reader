# Chapter 306 - TeSTAR Songwriting Camp, I

*Rattle.*

It was quiet inside the moving car.

The members sitting in the seats of the spacious van seemed to be listening to songs or looking at their smartphones.

Kim Raebin tried to think of today's schedule and detailed preparations, but gave up. His thoughts weren't moving in a productive direction.

And he felt guilty for that.

*I can't believe I'm being so lazy.*

It was more a case of exhaustion than anything else, but he interpreted it as laziness.

*...Everyone is working hard.*

And then, he suddenly remembered the scene where the manager and the members were talking about today's schedule.

*– We're filming at a studio on the outskirts, right?*

*– Yes. That's right.*

*– Then it will take a while~*

Certainly, it seemed like a long time had passed since he got into the car.

He didn't know if it was because his aimless anxiety had been endlessly circling in his mind.

It was the hardest when he was still.

Previously, at times like this, he'd jot down plans, melodies, and ideas as they came to him, and the time in the van would fly by.

Kim Raebin turned his head to avoid the memory of failure being replayed.

A flash of green caught his eye.

*Green?*

Looking out the window for the first time in half an hour, he saw they were on a wide road flanked by mountains.

The van... It was driving down the highway.

"...?"

They said they were going to the outskirts, but if they entered the highway like this, wouldn't it be a completely different area from the destination?

Kim Raebin did not have a driver's license, but he'd been to events in other regions, so it was a reasonable question. Still, he held it back.

*I'm in no position to be asking questions.*

Having decided to do as he was told to the best of his ability, Kim Raebin gradually stiffened in his seat as his worries and speculation swept over him again.

And Park Moondae, sitting next to him, glanced at him before looking away.

*He didn't notice.*

A while later...

"Oh, we have arrived."

The members started to get out of the stopped car. Kim Raebin followed them automatically, but he soon froze.

They got out halfway up a mountain on the side of the road.

There was only one old, tasteful house, but it at least seemed to be their destination.

*Is it a nature-friendly concept...?*

And before Kim Raebin could blame himself for failing to keep track of their schedule, the manager climbed back into the driver's seat and bowed to the members through the window.

"Then, I'll wait down at the foot of the mountain."

"Yes! We'll be in touch!"

"...??"

This was also quite odd.

*Isn't he going to accompany us during filming?*

This was the first time since TeSTAR's dedicated team was formed that they'd been left to work without their manager! Kim Raebin shook off some of his helplessness in this unfamiliar situation and looked around him.

A cool breeze was blowing in the beautiful autumn mountains.

As if a tight knot had loosened, the wind blew into his heart.

*I didn't know there were mountains like this in Seoul.*

He reflected on his own narrow perspective and assumptions. Wasn't it wonderful scenery like one of the Taebaek Mountains in Gangwon-do where he used to live?

Then, as he turned his head, a road sign came into his eyes.

<br>
#>< $tt [Seorak Mountain ↑ 32 km] tt$ ><#
<br>

"...??"

Kim Raebin was confused.

Mt. Seorak... It was in Gangwon-do.

So, this was not the outskirts of Seoul... It was really Gangwon-do!

At this point, even someone as steeped in self-blame as he was couldn't help speaking up.

"I-I'm sorry, but have we come to the right place?"

The members looked back at Kim Raebin's sudden outburst.

"Why?"

"This isn't the outskirts of Seoul. It's Gangwon-do!"

"That's right."

"What?"

Ryu Chungwoo replied calmly.

"This is our destination, Raebin."

"...??"

Kim Raebin fell into confusion again. Then, Park Moondae, who'd seen the manager off, finally looked back at him.

His respected hyung was smiling.

"What are we filming..."

"We didn't come to film."

"Then...?"

"We just came to the mountain. Let's do some self-development training."

Park Moondae answered quickly as if he didn't like the words coming out of his mouth, but in the end, he smiled again.

"This is a songwriting camp."

Kim Raebin opened his mouth wide.

The quaint brick cabin looked perfectly ordinary inside, except for a state-of-the-art TV that seemed out of place.

However, Kim Raebin was still standing in the middle of the living room covered with floral wallpaper, still unable to process what was happening.

"Kim Raebin, sit down!"

"Wow~ It seems like we really came on an MT[1]{T/N: MT: Membership training}, yes? The accommodations and everything."

"W-would you like some fruit?"

And before he knew it, he was sitting on a slowly warming electric heating mat, holding a slice of pear.

"...?"

*What the hell was going on here?* Without even noticing that everyone except himself was calm, Kim Raebin analyzed Park Moondae's words and fell into deep thought.

...Songwriting camp.

*Obviously, he seemed reluctant to let me continue to compose...*

Was Park Moondae so frustrated that he'd brought him here to focus on composing until he came up with something usable? Kim Raebin was scared for a moment, but it was also something he'd been hoping for.

*If I take hyung's feedback and apply it, I might be able to write good songs like before.*

It might be very painful, but now he felt like he had no time to lose.

He wanted to start writing a song right away, get it evaluated right away, escape this helpless state he'd been in for weeks, and go back to his old self...

So Kim Raebin immediately shouted as soon as Park Moondae, who had gone to the kitchen, entered the living room.

"C-can I ask what a songwriting camp is?"

"It's the literal meaning."

Park Moondae took out some equipment from his bag. It was Kim Raebin's own laptop.

Kim Raebin felt himself stiffen with tension, but he quickly reached out to receive it.

*Tok.*

It felt heavy in his hands; he'd bought it with performance in mind rather than portability.

He spoke in a trembling voice.

"Then... From now on, when I write songs, you'll all give me feedback..."

"You're not the only one composing."

"What?"

Park Moondae took out a new laptop from his bag without stopping.

"Oh, is Moondae flexing?"

"Take out the red one! REDMAN~!"

And he distributed the laptops one by one to those who reached out.

"Everyone will compose on their own. And negative feedback is prohibited."

"...!"

"During camp, we can't get any feedback from the outside. We haven't installed KakaoTalk on the laptops. Email is prohibited."

It was practically tyrannical, but Kim Raebin couldn't think of anything to say in response.

Lastly, Park Moondae took out his laptop and opened it on the table with his back to the TV.

*Tak.*

Reflexively, Kim Raebin followed and opened his laptop.

Then, seeing the program open on the screen just as he'd left it, he asked in a slightly trembling voice.

"Then, for what purpose are the songs..."

Cha Eugene intervened.

"There's no goal!"

"...!"

"It's as Park Moondae said. It's a camp."

Bae Sejin calmly concluded his words.

"We're just making songs."

* * *

The idea of shutting ourselves away in a mountain cabin, cut off from the outside world for 3 days and 2 nights, received more enthusiastic support than I'd expected.

*...I didn't know that the cabin would be like a family home.*

My eyebrows twitched when I spotted the ginseng wine on the veranda, but I said nothing.

Keun Sejin and Ryu Chungwoo had rented a mountain lodge that looked like a relative's house in the countryside.

*I should've let Bae Sejin choose instead.*

Just then, Bae Sejin opened his mouth.

"...This place feels nostalgic."

"Y-yes, it is."

*I changed my mind. I should've chosen it myself.*

But it was my job to make the plan, so I couldn't help it. It had been a long time since I tried to do something so unproductive, so there was trial and error.

I looked at Kim Raebin.

He was looking between his laptop and the others with a disturbed look on his face.

Okay. It was understandable. He'd just been told to compose whatever he wanted on his own.

So, I had decided on the candidates for the subject of composition.

"I thought about what themes we could pick to make this more fun."

I turned my head and turned on the TV. The only state-of-the-art thing in this house, the smart TV, opened to WeTube.

There were several conditions for choosing the songwriting theme.

First.

**– It shouldn't be related to TeSTAR's activities.**

Kim Raebin was in such a state that if there was even a chance the song could be used for TeSTAR, he'd imagine people's criticism and worry about it even without any actual feedback.

*It should be a pure hobby.*

In the same sense, the second.

**– It wouldn't have anything to do with TeSTAR in the future.**

In a word, there had to be no possibility of the song ever being officially used by us, so there would be no room for useless thoughts.

Of course, even if the music leaked, it had to be a topic that wouldn't cause any trouble, so he wouldn't have to worry.

*Then the scope narrows dramatically.*

And lastly.

The most important point.

**– Something that would pique his interest.**

From the second team match of ⟨Idol Inc.⟩, his preference was clear.

*Inspiration.*

Something that he would want to make into a song. So the first topic I picked was this.

I operated WeTube with the remote control.

And after logging in, I clicked the playlist.

Electronic sounds, 8-bit sounds, and text floating on a black screen.

"The first theme is making a boss theme BGM for the sequel to ⟨127 Section⟩."

"Oh~"

It was the sequel to the well-made game ⟨127 Section⟩, which we'd collaborated with early in our career.

*We can never use this for work.*

Because it'd be copyrighted.

And in the future, this copyright problem wouldn't be solved. Our first collaboration had ended cleanly, and everyone involved knew doing another would be too much of a headache.

*But it's fun.*

Because...

"As it happens, each boss in the sequel... They said each one was based on one of the tutorial characters we played in the first game."

Because of this.

A connection, and room for creativity.

*I think they said something about spirits from a parallel world.*

In any case, what was certain was that it was a choice that brought back memories of past work but was not burdensome.

+=
[Sold Out – II8: BGM]
=+

On the screen, the BGM for the boss based on 'B11', the character I'd played in the trailer, was playing.

"How about arranging the BGM for the character each of us played, in whatever style we want?"

"Oh, interesting? Moondae recreation instructor. Hey~"

The guy who'd been briefed yesterday and agreed to every suggestion was making a fuss.

However, this structure itself was necessary. A structure in which each person attempted to compose.

We should've been doing this a long time ago.

*We put too much pressure on Kim Raebin.*

Right now, it wasn't like I sang entire verses alone just because I had the highest singing stat.

It had to be the same in producing.

As for the music, we should've gone beyond simply giving feedback, taken a more active part in production itself, and increased our proficiency, but we'd put it off.

Because Kim Raebin was so good.

*Music is the most important thing. We shouldn't have neglected it.*

We should never have let Kim Raebin become known as the soul of TeSTAR's music.

Instead of stopping at concepts and genre outlines, we should've tried our hand at the actual music at least once.

I thought as I checked the composition software installed on my laptop. 

*I hope this has the effect of reducing the pressure.*

Okay. Anyway, I'd have to check his reaction.

I looked up at Kim Raebin and opened my mouth.

"And next..."

No, we didn't need another one.

I shut my mouth. Kim Raebin was watching the video on the smart TV with rapt attention.
