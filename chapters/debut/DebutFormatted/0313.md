# Chapter 313 - Conversation, I

Unexpectedly being thrown out of Park Moondae's body and returning to my own?

Of course, I'd considered that possibility. In the first place, I'd been looking for a way to return.

The problem was... I'd never considered returning to my own body and bringing Park Moondae along with me.

[...]

"......"

Both of us were silent for a long moment.

[Uh...]

"Be quiet. I'm thinking."

[Yes...]

Hearing his voice echo, almost tearfully, I massaged my forehead with both hands.

My head hurt.

*Fuck.*

I couldn't figure out what the hell was happening... No, the first thing I needed to do was gather information.

"You... You're Park Moondae?" I asked.

[Y-yes...!]

There was nothing particularly distinctive about his voice, but I could make out things like the way he spoke.

In other words, I was dealing with a young kid who was a little intimidated right now.

*Damn it.*

*Could this be the reward I received for clearing that mission, 'A conversation with Park Moondae'?*

I was at a loss for words. This was one hell of a reward to have forced on me just as I was about to give my speech onstage.

*But wait.*

"...!"

I'd just been about to give my acceptance speech after we received the grand prize. At a music awards ceremony being broadcast live.

In an instant, my blood ran cold.

[Gunwoo-hyung...?]

I ignored him, got up immediately, and started looking around.

[Wh-what are you doing?]

"My phone."

After tossing aside the blanket, I asked in a low voice.

"Where's my phone?"

[I don't know... I don't know why I'm here...!]

I searched all around the room and finally found a small TV on the table next to the sink, which served as both a dining table and a desk.

*TV?*

I'd never owned a TV... But that was beside the point. I turned it on.

A news channel flickered to life on the screen. I wondered if there might be any breaking news about me. And... I saw it.

The date was displayed in small print at the bottom of the news channel's screen.

"...!"

+=
[December 15, 202X]
=+

*Four years ago.*

It wasn't the day I'd just been living through—the awards ceremony at the end of November, when TeSTAR received their first grand prize of the year.

*December 15.* Park Moondae's birthday.

It was the same day Park Moondae went to a motel and took sleeping pills, which led to me entering his body.

The fateful day everything had begun.

"...Hoo."

As I stepped back, I nearly tripped over a chair.

[Are you okay??]

*Was I back here again?*

*No.*

That couldn't be.

So, this... It was all too similar to when I'd fallen into a coma, and if I remembered correctly, that time had also been...

*Yes.* I knew for sure now.

"Is it a dream again?"

[What? Hyung!]

I sat down in the chair and intertwined my fingers. My hands slowly stopped trembling.

"Okay. Let's talk."

Since this was meant to be a fucking reward, there had to be something I could get out of it.

I dove into the conversation without hesitation.

"Park Moondae. You said you didn't know why you were here."

[What? No. I don't.]

Whether that was a lie or not, he probably couldn't explain why I'd entered his body. I decided to push on without waiting for him to elaborate.

"Then what were you doing right before all this started?"

[I-I... ]

His voice shook a little uneasily. I waited patiently for an answer.

[...I was visiting someone in the hospital.]

"Visiting?"

Who had he been visiting? Family? I hadn't heard of Park Moondae having any family.

However, an unexpected answer came back.

[Gunwoo-hyung... I was visiting you, hyung.]

"...!"

It was me.

*Visiting me at the hospital?* What was going on... No, there was something I'd noticed from the moment we met.

*This guy knows me.*

From the beginning, without hesitation, he'd called me 'Gunwoo-hyung'. It meant that, at least from his point of view, we were friends.

That was a clue. I continued asking questions.

"When?"

[Well...! That's what puzzles me, too. I'm sure I was living in the year 202X, but the news channel just now showed a different year...]

I had to admit it. This guy... He was the Park Moondae I'd seen the last time I used Reveal the Truth.

The Park Moondae from a future I didn't remember, in which we'd become friends after I bought him a meal.

The trembling voice continued.

[By any chance... D-don't you know me, hyung?]

"I'm not sure."

[What??]

*I mean, I couldn't be sure whether I even knew him or not.*

"First, tell me the last thing you remember about your situation. I... My memories seem a little jumbled right now."

[L-let me see...]

Park Moondae hesitated, but soon began telling me what he remembered.

[Hyung... You were hospitalized, so I went to see you because I was worried. But then, all of a sudden, I was here...]

"Why was I hospitalized?"

[...Uh, you were overworked.]

I nodded to myself decisively.

"Don't lie."

[...!]

It was nonsense to say that I was hospitalized because of overwork. And this guy, when he lied, the tone of his voice changed.

"That's not the reason."

A possible answer came to me immediately.

"...Did I try to kill myself?"

[...Yes. Hy-hyung... You were mentally exhausted... So you remember that?]

*As expected.*

I'd tried to die the last time I used Reveal the Truth, but I'd failed and must have been taken to the hospital before suffering any serious harm.

It seemed my luck wasn't half bad, though. The situation was baffling, but I nodded anyway.

"Somewhat. So, what did you talk about when you visited me at the hospital?"

[We couldn't talk. Hyung was still unconscious...]

"......"

*So I was in a coma.*

I asked some more questions, but he hadn't done anything special during the hospital visit, either. He didn't seem to be lying.

There was one more thing I needed to find out.

"What were you thinking?"

[What?]

Based on what I'd cross-checked with the others, I hypothesized that those who returned to the past seemed to be allowed to do so based on their thoughts and desires in their final moments.

If so, I needed to know what had happened in this Park Moondae's final moments. I'd never wished to enter Park Moondae's body.

"I wonder if you remember the last thing you were thinking about back then."

For example, on seeing me, he might have wanted to run away if he believed what he saw was his own future.

[...]

His voice shook even more, but he spoke without hesitation.

[It was nothing. I just thought it would be nice if hyung lived a long, healthy life...]

"...!"

It was an answer I did not expect.

A little taken aback, I suddenly couldn't find the words.

His answer made me feel a little... ashamed of myself.

*Damn it.*

It took me a moment to gather myself.

"First... I'm sorry."

[...What??]

"I'm sorry for suddenly demanding answers as if I were interrogating you. You must be just as disoriented as I am."

No, it wasn't surprising he'd panicked, finding himself suddenly without a body, nothing more than a disembodied voice.

Dream or no dream, it was wrong of me to suddenly take such a harsh tone with him. I needed to be more level-headed.

"Anyway, when you came to visit... Thank you for wishing me well like that."

[Ah...]

His voice sounded slightly bewildered and hesitant.

[No! You've been helping me all along, but I haven't paid you back for any of it...]

*Pay me back? Don't be silly.*

As I'd expected, he didn't seem to have had much experience being helped by others. I'd lost count of how many times he'd said that after the single bowl of soup and rice I'd bought him when I was shown our first meeting.

I was grateful, but I decided to move on to something more productive.

"That's fine, but more than that, how exactly are you now?"

[Ah! Well, I can't move this body myself... But when hyung moves, I can feel it too...?]

"Okay."

So we shared the same body, but I was in control of its movements.

Anyway, I really was getting my 'A conversation with Park Moondae' reward. It was a bizarre way to make us talk in the same body, though.

*Why is it like this?*

If we were going to be transported into a dream like this anyway, couldn't we simply have been given our own bodies?

Perhaps... The real Park Moondae's consciousness was no longer around in the reality I'd occupied, and that was why this was happening?

"Hmm."

It wasn't a very pleasant hypothesis, especially since it led to no clear conclusions. I'd have to examine this dream more closely.

I got up and started searching my phone again. It was then.

[Um, hyung, are you looking for your phone?]

"Yes."

[That... Maybe it's in your back pocket.]

The voice added uncertainly.

[I think I can feel something in my back pocket right now...]

*Ah.*

I immediately reached into the back pocket of my jeans and found the familiar device.

I sighed.

"Thanks."

I'd been too disoriented to even think of checking there. I clicked my tongue and turned on the phone's screen.

Then a pop-up appeared.

+.
#><[No network connection.]><#
.+

"...?"

This... It was the sort of pop-up I'd expect to see on an unregistered phone. One without a number...

*Wait...*

A strange hunch suddenly struck me, and I immediately looked around.

I hadn't noticed until now because I hadn't changed anything about my apartment at the time, but something felt off.

And I found the cause.

[Hyung?]

It was a family photo sitting on the shelf in the open wardrobe.

It was a photo of four strangers. I knew I'd never taken it, bought it, or placed it there.

Somebody else had.

"......"

All of a sudden, a thought crossed my mind.

When I first entered the body of 'Park Moondae', I'd checked Ryu Gunwoo's active accounts, phone number, website usernames, and so on.

Everything was gone.

At first, I'd thought it was a parallel world, but I eventually concluded that they'd vanished by supernatural means, since all the accounts and records I'd abandoned in the past still existed.

*'Ryu Gunwoo' disappeared from all places, including the Internet and the real world, starting from the moment I entered the body of 'Park Moondae'.*

And if, now... What if I'd suddenly appeared again at that point four years ago?

Without any connection to the reality I'd once occupied.

To make sure, I slapped myself in the face.

[Hyung!]

"......"

*That hurt.*

*Fuck.*

I got up right away. And I took a closer look around.

It confirmed what I'd suspected.

*This isn't the apartment I used to live in.*

And judging from the pain, it wasn't even a dream. This was fucking reality.

*Then, practically speaking, the first thing I had to do was...*

"We need to get out of here."

[We're leaving??]

*Yes.* If the rental contract had also been reset, that meant we were in someone else's apartment!

I hurried out of the apartment that was no longer mine.

I hadn't touched anything in particular, so they probably wouldn't notice someone had been here.

A new problem arose.

*What I have now is...*

A flip phone, not even a smartphone, that had no network connection. And I had only about 140,000 won in my wallet.

"......"

Of course, I wouldn't have a bank account, either.

*I want to kill whoever's responsible for this.*

I ground my teeth. And called out.

"Status window."

At least a window did appear.

"......"

*I had to stay calm.*

I tried to look on the bright side. Perhaps things would go back to normal after receiving the reward.

I quickly put aside thoughts of how this had happened right before my grand prize acceptance speech. Dwelling on it now wouldn't help.

"Hoo."

Moondae, who'd also roughly grasped the situation, hummed softly in my mind.

[Hyung, uh... Are you okay? What do you think is going on here...?]

"I'm okay."

Not really. But no matter how much I cursed, it wouldn't change anything, so I shut up and sat down.

There was one thing I needed more than anything else right now.

*I don't even have a place to stay.*

No, it wasn't just a home I lacked. I had nothing at all because all my contacts within society had been reset.

I didn't know how long this damn situation would last, but at the very least I needed a working phone.

*...Then there was only one thing to do.*

Having made my decision, I started walking again.

[Excuse me... Where are we going now?]

"Convenience store."

I added casually.

"I'm going to buy some lottery tickets."

[Lottery tickets... What? W-well, I think I've come to the past, but I don't remember any winning lottery numbers...]

He seemed to have misunderstood.

"I know the numbers."

[...]

I opened my phone again.

Even though I couldn't make calls with it or anything, I could check the day of the week.

*– Friday*

And tomorrow, Saturday, was the lottery winner announcement day.

I thought about it.

As soon as I'd entered Park Moondae's body, I'd realized I didn't know any stock or lottery numbers. But of course I couldn't resist looking at the winning numbers announced the very next day.

Even though it was of no practical use, I'd looked them up out of disappointment.

And such a memorable experience was bound to stick in my mind for a while.

*I still remember them.*

As soon as I entered the convenience store, I bought about twenty lottery tickets, including a couple with numbers I wasn't sure about.

"One of these is bound to be the winner."

The voice in my mind seemed to swallow.

[Hyung... You're really smart.]

It had nothing to do with how smart I was. It was a situational problem. To understand that, he'd need to hear about what I'd been through in his body.

*But right now... This guy seems tired, too.*

His voice had started sounding anxious a while ago. The situation was beginning to sink in, and he was feeling scared.

*For now, I'll give him time to rest.*

I then booked somewhere inexpensive to stay. I only needed my money to last until Monday morning, so I had enough cash for now.

*Let's rest first, and then talk after we've both calmed down.*

As soon as I woke up from my nap, I'd tell this guy what I'd been through and explain the exact situation.

I spoke as I lay down on the bed.

"If you can't sleep after I fall asleep, wake me up."

[What? No! It's okay...]

"Wake me up."

I checked the status window, which still showed the same message, then fell asleep.

***

Light fell over my eyelids.

[Gunwoo-hyung...?]

"Hoo."

I gasped and opened my eyes. I must have slept more deeply than I'd expected. My mind felt clear.

And I heard a voice that was almost crying.

[Hyung! Oh, I knew you'd still be around...!]

Why was he so happy I was here? I sat up.

And I was bewildered.

"...?"

This wasn't the motel I'd fallen asleep in. I could see what looked like the inside of an officetel. Bright sunlight and green trees were visible through the window.

"......"

*No, it couldn't possibly be December anymore.*

"What?"

Before I could think any further, the voice began chattering away.

[Ah, hyung! We won that lottery, just like you said we would! We won 1.2 billion won!]

That was good news. But it meant enough time had passed for us to get the money and buy an apartment with it.

I asked him about that.

"It seems like a while has passed since we bought those lottery tickets."

His voice grew much quieter.

[Yes. You bought the lottery tickets then. I woke up the next day... Hyung was gone, and I was you...?]

*What?*

[S-so I've been living that way for some time...]

That made absolutely no sense, and it made my head spin.

*...Let's stay calm.*

Still, at least we seemed to have no money problems after winning the lottery.

Looking around the apartment, which looked much better than the one I'd lived in before, I had one less thing to worry about.

But the biggest surprise was still to come.

[Oh, and, uh... Hyung. After you disappeared, something strange popped up...]

"What?"

[L-look at this...]

A moment later, I was staring at a window.
