# Chapter 311 - ⟨Wave for Me⟩, II

Act 2 of TeSTAR's concert was a series of endless climaxes.

The story continued from Kim Raebin and Seon Ahyeon's unit stage, and the boy, having given in to his impulses, eventually veered in a dangerous and dark direction.

A stage expressing the violence of that emotional change.

$p
♪ *I'm finally here*\
*Present* ♫
p$

Fragmented electronic sounds replaced the traditional instruments of the original song, creating an ominous soundscape.

$p
♪ *Today's the day*\
*Never get away from me, ya* ♫
p$

This version of 'Present' was much darker than the original, intense and sexy enough to seem almost otherworldly.

Ryu Chungwoo, standing in the center, nodded to the beat with a blank expression.

*Joseon Zombie...*

The gamer couldn't imagine anything more intense than this. Surely this was the moment the conflict reached its climax.

But no.

Every time she thought they'd reached the climax, a new build-up began, elevating the performance even further.

*Huh? What?*

⟨Better Me⟩, which depicted the confusion of a boy who'd overcome his enemies only to find himself beset by new doubts, followed.

It was an overwhelming shadow performance.

*This is the climax!*

It wasn't over. 

After his period of wandering, the boy came to a new understanding of himself and returned to who he'd once been. What followed was an elaborate performance about his return to himself.

"...??"

The next song was ⟨Daybreak⟩, a combination of the two title tracks from their previous promotional cycle, 'Wheel' and 'Drill'.

Fireworks went off, and the audience's jaws dropped at the dazzling, exhilarating performance.

$p
♪ *Turn my Ferris wheel around*\
*The lights go off like stars* ♫
p$

A shiver ran up and down her spine.

Now *this* was truly a stage worthy of the climax of a concert... But the gamer had caught on to the pattern by now.

It wasn't over yet.

*There's something more, isn't there?? I know there is!*

At this point, she was starting to look forward to being proven wrong.

Deep down, she just wanted this crazy performance to go on forever.

Because the intensity and immersion were just that good!

And as if to grant her wish, the next song began.

*Tung-.*

But this time something was different.

*It's taking a long time...?*

The intro was longer than before.

Long after the VCR ended, drums continued to pound out from the dark stage.

*What?*

Then everything came to life onstage at once.

With an exciting whistling sound.

*Whiik!*

"...!"

Water began pouring onto the stage.

*Crazy!*

She didn't know how much money they'd poured into it, but water like something from a massive water show was flowing across the floor, forming a shallow pool like a spring.

Then the lights began to glow.

*Whihwihwi-ik!*

The lights followed the streams gathering on the floor of the stage, creating a fantastical sight.

And at that moment, a performer appeared.

The performer leaped down from the darkness above the stream at the front of the stage and landed in the water.

"...!"

It was Cha Eugene.

Unlike the unit performance he'd taken part in earlier, Cha Eugene had come out alone this time—the first solo appearance of the concert.

$p
♪ *Yeah, yeah, yeaaaaah* ♫
p$

And without further ado, he launched straight into his solo performance.

Like a fish in water, he delivered an elaborate solo intro, standing calf-deep amid the illuminated streams.

The sprays of water arced through the air, scattering and reflecting light in all directions. Yet all those effects were eclipsed by the solo performer's movements, mere props to his charisma.

Cha Eugene's trait allowed him to take control of the stage in an instant.

The gamer was involuntarily amazed.

"Wow, seriously."

*Was he even human?*

The moment Cha Eugene finished the intro performance, he yanked off his goggles, tossed them aside, and grinned at the audience.

**#^#Aaaaaaaaa!!!#^#**

Despite the deafening shouts, the gamer didn't frown this time.

A familiar prelude was playing in the background.

*Ah, this!*

TeSTAR's latest hit song. It was ⟨Promise⟩, which was still firmly holding onto the music charts.

It was TeSTAR's most successful song to date. Even the gamer knew it well because she heard it twice on average whenever she went out.

So she made a pretty convincing guess.

*Ah, then, it's like, 'Let's all sing together like it's a festival?'*

Unfortunately, not this time either.

Instead, something that had been temporarily absent returned to the venue.

The lighting.

*Uuung~*

Wavy blue lights covered the performance hall. And this time, instead of dry ice, soap bubbles poured like air bubbles toward the audience.

"Uh...?"

This was the finale of the concert.

The venue turned into the sea again.

However, this time the boy did not sink.

$p
♪ *Umm* ♫
p$

Cha Eugene turned his back, shrugged, and looked up at the ceiling, toward the surface of the sea.

The stage floor opened, and six figures rose up around him.

The other members of TeSTAR.

$p
♪ *Take your STAR* ♫
p$

They were rising towards the surface.

"...!"

As soon as the first line began, TeSTAR broke into wide smiles and began dancing, kicking up the water.

$p
♪ *The stars are falling from on high* ♫
p$

The performance they'd once delivered in the pouring rain now unfolded on a grand, elaborate concert scale, just as they'd intended from the start.

The ease and enjoyment that came from countless practice sessions dispelled the fans' anxiety about the rain.

The water drained off the stage until it was only ankle-deep, and the beautiful, upbeat choreography unfolded to their refreshing vocals.

$p
♪ *Kick the waves and run*\
*Toward the sky, run and fly* ♫
p$

With more members onstage, the splashes of water became even more intricate.

"Wow."

The familiar song, combined with the intensity and scale of the performance, drew the audience into an incredible state of immersion.

The melody flowed, and the song passed in an instant.

As the audience waved their lightsticks, completely immersed in the performance that made full use of the water...

$p
♪ *My mood's shining like the stars*\
*Promise me you'll come with me*\
*Make you fly* ♫
p$

The song hurtled toward its end, passing the bridge before long.

The members' choreography grew more subdued, and the lights illuminating the water dimmed. It seemed almost too dark.

*Pas!*

In an instant, the inside of the concert hall itself began to sparkle.

"Oh my gosh."

And the gamer was taken aback when her own lightstick began flickering wildly.

All the lightsticks in the concert hall—or perhaps it was the countless tiny lights on the ceiling above the stage—shone like constellations.

$p
♪ *To the island beyond the horizon, beyond the sea*\
*It'll be a glittering day* ♫
p$

It was beautiful.

The boy, who'd sunk to the bottom of the ocean under the weight of his past, returned to reality, to a world beneath a star-filled sky.

With the water almost completely drained away, the members left the stage and ran toward the stars.

$p
♪ *Umm, Umm, Umm~* ♫
p$

It was time for the ending.

After getting out of the water, the members ran to the very front of the thrust stage and launched into the final choreography.

The lights were no longer blue. Golden light and fluttering confetti made the venue feel like a warm desert in midsummer.

A true climax and finale. A clean, enjoyable end to the story, without a single loose end.

**["Thank you!"]**

In that way, TeSTAR finished the story, with the boy having become a fully realized idol.

"......"

The gamer watched the whole scene with a somewhat dazed mind.

An intense yet refreshing feeling coursed through her.

After a while, TeSTAR's members came out one by one and bowed to the audience, just like at a theater curtain call.

The music playing was their first fan song, 'You Are the Magic'.

$p
♪ *I feel good today*\
*Like something great's about to happen* ♫
p$

When their respective parts began, they jumped around the stage.

With head mics on, they spread out along the branches of the stage to interact with the fans.

"Moondae, aaahhhh!"

"Ahh, here!"

It was a special moment for the fans who'd been disappointed by the lack of interaction, and the second fan song and 'Picnic' stretched on as everyone sang together.

However, instead of being bored, the gamer sat for a long time after the curtain call, like other fans.

*...*

And after everything was over, she finally checked her smartphone.

★:
#*10:31 PM*#
:★

The show had started at 6:30 PM. Four hours had disappeared in an instant, as if by magic.

"Crazy."

She pressed her forehead against the chair in front of her.

On the way back...

The two friends stifled their laughter as they watched their gamer friend, who'd clearly been moved by the concert.

"How was it? Was the ticket worth it?"

It was obvious what they wanted to hear, but the gamer gave them what they wanted.

"Oh, it was fun... I didn't even realize how much time had passed."

This kind of entertainment was worth spending money on. She could feel herself growing fond of the idols even as she spoke.

In the end, she murmured in wonder.

"Hey, really... he was good."

"...!"

The two friends exchanged glances. If she was referring to the vocals or fan service, it was likely she was talking about Park Moondae!

"Who?"

"Who do you like best??"

In that tense moment, the gamer opened her mouth with a serious look on her face.

"I... liked all of them."

"...??"

"I love the way they work together as a group, really... They're perfect."

Yes.

She left the concert as an all-member fan, a rarity more precious than wild ginseng.

Similar confessions were appearing all over the Internet, but the performers themselves were experiencing a much simpler joy.

* * *

11 p.m.

We hadn't been able to go home yet because of the post-concert feedback, and were still working overtime in the practice room. But the atmosphere was good.

The concert had been a great success.

"It was a great success~"

"Ah, the best of the best!!"

"It was fun!"

Everyone still seemed to have energy left after performing that crazy setlist, and they couldn't stop smiling.

*It was worth the money.*

The thrill of succeeding at something new was always extraordinary, especially with something as big as a concert.

I summed it up neatly.

"Your idea was fantastic."

"...! Ay~ What are you talking about! It's not just me. I only added a few things to the setlist that was already done~ A lot of Moondae's ideas went into it too!" Keun Sejin said.

Why was he bothering to play it down when he was grinning from ear to ear?

Anyway, the W Live after the final concert was going to be long, like an end-of-tour wrap-up party, so today we just had to celebrate our success and do a little housekeeping.

However, I had a few more things to say.

"Hmm, Eugene, this part looked a bit dangerous."

"I did great!"

"Yeah, you did, but I'm asking you to land more safely next time."

I passed by the guys giving each other feedback and found the person I was looking for.

"Kim Raebin."

"Yes!"

Kim Raebin was talking with Seon Ahyeon about their unit stage. After exchanging a few words with Seon Ahyeon, I sat down beside Raebin.

And I got straight to the point.

"How was it?"

"Yes?"

"How did the performance go?"

"......"

Kim Raebin had a slightly nervous face, but answered without hesitation.

"I was very concerned about whether I'd be able to interpret someone else's production properly and do their intentions justice as a professional, but..."

After a long introduction, the answer came out.

"...This time, I think I pulled it off quite well."

*There we go.*

"Of course, I'm aware that Ahyeon-hyung, who performed with me, did most of the heavy lifting because he was so outstanding, but..."

Kim Raebin's eyes shone.

"I think I'm capable of doing it...!"

That answer told me he'd gained the inner strength I'd been hoping for. I smiled and asked again.

"You think you can do it again?"

"Yes!!"

*Good.*

After hearing that firm answer, I called up his status window without hesitation.

And I saw exactly what I'd been expecting.

★!
@c@Status Abnormality:@c@
#><[Repeat Sign (deactivated)]><#
!★

His status abnormality had been deactivated.

*Finally.*

Of course, it hadn't disappeared from his status window entirely. Maybe someday the feeling that "my producing is ruined" would come back to haunt him.

But I wasn't worried anymore. Now this guy would be able to overcome his slumps on his own.

Because he had already escaped once.

And... He knew for sure now that things would be okay even if he stopped producing our music himself.

That was enough.

"Good job."

"Not at all... I'm very grateful! I'll continue doing my best."

Kim Raebin bowed his head. Seon Ahyeon, who was next to me, clapped, his face slightly red.

Things had turned out well.

This was the end of worrying about the music for the next album.

*Well, then there is only one thing left.*

With my back against the wall of the practice room, I mentally rehearsed how to present the idea I'd been considering for days.

"I have a suggestion."

"Huh?"

On the way back to the dorm in the van just before midnight, I raised my hand.

"That arrangement account."

"Ah, that."

I was talking about the official offer concerning the game theme song, which we'd set aside because the concert was so urgent.

I spoke calmly.

"If we want to give it a try, I think it'd be fine to accept their offer."

"...!"

"B-but how...?"

*Simply put...*

"We can donate the proceeds."

"...??"

We could give up the money. It was that simple.

"When we sign the contract, we'll donate the profits to a charity."

After all, nobody here was particularly hung up on the money.

It would be a waste to just get rid of the WeTube account, and we wanted to avoid unnecessary attention if possible.

So we could accept the game company's offer just this once and keep using that same account.

"If we donate anonymously, we won't have to worry about tax evasion, so there won't be a problem if people find out later. As for our identities, we can just create a corporate entity."

"Aha..."

The person behind the offer at T1 Plays wouldn't go so far as to illegally dig up personal information about some beginner business.

"Okay! I have no objections to that since I'm not too worried about the money, either."

Kim Raebin answered brightly.

Raebin himself didn't seem aware of it, but as the person with the greatest claim to the proceeds, his answer would force anyone else who wanted the money to shut up.

Well, it probably didn't matter, since nobody seemed particularly interested in the money in the first place.

However, Bae Sejin asked with a confused face.

"Can't we just say we don't want any money for the music?"

That couldn't be done.

"That... If people find out it was us, they'll say we're so rich we don't care how intellectual property is handled."

"......"

"It won't just be a controversy over our attitude. People will say we're having a negative impact on the industry where copyright is concerned."

Public opinion had never been kind to idols.

Everything. We had to consider everything.

I looked past Bae Sejin, who seemed both shocked and disillusioned, and asked.

"So, if we're going to do it, I'm thinking about handling anonymous donations like this... What do you guys think?"

As I'd expected, the others readily agreed.

"I like it~" Keun Sejin said.

"Me too!" Cha Eugene chimed in.

Even Ryu Chungwoo smiled and opened his mouth.

"Yeah, it sounds fine."

It was unanimous.

I rubbed the back of my neck.

With this, the composing camp account could continue to operate without any problems.

"Yes. Then let's do that."

TeSTAR's arrangement account, most of whose content belonged to Kim Raebin, had been given the green light...

"Song of the Stars is going to have its own career, too!"

...Yes, our account, 'Song of the Stars', would be replying to T1 Plays soon.

"......"

For the record, I had objected to that account name.

Why did they want to connect it with TeSTAR...? I just wanted to make it clear that I'd been outvoted.

*Because not all the plans I made worked.*

The funny thing was, this time, my plan didn't go perfectly as expected.

A few days after we officially signed a contract with T1 Plays, posts like these started gaining traction on the Internet.

It was just... A T1 Plays staff member had left a public comment on one of our arrangement videos.

"Are you going to ignore it?"

"Yes."

We sent the person in charge one complaint email about the matter being made public, then stopped responding.

Amid the sudden surge in attention to our secret account, the weather grew colder as we raced toward that important date.

The end of the year was drawing near.
