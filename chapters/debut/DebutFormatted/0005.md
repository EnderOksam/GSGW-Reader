# Chapter 5 - Initial Evaluation, III

The intro to the wildly famous song passed by, followed by the lively vocals.

I struck the most iconic starting pose I could remember.

Then one of the judges spat out the water he was drinking.

♪ *Oh, me too! I like you~\
The feeling of my heart beating~! Is like popcorn!\
POP! POP!\
The popping beat is just ringing~\
My heart is overflowing with this feeling!* ♫

*Tap, tap, tap.*

Youngrin, the judge who asked to see me dance, was the only one who nodded along attentively to my simple steps. It seemed that she was the only professional here.

Soon, the part of the song that half of all people my age could dance from memory came on.

♪ *POP! POP!\
You're my Popcorn~ (Oh yeah!)\
My heart\
CON CON CON CON\
Control it!* ♫

The chorus of SoftSweet's ⟨POP☆CON⟩, which was released 10 years ago, exploded from the sound system and rang in my ears.

Infamous as one of those songs you should never listen to while studying for the CSAT, ⟨POP☆CON⟩ made numerous students fail their listening tests after its bizarre lyrics and addictive melody got stuck in their heads.

For the record, I had been one of those victims, but I couldn't retake the test because I didn't have the money to do so.

The song was so popular that lots of people made dance covers and uploaded them online. Even elementary school kids could easily pick up the choreography with a little practice.

If I had performed this ten years ago when the song was still popular, the judges would have roasted me alive. However, I chose the song because I thought I could evoke a feeling of nostalgia and turn the judges' reaction into a comedy.

Of course, I was dancing as best I could with a completely straight face. Even if others found my performance funny, it would look bad if I gave the impression that I wasn't taking it seriously. I needed to show them that I had worked hard to prepare the dance to the best of my abilities.

Of course, it was also true that I worked really hard. Through repeated practice, I leveled up and completed the ⟨First Movement⟩ and ⟨10th Movements⟩ missions.

It seemed like I had made a few mistakes since some of the judges were laughing hysterically, but whatever. They'd evaluate me however they wanted.

I completed the choreography up to the final pose of the first verse.

As I hung my head and caught my breath, the applause brought me back to reality.

"Thank you."

Youngrin was the first to take the microphone, but the choreographer spoke first. His eyes were red, most likely from laughing so hard that he'd cried.

"Whoa... Oh, I never dreamed it would come out. Moondae, weren't you like ten years old when POP☆CON came out? Did you perform that for a talent show as a kid or something?"

"No, nothing like that. I chose this song because I'm a huge fan of SoftSweet."

"Pfft, hahaha!"

For reference, SoftSweet's most recent album was released 4 years ago. It was so late that it seemed unusual to say that a 20-year-old was a fan even after their golden days.

Because the reason can't be that he wants to be an idol.

Sure enough, the choreographer who asked me this question laughed again.

Next to him, Mudie muttered delightfully.

"Ah... So cute~."

"You worked hard. First of all, you work hard!"

The male idol judge chimed in. It seemed any suspicions he'd had about my lack of experience had completely disappeared.

Finally, Youngrin spoke.

"But that doesn't mean you did well."

I knew this would happen. It was a situation where close-ups and sound effects could be included through editing. They'll probably also add a huge red caption of what she just said, repeated three times, with a "da dun" sound.

I roughly anticipated the editing angle and waited for the rest of the comments.

"First of all, I can see that you have no skill when you start moving your body. Although you may receive a positive evaluation for our current atmosphere, future missions will be difficult."

"I will be prepared."

Not me, but the status window.

Regardless of the words I swallowed, Youngrin nodded gravely.

"Yeah."

And she added with a relaxed expression.

"...Anyway, I can see your effort. Well done."

"Thank you."

I felt like I'd said "Thank you" at least ten times today. Is it always going to be like this?

By then, the judges were exchanging opinions without holding the microphone, so I couldn't hear them.

Now, the program's next cruel twist was about to be introduced.

From the first round, the contestants were ranked from first to seventy-seventh place and seated accordingly. The contestants immediately went to their assigned seats after the judges gave their evaluations, even though the rankings were only temporary.

If a seat was already filled but the next contestant performed better, everyone would move down a rank and yield their seat. One of the show's selling points was the frequent shots of lower-ranking contestants moving to their new ranks in tears.

Fortunately, I won't be in such a situation.

I guessed I'd rank at about... twenty-fourth place?

"Park Moondae's ranking is...... 17th place!"

They had probably seated me about five ranks higher so they could film me moving down in rank, which meant that my actual rank was closer to my expectations.

*I think I went up about four or five places because of the atmosphere.*

I'm not sure if the judges were distracted by the atmosphere, but as the time went on, they should have been busy leaving the seat to the middle and high school students.

It was a little funny when I lowered my head and thought about it sullenly.

"Thank you."

"I'll teach you hard, so you'll rank higher next time."

The choreographer said with a smile. If I don't get better, I'll be replaced immediately, but I have a status window to work with.

Hopefully, I can create a scenario in the future where the choreographer is moved by my progress.

I nodded once again and headed for the set where the seats were placed.

The seats were each labeled with a number, and the higher the rank, the more luxurious and comfortable they were—the difference was glaring.

The 17th place I would sit was a leather chair on which a corporate executive would sit.

As I sat down, the participant who was sitting in 15th place on the opposite side greeted me. He had a friendly demeanor and seemed to be around Moondae's age.

"Hello~"

"Hello."

"Your singing was amazing!"

"Thank you. I'm sure you did great as well."

"I'm sorry? Hahaha!"

I couldn't be sure since I hadn't seen his performance, but judging from his ranking, he probably did well.

The participant laughed aloud and pointed to the name tag on his chest.

"My name is Lee Sejin!"

"....!"

I almost let out a sigh. I replied with the most brazen face possible.

"I'm Park Moondae. Nice to meet you."

"Same here."

I ended the conversation naturally and looked ahead, and the next participant came in with good timing. But my mind kept wandering back to a name I had just heard.

Lee Sejin.

That was the name of one of the contestants who had successfully debuted through the show. You could say I'd just met someone who was destined to emerge victorious from the show.

But there was a problem.

It will be revealed that the punk was involved in a drug scandal one year after this program ended. Worse, he was responsible for distributing drugs.

I was eating at a soup restaurant near my studio apartment when I heard his name on the nine o'clock news.

[Lee Sejin from "Idol Incorporated" arrested on suspicion of distributing drugs]

...Let's not talk to him again.

It is important to know when to cut off. All right, I should use this time to check my status window.

★-
[Name: Park Moondae (Ryu Gunwoo)]

Level: 5

Title: None

Vocal: A-

Dance: D

Visual: C+

Talent: C

Characteristics: Infinite potential

!Status Abnormality: Debut or die

[Points Remaining: 1]
-★

I must have received a point after leveling up just now, most likely because I'd completed the ⟨First Performance⟩ mission.

I'd also been assigned stats for dancing and charm, which hadn't been given any grades while I was practicing. Did that mean I could only receive points from official performances? I don't know the clear criteria.

In any case, just as I'd assumed while practicing, this Park Moondae really had no talent in dancing. Couldn't any average person manage a D for dancing? I concluded that I'd have to invest points in dancing two or three times if I wanted to get anywhere at all.

On the other hand, the physical attractiveness stat gave me hope. Even though I didn't spend any points on it, my grade improved since I paid attention to the way I looked. In other words, I was able to raise my stats independently of the level-up effect shown in the status window. It was promising news.

Finally, there was one more unusual pop-up. A small one next to the status window.

★=
#>< Successful performance! ><#

You deeply impressed the majority of people!

}Draw General Characteristics} ☜ Click!

=★

*Something weird popped out again.*

Still, given the use of words such as "success" and "impression," the nuance of the rewards should be great.

I pretended to scratch my head and subtly pressed the button. Then something quite interesting popped up.

It was a picture of a gray slot machine. The images in the slot were already spinning beside the lever that had been pulled down. But the speed gradually slowed down. Soon enough, I could even check the contents of the slot at a glance. Several of them were yellowish copper, while most were gray.

★=

[
#yStrong hearty#
The Misfortune of Being Humiliated Eraser
Alligator's tears
Sleeping is for the Dead
Look at me!
....
]

=★

"I mean, it's nice that I can guess what each trait is for from the slogans, but... aren't they a little cheesy?"

I looked at the slot machine with no expectations. Anyway, it looked like it was going to stop soon.

With several dramatic "poof" sounds, pieces of confetti exploded from the slot machine. It finally stopped on a yellowish copper slot.

★-
\#><✦ Characteristic: 'Sleep is for the Dead (D)' obtained!><#

When activated, you will not feel tired even without having slept.

Comes with an additional 30% increase in the rate of acquiring experience points from 00:01 a.m. to 4:00 a.m.

Lasts for 1 week (single-use only).
-★

"....!"

This would absolutely come in handy in the future. The name is— a bit like that, though.

In any case, it was literally a random prize system—an essential feature of any luck-based, doomed-to-fail game. Things were getting fun.

I noticed that, at the very bottom of my status window, "Sleep is for the Dead (Deactivated)" had been added to the trait category. Let's think about when it's the best time to use it.

"Participant Kang Minjo... 56th place!"

The evaluations continued, and aside from the potential druggie next to me, I hadn't seen any more of the winning members yet.

I stood there and watched as the next participant walked onto the stage.

He had long limbs and a slender build. He was alone, so I could tell he didn't have connections with any of the other trainees.

The contestants already seated by their rank started whispering to one another. Of course, this was not because the participant came up alone. It was because he was incredibly good-looking. I could even hear the judges openly admiring from below.

"He's got such big, beautiful eyes, like a deer."

Even I could tell that, based on looks alone, this guy didn't have to worry about not being able to debut. Frankly, he needn't have bothered going on a survival show. I'm pretty sure he had a future in the entertainment business no matter what.

However, when I checked the name tag on his chest, it was a name I had never heard of before.

[Seon Ahyeon]

If he hadn't been able to debut despite being so good-looking, he must have been incredibly untalented. He was more handsome than most of the idols I took fancams of.

Well, in terms of the status window—all his stats excluding physical attractiveness would have to be between D and F.

*If only I could see other people's status windows, I'd know for sure.*

Much to my surprise, the moment I had that thought, a window appeared next to the contestant.

"....!"

Does this mean I can see the status windows of other people as well?

★-
[Name: Seon Ahyeon]

Vocal: B- (A)

Dance: A (EX)

Visual: A+ (S+)

Talent: B (A+)

Characteristics: Fortitude (Deactivated)

!Status Abnormality: Lack of self-esteem.
-★

Those were some impressive stats. With those grades, it'd be a breeze for him to rank within the Top 10 in this round. Did the letters inside the parentheses indicate his maximum potential? If so, he has a promising future ahead of him.

However, it was the last category that stood out to me the most.

[Status Abnormality: Lack of self-esteem.]

Seriously? With those looks? It'd make more sense if his status abnormality was "arrogance."

Regardless, it was a huge advantage to be able to check the other contestants' stats. If things didn't work out, I might even be able to make a living working at an entertainment agency as a casting manager.

While I was looking over the menu with great interest, I heard the voice of the MC.

"All right, could you please introduce yourself?"

Seon Ahyeon picked up the microphone with trembling hands.

"U-uh... H-hello..."

"....!"

The kid wasn't stuttering merely from stage fright. He was showing clear symptoms of a speech disorder.

In addition, I felt that he was very conscious of his speech disorder. Was it right for me to mention that I could feel his anxiety and embarrassment so heavily?

That explains it. I immediately understood Seon Ahyeon's stats better.

But with his good looks and talent, the production team could have made a dramatic, moving story about his speech disorder. So why wasn't he able to debut in my previous life? Is it because of the "lack of self-esteem" status abnormality? If I can find out more detail—.

★!
#><[Lack of self-esteem]><#

Despises himself.

All stats decrease by two levels.
!★

"....!"

Suddenly, a pop-up came up again. I wasn't expecting to be able to check the details just by thinking about it.

*It's very useful.*

Maybe I should check my own status abnormality to see how many days I have left to live.

"Ahyeon, by any chance...do you have trouble speaking?"

"I, I... Uh, when I was a kid, I had an accident...."

"Ah..."

On the stage, the storytelling was just getting started.

To be more precise, Seon Ahyeon seems to be trying to explain his condition, while the production team seems to be trying to turn it into a story.

Fake responses are also erupting from the participants surrounding me.

In particular, the guy who shook his head and had a sad expression on his face seems to have been used as a reaction cut.

They will even include these lines in their final result.

"What to do...."

If the editors didn't incorporate his response carefully, he'd receive backlash from viewers for being insensitive. I stared straight ahead without any change in my expression.

After saying a few empty words of encouragement, the judges gently asked him to perform.

"Well then, Ahyeon, let's see what you have for us."

"Y, yes..."

His performance wasn't that bad. Overall, it was a stage the judges would view favorably. Once again, the information in the status window was correct—his performance was weaker than his actual abilities.

"Seon Ahyeon ranks in 18th place!"

This is what happened when you didn't have a steady mindset and a good strategy. He's ranked lower than me even with that status window.

The judges' evaluation was good overall. Either the judges thought he was talented, or they just didn't want to voice any negative remarks.

"Good job~"

"I'll look forward to it."

The judges each offered a heartwarming compliment or two. Some people even raised their thumbs.

But Seon Ahyeon looked dejected and hung his head in disappointment. Following the MC's instructions, he left the stage and sat down next to me as if nothing had happened.

"...."

I think I should say hello—.

I didn't want to make it seem like I was snubbing him. But it was too much of a hassle to strike up a conversation, so I just nodded to him in greeting.

Seon Ahyeon jolted in surprise and nodded awkwardly several times.

"There are 5 participants who will be judged next! Choi Jinsoo-gun, Hongseong-gun—"

I counted the number of remaining participants in my mind and sighed softly.

There's a long way to go.
