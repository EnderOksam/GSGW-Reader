# Chapter 156 - The National Pride Strategy

Ever since acquiring the characteristic 'Catch-All Ears', which heightened my ability to recognize good songs, I'd hardly ever misjudged one.

In that sense, the chorus of this song sent by Gold 1 as their title track this time... It was really good.

I wasn't talking about perfection or art. It was a song that stayed in your head even if you just listened to it once.

It was extremely addictive.

*Damn it.*

Of course, there was still no possibility that TeSTAR would be pushed back in the rankings.

But, the problem was, some people would start talking nonsense.

*I'm not in a situation where I can tolerate that right now.*

This was the atmosphere surrounding TeSTAR's latest album, *Tiger Hunter*[1]{T/N: Chackhogapsa = Military who were selected and deployed to catch tigers in the Dynasty Joseon.}.

First of all, the critic's rating on the most famous music review site in Korea was 4 stars.

It was the first idol album that year to earn four stars from the country's top review site.

$★

The only reservations in the second half of the review were that the album was 'obsessively well-made' and that the title track followed a familiar structure.

The praises by critics were more than I had expected. Of course, it helped the group's status.

In addition, there were clear responses from overseas.

With clear results shown in music video views, album sales, global music sites, etc, we received love calls from abroad.

Right now, we were scheduled to perform on a semi-major English-speaking talk show and a Japanese morning broadcast.

Even though the song wasn't easy to listen to because its purpose was to make an extreme performance, the domestic music score was good.

*We are in 4th place right now.*

The company said official fandom sign-ups were climbing far faster than with our previous album, perhaps the clearest evidence of our success.

So, to sum up all these indicators, this activity was a great opportunity to fly to the top-tier stability zone by capturing both commerciality and artistic value.

In short, we were about to open the door to the beginning of a crazy heyday.

But here, Gold 1's group, Golden Age, collectively bit TeSTAR and hung onto us, and they even came out with a filthy good song.

*Naturally, noises like 'Golden Age's song is better, but TeSTAR won thanks to the fans' will come out.*

In short, the fans’ and the public’s perceptions would be negatively affected.

I could already see the risk that TeSTAR's image reputation as a 'Popular group that is doing well in all areas' would be reduced to 'A male idol group with a big fandom'.

We were about to enter the top-tier category, so I refused to get caught here.

*We have to go on a large-scale tour.*

In order to avoid sudden death due to status abnormality, TeSTAR shouldn't lose their momentum until the next comeback.

*I have to make the public continue to appreciate TeSTAR's new album.*

After thinking about it, I quickly came to a conclusion.

*Gukkpong[2]{Editor's Note: Guk-ppong is Korean slang for an intoxicating surge of national pride, sometimes used ironically or critically. Here Moondae means promoting Korean traditional music to kindle audience pride; the translator had considered 'Overproud Nationalists' but retained the Korean term.} is the only answer.*

We needed to make audiences in Korea feel a sense of pride in TeSTAR's connection to Korean traditional culture.

But we had to be subtle about it, without making the appeal feel forced or excessive.
