# Chapter 329 - Independent Label, IV

"Congratulations."

"Congratulations."

In January, I bowed my head and greeted a senior I met in the hallway of the Gold Disc Awards. 

It was one of the last award ceremonies to wrap up the year's award season, but unfortunately, we did not receive the *daesang* here.

*There was no possibility in the first place.*

It was because there were only two categories, physical albums and digital music. There was no artist award based on a combination of criteria.

Again, the result was obvious.

*Youngrin and VTIC each won a *daesang*.*

Yes... This bastard who'd just greeted me was a member of the latter.

Cheongryeo smiled brightly.

"Best Group and Best Performance... You've won a lot of good awards. The fans must be thrilled."

No matter how much he sugarcoated it, he meant that no matter how hard our fans tried, sales determined the results, so we hadn't won a *daesang*.

"Yes. I think it's because we worked hard and the fans voted passionately. Though it can't compare to sunbaenim's album *daesang*."

I was pointing out that we'd beaten them in the voting, at least.

"Haha."

"Hahaha."

I had a good laugh with him. The staff in the hallway passed by, watching with warm eyes.

Cheongryeo stopped laughing, changed his expression, and asked.

"Hmm... You said you had something to tell me the next time we met. Have you sorted out your thoughts?"

Oh, yes.

I'd agreed to talk to him again about what I'd experienced while in the body of 'Ryu Gunwoo'.

*Both of us were busy during the season, so we didn't really have a chance.*

I ended our little staring contest and got straight to the point.

"I found the person I was looking for. He's going to help us with what we were originally trying to do."

"Aha."

Cheongryeo's eyes narrowed.

"It would be interesting to meet him together sometime."

"When the opportunity arises."

He seemed to understand that I'd found 'Keundal' and that he would help us deal with the system.

*That's probably enough for now.*

It wasn't something to talk about in an open hallway. I just needed to make sure this bastard would cooperate with us in the future.

Cheongryeo smiled.

"I wonder how he'll help. Are there any other problems?"

"Problems?"

"Yes. I heard you're setting up an independent label. It's more complicated than it seems, you know."

Judging by the way he spoke, it seemed that he already knew everything about the conflict between the T1 headquarters and the company.

"It's okay. It's going well."

"Is that so?"

"Yes. We're just focusing on our plans for next year and how to get the best results from our promotions."

In the end, the goal was to clear the 'K-pop record-breaking' mission.

Of course, it would be ideal if we could snatch the album *daesang* from those bastards too.

I shrugged.

Of course, if it was this bastard, he'd say something like, 'Oh... It's going to be difficult,' and try to provoke me again.

I looked at him, preparing to retaliate.

However, Cheongryeo only muttered with a normal expression.

"Hmm... Is that so? Good luck."

"......"

I stared at him.

"Well... It might be a little easier this year."

What?

"Moo-Moondae!"

I heard a voice calling me from behind, but I stared at Cheongryeo.

But he just shrugged his shoulders and turned around.

"...Easier?"

"Didn't you say that you were going to work hard? Let's talk again when we have the time."

That was it.

*What kind of ulterior motive?*

Wasn't this the guy who'd been like a poisonous snake whenever we talked about our groups' records throughout the year? It wasn't like he lacked confidence in next year's results.

Strangely... he seemed bored.

Why was this bastard acting like that when he still had half a year before enlisting? I stared uneasily at the back of his head before looking away.

*Should I look for some shady VTIC rumors online?*

I didn't know if it was a good thing, a bad thing, or if I was just being overly sensitive.

"Sorry, I was saying goodbye."

"Yeah. I-it's okay."

Seon Ahyeon, who approached, looked at Cheongryeo with a slightly nervous face, but soon looked back at me and smiled.

"W-we met Youngrin sunbaenim and were saying hello...!"

"Really? Let's go."

"Yes!"

I followed him down the hallway.

As we turned the corner, another *daesang* winner was smiling. 

"Moondae-ssi."

"Sunbaenim."

At the awards ceremony, which was split into digital music and album days, Youngrin had received the digital music *daesang* the day before.

However, she also received a main award in today's album category, so she attended both days and even performed onstage.

*People usually only attend on the day they receive a *daesang*.*

It was a rare display of sincerity. 

"Now it really is a new year. I look forward to seeing great things from TeSTAR this year too."

"We will do our best."

Youngrin smiled lightly, but the dancers behind her began to fidget and make a little fuss. *What?*

Perhaps noticing my gaze, Youngrin introduced the dancers.

"They're debuting from my agency next year. Say hello, guys."

"Hello!!"

They seemed to have been given the chance to perform as dancers to gain stage experience. It was common practice.

Seon Ahyeon looked at them with a happy face and spoke to Youngrin in a small voice.

"You must care about these hubaenims very, very much..."

"Does it look like that? That's right. I'm in charge of producing their music."

"Sunbaenim..." the trainees said.

The trainees, Youngrin, and Seon Ahyeon were creating a very warm atmosphere. Of course, I had no intention of joining.

*If Youngrin is producing for them, their music should be excellent, at least.*

I filed away the information about the prospective rookies and parted ways with Youngrin.

Returning to the lobby, Seon Ahyeon fiddled with his hands as if reminiscing.

"We, too, have already become sunbaenims..."

"Yes. We are no longer rookies."

With the new year, TeSTAR was already in its fifth year.

Since it had been three and a half years since our debut, we could say that we had taken off our rookie mask. Now we had to keep an eye on those who were slowly coming up. 

*We have two direct juniors.*

I thought of two groups of juniors from ⟨Idol Inc.⟩.

However, depending on what we did from now on, we might or might not stay in the same agency as our 'direct juniors'.

Would we escape this agency and become a direct subsidiary of T1? Or would we stay and whip them into shape?

"Hmm."

In fact, it was as if the conclusion was reached the moment Kim Raebin's opinion came out.

*So many members are attached to the people of the planning team and the A&R Team.*

The people calling the shots at the company were infuriating, but of course, the members didn't want the staff we'd grown close to to suffer.

All the more so given how close they were in age.

As awards season wore on, I could see the kids considering the latter.

It was the same with Bae Sejin. He seemed to have considered it from various angles and finally came to this conclusion during last night's practice.

– If it has the same effect, it might be right to stay. I guess that would be a better lesson.

It was the moment we'd more or less reached a unanimous agreement. I smiled and opened my mouth.

"Seon Ahyeon."

"Yes?"

"If we stay here, who do you want to shut up first?"

"Sh-shut up...?"

He looked bewildered. However, Seon Ahyeon soon regained his composure.

"R-rather than shutting up... I wish they could be more considerate of the people they work with and not bully them... Us, the other staff, and the idols..."

"Okay."

Stop mentally harassing the people who work for the agency. Treat them with respect.

That was right... That was a good reason to stay.

I grinned.

"That sounds good."

"Th-thank you...!"

A few days later, we won the only *daesang* at the last awards ceremony, the 'Korean Gayo *Daesang*', and ended the awards season on a high note. 

And the moment we entered a short break before the tour, the company finally began to back down. 

Of course, I doubted they really wanted to back down.

* * *

*These vulgar bastards...*

The general manager was sitting at his desk, shouting inwardly a profane curse that he did not normally say.

There was only one reason.

This was because those tantara bastards[1]{T/N: In case you forgot, tantara/딴따라 is a derogatory term used to belittle celebrities, idols, etc.} who didn't even have the blood of a human in their veins were about to ruin his spotless record at the most critical point in his career.

*Do they think they're doing so well thanks to their own efforts? It's all thanks to the product quality created by the company!*

Didn't they even receive the *daesang* thanks to the company's extensive financial support and quick decision-making?

He was thinking of adding that *daesang* performance nicely to his portfolio and stepping down for a new business!

After that, couldn't they just take care of the label or whatever?

He'd only wanted to hand things over smoothly to his successor without anything happening during his tenure...

*These fucking things! I can't stand it because they don't know patience!*

They'd made such a fuss over winning their first *daesang*, and now he'd been backed into a corner.

He was especially annoyed by the guy who'd announced it in his acceptance speech.

Park Moondae.

*A punk who only knows how to use tricks.*

He should have known from the time he started playing tricks with additional contracts.

It was clear that he could only resort to such low and vulgar means because he hadn't gone to school properly or learned anything, inciting those around him to join in a witch hunt.

The general manager huffed and put his hand on the Bluetooth keyboard.

Anyway, now that the stupid board members had antagonized the parent company, the cards he could play were limited.

But at the same time, hope flickered precisely because of that. 

*If I settle it here, it will be a blessing in disguise.*

This was because TeSTAR was still dragging on the offer from T1 Headquarters instead of shouting OK.

Clearly, there was fear and reluctance about making a fresh start among themselves. 

And in this situation, if he could get them to set up their label as a subsidiary of the agency, he could still look like a competent general manager in the financial statements when he left...

*I'll go on the offensive now!*

He set aside his usual professional English vocabulary and laid-back tone.

Instead, he prepared a letter to send to TeSTAR in a serious and candid tone.

★$

*But there are numerous communication errors and misunderstandings.*

The four-step apology of admitting fault, apologizing, making subtle excuses, and appealing to their emotions worked everywhere. His fingers flew over the keyboard.

★$
Of course, if you leave, this agency will take a big hit. I know it can't be helped, but I'd like you to remember that the staff here who worked for you had no bad intentions...
$★

He even emphasized the working staff. 

As a person with a lot of experience in various businesses, the general manager identified TeSTAR's weakest psychological point with surprising accuracy. 

And as if in response to his brilliant letter, a reply came the very next day.

"...!"

It was sent by TeSTAR's leader, Ryu Chungwoo.

★$
We read your message carefully.
$★

The letter that began like that conveyed TeSTAR's discomfort, politely rebutted his points, and carried a slightly gloomy tone.

★$
You had no intention of honoring the contract, so how can we trust you anymore? We just don't know anymore.
$★

*That's right!*

These guys really were conflicted!

The reply contained no sarcasm or personal attacks, so there was still room to persuade them.

He wrote another letter, burning with enthusiasm.

*It's not that I didn't want to keep the contents of the contract. It's embarrassing, but it's the end of the year, so the company doesn't have enough manpower...*

A few more letters were exchanged like that. 

There was also an attempt to meet in person, but TeSTAR canceled the meeting on the pretext of scheduling.

The general manager keenly caught that point.

*They're wavering.*

Originally, when people talked and met face-to-face, they were bound to tell deeper stories. 

They were afraid they'd be persuaded!

*I'm really almost there.*

He analyzed TeSTAR's writings and read their desires remarkably well. 

And he threw bait that would satisfy them in his next letter.

*More staff for the independent label, okay.*

*This... I can approach it by proposing unified management through the Management Office.*

*If I talk about organizing their direct A&R Team, they'll have no choice but to come over.*

Every time he sent a letter like that, TeSTAR's replies became more and more emotional. 

*Good!*

Pass. Another pass. There was a sense of pleasure, as if he were clearing one stage of a game. 

And finally...

In the fifth letter, TeSTAR accepted the meeting.

"Hoo...!!"

A feat accomplished in just one week. 

The general manager prepared for the meeting with a clear mind, sending a message to the board of directors, saying, 'The progress is positive.'

*The moment they show up, my problems are over.*

Last time, the problem had been that guy who'd been in a coma, stooping so low as to threaten legal action and all that.

To him, coaxing guys in their early and mid-twenties wasn't even difficult. 

"Hello."

And on that day, only the leader, Ryu Chungwoo, was present.

"I consulted with the members, but they said it would be better for me, as the leader, to be seen as the representative, so only I came out."

"Ah, I see."

It was better.

*It's the best setting.*

Chungwoo was a former athlete, so the general manager didn't consider him particularly studious. He'd already heard from the staff that Chungwoo had a gentle personality and didn't like making things uncomfortable.

A predictable person like this was the easiest to persuade, even when alone. 

The general manager had considered the possibility that the madman would show up too.

*Perhaps those guys kept him from coming on purpose.*

He was overly impulsive and lacked tact, so he was not allowed to come to a place where he needed to have a serious conversation. 

*In other words, this place was considered important.*

It seemed that the finish line was already in front of him. The general manager started the conversation by offering coffee to Ryu Chungwoo, who put down his umbrella.

"A latte?"

"Thank you."

After a little small talk, the main topic came up in a relaxed atmosphere.

Ryu Chungwoo spoke in a low voice as he picked up his coffee cup.

"We're having trouble deciding what to do. To be exact... Most of the members want to leave the agency, but some have changed their minds a little after exchanging letters with the general manager."

"Is that so? I'm curious about what's bothering them most."

"That..."

Ryu Chungwoo seemed conflicted, but sighed several times.

*Hurry up and say it!*

And it was only when the general manager was growing impatient that Chungwoo spoke again.

"Hoo, I guess there are still trust issues."

"Trust issues?"

"Yes. We're lucky enough to be able to set up a label, but if it were other idols, they wouldn't have been able to... That's the doubt we have."

Ryu Chungwoo smiled bitterly.

"I'm sorry to say this, but one of the members thinks the general manager is lying to us. He's very interested in idols' rights... He's pretty outspoken, too."

It was the idea of a bastard with too many ideals. 

*These guys make billions, and yet they're listening to this rubbish. Ha!*

Although he thought it was utter nonsense, the general manager racked his brain for an appropriate answer.

It seemed they wanted him to show them some kind of ethical gesture that would satisfy their sense of morality rather than concern for profit. 

*Other idols can't.*

"...!"

At that moment, an amazing idea popped into the general manager's head. 'With this, I don't have to give anything away!'

He suppressed a smile and opened his mouth with a grave expression.

"Then..."

* * *

"I got it."

"Ohhh!"

"It was raining, but you worked really hard!"

Ryu Chungwoo returned from the meeting with a smile and shared a photo he'd taken of the document in the group chat.

★:
-RCW: T1 Stars artists can sign contracts with affiliated labels when they renew their contracts, provided this is agreed upon beforehand.
:★

It was the guarantee that 'anyone can go to the label when renewing their contract.' 

"They really gave it to us."

"I know."

Ryu Chungwoo shrugged.

"Actually, I didn't expect him to concede so much in those letters, either."

"That's right!"

"Moondae-hyung is a wizard!"

What was he talking about? We'd written the letters together.

"It's because we have the upper hand. We just made things a little easier for them."

The agency stood to lose a great deal if we left. If they could keep us by offering better terms, of course they'd do it.

We just wanted the company to make the offers voluntarily instead of having to demand them ourselves.

*So they can't say anything else later.*

But the guys next to me giggled.

"Oh~ humble~"

Forget it. I shook my head and laughed.

"Chungwoo-hyung is even more amazing. You got almost everything we wanted, down to the letter."

"Well. They just gave it to me on their own...?"

However, Bae Sejin also gave him a thumbs-up.

"...It's great!"

"Um, thank you."

It seemed that he had been greatly impressed by the change in company guidelines. He had 'We did it!' written on his face.

*It's amazing.*

Being able to choose an affiliated label without question when renewing a contract? That wasn't a condition most large agencies offered.

*It must have been possible because of the unique structure of this agency.*

After all, the agency was set up to take in the hottest group from each season of the audition program.

The board of directors must have been okay with it because they thought TeSTAR's longevity was a special case, and that they could use the later groups for five years and replace them once their popularity waned.

Even if those groups went to a label, the agency would still get the sales revenue anyway.

*Stupid bastards.*

Conversely, who would the groups that were confident they'd still do well after five years choose?

It would be the label that the most popular, top-tier idols belonged to.

*And if the idols create a good working atmosphere, the working staff will come on board too.*

In the end, it was a matter of influence. I closed the document on my screen.

*I'll poach them one by one.*

If it went on, eventually the label would grow, and the agency would remain a shell in name only. 

I finally smiled and opened my mouth.

"It's nice that it ended well. Everyone has worked hard."

"You worked hard!"

"Ah~ Let's quickly decide on a label name!"

High fives and laughter filled the living room. It was heartwarming. 

Now, we could do something fun with this label... But we had something to do before that.

"Before that, we have something to do first."

"Yes?"

"We need to prepare for the next album."

Kim Raebin tilted his head.

"Weren't we going to work on it during the tour...?"

"That's right."

I smiled and crossed my arms.

"Speaking of that plan, during our tour we spent one night in the same room once a week, right?"

Cha Eugene's eyes lit up. I think he remembered it right away because it was a tradition that came about because of his escape.

"That's right! Yes!"

"...Ryu Chungwoo made a good suggestion."

"Haha."

Ryu Chungwoo looked a little proud. Between them, Keun Sejin intervened.

"Oh~ Shall we gather like that this time to prepare for our album? Like composition camp back then?"

"W-wow."

"I am in favor!"

The guys smiled at one another. I also smiled and opened my mouth.

"But I have another suggestion."

"Huh?"

"Not one day a week, but three days a week."

"......"

"I think we can do it because the tour schedule isn't that packed. And one more difference."

"What, what is it?"

I looked at Bae Sejin and laughed.

"We're putting an album together, so we'll have to work ourselves to death."

"...!"

"Those three days a week are going to be a hell camp, not a hobby camp."

*Kwagwawang!!*

Just in time, lightning struck, casting shadows on the faces of those with open mouths.

And that was how I laid out my plan for 'K-pop Hell Camp – TeSTAR Version'.
