# Chapter 302 - ⟨T-Holic's Show Business⟩, II

There were already certain expectations for the TeSTAR episode of ⟨T-Holic's Show Business⟩. The show had become so popular lately that almost everyone had seen a clip of it at least once.

★$
- T-Holic's older guys are going to have so much fun teasing TeSTAR ㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋ

- Ah, I think the kids will be really cute.

- I hope they have fun!
$★

Funny T-Holic, and the fresh, lively reactions of their guests!

The show mainly relied on T-Holic's antics and their guests' reactions for its humor.

At the same time, it skillfully promoted the guests as intended, which was why it was so popular.

It was the ideal setup where both the guests’ fans and T-Holic’s fans could enjoy themselves.

However, this particular episode with TeSTAR... The atmosphere was quite different.

+=
[Generation M gets a taste of Generation Z!]

[Cha Eugene: I'm a tiger!]

[Ha Jintae: I see. Our friend Cha Eugene is a tiger.]

[They don't know how to react to the tiger who was born in '0X.]
=+

T-Holic was flummoxed from the moment the introductions began, and before long, they were filling up the running time with their own reactions instead of the guests'.

★$
- ㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋ

- Crazy, Gen Z TeSTAR is absolutely nuts ㅋㅋㅋ
$★

Clever editing framed everything that happened between them as a generation gap and stitched together every conversation and reaction that could make the audience laugh.

It felt different from the show's usual content, but there was no denying it was funny.

*The TeSTAR kids are really full of personality!*

The graduate student burst into laughter several times while watching the teaser and the beginning of the main episode.

In the meantime, she was proud to see that Moondae seemed to be T-Holic's only respite so far.

+=
[Park Moondae: In that case, I'll just sing a few measures...]
=+

Park Moondae reined in the two youngest members, who were running wild, and calmly handled everything that needed to be done.

He promoted their tour, mentioned their connection with T-Holic, and even sang a song...

Park Moondae's fans watched with proud, sympathetic smiles on their faces.

★$
- Moondae is such a great member. You can really tell he's older.

- Actually, my child is in the maknae line, but he really seems like a leader at times. ㅠㅠ

- Moon Puppy, you're all grown up
$★

It was the textbook example of an idol leader on a variety show! In the end, the generational differences that had spiraled out of control on screen gradually settled down.

And when the main game began, T-Holic took control of the situation once again.

They won the lip-reading mini-game and chose the easier statement, only to be devastated when they realized that the point was to be persuaded.

+=
[Receive one billion won VS be stranded in a remote location]

[MC Kim Maknae: Now, make good use of your opponent's statement and explain clearly why you were persuaded~]
=+

It was full of the funny twists you'd expect from a variety show!

"Ahahaha!!"

It had been hilarious when Cha Eugene and Kim Raebin lost the mini-game, but as it turned out, losing had worked in their favor!

On screen, Cha Eugene was giving his members a thumbs-up.

+=
[Cha Eugene: We did good! Kim Raebin! Good thing we lost!]

[Kim Raebin: We lost because you mouthed the words too quickly and inaccurately. In fact, you're the one who gave us the advantage!]

[Uh... That's a compliment, right?]
=+

Those little reactions were even funnier when combined with T-Holic's despair.

And Park Moondae neatly wrapped up the situation once again.

+=
[Park Moondae: You both did great. Let's try to win from here on out.]

[Cha Eugene: Go TeSTAR!]

[Kim Raebin: Yes!]
=+

★$
- He looks like a kindergarten teacher ㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋ

- If you showed just this clip, people who didn't know him would think it was from a sports match.

- Park Moondae is pretty mature.
$★

Viewers laughed in the channel app's comments and praised the group's chemistry.

At the same time, they were excited to see how the usually calm Park Moondae would react to T-Holic's absurd arguments.

★$
- Is the Tibetan Fox finally coming out?

- He's pretty smart. I'm looking forward to seeing how he reacts.

- The more rational the guest, the more confused they get. I've seen it happen before ㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋ
$★

Their expectations seemed about to be fulfilled as T-Holic continued the game in their usual playful style.

That was until Park Moondae showed his true colors.

+=
[Park Moondae: I think our team would choose to be stranded over receiving 1 billion won.]
=+

Park Moondae said complete nonsense with a perfectly serious expression.

"...??"

The graduate student wondered for a moment if she'd misheard.

But Park Moondae was building up steam and kept spouting nonsense with a perfectly straight face.

It was a seriousness completely different from T-Holic's brazen humor.

Instead of trying to win, he was passionately arguing against himself!

As if to underscore how serious he was, captions appeared on screen.

+=
[Park Moondae: We can't give up our values over one game.]

#r[Descends into madness 🔥]r#
=+

★$
- Fuck, Park Moondae ㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋ

- So we get Tibetan Moondae's face, but not the way we expected...

- What bullshit is this?
$★

Even the way the other TeSTAR members fell in line felt like a comedy skit. One conversation was all it took to persuade them.

+=
[Wait, why are they so easy to convince?]
=+

Park Moondae's logic was simple and easy to understand.

He was speaking from experience when he said being stranded was more meaningful, so what could anyone say to that?

★$
- He's persuasive, and that pisses me off.

- Okay, being stranded is the best. Always get stranded, definitely get stranded.
$★

At that point, T-Holic got dragged into the argument and started refuting them, completely reversing the usual dynamic.

In fact, TeSTAR's argument should've been easy to refute, but T-Holic hadn't expected such nonsense and reacted in shock instead of coming up with arguments of their own.

+=
[Martin: Viewers! Let's think realistically... How does it make sense to prefer being stranded??]

[Kim Raebin: It makes even more sense if you look at it realistically. As Moondae-hyung said earlier, even if we do get stranded, we won't be far from civilization!]
=+

Then a few clips played on screen, taken from TeSTAR's island-stranding variety show.

+=
[*He speaks from experience*]

[What he received: A hit variety show, the title track for the next album, money, fame, understanding]

#y[☆ Wow, being stranded is actually better than 1 billion! ☆]y#

['Enticing...' – Production team]
=+

The moment the production team gave in, the viewers had no choice but to concede as well.

★$
- The premise of the game is wrong.

- Fuck, the kids must've really enjoyed themselves ㅋㅋㅋㅋ

- Oh, TeSTAR must have had the time of their lives on that island variety show ㅋㅋㅋ

- Isn't it Stockholm Syndrome? They fell in love with being stranded.
$★

Of course, T-Holic couldn't see those clips on the set, so they tried moving on to another argument.

Their main point was, 'But it could be dangerous! Someone could get seriously hurt!' But Park Moondae adopted a sad expression and simply muttered.

+=
[Park Moondae: That's... Wouldn't that be a sad accident, rather than a case of being stranded?]

[Ha Jintae: Being stranded is an accident too!!]
=+

★$
- ㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋ

- The veins on Jintae's neck are bulging ㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋ
$★

T-Holic was supposed to be persuaded, but before they knew it, they were doing their best to lose. It was confusing for them.

And for the producers, too.

The production crew even edited in a spinning image of space full of galaxies and stars, along with shaking captions.

+=
#r%~[Generation Z... terrifying!]~%r#
=+

★$
- Please don't lump us in with them.

- No, Moondae is just being Moondae.

- Moondae of chicken feet and POP☆CON fame isn't losing his touch.
$★

And the graduate student realized.

*Right... Moondae was a four-dimensional character. He wasn't supposed to be predictable.*

His recent cool and sensible image had made her forget, but her idol had danced to 'POP☆CON' during his very first TV appearance...

In the end, T-Holic's last attempt at an argument became rather serious and logical as well.

+=
[Martin: But! That only applies to TeSTAR, not us! Probably not, anyway. We hate being stranded!]
=+

However, Cha Eugene asked brightly.

+=
[Cha Eugene: Sunbaenim, have you ever been stranded before?]

[Martin: N-no...]

[Cha Eugene: Then how would you know if you hate it? You won't know until you try it!]
=+

The opposing team gaped as Park Moondae intertwined his fingers and spoke calmly.

+=
[Park Moondae: Wouldn't it be more reasonable to trust the testimony of juniors in the same field who have experienced it firsthand?]

[Cha Eugene: That's right. Experience is important!]

[Kim Raebin: Wise words.]
=+

★$
- Wise words, my foot
$★

The chat continued to fly by as a giant seal was stamped over the bewildered faces of the T-Holic members.

+=
#><[Words of Persuasion...!]><#
=+

And that was how T-Holic ended up with a hollow victory...

+=
[MC Kim Maknae: Now, it's time to wrap things up~ Everyone, are you ready to explain why you were persuaded?]
=+

T-Holic was forced to declare bullshit with their own mouths.

+=
[Ha Jintae: Being stranded is... Well, we're idols! New experiences offer opportunities for growth and financial gain...]

[Ha Jintae: What I mean is... Uh... It's a better goal for next year than a mere 1 billion won! Ahh! What am I even saying?]

[MC Kim Maknae: Okay~ Very persuasive!]
=+

The moment he said that, the production crew burst into applause, laughing hard.

+=
[Ha Jintae: Don't laugh, you guys!! Is this funny?? Now our group's goal for next year has to be getting stranded!]
=+

Meanwhile, the three TeSTAR members nodded.

+=
[Park Moondae: Although we lost, I think we fought well.]

[Martin: What are you talking about?! You did your best to lose!]

[ㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋ]
=+

The camera zoomed in on each of the TeSTAR members' complacent expressions, like a comedy skit in itself.

"Oh really!!"

The graduate student was on the floor laughing!

Only then did Park Moondae relax his expression a little and start saying how much fun he'd had, making it clear that it had all been for laughs.

Only Kim Raebin looked confused by the shift, his shocked expression drawing one more laugh...

Then came the next segment.

+=
[Win the game, and get free promotion. Lose, and get a penalty.]

[*What penalty will TeSTAR receive*?]
=+

Until now, the penalties had mainly been reenactments of ridiculous memes or hilarious performances.

The graduate student stared at the spinning roulette on the screen with a big smile, her heart racing.

The roulette gradually slowed down...

**[!!!!]**

A shocking penalty was revealed!

***

"Ah~ Today's shooting went well."

"You've worked hard."

"Thank you!"

I bowed to the T-Holic guys alongside the two members.

The moment the cameras were off, these guys immediately became calm.

*So those personalities were just for entertainment?*

Their nonstop back-and-forth vanished, and they went back to acting like coworkers who'd known each other for a long time.

*Maybe that's the secret to their success.*

Just then, one of the guys who'd argued most vehemently during filming spoke to me quietly.

"You're good at talking. Are you interested in variety shows?"

"...?"

Was he trying to recruit me for variety shows? I answered clearly.

"Yes, of course. Mmm, but according to our company's policy, we're focusing more on our albums for now."

"Ah~"

The guy understood at once. Then he took a sip of water and spoke slowly.

"This is my advice as a sunbaenim. It's difficult for a group to stay popular for a long time through music alone. It's a little ironic, but for the group to stay relevant, each member needs to do well in their own right."

"Hmm."

"People get tired of things quickly. They don't stay attached for long."

Was he speaking from experience?

Coincidentally, a similar conversation was going on next to me.

Kim Raebin was carefully holding out a T-Holic album he'd brought with him to get signed.

"Thank you, sunbaenim!"

"Ah~ Don't mention it. Do you need anything else?"

"W-well, then! I would be grateful if you could give us some advice on how you've managed to keep your group together for so long!"

Kim Raebin's eyes sparkled, and the T-Holic member scratched his head.

"Hmm. Not expecting too much from each other, maybe?"

"I'm sorry?"

"We didn't become friends because we wanted to be friends. We became friends because we're in the same group. Things are a little easier if you don't forget that."

That was some serious advice.

*Is he saying that it's important to accept each other as business partners as well as friends?*

Seeing Kim Raebin bickering with Cha Eugene, he seemed to have a lot on his mind.

Even the T-Holic member himself looked a little taken aback, probably not having expected to give such serious advice. It must've been because of Kim Raebin's earnest tone.

"I see!"

Kim Raebin interpreted this in his own way.

"That's just like a family. You can't choose your own family."

"...??"

"Even though we didn't meet for the sake of friendship, I believe we can still become very good friends!"

"Uh... Right. I wish you the best of luck!"

The T-Holic guy looked puzzled, but didn't try to argue. They'd probably seen their fair share of peculiar characters.

*...Funny guy.*

I grinned and looked back at the guy who seemed to have been making me an offer about variety shows.

"But I'm still going to focus on group activities for the time being."

The guy shrugged.

"Okay. If that's your company's policy, it can't be helped."

After wrapping up the conversation politely, I approached Kim Raebin, who'd just put away his new autograph.

"Do you think you can prepare the penalty within three days?"

"Yes. No problem."

Kim Raebin smiled brightly.

This time, the penalty involved something Generation M had loved as children...

A theme song from an anime.
