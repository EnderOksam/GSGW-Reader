“Finally.”

The grad student, who had been released from the lab after two days of staying up all night, staggered past the front door. Her dog was enjoying a nap in the living room.

But she couldn’t sleep.

*I have to watch TeSTAR’s music video.*

Even though it was released at midnight yesterday, she still hadn’t seen it.

Thanks to this, all she knew about the song came from the teaser video she’d seen a few days earlier.

That one didn’t even reveal much because it was just a combination of short scenes similar to concept photos that were tied to one melody.

Thanks to that, she was really curious about the music video, but...

It was late anyway, so she clenched her teeth and rushed home with the thought of watching it on a big screen.

As soon as the grad student washed her face with cold water, she immediately sat down in front of the computer. And with a pounding heart, she entered WeTube.

Without further ado, as soon as she entered the Trending tab, the title that she had been waiting for appeared at the top.

★$
[TeSTAR ‘Airplane’ Official MV]
$★

“...Whoa!!!”

She could finally see it. Finally!! She clicked on the music video right away, without even looking at the thumbnail.

The first thing that appeared was the white floor of a school corridor, floating in the blue sky.

The fantastic screen, like an abstract painting, strangely reminded her of the previous Magical Boys concept.

But before she could take a good look at it, the cut suddenly changed to a pitch-black hallway that lined up perfectly with the previous one.

*Huh?*

The black corridor was dripping with water from all sides. Green graffiti drawn with glow-in-the-dark paint and flickering emergency lights were distorted by running water and flashed strangely.

And at that moment, someone appeared at the end of the hallway.

“...!”

A dark figure hurried forward with one hand on the wall.

The moment she thought she caught a glimpse of who it was.

The black figure suddenly outstretched his palm and covered the camera.

[...]

The screen turned black. And...

The strumming of an acoustic guitar began to resound.

“...?!”

It was an unexpected, frighteningly clear and wistful sound.

And the palm moved away from the camera.

A clear blue sky and a sky full of white clouds. Beneath it was an old school.

But it didn’t look run-down.

The small school, nestled between the mountains and the trees like a painting, looked like something out of a romantic teen novel.

And the next moment.

A boy was standing in front of the school gate.

[...]

With his back to the camera, the boy seemed to hesitate for a moment, but he soon grabbed the gate’s handle.

The moment the gate slowly started to open.

*Without leaving a single trace.* ♫

♪ *I’m opening the window now*

*I’m going to send it to the sky.*

♪ *Tang Tang! Drdrdrdrdr!* ♫

Each of the boys inside looked back at the camera with their eyes wide open.

TeSTAR took classes at a small rural school and enjoyed everyday life.

The music video’s mix of youth and nostalgia had already won the graduate student’s heart, but this was not the end.
