# Chapter 206 - Steampunk Spring

TeSTAR's teaser video began with a steam locomotive speeding through a desolate field.

*Chooo!*

Steam billowed from the brass-colored train as its creaking cogwheels turned and it approached the screen from a diagonal angle.

Then the camera moved through the train’s window as if being sucked inside.

**[...]**

**[...]**

The cabin incorporated an antique, mid-Joseon-style decoration.

Inside it, two passengers seated across from each other were each wearing black and white coat-like dopo[1]{T/N: Dopo = a hanbok's long outer coat for men with wide sleeves.} garments.

Underneath the outer garments, modern-looking clothes, completed with leather straps and buckles, could be seen.

Suddenly, the camera captured the side profile of the one wearing the white gown. Seon Ahyeon, with his hair neatly combed back, was looking down.

And across him.

A figure was reading a newspaper with the headline *'Shocking news! Yokai[2]{T/N: Yokai, better known as yokai in Japanese, are supernatural spirits or entities born from the energy of nature. They can be playful, peaceful, or evil towards humans. They are described as 'demons' in the Western sense but are not literally that. Ex. nine-tailed fox, Hanako-san, kappa.} are being issued citizenship.'* Then he lowered the paper.

It was the silver-haired Park Moondae.

Park Moondae, without expression, raised his head and stared at the camera.

At that moment, the music started.

♪♩-♪♬ ♩ ♩ ♩

As the rhythmic drums and the tinkling of wind chimes lent the scenes a peculiar feel of a historical drama, the video quickly began to pass through the cut scenes.

There was a brief glimpse of Lee Sejin wearing a monocle and flicking a gold coin, soon followed by Kim Raebin in a suit carrying a gas lamp in front of a coal mine.

Then came Bae Sejin with light brown hair, peering into the peculiar-looking brass analog gauge.

And the moment he looked down out the window, the camera followed Bae Sejin's gaze, revealing the train as it entered a strange city.

The city, with its disordered mix of traditional architecture and symbols of the Industrial Revolution, was briefly shown before the focus shifted to the train running above the buildings.

And the next moment.

The train derailed.

*Creaaak!*

As it crashed into the building, roof tiles came barraging down. Clouds of golden dust and debris flew.

A pair of feet in military boots emerged from the debris.

*Thud!*

The figure kicked off the debris and hung onto the side of the train. Metallic badges attached to the front of the outfit sparkled.

**[Woohoo!]**

Debris scattered behind the brightly smiling figure of Cha Eugene, wearing a refashioned military uniform while clinging to the train.

Then the camera followed the oldest-looking among the many wanted posters spilling from the train.

Suddenly, the camera, that has been showing the scene, shook as if it had been externally hit, and then fell.

The instrumentals cut out as if they had broken.

Ryu Chungwoo, against the shaky dark background and a musket over his shoulder, looked down at the camera with his chin lifted.

Like in the poster, his eyes shone bright blue.
