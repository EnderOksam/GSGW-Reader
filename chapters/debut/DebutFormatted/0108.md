# Chapter 108 - First Comeback, II

The stage was still dark.

On the stage without lights, a subtle and heavy bass sound resounded as if a dull machine was moving.

%%*DOON DOON DOON DOON*%%

%%*DOON DOON DOON DOON*%%

Most of the fans had already listened to it while streaming, but the power when listening to it live was different.

Tension was created at a faster tempo than expected.

And on top of that, an electric sound was plugged in.

@ll@*Dvvvviiiiii-!*@ll@

It was the thunderous riff melody that appeared in the trailer.

As if surprised by thunder, the lights came on the stage like lightning.

A stage set resembling a back alley in a sci-fi game was revealed under the pale lighting that didn't have any shade of yellow. However, school props such as desks and blackboards were placed here and there and looked as though they were being thrown away.

TeSTAR turned back and looked in different directions.

Cha Eugene slowly turned his head and faced forward.

$p
♪ *Do not trust it*\
*There is no justice*\
*Oooohh—.* ♫
p$

The moment the low-pitched rap was over, the slashing riff melody disappeared.

And on the spot, a moombahton[1]{T/N: Moombahton: electronic dance music genre.} unfolded.

The first dance break had an insistent rhythm without being especially bouncy.

It was a group dance centered on Cha Eugene, with a slightly mechanical feel.

And the moment Cha Eugene disappeared behind the formation, the song began.

$p
♪ *No amount of looking back*\
*Umm*\
*Can tell me what happened*\
*Target Target Target*\
*GOT IT*\
*Make a run for it, grab it* ♫
p$

It was a completely different vocal melody from the song in the trailer.

She didn't know, but this b-side track, 'Better Me', seemed to have only sampled the accompaniment from the song in the trailer.

However, this was a trendy dance song that seemed much more befitting of an idol.

Thanks to this, TeSTAR on the stage was almost flying.

It was a composition that made her wonder if there was room to breathe.

$p
♪ *This clear feeling.*\
*(I can't erase it, I can't let go)*\
*I know even now.*\
*(I got that CODE)* ♫
p$

The moment they entered the pre-chorus, the voices of Kim Raebin and Lee Sejin intertwined and spread.

The two parted ways after showing a violent pair of dance moves that seemed to capture each other.

And in the second part of the pre-chorus.

Park Moondae came out, and suddenly, the electric sound of the trailer returned.

And lyrics similar to the trailer came out.

$p
♪ *I will never die*

*Like I did before*

*I'm gonna keep ME*

#^#*Alive-!*#^#♫
p$

The high notes rose flawlessly.

The trailer and vocal melody were completely different, but the level of difficulty was indisputable.

And Park Moondae sang this high note with a dynamic choreography that came out by intertwining with other members.

In a slightly gloomy mood at that.

*Wow...*

Kim Raebin's fan forgot to cheer and admired it as if she was watching a competition program, but soon she came back to her senses and focused on the stage.

Finally, Kim Raebin came out in the chorus!

Kim Raebin sang the chorus in a slightly melancholy mood with glaring eyes.

$p
♪ *BUT.*\
*It's just confusing*\
*I don't know when it started*\
*I'm just confused*\
*Umm*\
*Where is this taking me?* ♫
p$

The chorus returned to moombahton and was tightly woven with low-pitched vocals and bright beats.

Then Ryu Chungwoo came out and took over the next chorus.

$p
♪ *Could be confusing*\
*What do I really want?*\
*Maybe I haven't figured it out yet*\
*Umm*\
*What am I supposed to do?* ♫
p$

The lyrics and atmosphere seemed to wander, but it was somehow meaningful and abstract.

Thanks to the combination of the stage, choreography, and costumes, the stage looked as though they were wandering in a dystopian worldview, not reality.

Thanks to this, neon signs and devices that could be distracting, on the contrary, saved the slightly melancholy taste at the center of the intense song.

$p
♪ *Take me and get that might* ♫
p$

As soon as the melodious chorus ended, the choreography for the second verse with Cha Eugene's sharp rapping became more intense and complex.

$p
♪ *This distant feeling*
#f#(*I want to erase it, I wanna let go*)#f#

*But I know*
#f#(*I got that CODE*)#f# ♫
p$

Elements continued to be added to the accompaniment, and the beat became more intense as if it were climbing endlessly until it was about to burst.

And the moment the chorus burst through the bridge.

Instead of the song being dropped, the accompaniment was all removed. And only that intense riff melody came back alone.

@ll@ *Dvvvviiiiii~!*@ll@

And in the absence of the beat, the last chorus broke out, competing only with the riff melody.

$p
♪ *BUT.*

*It's NOT confusing!*

*I know who I am*

*I'm NOT confused*

*Oh!*

#^#*I'm going to find the BETTER ME*#^#♫
p$

Park Moondae, who received the note from Ryu Chungwoo, captured the note by playing it up to a super high pitch.

It was so loud that the AR could not be heard properly.

*Heol!*

The audience cheered internally.

When she listened to the song, both the riff melody and the voice were bright, so she got tired after listening to it a few times, but it was incredibly dramatic on stage.

In addition, the moombahton, which started again shortly after, stuck to her ears.

$p
♪ *BETTER ME* ♫
p$

With a big smile, Lee Sejin jumped out to the center at the last dance break.

And the accompaniment of the first dance break was changed.

As if a conflict had been resolved, a lighter and more upbeat atmosphere continued.

However, the choreography was more impressive in terms of difficulty and attraction because the number of foot movements increased and became extravagant.

$p
♪ *BETTER ME* ♫
p$

It wasn't until the end of the song that the song ended by singing the title that popped out as a variation hook once again.

And the internal shouts immediately burst out.

"Waaaaaaaa!!!"

"Aaahh!!"

"Oh my gosh!!"

The B-side was clearly built for live performance, and it was gripping to both watch and listen to.

Even better, they would be recording it repeatedly, so the audience would be able to watch this stage several more times!

TeSTAR stumbled and gasped for breath because of the song composition that had no structure to rest, then waved their hands in gratitude.

Perhaps it was too hot, a few members secretly picked up the techwear props and fanned themselves.

Then, a moment later, Ryu Chungwoo talked to the staff.

"Huft... hu, next take... Yes? Like this?"

TeSTAR's expression brightened.

"Wow, they said we did it in one go!"

The sentence was like a lightning bolt.

*No!!!*

The rehearsal had already taken place before the fans were let in, while the staff were doing equipment checks, so this was the only performance they would see!

But, of course, there was no turnaround. TeSTAR belatedly figured out the situation and made a vain attempt.

"Director, don't you think we made a mistake with the vocal?"

"I didn't see the camera!"

But that didn't work either. Thanks to this, they showed aegyo or danced apologetically and disappeared.

"..."

Thus, the fans came out of the recording studio 20 minutes after entering.

Each had a cream bun and an Americano from a well-known café, bought for them by TeSTAR.

*...Kim Raebin!! Park Moondae!! Who told you to be so good!!*

Not knowing what she was thinking, Kim Raebin's fan tore off the cream bread.

That it was delicious made her even more pissed off.
