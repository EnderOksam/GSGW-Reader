# Chapter 242 - As the Heart Takes Us, III

A few hours earlier, Cheongryeo was backstage at TeSTAR's online charity concert.

"Do you need water?"

"I'm okay."

Cheongryeo was used to waiting backstage.

He'd waited at struggling rural festivals and in stadium backstage areas reserved entirely for VTIC's staff.

He'd experienced every level repeatedly; the familiarity had built up inside him like rings in a tree.

That was why the members of VTIC were also used to the calmness of the leader.

He was never shaken, always ready for anything, as though born for this work.

So, after their debut, they quickly became calm as well, as if it were contagious.

"Wow, I'm actually pretty nervous!"

"Me too. Is this what it feels like to be a guest?"

"That's right. Originally, we should do better at someone else's party!"

VTIC chatted eloquently in front of their WeTube upload camera and waited for Cheongryeo to come in at the right time as usual.

As usual, they were not disappointed.

"It's a meaningful project, so I hope it will be helpful to TeSTAR Hubaenim."

"That's right~ Thank you for inviting us to a good place, TeSTAR Hubaenim!"

The members smiled and waved at the camera, and Cheongryeo thought to himself with a smile.

*This isn't good for us.*

Seriously, did they have to say such nonsensical things?

He'd chosen the members for their low scandal risk and high performance skills, not their brains. None had realized they'd fallen into a trap.

If this was the case, then it'd be shameful to do badly.

*If we do well, we'll look like fools. If we do poorly, people will think we're washed up.*

All of them were guests at their juniors' concert, and besides, they were seniors in the same industry who were going to sing songs made by said juniors.

Of course, he couldn't blame only the members.

*Perhaps there'd been no escaping such an enticing trap.*

Cheongryeo recalled the conversation during his visit to Park Moondae in the hospital when this appearance was made.

Park Moondae's first proposal for a donation concert.

– How about co-hosting?

– Hm.

At this time, VTIC's schedule was on the verge of turning murderous.

It was because they were preparing for a complete comeback.

Cheongryeo's successful solo album had opened up a much wider range of opportunities for VTIC.

The executives' fears of a domestic-album release exposing VTIC's decline had vanished after his successful solo.

Thanks to this, the preparation of the album gained momentum without the need for Cheongryeo to make any adjustments.

Like other idols at VTIC's level, they'd promote for a week on Korean music shows before heading overseas.

But rehearsing for the stadium tour and preparing for a new concert at the same time?

*It's impossible.*

There would be no time to sleep. Even now, Cheongryeo's sleep time had decreased just by visiting Park Moondae's hospital room.

If it was like this during the preparation period, it would be even worse after the comeback. It was a future determined enough to be uncomfortable to even call it a prediction.

Cheongryeo told the truth.

– We won't have time. We'll be in the middle of promotions.

Park Moondae was not too shocked.

– Then, as a guest?

– Hmm.

– If the event looks like it'll do well, LeTi will want a share of the publicity.

Park Moondae's explanation was clear.

The format of this donation concert would be exclusively contracted with LeTi's platform.

Then, as a gesture of friendship and publicity, LeTi would make Cheongryeo, who coincidentally had a friendship with Park Moondae, appear.

– You can perform whatever you want. You won't have to do anything else.

Cheongryeo knew it at that moment.

*So you knew exactly what you were doing.*

Knowing that joint planning was impossible due to VTIC's current schedule, Park Moondae led the discussion with this structure.

Cheongryeo narrowed his eyes.

*Using me as a guest...*

He'd even told Cheongryeo to choose whichever stage he wanted.

It sounded considerate, but it really meant Cheongryeo could simply appear alone.

While TeSTAR performed a special concert stage that they trained on separately, Cheongryeo would perform a general performance alone.

It was a matter of weight class.

If Cheongryeo did this, his reputation would be damaged.

It may not be as much as Park Moondae appearing as a guest on VTIC's tour, but it was still a risky act.

– How is it?

However, unilateral refusal was also a dangerous situation.

He didn't forget.

*The recording.*

The recording from their kidnapping conversation was stored somewhere in Park Moondae's cloud.

The main vocalist had already made headlines once. The moment this exploded, it was safe to say that VTIC's career would be over.

However, Cheongryeo judged that there was little possibility of Park Moondae releasing the recording.

*It's going to be a mud fight.*

Park Moondae would also have a big hindrance to his activities. So, it was a hand that could never be used when he still had a mission he had to do.

But... Park Moondae's survival was at stake right now, so if he had to, he could threaten Cheongryeo by mentioning the recording as a bluff.

That bluff would bring their friendly relationship to an end.

Cheongryeo didn't want that either.

So after quick judgment, he smiled.

– Great. A guest appearance.

– Then...

– Ah, all the VTIC members will be there.

– ...!

– You're okay with that, right? It should help boost the viewer count, too.

It was crazy.

If Cheongryeo appeared alone, he could end up with the least damage with the image of a 'guest invited thanks to friendship' even in the event of an emergency.

Perhaps Park Moondae also thought only that much of the worst case that would go back to Cheongryeo.

However, Cheongryeo had no intention of being the only one to take the hit.

– ...

Park Moondae was silent for a moment, but then nodded calmly.

– Okay.

– Yes. Then, let's do that.

It was a risky move.

VTIC would come out, so there was a risk of being hit much harder than Cheongryeo alone.

But at the same time, the gains were bigger. Because the two groups would be directly compared.

And since Cheongryeo knew VTIC's stage quality, he clearly judged the odds.

*More than 70%.*

And it made the scale bigger.

– I think it would be interesting if we swapped songs.

It was certain that this would fill up the number of audience members that Park Moondae needed.

"......"

After reminiscing, Cheongryeo smiled.

He'd made the decision to get involved himself.

His members would naturally go along with a fun charity appearance. He'd deliberately chosen people who were easy to control.

However, their strength was entirely their main job.

Cheongryeo recalled the condition of Park Moondae.

*400,000 people.*

Their online concert the previous year had drawn 610,000 viewers in a single day.

It was 610,000.

That count included only those who paid and logged in to VTIC's official platform.

So, as long as VTIC's participation in the group had been decided, there would be no problem with Park Moondae's survival.

*He'll have to accept losing some of TeSTAR's fans to VTIC.*

Wasn't this what he did to himself?

He couldn't take responsibility for Park Moondae's regrets.

*Well, should I let him see Kongi?*

Cheongryeo thought about his dog and thought briefly about how to comfort Park Moondae using it.

"Let's go."

"Let's have another VTIC day! Victorious!"

At the time of their debut, the clumsy cheering slogan given by the director jumped out as a joke from one of the members.

Even after so many restarts, why did that one thing remain the same? Maybe it was because he didn't bother to change it.

Because he always wanted to win.

"VCR ends in 10 seconds!"

Seeing the stage structure open in front of his eyes, Cheongryeo felt a strange tingle creep up his back.

It had been such a long time, so it was unfamiliar.

The feeling of taking a risk and charging toward an uncertain outcome.

It was a challenge.

**[Testing.]**

A short test sound was heard through his in-ear.

And the stage began.
