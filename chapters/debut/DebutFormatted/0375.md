# Chapter 375 - The Wise Survival, III

Joining a survival show halfway through and immediately singling out the best contestant for a death match?

I'll be honest.

*That's a good idea.*

If you were the rolling stone trying to dislodge one already in place and were confident in your skills, it was a very good strategy.

In any case, this wasn't a survival show decided by public vote, but one run within a single agency. The public knew the boss would have the final say and accepted that to some extent.

If someone really good came along, the public would prefer the rules to be changed to let them into the team even if they lost.

*But even so...*

*You picked Cheongryeo?*

*Cha Eugene, you bastard, you were supposed to compete with me!*

– *I think that would be best since our positions don't overlap, and it won't look odd even if we seem friendly later on.*

– *Okay. I'll perform onstage with you, Moondae-hyung!*

That was exactly what we said last night.

*I had everything planned out, and this bastard just abandoned the plan?*

I stared at Cha Eugene, trying to keep my eye from twitching.

If I let my agitation show on camera, I'd be the one screwed.

"Great. Those standing in front of you now become your rivals and competitors."

The president read his lines solemnly, but I was so upset that I couldn't react properly for the cameras.

I pretended to be nervous and took a deep breath.

"Of course, competition must have its rewards. Those who defeat a member of the supplementary group will immediately move into the honors class."

"...!!"

The anxiety on the trainees' faces diminished somewhat at that piece of bait. *You guys are too easy.*

"And trainees who aren't nominated will also receive an evaluation task: 'Perfecting Teamwork.' Work with two people competing against each other and complete a three-person stage."

I nodded.

In other words, the trainees who hadn't been chosen would get dragged into someone else's messy death match as an innocent but hapless third member of the group.

*What a way to put it.*

By the way, the person who joined my group was none other than...

VTIC's Sino.

"......"

*Oh, great.*

"I look forward to working with you!"

"We'll do our best!"

I was assigned a vocal practice room with the two guys. Once we'd gone inside, I looked at the song we'd been assigned.

*So there's no competition to choose songs or anything.*

It was a song the president—or rather, the company—had selected on the grounds that it would suit the three of us.

– Blue Jack's ⟨Icy Eyes⟩

It was an exciting EDM debut song by a male idol group that had taken the public by storm in the late 2000s, when auto-tune was all the rage.

To put it roughly, it was the cliché story of a guy with an unrequited crush on a girl with icy eyes, trying to work up the courage to approach her.

Listening to it now made me cringe.

♪ *Oh, oh, oh, icy eyes so cold!* ♫
p$

A typical hook-focused song with a short line repeated over and over in the chorus. The only part I could use to show off my vocal skills was the ad-libbed high notes later in the song.

"......"

*Hmm.*

"Oh, I like this song!"

The future VTIC member in the supplementary group was doing his best to be pleasant.

*...Chaeyul.*

I stopped analyzing the song for a moment and remembered my conversation with Cheongryeo.

It was when we'd come off the stage and were briefly replacing the tape on our microphones.

– *Hubaenim. I didn't contact Chaeyul separately here. I had nothing to do with bringing him here, either.*

– *Are you saying this is a coincidence?*

– *No... It was bound to happen.*

The guy grinned.

– *Chaeyul always shows up in the supplementary group of the survival show.*

– *...!*

I suddenly understood what he meant.

– *...Always?*

– *That's right. Chaeyul was in the group from the very beginning. I never chose him.*

I remembered Chaeyul's status window.

He had top-notch all-rounder potential.

It was no surprise he'd become a first-tier idol, but for now, his skills were only good enough to be a passable lead dancer.

However, his Talent was off the charts.

*His Talent is almost on par with Cha Eugene's during ⟨Idol Inc.⟩.*

Moreover, his Trait further supplemented it.

Dancing must be fun.

+150% to stage adaptation

– *He has no personal issues and has never fallen out with any of the other members. The public always likes him.*

What if a guy like this stood out in a survival show?

*I'd pick him, too, if I were the boss.*

It all made logical sense to me.

The problem was what came next.

– *And interestingly, every time he appears in the survival program, he always picks someone in the same position to compete against.*

– *The main vocalist.*

And this time, the main vocalist was me.

Cheongryeo explained, "I didn't know he would choose you because the person he normally picks was still on the team," but I wasn't sure I could believe him.

The important thing was that I was going up against Chaeyul anyway.

*Besides, check out the song selection.*

I had to suppress a laugh while listening to the song.

Was he really pushing an EDM hook song where I'd have only a 5-second window to show off my vocal skills with an ad-lib?

*I focused solely on singing in the previous performance.*

Regardless of who picked the song, they clearly intended to put me at a disadvantage by blocking my strength.

*Fine.*

I'd welcome the challenge.

$p
♪ *No! No no no...* ♫
p$

The song had just ended. Conscious of the camera that had followed us to film our reactions to the song selection, I clapped along with the others.

And a slightly excited voice asked us a question.

"Well, what kind of arrangement would you guys like to do?"

It was Chaeyul. For now, I decided to stay neutral.

I smiled faintly.

"I'm good with most genres. Anything's fine as long as I can perform it."

"Wow..."

*Where's this admiration coming from? I meant for you to reveal your cards first.*

The way they responded next was the worst possible scenario for me.

"Me too!" Chaeyul said.

"Me too... Well, I'll work hard no matter what we choose. It'll be a learning experience," Sino said.

"......"

I realized.

These guys... They were yes-men who'd agree to almost anything.

I was starting to see why Cheongryeo had chosen them as members. I spoke as calmly as I could.

"Then since it's a contest, why not make it intense?"

There was no rebuttal.

"Intense things are really cool and good!"

"Yes, then... Our team, fighting!"

*Wait, what?*

*Anyone watching would think it was a team match.* All of a sudden, I felt like an older college student doing a group assignment with two freshmen.

*Ryu Gunwoo's supposed to have been a trainee for only 3 months, so what the hell?*

If I took a great part for myself, I'd look greedy, but if I gave Chaeyul something he couldn't handle, I'd be accused of doing it deliberately.

*I have to prevent it.*

I'd have to shed light on this unfair situation during the interview later, in a way that wouldn't be edited out. I nodded.

I needed to change the way I spoke to them as well.

"Then tell me which parts look cool to you."

These were the people who would give me specific answers if I used more objective wording instead of asking what they wanted or hoped for.

Sure enough, some useful answers finally came out.

"I think the intro part looks cool!" Chaeyul said.

"Um, here on the bridge," Sino said.

"Then I think we should arrange it in a way that emphasizes those two parts."

"Yes!"

I made sure to phrase everything as though I were fully taking both of their opinions into account.

I'd made sure they were assigned the parts they thought were 'cool'.

"......"

Doing all this for the sake of the camera was quite a tedious task.

It made me think of the guy who'd always worked closely with me on things like this...

– *Moondaemoondae!*

*Stop.*

What was the point of wishing Keun Sejin were here right now? I just had to do my job properly.

I quickly outlined our performance. At least this was better than ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ in one respect: the other two were extremely cooperative.

Finally, there was a short break that came late at night.

*I guess we'll just go ahead with this.*

Holding a notebook with the arrangement directions and part distribution written in it, I walked down the hallway to get some air.

It was getting closer to dawn, and the other contestants didn't seem to be around.

*Has everyone gone back to their dorms?*

It was then.

A man walking with great strides appeared around the corner on the other side of the hallway.

*Cha Eugene!*

The guy raised his hand and quickly got closer.

"Hyung! Mff!"

It was just the two of us. *Great. Don't say another word, just follow me.*

I took him with me to a blind spot where the cameras couldn't see us.

"Uh—"

Cha Eugene was about to open his mouth, but when I put a finger to my lips, he closed it again. *The cameras could still hear us.*

Instead, I held out the notebook and pen I was holding.

*So he picked Cheongryeo instead, remembering what Cheongryeo had told him? That bastard said Chaeyul wasn't his doing—but apparently Cha Eugene was, which was why he hadn't mentioned it.*

*Since things have come to this, catch as much of the public's interest as you can.*

*I'll do the same.*

After parting ways with Cha Eugene in the hallway, I immediately returned to the practice room.

Sino had curled up in a corner listening to his part over and over again, but had fallen asleep. Chaeyul reacted as I entered.

"You're here!"

"Yes."

Then, he said this.

"Hyung, you can speak casually to me now. I'm only nineteen!"

"...Mhm, okay."

He was very much my sunbaenim in the real world, so trying to speak casually to him felt rather odd. I nodded.

"If you want to talk comfortably too, that's okay as well."

"Yes!"

Seeing as he didn't seem to think it would be difficult at all, I figured he was simply the cheerful type who didn't think too deeply about things.

I sat down. He was still looking at me, so I decided this was a good time to ask.

"Speaking of which... May I ask why you chose me?"

"Ah, ah, that's it."

My question could have sounded like I was asking why he'd thought I was the easiest target, but Chaeyul didn't seem to take it that way. He just rubbed the back of his head.

"Actually, I'm not even a LeTi trainee... So I didn't think I'd be likely to become a contestant on this program."

A surprisingly realistic answer emerged.

"That's why I wanted to perform onstage with the most talented person! I knew I stood to gain something from the experience."

He added excitedly that he'd seen my first solo performance.

"If I'm going to drop out anyway, this is probably my best option."

*Hmm.* From what I'd heard, he'd never once been disqualified. If he were an ordinary trainee, people would've accused him of being deceptive.

I chuckled.

"I don't think you'll be eliminated."

"Really??"

"Yes."

"Thank you!"

He bowed to me, his face full of anticipation and excitement.

*Wait...*

I'd been trying to make it a pleasant conversation, implying that both of us should somehow stay on the show...

He seemed to have forgotten that if he passed, I'd be the one forced to drop out.

I fell silent for a long moment, thinking about it.

*What the hell...*

*...Shin Jaehyeon will take care of it somehow.*

*Whatever. There'd been no problems throughout VTIC's 10-year career, so he must have his own way of controlling him.* I just needed to take care of my group.

"Let's practice a little more and then go to the dorm."

"Yes!"
