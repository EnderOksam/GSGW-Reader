# Chapter 435

*Hospitalized when I'm not even hurt?*

The days of deceiving people with my superhuman recovery, thanks to my 'Bacchus' or 'Nectar' traits, were over. I never thought I'd have to pull this kind of nonsense.

*This won't do.*

*My brain feels like it's going to atrophy at this rate.*I put down the ebook reader that Bae Sejin had brought me as a gift.

This was four days after I had been hospitalized.

"There's a shortage of hospital beds. Isn't it a bit wrong to just use one like this?"

"Ah, they said this is a private suite, so it's rarer for it to be fully occupied."

"Still, I think it would be better to be discharged."

"Moondae Moondae, even if you tell me, I'm not the medical staff. You understand that this is a situation I can't do anything about, right?"

"......"

*This detestable... wait. Is this bastard copying what I did during the building collapse?*

I stared at Keun Sejin in disbelief, but he just grinned widely and left the room.

"Rest up~"

"......"

Let's try the next guy.

Kim Raebin surprisingly came to visit in the evening, carrying a turntable that could play LP records.

"Classical music is said to help stabilize the mind and body!"

*He's avoiding modern music, is that it.*

I approached him logically. He was clearly blocking topics like 'album' and 'promotions' from his mind.

"This situation is strange. I'm still hospitalized even though I'm not hurt."

"They said that since you went through a very dangerous situation, your progress needs to be monitored for a few more days."

Kim Raebin seemed to truly believe it. It looked like a few of them had conspired to create a very plausible excuse.

"I can do that at the dorm, too. Honestly, it's a waste of money."

"You don't have to worry about that. They said the company will cover the costs!"

"If the company covers it, the net profit decreases. That means our final payments will be reduced."

"......"

For a moment, question marks and exclamation points flashed across Kim Raebin's face. He soon nodded as if he understood.

*Did it get through?*

"It's alright! I believe all the members won't mind that level of expense for your peaceful rest, hyung."

"......"

The halo of a copyright holder seemed to shine from his face as he said he would pay for it himself if I was really bothered.

I suppressed a long groan and lay back on the bed.

Next up.

"My muscles are going to disappear. I should start getting my routine back on track."

"Really?"

Ryu Chungwoo, who visited in the morning, reacted quite positively. I planned to build my case slowly from here, but...

The guy booked a private exercise room for me next to the hospital's rehabilitation center.

"......"

*Come to think of it, if the company was covering the hospital room, this guy, the Leader, must have been the one to persuade them.*

I had tried to build my case with the person who was practically the mastermind of this whole situation. Of course it wouldn't work.

"The facilities are nice. Ah, if you want to use it for longer, just let me know."

Forget it.

I gave up and approached the equipment.

*Might as well do legs...*

"You reap what you grow."

"It's 'you reap what you sow'."

"Whatever."

Cha Eugene, sitting in the caregiver's chair, shrugged and bit into a whole apple. I gritted my teeth and spoke in a low voice.

"Anyway, give me my smartphone."

"I don't have it. Watch the smart TV, hyung."

"......"

I fumed at the private suite, which pointlessly had four different OTT services installed.

The Wi-Fi was so strong there was not even any lag, yet I was in a situation where I couldn't use that amazing Wi-Fi for the internet.

*They're crossing the line.*

I had also asked the manager I saw two days ago for my smartphone, but the excuse I got back was obvious.

[The doctor advised that it could have a negative impact on your mental health, so we ask that you refrain for just a little while.]

*They're pulling tricks.*

It was too much. Way too much.

"But you have to endure it, hyung."

"......"

[You already know, don't you, hyung? You're in a situation where you have to regain the most important thing. Trust.]

Right.

I pressed my temples.

To be honest, it wouldn't be that hard to find a pretext to get discharged from this ridiculous situation.

The difficult part was persuading the guys around me to accept my discharge.

However, this time I was reluctant to ignore them. After all, they were the ones who had been trapped in a collapsing building because of me.

*They must have been on edge, worrying that I might have died.*

Since it all became something that never happened, a situation they couldn't even consult anyone about, I could let it slide if they considered this their own form of revenge.

...However.

"There has to be a limit."

No, just how long did they plan to keep me cooped up here? When were we supposed to release the album, prepare for the year-end, and plan for next year's activities?

There was a limit to how much I could endure just to keep the peace.

Still, I decided to exercise my last bit of patience.

"Hey."

"......"

"At least tell me what's going on in the outside world."

I could hold out for a few more days with just information gathering. Cha Eugene shrugged.

"Nothing special. [No terrorism, no big news, hmm, I think I only saw routine traffic accidents in the headlines? Oh, and bank fraud.]"

"...Not that kind of stuff."

Who asked for a current events lesson on modern society?

"I'm asking how the public opinion online is for our group or for VTIC."

"I don't know about that stuff. It's not my interest."

I was about to clench my fist, but I calmly imagined Cha Eugene browsing internet communities and social media.

I had to admit, it was a terribly unfitting image.

Let's find the next guy.

For the rest of the afternoon, I soothed my stress by listening to jazz on the LP and reading a mystery novel.

And when I met Bae Sejin who visited in the evening...

"No."

"......"

Bae Sejin avoided my gaze.

"...I can recommend more books."

I don't need them.

I resisted the urge to press Bae Sejin into accidentally letting something slip. Instead, I just shoved the mung bean samgyetang he brought into my mouth.

I felt like a prisoner receiving a meal from a visiting lawyer.

The last remaining candidate... though I thought there was virtually no hope.

Seon Ahyeon visited quite often.

However, after the first day, I hadn't bothered asking him to let me be discharged.

Not only was it surprisingly ineffective, but he would also get a look on his face like having this conversation was killing him.

"Moondae, your body, is it okay?"

"As you know, I couldn't be more fine."

Look at that. He was avoiding my eyes and peeling a tangerine again.

But now, it was time to say something.

"I think it's harder to stay hospitalized. I want to practice, too."

"......"

Seon Ahyeon avoided my gaze. As expected, he didn't have the shameless social skills to say something like 'I don't know~' like Keun Sejin.

So I cut straight to the point.

"I'm being serious. I couldn't be more fine, so let's get out of the hospital now."

"...But."

Seon Ahyeon's lips trembled.

"You, you lied. About... bigger things, too."

"......"

The guy peeled a tangerine and placed it in front of me. His hands trembled slightly.

I held back a sigh.

*The shock must have been huge.*

When I lied about the time I had left and bolted up to the second floor.

After that, the sound of the building collapsing was followed by a loss of contact, so this was unavoidable.

*If I were to just... lay it all out and explain.*

"I did it because I was scared, too."

"...!"

"How could I not be scared when I was suddenly told I was going to die? Besides, other people could have gotten caught up in my death."

"......"

"But it felt like there was nothing I could do, and it wasn't a situation where anyone could help."

It was my first time experiencing something where it felt like I would die no matter what I did.

"That's why I couldn't explain it properly. ...But if you felt like you were deceived, I'll say it again. I'm sorry."

This was sincere.

Seon Ahyeon opened and closed his mouth, but soon managed to piece together his words with difficulty.

"N-no. I'm, I'm sorry... for rashly, ah. For saying I didn't believe you..."

"It's not something you should apologize for."

Honestly, for someone who had been blindsided like that, it was a pretty mild reaction.

I chose my words carefully.

"I like just resting, too, but right now, with so much on my mind, it doesn't really feel like resting."

"...!"

"I want to leave the hospital."

The guy looked like he was holding back tears, but he soon put on a fairly resolute expression and nodded.

"I'll. I'll try to talk to them...!"

"...Thanks."

A sign of a solution appeared.

~~~

And that evening.

"Did you pack everything?"

"Yes."

I was finally able to go through the discharge procedure.

It was well over a week after I had been hospitalized.

*It's done.*

I felt relieved, but there was a lot to do. First, I would have to check public opinion, then slowly build an activity plan and head to the company.

I also planned to make time to go back to the dorm and have a proper talk with the other guys.

"Let's go."

"Yes."

Ryu Chungwoo had willingly volunteered to drive and helped with the discharge process.

However, the car he was driving didn't head for the dorm. Instead, it sped cheerfully in the opposite direction.

"...?"

Wait a minute.

"Where are we going right now?"

"Ah, we're not going to a hospital."

"Then where are we going?"

"Haha."

Answer me.

The car got on the highway and soon headed to the outskirts of Gyeonggi-do. It eventually arrived at a secluded country house with a beautiful white stone wall.

Ryu Chungwoo said brightly.

"We're here. You can get out."

These bastards... they've stuck me in a villa.

*Seon Ahyeon!*

How is this any different from being hospitalized? I didn't mean I just needed to be discharged!! But my escape route was already blocked.

"As it happens, we decided to remodel the dorm for a bit, so we were all planning to stay here anyway."

"......"

"We decided to refrain from going out. It would be a bit troublesome if witness accounts were posted online."

When did these bastards get so meticulous?

When I went inside, Kim Raebin held a cake that said 'Congratulations on your discharge'. The other members laughed and set off party poppers.

POP!

"Congratulations on your discharge!"

"......"

No wonder only Ryu Chungwoo came to pick me up. This was all part of the plan.

I'll admit it.

*This is my defeat.*

I silently picked the confetti off my head.

Still, the fortunate thing was that my efforts weren't entirely fruitless. It was revealed a moment later.

"......"

After the infuriating celebratory meal, Cha Eugene called me to his room.

He held out a smartphone with a white dog's head drawn on it. It was my smartphone.

"...!! Thanks."

The reward for persistently demanding outside information from this guy throughout my hospitalization had finally paid off.

"Hyung, you have to buy me delicious food. I'm a traitor right now."

Cha Eugene shrugged, saying he had a hard time persuading them and that he hadn't even told Bae Sejin the truth yet.

[Let's rephrase that as you being loyal to me.]

I willingly replied in English.

[That's fine. To be honest, everyone's going a bit overboard. I don't think forcing someone to rest means that person can truly gain "recovery".]

The guy even emphasized 'recovery' with one hand as he explained.

[Still, if you overwork yourself, hyung, then I'll be wrong. Promise me that won't happen.]

"Alright."

I willingly made the promise with him and returned to my assigned room. I plugged the charger into my dead smartphone.

As I turned it on, I spoke.

*Can you hear me.*

To Keundal.

A pop-up appeared instantly like a thunderbolt.

[Hyung, are you discharged now?]

*...Yeah.*

I replied gloomily. The pop-up flinched.

Even this guy had kept his mouth shut and hadn't uttered a single word about the outside world while I was hospitalized.

"Just who the hell ordered this gag order?"

Of course, we had a pretty useful conversation instead.

I recalled the conversation I had with him on the day I was hospitalized.

About the recent status abnormality failure and the reward pop-up that appeared at the end.

[Mission Failure Scenario Complete]

Victory: Player Park Moondae

Reward: Fragment of ■■■ 1 (1/4)

*What the hell is this 'Fragment' reward.*

I had already roughly figured out the part about the mission failure being treated as a scenario.

Perhaps because the building collapse was undone, it was treated as a scenario instead of reality, but the reward, 'Fragment of ■■■'... honestly, it led to a rather pessimistic prediction.

[I'm not sure about that either. As I was trying to make it a mission failure... the Status Window naturally created that form on its own!]

Is that so.

[Yes!]

So I replied like this.

[Then how about this interpretation. The System actually remains, just shattered, and I defeated a fragment of it and received that piece as a prize.]

[]

The pop-up froze as if it had hardened.

Then a message appeared in a shaky, messy font.

[Actually, I also... was thinking that maybe the System... was left as just a fragment, and that's why I didn't notice...]

*As I thought.*

[I-I'm sorry ㅜㅜ]

[What do you have to be sorry for? I was the one who mistakenly thought I got rid of it.]

I had a bitter taste in my mouth.

*I thought I had completely destroyed it.*

To think it would remain in a shattered state, like it's some kind of bead or a pizza. The fact that the description even has (1/4) attached is seriously bothering me.

One-fourth.

"Since I'm discharged, I might as well ask."

[Yes!]

"Don't tell me this kind of thing is going to happen three more times."

A short, dizzying silence followed.

[N-no, it probably won't! I missed it this time because I didn't know the System had shattered, so I analyzed the recent repercussions and scanned your Status Window thoroughly!]

[You're clean right now, hyung!]

Right.

What positive feedback.

However, I decided not to believe those words completely. It wasn't the first or second time I'd been blindsided after thinking that way.

In that sense, let's start thinking now to prevent a second status abnormality failure from happening.

*Let's see.*

They said the symptom of a status abnormality was developing a strong desire for a specific goal, right?

Just like how I was obsessed with the album right before.

Yeah, looking back after it was all over, I think I was being a bit unusually desperate to release an album. I must have been influenced.

*In that case, the thing I want to do most right now is...*

What else could it be? Right now, figuring out the situation is urgent, so that's all I can think about.

So, before searching on my smartphone, I decided to listen to a summary of the information I could get right away.

First, to prove that I had been discharged, I sent a text to Keundal with my smartphone. Then I asked this.

*By any chance, what happened with the VTIC charity concert?*

Was it canceled, or downsized?

After all that chaos, it had to be one of the two.

I was planning to start from there and get ready to search social media, but...

[Uh, it's being promoted with great success...?]

"......"

What kind of magic did they pull?
