# Chapter 526

News of TeSTAR's new album was already online, but the group had not released a promotion schedule. The intro trailer's release therefore caught everyone completely by surprise.

At midnight, fans scrolling through their smartphones in bed received a sudden notification from TeSTAR's official WeTube channel.

"...?!"

*What is this?*

This was the first video titled 'trailer' released since their collaboration with 127 Section.

Sleepiness vanished as fans tapped the pop-up. The thumbnail immediately appeared before their eyes. It showed a dark space with a small, cracked, old yellow lightbulb glowing faintly.

Fans held their breath, tense to the core, and clicked play.

[......]

The video began on a desolate street in a gloomy modern city.

Pitter-patter, pitter, thud, patter...

Rain drizzled down, its low noise depressing. The camera panned from a back alley in the desaturated, dark city toward an almost gray sky. The back alley was empty and dirty, littered with weeds, a manhole, and haphazard electrical wires.

A distinct shadow then appeared between the alleys.

SPLASH!

The figure of a person stood without an umbrella.

Kim Raebin, wearing a worn-out bomber jacket, stood backlit beyond the back alley and looked into the camera.

"...!!"

Kim Raebin's tired and weary appearance made his decadent eyes stand out. He seemed to look directly at the viewer through the camera lens.

Just as that feeling became distinct, the screen's focus shifted.

THUD!

Something had suddenly popped up right in front of the camera.

It was a white hand.

The camera panned down eerily to find the hand's source.

A manhole.

A single white hand stuck out from the half-open manhole, frozen like a mannequin's.

[......]

Tension filled the video.

Kim Raebin approached with heavy steps. He grabbed the white hand.

GRAB.

In that instant, everything changed.

[Calling]

A brilliant signature sound of intersecting trumpets and trombones erupted. An extremely modern, re-tailored swing house track burst out rhythmically. The sound was cheerful and addictive.

Pulled by the white hand, Kim Raebin fell down the manhole to the beat.

A sewer.

[Oooo-!]

The place was surprisingly a whole new world. Unlike the faded city above, colorful lights, shining water, and emergency lights flashed to the beat.

Melody and color overflowed. A mix of cartoonish CG effects and real colored fireworks burst and spilled over. The structure, however, was still a sewer.

Kim Raebin looked around and noticed something hanging on a protruding concrete pipe.

A black rabbit mask.

It was a furry mascot head that covered the entire head, so realistic it looked like a detached rabbit's head.

Kim Raebin picked it up without hesitation. He put on the black rabbit mask and ran joyfully through the sewer as if dancing.

A hip-hop beat filled the space as the brass faded.

[Starlight shines bright

I've lost my way

In the dark abyss

A hopeful enigma]

Lights flashed all around him. Beyond a steel fence, silhouettes of what seemed to be partying figures appeared and disappeared. The figures wore a variety of outfits, sometimes with animal masks. The outfits ranged from summer school uniforms to harness uniforms and coats inspired by hanbok.

These were the stage outfits TeSTAR had worn in music videos from ❬Idol Inc.❭ until now.

Cheers and confetti flew through the air.

[There is no answer

I just keep running

New track is rough

Your trigger is born]

Though the lyrics hinted at a new, rough path that would cause trauma, the masked Kim Raebin looked happy.

The moment he landed an energetic, excited step, the scene cut to a small, cramped room.

BAM!

Kim Raebin sat on a lone chair in the middle of the cramped room, rapping in a form-fitting black stage outfit. He looked incredibly cool, but the background gave off a somewhat gloomy feeling.

An old light flickered inside the dark room, where the faded city was visible beyond the window bars. The scene cut back to the sewer in time with the flickering.

The sewer where the masked Kim Raebin ran, however, grew increasingly bizarre and distorted in color. Each time the lights went out, the sewer's true, grim appearance was revealed for a moment before it vanished.

In contrast, Kim Raebin's performance in the small room grew more exquisite and intense.

[The future seen through the narrow gap

The accompaniment faded as a virtuosic trumpet solo rang out.

He grabbed the camera and shouted with a blank expression.

[The escape route has collapsed. Run!]

BAM!

The music returned, wilder than before. Lights exploded around the sewer. Primary colors vibrated, pounding on the mind.

The rabbit-masked figure, who had looked happy and danced, now ran forward as if struggling.

Finally, the rabbit mask barely reached the sewer's end.

[Whoa!]

An emergency lamp flickered and buzzed with green light. A phrase was roughly written below it.

Silence.

There was no color, cheers, or confetti.

Amid the quiet white noise, the panting rabbit mask reached out, grabbed the emergency exit handle, and opened the door.

[This is the end.]

A vermilion light flooded in from beyond the door.

It was a circus tent.

The vermilion tent and round lightbulbs filled the camera. They packed the outdoor space in a way that was both enchanting and eerie.

The music returned.

[End of the path

The emergency exit light went out.

It was a late autumn morning. Park Moondae held his smartphone and scrolled casually.

Kim Raebin's solo trailer, released at midnight, was already at the top of WeTube's trending videos chart.

*I expected this, but it's still surprising.*

It was not a game collaboration or a blockbuster project planned for months with a set concept. The reaction was unbelievable for a short, two-and-a-half-minute trailer starring a single member.

It was intense, and it somehow evoked emotions.

[Did you see TeSTAR's new comeback trailer?]

[When is the song coming out]

[Is it normal to feel so creeped out and sad watching the trailer?]

[Looks like Syeombyeol really went all out this time]

The buzz was more than simple compliments like 'The song is good,' 'The video is good,' or 'He's handsome.' It kept regenerating.

This meant there was something to talk about.

[The Rabbit Hole. This trailer, clearly inspired by Alice in Wonderland, shows a great deal of symbolism. The strange land represented by the sewer, the stage represented by the small room... (Read More)]

[About the last cut of the trailer OOO (spoilers)]

[I think Kim Raebin made this with TeSTAR's slump as the theme for this album]

[Maybe the 'strange land' refers to the idol industry]

Of course, some people were unenthusiastic or scoffed, calling it overly convoluted.

[Is it a national rule for producing members to become pretentious try-hards?]

[Honestly, they're a group that debuted from a super successful survival show and have been on a roll ever since, so what's so hard that they're whining about a dead end lolㅋㅋ]

└Who knows, maybe he's suffering from the pains of creation~

However, those opinions, often prefaced with 'Am I the only one who thinks this?', received little attention. People instinctively felt that these opinions were not even worth a logical rebuttal.

*Hmm...*

*Are they an anti?*

In other words, everyone had a gut feeling that few people would sympathize with such comments.

*That's how well-made this trailer is. This has become the public consensus.*

Park Moondae analyzed the situation quickly.

Kim Raebin seemed to pour out a very personal story, yet he exquisitely adjusted the video and lyrics to frame it as a metaphor for the new album. He also secured mainstream appeal with the visuals and melody.

*The truth is, regardless of the lyrics, people will listen as long as the song is good.*

The melody provided the mainstream appeal. Therefore, Kim Raebin's lyrics were not so convoluted that they affected its mainstream appeal. Instead, they provided just enough material for discussion.

In short, it was great to talk about.

It was captivating.

[TeSTAR Overture lyrics analysis]

[Rabbit hole lyrics analysis]

The already trending related search terms supported this fact. Park Moondae laughed softly.

*Analysis, huh.*

However, only Kim Raebin, the song's creator, truly knew the meaning embedded in the lyrics.

*Even we have only been guessing until now.*

He recalled a memory from a dawn conversation with Ryu Chungwoo. He had been startled on his way back to his room to see Kim Raebin suddenly sitting at his desk.

"Raebin."

"Hyung, my memories have returned."

The kid stared intently at his laptop with bloodshot eyes, said that, and then immediately immersed himself in his composition program again.

Even the great Park Moondae was a little flustered at that moment. He watched, slightly dumbfounded, but did not interrupt.

[Maestro ]

A small trigger is all that's needed.

Upon receiving inspiration, creation speed +150%

He had confirmed that Kim Raebin's characteristic had activated.

*That kid definitely seemed to have heard our conversation.*

Park Moondae worried inwardly that their conversation had been the trigger, causing Kim Raebin to regain his memories in a bad way, but he did not press the issue.

*I'll wait until he's stable.*

He and the other members simply gave sincere and proper feedback when Kim Raebin brought them a provisionally completed song a few days later.

"Thank you."

Kim Raebin calmly absorbed all the feedback without fuss, as if soaking it in.

After that, he poured his soul into the work. He focused all his time on the song and commissioning the trailer, completing it faster than Park Moondae had expected.

And finally, today was the day the trailer was released.

"Raebin, the reaction is seriously amazing...!"

"Raebin, you worked so hard! Congratulations!"

"Um, I have something to say."

"...? Oh. Okay."

Kim Raebin, his eyes hollow, finally stood before the members as if he had made up his mind.
