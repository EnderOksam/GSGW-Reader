# Chapter 567

The music video unfolded like a seamless single take, drawing the viewer in without any cuts.

It had a simple storyline. Ryu Chungwoo, from ❬The Procession❭, stepped from a tent. He surveyed the base camp in a frontier land as the camera excitedly followed, introducing the other camp members. They were a camp of adventurers exploring a new world.

*Indiana Xes...?*

The outfits and props set the mood, reminiscent of a classic adventure movie.

[My grandfather used to show them to me often when I was young! If commercial appeal isn't important, there are some elements I'd like to try using at least once.]

This was Kim Raebin's wish. He had compiled a list of classic adventure genre materials, including gas lanterns, old maps, pocket watches, and fountain pens. Thanks to him, classic adventure elements were exquisitely added to the outfits, just enough to avoid being over the top.

The members appeared one by one, wearing well-worn leather walkers, linen shirts, bomber jackets, and metal emblems.

Park Moondae sat idly with a telescope and smiled faintly at the camera. Kim Raebin drew a map with a fountain pen, then crumpled it and tossed it aside. Ryu Chungwoo inspected a firearm. Even Seon Ahyeon appeared, caring for a carrier pigeon with a white bird perched on his hand.

[How about a cheerful and bright atmosphere...?]

Just as Seon Ahyeon wished, the video was set on a bright, beautiful day. The screen overflowed with vibrant energy, hope, ambition, and a fresh sense of adventure. As the song progressed, the members grew more and more dynamic.

During the chorus, for example, Bae Sejin sat on a stump wearing old-fashioned glasses and looking at a journal. He slowly walked out and naturally performed the choreography with backup dancers, perfectly in time with the song.

[I turn the page

To the next page I go

The needle shines

The pain becomes my guide]

However, the element Bae Sejin contributed was conveyed not through sight, but through sound. It was these lyrics.

[For the lyrics, I think it would be good if they felt realistic, giving a sense of moving toward the future without being overly optimistic...]

[So, instead of just saying life is wonderful, how about a feeling of 'it's hard, but I'll keep going'? ...I'll try writing it like that.]

Thanks to him, the song spoke of hope while deeply reflecting on the past. It also acknowledged the pain.

[Let me tempt fate

Yes, beyond the signposts and crossroads

Struggling

I'll go just one step further]

As a result, a solid, subtle emotion permeated the entire piece.

[Towards the limit]

As pages from the journal Bae Sejin threw fluttered down, the camera raced forward again. It passed between outstretched hands as if giving a high five. The view then widened to reveal a natural backdrop of wilderness, mountains, fields, forests, and a blue sky with floating clouds.

Within the narrow aspect ratio, the members performed their parts, seeming to overflow from the screen. They perched on a giant rock or emerged from between colorful hammocks strung between trees.

"Huh?"

The fansite master frowned, a strange sense of déjà vu washing over her. She soon realized why. The members were appearing in compositions that paid homage to their own famous stage performances or close-up shots.

"......"

*Come to think of it, the lyrics are about looking back on the past.*

*They must have planned this.*

The fansite master felt a lump form in her throat.

Then the chorus returned. This time, the camera focused on an open tent piled high with splendid treasures. A tall figure sat loosely among them.

[I turn the page

To the next page I go

The goal shines

The pain becomes the proof]

He casually set down a golden artifact and stepped smoothly from the tent. It was Lee Sejin.

[Um, there are so many good opinions already. Rather than having something specific I want to add... let's aim to make the final product truly amazing, without settling for just 'good enough'!]

True to his words, the most spectacular and eye-catching sequence yet unfolded, mobilizing the largest number of people so far.

[Let me tempt fate

Beyond the signposts and crossroads

Struggling

I'll go just one step further]

As the choreographic climax passed, Lee Sejin gave a polite, slightly playful bow amidst a shower of confetti. Following his gesture, the camera moved again, as if to naturally show the next member's part.

But no one was in the frame.

"...?"

Instead, the camera zoomed in on someone's pass. Just as a smiley face mark and the powerfully scrawled phrase 'MAKE IT HAPPEN!' became visible, the video suddenly cut for the first time.

The instrumental faded, and a voice rang out clearly once more.

[Let me tempt fate

Yeah!

Beyond the signposts and crossroads

To the very end of today]

The video returned. At the edge of a vast field, Cha Eugene stood against the wind. With a horse. He took hold of the horse's reins.

[Beyond the limit]

Simultaneously, the screen opened up. The view expanded from a 4:3 retro ratio to a 21:9 widescreen, creating a liberating feeling, as if one could finally breathe.

"......"

Amidst the suddenly sharper colors and resolution, Cha Eugene mounted the horse.

[Can't stop, on to tomorrow]

He galloped across the screen, bursting out of the meadow.

His request had been simple and clear.

[I want to ride a horse!]

Thanks to his wish, he shot forward like something from an old Western. Only the sound of his breath broke the intense silence. Then, the drums rushed back in.

[Yeaaaaaaahhhhhhh!]

With an instrumental that sounded like stomping feet, the point of view briefly shifted to the sky, a bird's-eye view looking down on the running horse. Then, as if pulled in, the camera closed up on the rider. It was Cha Eugene, smiling with his teeth showing.

[Running breathlessly

The city center beneath my feet

Reaching my hand higher toward the sky]

Over the classic band instrumental, Park Moondae's refreshing high notes rang out, as if racing forward.

[(Over and over)

Even if I can't reach the stars

I'll live for today]

[Woo-]

As the chorus rang out, the horse kicked up dust and galloped fiercely. The moment Cha Eugene pulled the reins, it stopped dead in its tracks. A sunset spread across his view. A vast, sunset-lit sky.

[Don't sleep

Just- dream]

With the thrilling final note held for a long breath, Cha Eugene, now off the horse, tossed his hat to the side. The moment the hat disappeared effortlessly off-screen, Cha Eugene turned to the camera and grinned.

As Cha Eugene turned his head back and walked away, the ending credits appeared behind him.

[Production / TeSTAR

Director / Lee Sejin, Kim Raebin

Cinematography / Park Moondae, Ryu Chungwoo

Lighting / Ryu Chungwoo, Lee Sejin, Seon Ahyeon

Screenplay / Bae Sejin, Seon Ahyeon

Costumes / Kim Raebin

Music / TeSTAR

...]

White sentences, all covered with the name TeSTAR, scrolled up over the sunset scenery, stirring people's hearts.

*Ah...*

In the distance, a small shot captured the running Cha Eugene meeting six black silhouettes backlit by the sunset. The scene of them giving high fives or putting their arms around each other's shoulders flowed on screen without a cut.

This continued until all the ending credits had scrolled by, leaving only one word.

[Loviewer]

"......"

That single word remained, occupying the entire screen until everything else faded.

The fansite master realized it instinctively.

*It was a fan song.*

This wasn't a collaboration, a commercial, or a lore-connecting video. It was clearly a new fan song.

***

"Everyone thinks it's a fan song."

"I know, right? It's become the accepted theory."

"......"

"......"

"Should we just say it was a fan song?"

"Yep."

The motion passed without opposition. The monitoring continued.

[Guys, an Epic article is out]

'A story of looking back on their journey since debut with various emotions, and resolving to move forward once again'... it says...

└Fuck, that's so cool

[Crazy, looks like they filmed the music video themselves. Why are the members' names in the filming and editing credits? (ending credit screenshot)]

[The director who acted as an advisor for the MV shoot appeared on social media. It's the person who filmed the Roll the Dice game ㅠㅠㅠ]

While most posts were from excited fans sharing thoughts or seeking behind-the-scenes stories, comments like this also appeared frequently.

[Listening to this right at the start of the new year is so fucking overwhelming. I recommended it to my non-fan friend and they already knew about it (?). What is going on?]

└Even non-fans know a good thing when they see it lol

[TeSTAR's new song is perfect for the new year. I'm using it as a work song to exorcise my three-day resolutions.]

[Are they really not promoting? The reaction is so good.]

"......"

Bae Sejin opened his mouth, looking like he was about to break into a cold sweat.

"Why... is this doing so well?"

That was right. The fans' reactions had been quite good from the very beginning. We had even seen a fair number of comments like, 'The song is so good, it's a shame they're not promoting it,' and had pictured pulling this out during a concert's climax.

*And it's not exactly wrong to call it a fan song.*

Until then, everyone was just happy.

[Our TeSTAR is saying they'll cherish the time we've spent together and run hard in the new year too!]

[(Please move along, I'm an overly-invested stan)]

[It was like the epilogue to a grand finale...]

However, this story, distorted in a positive way, started going viral on InHeart's social media, and then...

[How many of these scenes do you recognize? (Comparison of TeSTAR's legendary shots and music video cuts)]

It came to this.

"They're predicting it'll be number one on the music charts in the next hour."

"......"

[Wow TeSTAR predicted #1 congrats congrats]

A number one on the daily music charts, something difficult to achieve even when gritting your teeth through a meticulously prepared album release, was right before our eyes.

"Really?"

"For real."

[The boy group song that seems to have been chosen for new year's resolutions]

*Is this for real?*

There was no grand strategy. Strangely, luck and timing aligned perfectly, and we hit the number one jackpot on the music charts. The funny thing was that this was a digital single with no physical album.

And a low-budget, self-produced video made in just two weeks... had to be the one to blow up like this?

"...Should we have prepared more and made it the title track of a full album?"

"What about the title track we already have?"

"We could have made it a double title track."

*Is that so.*

But it was already in the past. The song was already released. All we could do was be satisfied and happy about this unexpected, accidental hit.

"Oh, as expected~ It doesn't seem like a coincidence, does it? I think people recognized it because all our group members have great sense. Right?"

"Y-Yeah...!"

"......"

"Of course, the timing was great too! Still, we don't know when timing like this will come again, so let's work even harder from now on~!"

"Yes, I'll take it to heart!"

"......"

*He's using this to boost morale.*

Watching Lee Sejin deftly use this achievement to boost the team's morale, I nodded, thinking he really hadn't changed a bit. For reference, Ryu Chungwoo was looking at his smartphone next to me.

"I got a call from the company. It seems we're getting a lot of appearance requests from broadcast stations. They're asking what we think about appearing on the good ones."

"Wow..."

It made sense.

*Released a fan song with no promotions, it went viral organically, and hit number one'? The story itself is just too good, isn't it?*

It would have been more impactful if we were unknown singers, but being a top-tier idol group probably helped attract more attention.

*Even I would book us.*

I fiddled with the back of my neck. It had been a while since I had experienced a song itself becoming a public hit, not just one buoyed by our name value or fan recognition. It was a situation where I should be thrilled and happy, but...

"But this is all we have."

"That's... right."

This is it. There's no other content.

*What do you expect from something we crammed together in two weeks?*

We didn't even create choreography. It was all freestyle, improvised on the spot. However, in this situation, everyone naturally expected more content. There were even people who firmly believed there was a bigger picture.

[This is definitely a build-up. Look, Gloomy-Moondae's taste is rock solid lol]

└Ikr lololol

*But there isn't any.*

However, no one on this team could ignore an opportunity that had rolled right into our laps.

"......"

"......"

"Should we... do something?"

Overtime confirmed.

It was the moment Kim Raebin's energy drinks became a group-wide ration.
