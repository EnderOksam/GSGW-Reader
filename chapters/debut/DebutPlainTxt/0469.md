# Chapter 469

"These are the guys?"

"Yeah."

Lee Sejin, having just returned from his solo schedule, checked the link on his smartphone.

After agonizing over various individual gigs, from performance competitions to exploration variety shows, Lee Sejin had made a rather unexpected choice.

[Hope Game – Choice and Reversal]

It was a death game survival reality show. The program, produced by a public broadcast affiliate's WeTube channel, was not popular with the general public but had a solid fanbase and generated significant online buzz.

[And they subtly use dice as a symbol here, too~ Since our album is 'Roll the Dice', I thought it would be a good chance to sneak in some promotion, you know?]

[I had a feeling that if I did well, they'd use clips from our game video in the edited footage.]

As expected of his social skills and quick wit, he built his image as a likable supporting character, shining only in a few decisive moments. It seemed his character was becoming clearer through this show, even more than on conventional variety programs.

They filmed two episodes at a time, and he said he had survived up to the sixth episode, which was currently filming.

*He's an experienced survival show contestant, after all.*

He surely handled it without ever forgetting the cameras were rolling. In any case, it also meant that despite being mentally drained, he had grasped the situation sharply enough to check on it the moment he arrived.

"T-Holic's juniors... Hmm, the agency Wonder Hall."

The group's name was Aether. Their teaser video had just been released.

I turned my gaze back to the smartphone playing the video.

"......"

A hazy color palette filled the background, with a slight sepia filter and the feel of a film camera. A grassy field and smiles unfolded with gentle saturation and a soft look, free of harsh highlights.

And then, the boys.

They ran, leaping through countless spaces. An old school rooftop, a bus stop, a junkyard, a bridge glittering with the night view, a grass-covered sports field...

Props from the landscapes they passed appeared, arranging themselves in the air as if following the boys and creating a dreamlike effect.

They arrived at a desolate four-way intersection. Though empty of people, a clean filter gave it a hazy, wistful look. A few stylishly edited dance cuts flashed by, timed to the dreamy and fresh music.

Then, only the sound of a song echoed in the silence.

[Leaping over the line of time

To you

Whom I haven't yet seen]

A boy stood on a traffic light, bathed in the gentle light of dawn. He kicked off the ground as if soaring toward the sky. A white hand covered the sky, and the sunlight flashed brightly.

[Show the way

[ Aether[]

White text silently appeared on the screen, and the video ended.

"......"

Lee Sejin watched the entire thing without a word, then checked the view count. It was in the millions.

"Hmm."

He grinned, furrowing his brow.

"Shall we hear the expert's opinion first?"

"So, what's our Raebin's take on it?"

"There are some interesting parts in the chords and instruments, but..."

Soon, among the now fully assembled TeSTAR, the 'expert' began his commentary on the teaser.

Kim Raebin.

He was currently appearing alone on a program where he gave songs to once-unknown singers getting a second chance. Of course, the composer's identity was not revealed at first. Singers who passed the auditions listened to the songs for only thirty seconds before fighting over them, choosing, and evaluating them.

Only after a song became theirs could they see the composer's identity and listen to the full track.

[MC: The identity of the White Rabbit composer who created song number 5, 'Melody', is none other than... TeSTAR's Kim Raebin!]

[Kim Raebin: I look forward to working with yo—]

[Contestant: Aaaaaah!! Ah!!]

[Contestant: Thank you!]

[Kim Raebin: ...??]

Even though it was a position where he could fully enjoy the prestige of TeSTAR's name, he was not enjoying it at all. Only the viewers were.

Anyway, after watching the entire teaser, Kim Raebin gave his assessment.

"It seems their reference points for melody development and choice of supporting instruments are similar to mine! I'd like to ask them how they came to choose this method."

"......"

Bae Sejin, who had been quietly holding it in, could no longer stand it and shouted.

"They copied it!"

"What?!"

That's right. I rubbed my chin.

"They analyzed your songs and used them as a reference."

"...!"

"To my ears, it sounds like they referenced songs in the vein of 'Magical Boy' or 'Wheel'."

Kim Raebin himself had probably already found many more partially similar songs in his head.

Seon Ahyeon blinked, looking bewildered.

"Uhm, now that you mention it, this teaser image, too..."

"Yeah. It has a similar feel to our debut teaser."

However, they did not just reference the 'Magical Boy' teaser. For instance, the teaser's length, editing, and composition were more similar to our 'Procession' era. The color scheme was 'Airplane', the props 'Picnic'.

It felt like they had taken various elements from TeSTAR's past activities, mixed them with other things that looked good, and re-established them.

*It's like a puzzle.*

"...! Don't tell me they made a whole universe for themselves, too?"

"Of course they did."

It was probably harder to find a male idol group without a universe these days. I immediately searched for a summary.

[Superpowered boys who travel between the past and future within a virtual reality.]

Dreaming guides of eternity. Wandering through their respective youths, they set off in search of light. Show the way - Aether.

*Oh.*

Time travel, magic, and even dreams.

Bae Sejin's hands trembled.

"...This is too similar to our previous concepts...!"

"To be precise, only the keywords are similar," I assessed coolly. "And the keywords don't completely overlap, either."

They subtly twisted it, changing time travel to virtual reality and magic to superpowers.

*A bit more modern and technological.*

It would be easy to tie into modern platforms.

"Besides, they've clearly established their own identity."

"Where!?"

"Here."

I pointed to the group's name.

[Aether]

"It means 'path' or 'journey' in Latin. They took 'guide' as their group concept and attached a unique image to it."

"......"

I should have expected something this conceptual from a group named 'Aether'.

Bae Sejin muttered in a somewhat hollow tone.

"So... they didn't copy us?"

"They did copy, but it's what you'd call benchmarking."

Strictly speaking, that meant it was not plagiarism.

"It's more like they researched the most popular product on the market and made their own version of it."

"......"

Bae Sejin sat down, his face showing his unease.

*I get it.*

I did not feel great about it either. They had clearly patched things together from various references and mixed in their agency's style, so it was definitely not plagiarism.

However... it was also true that this group could not have existed without TeSTAR.

*Hmm.*

It seemed everyone felt the same way.

Bae Sejin eventually muttered with a slightly bitter expression, "It's not like we're the only ones who can do this kind of concept... but still, it felt like we were the only ones doing it until now."

"......"

Lee Sejin hesitated for a moment before speaking with a reluctant smile.

"Mmm~ Actually, there have been a lot of groups like this before, you know?"

"Uh, uhh??"

Bae Sejin was taken aback, but it was an undeniable fact.

*It's common for latecomers to put out whatever sells well in the market at the moment.*

Countless groups had tried to benchmark TeSTAR's conceptual feel over the past few years. There was just one crucial difference.

"But none of them really got a huge reaction~ That's why you probably didn't notice them, hyung."

"......"

"This kind of concept is actually hard to pull off stylishly."

That's right. It required planning and capital. Trying to copy it in a short time with a small budget and limited manpower was the kind of concept where you would not even break even.

"Then these guys..."

I answered.

"Twenty billion won over three years."

"......"

"They said they even established a new project planning team to run it... so they must have invested a lot of manpower, too."

It was information I had gotten from a hint.

So... planning, capital, and manpower. They had prepared with all three elements in place. For three whole years.

"......"

A brief silence fell.

Ryu Chungwoo, who had been listening quietly, finally spoke.

"Then... they must have come thoroughly prepared."

A short while later, their title track was released.

Since the basic concept was different, the general public probably would not make the connection immediately.

*Still, at this point, anyone who's in the know would have figured it out.*

No matter how much more keenly we felt it as the ones who self-produced our albums, there was no way we were the only ones who noticed.

"This... it's being talked about by the fans, too, right?"

[Is it just me or do they look like just another TeSTAR copycat? :(]

"...I knew it!"

"But it's hard to see that as a good thing."

"Ahem."

Bae Sejin looked like he was about to scream 'Why' but seemed to hold back, trusting in years of experience.

Seon Ahyeon muttered a little cautiously.

"...Ah, because, um, you can't really call it plagiarism?"

"Right."

On the contrary, it could become a source of ridicule. Like this.

[Did TeSTAR get a monopoly on the refreshing-dreamy concept?]

[The quality is just good, so I guess they're saltyㅋㅋㅋ Like only our TeSTAR is allowed to do this stuff!]

[Ah, Magical Boy was years ago, what are you even talking about? Besides, Aether? They're not even wearing school uniforms lol]

[If you cut up all of TeSTAR's music videos and compare them frame by frame like this, then all idols are plagiarizing. What a stretch.]

The discourse was clashing. It had become a topic worth fighting over.

*This is not good.*

Even if public opinion swayed to 'they copied, they're a copycat' over something this ambiguous, it could be overturned at any time.

*As long as the other party gains popularity!*

I immediately went to an entertainment gossip forum and scanned the popular posts.

[They say this is Aether's visual member]

[Wonder Hall's new group reminds me of TeSTAR, not T-Holic]

[Have you heard Aether's debut song?]

[Wonder Hall's new boy group feels too much like a small agency groupㅠㅠ]

Posts fiercely criticizing the rookies, praising their looks, and evaluating their song were all shooting up the popular posts list.

"......"

*Fuck, we've been had.*

"Hyung, you don't look so good," Ryu Chungwoo said in a comforting tone. "Is it because of what you called a backlash?"

"...It doesn't even need to go that far."

"This group... it's becoming a hot topic."

"...!"

"This kind of controversy is the type that can be pushed aside once they gain fans over time. If the song is good and the choreography is good, getting people to click on it one more time is always a gain."

"And that's what's happening," Lee Sejin added.

That's right. In other words, this just massively increased their buzz. Viral marketing.

It was the time when TeSTAR was at its weakest. A time when we were coldly abandoned by the broadcast affiliates we were so firmly connected to, when a distance could easily form with the public and our exposure dropped. A time when it was hard to respond to a rising star coming up fast.

"Right now, we're in a state where we can't do any schedules related to T1. Whether it's broadcast or WeTube."
