# Chapter 424

"Is my landing okay when I turn here?"

"Yeah, but let's be careful with our arms."

Mirynay, the junior group from the season after TeSTAR's 〈Idol Inc.〉, diligently prepared for a broadcast performance. It was only a supplementary stage for their seniors, TeSTAR, during a live concert broadcast.

The situation felt awkward.

*Hasn't the time for us to be involved in something like this passed?*

She was not trying to be arrogant. It was just common sense. Mirynay was not a rookie group struggling to find its footing. Piggybacking on a performance by male seniors seemed counterproductive.

*Still, I guess Spacer stands to gain from this.*

They were not fools. The idols knew what picture their agency was painting.

The Mirynay member performed her solo dance with a bitter feeling.

"Hah!"

She was not on Yulgi's level, the group's de facto center in popularity and visuals, but as the main dancer, she had to deliver her parts.

Right next to her, another member with a significant part stared at a tablet with a serious expression. It was Park Minha, who debuted in second place on 〈Idol Inc.〉.

The group had no official leader, but Park Minha practically filled the role. The member was both grateful for and a little sorry for her. However, her behavior had been strange lately.

*...Minha, are you by any chance dating someone?*

Just a few days ago, she saw her looking restless with her smartphone or sneaking away with a determined look. Crucially, she had unintentionally overheard Park Minha making a secret call in the dorm bathroom on their manager's smartphone.

[Um, sunbae.]

A pleasant, calm, and beautiful male voice echoed from the narrow tiled walls.

[It's okay.]

She even knew who it was.

*Park Moondae...*

He was TeSTAR's main vocalist, also known as Moon Puppy.

"Okay!"

The member stopped dancing and wiped her forehead. Honestly, she could understand developing romantic feelings for a male peer.

*He was handsome.*

TeSTAR as a whole had an unbelievably high average for looks.

*It's a scam.*

Instead of a fluttering heart, a sense of crisis from their shared survival show background rang a louder alarm. The Mirynay member finished practice, thinking gloomily,*We're screwed... well, not completely, but I wish we were more successful.*

"Should I get our Minha some water? Do you want some?"

"Ah, it's okay, unni."

She put in her earphones and leaned against the mirrored wall, listening to Jeong Yulgi and Park Minha's conversation. Then, it happened.

"Hey, Harin."

"Hm?"

Park Minha, sitting next to her, spoke, but her expression was strange. It looked like she had made up her mind about something.

"Even if it's a little dangerous... never mind."

"......"

"No, it's nothing. I must've been mistaken!"

*Even if it's a little dangerous... what? Don't tell me you were about to say you know someone so great it's worth the risk and you want to introduce them to me?*

A sense of unease crept up on her, but she ignored it. The Park Minha she knew was not the type to introduce someone. She was the type to worry if another member might do something like that.

*Still, I should try to sound her out.*

The member's eyes darted around as she spoke.

"Minha, I'm just asking... but are you, by any chance... getting close with that... sunbae?"

"Huh? Who?"

The member whispered, "Park Moondae." He was the undisputed top-tier male idol.

And Park Minha's expression turned dead serious.

"No. Don't ever say that anywhere. Absolutely not. It's impossible."

She thought Minha was rapping.

"Uh, okay..."

It really must not be true. The member was reluctantly convinced as she watched Park Minha react like she had just been told she was dating a ghost from hell.

*Then what on earth was that phone call about?*

The atmosphere had seemed so tense, yet good. The member, Seong Harin, shrugged and continued practicing.

*...Aren't we practicing the new song?*

She thought of the choreography for their new album's title track. It had been postponed indefinitely and was no longer mentioned.

And a few days later, for the first time in a while, they had a schedule that was not practice or an event.

"What's this?"

"Ah, it's like a photoshoot and ad."

It seemed to be a fashion magazine photoshoot.

*For something like that, the outfits look strangely unimpressive.*

The Mirynay member sharply checked the fabric and lines of her outfit. A job was a job.

*I'll nail this photoshoot.*

Thinking this, she entered the set. They ran into TeSTAR, who were finishing their shoot. Their schedule was right before Mirynay's.

"Hello."

"Hello!"

*Oh.*

Their clothes were also mediocre, but their great figures and faces made them look good.

*Wow, I'm really curious how they maintain themselves.*

The member bowed ninety degrees. As she idly watched Kim Raebin pass, she realized he wore an outfit similar to hers. She sighed.

*Don't tell me we're doing another ad together?*

She remembered the ridicule from the smart device ad in their early debut days. Her composure almost wavered, but she held it in with her years of experience.

*As long as I do well, that's all that matters!*

"Please wait for a moment."

"......"

Mirynay moved to the waiting area without complaint.

"We're just going to adjust the fit of the outfits one more time~"

"Yes, yes!"

Jeong Yulgi was up first. The moment she got up and excitedly went to prepare, Park Minha cautiously slipped out the back door. She looked like she was meeting someone in secret.

"...?"

*Wait, if there's someone to secretly meet at this film set... no, if it's a group, doesn't only one come to mind? Isn't this... too much of a signal?*

No way, no way!

Seong Harin stealthily followed Park Minha to prevent a potential disaster, and she saw them. Park Minha and Park Moondae stood together in the hallway.

"...!"

The member averted her startled eyes and calmly assessed the situation.*Hey, these two... Come to think of it, don't they have the same surname and clan origin? Ah, no. Calm down.*

They stood a bit close, with no physical contact, but a strange tension hung between them. She strained her ears to listen.

"Sunbae, don't tell me this is what you mentioned last time..."

"Yes, but I didn't know it would be in this manner either."

"...I understand!"

Their conversation sounded less like flirting and more like secret agents plotting something.

Seong Harin's pupils dilated. She sighed and backed away from the hallway.

*Ah, whatever.*

Minha was not the type to do anything foolish. It seemed best to stop here. Harin felt she had already crossed a line.

*Pestering Minha with questions would be a bit much, too.*

She returned to the waiting room, thinking,*I should just focus on my idol life.*She proceeded with the shoot without issue. She checked the monitor occasionally. Thankfully, the photos looked good.

*On days like this, this is the move!*

She logged onto her social media.

——————————

Take today's Harin 🔥

——————————

She already had permission to post a picture of the outfit from the waist up.

...The clothes?

*Wait, wait!*

An ominous premonition made her pause the upload. She searched for a certain account and checked the latest posts. As expected.

——————————

It's hot but I'm enduring it 🙄 🔥

(Photo)

——————————

*Ugh!*

It was TeSTAR's social media. A humorous, well-colored snapshot was posted. It showed a playfully smiling Lee Sejin and Cha Eugene, with Park Moondae looking at them with incomprehensible eyes.

*They filmed first, so they posted first!*

The composition was similar, too. Similar outfits, accessories, background, and the same emoji were a recipe for controversy. Even if an explanation about overlapping schedules came later, she could not risk accusations of dating rumors or attention-seeking, even for a moment.

*Ah, this is so annoying!*

The Mirynay member thought of the detestable rumor channels on WeTube. She gritted her teeth and deleted the post she was about to upload.

*I'll make it big, no matter what...!*

She would become an idol whose music charted better than TeSTAR's. Then, if a dating rumor ever broke out, people would say she was too good for him. She felt the possibility was remote, but ambition was meant to burn brightly.

Then she remembered her own juniors.

*...Will they be shooting this too?*

The new girl group had taken all the staff she used to work with. Seong Harin hesitated, then decided not to think about it.

*Let's just practice.*

That was not something she could control. It was better to do her best with what she could.

A few days later, a news article proved Seong Harin's gloomy guess correct.

[〈T1 Stars〉 X 〈Teusle A〉 Joint Line Launch]

[Idols from Survival Show Powerhouse Tnet, Reborn Through Branded Apparel... An Innovative Attempt by 'Teusle A']

Media reports overflowed with flashy rhetoric. The story was simple.

[T1 Stars is launching a collaboration line with a clothing brand! They are considering having their own idols as exclusive models and even participating in the design process.]

*Ah, as expected.*

It was tacky because the agency did it in-house.

Seong Harin nodded. She ended her thoughts there. She decided not to waste her feelings by clicking on the rookie group's photoshoot or browsing comments.

So she did not know.

[Did you see this? The T1 idol clothes (12)]

[I knew they were money-grubbing bastards but they're really doing all sorts of things... (7)]

[Let Kim Raebin design (8)]

[But I kinda like it since I like merch lol (3)]

The lukewarm and so-so reactions that had been continuing like this...

[The content of this article is a bit weird (534)]

...had changed significantly from this point.

A short while later, on the day of TeSTAR's concert broadcast.

[Clothes Only Idols Can Wear, Such a Blatant Class System in Fashion]

When articles like this began to pour out.

]"The Platinum grade is not for you to wear"... The Influence of Tnet's Survival Shows on Us]

[T1 Stars Fans Begin Boycott... A Mistake by 〈Teusle A〉?]

Backstage, the Mirynay member waited for the live broadcast, flustered by the commotion.

*Wow, it's chaos.*

Even their manager took a call and ran off. She knew agency boycotts were common. However, the atmosphere felt unusually serious.

*What is it?*

In truth, these articles had not appeared suddenly. She had been off the internet for a few days. She did not know the issue had already caused an uproar on social media, been picked up by WeTube, and simmered like lava before erupting.

The other members tried to change the mood, since the agency seemed to be taking the heat, not them. Then she saw Park Minha breaking out in a cold sweat, looking uneasy.

"...?"

*The pressure of being the Leader...?*

"Minha, are you surprised? What's wrong?"

"...No, uh, I was just a little surprised!"

Park Minha flinched, then quickly put on a determined expression. She looked like an energetic chick, not someone in a dangerous situation.

*She seems fine?*

"Just a slight makeup touch-up here."

"Yes."

A stylist came to fix the spot where Park Minha's cold sweat had been. Just then, TeSTAR's Park Moondae appeared. He was briefly visible through the open waiting room door, talking with a staff member as he walked down the hallway.

Seong Harin saw him glance toward Park Minha for a moment before he moved on. It was a light, passing glance, but Seong Harin froze in shock.

*Did that person... just smile?*

The misunderstanding deepened.

***

"It's noisy."

"It is."

The bomb finally went off behind the pre-recorded concert stage as they waited for the special broadcast performance. The staff still moved in perfect order, but the company officials' demeanor clearly showed the problem.

Waiting right behind the stage without our smartphones, we had no way to check.

"Hmm~ It looks like some kind of article came out," Keun Sejin said.

Kim Raebin raised his hand. "Should we consider the possibility that this problem requires us to modify the performance?"

It was a necessary question.

"No."

Ryu Chungwoo stated with refreshing certainty, "We just have to go out there and do well, just as we planned."

Smiles spread among the members.

"Nice."

"Understood!"

The standby signal came.

"Hyung, do well!" Cha Eugene slapped Seon Ahyeon on the back.

Seon Ahyeon looked surprised but soon replied firmly, "Got it."

The damned System virtual reality had been exhausting, but returning to reality had its good points.

*For example.*

For example, the years of professional ballet experience that guy had acquired.

*The viewers are going to be really surprised.*

The agency was in an uproar, but they were on air in twelve minutes regardless.

Seon Ahyeon began his intro solo dance.
