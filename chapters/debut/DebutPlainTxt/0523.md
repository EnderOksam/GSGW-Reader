# Chapter 523

Kim Raebin was unable to perform on stage.

However, the STier period that was likely the cause had already vanished from his mind.

*This is driving me crazy.*

What could I possibly say to a guy who couldn't even remember? For now, I sent him back to his room. I told him not to worry too much since it could just be fatigue and that he should rest.

But everyone probably knew that was bullshit.

*Fatigue, my ass.*

If that were the case, it should have happened during our debut when he slept three hours a night. No matter how you looked at it, this was a trace of STier Kim Raebin.

The only silver lining was that the member who spent the most time with him back then, who was the same age, was here.

As luck would have it, that guy had no evening schedule and was at the dorm.

"Tell me everything you suspect might have happened to Raebin during your STier days."

I would get everything out of him.

I summoned Cha Eugene to the kitchen. He didn't pout at my question but instead turned serious.

It seemed this guy had also been thinking about the situation.

"A lot happened, but Kim Raebin was fine. [Of course, he wasn't as happy as he is now, but there was no extreme despair or mental issues.]"

"Hmm."

That was a rather unexpected answer.

[STier Kim Raebin was fine. (Cha Eugene's testimony)]

"So, you're saying that, as far as you can remember, STier Kim Raebin had no aversion to the stage?"

"That's right."

Cha Eugene nodded with a furrowed brow.

"I asked Kim Raebin a lot of questions a few days ago, but Kim Raebin only said he couldn't perform. [Sometimes, it was as if he didn't know the cause himself.]"

So he had conducted his own investigation before STier Kim Raebin's memories disappeared, but he had failed.

I nodded.

Good testimony.

"Then it's simple."

"What?"

"Here, look."

I drew on the dining table with my finger.

"The last time you saw Raebin during your STier days was when you went to America right after the group disbanded."

"That's right."

Cha Eugene's departure for America.

I marked a dot there and continued drawing a line with my finger.

"But the Kim Raebin who regained his STier memories was a guy who had even experienced military enlistment. He remembers things that happened after you went to America."

"...Yep."

Just as Cha Eugene had worked at a repair shop in America, and just as Lee Sejin A had finally won his lawsuit, Kim Raebin had also experienced something more during that period.

"The only difference between the STier Kim Raebin you remember and this one is this period."

[The period after the group's disbandment.]

I dusted off my hands.

"So, the problem occurred after you went to America."

"Oh."

In other words, the short period from STier's disbandment to the completion of his basic military training was now the prime suspect.

*Of course, this is all under the assumption that Cha Eugene had a good grasp of Kim Raebin's condition during their STier days.*

I believed this assumption had an extremely high probability of being correct.

Cha Eugene was a pretty sharp guy. The problem was that he usually didn't use his perceptiveness.

*But during STier, he would have had no choice but to use it.*

In extreme situations, people develop senses they never knew they had. A guy who was already perceptive would become even more so.

If this guy said that his fellow group member, a close friend of the same age, had 'no mental issues', it meant he really didn't have any.

Cha Eugene, who had been listening, readily agreed.

"OK. So what do we do?"

He didn't seem to doubt for a second that I would have the answer. I appreciated that.

I nodded and, as always, spoke without a moment's hesitation.

"We'll have to start thinking about that now."

"...What?"

"Let's brainstorm based on this information."

You use that perceptiveness of yours too.

After assigning the task to the bewildered Cha Eugene, I returned to my room.

For the record, the person in question who couldn't perform, my roommate Kim Raebin, had his head down on his desk.

*I told him to rest.*

He looked less like he was resting and more like he had completely collapsed.

The guy wore the headphones he received from a fan during our early debut days, the ones with a rabbit and an energy drink on them, and was completely still.

"......"

He looked scared and also dejected.

*Good grief.*

I need to solve this quickly. I sat on the edge of my bed.

Well... I had told Cha Eugene it was as if I had no immediate ideas, but I actually did.

It was a method I had even put my thumbprint on, promising to 'do it after reaching an agreement'. I was just waiting for a better time to bring it up rather than saying it right away.

Forcibly digging into the System.

I rubbed my chin.

*If I dig in, I feel like something will come up.*

If I accessed the System one more time, just like when I found Lee Sejin A...

[Aaaah, don't even dream of it, Hyung!]

"......"

Seriously... when did you hear me? It popped up before I could even say anything.

I shut my mouth upon seeing Keundal's pop-up.

Well. Since I was accessing it by taking over the Ryu Gunwoo body that he was supposed to be using, I couldn't really complain if he felt resistant.

*Still, the first attempt ended safely, so I feel like I can do it even more safely this time.*

I responded as calmly as possible.

The pop-up was silent for a while before another one suddenly appeared.

[Th-Then I'll try, so please wait a little!]

What?

What was this now?

*Keundal.*

But there was no reply. It seemed the guy had accessed my System.

*Hey, I can do it, so why should I let you?*

This kind of thing is best done by someone who's done it before.

I called out to him a few more times, but as expected, there was no answer.

*Damn it.*

I clicked my tongue and stopped calling him for now. Since he had already logged in, interfering could mess things up, so it was best to leave him be.

Since he had lived as a Status Window, his understanding of the System was high. He might come back with an unexpected gain.

*Should I work on other things first?*

I immediately turned my head and opened my mouth.

"Raebin."

"Yes, yes!"

Kim Raebin, who had his head on the desk, shot his head up.

Having him as a roommate is convenient for things like this.

Instead of asking things like 'Do you really not remember?', 'Does anything come to mind?', or 'Let's try to recall the situation on stage again', I said this.

"Let me see your work."

"Pardon?"

I demanded he open his hard drive as if I were shaking him down for money.

Kim Raebin nodded with a dazed expression.

"These seven songs are the ones I've worked on recently."

On the screen of the heavy laptop he presented, several files were open in a composition program.

I took the spare headphones he offered and started listening to the songs.

"Thanks."

"Not at all! Um, but for what reason are you listening to the songs..."

I glanced at his complexion.

Tension, anxiety, confusion.

*He's conscious that I might bring up the stage.*

Bringing up that topic in this state would be a bad move.

So I replied nonchalantly.

"I was curious. I also felt like I haven't been paying much attention to producing lately."

And I gave a slight smile.

"It's nice being roommates. I can listen in whenever I want."

"Hyung..."

The guy, who was about to say, 'No, you've paid more than enough attention', seemed quite moved.

It meant I had succeeded in diverting his attention.

*Good.*

I listened to the seven songs on repeat, analyzing their structure, and even after hearing his explanations, I pressed the play button several more times.

It was to buy time.

"Oh~ Moondae Moondae, are you listening to Raebin's songs?"

"What are you guys doing? I want to join!"

As I was listening on repeat, the guys who had just finished washing up and the ones who had returned from their schedules joined in, turning it into a group listening session.

Seeing even the members who wouldn't normally come, it was clear they had come out of concern for Kim Raebin and decided to stick around to offer some encouragement after seeing the atmosphere.

"Alright, now playing track number 3~"

"Yay!"

CLAP, CLAP, CLAP.

And in this sudden listening party atmosphere, Kim Raebin finally started to get nervous for a different reason.

Soon, a relaxed, rhythmic, and smooth ballad filled the room.

After everyone finished listening, Ryu Chungwoo smiled and said to him.

"This song is really good, Raebin. I especially like the melody."

"Thank you! This is a demo version where I composed the top line first and then built a beat that suited the melody..."

Finally, he was reacting instinctively without thinking.

After a few more songs passed and I confirmed that his prepared anxiety had disappeared, I subtly made my move.

"Oh Raebin~ You've been using those headphones for a long time, huh? How about taking a picture of you wearing them and posting it on your account?"

While Keun Sejin was talking to Kim Raebin, I took the chance to grab the laptop mouse again and opened the file explorer.

What Kim Raebin had shown me was the 'latest songs' folder. He had picked out seven songs from there.

*But if you sort all the files in the folder by date... there was one more song among the seven.*

[62_06.wav]

This was it.

I looked at the file and then threw a question at Kim Raebin as if I had just remembered something.

"Did you make this number 6 song recently too? The save date is recent."

"Ah, that... I think it's a song I made when I didn't have my memories."

"Ahh."

Meaning, he made it when he only had his STier Kim Raebin memories.

I actually already knew.

*I checked all the file creation dates.*

It was from that exact period.

However, I nodded naturally.

"It must have been awkward to introduce it since you don't remember making it, but I bet you've listened to it."

"Yes, since it was saved on my PC, I naturally went through the process of checking it!"

I quickly asked him as he nodded eagerly.

"Was there any part of this song that made you think, 'If I had made this, I wouldn't have made it this way'? Not in terms of whether the song is good or bad, but in terms of the composition method."

At that moment, the other chattering members subtly quieted down. Even the ones who weren't usually perceptive seemed to have squeezed out some awareness, given the situation. Good job.

Kim Raebin's face briefly became more intense as he fell into thought, but he soon widened his eyes and answered.

"Ah! I did feel a sense of incongruity."

"You did?"

"That song seemed to be made with the members of our group in mind, but my part was missing!"

"Huh?"

At that moment, unable to hold back, Bae Sejin cut in.

"What do you mean?"

"Well... when I compose with the owner of the song already decided, I tend to write while being conscious of the individual characteristics of each performer."

Kim Raebin explained earnestly.

"This song seems to have been made in a similar way... but there's no part that seems to be allocated to me!"

Bae Sejin's expression hardened.

"So... this song seems to be written for TeSTAR, but you're the only one without a part?"

"That's how I analyze it."

Kim Raebin nodded.

"So I guessed that perhaps... the person who made it had no intention of performing on stage in the first place..."

"......"

It seemed Kim Raebin had been making these kinds of deductions when he had his head on the desk earlier.

*Hmm.*

I looked at the file on the screen again.

STier Kim Raebin had definitely tried to recover his TeSTAR-era memories 'to contribute to this team by performing on stage again'.

In that situation, wouldn't he have naturally tried to make a group song assuming that his recovered self would be performing?

*He wouldn't have not at least tried.*

If so, this meant that Kim Raebin had failed even when he tried to put his own part into the song.

In other words, his aversion to the stage was so strong and resolute that he 'couldn't' add his part, not that he 'didn't'.

"......"

I held back a sigh. I had been complacent.

Because STier Kim Raebin seemed to enjoy composing and was adapting so well to the team, everyone was relatively less worried. However, the one who was actually struggling the most might have been him.

*I need to approach this more delicately.*

I deliberately spoke to Kim Raebin in a calm and relaxed manner.

"In any case, you want to perform on stage right now, right?"

"Of course!"

Kim Raebin answered in an instant. I smiled faintly.

"Then that's all that matters. With seven of us, if we keep trying, one of our attempts is bound to work."

I nudged Keun Sejin's shoulder. He immediately provided backup.

"Ah, of course~ With this kind of mental stuff, the most important thing is that the person himself wants to do it! Raebin has already met all the conditions, right, Ahyeon?"

"Y-Yeah, that's right...! I hope... you don't worry too much."

"...! Yes, thank you!"

Kim Raebin's complexion brightened, as if he felt a little relieved.

And I thought. I need to find this guy's memories, and fast.

However, that night.

[There's no memory data, Hyung!]

I almost slammed my head against the headboard.

*There's none?*

[Yeah... I even went to that terminal you mentioned, but it wasn't there ㅠㅠ]

Son of a bitch.

As I cursed at the System, a small, dejected-looking pop-up appeared.

[I'm sorry. Maybe it's because I'm not skilled enough to find it...]

No, that couldn't be it.

*He's not the type to do a half-assed search.*

If Keundal searched this thoroughly and still couldn't find it, there was a high probability that it really wasn't there.

In that case...

If those memories didn't return to the System, then.

[The memories of STier Kim Raebin must still be with him.]

In that moment, a realization struck me.

I had been mistaken.

Because it was a 'preview', I had assumed that the moment the function ended, the System also retrieved Kim Raebin's STier memories.

*But that wasn't it.*

It meant that the STier-era Kim Raebin was absorbed into his current self, but wasn't coming out.

"......"

I sat on my bed, reviewing the chain of reasoning and thought several times, and finally came to a conclusion.

*You have to knock on the door for someone who's holed up in their room.*

A stimulus was needed.

***

After failing to properly perform at the wedding.

While resting in his room at the dorm, Kim Raebin was constantly thinking.

*What on earth is the cause?*

He was thinking about a rational reason for experiencing such severe stiffness on stage.

In fact, he had tried a few solutions over the past two days.

He received gentle suggestions from Bae Sejin and Seon Ahyeon about professional counseling, and an invitation from Ryu Chungwoo to join him in physical leisure activities like shooting or hiking.

However, none of it could alleviate his anxiety. Since he was fine during practice, he wouldn't know until he was in a 'real' situation.

That was why Kim Raebin, who had become more obsessed with the cause, even made this gloomy guess.

*Maybe the problem is that I can't remember.*

Even Cha Eugene remembered!

Park Moondae had dismissed it, saying 'it's just a matter of method', but in this situation, Kim Raebin couldn't help but worry and reflect.

But he also knew that thinking too deeply would lead to being swallowed by it, so he tried not to obsess over the stage itself.

He had experience with this before.

*This is a similar situation to when I had a slump with composing.*

Kim Raebin, who was fiddling with his composition program, nodded resolutely.

Then, it suddenly occurred to him.

*Wait, composing?*

A spark went off in Kim Raebin's head like a firework!

If this situation was similar to his composing slump, what if he tried a similar response?

Enjoying the stage purely as a hobby while remaining anonymous.

*Back then, with the hyungs' help, I even made an account called Song of the Stars!*

Thinking about that time, a warm feeling settled in his chest.

It was hope and happiness.

Slightly flushed, he got up to convey his reasoning and looked for his roommate Park Moondae, who happened to be right next to him, but...

"Good timing, I was about to talk to you. It's about this album's teaser."

"Yes?"

"What do you think about making an intro track for it? A short one, something we can do within the current timeframe."

Sometimes TeSTAR put a lot of effort into their teasers to create a detailed worldview, but other times they didn't.

This time, they had been thinking of the latter and putting their energy into the album itself.

Adding an intro now could mean an increased workload, but Kim Raebin readily nodded his head.

The plans Moondae proposed always went well!

"I'm fine with it! Since it's a video introducing the album, I'll make sure to structure it well so that each element and member can appear harmoniously."

And now, he was about to bring up his own matter...

"No. You're going to do it alone."

"......"

"This album's intro is your solo song."

Kim Raebin doubted his own ears, but Park Moondae was still smiling faintly.

"Make a song you want to sing."

Kim Raebin's vision went blank.
