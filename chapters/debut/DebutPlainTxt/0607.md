# Chapter 607

"Kim Raebin! Kim Raebin!"

"Thank you!"

The filming for ❬Random Play Dance❭ wrapped up. I watched the Green Team become a frenzied Kim Raebin praise squad and shook my head at them and the excited camera crew.

*They'll probably be like that for a while.*

It was understandable. After the ghost story courage test, Kim Raebin scored a second consecutive solo win. He was the main reason his team won and the key to TeSTAR's variety show victory.

*To think he'd win like that.*

A rapper had pushed aside all the formidable dancers to win. He was a walking hot topic. It was rare to find someone who pulled their weight so thoroughly, even in unexpected areas.

*He delivers more than one big moment every time.*

I chuckled as I watched him look so happy.

Kim Raebin finally finished his interview with the production team. He broke away from the Green Team and received congratulations from the TeSTAR members.

Then, someone brought it up.

"Ah, now that I think about it, Eugene was eliminated first and Raebin lasted until the very end."

"Oh, you're right!"

They were talking about how Kim Raebin and Cha Eugene had bickered over who was better at Random Play Dance. It had been a while since we had seen such a clear-cut victory.

"Wow~ Raebin's method was the right one!"

"Yeah, that's amazing!"

"That is correct," Kim Raebin said proudly. "It's a claim proven by fact, so I won't hesitate to make this immodest statement!"

Kim Raebin turned his proud face toward Cha Eugene, who sat in the loser's section after being eliminated early.

He then shouted, "I won, so acknowledge it and strive to improve!"

"Uuuu..."

Cha Eugene looked sullen, but he eventually slumped his shoulders and nodded.

"OK. Kim Raebin lasted the longest."

"...!"

"Congratulations."

I saw it. Kim Raebin made a small, emotional gesture, looking up at the sky. It was a rare sight.

*Is that a goal celebration?*

Of course, being thoughtful, he immediately tried to encourage the loser instead of teasing him.

"Confidence is a virtue for an idol, but from now on, you should get into the habit of listening more to the advice of others, Cha Eugene!"

"OK."

Cha Eugene nodded obediently. Kim Raebin reverted to his serious youngest member persona, bowing politely to us before leaving.

"Then I'll go and change my clothes!"

"Alright."

His steps were still full of excitement. I watched Kim Raebin enter the MT building, then turned to offer some comfort to the defeated Cha Eugene.

He already had a nonchalant expression on his face.

"......"

"...You lost, are you okay?" Bae Sejin asked, bewildered.

Cha Eugene shrugged. "Lasting a long time in the Challenge isn't the most important thing. I was cooler than Kim Raebin."

"...!"

"Then why didn't you talk back earlier?"

"Because I'm a Good boy who is considerate of his friend's feelings!"

Fluent and cheerful English followed.

[Kim Raebin got to have an experience he'll never have again, thinking he beat me in dancing. I'm just letting him enjoy it, you know.]

He lay back on the grass, whistling as he started to enjoy his break.

"......"

"......"

He was truly an amazing guy.

*Neither of them will ever admit defeat to the other.*

They were just like two guys of the same age with similar abilities.

Bae Sejin, who had watched the whole scene, quietly opened his mouth.

"It seems a little annoying... but I think I can understand the feeling of satisfaction."

"You think so?" I asked.

Well, he might think that way since he, like Cha Eugene, was eliminated after leaving a strong impression.

*That basic practice song.*

The intense choreography, filled with all sorts of steps and moves, was probably not what the public expected from Bae Sejin.

"No, I'm not saying I danced as well as Cha Eugene!" Bae Sejin muttered with a hint of pride. "The song selection was lucky... and Lee Sejin gave me the signal right on time."

"Still, it was the result of your practice, hyung."

Bae Sejin did not deny it. He talked about how he felt with a shy expression, then resolved,*I should definitely thank Lee Sejin.*

"......"

I watched Bae Sejin head toward his teammate with the same name.

However, another person besides Lee Sejin had contributed to this situation. I walked over and stood quietly next to the guy sitting with a pleasant expression among the Green Team members praising Kim Raebin.

Ryu Chungwoo.

*Bae Sejin thought he was lucky, but...*

"You did it, didn't you, hyung. That."

"......"

"Bae Sejin's song selection."

"Ah. You saw."

It was obvious even without seeing it. After Ryu Chungwoo said something to T-Holic, Bae Sejin's practice song came on right after. Anyone who knew the situation could have guessed it.

He had specifically picked that practice song and pushed for its selection so Bae Sejin could shine.

"Sejin lasted for so long, so I hoped he wouldn't feel like it was a waste that he held on."

"That was an excellent judgment."

"Thanks."

Ryu Chungwoo accepted the compliment without deflecting. "I don't think there's a need to emphasize it to Sejin," he added, then grinned.

"Ah, but I wasn't the one who requested that trot-style art song in the final round."

"......"

"It was a song I didn't even know in the first place."

Well, that made sense. Seomjin River Lover. The only person among the cast who would have watched and remembered it well enough to know the choreography was Kim Raebin.

He might be the only one not just among the cast, but among all active idols.

*The production team probably put it in as a joke, for a choreography creation time or a break.*

"Raebin was probably the only one who knew it."

"That's right," Ryu Chungwoo readily agreed.

"Raebin really is a genius."

"Yes."

"Yeah... It's nice to see everyone showcasing their skills like this."

"......"

Before I knew it, a changed Kim Raebin was running out of the building. Bae Sejin was talking with Lee Sejin.

Ryu Chungwoo glanced at them for a moment, then turned to look at the red sun. It was about time for sunset.

"You also showed your skills as a Leader, hyung."

I was talking about his split-second judgment to put a member in the right place at the right time.

Ryu Chungwoo looked slightly surprised before his smile returned.

"Really? Hmm, my skills..."

His words trailed off.

A soft voice spoke amidst the quiet sunset.

"It would be nice if we could keep doing this."

"...?"

*What was that? Why is he using such a meaningful tone again?*

I asked hesitantly, "Are you perhaps thinking of not renewing your contract?"

"Huh? Moondae, it hasn't even been that long since we renewed our contracts."

Fortunately, Ryu Chungwoo laughed as if the idea was absurd and denied it immediately.

"Your tone sounded like you weren't sure about the future."

"Is that so?"

Ryu Chungwoo looked back at the sunset.

"Maybe it's because our promotion period is starting soon."

"......"

It was true that this promotion period was unique. We were betting everything and charging straight in, unable to rationally predict our chances of winning.

"I'll do my best."

"Me too."

We sat silently during a quiet break in filming for interviews. Ryu Chungwoo and I thought about the schedule that would follow this variety show.

The long-awaited new song release.

***

After the sun had set, during a lull in the interviews, Kwon Heeseung of Spacer approached.

"Ah, that was really fun, hyungs!"

"Hey~ We had fun too!"

He grinned and shook hands enthusiastically with Lee Sejin.

*His expression looks good.*

It seemed he felt that Spacer's screen time would turn out well.

The production team's style suggested they would distribute screen time evenly among the groups instead of focusing only on big names. On top of that, there was the position as T-Holic's successor.

[They're calling them the MGen Z T-Holic? Hey, we're MZ too!]

[Does anyone have a picture of us promoting in our 20s?? I feel like we were kind of similar...]

[To all Spacer fans. The opinions above are the personal views of Martin and Ha Jintae ahjussi. We earnestly plead that you understand this is not the official stance of T-Holic...]

It was pretty funny.

Kwon Heeseung, perhaps anticipating this, sidled up to me. "Actually, if you TeSTAR hyungs hadn't appeared, I was thinking I might have to use the wish coupon you gave me last time..."

Hmm.

He meant the one I gave him as a reward for the trouble with the server and the System, but we appeared before he could even use that 'wish coupon'.

"You were being considerate of us, right? Aigoo, at this rate, I think I can count this as having used the wish coupon!"

I laughed quietly.

"There's no need for that."

"...Wait a minute. Does that mean this appearance was also part of your grand plan, Moondae! Woah, as expected."

It was not.

He went on about how it would 'ignite the buzz' and marveled at our bold decision to have a simultaneous release with VTIC.

"Honestly, maybe because I'm not that magnanimous, I was the one who felt nervous... If it were me, I would have avoided it."

No, we wanted to as well.

*We were blindsided.*

But Kwon Heeseung just smiled brightly.

"But, I think I know why you hyungs are so confident."

"Oh?"

"I saw it at the company. That."

He winked and mouthed the words 'TeSTAR' and 'this album's content'. Then he bowed deeply and quickly disappeared.

"I'll look forward to the release!"

"......"

He was clearly trying to get on my good side, yet he strangely did not make me feel bad.

*Is it because he's surprisingly good at figuring out the truth?*

I chuckled.

It was then.

"Hmm, it seems your junior's company is confident about this album."

Fuck.

*He made no sound.*

I turned my head. Cheongryeo, who had finished his interview, stood next to me.

He smiled.

"Surprised? I'm sorry. Anyway. I'm curious if you enjoyed this shoot."

"......"

It would be easy to retort with 'Do you think I would have?' and keep us in check. After a brief thought, I opened my mouth.

"Sunbae."

"Hmm?"

"Why did you suggest this variety show?"

"......"

I realized it during filming.

"It's true that this variety show was beneficial to VTIC, but I think it was just as beneficial to us."

He had certainly said it would be 'mutually beneficial'. I had expected some brutally savage strategy to beat and devour TeSTAR, who are good at variety shows, even with the 'no-fun' VTIC members.

In the end, the show was not that savage.

VTIC simply did their best to secure their own parts. They did not try to crush TeSTAR's characters. It meant Cheongryeo had not adopted a strategy to kill his opponent.

I wondered why he would play this fair, especially after blindsiding us with the album release date. It was not unthreatening, but it was reasonable.

*What's the reason?*

I stared at him.

Cheongryeo calmly opened his mouth.

"Ah. I already told you. This variety show is meant to expand the scale of the buzz. Hmm... should I put it this way?"

He smiled and tapped his chin.

"Junior. This group variety show is content that shouldn't create excessive fatigue."

"...!"

"Fomenting conflict in a mere variety show is tiresome... That's a failure of pacing. The climax has to happen in our respective content."

Cheongryeo explained in a low voice.

"The dynamic of VTIC and TeSTAR devouring each other is a one-time event that can only be used at this exact moment. We can't do something so wasteful."

"......"

TeSTAR and VTIC's promotions had overlapped a few times before, but this was the first time their levels were so similar that the outcome was unpredictable.

And perhaps it might be the last.

"Even if we have a simultaneous release again in the future, it'll be hard to create this kind of dynamic. Buzz is like the sense of smell."

"The sense of smell."

"It's the human sense that tires the fastest and grows dull. You could say it adapts quickly to the situation."

Cheongryeo gave a slight smile.

"The public gets tired and accustomed to things just as quickly."

So, he used this variety show purely as an amplifier.

"Since it's a moment that won't come again, we have to cultivate it carefully, maintaining balance... and use it well."

"......"

I never thought I would say this, but...

*A moment that won't come again.*

*Now this bastard knows how to say something sensible.*

I laughed quietly.

"It's strange hearing those words come from your mouth."

"Is it?"

Yeah.

"But you're right."

"...!"

The comeback date still pissed me off, but still.

I extended my hand to him.

"Thanks to your recommendation, I enjoyed the shoot. Sunbae, ...let's see each other at a music show next time."

"...Don't mention it."

The handshake was brief and firm.

And now, it was time for the variety show MT filming to reach its grand finale, along with the main purpose for this show.

"Resuming filming!"

"Yep!"

I did not know VTIC's new song, and the VTIC members would not know TeSTAR's new song either. The only certain things were what I had planned and prepared.

And what I prepared with the other TeSTAR members... as I have said time and again.

*I'm confident.*

"Now, please watch!"

We ran onto the grassy field that had become familiar over the past day. We smiled and took our positions.

"TeSTAR's new song performance starts now!"

***

A few days later, at midnight on the long-awaited comeback date.

"......"

Amidst anticipation, impatience, resentment, excitement, anxiety, and a pounding heart, TeSTAR's music video was released.
