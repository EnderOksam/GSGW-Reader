# Chapter 502

An old public broadcast program once existed.

The show had passed its peak and was barely clinging to life before it ended with a whimper.

*But its name is still famous thanks to its glory days.*

In other words, while most of the nation knew the program, they no longer wanted to watch it.

It was an entertainment show that had been sucked dry of all its buzz and fun before being put out of its misery.

If this show, through connections, received a charitable opportunity to launch a renewal season, would anyone have expectations for it?

Would it be possible to cast guests worthy of the reputation it earned in its prime long ago?

The answer was obvious.

*It wouldn't work.*

Celebrities and their agencies weren't fools. Popular people appeared on currently popular programs. They would get offers anyway.

*Why would they choose something so risky?*

When better alternatives existed, it was an automatic pass.

*And... considering the nature of the show, it's impossible.*

This variety show was a competition where rankings were assigned.

❬Genuine Match ❭.

Perhaps some people might remember this name.

On the very first day of the meeting, after reading the documents, even Kim Raebin raised his hand and exclaimed.

"This is the show we appeared on when we debuted as Wish!"

"That's right."

It was the program the joint TeSTAR and VTIC group, which debuted under LeTi, appeared on in the virtual world the System created.

"Oh, I remember. That show was famous, but why is it failing now?"

"Because that was already several years ago."

"......"

In that world, it was a successful competition show that thrived to Season 7, but here, with more time having passed, it faced a different reality.

Its buzz gradually diminished over Seasons 15 and 16. After the original PD left again, it failed.

I calmly stated the solemn truth.

"And the general consensus in the industry is that this upcoming renewal will probably fail, too."

"......"

*It can't be helped.*

With rumors of low expectations spreading this far, casting famous celebrities would be difficult.

However, it still had name recognition. If it failed to cast famous celebrities, any hope of a rebound was out of the question.

It would just fall into obscurity without getting any attention.

In short, it was a failed program destined for failure.

Even Keun Sejin, who flipped through the documents, clicked his tongue.

"Wow~ They even added survival rules."

"Hmm, casting must be really tough for them."

On top of that, they had apparently adopted a full survival format to make it more sensational. This would make casting big names even more difficult.

*Why would someone who's already famous appear on a show where the probability of loss is higher than gain?*

Existing survival shows usually featured amateurs who hadn't debuted or people who were not well-known to the public for a reason.

They had to have more to gain than to lose to risk situations like elimination and ridicule.

*The PD must have a headache.*

Therefore, there was realistically only one way to overcome this situation.

[Securing the appearance of at least one star with immense name value.]

From there, they could open the floodgates for casting.

Big names willing to participate could certainly emerge, depending on their circumstances, once it was confirmed they could build on that foundation and be acknowledged by the public with a 'they fought well' or 'it couldn't be helped' even if they lost.

However, even with the show's programming just around the corner, they naturally couldn't secure such a superstar cast.

"...But you're saying we should go on it anyway."

"Yes."

Bae Sejin narrowed his eyes. His expression clearly showed he had a lot to say. Ryu Chungwoo laughed awkwardly.

"The production team's reaction was good."

"Of course it was!"

Right.

Just as expected, the program's side bit immediately the moment TeSTAR dangled the bait.

[I don't think we've ever had this happen with another broadcast company... You're being very proactive.]

According to an agency employee, they had never heard of a public broadcast production team making such a polite and desperate meeting request.

That's right. These guys were desperate for a single, popular celebrity to act as their totem.

What if TeSTAR became that very totem?

"If our participation solves their casting difficulties, it seems likely they'll give us a good edit, and..."

"And?"

"I think we can leave a good impression on MBS through this program."

"...!"

We'd become the founding contributors of the renewal.

We could secure the same position we had during ❬Re-listed ! Idol Inc. ❭.

Although public broadcasters now lose out on buzz to OTT programs, there are still plenty of benefits to reap, from year-end music festivals to music shows and weekend variety shows.

*And they won't be able to work us to the bone as if we owe them for our debut.*

We could establish a cooperative relationship on equal footing.

Nominally the Son of Public Broadcasting, but in reality, more like an ally.

Besides, there was one more advantage.

"And the PD's rank is high. I think the effect will be even better."

"Ooh... That's true."

A person promoted due to their years of experience and good connections would naturally have more influence within the company than a rookie who just got appointed.

However, Keun Sejin immediately cut to the heart of the matter.

"But isn't influence more important than rank? Seeing as he took on this program, I'm guessing he's the type to work according to his own style rather than chasing success~"

He implied that taking on a failing program meant the PD had been cast aside in internal politics and was left with an empty title.

I let out a small laugh.

"It seems there's a separate reason he took on this program."

"Really?"

"Yeah. He apparently started his career as a PD at the broadcast station with Season 3 of this show."

"Ah."

He probably took on the renewal out of a lingering attachment. The fact that he couldn't secure a large budget even with his rank meant the station had no expectations for the show.

This was despite the fact that the programs this PD had directed in the past had a considerable success rate in terms of entertainment value.

"Then if this goes well... Oh, the broadcast station would be very surprised, and that PD would get recognition, too~"

"Yeah, a good result for everyone...!"

Keun Sejin meant that all of this would create a synergy from which we had a lot to gain. Seon Ahyeon, at any rate, seemed pleased that it would be a great help not only to us but also to the production team.

*Well, they're both right.*

However, for all of this to come true, there was one prerequisite.

"For that to happen, we have to win, no matter what."

An overwhelming victory, acknowledged by the vast majority of viewers.

The performance.

Without that, this whole endeavor would be a complete net loss.

It meant that the slightest mistake would be an act of madness, chipping away at the top-tier reputation TeSTAR had built up until now.

However.

"If we just win, this is the best possible move."

Once we broke through to a public broadcast network, it would be easier to negotiate with other platforms. The hurdle would be lower.

If things went well, we might even be able to appear on one of T1's affiliate's variety shows.

*If that happens.*

Then our domestic promotion worries would be over.

"Hmm... We need to maintain our popularity and buzz in Korea so that people will listen to our music even when we target overseas markets."

"Yes."

"Wait a minute."

"Isn't this a bit reckless? There's no guarantee the production team will follow through. Even if we do well, this is an industry where things can fail... due to other factors."

"......"

"...I'm not trying to ruin the mood. I just... felt like I had to say it at least once."

I know.

"Yes. You're right, hyung."

"...!"

"And to be honest, even if we don't appear on this show, it's not like we'll suddenly fail."

If TeSTAR was satisfied with its current position and wanted to face a gentle decline, then we wouldn't have to do something like this.

We had won a Grand Prize and done a world tour. TeSTAR's name value would surely remain strong for at least two more years.

However.

"But if we want to keep climbing even as we gain more experience, we have to secure a platform somehow."

Just like VTIC was doing now.

This was the most crucial time.

"......"

Bae Sejin nodded with a slightly heavy expression. A hint of grim determination also flickered across the faces of the other guys.

"OK, I'm totally ready for that! Let's go on that show already!"

Except for that guy.

As if by agreement, the other guys ignored Cha Eugene's shout and nodded.

"Let's put it to a majority vote."
