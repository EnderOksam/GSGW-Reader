# Chapter 515

Ryu Chungwoo was submerged in his own subconscious. He recalled the days he had spent desperately adapting to his sudden new environment and circumstances. He also remembered the tenacity that flared like a flame when he met his last chance.

[Chungwoo?]

However, that too was over.

"Chungwoo!"

*Ah.*

Ryu Chungwoo lifted his head. An unfamiliar manager had called to him. The man had said he was a new manager for TeSTAR, and Ryu Chungwoo had only recently memorized his name.

*And the first manager... I think they said he caused a car accident...*

The information held little meaning now. For some reason, just thinking about it made Ryu Chungwoo's mood sink. He hid his feelings, however, and responded calmly.

"Yes."

"I'm going to buy some drinks. What would you like?"

Ryu Chungwoo knew the manager's routine. He bought drinks once in the morning when TeSTAR's schedule began, and again between 2 and 3 PM after lunch. This situation was a special case, falling outside those times. The practice room was in the middle of filming.

"Ah, thank you. In that case, a latte..."

He could hear the low murmurs of the production crew as other company employees asked them for drink orders. Ryu Chungwoo impassively scanned the ❬Genuine Match❭ staff filling the edges of the practice room, then spoke.

"Whatever's easiest for you to order, please."

That was enough.

And so, the final days of STier Ryu Chungwoo began.

***

Originally, STier Ryu Chungwoo had planned to disappear, or rather, to 'regain his memories' as soon as his schedule ended on his declared day. However, things did not go according to plan.

[That would be difficult.]

Park Moondae, the unfamiliar member who was supposedly his relative, had disapproved. He cited 'variable risk' as the reason.

[I think you'll be confused for a while after you get your memories back. Wouldn't it be a bit dangerous during a competition program? There will be a lot of cameras on you.]

It was a logical argument. That was why Ryu Chungwoo now sat here, feeling completely useless. He could have retorted, "Why should I care?", but he did not.

*It's not like I'm performing on stage anyway.*

Filling an empty spot for a few days was just a waiting game. It was tiring, but manageable.

Ryu Chungwoo drank the beverage he was given. It was a hot Americano.

"They said that one's decaf."

"......"

"Sejin did."

The person who approached him with a blank expression was also out of the camera's spotlight. He was the same person who had just popped into Ryu Chungwoo's mind.

*Park Moondae.*

This member was sitting out of the competition, just like Ryu Chungwoo and Kim Raebin. He did not know the exact reason, but he figured it did not matter.

Before, he might have subtly tried to find out if Park Moondae had dropped out to watch him, or what kind of plan he was hatching. He no longer had the drive for that.

Ryu Chungwoo just nodded slightly and kept drinking his Americano. Park Moondae also said nothing. He held his own drink and watched the 'TeSTAR unit' members practice for the camera.

"......"

A silence fell between them.

"Thank you~ Oh, coffee!"

"Is that my cake? Wow."

The practice room grew noisy. The filming of the practice session must have been sufficient, because the 'TeSTAR unit' stopped their choreography and accepted the drinks and snacks with smiles.

At that moment, Park Moondae, who had been sitting beside him, stood up as if on cue.

"Then let's get moving, too. The preparations are done."

"...Preparations?"

"Preparations for filming."

"I said I wouldn't be performing on stage for this competition."

What filming was he talking about?

Park Moondae looked back at him as if the question was strange. His gaze was almost bewildered.

"Just because we're not performing doesn't mean we won't be on the show."

"......"

"We have to help create the stage. A ton of work goes into completing a performance besides just being on stage."

It was at this point that Ryu Chungwoo began to feel that something was off. By the time he came to his senses, he was already in an interview with Park Moondae and Kim Raebin.

"Ta-da. We're the three contract producers for this first round of the competition."

*...?*

"We'll work hard."

CLAP, CLAP, CLAP.

With Park Moondae clapping in the center, he could see Kim Raebin on the other side, clapping along in a daze.

"......"

*Should I clap too?*

Before that question could fully form, Park Moondae's words came rushing out. He mentioned Kim Raebin's arrangement and his own theme selection.

"Since we're a group that self-produces our albums, we wanted to show that side of us, and I think this worked out well. As you all know, when it comes to producing, we're the top three elite members of this group."

"For real?"

"Of course. It's the absolute truth."

The cameraman held back a laugh at Park Moondae's shamelessly quick reply to the PD's amused question.

"And."

All eyes turned to him.

"Chungwoo here will be in charge of the overall picture and review. The feedback."

"WOAAAAH!"

Led by Park Moondae, the production crew responded enthusiastically.

"...Thank you."

Just like that, Ryu Chungwoo was also given a role.

[Head Producer.]

He immediately realized it was just a title, a position to watch the stage and do nothing else. No other demands were made of Ryu Chungwoo in the following days.

Ryu Chungwoo thought the word 'producer' was just a nominal title, so he spent his time uninvolved. Because he did nothing, time flew by.

He wondered if he had ever lived like this before. His life had always been busy, a cycle of extreme training, achievement, and the inevitable downward slide.

*Was it similar to when I quit archery?*

He had not felt so depleted and adrift back then, because it had been painful. Pain makes time slow down, but with no immediate pain to worry about, the next filming day arrived in a flash.

Surprisingly, they had created a performance outline in just three days.

"They said they need our reactions to the performance, so it seems we'll be filmed together."

And so, they moved to a large, lecture hall-like space in the company.

"How's it going?"

"Great~"

The smiling members of TeSTAR stood on a makeshift stage in the cleared center platform. Park Moondae sat in the very front row, exchanging small talk.

"Hey, Raebin...! The song, it was really good!"

"...! Really?! Thank you!"

Even Kim Raebin was talking with members other than Cha Eugene. It probably meant he had communicated often with 'TeSTAR' while working on the arrangement.

*Was that what truly natural adaptation looked like?*

Ryu Chungwoo laughed self-deprecatingly and sat down. He did not feel particularly wronged. After a few adjustments, the performance began right away.

"Ah, but with us standing like this... hmm, I think the balance might be a little off~"

As it happened, the unit of four was a combination of three dance-position members and one vocal-position member. To make matters worse, the three dancers were quite tall, creating a strange disharmony.

"...Me?"

NOD, NOD.

After a gloomy question from Bae Sejin, the 'one remaining member', they readjusted their positions.

"Center~!"

"Oh! Center!"

"...!"

Bae Sejin awkwardly moved to the middle in response to the members' cheers. When Bae Sejin stood in the center, the balance was indeed perfect.

"Hahaha!"

"Are you laughing?"

"......"

The writers desperately covered their mouths. Seon Ahyeon's face turned bright red as he tried to hold back his laughter. That was right. Bae Sejin reacted as if he were annoyed, but the atmosphere was not serious. It was the honesty that came from the unreserved relationships of a good team.

"......"

He could have belonged in this atmosphere. He felt a faint, flashing impulse again, but it soon vanished.

"So! Please keep in mind our formation is temporary since we just adjusted it~"

And the performance began. A bright, cheerful band accompaniment played as a retro theme with a musical-like composition unfolded. The part distribution had clearly been done elegantly to account for differences in individual skills.

*Did I develop some kind of occupational hazard?*Ryu Chungwoo thought with a bitter smile.

However, that was all.

[It's as if between us

There's a special void]

The music swelled. The performance continued. He recalled the fierce and exhausting deliberation over what level of performance quality needed to be secured. Watching a performance without such concerns was comfortable.

[What will become of this gap

We don't know yet

That's true]

For the first time in a very long while, Ryu Chungwoo watched a competitor's stage without a single thought.

[Yes!]

The one-minute demo version of the performance was over in an instant.

"That's all from us~ Thank you!"

Just as he was briefly enveloped in the afterglow, Park Moondae's indifferent words cut in as if he had been waiting.

"Your feedback."

"......"

Ryu Chungwoo felt a slight resistance and bewilderment. He was actually asking for feedback.

*Wasn't it just a title?*

Park Moondae's face was nonchalant, as if he were just asking for something to say on camera.

*I see.*

Ryu Chungwoo realized he did not need to give intense, precise feedback and nodded slightly. Park Moondae immediately asked.

"First, tell us what you liked."

Ryu Chungwoo answered without much thought.

"The movements were light."

"They were. I thought it'd be even better to use a live band to match that, but..."

For a moment, the thought that he had felt a similar impression briefly crossed Ryu Chungwoo's mind, along with a faint sense of familiarity. However, Park Moondae did not press for agreement and continued the conversation smoothly.

"Well, anyway. What are your thoughts on the choreography?"

"It was fine."

"Yes. It's pretty well-made, but... any parts that were lacking?"

He had not paid attention to things like that. Ryu Chungwoo momentarily found a connection from his previous answer.

"Were the movements too flashy for the music?"

"Right. Since the track isn't finished yet."

Park Moondae nodded, glanced at the performance members drinking water, and then took out his smartphone.

"This is the version Raebin is working on now. Ah, Raebin. Is it okay if I play it?"

"Yes...!"

Kim Raebin, who had been applauding the members, jumped in surprise and affirmed. Park Moondae then nonchalantly played the music on his smartphone and passed over a pair of wireless earphones from his pocket.

Caught up in the naturalness of the action, Ryu Chungwoo found himself listening.

*...?*

It was not peculiar enough to refuse, so he listened without much thought.

"It's gotten a bit more flashy, right?"

"It has."

"I think this part could use some more string instruments."

"Ah."

"Would brass be better?"

Strings and brass.

*Hmm.*

Ryu Chungwoo momentarily focused on the music.

"......"

And Park Moondae observed him intently. He reaffirmed his own judgment. It was the same judgment he had made a few days ago when he heard STier Ryu Chungwoo declare he would 'regain his memories'.

[Not like this.]

Nothing good would come from the guy getting his memories back in such a burnt-out state. This was especially true because Ryu Chungwoo was originally mentally strong and so deep it was hard to read him.

So why did the past STier Ryu Chungwoo get so burnt out? There was a clear answer to that.

*He spent too long doing work that gave him no reward.*

It was only natural to get sick of a job that did not suit him and that he did not enjoy, especially if it all ended without any results. The bigger problem was Ryu Chungwoo's mental fortitude. It was so strong that he had endured for an incredibly long time.

Bae Sejin had agreed.

[Besides, I... I mean, hearing the news of the lawsuit victory from my past self also weighs on my mind.]

He had seen the conclusion to the event that motivated him to do such things. It was not refreshing. It just left him feeling empty.

*There's a high probability that became the trigger for Ryu Chungwoo's motivation to disappear completely.*

So, after consulting with Bae Sejin, Park Moondae finalized his plan. It was called 'Pattern Change'.

First.

[Make him break out of the typical role patterns of an idol member and a group Leader.]

And the next part was important.

[However, make it so he can still feel a sense of efficacy from his work.]

*And this guy has already set foot on that path.*

Park Moondae thought this once more and looked at Ryu Chungwoo. Just then, the guy opened his mouth.

"Brass would be better."

He began to offer his opinion.

*That's right.*

Park Moondae smiled inwardly but kept his expression neutral as he continued.

"I guess so... Yes. Raebin said he'll keep working on it tomorrow morning, so I'll have him check it out."

"Mm, okay."

Like clothes soaking in a light drizzle, Ryu Chungwoo slowly but surely began to participate in completing the stage.

That was right.

Park Moondae planned to make the real Ryu Chungwoo the head producer.
