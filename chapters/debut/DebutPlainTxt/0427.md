# Chapter 427

VTIC's charity concert quickly took shape. The lineup was spectacular.

[Whoa, Maximite is performing too.]

[Didn't VTIC just hang out with their own crowd? How'd they convince everyone?]

[Wait, the people they invited are inviting more people. Isn't this a pyramid scheme? A chain-letter style of guest recruitment, hahaha.]

The guest list included famous idols, solo balladeers, rappers, and even trot singers.

News even broke that SoftSweet would reunite for the first time in a while to perform. The event's popularity soared to the level of a show hosted by a major variety program.

[So if I go to see TeSTAR, I get to see all those other people too?]

That wasn't the case.

*The guests were planned exclusively for the broadcast.*

The concert would be divided into two parts. Part 1 featured only VTIC, while Part 2 featured the guests.

Only Part 1 would be an offline event with fans. The pre-recorded Part 2 with the guests would be mixed in and broadcast simultaneously online and on CVN.

*They're smart.*

Fans who bought tickets to see the full VTIC lineup before their hiatus would have been pissed if guests filled half the concert.

By avoiding that issue, and since every guest had great name recognition, the event's prestige also rose. This nicely solidified the public standing of the host, VTIC.

*This must be Cheongryeo's handiwork.*

Thanks to him, the broadcast station was thrilled and already advertising like crazy.

*It would have been a bit awkward if we had refused this.*

Seeing how things played out, I had no regrets about deciding to appear.

Since the format was similar, some WeTube drama channel bastard would have definitely tried to compare it to our ambiguous, bundled-in public broadcast concert.

[Is this the difference in planning? The class and dignity of LeTi's VTIC VS the fallen T1 Stars' TeSTAR.]

Something like that.

As expected, you had to check for traps first when getting involved with the VTIC guys. I nodded, having dodged a landmine.

Time passed, and it was the afternoon of the second Sunday in October.

TeSTAR was in the broadcast station's waiting room, changing into our outfits.

"Come to think of it, it feels like it's been a while since we've all done something together as TeSTAR."

"You're right~ It's probably because it's been quite a while since we've appeared on a music show."

Today, we were scheduled to go on stage for the pre-recording of the charity concert, ❬VTIC's Save the World❭.

In other words, the audience was filled only with TeSTAR fans who had been invited for the broadcast.

*This is going to be just like a music show.*

I chuckled, suddenly remembering our first pre-recording right after our debut. Hadn't I been startled by SNS notifications in the middle of the night, all because I wanted to post proof that we received lunch boxes?

"SoftSweet was right before us... and yeah. I heard VTIC are doing their pre-recording after us."

"Ah~ Is it today?"

"In that case, I should contact them and let them know we'll come by to greet them after our stage!"

After finishing our preparations while chatting, we headed to the stage.

A stage lined with cameras stood before us.

*There are drones, too.*

Even for a broadcast station without a regular music show, they seemed to have put in a lot of unique effort.

The staff seemed a bit short-handed, but it wasn't a live broadcast, so they could probably cover it in post-production.

First, we win.

"Hello, everyone!"

"Is everyone hungry? Ah, you're okay? Okay!"

Lights and cheers shone before my eyes.

The rather large audience section, which could hold about a thousand people, was completely full. Keundal was probably somewhere in there, but it was just like him not to send a pop-up message and to read the room.

I chuckled and waved my hand. The end of the harness belt on my outfit swayed with the movement.

*We were assigned 16 minutes, I think.*

It was the perfect amount of time for four songs and a short special performance.

[Then should we go with 'Savior', 'Promise', 'Black Hole', and 'Wheel'?]

It was a spectacular composition that added Drill to the final arrangement of Wheel, sublimating it into Daybreak.

I thought we had struck the best possible balance between our latest songs and public appeal.

*We can probably set aside about two hours for the recording.*

"Then we'll get started."

Just like any other pre-recording, we finished our light chatter and fan service for the audience, then got into formation.

From my position on the outer right, I bent down and thought.

*This is the spot where I pop out from behind the dancers and appear on camera. So I need to be careful with the angle....*

Just then.

THUD.

Suddenly, I couldn't breathe.

"Heok."

My head spun. The lights and the instrumental distorted, my ears rang deafeningly, and colors bled into my vision.

*Something's wrong.*

"...Moondae?"

My body felt like it was under sleep paralysis. This wasn't normal.

*Poisoning?*

It felt like a strange chemical reaction was happening inside my body. My heart pounded, and the hair on my arms stood on end.

What did I do?

"Park Moondae!"

I staggered, then caught myself with my arms.

My elbow was cold. The texture of the dusty stage floor pressed against my cheek.

No, was my face planted on the floor?

"...!"

In that moment, my hearing returned.

"Over here!"

"First, move him, we have to move him."

WHOOSH!

I felt tension surge to the tips of my head and limbs. When I came to my senses, I seemed to be backstage, covered with a blanket or something.

A hand grabbed my shoulder.

"Park Moondae! Park Moondae, look at me. Where are you right now?"

Lee Sejin.

I gasped for breath.

"...The broadcast station."

"What were you doing?"

"The stage, keuk!"

*I can't breathe again, fuck.*

A cough escaped me as if I'd choked on something. It wasn't because of pain.

It was because of shock.

*Suddenly.*

An immense pressure washed over me, like I was being chased by a murderer in the middle of the night, or like I was standing precariously on one foot on a rooftop ledge.

My vision swam.

"Call 911! Call an ambulance. Quickly!"

"Let's just watch the situation a little longer..."

"Park Moondae can't breathe, what situation are you watching? Right now!"

I raised my hand.

And slapped my own face.

SLAP.

I couldn't make a fist, so the sound was a bit pathetic, but the pain helped my head start working again.

"...Wait."

"...!"

"I-If I get up...!"

I didn't refuse Seon Ahyeon's attempt to help me up. I swallowed hard as I spoke.

"I think I'll be fine if I just rest a bit."

*This is a mental problem. My body is fine.*

The problem is that I...

"...I don't know the cause."

It's not like there are only one or two guys with panic disorder in this industry, so everyone probably thinks it's something like that, but shouldn't there be a trigger for it to make sense?

*There wasn't one.*

Fuck, I just looked at the audience without thinking and got into formation, but wait, if that's the case...

*...I just collapsed on stage.*

Damn it.

"How's the mood. Up there."

"Is this really the time for you to be worrying about that?!"

"Hyung, wait a second."

Lee Sejin said calmly.

"They probably didn't see it well. You were in a spot that wasn't very visible."

"......"

"Got it? They'll probably just think you missed a step or something."

I got it. I got it, but...

"Hyung, are you perhaps cold? Do you feel like your body temperature has dropped?"

"W-Wait."

It seemed my hands were shaking. I heard a few people around me running to get a blanket.

And I heard Ryu Chungwoo speaking to a staff member.

"Give it to me. I'll call the ambulance."

"No, wait."

*My brain isn't working. I'm telling you not to call.*

"Give it to me."

Then he asked in a low voice so others couldn't hear.

"...Hyung, did you happen to see something unsettling up there? Tell me, I'll have them remove it. I'll relay it tactfully."

"No, it's not that."

That's not it.

No, my head feels like it's going to explode. No, it's more my spinal cord or heart than my brain...

*What is this.*

My thoughts are a mess. To think there'd be a situation in my fucking life with no logic or reasoning like this.

The sound of my heart beating echoed in my skull. The hair on my head stood on end.

Meanwhile, I could hear bickering next to me. It sounded like the manager was arguing with some other guys...

"Why would he take a call right now, come on...!"

A call?

"Yes, for now, I'll try to end it on my end..."

"Who is it."

"...!"

*Well...*

I read his lips.

Damn it.

"...Let me have it."

And I brought it to my ear.

Cheongryeo.

It seems the news that I'd lost it had already reached their waiting room, even though they just arrived. Let's think. I have to think this through.

It's obviously not out of concern.

...It's when he's guessed something.

[Pressure without context, hyperventilation if it's the first time, fear, a sense of crisis, a feeling that nothing is in its right place.]

A low voice, as if drained of all color.

It's been ages since I got rid of it. No, it's been a long time since it passed from me to Gold 2. So what does that mean...
