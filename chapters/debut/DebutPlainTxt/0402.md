# Chapter 402 - Timer

The new song to be released had already been prepared while we were getting ready to debut.

Even when the production team manufactured a crisis for Kim Raebin for the sake of drama, the next episode would reveal that it had all been staged, only reinforcing viewers' opinion of him.

And now, all that remained was the finals.

Ryu Chungwoo squeezed in next to Kim Raebin.

"How are you getting along with the other contestants? Is everyone still nice to you?"

"Yes. I think they're being extra considerate because I'm younger than them."

Kim Raebin nodded. Apparently, he thought they were being considerate of him, when they were actually intimidated by the talent of this 17-year-old kid and were just being nice to his face.

However, it seemed even he had something on his mind.

"I... But is it really okay for someone else to feature on my final song? The viewer ratings are great, so I thought it was a chance to promote our group that we shouldn't pass up..."

He was asking why the person featuring in his final performance wasn't a member of Wishze.

Instead, a popular member of Marshmallow, their lead vocalist, had been selected to feature in Kim Raebin's last song.

I chuckled.

"A feature is supposed to be a collaboration with an outside party. It'll be more meaningful if you perform with someone other than us."

"Indeed...! That's true."

*Of course, that wasn't the real reason.*

We couldn't give people a reason to get sick of us.

*We shouldn't make people feel like we're forcing our group down their throats.*

Such blatant promotion would only invite resentment.

We needed to carefully control the situation to prevent the now-inflated buzz from dying out or turning into antipathy.

Of course, that didn't mean we'd let people forget about us entirely.

"Still, we're all coming to watch the finals in person. Is that okay?"

We had nothing to lose by cheering for him as a group. It would be heartwarming, at least.

"...! Of course, I'd be grateful!"

"Uh, Raebin, we debuted together, so just call me Yunshin-hyung!"

"Oh!"

I took my eyes off Kim Raebin, who was having a rather comical conversation with Oh Yunshin, and checked the schedule again.

Wishze's debut mini-album catered to the tastes of K-pop fans, based on ghost stories set in a European-style boarding school. It was a niche but certainly artistic concept.

The stage was great too. The choreography using long props seemingly inspired by conductors' batons was unique and charming.

So much so that he had almost sent a long message with his thoughts on the performance to a certain person.

*It's a good thing I didn't send it.*

The man let out a small sigh of relief.

Such a commercial, intense performance wouldn't normally have been to his taste... Yet somehow, he kept coming back to it.
