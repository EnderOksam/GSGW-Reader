# Chapter 535

TeSTAR gathered in their waiting room after the mid-evaluation.

"They did well."

That was the members' brief assessment after watching Aether's stage.

Half of the members looked determined, their faces saying,*We need to work even harder!*

The other half, who instinctively understood the situation, looked more like they had been had.

"Will this air before we film the final stage? The schedule seems to point to that, right?"

"Yeah. A pre-release clip will probably come out before the final stage, at the very least."

"...Hoo."

This meant it would be difficult to flip the production team's narrative by re-strategizing. Pre-release clips would be based solely on this mid-evaluation.

*If I were on the production team, I would never let this narrative go.*

A rising group, born from controversy, had prepared desperately and exploded with potential on stage. Then there was the senior group, the other party in that controversy.

Our role would be to prompt the question.

[Whoa, how did TeSTAR react?]

If we didn't act magnanimously, we would instantly look petty. This was especially true since the show had already chosen Aether as its target for highlighting.

*TeSTAR has been pushed out of the narrative, damn it.*

*Well, at least the viewers will find it exciting.*

Unless the production team were fools, the narrative of 'Aether overcomes adversity' was set in stone. The picture was painted perfectly.

Keun Sejin let out a long sigh instead of a rebuttal. He didn't even offer his usual bitter smile.

I felt the same.

*This feels shittier than I expected.*

Sensing the strange atmosphere, Kim Raebin cautiously raised his hand.

"...? Um, do you think the stage we prepared will be lacking compared to Aether's?"

Ryu Chungwoo looked at Kim Raebin, who seemed ready to blurt out, 'But we did better,' and smiled bitterly.

"No, I don't think so."

Objectively, TeSTAR had performed better in the mid-evaluation.

Kim Raebin nodded emphatically.

"As I thought! I was worried I might have missed something."

He probably wanted to ask why the atmosphere was so strange.

I gave him the answer.

"It's because we're expected to do well."

"Yes...?"

"It means that even if we do well, people won't be moved or surprised. It's because of the expectations we've built up."

"......"

This wasn't just about a rookie group outshining TeSTAR on stage. It was a matter of novelty.

And...

*The problem is I can already see how far those bastards' agency will milk this.*

They would get excited and push it to go viral, spouting some nonsense about turning a crisis into an opportunity.

[Participant team with a good reaction on Genuine Match today (spoilers)]

[Update on Aether, who we thought were headed for rock bottom]

[Rookie group that are bigger practice fiends than you can imagine]

[Yo what the heck, Aether is good?]

I could already picture the headlines.

Meanwhile, the Aether members were just working hard. It would look bad for us to find a weakness and start a media war. It could even backfire.

*People have a sixth sense for that kind of thing.*

Once the mocking atmosphere from Wonder Hall's self-destruction vanished, the narrative would shift. People would see Aether's members as just gritty, down-to-earth guys. To go against that tide, we needed something bigger.

*This has become a headache.*

"M-Moondae, do you have a headache...?"

"...I'm fine."

I took my hand off my temple. I had a feeling things had become complicated.

The situation unfolded as expected a few days later.

[❬Genuine Match❭ What is happening? A mid-evaluation of twists and turns | Pre-release]

As soon as the pre-release clip dropped, people who had only read the title reacted predictably.

[Is Aether finally done for? But if it's a twist, are they trying to promote them?]

[I knew it, this broadcast company is close with Wonder Hall ffs]

[They're writing a whole narrative for them lol ugh]

But after watching the video, their opinions changed.

[They're good?]

[They're better than I thought]

[Seeing them trying so hard not to cry is making me emotional..]

Aether's desperate attitude felt too real to be a product of editing.

*And they actually produced results.*

I could feel public opinion wavering. I could almost hear the production team celebrating their successful clickbait from a distance.

*Hoo.*

Our stage wasn't ignored, but we were pushed back in terms of sheer buzz. Everyone expected us to do well, so there was nothing to criticize and, therefore, less interest.

I stared at my smartphone screen.

*Can we turn this around?*

Realistically, the safe play was to concede this round and plan for the next one. We could accept that a rookie group timed their rise perfectly and prepare to share the pie.

*...is what I would have thought, if this were any other rookie group.*

I didn't want to. Those bastards' agency had accumulated too much bad karma.

Right after the video's release, Kim Raebin discovered the final, decisive blow of that karma. It wasn't created by the agency, but by the Aether members themselves.

"I might be mistaken, but the more I watch Aether's pre-released stage, the more I feel a sense of déjà vu from their composition and expressions."

"Déjà vu?"

"Yes."

Kim Raebin, who had been pondering at his laptop, suddenly exclaimed with wide eyes.

"I've got it. I can see gestures that our members usually use in Aether's performance!"

"...!"

"For example, in this scene where they're raising their hands..."

We listened as Kim Raebin skillfully navigated the video with hotkeys. We were immediately convinced.

"It seems... similar..."

"OK. Literally I see."

The expressions and gestures TeSTAR members used out of habit appeared right on cue. The number of members and the song were different, and the moves were inserted naturally, so it wasn't obvious at first glance.

*...They must have analyzed us thoroughly.*

It wasn't a half-assed copy. I could tell they had seriously considered*why*it was good.

From the perspective of the one who was copied, however, only one thought came to mind.

"I-Isn't this going too far?!"

That was it.

I calmly shook my head at Bae Sejin's flustered words.

"You can't really say anything just because gestures or expressions are similar. They're not copyrighted."

If they couldn't pull it off, they would look awkward and get criticized for 'thinking they're TeSTAR.' But if they did it well, people would just brush it off, asking if we had a monopoly on gestures.

"Besides, everyone practices with cover stages before their debut. They start by copying idols they like. You can't practice without a reference."

In short, it was ambiguous enough that we couldn't accuse them of having no professional courtesy.

"...So you're saying this is also a reference?" Bae Sejin gritted his teeth. "That they're only copying us?"

"......"

They probably didn't set out to copy only TeSTAR. They just didn't have time.

*They probably couldn't have studied multiple teams.*

So, they likely focused on rewatching the stages of the senior group they were most conscious of, trying to pick out the strong points.

From Aether's perspective, they probably just thought they worked hard, but for a group that had been dealt this hand several times, it was natural for some members to get pissed off.

"This isn't the first or second time they've used us as a reference. Shouldn't we protest or something?"

"Hyung, that kind of thinking is useless," Keun Sejin said bluntly. He pressed the bridge of his nose and continued in a low voice. "It's a stage. If you pull it off well, it becomes your own."

"...! What kind of sophistry is that..."

"No! The situation isn't going to change just because you can't accept it, Hyung."

"...!"

"Look at the situation. If we say something, people will just think TeSTAR is being petty with a rookie group. What's the use... Hoo, I'm sorry."

"......"

Keun Sejin barely calmed down. Bae Sejin replied with a stiff face.

"...It's fine. You must be frustrated too."

"......"

The atmosphere was just fantastic.

I pressed the corners of my eyes with both hands. The more maddening part was that the incident's butterfly effect didn't end here.

The bigger problem?

*It's that TeSTAR is leaving this program after this stage.*

If Wonder Hall fanned the flames, we would get comments like 'Aren't they leaving to avoid Aether?' or 'They're leaving because it looks like they might lose.' You can attach any meaning to timing.

[Kinda kills the vibe. TeSTAR is playing it safe now too]

[If they were going to play it safe, they should've just said so. Why pretend to take on a challenge on a survival show only to pull out the calculator and leave? It's so lame.. ah]

[Idol Inc. has completely lost its original spirit. Was renewing their contract TeSTAR's limit?]

This became a problem for the group's image. It also meant the Aether side, with their underdog narrative, got to enjoy a one-two punch against TeSTAR.

*They'll think they've succeeded in their revenge.*

It was annoying how obvious the thoughts of that agency's people were.

I held back a sigh as silence fell inside the car.

"......"

"Let's just prepare for the final stage for now," Keun Sejin said, placating the others. "We have a group shoot soon, so we can gauge the atmosphere then. How does that sound?"

"...Alright."

"Mmm..."

His own complexion wasn't great either.

***

The day of the ❬Genuine Match❭ recording returned.

"You did so, so well, guys!"

"Thank you!"

Celebratory voices echoed in Aether's waiting room. The atmosphere, stagnant for days from controversy, was finally reviving.

For Aether, creating this opportunity by gritting their teeth and working hard filled them with an overwhelming, chest-aching pride.

*Is this what real achievement feels like?*

The Leader nodded. He realized again how an artist's stage results were connected to the joys and sorrows of many industry officials. He felt a greater sense of responsibility.

"Our Leader is all grown up. The company is really counting on you."

"Thank you!"

Carrying that encouragement, Aether's Leader left the waiting room.

*I should go greet the staff and cast members in advance.*

He planned to wash his hands first before shaking hands. He casually entered the staff restroom.

*Oh.*

Someone was already at the sink.

"Hello~"

"...Hello, sunbae."

Aether's Leader bowed his head deeply.

TeSTAR's Lee Sejin smiled, nodded slightly, and shook the water from his hands. He showed no trace of agitation.

*...He's still so composed.*

For some reason, the Leader felt a little irritated. Did he not feel a sense of crisis just because a junior competitor did well on one variety show stage? Was he in a position where that was acceptable?

*Then why did you leak something like a recording?*

As the Leader stewed in a sense of injustice, a calm voice came from above.

"Ah, the mid-evaluation stage was really great~ The reactions were good too, congratulations."

"Ah, thank you."

The Leader couldn't hold back the greeting he had been thinking of.

"Uh, TeSTAR's stage was also good. As always!"

The words had a double edge. It could mean they were always good, or that they showed no improvement. A sinister mind would take it sarcastically. A good person would take it as a compliment.

*I think I said that well!*

Aether's Leader thought with satisfaction.

Lee Sejin gave a friendly, broad smile.

"Oh, really? Gosh, thank you~"

"...Yes!"

"Well then, let's do our best for today's shoot too!"

With that encouragement, Lee Sejin left. Aether's Leader finally had to admit it.

*He seems like a good person.*

But he didn't know why he felt a sense of disappointment.

At the same time, in TeSTAR's waiting room, Lee Sejin tapped Park Moondae's shoulder.

"Moondae Moondae. A moment."

"......"

Despite the indifferent reply, Lee Sejin knew he was listening.

"Remember what I said? That this time... we should just watch how things play out."

"I remember."

I also knew his reason.

*TeSTAR already has too much to lose, so we can't put it all on the table as stakes.*

The situation was unsettling, but not acting rashly was safer and had a higher expected value, even if it felt dirty.

TeSTAR was no longer at a stage where going all-out against a rookie would be seen as fighting spirit. There was a great risk of being seen as a powerhouse acting out of line.

*Playing it safe here is the wise move.*

Park Moondae guessed that Keun Sejin had reached the same cool-headed conclusion.

But...

"Sorry, but I want to take it back. What I said."

"...!"

"I don't know. Usually, I'm the one in this team who says things like 'Let's look at the big picture and move on'..."

Keun Sejin smiled, his eyes shining.

"I don't want to do that. Not this time."

It was D-3 until the final stage.
