# Chapter 382

Looking into Bae Sejin's current situation, surprisingly enough, required only a single Internet search.

*Lee Sejin.*

His profile already appeared at the top of the search results.

-Lee Sejin (actor)

Latest Filmography: Seahorse (20XX.12.15.)

This guy was an actor whose career was currently soaring.

Looking through his entire filmography, every movie he'd appeared in had drawn at least two million viewers.

At this rate, anyone who watched movies would have heard this guy's name at least once.

[Oh, but he hasn't filmed any dramas?]

Probably on purpose. It looked like he'd carefully managed which projects he took ever since his child-actor days.

Of course, his name value was high. Considering how actors were valued more highly than idols these days, it was almost as high as TeSTAR's name value.

Everything seemed to be going well for him, but... the next item was the problem.

-Agency: Dream K Company

This guy was still under his old agency. They were infamous for exploiting child actors and spreading rumors around contract-renewal season to drive down their value.

*How shitty.*

Here, instead of protesting against the agency and taking a break, he seemed to have resumed acting almost immediately after a short transition out of child roles.

A career that has been solidly built in lieu of a long hiatus.

His agency diligently promoted the name 'Lee Sejin' through the media and social media.

I smoothed my chin.

*It'll be easy.*

[What?]

So information about his schedule probably wasn't tightly guarded.

Unlike idols, actors rarely had people camping outside their dorms or agencies, so information about their movements was easier to dig up.

*Good.*

Using clues from there, I found the employees' personal Inheart accounts and went through them in chronological order, and came to a conclusion.

*Bae Sejin is currently working on an unannounced film.*

[Oh, I guess he's at the company often.]

Right. Mentions popped up especially often when Bae Sejin was working on a film. It was almost as though he stayed at the agency whenever his schedule didn't take him elsewhere.

So even now, when he wasn't filming, there was a very high chance that he was at work.

"Hmm."

*He finished filming early in the morning, got into a van, entered the building, and met the agency president?*

Where was the boss? He must be in the company building.

Looking at this, the probability that he was in the company was quite high.

[Then... Would you like to go?]

"Okay."

Let's check it out for ourselves.

So, security was not that tight on the first floor.

It seemed like they'd just leave a sign on the back door where the people who managed the building would come in and out that said, 'No entry except for authorized personnel.'

*Is it over there?*

Sure enough, as I waited in the right spot, a truck stopped and uniformed people came in and out of the back door.

I looked at the timing and naturally moved in that direction. I moved naturally enough that I could be mistaken for a worker carrying in a delivery.

Then Keundal's pop-up appeared, shaking slightly.

[Hy-hyung. If you get caught trespassing... Won't there be a problem?]

*I just need word of this to reach Bae Sejin, and I'll make sure it does. It's fine.*

Anyway, this was an agency building, after all. Getting caught wasn't necessarily a bad thing.

*Because I'm also half a public figure.*

Here, I could see the benefits of becoming famous through idol survival shows.

If I said I'd arranged to meet Lee Sejin and wanted to keep it quiet, they'd probably believe me—especially if I was someone with a face known in a similar industry, like "Lee Sejin."

*No, whether they believe it or not, it just needs to be able to reach Bae Sejin's ears.*

The fact that he woke up and changed his name to Bae Sejin clearly meant that he found his memories from the time of TeSTAR.

Then, even if the situation confused him, he'd probably still try to meet a stranger who suddenly appeared claiming to know him.

*But the worst-case scenario is that the agency keeps word of my visit from ever reaching him.*

The problem was that assuming that there was no contact information for Bae Sejin, that possibility was not out of the question.

Tuk.

[Bae Sejin: Washing face with cold water (#ㅅ#)=3]

Squeak.
