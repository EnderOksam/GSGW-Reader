# Chapter 382 - The Wise Survival, X

Looking into Bae Sejin's current situation, surprisingly enough, required only a single Internet search.

*Lee Sejin.*

His profile already showed up at the top of the results when I searched his name.

★$
[Lee Sejin (actor)]

Recent filmography: ⟨Seahorse⟩ (20XX.12.15.)
$★

This guy was an actor whose career was currently soaring.

Looking through his entire filmography, every movie he'd appeared in had drawn at least two million viewers.

Anyone who was into movies would have heard this guy's name at least once.

$p
**[Oh, but he hasn't filmed any dramas?]**
p$

*Probably on purpose.* It looked like he'd carefully managed which projects he took ever since his child-actor days.

Of course, he commanded a high fee. Considering how actors were valued more highly than idols these days, he was almost at TeSTAR's level.

Everything seemed to be going well for him, but... the next item was the problem.

★$
Agency: Dream K Company
$★

This guy was still under his old agency. They were infamous for exploiting child actors and spreading rumors around contract-renewal season to secure a favorable deal for themselves.

*How shitty.*

Here, instead of protesting against the agency and taking a break, he seemed to have resumed acting almost immediately after a short transition out of child roles.

A solid career built in place of a long hiatus.

Even though he mostly kept a low profile, his agency diligently promoted the name 'Lee Sejin' through the press and social media.

I rested my chin in my hand.

*It'll be easy.*

$p
**[What?]**
p$

Security around him probably wasn't particularly tight.

Unlike idols, actors rarely had people following them to their dorms or agencies, so information about their movements was easier to dig up.

*Simple enough.*

I browsed Dream K's official SNS.

Using keywords I found there, I tracked down the employees' personal Inheart accounts, compared their posts by date, and came to a conclusion.

*Bae Sejin is currently working on an unannounced film.*

Although no names were used, I could gauge how often the employees indirectly mentioned someone I presumed to be Bae Sejin...

$p
**[Oh, I guess he's at the company often.]**
p$

Right. Mentions popped up especially often when Bae Sejin was working on a film. It was almost as though he stayed at the agency whenever his schedule didn't take him elsewhere.

That meant there was a very high chance he'd be at the agency even now, if he wasn't filming.

"Hmm."

So, the very next morning after awakening Bae Sejin, I showered, put on my hat and mask, and left the officetel.

Of course, I wasn't just taking a shot in the dark.

★-
\
[Bae Sejin: Staring at the president, who's wagging his finger (｀ㅅ '+)]
-★

I'd been keeping an eye on his status.

That gave me a pretty good idea of what he'd done today.

*He was filming early in the morning, then got into a van, entered the building, and met the agency president.*

And where would an agency president usually be? In his own company building.

That made it highly likely Bae Sejin was at the agency.

$p
**[Then... Are you going there?]**
p$

"Yes."

*I'll check on him in person.*

His company was located in a corner of Gangnam.

It wasn't a fancy headquarters building like those of large idol agencies. It was an old four-story building with no signboard.

So, security was not that tight on the first floor.

I had a feeling they'd simply have a sign reading 'No entry except for authorized personnel' on the back door used by the building's maintenance staff.

*Is this the place?*

Sure enough, as I waited in the right spot, a truck stopped and uniformed people came in and out of the back door.

I waited for the right moment and naturally moved in that direction, looking enough like a delivery worker that I could be mistaken for one.

Then Keundal's pop-up appeared, shaking slightly.

$p
**[Hy-hyung. If you get caught trespassing... Won't there be a problem?]**
p$

*I just need word of this to reach Bae Sejin, and it definitely will. It's fine.*

I was only sneaking in so I wouldn't be stopped at the entrance or have people think a LeTi trainee was making an official visit to the agency.

This was an agency building, after all. Getting caught wasn't necessarily a bad thing.

*I'm already fairly well known myself—something of a public figure.*

There were benefits to becoming famous through an idol survival show.

*Employees only? No problem. I'll say I'm something like that.*

Since I worked in a similar industry and my face was known, they'd probably believe me if I said I'd arranged to meet Lee Sejin and wanted to keep it quiet.

*No, it doesn't matter whether they believe me or not. Word just needs to reach Bae Sejin's ears.*

The fact that his name had changed to Bae Sejin after his awakening clearly meant he'd regained his memories of TeSTAR.

Then, if only out of confusion, he'd probably try to meet a stranger who suddenly appeared claiming to know him.

*But the worst-case scenario is that the agency keeps word of my visit from ever reaching him.*

Since there was no personal number listed for Bae Sejin, that possibility couldn't be ruled out. It was certainly a potential problem.

*Tuk.*

I went through the emergency door on the outside of the first floor, entered the bathroom, and hid myself in a stall.

I was planning to check whether Bae Sejin had left the building in the meantime and then work out where to go...

*Bang!*

Just then, the bathroom door flew open, and I heard someone come trotting in.

*Security can't be here already.*

Sure enough, I heard only the sound of someone using the sink. I shrugged and turned back to the status window...

★-
\
[Bae Sejin: Washing face with cold water (#ㅅ#)=3]
-★

"......"

*No way.*

I carefully opened the stall door.

*Squeak.*

Then I saw a man standing at the sink.

The guy in a suit was bent over, splashing cold water on his face.

And the moment he raised his head, his face was reflected in the mirror.

"...!"

It was Bae Sejin.

With his undyed hair, no colored contacts, and a suit, he had the unmistakable look of an actor.

*So this is where we meet again?*

There was a memory that came to mind right away.

It seemed that whenever I was Ryu Gunwoo, I was destined to meet this guy in front of a sink.

– *Are you Lee Sejin? Sorry. I'm a fan, and I was just surprised to see you here.*

It was strange. But I had no time to dwell on it right now.

After confirming that there was no one else in the bathroom, I spoke to Bae Sejin.

"Hello."

"Yes..."

The moment I stepped out of the stall, an odd look crossed his face, but he immediately concealed it. Indeed, he was an actor.

"I'm a fan."

"Uh, thank you... Are you a new employee here?"

As I saw the tension begin to leave his face, I got straight to the point.

"That's not it. I'm a fan of TeSTAR's Bae Sejin."

"...!"

He reacted immediately.

"Who are you?"

Bae Sejin's voice immediately sharpened.

It looked like he was going to grab me by the collar at any moment or lock me in the bathroom.

*Was being direct the answer after all?*

I opened my mouth right away.

"I'm Park Moondae, hyung."

"...!?"

*Tuk.*

Something fell behind Bae Sejin.

When I looked down, I saw something shaped like a ballpoint pen.

It was probably a recorder.

"Pa-Park Moo—"

"Yes. It happened like this."

I guess I should start explaining before this guy freaked out any further.

***

Unfortunately, it was impossible to take this guy to any suitable restaurant or cafe.

Both of us were too well-known, and his current situation wouldn't allow it anyway.

"I can't leave this building right now."

"What on earth is going on?"

"...It's a long story. Anyway, wh-where's your original body? What are you doing here?!"

I didn't know if he really believed me or if he was just pretending to believe me, but still, the fact that Bae Sejin was willing to question me seemed like a good start.

"Actually..."

"Actually?"

"This *is* my original body. Park Moondae's body wasn't originally mine."

It sounded absurd, and Bae Sejin looked completely shell-shocked.

"Y-you, are you really a ghost...?"

"No."

*Wait a minute.*

*I died and found myself in someone else's body. Calling me a ghost wasn't completely off the mark.*

$p
**[It's different! It's completely different!]**
p$

"No. Now that I think about it, it seems similar..."

"No way..." He groaned, looking as if he was about to faint.

I corrected myself. If I spoke well, I might be able to get him to believe me.

"Anyway, I started living as Park Moondae right before I participated in ⟨Idol Inc.⟩. It wasn't my choice."

"...Are you saying you were in a situation like this back then? With the world changing and everything...?"

"It might have been similar. Anyway, the Park Moondae you know was me from the beginning... Though I don't really have any way to prove it if you don't believe me."

However, Bae Sejin, whose face was turning various shades of purple, reacted surprisingly to this remark.

He tried to speak but ended up sighing four or five times before answering.

"...I know. You've never changed once."

"...Thank you."

I didn't know if he was acting, but it still felt good to hear him say that.

"But that doesn't mean I'm letting this go! This situation makes absolutely no sense, and you... Explain in detail!"

*Even if I'd love to explain it in more detail...*

"...In the company restroom?"

"......"

The guy sighed and motioned to me.

"Follow me."

Bae Sejin took me to a storage room near the emergency exit on the third floor.

*Does he come here often when he wants to be alone?*

He moved as if he was familiar with the place.

*Click.*

He closed the door behind him, grew serious, and spoke gravely.

"...We should have some privacy for a while. Now, tell me."

"Okay."

I nodded and began to explain the situation so far as succinctly as possible.

"So..."

*I can't get used to it no matter how much I try.*

Still, maybe because I'd already explained it once to Cha Eugene, the story flowed quite smoothly.

Bae Sejin turned pale, then ashen, then a sickly yellow.

But in the end, his face turned red.

*I understood the first few reactions, but why had he turned red?*

"S-so... Are you that person?"

"What?"

"That... Ryu Gunwoo-ssi. The one who cheered me on!"

*Ah, that.*

*No wonder this guy had a strange expression earlier.*

Even though my current face was a mix of Park Moondae and Ryu Gunwoo, he seemed to have recognized Ryu Gunwoo to some extent. I rubbed the back of my neck and opened my mouth.

"...That's right, but I wasn't trying to deceive you on purpose. I really meant to encourage you."

Bae Sejin hurriedly waved his hand.

"N-no. I just wanted to thank you... Wait a minute. But you were Park Moondae at the time, so how does that make sense...??"

"That's also complicated."

I never thought I'd have to explain this to him, too. I briefly explained the story of the real Park Moondae and Keundal.

Keundal was surprisingly excited and chimed in from time to time.

$p
**[Tell him I passed the civil service exam thanks to you, Gunwoo!]**
p$

And surprisingly, Bae Sejin was not overwhelmed by this unrealistic situation. But unlike Cha Eugene, he didn't simply accept the situation as-is.

On the contrary, his expression grew increasingly grave, and he began coming up with various theories of his own.

His appreciation for the humanities was showing.

"Do you know what a time paradox is? I think your situation might be a paradox..."

"Then isn't this situation like some kind of mock test? It's similar to reality, but less difficult."

"Maybe something like virtual reality. Like in movies, you know? Digital worlds and people in tanks and all that..."

I did my best to answer all his questions.

But the bottom line was this.

"I don't know. I'm just guessing."

Nothing was certain yet.

"Anyway, based on how things are structured here, I'm betting that we'll be able to go back."

"And your goal is to become an idol and win a *daesang*?"

"Yes."

"...Okay. That makes sense."

Bae Sejin took a short, deep breath. I guess he was running out of room to take in all the absurd things he'd been hearing.

And perhaps because of that, Bae Sejin said something similar to what I'd been thinking.

"Then we're going back to reality anyway, so this is like a dream."

"Yes."

"That's good."

"...?"

*What do you mean, good?*

"Give me a second. Let me at them."

"What?"

"This agency!"

Bae Sejin spoke with burning eyes.

"This isn't reality anyway, so that's good. The aftermath be damned—I'm going to take the proper legal approach."

"Uh..."

Of course, I had no intention of stopping him.

"But if acting here is fun for you, why not enjoy it a little?"

When he was in TeSTAR, his solo activities were always delayed, and his acting career was practically nonexistent.

I thought it wouldn't hurt him to get some practice here and perhaps adjust his solo activities once we returned to the real world.

But Bae Sejin was having none of it.

"I like acting! But not like this."

"Why?"

The guy clenched his fists.

"These jerks always force me to play the same type of character...!"

"......"

"Here I am, playing the same psychopath role for four years now. Even I can see it's getting old!"

"Hmm."

Yeah, I could understand why he was pissed.

'Lee Sejin' was a prominent actor in every sense, so it made no sense that he had no say in which movies he acted in.

*Shouldn't someone at his level have the final say in which movies he takes?*

He was obviously being treated unfairly.

Then something suddenly came to mind.

"Hyung, don't tell me the reason you don't have a personal number is—"

"...! How did you know...? That's right. They say it's to help me manage my mental health or something, and they don't even let me have my own phone."

Bae Sejin muttered coldly.

"They're treating me like I'm under a conservatorship."

I was pretty sure that was a legal term, and it seemed like he'd been thinking about this even after joining TeSTAR.

"...It must have been difficult."

"Actually, I didn't care much as long as I could act. The agency had to be mindful of public opinion, so they didn't give me a murderous schedule like when I was young, and they pay me enough to make sure my mother has everything she needs..."

The guy's expression darkened, and then his eyes lit up.

And then he clenched his fists.

"But I am me, and I don't have to put up with this anymore—even if this is the real world."

"That's right."

The guy declared confidently.

"I'm going to change things right now! Are you preparing to become an idol now?"

"Yes, that's right."

I briefly told him about LeTi's survival show. After nodding, Bae Sejin seemed to make up his mind and shouted.

"I'll join that show too!"

*Hmm.*

"I don't think it'll be easy."

"Why not?!"

"You're 25 years old."

"......"

Yes.

Bae Sejin, 25, by far the oldest among those I'd checked so far, turned red and lowered his head.

*Maybe this was inevitable. He had to be around this age to have succeeded as a full-fledged actor rather than a child actor.*

By the time the brief speculation passed...

"...Am I too old to be an idol? When we did ⟨Idol Inc.⟩, there were contestants around my age..."

"No. That isn't the problem."

I explained slowly.

"Because you've already built such a successful acting career, people are going to resent you appearing on an idol survival show at all—though I don't think it's right for them to feel that way."

The problem was that he was a 25-year-old actor with an established career.

He was at his peak as well. Unless Bae Sejin was overwhelmingly better as an idol than as an actor, it would only bring him pain.

If we were going to return to reality, there was no need to endure that kind of pain.

Bae Sejin, who was groaning, eventually sighed and admitted.

"...You're right."

"Hmm."

However, it seemed that he hadn't given up on being supportive.

The guy clenched his fists.

"Then I'll support you properly."

"What?"

An ambitious smile appeared on the guy's face and then disappeared.

"Anyway, if your hypothesis is right, there's no need to worry about the long-term future or my influence here."

"Y-yeah, that's right."

The guy gave a quiet laugh.

"I... I've already discovered more than one or two shady things this agency did back in the real world."

*Wait...*

"I'm going to hire a lawyer and go after them. Let's see if they can stay that shameless once all their weaknesses are exposed. If I'm not leaving, I'll fix this agency properly!"

The guy's eyes gleamed with the desire for revenge. Okay... He wasn't an idol but an actor, and rather than fighting over public opinion, he was just trying to teach the agency president a lesson.

"Good luck."

"Yeah!"

The guy looked proud, then excited as he made a suggestion.

"When that's over... I'll have a say in my activities. Then, why don't I have them make me a social-media account so I can vote for you?"

"No," I said immediately.

That was a terrible idea.

After discussing the issue of fairness for a while, in the end, he agreed to put the idea off until after my debut.

*He's really full of drive right now.*

With no need to worry about the future, Bae Sejin had suddenly become the embodiment of justice.

"Then, you..."

Just as he was about to ask how I'd been doing lately...

"Oh, I have to buy a phone too."

"I'll give you my number first."
