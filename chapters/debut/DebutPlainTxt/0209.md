# Chapter 209

There was no place as freezing as inside the car.

And I wasn't talking about the temperature but the atmosphere there.

*This is damned.*

Although Ryu Chungwoo was not particularly angry or unfair towards anyone, he was by no means in his usual state.

Let's look at what happened in the waiting room of the music show a few hours ago.

- Hyung, I want to eat more snacks! I want to eat a lot more!

- Sure.

That was it.

Ryu Chungwoo sat on the sofa, just squeezing the stress ball without adding anything more.

Since then, no one touched Ryu Chungwoo, and he remained silent.

Although there was going to be a shoot at dawn tomorrow, it was the first time in a while that we could return to the dorm before dinner time. Still, the guys who were obviously thrilled by it were sensible enough to read the mood and keep their mouths shut.

Me? It wasn't like I was a big talker by nature. So, I was just looking up public opinion on my smartphone.

*It has generally settled down.*

[Direct footage of Ryu Chungwoo from a different angle]

[Ryu Chungwoo didn't hit him XXX]

Only after the clarification was spread did the atmosphere subside with words such as, 'No wonder it was like that.'

Still, those who insisted that he 'broke his image' kept appearing... but that was unavoidable anyway.

In the first place, it was unlikely that Ryu Chungwoo's condition became like that due to a controversy on the Internet.

For the record, this atmosphere stayed the same even after we entered the dorm.

"I'm going in first."

"Yes!"

As soon as he finished eating, Ryu Chungwoo entered his room. It seems like he was going to go straight to bed.

Clack.

As soon as the door closed and Ryu Chungwoo disappeared behind it, there were a few worried sighs with bitter smiles here and there.

Bae Sejin muttered solemnly.

"...Honestly, he's right on feeling upset."

"That's right!"

"D-do you think Chungwoo-hyung is feeling very hurt? H-he did a good job keeping us members from harm..."

Seon Ahyun cried out worriedly.

Though, everyone has been keeping their voices down as much as possible because of Ryu Chungwoo who had gone to bed.

"Well, from the company's point of view, wouldn't they be thinking that he could have just dodged it so why did he have to elbow like that? They must be so much under pressure because of the talks that broke out~"

Keun Sejin sarcastically acted sympathetic. I knew him as much.

I spoke as I brought my bowl to the sink.

"All this time, Chungwoo-hyung has been way too good to the company."

If they were to start cussing and making a ridiculous fuss, he would most likely just meekly repeat 'I'm sorry', which is why they crossed the line with the good-natured and responsible guy who easily accepted things.

"Why don't we just use this opportunity to let Chungwoo-hyung rest? If we need something or if there's a problem, we can just talk to the company."

The majority nodded with compassionate looks on their faces.

"That is a sensible suggestion."

"...Okay!"

Now that Ryu Chungwoo was absent, Bae Sejin seemed very conscious of the fact that he was the oldest member. I wanted to tell him not to waste his energy.

"W-wouldn't he be all right after taking a rest...?"

"That's right. That's how you regain your strength, by resting. We have to assist Chungwoo-hyung a lot during this promotion period."

"Yeah."

"Well then, we should also quickly go in."

The short discussion concluded with, 'From now on, let's not put more burden on Ryu Chungwoo.'

It was sensible and thoughtful teamwork, but it did not actually eliminate the root cause.

*There are limits to going like this.*

In the meantime, I didn't expect any of the managers to cleanly do the work Ryu Chungwoo has been doing so far.

The chief manager would be perplexed as to why he suddenly had to take on the load and would pass it on to the first manager, and that guy, well... it was obvious how he would handle that.

I'm sure at some point they would subtly pass the work back to Ryu Chungwoo again and at that moment if things went wrong, the landmine would really detonate.

*We'd be lucky if it only ends with Ryu Chungwoo getting pissed off and punching them once.*

Therefore, something had to be done before that happens. Whether it was changing managers or knocking some sense into them.

*We'll also have to check how well Ryu Chungwoo's mental state is recovering.*

It would be difficult if any major mishaps happened during our activities. Because the status abnormality of bringing in 400,000 people was still there and thriving.

So, for now... Let's keep an eye on Ryu Chungwoo's condition tomorrow. Even if he appeared fine, I shouldn't let my guard down.

I fell asleep with that conclusion, but the next day I realized that there was no need for me to overthink it so much.

"We'll stop by the salon before shooting today's variety show. Do you have anything else to relay?"

"No."

This was because Ryu Chungwoo's condition remained the same as yesterday.

Moreover, he looked at the first manager's face as if he was looking at an object.

*It's scary!*

*Quiet.*

Why was this guy's volume so loud even after lowering his voice?

I shooed Cha Yujin away from me who was exclaiming into my ear and climbed into the car. Then I intentionally sat close to Ryu Chungwoo.

*Let's test the waters a bit.*

Ryu Chungwoo did not appear particularly uncooperative nor was he harsh with his responses. It was only that this was too contrasting to his usual gentleness.

*...Actually, he doesn't seem that different from how I am on a daily basis.*

That thought left a bad taste in my mouth.

In any case, I saw an opportunity and casually spoke to Ryu Chungwoo as we got off.

"I'm going to buy some drinks. Hyung, what would you like?"

"I'm fine."

He didn't look fine at all, though. But well, I got it.

"COKE! COKE!"

I only received the backing of Cha Yujin who strongly insisted on soda.

And I also got some 'That was a brave challenge!' looks from the other teammates... But, well, it wasn't even a challenge in the first place but just a build-up.

And on the next day.

"Chungwoo-hyung."

Without a word, I handed him some fresh fruit juice.

"I'm fine."

"Think of it as supplying your glucose intake since we've had less than three hours of sleep."

"......"

Ryu Chungwoo didn't have the energy to argue, so he just picked up the drink and drank it.

Then his eyes opened wide.

"...! It's too sweet."

It's got to be. I asked for a lot of sugar and syrup to be added.

I actively used the sugar high method I used on Cha Yujin during

. It was a simple and stimulating approach, so the hurdle was low.

"That's why it's tasty."

"...Hm."

Ryu Chungwoo eventually finished his drink.

He probably couldn't bother to say anything more nor did he seem to have the motivation to exercise restraint over his diet.

However, it seems that my objective was successful as he drank at a faster pace and the color returned to his face.

I threw in one more question.

"How is your condition? You seemed to be especially tired this time."

"Yeah. I've just... been less able to recover from fatigue."

"I see."

As I thought, was it due to burnout exhaustion?

For the first time in the past few days, Ryu Chungwoo articulated his words. His energy level must have risen momentarily due to the power of sugar.

"Still, it definitely seems like high-fructose syrup is effective! I am feeling more energized!"

"Mm, I know right."

When even Kim Raebin impatiently cut in with a relieved look on his face, the usual gentle response came out.

It was more effective than I thought?

I touched my chin.

*...Seems like he's the type to relieve mental stress through external activities.*

It was not just about resting. It seemed like he was the type of person whose head was cleared after feeling physically refreshed by a good external stimulus.

"Hmm."

It was a bit of a hassle, but not really a big deal.

*I'm also indebted to Ryu Chungwoo.*

I picked up my phone and searched for a place.

~~~

The next afternoon, the shooting for a weekend variety show, where you were automatically dropped into the pool if you got the lyrics for nostalgic hit songs wrong, went well.

Meaning, we went into the water without worrying about our bodies.

"Uack!"

"I'm going to get you!"

"What do you think you're doing after inviting our TeStar, MC Tong Tong!"

In the end, there was no one who didn't get soaked, and only after being barraged with towels and dryers, did we manage to get back in the car.

I was completely drained.

*I want to go back like this and take a bath...*

However, there was another schedule right after.

Although, it was going to be relatively casual because it wasn't a fixed schedule but an optional one that we decided on.

It was a W Live broadcast.

Nevertheless, it was the kind of schedule that could just be shoved into the vacant hours, so if things got too tiring, we could just adjust the length and the amount of the content.

However, this time, instead of doing it in the comfort of our dormitory, we agreed with the members to seriously go all out with the filming.

Of course, excluding Ryu Chungwoo.

"It will take an hour, right?"

"Yes, I'll start driving~"

TeStar members took a brief nap as we rode to the prearranged location.

Ryu Chungwoo, who would have typically asked where we were going next, similarly fell asleep without saying a word.

Hence, he was even more flustered when we arrived at the site moments later.

"This place..."

The members surrounded the surprised Ryu Chungwoo with contented smiles, and with open arms, introduced the place.

"The location for today's W Live, the shooting range~"

"Ta-ta-taah!"

"...!!"

A massive air gun shooting range in the heart of Seoul.

It was the place I searched for the day before.

"You seemed to enjoy shooting, hyung, but these days we don't have any time for full-fledged hobbies~ So, we wanted to have fun at least like this!"

"Let's have fun! Let's play!"

"......"

Ryu Chungwoo looked like he had just been slapped in the face.

However, soon, you could subtly see a moved expression on his face.

"...Thanks, guys. It's going to be fun."

"Hehe."

He seemed to be deeply moved.

It looks like this has worked as intended.

The thoughtfulness that was submerged under the water for several days finally began to emerge from Ryu Chungwoo's mouth.

"You must be very tired, though. Are you sure you'll be all right? It's going to be tough doing something so active at this time of the evening..."

Bae Sejin averted his gaze.

"...We agreed to this since it looked fun when we saw it during the filming of the music video. We all wanted to do this so don't get it wrong."

"T-that's right! It seemed fun...!"

"Haha, okay. I got it."

"Hum, hum."

For someone who was the first to agree, excitedly saying it was a good idea, he was saying rather rational words.

"Then we'll pair up~ and beat Ryu Chungwoo! Let's go with this theme for today's W Live!"

"There's no holding back! No mercy!! I'll win!"

Ryu Chungwoo smirked for the first time in a while.

"Are you sure you'll be okay? You can team up in groups of three."

"Wow, hyung, you're looking down on us too much."

For the record, it only took an hour for us to realize that he wasn't looking down on us but expressing the cold face of reality.

"Euck!"

First of all, let me just say that Bae Sejin and Kim Raebin's team was sunk by Ryu Chungwoo's double score.

- ㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋ

- Guys, why would you take on such a reckless challenge?

- Let's pretend we didn't just see that.

- ㅋㅋㅋㅋ So cute ㅋㅋㅋㅋ

These were the live comments I checked while watching the other teams.

And Ryu Chungwoo boosted up more and more as time passed.

"Heol."

"...How is that possible?"

The spectacle of a former gold medallist in archery, running wild among the unlevel common people was likely to remain a source of laughter for the fans...

"It should also be considered that Chungwoo-hyung has a bit of an advantage because he has already used a firearm once in the music video!"

"...That was a musket, and this is a rifle, so they're different."

"Gasp!"

"T-the rifle gun was created with the improvement of the musket, that's what the staff said...!"

"As I thought! This is absolutely not a defeat for Bae Sejin-hyung and me, but the result of fighting to the end..."

"Oh~ Another double score!"

"Oh, my god."

Whether or not this comical rant ensued, I silently fired the gun.

Tat-tat.

Even though it wasn't to Ryu Chungwoo's level, I think we did rather well. The evidence of this was that Seon Ahyun and I were the ones who followed most closely to Ryu Chungwoo's score.

- Oh, he's kinda cool though? ㅋㅋㅋ

- That's a pretty good hit.

- When is TeStar going to go to the army? Hurry and leave ㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋ

- ㅠㅠㅠㅠ Read the mood

As the location and activity were what they were, some aggro comments about the military could be seen, but in any case, the W Live ended rather successfully with both laughter and thrill.

"See you again~"

"I will do this more often!"

And with the usual closing remarks, everyone, including the staff, returned to their worn-out state.

"Haa."

"I have no regrets..."

"It was fun! Let's do it one more time!"

Ah, except for Cha Yujin.

In any case, the idols were given a moment of free time as the staff was in the middle of cleaning up the scene.

It was then that Ryu Chungwoo made his move.

"Drink some of this."

"Thank you."

Perhaps feeling better after having a good time shooting and winning the competition, Ryu Chungwoo bought drinks for everyone at a nearby convenience store.

Then he stood beside me as I accepted the can and began to insinuatingly talk.

"Ahyunie said that you're the one who asked to come here."

There just had to be some guy who blabbed their mouths again.

Before I could answer anything, Ryu Chungwoo concisely finished his speech.

"Thank you... I'm much better."

"......"

I opened the drink he offered me. There was a refreshing sound of carbonation coming up.

"Are you feeling more refreshed mentally?"

"Yeah."

Then that's enough.

I silently gulped down the zero-calorie drink. Ryu Chungwoo opened his plastic bottle and calmly continued his words.

"Actually, it wasn't really a big deal... but I felt strangely exhausted."

"......"

"I guess I got reminded of the time I just quit archery."

He was even sharing this much?

It seems that the physical activity medicine was greater than I thought.
