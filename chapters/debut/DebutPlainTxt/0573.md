# Chapter 573

Concert Ticket.

The former graduate student stared at the video, captivated. Ryu Chungwoo held a ticket out to her. It was a gold-foiled invitation with a punch hole. She had first thought the ticket's design was a vintage concept, but seeing it now in a video with a perfect period setting, it felt natural. The impact of a real-world object suddenly appearing mixed in, leaving a peculiar impression. The ticket drew closer on the screen, and the meaningful atmosphere intensified. Then the video ended.

*Huh?*

The former graduate student blinked. She stared at the black screen, which showed only a replay icon.

*Is that it?*

It felt empty somehow.

*Of course, it was fun, but...*

It felt cut off in the middle. The nagging feeling of unexplained and unresolved things remained.

*Hmm, maybe it's just me.*

She cautiously clicked on the comments and saw one.

[Wow, if you rewatch this...]

Ugh. She immediately scrolled away from the comments without reading further. That was definitely a hint.

*There must be more!*

Her heart pounded. She pressed the 'replay' icon and began to watch the video carefully from the beginning. The scene showed Ryu Chungwoo speaking to her and walking to the lobby door.

*It's the same up to this point.*

There was nothing unusual in the beginning. When Ryu Chungwoo stopped walking and started talking, she scanned the screen more thoroughly.

[Come to think of it... a staff member asked me to give you something.]

Then she found it.

*Huh...?*

[Z9nIgQhLdw]

Clearly printed text was in the serial number field of the ticket as it zoomed in.

Click.

She instinctively paused the video and looked at the text. She was about to write it down when an idea struck her. She clicked the 'Show more' section of the video.

"...!"

As expected, the code was also in the video description, so she could easily copy it.

*Of course!*

This is it. Convinced, she wracked her brain, wondering where she could use the code. It didn't look like a computer language or an error code.

*If it's a code related to the video, then...*

The former graduate student stared at the screen. The moment she looked at the very top, she realized.

*It's an address!*

The address bar of the very video she was watching had a long string of random characters after the slash, similar to the code. Her heart pounded. She deleted those characters and pasted the code she had found into the address bar instead.

[https://wetu.be/Z9nIgQhLdw]

After typing it in, she pressed Enter.

Click.

The page connected.

"Wow!"

*I found it!*The thrill, like solving a difficult quiz and winning a prize, made her clench her fists. A new video started to play.

[HUNTING CIRCUS]

The tour ticket from the previous video appeared on the screen. It soon disappeared from view, as if someone were lowering a hand that held it. The scenery the ticket had obscured was revealed. A roller coaster. The perspective was as if the 'protagonist' was riding in the very first car.

"Oh?"

The camera, which had shown the front of the red roller coaster, moved as if lifting its head. It revealed a dark tent ahead with swirling crimson stripes.

*A circus tent!*

At that moment, the roller coaster launched.

"......"

The tent in front of her opened, and the roller coaster was sucked inside. It was a different world.

[Welcome.]

Inside the giant circus tent, Kim Raebin greeted them from the dark stage, dressed in a classic suit. Shining spotlights crisscrossed around him. Light bulbs twinkled on the temporary structure built with colorful fabrics and steel frames in the background. Against a backdrop of exploding flower petals, Kim Raebin, bathed in the spotlight, seemed to bow politely, but as he gestured to grab his chin, hands reached out from the darkness behind him.

The tickling sound of an accordion played. The hands moved like spiders, gesturing elegantly.

[The tables have turned]

Today, you're the sheep

Fingers pointed in unison at the person beyond the camera. Gun barrels popped out from both sides of the roller coaster and fired at the screen.

"...!"

Confetti poppers exploded instead of bullets, teasingly sparkling in the light.

[Run farther, go ahead]

How fun, watching you do tricks

As a slightly eerie minor brass melody rang out, the roller coaster moved as if fleeing, circling half the stage. In response, TeSTAR's formation changed direction as if chasing the roller coaster.

"......"

At the front of TeSTAR's diagonal line, Ryu Chungwoo gestured with his chin and turned to sit.

[(Run, Run!)]

Circling round and round below the stage

Back to your place, Come on

The wild choreography, mixed with a bit of tutting, was exquisitely controlled and added flavor. It was a completely different arrangement from the original ❬Hunting❭.

*Whoa!*

Not only the arrangement but also the choreography had changed. Originally, ❬Hunting❭ was a wild song with a heavier, almost brutally sharp group dance, but now, a more playful and mischievous feeling came to life.

[Show must go on, like me]

You can't leave either

You said it yourself, Just enjoy it

More rhythmic full-body movements followed. The choreography, performed by center Cha Eugene who came forward with a grin, seemed to spring from his limbs. It extended right up to the roller coaster, moving the viewer's gaze with his fingertips. The more splendid and classically arranged ❬Hunting❭ relentlessly pounded in her ears. The flashy choreography, with as many intricate beats as the arrangement, was perfectly synchronized and captivated her eyes.

[Just enjoy it]

"Hah..."

It was clear that every element of the original song had been boldly yet delicately adjusted. It was that intense and that good. The viewer naturally came to a realization.

*This is the real tour promotion video!*

However, TeSTAR didn't openly display this performance, which they seemed to have poured money and time into, begging people to watch. Instead, they hid it.

[A night of twists]

Time for the hunt (Yea-h)

You couldn't see it if you left the hotel satisfied with the happy ending. Only those who stayed with TeSTAR until the end, who thought and experienced it for themselves, could reach it on their own. They could reach the thrilling performance video given as a reward at the very end. It was the maximization of accomplishment.

[Call up, it's hunting season]

"......"

The chorus began. With Seon Ahyeon at the center, a flashy and technical circus-like choreography unfolded, seeming to follow the camera's movement.

POP!

Fireworks and lights exploded. The roller coaster had stopped again at the front of the stage. The lights grew brighter.

[Woooo!]

A rushing finale matched the mood. In time with the arrangement that grew faster like an acrobatic feat, the final dance break filled the screen in a full shot. The movements of hands wrapped in white gloves and black shoes strongly filled the frame to the camera's edges. The performance raced towards its climax with an explosive drum and brass solo. It reached its ending with an intense close-up.

Following the final instrumental and the heart-pounding drum roll, the TeSTAR members bowed politely, one after another with a time lag. The moment the final member, center Lee Sejin, bowed, the spotlight went out.

BAMMM!

It was the end.

"......"

The former graduate student realized she had been staring intently at the screen in a posture perfect for developing tech neck. She hastily corrected her position.

*That was so good...*

But the video wasn't over yet.

[......]

The video's first-person perspective looked down from the darkened stage to see the front of the roller coaster again. It was dead silent, but soon, the engine started with a vibration-like tremor.

*Oh.*

The camera confirmed the roller coaster's engine was running, then turned its gaze back to the stage.

[It'll be more fun this time.]

Seon Ahyeon, whom she had met in the elevator, was smiling and looking at her.

"...!"

On the screen, which only showed small, silent subtitles and his moving lips, Seon Ahyeon reached out a hand and pulled the camera closer.

*Huh?*

The moment he suddenly let go of the camera, the roller coaster shot backward.

WHOOSH-!

With a thud, it went outside the tent. Then, lights came on in the re-closed circus tent. Finally, a huge title appeared above it.

[Prologue : Welcome to the hunt house]

"......"

An accordion arrangement of ❬Hunting❭ played like BGM as text scrolled up. It was the ending credits. The former graduate student realized something. This wasn't a hidden 'Hidden Ending'. This was the final destination of all those complicated choices, and one more thing.

*It was a prologue.*

All of this was a preface, an introductory background explanation. The main chapter would probably begin at the place you go to with that invitation. The concert venue!

[Who said TeSTAR was taking it easy?]

[The choreo is insane, it's too good to only use for the concertㅠㅠ]

*It was a clean sweep.*

[Isn't this just stuff that was planned last year? We'll have to see the next album to be sure... Hmm]

└Saying this to a crazy, determined idol group that completely revamped a title track and added new choreo just to fit a concert concept

In truth, these under-the-surface reactions were just for gauging the flow. I was just satisfied that the fans' visible reactions were good. Meeting expectations came with a thrilling sense of accomplishment.

*Good.*

However, the fun part didn't end there.

[Anyone tried this? lolololol]

People who weren't fans started trying it. This happened despite the fact that it was content aimed squarely at the existing fanbase. In fact, we didn't sell any additional concert tickets since the purpose wasn't to sell more tickets but to have an excuse to pump out content. To be honest, I hadn't expected it to have that much of a ripple effect.

*We hid the performance video so well, after all.*

This was because only those with the will to actively dig for content could properly enjoy it. Since it wasn't an official music video, we didn't have to worry about view counts, which allowed us to choose this method, but it seems that subtly piqued people's interest.

*Should I call this... the feeling of lining up for a popular restaurant that's hard to get into?*

It was a desire to take on a challenge.

[Ah, I shouldn't have clicked the promo video link beforehand...]

[You can find it just by clicking around without a guide for the TeSTAR promo, so please just do it on your own the first time]

[Please tell me the choice to get Lee Sejin to befriend the room service fox ffs]

Moreover, another factor lowered the barrier to entry.

*It's not paid content.*

Because of the improved accessibility, it seemed like genuinely bored people were clicking on it. This was unlike 'Roll the Dice' where you had to buy the album to legally play. The fact that it was a series of short WeTube videos also seemed to play a part in lowering the psychological barrier, and so...

[When is TeSTAR's concert ticketing?]

[Can you only see the rest of this at the concert?ㅠㅠ]

...Surprisingly, additional demand was actually created.

"Um, what if we... scheduled more concert dates?"

It was to the point that even Seon Ahyeon could feel it and said something like this. Of course, we couldn't just get excited and schedule additional shows, as that could mess up our entire schedule.

*I think we can satisfy the demand in other ways.*

I quickly formulated a few plans. However, I had to do something before putting these plans into action. TeSTAR had existing schedules, after all.

"Moondae, you know we have a joint concert in Singapore next week, right?"

"Ah, yes."

"Do you want to perform the newly arranged version of ❬Hunting❭ there too?"

A K-POP group concert abroad, run by a major broadcasting station, was just around the corner. I felt we had to appear at least once since we had already gone down that route, so I agreed. They said it was to commemorate the 50th anniversary of the broadcasting station's exchange program or something, but that wasn't really important. Well, as long as we do well, it's fine, but...

"I think that would be fine."

That new co-ed group was appearing at this joint concert. Halo Heim. The new group formed with members of ❬Be My Girls❭, who were originally under this agency, and... Yeongrin, who was said to have produced the group, and so, a few days later, TeSTAR would meet that sensational group and its affiliates in person for the first time.
