Seon Ahyeon took a deep breath.

His roommate, Kim Raebin, told him to rest easy, packed up his electronic devices, and went to Bae Sejin and Cha Eugene's room.

Ahyeon couldn't get his thoughts to settle. His mind kept repeating the same resolve.

*This time, I need to react properly.*

Yes. Maybe this was an opportunity. A chance to alleviate the shock Moondae may have received due to his negative reaction.

"Whew."

*First of all, I'll thank him for taking me seriously...*

After imagining his reaction several times, Seon Ahyeon planned to visit Park Moondae's room at the agreed-upon time.

He didn't get the chance.

*Knock, knock.*

"I'm coming in."

"...!"

Moondae came to see him before that time even arrived.

And he went straight to the point.

"I've come early because I need to set something up. Let me use Raebin's computer for a moment."

His computer?

"Let's begin. Ah, Kim Raebin asked me to deliver this."

"Yes."

"Listen while drinking."

Moondae held out the tea he'd been carrying, then placed a notebook and pen in front of him.

*Notebook...?*

What followed was a flood of new information.

"Let me start with the witnesses."

"W-witnesses...?"

Moondae paused for a moment, then spoke in a firmer tone than usual.

"...I didn't say anything before, fearing that you would be more confused, but in fact, I'm not the only person who has returned to the past."

"...??"

The statement caught Ahyeon completely by surprise.

Ahyeon froze with the tea in his hand.

However, Park Moondae immediately began to bring out the people he invited.

Ahyeon could never have imagined it.

[Hmm.]

VTIC's Cheongryeo. The great sunbaenim he had just met had an openly insincere smile.

[Couldn't we have done this earlier, when we met? It would have been much easier.]

"Yes. Things have changed."

[Well, that was quick.]

Even after that, Cheongryeo had a brief conversation with Park Moondae, not knowing if he was being sarcastic or laughing, but soon admitted it.

[Okay. I was the person who traveled to the past before Moondae. Moondae—that is, Gunwoo—came after me.]

"......"

Was this really real?

"He was technically born before me, and he doesn't use formal speech with me. Perhaps you didn't notice earlier?"

Ahyeon had assumed it was because they were close, and he had envied that relationship a little.

"It's hilarious that you're sticking to formal speech now, even though you said you weren't going to record this."

"Take care."

[Haha!]

"It looks like you have more to repay me now, Moondae."

"You can leave now."

[Oh, my. You aren't denying it.]

Cheongryeo vanished from the screen.

"Hoo."

Park Moondae took a deep breath as if holding back his irritation.

"He's annoying, but anyway... For that reason, I meet him from time to time."

"......"

Then, while operating the computer again with a stiff face, he added in a slightly proud tone.

"This isn't a scam. This isn't even my computer, and you saw that I didn't touch the storage beforehand."

"......"

"If you want, we can go see him again right now, in person. Maybe we should."

"N-no, that's not necessary."

Seon Ahyeon responded reflexively.

His head felt like it was full of exclamation marks and question marks.

"Moving on, then. The next person is the one who came back to the past after me."

"Yes..."

"Ah, if you have any questions, feel free to write them down in this notebook. It doesn't matter what they are."

Seon Ahyeon immediately grabbed the notebook, but he moved stiffly, like a rusted robot.

[No way, hyung, it's been a while! How have you been?]

[I'm talking to Ahyeon-hyung this time... Ah, hyung, hello.]

Seon Ahyeon pinched his thigh for a moment to see if this was real, and it really did hurt.

"Now, that's why he's the one who came back to the past after me."

"So I arranged for you to meet yet another person who is closely involved."
