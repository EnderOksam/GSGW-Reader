# Chapter 639

"Did a lot of people show up?"

"A lot."

Lee Sejin smiled and stepped aside, revealing the audience seats through the window behind him.

Jamsil Olympic Stadium. Nearly 50,000 people, an astronomical number, filled the massive space.

"WAAAAAAH!"

A cool dusk breeze blew through the open window, carrying the resonating sounds of excitement all the way to their room.

"......"

*It's a magnificent sight, no matter how many times I see it.*

"I remember. During our first concert, Raebin and Eugene pretended to go to the bathroom just to sneak a peek outside. They came back all excited, saying how many people there were."

"Ooooh, I remember that!"

"It was fun..." Kim Raebin muttered blankly.

The electrifying thrill from their first solo concert, an unprecedented feeling, seemed to rush back to him. Now that he knew the feeling, the adrenaline would be even stronger.

*Is it true that a familiar taste is the one you crave most?*

I was no different. I looked out the window and took a deep breath. Hormones felt like they were surging to the top of my head.

"Well then, let's get ready..."

"Wait, wait!" Lee Sejin quickly cut in. "We have to do our cheer~"

"Ah."

"Right. That's important."

The members laughed and gathered close. Instead of their usual 'fighting' pose with hands together, they had started huddling shoulder to shoulder with linked arms for this promotion.

With their heads bowed and almost touching, the cheer passed between them. Their voices were more powerful and serious than usual, as if they were before a final match.

"TeSTAR, let's show them something today."

"YEAH!"

A strange tension and excitement filled the huddle. They could feel the breathing and body heat of the members beside them.

Then, they all stood up at the same time.

"Let's go."

The concert began.

***

A TeSTAR all-fan fidgeted with her hands, sitting in the affiliate section and enduring the suffocating wait.

*I-I should just be excited.*

The high school student felt excited but also pressured by her surroundings, swallowing nervously. The affiliates. Even the handsome man next to her, who she presumed was a celebrity.

*No. This will be a really fun memory when it's over...!*

She wanted to ask for 'Big Moon', the person who gave her the ticket on SNS, but the internet connection was too spotty with so many people around.

*Hoo...*

She felt relieved that she had pre-synced her new light stick. She turned the light stick on and off for no reason. The small, new employee ID keychain she bought dangled from it.

The person next to her also fiddled with something. She thought he was taking out a light stick, but... it was a camera.

"Huh."

The high school student let out the sound without thinking. She almost screamed when the person next to her turned around.

"Ah, I... It won't get in the way of your viewing experience." His tone was surprisingly gentle.

The high school student swallowed. "Ah, no."

Some fansite masters and data sellers would stand behind the audience and commit rude acts to film, like resting huge telephoto lenses on people's shoulders.

But the camera this person held was small. He had a strangely relaxed air and didn't seem like he would obstruct her view.

*Maybe he's an affiliate taking official photos for something.*

She was half right.

*I got permission!*Keundal thought confidently.*I even got permission from my hyungs!*

[As long as it doesn't interfere with the viewing experience, it's fine.]

He couldn't have guessed Park Moondae would even shield him by using his photos officially if it came to it. In any case, Keundal firmly reminded himself of the proposition, 'It's fine as long as it doesn't interfere'.

*It's small... I should secure it carefully on my lap so it doesn't bother the people around me.*

Of course, Keundal knew he had absolutely no talent for photography.

*Heuk...*

Besides, once the concert started, he would likely forget everything and fail to take a single shot, just like before, but he still wanted to keep trying.

*Because my hyung used to do it...*

The memory of his younger self, a high school dropout who found strength watching the videos his hyung filmed, was still vivid. Even without that talent, he wanted to find out. He wanted to know what his hyung felt while filming this.

"Hoo."

Keundal, Park Moondae in Ryu Gunwoo's body, took a deep breath and finished checking the camera. He also had his light stick, properly connected to the central control system. Not much time remained until the concert started.

However, something bothered him slightly.

*There's still no one here.*

The seat to his left was empty. The ticket holder hadn't arrived.

Could they have gotten a ticket and just not shown up? He heard this happened often in the affiliate section. Still, it felt like a waste, and he even felt a little resentful. So many people on SNS were crying because they desperately wanted that seat!

*It would have been better if a fan who understood that desperation could have watched the concert instead of someone who doesn't.*

But before he could glare at the empty seat any longer, the surrounding lights went out.

"...!"

Just as the lights on the audience disappeared, the commercial on the main screen also vanished. The band's instrumental began to play in the darkness.

A sentence appeared on the central screen.

[See you in the epilogue]

[by TeSTAR]

Fireworks and cheers erupted.

"Ah."

*They're coming out.*

Keundal held his breath, his eyes wide as he stared straight ahead.

TeSTAR's theme for this concert was 'Epilogue'. It seemed to conclude the story that had continued through games, VR, attractions, and remix promotions.

He felt someone finally arrive and sit in the seat next to him, but he no longer had the capacity to care. He poured all his focus into the VCR.

The grand orchestral music faded. The screen filled with a comfortable-looking attic and the soft sound of a piano. Seven scattered cushions, blankets, and the warm glow of small light bulbs.

[......]

It appeared to be the setting from the lore video where TeSTAR played a board game during their album promotions, but the seven members who had been gathered cozily were gone. Only Seon Ahyeon sat in their place.

In the middle of the day, with dust motes reflecting in the sunlight, the Seon Ahyeon on screen quietly put away the board game. The camera panned slightly to show a high school graduation album in a corner of the attic, with sticky notes attached.

[Let's hang out again!]

[Keep in touch after graduation.]

It seemed the members had grown older and, after graduating, could no longer come to play the game often.

Seon Ahyeon stopped cleaning up the board game. The camera closed in on his side profile.

[If we can meet again.]

The camera panned down to the board game.

[Again, we.]

The screen was sucked into it.

"...!"

The image warped through blue and red graphics like a roller coaster before shooting back out into the attic. Seon Ahyeon, who had been looking at the board game, lifted his head.

The attic was no longer empty.

[So... a board game? You want us to play a TRPG?]

[Oh my god.]

[Why? It looks fun.]

From a grumbling Cha Eugene and a flustered Lee Sejin, to Ryu Chungwoo trying to lift the mood. The small room, filled with the seven members of TeSTAR, was bustling. It was the first day Seon Ahyeon had called them together.

[So... you're going to run the game?]

[Yeah.]

[Have you played this kind of game a lot?]

Seon Ahyeon lifted his head and gazed at the audience beyond the screen.

[Yeah.]

Keuk.

Keundal heard his SNS friend next to him stifle a sound with her hand.

Meanwhile, the VCR's camera moved to show each member's face in turn.

[Alright, good.]

The members grinned.

[Let's have fun.]

The last words echoed from the turned-off screen as the stage lights turned on.

Flick.

A light shone on the giant, glittering golden 'Epilogue' sign. The cogwheel-shaped stage set moved with it as if by magic. The only background sound was the noise of machinery.

A voice rang out, a cappella.

[Bad Feelings-!]

KYAAAAAAAAAH!!!

AAAAAAH!

The main screen opened, and the seven members of TeSTAR ran out from within, accompanied by numerous dancers. They wore brilliant white suits fit for a concert.

"......"

❬Magical Boy❭ opened the stage, exploding like fireworks with a grand and brilliant orchestral arrangement, just like the original version.

[Even when the sun sets and the moonlight falls, these are unforgettable feelings]

His heart pounded every time a face filled the screen. Their faces shone with immersion, passion, joy, anticipation, and determination. Just like the people in the fancams Ryu Gunwoo had filmed before.

*No, even more so!*

[Reaching your hand, it rewinds again, My tape!]

The setlist stormed on, continuing with ❬Dice❭!

[Let's Start, begin the free-for-all]

Cha Eugene burst forward, and a new, spectacular dance break started from the center.

Knghh.

From his right, he heard a sound that was part scream, part groan, and part cheer. Even in his trance, he thought he had done a really good job giving away the spare ticket.

*Hyung! I think I, I used it really well!!*

[Just roll the dice!]

He watched his hyung perform the group choreography, shaking his light stick enthusiastically. He felt his knees move and the object on them shake slightly.

*Ah, the camera!!*

Thankfully, it was still recording. He carefully readjusted the camera as the song ended. Even then, he couldn't take his eyes off the stage.

[WAAAAAAAH!!!]

A roar so massive it felt cool echoed through the hot outdoor stadium. It was loud enough to deafen him, but the noise magically subsided as the members caught their breath and held their mics again.

[Hello, Loviewer.]

Hello!

AAAAAAH!

Aaaah-helllooo...

A mix of indescribable personal comments and screams ran rampant. The audience quieted for a moment, then burst into laughter.

TeSTAR's shoulders shook with laughter before they shouted cheerfully.

[To everyone who came to TeSTAR's concert, welcome!]

[Thank you! Let's have fun together!]

Another explosive roar of cheers erupted.

TeSTAR moistened their throats with water hastily handed up from below, then continued speaking.

[We've made a lot of fun and unique attempts in the past, right? Because we wanted to give you all various kinds of enjoyment.]

TeSTAR certainly had a unique theme for each concert and had often used interesting items or new technologies. Keundal recalled SNS opinions had been divided on whether there would be a VR choice this time too.

[But this time, it's a little different.]

"...?"

[Because we wanted to move you in the biggest way with the most fundamental thing.]

The TeSTAR members exchanged meaningful glances. Kim Raebin's eyes sparkled as he raised his mic.

[That's right. This concert... we've filled it completely with just our songs and performances, without anything else! We added as many songs to the setlist as possible!]

The essence of a concert. The joy of hearing a song you know. Kim Raebin's words carried an emotional weight, and cheers and shouts erupted from the audience once more.

Lee Sejin laughed loudly and waved his hand.

[We're going to run through this with almost no talking from now on. All the songs you know will be performed, for real!]

WOAAAAAH!

Another crazy roar of cheers. Hearing it, Keundal felt a tremendous internal conflict.

*Should I watch it with my bare eyes, or should I try to film properly with the camera, at least during the talking segments...!*

He was fiddling with the camera with a pounding heart.

"Shall I hold that for you."

"...?"

He looked to his side and saw that someone now sat in the seat next to him. The person wore a black hat and a black mask. It was... Cheongryeo.

"......"

*What... did I just see?*

Moreover, another masked face suddenly popped out from beside Cheongryeo.

"Oh, hello! So you're the one. Have you been well?"

"Ye-yes...?"

It was Chaeyul.

Keundal's pupils trembled.

*Why is VTIC here...?*

Keundal, who couldn't imagine his hyung would have given tickets to VTIC, sucked in a breath. It wasn't actually that unusual of a situation. Since the two companies were tied by capital and had become a sort of 'same line', the public would find it plausible that they were visiting the concert as a form of business.

But at this moment, Keundal's bewilderment surpassed all of that.

*And why, why my camera?!*

Next to him, Cheongryeo's face was hidden in shadows. He was still pointing at Keundal's camera, the corners of his eyes crinkled. It seemed he was smiling gently, even though it couldn't be seen.

"......"

It was a little scary. Just a little.

"I-it's okay."

"......"

Cheongryeo readily gave up. Keundal swallowed and quietly turned his gaze back to the stage. On one side, his high school student SNS friend. On the other, his hyung's rival idol.

It was a fantastic stroke of luck with seating that completely scattered his attention...

*Ugh.*

However, once you realize you're at a concert venue, such thoughts truly become insignificant. When you're watching the idol on that stage holding a microphone.

[We'll move on to the next song.]

Keundal blankly stared forward again.

On stage, TeSTAR's Park Moondae smiled nonchalantly as he wiped the sweat beading on his temple.

The concert had only just begun.
