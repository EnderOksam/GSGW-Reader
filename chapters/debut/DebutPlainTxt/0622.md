# Chapter 622

What would you think if you secured a spot on the main stage of a renowned global music festival, only for your rivals to show up as the headliner?

"Haha..."

"Hoo."

It obviously felt like shit.

When the media reported on the festival, the headliner would naturally sweep up all the attention. It also seemed like a blatant attempt to show the difference in our international status.

[In Korea it's TeSTAR, but globally it's still VTIC]

I was sick and tired of hearing it.

We clearly had a better response here, but this wasn't the first or second time we had to watch them be treated as a higher class because of the international results they'd accumulated over the years.

It was the same with albums. We definitely sold more domestically, but our total sales fell far behind theirs.

"......"

This time, it was genuinely exhausting.

I silently processed the news from the company as a light silence fell over the living room. Then, a question suddenly occurred to me.

*VTIC is the Glastonbury headliner.*

We were on the main stage. Okay, let's say VTIC had better global appeal than us. Even so.

*Are these guys really at a level to be headliners?*

I wasn't ignoring what VTIC had built up. Besides, in that scene, releasing albums every one or two years was common, so their hiatus was probably just treated as a normal album cycle. If anything, the unique reason for their break might have even generated more buzz.

However.

*Still, they aren't singers that make everyone gasp in admiration.*

That had always been K-Pop's weakness. Public recognition.

[VTIC? Don't tell me they're a K-Pop idol group?]

[Oh my god, the K-Pop plague has reached the UK]

[Who would go to a show on a day when a 'K-Pop boy band' is performing instead of a living legend of our time?]

[I like it, I've been curious about them sometimes, I should check them out that day :)]

Sure enough, as soon as the rumors started, people in the English-speaking world began to talk. Of course, they were getting into dogfights with VTIC's fans, but the point was that VTIC fell short in public appeal compared to the rest of the lineup. There was just blatant noise, and no 'wow' of admiration.

[Not going on Saturday]

Even setting aside all prejudice, it meant they were the weakest in the lineup.

Well, it wasn't so strange as to suspect corruption. K-Pop had weak public recognition, but it was always strong in ticket sales.

*Since the festival runs for three days, they sometimes give one day to an experimental headliner.*

However, if they had played it safe, getting a main stage slot like us would have been more appropriate. Many other famous pop singers started by just getting their names on the main stage lineup.

*Were they in a hurry?*

Besides, those guys didn't have a main vocalist. I knew Judan and Shino had decent vocal flavors, but you needed at least one power vocalist at a concert like this to get a good audience response. I didn't know if forcing a headliner spot above their weight class was a wise idea on a global scale.

*Hmm.*

I calmed my anger and felt composure return in its place. The other guys also seemed to be calming down as they started discussing a rational plan. First, we needed to assess the situation.

[6 / 21 / FRI - TeSTAR]

"We're on Friday. VTIC is on Saturday."

The two groups performed on different days.

"Our dates are different. Hmm, is that a little better?"

I crossed my arms. "It could actually be worse."

"Why?"

"Because people who are friendly toward K-Pop, or just like K-Pop in general, will most likely focus on the day VTIC is headlining."

"...Ah."

If we were the only ones invited, people interested in K-Pop might have come to see just us with more interest. However, as long as there was a K-Pop headliner on a different day...

"Is there a high chance we'll be performing in front of an audience that doesn't view us very favorably?"

That was right. It was an unavoidable problem of proportion.

*On top of that, they're hogging all the buzz about being the first K-Pop headliner.*

That was actually good in terms of generating buzz.

For various reasons, I was coming to the conclusion that those bastards making a somewhat unreasonable move against us was a smart choice, which pissed me off even more. Fuck.

I was trying to cool my simmering anger again when Seon Ahyeon began, his face slightly flushed.

"I-I don't think we need to worry, though...!"

"A good performance... um, I think it can connect with people, beyond language or borders... As long as we can show them, that is."

"......"

Cha Eugene shrugged, then snatched the paper in front of us and tossed it behind him.

"Cha Eugene!"

"Stop worrying about who's performing when. If we do better than the Headliner, that's the right answer."

"......"

"I always say the right thing. You know that, right?"

A few small laughs escaped at his words.

"Huh? I like that you're laughing, but what's the reason?"

"No, ahem, it's just... we're used to it," Lee Sejin grinned. "Yeah. Even if we're not the headliner, as long as we do well, that's all that matters."

Of course, this guy wasn't trying to say something idealistic like Seon Ahyeon.

"Because, um, fancams will get uploaded anyway, right?"

Correct.

*The buzz from the simultaneous release with VTIC will continue.*

[Comparing the stages of the two K-Pop boy idols who appeared at Glastonbury]

All sorts of comparison videos would float around on WeTube. If we just tried, we could create plenty of opportunities to turn the tables there, and wasn't that what we did best?

Live performances.

"Let's take them on."

"Sounds good."

The conversation ended with morale soaring. However, one person had a slightly peculiar expression. It was Ryu Chungwoo.

"......"

I quietly nudged him. "What's on your mind?"

"Ah." He answered with a faint, bitter smile. "I want to practice together, but... if I do, it'll be confusing when I'm not in the formation on the final day."

He was saying that for the performance's quality, it would be better for him to sit out starting now. Good grief.

I smacked him on the back.

"...!"

"Hey. Cut it out."

Ryu Chungwoo's eyes widened.

"It's not like we're rookies in our first or second year. You think we'd get confused by something like that?" I leaned back against the sofa. "Even if you say you can't do it right before the show, adjusting the formation won't be a problem. Stop talking nonsense and just practice. If you feel like it."

"...Yeah."

I ignored Keun Sejin mouthing 'Ooh', and so, Ryu Chungwoo continued to participate in practice. He worked really hard.

"I'll do it one more time."

"Okay."

He naturally cut into his own break time and even seemed to practice for this stage when he couldn't make it to live broadcast schedules.

"Hyung looks like he's having fun, doesn't he?"

"Yeah. Let's leave him be."

He gritted his teeth and raised the level of perfection even higher than usual, but right before we left the country, he finally made his decision.

"It would be better if I don't go on stage."

"......"

[Ryu Chungwoo will not be on stage this time.]

"If I go up there and freeze, I think it'll be really difficult to handle the aftermath this time."

In front of an unfamiliar, less-than-friendly audience, what kind of impact would one member completely missing his parts and choreography have on the group's name?

"This time... I think it would be better for the group if I held back."

It seemed like a decision he made after thinking over the potential fallout of such an incident multiple times.

*If I convince him he can definitely do it and it ends up being a disaster, he'll have to bear the mental burden of the fallout all by himself.*

That would be a terrible thing to do. In the end, we all nodded heavily at Ryu Chungwoo's words.

"...You'll still come with us, right?"

"Of course."

And so, TeSTAR left the country. During our standby and rehearsal preparations, we ran into them. The K-Pop headliner performing the day after us.

"Thank you so much!"

VTIC was dressed in denim outfits, lighter and more free-spirited than suits, fitting for a festival. In the past, I probably would have just said they were wearing jeans. I guess I'd grown. I could even say this line without feeling anything.

"Congratulations on being the first K-Pop headliner at Glastonbury."

"Hmm, well, the first appearance, if you go by date, is you, junior. We're on Saturday, and TeSTAR is on Friday."

It must be hard to pick only the words that twist people's insides, you bastard.

However, instead of raising an eyebrow, I just gave a slight nod. A lot of eyes were on us. I had to maintain a professional appearance.

But that bastard Cheongryeo waited until people had cleared out before speaking to me again.

"You must be very upset that we've overlapped like this again."

"......"

*What... are you expecting me to say?*

I stared at him with a look of disbelief.

"Well, everyone makes the best choice for themselves."

"Yes. The best choice..." Cheongryeo fell silent.

*Should I get going.*

Just as I was briefly wondering, he suddenly opened his mouth.

"Hmm, I wonder if you know, junior. About the future that would have happened if I hadn't made this choice."

"This choice?"

"It's the comprehensive meaning of the current situation, but specifically... yes. Let's say I'm talking about the comeback timing."

VTIC's comeback timing. I realized he meant the simultaneous album release with TeSTAR.

"I told you, didn't I? Thanks to that, even people who wouldn't have been interested originally are showing interest."

However... if they hadn't done that.

"No, I still think we would have sold more albums than you, junior."

"......"

"That would have been it."

[Ah, VTIC is making a comeback]

[Discharged from the military? ㅋㅋ That was fast]

[Oh, did you get on Bill? Congrats congrats, but the rank is a bit lower than before ㅋㅋ]

Passing interest. It was the limitation of a senior idol group that had already done everything, no matter how carefully they managed their image consumption. Especially if they had focused on overseas activities.

"Our digital music might not have even made it into the charts," Cheongryeo calmly stated. "You know where a boy idol group with no domestic buzz ends up, right?"

Fans become conscious of that too, and there's a limit to how much they can overextend themselves buying albums to maintain the group's status. Glory in name only. Status only in the past. VTIC would have slowly sunk like that.

They say a great merchant lasts for three years even after going bankrupt. While the public was thinking, 'Huh?', they would have quietly faded away into being singers of the past.

"That's why I took one last challenge."

I realized. VTIC's appearance as the headliner at this festival was also a choice made with the domestic audience in mind. This time, they sold a lot of albums, and their digital music chart performance was decent, though not as good as their peak.

However, as he said himself, that was purely the result of their fans' purchasing power having some leeway, combined with the buzz from releasing at the same time as us. To make that into something with real substance...

*They have to make a proper mark with this album, no matter what.*

So that's why they pushed themselves to get the headliner spot. They wanted to send a sign to the domestic audience that 'VTIC will remain an eternal legend'.

"That's why we secured this spot."

"......"

"It might have been a bit harsh for you, junior."

I held my breath for a moment.

"What are you talking about. You're the one who lost on the digital charts."

"...!"

"And..." I cracked my neck. CRACKLE. "It's not over yet."

"......"

Cheongryeo's eyes seemed to reply.*The first-week sales period is over, and domestic music show promotions should be finished too, right? What on earth isn't over?*

*Just watch.*

Instead of saying anything more, I left.

"Good luck with your stage, sunbaenim."

The promotion period wasn't over yet.

[The Unpredictable Glastonbury Festival... Will K-Pop Idols Show Their Skills?]

I only heard bits of news that VTIC had finished their festival rehearsal for the day without any issues.

*It doesn't matter.*

We just needed to do what we had to do. I silently prepared for the stage. The other guys were doing the same.

"......"

However, a loose thread came out from the very first button.

"Our... headliner supposedly dropped out."

Ah, fuck. Seriously.

"...The audience is going to be lukewarm."

The people who came to see the headliner were likely the majority. It was obvious how deflated those people would be after not being able to see the artist they were looking forward to. People would sell their tickets or just leave. Even if not, we would have to hype up an audience whose excitement had completely died down.

In a language they didn't know.

"......"

Why the fuck was so much bad news being shoved down my throat one after another?

I held back a sigh, my eyebrow twitching. Well, it wasn't like this kind of thing never happened, but at this rate, we were set to lose all the buzz despite the headliner's absence. Our public recognition was already on the weaker side, and now the audience wouldn't even be in a good mood.

*This is the perfect situation to hit rock bottom.*

A solid mid-low range that leads the song. A robust vocal that is stable like a bass, but also knows how to soar like a piano. His vocal tone is good, so he's great no matter where he's placed, and his stage acting isn't limited by genre, making him perfect for verses and pre-choruses.
