# Chapter 332

What kind of situation was this?

I was lying in bed. I had no idea what was going on. 

The situation just before I went to visit Lee Sejin, who had left the hotel room for camping...

– Beep.

I pressed the doorbell of Lee Sejin's hotel room, but there was no response.

– ...

He was obviously inside.

– Lee Sejin.

I waited a moment, then rang the doorbell again.

Then came the response.

*Ding.*

Not at the door, but on my smartphone.

★:
-Lee Sejin: Let's talk next time, Moondae. I'm a bit tired right now

"......"

...I had to do what I had to do.

"......"

Was that so?

– A few days ago, I was able to open the status window by calling it from my side. So I wondered if you could see it on the screen...

"Okay."

It was something worth making a video call about. I understood his intentions.

And when he shut his mouth, the room became quiet again.

"......"

A cautious voice was heard.

– Um, hyung. Also, what happened earlier?

"It's nothing."

Couldn't he stop asking me about that?

I almost felt annoyed at the moment, but I realized there was no need to be and suppressed it. 

...From his point of view, the phone suddenly hung up, so of course he'd ask.

"I hung up because it was someone who didn't know you, but there was a bit of a misunderstanding."

– Ah...

After a brief exclamation, he was speechless again. He seemed to have understood the situation roughly.

*Is the story about to end?*

He spoke at the moment when I wondered if it would be okay to end the conversation and hang up.

– I... Then wouldn't it be okay if you told him the circumstances?

"What?"

– Lee Sejin, right? He is hyung's close friend. Then...

"I returned to the past in someone else's body and became an idol, because if I didn't, I would die, so I could just say that I leveled up and came this far."

– ...

"Common sense, this... No, sorry."

At that moment, I realized why Lee Sejin stopped the conversation and left. I guess I did this to avoid needlessly shouting.

I shook my head and continued.

"I'll organize and analyze the status window problem you just mentioned, so you should rest today..."

– No. Hyung.

The guy stopped talking.

– I think it's really the first time that hyung isn't paying attention to the system story like this. Normally, you would have asked a lot of questions.

"...!"

– I think you are concerned about the current situation.

"......"

And a small voice was heard.

– Then just say it... How about telling him comfortably?

I involuntarily clenched my fist.

That was the moment.

I suddenly recalled the afterimages derived from those words.

Mistakes this guy made every time I met him until now.

– Hyung!

– Gunwoo-hyung, uh, uhm...

Errors in titles or endings.

I knew it was because he was so bad at deceiving others.

But now that I thought about it, how was it that the guy who lived in the body of Ryu Gunwoo for several years made such a mistake every time...

Wasn't it unnatural?

"You, no way, you made mistakes whenever I was with the members..."

– ...

"Is it because you wanted me to be found out?"

After a moment of silence...

A voice came from the screen.

– Maybe...

"...!"

– I wasn't conscious at the time, but I think I did it unconsciously... I thought that it would help hyung.

"What."

– TeStar are people who are close to you and have lived with you for several years. I wanted hyung to feel at ease after revealing his secret...

It was an action based on goodwill.

However, it reminded me of something, in that he did not consider the other person's intentions and feelings at all.

And he talked about it himself.

– But that's what the system does.

Yes.

– Hyung, I'm ignoring your thoughts and feelings and just doing it.

It was an overly mechanical and inhuman impulse for this frail fellow.

I opened my mouth slowly.

"...So, did you assume you were being affected by the system?"

The last time we met, it reminded me of a guy who was especially afraid of the system.

– I feel that the part of me that has been assimilated with the system is getting stronger. Even if it moves to another body, it seems to have some effect.

And a bitter voice came out of the screen, as if a little daunted.

– Yes. So, since I noticed, I'm really trying not to make mistakes...

"......"

– It wasn't really intentional. It must have been uncomfortable for you, but I'm really sorry. I will be very careful not to be swayed in the future...

"...Okay."

A sigh of relief was heard from the screen.

*He must have had a hard time.*

Being influenced by something he didn't know must have been quite unpleasant.

*I'm going to have to pay close attention to this guy's condition from now on.*

As if I had been bathed in cold water, I came to my senses a little and looked at Keundal.

But the topic had returned.

– Yes, but hyung, this is really a separate message. That friend will definitely trust you.

I was going crazy.

I spoke while holding back my saliva.

"If he had the personality to believe such science-fiction stories, I would have finished organizing it by now."

However, Lee Sejin had the most realistic personality.

I conceded.

*So... I was procrastinating.*

Because we had to work, and because the timing wasn't right.

With such an excuse, I was justifying the situation where I kept my mouth shut and procrastinated.

I was intoxicated with the atmosphere and said, 'I'll tell him when the time is right'... No matter how I conveyed it, the probability of him not believing it seemed too high.

I sighed.

Then, a voice that sounded a bit ridiculous on the screen... What, ridiculous?

– Hyung, I'm trying to tell you in case you forgot... But, hyung, I was your status window??

I knew that.

– I know how Lee Sejin-nim and hyung have been!

"...!!"

– I don't want hyung to get into fights... Anyway, I am speaking from the point of view of a third party who knows all the information. Please trust me...!

Seeing all of that, it was quite embarrassing.

However, Keundal was embarrassed and annoyed, and now he was almost wailing.

– As much as that friend is realistic, you know him well...! And you trust each other a lot.

It was funny to hear that.

– If hyung explains it seriously and honestly, he will believe it.

"......"

I lay down on one arm.

– Hyung...

I didn't know.

"I don't even know how to say it. It just happened like this."

– Ah...

"First... Take a look at the situation."

I was going to tell the truth now, but I didn't know how this guy would come out tomorrow.

There was a possibility that it was because of his immediate emotions and that it was not a big deal. If he cooled down, I could explain the misunderstanding and be a little embarrassed, and then it would end.

– Hyung, so...

The guy on the screen seemed to be trying to say something of encouragement in his own way, but it didn't really come across.

"Okay."

I soon ended the call, and after the conflict, I replied to Lee Sejin's text message.

★:
Sorry. Rest well-
:★

And it was funny, but it really didn't matter.

Lee Sejin liked concerts.

If you were familiar with him, it would be easy to misunderstand that someone like Lee Sejin would prefer variety shows or appearances on famous WeTube channels.

Originally, what people thought with their heads and what they felt with their hearts were different.

The concert was no different from proof of oneself as an idol.

The existence of dreams and ambitions.

*That's why I like it...*

But today, he couldn't fully enjoy that good concert.

Originally, at a concert, it was normal for the members to get together and enjoy a meet-and-greet around the time of the encore.

Even if it wasn't that, when all the members were together on the stage, it also inspired immersion and a sense of belonging.

But that was impossible today.

*Hoo.*

It was because after the uproar a few days ago, he stood on stage together without having had a proper conversation with Park Moondae.

He was conscious of it moment by moment as if a stone had gotten into his shoe, and he felt down every time.

*Why...*

In fact, he already knew that Park Moondae didn't take him 'as easily' as the other members.

In fact, it was rather pleasant.

It was because it felt like a kind of relationship establishment. 

*Equal friends.*

He was even a little proud. Even if the group disappeared, he was confident that he and Park Moondae would continue to work together and remain friends, even if no one else knew.

That reassurance.

However, once he knew that it was an illusion, with that certainty... He felt betrayed.

*It's really funny.*

No, Park Moondae didn't even say that he trusted him the most, so what kind of betrayal did he feel?

Lee Sejin laughed.

He knew very well that being a human being meant that positions could easily change depending on the environment. And there was nothing particularly sad about it.

*This industry is the most like that.*

So he couldn't understand at all why he was so disappointed with Park Moondae, as if they had become great friends.

But if he thought about it again, wasn't that enough?

*It's not like we were just a moderate group.*

They spent a lot of time talking about all sorts of troubles, accidents, and personal affairs.

And yet...

Lee Sejin clenched his fists.

*It ended with an apology through text.*

It meant that Park Moondae had nothing to regret about right now. 

What was even funnier was that he was thinking about whether to talk to him first and solve the situation.

*Wow!*

He wasn't just out of breath.

It was because he felt like an idiot who couldn't distinguish between that kind of structure.

So he paid more attention to performance and live. If he lacked immersion, he would have to do it elaborately.

"Thank you for your hard work~"
