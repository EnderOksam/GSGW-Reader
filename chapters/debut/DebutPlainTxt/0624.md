# Chapter 624

The concert at Glastonbury was in full swing.

Haena, Cha Eugene's middle school classmate, stared at the stage with her mouth agape. She had followed TeSTAR with interest ever since running into them at a hotteok shop.

THUD.

A dark, old-school hip-hop sound rang out.

SCREEECH!

The stage's electronic display flashed before her eyes.

A deep, low-toned rap burst forth, piercing the distorted band sound like a rough engine or a beast's howl. It was the altered intro to their title track.

[Get this feeling

Then I'll fall and fall]

The choreography seemed to stomp on the floor with heavy steps, matching a building drumbeat.

A performer with a decadent impression bobbed his hatted head to the rhythm. His beauty mark and sanpaku eyes stood out.

Kim Raebin.

[Umm-]

He lowered his stance.

An electric bass distorted the air.

He popped from his shoulders to his toes, bouncing as he opened his mouth to rap.

[I sleepwalk

Walking on the asphalt, slowly, on and on

Chill out, still nonchalantly, step step step,

(Reload)]

Behind tossing hand gestures, the members' choreography exploded in all directions. They then lined up and crossed their arms.

A threatening movement spread along the heavy rhythm, making them look like they were prowling closer.

A rough rap was laid over it.

[Still reloading

Fearlessly, ardently, without you, click-clack]

The next member popped out from behind the hand gestures, bobbing his head.

Cha Eugene.

[(yeah, Reload-)]

"......"

Beneath his grinning face, his bounces and steps flowed playfully.

Instead of a fitting accompaniment, a super high note soared through the venue.

[Woo-]

Park Moondae's harmony, playing in that high octave, surged in and seemed to lift everything up.

[Today I'm sleepwalking again, every day

You even appear in my dreams

No resolutions or gritted teeth are of any use]

As the drawn-out, super high-pitched voice faded, the rap poured out again.

The members stood scattered across the stage, using their limbs rhythmically to control the beat's dynamics. They looked like they were just bouncing around, but they were perfectly synchronized.

They threw their bodies down as if collapsing onto the floor.

[Get out]

It was extreme.

Haena gasped, her eyes feeling like they would pop out.

*This is... so different from the last song!*

But it wasn't just smooth, bright, and exciting like the original song either.

[Because I've received so much]

THUD.

The choreography changed in time with their steps.

[I can't throw a single thing away]

VWOOM!

An intense, piercing high note cut through, followed by a rap that hammered down relentlessly.

English translations appeared on the screen behind the stage, but unfortunately, almost no one paid close attention to them.

The performance, vocals, and rap attacked them all at once.

[I'll give you a present too]

The performer spat out a heavy, fast, old-school hip-hop verse, gripping the handheld mic tightly.

If the song had continued like this, it might have conveyed a sense of respect for historical tradition.

However, the moment the chorus was supposed to begin.

[Bad feelings.]

A tremendous, twisted electronic sound slammed through the speakers.

"......"

If this were traditional old-school hip-hop, this would be the time for a simple, repetitive phrase that was easy to sing along to.

Instead, a crazily heavy and chaotic MIDI sound, almost like complextro, assaulted the air.

And an electronic melody.

[Bad feelings

fill me up,

roll it underground]

A slow but powerful move, using their entire bodies, filled the stage.

Cha Eugene bobbed his head and twisted the corner of his mouth, completing the gesture and choreography.

This was far from natural, far from hippie. It was a progression that basically screamed 'fuck it all'!

The performance and synchronized choreography were crafted purely for impact, bordering on madness.

It was closer to the very essence of the modern entertainment art industry.

[Bad feelings

pick you up,

roll it underground]

*To hell with attitude, I'll do whatever I want.*

As if to assert that, as if to scream 'Look at me', they raced across the stage.

*What is this...?!*

In fact, the moment she saw TeSTAR's first live song, she had thought something else.

*[They must have arranged their performance to fit Glastonbury!]*

The atmosphere had felt more like a live band than usual, emphasizing vocals and emotion.

A lyrical and overwhelming emotion.

It had a more 'natural' feel.

It aligned with the spirit of the Glastonbury Festival, which is rooted in hippie culture. It also seemed like a desirable attitude for a performer attending this glorious event for the first time.

Besides, hadn't they succeeded in changing the mood with an English-lyric movie OST?

She thought they were trying to restrain their 'K-Pop-ness' as much as possible and go for a more traditional, European-friendly approach.

However.

[Fill in the blank]

Before her eyes, a rough choreography exploded along with LED fireworks.

The largest member, who had been flicking his hand, led the choreography without a hint of a smile. He used his body's movements to their fullest in an overwhelmingly intimidating way.

Just seeing the group dance on the large screen made her flinch.

[Ururururu-]

Their intense individual personalities were like a patchwork of various wild animals torn apart and stitched together, threatening to become a chaotic mess at any moment.

It was closer to an acrobatic feat.

Difficult individual choreography, vocals, and rap ad-libs popped out ceaselessly. They continued as if they were showing off their personal skills.

It left her breathless.

[Fill in the blank]

Then, the turbulent electronic sounds finally calmed.

[Fill in the blank with...]

THUMP. THUMP.

As the members lined up to the low notes of the leisurely pulsing bass, one person walked out from between them.

A member in a black jumper.

He looked around as he walked out, as if in no hurry, and took his place in the overall formation.

[These bad feelings]

Ryu Chungwoo.

He stood in the center, right where the original song's chorus melody finally returned.

***

Ryu Chungwoo remembered.

He recalled the conversation they had after seeing the composition for this stage.

"[I guess Eugene or Raebin will be the focus.]"

It was only natural.

For a rap-centric song where a strong image and individuality were important, those two were the right people for the job in this team.

Park Moondae had nodded as well.

"[Those two do pull off this kind of concept well.]"

However.

"[They're guys with such strong individual colors. For them to do their own thing while still coming together as a group performance, you need something more.]"

"[Something more?]"

Ryu Chungwoo walked to the center.

"[A center.]"

What is the one thing that strong personalities must be most careful of?

Distraction.

As the number of elements increases, the possibility of them looking unnatural together and failing to form a single picture, rather than creating synergy, increases exponentially.

On the other hand, if they try too hard to match each other, the impact of those powerful individual parts feels a little lacking.

In the end, for each individual to act as they please and still look like a single team.

"[Balance and power.]"

The center of the song, the chorus.

On stage, Ryu Chungwoo bobbed his head.

The members around him formed the surrounding formation with knife-like precision.

And then the vocals dropped.

[Don't you try to escape either

Think of me every morning]

At every point where the choreography paused, his power was precise, and his gaze locked in perfectly.

The song and the group dance merged into one.

And a pleasant chorus melody.

[These Bad feelings]

As if taking a stroll.

Whoosh. Over the heavy accompaniment, a clear vocal led the song in an easier-to-listen-to direction, as if for a breath of fresh air.

The choreography no longer madly changed colors, competing with itself.

It simply focused skillfully around the center.

[These Bad feelings]

The focal point of concentration.

Within it, Ryu Chungwoo finally thought.

He never needed to think about it.

[You take it with you too

Look at it every day, just like me]

*This was the feeling.*

He didn't need complex, deep thoughts. He knew what he had to do right now, and that was enough.

*Now I get it.*

[These bad feelings]

*The words saying it's okay to give up. I hate hearing them.*

*The words telling me to sit down and rest. I hate them.*

*Because...*

*[I am someone who doesn't need to do that.]*

The reason he didn't want pity or concern was that he didn't need them.

He was used to being someone who could do anything.

Self-efficacy.

His generosity in taking care of others actually stemmed from that. As someone filled with trust and faith in himself, he could afford to be relaxed and gentle in any situation.

Ryu Chungwoo was not a person who dwelled on failure.

Thanks to the words that served as a clue to reawaken that proposition, Ryu Chungwoo could now sort things out without hesitation.

Like his old self.

[I'm chasing after you

Pop up tomorrow morning too]

No. The worries and doubts should rightfully disappear.

[You take it with you too

Check it every day, just like me]

No, all those worries and conflicts were not like him.

*Worrying about a failure that hasn't even happened isn't me.*

*Worrying about a failure that has already passed isn't me.*

*I am not that kind of person.*

[Get out]

The band stopped.

Ryu Chungwoo, who had caught the melody that cut in, aimed for his cue during the brief moment of silence on stage.

[-]

*Now, let it go.*

*Like an arrow leaving the bowstring. Let it cut through the air, past the inflection point, and shoot straight toward a single point.*

Thump-thump.

*In the right direction. To the right place.*

[But no more bad feelings!]

VMMMMMM-!

The beat dropped.

"Hoo."

His eyes shone, his breath was short, and everything on stage was in its right place.

Ryu Chungwoo knew.

Because he had known all along.

[Full Draw (S)]

[When you draw the bowstring, I will be in the correct position.]

: Performance Composure +150%

*I can do this.*

[Release (EX)]

[The arrow that was finally shot.]

: Performance Effect +180%

Fireworks exploded on stage.

[No more bad feelings]

A clear, standard-setting vocal hit.

Powerful choreography, holding each move with weight and strength, cut through the rap sampling and riff melody.

The focus shifted to the center, as if converging on a single point.

With an unwavering concentration.

[Fill in the blank]

Before long, the latter half of the song surged forward.

[Bad feelings

fill me up,

roll it underground]

Screams and gasps poured out like a spinal reflex at Seon Ahyeon's bewilderingly sudden backflip.

However, the song's center did not waver.

*Here.*

Holding it tight, the song's focus came alive.

Beneath the low-mid range voice, the drums beat like a heart. Ryu Chungwoo, leading the chorus melody, remained unchanged.

[Bad feelings

pick you up,

roll it underground]

Behind Cha Eugene and Lee Sejin, who burst out for the dance break, Park Moondae hit a high note, and the song's progression shot forward again.

[But no more bad feelings]

However, the melodic chorus kept the song moving in the right direction.

Holding the mic, Ryu Chungwoo came to the center again.

[I can run

Up there

Not really

With words of concern

To the end of my thoughts]

It was a song filled with an unfamiliar language and an unorthodox progression.

However, what reeled it back in at the end was Ryu Chungwoo's chorus.

It raised the song's intuitiveness to a level the audience could sing along to.

[Bad feelings]

As hands reached for the sky, the members' formation broke apart in all directions.

The LED lights flared.

"......"

Silence.

The song was over.

The people who had been confused, wondering what this was. The people with their mouths agape. The people who had been frowning but were now watching as if it were a spectacle, and among them...

The impressed audience members.

WAAAAAAAH!!

In their midst, Ryu Chungwoo gasped for breath.

And he realized it completely.

It was fine if opinions were divided.

[Thank you!]

The stage was perfect as one.

TeSTAR had definitely left an impression.
