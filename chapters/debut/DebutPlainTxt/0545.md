# Chapter 545

A cold air flowed through the JSA common area.

A cautious voice came from the receiver.

[Um... who is this, please?]

Click.

Shin Jaehyun ended the call.

The name 'Seon Ahyeon' vanished from the screen.

Beside him, a trembling voice asked, "He... doesn't remember, does he?"

Cheongryeo did not answer.

However, Keundal, who asked the question, also knew an answer was unnecessary.

Seon Ahyeon did not know Park Moondae.

He did not remember, even though Cheongryeo had called him from Park Moondae's smartphone.

"......"

Cheongryeo's judgment to single out and call Seon Ahyeon immediately was not wrong. He made the call as soon as the unexpected situation arose.

He had already seen Seon Ahyeon cause an error in the System's virtual world.

"He has a trait that nullifies status abnormalities..."

But now, even Seon Ahyeon could not remember Park Moondae.

"Hoo..."

Because of this, the associate he called before Seon Ahyeon, Keundal, was on the verge of a complete panic.

It was only natural.

"This... this doesn't make any sense."

Keundal shot up and stretched out his hand.

"Moondae-hyung is right here...!"

His hand pointed to a figure lying on the sofa, unconscious and still as a mouse.

It was Park Moondae.

He looked peaceful without a single external injury, but one could only think that if they were unaware of the current situation.

A few minutes ago, Keundal had arrived at JSA in a hurry.

[E-Excuse me.]

[Yes?]

[Over there...]

Keundal, with a deathly pale face, had grabbed an employee and pointed toward the sofa where Park Moondae lay.

[That person, what do they look like to you?]

The cleaning staff had replied.

[Who are you referring to?]

They could not perceive Park Moondae, who was clearly right there.

"......"

The situation was almost terrifying.

Only then did Keundal realize why Cheongryeo had not called 119.

Even if he did, they would not be able to recognize Park Moondae.

As Keundal swallowed hard at the memory,

Bzzzt.

"......"

Park Moondae's smartphone vibrated intensely in Cheongryeo's hand.

It was a call.

Cheongryeo glanced at the screen without expression and answered. A soft, urgent voice echoed from the speaker.

[S-Sorry! The call suddenly disconnected.]

It was Seon Ahyeon's voice.

When the call ended without context, the polite young man had decided to call back himself.

He kindly asked again.

[Um, by any chance... is the person you mentioned earlier, someone related to me?]

Keundal let out an indescribable groan and buried his head on the table.

Cheongryeo watched him impassively and spoke in a dry voice.

"Go to a portal site on the internet right now."

[...? Y-Yes...]

The faint sound of fingers tapping a screen echoed as Seon Ahyeon used his smartphone.

"Search for Park Moondae."

"......"

At that moment, Keundal snapped his head up and also hurriedly searched for Park Moondae online.

*...He's there!*

Park Moondae's profile appeared, intact.

His career, activities, and all sorts of photos and histories filled the page.

*Thank god...*

Park Moondae was still here.

Feeling a sliver of relief, Keundal turned his head to Cheongryeo.

"......"

Over the phone, Seon Ahyeon seemed to have checked the search results as well.

[Who... is that?]

"......"

A cold sensation brushed past Keundal's spine.

An equally cold voice instructed the smartphone.

"Tell me the first profile you see on the screen."

[I, I don't particularly... see a profile.]

"......"

Keundal held his breath.

With a trembling hand, he quickly went on social media and typed in a search.

[Park Moondae]

Results poured in.

They continued right up until just before Park Moondae retrieved the fragment.

After that, there was no mention of him at all.

"...W-Wait."

This time, Keundal searched for TeSTAR.

[It's amazing how TeSTAR has exactly two members of the same age lol]

[Ryu Chungwoo, Bae Sejin, Seon Ahyeon, Lee Sejin, Cha Eugene, Kim Raebin, I love youㅠㅠㅠ TeSTAR forever]

He was not there.

Not in articles, not on social media, not in communities, not on video sites.

Everyone ignored Park Moondae's existence as if by agreement.

Keundal lowered his smartphone, his face deathly white.

He felt like he was going crazy.

"This is too weird... He's in the videos, and all the official records are there! But everyone's acting like they're under some kind of hypnosis..."

Even though Park Moondae's activity information still appeared in searches, everyone acted as if they could not see it.

Keundal fell into a complete panic.

However, Cheongryeo looked at Park Moondae on the sofa instead of glancing at Keundal.

"......"

He had to think of another way.

[......]

[Um, what should I look at now...]

"It's fine."

Cheongryeo hung up the phone again.

Then he opened his mouth, his voice devoid of emotion.

"Why didn't you stop him."

"...Excuse me?"

Keundal realized Cheongryeo was speaking to him.

"W-What do you mean, I didn't stop him."

"You were clearly wary of your junior using the System. This time, you were quite..."

Cheongryeo looked at Keundal without any expression.

"Cooperative, and passive."

"...!"

Keundal flared up for a moment.

"If you're going to say that, you were the one trying to help him in the first place! You've been preparing for a while. That's why you regularly, the System..."

Keundal's words trailed off. Cheongryeo asked back coolly.

"The System?"

Keundal realized.

"He used the System, and swapped bodies..."

The more he repeated those words, the stranger they sounded.

For Park Moondae, who loved control so much, to so easily use an untrustworthy System to swap bodies.

Was that a conclusion one could reach after a brief thought?

"Hyung... Hyung was also really wary of the System, but he started using it at some point. Slowly, becoming less wary..."

"......"

It was the same for him.

He... at some point, he had stopped trying to stop his hyung.

[Okay. Hyung! But if anything feels weird, you have to tell me...]

The memory echoed in his head.

"......"

Keundal's complexion turned a ghastly shade.

*From what point were we influenced?*

It felt almost like a horror movie.

When he turned his head, he could see Park Moondae, still frozen silently on the sofa.

"Moondae-hyung... what in the world is he going through?"

Restart Cheongryeo did not even deign to reply. It meant 'don't talk nonsense'.

"More than that, the trait is important."

He shifted his gaze to his own trait.

Appraisal.

Surprisingly, this guy seemed extremely satisfied with his trait.

"As I said. The combination is important."

You got it.

Congratulations on having the most advantageous trait for creating an idol group.

[By any chance, do you want a different trait?]

The special reward that Keundal had once provided me.

The trait-drawing roulette machine.

I knew he would not listen.

The atmosphere was slightly different. He seemed faintly amused by my reaction.

*Is this fun for you?*

*Is this the result of all the nonsensical small talk I've been making?*

Of course, there was no sense of accomplishment. I held back a sigh.

*I'd really like to know why I have to keep doing this in this state.*

Being stuck here without any hints was suffocating.

If it were a status abnormality that had occurred to me, I would have at least noticed it.

*...I'll have to dig deeper into the System.*

I thought about trying to enter the deeper parts of the System where I had not yet created a terminal. I had refrained so far because the resistance was so strong I was afraid I would be bounced back out, but I could not hold back any longer.

It was then.

Hm?

"Who were you?"

So this question is finally coming out.

Now that a bit of familiarity had formed, it seemed he finally felt like interrogating me.

[I wasn't in a profession where it was good to drink and spout useless stories.]

Though not in this much detail.

"Was it a member I used about... ten times? He maintained his composure well during incidents, so I tried telling him as a test."

"It wasn't a pleasant ending. Of course, I told him right before a restart, so there was no loss."

Hmm.
