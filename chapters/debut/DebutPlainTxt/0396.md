# Chapter 396 - The Wise Finals, II

It took quite a while to calm down the VTIC guy who wanted to drop out.

The production staff almost came running in with a camera, probably thinking he wanted to leave because he couldn't handle the pressure. Fortunately, we managed to calm him down before that.

*That could've gone really badly.*

And I called in Cha Eugene, Ryu Chungwoo, and... Cheongryeo to help subdue this guy.

It couldn't be helped. As annoying and rude as he was, he was still the leader of VTIC, and a situation like this required his involvement.

"......"

"......"

And in the narrow vocal practice room, five adults were crammed together, creating crushing silence.

*Fuck...*

I didn't know what to think, seeing even Ryu Chungwoo, who should've been figuring out how to win votes from the other trainees, standing there with a nervous smile on his face.

*From now on, if the intermediate reward is a random package, I will ignore it.*

*This damn system and its mass-produced mobile game gacha nonsense.*

I massaged my brow and opened my mouth.

"So... Sunbaenim, we are all in a similar situation right now."

I looked at Judan, who was staring into space with a completely distraught face, and spoke with conviction.

I skipped the unnecessary details and went straight to the main points.

– *We've been dragged into some strange parallel world, and we're trying to escape.*

I wondered if anyone would believe something like this without going into detail, but it couldn't be helped.

To someone who knew absolutely nothing, starting with something like, *I'm not actually Park Moondae...* would really make me sound like a crazy person.

*First of all, I'll take it slowly and answer his questions as they come.*

There was no other way.

At the time, Cheongryeo was smiling and not bothering to intervene, so I had no choice but to put on a one-man show.

"Wait a second."

Finally, Judan raised his hand.

"Let me ask you a question."

"Yes?"

"What is 32 times 6?"

"...192?"

"Yes."

He went around asking each of us a math question. It was just multiplication, so after a little thought, everyone answered correctly.

*What on earth is this guy doing?*

After turning around and getting an answer from Cheongryeo as well, the guy nodded.

"So it's not a dream. Hmm."

We stared at him.

"......"

"I have a general idea of what the situation is."

"...??"

"It's a concept that anyone familiar with the conventions of those subcultures can easily understand. I never thought I would experience it firsthand."

He muttered that with a blank expression and rubbed his chin.

"No, there's still a chance it's a dream... Anyway, I'm going to take a moment to wash my face. I have to check whether cold water actually feels cold."

The guy shot to his feet and headed to the bathroom.

*Drurruk-tak.*

The door slid open and shut.

All of this happened within 10 seconds of Judan starting to talk to himself.

"......"

I answered with difficulty, starting to get a sense of what was going on.

*...By numbers.*

$p
**[???]**

*Because logic is poor in dreams, it's usually difficult to calculate quickly and accurately.*

$p
**[Ah...]**
p$

But normally, people would just pinch their cheeks instead of going to all that trouble.

This bastard... Was he originally like this?

"He's always been interested in unique concepts, so I see he hasn't changed. He's trying to adapt to the situation."

Cheongryeo lowered his voice and whispered, conscious of the camera that was probably being installed in a corner somewhere beyond the door.

"He's your member. Why are you talking about him like you're in different groups?"

"Well, I haven't used him in a while."

The guy grinned softly, lowering his voice even more so that only I could hear.

"He might seem obedient, but he can be quietly uncooperative at times... It was a hassle having to take that into account when handling him."

*His choice of words was fantastic.*

"In any case, he's not impossible to deal with, so he won't introduce any unpredictable variables."

"......"

He came to this conclusion casually, but that made it even more strange.

*Doesn't this guy feel anything?*

One of his members had gotten his memories back. I'd expected some kind of reaction, like finding it amusing or wanting to kill me. He was very strategic after all.

The guy tapped his chin with a finger, then raised his voice so the others could hear and moved on to the next matter.

"I'll need to work through the details with him, but, hmm, we'll have to send those two away first."

"...!"

Cheongryeo smiled and turned his head. He was implicitly referring to Cha Eugene and Ryu Chungwoo, and they probably knew it. However, Cha Eugene did not blink an eye.

"I'll stay where I want to stay."

"Then we'll leave instead and you can stay here. Let's go to the practice room next door."

"Wait a minute."

It seemed these guys quarreled every time they talked.

However, before I could mediate, Ryu Chungwoo intervened with an awkward smile.

"Eugene. Let's go out first. Since they're in the same group, they probably want to talk privately."

Cha Eugene had no objection to that explanation.

He shrugged his shoulders.

"OK. You two sunbaenims can talk, then."

But he added something else.

"But let's go together, Moondae-hyung!"

*No, thanks. That was a bit much.*

"...It's better for me to help explain, since I've been doing this for so long."

"Moondae-hyung, are you a VTIC fan?"

*You think that's the reason?*

"I'm going to charge them an explanation fee later."

"Wow!"

*Go now.*

I let Ryu Chungwoo lead Cha Eugene away. However, Ryu Chungwoo looked back as he was leaving.

"Ah, Moondae."

"Yes?"

"Don't worry too much about getting votes for me. If I get eliminated, that probably means I wouldn't have been much help to the team."

"...!"

"And I have no intention of letting that happen."

He finished gently but firmly.

"I'll earn my own votes."

"......"

Since he'd even called me Park Moondae, he was saying this as the leader of our group.

*If that's the case...*

"Alright."

I nodded obediently. Ryu Chungwoo smiled and left the vocal practice room, dragging Cha Eugene, who had a sour face, along with him.

*Drurruk-tak.*

The door slid shut.

"......"

"You're really going to charge us an explanation fee?"

"Do you think I should?"

"Haha."

I resisted the urge to slap myself in the face.

Fortunately, Judan came back before I gave in to the urge to flee the room.

Water was dripping from his hair.

"It seems to be real."

"......"

The guy muttered.

"Am I crazy, or is this a dream... In the worst-case scenario, I thought I might have been caught up in some kind of supernatural phenomenon, like a ghost story. Who knew that last one would be closest to the truth?"

*Wait a minute.*

"Is that really why you wanted to drop out?"

"Yes, people are supposed to assume the worst and act accordingly."

"...Ah, yes."

*This... No, never mind.* Anyway, now that he'd accepted that this was reality, it would be easier to explain the next part.

I got to the point.

"We're doing everything we can to return to our reality, and we hope you'll cooperate with our plans."

This was where things got difficult.

If this guy saw the situation as another opportunity for himself, I'd have quite a headache on my hands...

"Of course."

"...?"

*That was fast.*

"When I was young, I dreamed of living out a fantasy like this, but now that I've grown into an adult with no dreams or hopes, I want to go back as soon as possible."

The guy continued speaking with a blank expression.

"I can't give up on the life of a *daesang*-winning idol with ten years of experience, a building, a hefty bank account, and fame."

"......"

I barely opened my mouth.

"But, sunbaenim, you still have to serve in the military."

"I'll have to go to the military here as well."

That... was true.

"But here, I'd have to start over from scratch, with no money or status, and go through all the hard work from debut all over again. It's obvious which situation is better."

The guy nodded.

"Adults don't really mean it when they say they want to go back to the past. Once you've built a foundation, most people don't want to go through all that trouble again. It's just a convenient thing to say."

I almost looked back at Cheongryeo.

"Anyway, this is a type of game, and Moondae Hubaenim is the key player."

"It seems like that's the case for now."

"Yes, thank you for your hard work so far."

The guy nodded his head expressionlessly.

"Then I... I must be a character who appears late in the story. I won't have much screen time, but I think I'll be a supporting character who provides quite useful assistance."

*What was this crazy guy saying?*

"Should I say I'm glad I'm not the antagonist...? Oh, sorry. I've been binging novels, movies and other videos lately because I have nothing to do."

*That sounds like an excuse.*

However, given the situation, it was better to keep him feeling stable, so I played along.

"Those are hobbies that idols can enjoy without much pressure."

"That's right."

The guy nodded, looking somewhat satisfied, and opened his mouth as if he wanted to say more, but soon returned to his businesslike tone.

"Then I guess I should vote for Ryu Chungwoo Hubaenim—no, I should call him Ryu Chungwoo-hyung now. Anyway, that's all I can do at the moment, right?"

Just as I was about to agree, another voice cut in.

Soft and cold.

"Not really."

"...!"

There was a hint of politeness in Judan's voice as he flinched.

"Hyung."

"You aren't as skilled as you were in the real world, are you?"

The VTIC guy glanced cautiously at Cheongryeo and nodded quietly.

"No..."

"Then you have to recover your skills first. No matter how good a plan we make, we won't get anywhere if our performances are subpar."

The guy with a faint smile looked at Judan and continued talking.

"Even if, as you said, your memories and self as an idol of ten years have returned, you can't claim any of that as yours if you can't back it up with your skills."

"...!"

"I hope you'll show the people who willingly gave you wealth and honor that their investment was worth it. Do you understand?"

"...Yes."

The guy who'd been saying shamelessly weird things suddenly stood at attention. He didn't seem to have any intention of resisting or talking back.

*It looks like Cheongryeo has been whipping him into shape for a while. He's got him completely molded.*

He must have been tightening and relaxing his control whenever he saw fit.

"Good. Still, I'm glad you're here now. You know that, right?"

"Yes... Thank you."

Judan was able to leave the vocal practice room only after receiving a full training schedule disguised as advice on his practice routine from Cheongryeo.

"......"

*He's really good at this.*

It looked like he'd developed a knack for controlling people over the course of his many resets.

He was still the same.

I looked at Cheongryeo, who was smiling and crossing his arms.

"First of all, I tried to steer things in the most efficient direction. It's turning out pretty well."

"I think it would have been better for his mental health if you had been more soothing."

"It's okay. He's the type that only moves when he's pressured to. On the contrary, if you give him too much wiggle room, he'll try to get smart and run wild."

The guy answered calmly.

"If you poke at him in the right way, sometimes he comes up with good ideas for albums. He'll be useful to the team."

"...Okay."

Since he'd worked with Judan for so long, he probably knew the guy and his strengths better than I did. I didn't bother to argue.

The guy cupped his chin in his hand.

"We need to revise our post-debut plans a bit more. Well, there's never a dull moment."

*Wait.*

"What about the person you're going to eliminate?"

*You haven't forgotten that's the priority, have you?*

But he tilted his head to the side.

"Hmm? I've already taken care of that. The elimination will happen during the finals."

*What?*

"Wow, Hubaenim, you haven't even started yet? Or are you going to trust yourself and see how it goes, like you said before?"

"I can take care of it."

"Haha."

He didn't provoke me anymore. I uncrossed my arms and opened my mouth.

There was something I originally wanted to say.

About Judan getting his memories back.

"...What happened just now was completely random."

"Yes."

What?

"But as we become more famous as idols and I continue to look for companions, I might be able to return the other VTIC members' memories to them."

"Hmm."

Cheongryeo narrowed his eyes.

"We'll have to think about that. It seems like we have more cards in our hand than we expected, but that doesn't necessarily mean it's a good thing."

"Okay."

*If you say so.*

I decided not to ask further.

It was then.

"Still, it's my first time."

"...!"

The guy lowered his gaze and muttered nonchalantly.

"So this is what it's like."

"......"

I asked.

"Are you upset?"

"Not particularly, but... It's been a while since I imagined something like this happening. It's unfamiliar."

The guy didn't explain any further. His gaze was unfocused, his face expressionless. As if he was vaguely recalling a time when he'd looked forward to or imagined "a situation like this."

*...Okay.*

I guess this had affected him in some way.

I sat on the floor for a while without bothering to ask about his condition.

And only after hearing the staff's voice announcing the start of filming did I slowly get up and leave the practice room.

He never brought up the topic again, but after some thought, I went back on my decision to never accept another reward package. I'd wait and see.

And time passed again.

As if Judan's unexpected situation had never happened, the filming site was still in a state of strange tension. The cameras were already running.

*I'll wait.*

I waited until the day's filming was over and the cameras ran out of batteries again, giving me an opening. By then, it was midnight.

And among the guys who were busy practicing until dawn just before the finals, I found someone who was conflicted about whether or not to go to bed.

"Judan-sunbaenim."

Judan got up from the practice room floor and muttered quietly.

"We should avoid anything that might raise suspicion. Let's act like we usually do and speak comfortably, like before."

*Well, if that's what he wants.*

I leaned in and asked.

"Woodan-ah. Who among the participants are you close with?"

"...Well, I don't have anyone I'm particularly close to. The trainees I used to practice with couldn't participate."

*Got it.*

*Well then, different question.*

"Do you know roughly who's close to whom, or who dislikes whom?"

"I know as much as everyone else knows."

*That was enough.*

I needed information from an existing trainee at the agency.

And I needed someone undercover who could go around talking naturally.

"Can I get some help?"

The guy was silent for a moment, seemingly deep in thought, then suddenly answered.

"I'm not good at persuading others."

It seemed that he perceived it as a request to at least join Ryu Chungwoo's election campaign.

I smiled and sat down comfortably.

"I don't mean to ask for that."

"Then?"

"All you have to do is talk to me. In front of the others."

There was a limit to how many votes Ryu Chungwoo could get.

If the trainees had to choose anonymously between someone they'd already trained and grown close to and a new face who was a strong competitor, the former was the obvious choice.

They probably wouldn't bother voting for Ryu Chungwoo unless there was some kind of incentive.

So what should we do? I had a plan for that.

*All I have to do is scatter the votes.*

I was going to influence which other participants the trainees voted for, apart from Ryu Chungwoo.

"It won't be anything unpleasant. There's no need to curse anyone. Rather, we'll be giving some compliments."

As Ryu Chungwoo had requested, I'd let him earn his own votes.

I wasn't trying to make them vote for Ryu Chungwoo. I was just trying to steer them away from voting for the others.
