# Chapter 612

Silence filled the elevator.

A stiff Cha Eugene moved only his eyes, scanning the manual I held. A voice could still be heard from outside.

[I'm Intern Kim Raebin.]

Click.

A sound then echoed in the elevator, as if someone could no longer hold back. It was not the sound of the doors opening.

It was Bae Sejin clicking his pen, frantically scribbling on an A4 sheet he had hurriedly brought. He then rolled a handful of pens onto the floor with his other hand.

*Here!*

The moment they saw this, everyone picked up a pen as if by agreement and bent over their papers.

[Should we just ignore him?]

[Is that really Kim Raebin? We have to check.]

[No, how?]

[What if we ignore him and he opens the door and comes in?]

[Seeing as the manual said not to welcome or react, it seems like he'll disappear if we just stay still.]

[Should we try pressing another floor?]

At Ryu Chungwoo's last words, everyone's eyes turned to the buttons.

And they saw it.

*...That!*

The button for the 4th floor was lit up, as if someone had pressed it.

Even though no one had.

"......"

An eerie silence once again filled the elevator.

I raised a hand, signaling the others to calm down, and carefully moved my feet.

[Assistant Manager?]

I ignored Bae Sejin trembling behind me and stood before the elevator's control panel.

"......"

"......"

After pressing our destination...

[4]

I turned off the light for the 4th floor.

And then.

WHIRRRR-.

The elevator started moving again as if by magic.

"Hoo."

The elevator lights returned to normal. The members wiped away cold sweat and sighed in relief.

Just as the announcement 'Going down' played.

[Ah, are you going to the basement? I understand.]

"......"

[I will use the stairs.]

The elevator indicator went down.

3.

2.

1.

"Run."

[This is the first basement floor.]

The moment the elevator doors opened, we burst out and ran at full speed.

"To the side...!"

I turned my feet according to the voice shouting directions from the map.

"There it is!"

Past a dimly lit, old concrete corridor, a grayish-brown fire door came into view at the very end. A tablet PC was attached to the door.

[Would you like to register the by-product?]

"Yes! Yes!"

I slammed the 'Yes' button.

A small slot opened at the bottom of the fire door with a creaking sound, like a mailbox or a package locker.

CREEEAK!

It was pitch black inside.

*Are we supposed to put the by-product in here?*

"Department Head Ahyeon, the music box!"

"Here you go...!"

Seon Ahyeon quickly handed over the music box he had been clutching. He then took out Kim Raebin's employee ID card, but...

"If we throw away Kim Raebin's, we can't ride the elevator!"

"Ah!"

For that logical reason, we only put the music box in for now.

Of course, it was uncertain whether logic would even work in this crazy variety show.

Fortunately, a few seconds later, the tablet PC calmly displayed some text.

[Registration Complete]

However, there was no other reaction.

Silence.

"...Is this it?"

The fire door that took the music box did not open. Ryu Chungwoo suggested that Lee Sejin try pulling on it, but the door was firmly locked.

"......"

"......"

"What about us getting off work?"

"I guess they want us to work overtime."

A few powerless, joke-like words were exchanged for a moment.

Flicker.

Far away, at the opposite end, the light of the emergency exit began to flash.

A sound was heard again.

KNOCK, KNOCK, KNOCK.

[Assistant Manager? Are you there?]

"......"

"...Looks like our friend from the 4th floor came down."

The sound of knocking on the emergency exit door echoed through the corridor.

Lee Sejin muttered with a hollow laugh.

"Was putting the employee ID in with it the right answer after all?"

I looked down at the tablet PC again.

[Time until next delivery

[ 00:24:56[]

...A new line of text had appeared at the bottom.

It was the same question I had seen before.

[Would you like to make a delivery?]

The emergency exit light flickered more and more erratically. It was as if the music box monster we saw earlier, not Kim Raebin, was lurking behind it.

However, everyone knew what happened when we pressed 'delivery' here. Forgetting their fear, they hesitated.

Lee Sejin placed a hand on my shoulder.

"...Let's do it."

"......"

"There's no other way."

"Is there really, really no other way...?"

No one could answer Seon Ahyeon's question.

In the end, I sighed and asked.

"Should we vote on it?"

No one could object.

And the result.

"We'll make the delivery."

"......"

I pressed the button.

[Delivery Complete]

As the screen changed, both the flickering emergency exit light and the knocking on the door stopped.

However, everyone knew from past experience.

This was not the end.

[Commencing requisition of employee for delivery.]

The lights went out again, and a red siren blared.

"......"

The members formed a circle and held hands, as if they were about to win a grand prize. The same happened next to me.

I did not pull away and just stood still.

[Assistant Manager Lee Sejin. Your duties for today have ended.]

[Please check the by-product.]

When the lights came back on, my hand was empty.

***

"Everyone was really serious back then."

We knew it was for a shoot, but the shock of having a member disappear one by one in a creepy situation was greater than expected.

In the middle of the night at TeSTAR's dorm, Ryu Chungwoo watched Park Moondae nod silently from across the dining table.

His face, as serious as it was back then, seemed to be recalling that time.

Ryu Chungwoo suppressed a laugh that was about to escape and continued his own recollection.

Of those slightly spooky moments.

"After that, the members kept coming to find us."

"......"

"The members who had disappeared, I mean."

***

We first returned to the 7th floor to look for clues.

Of course, during that process, we again experienced the phenomenon of hearing the voice of a suddenly vanished member.

This time, it was not Kim Raebin.

[Supervisor.]

[Is everyone okay? This is Assistant Manager Lee Sejin.]

This time, it was not even from behind a door.

It was right behind us.

"......"

Everyone froze at the vivid voice. Without daring to look back, we boarded the elevator and went up to the 7th floor.

[Hey!]

"It felt strange hearing Lee Sejin talk like that..."

"......"

"No, Assistant Manager Lee Sejin."

I nodded my head as I listened to Bae Sejin.

In the now dark and gloomy 7th floor, I summarized our findings so far.

Not that there was much.

"The sleeping room and the pantry doors are locked."

"Both are related to the manual, though."

[The head office operates on a flexible work system and does not have end-of-work notifications. Please recommend the sleeping room to any employee who reacts to a notification.]

[Only permanent employees may enter the pantry.]

Recalling those items, I unconsciously leaned my body against the sleeping room door.

The moment my hand naturally touched the door, I felt a texture different from what I expected.

*...! This is.*

A cold, smooth surface.

*A screen.*

When I turned my head, a small screen, half-hidden by my body, activated as it met the skin of my fingers and lit up.

A tablet PC was attached to the sleeping room door.

I immediately read the contents.

[List of Employees for Delivery]

[Kim Raebin]

[Lee Sejin]

[]

At the very end, a cursor was blinking.

As if telling me to input something.

"......"

*Wait a minute. This is...*

*Are they letting me choose?*

"Assistant Manager Moondae-hyung, did you find something?"

"Assistant Manager Park?"

However, before I could answer properly, the lights started flickering again.

"...!"

CLATTER, CLATTER, CLATTER!

"That thing is back!"

"Aaaargh! Seriously!"

Handprints started appearing again on the office window we had come out of.

Now accustomed to the mood-setting, the members jumped and screamed.

"This won't do! Let's just not make the delivery this time. Let's just see what happens!"

"Yep! That's right! You're right!"

"U-ungh!"

Bae Sejin was now speaking informally even to Department Head Seon Ahyeon, but no one cared and just nodded in agreement.

However...

*I don't think they'll let that happen.*

And sure enough.

The office window where the handprints were appearing finally shattered.

[!!!!]

With a loud shattering sound, another old music box fell to the floor.

From within it, limbs stretching out like a spider's suddenly shot out and crawled toward us like a zombie...

"AHHHH!"

"Ack!"

"Run!"

"No."

However, I stopped the members.

"Why!"

"Moondae, can't you move your feet? Get on my back!"

*That's not it.*

I gently sidestepped Ryu Chungwoo's support and thought quickly.

*Alright, let's look at what's happened so far.*

*No matter how I think about it, this seems to be based on a horror movie motif.*

*It's a structure where things are unlocked as members are slowly eliminated. So it's inevitably designed for only a few to survive, figure out the truth, and see the ending.*

*So...*

*If I have a choice, shouldn't I use it?*

I quickly looked over the faces.

Bae Sejin, Seon Ahyeon, Cha Eugene, Ryu Chungwoo.

Two have high ranks, and two are physically strong.

*Since the setting has an effect, the guys with high ranks will be useful somewhere. There must be a reason they're eliminating the assistant managers and interns first, and anyway, I'm... yeah, let's admit it. I'm not exactly strong against ghosts. So.*

"......"

I touched the blank space at the bottom of the list displayed on the sleeping room door and activated the keyboard.

"Assistant Manager Moondae-hyung?"

"Park Moondae, what are you doing?"

*I'm writing that name.*

[Park Moondae.]

At that moment.

The siren blared again.

[Commencing requisition of employee for delivery.]

"It's okay."

*I don't know if they heard me, but anyway... I thought this was the right direction.*

I patted the back of the member next to me, whose face I could not see, and quietly closed my eyes.

[Assistant Manager Park Moondae. Your duties for today have ended.]

[Please check the by-product.]

And so, I... was delivered.

***

In the silence.

"Hyung, are you all right?"

I opened my eyes.

The first thing I saw was Kim Raebin, his eyes shining.

He looked very... neat.

"......"

*What the.*

"Assistant Manager Moondae is here~! Welcome!"

Lee Sejin, who popped out from the side and hugged me, also looked well. No.

"Why are you guys perfectly fine?"

*I thought they'd be in ghost makeup or something.*

"Assistant Manager Moondae is fine too~ Ah, but you're not an assistant manager anymore!"

*What was that supposed to mean?*

"......"

I was about to turn my gaze to where Lee Sejin was pointing, but I saw my surroundings first.

*What the hell.*

It looked like a neat company lounge, but like something out of a spy movie, a giant hologram panel was floating on one wall.

[Appointed as Permanent Employee]

Park Moondae A 12:33:52

[Congratulations!]

"......"

*What is this.*

"No, that 'employee requisition' thing, it turns out it was about recruiting one more employee to be in charge of deliveries?"

"......"

"And it seems the employees who handle the deliveries are the permanent employees!"

"Right, so it's basically a promotion~ Wow, to think we were all contract workers and not permanent employees."

*Not permanent employees?*

At that moment, I remembered.

...That line from the manual.

[11. Only permanent employees may enter the pantry.]

"......"

*So that's why it was locked. I thought it was about the intern, but no... it meant none of us could enter.*

"But why do we suddenly disappear?"

"Maybe it's because we're secret agents? Like the National Intelligence Service."

"......"

While my head was still reeling, the text on the hologram changed.

[Your duty is the maintenance of the quarantine facility.]

[Capture the creature in the music-box-type control device and deliver it to the KIS Foundation.]

"......"

*A foundation?*

"So, we've been doing all this just to become permanent employees of some pseudo-religious foundation..."

"Hey, pseudo-religious is a scary word! You should call it a secret foundation! Secret agents!"

"That's right! They said it's a job to protect the world by safeguarding dangerous objects inside this company!"

"......"

*Let's just move on.*

Meanwhile, Kim Raebin muttered in a slightly dejected tone.

"I wanted to try persuading them first, but it seems communication wasn't smooth because information disclosure is restricted to non-permanent personnel..."

I held back a groan as I watched him cheerfully say that he had still tried his best to appeal, saying things like 'I can use the stairs, which is forbidden in the manual'.

"......"

"Anyway, if we deliver all the music boxes... to that foundation, we can go home, right?"

"Yes!"

And in the process, all the members would naturally be converted to permanent employees.

*Good grief.*

[Facilities Team / Park Moondae]

[Begin Work]

I let out a hollow laugh as I looked at the display on the electronic board.

***

"......"

After the 'delivery' was complete, the remaining members gathered in the dark and gloomy 7th-floor corridor.

"Moondae..."

I heard Seon Ahyeon's gloomy mutter of my name, as if he had forgotten he was supposed to call me 'Assistant Manager'.

All that remained now were Department Head Seon Ahyeon, Section Chief Bae Sejin, Supervisor Cha Eugene, and Intern Ryu Chungwoo.

[KIS Company]

Assistant Manager Park Moondae / Music Box

[Keep it Safe]

*They were probably looking at a message like that on the tablet PC, right? Just like I had done right before.*

With half the members retired like in a horror movie, it was a situation where gloom was likely growing as much as fear.

*Hmm. The kids must be getting exhausted. It's about time to speed things up. Let's see.*

*Don't disclose information for permanent employees, and call out the rank of the next person to be delivered. Alright.*

KNOCK, KNOCK.

First, I knocked quietly on the door.

From inside the locked pantry, I knocked toward the 7th-floor corridor where the undelivered members were huddled together.

And I said.

"Supervisor."

"......"

At that moment, a strange sound, like a stifled scream, came out.

Then, from outside the door, the noise suddenly died down as if a mouse had been caught.

*...Are they covering their mouths?*

"I know you're in there."

Then came the clattering sound of the members tumbling around. It seemed like they were trying to run away.

*...No.*

I spoke as gently and non-threateningly as possible.

"Can we talk for just a few words? Just for a little bit."

A deep silence.

And a hushed voice whispered.

[Don't answer!]

"......"

*The soundproofing is so bad I can hear everything. You guys.*

And so, my one-day experience as a part-time ghost began.
