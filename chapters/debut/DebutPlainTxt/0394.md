# Chapter 394

Lee Sejin knew that he was living a successful life.

He became a trainee at a large agency, overcoming a competition rate of hundreds to one, and survived fiercely within it, ultimately debuting.

And among the dozens of idol groups that debuted that year, they received the best results and even received the Rookie of the Year award, and were on track.

*Objectively... It's a success.*

Of course, he thought he would be happy. A prime time filled with a sense of accomplishment.

But it was said that if your standing position changed, the scenery you saw also changed.

*That's funny.*

It was amazing how he could feel anxious and frustrated at the same time every day.

His team members were addicted to their own mannerisms, the company's new plan was in vain, and his opinions got blocked and disappeared.

Being unable to do anything even when he saw something to fix made him feel helpless.

- Hey Lee Sejin, can't you do it in moderation? Can't you read the mood right now? I'm so fucking tired.

-... Oh, that's right, I'm really tired as well... But if we do it now, tomorrow will be easier. Let's go just once~

He knew that reality and dreams were different. His head, which had been pounding, had now cooled down.

Still, sometimes he felt like things were unfair for no reason.

When moving between schedules, preparing an album, and getting a quick nap in the early morning...

*...*

The feeling of being deceived came suddenly and subsided in frustration.

That might have been why. The words of this survival contestant, whom he had just met, pierced his heart.

-There's more to your success somewhere that you haven't noticed.

Effort and achievement. Restoration of that cycle. It may not have been exactly those words, but it sounded like that.

*What am I expecting?*

It could have just been a trivial psychological test floating around somewhere on social media. No, it probably was.

Lee Sejin guessed that he brought it up to vent because the atmosphere suddenly became strange while they were watching the concert.

Nevertheless, he rushed forward with a straight face. He couldn't stand it.

-Let's try it.

As if some button had been pressed.

*Why is he doing this, really?*

Still, he had no intention of withdrawing. Lee Sejin closed his mouth and looked at Ryu Gunwoo in front of him.

"......"

Instead, he stretched out his hand.

*... ?*

And then, in the air, he made a strange movement as if he was pressing a button...

-... !!

"Ah."

At that moment, a feeling exploded like a firecracker in his head.

It felt like a stream of water that was blocked by a dam had been opened.

Things that were originally there were rushing back into place.

-Thank you again for stopping me from getting off.

-Oh, our group is so good?

-TeStar will all renew their contracts. Everyone believes it~?

Memories and emotions filled his head. Emotions that were once taken for granted, countless memories created at turning points in life, pain, excitement, feelings of inferiority, reward, and victory.

Achievements.

His own place.

-I wish the Seoul Concert could last just one more day.

-That's right! Let's do it!

-Stop talking about things that are realistically impossible and making everyone feel burdened...

-Y-yes... H-how do you feel about it, Moondae?

-... I don't regret it.

-So everyone feels the same way? The company is at fault~ It was too short~

At that moment, Lee Sejin realized the cause of the helplessness he felt.

*Because I lived my dreams already.*

Yes.

He has already lived a life where his efforts were rewarded with achievement.

Realizing that even if he hit rock bottom, he could recover and try again, and realizing that that final challenge may succeed...

A life where he knew what it felt like to live with trustworthy and respectable people and work passionately.

"......"

The dizzying outpour ended. Lee Sejin raised his head. He could see his friend's face quietly examining his condition as if concerned.

A real teammate he hadn't seen in a long time.

*The results of my efforts are different.*

How could he talk in such an unsuitable way as if he was subtly trying to trick someone?

"I see."

It was as he said.

The achievements that Lee Sejin worked hard to achieve were elsewhere.

"Somewhere else... There was."

And it was like that from the beginning.

"Ah, Moondaemoondae, how do you always say the right things?"

"...!"

Lee Sejin laughed out loud.

The feeling of seeing Ryu Gunwoo, or rather Park Moondae, widening his eyes as if he was surprised that he recognized him couldn't have been more pleasant.

What preceded the confusion and curiosity about an incomprehensible, surreal situation was...

It was a cool relief.
