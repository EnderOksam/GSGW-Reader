# Chapter 603

"Then the opening game is... Unison!"

"Ugh."

This joint agency variety show initially followed a very common MT structure. It involved creating team slogans and simple choreography, answering quizzes, and giving out rewards and penalties. The activities were meant to strengthen the still-awkward team identity and sense of belonging.

Of course, some teams did not need such things from the start.

"I really think our Sunflower Team is going to win now!"

"That's the spirit, Chaeyul! We all made a good choice picking yellow, right?"

"Yes!"

"It looks like our team is all of one mind!"

*Yeah. You guys were like that even before we started this.*

I looked around at my teammates' faces. They all beamed, even though we were in last place. We were the 'happy fools' team that seems to exist in every one of these games.

*Me... a fool.*

"Moondae, you think so too, right!"

"Yes. I'm very happy."

I just gave them a double thumbs-up.

"Pfft!"

That bastard Lee Sejin, standing over there with a blue sash, saw my state and burst out laughing.

*Don't laugh. Just look at your own team.*

"Um, although we're all from different groups, I'll do my best for the blue team's victory today."

Cheongryeo smiled and neatly struck a 'fighting' pose. An energetic response poured in as if on cue.

"I respect you, sunbaenim!"

"This is what you call telepathy~ Blue Bae Team, fighting!"

"......"

*Those guys... lies just spill out whenever they open their mouths.*

I watched the blue team smile warmly while asking their leader, Bae Sejin, for a word. Then I glanced back at my own team, who were just... smiling.

"Hehe!"

"......"

*Isn't the balance completely screwed?*

For reference, those guys on the blue team came in second in this opening game. They tenaciously answered everything correctly, but the moment they saw a chance for some variety show entertainment, they could not resist and deliberately got it wrong.

Thanks to them, the Green Team smoothly took first place.

"Haha."

I just smiled and watched Cheongryeo, who had quietly stepped back behind his teammates.

*It's not funny. You're boring.*

Cheongryeo, quietly grinning while taking care of others or yielding to them, seemed to have absolutely no desire for variety show screen time. He was not creating any good angles for the editors either.

*Hmm. Why is he making choices like that?*

However, the first main game began right away, leaving little time for investigation.

"This game is... Psychic Tag!"

"It's a game where you literally use psychic powers to chase each other! You'll alternate between offense and defense."

Psychic Tag, which seemed inspired by a mobile battle game, had a moderately embarrassing and fun structure. When the person with the power shouted a slogan and gave a signal, everyone else tacitly pretended to be affected by that power.

That was right. Only the shameless could survive.

"I am... ❬The Flying Puppy❭!"

"Moondae, point your hand to the sky!"

...Like this.

I dramatically raised my hand toward the empty air. All sorts of guys followed my gesture, looked up at the sky, and started a Hollywood-level performance.

"Ohhhhh, a puppy is flying in the sky?"

"I can't move my body because of this amazing sight!"

"Hmm. This is a ❬Freeze❭ ability that affects both allies and enemies."

"I-I see...!"

*...I did wonder if they really needed to go that far, though.*

I watched the guys make a huge fuss, remaining silent for a moment while wearing a puppy-ear headband.

[ㅋㅋㅋㅋLook at his expressionㅋㅋㅋㅋ]

[Park Moondae was so shameless, saying he's used to being a puppy, but he's breaking down hereㅋㅋㅋ]

[I guess he didn't expect them to react this muchㅋㅋㅋㅋ]

[Aigoo, Moondae, are you flustered because people are reacting so well?ㅜㅜ]

Based on my years of experience, I had a rough idea of what I would be hearing.

*Anyway, thanks to that, I won.*

I froze the Red Team's last runner, Cha Eugene, with my psychic power and then narrowly tagged him.

"Oh~ The ❬Sunflower❭ Yellow Team wins!"

Cha Eugene shook my hand with a grin and whispered to me. "If this were real tag, you would've lost to me, hyung."

"I know. You lost, Eugene of the red team."

"......"

"Bbooi bbooi bbooi~"

With Ha Jintae's jeers in the background, Cha Eugene looked at me with a shocked face. He then disappeared with his cheeks puffed out in a pout. Next to Cha Eugene, Judan made his pout even bigger by analyzing, 'Your reluctance to use your psychic power was the decisive cause of your defeat.'

"In this game, the most powerful thing isn't physical ability, but psychic power. If you assume you have a psychic power, you should use it proactively without hesitation..."

*Well, you grow up strong too.*

However, I did not expect to witness something even more shocking in another team's match.

"Alright, next up... a match between the ❬Safety First❭ Green Team and the ❬Blue Bae❭ Blue Team!"

This was where Cheongryeo made his move. The guy used his psychic power without hesitation.

"Whoa, whoa?"

"Wait a minute."

At the tense moment just before his teammate was about to be caught, Cheongryeo ran in front of the attacking team. He swiftly pulled something out and held it before their eyes, then coolly made a gun shape with his other hand and aimed.

And finally, the slogan!

"❬There will be a brief inspection❭."

The police badge Cheongryeo held glinted in the sunlight.

However.

"Huh?"

"The badge... you don't have to show it."

"Ah."

"Aigoo, Cheongryeo."

It was an unnecessary action!

"...Anyway, psychic power used, safe!"

At the referee's declaration, Ryu Chungwoo of the Green Team, who was forced to sit down for three seconds, smiled and let the Blue Team's Spacer member go.

"Waaaah!"

"Cheongryeo! Good!"

"Thank you. Ah, but I guess this wasn't it..."

Cheongryeo lowered the police badge he was holding and looked slightly embarrassed, as if he had inadvertently raised it in his concentration and was now a little shy about it.

"Hahaha!"

"Cheongryeo is cute~"

The T-Holic guys started spouting some ridiculous nonsense. A guy who could pull off even more outrageous things shamelessly without a change in expression was putting on such a detestable act.

"...!"

*I see. I realized.*

*Did you get it wrong on purpose?*

This was that bastard's aim. Let's think about it. Why do idols on variety shows try to be funny?

*For buzz and screen time.*

But what if you already have both? This joint MT variety show had already secured buzz with its insane cast combination. The biggest pillar of that was VTIC. It was the same for screen time.

*What crazy production team wouldn't give VTIC screen time?*

They would squeeze out and create screen time even if there was none. In that case, how to create that screen time was entirely up to VTIC.

And what Cheongryeo chose was...

[Their concept is strong, but they're surprisingly clumsy?ㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋThe guys are so innocentㅋㅋㅋ]

[I like that they're boring]

[Boring-TIC is what the people demand!!!]

*You guys can be the funny ones. We'll take the favorable image.*

Since they were making a comeback with a strong concept, they were trying to gently melt the resistance of those who dislike dark songs. They even wanted to create a 'they're such hard-working young men~' kind of feeling.

"......"

My eyebrow twitched.

*Do you really believe that... such a move to just snatch the good stuff for yourself will work properly? That's a delusion.*

I would show him that in the end, the one with the most impact takes it all.

"The next game is ❬Hide-and-Seek❭!"

***

"Hoo."

After one round of Psychic Tag ended, Park Minha of the Green Team wiped her forehead and plopped down on the ground.

Then, a concerned question was heard. "Are you alright? Would you like some water..."

"I'm fine! Hahaha!"

Park Minha shot up, breathless, and hastily grabbed some water for herself.

*Survival! I have to stay sharp!*

Although Kim Raebin's attitude was merely one of respect(?) for a teammate and junior, she did not let her guard down.

*Anything can look plausible after editing.*

Even if the production team did not do it, the world was full of Cyber 'wreckers'. The hell of dating rumors... No way. That was why she had even sent her very easygoing and friendly Yulgi multiple warnings and a Q&A of anticipated questions.

"Sua!"

"Uwaaah, Yulgi!"

...She was already sticking close to her SoftSweet sunbaenim, naturally drawing all the attention in that direction. It was a relief.

*Hoooo.*

The de facto Leader of Mirynay, Park Minha, let out a long sigh of relief and sat down. It was a good opportunity, but it was just as dangerous, so she would survive until the very end without ever letting her guard down!

*I have to make a mark, too!*

Park Minha's eyes sparkled as she stood up. Fortunately, the Green Team's performance was decent. Although they were eliminated just before, they had won first place in the opening team game, and the team atmosphere was good. This meant it was not the kind of atmosphere where she could not join conversations because she was worried about what her seniors thought.

*So, I need to secure my own screen time. If I just keep doing well like this, it'll be fine!*

"Alright~ They say the next game is starting!"

"Yes!"

Hearing Lee Sejin, who had been scouted as an impromptu MC by T-Holic, shout, she swallowed hard. The game that followed was hide-and-seek.

"There you are."

"Waaah!"

A huge, playground-like set had been erected in the backyard. It had a simple and cute format where one person in costume had to find all the hidden members of the opposing team. This time, it was the Green Team's turn to hide.

*G-Good. It seems like I can get screen time just by reacting well when I get caught!*

So, Park Minha hid quietly next to a pile of boxes.

How much time had passed?

TAP, TAP.

In the silence, someone touched her shoulder.

*Is it the production staff?*

Wondering if she was not showing up well on camera, she turned her head, and...

"Hello."

"Hyaaaaak!"

There was an axe! Ah, no, it was Park Moondae in a tracksuit holding an axe, looking at her, but he had pigtails!

"......"

As if noticing her gaze, Park Moondae touched his own hair and muttered, "I got a really scary prop... so my teammates suggested I neutralize it."

"......"

*It's cute... but it's even scarier! How on earth did you get here without making a sound!*

Moreover, a wind-up key was attached to Park Moondae's back. The game would end due to a time-out if that key stopped. Just as she was wondering how he could move so stealthily with that thing on, Park Minha realized that Park Moondae was not wearing shoes, but fuzzy socks.

"......"

*This is what a person who's truly serious about the game looks like.*

"Yep. Found you."

"...Yes."

In any case, she was eliminated just like that, leaving behind a great reaction.

*...I'll get some screen time, at least...*

Trudging back to the bench, she joined the group of people who had already been eliminated.

"Welcome!"

*Please don't welcome me...*

Park Minha bowed her head deeply to Kim Raebin in greeting, then watched the rest of the game.

However...

"...!"

"Only Chungwoo is left!"

The game had suddenly turned into a one-on-one match. Park Minha struggled to keep her narrowing eyes open and observed the situation. Surprisingly, Ryu Chungwoo was observing Park Moondae in return, subtly moving from place to place to stall for time. It did not seem like he would be caught easily.

*He's so... calm.*

Park Minha felt it anew. Come to think of it, Ryu Chungwoo had been like that throughout this whole MT. He never shied away from anything he was asked to do, and he was good at anything physical.

*Is that the posture of a Leader? I have to show that side of myself in the next game...!*

Park Minha resolved, but at that moment.

"......"

Park Moondae, having finally found a trace of Ryu Chungwoo's movement, was stealthily approaching!

*H-He's been found!*

How much time was left? She looked at the wind-up key on Park Moondae's back. It still turned steadily, and Ryu Chungwoo still had a calm expression, as if he had not noticed.

*No!*

Finally, the moment came. Park Moondae slipped behind Ryu Chungwoo, reached out his hand, and shouted.

"Found..."

At that moment, without even looking back, Ryu Chungwoo slid down under Park Moondae's axe. The move was to prevent Park Moondae from moving recklessly and getting hurt.

"...!"

With surprising speed, he got behind Park Moondae and grabbed his back. To be precise, he grabbed the wind-up key on Park Moondae's back and turned it in the forward direction.

"Ah."

That was right. Having manually stopped the wind-up key, Ryu Chungwoo grinned and said, "If the key stops, the time is up and it's over... right?"

"......"

It was a reverse-thinking move.

"Woooah!"

"Amazing!"

As people poured out, Lee Sejin saw a signal from the production staff and cheerfully shouted, "Ah~ The unstoppable Green Team! They rise to first place overall!"

"Ooh!"

Park Moondae, still in his pigtails, applauded solemnly. Seeing the yellow team members comforting Park Moondae with words like, 'It's okay!' and 'Moondae, you did really well!'...

*Wait, i-is this okay?*

Park Minha suddenly came to her senses.

*This... it's not about me winning! In the end, the TeSTAR sunbaenims took all the highlight clips!*

It ended up as the showdown of the century between Park Moondae and Ryu Chungwoo. In that case, rather than her, it would be more helpful for their group to give Yulgi a chance to shine in the final round.

"Could the Green Team really keep winning like this? Our Blue Team needs to step it up~"

"......"

"Don't forget the food truck prize for first place!"

*Still, first place is great!*

Park Minha decided to just enjoy it for now!

A few hours later, the first day of filming for the MT finally came to a close.

"Does TeSTAR eat like this every day?"

"I'm jealous."

"As expected, the most important position on a team is the cook."

I easily won the ❬Dinner King Challenge❭ and distributed the well-boiled suyuk and hand-cut kalguksu to my teammates. The production team did not bother stopping us from sharing food with other teams. Thanks to that, the first day of the MT ended on an even warmer and more comfortable note.

*They know how to control the pace.*

And now, it was the middle of the night. During this time, when filming was temporarily halted for camera data replacement and maintenance, people scattered about, talking quietly amongst themselves. The bright commotion from earlier had vanished as if it were a lie.

I was no exception.

"Moondae, there you are!"

I stood corrected. There was also someone who was exactly the same as before.

I watched Jin Chaeyul sit down next to me with a smile. I wondered what he was about to say.
