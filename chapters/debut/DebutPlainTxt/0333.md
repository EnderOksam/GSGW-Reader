# Chapter 333

Lee Sejin also speculated about the 'secret' of Park Moondae in his own way.

And some of them were unrealistic, but he thought they made a lot of sense.

*Is he Park Moondae's cousin?*

In fact, Park Moondae, whom he knew, was older than him, but it was speculated that he was using his dongsaeng's identity due to the situation.

Of course, when he recalled the reason, the rationale was so fuzzy it was absurd, so he quit right away.

*Because he said he'd tell me.*

While listening to those words, he accurately picked out Park Moondae's sense of incongruity and recognized it.

Still, because he had been guessing for a while, there was even a time when a North Korean spy suddenly came up in his reasoning. 

Amnesia, overdoing it to produce results, and even controlling public opinion fit perfectly.

Of course, he immediately laughed and passed it on.

*It's not a drama, so why would he be a spy?*

But now, Park Moondae said something more unrealistic than that.

*– I am not Park Moondae. I've returned to the past.*

It was a remark worthy of a pseudo-religious leader.

He broke out in a cold sweat.

*What?*

A dream... Weren't dreams usually like this?

Lee Sejin unknowingly turned his hand behind him and pinched his thigh.

*Ah, damn it.*

...It hurt.

Meanwhile, Park Moondae continued to speak.

"...I was originally a civil servant named Ryu Gunwoo."

"......"

"The video call you saw yesterday was with that body, and the original Park Moondae is in there now."

Lee Sejin tried to answer calmly.

"Ah... Really?"

Why did he say 'really'? He was about to freeze to death.

He had to think properly and react, but this was just what came out of his mouth.

Lee Sejin stood still and tried to figure out what Park Moondae meant...

"Yes. I can make a video call again right now if you want. Shall we check?"

He was going crazy.

"...Hmm."

Lee Sejin was confused. Now he was speechless.

At that moment, Park Moondae, who was looking at him in a daze, smiled bitterly.

"You don't believe me."

"...!"

"Well... I thought so."

Park Moondae said as if it was not a big deal.

"That's why I've been putting off talking until now, and there's no particular visible evidence. It is all testimony."

But the body language of the other person said otherwise.

His friend was in a cold sweat.

*Ah...*

Lee Sejin was completely dizzy. Contradictory as it was, at the same time, he woke up as if he had been electrocuted or iced.

Was Park Moondae, the person he knew, the kind of person who would say something that would not work in this situation?

*No.*

Absolutely not.

There was no way the guy who performed concerts without hesitation, even with cracked ribs, and the guy who kept up his schedule even though he couldn't sleep as if his dreams were disturbed would do something like that.

He had never seen anyone who lived as fiercely as Park Moondae.

And if he wasn't mistaken, his friend was sincere now.

"I knew you would suspect it was a mental illness. But..."

"No."

"......"

Lee Sejin moved his legs and walked towards him.

Then, with the table in between, he sat across from Park Moondae.

"I will listen, so speak slowly."

"...!"

Then, after a brief surprise, a faint expression passed over Park Moondae's face.

He smiled.

It was a brief relief.

"Is that so?"

And Park Moondae's words became a little more natural.

"Then... To start with, it was a few months before Idol Co started when I woke up as Park Moondae."

From then on, the story started to unfold from an unexpected perspective on a story that Lee Sejin knew well.

The circumstances of Park Moondae, who participated and was successful.

Of course, some stories were hard to understand.

"If I didn't become an idol, I would die."

"Mmm."

He said that some kind of instruction floated in the air, but Lee Sejin kept his mouth shut because the symptoms of psychosis really came to mind.

*That is... Is it schizophrenia?*

Of course, Lee Sejin was not interested in this field and had no intention of looking into it, so I couldn't be sure, but fortunately, he was able to soften the impression soon.

This was because Park Moondae did not appear to be suffering from delusions of persecution or going back and forth.

More than anything, he didn't want to question Park Moondae right now. He thought that he shouldn't.

So instead, he just listened.

With a light welcome.

"Okay."

"Hm, that's right."

He even made a joke like this.

"Then, is that why you became close with Ahyeon? Was he doing well in the future?"

Although, of course, there were hidden meanings.

At that time, Park Moondae looked at him with a slightly comfortable expression and smiled.

"I had no intention of becoming friends, but somehow we ended up being tied up together."

Park Moondae said that some of the debutants he had heard of as a public official weren't Lee Sejin or Seon Ahyeon.

"Ah."

He didn't intend to do it in this situation, but it was a little pleasant.

In the end, whatever the truth, it meant that it was really Park Moondae's pure kindness that solved the photoshopped picture of his cigarette.

*It's funny, really.*

Even while listening to these unrealistic stories, he was immersed in them and argued about these things.

In the meantime, the story of Park Moondae passed his debut and entered the TeStar activity period.

And among them, there were contents that were so unrealistic that they seemed to fit one empty spot in a puzzle.

"He's the guy who came back to the past before me."

"...!"

VTIC's Cheongryeo.

"...Cheongryeo."

"I guess sunbaenim didn't want to be stuck with me."

"What kind of sunbaenim is he? Really..."

After all, he was a real madman.

Park Moondae only informed him that Cheongryeo had returned to the past in moderation. And he drew himself to the point of intimidation to know about the future, but Lee Sejin read between the lines.

The nonsense of Cheongryeo.

*... Isn't he a psychopath?*

If Park Moondae did not properly inform him of his future knowledge, he might have really tried to kill him out of anger.

Goosebumps ran up his spine.

However, Park Moondae's attitude was bizarrely gentle.

"Don't worry, he's managed to get his act together."

It wasn't a simple 'reporting it, we'll lose too, so let's move on'.

Park Moondae's unique generosity in close human relationships.

*...They shared the same experience.*

Lee Sejin cut it short and suppressed his rising antipathy. 

In fact, even now, his sense of reality had long since exceeded the permissible limit, so it was time to move on instead of arguing. 

Besides, the next bomb was waiting.

"And speaking of Chungwoo-hyung... He is my relative."

"...!"

"We're in the same Pungsan Ryu clan. It was closer than I thought."

As if putting another puzzle together, the situation became clearer.

Why Park Moondae was reluctant towards Ryu Chungwoo, and after becoming roommates recently, they quickly became close.

And when the two of them went out.

"...We went out to meet Ryu Gunwoo."

"......"

Park Moondae did not bother to mention the accidents involving his parents and Ryu Chungwoo, but the structure was sufficiently completed with just that.

*So, Chungwoo-hyung...*

Oddly enough, has his attitude toward Park Moondae changed recently?

All circumstances fit. In the end, Lee Sejin emotionally acknowledged Park Moondae's excuse that he was 'caught'.

In addition, Park Moondae also took care of the explanation for the next person.

"Bae Sejin-hyung knew during Chuseok. I was at hyung's house. He doesn't know the details at all."

"......"

Having gone there, Lee Sejin began to feel even a slight sense of shame.

*It wasn't anything special, but...*

The explanation of Park Moondae he had heard so far was unrealistic enough to make him doubt his ears, but there was a neatness to it that came from careful thought. 

Lee Sejin could easily picture how nervous he must have been to tell him this nonsense.

*I shouldn't have done that.*

He murmured frankly.

"...I'm sorry. I rushed you."

Park Moondae laughed.

"You deserve it. When else would I get to tell you?"

Even after that, Park Moondae's experience was organized through his mouth.

In fact, Park Moondae omitted daydreaming in his coma and some truth-checking that didn't need to be said, but the content was still extensive.

Park Moondae, who briefly returned to Ryu Gunwoo's body.

*How is it?*

*I don't think a movie would sound like this either.*

"I really believe in what Moondae says~"
