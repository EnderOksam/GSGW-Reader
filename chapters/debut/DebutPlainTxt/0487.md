# Chapter 487

TeSTAR were trapped in their old dorm. After opening the inner door, they finally reached the front door, where a panel glowed.

[Sacrifice one job to open the front door.]

The moment I read it, I knew someone would be eliminated here.

*Is this a survival format?*

Did that mean we had to vote among ourselves to choose one job? It was a thrillingly provocative idea. I nodded and revised my evaluation of Ryu Seorin upward once again.

*Wait, but if we vote while we're separated like this...*

We didn't know who had which job, and communication with the other side was impossible. Would they really jump straight to a boring vote, without the entertainment of us talking, deceiving, and making each other laugh?

*There's no way...*

Ah!

I turned and shouted.

"Hyung!"

"...! What?"

"The walkie-talkie!"

I also yelled toward the outside of the front door so the other side could hear.

"Did you find the walkie-talkie? The walkie-talkie!"

[A walkie... talkie? ...dae?!]

[...Yeah!]

I faintly heard them scrambling around on the other side.

"...! Should I use the walkie-talkie now? I just press this and talk, right?!"

"Yes, but keep it short since you can't hear their response while you're pressing it!"

"Got it!" Bae Sejin grabbed the walkie-talkie and started shouting.

*If we're lucky, they might hear the transmission from the other side and find the walkie-talkie's location...*

Did that mean we were supposed to communicate via walkie-talkie and discuss which job to sacrifice?

Just as I thought that, the panel on the front door suddenly updated.

[☜☞]

[Sacrifice Selection Complete!]

What?

"Huh?"

"Moondae? D-Did you happen to make a choice?"

"Of course not."

Before I could say another word, the door opened.

CLICK.

"......"

The front door was open. Before us stood the other members, panting as if they had just run over with a walkie-talkie. It was the other four members of TeSTAR.

Their eyes widened.

"......"

"Moondae Moondae! Raebin! Hyung!"

"E-Everyone, thank goodness...!"

In that moment, we forgot the game and huddled together in relief, like a flock of penguins. It was chaotic, like a family reunion after being stranded.

"Hyung! We came to save you!"

"Yeah." I half-heartedly accepted Cha Eugene's hug and counted our heads again.

*All seven of us are here.*

Right. Having more people definitely brought a sense of stability.

However.

*Why did the door open?*

The most likely possibility was that they had made the choice.

"Did you guys happen to choose the job to be sacrificed?"

"Nooope! The door just opened all of a sudden!"

"...!"

Ryu Chungwoo also shook his head firmly. "We thought you guys chose. We figured since we were the team trapped in the dorm, we didn't have the right to choose..."

What?

"W-What are you talking about? We were the ones trapped in the dorm!"

"...? Sejin, what are you..."

No way.

I pushed through the gap between the four of them. With my view clear, I looked outside the front door to the other side.

I was horrified.

*This is crazy.*

The other side was also the dorm.

"Wait, outside the front door is..."

"H-Here, too, is the dorm...?"

The members who noticed whipped their heads back and forth in confusion, with the front door between them.

"Holy moly! There's a living room!"

"Wow~ This is insane, for real!"

Before I knew it, Cha Eugene and Lee Sejin had run all the way to the living room we had just escaped from and were shouting.

I also stepped past the inner door on the other side.

"......"

"I-It's really, exactly the same..."

Oh.

*So, they were also trying to escape?*

We had both mistaken the other for the team outside, but in reality, we were Dorm A Team and Dorm B Team.

*What in the world is this place?*

Was it a set? It had to be. It wasn't just the props decorated like our old dorm. The entire house was a replica.

"Hah."

We had been thoroughly blindsided. I smirked and surveyed my surroundings again.

I knew there would be an escape method at the very end. There had to be for the catharsis.

However, the front door itself was a fake, and the structure was a mirror image. It was a magnificent double-locked room that further immersed us in fear and tension.

*In that case, where is the escape route?*

No, before that.

I turned my head. The members flinched.

"First of all... then neither team touched anything on that panel, right?"

"Right."

I reached out and pointed at the panel, toward the sentence still clearly displayed.

[Sacrifice Selection Complete!]

"Then why does it say 'Selection Complete'?"

"......"

Lee Sejin furrowed his brow in thought, then crossed his arms. "Maybe it's part of a job's mission?"

"......"

"You know, one of the jobs might have the role of choosing the sacrifice. Hmm~ if you think about board games?"

That made sense. It was a sharp thought.

"Then should we all ask each other our jobs first?"

"Ah~ about that..."

Just as Lee Sejin was about to answer, a modulated, mechanical voice spoke.

[Participants.]

"Ugh!"

"What the..."

A voice now came from the front door panel.

*They packed a lot of functions into this thing.*

We immediately ran and stood before the panel. The screen changed and displayed a new sentence.

[The sacrificial job has been decided.]

[The mark of fate has now been bestowed.]

The white text flashed ominously on the black screen.

"It's already over...?"

"What's going to happen?"

The air grew chilly with confusion and tension.

However, I was tense for another reason.*Hey, didn't they say the game had a three-hour time limit? Then how are we supposed to fill the remaining time? Isn't this going to kill all the tension?*

Just then.

CLICK.

A part of the wall next to the front door opened, and something poured out.

"...!"

"......"

While a few of the guys made a fuss, I identified the objects.

*Cards?*

They looked like poker cards at a glance, and there were seven in total. Our respective names were written on the back of each one.

"What's this?"

"Is this a QR code?"

"Hold on." I tapped the other guys. The text on the panel wasn't finished yet.

[Among the 7 cards, there is one belonging to the participant with the job chosen as the sacrifice.]

An animation of seven cards flipping over played on the screen. One of them zoomed in, and a bright red mark was slapped onto it.

A ram's skull.

[That is the Sacrificial Token.]

"......"

*It's getting serious.*

[For 3 hours, you may exchange this 'Token Card'.]

[And at the end of the game, the character holding the 'Sacrifice's' Token Card will become the final sacrifice.]

On the screen, the cards spun face down like a shell game, then flipped over one by one to show their backs. The card with the bright red ram's skull mark blinked...

[POW!]

It exploded.

[02:59:59]

Only a black screen remained, with a white countdown blinking at the bottom.

"......"

I immediately re-examined my card. One side had a poker pattern and my name, and the other had a QR code engraved on it.

I tapped the frozen panel and asked, "How do we exchange them?"

There was no narration. A new phrase just appeared on the black screen.

[Please show the QR code.]

Hmm. I turned my card to the QR code side and looked at the area next to the front door panel.

*Do I scan it here?*

"Moondae Moondae?"

"Just a second."

I stopped a few of the freaked-out guys from grabbing my shoulder and scanned the card's QR code.

*And now?*

The screen changed again.

[Required Currency. 1 Gold]

"......"

Gold?

"This... I don't think we can just exchange them, can we?"

"No." It seemed there was a cost.

*Then again, being able to exchange them endlessly would be overkill.*

At that moment, Cha Eugene's hand shot up.

"I know!"

"...!"

"Wait a second!"

He dashed off, kicked open the bathroom door on his side, and came out with something.

"This is gold!"

It was a gold coin with a QR code on it.

"......"

"Wow~ where was it?"

"Under the shelf."

"Eugene, you're amazing...!"

The members gathered and showered Cha Eugene with praise for his sharp eyes. He grinned from ear to ear.

Then came the decisive remark.

"Hmm~ I bet there's more of this hidden in these two dorms, right?"

"Probably?"

If that was the case, I immediately pulled back the QR card I was scanning.

"Then should we look for gold first?"

And so, the treasure hunt began.

"Moondae Moondae~ Didn't you miss us members so much?"

"Yeah, I did."

Lee Sejin, who had fussed about how happy he was to see us, chattered next to me about what they had gone through. I silently turned over the living room on the side they had escaped from.

"Ah~ but I don't know if there's anything left here. We really tore this place apart."

Seeing they had even ripped up the space under the living room sofa, I could tell they had searched thoroughly. I dusted off my hands and stood up.

Come to think of it, there was something I was about to say to him.

"About what you were going to say earlier."

"Huh?"

"Did you guys not talk about your jobs?"

I meant their jobs.

*It wasn't mentioned even while he was rambling on about what they went through.*

At that, Lee Sejin shrugged. "Hmm~ that's what I was about to say. We were so busy searching that we didn't get to talk about our jobs at all, you know?"

"......"

Hmm, you?

*It wasn't intentional?*

The guy who is best at variety shows just skipped the introduction part the writer had specifically requested.

As if reading my mind, Lee Sejin shrugged again. "I thought it would look better on camera if we all did it together... hmm, but looking at it now, it seems like people will lie, right?"

"Probably."

Anyone who got a troublesome job would lie. I nodded in agreement.

*...This is a very him-like idea, though.*

The nature of this game made it difficult to trust anyone completely. I just shrugged inwardly and ended the conversation.

As I was on my way to the kitchen, Bae Sejin suddenly approached me and spoke in a very small voice.

"...Park Moondae."

"You really didn't get a weird job, right? Are you sure?"

"Yes."

This was unusual.

However.

"But wouldn't someone who did get a weird job answer the same way?"

"That's true, but... you scanned your card right there just now. I feel like someone trying to hide their job would have been too anxious to do that."

"......"

Sharp.

"And you're good at this kind of stuff, so I'm taking a chance on you," Bae Sejin said, swallowing hard.

"My job, the 'Lawyer' one."

Right.

"It has a special ability."

"...What kind?"

"...'Can know the circumstances of other jobs.'"

"...!"

"That's what was written... so I knew what jobs there were." Bae Sejin lowered his voice even more.

"Raebin said he was a Reporter, right?"

"Yes."

He swallowed.

"There is no Reporter...!"

"...!"

"Among the jobs, there is no such job as a Reporter!"

It was a completely unexpected blow. I immediately reviewed Kim Raebin's attitude but hadn't seen the specific awkwardness of a guilty conscience.

*Is Kim Raebin that good at lying?*

Was that even possible, no matter how much he had trained through past variety shows?

*I'm starting to have some doubts about Bae Sejin, too.*

Without showing it, I widened my eyes in surprise, then quickly adopted a solemn expression as if my ears had perked up.

Bae Sejin's expression also became solemn.

"Hyung, first, could you tell me the jobs you know? I want to try and list some possibilities for Raebin's real job."

"Right, but before that... I want to hear your ability too."

He knew how to negotiate.

I nodded immediately. As Bae Sejin swallowed nervously, I revealed, "My ability was a question mark."

"...?"

No, I wasn't joking.

[Special Ability]

[?? (Revealed as the game progresses)]

That was what was written on my profile.

"I think they didn't tell me because it would be a spoiler for the game's progression."

"Ah."

Of course, I wouldn't gain his trust if I left it at that. I added my own speculation.

"If this is a Mafia-type game, the Doctor's role is usually to protect the victim."

"...!"

"So, I think they'll let me know around the time someone is about to become the sacrifice."

"...That makes sense."

Right. It seemed I hadn't lost his trust, as I was able to get the list of jobs from Bae Sejin.

*Doctor, Sheriff, Detective, Scholar, Mortician, Lawyer... and Jester, I guess.*

They were a group of modern-day professions. In any case, it was very plausible and useful information.

*This is a gain.*

If it was true, that is. I willingly bowed my head with a word of thanks, and Bae Sejin looked slightly proud.

"But this way, there's not much point in you trusting me with this... should I recruit one more trustworthy person?"

"Huh? Who..."

I raised my hand and pointed to one person. It was Ryu Chungwoo, who was diligently searching the kitchen of our dorm.

Bae Sejin became anxious. "...Hey, this has nothing to do with his usual personality, right? The job is what's important. This is a variety show."

"That's right."

*No, that's exactly why I'm saying this.*

I nodded in willing agreement.

"Think about it, hyung."

"Huh?"

"Last time, in our self-produced content, Chungwoo was the traitor, right? The zombie."

"R-Right."

"Then according to the Cooldown theory, it won't be Chungwoo this time."

"......"

"What do you think?" Wouldn't it be boring if the same guy got picked again?

My judgment was that even if it were a random draw, the production team would have pulled some strings to prevent that.

*If it happened, they probably would have claimed there was an error and made them draw again.*

My theory must have sounded plausible because Bae Sejin eventually clenched his fist and agreed.

"...Ahem, okay. Let's do it."

Right. I immediately sidled up to Ryu Chungwoo and whispered.

"Hyung."

"Hm?"

"Come over here for a second."

Without being noticed by the others, the three of us began to talk as if we were each searching a similar area.

"Sejin suggested an alliance, but he says it's only possible if we reveal our jobs."

"Ah."

"We've already introduced ours to each other."

The meaning was clear. If he just told us his, we would form a party right now.

However, Ryu Chungwoo, who was looking under the dining table, gave a small, troubled smile. "Um... I can't tell you. I'm sorry."

"...!"

Bae Sejin's expression changed. "Don't tell me you're..."

"Wait a minute, hyung." I quickly cut in before he could fly off the handle.

"It's not that you don't want to tell us, it's that you can't, right?"

"......"

Ryu Chungwoo gave a vague smile.

*Just as I thought.*

I nodded, then whispered to Bae Sejin. "I think Chungwoo has a job that prevents him from revealing it himself."

"...Ah!"

"If he were Mafia, he'd try to deceive us, not answer so honestly."

"Ugh..." Bae Sejin groaned but soon nodded.

"Then... he could be the Detective. In detective novels, the detective usually plays a big role at the end."

Oh. That was a deduction with a good sense of context for the content.

"So?"

"...Alright! Let's talk."

Bae Sejin nodded. I let Ryu Chungwoo listen to Bae Sejin's explanation.

Ryu Chungwoo was impressed. "So there was an ability like that. That's amazing, Sejin."

"Ahem, it's nothing."

"If what you say is true, Raebin is definitely suspicious... but the urgent thing is that someone is about to become the sacrifice. I want to talk more about that."

Hmm?

"...Right."

"Let's say one of us could directly choose the job to be sacrificed. What job do you think it would have been?"

That was easy. I answered without hesitation.

"Detective or Jester."
