# Chapter 453

For those who hadn't noticed the signs, everything happened too suddenly.

It couldn't be helped.

[That's T1 for you lol]

Even when T1 received a corrective order and Tnet got a warning from the Korea Communications Commission, the company never had a clean image. Most people just clicked their tongues and moved on.

It was a time when attempts to drag TeSTAR into the mess failed to gain traction. The general sentiment was simply, 'T1 is being T1 again,' just more frequently.

[Three T1 Stars Board Members Arrested for Stock Manipulation... "The True Face of T1"]

Then one day, this headline suddenly appeared in the news.

The article plastered the faces of TeSTAR's agency board members all over it.

[????]

[T1 you crazy bastards]

[Is this related to TeSTAR?]

[Ah fuck, here we go again]

At first, even the fans thought it was just another piece of bad news. It was the kind of trouble where the agency takes some heat and its artists get dragged into the controversy.

*They'll probably just replace the board members in a bit and pretend nothing happened.*

However, the articles kept coming.

[❬Stock Manipulation Controversy❭ Why Did T1 Create a Major Entertainment Company?]

[T1's Allegations... Prosecution: "We've Found Evidence of Collusion with the Head Office"]

The agency remained silent.

T1 also failed to issue any proper response articles.

By this point, even fans who knew nothing about the industry noticed something was wrong. An ominous premonition settled over them.

[They've turned off their fax and aren't answering their phones]

[What the hell is going on]

[What exactly is T1's situation right now?]

The situation was as quiet and anxious as the eye of a storm.

TeSTAR's ambitious comeback schedule ended provisionally after they barely finished two weeks of music show promotions.

They completed all previously set or announced schedules, but there were almost no new ones. Any unannounced schedules they must have had seemed to have been canceled.

At this point, most hardcore fans understood.

*The company is in such a state of emergency that it can't even afford to care for TeSTAR, its flagship group.*

[The Syeombyeol official account and W-App are both quiet]

[Ah, I'm so fucking pissed. The concept was great this time and the song did well on the charts because they didn't do any of that pointless otaku-pandering, but now they can't even promote]

[All the board members can go die]

[TeSTAR isn't directly under them anyway, they're under a label, so isn't it fine? Seems like they'll be okay if they just draw a clear line. It's not the members' fault.]

All sorts of talk circulated, but the prolonged silence only amplified the possibility that the situation was serious.

And then one day, an article appeared out of nowhere.

[Tnet's ❬Be My Girls❭ Fairness Controversy, Are the Insiders' Testimonies True? Prosecution: "Nothing to Say Yet."]

It was an article with a strange nuance about Tnet's latest hit survival show.

[Huh]

[Crazy, was Be My Girls rigged?]

[They say all the contestants were pre-cast, the auditions for regular people were just for show...]

[The PD and writers all made their SNS private, wow]

People turned their attention to the fresh new controversy.

Tnet was hit head-on by the growing suspicions and articles, unable to do anything. They canceled several upcoming programs and quietly scaled back their aggressively expanding 'Global K-Pop' business.

Amidst it all, the story about TeSTAR's agency became old news. Fans were just starting to feel a little relieved.

Faced with bombshell-like pressure and a broadcasting station on the verge of implosion, T1 Ent finally made a decision.

[T1 Ent Pushes for Subsidiary Independence, Agencies Begin to Stand on Their Own]

Another announcement came without warning. The entertainment agencies under T1 Ent were gaining their independence.

T1 Stars, home to TeSTAR, Mirynay, Spacer, and others, was no exception.

[Crazy, T1 Stars is going independent?]

[T1 is in so much trouble, it looks like they all agreed to go independent together]

[Wow, T1's making its own subsidiaries look for a way to cut ties. Their standards are amazing lololol]

Because the article strangely focused on the 'subsidiaries' active push,' and T1 Ent had been struggling so much lately, the public understood it that way.

The subsidiaries were escaping T1. It was an exodus.

Of course, deeper thinkers exchanged sharp insights like, 'Is T1 just pretending to cut ties to save its subsidiaries?' but that was the extent of it.

The important thing was that most people who heard the news assumed the subsidiaries would be happy.

[TeSTAR is a label anyway and they're already a top-tier group, so it's fine. The members self-produce, so the quality won't drop lol]

[This is actually better]

[Good riddance. The board is all gone now, right?ㅠㅠ The idols at the agency can probably promote with more peace of mind too]

The fandom's atmosphere leaned heavily toward this positive interpretation, especially on TeSTAR's side.

Hadn't they already made it big?

If they were a group that hadn't established themselves, it would be worrying, but TeSTAR no longer needed T1's halo. In fact, they were sick of T1 trying to bundle-sell them with every little thing.

Perhaps now was the right time to part ways, rather than getting further entangled and hearing nonsense about ❬Idol Inc.❭ being rigged.

That's right, we have nothing to lose!

[Let's go, ORBIT label independence~]

It was irreversible, something they couldn't interfere with. If so, they might as well manage the mood.

The fans quickly silenced any doubting opinions and deliberately steered public sentiment in a positive direction, dreaming of TeSTAR's hopeful future activities.

***

*We're screwed.*

I gritted my teeth.

Beside me, Lee Sejin scrolled through the articles with an unsmiling face.

"They abandoned us, right?"

"They did."

T1 had cut ties with its entertainment subsidiaries. It was a signal to the political world, a plea saying, 'You've hurt me this much, so please back off.'

It was a sign of surrender and negotiation.

And that meant these subsidiaries were now like kites with their strings cut.

*There's no way they'll support us after cutting us off.*

They would do it just because they wouldn't want to see us succeed after being cast aside. This was the end of this company benefiting from T1's platforms.

Ryu Chungwoo rubbed his chin, his brows furrowed with worry.

"Our contract renewal negotiations start next week."

"We did have a positive conversation a few months ago, but..."

"No way."

"No way!"

The two guys with the same name, who usually never agreed on anything, spoke at the same time and then stared at each other.

For the record, I completely agreed.

*We have to run.*

If we stayed on this sinking ship, our fate was obvious. We were already labeled as part of the T1 line, which made it hard to join other lines, and now we were an agency that T1 had disowned.

*It's over.*

No matter how famous we were, we wouldn't last long if we couldn't promote properly.

"We absolutely cannot renew our contract with this place."

"...!"

If anyone had suggested renewing, I was prepared to persuade them for three days and nights without sleep. I had plenty of time for that.

*Since all our schedules have been canceled anyway.*

From the label employees to our managers, no one could get in touch with the company, for fuck's sake.

I clenched the smartphone in my hand so tightly it felt like it would shatter. One or two of the more soft-hearted guys hesitated, flustered, before nodding.

"R-Right..."

"But... doesn't that mean we all have to sign with another agency together? Do we have to start looking for a suitable one now?"

Right. That was the problem.

Not only was time short, but it was also questionable if any agency could coordinate things to let TeSTAR continue promoting as we had been.

*We have to find a place that won't just turn us into cash cows by sending us on endless tours.*

I had a lot to say about creating a label with decent people only to be forced to leave that manpower behind, but the biggest problem was this.

"And... there's a high chance we won't be able to use the name TeSTAR."

"...!"

"Ah, ah... r-right."

TeSTAR was a group name from ❬Idol Inc.❭, and this agency held the trademark rights.

*The company bastards know this is their lifeline.*

Whether by convention, financially, or in emotional terms, they would never let it go easily.

"......"

The atmosphere in the silent room grew darker. It was impossible not to be attached to a group name we had used for five full years.

However, even in the midst of it, Cha Eugene just shrugged.

"TeSTAR is TeSTAR even if we change the name. I'm fine with it. We can make a cool new name! A fresh new start!"

"Ooh."

A few members nodded at his spirited words. Kim Raebin also agreed.

"Then that seems to be a clear advantage. Indeed, there are times when Cha Eugene is right, and it seems now is one of them!"

"I'm always right! Kim Raebin is mistaken that I'm wrong. So Kim Raebin is wrong."

"...?? ...! I retract my statement! Cha Eugene is only right this one time!"

"Haha!"

Thanks to the two who never changed, the mood lightened a bit. As expected of guys who had been through all sorts of things since before their debut, they were starting to manage their mental states brilliantly this time as well.

I let out a small laugh. Lee Sejin clapped his hands.

"That's right~ Our Eugene can come up with a cool English acronym for us!"

"Yep~ I have many ideas!"

"Ooh~"

Lee Sejin responded with a laugh, but in truth, I had seen that guy force a bitter smile when the group name was mentioned earlier.

That couldn't be helped either.

*Right. If at all possible...*

If at all possible, I wanted to take this group name with us. It was already a symbol. Brand value wasn't something you could easily build again, and countless cases showed a name change could alter a group's entire feel.

*In a business that sells image, it's a pretty big risk.*

I'd have to rack my brain a little harder.

I massaged my hands, calculating the possibilities.

"First, let's avoid the company's suspicion."

And so, we each started getting our assignments.

First, Ryu Chungwoo, as the group's representative, said some carefully planned words at the contract renewal meeting the company had barely managed to set up.

"Um, would it be alright if we discussed this a little more before drafting the contract again? It's quite chaotic right now."

He deliberately expressed his intentions vaguely to buy time. The goal was to make them misunderstand that we were scared because of the chaos at their head office.

[We'll renew, but just give us a little more time.]

We decided to go with that tone. It worked well because Ryu Chungwoo had such a trustworthy image.

Bae Sejin did a pretty good job using the time we'd earned to hire a lawyer, just in case.

Now we had to start seriously looking into other companies that might contact us, but the thing is...

*...The picture-perfect scenario is for them to come to us directly.*

Have you ever seen a hot free agent on the market go out and pitch themselves? The moment we bow our heads and approach them is a shortcut to worse contract terms.

Besides, if our current agency caught us running around looking for a new company, it could create unnecessary gossip.

However, almost no one was approaching TeSTAR, even though it was our contract renewal season.

*There's no contact from other companies.*

The problem was that it was a somewhat known fact in the industry that TeSTAR was going to renew. In fact, it had been treated as a near-certainty since we created the label, so almost no company was trying to contact TeSTAR.

*Even if there are, they're just second-rate.*

They were just the type to think they could hit the jackpot if they got lucky once, but no matter how desperate we were, we couldn't go to an agency on the level of Dream K, where Bae Sejin used to be.

I remembered Bae Sejin deleting chat messages from companies that were just testing the waters, looking like he was about to breathe fire.

Eventually, it got to the point where the guys sprawled out in the living room were saying things like this.

"Ah~ Should we just start our own company?"

"That... might actually be better..."

Seon Ahyeon sighed, a rare sight for him.

"Would it be impossible to just go independent with the people from our label... I'm starting to want to try it with just a different name."

"I believe that would be impossible..."

"Right..."

Ryu Chungwoo let out a low groan at Kim Raebin's words. This was also a rare sight.

*Everyone's exhausted.*

I held back a sigh and picked up my smartphone.

Right, if there's really no contact, then there's no choice. Since things have come to this, I should prepare to poke a decent place and gauge their reaction...

"......"

But there was a text message on my smartphone. It wasn't an ad, a friendly check-in, or a stalker.

It was a congratulatory message.

[VTIC's Shin Cheongryeo: Congratulations on your independence ^^]

This is absurd.

*This bastard... how long has he known?*

However, I paused when I read the next message.

[VTIC's Shin Cheongryeo: It looks like you'll need a new home.]

[VTIC's Shin Cheongryeo: What do you think?]

"......"

A new home?

*Don't tell me... is it LeTi.*

It was the first scouting offer.
