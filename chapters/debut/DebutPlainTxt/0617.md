*Damn it.*

*It's right to decide the group's stance.*

I thought that would be the only way the guy taking a break could leave with at least some peace of mind. Still, there was no easy answer.

"Well, if it were me, I'd rather hear something like, 'We're sad you're not here, your absence will be felt until you recover and come back'..."

Ultimately, it was a matter of what wouldn't completely shatter Ryu Chungwoo's spirit. He might be unable to stop his activities due to pressure, or he might be disheartened if we let him go so readily, thinking his role wasn't important.

It was a damn pain to decide. At a glance, every argument seemed to have a point.

It did seem like everyone agreed on one premise, though.

"...The treatment won't be easy."

"......"

Recovery would take a long time. Because of that, we had to be careful with him. The situation was so bad that even Cha Eugene and Kim Raebin, who had already talked to him, just buried their heads in the sofa with gloomy faces.

This was a sudden crisis we couldn't have imagined when planning our activities. For now, we focused on placating Ryu Chungwoo and getting him to receive treatment as peacefully and effectively as possible, but what would happen after that?

*My head will hurt figuring out the next steps...*

It was just one thing after another.

*...We prepared so much.*

*He must feel so shitty himself.*

"Um... Moondae."

"......"
