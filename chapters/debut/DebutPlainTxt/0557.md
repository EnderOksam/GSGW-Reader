# Chapter 557

[I found it. That actor's weakness.]

I declared.

In the agency's recording studio, Cheongryeo sat alone looking at lyrics. He lifted his head toward the pop-up I displayed.

He looked somewhat intrigued.

"That's faster than I expected."

I grinned. It was late May. Only twenty days had passed since the movie's promotional schedule started. Normally, that was an absurdly short time to find a complete stranger's weakness.

*But I got it.*

Watching Cheongryeo wait with his arms crossed, I continued typing in Help.

[He lied about his age. He's actually two years older.]

[And he's been going on illegal overseas gambling trips. He's contacting a broker using a separate, unregistered smartphone his agency doesn't know about.]

Since he was a criminal, I could spit out the information without hesitation.

Cheongryeo gave a slight, silent nod.

"The proof?"

[I confirmed it at a drinking party. He gets extremely loose-lipped when he's drunk.]

*It's a good thing I can hold my liquor.*

[Sunbae, are you okay?]

[Ah, uh...]

I drank moderately. When the mood was right and the actor stepped out to take a call from his manager, I discreetly checked his things.

*Thanks to that, I drank to my heart's content for a few days.*

Of course, this wasn't an effective way to find a weakness from scratch. Who would suddenly admit they were two years older while drinking? Evidence of illegal gambling wouldn't just pop out of nowhere. Even if the actor had slipped up, the chances of me noticing were slim.

So how was it possible?

I grinned.

*Because I was trying to prove something I already knew.*

It was the controversy that would blow up in this actor's future. That's why it was so easy. I already knew what would happen to this actor a few years down the line.

[❬Entertainment Tidbits❭ Hallyu Star A... Why Did He Lie About His Age?]

Since he was a big-name actor, every controversy went through the gossip rags before hitting mainstream media headlines.

*I saw it naturally since I was constantly monitoring public opinion.*

So, all I had to do was verify whether the scandals that would erupt around this actor in the future were true.

And this...

"Did you just verify information you already knew? If he's that famous of an actor, his current status would have been consistently reported even a few years later."

[......]

Right. This bastard could figure it out too. After a hundred restarts, he must have milked similar patterns for all they were worth.

My silence served as confirmation. A faint smirk twisted his lips.

"Even considering that, your verification was faster than I thought."

[Yeah. Thanks.]

I shut my mouth. Obviously, that actor was no fool. He didn't spill his secrets so easily that someone could figure them out after just a few drinking sessions.

*I just gently poked and prodded at the exact right moments to indirectly test the waters.*

For example, when the actor accidentally spilled a drink and carelessly put down his bag, I pretended to look for a napkin while confirming the burner smartphone inside.

Pulling this off quickly, quietly, and without suspicion requires an almost instinctual sense of timing.

...Which meant I used a bit of Ryu Chungwoo's blessing. That guy's special ability was amazing.

*I used a gold medalist's ability to spy on someone...*

I was just grateful that Ryu Chungwoo didn't smash my head in for using his blessing this way. I grimaced, recalling the pop-up that kept appearing. 'The shooter of the silent list smiles'. It was a misuse of his power, but I used it well.

[Anyway, I even told you about the burner phone, so you can use it as you see fit now.]

I had finished relaying the information. There was no need to ask what he needed it for. I would find out naturally while sharing this guy's vision anyway. This was the end of it.

"Zero."

Cheongryeo leaned back against his chair and picked up his smartphone. I hated the sight of him grinning like that.

"The lack of physical evidence is a demerit, but considering the time frame..."

Just then, the guy, who seemed about to say something infuriating, suddenly shut his mouth.

"......"

[What?]

I shared the vision of the smartphone screen he was looking at. It was an urgent message.

[Chaeyul: Hyung, please come to the dorm quickly. I think Heewoon snuck out.]

[Chaeyul: I think he went to see his girlfriend.]

"......"

Ah, damn it. The current lovebird of VTIC had gone AWOL.

***

VTIC's Heewoon. The bastard who had even set up a cozy love nest with his girlfriend. The guy who, probably for this very reason, was cut from VTIC in my reality, had suddenly disappeared without a word.

"Did you contact his parents' home?"

"They said he's not there, and there are no sightings on social media."

Cheongryeo immediately left the recording studio to meet his manager and began moving quickly.

But he went to see his girlfriend?

[He said he broke up with his girlfriend.]

"You can say anything with your mouth, can't you?"

A bastard who says one thing and does another behind your back... Hmm, a common type of person, I suppose. I nodded.

The manager, his face etched with anxiety, asked Cheongryeo, "What did Chaeyul say he texted her? Jaehyun, did you notice any signs or get any contact from him?"

"I didn't. I'll start looking now. Hyung, please start by adjusting the group's afternoon schedule."

"Right, right. Got it."

The manager nodded and hurriedly made a call on his smartphone. Although he and the rest of the LeTi staff seemed flustered, no one was making a huge scene by calling all over the place.

*Right. The search for a member who went AWOL has to be done with minimal personnel.*

A leak would be the most fatal blow. I knew that well. We went through the same thing back when STier's Cha Eugene was in that state. Worse, they had a photoshoot scheduled for 5 PM.

*...Hmm.*

This didn't feel like someone else's problem. I asked casually.

[Should I give it some thought too?]

Cheongryeo didn't refuse. I took that as a yes. Alright, let's see.

*First, it's daytime.*

In a situation like this, the lack of member sightings meant he either didn't use public transportation or used it for an extremely short time. It also meant he didn't walk on the streets for long.

*In that case, I can't ignore the possibility that he's near the dorm...*

Before that, let's consider the possibility that the answer is right under our noses.

[What are the chances he's at his girlfriend's house?]

"......"

Cheongryeo said firmly, checking a new message on his smartphone. It contained a report from an agency employee stating that the member's 'girlfriend' was also not at home and had gone out.

*They're fast.*

I watched as the staff who checked the CCTVs near the dorm replied to Cheongryeo, 'There's no sign of him taking a taxi,' and made my conclusion. As expected, the lovebird was near the dorm.

[How about we check all the room cafes and restaurants with private rooms around the dorm?]

"He has a record of doing that already."

Cheongryeo got into the car with a cold look in his eyes.

"He's not so simple-minded as to use a method that got him caught once before. We need to look elsewhere."

So we were stuck here.

*Elsewhere.*

The current VTIC lived in a high-end villa very close to the company. There was no way I would suddenly know the geography of this neighborhood inside and out. I thought for a moment, then something came to mind.

[Wait a minute.]

The logic was simple. If I don't know, I can just use someone who does.

[I know a guy who's tight-lipped and thinks outside the box. He's also familiar with the area around your company.]

"......"

Cheongryeo just shifted his gaze to look at the pop-up.

[He's with LeTi.]

Security problem solved.

"Tell me."

I placed my hands on the keyboard. After that quick and concise conversation, I temporarily disconnected from Cheongryeo and got up from my seat in the officetel.

The guy who had arrived early and was waiting for our 'regular strategy meeting' quickly spoke from across me.

"Is the communication over?"

"No."

I cracked my neck and asked, "Are you by any chance interested in deduction?"

Judan's eyes lit up. That's right.

***

"So, you're looking for a place where a small number of people can stay for more than half a day without being noticed, excluding commercial establishments."

Judan clasped his hands together after hearing the full explanation. He looked thoroughly intrigued.

"Yeah."

Honestly, I wasn't expecting any amazing cooperation from this guy, but it was true that he sometimes came up with good ideas...

"Then it's simple."

"Hmm?"

"Look at this."

Judan immediately held out his smartphone, showing a place he had searched for.

[BizLounge (Unmanned Venue Rental, Open Year-Round)]

"There's a seminar room rental company near the office, and it's easy to book on weekdays. The price is reasonable, and since it's unmanned, it doesn't leave a trace."

Judan spoke with a confident expression.

"And there are no CCTVs inside the seminar rooms, except for the entrance and the lounge."

"......"

"It's not a place people usually go to rest or hang out, so it's hard for the company to predict. It's not crowded, so it's quite pleasant. It's the perfect escape space."

I looked at the business marked on the map and matched it with all the given conditions. Near the company, no sightings, real-time booking availability... It was a surprisingly perfect match.

"It's a bit of a shame to reveal it like this. It's one of the places I frequent."

"......"

"It's a survival tactic one must naturally learn when living with dozens of peers."

Finding a comfortable place to hole up by yourself? However, now was not the time for such remarks. It was time for praise.

"Impressive."

"...! You're too kind."

Judan seemed moved.

*What a funny guy.*

No need to worry. I won't reveal only the specific place you visit.

*I have to check all the ones that fit the criteria.*

I swiped my smartphone screen and, besides the specific company Judan had chosen, found a few other similar candidates near the company. Then I contacted Cheongryeo again and sent a message.

[There are some seminar rooms near the company...]

"......"

And a short while later.

"Hyung! They found him!"

Amazingly, the lovebird was indeed discovered in a seminar room and quietly taken away. Although it wasn't the 'perfect' place Judan had recommended, he was on a date with his girlfriend in a nearby seminar room. Judan's idea was a success.

After confirming the news through Cheongryeo's vision, I returned to my own body in the officetel.

"......"

My vision returned. After checking my expression, Judan's face became slightly triumphant. He then shrugged, holding out a cup of coffee he must have made at some point.

"I take it I was right."

You bet.

"Please give my regards to Cheongryeo."

You bet. I downed the coffee the guy offered me.

***

After a cup of coffee, I reconnected to Cheongryeo's vision, and it was hell. I mean that literally. A bastard with hellish ideas was sticking his head into the jaws of hell.

The lovebird, his eyes bloodshot and veins popping on his neck, was wailing like a drunk.

"I fucking know you all blame me. Did you think I wouldn't know? The change in your attitude? Hey, do you have any idea how suffocating it is to be with people who resent you all day long?"

A guy who should have been kicked out was still sticking around, spouting this kind of nonsense.

"I was going to come back before the photoshoot anyway, but you couldn't fucking trust me and told the company everything, right? Wow, I really..."

"Hyung..."

"Guys. Go to your rooms for a bit."

However, Cheongryeo was unfazed. He calmly sent the other members to their rooms.

"What are you doing? Hey, what the fuck."

Seeing this, the lovebird's shoulders tensed as if he was nervous. He started acting even more aggressive to hide it.

Cheongryeo stared at the spectacle for a moment, then actually smiled and nodded.

"I know what you mean. Right. Rest as much as you want, hyung."

"...Wh-what?"

"Doesn't this mean you need a break?"

Cheongryeo checked his watch and muttered, "This album is just a small test run anyway... Hmm. It should be fine for you to have some time to yourself, hyung."

The lovebird swallowed hard.

"A-are you telling me to leave the group?"

"What are you talking about?"

Cheongryeo said gently, "You have to keep promoting too, hyung. Hmm... how about something like a drama."

"...A drama?"

"Yeah. It's convincing if we say you're temporarily sitting out of album promotions because you're so busy with acting. There are many cases like that among our seniors."

The lovebird looked up at Cheongryeo, seeming tempted. His head seemed to cool down quickly, as he wasn't the type who was used to being angry.

Just then, Cheongryeo slightly changed his tune, as if reeling in the bait.

"Of course, it's not my place to recommend it... I was just guessing if you had such thoughts. Do whatever makes you comfortable, hyung. The company won't be angry either."

The lovebird hastily grabbed the bait.

"...Yeah. That sounds good. I'll think about it. I'm sorry. The environment was just so high-pressure... I think I showed you guys a side of me I shouldn't have."

"We should all be careful with each other."

Cheongryeo replied softly. The lovebird, looking almost moved, quietly staggered toward his room.

Hmm. I watched Cheongryeo send a message to the company saying, 'We had a good talk.' Then I asked.

[If that guy skips group activities to act, isn't he finished?]

It was an act that would send the lovebird's already rock-bottom reputation within the VTIC fandom plummeting even further. Wouldn't this be enough to make fans send protest trucks to the agency?

"It would."

Cheongryeo smiled gently and closed his smartphone.

"That's why I'm telling him to do it."

[......]

I understood.

*So this is how you're pushing him, step by step, toward a 'natural' departure...*

He said he wouldn't restart on his own and would live normally, and he was really living up to it.

[Got it.]

However, a question arose at this point.

[Why don't you put Jung Woodan on the team? He seems fine to me.]

Instead of trying to make that bastard a permanent member, wouldn't Judan be better, even if he's a bit inconsistent?

[He was the one who came up with the seminar room idea.]

"......"

Cheongryeo headed for the front door to go back to the recording studio. He answered lightly.

"Yes, Jung Woodan. He occasionally comes up with brilliant ideas, and he appears to maintain his composure well in unpredictable situations."

Cheongryeo murmured quietly, "But not always."

[What do you mean?]

A simple answer came back.

"He didn't believe me."

*...?*

"I told you before. That I tried talking to someone about it as a test."

No way. I looked at Cheongryeo.

"About my situation."

His expression was placid. He seemed to be briefly recalling the situation back then.

"Hmm, he asked me again, I think."

[Can you prove it?]

"So I proved it to him."

Cheongryeo grinned.

"The next morning, he left a note and ran away from home."

"......"

"He didn't return to the dorm until I reset on the second night."

THUD.

Without looking back at the front door, he got on the elevator.

"If a factor evaluated as a strength is only expressed selectively, it's inefficient to use it further."

The underground parking lot. Cheongryeo walked toward his car without hesitation and finished his words gently.

"I trust statistics."

[......]

I pressed my fingers against my eyes.

*Is that what happened?*

The guy this bastard first confessed his regression to. That was Judan.

"......"

Hey, but hold on.
