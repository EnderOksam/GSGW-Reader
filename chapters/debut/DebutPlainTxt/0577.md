# Chapter 577

Around 11 PM.

"...He was rescued safely and will be hospitalized for about a day."

[Ah... That's a relief.]

I stood in the quiet, dark corridor outside the private hospital room and lowered my voice as I spoke on the phone.

[Are you okay?]

The person on the other end of the call was Lee Sejin. A report like this should have gone to our leader, Ryu Chungwoo, but I knew why the missed call came from this guy instead.

*...He must be handling the aftermath.*

Ryu Chungwoo was still talking with the company. The fact that the company had not contacted me yet made it almost certain. It made sense. A member had suddenly deserted a concert.

My grip on the smartphone tightened.

[Hyung, the concert...]

When I did not answer, I thought of Keundal. He had just asked me about the concert and now looked dazed as a doctor checked on him. Normally, I would have given him some plausible reason and told him not to worry. I could not do that now.

It was not that I deliberately refused to answer. I had no answer to give.

...Because I had not been able to check myself.

The smartphone was in my hand, but I could not bring myself to open a web browser. Not even now.

*...Right.*

Right. I had deliberately avoided it. I needed to think and reason properly without being shocked. However, the time had come to face the potentially unbearable results.

I asked Lee Sejin.

"...The concert."

[Of course, it ended well. Moondae Moondae, just don't worry for now and rest until tomorrow morning.]

He answered casually. However, Lee Sejin was the type who could respond calmly no matter what kind of hell had broken loose. Still, since we had another concert tomorrow, I expected a more realistic answer.

*To just brush it off without explaining the specific situation...*

This was troubling. I swallowed hard. I was just about to ask for the details.

[I-Is that Moondae on the phone?]

"...!"

A small voice came through the smartphone.

[It's Park Moondae?]

[Hey!]

Then, I heard exclamations and the sound of footsteps rushing over from all directions.

[Ah, they said he was rescued safely!]

[That's such a relief!]

[You went through a lot, Park Moondae, really...]

Relief and joy overflowed from the other side of the phone. They should have sounded more exhausted after finishing a concert in such a terrible situation because of me, but they only sounded overjoyed.

They must be doing it on purpose.

They were a strange bunch.

"......"

I swallowed my words, then spoke again.

"...Thank you, and I'm sorry."

"......"

"For making a choice that harmed the team."

Confused interjections followed, and then they quickly refuted my words in surprise.

[What are you sorry for! You did what you had to do!]

[The taxi, the, the members called it together...!]

[Hey, listen! That wasn't your choice. We talked about it together. Chungwoo-hyung said so, remember?]

Me being the only one to benefit? Society called that 'consideration', not 'together'. However, he had brought up a good point.

"Right. So... where is Chungwoo-hyung now?"

[Ah.]

At that moment, the reply paused amidst a strange atmosphere.

[Ah~ He's just talking with the company for a bit, why? Is there something you want me to pass on?]

Lee Sejin quickly answered brightly, but I had already realized my prediction was correct.

*As I thought, the company is in an uproar.*

Frankly, it was grounds for a lawsuit, so it was only natural. I pressed the bridge of my nose.

"Tell Chungwoo-hyung to just forward the call to me. I'll talk to the company directly."

[What?]

"I'd understand if the company is angry, but there's no reason for him to take the heat for it. I'm the one who caused this."

[Hey, wait a minute...]

[I! I have something to say!]

Kim Raebin cut off Lee Sejin. I gave him the floor. Immediately, a line that sounded prepared in advance came pouring out.

[Hyung, do you remember what you told me a few years ago when I was worried about having to miss a schedule due to my grandmother's health problems?]

"......"

I remembered.

[It's no problem to leave in the middle of work.]

I had said it was okay. Words like that.

[I would like to say the same words back to you, hyung.]

Kim Raebin spoke with a voice full of conviction.

[Going to save a family member in crisis is not a problematic act at all!]

[And since it's something that could happen to any member, I believe it's natural and right for us to rely on each other.]

"......"

I covered my face with my hands.

Thanks, but...

*No.*

That's different. Kim Raebin's grandmother is his legal guardian. It is a situation people can universally sympathize with. Anyone who would curse him for that would be a psychopath.

However.

*I'm... not family.*

Park Moondae has no family. That has been a publicly known fact since my debut. Anyone who has searched my name knows it. So... officially, I just recklessly took a taxi and left right before a concert because of an acquaintance's problem.

There is no room for extenuating circumstances here.

The people who took vacation days, canceled plans, booked accommodations, or received tickets as a special gift... Do you think those people, whose time and money turned into scrap paper, would listen to an excuse like, 'My friend got stranded. I was so scared and sad so I ditched the concert ㅠ'?

*Even I wouldn't be able to.*

My fingertips grew cold. However, refuting Kim Raebin by spouting all this right now would be the height of unsightliness. I could not do that to this innocent guy after I had already ruined the first day of the tour. I bit my tongue.

"...Yeah. Thanks."

[Not at all!]

Kim Raebin's voice brightened. The atmosphere on the other end of the call relaxed slightly, as if in relief. That was enough.

[Moondae-yah, we... really, we finished the concert safely. I'm not lying to reassure you. I don't think it was sloppy.]

"...I know."

That was why it stung even more. A concert we aimed to be insanely good had to settle for just struggling not to be ruined because of my desertion. Of course, this was not something I could say out loud either.

"I believe you."

[...Yeah.]

At that moment, a voice suddenly burst out from the other side.

[That's right, and Moondae-hyung, there's something you're completely mistaken about.]

"......"

Cha Eugene replied in a serious voice.

[There are more people who come to see me than you, hyung.]

"......"

[There aren't many audience members who only watch Moondae-hyung. Are you worried people will be disappointed? It's useless. Everyone liked me...]

[Cha Eugene!!]

[Kim Raebin used violence! Scold Kim Raebin!]

It was a truly familiar mess. In an instant, the density of the conversation drained away.

"......"

[Hyung, did you laugh? Are you feeling better?]

"......"

I had just heard a good reason why they did well without me. It would have been stranger not to let out a dry laugh when these guys were going this far to cheer up the bastard who deserted them.

"I'll get my head straight and be back tomorrow."

[Okay!]

After that, the conversation continued in a more relaxed atmosphere. There was even a suggestion like this.

[If you're okay with it, um, what do you think about us coming to the hospital too...?]

"No, it's fine. You have a concert tomorrow, too."

[Ahem, that's true, but.]

They had a concert tomorrow, so they should be resting. If the members came to visit now, Keundal would turn pale with guilt. Considering the physical and mental states of both sides, I cut off the suggestions that went too far. Still, the atmosphere was good.

After a few more refusals and adjustments, the call ended.

"Then I'll see you tomorrow morning."

[Alright!]

[Sleep well, Moondae-yah...!]

Click.

The kind words lingered in my ears. However, until the very end, there was no specific talk about the public opinion that must have been ruined by my desertion. They never mentioned how they explained it to the audience or what kind of reaction they got.

It was natural that those guys could not bring it up first. So I should have been the one to actively press the issue. No, before that... I should have checked beforehand and then started the conversation.

I could not.

"......"

I pressed my hands to my face.

If it comes to it, after this tour ends, I... I will have to suspend my activities. As a sign of self-reflection. It seemed the group's image would only recover if the guy involved in the controversy stepped away from TeSTAR's activities for a while.

*...I can't let this become a stain on our full-length album.*

If I just hole up during that time and do nothing but practice, public opinion will probably calm down a bit by the time the next album comes around. ...No, maybe not. Would suspending activities actually make public opinion worse?

I do not know.

"Ha."

I let out a bitter laugh. Until yesterday, no, until this morning, I never would have imagined having such absurd worries.

*Originally...*

Right now, I should have been ecstatic after finishing the first concert. A state where regret and satisfaction had been poured out to the limit. My body would be limp, but adrenaline would be surging. I should have been exchanging congratulations and feedback, quickly preparing for the next concert.

Yes, tomorrow.

*...There's a concert tomorrow, too.*

But right now, I...

"......"

Of course, Ryu Chungwoo's judgment was right. I was grateful. I knew it too. If I had stayed at the concert and Keundal had actually frozen to death, that would have been true hell.

Therefore.

*I have no regrets about this choice.*

I concluded as much and sank down in the corridor. The smartphone screen went dark. All the noisy, warm things were gone. All that remained was the dark winter night of the hospital corridor.

"......"

For some reason, it felt terribly cold.

***

"...Hyung."

When I returned to the hospital room, Keundal, who sat quietly on the bed alone after his check-up, quickly raised his head.

"Um, I've been thinking. What if I write something? On the internet... you know, since you couldn't do the concert because of me, if I explain clearly that it wasn't your fault..."

"Don't."

Who do you think the arrows would be aimed at then?

[He skipped work without permission, went into the mountains on his own, and got stranded in a restricted area. Isn't he a crazy bastard? What a waste of taxes.]

[Hey Gommeo, are your clout-chasing connections all mentally ill?]

[How much did you get paid by ORBIT?ㅋㅋㅋㅋ]

Keundal would literally be torn to shreds. No matter how carefully this guy polished his post, pissed-off people would curse him out and dox him. Coworkers or people who had only heard rumors would pop up and spew all sorts of nonsense. Why would he even do that?

It would only further ruin public opinion of him, a civilian, with no guarantee that my own would improve.

"Don't do it."

"But..."

"I'm thinking of another way, so you don't have to. Don't even think about posting anything yourself."

"...Yes."

Keundal nodded cautiously. Then, as if testing the waters, he spoke.

"...Um, hyung. You should go back and rest now. The doctor just said I'm in good condition."

If I left in this atmosphere, who knows what kind of depressive state this guy would fall into.

"I'm too tired to go all the way back to the dorm. Are you kicking me out?"

"Ah, no!? You can stay as long as you're comfortable!?"

"......"

"Then I'm using the bathroom."

"Yep!"

After finally washing my face, I lay down on the simple cot for guardians next to the private room. I had to wake up at 6 AM to not be late for tomorrow's schedule. No matter what happened, even if there were boycott signs at the venue, I had to perform unconditionally.

*I have to manage my condition properly.*

And so, I closed my eyes.

I woke up at exactly six in the morning.

Beep.

I quickly turned off the vibration before it could wake Keundal. However, I did not get up right away. Instead, I picked up my smartphone and took a silent, deep breath.

"......"

It was time to check. I could not put it off any longer. I had considered checking before I went to sleep but postponed it in case it ruined my sleep. I could not ruin today's concert too.

*...6:00 to 6:30 AM.*

It was a time with few internet users, so I would not get caught up in the endlessly refreshing tide of public opinion.

*Check now, then go.*

I tapped on the social media app I used for monitoring.

"......"

As soon as I clicked, a popular post appeared at the top.

[Moondae what do we doㅠㅠ]

My eyes froze on the introduction, which was clearly sarcastic.

*Read it.*

I gritted my teeth and forced my eyes to keep moving. The full post loaded.

[Moondae what do we doㅠㅠ Is he coming to the concert today? I'm so worried about whether he's in any condition to perform..]

"......"

*Is that... sarcasm?*

I subconsciously scrolled down. New popular posts kept appearing before my eyes. Posts written in the early morning, posts from just as the concert was about to start, all mixed together... on and on.

[Is the announcement of Park Moondae's absence for real?]

[Ah, this is crazyㅠㅠㅠ]

[He's the kid who couldn't contain his excitement whenever the concert was mentioned on WeApp for the past month. I can't even imagine how Park Moondae must be feeling right now.]

[I'm just... a wreck. The first concert turned out like this. I feel so bad for myself and for Park Moondae.]

[Why isn't he performing?;;; I'm so baffled and pissed off, but I'm worried about my idol. Fuck, I stayed up all night.]

No matter how many times I reread them, they did not change. There were hardly any posts cursing me to death.

"......"

With trembling hands, I hurriedly scrolled back up. I looked at the popular post I had read earlier again.

[Moondae what do we doㅠㅠ Will he be at the concert today? I'm so worried about whether he's in any condition to perform.. Ah, I'm going crazy.]

Now, I read it properly to the end and checked the context.

This was not sarcasm. It was a post genuinely worrying about whether I was in any condition to perform at the concert.

"...Ah."

I moved my hand. New posts kept appearing.
