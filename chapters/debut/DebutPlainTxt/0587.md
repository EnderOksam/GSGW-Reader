# Chapter 587

Off the Record.

It was obvious what Bae Sejin wanted to say after turning off his mic during the variety show filming.

"So what you're saying, Hyung... is that the production team is tormenting the cast more than necessary."

"Yes."

He spoke with a tense boldness, as if he had mulled over his words for a long time.

"For example, getting stranded on a deserted island for that other variety show was an accident, but this time, all of this is deliberate harassment."

"......"

"It's fine to show us struggling or making mistakes, but deliberately pushing us to fail and then laughing at us for it... that's not something we should find entertaining."

Bae Sejin gripped the microphone he had turned off.

"So, I want to talk to the production team about this directly."

A short silence followed.

*Hmm.*

I scanned the members' faces. They looked shocked, lost in thought, or flustered. It seemed I would be the first to speak.

"I understand what you mean, Hyung."

"......"

Bae Sejin swallowed hard, as if he already expected someone to object.

However.

"And I think you have a point."

"...! Really?"

"Yes."

*Why is he so surprised that I agree when he was the one who brought it up?*

*This shoot has definitely been a bit excessive.*

I rubbed my chin. The controversy over cruelty in variety shows was a classic, time-honored debate. There was a saying, wasn't there? A tragedy up close is a comedy from a distance.

Through the magic of screens and editing, the cast's sudden hardships were often reborn as funny content. Even without such filters, the person suffering could find some enjoyment in it, as long as it was not too serious.

*But controlling the intensity is key.*

The moment this crossed a line, it made viewers uncomfortable. They would think, 'No matter what, this is a bit much.'

Of course, Bae Sejin was not talking about this from a commercial perspective. He evaluated it from a moral and ethical standpoint.

However, I reached a similar conclusion, even when considering how the fandom would rate the program after it aired.

*If the editing is off by even a hair...*

Hmm.

[Why are you giving feedback however you want? Messing with someone's passion and career isn't funny at all.]

[lol Hey production team, I hope you can still laugh after your edits get rejected over and over, you pull all-nighters to fix them, and then someone says 'It was all a joke~ The first version was better lol'. Go on, laugh, you fucking bastards.]

[Where did you learn your manners, making them work without even letting them eat properly?]

I inwardly nodded.

*It does seem like they've crossed a line.*

The current structure relentlessly pressured the cast in all aspects of their work, food, and shelter. Moreover, that pressure looked unreasonable even to an outside observer. It did not seem like TeSTAR had brought any of it on themselves.

*Though I suppose you can still extract humor from that absurdity.*

It was a tightrope walk. The balance was maintained because this would end with TeSTAR somehow landing a satisfying counter-punch on the production team.

I would not know for sure until filming ended, but it was possible that the combination of that variety PD corps and writer Ryu Seorin had created a synergy in a bad way.

*It would be best to check if this program is on the right track and what their intentions were for doing this.*

However.

"But if we tell the production team this directly, won't they misunderstand?"

"...Misunderstand?"

"Like, 'Why are you making us suffer when we just want to film comfortably?'"

"...!"

That was right. First of all...

*If we bring this up the wrong way, the atmosphere will be shot to hell.*

The relationship with the production team was the problem. We still had half of the filming left to do.

If the cast confronted the production team with serious faces, future filming and editing would likely become stiff, even if they got their point across. In a variety show, that was a truly terrible move.

Why did we squeeze our non-existent time to come here in the first place? To create high-quality content. If that quality dropped, it would be a loss that threw all this suffering into the dirt.

"Even if that's not your intention, Hyung, the production team might take it emotionally. They might think, 'We worked hard too, why are you making us out to be the bad guys?'"

Human relationships were the blind spot in any kind of work.

"That... That could happen."

Bae Sejin's face darkened, but then, surprising support came from an unexpected corner.

"Moondae Moondae, do we really have to be the ones to say it?"

Keun Sejin opened his mouth. Bae Sejin stared at him, his mouth agape.

"Huh?"

"The company can say it for us~"

"...!"

"Hey, we're part of a company so they can handle things like this for us. Coordination!" Keun Sejin shrugged.

"TeSTAR is in the middle of a concert tour, and you, Hyung, even have a drama schedule, right? We have a perfectly good reason, like, 'What if they get hurt from all this?'"

Ryu Chungwoo agreed. "Hmm, they could definitely do it from an artist management perspective."

"Exactly!" Keun Sejin nodded at Ryu Chungwoo's agreement.

[Let's have the company protest!]

The idea was that TeSTAR could just maintain a friendly relationship with the production team without saying a word, positioning themselves as just being a little troubled.

"There were a few times when I also thought... hmm, is it okay to go this far? So I thought it might be okay to gently test the waters," Ryu Chungwoo added.

"R-Right."

Bae Sejin looked slightly moved, as if he had naturally expected Keun Sejin to oppose him.

*Though it seems he hasn't noticed that Keun Sejin's objective is completely different from his own...*

*Still, isn't this much better than them yelling at each other about whether this made any sense?*

However... I shook my head. There was something they were forgetting.

"Don't expect the company to handle it that well."

"...Ah."

The disadvantage of indirect communication was that a proxy has its limits.

[A proxy has its limits.]

The company was not TeSTAR. It could not fully understand our position or represent us exactly as we wanted. On a sensitive matter like this, our message could even be distorted.

*If they can't protest at just the right level we want, it would be worse than doing nothing at all.*

What would happen if they came on too strong? Starting from the editing, anything that could be even slightly uncomfortable would be cut. The variety show would lose its fiery flavor and become bland.

Then other complaints would arise. The production team from TeSTAR's previous variety shows was the same this time. No, with two famous producers collaborating, it should have been a fresh hellfire. I could already picture people's bewildered reactions, wondering why it had become so tame and boring.

*Everyone will probably guess that the company sent down guidelines.*

Then, criticism and condemnation would run rampant, as much as there had been anticipation.

[They suck at their jobs.]

[This is why they only get boring variety shows that coddle them, turning them into has-beens lol smh.]

[Is it really that tragic for idols who earn billions a year to suffer for a few days on a variety show? The company needs to get a grip.]

[They're just pandering to the fans... That's why you all should stop projecting onto the idols and attacking the staff. You're like those crazy, overbearing stage parents.]

[TeSTAR is the same, but the company and the fans are making a scene tsk tsk.]

*Well, that's a mess.*

A whole panorama of reactions and provocations already unfolded in my mind.

"We have no way of knowing if the company can handle it skillfully."

The atmosphere briefly turned solemn.

"Umm..."

"Then what should we do?"

A few opinions were raised and dismissed, but none received overwhelming support.

Cha Eugene spoke up. "Let's just buy the production team a late-night snack right now, and act cute while saying we're having a hard time. That's the answer."

"We don't have any money."

"Oh..."

The reality was that Cha Eugene's suggestion was the most tempting one.

*Should I contact the company and get some money?*

I was seriously considering it. Just then, Bae Sejin opened his mouth as if he had made up his mind.

"Hey... what if I go talk to them alone?"

"......"

"If things go wrong, I can say it was my own unilateral decision. Then the relationship between TeSTAR and the production team won't get weird. For the rest of the shoot... I'll just have to be a little more careful."

"Hyung..."

"I know Cha Eugene's method is better for getting past this with a smile, but I don't just want to change this immediate situation, I mean..." Bae Sejin chose his words with difficulty.

Then, another unexpected person spoke up.

"Excuse me, could it be something like this...?"

"Huh?"

It was Seon Ahyeon.

"To be honest, I'm okay with struggling a little... but I'm worried that the other members can't eat properly because of me."

He continued speaking softly.

"If we film like this, other similar programs might say, 'TeSTAR did it, so you should be able to endure this much too'... so I'm a little worried."

"...!"

"That's right. That's it...! That we'll be setting a bad precedent!" Bae Sejin lifted his face, slightly agitated.

Then, as if snapping back to his senses, he hastily added, "But, I'm not going to say it like we're taking one for the team for the sake of the industry environment! It's not a criticism, I'm just... going to try to explain it as gently as possible!"

"......"

"Sejin, don't worry."

Keun Sejin did not get angry either, just offered a bitter smile. He probably did not want to resort to sending Bae Sejin.

*He probably thinks Bae Sejin is being too excessive and inflexible.*

However, one thing had changed. It seemed he now trusted that Bae Sejin would not do anything to harm the team.

*They've come a long way.*

Watching the guys who seemed to have almost reached the right answer, I inwardly nodded.

Ryu Chungwoo asked, "So, Sejin, you want to go talk to them alone?"

"...Yeah. If you guys are okay with it, I'd like to try."

Of course, we could not let one guy bear the entire burden. I smiled and opened my mouth.

"Then you go, Hyung, and I'll go with you."

"Huh?"

"Just as someone who tagged along."

Sending him alone would make it look too much like Bae Sejin's unilateral decision, no matter what.

*And if the Leader or Keun Sejin, the variety show member, got involved, it would be too much.*

*Someone like me would be just right.*

I was also confident I could step in and smooth things over if needed.

"I'll read the room and position myself accordingly, so don't worry about me."

"...Okay. Thanks." Bae Sejin's face brightened slightly.

"Then... I'll contact them now!"

"OK!"

A short while later, Bae Sejin and I left our lodgings and entered the mountain cabin where the production team was staying nearby.

"Ah, it's cold out here. Please come inside, TeSTAR."

We were finally meeting face-to-face the production team members who had been tormenting TeSTAR through video calls throughout the entire shoot.

***

Bae Sejin lightly scanned the inside of the cabin. It was an older place than where TeSTAR was staying. Furthermore, no one in the production team's lodging was asleep, even though it was late at night.

A few people could be seen catching some shut-eye, but most seemed busy checking footage or preparing something. They also seemed a bit flustered as to why we were here.

*...Everyone is working so hard.*

For a moment, he worried that what he was about to say would sound like a child's tantrum. Bae Sejin's resolve wavered for a second, but he steeled himself. It was still the right thing to do. He just had to do it well.

The PD spoke. "So... umm, Sejin, you said you had something to discuss privately."

"Yes."

Bae Sejin clenched his fists. He felt sweat pooling in his palms but maintained a calm expression.

*I said I would do it, so I have to do it right.*

Conscious of Park Moondae standing beside him, he calmly opened his mouth.

"After thinking it over, there are a few things... that have been bothering me."

He calmly explained, giving one example after another. He mentioned the composition feedback that was close to a prank, the issues with food and shelter, the guilt and fatigue, and the worries about the song's quality.

"...So a member is having a really hard time because of guilt, and I think it would have been just as fun even if you hadn't done that."

Bae Sejin chose his words carefully several times to avoid sounding aggressive.

"...It's fine to show us struggling or making mistakes, but I don't think you need to... treat someone's feelings so harshly."

"......"

A brief silence fell.

*I've said everything I needed to say.*

Bae Sejin took a measured breath. He suppressed the urge to squeeze his eyes shut and kept his gaze fixed forward.

And then...

SMACK!

A sharp slapping sound rang out.

"......"

The writers had started smacking the PD's back.

"Ah, I knew it! I knew this would happen!"

"PD, what did I tell you!"

"Ow! Ow!"

"Aish... really."

In truth, staff from various departments were mixed in, but not a single person tried to stop them. On the contrary, the reaction was closer to 'It was bound to happen.' Bae Sejin looked around at them, bewildered.

Amid the bitter smiles of the people around them, the PD waved his arms.

"No, first of all... there's a bit of a misunderstanding."

"Yes?"

"The roles other than Raebin. They were truly the result of a pure game."

"......"

"We really didn't rig anything. Really!"

That was right. All the suffering related to food and shelter, aside from separating Kim Raebin, was genuinely a matter of luck.

*Is this for real?*

Park Moondae was not sure if he should call this good luck or shitty luck.

Bae Sejin also swallowed and asked, "Then... what about Raebin?"

"That... well, it seemed like if Raebin was there, the song would be finished in an instant. Then we wouldn't get enough screen time," the PD said with a somewhat serious tone.

"Besides, Raebin is the type to comfortably enjoy resting alone, so he's not a character who would be disliked by viewers."

"Ah..."

"Making them likable is just as important as being funny. It's idol content, after all."

Bae Sejin understood the production team's way of thinking. It was better for TeSTAR's image to be protected, even if the production team took some flak. Even if TeSTAR had to suffer a bit in the process.

Ryu Seorin, who had been standing with her arms crossed, threw out a comment. "Programs with a story like this need a villain, and we can't make TeSTAR the villains."

"So the production team takes on that role. We need that kind of stimulus, too."

"...!"

*That's... not what I was trying to say.*The problem was not that TeSTAR was having a hard time, but the method and type of hardship.

Bae Sejin was about to object, but the PD cut in first.

"So what Sejin is saying is that we weren't funny as villains, but were just unpleasant, right, Seorin?"

"...That's right." Ryu Seorin sighed, looking embarrassed.

"Watching what you did with the previous PD, I saw that TeSTAR is really good at improvising. So in an attempt to draw out more natural reactions... I guess we went too far without any prior discussion."

"...!"

The PD scratched his head. "Right. This happens sometimes, you get tunnel vision... I'm sorry you had to come and tell us this, and thank you, Sejin."

"We've had a lot of fun doing all sorts of things with TeSTAR... you guys always responded well and overcame everything, so we just kept pushing. We shouldn't rely on the cast like this. I'm really sorry."

"...It's alright."

And so, Bae Sejin relaxed a little and began to listen to the production team's explanation.

"This is what happened..."

His words were being taken seriously.
