# Chapter 366

The shocking, surreal evidence of confirming one's 'mission' through a hologram!

It took Kwon Heeseung several moments to come to his senses.

"H-h-h-h-how uh, does this... How does this work??"

Correction. I thought he'd come to his senses, but apparently his mouth was still moving faster than his brain.

*Ahhh!*

What kind of sci-fi movie was this? Limestone movie?

However, Park Moondae's voice that was heard from the smartphone was calm.

– I don't know the exact principle, but... It's like hacking.

"Hacking...? But this isn't a computer!"

– ...

He didn't say anything. Park Moondae was surprisingly silent, as if he was lost in thought.

And he answered with some interest.

– You might be onto something.

*Onto what?!*

This was not some kind of Metaverse virtual reality AI!

Kwon Heeseung, who had been using all the IT technology words he knew, had recovered from the shock and was about to ask in more detail, 'How on earth did he do this?'

Park Moondae struck first.

– Anyway, what did you see when you looked earlier? The mission content.

"Hmm."

Although the rather unpleasant phrase 'status abnormality' bothered him, perhaps what this sunbaenim was asking was not that, but the content underneath it.

Kwon Heeseung opened his mouth reflexively.

"Well... Something about releasing a collaborative work, I think?"

– Okay.

"It's amazing. So it's really a self-produced album..."

Kwon Heeseung felt thrilled, like someone who had been struck by magic.

He felt like the protagonist of a movie. He wasn't sure if that was good or bad, though.

But Park Moondae was cool-headed.

– No, it's different.

What?

– The standard isn't a self-produced album, but rather, a collaborative work.

"Yes... But isn't that what it is?"

– It's different. A collaborative work doesn't necessarily have to be an entire album.

"Ah."

Park Moondae smoothly applied his extensive experience to the problem.

– It is much easier to get the public to recognize that it is simply a 'collaborative work'.

"Wow."

He sounded like a real secret organization agent.

Kwon Heeseung, who became a little more serious, politely held the smartphone with both hands and asked a question.

"Then... I can probably succeed with this album, right? The odds are good?"

– Yes.

Oh, oh.

Kwon Heeseung briefly expressed his admiration but calculated the next moment.

He wasn't stupid either. He considered the possibility that Park Moondae was plotting something or lying to him.

*Hmm...*

However, no matter how much he thought about it, there was no need to lie.

*If he wanted to screw me over, he's already had plenty of chances.*

Park Moondae had been taking good care of him so far, so there wasn't a need for him to go this far... There was no need to get in trouble by sharing things like superpowers.

*It's not like he's some psychotic supervillain. If he were planning something like that, I'd already be screwed.*

Kwon Heeseung decided to accept Park Moondae's favor this time too!

But before that, a cautious voice was heard.

– Want me to help you?

"Oh my gosh!"

Kwon Heeseung asked quickly.

"I'm so grateful, hyung! But does this mean I'm going to owe you one...?"

– I'd tell you beforehand if that was what I wanted.

"Sorry. That's usually how it goes in movies, you know? Are you still helping me? Hehe..."

There was a sound of laughter.

– Sure.

And Park Moondae really began to thoroughly help Kwon Heeseung.

The production process began.

Park Moondae skillfully picked out an aspect of Spacer's upcoming album where they could earn some easy extra credit.

– The lyric book is your best bet. It doesn't get much scrutiny, but unlike a photobook that's mainly for fans, it's still an important part of the album.

"Oh, oh."

– Even if an amateur contributes, it's unlikely to ruin the result. And since it isn't revealed in advance, it's comparatively easy to change.

"It's good... Really!"

Kwon Heeseung thought he should tell this to the member who did not believe in his creativity and refused to participate in the production of the album until the end.

However, the very next day, he saw that the member gave his opinion on the design of the lyrics book at the company's recommendation.

"Oh, what's this?"

"I don't know. The company told me to do it."

The revised lyric-book design was approved within two days.

*...This fast?*

Did their company make decisions this quickly?

Kwon Heeseung was a little embarrassed, but work continued.

The physical albums were manufactured, and the promotional material was revised and sent out.

★$
Leaps and bounds toward the dream!

Please look forward to this album, ⟨Fighting⟩, which has been handcrafted by all Spacer members from the music to the album inclusions! 💥
$★

It didn't come to the forefront of publicity. It was just an additional introductory phrase that made fans think 'That's right' and accept the information.

It fit perfectly with the album's concept, which centered on their dreams and aspirations as idols, so nothing felt out of place.

A finishing touch that neatly added only the correct options without damaging Spacer's original plans.

*Wow. I didn't think this would actually be possible.*

Kwon Heeseung was impressed.

Even though Park Moondae lived longer than he did and returned to the past, wasn't this level of skill—and, dare he say, authority—another matter entirely?

*Isn't it true that Moondae-hyung really set up the entire label?*

The last time he asked him about this rumor going around the company, Moondae had denied it, but apparently he'd just been modest.

Kwon Heeseung nodded, but at the same time felt a little creeped out.

On a day when Spacer was big enough to become a competitor to TeSTAR...

*...TeSTAR sunbaenim will become independent!*

Yes, he heard that next year was contract renewal season. Of course, they would run away from this crappy company. Haha!

Kwon Heeseung decided to move on for now. What was the use of worrying about a problem that had no answer? Let's live in reality!

So, he immediately thanked the person he was grateful for.

"It's all thanks to you! Oh, thank you so much! I will repay you!"

– Alright.

Park Moondae's answer was short, but not blunt. On the contrary, it felt soft.

After making a fuss about treating Park Moondae to lobster once he got paid, Kwon Heeseung happily shouted.

"I think I'm in the clear. I am, right?"

Now, he was free!

But his confidence was too quick. This was because Park Moondae gave an ambiguous answer.

– It's true that the conditions are met.

"...Uh, is it different?"

A calm voice answered.

– You'll see soon.

"What?"

There was a burst of static.

"Wow!"

A glitch appeared before Kwon Heeseung's eyes. And a strange hologram appeared again.

★-
\
#><[Successful release!]><#

You've succeeded in releasing your collaborative work!

!Time limit: Met (Success)

!Status Abnormality: #*'Make It Work'*# removed!

**{[: Reveal the *^@5#&{** ← Click!

"...!"

The word 'success' appeared multiple times, and things looked much better than the last time Park Moondae had shown it to him.

The button glittered brightly, making it hard to ignore.

*This... Can I just press it?*

Kwon Heeseung looked at the 'Click!' button. He checked the button and raised his arm as if possessed...

Park Moondae's voice sounded like cold water being poured on him.

– I mean, you said you'd repay me, right? About that.

"...?"

Kwon Heeseung, who was reaching out for the hologram, paused for a moment.

That moment...

– Don't press it.

"Wh-what?"

– Waiting a few days before pressing it is how you can repay me.

No, how did he know? Kwon Heeseung lowered his hand as if he had put it on fire.

"Why, why?"

– Hmm.

After being silent for a moment, Park Moondae spoke in a voice with a hint of laughter.

– Are you interested in doing something useful with a single day of your vacation?

"...??"

Beneath the stage of a large concert hall in Seoul, the audience's cheers still rang in our ears. The members around me laughed.

"Oh my, everyone worked hard~"

"It was fun!"

It was the end of a joint concert hosted by a credit card company.

"Moondaemoondae, are you going to sit in the back?"

"Yes."

I sat in the back seat of the car among the guys who were happy on their way home from work after successfully completing the performance without making any mistakes.

And I looked back at the conversation I had with Kwon Heeseung yesterday.

*Here's the premise of my plan.*

I could control when the status abnormality cleared by choosing when to use 'Reveal the Truth.'

And that was entirely thanks to the status window.

*So I thought about it the other way around.*

Then couldn't anyone do the same thing, as long as they had access to a status window?

– If you can access the status window even for those with status abnormalities, wouldn't you be able to control when it gets cleared?

So, Keundal accessed Kwon Heeseung's system and opened a status window.

[It's a bit uncomfortable... Ughhh]

Of course, he said it was more inconvenient than using my own status window, but this guy succeeded without any other side effects.

Since this access came from legitimately clearing a mission rather than trying to cheat the system, Keundal seemed to have even more control.

*Good.*

It was inspired by Keundal changing the state of my status window, so I was glad that it worked without any problems.

And Kwon Heeseung quickly accepted my suggestion that he lend us some support before we destroyed the system.

– Of course, it's good! Doesn't it feel like we're on a secret mission?

...It would have been better if he hadn't talked as if we were at a VR arcade. Anyway, I was glad there was a mutually supportive atmosphere.

*Good.*

I nodded and adjusted my neck pillow.

The way I'd arranged the timing gave me some wiggle room too.

There was a slight lull in my schedule after promotions ended and before preparations for the tour began.

*I guess I can take at least a day off.*

If all went according to plan, one day was enough to see the results. I nodded to myself as we arrived back at the dorm.

I planned to spend today reviewing my plans...

"Moondae-hyung, could we please talk for a moment..."

"Mhm, sure."
