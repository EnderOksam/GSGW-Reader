# Chapter 592

DUDUDUM.

The thick door opened with the weight of dramatic background music, revealing Seon Ahyeon's room.

However...

"......"

"...A room?"

This was not a room.

I met Keun Sejin's eyes. He smiled, but his pupils trembled.

Inside was a space like a small living room, furnished with an elegant table, paintings, and a piano. Next to small, knitted trinkets and flower vases, another door was visible.

That was right. It seemed there was another room inside.

*What is this?*

Seon Ahyeon explained brightly.

"They said it was a space made to be used as a reception room...!"

Was such a thing even possible in Seoul?

Before I could ask, the owner of the room shyly added another detail.

"To be honest, I haven't had many guests until now. When I was young, I sometimes used it as a dance practice room..."

Ah.

Seon Ahyeon quickly walked over and opened a cabinet. Inside, a huge, neatly folded, screen-style mirror was visible.

"Ah, they put it here...!"

Next to it, a few ballet-related items were still in storage. They were items from Seon Ahyeon's childhood.

Seeing him act as if he were hosting a housewarming party, I found myself asking a question.

"Are we the first people to visit you here since your debut?"

"Y-Yeah. You're the first!"

I see.

"We're honored, Ahyeon~"

"Ah, no..."

Despite his denial, Seon Ahyeon continued his explanation with an excited expression, diligently introducing the space he called a reception room.

"Before I lived alone in the officetel for a while, I lived here..."

He said his mother was transferred overseas right around the time of ❬Idol Inc.❭. She had gotten him a separate officetel near the filming set, thinking it would be too hard for him to live alone in this spacious house.

"Oh~ So is the contract for that officetel over?"

"......"

Seon Ahyeon moved and finally stood at the door inside the room.

"This is where I've lived since I was young."

When he opened that door, the bedroom finally came into view.

"Wow!"

The interior was clean and modern, with a prominent wood-tone design.

However, that was not why Keun Sejin exclaimed. A custom-built glass cabinet was installed on one wall, its entire front filled with TeSTAR's merchandise.

"Oh my god."

It was not just Seon Ahyeon's items. Slogans, merchandise, dolls, and even drawings for each TeSTAR member were meticulously displayed, sparkling as they caught the high-quality sunlight. I could feel the loving care put into it.

"My dad displays the items here whenever we get new ones!"

"Ooh."

It now made sense why Seon Ahyeon's room at the dorm had so much merchandise for the other members, not just his own.

*Is it a family tradition?*

I peered inside the display cabinet. Looking closer, I saw many other things on the left side. There were wooden toys, a box of crayons, a dog doll, and even some kind of ring case. It seemed they had collected and displayed meaningful items from Seon Ahyeon's life as he grew up.

As my eyes moved to the right, the cabinet became more and more packed with the stories of the TeSTAR members.

So this was not just a shelf for TeSTAR merchandise. It was a display cabinet filled with things that were meaningful and precious in Seon Ahyeon's life.

And the TeSTAR members were among them.

"......"

Keun Sejin seemed genuinely moved. His voice was a little choked up, but he deliberately spoke playfully.

"Ahyeon, you know... I'm so happy... to have met a member who cherishes the group this much, you know?"

"...! Y-Yeah, me too...! Meeting such good friends like this... I'm really, truly grateful and happy!"

"*Sob*, Ahyeon, we're friends for life!"

Good grief.

I watched the two of them, who looked like they were about to hug, and just laughed softly. Well, it was a good thing.

"Over here... there's an open-air bath!"

"That's insane."

Riding that momentum, Seon Ahyeon even showed us around the house. We enjoyed the tour while his parents watched us with pleased expressions.

After that, we hung out in his reception room. Keun Sejin could not stay the night since he had to spend the holiday with his own parents, but he stuck around tenaciously, chatting late into the night.

It was even fun when Seon Ahyeon's parents brought us a huge bowl of some expensive salad, calling it a late-night snack. That was definitely the choice of someone who did not eat late-night snacks.

"Hey, at least they put beef on top."

"I'm grateful."

Nodding and saying it was great for managing our figures, we nibbled on the greens and looked through Seon Ahyeon's childhood photo albums.

"Ooh, Ahyeon grabbed the thread at his doljabi~ I grabbed the microphone."

"......"

"What about Moondae?"

"Probably a pencil."

It was new. Honestly, the three of us chatting at the dorm was a common occurrence. However, this was almost the first time in the six or seven years we had lived together that we had spent time just hanging out and talking about personal things completely unrelated to work.

Just like real school friends.

The homeowner seemed to feel this situation was just as meaningful.

"...Um, guys. If it's okay... can I post about today on my account?"

At Seon Ahyeon's request, Keun Sejin and I immediately nodded.

"Of course it's okay~"

A few minutes later, a post like this appeared on TeSTAR's SNS.

[I came home with my friends. It's so much fun. I hope Loviewer has a happy evening too! (Photo)]

The attached photos were selfies taken in the reception room and living room, along with the salad we ate as a late-night snack. It was an unadorned, honest post.

Not long after he uploaded it, comments arrived from the other members who were scattered for the holiday.

[Kim Raebin: I saw the photo Ahyeon posted. It is wonderful to see the three of you having a harmonious holiday. (Elderly person thumbs-up emoticon)]

[Kim Raebin: I have arrived safely in LA and am heading to Cha Eugene's house.]

[Cha Eugene: My dogs are following Grandma Kim Raebin around! (Blurry photo)]

[Ryu Chungwoo: That sounds cute My dog is sleeping well (Photo)]

Suddenly, everyone's pets were taking over.

However, a message arrived that would quell them all.

[Bae Sejin: (Video)]

Bae Sejin majestically posted a single video without a word. It showed a small, white furball in his arms, howling as if it recognized them while watching a TeSTAR video. It was Mungge, the little dog that had lived with us on ❬The House's Grandson❭.

"Moondae, Moondae, Mungge remembers us, oh my god!"

The group chat went wild.

[Cha Eugene: omg]

[Cha Eugene: Bring Mungge to America]

Bae Sejin coolly did not reply. He had the air of a victor.

*These funny guys.*

I let out a dry laugh.

Keun Sejin nudged my side.

"How about it? Better than resting alone at the dorm, right?"

"......"

I would not deny it. I had originally planned to just chill at the dorm for a day or two, not to spend my time this boisterously.

However.

*This kind of thing is pretty nice too.*

I met lots of people and ate a ton of food. It was a holiday where no sudden problems arose, and no one had to rush off to work.

"......"

I tossed and turned in the 'my room' I had been shown to, trying to sleep. I remembered what Seon Ahyeon's parents had told me earlier.

[You can come whenever you want to rest!]

[...Yes. Thank you.]

I had answered that way, but I had no intention of being so thoughtless as to come and go from Seon Ahyeon's house as I pleased.

*Still...*

I turned my head to the side. A few Polaroid photos of me were stuck there. Some of them looked familiar. They were photos Seon Ahyeon had taken, and it seemed he had shared them with his parents.

"......"

So this was not just a guest room. It was a room truly prepared for me. Even if it was not the dorm, I had one more place to stay.

That was not a bad feeling at all. It felt as if I had a real family home.

"...Hah."

*Thinking about it like that, it really does feel like the holidays.*

I let out a small laugh. It was an interesting development. I had been planning to do something truly holiday-like during this break anyway.

*Of course, I have to take care of something else first.*

I fell asleep thinking about the place I would visit tomorrow.

The guy in the military uniform clasped his hands together seriously.

"So... what brings you to visit a senior private who's about to be discharged?"

"......"

*Maybe I shouldn't have come.*

I faced Judan, who stared at me with curiosity, and felt a brief moment of disillusionment.

[Ah... Is Moondae leaving already?]

*To think I'd deliberately leave Seon Ahyeon's home, where I was so warmly welcomed, just to visit a soldier. Wasn't this a terrible trade-off?*

"Ah, I'm not saying this with the base intention of making you feel uncomfortable. It's just pure curiosity."

"......"

"A visit and even a home-cooked meal? I don't recall ever helping you this much, Moondae."

"Ah, yes."

He really never changes.

I muttered reluctantly.

"I just came because I wanted to see you after a while."

"...!"

*I should just go home.*

"Well, if you're uncomfortable, I'll just get going..."

"Ah, I didn't mean I'm uncomfortable."

Judan quickly stopped me. He replied with a slightly excited face.

"Actually, I think it's something to be grateful for. For someone who doesn't make many acquaintances, a visit is a big event."

"......"

As he said that, I thought I could almost see the expression of the young trainee, the teenage Jung Woodan, on his face. He was the kid who had believed without a doubt that I would be a close hyung debuting with him in the future.

*Good grief.*

In the end, I sat back down.

"Still, there must have been a reason you suddenly decided to see me... Is there a special reason, perhaps?"

"Hmm."

*Well, with this guy, it should be fine.*

"I did have a rather unique experience."

After checking once more that the visitation area's dining hall was empty, I began my story without holding back.

"A while ago... I received help from a sunbaenim who was a trainee, before his debut."

"...!"

I was talking about my recent experience during the 100th restart, when I became Cheongryeo's Status Window and met the Judan of that time.

Judan listened to the supernatural story with great interest, then nodded.

"Hmm, so a Jung Woodan from a parallel universe, I see."

If you had to put it that way... yes.

"Hmm, this is just my speculation... but in that case, did you build some sort of rapport while receiving my help, and is this visit a way of repaying that kindness?"

"......"

*It's not quite to that extent.*

Well, it might be similar.

"It's similar."

"I see."

Judan shrugged.

"Unfortunately, strictly speaking, I am not the Jung Woodan who helped you, Moondae. A person's identity is formed through experience and memory. I have no memory of it."

"...Ah, yes."

I held back a sigh.

*Right. I pretty much knew you'd say something like that.*

However, I did not expect his next words.

"Still, in a similar situation, I believe the me of that age would have definitely made the same choice."

"...!"

"In that sense, if I were to acknowledge it... even though I have no memory, isn't it true that it was something I experienced at some point in the past?"

Judan nodded seriously.

"So I will accept your words as an indirect memory. That it was me."

"......"

Good grief. You, I swear.

If it were not for the 'A super fun situation like this is happening to me too' expression on his face, it might have been a little touching.

However... well, I could not deny that it was a very 'him' thing to say.

I laughed like air escaping a balloon.

"Yes. Thank you."

"Don't mention it."

Despite having no memory of it, he started eating his meal with a slightly triumphant air.

"Tell me more of the details when I'm discharged. I'll try to make the memory more concrete."

"Ah, yes."

He was a funny guy.

However, for some reason, I felt a sense of relief, as if I had finished a task that had been weighing on my mind.

As the visiting hours were about to end, the conversation shifted to my recent activities.

"I see. A belated holiday vacation."

He stared into the air as if reminiscing.

"It reminds me of my first holiday vacation."

"What was it like?"

"Jaehyun came to visit the family homes of all the members, including me."

"......"

"He said he was stopping by to thank the members' parents, but it must have been for surveillance purposes."

Of course he would.

"Speaking of which, aren't you going to visit Jaehyun?"

"Well... I'll see if I have time."

*Yeah. I'm not going.*

His service is only six months long. Besides, I caused too much of a scene during my last visit to go again.

*He probably had a hard time cleaning up the mess.*

It is better for both of us if I do not go.

"Then do you have any other plans for the rest of your vacation?"

"Mm."

I gave a small smile. This evening. It was the exact time I had planned.

"It's a vacation I got for the holidays. ...I'm thinking of going to see my family."

And so, Keundal was now... free!

*Hyung is at Ahyeon's place.*

Since there were also witness accounts from Lee Sejin's house, it seemed he planned to spend this vacation at those two members' homes. Mm, that was a good thing.

*Come to think of it, the new season of ❬Doll Hunter❭ is coming out soon!*

Then I will be able to see the making-of for TeSTAR's OST soon.

Thus, Keundal tried to lighten his heart and quickened his pace.

However, someone was already standing in front of his officetel door.

"Hey."

In his hands was an armful of... signed TeSTAR merchandise and albums.

The hyung standing at the door grinned.

"If you have time, let's rest together."

To celebrate the belated holiday, Park Moondae had come to visit.
