# Chapter 432

A few hours ago.

Park Moondae rushed onto the stage. Then, like a cruel joke, the set collapsed on top of him.

AAAAAH!

Ugh, gasp!

Amid the backstage chaos of screams and stumbling, Lee Sejin stood frozen.

*Is this a dream?*

It felt like a strange nightmare. A perfectly fine member wouldn't just collapse, run onto the stage, and get crushed by the set. Even after experiencing supernatural things, this was too absurd. It was unbelievable.

*Something like this can't be happening...*

SMACK.

Pain shot through him.

"Snap out of it!"

He turned. Bae Sejin, his face pale as a sheet, had just slapped his back.

"He could be alive, he must be alive, we have to check...!"

"......"

Lee Sejin gasped. The sounds of chaos flooded his ears. He looked back at the stage, where massive, heavy equipment lay in a tangled mess. The wreckage was too dense to see a person, but maybe there was a pocket of space inside.

"We have to, check inside."

As if hearing the dazed mumble, Ryu Chungwoo's uncharacteristically sharp voice cut through the noise.

"The other side of the backstage!"

"...!"

"Let's go that way!"

Cha Eugene shot forward like a spring. The other members slipped past the distracted staff and followed, circling around to the opposite side of the backstage.

But the other side was just a pile of completely collapsed steel frames, a terrifying sculpture of metal.

"...Hk."

FZZZT...

Sparks flew from a broken light. A steel beam was stained with a bit of blood. It was horrifyingly easy to imagine what had happened the moment he was crushed. This shouldn't have happened.

*To be alive, in here...*

THUD. KRRR-CRACK-CRUMBLE-BOOM!

As if to crush that very thought, the concrete ceiling above the stage collapsed.

"......"

*This way! Quickly!*

The walls shook.

"Just get in!"

Cha Eugene rushed to an emergency staircase and yanked it open. The members scrambled up. Hearing the screams and footsteps all around, they would have followed the staff and escaped in the confusion.

If only they hadn't seen it.

"...Here, a bloodstain!"

Bae Sejin had found a trail of Park Moondae's blood leading up.

***

"...That's what happened."

Ryu Chungwoo finished his brief summary. They had gathered the people left in the building to make escaping easier, but another collapse blocked the doors on the first and third floors, isolating them on the second.

I realized something.

The second-floor iron door I saw with the drone, across from the collapsed emergency staircase, wasn't the door I used to escape the backstage. I must have gotten confused while frantically running up.

*Based on the height, the backstage door must have been next to where we launched the drone.*

The firmly shut second-floor iron door actually connected to this office space.

Ryu Chungwoo finished his explanation.

"So Sejin came up with the idea for the announcement. Luckily, the walkie-talkie was rechargeable, so we could use it."

"......"

They missed their chance to escape because they couldn't abandon the possibility that I was alive.

This is driving me crazy.

I barely managed to speak.

"Thank you, but from now on..."

"...From now on?"

I started to say, 'Don't do anything so reckless,' but I shut my mouth. I was in no position to say that. If I hadn't done something that looked like a suicide attempt, they wouldn't have stayed behind.

"...I'll explain when we have time, but right now, we're in a real hurry."

"......"

Ryu Chungwoo looked at me with an expression that said he had a lot to say, but he held it back. I had already given him an explanation.

"...You said we're short on time, so let's get up for now."

"Yes."

I had just explained that the Status Window, whether from the System or something else, had displayed a collapse message.

[00:26:03]

"So that means we need to find this person named Keundal first."

"Yes, but for everyone else, escape is the priority."

We opened the management office door and stepped out. Just then, someone popped out from the next room.

"Here they are!"

Cha Eugene held three or four employee ID cards. In just a few minutes, he must have turned every office in the hallway upside down to find them.

"I found them with the fans. Let's get out of here quickly!"

It really made a difference having more people. With enough people, we could also handle everything at once.

"Gasp!!"

I turned my head at a fan's short scream.

"P-Park Moondae...!"

"Hyung! You're safe!"

"...!"

I saw the two Bae Sejin had brought from beyond the wall. They must have used the rope we left to climb up, because they were even dustier than before. I guess I must have looked like that too.

"......"

Both of them looked like they were about to burst into tears. From their perspective, the building started collapsing early, and they lost contact with the guy who went up.

Damn it.

"Uh..."

My mind raced.

"I miscalculated the time."

"I-I don't believe you."

I'm in big trouble.

"Park Moondae, you..."

"It was really a mistake."

Under Bae Sejin's suspicious gaze, I started moving. Seon Ahyeon and Kim Raebin flanked me as if I were surrounded. It was surprising that Kim Raebin had even brought the laptop and the drone joystick.

"Kim Raebin! Are you okay? What's that? We need to travel light, throw it away!"

"You shouldn't recklessly judge its value with your knowledge alone..."

Kim Raebin, for once, seemed to grasp the mood.

"...It's an item that could potentially be used for our escape!"

This was the problem. Dozens of fans were staring at us. I could hear them whispering.

"Did all the kids stay behind?"

"Oh... what do we do."

If we disappeared for a private conversation about the Status Window, there was no telling what might happen. We were all trapped, but as public figures, we drew attention, and since they were fans, they were just as worried about us as they were about themselves.

That's why I didn't just step into a room with only Ryu Chungwoo earlier. I had to be careful what I said as information spread, which made things more difficult.

Of course, the remaining time was also tighter.

[00:24:01]

People were still shaken by the rapidly changing situation.

"Um... did something happen?"

"It's okay. Everyone's just surprised because I accidentally got separated at the beginning."

"Ah..."

I smiled as gently as I could. I hoped it felt like a fansign event. The fan glanced at the shirt I had changed into. It probably stood out since the other guys were all in their stage outfits.

*They probably didn't notice the wound.*

"That's right~ Still, it's such a relief we met like this, right? I was so surprised and happy earlier! I think we just need to focus on getting out safely now!"

Lee Sejin chattered, patting my shoulder. The guy who was cursing earlier must have had a hard time trying to soothe the atmosphere.

"Thanks..."

"We'll talk after we get out."

"......"

No, I told you there were circumstances... whatever. I started with the most urgent matter.

"By any chance, are there any male audience members here?"

Since people were scattered in various offices, there was a high chance Keundal was among them.

*He might have been so focused on accessing the Status Window that he didn't even notice this commotion.*

He did try to search over a hundred times, so he must have been pouring all his focus into it.

"If it's something that requires strength, can't we all do it together?"

"No. Ah. It's not like that, I'm just looking for someone."

"Ah..."

I must have looked desperate. Without asking why, everyone started looking, but he wasn't there. Most of the fans were female, and the one or two remaining staff members were also women.

"Hmm."

Delaying any longer would be foolish.

"What should I do..."

"It's okay. Let's start evacuating quickly."

I spoke to the fans while signaling Ryu Chungwoo with my eyes. Ryu Chungwoo calmly gathered people and told them to move to the basement.

"You see what Raebin is holding? It's a controller, and he says he used it to confirm there's a door in the basement. Let's maintain order and proceed one by one..."

At the hopeful news, people quickly got to their feet. Even those who had been scattered in the distance with anxious faces lifted their heads. Among them, a person who had been squatting in a corner room peeked her head out.

"M-Moondae?"

"......"

I had heard similar calls several times, but this one was different. I had just heard that voice.

*Ah.*

...It was the fan I saw in the supply room. The graduate student. She escaped safely.

"Uh..."

"Hello. We saw each other earlier."

When our eyes met, I could see she was holding back tears. It seemed she hadn't told anyone she thought I had fallen to my death.

*Telling them would only create more fear.*

It's an easy thought to have, but actually keeping it to herself was no ordinary feat. I spoke in the gentlest voice I could manage. There was no time, but damn it, I had to do this.

"Ahyeon caught me, so I got out safely. Thank you for worrying."

"No..."

Her face soon became calm, perhaps trying to maintain composure. She either had strong mental fortitude from being a graduate student or a high threshold for distress. In any case, this wasn't the time for sentiment. I should learn from her.

I asked the graduate student as well. Since men were scarce, I decided to try collecting eyewitness accounts.

"Have you by any chance seen a man about this tall, wearing glasses? He'd look to be in his late 20s."

"They say he's handsome."

"......"

Even while pissed off, Lee Sejin chimed in. I wasn't sure if it was helpful. The graduate student looked blank, as if she had no idea. I figured as much.

"I-I think I know him!"

"......"

"When I was leaving from the front row, I fell and couldn't get to the escape route in time... Someone helped me up then!"

What?

The graduate student gestured with her hands.

"Like this, wearing a hat and glasses... tall, pale skin, and, uh, right, I think he was handsome!"

A perfect match.

I asked hurriedly.

"Did he evacuate?"

"Ah, no, the escape route was already blocked, so when the members called out, we were all about to climb up together..."

The graduate student thought he had gotten separated in the chaos and was somewhere around here. However, no such guy was here.

"Huh? Where did he go...?"

Damn it!

"When was that?"

"It was during the second commotion! When the whole stage ceiling fell... that time!"

"You came up the emergency staircase, right?"

"Y-Yes!"

I took a deep breath, tracing the route in my head. This person was in the front left section.

*She must have run towards the back to get out.*

But she fell, and Keundal helped her up. By then, the escape route was blocked, so everyone went to the emergency staircase.

*But Keundal isn't on the emergency staircase...*

He wasn't on the drone, and everything above the third floor had collapsed. In that case...

"......"

Ah, damn it.

I turned and ran down the hallway.

"Hey!"

"Someone's left behind! Please have everyone else go down to the basement through the emergency staircase!"

I shouted, then climbed over the wall. Instantly, Seon Ahyeon and Lee Sejin were flanking me.

"You're now..."

"I think I found him!"

I passed the waiting room and headed for the collapsed stage where the rope still hung, and I saw it. The audience seating beyond the stage.

"W-Why are you back here..."

I grabbed the rope, panting.

"He has to be here."

"......"

Keundal is still in the audience seating.

*And the reason we didn't run into him even though we were here for a while earlier is...*

Because he's in a state where he can't be met.

"However, he's probably collapsed."

"...!"

Why else would he suddenly disappear? He can't move.

*Isn't it that he can't use his body?*

To stop this mess, it's highly likely he went beyond just concentrating and turned into a Status Window himself again. That's why he couldn't follow them to the emergency staircase. He's still...

"He's definitely in the audience seats."

My head cleared. I grabbed the rope and wrapped it around my waist. I was about to go down and search meticulously... but wait.

*There's a problem.*

As my head cleared, the vast, dark audience seating came into view.

"...Ha."

This is an auditorium that seats a thousand people. In an area this large, would I have time to find him, wake him up, and discuss anything?

[00:19:35]

Within this time? And I have to climb back up here and get to the basement.

"......"

I'm fucked.

I knew I should be moving, but my strength left me. Just as I let go of the rope for a moment, a low voice came from beside me.

"Park Moondae, you're injured right now."

"Not really."

"No, I'll go down and come back quickly, so give it to me."

Lee Sejin held out his hand with a firm expression. Seon Ahyeon also approached without hesitation.

"No...! I-I've used a rope before, and I know the area below well... I'll go."

This is driving me crazy. The rope was already secure around my waist. I was debating whether to just throw myself down and hope for the best, when...

TAP, TAP.

"...!"

From the far end of the vast, dark space, a small sound came from a corner where a blue emergency exit light glowed.

"That's..."

It was the half-blocked place where we sent the drone.

Then, impossibly, a man's formal dress shoe emerged from the shadows, dusty but intact. A long leg followed, and finally, a face came into view as the person dusted off his hands.

"......"

Cheongryeo.

The man didn't look around the audience seats. He turned his gaze directly toward us on the collapsed stage, where we held our flashlights.

"......"

What... is this? Cheongryeo smiled faintly in the emergency light's glow and opened his mouth.

"Are you looking for someone?"

In his hand, he held a portable monitoring device that looked like it belonged to the facility staff.
