# Chapter 604

The sun had set completely. Stars and the moon hung over the outskirts of the MT filming location.

VTIC's Chaeyul, sitting beside me, asked casually.

"Are you watching the stars?"

"Yes."

"I knew it!"

He looked refreshed as he watched the stars with me, then his eyes widened in sudden memory.

"I'm getting deja vu or something... I think it's like that time on the hiking show! You know, when we drank cocoa."

*...VTIC and TeSTAR were on a show together before?*

*Ah.*

I realized a step too late. That story... no longer existed.

It was from the world the System created, where I was Ryu Gunwoo and debuted with the VTIC members through the LeTi survival show.

The group, Wish.

"......"

*Hiking, huh. I guess we did go on a show like that back then.*

We must have done all sorts of things to win the Grand Prize in just one year. It was plausible we went on every show imaginable, not caring about overexposing our image.

Jin Chaeyul, who looked a few years older than he did back then, turned to Park Moondae.

"I used to call you Gunwoo, right?"

"The situation called for it back then."

Chaeyul laughed shyly at my response.

"We're on the same team for this MT too, just like then! The Yellow Team!"

"......"

"Let's drop the formalities like we did then. Do you remember? We said we should speak casually!"

I remembered.

[Hmm, it'll be hard to call you Gunwoo when we go back... but would it be okay to still speak casually? Not as Moondae, but as Moondae!]

He had told me to speak casually to him too, but I never answered.

This time, I just smiled again.

"......"

Perhaps vaguely sensing my silence was a tacit refusal, Jin Chaeyul didn't press further.

He quickly recovered, however, and continued brightly.

"Anyway, today was really fun, wasn't it!"

"Yes."

Well... we did a lot of ridiculous things, but nothing that would be risky for an idol's career.

They were careful to adjust things so we didn't cross any lines.

*The production team is good at their job.*

*Those T-Holic guys are lucky with their staff.*I smacked my lips, glancing at the distant production team and the T-Holic members talking with them.

"It's been a really long time for me too, being on a variety show with so many seniors and juniors. Is it the same for you, Moondae?"

"Yes, it is."

"I hope we get another chance like this!"

*Yeah. That's not happening.*

*By then, the provocative effect will have worn off, and it'll just stress out the fans.*

Jin Chaeyul, having just said something with terrifying implications, continued to chatter, stepping on one landmine after another.

"Um, about our group chat."

*You mean the group chat you guys invited me to without asking.*

*And the one that went silent as soon as our comebacks overlapped, because that bastard Cheongryeo cracked down on it... why bring that up...*

"Um, would it be okay for me to say hello in there now? Since no one's posted in a while..."

"......"

"It's nice to see you all like this since we're having a comeback at the same time... but that part is a little disappointing."

I turned to look at him.

It was unexpected.

*So he noticed.*

Two top-tier groups of a similar weight class, for whom this comeback is crucial.

He seemed to have a premonition that releasing albums at the same time would inevitably create an awkward relationship between us.

*But... he found that disappointing?*

"We could ask how each other's doing sometimes! And make jokes... What do you think?"

I let out a small laugh.

"That sounds good."

"That's a relief!"

For a moment, Jin Chaeyul's face brightened.

"Umm. I didn't want to be on awkward terms with the TeSTAR members. We went through something special together, after all."

Wish.

Even though it must be as faint as a dream by now, he seemed to have attached his own meaning to that event.

After a moment of thought, he added something else.

"Um, it's not that we changed our comeback to overlap with you guys on purpose. We were planning to release it on this date even before our enlistment..."

Right.

*The only one who would have considered that when setting the comeback date would be Cheongryeo.*

I figured the other VTIC members would just know it as 'a comeback right after military discharge'.

So, I wasn't actually angry at them.

"We've connected like this, you know. I hope we can stay friends, keep in touch, and meet up sometimes."

"......"

I gave a small nod.

"Right, and you're already close with Cheongryeo, Moondae!"

*That's not true.*

I glanced back at Chaeyul with a strange look. He had already said his piece and was now gazing at the stars with a relieved expression.

It would be weird to say anything now, so I just turned my head back to the sky.

Even on the outskirts of Gyeonggi-do, the stars shone brightly, unlike in Seoul.

"Being like this... it reminds me of when you guys in TeSTAR first debuted. We met at a music show."

We did.

*Was that already about six years ago?*

Recalling it now, it felt like we had been through all sorts of things to get here.

In this short-lived entertainment world, especially the idol industry, both of us had managed to cling on tenaciously for several years without falling.

I felt a little amused for some reason and replied casually.

"...Though the situation's reversed this time."

"Huh?"

"Because you seniors are making your comeback after ours."

"Hah."

Jin Chaeyul looked slightly sheepish. He seemed to know they had the advantage.

So I said, "We don't plan on losing, though."

"...!"

Jin Chaeyul stared at me with wide eyes, then finally burst out laughing.

"I know. We were the same!"

As I nodded, Chaeyul flopped down on his back.

"And really... we prepared a lot this time!"

"......"

"I don't think VTIC will lose."

*Is that so.*

"TeSTAR is the same."

"Heh."

Jin Chaeyul didn't reply.

We quietly reaffirmed our fighting spirit.

"......"

"......"

A question suddenly occurred to me.

"Come to think of it."

"Yes!"

"By any chance, when did you seniors film your music video for this comeback?"

With your hairstyles and all, there was no way you would have had time to film it.

*Even if you wore wigs, there wouldn't have been enough time for the CG post-production.*

Jin Chaeyul gave a clear answer.

"Ah, before we enlisted! We'd already decided everything about our comeback back then."

"......"

*Those terrifying bastards.*

"Ah, TeSTAR's intro video was really cool."

"Thank you."

It was a good thing I persuaded Seon Ahyeon to release it.

I was scoffing inwardly.

Jin Chaeyul held out his hand.

"Let's promote without any regrets!"

"......"

"Ah. Was this something I heard from Jaehyun?"

*Don't talk about that guy.*

I shook the hand Chaeyul offered. He smiled and waved.

*...Not bad.*

I let out a small laugh, too.

"You can speak casually, senior. We agreed to."

"......"

A pleased Jin Chaeyul rambled flusteredly about how he mixes formal and informal speech anyway, even with people he's comfortable with. Then, as if he couldn't hold it in any longer, he exclaimed.

"Ugh, can I just ask one more time? Let's speak casually together!"

*Geez. He's persistent.*

"......"

"...!"

"But only in private."

"Of course!"

Jin Chaeyul grinned and nodded.

Then he stood up energetically.

"Ah, they're giving out drinks over there. I'll go get one, Moondae!"

"Got it."

I gave a light wave to him as he left, then turned my gaze back to the sky.

*Is it almost time for the release?*

The MV will be released, the album will be released, the music will be released...

And the performances will begin.

An intense promotion period, clashing fiercely with a powerful competitor, was about to unfold.

It would begin in earnest after this filming ended.

The broadcast of this footage would also be released around that time.

*...I should film this variety show more properly.*

I resolved once more and thought about the next event of this two-day, one-night MT.

*What's left now... is it the random play dance?*

That was the real deal.

I was thinking that and was about to get up to return to our room with Jin Chaeyul, who was holding the drinks...

"It's too early to go to sleep, isn't it?"

"......"

"The production team has prepared one more game for you!"

"Ooooh!"

A sudden announcement.

"......"

"Oh, it's a test of courage!"

"......"

"I don't know many scary stories... Do you know a lot, Moondae?"

No.

[Start!]

"Aaargh!!"

"Aaaaaah!"

...I almost got left behind with Sua from SoftSweet, but I want to make it clear we weren't last.

For reference, Cheongryeo was putting on a hell of an act at this time too.

"Ah, ghost stories."

No matter how I thought about it, he wasn't the type to be scared of ghosts, yet he perfectly played the prim model student trying to hide his panic.

"I don't know many scary stories... but I'll try to pick from the ones I've heard."

"Ooh~"

He made it just scary enough, with just enough substance to avoid being boring.

But he didn't shut Judan's mouth, who excitedly spouted TMI like 'the storyline is from a classic novel so the copyright has expired and I can say it with confidence'. That made me suspicious.

*He's only being careful about himself.*

I stared suspiciously at the guy who, alone among the VTIC members, was acting like an actor on an interview show, carefully curating his image.

*Did he feel nothing even after seeing my axe earlier?*

Could it be that he's trying to establish his own image while pushing the impact onto the other members?

For a choice made by a bastard who aims for maximum efficiency, there was something fishy about it.

*Hmm.*

"The winner is... unbelievable, it's Green Team again! Kim Raebin from Green Team ❬Safety First❭!"

"Waaaaah!"

I gave a gesture of praise to the ghost story machine, Kim Raebin, from off-camera. I ended the night's filming while carefully observing the VTIC members.

And the next day.

"To think the day would come when we'd do this with this lineup... The writer's mouth is practically at their ears right now."

"They said they brought in more cameras just for this game."

The long-awaited random dance challenge had arrived.

And here, I would finally realize Cheongryeo's scheme.

The writer for ❬T-Holic's Show Business❭ picked up a pen.

The alpha and omega, the flower and fruit of group idol filming, was about to begin. The random play dance.

Until now, the production team had been fiercely deliberating.

How could they make a random play dance that was different from other MT content?

In truth, the basic formula was simple.

[1. Play KPOP songs at random.]

[2. Anyone who knows the choreography runs to the center and dances.]

For typical idol content, one more rule was added to create tension.

[3. Everyone must dance, and those who can't are eliminated!]

There were variations, like 'only one person per team needs to come out', but such rules tended to become less intuitive and less fun the more complicated they got.

So, instead of touching this proven formula, the production team decided to add another element.

A Cyber audience!

They would live-stream the ❬Random Play Dance❭ portion of the MT to just 300 people online. Then they would collect their reactions and apply them to the subtitles.

They could even read out funny, adoring comments to get the idols to burst out laughing.

*It's okay if some team spoilers get leaked in the process.*

If anything, that would fuel the fans' anticipation!

The writer checked the equipment with a sharp eye.

On the connected tablet in front, real-time comments and small monitor screens began to appear.

[What is this]

[Are we connected?]

[OMG OMGㅠㅠ]

Swear words and a few sensitive terms were filtered out, but most comments poured in freely.

When the camera properly focused on the scene, they practically went wild.

The sight of the idols preparing naturally on the grass was shown clearly and up close!

[This is crazy]

"......"

[Wow this lineup looks like a composite photo]

[Moondaeㅠㅠㅠㅠㅠㅠㅠ Please let our Moondae do the POP☆CON dance]

[Please give lots of attention to baby main dancer Seong Harin AKA K-Pop fanatic]

Amidst the flood of all kinds of reactions from the cast's fans, regular viewers of T-Holic's variety shows, and people who had just applied for fun.

"Then let's begin!"

The random play dance began.
