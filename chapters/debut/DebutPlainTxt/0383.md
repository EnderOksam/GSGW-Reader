# Chapter 383 - The Position Battle, I

It was another filming day for LeTi's in-house survival program, ⟨Wise⟩.

The atmosphere had changed a bit.

*They're starting to get a sense of who's going to debut.*

I could tell just from the CEO's greeting before filming.

"You're doing great. Let's approach this more competitively than ever before."

"Yes!"

It wasn't his positivity that gave it away.

"Jaehyeon, be especially careful not to overdo your vocal practice."

"I will take note."

How specific was his advice?

How much thought did he put into his words?

People who'd started working early as trainees could naturally sense the change.

"Excuse me... Your piercing looks really cool today."

"...? Thank you for your generous words!"

Thanks to this, Kim Raebin, who had essentially been marked as the president's pick after being told he'd suit the agency well in every way, began to get more attention from the other contestants.

*It's kind of interesting that his experience here is so different from Idol Inc.*

And me.

"Are you ready? Let's see everything you've got on stage."

"Yes."

This was high praise, too.

The cameras were off right now. If I were going to be eliminated, he wouldn't be cruel enough to say that to me.

*Thank you for letting me know in advance that I passed.*

"Recording is starting~"

Filming started with the announcement of the winning and losing teams – the honors and inferior classes that the previous evaluation had divided us into.

"Phew..."

"Thank you!"

Joy and sorrow mingled.

However, the most cutthroat segment was scheduled for the next step.

Announcement of eliminated candidates.

They called me and the other guy in the in-jeopardy group up to the stage.

"Since you guys are already in the in-jeopardy group, only those who did better than the 14 people standing over there can survive. And that person..."

The president skillfully took his time and then spoke with a stern expression.

"Ryu Gunwoo."

There was silence.

"That's all."

The contestant who lost to Kim Raebin was eliminated from the show.

The atmosphere had cooled, but the president didn't seem to have any intention of relieving the situation with good words.

He said this to me too.

"The moment you find yourself in the in-jeopardy group again, you will not be able to avoid elimination. Work hard with all your might. This is your last chance."

If I ended up in the in-jeopardy group again, it meant I was done for.

"I'll keep that in mind."

I welcomed the warning.

Then, he wouldn't deliberately put me at a disadvantage to force me into the in-jeopardy group again. No matter how I looked at it, the boss didn't seem to want me to be eliminated.

*This is proof that the production team won't pull any shenanigans in the next performance mission.*

*Thank you so much.*

I bowed my head. And I checked the current number of people.

Even after the CEO had kicked out the guy who'd shown a bad attitude onstage just before...

*15 people.*

Since the number of contestants had actually increased compared to the first time, I figured more people would be eliminated during this performance, which would raise the tension.

The moment I thought about it...

"But every event and performance has a maximum capacity. ... There can't be more than 14 people."

*Well, look at that.*

"Therefore, when a member of the in-jeopardy group is brought back in, the participant with the lowest level of achievement so far among the existing members will be eliminated."

*Right now?*

Then, the president named several members of the inferior class, listed their pros and cons, and said this.

"Participant Park Jonghoon of the 'Inferior Class' has been eliminated."

"......"

Suddenly, another elimination was announced.

The person called stumbled at the shocking result, and the participants standing around covered their mouths.

*Way to build tension.*

"Please leave now. Participant Ryu Gunwoo, take his place."

"...Yes."

It seemed that this was why he sent the other guy from the in-jeopardy group away at once instead of softening the blow with kind words.

To keep the tension high.

*Looks like all that buzz has paid off for him.*

As he gained popularity for being cruel, he must have developed a desire to become even more cruel. It wasn't a very good thing.

*In the end, even if it's not Idol Inc., will the show continue to follow this trend?*

The trend of being so cruel that it broke the kids' spirits, all to make the show more exciting and intense for viewers.

*It leaves a bitter taste... Well, I've got nothing to lose here.*

I waited for the eliminated contestant to move away, then carefully stepped into the empty spot in the inferior class.

The guys standing next to me looked frozen, unsure whether to welcome me or feel sad. I kept my mouth shut and put on a similar expression.

*I don't want to get criticized for this.*

And when I turned my head, all the guys I'd been on the team with before were in the debut group. Chaeyul and Sino's teams had won too.

I nodded inwardly.

*Not a bad lineup.*

It felt like questions such as, "Weren't you worried about being the only one in the inferior class?" would come up in my personal interview. That would get me more screen time.

"Thank you for your hard work."

After the bloody ranking and announcement of eliminated candidates, the lights went out on the stage. Our microphones were off, too.

"Hyung, I'm glad."

But the cameras were still running, and the quick-witted bastard immediately spoke up for their benefit.

Cheongryeo.

"Hyung!"

"Welcome back!"

I acted as though I was happy to see the other guys running up with him. I spoke to Cheongryeo in a low voice.

– *The way they're running the show is pretty cruel. I heard it wasn't done this way before.*

– *That's right. A little more than before... The way the president expresses himself has become less dignified. The stakes are higher this time.*

And he smiled and put his arm around me as if encouraging me.

– *The audience is being well fed. Right?*

*...*

He had an excellent knack for making his ideas sound like trash.

Anyway, the production team probably thought they had put too much pressure on the participants, so they lightened the mood a bit in the next content.

An event followed to adjust the pace, softening the mood and offering viewers a different kind of excitement.

The last time an event like this happened before a performance, I was excluded because I was in the in-jeopardy group and only did housework.

*Last time it was a public service advertisement... What is it this time?*

It was a photoshoot for the album concepts.

There were seven concepts.

"Now, let's discuss and choose the concept you want!"

They fought to secure the concepts they wanted, sometimes conceding and sometimes staking a claim. Since many of them already knew each other, they were pushing concepts onto one another under the guise of recommendations here and there.

What about me?

"I'm fine with anything. I haven't tried any of these before, so I don't have any likes or dislikes. Everything looks interesting and good."

"Oh, oh! If hyung says that..."

How many photo concepts has Park Moondae from TeSTAR taken so far? Was there really a need to be picky here?

It didn't matter what I got. All I had to do was coolly give in and take good pictures.

However...

"Yes! Show your teeth a little more."

"......"

I... I didn't know that the concept with the keyword 'Tooth' meant that it would be a vampire concept.

*Who was the bastard who put such an irrational concept in a survival show for people who hadn't even debuted yet?* I understood why VTIC disbanded the first time around.

Still, thanks to my experience, the photoshoot didn't turn into a disaster, and the reviews were very good.

The interview went well too.

"I used to be on the other side of the camera. It feels different now that I'm the subject. Still, I tried to remember my experience photographing other people... and did my best."

I just told them, "I was a college student in the photography club" and this and that.

Well, wasn't this a fresh and good story?

It might rub some people the wrong way, but that didn't mean I could say something crazy like "Haha, I'm tired of filming this kind of thing," so I chose the next best option instead.

"I think the photos will all turn out great. Thank you all for your hard work. Filming today was great!"

"Wow!"

Anyway, the event ended with a few compliments and a small amount of warmth restored... That night, the performance mission hit hard.

*It's merciless.*

"Contestants of ⟨Wise⟩. This is already the 4th performance. You'll have to do better than ever. The theme this time is..."

By now, the participants tensed up as if they had indigestion every time they were called to the stage.

"It will be a 'position battle.'"

"...!"

"A position battle...?"

As expected, the big picture remained the same. The challenges were still largely following what I'd heard from Cheongryeo.

Here's what the position battle meant.

– The next content is a group performance of a new song, and there is a preliminary competition to decide which parts the participants will get.

And the positions to be contested were revealed on the electronic display board.

And the president called the honors class.

"After looking at the pictorials you took, an undisclosed evaluation panel conducted a vote. The honors class has the authority to select parts first, in descending order of votes."

"Ah, ah..."

Some turned pale, while others brightened. The first name was called.

It was obvious who would be called.

"Shin Jaehyeon."

The guy stood up, bowed his head once in humility, and immediately moved on.

Next was Cha Eugene.

The members who were called individually took the remaining seats one by one after much deliberation.

And looking at all the positions filled, the president nodded and spoke.

"Now, each of you in the honors class has full authority to set the stage for this position competition."

"...!"

"Which song to choose, what kind of performance to put on – everything is up to you."

In short, it meant, 'Do whatever you want on stage.'

And at this time, the seven participants from the inferior class were put on the stage created by the honors class as challengers.

"Do everything you can to steal their stage."

Since there was a total of 14 participants, wouldn't it make sense if we paired against each other one-on-one?

No.

"You can apply for the part you want no matter how many other applicants there are. However..."

No matter how many people competed for a part... There was only one winner.

– *Only the person who wins can join the performance of the new song.*

The nervous contestants looked around at one another as they looked over the parts.

*They haven't even told us what roles we'll get, or what the new song is, anyway.*

In the end, the most important thing was who I competed against.

Of course, here too, the order was the same as the pictorial vote.

"The contestant who will be the first to choose a position in the inferior class... Ryu Gunwoo."

And number 1 was me.

*Oh.*

*What was I so surprised at, Ryu Gunwoo?* It would be even weirder if I couldn't beat young guys who hadn't even debuted yet, when I had five years of experience under my belt.

*If I couldn't do it, I would have had my head smashed by a sledgehammer.*

Instead, I bowed once and looked at the president.

"Please come out and choose your part."

*Okay. I got it.*

I quickly thought it through.

*If the positions are ranked alphabetically, the criteria are...*

*Did the letter rankings symbolize the length, level of difficulty, or order of appearance?*

*But Cheongryeo chose Dance A... or whatever.*

*In that case, the probability that the vocalist position would have more impactful parts if I chose A instead of B was slightly higher.*

*Still.*

I moved my feet without hesitation.

And stopped at the spot I'd chosen.

"...!!"

There was one obvious factor I needed to consider.

*My competitor.*

That was because the guy in the honors class who was the best singer here besides me chose this position as well.

"Contestant Ryu Gunwoo from the inferior class will compete with Choi Taejun from the honors class for 'Vocal B'."

Choi Taejun. His stage name upon debut was Beehan.

How did I know?

*Because I was the one who sold his fancams.*

Also, it was because he was the former main vocalist of VTIC who got kicked out because of that club scandal.

After renewing his contract, he was let go due to the incident. However, since he was the main vocalist of a first-tier idol group for 7 years until then, he wasn't an ordinary guy.

So if I chose this guy...

*It will definitely look like I'm taking on a challenge.*

And if I won, the narrative of this survival contestant, Ryu Gunwoo, would gain momentum once more.

*We're moving past the halfway point of the program. I need to take control of the flow.*

I politely bowed my head to the former main vocalist of VTIC.

"I'm looking forward to it."

"...Um, yeah."

The guy nodded his head hesitantly... Well, should I call this guy Club Main Vocal or Former Main Vocal? Anyway, he tried to smile.

I stood next to him.

And I watched the situation unfold slowly. The members of the inferior class chose their parts one by one.

"I will do Vocal A...!"

It may have been obvious, but even though they could apply for any part without a limit, everyone chose a part with just one member from the honors class, without overlapping.

*Everyone's calculating the odds.*

Even so, there was one part that no one dared pick, despite only one person standing there, perhaps because they felt it would be suicidal.

"There are currently no challengers for Dance A."

Because Shin Jaehyeon was the one in 'Dance A.'

*They know his skills very well.*

Since some were trainees at the same agency, they knew each other well and had enough information to know when they had no chance of winning.

*Hmm.*

I saw Shin Jaehyeon standing alone in front of the position display board that read 'Dance A.'

And this point was the difference from the flow that Cheongryeo talked to me about in advance.

– *The original format was one person from the 'honors class' battling one person from the 'inferior class'. A one-on-one match.*

Originally, no one was supposed to win by default.

And in my opinion, in a survival stage like this, they might abandon the one-on-one format, but they wouldn't let anyone advance unchallenged.

So, there must be other elements at play.

– *Something's different this time.*

What could it be?

"Of course, it won't end with just this. There will be other competitors."

"Ah, ah..."

"No way."

"The new contestants will be... A supplementary group made up of viewers!"

"...!!"

*Okay.* He meant the new participants they'd recruited with an advertisement at the end of the third episode.

Seeing the participants reacting softly without realizing it, the president spoke as if making a statement.

"Please come out."

*Pang!*

With a small burst of fireworks, ominous background music played and another electronic display board opened.

*They sure turned up quickly.*

Well, if they'd delayed it any longer, the participants' fan bases would have become entrenched, and the supplementary group would've felt like nothing more than an obstacle.

I nodded inwardly and clasped my hands behind my back, pretending to listen to what the president said.

"Don't think of them as the same as the last supplementary group. These participants have already seen how harsh the program can be, and they were selected from a larger pool of applicants across the country."

He sure made the people who'd been selected in less than a week sound impressive.

Even if he said it like that, the chances of anyone really impressive showing up were extremely low.

*I guess I'll just check their stats and move on.*

Unimpressed, I looked at the figures appearing beyond the dry ice.

*Seven people again, and no one really stands out... Wait a minute.*
