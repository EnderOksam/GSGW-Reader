# Chapter 440

Almost five years had passed since our debut. By now, everyone had their own pattern for preparing for award ceremonies and year-end music programs.

1. The type that prepares with composure

This type has peace of mind from years of experience but works their body hard. Or perhaps, they have that peace of mind because they work their body so hard.

"Hoo... this next move, I think it'll be okay... to take a bit more time to master it."

"Okay~ Then let's skip it and make fixing B-part our goal for today. Chungwoo, is that okay?"

"Yeah. Should we extend our practice time a bit then?"

"Let's do that."

The majority belonged to this group. Next.

2. The type that prepares anxiously

This type is full of worry, concern, and anxiety about making mistakes. They fall into anguish whenever their body is at rest, contemplating various possibilities.

"Are we taking more time to learn it because of me? No, don't answer. Either way, I just need to work hard. Right, since they gave me a lot of time..."

"Hyung, in my opinion, if we allocate that much time to the bridge choreography, the key point of the dance will shift there! If that happens, my pre-arranged composition might sound unnatural..."

"Stop."

If you cut them off at the right moment, they get influenced by the guys from type 1 and calm down.

"Don't think about anything when you're resting."

"But..."

"Don't."

I made sure the guys' break time was an actual break.

And the next type, which had the fewest members...

3. The type that plays around with composure

[You know it too, hyung, right? Cramming is basically a form of cheating.]

"Get it?"

"Heehee!"

One guy fell into this category. He practiced because his members told him to, but his moves and gestures were already amazing enough to go on stage as is, aside from synchronizing the group dance.

"Your original skill is what's important!"

"...Does that mean it's pointless for people who aren't good at it to practice?"

"Nope! Effort will become your original skill."

"Ahem."

He sure ran his mouth well.

Anyway, with these guys gathered together, our year-end preparations rolled along smoothly, as always, despite some creaks and groans.

"Stop it!"

"Kim Raebin, eat this!"

I dodged a dried sweet potato Cha Eugene threw at Kim Raebin. Then, I replayed the practice video we just filmed. It looked good.

"...Hmm."

To be honest, if I continued this classification, there was one last number.

Number 4.

The type that's anxious yet still plays around.

However, no one like that was in this group. It was a situation everyone experienced at least once while preparing for exams, but it was a serious injury that naturally healed once you started getting paid. In other words, it did not apply to us.

However, it did apply to a part of me in a different, unpaid field. A situation where I was anxious but could do nothing about it.

*Don't tell me getting a national medal is the next state abnormality.*

[Yes?]

On a weekend night after practice, I muttered to myself in the bathtub while drinking a cold non-alcoholic beer. Of course, I was not acting like a madman all by myself. I held a waterproof smartphone.

"Isn't it weird that I'm this obsessed with a medal that doesn't even grant an exemption from military service, just a deferment for a few years?"

[I told you it's not! Hyung, it's really not!]

Keundal practically screamed from the other side of the phone. It had been a while since we communicated by voice, and it certainly made me feel the difference from the chat pop-ups. Anyway, his opinion was consistent. He said that no matter how much he searched, he could not find any strange signs on me.

And the reason I was obsessed with the national medal was...

[...Everyone hates the idea of going to the military, you know.]

"......"

[Especially as the date gets closer.]

That was true. Since I had to go twice, it was like an unavoidable biological reaction, becoming more obsessed as the time approached. The one who has been through it knows better how shitty it is.

But still.

"What does a guy who doesn't have to go anymore know so much about it?"

[I'm sorry.]

Sorry for what? You swapped into an older body, so getting a military exemption was the only way it balanced out. I also knew that what he knew well was not the military.

"No, you're just confident because you've examined my condition so thoroughly."

[Yes! That's it!]

Keundal explained without taking a breath.

[I really immersed myself to the point where I almost became another Status Window and thoroughly combed through everything, but there are no traces of the System 1/4 on you, hyung! Really!]

"Yeah, I get it."

You are going to bring the walls down. I lowered the call volume and acknowledged it.

"Then what about a mission failure?"

[...I told you, it's not that.]

I said I get it.

After Keundal said he would perform regular check-ups on my status window, almost as if he were rapping, I decided not to bring up the topic anymore.

*If I get a bad feeling like last time, I'll have to run somewhere with no people, no matter what.*

After all that passed, the exciting year-end settlement period finally arrived.

"Moondae Moondae~ Our third-quarter settlement payment date is December 20th, right?"

"Yeah."

No, not that kind of settlement. I meant the period when everything we had done over the past year was evaluated by the quantitative measure of our results and the qualitative measure of our recognition.

Right. It was finally the awards season.

"The Artist of the Year award goes to... TeSTAR!"

"Congratulations!"

"The Album Grand Prize, it's TeSTAR."

"The final Grand Prize, Artist of the Year is... Congratulations, TeSTAR."

POW! POW! POW!

Countless fireworks, drumrolls, announcements, and cheers erupted, with TeSTAR's representative song of the year playing as background music.

"Thank you! We'll work even harder!"

"We're having a comeback soon!"

Screams, applause, and cheers followed.

At every award ceremony we attended, we cleanly took home one Grand Prize. This was because the company did not send us to ceremonies that would not give us a Grand Prize.

*Hmm, it's efficient, I'll give them that.*

Back at the company, while moving to take promotional photos with the trophies, we received a string of congratulations from our colleagues.

"Hyung! Whoa~"

Spacer, the newly debuted rookie girl group, the A&R team staff, the management team leader... Even Mirynay.

"Congratulations!"

"Congratulations..."

This was an amazing industry where a competitor could look at you with eyes that said 'I want to smash your head in and take that Grand Prize trophy for myself' but still offer congratulations with their mouth.

"...That junior, Seong Harin? Does she hate us?"

"Probably."

Anyway, the year-end was quite fun, with no particular incidents. Who would hate receiving an award for doing well? Our special stage was also as well-received as the effort we put into it.

[TeSTAR's Saber stage was insane.]

[What do you call that? A sword dance? I almost drooled watching it]

[TeSTAR's T1A stage is currently fixed on the real-time popular videos list]

It was the taste of our prime, like the end of all hardships and adversity.

Of course, this did not mean we received the Grand Prize in every category. The digital music award went to Yeongrin. The album award went to VTIC.

Even after VTIC went to the military, this structure was still solid because their Leader remained and attended the award ceremony.

"Thank you."

Cheongryeo came to the most prestigious year-end award ceremony not in a stage outfit, but in a navy blue semi-formal suit for the red carpet.

"I want to give all the congratulations and the joy of this award to my grateful members who are faithfully carrying out their given duties."

The guy did not look the least bit awkward or rushed about attending alone. Instead, as always, he seemed perfectly composed and flawless, without a hint of nervousness.

And he also dropped this hint.

"We will show you a side you can look forward to next year as well."

"WAAAAAH!"

"Thank you."

Applause rang out for Cheongryeo as he slightly bowed his head. Keun Sejin, who had been smiling respectfully, trusted the grand BGM and whispered.

"He's saying he's going to release a solo album, right?"

"Yeah."

*It's going to be another dogfight.*

Moreover, knowing that guy, I would bet this 'Colorful K-Pop Star Award' that he would also subtly slip in a song that hinted at a full VTIC comeback or one that could be arranged into a group song later.

I glanced at the clumsily designed award plaque on the table. It was a category suddenly created this year to appease a sponsor. We received it without any reason or corresponding results.

*They should have subdivided the rookie awards instead of wasting time creating something like this.*

Still, it would probably be gone next year, so did that not make it rare?

"That's quite an award. Congratulations."

"Yes."

As it happened, Cheongryeo, whom I met backstage, also praised this award. I quietly handed the plaque to a staff member who was collecting the awards. Then Cheongryeo, who had been watching, spoke. He said something out of the blue.

"Haven't you heard some good news?"

What?

"Did your pet dog have puppies, sunbaenim?"

"Hmm, hoobaenim, did you happen to doze off during science class?"

It meant, 'What kind of nonsense are you spouting?' I felt the same way.

I stared at the guy, signaling him to speak properly. Cheongryeo gazed at me for a moment, then smiled slightly.

"Hmm, I guess it doesn't matter."

"Yes?"

"No, I just meant that if you get some good news, I'd like you to let me know too."

"Why?"

"Because I'm curious."

The guy left those words behind, got in his car, and left the award ceremony. Then he sent a short video via text, along with a message.

[Happy birthday. (video)]

"......"

The video was about some dog receiving a new cushion to replace its ruined one.

*What does that mean?*

Of course, I figured he would just find it amusing if I asked, so I let it be. By now, he must have learned that if he did anything to screw me over, he would get screwed over himself.

Anyway, leaving behind the numerous temporary awards, the last music awards of the year came to an end. All that was left was...

"We're having a comeback!!"

That's right. A new year, a new promotion period. We would make our comeback this coming January, at a time when all our results could be fully reflected.

And we planned to use a method with much higher visibility and more time than a regular music show.

Which was.

"We're going to have our comeback at the January awards ceremony."

"Yeeeaaah!"

Even though they already knew, their reaction was fiery. They were clearly excited about the comeback after such a long time.

In fact, we had received an offer from the head office that was practically pressure to have our comeback at ToneA in December, since it was their own awards ceremony. However, we shot down that opinion.

"It was a good thing we pushed back, saying we couldn't guarantee the stage quality by then!"

"It was an accurate judgment!"

"A Happy New Year Comeback? It's the best!"

They seemed a bit overly excited, but the sight of these guys, already drunk on the taste of victory and humming as they practiced all night, was quite something.

"...Good. Yeah, I have a feeling it's going to go well no matter what!"

"Hyung! I have a jinx about that!"

"......"

"I'm kidding."

"Hey!"

And so, on Monday, January 9th, right before our comeback, we were scheduled to appear on a late-night radio show to commemorate the new year. Of course, it was all a calculated move.

"It's not real promotion, more like a form of viral marketing."

"I like that it's not overly bold but has a slight element of unpredictability!"

Since talk was the main content, we could say just as much as we wanted to. We were not at a point in our careers where we would get nervous on the radio anymore. We also wanted to give the fans the fun of guessing and speculating about what kind of album would be released.

Because it was a limited medium, there was no worry that all the promotional effect would be consumed there.

*It's a kind of eve of the festival.*

I smiled and greeted everyone in the media room in front of the radio booth.

"We'll be in your care."

"Waaah! Yes!"

The writers and staff welcomed the group more warmly than I expected, and everything was going smoothly.

"TeSTAR, you're scheduled to go on in 16 minutes!"

"Yep!"

We sat on a sofa set up on the periphery and began to wait. Since it was a surprise appearance, we planned to make it feel like we were barging in.

"Moondae, this..."

"Ah. Thanks."

I squeezed the stress ball Seon Ahyeon handed me. I slowly blinked my eyes.

*This waiting time is when there's the least to do.*

I meant this moment when you were doing nothing, yet you had to stay focused so you did not miss the timing by getting lost in thought. The only thing to do was to organize my thoughts one more time about what to say on the radio.

Blink.

*First, greet everyone, then manage the audio so we don't talk over each other...*

Blink.

*Check on Kim Raebin so he doesn't get too excited and spoil too much about the song.*

Blink.

*And when we say our final goodbyes, Bae Sejin...*

Blink.

I saw a dark ceiling. I closed and opened my eyes again.

Blink.

It was the same.

"......"

Wait, it was dark?

"......"

I pushed myself up and realized I had been lying down.

*What?*

It was a bed. No, a bed?

*What is this.*

I fumbled with my hands and got off the bed.

*The light.*

CLICK.

I pressed the button on the wall, and the light came on. The scene of a comfortable bedroom was revealed.

"...!"

A room with a TeSTAR poster on the wall.

*The layout looks familiar, no, first, the more important thing to think about is... I just suddenly woke up in a different place.*

"Hoo..."

I took a deep breath. Honestly, I had been through so much at this point that I thought I would not be surprised no matter what crazy thing happened.

*But still, how can something like this happen without any warning signs?*

There was no pop-up, no Status Window, not even any psychological premonition!

*Still, first, the Status Window...*

It was then.

[Hyung!]

"Ugh."

A sound echoed in my head. A vibration-like sound carrying meaning.

*Vibration?*

I realized that I had already experienced this vibration that conveyed meaning. It was back when Keundal, who was studying for the civil service exam in Ryu Gunwoo's body, would sometimes lose control and I would use the body.

"...No way."

Hey, this could not be. I suppressed a groan and thought that I wanted to reply to this voice in my head.

And then.

WHOOSH!

A strange calculation and process flashed through my mind in an instant. Then a pop-up appeared before my eyes.

[______]

What is this.

[What is this_]

Stop, I get it.

*This is a chat pop-up.*

The kind Keundal used to send me, and that meant one thing.

"Damn it."

I realized my voice sounded familiar in a different way. I picked up my smartphone and checked my face with the front camera.

"......"

*It really is Ryu Gunwoo, fuck...*

"Damn it."

[Hyung? Don't tell me you're in my body right now?]

"Yes."

Hey, you said there was no System left in me! That last thought was also sent as a pop-up chat, it seemed, but I ignored that for now.

*Let's think, what can I do? 'Connect'. Keundal can also access Park Moondae's Status Window! While sharing his vision.*

I did not know if I could do it, but I had to try.

*Would... the way I call up the Status Window work?*

I focused my thoughts.

Park Moondae, Status Window, System, access, connect... Connect.

[-CONNECT-]

A vision and senses surfaced in my mind. They were the senses of Park Moondae, who was on standby for the radio show. It was true that I could use the sense sharing Keundal had talked about.

"Hoo..."

My relief that it had worked was fleeting. A hologram popped up in the shared vision of Park Moondae. A familiar Status Window pop-up.

[Sudden!]

State Abnormality Mission Failure: Revert to Original State

[Return everything to its original state as if it never happened]

: Time remaining 23:59:59 (1 day)

"......"

[I'm so sorry, I don't know what's happening! All it says here is something about a mission failure...]

And I instinctively realized what was happening.

Ah, fuck.

"It was on you."

[Yes??]

"It was on you."

This damn System 1/4 state abnormality did not trigger on me, but on that guy, and it had to be a body swap ten minutes before a radio show standby!

[Aaaaargh!]

That was the beginning.
