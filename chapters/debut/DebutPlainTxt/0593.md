# Chapter 593

Just this situation.

Do you know the feeling that everything would be okay if you could just escape this one situation, even by an inch?

Back when Keundal was still Park Moondae, he often had such thoughts while sitting idly on the worn-out mattress of his moldy studio apartment.

The what-ifs.

If he had parents.

If he had a family to live with.

If he had someone to talk to.

The shrinking scope of "family" eventually became just 'if there was someone'.

He used to imagine what he would do if that were the case.

Of course, the motivation and energy for such meaningless thoughts eventually faded and disappeared entirely.

Perhaps that was why he only realized it now.

*Even if the situation changes, I'm still the same.*

It was strange.

He knew the Status Window that made him help someone was gone, and the pop-up that let him talk to his hyung had vanished.

But wasn't his current environment a world of difference from when he was a dazed 20-year-old orphan in that old studio?

He now had a respectable job and a home. He was no longer the Park Moondae who knew nothing.

But even with such a dramatic change, he felt no surge of energy or desire to be proactive.

It was strange. Wasn't his environment one where he should be okay?

Why couldn't he be? Why was he sitting alone in his apartment, feeling the same as he did in the past?

In the end, until the disappearance incident, he had gradually become as lethargic as before, unable to even convince himself.

Alone.

But now, someone was in his house.

"Are you eating japchae?"

"Ah... huh? Yes!"

"Good, then."

Keundal stared blankly as his hyung boiled the glass noodles.

TeSTAR's Park Moondae.

His hyung handed him a bundle of signed TeSTAR merchandise and albums. Some members had even written letters. He had even gone grocery shopping.

*It must be his precious vacation!*

But before Keundal could feel sorry, his hyung moved quickly.

He cooked holiday food, urged Keundal to unbox the merchandise, and laughed at his reactions as he opened the TeSTAR goods.

"I'm turning on the TV."

"Ah, yes!"

They ate, chatted while watching a program, and discussed everyday topics.

"Did the leave of absence you submitted get processed?"

"Ah, yes! I'm on a one-year leave starting today. It's called a self-development leave..."

As they shared such trivial stories, Keundal realized he was sitting comfortably on the sofa.

*Ah.*

He had felt this before.

It was when he had the civil servant status abnormality.

The feeling was similar to when his hyung, the Status Window, would return for one day a year to check on him while he studied for the exam.

The difference was that his hyung now had a physical body. Seeing him today didn't mean waiting a whole year.

[I think I'll... probably stay at this house for a few more days before going back up.]

[...Pardon?]

[Let's rest together.]

His hyung settled in as naturally as if visiting his family home.

And now, his hyung treated him to a holiday meal and was clearing the table.

"Oh, oh, I'll get it!"

"It's fine."

His hyung waved his hand, saying he was well-rested. He neatly finished cleaning up and then suggested they go out for ice cream.

They sat in front of the TV with ice cream, and his hyung asked casually.

"Anything fun to watch these days?"

"Ah..."

Keundal, who had been swept along until now, was slightly flustered.

Honestly, he hadn't done anything with genuine enthusiasm this time either.

He had spent all his time after the TeSTAR concert dealing with his unauthorized absence from work.

He racked his brain and finally recalled one thing.

"That... ah! On WeTube."

A video that soothes the soul!

He sometimes watched it before bed.

Keundal quickly used the remote to open WeTube on the smart TV.

However, the first recommended video that popped up was a well-produced stage performance.

But it was an idol group he didn't know.

*Huh?*

Keundal blinked, then soon realized.

He had stumbled upon this idol group by chance and remembered watching them intently. The long watch time must have triggered the recommendation.

"Ah, that group debuted around the same time as 'Halo Heim'... They aren't as popular, but their songs are good and they work hard!"

He felt a steady gaze on him.

"Not TeSTAR?"

"Ack."

"......"

"Just kidding."

His hyung chuckled.

"Do whatever you like. Don't worry about what anyone else thinks."

The unspoken words seemed to be, 'even if that someone is me'.

"......"

Ah.

Keundal suddenly froze.

The attitude that he wouldn't be disappointed or upset no matter what Keundal did.

A feeling suddenly surged through him. He felt his hyung was just putting up with him out of pity because they happened to form a connection.

*Why am I like this.*

He was embarrassed.

But as if sensing it, his hyung continued speaking casually.

"Are you planning to suddenly ignore me if I quit TeSTAR?"

"...! Of course not!"

"See?"

"......"

"So you should also support whoever you want without worrying about it."

Slowly, Keundal properly understood his words.

It didn't matter whether he was a fan of TeSTAR or not.

Because.

"In the first place, you didn't even know me when I was an idol, so why worry about something like that?"

"......"

Keundal nodded his head vigorously.

While doing so, he mumbled.

"...Still, I think I'll keep cheering for TeSTAR..."

Yeah, I know.

TeSTAR's Park Moondae burst out laughing.

"Isn't the group half your creation anyway?"

"What do you mean I created half of it, hyung!"

"You made me debut."

Park Moondae pointed at himself seriously.

"As the Main Vocalist, I am the voice of TeSTAR."

"Ack! Hyung, why are you suddenly like this!"

While shouting that it didn't suit him, Keundal fiddled with the WeTube controls.

His heart felt light.

Then, an equally light question came back.

"So... what should we do tomorrow?"

A question about the near future.

Tomorrow.

He was surprised at how he instantly thought of numerous answers.

"......"

So it was something that could be done this easily.

Just by having an older person he could talk to freely, who was comfortable, friendly, and reliable, it was this...

*There were things I wanted to do...*

Feeling a lump in his throat, he hurriedly composed his voice.

"Then... um, how about skiing?"

"......"

His hyung accepted the proposal on the spot and immediately finished making reservations.

And so, in the late early spring, the two of them tumbled a few times and had a blast skiing at a quiet ski resort.

"Wow! Hyung, you're really good!?"

"It's a spoiler for the next variety show, but we go skiing there too."

"What! In Part 2?"

They talked about whatever they wanted, exchanging words freely.

"Hyung, up for some night skiing?"

"Let's go to the intermediate slope."

Keundal became more and more comfortable and happy.

Even when they returned home from the ski resort, he no longer hesitated to suggest doing other things.

He no longer felt self-conscious when talking about TeSTAR.

"Ah, the new season of ❬Doll Hunter❭... it's released tomorrow at 12, right!"

"......"

"We have to watch it live then!"

"What do you mean live on Netplus... well, sure."

And so, the next day.

The two sat in the living room, ready to watch the drama with leftover mung bean pancakes and kimchi stew made with japchae.

*...It's warm.*

The satisfying peace and stability made the tip of his nose tingle.

Keundal blinked a few times to keep from crying, then looked at the screen.

"Pressing play."

"Yes!"

It was a normal day, spent with family on a day off.

For reference, a similar situation was unfolding at the same time in San Diego, USA.

***

8 PM.

"Todo estaba muy bueno!"

The large family's dinner table, after a long meal, bustled with the younger brother's guests as well.

*It's been a while since I've seen this, huh?*

Cha Eugene's older brother whistled as he cleared the dishes with his younger siblings.

His hyungs and sisters had all left this large family home to live independently as soon as they graduated high school.

However, when they heard the second son, the first to leave, was coming, everyone except his younger sister, who was in the middle of her semester, gathered at the house.

Cha Eugene's declaration of independence had been that shocking.

[I'm going to Korea.]

"......"

[I'm going to be a K-POP singer there.]

[Did you get hit in the head with a football?]

This family was energetic and strong-willed.

Among them, his young brother, who had a strangely good intuition, eventually made his words a reality.

To think he would return this successful.

*We even joked about passing down the surf shop to him if he came back a failure.*

They had said they should welcome him as warmly as the prodigal son in the Bible.

Stroking his chin, Cha Eugene's brother turned his gaze to Kim Raebin, who struggled on the opposite side to help clear the dishes.

*The fact that Eugene made many good colleagues must have certainly influenced his success.*

Meeting them more closely than when they had visited with a camera crew a few years ago, they were still as thoughtful and full of personality as ever.

*These guys are trustworthy.*

It was certain, seeing how much his intuitive younger brother liked them.

He skillfully took the plates from Kim Raebin and greeted him with a grin, speaking in Korean that was even more fluent than Cha Eugene's.

"Thank you for taking care of our mischievous Eugene for the past few years."

Kim Raebin was slightly flustered.

"Ah, not at all. I'm the one who receives help from Cha... Cha Eugene!"

"Heehee!"

"......"

That Cha Eugene, who walked by laughing, was so annoying.

However, he couldn't bring himself to grab the guy by the collar in a place where Cha Eugene's siblings, parents, and grandmother were present.

Especially not when that grandmother was offering him a snack.

"Toma unos bocadillos, mi dulce!"

"Th... Gracias!"

Beside him, Cha Eugene's brother passed by with a wink.

*I'm... overeating...*

Feeling a cold sweat, Kim Raebin accepted the cinnamon churro and slowly walked away.

Still, he was spending his vacation in San Diego moving as much as he ate for the past few days.

[It's fine to surf during the day! Kim Raebin, you try too!]

[Uwaaah!]

...He had learned to surf, too.

*To think that even the dogs in Cha Eugene's family can surf!*

He was a bit nervous among the three families, who were as boisterous as if they had always been one big family, but as someone who grew up in a rural village with many neighbors, he adapted steadily.

For reference, Ryu Chungwoo had adapted right away from the first day and was now at the point where his Spanish skills were improving.

*As expected!*

The leader is different, Kim Raebin thought with renewed admiration, then suddenly felt a pang of regret.

*It would have been nice if the other hyungs had come too.*

It was great, but it felt a little empty.

After all, didn't a certain portion of the gratitude he received from this family belong to his hyungs?

*Because we're a group.*

Kim Raebin nodded inwardly.

He too was in a state where his group spirit was at its MAX after seeing Seon Ahyeon's meticulously arranged TeSTAR merchandise display.

But at the same time, he recalled Bae Sejin, who had been flustered by Cha Eugene's unannounced video call yesterday, clutching a Spanish translator app.

[S-So... yes. Hola? Me alegro de verte...]

[Ohhh!]

The cheering Cha Eugene was truly annoying.

...It seemed like enjoying this time without Cha Eugene would make for a more satisfying vacation.

He nodded, took a bite of his churro, and continued writing in his journal.

Jotting things down in his spare time would help with musical ideas when he looked back on it one day.

Of course, even if it didn't, it had become a memory.

[...so Grandpa and Grandma boarded the cruise, and I challenged snorkeling with Chungwoo-hyung. Hyung skillfully navigated the currents without excessively touching the marine life...]

"Raebin-ah, it's starting!"

"Ah, yes!"

He quickly closed the journal and ran out.

On the TV, Netplus was open to the new season of ❬Doll Hunter❭.

[Have you guys seen the other seasons?]

[Of course! Eugene talked about it so much that we watched it as soon as it was released.]

Kim Raebin sat down next to Ryu Chungwoo to get a spot. Next to him, Cha Eugene sat on the rug below the sofa.

"......"

In that moment, Kim Raebin felt a sense of deja vu from this arrangement.

[I'm playing it.]

"......"

*Ah.*

STier's final activity.

He thought the setup was similar to when they watched their last music video.

Of course, that was in a much smaller place, STier's last dorm in Korea.

Kim Raebin, Ryu Chungwoo, Cha Eugene.

It happened because the number of people matched.

"......"

Kim Raebin turned his head and realized that Ryu Chungwoo had already glanced over him and Cha Eugene.

"...!"

"What's wrong?"

"...I was just looking around the living room!"

Ryu Chungwoo smiled and turned his head back.

But Kim Raebin thought to himself.

Perhaps Ryu Chungwoo had recalled the memory of that time much earlier than he did.

Almost reflexively.

"......"

Kim Raebin had mostly digested his memories of STier while releasing . Now, it just remained in his head like a unique event he had experienced.

Perhaps Cha Eugene had done the same through the Water Bomb stage.

However...

*Did Chungwoo-hyung have such an opportunity?*

Kim Raebin thought deeply. Something that fit the bill...

Nothing came to mind.

*...*

For a moment, his heart sank.

Sometimes, when situations didn't align properly, people couldn't easily escape the past.

It would unintentionally pop up and torment them, or hold them back as they tried to move forward.

Even Kim Raebin, who was dull to others' emotions, knew that.

"......"

*No, wait a minute.*

Chungwoo-hyung has recovered safely, yet here I am, having such rude thoughts just because our eyes met!

The important thing is that now, the family can watch a member's activity together.

*Get a grip!*

Kim Raebin snapped out of his thoughts.

He lightly slapped his cheeks and focused again on the first episode of the new season of ❬Doll Hunter❭.

[This is hell, are you looking for another hell in this world?]

In an instant, he was sucked into the drama.

Battle, pain, thrill, horror... this drama, a collection of everything that stirred emotion, was incredibly captivating this season as well.

He diligently watched the story unfold.

Stab.

He was startled once more when the character played by Bae Sejin, a medical student, appeared in the protagonist's second life and was murdered.

"...!"

It was partly due to the surprising and impressive acting, but that wasn't the whole reason.

*The BGM... it's our song!*

That's right.

This induced dissonance was the theme song they had created for Jeong Isol, 'misapprehension'.

And that wasn't even the end of it.

*It's... playing again!*

When he first appeared on the snowy mountain.

When he comforted the protagonist for the first time.

The music director had skillfully cut and inserted TeSTAR's OST into all of the medical student Jeong Isol's impressive scenes.

And finally.

[AAAAACK!]

In the scene where the protagonist discovers Jeong Isol's body, everything connected and exploded, creating an incredible synergy.

"...!"

Kim Raebin felt an absolute thrill.

The pleasure of seeing a song he created being used in a new way by someone else, and it fitting their intention perfectly.

And separate from that, the joy of watching a perfectly constructed work of art.

"Yes! That's the song we made!"

"Oh my god!"

This was a common reaction among everyone watching Doll Hunter.

Just one thing.

[What's the classical piece that plays when Jeong Isol from Doll Hunter appears?]

Only a very small number of people knew that TeSTAR had composed this song.
