# Chapter 481

This was the dorm. Almost no one was left standing.

"Ugh."

"Hah..."

It wasn't a disease apocalypse. We had been so busy we hadn't even unpacked in our new dorm. Now, with no one walking around, the place felt utterly desolate.

*They're all sick.*

What was more, even Ryu Chungwoo was down for the count.

"Haha, I don't know how many years it's been... since I've had body aches."

"......"

I glanced at the man in the next bed. He smiled while taking some fever reducer.

*He really got a persistent drenching.*

Of course, that probably wasn't the only reason. This was the same man who was perfectly fine after three consecutive concerts.

The accumulated stress from the company's collapse, the reckless album preparations, and the Cha Eugene situation had likely all exploded at once.

*As the Leader, he must have had it worse.*

Amazingly, even so, he was in the best condition among us. The problem was that he knew it.

"Hmm, once the medicine kicks in, I'll just pop over to the company for a bit..."

Geez.

"Just get some rest. You don't have a schedule."

"...!"

"It won't be too late to answer if the company calls. It's not like there's anything urgent."

"...Yeah. Okay."

Ryu Chungwoo smiled sheepishly and lay back down on the bed.

I also just lay back down on my covers.

*Well... I guess there are days for resting like this.*

At this point, all the important things were wrapped up. I figured a single day off wouldn't hurt.

WHIRR.

A breeze blew from the whirring circulator fan. That was enough.

It was ridiculous. On a hot summer day, no one complained about the heat even though the air conditioner was off.

*Hmm.*

But there was one exception.

[...It's hot.]

"Turn on the air conditioner."

"I'm not a piece of trash."

The sole survivor stood in the doorway with a sullen look. It was Cha Eugene.

"Why is everyone sick?"

The surprisingly energetic man had been wandering aimlessly in the living room since early morning. He couldn't go into his own room because Bae Sejin was sick in there.

The funniest part was that social media posted the complete opposite of this situation.

[Ah, Eugene insisted on performing no matter what, but it looks like his condition got worse so he's resting now ㅠㅠ]

└His professionalism is insane...

That's right. People firmly believed Cha Eugene had shown incredible fighting spirit. They thought he had created a legendary performance while flying across the stage, sick.

However, the one who was officially sick was, in reality, the only one among us who was perfectly fine.

"Everyone will be up and about by evening, so you don't have to worry."

"......"

That was why STier Cha Eugene, though pretending otherwise, seemed quite flustered. He had been unable to sit still for a while.

*...I can't just tell him to go out and have fun.*

Still, I didn't think this guy would run off again, spouting nonsense about control. STier Cha Eugene had become strangely quiet after yesterday's performance. He seemed to be lost in thought in a good way, which meant he had become quite stable.

*Good.*

In that case, I pressed the back of my hand to my feverish forehead and spoke calmly.

"Close the door. It should be fine to turn on the AC in the living room."

"......"

"No. Just switch rooms with... Lee Sejin. He'll switch with you right away."

Right, I should have done this sooner. If I had given him his own room and guaranteed his privacy, he might have calmed down.

*I was too obsessed with eliminating variables.*

Now that I was a bit more relaxed, I could see things more clearly. Being sick could be an advantage. I looked at STier Cha Eugene.

*The tension between us seems to have eased quite a bit after performing together.*

It was like how a bad opinion of a teammate disappears after getting an A on a group project, even if the process was terrible, as long as they participated.

*This guy did well.*

Cha Eugene. Without him, a significant amount of the buzz would have been lost.

First, he drew attention by suddenly appearing. Then his EX-rank talent devoured all sorts of popular video charts, generating a ton of buzz.

*...The reaction when *this* Cha Eugene returns is going to be a bit of a problem.*

I really hoped he had memories of this.

Anyway, it meant that in our current friendly situation, my suggestion would likely be well-received.

*I think it'll work this time.*

I looked at him with considerable confidence.

However.

"I have no intention of taking a sick person's room."

"...!"

[I told you. I'm not that much of a piece of trash.]

With that, the man flapped the collar of his T-shirt and left the doorway.

"......"

No...

*Then what am I supposed to do?*

I offer a solution and he just spits it back out. Why did he even say he was hot? Was it some build-up to declare he wanted ice cream?

I glanced at Ryu Chungwoo for an objective confirmation, only to find him sound asleep.

"......"

Let him be.

*...I don't know.*

Fine. Do whatever you want.

In the end, I decided to grant Cha Eugene a reasonable amount of autonomy. This meant I had to crawl out of bed to speak.

"This is the manager's number... It should be on your phone too. If you need anything, ask him and get some ice cream or something."

"......"

"Just don't go into the fine details, like who's sick and how."

Word had leaked to other agencies, so we needed to be careful until we could control the narrative. It was hard to expect that much from this Cha Eugene.

*This should be enough.*

However, STier Cha Eugene sighed.

What?

"OK. I get it now."

"What the..."

"Go to bed, now."

He then pushed me back into the room.

"...?"

When I looked back, Cha Eugene sat on the sofa, holding his smartphone.

*What's that guy doing?*

This question would soon be answered, step by step.

***

Not even an hour later, something arrived at the dorm in boxes.

DING-DONG.

*Ice cream and... ice packs?*

There were also a few daily necessities and groceries.

Did I assume Cha Eugene had contacted the manager? No. A company employee hadn't arranged it.

"You did this?"

"......"

He had ordered delivery from the supermarket himself.

"Moondae-hyung, you didn't like contacting the company. Right?"

"......"

"And any adult living in Korea can use this method."

No, it wasn't that I thought you couldn't order delivery.

*I just thought you wouldn't do it yourself. It seemed like too proactive a gesture toward this life.*

"I should organize this..."

"......"

However, Cha Eugene kicked a staggering Kim Raebin back toward his room. He then organized the boxes he had ordered himself.

Surprisingly, Cha Eugene's practice of 'doing it himself' was just beginning.

First, he filled tumblers with warm and cold water and distributed them to everyone.

"I-I'm fi..."

"Your face doesn't look fine."

Then he handed out ice packs wrapped in towels.

"Huh~? We had towels like th..."

"I bought them."

Eventually, he even started checking our temperatures and confirming when we took our medicine.

"Your fever hasn't gone down yet. Use the ice pack."

"......"

I watched him leave with the thermometer and suppressed a groan in my bed.

At this point, I was confused.

I was... being taken care of by Cha Eugene? By*that*guy?

*Is this for real?*

I turned my head. Ryu Chungwoo unwrapped the towel from his ice pack with a bewildered expression. I guess it was reality.

However, we didn't know that the finishing touch had yet to come.

An hour later.

"...Hyung."

"......"

"What's that smell?"

"...!"

I lifted my head and looked outside. Through the doorway, I saw a pot boiling in the kitchen. A distinct, savory smell of broth wafted from it.

"......"

Standing in front of that pot was... Cha Eugene.

"......"

Cha Eugene was cooking?

I was curious what on earth he was making, or rather, why he was bothering to cook for himself. Surprisingly, the purpose of his cooking wasn't self-sufficiency.

"Come out and eat."

"......"

He called the ailing members to the dining table.

The members haphazardly assembled around the dining table. They finally identified the food he had made.

"......"

"Oh."

"This is."

It looked familiar.

[Chicken Soup for the Soul!]

It was a recipe Cha Eugene had attempted several times before.

*I think he said his mother taught him.*

It seemed to be a flavor with its own memories. However, every time he attempted this dish, he had failed spectacularly. One time, he added honey.

[Gah!]

Another time, he almost started a fire.

[Aaaah!]

No one here didn't remember.

"......"

"......"

Bae Sejin's trembling voice broke the silence.

"...When ordering porridge was an option, you chose to do this?"

I couldn't argue with that.

However, I lifted my head and looked at Cha Eugene, who was still cleaning up in the kitchen. To be precise, I watched his hands move.

*Hmm.*

Those were the movements of someone with experience. This wasn't a reckless attempt. He had cooked routinely before.

If so.

*...Was there a time when the company didn't take good care of him?*

In short, his skills had likely improved because he had to do it himself.

I hesitated for a moment, then replied shortly. I couldn't explain everything.

"...We should be grateful, though."

"...Right."

At those words, everyone sat up straight, as if they had made a coordinated decision. They looked like people ready to wolf down whatever was served.

Soon, Cha Eugene sat down, holding a soft, white porridge-like dish he had prepared as a side. The members flinched, but STier Cha Eugene spoke without much reaction.

"It might be a little unfamiliar, but this is the only dish I know how to make for when you're sick."

"......"

Instead of someone saying, 'Then you should have ordered porridge,' Seon Ahyeon led with a cheerful voice.

"O-Okay, I'll eat well...!"

"Thanks."

A moment after they lifted their spoons with considerable solemnity.

"...!"

"This is good...?"

"It has a unique flavor!"

Surprisingly, Cha Eugene's Chicken Soup was quite good.

*No, it's actually really good.*

If it suited the palate of a sick person from another country, it had to be genuinely delicious.

STier Cha Eugene smiled faintly.

"......"

The meal began in a much more relaxed atmosphere. As the sick members filled their stomachs, they regained some energy and slowly started to talk.

"Yesterday's Water Bomb... was fun, wasn't it."

"Ah~ It really was. We had so much fun we all ended up like this!"

"Right, but Eugene, you got the best reaction, and you're the healthiest."

Ryu Chungwoo probably wanted to smoothly steer the conversation toward thanking Cha Eugene for taking care of them.

However, Cha Eugene responded with a smile that was almost a frown.

"That reaction was also for 'this' TeSTAR's Cha Eugene."

"...!"

I didn't step in.

*It doesn't seem like he's trying to pick a fight.*

If anything, it felt like respect.

Now, others besides me were there to refute him.

"S-Still... the stage people saw yesterday, that was you."

"That's right~ There were probably more people who just came for the event than our fans?"

"Th-Ahem, our Cha Eugene is really good too... but you were also really good. So people liked you because you did well."

Cha Eugene was silent for a moment. Then he answered obediently.

"I know."

"...!"

He calmly adjusted his posture at the table. His attitude was slightly formal.

"Thank you for giving me the chance, and I apologize for my rude attitude at the beginning. I'm sorry."

STier Cha Eugene bowed his head deeply. A few flustered members swayed, ducking their own heads toward the table.

Good grief.

"N-No. Uh, it was great performing with you...! I-I'm the one who's sorry for not understanding you well..."

"R-Right, yeah..."

The atmosphere relaxed even more. It was a complete mood of reconciliation.

"Yeah~ We just have to get along from now on!"

Well, this guy probably thought he deserved the apology. In any case, the atmosphere of having built a basic level of trust was not bad at all.

"You did well."

"I know."

I heard the small exchange between Kim Raebin and Cha Eugene and chuckled before continuing my meal.

A few hours later that evening, perhaps thanks to Cha Eugene's care, all of TeSTAR had more or less recovered.

"Go!"

"Sejin's playing rough."

This meant we were all gathered in the living room, sniffling and playing video games.

DANTARARA DANTARANG!

I sat behind Bae Sejin, who roughly turned the joystick, and a cautious Ryu Chungwoo. I watched the screen move without any particular thought.

*My head is still dizzy.*

Surprisingly, Cha Eugene hadn't gone to his room either and was sitting next to the sofa.

"Feeling more comfortable?"

[I suppose so. Thanks to my friend and my fan.]

Right, Kim Raebin and... no, I told you I'm not a fan. Well, whatever.

I asked casually.

"What about Chungwoo-hyung?"

I could understand Bae Sejin because of the drug issue, but wasn't Ryu Chungwoo okay? He should have been active and fine until the very end.

However, Cha Eugene suddenly shut his mouth and scanned me from head to toe.

What the.

[Sometimes, it's an artist's duty to protect a fan's dream.]

"...?"

What does that mean.

[Nothing.]

Cha Eugene backed off, but I could roughly grasp the mood from his tone.

*Nothing my ass.*

It was clear that STier Ryu Chungwoo's mental state had shattered too. He must have been a different kind of person back then.

*Well, does it matter?*

In any case, STier Cha Eugene seemed to have adapted quite well here. He didn't seem as uncomfortable or on edge as before.

Even right now, Seon Ahyeon, who had brought over a peach, was striking up a conversation with him just fine.

"S-Still, for the time we have left... I hope we can get along well. I-If there's anything you want to try, we can do it together."

"Yeah. Feel free to say it!"

STier Cha Eugene was silent for a moment but soon shrugged.

"OK."

"Ooh!"

A welcoming cheer erupted in the living room. The atmosphere was as if they were recruiting a new colleague.

*Hmm.*

Not bad.

I let out a small laugh.

It was then.
