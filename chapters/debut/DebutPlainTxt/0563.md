# Chapter 563

A few minutes ago.

Cheongryeo, the System's owner, checked the Status Window. Help had been deleted, leaving only a clean void. He felt an unpleasant premonition.

*Hmm.*

It was the displeasure of an event beyond his control.

The situation was no longer interesting, so he mechanically tried to call Help to confirm the details. That was when it happened.

It started with a familiar sensation on the back of his hand. Moisture.

DRIP.

It felt just like the cold sweat that had dripped there moments before. Cheongryeo lowered his eyes to check.

This time, however, it was not cold sweat. The water ran over his hand and fell to the floor.

SPLASH!

The hard floor of the officetel living room was gone. In its place, a rippling black surface of water gently swallowed the droplet, leaving no trace.

"......"

He realized it was a bottomless abyss.

Incarnation of an Idol suddenly stood opposite him in the dark officetel. The man wore an unwet black shirt and black pants. With Cheongryeo's face, he looked at the identical but slightly younger Cheongryeo.

He opened his mouth.

[Wait.]

Cheongryeo soon realized Jung Woodan was no longer in the living room. Then, the strange pop-up returned.

[Incarnation of an Idol is watching you.]

Incarnation of an Idol declared, [Let's both avoid any pointless actions.]

He looked at the past he had already lived, at the version of himself who believed time could capture a perfect moment forever.

Hmph.

[So this is what I looked like,[ Incarnation of an Idol finished, his hand on his chin.

The Cheongryeo opposite him instantly reviewed the clues. The same appearance, an older face, a Help from the future. He put it all together, and a plausible hypothesis emerged.

"Are you me from the future?"

Incarnation of an Idol just laughed.

Cheongryeo twisted his own mouth into a smirk.

[You don't seem surprised.]

"Because I know I'll succeed," Cheongryeo said calmly, as if it were obvious.

Incarnation of an Idol muttered indifferently.

[When the number of restarts is about to hit triple digits... right. This is about the time for this kind of thing.]

"......"

[There are times your competitive spirit gets ahead of your calculations. It's not a bad thing, but you'll learn to pick and choose when to use it.]

Incarnation of an Idol clasped his hands.

[But you'd better start getting rid of the habit of trusting your own calculations too much.]

"For example?"

Incarnation of an Idol immediately pulled up the history of this life. [Why did you make T-Holic retire due to an accident?]

"......"

That was right. In the last few restarts, Cheongryeo had forced T-Holic out of the entertainment industry in an unnatural way. Amplifying scandals had its limits. Even when their songs changed or their company relationship soured, T-Holic stubbornly survived on public recognition. So he meddled with factors outside the stage to force their exit, using injuries, family problems, and cruel rumors. He killed them without a fight.

[Because it's a less efficient method than you'd think.]

He had explained it that way to his Help. In truth, Cheongryeo had not ruled out 'extreme methods'. If it was useful, he would use it.

Cheongryeo looked at the identical face with a blank expression. "If you're me, you can't be unaware that T-Holic is the biggest obstacle to the mission."

[That's right,[ Incarnation of an Idol readily agreed. His smile deepened. [That's why you have to use them.]

"...!"

[Without proper stimulation and enemies, humans easily become lazy.]

[Only when a competitor is right in front of you, tightening the noose, do you give it your all. You cling to your work by squeezing out every last bit of strength. To give that up.]

Incarnation of an Idol delivered his assessment. [Since the obstacle disappeared without any reward or accomplishment, your group members must not have learned the pleasure of overcoming.]

He was saying that his past self, so sick of T-Holic, had unconsciously chosen a comfortable and inefficient method.

"......"

The expression vanished from Cheongryeo's face.

Incarnation of an Idol knew that face. It was the one Cheongryeo made when a point hit home and he was logically convinced. It rarely happened.

[......]

In that moment, Shin Jaehyun, the Incarnation of an Idol, let out a hollow laugh.

*Good grief.*

He suddenly understood the situation. He was teaching himself right now.

*I'm sure my junior did something similar.*

He muttered with a wide grin. [I ended up putting Judan in the group at some point. Was this what it was like?]

His past self seemed to want to know what he meant, so Incarnation of an Idol kindly explained. [It's a story about how I succeed.]

Then, as if about to say something important, he tilted his head slightly. [Ah. You have something you were originally going to do, don't you.]

"......"

[Try doing that first. I won't interfere this time.]

That was right. Shin Jaehyun had little interest in his past self. The long conversation was just to stall for time, to make him call for Help at the perfect moment.

Shin Jaehyun recalled his hurried, absurd conversation with Park Moondae.

[The guy who has the System in this timeline... I mean Cheongryeo, can you talk to yourself?]

[...What if it's possible?]

[Then after Keundal calls me, I want you to make the Cheongryeo here call for 'Help'. Ask him nicely or threaten him, it has to be done.]

Shin Jaehyun had accepted the proposal, but he knew himself.

*There's no way that would work.*

He knew he would not listen to requests or threats, so he chose to manipulate the situation instead.

It was the right answer.

*He'll try not to be swayed by the other's tricks...*

The more suspicious and worthless his words sounded, the more Cheongryeo would prioritize his original plan.

Indeed, his past self now opened his mouth, his face blank.

"Help."

Just like that, the System's owner called the System.

~~~

Park Moondae's inner world.

Two identical figures in the living room turned their gazes as the view outside the window flashed. Beyond the veranda, a white line shot across the fantastical sunset sky of ❬Magical Boy❭ and vanished. It was a visualized farewell.

"A shooting star...?"

"It's the System," I said calmly.

After Keundal separated me, Park Moondae, the System's remaining dregs were instinctively drawn to their owner's call.

*Shin Jaehyun succeeded.*

I could feel it through our remaining connection.

Keundal, once my Status Window and part of the System, nodded in understanding, but he was also anxious.

"Then, Hyung, does it still feel like you're connected even though the System's moved that far away?"

"Yes."

I gave a slight nod.

*It's more persistent than I thought.*

I worried that if this went wrong, it might stay connected even in reality, then snap back and stick to me like a bouncy ball. I kept that thought to myself and spoke reassuringly.

"Still, if we return to reality, the System left here will have to fall away."

"I guess so..."

Keundal looked out of the living room, slightly relieved. His anxiety seemed greater than the fury he felt when he had grabbed my collar.

"......"

"......"

Above the dim living room, the meteor's light flashed past and receded into the distance. I did not know why that disgusting thing had to be a meteor, but it made things much more peaceful inside the quiet officetel.

I sat in the living room and slowly opened my mouth. "...Am I going back to reality soon?"

"You're already on your way back, Hyung. I called you, remember?"

Is that so. It was so comfortable I had not noticed.

Returning to reality from an officetel with my debut view was definitely a good thing, far better than the brutish pressure from that dog of a System. It must be thanks to this guy calling me into his inner world.

I glanced at Keundal, then watched the receding meteor and the scenery. Like an airplane taking off, I slowly felt the connection to the System disappear.

The moment it passed the critical point, SNAP.

As the meteor touched the ground far away, I felt a refreshing sensation.

*Yes.*

The System had broken away.

But something unexpected happened.

"Huh?"

Instead of stopping, the meteor bounced off the ground and shot in the opposite direction. Farther and farther away.

"...?"

Keundal, who had been watching blankly, opened his mouth.

"The System is going... farther away. Because it broke off from you, Hyung."

"......"

"When you pull a rubber band until it snaps, the piece flies off in the opposite direction, right? It's like that."

Let's see. I am moving from the present to the future. In that case, the System dregs going in the opposite direction are...

"Is it going further into the past?"

"Probably."

The meteor passed through Cheongryeo's regressions and disappeared beyond the horizon, going further and further into the past.

"What on earth... what's going to happen to that?"

It grew smaller and smaller. It would stop somewhere and do some crazy System-like thing again, just as the fragment had stubbornly stuck to me. It would grant its host's wish, extract energy, and try to recover its power.

"......"

I suddenly realized. Was that it from the beginning?

"Is that going to become the complete System?"

"Huh?"

The thing that lost Park Moondae, its personality and intellect, would probably have only primitive intelligence and primal power. It would slowly recover by switching hosts and eventually become the highly intelligent System I met.

*And as I break away, it becomes primitive dregs again and falls into the distant past...*

It was a cycle.

"In time, that will become the very System we met."

"Ah..." Keundal slowly seemed to understand, then shot up with a horrified look.

"Th-Then are you saying we just created the System?"

What was he talking about.

"You can't say we created it. The one that already existed is just going to the past as dregs."

"Ah... r-right."

It was like the chicken and the egg. We were just witnessing a process in the middle.

But one thing was certain.

"So, that thing can't come any further into the future."

When it reaches this point in time, it returns to its start. The System was a completed entity, trapped in this timeline.

I smiled faintly. "I guess we really won't be seeing it again."

I felt refreshed.

"Then...! It's really, really over."

"Yes."

As I nodded, Keundal let out a long sigh of relief. His eyes shone with hope.

Ironically, however, the artificial glitches still flickered all over his body.

*This is how this guy perceives himself...*

Like a scar that remains after a wound heals, the System's influence on him was still present.

"......"

I shifted my feet and sat up straight.

I had felt something after becoming the System.

*Being in there felt like my sense of self was completely drained.*

It made me vague, like being blackout drunk and not knowing where I was or who I was with. It did not have feelings either.

*A person would become unshaken by normally unpleasant stimuli.*

It was an extremely stable state. In short, it was closer to a selfless AI than a normal human.

I finally, directly, understood the mental state of someone who had spent years like that.

"Keundal."

"Yes?"

Right. In the pop-up I saw earlier, Keundal's modifier was simply Keundal. His nickname was his identity, not Ryu Gunwoo or Park Moondae. The other guys had all sorts of descriptions, but this guy had none.

I finally asked, "You... what do you think your name is?"

"Huh?"

"Not a nickname like Keundal."

"Uh..." He scratched his head, surprised by the question, then answered. "Well... I'm fine with Keundal. I've never really thought about wanting a name. Ah, Ryu Gunwoo! I'm using Ryu Gunwoo."

"......"

"H-Hyung. Why are you sighing? It's scary when you do that right now..."

"You said it before," I recited slowly. "That Park Moondae doesn't really feel like your name, and... you sometimes call me Gunwoo-hyung. It's not because you think Ryu Gunwoo is your name."

Keundal still looked like he did not know why I was bringing this up. "Hyung, you're Gunwoo-hyung, aren't you?"

"To you... maybe, but the truth is, you're Ryu Gunwoo now."

"......"

"And if you look at it that way, to me, you were Park Moondae."

His face showed he was just confused. "Um... I'm not sure why we're talking about this. Honestly, there are more urgent things right now..." Keundal grunted.

I gave a bitter smile. "For you, too. I hope that both Ryu Gunwoo and Park Moondae can be your names."

"Huh...?"

"It means I hope you'll be a little more greedy about your identity as a person."

I remembered sharing the stage view with him, looking out at the overwhelming audience. That was the meaning I found as TeSTAR's Park Moondae. I hoped this guy would also have moments to share such scenes while living his own life, even if only through photos or stories.

"So... now that the System is gone, I hope you'll focus more on your life as Ryu Gunwoo. As a person. Think a little about what you like and don't like... things like that."

"...Yes."

Keundal nodded, but my words still did not seem to be getting through to him.

*Still.*

If he keeps at it, he will recover. Investing time and money can make the impossible possible, and this guy now had plenty of both to invest.

*I shouldn't push too hard right now. Maybe I'll just leave it at this.*

"Hoo."

I got up. It was about time.

"Hyung?"

"Shall we go out to meet him now?"

I was talking about Shin Jaehyun, who had performed his role well.
