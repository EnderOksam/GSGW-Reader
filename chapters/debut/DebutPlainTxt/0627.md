# Chapter 627

The release of a new track!

The audio for the version of the title track that TeSTAR performed at the Glastonbury Festival was released. It came as an update to the existing album.

[Not a repackaged album? Not a single? Not a new version released separately?]

TeSTAR's unique performance at the massive festival had ignited a firestorm of publicity. On top of that, Ryu Chungwoo's letter had been posted, which greatly reassured the fandom.

This music content, like a pleasant bonus, was readily welcomed.

[Thank you, guys.]

[If they released it and the performance was even slightly underwhelming, VTIC's fans would start a media campaign to tear them down. It's better to just ride the hype from the performance. ㅇㅇ]

All sorts of opinions were voiced. Since there was plausible logic based on the available information, most people just accepted it.

It was a mix of great joy and a slight sense of disappointment.

It seemed like it would end there as a one-off, fun piece of content, but...

[I'm so excited, you guys.]

[I think it's a tour announcement.]

[Isn't it something like a dance practice video for the Glastonbury performance?]

[But while it's great for us, the general public will just pass by without knowing.]

[The accessibility for this is soㅠㅠ]

It was the fear of it becoming a storm in a teacup.

Many people, both domestic and international, had been waiting with interest for the audio of TeSTAR's recent performance.

However, it was unlikely that all these people would spend over 20,000 won to buy a new album just to listen to the track.

Naturally, the overwhelming majority would try to listen to unofficial audio uploaded to WeTube for free. Inevitably, many fans lamented the resulting loss of money and records.

The poor accessibility was already a shame, but now they were announcing additional content while keeping it that way.

[If they wanted to do fan service, wouldn't it have been better to release it as a free track?]

[They should just do more promotions with this version, whether it's on music shows or at events.]

Voices of disappointment emerged, worried that this would further encourage leakage.

The fans' desire for more aggressive and public activities began to creep out.

Part of it was the anxiety of losing the momentum they had gained with the performance after going back and forth with VTIC.

*If we appeal to either the domestic or international audience right now, I feel like it would be more effective!*

People started popping up, declaring what kind of schedule they would have arranged for TeSTAR if they could do it instead of the company.

[There are many cases where they quietly switch the promotion track to a B-side if it gets a better response than the title track, please have some sense.]

[Honestly, the original Bad Feeling did great on the charts, but now we have to consider the hype. Let's push the hip-hop arrangement to the forefront, and run some WeTube promotions.]

[It would have been better to appear on an American or British talk show with this. I'm sure they received requests.ㅠㅠ]

People who had a say in the matter each became engrossed and presented their own solutions.

Even people who weren't TeSTAR fans slyly chimed in, trying to show off their insight as "veteran KPOP listeners."

[If it were me, I would have released the dance practice video for this TeSTAR arrangement first.]

[TeSTAR's company seems to show all the pros and cons of a startup.]

Ultimately, even the fans who didn't care about 'how much profit is being made' started to become conscious of the situation.

After all, everyone was talking about 'which method is more efficient'.

And so, a very strange effect occurred.

[So what on earth are they going to release? I'm going crazy.]

The content release announcement created a much heavier tension than usual.

Worried about missing the opportunity and it becoming a storm in a teacup, the fans paradoxically became more anxious and paid deeper attention.

Coincidentally, the major variety shows related to this promotion period were also ending.

[Is this the end of Ghost Story Office? It was super scary and gave me chills, but some of the foreshadowing wasn't resolved, was it...]

[In reality, they're at each other's throats over music, albums, and performances, but in the MT variety show, everyone's just laughing lol]

[Wow, all the variety shows for this promotion are over. My month-long joyㅠㅠ What a shame.]

From VTIC and TeSTAR's MT variety show to TeSTAR's bizarre Ghost Story Office.

Each one was either nearing its final episode or had just ended. It was a situation where, if there wasn't any new content soon, it would feel like the official promotion period was over.

What seemed like potential firewood had been dropped, but it was unclear whether it was real firewood that would burn brightly or just a small spark for fan service.
