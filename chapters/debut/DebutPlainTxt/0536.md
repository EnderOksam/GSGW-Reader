# Chapter 536

❬Genuine Match❭ began its second half with a large audience, almost like an opening ceremony. The show hoped for a stage so incredible that viewers would spread spoilers and create a narrative.

*There are a lot of TeSTAR fans.*

A twin fansite master for Park Moondae and Lee Sejin found her seat. Even as a TeSTAR fan herself, she could not help but notice the crowd.

As Ryu Seorin's younger sister and a senior from Ryu Gunwoo's old club, she still occasionally contacted Ryu Gunwoo, Keundal. She had no idea he had become Park Moondae. In fact, she had asked him today if he planned to come.

[I'm busy with work at the end of the year, so I don't think I can make it. It's alright.]

That was the reply she received.

*I wanted to get some photos from a different angle... Tsk.*

The office worker clicked her tongue, thinking he was still such a blunt guy.

The truth was that Keundal had freaked out, fidgeted, and shouted his reply, "I... am Gunwoo-hyung!" It was a secret that would never be revealed.

Anyway, she carefully set up her camera to avoid getting caught, then scanned her surroundings with a sharp eye.

*It seems like almost half the audience are TeSTAR fans.*

In that case, a spot in the top two was practically guaranteed, as long as TeSTAR did not completely mess up their stage.

However, that was not necessarily a good thing.

*They're being so blatant about wanting to promote Aether.*

If things continued like this and Aether placed third or lower, they could spin a narrative.

[Honestly, their stage didn't deserve that rank]

[I heard half the audience were TeSTAR fans. Seems like they gritted their teeth and sabotaged them lol]

It would be a story of 'the unfortunate rising star, underrated by the powerful'.

The thought of hearing such things about Aether made even the twin fansite master feel uncomfortable.

*It's hard to just think "they're just kids, what do they know" when you consider how much money they're going to make in the future...*

She believed that as long as they were getting paid, they needed to have professionalism and a sense of responsibility.

After all, was Aether not also benefiting from what Wonder Hall had done?

[...Now, we welcome our audience~]

Just as the office worker fansite master thought this while discreetly adjusting her camera, the MC's opening remarks ended.

*It's starting.*

She adjusted her posture and planned to take a few test shots of the other groups besides TeSTAR.

[The first stage is... Ah, they were also the winners of the last stage in the first half. SoftSweet!]

As soon as she heard the name of the first act, she made a prediction.

*Aether will be next or the one after.*

The production team must have deliberately given Aether an early slot. It fit their challenger image perfectly.

*And they probably wanted to separate their performance order from TeSTAR's.*

For the sake of ratings, TeSTAR would definitely perform last or second to last. That was obvious. Sandwiching two or three teams between them and Aether would prevent a direct comparison. Her analysis was sound.

[This next performance is... Aether!]

*I was right.*

After SoftSweet finished their stage, she watched Aether appear, her eyes narrowing.

"WAAAAH!"

Prompted by the staff, the audience's cheers served as a backdrop as Aether took their places and got into formation.

A brief silence fell before the stage began with an intense, explosive instrumental.

[Feel the sunrise!]

The song from the pre-release clip unfolded with powerful sound effects.

Their perfectly synchronized performance was to T-Holic's 3rd album title track, ❬Runaway❭. It was an intense and youthful song of social criticism that T-Holic had used only once, performed in classic black school uniforms to appeal to teenagers.

[Escape the pain and Gear up again

Fill this space with the sound of applause]

The lyrics perfectly matched Aether's current situation for anyone to see.

The stage even left room for various interesting interpretations when considered with the theme 'Who am I', but...

*As expected.*

To someone who consistently watched TeSTAR and found detailed joys in their performances, it was immediately obvious.

Gestures and expressions similar to TeSTAR's.

"......"

She crossed her arms. It was clear they had referenced them.

*That agency doesn't know when to stop.*

People tend to lose their reason when pushed to their limits. This was not the first or second time. Until now, everyone had held back, thinking it could cause a backlash against TeSTAR, but if this aired properly, who knew what the enraged TeSTAR fans would do.

*...Wait. Is that what they want?*

The twin fansite master questioned it for a moment, then soon realized the truth.

Wonder Hall had deliberately condoned Aether referencing TeSTAR.

[If TeSTAR fans pick a fight, it becomes noise marketing.]

As long as Aether's stage was good, the public would empathize with them and fight for them.

[They saw how much the rookies were struggling and they're still hating on them for no reason;;]

└Maybe the staff member cursed them out of resentment because TeSTAR fans are so viciousㅠ

└Totally reasonable suspicion lololol

[TeSTAR probably hates you guys more than Aether, you damn viewers]

And right now, Aether was definitely doing a good job on stage.

They performed with a desperation that seemed to pour out every last bit of their energy. They compensated for their lack of technique and talent with immense practice and a do-or-die spirit.

Against a backdrop of red and yellow lighting effects that resembled a rising sun, Aether struck their ending pose with full force.

[Feel the sun, rise!]

"......"

The stage ended with a roar of cheers.

Amidst the people cheering as if deeply impressed, the subtle expressions on a few audience members were barely noticeable.

"Hah."

The office worker watched Aether on stage with a complicated heart.

With tears welling in their eyes, they panted for breath while bowing repeatedly to the audience before leaving the stage.

*The fans who like rookies are going to go crazy.*

There were quite a few people who were only impressed by rising stars during that phase, the ones full of venom and desperation.

*Their value... is going to go up.*

The twin fansite master then realized that despite this, she had not taken a single photo of Aether.

Normally, she would have taken pictures of any team whose value was likely to rise, since it was always profitable.

*Is this my own brand of loyalty?*

She suppressed a dry laugh and watched the remaining stages.

Her uncharacteristically troubled heart had calmed down in the meantime.

And then.

[This next stage is TeSTAR!]

Finally, the reason for attending this recording had arrived.

WAAAAAAAAGH!!

The venue filled with scream-like cheers. Amidst it all, the fansite master calmly organized her thoughts.

*During the mid-term evaluation...*

For the mid-term evaluation, TeSTAR had arranged a famous 2000s band song in a K-pop idol style, presenting a pleasant and enjoyable stage.

It was a stage that transformed a nationally beloved song into something trendy, fitting their identity as idols. It had a relaxed and skillful charm.

However, seeing as they got almost no screen time in the pre-release clip, it seemed unlikely the production team had edited it very favorably.

*No, this is actually better.*

It was a stage with the complete opposite attribute of Aether's desperate performance.

This way, public opinion would likely settle on 'both did well' without any unnecessary comparisons. Then, even if TeSTAR's fans were angry at Aether, they could see the situation and hold back one more time.

This was indeed the wise approach.

*Since there are guys in the team who know how to use their heads for politics, they wouldn't have hastily changed things just because of the mid-term evaluation.*

She trusted the quick thinking and cool-headed judgment of the two members she photographed.

[......]

In the meantime, a deep darkness fell over the stage.

Then, with a flash, a light came to life.

Not just one, but a multitude of lights filled the stage with a starlike sparkle.

A voice soared over it, as if ascending.

[Wait, because you'll rise

Even if the floor is cold

Your life is So br-ight!]

"......"

The smooth dance-pop from the mid-term evaluation was nowhere to be seen.

It was a powerful rock sound, glamorous and sensual, yet elevated with a strong popular appeal.

[Yeees

Ride the clouds, and

Shoot out into the skyyyy-!]

Glam metal resounded everywhere.

*Ah.*

Riding the thumping sound, a group dance with terrifying tempo control formed up and surged forward.

The blonde-dyed hair, black rider jackets, and white printed t-shirts stood out exquisitely.

The performers shot forward to the front of the stage like beams of light, so glamorous it was almost aggressive.

[Pouring down like a comet

The pain of last night

Shines on the asphalt

City of lights]

Perhaps the theme of 'Who am I' was easier to understand in the mid-term evaluation version.

That version had shown their identity as 'Grand Prize-level K-pop idols'. The explanation, 'We thought this was like us because TeSTAR is a group that always takes on conceptual challenges,' was less direct.

However.

[Sparkling like a star

Your running tears

Shining like the nightscape]

This stage felt more direct.

The moment she saw Park Moondae, at the end of a high note, raise a hand to brush back his hair and flip his jacket open.

*This is insane.*

The office worker knew.

This was a stage they had changed after seeing the mid-term evaluation, a stage they were hell-bent on winning with.

The fleeting thought,*What on earth are they trying to do?*passed through her mind.

*Wait.*

[Now come to me

Across the sky

Over the sea]

TeSTAR's gestures were now flamboyant, as is common in metal bands.

And those gestures...

*They're doing the exact same things Aether did earlier!*

That's right.

Almost every gesture Aether had made appeared as the parts changed. Raising a hand, cupping a chin, lightly closing their eyes then opening them with a knowing look while nodding.

Although it was an attitude that fit this glam metal performance astonishingly well.

*These crazy bastards!*

[Now come to me

Forget the tears

Leave the pain behind]

Just like when they faced Onyx for the Rookie of the Year award, TeSTAR had come out swinging openly.

And this was a mistake the fansite master had thought TeSTAR would never make.

*One wrong move and it's all over.*

A strong competitor trying to win by consciously targeting a weaker one could provoke resentment. It was a surefire way to ruin their image, hearing things like 'they're trying to start a power struggle with their juniors' or 'they're just greedy'.

That's why she thought TeSTAR, who had never made a foolish choice, would absolutely not make this choice here, but...

[The world is yours

I want to give you everything

This must be love]

They chose it.

A thrill that felt like pleasant goosebumps rising from her chest made her head feel hot.

*Ah, fuck...*

The fansite master let out a sigh.

There was something about that momentum, going all-in like crazy without calculating, that strangely made the viewer's throat tighten.

On stage, Cha Eugene, his piercings glinting and his brow furrowed, performed with a handheld mic, while Park Moondae desperately added harmonies from behind.

[Wait, because you'll rise

Even without streetlights

Your life is So br-ight!]

A storm of performance and vocals poured down like a bombardment, almost to the point of being too much.

In the midst of it all, the office worker fansite master barely held onto her practical reason.

*Realistically... speaking.*

For giving off the impression of a direct comparison with a much younger junior to work, they had to be absolutely overwhelming in their expression.

They had to be so overwhelming that anyone could feel the originality coming from TeSTAR's side.

[Yeeees

Ride the clouds, and

Shoot out into the skyyyy-!]

And Lee Sejin knew that too.

[I'm really sorry, but can't we do it?]

He had insisted on pulling this off in just three days. He had ultimately choreographed the dance, distributed the gestures, and made it happen, barely sleeping at all.

Even in the midst of that, he did not skip a single thing he needed to do to manage himself.

He knew it too.

This was not a particularly wise or human thing to do, especially since they were not freshly debuted rookies.

Give what you can, and think about what you already have.

*A person has to live while controlling their tempo.*

Otherwise, do they not say your balance will collapse? It is a profession easily swayed by the public. It means you cannot live with the ego of a person, an individual, but only as an idol. So, it means a person needs time to rest.

*Forget it.*

He did not want to live like that.

Succeed and live like a human.

If that meant he could no longer overcome pain and grow.

He wanted to keep living desperately, anxiously, and impatiently.

That was why he wanted to surpass his limits.

As far as he could go.
