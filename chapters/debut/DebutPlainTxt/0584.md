# Chapter 584

Bae Sejin quietly looked down at the script in his hands.

It was the script for ❬Doll Hunter❭ Season 2. He had read and recited it so many times he could vividly hear the voices in his head. The script, filled with corrections and notes, lay open before him.

He had not done it alone.

*...I received a lot of help.*

Whenever he held the script, the members would sneak over and chime in.

He recalled the words from Ryu Chungwoo that started it all.

"I see. Would it be easier to conceptualize if you had someone to play the other part?"

Someone would read the other character's lines from the script. His mother had sometimes done that for him in his early days as a child actor. After that, he was fine practicing alone.

But instead of coldly telling them, "You're just wasting my time," Bae Sejin let the members recite the lines.

It was a little embarrassing, but he enjoyed it.

*This... has nothing to do with our group activities.*

It was not about stage performance or song production. It was not even a solo activity for Bae Sejin the idol. This was a personal project for Bae Sejin the actor, and the members' meddling was pure friendliness.

"Well, ahem, if you do that... I'd be grateful."

So, though he did not show it, he gladly accepted their goodwill. He figured he could just use his break time later if he ran out of time, but it was unexpectedly helpful.

*It wasn't a waste of time.*

Hearing different opinions allowed him to see the script from multiple perspectives. They saw the story from angles he could not, things he would never have noticed alone.

And so, Bae Sejin slightly revised his interpretation of the role.

"Sejin."

"...!"

Bae Sejin snapped out of his thoughts. This was the film set.

"Sejin, it's your turn."

"Yes."

At the manager's call, he put down his script and stood up. His eyes gleamed.

*Let's do this right.*

This was a snowy mountain in Gangwon-do. It was the first shoot for ❬Doll Hunter❭ Season 2.

Bae Sejin played Jeong Isol, a medical student with antisocial personality disorder. He was a psychopath who could not empathize.

Trapped in a strange high-rise building, he unhesitatingly murdered people to get food. He even killed the protagonist without a second thought.

But Jeong Isol failed to calculate one thing. The moment the protagonist died, he returned to the past, to the beginning of the story.

So, at the start of Season 2, the protagonist returned to the past and killed Jeong Isol as soon as they met.

"......"

Bae Sejin had already filmed that scene during Season 1 to establish the actors' relationships. One might think that since Jeong Isol was already dead, Bae Sejin would not appear much in Season 2, but his role did not end there.

*Clack.*

The slate signaled the start of filming. The moment the sound cut off, Bae Sejin forgot the cameras.

"......"

Jeong Isol opened his mouth.

"Hello."

"...!"

He sat calmly in a folding chair with his legs crossed. He wore a white gown and held a clipped questionnaire.

Facing him was the drama's protagonist.

They were in...

*Whoooosh...*

A desolate, snowy mountain, where endless white snow and a chilling wind raged.

The protagonist asked in a trembling voice.

"What is this?"

Jeong Isol answered nonchalantly.

"Your mental landscape."

"......"

That was right. This Jeong Isol was not real. He was a delusion appearing in the protagonist's mind whenever he was unconscious, whether from fainting, sleeping, or an extreme situation.

"Why are you here!"

"I'm probably your trauma. I can't use any other technical terms. I'm a product of your imagination, after all."

And so, the two sat facing each other on folding chairs in the desolate, snowy landscape, as if in a counseling session.

He evaluated the protagonist's actions one by one, pointing out his hidden intentions.

"Bringing Lee Myeonghui along was a rash decision. She died at the convenience store last time. Do you think she'll survive any longer this time?"

"A burn on your lower abdomen, a penetrating wound on your right shoulder, a fracture in your left ankle. Right now, you don't need a psychological counselor. You need a real doctor."

"You left Lee Myeonghui at the security office. Your choice right now is no different from the one I made."

The mental landscape was not always a snowy mountain. The protagonist also had hallucinations in reality, seeing Jeong Isol sitting right next to him, having a conversation.

Sometimes Jeong Isol gave pain, sometimes composure, and sometimes, surprisingly, comfort.

"...Even if you'd chosen the other option, the situation wouldn't have been any better."

However, tone changes the meaning of all words. Bae Sejin had planned to portray this imaginary character as even more aggressive and malicious than the original Jeong Isol.

*It's an imagination, so it should be even more so.*

He thought it was natural for Jeong Isol to be more frightening, since he existed in the mind of the protagonist he had murdered.

However, during script practice, the members who read the protagonist's lines offered different opinions. Intrigued, Bae Sejin seriously asked for their thoughts, especially from one person.

Seon Ahyeon.

"Um, I... well. This is just my thought, but..."

Starting with the album, Seon Ahyeon had been playing a mysterious and eerie character in the group's universe, a bit like Jeong Isol. Perhaps that was why he had thought so deeply about the psychological states of the protagonist and Jeong Isol.

"If this character is the protagonist's imagination... wouldn't he be more human? I think a wish like, 'What if Jeong Isol had been a good person,' might have been projected onto him... More than anything..."

Then came the decisive words.

"The, the protagonist... he killed Jeong Isol, didn't he?"

"...!"

"I don't think he would have been without guilt. Because the protagonist is a good person..."

Bae Sejin thought about it deeply. To his anguish, Park Moondae offered simple advice.

"Whichever way you act it, it'll be plausible enough. You're the expert, after all."

"But if I had to choose..."

Park Moondae offered an opinion from a different angle.

"I think people would like that one more."

"Ah...!"

A more amicable Jeong Isol was more fun to watch. It was the viewer's point of view.

"...Okay. Thanks."

And that is how Jeong Isol appeared in the protagonist's mental world. He said cold and cruel things, but seemed slightly shaken by the protagonist's pain. This made viewers wonder, or even hope, that he might be compassionate.

*For me, too, this version is more fun to act...*

To be honest, a character like Jeong Isol, who lacked empathy and only valued survival, was not to Bae Sejin's taste.

So he was a little excited to act out this subtle change in the mental Jeong Isol, who seemed to be recovering some sympathy for the protagonist.

*Even though he's a fake, imagined by the protagonist.*

He was a little excited to act this out.

This was the Jeong Isol perfected through Bae Sejin's heart. He lifted his head and looked at the panicking protagonist.

"My imagination? A dream... is this a dream?"

"Think whatever you want. For now..."

Jeong Isol clicked his ballpoint pen.

"It's time to wake up."

"......"

The protagonist collapsed onto the snow. Jeong Isol did not spare him a glance.

"Cut!"

And just like that, Bae Sejin's first scene of the season got a clean OK.

"Ah, that's great! I especially like how you don't look at him there. It's strangely aesthetically pleasing," the director muttered in satisfaction. The crew cheerfully packed up the equipment.

In the next block of filming, Bae Sejin did not get a single NG. Everyone on set realized something.

Bae Sejin was not ad-libbing, but in his tone, movements, and gaze, he was leaving 'room' in his portrayal of Jeong Isol.

Room for the client. For the protagonist.

"...Our Isol's character is changing a bit, isn't he?"

"Yes. I thought it would be more appealing that way."

"Uh-huh. That's right. Keep it that way. Do it just as you've planned, Sejin."

The director confirmed it immediately.

The set filled with admiration, not shock, despite his meticulous and delicate performance. Shock comes from the unexpected, usually from people with bad reputations.

When someone already insanely good performs as if possessed, it does not cause surprise. It adds another layer to the thick trust.

*A true genius is a true genius.*

Just like that.

"That was really... really good."

"...Thank you."

After the shoot, Bae Sejin accepted a warm decaf Americano. He knew even before reviewing the footage.

*...I must have done well.*

A soft yet tight sense of fulfillment welled up inside him.

After such a fulfilling shoot, the actor playing the protagonist approached him.

"Sejin!"

"...Ah, yes."

"It's a real shame you can't come to the company dinner at that famous restaurant in Gangwon-do today. I heard you have another schedule," the actor said in a friendly, sociable manner.

Bae Sejin answered a little awkwardly, but with surprising force.

"Yes... We have a group shoot nearby. I have to join them right away, so I can't make it."

"Aah... Wow, so the TeSTAR members are nearby right now?"

"Yes."

The old Bae Sejin would have wondered why he was being spoken to, thinking, 'What's your angle?' But not anymore.

Instead, he felt a little excited at his perfect timing. He even enjoyed some small talk with the other actors before the manager arrived.

"Um, could I possibly get an autograph from Ryu Chungwoo? I've been a fan since he was on the national team..."

"...I'll ask him as soon as I get there today."

"Oh, thank you!"

Bae Sejin felt satisfied with his improved social skills.

He glanced over and saw the manager approaching from a distance.

*Finally, time to go...*

But the manager was not alone. He was talking with a staff member from the production company.

When the manager made eye contact with Bae Sejin, his face lit up.

"Sejin, just a moment."

"...?"

"We were just having a conversation..."

After hearing some unexpected news, Bae Sejin quickly headed to the mountain cabin, a twenty-minute drive away.

His heart pounded.

*Everyone must be filming already!*

What could have happened in the half-day he was gone? Maybe they had already created three or four candidates for the CM song.

Bae Sejin knew he did not contribute much to the team's songwriting.

*...But I can contribute a little to the lyrics!*

If he participated diligently and told them how much their advice helped his filming, everyone would be happy. Thinking this, he excitedly quickened his pace after getting out of the car.

However.

"...What in the world happened to you...?"

He ran into a grimy Park Moondae in the yard, his face smudged with soot.

***

"A lot has happened."

"......"

Park Moondae guided Bae Sejin into the cabin. He moved furtively, scanning his surroundings for any production staff.

Anyone watching would mistake him for a zombie apocalypse survivor, his body steeped in tension and resignation.

*Wh-what is it?*

"Did something happen?"

"Something... did happen."

It was simple. TeSTAR was not just comfortably making a CM song with food, clothing, and shelter provided.

"Well, it turns out we have to fend for ourselves..."

"......"

Do your own work yourself!

"They did say that for every CM song that passes, they'll give us an allowance and let us go into town... Before that, they said they'd hunt us down like runaway slaves if we tried to leave."

"......"

Well, even that was fine, Park Moondae said. Everyone was decent at cooking, cleaning, or manual labor. Composing songs while living a simple, rustic life was not supposed to be difficult.

However...

"We didn't get to choose our own tasks."

The roles were distributed by drawing lots based on game rankings.

And the result was...

"Seon Ahyeon is cooking...?"

"......"

"And Park Moondae is... chopping firewood?"

That was what happened. Bae Sejin broke out in a cold sweat.

Just then, Park Moondae furtively showed him something hidden in his work gloves. It was a piece of foil smudged with soot.

"I snuck out to roast some sweet potatoes. The production team hasn't caught me yet."

"...You're saying that right in front of a camera?!"

"I've bribed them all."

The camera directors gave a thumbs-up. Such was the power of fire-roasted sweet potatoes.

"......"

Bae Sejin was speechless for a moment, but he could not stay that way for long.

"Wh-what about the other members?"

"They're the hunting team."

"...?"

"Cha Eugene, Lee Sejin, and Ryu Chungwoo. The three of them are the hunting team. That's all you need to know for now."

Park Moondae started walking again with heavy steps.

Bae Sejin was thrown into confusion.

*H-Hunting?*

No, in the first place...

"What about the songwriting?"

"We haven't even started."

Shocked, the word 'songwriting' made Bae Sejin think of one member. The person Park Moondae had not mentioned. The man who produced every TeSTAR album.

"Right, what about Kim Raebin? Raebin is the one who composes..."

"He's the Darling."

"......"

Park Moondae explained.

"Because he's the youngest, he's the Darling."

"......"

"It's a role where he's not allowed to do anything."

Kim Raebin, sealed.

"Th-then who's doing the songwriting?"

"You are."

"......"

*Me?*

"Yes. You. The last lot left was for the main producer."

Bae Sejin froze at the sudden new position.

Park Moondae, grimy from tending the stove, held out a roasted sweet potato and said solemnly, "Now that you know, let's get started. We're behind on three songs... They say everyone can participate once you get the ball rolling. Ah, except for Raebin."

"......"

"We need to finish sketching out one song today to meet our labor quota... no, our deadline."

Bae Sejin hesitated, unable to take the sweet potato. He finally spoke, wanting to squeeze his eyes shut.

"Um... It's not three songs."

"...?"

"It's four."

Bae Sejin avoided Park Moondae's gaze, recalling what the production company staff had said.

"O-Our drama's production company thought it would be fun, so they requested an additional auction with the production team here... and added a PPL."

"......"

TeSTAR's labor list was updated.

❬Doll Hunter❭ CM song... no, OST added.
