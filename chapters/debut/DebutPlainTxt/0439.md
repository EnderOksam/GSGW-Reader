# Chapter 439

Controversy, promotion, and synergy. VTIC's charity concert was a meticulously planned event. A former Reset Syndrome member, an idol his entire life, executed it with perfect timing. He was, of course, the one who planned the concert.

*Hmm.*

Judan nodded as he watched everything proceed smoothly. A catastrophic event, where a building had collapsed, was restored to normal as if it had never happened. The grand finale concluded so perfectly and happily that a tension-free success story naturally followed.

*Everything is going well...*

"Judan."

"Yes." Judan quickly put away his smartphone when he heard the voice.

He did not do it from a habitual sense of pressure from his Leader, but because it was a reasonable request.

*And in fact, isn't it natural to feel some pressure and fear, considering the difference in our species?*

Despite that strikingly youthful face, Cheongryeo was a being who had built his ego over an immense amount of time. There was surely a reason why humanity has respected long-surviving elders since ancient times.

*Calm down.*

Judan muttered the words under his breath.

Cheongryeo glanced at Judan's behavior and spoke.

"I believe I told you it's fine even if you don't check."

"It's a hobby."

"Hmm."

Cheongryeo simply gazed at him for a moment before leaving, instead of saying something like 'If I tell you not to, then don't'.

It was tacit permission.

*Quite generous... no, not generous. Permission! That's an excessive abuse of authority, even for a Leader.*

*But he's been that kind of character since before our debut.*He was not the type to lash out directly, but he gave off a feeling of being pressed down from a high place.

*As a group's Leader, he's an efficient type.*

*Isn't that why VTIC has remained strong for so long?*

*I thought I'd be ruined by the time our contracts were up for renewal.*

Another member did get ruined. Judan pushed that thought aside. He instead recalled the online reactions to VTIC's concert he had been looking at.

First, he recalled the negative reactions.

[Aren't they just copying TeSTAR's charity concert?]

└Did they rent out the concept of charity concerts or something

It was a minority opinion. Some thought it was like TeSTAR's charity concert, held on the same platform after their car accident, but the news of TeSTAR's guest appearance made that opinion mostly vanish.

He admitted TeSTAR had used a novel 'audience participation' method.

*But that's a minor-league policy that only works on heavy internet users.*

The concert he, or rather VTIC, had planned was more classic and glamorous. It worked well.

[This is actually fun to watch]

[It's been a while since I've seen a concert with only songs I know, and the live vocals are so clear, it's exciting hahaha]

He did not deny that TeSTAR had been helpful. TeSTAR's surprise VCR appearance in the second part, which was already announced, was a fun element.

[Park Moondae: This is your counselor for the day, Moondae-cat]

[Maltese: (Silent scream)]

A Maltese puppy character with raised paws covered its mouth. It was a fan who had requested a counseling session with Park Moondae. To ensure anonymity, applicants had the members' animal characters composited over them like VR avatars as they shared their worries. It was a cute, heartwarming, and funny element.

*A humorous device to control the tempo between intense performances.*

[Seon Ahyeon: First, please have a glass of warm water.]

[Rudolph: (Hiccup)]

[Seon Ahyeon: Th-That will help with the hiccups too...]

"Ah, he's so cute!"

"Ahyeon is working so hard, it's really nice to see."

Judan inwardly nodded a few times while filming the 'VTIC Charity Concert Reaction' with the other members. This reaction video was content that would be released soon.

It would be released right after they... enlisted in the military.

"......"

*My god. Enlistment.*

He thought he would have quit this job before the massive hiatus, but strangely, he was still doing it. Judan nodded.

*...Still, with all this content to consume, there probably won't be many fans who turn into outsiders during the break.*

Three types of original content, two free tracks, and two music videos. He felt a sense of certainty when he also thought of the cinematic universe trailer.

Of course, Cheongryeo had made it all.

*Just when did he start preparing all this?*

He himself was only going for six months next year, yet Cheongryeo prepared so much content for the hiatus. This might be why it was hard to carelessly rebel against his superhuman Leader.

The screen moved on to the next part. The VCR ended, but instead of the stage, a new clip appeared.

[A Visit to TeSTAR's Counseling Site!]

It was a clip of VTIC making a surprise visit to TeSTAR's villa.

[Gasp]

This was the real surprise. If Judan checked the internet on his turned-off smartphone, he would see countless exclamation marks and excited reactions.

[Huuuh!?]

[Huh? Sunbaenims?]

Even TeSTAR on the screen looked like their eyes were about to pop out. This part had not been agreed upon.

*Hmph.*Judan secretly felt a sense of pride. It was all for getting great reactions.

[The kids are gonna faint hahaha]

[Look at Moondae trying to act not surprised, my precious sorry daengdaengie ㅠㅠ]

[Bae Sejin came running out barefoot hahahahaha]

[Is that TeSTAR's dorm?]

└They probably wouldn't reveal the dorm, so it looks like they rented a place

They greeted each other warmly, shared pizza, and had a short conversation about the charity concert and its meaning. Then, the scenario called for them to join forces. TeSTAR's Park Moondae started it.

[We're very sorry and disappointed that we couldn't participate on stage.]

[Sorry? You even did the counseling session to participate somehow!]

[That's right, you did more than enough.]

The members of TeSTAR looked at each other shyly. Ryu Chungwoo smiled and opened his mouth.

[Thank you. Then we'll just leave it at being disappointed.]

[In that case...]

Chaeyul replied with a bright face. He was the perfect member for the job.

[How about we do a short performance right here? A stage!]

[!]

[There are lights here... and everyone's a prepared performance master!]

[WAHAHA!]

And just like that, a performance that looked completely impromptu was confirmed.

[Let's go outside!]

The sun had already set outside. The villa was in nature, far from any city nightscape, but it had one advantage.

It had a large front yard with a string of lamp lights, befitting a villa.

[OHHHH!]

[You did your counseling in a nice place.]

VTIC and TeSTAR excitedly ran out to the front yard. They paused for a moment, then giggled.

[What should we dance to?]

[Right? I haven't thought about it!]

TeSTAR's Lee Sejin put his arms around his members' shoulders and grinned.

[Since it's our sunbaenims' concert~ isn't it only proper to do one of your songs!]

[Woah~]

Of course, it was all a prepared lineup, yet Lee Sejin delivered the line so naturally. He was so sociable it made Judan uncomfortable. Judan nodded.

The idols clapped for the chosen song and gathered in the front yard.

[Music, please!]

The grassy field glowed in the night under the twinkling lamp lights and the stage lights turned on by staff. A familiar intro began to play against the dim background.

[AHHHHH!]

[Ah, I love this song!]

The idols who ran out exchanged excited, lighthearted steps. The intro rang out like a group sing-along.

[Hey partner!

I wonder if you're thinking of me]

Night Sign.

VTIC's timeless masterpiece, released around the same time as TeSTAR's debut song, filled the front yard. The song had a dark, languid, and sexy concept. That was why it had been excluded from the charity concert's setlist, but it was different here.

[I remember it again

Night after night]

The atmosphere grew as excited as a concert or a party. Even this song showed off the strengths of a hit track.

It was a song everyone knew, creating excitement from a shared experience. This happened even though the song's original choreography did not have that kind of feeling.

The two groups mixed together, smiling. They took turns coming forward to dance the choreography.

It was a picture of VTIC occasionally stepping back to let their juniors shine. TeSTAR enjoyed their seniors' song to the fullest without overstepping.

The balance looked easier and more natural thanks to the mix of members who had already spent time together.

Then the chorus came.

[Until the end of this night

Remember my eyes]

They instantly fell into formation and performed the signature choreography. The perfectly synchronized movements looked even more amazing coming from the impromptu party-like atmosphere.

[Um, Um]

The camerawork captured the individual members' smiling or enjoying faces. Their movements were so excited they almost did not fit the song, but strangely, it worked.

[Like a night sign]

Gestures and amazing impromptu solo dance moves filled the fast-tempo song. When it was time to let loose, they really let loose.

[Woo!]

The idols followed the exclamation-like sound as a group, breaking their choreography and smiling brightly.

"Ah..."

"That was so much fun!"

The VTIC members filming their reactions also made a fuss, recalling the moment as they watched the screen.

Judan turned his head and saw Cheongryeo smiling faintly.

"......"

[Night sign!]

They shouted the last line like a sing-along. The members checked each other's movements and struck a pose in an ending formation several times larger than usual.

SWOOSH - BANG!

Fireworks exploded in the sky above them, ending the impromptu stage along with the sound of laughter.

[Wow!]

[Thank you!]

The camera emotionally captured the colors of the fireworks staining the background behind their waving faces.

It was a mid-show finale that left a powerful, lingering impression, perfect for a charity concert. The viewers' reactions were fitting.

[ㅜㅜㅜ]

[This was nice to see, it's really touching]

*It was decent.*

It had been a while since I pretended to hear a song choice for the first time on the spot, but since we were all experienced professionals, it came out naturally.

Picking a VTIC hit from before TeSTAR's debut would have been safer, but it probably would not be a problem.

*Of course, I plan to be busy if any problems arise.*

Chaeyul nodded eagerly. He then lowered his head slightly and spoke in a small, whisper-like voice. "And once we all enlist, Jaehyun will be all alone. Emotionally, I mean."

*I doubt it was much different before they enlisted.*

"You'll keep in touch with him and Kongi, right? Like I said before, he's such a mountain spirit type of person, I feel like he might even skip meals!"

*No, Cheongryeo will probably eat well, focusing on protein and fiber.*

However, I nodded. "I'll do my best."

"Great!" Chaeyul gave me an unnecessary hug with a bright face before leaving.

Judan, who had been listening, sidled up to me with a peculiar expression.

"Chaeyul has some emotional and irrational parts. It's because he places too much meaning on interpersonal relationships."

"......"

"Still, socially, people tend to accept his words more easily. Surprising, isn't it?"

*No.*

Anyway, I decided not to forget Chaeyul's words. Just that much.

*That should be the next goal.*

"Why are you nodding?"

"Because I think a good song is about to be made."

Lee Sejin was next in line. He glanced at the VTIC news on my smartphone, and our eyes met. He said casually, "Hmm~ A January comeback?"

Global recognition and evaluation.

Simply being successful was not enough. You had to be the best in the genre.

That was the top tier VTIC had held in a complete stranglehold until now.

*I'll devour everything during their military hiatus.*

Starting with the special stage at this year's award shows, where VTIC will be absent, I will take a firm hold.
