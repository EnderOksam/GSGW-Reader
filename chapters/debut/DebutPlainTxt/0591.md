# Chapter 591

Triumphant Return Home.

Triumphant Return Home is an idiom about returning to one's hometown after achieving success. In modern Korea, people use it when someone passes college entrance exams or gets a good job and comes home for the holidays.

Idols can experience this every time they go home. They live in dorms, so they are always away from their families.

Of course, there is a condition.

*You have to have good results.*

Recognition.

You must be active enough to get reactions like, "Wow, that song?" or, "I think I've heard that before!"

If you meet these conditions, you get a taste of a Triumphant Return Home every time you visit.

In that regard, TeSTAR has likely never disappointed their families.

As proof, we do this every vacation season.

"Everyone, let's do our annual event well~"

"Yes, yes."

SWISH.

Albums passed from hand to hand with the skill of a seasoned drill instructor.

We ran a signing factory, taking turns signing the latest albums and merchandise. This was the portion our parents and relatives requested.

"Great work, everyone!"

After cheerfully finishing our quotas, it was time to prepare to go home.

"It's a week this time, right? Everyone, rest well."

"Yes...!"

And here, another interesting thing happens.

In our early debut days, we all made our own plans and scattered, but at some point, we started coordinating our schedules even during vacation.

*Our families have gotten to know each other.*

For instance, Kim Raebin was taking his grandmother and grandfather on a trip to the US. He was apparently going to stay at Cha Eugene's house for a few days.

It seemed Cha Eugene had often been indebted to Kim Raebin's family since their trainee days, so they had extended an invitation.

"I've accumulated enough mileage, so I believe I can escort them comfortably in better seats!"

"That's great."

That was a rather humble thing for a guy who had earned enough to just pay for first-class seats.

Moreover, while preparing for this trip, he had contacted Ryu Chungwoo's family. It looked like they were going to join midway.

"I'd like my family to enjoy their trip in the US a little longer even after my vacation ends. I plan to fly back first to match my schedule."

"Hyung, that's so sweet of you."

"Haha."

It was a truly heartwarming scene. If we posted this on social media, the fans would probably love it.

However, as you might have guessed, I was the exception.

Well... I haven't had anyone to go with.

*They've been glancing at me ever since they started signing.*

It was because whenever we did this, I was the only one with almost nothing to take.

I would grab a few out of courtesy, but I didn't have relatives to give signed goods to like these guys did.

Not as Park Moondae, and not as Ryu Gunwoo.

Because of this, they seemed subtly concerned, and I had been receiving secret proposals for a while.

"Moondae, you should come to my house this time! Let's go surfing."

I appreciated the thought.

But I had a few things planned in Korea starting in a few days.

*I'll rest a bit, then get to work.*

I politely declined.

"Ooh, Moondae, does that mean you'll be in Korea for the whole vacation?"

That's right.

"Then stop by our house for a bit and hang out~ It's nearby. My mom really wants to see you."

"...?? Why me?"

I stared at Keun Sejin, baffled, but he put on a serious face.

"My goodness, my mom packed you galbi-jjim for your birthday every year... and you don't want to see her even when you have the time... Have you forgotten the taste of her galbi-jjim?"

"......"

"So Park Moondae was this cold of a person. I can't imagine how disappointed my mom will be..."

Stop.

"I'll go."

"Hm? What was that?"

He still has a talent for getting on my nerves.

"...I said I'll go."

The guy grinned.

"Good thinking~!"

"......"

I wanted to punch him just once. Just once.

Just as I debated whether to clench my fist.

TAP, TAP.

"Uh, um."

I turned my head and saw Seon Ahyeon looking at me with an expression full of anticipation.

"My family, they want to see you too, Moondae... Um, they were wondering how you're doing."

"......"

"Ah, if it's too much trouble...!"

"...I'll go."

"Kehahak!"

And just like that, my plan to 'laze around the dorm at the beginning of the vacation' was canceled.

"Ooh, then Ahyeon, you're stopping by my house first, right?"

"Yup!"

"Great, great... Good, good. We're going to have a fun first day of vacation~"

Keun Sejin packed his bags, overjoyed.

However, I didn't see him on his smartphone, chattering away with something like 'add two more people here'.

"Aren't you going to call your parents?"

"Hm?"

"I think we should at least tell them we're coming."

Keun Sejin grinned meaningfully.

"Actually, my mom... she doesn't even know I'm coming today."

"...!"

"She's always so busy with this and that... She's holding an event today, too!"

What kind of event?

***

Lee Sejin's mother. Who was she?

She held a position in a local community volunteer group and was a neighborhood celebrity, even without her son's fame.

As befitting a family of extreme E's on the MBTI, her house was bustling with acquaintances today as well.

This was a sort of after-party for their volunteer work.

Her eldest son had already succeeded as an idol, and her second daughter attended university and lived in a dorm, so it was easy for her to invite people over as she pleased!

"It's all because the kids grew up so well~"

"Oh, what are you talking about. The house feels so empty these days!"

In this gathering, a few middle and elementary school students sat to the side with dead-fish eyes, fiddling with their smartphones. Their parents had dragged them along under the pretense of 'this is an experience too'.

*So boring...*

She had been told it was rude to wear earphones, so watching TikTakTok on mute made her wonder what she was even doing here.

[Her son is some famous celebrity? An idol, they said. Who? My mom doesn't know today's kids well..., but she said the name sounded familiar!]

That story had slightly tempted her, but of course, there was no trace of that idol in this house!

It was also awkward to ask who it was directly.

Even if she asked, she knew there was a high chance she would be disappointed. She wasn't a kid full of dreams and hopes anymore!

*There aren't even that many famous male idols these days, what was I thinking?!*

Feeling ashamed of herself for falling for such words as a middle schooler, she lowered her head back to her smartphone.

The gathering was about to end soon anyway. She just needed her battery to last a little longer.

It was then.

Ding-dong.

"Jeong-ah, someone's here!"

"Hm?"

The intercom rang.

It seemed someone had arrived very late.

The homeowner reacted with some confusion, but the middle schooler paid it no mind. She just reflexively glanced at the door as it opened.

And so... she witnessed it.

"Oh my, there are a lot of people here!"

A tall, well-built man easily opened the heavy front door and stepped inside.

He took off his black hat and mask, revealing his refreshing and well-defined features.

"Just a moment. Coming through~ Is my mother inside?"

The person who greeted everyone while fanning his face... that person was.

TeSTAR's Lee Sejin!

The Grand Prize-winning idol, number one on the music charts, the person she had just seen in a multi-million view video on her InHeart page, had now appeared in the same space as her.

"......"

But the word ended in a single scream.

Because it wasn't just one person coming through the front door!

"Uh, hello...!"

A person wearing a neat, well-worn coat of clearly expensive material bowed his head deeply as he entered, then looked up.

The face that was revealed was...

*Oh, gasp.*

An incredible beauty.

He looked like that in photos and videos, but seeing him in real life made her wonder if he was even human. His face was the size of a fist, yet his eyes, nose, and mouth were all clearly defined within it.

That's right. This one looked familiar too!

*S-Seon Ahyeon!*

And following behind him was a person with a characteristically indifferent expression.

He had all sorts of cute nicknames like 'puppy' and 'apple', but in person, he had a pale, cool, and aloof impression.

Which meant he was handsome.

"I'm sorry for showing up so suddenly."

Park Moondae.

*P-Park Moondae!!*

Three members of TeSTAR! In the flesh!

"Son!"

"Mother~ You have a lot of guests!"

Seeing the homeowner, the team leader of the volunteer group, warmly embrace Lee Sejin, the middle schooler had a lightning-bolt realization.

The son of this house was from TeSTAR!

"Ah, it's nothing much, but... I happened to pick something up on my way home! The timing is perfect."

In the midst of it all, Lee Sejin chattered like a living person(?), smiling and greeting everyone. He and his members even handed out the items they brought.

And that was how an album signed by TeSTAR fell into her hands.

*Holy crap, what a score...*

She even got a handshake from one of the members while he was handing it to her.

"Um, this..."

"Yes, yes yes, yes... Oh my. Um, a handshake... just once. If it's possible!"

"Ah, yes. Of course...!"

The middle schooler's hands trembled as she shook hands with Seon Ahyeon, and she almost cried.

*Even his fingers are handsome, this is crazy!*

Thank you, Mother!

[OMG OMG I just saw T-STAR]

A little later, on the way home, the middle schooler logged into her messenger app with trembling hands. She resolved to be a very good daughter to the mother who had dragged her to this event!

***

"What do we do! Oh my..."

Is this the satisfying taste of cider you get when you reveal your power in a 'hidden-power-loser' meta?

After the album presentation ceremony ended, I watched the backs of the guests who reluctantly left.

The shocked and ecstatic reactions of the people, whose eyes looked like they would pop out when TeSTAR's Lee Sejin appeared as the son of the house, were quite... well, impressive.

*So this is what it feels like to live as a celebrity.*

I usually didn't get to feel it since we were always among ourselves. Experiencing TeSTAR's fame in daily life for a change wasn't bad.

And after the people left.

The mother of Keun Sejin, the star of this surprise, was...

"You, really! Aigoo!"

"Euhahat!"

She smacked the punk on the back.

*He deserved it.*

An unannounced son who barges into a house full of guests.

Of course, even while getting hit, Keun Sejin grinned broadly.

The person hitting him also looked proud, so it could be seen as a wise decision.

"Ah, this is what you call a surprise act of filial piety!"

The guy skillfully avoided more beatings, stretched out coolly, and sat down comfortably in the living room.

I accepted the drink he offered and nodded slightly.

"Did you do it to save your mother's face?"

"There was that, and also, it seemed like some people were developing a grudge against my mom when they were blocked from asking me for favors through her~ Hmm, so I thought I'd block them directly this time."

Right.

I had cooperated partly because I roughly understood that psychology.

[But I'm going to stop by my house first. You two can rest at the dorm~]

I had caught the guy as he was about to leave with this message, heard the situation, and accompanied him.

It had been several years since TeSTAR became this famous.

By now, it wasn't rare for people smelling money or connections to approach systematically.

*I have to cooperate with things like this.*

If one person in the group gets screwed, the whole group takes a hit, doesn't it?

I nodded, and Keun Sejin smiled sheepishly.

"Hmm, so it would have been fine for me to come in alone first... but thanks, really!"

"It's nothing...!"

Seon Ahyeon smiled happily. Seeing Keun Sejin sitting there with a similar expression was so cheesy it made my face itch, but...

*Well, it's a good thing.*

I just smiled too.

After seeing the guests off, Keun Sejin's mom returned and shot an affectionate glare at her son.

"You made your friends who came to visit work, really! Still, he really likes our Moondae and Ahyeon. He's all grown up, but he brags about his friends to his mom so much..."

"Noo, Mother! Is there an age limit to cherishing friendship!"

"Aigoo, listen to him, so silver-tongued!"

"I take after my mom!"

Well, a happy family.

After that, we had a fun time without any particular incidents.

"I'm always on the receiving end... It might not be as delicious as yours, Mother, but I gave it a try."

"Oh my, oh my!"

We had brought some holiday food with us, as if on a belated holiday vacation. Lazing around in someone else's house while eating it was a very unique experience.

It was like that at Bae Sejin's house during a past holiday vacation, but I guess I still couldn't get used to it.

Still... I didn't hate it.

[KYAAAAK?!]

"Euhaha! Ah, I should have given it to her sooner!"

Eventually, we watched a reaction video sent via messenger of Keun Sejin's younger sister screaming upon receiving Ryu Chungwoo's signed merchandise.

"We'll be back after a quick stop at Ahyeon's place!"

We moved on and headed to Ahyeon's house.

And... we received a fervent welcome there as well.

In a slightly different sense.

"Welcome~ Ahyeon, and Moondae and Sejin!"

POP!

Confetti exploded in front of my eyes.

This happened the moment we stepped into the entrance of a house in Seoul with the outrageous spec of having a garden.

In front of us stood Seon Ahyeon's parents, their faces practically screaming with excitement.

"...Thank you."

This wasn't a surprise visit, but perhaps because of that, the feeling of 'We prepared for this day!' was openly conveyed.

*Just like Seon Ahyeon's family.*

I was truly grateful, but it was just as burdensome.

However, just as I was thinking that, his parents carefully spoke to me when the other guys had stepped away for a moment.

"Thank you so much for always getting along so well with Ahyeon... Every time I see, Moondae is taking care of our Ahyeon... I always wanted to do something for you, but I never had the chance."

"...It's nothing."

"What do you mean, it's nothing. Even family would find that hard to do."

It seemed this family hadn't prepared for a simple visit from their son's work colleague, but for someone they were genuinely grateful for and close to.

*Good grief.*

I pressed the back of my neck.

"You're sleeping over tonight, right?"

"Um, if it's not too much trouble..."

"What are you talking about. Of course, we'd love that!"

And so, I was treated very well, even being assigned a room.

That's right. I wasn't crashing in Seon Ahyeon's room... they assigned me my own.

*What kind of house is this in Seoul?*

I had suspected it since getting a ride in their foreign car, but it seemed they were definitely from a rich family.

After unpacking my things in 'my room', I followed an excited Seon Ahyeon and ended up...

"This way, this is my room...!"

I got to see his room.
