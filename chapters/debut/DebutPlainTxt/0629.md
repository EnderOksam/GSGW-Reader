# Chapter 629

An hour and a half remained until the attraction ride.

Park Moondae's first fansite master simply endured.

"Yeonju, I think your hair's caught. If you take off that shark head for a second..."

"It's fine! I'm comfortable!"

"Uh... okay! Wow, look at this. The wall here, it's like the graffiti our boys drew..."

"Oh, that's so cool. I'll take a picture right away."

*Save me.*

The fansite master, her face buried in the shark plush, screamed internally.

TeSTAR had not yet noticed the strange phenomenon happening here. Fortunately or unfortunately, her friend, a former graduate student, also did not seem to realize why she acted this way.

Her friend was so carefree, unable to imagine that the three men moving to ride the attraction with them were TeSTAR.

In that moment, the fansite master snapped to her senses.

*Should I tell her?*

That right next to her was Park Moondae.

*We've been fans together for so long...*

She felt her friend would feel betrayed and regretful if she found out later, but telling her now would probably make it difficult to get on the attraction.

After imagining her friend stopping, screaming, and then regretting it, the fansite master made a decision.

*...I'll wait for the right moment to tell her!*

She decided to wait for a quiet moment.

Meanwhile, she could hear the TeSTAR members conversing in English beside her.

[It's darker in here than I thought.]

[Hmm... Sci-fi, horror, spy. All the keywords are screaming 'I'm dark,' aren't they? Hyung, of course it is.]

Even though it was English she could not understand, their voices pierced her ears.

One was a clean accent, the other a sharp accent that popped as if tossed out casually.

*Oh my god.*

The fansite master swallowed hard.

She desperately tried to ignore her peripheral vision, which the shark plush obscured, but it was not very effective.

[...but, don't forget. This is an investigation.]

[Don't worry. If I don't know, who would?]

[Mhm. I'll examine everything carefully... down to the smallest details.]

*I wish I was good at listening to English...!*

*Wait, if I could understand what they're saying here, would that be eavesdropping?*

As she thought, "Even so...", she missed a step.

"Ah."

It was the natural consequence for someone so distracted.

The moment she quickly regained her footing, a concerned voice spoke in English from her side.

[Is your ankle okay?]

It was Seon Ahyeon.

The fansite master almost leaped like a cat but managed to contain it with a gesture.

"Fine, okay!"

Seon Ahyeon, in his navy blue baseball cap, gave a small nod.

*His face is crazy small, the size of a fist,*the fansite master thought. He quietly turned his head away.

Phew.

*I'm so happy I could die.*

After an hour of waiting in what felt like heaven or hell, an announcement came.

[It is time to clock in.]

Attraction staff wearing suits and masks that shimmered with holograms guided them to a door.

"Wow..."

A steel door, covered with "Handle with Care" warnings and company logos, seemed to lead to a basement.

SCREEECH!

The door opened with a heavy sound. Inside was a room that felt like a spy's waiting area, with a giant holographic screen floating on one wall.

It looked like it was about to deliver a mission.

A video began to play.

"Oh."

[This company is too suspicious.]

A short, edited clip of TeSTAR's own content, dubbed in English, played like a cinematic trailer.

The employees went missing after a monster chased them in a suspicious company building, only to be reborn as AIs and cryogenically frozen.

Then, a notice and an announcement appeared.

[You are their replacement.]

You are their replacement.

PSSSHHH.

Dry ice billowed out, and the emergency lights inside turned on.

As hearts began to pound, a new message appeared.

[Would you like to record your clock-in?]

Blink. A code reader next to the hologram flashed.

"Our employee IDs!"

"Ah."

So this was the attraction's interactive part.

When they held their purchased employee ID merchandise over the device, a light flashed. The message 'Welcome to KIS, employee-nim,' appeared, and the clanking code reader transformed into a music box.

"That's amazing!"

"Whoa!"

Moreover, there was an additional effect.

Flash. Red energy appeared in the music box's cracks, and it began to bang as if it would burst open.

[AI Hidden Code Confirmed / Danger!]

This seemed to be the effect of reading the code attached to the album app.

"Ooh!"

The alarm rang for a moment before it turned off.

It was a little creepy. Their hearts pounded from the incredible realism, which felt like casting a spell or activating a secret function.

[Oh, that's cool!]

Then, something truly heart-pounding happened.

Cha Eugene suddenly leaned his upper body forward.

*Hey!*

[Is it okay with you if I film that?]

"Ye... Yes."

He held a GoPro and said something like "Can I," so he must have wanted to film it.

The fansite master nodded, and Cha Eugene swiftly bent down toward her.

"...!"

The fansite master held her breath. Her focus unknowingly drawn to the feel of the air displaced by Cha Eugene's torso as he swooped right past her.

The scent of the wind, and the smell of skin lotion...

*Stop!*

*What the hell is this nonsense!*

She barely resisted the urge to rip the shark head off.

Meanwhile, Cha Eugene finished filming the warning light with his GoPro. He tipped the brim of his bucket hat and straightened up.

[Cool. Thanks.]

"......"

*So... this is what it felt like.*

He was one of the members famous for being devastatingly charming at fansigns, but seeing him without a table in between was even more intense.

It was natural for him.

*Even as a civilian, you'd be guilty, you crazy bastard.*

Muttering things in her head that she could never say out loud, she continued to follow the staff's guidance.

[I will guide you to the office.]

Faithful to the AI concept, the attraction staff seemed to check the queue, changing the expression on their hologram mask to a smiling face.

*The staff here are all well-built too...*

Nodding to herself that this was indeed a major corporation's theme park, she walked into the smoke. She finally stood before the ride vehicle on the rails.

The vehicle she was about to board was...

"Gasp!"

It was a very conceptual and realistic roller coaster that looked like a modern office chair and desk set. It was a unique ride vehicle that fit the concept perfectly.

"It's so strange..."

"That's cool, isn't it?"

The three-seater in the back where TeSTAR got on looked like a company reception sofa, complete with a small table.

Just as the fansite master tried not to stare too obviously at them enjoying the ride, her friend next to her even attempted a little translation.

"Doesn't it sound like they're saying they're so excited they can't breathe? I guess people think the same way in every country."

"I-I guess so."

*They're from the same country.*

Her friend still had not caught on to the situation, even though she had briefly made eye contact with Seon Ahyeon when she almost fell earlier.

However, there was no time to think about it any longer.

The safety bar came down, and a staff member displayed a giant holographic sign.

[10 seconds until elevator opens]

The chair vibrated from the engine.

When the countdown finished, the vehicle shot forward through the elevator shaft structure in the ceiling.

"Whoa!"

"Awesome!"

With a burst of speed, the rail was sucked into a dilapidated and broken seventh-floor office hallway.

Feeling the wind, the fansite master momentarily forgot about TeSTAR in the back seat and screamed with joy.

*This is fun!*

It was a premature judgment.

"Aaaaargh!"

"Aaaah!"

This attraction boasted thrills on par with a roller coaster.

The structure combined a haunted house with a dark ride. You would race on the vehicle, then get off to explore an area, repeating this pattern two or three times.

*So that's why multiple teams entered at once!*

Since the space was large, the turnover rate seemed surprisingly good. However, she could not dwell on that thought for long.

A monster was chasing them.

"Aaargh!"

"Crazy, crazy!"

Very cleverly, the music box monsters would only chase them up close when people were on the ride, drawing out scream after scream.

Right in front of her, the music box monster opened its mouth wide.

"Gulp!"

*I-I can feel its breath...!*

She got goosebumps, wondering why they had implemented such a detail.

So TeSTAR were not cowards in their own content, they were brave. If she had to run away from this, she would have fainted. For real.

"M-my legs have no strength."

"......"

In the end, when she got off the roller coaster for the second time, her legs trembled.

The sign telling you to call a staff member if you wanted to leave mid-ride was not there for nothing.

*No wonder the monster's quality was too good to just be for their own content...*

They must have brought this one from here.

She walked unsteadily, refusing her friend's offer for help, down a creepy underground hallway with flickering lights.

"Are you okay?"

"Ah! Yes..."

Someone spoke to her.

No, not just someone. Park Moondae.

A tall man with a hoodie pulled down low over his head.

"...??"

*I thought my heart was going to drop out in stages.*

*What? Is this real? No, why is he speaking to me in Korean?*

*Hey, what are you going to do if you get caught...!*

Of course, usually, only a fansite master could recognize a celebrity just by hearing their deliberately altered voice in a foreign country, but still.

*What if I continue the conversation by asking, "Are you Korean?"...*

However, unable to miss the chance for a 'conversation with her favorite,' she ended up asking reflexively.

"Are you Korean?"

*Damn it!*

However, Park Moondae was unfazed.

Under the dim hallway lights, a low, calm voice came from behind his sunglasses and white KF mask.

"I am Korean."

His words carried a strange nuance, as if the people he was with were not.

*Such a smart kid. I wonder whose idol he is...*

"......"

"Ah, look over there."

Just as the fansite master was about to fall into a trance, her favorite pointed at something beside her.

The end of the hallway was visible.

"A basement?!"

[Employee Storage]

In the end, the attraction concludes with a grand finale where you destroy a terrifying altar inside, save all the employees, and free the AI from the computer.

[AI Release Complete!]

"The scale is incredible."

"Wow, this is fun."

It would be even more fun for someone who had seen TeSTAR's own content, especially a fan.

*And I even have the main characters right next to me...*

This time, she stuck with her friend and enjoyed the taste of the attraction, which stimulated all five senses.

However, the ending left her with a slightly peculiar impression.

"I think we go that way!"

A back door opened as the computer monitor turned off.

The attraction's visitors passed through a final hallway where, thanks to the darkness, the reflections on the glass windows shimmered like mirrors.

"Huh?"

"......"

The silhouette reflected in the glass was not her wearing the shark plush.

Reflected within it... was a black torso.

"Wow!"

The black torso, which moved just like her, was fascinating and eerie.

It was probably an LED screen.

*Oh my gosh!*

After marveling at the amazing technology for a moment, she felt a strange sensation as she pondered its meaning.

*If this is a kind of mirror... does that mean I'm the torso?*

To think the visitor was the torso.

The premise was that the visitors of this attraction had come to save, or perhaps confirm, TeSTAR.

But their true identity...

*Is a doll...*

It was a chilling twist that everyone was an AI doll, but a fan of TeSTAR would probably think a little differently.

*...Hmm.*

In the intro, Seon Ahyeon had danced while hugging the torso, shaded it with a parasol, and then held a grand funeral for it.

When she saw that intro, she had thought it was a bit creepy and had an artistic beauty.

Thinking about it again, it also seemed sad.

This situation, too.

*In the end, did the audience replace TeSTAR's position?*

Or perhaps they were holding a funeral with courtesy and affection for the visitors who failed.

Mulling over that thought with a lingering feeling, she slowly exited the torso mirror hallway.

She saw a light.

"Ah, over there!"

"This must be the last ride, right?"

Beyond the door, the final roller coaster was waiting.

There, too, was a reader that recognized the employee IDs.

[Would you like to record your clock-out?]

A fun, magical effect popped up, just like at the entrance.

The effect of the Hidden Code was added as well.

[AI Hidden Code Confirmed / Danger!]

"......"

With a flash, her name appeared on the hologram.

[AI Yeonju / Staff]

"......"

It was the nickname she had set in the Dreamland app.

She stared at it with her mouth agape at the amazing interaction that enhanced the immersion.

"......"

Feeling a gaze, she turned her head to find Park Moondae beside her.

When their eyes met, he smiled as if to say, 'Isn't this fun?'

She could tell.

Even though the lower half of his face was covered, she could see his eyes crinkling behind his dark sunglasses.

"......"

*You crazy bastard.*

*The fact that he seems to have no ulterior motives is the bigger problem. My heart! Huh?!*

"Aaaargh!"

"Aah!"

Using the final roller coaster course as an excuse, the fansite master screamed her heart out and finished the attraction with a sense of release.

*That was fun.*

Wrecked by the immense mental exhaustion and thrill, the fansite master trudged toward the exit.

She was just about to step out into the bright sunlight of the amusement park.

Thump.

She heard footsteps stop behind her.

"Thank you. I saw something amazing thanks to you, ma'am."

Park Moondae had spoken to her again.

He pointed at the fansite master's employee ID.

"Thank you for sharing."

His tone was even softer and more gentle than his usual way of speaking on TV or during promotions. It was as if a foreigner or a Korean living abroad had learned a Seoul accent. He seemed to be deliberately using that voice to avoid being recognized.

*Even though I already know everything!*

*Fuck...*

*Thinking about it again, how could he be so smart and cute?*

How many days in her life had she cursed so much as a high compliment? Come to think of it, they were all related to Park Moondae. Anyway.

"Ah, it's nothing."

"It is."

Park Moondae firmly denied her and gave her a nod.

The moment she thought it was a farewell, he spoke.

"Thank you. Then I'll see you at our concert, noona."

"......"

Park Moondae ran off with the other members and disappeared behind the attraction.

"...? ...!!"

*Did I, did I just...*

"Ye-Yeonju!"

CREEEAK!

The fansite master turned her head to the side so stiffly it almost made a sound. Her former graduate student friend, who had run out late, shouted with a beet-red face.

"Moondae! Moondae! TeSTAR!"

"Uh, uh-huh..."

It seemed her friend had also noticed at some point but could not find a way to tell her. No wonder she had gotten so quiet.

However, the main reason her friend spoke to her was more than that.

Her friend held out her arms.

In her arms were things that had not been there before.

"Th-th... this, the boys gave me this and left..."

It was the business card merchandise with Park Moondae, Seon Ahyeon, and Cha Eugene's names on them.

They were items for sale. The only difference was they had personally signed them.

[Park Moondae]

"......"

"They said thank, thank you! They said thank you!"

*Those crazy bastards... they already knew.*

*That they were pretending not to know for her sake.*

"......"

"Yeonju?"

The fansite master sank to the ground.

*I hit the jackpot...*

It was a golden shackle that would last a lifetime.
