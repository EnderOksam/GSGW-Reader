# Chapter 521

Ruined Industrial Complex

That day, TeSTAR received an enthusiastic welcome from the husband-and-wife CEOs of Ruined Industrial Complex. The group was confirmed to sing the wedding song.

"Oh, it's been a while since we've sung a wedding song."

"Sejin, have you... ever sung at a wedding before?"

"Not by myself, but when my high school teacher got married, I sang with all the other kids!"

For reference, a video of that performance had been discovered on WeTube early in our debut. It was titled 'TeSTAR Lee Sejin's Wedding Song lol'.

[Anyone can tell he's an idol trainee]

[His posture is on a whole other level lololol]

The video had become a minor topic of conversation.

As expected of men who became idols in their early twenties, that was the full extent of this team's wedding song experience.

Me?

*As if.*

I had cut off contact with everyone while preparing for the civil service exam. How could I have possibly sung at a wedding? I did get a request to take pictures once, but they wanted unpaid labor, so I didn't reply and just blocked them.

Thanks to that, our wedding song preparations began with preliminary research. The members gathered in the living room as always, passionately pitching songs that came to mind.

They wanted bright and happy songs.

"What about... 'Picnic'!"

"We have quite a few ballads in our albums, right?"

Title tracks, B-sides, and various love songs appeared as candidates and were listed out. However, not a single song felt like 'this is it'.

"Hmm, they all feel a bit bland," Ryu Chungwoo said.

"I know, right," Bae Sejin agreed.

Even songs that we had sung but weren't technically ours were nominated.

"Park Moondae, you know that song you sang on ❬The Singer I Made❭? The one that's famous as a wedding song."

"That's a good one too, but the part distribution might be a bit difficult."

"...You think so?"

You bet. Of course, there was another reason.

*It's a song that's excessively well-known for being sung by me alone.*

I could already imagine the countless bastards who would try to compare the version I sang by myself to a version sung by TeSTAR as a group.

*There's no need to give them something to pick on.*

This was especially true since a recent surge of people had been trying to provoke TeSTAR by using Bae Sejin. They wouldn't miss this sweet opportunity to tear down either me personally or TeSTAR.

In the end, Bae Sejin accepted his defeat in the majority vote with a slightly dissatisfied expression.

"Alright."

Every time an opinion was raised, it was put on hold or rejected for various reasons. Eventually, the suggestions grew random, with songs completely unrelated to us popping up.

"I want to do this one!" Cha Eugene shouted.

"Eugene, let's save this for when we're proposing to our fans," Keun Sejin said, shutting down the idea of a 30-second proposal challenge song trending on TikTakTok.

"A better song!"

"I'll try... to think hard...!"

The discussion grew more and more serious. Honestly, it felt like we were overthinking it for a wedding song.

*I was just thinking we should do something cute and simple.*

Meanwhile, I tried to subtly coax some opinions out of STier Kim Raebin. He just listened attentively in a formal posture, probably thinking he had no authority here. It was driving me crazy.

*Should we just sing a medley and get it over with?*

Fortunately, after about an hour, Ryu Chungwoo, acting like a leader, sorted out the situation.

"Well, shouldn't we just perform the song that the couple getting married wants?"

"Ah."

It was a very reasonable opinion. It was someone else's celebration, so taking a risk with a surprise was a bit much. It was better to play it safe.

"I'll contact them right away," Ryu Chungwoo said.

"Good."

And the wedding song that the husband-and-wife CEOs of Ruined Industrial Complex wanted was...

[Wow! Then, you know that first collaboration song we did, the Section 127 trailer song! I'd love to hear that!]

"......"

That was a bit...

*Weren't the lyrics something like 'I won't die, I'll survive'?*

Singing that at a wedding might sound like we were encouraging discord.

[Or this comeback's title track is great too! 'Roll the Dice'!]

*They want us to start a free-for-all at their wedding?*

It seemed these people's thought process had been distorted. They were just requesting songs they wanted to hear, regardless of the wedding.

"Haha, yes. Just a moment."

I briefly paused the call, muted the sound, and looked around. The members desperately started throwing out opinions.

"Don't we have a song that sounds like it's congratulating a couple! How about recommending something like that?" Keun Sejin asked.

"I know. We don't have one," Bae Sejin replied. "Why! Something like 'High Five'... oh, that would sound like we're proposing."

"Exactly. That's why my suggestion was rejected," Keun Sejin grumbled.

"...Then what song should we sing?"

A dizzying silence fell. Just then, Seon Ahyeon raised his hand.

"Um, can't we just... sing what they want?"

"Huh, Ahyeon?"

"Even if it's a little awkward, I think those songs would be the most meaningful for them...! It would remind them... that they achieved great results with the company they run together. I think it would make them happy."

"......"

"......"

A few of the members seemed moved by his sincere expression. However, I quickly got my brain working. I did some calculations.

*It would definitely generate buzz.*

There was a story to it. The CEOs of a game company get married and are congratulated by a top-tier idol group with a song from their game. I could already hear the clicks rolling in on WeTube.

But there was no need to voice this conclusion. I nodded in agreement, pretending to be one of the moved members.

"That's true. Since they said they want to hear it."

This time, there were no particular objections.

"Eung!" Seon Ahyeon nodded with a bright face.

"Ahem, so it's decided then?"

"Yep~"

And so, we politely resumed our call with the CEOs of Ruined Industrial Complex.

"We'll prepare one of the two songs you mentioned."

It was the wedding song they chose. Let them take responsibility for any sense of incongruity with the wedding.

[Really? Just a moment... come here, quick. They said they'll do it!]

[Awesome.]

[Wow, I'm so excited already! Thank you so much!]

They were people full of the will to live life doing what they truly wanted. After listening to the CEOs chatter away live, the call ended on an amicable note.

[Well then, we'll see you on the day!]

After hanging up the phone, we looked at each other, then nodded.

"...Should we practice?"

Right. A conceptual male idol group's intense and powerful title track was confirmed as a wedding song.

"Um... but what kind of song is 'Bonus Book'?" Cha Eugene asked.

First, let's educate this guy.

~~~

After the call, I returned to my room and played the song that STier Kim Raebin hadn't heard yet. It was 'Bonus Book', the background music from the trailer for our collaboration with ❬Section 127❭.

"This is also a song I composed..."

"That's right."

Sitting between the beds, STier Kim Raebin watched the trailer, mesmerized. I naturally followed up by playing 'Better Me', the title track from that era which shared some of its melody with the collaboration song. I even showed him the performance.

Specifically, Kim Raebin's one-person fancam.

[FIND A BETTER ME]

With every rhythmic move that matched the riff melody, cheers erupted, piercing through the music. When Kim Raebin, wearing a mask with a zipper over its mouth, ripped it off and threw it away to start his rap, the cheers became a deafening roar.

WAAAAAH-!

I opened my mouth. "You're pretty good, aren't you?"

"......"

Kim Raebin didn't answer for a long time. He just stared at his own image in the vertical video. An answer finally came around the time the song ended.

"I'm not sure. Was my performance in this stage well-received?"

Yeah. You were told you looked like you'd jumped right out of a cyberpunk comic, but Kim Raebin's expression was ambiguous.

"You don't like it?"

"I can see that the audience's reaction is very favorable, but I can't judge if I'm doing well..."

Hmm.

"Ah, but I am indirectly aware of it by seeing the reactions!"

"Right."

I crossed my arms. As I recalled, there was fundamentally no difference in the way STier Kim Raebin and TeSTAR Kim Raebin managed the stage.

*If I had to point out a difference, is it just that the songs TeSTAR Kim Raebin performed were better?*

And he was more proactive, using his movements and expressions without being self-conscious and throwing in ad-libs, but it wasn't a difference so stark as to make them seem like different people. They were the same person, after all.

*But STier Kim Raebin has concluded that he's bad on stage, while TeSTAR Kim Raebin isn't sure if he's good?*

This was the puzzling part. Originally, this guy had a very good eye for stage presence.

*As someone with a talent for producing, he makes sharp judgments.*

He was almost cruel about it. This was an attitude and ability he'd had since before our debut, during ❬Idol Inc.❭.

For example, do you know why he picked Bae Sejin out of so many people during the second team competition, the first time he and I were on a team together? Even though Bae Sejin at the time was a beginner who had reluctantly joined the idol survival show due to pressure from his agency.

[Because he doesn't seem like the type to cause conflict in the team to get more parts, and his expressiveness in his given parts is good!]

In short, he judged that since Bae Sejin's skills were lacking, he would take fewer parts, but he would clench his teeth and properly deliver the parts he did receive.

*He identified Bae Sejin's only high stats, talent and perseverance.*

His performance evaluation was coldly clear. For a guy like this to have problems with his self-assessment, this obsessive talk of 'being bad on stage' might be related to a deeper trauma than I expected.

*I'd really like to talk to him a bit more.*

I wanted to gently coax him, give it a few days, and fish for a deep conversation. The problem was that Kim Raebin's own intention was now firm.

"As expected, I would like to request that you help me regain my memories as quickly as possible!"

Hmm. If I refused to restore them now and tried to continue the conversation, the probability of making him more anxious...

*Would be higher.*

I quickly made my decision. Without changing my expression, I gave a gentle nod.

"Alright. Then shall we do it today?"

"Yes!"

That night, before midnight, I called the other members.

"You're going to restore them now?"

"Yeah. He said he wants them back before we sing the wedding song."

"Mmm... Okay."

Soon, Kim Raebin was settled in the middle of the living room as if he were about to receive a birthday celebration.

"Kim Raebin, did you eat a lot? You're going to sleep soon, so eat well!" Cha Eugene said.

"I've eaten enough! And I've already made my own preparations after seeing Chungwoo's case!"

STier Kim Raebin, who answered quite bravely, lay down on the bedding.

"I'm counting on you!"

You got it. I brought up the Status Window, sifted through the two types of pop-ups, and called up the one I wanted. The Preview guide pop-up.

I looked at it and muttered.

*End Preview.*

Without any major effects, the hologram flashed.

Vmmm.

And, in an instant, the characteristically unfinished interface design of Preview vanished.

[Complete!]

And then everything fell silent.

*Hoo.*

I tore my gaze from the Status Window and turned my head. Kim Raebin was probably now, like the other members, deep in sleep for a few hours...

*Wait.*

Kim Raebin, lying on the bedding, was staring with his eyes wide open.

"...?"

"Um... Moondae, you just did it, right?"

Yeah. We looked at Kim Raebin again.

*What the.*

Kim Raebin opened his mouth.

"I apologize! I seem to have felt dizzy for a moment... So, has Chungwoo's memory returned?"

"Huh?"

"Wha- Whaaat?"

Seeing the members' simultaneously dumbfounded expressions, Kim Raebin nodded as if he understood and shot up from the bedding.

"...! It seems Moondae hasn't started yet. Did you perhaps wait because I dozed off?"

"Raebin."

"Yes?"

"What were we just doing?"

The guy answered with a slightly puzzled attitude.

"...? Moondae was about to try something called 'Preview'."

And then everyone realized. TeSTAR's memories had returned to Kim Raebin... but it seemed his STier memories had evaporated. Including the entire last few days that STier Kim Raebin had experienced.

"......"

"I apologize. I will maintain a clear mind so as not to further disrupt the proceedings!"

"...About that."

I pressed my eyelids.

*Preview, you son of a...!*

A few days later, at a wedding hall located in Seoul.

"Congratulations."

"Oh my, it's been a while!"

A guest attending her cousin's wedding was inwardly impressed by the hall entrance. It was filled with strangers and various congratulatory wreaths.

*He's made it big.*

The hot gossip in the family was that her grand-uncle's son, her cousin, had hit it big with some game company and even bought a building in Seoul. The elders, who used to detest computer games, loved money and business.

*Capitalism is really something...*

The sociology major shook her head and got in line to give her congratulatory money. Of course, she had no interest in games either. She only had a vague impression that her cousin had made some famous mobile game.

Anyway, they said he'd made a lot of money, and the wedding that had just begun certainly showed it.

*They're doing it in a really big and unique way.*

The hall was filled with people of various young ages, likely met through business, and company employees. The slightly unconventional proceedings and unique events, seemingly taken from a game, gave it a fresh feel.

*The food will probably be delicious too.*

As the guest made that judgment, the wedding ceremony entered its final phase after a very short and concise sermon. It was time for the wedding song.

[For the wedding song... Wow, this is just what you'd expect from the wedding of the CEOs of Ruined Industrial Complex.]

*What's he talking about.*

She looked at the host indifferently. Other people had similar expressions. The host, looking nervous, shouted with a smile.

[For the wedding song... we have the idol group TeSTAR!]

"HUUUUH!?"

In that moment, she and most of the other guests nearly jumped out of their seats.

*What, what?*

The moment she doubted her own ears, the host's voice rang out again.

[We will now begin the wedding song.]

As she hastily turned her head to follow the spotlight, someone ran in from the other side of the darkened hall.

"......"

It was TeSTAR, dressed in suits.
