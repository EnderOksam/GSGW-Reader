# Chapter 544

VTIC Cheongryeo's daily routine was very neat.

"Should we add another 2kg?"

"Yes."

His activities had ended after just one week due to a dating scandal. Now, the guy executed a Tetris-like schedule, juggling practice, workouts, and company meetings. It sounded easy, but the schedule was grueling. He split his time down to the minute. Still, he moved with an effortless simplicity, like someone accustomed to constant, repetitive tasks.

*And the fact that I'm watching this crap all day is legendary...*

I pressed my eyes and sighed deeply. Since we couldn't communicate, there was no damn way to persuade him.

Of course, the reason wasn't just that the guy had gone mad from living the life of a perfect idol about one hundred times.

*He's doing this on purpose.*

The moment he realized that I, the Status Window, had a personality, he chose the easiest way to handle me.

[I believe I told you not to come out unless I called for you.]

It was about establishing a pecking order.

*He's trying to discipline and tame me.*

He wanted to crush my spirit. He was making an impactful display of his decision-making power and the fact that he held my life and death in his hands. It was the fastest and most effective method of control. He had probably used it in various relationships for years, no, decades. It was only natural he could use it as easily as breathing.

I cracked my neck.

*Well... it's a little annoying, but.*

This Cheongryeo was mistaken about one thing. This wasn't the first or second time I'd seen him pull these kinds of tricks.

*I can clearly see what you're going to do next.*

First, once he thinks he's broken my spirit, he'll offer a small reward. The so-called carrot and stick. That's how he'll guide me in the direction he wants. Punishment and reward. That's how all control works.

So this bastard will show me the tip of the carrot at the right moment...

*Then I'll have to devour his hand along with it.*

And I'll have to negotiate a good price for the watch. I crossed my arms and smiled.

As expected, it wasn't long before the guy spoke to the Status Window.
