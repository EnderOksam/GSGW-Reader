# Chapter 538

It was the practice notebook of Nu-ah, Aether's leader.

He had sacrificed his sleep to create it, endlessly analyzing his sunbae's music videos.

[I did my best to distribute the parts for our members' positions. You guys wanna try it like this?]

[As expected of our hyung!]

The memory of his members' admiration, and the pride he felt, was still vivid.

Then came the staff's sincere praise during the interim evaluation. The company employees looked thrilled after the pre-release video. The good feedback let him practice for days without a single worry.

But a few days later, on the main competition stage, everything changed.

[WAAAAAH!]

[Did you just see that?]

TeSTAR delivered a powerful performance, completely overturning the choreography and arrangement they had shown before. It felt like a lie. It felt like they were targeting Aether.

The shock that hit Aether's leader was as immense as the confidence he had felt just moments before.

For days, afterimages of that performance swirled in his mind, along with regret, resentment, and sadness. He barely got through his schedules. When the cameras were off, he forgot the little things he usually managed so meticulously.

Among them was the practice notebook. It had already served its purpose and was no longer needed.

Now, it was in the hands of a TeSTAR member, the very sunbae he had analyzed. The man held it out, asking, "Isn't this an Aether member's?"

Nu-ah had never imagined this situation. His blood ran cold.

*What do I do?*

*Did he look inside? No, I don't even need to ask. He must have. He had to have checked the contents to know it belonged to Aether.*

His mind went blank. Nu-ah desperately searched for an answer, but a reflexive response escaped first. "I... I don't know."

He had panicked. Unable to think, he resorted to denial out of pure anxiety.

"I don't think it's one of our members' notebooks."

Seon Ahyeon didn't answer. He just held the notebook quietly and stared.

Nu-ah realized he had said something completely absurd. To say it wasn't Aether's! He had just given the sunbae a reason to show the notebook to other people and ask, "Is this yours by any chance?" If someone else saw it... then.

*Ah...*

A cold sweat ran down his back. He stood frozen, unable to believe this was happening. It didn't feel real.

*Is this a nightmare?*

Just as he had that thought, Seon Ahyeon spoke. "I see... In that case, could you perhaps find the owner for me? It seems... the owner isn't on our side."

Nu-ah held his breath. The words were so unbelievable he wondered if he had misheard.

"......"

Seon Ahyeon extended a hand, offering the notebook.

There was no reason to ask a junior who claimed "I don't know" and "It's not ours" to find the owner.

Nu-ah knew instinctively.

*He knows. He knows this notebook is mine. He knows I'm lying.*

Seon Ahyeon couldn't have done this unless he was certain.

But Nu-ah couldn't miss this heavenly lifeline. He nodded hurriedly and reached out with both hands. "Yes...!"

The moment he grasped the notebook, the dizzying fear subsided, replaced by a wave of relief.

*Ah.*

He gripped the notebook tightly. His heart slowed, as if a huge weight had been lifted from his chest.

Seon Ahyeon watched him but said nothing. He just gave a slight nod, his expression calm, then picked up his drink and walked down the hallway.

Only then, after the relief, did another feeling surface for Aether's leader. It was shame.

He thought about how he must have looked to Seon Ahyeon.

*Ah, no.*

He hurriedly stopped that line of thought. This was just because of the special circumstances. Isn't it natural for a hardworking idol to analyze a competitor and sunbae's performance?

*That's right, it's natural... it's just that the situation is strange.*

The situation was strange because of the unsettling rumors that Aether's staff was trying to check TeSTAR. He had panicked, thinking TeSTAR would misunderstand if they saw a notebook where Aether studied their gestures.

*It's not like I only studied TeSTAR's gestures on purpose.*

He had analyzed his current competitors because he was short on time. We were just trying to work hard, Aether's leader thought. He hadn't intended to do anything shameful, but given the circumstances, he knew it could look that way to the other party. That was why he had panicked.

Because...

*TeSTAR doesn't know my circumstances.*

But in that moment, Aether's leader felt a dissonance in that thought. It wasn't just TeSTAR who didn't know his circumstances.

*...Anyone would have seen it that way.*

Anyone outside of Aether would have a similar reaction to the notebook. They would say something like, "Aether obsessively copied TeSTAR's gestures." Because that was the result.

*I'm the exception.*

He was the only one who saw himself with such leniency.

*I know all my frustrations and circumstances. Because it's my business...*

The realization hit him like a cold draft, shattering a protective barrier in his mind.

With a trembling hand, Nu-ah tucked the notebook into his chest as if to hide it. He felt like he had just woken from a dream.

*I see.*

Everyone has their own circumstances, but because I know all of my own, I'm especially lenient with myself. That was called self-justification.

If he stripped away that self-justification and saw it as someone else's business...

*...It really was something to be ashamed of.*

And wouldn't he also think that a sunbae who treated a junior so graciously, despite being copied without permission, was a much better person?

He recalled Seon Ahyeon silently handing him the notebook. He remembered Lee Sejin responding gently to his ulteriorly motivated words.

He realized that in all those situations, he had wanted to find proof that his sunbaes were bad people.

*Because that would be more comfortable...*

It's more comfortable to believe that I and my people are the good ones, and those who get in my way are the bad ones. Things were already hard. It was too difficult to resent his own company, which he couldn't control. He had been unconsciously ignoring it.

Aether's leader slowly left the hallway and headed back to the waiting room. His mind was now cool and clear.

That day, after filming ended, he used a laptop he had secretly brought into the dorm. For the first time, he looked up the public's evaluation of Aether's incidents.

——————————

3.1. TeSTAR Reference Controversy (7)

——————————

He tried to avoid the indiscriminate insults, but just reading the compiled posts was a huge mental blow.

[...Various materials on composition, props, and choreography emerged, and the opinion that 'for it to be a reference, the examples only come from TeSTAR' (9) spread, centering on entertainment communities...]

He sniffled as he scrolled through photo evidence claiming they had copied TeSTAR since their debut. Tears welled in his eyes. He wanted to run away.

*Aren't these people mistaken?*

He tried to shake off the thought and view the materials as objectively as possible.

But the more he did, the more it seemed Aether was a flawed group from the very beginning, right from the planning stage.

*Is that it?*

He felt that everything about him as an idol was wrong. He even felt the urge to retire.

Then he found it. It was a screenshot of a fan's social media post.

[It seems like Aether has almost no participation in their producing, so this kind of problem needs to be fixed at the company's planning team level. It seems there's nothing the Aether members can do even if they don't like it]

He realized something.

*Ah.*

The fans didn't see the company and the group as the same, either. The post made a proposition.

[The company and the idol are not the same.]

He wiped his tears and reached a conclusion. First, he would reflect on his mistakes and fix them. As for the company's mistakes, he didn't need to defend them or get overly immersed. It was okay to have a dissenting opinion.

*The company isn't me.*

His chest felt much lighter.

TAK.

He closed the laptop, his painful ego-search finally over.

Then he recalled the results from that day's filming.

[First place is... TeSTAR!]

[Wow!]

TeSTAR's area erupted in happy applause.

Aether finished in third place. The rank wasn't a failure, so it earned no sympathy, but it wasn't high enough to attract attention either. The company's expectations were not met. The group's goal was not achieved. Nothing had gone the way he wanted.

But Nu-ah no longer felt resentful.

***

[Sunbae, thank you for being so kind and saying good things to me until the end, even though I've caused you a lot of trouble. If I get to see you next time, I want to be a more mature person...]

"That kid from Aether sent this?"

"......"

Seon Ahyeon nodded. It wasn't a text or a KakaoTalk message, but a handwritten note.

He likely chose this method because they weren't close enough to have each other's numbers. Asking a manager was also difficult, since the staff from both groups were on such bad terms.

*Hmm.*

I quickly scanned the note. It was a groveling apology and a pledge that it wouldn't happen again.

"He gave it to me, but actually... I think he gave it to our group."

"Really?"

I couldn't agree, but Seon Ahyeon just nodded with a faint smile.

"Yeah. You were the one who found the notebook in the first place, Moondae."

Well, that was true.

I had found the notebook on set a few days ago.

[...? That thing.]

*I saw him leave it on a chair while I was watching their staff's movements.*

The moment I checked the contents...

[He's leaving this behind?]

I thought it was a crazy jackpot.

Several ways to expose them and shake up the situation immediately came to mind.

*I didn't use them.*

I discarded them all. The reason was simple. TeSTAR had already gone all-in, gambling on the performance at Lee Sejin's persuasion. Even for me, adding more variables was too much.

*If we stomp on Wonder Hall that hard, there's no telling what might pop out from the balloon effect.*

Wonder Hall was a huge company that had been in the industry for decades with hundreds of employees. I didn't know what kind of trouble might spark. We were still a small, new company without the staff to handle that. So I held back.

You could say I chose a political detour.

I placed the Aether leader's letter on the table and smirked.

*Well... to be honest, if it blew up, the Aether members would be more screwed than Wonder Hall. It wasn't very efficient.*

You have to crush the company. Crushing that kid, who was basically a non-regular employee fused with his company, would only leave a bad aftertaste.

And the notebook, which I had Seon Ahyeon return based on his own suggestion, seemed to have worked well.

*To think a conscience attack actually worked.*

A kid is a kid, after all. It looked like we wouldn't have to deal with any annoyances for a while.

"You worked hard."

"Ah, no."

Seon Ahyeon didn't seem to be in a bad mood. He looked back at the letter with a pleased expression.

*So it ended this cleanly,*I thought with relief.

A few days later, however, I got a call from a Wonder Hall affiliate.

[Oh, I heard you got first place. Congratulations, TeSTAR!]

It was none other than Ha Jintae from T-Holic, but his "Wonder Hall affiliate" status was now on a timer.

"...Congratulations on your independence too, T-Holic."

[Haha! Well, the conditions just happened to line up, so we just became co-CEOs in name only.]

That was right. They were planning to go independent soon, putting up their own sign as CEOs.

But how could they screw over a major company like Wonder Hall while barely going independent? Of course, they had something to fall back on.

[So it looks like we'll be seeing each other a lot more, right?]

"...I suppose so."

That wasn't just a courtesy. It was a plain fact.

*Damn it.*

Surprisingly, T-Holic had also received investment funds from LeTi, just like us. They weren't even hiding it, blasting out news articles.

*The idea is that their position doesn't overlap with VTIC's right now anyway.*

T-Holic was an entertainment-focused idol group centered on public appeal, already past their prime as performers. In this case, T-Holic's fans would prefer knowing a large agency's capital and organizational power were involved, rather than worrying about the members going their separate ways.

Yeah, that was true, but look at this mess.

*Suddenly, the entire lineage of Grand Prize-winning male idols has become part of the same line.*

Sunbaes and Hubaes, all tied together by capital.

And that was how this crazy board was set.

[We'll be seeing a lot of the Hubaes at TeSTAR's company, that ORBIT, too~ Ah, maybe we could all really get together and do something like a sports day this time? What do you think?]

The suggestion was terrible, so stop.

"Wouldn't it be hard to match our schedules?"

[Hey, of course us old guys who are just playing around should match the schedule of the young and busy TeSTAR!]

Fortunately, Ha Jintae, who had been cackling, seemed to have some sense and soon switched to well-wishes.

[Ah, I hear you're super busy this time too. That's all proof that things are going well, so enjoy it. This album is a huge hit, isn't it!]

"...Thank you."

It was a plain fact. The buzz from my American awards show appearance and the synergy with internet memes TeSTAR had planted in the English-speaking world pulled in a steady stream of overseas fans. Thanks to this album's promotions and Bae Sejin's recent acting, the System's company grade rose steadily.

*I just need to pull in a bit more proof of our weight class.*

And what was that?

[You're going on tour now, right?]

"Yes."

That's right. A concert tour. As they say, nothing gets articles written about your overseas status better than audience numbers.

*We're going by the book.*

Of course, we always arranged for original content during any tour hiatus. This time was no exception.

[TeSTAR's Hotteok-Selling Travel Captain Battle ... Do you remember? 👀 We're back!]

We finally got the production team from the hotteok variety show back. There was, however, a slight difference.

[But now it's the opposite]

"......"

[Please watch TeSTAR work like dogs to earn money just to eat a single piece of hotteok (link)]

...Program name ❬TeSTAR's Not-So-Lonely Gourmet Trip ❭.

Planning complete.
