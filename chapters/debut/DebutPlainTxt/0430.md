# Chapter 430

Drip.

A single water droplet echoed through the dark hallway.

"Looks like all the power went out."

"It's a relief the emergency lights are still on, though."

The flickering lights must have died while I got my back treated and changed my shirt in the waiting room. The second-floor hallway had become incredibly eerie.

*One of the corner walls even collapsed...*

The area was extremely dangerous. We could only walk slowly, thanks to the flashlight Kim Raebin had brought.

"M-Moondae. Let me support you..."

"I told you, I'm fine."

We couldn't afford to slow down any more. Besides, I could walk just fine.

A moment later, we stood before the stairs to the backstage area and faced reality.

"Hm."

A chunk of concrete was visible through a torn gap in the door, which debris from the collapsed stage ceiling had struck.

"It got blocked when we heard the second collapse."

This area was smashed at the same time the third-floor hallway I was in had collapsed.

*It seems they told people not to come this way after the first stage set fell because it was dangerous.*

Trying to move the rubble would take at least an hour. We had to abandon the idea immediately.

Ultimately, there was only one way to go.

"We have to go down this way."

I walked to the spot where Seon Ahyeon had grabbed me earlier. An entire wall on the side of the hallway next to the stage had collapsed, leaving a gaping hole.

"......"

I shone the flashlight down below.

WHOOSH!

A stuffy, cool draft brushed against us, created by the widened space.

I could see the wreckage of the collapsed stage. Since everything that could fall had already fallen, it looked relatively stable.

"Let's get down there from here."

We would climb onto the collapsed stage.

The massive stage spanned the first floor, where the audience seats were, and the second floor, which housed the stage equipment. The stage split the second floor in half, and the right side with our waiting rooms was cut off from the upper floors.

This design was proper, as it kept singers and staff, who were outsiders, from accessing the upper floors used by venue personnel. For us, however, it meant we were completely trapped.

*So in the end, this is the only way to move.*

Let's use it.

I grabbed what I had prepared in the waiting room. It was a makeshift rope made by twisting and tying together the thick curtains from the changing area. It had caught my eye while I was changing.

"W-Will the curtains be okay?"

"I twisted them several times, so it's fine."

Time was steadily ticking down.

[00:33:12]

"We have thirty-three minutes left. We're short on time, so we have to at least try to move."

"......"

We had to do this if we wanted even a chance to prevent the insane disaster of the building collapsing again.

Kim Raebin's face set with determination.

"I will go first."

"No, let me first..."

"N-No! I'll hold it for you...!"

"Thank you!"

"......"

*Fine, do whatever you want. I'll just crouch down so I can grab them if anything happens.*

Kim Raebin tied the rope securely around his waist. After watching Seon Ahyeon anchor the other end of the curtain, he asked a question.

"Moondae, do you happen to know how exactly this one-hour time limit was measured?"

"I'd like to know that too."

"So it's an uncertain situation."

Kim Raebin nodded and carefully lowered his foot from the concrete edge.

"If the building collapses in exactly one hour, it sounds less like a prediction and more like a warning that someone intends to bring it down. That's why I asked."

Look at this guy.

"...Let's talk after we get down. Be careful."

"Yes!"

He really was sharp when it came to theory. Even earlier, he had clearly struggled to hold back the question of*How does the Status Window know such a thing?*

*Telling them the System is targeting me... would just be a hassle.*

The problem wasn't that they would blame me. They might become needlessly overprotective or worried, which would prevent us from making any progress.

*I'll keep my mouth shut.*

After Kim Raebin, I carefully lowered myself onto the stage wreckage. I confirmed that Seon Ahyeon, the last one, had landed safely, then turned to look at the audience seats.

"......"

"...Is anyone there?"

No answer came from the dust-and-darkness-filled seats.

——————————

Only a small echo reverberated. A sense of unease crept in.

*Why is no one here?*

When I met the fan who was a graduate student, she had implied that others besides her hadn't escaped.

*In the first place, anyone in the audience would have reacted if someone was coming down from a collapsed stage with a flashlight.*

The complete lack of sound or reaction was deeply unsettling.

"M-Moondae. First... I'll look for a path."

"...Right."

I collected the curtain rope and carefully stepped down from the stage. We spread out and searched the dark audience area, and soon we found the result.

*...The doors are crushed.*

Just as the graduate student had said, all the escape routes were blocked. The weight imbalance must have twisted and then crushed them.

*If we try to force them open and cause another collapse, we're screwed.*

There seemed to be no way to get to the first-floor lobby, and no one else was here.

"The Keundal you're looking for, Moondae, probably came as an audience member, so it's highly likely he already escaped."

"Y-Yeah, but, Moondae is so sure he's still here... If so, doesn't not being here mean he moved somewhere else?"

"Moondae, have you seen anyone else?"

I had.

"...There was a fan, over by the emergency staircase on the other side."

"......"

"We talked through a collapsed wall, but the room I was in caved in, so I don't know where she might have gone after that."

Worry and doubt flickered across their faces, but they offered a practical answer first.

"Then she might have gone upstairs."

Right.

However, the emergency exit to the staircase that the graduate student and I had used was also half-blocked with debris.

"When... w-when Moondae fell, I think it got blocked by the debris then."

Isolated.

[00:26:51]

Meanwhile, the timer dropped below thirty minutes.

*...Stay calm.*

Panicking wouldn't help. I had to use my head.

*Clearing the front of that emergency staircase in under twenty minutes seems too risky.*

Would it be better to force a door open in that time and at least get these guys out?

I suppressed a groan. I looked at the darkness beyond the half-exposed emergency staircase. We couldn't just pass through. The gap was too narrow.

*All three of us are too tall to fit.*

Should I try shouting? But could that negatively affect the building?

*If only we could communicate somehow.*

It would be great if we could make a broadcast... wait.

"...Raebin."

"Yes?"

I lowered my gaze. All the escape routes were blocked, but there was still one place we could go. The backstage area, where staff stayed during shoots and the place I had run from in a panic.

And speaking of filming, something came to mind.

*They had prepared new equipment for this shoot, too.*

"Do you know how to fly a drone?"

"......"

This broadcasting station had tried to film with a new product to get some PPL for new technology. One or two had flown around during our shoot, and I had seen some wreckage in front of the stage earlier.

*If that's the case...*

Wouldn't there be one or two spares?

I entered a makeshift editing room inside the backstage area, where they managed the temporary stage broadcast. The place was pitch-black, but it was obvious where they would keep such heavy and expensive equipment.

"...There it is."

I found it right away. A drone sat in an already opened box under a desk. I picked it up.

"Let's send this through the gap in the emergency staircase."

"Indeed, an excellent idea! I didn't know you had a hobby of flying drones, Moondae."

"I don't."

"...?"

I grabbed Kim Raebin's shoulder.

"I believe you can do it."

"......"

Wasn't he the guy who had mastered musical equipment and all sorts of electronics? I believed his amazing creativity and intuition would let him handle something like this.

"I-I'll do my best..."

Good.

"Um, M-Moondae, the computer won't turn on..."

"Let's use the laptop."

It was a shame the internet wasn't working, but the drone had its own cellular system built-in, so it could operate.

In that case...

[WHIRRR]

"...! It's working!"

Kim Raebin quickly skimmed the drone's manual, grabbed the joystick, and with a glint in his eye, instantly finished the connection and activated the drone.

"That's amazing, Raebin...!"

"Yeah, that's brilliant."

"Not at all! It seems someone did a test run, so it was luckily already connected to this laptop. I will move it to the front of the emergency staircase now!"

"Okay!"

*...Is he too talented to just be an idol?*

I briefly considered his abilities but pushed the thought aside. This wasn't the time.

[00:17:52]

In that short time, the clock had been cut in half again.

*Hoo.*

I clenched and unclenched my fist and headed for the emergency staircase.

Creek.

I opened the steel door, and Seon Ahyeon held it in place with one foot. Kim Raebin sat down with the joystick.

"Well then... I'm sending it in."

"Go ahead."

[WHIIIRRR-]

The drone flew through the black darkness.

"Fortunately, the battery is sufficient, so the attached light is doing its job."

"It is...!"

The laptop showed a wobbly view from the camera, awkwardly framed by a beginner pilot. The drone quickly ascended the stairs, and its light shone on...

"The wall is broken through here."

*...So this is where she was standing.*

It was the same broken wall where I had talked with the graduate student. Now, the drone's light simply cut through the void where the tilted supply closet from the third floor had been.

"......"

On the opposite side was the path to the other backstage area I had escaped from, but it was a steel door. The drone couldn't open it.

...Where did she go?

"Keep going."

"Yes."

Let's at least look for a trace of a person.

The view rose higher, up the dark stairs stained by the blue light of the emergency exit sign and the red of the fire hydrant. Following the railing around the corner, it revealed...

"......"

"...It's blocked."

Thud.

Debris from the third-floor entrance blocked the way up. That was the floor where the supply closet I had entered was located. The impact of the floor collapsing must have blocked it.

"......"

The search was over. There was no trace, no result.

"......"

"What should we do."

[00:15:47]

Fuck.

I swallowed hard and racked my brain.

If the way up is blocked...

"Let's try the basement."

"Ah!"

The emergency staircase should lead all the way down to the underground parking garage. Psychologically, people might be reluctant to go into the basement of a collapsing building, but logically, it was another possible escape route.

A parking garage would be designed to withstand heavy loads, and its large entrance might not have collapsed.

*Keundal might have thought of that.*

"Then I'll head that way."

[00:14:25]

The drone took flight again. Down, down it went...

"...!"

"The door is open!"

Surprisingly, a steel door held open by a fire extinguisher came into view.

*They prepared for the door to get twisted from the collapse.*

"There must be people...!"

"Yes, I'm going in!"

With urgent hands, Kim Raebin carefully moved the joystick.

The light of an emergency lamp flashed across the laptop screen. Beyond the steel door was a hallway, but it didn't look like the path to the underground parking garage. It looked like a space for facility management staff, with a break room or a CCTV room, though it was too dark to be sure.

*Did everyone evacuate this way?*

But there were no signs of people... wait.

"That."

"Ah!"

"It looks like a person."

Something was visible next to a plain gray door at the end of the hallway. As soon as Kim Raebin saw it, he moved the drone faster to get closer.

"......"

"......"

It was a person, but...

"Sunbaenim?"

It was Judan.

The guy stood in front of the door as if on guard duty. He made eye contact with the drone, wore a strange expression, then propped his chin on his hand and nodded. Why isn't this bastard even surprised... no, more importantly.

*Why are you here.*

VTIC was in their waiting room, so they should have escaped right away... no, wait. These guys were also in their waiting room and couldn't get out.

I looked back at Seon Ahyeon and Kim Raebin. They were also dumbfounded by the unexpected, indirect contact with their senior.

*This is driving me crazy.*

[00:12:46]

I glanced between the remaining time on the pop-up window and Judan on the laptop.

*Think. If I just think...*

Just as my brain felt like it was about to overload, a bell suddenly rang from behind us.

[DING-DING-]

"...!"

No, it wasn't a smartphone ringtone. This was...

"An announcement...?"

At Seon Ahyeon's voice, I tore my eyes from the laptop and looked up. A poor-quality voice spread from the speakers hanging around the collapsed stage.

[This is an announcement for everyone still remaining in the building. The tremors seem to have mostly stopped. However, rash actions such as breaking down doors can be dangerous.]

[Please wait for the rescue team in a safe location, such as near an emergency staircase, in a restroom, or around a central pillar.]

It was a calm male voice with good projection. Anyone would agree it was the perfect voice for an emergency broadcast.

The problem was that I knew that voice.

And the guys next to me knew it too.

"...S-Sejin?"

That's Bae Sejin.

Why the hell is that bastard making an announcement in there instead of getting out?
