# Chapter 431

I took a deep breath.

*...Let's get this straight.*

The announcement came from Bae Sejin, but the drone feed showed VTIC's Judan.

*Are you kidding me?*

Only twelve minutes remained until the collapse. One guy was in the basement, and another was making announcements somewhere else. How could the managers and staff all escape while leaving only their company's idols behind?

[DING-DING-]

"The broadcast is over..."

Just like that, Bae Sejin's voice vanished.

*In that case.*

First, I had to quickly lure out the person we could see. If we just stood here mumbling, we could all die. I gritted my teeth and stared at the laptop screen.

Judan was... why wasn't he there?

"Where did this guy go now?"

"...! H-He probably just went into a room, didn't he...?"

Thankfully, Judan reappeared on the screen. He held a sheet of A4 paper with something written on it.

[Are you a survivor inside the building??]

He was trying to communicate.

"Hah!"

"Th-This drone doesn't have a voice transmission function. What should we do...?"

"Move the drone up and down."

"Ah."

At Kim Raebin's command, the drone's view bobbed. In response, Judan wrote something new on the paper and showed it.

[Are you looking for an escape route?]

Kim Raebin nodded.

"Hmm. We're looking for people, so to be precise, we should first answer no..."

"......"

*No, don't.*

"It's weirder for a trapped person not to be looking for an escape route. Let's just say we are for now."

"Indeed!"

We had to start there to move the conversation along quickly.

*He'll probably ask if we've made any progress, or try to guess who we are.*

I mentally mapped out the conversation, predicting his possible responses. Meanwhile, after the drone bobbed again, Judan scribbled on the paper once more. He held up the finished sentence.

It was a sentence I hadn't expected at all.

[There is an escape route here, in the staff area of the basement.]

"......"

"Uh, uhh."

The sentence wasn't finished.

[There's a door that leads outside, and it's operational because the emergency power is on.]

"Th-Then..."

"Stopping the collapse is important, but since we don't have much time, securing an escape route seems just as crucial!"

"R-Right, that's true!"

He had a point. If the worst happened, we had to get the remaining people out. The problem was...

*I need to ask how long it's been operational.*

For a building this size, the emergency power probably wouldn't last an hour. I had no idea how much time was left.

*And if there's a door, why hasn't this guy left yet?*

One more thing. A door shouldn't need power. You just open it.

Watching the bobbing drone, Judan added another line to the paper in real time.

[However, it requires a staff keycard.]

*I knew it.*

There was a catch. He must have found a staff-only door that required a security key.

[Please find a staff keycard and come to the basement.]

*The stairs are blocked, you bastard.*

I didn't know how he got stranded in the basement alone, but the mission had just been updated.

*...Find a staff pass and go to the basement.*

And do it within this time.

[00:10:34]

"......"

"The staff pass... I don't think there was one in the editing room earlier... Still, I'll go check one more time...!"

"In that case, I'll look for a way to get to the basement... Moondae?"

I placed the laptop on the floor.

"There are ten minutes left. It's impossible for both of you."

"......"

"We have to try another way."

"Yes?"

At this point, I had no other choice. I stood up and gripped the curtain rope I had set aside.

~~~

Judan looked up. A bright light shone on him from the end of the dark hallway.

[WHIIIRRR~]

A drone. It was a familiar element in modern disaster stories.

*Hoo.*

He rested his chin on his hand and nodded. It seemed the survivors trapped in the building were starting to find ways out.

*In that case, I suppose I should respond in a traditional manner.*

He quickly turned and went into the room.

"Could I possibly borrow the smartphone you're holding...?"

"......"

No response.

*Really, what did I expect?*

He quickly changed his approach and grabbed a sheet of A4 paper.

[Are you a survivor inside the building?]

The drone bobbed enthusiastically. Oh, this was someone who knew how to respond. Intrigued, Judan sincerely shared the information he knew.

*As for me going to find them... hmm. That's a bit much.*

One must live within one's means. Judan shrugged, then watched as the drone, staggering from the shocking information, suddenly dropped to the floor.

*There's a more than fifty percent chance they're now frantically searching for a staff pass.*

With this, he had sufficiently fulfilled his humanitarian duty.

"Hyung, a drone just came by and we had a short conversation. It's not a rescue team, but another survivor trapped in this building."

"I see."

And then there was his companion, who showed no interest in such a peculiar event.

Cheongryeo stood in the basement's facility management office, manipulating a device with a blank expression. I thought we couldn't see the CCTV because the power was out, but he had somehow used a wirelessly charged portable security device to review some footage from just before on a small screen.

This was something a normal idol would never know how to do.

*I guess that's the kind of EXP you gain from regressing.*

Judan decided to keep treating him politely. There was a chance that in one of his many lives, he might have been a murderer crushed by pressure.

Regardless of his thoughts, Cheongryeo continued to flip through the CCTV footage with mechanical hand movements. One dark screen appeared after another, since the lights were out.

"......"

Then.

"Ah."

There. Thanks to a lightstick's glow, Cheongryeo spotted the figure he was looking for beyond the broken wall of the second-floor emergency staircase.

Park Moondae.

He watched without a flicker of emotion as the supply room Park Moondae had been in collapsed entirely right after.

"......"

He now knew his experience-based deduction had been astonishingly accurate. There was no particular joy in it. It was merely a confirmation of what he already knew.

The supply room had collapsed onto the ruined stage. Since that area was originally covered by broadcast equipment, there was no CCTV. It was impossible to check from here.

*Although there was no blood when I checked with my own eyes earlier... he could be buried underneath.*

"......"

Cheongryeo lowered his hand. He knew he could check the surrounding audience seats with the remaining battery. However, he felt a sense of repulsion.

Because...

*It's quiet.*

If Park Moondae were alive, these absurd disasters should still be happening.

*But the silence... means it's over.*

"......"

*Why do I have to confirm that this guess was also correct?*

Cheongryeo stared down at the device, his face expressionless. Judan watched him, a bit blankly.

"...?"

*Is that hyung having a flashback or something?*

It was then.

SCREEEEEECHCRACRASH!

"......"

A tremendous vibration and noise shot down from above, striking even the basement. It was the third collapse.

"Ugh!"

Objects fell, and the whole place shook. Judan immediately dove under a desk to protect his head.

*Is this foreshadowing?*

He imagined the role of the guy who gives the protagonists good information, only to be found dead when they finally reach the basement.

*That would be troublesome.*

He decided to prepare for the worst. Out of conscience, he lifted his head to look at his companion and was surprised.

"...!"

Cheongryeo... was smiling faintly. Standing still amidst the shaking equipment and flickering lights, his smile seemed almost surreal.

*...Don't tell me he's feeling the sensation of being alive in danger?*

It was a wild guess. Amidst the roar and the collapse, Cheongryeo's murmur was heard.

"Shall I go find him."

~~~

Eight minutes before the collapse.

"M-Moondae?"

"Hold on."

I returned to the collapsed stage and carefully climbed back onto it.

*The place we came down from earlier.*

I re-examined the structure of the area, including the stage. The stage occupied the central space, splitting the second floor into a 'ㄷ' shape, with a left and right side.

*We used the curtain as a rope to drop down into the waiting room area on the right.*

From the right side, I could see the collapsed wall. The second floor on the opposite side was the office area for broadcast station staff. It was a place to go, but a place we couldn't reach.

*Hoo.*

I caught my breath and turned to the two of them.

"I have a favor to ask."

"Wh-What is it...?"

"I need to get back up there. Can you give me a boost?"

"...!"

Their faces filled with alarm at my words.

"Are you... going to look for a staff pass?"

"Something like that, but I don't have time to explain."

I spoke quickly.

"I-If I go..."

"There's no time to explain how to find it, hurry!"

Seon Ahyeon bit his lip. He looked like he was about to protest, but then he nodded with a determined expression.

"O-Okay. ...I'm doing this... because I trust you."

"...Yeah."

*Damn it.*

To avoid useless guilt, I turned my head and tied the curtain rope around my waist. If I hooked it midway, I wouldn't die if I slipped. If I tied one end to the wall after I got up, they could use it to climb up later.

"Y-You have to lean on me hard...!"

"You must step on us comfortably!"

"Got it."

I stepped on their thighs and shoulders, then leaped, reaching for the air.

*Got it...!*

I made it. After grabbing a steel bar, I used my arm strength and momentum to place my other hand on the second-floor landing. My stomach scraped roughly against the broken concrete.

*Ugh. Goddammit.*

That hurt like hell, but the important thing was...

*I made it...!*

"Hah."

I rolled onto the second-floor landing and untied the curtain rope from my waist. I heard shouting from below.

"A-Are you okay?!"

"Yeah! It's sharp and dangerous, so get off the stage and stand by the central pillar! I'll call you when I'm done!"

"Understood! How many minutes are left?"

[00:04:31]

"...Eight minutes!"

This way, they wouldn't panic even after what felt like four minutes had passed. After confirming they heard me, I started moving. I turned on my flashlight and checked a few rooms for a staff ID, but as expected, I couldn't find one.

It made sense. This was a space for outsiders.

[00:01:57]

*There was no staff pass backstage either.*

As I thought, to get one, I had to go where the staff were. The office space on the other side of the second floor, but what if there was no path?

*...I'll have to make one.*

I estimated the stage's position and walked to the end of the hallway.

"...Hoo."

Here. Beyond the wall of this small waiting room was the other side. Probably.

"......"

Of course, I didn't have the power to break this wall. It might be more realistic to clear the debris below, but there was no time.

*In that case.*

Doesn't a thought like this come to mind? Let's see. The building will definitely collapse soon, targeting me. However, another collapse won't clear debris that's already piled up.

*However, it can also demolish an intact wall...!*

[00:01:03]

I moved heavy objects from the waiting room and piled them against the wall.

*Earlier, a wall definitely fell, targeting me precisely.*

If Keundal changed the name to 'Mission Failure' because he found a contradiction, this disaster has likely become more localized and moderate. It even kindly informs me of a 'collapse', doesn't it?

*I'll use it to my advantage.*

*Fuck, I've survived this twice already, so I'll do better this time.*

I took a deep breath, cleared my head, and prepared. The countdown began.

[00:00:03]

[00:00:02]

[00:00:01]

Click.

The sound cut off. I was leaning against the wall.

[00:00:00]

I threw myself sideways.

"...!"

Simultaneously, with a tremendous explosion, the room shook. The heavy furniture and metal props I had piled up crashed down.

SCREEEEEECHCRACRASH!

Even the heavy ceiling light fell.

*Fuck.*

For a moment, regret flashed through my mind.*I've done something crazy...*No, shut up, this is the only way. This is right!

Move!

I rolled to the side.

"Hup."

I slid my body under a vanity. Sharp debris flew and stung my cheek.

SCRAPE!

As the roar and shaking of the collapse began to subside, an unexpected sound rang out loudly.

"AHHHHHH!"

"Oh my god!"

Screams and shouts.

"......"

I staggered out from under the vanity. All sorts of objects were shattered and rolling on the floor. Light and sounds leaked from beyond the half-demolished wall. I walked slowly toward it.

Stepping over the debris, I saw what was beyond the original wall...

"Th-There's someone here... M-Moondae??"

More than ten people were sitting in front of the elevator. That wasn't all. Sounds came from all over the connecting hallway, faintly visible in the flickering emergency lights.

"Who is it?"

"What's going on right now?"

"......"

All the people who weren't in the audience seats were here.

*What the.*

I blinked, my shock causing me to see it a beat too late.

[Control Room]

The nameplate was on an open door. Through it, a PA system with a microphone was visible. A guy came running out of the room as if he'd tripped.

"...Park Moondae?"

It was Keun Sejin, still in his stage costume. He looked at me in disbelief, then ran over and grabbed my head with both hands.

That wasn't the end of it.

"Moondae??"

"......"

One by one, the other guys came out from beyond the door.

"Moondae-hyung!!"

"You..., you...!!"

Cha Eugene with his torn harness, Ryu Chungwoo with his sleeves rolled up, and even Bae Sejin, who had been on the broadcast earlier.

*...No.*

This was the worst. All these guys being left here was a nightmare.

And yet.

I was so damn happy to see them. I managed to open my mouth.

"...I'm sorry."

"You crazy, crazy bastard...!"

"......"

It was the first time I'd ever heard Keun Sejin curse like that. In front of fans, no less. However, I wasn't angry. The pain in my ribs from his hug was bearable.

*You're heavy, you bastard.*

I paused all my questions and simply accepted the reunion. However, the pop-up was updating again.

[Sudden!]
