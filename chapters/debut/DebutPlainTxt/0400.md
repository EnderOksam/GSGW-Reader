# Chapter 400 - ⟨Bespoke Singer⟩ Revisited

Individual activities for idols.

It was usually done when a certain amount of experience had been accumulated, but some idols never engaged in individual activities even after years of experience.

An example was right in front of me. Cheongryeo.

"VTIC didn't have any individual activities until you guys renewed your contracts. That was probably due to your influence, right?"

"I would say that I helped the president in his decision-making."

*You mean to say that* he *helped* you *get what you wanted.*

In any case, it was common sense to carry out solo activities in a way that didn't stir up or divide the fandom too much.

Especially in the early days of debut, strengthening the group was a priority.

But... If we just needed one decisive hit, it didn't matter whether the fandom ended up divided. Gaining personal fans would be the fastest way to do it.

That meant we could go ahead with our plans.

"Let's distribute the work between us right away."

"There's no need to delay."

We had to call in all the connections we could. Since we were in a large agency, there were many programs we could request an invitation to, but...

*We can also take advantage of the fact that there are already successful people out there.*

The actor Bae Sejin and Xyrop's Lee Sejin.

The assistance of two people with the same name had already been promised.

★:
-Bae Sejin-hyung: I've already spoken to them.

-Bae Sejin-hyung: You'll be contacted in the afternoon.
:★

Bae Sejin arranged for us to work on the OST for the movie he was appearing in.

Originally, we could have participated as a group, but since we had to focus on the next album's title track, which would be produced quickly, only a few members would participate for promotional purposes.

And then there was Keun Sejin.

★:
-Keun Sejin: I've been offered a regular spot on a variety show, so maybe someone could appear with me? Haha. The PD already asked me if I had any recommendations for colleagues.

What kind of variety show is it?-

-Keun Sejin: A variety show about rehabilitating problem dogs~~~ (heartwarming bear emoticon)
:★

As expected from Keun Sejin, it was a successful show—he'd accepted the offer, after all, and he seemed to be enjoying it. I immediately recommended a qualified person who had raised dogs since childhood.

"I took care of Kkami, but... All I did was trim his fur a little."

"Most people won't even trim their dog's fur themselves."

So Ryu Chungwoo was immediately sent off to the show.

And...

"......"

"I don't think I've ever done badly on this type of program."

"Do whatever you want."

Cheongryeo would also be appearing. I didn't think he would do badly, but somehow the feeling of fraud got stronger.

Aside from that, we chose all the guys who could make an impression on TV and put them into programs that were doing well or would soon be doing well.

"I'm not all that eager to do variety shows—"

"Woodan, I heard you're the best fit for variety shows in VTIC."

"That's not true."

I pushed Judan and the yes-men into a disaster simulation observation show. *It was Cheongryeo's decision. Don't blame me.*

"What should I do?? I'm good at everything!" Cha Eugene said.

"You're enlisting."

"...!?"

And Cha Eugene was put into a military variety show. Since he wouldn't have to actually serve in the military anyway, he could endure this for now.

After organizing it like that, there was only one person left, and he was easy enough to find a good fit for...

I sent Kim Raebin to the variety show I'd picked for him, but he called me the day before filming.

– *Hyung... No matter how much I think about it, this doesn't feel right.*

"Why?"

Kim Raebin spoke in a low but intense voice.

– *This... Isn't this unfair to the others?*

Yes. I made this guy participate in the student rapper survival show called ⟨School Rapping⟩.

He probably felt burdened by the articles describing a '17-year-old rookie idol from a major entertainment agency' entering the student rapper survival competition.

– *I'm not a student or a rookie! This feels like fraud, like a kind of manipulation of my academic background...!*

Wow, it looked like I'd have to do some more work to convince him.

I opened my mouth.

"Whether you've been an idol for five years or one day, you're still an idol there. People don't think of idols as rappers."

– *...!!*

"And being a student doesn't have an age restriction. At any age, if you're attending school, you're a student."

– *That...!*

And actually, Kim Raebin here had transferred to high school and was attending it.

He was quiet over the phone for a while, but an answer soon came back.

– *That... I suppose you have a point, though.*

"Yes. So don't worry and do your best. It would actually be rude to do it half-heartedly. It seems like you're looking down on them."

– *...! Yes. I see!*

This meant that Kim Raebin became a chaebol[1]{Editor's Note: Chaebol/재벌 refers to a large, usually family-controlled Korean conglomerate. In this context, it refers to a member of such a wealthy family.} who intervened in children's fights and ruined the game. It was essentially child's play to him.

*I can already guess what the reactions will be like.*

I reflected on my choices and all my plotting with some satisfaction.

At that time, I got a message from the guy who'd be giving us an OST for one of his movies.

★:
-Bae Sejin: I'll see you on set soon. I heard we might be filming something together.
:★

I wasn't surprised. The guy had grown unexpectedly nostalgic at the mention of the OST, saying it reminded him of ⟨Cosmic Gunner⟩. He'd offered to let the members singing the OST appear briefly with him during his promotional schedule.

But I had a surprise in store for him.

★:
I won't sing the movie OST.-

-Bae Sejin-hyung: ???

-Bae Sejin-hyung: Aren't you the main vocalist?

I don't have time.-
:★

Yes.

I had arranged to do something else for the sake of efficiency. Lead vocalists with good voices could handle the OST just fine.

So, what would be the most efficient thing for a main vocalist to do?

*Kim Raebin is participating in a hip-hop survival show...*

It was a strategy that made use of our already proven abilities.

Actually, wasn't there only one obvious place for me to go?

I recalled an article I checked a few days ago.

★$
[Ideas and singing only… Special show ⟨Bespoke Singer⟩ in the works]
$★

This special program.

I would appear once more on ⟨Bespoke Singer⟩, which was still a prototype in this timeline.

Of course, I had no intention of going as the unconventional bouquet-headed 'May Bridegroom' again.

*...I need to do something else.*

More than that... I planned to create a character that would be even more immersive for the audience.

I turned on my laptop in the van and started drafting a document to submit to the company. Since my only schedule tomorrow was in the afternoon, I'd finish it today even if I had to stay up all night.

***

⟨Bespoke Singer⟩ was not a cutting-edge program using holograms from the beginning.

It started with disguises.

Contestants wore masks and special costumes that covered them completely, creating their own 'singer avatars' similar to characters at amusement parks. These costumes were custom-made by the participants themselves.

And now the show was about to air for the first time.

+=
[Bespoke Singer]
=+

"Oh."

An idol fan who happened to be sitting in front of the TV was staring at the screen blankly.

She'd always had a fondness for main vocalists, but she'd lost her passion for idols after entering college.

*I can't seem to find anyone I want to root for.*

These days, even when she saw performances in person, she wasn't particularly excited. It was clear that she'd hit a slump as an idol fan.

*Is this because I'm an adult now...?*

The college student stared, unimpressed, as the concept of the show was explained on-screen.

+=
[Singers re-created using their own ideas!]
=+

*...It's bizarre.*

It wouldn't find its rhythm until a few years later, but in the beginning, a lot of outlandish characters passed by, with costumes seemingly designed to attract attention. She wondered how much aggro it would receive.

*Wow, a spider? That just doesn't look right.*

+=
#><[The Dancing Spider Queen]><#

[Her eight legs produce a poison that rivets people in their seats and fills them with musical ecstasy!]
=+

*Seriously? Was she Medusa?*

To be honest, the sight of eight black legs sticking out from under what looked like a white spider-silk costume was something from a nightmare.

This was especially true because of the poor quality of the doll's mask.

"Ugh."

Still, the singers were good, and the show had a strange cult appeal that kept the college student watching. (And this was also the reason why this special program became a regular program.)

In the same way, about 7 or 8 singers in all kinds of strange costumes appeared and disappeared in an instant. Sometimes they only sang for one minute before the winner was declared.

"Oh."

The college student was starting to get tired of this weirdness.

The program, which had been moving quickly, finally slowed down and introduced the next participant.

As if he were an important person.

+=
[This time the singer is... a dog?]
=+

And the person waddling onto the stage holding a signboard was... a Welsh Corgi.

A full-body yellow dog costume.

*Oh my gosh.*

It was so ordinary that it felt a bit out of place.

*Isn't it like they put no effort in at all?*

The singer, wearing a blatantly ordinary, cheap costume that could be seen anywhere, struggled to move onto the stage.

Then, there was a close-up of the board he was holding.

+=
[Looking for a new owner tough enough to put up with me]
=+

"Ahem!" She repressed a laugh, nearly choking at the unexpected phrase when she'd been expecting something more along the lines of asking for love.

The singer's profile appeared on-screen along with intentionally cheap-looking, B-grade glitter effects.

+=
#><[The Welsh Corgi at Your House]><#

[He's looking for a job. He knows a lot of tricks!]

[Contestant statement: Keep me (—I'm sorry?) Feed me]
=+

*What is that!*

Somehow, the glittering eyes of the doll mask now looked almost shameless.

"He must be crazy."

Even as she said that, the college student raised her head and remained glued to the screen.

*Is he a comedian? For some reason, I don't think he'll be good at singing.*

But as the music began, the person opened his mouth and finally started singing...

$p
♪ *Far above those clouds!* ♫
p$

"...!"

It was a captivating band song with bright, beautiful vocals and strong high notes.

*Oh.*

It was exactly the sort of song someone would choose to blatantly show off their singing skills, and it left an impression strong enough to completely change the B-class atmosphere.

"Who *is* that? He's good~" her mother said as she passed through the living room.

"Uh huh."

She responded reflexively, but she also meant it.

*This person is really good.*

At that moment, the college student stopped thinking of the show as just a way to kill time.

She was curious about what the Welsh Corgi would sing next.

+=
[The winner is... The Welsh Corgi at Your House!]
=+

The Welsh Corgi impressively advanced to the second round with tremendous cheers. He somehow looked brave as he turned away and hobbled off the stage.

And he didn't falter in later rounds either. If anything, he showed an even more diverse side.

In the second round, he sang a wistful trot song as a team with another costumed participant, and in the third round, he sang a moody ballad.

His tone seemed to change every time, and each song was performed like a skillfully executed trick.

*I can't believe he manages to look handsome in that dog costume...*

The college student narrowed her eyes at the sight of the talented Welsh Corgi playing tricks, feeling as though she understood why he'd bothered to create a character.

And finally, the one-on-one match to determine the winner commenced.

There was essentially no tension at all in the editing or pacing.

+=
[All participating singers will sing the same song announced in advance!]
=+

If they sang the same song, the differences in their abilities would become even more pronounced.

And the Welsh Corgi's skills were truly outstanding.

$p
♪ *Be my light*\
*Let the time pass*\
*But you shall remain* ♫
p$

The Welsh Corgi performed the female singer's song in the original key and won an almost brutal victory over the spider.

It was overwhelming.

The crowd cheered. *Wow!!*

"Wow."

What was this? She found herself thrilled by the vicarious satisfaction of his overwhelming victory.

On the screen, the Welsh Corgi was doing a pretty cute victory dance. It made her feel like he might have worked a few stints at an amusement park.

Then, at the same time, she became curious.

*Who is it? Who is it?*

*In times like this, it's time to turn to the internet's collective intelligence!*

As soon as the broadcast ended, she opened a search bar online and checked the real-time search terms.

Sure enough, 'Bespoke Singer' and 'Welsh Corgi' were already popping up as search terms.

★$
-Wow, it's fun.

-I had my doubts, but I kept watching anyway LOL. You know it'll be legendary when you watch it and say, "Is such a thing possible?"

└ㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋ I feel the same way, yes yes

-No, but who is the real Welsh Corgi? Haha, is it Kang Seongchu?

└The voices are really similar.

└It's been a long time since he sang anything thoughㅠ Absolutely not~!
$★

However, no one had a good answer.

*Huh?*

There were all kinds of speculations, such as 'he is an established singer' or 'he is the vocalist of an indie band'. Some were even theorizing he might be the main vocalist of a famous idol group.

With so many people talking about him, you'd expect a few likely candidates to emerge.

And the college student thought seriously.

*He has to be an idol.*

People weren't receptive to idol fans' arguments at the moment, but his charm, acting, and dancing were dead giveaways.

They were clearly the product of training and time!

An unknown idol group's main vocalist who sang well! There'd already been similar cases, so she'd place her bets here!

*Wow, this is crazy.*

Suddenly, tension surged through the college student's body.

*Ah, I don't even know his face, so why am I nervous?*

It was the first sign that she was becoming his fan.

Even though she went through a strange period of denial, she ended up waiting with excitement for next week's broadcast.

She combed through the performances of all kinds of lesser-known idols for clues, her eyes bloodshot by the end, but she couldn't find a likely candidate.

*Please!*

Today was the last episode of this special program, so she desperately hoped his face would finally be revealed! Then she'd reach a conclusion—her excitement would either burn even brighter or die down completely.

She swallowed nervously and watched the program.

+=
[Bespoke Singer!]

[The battle of burning ideas and raw skill]

[The second half starts now]
=+

Once again, there were a lot of strange and bizarre characters, and every once in a while, an ordinary character appeared that seemed to have had more thought put into it.

But none of them had the wit of 'The Welsh Corgi at Your House'.

*He's unrivaled.*

*You have to give him credit for that.* She declared the Welsh Corgi the winner in her head at least eight times as she waited impatiently for the program to end.

And after about an hour, it finally became clear who was likely to win.

+=
[The final opponent of 'Stone Pot Spaceship' is...?]
=+

The dog holding a picket reappeared on the stage.

+=
[The winner of the first half]

[The Welsh Corgi at Your House]
=+

"Heok."

The college student took several deep breaths as she watched TV.

In the meantime, the sign the Welsh Corgi was holding had changed.

+=
[Looking for a new owner tough enough to put up with me]
=+

"Heob." She repressed a giggle.

*He lowered his standards...!*

*There is a narrative... There is a narrative.*

In an era where job hunting had become more difficult, the Welsh Corgi started the final battle with a bang, making the audience laugh and setting a fun tone.

Darkness slowly fell on the stage.

*Tak.*

The spotlight turned on.

The dog mask remained unchanged as he held an ordinary microphone in one hand. But a pleasant voice came from behind it.

$p
♪ *I'm looking for somebody* ♫
p$

The Welsh Corgi began the last song as if he were telling a story.
