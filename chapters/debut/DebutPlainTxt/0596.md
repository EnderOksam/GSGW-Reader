# Chapter 596

Looking back, these kinds of bastards had always been drawn to Seon Ahyeon, ever since ❬Idol Inc.❭. I wasn't talking about people who simply disliked him. I meant those with a twisted mentality who felt wronged, as if Seon Ahyeon gaining recognition was an unjust event that should never have happened.

[TeSTAR's Leader, Unable to Stand Seon Ahyeon's Shameless Pretense, Makes a Refreshing Statementㄷㄷㄷ]

This wasn't just them gathering to curse and pray for his downfall. Seeing them pour their heart and soul into creating rumors, just praying 'please let one of these stick,' I didn't even know where to begin.

*Fuck.*

I pressed my aching brow and looked at the WeTube video screenshot. I understood why Ryu Chungwoo had shown this to me before taking it to the company.

*The moment a member personally brings this up, it becomes a gesture demanding the company take it seriously.*

Then the situation would definitely escalate. This was his way of asking me to review whether this was the right approach before we proceeded.

Suppressing the urge to bash someone's head in, I turned to the next page. What a mess to deal with right after my vacation ends.

*First...*

A Cyber Wrecker spreading rumors.

*Things like this are hard to get taken down with reports, given the nature of the WeTube platform.*

However, if there were bastards this persistent, hounding him every single day, I felt like I would have known by now. Why didn't I? An anti this malicious should already be a well-known figure among Seon Ahyeon's haters.

"Just a moment." I held up a hand to Ryu Chungwoo. I then grabbed my own smartphone, went to the account spreading the rumors, and scrolled through it.

"......"

And then I realized why I hadn't been able to pinpoint this account.

*It's not just about Seon Ahyeon.*

[The Chilling Meaning Behind the Photo Mirynay's Jeong Yulgi Couldn't Resist Posting]

[Halo Heim's Villain, Lee Suhui's Diet Media Play, Fully Compiled!]

Unlike the screenshots suggested, this didn't look like an anti-account filled with malice only for Seon Ahyeon. It just looked like a common, low-quality WeTube channel that peddled celebrity gossip.

Besides, the volume of content was insane. They uploaded at least three or four videos on trending topics every day. The frequency only seemed high because Ryu Chungwoo had screenshotted only the posts about Seon Ahyeon. In reality, only about one in every five or six videos on this account was about him.

In other words, it was disguised to look like a typical Cyber Wrecker account.

"......"

Ryu Chungwoo waited until I had thoroughly examined the account before asking calmly, "What do you think?"

I was silent for a moment before I spoke. "It looks like they've put some thought into this. They're determined."

"...!"

*Wasn't this just a malicious account sniping at everyone?*

*No.*

This was more dangerous.

The Seon Ahyeon rumors and the general celebrity rumors were different.

*They've deliberately and cleverly mixed them.*

"The other rumors were all topics that had already become issues elsewhere before this account posted them," I explained. "Only the ones about Ahyeon are new fabrications they've inserted themselves."

So what happens when this bastard slyly inserts the rumors they fabricated about Seon Ahyeon among other well-known issues?

"Ahyeon's story will look like it was a pre-existing rumor too."

"Yes."

Seon Ahyeon's rumor would naturally be treated as just another 'hot topic of the day.' Besides, channels that deal with this kind of trashy celebrity news are a dime a dozen.

*There are even a few channels that get not just hundreds of thousands, but millions of views.*

So an account of this scale just looks like 'one of the more popular celebrity wrecker channels.' What happens to the people who mindlessly come across these rumor videos?

[He has rumors like this about him?]

[There's a lot... hmm.]

[I didn't know, but he seems kind of sly and two-faced? That's the vibe I get.]

"......"

That's how it seeps in.

Slowly, the factory-like Cyber Wrecker channels that just copy and splice content from more famous ones started to pick it up. That's how a baseless rumor created from thin air could spread into a real controversy and a major issue.

[The Truth Behind the Seon Ahyeon Ambassador Controversy That's Causing an Uproar?]

If enough people are talking, smoke can rise even where there is no fire.

"......"

Fuck. I deleted the search term.

*Ryu Chungwoo really looked into this properly. If we had missed the timing on this, Seon Ahyeon would have been tormented by it for a long, long time.*

Ryu Chungwoo smiled bitterly. "People often say the best policy for things like this is to ignore them quietly and only act if they cross a line... but I was wondering if we could nip it in the bud before it grows." He then sat on the edge of the bed and clasped his hands.

"If it's a problem we can solve by just catching one person, that would be better," he added.

A wise judgment. However...

"Well." I silently scanned the screen. "This isn't just one person."

"...!"

*Think about it. Pumping out three or four videos every single day? Even if they weren't creating everything from scratch and were just pulling from existing controversies, could one person do that alone?*

And they were even fabricating the Seon Ahyeon controversy themselves. There was no way one person could write a script, find suitable videos, edit them for impact, and maintain that kind of output.

"The AI-run content farms have unnatural transitions and grammar, but this isn't that either," I said.

"......"

"So, if you think about it in terms of the time and labor a person can realistically expend..."

"You mean it's multiple people."

That's right. Even if there was only one account owner, this was a group effort. They were probably the kind of people who get a thrill from belonging, where the pressure of responsibility is diffused because they're all in it together.

"But regardless of that, I agree with you, Hyung. We should inform the company." It felt like if we buried this, it would only blow up bigger later. It would be better for the company to start looking into legal options now. Of course...

"Alright, let's do that then."

I could already guess how this report would end, though. With a somewhat complicated feeling, I watched Ryu Chungwoo make the call.

A short while later, Ryu Chungwoo asked, "You already knew?"

[Yes... that's right.]

Ryu Chungwoo received the response from the company that they were 'already monitoring this account'.

*Right.*I knew this would happen.

The fans had probably already taken PDFs and reported it to the company multiple times. Statistically, it was far more likely that Seon Ahyeon's fans found out about this long before we did.

It seemed the company had confirmed it weeks ago. However, this wasn't a case of them neglecting it due to internal power struggles, like back in our T1 days. There was a practical reason for their inaction.

[We looked into how these kinds of accounts have reacted to lawsuits in the past, and they... they actually make videos about the results of the lawsuit.]

"......"

Oh, I remembered that too.

[Yeongrin's Agency's Rash Move! The Truth About Her Plastic Surgery That They, Fearful of an Issue WeTuber, Tried to Cover Up?]

[So we were worried it would backfire and be used as promotion, with them claiming, 'They're only doing this because we struck a nerve'...]

"I understand what you mean," Ryu Chungwoo said.

It was a simple principle. Even in a country like the US where lawsuits are easy and common, why haven't the paparazzi who invade people's privacy disappeared?

*It's more profitable to pay the fine and keep taking pictures.*

It simply makes more money. Similarly, once these accounts start hitting hundreds of thousands of views, it's far more lucrative to just pay the fine and keep operating. If you don't have any life goals that would be jeopardized by a criminal record, what reason is there to be afraid?

*They'll even turn the fact that they're being sued into content to stir up more drama.*

Moreover, bastards this persistent in targeting Seon Ahyeon would likely rejoice and use the lawsuit as an excuse to blow the situation up even further. It would be the perfect opportunity to bring the controversies they created to the public's attention.

*They'd use the logic that the agency must be really worried if they're suing over my video.*

And as fucked up as it is, the reality is that some people will be swayed by that argument. The company was being cautious because they knew they could be played by that very tactic.

[Still, we will prepare more thoroughly so that we can respond immediately if needed.]

"Yes." At least now that we had pushed them, they would be able to respond quickly if the situation truly spiraled out of control.

A hint of bitterness flashed across Ryu Chungwoo's face as he ended the call, but he quickly regained his usual calm and strength. "Hmm, let's look into other methods, too."

"Yes." Of course we would, but before that, just a moment.

"Hyung."

"Hm?"

I asked Ryu Chungwoo, as if in passing, "You said you don't usually look at this kind of stuff, right? Internet controversies."

"......"

"I was just curious how you happened to see it."

The guy had done a moderate amount of monitoring before, but that was always to listen to what the fans were saying, not to predict, grasp, and try to avoid potential controversies.

*He never needed to.*It wasn't Ryu Chungwoo's area of expertise in the first place, and with me and Lee Sejin around, there was no reason to push him to do it.

I met Ryu Chungwoo's gaze as he remained silent. He gave a small smile. "Hmm, it just came to mind on our way back into the country, so I checked, and I happened to find it. It was fortunate."

"Hmm, yes."

Hmm. It was a standard answer. Which meant...

*He was just saying something to brush it off.*

I knew. Back during his time in STier, this guy had watched his group get embroiled in all sorts of rumors and controversies. He had struggled desperately to defend them and lead the members.

*And the memories of that time must still linger.*

So his monitoring skills had improved by leaps and bounds? I could accept that. However.

*Being able to do something and doing it compulsively are two different things.*

For types like me or Lee Sejin, it's fine to glance at this stuff. We can handle it without much difficulty. As long as we don't look too deep and manage our mental state by taking breaks, it's perfectly manageable, but this wasn't something this guy was suited for or even liked, was it?

I wasn't sure if him being so on edge that he felt the need to do this was a positive sign, especially on the airplane, on his way back from a pleasant vacation.

However, if I spelled all this out, he'd probably just think, 'What is this guy talking about? He wouldn't have even known if it weren't for me.' So I just patted his back instead. "You should rest when you can, Hyung. I'm grateful, but it makes me feel sorry."

"There's no need for that... Alright. Thanks, Moondae." Ryu Chungwoo chuckled, and the moment passed without any awkwardness.

*I'll have to keep an eye on his condition.*

"So, shall we look for another way?"

Alright. It was time to act. "About that." I nodded, glancing at Seon Ahyeon, who was happily admiring the diffuser he had made during the vacation.

For reference, Cha Eugene, upon hearing the gift was for him, had tried to refuse it and was now being discreetly pummeled by Kim Raebin and Bae Sejin.

"It seems so."

"First, Hyung, can I ask you to make sure Seon Ahyeon doesn't find out? I don't think it would do him any good to know."

"Of course." Ryu Chungwoo nodded without hesitation.

I continued in a serious tone. "In addition to that, I'd like to entrust you with all the real-world matters."

I nodded solemnly. "I need you to handle reality. While I'm on my smartphone."

"...Um. Moondae."

"Yes."

"So... you're going to try another method, on your smartphone?"

"Yes."

Ryu Chungwoo fell silent for a moment before speaking. "Um, I don't think you'd do this, but... you have to remember that if a team member gets caught posting anonymously on the internet at a time like this, things will get even more complicated."

*You don't think I'd do it, huh?*His face was overflowing with doubt and concern. I shook my head immediately. "No. I have no intention of posting anything on the internet."

The method I planned to use was different. I cracked my neck. So it was a group, not a lone wolf. That was even better.
