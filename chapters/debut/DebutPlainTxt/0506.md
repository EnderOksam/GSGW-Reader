# Chapter 506

A short, heavy silence fell over the practice room after STier Ryu Chungwoo's statement.

*Does he not think he needs to get his memories back?*

The members were probably also realizing what he meant. He was declaring his intention to stay here, in place of the original Ryu Chungwoo of TeSTAR.

Meanwhile, STier Ryu Chungwoo quietly awaited our response without another word, as if he were waiting for a verdict.

"......"

A moment later, Keun Sejin was the first to respond with a laugh.

"Hyung, what're you talking about? You'll be able to get your memories back. C'mon, don't worry!"

He spoke as if nothing was wrong, giving Ryu Chungwoo a chance to smoothly move on or take back his words.

However, Ryu Chungwoo did not take it back.

"Hmm, I didn't mean I was worried about something like that. Or did you mean you're the one who's worried, Sejin? Are you anxious because I don't have my memories?"

"...!"

Right.

*If he were the type to be swayed by that and take back his words, he wouldn't have said it in the first place.*

However, I did not expect him to ask so bluntly. He asked so nonchalantly if we were anxious because he was lacking compared to the original TeSTAR Ryu Chungwoo.

"If there's something you're worried about, tell me. I'll make up for it right away."

"Oh, no, we're very grateful for you as you are now! But isn't this too unfamiliar and uncomfortable for you, hyung?"

"It's not particularly uncomfortable."

STier Ryu Chungwoo smiled faintly.

"I'm enjoying it. I like the stage, and there are many new things to learn. The unfamiliarity is nice."

"......"

"I'm worried I won't feel this way if I get my memories back."

"C'mon, it'll be even better," Keun Sejin said gently, his voice quite warm.

However, Ryu Chungwoo did not waver.

"How can you be so sure?"

"Huh?"

"This isn't exactly a normal situation where you can be sure of things so easily, and I think I know best how I'm going to feel."

"...Um."

Even Keun Sejin looked momentarily speechless. He probably had a retort but hesitated, afraid he might needlessly provoke the other person and set off a bomb.

When someone says that is how they feel, directly refuting it is the same as saying they are wrong.

*Fuck.*

This was getting complicated. I needed to change the mood right now. I quickly decided what to say and was about to jump in.

"Practice!"

"...!"

Someone suddenly shouted at the top of their lungs. Surprisingly, it was Bae Sejin, who had been tucked away in a corner reading an e-book.

"We only have a week! Break time's over. Whatever it is, let's practice first and talk later!"

"Ah."

He shouted with veins popping on his neck, looking utterly desperate.

The atmosphere changed completely, as if someone had thrown a bucket of cold water on us. We were reminded of our most urgent priority. It was not the way I had planned to do it, but it was extremely effective.

Bae Sejin kept shouting.

"If we delay any longer, Kim Raebin won't have time to work on the arrangement!"

That's right.

"If he pulls another all-nighter to meet the deadline, I'm sure his roommate will do a great job stopping him. His roommate is Park Moondae!"

"Ah."

Hey, that was a bit much.

"...! That's not true! I will finish within the deadline without having to do such overnight work. Please believe me!"

"R-Right, I believe you...!"

"Of course Raebin can do it!"

The tension momentarily broke with the everyday conversation.

"Right. Let's work hard."

"Yes, fi-fighting...!"

And so, the topic was smoothly changed. As if his earlier statement had been a lie, Ryu Chungwoo also actively participated in practice with his usual gentle and cooperative attitude. After some time passed, the tension from before started to feel like something that could naturally arise in such a special situation.

*He must be confused, so it's understandable! Cha Eugene even ran away from home.*

Something like that.

However, I had not forgotten STier Ryu Chungwoo's attitude from earlier.

*He's not confused.*

He was certain. The guy had already come to a conclusion in his head.

To remain here as STier Ryu Chungwoo.

If so, all the effort he had put in until now fit together perfectly like a puzzle. He learned all the performances for TeSTAR's representative songs and latest tracks, asking for more than what he was told. He absorbed the history of the group TeSTAR and the personal story of 'Ryu Chungwoo' here.

It was as if he were training. All so that he could remain as STier Ryu Chungwoo, without regaining his memories as TeSTAR's.

*Was that his goal?*

During break time, I saw Bae Sejin let out a small sigh in a corner of the practice room.

"Hyung."

"...Park Moondae."

Bae Sejin glanced at me, then turned his gaze away again. He looked toward Ryu Chungwoo, who was listening to Kim Raebin and Seon Ahyeon explain the song.

"...I've been thinking about this for a while," Bae Sejin murmured quietly.

"He was uncomfortable with me too. Just like with Cha Eugene before."

"......"

"But... he's desperately trying not to show it. I don't know why, though."

So he had noticed. It seemed his delight a few days ago when STier Ryu Chungwoo accepted the water bottle was not just because his goodwill had been accepted.

*He had interpreted it as a signal that said, 'I'll try to get along with you.*

Just as he said, even if he did not know the reason, it seems he saw hope in that act itself.

Moreover, Bae Sejin was not finished.

"...And I don't know why they were both uncomfortable with me, but."

Damn it.

"That's,"

"Forget it."

Bae Sejin sighed.

"It's obvious, isn't it? I probably caused trouble for the group and ended up getting dragged back to my former agency or something."

"......"

"It'll be fine if I show them how much I've changed since then."

He put on a rather determined expression, but his face soon grew grim.

"More importantly, what should I do now... I don't know."

"......"

We stood side by side, watching STier Ryu Chungwoo. The way he spoke to the members was so friendly that one could almost mistake him for the original TeSTAR Ryu Chungwoo.

However.

"If he doesn't get his memories back, what happens to... the Chungwoo we originally knew?"

I could not answer.

***

One week later, the day of the first competition stage for ❬Genuine Match❭ arrived.

[You will now see TeSTAR's performance.]

We walked onto the stage for our performance, sixth in the lineup. Following the MC's introduction, I adjusted my in-ear monitor.

No matter how confident I was, this was a survival show. One wrong move, one slip-up, and all the career and fame we had built could come crashing down.

However, I was not anxious. Normally, it would have been because we were well-prepared, but that was not the reason now.

*My mind couldn't even get there.*

Because there was a bigger source of anxiety.

"......"

"WAAAAH!"

Cheers and applause erupted. Amidst it all, TeSTAR began to move.

[The sound of falling raindrops]

Knocking on the windowpane

Blue pouring over my head

The hit song by Myudi we chose was a ballad called ❬You Wouldn't Know❭. With its sorrowful, early-morning vibe, it was a song that had become famous on social media as an 'insider's pick'. Its repetitive chorus was addictive and popular, sticking in your ear after just one listen.

Kim Raebin had rearranged it to be much more melancholic and heavy.

[Tired of Love and Sick]

Still waiting for the notification to ring

The intro started just like the original, flowing calmly as if spoken, but the moment the part changed, a slow, low bass rumbled through the speakers.

[You wouldn't know]

Then, a performance with real weight began. The concept was inner melancholy.

The song incorporated a classical piece, becoming more elegant and grand as it progressed. A rather artistic performance was layered on top. It even included Seon Ahyeon's solo dance during the instrumental break.

After veering away from the symbolism of K-pop in our opening performance, this time we tried to create a stage that would appeal as much as possible to a more refined taste.

And for the finale.

[I can't fall asleep]

I'll try saying it once

Goodbye, goodbye

This part, where the accompaniment dies down and only the voice comes through... Kim Raebin had impartially evaluated only our vocal tones to select the right person for it.

Ryu Chungwoo.

[You wouldn't know]

He delivered it flawlessly.

With the sound of the bass, the accompaniment returned.

[Umm... umm.]

The performance faded out as if stagnant and muffled, all the way to the ending.

"......"

We chose a concept so dark and atmospheric that it would have been a disaster with a mediocre stage presence. We did this so that when the right impact hit at the right time, the catharsis and charm would be even greater.

And that strategy succeeded.

WAAAAH!!

As the lights went out, an incredible roar of cheers and applause filled the audience, a stark contrast to the dark atmosphere of the stage.

WHINE-

Bright lights came back on. Before exiting, the members, drenched in sweat and taking deep breaths, bowed brightly, high on the excitement of the performance.

"Thank you~"

"Thank you."

The same went for STier Ryu Chungwoo.

"Thank you!"

Ryu Chungwoo did well, and he looked happy.

*Damn it.*

That just made things more complicated, so I held back a sigh.

However, I could not put it off any longer.

*That would be escapism.*

I had to talk to him before the Update was complete.

A short while later, during the waiting time while the stage was being cleared, we were filming the final cut for the ❬Genuine Match❭ competition.

"There's something I need to talk to you about."

"......"

I called STier Ryu Chungwoo into the small sleeping room inside the waiting room. He did not particularly resist and followed me. Then he asked.

"Is this about what I said a few days ago?"

I was not surprised and nodded. If we went back to the dorm, it would just get pushed aside in the name of work, so now was better.

*And for this... it would be better to approach him as a relative rather than a younger team member.*

That would be more comfortable for both of us. So, I sat down next to him and dropped the formal speech.

"Can I ask why?"

"......"

"I'm not trying to say anything. It's just that we were practicing then, so I didn't get to properly hear why you thought 'it wouldn't be good to get your memories back'."

This time, STier Ryu Chungwoo was not uncooperative either. Instead, he was silent for a moment, as if thinking or choosing his words.

Then he answered slowly.

"Because I don't think that person would be me."

"......"

"I thought about it after seeing Cha Eugene, um, Eugene."

He continued calmly.

"He's very different from the Cha Eugene I knew."

"......"

"He seemed to have the memories of living as the Cha Eugene I knew, but he was still different."

*So that was it.*

That's right. Even though the current Cha Eugene has regained his memories from his STier days, he still prioritizes his memories of living as a member of TeSTAR. It is natural. STier was a long time ago.

There was no way this guy, who was in the same group as STier Cha Eugene, would not have felt that sense of dissonance. In other words, it is natural for him to think that if he regains his memories, his current self will not remain as it is.

*Damn it.*

"I feel like I'll become a different person like that if I get my memories back. I don't want that."

STier Ryu Chungwoo said it outright. I gritted my teeth.

His words did not even end there.

"I know, but, um, I guess you think that's still the same person."

"......"

"If that's the case, then the current me without memories is also the same member, right? Right?"

Wait.

*This... sounds like a prepared speech.*

It was strangely smooth.

*The same member?*

No, that was not it.

*We didn't treat him that way.*

In that moment, I realized the contradiction in our attitude toward STier Ryu Chungwoo. No matter if someone loses their memories, people around them usually treat them the same as before out of habit. Just like how we unconsciously did it several times with STier Cha Eugene before.

*But are we currently treating this guy... as the same person as TeSTAR's Ryu Chungwoo?*

STier Cha Eugene, on the contrary, got into a huge fight and ran away because we treated him like the same person as the TeSTAR Cha Eugene we knew. He vehemently insisted, 'I am a different person from the one you know'.

As a result, we had now come to subconsciously treat the 'original Ryu Chungwoo' and this 'STier Ryu Chungwoo' as different people. All while saying that he had only lost his memories.

*Damn it.*

We were screwed. There was no way he would not have noticed that dissonance.

However, instead of pointing it out, he calmly spoke as if trying to persuade me according to my own logic. As if it did not matter which side was persuaded, as long as one was.

"And in another month or two, no, just a few weeks, I'll be able to fulfill my role sufficiently without any help."

But at the end, his calm voice trembled slightly.

"So please... just let me stay like this."

That day, TeSTAR's performance received tremendous praise and took first place in the live audience vote. Online, a flood of early reviews from the audience members raised expectations for the program and brought relief and excitement to the fans.

It was all according to plan.

However, that was the only thing that went according to plan.

...I received the 'Update Complete' pop-up.
