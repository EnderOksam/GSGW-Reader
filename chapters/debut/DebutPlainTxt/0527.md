# Chapter 527

The teaser was released that morning.

Kim Raebin gathered the members in the living room. He stood before them solemnly, as if preparing for a formal New Year's bow.

Park Moondae felt strangely like a parent awaiting a report card as he looked Kim Raebin over.

*Good grief.*

Kim Raebin was clearly exhausted. He hadn't slept properly in weeks.

Park Moondae spoke nonchalantly.

"...Shouldn't we talk after you get some sleep?"

"If you're saying that because you're worried about me, I'm fine. I'll say what I need to say first."

Kim Raebin replied with firm resolve. He squeezed his eyes shut and exclaimed, "First of all, I'm sorry."

"......"

*Is he sorry for making an amazing trailer and starring in it alone?*

*...Is he sorry for hiding his skills when he could have made something like this all along?*

Absurd interpretations flashed through the members' minds before they were all discarded.

As the members decided to just offer kind smiles, Kim Raebin slowly continued his explanation.

"That teaser heavily reflects my personal feelings."

*We know.*

"However, this teaser is the representative work introducing TeSTAR's new album. It wasn't my personal project."

The members realized what Kim Raebin was trying to say.

[I'm sorry for putting my personal story into our album!]

"I did create a connection to the album. However, I failed to systematically report what symbols and metaphors I used in the process of creating the work."

Kim Raebin swallowed, then said firmly, "I felt I absolutely had to apologize for that..."

He bowed his head slightly.

Before a silence could fall, someone cut in.

"Kim Raebin, you idiot? Sit down, quickly!"

"......"

When Kim Raebin came to his senses, he was sitting on the living room rug, leaning against the sofa.

"Raebin, you've been overthinking things~"

"I know, right? After making it so well."

Warm rebukes without any blame followed. Kim Raebin reflexively accepted the honey tea Bae Sejin offered with a blunt expression.

The person sitting on the sofa slowly patted his back.

It was Park Moondae.

"Even if you don't explain it in detail, we can see that the teaser and the album are well-connected. Because we made the album together."

He continued calmly.

"In the first place, we monitored and gave feedback on the storyboard and the progress you made. You know that, right?"

"...Yes."

"Good. Then don't overthink something you got confirmation for."

He seemed to hesitate slightly, then added, "...The views are good too, so enjoy it a little."

As he said that, Park Moondae smiled faintly.

Kim Raebin fought the urge for his eyes to redden and nodded.

"Yes."

However, there was a secret.

In truth, he had vaguely expected this reaction from the members.

He had learned firsthand from crying and laughing with them over the past few years that they weren't teammates who would rebuke or criticize him.

*I said it even though I knew.*

It was because he wanted this reaction.

The flood of memories from STier had left a shockingly deep mark. He was now extremely sensitive to any situation that might recall those memories.

He was sensitive to criticism and blame.

Kim Raebin needed a counterexample to break the chain of justifying criticism and constructing logic for why he deserved it, no matter what he did.

He wanted to confirm that it wouldn't happen in reality.

"You worked hard. You did well, Raebin."

The attempt was very successful. Kim Raebin finally broke free from the premonition that had gripped him for weeks.

His eyes felt hot, and his chest felt lighter.

He gripped the cup of honey tea tightly.

"Raebin, the teaser, do you like it...?"

"Honestly, it seems like it would be cool even for the person who made it to watch~ What are your thoughts!"

"That is..."

Now, without any more worries, Kim Raebin could purely accept the question, consider it, and give his answer.

"...It was cathartic. I was able to organize my thoughts by embodying the images I felt."

His answer was more about his feelings while making the teaser, not about the teaser itself.

He had already debuted as TeSTAR and experienced many successes. Rationally, he could refute the self-abuse from his STier days with one counterexample after another.

However, he could understand why his past self from the STier days had accepted such malicious and sophistical logic.

His heart ached with understanding.

Because they were the same person.

"It's embarrassing, but... this teaser is actually a song I made to comfort myself."

The room grew a little quiet.

Kim Raebin lowered his head slightly.

"Since this isn't a story that has been revealed to the public, I thought there might be a problem with its public appeal in that it's somewhat ambiguous and can be interpreted in many ways..."

"......"

His concluding phrase, 'I tried my best to compensate for it,' was cut off sharply.

"You idiot, it's fun because all the interpretations are different! That's why it was cool. [That teaser was literally cool!]"

Kim Raebin stared at Cha Eugene with a dazed expression.

*I can't believe Cha Eugene called me an idiot and I don't even feel bad!*

And...

*Is he serious?*

*It's more fun because there's no clear answer to what I'm trying to say?*

As if answering that thought and supplementing Cha Eugene's words, Park Moondae continued.

"Yeah. People will interpret and understand it based on what they know and what they've experienced."

Park Moondae swallowed the thought that it also made for good viral marketing, then nodded.

"...And millions of people like that have seen the teaser. Among them, there must be someone who understood the teaser exactly as you intended."

Those words had a certain resonance.

Kim Raebin found himself briefly imagining the scene.

Someone agonizing over criticism and blame.

That person happens to see the teaser he made, discovers the hidden story, and feels empathy and comfort.

The scene of them thinking, 'This is my story.'

His throat felt a little tight as he nodded.

"Yes..."

This was an emotion he wouldn't have felt if he had anxiously re-edited the work for public understanding.

Kim Raebin renewed his resolve.

[Take it as a reference, but don't be swayed by it.]

His mind felt clear!

With a clear head, he answered firmly.

"Thank you!"

"Don't mention it."

Lee Sejin smiled and wrapped up the situation.

"Now, since our album has such a cool trailer, shall we have a toast?"

"Yeah, sounds good...!"

The members nodded and poured whatever drinks they wanted, from cold water to raisin tree water, to set the mood.

Bae Sejin started talking about the 'Alice in Wonderland' and 'Alice Through the Looking-Glass' references he found in the teaser. Lee Sejin introduced a fan's interpretation video on WeTube.

Kim Raebin took it all in seriously, but joyfully.

It was the same daily routine he had experienced for several years.

*This is nice.*

In that space, Kim Raebin regained a deep sense of stability.

For the past few weeks, he had successfully sublimated his overflowing pain into stimulating inspiration.

That stimulation fueled his creation, but he couldn't produce creative work continuously amidst constant stimulation and no rest. He would get exhausted and depleted before that.

A period of stability and recovery was also necessary for what comes next.

Kim Raebin had just now finally found that mental rest.

*I'm grateful...*

He set aside his feelings and finally laughed with a relieved heart.

And then he fainted.

"...?! Raebin??"

"Ooooooh Kim Raebin!"

As his tension relaxed, the exhaustion from his all-nighters finally hit him.

You could say it was a happy faint.

"......"

"Cha Eugene. You take his legs. I'll take his head."

"Moondae Moondae, you should rest too."

The members scrambled to their feet and carried Kim Raebin, who had already fallen into a deep sleep, to his bed.

This was a point in time with only ten days left until the new album's release.

"......"

As Park Moondae lifted one of Kim Raebin's arms, he made a resolution.

***

Kim Raebin was submerged in a peaceful unconsciousness.

He was having a vague dream in a warm and pleasant mood.*It would be great to sleep deeply like this and wake up much later.*Such thoughts echoed softly in his mind for a while...

A low voice, like a splash of cold water, was heard.

"You awake?"

*Grandma!*

Kim Raebin's eyes shot open. He uncharacteristically wanted to cry out for his grandma as he met Park Moondae's sulky expression.

The hyung, whose expression perfectly matched his 'Tibetan fox' nickname, nodded at Kim Raebin and stood up.

*Huh...?*

Looking again, the hyung wore a sherpa fleece and was dressed to go out.

"Are you going somewhere...?"

"Yeah. To the salon. We have a promotional WeTube channel filming."

*Come to think of it, the comeback is just around the corner!*

This time, Kim Raebin truly came to his senses and hurried to see them off.

*When on earth... did I fall asleep??*

He felt like his vision was spinning!

However, the three hyungs had already finished preparing. They stood at the entrance, smiled, waved, and left.

They were Park Moondae, Seon Ahyeon, and Lee Sejin.

The three were the same age, a combination that worked well for talking about their dynamic on broadcasts.

"W-We'll be back...!"

"We'll do a great job promoting the album and come back~"

Click.

The front door closed with a light sound.

Kim Raebin was left standing blankly at the entrance.

"......"

*Who... am I supposed to ask about this situation?*

It was then.

TAP, TAP.

Someone tapped his shoulder. Kim Raebin quickly turned his head.

Bae Sejin-hyung had a serious look on his face.

"Uh, hmm, let's eat."

"Yes..."

Answering in a daze, Kim Raebin unconsciously followed him to the dining table.

Surprisingly, food was already set on the table.

*Chicken Soup...*

A white soup dish was served in a bowl.

Only one person on this team made it.

*Cha Eugene!*

That's right.

However, this time, Cha Eugene had made a traditional soup using his mother's recipe as is, instead of adding gochujang and turning it into something like dakbokkeumtang.

"Bon appétit."

Cha Eugene shrugged and casually said the idiom for 'Let's eat' before sitting down. The action was so natural that Kim Raebin forgot his question. He simply took a spoonful of soup to his mouth.

It was somehow more familiar than before...

*Ah.*

Kim Raebin realized.

It was a taste he had occasionally had during his STier days.

*Come to think of it, Cha Eugene used to cook often back then...*

Unlike now, where a company assigned people to care for them and a member was good at cooking, back then they would order delivery or take turns cooking.

As a faint memory surfaced, Kim Raebin found himself looking around.

Only the members who had been together during STier were sitting at this table.

Coincidentally.

"Is it good? Do you remember eating it before?"

Cha Eugene even asked so openly.

A brief silence passed.

Bae Sejin was the first to speak, cautiously.

"...I don't remember eating it. No, well... I did eat it last time when you lost your memories."

"That makes sense. Eugene started cooking a full year after our debut."

*After you left.*

Ryu Chungwoo didn't explicitly add those words, but everyone understood the implication.

Cha Eugene shrugged and opened his mouth.

"Oh... Hyung, you have a lot of memories. Then do you also remember not eating my food and throwing it away?"

*Th-that doesn't seem like something you should bring up so lightly!*

Even Kim Raebin thought so, but this atmosphere was somehow reminiscent of their STier days, and he couldn't bring himself to say it.

Because back then, talking back at the dinner table was absolutely forbidden!

"Don't you remember? Christmas Eve."

"......"

Ryu Chungwoo didn't answer for a moment.

He hesitated as if internally conflicted, but soon replied calmly.

"I wasn't the one who threw it away."

"What?!"

"It must have been Min Jeonghun."

Ryu Chungwoo mentioned a specific name. It was a certain STier member who had been involved in a dating scandal.

Cha Eugene almost slammed his spoon on the table.

"Holy sh..., then why didn't you say it was a misunderstanding?"

"There was no need to."

Ryu Chungwoo seemed to debate whether to say more, but soon replied in a rather emotionless tone.

"I figured you guys would fight, so I just took the blame. It seemed easier to control that way. Since you listened to me."

"......"

Soon, Cha Eugene let out a low sigh.

"OK. [That's a typical "STier Leader" move. My fault for asking.]"

Cha Eugene replied cynically, but his tone held no malice. Even someone who didn't know English could tell.

So, no tension was created. Instead, the conversation ended with Ryu Chungwoo letting out a faint chuckle.

A slightly awkward but gentle atmosphere settled over them, like the kind that follows clearing up a misunderstanding.

The silent scooping of spoons resumed.

*Hmm.*

Kim Raebin felt an unexpected emotion.

They were sitting around, bringing up stories from the distant past, from an uncomfortable time at that.

It was a situation where it wouldn't have been strange to feel uncomfortable, but this atmosphere even felt slightly nostalgic.

*Why is that?*

The reason was simple.

Even in very difficult and trying times, when you reach a good ending, there are always things to look back on and reminisce about.

Especially if the people who endured that time together are with you.

"Just how much pepper did you put in this?"

"If you're not sick, you can eat it. This is right!"

They silently finished all the soup.

In doing so, they sorted out the past they had once forgotten and then remembered.

They finished preparing to move forward again.

***

At the same time.

Park Moondae successfully finished filming a promotional mukbang entertainment show for WeTube. He then received a text.

It was very sudden news.

[VTIC Shin Cheongryeo Sunbaenim: Junior, let's meet within three days. Preferably in person.]

*You crazy bastard, I have a comeback,*Park Moondae thought. He was still on set, so he was conscious of his surroundings and planned to send a polite but noncommittal text.

[VTIC Shin Cheongryeo Sunbaenim: Because I'm enlisting in four days ^^]

"...?? ...!!"

VTIC. With Leader Cheongryeo as the last one, there were four days left until all members had enlisted.
