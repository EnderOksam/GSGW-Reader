# Chapter 608

Just before midnight, office worker Ryu Seojin sat down at her PC. She was the twin fansite master for Lee Sejin and Park Moondae, and she had just finished a long night of overtime. The past two months had been long. She had slaved away with overtime just so she could use all her annual and half-day leave during TeSTAR's promotional period.

*In a way, it might have been a relief.*

Her busy work schedule meant she had no time to properly check people's reactions. Right now, the internet was a veritable melting pot of chaos.

[TeSTAR's release is today]

[Crazy, for real, their comebacks are exactly one week apart]

[My hands are shaking]

[Tuesday TeSTAR, next Friday VTIC... Wow, I'm so nervous I could throw up]

[This is gonna be so fun]

The tension was like a balloon stretched to its breaking point, ready to pop at the slightest touch.

When the trailer for their joint agency variety show dropped, more than half of all trending posts were about it for a time. This fueled suspicions that they had intentionally planned their comebacks to overlap.

[What in the world is happening in Korea this year]

[Those T-Holic bastards are old and greedy, this is driving me crazy, ah, my head is going to explode]

[No matter how I look at it, this seems like a crazy plan LeTi cooked up for buzz or something]

[Definitely gonna watch lolololol]

Of course, the conclusion was, 'Would LeTi be crazy enough to do that?' but public opinion was so unhinged that the very speculation became a controversy. On related communities and social media, the fervor was so insane that the small minority of uninterested people avoided logging on, sick of their timelines. The situation could no longer be dismissed as a few individuals or a single fandom misleading public opinion.

*I don't know what's going to happen now.*

She grew so uncharacteristically anxious that she broke an unspoken rule she had set for herself. She discreetly asked her older sister, Ryu Seorin, a former writer for ❬Idol Inc.❭, if she knew why TeSTAR's comeback overlapped with VTIC's.

Her sister's vague answer made her feel like she was losing her mind.

[...It doesn't seem like they planned it.]

"Hoo."

Even industry insiders did not know. The lack of a clear conclusion only made people more anxious.

*In a way... they really succeeded in making people unable to look away.*

That included herself. She ended up mixing a highball before sitting back down at her PC.

Then, it was finally time.

"......"

A new video notification popped up on her silent screen.

[TeSTAR - Bad Feelings Official M/V]

The thumbnail showed the interior of a dark, shadowy warehouse. In it was the pale face of Park Moondae, who looked straight ahead with a scratch on his face. His mouth was slightly open as if in surprise, and his default expression was rather blank. His slightly long, black hair was scattered, and he looked handsome at a glance.

He was definitely pretty and handsome, that was for sure.

"Ha."

But the scratch and that composition... there was a ninety percent chance it was another supernatural universe concept.

Ryu Seojin pinched the bridge of her nose.

"Of all things..."

*If it's too conceptual this time, the side effects will be huge.*

She knew TeSTAR's original formula was to tackle both domestic recognition and the fandom with 'intense conceptual content' plus 'mainstream songs and choreography'.

*It was an understandable strategy.*

No, she even thought it was quite clever and felt proud of them. However, that was only possible because the general public had little interest in idol universes with their music videos and lore.

*But this time, everyone is going to be overflowing with interest.*

It was a side effect of the buzz. Many people were just waiting for something to come out simply because they were curious.

*And as soon as it's out, it'll be compared to VTIC.*

To make matters worse, VTIC's conceptual work was famous for being well-received overseas, so the domestic public evaluated it more generously.

*Compared to that, if TeSTAR's new title music video looks even a little bit ridiculous, it's an outright minus.*

Quality, content, composition. Ryu Seojin pressed her eyes, thinking of the public opinion that would relentlessly mock any small detail. Of course, this also prevented the unfortunate fate of being buried despite being genuinely charming.

*The side effects will be severe, though.*

[What's that, you damn nerd lol]

[TeSTAR did this kind of stuff?;;;]

[Didn't know idols did this stuff these days lol, feels kinda disconnected?]

The twin fansite master's eyes twitched at the vivid, expected reactions. She pressed down on her furrowing brow. Then again, if TeSTAR released a completely mainstream MV, their overseas reputation as 'artists with a great experimental spirit' would wither. There was no way TeSTAR would choose that.

"Hah..."

Right. They just had to endure a little mockery. It would all disappear if the song was good and the performances were great.

*It'll probably be on the level of the mermaid clickbait in the ❬Promise❭ music video.*

She drank her highball with a sense of half-resignation. Then, for real this time, she pressed the play button on the MV.

CLICK.

The black screen first showed a corporate-style logo.

[TESTAR in

[Keep It Safe[]

The words vanished as quickly as they appeared, and the video began.

WHOOSH!

Bright images flashed by. The sound of laughter, a bell's chime, curtains fluttering by a window.

*...A classroom.*

School. A sports field, a broken school gate, a clock, a hallway, school uniforms, desks, a messy chalkboard. The scenes flowed past, showing outer cuts from their debut album ❬Magical Boy❭, through ❬Airplane❭, and beyond ❬Promise❭.

That panorama was like the growth process of boyhood, or perhaps the growth process of TeSTAR. That feast of countless short images...

TAP!

...stopped on an office keyboard.

TAP, TATATAK, TAP.

The sounds of a classic telephone ringing, voices, and rustling papers filled the background. A man's white hand, emerging from a dress shirt's sleeve, typed on a black typewriter. The camera panned up to his face.

It was Park Moondae, wearing a neat office suit and black glasses.

"...!"

Over a bassline that sounded like a pen tapping, tok tok, Kim Raebin's low singing rap overlapped distinctly.

[Get this feeling

Then I'll fall and fall]

The music surged. A sentimental, rhythmic beat and a smoothly popping, modulated guitar sound flowed into a stylish pop melody. The mainstream and mature accompaniment stepped forward smoothly.

At the same time, Park Moondae lightly kicked back from his seat, threw off his glasses, and stood up. He wore a perfectly fitting gray suit.

[I walk on the asphalt

Barefoot

Slowly, calmly, still]

Lee Sejin, who sat behind him, grinned. He put on his employee ID, stood up, and moved in front of Park Moondae. With light steps, they raised their hands in a pair dance.

[Still Reload

In the middle of reloading

Without fear, without fever, without you]

As they moved their feet and flicked their hands, the camera turned. Seon Ahyeon, who had been watering a plant, smoothly joined in the center and began to sing.

[Even in the dreams I have every day

You appear

So today too, I'm sleepwalking]

Every time the camera turned, another member appeared, performed, and joined the formation to create TeSTAR. Cha Eugene had his tie nearly undone and slung over his shoulder. Bae Sejin wore a coat. Kim Raebin was in a perfectly fitting black suit. Finally, Ryu Chungwoo appeared with his shirt sleeves rolled up to his elbows.

Even the twin fansite master's eyes widened.

*A one-take performance with an office concept?*

What fan would dislike seeing handsome, well-built idols performing in sharp suits?

[No promise is of any use]

In time with the rhythm, the screen occasionally whipped around. It matched the modern movements of two white hands racing across a black keyboard with the choreography. It was sensational.

Just as she thought it might be a modern and safely artistic music video...

[I've received so much

I can't throw any of it away

I'll give you a gift too]

Suddenly, a ripple spread across the keyboard.

DRIP.

[......]

In the silence, Park Moondae raised his hand. He wiped the back of it where the ripple had touched.

A drop of blood smeared.

[These bad feelings!

Don't you escape either

Think of me every morning]

Inside the office, TeSTAR performed on stage with dancers. Each wore a perfectly fitting three-piece suit. They pushed aside the office desks and unfolded an intense, lively dance formation using gentlemen's umbrellas.

[These bad feelings!

You take them too

Look at them every day, like I do]

But on the other hand, a strange storyline developed. Scenes showed TeSTAR discovering strange traces while working overtime or drinking coffee in the break room. A burnt-black logo, a secret passage, a hidden symbol.

[Umm- um, Dduru- du

Even when the sun sets and the moonlight falls

Unforgettable feelings]

There was a suspicious black torso covered with a white cloth. There was even a scene of them startled after discovering a strange altar in a basement warehouse. An explosion, a face turning away, a hand knocking on a door, and A4 papers flying through the air. Bae Sejin's hands trembled. Ryu Chungwoo gave a signal with his eyes. Kim Raebin's awl shattered the office window.

[Uuuuuu-!]

All the cutscenes flashed by quickly. It was like a highlight trailer for a drama.

*What... is this?*

[Bad feelings!]

Meanwhile, TeSTAR's performance, singing of obsession over a past relationship, was nearly over. Cha Eugene, standing in the center, did a quick turn, landed on his knees, and grinned.

[These bad feelings-!]

The ending took place in a dark office, between shining spotlights from the lamps.

[Hoo!]

Past Lee Sejin blowing a kiss, colorful paper clips rained down on the camera.

Ending credits.

*...*

"Ha."

Only after the music video ended did the twin fansite master finally exhale. She moved her hand reflexively.

*The comments...*

As expected, it was a mess of single-word screams in all sorts of languages. The ones with any substance were about this much.

[FUCKING SUITS]

[I love you]

[This is it, crazy crazy]

[You crazy bastards dressed them so damn well in all kinds of suits, thank you]

[Preserve Seon Ahyeon Black Sheep horse sock garters]

[Chungwoo pomade slicked-back-hair photocard please]

It was understandable.

*They definitely came out with a plan.*

The destructive power of TeSTAR, who had unexpectedly unleashed the stylish and sexy suit concept they had been saving, was immense. Because they had worn either school uniforms, techwear, old-fashioned uniforms, or reformed traditional clothing until now, the satisfaction of this basic thirst was enormous.

Even she, who was somewhat cynical, almost had her mind flooded with calculations at the thought of taking stage photos right away.

[The grace of the suits is endless...]

*...*

She agreed. However, once her reason returned, her mind began to score the strange storyline that started from the first chorus. It certainly was not terribly cringey. That was a real relief. However...

*It felt a bit superficial somehow.*

Usually, an idol's music video storyline was either part of a universe for the fandom or for the song's impact. In other words, the exaggerated, theatrical, and surreal aspects were often prominent. It was clearly acting solely for the short-term impact of the music video.

*That was the same for TeSTAR, too.*

Moreover, because a famous actor was among the members, it was well-controlled and stood out even more.

*...Bae Sejin.*

But in this music video's short cutscenes, Bae Sejin did not give a theatrical performance. He was shown on screen with a slightly awkward, natural attitude and expression, with a feeling more realistic than a drama.

*Did they go this route to avoid being cringey?*

As a fan, it was interesting and experimental, but since the scenes were like still cuts from a trailer, and the performance without them was so good, it was actually a bit of a letdown.

*There's no element that will help its chart performance.*

Didn't it just eat up the runtime? The twin fansite master thought with a cold, unimpressed look.

*If they're going to overdo it by releasing a 30-minute 'MV Story Extended Film' or something, please, release it much later.*

The views would just be split, and the public who could not relate might react lukewarmly. She was a little worried, but for now, she decided to trust the sense of the idols she photographed. They had gone as far as to become independent from their company, so of course their say in matters would have grown stronger.

*They said the detailed activity schedule would be out tomorrow, right?*

It would be clear once she checked then.

***

But the next day, she confirmed the shocking news.

"......"

There really was a release schedule for additional content, which seemed related to the short cutscenes from the music video, and it was next week.

*Then it overlaps with when VTIC's music video is released.*

However, it was not the worst-case scenario the twin fansite master had expected. It was not some half-baked MV extended version.

[TeStar's Bizarre Office Episode 1 Release]

It was their own original content. The content that TeSTAR was famous for traditionally making and supplying well during their active and inactive periods. Those cutscenes in the music video were actually highlights from an office reality show that TeSTAR had filmed themselves.

"......"

It really was a trailer.

[This Friday, Pre-release Video Release.]

The twin fansite master, who had been staring blankly at the screen, suddenly snapped to her senses.

But wait a minute.

The images of those meaningful, unique, and even a little creepy cutscenes flashed through her mind.

*If the still cuts were like that...*

What on earth kind of original content did TeSTAR film?
