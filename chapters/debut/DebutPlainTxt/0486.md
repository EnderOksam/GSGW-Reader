# Chapter 486

After I absorbed all the information in my assigned 'profile' file, I opened my bedroom door. I stepped out into the dark 'former TeSTAR dorm'.

*The door was locked.*

The knob resisted slightly. I applied a little force and pushed gently.

SHHHK.

"Wh-Who is it?"

"It's me."

"Park Moondae!"

A few guys were already in the living room. They brightened when they saw me. It was Kim Raebin and Bae Sejin. I could make out their builds and faces in the dark. They seemed to have been huddled on the floor, not the sofa, and had scrambled to their feet.

Hmm.

"Did everyone wake up in their own rooms?"

"Yes, that's right."

Kim Raebin nodded and added that he was the first one out. Then Bae Sejin asked directly.

"How on earth did they do this? It's exactly like our old dorm!"

He was not just talking about the layout. We had acquired our old company, T1 Stars, so I heard the lease on our former dorm was still active.

*They could certainly use that dorm for this reality show.*

But that probably was not why he was so surprised.

I looked at the plaster air freshener in front of the turned-off TV. It was shaped like a flower crown, very similar to the one Seon Ahyeon had in the old dorm and later brought to our current one.

"You're right. The items are almost identical."

They had replicated the props from when TeSTAR lived here, making them almost identical, and what's more...

"But because the lights won't turn on, it feels rather eerie..."

"...You're right."

The light switches did not work. Only a few indirect lights in the hallway and along the ceiling were on.

"......"

The desolate atmosphere made it feel like something could happen at any moment. It was an environment that would make anyone with a rich imagination uneasy.

*Hmm.*

Come to think of it, the guys who were particularly strong in these situations were not here. Guys like Ryu Chungwoo or Seon Ahyeon.

*...! Wait a minute.*

I looked at the others' faces again and asked, "Were your roommates not in your rooms?"

"Ah."

The others seemed to realize something too.

"Come to think of it, we were all alone in our rooms, right?"

"Yes! I was, at least."

That was right. The three of us were from three different rooms. Kim Raebin's roommate is Seon Ahyeon. Bae Sejin's is Cha Eugene. Mine is Ryu Chungwoo. Each of our roommates was missing.

*If that's the case... was one person taken from each room?*

Then we could assume that we three had become the 'dorm team'. Of course, it was a group reality show, so we would not be filming by ourselves for long. We would be able to contact the others later, but if they divided the teams this way, one room was left.

*The single room.*

As if realizing the same thing, Bae Sejin clenched his fist.

"Ah, right! Then let's look for Lee Sejin, he's the only one with a single room...!"

"......"

We approached Lee Sejin's single room, but when we carefully tried the handle, the door was locked.

"......"

Not a single sound came from the dark dorm.

"...You don't think he's in there, just being quiet to surprise us?"

"It's possible."

He was a guy who knew his variety shows, but Lee Sejin did not suddenly burst out. For at least three minutes, it was dead silent.

"......"

"......"

At this point, we were creating dead air. A guy with such good timing would not stay cooped up in his room this long and derail the main story. We had to assume the door could not be opened right now.

"I don't think he's here."

"Or maybe he is, but has no intention of coming out?"

We could not know.

"Ugh."

Bae Sejin and I felt a strange sensation as we stared at the firmly shut door.

Just then.

"Um, there's something under here."

"......"

Kim Raebin bent down. He picked up something sticking out from under the door. It was a white piece of paper with writing on it.

*An instruction?*

There was only one sentence.

[I Open at the Close .]

It was cryptic.

"It sounds like a hint for an escape room."

"I know, right."

We stared at the paper with serious faces. A cool breeze, like from an air conditioner, touched the back of my neck at the end of the hallway, where even the indirect lighting barely reached.

*They're really setting the mood.*

Give us a flashlight, come on. The atmosphere was so eerie that it would not be strange if a zombie suddenly popped out, turning this into a survival game.

*It's possible.*

I nodded. First, we should proceed with what the production team asked for.

[Please provide an editing point where you introduce yourselves to the viewers.]

"Should we sit down for a bit and talk about ourselves?"

"......"

It was the self-introduction part.

***

"First, I'm the 'Doctor'."

"Oh."

"That's a fitting profession for you, Moondae!" Kim Raebin exclaimed in admiration, as if someone had picked it for me.

*No, we all drew lots before we were put in here.*

I did not know for sure, but they must have distributed the files based on that. They might have even divided the teams that way. I decided to leave that as a fun behind-the-scenes tidbit for the viewers and just nodded.

"Thanks for the compliment. Um, then Sejin is..."

"Ah, I'm... a 'Lawyer'."

"......"

"What, what."

"Nothing."

*I bet my interview will be inserted here, saying something like, 'Wouldn't a prosecutor have suited that hyung better?*

And everyone would agree.

"But if you're a lawyer, I feel like you might have received a mission to deceive people..."

"What are you talking about! I didn't!" Bae Sejin flared up.

Ah, the mission?

[Job Mission]

: Must be completed before the first game ends.

That was it. It was written in my profile file. It was probably different for each job. I recalled mine and nodded.

"I understand. I'll be suspicious of you for now, hyung."

"Usually, you'd say you'll trust me for now...!"

"I'm not usual, am I."

"...?!"

We livened up the mood with a little skit while also building the survival feel. After we shouted at each other, the awkwardness of a variety show opening definitely subsided.

"Then what about you, Raebin?"

"I am a 'Reporter'. It's a pleasure to work with you."

"......"

After we heard Kim Raebin's job, which was both strangely fitting and not at all, Bae Sejin spoke.

"So now... shall we look around here a bit more?"

"Let's. I think we need to find an exit."

This was clearly set up for an escape room. The large window next to the living room, which should have led to the veranda, was blocked by a thin temporary wall. It was clearly meant to be a locked room.

And the decisive fact. Bae Sejin had approached the entrance and reached for the translucent middle door installed in front of the main door.

CLUNK.

It did not open.

"...The middle door is locked too."

As expected. The keypad on the middle door, installed for double security in the old dorm, held it shut.

"It seems the power isn't on here either."

"Hoo."

After checking that again, we resumed our search.

"Splitting up to look is..."

"It would be better to move together to prepare for any unforeseen circumstances!"

"Right!"

"......"

Well, if you say so. The three of us began to enjoy the 'escape room content', moving slowly as a single unit. We searched our rooms, gathered hints leading to the kitchen, and finally ended up before the bathroom door, which was secured with a padlock.

"If we substitute the numbers here in the order of the rainbow colors..."

"2724! That's it!"

"Excellent!"

These guys were enjoying this more than I thought. Once our eyes adjusted to the dark, the atmosphere felt just like a regular escape room cafe. There were no particular horror elements. No zombies popped out, not even a sudden spooky BGM.

*This is a bit boring.*

Surely writer Ryu Seorin was not hiding her abilities.

*I'm expecting a twist.*

I anticipated a heartwarming and fast-paced editing point and diligently solved hints with the others. Soon, we unlocked the padlock on the bathroom.

"2724... that's right."

"Ooh!"

"With this, we can at least maintain our dignity..."

TAK!

The moment we opened the bathroom door, the light inside turned on.

"Huh?"

"Ah, I think the electricity is back on!"

The fluorescent light was momentarily blinding. Following a natural human instinct, everyone walked into the newly opened, bright bathroom.

*Hmm.*

I immediately checked the interior. This place also almost perfectly replicated the old dorm, but one thing created a sense of unease. A black, rectangular portable device sat on top of the sink.

"A walkie-talkie?"

"Why is there a walkie-talkie in the dorm?"

"......"

No way. I immediately walked to the sink and picked up the walkie-talkie.

*...It has batteries! It works.*

I immediately pressed the button and spoke.

"Can you hear me?"

[...]

"Chungwoo? Ahyeon?"

"...Lee Sejin, Cha Eugene!"

The other side of the walkie-talkie was silent. I shook my head and lowered my hand.

"There's no response... I was wondering if this was the way to communicate with the other members."

"Ah..."

"Perhaps the hyungs or Cha Eugene haven't found it yet."

"That could be it. Um... hey, can I take a look at the walkie-talkie for a second?"

"Of course."

As we were exchanging our deductions,

FLICKER.

"......"

"Hey, just now."

FLICKER, FLICKER.

"......"

This time, it was definitely not an illusion. I looked up at the ceiling.

*The bathroom light...*

It flickered twice in a row.

*And usually, this kind of directing means...*

Ah, damn it.

"Park Moondae?"

I grabbed the scruffs of the two guys' necks and pulled them back.

And then,

Flicker, flicker, flicker, flicker, flicker, flicker, flicker, flicker, flicker, flicker, flicker, flicker, flicker, flicker-

"Uwaaah!"

"Waahk!"

I pushed the two screaming guys and immediately fled the bathroom. My vision flickered rapidly. Behind me, something like a shadow... damn it!

*Someone's in there!*

As soon as I barely made it out, I reached back.

SLAM!!

"......"

I slammed the bathroom door shut and re-locked it with the padlock.

"Wh-What was that??"

"I just had a bad feeling!"

That was clearly the kind of setup where a ghost or a zombie pops out of a corner cabinet!

"Did anyone see anything?"

"I-I didn't look back..."

"...Me neither."

Hoo. I stared at the deceptively quiet bathroom door, then sighed in relief, holding my breath.

*Well, we didn't get caught.*

It would be better to go back to the bathroom only after we have more people.

*...I probably got filmed looking like a coward just now.*

Bae Sejin patted my shoulder as if to comfort me. For some reason, that pissed me off.

"I-I grabbed the walkie-talkie."

That was a good move. It is an unwritten rule that the last person holding an item does not drop it.

"That's amazing! Ah, it seems the living room switch is working now too!"

In the midst of it all, Kim Raebin cleverly realized that the other lights in the house were now working.

*Then maybe things that use electricity are working too?*

I got up.

"The middle door keypad?"

"...! Yes! The light is on!" Kim Raebin waved his hand.

I caught my breath.

*...Okay.*

Looks like the middle door will open soon.

*This is probably enough footage for the early part of the show.*

I immediately started walking towards the middle door.

"...Hey, Park Moondae."

Bae Sejin lowered his voice so Kim Raebin would not hear.

"Yes."

"About this... there's something like blood on the bottom."

"...!!"

I focused on the bottom of the overturned walkie-talkie he held out. I could clearly see something dark red and sticky.

*...Is it time?*

Is it really zombies? While I was checking camera angles and predicting where they might appear, Bae Sejin whispered softly.

"Um, Raebin will probably be scared, so... let's not tell him."

Hmm?

"...Um, yes."

When I nodded, Bae Sejin nodded back. He firmly gripped the walkie-talkie and headed forward.

And I thought.

*...Should I retrieve the walkie-talkie?*

That guy was acting a bit unnatural just now. Kim Raebin would be scared? Kim Raebin is the type to react with something like, '...? It's obviously fake blood for a variety show, so why would I be scared?' He is a surprisingly wild guy.

*He's the type who would even catch a chicken with his own hands.*

Perhaps because he grew up on a farm, he has a particularly strong stomach. There is no way Bae Sejin does not know that.

*Well, I could let it slide by assuming he was flustered and just trying to protect the youngest.*

However, one more thing bothered me.

*Bae Sejin's job.*

A lawyer. A lawyer ultimately represents the accused in criminal law, and the accused has a chance of being the culprit.

*What if the game focused on that characteristic?*

What were the chances that Bae Sejin received a mission to protect a troller, or to do the trolling himself?

*...For now, I'll keep it in mind.*

I decided to keep an eye on 'Lawyer' Bae Sejin. It was just a variety show, so we did not have to win... but I was done with being backstabbed so one-sidedly like during ❬The House's Grandson❭'s Carrot Coin Phantom Thief.

I quickly pretended not to know anything and joined them at the middle door. Bae Sejin and Kim Raebin stared at the message 'Incorrect number' with serious faces.

"The middle door number... it's not right."

Of course. They would not have used the original TeSTAR dorm password, if only for security reasons.

*But isn't it a bit slow-paced to have to find another number?*

After a brief thought, I answered.

"How about 0000?"

"Ah."

The default number.

BEEP-BEEP-BEEP-

"Oh!"

"It-It worked!"

Finally, the middle door opened. Beyond it, the main entrance door was visible.

*First, check what's ahead.*

Just then.

[...Hyung!]

[...Guys~!]

"...!!"

"Th-The members?"

Shouts came from the other side of the entrance.

"It seems they were waiting on the other side!"

"Y-You're right!"

*So we are joining up after all.*

We quickly approached the entrance. The voices grew louder.

[...cked! Let us...!]

It sounded like they were saying 'let us out'. I nodded and took a step forward. At that moment, the front door began to glow.

"...!!"

"Wh-What is it."

The two guys jumped back. It was good that they were reacting quickly based on their bathroom experience.

"It's okay."

I raised my hand and brought it to the door.

SHHHK, RIIIP.

A layer of wrapping peeled off smoothly from the corner, like a sticker from its backing paper. It was coated paper.

"A cover... is it?"

"......"

The outline of the paper cover, hard to see in the dark, became visible with the light from behind it. The source of the light was...

"A panel?"

It was a pad, like a smartphone or monitor screen.

"......"

"I-Is it okay to install something like this on a perfectly fine door of a family home?"

Confused voices came from the other side. It seemed something had happened over there too.

Just then.

BLING.

[Welcome to the Lamb Game!]

"......"

Light burst from the screen on the front door, and a sentence appeared with a sound effect. It was very large. Kim Raebin covered his mouth, staring at the screen as if his eyes would pop out.

"Wh-What is this?"

"Let's, let's back up a bit."

SHUFFLE.

The three of us moved back three steps from the door in perfect unison. At the same time, the screen changed dramatically.

[First Game]

[Choose a Sacrifice]

"...!!"

[Sacrifice one job to open the front door.]

Time until results are announced: 3 hours.

What?

***

The internet.

At the very moment TeSTAR began filming, a random post appeared on their company's, 'Orbit Stars'', account.

[Please choose a character to be the sacrifice. (Link)]

[??]

[What is this]

Many suspected it was a hack, but there are always pioneers. When curious people clicked, a list appeared along with a vague black silhouette.

——————————

1. Doctor

2. Sheriff

3. Detective

4. Scholar

5. Mortician

6. Lawyer

7. Jester

——————————

It was a public vote for a scapegoat.

It was the moment a survival variety show in the truest sense began.
