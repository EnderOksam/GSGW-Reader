# Chapter 513

I gathered the STier members at dawn for a long talk.

"Hoo."

A few hours later, I got out of bed. The light from the window was still dim.

It was earlier than my usual wake-up time.

To be honest, I hadn't really slept. I had just closed my eyes, unable to fall asleep.

My head throbbed a little. I wasn't sure if it was a side effect from messing with the System or the aftereffect of an all-nighter in a thirty-year-old's body.

Since Keundal seemed to exercise regularly, it was probably the former.

*It's not even my body anymore, so I should be using it properly.*

It was a relief that today was a day off.

I checked the condition of Ryu Gunwoo's body, then looked at Keundal sleeping on the floor.

For the record, a lot of conversation went into deciding the sleeping arrangements.

First, I had to listen to them worry about my mental health for a few minutes. Then Keundal shouted.

[You should sleep in the bed! It's your bed in the first place...]

[Okay.]

[...? R-Really?]

[Yeah.]

He would be the one sleeping in the bed once this was over and we swapped bodies anyway. I looked at the guy passed out on the floor with a blanket and nodded to myself.

*...Perhaps, now is the time.*

I closed my eyes for a moment and immediately called up the Status Window. I had to confirm if this matter was truly over.

Then I called for him.

*...Lee Sejin?*

STier Bae Sejin. The Lee Sejin A I had been talking with through pop-ups.

"......"

But there was no response.

Only the last pop-up I received remained in the air.

[Lee Sejin A: Thanks.]

"......"

He was no longer here.

That meant the matter on that side was finished.

The guy who said he 'wanted to check for himself' and went to find Bae Sejin's consciousness was gone.

*Right.*

I was lost in thought for a moment before I lay back on the bed.

There was no point in waiting. I had done everything I needed to. It was time to go back.

*I should wrap this up quickly without waking the sleeping guy for no reason.*

A translucent Status Window floated in the air next to the pop-up from Lee Sejin A.

[Viewing Fragment Record]

[Mission Failed: Return to Original State]

The elapsed time was written at the bottom.

(04:11:21 / 48:00:00)

Four hours.

It felt like a day or two had passed, though.

[Would you like to end the fragment record viewing?]

As expected, I could stop the viewing at any time within the given period.

*...Right.*

I nodded.

[End]

My vision flickered, and I felt a brief dizziness before it returned to normal.

I realized I was lying on the floor with my eyes closed.

I was back in Park Moondae's body.

There was no new pain.

*Ugh.*

But the headache wouldn't stop. It felt like a needle piercing my temples, a side effect of messing with the System.

*Is this damn pressure going to stick around?*

But Keundal was still sleeping soundly as Ryu Gunwoo, so it seemed the pain hadn't stayed in that body.

*Then this is better, I suppose.*

It was better for the pain to be tied to the mind of the one who caused it. It would be damn annoying to suddenly switch bodies in the middle of the night and get a bonus headache, wouldn't it?

I wouldn't stand for it. In that sense, having to deal with the situation I created wasn't so bad.

*It'll probably last a few days at most.*

Keundal seemed to recover after sleeping, but my head just throbbed because I couldn't sleep.

I pressed my temples and sat up. I planned to get a glass of water and wait in the kitchen before the other guys woke up.

The moment I opened the door and stepped outside.

I saw him.

"......"

The kitchen was dark and silent in the early morning.

Someone was already at the island counter.

The dark silhouette... was Bae Sejin.

"...Hyung?"

"......"

No answer came back. He just stood there blankly, a glass of water in his hand.

I didn't rush him and waited for a response.

A moment later.

"......"

He nodded, drank the water in his hand, and then asked me.

"Are you going to get some water?"

"...I was thinking about it."

At my answer, Bae Sejin quietly went to the water purifier and got another glass of water. It was no different from his usual early morning routine...

*No.*

*...Is that really Bae Sejin?*

Watching his unusually calm demeanor, I suddenly considered the possibility.

*...Lee Sejin A?*

He finished using the water purifier. Then, holding the glass, he started walking over.

As he passed the window, the faint sunlight illuminated his dark figure.

He... looked sheepish.

"It... it was you, right?"

"Huh?"

"The one who sent him. That...!"

It was Bae Sejin, all right.

It was so typically Bae Sejin that the tension instantly drained away. I also confirmed one more thing.

I nodded.

"You met him."

That 'Lee Sejin A' had successfully communicated with him.

"I did."

Bae Sejin fell silent for a moment, as if unsure what to add, then slowly opened his mouth again.

"And... I received the memories."

"......"

So that's what happened.

I nodded.

Just then, he added nonchalantly.

"Not all of them, though."

Hmm?

***

A few minutes ago.

Two men with the same face were in a standoff. The one with slicked-back hair and a more resolute gaze spoke firmly.

[You can't claim ownership over a time you didn't experience. Stop it.]

"What have you been talking about? If that's my past, then it's something I experienced! Does forgetting it mean it never happened?"

[...!]

An endless back-and-forth ensued. It could be summarized as 'Did I ask you to hold onto it?' and 'It's my decision'.

A tense argument with no room for compromise unfolded within the sleeping Bae Sejin's subconscious. It was a true internal conflict.

[I am an embodiment of your consciousness that received past memories. Therefore, it's no different from you yourself trying to regain your memories as a test, and then deciding not to.]

"What are you talking about? You don't know how I've been living either."

The one with the more blatantly shown expression frowned. It was TeSTAR's Bae Sejin.

"So how can you be so sure you'd make the same decision as me? That's just stubbornness!"

[That's sophistry.]

"You're the one spouting sophistry!"

And so, both of them, mentally panting, reached an implicit truce.

[......]

"......"

They looked at each other as if fed up, but their expressions remained firm.

It couldn't be helped. Even though their experiences were different, they were the same person, so their ways of thinking were similar.

The one who spoke first was Lee Sejin A.

[I understand your point.]

Years of legal battles and prison life had taught him to accept unavoidable situations and quickly find another way.

However, the destination remained unchanged.

[But the decision is mine.]

"...!"

He gave up on persuasion and decided to act immediately.

"Wait...! Then let's do this."

However, Bae Sejin's side was a bit more unconventional.

He had learned over the years that acting as the heart goes without agonizing over it could lead to good results.

*If I do this...!*

He boldly opened his mouth.

"Just give me a part of it."

[......]

"When I wake up, I can hear the story from the other members anyway, but hearing it directly won't be distorted, and I can understand it for myself."

The truth gets distorted when it passes through someone else's mouth, whether from concern, discomfort, or some other reason. It happens voluntarily or not.

Lee Sejin A knew that part well.

"It's okay if you don't give me the parts you're uncomfortable sharing."

[......]

"But I need to receive enough to make a proper judgment."

So that I don't misunderstand.

Lee Sejin A silently stared at the man with the same name and face as him. Bae Sejin felt a tension that made him want to swallow.

A moment later.

"...!"

The calm-faced Lee Sejin A extended his hand.

[Alright.]

It was a handshake.

Bae Sejin cautiously reached out and took it.

——————————

Memories poured into his consciousness.

His vision disappeared.

"Gasp!"

Bae Sejin held his breath for a moment at the overwhelming pressure he was experiencing for the first time.

Still, the feeling of being overwhelmed was brief. Soon, his consciousness became clear within a sensation of being completely soaked, as if submerged in water.

The first thing he felt was a message.

[Who are you?]

It was a message he had sent to someone.

In the black space, a strange figure.

Ryu Gunwoo. In other words...

*...Park Moondae??*

[I'm a member of the same group as the current you.]

Bae Sejin realized.

What he had received was not Lee Sejin A's life.

It was the memory from just before, the tail end of the short time Lee Sejin A had spent talking with Park Moondae inside the System!

*...! Just this??*

Bae Sejin was flustered at first. Immediately after, he was almost furious, thinking he'd been scammed, but he soon grasped the situation.

*Ah.*

Lee Sejin A's experiences and background knowledge that led to this point also came to him naturally.

He naturally acquired the feelings and knowledge as if he had experienced it himself.

*My god.*

It seemed it wasn't a lie that this STier Bae Sejin was an embodiment based on his consciousness.

And because the content was quite shocking, he understood why his past self had tried so hard not to return the memories.

It was pure goodwill.

*...Accused of drug trafficking.*

The knowledge alone was horrifying.

However, the memory he received now was just background knowledge being recalled.

The terrible arrest, the arduous trial, and the time of suffering could be understood in a refined way, as if filtered through a state of having already overcome them.

So Bae Sejin slowly digested it all.

He could accept it calmly, as if faintly recalling something long past.

But there were some things he couldn't take so calmly.

[It's been a while.]

The situation where he, borrowing Ryu Gunwoo's mouth, spoke directly with the STier members.

The real-time feelings Lee Sejin A felt while revealing the truth to them was so vivid it was as if Bae Sejin were experiencing it himself.

[I won the case.]

A faint sadness, composure, relief, a clean aftertaste.

And... conviction.

*So that's how it was.*

Bae Sejin understood then.

The firmness he had seen in Lee Sejin A, his past self, was the unique conviction of someone who had struggled alone and finally achieved what he wanted.

It was strength.

At the same time, it was shocking.

*I... could be like this?*

Lee Sejin A was his past self.

That meant he was the kind of person who could endure such an extreme situation without breaking.

"......"

In fact, Bae Sejin had thought his growth as a member of TeSTAR was a miracle in itself.

He believed it was only possible because he had luckily joined a team with good people who encouraged him and strengthened his resolve.

He knew his own sensitivity, so he had subconsciously thought that if his environment had been just a little worse, he wouldn't have been able to handle it.

However, that might not have been true.

*I... could withstand the worst.*

And I could win.

It was a thrilling realization.

So when the memory playback ended, he felt so overwhelmed that he spoke out without thinking.

To Lee Sejin A.

"Hey..."

[......]

But no answer came.

He had already disappeared. Or perhaps, he had been absorbed into him.

*...Ah.*

He felt a bit lonely. Even though he knew that person was him.

So, even knowing he was in his subconscious, he tried saying it out loud.

"You worked hard."

Strangely, it was comforting.

Feeling his opaque vision gradually brightening, Bae Sejin had a hunch.

*I'm waking up.*

And so, he woke from the dream.

He didn't plan to explain everything in detail, but he thought he should at least explain the course of events.

***

"...That's what happened."

"......"

After finishing his story, Bae Sejin took another sip of water. I nodded to myself.

It seemed he had a better experience than I thought.

*So in the end, two identical guys argued their hearts out and agreed to transfer just enough memory to grasp the situation.*

Such a consistent guy. I laughed and drank my water.

"That's amazing."

"Are you being sarcastic? ...No, you probably aren't."

"I'm not."

Bae Sejin meekly accepted my answer and plopped down onto a chair.

Then he spoke with considerable force.

"Anyway... about that."

"Yes?"

"What are we going to do about them? Especially Ryu Chungwoo."

"......"

"I felt it while getting the memories, but earlier... he looked so exhausted," he added seriously.

It was a surprisingly proactive attitude.

"That guy, during STier, he seemed to be shouldering too much, but he had no one to rely on and no reward for it."

Right.

"The problem is that I get why he wants to stay here... Right, I get why you were so worried."

Bae Sejin furrowed his brow as if in thought, but he soon lifted his head.

He had a rather reassuring expression.

"So, I've been thinking about it too."

Oh.

"Yeah."

"Listen. So..."

Bae Sejin began to seriously offer his opinion, and I was quite surprised.

*This is good.*

His opinion was quite good.

As a side effect, I realized listening to him had cooled my head considerably.

*Sharing the burden, huh.*

Frankly, it was a pretty good thing to work out a solution with an outsider who knew the whole story.

Two heads were better than one.

*Not bad.*

Like that, I forgot my headache and had a short conversation with Bae Sejin.

Until the other members woke up, and even Keundal woke up, turning the morning into a chaotic mess.

"Huh? Gunwoo... Ah, it's Keundal!"

"Y-Yes! That's right. Um, I should get going..."

"Hey, hey, stay for breakfast! How did you end up here so early in the morning?"

"Hup, ah, well, umm..."

For the record, Keundal was tossed back and forth like a ping-pong ball between Lee Sejin and Seon Ahyeon throughout the meal. They shook the entire story of what happened out of him.

That meant the guys who heard the story and understood the exact sequence of events were now glaring at me.

That I had recklessly rushed into using the System.

"......"

"......"

"So... you switched bodies and came back?"

It's fine. I was prepared for this.

"M-Moondae..."

"......"

"Park Moondae, are you really going to be like this?"

"No."

I won't do it again.

*Because now I know how to handle the System.*

The calculations are done.

"Ah... Hey, this won't do! You should write a pledge or something..."

"I will."

"......"

I willingly signed a promise to prevent the recurrence of 'reckless use of the System without consultation'. The members looked like they were about to burst into applause.

"Let's make him use his thumbprint."

"Y-Yeah, right...!"

"......"

Do we have to go that far?

Of course, even in the midst of this, the two who weren't original members were an exception.

The STier guys.

*Hmm.*

Cha Eugene diligently explained what was happening to Kim Raebin, but Ryu Chungwoo showed only a faint reaction, as if he hadn't decided how to respond.

His expression was still gentle, but that was all.

Normally, he would have actively jumped in to grasp the situation and then tried to blend in naturally.

*Hmm.*

It seemed that after our conversation at dawn, he had been having some complicated thoughts of his own.

I met Bae Sejin's eyes and gave a nod. Then I opened my mouth.

"By the way, we have a new shoot tonight, right?"

"Y-Yeah...! The, ❬Genuine Match❭."

This competition program filming.

I planned to make my move there.

However, when I actually got to the filming location, another variable had appeared.

❬Genuine Match❭ had adopted a survival competition format and eliminated the last-place contestant from the previous stage. Now, a new contestant had joined.

"Oh my, they're rookies."

"Let's do our best~"

Those guys were the first ones on set. They went around greeting everyone with nervous faces.

But that contestant was.

"Please take care of us! We're the rookie group Aether from Wonder Hall!"

It was Aether, the rookie group that had benchmarked us.
