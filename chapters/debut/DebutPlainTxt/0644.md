# Epilogue

It was a sunny autumn morning.

BEEP! BEEP!

CLICK.

"......"

The man who turned off his smartphone alarm slowly sat up. His well-built upper body, still heavy with sleep, hung low for a moment. He then lifted his head with a burst of energy.

He shouted.

"Fighting! Time for work!"

It was Keundal, Ryu Gunwoo.

He quickly hopped out of bed. His alarm was originally a TeSTAR song, but he changed it after hearing that a song used as a morning call could easily be ruined.

*I can listen to them plenty during the day anyway!*

Just as he did as a 7th-grade civil servant, Keundal quickly washed up, gulped down cereal, and dressed. His old work clothes were a black suit.

"That's not the case anymore."

He smiled and touched his sleeve. He wore cotton pants and a neat shirt, looking more like a college student preparing for a presentation. His new workplace only required this level of formality.

*It seems like a good place.*

Orbit Stars Entertainment. He walked energetically toward that very building, which was also TeSTAR's company.

***

The city bus was packed with people on their way to work.

Keundal habitually sat in the very back and picked up his smartphone.

[TeSTAR in animal pajamas at their first Seoul concert ㅠ (Fancam)]

[Taking a survey for Agent Chungwoo plushie demand.]

[Our boys have another Bill interview up! (Link)]

TeSTAR's remix promotions were over. However, their achievements and stories continued to pour out endlessly.

Serious talk on the internet even suggested they might receive the Order of Cultural Merit. His own social media feed was free of such things, filled instead with people who were simply happy because of TeSTAR.

Keundal scrolled down his timeline with a pleasant look, then checked a DM he had received.

[Thank you so much for the concert ticket, Big Moon..ㅠㅠ Even thinking about it again, really...]

The social media friend who got the concert invitation still sent him sporadic thank-you messages. They even sent a gifticon a few times, which he had to politely decline.

He could not accept them.

*...They were a high school student.*

He also could not bring himself to reveal that he was the 'ahjussi' who sat next to them at the concert. Keundal's pupils trembled when he remembered receiving snacks from them, but he soon replied cheerfully.

*Not at all! I'm glad you enjoyed it ㅎㅎ*

His timeline overflowed like the Milky Way with people sharing their daily lives related to TeSTAR. Some took a day off for a birthday cafe, others found happiness in small things that reminded them of TeSTAR, and some cheered themselves up on gloomy days by watching their videos.

Unbeknownst to Keundal, his feed also included casual chat accounts of fansite masters he was indirectly connected to.

[Let me know when Lee Sejin appears on a variety show]

[ㅠㅠㅠMoondae cut his hair. Remembering our Moondae's mullet and long hair.. (Photo)]

[I'm planning to go to one of the U.S. shows for this tour too! ㅎㅎ Starting to save up my vacation days...]

"Oh, I'm jealous."

He then realized he had no reason to be. If he just said the word, his hyung would immediately send him a ticket.

*No! Let's not be dependent.*

However, he had already received a ticket for a K-POP omnibus concert in Seoul.

[It's 2 tickets again, is that okay? Sending them now.]

[No, Hyung... Ugh... Th-thank you.]

Keundal held back a sigh, blaming himself for his weak resolve. Still, he could not help his good mood. He failed to suppress a smile as he happily refreshed his timeline.

*What kind of supportive post will there be this time...*

[Kim Raebin lip-syncing to a pre-recorded track at a concert? ㅋㅋ Pathetic, they're just squeezing out rumors ㅠ How bad at live singing must the idols you stan be for you to think this would work ㅠ Our god-baby-rabbit sings everything live, you f...]

*Gah.*

He quickly hit the back button. The ferocity from his timeline made him break into a cold sweat.

*Is that a fan of Raebin?*

*They're full of energy this morning.*

Keundal swiped his smartphone screen once, then shot up from his seat. It was his stop. Through the bus window, he saw the Orbit Stars company building.

"Gunwoo, you're here~"

"Hello!"

Keundal diligently greeted everyone in the office. His previous job got him treated like an experienced hire, but he was really still on probation. It had not even been a month since he started.

The department itself had just been created.

"Now it's up to us to build it up."

As expected of a new agency, they had few trainees, and all had joined recently.

"We're planning to start recruiting trainees in earnest, but there aren't many suitable kids these days."

"I don't know how LeTi manages to snatch up all the kids when they barely debut anyone," a colleague grumbled, saying their skills were on par with a scammer's.

LeTi was an old, major agency, and the brand power of the currently active VTIC had been immense for the past few years.

"Of course, the game has changed now!"

It was not just their department that was grinning over TeSTAR's recent results.

"We have TeSTAR."

"Of course. They'll definitely come!"

Keundal, who had only been listening, straightened his shoulders with pride.

*That's right, my hyung is that amazing...*

"...so, Gunwoo."

"Yes?"

He had missed what was said and asked again. His superior kindly repeated it.

"For today, how about we just go out and get a feel for street casting?"

Huh?

Huhhh?

***

*Oh my god.*

And so, 'Employee Ryu Gunwoo' ended up wandering the nearby streets that morning.

Of course, they had not sent him out expecting him to successfully cast someone. It was hard to even see middle or high school students on a weekday morning.

[Just go get a feel for it. Just a feel!]

He had been tipped off that they were just giving him a few generous hours to slack off, since he had worked for the past week with almost no days off.

*This is nice!*

Besides, it was hardly slacking off. Keundal had not come out alone. They had even assigned a trainee to him as a casting reference, so they went out together.

"......"

*And I haven't seen anyone I'd particularly want to cast so far...*

He felt heavy-hearted, thinking he was just making the trainee walk around for nothing.

As Keundal trudged along, BUMP, his shoulder bumped into someone.

"Ah. I'm sorry."

"...No."

He turned his head and saw the trainee who had come with him. He was handsome but had a sharp edge that would make people question his school's discipline, or even his attendance.

For some reason, Keundal felt a little anxious.

*Even if he's a trainee, right now... isn't it the time when students are usually at school? I heard he attends school.*

"School... are you not going by any chance?"

"Today is the school's founding anniversary."

*Phew, I see.*

Keundal felt a little relieved by the student's surprisingly compliant answer and turned his gaze away. As if on cue, a familiar melody flowed from the electronic billboard.

*It's on!*

On the way back to the company, he had asked the trainee for a moment and stopped inside a nearby subway station to watch.

Keundal caught his breath.

[TeSTAR WORLD TOUR Official Trailer]

The music began. The screen showed Cha Eugene opening his eyes with a grin on stage, then widened as if being pulled back.

WAAAAAH!

A vast main stadium appeared amidst the cheers. It was the promotional video for TeSTAR's upcoming world concert tour.

The video showed the seven members' dynamic group dance, spectacular stage production, hot and passionate live vocals, and their overflowing talent. Even in the subway billboard's low resolution, it shone like fire.

*I'm glad I came to see this.*

He was a little embarrassed that he had even texted his hyung to say he was going to see it, but he did not regret it.

"Um," the trainee next to him asked in a trembling voice. "TeSTAR, the sunbaenims. Have you ever seen them in person? I mean, uh..."

"Yes."

Keundal slowly nodded, his eyes glued to the screen.

"I've seen them."

He remembered the concert, its captivating power, and the light that made his heart race. Keundal was no longer a Status Window and could not summon the hologram that graded an idol's abilities.

Still, he could see it.

He could see the power of the artist called TeSTAR as clearly as the cast introduction subtitles appearing in the promotional video.

[Cha Eugene]

On the screen, a person with reddish-blond hair grinned, revealing his canines. He was a Black Hole, an energy and talent that commanded attention. He was an idol whose explosive stage performance was imprinted in your mind like a signature.

Beyond his playfulness, an intuition that seemed to pierce to the core made his attitude even more charming.

As if passing a baton, the next person appeared. He was a performer with a decadent impression, with sanpaku eyes and a mole on his cheek.

[Kim Raebin]

The screen filled with a scene of him sprinting across the stage with a single microphone during his solo intro. His incredible artistic talent made even recklessly experimental music shine as unimaginably beautiful songs.

His warm and polite demeanor, which contrasted with his cool appearance, created an intriguing and unique off-stage charm.

The next member also possessed such a charm.

[Bae Sejin]

His incredible acting ability, presented with a neat appearance, filled the screen with something close to a heartfelt appeal. His acting skills did not just stand out in his projects. His amazing expressiveness elevated the quality of the stage's story.

Comparing his pre-debut self, who had no foundation in dancing or singing, to his current, outstandingly grown self would give you goosebumps. A similar feeling came from realizing just how dedicated Bae Sejin was to this team's stage.

Then, the person who supported the team he was so dedicated to appeared.

[Ryu Chungwoo]

The unwavering, textbook power from his well-built body and vocal cords made him a focal point. That power balanced the performance and captured the focus of the entire stage.

The stability and confidence felt whenever Ryu Chungwoo stood in the center was charming. He even used that as a foundation to not shy away from a bit of mischievous boldness. He was the wick that sustained the team.

There was one more person on the team with such a wick.

[Seon Ahyeon]

On the screen, long arms and legs drew elegant lines. He posed in a brilliant turn during a solo dance, as if doing a handstand on his legs alone, and his beautiful face was captured. He had an incredible appearance that could be called a born dancer or a natural beauty.

But in truth, the firmness and strength that overcame adversity were likely idol Seon Ahyeon's greatest charm.

Following him was someone who seemed born to be an idol.

[Lee Sejin]

A flash of ecstasy crossed his face as he delivered a performance that cut across the stage. The intimidating presence from his large physique blended with his idol-like dance lines, creating a thrilling sense of rightness.

His skills, honed over long years of passionate deliberation and practice, shone like a jewel. A refreshing pleasure filled the screen, as great as the satisfaction he felt as an idol.

And a voice just as refreshing rang out from behind.

The final member.

[Park Moondae]

On the screen, an idol raised his microphone with a grin. Beads of sweat scattered, reflecting in the light. He was an idol equipped with all the virtues of a main vocalist, with overwhelming vocal talent, a tone full of sensibility, and a voice that filled the hall.

However, his charm went beyond his vocals. The more you watched, the more witty ideas and surprising actions would pop out from his unique personality. For instance, contrasting with his cool and rational side was his love for the stage and the audience.

[TeSTAR]

Ending on Park Moondae's smiling face, the promotional video concluded spectacularly. Even through the camera, the concert stage did not lose its starlight-like sparkle that captivated the eyes. It was just like a fancam from any other day.

"......"

For some reason, his heart ached with emotion.

He suddenly turned his gaze and saw the boy next to him also staring at the billboard, completely entranced.

*An aspiring idol...*

Seeing that, he felt he could understand a little about the job of casting. He knew what kind of standards to set.

Keundal hesitated for a moment before he spoke.

"Do you like TeSTAR?"

"......"

The boy standing next to him gave a slight nod.

This time, Keundal spoke without hesitation.

"I have a ticket to a concert that TeSTAR will be in. Do you want to go?"

"......"

He gave the spare ticket for the K-POP omnibus concert to the trainee, who bowed his head repeatedly, and sent him on his way.

*Yeah. This feels right.*

He was about to reflect on his choice but forgot all about it the moment the promotional video started playing again.

"I'll head back after watching a little longer."

He stood for a while, watching the billboard. He wanted to see the TeSTAR tour video, which would come around again, just one last time.

"Hoo."

At last, the video was about to appear on the billboard again.

TAP, TAP.

Someone touched his shoulder. Had the trainee come back?

"Ah, did you leave something behi—"

"Park Moondae."

He turned his head, unable to breathe. A person with a hood pulled down low was looking at him and smiling. It was a man with neatly cut black hair covering his pale face.

TeSTAR's Park Moondae.

"You weren't answering my texts. So this is where you were."

His hyung said it took him a while to find him in the station. He looked at his smartphone, then raised his head and grinned.

"Why are you here...?"

"I heard you had to work today, so I came to have lunch with you."

"Aaaack!"

"The guys are waiting."

"W-Wait a minute!"

Soon, Ryu Gunwoo ran out of the underground station with Park Moondae.

The sunlight sparkled.

[Epilogue End]
