# Chapter 397 - The Wise Finals, III

Practice was in full swing on the ⟨Wise⟩ filming set...

The trainees seemed grim and focused solely on the finals, but that didn't mean there weren't quiet conversations going on behind the scenes.

Who among the bottom-ranked contestants would survive?

– *Who are you going to choose?*

– *Depends.*

Although the vote was supposed to be anonymous, the contestants were asked subtle questions about it in their interviews.

– *Have you already made up your mind about who to vote for?*

One contestant from the low-tier team gave a suitably noncommittal answer, like 'I'm thinking hard about it,' but in fact, he had almost made up his mind.

*I have to vote for two people, so there's nothing to worry about.* It would've been difficult if he had to choose one person to stay and one person to eliminate. But since he could vote for the best person and then the second best as well, there wasn't anything difficult about it.

*I can just leave out the people I don't want to vote for.*

First of all, one person had already lost his vote.

– *What did you say to me, you bastard?!*

*Ugh.*

*Who would want to work with a bastard who suddenly loses his temper in the middle of a meal and tries to beat someone up?*

The contestant nodded decisively, completely unaware that Shin Jaehyeon, who knew about the assault incident that would occur three years later, had engineered a situation where the guy would act this way and arranged for rumors about his bad attitude to spread.

So naturally, after excluding one person, there were three people left.

And it was simpler to exclude one more person from there.

Someone he wasn't close to. Someone he didn't know very well. Someone who'd never been a trainee.

It was the supplementary team member who'd joined the show most recently.

*Ryu Chungwoo.*

*He'll get by just fine even without this show.*

Former national athletes received annuities from the government. The contestant had no close relationship with him, nor did he feel any sense of camaraderie toward him.

*Then, I can just choose the remaining two and it's over.*

The existing trainees had already made up their minds to choose the two familiar, safe picks they'd practiced with and somehow felt uncomfortable about seeing eliminated.

The four contestants in danger of elimination could also vote, and alliances had already formed through their existing friendships.

*I guess that's how it goes.*

Having come to that conclusion, the contestant went back to lounging around, enjoying his short and precious break.

*Click.*

The practice room door opened, and he heard someone speaking.

"...That's why I'm not sure who to vote for."

"Yes, that's true."

He heard familiar voices. Some of the other contestants.

*Uh.*

The contestant was lying behind the sound system in the practice room, taking a break, so he was out of sight.

Ryu Gunwoo and Jung Woodan continued their conversation.

"Do you have anyone in mind?" Ryu Gunwoo asked.

"Well... Are you going to vote for Ryu Chungwoo?"

"For now. What about you?"

They were chatting comfortably, apparently unaware that someone else was there. The low-tier team contestant found himself quietly overhearing their conversation.

"I was thinking about voting for Dahwan."

"Ah."

Kim Dahwan was one of the two people he was trying to vote for.

Since the topic he'd just been thinking about had come up, the contestant held his breath and listened closely.

"It's not a bad choice. Although the dance positions seem a bit full."

Ryu Gunwoo hesitated a little, then seemed to reassure himself that there were no cameras around and spoke freely.

"Well, it doesn't matter to us. We're vocalists."

*...!*

*The dance position?*

"Yes. It'd be good if the more skilled people got through and raised the group's overall skill level."

"Yes. But we don't need to worry about that sort of thing. I'm sure the president will handle it for us."

*Ah.*

The two talked a little more about who they would vote for, resolving to stick to their decisions before leaving the practice room, which still seemed empty to them.

"Not everyone is here yet."

"It's too early. Then, why don't we step out for a bit and—"

*Click.*

"......"

The door shut. However, the contestant felt somewhat disoriented.

*What did he say? The dance position was full?*

"...He's right."

That was right. Of the people left now, only four were vocalists, including those two.

There was only one rapper left, the fierce-looking guy from the supplementary team, while there were a total of 7 dancers. The dance position was packed.

And at the top were Shin Jaehyeon, who was a monster, and Cha Eugene, a supplementary team member who came out of nowhere...

*...There's no room.*

Almost twice as many dancers as vocalists. The contestant, who was also a dancer, swallowed nervously. Moreover, something else bothered him.

– *But we don't need to worry about that sort of thing. I'm sure the president will handle it for us.*

The final lineup was chosen by the president, not by individual viewer votes.

Naturally, given the president's inclination, he'd select the debut members based on position balance, so... That put the contestant in a dangerous position.

"......"

The contestant bit his lip, overcome by a sudden sense of crisis.

When people had the freedom to vote for whoever they wanted, they'd eventually rationalize the choice that benefited them personally.

Especially when the huge prospect of debuting was hanging right in front of them.

So the conclusion was quick.

*...It's only right to eliminate competitors as much as possible. I'm not stupid. I'm not a pushover either.*

*The dancer who's at risk of elimination has to go.*

He hesitated for a moment, but then made up his mind and quietly left the practice room and ran down the hallway.

Ryu Gunwoo and Jung Woodan, sitting in the vocal practice room opposite, watched him go.

"He really came out."

"He did."

"Are you sure that worked?"

"Yes."

Ryu Gunwoo chuckled. There was one thing he was sure of.

His own position.

Among the contestants, he was at the top in terms of public recognition, popularity, and skills, and he had the president's favor and high praise.

*It's hard for the other contestants to ignore my opinion.*

It wasn't about closeness, but the power of hierarchy and authority.

Since it wasn't the same as personal liking, trying to persuade them directly might make them resist, but an indirect approach would be more than enough.

*They'll believe it more if they think they have figured something out on their own by analyzing another person's words.*

In fact, an overabundance in one position could work in a group's favor, depending on the group's direction and whether it leaned into that strength. But now they wouldn't be able to think of it that way.

Of course, Ryu Gunwoo had no intention of explaining all that, so he kept it brief.

"I can bet money that the mood will change by tomorrow."

"I'm not one for uncertain gambles, but I'll respect your opinion as a courtesy to the key player."

"......"

Ryu Gunwoo kept quiet. Anyway, after marveling for a moment, Judan placed his hand on his chin.

"How many more times do we have to do this?"

"One more time, but a little differently this time."

"Will that be enough?"

"Words are bound to get around anyway. The remaining trainees are all in similar situations, so they'll try to get together. You saw him run out, didn't you?"

Now that TeSTAR and VTIC had taken over the vocalist positions, the remaining trainees were all vying for dancer positions and were essentially in the same boat.

They'd share this strategy among themselves. They needed to make their votes count, and since they were in similar situations, their interests would naturally align.

*Then, no matter how the votes are distributed, as long as Ryu Chungwoo gets at least 1/3 of them, he's guaranteed to pass.*

There were 6 people from VTIC and TeSTAR whose memories had already been restored.

That was half the contestants, so it should be enough.

Ryu Gunwoo shrugged his shoulders, and Judan looked quite serious... He agreed with him.

"It seems like you think a lot about human psychology. I do that too sometimes. It's a pretty interesting habit."

"......"

Ryu Gunwoo did not bother to reply, but decided to quietly continue explaining the next target.

Meanwhile, in the hallway they were no longer watching closely, something that even Ryu Gunwoo couldn't have predicted was happening.

"Heok."

1. The contestant tripped and fell while running down the hallway.

"Ouch!"

2. He felt a sharp pain in his ankle.

"Are you okay?"

3. Ryu Chungwoo, who was returning from filming his interview, witnessed the fall.

And he reacted immediately.

"Wait a moment. Don't move."

Ryu Chungwoo reflexively responded to the situation as he had been taught during training.

"Does it hurt here?"

After pinpointing where it hurt...

"I think your muscles are a little strained, but you don't have to worry about an injury."

He offered advice and reassurance based on his own experience. He even found a pain-relief spray in a nearby break room and handed it over.

"Use this."

"Ah... yes. Thank you."

And then he left without saying anything more or offering any further advice.

It was a light favor that could perhaps just be treated as routine. Something Ryu Chungwoo always did.

However, the situation was a bit special.

"...Hmm."

The contestant got up after spraying the medication on his ankle and started thinking about what had just happened.

Ryu Chungwoo had been calm, generous, and quick to react. He hadn't been a national athlete for nothing.

*He'd be a dependable member to have on the team.*

And... He was a vocalist, too. There was no need to compete for positions.

When the contestant thought about it, if he didn't vote for Kim Dahwan, who was a dancer anyway, the only remaining candidates were Ryu Chungwoo and the idiot with anger management issues.

At this point, common sense kicked in.

*...Isn't it better to choose someone who's a good team player rather than that guy with anger management issues?*

The contestant quietly decided to change what he'd been about to tell the others about the vote. Then he walked slowly down the hallway toward his dorm.
