# Chapter 509

*Swap bodies with Keundal again?*

From the building collapse to the body swap. The Status Window called it a 're-Update', a function to 'recreate' the situation from the last 'Mission Failure'. I stared silently at the absurd option.

*Who in their right mind would welcome a feature like this?*

I went through hell last time. I didn't want to voluntarily experience that again. Who would want the thrill of being trapped in a collapsing building or ruining their schedule?

*Is it doing this because I've been thinking about absorbing a 'Fragment of ■■■' recently?*I had acquired a fragment every time I resolved a 'Mission Failure'.*Is it trying to find a connection?*

*Even a corner store would fire you for making demand predictions like this, you bastard.*Besides, I had applied the System to the company. Why was it giving me an extra feature that had nothing to do with it? It wasn't like it was borrowing an IP from a famous webtoon to churn out a contextless mobile gacha game.

Then, a pop-up appeared as if in response.

[Mission Failure. Revert to Original State]

Maximum Viewing Time. 48 hours

[Would you like to view it?]

The bastard couldn't even read the room.

*Just go away.*

I dismissed the pop-up and closed my eyes. My temples throbbed.

...First, I needed to organize the situation.

*The members who have lost their memories right now are Ryu Chungwoo and Kim Raebin.*

Objectively, both were cooperative and didn't act out. However, Ryu Chungwoo was reluctant to regain his memories, and Kim Raebin was reluctant to perform.

That wasn't all. I recalled Kim Raebin's expression from just before.

[B-But...]

*Earlier, STier Kim Raebin was watching Ryu Chungwoo's expression.*

He looked a little scared. It was a rare sight for him as a member of TeSTAR, but he did it almost like a habit.

"......"

I fell into thought, recalling the strange unease I had felt from STier Ryu Chungwoo over the past few days. I reached a conclusion.

*There's no other choice now.*

I had been holding back, afraid of making things worse, but things had come this far. Continuing to hold back would just be escaping reality. If the gentle approach didn't work, it was time for a direct one.

*I'll dig for answers.*

I knew the most suitable person to ask. The one who was complete, with both sets of memories.

~~~

I called Cha Eugene into the kitchen. He was at the center of the last incident and had successfully regained his memories.

"Tell me everything you remember," I began.

"......"

"The internal conflicts during the STier days."

Cha Eugene stared at me with a sour expression. "You said you'd cook something delicious for me! You tricked me!"

Right. This was why I had recruited Lee Sejin and Seon Ahyeon to separate the two STier members into the dance studio and the workroom, but it wasn't a trick.

"I'm going to do that too."

"Oh."

"What do you want to eat?"

"Kimchi fried rice, please."

Request received. I opened the refrigerator and took out the ingredients.

"Add cheese, please!"

You got it.

The instantly pacified Cha Eugene stood next to me as I started chopping kimchi.

"I'm asking because with the situation like this, I need to gather as much information as possible to handle it," I said.

"......"

"It means I can't go on not knowing things anymore."

Silence. That was tacit agreement.

I brought up what bothered me most. "First, it seems like Raebin is wary of Chungwoo."

"Umm... Yep."

An affirmation.

"What's the reason?"

Cha Eugene looked sullen for a moment but soon composed himself. "Do you really want to hear it?"

"Yeah."

"OK."

Cha Eugene fell quiet. I didn't rush him and silently continued preparing the ingredients. Soon, a calm voice echoed over the sink.

"Back then, STier's Chungwoo was a scary person."

"...!"

That was an unusual choice of words.

*Scary?*

"What was he like, specifically?"

"[You know, half of STier's initial members were a mess. So he led the team like a high-ranking alpha. Like a very rough football captain.]" Cha Eugene shrugged slightly. "[So what I mean is, he didn't treat the team members with the same accepting and gentle attitude he has now.]"

"...For example?"

Cha Eugene furrowed his brow, recalling a faint memory. "[He once locked a member who skipped dance practice out of the dorm and made him stay up all night in the practice room.]"

"......"

I gripped the frying pan's handle for a moment.

Cha Eugene continued, his tone mixed with a scoff. "[That stupid guy had to practice the choreography all through the night, almost on the verge of tears.]"

"......"

"[And that kind of punishment continued for a few years.]"

*It's hard to believe.*

It wasn't that it was impossible for someone with Ryu Chungwoo's character. It was a question of whether it was realistically possible.

*This isn't the army, after all.*

Even if he was the eldest, they were all peers. How could a leader in name only, in the same boat as everyone else, punish them like a drill instructor? As long as the other members had their own minds, there was a limit to its effectiveness.

Besides, they had all just debuted from a popular survival show. Their egos would have been at their peak.

"That actually worked?"

Cha Eugene answered immediately.

[It worked.]

"......"

*I see.*

*It means that Ryu Chungwoo, surprisingly, had a knack for ruling by fear, to the point where common sense didn't apply.*He had used a hardline approach to take control of the group's chaotic atmosphere.

I silently flipped the bacon. Then I started to stir-fry the chopped kimchi in the grease and confirmed, "So as a leader, he was properly coercive."

"Something like that."

Hearing Cha Eugene's affirmation, I mechanically added the cold rice to the pan and broke it up. Repetitive labor made it easy to think, and I quickly formed a rebuttal.

"But Raebin would've listened even without all that."

"What about me? I listen too!"

"Do you have a conscience?"

"Ooh."

Cha Eugene made a sullen face, as if wronged, but he soon gave a proper answer. "It causes problems if you discriminate. In my opinion, that's why Chungwoo treated all the members with the same strictness."

"......"

So he had to dominate them from the start so they couldn't even squeak, which meant he couldn't make exceptions.

*So that's why even Kim Raebin became wary of him... huh.*

Come to think of it, even the STier version of Cha Eugene was surprisingly obedient to Ryu Chungwoo at first. It showed just how grim the team atmosphere was, with the former national athlete Ryu Chungwoo taking control 'without holding back'.

*The rule-by-fear meta must have been that intense to work long-term.*

It suppressed rebellion for years. Half the members eventually chose to leave rather than cause problems within the group. He had taken a group the agency had given up on and somehow managed to run it.

However, Cha Eugene seemed to slightly regret his words.

[As our fan, you have the right to protect your fantasy. That's why I didn't mention it specifically but... yes, that's the truth.]

"......"

And I knew.

"You have no reason to lie."

"That's right. I'm an honest person."

I finished the kimchi fried rice by adding cheese. I spoke calmly to Cha Eugene, who was chasing away his gloom with his appetite.

"It's not like any particular fantasy of mine was shattered."

"...Are you being serious?"

"Yeah."

In the end, it meant Ryu Chungwoo had made the best choice he could within the given environment. I couldn't blame him because it wasn't the absolute best choice.

*It's a miracle he managed that much with a bunch of brats who wouldn't listen.*

More importantly.

"Then that means the current STier Ryu Chungwoo is desperately trying to act gentle on purpose."

Cha Eugene seemed lost in thought for a moment but eventually nodded. "I think so too. People hate showing their weaknesses. Chungwoo is no exception."

In other words, STier Ryu Chungwoo considered his 'coercive Leader' image a weakness that wouldn't help him settle in here.

*...In the first place, he probably wasn't very keen on acting so coercively himself.*

I predicted this as I cut a pat of butter in half and put it in a new pan to fry an egg. I then asked Cha Eugene, who couldn't take his eyes off the kimchi fried rice with its melted cheese.

"Got it. Then what about Raebin?"

"......"

"Do you know why he hates getting on stage?"

At that, Cha Eugene's shoulders slumped slightly.

"I don't know either."

"......"

[The STier Kim Raebin I remember... yes, he was less active than now, but he didn't hate the stage.]

"......"

I didn't mention that even now, he didn't seem to hate the stage, just that he didn't acknowledge himself as a 'performer'. The result was not that different.

*In any case, it seems Cha Eugene doesn't know the exact reason either.*

A decisive incident might have happened to STier Kim Raebin after Cha Eugene went to America.

[After the group disbanded. After parting ways with all the members.]

I decided to remember this timing.

"Let me ask one last thing."

"...OK."

"...What happened to Bae Sejin?"

SIZZLE!

[I think we lost contact a few years ago, and I remember he kept appealing the trial.]

*He appealed?*An appeal meant he didn't accept the court's verdict. That also meant his drug distribution charges were confirmed, and he was sentenced to either prison or a fine.

*Shit.*

If it was distribution, a prison sentence was more likely. I held back a sigh.

"What was the result?"

"It's not in my memory. [I think the STier members probably didn't look up the final verdict on purpose.]"

Cha Eugene's voice sounded a little bitter.

"But I know now. Sejin is a completely moral person. So... [Anyway, the me of now thinks it was probably just a coincidence with bad timing.]"

"...Right."

That was all.

*In that case, it means all the STier members knew Bae Sejin's charges were confirmed in court.*

That made it even more surprising that STier Ryu Chungwoo was acting so gentle and kind to Bae Sejin here.

*Hmm.*

I formed a few hypotheses as I lowered the heat on the induction stove. Then I placed two sunny-side-up eggs on the kimchi fried rice, where the cheese had started to stick to the pan.

"All done."

"......"

*Whether they regain their memories or not ultimately depends on this System Update.*

[Why did their memories from the STier days return under the guise of an Update.]

[When will they regain their original memories.]

The principles of the System, and under the current conditions, a way to find out came to mind.

*Then I have to find out.*

If I hesitated, things could drag on, and the bomb might go off when it was out of my hands.
