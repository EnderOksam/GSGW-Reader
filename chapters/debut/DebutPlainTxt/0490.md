# Chapter 490

Kim Raebin reviewed his job ability again.

Sheriff.

If he found the culprit before the game ended, he could shoot them.

*Then I can just catch the culprit and save the member who became the sacrifice!*

The original rules of this game were simple.

[Participants can pass the Sacrificial Token to each other through card exchanges.]

[If you correctly deduce the identity of the culprit who last passed the Sacrificial Token, also known as the Black Sheep, then that culprit and the sacrifice will die.]

[If you fail to deduce it, everyone except the culprit and the sacrifice will die.]

It was a team-based deathmatch. The culprit and the sacrifice automatically formed a team of two, while the remaining five members became another team. Only one side could survive.

However, the story changed if the Sheriff successfully shot the culprit. Instead of both the culprit and the sacrifice dying, only the culprit would be offered as the sacrifice, and the game would end.

This meant the sacrifice could also live. It was the best possible route, resulting in the minimum number of deaths.

There was a condition, however.

[If the Sheriff shoots the wrong 'culprit', that innocent person is immediately made a Burnt Offering.]

If Kim Raebin accidentally shot someone who was not the culprit, that person would die. A Burnt Offering meant they would burn to death on the coffin-shaped altar. He had to catch the correct culprit in one go.

*Let's do our best!*

Kim Raebin inwardly clenched his fists.

Meanwhile, the situation in the room descended further into shock and confusion after Seon Ahyeon's revelation.

"Y-You're the detective?"

"...And you think the Sacrificial Token was passed over when you exchanged cards with Moondae?"

"Y-Yes."

For the first time since the deductions began, the two Sejins stopped fighting and questioned Seon Ahyeon in unison. Both of them wore peculiar expressions.

"Then, if what you're saying is right..."

——————————

1. Seon Ahyeon <-> Park Moondae

2. Cha Eugene <-> Ryu Chungwoo

[Mid-check]

3. Ryu Chungwoo <-> Bae Sejin

4. Cha Eugene <-> Park Moondae

——————————

Based on this order, the person Park Moondae exchanged with after Seon Ahyeon was the last one, number four.

4. Cha Eugene <-> Park Moondae

Everyone turned their heads.

"Cha Eugene."

One hour before the card exchange ended, Cha Eugene had found a new coin near the entrance. He exchanged a few words with Park Moondae and completed the exchange right on the spot.

Kim Raebin swallowed hard.

*Really...?*

The person in question, Cha Eugene, however, had an indifferent expression.

"I'm not the sacrifice. Because I'm not the scholar."

"......"

"I can swear on it. I swear."

He was perfectly composed. The members were a little flustered.

"Th-Then what's your job?"

"I'm the doctor."

"......"

The members began to murmur at Cha Eugene's unhesitating words.

"Hmm, right after it was revealed that Moondae isn't the doctor but the Mortician? The timing is a bit strange~"

"I didn't know Moondae-hyung was the doctor! I was just being quiet. I was trying to observe what the members were saying and figure out the answer."

Bae Sejin asked cautiously, "Can you prove it?"

"No one can prove their job, but I'm definitely the doctor! If there's anyone else here who's the doctor, come out and say it."

"......"

"No one is speaking up. I know why. Because I'm the doctor!"

Cha Eugene shrugged. His confidence certainly seemed real.

*Is he telling the truth?*

If so, they had to restart their deductions from the beginning. Kim Raebin tried his best to rethink who the scholar and the culprit were.

Just then, Park Moondae, who had been silent as if lost in thought, brought a hand to his chin and spoke.

"...Ahyeon, you said you're the detective, right?"

"...Y-Yes."

"In that case... even if it's true that you're the detective, there's another possibility."

"What is it...?"

"The first sacrifice chosen by the viewers' vote. It might not have been the detective, but the clown."

"......"

That was right. What if the Sacrificial Token started with the clown, not the detective?

"If that's the case..., it makes me think."

Park Moondae turned his head.

"Among the suspicious candidates who still seem to be lying about their jobs, there's only one person with a card exchange record."

He naturally looked at that person.

Bae Sejin.

"......"

"Hyung. You exchanged with Chungwoo-hyung after the mid-check, right?"

Bae Sejin's eyes widened.

"And you said that Kim Raebin claimed to be a reporter even though there's no reporter job, right? But Kim Raebin has no card exchange record."

"......"

"You claimed that Lee Sejin was the clown, not the lawyer, but that Lee Sejin has no exchange record either."

So, among the people who had not clearly claimed a job and were surrounded by controversy, only Bae Sejin had a card exchange record.

*No way!*

Kim Raebin stared at Bae Sejin, his mouth agape.

"......"

Bae Sejin responded in the calmest tone he could manage.

"Cha Eugene is just saying that confidently, there's no proof. Someone else could be the doctor but is staying silent because of a job mission."

"Hmm."

"And... right! Cha Eugene also exchanged cards with Ryu Chungwoo before Park Moondae. I-If Chungwoo was the clown, and like you said, the clown was the first sacrifice by the viewers' vote..."

Ryu Chungwoo raised his hand with a troubled expression and answered.

"Um... but in the end, Eugene exchanged cards with Moondae. Then wouldn't the Sacrificial Token move from Eugene to Moondae?"

He was right.

"Then Moondae should be the sacrifice, but that's impossible since Moondae's job is Mortician, not scholar."

"Ah."

In the end, it was a contradiction. It did not match the final job.

*It's impossible.*

Kim Raebin swallowed.

"Even if Eugene was the clown and had the sacrifice card at first, it's strange. After he exchanged cards with me, the token would have passed to you."

"......"

"Sejin-ah, are you the scholar? The final sacrifice."

Bae Sejin's face hardened at Ryu Chungwoo's question.

"No way! And if that's true, it means you're the culprit! Since you were the last one to exchange!"

"Right. So that means it's not true. If we were on the same team, we wouldn't be questioning each other like this."

Ryu Chungwoo continued to speak calmly.

"And Sejin-ah. Weren't you insisting earlier that Lee Sejin was the clown?"

"......"

He had a point. Until now, Bae Sejin had fought tooth and nail with Lee Sejin over the lawyer job, each claiming the other was the clown. It was a bit awkward to suddenly suspect someone else of being the clown.

In the end, Bae Sejin groaned.

"Seriously... Hey, Lee Sejin! Just reveal your job honestly! We can't make any deductions!"

"...Hah! Hyung-nim, you're a really good actor! Wow~ Why don't you be honest for a change?"

"......"

Lee Sejin looked like a vein was about to pop on his forehead. He explained coldly with a smiling face.

"In the first place, hyung, even if I were the clown, it's impossible for me to pass the Sacrificial Token~ I didn't make any exchanges."

"......"

That was right. Lee Sejin was one of the only two names not on the card exchange list.

*The other one is me...*

Kim Raebin swallowed.

Lee Sejin's kind explanation continued.

"But the final sacrifice's job is 'scholar,' right?"

It was not the detective or the clown, who were likely to be chosen by the vote. The final sacrifice's job was scholar.

"Regardless of whether the viewers voted for the detective or the clown, the person with that job isn't the sacrifice now, right? It means they passed the token through a card exchange."

"......"

"I don't know how it got passed around... but it ended up with the scholar."

There was not a single logical flaw in Lee Sejin's words. Even Kim Raebin found himself nodding unconsciously.

*I see.*

Then it must be true. Lee Sejin and he, who had no exchange records, were innocent.

"Then... that means Cha Eugene is the scholar, and Park Moondae passed it to him!"

"I'm not the scholar. There's definitely another possibility."

"That's why I'm saying I'm not the culprit either..."

"......"

Silence fell. No one seemed to be lying.

Even Park Moondae said, "...Don't tell me the viewers chose the scholar from the very beginning."

"The production team pulled some trick?"

"Hmm."

The groaning members now looked like their heads were about to split. Because the jobs were all mixed up, no scenario seemed to fit, no matter how they looked at it.

However, Kim Raebin knew one more certain piece of information about the jobs. It was a piece of information no one else knew.

About himself.

*I am the Sheriff!*

He began to fill in the blanks from there. Kim Raebin clasped his hands together. The two men with the same name fought over one job, each claiming the other was the clown and that they were the lawyer.

*Let's assume that one of them spoke the correct deduction and truth.*

If so, the chart could be completed easily. He slowly recalled everyone's testimony, matching people to their jobs.

If he continued to eliminate possibilities like that...

"......"

Only one slot remained.

He realized.

"...!"

Kim Raebin took a deep breath and called out one person's name. The person who was left.

"Chungwoo-hyungnim."

"Yes?"

Kim Raebin turned his head toward him, recalling the chart he had created. It matched members and jobs.

——————————

1. Ryu Chungwoo - ?

2. Bae Sejin – Lawyer (or Jester)

3. Seon Ahyeon - Detective

4. Lee Sejin - Lawyer (or Jester)

5. Park Moondae - Mortician

6. Kim Raebin - Sheriff

7. Cha Eugene – Doctor

——————————

If you fill it in like this, there is only one question mark left, and... only one job left. If you put it there, it becomes this.

——————————

1. Ryu Chungwoo - Scholar

——————————

*Ah.*

This way, there were no contradictions in the job testimonies. It was the first set of job information without any contradictions.

*I have to start my deductions from here!*

Now, he had to say it.

Kim Raebin opened his mouth resolutely.

"I am the Sheriff."

"......"

"Therefore, hyung, you are the scholar!"

"......"

"I've matched all the job testimonies, and... the only one left is you, hyung-nim."

Kim Raebin eagerly explained the chart he had made. He could feel the members listening to his words with intense concentration.

"...That's all!"

"......"

Kim Raebin caught his breath.

*Will they believe me?*

He looked around with desperate eyes.

And... Park Moondae raised his hand.

"Wait, what's the probability that neither Bae Sejin-hyung nor Lee Sejin is the clown?"

"That's..."

"It feels a bit inaccurate to me. Since there's no proof."

How could he. Kim Raebin was shocked and about to become disheartened.

"......"

"...!"

Bae Sejin interjected, narrowing his eyes.

"Park Moondae, you... aren't you trying to protect Ryu Chungwoo? That's how it feels to me."

"Excuse me? What are you talking about..."

"Th-That's right."

"...!"

Seon Ahyeon also chimed in calmly.

"The usual Moondae would have said, let's at least think about it... Since Raebin worked up the courage to say it."

"Hmm. He could be trying to trick us."

"Raebin...?"

"......"

Everyone fell silent.

*Huh?*

Kim Raebin was a little flustered, but Bae Sejin nodded emphatically.

"Right. If Raebin is the Sheriff, he must have been pretending to be a reporter because of some mission or ability. That actually makes more sense to me."

"Sejin-hyung..."

"Sheriff just sounds like a good job. He probably tried to hide it because it's like a police role."

Kim Raebin was deeply moved.

However, Park Moondae was nonchalant.

"Yes. I'd also like it if this were correct... but if Chungwoo-hyung is the scholar, then based on the exchange record, the culprit is you."

"What...?"

"You, hyung."

"......"

3. Ryu Chungwoo <-> Bae Sejin

"Chungwoo-hyung's last exchange record is with Bae Sejin-hyung."

Bae Sejin's face turned pale.

Kim Raebin realized it too.

*Then if we assume Bae Sejin-hyung is the clown...*

Bae Sejin the clown was the first sacrifice, and he passed it to Ryu Chungwoo the scholar. Everything fit perfectly.

*Oh, my god.*

Just as he was debating whether to prepare to shoot, bewildered, someone raised their hand and shouted.

"That's strange!"

"...!"

It was Cha Eugene. He spoke clearly.

"Then Bae Sejin-hyung would have opposed Kim Raebin's story. Because he couldn't get caught."

"......"

"But Sejin-hyung was the first to say we should believe him. However, Moondae-hyung opposed it. So Moondae-hyung is more suspicious."

"......"

"Maybe Moondae-hyung exchanged cards in secret?"

"......"

*Ah!*

*Right, if they hid and exchanged cards secretly without telling anyone...*

Just as Kim Raebin was about to consider that, he realized the reality of the situation.

*...Huh?*

A small laugh was heard. It was Park Moondae.

"Impossible."

"......"

"As you all know, the front door is in the very center of the two dorms. This means it's impossible for people trying to exchange to approach without being seen."

That was right. The front door was right in the center. If two or more people approached it, they would be seen one way or another.

Turning to Cha Eugene, Park Moondae kindly added, "If you try to exchange there, you'll stand out. We were all watching whenever two or more people moved, weren't we?"

"...Umm."

On top of that, Park Moondae gave a crucial testimony.

"Besides, I never even went toward the front door."

"What...?"

"After the mid-check, I stayed in the dorm where I started. Sejin-hyung, you saw me, right?"

That was right. After the mid-check, everyone had checked their cards alone in their starting room to see if they had the Sacrificial Token.

[Bae Sejin, Park Moondae, Kim Raebin.]

These three woke up in the same dorm. Park Moondae had continued his search without leaving that place.

"...Um, why did you do that?"

"I was wondering if there was a way to get rid of the bathroom ghost."

Park Moondae smoothly explained that he wondered if his Mortician ability, which allows communication with the dead, could be used there.

"Since my job is Mortician, I wondered if I could talk to the ghost."

"Huh."

So the conclusion was this: Park Moondae had not approached the front door since the mid-check and had no coins.

Therefore...

*Moondae-hyungnim never even tried to make an exchange...!*

"In conclusion, circumstantially, I'm someone who couldn't have exchanged cards."

"......"

"With this, I believe everyone is convinced of my innocence."

It was only circumstantial evidence to begin with, anyway. Park Moondae added with a shrug.

*...I can't refute that.*

Kim Raebin glanced to the side. The person who had revealed his job as detective was standing there, his eyes wide as if in shock.

*This can't be.*

Kim Raebin hurriedly spoke to him, deliberately trying to offer encouragement.

"Uh, Ahyeon-hyung. Then I guess we should suspect the other people who tried to exchange..."

"......"

"......"

"It's possible. For Moondae too...!"

Seon Ahyeon muttered resolutely, his face pale.

Park Moondae asked back without wavering, "How?"

"......"

"You're not going to say something like he used a walkie-talkie, are you? The production team aren't fools, there's no way they'd put something that unfair into a deduction variety show."

Seon Ahyeon quietly shook his head and made his statement.

"Moondae... doesn't need to go to the entrance."

"...?"

"Only Moondae's card needs to go to the entrance...!"

"...Ah!!"

Bae Sejin let out a cry of realization. He understood what it meant.

Seon Ahyeon explained clearly, "The person trying to exchange just has to take Park Moondae's card to the entrance with them...!"

"......"

"Right! And while pretending to just pass by the entrance, they could use both cards by themselves to exchange them quickly!"

"Aaaah!"

The atmosphere heated up in an instant. Kim Raebin's face flushed as he gave a thumbs-up.

"That's exactly right!"

"Th-Thank you...!"

Seon Ahyeon, who had found the loophole that two people did not need to go in person, smiled faintly.

However, Park Moondae... nodded.

"...?"

"That could be possible."

*He's admitting it just like that?*

Of course, that was not the case.

"Then anyone could have done that."

"......"

"It seems like a method that's possible for anyone, not just me, as long as they hand their card over to the other person."

He was right.

"......"

For a moment, silence fell again.

Lee Sejin asked, holding back a sigh, "...Does anyone remember which members were loitering near the entrance?"

"......"

From here, it was once again a complete mystery, and, in fact, it would be the same even if someone's testimony came out. Anyone could give false testimony.

Everyone emotionally suspected Park Moondae was the culprit, but they had to admit there was nothing more to discuss. Park Moondae was very skilled at refutation.

*So that's how it is.*

And so Kim Raebin realized.

*When there's no physical evidence, I have to use the Sheriff's ability even more.*

Only then could he avoid the biggest potential risk during the culprit deduction: the annihilation of the other five members.

*I'll verify it once with the Sheriff's ability.*

Of course, the risk was huge. If he shot the wrong culprit, an innocent person could die, and in fact... in that case, the Sheriff would also die.

Kim Raebin remembered the next line of the rule explanation.

[If the Sheriff shoots the wrong 'culprit', that innocent person is immediately made a Burnt Offering.]

[And the Sheriff is also made a Burnt Offering along with them.]

In other words, the Sheriff's bullet was fired at the cost of his own life. Still, that would be better than pointing out the wrong culprit and having everyone die.

*Now is the time to try.*

Kim Raebin steeled his resolve. He raised his hand.

"...?"

"Kim Raebin?"

He shouted.

"The Sheriff shoots participant Park Moondae!"

"......"

At that moment, a dazzling light effect burst out from the giant coffin placed in the center.
