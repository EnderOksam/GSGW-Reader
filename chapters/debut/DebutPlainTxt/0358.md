# Chapter 358

At the end of May of this year, TeStar became a flawless group both at home and abroad.

So what did Mirynay's leader, Park Minha, think of TeStar since she was their direct junior?

*Well... I just think of them as my sunbaenims.*

To begin with, she hadn't seen much of them recently. This was because, even in the company, the labels were created in separate areas so their movement lines did not overlap.

But there was one thing.

*... I envy them! I envy them!*

TeStar was able to escape from the hands of the disgusting general manager. In fact, there were no role models that she was more envious of than them.

...And there was a certain sunbaenim who contributed greatly to her thinking that way.

*... The fact that he mentioned the label and announced it during the awards ceremony... He must have done that on purpose, right?*

Yes.

This was Park Moondae, the main vocalist of TeStar and the person in charge of being the 'Soft Bean Rice Cake and Apple Rice Cake Puppy'.

Park Minha screamed internally.

*What kind of puppy are they even talking about!*

She didn't know what he was thinking, but when she came to her senses he was in the right place at the right time... Anyway, what did he want?

"......"

*Thinking about it like this, he has the constitution of a heavenly idol.*

Anyway, it was scary.

Thanks to this, she almost broke out in a cold sweat trying to stop her own members who wanted to try to get along well with him because he was in a popular senior group.

*What if we all get in trouble because friendship with the opposite sex is the poison of idols?!*

She didn't know how such a scary person could look so harmless when the camera was rolling.

*... In reality, he seems a bit difficult to approach.*

With years of self-management and investment in stats (although Park Minha didn't know), Park Moondae was already engaged in his career that had a lot to do with cameras.

But even now, when she watched the videos he was in, it was scary how well the image of a friendly puppy fit him.

He was worthy of being a role model.

*... He's amazing. Really.*

So, she made her own resolutions.

*When the contract renewal season comes, we'll really do it right!*

She would protect this group by handling things clearly and never getting caught...!

However, as she was doing this for a while, she received a phone call from the person in question.

It was also very meaningful.

-Do you have any concerns these days?

"......"

The moment she heard those words, all kinds of possible bad endings came into Park Minha's mind.

What? What?

*W-what is he thinking...?*

So, like a reaction robot, an acceptable answer came out.

"I worry just as much as other people do! Still, I am satisfied with my life as an idol where I can meet fans like this..."

-Hmm. Yes.

Park Moondae listened to the whole story without stopping.

Thanks to this, the repertoire for social life answers was quickly depleted.

*Ahh!*

The pressure to say more increased.

*Oh, please!*

We aren't in a relationship where we have to discuss concerns, right, sunbaenim?

- Well then, I'm glad. But do you have any other concerns?

"...Well, when you say other concerns, what kind of things are you talking about...?"

-I was wondering if the company has been doing well recently.

"The company?"

-Yes. I don't know much about them recently because there has been some distance since TeStar became involved in an independent label.

Park Moondae described calmly.

-But it seems like they continue to run all groups centered around the United States. Since we are also active in that direction these days, what do you think about it as our hoobaenim?

"Ah."

If that was the case...

At that moment, Park Minha opened her mouth as if it were a conditioned reflex.

Because it was a problem she was really thinking about, and because there were a lot of things concerning her regarding that topic.

Even after speaking, she regretted it and thought, 'I was caught.' But it wasn't actually a trap. It was really a stepping stone to a good proposal for Mirynay.

Of course, it was true that Park Moondae caught her.

***

-... So, I am still working hard!

"Hmm."

I compiled information about Mirynay's current situation, which Park Minha explained in quite detail, and about the new group that I secretly obtained between the lines.

First of all, the current position was...

*It's ambiguous.*

It was stagnating.

She also had an idea, so instead of criticizing the company, she glossed it over, but she seemed to know that the momentum and controversy they had at the time of their debut had largely died.

It was understandable. As they continued to make music that would be popular overseas and focused their promotions on that, their connection with the domestic public would weaken.

*But the fandom is okay.*

Since their content was good and the quality of their activity capital was good, the inflow of the fandom itself was steady.

*It's patterned like that.*

In short, they had become fandom-type idols whose music had weakened slightly as public interest had declined, but whose album sales were still rising.

*At this level, they've settled down as successful idols anyway.*

However, if they managed to gain popularity just once, they could truly become a group that had it all, but the company would continue to target only the US, which would be upsetting.

Meanwhile, a new female idol came out from Youngrin's company.

And they hit the jackpot.

-That's right, there are a lot of people who are really good these days! We were thinking that we need to work harder too...

That was ultimately what this meant.

*They feel threatened because of their overlapping positions.*

A trendy female idol who was recognized globally, especially in Western countries.

Two similar images.

The problem was not only that their images overlapped in the American market, but also that the selling point in Korea was blurred.

And stronger latecomers would usually take their place naturally.

So maybe now was the right time, before the popularity moved towards the new female idols.

I crossed my arms with my smartphone between my neck.

*... It's okay.*

Mirynay had an established first-tier group image and strong album sales.

Rookie female idols, such as Powty, may have had a weak album currently, but their overseas music was strong, and based on their popularity, their domestic music sales were also on the rise.

Everyone had strengths and weaknesses.

*The game is perfect, so it's easier to plan.*

There was nothing more fun to watch in a fight than seeing similar positions being traded back and forth.

I came to a conclusion. But there was one more hurdle left to go through.

*Because it's someone else's business.*

The intention of the parties concerned.

"Yes. Thank you for telling me. And I have one more question."

-...

*It means being criticized.*

Of course, the lower the popularity, the less criticism there was.

Speech, hand gestures, plastic surgery, past photos... The question was whether you would rather have a stable current situation than feel the pain of having your brain beaten by absurd rumors.

'For those who have experienced

, it's not strange even if there is trauma.'

-Of course.

Good. She had guts.

Like this.

Because rivalries were good for motivation.
