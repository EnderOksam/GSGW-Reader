# Chapter 358

By the end of May, TeSTAR had undoubtedly become a top-tier idol group both domestically and internationally.

So what did Mirynay's leader, Park Minha, think of TeSTAR as their immediate junior?

*Well... I just think of them as my sunbaenims.*

To begin with, she hadn't seen much of them lately. Even within the company, the two groups worked in separate areas, so there were few opportunities for them to run into each other.

But there was one thing.

*...I envy them! I envy them!*

TeSTAR had managed to escape the clutches of the disgusting general manager. In fact, there was no group she envied more or considered a better role model.

...And there was a certain sunbaenim who contributed greatly to her thinking that way.

*...The fact that he mentioned and announced the label at the awards ceremony... He must have done that on purpose, right?*

Yes.

This was Park Moondae, TeSTAR's main vocalist, also known as the 'Soft Bean Rice Cake and Apple Rice Cake Puppy.'

Park Minha screamed internally.

*Puppy, my ass!*

She didn't know what he was thinking, but he always seemed to appear in the right place at exactly the right time... Anyway, what did he want?

"......"

Thinking about it that way, he really was the perfect idol.

Anyway, it was scary.

Thanks to this, she always broke out in a cold sweat whenever she had to stop her members from trying to become friendly with him.

*What if he caused trouble for our group because friendships with members of the opposite sex could threaten an idol's career?!*

She didn't know how such a scary person could look so harmless when the camera was rolling.

*...In reality, he seems a bit difficult to approach.*

Through years of self-development and careful distribution of stats, Park Moondae had already become a flawless celebrity. But even now, when she watched the videos he was in, it gave her chills how perfectly the image of a friendly puppy still suited him.

He was worthy of being a role model.

*...He's amazing. Really.*

So, she made her own resolutions.

*When contract renewal season comes, we'll make sure we do things right!*

She would protect the group and make sure they wouldn't lose out in any way.

However, as she was doing this for a while, she received a phone call from the person in question.

It was also very meaningful.

– Do you have any concerns these days?

"......"

The moment she heard those words, all sorts of disastrous outcomes came to Park Minha's mind.

What? What?

*W-what is he thinking...?*

So, like a reaction robot, an acceptable answer came out.

"We have all the usual concerns. Still, I'm very happy that I can work as an idol and bring happiness to my fans..."

– Hmm. Yes.

Park Moondae listened to the whole story without stopping.

As a result, she quickly ran out of safe things to say.

*Ahh!*

The pressure to say more increased.

*Oh, please!*

Come on, please! You're not my therapist, sunbaenim!

Fortunately, Park Moondae gave Park Minha a hint just before he ended the conversation.

– Well then, I'm glad. I'm wondering if there's something more specific bothering you.

"...Well, when you say other concerns, what kind of things are you talking about...?"

– How are things going with the agency these days?

"The agency?"

– Yes. I don't know much about them recently because there has been some distance since TeSTAR became involved in an independent label.

Park Moondae described calmly.

– But it seems like they continue to run all groups centered around the United States. We're also targeting the American market these days, so I was wondering what you thought about it.

"Ah."

If that was the case...

At that moment, Park Minha opened her mouth as if it were a conditioned reflex.

It was something she had genuinely been concerned about for quite some time. Even after speaking, she regretted it and thought, 'I was caught.' But it wasn't actually a trap. In reality, he was about to bring her group an offer that was almost too good to be true.

Of course, it was true that Park Moondae caught her.

---

– ... So, I am still working hard!

"Hmm."

I compiled information about Mirynay's current situation, which Park Minha explained in quite detail, and about the new group that I secretly obtained between the lines.

First of all, the current position was...

*It's ambiguous.*

Mirynay had hit a plateau.

She also had an idea, so instead of criticizing the company, she glossed it over, but she seemed aware that most of the popularity and momentum surrounding their debut had already died out.

It was understandable. As they continued to make music that would be popular overseas and focused their promotions on that, their connection with the Korean public had grown weak.

*But the fandom is okay.*

Because they produced high-quality content and had plenty of financial backing, the inflow of the fandom itself was steady.

*They've settled into a pattern.*

In short, they had become fandom-type idols whose digital sales had weakened as public interest declined, while their physical album sales continued to rise because of their strong fandom.

I guess you could say that they had successfully carved out a spot for themselves.

However, if they managed to gain popularity just once, they could truly become a group that had it all, but the company would continue to target only the US, which would be upsetting.

Meanwhile, Youngrin's agency had debuted a new female idol group.

And the group was experiencing massive success.

– That's right, there are a lot of people who are really good these days! We were thinking that we need to work harder too...

That was ultimately what this meant.

*They feel threatened because of their overlapping positions.*

They were both trendy female idol groups with global reach, especially in the West.

Two similar images.

The problem wasn't limited to the American market; their similarity also made Mirynay less distinctive in Korea.

Newer groups usually made a stronger impression and eventually took their predecessors' place.

In a sense, this was the perfect time to act before the new group took all the buzz for themselves.

I crossed my arms with my smartphone between my neck.

*... It's okay.*

Mirynay had an established first-tier group image and strong album sales.

Powty, the new group, was stronger in terms of overseas digital streaming, even if their album sales weren't as high.

Everyone had strengths and weaknesses.

This would make strategizing even easier, since the two groups were so evenly matched. Few things were more fun than watching similar opponents struggle against one another.

I came to a conclusion. But there was one more hurdle left to go through.

*Because it's someone else's business.*

The intention of the parties concerned. I don't have the final say in this. Mirynay did.

I waited, agreeing with what Park Minha said, and when it was over, I opened my mouth again.

"Yes. Thank you for telling me. And I have one more question."

– Yes?

I asked slowly.

"Would you prefer that more people take an interest in your group, even if things get a little... noisy?"

– ...

She knew what it meant when things got "a little noisy."

I'm saying that people will criticize your group.

Of course, the lower the popularity, the less criticism there was.

Speech, hand gestures, plastic surgery, past photos... I was asking whether she preferred the stability of the status quo or being bothered by all sorts of silly rumors.

She might have been a little traumatized by the idea of public scrutiny.

I waited.

But after a while, a firm voice came from the phone.

– Of course.

Good. She had guts.

I passed the conclusion that I was satisfied with.

"How would you feel about the agency doing this?"

– Uh, how?

Like this.

The very next day, celebrity news articles began pouring out in the dozens.

Powty defeated Mirynay the moment they debuted!

The article was filled with content that subtly predicted Powty's superiority.

And of course, noises began to come out in response.

All kinds of extreme clamor.

★$
– Fuck, get out of my head

However, there was one thing in common.

Everyone naturally assumed that Youngrin's agency was responsible for these cookie-cutter promotional articles.

Targeting an older group with a similar concept through the press had long been an effective way to raise one's own reputation.

Of course, unless Youngrin was an idiot, she would have been planning to issue a similar press release, and it was confirmed that this was indeed the case.

An employee from our label contacted the press and was told that Youngrin's agency had already promised them promotional material.

Thanks to them, it was comfortable.

– It's not bad? It would be perfect if we just changed the subject.

The employee said that in a slightly excited voice.

So... we're helping them.

I nodded.

We were essentially donating to them.

Didn't I play a role in helping? It was something the company should be thankful for.

No doubt they had intended to target TeSTAR rather than Mirynay, but that was none of my business.

I coolly lowered the smartphone screen.

All sorts of press agencies were running articles with the same information, and it was making quite a buzz.

"Good buzz."

Mirynay's fans would probably be stressed out, but it was better to talk about a hot topic rather than something that was mentioned as dead and stagnant.

Rivalry tended to be a good source of motivation for everyone involved.

*Even Mirynay is making a comeback soon.*

I hoped this would lead to even more buzz domestically.

– Yes. I like the song!

She seemed confident.

I laughed and threw the smartphone.

Now all I had to do was wait until Mirynay made a comeback and the results came out.

*When the time comes, the company will put out a press release about our record... I should have the mission results in two weeks at the earliest.*

If nothing went wrong, this most recent hurdle would be a thing of the past.

*Neatly done.*

I stood up, stretching lightly.

"Ah."

There was one more thing to take care of. Until the results came out, TeSTAR had to remain visually separate from Mirynay in every way.

To help with that... We needed an independent activity that could generate buzz while making it clear that we were doing our own thing.

A popular variety show, obviously.

Coincidentally, the production team of the variety show that had made us sell hotteok in America contacted us again.

Perfect timing.

"Hello~"

"Hello!"

"Oh my, TeSTAR, please sit down quickly! Oh my!"

A few days later, we were invited to a meeting about the new variety show.

"First, here's the name..."

When I looked at the presentation materials, even the logo was already drawn out.

★$
@c@#*⟨The House's Grandson⟩*#@c@
$★

The title alone gave me a hint about the show.

*It's going to be random as hell.*

It was obvious that they were going to throw us into some unconventional situations.

Several familiar writers explained this with excited faces.

"You'll head down there, do some cooking, grow some vegetables... You know, something peaceful and relaxing."

"Ahaha. Yes!"

Of course, it was probably a trap. The members looked at each other and nodded their heads.

*What is this really going to be?*

*I think it's going to be some kind of boot camp.*

*That also makes sense.*

I wondered if we'd be forced into some ridiculous situation in the countryside again, like having to make money by working on a fishing boat.

But the writers seemed very sure of themselves.

"Ah, this time! We're definitely not lying! You're going to have some fun with mini-games... In any case, it's not anything you need to be nervous about."

Okay. Let's pretend to be interested.

"Really?"

"Yes!"

One of the writers said emphatically. We tried to smile and nod, but before we could, the writer added something.

The writer added, eyes burning with indignation.

"You can sue us if we lie, really!"

"......"

Sue you?

An embarrassed smile appeared on Ryu Chungwoo's face. The other guys had similar expressions.

*Hey, I think they're serious.*

Were they really trying to make a relaxing rural program that would help tired modern workers unwind?

"Since you're idols, we can incorporate aspects of your profession and give the show more meaning! We can also have you perform for the people living nearby..."

Kim Raebin was amazed.

"...! I think that would be a really meaningful opportunity!"

"Hm, sure. It has a good purpose."

Bae Sejin looked a little relieved as he went through the material again, and Seon Ahyeon was writing something down.

Everyone was in a peaceful mood.

"...Yes."

And I looked back at Lee Sejin.

He was doing his best to maintain a cheerful smile, but the moment our eyes met, the corners of his eyes trembled.

*Okay.*

There was going to be a problem.

*This sounds like pure healing.*

The production team had completely lost its touch when it came to variety shows.
