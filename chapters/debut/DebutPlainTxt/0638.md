# Chapter 638

Keundal, Park Moondae, had gotten into Orbit Stars Entertainment. In other words, our company.

[Actually, I've been preparing for a while! But since this was my first time applying, I thought I might get rejected...]

He rambled, but I understood. He wanted to succeed on his own, without my help, and only tell me after he got in.

[But I got in! I really got in!]

And now he was thrilled with his success.

His loud voice made the others notice, and they started chiming in.

"Wait a minute, Gunwoo is joining our company?"

"Ohhh! BM, congratulations!"

[Huh?? Ah, hello. Thank you!]

The dance practice room grew noisy. If this continued, he would smoothly land a cushy position as a parachute hire. No, that was not important.

I remembered something important he had said a few months ago, after his rescue from the mountain.

[I'm not sure what I should do, and I don't really... know what I want to do.]

I had told him we should figure it out together. Since then, I had steadily heard about him trying various things to live his own life. We even met up sometimes when I had a day off.

[Um... Hyung, what kind of work does an administrative position in a private company involve? I mean, I'm also curious about how it is at your company.]

He had occasionally asked for advice like that. It seemed he was planning to apply to our company all along.

*He must have been trying to achieve the goal he set for himself without relying on me too much.*

He asked for my advice but not my consent. The fact that he did this on his own was a very positive sign.

*Thinking about it that way, I'm actually a little proud of him.*

I smiled faintly and spoke into the smartphone.

"Then I'll be seeing you at the company soon."

[Yes!]

"Which team are you joining?"

I considered the teams currently in charge of TeSTAR.

*Planning would be fine, and so would Management Support.*

He was probably decent at English, so Global Marketing would be fine too. He had experience with paperwork and public-facing work. Management tended to hire younger people, but a transfer could be arranged if we put in a good word.

[Rookie Development!]

"......"

*Not a team in charge of TeSTAR?*

"Rookies?!"

"Aww~ You're not going to work with us?"

The members' voices made the man on the other end of the call jump.

[Aaah. Yes, well, I did think about that, but...]

His voice grew smaller, as if he was avoiding eye contact.

[It felt like... too much of a personal bias.]

"......"

*No.*

[What if they did a reference check and found out I'm a TeSTAR fan! I could get fired...]

"What are you talking about? You've even been a manager-for-a-day."

[Ah.]

His voice on the phone stopped. He perhaps recalled what he did when we switched bodies, but then he suddenly blurted out.

[Th-That was something you did, Hyung...!]

"Even if I did it, the record is under Ryu Gunwoo's name. Did you forget you're Chungwoo-hyung's relative?"

[Huh...?]

A dumbfounded sound came through the phone.

*Good grief.*

I pressed my brow and then chuckled softly.

*Whatever.*

*As long as he got in, that's all that matters.*

No, it was actually a good choice. For management, age is a huge factor. It is hard for someone over 30 to get hired as a rookie without a personal recommendation. He might have insisted on getting in fair and square, only to get cut at the document screening and say, 'I guess this path isn't for me.'

As for the Planning Team, his commerce background was not impossible, but I was not sure if his civil servant career would help his portfolio. The A&R team was full of specialists, so that was out.

In the end, applying for Rookie Development was a good move. It was a unique position in a company that did not have any rookies yet. They likely hired people from various professional backgrounds for this role. This department would usually be subdivided into management, casting, and planning, but it seemed they had not gotten that far yet.

*Since it's a relatively new company, they probably wanted to build it in a fresh and unique way.*

A new agency with a lot of money but no fixed artist identity might look at factors besides industry experience. A former Grade 7 civil servant? They would want to interview him out of sheer curiosity.

So.

"Still, you applied well, playing to your strengths."

[R-Really?]

"Yeah. That's why you got in. Congratulations."

"......"

I rubbed my chin as I listened to his bright voice.

*Besides, it's better if he doesn't get too mixed up with TeSTAR.*

He was finally doing the work he wanted to do. If he ended up working with us, it would be difficult for him to open up if he had a hard time. Turning TeSTAR content, which he enjoyed as a hobby, into his job did not seem desirable, especially for someone just starting to build his own life.

"Yeah, getting in is the amazing part!"

"Congratulations, really!"

[Th-Thank you.]

Amid the congratulations, Ryu Chungwoo added gently.

"So you'll be raising our rival group, then."

[...Pardon?]

"Gasp, that's right~ Wow, so later you might show up with the rookies and say, 'Our kids have to debut this quarter, so TeSTAR's comeback? Postpone it!'?"

[No! That's not it!]

"Hahaha! It's a joke!"

Laughter filled the practice room. I saw one of the guys looked relieved that the break was extended, but I pretended not to see. In this heartwarming atmosphere, we all greeted the new employee.

"Um, when we see you at the company from now on... I hope we can say hi and talk...!"

"That's right~ We're not strangers!"

[Th-Thank you...]

Just like the fan who had applied for a fansign, Keundal seemed moved by TeSTAR's reaction. What a funny guy. I chuckled as I watched, then suddenly remembered one thing.

*This guy is currently employed.*

"What about your civil servant job?"

[I have to resign!]

"......"

He had studied his ass off for that position, under a status effect that would have killed him if he did not pass. It came with the amazing benefit of a self-development leave, an iron rice bowl he could never be fired from, and a fixed work location in Seoul.

To quit being a Grade 7 civil servant for Seoul City and join a small entertainment agency...

*If he's happy, that's all that matters.*

He was living his own life happily. He had money, so he would be fine. If worse came to worst, I could even get him a house.

I nodded without any unnecessary comments.

"Okay. Wrap things up well."

[Yep!]

He answered excitedly, then seemed to realize he was taking up our time and hurriedly said his goodbyes.

[Then, if we happen to run into each other at the company... Oh, right!]

Hm?

He suddenly hesitated and lowered his voice.

[Hyung, so... um, I got into the company, and, since it's a good thing.]

"Ah, is there something you want as a celebration gift?"

[......]

*Oh.*

I thought he would scream that he did not want anything, but it seemed he surprisingly did. I spoke coolly to the silent man. Considering his personality, he probably just wanted to have a meal together.

"Just say it. What is it?"

[Then, I'll...]

His voice became extremely quiet.

[Th-The tickets for this TeSTAR concert.]

"......"

[I... failed at ticketing. I was preparing for my application and interview, so I couldn't even grab a canceled ticket...*Sob*.]

His voice was desperate.

"...I'll send them to you."

[Uwaaah, thank you!]

I decided to give him my allocated invitation tickets. As Keundal's voice chanted thanks and cheers in the background, the members stared at my face with warm expressions.

"...?"

"No reason. It's just nice to see."

Seon Ahyeon smiled and spoke.

"Moondae, giving away invitation tickets... I think this is the first time I've seen it."

Ah.

*Usually, people give them to their families.*

That was probably right. The other members also usually gave them to their families, so their parents, siblings, and sometimes even grandparents would come to the waiting room after the concert. I just... well, I did not really have anyone to give them to, so I would either return them to the company or pass them on to a staff member.

It made me look at my smartphone in a new light.

*I wonder how many years into my debut it's been since I've used these.*

"Should I send you four?"

[Huh?? No?! One is enough for me...]

"Right."

*Sending two should be enough.*

[Thank you. Thank you, Hyung!]

"It's nothing."

*I should send them to him whenever I get the chance in the future.*

I ended the call.

"Moondae, you look happy."

"......"

Ryu Chungwoo patted my back and returned to the center of the practice room.

*What a bunch of funny guys.*

"Alright, thanks to Moondae Moondae, we got a lo~ng break, right? Back to formation!"

"Yeah..."

We followed and got into position. As the concert's opening music played, the scene naturally formed in my mind.

*Maybe it's because we were just talking about concert tickets.*

"Moondae, left foot forward!"

I quickly corrected my position.

"That's it!"

As I followed the intense dance break, a thought suddenly occurred to me.

*Even after sending two tickets to Keundal, I still have two left.*

"......"

*Normally, I would have just returned them to the company...*

During the next break, I hesitated for a moment before sending a text with a picture of the concert ticket attached.

[Come if you want.]

The hot summer passed, and the autumn of harvest arrived. TeSTAR finally began their concert, the harvest of this promotion period.

~~~

A few days before the concert.

"Th-This is insane."

*It's a real ticket!*

The all-fan trembled, clutching the TeSTAR concert ticket that had arrived by mail. Surprisingly, she had received it from an SNS friend. That day, the SNS friend, who had briefly set her account to private, had posted.

[Is there anyone who still hasn't gotten a ticket for the TeSTAR concert..?ㅠㅠ]

She thought it was a call to lament together. The person had seemed busy with real life and had not been on SNS much.

*She did say she failed at ticketing...*

She must have gone private to vent. Thinking that, she also tearfully left a comment.

[Me... me..ㅠㅠ]

*Let's lament together, get stronger, shake it off, and then watch the online live stream!*

That was the kind of conversation she expected, but suddenly, a DM arrived.

[Um, Soft Star. I ended up with an extra ticket... so if you're free for the first concert on Friday, I'll send it to you!]

*This is crazy.*

The TeSTAR all-fan held her breath and furiously typed on her smartphone. Her fingers trembled, but she was so heightened that she made no typos.

[Huuuhhh thank you so much I don't know what to say is this real? Where should I send the money?]

[Haha, you don't have to send any money.]

"......"

[It's an invitation ticket!]

And an invitation ticket really did arrive.

*I even checked the ID several times, wondering if it was a scam to get my address...*

She felt sorry for doubting. Her SNS fan-friend had given her such a gift. She could have posted it publicly and received countless pleas.

*Really, I really want to give her something...*

*Maybe she'll be in the seat next to me?*

She wanted to work up the courage to talk to her and maybe have a late-night snack after the concert. Or at least give her some snacks.

*I should bring some!*

However, on the day of the concert, she found her seat and stood there blankly.

"The, the seat..."

It was a seat on the first floor, near the central console. The location was really good, just as she had thought when she checked the ticket.

However.

*They're not holding light sticks or slogans...?*

The people around her seemed ready to enjoy the concert, but they had a strange 'muggle' feel, and what's more...

*The seat next to me...*

Next to her sat a tall man wearing a cap pulled down low. A quick glance at his shadowed jawline revealed he was handsome, almost like a celebrity.

The all-fan sat down and stared intently at the empty stage, wanting to squeeze her eyes shut. She could not bring herself to turn her head.

*Th-This is the guest section! Here!*

She was happy, but it was so overwhelming that her heart felt like it would explode.

*Where are you, Big-Moon!*

*Where on earth did you get this ticket!*

For reference, the man in the next seat, Ryu Gunwoo, was in a similar situation.

*She was a high school student!*

That was right. The TeSTAR all-fan who became a fan after watching ❬Magical Boy❭, the SNS friend who received Keundal's spare ticket, was a high school student. If you did the math, she was in elementary school back then.

*❬Idol Inc.❭ was rated 15+?!*

...And so, amidst their internal screams, they waited for the concert to start. It would be a tremendous festival that would blow away all these stray thoughts.
