# Chapter 642

The artists performed a new song, moving to the very front of the audience. This emotional performance marked a turning point. The concert's second half rapidly transitioned to TeSTAR's latest songs, the energy building like a strengthening current.

[Let me spin the wheel

So I can visit your dreams]

The members sang an arranged version of ❬Magical Boy❭, the beautiful song Park Moondae performed alone at the last concert. This continued the moving atmosphere. Then, the music switched to ❬Call Me❭, aggressively ramping up the tempo.

Next came ❬Promise❭, a song with a bright, refreshing melody but an incredibly flashy performance that used water.

Their hit songs poured out one after another without stopping.

The intense and fierce performance of the powerful ❬Haetae (TL/N: mystical beast of justice and righteousness) ❭ concept was followed by the captivatingly beautiful instrumentals and vocals of ❬Black Hole❭.

[Black Hole

Let me swallow it]

During the final chorus, Kim Raebin swept his hair back. Cheers and light sticks flooded the venue for his slightly husky vocals.

TeSTAR had paid close attention to the song arrangement for the concert's latter half.

It was a masterful arrangement. The setlist alternated between emotional, famous tracks and performance-heavy songs that made the audience scream, ensuring no one would collapse from exhaustion or lose focus.

*I love this so much...*

No, perhaps the all-fan thought the very flow of TeSTAR's entire career was like this. The songs, arranged chronologically, now presented such an exquisite tempo.

[In the pose!

Don't be too precious with today

What's important is right now, Right now]

The activities TeSTAR had undertaken unfolded on stage like a highlight reel. The setlist moved from the mainstream American high-teen concept of ❬Pose❭ to the highly conceptual ❬Hi-Five❭. After the easy-listening ❬Magical Boy❭ set the mood again...

[Bad feelings

follows you til the end]

A remix of ❬Bad Feelings❭, their opening song, finally appeared. It was the performance piece with the secret agent concept that had taken this year by storm. Dressed in perfectly fitted professional suits, TeSTAR delivered a sharp, synchronized performance.

This was their present form.

TeSTAR had finally arrived back at the present.

*This must be... the last one.*

"......"

It wasn't supposed to be an emotional moment, but the all-fan felt a lump in her throat and swallowed hard. She felt happy, proud, and grateful, but it was also such a shame.

[This was our last song. Thank you.]

She knew those words were coming.

Huhhh?

Aaaaaah!

Gasps of disappointment and surprise erupted from all corners of the audience. However, everyone must have had a rough idea. TeSTAR had already poured their heart and soul into performing every song from their career, right up to their current promotional track.

The stage darkened, and the members of TeSTAR disappeared with a nod.

Of course, like any concert, there would be an encore. For some reason, however, it felt like time had flown by too quickly.

*I don't even have a ticket for tomorrow...*

Hoping this moment would last just a little longer, she sat in her seat with a slightly empty heart, even while shouting "Encore!"

[That's right!]

The stage lit up dramatically with bright fireworks and stage effects. As expected, the silhouettes of seven members reappeared on stage.

She had expected that much.

However, TeSTAR stood in a triangle formation, wearing their old uniforms from a long, long time ago.

"......"

With Park Moondae winking at the center, the choreography began.

[Me, standing on the stage

You probably don't know yet

The STAR LIGHT churning inside me]

❬Idol Inc.❭.

Their perfectly synchronized movements showed none of the tension or stiffness of a first performance. Instead, they moved with relaxed confidence and plenty of fan service. The performance brought back sentimental memories of that time.

[The one shining on stage today is...

It's Me!]

"Woahhh!"

Random cheers, gasps, and something resembling a sing-along overflowed for a song that didn't even have an official fanchant. Recalling the survival program that had swept through Korea a few years ago, the audience enjoyed the surprise amidst a strange and nostalgic delight.

"It's Me!"

The moment the music ended, the members of TeSTAR looked embarrassed and hung their heads.

[I really didn't think we'd do this again.]

[Still, since these are our roots, we all agreed we should show it to you once... Aigoo.]

Hahahaha!

Bursts of laughter erupted from the audience. TeSTAR laughed awkwardly, dusting off their heads and clothes. It was the start of an unexpectedly humorous encore.

Forgetting her melancholy for a moment, the all-fan laughed cheerfully and waved her light stick. TeSTAR also smiled and ran around the extended stage.

[Is there anything else you want to hear? We'll do it all for you, everything!]

[Let's see if we missed anything!]

Laughter and comments poured out from everywhere at those words. Even though all the famous songs had passed, everyone had their own personal favorites that they remembered.

[The Tiger-Hunting Officer solo songs? Wait, for the encore... all seven songs?]

[Indeed... We will attempt the challenge!]

[Raebin, no!]

[That's not it.]

[The subways will stop running! Loviewers won't be able to get home!]

As the members frantically stopped a grimly determined Kim Raebin, laughter erupted from the audience again. They started teasingly shouting for TeSTAR to actually do it.

[But.]

But?

[What if we told you... we knew this would happen and prepared a medley?]

...!!

[Here we go. The Tiger-Hunting Officer medley.]

Amidst tearing cheers and screams, the solo songs followed one another like teasers, accompanied by various traditional instruments. The soloists used the other members as makeshift backup dancers and completely filled the stage, unleashing an insane amount of fan service and live energy.

A hastily thrown-on Daoist robe, a traditional overcoat, fluttered from their shoulders.

The fun, playful stage was truly worthy of an encore.

[Shall we check out some cover songs too?]

"Aaaah! Yes!"

"......"

The all-fan was no longer surprised that the person next to her was screaming along with her.

Just like that, TeSTAR had a great time playing with their fans all throughout the encore.

[Whoa, it's amazing!

Everything about you

Makes my heart pound every day!

I can't sleep again today]

Various cover songs that units had performed popped up here and there with laughter, regardless of the original members. Despite having run through an insanely intense, performance-focused set for a long time, the members of TeSTAR flew around the stage without a single sign of exhaustion.

It was gratifying to see them looking so happy.

*Thank you.*

The all-fan took a deep breath. Her heart felt warm.

[Thank you!]

With that, TeSTAR finished their fun encore, and this time, the stage lights truly went out.

"......"

The all-fan quietly watched the scene. As always, the credits rolled with the names of the concert staff and behind-the-scenes practice videos, signaling a lingering end.

After all of that was over.

"......"

As the all-fan debated whether to get up, the lights turned on again before her eyes.

*Huh?*

On the darkened concert stage, a large sign flickered. A warm light came on as the surrounding gears slowly began to move. Then, the attic from the opening appeared on the screen again.

Seon Ahyeon was alone in the attic, tidying up the board games with a gentle and delicate touch.

A narration was heard.

[Do you know what 'epilogue' means?]

[It means 'a story that comes after.']

In the warm colors of the screen, Seon Ahyeon put the board games back in their places and read a graduation album. The hand that touched the attached sticky note looked soft and full of longing.

[Even after a story ends... you're still left with questions, aren't you? Like if the people in the story are living well afterward.]

[The epilogue is for answering those questions.]

[It tells us that even if one story ends, we continue to live on beyond it.]

"......"

Plink.

*Huh.*

Feeling something tap her head, the all-fan looked up.

It was raining.

[We can always meet again in the epilogue.]

The narration echoed.

On the screen, Seon Ahyeon finished organizing the graduation album. He peeled off a sticky note and stood up. He walked over to an old-fashioned telephone in the corner of the attic and dialed the number written on the note.

It was a call to those friends he had enjoyed playing games with before.

[Should we meet up?]

The screen gently faded to black.

Pitter-patter.

In the silence, only the sound of a soft, dense shower falling on the midnight outdoor concert venue could be heard.

In that silence, a single voice rang out over the empty stage.

[Everyone.]

[You can only say you've been to a TeSTAR concert if you stay for the double encore.]

"......"

[Ta-da!]

The stage flooded with a blinding light. With the triumphant sound of drums, images poured onto the screen. Countless choreography videos, stage performances, and practice room photos that TeSTAR had released throughout their career appeared, as if rewinding time.

Scenes from their past album promotions flashed by.

*Ah.*

The all-fan suddenly felt like she understood the theme TeSTAR was trying to convey with the concert.

If an album is an idol's story, then a concert, a performance, is the epilogue. At a TeSTAR performance, you could open any album and look through it together, no matter if that album, that story, was several years old.

Right now, she was participating in that epilogue.

"......"

Perhaps someone on the outside might think TeSTAR chose 'the trajectory of our group's activities' as a theme simply because it was a good topic to use at the peak of their success.

However...

*No.*

The all-fan thought that TeSTAR just... wanted to tell this story. To say that they could meet anytime.

Everyone in that venue had the same thought.

The screen ended with a single phrase.

[Yeah, let's meet!]

Amidst the cheers, the drums faded, and a new melody began to ring out. It was a pleasant instrumental that sounded good no matter when you heard it, as if made by picking only the most cheerful and fun instruments.

And a voice.

[I feel so good today, it's like

Something good is about to happen]

TeSTAR's first fan song. That pleasant melody filled the entire concert venue.

[With you, everything will be okay

It's unfamiliar, but it'll be good somehow]

The back of the stage opened again. Just like in the choreography video from before, tall figures in animal onesies appeared from within the dry ice.

Bae Sejin, in a hamster onesie in the center, grinned coolly. He wasn't embarrassed anymore.

[Reality shines like a dream

And in my dreams, I dream of a wonderful tomorrow

When I meet you tomorrow

I'll only tell you of sparkling dreams]

As Cha Eugene, in a white tiger onesie, sang while holding his tail, the guys who had been cautiously walking onto the stage with smiles started dancing and running. They moved as if they didn't care if they slipped in the sudden shower.

The familiar melody, both moving and nostalgic, filled the venue.

[My unsinking dreams

Make me kick off from reality and run]

Lee Sejin, waving both hands of his onesie, appeared on the screen. Behind him, Ryu Chungwoo, wearing an eagle onesie like during their debut, burst into laughter in a black baby bear onesie.

[Reality is breathing, Yeh

YES breathing (breathing!)

Hoo-ha! Hoo-ha!]

Seon Ahyeon, no longer embarrassed, smiled and sang his rap part. A white dog popped out from behind the deer onesie.

[I'll run breathlessly to you

Will you smile when you see me?]

Park Moondae's smiling face appeared on the screen. His expression, in a puppy animal onesie, was no different from the one from his early debut that had just flashed across the screen. It was still there.

[Even if nothing lasts forever

The magic of this moment is]

The idols in animal onesies spread their arms wide, as if to embrace everyone.

[Maybe it's you]

The venue roared in response.

[I feel so good today, it's like

Something good is about to happen]

They all sang the returning chorus together at the top of their lungs. The falling raindrops sparkled like tiny lamps in the light of the stage lighting and light sticks. All over the stage, devices released colorful balloons that seemed to fill the sky.

Colorful images ran around the venue.

[Life is strange

I kick off the ground lightly

And try walking on the sky

Even if it rains, I don't know

My life is-]

"BRIGHT!"

The word they all shouted together echoed through the venue.

Kim Raebin grinned.

[The pouring light

All right, you're right

I'm shining today]

The song raced towards its end. The smiling members came to the very front of the extended stage and sat down. Facing the shower of light and the palms of their hands, they held their mics with smiling faces.

[Meeting you yesterday

Became today's shining dream

And tomorrow's magic]

The last verse was sung with sincerity.

[Yeah, the magic is you]

There was light everywhere.

Flower confetti exploded. Amidst the cheers and laughter, TeSTAR sat on the extended stage for a very long time, waving their hands.

It was joyful, fulfilling, and overwhelming.

"Wow!"

The TeSTAR fan laughed out loud.

She was happy.
