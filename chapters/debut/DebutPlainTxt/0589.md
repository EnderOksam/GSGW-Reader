# Chapter 589

"I believe there's a clear distinction between a song that stands alone and one that's part of a larger narrative. An OST is the latter."

Nod, nod.

"That's why last night, I prepared by rewatching all of ❬Doll Hunter❭ Season 1 in the 'Room of Dreams and Hopes'! I want to make it clear I didn't waste a single moment of my break!"

I stared blankly at Kim Raebin. He had only managed a single bite while delivering that speech, while everyone else polished off their bowls of rice with pork belly kimchi stew and rolled omelets.

"Raebin."

"Yes?"

"You're happy right now."

"Yes!"

Then that was all that mattered.

He had been released.

After dramatically escaping the hellish 'staycation' room by winning last night's game, Kim Raebin became the very incarnation of passion and a workaholic. It seemed that being forcibly separated from composing had let his body and mind rest, but now he looked overcharged, as if he had been doping.

Just look at the glint in his eyes.

"I can say without a doubt that I'm fully prepared to begin work at this very moment..."

"I know, but eat first."

"Yes, sir!"

Right. Eating was what mattered most. Besides, did he have any idea what I went through to get this meal?

[Again.]

[Aaargh! Park Moondae's eleventh attempt!]

[Ohhhhh!!! You can win!]

I won this cooking duty by redrawing lots five times this morning, even memorizing nursery rhymes with matching dance moves.

I had to get my money's worth.

"The person who feeds you is your guardian. You're worthy of my filial piety."

"Alright."

After the night two days ago, when Bae Sejin and I pushed for that radical meeting, the production team fortunately did not go back on their word. They immediately began to improve TeSTAR's environment the very next day.

It was fun to see them naturally integrate the better treatment into the show's narrative, making it seem as if TeSTAR had earned it all.

If they had made it obvious, I was planning to schedule another meeting to tell them to get their act together.

*Looks like a second meeting won't be necessary.*

If it had come to that, I would have cut ties.

Anyway, after excitedly helping with the dishes, Kim Raebin pulled out his beloved laptop. He tapped his foot impatiently and sat down in the workroom.

"Then... shall we begin?"

"Yeah."

It was the last and main task given to us in this eventful variety show.

[It's composing the OST for ❬Doll Hunter❭ Season 2!]

After putting on a nonchalant act—the 'Oh my god, we never saw this coming' and 'TeSTAR, once again shocked' kind of reaction—we finally sat down as a complete group with Kim Raebin to discuss the song.

*This is probably where we're meant to show our systematic composing process. We'll appeal to the audience with our professional side and offer a different kind of entertainment, I suppose.*

Well, if I were to put it into words, the steps were simple.

[Step 1. Research.]

"First, should we look at the BGM or OSTs used in the last season?"

"Yes!"

We thoroughly picked apart the tracklist for ❬Doll Hunter❭ Season 1. Most of the BGM was intense, full of heavy beats, or designed to build tension. Even in climactically heightened scenes, a tragic minor-key melody would suddenly drop in.

*They're just hammering it in hard, no matter what.*

"I see. It seems there's more BGM for impact rather than for calmly setting the mood."

"Mmm~ ❬Doll Hunter❭ originally focused on keywords like disaster and survival... That's why it was fun!"

Right. You could tell they bet everything on making viewers lose their minds as they clicked to watch the next episode. The drama producers' request for the OST was in the same vein.

"They said they'd like it to have a 'Holy crap, it's Jeong Isol!!' kind of feeling."

"Oh."

In short, it could be summarized as, 'Gentlemen, please just make it similar to the existing vibe.' This meant they were not being picky.

*Their expectations aren't high.*

It seemed less like 'Please craft an immortal masterpiece' and more like they just wanted to use TeSTAR's variety show for some marketing. They probably also trusted the successful Kim Raebin's composing skills.

"Then... should we go in the direction of highlighting the character's impact...?"

"Oh~ If you put it simply, that's exactly it!"

This happened to be the next step of composing.

[Step 2. Identifying the Needs.]

This step involved taking the research, figuring out the direction, and deciding what kind of song to make. As it happened, we had an expert reader to help determine this.

The person who played Jeong Isol himself.

The person who knew Jeong Isol's actions inside and out.

"We have Sejin with us."

"Uhm...! Hey, Sejin. In Season 2... how do you first appear, may I ask...?"

Amidst expectant gazes, Bae Sejin avoided their eyes.

"I die."

"......"

"Ah, right."

He appeared so much on the snowy mountain that I got confused. The guys who had peeked in and played the other roles while Bae Sejin practiced his lines nodded solemnly.

Jeong Isol this season? Ah, that was just a delusion in the protagonist's head.

"A dead person... hmm."

"Then should we try making it a bit scary? The character Jeong Isol himself is a bit brutal."

"Right. It might be good to sample the melody from the BGM used when Jeong Isol betrayed them in Season 1."

"Indeed, an excellent idea!"

An interesting dynamic occurred here.

*They're not getting pushed back.*

Perhaps because the TeSTAR members got used to composing the CM song for a few days without Kim Raebin, their attitudes had changed. Before, they were more cautious, afraid they might have a negative influence on the expert composer.

They were filtering their thoughts.

*As they gained more experience, that tendency became more ingrained.*

However, after tearing their hair out to compose a song in a day without Kim Raebin and aggressively exchanging feedback, it seemed to have become second nature.

"This, this is good! What song is this part from?"

"Ooooh!"

"Found it. I'll put it in!"

The interesting thing was that Kim Raebin also seemed to be enjoying it even more.

*This is fun.*

Opinions were exchanged without rest, and revision after revision shaped the song.

[Step 3. Demo Production.]

Do you know how long it took to produce that one-minute demo?

Just three hours.

"Wooow."

"Wow... With Raebin here, the speed is on another level!"

"Thank you!"

With the file open, they exchanged drinks and compliments. Basking in a workaholic's joy, Kim Raebin moved the mouse again with practiced, masterful skill.

"Then I'll play it now!"

"Okay!"

Click.

He pressed the button.

[...♪♬♬♬♬-♪-♪♬]

The strings trembled.

"...!"

"......"

It coldly cut through the thick bass. The main melody, choosing a heavy presence over harmony, was relentless. On a vacant, empty snowy mountain, the sleek melody of a cello rushed in like a shiver.

It was the classical melody used when Jeong Isol killed his colleague in Season 1. It highlighted the character's connection and maximized his presence.

"......"

It was not a real cello performance, just notes punched into a program, yet the composition was so immense and the resolution so high, it was as if you could see a performer's hands racing across the instrument.

The resolution.

"......"

It was an OST that reconstructed a classical piece into a powerful, modern pop song. The moment the 1 minute and 12 seconds of playback ended, everyone was speechless.

"What do you think?!"

"It's so... amazing."

That was the right answer.

*This... this tastes like they just threw in everything the production company wanted.*

It was the kind of thing overflowing with MSG that makes your mouth water. If you put some meaningful lyrics in a foreign language on top of this, it would perfectly capture the essence of ❬Doll Hunter❭ that the client wanted.

*They nailed it in one go.*

The drama production company's bet had paid off. They would probably be able to use it for marketing, like, 'Did you know? One of our OSTs was made by TeSTAR hehe'.

"Wow... I love it. I think we can just build on this and increase the volume. What does everyone else think?"

"I agree!"

Just as everyone excitedly gathered around the music program again, Bae Sejin raised his hand.

"Um."

"Yes?"

He looked around, then opened his mouth as if he had made up his mind.

"It's amazing, but... Yeah, Ryu Chungwoo. In your opinion, what kind of scene would this song be used in?"

"...?"

"Hmm... What about the scene where Jeong Isol dies at the beginning?"

Bae Sejin shook his head.

"That scene is intentionally directed to feel empty. A song this intense wouldn't match it at all."

"Hmm..."

"And I've been thinking," Bae Sejin said, taking a deep breath. "Among the scenarios I acted in for this season... there isn't a single scene that really fits this song."

"...!"

The atmosphere became quiet, as if doused with cold water.

*I see.*

Bae Sejin knew every scene where Jeong Isol appeared. From his perspective, there was no place for this song, no matter how amazing it was.

"Don't get me wrong! The song is really good. Still... I thought it would be better to say something."

Bae Sejin then more firmly explained his death sentence for the demo.

"Earlier, Kim Raebin said that an OST is part of a comprehensive content, right? So... I considered this song once more as a component of the drama."

"......"

"And it felt a bit awkward no matter where I imagined it."

Kim Raebin opened his mouth.

"I see. Since you, who personally played the character, are saying so, it's even more trustworthy!"

The guy nodded seriously, then without hesitation, deleted the melody from the music file.

"Aaargh!"

"Don't delete it! It's a waste!"

"Huh? Ah, I'm not deleting it completely! I've backed it up separately."

"Phew."

One member after another pretended to wipe away cold sweat. It was understandable.

*The song itself is good.*

I was sure of it. If we just submitted it as is, the drama production company would eagerly accept it. The song was good, and it fit the character. The marketing through TeSTAR's variety show would also get a passing grade. They would probably find some scene that fit.

However, that would just be 'making it fit for appearance's sake'.

"...I thought about this while composing the CM song a few days ago, but I know I can't contribute to composing like Kim Raebin right now," Bae Sejin said firmly.

"However! It's a drama OST. This is a field I know well. So I want you to trust me and revise the song."

"...!"

It was the first time Bae Sejin had ever made a statement like 'I'll take responsibility' with such confidence.

And I knew why.

*He realized he could take responsibility.*

The experience of protesting firmly yet politely to the production staff and getting a good result must have been a new challenge for him. Since that courageous attempt returned a positive outcome, the behavior was reinforced.

Just like this.

"What do you think?"

I looked at Bae Sejin's confident expression and then around at the others. It seemed like everyone was thinking roughly the same thing.

Of course.

"I'm for it."

"...!"

"That's right. We're not the kind of group that settles for 'good enough'~ Right, Moondae Moondae?"

"Yeah."

That was right. If we compromised here, only the drama production company would reap the bigger benefits from TeSTAR taking on the OST.

*Let us get a taste of marketing with you guys, too.*

We had to make even people who knew nothing about us search for the song the moment it played, thinking 'Wow, what the hell is this,' and then be astonished to see TeSTAR's name. That was the maximum benefit TeSTAR could extract.

"We have to create something that will give people goosebumps the moment the OST plays in the drama."

"...! I like it."

Good answer. I inwardly let out a small laugh.

"Then, to help with the OST revision, is there an 'impressive scene of Jeong Isol' that you'd recommend?"

In the past, Bae Sejin would have turned pale, hesitated, and then added, 'This is just my opinion, but...'

However.

"There is one scene I have in mind."

Now, Bae Sejin opened his mouth immediately.

"What is it?"

He gave a confident answer.

"The true climax of Jeong Isol this season."

***

A few days later.

"They said the OST has arrived."

"Ah, okay."

The director of ❬Doll Hunter❭ received a music file from a staff member.

*It's here.*

Jeong Isol's theme song. Originally, the people in charge should have been overseeing this from the demo stage, with complex feedback exchanged back and forth. However, it had become a marketing method, a 'TeSTAR, please take care of it' situation.

This was because the higher-ups predicted it would be better for attracting attention.

"Hmph."

The director had a bitter taste in his mouth. Bae Sejin was an excellent actor, but his main activity was ultimately K-POP idols.

K-POP idols. The essence of the modern broadcast entertainment industry, which staked its life on commerciality. The OST would have been made following corporate logic rather than artistry.

Of course, the song would be good. However, they would have made a safe choice, a song with popular appeal, not a film score that perfectly fit the story.

That was what stung.

*Jeong Isol is such an important role.*

At a point where more investment capital came in under the platform's name than his own, there was nothing he could make 'as the director wishes'. It was all a series of compromises, but he still felt bitter.

*I'll have to find a way to fit it in somehow.*

He resolved with resignation and pressed the play button.

Click.

Thin strings began to ring out.

"...?"

What was this? A strange dissonance that sounded like it tried to be intense but suddenly failed. It creaked as if trying to rise, then abruptly fell.

And then, silence.

"......"

This was Jeong Isol's theme song? Just as the director's bewilderment was about to turn into anger, the strings flowed again.

♪♬♩-♪-♪♬...

This time, it was the harmonious and stable sound of a soft orchestra, and the melody flowing elegantly over it was a human voice.

[Um, Ummum, um...]

The classical melody from Jeong Isol's murder sequence in Season 1, now an a cappella of a beautiful voice, danced above the strings. The way the slightly eerie, sharp minor key mixed with the soft strings was mysterious.

*Hm?*

In between, the sound of wind instruments like a whisper could be heard, along with percussion like a pen tapping.

*Aha.*

The director nodded. It seemed they had embodied the image of the fictional Jeong Isol imagined by the protagonist in the snowy mountains.

*Not bad... Hmm, it's okay.*

He listened with a hint of satisfaction. Then a question suddenly occurred to him.

*Wait!*

If it was meant to express Jeong Isol in the snowy mountains, what was that strange dissonance at the very beginning?

*There must be an intention...*

He came up with a rather convincing guess.

*Oho.*

Perhaps it was meant to express the scene where the protagonist murders Jeong Isol? The image of Jeong Isol dying in vain. He nodded and focused on listening again.

As he wondered when the singing would start, his interest was piqued, but a sense of indifference remained.

However, that indifference was soon shattered.

"...!"

No matter how long he waited, the lyrics never came. Instead, the a cappella humming broke in with a new melody, accompanied by brass instruments.

Aaaaaa—! Hummmmm-

However... that melody was very familiar. It was the protagonist's theme song. A famous classical march.

"......"

Why on earth was the protagonist's theme song in Jeong Isol's theme song?

*Did they misunderstand the composition request?*

Bewilderment. However, as he listened to the OST, which mixed complexly and rushed towards a climax with the sharp sound of a string snapping, he realized.

That destructive sound.

*Ah.*

This was not just a song that listed Jeong Isol's appearance scenes in chronological order.

It was expressing a single scene.

Jeong Isol's highlight scene.

"......"

However, that was not a scene where Jeong Isol actually appeared. It was the protagonist. In the final episode of Season 2, he is chased by former colleagues due to the Butterfly Effect of a selfish choice. He frantically retraces his steps and flees to a convenience store where his home used to be.

There, he sees it.

The corpse of Jeong Isol, whom he had killed, and sitting next to that corpse, the Jeong Isol from his own delusions.

[......]

[What are your thoughts?]

The flashback sequence the protagonist recalls while looking at Jeong Isol's corpse, under extreme stress. Those short, intense cuts. From the murder to the confrontation in the snowy mountains, then conversation, confusion, hatred, and comfort.

Everything pours out rapidly. Like a revolving lantern.

*My god.*

That was what this song was.

In the meantime, the song drove deeper. The a cappella jutted out here and there, and around the strings, modern, sharp sounds flashed like electricity. The protagonist's guilt, collapsing mentality, physical fatigue, anger, and sense of kinship all raged and rushed forward.

[AAAAARGH!]

Until the protagonist runs out of the convenience store and voluntarily jumps into the attacks of his former colleagues.

Until the Jeong Isol in the snowy mountains disappears.

♩! ♩! ♪♬!

"......"

The end of the intense high notes, the screeching cello, and the drums. The flashback was over.

WHOOOOM...

Only white noise remained, flowing slowly.

"......"

The director raised his head. The moment he checked the length of the song, he shuddered.

8 minutes and 14 seconds.

Without a single lyric.

*So...*

He summarized. TeSTAR had boldly thrown away the vocal track. Instead, they had produced a truly traditional OST, an instrumental piece without lyrics. They had completely excluded the lyric-writing part, focusing solely on the auditory elements of the drama to maximize the scene's impact.

"This..."

To think they took on such a risky challenge. However, he knew. There were no lyrics, but there was a development and a story made of melody. Very smooth and intense. So if you cut it into parts, you could even apply it to all of Jeong Isol's scenes this season.

*Unbelievable.*

Popular appeal? He did not know, but one thing was certain. When this song met the Jeong Isol played by Bae Sejin, it would have a tremendous impact.

And was that not the popular appeal of an OST?

"Good heavens."

He could not know what kind of reaction this OST would get, but the director was sure of one thing. At the very least, it would not be the kind of reaction the drama's marketing team had expected.

*Really... beyond imagination.*

It seemed that once you surpassed a certain level in any field, you developed a way of thinking that defied expectations. Even if you were an idol, the king of commercialism.

"Incredible."

Listening to the track again from the beginning, the director was certain. TeSTAR must also be waiting for the reaction with tremendous determination and nervousness.

***

However, at that same moment, TeSTAR were finally leaving the mountain cabin. They came across the reactions to the first and second episodes of their variety show, which were released before the OST.

"H-Hey! We have a huge problem!"

"Why."

"The internet is flooded with slander and curses aimed at the variety show's production staff and the person who commissioned the CM song..."

"Oh."

"...?"

"Ah, it's fine."

"......"

"Just leave it."

Park Moondae was savoring the sweet taste of justice served without any backlash.
