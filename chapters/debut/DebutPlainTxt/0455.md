# Chapter 455

An unsettled atmosphere hung over T1 Stars.

The company had just become independent from its parent, T1 Ent, and was no longer a subsidiary of the octopus-like conglomerate. While that might sound good, it brought enormous uncertainty. The meddling higher-ups were gone, but so was the backing that always took responsibility.

[What are your thoughts?]

[Assistant Manager.. haha, should we look into it together?]

The working-level staff exchanged concerns about their future and joked about changing jobs.

The same went for their superiors.

The board of directors had vanished, arrested in a political offensive. The only superiors left were the division head, a professional CEO, and various department heads and team leaders. Everyone watched their every move, on high alert. If those people sought an escape route, the staff had to act quickly too.

[The Division Head will probably go to another company, right? ㅋㅋㅠㅠ]

[Maybe he won't leave?]

Yet, a glimmer of hope remained.

[Our current artist lineup is still good, you know.]

That was right.

They clung to their roster of artists. A few groups had solid fanbases, toured, and secured fame and a steady income.

Among them, one group's name value was in a league of its own.

[And they say TeSTAR is renewing their contract.]

TeSTAR!

They were the undisputed top idols and winners of the Grand Prize for three consecutive years.

Since they produced their own music, the disappearance of the higher-ups was not a major problem.

The staff, unaware of the details of the fallout between T1 and its subsidiary, were convinced the company could survive as long as they held onto TeSTAR.

[Ah, I should've done whatever it took to get into the TeSTAR label.]

[Even if this place goes under, the label will survive, right? ㅠㅠ]

People engaged in heated, happy speculation. They wondered if more personnel would be absorbed into the label, or if the label might even take over the company itself.

So, this became the public-facing atmosphere.

Let's look at an anonymous employee community.

——————————

[To the hyungs and noonas at the T1 Entertainment subsidiary?]

Curious what the atmosphere is like lately. Are you all happy about the independence?

——————————

[Hahahahaha]

[It's... peculiar.]

[Well, we're just going to work. No major issues so far.]

Instead of voicing complaints, they gave vague answers, a reaction that hinted at a real problem.

But within that peculiar atmosphere where hope still existed...

[TeSTAR Park Moondae: Then I will see you tomorrow.]

"...!"

In his office, the division head had finally received the text message.

~~~

TeSTAR's Park Moondae was a crazy bastard.

At least, that was how the division head saw him.

He had previously thought of him as a precocious brat, but that assessment became fixed after Park Moondae semi-coerced him into signing the label contract.

The timing of his actions and words was too cunning and exquisite.

*I should have known the moment I found out about his family situation.*

An orphan who won first place on a survival show at that age...*Just how worn down and jaded must his character be?*

He had misstepped by treating him too softly, judging him only by his age.

*Who would have thought that young kid would do something like that?*

The division head clicked his tongue, trying to dismiss the shame he felt from being practically blackmailed into signing the label contract.

Of course, he had no way of guessing that an older adult resided inside Park Moondae's soft appearance.

In any case, time had passed since the label incident. TeSTAR worked hard without any other notable actions, and Park Moondae had not contacted him separately.

However.

"I'd like you to read this."

"......"

With stiff hands, the division head barely picked up the stack of papers Park Moondae had set down.

The documents, with crucial parts blacked out just like before, were identical to the ones from the label blackmail incident.

Lawsuit materials.

The problem was that they were not just for TeSTAR.

"Ah, from page seven onward, it's about other groups."

Mirynay, Spacer.

The documents were neatly organized, detailing materials for the two groups that were the agency's next most profitable after TeSTAR.

*This is...*

This, too, was about the agency's unfair treatment, just a little less critical. For Mirynay, the main issues were unconsented tours and American promotions. For Spacer, it was the neglect of its members.

Even so, he had created this for only one reason.

"Hmm, next month. We plan to file a class-action lawsuit by the month after that at the latest."

"...!"

The declaration was a bolt from the blue.

Moreover, this was an act devoid of business ethics, especially from TeSTAR.

He barely calmed his rising sense of crisis. Then, he skillfully shook his head.

"No... even if you show me something like this."

The division head tapped the papers once and forced a laugh.

He deliberately let a hint of his suppressed anger show.

"TeSTAR, you already used this to get a label contract last time. Doing this again puts the company in a very difficult position. If you make proposals so rudely, who would want to work with TeSTAR?"

It was true.

It was also a subtle threat. If they sued, he would somehow drag the label story into it and blow everything up messily.

"And the other groups, honestly, this isn't grounds for a lawsuit, is it? You're just nitpicking at mistakes anyone could make while working. The judges aren't fools."

This was also half-true.

However, Park Moondae was composed.

"Winning isn't what's important."

Huh?

"What's important is what happens to this company the moment these three groups stop working."

"......"

"The funds will stop coming in."

That was the core of it.

The moment they sued, all activities would halt.

Album and merchandise sales would plummet because the fans would not buy them.

The division head reflexively imagined the consequences.

*If this small-to-medium-sized entertainment agency, no longer a T1 subsidiary, has all its funding cut off...*

It's over.

The longer the lawsuit dragged on, the longer they would be unable to generate profit, while fixed costs like the building would continue to drain their funds.

However, T1 had already cut ties and would not bring in more money.

Other investors would not dare get involved once the company's three most successful groups filed a joint lawsuit.

In that case...

"Whether it's short or long, it's bankruptcy," Park Moondae declared.

"Then, since the company would be bankrupt, we'd automatically become free agents."

"......"

A chill ran down his spine.

It was the strong premonition that only comes when you hear a prediction about the future with a very high probability of being the truth.

One that could be summarized as,*I'm fucked.*

They could ruin the company just by filing a lawsuit.

*These damn...*

The division head swallowed a few curses, a mix of English and Korean, and changed the subject as calmly as possible.

First, he had to pretend to be calm and placate him.

"That's a bit of a leap in logic. Haha, um, can't TeSTAR just leave by not renewing your contract? I really don't understand why you're choosing to go down such a difficult path."

Park Moondae brilliantly caught the underlying meaning—*Please just ask for better renewal terms instead of doing this*—and gave a slight nod.

"To help the other groups. As their senior."

*These crazy sons of bitches!*

They had been making good money, so what kind of grudge did they have to pull something like this?

*The moment you say you're not renewing, you should know your reputation is finished too.*

The division head, who had now picked up on the general flow of the entertainment business, resolved to use public opinion to finish them off one way or another.

Just as he was about to lay out a warning while feigning nonchalance, Park Moondae spoke.

"That's why I'm telling you this first, Division Head."

After a deliberate pause, Park Moondae added quietly, "You were good to us during the label negotiations, after all."

His voice was tinged with a hint of goodwill.

"It means you should escape first."

"......"

"Before we file the lawsuit."

The division head was momentarily taken aback.

Park Moondae showed no particular sign of contempt or arrogance.

It was as if he had debated whether to say it and, despite the potential downsides, had decided to speak up.

*...That's right.*

If he had such a plan, telling a company official like himself was a risk. The fact that he said it despite the risk... it might really be a gesture of goodwill.

No matter how much money he had earned, he was still just a rookie in society. He must feel sorry for an adult he had already screwed over once with an additional contract. The division head painted a picture in his mind.

...Of course, it was not the truth.

*This bastard took the bait.*

That was what Park Moondae thought as he looked at the division head.

Because he never intended to sue in the first place.

As TeSTAR had discussed, suing the company would deal a huge blow to an artist's image. In crude terms, they would be "blacklisted" in the industry.

However, the division head, not originally from the entertainment business, failed to grasp such experiential details.

Instead, he caught the signal Park Moondae was dropping.

*I don't want to harm others, especially people I know.*

That kind of soft-hearted, immature side that had not yet been hardened by the world.

And so, the division head roughly assessed the situation. Logically, if TeSTAR had little to gain from a lawsuit...

*Was he gently persuaded by the other groups to take the lead, and now he feels like he has no choice?*

He stood up for others, so now he worried about the "other people" who would be harmed because of it.

His words reflexively started to come out stronger.

Because he saw an opening.

"Look, changing jobs isn't child's play... Do you think I can just switch companies overnight, Moondae?"

His plan was to push forward and get TeSTAR to back out of the lawsuit.

Park Moondae blinked.

"Is it difficult for you."

"It's practically impossible. No, just tell me when you're planning to sue."

"Hmm."

Park Moondae fell silent for an agonizingly long time before speaking slowly.

A rope of salvation.

"Come to think of it, instead of suing... what if we did this."

"...!"

"How about this."

Park Moondae furrowed his brow as if the idea had just struck him and threw out a few words as if reasoning it out.

In that moment, the division head's mind snapped to attention.

*If they did that!*

"...This way, you can buy some time, and there shouldn't be any problems with your portfolio or anything when you change jobs."

Park Moondae finished his sentence and stared intently at the division head.

"You know it too, Division Head. That this company has no future anyway."

Because T1 had washed its hands of it.

Yes, it was a fact the division head already knew.

He had been wondering if he could squeeze a bit more out of it since they still had TeSTAR, but now that things had come to this...

*...Right. All business is about timing.*

The one who strikes first suffers the least loss and reaps the most profit.

Having made his decision, the division head opened his mouth.

Park Moondae smiled faintly.

The next evening.

"Hyung~ what did they say?"

Ryu Chungwoo hung up the direct call from the company's higher-ups and grinned, his smile filled with surprise and relief.

"The company... wants to talk about our group name. It seems like they're going to sell us the trademark rights."

"...!"

"R-Really...?" Bae Sejin asked.

"......"

"......"

The living room soon filled with excited noise at the sign that their plan had succeeded. Bae Sejin swung his arms in excitement.

"You mean it worked?"

"Yes."

*I told you it would work.*

I let out a small laugh, recalling my conversation with that bastard of a division head.

*That selfish bastard.*

I knew he would take the money and run.

It was not some grand proposal I made. It was something like this.

*The company's going to fail anyway, so make one last score before it does.*

[The trademark rights will go up for auction after the bankruptcy anyway. Rather than let that happen, you can transfer the ownership to the groups for a fee beforehand.]

We could buy the trademark rights at auction when the company went bankrupt anyway. However, this gave the division head a chance to sell them first and bolt.

He could milk the situation for all it was worth by creating a beautiful agency image of giving the artists their names, all while posting a short-term profit.

And the division head would get to complete the final page of his portfolio and ditch the company before it went under.

As for us? I had prepared an excuse about how we would benefit too.

[Wouldn't the other groups feel secure enough to stay with the company then? It means they can take their names and go elsewhere even if the company fails.]

[And it's better for us to just buy it now rather than waiting and pushing the company to the brink of bankruptcy.]

Meaning, it was a win-win for everyone.

"...In that case?"

"Yes."

I grinned.

"We don't have to sue."

"...!"

"Oh!"

"Then we can leave amicably without renewing our contracts!"

"Okay~ This is the best!"

As everyone started high-fiving each other out of context, saying all they needed now was a company, Seon Ahyeon timidly raised his hand.

"Um, but, then... are the other groups going to stay at this company...? If we don't sue?"

"...!"

I let out a small laugh.

I remembered Gold 2, who had been so anxious throughout the process that he looked like he was about to bite his nails off, despite having shouted that we should go all out.

[Hyung, you're going to save us, right?? You're not just going to let TeSTAR leave by yourselves after all this... are you?? ㅠㅠ]

"No."

I promised I would get them out. It would be fraud otherwise.

"It'll happen in due time."

The great agency escape.

Now, all that was left was to wait.
