# Chapter 488

I looked at Seon Ahyeon.

*He wants to exchange cards?*

The man who had called me to the edge of his room then took something from his pocket.

"...!"

It was Gold with a QR code attached. This was the currency for card exchanges.

"Where did you find it?"

"U-uhm, actually..."

Seon Ahyeon whispered quietly. He explained that after waking up in his room, he had searched it while reading the profile.

"Back then, under the bed... I found it."

"......"

In other words, he had found it right when the game began.

*And he kept his mouth shut about it until now?*

Seon Ahyeon did that? I felt an unexpected sense of admiration.

*Everyone's got a taste for variety shows.*

It was almost admirable. I looked forward to seeing how this would look after Ryu Seorin's editing.

"However, I'm not sure if it's okay to use it like this. We might be able to use it at a crucial moment."

Seon Ahyeon nodded with a determined expression at my words.

"We need information, so it's okay to use my Gold...!"

"Hmm."

Normally, the most efficient play would be to wait for someone else to act first.

*But this is a variety show.*

Making the first move early was good for the show's pacing. I nodded.

"Then let's do it after we both know each other's jobs. I think that would be better."

"U-uhm, I'm... a Scholar."

Oh. I nodded.

"Moondae, you're...?"

"I'm a Doctor."

"Ah, aha!"

Seon Ahyeon blushed and nodded, saying it was a good job. With that, our conversation ended.

"We're going to exchange cards."

"Huh?"

"Did you find Gold?"

I gathered the other members and stood before the main door.

"We're just trying it out once as a test."

"......"

Keun Sejin nodded. I reviewed the game rules one more time.

"If one of us is the sacrifice, this card exchange will switch who it is, right?"

"U-uhm, that's right."

It was like passing the joker in a card game.

*Is this like passing the bomb?*

This seemed to be a survival game with a hidden culprit, like a mafia game, but whether you were mafia or a citizen, jobs like 'Scholar' or 'Doctor' were unlikely first targets.

I recalled the answer I had given Ryu Chungwoo earlier. Which job did I think had the highest chance of dying?

[The Detective or the Jester.]

If the mafia chose the sacrifice, it would be the Detective. If a citizen chose, it would be the Jester.

*That's the standard route.*

Jobs like Scholar or Doctor did not draw aggro like those two. We stood before the dark entrance, the only place where the lights remained off. Relying only on the panel's light, we held up our cards.

"We're doing it."

"U-uhm."

I scanned the QR codes on my card, Seon Ahyeon's card, and the Gold.

"......"

The screen on the main door's panel began to swirl silently with a dark red color.

"Woah!"

"Aish, this is scarier than I thought."

Listening to the members' tense voices, I fixed my gaze on the panel. The swirling lasted for about four seconds before disappearing. Only the backs of two cards remained on the screen.

[Token Exchange Complete]

[Park Moondae <-> Seon Ahyeon ]

"Oh."

"So this is how you do it."

The members' soft sounds of admiration echoed. I fiddled with my card.

*There's no other information.*

It seemed only a clean record of the exchange was saved.

"That was nothing special."

"U-uhm. Still... it was amazing!"

"I guess so."

There were no other frills. As if satisfied with the demonstration, Seon Ahyeon and I readily stepped back from the main door. After that, we continued our exploration.

"Moondae Moondae~ Don't tell me you couldn't open the bathroom door in this dorm? Oh, if it's this difficult, I bet there's Gold inside..."

"There's a ghost inside."

"Euh-heok."

A comedy bit followed.

"There's chicken in the fridge!"

"......"

"They're giving us food, I'm so touched."

Just as another comedy bit unfolded and the atmosphere began to loosen, the members grew numb to the environment. They scattered and started poking around the two large dorms.

*Has it already been two hours?*

A moment later.

BEEEEEP!

"...!"

An alarm blared from the entrance. A voice began to repeat with the siren, like an emergency announcement.

[Warning, please enter the starting room.]

[Warning, please enter the starting room.]

[Warning, please enter the starting room.]

"W-What is it."

"The starting room?"

The scattered members came running out. Conscious of the siren, I shouted loudly.

"I think it means the rooms we each started in!"

"Ah!"

"Everyone, I'll see you in a bit!"

The men who had run to the entrance soon scattered in perfect order. They were truly broadcasting veterans. I also returned to my starting room, a re-creation of my room from our previous dorm.

Click.

The moment I entered and closed the door, the siren's sound was muffled.

DING DONG!!

"......"

[This is the mid-game announcement time.]

...So there was a speaker in here too.

*...Hoo.*

They must be doing this on purpose to startle people. I sat on the edge of the bed in a corner of the dark room, where the fluorescent lights were off again. The bizarre mechanical voice came through the speaker.

[Check the drawer of the headboard.]

Alright. I moved my hand. Inside the headboard drawer was an electronic device. It definitely was not there before.

*Did a staff member who's filming over there come out and put it in?*

You're working hard. I muttered inwardly, glancing at the black box in the corner where only a camera lens peeked out. I carefully turned on the screen of the electronic device. It was an Oseon tablet, perhaps for product placement.

Beep.

The lit-up screen cut through the dark air and flashed.

[Job Ability Revealed]

[Your job ability is...]

*Hoh.*

So they separated the members for this. I slowly read all the text.

"......"

*So this... is what this was for.*

It was quite interesting. As I rubbed my chin, thinking of ways to apply it, another notification sounded.

*Hm?*

The pad's screen changed again.

[01:00:00]

[※Token Can Be Checked※]

The time remaining until the end of the game, and... the token on my card. I see.

"......"

I raised my hand and touched the pad.

[Kyahahaha!]

The first thing I heard was a child's laughter. The innocent sound created an unsettling feeling, sending a chill down my spine. Without warning, a giant red circular pattern filled the pad.

Lamb Skull Pattern.

[Your card is the 'Sacrificial Token'.]

[the Current Lamb : Park Moondae ]

"......"

I see. I looked away from the pad's screen for a moment and caught my breath.

*The Sacrificial Token is with me. So if things continue like this, I die.*

~~~

"Eussassa~ Did everyone see that well!"

"Yeah, bro!"

The siren blared again, and the members emerged from their rooms. The remaining hour passed in an instant. From then on, it was less about searching for Gold and more about conversation and surveillance.

"Hey, hey? Where are you two hyungs going? Are you maybe exchanging cards?"

"...W-We're just going to heat up the chicken."

"...Yep."

Publicly, the hour passed smoothly without any major incidents. However, a strange tension was building.

*Soon.*

It's the end. I watched the time, waiting for the ending. A moment later.

BEEEEEEP!!

"Oh."

"Looks like time is up."

This time, they were not as surprised. However, everyone wore slightly tense expressions.

*The one to be eliminated has been decided.*

After being inside for several hours, our immersion had naturally deepened. The word 'sacrifice' now felt slightly terrifying, like entering a haunted house at an amusement park.

"It's up."

On the main door's panel, the countdown had indeed finished.

[00:00:00]

Giant letters appeared, covering the timer.

[Exchange Over]

"......"

The modulated mechanical voice flowed from the speaker again.

[This is a notice before the announcement.]

[The first Sacrificial Token you received was determined by the votes of sponsors.]

"S-Sponsors...?"

[As a result of the votes from the fans who love you,]

*...Fans?*

A bright red graph on the screen rose endlessly, and the number 20,374 votes appeared. Putting that high number and the explanation together...

"No way... this was a fan vote?"

"When on earth did you hold this?"

*My thoughts exactly.*

The panel silently spat out the narration, regardless of the members' shock.

[The one that received the most votes was one job with 20,374 votes.]

Now, everyone held their breath, watching the panel.

[And now that the time limit has passed, the job that holds this Sacrificial Token is...]

Gulp.

Someone audibly swallowed amidst the grim tension. The result was announced.

[It is the Scholar.]

"......"

"Ah."

I reflexively glanced back at Seon Ahyeon. His face was pale.

"......"

[U-uhm, I'm... a Scholar.]

Seon Ahyeon had definitely said that. The members immediately started looking around to find the person in question.

"The Scholar?"

"Who's the Scholar? Say something!"

"......"

However, there was only silence. No one came forward, and Seon Ahyeon also kept his mouth shut.

"Huh?"

"...Why isn't anyone coming forward?"

Bae Sejin narrowed his eyes, and Kim Raebin looked around in bewilderment.

"R-Right now, it's a situation where we can't change the result anyway, isn't it?"

"That's right!"

"But why...?"

As everyone chattered with confused expressions, the announcement continued.

[The Sacrificial Room has opened.]

"Kiiik!"

"Heok!"

The main door opened automatically. Behind it, another door opened on its own. It was the solitary room in our dorm, the one that had not opened until now.

"Is that place the Sacrificial Room?"

"Seems like it."

The note Kim Raebin had picked up from under that door came to mind.

[I Open at the Close .]

*Did that mean the room opens when this game ends?*

Or perhaps it meant when it was decided who would die, who would meet their end. I followed the others, guessing as much for now. I thought I should probably say this during the interview. We gathered in front of the opened solitary room door.

"I'm going in!"

Cha Eugene stepped forward without hesitation. However...

"Oops!!"

"Why, why."

He frowned and pulled his foot back.

*What is it?*

Surprised by his reaction, everyone peered inside the room.

"......"

The room's interior looked like some kind of cult's altar. Torn black wallpaper and burning red candles flickered ominously. In the very center was a huge, coffin-shaped altar with a bright red lamb's skull pattern carved on top.

*This is insane.*

BOOM!

"Ugh!"

They even had a thunder effect. The members became a flock of penguins again in front of the space where the art team had clearly poured their souls.

"Moondae Moondae, I don't think I can sleep in this room anymore..."

"It's a good thing we moved."

"......"

Anyway, we carefully entered.

"...There are name tags."

"Ugh."

Soon, we were arranged around the coffin-shaped altar according to the names displayed like reception table placards. Just then.

[Participants.]

"I knew this would happen."

"Me too."

The bizarre narration began again from a speaker. A camera emerged from the coffin and rotated, capturing our faces.

[Welcome to the Sacrificial Room.]

"Don't tell me we really have to offer a member as a sacrifice here?!"

"Calm down, Raebin."

[Participants, you must find the Black Sheep here.]

"......"

Black Sheep?

[The participant who passed their fate as a sacrifice to another participant.]

[In other words, the culprit who made the 'Scholar' the sacrifice through the final token exchange is the 'Black Sheep'.]

"......"

"Wow, I'm getting more and more lost."

I quickly organized my thoughts.

*Hmm.*

So, we had to find the person who last passed the token to the 'Scholar', who became the final sacrifice. It seemed they judged that act as being the 'mafia' or 'culprit'.

*So we just need to find the person who exchanged with the Scholar last.*

Just as I thought it might be too easy, another condition was added.

[If you correctly deduce the Black Sheep, that culprit will become a Burnt Offering along with the sacrifice.]

[However, if you fail to find the Black Sheep,]

"...Then?"

[Everyone except the Black Sheep and the sacrifice will become a Burnt Offering.]

"Aaaargh."

"Wow, this is a total horror game!"

The word 'annihilation' caused screams and intense reactions to erupt from all over.

*Oh.*

So, this was a structure where the sacrifice could also live if they just kept their mouth shut.

*In that case, they won't say who they exchanged with.*

I marveled at the production team's viciousness.

[Then, I wish you good luck.]

luck-, luck, lu-...

The echoing speaker soon crackled and cut off. A red light lit up on the coffin's skull pattern.

"Oh!"

"I think it's telling us to start."

While clicking their tongues at the incredible room, the members soon settled down. They seemed to be organizing their thoughts and preparing to talk. Of course, the first priority would be this.

*Figuring out who the Scholar who became the sacrifice is.*

If we could just interrogate that person and find who they last exchanged with, it would be over. However, the first one to speak was Bae Sejin.

"Wait! I have something to say first."

He calmly continued.

"I knew all the jobs that exist."

"Yes?"

"I'm a Lawyer."

Just as he had told me, he began to reveal all the information he knew.

*Since it's the final round, is this a rational move?*

I inwardly nodded. If you knew all the jobs, it was easier to deduce who had which job through elimination.

"However, there's one person, someone who claimed a job that doesn't exist! That person is..."

Just as Bae Sejin was about to say Kim Raebin's name.

"Uh-oh? Just a moment."

*...?*

Keun Sejin suddenly cut in. He immediately asked the confused Bae Sejin.

"What did you say your job was, hyung?"

"...A Lawyer. The Lawyer's job ability is to know the other jobs..."

"...? No, you're the Lawyer, hyung?"

Keun Sejin let out a dry laugh and dropped a bombshell.

"That's strange. Because I'm the Lawyer."

"......"

"Huhhh?"

"There can't be two of the same job. Right?"

"......"

The members, mouths agape, looked back and forth between the two men. Keun Sejin rubbed the back of his neck.

"Hmm. Seeing the reactions, it seems Sejin-hyung has been going around telling everyone he's the Lawyer, but, uh, the Lawyer isn't supposed to do that for the mission."

"......"

Keun Sejin observed the members' reactions, then frowned and gave a bitter smile. He sighed.

"Aigoo~ You were all fooled by hyung-nim. Completely fooled."

"......"

A brief, bewildered silence fell. In that moment, Bae Sejin spoke resolutely, his face pale.

"You're the one who's lying!"

*Good grief.*

I swallowed a groan.

*So one of them is definitely trolling, is that it?*

I could already picture the viewers holding their breath and munching on popcorn. It was, quite literally, the beginning of a free-for-all.
