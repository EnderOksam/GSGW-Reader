# Chapter 625

A few hours ago.

*Ah... what should I do?*

Ryu Gunwoo, or rather Keundal, groaned as he scrolled through English-language websites while on leave from work. His English studies had paid off. He could read everything with ease.

Of course, he was only looking for one thing.

The Glastonbury Festival, where TeSTAR was performing.

He remembered when he received the news from his hyung, even before any articles were published.

[It's happening.]

[Aaaargh!]

He could vividly picture himself screaming about why the ticketing had to end so early last year.

*Since it's come to this, I thought I'd at least try to find a live text commentary...*

However, today, the day of the Friday performance, a state of emergency had occurred. The headliner had vanished due to an injury right before the show.

Even the hastily recruited replacement headliner was seen as a bit of a miscast, depressing the people in the English-speaking communities.

[I risked everything to get a ticket.. this year's Glastonbury is cursed]

[People say there are plenty of other good stages to watch, but I was really looking forward to Red Summer.]

At this rate, the outcome was obvious.

The no-show incident would naturally get more attention, and the Friday performance itself would likely pass by uneventfully, like an extension of the pre-festival celebrations.

It seemed that those who couldn't get tickets, in particular, thought this way with the somewhat desperate mindset of a loser...

[What the fuck just happened?]

Suddenly, starting in the afternoon, some band had started doing crazy things on the main stage.

Who else could it be?

*Hyung!*

It was the very group that had made Keundal search through English websites. TeSTAR!

[I sincerely want to share this shock]

[lololololololololololololol]

TeSTAR performed a live band version of a movie OST song, one that would be most popular in the English-speaking world, as their first song. They then followed it up with an insanely experimental, heavy hip-hop track.

They even went out of their way to transform their latest title track into a style they had never even officially released on an album before!

*Aaaaaah!*

There was no need to scream about what they were doing, performing something they hadn't shown in Korea and wasn't even going to be on a tour VOD.

That was because even though there were few TeSTAR fans in the audience to begin with, everyone was screaming.

The choice was so unexpected that even in the few WeTube fancams being uploaded, you could clearly see the cameras shaking from exclamations of bewilderment and surprise like 'What?'.

But what was even more surprising was this.

[This is our next song.]

They blindsided the audience again with the very next song.

*Gasp.*

TeSTAR chose one of their previous title tracks, ❬Promise❭, known for its bright, beautiful melody and soft, dance-like lines.

[The keyword is mermaid.]

The song choice seemed intentionally meant to tease.

According to the stereotypes spread across the global internet, it was a completely typical K-POP-style performance. It had bright colors, wholesome and cheerful lyrics, and even a signature choreography using water that was smooth rather than rough.

[You could say it's a song for a rainy day like today.]

[Yep, we checked the U.K. weather for June very carefully. So this is a natural song choice.]

And they attached such a plausible-sounding explanation to it!

However, at this point, people started to react enthusiastically to that very fact.

[It makes you feel the spirit of resistance]

[Literally, it's the pure spirit of Glastonbury. Fuck you all, we'll live freely and do whatever we want LOL]

If anything, it was consistent.

That attitude of not being fixed in one style, of seeming to do whatever they wanted.

And yet, the quality didn't drop one bit.

They knew what they were doing, and they did it well!

[It was so much fun because you couldn't predict anything!]

[Honestly, I think that was the moment I enjoyed most as a 'performance experience' during the Friday shows.]

It all looked very experimental, and that 'experimental nature' itself was enough to make it seem like the band's signature color.

Needless arguments didn't work either.

[Well. Aren't they just like skilled laborers who have mastered what their company planned for them?]

└Stooooop. I'm sick of this. They're singer-songwriters, okay? How many times do I have to explain this?

└What year of K-Pop is this fossil thinking of?

└Read this article (link to TeSTAR's tour review)

It was thanks to TeSTAR having already made an impression on the English-speaking internet bit by bit with their unique tour.

The defense was successful!

*Wow.*

Keundal scrolled down excitedly.

If the headliner had been present as usual, it might have ended as a minor happening, a sort of 'oh, so guys like this exist' moment.

But there was no headliner.

Thanks to that, TeSTAR had sucked up all the attention of that special period.

[Did you know these crazy dudes held a concert in a VR game?]

[I'm in a Glastonbury tent right now and I want that band's next tour schedule]

The story of the K-POP band that suddenly appeared, performed on the main stage as they pleased, and then left became quite an interesting topic of conversation on the internet.

Then, by evening, when the fancams started to be uploaded, the volume of mentions was enough to get them into the top ranks of search engines.

*Amazing!*

Keundal, who had now more or less mastered advanced search engine usage, looked back at these phenomena with a flushed face.

It was a shame he couldn't go in person.

He even thought about wanting to see the situation through a shared vision with his hyung, but that would be crossing a line, and it was a difficult thing to do now anyway...

*I can't even see the Status Window anymore.*

He could only watch the fancams and pick out the subtle change in the person standing in the center during the most impressive song, Ryu Chungwoo.

His presence cut through the shaky focus and video noise.

[No more bad feelings]

The solid, clear, and calm aura captured on the big screen caught his eye.

*It's like... he's overcome some kind of limit.*

He, who was once a Status Window, watched the scene again with a sharp eye.

Keundal knew about Ryu Chungwoo's hiatus, but he wasn't that worried anymore.

[Bad feelings]

For some reason, he had a good feeling.

[These bastards are doing EDM this time!]

[Suddenly a mysterious Eastern instrument started playing]

And so, TeSTAR filled a setlist of over five songs, each with a different feel, changing their single outfit by putting on or taking off a hood, and stubbornly did everything they wanted to do.

Thanks to this, VTIC's headliner performance the next day received a slightly strange kind of attention.

[Yeah! This is what I was expecting]

[This is the K-Pop I know. Group choreography, performance, well-structured songs, and flashy outfits!]

VTIC had instead become the stable control group.

Originally, just being a K-POP headliner was expected to be quite polarizing and be received as a somewhat unconventional or different kind of stage.

But not this time.

There had been a K-POP group on the previous day, and it was thanks to them throwing out a series of unexpectedly mind-blowing song choices.

[So those guys were the weird ones after all!!]

On the contrary, it created a strange effect where VTIC's side felt lacking in surprise.

It couldn't be helped.

VTIC had already taken a strong measure by considering the risks of being a headliner, and thanks to that, they had wisely removed more dangerous attempts from their options. They created their stage by appropriately mixing a structure familiar to the English-speaking world with their own conceptual identity.

If things had gone as originally planned, it would have been a quite successful stage and would have received praise here and there.

In reality, good reviews were pouring in, saying they were indeed worthy of being headliners and were cool and weighty, but the problem was the buzz.

[So that band from Friday really were the crazy ones, right?]

If only TeSTAR hadn't been there.

In the end, all buzz was a double-edged sword, and a winner was bound to be decided.

[Glastonbury Day 2. I still can't get over that crazy K-Pop band...]

His hyung, TeSTAR's Park Moondae, had actually made this diagnosis in an instant, but Keundal didn't know that much.

However, Keundal also had faith.

That his hyung would definitely make good use of this situation.

*Hyung was always like that...*

Even when he, as the Status Window, displayed absurd traits or status abnormalities. He always found a way out, a different way to use them.

Recalling those times, Keundal felt an unexpected sentiment.

It wasn't longing, anxiety, or a sense of debt...

*I think it was rewarding.*

A light sense of accomplishment.

He genuinely thought that if he hadn't been the Status Window, the situation would have been really good. That is, if he had been a sort of helper!

Of course, that thought passed quickly, and what caught his eye were new English comments.

[A USB album? That's very eco-friendly.]

[So... it was a remix just for the performance, not even officially released?]

[Kill me]

Posts clamoring for TeSTAR to release the Glastonbury version of the remixes were flooding in.

Like a meme of sorts, TeSTAR's performance went viral.

And that began to be re-imported directly back into the domestic market.

[TeSTAR lolololololololol]

[Wow, this is fun]

[TeSTAR, who also gives you the taste of a foreign band. If you become a fan now, it's 1+3... no, 4!]

[Buying Glastonbury data.. please sell it to me]

"......"

Keundal chuckled as he looked at the compliments.

It was a pleasant feeling to have people who liked the same thing he did.

Then, a page he had visited before flashed in the address bar, and he accidentally clicked and opened it.

"Ah."

It was the social media account that showed album sales figures.

It was a page he had looked at while they were fiercely competing with VTIC, which had left him a bit discouraged.

Of course, the one-week first-week sales period had ended long ago, so it didn't mean much now...

"Huh?"

Keundal blinked. The change in the numbers was strange.

[Sales : 9,126]

This was the sales figure from a few days ago.

And now...

[Sales : 20,274]

Today's sales.

The number displayed was more than double the figure from the day before the Glastonbury performance.

"...?"

Several weeks had passed since the album's release, yet the sales had actually gone up.

For a moment, Keundal wondered if he had seen it wrong, but he soon realized.

*Ah, it's the promotional effect!*

This too was an effect of the Glastonbury Festival.

However, in reality, since the original title track and the remixed song from the performance had completely different vibes, it didn't seem to show that dramatic of a change in the graph.

*Hmm.*

So he returned to the English-language page.

[This is a kind of fraud. I can barely find any videos of them doing old-school hip-hop!]

[I found out a lot of things about the frontman of the chorus this band sang. He was surprisingly an Olympics gold medalist, and he's the hottest Korean I've ever seen.]

But I couldn't find any more scenes of him doing hip-hop. This is literally hell.

"Aigoo."

Keundal tilted his head at the interesting posts, but...

BZZZT!

"...!"

He was startled by the vibration and quickly straightened up.

It was a notification from his smartphone. He snapped to his senses.

*This isn't the time for this!*

[❬Notification❭ Break time is over!]

Seeing that his time was up, Keundal quickly turned off his smartphone.

As promised to his hyung, he no longer blindly entrusted all his joys and sorrows to TeSTAR.

But at the same time, he didn't completely exclude them from his life and make himself dull.

He was trying to maintain a moderate balance, striving to keep his monitoring of TeSTAR as a single pleasure within his own life...

In fact, he had a lot to do today too!

[Excuse me, are you the member Ryu Gunwoo who registered online? This is from , I'm calling because I received your contact information...]

"Ah, just a moment!"

Keundal stood up from his seat.

During his leave of absence, he now had something he was preparing for.

~~~

And at the same time.

TeSTAR was also in the middle of diligently preparing for something.

Of course, they were enjoying a brief moment of pleasure before that.

"Oh, how about this one? 'TeSTAR, the Challenger's Words that Rang Out at Glastonbury'!"

"Coooooool."

"It's an honor!"

Lying in the van, with their seats reclined so far back they were almost like beds, the members of TeSTAR applauded each other.

"Hyung, that was amazing!"

"Thanks."

Naturally, the MVP among them was Ryu Chungwoo.

And there was something he wanted to say.
