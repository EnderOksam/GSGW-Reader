# Chapter 619

"Mr. Ryu Chungwoo, you're feeling that, 'Ah, my left arm could suddenly start hurting again, just like back then,' is that right?"

"...Yes."

"That must be difficult for you. It's completely understandable to feel that way."

Time passed slowly and heavily. Ryu Chungwoo listened to the counselor's words but didn't truly hear them.

This was the same specialist who had counseled him when he quit archery as a child. He had received a great deal of help back then, which was why he came to see him as soon as he halted his activities this time. However.

"How do you feel while having this conversation?"

"......"

He felt no relief or comfort, no sense of a burden lifting.

"I'm not sure."

There were too many things Ryu Chungwoo couldn't say.

Counseling was like getting medicine. You couldn't get the right prescription without describing your symptoms properly, but Ryu Chungwoo couldn't state the exact cause.

He wasn't simply afraid of the aftereffects of a past injury. The experience of a currently progressing injury remained as a memory.

STier.

And the cause of that injury was...

[I heard that because of the excessive fatigue in my shoulder, it'll be difficult to dance at the level of our previous choreography.]

He had definitely told the members that. It wasn't a lie.

During his STier days, he had undoubtedly pushed his body far more harshly than he did now as a member of TeSTAR.

It might seem strange. How could a fading idol from a sinking group be more overworked than a member of TeSTAR, who had won a Grand Prize?

It was because he didn't have the luxury of choosing jobs that wouldn't overexert him.

Amidst activities riddled with crises, no new side of Ryu Chungwoo gained attention. In the end, he just tiredly rehashed the same selling point emphasized since ❬Idol Inc.❭.

A former national athlete.

[As expected of Ryu Chungwoo, amazing Ryu Chungwoo! The record is... 12 minutes and 22 seconds! The winner of this episode's obstacle course on ❬Challenge Race❭ is~ Team A!]

So, requests for him to appear on programs showcasing his physical abilities kept coming. The company, relieved to get any calls, booked schedules indiscriminately.

Ryu Chungwoo diligently carried out those schedules. The result was...

[Sir, your shoulder is completely shot right now. Right here.]

"......"

So, Ryu Chungwoo had told the members the truth. He had just left out a few parts.

"Then, Mr. Chungwoo, shall we try to imagine it? The left arm injury you're afraid of right now."

Normally, someone who managed their body as well as he did wouldn't break down just from accumulated shoulder fatigue. If they did, there was a more decisive factor.

"Could it be... the aftereffects of the car accident you were in during a family trip as a child?"

"......"

"This time, too."

[My aftereffects... I was told the only symptom was a slight tremor, and that it definitely wouldn't affect my daily life.]

[That means you can live a normal daily life, not that you can overexert yourself and do strenuous work whenever you want.]

[...Then.]

[You've overused it. I saw you on TV, running while carrying a chunk of metal.]

[......]

[Car accidents are this scary. Especially during childhood when your bones are growing. The aftereffects linger.]

"Yes."

*Why of all things? Why did I have to go on that family trip? What was it even for?*

*Why did something completely unrelated to my choices, efforts, and achievements keep popping up and forcing me to give up, unable to do anything?*

Any person would have felt a spark of such resentment, but Ryu Chungwoo couldn't tell the members.

"Has anything impressive or frightening happened recently that might have reminded you of that car accident?"

"......"

In truth, he couldn't tell the counselor either. The person who shared the trauma of that car accident was right beside him.

His cousin, who hadn't been on that trip.

[I'm jealous.]

[Your parents are fine and you get to live doing everything you want... I'm jealous.]

Their traumas were polar opposites.

[Me talking about the aftereffects of the car accident... must have sounded like a spoiled complaint to you.]

He also felt a strange inability to tell that person this had all happened again because of the injury's aftereffects. He couldn't identify the feeling as consideration, discomfort, or guilt.

Nor could he mention how he sometimes wanted to snap back, "So should I have been grateful that I went and shattered my shoulder?" only to wake from sleep with a long sense of exhaustion and self-loathing.

"Then I'll see you again in three days."

In the end, Ryu Chungwoo finished his session with a prescription for tranquilizers, antidepressants, and a few supplements.

"Thank you."

The counselor didn't force more out of him, likely planning to proceed step by step.

*It'll be useless, though.*

"......"

On the way home, a long, silent breath escaped Ryu Chungwoo's lips. The warm air of early summer stung his skin, but he preferred the sting.

*I don't want it to be quiet.*

Ryu Chungwoo suddenly realized he didn't want to go into the dorm. Sitting still in an empty place was now more agonizing than just feeling restless.

*But I don't want to go to my parents' house either.*

The thought of their concern, of being told to rest without worry, felt like it was clogging his throat.

However, Ryu Chungwoo showed no sign of this. He just continued on his way with a weary expression and arrived at the dorm.

*It'll be empty.*

Even the manager wouldn't be there at this time.

He silently opened the door.

"You're back?"

"Hyung, you're here~"

Words poured out as the sounds of daily life spread from beyond the open door.

"......"

Ryu Chungwoo froze for a moment.

The members who had turned to the entrance or cheerfully raised their arms in greeting casually dispersed, returning to their own activities.

Kim Raebin, who had been crouched with his nose buried in a laptop, belatedly raised his head and exclaimed brightly.

"Have you had breakfast? I had a dried persimmon blended with milk!"

"It's Moondae's recipe. I'm gonna have one too."

"It's easy. Make it yourself."

"Aww... OK," Cha Eugene conceded dejectedly.

Park Moondae seemed to notice Ryu Chungwoo. He gave the Leader a slight nod before slumping back onto a kitchen chair and scrolling through his smartphone.

Relaxed.

Ryu Chungwoo found himself asking, "What about your schedule?"

"The main photographer caught the flu, so it was postponed," Park Moondae replied without looking up from his smartphone.

"So we moved the shoot to the day after tomorrow. If you have time, you should come too. It's our own photoshoot, so the timing isn't specific."

"......"

"If you don't want to, that's fine."

"No."

"Yes. I'll take that as you're coming with us."

It was fast and concise.

Slightly flustered, Ryu Chungwoo started toward his room with the medicine bag but was met by a beaming Seon Ahyeon.

"Um, the prescription... did you get it?"

"Yeah."

"Ah, I hope it's a good fit and works well for you! Um, and... I heard that taking it consistently is important, so I tried my best to stick to it."

"...I see."

"Yes!"

Seon Ahyeon kindly explained what to tell the counselor if the medicine didn't work, then cheered "Fighting!" and walked past.

"......"

Somehow, their reactions seemed to release the tension in his shoulders. Ryu Chungwoo hesitated, stopping in his tracks.

It was then.

"Ah, Ryu Chungwoo."

This time, Bae Sejin, who was watching TV in blue-light-blocking glasses, gently tapped his back.

"You... you must not be in a good mood. Instead of just counseling, you should go out for a change of pace."

"......"

Ryu Chungwoo's brow furrowed slightly in a troubled expression.

"I never said I wasn't in a good mood."

"What are you talking about? There's no way you'd feel good about taking a break from activities."

"......"

"Let's go blow off some steam!"

"Ooh~ Sejin... after following Chungwoo around to exercise so much, your stamina really isn't what it used to be."

"Me, me, me! I'm going!"

"Are you seeking new inspiration for the album through a change of pace? A truly excellent choice..."

In an instant, the dorm filled with voices shouting jeers, interruptions, and debates about joining in.

"But I don't want to go hiking."

"Ah. Me neither."

"Chungwoo! Let's go somewhere other than hiking! Let's go shoot guns instead!"

"The American is talking about shooting."

"He must mean target shooting!"

The commotion, unrelated to his own problems, wasn't bad. Ryu Chungwoo found himself nodding.

"YESSSS. Then are we going shooting? I'll go change. Wait 10 minutes."

"......"

*But... what in the world is all this?*

~~~

"Something happened with Ryu Chungwoo!"

"......"

"Park Moondae, you joined us thirty minutes later than scheduled? Something definitely happened!"

That guy was sharp as a ghost.

After talking with Ryu Chungwoo, I had belatedly joined them in the waiting room, where Bae Sejin almost grabbed my collar.

Of course, even if he hadn't, I had something to say. I had thought a lot on the way here about the best thing to do.

*First... I'll leave out all the stuff about him suffering while monitoring himself and all that.*

Talking about Ryu Chungwoo's detailed situation would be uncomfortable and just make everyone more uneasy.

I had to get to the other core issue.

Us.

"We've been mistaken about something."

"...?"

About the stance we had been taking all this time.

"What is it?"

"......"

I pressed my eyes shut.

"I think we've been making it too obvious that we're being considerate of Chungwoo."

"......"

A brief silence fell.

"Huh?"

"I-isn't it the o-opposite...?"

"No."

Think about it.

"I think it was extremely unnatural and burdensome."

"...!"

"Think back on it. We didn't say it out loud, but it was like we were screaming that we were being considerate."

The members' mouths fell open.

*Yeah, I almost bit my tongue thinking about it on the way over. It became obvious in hindsight.*

"Wait... you don't think calling you guys to come quickly when Ryu Chungwoo woke up, was that it?"

"And buying the gamjatang that Chungwoo likes was probably the final confirmation, right?"

"I was right. It would've been better to just say we bought it because Chungwoo likes it!"

"...The excuse for canceling this week's music show too?"

"That one was really obvious."

Right now, Ryu Chungwoo seemed confused about what he wanted to do. Everyone around him was acting like, 'I'm doing my best to be careful and considerate of you.'

He had fallen into an endless loop of pressure, feeling he had to be considerate of us rather than his own feelings.

*It might be touching for some, but for others, it could be a suffocating burden that makes them want to scream, 'Ah, for fuck's sake, just stop!'...*

Our attempt to be considerate without making a show of it had backfired. It made things painfully obvious and put even more pressure on him.

Finally, the members, who had viscerally realized this fact, asked themselves.

"Why... did we act like this?"

"That's because..." Keun Sejin summed it up simply. "Whenever we had a problem, Chungwoo always handled it maturely for us."

"......"

"People naturally want to do the same in return. Honestly, is there any member here who hasn't felt grateful to him at least once?"

That was right.

There wasn't a single person on this team who hadn't experienced Ryu Chungwoo subtly helping them with a worry or problem.

He wasn't loud about it. He was straightforward.

Everyone had thought it was great when he calmly offered a word or took action, whether it was comfort or help.

*...Me too.*

[Is this a situation where you need help?]

That guy had good sense, in a different way from Keun Sejin. I suppressed a groan.

"So we wanted to do even better for him."

Then, when Ryu Chungwoo's mental state collapsed, everyone panicked. We tried to act similarly to how he had helped us, but with even more care.

Since we all did it, we failed to notice each other and got completely absorbed.

"I knew it."

"Be quiet, Cha Eugene."

Bae Sejin groaned and pressed his eyes shut.

"...He might've had a harder time because he felt like we were uncomfortable with him."

"......"

"It's right to treat him more honestly and casually."

"Yeah."

"You guys can't pretend to be nonchalant. That's the problem."

"Honestly, you're not good at it either."

"What?"

The bickering members soon calmed down. Thanks to that, I was also able to speak calmly.

"From now on, it'd be better to just cheer for him honestly. Whether Chungwoo can get on stage or not is honestly up to him."

"......"

"And there's probably no need to add things like, 'It's okay to rest'."

"That makes sense."

The members nodded.

Bae Sejin let out a deep sigh.

"Then... let's stop talking amongst ourselves like this behind his back. It looks like we're plotting something."

"You're the one who started it."

"Th-that's why I'm the one saying we should stop!"

Through that process, this was what TeSTAR chose to do.

Be mindful of making Ryu Chungwoo happy and comfortable, but don't obsess over it.

*As long as we're not whining about how hard things are for us, it's okay to say things like, 'We picked this because you like it'.*

Just don't approach it in a complicated way. Be upfront about what's not good.

And that was how we ended up dragging Ryu Chungwoo out to go shooting.

*It's a new hobby he picked up after debuting.*

Since he liked physical activities, shooting was a decent choice as long as he used a model with low recoil.

However, as he got out of the car and walked toward the shooting range, Ryu Chungwoo hesitated.

"It wouldn't be good if word gets out."

He worried people might say things like, 'How dare he go out to play when he's on hiatus for being sick?'.

Keun Sejin waved his hand cheerfully.

"Ah, that's fine! We didn't tell the company~ and this place is famous for being private where we can just be by ourselves..."

[Closed Today]

[Closed for personal reasons.]

We were screwed.

"You said they were open today!"

"No, it was definitely right... Aha, they only announced the closure on InHeart."

"......"

It was truly the age of social media.

When I turned my head, I saw Bae Sejin about to sneak a glance at Ryu Chungwoo.

*No. Dammit.*

*Just say we should call it a day and come back next time.*

I was about to quickly intervene.

"Um, excuse me, but there's a facility with a similar sport next to this one..."

"Let's go there!"

"Sounds good!"

"Ah, yes. Understood!"

Kim Raebin, who had quickly started a search, found a new destination.

*The guy who pulls his weight scores another point.*

Amidst everyone's enthusiastic agreement, he led us on a ten-minute drive. We then realized why he had called it a 'similar sport'.

"......"

"This is the place!"

That rabbit from Wonderland.

The place Kim Raebin brought us to was an archery range.

"...No way."

It was shooting, alright. Except it used a bow instead of a gun.

The very thing Ryu Chungwoo had quit due to his injury's aftereffects.

*Is this... a real disaster?*

*Did he really pick the number one thing that would remind him of his injury, just like that?*

A chill ran down my spine. I turned and saw Kim Raebin's slightly tense face.

He wasn't panicking or looking like he had just realized his mistake. He wasn't about to bow his head in apology.

Instead, his expression was somewhat determined.

"......"

Hmm.

"Should we go in?"

"Ah, yes."

I silently followed Ryu Chungwoo, who walked calmly.

~~~

The archery cafe, located in a secluded spot, was very quiet on a weekday afternoon.

*I wonder if they run this as a hobby.*

The owners weren't particularly interested in celebrities. TeSTAR, wearing sporty hats and masks, just looked like a university club gathering.

Like that, TeSTAR went out to the fenced outdoor field without any problems.

The targets were visible in the distance, with the shooting line right in front.

"They said since it's outdoors, we can even shoot from 40M."

"Oh, even at the Idol Athletics Championships, the max was 20M."

Ryu Chungwoo quietly stood at the shooting line. It wasn't a professional line, just old red and blue stickers on the straw-like ground.

The members each held a bow, handling them awkwardly. Still, it wasn't their first time. Their hands moved with the familiarity of having held them on broadcasts before.

They even asked each other questions.

"Ah~ so in archery, is having a strong one-handed pulling strength the most important thing?"

"Not necessarily," Ryu Chungwoo replied, habitually touching an arrow's nock to check it.

"You pull the string with your right hand, but your left hand supports it."

The hand that pushes the bow, the one that acts as a support, has a very important and definite role.

"It's to hold the target steady."

Unwavering composure.

"Strength and stability. Being able to use both is the beginning of archery."

A sport of balance.

If the pulling force is excessive, the arrow's direction is more likely to be off. The role of the hand holding the bow was to suppress and control that.

In the end, archery is a tug-of-war of capabilities between the right and left hands.

"In that case, are the eyes also an important factor? To aim accurately, it seems like getting the position right would be important."

"Well... surprisingly, that's not always the case."

Ryu Chungwoo held the bow firmly, turning it to check its level.

"It's more about feel than sight."

*If I shoot like this, it will hit.*

*If I shoot from around here, it will hit.*

Position and timing were all adjusted by an archer's individual sense. Some said that was what separated a pro from an amateur.

Whether you have that sense.

"Actually, there was a senior who had such bad eyesight they couldn't even see the target well."

"That's amazing, for real."

Bow tip, stabilizer, string.

As Ryu Chungwoo continued his inspection, he reflexively caught a bow another member was about to drop.

"Ah."

"Are you okay?"

"Yes, I-I'm okay...!"

It was Seon Ahyeon. He had almost dropped the bow when he tried to draw it, his strength not quite matching.

With his good reflexes, Seon Ahyeon quickly caught the bow, but he seemed hesitant.

So Ryu Chungwoo moved reflexively, as if to demonstrate.

"You should place your feet a bit wider apart."

He moved his feet. When he placed them in their usual position, the posture came naturally.

Towards the red circle up ahead.

"And... like this."

Ryu Chungwoo looked at the target and drew his arm back.

For the first time in a very long time.
