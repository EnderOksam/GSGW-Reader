# Chapter 616

From a very young age, adults often told Ryu Chungwoo that he was truly born blessed.

[He's so young, but he's already so handsome and sharp. The baby takes after his mom and dad, he's so blessed. Oh my~ Isn't it wonderful?]

This narrative followed him throughout his life. Only the tone changed depending on his environment.

[Hey, I should start calling you Hyung-nim. You're fucking good.]

[Ah, you should call Chungwoo 'hyung', you dumbass, haha.]

[I heard Ryu Chungwoo was selected for the national team? That bastard really has it all.]

Sometimes, it even became a subtle attack.

[Honestly, he was born like that. Isn't it weirder if he*can't*do it?]

[Isn't it kinda lame that he chose archery with that body? It's not like soccer or baseball. It's all about the gear for that guy, you know?]

Ryu Chungwoo never placed much meaning on those opinions. He felt no rebellious urge to question them, nor did he feel any excitement or arrogance from thinking he was superior.

*What's the point? It's not like anything would change.*

His life was a simple one. He did what needed to be done each day, enjoyed the good things, and quickly overcame or let go of the bad.

This easygoing disposition naturally made Ryu Chungwoo's position even more solid. A person unshaken by the noise around them is attractive anywhere.

Thus, he lived diligently, feeling little excitement and receiving little influence from the various meddlings and prejudices.

He was equipped with the mental strength, will, talent, and diligence to break through adversity. He was able to run without hesitation toward what he wanted to achieve throughout his life.

Until that incident.

[Chungwoo-ya! Our Chungwoo's arm... Ah, please, please...]

"......"

Ryu Chungwoo blinked his eyes open. He must have dozed off. He lay on his bed, his back damp with a cold sweat.

*I should take a shower.*

He rose from his spot, his mind still dazed.

CLACK.

He reflexively snatched the falling smartphone he had left beside him.

"......"

The room was silent. His roommate, Cha Eugene, was out. The football that had been carelessly tossed on the adjacent bed was also gone.

*He took that thing with him again.*

Cha Eugene never let that thing out of his hands. Even on the days they were on an indefinite break due to the controversy, he would suddenly disappear without a word.

"No."

*That isn't now.*

Ryu Chungwoo stopped in his tracks. Cold water seemed to pour over his head, though he hadn't even entered the shower. It felt like things were getting mixed up.

A past that no longer existed.

"......"

He stood there motionlessly for a moment, lost in unspeakable thoughts, then heavily moved his feet again.

*Just do what needs to be done. If I just do what I have to do... I'll snap out of it at some point.*

After washing up with cold water in the attached bathroom, he came out to find someone in the living room.

"...! Ryu Chungwoo."

Bae Sejin brightened up. He had been reading on his e-book reader with the TV on for no one.

It was a short morning break after they had hastily finished a pre-recording for a music show without an audience at dawn.

"Did you get some rest?"

Ryu Chungwoo nodded.

"What about the others?"

"Ah,"

Bae Sejin hesitated a few times, then asked as if he had practiced.

"Well, it's lunchtime. Should we... order something? Is there anything you want to eat?"

*Is he planning to bring up the main topic once the mood lightens?*

Just imagining it washed a wave of premature fatigue over him.

"......"

This wasn't familiar. He couldn't just accept others' goodwill at face value. He had to guess their intentions beforehand. He used to just accept help if he needed it, but recently, he couldn't. Strangely.

Ryu Chungwoo just shook his head stoically. A refusal without conversation.

"Ah, okay..."

Bae Sejin quietly acquiesced. A faint sense of guilt brushed past Ryu Chungwoo at the sight, but he saw no point in expressing it.

Bae Sejin soon made a quiet phone call.

"Hyung."

Not long after, the other members rushed into the house, plastic bags dangling from their hands.

"We heard you both haven't had lunch yet, so we bought some."

They brought gamjatang. The members didn't mention buying something their hyung particularly liked. They just said they wanted to eat something hot.

*Are they doing this because they think I'd feel burdened if they were too obvious?*

Perhaps the act of guessing was so unfamiliar that it was even more exhausting.

Ryu Chungwoo silently helped set the table and sat down.

"Let's eat~"

The thought that they should have been hastily eating a portable meal in the car never left his mind. He felt he should confirm if the members had left the dorm for another show or other business.

He didn't want to ask. However, he had to do what he had to do.

"We have a performance schedule this afternoon."

"......"

At Ryu Chungwoo's words, the sounds at the dinner table momentarily ceased. Everyone seemed to think before reacting.

"Yes, but they're all studio shoots anyway, no live broadcasts. This is why WeTube is so great."

"Right, right."

The point they wanted to emphasize was not that it wasn't a live broadcast, but that it was a studio shoot.

That there would be no audience.

It was a signal that it was okay if he couldn't perform again, just like what happened at dawn.

"......"

Still, if he froze on stage one or two more times, word would definitely get out. That was the nature of controversy.

The stories were already circulating.

"......"

*At times like this, did I used to say thank you and practice hard?*

Ryu Chungwoo repeated to himself that he didn't know.

*I don't know.*

***

"What should I do? It's awkward to offer comfort, and it's also awkward to just bluntly ask if he's sick somewhere."

"......"

"I don't know. No..."

Bae Sejin was practically out of his mind. His attempt to have a heart-to-heart with Ryu Chungwoo while they were alone in the dorm had sunk.

*I forgot this guy is the type to agonize over his many thoughts...*

He charged headfirst into problems of right and wrong, but he treated someone else's mental state like a minefield, running simulation after simulation.

*He's being more cautious because he's also struggled with mental issues himself.*

It was probably even more so because Ryu Chungwoo seemed to have slightly recovered.

I pressed my brow and recalled.

*He was fine again during today's recording.*

Even if we were to skip next week's music show, suddenly pulling out starting this week was impossible due to many practical issues.

To cancel a schedule set just a day or two in advance required a proper reason, and we didn't have a doctor's note. An official announcement was also impossible.

So, we reluctantly proceeded at dawn, excluding the audience with the excuse of 'safety issues'.

[We're starting the shoot!]

During the performance, Ryu Chungwoo calmly carried out his parts as if nothing was wrong. However, during the breaks, it was painfully obvious that the guy was sinking.

Cold sweat. Silence.

...It was touch-and-go.

*Still, it probably wasn't noticeable on camera.*

That was a relief. By now, the fans were probably trying to bury this issue themselves. The fact that we proceeded with the stage recording looked like a signal that there were no physical problems.

[I think it's friction with the broadcast station because it's T1..ㅋㅋㅠㅠ sigh]

[Our boys are performing perfectly fine on stage, please stop with the rumors and bullshit]

[They said it's a safety issue, maybe it's related to sensitive personal info of someone in the audience so they're not mentioning it, please take a hintㅠ]

Of course, since eyewitness accounts had already spread, it was impossible to completely block the rumor that Ryu Chungwoo had developed a panic disorder.

*...We just have to resolve this within the week.*

Then we could shift the public's attention with pre-filmed content. People would assume that what happened was due to broadcast station or company circumstances.

That is, if it gets resolved.

*Fuck.*

I held back a groan. I recalled my theory about Ryu Chungwoo's state, which I hadn't yet dared to tell the others.

*Is that... something that can be resolved in a short period?*

Bae Sejin groaned at a similar timing.

"If it were me, and things weren't going well, I think I'd feel pressured if people were obviously worrying about me..."

"Ah~ That's something that varies from person to person."

There was a mix of responses. One guy said he'd work harder out of guilt, another said he'd be touched, and another said it would give him strength.

But no one could be certain.

"...Since we don't know the reason, we don't know what the right answer is."

The conclusion was that they didn't know how Ryu Chungwoo would react. Everyone tacitly agreed.

"......"

*In the end... is figuring out the exact reason the priority?*

*I was trying to avoid it because it felt like it could cross a line.*

There was no choice now. We were out of time. I knew sending Ryu Chungwoo to counseling wasn't an environment where he could speak honestly.

*Then I'll just have to pair him with someone he can be honest with.*

I finally decided to implement Plan B. I called to the guy on the veranda.

"Chungwoo."

"...!"

"Cha Eugene is calling for you."

***

With Park Moondae watching, Ryu Chungwoo opened his bedroom door.

"OK. Hyung, come in."

Inside, his roommate beckoned with his hand. The football was back in its place.

However, Cha Eugene was not alone. Kim Raebin sat on the floor with a tense expression.

THUD.

As soon as Park Moondae closed the door, Cha Eugene spoke.

"We don't have time, so I'll speak quickly."

When Ryu Chungwoo looked at him, Cha Eugene propped his chin on one hand. His eyes glistened.

"From now on, I'm Bad Cha Eugene."

"...?"

For a moment, Ryu Chungwoo suspected Cha Eugene was joking.

"Eugene."

"Nope. I'm talking. You listen first."

Cha Eugene frowned. With his next words, Ryu Chungwoo understood what 'Bad Cha Eugene' meant.

"Ryu Chungwoo, do you remember when we had our last activities together?"

"......"

His tone had changed. It was slightly cynical and cutting. Fluent Korean. It was a way of speaking Ryu Chungwoo had heard many times before.

I had suggested that if Ryu Chungwoo really had a problem during the STier days, talking with someone who knew him back then would be most effective.

Cha Eugene had willingly accepted the suggestion and taken on today's role.

[OK. I'll do it.]

He was now speaking in the exact same way he did back in STier, as if the Cha Eugene from back then stood right in front of him.

Cha Eugene threw the ball he was holding behind him.

"We didn't renew our group contract and each went to find our own thing to do. I went to America, and Kim Raebin went to the military."

"......"

"What did you do then?"

Silence.

However, Cha Eugene didn't bat an eye.

"Not answering isn't an option. Don't think you can get away with not answering after always making us answer."

Kim Raebin's face turned pale, but he didn't try to stop him. It was already agreed upon.

Ryu Chungwoo opened his mouth without a bitter laugh.

"I made you answer?"

"Sure. So you have to answer right away too. Because I always answered well."

"You did?"

"...!"

"You shouldn't make up stories that didn't happen, Cha Eugene."

"Oh. [That's quite aggressive.]"

In an instant, tension filled the room.

"......"

Amidst Kim Raebin's breathing, Park Moondae stood quietly in the back as if he wasn't there.

Cha Eugene unabashedly opened his mouth again.

"Are you not going to answer my question?"

"......"

"Fine. Then answer the fan."

A fan?

"Moondae!"

"...!"

When Cha Eugene called, Park Moondae, who had been leaning in the back, looked at him without surprise.

Ryu Chungwoo met his calm eyes. A heavy pressure settled in his chest.

"After STier's activities ended, there was no news from me or Kim Raebin, and Moondae was disappointed that there was no news from you either, Ryu Chungwoo."

"......"

"Why didn't you continue your activities? You wanted to keep doing KPOP. That was..."

"Because it seemed like a physical problem."

"...!"

"That's right."

Perhaps, Ryu Chungwoo thought, he had expected this conversation from the moment he entered the room. From the moment Park Moondae had pointed to his left arm, and from the moment he saw this group of people.

Ryu Chungwoo glanced at his own left upper body, then muttered.

"They said that due to excessive fatigue accumulated in my shoulder, it would be difficult to perform dances at the previous choreography level."

"......"

"The pain started about half a year before the contract ended."

Kim Raebin barely managed to ask.

"A-Are you okay now..."

"Yeah. I'm fine now. You guys saw it too."

His physical examination results.

"......"

"That's all."

"Yes...?"

Ryu Chungwoo continued to explain.

"My body is fine now, but I think a habit from back then remains, and I reflexively hesitate to use my body. I think my body is probably unconsciously afraid of getting injured again."

"That's..."

"Once I recognized it as the period when I had quit my activities, the symptoms started getting worse and worse over time."

The explanation flowed like a river, logical and without any confusing parts.

And so, Park Moondae realized.

*...So it wasn't simple avoidance.*

Ryu Chungwoo wasn't trying to avoid the situation or pretend he didn't know the problem.

"I couldn't seek medical help because I couldn't explain the cause. Because it was something that never happened."

He had already gone through his own worries and agony to arrive at a complete diagnosis. It just seemed he couldn't bring himself to declare it so openly.

"That's all."

A sentence that declared, 'My career is over.'

"......"

"......"

Ryu Chungwoo muttered.

"What can I do?"

The very act of saying those words seemed shameful to him. It was likely because he already anticipated the answer.

"......"

There was no solution.

And the most important period of his career was slipping away mercilessly.

*Fuck.*

Park Moondae thought that the fact this guy knew it so well made it even worse.
