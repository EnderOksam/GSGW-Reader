# Chapter 482

I once had a theory. The 'System Update' had reverted Cha Eugene to his persona from his time in STier.

I didn't know the exact mechanism, but the timing and circumstances made it seem plausible. The Status Window had said the Update would take 30 days. Everyone naturally expected to spend another three or four weeks with STier Cha Eugene.

However.

A completely unexpected pop-up suddenly appeared.

[Update Complete!]

[Achieved Early]

[Applying '■■■ (ver.2 Beta)']

ZZZING!

Color flooded back into the hologram, which had been just basic lines and fonts. The UI returned as a brilliant, game-like System framework. More refined graphics formed a translucent window.

[Complete!]

"......"

I didn't know why, but it was over. I knew it instinctively.

I turned and looked at the guy watching the chaotic kart race on TV. Then I opened my mouth.

"...Cha Eugene."

The early Update meant the strange phenomenon affecting Cha Eugene was also over...

"Yes?"

"...?"

*...It's not over?*

Cha Eugene seemed perfectly fine. He rested his chin on his hand with no strange reaction, looking exactly like STier Cha Eugene.

When I didn't respond, he just shrugged and turned back to the TV.

"It's fine. You can tell me when you remember."

"......"

*Wait a minute, was my basic reasoning wrong from the start? Was this completely unrelated to the Update?*

My thoughts were about to get more complicated.

TIRING-!

"...!"

Another pop-up appeared.

[Reactivate 'For Business Use'?]

"......"

*Ah, I see.*

I pressed a hand against my eyelids. I even had a choice about when to do it.

***

Now, let's see.

*If I don't reactivate it and just cancel the System, Cha Eugene might never return, but if I reactivate this damn System right now... setting aside my own unease...*

*Will this Cha Eugene... disappear immediately?*

*...Probably.*

The right thing to do was obviously to send him back quickly and bring back the original Cha Eugene.

But now that I could make the choice, I found myself debating whether to tell him. If I just told him to his face, 'You can be restored now, so go back,' would it all resolve cleanly without any mental damage?

*...His memories are of Cha Eugene right after STier disbanded.*

We had only just started to connect. Was there any guarantee he wasn't looking forward to enjoying the stage here at least once or twice more?

*Hoo.*

I held back a sigh and sat on the bed's edge. This problem had no clean answer.

*For now... everyone's not in good condition, so I'll put it on hold.*

I would decide tomorrow.

"Good night, Moondae."

"Yes. You too, hyung."

Even after Ryu Chungwoo, who had been in a neck-and-neck race with Bae Sejin, fell asleep in his own bed, I couldn't fall asleep right away.

An hour passed like that.

"......"

My consciousness was a mess, probably because of the medicine I took for my body aches.

*I knew this would happen.*

On top of that, the damn pop-up was so bright I felt like I needed an eye mask.

I finally grumbled and got out of bed.

"Hoo."

I went out to the living room for some water.

"You're not sleeping?"

"...!"

I found Cha Eugene still lying on the living room sofa.

"I slept too much during the day."

"That wasn't sleeping. That was being sick."

He had a sharp tongue. For someone who had volunteered as a caregiver all day, he looked far too energetic for past midnight.

I chuckled and got water from the kitchen.

*Why isn't he going to his room... Ah, is he uncomfortable with Bae Sejin?*

"Want to switch rooms?"

"No thanks."

The guy answered firmly and stretched as he muttered.

[I was just... you know, when you're talking with a friend? I just wanted to stay up a little longer.]

Alright.

"Did you talk with Kim Raebin?"

"Yeaap."

He then said something about not spilling the tea, which I guessed was American slang. It probably meant they weren't talking behind our backs.

"I don't know that kind of slang well."

"Sorry, but it looks like you understood, Moondae?"

"Through context clues."

"Got it."

Cha Eugene replied casually, chuckled, and lay back down on the sofa. He looked the most comfortable he had since arriving here.

"......"

I drank my water, thinking briefly. I wondered if now was the right time.

Just then.

[Hey, should I take a guess?]

Cha Eugene used the sofa's rebound to shoot upright.

[Did you find a way to send me back right away, Moondae?]

"...!"

[Yep, I knew it.]

Cha Eugene grinned and set down the cola he was drinking.

[We've formed some kind of relationship, and everything seems to be going well, so why would you suddenly be looking at me and worrying?]

"......"

[The tables have turned, haven't they? Right?]

There was no getting out of this.

"......"

I sat across from the sofa. It was better to explain things properly than to complicate the situation.

*Should I subtly leave the option open that it's okay to fill the 30 days if he wants?*

I had just had that thought.

But before I could speak, Cha Eugene grinned first.

"Why are you hesitating, hyung?"

"...!"

[Don't get confused. I'm not your team member.]

Cha Eugene said firmly.

[Oh, but I am your KPOP idol. Right?]

He added it like a joke, but I understood the nuance.

*He's telling me not to pity him unnecessarily.*

He was telling me not to dare think about giving this STier Cha Eugene more stages, using TeSTAR's fame like a boosting service. Or that he would miss it and couldn't give it up.

No fan would want that kind of treatment. That was probably part of it, but... well, that was a really shitty thing to do in the first place.

"......"

So I just answered that way, but I had to add one thing.

"That said, I was happy to see you perform after so long."

"......"

STier Cha Eugene was silent for a while. Silence fell in the living room, lit only by a single indirect lamp.

"Maybe... hmm, we might meet again."

Cha Eugene shrugged. I didn't ask how that would be possible.

"I'll show you an even cooler performance then."

"...Yeah."

I just agreed.

"Great."

Cha Eugene smiled and crossed his arms. It was a silent signal.

"As for goodbyes."

"I've already said them."

The guy shrugged.

"I just said them to my friend, and I apologized to the others, so I'm satisfied, and..."

And?

"I'll say it to you now, hyung."

He held out his hand.

His hand wasn't positioned for a handshake. It was for a high five or interlocking fingers, where palms meet. It was for a fansign.

"Thank you. My fan."

"......"

I held out my hand too, not angled for a handshake, but to meet his head-on.

"Thanks. For showing me a great performance."

"You're welcome."

We shared a short high five.

[Reactivate 'For Business Use'?]

"......"

*Yes.*

WHOOSH!

Light burst from the pop-up. The sounds of an update, new construction sound effects, and notifications flashed quickly and loudly, as if desperate for attention.

But I didn't do something as stupid as looking at it. Instead, I saw him off.

"Bye."

Cha Eugene didn't complain of any pain, but he blinked as if slightly dizzy. Then... he leaned back on the sofa and slowly fell asleep.

Finally, I could hear his steady breathing.

"......"

I quietly went into his room and took out a blanket without waking the sleeping Bae Sejin. After covering the guy on the sofa, I brought my own bedding from my room and laid it on the living room floor.

"......"

Sleep came very slowly.

***

The next morning.

"Hey."

"......"

I opened my eyes. I immediately recognized the voice.

*Cha Eugene.*

...I turned toward the voice.

There, right by my head, was Cha Eugene, holding a large pot with both hands...

Wait.

*...A pot?*

"......"

What was this random situation?

At that moment, Cha Eugene, who stared at me with wide, alert eyes, shot up and shouted.

"Moondae is awake!"

"Pa, Park Moondae!"

"He's awake?"

A frenzy of voices called out. What was going on? The members rushed over with brightened faces, as if they had found a lifeline, and started rambling.

"Cha Eugene is back!"

Yeah.

"B-But... he, cooked!"

He... did?

"What are you talking about..."

"I'm making breakfast."

Cha Eugene ambitiously opened the pot's lid and unceremoniously shoved it in my face.

*Hey, come on.*

Anyway, this smell and appearance...

*...Dakbokkeumtang.*

That's what it was. A really fiery breakfast choice for people who had body aches yesterday...

No, wait a minute.

More importantly, his memory was back, but instead of a tearful reunion, he made dakbokkeumtang and shoved it in a sleeping guy's face?

*This is chaotic.*

But Cha Eugene was consistent. Really, consistent... He even bombarded my brain with new information, giving it no time to rest.

"This is a token of my apology and gratitude! A lot has happened until now."

What?

*Wait, does that mean...*

"You have your memories?!"

"I have some. Bad Cha Eugene enjoyed Water Bomb. I want to do it again."

"......"

"Members, let's eat! Breakfast!"

Bae Sejin was thrown into confusion, but Cha Eugene briskly called everyone to the dining table. The dining table then fell into even greater chaos.

"Eugene, are you okay? Your memories?"

"I'm healthy. I have memories."

First, Cha Eugene's condition was determined to be completely normal. Apparently, he woke at dawn with natural memories of the past few days. After some deep thought, he decided he had to make breakfast.

"Bad Cha Eugene is me too, so I have to take responsibility. One meal isn't enough. So I made this too."

"Eugene..."

However, it seemed the nickname for STier Cha Eugene had completely solidified as 'Bad' in his mind.

Just when I thought a tearful reunion would follow.

"Ch-Cha Eugene!"

"Kim Raebin is a traitor."

"......"

"You told my secret!"

"I didn't tell!"

"You told the mean Cha Eugene!"

"You just said with your own mouth that you're the same person! It doesn't count!"

Hmm. It was a mess.

However, I heard a slightly emotional mumble from the side.

"It's been a while..."

"......"

I decided to leave Bae Sejin, who was finally reunited with his dear roommate, and just eat.

When I took a bite of the dakbokkeumtang.

*Hmm?*

"......"

"Wh-What's wrong, Moondae...?"

"No."

Cha Eugene suddenly cut in.

"Moondae, is my cooking bad?"

"No, it's delicious."

"Hehe."

Right. It was tasty.

However.

*This isn't just dakbokkeumtang.*

It was Chicken Soup. The taste was almost identical to when I salvaged Cha Eugene's failed Chicken Soup on W Live by turning it into dakbokkeumtang.

On top of that.

*I don't think Cha Eugene has ever made dakbokkeumtang before.*

And the one who was good at making Chicken Soup... was STier Cha Eugene.

"......"

I looked at Cha Eugene, who was still bickering with Kim Raebin.

*No way.*

*Does he have... all of his memories from his STier days?*
