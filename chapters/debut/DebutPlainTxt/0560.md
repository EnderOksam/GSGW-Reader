# Chapter 560

I had a question from the very beginning.

*Why is Cheongryeo trying to get information about the acting world from me?*

From a rookie actor, no less.

A guy who restarted one hundred times surely secured a better source than me.

So I guessed he might be after one specific piece of information, something only obtainable at this exact time.

However, that wasn't it.

*Information wasn't his goal in the first place.*

"You could have neglected your schedules while claiming to look for information, but you took the time to properly prepare for and answer every single interview, every single promotional outlet. It means you didn't waste a single moment in the spotlight."

"......"

"It means you were born with great talent and a good attitude, Gunwoo."

Cheongryeo met my gaze without blinking.

"And it seems the one attending university is the same."

He wanted to see my attitude.

He supported my acting career to see how well I would adapt to this industry and whether I would pull my weight and perform well.

And crucially...

*To check on the university student Ryu Gunwoo without my unnecessary interference.*

The way he schemes is as infuriating as ever.

I replied in a low voice.

"...Why would a guy doing just fine in college want to become an idol? The me of this period has no interest in idols."

"For someone who's not interested, you were pretty dedicated to taking pictures."

"......"

"Some of the photos of me in the dorm were taken by you, Gunwoo."

He even found out I was a fansite photographer selling photos.

"I do it because it makes money."

"With your academic background, aren't there more ways to make money? Why would you do something so unprofitable like running around event venues to take pictures of idols?"

"It pays better than you think."

"Then it's an honor to introduce you to a job that pays even 'better'."

Cheongryeo replied nonchalantly.

I wasn't surprised.

If words could persuade him, I never would have needed to smash this bastard's head in handcuffs.

"......"

I shut my mouth.

It was at that moment.

"Just a moment. I'd like to say something, if I may."

Judan raised his hand and interjected.

"Why are you telling us about this plan?"

I lifted my head.

"The moment you return to the past, everyone's experiences and memories will disappear, except for yours, sunbae."

It was true.

"There's a high probability we won't remember either. So for you to tell us... is it for some emotional factor?"

"......"

That guy Judan looked calm, but his hand trembled slightly.

*He's trying hard.*

I suppressed a groan and answered.

"He's trying to see our reactions."

"Pardon?"

"He wants to check our reactions in this ridiculous situation and plan how to use us in the future."

In short, it was a human potential measuring device.

He didn't have to worry about side effects either, since we would forget everything anyway.

Judan's expression became an unreadable mix of admiration and fear.

"I see..."

"You're not guessing. You're certain, aren't you?"

"...!"

Cheongryeo grinned.

"It seems you and I knew each other in the time you lived in. Quite well, at that."

"......"

"First, you both seem very flustered. It would be good to calm down a little."

Cheongryeo said calmly.

"My conditions haven't changed. No one loses out. Things will only get better, and..."

He looked back at me quite kindly.

"Even if Gunwoo loses his body after I restart, don't worry. I won't delete you even if you remain as a Help."

How very gracious of him.

I wanted to be sarcastic, but Cheongryeo maintained his friendly demeanor.

"I'll release the album as promised, so don't worry about that either. There are still things I need to check and things I need to do."

However, that was all.

I predicted what this guy would say next.

"However, as you heard... even if the album is a success, staying here isn't a good decision for the group. The restart is a set course of action."

Cheongryeo tilted his head.

"We can't be satisfied when we can start over with better conditions."

Reset syndrome.

The habit of willingly restarting whenever better conditions arise.

This bastard, terrifyingly stuck in a phase the real Cheongryeo had already overcome, was disgustingly, utterly impossible to reason with.

*Fuck.*

I closed my eyes against the throbbing pain in the back of my head.

*Is there... a way to persuade him?*

"......"

There isn't.

This was different from the reality where the System passed from that bastard Cheongryeo to me, making him mistakenly believe I had the restart button.

Unlike in reality, where he had to persuade me to restart, in this past, he could restart whenever he pleased.

*I have no cards to negotiate with, fuck.*

"......"

Besides, a fundamental question started to surface.

From the very beginning.

*Why do I have to stop this guy?*

Why do I even need to negotiate?

Isn't it enough to just buy some time?

*This isn't the reality I live in, anyway.*

All I have to do is return to my reality.

After that, I don't care if this guy restarts or not. I was just flustered because the story of my university-student self from this timeline came up.

*What do I care what he does, whether he makes VTIC or not.*

It doesn't matter.

*Right.*

My head throbbed.

And... I was sick and tired of it.

*Why the fuck... do I have to keep dealing with this bastard every single time?*

How many times do I have to repeat a story I already know? How many times until I can escape this damn System's bullshit?

Heat rushed to my brain.

In a situation where I already didn't know what was going on, here I was, stupidly stuck again, gritting my teeth and trying to get out.

When I was in deep trouble myself, what luxury did I have to try and resolve misunderstandings between the VTIC guys?

*It was a stupid thing to do.*

I wanted to go back.

...There were too many things I couldn't give up. Too many reasons.

That's why I kept trying to look at the situation positively, which was so unlike me.

I believed there was a clue to return within the System. I believed I could somehow use it. I believed the pop-ups were really Keundal and the members...

However, if I looked at it really, truly realistically...

*There's nothing I can be sure of.*

Nothing has been proven.

Everything I've done so far to get back might have all been a useless waste of time.

Cold sweat ran down my spine.

I felt like I was being consumed.

"......"

This... damned fear that I wouldn't be able to return as TeSTAR's Park Moondae, that I'd be dragged along by this shitty situation forever.

*No.*

I gripped my thigh until the veins bulged.

A drop of cold sweat fell.

[A dancer with a noble mindset...]

"......"

Just then, a faint light shone above my head.

I raised my gaze.

Right beside me, the text in the pop-up twinkled like a star.

[A dancer with a noble mindset does his best.]

[A dancer with a noble mindset bestows a blessing upon you.]

[Trait: Status Abnormality Nullification]

In that moment, I began to separate from the murky confusion.

First, I took a deep breath.

*Hoo...*

I became aware of my body with my breathing, and then I became aware of the thoughts in my head.

*I was scared.*

I diagnosed my condition objectively.

I hadn't come to a realization about reality. I had simply fallen into a panic.

I tried my best to ignore it, but the extreme stress must have kept building in my subconscious until it passed a critical point.

*It's understandable.*

Let's not blame myself.

I gave myself some points for acknowledgment, a little objectively.

I tried my best without knowing a damn thing.

*I managed to drag things this far, didn't I?*

The terrible ideas and foul mood that had turned my mind to mud had not magically vanished.

The confusion remained. The fear remained.

It was just that I now had the strength to handle it.

As if my stamina had suddenly surged during a marathon, the will to gauge the distant finish line and run toward it filled my heart.

Grit.

Resilience.

Perhaps some of it was acquired from Seon Ahyeon's experiences, but there must also be an innate, qualitative difference.

[A dancer with a noble mindset struggles for you.]

Looking from that higher perspective, I could see the path I had walked, and my heart hardened.

*I don't think I could do any better if I did it all over again.*

As Cheongryeo's Help, as an illegal worker in America, as a debuting actor.

The conviction that I had done my best at every single moment solidified into faith in myself.

Then, I also started to think that there was no need to overreact to my current situation.

It wasn't about giving up. It was about simply accepting it.

*It's only natural that the Cheongryeo here has reset syndrome.*

Because this is a point in the past.

There's no need to be so surprised, flustered, or frustrated by comparing it to reality.

If anything, it's amazing that I managed to pass and become a member by starting from scratch with a guy who distrusts people due to his reset syndrome.

*Not that I ever wanted it.*

Well, I guess it means I'm a pretty useful guy. Let's just say that, as expected of a perennial A-lister, he recognized a top-tier idol.

*Right.*

If my stress hadn't passed the critical point, I would have normally thought so shamelessly.

*And then I'd rack my brain for the best solution.*

Isn't that what I've been doing every day since I started living as Park Moondae?

Finding an answer in a seemingly hopeless situation.

Courage and energy seemed to seep out from somewhere and permeate my brain and spine.

Like a resilient ball, my heart, my mental strength, bounced back.

[A dancer with a noble mindset exhales.]

*I can do this.*

I thought, looking up at the pop-up.

Now, with a firm heart, if I were to choose what to do right now...

I placed my hand on the table.

"Well... I appreciate you thinking so highly of me, but."

"Hm?"

"Take your time thinking about making me a member. I have something to do right now... so I'll be stepping away for a bit."

And I dove back into Cheongryeo's System.

To finish analyzing the terminal.
