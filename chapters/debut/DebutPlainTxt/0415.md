# Chapter 415

Kwon Heeseung had a rather creepy dream.

It was a strange configuration where some kind of alien created itself in virtual reality and was like an AI housekeeper.

In addition, there was a bizarre development in which all kinds of idol seniors appeared!

In the end, he woke up screaming in a place where the world collapsed in a spectacular way.

"Ahhhh!!"

However, he had no idea it would be this loud.

Kwon Heeseung opened his eyes in a cold sweat so much so that the airplane seat was soaked.

"Huh, huh..."

"Are you okay?"

A hand holding a drink appeared in front of him. Without even having time to think, Kwon Heeseung grabbed it and brought it to his mouth.

Gulp, gulp.

As the cool, smooth, sweet drink went down his throat, a rush surged all the way to his head.

He felt like he was fully hydrated for the first time in hundreds of days.

*Wow.*

He shook his head and finally came to his senses after emptying the entire liter of drink.

The person who handed him the energy drink was Park Moondae of TeStar, who was sitting across from him. Kwon Heeseung stuck out his tongue.

"Wow, really... Oh, maybe it's because I had a strange dream, but it feels like it's been a while since I've had a drink?"

"...Is that so?"

"Yes! I guess I had a nightmare. Well, it looks like there's something to do..."

Wait a minute. Now that he thought about it, why was he on a plane?

And the moment he made eye contact with VTIC's leader, the owner of the chartered plane, he remembered everything that had happened right before he fell asleep, as if seeing a flash.

The status window crashed.

Moondae-hyung was vomiting.

And...

"...Um, excuse me."

Kwon Heeseung asked while sweating profusely.

"It... It was a dream, right?"

"......"

Park Moondae made a strange expression while avoiding his gaze, which was rare, but soon answered with an expressionless expression.

"I think it's better to think of it as a dream."

"That means it was real...!?"

Kwon Heeseung held his face and screamed, but Park Moondae just kept his mouth shut.

This was because he roughly understood the situation.

*He chose not to remember the specifics.*

This was the choice Keundal gave him.

-Do you want to remember what happened here, or will you just forget it or vaguely remember it like a dream?

Kwon Heeseung must have chosen to treat it as a dream there. Park Moondae frowned inwardly, guessing clearly.

*... It must have been quite difficult.*

If so, compensation would be needed for Gold 2... who was Kwon Heeseung.

So, the moment he was about to open his mouth, Kwon Heeseung, who had finished thinking, was shocked and screamed first.

"...! Wait, if that's all real... Are we in a situation where Korea just collapsed and we escaped by plane?!"

"...There is nothing like that, and this is the LA Airport, which was our original destination."

"Phew."

He guessed it was something similar to a dream!

Kwon Heeseung let out a long sigh of relief. And Park Moondae held back a sigh and continued speaking in a different way.

"Anyway, since you got caught up in this and went through a hard time... Please at least get some compensation."

"What?"

"...!"

"One wish."

Park Moondae continued speaking without any agitation.

"I will grant whatever you request to the best of my ability. How about that?"

Oooh.

Kwon Heeseung instantly thought back on Park Moondae's performance so far.

⟨Idol Inc.⟩. From the chicken feet and driving the company crazy to creating his own label.

And he came to a conclusion.

"I will use it well."

"Okay."

Oh, yes.

The words 'A genie usually grants three wishes, so can you change it to three wishes?' came up in his throat, but he held it back.

He was a person who knew how to get around.

Kwon Heeseung, who had achieved the one-size-fits-all wish, finally relaxed by looking outside and checking out LA Airport.

*Wow.*

The day was clear. Even though the sunset sky was from a foreign country, it felt familiar and so beautiful that it brought tears to his eyes.

"......"

"You've worked hard."

"Hey, I was just dreaming."

Park Moondae did not reply, but he smiled slightly bitterly and looked at the setting sun.

It was quiet.

Kwon Heeseung was soaking in the quiet rest and inadvertently asked a question.

"Then, hyung... The system...?"

"I got rid of it."

"Okay."

No wonder it felt so cool. Even though it was a dream, it looked like he had a hard time.

*Ah, it's so satisfying.*

He sat down again, putting cheese, salt biscuits, and chocolate from the cart into his mouth.

"Huh? When did you eat this?"

"While you were sleeping."

"Wow, is it expensive?"

"I don't know."

Kwon Heeseung opened some new champagne and drank it while enjoying his leisure time.

The charter plane owner, who was reading a book, did not bother to comment or even look at them.

*Then, I can eat anything.*

Just when he was feeling optimistic, the sound began to return to the charter plane.

The sound of doors opening and people moving.

"The preparations are done."

Cheongryeo opened his mouth for the first time. When Kwon Heeseung turned his head, he actually saw the workers returning to their seats.

*Huh?*

Kwon Heeseung cautiously opened his mouth.

"Hyung, can we get off now?"

"No."

"...?"

"We also refueled at the airport."

Park Moondae laughed.

"We have to go back now."

"...!!"

No, they came all the way to LA just to leave like this? They should only return after playing for a bit!

Kwon Heeseung immediately thought of a thousand things to say in response, but he unconsciously kept his mouth shut.

Somehow... This was because he realized that he couldn't bear it anymore and wanted to go back.

To everyday life. To his home.

"Let's depart."

The three idols fastened their seat belts. The plane began to return to its departure point.

Tirik!

Footsteps.

A sign of seeing a loving family member.

"Kongi."

"Woof!"
