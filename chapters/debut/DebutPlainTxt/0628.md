# Chapter 628

Theme Park Attractions

Theme park attractions classically brought to mind rides enjoyable for the experience alone, from carousels to roller coasters.

However, a deeper look revealed attractions that combined stories with the experience.

Fairy tales, magic schools, villains, and heroes came to life from famous movies, animations, and games.

More attractions allowed users to step directly into the scenarios of well-known content, immersing themselves in the experience.

This trend grew from investments in technology and capital. It was also thanks to the massive success of theme parks owned by large content corporations that sold tons of merchandise.

"I never could have imagined this...!" Park Moondae's first fansite master screamed.

"C-calm down, Yeonju!"

The Space Dog headband she had diligently purchased wobbled on her head. It was merchandise from the SF hero movie where TeSTAR created an OST and made a cameo.

That's right. She was now in the perfect place to have bought and worn this headband.

Aaaaaah!! Whoaaa!

Screams from roller coasters echoed as she stood at the entrance of Dreamland, the land of dreams and fantasy.

Because of TeSTAR!

"But it looks so fun!"

Her friend's eyes sparkled beside her.

Her friend Minso, a recent graduate school graduate with a new job, had finally used two months of precious accumulated leave to create a four-day vacation.

Usually, this would mean a trip of three nights and four days, or four nights and five days at most.

"I'm so glad we came to the US!"

"...Yeah."

They had come all the way to Florida in the United States.

They had insanely thrown away a precious vacation day on an airplane without a second thought, all for a single attraction. It was truly insane fanaticism.

*...She might be even more intense than me.*

Minso did not know the dark underbelly of the internet, but she might be more incredible than her.

She had been thrilled when her friend became a fan of Moondae during ❬Idol Inc.❭. She had assumed Minso would just be a light fan for a while and then move on.

After all, Minso had been a graduate student and was not particularly interested in this kind of thing.

However, her friend was still cheering for the same idol with her after seven years. For some reason, a wave of emotion washed over her.

To think they would even travel together for fandom purposes. Park Moondae's first fansite master looked at her friend with new wonder.

"...?"

"Oh, it's nothing."

Saying her heart felt touched seemed out of place when they were about to dive into fandom activities at a theme park.

"It's that way. Hollywood Street!"

"Aha!"

They walked on with cheerful steps. Their destination was, of course, the TeSTAR attraction.

"Wow, the atmosphere..."

"It really is a land of dreams."

They had purposely rested after a day of city sightseeing on Sunday and came to the theme park on Monday, so it was not overwhelmingly crowded.

*Thank goodness.*

However, the area before the new attraction was much more crowded than they expected.

"Oh my god."

"It's like the stadium on a concert day..."

Fortunately, they did not have to wait indefinitely and could reserve a time to return later.

"They say we can book a Fast Track?"

"Let's enjoy the wait too!"

"O-okay."

And so, they headed straight into the massive attraction zone. A low-power sign flickered above broken streetlights and a crackling hologram.

The environment was decorated like a ruined office district. In the center, a black tunnel gaped open like a secret road from a spy movie.

"This is amazing."

"Wow..."

Their mouths fell open at the scale and quality. The two of them slowly walked forward.

This was not an entirely new attraction, however. It was an old, outdated classic attraction that had been refurbished with a new theme.

So why TeSTAR?

The fansite master did not know that TeSTAR had shared such a conversation, grinning as they snapped up the offer, but in any case, there had been speculation on the internet.

[Yes. They didn't want to attach one of their own currently successful movie or comic IPs, but they wanted to do a promotion when it was refurbished.]

[That's right.]

[Hmm~ ...So we're not turning it down?]

[Why? Why is TeSTAR showing up at an amusement park...?]

[Is the Limestone collaboration confirmed?]

[Was it an ad in TeSTAR's own content? But there was no PPL mark.]

[They already did a collab with a Limestone movie once, looks like they've properly established their position.]

[Crazy, so if I go there, I can experience the story from TeSTAR's own content myself?]

[No way, are they signing with the American label Limestone??]

[If it's Limestone... they must be rolling in money.]

[Infiltrate the abandoned company building]

[Become an employee of KIS]

The promotion ran from WeTube to TV commercials. For WeTube, they even created versions in various languages, not just English, for a global push.

The action seemed almost exhilarating.

*They're probably treating us better because the Glastonbury video blew up.*

And it was true. Limestone could not rashly entrust a permanent attraction to a K-pop idol group based on connections alone.

Their goal was likely to create buzz among K-pop fans, so they probably wanted to make a big, short-term impact and then pull out.

*Still, it's amazing.*

The fansite master's heart pounded anew at the unbelievable project.

Moreover, judging by the crowd and buzz, the seasonal concept worked perfectly. The slightly creepy, urban-legend-like atmosphere was a perfect fit for summer.

"Wow..."

The scorching summer heat cooled in the shade.

The two entered the huge, dark, concrete-themed tunnel. They swallowed hard at the sight of flickering holograms and bloodstains.

Just then.

They were selling employee ID cards!

"Hoooo..."

Her feet moved on their own. The crowd was so thick that a separate line had formed just for the stall.

*It makes sense.*

At least half the people who came for the opening must be here for TeSTAR.

Of course, reality was capitalism.

"It's expensive!"

TeSTAR's conscientious pricing only applied when it was not a collaboration with another company.

However, not buying one was out of the question. In the end, the two of them bought the employee ID merchandise, choosing different strap colors, yellow and black.

The photo on the shiny ID card was, of course, Park Moondae.

"Hmm... Since we came all this way, I'm going to buy Ahyeon's too!"

"Good, good."

The fansite master watched her friend boldly open her wallet, shaking off the sorrows of a former graduate student. She then used her smartphone to translate the English instructions.

"Ah, it says if you connect this to the theme park app, you can interact with things here. I think we can use it in the attraction."

"Wow! That's insane."

The two clicked their tongues in amazement and scanned the QR code on the back, linking it to the Dreamland app.

*Let's see, the option to change Moondae's picture to your own... yeah, absolutely not needed.*

However, at the very bottom, a strange field was labeled 'Album Code'.

*Code...?*

*Huh?*

*Korean?*

Their styling somehow gave that impression.

Seeing three men of the same nationality here was not common, so she was momentarily interested, but then, the men's voices, muffled by noise, vaguely reached her. Even from a rough listen, it was English.

"......"

*Maybe they're just K-pop fans.*

But for some reason, she felt a little bothered.

*What was that?*

No. This was not the time for that. She had to enjoy this attraction to the fullest and get her money's and time's worth.
