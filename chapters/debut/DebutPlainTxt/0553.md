# Chapter 553

Park Moondae sprawled out in his studio apartment, feeling dazed. His painstakingly created identity as an aspiring actor, meant for gathering information, was about to vaporize because he couldn't enter the country. He planned to pull himself together and create a new plan, but...

*Damn it.*

He let out a light curse.

Just then, the pop-up changed.

[The Focused Genius Actor has left the channel.]

"......"

Park Moondae shot up instantly.

*...Left?*

A faint sense of foreboding and emptiness brushed past his mind. Before he could start racking his brain, a gentle sentence took its place.

[The Dancer with a Noble Heart has entered the channel.]

Noble, heart, dancer.

Only one member fit those modifiers and that noun.

"...Seon Ahyeon?"

As if answering his question, the pop-up flickered and displayed another sentence.

[The Dancer with a Noble Heart wishes to move your location.]

"...!"

~~~

A few minutes before Seon Ahyeon's pop-up appeared, the group was at the JSA.

"First of all, no crimes," Ryu Chungwoo began calmly. "Even if this looks like a game to us, what Moondae is experiencing is real."

"Of course."

"That's a given."

Everyone nodded. They had joked about virtual worlds and duplicating money, but none of them wanted Park Moondae to get arrested.

Kwon Heeseung also participated enthusiastically.

[I just looked it up. What about a police report? It seems you can report your passport stolen in the US and then enter the country!]

However, a cold voice cut off the conversation.

"That's impossible," Cheongryeo said, scanning the others with his chilly eyes. "Our junior probably doesn't even have a record of entering the US in the first place."

He continued, "You should all realize that any idea this group can logically come up with is something our junior can also think of."

"Are you saying there's no realistic way?" Ryu Chungwoo asked calmly.

Cheongryeo replied with a blank expression. "I'm saying we never had time for this in the first place."

"...!"

A day passed for Park Moondae in one or two minutes. It was an unprecedented situation. They didn't know how much time was left, and no one else could perceive Park Moondae.

Cheongryeo's conclusion was simple. They should focus on pumping energy into Park Moondae to restore him and bring him back. Reacting to every one of Park Moondae's intentions was a stupid thing to do.

It was just like what these people were doing.

"In this situation, does it really matter what our junior wants to do over there?"

[......]

"Instead of doing useless things, I think it's right to only think about restoring his stamina."

*Haha, you have a real talent for saying the right thing so cruelly, Sunbae!*

Kwon Heeseung swallowed the words, feeling like he might burst into tears.*Aren't they going to start fighting again?*

However, the situation didn't turn hostile.

"Yep. I understand what you're saying. It's logical," Lee Sejin said. Instead of getting angry, he shrugged. "Of course, time's of the essence, so we should move efficiently."

Now that the situation had passed its dangerous peak and his anxiety had subsided, he took on his usual role as the team's coordinator.

"But the truth is, even you don't know exactly what Moondae is going through, do you, Sunbae? We don't know, but entering the country might be a desperate problem for Moondae right now."

Lee Sejin smiled bitterly. "So... let's not act like we know everything from the outside."

"......"

The atmosphere calmed down slightly.

"OK. We don't have time for this. Let's all stop fighting with words and use our brains. Right?"

At Cha Eugene's words, everyone silently nodded.

Then, Keundal quietly raised his hand. "In that sense... I just thought of a method that addresses what both of you said."

"...!"

"Wh-What kind of method...?"

Keundal swallowed. "I... 'pull' Park Moondae to Korea."

"......"

The reason they wanted to restore Park Moondae's stamina was so he could endure it when Keundal attempted to pull him back to reality. If someone was going to jump from a moving train, they at least needed to be able to move their limbs.

In other words, Keundal could move Park Moondae.

*Within the limits of his stamina!*

Just a tiny bit. Just enough to bring him spatially closer to their current reality.

"It would also serve as a sort of practice attempt before he fully recovers his stamina."

"You mean before we bring him back to reality for real?"

"That's right."

Keundal nodded seriously. The listeners slowly began to see this abstract concept in a positive light.

After some discussion about whether Park Moondae would just fall into the JSA, or if he could even be pulled precisely, they reached a conclusion.

Let's pull Park Moondae!

"It was an attempt we had to check."

"Mhm."

The members nodded, quickly reaching a near-unanimous decision.

"Then we need to convey this to Park Moondae now."

"Right. He needs to be mentally prepared so he won't get unnecessarily anxious or misunderstand!"

However, the channel that allowed for communication with Park Moondae was a gamble. Sometimes it showed his true feelings, but other times, a random question from their messy conversation would pop up.

"The intention has to get through all that noise properly."

The person who was calmest, clearest in thought, and most likely had Park Moondae's trust was...

"...Ahyeon, right?"

"Yes."

With everyone's agreement, they had Seon Ahyeon connect to the channel.

[Park Moondae: Figuring out your identity!]

"He wishes."

"I knew it."

Seon Ahyeon calmly organized his thoughts, trying to convey a single, clear intention.

*I want to help.*

*We want to move you.*

*To Korea, so you won't be in trouble, and further, eventually... back to reality.*

"......"

And that attempt got through.

[Park Moondae: Awaiting your idea...]

It worked!

"Okay. Do it now!"

"Y-Yes."

Keundal took a deep breath and closed his eyes. He once again felt the hand extending beyond the passage created with Cheongryeo as the medium. At its end was Park Moondae.

*Carefully...*

Keundal focused his mind and moved it just a little.

He moved it.

"......"

In that moment, the screen with the pixelated Park Moondae went blank.

"......"

"Let's wait a moment," Ryu Chungwoo said, calming the members.

Keundal broke out in a cold sweat, struggling to maintain contact with Park Moondae.

A few dozen seconds later, the screen reconnected.

[Park Moondae: Surprised by the changed environment!]

"It worked!"

Park Moondae was in Korea!

[Ooh! Did it work??]

"......"

A sense of relief, like a successful rocket launch, filled the space.

Ryu Chungwoo smiled slightly and looked around, his gaze lingering a little longer on Cheongryeo.

"Let's continue to have thorough discussions like this from now on. We need diverse perspectives to ensure things proceed safely."

"......"

"And... we might have been completely unaware of this whole situation, so thank you for contacting us."

Cheongryeo didn't seem to feel any particular emotion, but he didn't argue either.

Ryu Chungwoo realized he had at least succeeded in building a cooperative foundation.

*Hoo.*

He pressed down on his own hand. As the leader of STier, Ryu Chungwoo had to handle all sorts of tasks. This meant he was painfully accustomed to working with people of peculiar temperaments.

*To think this would come in handy.*

Ryu Chungwoo couldn't bring himself to laugh, but he did feel a strange sense of efficacy.

"Let's hurry and restore Moondae's stamina like this."

"Yep!"

I closed my eyes and opened them.

The wringing pain lasted only a moment. It was replaced by a brief pleasure, like a rewinding tape, a feeling of everything returning to its rightful place. The sensation was cut short, like a teaser.

*They're doing something.*

This was probably what Seon Ahyeon had been trying to tell me. At the end of the strange dizzy spell, I calmly got my thoughts in order.

When my vision cleared...

"...Good heavens."

I came to my senses on a street somewhere in the Gangbuk district of Seoul.

*It actually worked.*

[The Dancer with a Noble Heart is pleased.]

I gasped for breath.

"...Thank you."

After muttering those meaningless words toward the pop-up, I started walking. My mind raced.

*Should I try using public Wi-Fi first?*

I had to contact the only person here who knew my situation.

A moment later, Judan exclaimed the same thing when he saw me at a franchise cafe. "Good heavens."

After hearing my explanation, he muttered, "Spatial teleportation... That's a part where even science fiction glosses over the scientific proof. I never imagined I would witness it in person."

*Is that so? I never dreamed someone would actually say something like that.*

In any case, the fact that Judan's trust in my 'supernatural aspect' had deepened was quite beneficial.

*I'll be able to get more active cooperation.*

"So, are you planning to continue working as an actor here and gather information?"

I lifted my head and glanced at the pop-up.

[The Dancer with a Noble Heart wants to sponsor you.]

[The Dancer with a Noble Heart wants to bestow a blessing.]

"...Yes."

*Now, let's think.*

These guys keep wanting to give me something called a sponsorship.

*And it seems they can only give that sponsorship if I successfully use the blessing they provide. Also, they can only give a blessing in a situation where it would be useful.*

It was like when I received Bae Sejin's blessing while filming as an actor, or Kim Raebin's blessing while making donuts.

*In other words, I have to create the right situation.*

The more I work, the more opportunities I'll have to receive this energy sponsorship, and the more I can recover. I'll also get closer to understanding why those guys are so desperate to give me energy.

*Good.*

I immediately contacted LeTi, telling them my schedule had changed and I had entered the country early. I received an instant reply.

[That's even better ㅠㅠ I was worried because the schedule was tight, but now we can proceed more leisurely!]

*What on earth is this schedule?*

[Your profile photoshoot, Gunwoo~^^]

"......"

[I just checked to see if there were any studio cancellations for tomorrow, and what do you know, there's one available. Is that schedule okay with you?]

[Where are you staying right now? We're planning to send a car for you~]

"If it's for about a month, you are welcome to stay with me in my studio apartment. It's a place I got because I find dorms uncomfortable," Judan offered.

"Thank you."

I resolved to visit Judan when I returned to reality.

At dawn the next day, I stood in front of the camera after some guidance from the gruff studio staff.

"Hmph. We don't have much time, so we'll proceed right away."

"Yes. Look here."

Being filmed was something I did almost daily as TeSTAR's Park Moondae. However, there was a slight difference.

"A little neater~ but with more atmosphere!"

Amidst a barrage of vague requests, the face and body I used to perform felt completely unfamiliar. Almost all of my photoshoot experience was in Park Moondae's body.

*So this is how it is.*

In the System's virtual world, some of Park Moondae's appearance remained, and I was younger, but now, even my bone structure was completely different. I had no idea how to make myself look good.

So, I muttered to myself.

*This is extremely difficult.*

*Isn't it mentally distressing to struggle with something I'm usually familiar with?*

*It's so, so difficult that I wish someone would help me.*

*Anyway, it really is.*

The moment I murmured that, a new pop-up appeared.

[Supermassive Black Hole (★★★★★) has entered the channel.]

Before I could even register it, everything happened in an instant.

[Supermassive Black Hole whistles.]

[Supermassive Black Hole bestows a blessing upon you!]

[Trait: Immersion]

~~~

There's a saying about the gap between imagination and reality. Anyone can create a plausible plan, but the result is often different when you try it yourself.

It was a saying I felt to my very bones when I first learned to dance during ❬Idol Inc.❭.

*Even if I had a concept, my body would just move on its own.*

It's a common thing. Ordinary people have to pour time and effort into controlling their movements and expressions to make them as close to their imagination as possible.

"Just one more shot!"

But there are also people who don't need such things.

I molded every muscle in my face into a precise shape and slowly aligned my head with the camera.

Exactly as I wanted.

*I get it.*

My mind was empty. No calculation, exploration, or practice was needed. Imagination naturally became reality, like breathing, walking, and talking.

"Okay, stronger eye contact with the camera!"

The abstract instruction exploded in my mind and was expressed through my entire body. I could implement every impact. I already knew how to make people like it, how to look charming.

"Ah, that's great! One more time!"

...how to capture their immersion.

This was the realm of instinct.

*There's a guy who lives like this?*

It was a talent so immense it was hard to believe someone lived this way.

In the end, the person holding the camera would say this, their eyes sparkling.

"It came out really, really well."

"......"

"I love it so much."

That was Cha Eugene.

[Supermassive Black Hole is delighted!]

[Supermassive Black Hole has sponsored you! : Energy +]

A vigorous vitality returned, flowing through my head like blood. Strength and control filled every one of my limbs.

*Good heavens.*

I gasped for breath.

"You worked hard."

The Black Hole was truly a Black Hole.

...However, I worried that by the time this procession of blessings was over, Ryu Gunwoo would become some comet-like rookie actor.

After the profile photoshoot ended, I found myself with a more urgent worry. I came to regret not bolting from the set immediately.

Do you know who was waiting to use the studio after me? It was the idol debut group from a famous agency, one that would soon go bankrupt after its own artist sued it.

And as for who was in this group...

"Oh! [You're really talented!]"

"Cha Eugene!"

It was none other than a teenage Cha Eugene and Kim Raebin. I'd heard they were in that agency's debut group around this time, and it seemed they were here to shoot their profile pictures.

...Let me tell you something even more absurd.

At the same time, a celebrity appeared, claiming, 'I was just in the neighborhood, so I stopped by to treat our company staff to some coffee.'

"My goodness, what a surprise!"

"Hello."

It was Cheongryeo.

"Ah, is this person also from your company?"

Seeing the guy smiling and extending his hand to me, I had a gut feeling.

*This bastard came here on purpose to check.*

He had finally found the time to come and 'Appraise' Ryu Gunwoo's face and stats.

In other words, Cheongryeo was in front of me. Behind me were Kim Raebin and Cha Eugene, and Judan would be arriving here soon.

[Help.]

[Bring up Ryu Gunwoo's profile.]

On top of that, Cheongryeo was even calling for me in his head, in my role as Help.

*My head is going to explode.*

[The Maestro of the Deep Night is horrified!]
