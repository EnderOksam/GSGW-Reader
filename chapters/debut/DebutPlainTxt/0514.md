# Chapter 514

Those Aether guys were cast in the renewed version of ❬Genuine Match❭, a show we had dibs on.

I arrived at the filming location and saw them in the hallway. They were going from one waiting room to another to greet the other cast members. My first thought was immediate.

*Why didn't I hear about this?*

Why didn't we know those guys were going to be on the show?

I briefly asked myself the question before I clicked my tongue. The answer was obvious. The production team must have deliberately kept it from us.

*They must have known we'd raise hell.*

Anyone could figure out that TeSTAR wouldn't be pleased with Aether just by talking to a few people at our company.

As it happened, none of the current cast members were from a company like LeTi, which would meticulously check for additional cast members and keep them in check ahead of time. So they must have stalled, saying things like, "The casting isn't finalized yet."

My eyebrow twitched.

*They're looking down on us.*

To be honest, I hadn't expected this kind of situation. No, there was no need to even predict it. My calculation was based on the assumption that Aether's agency, Wonder Hall, wouldn't dare try to push them in here.

Didn't they already learn their lesson from the Water Bomb incident?

*A rookie with no experience would get completely crushed in a head-to-head comparison.*

That's why I had even left their mole in this company alone. I planned to dangle some bait and make them bite back.

And they just walked into the wrong place on their own two feet?

*Is this a last-ditch effort?*

The fact that a company like Wonder Hall was unlikely to use such a shallow trick made my head spin even faster.

"Alright, alright, let's head inside first~"

"Yep."

For now, TeSTAR entered our designated waiting room without a word. However, just by looking at their faces, our staff seemed to have a lot to say.

*STier Ryu Chungwoo must have seen it too.*

He was usually the type to subtly read the room and grasp the situation. For some reason, he just followed along silently.

*Hmm.*

After we entered the waiting room, I spoke to him.

"They're a group called Aether. They benchmarked TeSTAR for their debut."

"I see."

His answer was reflexive. A guy who investigated TeSTAR as if dissecting it would have surely known, yet he was overly calm and serene, like he was reacting to a topic he had no interest in.

"......"

I noted his reaction and turned my gaze away.

The enraged company employees spoke as if they were breathing fire.

"No, this is just completely disrespectful to us..."

"How can we not say something?"

They were genuinely pissed. Of course, they were probably also acting extra furious, preparing for TeSTAR to grill them with, "How did you not know about this?"

In any case, a few of the higher-ranking staff members even stormed out of the waiting room to confront the production team. It wouldn't do much. The broadcasting station held all the power, making it a mere face-saving gesture. Still, protesting with a controlled tone was better than doing nothing.

I noticed a few members who caught on to this either smiling wryly or shrugging.

"How could they do this? It's no different from tricking us!"

"Uhm... yeah, I guess so, Hyung."

...Of course, there were also guys like him who were just plain pissed.

And there was one who came to me with a tense face to ask a question. It was STier Kim Raebin. He had said he "wouldn't go on stage" but had diligently come with us to the filming for now.

"Um, is there anything I need to do...?"

"It's not a big deal. You can just rest. Sit down."

"Yes..."

He sat on the sofa with a peculiar expression.

I nodded.

"And we'll be seeing the parties responsible soon enough."

"...?"

I waited. A short while later, a visitor came to our waiting room.

"Hello, Sunbaenim!"

Aether. As expected, the guys who had been making their rounds to greet everyone had come to us as well.

And I realized what Wonder Hall's game was.

"Please take good care of us!"

The guys bowed as they greeted us. Meeting them in person, Aether was a group of unassuming, introverted guys. They had no hint of cunning.

In other words, there wasn't a single sly snake, a clever schemer, or even a strong-willed character among them.

I swallowed a groan.

*Damn it.*

So this was what those Wonder Hall bastards were aiming for. These Aether guys had socially impeccable, gentle personalities.

*This is what they wanted to show the public.*

They planned to cash in on the build-up from maintaining an air of mystery until now. This was a ploy to completely defuse the conflict with TeSTAR. If they couldn't kill the "successor" image, they had pivoted to a new strategy of getting the most benefit out of it.

By strengthening the relationship.

*It's obvious.*

Aether, learning a thing or two from TeSTAR in a heartwarming senior-junior relationship, even on a competition program! Add a bit of their human side to that, and the public is bound to be generous to young, innocent-looking rookies who don't seem to have a calculating bone in their bodies.

The ridicule and controversy for "copying TeSTAR" wouldn't fit this new image, so it would naturally fizzle out. The moment you feel a sense of familiarity with someone, your aversion to them also becomes selective.

That's how it would go.

[The Aether kids remind me of when I first joined the company lol ㅠㅠ]

[Sigh ㅠㅠ They're working so hard]

[I hope their performance goes well for some reason]

By humbly showing how terrified they were of the "TeSTAR's successor" talk, they could safely secure the super rookie image. Their performance just had to avoid being a complete disaster.

*Solidifying their image as promising rookies.*

Minimizing the risk.

I resisted the urge to press my fingers to my brow.

*They've thought this through well.*

The bigger problem was that you couldn't say it was an absolute lie. I scanned Aether's members, who were huddled together in a group.

*These guys are really just kids.*

They were kids in their teens who knew nothing about their agency's schemes and had just debuted after practicing their hearts out. They were model students who didn't really know what was going on, and even if they did, they only had a superficial understanding. There was a high probability that their smartphone use was also prohibited.

*They deliberately weeded out everyone else and debuted only kids like these.*

I could see Wonder Hall's calculation. The agency would continue to take all the heat, and they could use the "let the kids off the hook" card a few more times. Because the fans would know that they were truly well-behaved and innocent kids.

"...And I'm Nua, the Leader. We've been watching your performances very closely. We respect you, Sunbae!"

The faces of the members introducing themselves were a mess of tension, discipline, and shyness. The silver-haired guy who seemed to be the Leader clenched both his hands and shouted energetically.

"We'll work really, really hard!"

In a competition program, wasn't saying something like that to a competitor basically picking a fight? However, it was obvious the ones saying it had no such intention, so the members just gave a moderately positive response.

"Yep, we look forward to it."

"Good luck with the shoot today...!"

"...! Thank you!"

It was just the usual kindness they showed when talking to a junior they didn't have particular feelings about. Wonder Hall probably aimed for this too.

*They know our agency basically moves at our command.*

So they were trying to placate TeSTAR's animosity toward Aether. They wanted to make it so we couldn't be harsh on these kids. To make us hesitate before making a move. Moreover, it would be even more so when the cameras were rolling.

*If we react poorly during filming, TeSTAR will just end up looking petty.*

I inwardly nodded, then smiled nonchalantly and shook the Aether Leader's hand.

"I'll be looking forward to a great performance."

"Thank you, yep!"

After cautiously asking, the guys even delivered an album with a long letter attached to it. They left the waiting room with flushed faces.

I closed the album. A long letter was written on it, starting with "I've been a fan since Park Moondae-sunbae sang." The other members seemed to have mixed feelings as well, as most of them took a look at the album.

*This album benchmarked 'Procession'.*

The fact that they could present it with sincere respect was probably due to the dissonance between that company and this group.

*Well, it's not enough to shake me.*

I shrugged and put the album away. It wasn't the first or second time something ridiculous had happened in this industry. Besides, we had experienced unbelievably supernatural things. The guy sitting right next to me was living proof of that.

"......"

I subtly turned my head. STier Ryu Chungwoo had long since placed the album on the table. He was lost in thought with his fingers interlaced. Since "Ryu Chungwoo" was TeSTAR's Leader, he had moved to sit here while greeting the juniors. However, he showed almost none of the proactiveness expected of a Leader.

"Noona, we're starting filming in 30 minutes, right?"

"That's right. Oh, and this has gone all the way up to our CEO. We're going to lodge a formal complaint, so don't you worry!"

"Aigoo... Is that so?"

"Why complain? We're strong. So we don't need to... What? Sejin-hyung?"

"Be quiet, Cha Eugene."

Ryu Chungwoo remained silent through the boisterous preparations for filming. He felt like he was in another world.

"......"

Well, it wasn't like I was talking either.

*I should probably start getting ready.*

I shrugged and was about to get up.

It was then.

"I have something to say."

STier Ryu Chungwoo quietly opened his mouth.

*Hmm.*

I stopped moving and turned to look at him from my seat.

"Go ahead."

"Thank you."

Ryu Chungwoo replied curtly, then slowly continued on a completely unexpected topic.

"About my... 'memories'."

"......"

"I don't think it would matter if you gave them back."

"...!"

Thirty minutes before filming, STier Ryu Chungwoo had suddenly retracted his refusal to get his memories back.

"I've been thinking... I feel like I've been a huge burden. I acted a bit excessively, didn't I?"

STier Ryu Chungwoo smiled faintly as he spoke. He looked as if he had regained his mental stability and become peaceful.

But I knew. There was no way that was it.

*There's no reason.*

There was no catalyst for it. This guy wasn't saying this because he'd had an epiphany or had come to terms with the situation.

*No, if anything...*

I remembered his face from last dawn, after he had heard everything "Lee Sejin A" had to say.

[If you want to do well here, I'll support you. ...You've been through a lot. Thank you for believing in me, and I'm sorry.]

Right after those words, his expression was clearly one of fatigue.

Burnout.

*That... is exhaustion.*

A hint of terrible listlessness, hidden by inertia, flickered across his face before disappearing. Then, in a gentle voice, came words that were advantageous to TeSTAR's situation.

"We can proceed right after this shoot is over. Hmm, we were planning on doing a unit stage this time, right? I'll sit this one out for now."

A unit stage. I recalled the conversation we had this morning. It was a condition designed to break away from the competition program's mannerisms.

[Since the group is large, the performances can get repetitive, so let's split into smaller groups once or twice. We can schedule individual activities too.]

"...You might want to participate after you get your memories back."

"That's possible."

STier Ryu Chungwoo readily agreed and answered nonchalantly.

"That's for that 'Ryu Chungwoo' to handle."

It was a composure that was almost chilling.

"......"

I narrowed my eyes for a moment, keeping my mouth shut as I thought.

And then.

"Ah, yes."

I immediately agreed. Then I looked around and opened my mouth.

"First, about you not performing this time. I was planning on getting the other guys' opinions anyway, so let's gather them now."

I immediately called over the chattering members and had them sit around the table. The staff seemed a little flustered, but it was fine. I had no intention of wasting time.

"About the unit. Should we take volunteers now and get it sorted out quickly? I wanted to get a headcount before filming."

"Ah, sounds good."

Since it was a topic we had already discussed, the other guys weren't fazed either.

"Then let's have a show of hands. Hands up."

The response was immediate. Cha Eugene and Lee Sejin raised their hands without hesitation. The guys who never miss a chance to perform.

"......"

The next to naturally raise his hand was Seon Ahyeon, the prudent and diligent one, and finally, a grim-faced Bae Sejin. It seemed like everyone had raised their hands except for the two who only had their STier memories.

A faint, bitter smile seemed to flash across Ryu Chungwoo's lips.

I nodded and declared.

"A total of four. This should be fine. The number of members is just right, too."

At that, STier Kim Raebin, who was next to me, cautiously raised his hand. He spoke only after receiving permission.

"Um... isn't it five?"

"It's four."

"...Ah, you were excluding yourself. I'm sorry."

"Hmm?"

I tilted my head.

"I'm not doing it."

"......"

"I didn't raise my hand."

"...!"

I declared.

"Cha Eugene, Lee Sejin, Seon Ahyeon, and Bae Sejin-hyung. It'll be these four."

*Let's not do this amicably.*
