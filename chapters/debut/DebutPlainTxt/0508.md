# Chapter 508

[During 'Preview']

The Status Window regained its color after I activated 'Preview', but it looked strange. It had no font and only a barely implemented interface. It resembled a computer's emergency mode, with just a few available functions. I had never seen such a unique state before.

However, I had no time to focus on it right now.

"Kim Raebin! Are you okay?"

"Ugh..."

Kim Raebin staggered, his head cracking against the hallway wall.

"Lay him down first."

I rushed from the room to support Kim Raebin. Cha Eugene silently helped me lay him on the floor while Seon Ahyeon ran for a pillow.

"Ugh..."

"A headache?"

"Raebin, are you in pain?"

"......"

Kim Raebin did not answer. His breathing gradually calmed, and he grew quiet as if his consciousness was fading.

"Gasp!"

"...!"

He shot up as if waking from a nightmare. He opened his mouth, his eyes wide.

"Wh-Who are you?"

"......"

I had heard those words before.

*Fuck...*

I pressed the bridge of my nose with one hand. Meanwhile, a dazed Kim Raebin looked around until his expression brightened at a familiar face.

"Cha Eugene?"

But his face soon filled with horror again.

"Cha Eugene! How did you enlist? Don't tell me you gave up your citizenship to volunteer for the army..."

What was this nonsense now?

Cha Eugene, who must have had a similar thought, spoke with a bewildered look.

"...?? Kim Raebin, have you lost your mind?!"

"...! Could it be that you came to visit me, but I fell, lost consciousness, and can't remember?"

No.

Beside me, Keun Sejin spoke hesitantly.

"Enlisted? Don't tell me, Raebin, you..."

He said what everyone had vaguely thought but dared not say aloud.

"You went to the military?"

"...? Yes. I've just completed my basic training!"

A deathly silence fell over us.

I squeezed my eyes shut.

*...Good grief.*

STier Kim Raebin, no... former STier Kim Raebin, had gone to the military.

***

"Oh, so... you're saying you received your draft notice."

"Yes! I completed all the bachelor's degree courses using the academic credit bank system, so my enlistment deferral period ended."

The probable STier Kim Raebin was more spirited than I expected. It seemed he had finished a bachelor's degree in music online, then immediately received his draft notice and enlisted.

*...Since STier disbanded, there would have been no reason to come up with another excuse for deferment.*

And it seemed he was doing better than expected in the military.

Kim Raebin then summarized his life as a private.

"Although there are a few things I can't understand, I'm recalling the advice I received from my older sister and refraining from asking questions."

What a relief that was.

*He got lucky.*

With his personality, some bastard likely would have bullied him, but it seemed there were no twisted guys among his seniors. In that case, Kim Raebin was actually well-suited for the military and needed no correction.

*Because he's the type to do whatever his superiors tell him to.*

Still, it left a bitter taste in my mouth.

*The military at that age.*

Of course, 24 was late for a regular college student, but it was too early for an idol. The dominant meta for reasonably successful idol groups was to delay enlistment as long as possible in their twenties.

*To put it another way, it means this guy is no longer part of a 'reasonably successful idol group'.*

...STier Kim Raebin, that is.

"So... you're saying that time has turned back once, and the 'me' who debuted in a different group is still active as an idol...?"

"That's right."

"That's..."

After hearing the explanation, STier Kim Raebin was at a loss for words, his head spinning from the supernatural situation. Then, as his vision cleared, he was startled by the figure standing behind me.

It was STier Ryu Chungwoo.

"Chungwoo?"

"You're here too."

Ryu Chungwoo closed his mouth. His expression remained unchanged, but I felt a strange sense that he wasn't in a good mood.

*Why?*

Shouldn't he be happy to see a guy he spent years with, who's in a similar situation? It's not like Kim Raebin was the type to cause trouble.

"......"

Just as an awkward silence was about to fall, Keun Sejin sensed the mood and consciously continued the explanation in a soft, cheerful tone. He explained that the Ryu Chungwoo here was the same STier Ryu Chungwoo he knew, and that Cha Eugene also had his memories.

And finally...

"Umm, so to continue, we, I mean TeSTAR, the group you're in now, Raebin, are preparing for a performance on a competition show~"

"Is that so..."

"Y-Yeah, this, this too... Raebin arranged it!"

Before I could speak, Seon Ahyeon hurriedly played music from his smartphone. It was the completed arrangement of Myudi's ❬You Wouldn't Know❭, which Kim Raebin had made for the competition a few days ago.

[Umm, umm...]

The music flowed slowly, along with my humming from the demo recording.

"......"

STier Kim Raebin listened with a blank face to the low-quality, muffled sound from the smartphone. He listened to the exquisite and delicate composition and melody.

Cha Eugene looked at the smartphone, uncharacteristically anxious.

*It must be because he knows.*

He knew that during their STier days, Kim Raebin couldn't participate much in songwriting. It was just a guess, but even if he had, he never would have had full authority like now. If the higher-ups at the agency pushed a crude, tasteless direction, wouldn't he have struggled to somehow accommodate it?

It was just like the mess that happened when he first arranged 'Burning Like the Sun' during the second team battle of ❬Idol Inc.❭.

*And... they probably siphoned off royalties by adding relatives to the credits.*

In a word, it was exploitation. So, for that guy to hear the result of TeSTAR Kim Raebin fully unleashing his talent might be a bit bitter, no matter how necessary it was.

Just like STier Cha Eugene before...

"Amazing! Did I really arrange this?"

"......"

I took back all my assumptions.

Kim Raebin's eyes sparkled. Though his expression was a bit fierce, anyone could see he was excited. He even looked thrilled.

Seon Ahyeon's face flushed in response.

"Y-Yeah! Do you, like it...?"

"Yes. Indeed... Yes."

Kim Raebin seemed so overwhelmed that he couldn't even do his specialty of gushing excitedly about a topic he was interested in. He genuinely seemed to feel joy or awe rather than any major awkwardness in this situation.

*Ah.*

I realized why that was possible.

*He believed that he had re-debuted as TeSTAR.*

He did not separate himself from 'TeSTAR Kim Raebin'. He perceived the current situation as a point on a continuous timeline, a version of his own future.

And this wasn't possible simply because Kim Raebin was naive.

*It's because of our attitude.*

We were treating this Kim Raebin exactly the same as before. That was why Kim Raebin also accepted us without issue as 'acquaintances he couldn't remember'. Besides, two people already had memories of STier, and one was a friend from his trainee days.

His guard was bound to be lowered.

*All the conditions were met.*

It was a stroke of luck we got because Kim Raebin was so similar to his old self, and because it happened so suddenly that we had no time to prepare.

*Phew.*

I had been quite tense, but I gradually grew calmer.

The atmosphere was getting warmer. Swept up by the impressed STier Kim Raebin's excitement, Seon Ahyeon was about to play another song.

Cha Eugene casually asked, "Then Kim Raebin, do you want to arrange the next stage?"

"Me?"

"Oh~ Right. If you're interested, just say the word, Raebin!"

Kim Raebin's eyes widened, but his face soon turned quite serious.

"I believe it might be an overly generous offer, but if I take on the challenge and produce a result... could you consider it as a candidate?"

For a moment, relief and joy flashed across the members' faces. They were clearly thinking something like, 'Kim Raebin is still the same'.

"Of course!"

"Thank you!"

As the members smiled warmly, Keun Sejin spoke up energetically.

"Then shall we start preparing for our performance?"

"...?"

It was at that moment.

"I... have to perform on stage!"

But Kim Raebin's tone was one of horror, not realization. It was as if he had suddenly been pelted by hail. In other words, it was negative.

"Y-You don't want to do it?"

"It's not that I don't want to, but I think it would be better if I didn't."

"...?"

Kim Raebin answered as if it were nothing.

"I've realized through experience. I am not cut out for broadcasting or stage activities."

"Wh-What?"

The members froze.

My expression hardened too.

*What does that mean?*

Even during his STier days, there was no problem with his stage abilities.

*Inside information.*

I looked back at Cha Eugene, who would have memories of being his fellow member. He wore a slightly bitter expression.

"......"

I had a bad feeling about this.

Fortunately, there was someone here who would jump the gun... no, not him.

However, Bae Sejin had already opened his mouth.

"What are you talking about! You're great on stage!"

"Pardon?"

Kim Raebin flinched at Bae Sejin's shout. His expression then shifted to confusion as he saw Bae Sejin's face.

*...Did he just notice?*

That it was the face of 'Lee Sejin', who was kicked out in the early days of STier due to a drug scandal.

Sure enough, Kim Raebin, with a determined face... asked politely.

*...?*

"Excuse me, but may I ask your name?"

"It's Bae... Bae Sejin."

"...! Do you happen to know a former child actor named Lee Sejin?"

"......"

Bae Sejin's face filled with bewilderment, as if he wanted to answer, 'That's me'.

And he answered just as his expression suggested.

"That's me!"

"......"

Kim Raebin was horrified. I put a hand to my forehead.

At the same time, another thought occurred to me.

*Is it better to let this explode in a less serious atmosphere like this?*

The drug scandal whatever it was.

*We might be able to stamp it as a misunderstanding for good, curse Bae Sejin's biological father a bit, and be done with it.*

Circumstantially, the drug trafficking issue was definitely because of that bastard.

However, Kim Raebin, about to speak, stopped and glanced over to check on someone.

*...?*

STier Ryu Chungwoo.

"B-But."

"Raebin. You must be very confused and tired right now, so get some rest first."

Ryu Chungwoo answered immediately. Then he turned to us and gave a bitter smile.

"Is that okay?"

"Ah... o-of course!"

"Kim Raebin, your room is this way!"

"Th-Thank you..."

At Ryu Chungwoo's reasonable words, everyone hurriedly helped Kim Raebin to his bed. Though bewildered, he did not resist and lay down.

"......"

I became conscious of STier Ryu Chungwoo. The sense of unease I felt earlier returned.

*Just now, it felt like he cut Kim Raebin off.*

I decided to remember that. Of course, for now, I needed to dig a little deeper into Kim Raebin's statement about avoiding the stage.

***

"Amazing..."

This was STier Kim Raebin's reaction after seeing his own fancam with millions of views and enthusiastic comments from fans.

The members who had gathered and shown him the videos held a little hope.

"Yeah, see? That's right, isn't it?"

"As I thought, it seems impossible for me to go on stage."

"Huh...?"

"It seems impossible for me to perform at this level."

The members were bewildered.

Even in the midst of it all, Seon Ahyeon said with a firm expression, "B-But. When you were in our group... you were really, really good on stage!"

"...? There must have been some turning point or blood-sweat-and-tears effort."

Kim Raebin retorted calmly.

"I'm sorry, but that's not me."

It was an almost chilling assertion.

All of TeSTAR was momentarily at a loss for words.

*Fuck.*

I resisted the urge to squeeze my eyes shut.

*This guy had problems too.*

This was definitely a rejection of idol activities. It was not normal.

However, this was not the end of the day's absurd events. While Kim Raebin was watching the stage videos, I had casually turned my head, and something finally caught my eye.

It was the content that appeared in the pop-up on the Status Window where 'Preview' had been activated.

[New Feature Preview]

[View Fragment Record]

*Fragment Record?*

The moment the word that was practically the cause of this whole mess appeared, my concentration sharpened.

I reflexively skimmed the next line.

[Help: Through 'View Fragment Record', the System owner can recreate the mission failure scenarios from when the fragments were absorbed.]

What?

Wait, if it's the mission failure scenarios I've been through...

*No way.*

I reflexively checked the feature.

[View Fragment Record]

[Mission Failure: Building Collapse]

[Mission Failure: Return to Original State]

The first one was the disaster where the building literally collapsed. Unless my head was split open, I would have to be crazy to recreate that.

However.

*...The second one.*

[Mission Failure: Return to Original State]

Returning to Ryu Gunwoo's body.

In this moment, the function to swap bodies with Keundal had been unlocked.
