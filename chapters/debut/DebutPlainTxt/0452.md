# Chapter 452

"Hey, Moondae...! We're, we're getting off now."

"Ah."

I followed Seon Ahyeon out of the van.

Several days had passed since the absurd System pop-up appeared. There were still no problems.

TeSTAR was so busy with the comeback schedule that we'd fall asleep face-first into our food. The response was good.

"Moondae Moondae, are you okay?"

"Yeah, I'm fine."

I had also told the guys that the mission failure was over. With the risk of a member body-swapping and fainting mid-performance gone, we could start our activities with more peace of mind.

And that pop-up about collecting two System fragments... that incomprehensible nonsense was sorted out too.

[■■■(ver. Beta) Binding Complete!]

[Owner: Player Park Moondae]

*Status Window.*

I checked my Status Window.

[Item: ■■■(ver. Beta)]

An item entry had been added at the bottom. To be honest, this wasn't originally there. Keundal had simply manipulated the Status Window to add the entry.

I recalled my conversation with the guy.

[Ugh?!]

The guy had freaked out about the System's possible comeback. He soon regained his composure after I summarized that it seemed dead and wasn't responding.

He then searched the Status Window.

[Uh... It looks like some kind of item?]

[...??]

Surprisingly, this meant the fragment was truly 'bound' to me. The System wasn't attached to me. I possessed it. It was my property.

[Is it the System?]

[Hmm... It feels like it, but it doesn't seem that powerful or ominous! Ah, I'll mark it so you can see it!]

And so, Keundal added it as an item in the Status Window.

A thought naturally occurred to me.

*...If it's an item, can I use it?*

With that idea, I tried clicking on it.

[Use 'Item: ■■■(ver. Beta)']

[Target: _______ ]

I turned it off the moment that pop-up appeared. Keundal screamed too.

*Are you crazy?*

Seeing the target designation pop up confirmed it. This was undeniably the System.

*Who on earth would I use that on? From now on, this is permanently sealed.*

*Let's just shove it away.*

After closing the item pop-up, I started monitoring again while the guys checked the schedule.

"Manager, weren't we supposed to appear on Tnet's new variety show today?"

"Ah, that was postponed due to broadcasting issues, so it was changed to a corporate event."

"Understood!"

*That new variety show was something we were forced into anyway because T1 pushed it so hard.*

I planned to check feedback on other variety show appearances or our stage reactions. Then I saw a popular post.

[Fansite photos from the Cy-Chart that are currently causing a stir]

"......"

I clicked on the post reflexively and saw them. They were the photos I took at the awards ceremony a few days ago.

——————————

An anonymous account suddenly appeared, dropped a ton of photos without a word, and left

But they're so well-taken... (Photo)

There are all sorts of rumors about whether they're a major, a professional, or a fansite master, but anyway, the timelines of K-Pop multi-fandom stans are in a festive mood

(Link) (Link) (Link)

——————————

*Why did this become a hot topic?*

A few days ago, after we finished talking about the item, Keundal had asked for my permission.

[Would it be okay to share the photos you took with the fans? If you don't want me to, I definitely won't!]

I saw no reason to stop him. Honestly, I hadn't taken photos in a while. I didn't think they came out any better than before.

*And it's not like there were only one or two people holding cameras at the Cy-Chart.*

Besides, the guy bought the camera. He could do whatever he wanted with it.

[It should be fine as long as it's anonymous.]

[Okay!!]

That was yesterday. Now, it had led to this result.

[Holy crap, this person is back]

[Is this the person who filmed that Yeongrin fancam?]

[Awesome lololol]

[Were they job hunting?]

[Holy shit, that fluffy shot of Park Moondae T_T I'm so overwhelmingly grateful]

"......"

First, there was the way Keundal posted them. Usually, you would exchange data with other artists' fans or post separately in that artist's fan community, even if you don't sell it.

It's rare for someone to upload a ton of photos of every artist they shot onto a single account, especially in a flood with only tags and no introduction.

Of course, Keundal must have thought this.

[Alright, I'll create an anonymous account and upload them all at once!]

So this efficient, common-sense action unintentionally drew a lot of attention in the idol world.

People eventually compared the Yeongrin shots Keundal released with the Cy-Chart Yeongrin fancam, which led to this conclusion.

[OMG, I think this is the person who filmed that fancam, the legendary Yeongrin fancam!]

[Amazing... Did she recognize them TTTT]

Heartwarming posts about Yeongrin remembering 'that fancam-person from her debut days' took over the internet. As a result, the account received even more attention and suddenly became a hot topic.

[Looks like this person stans TeSTAR, what's with the number of photos lolololololol]

└I know, right? It looks like they photographed all the members, wow.

"......"

I looked at my photos again.*Can they really tell?*No matter how I looked, I couldn't see much of a difference.

*Did I take too many?*

The cause was likely the exceptional number of photos I took of TeSTAR while checking on Keundal's condition. Of course, TeSTAR wasn't the only group mentioned.

[They took amazing shots of Cheongryeo too, but why are there no stage photos of Cheongryeo? Such a shame TTTT]

[They must have them, right? There's no way they didn't take any. I hope they're just editing them. TeSTAR stage cuts, Cheongryeo stage cuts TTTT]

There weren't any. When that bastard performed, I was backstage too.

*...He did do well, though.*

I recalled Cheongryeo's performance. He had blatantly used a VTIC song as an intro to his solo stage. He cut the VTIC song exquisitely and filled the entire stage by himself.

It was practically a declaration.

*He's saying he's going to make a solo comeback on the level of a full group.*

*It seems this scene won't be easy this year either.*

Still, TeSTAR got off to a good start with this comeback. There was no reason for us to lose momentum.

*Let's keep going like this.*

Anyway, after checking the reactions to the photos, I relayed them to the person responsible.

Keundal was horrified.

[Gasp! I'll delete the account right away...!]

*...To that extent?*

*No, it's fine.*

That would only draw more attention. I told him to let it pass as a happening.

*As long as you don't post more, it's fine.*

[Okay...]

People's interests shift quickly. This would just be a fleeting moment. I looked over the reactions once more, feeling a little strange.

Surprisingly, no one accused me of being a data-seller. It seemed my previous WeTube account for unsold fancams had diverted that attention.

[Minha is so pretty... Thank you, Ribbon Minha is the best in the world]

[You're creating art with Heeseung, thank you thank you]

In any case, it was a strange feeling. Originally, I would have sold all the photos that made money. My photos were always selected and edited by the fansite masters who bought them before being released.

This kind of direct reaction was a first.

*...It's not a bad feeling.*

I looked over Keundal's account, ID 'gun4321'. After confirming there were no other issues, I was about to end my search.

It was then.

"M-Moondae. Is that... me, by any chance?"

"...!"

When I turned my head, Seon Ahyeon had an expectant look on his face.*What's this?*

"You, you took that, didn't you, Moondae?"

*How did he know?*

"Yeah, when our bodies were swapped."

"I see...! Ah, I saw you editing them, Moondae."

"......"

Come to think of it, I had been working on my laptop in the living room. Anyone could have seen me if they looked.

Seon Ahyeon's words drew the attention of the others in the waiting room.

"Whoa, awesome! Moondae Moondae took this? Did you post it online?"

"I look cool! That's me and Chungwoo-hyung!"

"...Park Moondae, you're really good at taking pictures."

The members chimed in from all over, wanting to see. They passed my smartphone around.

*Don't you guys have your own phones?*

Anyone would think my smartphone was the group's communal property.

Kim Raebin's eyes sparkled.

"Uploading your work separately for your hobby... It's just like the 'Song of the Stars' account where we uploaded our arrangement work!"

Right. Except they'll never find out it's me. It's physically impossible for me to take a picture of myself.

*...? Come to think of it, I wasn't the one who posted it either.*

But before I could say anything, the guys, who were having a blast with the photos, started chattering again.

"Should we film another travel reality show when this promotion ends? It'd be fun if Moondae took pictures. We could try swapping hobbies."

"Oh, that's a great idea! It'd be nice to all go on a trip together, and a concept where we work part-time at a theme park would be fun too~"

"Great! I want to try that!"

*Did they start chattering like high school students because we came back with a high school concept?*

In any case, it was a good thing the atmosphere wasn't weighed down by fatigue, despite our grueling schedule.

"Moondae-hyung, what do you think?"

"...A theme park part-time job sounds fine."

"Oh, as expected of a pro, Moondae~"

I laughed softly, shook my head, and took my smartphone back.

*Hmm, should I take pictures sometimes from now on?*

Well, it was a good incident for a refresh.

***

That day's schedule also ended without any problems.

It was the first time in a while we could sleep for more than six hours. Everyone happily headed back to the dorm.

"We have to leave at 7:30 tomorrow."

"Oh, nice."

I agreed. I also washed up quickly, changed, and willingly went to bed.

How much time had passed?

"...Moondae!"

*Hmm.*

"Park Moondae...!"

The alarm didn't go off.

"Wake up for a second! This article...!!"

An article. At that word, sleep vanished from me.

I immediately opened my eyes.

A pale-faced Bae Sejin stood before me in the darkness, holding a smartphone.

*Why is this guy in this room... no, that's not it.*

"What article?"

"......"

Bae Sejin immediately held out his smartphone. The current time reflexively caught my eye.

*...6 AM.*

It was still twenty minutes before my alarm was set to go off. It was also the perfect time for overnight news to be organized and hit the main page.

*What is it?*

I immediately read the article's title.

[(Breaking) Prosecution Requests Arrest Warrant for T1 Ent's CEO]

"...!"

"It's not an article about us, but... the company. It doesn't seem like a small matter."

Bae Sejin must have gotten a strange feeling as soon as he saw the article, having suffered under an agency before.

"Somehow... you're the best at handling things like this."

"......"

"...Sorry for waking you."

"No."

He did well to wake me. I immediately took the smartphone and read the article.

*Please let it be embezzlement.*

The best-case scenario was if the CEO alone went crazy over money, something that had nothing to do with us.

Unfortunately, it wasn't.

[CEO Lee (58), arrested for violating the Political Funds Act, has...]

"......"

*Politics?*

Right. Bae Sejin must have acted immediately after seeing the contents. It was a natural reaction.

*Fuck.*

I read more closely. The CEO of T1 Ent had apparently poured money into a political party after being promised a National Assembly nomination. Several others were arrested on similar charges, not just the CEO.

"......"

"What... do you think?"

Wait.

"Just a moment."

I filtered a few keywords from the article and reset the date range.*Latest 3 months.*The criteria was not T1 Ent, but 'T1' as a whole.

I immediately sorted by popularity and started scanning the articles.

[T1 Foods Violates Imported Food Safety Act, Ministry of Food and Drug Safety Issues 'Corrective Order']

[Tnet's ❬Voice of You❭ Receives Warning from Korea Communications Standards Commission]

[T1 Running Delivery and 3 Other Companies Collude in Port Distribution, Fined 5 Billion Won]

"......"

Indictments and fine-related articles poured out every few days. Even when several companies were on the list, the titles always emphasized 'T1'.

It was an unusual frequency, even for a large corporation.

"......"

I quickly swiped through the screen. Bae Sejin clenched his teeth.

"There's a problem with T1 itself, right?"

"...You could see it that way."

I decided to be frank.

Tnet's shady dealings during its expansion were nothing new. Until now, they had smoothed everything over with capital and connections, as expected of a large corporation.

But for the number of newsworthy incidents to suddenly explode in the last few months?

"T1 has always been like this. Don't you think it's more likely the 'ones doing the catching' have suddenly gotten stricter?"

"...!"

Bae Sejin swallowed hard.

"If that's the case..."

"Yes."

I muttered grimly.

"Hyung, you know there's a presidential election this year, right?"

"......"

This was tied to the political world.

"......"

*T1 has been targeted.*

I don't know why, but it seems they're screwed anyway, and the one chosen as an example was the entertainment affiliate.

*The more recent the articles, the more they're about the entertainment business.*

After slowly scanning through two months of records, I found the item that appeared most frequently.

[Tnet's ❬Be My Girls❭ Sued for Unfair Contracts]

[T1's Abuse of Power... Tears of ❬Be My Girls❭ Cast]

[T1's Studio's ❬Affectionate Ending❭ Faces Deliberation Difficulties, "Expression is Vulgar"]

*Tnet.*

In other words, T1 Ent. From the broadcasting station to the production studio. They were probably striking here because it's the business division where public image is most crucial and has the greatest ripple effect.

So, how would the corporation respond? If they had to give up some flesh right away...

*...They have to cut off the tail.*

Wouldn't they have to cut their losses with one of their most problematic parts and decide on a course of action, whether to lie low or act tough?

But giving up the broadcasting station in the entertainment business was insane. They would try to hold onto the station at all costs and choose a tail to cut instead...

What would they discard?

*It's obvious.*

A business where they were confident they could replace anything as long as they held onto the broadcasting station. A business whose poster children were nearing their contract renewal season.

Namely, the subsidiary entertainment agencies.

"...Let's wake everyone up."

T1 Ent kept various agencies as subsidiaries.

A few days later, the board of directors of T1 Stars, TeSTAR's agency, was arrested.
