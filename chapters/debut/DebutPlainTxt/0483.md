# Chapter 483

The returned Cha Eugene spent an incredibly energetic day. This didn't just mean he was playing around.

"I'm practicing!"

He checked his choreography, worked out, and even personally called our sobbing manager. After calling his family in the US, he started a racing game with Kim Raebin.

"Yeaaah!"

"W-Wait!"

"Left! Press the jump key on the left!"

The members were swept up in his whirlwind of energy. It was as if he was checking off a bucket list while putting his daily life back in order.

He even video-called Mungge, who was at Bae Sejin's family home.

[Woof!]

"Ooooh. Hi, buddy!"

The little furball, once small enough for one fist, now overflowed from two hands. He still had a habit of flipping onto his back.

*He still looks just as dopey.*

A white head and tail wagged relentlessly on the screen.

"Since we moved, should we invite Kkami over too?"

"I'd love that!!"

He seemed ready to video-call his own dog from the US and host a group housewarming party for all the members' dogs.

*

"......"

I silently observed him.

An unexpected person took the bait.

"Um, Moondae. Are you... disappointed by any chance?"

"...?"

Seon Ahyeon spoke to me cautiously.

"Me?"

"Y-Yeah."

He blushed slightly, then nodded firmly.

"Of course, I'm really happy and relieved that Eugene is back... but it's also possible to feel sad about the parting."

"......"

"I think... it's natural."

No, I was pondering it because that very point was ambiguous.

In any case, Seon Ahyeon also seemed quite bothered by the STier Cha Eugene who had simply vanished.

*I did give them a rough explanation.*

I explained that the situation ended because that Cha Eugene himself noticed the change in me and made a request.

Still, from their perspective, he had suddenly disappeared without a real goodbye. They were relieved and happy that our member Cha Eugene had returned safely this morning, but now they were starting to feel a little uneasy.

Hmm.

"...Yeah. Thanks."

"......"

*So, maybe I should try bringing it up.*

"Yeeees!!"

"......"

I watched Cha Eugene perform a victory ceremony with his game controller and decided to find the right time. That time came sooner than I expected.

That evening.

"Oh, our Eugene is pretty good~"

"Heehee!"

After a whirlwind day, Cha Eugene was in Keun Sejin's private room, looking at his guitars. He had finally slowed his determined pace and was now relaxed.

I called out to him.

"Eugene."

"Umm?"

"I have something to talk to you about regarding Water Bomb."

"OK!"

My plan was to lead him to the veranda, talk about Water Bomb, and gauge his mental state.

*I can stop right away if he seems unwilling.*

Cautiously.

However, I didn't need to get that far. Cha Eugene spoke the moment he stepped onto the veranda. He got straight to the point.

"I remember."

"...!"

*What?*

I turned my head.

"About Bad Cha Eugene."

Cha Eugene's expression was calm. He shrugged and stretched, then walked past my stiff form to the veranda railing.

"But it's not exact. It feels like some kind of vivid dream, or... a childhood memory."

He squinted one eye, as if trying to recall a forgotten word.

"But the feeling that 'that' Cha Eugene felt, hmm, moving? Touching? Overwhelming? It's harder to find the right word than I thought, huh? Anyway..."

He leaned against the veranda ledge and turned his gaze toward me. He looked just like the STier Cha Eugene from that one day.

"I remember that."

"......"

I looked at him.

His positive thinking, proactive demeanor, and blunt honesty were all the same. This was Cha Eugene, the member of TeSTAR.

However, he didn't know 'STier Cha Eugene' as just a piece of information. In other words, Cha Eugene just looked like Cha Eugene.

He spoke as himself.

"In my opinion, I think I was tired. Really tired."

"......"

"Bad Cha Eugene probably wouldn't have admitted it, but it was a kind of burnout. That's something anyone can experience when they hit a wall."

Cha Eugene shrugged cheerfully.

"Even me. So it's not a particularly shocking fact!"

*Is that so.*

*Still, I'm not sure if realizing his past self didn't succeed is a pleasant experience. He was amazing on stage, but no one was watching. He didn't get any attention, and to know he eventually got tired of being an idol and quit... yeah.*

"...What do you think?"

"What? About what?"

"......"

The air on the un-air-conditioned veranda was hot and stuffy on the summer night.

"About this whole situation, in general."

"Oh... Hyung, are you worried about me?"

I nodded slightly.

Cha Eugene seemed a little surprised, but he soon lowered his head with unexpected seriousness.

"I won't lie. Honestly, when I woke up this morning, I think I was a little confused."

He continued calmly.

"At that moment, it felt like I was Bad Cha Eugene, and my memories as part of TeSTAR had just returned."

"...!"

"It wasn't long before I returned to being me, but, Hyung, you're right. It's an experience that's almost like a trauma. Five whole years, completely unrewarded."

*...So that's what happened.*

Cha Eugene sighed sharply. I calmly accepted his words. However, it didn't end there.

"But Moondae-hyung, I know this."

He raised his hand and gently patted my back.

"Time heals all wounds."

"......"

"So even if you hadn't come to the past, Moondae-hyung, I would've performed on stage again."

I lifted my head.

Cha Eugene was smiling.

"The others are the same as me."

*No way.*

Before I could ask, he mouthed the word 'STier'.

"...!"

"Five years. The idea of being unrewarded was just what that Cha Eugene thought at the time. Who knows what would've happened after that?"

Life is long, and opportunities keep coming, Cha Eugene said, shrugging.

"Who knows. Maybe after about two years, I would've returned to Korea and debuted again with one of Kim Raebin's amazing songs... Or maybe, we would've released an album in the US."

He wasn't talking about a huge success.

He was just saying that even that Cha Eugene, who returned to the US after the group scattered and gave up on being an idol, wouldn't have given up forever.

"We would've kept doing it somehow. Because we loved it so much."

"......"

Funnily enough, my own experience reflexively came to mind. My first concert.

[Countless voices formed a tapestry, pouring down from all directions like shooting stars along with the lights.]

My breath caught in my throat.

I understood Cha Eugene's words because I remembered the shock my mind received back then. I also understood his next words.

"Water Bomb just made Bad Cha Eugene realize it earlier."

*Right.*

*So... that's why he became so much more cooperative after that.*

"But, even without Water Bomb, whether I was running a repair shop or a surfboard store in the US, I would've eventually realized it. I know myself."

Cha Eugene said confidently and chuckled, "Heehee."

"Since I know, you should believe it too, Moondae-hyung. That's good."

"......"

*So, that's what it was about.*

*Ryu Gunwoo just didn't get to see it because he made an extreme choice, but in truth, STier would have recovered.*

Cha Eugene, having been in that person's shoes, said it without a shred of doubt.

"You're my fan, Hyung."

"......"

*I... don't know.*

*Did that STier Cha Eugene, the one who gave me a High Five, return to the place where he lived? Or did he temporarily revert to his past STier self, just like I regained my memories as the office worker Ryu Gunwoo?*

*And now that he has all his original memories back, he remembers the STier days like a distant past event.*

*However, one thing is certain.*

*...That Cha Eugene ultimately became this Cha Eugene.*

*Therefore, his statement is trustworthy.*

*...I'll choose to think that.*

"......"

*If Ryu Gunwoo hadn't died and had held on for a few more years, he would have been able to see these guys perform somewhere again.*

*I'll believe that too.*

WHOOSH!

The wind blew. I looked at the starlit night view from the veranda and opened my mouth.

"...Yeah. Thanks for telling me."

"Don't mention it!"

The expression was slightly more polite than what Cha Eugene usually used. I realized he wasn't speaking to Park Moondae, the hyung he knew, but to Ryu Gunwoo, who had cheered for him.

I nodded silently and stood with him a little longer on the hot veranda.

***

"So... I have something to say."

"......"

"Moondae-hyung, you're my fan."

"I told you I'm not."

"You are. I'll give you fan service! In return, you can cook me some dakgalbi."

"Why are you trying to get paid for something you call service?"

"That's Konglish. The original meaning of 'Service' is something you pay for!"

*Good grief.*

A moment later, he acted like the team's youngest member again, as if the serious conversation had been a lie. I left the veranda, feeling my energy drain as usual.

*Dakgalbi, huh.*

*Haven't we been eating too much chicken lately?*

As I watched him head back to his room, a sudden thought occurred to me.

"......"

I hurried to ask him.

"By the way, about Sejin-hyung."

"Oh."

Bae Sejin.

I was worried about what he might think now that he had his memories back, but...

"I don't know!"

His memories were already fading. He only vaguely remembered the mess around Bae Sejin's drug arrest.

Besides.

"But I think there must have been a misunderstanding. [Honestly, that hyung would rather enlist in the military than sell drugs.]"

"......"

After watching Bae Sejin's fervent desire for legal action over the years, Cha Eugene's trust(?) was solid.

"You're right."

"Sure. I'm always right."

"You're wrong sometimes."

"I might seem wrong in the moment, but in the end, I'm right!"

*Oh, really now.*

I laughed and hit him playfully.

"Alright. We'll see."

"Heehee!"

And just like that, Cha Eugene was truly, completely back.

The next day, Cha Eugene uploaded a social media post while munching on homemade candy from Bae Sejin.

The comments and share counts updated frantically, and the response was very positive. I nodded and looked at Ryu Chungwoo, who approached after finishing a call with the company.

Now, the preparations were complete.

"The reality show filming is on schedule, right?"

"Yeah. The discussion went well."

*That's right.*

With Cha Eugene safely back, the perfect time had arrived to film our group's reality show.

"It's next week."

*We can proceed as planned.*

"Yes."

All priority matters were now finished.

[New! Survey]

*Yeah. I'm not falling for it.*

*I have no intention of clicking it. I'll summon Keundal and have him pick it apart, and if there's no risk?*

*I'll tell it to get lost already.*

*Let's hurry up and cancel this damn System.*
