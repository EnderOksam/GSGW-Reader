# Chapter 512

"......"

Silence fell over Park Moondae and Kim Raebin's room.

The former STier member delivered his message through the mouth of the towering Ryu Gunwoo.

[I won the case.]

In other words, STier was not a drug group.

"......"

A silent shock vibrated through the air.

STier's Ryu Chungwoo muttered the words as if to himself.

"Won the case."

"That's right."

The image of the departed member, the actor-turned-idol Lee Sejin A, seemed to surface beyond the responding Ryu Gunwoo.

Ryu Chungwoo opened his mouth without thinking.

"How?"

He knew how that trial had unfolded. He had checked and remembered how STier's Bae Sejin reacted when he was arrested for drug distribution. He recalled the words Bae Sejin had said with a face pale from indignation, injustice, and fear.

[It's not true!]

He had believed those words were the truth. It was not the panic of being caught, but a sharpness born from injustice.

*It must be a misunderstanding or a false accusation.*

So he spent his nights worrying about how the group could possibly hold on until the charges were cleared. Looking back now, those times were better in comparison.

Contrary to his plans, Lee Sejin A's charges were never cleared. Fueled by fiery public opinion, the verdict came terrifyingly fast.

[❬Idol Inc.❭ Debut Group STier's Lee Sejin, Sentenced to Prison in First Trial... "Malicious Crime"]

He was sentenced to prison.

However, Lee Sejin A did not give up. The more he appealed, the worse the internet's opinion of him became. It was surprising there was still room for it to get worse.

[Tired of you, druggie]

[Anyway, it seems like bastards who sell drugs sell their brains and conscience too]

[Sucking money from his fangirls must have been too sweet, he can't give it up lol]

[Wow, I didn't know he could be so shameless after running it like a full-blown family business, they should investigate his close friends too]

Days like that continued. When an article was posted, excited people would flock to it, then disappear like the tide.

Then, a long time later, he was sentenced to prison again in the second trial. By then, the topic was no longer a hot potato. It had grown cold, with no room left for controversy.

[lol pathetic]

"......"

Ryu Chungwoo knew the chances of this verdict suddenly being overturned to 'not guilty' in the final Supreme Court trial were almost zero. The articles already said as much.

Even without considering complex procedural issues like the distinction between legal and factual reviews, common sense could be explained by a single comment.

[All the evidence is out, why is he like that]

The bankbook showed the transactions. There was testimony and even an accomplice.

"......"

It was only then that Ryu Chungwoo stopped deliberately looking up the trial results. He was already exhausted enough.

*This is hopeless.*

While trying to somehow support the collapsing group by scolding and coaxing them, he had no energy left for this kind of hopeful torture. He decided to be realistic.

Yet here was an explanation that everything had been overturned, as if by a lie. That in the very end, he had been found not guilty. The person himself was now delivering the news through another's mouth, with a consciousness from who knows where.

*Lee Sejin A, who was in STier, did not distribute drugs.*

The overwhelming meaning of that single sentence.

"......"

Ryu Gunwoo opened his mouth again. Lee Sejin A's words came out.

"I understand if you can't believe it."

It is rare for someone to suddenly be found not guilty only in the third trial.

[Lee Sejin A : However, if you can find a legitimate reason for appeal, it's not impossible.]

[Lee Sejin A : In my case, it was the witness's false testimony.]

Lee Sejin A had found a contradiction in the testimony of his biological father, who had thrown him under the bus for a reduced sentence, and overturned it. The result came from obsession, relentless perseverance, meticulousness, and a bit of bad luck.

"It took a long time, but eventually, the original sentence was quashed on remand, and I was able to get a retrial."

Ryu Gunwoo calmly read the message. It spoke of countless agonies, years of trials, and then the end of yet another trial.

"I won."

"......"

Not guilty.

"I wouldn't lie about something like this. There's no need to."

Ryu Gunwoo remained calm. That calmness seemed to guarantee that Lee Sejin A was truly speaking the truth without hesitation. Everyone there felt it.

"......"

One person burst out, breaking the silence.

"You fXXking did it!"

It was Cha Eugene. The room suddenly filled with noise and heat. He hugged Ryu Gunwoo roughly, reacting with the passion of a coach rushing to a newly victorious boxer.

Of course, Ryu Gunwoo refused him.

"It wasn't me."

"I know. Please pass it on!" Cha Eugene said, his voice trembling slightly with emotion.

[Tell him I'm sorry for not following up until the end.]

"......"

Ryu Gunwoo nodded with a faint smile.

"He says thank you."

"......"

The tense atmosphere melted away in an instant.

However, the remaining two people still sat motionless in their spots, like statues. It was a difference in shock. Unlike Cha Eugene, who had already resolved his internal conflicts as a member of STier, this whole incident was still ongoing for these two.

*I see.*

So, after roughly pushing Cha Eugene away, Ryu Gunwoo opened his mouth again to deliver the next words.

[Lee Sejin A : But I know, too.]

[Lee Sejin A : Winning the case is a big deal, but only to me.]

"For you guys, hearing this story now won't change what you've already been through anyway."

STier took a huge hit from a member's drug charges right after their debut. That became the starting point for their sudden collapse and disbandment. It was an unchangeable fact. Even if the truth were revealed now, the public would not care. How futile.

"...And, I have no intention of denying that the injustice I suffered caused you great harm. I can't."

However.

[Lee Sejin A : But when you recall the memories from back then, you can change how you feel about them, can't you?]

STier did not fail because they deserved to. You can come up with a reason to refute it yourself.

"...So I really wanted to let you know that it was legally proven and acknowledged."

[Lee Sejin A : That's all.]

"......"

With those words, Ryu Gunwoo closed his mouth. No more message pop-ups appeared, as if Lee Sejin A had said everything he wanted to and had no lingering regrets.

It was then.

"Um."

A hand went up from one of the beds. It was Kim Raebin, his eyes slightly red.

"Um... could you please tell him that he must have gone through a lot, and I'm sorry I couldn't be of any help..."

Ryu Gunwoo readily nodded.

STier's Kim Raebin seemed somewhat confused, but he organized his thoughts, choosing his words one by one and continuing slowly and seriously.

"And... congratulations, though it's late. However, what exactly do you mean when you say, 'you can change how you feel when you recall the past'?"

Lee Sejin A thought about it, but ultimately answered like this.

[Lee Sejin A : I think it's something you have to feel for yourself. If someone else tells you, it becomes their thought, and it also becomes an obligation or a compulsion.]

"I see."

Kim Raebin was lost in thought again. Thus, only one member of STier remained. He himself knew it.

STier's Ryu Chungwoo.

"......"

He also knew the appropriate response.

*Right, you really went through a lot. I'm glad your innocence was proven. I'm sorry I didn't find out the news sooner...*

However, such words did not come out easily. It was because of a sense of powerlessness. His original nature, the kind of person who always spoke such words with sincerity, resurfaced in his mind that had gone blank from the shock.

He did not want to resent Lee Sejin A now. It was just... somehow, it felt like his mind had been completely swept away by the overwhelming truth.

"...I see."

That fragmented phrase was all he could manage. He lifted his head and tried not to revert to a dry, weary expression.

It was then.

"Ryu Chungwoo."

[Lee Sejin A : You... seem like you're going to continue with the group here.]

"......"

Ryu Gunwoo relayed the message without taking his eyes off the empty space.

"If you want to do well here, I'll support you. ...You've been through a lot. Thanks for believing in me, and I'm sorry."

Ryu Gunwoo relayed those words without censorship as well, even though they must have been awkward for him as TeSTAR's Park Moondae.

"......"

"Although it took a long time..."

[Lee Sejin A : I won without compromising.]

[Lee Sejin A : It's probably thanks to you, too.]

Ryu Chungwoo did not answer. However, he did not deny it, change the subject, or offer a half-hearted agreement. He just silently held his head up, then eventually lowered it slightly, as if nodding.

[Lee Sejin A : Thanks for listening.]

And so, the conversation with Lee Sejin A ended.

Cha Eugene seemed to want to exchange a few more words with him, but the situation had no choice but to end with Keundal's sudden entrance.

"Hyung! You're going to collapse at this rate...!"

"No, I'm..."

With a brief, indirect briefing that Ryu Gunwoo was overexerting himself, the gathering came to a close. Thanks to that, the former STier members scattered and returned to their own beds, each lost in different thoughts.

"......"

In truth, Lee Sejin A was an implicitly forbidden topic within the group during their STier days. It had happened naturally. There was no opposition. This was because most of them were members he had only spent about half a year with, exchanging only formal conversations. Back then, STier's Lee Sejin A had been extremely defensive and reacted sensitively to his peers.

Still, no one thought that what had just happened was a meaningless event.

Take Kim Raebin, for example.

"Sleep well."

"Yes..."

He obediently lay down in bed at another member's suggestion, but he did not fall asleep right away.

STier's Kim Raebin continued to ponder. True to his diligent nature, formed from his innate disposition and childhood, he doggedly continued to think as Lee Sejin A had suggested.

*To have a different mindset when recalling the past... huh.*

He recalled his time in STier and tried changing the foundational fact, like modifying the beat during an arrangement.

[STier has a member who was arrested on drug charges.]

[STier's drug charge was an injustice.]

It was nothing more than an unjust false accusation.

*Then... my own shortcomings, too.*

Could it be that it was not a matter of talent and effort, but that there might have been another reason? The former idol Kim Raebin, who had given up on the stage, thought absentmindedly.

However, his consciousness soon scattered as sleep washed over him. It was a peaceful sleep.

***

At the same time, Bae Sejin, who had been fast asleep in his comfortable private room, suddenly realized he had regained consciousness.

However, he was not awake.

[Hello.]

*...?*

Before him stood a black-haired man with a calm expression. It was a familiar face.

[They said there was still time to talk with you, so I came.]

Because it was Bae Sejin, himself.

Of course, the sleeping Bae Sejin had no way of knowing that 'Lee Sejin A', returning after his agreement with Ryu Gunwoo, had ridden the System to get here. He reacted like this.

"......"

*What kind of crazy dream is this?*

[What do you think about your current life?]

"What? Who the hell are you."

[...It seems like you're living well, but your personality is the same. It really is me.]

It was an irrelevant answer. However, Bae Sejin would not feel it was unfair, as he also frowned and gave an equally irrelevant answer. A lightning-fast realization had struck him.

"You, are you perhaps the old me? From, was it STier? That group."

[......]

Seeing the other's reaction, Bae Sejin realized he had guessed correctly.

*Oh my god.*

He swallowed with a body that did not even exist.

However, for someone who had caused the team such immense trouble, the version of himself before his eyes looked incredibly solid and mature.

*What is this?*

In any case, he had an urgent question to ask.

"Wait... am I going to become you now, like the other guys? And lose my memories."

Fortunately, Lee Sejin A shook his head.

[That won't happen, but you could end up with these memories.]

"...Right."

Bae Sejin's expression hardened for a moment, but he soon finished his thought and nodded.

"Alright. That's fine."

In fact, he might even need them. He wanted to find out why the members who had regained their memories of debuting in the group 'STier' were all acting so strangely toward him, from Cha Eugene to Ryu Chungwoo, and even Kim Raebin.

He clenched his fists and spoke calmly.

"I'm ready to receive the memories."

Lee Sejin A immediately refused.

[I'm not going to do that.]

"...What?"

For a moment, Bae Sejin was at a loss for words.

[I'm sorry, but I only came because I wanted to check on my own state myself.]

'The former STier Lee Sejin A' was, after all, the consciousness of the sleeping Bae Sejin himself, just with different memories. In other words, once he had seen himself objectively, he planned to return the memories without any lingering attachment.

[You don't have to remember and know everything about the past. Growth can happen in other ways, you know.]

Pain was not the only thing that made a person grow.

*And it seems that the current me, TeSTAR's Bae Sejin, is growing well.*

Lee Sejin A nodded as if in acknowledgment.

However, Bae Sejin's expression turned serious.

"Why do you get to decide that on your own?"

[...!]

"I know my own condition better than you do."

Give me the memories.

...And so, the two consciousnesses of the same person began a fierce self-debate in the middle of the night.
