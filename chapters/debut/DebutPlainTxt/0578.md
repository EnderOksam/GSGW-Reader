# Chapter 578

Emotions from people who watched the concert without me poured in.

[This is so devastating... I cried the whole time during his solo song VCR because I was so worried]

[I endured this shitty January just for this concert, so why is Park Moondae the one who got screwed over instead of me]

[I gave up a trip to Hawaii to see our crybaby Moon Puppy at the first concert, and he's not even here... I can't believe this is real]

[Can't they make a proper announcement? The agency's handling of this is just pathetic]

Anger, devastation, disappointment.

The audience's disappointment over a member suddenly dropping out of a long-awaited concert was exactly what I expected.

But one thing was different.

[Ah, what's going to happen to Park Moondae]

*Me.*

The feeling of betrayal, that I, Park Moondae, had deceitfully ruined this important moment, was absent.

The reason was presupposed in every post as if it were obvious.

[We all know how much the concert means to him... This can't be happening. Moondae, you're not in serious trouble, are you? Ah, please]

It was because TeSTAR's Park Moondae had not lived his life that way.

Every time I scrolled, people shared and cited all sorts of examples.

Park Moondae, who cries the most at concerts. Park Moondae, who nonchalantly finished his solo stage even with a broken rib. Park Moondae, who would excitedly chatter about how the setlist was created during the after-party live stream every time a concert ended...

[He's the type of person who'd get on stage even with a broken leg]

Everything they had experienced became evidence.

I thought my desertion would draw even more criticism because it contrasted with Park Moondae's past actions, but the opposite happened.

My actions had become my guarantee.

They made people naturally assume I did not skip the concert, but that I*could not*attend.

Park Moondae was on their side.

"......"

I dazedly read through the posts filled with worry, confusion, anger, and anxiety.

The stories subconsciously took it for granted that I must have had a critical reason for not showing up.

"Hoo..."

A heat rose from deep within my breath and sent a tremor through my limbs.

Unsure if it was a thrill or goosebumps, I stared blankly at the screen.

But I soon realized the reality of the situation.

*No.*

This public opinion only exists because they do not know the exact circumstances.

I did not know what Ryu Chungwoo said, but if he said something appropriate like, 'Moondae was unable to join us today due to an urgent matter'...

*...They don't know why I left.*

Then their sense of betrayal would be even greater the moment they learned the truth.

Much greater.

"......"

I gripped the smartphone so tightly it felt like it would break.

My mind was spinning.

An excuse. An excuse.

*Is saying I was sick the best option?*

Saying I collapsed was the safest path with the fewest repercussions. It would simply mean I could not get on stage. If I used that, they might continue to understand, just as they were now.

But I soon realized it was practically impossible.

*Witness accounts will definitely surface.*

It was over the moment I got into the rescue vehicle wearing my stage costume without a mask.

They would appear. Unconditionally.

It was a fact I already knew.

Besides, using the excuse of being sick meant I would have to skip today's concert too, or at least pretend to be ill.

That in itself would be a deception.

"......"

The heat that had been surging through me cooled into a chill.

I looked at those unbelievably supportive posts and closed my eyes.

It was a waste.

*You conscienceless bastard.*

It was laughable. Instead of racking my brain on how to compensate for the ticket prices and wasted time, I was pulling this shit because I did not want to be criticized.

*Get a grip.*

I opened my eyes and started searching again.

First, I needed to check the official reason given for my concert desertion.

A look at posts from the start of the concert revealed it clearly.

[They said Moondae can't perform today, this is crazy...]

[Park Moondae's concert absence announcement (Video)]

[The guys can't even finish their sentences... Ah]

In the video, Ryu Chungwoo explained the situation with a calm tone and a gentle expression.

Just as I predicted, he did not lie. He concluded with something like, 'Moondae is not in a situation where he can come on stage right now. We are sorry.'

He seemed to be avoiding delving too deeply into bad news during a concert that was supposed to be fun.

It was a wise decision.

I also confirmed he had not given any specific details about my situation, just as I expected.

"......"

I stared blankly at the video of the concert without me, then quickly came to my senses and scrolled again.

*I should check the refund policy too.*

I was about to navigate to the company's official social media account.

Just then, a new post appeared on the page I had mindlessly refreshed.

*Hm?*

It was a popular post I had not seen before because it did not come up with the keyword 'concert'.

The post was from around the time I fell asleep.

Posted around midnight yesterday, the content had been shared an enormous number of times.

[Park Moondae's acquaintance got stranded and Park Moondae was the last person they contacted. This is insane. They said the person would have frozen to death without Moondae;;;]

"...!"

CLATTER.

My movement made the cot clatter loudly.

The owner of the hospital room seemed to hear it too.

Keundal stirred.

"...You're awake..."

He must have woken up when I sat up. He poked his head out, then immediately shut his mouth when he saw the smartphone in my hand.

"......"

When I stared at him, he could not hold it in for long and confessed.

"I didn't post it!"

Then who?

"But... I did agree to let your company post about it."

"...!"

"I got a phone call."

"From whom."

A bright answer came back.

"From Lee Sejin!"

***

"Yes, yes, thank you so much. We'll proceed as discussed then. ...Oh my, not at all. Please take care of yourself~"

Lee Sejin carefully finished his call with Keundal and hung up.

*He's not completely clueless.*

It was a relief he was so cooperative, even though he must have been disoriented after his rescue.

He stretched and mentally reviewed the last thing he had to do before bed.

Alright... then.

"Is everything settled?"

"......"

Ryu Chungwoo, still on the phone with the company, nodded slightly.

Park Moondae's worries were only half right.

It was true. Right after the concert, Ryu Chungwoo had explained the situation to the flabbergasted company and mediated the conflict.

But the situation changed after Lee Sejin received a reply from Park Moondae, who had rescued Keundal.

[If you could just wait a little longer, I think we'll have more news. Yes. Please.]

Until now, Ryu Chungwoo had stopped the company from releasing an official statement to handle the situation.

Because.

"Then I'll send the email as is."

Lee Sejin had prepared something else.

The days before his debut, when he was unfamiliar with the internet's nature, were long gone.

Since his debut, he had continuously monitored feedback, so he was bound to become familiar with the field.

He now understood that ecosystem well enough to grasp how skillfully Park Moondae had handled his own school violence controversy during ❬Idol Inc.❭.

Moreover, he learned even faster in a field where he had a natural talent.

Public opinion warfare.

*The atmosphere and the justification are just as important here, too.*

Whether the stage was a classroom, a practice room, or a filming set, Lee Sejin's specialty never faded.

He had been racking his brain since he received the long text from Park Moondae explaining the situation.

Part of his plan was stopping the company, which kept trying to spin the story as Park Moondae collapsing and going to the hospital, and silence witness accounts from the rescue team.

Of course, Lee Sejin understood why the company suggested that.

*They think the audience needs to sympathize with and relate to the reason Park Moondae couldn't perform...*

But isn't sympathy a rather ambiguous feeling?

Even among a few dozen teenagers, opinions on whether to sympathize are easily divided.

But trying to explain it so countless people would willingly offer emotional support? In Lee Sejin's opinion, that was a reckless attempt.

*It's not sustainable in the long run.*

That was why Lee Sejin disagreed with the company.

There was a surefire method, so there was no need to do otherwise.

What was needed was not sympathy, but...

*A hierarchy.*

A top and a bottom.

He had to make it so people would not dare complain after hearing the reason for Park Moondae's absence.

He had to make them assess the values and willingly admit, 'My inconvenience is nothing. Park Moondae's absence was truly necessary.'

And he already knew one justification that trumped everything else.

*A person's life is paramount.*

Saving a person could win almost any value judgment.

And Park Moondae happened to be in that exact situation.

[Rescue... successful.]

He had not just run out, worried about a relative's death.

*Moondae was helpful.*

He had to emphasize that Moondae was necessary to save a person's life.

That was why Lee Sejin asked Park Moondae about the situation several times as soon as they connected on the phone, just to get a clear picture.

[So you narrowed down the search area, and what you said at the end was spot on, which is how Ryu Gunwoo was rescued, right? Phew, that's a relief.]

[...Yeah.]

Then, all he had to do was reconstruct the incident around the facts he wanted to publicize.

[TeSTAR's Park Moondae, Absent from Concert to Aid in Rescue of Missing Person... "Diligently Cooperated as the Last Witness"]

If he added the details reporters confirmed by calling the rescue workers...

[Whoa]

[Ah, Moondae-yah ㅠㅠㅠㅠ]

[You must have been so shocked. Oh my god, I'm so glad they were rescued safely]

[They said without Moondae, they couldn't have narrowed the search area and the person might have frozen to death. Wow, that's terrifying]

[You did well, staying calm, Moondae-yah... It must have been hard for you, not being able to be at the concert. You really went through a lot...]

The choice Park Moondae was forced to make became 'Concert VS Saving a Life'.

With this framing, choosing the concert would make him a piece of trash who only cares about his work. Park Moondae simply made the obvious choice.

The atmosphere would shift, making it extremely difficult to criticize Park Moondae.

*Hmm~ Nice.*

Lee Sejin grinned.

Besides, it was the plain truth anyway.

*That person even said himself that he was saved thanks to Park Moondae.*

Releasing an official report with this framing also drew a line.

There was no need to appeal to feelings or publicize how close they were, whether they were sworn brothers or anything similar.

This became an issue where anyone would have to help, even a coworker. Their closeness became irrelevant.

The focus would be on Park Moondae's reasons for missing the concert, and Ryu Gunwoo's personal information would be diverted from the center of attention.

[I hope Moondae gets plenty of rest. I want to see him healthy and bright at tomorrow's concert ㅠㅠ]

*That's right~ I feel the same way!*

And so, the situation was resolved.

Lee Sejin smiled affably. He could finally go to bed and prepare for tomorrow's concert.

He felt a strange nostalgia, as if he were repaying a very old favor.

***

[Being honest is the best, isn't it~?]

"......"

I pressed the bridge of my nose.

Around 6:40 in the morning, Lee Sejin, who had called first, answered my question, 'What is all this?' just like that.

*This sly snake.*

Look at the board he set up. It was obvious he had considered everything, so what was he talking about?

He must have been racking his brain the moment the concert ended.

Thinking about that, I felt... something.

I closed my eyes and looked at the articles that had neatly eliminated all my worries.

[...Thanks.]

[Hey. It's just the facts being reported. Why do you keep thanking me?]

He feigned modesty a few more times. Then, perhaps noticing my voice had lightened, he asked comfortably.

[You're coming to the practice room now, right?]

It felt like the words 'without anything weighing on your mind' were tacked on to the end.

"......"

I finished my recollection and picked up my smartphone again.

It was not that no one was cursing me out, despite the consideration my members had shown.

My bailing on the concert in real-time was an undeniable fact.

[Gommeo ran away ㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋ]

[Give me my money back, you fucking bastard]

[Ditching the first concert in real-time... Truly legendary, for real ㅅㅂ ㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋ hilarious]

[Wow, screwing over people who bought plane tickets, booked accommodations, and traveled a long way just to see him months in advance, and now he's the one getting comforted. It's so off-putting]

[That missing person should have died. Then Gommeo would have regretted not just doing the concert. So annoying ㅜ]

But these were just the kind of opinions that would pop up no matter what controversy broke out.

I suddenly realized.

*Right.*

That was how it was.

At some point, I had started looking too much at what lay beneath the surface. I had begun to think this was how people truly felt.

I suppose I had unconsciously judged this was the kind of opinion someone would have if you removed all courtesy and morality.

But just because the expression was provocative and shocking did not make it the truth.

I tapped the smartphone screen.

*It's just... there are bastards like this out there, that's all.*

Sometimes I might refer to them to strategize, but it was ridiculous to be influenced by them.

This was just a current, just a reaction.

*It's fine just to know that they exist.*

I left that page without lingering and returned to the social media feed I was originally looking at.

New stories from people with my picture as their profile poured in.

[Moondae will probably go even harder today... Ha, I'm so glad I got tickets for both days. I was going crazy]

[I know TeSTAR. They'll 100% schedule an additional concert and give priority ticketing to the audience from the first day. I'm calling it]

They made predictions as if they were obvious, free of anxiety.

"......"

You know what?

I used to think that gaining seniority was a burden.

The range of things we could do would shrink. People could roughly predict the group's scope of action, so they were not easily surprised.

It becomes harder and harder to receive fresh interest.

So I thought the disadvantages overwhelmingly outweighed the advantages...

In truth, there was an irreplaceable advantage I had not perceived.

*Was this it?*

It was trust.

If we had done well in our activities up to now, that also fell within people's range of predictions. That prediction suppressed anxiety and sustained their faith.

*Right.*

So... I would have to repay that, too.

I swiped to the next page again.

This time, my wiki page came up.

On the profile, a tag read, 'Cannot be edited by users who have not been registered for 15 days'. A separate page had finally been added.

[Incidents and Controversies]

But it was okay.

*It was bound to be added.*

I had become the fourth member of this team to have that page separated and attached.

And just as the others had done during their own times, it was now my time to make up for it.

To push the controversy off the table.

"Keundal."

"Yes?"

"About today's concert. They're streaming it, you know."

"......"

"If you have nothing to do at the hospital... watch it."

"...! Yes!"

I rose from my seat.

For today's concert.
