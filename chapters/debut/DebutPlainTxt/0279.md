# Chapter 279

Five minutes after the psychological test turned into a zombie escape...

"Watch your head."

"Hoo."

Seeing that there were pre-installed cameras in the middle, I figured this was the correct route.

But in fact, finding the production team in an unexpected situation was a natural flow, so the the transition made sense.

It was a cliché, but it was good to focus on because the air vents were dark and the atmosphere was eerie.

"...!"

*Kwagwagwang!!*

"Aaaagh!"

"I-I'll open it right away!"

Beside Kim Raebin, who was screaming, Seon Ahyeon reached out and pulled the side of the vent.

I immediately opened my mouth.

"I can't hear any zombies here."

Ryu Chungwoo immediately responded.

"Let's go in. Be careful of the person in front!"

"Yes!"

One by one, we exited the vents.

Relying on the dim light from the vents, I made sure that those who went down first supported the others, and then came out almost at the same time as Ryu Chungwoo.

And closed the vent.

*Bang!*

However, it came off again. Lee Sejin calmly exclaimed.

"This cannot be fixed! Check if there is anything we can use to fix it!"

"Wait, wait! If we push this..."

Bae Sejin pointed to something dark around where he was standing. Kim Raebin ran and shouted.

"It's a cabinet!"

"Move!"

And just in case, the personnel were immediately divided into three holding the vent closed and four moving the cabinets.

But There was no time to spare.

*Thud.*

...Vibrations were transmitted to the hands holding the vent.

"It's open."

Zombies had smashed the vents and entered the passage. I turned the corner and checked the zombies that appeared.

"Right there..."

"Hey, it's coming!"

The atmosphere was convincing enough to make me sweat.

*They must be controlling the speed, right?*

Since this was set in a research institute, so it was unlikely that they wouldn't give us even the minimum amount of time to find a solution.

However, the zombies who came from the end of the vent passage, twisting their whole bodies, were quite... No, they were fucking fast.

*Damn it.*

They were already right in front of us.

Just in case, I put my weight on the two hands holding the side of the vent.

I was grateful for the enthusiastic performance during filming, but they should tone it down a little...

"Get out of the way!!"

I reflexively turned my body to the side. Iron furniture kicked in at the spot where Ryu Chungwoo retreated in the same direction.

*Bang!*

It was the cabinet. Huge furniture covered more than half of the vents and shielded us.

And at that moment, The zombie slammed into the cabinet.

"Kyaaaagh!"

*Thud!*

The cabinet, which had taken four grown men to move, was heavy enough, but the zombies couldn't penetrate it.

"Ah!"

However, Cha Eugene, who was at the front, almost collided with a zombie in the vent due to the force of his push.

"...!"

"Kyaaak!"

Ryu Chungwoo and I reflexively pulled Cha Eugene. The guy jumped up and backed away.

"Are you okay?"

"No problem. I avoided it!"

Cha Eugene smiled with a normal face. It looked like blood didn't get on him.

"Huh, you have good luck. I thought we would have to tie Eugene up~"

"Haha!"

It was only then that a few people laughed at Lee Sejin's joke. It seemed that the excessive immersion in the bloody situation had been relieved.

And, of course, as soon as I came to my senses, I was flooded with irritation.

It was even worse than when they said they would let us travel but then made us work part-time at a hotteok business.

"Wow, but really... I never thought this would happen! The producers, really!"

"I really didn't expect..."

"...Somehow, when we came in, we signed a consent form. I thought it was because of the inspection."

"Shh!"

In the meantime, Cha Eugene seriously put a finger on top of his mouth.

"There's another zombie out there! Let's see here first!"

He was excited.

It was obvious from the twinkle in his eyes that he found this amusing.

"Yes, Eugene is right. Let's start by checking where this is. It's dark, so let's make groups of two... I will go alone."

"All right. Leader!"

Realizing that we were filming again, we quickly resumed our search.

*They're going to edit it anyway, so it would have been fun to talk about it a bit more.*

I shrugged and started looking around, relying on the dim light coming from the vents and past the zombies.

"Hyung, although my vision is narrow due to my eyesight since it is dark, I can always move as directed, so just tell me..."

"Okay. Don't worry, I'll tell you if there's a problem."

"Yes!"

*I don't think any zombie actors can get in here. It's an enclosed room...*

I quickly moved to the wall with Kim Raebin, who was nearby. And the moment my hand finally touched the wall...

"...!"

...Wood.

*Dark, closed, unfamiliar wooden space...*

A little, I was feeling down.

"Hyung? If there's a suspicious liquid on the wall over there..."

I moved my hand again.

"No, it's clean. I slowed down because I was careful."

"Okay! You are smart. We never know where the zombies will appear!"

Okay. This was an overreaction.

*It's a shoot.*

It was already entertainment enough at the point of setting up a zombie virus.

It was obvious that they were all dressed up anyway, so it didn't matter if it was sealed or not...

"Wait, this."

"Yes?"

"On the wall... It has something like a metal box on it, but it's rather big."

"...!"

"Moondae, what did you find?"

"Oh, hold on."

"I will check this way!"

"There's like a lock on the other side! Shall we open it?"
