# Chapter 504

After STier Ryu Chungwoo woke up, stage practice went as smoothly as possible.

Ryu Chungwoo quickly learned the latest TeSTAR songs and hits that the members had painstakingly selected. He didn't complain once.

He learned so proactively, going beyond just being taught the songs and choreography, that even Keun Sejin was taken aback.

"Aigoo, hyung. It's no problem if you want to take a break and slow down the tempo. Our tour is on a break since we just got back to the country anyway!"

"I see."

However, Ryu Chungwoo stuck to his attitude.

The guy wasn't unnecessarily wary or prideful. He straightforwardly asked for what he needed and practiced both diligently and quickly.

*Hmm.*

He didn't stop there. The guy even started monitoring the original TeSTAR Ryu Chungwoo's stage videos and other various TeSTAR videos.

"I-If you need, I can tell you... in more detail...!"

"Ah. It's alright."

Despite Seon Ahyeon's fidgety offer, the man simply continued his monitoring. The reason was simple.

"These are songs I'm performing for the first time in my memory, so I have to make up for my lack of stage experience this way."

"......"

"If I need to, I'll shamelessly ask for help."

"N-No...! Please, feel free to ask me anytime...!"

And so, STier Ryu Chungwoo eventually replicated the original Ryu Chungwoo's feel in the latest song with remarkable similarity.

"...It's almost identical, right?"

"Yeah."

It took him exactly three days to reach this point. It was like watching some extreme rehabilitation training.

So, that evening, I couldn't help but ask.

[Just roll the dice-]

I saw Ryu Chungwoo through his doorway, rewatching a stage performance on a large monitor. I turned and asked Cha Eugene, who was raiding the kitchen.

"Got any idea why he's acting like that?"

*Isn't it good that he's being cooperative?*

Of course, it's good for work. I could even feel relieved, thinking what a stroke of luck this was when I expected things to go to shit.

However, whether this is normal is another matter entirely.

*He wakes up to a different world and accepts it that quickly?*

When I woke up in Park Moondae's body, I fell out of bed. I only accepted reality because the Status Window popped up, and I figured I'd die if I didn't get my act together.

But to think someone can be that calm just because people around him talked a bit.

He even treats the members who were originally his STier members, and Bae Sejin who was a former member, without any particular agitation.

Is it possible for a human to be like that?

*Even for an archery gold medalist, this is too much.*

Isn't this a bit strange? If I were in his shoes, I might have grabbed someone and started a hostage situation to dig out the unadorned truth.

*It's to the point where Cha Eugene running away from home feels like the more normal reaction.*

That's why I asked the person who had experienced it himself. This was the answer I got.

"Umm... yeah. It's weird, but I can't be sure."

"......"

Cha Eugene himself remembered his time in STier not in detail, but in terms of major events and feelings, so a specific analysis of behavioral principles was impossible.

However, the guy shrugged and said with a rather serious face.

[What's certain is, I don't feel like that mysterious hyung is 'pretending' to cooperate with this team.]

*Hmm.*

*Well... I agree with that, but still.*

If he were only pretending to cooperate while planning to backstab us, it would eventually show when he's practicing to the point of exhaustion.

Isn't a person's composure derived from their physical stamina?

*If his priorities were different, it would be obvious.*

However, that STier Ryu Chungwoo was consistent. He was truly preparing his stage parts with 'sincerity'. I don't doubt that either.

*In that case.*

I guess I can ask him directly.

He wasn't as impossible to talk to as STier Cha Eugene was. Besides, he seemed to be observing his surroundings, so he would probably sense that I had no ill intentions.

"Give me one."

"Ooh."

I snatched some of the popcorn Cha Eugene had bought, popped a few in my mouth, and headed to the guy's room with him.

Conveniently, my roommate is the target.

"Hyung."

"Ah."

STier Ryu Chungwoo tore his eyes from the monitor and turned his head. A friendly smile appeared on his face out of habit.

"Is there something you need to tell me?"

"No, it's not like that."

I sat down next to him and offered the popcorn. The guy seemed a bit surprised, but he took some without a word. I also tossed some popcorn into my mouth and chose my words.

*I'll keep it from getting too serious.*

"I was just wondering if we were putting too much pressure on you. It won't cause any sudden problems if we take a break from group activities, so it's okay for you to take it easy until you've adjusted."

"......"

"Although, we might have to beg you if things get urgent."

"Hm."

Ryu Chungwoo chuckled, like air escaping a balloon. Right, I said it to be funny.

*Though I'm serious.*

*Come on, open up.*

Fortunately, the guy massaged the back of his neck as if to loosen it, then soon spoke with a faint smile.

"Yes, I don't feel pressured. It seems like a successful group, so I thought each and every member had to do well."

"......"

"For now, I'm a member of the group."

I sensed a strange sentiment in his tone.

*...Is he overlapping this with his own group?*

The STier this guy originally belonged to had disbanded, but here, the current TeSTAR is doing well, even having renewed their contracts.

In Cha Eugene's case, he felt a sense of disconnect from this difference, but this guy seems to feel a kind of... responsibility even in the current situation. About maintaining the status quo.

*Hmm.*

Well, it's all speculation, but I can at least be sure that his actions aren't driven by any ill will toward the team. I nodded inwardly.

*But he could have ulterior motives beyond what he's said.*

Besides, I still don't know what this guy is thinking. From now on, I'll try to steer the conversation in a way that eases his tension.

"Thank you, and please speak casually. You're older than me."

"Hmm... haha, should I?"

STier Ryu Chungwoo seemed to hesitate for a moment, but he soon dropped the formalities coolly. That part of him hadn't changed.

*That's right.*

Just as I was about to slowly bring up other topics to gauge his remaining wariness...

"Moondae-hyung is actually older."

I almost threw the popcorn.

*When did you get here?*

When I turned around, Cha Eugene was staring intently at my popcorn with a blank face.

*Go pop your own.*

Why is a guy with a whole box full of it still hung up on what's already been taken? But seeing him happily munch away when I offered him some, I couldn't really say anything.

In the moment I glanced at Cha Eugene, I heard Ryu Chungwoo ask back in a puzzled voice.

"He's older?"

"Moondae-hyung was originally an adult."

"You're an adult too."

"You know what I mean! Moondae-hyung's mental? age is older than Chungwoo-hyung. Yep!"

"......"

*Oh, great.*

I had already given him a summary of my eventful supernatural experiences, so Ryu Chungwoo wasn't shocked. That doesn't mean he laughed and accepted it. He was clearly flustered.

*No, why did that punk Cha Eugene bring this up... well, I get it.*

Maybe I should just take this as an opportunity and talk about it.

"Strictly speaking, that is correct."

An opportunity to naturally and completely lower his guard. I rubbed the back of my neck and opened my mouth.

"Well, since it came up... I think the more important fact is that I'm originally your relative."

"Huh?"

*Isn't the world all about school ties, regional ties, and blood ties? Let's go with the blood-tie meta.*

"Um, my old name was Ryu Gunwoo. As you can tell from the name, we're from the same generation of the Pungsan Ryu clan."

STier Ryu Chungwoo wore a dazed expression, as if he'd been hit over the head.

"We only went on a few family trips together when we were young, that's all. You probably don't remember. You didn't remember before either, and there's only a family trip... video."

"......"

No response.

*Was it a bust? If he thinks I'm lying, this is all for nothing.*

It was at that moment. Ryu Chungwoo slowly opened his mouth.

"If you're older than me... did you happen to wear glasses and read a lot of books?"

"...!"

*No way.*

"You remember?"

STier Ryu Chungwoo furrowed his brow slightly.

"Um... I don't really know what you looked like, and I didn't know your name. Now that I try to recall, I just have a vague memory that a relative like that existed."

His face brightened slightly.

"Seeing your reaction, I guess my description was right."

"Oh!"

Cha Eugene exclaimed in his place. I... was a bit dumbfounded. I hadn't expected this.

*He remembers me?*

Come to think of it, the TeSTAR Ryu Chungwoo had never tried to recall who 'Ryu Gunwoo' was after hearing such specific conditions. I had just abruptly played the Family Trip Video for him before.

In any case, I pulled myself together and answered.

"Yes. I think that's me."

You're right.

"Ooh!"

*You can stop being impressed now.*

Anyway, a faint smile spread across Ryu Chungwoo's face as well.

"This is amazing. I never thought I'd meet a relative like this."

"Me neither."

"Haha."

I realized anew that STier Ryu Chungwoo had been quite tense until now.

*His attitude has changed.*

He was still gentle, but his gestures looked much more relaxed.

*He was on guard after all. He couldn't not be.*

Now this guy finally seems a bit more human. In this situation with strangers, or familiar people who had become strange, he seemed to have loosened up after meeting a simple relative. Because it's a simple and uncomplicated relationship.

"Then from now on, um, should I be the one to speak formally? How did you two usually talk?"

"We just mixed formal and informal speech comfortably."

"Ahaha! Alright, I'll do that."

And so, I believe the blood-tie meta was a success.

"Let's start from here."

"Yep!"

After that, Ryu Chungwoo treated the members with a more relaxed demeanor. It seemed he soon started speaking casually with all of them.

"I don't think we need to postpone the schedule for this program either. I'm sure I can do it."

Following his firm decision, TeSTAR's participation in the survival program proceeded as scheduled, and so, the filming for the first stage began.

The fortunate thing was that it wasn't a survival stage right from the start. It was a pre-release for the program's promotion. An introduction stage.

***

The renewal of ❬Genuine Match❭.

For a name as childishly dramatic as ❬True Competition Final❭, it generated a pretty decent amount of buzz online. It was thanks to the lineup.

[The Reborn ❬Genuine Match❭, Grand Reveal of the Star-Studded Cast... From Grand Prize-Winning Idols to Legendary Vocalists.]

So many big names were appearing that it was hard to believe it was a renewal of an already declining program. The production team's casting ability, which had worked hard to sell the program using TeSTAR's name, had exploded.

[As expected of a highly anticipated variety show from a public broadcast network, the singer lineup is great. So many talented peopleㄷㄷㄷ I'm looking forward to it]

[SoftSweet reunion? I have to watch this live!]

[Honey Voice Myudi goddess~ I want to see your stage soon^^♡]

[Looks like MBS is betting everything on this. Amazing!]

Although reactions from part-time commenters initially flooded the articles and promotional videos, genuinely surprised comments soon pushed them aside without much ridicule.

Of course, one name in this lineup received the most attention.

*TeSTAR!*

[TeSTAR is really appearing? This is for real?]

Usually, a group big enough to win a Grand Prize only appears on a survival show long after their heyday has passed. Even then, they usually only appear after much deliberation.

For a group whose status had already risen as high as it could, the program was nothing more than a poisoned chalice. No matter how promising it was, the probability of damaging their own reputation was higher.

They were likely to become fodder for buzz, or a sacrifice for a new name to make their mark as 'the rookie who beat that famous group'.

It was the same from the program producer's perspective. Having an already famous group win lacks surprise and isn't fun.

Moreover, TeSTAR was a group that debuted from another broadcast network's survival show! If there was a contestant who could pull off a stage better than, or even comparable to, TeSTAR, they would be favored!

[If TeSTAR wins here, it'll become the official formula that T-Net ❭ Public Broadcast, so they'll definitely make sure they can't win ㅋㅋ]

[Seeing TeSTAR fans already laying the groundwork in preparation for their failure is so solemn ㅠ It's pitiful]

[Why on earth did they appear]

On the internet, where people were unaware of the secret agreement between the PD and TeSTAR, anticipation was building. Even if half of that anticipation was the hope of seeing TeSTAR fail.

However, a few days later, the moment the opening stage for ❬Genuine Match❭ was released on WeTube.

[Dream a dream as blue as day]

People felt a sensation as if they were being jolted awake.

[Ah ㅋㅋ]

[They rose to fame with their stages, so of course]

[They're gonna dominate again, I see ㅇㅋㅇㅋ They're confident, is what this is]

The stage was structured with each contestant performing for one minute and passing the baton.

TeSTAR's introduction stage appeared right before the end. It featured an exquisite remix of their debut song and latest hit, and was as intense as it was short.

[Yeah!]

Befitting the one-minute time limit, it was a powerful stage that seemed to have stripped away the introduction and left only the core, like a summarized edit of the main points!

Just as any movie seems fun when you encounter it through a plot summary video, the stage, which left only a fast pace and 'wow' points, was perfectly suited to the tastes of modern people.

[This is it]

[Keuh, just like a survival group]

[I'm so freaking excited for TeSTAR. This is crazy.]

Of course, it wasn't all good.

TeSTAR was already a group with extremely high recognition and popularity in this program. 'Doing well' was just breaking even. On top of that, they delivered an impressive opening stage, which raised the already high expectations even further.

Even the twin fansite master who photographed both Park Moondae and Lee Sejin gave this cold assessment.

*It would have been better if they were just moderately, ambiguously good.*

However, the die had already been cast. TeSTAR had to begin the first survival competition under these conditions.

And it wasn't that there was no way out.

*If they do so well in the next competition stage that they meet the already sky-high expectations...*

An irrefutable image would be completed. The most important thing in a survival show is an impactful first impression. TeSTAR could start by firmly planting a positive prejudice.

And TeSTAR themselves knew that.

***

Right after the opening stage ended.

"Hoo."

"You all worked hard~"

Backstage at the ❬Genuine Match❭ filming set, I had a gut feeling.

*We did well.*

I was sure of it without even seeing the reactions.

To be honest, I wasn't worried. The only variable was STier Ryu Chungwoo, but just by looking at his practice volume and rehearsal stages over the past few days, it was clear.

*He's not the type to make a mistake.*

And indeed, the guy pulled off a perfectly synchronized performance. So much so that for a moment, I mistook him for the original Ryu Chungwoo.

However...

*He looks dazed.*

The problem was that right after coming off stage, the guy's condition was a bit strange.

"......"

STier Ryu Chungwoo stood there motionlessly, panting without a word. He was so still that even the staff were looking, as if he were in shock.

*Has it been too long?*

It must have been his first stage since STier's disbandment. I think Cha Eugene had similar symptoms.

"Are you alright?"

I approached him and offered the water I was holding.

Ryu Chungwoo blankly accepted it, then suddenly lifted his head as if he had come to his senses. He coolly opened the water bottle and drank.

GULP, GULP.

"Hah..."

After emptying almost the entire bottle, Ryu Chungwoo smiled quite brightly. He focused on monitoring the performance himself, behind the other members who were also watching.

"Thanks. ...It's been a while since I've done something like this."

*He seems fine.*

I let out a small laugh.

It seemed like everything would go well.

Until then.
