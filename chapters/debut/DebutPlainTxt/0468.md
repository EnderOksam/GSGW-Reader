# Chapter 468

Seoul. Inside a quiet office.

"I'll get straight to the point!"

"Yes. Thank you."

Bae Sejin sat across the table and tried his best to remain calm.

*It's really... happening.*

It had been a very long time since Bae Sejin had a meeting for a project.

He had memories of acting for several years in a bizarre virtual world. Despite being recent, that experience felt hazy, like a distant event or a vivid dream.

Wondering if that was why he felt so nervous, Bae Sejin took a short, deep breath.

Soon, the person he waited for appeared in a hurry.

"Sejin, you're here!"

"...! Hello."

It was the producer, holding a stack of documents. He was the one who offered Bae Sejin the role.

And he had brought someone else with him.

"Producer, Director."

"Yeah~ Sejin, you've grown up so much."

The director, who had a magnanimous air, grinned and sat on the sofa.

*So it was him...!*

He was the director of a project where Bae Sejin, as a child actor, had played a kidnapped child.

His track record was good. He produced a hit about every other project.

*...Good.*

This was worth betting on.

Bae Sejin bowed his head deeply in greeting before he sat down again.

"The kid is exactly the same. He grew up just as calm as he was."

"Isn't he? He still has that upright, model-student vibe."

*Ahem! I'm not *that* straight-laced.*

Just as Bae Sejin thought about how he had gone off the rails a fair bit (?) himself, the proposal finally arrived.

FLUTTER.

A document passed across the table, and the project was briefly explained.

*A modern mystery horror...*

Based on a webtoon, it depicted a slightly bizarre and chilling modern-day Korea in the 21st century.

As Bae Sejin quickly scanned the contents, a friendly explanation followed.

"It's a Netplus original drama."

"Ah."

In that case, the capital was completely separate from T1, as long as the production studio was not involved.

*Then... I can do this properly.*

*If it's a drama and not a movie, the filming period will be longer, but wouldn't it be fine if I promote the next album then?*

It was then, as Bae Sejin wracked his brain.

"But actually... we've already filmed quite a lot of it, you see?"

"...!"

*Wait a minute.*

"...And you're casting me now?"

*Is it a seasonal show? Maybe I'm supposed to appear in season 2...*

"The thing is, we tried using a rookie who showed some promise... but that crazy bastard filmed some video and an article came out. Something about regional discrimination or whatever!"

"...Ahem."

"Our Sejin has been in the industry for almost 20 years and he's still clean, right? But some rookie has to go and lose his mind!"

The director was about to foam at the mouth, ranting that kids these days have nothing but WeTube, InHeart, and TikTakTok in their heads.

The producer quickly steered the conversation away by offering the director a sugar-bomb coffee.

"Anyway, because of that, we had to cut out his scenes entirely... the schedule got pushed back, and we had an opening. Then Sejin contacted us at the perfect time, so we could work together after all this time, see?"

"Ah... thank you."

"Don't mention it. It feels like fate brought us together again!"

"It really does~ Thanks to our producer here~"

"Hey, it's only happening because Sejin gave us the okay~"

The director and producer grinned slyly, and Bae Sejin's nerves sharpened.

"...Then is the role you're offering me the one that rookie was playing?"

"...! Ah, but the role is definitely significant!"

A hurried explanation followed.

"We met with this person and that person, held a ton of auditions, but we just couldn't find the right fit."

They said the rookie was the one they finally managed to find. They had to persuade the investors to let them use him since there was no other replacement.

"Netplus is pretty generous about these things, you know? But it was such a good role that they even asked if we couldn't use someone with more recognition!"

"And since he was a rookie with no promotional value anyway, no articles came out about it! So there won't be any talk about you being a replacement or anything, you know?"

"Ah, yes."

Bae Sejin nodded, but the producer and director watched him carefully. They worried he might be offended, thinking he was being treated as a mere 'fill-in'.

However, that was not what Bae Sejin was concerned about.

*Since the filming period is shorter, the release date will probably be sooner than I thought.*

Being able to see the final product sooner was not a bad thing.

But at the same time, one thing went down the drain.

*The album promotion... becomes awkward.*

This was because Netplus had a policy of releasing all episodes of a drama season at once, or in two batches at most.

They needed to secure that much editing time, so the release would be subtly delayed.

In other words, this drama would only be released around the end of the current album's promotional activities.

*...Maybe a month or two.*

If so, it really would not be helpful for the album promotion.

"......"

Bae Sejin managed his expression and asked himself a question.

*Could they really not have known?*The members knew far more about idol activities than he did.

The fact that they let him lump an uncertain project like a drama or movie, with its unknown release date, under the umbrella of 'album promotion' was...

*...They were just supporting me.*

Whether it helped the group's activities or not, they did it just for him, their fellow member, Bae Sejin.

Bae Sejin fought back the surge of emotion welling up inside him again.

"...First, let me take a look at the role, then."

"Yes, yes!"

"Wow, Sejin talks exactly the same as when he was a little kid."

Bae Sejin pushed the documents aside and picked up what was underneath. It was a new, neatly completed script.

He flipped through it with a fighting spirit.

*I'll do well.*

*Whatever the role, I'll pull it off perfectly, no matter what.*

*That would be my way of repaying their support...*

"......"

"How is it, Sejin?"

*Re, repaying.*

"A lot of idols tend to start with safe, supporting roles... but with his acting skills, Sejin shouldn't be treated like he's on a similar path!"

"......"

"Don't you think it'd be great to strongly imprint your image as an actor with a psychopath role like this?"

"Th-"

Bae Sejin started to break out in a cold sweat.
