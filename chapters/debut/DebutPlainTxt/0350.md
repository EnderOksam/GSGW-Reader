# Chapter 350

During the flight home, I suddenly realized the problem we were facing.

*When should I speak?*

We weren't in the middle of a tour, which meant I couldn't just make time whenever I wanted.

We were in the early stages of promotions, with our schedule packed down to the minute.

*We can't even go out easily right now.*

With everything going on, would I even be able to find time to talk to Seon Ahyeon alone?

But the answer was easy to find.

"You can just use my room, right?"

"...!"

"I have a single room~ Let's talk at night after schedules!"

Yes.

As it happened, the one person in the dorm who had a room to himself also knew everything about the situation. That meant we wouldn't have to leave the dorm to talk privately.

"I need to sleep too, of course, so if the conversation takes too long, I'm just going to use your room... Moondae? Are you listening?"

"Oh, thank you."

After adjusting a few details in our schedule on the plane, I decided to ask Ahyeon to talk as soon as we got back to the dorm...

"I'll stay in the room with you at first. No matter how much he likes you, we need to set the right mood if you want him to believe you~"

He had a point.

Ahyeon seemed concerned that Sejin and I shared some secret that we hadn't told him about.

I'd leave the other parties alone.

We were taken straight to an ad shoot after arriving at the airport, and I approached Ahyeon on set.

"Ahyeon-ah."

"Yes?"

"After this is over and we go back to the dorm, would you be willing to talk with me and Sejin?"

"Yes! O-of course!"

"Yes."

He was very active. Thanks to that, the appointment was made in an instant.

In the end, the three members who were the same age, at least physically, found themselves sitting together on the sofa in Sejin's room.

"Come on, everyone, sit comfortably~"

"Ah, yes...!"

"......"

I stared at Sejin's bed, where ten dolls designed to look like him were lined up neatly. How did he sleep with all those dolls staring at him?

Anyway, I turned my head and looked at Seon Ahyeon with the plushie heads behind me.

He seemed nervous, but also expectant.

*His eyes are glowing.*

No, such a huge secret... It made sense.

Yes. Just last year, I didn't know that I would be able to say this openly.

"Hmm."

I cleared my throat and opened my mouth.

"First of all... I'm sorry if you've been feeling a little left out lately."

"N-no! Of course, um, you don't have to tell me... You guys have been working hard, so that's why..."

"...?"

*Wait a minute.*

I turned to look at Sejin. He seemed just as taken aback.

In the meantime, Seon Ahyeon blushed and continued to speak.

"I-I haven't exactly been impressive, you know, work-wise, or particularly charismatic... So I guess it wouldn't have been easy to share with me..."

"......"

*Hey. This guy...*

Apparently, Ahyeon thought Sejin and I were working on some secret, complicated, and important business.

He seemed to think we hadn't told him, despite it being related to the group, because we didn't consider him trustworthy enough.

For a moment, I looked back at Seon Ahyeon's recent activities and stayed silent.

*So is that why he was so eager to have his voice heard recently?*

Now the puzzle fit perfectly.

*Damn it.*

He had it twisted.

I put strength into the hand that was about to press down on my forehead.

*This is my fault.*

Chungwoo and Sejin had both gotten caught up in this secret, so I'd fixated on the idea that Ahyeon must also be asking about it.

If he wanted to hear anything from me, it was probably about my secret.

"......"

Okay. Then again... There was no need to tell Seon Ahyeon about my identity.

*He can't even guess.*

At this point, I could just make something up about the label or the press and move on. Seon Ahyeon would move on.

*... But is that okay?*

I clenched my fist.

Whenever I had to deal with system-related matters in the future, I'd have to leave him out again. Wouldn't that make him feel even more deceived?

If that was the case, I should have stopped talking.

*At that point, I'd have no more excuses to give.*

It was not a work-related issue, but it was right to say clearly that I couldn't talk about it because it was a personal matter. Because we had a misunderstanding.

Seon Ahyeon might be a bit disappointed, but anyway, things would be sorted out there.

The problem is... I wanted to tell him the truth.

It was funny.

"......"

I half-impulsively opened my mouth.

"What's been going on... isn't actually work-related. I've never thought you were incompetent, either."

"What?"

"It's just my own personal problem."

I swallowed. Somehow, my mood was lifted.

"I'm not actually Park Moondae, but I've been living as him."

"...??"

"I kept quiet because I couldn't talk about it."

"...What?"

Just like I told Lee Sejin, I started to explain it to Seon Ahyeon as well.

"When I woke up a few years ago, I was in someone else's body."

Apparently, having told someone already meant I could explain everything much more succinctly this time.

*... This is really something I shouldn't be saying while being sober.*

"So, in my eyes, there's a kind of hologram..."

"Pffftt!"

"...If you laugh one more time, I will send you out."

"Huh? It's my room, but Moondae wants to send me out... Sorry. I got that plushie from a fan, Moondae-nim. Be careful. Don't throw it at me."

I came to the crisis section desperate for alcohol a few times, but I was able to say the big line anyway and passed it safely.

*I'll tell him about Cheongryeo and Goldie #2 later.*

I didn't need to get into all the complicated details when I was talking to someone who had no idea what was going on.

Sejin occasionally added a few details, and I finally finished explaining.

"...So now I'm doing fine."

"......"

I'd finished my story, but there was no reaction.

Feeling rather flustered, I spoke again.

"I know it's hard to believe. That's why it wasn't easy for me to come out and tell you. As for him, he sort of just figured it out."

"Yeah. You know how perceptive I am. Moondae tried to keep it from me, but I figured most of it out anyway."

"......"

And when I raised my head, I saw Seon Ahyeon's blank face.

The guy looked back at me and Lee Sejin a few times, then slightly lowered his gaze.

"...Th-that's right."

"Yes."

And there was silence.

"......"

"......"

He was thinking... for a very long time.

*...? Why.*

Why wasn't he asking me any questions?

By the time a cold sweat formed on the back of my neck...

Seon Ahyeon opened his mouth again.

But he wasn't talking to me.

It was Lee Sejin.

"...Th-there, Sejin also heard this story before, right?"

"That's right. It hasn't been that long... Not even a few months."

"Wh-what... did you think? When you first listened to it."

"Hmm?"

Lee Sejin shrugged.

"Is there any reason for Moondae to lie about something like this?"

"...!"

"Because of the way I've seen Moondae's personality so far... I just thought he wouldn't lie about this."

"......"

At Lee Sejin's words, Seon Ahyeon lowered his head again.

*What is he thinking?*

Why was he doing that without asking me about the content? No, of course it was pretty shocking... But...

I bit my tongue.

*Stop making unnecessarily excessive guesses.*

It was natural that he would want to know what the person who heard the secret first thought about it.

Still...

I opened and closed my hand a few times, then let go and opened my mouth.

"If you find this hard to believe—"

But this sentence was not finished.

It was because Seon Ahyeon interrupted me.

"N-no. I believe you."

"...!"

He raised his head and smiled softly, looking a little shell-shocked.

"Yes... Th-that's right. Moondae is not the kind of person to lie about something like this... Of course, I know."

"I-it must have been tough for you... Moondae. All this time... you must have been through a lot."

Somehow, I was speechless.

After clearing my throat a few times, I was able to answer calmly.

"Well, I made plenty of money and all that. We're successful. That's what matters."

"Yes..."

Seon Ahyeon answered quietly.

"Still... If you are having trouble, tell me."

"Moondae, you must be feeling pretty reassured right now! You've got two people backing you up~ Oh, Chungwoo knows quite a bit, too."

"...Ch-Chungwoo-hyung too...?"

"Ah, that hyung had some family circumstances..."

I confirmed that Seon Ahyeon listened intently to what Lee Sejin explained.

Concentration returned to his face.

"......"

Okay... was it over now?

Lee Sejin trembled at the end of the explanation.

"We were special from the start, being the same age and all. In the end, the two of us are the only ones who really know everything about Moondae~ You'd better appreciate us, Moondae."

I ended up laughing in the end, too. After thinking about it for a while, I reached out my hand to Seon Ahyeon.

"Yeah. Thanks for listening, Ahyeon."

His hand was a little cold, but his grip was firm.

"...Yes!"

I could see the joy in his eyes as he answered me. That was enough.

Ahyeon's attitude toward me hadn't changed the next day, and I think I was relieved by that.

A few days later, a somewhat unusual event came up.

"Well, tomorrow is the day we go to Yeonhee University."

"Oh!"

We would be performing at a college festival, something we hadn't done for nearly two years.

Although the fees for these performances had risen sharply last year, we'd been attending college festivals regularly since our debut.

Our successful debut after ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ meant that even ordinary college students knew enough about us to enjoy our music.

And college festival performances were fun events, so the audience's reactions were always good.

"Oh~ It's been a while. It sounds quite fun?"

"I know. Will it be an outdoor concert hall?"

Thanks to that, the conversation started like this, and after a few minutes, we went on to talk about the university itself. Well, it was always like that when we chatted in the car.

"...If I get a chance later, I would like to attend a lecture."

"I see. I don't regret completing a cyber university to do my work, but I'm still curious about other options."

And Cha Eugene shrugged.

"I was offered a college scholarship, but I came to Korea anyway. I'm pretty amazing, right?"

"Wow."

"That's really great."

No one would be able to refute it. Kim Raebin was also nodding his head.

"Why did you do that?"

"Um, because dancing is more fun? It's just there. Because I wanted to."

Given how successful things had turned out, no one could really criticize him for that decision.

Even I, who had supported myself while trying to keep my scholarship, had ended up with a job completely unrelated to my major.

"Hyung! Are you thinking of going to college?"

"Not at all."

I had no interest in attending college twice.

The thought of being forced to go through it twice reminded me that I might even have to serve on active duty this time, but I put that thought aside for now.

*Phew...*

*I envy the gold medalist and the foreigner.*

"Okay~ Let's just look around the campus! We can do it secretly!"

"Well... When the time comes."

I looked at the festival listed on the schedule with a strange feeling as I listened to Eugene ramble.

There was only one reason.

"Moondaemoondae, is this the university you graduated from?"

"...Yes."

When Cha Eugene stopped slapping me on the shoulder from the back seat, Lee Sejin spoke virtuously.

This was the university I attended.

*Is this the first time with this body?*

I mean, we didn't have to go here.

Our agency simply chose college festivals based on which ones offered the most money.

This time, it seemed that this college had offered the largest sum.

*It's one way to spend the students' tuition money, I guess.*

This college had plenty of money, but they still raised tuition every year.

Raising an eyebrow, I flipped through the schedule. I was squished between Lee Sejin, who was peering over my shoulder, and Seon Ahyeon.

"Hey, Ahyeon~ Moondae used to attend this university."

"...I-I see."

Seon Ahyeon asked me cautiously.

"How... does it feel?"

"...Well, it's just so-so."

I chuckled.

"It's been a long time since I graduated, and the campus has been renovated since then. To be honest, it doesn't feel like a big deal."

Lee Sejin smiled.

"Got it, Big Bro."

"......"

*That's enough, you little bastard.*

"Hehehehehe..."

I listened to Sejin stifle his laughter, his shoulders shaking, and felt the urge to sigh. Seon Ahyeon smiled awkwardly.

*Well, what does it matter as long as we perform well?*

This would be our first event in Korea in a while. I cracked my neck.

We would be the ending performance.

If time allowed, we would be able to perform three or four encores.

*Though I don't think we'll have enough time for that many...*

But, as always, things did not always go as expected.
