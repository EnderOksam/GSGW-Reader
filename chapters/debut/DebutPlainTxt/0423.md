# Chapter 423

Kim Raebin spoke with a tense expression.

"So, to summarize, the company expects a demand surge from broadcasting our concert. They're planning an encore tour with more shows to match that. Is that right?"

"That's right."

"But is this all a planned path? A... conspiracy to make us accept more tours without resisting?"

"Something like that."

It was less a conspiracy and more them just trying to humor us.

At that, Kim Raebin's expression turned hollow, as if he had just discovered his company was a global drug smuggling ring.

Anyway, it was good that he understood.

"Won't the fans get suspicious if the company keeps holding back our album like this?"

"Even if the fans are suspicious, the public won't know. Public opinion won't form against them."

*I bet even casual fans won't think that far.*

From a market perspective, it made no sense for TeSTAR not to release an album. People would just assume it was delayed because we were filling it with self-composed songs.

"It's not like we're veterans deep in our careers, and our last album didn't do poorly."

"...That's true. We've technically only just finished our fourth year."

Objectively, TeSTAR had just won a Grand Prize. The group was at the start of its prime, an undisputed top-tier idol group.

Keun Sejin smiled bitterly.

"And yet, we have a five-year contract instead of a seven-year one, so I guess there's nothing we can do~"

Right.

The problem was that deformed five-year contract, and...

*The fact that our agency is tied to a broadcast station that can endlessly churn out fairly well-known rookie groups through survival programs.*

We created a label to stop them from poaching our planning staff, but they just didn't schedule any activities for us. One by one, everyone sighed at the situation.

"Still, forcing us on a tour like this is a bit much."

"I like performing! But losing isn't okay. We will now boycott the tour."

"Isn't a boycott a little weird coming from the performers? It's still our company."

"Um, if we just say we don't want to do it... would that not work...?"

Protesting the tour. I stroked my chin and imagined that future.

[Famous Male Idol Group... Causes Scene in Meeting Room, 'We Won't Do the Concert']

[Who is it?]

[Give us the initials]

[The rumor says it's TST based on the situation. Can we trust this gossip?]

[It's a rumor, but if it's true, isn't that RJD?]

"They'd probably think we're uncooperative and don't intend to renew our contract. Then they'd use the media to bash us and lower our value."

"......"

Bae Sejin's expression turned strange, as if recalling a bad memory.

"You know your stuff."

Yeah. I looked into it after seeing what happened with your shitty former agency around our debut. Those bastards were an extreme case, but it's rare for companies not to pull something similar.

Anyway, that was why we decided not to refuse the public broadcast of the concert and to wait for the right time.

"We'll just keep preparing the album."

"......"

And just as expected, the broadcast eventually changed. It was no longer our full concert, but one where parts of the setlist were given to guests.

"These bastards, really...!"

"As expected of Moondae, you're a psychic~"

They also used the phrase 'agency juniors appearing on a secondary stage'. It was a clever way to avoid calling it an agency concert like last time.

The title was still a TeSTAR concert.

*Looks like they want to use all of TeSTAR's buzz for themselves.*

Anyone would be annoyed, but it was tricky to refuse.

The broadcast station, the agency, and investors were all involved. If we dragged it out over copyright issues, we would just look like we were getting angry for no reason and gain nothing.

*We have to aim for a single, decisive blow.*

Until then, we had to lay low. We had to act like we didn't fully understand what was happening.

"Let's practice."

"Of course. Alright, everyone, let's fix the angles of our feet again."

While we polished our concert performance for the broadcast, an unexpected call came through a company official. It was from a junior, but it wasn't Spacer.

[Hello, Sunbaenim!]

It was Park Minha of Mirynay, the idol who placed second on ❬Idol Inc.❭

Her stated purpose was 'inquiries regarding the secondary stage', but once I took the call, she started talking about something else.

[Um...]

"Yes."

[Sunbaenim, uh, I have something urgent to tell you... no, first, I'm sorry for taking up your time. May I take up your time?...?! Wait, no.]

"Yes."

*Why is she so flustered?*

"I don't think you're taking up my time. Please, feel free to get to the point."

[...! Yes, it's just that...]

Mirynay cut straight to the chase.

[Is there... any way for other groups in the company to move to your label, Sunbaenim?]

"...!"

She said 'other groups', but there was only one group this junior would be asking about.

Her own.

"Are you talking about Mirynay?"

[...Y-yes. Including us. Since we're fundamentally the same company, I was wondering if there was a way to change our affiliation, even if the profit-sharing ratios stay the same...]

Just as I thought.

*But this is something she should be asking the label's CEO. Why is she asking me?*

I felt a little sheepish, wondering if my awards show announcement about the label had been too impactful. For now, I gave her a common-sense answer.

"You could probably include it as a condition during contract renewal, but isn't Mirynay's renewal still more than a year or two away?"

[Ah. Um.]

Her voice lowered slightly, and she hesitated.

*Hm.*

*Could this be?*

I decided to ask her directly. "Is there another problem right now?"

[The thing is, actually...]

She took a deep breath.

[Our album... seems like it's going to be delayed by a lot.]

"......"

[A lot of our dedicated staff recently left because of a personnel reshuffle.]

Right.

"They didn't quit, they were moved to another team?"

[...Yes.]

I understood the gist, but their case was slightly different from ours.

[You know the group debuting from this agency soon? The ones from the program ❬Be My Girls❭.]

"Ah."

The junior was talking about Tnet's new survival program that had just ended. That broadcast station just couldn't give up on idol survival shows.

It lacked the impact or buzz of Idol Inc., but it did reasonably well overseas. Thanks to that, it secured a basic fanbase.

And from what I saw on the internet...

[The Survival Program Whose Final Debut Lineup is the 'Best-Case Scenario' of All Time]

I saw a post saying that interest exploded as soon as the debut lineup was announced.

A rookie group with a clean image, a secured fanbase, and none of ❬Idol Inc.❭'s disastrous reputation? The company was probably thrilled and pouring all its manpower into it.

And where would they have gotten that manpower?

They ripped them away from people working in the same department.

[The people who were working with us went over there... and now our album production has come to a halt.]

So for them, this wasn't an issue of contract renewal periods at all.

An attractive new business item appeared. Now, even their existing planning staff was being stripped away and attached to it.

They weren't separated into a label, so it was hard for the artists to defend against staff being taken away. The staff were company employees from the start.

In that case.

*They probably talked about adding a tour for them, too.*

Mirynay was definitely among the guests included in our concert.

The company would probably talk about promotional effects, schedule a tour for Mirynay, and use it to buy time and money until they could hire new planning staff. After all, the group had a decent overseas fanbase.

Even if a tour was already scheduled, the company wouldn't tell the group in advance.

Not many idols would welcome touring for a whole year with open arms.

In any case, the Mirynay member continued to speak calmly.

[An independent label has its own separate staff, so I thought your situation might be a little different from ours... I wanted to ask what it's like.]

It was a frank answer, not one that tried to laugh it off. She seemed to be taking the situation quite seriously.

*And it seems she's guessed that we're in a similar situation.*

Still, no matter how similar our situations were, she must have been reluctant to discuss this with a sunbae from another group.

*I'll give her credit for her guts.*

Still, there was no way for them to change their affiliation right now. Even if I knew a way, I wouldn't tell them.

"Even if it's independent, it's still a subsidiary, so there's not much difference. As for transferring now... to be honest, I'm not an employee with that kind of authority, so I don't really know."

[...Ah, yes! I'm sorry. I brought this up too suddenly!]

But before she finished, a pretty good idea came to mind.

After hearing her case, one thing became certain.

*In the end, it means the company's eyes are drawn to new, promising projects.*

"......"

*Hmm, it's worth considering.*

*Should I lay some groundwork first?*

[I should have organized my thoughts more before contacting you. I made a mistake with my first greeting and showed you a bad side of me. I'll be more careful in the future!]

"It's fine."

Anyone watching would think I was her boss. I held back a sigh and started laying the groundwork.

"And about the album."

[...Yes.]

"Come to think of it, I don't think it's impossible to release it, even without transferring to the label."

[...!]

"Are you willing to cooperate? I can't say it won't be risky, though."

A short silence followed from the other end of the line. Then a question came.

[What kind of risk?]

That basically meant she was willing to do it, no matter what.

I smirked and opened my mouth.

"The risk of the agency disappearing."

"......"

"So, the TeSTAR matter is settled, right?"

"Yep. We'll proceed as is without any other special issues."

The largest conference room at T1 Stars.

The working-level staff attended the Monday morning general meeting, talking busily.

To be precise, it was a time for nodding, giving plausible answers whenever directors spoke, and hoping for time to pass quickly.

"We need to focus on securing the time slot more than the running time. Right?"

"Yes, yes, of course..."

This week's topic was planning the agency concert version 2. It was disguised as the TeSTAR concert that would be broadcast on a public channel.

The topic of recruitment also came up briefly before disappearing... but anyway.

An employee from the PR department suddenly had a thought. From years of experience, he could keep his eyes sparkling while feeling like he was going crazy from boredom.

*Come to think of it, why are we doing this with a public broadcast station and not Tnet?*

Usually, the moment broadcast rights like this were mentioned, Tnet would have clawed its way in to snatch them up.

The employee grew more puzzled when no suitable answer came to mind, but he soon erased the thought.

Who cared. As long as he got his paycheck, that was all that mattered.

"Good work, everyone~"

"Everyone, let's be a little more attentive this week, please."

After hearing the same old speech, he quietly slipped out of the conference room. Only then did he start whispering with people of a similar rank while walking down the hall.

"So, is Spacer's side getting the support now?"

"It seems that way, doesn't it?"

They gossiped about promotions and power struggles between departments and teams.

"There was a lot of talk about T1 pushing the label and all that, but honestly, I'm starting to think I have no idea what will happen..."

"Ah, but the people at ORBIT looked pretty relaxed."

"Right! The way they say 'a job is a job' totally gives it away, don't you think? Normally, you're supposed to say you're dying~"

The employees recalled how the people from 'ORBIT', TeSTAR's so-called label, always had good complexions and seemed relaxed. Their mouths got busy.

'The planning is fun', 'There's a lot of autonomy', 'I heard the artist has a good personality', and so on.

There were typical stories of a good workplace, but also malicious worries about whether their careers would sink as they became an isolated island.

After all, the way the company was running right now was complicated.

*It means you never know who will rise and what will fail.*

Wasn't it the same with the company pushing a new group instead of TeSTAR, who was just starting to hit it big?

Ah, the entertainment business!

Even after sitting down at their desks, they continued exchanging such stories via messenger.

[That punk looks good too ㅡㅡ]

[Ugh, I hate him so much ㅋㅋ[[1]

This was said as the head of the management department walked by.

That guy had solidified his position through matters related to TeSTAR's dedicated team. He still had that smugly handsome face.

The employee wondered thoughtlessly.

*Wouldn't TeSTAR be the same?*

*Well, they've earned a lot of money... I heard the tour settlement payments are no joke.*

It might even be better for them to have less frequent comebacks. I heard that when you're on tour, you can go sightseeing on weekdays when there are no performances.

*Whatever the case, it's certain their fate is better than mine.*

*So, I should just do the work I'm paid for. Only as much as I'm paid!*

The employee double-checked and polished the press release materials for Spacer.

He had no idea that around that time, a director would suddenly receive an urgent verbal report. In a state of excitement, the director would call for an emergency meeting.

And regardless of how all those uncertain elements would play out, the 'TeSTAR Concert' plan was successfully realized. Its broadcast date began to steadily approach.

[1]: ㅋ appears a lot in chat, it's a "kh" sound. i.e. ㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋ is a giggling sound like "kukukukuku". Remember this, it appears very frequently.
