# Chapter 454

First, I considered the pros.

*The fact that I've already done it.*

I already knew the CEO's personality and how the planning team operated. I even went through a debut survival show there, though it was a few years ago in a virtual simulation the System created.

*I'd adapt quickly.*

Since LeTi was a large, publicly traded company, they would likely have no problem handling TeSTAR's brand value.

Besides...

*They don't have any new male idol groups.*

While VTIC was in the military, TeSTAR had a high chance of receiving substantial investment.

*It would buy us a few years.*

With T1 Stars sinking, this seemed like a very decent option.

*If we negotiate the contract terms well, it might even be possible to maintain TeSTAR's color.*

However, there was a critical, non-negotiable problem. My answer could only be one thing.

After finishing these thoughts in thirty seconds, I replied immediately.

[It's alright. Thank you for the congratulations.]

*Yeah, not happening.*

A reply came back shortly after.

[VTIC Shin Cheongryeo: That's a shame]

[VTIC Shin Cheongryeo: What about joining as a label?]

Well now.

[Are you available for a call?]

"Sunbaenim."

[Hoobaenim.]

This guy probably wouldn't release a recording of the call, but I started the conversation politely just in case.

"What do you mean by a label?"

[Exactly what it sounds like. Hmm, is it too difficult?]

The smooth voice continued.

[I was thinking of creating a new label under LeTi.]

"...!"

[Most well-known agencies have their own distinct color. It would be a burden for TeSTAR to join a place like that directly.]

[I thought it wouldn't be a bad deal if you could continue making albums the way you want in an independent label, just like now.]

Cheongryeo said all the right things, as if he were scratching an itch. Then he made his offer again.

[I contacted you because TeSTAR seems like the perfect group for this label. Am I wrong?]

"......"

I opened my mouth.

"About that label."

[Yes.]

"Are you saying the advantage is that TeSTAR can handle its own planning?"

[That's right, and it'll be much more comfortable to work, too. The company's system is pretty well-established.]

"Yes."

He must have established parts of that system himself. He probably wasn't lying about the planning being autonomous.

However.

"But aren't promotions and schedule adjustments handled by the LeTi headquarters, not the label?"

[......]

"And LeTi is your agency, Sunbaenim."

That was right. What did going to LeTi really mean?

I pulled the smartphone away from my face and glanced down at the caller's name.

[VTIC Shin Cheongryeo]

...It meant this guy could hold TeSTAR's schedule in his hands and control it.

Even if I tried to play the game now, it would be hard to beat a guy in a few months who had run things at that agency for ten years. This was especially true if that opponent had conquered the same company dozens, or even hundreds, of times.

*I'm not an idiot.*

Giving a competitor the power of life and death over the group was a foolish move.

*But from the competitor's perspective, it's an incredible advantage.*

That must be why he proposed it.

I recalled the strange text Cheongryeo sent me at the awards ceremony last year. It had a video attached. The video was about some unknown dog receiving a new cushion to replace its ruined one.

A new cushion.

*Did it mean a new company, a new home?*

He might have been sizing things up since then, having already grasped the situation at T1. It was frustrating that I was losing in terms of information, but his pattern of behavior was clear.

*It was calculated.*

This bastard wasn't some saint. He obviously made the offer for his own benefit.

After a short silence, the smooth voice returned.

[Still as smart as ever.]

*Wouldn't it be stupid not to know?*

[Hmm, but you have nothing to worry about, Hoobaenim. I have no intention of doing anything to harm you.]

"Even if a moment comes when you have to choose between VTIC and TeSTAR?"

[Of course not. I'll pick good schedules so we don't interfere with each other. Then you'll just have a good agency, Hoobaenim.]

"......"

[Isn't that a good thing for everyone?]

I looked down at my smartphone. After a brief, intense moment of thought, I spoke again.

"...I'll think about it."

[Ah.]

It must have sounded like a declaration of surrender. His tone became even more amiable.

[A wise decision.]

"But could you spread a rumor for me to raise our value a bit?"

[Hmm?]

"A rumor from an 'insider' that TeSTAR isn't likely to renew their contract."

It should be in the form of a simple gossip column so the company wouldn't get suspicious, but it would be best if outsiders were half-convinced.

I calmly added my reasoning.

"There needs to be some competition for LeTi to offer us a better deal, don't you think? I figure that's the best way to get good contract terms."

[Hmm.]

Cheongryeo gave a short nod of understanding.

*Good.*

If the rumor spreads like this...

[Hoobaenim. You have no intention of coming, do you.]

"......"

This perceptive bastard.

*He figured it out immediately?*

That was right.

I had no intention of going to LeTi if I could help it. No matter how I thought about it, it was like sticking my head in that guy's jaws. I didn't think he'd come at me with a sledgehammer at this point, but this was a matter of risk management.

Besides, think about it. TeSTAR going to LeTi, the agency of VTIC, who we had fought with like dogs since our debut?

*The fans would be sick to their stomachs.*

They would be thinking the same things I was, so it was natural to want to choose a better option if possible.

*If I want to make a deal out of spreading this rumor, I need to put something on the line.*

Should I test the waters first?

I shook my head and was about to speak, but the person on the other end of the line spoke first.

[Well... fine. It's not a difficult thing to do.]

"...!"

He agreed surprisingly readily. Of course, that wasn't his main point.

[But I'd like you to remember that my offer is valid until you re-sign.]

"Yes. I will."

He agreed so readily to gain the momentum to say that, just as I thought, but I would gladly accept.

Just as I was accepting this, Cheongryeo suddenly changed the subject.

[Hmm. I don't think I even need to say this.]

"What do you..."

...mean.

Then, a voice tinged with laughter came through the smartphone.

[Because the more contacts you get, the more you'll think about it. This offer.]

[I'll send the contract terms by email. Once again, congratulations, Hoobaenim.]

*And as for me...*

*These bastards sent one too.*

Indeed, he was the type of guy who would be LeTi's preference from head to toe.

*We have to choose quickly.*

Since each member had different ideas about what was 'absolutely necessary,' few agencies could satisfy everyone. Most of them also had drawbacks or some risky element. They were either too small to provide proper support, too big with a male idol group in a similar position, or had too strong of a personality to handle TeSTAR.

Looking at it this way, LeTi's offer of an independent label certainly seemed like a godsend.
