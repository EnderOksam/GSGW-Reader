# Chapter 558

Cheongryeo had once confessed to being a regressor, but someone apparently did not believe him.

*That's possible.*

And that someone was Judan.

The two statements did not add up.

*This guy?*

I lifted my head. Judan was shoving dishes into the dishwasher. It was convenient that he moved on his own after a hearty meal of stir-fried pork, but still.

"By any chance, will dishwashers have artificial intelligence in a few years?"

"...To some extent."

"I see."

This was the kind of guy Judan was.

I watched him with suspicious eyes.

This was the same man who believed a homeless person he met on the street in a foreign country when they claimed to be from the future.

I was talking about me.

Yet, when a member he had been with for years suddenly got serious and said, 'I'm from the future,' he didn't believe him? He even ran away from home?

*Don't tell me he got all excited, ran out to do some research, and just forgot to contact him.*

The funny thing was, that actually sounded somewhat plausible.

Well, it was not really my business.

I decided to just stop thinking about it.

Who cares.

Instead of worrying about other people's problems, I should focus on my own damn situation.

"Are you going to be working as an actor tomorrow as well?"

Right. This was the kind of thing I should be worrying about.

I accepted the instant coffee mix Judan brought me after starting the dishwasher.

"Yeah. I have one variety show left."

I had been diligently working as an actor for several weeks. That meant promoting the already-filmed movie on TV and various internet shows.

I did waste some time drinking with that actor to dig up information, but that was over now. I would wrap things up without raising any more suspicion.

Judan rubbed his chin.

"Hmm. It seems like it's about time for theaters to switch their main commercial movie feature. The promotion seems a bit inefficient."

I answered nonchalantly.

"I'm not trying to promote the movie."

"...?"

"I'm promoting myself."

That was right.

This was not a schedule set by the movie's production company. It was a schedule arranged by actor Ryu Moon's agency.

"...! You're planning to expand the recognition you've gained from this movie."

Correct.

[It's an observation variety show on MBS, and it includes a short interview, so it's perfect for actors who are hot right now. You have to go on and make a good impression!]

The agency was also putting its all into this.

The 2010s. This was still an era when public broadcast networks were powerful. It was also the beginning of observation variety shows becoming a major trend after a series of hits.

*It's appropriate in many ways.*

Even for someone trying to maintain an air of mystery, it was better than getting every little detail picked apart on a talk show or in a professional interview.

The problem was, a rookie would usually go in with their shoulders tensed up, making them look even more unnatural. This was especially true for an actor who had only ever focused on acting.

"Hmm, but for you, who was an idol that debuted from a major agency in the future, this must be familiar territory."

I just let out a small laugh.

I would just let him think he was more or less right.

***

"The weather is nice..."

❬A Trip for One❭.

It was one of the many observation variety shows that popped up like mushrooms around this time, all centered on sending a celebrity on a healing trip. However, this program sent its cast member out all alone.

*They said since there's no one to talk to, they want me to do a lot of monologues.*

Because of that, some reviews said it was close to the essence of an observation show, but Park Moondae had a hunch.

This was a format that would only be entertaining if the editor worked some magic. If the editing could not capture it, it would just be a story of someone else's silent meditation.

In other words, it was a program where the production team was more important than the cast member.

*It went downhill after the production team changed.*

But right now, the production team had not changed yet.

Trusting that they would create a narrative structure on their own, he genuinely enjoyed the two-day, one-night trip. It was a sweet deal.

*If I try too hard, I'll just look unnatural.*

He was just in a remote countryside village, making food and enjoying the scenery.

Park Moondae lay on the wooden porch, watching the floating clouds before closing his eyes.

WHOOSH!

A breeze from the mountains passed over his forehead.

It was late spring with the sun shining brightly. Lying in the shade, feeling the breeze, and quietly listening to the sound of the grass had a way of soothing one's heart. This was especially true for someone familiar enough with cameras not to be uncomfortably conscious of them.

This was the first real rest from a trip that Park Moondae had felt since being thrown into this bizarre situation.

It was just that before, there was never a moment of silence in situations like this. All seven members moved together.

*There was even a dog.*

A slight bitterness lingered in his mouth.

Still, the dog that Bae Sejin's mother took in was probably doing well, and the other guys were too.

He blinked while still lying down.

For about an hour, this should be fine.

[The unsatisfied idol relaxes his heart at your rest.]

It was Lee Sejin's pop-up.

*You weren't relaxed before?*

Park Moondae was bewildered, but he eventually let out a small laugh. It was not so bad to have a companion, even if it was like this.

"Maybe I should prepare some wild vegetables."

That evening, Park Moondae made and ate stone pot bibimbap with wild vegetables he had picked himself. It was a solid meal, complete with sesame oil he borrowed from the house next door, where they pressed it themselves, and a fried egg from a chicken raised right there.

"He's so natural and nice to watch."

"I know, right."

"He's good at cooking, too."

The production staff, who had watched the entire process, murmured amongst themselves.

He really seemed unaware of the camera, yet he also ghost-like avoided any inappropriate expressions and perfectly timed his reactions when he needed to. No matter how much magic the editors worked, it was impossible without good material.

But this material was more abundant than ever. The poise of a seasoned celebrity who had been on observation shows for a decade! But he was a rookie whose image had not been overexposed!

*Why... is filming so easy?*

The production staff was confused but soon satisfied. A good thing was a good thing, right?

*I should... put more effort into the interview.*

A few of the writers simply made that resolution.

The interview.

Just as you could not have an idol on a show without talking about their number-one hit song, you could not have an actor on a promotional variety show without mentioning their work. This was especially true if it was a recent blockbuster hit.

"I received this watch as a memento. It's the children's toy wristwatch that the pickpocket always wears in the movie."

This was how the program worked.

During a break, the cast member would look at an item related to their recent hit and start reminiscing, which would lead into the story. Then they would film a separate interview to insert into that segment.

Like this.

"Of course! The rumors are everywhere that the filming atmosphere was very friendly."

"Ah, thank you."

That night, after filming the scene where he fell asleep on a blanket over the heated ondol floor, Park Moondae got back up. He conducted the interview in a storage shed behind the country house.

The writer, determined to scrape up any movie-related backstory, meticulously asked him about this and that.

*Being nice is one thing, but if I can pull out something a little controversial, that would be great too!*

He even subtly probed about the actor who was originally cast for his role but was fired due to a sexual misconduct scandal.

"I imagine you must have studied the character by watching the pre-edit footage of the pickpocket. How was that?"

"For character study... since I'm still a rookie, I focused mainly on the script to make sure I didn't miss any details. It's a bit embarrassing... but it's gotten all tattered. Would you like to see it?"

"Oh, yes!"

However, Park Moondae did not get serious and casually deflected the question with his answer.

*...He fell for it!*

The writer conducting the interview only realized it after being handed Park Moondae's script.

LeTi must put their actors through rigorous, comprehensive training too.

*There are no openings.*

The writing staff let out a hollow laugh.

Still, the interview continued.

"Still, being suddenly cast in such a big movie at a young age must have come with many difficulties."

"Mm... that's true, but thankfully, everyone was so kind that I was able to focus quickly."

"Ahh! I heard you were the youngest."

"Yes."

Park Moondae's eyebrow twitched slightly.

The director, who had treated him poorly to the point of being infuriating during the reshoots, had changed his tune. He was now going around in interviews talking about what a diligent, lucky charm the youngest cast member was.

*There's no need to say he treated me like shit here.*

Seeing as his attitude was similar even after the cameras were off, it seemed he had glorified the memory in his own head.

While diligently continuing the interview, Park Moondae made a calculation.

*If I play my cards right, I might even get an offer for his next project.*

Of course, he planned to disappear long before that.

[The focused genius actor is stomping his foot.]

*Right.*

I will find a way to return before I have to borrow Bae Sejin's ability again.

Park Moondae discreetly glanced at the pop-up and gave a slight nod.

Come to think of it, these kinds of expressions had been appearing frequently in the pop-ups lately. They were gestures or expressions the members would use when they were nervous before a big event.

[The Midnight Maestro's eyes widen.]

[The dancer with a noble mindset clasps his hands.]

Things like that.

Park Moondae had a hunch.

*It looks like their preparations are in the final stages.*

Preparations to bring him back, that is.

To avoid passing the risk onto them, he had to act before the members finished their 'preparations'.

*Proceeding smoothly according to plan.*

He had already proven his worth to Cheongryeo as an informant in the acting world through several deals. Now that he had bought some time, it was time to use it properly.

*I'll pour everything into properly analyzing that strange terminal inside Cheongryeo's System.*

That was Park Moondae's judgment.

After steadily digging into it over the past while, it seemed he would soon be able to stably access the terminal's core. As soon as he confirmed it, he had to focus all his energy on finding a way to use it as a hint to return to his reality.

"......"

*This is the right...*

"Ah, Ryu Moon."

The writer called to him again.

"Yes."

"This is a personal question, but..."

It was the phrase used when trying to wittily gloss over an out-of-context or private question.

Park Moondae calmly refocused his attention on the interview.

The writer asked with a smile.

"By any chance, what are your thoughts on the pickpocket's ending?"

"...The ending."

As expected, the context had gone off the rails.

Park Moondae thought he should ask his agency to have this part cut before the program got slammed for movie spoilers.

However, the writer was serious.

"Yes. Such a sad ending happened because of a single, slight misalignment, didn't it? As the pickpocket, did you ever wish for a different ending?"

Dying in handcuffs during the climax? It was symbolic, a sweet deal. Of course, it would be a bit weird to answer like that.

"Mm. That's right."

*Maybe something like... As an actor, I was sad, but the pickpocket himself probably wouldn't have had any regrets...*

However, just as he was preparing a safe and sophisticated answer, Park Moondae paused.

Something in the writer's words had sparked a thought.

*Hmm... a slight misalignment.*

It was the case of Cheongryeo and Judan he had just heard about yesterday. The incident where Judan panicked and ran away after Cheongryeo talked about his surreal experience.

Perhaps because of that, the Judan who was in VTIC in reality was stuck here as a trainee, unable to debut. This was because Cheongryeo had already undervalued him, having appraised Judan as 'below standard'.

This was...

*...It feels like it would go back to normal if I just adjusted it a little.*

Of course, he knew it was an issue that had already been resolved in reality, but the situation unfolding before his eyes right now... did it not make his hands itch?

It felt like it would work with just a little nudge, a little bit of control.

"......"

Ah, damn it.

Park Moondae suppressed a groan.

I will do it just this once.

*No more, you bastards.*

And so, the morning after filming wrapped up, Park Moondae called Judan.

[Gunwoo?]

"Jung Woodan."

It was the weekend, so he was not at school. Having just finished his morning practice, he answered the phone immediately.

[Is this an emergency?]

"No, it's not like that."

Park Moondae said in one breath.

"I'm about to describe a specific situation to you, and I want to know what choice you would make. So..."

The right word.

"It's a kind of human psychology test."

[...!]

"I need you to answer carefully."

***

Judan listened intently to the person on the other end of the call.

Blunt, wise, slightly mysterious, and at times, bold. This person from the future had completely captivated Judan.

And it was the same now.

"So, I've debuted as an idol and am actively promoting. Then one day, a group member suddenly says, 'I went back to the past to live again for the sake of success'... is that it?"

[Mm. Yeah.]

Hearing that nonchalant reply, Judan was struck by a sudden, lightning-like inspiration.

*Has he experienced a similar situation?*

It seemed the other person was talking about something he had experienced firsthand.

[Repeating it until you succeed.]

However, Judan did not let it show. Getting swayed by such predictions would only lead to a common, illogical answer.

*An objective answer comes first.*

So, after a moment of meticulous thought, he added.

"In that case, this is a situation where he's confessing after the group has already succeeded."

[No.]

The person on the other end continued in a low voice.

[It's right before he presses 'New Game'.]

In other words, it was a situation where he had failed, not succeeded, and was about to restart.

"......"

Judan squeezed his eyes shut, then opened them.

It was a hypothetical situation, but the answer had already formed in his mind.

It was simple.

"I would have to run away."

[What?]

"It's dangerous, isn't it?"

Judan coolly drew his conclusion.

"Right now, the other person is in a situation where they have nothing to lose and nothing to gain, no matter what they do. In that situation, there are only two reasons why they would bother to tell me."

[...Two.]

"Yes."

Judan swallowed.

First.

"Over the past years, they've built up trust in me and are telling me as a test."

In short, recruiting a colleague.

If so, a situation could arise where I would willingly lend my abilities as a colleague to this protagonist of destiny. Just as he was doing for Gunwoo right now.

However.

"But there is another possibility, and that is..."

Judan swallowed.

This was the problem. The other case.

Second.

"Because that person was curious. About how I would react."

[...?]

"After repeating the same work for so many years, wouldn't boredom and weariness inevitably follow like a companion?"

A situation right before starting a new game. If they were confessing to me out of frustration or anger.

In that case.

"In the worst-case scenario, couldn't I be murdered just because my reaction was boring?"

[......]

[He's a group member, not a serial killer.]

"There's no law that says an idol member can't be a serial killer. I always operate by assuming the worst-case scenario."

An idol and a serial killer. The intersection was always possible.

*Especially if it's someone who has repeated the past over and over to succeed!*

Would it not be the perfect mindset for disregarding human life? After all, they will just come back to life when he goes back to the past.

Judan responded based on a perfect Venn diagram.

"But running away immediately... hmm, I see. That could unnecessarily provoke them."

In that case.

"First, I would have to send a signal along the lines of, 'Thank you for telling me, but I need to think about it more deeply.' And then, I would run away in secret."

[......]

"It seems like the perfect survival method."

Park Moondae slammed his head down.

[You just wait right there.]

"Yes?"

[I said, wait.]

He immediately shot a Help message to Cheongryeo.
