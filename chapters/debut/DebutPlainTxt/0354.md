# Chapter 354

A member had left the dorm without their smartphone early in the morning during promotions and remained unreachable for several hours.

Worse still, I was the reason the group's schedule had been put on hold for three whole days.

There was no excuse for what I had done. It was complete lunacy.

*Cha Eugene even left a note.*

There must have been an emergency in the dorm.

At worst, even the agency might have heard about it by now.

*... Damn it.*

It was a bomb waiting to explode.

I could already imagine the look on Seon Ahyeon's face.

I gritted my teeth and looked at the intercom again.

Ding-dong.

Seon Ahyeon was standing right in front of the door, not merely at the entrance on the first floor. I didn't even know how this was possible.

So it was even more confusing...

"It's one of Hubaenim's members. Shall I open the door for him?"

"...Wait a minute."

After looking at the front door, I took a slow, deep breath.

"I want to open it myself."

"Hmm? Is that so."

I headed for the front door, leaving the guy who openly expressed his expression: 'Why?'

And I pulled the door open.

The same person I had seen on the intercom screen appeared through the doorway.

"...!"

Seon Ahyeon's face was pale despite the mask covering it. However, the moment the door opened, the expression on his face changed. I could see his relief.

"S-so you were here..."

I was going crazy.

I barely opened my mouth.

"I didn't realize I'd left my phone behind this morning... I'm sorry I made you worry."

"...! N-no. I..."

Seon Ahyeon took a deep breath and spoke without raising his head.

"I'm sorry..."

"......"

Judging from his appearance, it seemed like he had been running around since dawn, so why was he sorry?

"I-I caused a commotion... Moo-Moondae, m-maybe you need some alone time, but... I-I don't know..."

"No."

I wanted to stop hearing these fucking apologies.

I tried my best to calmly respond, suppressing those insignificant sentiments.

But I ruined it.

"Cut it out. It's my fault for disappearing without a word."

"......"

"......"

Seon Ahyeon immediately shut his mouth. I wanted to bite my tongue.

*You fucking idiot.*

Why did you answer him like that?

The silence was awful.

At that moment, a voice without inspiration intervened.

"Kongi is likely to think we're taking him out on another walk. Please come in, or leave—whichever you wish."

Woof!

A dog barking echoed through the hallway. Seon Ahyeon suddenly raised his head.

"...! S-sorry. Then... Moo-Moondae."

His eyes did not meet mine. A smile appeared on the face of the guy covered by the mask.

"I-if you start feeling better... come back whenever... Here."

"......"

He carefully handed me my smartphone.

"I-I thought that you might need your phone..."

I looked at the distorted apple pattern and clenched my fist.

And I accepted the smartphone.

"I was going to leave anyway. I'll come with you."

"......"

"Let's go."

"Y-yes...!"

I took my phone and went out the door. Seon Ahyeon hurriedly moved his feet to press the elevator button.

"Woof!"

The dog barked excitedly and ran out with a harness in its mouth. What are you doing out here?

"It looks like he already misunderstood. And thanks to you, today's walk did end rather early, Moondae."

"......"

"Kongi, wait a minute. Let's do this."

I barely opened my mouth.

"I'm sorry."

"Yes."

No, I was talking about his dog.

The dog was the only one excited as the three of us entered the elevator and rode down in silence.

I opened my mouth first.

"How did you know... I was here?"

Seon Ahyeon flinched slightly.

"Th-the security footage."

"...!"

CCTV?

Unexpected extreme measures came out. The natural thing would have been to call the police or perhaps tell the agency. Seon Ahyeon didn't say anything else.

*No way?*

"I-I asked urgently... saying it was an emergency... So I filled out an agreement, and they showed me..."

Did this guy go to the management office alone and ask for that much?

I looked down. He wasn't wearing socks, and his feet had been crammed into his shoes haphazardly.

"......"

This situation wasn't particularly pleasant, but even so, there was an odd feeling stirring in me.

*Still, it doesn't seem like it's completely over yet.*

The alarm that had been blaring in my mind grew a little quieter.

I answered quietly.

"So that's how you... knew that I was following this sunbaenim."

"...Y-yes."

Seon Ahyeon nodded vigorously.

Just when the atmosphere was about to soften...

"Strange... I've never told either of you which unit I live in."

"...!"

"I don't think I've ever shown you the interior of the building, either... That's hardly enough information, is it?"

The other party stabbed in the middle.

However, Seon Ahyeon was not surprised.

"N-no. I asked one of the VTIC members over the phone..."

"......"

"Who?"

"Chae-Chaeyul sunbaenim. I-I was really thankful because he let me know right away..."

"Ah."

I was witnessing a disaster rapidly unfolding for the guy who was about to enlist.

*That's too bad.*

Originally, bad things came all at once. I had to endure it.

After that conversation, we split up from Cheongryeo on the first floor.

No matter how he felt, he knew he had to take care of his dog, so he'd have to continue to walk the dog as it was.

Even so, he did leave a word.

"Hmm. I see why you were worried. He's the sort that can be emotionally unstable..."

"...!"

"Haha. So this affects your group's activities, then. No wonder you were worried."

*This bastard...*

He had immediately recognized that Ahyeon's stats were in jeopardy.

*... It's not like the characteristics were attached for no reason.*

I considered Cheongryeo's trait once again.
★-
\
#><✦ Characteristic: Appraisal (A)><#

That which is valuable is rare, and there's garbage everywhere you look.

– Human resource appraisal ability +150%
-★

*...I'll have to be careful.*

In the meantime, his physical attractiveness had even gone up. It seemed that he wasn't anywhere close to running out of steam anytime soon.

Cheongryeo lowered his voice and stopped talking at that point.

"My advice still stands."

"Yes."

Right.

"See you."

I put a hand to my face, then finally said, "Thanks for the... tea."

"Haha."

Cheongryeo walked to the other side with the dog.

And then silence came again.

Seon Ahyeon did not ask why I had followed Cheongryeo, nor did he ask why I had gone outside that early in the morning.

We kept our mouths shut and quietly walked along the other side of the promenade.

And surprisingly, Seon Ahyeon opened his mouth to a different topic first.

"...I-is it okay if I contact the others that... I-I found Moondae?"

"...If we go into the dorm right now, won't everyone be there?"

Had a few of them gone to the police station? I felt a chill go down my spine, but thankfully there was no mention of the police.

"M-maybe... I-I came out alone, j-just in case..."

"......"

It seemed that he had also left the dorm without thinking it through fully or telling any of the members. And it seemed that he had come to his senses now. Seon Ahyeon lowered his head, perhaps embarrassed at the thought of himself.

I hesitantly opened my mouth.

"Thanks for worrying about me."

"...! N-no..."

Seon Ahyeon suddenly stopped walking.

I heard him making a muffled sound... Don't tell me he was crying.

"Seon Ahyeon."

But he didn't cry.

Instead, he continued speaking in a trembling voice.

"Moo-Moondae. I-I'm sure you're tired, but... C-can I... say one thing?"

"......"

I silently nodded.

The words followed right away, as if they had been chewed over and prepared several times.

"You're... an amazing person, and you've always been great at everything you do. Always. You've never failed. I mean that..."

Ah.

I remembered what he said yesterday.

-Now- y-you, it's not the time to be a-active. You're getting worse...

He was trying to counteract all the negative meanings I could have inferred from those words.

"I-I... I used the wrong words... Moondae, you've always done so well..."

I stopped walking on the street in front of the dorm where no one was.

I blankly listened to him. Seon Ahyeon's words did not end.

"I-I'm the problem."

"...!"

"I-I know... I'm always shaky, and I'm bad at managing my emotions. I've always been that way..."

What?

As if he had already felt himself slipping into the same mistake again, he looked down at his feet.

It was brutally objective.

"Before... It was because of me as well."

As if he had already felt a similar sensation, he looked down at his feet.

"I-I might seem a lot more awkward than usual at the moment... And that could be a bad thing for the team..."

Seon Ahyeon ended his speech with a calm tone typical of a person who had already reached a conclusion.

"S-so we can simply say that I'm ill."

"I-it's my own problem. So... You don't have to do anything."

Seon Ahyeon swallowed.

"And as for the schedules I'll miss... I-I'll practice. I'll practice hard..."

His voice became increasingly desperate.

"Our performances... sh-shouldn't be affected if I do that... There will be no problem in the team..."

"......"

"I'm the one who needs to see a doctor, not you... So please don't worry about what I told you yesterday... Please forget about it..."

"Wait."

Don't say that.

However, before I had time to say anything, Seon Ahyeon hurriedly continued talking.

"B-but, Moo-Moondae. I know it feels bad, but... J-just once, please listen to me."

There was a trembling voice.

"Could you please speak to a therapist just once?"

I turned my head.

Seon Ahyeon trembled.

"I-I'm sorry. Talking about this again..."

"......"

"N-next time, I'll listen properly. I promise. I really will. I'll listen to everything you say..."

Seon Ahyeon never cried or screamed, even though his shoulders trembled.

He only stuttered, but in fact, inside Seon Ahyeon, a conclusion had already been drawn.

-All conflicts are due to my problems, and I should take care of them myself.

So, he was telling me not to worry about it, and to look into my own condition at least once.

"......"

I chose what to say a few times, then erased the thoughts.

The calculations were a mess.

But I knew what conclusion I wanted to reach.

"You say you're ready to listen to whatever I have to say?"

Seon Ahyeon nodded quickly.

And again, he spoke in a low voice.

"I-I asked you to share, but I failed to—"

"No, it's my fault."

I spoke without hesitation.

You have reached your conclusion, and now I have reached mine.

"It's just not something anybody could be asked to easily believe. In hindsight, I realize that it just sounded like the ravings of a lunatic."

"Moo-Moondae-yah..."

Seon Ahyeon seemed to hold back his tears.

I nodded seriously. The thoughts came naturally.

Okay.

The source of my mistake was there.

"I should have told you in such a way that you had no choice but to believe me."

"...Wh-what?"

Let's think about it.

Ahyeon was not the sort of person who came up with sudden, intuitive ideas or jumped to logical conclusions based on evidence.

He was the sort of person who filtered through all the inconsistencies that needed explaining and questions that needed answering until the most suitable answer remained.

That's how he contributed to our recent album.

I knew everything.

Then, I should have explained it accordingly from the beginning.

Things were clearer now.

This is not a matter of trust or closeness, but of timing.

And normally, I would have taken all of those things into consideration and controlled the game, but the problem was that I misunderstood that this guy would believe me right away.

"You said you would listen. All right. I'm going to explain to you one more time, so listen."

"...!! Th-that..."

This time, I would pack my explanation with witnesses and evidence so that he would have no way to refute what I was saying.

No matter what came of this, by doing this, I would come to a result that I would have no regrets over.
