# Chapter 620

Archery is an outdoor sport. The standard Olympic target is 70m away. Even in more detailed events, the minimum distance was still tens of meters. The space required was too vast for an indoor setting.

To hit targets at such distances, archers sometimes shot from indoors through a window in the dead of winter. Ryu Chungwoo knew this windy, outdoor environment well.

"......"

He held the bow and took a breath. His senses mapped the open space around him. The presence of others practicing nearby felt familiar. These sensations awakened a part of himself he had long pushed away. The memories returned, vivid as yesterday.

"......"

Ryu Chungwoo nocked a carbon arrow. The old, standard-issue recurve bow didn't matter. He was never one to obsess over jinxes or equipment. He had even trained for adaptation, randomly switching between various products and finger tabs in all sorts of conditions just in case.

Days filled with confidence in himself flashed through his mind.

"......"

He placed his left hand on the handle's grip. He gripped it tightly and drew the string with his other hand.

KRRREAK.

The public archery equipment screamed under a strength it had never experienced, but it soon adapted.

*The arrow is different.*

It wasn't his own, which he always kept perfectly tuned.

However...

*It doesn't change anything.*

He faced the target. His body turned sideways. His gaze fixed forward. His right hand pulled while his left hand held steady. He drew the string to its limit, balancing tension and stability on a razor's edge.

Then he released.

WHOOSH!

A light breeze brushed his cheek.

THWACK!

The pleasant recoil vibrated through him as the arrow soared through the air.

"......"

He knew without looking, just like an announcer's call from one of those days.

[It's a direct hit!]

A brief thrill, like goosebumps, ran through him.

Ryu Chungwoo immediately nocked the next arrow. He drew the string and released.

TWANG.

And the next.

"Hyung?"

His speed gradually increased. The grip in his palm and the smooth, cold arrow between his fingers felt the same. Even the slight throb in his hand was identical.

But the target was too close.

*This much is...*

Too easy. Comfortable, simple... No, that wasn't it.

*A little further.*

Ryu Chungwoo took a step back.

"......"

"Shh."

The surrounding sounds entered his ears but faded into the background, unprocessed. Sensation came first. He aimed the bowstring at the now smaller yellow circle.

It was still too big.

THWIP.

He continued. Arrows pulled from the quiver at his hip shot forward, cutting through the air with fierce elasticity.

One more time, and again.

THWIP.

He released.

Ryu Chungwoo moved his hands as if in a trance. The bow rose and fell in his grasp, following a natural rhythm. He kept reaching to his hip for a new arrow.

At some point, his hand grasped only empty air.

"......"

He lowered his gaze. The quiver was empty.

Ryu Chungwoo raised his head and focused on the distance. The yellow dot was no longer visible.

"......"

Forty-five meters ahead, the yellow target was so densely packed with white arrow fletchings it looked like a pincushion.

"Hyung, you're seriously insane."

"You're not human."

"......"

Ryu Chungwoo laughed, looking a little embarrassed. He fiddled with an arrow out of familiar habit as he answered.

"It's because the distance was short."

"......"

He looked completely at ease. His face made it hard to believe he had just performed such a crazy feat. I turned my head and glanced at the target he had shot.

"......"

About twenty arrows were stuck in the yellow section, packed together like thorns. They clustered even more tightly toward the very center, the 10-point zone.

*Is he crazy?*

The sight, which resembled some kind of modern art, left me speechless. His strength was so great that not a single arrow had bounced off. I held back a groan when I saw one arrow that had squeezed its way in past another.

*If the owner saw this, they'd definitely suspect he's a professional athlete.*

Still, they didn't seem to recognize TeSTAR. We had better bolt as soon as our rental time was up, before we ran into them and they asked who we were.

Even as I reached that practical conclusion, I found myself looking at Ryu Chungwoo anew. Nearly ten years had passed since he quit archery. In that time, he probably hadn't trained properly, aside from a few variety show appearances.

*And yet he can still do this.*

Was it like learning to skate as a kid and still being able to do it twenty years later?

Above all...

"Hyung, this is more fun than I thought. Should we shoot a little more before we go?"

"Ah, should we?"

He seemed uplifted by the situation. It wasn't obvious, but I could tell from his lighter, more stable expression. The depressed and confused look he had worn for the past few days had vanished in an instant.

*...This was a better idea than the shooting range.*

I had to admit it as I watched Bae Sejin and Seon Ahyeon eagerly get advice from Ryu Chungwoo. Instead of joining them, however, I sidled up to Kim Raebin.

"Raebin."

"Yes!"

I slung an arm around his shoulders, something I rarely do.

"......"

I whispered quietly to the slightly bewildered guy.

"You knew beforehand that the shooting range was closed, didn't you?"

"......"

I heard a hiccup. It was obvious.

"Were you keeping your mouth shut so we'd naturally come to this archery range?"

"Th-tha..."

"Tha?"

"That's right...!"

Watching him confess as if admitting to a crime, I let out a small laugh. Then I gave his back a light pat.

"......"

"I'm not scolding you."

How could I scold him when the result was this good? His face clearly showed the careful thought he had put into it.

"It looks like you put a lot of thought into choosing this. What was your intention?"

"Ah...!"

Kim Raebin's face relaxed slightly before he moved past relief and became serious. After thinking carefully, he answered.

"...I chose it by reflecting on my own experience."

Experience.

"To be honest, I'd been thinking for a while that Chungwoo-hyung had changed a little. There were a few times he made negatively profound remarks..."

"......"

I almost retorted,*You noticed that?*Then I understood after hearing his next words.

"Perhaps because I've also experienced a few slumps, I felt a similar sense of unease when Chungwoo-hyung spoke."

"......"

*So that's why he mentioned reflecting on his own experience.*

A person becomes most perceptive when someone else is in a situation similar to their own. Even though Kim Raebin was a bit dense about people's subtle psychology, the same principle applied. He had recently experienced similar pain.

He knew the feeling of being unable to do what you're good at, no matter how much you want to. He had faced it in both producing and stage performance, and he had overcome both.

"Of course, I didn't conclude that everyone recovers in the same way I do, and I fully considered that I might have been judging rashly, but...!"

Yeah, I didn't doubt it.

"So, after careful consideration, your conclusion was this place?"

"Yes."

Kim Raebin's eyes shone faintly.

"During my slumps and traumas, what I ultimately needed was... the experience."

Kim Raebin clenched and unclenched his fist.

"The feeling of 'I can do this', felt directly in your own skin."

"......"

"When I directly experienced a counterexample completely different from my worries, anxieties, and sense of defeat, I was able to escape my slump."

That was right.

[I was worried, but when I actually tried it, I did well!]

Kim Raebin carefully confessed that he wanted to give those same relieving words to Ryu Chungwoo.

"So... I thought that if it's difficult for Chungwoo-hyung to experience the stage directly right now, perhaps he could try something in a similar form..."

And that was archery. He wanted Ryu Chungwoo to directly challenge the very thing he had given up on because of an injury to his left arm.

*...Come to think of it, the body part was the same too.*

Kim Raebin had discovered the InHeart notice that the shooting range was closed. After deep contemplation, he made his decision.

"To carefully suggest we go to the archery range."

He explained that he had even meticulously considered how the shorter distance would be a piece of cake for Ryu Chungwoo. He finally seemed a bit relieved.

"Of course, if Chungwoo-hyung had shown a negative reaction, I planned to immediately apologize for my mistake and turn back, but...!"

"There was no need for that."

"......"

Ryu Chungwoo had walked right into the archery range. To be honest, I had also been worried about how he would react.

"It means you made the most insightful and best judgment among us. Good job."

"......"

Kim Raebin took a breath that sounded like he was about to cry. He must have been quite worried, especially since he hadn't asked for permission from the others.

*He probably didn't have time for that when the opportunity arose.*

In any case.

"I'm glad that Chungwoo-hyung seems to be enjoying himself."

Yeah.

"Truly... I think that feeling of 'I can do it' is important."

I nodded silently at Kim Raebin's words. Whether it helped with his left arm freezing on stage or not, it would at least help him feel more at ease. Willingly doing something he liked and was good at was bound to be good for his mental health.

"Hyung, can you shoot the bow even if you turn around?"

In the end, after making full use of the archery range that day, Ryu Chungwoo decided to come back periodically to practice, at the members' suggestion.

"There's no one around and it's quiet, so it's perfectly comfortable."

"......"

Good teamwork. I nodded, feeling good that the situation was resolving itself.

Of course, it wasn't long before I was blindsided.
