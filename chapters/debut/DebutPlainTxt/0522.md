# Chapter 522

Wedding halls were usually spacious, but this one was filled with guest seating, decorations, and event equipment. There was very little actual free space.

This meant the celebratory song was performed very close to the guest seats. Guests in the front rows could see the guest singer right in front of them. Because of the limited space, even people not in the front row could see the singer up close.

One such person was a female sociology student attending her relative's wedding.

*Whoosh.*

From her seat at the outer edge of the groom's side, she saw several well-built figures turn and run right past her.

Seven men. They wore matching navy-blue suits, and though they passed in an instant, their faces were unmistakable. Faces she had only ever seen on screen.

*TeSTAR.*

They were the most famous male idol group in the country this year. They had emerged from the flat screen, now three-dimensional, and moved with an immense presence and realism she had never imagined. The wind they stirred as they ran past was so close it brushed her cheek.

"...??"

"Huh?"

"......"

Confused murmurs rippled through the area. She was just as bewildered, struggling to process if the situation was even real.

*Fweee-ing-*

The seven men reached the performance spot in an instant. They unhesitatingly grabbed the microphones and shouted.

[To the two CEOs of Ruined Industrial Complex, we sincerely congratulate you on your marriage!]

A mix of surprised gasps and ambiguous cheers erupted. The men holding the mics, however, showed no hesitation. They smiled and spoke with clear voices.

[This is our first time singing a wedding song, so we studied hard. We heard they're often sung by close acquaintances of the couple, who put their hearts into it.]

[Yes. In the hope that the two of you will continue to live happily and joyfully together...]

[That sounded just like us, right? So we prepared this song with that same heart~]

Around that point, the guests finally came to their senses.

It's TeSTAR!

*Gasp...!*

The sociology student swallowed hard. She had wondered what it would be like to see a celebrity in person. Would they look better or worse than on screen?

Their features were just as handsome. They were a group famous for their good looks, so that was not surprising. The screen, however, could not fully capture the unique aura a person possessed. Their physique, appearance, and expressions were all sharp and polished, honed daily from already excellent features.

The destructive power of it all becoming real!

*Oh my god.*

Seeing them up close was a huge bonus. The guest belatedly realized just how incredible her seat was.

Ryu Chungwoo, solidly built, smiled and changed the flow. He mentioned what wonderful producers and partners the marrying couple were.

[We had the opportunity to participate in a wonderful project the two of you created together. I wonder if you know what it is.]

The moment he finished, groans and screams erupted from the guest seats, as if they had anticipated something.

*What? What are they doing?*

Until now, TeSTAR had spoken as if they had a personal friendship with the couple. This implied they were not here through a third-party request or their agency.

*Come to think of it, I think TeSTAR advertised some game...*

Before that thought could finish, a voice shouted.

[Let's begin!]

The instrumental started. The glorious sound of an electronic guitar MR rang through the hall like a lightning strike.

"......"

"Waaaaah!"

Excited screams and confused questions crossed in the guest seats. It was a mix of those who knew and those who did not.

In that moment, the song cut through all the noise.

[I'm gonna survive-

Like you did before!]

A thrilling high note.

*Holy crap!*

An incredible vocal volume with a clear high note, something one would never expect to hear in a wedding hall. At the sound of Park Moondae's voice, all murmurs vanished and cheers erupted.

The catchy introduction followed.

[Ash to ashes, dust to dust!

But NOT for me, that's not me

I'm alive, that's right, like-

Legends never, die!]

Cha Eugene winked and extended a hand toward the couple. The CEO couple, unable to contain their excitement, threw their hands in the air.

"Woooo!"

Just then, the giant white screen at the front lit up and a video began to play. It showed image cuts flashing by, from their Abandoned Factory days, through T1 Plays, to the current Ruined Industrial Complex. The images showed the CEOs passionately creating games, sacrificing sleep.

This was a request Park Moondae had made after much deliberation, when the CEO couple had told them, 'Tell us anything you need'.

[It would be great if we could play a video during the reception.]

The video told the story of the couple about to be married, a common feature at weddings. It was unique content that fit the CEOs' special circumstances, but it conveyed the same warmth and relationship story as any other.

The moment the trailer TeSTAR had filmed also flashed by, all the guests who did not know the situation finally understood.

❬'Bonus Book' by TeSTAR❭

This was the song from the game the CEO couple had poured their hearts into creating together. In other words, it was the CEO couple's theme song.

[That's what keeps me alive!

I fiercely chase

The steps that will complete this life]

The synergy between TeSTAR, the screen, and the CEO couple seemed to heat up the atmosphere.

What is the side effect of inviting a very famous celebrity to sing a wedding song? The main characters of the wedding can change. Guests focus more on the singer than the couple, cheering for them and talking only about the performance, even during the meal.

That is why, contrary to expectations, inviting a singer famous as a person, not just for their songs, might not be very satisfying. The wedding itself gets overshadowed.

[If I reach out, I can hold it.]

But in this hall, that side effect was absent. TeSTAR sang a song related to the work this couple had created together, their chronicle. It was no different from singing the couple's symbolic song.

Since it was also TeSTAR's own song, the strong narrative and connection were conveyed to the audience without awkwardness. At this wedding, there was a clear reason to have TeSTAR as the guest singer.

[I will never die,

Like I did before!]

TeSTAR sang the rap parts all together. They even asked the bride, groom, and guests to sing along, adding a cute touch to keep it from getting too serious. It was a composition that fit the festive atmosphere of a wedding.

The lyrical dissonance was actually fun. Because there was a story behind it, it created a unique charm and depth.

[Choose your way...

Choose your side.]

[Yes, choose your side!]

Furthermore, TeSTAR naturally connected the lyrics, transitioning from the game song to their latest track.

*Roll the Dice.*

It was the song that gained complete public recognition through Water Bomb this year. The fact that the couple getting married had made the game featured in that album made the attending relatives and acquaintances slightly excited.

[Just roll the dice

Trust take my side]

In this way, TeSTAR perfectly celebrated their marriage with a song whose lyrics seemed not to fit at all. It was not a TeSTAR performance, but a wedding celebration stage.

[Be happy!]

The 3-minute and 30-second stage concluded precisely as the video ended. Applause and cheers poured down on TeSTAR and the couple.

As the instrumental stopped, the warm solidarity created by the video and song began to fade. Interest in TeSTAR themselves was about to explode, but by then, TeSTAR was already exiting quickly.

"Ooooh!"

People who already had their smartphone cameras out cheered while still filming TeSTAR's exit.

The guest who had been watching blankly, clapping, finally snapped out of it.

*Ah, me too!*

She was the one who could film this from the closest distance! She hurriedly raised her smartphone and managed to film TeSTAR just as they passed right by her.

Her eyes, however, were not on the smartphone screen, but directly on TeSTAR.

*...Wow.*

They were the people she had seen on TV. Cha Eugene smiled, his canines slightly visible. Seon Ahyeon had long eyelashes and red cheeks. Lee Sejin was friendly and refreshingly handsome.

Kim Raebin looked flamboyant and decadent. Ryu Chungwoo was well-built and handsome. Park Moondae looked indifferent, with a fair, pretty face. Even the neat face of Bae Sejin, currently popular for ❬Doll Hunter❭, was there.

These handsome celebrities, whom she only knew one-sidedly, drew in her gaze.

*Wow.*

Her throat was tight with wonder and excitement. She held her smartphone for a while, forgetting even to stop the video recording. She was not the only one in this state.
