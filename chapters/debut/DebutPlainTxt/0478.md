# Chapter 478

*This is incredibly bewildering.*

*This whole situation.*

"What were you interested in?"

"......"

"What activities did you watch?"

"Just... everything in general."

*Isn't Cha Eugene the one who ran away from the dorm?*

*I don't know why it feels like I'm the one being interrogated.*

*He said he didn't want to talk about STier.*

I never thought the guy I spent all night looking for would dig up my past in a Ganghwa Island diner.

*But I can't just not answer him.*

Kim Raebin had managed to calm Cha Eugene down, and he finally seemed relaxed. I couldn't be the one to ruin it. So I answered every question diligently, but the questions were endless.

"Why did you watch us?"

*Damn it.*

*So this Cha Eugene is a question mark murderer too, huh.*

I decided to think of it as a good thing since I was used to it. I had to keep my composure. Meanwhile, STier Cha Eugene's questions became more specific.

"[I mean], there must have been a reason, a trigger, right?"

"......"

*Hmm.*

I studied the guy. STier Cha Eugene had become more direct, as if he had lowered one of the hurdles he had put up.

That meant I couldn't give a simple, noncommittal answer. I realized this quick-witted guy would see right through it.

*Right.*

*So that's how it is.*

A cleverly packaged answer would just backfire. So I just started saying whatever came to mind.

It was a story I had never told before.

"I saw a performance fancam. You were good."

"......"

"I think it was a festival in Pohang."

In truth, I even vaguely remembered the name. It was the Pohang Sea Festival, hosted by the Ministry of Oceans and Fisheries.

[2X0712 Pohang Ocean Leisure Festival STier Full]

It was a coincidence.

"Back in college, I used to film idol events to make money for living expenses. The habit stuck, so I was watching idol videos and happened to see yours."

My instincts from my data-selling days were still sharp back then. The kind that made me think,*These guys would be worth about this much.*

So I had casually looked up their price and was quite shocked.

*It was lower than my predicted price.*

Worse, demand was falling at that point.

*I could see people were starting to pull out.*

They were an idol group that would clearly fizzle out unless a major event turned things around, and yet, when I watched STier's performances, I didn't see the helplessness of people who knew it was already too late. I wondered if that was why I had misjudged their value.

"......"

Maybe because of that, a thought suddenly occurred to me.

*[If I filmed and uploaded a fancam, what are the chances of hitting the jackpot again?]*

Just like with Youngrin's Downpour Fancam, but I also knew it was a statistically impossible delusion, and I had neither the money nor the time to try.

*Besides, there was no reason for me to do it.*

So, that was the end of it.

"...As I kept watching, the WeTube algorithm showed me related videos. Performances, variety shows, things like that. That's all."

"......"

Really, that was all.

I fell silent, and Cha Eugene fell silent too.

*He doesn't seem... suspicious.*

*...That's good enough.*

I held back a short sigh. That should be a good enough explanation.

"......"

Beside me, Kim Raebin seemed to be struggling to follow the conversation, but I decided to deal with that later. Right now, I was too busy trying to save my own skin.

*Honestly, it's ridiculous to even be talking about this now.*

To be precise, I had completely forgotten I had ever looked into a group called STier until recently. I had entered Park Moondae's body with my memories from that time gone.

My last memory was from my twenties as a civil service exam student, with only the fragmented knowledge that the ❬Idol Inc.❭ Re-listed season had been a success. It was only very recently that I regained the memories of Ryu Gunwoo's life after he quit studying and started working.

*I only got them back by chance when I was trying to destroy the System.*

So a question like this...

"So... are you our fan?"

"......"

*No, it shouldn't have even come to a question like this, I'm telling you.*

"I've never been to an offline event, so it's a bit of a stretch to call me a fan, isn't it?"

"Really?"

However, Cha Eugene just smirked.

*What's with this guy?*

[So, you're saying it's just a coincidence that you, who originally had no intention of participating, appeared on a survival show, and then a B-list boy band became a completely successful K-pop group?]

"......"

[You had no desire whatsoever to change this group's destiny. Right?]

*Hey.*

*I was threatened with death if I didn't debut, you bastard...!*

*Destiny, my ass.*

After that, I barely survived, facing death if I didn't get first place or win a grand prize. I couldn't say that, so I silently suppressed my anger.

But a doubt suddenly crossed my mind.

*Is it really a coincidence?*

Could the System have factored in my interest in STier, the group that debuted on ❬Idol Inc.❭, when it granted my wish?

*...It's possible.*

Anyway, I had to calmly clear up this misunderstanding. I answered sincerely.

"It's a complete misunderstanding. It's a kind of Butterfly Effect, I guess."

"Umm."

"My memories were a bit jumbled, you see."

After briefly explaining my situation, I emphasized it once more.

"All that aside, I just watched a few of your performances and variety shows. That's it."

However, it didn't work.

[A boy band that lost half its members to gossip, leaving only three?]

"...Like I said, I saw the performance fancam before I knew about things like that..."

*This isn't working. We're just going in circles...*

*Patience.*

I held back a groan and silently closed and opened my eyes.

Cha Eugene now leaned completely against the table, bobbing his head. He had shed all his wariness and regained his composure.

Next to him, Kim Raebin nodded as if he finally understood.

"Ah! So that's why you immediately recommended Ganghwa Island!"

"......"

*This is driving me crazy.*

"It's true that this is a place we visited as trainees, but according to Cha Eugene, it was also the filming location for his group's first variety show. It seems you knew that, Moondae. As expected!"

"......"

*That bastard acted like he would never talk about STier. When did he spill everything to Kim Raebin?*

Wait, his mouth was still open.

"That's right. Umm, the second one was also on Ganghwa Island."

"I see!"

*Hmm?*

I frowned.

*This... is a little strange.*

*STier's second reality show was definitely planned for Ganghwa Island.*

However.

"Wasn't that one cancel—"

"Oh."

"......"

"......"

"......"

I realized it immediately.

*This bastard set a trap for me.*

He was testing me to see if I even knew about a canceled schedule. A schedule from early in their debut, at that.

*This little shit!*

Cha Eugene chuckled and adjusted his posture. I rubbed my brow.

I was about to fact-check in case he started spouting crazy things like 'Is there a problem with your memory again?' or 'Are we in a different timeline?', but he used it to trap me.

"It's a misunderstanding."

"OK."

"I said, it's a misunderstanding."

*I'm someone who's never even bought a single one of your albums...*

*You can find out this kind of information just by organizing a timeline.*

However, Cha Eugene didn't even pretend to listen.

[I think everything is perfectly clear now.]

Cha Eugene extended his hand. It was quite polite.

"It's nice to meet you."

"......"

"It's been a very long time since I've seen a fan."

*Damn it.*

I reached out and shook his hand.

"......"

*Why is he surprised when he's the one who offered his hand?*

...And while it was a stretch to call myself a fan, it was true that I staved off boredom with the content this guy provided without spending a single penny. I supposed you could call that a debt.

"Yeah, it was nice to meet you."

"......"

STier Cha Eugene continued to shake my hand in silence for a long time. Then he suddenly shrugged.

"'Moondae' might be disappointed. I'm more handsome than this Cha Eugene."

That was a rather debatable statement.

"Is there a reason you're so confident?"

"I have a tattoo here!"

"What?!"

I decided to let Cha Eugene, pointing at his arm, and the horrified Kim Raebin have their fun.

Anyway, I thought it was done now.

*...I've handled it, right?*

If I had known this would happen, I should have just said I was a fan from the start. No, since I wasn't a real fan, that might have just made him more guarded. Maybe this was for the best.

*He seems less stressed now, so this must be the best outcome...*

Pressing my aching brow, I leaned forward to pour some water.

It was then.

Liquid suddenly splattered onto the table. It wasn't water.

"M-Moondae?"

*Ah, damn it.*

*It's a nosebleed.*

***

"Are you alright? We should really go to the emergency room..."

"I told you, I'm fine."

I muttered, taking the ice pack off my nose. The bleeding had already stopped.

This was the first time I had gotten a nosebleed just from staying up all night. It didn't even happen during ❬Idol Inc.❭. I guess I really did need that Bacchus.

*Did my brain overload from having too much to do?*

It felt like blood had rushed to my head, and the pressure was trying to escape through my nose. Anyway, it meant my head was clearer now.

"I'm starting to see people around."

"Yes. I think we should be careful."

We were currently sitting at a small parasol table in front of a convenience store. Only a few people were visible on the distant beach. Since we all wore hoodies and masks, no one had recognized us yet, but we didn't know when someone might, so we should probably call a taxi and head back soon.

"Hey, drink this."

This guy was the problem.

*I don't know if he'll follow us obediently.*

"Thanks."

In any case, I opened and drank the sports drink Cha Eugene handed me. Then I paused.

"......"

*Isn't this the drink STier advertised early in their debut?*

"Wow, Moondae, you know that too?"

"Know what."

I had read the summary on the advertisement tab of the STier wiki. I continued to drink the beverage.

Then, I heard a small voice from my side.

"I'm sorry."

"......"

"Are you... disappointed in me, by any chance?"

"No."

I lowered the drink from my lips.

"I should have been more careful. I'm actually sorry that I wasn't."

"......"

I added, as if joking, "We just came from a fight with T1."

"......"

*It's something to be that shocked about... well, I guess it is.*

"So all schedules related to T1 were blocked. That's why we were so concerned about our reputation. We were on edge."

"......"

"I'm sorry if you felt like we were trying to control you. You're right. We just met, we don't have that right."

Kim Raebin was right.

I glanced at the guy sitting next to me, who was busy packing up the remaining ice pack. He sat there, seriously worrying about my nosebleed, with a face that showed he had no idea he had just achieved a monumental social feat.

*Good grief.*

It was then.

"Hey."

Cha Eugene wrinkled his nose and hunched his shoulders.

"I'm not trash."

*What are you suddenly talking about?*

"I'm grateful to my fans. Since you're our fan, Moondae, I'm grateful to you."

"What kind of fan hasn't even bought an album?"

[...Some value might not be related to money.]

The guy made a statement unbecoming of someone from the heart of capitalism. He rested his head on his knees while still propping up his chin.

"Thank you."

"......"

I finished the rest of my drink.

"I'm grateful too."

We called a taxi from that spot and returned to the dorm. Cha Eugene didn't talk back or run away. He just quietly chatted with Kim Raebin and naturally got into the taxi with us.

It would have been great if that had solved everything.

"E-Eugene!"

"...Thank goodness."

If only I hadn't spotted Ryu Chungwoo, who looked unwell, among the guys welcoming the situation.

*What is it?*

I had a strong premonition of being fucked.

"Chungwoo, is something wrong?"

"...Well."

*That means there is. Damn it.*

"The company contacted us... but this is just what the staff are guessing. So don't worry too much and just listen."

"......"

"They said Aether is appearing as a special guest at an event called Summer Pool today."

"......"

I realized the hidden meaning behind his words.

"...It wasn't on their original schedule. It's sudden?"

"Sudden."

That's right. Aether had suddenly decided to appear at an event similar to Waterful but smaller, for a fee that was next to nothing.

But why did our company hear about it in such a hurry and contact us?

"It seems... word about Eugene might have leaked from the company."

"......"

So, the fact that Cha Eugene had run away had reached someone on their side through someone at our company.

"If you guys hadn't found Eugene, an article might have come out tonight."

The people on their side predicted that this Water Bomb event would become a disaster for TeSTAR. To create a point of comparison, they hastily inserted Aether into a similar but smaller-scale event.

*Fuck, they're smart.*

*This way, even the smaller scale of the event can be spun into a story about a rookie's passion and fighting spirit.*

The plan was to create a buzz by contrasting them with a top-tier idol member who got arrogant and ran away, or with an idol group that canceled their Water Bomb appearance. However, that was a story based on the assumption that Cha Eugene would go completely off the rails.

"...Yes, but since Cha Eugene returned safely, their agency won't get the effect they're hoping for."

"Right. That's why I told you not to worry too much. I was afraid you'd be more anxious later if I didn't tell you."

"......"

He had a point.

Ryu Chungwoo gave a faint smile.

"Anyway, get some rest. I can handle the communication with the company. You can still get... hmm, about four hours of sleep before the event."

"Hmm."

That wouldn't do.

"No, we need to go over the formation one more time. We have to check the parts too. Since the center was missing, I think we need to be more careful."

The song was created with Cha Eugene's presence in mind. Performing it without him required even more precise coordination.

However, Ryu Chungwoo shook his head.

"You look really pale right now. ...Then, just go to sleep for now. We'll practice first, so..."

"Do you have a group schedule tonight?"

"......"

I almost flinched. Cha Eugene had suddenly jumped into the conversation.

*What is it?*

He asked if there was a schedule, but he didn't look particularly curious, as if he had been listening intently.

"...That's right. It's an event."

"Are you in trouble because 'Cha Eugene' isn't there?"

"...A little."

"Umm."

STier Cha Eugene stared into space for a moment as if lost in thought. Then he glanced over at Kim Raebin.

*...Don't tell me?*

"[The fact that you even got a nosebleed is half my fault.[ I feel responsible too. So..."

"......"

"I have to do the schedule tonight."

"...!!"

"R-Really?!"

"I don't lie."

"Cha Eugene!!"

The living room filled with a mixture of awe, screams, and bewilderment. I almost let my jaw drop.

*...I never imagined this.*

STier Cha Eugene had joined the temporary schedule, and it was for Water Bomb.

But a moment later.

"Moondae Moondae, just a second."

"......"

Lee Sejin carefully called me over.

"It's great that Eugene is cooperating, but... you know we don't have much time until Water Bomb, right?"

Lee Sejin spoke quickly and precisely.

"If he can't perform as well as our Eugene, it might be better for him to just sit this one out. We've already prepared the story about his health issues, anyway."

"......"

"What do you think?"

It was a rational concern and a cool-headed judgment. If I didn't have any other information, I probably would have nodded.

But I let out a smirk.

"I don't think you need to worry."

"......"

"Let's start practicing right away."

No other words were necessary. Just one time. I knew his worries would disappear after just one run-through of the choreography.

*No, in fact...*

*Unless another feeling appeared in its place.*

"...Okay."

And thirty minutes later.

"......"

Let's just say my prediction came true.

And so, the time for Water Bomb drew near.
