# Chapter 399 - Wishze's Debut

Of course, before both TeSTAR and VTIC, there had been other first-tier idols.

*And right now, that's T-Holic.*

I was silent for a moment, remembering the guys who crumbled at our sophistry that 'being stranded is better than having 1 billion won' on their variety show.

Thanks to that, we got a lot of attention as intended by voluntarily doing a magical girl theme song as our penalty.

They'd acted like complete pushovers back then, but that was all for the variety show. In fact, they'd had an absurd heyday.

In fact, after TeSTAR emerged, only the male idol groups that were genuinely 'popular' with the general public managed to survive.

"This is their prime time before they enlist."

"Their title track, set to be released on March 22nd, will rank eighth on music streaming sites for the entire year."

Cheongryeo delivered a terrible prophecy for our future without even blinking. Judging by his expression, he'd probably had to restart more times than he could count because of T-Holic.

*But still...*

"Let's make this clear."

We weren't in a situation where we had to spend several years clearing multiple status abnormalities.

"What we're trying to do is win in the short term. We don't need to keep winning *daesangs* over the long term."

"We don't have to worry too much about the future."

Managing our image consumption and reputation, consistency, fandom retention, a sustainable concept... There was no need to worry about any of this.

All we had to do was beat T-Holic just this year.

"Let's fight with quantity."

*This year is the only one that matters.*

Cheongryeo stared at me.

"I didn't think you were the type to believe rookies could win with quantity."

"Of course it won't work if they don't know how to pull it off."

I smiled, baring my teeth.

"But all the members have already succeeded. Half of us remember how to succeed."

"......"

These were people who'd remained top-tier idols for anywhere from 5 to over 10 years. We'd already proven ourselves in the market.

"We just have to come up with a good strategy."

"Tell me."

I shared the plan I had in mind, including the timeline.

Two albums.

And a method that borders on fraud.

"Not bad."

Cheongryeo rubbed his chin and laughed quietly, smiling slightly.

"That's assuming we have good music to work with."

It was a comment from a guy with a complete mental database of the songs that would be released over the next eight years.

I laughed too.
