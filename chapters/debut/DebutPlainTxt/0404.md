"It can't happen just because I want it to."

– You've brought all the other kids back so far. Isn't there a chance for Seon Ahyeon as well?

He continued speaking in a low, fairly calm voice.

– To me, it looks like you're giving up without even trying.

*When did this guy get so sharp?*

I answered reluctantly.

"It's not that I'm giving up. It's just not necessary. It looks like Seon Ahyeon is doing well as he is. He's become successful as a ballet dancer, too."

– What? Just because he's successful doesn't mean he's perfectly fine.

He spoke firmly, but hesitated a bit in the next sentence.

– ...Well, if you mean that he doesn't seem to have any trauma or anything like that... I can understand.

But his voice became firm again.

– But just because he looks that way on the outside doesn't mean he's fine on the inside... Who knows? When I... When I was having a hard time, I didn't talk about it.

"......"

I remembered the guy from ⟨Idol Inc.⟩.

He'd been so aggressive and uncooperative back then that he was nothing like the person he is now. I'd thought he wasn't even worth trying to use, but after spending time with him, I found out that he had his own reasons and strengths.

*Oh, come on.*

I held back a sigh.

"...You're right. I'll take back what I said earlier... Let's just say I wonder if he already has some memories."

– Keuhum, yes.

For some reason, the guy seemed proud of himself. After a long silence, he suddenly sounded excited.

*What now?*

– ...! Then, why don't you try sounding Seon Ahyeon out again? I'll arrange a meeting!

"...??"

*And how's he going to do that?*

"You will, hyung?"

– Okay. Then you... you just need to make some time.

"Time for what?"

– Practice.
