# Chapter 636

Everyone knew it. Calling this July the month of TeSTAR was an understatement.

Their global music chart performance was astounding.

The reveal of their insane album sales, which skyrocketed with their digital success, caused a stir. People wondered, "Just how massive is their global influx?"

Of course, domestic fans had some disappointments.

[The guys are too good at English...]

All these career highs happened overseas, so domestic fans suddenly found themselves consuming only international content.

The only new Korean content they saw from the TeSTAR they knew came from behind-the-scenes footage on their WeTube channel.

[The guys speaking English is cool and makes me proud, but I'm starting to miss them just casually speaking Korean on a broadcast, not just in behind-the-scenes clipsㅠ]

[Guys, I know it hasn't been that long since your Ghost Story Office content and it's shameless of me to say this, but no, I have no shame. Give us more original content.]

[Give us some slapstick comedy we can laugh at without thinking, or something like a cooking competition. I'll watch it until it wears out.]

They felt shameless but couldn't help it.

Since their debut, TeSTAR had almost always focused their activities in Korea, and fans had grown accustomed to it.

The single week of music show promotions for the remix vanished like cotton candy washed by a raccoon.

[You guys made us this wayㅇㅇ TeSTAR, take responsibility.]

While making these pleasant complaints, everyone knew the truth. Filming new original content would be difficult during such a busy time.

No, even coming to Korea would be difficult.

*They'll probably film some original content somewhere once their schedule clears up and they get a break.*

Still, they believed things wouldn't just fizzle out and that there must be a plan. So, domestic fans patiently enjoyed this period in their own way.

Then, in mid-August.

[TeSTAR Airport Arrival Live]

[Huh?]

[TeSTAR's arriving at dawn tomorrow?]

[Looks like they told the reporters. I guess it's a public schedule.]

[Maybe they didn't announce it earlier because they were worried too many people would show up?]

Nevertheless, a few fans were already rushing to the airport.

Since their fanbase was extraordinary, even "a few" of them guaranteed a crowd.

[Wow, it's already packed here.]

The weekday airport, usually quiet at dawn, erupted into a commotion. An unexpected long line formed as people queued up behind the fences.

When the live broadcast started, the crowd was the first thing viewers saw, even before TeSTAR.

[Looks like even muggles who were passing by stopped when they heard a celebrity was coming.]

[Wow, but a lot of people really showed up in just a short time at dawn.]

[I'm worried a lot of stalker fans and sasaeng fansite masters probably went too...]

Because their status had changed over the past few weeks, a strange mix of curiosity, anticipation, and longing filled the air.

[The guys are finally coming to Korea.]

[I missed you.]

[Gasp]

[Ah, they're here.]

[Looks like they're coming in.]

[Thump thump thump thump]

A tall, handsome figure in a mask emerged from the open gate. He casually carried a suitcase like an accessory.

It was Ryu Chungwoo, dressed in light jeans.

[Ugh!]

[Ah, fuck, his form is insane.]

[Just how does he maintain his body?]

Starting with Ryu Chungwoo, the members passed through the gate and entered the airport one by one.

[It's Ahyeon.]

[Wearing a knit in the middle of summer, that's definitely Seon Ahyeonㅋㅋㅋ]

[Ah, I really like his dyed hair.]

[Moondae-yaㅠㅠㅠㅠMoondae-ya, I really love you.]

[I'm a little flustered by how good Lee Sejin looks in a black tracksuit.]

The most surprising thing was that viewers recognized all those individual traits.

[So handsome.]

[Wow, look at how tall they all are.]

[Only Bae Sejin is calmㅋㅋㅋHe's used to returning to Korea since he's been flying back and forth for his movie, right?ㅋ]

[Remember Kim Raebin, who never loses his swagger and fashion sense anytime, anywhere. He is a true idol.]

The sight of TeSTAR standing in a line before the fence for a moment evoked a strange feeling.

It was the first time in a while seeing "TeSTAR in comfortable, casual clothes in Korea."

[Welcome back, guys.]

Smiling faces were faintly visible above and below their masks.

The members bowed to the reporters a few times and waved to the fans, sharing a brief moment with them.

However, after just a few seconds, they quickly started moving again.

[They're running.]

[AhㅋㅋㅋㅠㅠI wish they'd stayed a little longer.]

[Ah, but there seem to be too many people.]

[ㅠㅠWhat a shame.]

While crying out that they wanted to see them longer, people accepted that safety concerns made it unavoidable.

At the same time, they found it a little cute.

They carefully entered the country while still waving to their fans, a friendly and pleasant sight.

[The guys must have been excited to come back after so long and told the press, right?ㅋㅋ]

[Here! Over here!]

*We were planning to turn on a live stream by ourselves once we got to the dorm.*

The word got out.

If the company hadn't received a report on the airport situation while we were on the airplane and taken emergency measures, it would have been a disaster.

Articles could have easily been about ❬Airport Photo Safety Issues, Is This Okay?❭ instead of ❬TeSTAR Returns to Korea❭.

"......"

After frantically bowing and escaping into the van, a few members even wiped away cold sweat.

"Seriously, we didn't even make an announcement..."

"I'm curious how they found out."

Who else could it be? A loose-lipped company employee must have leaked it.

*It's an understandable situation.*

Thanks to us, their workload had doubled or tripled, and they were dying from working overtime. It was impossible for them to keep their mouths shut.

It seemed the company was in an uproar trying to hire new staff because of it.

*And it looks like they're earning enough money for that...*

I had already recouped the investment I poured into the company a long time ago.

*I should reinvest it.*

The time to leave any room for being dragged around by capital had to pass, didn't it?

I followed my stream of consciousness, then wiped my forehead and looked outside. A data-seller, stuck to the window trying to get a photo, was being blocked by a security guard.

*They don't know when to give up.*

Thanks to that, the van finally departed.

"Aigoo."

"Ah, there are fans over there, too."

Keun Sejin, disappointed he couldn't open the window to greet the fans, gathered the members, took a few selfies, and uploaded them to social media.

They were already guaranteed a spot in the top five next week.

"Still, home is the best."

"I'll take a vote."
