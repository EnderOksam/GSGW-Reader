# Chapter 574

TeSTAR's next schedule was a joint concert in Singapore.

It was a typical cash-grab event where a broadcast station gathered various artists.

These joint concerts starkly revealed an artist's current standing through their treatment.

From the size of waiting rooms to the performance order, it was a free-for-all where agencies battled over rankings based on "how many conveniences are provided."

Especially the ending!

That spot certified the most popular and powerful artist, and by extension, their agency.

*...Or so many people in the industry think.*

Because of that, if two or more top-tier groups performed, talks about the ending stage began during casting.

Funnily enough, that was the situation right now.

[TeSTAR or Youngrin.]

Both were last year's Grand Prize winners. They were even splitting awards at the current ceremonies.

An exciting dogfight should have erupted over which of the two would take the ending stage, but...

"Yep, it's okay if we don't do the ending. Hey, don't worry about it!"

Sorry to the Cyber Wreckers, but neither we nor Youngrin cared much for that sort of thing.

*The only times we got the ending were a few times at their concerts back when we were under T1.*

Usually, if VTIC was in the mix, their golden balance of seniority and popularity always secured them the final spot.

*Right now... it seems likely the ending will go to Youngrin over TeSTAR based on seniority.*

Youngrin getting the ending was even better. If TeSTAR took the ending stage here, we might get caught up in useless online arguments.

The amount of stage time was a truer indicator of popularity than the ending.

We had already secured that indicator.

"TeSTAR is performing three songs, and they'll all be aired."

"Oh~ Yes!"

The three-song lineup was ❬Hi-five❭, ❬Spring Out❭, and ❬Hunt❭. A solid set.

We agreed to perform the original version of ❬Hunt❭ and then switch to the concert's circus version for the final climax, using the stage equipment.

"As expected, it's a great way to promote the concert and leave a strong impression!"

"That's right! I love this choreography!"

"Okay, but Eugene and Ahyeon, let's be careful with the backflip at the end."

"Yes...!"

Right after we finished a smooth but intense rehearsal, urgent news arrived.

"They're saying we're the ending."

"...?"

[Perf.#18 / TeSTAR]

At the very bottom of the new cue sheet, the circled artist's name was not Youngrin, but TeSTAR.

"......"

A fundamental question arose.

"Why?"

Of course, being more fandom-focused, we might have more overseas appeal than Youngrin, a public powerhouse who dominates music charts. Still, you couldn't ignore seniority.

*Why are they spoon-feeding this to us when we didn't say a word?*

I wondered if some fake news about TeSTAR hitting it big overseas had spread on WeTube, enough to make a major broadcast network move.

Of course, nothing that crazy had happened.

It was all the result of a calculation.

Lee Sejin took the cue sheet from Ryu Chungwoo, scanned it meticulously, and shrugged.

"Ah~ The members of Halo Heim are performing three songs."

"Hmm...?"

That was right.

They had a total of three songs. Two of their own, and one cover of 'SaintU', Youngrin's group.

*They got almost as much time as TeSTAR.*

The rookie co-ed group had secured an enormous amount of airtime, far more than their name value suggested.

Of course, they hadn't secured it themselves.

*The agency must have gritted its teeth and poured everything into the rookies.*

Since that agency had also risen to prominence thanks to Youngrin's group, it wasn't traditionally close with the broadcast stations.

So it seemed they were testing the waters... and the result was...

"...So they gave us the ending just in case we complained?"

Bae Sejin's words were probably correct.

This was a situation where Youngrin had given up her own benefit for her agency's rookie group, conceding to TeSTAR.

"Hmm."

A brief silence fell.

"Sh-should we refuse...?"

"That's not it."

It would be strange to refuse the ending they gave us.

That could lead to an even bigger misunderstanding than just accepting it, so going along with it was the right move.

*And it's not like we can throw a fit now asking why Halo Heim's stage time is the same as ours.*

I quickly and coolly finished my calculations.

"Since we've received it, I think we should just work hard. What does everyone else think?"

Everyone agreed, and the unusual situation was settled instantly.

However, Lee Sejin remained in his spot after the members dispersed, looking at the cue sheet for a while.

"What are you doing?"

"...Ah, Moondae Moondae."

The guy looked at the cue sheet with a strange expression, then crossed his arms.

"It's nothing... Hmm, I was just thinking."

"Thinking what?"

"I was wondering if Youngrin-sunbaenim has completely set her heart on being a producer."

Hmm?

"I mean, given the situation... doesn't it seem like a decision she made as the producer of that rookie group, rather than as a singer?"

That... was true.

Moreover, Youngrin was a director at that agency.

After her group went viral from a fancam, she had practically kept the company afloat, so her directorship wasn't just a title. Her voice in the company was firm.

So this meant...

*Does this mean she willingly conceded the opportunity to the rookie group?*

I also found myself looking at the cue sheet with a strange feeling. If I felt this way, I could only imagine how the guy next to me felt.

I glanced at Lee Sejin.

As someone who had run himself into the ground to be an idol, he probably wouldn't appreciate a successful senior willingly 'conceding'.

"You don't like it."

"Well... Hey, everyone lives their life differently!"

Lee Sejin brushed it off smoothly, as always.

*He's not denying it.*

Well, there was no need to discuss it further.

"Well, I get it. Ah, I had some feedback on the last move in the ❬Hunt❭ performance just now."

"Oh~ What is it?"

Let's just focus like hell on our stage, our concert.

The brief speculation ended there.

However, I would soon get an unintentional opportunity to test Lee Sejin's speculation.

"...Hello, Youngrin."

Right behind the concert hall building.

***

To be honest, this wasn't my destination.

It was my break, so I was heading to a vending machine on the outskirts of the first floor.

But I happened to make eye contact with Youngrin, who was standing in the backyard on the building's periphery, and ended up greeting her.

*It would be weirder if I didn't greet her.*

I just didn't expect Youngrin to be standing here.

Among the performers, even smokers increasingly chose e-cigarettes indoors, worried about being caught by drone shots if they smoked outside.

There was no reason for anyone to be standing in this unremarkable backyard.

*If I had to guess...*

Judging by Youngrin's expression, 'wanting to get some fresh air' seemed about right.

However, Youngrin instantly erased the troubled look from her face.

"Ah, Moondae. It's been a while. It's nice to see you."

"Yes. Thank you."

The moment I bowed my head, a few convincing reasons for her troubles came to mind, but...

"......"

Forget it.

I decided to stop the meaningless thoughts, exchange some quick pleasantries, and leave.

Let's see, what was something recent I could praise...

"The song you gave your agency's juniors was really good. I'm looking forward to your new album even more while I wait."

That should be safe enough.

Youngrin immediately returned a safe answer.

"Thank you."

But her next words had a bit more impact.

"Hmm... However, I don't have any plans to release a new album this year yet, so I'd appreciate it if you could wait patiently."

"......"

She wasn't releasing an album?

The nuance even suggested she had no plans at all.

*Is she really going all-in on producing?*

It seemed Lee Sejin's guess was right. I'd better move on quickly so it doesn't get awkward.

"That's a shame. There are so many people waiting for your stage... myself included."

"Is that so."

"...Yes."

But as I answered, I couldn't help but recall her stage.

That rainy day when I filmed a fancam of the unknown 'SaintU' Youngrin's performance.

And even the identical gesture Youngrin had shown through the viewfinder when I briefly returned to Ryu Gunwoo's body.

An idol who remembered her early days.

The stage put on by a person who doesn't give up, who doesn't change.

"......"

...For some reason, a bitter taste filled my mouth.

But I quickly erased it. This wasn't the time for me to get sentimental.

"Then until then, I'll be cheering for the juniors you're producing."

That was enough to play the part of a friendly junior. I planned to just say a quick 'goodbye' and leave.

If only it hadn't been for Youngrin's next reply.

"Thank you. Ah, now that I think about it, among the Halo Heim members, there were some kids who were originally from the same agency as TeSTAR, right?"

"......"

Hmm.

"Yes. I believe that's correct."

I played along, thinking I might get some information. Youngrin muttered as if to herself.

"They needed a stronger impression to supplement their existing image, so they ended up debuting in a unique way... but it's a relief they can stand on stage more now. They're kids who are very good at performing."

I read two nuances in her words.

First, it seemed Youngrin had indeed voluntarily given up the ending to give her juniors more stage time.

However.

"So you didn't produce that reality program."

"......"

Youngrin gave a faint smile.

"I do bear responsibility."

It meant she had tacitly approved. Because.

"If there's no one to watch the stage, all the practice is meaningless."

She had thought it was an effective promotion, regardless.

Making even one more person take an interest and watch 'Halo Heim's' stage.

"......"

As I kept my mouth shut, Youngrin added, almost like a joke.

"And there are already seniors who went through a similar process and ultimately succeeded with their performances."

"Pardon?"

From Youngrin's expression, I realized who those 'seniors' were.

"Is it us?"

It was TeSTAR.

I also realized what Youngrin was talking about.

Idol Inc. .

"Yes. Back then, there were few programs as provocative as ❬Idol Inc.❭."

But idol trainees had also made their names through that program, gaining the momentum and opportunity to succeed.

Youngrin, who had even been the main MC for that program, calmly declared.

"Of course, this is an even more extreme and provocative method. I also feel that promotions are increasingly flowing in a direction unrelated to the stage."

"......"

"But nevertheless, there are people who need even that kind of opportunity."

I coolly recalculated.

A survival group that ended in a rigging controversy.

Even if we had released them from their agency contracts, most places probably wouldn't have properly invested in and debuted those already-labeled trainees.

They would have probably spent a year or two squeezing the last drops from their remaining fans before their activities quietly disappeared.

But at Youngrin's agency, they invested properly, even if it was through the crazy method of an in-group dating reality show.

So you could say an escape route to a more successful future had been created... but.

"......"

Still, shattering the mentality and image of kids who wanted to perform with a stupid dating program...

"You seem to be in a bad mood."

"I'm not."

That was close. I quickly checked my facial muscles.

They were fine. She probably guessed because my answer wasn't quick enough.

But Youngrin was composed.

"No. It's natural to be in a bad mood. It's a good thing. There should be more people like that."

It's a relief she knows.

"Still, in whatever way, if they want a stage, I want to support them. Because I know what it feels like to be desperate for that opportunity."

The idol who had endured a long period of obscurity and climbed up by seizing a single, slender opportunity called a fancam, said.

"I think that's why I started producing."

"......"

"So this year, I ended up taking on producing before my own album."

I asked without thinking.

"Are you more satisfied with it than your activities as a singer?"

"Not really, but I won't know for sure until I try it more."

"......"

"I'm happy because I'm an idol singer, but..."

Youngrin finished her words with a faint smile.

"A moment will come for you too, Moondae, when you can give a choice to the 'you' that isn't an idol."

***

"......"

"M-Moondae?"

"......"

I pushed myself up.

*What a weird dream.*

It seemed the conversation I had with Youngrin at the last joint concert replayed in my mind.

It wasn't even a big deal, but it left a lasting impression.

*Why did I take her story about how being a full-time producer wasn't so bad so seriously?*

It wasn't a very good dream to have before an idol concert. I rolled my shoulders and got up.

"Moondae, were you dozing off?"

"For a moment."

I checked my throat. No problems.

Looking at the clock, I had only dozed off for about three minutes.

*Good.*

My condition was excellent. It had to be.

"Let's finish up our hair and go in right away."

"Yep!"

Because today was the first day of our renewed concert.

*The rehearsal was clean too.*

We had put a lot of effort into this concert, starting from the promotions, so we were going to show a proper performance.

*No doubt about it.*

I swallowed, my head buzzing with adrenaline.

As I was about to move...

"Just a moment."

I picked up the smartphone that caught my eye and, after a moment's hesitation, left a text message.

[Concert's starting soon.]

It was for Keundal.

After completely breaking free from the System, I had kept in touch with him occasionally, but it wasn't like before, when we would chat via pop-ups whenever we were bored.

Still, we exchanged texts or calls on important days like this. He even contacted me when the promotional video came out, saying he'd watched it.

However.

*...He didn't say he was coming to this concert.*

The guy who used to keep up with all of TeSTAR's schedules seemed a little less intense now.

It was a good thing.

I thought it would be better for him to have some time to himself, so I decided to send him a ticket later.

I put down my smartphone. I thought I wouldn't look at it again until the show was over.

That was the moment.

Vzzz-

"...?"

A call came from Keundal.

*Is he calling to cheer me on?*

I recalled his usual behavior and picked up the phone with a small smile.

But what I heard was a panting, unfamiliar voice.

[Sir, sir! This is the Incheon Ganghwa Fire Station.]

"......"

[I'm sorry, but does the person answering the phone know the owner of this cell phone? Ryu Gunwoo!]

"Yes."

[What is your relationship to him?]

I thought there was a high probability it was a phishing scam.

However, the number displayed on the phone was a regular cell phone number.

"......"

A strange premonition hit me, and I racked my brain.

"I'm his older brother, but why do you ask...?"

I deliberately let my words trail off to make them misunderstand that we were family. A frantic reply came back.

[Sir, please listen calmly. Your younger brother is currently missing on a mountain here.]

"......"

What?

I looked at the clock.

"He should be... at work right now."

[No, sir. He was absent from work, so we received a report and are currently searching. Has Ryu Gunwoo mentioned anything about a mountain recently?]

"......"

[Or another destination...Sir? Sir?]

It was one hour before the concert was scheduled to start.
