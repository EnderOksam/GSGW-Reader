[There is no need to leave room for Cheongryeo to hold animosity toward me.]

My heartbeat, which had roared like white noise, was still stable.

And so, I made the correct judgment.

"You don't need to see my profile."

So...

[It's me.]

"Your Help, that's me."

The expression vanished from Cheongryeo's face.

A direct hit.

"To be honest, it's true I didn't tell you at first because I was pissed off, but now that we've met like this, I figured it's better to just say it."
