# Chapter 580

A concert is a form of composite art. TeSTAR knew this better than anyone. They poured endless hours into the stage composition and direction, constantly discussing with various staff members.

There was, however, one exception.

For their solo songs, the members received only a keyword. The performer decided on the stage themselves. There had to be some spontaneous fun like that.

[Then Chungwoo will use a lift device on the stairs... What about Moondae?]

[Hmm.]

Park Moondae agonized for a while over how to present his solo stage. Should he show an unexpected side of himself? Or should he show what he does best?

*Both have their pros and cons.*

So he chose both.

*I can just assign one to each option.*

It didn't matter if the two choices were polar opposites. The burden was his alone to bear. Deep down, however, he leaned more toward the former.

*An unexpected side would be more interesting.*

Since the fans were likely familiar with many aspects of him, an obsession with showing something new unconsciously influenced his thoughts.

*...There's a limit to the impact I can make by just doing well what I've always done well.*

They already knew Park Moondae was good at that. They would be used to it. Even as he meticulously divided and allocated his practice time, that impression remained in his subconscious.

So, for the first concert, he inwardly thought he should carefully craft the choices to make people pick the 'unexpected side'.

Time passed, leading to now.

The audience's choice was made.

[Remain in the elevator.]

[YELLOW]

"......"

On the dark stage, Keundal held his breath and raised his gaze. A clear, poignant string melody echoed around the elevator, which hung alone in mid-air, bathed in a spotlight. It was a delicate accompaniment, full of flats that tickled the ear.

It was a familiar melody.

Before Keundal could recognize what it was, a voice poured from the slowly opening elevator.

[Spin the wheel

So I can fly far away]

"......"

[The melody rings out, beautiful

It reaches my heart, Let it pop]

The lyrics were familiar. Keundal realized it like a lightning bolt. This wasn't a B-side from their latest album, nor was it Park Moondae's personal OST. It wasn't an officially released solo song or a cover he had performed on a variety show.

It was a title track from TeSTAR's past.

[Spin the wheel

So I can visit your dreams]

Amidst a more lyrical and tranquil arrangement, Park Moondae's voice drifted out.

*Ah...*

It was the full version of a song he had not heard in a while. The song had not become a regular on TeSTAR's concert setlists. It had lost its spot to ❬Magic!❭, a new song that combined it with its double title track, ❬Hi-five!❭.

That made it all the more welcome, especially with Park Moondae singing it.

And he sang it so... well.

[Stay here again today, just like this

It's not over, Let me in]

A clear, breathy high note filled the speakers distinctly. The sound, more transparent and resonant than solid, made one listen intently.

*Ah.*

With just one chorus, people stared at the stage, their minds free of stray thoughts.

Then.

Tap. Tap. Tap.

A knocking sound came from inside the elevator as the doors opened completely.

"......"

The moment they swung wide, a lush, green scent poured out from within. The inside of the elevator was fully revealed.

[Wooo- wo, wooo-]

The interior, filled with the warm light of a lamp, overflowed with flowers and greenery. It was full of a blooming spring and summer.

"Ah...!"

Park Moondae stood in the center, in front of a rustic standing microphone. He hadn't brought any flashy choreography or a radical arrangement. He was simply standing inside a stage prop floating in mid-air.

Singing.

[Before I fall asleep

I always think of you

I want to see you

Still, wholly, forever]

Inside the elevator, a barefoot Park Moondae, dressed in a large white knit sweater, gently raised the head of the rusted, vine-covered microphone with both hands.

[Little wishes

Bubbling up

Upon that turning Ferris wheel

Sparkle dazzlingly]

The accompaniment was just a single, thin line of strings. His voice, almost like he was speaking, echoed to the far ends of the quiet concert hall.

[Lean against the window and imagine

Me,

Like in our bygone childhood]

An elevator is a space for movement. However, for those who chose to stay, he wanted to present the most fantastical form of staying. A beautiful reminiscence. A dream connected to the past.

[Your eternal childhood

That's me

Forget your insomnia and fall asleep-]

The voice, having touched a low note, soared back up, letting a thrilling pleasure suddenly brush past.

[Ohh- oh-]

He pulled it up in a single breath, without a pause. With the final note of his voice, all the restrained instruments surged in, washing over the audience to the very back like a wave. From the lowest bass to the highest riff melody. From low notes to high notes.

And at the very center, a voice shone as if it were burning brightly.

[Spin the wheel]

Soap bubbles poured down around the elevator.

[So I can fly far away]

Faintly glittering reflections flickered from the stage, shining out to the audience.

[The melody rings out, beautiful

It reaches you too, Let it pop]

The colors of the light sticks rippled like an aurora.

[Spin the wheel

So I can fly far away]

The chorus rippled along with them.

[The melody won't disappear

It will stay with you, Let it be]

Park Moondae raised his voice earnestly.

[Breathe in deeply

So this heat can seep into you too-]

He sang for the people who chose this option he hadn't expected. In truth, the option he had tried to guide them toward for the first concert was not this one.

It was 'RED'.

*Of course, they'll choose to get out of here.*

An elevator is a place for movement, and people like stimulating things. From these two facts, Park Moondae had easily drawn a conclusion. He assumed that when the binary choice appeared in the VCR, they would naturally choose to leave the elevator.

Anyone immersed in the story would excitedly choose to go outside, to run out into the flickering darkness. Something more stimulating and new. So, without hesitation, he had attached the 'unexpected side' to that option.

However, the concert did not go as planned. Park Moondae missed the first concert due to an unforeseen accident. In this situation, the people who were worried about Park Moondae unconsciously chose the more gentle and familiar option.

To remain in the elevator.

[YELLOW.]

And 'YELLOW' was the option that showed them what Park Moondae did best.

Singing a complete song.

...Park Moondae's vocals.

[Spin the wheel

So I can stay in your dreams]

The sound of his singing deepened. Inside the swaying elevator, Park Moondae looked at the lights filling his vision and had a fleeting realization.

*Is that so.*

He had been less confident in this choice, but perhaps that was why he wanted to show it to them more definitively. No, he wanted to show them. He wanted to satisfy all their expectations.

For the people who chose the familiar Park Moondae instead of something new. For the people who chose to stay with him.

To do that...

*I just have to do better than expected.*

Park Moondae gripped the head of the microphone.

[So our unending story

Can sink into your ears]

More desperately.

[Umm-]

His voice stretched out, seizing the magnificently blooming ensemble of strings. It neither showed off nor was it buried. The distinct presence of his singing voice became the core, wrapping all the melodies of the accompaniment to become a harmonious and powerful focal point.

The hotel lobby and the wires no longer registered. The lighting, the devices, the direction, none of it mattered. Only Park Moondae in the elevator and his voice captured everyone's attention.

[Wooo---!]

He wanted to satisfy them. He didn't even want to give them the leeway to think,*He's really good live.*

Park Moondae tuned every single syllable with all his might.

Intensely.

[The end will not come

In the shining lens

I will remain like a film]

His voice stretched out alone, without accompaniment. Inside the elevator, he slightly closed his eyes and lowered his hand. He filled the space with only his breath and voice, without a single other note of melody.

[Because I'm here]

Just like that, covering all past incidents, a single image was once again imprinted on the surface, as if overflowing.

Something that was already familiar to everyone.

Just how incredible of a singer he was.

[Endlessly draw me

Inside you]

Park Moondae smiled and pulled his lips away from the microphone.

[Still, wholly, forever]

"......"

A breathless silence fell. A single string instrument began a soft, solo accompaniment. Amidst it, the elevator carrying Park Moondae began to slowly ascend, its doors still open.

Park Moondae leaned loosely against the standing microphone and looked up at the audience in the upper levels.

The moment it reached the ceiling, the lights went out.

CLICK.

The song was over.

The fansite master clapped, barely breathing, before realizing they had almost broken their light stick. Their mind was completely blank.

***

Backstage.

"M-Moondae, that was really, really amazing... that stage just now...!"

"......"

"Moondae...?"

"Thanks," Park Moondae said, blinking. His head was spinning.

*What did I just do?*

It was the first time he had ever been so engrossed. He had always thought he wouldn't fall behind anywhere, thanks to his good concentration and even an S-rank trait that helped him immerse himself on stage.

However...

*It was different.*

It was the first time he had ever performed thinking only of singing. He had a mind that calculated the various effects that would stem from the stage. Since he had started his stage experience on a survival program, it was somewhat unavoidable. Rankings, schedules, public opinion, all sorts of external factors... a stage was something he climbed after putting all of that in his head and organizing it to avoid stepping on any landmines.

*However, this time... it wasn't like that.*

Nothing else was needed. Just a strange sense of mission and a primal desire to satisfy the people watching him right here, right now, had completely filled his head. With it came a sense of satisfaction and catharsis.

To think... a stage like this existed.

"......"

Park Moondae leaned his head against the wall and licked the saltiness from the corner of his lips, then abruptly came to his senses.

*This isn't the time for this.*

The concert wasn't over yet. He had to move now. He pushed himself up and placed a hand on the shoulder of Seon Ahyeon, who was waiting for the VCR to end.

"You're on standby for your solo stage."

"Yeah. My costume doesn't change, so." Seon Ahyeon, who was performing the last solo song among the members, smiled brightly.

He had such an innocent look that it was hard to believe he would have gone out with paint splattered all over that face and outfit if the 'RED' route had been chosen. However, that was precisely why it would have been an incredibly intense stage.

*I need to do the next part properly too.*

Park Moondae composed his feelings like that. There were still stages left.

"...I know you'll do well, so I'll just cheer you on."

"......"

Seon Ahyeon wanted to say more about Park Moondae's previous stage, but he decided to talk about it after the concert and moved his feet like a pro. His heart was pounding.

*It was amazing.*

He felt it even though he had only heard it indirectly from backstage. As is the nature of ephemeral beauty, he couldn't describe it precisely.

However, there was someone here who knew how to describe it.

"...Hyung."

Keundal was almost out of his mind, completely dazed until the next stage began. He finally regained his thoughts. The hand gripping the light stick was slick with heat. The audience was completely melted and mixed in a state of perfect heat and immersion, as if the previous turmoil of worry and interest had never existed.

It was understandable.

*That was...*

Even though he was wearing a VR headset, he almost deluded himself into thinking he was there at the venue. He was completely lost in the song. At the same time, he felt an almost maddening regret that he wasn't there in person. He felt the song would have been even more incredible live.

His heart pounded at the thought. He had such conflicting feelings.

*It was amazing. Really... truly.*

He wiped away his cold sweat and caught his breath. Neither he nor TeSTAR's Park Moondae had a Status Window anymore.

However, in the eyes of someone who once had a Status Window, it was visible.

How the singer of that stage would be expressed, and with what result.

Clearly.
