# Chapter 582

TeSTAR's dream-like concert was over.

Memories, joy, lingering emotions, and data remained.

"Hmm."

Ryu Seojin, the twin fansite master for Lee Sejin and Park Moondae, sorted through her photos with a hawk's eye. She was also Ryu Gunwoo's former club senior.

*They came out well.*

After the encore, TeSTAR had chatted with the audience several more times. They took song requests and even performed a second encore. The photos she managed to get during that time were quite good.

Once the official setlist ended, the members ran in all directions, waving, talking to fans, and focusing on fan service.

[AHHHHH!]

[This one! This one! Ahyeon!]

A frenzied mix of smartphones, dolls, slogans, and light sticks flew through the air. On top of that, her seat had been incredibly lucky on both days.

*The guys kept coming over.*

She nodded, looking at a clear shot of Ryu Chungwoo's abs. The quality seemed good enough to trade for shots of Lee Sejin or Park Moondae from different angles. In conclusion, her haul from both days was decent.

This was, of course, if you excluded the fact that she had no photos of Park Moondae from the first day because he was absent.

*That couldn't be helped.*

A person's life had been on the line. If Park Moondae had attended, he would've been cursed out as a psychopath. Instead, he skipped the concert and helped save someone, which was the best possible outcome.

*It was a bit of a mess, but at least it's being resolved.*

The twin fansite master clicked her tongue. She knew all this, but it was still disappointing. At the same time, she admired how the members barely showed any sign of trouble on the first day and carried the show professionally. Many fans seemed to feel deep emotion and relief after worrying and then seeing the members perform.

*Well, I expected as much from Lee Sejin.*

The way they handled the aftermath was also good. They didn't just let people choose between a refund and a VR concert ticket.

TeSTAR extended their Seoul encore concert, which would happen after their two-to-three-month tour, by two days. They gave the affected audience members priority booking rights for one of the dates. Naturally, the twin fansite master also chose the VR ticket and priority booking rights instead of a refund.

*The agency knows how to do business without pissing people off, huh?*

They didn't make it obvious they were looking out for their own profits while offering compensation, which would have upset the fandom, but they also didn't take a long-term loss. She nodded, thinking it was a smart, future-oriented solution.

With that, the controversy over the member's absence at the first concert quickly died down. However, that wasn't the end of it.

Click.

She clicked on the fancam she had uploaded and scrolled through the comments. It was her video of Park Moondae's solo stage, zoomed in as much as possible.

[Legendary]

[This is insane, I have goosebumps]

[Is he always this good? This isn't edited or something, right?]

[It's like a mountain spirit swapped his vocal cords as a reward for his good deed]

[Amazing.. Wow, just... amazing..]

Comments filled with shock and excitement flooded the section. Dogfights even broke out, with people arguing over whether the video was edited or calling out others for ripping the audio. Trolls having a field day was proof of a viral video.

That's right. Park Moondae's solo stage hit the real-time trends and was mentioned everywhere as soon as it was performed. At the time, however, there were no related videos because the official VOD from the stream hadn't been released yet. Anyone who recorded and uploaded the stream video got hit with a copyright strike.

[Please, a video before it evaporates from my memory, please]

[Get to work, ORBIT, fuck]

[When is the VOD coming out?ㅠㅠㅠㅠㅠㅠ]

So, as soon as a fancam full of live energy was finally uploaded, it racked up an enormous number of views.

[Thank you... Thank you...]

[There might be people who haven't seen it, but no one has seen it only once]

While stream recordings were subject to copyright, personal fancams were in a gray area that the agency mostly ignored, so they weren't taken down. As fans frantically clicked and commented, the video even rose to the top of the popularity rankings.

A short-form video on another social media platform, made by cutting her fancam without permission and titled 'Insane main vocalㄷㄷㄷ', had already surpassed two million views.

*Those InHeart business bastards...*

She was annoyed, but she had to admit they had played a part in getting non-fans to watch. Of course, that was because Park Moondae's singing was just that good.

"......"

She liked idols for being all-around entertainers, not just for insane vocal talent. Nevertheless, Park Moondae's solo stage this time was of such overwhelming skill that it made people ignore their personal tastes.

*What on earth did he do?*

The moment the song ended, she felt a thrilling rush of blood course through her body. It was sheer astonishment. The twin fansite master had a gut feeling it was probably more shocking because she hadn't expected it.

It was only natural that some fans who were there in person or watched the stream were going wild, high on the experience.

[The amazing thing is that this isn't even 10% of the emotion you feel seeing it live... You really have to hear singing in person]

[No, but it was even more amazing in person. Everyone, please watch the streamㅠㅠㅠ It's not too late, please buy the VOD]

└This is the right answer

└This is why so-called 'ppasuni' are such a problemㅉㅉ You should be grateful when people praise him, but instead you go a step further telling people to buy things... It's shameless and unsightly how you're obsessed with one guy

└Ohㅋ a sudden scolding

└I'm not Park Moondae, so why should I be grateful? Are you going to marry me off to Park Moondae?

└Huh? Moondae isn't an adult man, he's a 17-year-old 'gatgi'

The twin fansite master looked at this mess and nodded indifferently.

*A lot of young kids are flowing in.*

In her prejudiced view, it was usually hot-blooded teenagers who left such aggressive comments. For a seventh-year group, TeSTAR's fandom was unusually active, but it seemed a huge majority of the new influx after this concert were teenagers.

She immediately identified the entry point.

*This is it.*

It was the 'video with a binary choice' that TeSTAR had used as a concert promotion. It had perfectly suited the tastes of the younger demographic. Honestly, people over their early twenties might find the format cringey, a flaw offset by its quality and fan appeal, but for elementary and middle schoolers full of dreams, it must have been a direct hit.

[Which one should I choose to see Eugene?ㅠㅠ Please tell me]

└In the next video, pick the first option twice and the last option once. Then Eugene will appear

└Thank you!ㅠㅠ

When the concert continued in a similar vein, having them choose between 'RED' and 'YELLOW', the kids got completely excited and attached. They had settled into the fandom.

In the past, the twin fansite master would have coldly judged that a noisy bunch of kids who wouldn't help with album sales had joined. However, a steady influx of new fans was crucial for the group now, so she nodded coolly.

*This way, the company can't get any other ideas, like changing their direction or whatever.*

Besides, teenagers had a huge influence on the internet. Thanks to them chattering about it everywhere, the story of TeSTAR's concert, combined with Park Moondae's absence, seemed to be trending as a topic of casual conversation.

[The Idol Who Achieved Vocal Enlightenment After Ditching His Concert]

[Here is an idol who, due to unfortunate circumstances, could not attend his own concert. It's none other than TeSTAR's Park Moondae.]

[TeSTAR, who were enjoying successful promotions and topping the music charts with their latest song, held a concert at the Seoul Jamsil Main Stadium this year, drawing tens of thousands of fans.]

[However, member Park Moondae did not show up to the concert without any notice. What on earth could have happened?]

[Surprisingly, he had become the last witness to a missing person case...]

WeTube clickbait videos using clumsy AI voices and stitched-together images flooded the platform.

*I knew this would happen.*

The surprising thing, however, was the number of comments from foreigners.

*For a factory-produced clickbait channel, why is it even supporting Thai subtitles?*

Seeing that it supported subtitles for a whopping twenty countries, even if auto-translated, showed how serious they were about views. Even the working-professional fansite master was amazed.

With Park Moondae serving as the gateway for buzz, the other concert stages naturally received a lot of attention. The other members' solo stages also made it to the popular videos list, and their unique concert format drew interest. VR and choices.

[They even released a game, and now their concert format is insane too]

[Just how much did they spend?]

[The ticket price must have been worth itㅋㅋㅋ It would have to be Jamsil Main Stadium-level to not be in the red. Amazing.]

It was a good thing. This wasn't just the reaction within the country, either. The twin fansite master quickly checked the view distribution.

"They must have added those subtitles earlier because they were getting views from overseas."

It seemed TeSTAR's concert had become famous among K-pop fandoms. She went to an account that translated reactions from international fans, read a few posts, and became even more certain.

[A review came out in another magazine... Wow (Translated Photo)]

In truth, Park Moondae's incident had merely acted as a trigger. The TeSTAR concert's format itself was interesting enough to suit their tastes. A circus from a ghost story, disguised as a hotel. Ghost stories about haunted houses were more popular abroad than in Korea, so the barrier to entry was lower.

Furthermore, the choice-based format and the quality of the stage sets, which were difficult to attempt due to cost and practice time, made it incredibly appealing. On top of that were the rave reviews from those who had seen it. In the end, the tour itself had gained recognition as a piece of content.

[I pre-ordered the MD online. I just can't wait. Please, why do they always come to Jakarta so late? ;(]

[The resale price is a burden, but I think it's worth buying]

[You have to watch the stream at the very least]

With even the concert merchandise gaining fame, TeSTAR's tour became a hot topic. A review even appeared in a famous music critic magazine from the English-speaking world. The post the twin fansite master checked was about that review.

[TeSTAR Review: Endless Surprise and Dynamism, Calling for an Autonomous Audience]

[★★★★☆]

That review, written in English, held a symbolic weight.

"......"

The twin fansite master rested her chin on her hand. In Korea, tours often felt like an off-season. Overseas, however, it was a time when fans could actually see TeSTAR. With performance fancams pouring out, many fans actually preferred the tour season. As a result, a peculiar phenomenon occurred where sales of their past 'Hunt' album were now skyrocketing.

To sum it all up...

*Their class has gone up.*

An image had become attached to TeSTAR abroad. That meant global recognition. She nodded, thinking that pouring such an enormous amount of capital into everything had paid off.

...And so had Park Moondae's suffering.

"......"

She recalled the recent concert. Only at the very end of his solo stage did Park Moondae look as if he was on the verge of tears. This was despite him being a member who boasted a high 'crying rate' at every concert. During the fan service, when the members took turns talking, or when the fans held an event...

*He used to cry at almost one of those three moments.*

That was how he got strange nicknames like Moist Steamed Bun Apple Rice Cake. This time, however, he only ever showed a smiling face from start to finish, though he occasionally looked overwhelmed.

[ㅜㅜㅜㅜMoondae is holding back his tears, so why am I crying]

[Be happy, Moondae...]

That also seemed to be a point that strangely drove the fans wild, but the twin fansite master tried to deduce Park Moondae's psychology.

*If he cried in this situation, it would look like a ploy for sympathy, which would seem a bit cunning.*

Apologizing was also awkward. Should he have refused the call from 119 to save a person? The logic was strange no matter how you put it.

*If he said, 'I'm sorry. Whatever the reason, it's my fault...' it would just sound insincere.*

So it wouldn't have been strange if Park Moondae had considered such things. However, on that day, Park Moondae...

*...He might not have been thinking that far ahead.*

He just looked happy to be standing at the concert and seemed very grateful to the fans.

"......"

The twin fansite master, Ryu Seojin, was lost in sentiment for a moment. She snapped back to her senses, bewildered that she had gotten sentimental at all. Anyway, if Park Moondae had his head on straight, it was a good thing.

*I should just keep editing.*

She tapped busily at her keyboard. It was an important time.
