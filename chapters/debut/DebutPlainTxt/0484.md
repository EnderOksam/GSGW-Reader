# Chapter 484

Featuring.

In music, featuring is when a guest artist participates in a singer's song to perform a certain part.

The mainstream approach typically involves a guest with different attributes from the original artist. This adds a unique flavor or strength to the album.

For instance, a rapper might feature a vocalist, or a male artist might feature a female artist. A senior artist might also feature on a junior's track for promotion, especially if the junior lacks recognition.

But then.
