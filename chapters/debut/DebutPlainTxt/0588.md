# Chapter 588

The production team diligently explained the current situation and their future filming plans.

"You'll take turns with the assigned duties. Starting tomorrow, we'll also bring in local specialties and food from famous local restaurants..."

They were not defensive. They were sincere.

The production team delivered the final blow by revealing the rough cut of the first episode they had been editing.

When they said, "We'll show you the context behind this extreme composing environment," even Bae Sejin could not help but focus.

Videos with rough placeholder subtitles then played on the monitors of the editing equipment, which had been painstakingly moved to the Gangwon-do mountain village.

The content was beyond anything he could have imagined.

[We asked TeSTAR.]

[Great creation comes from .]

"......"

[Kim Raebin: Yes! I believe it comes from ❬inspiration❭!]

It was a short, passing interview he had done a few weeks ago. People who he thought were the behind-the-scenes crew had approached him as he left an awards ceremony.

"W-Wait a minute. Those people..."

"Yes. They were all actually part of our production team."

"......"

The opinions each TeSTAR member held about 'creation' appeared in quick succession.

[Great creation comes from ❬numerous attempts❭.]

[Lee Sejin: Getting skilled at something is what's most important in the end~ That's what I think!]

Numerous attempts.

At the time, he had heard it and simply thought it was very much like Lee Sejin, but hearing it like this...

*...No way?*

Bae Sejin swallowed hard.

On the screen, various opinions were shown, from his own answer, 'intense deliberation,' to Seon Ahyeon's 'time.'

And finally, Cha Eugene filled in the blank with a bright smile.

[Great creation comes from ❬hardship❭.]

[Cha Eugene: Did I get it right?]

He had mistaken it for a quiz on Korean proverbs.

Then he cheerfully followed his hyungs, leaving the awards ceremony's red carpet behind.

[Writer Lee: Oh, no.]

[Writer Park: Aaargh, Eugene!!]

[PD: Heh]

[PD: Hahahahahahahaha]

A huge, movie-style subtitle then appeared.

[TeSTAR's Fate]

[Was Decided by TeSTAR!]

"......"

"......"

"So... this is where it came from."

"Yep."

Bae Sejin squeezed his eyes shut.

"We structured the games and roles based on this..."

This whole situation was an environment created by piecing together the keywords TeSTAR had provided.

"Actually, it wasn't all bad. The environment was supposed to change drastically depending on the game results."

"......"

"But as luck would have it, your luck in the games was terrible... so the results ended up being extreme."

An accidentally extreme situation? It was bound to be funny.

In truth, the uneven food supply was a point of slight disagreement among the production staff, but most thought it would be fine since it was just for a day. Many variety shows have games where meals are on the line, and it is common for contestants to get nothing if they fail. Besides, TeSTAR seemed to be overcoming it all so cheerfully.

However, they had not properly considered how anxious the cast would become by not being told how long this situation would last.

"It's hard to get footage like this even when you try to force it, but it came out so naturally... I guess we got too greedy. We're truly sorry. Haa..."

"No... It's, yes."

In the end, even Bae Sejin had to nod. It was hard to say anything more when the production team so readily admitted their fault.

There were, in fact, a few answers that missed the key points he wanted to address.

*This should be... good enough.*

Once he realized the other side also had their own hardships, dilemmas, and various circumstances, Bae Sejin became hesitant to probe too deeply into their reasons. If he were alone, he might have pressed on, but he was in the middle of filming team content.

*...Right!*

*I should wrap this up and go back to the members with the news that 'the conversation went well'.*

Just as he made that decision, someone suddenly raised their hand.

"Excuse me."

"I have a question."

"...?"

It was Park Moondae, who had stood silently behind him like a bodyguard the entire time.

"What happened to the CM song we made?"

"Pardon?"

"Raebin didn't participate, did he?"

*H-He's just going to hammer them with it like that?*

Even Bae Sejin, who had bulldozed his way to the production team, was slightly flustered.

Park Moondae threw a slight curveball.

"I was expecting Raebin to compose, but he wasn't on the list. I spent all last night wide awake, worrying about what we'd do if the advertisers sued us..."

"......"

He lowered his head with a blank expression, but somehow he looked resigned and dejected.

"We're not going to get sued, are we?"

"Of course not!"

*I-It's serious... but not grave.*

The atmosphere did not turn heavy, but the production team could no longer avoid answering.

Bae Sejin's mouth fell open as he watched Park Moondae interrogate the production team.

***

I watched the production team diligently explain the situation to Bae Sejin. As time passed, the atmosphere improved.

*I knew it.*

*Bae Sejin's fighting spirit drops the moment he starts sympathizing with someone else's situation.*

*He seemed to accept that the production team had probably crossed a line, but since we were so good at rolling with the punches and turning it back on them, they couldn't stop and just kept flooring the accelerator. As for the cast suffering more than expected, his subconscious probably dismissed it with a 'Isn't it a national rule to suffer when you appear on our variety shows?*

*They were just excited.*

I had a rough idea of how things were playing out. However, I also knew their ulterior motive for being so cooperative and even showing us the rough cut of the first episode.

I rubbed my chin inwardly.

*So... they must have already talked about this among themselves.*

The whole 'Aren't we going too far?' thing.

When Bae Sejin contacted them, they must have panicked and prepared their answers in advance. It was obvious. Just look at how they were patting the PD's back earlier.

*They were planning to offer the PD as a sacrifice to lighten the mood.*

Well, thanks to that, the atmosphere was resolved nicely, and we did receive a sufficient apology, but was it okay for them to only answer what they wanted to answer?

*What about the CM song?*

*Did you tell the advertiser that you made it without the team's official producing member, and that you messed around with the feedback?*

This was a matter of the team's career, the most serious part of Bae Sejin's complaint, yet they were gently avoiding just that question.

Of course, neither that PD nor Ryu Seorin were people who took capitalism lightly. On the contrary, they were the type to fully savor its taste. They must have prepared countermeasures.

But this was the one thing they did not want to tell the cast yet.

*They've probably set it up so it resolves itself as filming progresses.*

That was my guess. They were probably afraid it would make the filming look unnatural.

However, what would happen if I let it slide here, thinking,*Oh, well. Let's take it into account. The production team is going through a lot.'?*

It would no longer be us letting them off the hook. The production team would think they handled the cast's complaint successfully and skillfully because they were well-prepared.

*That won't do.*

That was why I called them out on it directly.

"Ah... that."

The truth that the production team, finally unable to avoid it, confessed was...

"Actually, about the CM song... the advertisers didn't know TeSTAR was composing it."

"......"

"......"

"Pardon?"

"It was originally meant to be used as BGM for the PPL in our show."

In other words, it was an in-house CM song that came as a bonus with the PPL. It was not the pinnacle of an advertising campaign on which a company's fate depended.

"Th-Then what about the CM song auction?"

"In reality, it was an auction for the PPL's prominence. Like, 'The grand prize-winning K-pop idols TeSTAR are appearing, so how much sincerity are you willing to show?' That sort of thing."

The advertisers had not expected it to go as far as a CM song composed by TeSTAR themselves.

I turned my head and saw Bae Sejin's jaw hanging open.

*Should I call them... a bunch of scammers...?*

Wait a minute.

A sudden thought made me lift my head.

"Then the songs we've made so far...?"

"They probably just thought our outsourced staff were making them, right?"

In that case, that infuriating, insane feedback we got for the first song... surely it was not...

"Yes. The person in charge at the advertising company really did give that feedback..."

"......"

"They probably gave it thinking it was for some unknown outsourced contractor, right?"

My god. All that nonsensical feedback... so this was the reality for a rookie, unknown composer.

*Fuck...*

This was practically a social exposé documentary.

I roughly pressed my eyes.

"...Won't the company that bought the PPL complain that their image is being ruined?"

However, Ryu Seorin was composed.

"Well... it's not a criticism of the PPL product, nor of the company. We can't be held responsible for public opinion that naturally arises because of one of their employees."

"...Ah, yes."

It could be summarized as, 'Well, who told them to hire such a boneheaded employee?'

"Anyway, that's what happened."

Ryu Seorin hesitated slightly before adding, "Since it was made in a poor environment in just one day, we thought you'd actually be freer from judgment on the final product than if you'd made it in the best environment."

"......"

"Even if people want to criticize the song for being bad, there's the possible rebuttal of, 'How can you say that after seeing the environment they were in?'"

If the song turned out well, it was thanks to TeSTAR. If it did not, it was thanks to the production team.

*So they did pull some strings in their own way to reduce the pressure.*

"And if the reviews are good... you'd feel a sense of pride, like, 'Wow, even without people knowing it's TeSTAR, and without our main producing member, the song we made is getting praised!'"

Then, after gradually getting the viewers accustomed to the composing process, they would unleash the potential with the real composing content.

Which was...

"The ❬Doll Hunter❭ OST."

"...!"

That was, in fact, this program's real main sponsor and main content.

Bae Sejin's pupils trembled.

"B-But. Right before I joined, the drama production company decided on the PPL on the spot, saying 'it looks fun'..."

"We were acting to reel you in, Sejin."

"......"

Bae Sejin collapsed into his chair. He seemed past the point of feeling betrayed.

*...It must have been funny.*

I briefly acknowledged it, imagining the moment all these twists were revealed.

***

"For the main content, you'll be able to compose in a truly proper environment!"

"That's right! Don't you worry."

"...Yep."

And so, the meeting with the production team concluded well. We left their quarters and returned to the mountain cabin.

*It was okay.*

I now clearly understood these people's intentions, and we did receive a definite apology.

The PD even came out to see us off, saying he was 'heading out for a smoke,' and confessed something quite frankly.

"Since you guys from TeSTAR are so successful... we figured you were at a level where you didn't need to worry about what we thought. We just assumed, 'If they really hate it or find it difficult, they'll say something~'."

"......"

So they had cherry-picked the traits of skilled entertainers and top-tier idols to create their own internal formula for TeSTAR.

*It's a relief that you've reflected on it.*

I stared blankly at the PD.

"Do you think it was a misjudgment?"

"...It was, wasn't it?"

"Then I can cook starting tomorrow, right?"

"...You still have to do the game."

I stared at the PD again without a word. He soon lamented.

"Ah, we'll let you keep retrying until you get picked for kitchen duty!"

"An excellent choice."

CLAP, CLAP, CLAP.

And with that, that conversation also came to a close. Although the PD was a bit clingy until the very end.

"...We were planning to reveal the secret about the CM song after the Loviewer online listening party... Could you perhaps keep it a secret from the other members until then?"

"No, I can't."

"......"

"I'm a very honest person with my members."

"I know that all too well..."

Not a chance.

"Even if they know, they're good at pretending to be surprised, so don't worry."

"...!"

I turned back to the production team with a faint smile.

"You should have a little faith in TeSTAR."

"...Of course. All of us on the production team have immense faith in our variety show geniuses, TeSTAR!"

Such a smooth talker.

In any case, the other staff members who had been secretly watching from the doorway chuckled. We also smiled as we said our goodbyes to the PD and left.

"Get back safely!"

The aftertaste was not bad.

Although a commotion did break out back at TeSTAR's lodging.

"Ah, guys. Did the talk go well?"

"You... don't be surprised when you hear this."

"......"

The members shuddered as they heard twist after twist. Kim Raebin steeled his resolve at the news that he would escape the 'hotel-vacation' hell tomorrow.

By the time we went to bed after all that chaos, it was already dawn.

"......"

Bae Sejin, who had been lying still, spoke quietly after confirming I was not asleep.

"Park Moondae."

"Yes."

"You... were amazing."

Hm?

"I mean, the way you draw out the answers you want without ruining the atmosphere of the conversation."

I wondered what he was getting at.

"It's all just cheap tricks."

Bastards who are good at running their mouths like this usually have tiring personalities. Everything has its pros and cons.

Besides.

"What are you talking about? None of this would have happened if you hadn't decided to speak up in the first place, hyung."

"...!"

If this guy had not bothered to say anything, most of the members in this team, given their personalities, would have just endured it and somehow finished the filming well.

*But they would have ended up completely fed up.*

What would have happened then? If things had continued like that... perhaps next time, they might have had a real falling out with this production team.

In that sense, this guy's inflexible ethical nature contributed to the team in a unique way. More than enough.

"It was a relief that you calmly said what needed to be said, hyung."

"...Thank you."

Bae Sejin was silent for a moment. Then he added in a slightly brighter voice.

"I think it was a good thing we talked today."

"I think so, too."

"Me too."

"......"

"...Cha Eugene, why aren't you asleep?"

"This is a three-person room. I can do what I want."

"Haha!"

That night, Bae Sejin reportedly slept very sweetly and comfortably, even though it was only for three or four hours.

***

And a short time later.

As TeSTAR's grueling confinement in the mountain cabin aired, they finally began their 'real' composing.

The OST for ❬Doll Hunter❭ Season 2.

An imaginary world covered in snow.

Jeong Isol's theme song.
