# Chapter 433

"......"

I stopped what I was doing. An unexpected face in a collapsed building wasn't new, but this felt surreal.

It was Cheongryeo.

*I thought a guy like him would already be outside giving an interview.*

*Doesn't he have experience with this?*

The audience seats were dark, but the Cheongryeo standing under the emergency light was no hallucination. Even if he had stayed behind, how did he get out of a half-blocked emergency exit?

There was no time to dwell on the question. The atmosphere froze as if everyone had seen a ghost. He began to walk. The sound of his dress shoes echoed before his voice sounded again.

"Junior?"

In that moment, I knew what I had to do.

"That thing in your hand."

I swallowed.

"Is that a CCTV?"

"......"

He stopped in front of the stage and looked up.

"So you were looking for something after all."

"A person."

I picked up the rope again.

"There's someone who collapsed in the audience seats."

"......"

"I have to find them."

Cheongryeo stopped and turned his gaze toward the audience seats.

My palms sweated as I stared at him. I barely noticed Seon Ahyeon snatching the rope back from me.

His answer came.

"Hmm, I think I've already found them."

"...!"

He held up the CCTV device. An infrared camera was running near the main entrance escape route.

"There!"

I quickly turned my flashlight. Under a back seat on the far left, I saw a pair of collapsed legs.

Keun Sejin ran closer and shone his own flashlight on the upper body.

"......"

He turned over the torso slumped under the chair, revealing a familiar face. Ryu Gunwoo's hat had fallen off.

It was Keundal.

*Hoo.*

I let out a breath.

Cheongryeo claimed he was 'coincidentally' watching the audience on his CCTV. I could rewind the recording and see Keundal suddenly collapsing during the evacuation. I had a lot to say about that.

"Let's get him up first."

"......"

This was more urgent. While holding onto Keundal, I checked the Status Window.

[00:15:53]

...No change.

*Just touching him isn't enough.*

*As I thought, I'll have to talk to him directly to figure out what's going on.*

"Keundal, Park Moondae, Ryu Gunwoo!"

I tapped and shook Keundal's shoulder, using every name he had ever gone by. Keundal didn't even flinch. He just swayed with the external force.

*...Don't tell me.*

I placed my hand under his nose. Thankfully, he was breathing. It was steady, as if he were sleeping. If he still couldn't wake up...

*Something might still be happening inside the Status Window.*

I decided not to waste any more time. The time limit was pressing.

*Still, getting out won't be a problem.*

*First, I'll carry him on my back and move...*

"Are you crazy?"

"I-I've got a cramp...!"

"......"

...I was about to, but I ended up supporting him with the others.

"We have to move in a single file line."

"...Right."

The path down to the basement wasn't wide enough for all of us to move together. Still, it was a relief that the passage to the emergency stairs where Cheongryeo had come from was open. It saved us time.

I lifted Keundal with Seon Ahyeon and Keun Sejin. Lifting one person didn't seem like a big problem as long as we kept our balance.

"Hmm, shall I help carry him?"

"We're fine."

I had no intention of letting that guy help. Surprisingly, Cheongryeo moved to the front and supported the area around Keundal's neck.

"...!"

*Is this bastard hiding something?*

I would save my thoughts on his surprising cooperativeness for later.

"Let's just go."

Escape was the priority. We started moving quickly toward the emergency stairs. The dark audience seats flew past us.

From the front, Keun Sejin's dark silhouette spoke.

"Sunbaenim, I didn't expect to see you here. How did you end up staying behind?"

"Who knows."

The flashlight beam revealed Keun Sejin clenching his jaw before forcing his expression back to neutral with superhuman control. I could relate.

*He shouldn't have asked.*

[00:13:06]

Thirteen minutes left.

When we arrived, the area before the emergency stairs was cluttered with debris, but it was passable if we watched our step. I didn't know when it was cleared, but...

*It's enough.*

"Let's go in carefully!"

"......"

We entered the emergency exit, careful not to let Keundal's neck or limbs bend at an odd angle.

Then, a voice suddenly sounded right next to my ear.

"So you've come."

"......"

I almost punched him.

I turned my head and saw the guy from earlier with the drone. Seon Ahyeon raised his flashlight and spoke in a flustered voice.

"S-Sunbaenim...?"

"A pleasure to meet you, members of TeSTAR. As expected from the situation, I thought you'd be rescuing at least one person."

It was VTIC's Judan.

Why was the guy who had already checked the escape route from the basement here? Don't tell me.

I scanned him up and down. Unlike Cheongryeo in his MC suit, Judan wore casual clothes, as if he had just arrived in the waiting room. His clothes were more suitable for manual labor.

*...This guy must have cleared the debris with him.*

No wonder it didn't make sense for Cheongryeo to have cleared it alone. There was another person providing the labor.

Judan dusted off his gloves and shrugged.

"I was cooperating quite diligently for the perfect timing."

I had no idea what bullshit he was spouting.

"And as expected, we're heading for a textbook successful happy ending."

*Think whatever you want.*

"Yes, thank you anyway."

My eyes, now adjusted to the dark, finally spotted the fans moving behind him, covering their heads. It seemed they had finished organizing and started evacuating.

"J-Judan??"

Someone who spotted a VTIC member would unconsciously call out their name, but most people kept their mouths shut and moved frantically. Trying to move an unconscious person through that crowd could get someone hurt.

"We should let them pass, then move."

"Right."

We paused for a moment. We let Keundal lean against us as we caught our breath.

*Hoo.*

I watched the people descending with space between them to avoid falling. I suddenly spoke up. The staff had collected them to prevent spoilers, so the audience members didn't have them, but I remembered Cheongryeo did.

*He was on a call.*

A smartphone.

"Did you report this, by any chance?"

"Ah."

He glanced down at his suit jacket pocket. From the looks of it, he hadn't. At this point, escaping would be faster than reporting it, so it was better to just keep moving.

"Hmm, that's strange."

"What is?"

"Nothing."

"......"

*Don't question it. Escape comes first.*

But then he spoke up again on a different topic.

"You seem quite stable."

"What does?"

"Your condition, Junior."

He probably meant I didn't seem to be in immediate mortal danger for someone about to die from a 'Status Abnormality Failure'. My mental state looked decent too.

"What do you know?"

I could have ignored him, but I felt gazes on me from both sides. It was Seon Ahyeon and Keun Sejin.

*Damn it.*

This... It would be better to give a rough explanation.

"...I can see the time of the next collapse."

"Yes."

"...The next one is the final one. It said it was the last."

A momentary silence fell. The others who were listening had figured it out too.

"The last one?"

"Yeah. It seems like it's ending."

"Ah...!"

It could be interpreted in many ways. Before anyone could ponder its meaning, a voice came from above.

"They're over there!"

"Ah!!"

"...! They cleared the stuff from the door!"

The guys who had sent the audience members first finally started running down from the second floor.

"Is that everyone?!"

"......"

The other TeSTAR members' faces lit up as soon as they saw us, and they ran over.

"You!! Please consult with us before you act!"

Let's just say they were happy to see me.

Cha Eugene, the first to run over, shouted.

"Hyungs are here! Everyone is Oka... Who the hell was that?!"

"The person I was looking for, remember?"

"Oh-"

He drew out a long exclamation, then nodded after checking the limp Keundal's face.

"Give him to me! I'll carry him."

"No, it's fine."

Keundal's body was passed from person to person like a restaurant bill until he was settled on Cha Eugene's back.

"Let's go!"

The guys seemed surprised to see the VTIC members, but they started moving again. A tacit agreement formed that getting down was the priority.

"We just have to get out now, right?"

"Yes."

We had done everything we could. The atmosphere was that of an escape, the pressure and tension slightly eased.

Cheongryeo's muttering cut through it.

"Hmm. It must be one of two things. Either the disaster ends with that, or..."

*Were you still calculating?*

"Your junior's opportunity ends."

"......"

*Hoo.*

"What does that mean?"

"Park Moondae?"

I kept my mouth shut. Cheongryeo finished his sentence.

"The building completely collapses."

Just then.

THUD.

RUMBLE-!

"...!"

"A tremor?"

"Hyung, don't tell me you distorted time again..."

"No."

I hurriedly checked the pop-up.

[00:09:59]

There was definitely time left.

"There's still time, I'm sure of it."

Then, Bae Sejin muttered with a pale face.

"...Doesn't a bigger collapse mean the warning signs come earlier?"

"......"

A brief silence passed.

Cheongryeo muttered again.

"Hmm. So it was the latter, after all."

*Is this really the time for that?*

"If you know, then run!"

I started running fast, pulling the others along.

BAM!

"Aaaah!"

A scream echoed from the basement.

I shouted.

"The key! Where's the key?"

"I gave it to the person at the very front! Both of them!"

Ryu Chungwoo wouldn't lie, so it was the truth. Then people should be getting out right now...

*Why are they all clustered together?*

The basement corridor we arrived at was packed with dozens of people.

CREAK, BAM!

"Ugh, sob!"

Every time a loud noise came from above, people covered their heads with their light sticks or hands.

*What the.*

*Why can't they get out?*

"Excuse me!"

I pushed through the crowd. At the very front, a person stood before a steel door labeled 'For Facility Security'. They urgently shouted as soon as our eyes met.

"This door won't open!"

"......"

What?

I pushed through the crowd and took the keycard from the audience member's hand. I tried it myself.

BEEEEEP-!

[Unauthorized Card.]

Denied.

*Fuck.*

I tried the other card.

BEEEEEP-!

The next one too.

BEEEEEP-!

"......"

It didn't work.

"W-Why isn't this working??"

"Huuuuuh, huh."

Among the people who looked like they were about to hyperventilate, I racked my brain. There had to be a reason.

*The reason it's not working is...*

*Think, think.*

*Unauthorized?*

I quickly examined the keypad and the steel door. I looked again at the words written on the door.

[For Facility Security]

"......"

Ah, damn it.

"Moondae?!"

"Just a second!"

I practically yelled as I pushed my way back through the clustered people.

"What are you doing?!"

"The facility manager's card!!"

I shouted.

"The ones we brought are for office staff, we need a security team card!"

"...!"

This was a space for the security team. It wasn't strange for the permissions to be set that way.

I gritted my teeth.

*Isn't there a chance one of them left it behind?*

*If I go to the next room and search... yeah, fuck. I know.*

*Even Judan, who came first, didn't find it.*

Still, I had to try. I was about to tell the people to start a coordinated search.

It was then.

"Junior."

TUK.

A rectangular plastic badge with a golden logo dangled before my eyes. Cheongryeo had just taken it out of his inner suit pocket and was holding it out.

"......"

"......"

[Seton Security]

"It was in the drawer where the CCTV was."

I immediately snatched it and ran to the front. I held it to the reader.

CHIME!

A short, cheerful sound in a major key played.

CLICK.

"It opened!"

The door was unlocked.

Cheers, screams, and desperate footsteps mingled with the sound of cracking as people poured out. The outside air hit my face.

"Ha."

I covered my face with one hand. That was a close call.

*No wonder that bastard wasn't escaping.*

He was clearly holding out because he had a way to get out with that card.

"Cheongryeo-hyung must not have shared it in preparation for me betraying him in an extreme situation."

"No. You were right next to me when I used it to disable the CCTV security."

"......"

*That bastard Judan... don't tell me he didn't recognize it as an ID card because it was a badge...*

*No, anyway, it's open, so that's all that matters.*

I deliberately held the door and stood aside. I pretended to direct people out smoothly while stalling for time.

[00:06:16]

The time was fine.

"All of you, get out quickly!"

"We will."

They would have left quickly anyway, just like the fans who were now leaving and calling out a word or two over their shoulders.

"Is everyone out?"

"Let's go now!"

The guys, with their uselessly strong sense of responsibility, insisted on waiting until the very end. I held the door open again, gesturing for them to go first.

A few of them narrowed their eyes.

"You're not thinking of..."

"I told you, no."

Hey, do you really think I'd voluntarily stay behind in a collapsing building?

THUDUDUK, THUMP!

The heavy sound of cracking came from above again. I quickly opened my mouth.

"I can see the time, so I want to be the last one out. Just go! The more you hesitate, the later it gets."

"......"

However, Seon Ahyeon took a deep breath and stopped.

"...!"

"I-If we don't leave at the same time, I'm not leaving."

Ah, damn it.

*I was just trying to create some distance in case some other crazy shit happened.*

I gestured to Cheongryeo with my eyes, but he just stared back without reacting. It wasn't just Seon Ahyeon. The other guys also started holding the door and staring at me intently.

*Are we having a staring contest? This is driving me crazy.*

*There are five minutes left, you crazy bastards.*

"......"

I looked back and forth between the outside and the guys. I held back a sigh and finally spoke.

*I'm sure I can find an opening to push them out or something.*

"...Fine, let's go out together."

"...!! Yeah!"

I pushed Seon Ahyeon's back and stepped outside.

In that instant, the outdoor air rushed in. The cool air of early autumn.

The dark, backyard-like area was hidden by a container wall. As soon as we rounded the corner, sunlight poured down.

"...!"

...It was the sunset.

In that moment, the anxiety, impatience, the sharp warnings of my survival instinct, and the cold fear all vanished. Everything was sucked away, and only a complete sense of peace remained.

"Ah..."

I took a deep breath. My head felt clear.

As if on cue, the pop-up changed.

[00:04:47]

[00:04:47]

[Target Lost]

[Deactivated]

A sense of stability returned. I turned and looked at the building we had just exited. The roaring sounds had disappeared.

I unconsciously opened my mouth.

"...It's over."

"Huh?"

"I think it's over. The collapse."

The other guys also looked back at the building. They let out long sighs or finally broke into small laughs. It couldn't have been more refreshing.

***

My body was heavy, but the atmosphere was friendly and hopeful.

"Hoo."

"Everyone worked hard."

"I-I'm glad... that everyone made it out safely..."

"Still, we should move quickly now. If it collapses, we could get swept away."

"Th-That's true."

At Keun Sejin's words, everyone quickened their pace. Even so, they didn't stop talking.

"Where are we?"

"I suspect it's a secret entrance behind the building that not many people know about."

"Ah, then the front is probably swarming with the media."

That... was a certainty.

*There couldn't be better bait.*

The rescue team's delay might even be because the media had already taken all the spots and were causing a scene. Well, since I came out, the collapse seems to have stopped. Even if staff members were isolated on the third floor, they'll probably be rescued soon.

After all, hadn't all the people the media were after come out?

"The aftermath... hmm."

Top-tier idols trapped in a building at a charity concert hosted by top-tier idols? Honestly, I could foresee hell.

"Moondae, stop thinking about the aftermath and let's go to the hospital first."

"......"

*Hey, anyone would think I'm someone who doesn't believe in modern medicine.*

"...Yes. Anyway. This seems to be the front, so if we go around..."

"Right."

We trudged along in our rather shabby state. We rounded the weed-covered back path to the front of the building.

*Soon, people's screams...*

However.

"Why aren't there any people?"

There was nothing in front of the building.

"......"

Pedestrians, sirens, reporters, onlookers. Everything I thought would surely be there was gone without a trace.

Only silence flowed. There wasn't even a single car on the road.

*What the.*

"Wait. ...What about the fans who came out earlier?"

"......"

Bae Sejin urgently shouted.

"Even if there's no media, we should at least see those people!"

However, the front of the building, bathed in the sunset, was still empty. It was as if everyone had exited somewhere else.

"Ah, so it was this."

I turned my head. Cheongryeo looked down at his jacket with a strange expression. At the spot where his smartphone was.

"I thought it was strange that I hadn't received a single call."

"...!"

That's right. If the building had collapsed, the smartphone of someone presumed to be trapped inside would be flooded with calls. The fact that he hadn't received a single call meant...

*...We were cut off from the outside world.*

Or, we had been cut off from a certain point.

And just then.

"...Heup!"

"Hup!"

Keundal, who was on Cha Eugene's back, opened his eyes. He shouted this.

"I-I won!"

"......"

A new pop-up appeared.

[Mission Failure Scenario Complete]

Victory: Player Park Moondae
