# Chapter 637

At some point, seeing that bastard Cheongryeo became a package deal.

"......"

I watched the dog run over, wagging its tail so hard it looked like its butt would fly off. I had only looked in on the dog a few times while Cheongryeo was in the military, but somehow it had gotten even friendlier.

"Is it time for a walk?"

"That's right."

This wasn't the cafe where I usually met him. It was a walking path in an apartment complex that often grew foggy at dawn due to its location.

This was TeSTAR's old dorm before we moved companies. It was also where VTIC's current dorm was located.

*Did he say they only live together during promotions?*

The path was for residents only, and since it was dawn, no one was around. The fog made it hard to see clearly. This meant I was meeting Cheongryeo here for a reason beyond our busy schedules only allowing for dawn meetings.

Cheongryeo, dressed in black on a bench, spoke.

"I figured it'd be better to completely eliminate any 'they met up on purpose' witness accounts right now."

He was right.

He was absolutely right.

We had just gone through a period of gritting our teeth and going crazy over chart performance after our groups released albums at the same time. There was no need to make fans feel like, 'We're all getting along just fine as friends, haha. You're the only ones fighting.'

*Ugh, just thinking about it makes me want to pull my hair out.*

It was convenient that we both understood this without needing to explain it.

"Whine..."

The ball of fur pacing before my knees looked between me and its guardian, as if asking why we weren't walking. It seemed to give up and plopped onto the ground.

A short silence passed, filled only by the dog's panting.

"......"

Come to think of it, we didn't have anything specific to say just because we were meeting face-to-face. We had released our albums in a way that was like openly punching each other, so it wouldn't be strange to think we had something to settle.

*It's not like we made a bet or anything.*

Circumstantially, I had won, so there was no need to laugh in his face and ask if he conceded.

*Am I a child?*

I could have said something like, 'Then why the fuck did you throw down the gauntlet?' but I just leaned back on the bench and patted the dog.

The winner could afford to do that.

TeSTAR had definitely won.

"Hoobae. It wouldn't be strange for you to be very happy right now."

"I don't know."

However, the question of whether we had completely crushed our opponent was rather ambiguous.

"Our song only really started charting in the US with the remix, so our promotion periods ended up a bit different from yours."

TeSTAR's remix promotions began around the time VTIC's comeback activities had finished. The timing was slowly thrown off. A fire needs a steady supply of firewood to keep burning, and public interest was the same.

*We milked it for about a month. By the time the MT variety show was over, the simultaneous release gimmick wasn't drawing any more attention.*

Both of us appearing at Glastonbury was the last piece of firewood. Modern society gets bored so quickly, doesn't it? People wouldn't keep comparing us for months just to debate who won.

Besides, we did exceptionally well. I heard that even when it came up online, it didn't become a controversy and was settled with comments like, 'This year belongs to the TeSTAR remix'.

If you call that a true victory, I suppose you'd be right. However...

"You got everything you needed out of it, too."

A refresh after military service, a consolidated fandom, and domestic buzz. VTIC also gained a considerable number of new fans. If they make their next album well, they'll maintain their momentum.

They must have known they'd lose to us on the music charts anyway. The only unexpected loss from this battle was maybe the Glastonbury buzz?

*Even then, they still secured the honor of being the headliner.*

So, to sum it all up, I thought this sword fight would be a zero-sum game where the winner takes all, but now that it was over, it was closer to a win-win for both teams.

Suddenly, I remembered what Cheongryeo had said during the MT variety show filming a few months ago.

[It's a moment that won't come again, so we must cultivate it with care, maintain the balance... and use it well.]

"You saw this far ahead?"

"Who knows."

Cheongryeo looked at me, moving only his eyes. The corners of his lips lifted faintly under his black cap.

"Let's just say... I didn't think there was zero possibility of me losing."

"......"

I let out a small laugh. Well, should I thank him for not underestimating me? I turned my gaze to the dog, which was rubbing its face against my ankle. It tickled.

"Thanks for setting the stage. I had a great time."

"......"

"How does it feel?"

"Not bad."

The guy rested his chin on his hand.

"That's too harsh. I always worried whenever you were at a disadvantage, Hoobae."

"Are you serious?"

"Of course."

I shook my head, and the guy laughed.

*Can't be helped.*

That's just how he is.

The atmosphere softened. As if sensing the change, the dog stood up and started playing with its front paws. It was going to put a hole in my pants.

"This little guy was in the post-credits scene of the MT variety show."

"They insisted on using Kongi. Your group also had a puppy that appeared on a variety show, didn't you, Hoobae?"

"It's Mungge."

"Hmm?"

"His name is Mungge."

"Is that so? I thought you always called dogs 'dog' and didn't bother memorizing their names."

"......"

I declared that I would call that bastard Cheongryeo's dog... no, Kongi, by his name.

"......"

This bastard was laughing too much, but I decided to let it slide just for today.

"I heard you're preparing for a concert."

"......"

Until the fog lifted, we sat in a corner of the walking path, sipping sports drinks and talking. We didn't settle any scores, but we did get some closure.

It was more comfortable than I expected.

***

*Right. It was fine.*

Cheongryeo finished reminiscing about his talk with Park Moondae.

[VTIC didn't really lose anything.]

That was the conclusion from their dawn conversation.

However.

*But the actual feeling is a different matter.*

In the waiting room, Cheongryeo didn't flinch as a brush moved around his eyes. He only shifted his pupils to survey his surroundings. It wasn't a matter of public opinion.

It was a matter of morale within the group.

Walking while others are running means you're falling behind. The pressure of knowing that a male idol group in the same position released a song that did better than theirs was palpable.

The company staff, perhaps out of defiance, frequently praised VTIC's recent performance, but their boisterousness felt so artificial that it actually soured the mood.

*I'm sure I warned them not to act like this.*

It seemed that function had atrophied from lack of use. Because 'this' VTIC had never once struggled with their chart performance. They had never been pushed aside by another group, nor had the checks from senior or junior groups ever dealt a significant blow or caused fans to leave.

There was the huge incident of a member leaving, but that was an accident, a disaster. Promotions may have been canceled, but it meant they had never witnessed failure with their own eyes.

In short, this VTIC was internally digesting the feeling of defeat for the first time.

"......"

Cheongryeo suddenly had an unfamiliar realization.

*'This' VTIC.*

That expression wasn't appropriate. There would be no more numbering them. The life that was like an endlessly reloaded bullet, an existence replaced like a consumable part, was now over.

All that remained was a single bullet that had already been fired. Its trajectory and result would remain in this place.

Without disappearing. Forever.

Along with defeat.

"......"

"Um."

After the stylist left, the member sitting next to him spoke up. It was Judan.

"Hyung, I heard you met with Moondae."

Chaeyul, sitting across from them, smiled audibly.

"Ah, Woodan, you want to see Moondae too? I've been wanting to see him lately as well."

"No. The idea that meeting in person is always better is an old saying. Communication technology has advanced so much. It's more economical to save on costs."

"Our maknae is so smart..."

Momentarily dumbfounded, Chaeyul nodded.

Shino chimed in from the opposite side.

"Moondae must be in a good mood. TeSTAR did really well this time."

"That's right!"

The stylist returned and used a hairdryer to warm up a metal body chain for the final touch.

WHIRR...

The hairdryer's noise filled the room, pausing the conversation.

"Hoo..."

A slightly sorrowful sigh quickly vanished.

When the chain was on and the members were once again sitting idly, Chaeyul muttered without lifting his head.

"Actually... even while telling the TeSTAR members that we should all promote well together, I naturally assumed we would win."

"......"

"It's just, I... Ah! I sounded like a jerk just now, didn't I!"

"A little."

"Our maknae is so scary..."

"However, I also consider it to be a natural thing."

Judan fiddled with the body chain around his neck.

"Humans are creatures that think based on their experiences, after all."

When TeSTAR first debuted, they had pitied the rookie group that released an album at the same time and naturally predicted their own victory. They had always been able to win, no matter what, as long as they worked themselves to death. That sense of inertia had formed over several years and become a sentiment naturally established within the team.

Therefore, the shock of that sentiment being broken was also unavoidable. At least, that's what Judan thought.

Chaeyul replied in a slightly emotional voice.

"Thank you so much, Woodan. To think we're having this kind of conversation! If you also felt that way..."

"Ah, I didn't."

"......"

"I have a habit of always assuming the worst-case scenario. I'm always mentally prepared."

The members were horrified.

"I-Is that okay, though?"

"Isn't that too extreme of a way to live? Woodan should practice relaxing a bit... We should all think about it..."

That was enough.

"Well. Let's save the worrying for a more appropriate time."

The waiting room fell silent.

Cheongryeo smiled faintly.

"Now isn't the moment for that."

"Yes, sir."

The members shut their mouths and quickly got up from their seats. Their sharp, swift movements were no different from their debut days.

Yes, it was the same as always.

"Ah, our cheer!"

Even if the feeling of defeat was clear, their slogan didn't change.

"Today, too, the winning, VTIC, ah, Victorious!"

Cheongryeo turned his head. Light from the stage was leaking in.

"Time to go."

September. The day the postponed Coachella opened.

WAAAAAH!!

A huge audience, already hyped from the previous performances, roared. Dry ice billowed across a stage where they started without any greeting.

[Coming up next... VTIC]

The electronic screen turned red.

A beast-like performance unfolded, featuring a marching band-like group dance with dozens of dancers. They tore through an old flag used as a screen.

WAAAAAAAAAH!!

Then, a group dance began. They walked along the giant flagpole where the cloth had been torn before dropping onto the stage. Bursting flames and fireworks, along with the thrill of the live band, stimulated the audience's nerves and drew out their fervor.

Coachella, filled with fans who had managed to get tickets, boiled like lava and spewed heat like steam.

Chaeyul ran out to handle the chorus, Judan added a doubling from behind, and Shino emphasized his gestures. Their faces, completely immersed, showed they had forgotten their waiting room conversation. They overflowed with excitement and passion.

*That's right.*

Everything was in its place.

[Look at the Serpent]

Cheongryeo stared out at the audience. How many times had he made everything into nothing before he could even feel defeat? At some point, he had started living, having forgotten the feeling of defeat.

And now, that 'feeling' he couldn't avoid and had to face was...

WAAAAAH!!!

Nothing.

Nothing had changed. The cheers, the audience, the members.

The stage continued.

[Venomous teeth

Digging into the heart, poison]

Cheongryeo took a deep, slow breath.

He lifted his head.

The taste of defeat was better than he thought.

***

[VTIC's Historic Stage as Coachella Headliner]

"Oh."

The screen showed a center shot of Cheongryeo, his hand on his in-ear monitor. It looked like it was shot for a photobook.

"The reaction is good."

"......"

I saw competitiveness on the members' faces as they peered at my smartphone, but no resentment or dismay remained.

"Let's do Coachella next year!"

"Sounds good, but first, let's work hard on our own stuff."

Lee Sejin grinned.

"Five-minute break is over!"

"Ugh."

"Haa..."

Bae Sejin, who had been lying on the floor as if dead, stirred and got up. In the waiting room while preparing for the concert, there was no time to rest.

*Hoo...*

Just as I was getting up and suppressing a groan, my smartphone suddenly started ringing loudly.

"...!"

"What's wrong?"

"Just a second."

I checked the caller ID, instantly raised a hand to stop practice from resuming, and answered my phone.

It was an irresistible force.

[Ryu Gunwoo]

*Keundal... Park Moondae is calling out of the blue?*

*He's not the type to do that.*

I answered the call without delay.

[Hyung!!]

*Hey.*

The voice burst out.*My ears are going to explode.*

Fortunately, the voice was happy and bright. No, it was practically ecstatic.*What is it?*

[I passed!]

"Congratulations."

Congratulating someone for passing is just common courtesy. After giving the appropriate reaction, I asked.

"But for what?"

[Ah.]

An awkward exclamation came from over the phone, realizing he hadn't explained. Right, so now for the explanation...

[I passed the Orbit Entertainment interview!]

"......"

[Your company, hyung! TeSTAR's company!]

"......"

"What's going on... Moondae?"

"Moondae-hyung's eyes just doubled in size."

A guy I didn't even introduce went and got into the company on his own.
