# Chapter 479

Seoul Waterbomb.

"WAAAAAH!!"

"Ack! Hey!"

Streams of water, laughter, screams, and music erupted from all directions.

The frenzied summer festival, a trade-off between fun and stamina, raged since noon.

Usually, people came to these events just to have fun, but today's Waterbomb crowd was different.

"This is the kids' last one, right?"

"Yeah, yeah. Probably?"

They were fans of TeSTAR.

Not everyone was a fan, of course. TeSTAR was famous, and this wasn't their solo concert.

However, many people who would normally skip a ten-hour party of water guns and screaming had shown up today.

Even non-fans who came to Waterbomb today looked forward to enjoying the performance of TeSTAR as 'famous singers'.

"Ah, it's so cold."

"Hey, the more famous acts are on in the evening. TeSTAR probably won't be on until after 9, right??"

"Okay. Let's hold out. ...Whoa, hey, Myudi is coming on stage!"

"Awesome!"

Unlike the general attendees focused on the festival, some of TeSTAR's fans were plagued by a different kind of anxiety.

It was, of course, because of Cha Eugene's absence.

[It's such a shame Eugene isn't coming ㅠㅠ]

[Cha Goyoung would have been flying all over the stage. Aigoo, he must be the most disappointed one...]

[People who are never sick have it rough when they finally get sick... Loviewer, don't worry, just rest well. We want to see you healthy, our Eugene (Photo)]

The announcement that Cha Eugene was halting his schedule for health reasons had been posted a few days ago. Most people naturally predicted he wouldn't be at Waterbomb today.

Posts expressing concern for Cha Eugene's health and wishing him a speedy recovery were easy to find on social media.

However, one problem prevented things from ending on a supportive note.

Cha Eugene's star power was just too great.

[?? A sudden break without even telling us the illness??]

[ㅋㅋㅋThe fact that Cha Eugene hasn't posted a single thing on his official account is super suspicious, for real]

[Don't tell me his Chamera acted up again]

[Huh, Cha Eugene on a stress-related break? ㅋㅋ Why does that sound like such a lie? It doesn't suit him at all]

Had Cha Eugene ever been sick since his debut? Sick enough to suddenly halt his schedule indefinitely?

Moreover, the company never announced the specifics of his illness. The statement only said he was advised to take a temporary break due to 'physical and mental strain'.

So, subtly or overtly, such thoughts began to surface.

*I'm curious.*

Why exactly is he sick?

Although nothing had surfaced publicly, all sorts of speculation circulated beneath the surface of the fandom.

On top of that, a few trolls suddenly started appearing at dawn.

[Cha Eugene isn't sick, he's not here because he's meeting his girlfriendㅋㅋㅋ^^ The company went crazy yesterday because he stayed out all night without permission, you idiots]

[Account for finding the lost rookie spirit of Cha Eugene Ignacio who ran away from home (★★★He actually ran away from the dorm★★★)]

They were anonymous accounts. These accounts, all with similar tones, appeared out of nowhere and spouted a stream of provocative rumors.

They said all sorts of things, from him having a girlfriend to fighting with the members and leaving the dorm. They even claimed there were issues since the contract renewal.

Furthermore, the fallout led to rumors that TeSTAR might not appear at Waterbomb at all.

[Just watch and see if TeSTAR shows up or notㅋㅋㅋ I'm telling you this out of pity]

[If a sudden cancellation notice pops up, don't you dare try to spin it as a mental victory]

At that point, it was inevitable that some impressionable people would start to think,*Where there's smoke, there's fire*.

[Looks like something is going on]

[I know it's wrong to consume rumors from sasaengs, but honestly, I'm anxious. It's been obvious since debut that he's on a different wavelength]

[Please, ah Gommeo, ah Big Bird, you guys search for this stuff, right? Do something. Your silence makes it seem more real ㅠ]

Things quieted down by noon, but the fans who had already seen the posts felt uneasy.

*Ah, this is so annoying...*

A Kim Raebin fan, here at Waterbomb with her friends, felt the same. She never expected to attend an event like this.

*Cha Eugene, did you really have to get sick right now?*

She already thought they should get a partial refund since one of the members best suited for Waterbomb was absent.

Of course, she wouldn't actually do it. She was here for Kim Raebin.

*Kim Raebin going shirtless... Yeah, he wouldn't do that.*

Muttering that she wasn't expecting anything, the Kim Raebin fan stealthily squeezed forward and sideways. She got as close as possible to the extended stage's fence.

The sticky, cold feeling from standing outdoors for hours while soaked couldn't hinder her movements.

"Huh? You want to watch from closer up?"

"Uh-huh."

Her friends followed along, swept up in the moment.

She looked around and saw many people in a silent battle, filling any empty spaces. It was clear they were all recalling the timetable in their heads.

Right. The uneasiness was one thing, but seeing TeSTAR in person was another.

*What do you mean, TeSTAR isn't coming?*

In the end, no cancellation notice was ever posted.

Now, it was time for the final segment.

*They're coming out!!*

[Hello Seoul~]

PAPAPAPAPANG!!

Amidst water cannons, intense sound, fiery effects, and flashing LEDs, six tall figures ascended the massive outdoor stage in the dusky midsummer evening.

[Make some noise!]

It was TeSTAR!

"WAAAAAAAH!!!"

Even as she screamed reflexively, the fan noticed.

Cha Eugene really is missing.

That thought lasted only for a moment. Her eyes were immediately drawn to TeSTAR's outfits.

They wore simple white t-shirts and jeans. It was a casual look, with only two or three members wearing denim jackets.

But in a place like this, simple was always best.

Because water became every other key element.

AAAAAAAAH!!!

With deafening screams as a backdrop, a familiar intro began to play.

It was an exciting, fast beat.

The song was ❬Promise❭, arranged with a slightly more rhythmical feel for the festival.

[Take your STAR

On a day the stars pour down

Kick through the waves and run

To the sky, Run and Fly!]

The song had a refreshing idol flavor and had also become a mainstream hit.

It had a wholesome feel for Waterbomb, but it was still fun, like a university festival where everyone sings along to a song they know.

Moreover, the common keyword 'water' made it a perfect fit for the performance.

"Whoa!!"

"They're so fucking good!"

Primal cheers erupted for TeSTAR's performance. They were already running around barefoot with only specially made anti-slip pads, splashing water everywhere.

Furthermore, the TeSTAR members didn't hold back at all.

[Today is good as it is,

Let's go!]

For the second verse, they even added an ad-lib instrumental section and started running freely around the extended stage.

"Ahyeon!"

Seon Ahyeon dashed to the front of the extended stage. He spun elegantly and nimbly while a spray of water hit him.

"WAAAAH!!"

Another stream of water poured down on Seon Ahyeon as he waved with a slightly awkward and shy expression.

And if you looked to the side...

"Ryu Chungwoo! Ryu Chungwoo!"

"Please, over here!"

*Wow...*

You could practically see the desire swirling around. Even people who didn't seem to be TeSTAR fans were shouting.

Ryu Chungwoo showed no displeasure as he provided fan service, letting water pour onto his neck and back.

*Those insane abs...*

The Kim Raebin fan restrained her head from naturally turning and looked for her favorite member.

[Yes, just like this, fly so far]

He was right in front of her!

Kim Raebin's pale face, adorned with particularly flashy piercings, was right before her eyes.

*K-Kim Raebin.*

The Kim Raebin fan almost lost her mind for a moment.

However, she had to quickly come to her senses.

Kim Raebin, one of the members wearing a jacket, was right next to her. He faced the excited demands of a mischievous couple in the audience.

"Take it off! Take it off!"

The idea was for them to all take off their jackets and have some wild fun together.

*As if Kim Raebin's personality would ever let him do that!*

However...

Kim Raebin, who had been tilting his head, actually took off his jacket!

"......"

And he handed it to the audience.

"UWAAAAAAH?!"

"Oh my god!"

*You crazy bastard!*

The area became a cauldron of madness with the screams of the person who received it and the people around them.

Kim Raebin didn't seem to understand why he was being blasted with water guns instead of thanks, but he seemed to assume it was a sign of gratitude.

As Kim Raebin bowed his head and ran back onto the stage, his white shirt was soaked and clinging to his back.

*F... Fuck.*

Thank you.

She wanted to slap her own mouth for cursing them out for coming to a dangerous event like Waterbomb.

The high-level stimulation was insane.

*You, you had a good body too...!*

The Kim Raebin fan once again admired her decision not to hold up her smartphone.

Someone else will film it. It's more important for me to see it with my own eyes!

[Today will sparkle-!]

After circling the extended stage, TeSTAR performed another exciting dance break before finishing their first song.

Screams that seemed to shake the outdoor venue tore through the air.

[Hello~! It's great to see you!]

It was the same when TeSTAR tried to start a light greeting and talk segment.

[Aigoo, we'd be more grateful if you could avoid our faces so we can sing... Oof.]

Even as he was talking, streams of water mercilessly splashed onto his face.

However, Lee Sejin just grinned, lifted his shirt to wipe his cheek, and laughed it off.

[I get it! But having fun is more important, right~?]

In response, deafening cheers of agreement echoed through the venue.

But amidst that hot and exciting atmosphere, the Kim Raebin fan briefly furrowed her brow.

*Ah, this is why I was a bit hesitant about Waterbomb.*

It could be dangerous because of thoughtless bastards who aim for your eyes, or psychos who mix alcohol with the water.

This was especially true for famous male idols like them, who often attract strange fandoms or haters full of inferiority complexes.

However.

Park Moondae, who already looked like a drenched puppy, made a bombshell declaration in a serious tone.

[Then just... shoot comfortably.]

"......"

[We'll block it ourselves.]

Then, the TeSTAR members all started fumbling in their pockets and taking something out.

They were waterproof goggles.

"Hahaha!"

They didn't wear them when they came out, but now they were putting on their armor!

While the people, hit by the delayed attack, laughed, TeSTAR also laughed foolishly as they firmly put on their goggles.

They were quite stylish fashion goggles.

Without a moment to breathe, one by one, as soon as they finished putting them on, they ran out onto the extended stage!

[We're going right into the next song!]

"WAAAAAAAH!!"

"AAACK!!"

*I picked the best spot!! This is crazy!!*

While the Kim Raebin fan was screaming and grabbing her friend's arm, a barrage of water guns was unleashed on Bae Sejin, who was standing right in front of them.

Jacket or whatever, his white shirt underneath was soaked through.

*Poor thing!*

Even so, she didn't stop her friend from diligently shooting water at Bae Sejin's stomach.

In that atmosphere, where excitement soared with fun and a rush of hormones, the intro began.

[DOONG...!]

"Ah, their latest song!"

It was 'Roll the Dice'.

This one also seemed to be arranged more intensely, and the beat felt a little faster.

Just as people were about to start screaming and cheering along.

[Ah, and... we have a guest.]

"Huh?"

Ryu Chungwoo suddenly said with a smile.

However, he gave no explanation, and there was no time for people to murmur in curiosity.

[It reaches your hand

My tape rewinds again!]

The song began with Park Moondae's high note.

"Waaaah!"

Wearing goggles, Park Moondae now moved his body exhilaratingly, as if he didn't care who shot water at him or where. People cheered and followed his movements.

[The moment my heart pounds

All my senses go Slow]

TeSTAR executed their characteristic and impressive choreography with razor-sharp precision. Otherwise, they moved freely, running around the extended stage with flexible formations and gestures.

Flashes sparked from people's cameras.

The song raced on, the beat pounded, and in that moment of performance, the summer evening air felt like it would explode with heat and water.

It was the final part right before the pre-chorus!

[In search of the certain answer-]

And then.

Suddenly, the music stopped.

"...?"

It stopped at the very moment it should have exploded with climax.

Just when everyone thought it might be a sound system accident.

PING!

Far behind the extended stage, brilliant LED lights lit up the main stage's screen.

And there... someone was standing.

Blond hair. Jeans and a well-fitting white t-shirt.

A seventh figure, wearing goggles.

Cha Eugene.

[......]

Holding a microphone, he made a short sound, as if testing it.

[Ah, ah.]

In that instant.

"WAAAAAAAH!!"

"Eugene!!"

"What?? Who is that?"

In the midst of that fiery atmosphere, fans were shocked in the best way by his sudden appearance. Even those who didn't know him were swept up and screaming.

The band accompaniment returned like a whirlwind.

[!!]

And Cha Eugene started sprinting toward the extended stage.

"C-Crazy!"

Cha Eugene swept his hair back and charged straight ahead. He seemed to pierce through the air, heedless of the wet stage.

Even then, his breath didn't waver in the slightest. He relentlessly belted out his part, driving it through the microphone.

[There's only one choice

Yeah, take it all, one more

Even if you let go of this tightly held hand]

And at the very front.

The moment he stepped into the very center of TeSTAR's formation.

[Let's Start, begin the melee]

The beat dropped.

A spectacular dance break that wasn't in the original song began.

[side side side]

Cha Eugene shot his body forward.

"...!!"

Insane.

Insane, insane!

The water gun assault at the front of the extended stage momentarily ceased.

He had an incredible three-dimensionality, a presence so impressive it was almost violent, and a thrilling explosive power.

*What is this??*

[Just roll the dice]

Cha Eugene performed the dance break in a style close to a solo dance.

He kicked off the ground and used his body's rebound to spring up as if sweeping the floor. The move looked incredibly cool, but it also seemed to put a heavy strain on him.

*Whoa??*

However, there was no time to process it.

Cha Eugene's next move was to casually lift and throw off his shirt amidst the dumbfounded crowd.

His well-toned upper body was instantly revealed.

"......"

"UAAAAAH!!"

It continued like that.

He kept creating moments that made it impossible to look away.

When a slower section began, Cha Eugene deliberately sat down and let the water hit him.

They could hit his face directly.

*In... Insane.*

Without even closing his eyes, he stared intently at each person shooting water guns at him. Then he ripped off his goggles and rose to his feet.

Very naturally.

At the perfect moment the instrumental ended, he rejoined the formation.

And then, the final chorus performance of the driving title track began.

[Pull me in]

Fireworks exploded.

However, he burned so brightly, crossing every line without holding back, that even the loud and spectacular finale seemed like a mere backdrop.

And so, at that same moment.

[C-C-CRAZY Waterbomb Cha Eugene appears]

[Aaack Cha Eugene]

Social media was in an uproar.

There was one reason all of this was possible.

STier Cha Eugene did not have higher absolute stats than TeSTAR's Cha Eugene.

The lack of management and training was that painful.

He just had experience.

He had gone through so many incidents and accidents with his group.

It was an environment with so many sudden changes that his ability to instantly learn and apply choreography had no choice but to increase to an extreme level.

If he didn't improve, he couldn't survive.

[I've learned it all.]

[Gasp!]

That was why he had completely mastered the choreography for TeSTAR's latest title track remix and two other event songs in just four hours.

And...

He had desperately endured, adapting to very uncomfortable and unfamiliar situations to somehow put on an impressive stage.

There was one stat that he himself had developed to its extreme limit in order to seize opportunities.

[Talent : EX (EX)]

If only the opportunity came.

He was someone who would not let go of the spotlight.
