# Chapter 640

The weekday concert started in the evening. As was often the case, the sky above the venue had already darkened past a red sunset.

Beneath that sky, however, the concert hall burned brighter and hotter.

WAAAAAAAH!!

The same was true for the audience members not physically in their seats.

[ㅠㅠAh, I'm so excited]

[Whoa, what is this atmosphere]

These were the online viewers.

Fans once had to settle for crude, low-quality videos secretly filmed by the audience. That was when TeSTAR belonged to a company that didn't provide live streams, but those days were over.

Ever since moving platforms, TeSTAR had made sure to provide a live broadcast for every concert, and even a VR broadcast.

Today was no different.

For this concert, the very first day was the streaming day.

All sorts of people, various netizens who held an interest and fondness for TeSTAR, gathered in their respective communities and on social media to watch the event in real time.

[This is my first time watching a concert]

[I even bought a light stickㅋㅋㅋㅋ Whoa, it's so cool seeing the light stick on the VR screen]

As if to meet those expectations, TeSTAR unleashed a storm of their latest title tracks and lore-based performance songs for the opening.

People went wild every time a high note or a performance exploded onto the stage.

[They're crazy good]

[Whoa damn, this is itㅋㅋ]

[I want to be there in personㅠ]

Just as the atmosphere heated up with admiration and cheers, TeSTAR started their talk. They announced they had "come with a packed setlist," and the mood once again ignited with anticipation.

[Whackㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋ]

[Look at their expressionsㅋㅋㅋ So triumphant]

[It seems like it's really packed with just songs]

[The people who went won't regret the money they spent]

[Are only fans enjoying this? Let the muggles enjoy it too. Muggles know TeSTAR songs too. Leave some concert seats for the general sale instead of just for the fan club pre-saleㅠ]

└So funnyㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋ

However, even the people laughing and chatting fell silent the next moment.

After finishing their talk, TeSTAR waved and suddenly went backstage as a backlight turned on.

A white cloth projected their silhouettes.

[Oh, that]

The flickering shadows of TeSTAR showed them leaning, standing, or sitting on something.

The props were chairs and desks.

[A classroom?]

Warm light filled the interlocking gears on the stage ceiling as they moved.

The sound of a turning film reel could be heard.

WHIRRR, CLICK.

[When I was a child.]

Cha Eugene's narration began.

It was a voice that could only be heard during their early debut days, a voice Cha Eugene had recorded under emotional direction.

His intonation wasn't its usual free-spirited, bubbly self but had a somewhat static feel.

That voice, both childishly immature and calm, spoke the nostalgic English narration.

[When I was a child, I always went home before the sun set.]

[There were no moonlit nights in that daily routine.]

[And now]

[Inside the classroom, I look out the window.]

All the silhouettes looked up at the sky.

They looked up at the darkened sky of the outdoor venue.

[The sun was setting.]

A clear and refreshing sound, like a music box, rang out from the venue's speakers.

A sound rolled through the air like a water droplet.

It was a familiar melody that everyone knew.

♬♪♩♪- ♬♪♬♪- ♪♩-

[It's Magical Boy]

Against the indigo sky where the sunset still lingered, lights rose in all directions. They burst like a pinkish-red and yellow sunset.

From the perspective of the streaming camera capturing the stage from the center, people stared up as if mesmerized.

The white cloth on the stage descended, revealing the figures that had appeared as silhouettes.

They were boys perched on classroom desks.

[The you I'll meet tomorrow

I think about you all day

I dream a dream as blue as day]

After all this time, they were still in their summer uniforms.

They sang with smiles, walked onto the stage in formation, and performed the choreography. Their hair colors and styles had reverted to those of their early debut days.

They had the same faces from when they had just met the public.

[Cha Eugene's pink hair.. right, he was the one with pink hair in the beginning]

It felt strange.

When idols sang a song that was several years old like this, they usually used a remix or a new arrangement.

After all, as time passes, their singing style and way of dancing are bound to change as they grow.

It's impossible to be exactly the same as they were back then, so such stages were usually for reminiscing about shared memories and feeling nostalgic.

However, TeSTAR on the stage right now was exactly the same.

[All that time,

Perhaps it was necessary

The magic to go to the future

That's why I]

They perfectly recreated the singing style and movements from that time.

That exact image appeared, vividly.

The feeling and gestures from back then, with less technique and more honest power instead of ease, reappeared on stage.

TeSTAR used their improved skills to bring the 'ideal' ❬Magical Boy❭ stage to life, recreating the perfect version that had only existed in people's minds.

"......"

[For some reason, I feel nostalgic]

A strange sentiment arose.

It was as if they had returned to that time. People thought of their past selves while watching that stage.

[I used to secretly watch this every day at my academyㅋㅋ]

[When the bubble Seon Ahyeon pictures came out, I stayed up all night editing them. Ah, it was fun]

Even after the ❬Magical Boy❭ stage ended, those feelings didn't fade because the very next stage followed immediately.

Cha Eugene, who had been sitting on a desk, grinned and shot up from his seat.

[High five~]

[They're doing High Five!]

[I wanted to see them perform High Five in their Magical Boy uniforms, and I'm finally seeing it...]

[Whoa, right, I imagined it would feel like this]

[Finallyㅠㅠㅠ]

The bright and cheerful song raced forward emotionally.

Even the slightly childish and strange lyrics, which you couldn't see anymore, were the same.

❬Airplane❭ naturally followed with the same high school concept. A stage with bright, transparent, firework-like colors embroidered the dark sky.

The members briefly disappeared backstage as if chasing a paper airplane, and a red cloth rose in their spot.

Behind it, dancers came out and put on a silhouette performance using shadows.

After those shadows, which seemed to be fighting as if in a war, overlapped, fell, and collapsed, the red cloth dropped to reveal a half-destroyed classroom.

[Better Me!]

[This is that game collaboration]

[Section 127!]

In the darkened atmosphere, TeSTAR burst out as if breaking through a window. They rode the intense and smooth band music as they advanced across the stage.

[Ashes to ashes, dust to dust

But NOT for me, not me

I'm alive, right, like

Legends never die]

They stormed through the setlist with such fervor.

There were almost no cover songs, solo, or unit stages, which were usually included in concerts.

The seven of them filled the stage completely, showing the trajectory they had run so far.

[The day that has finally come

Procession!]

A moment later.

Against the backdrop of a red full moon with a howling wolf's silhouette, another silhouette shooting a longbow split the moon's shadow. It landed on a side stage with the sound of exploding fireworks.

[Ah, I love this so much]

[I think Procession is still my favorite concept..]

[I became a casual fan during this era]

With even VCRs minimized, the online viewers were so immersed in TeSTAR's non-stop chronicle that they couldn't bring themselves to get up from their seats, just like the live audience.

The intense speed prompted a flood of single-word impressions from people unable to contain their overwhelming emotions!

People were thrilled by the joy of another welcome song appearing right after the previous stage ended, enjoying familiar stages and songs with so many others.

[This is insane, isn't it?]

However, there was someone who thought that in a slightly different sense.

~~~

"Wow, that's insane."

"Huh?"

It was the Orbit Stars Entertainment building.

Kwon Heeseung had been dozing in the artist lounge, but he suddenly snapped awake. He saw his own group members, Spacer, completely absorbed by the smart TV, as if it were sucking them in.

A concert was being broadcast on the TV.

"Gasp, a TeSTAR concert!"

"Heeseung, you're awake?"

Kwon Heeseung felt his drowsiness completely vanish.

"Hey, you should've woken me up when it started! How much did I miss?"

The members, staring at the screen as if possessed, waved their hands dismissively, as if they didn't even have time to answer.

In the end, Kwon Heeseung smacked his teammates on the back and looked up the concert setlist himself. Soon, his eyes blinked.

*What is this?*

"Whoa, this lineup is insane."

As if determined to show every single title track, promotional song, and every song of theirs that had ever made it onto the yearly charts, TeSTAR was pouring their souls into the performance.

As a junior in the same industry, it was understandable why they were watching as if overwhelmed by that career and fighting spirit.

Kwon Heeseung looked at the past stages he had missed and licked his lips.

*I missed everything up to ❬Procession❭.*

*That must have been really fun.*

He couldn't go today because of his schedule, but he nodded to himself. He decided he should use the company perk to go and enjoy the concert tomorrow.

A desire sparked within him.

*...We, too, want to put on a performance like this someday.*

However, as he thought about his own concert and looked between the setlist and the screen, Kwon Heeseung suddenly noticed something strange.

"...?"

*Wait a minute.*

"...There are no breaks, are there?"

"I know, right!"

The members finally turned their heads, nodding vigorously and shouting.

"They've only had one VCR so far and have been performing non-stop!"

"It's seriously insane. No, how are they even doing this?"

It was such a vehement reaction that they even forgot to use the senior honorifics.

And Kwon Heeseung was also thrown into confusion.

*Huh...?!*

The VCRs and unit stages inserted between concert performances weren't just for composition and direction.

Singers needed time to change clothes and catch their breath. It was a distribution of time for minimal stamina management and tempo control.

This was especially true for a performance group like TeSTAR, whose choreography was intense and who had to pour out live vocals like crazy.

He himself would need a 10-minute break every 30 minutes to function normally.

Right now, TeSTAR was only taking enough time to barely change their clothes.

And that was only once every four or five songs.

"...? ...!!"

While Kwon Heeseung looked back and forth between the screen and the setlist, one of the group's composer members clicked his tongue and muttered quietly.

"If it were me, I would've collapsed. Seriously."

~~~

"Hah."

I pressed my head against the wall, leaning on both arms.

It felt like my lungs were going to tear.

Someone barely managed to pat my back. They seemed to be asking if I was okay, but they didn't seem to be in a state to open their own mouth either.

I just nodded and lifted my upper body.

"Respirator!"

An oxygen respirator. Honestly, it didn't help much. Still, it was better than nothing.

Someone took off my sweat-soaked durumagi coat. A hand wiped my face, and other hands dressed me in new clothes.

*I'm not a newborn baby.*

But this was all agreed upon beforehand. It wasn't like we didn't know this would happen.

Someone might ask how on earth we planned to tour with this setlist. What if something went wrong?

*It won't.*

We ran through it countless times.

We could endure it even in bad condition. There was no one here who couldn't.

"......"

To conserve stamina, even the most talkative guys in the group were completely silent.

Their shoulders and arms, heated to the point of steaming from the abusive level of exercise, bumped against each other, but no one threw out a single joke.

That, paradoxically, inspired trust.

*Let's go.*

We stood on the stage lift again.

The climax wasn't here yet.

~~~

*Insane. This is insane...*

A dedicated TeSTAR fan gripped their light stick so tightly it felt like it might break.

The light from the stage before them seemed to blur.

From screaming and shouting the fan chants non-stop, their voice had gone completely hoarse in a short time.

Every song the members performed was a stage they were so familiar with and had desperately wanted to see in person. Since it was a relentless onslaught, they were in a constant state of dopamine overload.

*My head is spinning...*

As the fan took a deep breath, someone from the next seat suddenly offered something.

"...?"

It was a small, unopened bottle of water.

A sticker with a phrase like 'Watch the TeSTAR concert and drink deep sea water' was on it, likely from a corporate event giveaway before entry.

"Th-thank you."

The person next to them, who wore a cap, nodded slightly and turned back to the front.

The fan reflexively twisted open the bottle and drank, realizing they had been extremely thirsty. Their vision became clearer.

They were grateful.

*Ah, my snacks!*

Taking the opportunity, the fan tried to offer their own snacks to the person next to them, but the VCR on the stage's main screen had already ended.

"......"

The fan almost waved the water bottle instead of their light stick but managed to switch them just in time. They steadied their trembling hands and stared at TeSTAR appearing on stage.

The smiling members of TeSTAR were in their lightest outfits yet, and they looked like they were having a great time.

[Hello.]

WAAAAAAAH!!

At their clean appearance, friendliness, and pleasant vibe, which made it seem like a lie that they had just been running around and performing like mad, the fan also sent cheers without holding back.

It was TeSTAR, holding microphones for the first time in a whole hour. The fan thought it might be a relaxed moment where they would fill time with some talk.

[You all know these songs, right?]

Yes!!

[Great.]

*I think it's great too...*

Watching Ryu Chungwoo sweep his hair back, the fan shook their light stick slightly.

A similar scene was unfolding throughout the audience, so TeSTAR watched the cute sight with amusement and waved their hands.

Then Park Moondae looked around at the members, smiled, and took the microphone.

[But you know. Actually, we put in just one song you might not know...]

Huhhh?

[It's the song we're about to perform for you right now.]

[It's a new song.]

Huh?

Before the fans could even show a standard reaction, the members on stage lowered their microphones. They scattered and stood in their respective positions.

*What is this...?*

The confusion was brief.

A device like transparent wrapping paper shot up from the stage floor and enveloped the members.

And it formed a protective hemisphere.

"...!"

[We're coming now.]

A soft melody began to flow.

The transparent hemispheres, each carrying a TeSTAR member on a piece of the stage floor, began to rise. They moved through the air like drones or hot air balloons.

They flew over the standing section and towards here.

"...!"

They were coming towards the audience seats.
