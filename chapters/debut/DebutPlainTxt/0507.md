# Chapter 507

[Re-Update Complete!]

[First Adjustment]

A pop-up appeared, similar to the one I had seen before.

Then another one followed.

[Applying '■■■ (ver.2.1 Beta)']

[Complete!]

It was a System Update completion notice.

"......"

A question surfaced in my mind.

*Why?*

I thought the last update finished early because STier Cha Eugene's condition recovered.

This time, nothing was resolved. Ryu Chungwoo never wavered from his position of not wanting his memories back.

*Did it finish because we performed on stage together? But this wasn't our first stage, so on what basis was it completed early...*

*Wait.*

I took a deep breath.

*My thoughts are running wild. I can't let them get cluttered.*

I finally regained my composure and reached a rational conclusion.

An early completion before the deadline?

*There was no such thing.*

There was no deadline in the first place. Unlike before, the System gave no explanation like 'D-30 until completion'.

This wasn't a case of absorbing a fragment to update to a higher version. Since I wasn't using it, the system was probably just recalibrating itself.

*It's not ver.3, it's ver.2.1.*

So this was just completed because it was time. The System finished on its own.

DING.

As if responding to my conclusion, another pop-up appeared.

[Would you like to 'Preview' 'For Business Use'?]

*...Preview.*

My mind filled with questions again.

*If it's a Preview, does that mean the System isn't restarting?*

The previous pop-up mentioned a 'First Adjustment'. Maybe it would gauge my satisfaction through this 'Preview' and then update the System again if needed.

What would happen to Ryu Chungwoo's memories? Would it return them as a test? Or would it not, since this was just a Preview and not a full reactivation?

What was the penalty if I held out and didn't press this either? And what was the risk of the System acting on its own again?

Which was more likely? What standard should I use to judge...

Ah, damn it.

I slammed my head against the bed's headboard.

THUD.

*Fuck, fuck...!*

It was so frustrating that not a single conclusion was clear.

How many times did I have to go through this bullshit? Why was this kind of thing necessary for a so-called Update?

"A-Are you okay, hyung?"

"......"

"If you're dizzy or have a headache, maybe an ice pack..."

"...I'm fine."

I slowly lifted my head.

My roommate, Kim Raebin, looked at me with a worried face, as if he was about to break into a cold sweat. He probably thought I had accidentally hit my head.

That guy likely didn't get to rest properly while preparing for the last stage.

"Are you getting enough rest?"

"Yes! I'm following the instructions to recuperate until the next shoot."

"Good."

"Hyung, is your head really okay...?"

*Yeah. It feels like it's about to shatter.*

Though not because I hit it on the bed.

"That part's fine."

I stared at the pop-up again before finally speaking.

"I have something to say. Let's get all the other members, including you, to gather in the living room."

Delaying and hiding it any longer was pointless.

***

One hour after Park Moondae's summons, the occupants of Seon Ahyeon and Lee Sejin's room were talking quietly in the TeSTAR dorm.

Their conversation had a single topic.

It was the story Park Moondae had shared after seeing the pop-up and calling them to the living room.

[...The Update is complete.]

He didn't hide anything, sharing the details he received almost exactly as they were. Park Moondae was surprisingly kind, even using examples and analogies to explain things to those who hadn't experienced the System firsthand.

But at the same time, he looked utterly exhausted.

[I honestly don't know what will happen if I press this... but I'll think about it more and look into it, so don't worry too much.]

That was why Seon Ahyeon was here, confiding his worries to a friend of the same age whom he had trusted for several years.

Seon Ahyeon sat on the bed with his long legs drawn together and muttered anxiously.

"I'm worried about Moondae."

"......"

"I think... he's pushing himself too hard..."

Lee Sejin had no intention of refuting those words. He had thought something similar as soon as he heard Park Moondae's briefing.

*He's going to be in real trouble if he keeps this up.*

Of course, his first concern was how the current Ryu Chungwoo, who seemed like a different person after losing his memories, would react. Everyone, even Park Moondae, probably felt the same.

However, it was absurd to think they wouldn't notice something was off with Park Moondae. The light was almost gone from his eyes.

*It's not like this is the first or second time he's overdone it.*

Lee Sejin frowned for a moment. A bitter taste filled his mouth.

Normally, Lee Sejin would have said something smooth and reassuring here. He believed that frankly admitting his own anxiety would only make the situation worse.

But this time was different.

"...Hmm, yeah. Anyone can see he's pushing himself too hard."

A solution was needed. Although Seon Ahyeon could be clumsy in interpersonal relationships, he was a serious and meticulous person.

*He's worth discussing this with.*

Besides, Seon Ahyeon wasn't the type to be placated by insincere reassurances.

So Lee Sejin spoke the truth bluntly.

"Chungwoo-hyung doesn't seem to want to remember. So imagine how much pressure he's under now. Moondae is the only one among us who can see that Status Window or whatever it is."

"...Moondae."

Seon Ahyeon bit his lip.

"Th-Then, if Chungwoo-hyung refuses until the very end... will Moondae not press it?"

Lee Sejin already knew the answer.

"No. He'll do it."

If he judged it to be the rational choice, Park Moondae was the type to act, regardless of how much it hurt.

Perhaps that was how TeSTAR had pushed forward with unstoppable momentum since their debut, despite so many controversies and incidents.

However, Lee Sejin also knew the aftereffects that followed.

"But he'll have a really hard time after doing it."

"......"

"Moondae is a bit... he tries to take responsibility for everything."

He was the type to think that this current situation was also his fault.

*If things go wrong, the stress could really make something happen to him.*

Lee Sejin still remembered the chaos during the Summer Package filming in their early debut days, how Park Moondae had stubbornly pushed through while practically having a trauma-induced fit.

He clenched and unclenched his fist.

*...Ah, how did it come to this?*

Succeeding as an idol was like a dream. For a realist like Lee Sejin, it was baffling that things that literally only happen in dreams kept occurring. He never thought he'd meet a friend he cared about this much, only for these ridiculous things to happen to that friend.

Just as Lee Sejin fell silent, trying to think of a solution, Seon Ahyeon spoke first.

"Hey, let's... talk about it more."

"Huh?"

Seon Ahyeon's face was resolute.

"Let's talk to the other members, too... and let's talk with Chungwoo-hyung again. We haven't had time because we were practicing for the stage. I think we've barely talked because we were being so careful... because of what happened with Eugene..."

The words struck Lee Sejin.

*That's right.*

They had only tried not to upset Ryu Chungwoo. They hadn't thought to have a deep conversation or try to persuade him. They had unconsciously ruled it out after seeing how badly things went with Cha Eugene.

Seon Ahyeon paused in thought, then continued.

"The current Chungwoo-hyung, he's good to us, but he must be anxious. That might be why he doesn't want to find his memories... I think... it's right to try and have a deeper conversation."

"......"

Lee Sejin listened intently as Seon Ahyeon's words started to come a little faster.

"Because we're not doing that now, I think it's harder for Moondae, too. If there's no conclusion, no one can help him..."

"You're right."

Lee Sejin agreed immediately and stood up. He knew his friend wouldn't be offended if he suddenly ended the conversation.

"Let's talk, right now."

"......"

Seon Ahyeon also nodded and got up.

Lee Sejin and Seon Ahyeon left the room together.

And again, one hour later.

"Park Moondae."

The two of them called out to Park Moondae, who sat on his bed as still as a mouse with his eyes closed.

When Park Moondae opened his eyes, he realized his roommate was not in the room.

He then found his roommate outside the room. To be precise, all the members except for himself were there.

"What the..."

"We had a talk, and we have something to tell you," Lee Sejin said like he was making a declaration.

He thought,*Do whatever you're comfortable with' won't work.*

They weren't going to take responsibility for the result, were they? That was no different from just pushing the decision and responsibility onto him to make themselves feel better.

Therefore.

"Press the Preview now."

The decision had to be made by them.

***

"What?"

I doubted my ears.

"Do you know what you're saying right now?"

"Of course."

Lee Sejin was nonchalant for someone who had just barged into another person's room with all the members. The other members who came with him were the same.

"This is the result of a discussion among the members, decided by a majority vote."

"......"

"Hey, didn't you say we'd all use that company System thing together? We did that when we were making the album last time."

Lee Sejin shrugged.

"So just surrender to the majority vote and press it~"

It was absurd. What was even more ridiculous was that a few of them were nodding in agreement.

Moreover, the story didn't end there.

"We've already talked it over with Chungwoo-hyung here."

"......"

I reflexively turned my head.

STier Ryu Chungwoo, standing in the back, had a rather calm expression. He didn't seem to harbor any resentment from being pressured.

"I heard that if we keep delaying, something dangerous might happen, and that there's a high chance my memories won't come back from this."

"......"

"I'll trust you. I know you had no reason to lie to me. You could have just returned my memories without any explanation, but you didn't."

The guy gave a faint smile.

"...I felt it while preparing for the stage, but you all seem like trustworthy teammates."

"Hyung..."

Reactions came even from the surrounding members. It was a statement that was actually quite moving.

To hear directly from someone who had become a stranger that he trusted us after just a few weeks was enough to sway me for a moment.

But at the same time, I felt a strange sensation. A faint sense of unease.

*What is it?*

That nagging feeling passed through my mind like a fleeting thought before it could take shape.

Right now, a more certain situation was unfolding before my eyes.

Everyone had reached an agreement and informed me of it.

To press 'Preview'.

"......"

The funny thing was, my mind suddenly became calm, as if I was relieved.

*Am I crazy?*

It's always better to have the choice. So what the hell was I feeling relieved about after being told it was decided by a majority vote?

However, I couldn't deny that my head felt lighter.

*...Yeah. It is less of a burden.*

I finally summoned the pop-up.

"Then I'll press it."

"Okay."

[Would you like to 'Preview' 'For Business Use'?]

I pressed 'Yes'.

Immediately, the pop-up began to change rapidly, a flashing hologram as its color returned.

[Initiating 'Preview']

I considered a few possibilities. The System could restart without Ryu Chungwoo's memories returning, the Survey function could activate, or, in a slim chance, Ryu Chungwoo's memories could return just like that.

But none of them were correct.

"...! Ugh,"

"Raebin?"

I watched the members stare with dumbfounded faces at the collapsing Kim Raebin.

At the same time, I realized.

Preview meant the next target.

The current target, Ryu Chungwoo, was put on hold.
