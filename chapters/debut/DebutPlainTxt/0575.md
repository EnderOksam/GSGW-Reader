# Chapter 575

Keundal had disappeared.

Ryu Gunwoo was missing. The phone call...

[Sir! Please calm down. We can't talk unless you're calm.]

The voice from the smartphone rang in my head. It told me to be calm, just as bad news always did.

"Yes."

[So you weren't aware he was absent from work.]

"Yes."

[When was the last time you contacted your younger brother?]

I reflexively recalled my last contact with Keundal. He had called after the joint Singapore concert featuring TeSTAR aired, saying he enjoyed watching it.

[The ending was really cool.]

It was a greeting to say he had seen me in the ending and that he was also doing well.

That was...

"Three days ago."

Urgency entered the voice on the phone.

[Did your brother mention anything unusual at the time? Like a specific place he wanted to go, or that he wanted to see the sun... anything like that?]

"......"

There was nothing. He just asked about TeSTAR Park Moondae's recent activities and replied that he was doing well.

[Of course I'm doing well.]

That was all. He didn't talk about himself at all. Not a single thing.

Damn it.

"...No."

A short, heavy silence settled. The voice returned, trying to soothe me while suppressing its own impatience.

[...Sir, your brother has been absent without leave for five days now.]

Five days.

[We tracked his phone's location today and found the smartphone. We also received a witness report that he went to a mountain yesterday, so we're in the middle of a search.]

[We're a little past the stage of thinking, 'He's a grown man, what could possibly happen?' right now.]

"......"

[You said you contacted your brother three days ago, right? That means he contacted you while he was already absent from work.]

*He was already skipping work then. In this cold... he went into the mountains alone, but why?*

[Sir, you're the only one with a record of contact from that time.]

"......"

[You need to think calmly right now. The temperature drops as night falls, making the search more difficult. The last sighting was a day ago, so finding him quickly is the best course of action.]

I had to figure out where he was headed and why.

*...Right now.*

I lifted my head. I saw a huge electronic wall clock, its red LED numbers displayed on a black background.

"......"

It was 5:04 PM. In early February, during a winter cold snap, the sun was about to set.

And it was 56 minutes before the TeSTAR concert.

Tick.

Time was ticking away.

[You're the only family member saved in this phone right now.]

[The mountain hasn't been specified. We need to narrow down the area where Ryu Gunwoo might have been heading to speed up the search.]

Tock.

So, I had to say something within the 56 minutes, no, 55 minutes now, remaining until the concert.

[Or is there anyone else he's close to, like family? Anyone he met recently?]

Tick.

"As far as I know, there's no one."

Keundal had no direct biological family. Not as Park Moondae, and not as Ryu Gunwoo. Besides, after living as a System for so long, he had little interest in forming close relationships.

That meant I was the closest person to him. If anyone could find a hint in his words or guess his thought process, it would be me.

I had to find a hint.

[Then... Sir, for now, just try to tell us anything you can remember from that conversation.]

However.

Tock.

"I think he was calmer than usual."

[And?]

Nothing. That was it. I hadn't heard a single word from him about a mountain.

"......"

*Why don't I know anything? Why?*

I wasn't too late this time. This wasn't like before, when I had only checked the missed call hours after everything was over. I thought I had answered the phone in time, but if nothing changed even when I did...

*No.*

The smartphone hit my chin. I realized my hand was shaking.

*Fuck.*

My brain just wouldn't work. I tried to find a clue about what Keundal was thinking or where he went, but my brain refused to cooperate at the very moment I needed it most.

*You're good at this shit, damn it. Hey. There's no time, you fucking bastard.*

Still, it didn't move. The sound of my heart pounding shot up to my head. My brain's efficiency hit rock bottom. It wouldn't budge.

Tick.

However, time waits for no one.

*So... what if I'm late again? What if my brain only starts thinking after the concert has already begun?*

Tock.

Could I find time to make a call during the performance? No, there wouldn't be a moment to spare. A proper call was impossible during the five minutes spent changing costumes, rehydrating, and using an oxygen mask. There wasn't even a split second to grab the phone.

Then... I...

Tick.

I...

[Sir? Calm down.]

"Hup."

I took a short, sharp breath.

[Now, breathe out. Listen. We can continue the search at night, so don't be too pessimistic...]

[This is the Incheon Ganghwa Fire Station 119 Mountain Rescue Team. If you search for the address, it will show you the way, so you can continue talking on your way here. Calmly, steadily.]

"......"

The rescuer explained the directions as if it were the most natural thing in the world. He assumed I was too flustered to even ask for the address. He obviously expected me to run out, grab a taxi, and head to the search site the moment I heard.

However.

*I... I have a concert to perform.*

TeSTAR had poured a regular album's worth of ideas and investment into this tour. This was a precarious time that could determine whether the team's trajectory would decline or rise. This was the very first performance, the one that would set the impression for the entire tour.

The audience was already seated outside this waiting room, filled with people looking forward to the show.

Moreover, this concert emphasized each member's individual character, with a story that organically connected their solo stages.

*But if I'm absent... what about my solo stage? The formations? How on earth would they handle my parts? Who would fill that void?*

There had been no discussion, no preparation. The quality of the performance would be shattered. The tour's reviews would be ruined, and so would the fans' perception of TeSTAR.

[Wow, Gommeo who performed even with a broken bone..]

[He's totally lost his original passion, hasn't he]

[I'm so disappointed. Really, a concert of all things... What must the team atmosphere be like for this to be possible?]

If only I had at least three days, no, even just one day...

I looked down at my hands. My tightly clenched knuckles had turned white. Not even a cold sweat broke out.

I opened my mouth.

"I'm... in the middle of work right now."

[Excuse me?]

He reacted with disbelief, certain he had misheard. Of course. Who would say such bullshit like 'it's hard to talk because I'm working' when their family member is missing?

Someone who couldn't answer that call, who would feel like shit every year on that day, and who would have dreamt of it every night.

Me. Just like I did.

"......"

Suddenly, a sense of reality returned.

*Am I... really on a call about my family member possibly being dead, while worrying about my performance goals? What... is this right now?*

"Moondae-yah...!"

*However, if I think rationally...*

"Park Moondae!"

"...!"

A hand gripped both my shoulders, and I lifted my head. It was Seon Ahyeon. The spots he gripped felt strangely hot, but I realized it was because I was deathly pale, as if drained of blood.

It was no wonder my head wasn't working in this state.

"......"

Seon Ahyeon seemed to scrutinize my complexion, then quickly let go, but a firm voice followed.

"Moondae, I think he's sick."

"......"

The surroundings grew noisy. Only then did I tear my focus from the smartphone and look around. It was time to get on standby, but I was still rooted to the spot. The others had rushed over.

All eyes gathered on the smartphone in my hand.

"Who's on the phone?"

I didn't even need to answer Seon Ahyeon's question. A hesitant voice was already coming from the smartphone.

[...Yes. Well, we'll assume the family member is busy for now and continue with the search.]

"...Search?"

The atmosphere shifted at the unusual word.

"Excuse me, but who are you searching for right now?"

[Excuse me?]

I watched the members of this group snatch my smartphone and start talking. Seeing their faces just before the concert, my breathing and my mind finally began to calm down.

"......"

As I broke through the panic, a clear answer formed in my mind. It was obvious.

*There's nothing I can do anyway.*

Whether it was trauma or something else, my brain wasn't working properly. There was no way I could help find Keundal, Ryu Gunwoo, by joining the search in this state. However, not doing the concert would definitely fuck things up.

The answer was already there, wasn't it?

"...Yes. Thank you."

Just then, the members finished their conversation and pulled their faces away from my smartphone. Judging by their grim expressions, they had heard the whole explanation.

"......"

I lifted my head. 38 minutes until the concert... no, 37 minutes now. It was time to hang up and get ready, or there would be a problem with the opening.

*Let's hang up and get ready.*

I had to say it, but my damn mouth wouldn't open.

*Say it.*

If I didn't, these guys would have to. I couldn't push that onto them. Biting my tongue, I managed to open my mouth again.

"...So."

Before I could even finish, Ryu Chungwoo spoke first.

"Moondae-yah, go."

"...!"

"If you take a call taxi, you'll get there in two hours. Talk on the way, quietly and without being disturbed."

The words, telling me to throw away half the concert's duration on travel, came out of his mouth so casually.

"That's..."

I was about to retort and ask if that even made sense.

"No, I'm not going to listen to your judgment, so just do as I say."

"......"

"Got it? Go and do everything you can."

Like a stupid bastard, I asked back.

"The concert."

"We'll handle it."

"But..."

Ryu Chungwoo looked at me, then pulled me to my feet. He held out the smartphone, the call still connected.

"Don't ask. I have no intention of persuading you."

"......"

"Don't waste time. Go now."

I took the smartphone he held out. No rebuttal came out.

*No.*

I turned my gaze to the other members' faces. I thought guys like Lee Sejin or Cha Eugene would surely offer a rational counterargument. At the very least, I thought it would show on their faces. I thought that would help me open my damn mouth.

"......"

However, no one stopped me. In silent cooperation, they moved me.

"Please be quick."

In an instant, I was inside a call taxi.

"Don't, worry about anything else."

The door closed on Seon Ahyeon's final words.

Thud.

And just like that, I was moving, sitting in the back of an unfamiliar car.

"......"

Thirty minutes before the concert, the singer had left the venue. It was an act so crazy it didn't feel real. It was only right to feel more anxious. I had ruined the first day of a concert that the group's future depended on.

The audience... would surely be disappointed.

"......"

My hands trembled, but what's funny is, I was no longer in that god-awful state of panic. As if by a lie, blood started flowing to my brain again.

*Fuck.*

It was already too late to go back to the concert. There was no more room for hesitation. I could pour all my focus into Keundal's well-being.

This time, I might not be too late.

Thinking that, funnily enough, my head started working even amidst this extreme anxiety.

"Ha."

It was absurd how my brain sweetly accepted the small bit of peace that returned even when the situation was so fucked. I scoffed at my own brain and closed my eyes. Then I called the rescuer from before and asked for a more detailed explanation.

Let's think again. A place Keundal would go. His whereabouts for the past few days.

He had been absent from work without leave for five days. It seemed his workplace finally filed a report this morning. Missing person reports for adult men aren't often taken seriously, but since he was a civil servant, it was probably easier.

As a result of tracking his smartphone, it was found at a supermarket on Ganghwa Island. Ryu Gunwoo had left a message there yesterday, saying he was 'on his way to the mountain', and then his trail went cold.

*Because they don't know which mountain it is, the search is slow... is that it?*

Ganghwa Island had numerous mountains suitable for hiking. If he had been in the mountains for two days in winter, he had to be found quickly. At worst, he could freeze to death.

However, I knew the rescuer had also worried with a different nuance. If he had suddenly climbed a winter mountain in this weather, while absent from work and leaving his smartphone behind... he might not have intended to come down in the first place.

*No.*

I gripped the smartphone with both hands.

*...He wouldn't have done that.*

He had no reason to. He was the one who had even traveled to Sokcho, saying he would try to find what he wanted to do, but in that moment, his recent messages suddenly came to mind.

[Thank you.]

[Of course, I'll do well.]

"......"

*That calmness of his, could it have stemmed from depression?*

I couldn't know. We hadn't had such a deep conversation recently.

However.

*If I start getting swayed by this kind of logic, it's over.*

It was a line of thought that was of no help in a deduction aimed at his survival. I gritted my teeth and forced the thought from my mind. I returned to the keywords.

His purpose.

*Why a mountain on Ganghwa Island, of all places?*

There were plenty of similar mountains in Seoul. There was no reason to go all the way to Incheon...

"......"

This body, Park Moondae, was from Incheon. Feeling like I had found a connection somewhere, I pushed my brain harder.

*Are there any other connections? Ganghwa Island, Ganghwa Island...*

And then, it suddenly came to me.

[Ah, um. It is Ganghwa Island...]

"...!"

I had been to Ganghwa Island with him. I remembered the reason clearly.

[They're in the sea. He said he'd wanted to do that for a while...]

"......"

I immediately opened a map app and checked the geography of Ganghwa Island. Then I called the rescuer.

"I've found a place you should search first."

[You have?]

"Mount Mani."

I named a mountain.

"The southern side of that mountain... the direction where the sea is clearly visible and close."

From that mountain, you should be able to see the waters off Ganghwa Island where he said he scattered his parents' ashes.
