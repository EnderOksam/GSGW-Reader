# Chapter 546

VTIC's tour was a great success.

[Thanks LA!]

[WAAAAAH!!]

Despite one member's dating scandal, almost no international fans canceled their tickets for the prescheduled concerts.

Since it was not a media-related activity, no one in Korea made a fuss about it.

LeTi's decision to focus on the tour made perfect sense.

Thanks to that, the member who caused the trouble looked healthier, which was amusing. The other VTIC members also seemed a bit relieved.

"Today was great~"

"I know, right."

They probably hadn't grasped the reality yet. If their next domestic comeback failed, they would be finished.

But one guy had experienced that reality dozens of times.

[Isn't today supposed to be a day off?]

He was practicing like a dog.

He claimed there was a problem with the concert production.

[Our feet aren't in sync.]

With that one comment, he sucked the life out of everyone and kept them from going home.

*So much for restarting.*

*He still has that greed for the stage.*

The VTIC members followed his lead without a single complaint. It was quite a spectacle. Even the one who caused the dating scandal watched him cautiously, not daring to slack off.

Meanwhile, two actual VTIC members amusingly enjoyed themselves.

"Oh! Our feet are perfectly in sync!"

"Doesn't seeing this just make you feel good?"

*So those are the kinds of guys who get full-time positions.*I inwardly nodded.

Cheongryeo watched the two with the eyes of an interviewer grading new recruits. He answered my unspoken question.

"The concert is on break, not us."

It was a standard line.

Come to think of it, I don't think I ever really rested mid-tour either.

*We were usually busy filming our own content.*

I sometimes wanted to go home, but it was generally a good experience. I'll admit it was a bit tiring.

Running around with guys I had spent years with and was comfortable with felt like a trip and was quite fun...

[......]

"Your attitude is strange."

[No.]

I decided not to get needlessly sentimental about something with no immediate solution.

[Watching others work just reminded me of what I used to do.]

[Don't mind me.]

"......"

Cheongryeo gave a barely perceptible nod and continued practicing as if nothing had happened.

During a break in the choreography practice, however, the guy stopped drinking his water and asked a question.

"If I restart."

Hm?

"What happens to you?"

Let's see.

He seemed to wonder if I would return to my original body instead of disappearing.

[I don't know.]

I answered honestly.

[That's what I was worried about. Because I don't know what will happen.]

"Hmm."

Cheongryeo neatly put away the water bottle he was drinking from.

After about three months with this guy, his domineering attitude when things didn't go his way had appeared much less frequently.

...Hmm, not that it was completely gone.

I recalled a conversation from a few days ago.

[Did T-Holic go to jail?]

[That doesn't seem like a question a Help who's supposed to tell me the future should be asking.]

[......]

[What do you think?]

[Yeah. In the future I knew, T-Holic ends up under the same roof as you guys.]

Being this blunt was actually more comfortable.

In that sense, I anticipated the next part of the conversation.

*Okay, I told him his restart is dangerous for me. Now he'll probably say something like, 'Then perform your role better'...*

"Certainly, an incalculable risk is troublesome to handle..."

Hm?

I stopped my prediction.

Cheongryeo muttered as he returned the water bottle to its original spot.

"That's true."

Oh.

*This looks like something I can exploit.*

[Right.]

[Besides, if Help blows up, it's a loss for you too. Wouldn't it be better to look into it a bit before you act?]

Cheongryeo smirked.

"You're still trying to persuade me not to restart."

Damn it.

If I had hands and could just smack this guy on the head once, I'd willingly give up this quarter's settlement amount.

"However..."

Cheongryeo continued as he returned to his position.

"I suppose spending about 359 days on information gathering would be fine."

[...!]

"Just as you said."

I realized I had successfully exploited him.

In the end, Cheongryeo himself had admitted that he didn't need to restart until he saw the results of the next album.

*This guy, always trying to get the upper hand!*

[Thanks. For your help.]

[ㅁ_ㅁb]

The guy smiled faintly. The atmosphere was pretty good...

"For Help to be receiving help, perhaps you should reflect on your own reason for existence."

[I know you're not losing out on anything, you bastard.]

A few more words were exchanged, but Cheongryeo didn't take back what he said.

Hmm. I mentally rubbed my chin.

*So this really works.*

Becoming a comfortable conversation partner, that is.

We had reached a point where he would consider my situation, as long as he didn't lose out on either option.

So this was how I reaped the rewards of acting like a suck-up for three months.

*If things keep going like this, I might even be able to suggest getting a dog...*

No, that seemed impossible. I discarded that false hope.

*I need to find a clue before that.*

"Let's get to it!"

"Alright."

VTIC's practice finally ended late that afternoon, and Cheongryeo returned to his hotel.

[What are you going to do now?]

"......"

The guy sat down in a chair by the table and briefly closed his eyes.

"......"

He was ignoring the bed?

In any case, it had nothing to do with me. I couldn't sleep at all right now.

It wasn't a pleasant feeling, but considering the danger of losing consciousness in this situation, this was actually safer.

I decided to make good use of this free time.

*Time to dig deeper into the System's core.*

Thinking there might be a clue, I decided to take this opportunity to delve deeper into the System.

Thump.

If there had been a sound, that's what it would have been.

I pushed through the pressure and advanced smoothly.

*Hmm.*

Come to think of it, the System's unique pressure had stopped feeling so unpleasant at some point.

It was like sitting in a massage chair. Your body is pressed down, but it feels refreshing and stable.

*This is strange.*

Much more comfortably than before, I sank into the System as if surrendering my body to a current.

Deeper.

Deeper.

Deeper still.

[......]

This was the first time I had come this deep.

I was enduring the grogginess in my head, like being crushed under a thick blanket.

*Ah.*

I saw something.

In the deep interior, a place I had never reached before, there was a single, solitary shining object.

*Hm?*

It was... a terminal for the Status Window.

It was one that I had never made.

"......"

*Strange.*

*Weren't these kinds of terminals something Keundal started making to implement the Status Window?*

*This is the System from before Keundal existed, so there shouldn't be any terminals at all.*

Why was something like this left here?

I reached out and, as carefully as possible, made contact with the terminal.

Just like with the other terminals, a Status Window materialized.

The problem was, its contents were familiar.

[Reboot Initiated]

[Learning in progress.]

"...!!"

Reboot.

It was the same pop-up message I had seen before I collected all the System fragments and ended up here.

However, the explanation attached below it was something that hadn't been there before.

*Learning?*

Just then.

[Help.]

A voice called out, as if from above the surface of water.

Ah, damn it. I held back a groan.

*Just wait a bit.*

*This is a clue I barely managed to find.*

*I want to take my time and think about why this is here and what role it plays...*

[Help.]

Persistent.

I suppressed a sigh and pulled my hand away from the terminal.

*If I don't answer, he might get the wrong idea.*

I had memorized the terminal's location, so coming back would be easy.

I quickly escaped the pressure and headed for the chat terminal on the System's surface.

Then I spat out a reply.

[What?]

"......"

Hey, why call me and then say nothing?

Strangely, Cheongryeo was already awake and dressed to go out.

[Do you need me to pull up someone's profile?]

"It's not that."

But instead of heading out, the guy walked over to the refrigerator in his hotel room.

From inside, he took out a can.

A can of beer.

"...??"

"You said you wanted to drink."

He returned to the table and slowly took a sip.

His expression wasn't great, suggesting he didn't particularly like beer. The fact that he specifically mentioned my preference... it seemed this was his version of a considerate gesture.

"Geez..."

I let out a small laugh.

Sorry, but the taste isn't shared.

Watching someone else drink gave me a level of satisfaction similar to playing a VR game.

It's funny to say that when it's just scratching an itch, though.

[Thanks.]

The attempt was commendable. I decided to just think of it as non-alcoholic beer.

[But why the sudden beer?]

The guy answered curtly.

"A reward."

*...?*

[A reward for what?]

"......"

Instead of answering, Cheongryeo averted his gaze.

[!Unauthorized Error Occurred]

[Do you want to remove the Status Window?]

He looked at that very pop-up.

*Wait.*

[What are you thinking right now]

"I thought about it while I was resting."

Before I could even finish my sentence, the guy started muttering.

"If it's hard to predict what will happen, getting rid of it is the most stable choice."

"......"

"Even knowing that, it's not rational for me to choose the option of delaying the restart instead of eliminating you, is it?"

"......"

Cheongryeo tapped the beer can with his finger.

"In fact, this whole act is strange. I could just delete it, but the fact that I'm trying to have a conversation and give a reward before proceeding."

The guy tilted his head.

"It's a worthless endeavor."

"......"

Cheongryeo moved the beer away from the table.

He spoke with emotionless eyes.

"Being influenced like this in just 90 days is not acceptable."

[Wait a minute.]

*I'm screwed.*

This bastard realized he had started to act leniently toward me.

And it seemed he didn't... evaluate that development favorably.

*Fuck.*

If I had a spine, a chill would have run down it. I gritted my teeth.

*I have to be rational.*

I started with my usefulness.

[Not only can I add new characteristics, but I can also prepare ways to quickly raise your stats.]

[Besides, the real-time display of status effect durations is for you]

"Unnecessary. My plan is already perfect."

Cheongryeo interlaced his fingers and smiled serenely.

"I have all the information I need. All that's left is to restart and find the perfect combination."

*You crazy bastard.*

"I just need to try a few more times. It's not even difficult. The ability to start over like that..."

Cheongryeo stared at the pop-up without moving.

"...is already mine."

Powerful conviction.

For a moment, I froze at the terminal.

"......"

In that moment, Cheongryeo took his hand off the beer and raised it to the pop-up.

"Well then."

No.

[I'll just shut up, so don't press it.]

[You're agitated right now. You're making an emotional choice]

"Enough."

And Cheongryeo.

TAP!

He lightly touched the remove button.

In that instant.

[Status Window Removal Complete]

I was torn away, ripped apart.

*Oh, no.*

That was the most terrifying thing.

[Moondae-hyungnim, maybe he got sucked into this System thing and became a part of it? Like a component!]

I gasped for breath and pushed myself up.

A wave of nausea twisted my nerves, and pressure swirled all around me.

It felt like suffering from a deadly hangover, or like my insides were twisted and my semicircular canals were shattered.

*Damn it...*

I pressed my hand to my temple... wait.

"......"

I pulled my hand away from my temple.

There was a sense of touch.

When I lifted my hand in front of my eyes, I could see it.

"......"

What is going on.

*Am I back?*

*Did that damn bastard Cheongryeo pressing 'Yes' on the Status Window deletion pop-up turn out to be the right answer?*

I staggered to my feet. I was about to check how much time had passed and where I was.

But as soon as I lifted my head, I realized.

This wasn't JSA.

"...!"

My surroundings were shrouded in the dead of night.

It wasn't even... Korea.

This was LA.

It was so familiar that I recognized it immediately. It was the place I had just been looking at through that bastard Cheongryeo's Status Window.

This concert venue was the exact place where VTIC held their tour concert.

*Damn it.*

This place, originally a sports stadium, had been redecorated with VTIC-related promotional tents and decorations, but the crowds that should have been enjoying it were already almost gone.

*What is this now.*

This was absurd.

I limped, nearly falling over as I tried to lean against a wall.

It was then.

Someone tapped my shoulder.

"......"

*Right now, am I in a state where other people can see me?*

I immediately turned my head.

A guy with his hands up was visible under a dim streetlight.

"You look like you could use the help of law enforcement."

"......"

With a dazed expression, I looked at the figure standing across from me.

"I do know the embassy's number."

It was a person who was still just a trainee on this side.
