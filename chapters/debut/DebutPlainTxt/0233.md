# Chapter 233

Kim Raebin walked down the corridor of the hospital room, holding a box in one hand.

His other arm was still in a cast and could not be moved freely, so he had to be careful.

Kim Raebin kept his mouth shut.

*I can't let any misfortune happen, such as missing this box!*

This was because quite a bit of time had passed since the news of the shocking car accident.

When the title of 'recovering from surgery in a coma' lasted for more than ten days, they left in search of other prey.

It was like the entertainment industry where incidents happened every other day.

However, the internet was still in chaos with all sorts of pessimistic speculation articles, columns, fan concerns, and anger swirling around.

*I'm glad Cha Eugene is stupid.*

Kim Raebin briefly envied Cha Eugene, then quietly moved on.

"Are the fans sending you letters? They should use birds. [Cool. I guess every country has a similar story. Anyway, it's good that they want hyung to recover, right?]."

His middle opinion was that Park Moondae might be listening, but the members did not stop Cha Eugene because it was true that it strangely gave hope.

"It seems that the other hyungs have already come and gone."

"...E-Earlier, they went to work."

"Alright."

Kim Raebin nodded and waited for Cha Eugene's turn to end.

It was because what he brought was very important news, so he couldn't be bitten by Cha Eugene's voice.

And seeing Cha Eugene talking for a long time, he resisted the desire to shout, 'Don't waste your visit time with repeated stories!'

*Because we have to be quiet in the hospital room!*

Kim Raebin told himself that he would show a well-mannered appearance in front of the great Moondae-hyung.

And in the end, he was able to quietly take over the baton without quarreling with Cha Eugene.

"Hyung, how are you..."

Of course, there was no answer from Park Moondae, who was lying down.

For some reason, Kim Raebin felt like he was going to cry. It might have been because it reminded him of his grandmother's time, but he held back anyway.

"I-I have something prepared for you."

Trying to stay calm, he lifted the box and opened it.

The inside of the box was full.

Several sheets of paper with all sorts of lines and musical notes drawn on them, iconic props, and... a laptop.

Seon Ahyeon, who had been standing quietly, widened his eyes.

"This, this..."

"...This is the concept material for the next album."

Kim Raebin answered courageously.

"We are preparing hard, so don't worry at all about your future schedule, and wake up comfortably!"

It was something he did right after he was discharged.

"......"

Even Cha Eugene was speechless and looked at Kim Raebin.

In fact, this was a very unreasonable act.

Even if Park Moondae woke up quickly, no one knew if rehabilitation and return would be possible right away.

Moreover, it had been a longer time than expected since Park Moondae had not regained consciousness.

It was said that among the medical staff, there was even a story that a full-scale retest would be needed.

Nevertheless, Kim Raebin had been specifically 'preparing' for the next activity as if it were possible right away.

It was to report to Park Moondae, who couldn't even see it because he was asleep.

*I know already.*

Kim Raebin thought to himself.

It was hard to believe that he had spent such a ridiculous amount of time expecting an uncertain future positively.

*But... I've experienced it.*

His grandma had woken up too, so he was sure that Moondae-hyung would wake up soon too.

"While preparing... I'll wait!"

Recalling his illogical conviction, Kim Raebin took the box and sat down on the chair next to the bed.

Then, a loud noise erupted from the side.

"That's right!"

"...!"

"Hyung, wake up, let's have another concert! Let's do a lot!"

"Y-yes! Moondae, I'll prepare... !"

Following Cha Eugene, even Seon Ahyeon, who was downcast, clung to his knitted yarn and shouted as if he had been encouraged.

If someone else had been here, they might have clicked their tongue or looked at them with pity, wondering what kind of blueprint it was.

Still, they persevered.

"......"

Of course, there was still no answer from Park Moondae.

Since reality was not a cartoon, there was no way that a person in a coma would wake up with a call from coworkers.

But they decided to wait and see.

Instead of making plans for a future without Park Moondae, they made a different choice instead.

And that moment of choice was coming to the sleeping Park Moondae.
