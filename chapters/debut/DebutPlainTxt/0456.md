# Chapter 456

"Minha!"

"...Oh, uh, Harin."

Lately, Park Minha of Mirynay had walked around like her soul had left her body.

With the company in chaos, the group's schedule was uncertain, so they all spent more time at the dorm. Each member tried to shake off their anxiety by focusing on hobbies or practice.

However, Park Minha was the only one acting this way.

*Is she under too much stress?*

*The weight of being the Leader again...*Mirynay's Seong Harin crossed her arms, her expression serious.

Just then, Park Minha suddenly spoke.

"Harin, if we... if we could go to another company, would you?"

"......"

"Ah, no. Just pretend you didn't hear that."

Who could pretend they didn't hear that and still be an idol!

*Another company? Another company?*

*Did she get some kind of signal?*

Seong Harin knew it too. The company was in turmoil.

But escaping an entertainment company with time left on your contract was next to impossible.

*If that's the case, and something realistic were to happen by taking advantage of the situation... No way!*

"Are we moving to the TeSTAR label?!"

"Shh, shh!"

Park Minha looked horrified. She glanced around nervously and hastily covered her mouth.

"Is it true?"

"No, no, that's not it!"

But she hesitated before adding.

"...I think there might be a possibility, though."

What the heck!

Park Minha wasn't the type to make empty boasts. That meant she must have heard something real.

A forgotten suspicion struck Seong Harin. She remembered the two of them having particularly meaningful conversations.

"......"

*Could Minha's source of information be...*

"Don't tell me... did Park Moondae tell you?"

"......"

Park Minha froze.

Her reaction was a direct hit. Seong Harin felt her buried suspicion resurface.

*Are the two of them dating...?*

"Harin, don't tell me there are secret rumors going around that we're close? Not among company officials, or other idols, or—even worse—a terrifying situation where fans are talking about it..."

*I guess not.*

"N-No! It was my deduction, mine!"

"Hoo."

Park Minha sighed deeply. She sank onto a chair, looking completely drained.

"Your deduction is correct..."

"...Yeah."

It seemed so, but Seong Harin decided not to bring up her other suspicion again.

*Right, someone as serious about being an idol as Minha wouldn't be so obvious about it already!*

That meant she was talking with Park Moondae on a purely business level.

"Then what on earth is going on?"

"...He said to wait just a little longer."

Park Minha stared at her smartphone with a melancholic expression.

"...?"

Seong Harin was filled with questions, but Park Minha offered no more answers.

A few days later, a cautious message arrived from the company. It announced that, of all things, they would allow the group to own its name, not the company!

"Why would they do that?"

"They're probably trying to turn the mood around. Something like image-making?"

"Aha."

The members discussed it. In any case, the offer to buy their group name was incredibly attractive!

They could take the group name with them even if they changed companies!

*Amazing.*

Seong Harin covered her mouth but quickly realized the reality of the situation.

*...We haven't been paid enough to buy this easily.*

*Wait a minute.*

*Then, are they using this as an excuse... pretending to be generous by putting us in debt, and then fleecing us even more by deducting it from our payments?*

If they were just in it for a quick buck with no intention of managing the group for long, then they wouldn't care about the group's trademark rights, would they!

*Aren't they doing this because they have no intention of nurturing Mirynay for the long haul!*

However, the company named a more conscientious price than expected.

"...?"

The figure was still staggering, of course, but it wasn't some insane number in the tens of billions.

"I really hope we buy it! Yes!"

"If Minha says so, I'm all for it!"

Led by Park Minha's active encouragement and Jeong Yulgi's cheerful words, the members readily pooled their money. They bought the trademark rights under their joint names.

"What about the other groups?"

"It seems like they're buying them too."

Of course they would. Excluding the rookies from the survival show ❬Be My Girls❭, who were sinking after a controversy, no one would miss this chance.

...The company played the media as if they had graciously handed over the rights after selling them, which was annoying. Still, the atmosphere was good.

The public reacted positively, saying things like, 'They're on a completely different track from T1, which uses its artists as consumables.' While it was bittersweet, it was a good thing in the long run.

*We still have several years until contract renewal.*

TeSTAR's contract period was five years, but after that season of ❬Idol Inc.❭ became a massive hit, contracts were tied to seven years.

*Alright. Our next album will be a huge hit! It'd be great to join the TeSTAR label too!*

Seong Harin decided to think as positively as possible!

...That is, until this article appeared.

[TeSTAR's Contract Renewal Falls Through... Where is the KPOP Star's Next Nest]

"......"

TeSTAR wasn't renewing their contract.

*W-Wait.*

*The TeSTAR label is about to be dissolved?!*

*These people are escaping!*

*They're running away by themselves!*

Then what on earth was Park Minha talking about? Seong Harin whipped her head away from the WeTube screen to look at Park Minha so fast she nearly broke her neck, but...

"Minha?"

"Hehehe... hehe."

Park Minha stared at the WeTube screen, laughing emptily.

She didn't look surprised.

She just looked like someone who had made an irreversible decision. It was like she had just swiped her card for an incredibly expensive item on a 24-month installment plan.

"......"

Park Minha let Jeong Yulgi poke her repeatedly as she reflected on her past choice.

*This... I bet on the right horse, right??*

*I've never been wrong before, so I absolutely can't get thrown aside this time.*

*Please!*

Limp as an inflatable tube man at a grand opening, Park Minha picked up her smartphone.

***

BZZZT.

[Mirynay Park Minha: I heard you aren't renewing your contract... Congratulations on moving to a new place.]

Right. It was about time for the articles to appear.

I deleted the flood of messages and replied to that one.

[Thank you]

Of course, she probably hadn't sent it just to congratulate me.

*She's testing the waters.*

I added my next words.

[You don't need to worry]

The reply came a few dozen seconds later.

[Mirynay Park Minha: Yes...]

[Mirynay Park Minha: Of course. You even invited me to the group chat, so I trust you.]

Well, well.

*She's saying that if things go south, she can expose the group chat, so I'd better not betray her.*

*She's gotten quicker on the uptake.*

I laughed quietly, sent 'I understand,' and turned off my smartphone.

I wasn't the only one looking at messages.

"Wow, the messages are coming in like crazy."

"Ugh, I know..."

Messages about the article poured in for the members from all sides.

I recalled the news I had heard yesterday.

*The Head of the Department quit.*

That bastard bolted without missing his chance.

On top of that, news that TeSTAR wasn't renewing its contract was now everywhere.

It wasn't an article released by the company, of course.

*We were the ones who tipped them off.*

We didn't do it directly, of course. We leaked it through an affiliate and made it look like a mistake.

1.  T1 cutting its losses.

2.  The Head of the Department cutting his losses.

3.  TeSTAR cutting its losses.

The company had experienced a crazy series of loss-cutting moves, one after another, in a short and completely unexpected period.

This was the moment the company was at its most vulnerable.

"Are we moving on to the next phase of the plan~?"

That's right.

I turned my head to the person in charge. He was the guy in this group with the most trustworthy and stable image.

"I'm counting on you."

"I should be the one counting on you."

Ryu Chungwoo, already prepared, smiled and left the dorm.

His destination was the company meeting room.

However, it was not T1 Stars, but the meeting room of its subsidiary, the TeSTAR label.

***

*Hoo...*

Kwon Heeseung, the idol Park Moondae still called 'Gold 2', looked through the group chat's history.

It was the group chat Park Moondae had created for the 'Great Company Escape'.

*I thought I'd have to bring up the wish ticket to get his help.*

A company problem like this was too difficult. Kwon Heeseung had been prepared to use the wish ticket he received after suffering in that bizarre virtual world.

But Park Moondae offered a hand first, so he didn't have to.

There was just one condition.

[Stay still, no matter what happens.]

That was it.

So, Kwon Heeseung remained steadfastly still through all the chaos: the board's arrest, the trademark sale, the department head leaving, and TeSTAR's departure.

*He must be painting a big picture.*

He knew Park Moondae's methods were a bit extreme, but he also knew Park Moondae was a loyal and affectionate person!

...Though he had to admit, his heart nearly dropped when TeSTAR actually left without renewing their contract.

*It's fine. He wouldn't cut us off!*

But the next piece of news almost made even his optimistic heart stop.

"Hey, this is crazy, I heard everyone at that label quit."

"...!? W-What?"

"The TeSTAR label, ORBIT? People there are resigning one after another!"

*Oh my god.*

Kwon Heeseung soon learned through word of mouth that it wasn't an exaggeration. Almost the entire staff had really resigned.

Naturally, the original company was in an uproar.

[This is no ordinary matter, is it?]

[Ah, don't tell me something else is about to blow up...?]

Slowly, those who could change jobs began leaving the company in a relay.

With nearly half of the company's personnel deserting in this major incident, the company, which was already failing to function properly with most of its superiors gone, seemed completely paralyzed.

"......"

Kwon Heeseung grew more anxious, but he decided to trust Park Moondae.

A non-A-list celebrity couldn't do anything about the situation anyway, so thinking positively was better for his mental health.

Of course, he still sent a message.

[The company's people are vanishing]

[And where is TeSTAR going? ㅠ]

"......"

The reply came almost immediately.

[They're not vanishing]

[Wait a little longer]

"...?"

That very evening, a spectacular article appeared on the main entertainment news page.

[TeSTAR's Special Loyalty... Not a New Nest, but Their "Label Head's" Agency]

[TeSTAR Signs with a New Agency... Turns Out to Be Their Resigned "Label Head"]

"...!"

That's right.

TeSTAR hadn't openly established their own agency or moved to an existing one. Instead, they transferred to a new agency founded by their former label head after he resigned.

In that moment, Kwon Heeseung realized.

*...Don't tell me, the reason all the label employees resigned was?*

That's right.

If Park Moondae handled the Head of the Department, then Ryu Chungwoo handled the label's people, winning them over.

Amid the company's precarious future, exploding scandals, and the anxious feeling that everyone had to 'cut their losses,' he had stimulated a herd mentality.

[We really... think we would reconsider renewing our contract if we could continue working with the people here.]

The label head gladly grabbed the lifeline.

At the news that TeSTAR would follow, most of the label's employees glanced at each other and jumped from the sinking ship together.

And that was how this situation came to be.

They couldn't become independent as a whole label, but they scattered as if disappearing, then reassembled to become a new agency!

*Amazing. So that's why he said they weren't vanishing.*

Kwon Heeseung was in awe but soon became aware of his own situation.

*Wait, but what happens to Spacer? Are we... are we being abandoned just like this...?*

"......"

[ㅠㅠ??]

The reply came quickly again.

[Just a little more]

*Just how much more?!*

Kwon Heeseung wanted to scream, so he sent the message just like that.

After sending it, he worried it might seem like he was angry, but Park Moondae didn't get mad.

He simply replied.

[Until people are more anxious than you]

***

"...Huh?"

A short while later, unimaginable news arrived.

"The company... it's been acquired."

"......"

"TeSTAR's new agency bought T1 Stars..."

What??

Kwon Heeseung almost dropped his smartphone at his manager's words.

Then he saw a new message from Park Moondae.

[It looks like you guys can terminate your contracts]

[Should I release you now?]

"......"

*Oh my god.*

They had heightened the psychological belief that the company would fail as much as possible. They had dropped its market value and future prospects to their lowest point.

Then, at the most favorable time and for the lowest price, TeSTAR's new agency had devoured T1 Stars.

"......"

*So this is why... he told me to stay still.*

Kwon Heeseung realized instinctively. He had been ordered to 'stay still' because if he showed any sign of having someone to rely on, or acted proactively, it could have disrupted the atmosphere.

*Wow.*

It was truly incredible. An escape from the company beyond imagination.
