# Chapter 559

LeTi's recording studio.

"We'll proceed as you suggested, Jaehyun."

"Yes."

Cheongryeo resumed recording their new song. The session had been halted because a member suddenly deserted the group.

The schedule became incredibly tight to make up for the delay. However, that did not matter as long as the quality was perfect.

He planned to discard and restart this life anyway, but he identified flaws and polished the path to perfection out of habit.

*Hmm.*

The layers of calculations in his quiet mind were solid, as always.

Cheongryeo gently tapped the lyric sheet.

It was then.

[Let me ask you something.]

A sentence broke the silence.

Cheongryeo narrowed his eyes. The message from Help seemed to have popped up in a hurry. The track was mixing, so he could talk, but...

*How rude.*

He thought he should have trained Help to ask for permission first in these situations. He considered it for a moment but decided to let it slide.

"What's the question?"

The moment he gave his tacit approval, sentences poured out.

The question.

[About that story you told me last time, the one about Jung Woodan. The one you confessed right before a restart.]

[Do you remember the exact situation back then?]

He remembered. He had neatly recorded the members he used for VTIC in his mind, as if organizing a table. This included the reasons for using them and the reasons for no longer using them.

The 'Jung Woodan story' that Help mentioned belonged to the latter category. It was one of the memories he had intentionally recorded.

However.

*There's no reason to tell him.*

Cheongryeo gave Help a brief, emotionless glance. For some reason, however, he soon opened his mouth.

"I believe I already told you. It was right before a restart."

He began to explain the situation in a monotone voice.

It was the 21st VTIC. A Butterfly Effect during a new album's production caused the agency's financial manager to be replaced. This led to major trouble with tax issues.

As a result, VTIC's tour for the second half of the year was canceled. Cheongryeo realized.

*Is this the end?*

The time had come to restart again. He felt a little resentful or annoyed. It was still an early period, and there had not been many times when the group failed because of some absurd incident, even when the group itself was solid.

So, he made a decision on a rather cold impulse.

*Should I tell someone?*

About his situation. There was nothing to lose by telling them now. He chose a member with decent crisis management skills, a unique way of thinking, and no ethical problems.

Judan.

At first, the guy listened to the explanation and even demanded proof, examining it meticulously. Then, surprisingly, he immediately nodded.

[I see. Indeed... I understand.]

And he asked.

[Then, is there something you need my help with...?]

[Your help? Hmm. I don't need your help right now.]

[...Pardon?]

[The moment we failed to meet the required audience numbers, this group became a failure. It's over. Even if someone helps, I have to restart. Haha...]

[...Then why are you telling me this... hidden truth?]

[To see if you'd believe me.]

Judan was on the list of members to recruit again after the restart. This was a sort of test run to check if this process would work well.

Until that point, Cheongryeo had judged the attempt a success.

[It's a relief. That you believe me.]

[...Yes.]

The conversation that day ended like that.

Cheongryeo finished his recollection and smiled faintly.

"But Jung Woodan disappeared from the dorm the next day. He only left a note for the company saying, 'I want to take a break from activities because I'm not feeling well.'"

After he cooled his head, he realized he had done something that did not align with his cost-benefit analysis.

"Running away from home. If he had reported to the company that the Leader had gone insane, I would have lowered his evaluation less."

[......]

*No, it seems like he believed you and got scared shitless.*

*Even I would've fucking bolted.*

Park Moondae understood instinctively. To anyone, Cheongryeo must have looked like he had lost his mind. Judan was probably scared like a dog that had run into a Tiger.

Cheongryeo continued his explanation in a monotone.

"Having used up all my spare time, I restarted the next day. Until then, Jung Woodan had not returned to the dorm from his hiding spot."

*...*

Park Moondae rubbed the back of his neck. He noticed the meaning implied in that sentence. Cheongryeo had specifically mentioned a hiding spot.

[Did you know where that guy was?]

Cheongryeo answered plainly.

"Near the company."

Come to think of it...

"It was a rental meeting room."

Cheongryeo realized. The VTIC member who ran away this time also went to a seminar room.

In other words.

"It wasn't a unique idea, just a coincidence similar to his own situation, was it?"

[......]

"Hmm. As I thought."

Cheongryeo concluded there was no value in reconsidering Jung Woodan's evaluation. He turned back to the recording.

[Wait a minute.]

Help pressed on the empty air.

"......"

Park Moondae felt a sense of unease.

*Something's strange.*

A seminar room.

[It's not a place where people usually rest or hang out, so it's hard for the company to predict. It's not crowded, so it's even comfortable. It's the perfect space for an escape.]

Judan had definitely explained it that way.

*That means it's an escape space for everyday use.*

It was a place where an introvert, tired of dealing with people during practice, would hole up alone to find mental stability. That was where Judan had gone. It was not a place to lie low like a dead mouse while hiding from a serial killer.

*...Perhaps.*

Park Moondae was silent for a moment before he started typing.

[I think that guy believed you.]

"Hmm?"

[Jung Woodan believed me right away even when I said I was from the future. He's the one who helped me.]

*Thanks to him, I didn't starve to death.*

"......"

Park Moondae ignored the look of disbelief that flashed in Cheongryeo's eyes. Instead, he kindly completed the sentence for him.

[Realistically, if he believed a complete stranger, isn't it more probable that he'd believe his own member? You said you even showed him proof.]

"I'm not sure if you want to debate an obvious phenomenon... but have you forgotten that he went into hiding?"

[Right, about that. I actually asked Jung Woodan himself after hearing what you said.]

Park Moondae relayed a refined version of the idea he had gotten from Judan through the psychological test. He would have bolted at the thought that his fellow member could do anything, even in a ruined situation, since a restart would erase whatever happened.

[In other words, he went into hiding precisely because he believed you.]

"Hmm."

Cheongryeo readily nodded.

"That... is certainly a decent idea."

[......]

"It's a rather individualistic way of thinking for group activities, but as a flaw, it's acceptable..."

Just as Cheongryeo was about to put a hold on his evaluation of Judan, Help's main point began.

[However.]

[I think that's strange, too.]

"......"

Cheongryeo narrowed his eyes. Park Moondae had a hunch that Cheongryeo was starting to think this was a waste of time. He had to choose his words carefully.

"What part of it."

[The location.]

[If that guy had truly felt his life was in danger and tried to escape, he would have already fled overseas. At the very least, he's the type who would have run away to the countryside.]

He would not have just holed up in a seminar room. He would have run with all his might. He was the guy who declared he would drop out the moment he realized things were going strange in the System's virtual world. In a life-threatening crisis, there was no way he would have stayed for two days in a facility near the company.

*He probably thought something like, 'A supernatural person who can go back in time would be able to find my location if it's this close.*

[Seen that way, a seminar room near the company is too complacent.]

Besides.

[The answer I brought you was from him as a mere trainee during a psychological test. It wasn't from your member back then who had been in VTIC for years.]

[I'm not saying he's a different person, but the conditions and environment were different.]

The Judan here, the trainee Jung Woodan I talked to, does not know Cheongryeo well. He was a young kid who had not even debuted as an idol and lived rubbing shoulders with his members. The answer that kid gave is likely far from the actual feelings of the Judan who was a VTIC member.

*And considering this guy is also a surprisingly affectionate type...*

Watching the situation from nearby meant one thing.

[Judan ran away because he believed Cheongryeo's words.]

In addition to that.

[Once he sorted out in his head that you weren't dangerous, he probably would have come right back.]

It meant Judan had intended to return soon.

~~~

I looked at Cheongryeo's calm face. On the surface, he looked just like a K-pop artist focused on recording, but I noticed a crack forming in his surface thoughts. It must be because of the new interpretation.

[Judan believed Cheongryeo's words, and he wanted to interpret Cheongryeo's intentions in a positive light.]

[This is also a sign that he thought there was a high probability you wouldn't harm his well-being.]

Cheongryeo did not even move an eyelid.

[That must mean you were a decent Leader back then.]

From Jung Woodan's perspective, it was worth taking a risk to organize his thoughts nearby so he could return at any time.

"......"

I was not saying this to make him feel good.

[When you connect the dots logically, this is the answer with the highest probability. Also, isn't it certain that the idea he ran away because he didn't trust you actually has the lowest probability?]

Cheongryeo's finger twitched slightly.

I completed the sentence without hesitation.

[The probability that your attempt to choose Jung Woodan was a success is much higher.]

[Don't write it off as a complete failure based on that one case.]

"......"

Cheongryeo tore his gaze from the lyric sheet. He slowly expressed his thoughts.

"...I don't believe I ever said I only told a group member my situation once."

*...What.*

"I tried it a few more times with other targets after that, but the expected value was so miserably low that I scrapped the idea."

However, Cheongryeo's expression was not cold. A natural, blank expression had settled on his face instead of a picturesque smile.

"But the first attempt being a failure has a big impact on the statistics."

*...!*

"Because it starts with a complete failure."

And now, that had been overturned.

[So, how does it feel knowing your judgment might have been right?]

Cheongryeo did not answer for a moment, but he soon opened his mouth.

"It's not bad."

*Alright.*

I grinned.

Cheongryeo put down the lyric sheet a little more gently than before. I realized that a slightly more relaxed emotion had activated in his surface thoughts.

Well, it was not bad.

I typed cheerfully.

[So are you going to use Jung Woodan as a member now?]

"I might add him to the list of candidates."

*...!*

*That's right.*

...No. Why the hell am I feeling so proud? In reality, he is just a guy who is doing well with VTIC while buying up buildings.

*What's with this over-immersion?*

If anything, I should be thrilled that a rival group is failing, but seeing T-Holic get decimated here, it seems I have developed some strange form of empathy.

Just as I was smacking my lips.

"In that sense."

Hmm?

"It seems you're in contact with Jung Woodan."

Cheongryeo said with a smile.

"Should we hold an interview?"

"......"

It was something I could have never imagined.

~~~

Three hours later, at the officetel.

"I never thought we'd meet in this capacity. I'm Jung Woodan."

"Yes. It's nice to meet you."

The guy who gave me the officetel and the guy who helped me move were sitting around the table. Listing only the facts made it sound heartwarming, but in truth, it was not a very realistic scene.

Cheongryeo, finished with his recording, making time to come here?

*I never thought the bastard who would've ignored my suggestion as a waste of time would volunteer to come on his own.*

Moreover, Cheongryeo and Judan were conversing in a surprisingly good atmosphere. A big part of it was probably that Cheongryeo responded to all of Judan's nonsense and questions with a smile, completely unfazed.

"I see! Thank you."

"Don't mention it."

To my surprise, he was not just brushing off Judan's questions but was answering them quite truthfully. There were parts he glossed over, but he had at least confessed that he went back to the past to restart, without much concealment.

*Hmm.*

I watched as Judan looked slightly flustered after finally hearing the words, 'You might debut with VTIC next time.'

"Indeed... It would be an honor from my perspective, but haven't you put the restart on hold? I heard Gunwoo was a great help."

*He's asking this so bluntly?*

"Well. Hmm, if we win the Grand Prize, I suppose I don't have to restart. Haha."

*And you're answering this so honestly?*

*Is the album recording going that well?*

I turned to look with a slightly dazed expression.

Cheongryeo continued with a smile.

"I'm still going to restart, though."

"......"

*What?*

"Because once I succeed, it's over. This reality becomes fixed."

"...Pardon?"

"But I'm not particularly fond of the current members."

Cheongryeo kindly explained to Judan.

"If we continue like this, even if we win the Grand Prize, this group's lifespan will be over within a few years."

"......"

"It works out well. I'll be using you, Woodan."

Cheongryeo turned his head.

"And you too."

Towards me.

"......"

"You have a good sense for working in the entertainment industry, and, hmm, I saw the scouting video. You sing well. Our vocal pool was lacking, so this is great."

*What on earth is he talking about.*

"I know that if you restart..."

"Right. You said you might not be able to use that body if I restart."

Cheongryeo responded calmly.

"It doesn't matter. You can just stay as Help."

Because.

"There's one more of you here."

"......"

I realized what he was talking about.

Ryu Gunwoo!

My past self, who is currently attending university.

"His grades are good. He's diligent... hmm. His financial situation isn't great, so he'd be easy to persuade if I offer to support his basic needs."

"......"

"If I aim for when you just entered university, it should be perfectly possible."

There was conviction in his calm tone.

"When did you investigate this."

*He should know that I can share his vision anytime I connect as Help.*

"When do you think."

Cheongryeo smiled gently.

"When you were busy with your acting schedule, of course."

"...!"

"You worked hard searching for information. That's probably why it was harder for you to connect as Help."

That son of a bitch.
