On the contrary, a little... It motivated me.

My head became clearer.

[Lee Sejin: Memorizing the script (｀ㅅ//)]

It was then.

"Excuse me..."

"...!"

*But why am I hearing his voice right now?*

When I immediately turned my head, I saw the same guy as I expected.

"Where can I wash my hands?"

"......"

Yes. Since it was break time, this guy could wander around here as he wanted.

I pointed with my hand.

"It's over there."

"Oh~"

Go quickly.

But instead of leaving, the guy lingered.

"Thank you. Ah, Gunwoo-ssi, is it okay if I call you this?"

"Yes."

"Oh~ You're refreshingly straightforward!"

And he grinned.

"Thanks to you performing so well today, I had a lot of fun too. Thank you~"

"I..."

*Get it together.*

"Thank you for your kind words."

"Hey, it's not an empty remark. I also saw Gunwoo's first performance, and it was really good. It looks like you're going to debut, really!"

Did this guy remember something? Was he testing me?

*That can't be possible.*

He probably just thought I had potential and wanted to make a connection. Wasn't it obvious?

*That hasn't changed.*

I relaxed my arms.

Yes, I was going to find him and awaken him after I was done gathering reputation anyway, so he could do whatever he wanted.

I should compliment him a little, too.

Well, it wasn't like I was going to make things up.

"Thank you. I also learned a lot from watching sunbaenim perform. Every movement and expression suited the song perfectly, and I took inspiration from that."

"Ah."

The guy suddenly fell quiet.

*He didn't expect it?*

But soon he opened his mouth like nothing had happened.

"Thank you. I feel energized too~ Well, then, since this must be fate, shall we exchange contact information?"

This happened in *Idol Inc.*

as well.

It was more of a strange nostalgia than bitterness. I shrugged.

"I don't have my phone right now... If it's okay with you, I'll give you my contact information."

"Yeah, that's good!"

I put my number into his smartphone. I felt strange.

"I'll text you and say hello later~"

"Yes. Then I'll go in and get ready. Thank you."

"Mhm, yes."

I just turned away, as if I didn't notice that he wanted to talk more.

*I have to put my brain in order.*

He had always been a talkative guy. It felt like I would make a mistake the moment I relaxed while talking to him.

Anyway, it was a polite response, so he wouldn't have been offended...

*Things ended up unfolding similarly here as well.*

For networking, that was. Our relationship had started as a mutually beneficial connection.

After smiling, I hurried my steps around the corner of the hallway.

And just before colliding with the guy on the other side of the corner, I stopped a short distance away. It was Cheongryeo.

"So you were here. You've come a long way."

"It just happened somehow."

"I don't think it happened 'somehow'... Ah, as expected."

He glanced around the corner and looked at Lee Sejin's back. And he muttered languidly.

"I noticed you were gone. I'll tell you in advance—it'll be hard to recruit that one. He's already debuted, and he's in his third year."

Didn't I already know that?

"We just exchanged contact information. Nothing more."

"Hmm, are you thinking of trying to persuade him by explaining what the situation is?"

"......"

"You've done that to him before."

Explain everything to him out of the blue?

For a moment, I thought it might actually be possible, but...

"No, the stage comes first."

Something wasn't unreasonable because it was impossible. It was unreasonable because nobody did it, even though it was possible.

I had no intention of telling him anything unnecessary until he awakened.

"Is that so? It's well thought out."

He was saying something obvious.

I returned to the practice room to continue working on the performance.

And a few days later...

Finally, for the first time in this program, stage filming with an audience had begun.

"Please take care of me~"

GY-ROP's Lee Sejin, who came again as a special judge, greeted the audience warmly and sat down.

Among the idols who came to the mid-term evaluation, those who were less busy were invited back, and it didn't feel particularly great to have earned a spot here simply by being less busy.

Nevertheless, there was interest in the stage.

Monitoring those who were likely to become competitors was bound to be beneficial. Plus, there was someone he had just exchanged numbers with.

Ryu Gunwoo.

The trainee from the jazz performance.

Lee Sejin frowned slightly, pretending to wrinkle his nose.

*It was a little strange.*

Originally, he'd thought Gunwoo was particularly talented and eye-catching, so there was nothing to lose by making a connection.

But when they actually started talking...

*I guess I should say that I just felt like I wanted to become friends with him.*

Why? It was like he wanted to become closer to an old friend he'd somehow grown apart from.

*Have we known each other before?*

Well, like a friend he used to play with in the neighborhood when he was young.

No, would he know him because he was older than him? Like a neighborhood hyung?

*Oh, I don't know.*

Lee Sejin just erased the questions that couldn't be answered.

*There's no reply yet anyway.*

After this stage was over, he could try to find out a little by keeping in touch.

Even though the person seemed a little blunt, he was clearly talented, so Lee Sejin didn't regret approaching him. Lee Sejin straightened his posture more.

Because filming would start soon.

"⟨Wise⟩ 3rd stage begins."

At the president's grandiose announcement, the set was quickly prepared.

And what unfolded were stages worthy of a debut group from a major agency.

*... It's annoying.*

Lee Sejin suppressed his boiling emotions.

A competitive urge and a sense of crisis.

And then he ranked them.

Groups that were worse than GY-ROP, and groups that were similar to GY-ROP...

*Well, just because they're good at performing doesn't mean they'll be popular.*

Wasn't that like what happened to the agency's previous female idol group, FluffySweet, who never really took off despite their solid skills?

But when he considered that half of these contestants would be eliminated before the final team...

*Ah... Damn it.*

Lee Sejin decided that he had to think of a way to whip the group into shape somehow.

Dealing with people was the area he was most confident in. It exhausted him to realize that he seemed to spend more time worrying about things like this than the actual stage.

His eyes darkened, but this was offset by the light.

"We are revealing the concepts for the next match."

And as time passed, the most awaited topic came.

The concept appeared on the electronic display board.

[Sexy]

Wow!

There were groans and cheers among the visitors, unsure of whether they were sighs or anticipation.

*Ah~ The moment I saw the concept, I knew they'd go all in.*

Lee Sejin suppressed a bitter smile and gave an appropriately cheerful reaction for a third-year idol. And then the first team stage unfolded.

-Get on your knees, ooh!

The tightly synchronized choreography, auto-tune, and body waves practically screamed lethal charm.

*Hmm.*

Well, there was demand... he supposed.

Lee Sejin prepared a review in preparation for when he had to speak to the trainees and jotted down a few suitable keywords in case he was asked to comment.

And he roughly predicted the reaction of the next and final stage.

*... That team.*

He had already been familiar with it since the mid-term evaluation. A team that included many of the people with the best output in this survival program.

The team that Ryu Gunwoo belonged to, with whom he exchanged numbers with.

*There's a reason why they're going last.*

He looked it up and found that spoilers were circulating on the internet and everyone was looking forward to it.

Most of the audience members sitting there right now would be busy using KakaoTalk or social media and guessing the members of this team by thinking about the contestants who 'hadn't appeared yet'.

And the moment it appeared on the electronic display board...

[Shin Jaehyun, Kim Raebin, Jung Woodan, Cha Eugene]

[+Ryu Gunwoo (at risk of elimination)]

Argh! Yeaaaah!

There was a mix of indescribable screams and moans along with tremendous cheers.

This was a phenomenon in which fans who saw for the first time that Ryu Gunwoo was marked as being at risk of elimination were surprised but had expectations for the team itself.

*Everyone will end up going crazy.*

He could already see how successful this would be. Lee Sejin suppressed a bitter smile as he pictured exactly how the program would promote it. Their song selection was a song by a top male idol group that was released two years ago.

Wild, masculine, and sexy.

It was a similar goal to the previous team.

*They will win.*

If the feeling was similar, wouldn't the one who was better at it win? It was a matter that had already been concluded the moment he saw the mid-term evaluation.

*... Because they did well.*

He could already tell from just the first verse, but he seriously focused his eyes on the stage.

The participants gathered in a large formation and stood in the shape of a bud. Colorless lights flickered on the stage.

-Cut off.

And the moment when an elegant accompaniment was played with a low voice.

"...!"

The lighting changed to purple, and the members moved their bodies gently.

Their upper bodies moved in broad, flowing waves.

Lee Sejin realized it right away.

*They changed the arrangement a little bit.*

Some instrumental elements were missing. The electric guitar had been removed, while the bass and strings were brought forward.

And the movements...

Instead of being powerful, they were slow and leisurely. Their velvet shirts and black pants suited the movements well.

-Cut it and leave.

this is your way

(So sick)

When Kim Raebin finished the intro, sweeping his hair back, Shin Jaehyun, who received it smoothly, performed his part with gestures.

Without any group dance. Just with facial expressions and movements.

Yet the audience was watching it intently.

*It's difficult.*

A stage that seemed to have no fixed choreography, with deliberate gaps in the movement. Doing something like that and making the stage look full was difficult even on a professional stage.

Moreover, it was not just filled with that.

-Do not look back

Because it's only painful, it's meaningless

You lost your game, stop it

Just leave

The interlude began, and the group dance unfolded. The difficult choreography presented interlocked like gears, creating synergy.

Heek.

The surrounding arms and legs moved as if in sync with the person standing in the center, forming a flower bud around the center member.

In this way, the members interacted like props and brought the center to life.

The goal was to flexibly move between tight choreography parts and loose parts to maximize the appeal of both.

-Story that has already ended

By the time the second verse was in full swing, Lee Sejin came to a conclusion.

*It's clever.*

It was a choice only performers of absurd skill could pull off, but with a team like this, he would have gladly chosen it himself.

"......"

The stage was lively.

He shook off his thoughts and focused again on the performance, which was completely different from the mid-term evaluation.

Then another question came up this time.

Was the arrangement really the only reason?

"...!"

And then he realized it.

Cha Eugene.

Cha Eugene had been the main reason the stage felt so wild during the mid-term evaluation. He always had rushing energy and explosive excitement.

However, here, it felt like that explosive intensity had been toned down.

*But that doesn't mean he isn't standing out.*

For example, the last refrain of the second verse.

-Love is a kind of disease

A late realization comes

So-

Cha Eugene stared into the camera with half-lidded eyes as someone grabbed his neck.

By holding back the force of his usual intense gaze, he heightened the mood.

A cold, subdued charm.

It was so good that it would be believable if someone told him Cha Eugene was actually a LeTi trainee from the beginning.

It was amazing to be able to control that, and at the same time, it was a very strategic choice.

It would certainly have seemed different and attractive to viewers. Even LeTi's president, the one making the decisions here, was clearly reassessing him.

Moreover, the structure of this stage had strong support.

-So, oh, oh, oh

And this performance had a strong backbone: Ryu Gunwoo.

He was adding difficult, high-pitched parts live at the right time.

*I can feel that the audience is surprised.*

But if that were all he could do, he might have seemed like nothing more than a flashy stage prop.

But he knew how to make himself stand out in other ways, too.

*Right there.*

It was also known as the "Saint" part.

It was a section that suddenly created a reverent atmosphere and drew all the attention to the singer.

It wasn't super high-pitched. It had a moderately high vocal range.

In other words, that section was extremely difficult for anyone to sing reliably.

-It's done

It's over

Cut it off

Ryu Gunwoo dreamily performed it in a breathy tone as if he were reciting it.

His rhythm was unbelievably clean, especially for someone who had been throwing out those insane high-pitched ad-libs just moments earlier.

It would have been easier to just scream out a high note.

How was he sustaining that for so long without taking a single breath? And without a backing track?

-Cut it and leave.

this is your way

(So sick)

Ryu Gunwoo made the most of that one killing point very well.

He was able to raise the quality of the stage several levels higher using his vocals.

A presence that practically demonstrated exactly why a group needed a main vocalist.

"......"

He felt a strange sense of déjà vu.

*Why?*

However, the stage just rushed towards the end after the bridge.

All members performed their roles perfectly and appealed to the audience as much as possible.

And the ending...

-Cut off.

Ahhhhh!!

There was so much loud cheering and applause that it felt like his ears were going to bleed. The waving of shining light sticks filled the area as well.

Lee Sejin watched the stage intently without looking away until the end.

He still felt a desire to win against them, but there was no longer a sense of crisis.

What took its place was...

*... Ah.*

It was a sense of loss he couldn't quite put into words.

That strange feeling that someone had just taken his place...

*What am I saying right now?*

Did he eat something wrong today? Lee Sejin chewed the inside of his mouth and controlled his expression.

And when it was time for the judges to comment, he opened his mouth with a smile.

"It was really good, really!"

That very day, Episode 3 of *Wise* aired before the audience members could spread spoilers about the at-risk contestants who had lost to members of the supplementary team.

And finally, viewer voting began.
