"Hoo."

**[?? Hyung?]**

*No. This isn't what I want. Not now.*

Only then did my brain kick back into gear.

*I'm currently in the middle of filming.*

I'd even had to turn in my smartphone. Even if I awakened this guy now, I wouldn't be able to contact him for the next four days.

I couldn't let him spend those four days alone and confused as his memories returned to him.

$p
**[Ah... Yes.]**
p$

I'd finish filming first, then awaken him.

$p
**[Yes! Go for it!]**
p$

I dismissed the pop-up. But I wasn't as anxious as before.

On the contrary, I felt a little... more motivated.

*I'll awaken him and contact him as soon as filming's over. I'll tell Cha Eugene too.*

Of course, persuading this guy wouldn't be easy... But just knowing he would remember us made me feel much better.

My head became clearer.

-★

...I don't know why his emoticon was suddenly shaded, but for now, I would content myself with doing nothing.

I chuckled, closed the pop-up, and prepared to return to the practice room.

It was then.

"Excuse me..."

"...!"

It was a voice I knew. But...

*But why am I hearing his voice right now?*

I turned my head and immediately saw exactly who I'd expected.

It was Lee Sejin with an awkward expression.

"Where can I wash my hands?"

"......"

Yes. I stared for a moment before realizing it was break time, so this guy could wander around here as he wanted.

I raised my hand and pointed.

"It's over there."

"Oh~"

*Go on, then.*

But instead of leaving, the guy lingered.

"Thank you. Ah, you're Gunwoo-ssi, right? Is it okay if I call you that?"

"Yes."

"Oh~ You're refreshingly straightforward!"

And he grinned.

"Your performance was amazing today, and I had a lot of fun watching it. Thank you~"

"No, thank you..."

*Give me a moment to get myself together, will you?*

"Thank you for your kind words."

"Hey, I meant it. I also saw Gunwoo's first performance, and it was really good. Seriously, I think you're going to debut!"

*Does this guy have his memories back? Is he testing me right now?*

*That can't be possible.*

He probably just wanted to make a connection because he thought I had potential. Wasn't it obvious?

*That hasn't changed.*

I unfolded my arms.

*I'll draw your name and awaken you when I'm done gathering reputation anyway, so do whatever you want.*

I decided I should compliment him a little, too.

Well, it wasn't like I was going to make things up.

"Thank you. I also learned a lot from watching sunbaenim perform. Every movement and expression suited the song perfectly, and I took inspiration from that."

"Ah."

The guy suddenly seemed to withdraw into himself.

*He didn't expect it?*

But he soon spoke again as smoothly as if nothing had happened.

"Thank you. That's encouraging~ Well, then, since this must be fate, shall we exchange numbers?"

The exact same thing had happened in ⟨Idol Inc.⟩.

It filled me with a strange sense of nostalgia rather than bitterness. I shrugged.

"I don't have my phone right now... If it's okay with you, I'll give you my number."

"Yeah, that's good!"

I put my number into his smartphone. I felt strange.

"I'll text you later~ Let's keep in touch!"

"Yes. Then I'll go inside and get ready. Thank you."

"Mhm, yes."

I just turned away, as if I didn't notice that he wanted to talk more.

*I have to organize my thoughts.*

He had always been a talkative guy. I felt like I'd slip up the moment I relaxed while talking to him now.

Anyway, I'd been polite to him, so he probably wouldn't feel offended...

*So things between us seem to be unfolding similarly here as well.*

For networking, that was. Our relationship had started for purely practical reasons: gaining mutually beneficial connections.

I grinned, picked up my pace, and turned the corner of the hallway.

I stopped short just before colliding with someone on the other side of the corner. It was Cheongryeo.

"So you were here. You've wandered pretty far, haven't you?"

"It wasn't intentional. It just happened somehow."

"It seems intentional to me... Ah, as expected."

He glanced around the corner and saw Lee Sejin walking away. Then he muttered languidly.

"I noticed you were gone. I'll tell you in advance—it'll be hard to recruit that one. He's already debuted, and he's in his third year."

*Yeah. I already know that.*

"We just exchanged numbers. Nothing more."

"Hmm, are you thinking of trying to persuade him by explaining what the situation is?"

"......"

"You've done that to him before."

*Explain everything to him out of the blue?*

For a moment, I thought it might actually be possible, but I soon thought better of it...

"No, the performance comes first."

What made an unreasonable thing unreasonable wasn't that it was impossible. It was that nobody did it, even though it was possible.

I had no intention of telling him anything unnecessary until he awakened.

"Is that so? Good decision."

*Way to state the obvious.*

I returned to the practice room to continue working on the performance.

And a few days later...

Finally, filming for the first battle on this program before a live audience had begun.

"It's nice to be here~"

Xyrop's Lee Sejin, who had returned as a special judge, greeted the audience warmly and sat down.

Among the idols who'd come for the interim evaluation, those who were less busy were invited back, and it didn't feel particularly great to have earned a spot here simply by being less busy.

Nevertheless, he was interested in the performances.

Watching those who were likely to become his competitors was bound to be beneficial. Plus, there was someone he'd just exchanged numbers with.

Ryu Gunwoo.

The trainee from the jazz performance.

Lee Sejin frowned slightly, pretending to wrinkle his nose.

*There was something a little strange about that conversation.*

Originally, he'd thought Gunwoo was particularly talented and eye-catching, so he'd decided it would be worth making a connection.

But when they actually started talking...

*I guess I should say that I just felt like I wanted to become friends with him.*

Why? It was like he wanted to reconnect with an old friend he'd somehow grown apart from.

*Did I know him before?*

*Well, maybe he was a friend I used to play with in the neighborhood when I was young.*

*No, maybe I knew him because he was older than me? Like a neighborhood hyung?*

*Oh, I don't know.*

Lee Sejin always put aside questions that had no immediate answer.

*He hasn't replied yet anyway.*

After this battle was over, he could try to figure out what was going on by texting him.

Even though Gunwoo seemed a little aloof, he was clearly talented, so Lee Sejin didn't regret approaching him. He sat up straight in his seat.

Filming was about to begin.

"The third battle of ⟨Wise⟩ begins... now!"

At the president's grandiose announcement, the set was quickly made ready.

And what unfolded were performances worthy of a debut group from a major agency.

*...It's annoying.*

Lee Sejin made sure to hide his boiling emotions.

A competitive urge to outperform the rookies, and a sense of crisis.

Then he began to rank the teams.

*Groups that are worse than Xyrop, and groups that are on par with Xyrop...*

*Well, just because they're good at performing doesn't mean they'll be popular.*

Wasn't that what had happened to LeTi's female idol group, Marshmallow, who never really took off despite their solid skills? They'd gone with a cheesy concept instead.

But he had to take into account that half of these contestants would be cut to form the final team...

*Ah... Damn it.*

Lee Sejin decided that he had to find a way to motivate his group to improve somehow.

Dealing with people was the area he was most confident in. It exhausted him to realize that he seemed to spend more time worrying about things like this than his actual performances.

His gaze darkened, but the lights over the stage offset it somewhat.

"And now, the concept keyword for the next battle!"

Some time passed, and the battle he'd been looking forward to the most arrived.

The keyword appeared on the electronic display board.

+=
#><[Sexy]><#
=+

*Wow!*

The audience groaned, cheered, and screamed, though it was hard to tell whether they were lamenting or just excited.

*Ah~ The moment I saw the concept, I knew they'd go all in.*

Lee Sejin suppressed a bitter smile and gave an appropriately cheerful reaction for a third-year idol. And then the first team's performance began.

$p
♪ *Get on your knees! Woo!* ♫
p$

The tightly synchronized choreography, auto-tune, and body waves practically screamed lethal charm.

*Hmm.*

*Well, I suppose there's demand for this...*

Lee Sejin prepared something to say in case he was asked to comment. Since the cameras were on, he jotted down a few suitable keywords.

Then he roughly predicted how people would react to the next and final performance.

*...That team.*

He'd had his eye on them since the interim evaluation. Their team included many of the most promising contestants on the show.

Including Ryu Gunwoo, with whom he'd exchanged numbers.

*There's a reason why they're going last.*

He'd looked them up and found that spoilers were circulating online and everyone was looking forward to the performance.

Most of the audience members sitting there right now would be busy using KakaoTalk or social media, trying to guess the members of this team by remembering which contestants 'hadn't appeared yet'.

And the moment their names appeared on the electronic display board...

+=
[Shin Jaehyeon, Kim Raebin, Jung Woodan, Cha Eugene]

[+ Ryu Gunwoo (in jeopardy)]
=+

*Argh! Yeaaaah!*

There was a mix of indescribable screams and moans along with tremendous cheers.

The fans, seeing for the first time that Ryu Gunwoo was marked as in jeopardy, were taken aback but still excited about the team itself.

*Everyone will end up going crazy.*

He could already see how successful this would be. Lee Sejin smiled bitterly, picturing exactly how the agency would promote this episode. Their song was by a top male idol group and had been released two years ago.

Wild, masculine, and sexy.

It was a concept similar to the previous team's.

*They're going to win.*

If the concepts were similar, the team with the better skills was bound to win. He'd reached that conclusion as soon as he saw the interim evaluation.

*...They were good.*

He'd only seen the first verse, but that had been more than enough. Now he focused intently on the performance.

The contestants stepped onto the stage and gathered into a flower bud-shaped formation. Colorless lights flickered on the stage.

$p
♪ *Cut off.* ♫
p$

A low voice began to sing, accompanied by an elegant instrumental.

"...!"

The lights turned purple, and the members moved smoothly.

Their upper bodies moved in broad, flowing waves.

Lee Sejin realized it right away.

*They changed the arrangement a little bit.*

Some instruments were missing, including the electric guitar. The bass and strings had been boosted in volume.

And the movements...

Instead of being as energetic as the original, their movements were relaxed and leisurely. Their velvet shirts and black pants suited the movements well.

♪ *Cut it off and let it go*\
*This is your way*\
*(So sick)* ♫

As Kim Raebin finished the intro, sweeping his hair back, Shin Jaehyeon smoothly took over with an elegant hand gesture.

There was no group choreography here. They focused instead on their individual movements and facial expressions.

Yet the audience was watching it intently.

*That's a difficult thing to pull off.*

A performance that seemed to have no set choreography, with deliberate gaps in the movement. Making it look full despite that was difficult even for professional performers.

And that wasn't all there was to it.

♪ *Don't look back.*\
*It'll make you sad. It's pointless*\
*You lost your game*\
*It's time to move on* ♫

An instrumental break began, accompanied by a synchronized group dance. The difficult choreography interlocked perfectly like gears, creating synergy.

*Heek.*

The surrounding arms and legs moved in sync with the person standing in the center, forming a flower bud around him.

In this way, the members interacted like props, drawing attention to the member at the center.

By alternating smoothly between tightly choreographed and relaxed sections, they satisfied the audience on both fronts to maximum effect.

$p
♪ *The story's already over* ♫
p$

By the time the second verse was in full swing, Lee Sejin came to a conclusion.

*They're clever.*

The performance was of an absurdly high quality. It was a choice only incredibly skilled performers could pull off, but with a team like this, he would have gladly chosen it himself.

"......"

The stage was lively.

He brushed those thoughts aside and focused again on the performance, which had completely transformed since the interim evaluation.

Then a question arose in his mind.

*Was the arrangement really the only reason?*

"...!"

And then he realized it.

Cha Eugene.

Cha Eugene had been the main reason the performance felt so wild, just like the original track, during the interim evaluation. He'd brought that intense energy and explosive excitement to the performance.

However, here, it felt like that explosive intensity had been toned down.

*But that doesn't mean he isn't standing out.*

*Take the final part of the chorus of the second verse, for example.*

$p
♪ *Love is a kind of disease*\
*I realized it too late*\
*So—* ♫
p$

Cha Eugene stared into the camera with relaxed, half-lidded eyes as someone grabbed his neck.

By holding back the force of his usual intense gaze, he heightened the mood.

A cold, subdued charm.

Cha Eugene had adapted so perfectly that he could almost pass for someone who'd been a LeTi trainee from the start.

It was amazing that he could control it at all, and at the same time, it was a very strategic choice.

The viewers would find this to be another charming side of him. Even LeTi's president, the one making the decisions here, was clearly reconsidering his stance.

Moreover, the performance had solid support.

$p
♪ *So, oh, oh, oh—* ♫
p$

Its backbone was Ryu Gunwoo.

He was nailing those incredibly high notes live with perfect timing.

*I can feel that the audience is surprised.*

But if that were all he could do, he might have seemed like nothing more than a flashy stage prop.

But he knew how to make himself stand out in other ways, too.

*Here it comes.*

It was the so-called 'saintess' part.

It was a section that suddenly created a reverent atmosphere and drew all the attention to the singer.

The notes weren't extremely high, just moderately so.

In other words, it was extremely difficult for anyone to sing that part steadily.

$p
♪ *It's done*\
*It's over*\
*Cut it off* ♫
p$

Ryu Gunwoo delivered his lines in a dreamy, breathy tone, almost as if he were whispering.

His rhythm was unbelievably clean, especially for someone who had been throwing out those insane high-pitched ad-libs just moments earlier.

It would have been easier to just scream at the highest pitch possible.

*How is he able to sing for so long without taking a single breath? And without a backing track?*

$p
♪ *Cut it off and let it go*\
*This is your way*\
*(So sick)* ♫
p$

Ryu Gunwoo absolutely nailed that one killing point of the song.

He'd also raised the quality of the performance by several levels with his vocals.

He was practically a textbook example of why a group needed a main vocalist.

"......"

Lee Sejin suddenly felt a strange sense of déjà vu.

*Why?*

However, the performance rushed past the bridge toward the end.

Every member played their part perfectly, leaving the audience endlessly pleased.

And the ending...

$p
♪ *Cut off.* ♫
p$

*Ahhhhh!!*

There was so much loud cheering and applause that it felt like his ears were going to bleed. Shining lightsticks waved throughout the audience.

Lee Sejin watched the stage intently without looking away until the end.

He still felt a strong desire not to be outdone by them, but he no longer felt threatened.

Instead, he felt something different brewing in his chest...

*...Ah.*

It was a sense of loss he couldn't quite put into words.

That strange feeling that someone had just taken his place...

*What is that even supposed to mean?*

*What's wrong with me today? Did I eat something wrong?* Lee Sejin bit the inside of his cheek and struggled to keep the smile on his face.

And when it was his turn to comment, he opened his mouth with a grin.

"That was amazing! Really!"

That very day, Episode 3 of ⟨Wise⟩ aired before the audience members could spread spoilers about the in-jeopardy contestants who'd lost to members of the supplementary group.

And finally, viewer voting began.
