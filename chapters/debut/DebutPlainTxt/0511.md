# Chapter 511

STier's Lee Sejin.

The Bae Sejin who did not change his name.

The System I was digging through contained the memory of the man tried and appealed for past drug distribution charges.

*...I need to calm down.*

This was not something to be surprised about.

My experience suggested the System stored past life memories like a backup.

*I've already seen memories from the STier days return several times, so why would I be surprised now?*

I had even regained memories of my time as a company employee while beating up the System.

The problem was this.

*Is this a situation where it's okay for me to talk to him?*

This was not just a memory. I could have a conversation with it. The potential effects were a complete unknown.

Normally, I would have backed out without touching it, but this was a special situation.

I had swapped bodies just to forcibly access the System.

*I'd be an idiot to just let this go.*

Trying everything I could was to my advantage.

[Lee Sejin: I asked who you are.]

Just then, the man sent another message. He had somehow recognized my presence.

"......"

Right.

I touched the mass of information like a terminal. Then, just as Keundal sent messages to 'Park Moondae', I slowly sent the words.

I started with an expression the other side could easily understand.

[I am a member of the same group as the current you.]

A reply struck back instantly the moment I sent it.

[Lee Sejin A: STier?]

The data was unresponsive for a while.

A significant pause filled the space, as if a person were hesitating. Then, a reply finally came.

It was cautious.

[Lee Sejin A: Ryu Chungwoo?]

"......"

*Strictly speaking, I guess two of those syllables are roughly correct.*

[I'm not Chungwoo. I'm probably not anyone you're thinking of.]

Just as I was about to continue my explanation, a sharp reply arrived first.

[Lee Sejin A: I'm not in STier anymore, and STier never recruited a new member.]

[Lee Sejin A: If we were in the same group, there's no way I wouldn't know. State your identity specifically.]

*...!*

This man knew the situation in more detail than I thought.

*It means he has enough background knowledge to argue logically.*

It was strange. Why would he care about news of his former group after leaving and going through a legal battle? Normally, it would be hard to care at all.

The fact that he knew such details meant he had looked it up himself.

I deliberately poked at that point.

[Have you been keeping up with news about STier?]

[Lee Sejin A: You answer first.]

He was solid.

If anything, he felt less sensitive than he did during ❬Idol Inc.❭. His wariness was higher, but he did not fluster easily.

*Hmm.*

In that case, this should be fine.

I decided to lay out the situation honestly.

[I'm not from STier.]

[Lee Sejin A: What?]

[Right now, you didn't debut with STier but with a group called TeSTAR. I'm a member of that group.]

Then I tried something new.

*It doesn't have to be in the form of a message.*

Instead of refining it into words, I would transmit the information I wanted to convey in its entirety.

Information about the situation I had experienced so far.

I focused my consciousness while in contact with the terminal.

[Lee Sejin A: 'Hyung'? What does that mean...]

In that moment, the things I had experienced flowed into the terminal, raw and unorganized.

[Lee Sejin A: !!]

*It's an unbelievable story, so this is more efficient.*

The pressure was intense but bearable. I pushed the information to its breaking point, then pulled my consciousness from the terminal.

'Lee Sejin A' seemed to silently accept the information I sent, offering no other reply.

Enough time passed for him to digest it.

[Lee Sejin A: So.]

[Lee Sejin A: You're saying I debuted... in a different group.]

A reply came back.

*It worked.*

Although I almost bit my tongue in the process.

I gathered my focus and answered.

[Yes.]

[Lee Sejin A: Then... how am I living now?]

[You're doing well as part of the group, and you changed your name to your mother's surname. To Bae Sejin.]

This meant the problem that made him leave the group never happened, and the issue with his blood relatives was cleanly resolved.

A blank space that felt more like a void than silence passed before a reply returned.

[Lee Sejin A: So he's living well.]

It was a remarkably composed reaction.

[He's living diligently.]

[Lee Sejin A: I was never lazy either, but living diligently and living well are two different things.]

"......"

I hesitated for a moment, then decided to ask him outright.

It would be ridiculous to pretend I did not know at this point.

[Do you think you didn't live well?]

The former child actor, the former idol whose career was ruined by drug allegations, who had held on while appealing his case.

[Lee Sejin A: Not necessarily. It's not like there was no one who believed in me.]

[Lee Sejin A: But I won't deny that the vast majority of people didn't believe me.]

*He's calm.*

Honestly, it would not have been strange if he had shown more mental anguish or even attacked me for asking. His situation more than warranted it.

However, the STier Bae Sejin, designated 'Lee Sejin A', maintained a firm tone without a hint of agitation.

*Is it because he's just data and can't be shaken?*

No, that was not it.

If that were the case, the feeling of such a detailed conversation could not be conveyed like this.

My question was soon answered.

[Lee Sejin A: Anyway, I get it.]

[Lee Sejin A: Then is this conversation... like a dream that the 'me' who already escaped this situation is having?]

[A dream?]

[Lee Sejin A: Yes. If I were just a memory, there's no way I could be talking to you like this.]

It was an interesting hypothesis.

*Certainly... the current Bae Sejin is probably asleep right now.*

Based on that Bae Sejin's consciousness, the memory of this STier Bae Sejin could emerge from the mass of information and hold a conversation.

The underlying principle was the same as the previous STier cases.

*In any case, the fact that he isn't panicking and just saying he's a memory is extraordinary.*

Frankly, I had predicted that STier's Bae Sejin would be a considerably twisted person. From his child actor days, not a single thing had gone right for him, as if his environment had conspired to drag him to rock bottom.

*After going through shit like that, anyone's personality would naturally be ruined.*

However, this man I was talking to now had the air of a seasoned veteran who had been through it all.

[Aren't you resentful?]

[Lee Sejin A: I was.]

[Lee Sejin A: But I also learned that just being resentful doesn't solve anything.]

Aspects like this.

He was incredibly solid.

Moreover, the man even made a counter-proposal.

[Lee Sejin A: Your situation doesn't seem good right now. If you have any questions, ask. I'll answer.]

He was more cooperative than I could have imagined.

*I can't miss this chance.*

I was about to quickly formulate my questions when a better idea came to mind.

*Hmm.*

If he was this calm and mentally resilient, this method would be better.

[How about this?]

I explained my proposal. Lee Sejin A seemed to ponder it for a moment before readily agreeing.

[Lee Sejin A: Yes. That sounds good.]

Okay.

I immediately grabbed his terminal.

Then I began to tear it away from the System.

Pressure swept over my body.

~~~

Late at night, at the TeSTAR dorm.

Keundal sat on the living room sofa, so anxious he was practically biting his nails. He finally received the pop-up he had been waiting for.

[I'm out of the System now]

"......"

It was a message from TeSTAR's Park Moondae, who had just come out of the System.

Finally!

*Thank goodness!*

Keundal sighed in relief, wiped away his cold sweat, and shouted in the lowest voice he could manage.

"Take your body back now. No, just don't ever go back into the System, for now!"

[Hold on. I'd like to keep the bodies as they are until tomorrow morning, is that okay?]

"...?"

[I have something to do.]

[I'm heading over. Get some sleep.]

"......"

A few minutes later, Keundal unexpectedly greeted Ryu Gunwoo, who silently punched in the passcode and slipped into the TeSTAR dorm.

*Hyung!?*

[I told you to get some sleep.]

TeSTAR's Park Moondae, who had been quietly keeping his mouth shut.

His hyung, now back in Ryu Gunwoo's body, seemed to keep his mouth shut to avoid waking the other members, replying silently.

He strode into the living room and said.

[I'm going to get ready, so you rest.]

*G-Get ready for what?*

Ryu Gunwoo's face looked at Keundal with a blank expression, and a pop-up appeared.

[A conversation.]

He recalled his conversation with STier's Bae Sejin inside the System.

[Do you have any interest in talking to someone else directly?]

[Lee Sejin A: I'd like that.]

About an hour later, that conversation would actually take place.

~~~

Ryu Chungwoo woke up to a hand shaking him.

His mind was groggy, and the room was dark.

*Is it still dawn?*

He did not feel particularly annoyed at being forcibly woken up.

He had lived like that for years. It was something you had to get used to if you worked in the entertainment industry.

However, he could not help but be startled the moment he saw an unfamiliar person in the dorm.

"...!"

A man with a cold impression wore glasses.

His appearance resembled Ryu Chungwoo's in some way.

"Who..."

"Ryu Gunwoo."

"Ah."

It was a name he had to remember.

Sleep vanished instantly as he quickly sat up in bed.

Ryu Gunwoo was the person TeSTAR's Park Moondae had revealed to be his previous life.

*And my relative.*

Ryu Gunwoo quietly gestured for him to follow.

"...?"

"Follow me."

Ryu Chungwoo did not object and quietly followed him.

The destination was the room across the hall.

It was Park Moondae and Kim Raebin's room.

"H-Hyung-nim."

"Oh."

Kim Raebin and Cha Eugene were already awake, visible in the light of a desk lamp.

The moment Ryu Chungwoo saw the lineup, blood rushed from his head to his toes.

*It's just us.*

Everyone with memories of STier.

He raised his tension but did not let it show, asking as if slightly bewildered.

"Um... what's going on?"

"We swapped bodies for a bit."

Without needing to ask if such a thing was possible, Ryu Chungwoo instinctively realized that he was TeSTAR's Park Moondae.

His tone, gaze, gestures, and even the length of his stride were a perfect match.

*Come to think of it, he did say the person who was originally Park Moondae was using his body right now.*

Once he recalled the general explanation, Ryu Chungwoo accepted the situation. Refusing to accept it would do him no good.

It was a bit suspicious and awkward, but there was no need to voice it.

So instead, he replied in a manner befitting the Leader of TeSTAR, Ryu Chungwoo.

"There's no problem, right? It's strange seeing you like this, Hyung... but your safety is more important."

"There's no problem, and thanks for your concern," Ryu Gunwoo replied as if he had chosen the correct answer.

However.

"However, this isn't us 'seeing each other again'."

The words took a calm turn.

"Because you don't remember Ryu Gunwoo."

"......"

Ryu Chungwoo responded without showing any agitation.

"Um, I guess I didn't recognize you for a moment because so much time has passed. Sorry."

"Right. That could happen."

Ryu Gunwoo nodded easily.

"But you flinched when I answered that I was 'Ryu Gunwoo'."

"...!"

As if you had made a mistake.

In a word, he was conscious of the fact that he had not recognized Ryu Gunwoo.

Because it was a deliberate lie.

"Was it true, what you said about remembering Ryu Gunwoo?"

"......"

After a moment of silence, Ryu Chungwoo soon let out a bitter smile.

"No."

"......"

"I saw a video in my smartphone's photo album. I was wondering why a video of a past family trip, centered around a relative I don't even remember, was saved with a recent date."

Back when TeSTAR's Ryu Chungwoo was looking for Ryu Gunwoo at Park Moondae's request.

He had saved the scenes of 'Ryu Gunwoo' from his family trip video, which Park Moondae had recognized, onto his own smartphone, just in case it might be useful.

"So I memorized it... and when you asked, it just came to mind, so I said it."

It was a calm explanation that it was not intentional.

It was convincing enough, but Ryu Gunwoo did not fall for it.

*So he meticulously went through every single thing in TeSTAR Ryu Chungwoo's phone gallery.*

He deduced that Chungwoo had deliberately said he remembered to make it easier to settle in here.

He did not point it out.

"I'm sorry if that felt deceptive."

Ryu Chungwoo apologized meekly, just as TeSTAR's Ryu Chungwoo would.

However, at the same time, he saw Kim Raebin flinch at his gentle tone.

An ambivalent emotion, a little pitiful but also sharply on guard in case he said something unnecessary.

*It can't be helped, though.*

It was not a pleasant feeling. Ryu Chungwoo thought with a bit of self-deprecation, clenching and unclenching his fist.

Ryu Gunwoo heard the apology and nodded calmly.

"I don't think it was deceptive, but that's not what I was trying to talk about."

Ryu Chungwoo felt a brief sense of relief, but Ryu Gunwoo soon dropped a bombshell.

"Actually, what I have to say here isn't that important."

"...?"

"Because I'm here as a proxy."

He began to stare into the empty air.

"From now on, I will relay that person's words."

Invisible to the others, a new message pop-up existed, one he had 'torn from' the System and 'implemented as a Status Window'.

[Lee Sejin A: It's been a while.]

"I am Lee Sejin. The Lee Sejin who was in STier."

He began to read the contents of the message pop-up.

A chilling silence descended upon the room.

"What are you,"

"Y-Yes...?"

Cutting through the STier members' questioning words, Ryu Gunwoo's voice rang out.

"I've come here today. Because I have something to tell you."

[Lee Sejin A: I doubt you're curious about my thoughts, and there's no need for you to be, so I don't plan on saying anything else.]

[Lee Sejin A: But there is one thing I have to say. Because there's something I remember clearly.]

Ryu Gunwoo looked at Lee Sejin A's message pop-up and relayed its contents, enunciating each word clearly.

"I won the case."

In that instant, everyone in the room lifted their heads.

Among the dazed, shocked faces, STier's Bae Sejin spoke through Ryu Gunwoo's mouth as if making a declaration.

"I appealed the second trial, and in the final third trial that went all the way to the Supreme Court... I won."

The message declared, word for word.

[Lee Sejin A: Which means STier is not a drug group.]
