# Chapter 570

The next morning, TeSTAR's vehicle was on the way to a scheduled event.

"Moondae...! Um, the manager is asking what we want to do for breakfast..."

"......"

"M-Moondae...?"

"...Yeah. Anything is fine."

"...Okay."

I rested my head against the window. Calculations ran furiously through my brain about this group's future activities.

*We're at a crossroads.*

The problem was that both paths led to a cliff's edge.

If we release an intense full album now, the public who expected easy-listening music will turn away. However, if we continue down the easy-listening path, we won't be able to stop the fandom from leaving.

*And that's the end of the Order of Cultural Merit, too.*

It was hell.

If I had to filter the downsides and pick something, there was the hybrid option of easy-listening music with intense choreography, like our debut song, ❬Magical Boy❭.

*But that won't get a reaction from overseas.*

What works for global K-pop fans is always an intense concept. Group choreography, intense beats, and a strong melody are essential.

Of course, to gain the Western public's support, one could target the easy-listening trends popular there. However, that was a method for a higher class of artists. It was only possible after securing a global fanbase on the scale of VTIC. If we weren't careful, we could lose our group's color and struggle with the next album.

Just like now.

*Fuck.*

Since the fandom had already started to leave, even this 'easy-listening with high-level choreography' approach would likely be seen as us 'testing the waters.'

[It was their turn for something intense, but seeing them stubbornly cling to easy-listening, the writing's on the wall lol]

They would take it as a trend leaning towards 'We don't do intense idol stuff anymore.'

I pressed the bridge of my nose.

*I don't care about getting hate.*

The problem was the significant number of fans who wouldn't complain and would just quietly leave, saying, 'I had fun being a fan, I'm leaving now~'.

This ominous feeling wouldn't go away, especially after seeing that rookie co-ed group succeed with an absurd strategy. It was a difficult time to maintain our position.

*The way groups get popular is changing.*

It felt like a generational shift was approaching.

"......"

In fact, TeSTAR's seniority might not have been the issue. With the new year, we were only in our seventh year. It had only been about five and a half years since our debut.

*There are plenty of groups senior to TeSTAR who are active and in their prime right now.*

The problem was that TeSTAR had been successful from the very beginning. Large-scale concerts, tours, charting on annual music charts, and breaking rookie group album sales records all happened within our first year.

Until now, barring special circumstances like a car accident, we had consistently broken our own records, drawing an upward-trending graph.

*So I thought we had managed things well, alternating between variety shows and albums so people wouldn't get tired of TeSTAR...*

In reality, the problem might have been the complete opposite.

[It wouldn't be strange for TeSTAR to think they've done enough now]

[Aren't they probably losing interest in group activities themselves?]

These were the kinds of perceptions. Even if people weren't tired of TeSTAR, they predicted that TeSTAR was tired of TeSTAR's activities. Just as market expectations are pre-emptively reflected in stock prices, people were already looking for other idols to replace TeSTAR in advance.

"Um, Moondae."

I turned my head.

"......"

Seon Ahyeon held out a warmly wrapped sandwich toast. It seemed the manager had gotten us breakfast.

"Thanks."

I carelessly tore open the wrapping and brought the sandwich to my mouth. I didn't have the luxury of tasting it.

Meanwhile, a quiet voice asked from beside me.

"Um... by any chance. Are you sick, or... are you worried about something?"

"......"

"You just... seem to be having a hard time."

*Should I tell him?*

Seon Ahyeon was a good listener. He would listen intently if I brought up a problem like this and then fret and worry about it with me. No, most of the guys in this group were like that. So...

I opened my mouth.

"It's because I worked overtime early yesterday morning."

"...?"

"I was looking up the rookies who beat us for number one on the music charts."

I pointed a finger at two of them.

"Along with them."

"Wha?"

Keun Sejin and Cha Eugene, who were stuffing their own toasts into their mouths, blinked. Of course, both of them were perfectly fine. They didn't have the kind of stamina to get tired from lack of sleep, nor were they the types to show it.

In that sense, this situation where I was the only one saying I was tired...

*Is this weird?*

Cha Eugene opened his mouth with a pitiful look.

"Hoo... Moondae-hyung, is it because you're old now?"

"......"

*There's only a two-year difference between us, you punk.*

He had been like this ever since the new year started.

"Is that toast good?"

Realizing he might be stuck chewing on toast for dinner too, Cha Eugene flinched and quickly corrected himself. He really was quick-witted.

"I made a mistake! There are hyungs older than Moondae-hyung! Bae Sejin-hyung is the oldest!"

"......"

Bae Sejin, who was suddenly thrown under the bus, wore a vacant expression. Keun Sejin, who almost burst out laughing, smoothly changed the subject.

"Hey, what are you talking about! In Korea, the international age is the official one! We've still got plenty of our twenties left~"

"A reasonable statement!"

The guys chattered boisterously while eating their warm breakfast and didn't press me about my condition anymore.

I went back to chewing my own toast.

"......"

This was just right.

*There's no need to blabber on about my predictions.*

Get ready to face a decline no matter what we do? The Order of Cultural Merit is a lost cause, so let's just prepare for our joint enlistment next year?

*Saying it would only scare them.*

I couldn't shatter their morale when I should be boosting it. I was satisfied with having appropriately lightened the team's mood.

"Wow, Park Moondae acted all cute for the camera during the New Year's countdown, and now his stamina is at a cute level too..."

That was that.

I sneakily took Keun Sejin's unopened can of sparkling water, shook it mercilessly, and then quietly returned it to its original spot.

Cha Eugene giggled and recorded the scene on his phone.

"Wah! What is this!"

"Hahaha!"

And so, my suspicious behavior seemed to be buried and passed over by the loud, heartwarming atmosphere.

However, even in this chaos, one person remained silent, lost in thought. There was one guy there who was well-versed in dealing with a team member who predicted a pessimistic future.

"Moondae. Can we talk for a moment?"

"......"

That afternoon, the moment I entered the waiting room, I realized Ryu Chungwoo had been waiting to talk to me.

~~~

I went with him into the inner room of the waiting area, where there was a cot. Instead of taking a nap, we both sat on the edge of the mattress.

"Ahyeon kept worrying. He said you weren't doing well today and wondered if you'd overexerted yourself early this morning."

I see. It seemed that even though I tried not to show it, the complexity in my head had leaked out. I sighed and nodded.

"As I said, it's just because I'm tired."

"Really?"

"......"

"Moondae. Right now, I'm not asking as your cousin, but as the group's Leader asking a member."

Ah, damn it.

I pressed my eyelids before opening my mouth.

"Just keep this between us, hyung."

And so, I began to lay out my analysis of TeSTAR's current situation to Ryu Chungwoo. A time of dilemma. How the song we made because I insisted 'let's do what we want to do' had ended up with this kind of Butterfly Effect.

"...So, I don't think we can avoid the image of being in decline, no matter when or how we release the next album."

Ryu Chungwoo wasn't shocked. Nor did he scoff at it as nonsense. He just listened quietly, nodding his head. So I was able to finish what I had to say in peace.

"...I haven't thought of a solution yet, so I was keeping my mouth shut for now."

"I see."

"......"

"I heard you out. Thanks for telling me."

Ryu Chungwoo smiled faintly.

"But... from my perspective, it doesn't seem like a big problem."

"......"

What?

"Moondae."

Ryu Chungwoo clasped his hands together and spoke gently.

"It's okay if TeSTAR gets an image of being in decline."

*What is this crazy talk all of a sudden?*

"That won't suddenly make our group disappear or all our fans vanish."

Did he not understand?

"I'm saying there's a high probability that fans will leave because of that image, leading to a real decline."

"Still, if we release another good album, they'll listen. If we put on a great performance, they'll watch."

"Yes, but only some of them."

People fundamentally pay less attention to things that are past their prime. TeSTAR will be in the same boat.

"Right. That our prime is passing... but Moondae."

Ryu Chungwoo gave a bitter smile.

"A prime is called a prime because it passes."

"...!"

Those words were somehow familiar. They were what I had said to Cheongryeo when he was suffering from Reset Syndrome.

[A prime is called a prime because it passes.]

That's right. Every group, no matter how successful, eventually faces a moment of stagnation. Whether it's T-Holic, VTIC, or TeSTAR. Whether it's a few years from now, or right now.

"Even if we receive the Order of Cultural Merit, it's just delaying the inevitable, and everyone except me and Eugene will have to go to the military."

"......"

"When that hiatus comes, you'll worry that our prime is over then, too, right?"

Even while adding, 'I haven't mentioned it until now because it's awkward for me to bring it up as someone who isn't going,' Ryu Chungwoo continued to speak.

"Still, we'll just make our activity plans according to that, won't we?"

His words were calm and logical.

"Of course, it would be great if our results were always good. However, even if our results drop, it's not the end of this group."

[TeSTAR can continue to release albums as much as they want and promote well, and there will always be people who listen.]

I quietly took in his words, sending them to my brain and slowly digesting them.

*I see.*

It wasn't like I was afflicted with some status effect like 'Grand Prize or Death,' but I had been thinking as if I were.

*As if TeSTAR would die if it wasn't a top-tier group.*

However, that wasn't true. This group will continue to exist. It's not going to suddenly disband just because our results aren't good. We're the ones who founded the company, after all.

TeSTAR will not suddenly disband.

*Right.*

I cleanly admitted to myself that I had been excessively buried in one way of thinking. After taking a deep breath, I responded with a much more organized mind.

"I understand. ...Thank you, hyung."

"Nothing to thank me for."

Perhaps because my complexion had improved, Ryu Chungwoo grinned as if relieved.

However.

"Still, I feel like crap."

"......"

"And I don't think I'll be able to sleep if we face a decline because of a misunderstanding like this."

Isn't it ridiculously unfair?

*It's not like we had a controversy, and it's not like the album quality was shit.*

Who would have known that a song released just for fan service would suddenly do well and lead to this kind of misunderstanding? Especially when we've already laid out the entire annual plan to climb even higher...

"Honestly, I don't see an immediate answer, but I want to find a solution somehow."

At that, Ryu Chungwoo grinned.

"Yeah. I knew you'd say that!"

"...?"

"Ah, sorry. Um... to be honest, even though I said all that, I was hoping you'd still want to give it your all and keep challenging yourself."

Ryu Chungwoo's smile faded slightly.

"Because not being able to do your best isn't a pleasant feeling either..."

"......"

It seemed he was recalling his time in STier. However, Ryu Chungwoo quickly erased that expression.

"But let's remember this."

He held out his hand.

"First of all... it's not your fault that the fans have this misunderstanding."

"...!"

Let's just accept that sometimes, unexpected things happen, Ryu Chungwoo said.

"...Thank you."

"Haha. It's the truth, so there's nothing to be thankful for. Hmm, and this next part is the most important."

Ryu Chungwoo smiled and grabbed my shoulder.

"We're not doing this because we're scared of our prime ending and just trying to do something, anything. We're doing it because we want to keep challenging ourselves."

The reason we're gritting our teeth, agonizing, and desperately pushing forward isn't because we'll die if we don't.

It's because we have a goal.

"We're not being chased, we're the ones chasing. Let's not forget that."

That was enough.

"Yes."

I shook his hand, acknowledging his words.

Alright, then...

"Let's call a meeting."

"Alright."

Let's have all seven of us squeeze our brains like lemons and create something, whether it works or not.

A solution.
