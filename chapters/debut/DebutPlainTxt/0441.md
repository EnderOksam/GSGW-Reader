# Chapter 441

"Hk."

I took a sharp breath, then exhaled. On the smartphone screen, Ryu Gunwoo's pale face moved with me.

*This is not a situation to be calm, but I have to sort this out as calmly as possible.*

First, Park Moondae and Ryu Gunwoo had swapped bodies. Second, Park Moondae was the main vocalist of a top-tier idol group with a comeback just around the corner.

However.

Third, Ryu Gunwoo, who was in Park Moondae's body, had no idol experience, comeback or not.

And here was the kicker.

Fourth, Park Moondae had a live radio broadcast in ten minutes.

"......"

This was insane. I had been through some crazy situations, but this one was truly nuts.

*A live broadcast standby in ten minutes, for real?*

My only hope was sharing Park Moondae's vision, just as Keundal once did.

*Forget the status abnormality. I have to get through this live broadcast somehow... Wait.*

*...Is Park Moondae's vision getting blurry?*

The radio waiting room looked hazy.

A bad feeling crept over me.*Shit. What if this is happening because I, a layman, connected instead of Keundal, the original Status Window?*

I could already hear the live broadcast going down the drain. I hurriedly opened the chat.

"Are you okay? There's a problem with the vision connection..."

[I-it's tears...]

"......"

[I'm sorry.]

*I understand.*

Through the damn, tear-filled vision, a few people walked quickly from the front of the radio booth.

*...It's the writers!*

[Hyung!! Hyung! They're telling me to come in!]

"Wait."

*This is no time to be sentimental.*

"Tell me your phone's lock pattern!"

[Uh, uh, our debut date! TeSTAR's debut date!]

*A male idol's debut date for a pattern? What a ridiculously non-intuitive choice.*

Anyway, I quickly thought.*The keypad has no '0', so that's out, but a three-digit pattern is too short. If I add the year...*

Five digits!

I connected the five numbers and lifted my finger. The screen slid open.

*There we go!*

I immediately downloaded the station's radio app. My plan was to use it to replace Park Moondae's hearing.

*This is better than trying to share Park Moondae's body and hearing and then messing up the live broadcast.*

Honestly, I had no idea how I was even sharing his vision right now.

Time to get to work.

I opened the app once the download was complete. In those few short seconds, I made, discarded, and reviewed plans.

I stood up from the bed.

"Listen carefully."

[Yes!]

"It's a surprise radio appearance before the comeback. The script is only five or six lines, and you can check it in real time. There are a lot of people, so only answer when a question is for Park Moondae."

"Otherwise, only speak when everyone answers together. When I give you a signal through the chat, speak slowly."

"You'll stall by using some interjections while I send you the answer to fill the silence."

But one thing was important.

"The interjection before your answer can't have a positive or negative nuance. You have to control that carefully."

The moment you slip up and sound sarcastic or wrong, you're screwed.

Once it's broadcast, it's over. Stay alert.

[Uuuhhhk...]

Keundal typed something like a death rattle into the chat. I held back a sigh.

*I shouldn't pressure him too much.*

Pressuring him too much would also ruin it.

"You probably don't have to worry about reflexive reactions. You don't have an aggressive personality."

But the chat wailed.

[Please! Just control me! I'll do all the reactions exactly as you say! Just as you type them, hyung! Please!?]

He could not have been more desperate.

But I could not accept that mutually beneficial offer.

"I can't do that."

[Yes...?]

I sat on the sofa and lowered my head.

"It's a viewable radio show."

[......]

That meant he had to control his facial muscles, too. Including all his reactions.

[Please save me.]

*Isn't that what I'm doing right now, trying to save you?*

I sighed.

"If you really can't do it, there is a way to avoid the broadcast itself."

[What is it!?]

"Just collapse on the floor right now."

It was the surest way to cancel a schedule. Getting carried away in an ambulance.

"......"

After a moment of silence.

[I'm sorry, I can't do it...]

*Yeah, I thought so.*

TeSTAR was already listening to the final briefing in front of the booth door. If he were shameless enough to collapse while sane, he would not have tried to save me in the first place.

"Nothing to be sorry about. Anyway, it must be hard for you to handle this alone right now."

[...Yes.]

"Alright."

I had already thought about it.

*An amateur with no broadcast experience thrown into a live show? He'll stand out with his unnatural tempo.*

*It's a bigger problem because he'll be compared to the on-air Park Moondae persona I've built.*

He needed an on-site assistant to back him up.

But telling all seven of them now would be a disaster. So... there was only one answer.

I pressed my cheek and opened my mouth.

"You're going to tell just one person."

***

The atmosphere before the booth was tense, but not overly so. This was natural after more than five years of TeSTAR's broadcast activities.

However, even the members who frequented variety shows were not completely relaxed.

For instance... like Lee Sejin.

He had already run through the script in his head and checked his lines.

And the next step was also important.

*Hmm, this part looks good too.*

He mentally noted the unassigned parts he could 'take for himself'.

It was a habit from the debut survival show that never disappeared. Taking his share.

TeSTAR as a group was indisputably huge, but as expected from a survival show group, the trend of comparing and ranking individual members had not vanished.

Besides...

*There's no reason not to, is there?*

Stage performances, photoshoots, producing, interviews... a member always stood out. I can say for sure that on this team, someone was always 'scoring points', no matter the activity.

Walking when others were running was the same as resting.

Lee Sejin would never do something as pathetic as missing a chance to score points. Not until he retired.

"Is there anything else you need?"

"Ah, we're fine~"

Still, by now, the other members besides Lee Sejin were also quite used to these kinds of talk-show broadcasts. He had to do even better.

*Well, it's good that the members are reliable.*

Lee Sejin recalled the waiting room. Everyone was proficiently managing their tension while they waited.

Just then, a small, crawling whisper reached Lee Sejin's ear.

"E-Excuse me."

It was a desperate, tear-filled voice.

For the record, it came from someone those two words should never describe.

Thanks to that, Lee Sejin turned his head in a reflex.

"...?"

He came face-to-face with Park Moondae, whose eyes were brimming with tears.

Lee Sejin got such goosebumps he almost jumped, but he held it in. The next words almost made him leap from his seat.

"I-I'm not Moondae-hyung..."

"......"

"My body suddenly swapped. I-I'm, uh, Keundal."

"......"

*Huh?*

"You know. The one in Gunwoo-hyung's body..."

He briefly considered if Park Moondae would pull such a prank four minutes and fifty seconds before a live broadcast, then immediately threw the thought away.

Meanwhile, the person in Park Moondae's body who claimed to be someone else earnestly explained the situation.

His words ended by explaining he was currently communicating with 'Moondae-hyung' through some magical method.

"Uh, as for how to speak on the radio, well, Moondae-hyung said he'd tell me..."

*Telepathy...*

A few cheesy movies and dramas flashed through his mind.

Still, perhaps because his past surreal experiences helped absorb the shock, something other than questions about the absurd situation came to mind. It was worry.

The last 'absurd incident' was a building collapse.

A chill ran down his spine as Lee Sejin opened his mouth.

"Is he okay?"

"Yes, he should be! I was in bed, about to sleep..."

Just as a brief sense of relief passed.

"And... ah, he asked me to tell you this..."

"...?"

A monotone sentence followed.

"First, don't tell anyone else, just you. You know what'll happen if the live broadcast is ruined...?"

"......"

*He's fine.*

*That's definitely Park Moondae.*

His vision swam.

And Keundal's desperate, mosquito-like speech ended like this.

"Please save me. Moondae-hyung said I just have to trust you on the radio, Sejin..."

*...Park Moondae!!*

Lee Sejin screamed internally, his expression unchanged.

Just then, Ryu Chungwoo turned his head from the front door.

"Is something wrong?"

Lee Sejin was briefly conflicted. However...

"...Ah, no! We should get going! Come on, let's nail this radio show too~"

*Park Moondae was right. The moment the conversation's tempo got tangled from sharing this, it would be hell.*

He gritted his teeth and smiled. Then he grabbed the scruff of Park Moondae's body's neck and cheerfully entered the booth.

However, Lee Sejin and Park Moondae had missed one thing.

Seon Ahyeon had been waiting right next to them. He was close enough to overhear most of the conversation.

It was a blind spot created by the combination of a live broadcast and a bizarre phenomenon.

"......"

"Ahyeon-hyung? It's time to enter the booth."

"O-Okay!"

Seon Ahyeon swallowed hard.

With a deathly pale face but determined steps, he followed them into the booth.

...And so, on Monday, January 9th, at 11:47 PM.

On that night, people were busy living their lives, frantically wrapping up the new year and the start of the week.

"Song DJ's Midnight Radio. It's Monday's specialty. We're back with the ❬Weekly Lucky❭ corner."

The show had just begun its final Monday segment.

"Today, we have some very special guests with us."

The middle-aged radio host smiled as she watched the seven handsome idols file in.

Her radio show, acclaimed for a soothing voice that comforted listeners at day's end, had not experienced such a commotion in a while.

*So young.*

TeSTAR entered, warmly responding to greetings from the directors outside the booth.

As befitting top stars successful for years, they all skillfully and naturally found their seats, smiling pleasantly.

"I'm sure our listeners will be very surprised."

She slowly looked over the table, checking their faces.

She had memorized all the guests' names. Surprisingly, all seven were so famous she did not need to study them.

*That blond young man is Cha Eugene, the spiky-haired one with a small ponytail is Kim Raebin, the pretty one in the hood is Bae Sejin...*

And the cute one to her right was Park Moondae, famous for his excellent composure... but.

For some reason, his hands trembled like a motorboat.

"...??"

The host almost rubbed her eyes, but her practiced mouth delivered the planned introduction.

"The winners of the Artist of the Year award at the Gold Disc Awards a few days ago, the undisputed kings of K-Pop, TeSTAR!"

When she looked again, his hands had disappeared beneath the table.

*Did I see it wrong?*

Naturally, Lee Sejin had nudged him with his elbow.

And so, the nerve-wracking live radio broadcast began.
