# Chapter 524

Kim Raebin had created songs in many genres since he first started composing at age 14. He wasn't picky and found something charming in every style.

[Raebin, we talked about you majoring in classical music yesterday... and you've composed a disco track?!]

[...Uh, student Raebin, I'm sorry, but are you sure you composed everything in this portfolio yourself?]

His comprehensiveness flustered everyone who ever taught or scouted him!

Even when his lack of theory resulted in less polished work, his songs were never bland. They always had a strong, distinct character.

After a period of focusing only on a song's completeness, he began considering external factors he couldn't control.

[Who will sing it?]

He learned to compose with the singer in mind, acquiring commercial viability. Kim Raebin enjoyed this process.

A song for 'the one who sings' was born through delicate crafting and adjustment, like fitting puzzle pieces into an exquisite picture. Kim Raebin found that very appealing.

He had never disliked it.

The problem was that the singer had never been solely himself! A song that considered only him!

*Uh, what should I do?*

Kim Raebin was completely flustered.

Of course, this wasn't his first time recording a solo song. More than half the B-sides on TeSTAR's full-length album, ❬Tiger-Hunting Officer❭, were solo tracks for each member.

However, that project involved five or six other producers besides Kim Raebin. His song was just one part of an organically connected album. It was like composing a long symphony and assigning one movement to himself.

He had never been so openly entrusted with an entire intro that would represent a whole album. The only clue he had was a trusted member's words.

[How about you try writing about the feelings you had while making this album.]

The advice came from Park Moondae, who had always been his guide.

*...Alright!*

*In that case, I'll make a song that meets the group's expectations!*

Kim Raebin resolved firmly. He completely ignored that this same Park Moondae was the one who had dumped the teaser on him.

Amidst it all, his goal of not focusing on his stage fright was subconsciously being accomplished.

[First Step: Preliminary Research.]

Kim Raebin first focused on the fact that this intro would introduce the group's album. He felt it was necessary to collect the members' opinions.

He started by visiting the member who had been at the dorm the least recently due to a packed schedule.

"...My feelings? Why do you ask."

It was Bae Sejin. He was ambushed with a survey while drinking orange juice, having just returned from a schedule for ❬Doll Hunter❭.

He replied with a bit of reluctance but his expression soon changed to regret.

"No, it's not that I don't want to answer, I was just asking out of pure curiosity! Purely!"

"...? Yes, I understand!"

Kim Raebin, who wasn't offended, earnestly continued with his questions.

[Currently collecting members' impressions of the new album!]

However, Bae Sejin, who had been trying to look friendly, returned to a cool expression at those words.

"I heard Park Moondae told you to express your own feelings. I agree with him. Don't be influenced by me."

Bae Sejin chugged his juice in one shot and left for the bathroom.

"......"

Kim Raebin was bewildered.

*A failure... I guess?*

He didn't give up and soon found his next target.

"What's up, Raebin, you looking for something? Why are you standing here~"

It was Lee Sejin, who had just returned from his personal workout. After Kim Raebin explained what happened, Lee Sejin burst out laughing.

"Hahaha! Oh man... Raebin, you must've been surprised!"

Lee Sejin inwardly chuckled at the other Lee Sejin's tactlessness and smoothly comforted Kim Raebin.

Kim Raebin found himself unwittingly venting his troubles.

"Since it's a song that introduces the group's album, I thought the entire group's perspective should be reflected. If it's just my own thoughts, I'm worried it might become too narrow..."

"Nah~ I think it'll be fine since you have such great sense, Raebin."

It wasn't an empty compliment. Kim Raebin generally boasted an outstanding artistic sense.

Lee Sejin briefly glanced at Kim Raebin's current outfit, then recalled the younger member's usual casual wear. Being able to pull off outfits that could look excessive or embarrassing was a sign of his unique sense.

*This isn't something you can learn.*

The world called it 'hip'.

Though he was a bit clueless in interpersonal relationships, he was a younger brother with more than enough talent to make up for it.

*He's surprisingly strong-willed, too.*

He's a stubborn one, so he won't run away.

After finishing his assessment, Lee Sejin grinned.

"And we're all going to give feedback together when it's done anyway~ For now, let's just have you do whatever you want, Raebin!"

"...I understand."

Just as Lee Sejin predicted, Kim Raebin seriously decided to focus squarely on himself.

*Just like with 'Song of the Stars' last time, I guess I just need to focus on making a fun song!*

"...So I've come to the conclusion that I should just think of it as a hobby and make it."

"Oh."

That night, Kim Raebin sat at the kitchen table, chatting with Cha Eugene about his situation.

Cha Eugene was gobbling up grilled bulgogi rice balls he had gotten from Park Moondae as a late-night snack. The fluffy rice, soaked in sesame oil and bulgogi marinade, was grilled to a savory crispness like nurungji. It was exquisite.

After hearing Kim Raebin's conclusion, he licked a grain of rice off his finger and scoffed.

"Kim Raebin, you're mistaken."

"......"

"I'm mistaken?"

"Yeah! This is different from a hobby."

Cha Eugene grinned.

"Kim Raebin, you're not just making a song right now. You have to make a Performance that introduces our album."

"...!"

"So it's also important that our fans like it."

The video, the concept, the composition. Kim Raebin had to consider countless things and produce a highly commercial result.

*Something the fans will like...*

He fell into deep thought. Cha Eugene, instead of picking up the last rice ball, thoughtfully asked a question.

"Kim Raebin, how did you usually make albums for us?"

Kim Raebin answered without hesitation.

"I made them so that each member could make the most of their own characteristics and strengths through the song."

Cha Eugene grinned.

"Do that again this time! The fans liked that!"

*Ah.*

*That's right.*

*I have to make a song that I can express properly.*

Kim Raebin's mind seemed to clear. He found a distinct goal, arriving at an essential question.

[As a singer, what are my characteristics and strengths?]

Going a little further, the fundamental question finally emerged.

[Who am I as a performer.]

Kim Raebin realized this was where he would start creating the intro. He moved on to the next step.

***

[Second Step: Conceptualizing the Work.]

A work filled with things Kim Raebin was good at.

First, Kim Raebin thought he had a good grasp of what he did well on stage. He had good delivery, song interpretation, and expressiveness. He was also skilled at creating unconventional 'wow' points.

*And yet, I suddenly became unable to perform well on stage...*

That meant there was another factor he hadn't yet grasped.

*It must be my memories.*

He had a strong premonition that he could only properly assemble this work if he considered his lost memories, including those of STier Kim Raebin.

However, reviving non-existent memories in a short time seemed distant.

*Moondae looked troubled too...*

So he decided to try an indirect approach. He would ask the member who had memories of STier until most recently. The group's extremely trustworthy Leader.

"...You're asking what he was like on stage during the STier days?"

"That's right."

"......"

In the living room, Ryu Chungwoo, who was reading a book borrowed from Bae Sejin, simply maintained his smile instead of answering.

Then he answered slowly.

"You did well. You weren't much different from how you are now."

"Still, if you have time right now, would it be alright to ask for a more detailed explanation?"

"......"

If it had been any other member, they wouldn't have bothered asking Ryu Chungwoo such a question. Ryu Chungwoo didn't particularly like to talk about his STier days and would have shut down the topic with something like, 'It's embarrassing. Sorry.'

It was an implicit request not to bring it up again.

However, Kim Raebin's memories of Ryu Chungwoo from that time were gone, and he wasn't the type to notice another person's subtle discomfort.

So in the end, he managed to get a specific answer out of Ryu Chungwoo.

"Specifically... back then, the company wanted to give each member a distinct character on stage."

At the word 'character', Kim Raebin immediately thought of the rabbit drawing engraved on one of his main headphones.

"Do you mean something like the animal characters the fans call us?"

"Similar, but not that specific."

Ryu Chungwoo added briefly.

"Like, one person would always take the bridge, another would always be in the center during the chorus... things like that. Dyeing hair or wearing glasses was also mainly done by specific members."

If Park Moondae had heard this, he would have dismissed it as 'a clumsy attempt to divide the kids into types according to target demographics', but the two, not knowing such specific vocabulary, simply nodded in understanding.

"I heard something similar at my former agency. It's an abstract image, separate from positions like dance, vocal, or rap."

"Yeah."

Ryu Chungwoo, who had responded curtly, saw that Kim Raebin was waiting for a more detailed answer and finally continued slowly.

"And you..., Raebin, you often played a slightly darker and more flamboyant role on stage than you do now."

"Is that so?"

"Yeah. You got a lot of high-impact parts."

Hearing that didn't exactly make him think, 'I lack the ability to pull that off'. However, thinking it through logically, Kim Raebin nodded.

"Then there's a chance I started avoiding the stage because I couldn't handle my assigned parts well!"

"......"

Ryu Chungwoo was silent for a moment, then he shook his head.

"No, you did well."

"Pardon?"

"Don't doubt it, just believe that. Got it?"

His strange tone could have sounded forceful, but it ended gently.

"Ah... I understand."

Kim Raebin nodded and got up. Even he, who was terrible at picking up on subtleties, could tell Ryu Chungwoo looked uncomfortable.

*He seems tired, I should get up and leave now.*

Besides, he had already thought of the next person to ask about this matter in more detail.

"Since you were a fan of STier, I thought you must have had some impressions of my performances. Was I really dark and flamboyant?"

"......"

"Hyung?"

*I told you I wasn't a fan.*

Park Moondae wanted to squeeze his eyes shut. He didn't show it, since saying that now would be a useless distraction from the main issue.

Instead, he said dryly, "Uh, you were kind of like that, but it suited you."

Kim Raebin was actually a bit confused by Park Moondae's assessment, who had swallowed the preceding words, 'Amazingly so, in that shitty environment'.

*So it wasn't because I was bad at it that I started avoiding the stage?*

"But why do you ask."

Even in his confusion, Kim Raebin answered honestly.

"I had a premonition that I somehow needed to consider my memories from back then."

"That's possible."

Instead of rebuking him, Park Moondae readily nodded. He spoke in an uncharacteristically gentle tone.

"However, what you're making right now is the intro for a TeSTAR album. I think it's more important to focus on the you who has lived as a member of TeSTAR."

"...!"

"Think about what you want to do right now, what you're good at."

That... was true.

Had he become so accustomed to inspiration from small clues that he was now obsessed with details?

"I'm not saying what you're doing now is wrong, so don't misunderstand."

"Yes..."

In the end, Kim Raebin couldn't ask any more questions and had to end that day's progress.

He returned to his bedroom and thought, repeating the words to himself.

*What I want to do...*

Just like that, he drifted off to sleep.

When he came to his senses again, it was early morning, past midnight.

"Ugh..."

Kim Raebin blinked his blurry eyes and suddenly realized the bed next to his was empty.

*...Moondae?*

He reflexively got out of bed and was about to walk quietly out the door.

It was then.

"About what you answered."

A voice came from outside the door. It was Park Moondae, who had disappeared from his bed.

"...?"

Kim Raebin reflexively paused and peeked his head out the door.

"That's not all there is to it."

"......"

In the dark living room, indirect lighting cast faint shadows on the faces of the two people sitting there.

Park Moondae.

And Ryu Chungwoo.

"After STier disbanded, you were probably the only one who knew Kim Raebin's whereabouts. Cha Eugene was in America, and Bae Sejin was in court."

"......"

"And... you were overly responsible back then, so I assume you at least checked on his condition."

Park Moondae continued calmly.

"What happened to Raebin back then."

Kim Raebin held his breath.

An immeasurable silence hung heavily in the living room.

And then, Ryu Chungwoo answered in a low, raspy voice.

"You're right."

"He saw what I told him not to see."

In that moment, something flooded Kim Raebin's mind.

"...!"

Like a dam breaking, images began to flow in.

It was a memory.

***

It began with a single sentence floating on a screen.

[This corrupt bastard is shitty as hell ㅠ]
