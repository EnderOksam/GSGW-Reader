# Chapter 437

*There are no crumbs...*

A Kim Raebin fan gloomily muttered while scrolling aimlessly through social media. A post shared on her timeline caught her eye.

It came from VTIC's official account.

[A shocking announcement to commemorate D-7 of the 〈Save the World〉 charity concert!

The indomitable guests are returning.

(Photo)]

The account that shared it added a short, ominous comment.

[Don't tell me it's our boys?]

"......"

Kim Raebin's fan felt a chill. Her premonitions were usually right.

*Oh, please no.*

Was TeSTAR really going to appear at VTIC's charity concert after all that chaos?

She had mixed feelings about it.

It was good that they had cleverly declared their participation, not missing the buzz surrounding the charity concert. More content was always better.

The problem was... it also felt damn annoying, like they were just doing VTIC a favor.

*No, it's their responsibility too. It's not like they were the ones who almost got into an accident, so where do they get off shamelessly pulling that 'we're victims too' bullshit...!*

The ploy was obvious. The broadcasting station was even more pissed off but let it slide, which was a source of eternal regret.

TeSTAR's appearance would make the charity concert an even bigger success! The Kim Raebin fan fumed for a moment before she finally accepted reality.

As an upperclassman preparing for employment, her fighting spirit on the internet had gradually softened.

*Well... I guess they'll perform.*

The incident had blown up before TeSTAR could perform that day, so she wasn't sure, but they would probably perform a few of their title tracks.

A thought naturally came to her mind.

*...Park Moondae is okay, right?*

Even with nerves of steel, almost being crushed by a stage set seemed like it could leave a trauma.

Still, just yesterday, TeSTAR had resumed using their social media.

[Fun board games 🐶]

The attached short video affectionately showed Lee Sejin rolling on the floor after a terrible dice roll while TeSTAR burst into laughter.

The scenery showed they were indoors. Seeing them all together, the atmosphere didn't seem bad.

*Of course, they'd only post things like that.*

She rewound the video to capture a fleeting glimpse of Kim Raebin in his neat pajamas, then decided to stop her useless worrying and just focus on the crumbs.

However, the scheduled 'crumb' was not what she expected.

That afternoon, a post appeared on TeSTAR's official account as well as VTIC's.

[TeSTAR's Counseling Center

Preparing to open 💪

Location: 〈Save the World〉 Charity Venue]

"...?!"

Flustered, she clicked on the description.

[Help support youth-headed households and consult with TeSTAR about the worries and difficulties of your own youth (Photo)]

That's right. TeSTAR wasn't performing.

If they performed the stage they had prepared for the charity concert, it would inevitably bring back memories of the stage collapse.

It was too ambiguous to frame it as overcoming adversity since no members were injured. The audience might also just think about the accident instead of enjoying the stage.

So, they chose this method instead.

A supplementary VCR content!

[Talk with TeSTAR!]

They called it counseling, but in reality, this was... a video call fan sign event!!

AHHHH!

The Kim Raebin fan quickly found a post on her timeline that summarized the details.

[You can enter by donating to the collection box, choosing the member you want, and writing your story. The face of the person receiving counseling will be hidden, but the story may be broadcast anonymously....]

[And the members will choose the stories!]

The event was for one day only.

"......"

She glanced back at the presentation she was making, then tore at her hair.

"Ahhhhh, seriously!"

Whatever, I'll just pull an all-nighter!

She had never won a fansign because of the insanely high cutoff, so she decided to make a futile attempt this time.

Of course, an enormous number of people must have applied. The chances of not getting picked were high, but...

*Kim Raebin!*

She opened a word processor.

Two days later, she would roar upon seeing the news of her win.

Naturally, she had no idea what kind of process Park Moondae had gone through to plan this.
