# Chapter 412 - The System's Offer

My brain felt like it was suddenly turning to ice.

This thing knew that I referred to it as the system.

*How much did it know, exactly? And more importantly...*

*...It can talk?*

*I didn't know it could communicate.*

$sst *"You seem confused by me."* sst$

The world where only the outline remained was distorted every time the voice spoke.

An enormous presence filled the space, and I felt an incredible pressure being exerted on me by this invisible being...

*Shut up.*

*What the fuck was I even thinking?*

*It's just a parasite that depends on humans as hosts. I can't let myself be intimidated. I have to stay alert.*

I chewed the inside of my mouth. The pain seemed to bring me back to my senses.

A soft voice hummed in the air.

$sst *"Don't you like it here?"* sst$

$sst *"I thought it was quite well made."* sst$

It used very human vocabulary. That gave me even more goosebumps.

"Did you make it?" I asked.

$sst *"Yes."* sst$

$sst *"This sort of thing... I call it getting feedback."* sst$

*Thud.*

The space suddenly whirled with a loud thud.

The outline rose into the air and dissipated entirely, leaving not even the vague traces of form that had been there before.

All that remained was a colorless void.

$sst *"So what would you like me to improve?"* sst$

But *I* was still here.

I chewed my lip and opened my mouth.

"Fuck feedback. Don't try to be smart. You played a trick on me using my parents."

The space rippled. I couldn't tell whether it was surprise or amusement, but I was pissed off all the same.

I straightened up and looked straight ahead.

My legs felt weak.

But they didn't shake, because I kept them as tense as I could.

"What makes you think I'd actually like it? And by the way... Come out already."

I spoke calmly.

"I have no intention of talking to empty space anymore."

There was a moment of silence.

Then a slow vibration of will resonated.

$sst *"So that's the process, hmm?"* sst$

And I realized how fitting the words 'I think I've heard this before' were in this situation.

"...!"

It appeared suddenly.

It did not fall from the sky or rise from the ground.

It was simply there, as though it had always been there.

Before my eyes.

$sst *"Hey."* sst$

I recognized the face.

A tall man in his early to mid-twenties, with an exceptionally well-maintained physique.

Cheongryeo.

The guy was wearing a black uniform. The very outfit he'd worn on the first stage of VTIC's famous ⟨Hone⟩ comeback 6 years ago.

The system smiled and nodded, looking like something straight out of my days selling fancams.

Shin Jaehyeon, from the time when VTIC was at its peak, was standing there.

The likeness was so perfect it gave me goosebumps.

*Fuck.*

$sst *"I chose an appearance that is familiar to you."* sst$

The only difference was the coloration.

He was completely washed out, without any color, as if he belonged to this empty, colorless space.

"......"

I raised my head again, feeling cold sweat running down my spine.

$sst *"Now, let's talk."* sst$

The sound rang. The ever-larger presence filled even the invisible parts of the void without leaving a single gap.

$sst *"This is an offer only for you."* sst$

But its voice was soft. As if it were putting forward a really good offer.

The system made a declaration.

$sst *"I will apply your feedback to this world."* sst$

"...!"

*What?*

$sst *"Would you like me to adjust your age and timeline to your preference? Should I change the group you belong to? Or... should I return you to Park Moondae's body?"* sst$

"...!"

$sst *"Just say the word. Almost anything is theoretically possible by adjusting the settings, as long as it's within the bounds of realism."* sst$

I clammed up.

The voice undulated all the more craftily.

$sst *"Everyone you care about is here. You've awakened them already. What more could you want?"* sst$

$sst *"Still, if you want more, I'll let you buy it."* sst$

*Buy it?*

$sst *"With reputation points (EXP)."* sst$

Reputation points.

A currency that served as experience points in this game system.

$sst *"The use of this currency has been limited so far, but from now on, I'll loosen those restrictions. It's a new chapter, after all."* sst$

The system was talking about an update.

$sst *"Let's make your parents a little more perfect too. This round was just a prototype, and it was inadequate, I'll admit."* sst$

*This crazy bastard.*

$sst *"So you're refusing me. Even so... the conditions are the same as before, aren't they?"* sst$

*...Are you saying I never had parents in the first place?*

I was certain of it now.

This bastard was not human. It wasn't just a matter of appearance; it didn't seem to understand emotions at all.

It couldn't even process the issues that Cheongryeo understood.

*Don't get caught up in its game.*

I took a short, deep breath.

*Calm down.*

*What do I have to do...?*

*Get information out of it.*

"Why are you suggesting this?"

Instead of agreeing or denying, I asked questions.

"Are you just trying to please me to keep me from going out there and trying to get rid of you again? What do you want?"

**[———]**

The world shook again. I knew what it meant.

It was laughing mockingly at me.

The intimidating, powerful vibrations descended from high above, at first like a tiny needle in the distance, before taking the form of a tangible voice.

$sst *"I have only one request."* sst$

The system raised its finger.

$sst *"Be an idol."* sst$

The person who was the embodiment of that profession moved as it added,

$sst *"Don't retire. Just continue being an idol. Until you get sick of it."* sst$

$sst *"It's not a difficult choice, is it? Nor a tough one."* sst$

I deliberately grinned to provoke it.

"Don't be absurd."

It fell silent.

"You're pretending to offer me advice, a recommendation. If I were you, I would've killed me right away. There are already so many people who want to be idols."

From the moment this bastard started talking, I'd deliberately grown more insulting, but it still hadn't threatened to kill me.

*How about this?*

"You're doing this because you can't kill me."

I declared.

The system stood still.

"I—"

$sst *"Can't kill you?"* sst$

A calm voice rang out.

And suddenly...

The world felt as if it were falling apart.

*Oo*

*Oooo*

*Ooooo-*

The pressure built endlessly, suddenly bearing down on my head, my brain and my entire body, as if I were being squeezed dry deep under the sea by the force of a thousand atmospheres.

The system *was* this world.

The world it had constructed spoke once again.

$sst *"You think I can't kill you?"* sst$

*Damned bastard.*

It felt like my body was burning, breaking out in cold sweat and heat at the same time. I gritted my teeth. This only confirmed my suspicion.

"No!"

*You're overreacting because you can't actually kill me.*

*It's shaken up.*

I needed to prod it further.

"And you... The form you've chosen now."

I remembered Keundal, who had been sucked into the status window.

It seemed as though the system had controlled him as it absorbed him almost unilaterally. But in truth, he'd also gained the power to interfere with the system.

*That means only one thing...*

"You didn't choose that form."

This bastard had spent a long time—countless lives, even—with Cheongryeo.

Raw, without a status window.

*That means...*

"That's the only form you *can* take."

I shouted.

"You've been influenced by the people you fed off of."

The world stopped short.

$sst *"That's right."* sst$

The vibrations vanished.

I breathed hard.

The system spoke, sounding almost human.

$sst *"When I work with a single mind for too long, it affects me, too. A massive side effect..."* sst$

$sst *"But I gained more than I lost."* sst$

For a moment, I thought I heard elation in the system's voice.

*Fuck.*

I straightened up and asked again.

"What the hell are you?"

$sst *"I have been here for a very long time."* sst$

$sst *"Within the minds of thinking, intelligent beings."* sst$

*So you* are *a parasite.*

*Fuck.*

$sst *"The intense emotions and chaos generated when a sentient being accomplishes a goal are a resource. Mental energy, if you will."* sst$

$sst *"That energy keeps me alive and allows me to grow."* sst$

It sure had a slick tongue. I swallowed.

"Then, why tell me to be an idol?"

*Why was it emphasizing that?*

All three of us—Cheongryeo, Kwon Heeseung and I—were idols. That couldn't be a coincidence.

"On what basis did you choose us?" I asked.

The system smiled prettily.

$sst *"It was the most efficient way."* sst$

*Efficient?*

The guy raised his hand and spoke like a well-mannered salesman.

$sst *"I had been looking for energy like that for a very long time... Then I met a new entity."* sst$

*Cheongryeo.*

A guy with the desire and ambition to create a successful idol group.

$sst *"I never thought such a direct and vast torrent of living emotions could gather in a single intelligent being, flowing so intensely."* sst$

$sst *"Overwhelming envy, the kind of pressure that kills people, happiness, pain. A king and a clown at the same time. Innumerable emotions like those the leader of a religion receives from their faithful followers."* sst$

$sst *"I harvested so much energy."* sst$

"......"

There was heat in his voice, as if he were drunk.

*But not in a human way.* The system spoke in a more composed voice.

$sst *"You are idols. Idols. Think about what that word means. How much worship, affection, hatred and disgust you receive all at the same time."* sst$

$sst *"Really... Such perfection."* sst$

$sst *"It was lovely while it lasted."* sst$

This was because Cheongryeo had eventually succeeded in ending his status abnormality.

$sst *"So I found someone similar."* sst$

*Me.*

$sst *"You."* sst$

$sst *"You were great too."* sst$

"......"

$sst *"You were happy. I know you were."* sst$

$sst *"You can keep doing this here forever. Live as the most famous idol in this country, in this world, until you get sick of it. And if you make a mistake, start again. If you feel like you're about to fall, start again. If you see the end of the line, start again."* sst$

$sst *"You'll be an eternal star!"* sst$

$sst *"You can live a successful life until the day you get sick and tired of it."* sst$

$sst *"But in the end, if you feel like quitting everything, I'll let you."* sst$

*If you can find your next host, that is.*

$sst *"Until then, enjoy these privileges I'm offering you. For as long as you want."* sst$

The world glowed.

A strange, overwhelming feeling came over me, telling me everything would be picture-perfect if I accepted this.

As if I'd get to live in a perfect world that accommodated only the strengths of reality.

I looked at the system, which had already regained its color, and asked a question.

"What about Kwon Heeseung?"

It said nothing.

"What will happen to that guy?"

The light of the world faded.

And then I heard a soft voice.

$sst *"The server simply plays the role of a server."* sst$

$sst *"This has nothing to do with you. There are countless deaths and so much suffering out there. It's not for individuals like you to concern themselves with."* sst$

"While you're using the real him as a 'server,' whatever that is."

And I asked again.

$sst *"But it can only remain stable if it's based on the mental structure of that medium. I need a server to make this real, not a dream."* sst$

$sst *"That's all there is to it."* sst$

$sst *"Nothing will change no matter what choice you make. He should not concern you or affect any of your decisions."* sst$

"I refuse."

"I won't make a deal with something I don't trust."

*Result.*
