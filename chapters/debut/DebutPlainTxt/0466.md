# Chapter 466

Seven figures ascended the stage.

This was TeSTAR's first comeback performance on a major broadcast network since their formation. Internet users had already settled in and were chattering away.

[It feels even weirder writing it out like this. TeSTAR not appearing on T-Net's music show.]

[Can we expect Music Bomb to grit their teeth and refuse to give TeSTAR first place next week?]

[Is TeSTAR the ending stage today?]

It was inevitable that T-Net, a show they were not even on, would be mentioned. Until now, TeSTAR had been the very icon of T1's KPOP business success.

That image had not faded even after they parted ways with T1.

As long as that image remained, and unless a massive upheaval erased their origins, it meant the major networks had no reason to be overly friendly to TeSTAR.

That was why TeSTAR received a spot two stages before the finale today.

[TeSTAR's already on?]

[If it were me, I'd use this chance and give them the ending stage first. Look at the pride of these major networks, lol.]

Considering their popularity, it was a rather 'strict' arrangement based on seniority.

Regardless, people considered TeSTAR's comeback stage the highlight of this week's KBC music show. That was enough.

[It's starting.]

A single stage. The performance began amidst a comeback buzz that justified all the effort poured into gathering an audience.

[Doong...!]

A minor key riff, like a modulated ship's horn, filled the air.

When the lights came on, they revealed a magnificent set resembling a study or laboratory, with the 'KIS' logo floating in the air. Figures in a symmetrical triangular formation stood at the very center of the elegant, conceptual stage.

Their backs were turned.

From that position, the seven men slowly moved their necks. The texture of leather shimmered under the lights. They wore tight-fitting leather suits.

[They're all in phantom thief outfits.]

[Insane.]

Just as the minor key MIDI and electric guitar sounds seemed to build, a static-like sound effect introduced the drop. A powerful major key intro followed.

[Touched by your hand,

My tape rewinds again.]

The formation shattered as Park Moondae crossed his arms in a solo move, and the other members' group dance moved in sync with him. Then, from the right, the next member slid forward to the front of the camera.

[The moment my heart pounds,

All my senses go Slow.]

Ryu Chungwoo performed his part with refreshing dynamic control. He knelt and rotated before extending one arm straight out. The group dance and formation shifted again, following his arm gesture.

[Oh, holy.]

[Look at Ryu Chungwoo's arm, insane.]

That was right.

The choreography, created with the theme of 'Butterfly Effect', was structured so that the formation changed with the solo gestures of the member singing their part.

[Wow.]

[Ah, please, the camera work.]

[They have to capture this well T_T.]

The stage raced on, making viewers desperately wish for a fixed full-cam. The drum beat rose like a pounding heart.

[Thrilling contact

In your silent eyes

I search for the clear answer.]

Kim Raebin, taking a vocal part for the first time in a while, wore a unique turtleneck-style phantom thief outfit. He finished his part with narrowed eyes, then high-fived the next person as if passing a baton.

It was Cha Eugene.

[There's only one choice

Yes, take it all (one more)

Even if you let go of this tightly held hand.]

His pair choreography with Kim Raebin unfolded nimbly, like a battle. Park Moondae and Ryu Chungwoo's ad-libs rose over the top, supporting the rap and making the live performance feel truly pop.

[The live vocals are so refreshing.]

[I can't even hear the AR.]

[As expected, TeSTAR has to do super difficult songs.]

Here, a rest note entered, controlling the tempo. A single phrase was sung in a low voice.

[Let's Start, begin the melee.]

A close-up showed Kim Raebin at the center, holding his chin and neck.

[T_T]

[Ack.]

Then came the pre-chorus.

[Find me, look at my face

I don't care what's next

Chaser, today too

Don't stop your hands.]

It was a difficult section with a complex off-beat rhythm and a wide vocal range. The choreography's difficulty shot up to match, becoming more spectacular. The powerful Lee Sejin and then Seon Ahyeon, with his exquisite dynamic control, led the mood in this part where the center was even more crucial.

[Find out everything

Don't be scared what's next

Chaser, tomorrow too

Don't end the twist.]

[Lee Sejin's phantom thief outfit... whoever chose that thin black lining, wow.]

[Seon Ahyeon, how many times did he just change the beat? What was that move? I couldn't even see it.]

Just as the visual pleasure seemed to peak, the chorus finally arrived. The beat dropped.

[Just roll the dice

Trust, take my side]

It was a contrast to the pre-chorus. The memorable chorus was composed of three core elements: a simple rhythm, a main melody that hit just right, and a catchy riff. A visually pleasing dance break filled the space.

[If we could hear their footsteps, I bet even those would be perfectly in sync.]

[They're insane.]

[When the hell did they practice all this while filming a game? Are TeSTAR superhumans?]

The signature arm movement, which seemed to snatch the air with unexpected acceleration, perfectly suited the phantom thief outfits. It was disciplined yet strangely sexy.

In the end, this kind of exclamation was inevitable.

[TeSTAR is still the same.]

[The self-producing was real.]

[Wow, the vibe is the same, but they're flying even higher.]

[Where are all the bastards who said TeSTAR was just coasting on T1's planning and capital?]

It was the thrilling satisfaction that came from having their expectations for TeSTAR met.

Why did this phenomenon occur? For example, if a group changed companies, could the group's color change even if most members remained? The answer is 'yes'.

If the staff who made the album and the planning team changed, it could happen at any time. This was not just a matter of quality, but also a question of whether 'that feeling', the reason fans and the public liked the group, would remain. The group's unique color. What the public expected from them. A lack of manpower or capital could make it impossible to create that anymore.

However, with this stage, TeSTAR had essentially disproven that.

*TeSTAR does what you expect!*

Thanks to that, the people watching the stage unconsciously realized this and became delighted.

[I've been doing nothing but marveling this entire first verse.]

[Hahahahaha, seriously, I don't know about anything else, but their stages are fun.]

TeSTAR was still soaring high on the same track they had debuted on.

[Yes, this Final round.]

The performance advanced without hesitation, reaching the bridge. The rough electric sound faded, and a swelling keyboard melody built up with the drum sound. Matching that atmosphere, Bae Sejin sang the melody clearly and firmly, moving from low to mid-high notes without unnecessary flourish.

[Pull it tight.]

At the end of his line, Cha Eugene burst out. He landed on both feet like a feline predator and took slow, powerful steps.

And then, the final dance break.

[Just roll the dice

Trust, take my side]

[Ack, paradise.]

[This is so damn good.]

As if they had made up their minds, TeSTAR did not let up. They filled the stage until the very end without missing a single move or note.

Ryu Chungwoo stood at the center for the ending. He looked back and playfully held up a glove he had taken off. It was a fitting choice of ending member for the phantom thief outfits.

WAAAAAAH!

Like the fan cheers that filled the screen, the internet chat window scrolled by rapidly.

[Hahahaha.]

[TeSTAR ! TeSTAR ! TeSTAR ! TeSTAR ! TeSTAR ! TeSTAR ! TeSTAR !]

[They killed it.]

[Oh.]

[Give me the replay.]

On social media, excited fans frantically poured out words, onomatopoeia, and emoticons as their reactions. It couldn't be helped. It was just too thrilling!

They only started forming complete sentences once their rational minds returned.

[I'm saving all the edited photos as they come up. When I came to my senses, I'd saved the same picture of Chungwoo ten times.]

[I'm so excited for this promotion.]

[The pre-recording spoilers mentioned straitjackets, right? Please tell me they did, I'm begging you.]

[Will they appear on a lot of major network variety shows this time? I actually had my eye on a few, lol.]

The moment they saw the stage and sensed the promotion's success, their happiness forecast hit MAX. Expectations for their schedule began to pour in. Amidst it all, truths and rumors subtly passed by.

[I hope they film another variety show with that PD from Carrot Coins, lol. We're gganbu, after all, lol.]

└Oh, right, lol.

└They probably already filmed it? Hehe.

└Um... sorry to butt in, but that PD is affiliated with T1, so I don't know if it's okay. Looking at the schedule, it seems TeSTAR isn't... appearing on T1's broadcast stations.

└Ah, please no.

└No way, is T1 going to block them?;;

└No rumors. The schedule might not be out yet, why are you all getting ahead of yourselves? Just wait and see.

However, most were too full of anticipation for the next stage, or too busy proudly checking the reactions to this comeback performance.

For instance, a best comment like this one.

[TeSTAR must know they're good at what they do, right? Hahahaha.]

└No way they don't, lol.

While grinning, some fans also suddenly had this thought.

*When that stage ended, what kind of feelings did TeSTAR have?*

***

"You all worked hard~"

"Waaaah!!"

Cheers even louder than their last comeback filled the waiting room. The sight of even the staff joining in was heartwarming, filled with heat and passion.

This was their first album, released after facing unprecedented obstacles in a new company and a new environment. Everyone couldn't help but feel deeply moved.

The members swarmed over to Park Moondae, who was already monitoring the reactions from the pre-recording.

"Moondae, Moondae, are you checking the reactions?"

"......"

"H-How is it...?"

Park Moondae stated simply.

"It's good."

"......"

It was rare for him to make such a definitive statement about public opinion without any follow-up.

*And that means...*

"Really?"

*It means that, even accounting for the fact that they were fans, the reaction is overwhelmingly positive!*

The members looked dazed for a moment, then broke into wide smiles.

"Wow... TeSTAR is still alive and kicking!"

"Haha, we really showed them something today!"

"Oh~ Chungwoo-hyung!"

Even Ryu Chungwoo excitedly adapted their pre-stage chant, 'Ah, TeSTAR is going to show something today'.

Bae Sejin, with a flushed face and catching his breath, approached and looked at the screen Park Moondae was watching. Seon Ahyeon smiled brightly and moved aside slightly.

"Ah, h-here...!"

"......"

Bae Sejin read the contents of the smartphone, which Park Moondae did not bother to hide.

*It's so good, I can't put it into words. You have to watch the live broadcast.*

*I feel like I'm going to cry from happiness.*

*I hope this promotion period is long.*

*I have a strong feeling my seat won't be at the concert.*

Those reactions, overflowing with expectation and happiness without a hint of pretense, certainly hit home.

"...This is nice."

"It really is!"

He himself had enjoyed it.

*That was good.*

He felt a sense of having done well. He felt he had kept up with the choreography and brought the song to life without lacking anything. After several years of stage experience, the discomfort of the leather outfit, which did not allow sweat to escape, was now barely noticeable.

"......"

So, that was it. Bae Sejin had no choice but to make a certain decision. It was inevitable.

"TeSTAR members, time to change your outfits~"

"Yes!"

Amidst the members busily getting ready, he was the first to change his clothes. He then headed to a sofa in the corner and turned on the memo app he had been writing in.

It was a note he had tried to write because his heart would not calm down, but had closed after thirty seconds, wondering what he was doing right before the stage.

[Thank you so much for the offer. I would also love to appear in your work, Director]

He gritted his teeth and began to write over the words he had typed.

[...I would love to appear in your work, Director. However, I feel I must focus more on my group activities right now so I won't have any regrets.]

This was the right thing to do.

*If it overlaps with the promotion period... I can't.*

Even if he could not have done it during any other album promotion, he definitely could not do it now.

*Everyone started over with such resolve.*

This time, he really felt that he had not just barely managed to keep up, but had properly contributed to the perfection of a work. He did not want to throw cold water on it with his individual actions. He now had a decent grasp of the idol group ecosystem.

*This is a time when I need to focus on group activities.*

Bae Sejin started writing again. He was refining the words he would use when he made the call.

[I'm sorry to decline the offer you gave with such good intentions]

It was then.

"Hyung, what are you doing?"

"......"

He thought his heart was going to drop.

When he turned only his eyes, he saw Park Moondae looking at him with a sullen face, as if he were looking at something strange.

"......"

Faced with an expression that clearly asked,*What were you looking at that made you jump like that?*, Bae Sejin ended up giving himself away.

"N-No! Just texting!"

"That's a memo app."

"......"

"Sorry, but I saw a few words because of where you are."

Park Moondae sat on the sofa next to him, leaving a reasonable distance. Bae Sejin froze solid.

"Did you by any chance get a role offer from that director?"

"L-Lower your voice."

"...?"

Park Moondae thought,*It's not that big of a deal,*but he decided to play along for now.

Bae Sejin continued in a low, serious voice.

"...Yes, but since it's our promotion period, I'm going to refuse."

"It doesn't seem like you don't want to do it, though."

For a moment, Bae Sejin felt a surge of emotion. Of course he wanted to! However, he knew he should not get angry. Park Moondae had not done anything wrong.

So, after taking a deep breath, he answered more calmly.

"...It's not about what I want to do! I'm saying that now doesn't seem like the right timing!"

"Hmm..."

Park Moondae hummed expressionlessly, then nodded after a few seconds.

"Then let's ask if everyone else thinks so."

"......"

The TeSTAR meeting was reconvened.
