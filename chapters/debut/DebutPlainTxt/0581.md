# Chapter 581

A few minutes later, Seon Ahyeon joined us backstage. He had just finished his solo stage and was hurriedly changing his outfit.

"Ahyeon, you were amazing today, too~!"

"Th-Thank you...!"

A staff member quickly wiped the red paint stains from Seon Ahyeon's neck with a wet wipe. I watched Lee Sejin give a thumbs-up and nodded to myself.

*Seon Ahyeon's stage was certainly impressive.*

It was the final part of the bizarre hotel story before the climax. The audience had to choose between two options. Go down to the dark basement, or go outside the hotel.

*And during the first concert, the audience chose the former...*

They chose the basement again this time. The first concert's stage had been shockingly impressive. It was Seon Ahyeon's 'RED' choice, where he was covered in paint.

[It's all just me talking to myself]

Don't listen.

THUD.

He performed powerfully between stage props designed like complex, web-like basement pipes. He moved back and forth, seeming to hang from them.

The song was a slow-tempo B-side track about an inescapable love. Kim Raebin had arranged it more experimentally, using a theremin to heighten the dissonance. Seon Ahyeon's performance matched the music, growing more destructive and aggressive as the song progressed. In between, his elegant choreography, closer to ballet than modern dance, created a jarring unease.

AAAAAAAAH!

I thought of the audience's recent cheers and laughed quietly.

*They must be enjoying this.*

We had unanimously saved Seon Ahyeon's solo for last for a good reason.

Ryu Chungwoo watched the others' conversation with a pleased expression, then spoke.

"The part with choices is over, so it'll be smoother from here. Let's stay focused and give it our all."

"Yes!"

That was right. The choice-based part was over. Not every stage had a choice attached to it in the first place.

*If we did that, we wouldn't be able to manage the quality.*

The setlist would double in size. Only about half the stages had choices, and solo songs made up a large portion of those to minimize the burden. Mine was like that, too.

My solo stage, I mean.

"......"

I recalled my own stage from just before. I had poured all my energy into the song, unable to think of anything else.

*...Hoo.*

Just recalling it felt overwhelming. It was strange to think that was only a few minutes ago. Truly.

"......"

"...Park Moondae."

"Yes?"

When I looked up, Bae Sejin wore a peculiar expression. I wondered if I looked out of it.

"You... you're a really good singer. I'm talking about your solo stage."

Fortunately, that wasn't it.

"...Thank you?"

"Ahem. Well, I'm sure this is nothing new to you, but..."

Bae Sejin seemed about to offer more praise but was cut off by another member. Cha Eugene whispered loudly enough for everyone to hear.

"Hyung, you don't need to say it. The camera already did!"

"...? What are you talking about..."

"The camera caught you closing your eyes like this while listening to Moondae's song. That's the face of someone who's moved, right?"

"...!"

Cha Eugene perfectly mimicked the camera director's gesture, then copied Bae Sejin's earnest, emotionally moved expression before winking.

"Everyone will know soon."

Bae Sejin's face turned beet red, like a yaksha.

"Bwahahaha!"

Lee Sejin led the charge as the members burst out laughing, and the backstage area grew noisy. Bae Sejin knew Cha Eugene had no ill intent and was just joking. He quickly relaxed his expression and just sighed.

A few years ago, I would've watched them, worried they might start a fight over this. Not anymore. The atmosphere was good.

*I guess we've all gotten used to each other.*

Everyone had enough energy to laugh and chat while panting from exhaustion. It must have been because they were satisfied with this concert. The backstage atmosphere at yesterday's concert definitely hadn't been like this, even though the others weren't mentioning it.

*...I was grateful.*

I looked at the others and spoke.

"...They said there are two minutes left on the VCR."

"Ooh!"

A staff member gave the signal. I nodded and straightened my posture with the others. The sound of the VCR's narration echoed from above. It was Ryu Chungwoo's voice.

[Look behind you.]

On the VCR, Ryu Chungwoo would be appearing from the pitch-black darkness, holding out a ticket. The video was the same whether you went to the basement or outside during Seon Ahyeon's stage choice.

[Hurry.]

The video ended from the camera's point of view as it slowly turned around.

RUUUUMBLE-

Now, we would emerge from the darkness. These were stages without choices, into which we poured our complete focus.

As the climax of this concert.

PSSSSSH.

The stage set before me opened. Dry ice seeped from the cracks. A wave of light from the light sticks spread out before my eyes. It wasn't the first time, but my heart still pounded.

*I have to show them my best.*

I truly wanted to. I dashed onto the stage, matching the click in my in-ear monitor.

The space revealed as the hotel lobby stage set opened was a circus tent.

[Welcome to the HUNTHOUSE!]

A series of spectacular, circus-like stages would now unfold without a break.

Cha Eugene grinned and raised his hand from the center of the formation.

"You, have my dice!"

WAAAAAAAH!!

From ❬Hi-Five❭ to ❬Procession❭. It was a medley of three or four recent hits and previous title tracks, performed one after another.

The most exciting part!

The relentless pace swept up both performers and audience, heating the venue's atmosphere.

"Woooo-"

I belted the climax into my handheld mic. I pushed my voice to its limit, and a super high note burst out effortlessly at the perfect, satisfying point. My throat was in unusually good condition. It was so good that Kim Raebin's expression changed slightly when our eyes met during the choreography.

*What is this?*

*No, there's no need to question it. Let's just use it to the fullest.*

I used my voice as much as I wanted, all the way through the final song, ❬Procession❭. There were no limits on my throat.

AAAAAAAAH!!

The moment the last song ended, an immense storm of applause and cheers poured down, all the way to the back of the audience. The audience's reaction filled my eyes and ears.

"Haaah."

Even as I panted for breath, it wasn't painful. I ran back from the extended stage with the others and got into formation, all the way to the same position as the main screen.

[THANK YOU]

We held hands and bowed. The screen flashed at that exact moment. The stage set descended like a closing tent flap, and we disappeared behind it.

Silence.

"......"

"...Haaah."

After a moment of silence, the backstage area became noisy again.

"Good work, everyone!"

"The outfits are ready!"

I stood still, however, recalling the audience I had just seen. That heat and their reaction.

*I satisfied them.*

*That much, I could guarantee.*

I showed a performance with no shortcomings at today's concert.

"......"

Even as I put down the handheld mic, I continued to stare at it intently. It was thrilling, but I couldn't let myself get overwhelmed.

It wasn't over yet.

***

WAAAAAH!

Keundal clenched both his fists.

*That was incredible!*

The sheer density of the last 15-minute medley was enough to leave a person breathless. Moreover, they didn't leave any room for lingering. When the song ended, TeSTAR cleanly disappeared from the stage. The set closed like a circus tent, and the stage went dark, as if to say it was all over.

Conversely, the audience lights came on, as if telling everyone to get up and leave, but no one stood up, and no one seemed disappointed. It was the same for those watching on VR.

Because...

*Encore!*

It was an unspoken agreement. Keundal excitedly moved his hands, swaying his light stick.

Usually, fans waiting for an encore would sing a fan song or another song with special meaning for them and the artist. Or they would hold an event.

However.

*Yesterday... they probably couldn't do it properly.*

The event had fizzled out because of Park Moondae's sudden absence.

*I heard they were originally supposed to sing the first verse of their latest fan song, ❬Epic❭, together a cappella.*

Today, perhaps because the situation was resolved so suddenly, they seemed cautious and hadn't planned a special event. There were only sporadic shouts for an encore, applause, and chants of the members' names.

*...I'm sorry.*

He felt like it was his fault.

"......"

Keundal's spirits dampened slightly. He listened to the sounds echoing through the audience and waited quietly.

Several minutes passed.

DUNG.

The old hotel lobby appeared on the VCR.

AAAAAAAAAAH!!

In the audience, the light sticks were once again remotely controlled, filling the space with vivid colors. Yellow, pink, and blue. They were TeSTAR's official group colors.

[Oh, you're back again?]

Lee Sejin's playful voice rang out from the VCR.

[What should we do? Our circus doesn't have anything else prepared...]

BOOOOOO!

Playful boos and laughter erupted from the audience. Then, a bright, laughter-filled voice came from the VCR right on cue.

[Ah, well the circus may be over... but how about watching something fun like us?]

[Yes!]

[Alright!]

[We'll get ready now..., Hup!]

The VCR screen fast-forwarded. Time flowed as if watching the hotel's history on fast-forward, until only the frame of the old hotel remained.

And then, daylight arrived.

[It took a little while, but we're ready!]

The building that was once a hotel was revealed, a bare skeleton under the blue sky.

[We're coming now!]

The lights returned to the stage.

"...!"

The stage was revealed once more. The magnificent hotel background set was gone. Only a skeleton-like frame of stage scaffolding remained.

"Whoa, whoa, whoa!"

Then, the members shot up from the center of the stage, not from the set.

AAAAAAAAH!

A powerful instrumental blared. The members fearlessly ran to the extended stage, took their positions, and began the choreography. It was a melody familiar to everyone.

The easy-listening song had topped the music charts. At the same time, it was the song that had caused TeSTAR such desperate anguish. It was their last promotional track and their fan song.

❬Epic❭.

[Heavy yesterdays felt like they would never end,

and the perfect today never came]

Ryu Chungwoo sang with a smile. The members moved, forming a new formation.

[Right,

even if there's no perfection, now is

the end of yesterday, the start of today]

The resounding live vocals echoed through the speakers. The members smiled and ran around the extended stage, as if it were a lie that they had just performed to exhaustion. The original, unarranged song sounded even better live than the studio version.

A fansite master sitting right before the extended stage realized.

"Ah...!!"

The choreography had changed. No, the moves were the same. The formation was different.

[I turn the page

move to the next

exhaustion shines

pain becomes my guide]

During Bae Sejin's part, where backup dancers were originally supposed to assist from both sides, Lee Sejin and Cha Eugene stood in their place. Then, during Seon Ahyeon's next part, Ryu Chungwoo received his turn instead of a dancer, completing the formation.

*There are no backup dancers...*

That's right. All the formations that originally used dancers just to fill space now included only TeSTAR. The stage was filled entirely with just TeSTAR.

As if this was how they had wanted to do it all along.

They didn't explain that the previous promotions were a hastily added, unplanned fan service. They chose to show it directly. They showed what the official promotions looked like.

That stage.

[Let me tempt fate

beyond the signposts and crossroads

struggling

to go just one step further]

The choreography interlocked seamlessly as they launched powerfully into the chorus.

"......"

Keundal realized that a massive singalong was happening all around him. The people around him sang with emotion and relief, as if finally singing the fan song they couldn't sing earlier, but together and more happily.

He clenched his fists. It connected to a feeling of ecstasy.

[To the end of today,

beyond the limits!]

The moment Cha Eugene's final chorus ended, Park Moondae's high note rang through the ceiling. As if pouring everything out.

[Running breathlessly,

the city below my feet

I reach my hand higher toward the sky]

The choreography snapped perfectly into place. The formation exploded outwards before locking powerfully into position.

[Even if I can't reach the stars,

I'll live for today]

Panting for breath, Park Moondae smiled instead of crying.

[Don't sleep

Just- dream!]

Amidst exploding fireworks and cheers, TeSTAR's concert came to a successful end.

No one yet knew what kind of repercussions this concert would bring.
