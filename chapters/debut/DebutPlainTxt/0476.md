# Chapter 476

A member went AWOL six years after our debut. It was a blood-chilling situation.

The elevator carrying STier Cha Eugene started its descent. I bolted for the stairs, but it was already too late. Our new dorm was on a high floor, and no amount of athleticism could outrun an elevator.

Just like that, the company erupted into chaos in the dead of night.

"]...What?]"

The news began with the manager team leader, who asked back in disbelief. It eventually reached the CEO, rousing nearly every sleeping employee to scour Seoul.

We were doing the same. Ryu Chungwoo, his face grim, ran back and forth between the security office and our dorm.

"Did they check the CCTV?"

"They confirmed he walked to the main gate, but they lost him after that. It was too dark. It looks like he hailed a taxi..."

That meant we would have to file a missing person report with the police to track the taxi. Filing a report would be insane unless we wanted to be on the front page of tomorrow's entertainment news.

*Damn it.*

I wanted to bite my tongue off. I monitored social media in real-time, hoping for a witness account while I wracked my brain for any place he might have gone.

*I caught him when he ran off during the last tour, too.*

If you know someone well enough, you can usually deduce their movements. The problem was, I didn't know him well at all.

*Because this was STier Cha Eugene.*

Right. I had to admit it.

Before becoming TeSTAR's Park Moondae, the office worker Ryu Gunwoo had been interested in STier and followed them closely. That must be why I recognized him in the hospital room as the guy from STier.

*But that didn't mean I knew him as a person.*

Everything I knew about STier Cha Eugene was a narrative filtered through the media. It was information about an idol. I had no way of knowing the places he personally liked or frequented.

On top of that, there was another problem.

"Uh, it seems like no one else is going to bring this up, so I will," Keun Sejin said, ruffling his hair with a bitter smile. "You guys know we have a group schedule tomorrow evening, right?"

No one answered.

"A festival."

That was right. Tomorrow was the last domestic group schedule of this promotion period. A summer festival.

"We already announced that Eugene would be absent, but if we pull an all-nighter trying to find him and then bomb the performance... I think that would be a problem too," Keun Sejin continued.

He was right. The only other alternative was to cancel the event entirely.

*Could an event of this scale even handle a same-day cancellation from a headliner like TeSTAR?*

It felt dirty to think, but different events had different levels of prestige, and the fallout from a cancellation varied.

*And tomorrow's event was...*

"It's Water Bomb."

We were screwed. Fuck.

If we canceled on the day of, the public would crucify us.

*No ordinary excuse would make a dent.*

The moment we canceled, it would become a massive news story, and if we performed, a witness account of a perfectly fine Cha Eugene wandering around would be just as bad.

[Is Cha Eugene crazy? Get a grip, all his devoted fans are about to leave the fandom. LOL. I'm already out.]

[This is why you shouldn't use foreign laborers in K-pop. Fuckers. It's always the same sh*t.]

[Yeah, yeah, Eugene Ignacio-cha-nim, stop sucking up that dirty Korean money and go back to America.]

Cha Eugene already had the camera assault incident. This would be a one-way ticket to rock bottom. A barrage of insults would rain down from everyone waiting to say, 'I told you so'.

*What should I do?*

The problem was, I didn't have an answer. All we could do was keep searching.

But by morning, there was still no sign of Cha Eugene.

None of us had slept. Half the members were still at the company. I was no different, greeting the dawn in the living room without a wink of sleep.

I rubbed my smartphone screen, lost in thought. My head was spinning.

*If this was going to happen, maybe it would have been better if the album hadn't done so well.*

I thought it was the best choice at the time, but if this was the result, what would happen to Cha Eugene's career? How was I supposed to fix this? My head ached.

*There's too much to do.*

Even if we found STier Cha Eugene, I wouldn't know where to begin cleaning up this mess. The company, public opinion, our promotions. I had no idea what to do with him for the next month either.

*Only extreme measures come to mind.*

I pressed my eyelids.

Just then.

CLINK.

A glass of cold water appeared on the table in front of me. I picked it up and downed it. The person who brought it sat down across from me.

It was Bae Sejin. He looked terrible. He probably thought Cha Eugene left because of what he had said.

"Hyung, you have a shoot tomorrow. You should get some rest."

"I'm fine. I've pulled all-nighters for shoots before," Bae Sejin answered curtly. He lowered his head, his expression confused. "I just... I don't get why he'd go this far."

He continued, "I know he's had different experiences for the past few years, but... that Cha Eugene is still Cha Eugene, right? I never thought he was the type of person to do something like this."

The truth was, while Cha Eugene seemed to do whatever he wanted, he was actually considerate when someone was in a tough spot. He was so natural about it that you wouldn't even notice.

*So for Bae Sejin, who saw him that way, to suddenly hear him say 'It's none of my business what happens to you'...*

He must have been stunned.

Right. Someone who didn't know the full story couldn't understand the stress STier Cha Eugene was under. The guy had already quit being an idol. This wasn't a '30-day trial of your successful self'. Of course he'd be pissed.

*I should have handled this more carefully.*

I was so focused on getting through these 30 days and returning Cha Eugene to normal that I failed to manage his mental state.

My head felt heavy as silence filled the room.

"Excuse me."

A low voice spoke up. "Is that really the case?"

I looked up. A pale-faced Kim Raebin carefully sat on the sofa.

"I... I don't think that Cha Eugene left with the intention of making things difficult for us," Kim Raebin said.

Bae Sejin looked up, startled. "I wish that were true, but you heard him. He said TeSTAR was none of his business."

"Th-that's true, but... I don't think saying it's not his problem means he intends to actively sabotage us," Kim Raebin argued.

"Huh?"

"Did Cha Eugene ever say that he would go out of his way to harm TeSTAR's career?"

"Not in those words," Bae Sejin admitted. "But given the situation, if he doesn't care, that's what will happen anyway."

Wait. It felt like I had been struck in the head. In a good way.

"Hold on."

"Park Moondae?"

"I think Raebin's right." With a slightly clearer head, I picked up my smartphone.

It was still quiet. There was still no news about Cha Eugene, but looking at it another way, it meant there wasn't a single witness account.

Did that make any sense?

*It's been over five hours since Cha Eugene ran off.*

He didn't even have his own car. Was it really possible for a celebrity as famous as a TeSTAR member to wander around without a single reported sighting? Impossible.

*Unless he was being careful himself.*

"Cha Eugene is deliberately moving in a way that avoids being seen."

Bae Sejin and Kim Raebin stared.

"If he really wanted to cause damage to TeSTAR, he would have gone to a club or something, but he didn't," I explained.

It meant he was still moving with the ingrained habits of an idol trying to avoid people. I didn't know if he was drawing a line even in this mess, but I unconsciously recalled his words.

[But where I live during that time is my choice. It's my body, I'll do what I want. OK?]

[Your consideration has a purpose. That's not consideration, it's control. Control.]

*Was he... perhaps scared?*

Just then, Kim Raebin cautiously raised his hand again. "I'm not sure if it's my place to say this, but..."

"It's fine, you can speak freely."

"Yes." Kim Raebin replied with a serious expression. "I believe we need to have more of a conversation with that Cha Eugene."

Bae Sejin muttered gloomily, "That didn't work."

"Yes? Ah, I believe my choice of words was too broad! What I mean is..." Kim Raebin chose his words with utmost care before correcting himself. "I believe that up until now, we have been attempting to persuade Cha Eugene, not converse with him."

Bae Sejin was stunned.

"Or rather, our conversations were for the purpose of persuasion," Kim Raebin continued. "Because we never had a conversation without an agenda, I think it was difficult for us to build mutual trust."

"Wh-why are you looking at me like that?"

"It's nothing."

I guess both Bae Sejin and I were staring at Kim Raebin with astonishment. It was an incredibly sharp analysis, so sharp it was hard to believe it came from someone so dense with interpersonal relationships.

"Ahem. It's because you seem to be right," Bae Sejin admitted.

"Yes. I believe it was an unavoidable development, as it is natural to worry about a fellow member."

I looked at Kim Raebin.

*This kid has been thinking a lot, too.*

For someone who struggled with hidden meanings and guessing others' thoughts, he must have gone through quite a process to reach that conclusion.

*So that's why he's been so quiet lately.*

Kim Raebin seemed confused by our reactions but confident in his words. "I don't know what that Cha Eugene is thinking either... but that's why I want to find out."

I looked at him and nodded.

"......"

Kim Raebin brightened.

"I agree."

I was curious what he was thinking, too. Since he had already crossed the line, it might be better to just have a frank conversation.

"Thank you!" Kim Raebin said.

I should have been the one thanking him.

"In any case, we have to find him first," I said.

"Yes."

I turned my smartphone screen back on. Now that I knew he was trying not to be seen, the list of possible locations narrowed. Both his transport and his destination.

*A place an idol would go.*

Bae Sejin nodded. "Then... we should be able to narrow it down based on what we know."

We focused on what we knew about the Cha Eugene from ❬Idol Inc.❭. It was obvious who would know the most about that.

"Did Cha Eugene have any places he used to go during his trainee days?" I asked.

It was Kim Raebin who answered earnestly, his expression serious. "I believe he preferred crowded places!"

That wasn't very helpful.

"Could you be more specific?"

"Outdoor cafes with nice scenery, or barbecue restaurants... I think they were noisy, open spaces. Ah, and I think he preferred being outdoors over indoors, no matter where it was!"

"Ah, that sounds like places that remind Cha Eugene of his hometown," Bae Sejin noted.

We continued narrowing down the possibilities. Then, a question came up.

*If he felt like he just wanted to escape from here...*

There was a chance he had gone far.

"Did you guys ever hang out outside of Seoul during your trainee days?"

"Excluding my family's home... hmm, we did go to the seaside once," Kim Raebin recalled.

"The seaside?"

"It was Ganghwa Island."

In that moment, something clicked.

*Cha Eugene lived near the sea in San Diego.*

When he ran off during the tour, I had found him in a cafe near the sea, and...

[That guy... he came to Ganghwa Island for a variety show he filmed back in his STier days.]

The coast of Ganghwa Island. It was the filming location for STier's first variety show.

"There."

"Yes?"

"Let's search it."

~~~

Two hours later.

"Cha Eugene!"

STier Cha Eugene was about to meet a friend.
