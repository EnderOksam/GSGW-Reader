# Chapter 634

An upward trend. Soaring high. People used that phrase when a celebrity suddenly blew up. When you were the one riding that momentum, you couldn't possibly ignore it.

First, the messages poured in.

[Spacer Kwon Heeseung: Hyung-nim! I saw the news lately and my heart started pounding, so I had to message you haha]

[T-Holic Ha Jintae: Oh, congratulations to our TeSTAR friends haha. So this is how a Bill artist is born]

[Golden Age Ha Iljoon: TeSTAR congrats congrats, I'm jealous and you're so cool, Moondae]

[Mirynay Park Minha: Sunbaenim, thank you for being considerate of our schedule for variety shows and such during this comeback. I'll be cheering for you to finish your promotions well. Thank you!]

Even without actively managing connections, people in the industry reached out when an acquaintance did exceptionally well. That was exactly our situation.

TeSTAR's already successful career was now on a remarkable upward trend. Our overseas career, built up over time, suddenly exploded. For the past few days, it felt like the internet and music sites had erupted with our name.

"I don't think I've gotten this many calls from my relatives since ❬Idol Inc.❭..."

"M-Me too."

"Everyone... do you know who Sejin even got a message from?"

"Who?"

"Don't be surprised. It was a sunbaenim from the disbanded group GY-ROP!"

"No way."

"...That's amazing in a different sense."

Even distant acquaintances contacted us to offer congratulations or ask how we were. The company staff even gossiped about who received the most messages. Our results at that time were...

[Bad feelings? / TeSTAR 4th]

Just before the third week of our remix promotions, TeSTAR had finally broken into the top five.

"Gosh..."

"Let's pray."

Naturally, the calls increased. The more we appeared in mainstream media, the more our stage and song were exposed, capturing a wider audience. It was also fun choosing which remix to perform based on the program's nature.

"Hmm, we didn't really have to go on that swimming variety show, though."

"It's summer and we're doing a sexy concept. Isn't that why we even tried on rash guards?"

Bae Sejin made the blunt remark, since he wasn't the one who wore it. The two who had appeared on the show just looked at each other and laughed.

Then, a staff member's voice called out.

"You have to go right now!"

"Whoops, yes!"

Our wait finally ended, and we ran to the backstage area. Of course, we first took photos for social media and filmed behind-the-scenes content.

"Won't wearing this many piercings be a bit polarizing for international audiences?"

"They said it looks cool, like death metal."

"......"

*I guess this is a season where we can really do anything.*

I touched the fake piercings on my lip and ear helix, checking my tight outfit for the day.

"You look cool, hyung! Don't worry."

"Alright."

Waiting below the stage always filled the air between us with a strange mix of tension, excitement, and anticipation. The way we got nervous and handled that tension had become lighter, but this one feeling remained the same.

*It might be because our hearts are still the same.*

Ryu Chungwoo looked back at me.

"It might be sudden to say, but."

He smiled faintly.

"The stage is fun."

"......"

I lightly tapped his shoulder.

"It looks like it."

"......"

The pressure to live up to expectations always existed because of what we had achieved, but I suppose the ones who could keep promoting were those who felt more fun than pressure. That was how we had spent the last seven years.

"Wow, look at Chungwoo-hyung's shoulders. Eugene, come here~ Let's stick together."

"My shoulders are bigger!"

Their bickering ended when the cue came. We rushed out onto the open stage.

Cheers erupted.

And so, week three of promotions began. The first Bill chart prediction for that week came out.

***

The hotel suite was filled only with the sound of breathing and moving hands.

HAA... HAA...

Silence. White noise.

Amidst it all, Ryu Chungwoo moved his smartphone. The members sat on the sofa, still in their makeup from the schedule. They tensed up, waiting for Ryu Chungwoo to speak.

The next moment.

"First place."

"...!"

"This one... second place."

First place. Second place. First, first. Second, second... The rank predictions kept coming in the silence.

"......"

All eyes turned to me.

I took the smartphone from Ryu Chungwoo and reviewed the score prediction method. After another piercing silence, I spoke.

"It's not certain, but, yes. The possibility is high enough."

"...Then?"

I grinned.

"We can get first place."

"......"

"Yeaaaaah!"

"Holy shit!"

In an instant, the room erupted with excitement and exclamations. Climbing the charts again with remixes was an unbelievable achievement. I grinned and handed the smartphone back to Ryu Chungwoo.

Just then, Lee Sejin suddenly slumped onto the sofa.

"...!"

"Are you okay??"

"Huh? Yeah."

Lee Sejin buried his face in his hands.

"It's just... for some reason, I can't quite believe it."

"......"

"Why."

In response to my question, a dazed mumble echoed through the room.

"I guess I... hmm, I never expected to become this successful. No, I guess I should say I couldn't..."

The room fell silent again.

Bae Sejin answered as if tossing the words out.

"...You always said TeSTAR would definitely succeed. You even said let's win a Bill award."

"Ayy, that was just... my feeling at the time, and for morale~ It's about saying the right thing at the right time! That kind of thing."

Lee Sejin laughed like air escaping a balloon, then quickly cleared his throat and adjusted his tone.

"It's just... uh, I never wished for something like this in my life. Winning a Grand Prize in Korea, and getting first place overseas, this..."

"......"

"And we also got caught up in... strange things and lived other lives, right? So if you look at it objectively..."

"Objectively?"

After a brief silence, he continued.

"Hmm, about Sejin's star quality as an idol? Haha."

He glossed it over like a joke, but I knew what he meant.

*...Did he think he wasn't at this level?*

That guy, who had always wanted to be an idol, held a brutally realistic standard for himself even while dreaming. He had likely already made his own judgment.

*Something like, 'Ah, this is the level of achievement I'm capable of'...*

It was unbelievable that he could push himself to the limit without giving up, despite thinking that. Now, the guy who had achieved an unbelievable result spoke in a quiet, slightly trembling voice.

"Thank you, my members, really. For being TeSTAR for so long."

*[We'll continue to do well like this, right?]*

Our seventh year. He had been saying those words since our debut. Recalling them now, from him who always encouraged team activities, they felt different from his usual jokes.

It seemed I wasn't the only one who noticed.

"N-No! It's because you did well, Sejin...!"

"...!"

"That's right!"

I added a word as well.

"Don't make us state the obvious again."

"......"

A thin smile formed on Lee Sejin's lips. His face was still covered by his hands. He was a fool in strange ways.

The moment I thought that, another fool appeared.

"However... th-that feeling of gratitude, I think I know what you mean. I'm... similar."

I turned my head.

Seon Ahyeon met my eyes, blushing, but spoke softly.

"I... rather than wanting to become a singer, I just wanted to keep dancing."

Seon Ahyeon, who had chosen a place to escape to instead of the dance industry, slowly continued.

"I thought it was a position I didn't deserve because I was lacking compared to the other members... Of course, even now, I think this group is too good for me...!"

"I was probably worse. I wanted to act, not perform on stage."

Bae Sejin, who answered without hesitation, cleared his throat and subtly lowered his gaze.

"Besides... my skills were ridiculously lacking compared to you guys."

"Hey, you weren't that bad."

"And you're definitely not now."

"No, I still am."

He added in a firm tone.

"However, I'm no longer at a point where I'm ashamed of myself."

This time, no one teased him. Instead, a faint smile spread among the members.

Ryu Chungwoo also smiled and spoke.

"Since this is my second attempt, it wasn't my dream from a young age either. Hmm... right. If you put it that way, I admit I didn't know I'd come this far."

Cha Eugene said with bright eyes.

"I knew."

"Hey."

Of course he would. The members snickered at Cha Eugene Ignacio, who just last week had no dreams about Bill. He grumbled nonchalantly.

"I meant I knew our team would do well! It's not necessarily about Bill. [I just believed that we would do well in any field.]"

Right, I get it.

*[I don't believe in frames created by others.]*

Frames. TeSTAR is only strong domestically. Throughout our promotions, since our debut, we had always heard we were weak overseas compared to our domestic popularity. The idea that we could never close that gap had become the default assumption.

For that to be overturned... right, there was something more to it than just the results.

"Success in the Anglosphere is not inherently more valuable than results elsewhere, but it is a great joy to have achieved success in such a huge music market."

Kim Raebin added, his voice slightly choked with emotion.

"I... despite the many ups and downs... thank you for believing in and trusting my producing. I'd like to take this opportunity to thank you all once again..."

"Raebin, that sounds exactly like an acceptance speech."

"Yeah. It's like when we debuted from the survival show."

Though their words were teasing, the members put their arms around Kim Raebin's shoulders or looked at him with broad smiles.

I raised an arm too.

"You worked hard."

After all this time, we were standing here.

Then, all eyes turned to the one person who hadn't shared his feelings.

"......"

Me.

I slowly looked around at the guys and recalled my past.

"I used to be the one taking pictures of guys like you performing on stage."

"......"

"I like being among you guys more. ...Thank you."

"...!"

*To think a day would come when I'm not gritting my teeth to change my shitty surroundings, but am instead grateful for my environment.*

If someone had told my college-aged self this... I would have told them to fuck off.

*You really never know what life has in store.*

"Then, Moondae Moondae, if you could go back to the past and take pictures of only one of us, who would it be?"

"I would have photographed all of you."

Because the price for each of you would have been good. I was a data seller, not a fansite master.

However, before I could add the last part, a barrage of accusations hit me, telling me not to evade the question.

"Just pick one!"

These guys, but it soon turned to laughter.

"It's all a joke. Hmm, really... we're the ones who are thankful, hyung."

"......"

"For being an idol with us."

I quietly nodded.

"Let's all thank each other!"

"That's a good idea."

The guys huddled together like penguins and started patting each other on the back. Doing this in a hotel room, not on a broadcast, was ridiculous, yet, strangely, some of them started sniffling.

"Ah, we're getting too emotional already. What if we don't get first place after all this~"

"It doesn't matter. The numbers don't change the fact that we did well."

"...That's true."

Still gathered like penguins, they started talking about the current situation.

"What do you think the trend will be from now on?"

"It's still on the rise."

It was now hard to find anyone saying our success was just due to the number of remixes. Both remixes went beyond getting a meaningful response. The song itself had become so famous that factions were forming and fighting over which version they preferred.

"They said even our original song has rebounded a lot."

"Really? That's amazing!"

As the song climbed the charts, more people listened, and disputes arose. Thanks to contrarians who wanted a third option, many people started listening to the original song as well.

"Hmm, this would be a difficult method to use again later."

"Right."

Just as Ryu Chungwoo said, it was a special situation that was very difficult to replicate.

"First, the song has to be good, all three versions~ It needs to have recognition, and at the same time, people can't get tired of it or lose interest from overexposure to the same song."

"So in the end, it wouldn't have been possible without Raebin."

"...?"

"That's right."

"......"

Kim Raebin whipped his head around to look at his team members who were patting his head. What a funny guy.

"And Moondae timed the promotion period perfectly."

"We decided on that after discussing it."

I was just trying to extract the maximum value from the two conditions: the combined remix scoring method and the collaboration with Limestone.

However, after Kim Raebin, I received a baptism of countless hands on my shoulders and head.

"I'm saying you did well."

"Anyway, you're uncharacteristically humble, Moondae Moondae."

...*I'll let it slide this time.*

"Let's promote without any regrets. Let's not get too obsessed with the numbers themselves, but let's be proud."

"Let's do a lot of activities the fans will like, too."

"Of course!"

That night, we deliberately spread blankets in the hotel living room and slept together as if we were at a training camp.

"This reminds me of ❬Idol Inc.❭."

"......"

Maybe it was because we had been teasing Kim Raebin and brought up stories from back then.

The night before the finale flickered in my mind. I remembered sleeping together in the dorm, uncertain of who would debut. That event was from years ago.

A quiet voice came from beside me.

"Moondae Moondae. I know we said it doesn't matter what the result is... but."

"......"

"Still, I hope the result is good."

I answered just by mouthing the words.

Yeah.

That summer.

TeSTAR hit number one on the Bill Hot100.
