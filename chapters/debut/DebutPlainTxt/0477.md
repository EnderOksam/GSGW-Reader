# Chapter 477

"......"

Cha Eugene took a slow breath. The sharp, fresh scent of the rough coastline filled his lungs. Even in summer, the dawn air by the water was bearably warm. This Ganghwa Island beach reminded him of the beaches in San Diego.

He used to recover his pace easily in moments like this.

His head, however, wasn't clearing up now. To be precise, Cha Eugene could no longer just blow away the 'loser-like feeling' like he was dusting it off. It wasn't possible anymore.

*This is fucked.*

He decided to at least try to be calm. It seemed he could manage to keep his mouth shut and endure for thirty days here, as long as no one was around.

However, a voice called out to him.

"Cha Eugene!"

"...!"

He turned his head and saw a dim, masked figure. The figure wasn't dark, but because he ran with the dawn sunrise at his back, the light cast him in shadow.

Cha Eugene knew who it was just from the dark silhouette. It was someone he had seen for years.

[...Goodbye. Cha Eugene.]

The colleague who had said a final goodbye on the way back to his home country.

"Kim Raebin."

The moment the name left his lips, he realized.

*He's not the same person.*

His stomach churned. In the midst of it all, 'Kim Raebin'—his name was the same, anyway—ran along the coast and shouted.

"You were really here?!"

*To think he found me.*

*Did the Cha Eugene of this place have some special memory of this location too?*

The thought was utterly sickening.

"I'm here, but aren't you looking for your friend?"

"......"

"Then go back. It seems that person isn't here. I'm not your friend."

The words flowed out smoothly. Cha Eugene didn't bother to express the anger welling inside him and silently turned his head away.

It was then.

"No! You are my friend."

"...!"

Kim Raebin's voice grew louder.

Cha Eugene knew, however.

*He's not angry.*

Kim Raebin's voice held the firm confidence of someone sharing a newly discovered fact.

"I agree that the time we've experienced has been different since we participated in ❬Idol Inc.❭, but I realized the friendship we built before participating hasn't disappeared."

*Before participating?*

Cha Eugene reflexively questioned it, then understood. It meant before ❬Idol Inc.❭, the time they trained together at the same agency.

"In that case, I am the friend you met during your trainee days, and you are the Cha Eugene I studied with during our trainee days!"

"......"

"We can conclude that we are the same person!"

Cha Eugene was momentarily speechless. It wasn't because he had no rebuttal. It was just that Kim Raebin was so full of conviction, seeming to believe without a doubt that he had found a perfect line of logic.

*Good grief.*

That was not a sight he had seen often in the past few years, but it wasn't entirely unfamiliar either. He remembered seeing that side of him occasionally before they debuted, and a few times after, when preparing an album.

[If we proceed like this, we will definitely be able to deliver a polished performance while conveying a modern charm.]

*...No, that's not it. My friend was always like that.*

Kim Raebin was originally that kind of person. He seemed polite and humble at first glance, but he had a stubborn side that wouldn't compromise. He had self-assurance, a worldview so firm it was almost brazen, and the talent that prevented it from being mere obstinacy.

Seeing a good side of his old colleague again brought a unique sense of emotion.

"......"

Cha Eugene knew for sure.

*He's not the same person.*

He had even said it himself several times. This was a different person, not the one he knew.

Still, he couldn't help it.

*...But, there are similarities.*

Cha Eugene was only in his twenties. He couldn't reject the definite friendship proven in this strange world, where he was cornered with no way out.

He had no choice but to answer in a strained voice.

[You're right.]

It was his first acknowledgment.

Just then, a wave crashed. Cha Eugene turned his head.

*He probably didn't understand.*

However, a bigger response came back without him needing to repeat it in Korean.

"Yeah!"

*Huh?*

Cha Eugene pushed himself up from the rock.

"Kim Raebin, did you understand me?"

"I'm studying simple, word-level English for songwriting. It helps with matching punchlines."

"Oh."

For a moment, Cha Eugene almost asked what song he was composing, but he quickly remembered reality and stopped. It was meaningless.

"...?"

Kim Raebin didn't notice the unnatural pause. He decided to continue the conversation as he had resolved, taking advantage of the slightly more relaxed atmosphere. He recalled Park Moondae's advice.

[If you really meet Cha Eugene... just speak comfortably, say what you want to say.]

So he did just that. Kim Raebin first asked what he judged to be most important.

"Have you eaten?"

Food, clothing, and shelter. He probably hadn't slept, so it was right to at least make sure he ate.

Cha Eugene was momentarily flustered. He soon let out a small laugh and turned his gaze back to the sea.

"No."

"Then let's have breakfast, even if it's early."

"I'm fine."

He wasn't in the mood for it. At that, Kim Raebin looked at him, horrified.

"Are you so stressed that you're skipping meals?"

"......"

"...! Or perhaps, because you've lived so freely, you're feeling immense pressure from the current situation?"

That damn freedom.

Cha Eugene stared at the waves with his chin in his hand and answered without thinking.

"I worked at a repair shop."

"......"

His five years of activities had ended as if they had never happened. It was the work he started after returning to his country.

"My parents offered to give me their surfboard shop, but I said no."

The thought of dealing with idiots who just surfed and goofed off all day seemed incredibly annoying. It was a contradictory feeling. He wanted to be stuck in a pathetic, wasted life, but he didn't want to meet idiots who were wasting their lives.

"A car repair shop?"

"......"

"Was it fun?"

"Not really."

But he didn't hate it that much. Cha Eugene had never been bad at anything. He quickly adapted to the manual labor at the repair shop.

"......"

"It was nice not having to think about anything."

"......"

Why did STier's Cha Eugene return to his country as soon as his contract ended? The truth was, he had received offers from other agencies. They weren't major, but a few smaller ones had reached out. He might have been able to release another album or two.

But he didn't.

[No.]

*I've already come too far.*

He realized he had done something stupid.

"......"

Originally, he had a very simple life philosophy learned from his grandfather.

[If you can't see a solution, find one.]

[If you absolutely can't find one, give up cleanly and try a new direction.]

Cha Eugene had lived his life thinking and acting that way until he debuted. However, the past few years as 'STier's Cha Eugene' had been different. He couldn't 'give up cleanly'.

*A member gets arrested for drugs, a song we didn't choose becomes the title track, the group's name is attached to a project I didn't participate in, an album gets canceled...*

And rumors. Rumors and more rumors, and a situation beyond his control. Even though no fundamental solution was in sight, he endured by making the best choice at every moment.

Of course, at the time, he felt it was all worth it. It didn't matter if it was a waste.

But after pouring everything into it, nothing was left in the end.

[STier, contract terminated.]

"......"

It was an ending with no growth, no reversal. Once he'd come that far, he had to admit it. He had missed the timing to give up.

"But this place makes me think too much."

That's why it's painful. This world, where the price for his stupid choice not to give up was given in a bizarre way, was too perfect. Everyone is happy, and everything is successful.

[I just... don't understand why I have to see this fucking world where everything is perfect.]

"......"

Cha Eugene muttered the words in Spanish, something he couldn't even bring himself to say in English.

Kim Raebin didn't press him on what he had said. He just sat quietly, trying to grasp the meaning in his tone with his own less-than-sensitive social skills.

*He seems to be having a hard time.*

That core understanding was enough. So Kim Raebin decided to just sit quietly next to Cha Eugene and watch the sea with him. He knew he wasn't good with words.

"......"

"......"

It was the right choice.

SHHHH-

The waves reflected the light and rippled gently. In front of the peaceful sea, Cha Eugene felt a slight sense of liberation, the kind you could get from saying words aloud that no one could understand.

Thanks to that, he was able to nod at Kim Raebin's next words.

"It's 8 o'clock now, so it's a good time for breakfast."

"......"

The two of them stood up from the seaside rock. Their footprints were left neatly in a straight line.

***

After they had breakfast at a Korean set-menu restaurant with a private room, which Kim Raebin had found out about from the members in advance, Kim Raebin felt comfortable enough to ask his own questions.

"Cha Eugene, do you happen to hate the hyungs?"

"......"

He just said it outright.

Cha Eugene, who was drinking water, almost choked but managed to hold it back. Thinking that he had left the dorm anyway, he decided to give an honest answer.

"They make me uncomfortable."

"Is it because you feel they're trying to infringe on your freedom for the sake of the other Cha Eugene? Or because they project that Cha Eugene onto you when they deal with you?"

"Umm... yeah."

Cha Eugene responded hesitantly, taken aback by how blunt Kim Raebin was. After some thought, he came up with an even more honest and specific answer. It was because of their goodwill, guilt, and a little bit of worry.

"And... 'Moondae-hyung'?"

*Huh?*

Kim Raebin's head snapped up.

Cha Eugene was frowning strangely.

"I think that guy is suspicious."

"...!"

"That guy, he knows too much about me."

Kim Raebin's jaw dropped.

"That... Moondae-hyung originally remembers a lot about all of us, and since he's in charge of this group's planning..."

"Nope! That's not what I mean. It's about me."

"...?"

"Me, not you guys."

Cha Eugene pointed to himself. He already had a feeling. The person Kim Raebin called 'Moondae-hyung' knew way too much about STier's Cha Eugene.

Objectively speaking, STier was a group that didn't end well.

*And they were even a boy band.*

It would be hard for another man to know the circumstances that well. The most he could expect was for someone to have heard the name once, and even then, only in relation to the bad rumors.

But what about the attitude of the person called 'Park Moondae'?

[Shut up.]

[...It's okay if you don't watch that.]

He stops me at crucial moments, exactly as if he knows what his group's triggers are, and based on facts, at that.

"Too much, I mean... it's not natural."

"......"

Cha Eugene couldn't explain it in detail to this Kim Raebin, but he wanted to at least warn him that something was off. So he said it.

However.

"Then let's ask him directly!"

"......"

Kim Raebin immediately picked up his smartphone.

"Kim Raebin, are you crazy?"

"I'm not crazy, you idiot!"

"......"

Just as Cha Eugene was taken aback by the Korean curse word he hadn't heard in a while, Kim Raebin nonchalantly made the call.

"Yes, hyung!"

A short while later, one more person was added to the room of the set-menu restaurant on the still-uncrowded Ganghwa Island beach.

"......"

"......"

"Hello."

'Moondae-hyung's' under-eyes had become sunken in just one day. He already had a tired, weary face, but now he looked even more exhausted.

*Hmm...*

Cha Eugene felt a faint sense of guilt, but he knew his wariness had to come first. He was about to compose himself, but Kim Raebin blurted out Cha Eugene's questions directly to Park Moondae.

"Hyung, Cha Eugene says he has some questions for you!"

*Kim Raebin!*

As Cha Eugene screamed internally, he met Park Moondae's eyes, sharpening his reflexes for any unforeseen situation.

The 'Moondae-hyung' in front of him slightly bowed his head.

"Um, that's."

Cha Eugene, who was sharp when it came to interpersonal relationships, immediately noticed the nuance of that gesture.

This person...

"Because I was interested."

"......"

"I was just interested, so I just kept up with your activities a bit."

This person was embarrassed.

*Good grief.*

Park Moondae. The former Ryu Gunwoo had just outed himself as a fan at the most unimaginable timing, right in front of the idol himself.
