# Chapter 533

Aether has a bad opinion of us.

"...Shouldn't it be the other way around?"

"I think they misunderstand, believing we're the ones who caused the controversy."

"......"

In TeSTAR's waiting room, after Aether greeted them and left, a moment of silence passed. Bae Sejin spoke with a sour expression.

"Considering what their company did to us?"

"They probably don't know."

They were just kids who probably hadn't even gotten their national ID cards yet. How could they be mature enough to grasp the whole situation? They were just siding with their company.

*And to think Wonder Hall still has a mole planted among us...*

Anyway, TeSTAR had been through too much to be shaken by some snot-nosed kids who held a grudge without knowing the full story. Hadn't there been that guy who smirked while fabricating controversy around TeSTAR's Rookie of the Year award?

*We've fought with broadcasting stations. We've come too far to be scared of some brats now.*

So, TeSTAR came to a conclusion.

"Well~ they'll figure it out. Let's just focus on our own work and get it done!"

"Yep!"

TeSTAR quickly changed and headed to the set.

"To all the artists who have returned, we offer our thanks and a warm welcome!"

"WOOOAH!"

The first episode of ❬Genuine Match❭'s second half opened with a party concept. The strategy was likely to raise the curtain with a glamorous party, then use editing to contrast it with the suffering of the survival show that would immediately follow.

*How typical.*

Still, the fans would probably love seeing TeSTAR in tuxedos.

I sipped non-alcoholic champagne and greeted the other cast members for the camera. Of course, these were people TeSTAR had already greeted in the waiting room.

It was a bit funny attending the opening event when TeSTAR planned to voluntarily withdraw after just one performance. That was all the more reason to work hard so no one could say anything.

Perhaps because they knew TeSTAR would be leaving soon, the other singers seemed less wary than before. Besides, TeSTAR had some recent news that was easy to bring up.

"Congratulations on your comeback, TeSTAR! The music video was amazing!"

This was it.

"Thank you."

After greeting a member of SoftSweet in a light green dress, I turned around. Another group of guys rushed over, bowing their heads deeply.

It was Aether.

"Congratulations on your comeback."

They were visibly tense, as if determined to be more careful in front of the cameras. However, they still couldn't hide the look in their eyes.

*Good grief.*

Moreover, it seemed the look in the Leader's eyes had spread to the others. They must have talked amongst themselves.

"Ah, thank you. Aether, your last performance was great. I'm looking forward to this one too."

Ryu Chungwoo offered some appropriate praise for their stage. Looking at the guy's eyes as he watched Ryu Chungwoo...

*Is it something like, 'I'll beat you no matter what'?*

A venom they didn't have before had appeared.

*Those Wonder Hall bastards are profiting from this again.*

Those guys probably saw the company's pain as their own. The basic logic that the company's position was their own must have subconsciously taken root.

*Once a mindset like this is established, it's hard to change.*

Why would idols, who are essentially freelancers, develop the sense of ownership that companies from the dark ages demanded of their employees?

"Yep~ Thank you!"

In any case, TeSTAR handled the situation smoothly and without incident. It takes two to tango, and since TeSTAR acted this way, there was probably no tension for the cameras to capture.

"Now then, the theme for the grand first act of ❬Genuine Match❭'s second half is..."

TeSTAR listened to the MC's boisterous introduction of the stage theme and safely finished filming the opening. Then, as usual, TeSTAR quickly began their stage meeting.

"So, should we go with this?"

"Sounds good!"

It was settled in an instant. This team had a wealth of experience with original performances and had already fully grasped each other's capabilities. On top of that, they were all close, so no disruptive elements in their meetings allowed them to reach a conclusion quickly. Winning first place might depend on the audience's taste that day, but this would easily be enough to rank in the top tier.

*We have some breathing room.*

Soon, after conducting the interviews, the filming wrapped up smoothly.

"We'll be moving in 10 minutes."

"Yep~"

It was a smooth process. They spent the short break in a mostly ordinary way.

"Kim Raebin, Smile!"

"......"

Choosing photos to post on SNS, packing clothes and electronics, and even one member solving a Sudoku puzzle. It was an everyday scene. Not a single member looked anxious.

"Um, is no one curious about what kind of stage Aether will do?"

Lee Sejin asked me while handing me a drink. I gave a short nod.

"Probably."

TeSTAR was already short on time because of the comeback. Everyone had unconsciously concluded that there was no need to split their time further to invest in meticulous information warfare.

One of the staff members even said that if TeSTAR's stage plan was leaked and Aether copied it and performed it first, they didn't think TeSTAR would lose. That's how big the difference in their capabilities seemed.

The guy who asked me this must have known all this, so for him to ask anyway meant...

*You don't like it.*

"Huh? Hey... it's not that!"

He said that, but his smile faded slightly.

"It's just... I feel a sense of alarm, I guess."

A sense of alarm?

I put down my drink to show I was listening.

"Do you remember our time on ❬Idol Inc.❭? I was so desperate to do well back then. I wanted to debut so badly."

I remember, and it wasn't just back then.

"You were like that even after debuting."

"Heh, I guess so, right?"

Yeah, he was even like that during TeSTAR's first concert.

[I have to do better than Cha Eugene in the unit stage this time.]

I remembered how this guy had insisted on a duet with Cha Eugene and succeeded in receiving high praise. He was also the type to grit his teeth and push through in the virtual world where he debuted as GY-ROP, so no further explanation was needed.

"But back then, the whole team's atmosphere was like that. How should I put it... like we were trying to sprint full-force through everything without pacing ourselves."

"......"

At least, I was. I had no choice, since I would die if I didn't get good results.

Keun Sejin's eyes narrowed slightly.

"But now, maybe because we've risen so high, it feels like that desperation is gone. ...Of course, that includes me."

"...!"

Keun Sejin muttered in a slightly subdued voice.

"What should I call it, a desperate desire for improvement? A sense of crisis...?"

"......"

"The team runs fine now even without that."

It was true. The team's atmosphere was less desperate than before. It had become much calmer. The group was stable, and there were no conflicts regarding direction, part distribution, or positions. TeSTAR had settled in well with the agency too.

*The days of walking on thin ice, pouring our all into every single schedule for fear of not making it, were over.*

The group wouldn't be shaken now unless some supernatural phenomenon occurred, like losing their memories.

*Even I was free from the situation where I'd die if I didn't get good results.*

Compared to the past few years of running for survival in one crazy situation after another, this was no different from soaking in lukewarm water.

*However, it also means we're living like human beings.*

A person can't live every day racking their brain like they're in a death survival game.

As if having a similar thought, Keun Sejin soon narrowed his eyes and added jokingly.

"Hmm, now that I say it out loud, it sounds like I'm complaining about having it too good, huh?"

"I didn't say that."

"Moondae Moondae, you're getting all serious again!"

The guy grinned. However, I didn't smile back.

"Of course, I'm not saying it's a bad thing... It's just, it feels a little strange."

He probably thought the situation was stable in his head, but a part of him seemed to feel uncomfortable. Now that the chaotic season with the members' memory problems had passed and things had quieted down, it seemed to stand out even more.

*...Hmm.*

I brought up the guy's Status Window.

[Name: Lee Sejin]

Vocal: B (B+)

Dance: S- (S)

Looks: A (A+)

Talent: A+ (A+)

Trait: Be Quiet

The stats this guy had raised over the past few years were already equivalent to 7p in System points. It was proof that he had been working on self-improvement to an absurdly relentless degree.

*His vocal and dance stats have changed by a whole letter grade.*

The stats, raised closer to their limits, truly showed his desperate attitude of never letting his guard down and training relentlessly even after his debut.

"Moondae Moondae, I was just saying, so don't worry about it too much..."

"Is that so. I kind of agree."

"...! Really?"

"......"

I nodded at the guy, who seemed a little surprised. It was only natural for a guy like him to feel anxious if things weren't intense. I was the same way to some extent.

"But how long do you think this stable period will last? With so many incidents happening every year, we'll have to get desperate again soon, even if we don't want to."

"......"

"Just relax a little, at least until an unexpected accident happens."

People need to recover, too.

"And you're already overflowing with the desire to improve. I can guarantee that."

"...Really?"

"......"

"Okay. ...Thanks."

"Don't mention it."

Without any protest, the guy opened his drink again and drank it, looking slightly relieved. I was lost in thought for a moment.

*Is this a wariness of falling into a rut?*

This might be a concern that naturally arises around this point in one's career after achieving success. Thinking about it, it reminds me of someone who was at this stage in his career when I debuted.

I'm talking about VTIC's Cheongryeo.

*I wonder if he had similar thoughts.*

Not that my attitude toward him would change now. However, I could now understand on a more visceral level why he had acted that way.

*Ah, come to think of it...*

I happen to have a short break tomorrow morning. Since it came to mind, I decided to take care of the favor he had asked of me when I had the time.

~~~

"......"

I casually scooped up the yellow furball that came running across the artificial grass on the rooftop. It was Cheongryeo's dog, Kongi.

"Hold on."

Let me take a picture.

I avoided the dog as it tried to lick my face and handed him over to the person called the 'dog teacher'. Then, before he could get sad, I quickly took a picture and took him back.

"Whine..."

"He's a very good boy, but he seems to miss his hyung a lot~"

How professional of them not to call him 'dad', considering his 'hyung's' profession is an idol.

Only after confirming that the dog was healthy, eating well, and playing well did I get up to leave. I was so damn busy I didn't have any more time to spend with him.

As I was leaving through the reservation-only passage, a person carrying a cage near the private parking lot flinched and started waving their hands frantically.

"Oh, oh, Moondae?"

What is it.

*Is it someone who recognizes me from TeSTAR?*

Only when the guy got closer did I recognize his face and realize it was someone I knew.

"Hello."

It was a member of T-Holic. The guy, Ha Jintae, stared at me with a look of wonder.

"Moondae, do you have a dog too?"

"No. It's a favor for an acquaintance."

"Oh... Ah, is it for that friend, Shin Jaehyun?"

How did he know.

"...Yes."

"Wow, you two must be really close."

*It's just that that bastard has no other acquaintances.*

However, I suppressed a twitching eyebrow and tried to start walking again. Let's not drag this out. I'm already short on time.

However, his next words almost made me trip.

"Woodan was the one who recommended this place to me."

I couldn't believe my ears.

"VTIC's Judan?"

"Yeah. He's good with this kind of information."

I couldn't believe that this friendly older guy was acquainted with that socially awkward guy, but I guess all sorts of relationships exist in the world.

I'm not interested.

"Ah... I see. I should ask him about it next time. Well, I hope you have a good time dropping off your dog."

"Oh, thank you. Thanks!"

However, instead of stopping, the T-Holic guy started following me as I walked toward the parking lot.

"...?"

"Well, I have something to ask."

"Yes?"

"About our agency. The kids from Aether who debuted from Wonder Hall, what were they like when you met them?"

He's asking this so openly?

*What's his intention?*

I replied nonchalantly, not showing any agitation.

"They seemed like hardworking juniors."

"Hmm... Right."

The T-Holic member nodded.

"You probably didn't get the feeling that they're comfortable enough to play around on stage yet. The agency is obsessed with stages for TikTakTok these days, so that's all they make the kids do."

I get it. He really must not be planning to renew his contract with Wonder Hall.

"Is that so."

"It is."

Despite my soulless reply, the T-Holic member cheerfully spoke again. However, his tone was slightly different this time.

"Still, don't underestimate them. Kids who've been trainees at a major agency for a long time usually have a certain skill."

The T-Holic member gave a slightly bitter smile.

"Perseverance."

~~~

At the same time Park Moondae was having an unexpected encounter with a T-Holic member at a pet facility, Aether's Leader was in their dorm. He monitored something with a serious expression.

It was a music video he had replayed several times.

[TeSTAR 'Hunting season' Official MV]

It was TeSTAR's latest comeback music video.
