# Chapter 347

That was Park Moondae, or rather Keundal, who was using Ryu Gunwoo's body. Right now, it seemed that he was controlling the pop-up.

No, I think he was having a bit too much fun...

[Can I use emojis too? Like this... ❭_❬ Heok!]

"Yeah, that's amazing."

He was doing things I never could have imagined him doing.

I held my breath and got up. It was a pity that Ryu Chungwoo was out on the phone with the company. At least it would have been funny.

[I'm sorry if I was reckless... Heck, it's the comeback season now, so if you're missing something...]

"No."

I stopped the pop-up window from expanding. Who did he think I was?

"I'm fine, so don't worry about that. I was a bit surprised because I was concentrating on checking my stats for the first time in a while."

[Yes...]

It seemed pretty genuine. I twitched my eyebrows at the pop-up window that seemed to convey even subtle nuances despite being only text.

Still, I didn't know where I could use it, so I checked it out.

"Looking at what you're saying now, it looks like you can see my situation. How much can you see?"

[Just to the extent that hyung sees... Yes. It seems to be the same!]

"Anything other than sight?"

[The sound is a bit muffled, but I can hear it. Other than that, I don't feel anything else!]

Now he had an answer ready.

"Always?"

[No! I think it only happens when you access the status window like this. Something... When you turn on the status window, it feels like an electronic signal is coming to me!]

Right.

So, when I turned on the status window, a signal was sent to this guy, and the two became synchronized.

*If I'm in a hurry, I could use it.*

The possibility was extremely low, but if, for example, I got stranded on an uninhabited island again within a few months, I could use it. I nodded.

"Okay. It's nice to have one more way to contact you."

Unless he was on the verge of death, that is.

[Yes??]

The pop-up window vibrated slightly. This... I hoped it was because he was excited.

Then, in an even smaller font, a new message slowly appeared, as if he were hesitating.

[I... Can I contact you when you open the status window?]

I chuckled.

"Of course."

[Thank you!]

The window grew again. It seemed to reflect his personality quite well.

And he started pouring out his impressions in earnest.

[Oh, my heart was so excited to see you checking your stats! I wanted to see the first live show in real life, but it's a shame I can't go!]

"Does it seem useful to you?"

[Yes! I'm so excited that my heart is about to come out of my mouth!]

He made watching TeSTAR a source of joy during his entrance-exam life, so why did he sound like he was using the words fans use?

I opened my mouth for an answer, but the text that came up quickly came first.

[Really, fighting! Oh, I'm going to go to work now! Lunch break is over.]

"......"

That was how the pop-up window disappeared with a glitch in an instant.

*It's like a real KakaoTalk.*

...I guess it would take some time to get used to. I rubbed my shoulders and turned off the rest of the status window.

It was time to move on and return to reality.

The important thing now was the first stage.

*The preparations are over.*

The amount of practice, actual performance experience, and even a trump card were all in place.

...Somehow, it didn't feel like a performance; it felt like we were going to the World's Best Martial Arts Competition.

I laughed while shaking my head.

*Well, there's not much difference.*

It was just that we wouldn't have to wield swords, so wasn't it the same to have results that wouldn't be embarrassing?

*Let's go to the real sword fight.*

Since it was a live performance, we couldn't add any special effects or devices. The only thing we had was a firecracker originally installed on the stage.

So, it was better. If people were impressed by this stage, they wouldn't be able to give credit to anything else other than the people who performed it, TeSTAR.

"Moondae. We have to go to the practice room...!"

"Okay."

I left the room.

The first stage performance was right around the corner.
