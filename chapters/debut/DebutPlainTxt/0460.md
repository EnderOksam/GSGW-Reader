# Chapter 460

"Gasp! Hello, TeSTAR!"

"Hello!"

We walked through the open office door. The creators of ❬Section 127❭ had opened it for us. Their new office was smaller than their old one at T1 Plays, but it overflowed with energy. A torrent of enthusiastic greetings poured out.

"I didn't expect you to contact us. It's a real pleasure to see you! It feels like it's been such a long time..."

"Haha, yes. This is the first time we're meeting in person since the collaboration. Ah, this is... a bit late, but it's a gift for your new office."

"You didn't have to... Wow, thank you."

Ryu Chungwoo handed them SF-concept mood lights, one for each employee. The representatives' faces lit up, as expected from a company so passionate about its themes.

*I knew this would work.*

I was glad I had ignored Cha Eugene's vehement opposition. Talk of the past came up naturally.

"Seeing this reminds me of when we worked on the tutorial characters together! You were so great to talk to and made it so much fun. We really enjoyed it."

"Really~? We felt the same way!"

The atmosphere grew even more amicable. It was true that we had worked well together back then without any friction.

That might have been part of the reason the meeting was arranged so quickly, but there was likely a more practical reason. I scanned the representatives' faces.

*It's about managing connections.*

These people had kicked T1 to the curb and walked out on their own. They had incredible guts, but they couldn't be completely free of worry. After all, they were on bad terms with a major corporation.

Even if T1 wasn't the biggest player in the gaming world, its influence on the culture industry and platforms was substantial.

*Starting over from scratch as a startup must be pretty stressful.*

In that situation, I would also accept a meeting request from a proven star marketing partner who had delivered great promotional results.

*And if our situations are similar, they'd be curious.*

Our side had also kicked T1 to the curb and even left the company that came after. So it made sense they might mistake us for 'comrades'. That was what I was aiming for.

We exchanged a few more words as we moved to a conference room and sat down. Game posters, drawings, and figures decorated the space.

*There are ❬Section 127❭ characters, too.*

They probably didn't have the rights anymore, so I guessed it was more for sentimental value than to show off the company's work.

My eyes scanned the room and finally spotted a poster of an unfamiliar character. He wore an engineer's uniform and held a grotesque weapon made of flesh.

*This is...*

It wasn't in the company representatives' portfolios. Good.

"Is this a game character as well?"

"Ah, yes!"

"I think he looks really cool."

"Really? So it's your type of style, Moondae!"

Kim Raebin peered at the poster with curious eyes.

"Is it for a new game, by any chance?"

"He's the main representative character for a new game we're currently developing, but the launch schedule is quite a ways off..."

"Around when would that be?"

"The plan is... um, around the end of next year."

That was ages away.

*And he's not mentioning a specific quarter.*

It meant development wasn't proceeding smoothly or on a tight schedule. It was vague. It could even be pushed to the year after next.

*Hmm.*

I smiled to myself.

I carefully examined the poster again. It had a grotesque yet modern feel, and the quality was good. It suggested they still knew how to stay within the lines.

The male representative's eyes lit up, thinking we were interested. "So we'll probably start official promotions around summer next year. It's a story about an underworld train..."

Good grief.

*He's probably hoping TeSTAR will do another promotion or collaboration for them then.*

They were definitely a bit clumsy when it came to sales.

While listening intently, I subtly shifted my gaze.

Keun Sejin nodded at the representative, acting deeply interested.

However, I remembered his reaction when I told the members I had found 'Ruined Industrial Complex', the new company founded by the ❬Section 127❭ production team.

[Wow... I knew you'd be the one to find them, Moondae, for real! But you don't want to do another game collaboration, do you?]

He had acknowledged my find but told me to drop any thoughts of running the collaboration concept into the ground. Of course, I remembered my response to that, too.

[Of course not.]

That wasn't what we needed.

"Actually, after hearing you talk, it seems the games you guys at Ruined Factory... no, the Complex, make are still wonderfully unique and will be fun."

"...! Ah, thank you."

"That's why there's something I'd like to propose." I got to the point, the very reason we came here. "I'd like to make a request. Could you create a game for us?"

Not a game collaboration, but a real game.

"...!"

The representatives' eyes went wide.

They seemed to scan the other members' expressions, as if checking if I was joking. Unfortunately for them, none of the other members were surprised.

Instead, Ryu Chungwoo simply said, "Um, the scenario is already fully prepared."

"......"

The representatives looked at each other before one of them spoke.

"Well, developing a single game usually takes a very long time. Even with a scenario, creating the concept art, graphics, UI, coding, and implementing it all is a different story. It requires complex steps."

Plus points for their attitude. Instead of yelling at us for making a crazy suggestion, they kindly explained it from a layman's perspective.

*I knew it would be good to work with them.*

I smiled inwardly but kept a serious expression as I nodded.

"Of course, we're not shameless enough to take up too much of your time at Ruined Industrial Complex."

"Pardon?"

"Actually, we're not talking about the same kind of game you usually develop..."

I continued my explanation. I described a form of game content that would fit within the existing idol business. As such, its volume was small and specialized.

*This is definitely possible within the timeframe.*

We just had to grind like dogs and squeeze out our own labor.

The more I spoke, the more the representatives' expressions changed. It was definitely interest.

"Aah~ That's..."

Okay, this was working.

***

"Thank you~"

After a few more explanations and meetings, the contracts were signed in a flash.

Ryu Chungwoo sent the signed contract to the company and said brightly, "It's convenient that we have the authority for things like this."

"Yeah! It's definitely nice not having to listen to any unnecessary nagging!" Bae Sejin chimed in. He turned his head, looking unusually excited.

"This settles it, right? Aren't you going to do that... album prediction thing again?"

"Yes."

I supposed I had to.

Just then, a voice cut in. "Ah, Moondae, Moondae, one thing before that! I'm sure you all heard this, but just one more time."

Keun Sejin grinned. "Let's not go around on any broadcast saying things like, 'We're the company directors~'!"

"Hm?"

"You know, let's not talk about how we have contract authority or anything like that. Let's just stick to what we said before, 'We're producing the album!' and leave it at that!"

Mm, right. This was important.

If we didn't want to be held responsible when the company messed up, we needed to draw a line early on.

It was even more important now that we were independent. There was a greater risk of people equating us with the company.

Look at all the flak T1 got. There was no guarantee that ORBIT wouldn't follow the same path.

...And sometimes, you have to make choices that get criticized because of circumstances outsiders don't know about.

So.

*It doesn't matter if we don't gain anything from the 'company director' image.*

The image we could have as idols was enough.

"Eung, okay!"

"Mm... right."

"Sounds good~"

Some of them understood, while others seemed to wonder if it was necessary. In the end, everyone agreed.

After we entered the company conference room, someone asked, "Umm, Moondae must be tired too, so... should we do the album prediction after we go back to the dorm and rest...?"

"Mm, it's fine to do it now."

It wasn't like it used up my physical energy. I immediately connected my laptop to the conference room screen and started typing into the album production hologram.

*Company staff could walk in here by some slim chance, so I should hide the personnel section...*

I filled in the other parts properly.

[Album]

[Personnel : A]

[Kim Raebin, Kim(★★★★), Park(★★★★), Park(★★★)...]

[Capital : S]

[\5,000,000,000]

[Period : B]

[60 Days]

(Personnel Bonus + 150%)

(Capital Bonus + 200%)

*Start Production ← Click!

Everything looked almost the same. The personnel, the period. However, something else was attached behind the 'Personnel' part, which was abbreviated with an ellipsis.

*External Personnel.*

I thought of the external personnel we had brought on through the contract with 'Ruined Industrial Complex'. I also thought of the album project's direction we had created based on that.

[We won't reveal the critical weakness... that T1 is trying to block us.]

...If we just stick to the plan.

*For sure, an impressive album will be made.*

I pressed the 'Start Production' button. The effect credits began to burst forth.

[External Personnel : Setting]

[Plus Effect Occurs!]

Besides, using this method, it was surprisingly fine even if the physical album's production company wasn't one of the industry's top two like before.

*It doesn't matter if T1 interferes.*

[Production Company : Setting]

[Minus Effect Offset!]

*The concept... is also decided.*

[Concept : Game]

[Plus Effect Occurs!]

It wasn't just a game, but the system seemed to have consolidated it under that label. Well, it didn't matter.

*The important thing is that effects have occurred.*

The letters flashed brightly.

[Plus Effects]

External Personnel : A New Attempt

[Promotion ↑↑]

Concept : Foreboding of Immersion

[Quality ↑]

[Prediction Results Complete!]

I read every word without looking away. At the last moment, the grade finally appeared.

"......"

I clenched my fist.

[Expected Album Grade : S+ ~ A+]

That's it.

"...Let's produce it."

[Crazy, TeSTAR is having a comeback!]

[fr?]

[How are they so fast?]

It had been less than a month since TeSTAR had moved companies. How was this possible?

The answer was revealed soon enough.

[It's a repackage]

[Whoa!]

A repackage.

It meant a new album release with only one or two additional songs from the previous album.

In fact, TeSTAR had originally planned a completely new mini-album or single. This was due to the insistence of a few members who argued, 'Let's not provoke T1 any more than necessary'.

But after T1's dirty tricks were revealed, the situation changed.

*We're already on their bad side, so what does it matter.*

Thanks to that, they decided to boldly suck the previous songs and concept dry and use them!

However, people were even more excited. TeSTAR had changed companies, yet they could bring their previous album and release it!

[Crazy, that company acquired T1 Stars so they have all the copyrights, right?ㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋ That's awesome, so that's how it's possibleㅋㅋㅋㅋ]

[Wow, TeSTAR's grand plan...]

[ORBIT, I pledge my loyalty]

[I'm about to cryㅠㅠㅠ]

The previous high-teen concept album, promoted for only two weeks without a proper concert, was being revived! Fans didn't care about the comeback interval. They just planned to pre-order with all their might.

[Make money, please]

[When does it openㅠㅠㅠ]

Park Moondae's first fansite master felt the same way. She clutched her smartphone as if she would tear it apart.

*I've been earning money for this very day...*

She had a grand plan. Even if attending every single fansign was impossible for a leader, she wanted to attend at least half of them, but first, the pre-order sales for this album had to be good to support Moondae's new start!

*Aaaah... They definitely won't be able to appear on Music Bomb.*

Casual fans might see escaping T1 as a refreshing victory, but she knew better. Losing connections with a broadcast station, especially the one they originated from, was a bad sign for promotions.

*This promotion is really, really important!*

She was a bit worried that the smart Moondae would rush a comeback like this. That was why the results mattered. Only the results! She nodded.

At that moment, an SNS pop-up appeared on her smartphone.

"...!"

*TeSTAR's official company account...!*

This had to be the album sales announcement. She clicked it frantically and recalled the information she already knew. This album had three versions, and...

[They're only producing 1,000 units of a 3-version set with some kind of special benefit, is the company crazy?]

It was an article about a 'limited set' of only 1,000 units. It had received praise for the kind notice that it would be 'scheduled for production as regular merchandise at a later date', but it was also criticized for being vague and not revealing the exact contents.

*...It also seemed like they were just being greedy.*

And now, the identity of that set was being announced. After instantly reading the SNS post, she was so shocked her eyes looked like they would pop out.

"...A board game?"

At midnight on the same day, TeSTAR released a teaser for their new song.

[Ummm...]

In a dark attic wrapped in blankets, light from a lamp and string lights illuminated the seven members. They wore comfortable, casual clothes. It was the high-teen concept TeSTAR from the previous album.

They sat in a circle on the attic's wooden floor with a board game in the middle.

[Let's start]

Start the free-for-all

The die was rolled.
