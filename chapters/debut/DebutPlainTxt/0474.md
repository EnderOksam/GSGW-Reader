# Chapter 474

Beep-beep-beep, beep-beep, beep-beep-beep-beep!

Chime!

Someone frantically punched the passcode at the front door. A figure with disheveled hair rushed inside.

It was Seon Ahyeon, finally back from the fishing boat. He looked completely out of breath, having rushed over after seeing the messages summarizing the situation: 'Cha Eugene collapsed, woke up, was discharged, condition being checked, holy crap Eugene's memories changed'.

"G-Guys, I'm ba..."

"......"

"......"

"H-Hello...?"

"Ahyeon, you're here."

"Welcome back, Ahyeon. You worked hard..."

However, the atmosphere in the dorm was a complete wreck. It was so grim you wouldn't be surprised if someone started chugging soju.

Though no alcohol was on the table, the mood was so ruined that no one would stop you from running out to buy some. It felt like a house in mourning.

"E-Eugene...?"

"...Um."

Moreover, Cha Eugene wasn't there.

STier Cha Eugene had unilaterally finished speaking and gone into the room he was told was his.

[I have nothing more to say.]

*Damn it.*

In a word, it was a bust.

"For now, Ahyeon, you should sit down and rest. You must have been shocked."

"Y-Yes..."

I moved aside to give Seon Ahyeon a seat and silently gulped down more cold water.

For the record, Cha Eugene's roommate was still the same as in the old dorm. It meant Bae Sejin, fuck.

"Thirty days... you said? If we do a good, hmm, a good job during that time... it'll be okay."

The guy nodded as if he understood the situation's gravity, but he didn't know the real problem.

*That guy thinks you're a drug dealer...*

Unaware of his roommate's tremendous misunderstanding, Bae Sejin, as the eldest, simply resolved to take good care of one of the youngest members until his memory returned.

"Right. Sejin is right."

He was not right. He would be lucky not to get grabbed by the collar. I pressed my temples.

*I don't know why an 'Update' suddenly gave Cha Eugene his STier memories, or if STier Cha Eugene himself has been inserted in there, but...*

I needed to make a new plan.

*First, I need to clear up the misunderstanding about Bae Sejin...*

But would that guy even believe me? No, it was a problem even if he did. Didn't that mean his group took a fatal blow because of a false accusation, not the truth?

*And on top of that, he has to watch him get lucky here, not becoming a drug dealer and doing well.*

The comparison might just piss him off more and make him uncooperative.

*Hoo...*

I heard Bae Sejin muttering beside me.

"Maybe it's only natural."

"......"

"From that Cha Eugene's perspective... he suddenly woke up in an unfamiliar environment."

"Yes..."

It was then. Keun Sejin, who had been quiet while observing the situation, stepped forward with a shrug.

"Well... I don't think we need to worry that much, do we?"

"......"

"S-Sejin?"

"So what if he had a slightly different experience? In the end, Eugene is Eugene, right? I mean, he grew up the same way until he was about nineteen."

"So?"

"Then, even if his personality and stuff changed over a few years," Keun Sejin glanced sideways. "Don't you think his taste in food would probably be similar?"

"...!"

His gaze landed on the brownies and chocolate chips that Bae Sejin and Kim Raebin had gathered.

~~~

KNOCK, KNOCK, KNOCK.

"I'm coming in for a moment."

Ryu Chungwoo spoke in a soft but firm tone. A few seconds later, he opened the door to the room Cha Eugene had entered.

CREEEAK!

Inside the room, Cha Eugene lay haphazardly on the bed among the still-unpacked luggage.

The guy had one arm covering his face and spoke without moving.

"...Now that's going too far."

"Hm?"

"I said I wouldn't answer. Twice."

Cha Eugene lowered his arm, revealing his eyes. This time, without looking around, he pointed directly at me.

"That person knows, so hear it from him."

Instantly, all eyes turned to me.

Hmm.

I crossed my arms. Keun Sejin walked into the room with a charming smile.

"Hang on, Eugene. We didn't come here to ask about that again. We just came to talk a little."

"Talk?"

"Of course! We're living in the same house now, so it'll be more comfortable for you if we're at least familiar with each other. You need to know the situation, too!"

Keun Sejin grinned and sat on the floor. Cha Eugene shifted on the bed and nodded slightly.

"That kind of thing is fine."

"Right? I thought you might be curious about things like how long you have to be here. Just my thought!"

Without waiting for a reply, Keun Sejin began to summarize the answers in a relaxed tone.

Keun Sejin's explanation was a well-edited summary of what he had heard from me and the strange situations we had experienced so far. It was easy to understand.

"...So, we think you'll probably only be here for about thirty days."

"......"

Cha Eugene didn't argue.

After listening to the end, however, he opened his mouth with a sullen expression.

"That's really..."

"You were about to say 'nerdy,' right? That's so Eugene. So very Eugene."

"......"

Cha Eugene stared at Keun Sejin for a moment, then burst out laughing.

"Hey."

"Hm?"

"The Cha Eugene you know isn't me. I don't think it's polite to talk to me that way."

"...!"

Kim Raebin, standing nearby, flinched even more.

"Ah, is that so?"

But Keun Sejin replied without getting flustered.

"Then shall we agree to respect boundaries?"

"Sure. [That's a good way to put it. Don't cross the line.]"

A smile still on his face, Cha Eugene started to lie back down on the bed.

However, Keun Sejin wasn't finished.

"But you know that lines are kept through mutual respect, right? I'm asking you. We at least have the connection of being participants in ❬Idol Inc.❭ together."

"......"

Cha Eugene paused.

At that moment, Ryu Chungwoo extended his hand.

"Right. It means we should show basic courtesy and respect to each other."

"......"

"Eugene."

Cha Eugene sat up in bed.

He seemed to scan the faces of those who had entered the room.

*A connection, huh.*

From this guy's perspective, Bae Sejin was the one who blew up his group with drugs, and Keun Sejin was the one who voluntarily dropped out of the program due to school violence.

*I wasn't even a participant.*

However, he made no such comments. Instead, the guy simply raised his hand.

"......"

He shook Ryu Chungwoo's hand.

"Alright."

"Thank you."

Ryu Chungwoo smiled brightly.

The strategy was to push forward the most charming guy and the one to whom Cha Eugene had the mildest reaction, one after another, to at least bring him to the table.

*Phase one, complete.*

Only then did the atmosphere in the room relax a little.

At that moment, Bae Sejin signaled with his eyes. Kim Raebin quickly held out a bag.

*Phase two begins.*

"...! Cha Eugene, you've been on an empty stomach for two meals now. Eat this, at least!"

"......"

It was the brownies and cookies.

Cha Eugene peered into the bag thrust before his eyes and asked, bewildered.

"Brownie? Don't tell me Kim Raebin actually bought them at the hospital?"

"...? Yes!"

"Oh."

At that moment, Cha Eugene let out a light, dry laugh, as if looking at a close friend. He quickly stopped, realizing that wasn't the case.

*I guess they were close during STier, too.*

Of course, I couldn't miss this timing. I immediately reached out my hand and demanded, "Just one."

"...Yes?"

The response was an ambiguous question and affirmation.

Without looking inside, I stuck my hand into the bag, grabbed the first brownie I touched, and tore open the wrapper. Then I shoved it into my mouth.

*...Sweet.*

It was a performance, a sort of 'certification that these are normal, drug-free brownies'. It was ridiculous that I was doing this, but...

*Bae Sejin went with Kim Raebin when he went to buy them.*

I had worried the guy might refuse to eat them out of suspicion, since he had talked so much about drugs. After swallowing the brownie, I nodded.

"It's good."

"...Moondae said so! That means there's a higher probability that it'll suit your taste too!"

"...OK."

In the end, Cha Eugene didn't refuse. He reached out, took the entire bag, and checked the packaging with a sulky expression.

Then he started unwrapping them one by one and popping them into his mouth. The speed was almost exhilarating.

TeSTAR watched the scene unfold.

"......"

"......"

*For some reason, it feels like we're feeding raw meat to a wild animal.*

If feeding him sweets was part of this guy's biological mechanism for improving his mood, there would surely be a reaction. I decided to wait a little.

And a few minutes later.

"Want to have pizza for dinner? Bulgogi pizza?"

"I like pizza."

"......"

Surprisingly, this conversation happened. The atmosphere had definitely softened.

*I don't know if it's thanks to the snacks.*

In any case, Cha Eugene sat on his bed and devoured all the brownies and cookies. Once he confirmed that people were no longer asking about his 'group', he slowly reverted to a demeanor similar to the Cha Eugene we knew.

*Although the level of conversation has become more primitive.*

Still, this was a huge improvement.

"Coke."

"Oh, thanks."

"Ah, if you have any questions about the current situation or about us, feel free to ask anything."

"Alright."

In the end, STier Cha Eugene came around to making light small talk while eating pizza with us at the same table.

And in the midst of it, a chance to talk about our current situation naturally came up.

"Ah, we're almost done with our promotion period right now. Just a few schedules left?"

Of course, this guy's answer was obvious.

"But I'm not on this team. I'm not promoting."

"Uhm."

"Right."

We couldn't force a guy who said he wouldn't do it.

*The amount of practice would be considerable, too.*

Besides, it was fortunate that TeSTAR's group activities were almost over.

*Cha Eugene finished his solo activities early, too.*

To sum it up in one phrase, 'If that's the case.'

"Our tour starts next month, right?"

"Yes."

Thinking of it as a short break wouldn't be a net loss. It might become an issue if Cha Eugene had no solo activities, but we could cover that with health issues for about a month.

So... there was just one problem.

"...What do we do about our reality show...?"

"......"

It was TeSTAR's group reality show, which we had been ambitiously preparing. It was about to be scrapped.

*Fuck.*

How could we film a group reality show when one member refused to participate?

"The filming schedule... we can't postpone it, can we?"

"No."

This had been difficult enough to arrange. Moreover, the reality show's format made it awkward for even one person to miss it for health reasons.

Even if someone did, we would have to insert their footage separately later. Otherwise, the program could spark rumors of discord or a transfer.

So, that's what I mean. I opened my mouth.

"...Let's film it anyway."

"Film it?"

"And when Cha Eugene comes back in a month, we'll just add in Eugene's parts separately. How about that?"

"......"

This was the best we could do.

However, Kim Raebin muttered gloomily beside me.

"The Cha Eugene who returns... will probably be mad that we filmed something fun without him..."

"......"

"...Oh, that's convincing, Raebin."

"Thank you..."

I covered my face with one hand.

*Damn it.*

Was it my fault for applying the System to the company in the first place, causing this mess?

*But if I hadn't applied it, the album might have been a mediocre flop...*

That was another maddening part. Whining and regretting it now was useless. What I needed was a solution.

*Think...*

I had to use my head. I wracked my brain in silence, then opened my mouth a moment later.

"Statistically speaking."

"...?"

"When Cha Eugene returns... there's a chance he'll have the current memories."

"Uh, that's... probably true?"

It was just like when these guys regained their memories in the System's virtual world. The memories they made there didn't disappear.

*If that's the case.*

"Having the current Cha Eugene participate in the reality show is the best option."

"...!"

"But... that Cha Eugene said he wouldn't participate in any activities."

"I'm going to at least bring it up."

Since this wasn't a stage performance, I thought we could handle it as a deal if 'STier Cha Eugene' wanted something in return. I also had something else I wanted to say to him separately.

~~~

"You have something else to say?"

I went out to the veranda. Cha Eugene stood by the half-open glass door, looking outside by himself. He turned to me with an unwelcoming expression.

I spoke calmly.

"I have something to say about your roommate."

"......"

And Cha Eugene... sighed?

[Hey, I'm not an idiot. Of course that person has nothing to do with drugs.]

"...!"

[I think you know 'STier' quite well, and it's obvious from the way you treat that "roommate" as a member. However.]

The guy said dryly, "I'm not interested."

"......"

[Of course, he's not the person I knew. I have no intention of treating him poorly.]

He tapped my shoulder.

[But don't cross the line. You get it? You need to respect the line too.]

Leaving those words behind, the guy left the veranda.

"......"

I stood by the veranda window where he had been. A chilly summer night breeze blew past.

I had a gut feeling.

*It wouldn't have worked.*

Deal or no deal, any talk about TeSTAR activities would not have gotten through to that guy right now.

"Hoo."

It might have been a relief that I didn't bring it up.

But no one could have predicted that the incident of crossing that damn 'line' would happen so quickly.
