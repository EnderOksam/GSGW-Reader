# Chapter 561

DRIP.

A drop of water hit the back of his hand.

*...?*

Cheongryeo did not move a muscle. He only lowered his gaze to his hand, where a transparent droplet had burst into tiny pieces.

*Where did it come from.*

He tried to lift his head to find its source. He tried to raise his hand for a closer look. He could not do either.

"......"

His body would not move.

*Why?*

He did not panic. He simply tried to think, to calmly deduce the cause.

Just then, another drop of water fell onto the back of his hand.

DRIP.

As Cheongryeo watched it happen, he finally realized.

It was his own cold sweat.

[The Incarnation of an Idol is watching you.]

[The Incarnation of an Idol is watching you.]

[The Incarnation of an Idol is watching you.]

[The Incarnation of an Idol is watching you.]

A feast of gazes rained down from the empty air. They pressed on him from all sides, freezing his body and exerting a terrible pressure. His body refused his attempt to lift his head and meet them.

*Ah.*

The mind is an appendage of the body. His mind had cultivated a profound depth over a long time, but his body, merely in its early twenties, felt an instinctual threat.

From somewhere far, far above, someone looked down on him. A higher being examined him, searching every nook and cranny.

Examining him.

——————————

A single, massive sentence rose above all the windows, like the moon surfacing on water.

[The Incarnation of an Idol is satisfied.]

Then, as if it were a lie, everything vanished.

WHOOSH!

Air rushed into the space where the suffocating tension had abruptly dissipated. The sound of breathing finally reached his ears, and voices, too.

"Don't tell me you missed the timing for some medicine you were supposed to take..."

"No."

Cheongryeo gently withdrew his hand from the table. The moisture dripped off, tracing an arc.

"......"

He turned his head to check the time.

*67 minutes.*

For over an hour, that bizarre gaze had held him captive. The pressure was so chillingly strong that he had not even noticed time passing. He slowly wiped the part of his hand stained with his own byproduct.

He thought about what it was and why it had appeared. He could not understand the principle, but one clear connection came to mind.

"......"

It had appeared in the empty space where Help usually appeared when he called for it. However, it did not feel friendly or personal like Help. It was a cold gaze, too impassive to be called hostile.

Overwhelming.

Cheongryeo slowly closed and opened his eyes.

What was its purpose?

*Scrutiny.*

It was looking into him, as if to confirm something, and...

*A declaration of intent that covered the empty space.*

It was a warning.

"......"

Cheongryeo made a cold judgment.

*Is it telling me not to call Help?*

It was a reasonable deduction, since it had appeared the moment he called for Help. He turned his head to look at his Help, who was still unconscious and leaning against the table.

What could be the connection? Should I be the one to find out?

Cheongryeo slowly tapped the table. His fingertips still felt slightly numb, perhaps from the pressure of the last 67 minutes.

A shock like this...

"It's been a while."

"Pardon?"

Cheongryeo did not answer. He had the impression that this restart had an unusual number of new variables. It was annoying that new elements could appear after so many repetitions, but Cheongryeo was used to even that. He was also used to the fact that any problem could be solved by repeatedly learning from it.

"Hmm."

So, just as he lifted his head to call Help once more, Cheongryeo's gaze froze in mid-air.

"......"

It was gone. The pop-up that asked if he wanted to delete Help was gone.

[!Unauthorized Error Occurred]

[Do you want to remove the Status Window?]

Even that red error removal window, which always appeared whenever he called Help, had vanished from his sight as if it had melted away.

It was as if Help no longer existed.

~~~

Inside the System.

[Phew!]

Park Moondae let out a deep breath and approached the terminal. The inside of the System was still empty and silent. At its center, only that one terminal drifted alone like a deserted island. The few terminals he had created were on the System's surface, so they were not visible. He remembered how it sometimes gave him the creeps and how he had tried his best not to think about it.

*You've been through a lot.*

Now, he could calmly acknowledge that fact. He had enough strength of heart to be okay with it. Park Moondae thought it was probably thanks to the blessing Seon Ahyeon had given him. Although he could not see their pop-ups while connected here, the effect still seemed to remain.

*It might be a placebo, but...*

Still, it was fine. He grinned and reached out toward the terminal. The analysis was almost complete. If Keundal had been working here instead of him, it might not have been this much trouble. However, as a layman, he had done his best. He attached himself to the terminal again and began trying to figure out what was inside.

Diligently, calmly.

"......"

The only surprising thing was that the expected interruption had not come, even after a considerable amount of time.

*I thought he might call for me soon.*

When he worked on the terminal like this, he would get called in the middle of it once every two or three times. Cheongryeo would call for Help. So, since he had connected so openly this time, he thought Cheongryeo might interrupt by calling for him repeatedly. Strangely, it was very quiet.

*Of course, this time I was planning to ignore him no matter what he called for.*

It was actually a good thing. Park Moondae stopped his stray thoughts and decided to focus on the terminal again.

He made contact with the terminal. The Status Window-like sentence that Park Moondae first discovered on this terminal still floated there.

[Reboot Start]

[Learning in Progress.]

It was the exact phrase he had seen before being dragged into this place. Until now, Park Moondae had been trying to find the source of 'why' this sentence was displayed.

*I thought knowing the reason would be a clue.*

It was a journey to find a kind of database. Now, the end was in sight.

*Let's do this.*

He conceptually closed his eyes. He delved into the terminal, searching for the origin as if climbing up a nerve.

Information... information...

From the point he had explored last time, if he delicately dug his way up again...!

TAP!

He got it.

*Got... it!*

Park Moondae gritted his teeth and finally pulled the database he had grasped out of the terminal. His non-existent grip felt like it was about to tear from the resistance, but he ignored it. Then, he formed a new terminal to display that database. It was not that difficult anymore.

*Good.*

He exhaled, feeling a sense of accomplishment that was close to ecstasy.

The database Status Window that finally revealed itself was...

[Learning History]

[Will to Survive]

[Negotiation]

[Confirmation of Holder's Wish]

[Quick Adaptation]

"......"

It looked just like a list of characteristics. It was a format Park Moondae was familiar with.

*It's similar to the characteristic-drawing roulette.*

It looked like the roulette window he had seen back when he was actively using the System. It listed the characteristics he could randomly draw and receive.

He reflexively clicked on one.

[Will to Survive]

: The biological instinct to do whatever it takes to survive as a being.

/Acquired through Reboot Start.

This time was similar, too. A description followed, just like when he clicked on a characteristic to examine it in detail. The explanation at the very bottom was about 'how it was acquired', and the title of the entire list was 'Learning History'.

*Does this mean the System has been learning this all this time?*

It meant that the System had been learning these things while undergoing 'reboots'. Park Moondae scanned the list with a sour feeling.

Survival, negotiation, wishes, adaptation...

*Aren't these all attributes this bastard already had?*

Aren't they just the cockroach-like traits of the System? The things he had suffered through so tiresomely were written there exactly as they were. Usually, a reboot is done to compensate for shortcomings and create a new product, but this felt like listening to the same song on repeat.

*Hmm.*

It was a slightly disappointing sight. It did not seem much like a clue. Of course, he had no intention of giving up.

*Should I analyze the terminal more from here?*

Just as he groaned at the feeling that he was running out of time, a final line suddenly appeared in the 'Learning History' window.

PLING.

[Craving for the Origin]

Park Moondae reflexively clicked it.

[Craving for the Origin]

: The desire to reach the essence through persistent analysis and development.

*What...*

It would not be strange to think that this, too, was an attribute the System originally possessed.

*It was spouting all sorts of nonsense about being an idol.*

Park Moondae lowered his gaze with a look of disillusionment. Below it, just like before, was the explanation of how it was learned.

/Acquired by analyzing the terminal.

*...?*

That... was what Park Moondae was doing right now. He had even created the term 'terminal' himself.

"......"

A chill ran down his spine. His head felt like it was spinning. A strange, ominous feeling tickled him, as if he was on the verge of understanding something...

*No way...!*

Park Moondae hurriedly clicked on the other learning histories. He began to read only the explanations at the bottom.

/Acquired through conversation with the holder.

/Acquired under threat of deletion.

/Acquired while adapting to a new environment.

The subject was hidden in these words.

And that subject was...

*Me.*

It was Park Moondae. The situations he had experienced since crash-landing in this strange place were organized and listed in System-like terms. Trying his best to survive, trying to negotiate with Cheongryeo, being deleted, trying to adapt to a new body...

In a shock as if he had been hit over the head, Park Moondae stared at the terminal.

[Learning History]

The System was learning through Park Moondae. It was acquiring his actions, now a Status Window, as its own.

So the reboot was...

*No.*

Park Moondae realized the logic was strange.

*The System is already that kind of bastard, so what is there to learn?*

So the learning, the learning was...

"......"

He just had to change the subject.

*The one who learned was... me, wasn't it?*

Park Moondae realized. It was not that the System learned Park Moondae's way of acting. It was that Park Moondae had learned the System's way of acting.

And at that moment, when he finally completed that sentence, which had no gaps in its plausibility, the terminal changed.

[Learning Complete]

The moment Park Moondae confirmed it with his trembling vision.

[System Reboot]

His vision turned black.

~~~

It's dark.

What was I doing?

*Probably...*

It didn't seem like a big deal. There probably wouldn't be any major problems. I had a feeling.

*Hmm.*

Somehow, I wasn't scared anymore. I sank comfortably into the space as if stretching. The faint feeling that I had been very uncomfortable just before made this moment even more peaceful.

*What is this?*

I didn't particularly want to use my brain. Still, if I had to make a rough guess...

*I think someone said they were going to do something.*

Right. I was probably waiting for something. I nodded and floated again in the empty space.

"......"

For some reason, my thoughts seemed to be slowing down. That was fine, too. Without resistance, I would wait for the reboot in this crazy space devoid of light and sound.

*Reboot?*

Anyway, there was something like that. It's a good thing.

I felt it was time to stop thinking...

[]

Just then, a small line appeared in the empty space.

[-]

It did not shine like a hologram. It was just a translucent line floating in the air. However, the line gradually grew longer, and it formed a small shape by meeting at the corners.

The frame of a rectangle.

*...A pop-up.*

I watched it as if mesmerized. Above it, white text appeared.

[Hubaenim.]

Inside the System, where the pop-ups from the friends who gave him blessings could not enter. No, in the place I thought was like that, a pop-up was quietly appearing.

[Hubaenim.]

It was a familiar title. The one he knew, the one who did not restart, the one who owned a dog.

*...Cheongryeo.*

It was the title Shin Jaehyun used to call him.
