# Chapter 517

"Are we... starting now...?"

"Yes."

Seon Ahyeon nodded vigorously at my answer. He got into position, his face a mix of tension and anticipation.

And Seon Ahyeon wasn't the only one with that expression.

The guys who had finished filming gathered in the living room. They heard Ryu Chungwoo's memories were coming back.

While everyone was tense, Lee Sejin made a lighthearted comment, trying to lighten the mood.

"Hyung, you're about to get several close younger brothers in just a few seconds. How's it feel? Sounds like fun~"

"It is, but that's not the goal."

"...!"

STier Ryu Chungwoo's reply sounded almost cold. He didn't seem displeased, though. He just answered naturally, true to his personality. It was more natural than before, when he had forced himself to appear gentle.

The guy chuckled softly, and Lee Sejin just laughed it off.

"It's nice to see you looking comfortable!"

"Thanks."

Ryu Chungwoo turned his head toward me.

"Then I'll go find them now."

I nodded, then checked the Status Window.

[Would you like to reactivate 'For Business Use '?]

The moment I reactivated this, Ryu Chungwoo's memories would return, just like they had for STier Cha Eugene.

For reference, it seemed Kim Raebin would automatically regain his memories if I ended the 'Preview' I currently used. I had worked the System hard to try and make 'Preview' and the reactivation coexist.

I did it because I worried he might be shocked by the sudden need to regain his memories and reject it, but...

"Um, Kim Raebin. Your symptoms appeared later than Chungwoo's, and it's okay to think about it more slowly. Don't feel pressured."

"...?? I never said I didn't want my memories back..."

"Ah."

"I'm really expecting that getting my memories back will let me find a new way to compose!"

...So he said.

*Did I mess with the System for nothing?*

It felt a bit unfair that I poured so much energy into it that my head was about to explode.

Anyway, I decided to stick to the plan for now.

I took my hand from my brow and said calmly, "I'm going to press it now, so you can lie down."

I suggested it because, based on past cases, he would most likely fall asleep.

STier Ryu Chungwoo lay down on the living room sofa without protest.

"Is this okay?"

"Yes."

After confirming he was in a stable position and mentally prepared, I activated the Status Window.

*Reactivate.*

FSHHH.

A light burst from the pop-up.

But regardless of the dazzling hologram in the air, Ryu Chungwoo lay on the sofa, breathing quietly without a single movement. He looked like he was peacefully falling asleep.

"......"

"Hyung?"

No answer came back.

STier Ryu Chungwoo just lay on the sofa with a peaceful expression. He was so still that he almost inadvertently blocked the light from my pop-up, which he couldn't even see.

"......"

"Let's wait."

"Yeah."

We moved carefully, not wanting to disturb the guy who seemed deeply unconscious.

On that holiday afternoon, the living room remained quiet for several hours.

And that evening.

TSSSS.

I was in the kitchen frying an egg.

Just then, a shout came from behind me.

"Oh my, Hyung!"

It was Cha Eugene's voice. He was on monitoring duty for Ryu Chungwoo from 5 to 6 PM.

Which meant...

*He's awake.*

I turned off the stove under the frying pan and immediately turned around.

In the living room, a disheveled Ryu Chungwoo sat up on the sofa. He bowed his head, covering his forehead with one hand, so I couldn't see his expression.

However, Cha Eugene was relentless.

"Moondae-hyung is making something delicious! Let's go, quickly!"

*That comes before asking if he's okay?*

*Isn't it just that you wanted to eat?*

However, when Cha Eugene pulled his arm, Ryu Chungwoo smiled cheerfully, his face slightly swollen.

"...Is that so? Thanks..."

"......"

It was a gentle and relaxed response, even in his confusion. It was the kind of reaction someone familiar with this situation would have.

Bae Sejin, who had rushed over, asked with trembling pupils, "...Ryu Chungwoo?"

"Yeah."

Ryu Chungwoo straightened his wrinkled clothes and brushed back his hair, sitting up completely. A somewhat sheepish smile played on his lips.

Just like the person we used to know.

"You've all been through a lot."

That was enough.

"...Hyung!"

"Aigoo, our Leader-hyung is back~!"

In an instant, the living room became so noisy it felt like the roof would blow off. A few of the guys hugged Ryu Chungwoo as if it were a family reunion. He looked slightly flustered but patted each of their backs.

"Sejin in particular had a tough time. You said everything that needed to be said during our schedules."

He concluded with this.

"There were many other members confused because of their memories, but instead of taking care of them, I only received help, so I'm sorry."

"Hey, days like this have to happen for all the work you usually do to be repaid~"

"No, still, thank you."

He replied quite firmly. Then he turned his head, looking for someone.

*Well, since there's only one person not there, I know who he's looking for.*

Me.

What was I doing?

"And Moondae..."

"......"

I was holding a crab.

"......"

Not a cooked crab, but a raw one marinated in soy sauce.

CRACK.

I pulled off the carapace. A satisfying sound revealed insides packed with yellow roe.

It was soy sauce crab, made with a plump crab.

"Come and eat."

"......"

I didn't choose it. His parents selected the menu. Honestly, I didn't have time to do anything more, so this worked out well. They said it was from a famous restaurant, so it should be decent.

"Hyung, Moondae just went and bought that, he even took a call taxi!"

*It's cheaper than the cost of cooking.*

"Moondae, if you had told us, we would have at least played rock-paper-scissors..."

*It's most reliable if I do it.*

"Moondae, you should have rested too."

*Good that you know.*

I didn't do much anyway. I just fried an egg and cracked open a crab.

"It's all set. Please eat."

He probably hadn't eaten anything today.

I gave a slight nod, waiting for him to sit down.

"Alright."

Ryu Chungwoo, smiling a little awkwardly, finally came to the table. The other guys found their own seats as well.

They started talking excitedly to Ryu Chungwoo.

"Wow, Hyung, even without your memories, things wrapped up so smoothly without any incidents."

"Th-That's right!"

"I know. Cha Eugene ran away."

"I went to the sea. There was no damage!"

"Hey, we couldn't even sleep that day..."

The table quickly became boisterous. Ryu Chungwoo looked embarrassed for a moment but soon laughed heartily.

"It was Eugene's first time. It can happen. I'm the one who's sorry for not being able to show a more mature side."

"Hyung..."

He ate while diligently answering each of the other members' questions about his condition.

And he didn't forget to say this either.

"I'll be the one to contact the company starting today."

"...Um, yes."

I nodded.

Then I turned my eyes to the dining table. It was set with marinated crab, butter, and eggs.

"......"

Ryu Chungwoo barely touched any of it, eating only plain rice.

*Hmm.*

I watched him carefully.

***

Ryu Chungwoo finally got some time to gather his thoughts when he went to take a shower.

"Hoo."

He hadn't had a moment for this, having spent all his time since waking up reassuring the other members.

It was only natural.

Thinking about what he, with only his past memories, had done to his younger brothers was beyond embarrassing. It was enough to make him renew his resolve. He had fallen not just into a slump, but into self-pity.

*I was like a child.*

However, it seemed he had done what he needed to do for now.

[So this is how it is...]

Even Kim Raebin, who hadn't regained his memories yet, seemed a bit relieved.

*...Good.*

TeSTAR Ryu Chungwoo, just as he always did, recovered his strong mentality. His mind was free of major worries or intense emotions.

*I belong to a good team.*

*I am handling my responsibilities well.*

He reflected on everything that had happened since his debut as TeSTAR, calmly composing his feelings.

Just as he finished his shower, clearing his head to prevent his thoughts from drifting to a dark place, he stepped out.

"......"

"Ah, you're out."

His roommate, Cha Eugene, was not there.

Instead, sitting at the table placed between the two beds... was Park Moondae.

No, it would be more accurate to say that his cousin, Ryu Gunwoo, was sitting there.

"Sit."

The one who had casually thrown out the informal words was arranging things on the table. There were a few strongly scented ingredients, and... bottles of alcohol.

Ryu Gunwoo had set up a drinking spread on the table.

Ryu Chungwoo was momentarily speechless, then barely managed to open his mouth again.

"Hyung, alcohol..."

"We all agreed that it's okay to drink occasionally now."

However, what was on the table wasn't a can of beer... but a pile of wine bottles.

"......"

*Is this really okay?*

Ryu Chungwoo looked at his cousin with a mixture of reluctance and worry, but he cautiously sat down across the table anyway.

*Did I do something wrong?*

He pondered for a moment but soon intuitively found something close to the right answer.

*Ah.*

*I wasn't paying much attention during the meal.*

That was something that could easily be upsetting. He had probably gone out of his way to call and find out my favorite restaurant, but I hadn't even eaten properly because I was so preoccupied with the mess I'd made.

*I feel bad.*

He immediately tried to speak about it.

However, the other person spoke first.

"I have something to say."

"Yeah."

Ryu Chungwoo straightened his posture, like drinking with a superior after making a mistake.

However, Ryu Gunwoo didn't talk about his mistake.

"A younger brother I know once told me this."

Ryu Gunwoo continued slowly. They were words he had heard somewhere before.

"He said that when faced with fear and pain... it's okay for a person to get a little angry and blame others."

"......"

Ryu Chungwoo's eyes widened.

Ryu Gunwoo smiled and reached out, picking up a glass from the table.

"If it was okay for me, then it's even more okay for you."

Because he was the one who had said it.

[How can a person live rationally all the time?]

Looking at the table laden with alcohol, Ryu Chungwoo finally realized the source of the deja vu.

Summer Package.

The other person had perfectly recreated the scene from that day, the day he had given Park Moondae a chance to open up.

*Ah.*

Ryu Chungwoo slowly closed and opened his eyes. Ryu Gunwoo held out a glass to him.

"You worked hard."

Alcohol was poured into it. Ryu Chungwoo stared at it blankly.

Then he reached out, took the glass, and brought it to his lips.

"......"

In truth, he was too strong against alcohol to get drunk from this.

However, the desire to drink made him realize.

*I wanted to run away.*

He had been deliberately trying his best not to recall the memories from his time as an STier. The very act of remembering was met with considerable resistance.

What STier Ryu Chungwoo could dismiss as 'childish' was limited to what he had done here. When he was in the thick of it, he was too buried to realize, but looking back after regaining his solid mentality, he could see.

His former self...

"Actually, I..."

"......"

Ryu Gunwoo asked inwardly.

*You...?*

However, Ryu Chungwoo didn't answer. He just silently drank the remaining alcohol in his glass.

"......"

Ryu Gunwoo didn't press his younger brother either.

The two of them slowly finished one bottle of wine and opened another.

"Want to drink more?"

"Yeah."

And one more bottle.

That was all.

His cousin was someone who demanded no role from Ryu Chungwoo.

Sometimes, that was enough.

***

TeSTAR rested well until the next day.

Their only movement was going back and forth between rooms to chat. Even Lee Sejin didn't go to work out, and the same went for Ryu Chungwoo.

Bae Sejin saw the amount of alcohol the two had drunk and made a face that said he had a hell of a lot to say, but he held it in.

Time passed by leisurely like that.

After dinner, they all gathered in the living room to watch their last competition performance, which had finally been released.

"Wow, as expected of TeSTAR, their stage presence is amazing."

"That's right. I'm especially good."

Tension-free conversation flowed back and forth.

"I want to see our unit stage soon, too."

"Y-Yeah. Me too...!"

"What about Raebin? Raebin arranged it~"

"...! Of course, I... would like to see it again too!"

"Ooooh."

They spent a gentle, comfortable break, talking about when Raebin might get his memories back and if there was anything he wanted to do before then.

Still, the TeSTAR on screen was magnificent.

Even while watching the cringey subtitles, TeSTAR giggled excitedly.

[The Stage of KPOP Kings Who Enthrall the Audience]

"We're kings?"

"Bwahahaha!"

A small round of applause was heard. Lee Sejin took a group selfie, saying he would release it to the fans, and Park Moondae chose the photo.

Ryu Chungwoo watched the peaceful scene with a relaxed mind.

And just as the competition video from their appearance on ❬Genuine Match❭ ended.

DOONG.

[I realized.]

With an eerie minor key resonance, a commercial began to play on the TV screen.

"Huh? That's..."

But it was a voice I'd heard somewhere before.

[In this building, a strange shadow]

After the narration, eerie images of a desolate hallway and an emergency staircase flashed by. It was a thriller steeped in a fantasy feel.

[Is chasing me.]

THUMP, THUMP, THUMP.

In time with the sound, close-up shots of actors standing under red, blue, and dark lighting passed by.

And among them was Bae Sejin, making eye contact with the camera!

"Oooooh! Sejin-hyung!"

"W-Woaaah...!"

[Doll Hunter]

[Thursday, October 31st, on Netplus]

That's right.

This was the drama Bae Sejin had filmed!

"Hyung, I think it's going to look... cool...!"

"I heard! The main character's friend!"

"Right, that was the role, wasn't it? Sejin, congratulations!"

The members excitedly patted Bae Sejin's shoulders and back. Even Park Moondae spoke to him with a smile.

"Looks like it's being released soon."

"...Huh? Y-Yeah."

Bae Sejin nodded.

However, in his heart, he couldn't bring himself to speak the truth and was breaking out in a cold sweat.

*I still... haven't told them...!*

Bae Sejin, D-3 until the grand reveal of his psychopath role.
