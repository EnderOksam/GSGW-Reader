# Chapter 525

STier Kim Raebin's life was not bad.

Of course, his group was plagued by misfortune, and he could not create or produce his songs the way he wanted. This weighed heavily on his mind and sometimes caused insomnia.

However, Kim Raebin had a family to confide in. On nights when his schedule was not packed, he often made calls from the veranda.

"You don't have to worry so much, Raebin. That's just how professional life is. Newcomers can't just do whatever they want."

His older sister often comforted him with examples from her own similar situation and always added words of encouragement.

"As time passes, you'll get another chance to do the work you want."

Kim Raebin believed those words.

He ended the call after expressing his gratitude and carefully returned to his room from the veranda.

Sometimes, he would get called out.

"Don't stay out too long."

"I'm sorry."

Ryu Chungwoo would just nod and walk past. A tense Kim Raebin would then sigh in relief.

*Still, it's not bad.*

Kim Raebin was the type to find the good in any situation.

"Kim Raebin, go to sleep!"

"You go to sleep too."

The group included a friend his age who had been a trainee with him since their teens. This was a welcome thing for Kim Raebin, who found it difficult to get close to his peers.

Besides, had he not gained the experience of debuting, performing on various stages, and releasing songs?

*Overall, my situation is something to be thankful for.*

Thinking so, he did not sink into depression or despair like some other members. He continued his activities and poured all his strength into his work.

A few years passed as if they would never end, then suddenly met a clean end, as if it were all a lie.

The group disbanded.

[Goodbye, Cha Eugene.]

Lethargy and burnout inevitably found their way to Kim Raebin. It was only natural after he poured his all into a difficult five years.

However, Kim Raebin did not let himself get bogged down.

*Let's just rest a little and proceed as planned!*

He had already decided on a new career path.

A singer-songwriter!

Although STier had ended on a sour note, they had enough momentum to do small tours and sell hundreds of thousands of albums. With the money he had saved, even after sending some home, he could invest in equipment and set up a decent studio.

*And if I get the chance, I want to try promoting with a song I made exactly the way I want.*

There were still countless things he had not tried. He could strive for more!

With that thought, Kim Raebin returned to Gangwon-do and melted into a peaceful daily life.

Until then, he had been slowly recovering.

[Ryu Chungwoo: Are you doing well?]

About two weeks later, he received a text from the eldest member, who had been the group's Leader.

*Ah.*

Kim Raebin reflexively straightened his back, his body tensing sharply. He then realized he no longer needed to and forced himself to relax.

*Yes. I am doing well. Thank you for asking...*

As he typed a message like that, he suddenly realized he could now use his smartphone freely.

To be precise, the internet.

[Rule number three, do not access SNS without permission.]

During their group activities, Ryu Chungwoo had monitored the team members with a set of strict rules, like a boarding school. Kim Raebin had naturally lived a life distant from the internet for several years.

That had continued until now. He never had much interest in it and only used it occasionally to anonymously upload songs, so he did not feel much inconvenience.

However, that was a group rule.

*Doesn't it not apply anymore?*

STier had already disbanded.

Logically, his reasoning had no flaws. The reluctance he felt was simply due to his own fear and a learned prohibition.

He almost felt a little gloomy but soon regained his sense of purpose.

*Alright!*

Kim Raebin first sent a polite reply to Ryu Chungwoo.

Then, after a little hesitation, he started searching his name on SNS to get feedback.

[Handsome again today (Photo) #KimRaebin]

[King Raebin.. Black Rabbit Emperor (Video)]

[I miss Raebinㅠㅠ]

Posts from their last promotional period poured in, along with affectionate posts from people wondering about his unknown whereabouts.

His heart ached a little, but he scrolled through the posts with wonder and gratitude.

*There are still a lot of fans on SNS.*

Years of distancing himself had erased his ability to search well enough to know which accounts were dead or inactive.

He browsed SNS a few times, focusing on popular posts using only his real name as a search term. He soon thought of another method and switched tabs.

*Let's look at various places.*

The moment he searched his name on a search engine, he saw a peculiar post on the first page.

*...?*

[...Kim Raebin, Cha Eugene, Min Jeonghun, STier, T1, VTIC, Mirynay, T-Holic...]

It was a post that seemed to have randomly slapped together all sorts of group and personal names.

*Why is it structured like this?*

Kim Raebin clicked on the post without thinking. The page changed, redirecting him to a new search engine.

"...!"

It was a gossip board.

The post, uploaded a few weeks ago, had ranked high. It unfolded before Kim Raebin's eyes on the very board that had been a hotbed of rumors during ❬Idol Inc.❭.

——————————

[Heard STier disbanded]

(Article Screenshot)

During Idol Inc., I really thought an amazing boy group would come out, but this feels kind of empty

——————————

He then realized why the post had such a strange structure.

The post, with hundreds of comments, was covered in keywords to get caught by search engines.

He unconsciously lowered his gaze to the best comment.

And then, long sentences with wildly divided pros and cons flooded his vision.

[The problem with STier was that they excessively pushed a producing member who was just for show and not even that good. He even had attitude controversies since Idol Inc., so I still don't get why they pushed him.]

[There were a lot of controversies, but I think the biggest problem was their planning ability. Anyway, I hope all the members live wellㅇㅇ]

[Planning ability = producing, and only one member participated in producing. That explains everything.]

[The promotion-killer Kim Raebin lololol]

"......"

Kim Raebin was not good at guessing the hidden intentions of people who spoke indirectly.

However, he could sufficiently analyze and distinguish linguistic rules and described characteristics.

So he realized.

[That corrupt bastard is so fucking annoyingㅠ]

It was about him.

Kim Raebin forgot to breathe and started checking related content.

[It was seriously sigh-inducing to see him greedily take his parts every time while making sh*tty songs.]

[Every time he acted like a polite maknae on broadcasts... ha, in that time he should have just taken the push well. He was neither here nor there, so even people who became fans after seeing him perform probably all left after seeing his no-fun personality pretend not to be greedy.]

[I wanted to puke every time I saw his eyeball-akgaes overhyping him. It's better that they disbanded. Yep, hope I never have to see him againㅠ]

[He must be embarrassed himself, seeing how there's no news from him lolol]

Comments with a similar tone were being posted as recently as a few hours ago, as if they were being spewed out because they were unbearable.

[Ah, so refreshingㅠㅠ Finally, even the muggles know how sh*tty this is.]

[I was annoyed being treated like a jealous akgae, so it feels like justice has been served lol]

If these were purely baseless accusations, sentences made only of malice, Kim Raebin would have been almost unaffected. He might have realized they just wanted to conveniently blame someone for STier's failure, and a member they found unsightly just happened to be there.

However, each sentence Kim Raebin saw had a logical, analytical structure.

They detailed why there were problems with STier's producing and marketing, why the part distribution was unfair, and why Kim Raebin was the cause of this whole situation.

A one-sided argument based on analysis sounded like the truth, especially to someone panicking from reading posts expressing intense criticism and contempt for him.

The calm rebuttal that he had not been involved in the overall production and that the majority of it was mere speculation did not come to mind.

He could not see the opposing opinions like 'Aren't these people all crazy' either.

All this criticism just seemed persuasive.

Moreover, these 'fans' were not just criticizing.

[I don't think Eugene will come back after returning to his home country... What do I do, I seriously want to die.]

[Honestly, isn't having problems with your main job more deserving of a beating than dating? Why was our boy getting beaten up for real? lol]

Affection for others, indignation, a sense of injustice, and exhaustion were palpable.

"......"

So Kim Raebin, rubbing his hands that had grown cold as if his blood had frozen, began to ponder.

*How much did I do wrong?*

Had he committed a great wrong?

"......"

He became a man of few words.

He was not the type to be skilled at sociable small talk, but his tendency to speak affectionately and endlessly about things he liked had vanished.

Before even three days had passed, his worried family finally asked him.

"Raebin, is something wrong?"

"......"

"Kim Raebin!"

"...So,"

In the end, Kim Raebin confessed to his sister everything, from the gossip board post he had seen to the anonymous site he had found through a link.

"Why did you look at something like that!"

His sister freaked out and started patting him on the back. Kim Raebin felt his heart warm, but he could not hold back and finally asked his question.

He needed an answer. How much of the fault was his that the group STier had not done well?

However, his family sternly cut him off.

"Raebin. They're all out of their minds. Why are they cursing out a celebrity like that instead of living their own lives? You didn't do anything wrong, so don't look at things like that anymore. Got it?"

He could not be convinced since they seemed to be defending him unconditionally, but he could not keep asking either.

The more he talked, the more distressed his family seemed.

The day he saw his grandmother secretly wiping away tears in her room, Kim Raebin decided to think for himself and find the answer.

Lying in bed before sleep, he decided he would think it over carefully without being afraid and make a judgment.

So he thought as hard as he could.

*First...*

The numerous criticisms could be broadly divided into two causes.

Issue 1.

[The album quality dropped because of Kim Raebin's producing.]

Kim Raebin had not been able to participate proactively in the producing. He had only been assigned parts, and the company modified the results as they pleased. He was not being selfish.

However, being well-intentioned and being competent were not the same thing.

*So, it seems it's my fault for not being able to express my opinion more wisely.*

He had to reflect on it, Kim Raebin concluded.

Next was Issue 2.

Kim Raebin felt his throat tighten and tossed in bed. He barely managed to recall the sentence.

[Kim Raebin monopolized good parts and failed to pull them off properly, having a major negative impact on the team.]

Kim Raebin... had tried to do his best with the parts he was given.

He really had.

*But, maybe that wasn't enough.*

According to the analysis posts, it was strongly written why that part should have gone to someone who suited it better, not the 'part-devouring' Kim Raebin.

Besides, there was empirical evidence.

*Our group didn't do well.*

He had failed to perform the important parts in a sufficiently charming way.

So, conversely, the possibility that it would have been more beneficial if someone else had performed those impressive parts was wide open.

And if the fans thought so... then that was probably right.

"......"

So, perhaps the answer was simple.

[Conclusion: Kim Raebin has no ability as an idol.]

It was right to stick to producing, as he had decided when he was young.

He inhaled and exhaled, trying to think correctly.

In a positive direction.

*I also felt there were problems with the elements of our promotional songs that these fans are criticizing.*

His eye for things was still decent. So, it was okay to continue with that work.

*Yes. Let's do that.*

Kim Raebin reached a conclusion and finally felt his head clear up.

He would make songs.

*I can do that much.*

Lying in bed and looking at the ceiling, he committed to his revised future plans.

"......"

No tears came out, but for some reason, his chest felt empty and sorrowful.

~~~

TeSTAR's dorm.

A flood of memories hit Kim Raebin as if he were being drenched by a waterfall. It was less like remembering and more like re-experiencing, the feelings of that time was so vivid.

It was as if he had heard words that had been held back for so long and were finally poured out.

Just like how one would deeply empathize and feel pity upon hearing a close friend sorrowfully confide their worries.

For Kim Raebin right now, that someone was himself.

"......"

The two hyungs were still talking in the living room.

However, he staggered, turned his back on the dim light leaking from the living room, and quietly returned to the dark room.

Then he sat at his desk and opened his laptop.

His mind was numb and pale, as if doused with cold water.

However, the final question that had emerged at the end of his data collection was resurfacing.

Clearly.

[Who am I as a performer.]

This needed to be revised.

Shorter.

[Who am I.]

And more specifically.

[In this moment, who am I.]

He placed his hand on the laptop and tried to accept what he was feeling now without distortion.

It was an emotion he had been able to avoid tasting deeply until now because he had debuted with TeSTAR.

An essential element that was most nakedly revealed when he was at his weakest.

Irreversible failure.

Pain.

"......"

Kim Raebin decided to speak about pain.

~~~

The teaser was released.
