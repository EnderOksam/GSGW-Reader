# Chapter 462

*Insane.*

The fansite master held her breath as the game finished installing. Normally, a modern person would kill time on their phone with messages or social media, but she was in no state for that.

*When and how on earth did they make something like this?*She lacked the mental capacity for questions or analysis. Her heart pounded not with tension, but with a mix of excitement, overwhelming emotion, and immense anticipation.

A moment later, a notification appeared.

[Installation complete.]

[Launch now ( )]

She checked the box without hesitation. The screen went black as logos flashed by.

[Ruin Factory Area]

[Stars Orbit]

A modified TeSTAR logo, designed to look like a corporate or industrial disaster symbol, descended from the top of the screen. The title appeared below it.

[Roll the Dice]

~please enter the code

The new title track's main melody played, remixed into a slightly eerie and modern tune. The background showed a dark wooden floor, a board game, and a pair of dice. The quality was perfect.

"Waaaaah."

It was this serious. The fansite master felt a joy that bordered on bewilderment, but a subtle worry crept in at the same time.

*But I don't really know much about games...*

She had expected simple fan service at best. Now that the game was so elaborate, she felt hesitant and wondered if she could enjoy it properly.

She didn't know gaming's unwritten rules or parts that required skillful control. She had only casually enjoyed ❬Section 127❭ because it was a mobile game. Since some people just don't like games, she figured this might be polarizing content.

*Hmm.*

Of course, her anticipation won out. There was no time to hesitate.

"Let's go!" she exclaimed, bravely pressing 'New game'.

The letters were sucked away, and the dice began to roll. After a short loading screen, the screen brightened.

[2025. 06. 01.]

[PM 07:03]

[Yoohoo!]

A sports car sped down a road at sunset, filling the computer screen.

"......"

*What is this?*

She didn't recognize the people in the sports car. The man and woman wore sunglasses, and the man definitely wasn't from TeSTAR.

How could she be so sure, even if they were game characters?

*It's live-action?*

It wasn't computer graphics.

[♩♪♬♬~ ♪♪♬~]

The sports car raced along to the upbeat music, transitioning like a movie scene. For a moment, she suspected she had the wrong product, but she already knew that wasn't the case.

*This is... 'Pose'.*

The song playing from the sports car was TeSTAR's previous single, 'Pose'. The cheerful dance track, perfect for a drive, flowed like background music.

*This is the right TeSTAR app.*

She decided to wait. The sports car continued racing down the mountain road, which was dyed red by the sunset. Soon, something unusual appeared. The road was blocked.

"...?"

A huge barricade with alternating yellow and black stripes and flashing emergency lights sat in the middle of the road.

[What the?]

The music volume decreased. Someone walked steadily toward the stopped sports car. It was a tall man.

[Excuse me, sir.]

"......"

It was Lee Sejin. Dressed in a neat police uniform, Lee Sejin smiled and leaned his head toward the sports car.

The woman in the driver's seat flicked her arm defiantly.

[What's the problem?]

[Ah, the road ahead is currently closed to traffic. I'm sorry, but please turn around and use the main road.]

[Where's this main road? It's gonna take me another 30 minutes to go around. How about you at least give me a reason?]

[I'd like to, but I'm on official duty, so it's difficult to explain the details. I'd appreciate your cooperation.]

Lee Sejin spoke with a friendly, gentle smile.

*He's so handsome...*

His hair, styled to reveal his forehead, suited him perfectly. However, the woman in the driver's seat, perhaps a very loyal girlfriend, seemed completely unfazed by Lee Sejin. Instead, the camera meaningfully captured her sizing him up with a strange look.

[......]

[Ma'am, would you please turn the car around?]

A strange tension mounted as the background music changed.

*What, what is it?*

[This police officer seems quite young.]

[Ah, I was just assigned here. Haha.]

[Is that so?]

[Yes. So, it may be an inconvenience, but I must insist.]

Lee Sejin maintained his smile to the end and gave a slight bow. However, the tense music and strangely amplified footsteps didn't stop.

Thump, thump.

"......"

After Lee Sejin, bathed in the red sunset glow, entered a guard post, the sports car turned around and began to leave.

Vroom.

*Phew.*

Just as the fansite master let out a strange sigh of relief, the quiet man in the passenger seat broke the silence.

[Huh? This isn't the way to the main road, is it?]

*Huh??*

[Oh, if we turn here, it connects right back to that road, honey.]

[What?]

[We're going on the road that kid blocked.]

*Excuse me??*

As if voicing the fansite master's thoughts, the man in the passenger seat was horrified.

[Why are we going there? Honey, the police blocked it.]

[Police?]

The driver scoffed.

[Have you ever seen a cop speak that nicely to a speeding sports car?]

[What?]

[And look. That thing blocking the road.]

As the passenger turned his head, the camera's view shifted with him. The barricade appeared on screen again, shown slowly and intercut with a flashback of Lee Sejin's smiling face.

The driver's voice echoed.

[That barricade isn't police issue. It's custom-made.]

[...!!]

[I don't think that punk is a cop.]

[Ah...]

[Look how young that kid is. What is he, a YouTuber? A streamer? Some kid pulling a prank. He probably has a hidden camera somewhere.]

At that moment, the bottom of the view turned translucent black, and something appeared on the screen.

▶[That could be it. -Relieved]

▶[How can you be so sure? -Wary]

It was a choice.

*Right. It's a game, so of course there are choices.*

It felt a little unfamiliar, but she decided to just pick the one that felt right. However, a time limit appeared.

[00:10:00]

*Ten seconds?!*

She panicked, but a gut feeling made her choose the bottom option.

[How can you be so sure?]

Her choice was born from movie clichés. It was common sense that deliberately entering a blocked-off area would lead to something bad.

*Sejin said not to go!*

*So don't go! Why are you going! Does it matter if he's a real cop?*

*Even if he's an idol, listen to him!*

She didn't know why she was making choices for people who weren't even on TeSTAR's side. Pressured by the time limit, she hastily made her selection.

[Ah, so you don't trust me.]

The driver got angry.

[Your companion is displeased.]

"Aaargh."

*Wait, was the right answer to pick the top one and placate her?*

She was thrown into confusion, but the game didn't stop.

[N-No, that's not it...]

[Then just watch. I'll get you there, honey, so you won't be late for your airplane.]

With those words, the driver sped off, steering the sports car wildly with one hand. The camera zoomed out, showing the car forcing its way onto a side path toward the road blocked by the 'No Entry' sign. It captured the eerie atmosphere of the darkening mountain.

"......"

She swallowed hard, watching the scene as if it were a thriller movie.

A short while later, the sports car, its headlights on, drove down the dark mountain path. It passed a strange, old building that had suddenly appeared.

[That's random.]

It was a large, gray building that looked crudely patched with concrete, but there was no sign of human presence.

[A factory? No?]

[Ah, maybe.]

[It's a bit weird for it to be in the mountains.]

The view switched to a slow-motion shot from the car's perspective, slowly scanning the building's exterior. It focused on an embossed symbol on the outer wall.

[K.I.S.]

The fansite master remembered it.

*I think that was in the music video?*

That's right. It was the initial on the floor of that gray corridor. Could these people be entering that prison as the players?

*Right, the perspective has shifted to their first-person view, so that could be it.*

Just as she was making her own deductions, something happened.

[Huh, what's that...]

At that moment, the car crashed.

THUD.

The sports car flipped over.

"...!?"

*Huh?*

Then it exploded.

[KABOOM!!]

The screen spun wildly, bounced, and rolled on the ground.

*Aaaargh!!*

*Crazy!*

The fansite master screamed without realizing it. The flickering camera view showed the half-destroyed sports car, then a hand groping the ground. A choice appeared.

▶[Grab the smartphone.]

▶[Call for your companion.]

*This... This is!*

*Calling for my companion is pointless! It has to be the smartphone!*

Calling out wouldn't heal an injured person. She had to do something productive first. She quickly pressed the top option. To her delight, the fumbling hand found the smartphone.

*Yay!*

Just as she, completely immersed, was about to cheer for the hand dialing 911, a scream was heard.

[Aargh!!]

The smartphone was broken.

"Ugh."

*Th-There's no right answer here?*

Thinking she must have chosen wrong, the fansite master broke into a cold sweat and considered restarting the game. Then, a voice called out.

[Are you okay over there?!]

The screen whipped around as if a head had turned. A tall figure in a police uniform rushed over. Lee Sejin was running toward them.

[Just a moment!]

*Huh? What's going on?*

She was confused and couldn't grasp what was happening. The approaching Lee Sejin immediately extended a hand.

[Give that to me.]

He gently took the smartphone that was in the way, then shrugged. He made eye contact with the player and fired a tranquilizer gun.

"......"

The view, the screen, momentarily blurred.

Silence.

[......]

After a short pause, the screen reappeared, showing the view from behind Lee Sejin's shoulder. The player was apparently being carried piggyback by Lee Sejin.

"......"

Still unable to figure out the situation, she heard Lee Sejin's voice.

[Ah~ It works every time, every single time.]

The screen moved again. A red frame, a crumpled steel body, and smoke. The player was being pushed back into the half-destroyed sports car.

"U-U-Ugh?!"

[Isn't it great how they break the rules on their own? I did my job, so my conscience is clear.]

"......"

*In-Insane!*

Lee Sejin's clean-cut face appeared outside the sports car again. He held a notepad.

[Now, this time as well...]

['No Entry sign'... check, 'direct notification'... check, 'politeness'... check, 'repeated emphasis'...]

Like a real police officer, Lee Sejin tipped his hat and winked.

[I did ask you to turn the car around, didn't I?]

[Guhk...]

[Yep, yep. I knew you wouldn't listen~]

SLAM.

The sports car door shut. A black vehicle towed the half-destroyed red sports car away. Lee Sejin's humming from inside the black vehicle echoed like background music.

[Mmm-hmm~]

It was a B-side track from TeSTAR's previous album, famous among fans as a relaxed, easy-listening song. Over that chilling dissonance, subtitles appeared.

[Prologue End]

[Welcome to the K.I.S]

The fansite master screamed silently.

*What is this!?*

***

Her mind completely blown, the fansite master stared blankly for thirty seconds before finally pressing the enter button to start Chapter 1. She continued playing entirely because she was won over by Lee Sejin's handsomeness.

*Moondae is probably going to look incredibly handsome too...*

Though she hadn't realized it, the shocking and dynamic story also played a big part.

*Alright!*

The main game, which she started with renewed determination, now proceeded from the members' perspective.

[Need a ride?]

On a rainy autumn day, Bae Sejin lost his wallet. He got help from Ryu Chungwoo and rode in his car to work. Then he received a sudden proposal from Ryu Chungwoo.

[There's something I want to steal.]

[...Excuse me?]

[I need a hacker.]

That's right. Bae Sejin was a hacker, but if that was the case...

*...What about Raebin?*

He was in the monitor room in the music video, wasn't he? Even now, sitting in the back seat with glasses and a laptop, he gave off that exact vibe. The fansite master gladly chose the option to ask about him.

[Isn't he... a hacker too?]

[No. He's just strong. Really... he is strong.]

"Pfft-hahaha."

Kim Raebin, who had tilted his head from the back seat, lowered it again. To the fansite master's delight, Kim Raebin later joyfully showed off his prowess as the strongest attacker after they infiltrated the facility.

Anyway, Ryu Chungwoo showed Bae Sejin information about a jewel called 'tutáculum' (Tutaculum).

[Tutaculum?]

[It means protection or shelter in Latin. It seems to be the key to accessing the facility where this jewel is located.]

Ryu Chungwoo said he received it from a colleague. He handed over a key-shaped device and asked Bae Sejin to analyze it. This was handled as a flashback, connecting directly to a cutscene of them infiltrating the facility.

[But I never imagined that the place would be this strange protective-management-confinement facility.]

From then on, the game was a continuous series of heart-pounding hide-and-seek and choices.

*Aaargh.*

After suffering Lee Sejin's spicy flavor in the prologue, the fansite master had no desire to get caught, no matter how handsome he was. No matter which character she controlled, she did her best to avoid the guards and chose her options carefully.

From this point on, another system was added. A 10-sided die.

CLATTER...

When she made a wrong choice, she could roll the die for a chance to recover if the number was low enough. The more critical the mistake, the lower the required number.

*Hooo!*

[Oh~ Today's coffee.]

She narrowly avoided Lee Sejin's gaze, barely hiding in a cabinet to catch her breath. Not long after, she saw her favorite's face.

[Mm.]

"Aaargh!"

Moondae was in a lab. The problem was that he was hooked up to an IV, sweating and looking like he was in immense pain, but he begged for the syringe not to be removed.

[...Don't, please.]

▶[Break the syringe. -Sympathy]

▶[Leave it. -Unmoving Heart]

*Aaaargh!!*

The fansite master, controlling Kim Raebin, was horrified. Her trembling hand ultimately moved toward the top option.

*I'm sorry, Moondae...!*

*But if I don't break the syringe here...! In movie clichés, this is where Moondae dies!*

Sure enough, Moondae stabilized as soon as the syringe broke. With teary eyes, she watched the close-up of Moondae collapsing unconscious with a peaceful expression and hit the screenshot button.

Fortunately, Kim Raebin picked Moondae up and carried him to safety.

*Thank goodness...*

The deeply immersed fansite master wiped away her cold sweat.

What followed was Ryu Chungwoo winning a battle of wits with Cha Eugene behind an iron door to get information, followed by a breathtaking chase scene. The phantom thieves soon deduced that this was a strange place that imprisoned test subjects. The information about the 'jewel' had been a trap to lure them in.

[We have to escape.]

They changed their route. Thanks to Cha Eugene's aggro, they soon found an official escape route, but a final obstacle lay there.

[If we just get past this outer wall...]

[Shh.]

*Gasp, Ahyeon!*

Thump, thump.

A riot baton, gloves, combat boots, his jaw, his eyes. The figure walking down the corridor was directed with cinematic mise-en-scène. Throughout this movie, or rather this game, Seon Ahyeon had only appeared with a cool and calm demeanor. It suited him so ridiculously well that it was an unexpected charm.

*It really is all about the face...*

The fansite master groaned. Anyway, she had a hunch.

*In the music video... there was a hint that it might have been Ahyeon who subdued Bae Sejin during his escape!*

This had to be that hint. With a pounding heart, she repeatedly mashed the 'Hide' option.

[......]

Surprisingly, even though it seemed like the right choice, the die appeared.

[Target Number: 6]

A sixty percent chance. The fansite master clenched her teeth.

CLATTER...

*Please, please...!*

The die slowly stopped. The number it pointed to was two.

[2]

"......"

Seon Ahyeon calmly disappeared down the corridor. Just like that, she safely passed the turn.

*Phew.*

[Let's go.]

She sighed and watched Bae Sejin approach a giant iron door. It was the scene from the music video. He placed the key on the lock pad. Now, he just had to enter the code.

*Huh?*

*Come to think of it...*

[______]

*What's the code?*

"......"

The game stopped. There was no ten-second time limit, meaning she could enter it whenever she was ready, but what on earth was the code?

At that moment, Bae Sejin's narration flowed across the screen.

[It's a code I already know.]

[I heard it from the phantom thief when I received this key.]

His recollection of Ryu Chungwoo's voice began to play.

[I need you to analyze the key.]

Bae Sejin's eyes appeared in a close-up.

[And I did analyze it. The information the phantom thief wanted was there. The location, the jewel's value, and so on, but there was also a lot of dummy data, seemingly inserted to increase the file size and make it harder to find.]

[Among the dummy data I almost overlooked, there was a single photo of a man... and on the back of that photo, there was a code.]

The narration ended.

*What, what is it?*

The bewildered fansite master reread the subtitles.

*A single photo... of a man.*

"......"

*Could it be?*

"......"

She shot up from her seat, grabbed the album box she had pushed aside, and pulled out the single remaining item. The photo card.

On the back of Kim Raebin's photo card, where he held an awl's tip in his mouth, a code was written with the logo.

[Roll the Dice]

~please enter the code

[AW23-5DQ2-B25E]

With trembling hands, she typed it in. The moment she hit enter, the Bae Sejin on screen also moved his hand over the lock pad.

And...

[Success... I did it!]

The door opened.

"Yes!"

She reflexively clenched both fists, then had a realization.

*Wow!*

*This structure really makes buying the album feel worthwhile!*

Sensing a happy ending, she stood up from her seat. It was the reaction of a game-illiterate person, unaware she had just seen something like 'Normal Ending - 3'. In any case, she was finally forming an evaluation.

*The immersion was seriously insane!*

She had no idea how they filmed this or how much the members had to grind, but she wanted to play it again right away and choose different options. Most importantly, she loved the outfits. Since the acting was mostly action-oriented, it wasn't cringey and felt good. Bae Sejin was such a good actor that he created a great synergy.

However, one thing bothered her.

*Ah, the phantom thieves were portrayed too much like the main characters, right?*

There was a slight screen time issue, and it felt like events were structured from the phantom thieves' perspective.

*Ah, and Ahyeon really had very little screen time.*

She still had unresolved questions, like what the board game was about.

*It was just... an unbelievable level of quality.*

She didn't know what to call it. It was like watching a movie where she got to choose what the characters did. She felt that even fans who disliked games would love this.

*Interactive movie.*

Having properly experienced the genre for the first time, she nodded eagerly.

*Thinking about it again, the outfits were so good... ha, it was just the best.*

As she waited for the epilogue to load, she decided to go to the bathroom and naturally picked up her smartphone.

[Hurry and play the other album versions, I'm telling you this out of loyalty, for real]

"......"

[Holy shit, the prologue is different when you play version 2, the prisoner comes out]

[The album had 3 versions, was that for choosing factions?? Phantom Thief, Prisoner, Guard?]

*What, what did they say?*

[The main stream is almost the same but the personal events are different, holy crap what is this]

"......"

The flustered fansite master forgot about the bathroom and ran to the delivery box. She opened the second USB box. Shoving aside Cha Eugene's photo card, where he had his tongue slightly out, she frantically took out the second USB album.

She swallowed hard.

*Sh-Should I start with this one?*

*Since Moondae is a prisoner, do I have to play this to see more of Moondae?*

Just as the fansite master was lost in confusion, her smartphone screen lit up again.

[Oh, if you don't break the syringe, Moondae joins the phantom thieves]

[Holy shit, is Park Moondae's phantom thief outfit insane or what, his thighs! HIS THIGHS!]

"......"

*Wh... What's that now...?*

Thus began the descent into the rabbit hole of TeSTAR's album lore.
