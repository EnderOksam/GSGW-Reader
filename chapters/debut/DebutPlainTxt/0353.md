# Chapter 353

"Come in."

"......"

Park Moondae, wearing a black hoodie, staggered a little as he entered the front door. Kongi groaned and hovered around him.

Only the dog's cry echoed in the spacious and quiet dorm.

"Kongi, paw."

Cheongryeo was about to pick up his dog, who clearly knew it was time to head back home.

*Hmm.*

He decided to ask his guest for help.

"Can you help me?"

"......"

Park Moondae silently wiped Kongi's paws, gave him some food, and even let him rest his head against his legs.

He was completely out of it.

He showed none of the reactions he usually had when Cheongryeo crossed the line.

Cheongryeo put the hot drink on the other side and spoke casually.

"Have you failed?"

"......"

The hooded head looked up. There was no rebuttal.

*Right.*

It would depend on what kind of failure it was, but it didn't seem to be work-related.

Otherwise, I would already know.

He kept a close eye on his competitors.

His access to information might even be faster than Park Moondae's mediocre agency's ability to realize its own problems.

Cheongryeo looked at the competitor who was still wearing a mask and then continued.

"I heard you had something to say. Say it. I will listen."

"......"

Park Moondae looked down at the dog and spoke in a low voice.

"You said you explained the situation to someone else."

"Yes."

Oh, did he fail in that way?

It appeared that Park Moondae had told yet another person the truth.

Park Moondae had already told one of the members who was the same age as him, at least on the surface, so it was very likely that the other person he told was also his age. That was simply how relationships and the pressures within them worked.

He could have said something like 'I told you not to do it,' but... Cheongryeo held back.

Even if he didn't say that out loud, it seemed like he knew it well.

"An excuse... You said you had an excuse that you liked to use."

"Yes."

He looked at him wondering if he was just asking for information, not a deal or threat, but his Hubaenim didn't seem to be in a state to make such calculations.

It was really out of the ordinary.

Cheongryeo confirmed that the reason Park Moondae didn't hold the cup in front of him was because his hands were shaking.

The sleeve fabric fluttered.

"......"

He looked like the bones of a house that had barely withstood some massive shock—outwardly intact, but full of tiny cracks that could cause it to collapse at any moment.

Cheongryeo found traces of his past self, so old that they were almost completely unfamiliar, in this version of Moondae.

So he offered the information without holding back.

"First of all, find a storyline similar to your own. You can refer to bestselling novels with good ratings, or perhaps movies."

Cheongryeo had even compiled appropriate examples for each year and put them into a list.

It wasn't a good idea to tell them what would be released in the future, even by mistake.

"Mention it in an interview before doing anything else, focusing on the keywords."

"......"

"Say that you're attracted to the concept. It'll be like foreshadowing."

That way, any exposure caused by a member who had heard the secret could later be dismissed as a joke or misunderstanding.

His method involved choosing the public over the personal relationship.

"It nullifies any potential threat the other person could pose. And it's effective, kind of like a vaccine."

However, no words came out of the masked mouth.

Cheongryeo made a guess rather quickly.

He was probably displeased. Perhaps he didn't like what he'd heard.

Park Moondae was too emotional to the point of being soft at times. Well, he had only been back once, so it was only natural.

Cheongryeo offered another alternative even though he did not like it internally.

"Perhaps you don't like that idea. Then just tell him that you made a mistake because the workload was too much."

"...!"

"You came up with this imaginative story as a joke at first, but at some point, you started thinking it might actually be real. That should do it."

Park Moondae raised his head.

This method focused entirely on making the other person feel at ease, so Cheongryeo himself had rarely used it except for a few times early on.

It could be unpredictable and inefficient.

If there was even a slight hint of insincerity, the listener might act unpredictably, and that annoyed him.

However, it seemed like the right way for Park Moondae.

Well, if he cared that much, he didn't think he would say anything insincere. Cheongryeo kindly added an explanation.

"They'll believe you even if the explanation doesn't seem perfect. It's not so different from what they're probably already thinking is happening to you, but it's also a positive answer."

Even though he didn't reply, Cheongryeo finished his words slowly as he watched Kongi give its paw to Park Moondae.

"Those sorts of people won't easily change the conclusions they've already reached."

And he laughed.

"They don't want to be proven wrong, do they?"

"......"

"Just give him the answer he wants to hear, and the problem will be solved."

In front of Cheongryeo, steam was still rising from the drink that Park Moondae had not touched.

And Park Moondae, who finally met his eyes...

His eyes were full of conviction.

"No."

Uh-oh.

My head was a complete mess.

As a result, here I was at dawn, sitting in an unfamiliar dorm. It had the same layout as our own, but everything about it felt different. Needless to say, it wasn't exactly a pleasant situation.

But I didn't know what to do, and even though my head felt like it was crawling with insects and I was about to lose control, there was one thing my mind could still do—reject something I knew to be untrue.

Denial.

"No."

Cheongryeo, who was sitting across from me, tilted his head to the side.

I suddenly felt much more awake, as though my brain had just been doused with cold water.

"Hmm?"

"He... He's not that kind of guy."

This, I knew for certain.

*Seon Ahyeon was the sort of person who could admit when he was wrong.*

In fact, he could be utterly cruel to himself about it.

That was how he had lived his life so far—always aware of his own trauma and flaws.

I heard what Seon Ahyeon shouted... I remembered it.

– I-I'm sorry... I-it's hard to believe you... I-I can't help...

"...He wanted to believe it."

"I guess so."

"But he thought it was right not to believe it."

"......"

"I didn't get to the conclusion in a proper way, so things ended up like this while he was trying to reach an uncomfortable answer."

I... Because I couldn't explain it properly, he misunderstood that mental illness was the correct answer.

*...In fact, the battles were also splendid.*

I smiled bitterly as I recalled everything from seizures to alcoholism.

...I didn't know why I thought Seon Ahyeon would believe it.

"So I'm not interested in using methods like that. They won't work. This situation is completely different."

If it were Seon Ahyeon, he might think about it again and realize that it was an appropriate excuse that I made.

The damage would really be irreversible then.

"Is that so? Alright."

Cheongryeo nodded as if he had nothing to regret.

"However... Don't you think that's a very presumptuous judgment?"

What?

"I..."

"No, not Hubaenim... that person."

"...!"

"Wasn't it that Hubaenim got into trouble because he was sure that the wrong answer was the right answer? I don't know why you're so concerned..."

"......"

"Isn't it unfair?"

I clenched my fist.

But I soon let go of my strength with a sigh.

"I'm the one who told him, so it should be me who deals with the repercussions."

The words came out softer than expected.

"It's not like somebody tortured me to get the answer out of me. I'm the one who told him, so it only makes sense that I take responsibility for it."

"Hmm."

The guy on the other side didn't seem to agree very much, but he didn't dispute it this time either.

"Really? Cheer up. You can give up anytime if you don't like it."

"Even if you quit the group, it's not like your life will be over. Isn't that right?"

This bastard. While his way of thinking seemed slightly healthier than before...

*Why did he still manage to tick me off like this?*

It was pretty upsetting.

Besides, it wasn't the time to think about my own situation right now.

*Somebody else is going to turn into a status window, you bastard!*

I gritted my teeth.

Even if I told him, he'd probably frustrate me further by saying something like, "I mean, it's not you who's dying..."

I just kept my mouth shut.

And then I looked at him again.

*He had so little empathy, and yet he was still listening to me and giving advice.*

I'd been sitting on the bench to clear my head, and I hadn't expected to end up being invited into his dorm and served tea.

Hmm. I guess this meant he was a little better than me in this regard.

I recalled how I'd closed the door in his face when he'd come to see me because of his dog, then remained silent for a moment.

But still, I hadn't suddenly shown up at his door without warning. Regardless, I should still thank him.

"Anyway, thanks for the advice."

"Even though you're not going to follow it? Haha."

That's up to me to decide, you bastard.

Anyway, I started thinking again with a clearer mind than the night before.

*First of all… I will never tell him that my group is going to suffer some setbacks…*

...He'd enjoy it far too much.

"Kongi, do you want a treat?"

"Woof!"

I watched him deftly handle his dog and chose to say nothing.

In the end, there was only one way to come back.

*I need to activate Seon Ahyeon's trait again.*

Resilience.

The surest way forward was to activate it and counteract his status abnormality.

But... Looking back, I didn't think my words would work.

*I'm not even sure he'll be willing to have a conversation with me.*

Perhaps he'd even written out a list of recommended hospitals for my benefit.

Enough.

I stopped myself as my thoughts wandered into useless territory again.

It was better to think productively.

*That's why someone else should do it, not me.*

What I'd done during ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ by relying on my trait, the other members would have to do in my stead.

"......"

Hmm. I needed someone else to help me.

I came up with a few ideas and stood up—or tried to, anyway.

"Woof."

The dog whined and stopped me from doing so.

Cheongryeo's eyes narrowed.

"Kongi seems disappointed to see you go. You can stay awhile if you want."

At your dorm?

"What about your members?"

I'd heard that they were releasing an album before they joined the military. So why weren't they anywhere to be seen?

"Personal schedules. Or because it's a vacation period."

Really?

I looked around again.

Well, I didn't want to closely observe where other people lived, but... There were some things that caught my eye.

Photo frames, souvenirs, and the like, which seemed to be gifts from the early days following their debut, were still displayed in the living room.

"......"

Hmm. I think I'm the one who took and sold that photo… Forget it. It can't be.

I turned my eyes and looked at the guy and the dog on the other side.

And I impulsively asked him.

"Do you really have no interest in telling your members right now?"

"No," he said instantly.

There was no sign of concern.

"I'm actually curious why you told them at all. There was no need to do that, was there?"

"......"

I'd love to know why, too.

Fuck, I didn't know why I did that...

"Dunno. I just… wanted to."

"......"

I answered resignedly, wondering if he would laugh at me.

However, Cheongryeo just replied like this after petting the dog's head.

"I suppose I understand feeling that way."

"...!"

"It's frustrating."

Surprisingly, a faint understanding emerged from the tone.

"Don't you feel frustrated?"

"Not really. Hmm. Actually, maybe I did feel that way in the past. But people can get used to almost anything."

Cheongryeo took his hand away from the dog and smiled a little.

"It's one of the things humans are known for. We adapt. I'm sure you'll feel fine soon enough."

"......"

My head thought that it was bullshit. But it was weird... It was funny that it was emotionally comforting.

I finally answered with a sigh.

"...Thanks."

"Yes."

He was holding his own dog, so I'd have to take advantage of this gap and get up soon.

I gulped down the tea he'd made for me, which was now lukewarm, and stood up.

I didn't even pause to consider whether he'd drugged it.

It seemed I still wasn't fully myself.

I shook my head and stepped away from the table.

*Ding dong.*

The doorbell rang.

I inadvertently looked at the intercom. I decided that I would hide if it was someone from his agency.

But on the screen was a tall figure dressed lightly and wearing a facemask.

It was Seon Ahyeon.

"...!!"

I reflexively backed away. What was he doing here?

He can't have come to see me.

That couldn't be the reason. He would have no idea that I was here.

There was no reason for him to come see Cheongryeo, either.

*I haven't received any contact from the company or the members.*

My phone wasn't on mute, and it hadn't vibrated.

"......"

Wait…

Ahyeon was holding two smartphones in his hands.

That apple-themed case.

It was my smartphone.

"Ah."

Then I realized what had happened. After the mess I'd made last night, I'd told no one where I was going and left the dorm early in the morning.

"A visitor so early in the morning."

Goddamn it.
