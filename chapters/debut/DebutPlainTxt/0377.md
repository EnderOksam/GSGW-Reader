# Chapter 377 - The Wise Survival, V

"Raebin, aren't you going to eat~?"

"I ate before I came home, thank you!"

Kim Raebin answered energetically as he came out of the bathroom after washing his face.

After coming to Seoul unexpectedly, he stayed at Ryu Gunwoo's officetel for a few days, but his parents soon spoke with his uncle in Seoul, who agreed to let him stay at his house.

However, after practicing all day, he would follow Cha Eugene to Ryu Gunwoo's officetel to talk strategy and more often than not end up eating there. So he felt he was still imposing on Gunwoo somewhat.

*Why is he so nice to me?*

Kim Raebin still wondered about that, but he diligently carried out his role in his new routine.

Yes. Although his life had changed tremendously, he adjusted to it surprisingly quickly and naturally. As if he had lived like this before...

Kim Raebin thought carefully and came to a conclusion.

*When I think about it, it's not much different from school!*

A structured daily life, as though he were taking classes—it was much the same!

Speaking of school, as soon as Kim Raebin signed with LeTi, it was arranged for him to transfer to an arts high school in Seoul, where entertainment-related activities could count toward attendance...

*The agency is a great place.*

Now that he thought about it, there was an interesting coincidence.

The agency both Eugene and Gunwoo belonged to happened to be the same company with which he'd signed his first song contract.

– *...You signed a song contract with LeTi?*

– *...? Yes!*

When Kim Raebin was brought in that day, he had no idea how delighted the person in charge of recruiting new talent at LeTi was.

And why had Kim Raebin so readily followed two people he'd never met before to Seoul and started living the life of an idol trainee?

They'd persuaded him on the way home from the strawberry greenhouse.

The hyung named Ryu Gunwoo (whom the other guy, for some reason, called 'Moondae') had calmly explained why they'd come to him.

– *I've listened to some of the songs you've composed so far, and there are a lot of them that go well with choreo.*

– *What is choreo?*

– *Dance. And most teams that focus on the latest trends in music and dance are idol groups.*

The hyung continued, as though posing a question.

– *So, I think experiencing the performances directly will be a great help in your future composing career.*

– *...!*

Kim Raebin had never done it before... Those were persuasive words!

After that, Ryu Gunwoo continued to speak fluidly.

About the method he would like to recommend.

In-house survival show.

– *A survival show is the fastest and most thorough way to experience performing as an idol. And if you get eliminated, you can walk away with just the experience you need.*

Kim Raebin was impressed.

– *...! I see. So I can gain a lot of experience that'll help me compose, then naturally get eliminated?*

– *Perhaps.*

No. That was Kim Raebin's mistake. Park Moondae's suggestion was based on the premise that Kim Raebin would be eliminated, but he had never promised that would happen.

He intended to keep Kim Raebin in the running, even if he had to resort to manipulation to prevent his elimination.

Kim Raebin was on course to debut without even realizing it, but he was still convinced that he would be eliminated.

*But I never thought I'd be the only one of us three to win the first performance...*

After all, it was a survival show. He couldn't shake the thought that the other two had put on excellent performances, yet were in danger of being eliminated.

Nevertheless, the fact that neither of them seemed bothered made them even more trustworthy!

He nodded again.

– *It's okay, don't worry about it and just practice.*

– *Kim Raebin, you're doing great!*

So, Kim Raebin was waiting for the broadcast of Episode 1 of ⟨Wise⟩ with excitement, even though he wouldn't be appearing in it yet.

This was because he wanted to see the performance of the hyung who'd helped him.

"Hmm."
