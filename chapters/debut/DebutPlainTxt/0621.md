# Chapter 621

Ryu Chungwoo looked at the internet for the first time in a long while. A few days had passed since Park Moondae caught him obsessively monitoring comments when he was supposed to be resting.

The reason was not good. His private trip to an archery range had been leaked and posted online.

However.

[Is that really a target? Not a hedgehog?]

└ㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋ

[This is K-Archery, where national team = gold medal.]

[No, Ryu Chungwoo is seriously insane. Hasn't he been on a break for almost 10 years? Is he even human?]

It was just as Park Moondae had said. The comments were full of exclamations that "wouldn't cause any problems." Instead of comments like, 'He's a former national athlete, why is he like this,' there were only expressions of pure awe and disbelief.

[Crazy, he's so damn goodㅋㅋㅋㅋ]

He felt a strange feeling. The space, once filled with comments like 'Just get some rest' or 'Don't force it, you should quit,' was now packed with pure admiration. Ryu Chungwoo briefly closed and opened his eyes.

A thought suddenly occurred to him.

*This is what it felt like.*

Originally, he had always lived amidst such reactions. He never placed great meaning on it or felt particularly proud, but the familiar environment brought back the feeling of those times.

[Ryu Chungwoo doing Ryu Chungwoo things.]

He remembered himself before all this happened. He remembered his everyday attitude and biological rhythm from back then.

That was why he answered so naturally when Park Moondae suggested, 'Want to try slowly participating in the schedule?'

[Should I?]

And now, Ryu Chungwoo was truly participating in TeSTAR's stage schedule as normal.

"We're getting out!"

The van, moving since dawn, arrived at its destination. They hurriedly moved to the studio, which was already fully prepared.

"We'll start filming now~"

"Yes!"

Today's first schedule was a famous talk show on WeTube. No member paid special attention to Ryu Chungwoo, asking careful questions or looking after him. Instead, they gave him the usual duties and rights of the group's Leader.

"Uh, Chungwoo, should we... go over there to greet them?"

"Ah, it's fine. We don't have to."

"Okay!"

Ryu Chungwoo handled things reflexively. Things that felt awkward in his head now unfolded naturally in the moment.

And then, the stage.

"I'm sorry, but we don't have many cameras... To upload individual fancams, I think we'll have to film at least three or four times," the stage filming staff said with a grimace. "Is that okay? Ah, I'm so sorry..."

"Hey, what's there to be sorry about~"

"Things like this can happen!"

This time, too, no member said anything like, 'Hyung, if filming again so many times is too much for you, tell us now...'

They just said this.

"Hyung, are we going with this?"

Then all he had to do was answer.

"Yeah."

The performance would begin, just like always. Then he, too, just had to move.

*Ah.*

[Bad feeling!

Take it with you]

That day, Ryu Chungwoo completed the performance without any issues.

"Cool!"

"Ah~ We're looking pretty good today, right? Hyung, look at this!"

Ryu Chungwoo caught his breath and watched the recorded camera data. Dressed in a neat, checkered suit, the Ryu Chungwoo on screen danced and sang passionately.

[Umm- um, Dduru- du

Even when the sun sets and the moonlight descends

Unforgettable feelings]

He did not notice anything particularly awkward, nor did he feel his skills were lacking.

*...I'm doing well.*

Was this what it felt like?

"......"

"Hyung, that last take came out great!"

In the end, Ryu Chungwoo quietly nodded, but to be honest, it was not always a success. Just because he tried did not mean everything was magically resolved.

"......"

"Director, can we go one more time!"

Even after that, whenever Ryu Chungwoo performed about five or six times, his left arm would either completely miss the choreography or react with a forced delay. It was not as intense as freezing on stage, but the shock was at least half as strong.

However, the members' reactions were different from before.

"Some days are just like that."

They did not force themselves to act okay, nor did they rush over in a panic. They also did not try too hard to seem unfazed.

"We have time, want to take a 10-minute break before we continue?"

They were considerate on a realistic level.

"Hyung, if we run out of stamina, you have to buy us beef, for real."

They even whined about being tired. He was actually grateful for that.

"That's not a problem."

"Ooooh!"

On the first day the filming was delayed, the members truly ganged up on him and made him treat them to beef.

[Beef bought by Chungwoo... Everyone was conscientious and restrained themselves, ordering only 20 servings (video)]

└His food-posting game is insane

└You guysㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋ

└Since when was 'conscientious' 3 servings per person

His experience was being repainted in a brighter, lighter way.

Of course, before he went to sleep, the memory of the day's failure would relentlessly consume his thoughts and senses.

*What if I can't recover any more than this, no matter what I do.*

That assumption alone made him more conscious of his arm. Ryu Chungwoo still did not want to talk to the members about such things.

However, whenever a live broadcast was scheduled, Ryu Chungwoo, who could not participate, would go to counseling instead and talk about what had happened.

"If I keep stopping once every five or six times like this..."

"Hmm... I see."

Then he heard something surprising.

"But Chungwoo, how about we try reducing your medication?"

"Pardon?"

The counselor suggested switching to a weaker prescription, even though Ryu Chungwoo felt his symptoms were still present.

"I... thought you would recommend increasing it, actually."

"Of course, just as you take cold medicine when you have a cold, it's natural to take medicine that helps when your heart is hurting," the counselor said gently. "But from what I can see, Chungwoo, you're recovering very quickly."

"......"

Ryu Chungwoo had escaped the numb depression of his daily life and was no longer in a state of panic from irrational anxiety.

The counselor explained kindly, then reminded him.

"Chungwoo, you're currently worrying about your work within a very realistic level."

"......"

He was recovering well.

"In that case, at this point, a different feeling might be more important."

"What kind of..."

"I can do this."

The counselor stated firmly.

"It's self-efficacy. Chungwoo, you're someone who originally had much stronger self-confidence than the average person."

"...I am?"

"Of course."

At that moment, a sentence flashed through his mind.

[Because you're someone who's always been good at what you do, hyung.]

They were words he had heard from Park Moondae.

"You believed you could do anything if you tried, and look. A tendency to not fear risks, the pursuit of distant goals, a challenging nature..."

"......"

"In fact, you also have the self-confidence that you wouldn't lose in most environments."

Ryu Chungwoo listened to those words in a slightly dazed state.

*Was I like that?*

He had never deeply reflected on 'who am I' to discover some 'ultimate self'. He had never felt the need or interest to do so. If Bae Sejin had heard, he might have been shocked, thinking,*There are people who have no interest in that at all...?*, but people's dispositions are naturally diverse.

Besides, Ryu Chungwoo had not completely escaped his fragmented self-doubt, resignation, or anxiety. After all, no one had even suggested he do a live broadcast performance yet.

"Hyung, again!"

"Alright."

After finishing a private pre-recording, he would unconsciously check the fans' reactions again, wondering if they still wished he would just rest.

[I miss the kids...]

[Does it make sense for the pre-recording to be private because of that bastard? Fuck]

[What's the point of a promotion period if we can't see them in person? Might as well just qui-]

"Hand over your smartphone."

"......"

Ryu Chungwoo obediently placed his smartphone on Park Moondae's palm, but he could not help but add one more thing.

"Then you shouldn't look either, hyung."

"I won't."

A lie. There was no way a person like him would not look when the situation was taking such an unexpected turn.

*I bet he'll look by himself, thinking, 'This much should be fine'.*

Ryu Chungwoo wanted to lecture Park Moondae instead, but for some reason, he could not think of the right way to do it.

*...How did I used to do it?*

He was sure he used to speak quite plausibly and firmly.

"......"

He definitely seemed better than his lowest point, yet he felt ambiguous and unsatisfied about where he stood. It felt as if he had passed his prime and degenerated, with no hope of recovery.

"......"

He ran his tongue over the slightly bitter taste in his mouth and got up. Let's go to rehab.

While Ryu Chungwoo was spending a less-than-perfect week like that, a piece of news that TeSTAR had also been waiting for began to be widely reported.

"It's out."

It was VTIC's staggering first-week results.
