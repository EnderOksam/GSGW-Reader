# Chapter 529

After Park Moondae switched bodies back.

"Of course TeSTAR will do well this time! I'll be cheering for you~"

"Thank you."

I sat in the salon TeSTAR had used since our T1 days. The director, whom I had known for a long time, gave my hair color a final check. Congratulations on the comeback accompanied her work.

She also offered some subtle probing disguised as blessings.

"Which shows are you appearing on this time? Are you going on that WeTube show where you eat?"

"If the company books it for us."

"Aigoo, right. What's important is whether the company schedules it for you, isn't it?"

*It seems she's changing the subject since she can't dig anything out of me.*

I chuckled inwardly.

"Promotions have become so important these days, right?"

"That's right."

That was why we were focusing on it so intensely. I reviewed the plan, recalling a conversation from a few weeks ago.

[I think we should be a bit more aggressive with this comeback's promotion.]

When one hears 'an idol's aggressive comeback promotion', what method comes to mind?

Is it a massive promotion on the scale of hundreds of millions or billions of won, funded by a huge investment? Or perhaps appearing on every variety show imaginable to imprint the news of TeSTAR's comeback onto the public?

*Neither of those is wrong.*

However, neither applied to the current TeSTAR. A promotion funded by a huge investment was now a mundane, everyday occurrence, so it was nothing special. Appearing on every show without a second thought would only accelerate the consumption of our image, so there was no need to do it.

This was a common characteristic of top-tier idol groups that were well-established in the country.

So, what was the 'aggressive comeback promotion' that TeSTAR was trying to push now?

[Let's not play it safe. Let's go for something risky.]

It meant we were willing to accept controversy and risk. We would be more blatant in pursuing our goal.

*And right now, the goal is the Order of Cultural Merit.*

Yes. I would be blunt. To receive the Order of Cultural Merit, who did we need to impress?

*The higher-ups.*

The political sphere.

However, TeSTAR's global standing was nothing special, especially in the West, which the higher-ups favored. We couldn't even hold a candle to VTIC, the previous recipients of the Order of Cultural Merit.

*Thanks to those bastards, the bar has been raised ridiculously high.*

All TeSTAR had were fragmented pieces of recognition from a cameo in a Limestone movie, a game collaboration, and a Netplus drama.

*All of it is from other content, not our own albums or music.*

This meant the fleeting buzz had barely translated into popularity for the group itself. The overwhelming majority of people who enjoyed that content probably did not know who TeSTAR was.

However, to put it another way, it also meant that TeSTAR had not yet formed a representative global image.

*It means we can plan and aim for one.*

Just as latecomers in a game use a guide to create the most efficient route, TeSTAR could also aim for a desired image.

And TeSTAR was a group that had already successfully completed such a strategy once, targeting the domestic market. This meant we had a foundation to build on, not starting from scratch, because we had done it before.

*If only we can pull these fragmented pieces of recognition together and connect them into one whole.*

Then, from that point on, we could get people to consume the content TeSTAR had steadily built up over the past few years. The content had already been proven to be entertaining.

"That's what it means to establish a position."

That was the very goal TeSTAR had to achieve with this promotion.

*First things first.*

[TeSTAR, First Nomination for 'American Music Awards']

After my hair was done, I looked at the now-outdated article and let out a small laugh.

*Alright, let's start by making good use of this.*

~~~

The American Music Awards, also known as the AMA, was one of the three major, prestigious music awards ceremonies in the United States.

[Congrats to TeSTAR on the AMA nom ㅊㅋㅊㅋ]

[Oh, good]

However, the article Park Moondae was looking at had not caused much of a stir when it was published. That was because the 'American Music Awards' had newly established a K-Pop category. Some people sneered at this, treating it as 'a league of their own'.

[There are three nominees this year, right? Hope K-Pop gets even bigger~]

[ㅋㅋㅋIt's an award they give to K-Pop to keep them separate and tell them to get lost, and you like this? What's with the bootlicking?]

└Did this guy get beat up somewhere before coming here?

Besides, it was no longer a rare occurrence for a K-Pop artist to be nominated for a global award.

[Doesn't count unless it's a Grammy ㅋㅋ]

[How many awards shows are there? Stop making a fuss over things like the Top Social category ㄴㄴ]

Since K-Pop artists had high album sales and social media mentions, three or four teams would always make it onto the lists of various famous awards ceremonies each year. This was because America, the headquarters of capitalism, would never miss out on a profitable venture.

Even if it was not for a major award, they would give a mention, making good use of the buzz generated by K-Pop fandoms. As a result, hardcore K-Pop listeners were becoming jaded about the situation.

[For real, it's not like one or two teams get nominated. It's meaningless unless they win an award like Best Group or something ㅇㅇ]

└I agree ㅋㅋㅋ

[Isn't TeSTAR a bit weak overseas? Are they still maintaining their class in Japan?]

└Seems like they're about to be overtaken by Aetherㅋ

└What a troll, they're still top-tier.

This nomination news was also expected to be a bland topic that would end with TeSTAR fans reacting and slightly increasing the mention count.

[Couldn't they possibly appear as performers?]

└What AMA? Syeombyeol kicked T1 to the curb, get a grip ㅋㅋ

The possibility that TeSTAR could have an amazing comeback stage at this awards ceremony? Most people had never even considered it. In terms of local recognition, company size, and connections, there was no reason to choose TeSTAR as a performer.

Furthermore, almost no K-Pop idol group had succeeded in gaining fame as a performer there. Except for the very first one.

[No group has gone viral from an AMA performance except VTIC. This settles the hierarchy, right?]

[ㅋㅋYou think VTIC's stock price held up during their military hiatus for no reason?]

[AhㅋㅋㅋA one-year hiatus is nothing in the land of the free, they said everyone will wait for them.]

VTIC, at the beginning of its peak, had managed to build a solid global fanbase. In the years since, almost no K-Pop group had seen any significant benefit as a performer at an overseas awards ceremony.

[Even if they do perform, if the reaction is bad, it's an instant path to failure.]

└Isn't TeSTAR good on stage?

└Yeah, but K-Pop is still a niche~ It's weeb subculture ㅋㅋ

└The person who wrote this comment is uncultured, but the general direction is correct. K-Pop is still a niche in America.

But it also means it is a market with the potential for entertainment company profits to grow dozens of times over.

└Did a stock market junkie show up?

And to go viral, it was not enough to just perform well. The local reaction had to be good too, which was an extremely tricky task given the different cultural sentiments. For that one day, the song, outfits, and choreography had to somehow align with their sensibilities.

And all the while, they had to maintain a delicate balance so as not to disappoint existing K-Pop fans.

[Just look forward to what game TeSTAR will release this time, that's more realistic ㅋㅋㅋ cya]

This was practically the common conclusion among those who were interested.

The same was true for this idol, who had heard some inside information from a manager thanks to being in the same agency as TeSTAR.

"M-Minha! Are the TeSTAR sunbaenims appearing on the AMA?"

Mirynay's main dancer, Seong Harin, ran in shock to the group's de facto Leader and received this answer.

"Uh, that... probably."

"......"

A confirmation! The shocked Seong Harin had a question pop into her head before she could grind her teeth at the thought of TeSTAR finally aiming for America.

*How?*

They had not signed with a major American record label, and the company was not insanely rich, was it? For that matter, it was not as if TeSTAR's last album was a huge hit in the US.

She wanted to bombard her with those questions, but Seong Harin held back. The face of the Leader, Park Minha, standing before her, looked as if she had reached a state of enlightenment.

"Haha, haha... Um, actually, I... don't really know either, and, it's confidential."

"Ah, ahh! Of course, I'll be careful!"

"......"

Park Minha nodded as if she naturally trusted her. Then she added.

"And let's not mention it to Yulgi either..."

"...Okay."

A moment of solemn silence flowed between the two. The first-place winner of ❬Idol Inc.❭, Jeong Yulgi, was the undisputed face of Mirynay.

*She's a good unni, but...*

She was... too innocent. It was to the point where they worried she might cause an unnecessary misunderstanding. Thanks to her unique character, she had not been heavily criticized yet, but they were anxious that it might happen.

*...Come to think of it, there's a similar sunbae in TeSTAR, isn't there?*

Seong Harin thought of an idol whose signature was his red hair. Cha Eugene!

She wanted to ask that sunbae for advice on how he managed to appeal with his character for so many years while masterfully avoiding controversy. Did he not handle the few rumors and problems that arose with perfect timing?

As expected, an idol who won a grand prize is different...

*...No. It's because Yulgi is a purer and better person!*

Mirynay's Seong Harin quickly broke free from her idolization of TeSTAR! Shaking her head, she calmly analyzed the current situation again.

"Then, are the TeSTAR sunbaenims seriously aiming for an American debut?"

"That was the feeling in the company meeting."

Hmm. Seong Harin rubbed her chin. In Mirynay's early debut days, they had also strongly aimed for an American debut, pushed by a department head who had a case of America-fever.

She had studied foreign languages hard back then and became conversational, but the group's results were not that great...

She thought with a slight sense of dejection.

*...As expected, localization strategies aren't that great.*

Desperation can sometimes come across as servility. If anything, it felt like they had lost quite a few domestic fans back then, so just thinking about their early debut days left a bitter taste in Seong Harin's mouth.

*They're popular singers in our country, so it would be nice if they could advance while maintaining that position...*

Seong Harin had mixed feelings, but she soon let out a deep sigh. Still... she really hoped that the TeSTAR sunbaenims' attempt would go well. It was because they were all in the same boat.

The company needed to have enough spare cash for investments to come Mirynay's way, after all.

*But please don't put any weird ideas into the company's head...*

Seong Harin did not want to send her precious Mirynay back to America!

"...I hope it goes well for them."

"Right."

And so, the conversation between the two Mirynay members concluded.

While occasionally suffering from anxiety, Seong Harin diligently handled her schedules. In her spare time, she examined TeSTAR's comeback teaser content with a hawk's eye.

Then, one day.

[This Year's AMA Performers]

*Finally!*

Seong Harin found the news she had been waiting for. Swallowing hard, she clicked on it and was bewildered.

"Huh?"

TeSTAR's name was nowhere to be found among the announced performers! No, there were no K-Pop artists at all.

*What, I thought they were performing?*

However, there was not even a single related article.

*Was it just a rumor? Or maybe they reviewed it and canceled.*

Seong Harin decided to think that for now and turned off the screen.

However, a short while later, Park Moondae was attending the American Music Awards to perform.

"......"

He checked his outfit. It was a white cotton t-shirt, jeans, and minimal accessories around his face. This was not a stage outfit an idol who had just made a comeback would choose.

However, Park Moondae was satisfied.

*Good.*

In the first place, he was not standing here to perform TeSTAR's comeback stage. This was the result of choice and focus.

*The only thing I want is impact!*

I will grab as much attention as possible under the given realistic conditions. This was the stage chosen based on that standard.

[The conventional way, just promoting our album as a masterpiece, won't work.]

So he decided to cover another artist's song at this awards ceremony. It was a tribute stage for a famous artist from the past.

He was not even doing it alone. It was a format where several people would split parts and sing one song in succession. This was practically shabby treatment, on the level of 'inviting' someone with no recognition.

Park Moondae's attitude towards it was simple.

*I actually prefer it.*

He was pleased.

*The conditions are perfect.*

If I have no local recognition, I can just borrow it by using someone else's famous song.

*Stats don't lie.*

[Vocal: S+]

If several people are singing in parts, it is better because there is a pool of candidates to compare me against.

Park Moondae grinned and raised the microphone.

The performance was starting.
