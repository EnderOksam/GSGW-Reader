# Chapter 611

"Hey, hey, isn't the first episode of that thing out today?"

The Kim Raebin fan in the living room ignored her younger brother's words.

Her brother casually walked past, opened the front door for no reason, and then returned to ask again.

"I asked if it's out."

"Argh, seriously! I said it is!"

What was? The first episode of TeStar's Bizarre Office.

Her brother grumbled, "Why's she suddenly freaking out," but sat on the sofa anyway. He had clearly known but asked because his pride wouldn't let him look it up himself. The Kim Raebin fan, used to this, simply focused on a more serious matter.

A behind-the-scenes video of TeSTAR's new music video had appeared on her SNS.

The choreography shoot must have been long. The footage showed them wiping away sweat, removing their suit jackets, and grinning or making V-signs for the camera.

Naturally, her eyes fixed on one place.

Kim Raebin! Her favorite wore a black suit with a matching black shirt. He had no tie but wore glasses.

[LMAO, you crazy bastard]

[A tie pin piercing with a black suit? Kim Raebin really knows what's up]

[My baby looks like a delinquent even in a suit!!!]

Looking at the posts on SNS, the reactions from other members' fans were no different.

*Crazy bastards.*

It was blatant every time something new was released.

*TeSTAR really... truly sharpened their swords and managed their appearances for this promotional period.*

*For what? To wear suits, of course.*

The timing was perfect for their age. The impact of custom-tailored suits, fitting each member's body perfectly without being over the top, was staggering.

*I thought my timeline was going to be paralyzed.*

The speed at which GIFs for each member appeared, all with different color corrections, was frighteningly fast.

Releasing such a deliberately targeted music video was a huge success. It made both fans and the public lose their minds. All the fans were cheering.

But they were using it to tease their own original content?

[Throw away the otaku crap, you crazy bastards]

She swallowed hard, recalling her private account's dizzying timeline.

*Hey... you can't get swallowed by the lore, okay?*

She felt anxious. She worried that TeSTAR, conscious of the massive praise from the game in their last album, had gone overboard with the lore this time.

She tore at the sofa.

*Do you think your title track is some kind of OST!*

Of course, some fans with matching tastes were thrilled, but a few fans who had cheered for the clean suit music video were in despair.

[Ugh, fuck, that damn otaku lore again...]

[They should have just stuck to the music video...ㅠㅠ]

[I'll just watch the performances, guys]

Kim Raebin's fan took a deep breath.

*Let's not be too pessimistic.*

*It'll probably turn around again.*

How many times had TeSTAR done this? They would slam down an unpredictable solution and content that completely wiped away all anxiety.

And the thrill and relief she felt then!

*They must have a plan this time too, right!?*

It was a kind of learned response. As proof, quite a few people below the surface felt like her.

[I believe in Gloomy Puppy's winning streak, fuck]

[Going all in]

[First of all, a Kim Raebin song never fails. It's always at least average. Didn't you learn anything from watching Song of the Stars?]

The last comment was true.

And how were TeSTAR's current music chart rankings?

[Bad feelings? / TeSTAR 1st]

A perfect, clean daily number one.

*I thought my heart was going to explode.*

Thanks to that, the scene was heating up even more.

[TeSTAR's new song debuts at #1]

[First results of the May comeback clash are out.jpg]

[The debut chart graph is insane]

This was due to the anticipation built by 'EPIC', the surprise fan song TeSTAR released at the new year, which became a huge public hit. It was also thanks to the buzz from releasing at the same time as VTIC.

It meant TeSTAR had taken a proper step up without collapsing.

But it couldn't end here.

"Maintaining it is what's important."

"What is?"

Kim Raebin's fan ignored her brother's question and scanned the communities with bloodshot eyes.

*We can't get pushed back when VTIC comes out.*

VTIC's comeback was just a few days away. This battle would be decided by how much TeSTAR was pushed back then. If they were pushed back a lot, it would be the end.

So, releasing major original content this week, when those bastards had their comeback, was fine.

But a fan service video this dedicated to the lore? That was actually a problem.

*The casuals will all run away!*

It was an even bigger problem because this original content had drawn attention as a pre-release. From her years as a Loviewer, she could tell public interest had reached even this lore-heavy video that looked niche to anyone.

That was why she couldn't feel at ease, even knowing TeSTAR's successful track record.

*Ah, casuals, fuck, just don't watch it.*

Kim Raebin's fan remembered a related video title she recently saw on WeTube.

[TeStar's Bizarre Office, what genre of drama is it? An analysis based on the music video!]

Even YouTubers jumping the gun had latched on.

*Ha.*

At least with the game included in the album, only fans or people who liked that sort of thing played it.

"Oh, please."

*Don't let this hype die down. Don't let it die down!*

She gritted her teeth amidst hope and anxiety. When the release time came, she played the first episode of TeSTAR's original content, ❬TeStar's Bizarre Office❭, on the TV.

And the first episode she watched was...

"...!"

Unexpected.

[???]

[No, fuck, what is this?]

[Is that a real ghost? Isn't it CG?;;]

[What is this? What is this?]

It was a mystery story taken seriously. The horror direction completely removed any humor. It used scene direction like a drama or movie, exquisitely highlighting the contrast with a skit.

Thanks to that, the shock and immersion were even more intense.

What shocked people even more was the video's production quality.

[I almost fainted when the music box came out]

[What did they do to an idol's original content?]

[How much money did they spend? No, but this doesn't seem like a quality you can achieve just by spending your own money... There's no PPL either, how did they do it??]

The quality of the props and the strange creature was on the level of a drama Netplus had funded heavily. The set looked like a real office, and the creature's movements were realistic, as if it were a real situation.

Moreover, the show didn't emphasize words or nuances like 'a world of their own that you can only understand if you know all the previous content'.

It was just a mystery thriller drama's introduction. There was no entry barrier beyond the genre itself.

TeSTAR's serious, thriller-like reactions, devoid of any playfulness, further fueled that atmosphere.

[To say it's CG, the reactions of the non-acting members are... no;;]

[What is this, looks like the members didn't know either??]

[It's like a drama based on a webtoon]

DRRT, DRRT, DRRT!

Episode 1 ended at the point where Kim Raebin disappeared.

[If episode 2 hadn't been released at the same time, I would have fainted right now]

"Hey."
