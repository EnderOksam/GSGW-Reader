# Chapter 422

You have to row when the tide comes in.

This formula applied to every field when making money, but it was especially true in the entertainment industry. The same content could either blow up or flop depending on the timing and trends.

A flood tide of variety show popularity was pouring in, and yet, the company wanted to postpone an album our subsidiary label had planned and prepared all on its own?

"Trends? They're important. However, TeSTAR is past the level of desperately needing a moment of buzz. They are, by name and in reality, a top idol group."

"......"

"Hmm, now that I think about it, I heard the previous director caused a huge mess by unreasonably demanding that TeSTAR self-produce their debut album in just one month."

"Yes."

*And it became a massive hit, you bastard.*

I stared at the company director, who spoke with a slick face. It was interesting to see some new guy, who replaced the department head we screwed over, suddenly call us in to run his mouth.

"Back then, it was important to debut quickly to capture the broadcast's impact, but now, TeSTAR is a brand in and of itself, isn't it?"

Yeah.

"So, I was thinking it would be a move more befitting TeSTAR's status to pay more attention to every single detail and elevate the album's quality."

The director advised this in an intellectual tone.

Kim Raebin immediately raised his hand. "However, the current plan isn't excessively rushed compared to what we've done before."

The director just shrugged at the workaholic's remark. He seemed to have anticipated this.

"But if you have enough time, you can discover new points for improvement and develop them further, right?"

"Um... That is true!"

The reasonable statement immediately convinced Kim Raebin. Cha Eugene almost shot Kim Raebin a rare, dead-fish-eyed glare.

Having overcome one hurdle, the director continued in a much calmer, more polite tone.

"Of course, if our TeSTAR members insist on releasing the album now, the company will support you with all our might."

*Is that so?*

"It's just an opinion that this other way might be a better fit."

"Is that your judgment, Director?"

"Hmm, rather than that, it's actually something that came out of a discussion at a company executive meeting. The CEO himself also agreed."

*Oh. Passing the buck.*

Since the last department head had been dealt with when our label went independent, this guy did not talk down to us. He acted like a polite business partner persuading an investor.

"After all, the members of TeSTAR are this company's most symbolic and highest-grossing artists. This is something we're saying after a great deal of consideration."

"Thank you."

"No need for thanks. It's what an agency should naturally do."

He threw in some pleasantries before seriously presenting the new plan.

"Of course, I'm not saying we should throw away the buzz from the variety shows. A proposal came in to broadcast TeSTAR's concert tour live on a public network, and we're thinking of proceeding with that."

The director, who seemed to have come from the entertainment side, explained the plan quite plausibly. The members listened seriously. The atmosphere was not bad.

Ryu Chungwoo spoke up. "First of all, thank you very much for the good words. Would it be okay if we talked it over a bit more among the members?"

"Of course."

The director readily let us go, as if telling us to make our decision comfortably.

So, after returning to the dorm, the atmosphere became like this.

"...I don't think this was something they needed to call us in so urgently for."

"It is a grateful thing that they are seriously considering the quality of the new album, but I believe we have been receiving sufficient support for our work recently!"

One guy muttered suspiciously while another took it in good faith. Both wondered why a high-ranking executive had bothered to summon them.

However, the one who had grasped the situation was smirking bitterly beside them.

"Ah~ So they're starting to feel like they don't want to give us as much, is that it?"

"......"

"The album. Releasing that is all an investment, you know."

Right, this was the core of the agency's story.

[We're not giving you an album.]

Bae Sejin's eyebrows shot up at Keun Sejin's nonchalant words.

"...This company has a lot of money, though."

"Hmm, well, it's not necessarily about money... Things like good personnel or promotion ideas are limited, aren't they? It's like that~"

Just as the question mark on Bae Sejin's face was about to turn into an exclamation point, I laid out the conclusion.

"It's more profitable for the company to just have us do tours and invest in raising the status of groups with a lot of time left on their contracts."

"......!!"

His eyes were about to pop out. Looking again, it was not just Bae Sejin. Kim Raebin and Seon Ahyeon had similar expressions.

"Then if this continues...."

"Yes."

This was the reality for a top-tier idol group with little time left on their contract.

"From now on, instead of new albums, we'll start heading in a direction that maximizes the spending power of the fans we've gathered."

"...!"

"An album... maybe they'll give us one early next year after seeing the nuance of our contract renewal."

It was a relief we did not have an album contract in Japan. Otherwise, they would have just worked us over there.

The color drained from Bae Sejin's face.

"...I don't get it. We even set up a label... and if we re-sign, we'll stay, so why are they doing this? No, what if we said we'd re-sign right now?"

That would not work. It would create a flaw in the contract negotiations. The side that bows its head first always loses out.

Besides, if the conditions were good, that would be a problem in itself. Keun Sejin shook his head and opened his mouth.

"Hey, if we re-sign, the profit-sharing ratio usually gets adjusted to give the artist more~ From the company's perspective, that means... the return on investment gets lower!"

"......"

Exactly. The better the artist's share, the more the company loses, and there was an even more realistic problem.

"Even excluding that, I think we'd still be pushed back on album investments."

"......"

I admitted the painful reality.

"We have to go to the military, too."

"......"

I had told them VTIC's situation was not some distant future event. We debuted older than them, after all. Still, we could compete for a few more years.

I waited for the person who would have to go first to digest the shock before continuing.

"And from a company-growing perspective, investing in a rising group always looks more attractive than an already established one."

"...Really?"

"Yes. Shareholders like to see promising new ventures. The higher-ups like it too because it makes them feel competent."

The board of directors would be even more inclined to think that way, especially after being hyped up by the current business-obsessed department head.

On top of that, we even signed a contract telling them to stop meddling as long as we made them money. The moment a junior group gets popular, TeSTAR becomes a cash cow.

The conclusion was simple.

[From a business perspective, they want to use the money they've made from TeSTAR to raise a new group now!]

"Hoooo..."

"Hey, hyung, take a deep breath."

Bae Sejin's face turned red and then pale before he finally calmed down.

I guess I should deliver some good news too.

"Still, hyung, it looks like you'll finally be able to start working on acting in earnest."

They would gently disperse our activities into individual ones instead of an album. I raised both fists in a cheer.

"Fighting."

"I've already done plenty of that......!"

Bae Sejin muttered about playing a psychopath four times in a row and sank gloomily into the sofa. It seemed his acting experience from the System's fake world had not gone anywhere.

Ryu Chungwoo smiled, looking a bit troubled, and made a suggestion. "Still, they said they wouldn't stop us from releasing an album, so should we prepare for it?"

"They won't stop us, and they'll act like they're going to release it, but the schedule will be continuously pushed back and postponed due to the company's 'sudden circumstances'."

"......"

The sub-label could plan independently, but in the end, the parent company set the release schedule.

*There are plenty of excuses.*

Like overlapping with the company's other artists, or the timing being bad... Then, if we were lucky, we would barely release one before contract renewal, and even that would have weak promotion. Wasn't that the typical route?

"Well, I actually expected this much."

Given the season, I had predicted this development the moment I heard they wanted to postpone the album, but there was one more thing.

"They're not telling us the whole story."

I let out a smirk.

Kim Raebin, who had been staring blankly as if denying reality, asked in disbelief, "There's... more?"

"......"

I brought my laptop from my room. A few members followed me like ducklings. I pulled up a file on the screen and opened it.

"This is our company, T1 Stars', plan for the fourth quarter of this year."

"Hey, Moondae Moondae, where do you get this stuff?"

"It was announced at the shareholders' meeting."

Where else? You just need the contact info of an employee with access to the company's document archives. Anyway.

"If you look at the monthly plan there... yes, October."

Right in the slot where we were originally supposed to release our album.

"......"

"......"

I nodded.

"It's Spacer."

[Mid-October - Spacer 1st Full Album Release / Goal: Increase in overseas sales volume]

That was right. They had blocked TeSTAR to make way for Spacer's existing album release plan, and that was not the end of it.

"So, this is how the picture connects...."

I said, dusting my hands off the mouse.

"You remember how they scheduled a public broadcast of our concert instead of an album comeback, right?"

"Uh-huh."

"They'll definitely sing a different tune once that materializes."

Something like, broadcasting the entire concert is difficult considering copyright and VOD sales, so let's just do a few stages, and let's fill the empty time with another group instead.

For instance, this agency's new boy group.

Spacer.

"They'll expand our concert broadcast into a part two of the 'agency special' to insert them, and then they'll make their comeback with that buzz."

"......"

In short, it was a bundle deal. It was the start of a generational shift, hoping our fans would switch to Spacer during our hiatus.

At that moment, a recovered Bae Sejin shouted. "W-Wait a minute, Park Moondae, didn't you say last time that the company wouldn't try a generational shift or anything! Because we're going to re-sign!"

"Yes, I thought so, but they're doing it."

"......"

*I thought that since we had built a good, friendly relationship, they wouldn't try something like this. They wouldn't want to lose the goose that lays golden eggs.*

It seemed their patience was wearing thin since we became an independent label they could not interfere with. Their dissatisfaction was piling up.

*These bastards dare to pull a stunt to get fans to transfer.*

And so blatantly, too.

"Boooo!"

"...Isn't this a violation of the principle of good faith? I think we can challenge it, maybe pressure them somehow......"

"Hmm. I think that would be difficult."

It seemed Bae Sejin's options had broadened after making an acting agency CEO beg on his knees. However, it would be hard to start a dispute with the company over this.

"In the first place, this isn't a fight against the agency."

"...Then?"

"They're just releasing our album late. We've been pushed down the priority list."

This was a natural course of action for any agency trying to raise a new group. It was not that this particular agency was especially malicious.

"......"

Bae Sejin sat back down on the sofa with a sunken face. Others added their own bitter remarks with wry smiles.

"This is difficult."

"I know, right."

"...I never thought we'd be having these kinds of worries after coming back from such a supernatural experience."

"Even heroes who save the world have their own issues. We just have to win again!" Cha Eugene shrugged.

It could be disillusioning that after working our asses off to succeed, we were swarmed by bastards trying to use us, but wasn't that just how capitalism worked?

The atmosphere lightened a bit at the clean remark, fitting for someone from the headquarters of capitalism itself.

"Right, there'll be... another way."

"That's right, we have Moondae."

"...?"

Anyone would think I had a dokkaebi's magic club or something.

Of course, I had no intention of just sitting back and releasing a new album next year. Improving the album quality was not bad, but that was not their goal, was it? They were just planning to suck us dry by harvesting our existing fans.

*Do these bastards think they made us big just because they funded us?*

I was inwardly clicking my tongue when it happened.

"Um."

"Hmm?"

Seon Ahyeon raised his hand and spoke quite firmly. "Shouldn't we, um, just do better than the Spacer members?"

"...!"

"Ooh."

"If... if we do better at the live concert broadcast and show them an amazing stage. Then. The company people might change their minds. Because it won't work...!"

It was an aggressive statement for Seon Ahyeon, but it was the standard approach. It was also a method we had frequently used. A head-on battle, crushing them regardless of rank.

However, this case was a little different.

"Spacer will still benefit from it."

The reason was simple.

*We're not in the same weight class to begin with.*

Us against them? It was a given that we would do better. This was a situation where we would only break even by being overwhelmingly superior.

Conversely, since we were from the same agency, they could even gain a halo effect as 'the direct juniors of such a talented group' and capture a different kind of demand.

"......"

"Oh, I see. I'm sorry...."

"Hey, no~ It was still a cool and refreshing idea."

"That's right! It was cool!"

"......"

While Seon Ahyeon was being encouraged, Ryu Chungwoo asked the members directly. "So, do you want to refuse the concert broadcast altogether?"

"Hmm."

Perhaps because they were angry, a surprising number of members considered such a drastic option. However, not everyone felt that way.

"Wait a minute! I think our thoughts are leaning too much in one direction right now. Why don't we each think about it a little more and then talk? We can just keep preparing the album in the meantime~"

"Ah, right."

Keun Sejin cut in at the right time. When our eyes met, he gave a slight shake of his head as if to say there was no other way, but his words worked.

The meeting came to a temporary halt.

"Let's take a break."

"Yep."

The members left their seats, each with a look of deep thought.

"Hmm."

I remained seated, propped my chin on my hand, and then made a call. There was, in fact, a perfectly clean method to resolve this without any further conflict.

*Eliminate the alternative.*

Meaning, get rid of Spacer. A way to erase their options so they had no choice but to invest only in TeSTAR.

[.......]

"......"

[...H-Hyung. Should I use my wish coupon now? Do I have to!?]

"No. Save it."

I could hear Spacer's Kwon Heeseung let out a long sigh of relief over the speaker. I had called to ask if he knew about the concert broadcast appearance, but he was quick on the uptake.

*...I owe this guy, too.*

Thinking about it, it was better to take a more long-term view. Tnet would continue to produce groups through survival shows, and this agency would snatch them all up. There would be plenty of investment options even without Spacer.

*I need to approach this from a different angle.*

I came to that conclusion.

In the meantime, the agency began to show a schedule that was increasingly in line with my predictions.

"They said they're probably going to add more tour dates."

This was the first step.
