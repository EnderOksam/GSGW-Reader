# Chapter 467

It was natural for members of an established idol group to pursue individual activities. Once a group's peak passed, their profitability declined. Each member needed to explore their own path to make a living.

Of course, many exceptions existed these days, and TeSTAR was one of them.

"We've barely had any proper individual activities until now."

"That's right."

The group had just been doing so well continuously.

*The most we've done for individual activities were variety shows or photoshoots.*

Since we came from a survival show, we had refrained from individual activities for a few years to help the fandom merge. That was how we ended up here.

I spoke up.

"But don't you think it's about time we start?"

"Hmm."

Bae Sejin's shoulders flinched. The members' gazes reflexively turned toward him.

Cha Eugene raised his hand.

"So are we having a meeting? Is Sejin-hyung going to act?"

*This guy's quick on the uptake.*

"...I did, receive an offer."

"...! So that's what it was!"

"Co-congratulations...!"

The kind but clueless members reacted positively first. Amidst their cheers, Bae Sejin's face turned red then pale.

"I-I'm not going to do it...!"

"......"

"I told Park Moondae earlier, but... we just had our comeback. I can't just leave to go act!"

"Ah...!"

Exclamation points appeared on Seon Ahyeon and Kim Raebin's faces.

However, Kim Raebin soon asked cautiously, "Um... is it structurally impossible to do both due to the schedule?"

*I see! Then you can just do both!*

*A question befitting a guy with a crazy efficiency trait that more than doubles his composing speed.*

However, Bae Sejin clenched his fists.

"...What if I make a mistake?"

"Pardon?"

"I'll... be honest. I'm the slowest at learning stage performances here. I started late, and there's probably an issue with... talent, too."

"...W-well, depending on the standard you use, the judgment is subjective..."

"But do you think I can juggle something completely different, a role in a production, and not make a single mistake on stage...?"

"......"

Bae Sejin lifted his head, his face pale.

"If a situation like that happens, what will people think?"

Bae Sejin, being the first to do a solo activity, would get bombarded with criticism. Surprisingly, however, that was not the point he was making.

"They'll think... 'Something must be wrong. For Bae Sejin, who never does this, to suddenly overexert himself by juggling group activities and acting, there must have been some internal discussion within the group'!"

"......"

"I'm not completely clueless about idol activities like I was when I first debuted. I... I understand the situation now, in my own way."

*Right. I know he watches those nationalistic WeTube videos.*

*If you do that, you'll usually get KPOP reaction videos, public reaction WeTube videos, and even gossip channels popping up in your related videos.*

Just reading the titles would have taught him that much. He would have learned about the secondary rumors that other rumors created, and which ones became hot topics that caused ripples.

"In a situation where we've just moved companies and had our first comeback... I can't."

Bae Sejin finished speaking with a rather firm expression.

"That's my judgment."

"......"

*Oh.*

I rested my chin on my hand.

First, regardless of the validity of his conclusion.

*He's drawn a rather logical and in-depth path to his conclusion.*

It was not an attempt to evade. It was a conclusion reached by tackling the situation head-on and analyzing it meticulously.

*He's really grown up.*

It was a respectable attitude, so I planned to start the conversation by lightly agreeing with parts of it, but...

"Why?"

"......"

*This guy is throwing out the question mark first?*

Amidst the wide-eyed members, Ryu Chungwoo continued to speak calmly while gazing at Bae Sejin.

"Sejin-ah. Why do you assume that if you act, it will unconditionally interfere with your stage activities?"

"Who said unconditionally?"

Bae Sejin seemed to choke up, but he quickly calmed down.

"...I'm saying the possibility is high. I can even imagine it right now. That situation...!"

"Imagination doesn't always become reality."

"...!"

"Sejin-ah."

Ryu Chungwoo said softly. His tone, however, had a somewhat strict edge.

"I've said this before... but when you imagine the future, you seem to think the possibility of it going badly is greater than the possibility of it going well."

"......"

"There's no rule that says you have to be right."

Bae Sejin's face went a little blank.

"When we had this kind of talk before, things didn't go the way you worried they would and were resolved well. Remember?"

"......"

It was a silent affirmation.

Ryu Chungwoo was deliberately vague, but I realized what 'the situation when we had this talk before' was. Bae Sejin glanced at Keun Sejin for a moment, confirming it.

*I think he's talking about when those two fought like cats and dogs.*

Back then, I was in charge of talking to Keun Sejin, and Ryu Chungwoo handled Bae Sejin. It seemed the two of them had that kind of conversation at the time.

*Bae Sejin probably said that since Keun Sejin hated his guts, he would never apologize first... and I think he also thought they wouldn't be able to reconcile even if he apologized first.*

It seemed Ryu Chungwoo had gently coaxed him out of it, and well, the roommates reconciled and had been getting along, albeit with some friction. In short, it was true that Bae Sejin had been overly negative at the time.

However, I understood why Bae Sejin had judged things that way.

Their incompatibility and annoying mutual feelings had piled up before their huge fight, which was part of it, but I also wondered if Bae Sejin's way of thinking had an experiential reason.

*...It must be because of his experiences as a child actor.*

He was ruined once by a shitty agency. On top of that, we even watched live as he was almost ruined because his biological father was such a mess. For him to consider the worst-case scenario when faced with a critical choice seemed like a natural instinct.

The problem was...

*And me too...*

*The fact that I can understand him.*

If we were talking about shitty childhood experiences, I had a few that came to mind right away. Situations where the worst-case scenario you never thought would happen becomes reality. Anyone who has been through that will always plan for the future assuming that 'worst-case scenario.'

*It's about being prepared.*

Well, I did not think I had ever lost out because of it, so I had no particular complaints about my own thought process, but it seemed Ryu Chungwoo felt quite sorry for Bae Sejin.

"You've barely made any mistakes on stage throughout our activities. Even when you couldn't sleep properly. So why would you suddenly make a mistake this time?"

"......"

"I don't think you will, and even if you did, there's a chance it would pass without any major issues... To give up for a reason like that, of all things, wouldn't that be too much of a shame?"

Bae Sejin's eyes grew misty.

I felt something close to astonishment toward Ryu Chungwoo.

*How can that guy be like that?*

A guy who had to retire as a national athlete right before his prime due to the aftereffects of a childhood accident he could not even remember. How could he live with that kind of mindset?

Even though we had lived together for years and I knew his whole situation... no, it was actually harder to understand because of that.

*Well, I guess not all humans can be the same.*

It took all sorts of people to make a team work, I supposed. As proof, Bae Sejin seemed emotionally swayed by those firm words.

Riding that momentum, Ryu Chungwoo spoke not to Bae Sejin, but to me.

"What do you think?"

"......"

*Really?*

*He must have thought that since I called the meeting, I'd back him up.*

I answered reluctantly.

"Well... what you said is right, hyung, but honestly, I think what Sejin-hyung is saying is also right. There is a possibility."

"...!"

"...Is that so?"

*Why are you making a gloomy face too, Ryu Chungwoo?*

Anyway, I shrugged.

"And even if we persuade him like this, I think Sejin-hyung will continue to be anxious. I don't think he needs to do his individual activities like that."

"......"

"So, I'd like to refute Sejin-hyung's reasoning entirely."

"......"

"R-refute... you say?"

*That's right.*

"It seems there's a fundamental reason why you're worried about making a mistake in the first place, hyung."

"...A fundamental reason?"

*His expression says, 'What on earth are you talking about?' Listen up.*

"The fact that our promotion period schedule is packed."

Since it was so packed, there was no leeway, no time to prepare one's mind and body for the stage. The worry was that in such an environment, his skills would inevitably lead to a mistake.

"If the schedule had a bit more room in the first place, wouldn't you have said it was fine? Because you'd have time to practice even while doing both."

Bae Sejin answered with a dazed expression.

"Th-that's true, but."

I nodded.

"Then you don't need to worry."

"...?? Why...?"

"Our schedule will be more relaxed than before."

"......"

I clasped my hands together.

"Because all the T1-affiliated schedules are blocked..."

"......"

"......"

*That's right.*

Bae Sejin had unconsciously expected a schedule as intense as our past albums. He had missed this loophole.

*We... don't have many broadcasts to appear on.*

We would do one or two public broadcast variety shows, appear on a few WeTube channels, and that was it. We could not appear on the rest, unintentionally, because they did not match our group's brand value.

*But the game has covered the buzz for us...*

In the end, it was as if we had pulled forward our album promotion activities into the crazy sixty days we spent preparing the album.

As if he realized this, Bae Sejin's eyes became dots. He looked flustered.

"I... need to think a bit."

"Yes, and while you're thinking, I brought another matter to discuss."

"Wh-what?"

*What. I didn't call this meeting just to talk about your acting career.*

"Instead of just Sejin-hyung doing it, I was thinking, how about we also split up and make a few individual appearances each during this promotion period?"

"What??"

"U-us...?"

"Hmm, the album just came out, so for all the members to do individual activities... Of course, anyone who wants to can, but I'm fine with doing it next time."

Ryu Chungwoo's cautious response was welcome, but that was actually the point.

"No, it's actually because of the album."

"Hmm?"

"Since the programs or events we can appear on as a full group are limited, I thought it would be better to split up and show our faces a few more times individually."

"Ah."

That way, we could compensate, even if just a little, for the schedules that were cut because of the T1 affiliation. We had to let the people who did not use SNS, online communities, or WeTube know that TeSTAR's album was out.

And... there was one more advantage, which Seon Ahyeon seemed to have noticed.

"Ah...! Th-then Sejin-hyung's acting will also be seen as part of those individual appearances... so it'll be like we're all doing it together!"

*Exactly.*

Although Bae Sejin's activity was uniquely different and more serious, if we leaked the circumstances a bit, he would not end up being the sole target of criticism.

"...! I see, of course! I was unable to deduce that far!"

"Then I'll go on this one!"

Kim Raebin started admiring Seon Ahyeon, while Cha Eugene actively searched for programs. The meeting room grew noisy.

Bae Sejin lowered his head deeply.

"...Thank you. Really."

"...!"

"Not at all! I admire your will to even consider giving it up for the album! I will cheer for your acting...!"

"Yeah. I'll cheer for you."

"F-fighting...!"

*Should I add a word too?*

"And if it's another psychopath role, ask them to change it."

"...Yeah."

A hint of laughter mixed into Bae Sejin's voice.

When he raised his head again, Bae Sejin was clenching his fists, his eyes shining.

"I'll do well."

*You'd better.*

"I'll... keep practicing for the stage, too."

"......"

"I'll... uh, I'll work hard to promote the album on set, too...!"

"That's alright."

*No, don't do that. That industry is brutal. You'll get screwed if you do that. Just focus on filming your project and come back.*

"Ahem, ah, still..."

Bae Sejin seemed to want to say more, but he suddenly met someone's eyes and shut his mouth. It was someone who had not said a single word throughout the entire meeting.

"......"

Keun Sejin.

He looked at Bae Sejin with an expression that revealed no particular emotion.

For a moment, Bae Sejin seemed to reflexively try to avoid his gaze, but that impulse soon vanished. Instead, he asked Keun Sejin seriously.

"What... do you think?"

"......"

"About me acting during our promotions."

Keun Sejin glanced at the table for a moment, then opened his mouth.

"Well, in a situation like this, shouldn't it be fine?"

"...!"

His tone was relaxed. It was a roundabout affirmation.

Bae Sejin was so surprised he almost shot out of his chair, but he quickly cleared his throat.

"Well, ahem, you weren't saying anything... I thought you were holding it in because you didn't like it!"

"Yeah, well... Yep. I figured you'd interpret whatever I said in a negative way anyway~"

"......"

*A direct hit.*

Keun Sejin deliberately raised the end of his sentence playfully, but the remark was clearly sincere. Bae Sejin seemed to have been struck by it too.

He probably would have suspected sarcasm even if Keun Sejin had said the individual activity was a good idea, and would have nodded in agreement if he had opposed it.

Keun Sejin rubbed the back of his neck.

"We have a few years under our belt... it is about time for everyone to start doing individual activities~ And you were planning to refuse anyway, hyung."

"......"

"It's good if we all do well together."

Keun Sejin finished his words like that. It really did not seem like an empty platitude.

"...Yeah."

This time, it seemed Bae Sejin was able to accept his words as they were.

"Alright. Then... I'll organize everything and contact the director!"

"Great!"

And so, TeSTAR finished preparing to officially enter their album promotion period in a slightly unique way, different from before.
