# Chapter 623

Festival Venue.

June in Glastonbury, UK, was colder than expected. It couldn't compare to the clear, hot skies of Los Angeles or San Diego. The weather was gloomy, threatening to pour at any moment. Showers repeatedly stopped and started, and seeing the sun was a rare event. The outdoor landscape was damp and dim.

However, a fiery joy and fervor overcame it all.

"This is insane!"

WOOOOOOOOH-

Anticipatory cheers, laughter, and murmurs merged into a single mass that rang in the ears.

Colorful tents, festival decorations, sculptures, and vibrant outfits brightened the green field under the gloomy sky.

Festival! Festival!

And music!

One American tourist stood frozen, her mouth agape at the majestic sight.

"Haena!"

"Oh."

She snapped back to her senses at her friend's call.

The other American tourist, who had roused her dazed friend, shrugged. She understood. More real, living people than she had ever seen filled the plain to the horizon, along with countless tents and blankets. It was even more crowded than the beaches of LA.

*I probably looked just like that.*

The American tourist nodded and moved on with Haena.

They sat in front of their tent and opened a pamphlet. They needed to check the lineup for the three-day show, which would start after two days of pre-festival events.

"Is there any artist you absolutely have to see?"

"There is!"

Her friend excitedly listed a few artists. She then hesitated before pointing to one more name.

"And... this band too."

[TESTAR]

She blinked at the unfamiliar name.*What kind of artist is this?*She was a little surprised by her friend's answer.

"K-pop?"

"Oh, um. Yeah."

She saw her friend look a little embarrassed and exclaimed.

"Haena, don't tell me you're a K-pop listener?"

"Well, to some extent."

"That's fine! I know a few songs myself..."

She recalled a few Korean words she had heard on TikTakTok and shrugged, then suddenly remembered something.

[Saturday Headliner – VTIC]

"Come to think of it, isn't the headliner for Saturday a K-pop artist?"

In fact, given the festival's uncommercial, open, and progressive nature, it wasn't unusual for them to choose a headliner from such a niche genre, so it was understandable. After all, they sold tickets before the lineup was even announced! Some people would sarcastically ask if they had a quota system for political correctness, but they were usually ignored.

She was vaguely aware that the K-pop band called VTIC was world-renowned, but Haena was firm.

"I'm not interested in them."

*Why?*

*Don't all K-pop groups share similar characteristics anyway?*

She didn't think she was looking down on K-pop. However, it was true that unique genres tended to have prominent similarities.

Certain features immediately came to mind when she thought of K-pop. Vibrant colors, synchronized choreography, flawless skin, teenagers, and a crazy liveliness or unconventional songs that seemed to mix various genres into one... and lyrics that mixed English and Korean.

Sure enough, her friend hesitated before confessing the truth.

"I just, um... an old friend of mine is in that band on Friday. TeSTAR."

"Really?!"

In a mix of shock and excitement, she grabbed the smartphone she had tossed aside and searched for the band's name.

Haena quickly tried to stop her.

"No, we just went to the same middle school, and we did homework together a few times..."

"Oh... it's a boy."

She thought Haena's face flushed red.

The two of them looked at a photo of a surprisingly well-built and hot blond member named 'Cha Eugene' and talked in low voices.

"Then he must be Korean."

"Probably. I actually thought he might be part Spanish, but I'm not sure..."

The topic naturally moved on. Up to this point, the American tourist felt only a slight curiosity.

She thought that even though Haena was now firmly saying she wasn't interested, she could watch the K-pop headliner's performance with her on Saturday if she showed interest.

*Still, it's human nature to want to see the more famous one.*

Even if she herself had no interest at all.

The festival's time flowed on.

"Look at that!"

They saw everything from yoga to a circus field.

After enjoying the pre-festival events in the countless concept zones, the main event finally arrived. It was the day of the performances.

"Further in! Further in!"

"WAAAAAH!"

It was Friday.

Already hyped from two days of art and concept zones, she and her friend willingly threw themselves into the excited crowd.

Cheers and colors erupted from the huge, famous Pyramid Stage, even in broad daylight.

POP! POP! POP!

*How many people are there? 50,000? 80,000? ...120,000?*

*Oh my god...*

She stood in the mud amidst the sea of people, holding her friend's arm and looking up at the Pyramid Stage.

It was the largest, most central stage in the magnificent festival.*What would it feel like to stand on that enormous structure in front of hundreds of thousands of people? To face a crowd staring at you with such anticipation!*

[Say, Good-]

OOOOOOH!!!

The first performance ended amidst fervent cheers.

The crowd, who had sung along to the veteran artist's acoustic guitar performance, gave thunderous applause. The singer exited with a moved expression.

*This is what a real concert is!*

She blinked, caught up in the warm, overwhelming atmosphere. It felt like the true music festival was just beginning.

Then, a sharp scream erupted from beside her.

"No!"

The smartphone in her hand shook wildly.

"Red Summer, the vocalist got injured!"

"What?!"

It was news of the day's headliner cancellation.

"Oh my god, so there's no headliner for Friday?!"

"What the XX!"

A few people who heard the news late spat out harsh curses, and the atmosphere soured instantly.

[Your attention, please!]

An emergency notice announced the stage would proceed as scheduled, but people already started drifting away toward the Other Stage.

The choice of a replacement artist was too predictable. People who had been waiting for the headliner, just killing time, went to find other things to do.

"I heard Timmy's at the Other Stage."

"Let's just go there."

The American tourist's buzzing excitement faded, and her head returned to reality.

"......"

She could feel her feet, stuck in boots in the mud, becoming sticky and sore. It was because countless people had been trampling the damp, soggy grass. The forgotten issues of exhaustion and discomfort suddenly rushed in as the adrenaline wore off. The fatigue of camping emerged with the disappointment and unease.

"...Should we take a break and come back?"

"......"

However, Haena seemed reluctant, so she decided to stay with her. There were plenty of other good artists in the lineup.

*No matter what others feel, we can just enjoy ourselves.*

A good number of people had left from the back. Thanks to that, they drifted much closer to the stage.

"I think I can see their faces on the stage from here!"

"That's crazy!"

They tried to revive their excitement, but the surrounding atmosphere was still unsettled.

*It'll get better if there's a good performance.*

If they heard a great song performed live with passion, the mood would change.

And then...

VMMMMMM-!

A loud band tuning sound echoed as the next artist's name appeared in black and white on the central screen.

[TeSTAR]

"Oh!"

She nudged her friend's shoulder. It was the K-pop group her friend had been waiting for!

However, the crowd's reaction wasn't enthusiastic. Only a few people holding flags waved them energetically, trying to liven things up.

People were surely cheering at the very front, but they couldn't be seen from this far back through the massive crowd.

People either didn't know who they were or weren't interested.

*Oh.*

She had a feeling. This wasn't it. This wasn't the performance that would change the mood.

She felt a little sorry for them. In this lukewarm atmosphere, it would be hard for the artist not to be nervous and to feel satisfied, no matter what they did.

The tourist thought she would have run away if it were her.

And then.

DRIP. DROP.

"Huh?"

Droplets finally began to fall from the gloomy sky.

"Ah...!"

It was rain.

***

[Five minutes to stage.]

Ryu Chungwoo heard the announcement in English and thought quietly.*It felt like it was five minutes just a moment ago, and it's still five minutes now.*

Multi-act live shows often had delays from encores. This time, however, was different. The headliner's cancellation had just been announced.

"How's the mood out there?"

"Ah, it's fine."

Cha Eugene nodded at the manager's response, but his face clearly showed he knew it was a lie.

However, no one in TeSTAR was fazed.

*Is it because this is nothing new to them?*

Ryu Chungwoo watched them, almost forgetting his worries, and let out a small laugh.

Somehow, he felt incredibly reassured. Of course, once that moment passed, his anxieties tried to creep back into his mind.

Deciding to go on stage was one thing, but calculating the repercussions, the worst-case scenario, and the potential damage to the group...

"Hey."

His thoughts were broken. Bae Sejin had spoken.

"I have something I want to apologize for."

"Hm?"

Ryu Chungwoo was almost taken aback.

"Sejin, what are you talking about?"

"I think... I was unnecessarily too careful with you."

"......"

Bae Sejin seemed to have thought about this many times. He continued speaking carefully but without hesitation.

"I felt like I shouldn't carelessly tell you not to worry. Because... I used to hate it when people would just say everything will be fine without taking any responsibility."

"......"

He seemed to be talking about his child actor days.

"But I think I was wrong."

Bae Sejin lifted his head.

"You're not me."

"...!"

"You didn't worry about things like that. You don't agonize over everything until the very end like I do. You're just... a person who does what needs to be done."

*Is that true?*

Ryu Chungwoo felt a strange unfamiliarity as he listened to Bae Sejin's description of him.

This was the third time he had heard someone describe him. Someone who is naturally good. Someone irreplaceable, and...

"A person who does what needs to be done?"

"Yeah. Without overthinking things."

Bae Sejin nodded and muttered again.

"So, for you, I think this is better than giving you time to worry."

Ryu Chungwoo listened quietly to his words.

"You can do anything. That's why... I've thought you were amazing since ❬Idol Inc.❭"

"......"

"You're good. I'm envious."

Ryu Chungwoo stared at Bae Sejin, at a loss for words. Bae Sejin met his gaze without looking away.

"......"

Finally, it was time.

"Let's go up."

Ryu Chungwoo walked toward the stage. None of the members said things like, 'It's okay if you make a mistake.' They just handed him a handheld microphone.

"Here."

He took the mic.

His heart pounded, but his mind was quiet. The chaos in his thoughts calmed as if it were all a lie. There was no need for it.

*So that's how it was.*

That was it.

The artist appeared in the midst of the rain.

[TESTAR]

WAAAAAAAAAH!!

A polite cheer.

However, it was directed more toward the new performance and the general atmosphere, not the artist.

Moreover, the falling rain seeped into people's hair and stung their eyes.

Ryu Chungwoo felt the weight of raindrops on his head. He looked out from the stage at the wave of countless spectators.

Since it was still light out, he could see far into the distance, even spotting figures in the back turning away.

"......"

Park Moondae's voice from last week's practice echoed in his head.

[People will definitely leave.]

The performances went on all day for three consecutive days. Naturally, if a lineup they didn't like or weren't interested in came on, it was common for audience members to leave, enjoy another stage or activity, and then come back. This was especially true for those at the back.

[So once the mood is set, they'll leave without any regrets.]

The audience was a group easily swayed by the crowd's mood.

However.

*To put it another way...*

It meant the way to make them watch was the same.

Ryu Chungwoo gripped the microphone.

[We'll start with a song you know.]

The stage's numerous lights flashed, cutting through the dark atmosphere under the rainy sky. A voice flashed just as brightly.

Black- hole!

The main OST from Limestone, the major comics company's SF hero movie, echoed a cappella throughout the venue.

Let me swallow iiiiii-t!

The band's ensemble burst out right after.

"...!"

A familiar song. A familiar, European band-style arrangement and progression.

Above all... the vocals!

[Exploding far-- away!]

Park Moondae let out a piercing high note, layering a high harmony over Ryu Chungwoo's solid voice.

A mixture of cheers, groans, and unexpected admiration rippled through the venue. Feet reflexively stopped moving.

It was a song they had heard before, with lyrics they could understand and a vocal power that resonated in their heads.

[Hit it like a comet,]

Over a lonely band instrumental, Kim Raebin's low, rough vocals harmonized with Seon Ahyeon's soft mid-low tones before fading away.

And the climax.

[Shatter like a shooting star!!]

Between the driving drum beats, TeSTAR's vocalists raced forward, holding standing mics.

Fireworks exploded from all sides.

WOOOOAH!

Haena and her friend screamed like crazy in response to the reflexive cheers.

*Wow!*

The song flowing live from the singer's throat was incredible to hear.

The movie had been a box office hit, so people who recognized the song hummed along excitedly, following the lyrics on the screen.

The band members didn't just stand still. They grabbed their mics and moved around as they pleased, singing and ad-libbing.

[Black- hole!]

WAAAAH!!

A roar of cheers finally erupted as the song ended.

The polite applause from the beginning had transformed into joyous shouts.

"HUUUUUUH!!"

Haena's friend also screamed from the pit of her stomach as she watched the stage.

The big screen focused on their faces.

*What, they aren't all just kids!*

Rather than immature teenagers, their frames showed the composure and strength of graduating seniors or college students.

However, true to K-pop fashion, their tops over various tones of denim pants were adorned with flashy jewelry and leather chokers.

Only then did their first ment begin.

[You don't know who we are, do you?]

"Pfft!"

The opening line was enough to make someone choke.

The largest member looked thrilled. He grinned and pointed at the screen where the 'Black Hole' lyrics had been displayed.

[We're the people who appeared as the crazy alien band in that movie.]

Someone next to them chuckled.

[We do actually make music like that.]

"HAHAHAHA!"

It was natural, imbued with the unique confidence of people who make their own music.

Once an audience's attention is captured, they become more open-minded to the next song. The barrier to entry was broken.

[So next... is a song to introduce what kind of music we make. It's from our new album that we just released.]

A light cheer followed.

The member with the best physique and a cool, manly impression took the mic again. Clear, smooth English flowed from his lips.

[We haven't even started touring for this album yet, but this is Glastonbury.]

He smiled gently.

[Love and peace, tolerance and dreams. Isn't that the spirit of Glastonbury? You can try anything.]

[So we've prepared something too.]

At that moment, the stage lights went out for a second.

"......"

The audience's reaction quieted instantly. They knew from the atmosphere that a new performance was about to begin.

The person on stage also went quiet. It was Ryu Chungwoo, who had been holding the microphone until just now.

"......"

He remembered.

[Hyung, if you go on stage.]

[For this song, you have to be in the center for the chorus. No matter what.]

Ryu Chungwoo took a ragged breath. Below the stage, the audience whispered in voices that couldn't reach that high.

"Haena! Do you know this song?"

"I do! This should be a song called Bad Feeling..."

The American tourist tried to get a preview from her friend's enthusiastic explanation.

"So, it's kind of a Broadway-style pop music..."

Haena's lips moved to describe suits, an office setting, rhythmic choreography, trumpets, and cheerful electronic instruments.

"It's starting!"

"Ah!!"

The lights came back on. A heavy, old-school beat echoed from the very back, as if chasing them.

THUD.

"......"

A light fell on the singers who had walked to the front of the stage.

However, the tops with reflective jewelry were gone. In their place were black and red hoodies or monotone jumpers.

Soon, the face of a man with sanpaku eyes under a black bucket hat filled the screen. Everyone else bowed their heads.

STATIC, ZZZZZING-

A low, coarse rap burst out against the background of a rough band ensemble.

[Get this feeling

Then I'll fall and fall]

THUD.

Bad feeling.

Their title track, completely remixed into old-school hip-hop, began to explode like fireworks along with the explosive band sound.
