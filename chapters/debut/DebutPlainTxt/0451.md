# Chapter 451

Keundal found himself in an unexpectedly dire situation. He thought he could relax after returning to his original body, but an unexpected ambush appeared.

The person beside him suddenly acted like they knew him. "It's been a while."

Keundal sweated so much he wanted to send an SOS to anyone and ask what on earth was happening. Worse, he could not just run from his seat in front of the stage while holding the camera.

"But it must be tough to sell data as a side job after getting a full-time one."

"......"

"Well, you know how it is."

*I don't know...! I don't get the context of this conversation! Not at all!*

The person seemed to be an old acquaintance of Ryu Gunwoo, but Keundal could not be sure.

*Is... is that person a data seller? A connection from back then?*

However, he could not call for Park Moondae. TeSTAR's stage was about to start. He could not interfere with their first comeback performance over something like this.

So Keundal repeated to himself.

*If I were... Gunwoo!*

What would he have done in this situation?

Keundal racked his brain harder than he ever had since the life-or-death test, trying to make a split-second decision.

*...I should say as little as possible!*

He answered calmly.

"...I'm not trying to sell them for money. It's just a hobby now."

"Is that so?"

It seemed appropriate.

Fortunately, the other person seemed to think so too, as no more personal questions followed.

*...I got through it!*

Keundal sighed in relief, but Ryu Seojin, sitting next to him, was inwardly thinking,*He's gotten a bit tamer.*

Thinking that a full wallet must grant one peace of mind, she asked again.

"Ah, you take pictures of TeSTAR too?"

"......"

"Who do you mainly shoot?"

"Moondae."

This, he could answer without a shred of shame.

He then saw the person next to him nod.

"You still have a good eye for market value."

"......"

That was an unusual choice of words. Keundal was almost certain.

*That person is a data seller!*

Ryu Seojin also came to a conclusion.

*So he's still selling data after all.*

It was a perfect misunderstanding.

It was then.

[...Let's meet them now!]

The MC's line ended.

At that moment, as if on cue, both of them reacted.

*Ah, it's starting.*

The two TeSTAR fans, each mistaking the other for a data seller, turned their gazes toward the stage in unison.

The white lights for the awards ceremony dimmed, and the intro finally began to play over the stage.

*It's Savior!*

Keundal's heart pounded as he gripped his camera. He planned to watch with his naked eyes and just try to get a few good shots.

[Click.]

Blue and red lights flooded the stage. They shone down on TeSTAR, who had already formed up in the center. Their outfits were magnificent coats inspired by Daoist robes.

*Hk.*

He could not believe he had seen those outfits up close just a while ago.

[That desperate call]

I hear it, the sound of my feet rushing in

The intro was so familiar he could almost recite it.

Park Moondae strode across the massive stage and started the song with a piercing voice.

[The first to appear]

The vanguard!

The high notes thrilled him live.

*Wow...*

Keundal stared at the stage, feeling as if his heart was about to be zapped.

This version of 'Savior' was arranged closer to retro rock, giving it a strangely classic feel. The intersection of band and orchestral sounds made one's heart pound primally.

*Raebin must be a real genius...*

Keundal, who had gotten to know Kim Raebin and had related conversations with him after the ice cream incident, swallowed hard.

[That's ma savior!]

Throw away your tension and enjoy

The night of victory!

'Savior' rushed forward as if running wild. It showcased a new dance break before concluding with a spectacular remix befitting an awards ceremony.

"WAAAAAAAH!!"

The deafening cheers overwhelmed Keundal with emotion, but he held back, knowing he could not shake the camera.

*I didn't bring a light stick...!*

The disappointment was fleeting.

The lights on stage changed, and music began to flow once more. A light interlude.

"......"

...It was a new song.

Keundal's eyes flew open.

[Clap clap clap- clap clap!]

On stage, a spotlight fell on the blonde Cha Eugene, who had broken from the formation and was sitting in a corner. With his hair slicked back with pomade, he clapped for the other members who had just finished their 'Savior' performance, looking rather impressed.

At that, the members sighed and broke their formation.

"......"

As some members stretched or did light warm-ups, Bae Sejin started walking around with a clipboard, gesturing as if giving directions. The camera captured a variety of reactions, from Seon Ahyeon nodding attentively to Kim Raebin scowling in annoyance.

*An act?*

It was a musical or theatrical composition.

Then, a cheerful whistle.

[Phrrreeeeeee!]

Instantly, all the members on stage froze and turned to look at the audience.

On the big screen, bold capital letters appeared, looking like something out of an American comic book.

[PRACTICE IS OVER!!]

A sound effect like a school bell played. An exciting riff and drum beat cut through it.

With relieved expressions, TeSTAR threw off their coats and ran to the front of the stage as if they were going out to play.

*Hup.*

Revealed underneath the coats were outfits reminiscent of 80s American teen movies.

As if they each had a role, their outfits and accessories, from knits to jeans, were all slightly different.

Cha Eugene, who had somehow picked up and put on a star-patterned jumper, swaggered over to join them. They formed a proper formation at the center.

"......"

Finally, a caption appeared at the bottom of the screen.

[TeSTAR – POSE]

A mechanical MIDI sound bounced between the tempo like splashing water droplets at various pitches.

TeSTAR also changed formation with bouncing steps.

[Make a pose]

Whatever you want

Find your own style

Following Cha Eugene's stylish mid-low tone, a similar verse melody continued with Park Moondae's raspy high notes.

[(Make a pose!)]

Something bold at times

Might be okay

The tempo rose.

A mirror ball spun above the stage.

Amidst the brilliant lights, light, fast, and complex steps fell perfectly into place. In contrast, there were eye-catching upper body movements using fingers and entire arms that looked easy to follow.

[Exploding like a dancing flame]

Melting hot summer night

Race through this night as if it's the end

TeSTAR shone on stage like a symbol of color, light, and youthful bravado.

The song seemed to soar endlessly upward, running cheerfully along with the dazzling steps of disco.

The moment the pre-chorus passed, the melody changed to a catchy minor key and the bass dropped.

[Drrrrrrrr-!]

The sound of an engine.

A chorus close to hip-hop exquisitely tied the retro sound together with a modern composition.

For the chorus, Kim Raebin stepped forward as if kicking his feet up, rapping in a popping style. His black fashion, like EMO or goth, further set the mood.

[Slowly, slowly]

Getting closer]

So you can't move, Grab this moment

Off-beat electronic sounds danced and flew over the heavy rock bass.

Cha Eugene, having thrown off his jacket, changed the formation with steps like a Latin dance.

[In the pose!]

Don't worry about going too far

Just think about now, come in now

That part, with the catchiest melody, was unmistakably the chorus.

[In the pose!]

Don't be too stingy with today

Now is what's important, Right now

(yes) Right now!

Then, a section where the riff melody raced on without vocals.

Lee Sejin, with his hair slicked back, came out with a grin. He seemed to give Ryu Chungwoo a high five, then performed a solo dance in the center. It was...

*Ah.*

It was really the choreography from their debut song, 'High Five'!

That's right. TeSTAR had set aside an instrumental section in the performance specifically for the members to take turns ad-libbing.

The members followed Lee Sejin's moves joyfully, as if bursting with laughter, but skillfully transitioned into the next part of the choreography.

[Yes, I'll remember]

Your moment right now

Slower, faster, more freely

Park Moondae, dressed in light jeans and a short denim jacket, came to the center with a move that looked like he was sliding on his knees, and sang the final verse.

[So hold on- and pose!]

BANG!

Fireworks exploded.

LEDs lit up the big screen like a fireworks display.

The cheerful and clean choreography overflowed with energy to the very end.

Naturally, a scream that seemed to tear through the air erupted from the audience like a conditioned reflex.

"WAAAAAAAH!!!"

"AAACK!"

It was a song like a party. It had all the elements people would expect from a popular male idol group. The flying confetti seemed to predict the future.

*...It's so good!*

Keundal was moved all over again. That was why he realized it a beat too late.

The person next to him was frantically firing off continuous shots with their camera, while he was just holding his own, dazed.

*Aah, right, the photos! I have to take them now!*

Just as he hurriedly lifted the camera to his eye, a pop-up appeared.

[Keundal.]

"Huh?"

Keundal reflexively looked up.

The pop-up glowed calmly.

[There's something I want to show you.]

It was Moondae.

...The one on stage right now!

*It's done.*

The comeback... seems to have been a success.

I was still in my ending pose, kneeling on the stage. The other guys were the same.

"Hoo."

I was out of breath, but none of us would dare to pant heavily here.

No matter how rushed the awards ceremony was, they had a few seconds to spare for the ending shot of the group that was practically today's headliner, and a few more seconds after the stage lights went out, right after that ending shot.

I sent the pop-up to Keundal at the perfect moment somewhere in between.

[Oh, is there something you want to show me?]

*Yes.*

I took a deep breath and murmured.

*Let's try sharing our vision.*

The pop-up seemed to waver in confusion, but it soon connected to my senses as I had said.

And then.

"......"

It seemed he had finally seen my view.

*This is...*

I did not know whether to call it magnificent or overwhelming.

Nearly 20,000 lights, including those in the standing area, surged from all directions. It was a scene that inspired an awe close to what one feels in nature.

The view from the darkened stage glittered even more brightly. It must have felt like being surrounded by a downpour of stars on the open sea in the middle of the night.

*To think I'd make a comparison like this.*

I let out a dry laugh, and Keundal seemed a bit excited.

[It's really amazing! Wow! But it's also a little scary to think that those are all people... Anyway, thank you! This is a first for me!]

Yeah.

Then I said what I had to.

*Thank you.*

"......"

[Wh-why? Uh... Because I acted well today?]

*It's not so much that...*

*It's a view I got to see thanks to you.*

[...??]

A hand tapped my shoulder. I got up from my spot, gazing at the stage once more.

And I reviewed the fact I had newly realized.

*Why does Yeongrin remember me?*

There were probably several reasons. Her professional nature, or the fact that I was unknown back then and did not have many cameras on me.

*However...*

If I had to pinpoint it, I think the meaning of that fancam was probably a big part of it.

*A chance to succeed as an idol.*

The impression and feelings of the person who pushed that chance forward.

In that sense, this guy is actually the most direct supporter who pushed forward my chance to see this view as an idol. From a time when I could not have even imagined I would become like this.

I said slowly.

*You said that with what I have, there was nothing I couldn't achieve... but that's not true.*

Because I was about to die.

If that guy had not wanted me to survive, and if he had not persistently remained as a Status Window to support me in passing the idol missions, this was a view I would have never seen.

*So, I wanted to show you this at least once.*

[...]

*That this is the view you gave me.*

I cannot make this guy perform on stage, but I figured we could at least watch this together.

[...]

The pop-up could not display any words for a few seconds.

However, for some reason, I had a feeling that the guy had given a small nod from his seat in the audience.

"Moondae, we have to go down."

"Yes."

I turned my head, left the audience behind me, and started to descend from the stage.

Keundal's pop-up appeared again only after that.

[I'm so happy.]

I let out a small laugh.

I had a feeling that everything was going to fall into place.

That must have been why. The first thing I checked after getting off stage, even before monitoring the performance, was this.

*...The mission failure pop-up.*

And the result was...

And it ends with the dog-shit result that I've so carefully helped this bastard run rampant again?
