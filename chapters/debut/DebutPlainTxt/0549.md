# Chapter 549

That's right.

I had just done something crazy. I signed an acting contract with the past's LeTi. I did it to gather information on Cheongryeo from multiple angles and to get an identity I could use in an emergency.

After I provided my personal information and signed the documents, the manager grinned at me.

"So, you'll come to Korea in two months when your trip is over?"

"Yes. Thank you."

Of course, I couldn't return to Korea right away. I had no passport, and my identity was fake.

*I probably don't even have a US entry record.*

That was the perfect way to get arrested.

So I delayed my entry with some nonsense. I said, "I came to the US for a two-month trip before the next semester starts, so it's hard to cancel."

LeTi seemed to have no intention of paying a cancellation fee, because they readily agreed.

"Understood!"

As we parted ways, however, the manager whispered conspiratorially, as if sharing a secret.

"But before you come to Korea... you might go straight to a screen debut. There's a perfect location available."

"Pardon?"

"Your look and your attitude were rated very highly during our internal review... You know?"

*I didn't.*

"It means you could debut right away."

*I have no intention of debuting.*

Instead of saying that, I deliberately hesitated before speaking.

"I think it's too soon for me. I feel like I should practice something first so I don't embarrass the company."

"Hey~ What are you talking about!"

He wasn't listening.

*Well, it didn't matter. I was going to disappear as soon as I finished analyzing the clues about the System anyway.*

I got up from my cafe seat and saw the manager out.

"Anyway, we're looking forward to working with you, Gunwoo!"

"Yes. Thank you."

Then, a guy stealthily rose from the table behind me and sat down, like he was taking a baton. It was Judan.

"It seems the contract negotiations reached a satisfactory conclusion."

"......"

*I don't know why he's doing this weird thing instead of just sitting next to me.*

I just nodded instead of asking.

"So you'll be staying in America?"

"Yes."

"It might be better than Korea, but America isn't an easy place for a stateless person to live, either."

"There are plenty of undocumented immigrants in the US. I just have to be careful."

I could speak English and knew how to cook or make coffee, so I could probably get a job at a fast-food joint or a cafe.

Judan shook his head.

"At the very least, I'll set you up with your first smartphone. A prepaid one."

I laughed shortly.

"What money do you have?"

"I'll consider it an investment in the future."

He still seemed to firmly believe I was part of his group.

His eyes gleamed, and he asked seriously, "Let me ask you just one thing. Will I have the financial means to buy a building in the future?"

"...Yeah."

*That... was certain.*

The guy nodded, satisfied.

"Then I'll be in your care. Let's stay in regular contact while I monitor Cheongryeo's movements."

He held out his hand for a handshake.

I smiled and extended my own.

"Thanks."

Then I realized it.

The other person's hand felt as hot as fire.

My own hand was unbelievably cold.

"...!"

The other guy must have felt it too. His eyes widened, but he quickly seemed to grasp the situation and nodded.

"This is what happens when you order a cold drink in this weather."

"...Right."

*No.*

*The truth is, I haven't even touched the drink I ordered.*

This meant it wasn't a temporary drop in body temperature.

"......"

I watched Judan get up to order another drink, then I lifted my hand and examined it.

When I gripped the glass table, the glass felt lukewarm, though it should have been cold. A dull ache was an added bonus.

*Hoo.*

*Yes, this is also a problem. My body's condition is rapidly turning to garbage.*

I had a stolen identity and no money, so going to a hospital was difficult.

*It's not like this is a problem a hospital could fix in the first place.*

I clenched and unclenched my fist.

: YES

The medium.

"It's too..., too far... and too fast."

"I don't know! It's just, just a feeling..."

"Ugh!!"

*How do I make contact?*

"Seon Ahyeon."

"That's what caused the error in the System. Can't we use him?"

That was why Cheongryeo had prioritized calling Seon Ahyeon as soon as the incident occurred.

"......"

"He...llo."

"Excuse us."

Keundal was dumbfounded. All of TeSTAR. Everyone had suddenly appeared at JSA.

"......"

Then he asked Cheongryeo.

"Who is Park Moondae?"
