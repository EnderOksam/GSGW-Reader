# Chapter 446

[Hyung, but why Judan?]

VTIC's Judan.

Why did this guy suddenly pop up on the list of brains to use?

*To be honest... there weren't many candidates.*

[...Ah.]

I hadn't told everyone about my situation. My body switched, I went back in time, the System this and that... I wasn't stupid enough to spout that crazy talk everywhere.

Only a few people, who got caught up in it, were forced to learn some of the situation.

*And among them, he's the one who seemed the least resistant to this kind of thing.*

He's a bit... strange, but I thought that might actually be helpful.

I contacted Judan, thinking I had nothing to lose. First, I politely asked how he was doing.

"How's military life, sunbae."

[I have quite a bit to say about the efficiency problems of the conscription system, but I will skip that.]

"...Um, yes."

I started to wonder how that guy was treated in his unit. However, I decided to focus on my own problems.

*Let's see... He's probably in the barracks, so there must be guys eavesdropping.*

A call with a celebrity. Even if they pretended not to, wouldn't anyone be interested if they overheard?

I had to make them misunderstand the topic.

"Actually, I was playing a 'game' and had a few questions about the 'System', so I decided to call."

Only after laying more groundwork, implying 'I'm just calling casually about a hobby, it's a hypothetical like a personality test', did I bring up the System problem.

I summarized how my body switched due to a mission failure, and how this and that status message appeared.

After a bit of Q&A, the guy finally responded.

[Body switching... a romantic comedy cliché. It's not a genre I'm well-versed in.]

"......"

*Should I hang up?*

*I'll endure it.*

I needed to use this guy's brain, so I would endure it this once.

"I wanted to hear a unique third-party perspective, so this is actually good. What are your thoughts?"

A moment of silence passed. Then, a short while later, Judan suddenly spoke an idea I had never even imagined.

[Have you considered the possibility that there was no mission in the first place?]

What?

A few days later, TeSTAR's final comeback preparations went smoothly without any major issues.

Since it was our first album in a while, we originally planned to go hard and strong, but considering our last album 'Savior' was also quite intense, we toned down the niche intensity.

*It was a Haetae (TL/N: mystical beast of justice and righteousness) concept, after all.*

To bring out something like that again, we needed a change of pace. We decided on a stylish, public-friendly song with the volume of a mini-album.

So the concept was...

"American public high school-style teen concept!"

The standard for idols.

...Alright, I won't lie. I did it with the thought that we should do it while we're still at an age where we can pull it off without being shameless.

"Ah~ I guess we don't have much time left to pull this kind of thing off naturally. Sob, so sad. I need to take better care of myself~"

"Actors in their 30s play high school roles just fine. Don't be so dramatic."

"Aah... Um, yep. That's true."

For the record, Seon Ahyeon smoothed over this awkward back-and-forth.

"E-Everyone looks good in the outfits, you look like high school students...! The stage, it'll be amazing too."

"Ah~ right, I can guarantee that!"

Right. We did make it properly.

[Oh... It doesn't need to be*this*childish.]

We brought in Teacher Cha Eugene for historical accuracy on the music video and stage. Cha Eugene was so excited he even assigned roles.

Everything had been proceeding smoothly on schedule, and it still was.

"Moondae Moondae, that switching problem..."

"You don't have to worry about it right now. Just wait a little longer."

"...Okay."

And so, the comeback preparations flowed smoothly. There were no problems at all.

However, I hadn't forgotten the Status Window.

A few days later, on the day I saw this, I realized something.

[Sudden!]

Status Abnormality Mission Failure: Return to Original State (2)

[Everything returns to how it was, as if it never happened]

: D-? until the next start

*Keundal.*

[Yes??]

I took a deep breath.

"I know the date we'll switch."

[...!!]

"Look at the Status Window. See how 'D-??' changed to 'D-?'."

I pointed with my finger. It was counting down one by one, so a two-digit number changing to a single-digit one meant only one thing.

It went from 10 to 9.

"Nine days left."

And if it was around the same time as last time...

"It's most likely... going to be around 11 PM that night."

The night of January 21st.

Fortunately, it was just before our comeback stage. I mentally ran through the schedule for that day.

[Then... are you going to clear your schedule for that time?]

"......"

This was the difficult part.

"No, we can't do that."

[...!]

"I go to work as Ryu Gunwoo, and you handle the schedule as Park Moondae."

I said slowly.

"That's what we have to do on that day."

[Why, why?]

"Because it seems to be the way to end the effect of the mission failure."

I recalled the conversation I had with Judan a few days ago. Those out-of-the-blue words.

[...No mission?]

[Yes. It's a hollow narrative where you try to eliminate the root cause, only to find there was no root cause to begin with. It's so common now that it has become a cliché that breaks clichés, however.]

Honestly, I couldn't agree with that bizarre logic. However, the moment I heard that sentence, it suddenly sparked a thought.

"You changed my 'status abnormality failure' into a 'mission failure'. That means the System never considered a mission in the first place. It was originally just a status abnormality. Also, to begin with, a 'mission'... is a goal I set for myself through the Status Window to get a reward."

Through the trait this guy gave me, 'Mission Constitution'.

"But you can't create one in the first place."

The pop-up paused for a moment.

"Because you don't have a Status Window."

In other words, the concept of a mission is impossible for Keundal.

[So... there is... no mission.]

Right.

*There was no mission to begin with, but the moment 'mission failure' appeared, it was forcibly brought into existence.*

Because it was substituted blindly, the algorithm got twisted.

So.

"The mission failure is now guaranteed to happen."

Instead of solving a non-existent mission...

"We have to approach it by releasing the target when the failure effect occurs."

[...If your theory is correct, sunbae, then what we did during the building escape game seems to be the right strategy.]

Just like how I escaped the collapsing building, causing the System to lose its 'target'. Instead of guessing at a mission that doesn't exist, I should focus on what happened after the mission failure.

*If I do that, the situation becomes glaringly obvious.*

I let out a small laugh.

"Do you remember what we did after we switched bodies?"

[Uh, I lived as a member of TeSTAR, and you, Hyung, followed along as a road manager...]

"Exactly."

So, to explain it again.

"You lived 'Park Moondae's' original daily life properly, and I escaped from 'Ryu Gunwoo's' original daily life."

[...!]

"That's why it seems only Park Moondae was released from being a target."

Like this.

[Target 'Park Moondae' Lost]

[Release in Progress]

[Remaining Target: Ryu Gunwoo]

Then the goal was obvious now.

"We make it think we haven't switched and are living our own lives properly. That way, we make it lose its targets."

Keundal didn't answer for a moment. Then a pop-up soon appeared.

[Then I'll live as TeSTAR's Park Moondae one more time, and you, Hyung, will live as the civil servant Ryu Gunwoo...!]

Exactly. I nodded.

*This is it.*

I felt relieved now that I had a perfect conclusion. The timing was set, too.

*Now, if I just execute it properly...*

[Wow, Hyung, contacting Judan was a really wise decision! I think Judan is a good person!]

"......"

*Hmph. That's... a bit much.*

[I wouldn't know. After work hours, we can all access modern civilization on our own smartphones, so there's no reason for everyone to gather and focus on mass media like TV.]

[......]

[Of course, the scope of what we can use is controlled for security purposes.]

[Yes.]

[Ah, please give my regards to Jaehyun.]

Besides, I didn't know why he told me this. Isn't he your group's Leader?

Anyway, well... I would probably see Cheongryeo somewhere even if I didn't contact him. It was awards season, after all.

This made things much easier, didn't it?

[But about Saturday night... I think I'll probably be out.]

This guy, at 11 PM?
