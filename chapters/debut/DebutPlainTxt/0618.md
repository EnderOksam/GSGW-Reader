"......"

*Ah.*

Ryu Chungwoo blinked. The dull glow he had stared at until his eyes stung was gone. Three-dimensional space returned to his vision. The front door was open in the dark distance.

"What are you doing."

A member with a hardened face stood next to him, holding the laptop he had just snatched away.

Park Moondae.

"Why are you looking at this stuff."

"......"

His distant relative. A member who had appeared out of nowhere.

Park Moondae's eyes quickly and sharply scanned the internet pages and posts on the laptop. He relaxed a little after confirming there were no strange insults or rumors.

*Most of it is worry or concern.*

Ryu Chungwoo already had a good image. Now that he was sick, this was the normal reaction unless one dug deep into the internet's underbelly.

Still, Park Moondae had to say something.

"...Didn't they tell you during your counseling sessions not to search your name on the internet?"

"......"

"Did you not hear them?"

"...They told me not to look at malicious comments."

A dazed, sunken voice replied.

"They said it was okay to occasionally look at fans' support or worries if I needed reassurance."

"......"

It was probably meant for him to see how many people supported him and to gain strength from it.

In Park Moondae's view, however, this was not that kind of situation.

*This guy looked like he was about to lose his mind.*

As if he were sinking into a swamp. It was so bad that Park Moondae had sensed something was wrong the moment he entered the dorm and had rushed over to snatch the laptop.

Park Moondae suppressed a sigh. It was a good thing he had stopped by.

"I just stopped by to check on something I needed to grab, but let's talk for a bit first."

"......"

"I don't think you're in a position to be worrying about public opinion right now. There's no need to. First, you should just rest with an easy mind..."

"I don't want to rest."

"...!"

The answer shot out, a sharp, instantaneous reaction.

*Ah.*

Ryu Chungwoo sat motionless on the sofa, head bowed and hands clasped. The words seemed to have come out instinctively from his subconscious, having been ambushed while alone.

"......"

Park Moondae's expression sank.

*If anything, this is an opportunity.*

He closed the laptop, placed it on the floor, and quietly sat on the sofa. Then he asked calmly.

"Then should we resume activities?"

"I can't."

"It's not that you can't. We can just say you'll gradually participate in activities again and make a comeback..."

"Hyung."

Ryu Chungwoo covered his eyes with one hand.

"How can I promote as a pro if I can't perform on stage."

"......"

"If I say I'll do it, can I? No, I can't. You told me to rest too."

Park Moondae reflexively formulated a solution. It was what he was good at. Shaping public opinion.

"That's something we can ask for understanding for."

"......"

"Even if you falter a bit on stage, I can make people understand..."

"I don't want that!"

"......"

"...No, I'm sorry."

Ryu Chungwoo immediately lowered his raised voice and regained as much composure as he could.

"I shouldn't have gotten angry. I don't think I have good control right now."

"No. This is better."

Park Moondae shifted on the sofa and lowered his gaze.

"I was thinking I didn't know what you were thinking, but it feels like I'm finally hearing it."

"......"

"So, you don't want to show yourself unable to perform properly on stage because you're a pro... is that it?"

Was it a matter of pride as a singer?

However, Ryu Chungwoo remained silent for a long time.

*...Looks like there's more to it.*

Park Moondae quietly waited for an answer. The awaited response came belatedly.

"They're telling me not to."

"Who is?"

"Everyone is telling me not to."

Ryu Chungwoo clenched and unclenched his fist.

"Telling me to rest."

"......"

He could easily identify people who held animosity toward him, but he had never lived his life giving such people much meaning.

However...

[Chungwoo, your shoulder is bad too, so that singer career... Ah. Our son is going to rest now, right? Yes! Mom and Dad are of course okay with it! That's a relief, a relief...]

From the people he had given his affection to, cherished, and thought were on his side.

...This time, too, it was no different.

"Worrying them... making them anxious, I can't do that."

"......"

"So I have to listen..."

"I'm sorry."

"......"

"I'm sorry."

Ryu Chungwoo slowly shook his head.

There seemed to be no right answer in their conversation, but Park Moondae thought.

*I can't leave him like this.*

*I have to do something.*
