# Chapter 626

We returned to Korea after the festival in England.

Lee Sejin's voice came from behind my seat. He sounded a little tired but happy.

"They're saying we did really well."

"...Nice."

This was another 'Cheering Up by Monitoring Compliments' session, a brief moment during TeSTAR's busy promotional period. Today's topic was the Glastonbury Festival, which had been a great success.

"Glastonbury was fun. I want to go again."

"Yeah, let's definitely do that."

After one round of monitoring, a pleasant lull settled over the van.

To be honest, I hadn't seen any of it myself.

[Moondae, you did the monitoring by yourself during the comeback... so we'll do it this time...!]

[...Fine.]

Still, I got the general gist, so it didn't matter. I didn't get so anxious about these things anymore.

Besides, I could tell just from listening that the reactions were good.

*It was a huge hit.*

"This is the first time I've seen so many posts about us in English."

"Cha Eugene! Did anyone mention the new arrangement?!"

"Yepp. Everyone loves it. You did a good job, Kim Raebin."

"Oh~ Let's see, there's even an analysis post on an English site comparing it to the original song?"

"Oh my god...!"

TeSTAR's name in English had its search engine trend index jump more than fifteen times overnight. Considering our search volume was already high from promotions, it was an insane return on our effort. A crazy achievement. The company was screaming with joy.

*I can't believe we could pull off something this big.*

Honestly, I had a feeling it would go well when I was on that stage. I leaned back in my seat again.

I remembered the heat, the growing cheers, and the crowd's reactions shifting from bewilderment to awe, then to ecstasy. It was the feeling of perfectly achieving the stage's goal.

[Hardly anyone there will know us well. So instead of aiming for empathy or emotional connection...]

[We have to go for pure impact.]

The thrill when all those calculated moves paid off was incredible. What Cha Eugene just said was spot on.

*...It was fun.*

As expected, working with talented and cooperative people made even a high-risk gamble for the peak worthwhile.

*No, looking back, it doesn't even seem like it was a gamble.*

I turned to the MVP next to me, the one I thought was a gamble but who had hit the peak for us. Ryu Chungwoo.

"How are you feeling?"

"Good."

His answer was simple.

"......"

But cheers like those from a baseball stadium erupted, just like when we came down from the Glastonbury stage.

[Hyung!]

Backstage, Ryu Chungwoo must have heard that ecstatic call even as sweat dripped from his face. Instead of answering, he muttered to himself while looking at his arm.

[This was the feeling.]

[Huh?]

[Nothing.]

He clenched and unclenched his fist, then smiled.

[I get it now.]

It was a refreshing smile, and those words were still coming true.

"Oh, right~ Hyung, you've been doing great on stage lately!"

"Yeah, tell me how you feel!"

Ryu Chungwoo smiled slightly and said coolly, "Hm, I guess it was important not to lose the feeling."

That simple, workout-tip-like comment was all the guy who had brilliantly recovered from the yips and torn up the stage had to say.

However, no one booed.

"......"

"......"

Ryu Chungwoo didn't act profound, saying a word or two as if he had reached enlightenment after long agony, nor did he launch into a long solo speech. He probably didn't feel the need.

We had all experienced the process of Ryu Chungwoo recovering that 'feeling' together. From the countless conversations to the performance itself, we were there. There was no reason to start the story all over again.

*Self-confidence was the keyword.*

So within this group of 'his people', Ryu Chungwoo had simply returned to his natural, confident, and gentle demeanor. He was back in his rightful place.

"...Right. You're someone who lives in reality."

"Ohhhhh."

"Ooh~ A famous line!"

Thanks to that, the target of the other guys' admiration shifted. Bae Sejin's face turned bright red, and Ryu Chungwoo burst out laughing as he watched him.

"Haha, thanks!"

His laughter was refreshing. His words transitioning to the next topic were just as crisp.

"The next schedule is... hm, the Summer Festival. A broadcast station event."

"Yes."

"Let's get the outfits first. I'll tell the manager."

Watching him meticulously start the briefing, I just let out a small laugh.

It seemed that was enough for us.

However, there was an exception. It seemed he also had someone he wanted to express his feelings to in a long post.

[It seems like Chungwoo is okay now, right? No, no, let's not jump to conclusions, it might pressure him ㅠㅠ]

[Ah ㅠㅠ I'm worried he might be overdoing it.. but I'm so happy]

[Chungwoo-yah, I love you]

He aimed it at the fans who still wavered between worry and joy after seeing his recent performance.

That night, a long post was uploaded.

***

"......"

In the middle of the night, after all his schedules were finished, Ryu Chungwoo sat before the laptop Park Moondae had carefully returned to him.

It felt a bit like getting a confiscated game console back from his parents.

*It's actually mine, though.*

The feeling wasn't bad, just a little embarrassing. He soon regained his composure and calmly began to write, his calm face reflected on the monitor.

——————————

[This is Ryu Chungwoo.]

Hello. This is the first time I've written a post like this since the hiatus announcement.

First, I want to say I'm sorry for making you worry.

I think I couldn't bring myself to write a long post because I was worried and anxious about what I should say.

——————————

Ryu Chungwoo paused after the first paragraph, then added a sentence as if he had never hesitated.

——————————

It was not a happy time.

——————————

Unrefined words flowed from his fingertips. He wrote about the bewilderment of facing sudden physical difficulties on stage and the sense of failure when he couldn't control his own body.

These were stories the usual Ryu Chungwoo wouldn't have bothered to write. He laid them out calmly, without excessive or unnecessary details, centering them on his feelings. He did this because there was a conclusion he wanted to reach.

——————————

I was sorry that my feelings and condition caused you to worry, and while I was grateful, my heart ached.

So rather than worrying,

——————————

The truth is.

Ryu Chungwoo took a breath and then exhaled.

——————————

I found myself wishing that you would rush me.

——————————

The typing continued, straight and true.

——————————

I am not weak.

Please rush me, tell me there's no reason I can't recover, that I can do it so I should hurry back.

*When will Ryu Chungwoo come back? How much did he practice during his break, how much have his skills improved?*

I hope you will expect that I can naturally do so.

Just like you always have.

——————————

So that I can realize for myself what kind of person I am.

——————————

Writing it out like this, it sounds very childish, but I won't take it back.

Please believe in me.

——————————

After uploading the post, Ryu Chungwoo slept soundly.

He woke up at dawn for a light workout, showered, and then woke the other members. After a short briefing, he got in the car and checked the day's schedule.

"We have a live audience today, right?"

"Ah, yes!"

He diligently carried out his schedule.

Whenever he had a short break, he practiced his moves and vocals, finding strengths and weaknesses in his performance and gradually improving them. He felt the joy of daily growth.

"Ah, Chungwoo. I heard your mental state wasn't good, but isn't that actually a physical problem? Who was it? That person who had a panic attack was like that too. Maybe..."

"Ah, I'm fine."

Even if stress built up sometimes, it was okay. He skillfully managed his condition, relieving frustration with a hand gripper, a stress ball, or short conversations with the members.

That didn't mean he let his guard down. He scheduled a counseling appointment to consistently manage himself for any 'just in case' scenarios. He would no longer worry, 'What if the symptoms really appear again?' It was an unnecessary concern.

Even if frustration from his circumstances were to strike again, he could get back up.

*That's right.*

Even when his archery career ended, he got back up and challenged himself to become an idol. Even when lost memories held him back, he recovered his sense of reality. He had overcome all difficulties to be here.

Just like always.

He always moves forward one step at a time. He doesn't postpone the path he must walk now, and he doesn't hesitate.

He doesn't stop.

"Hyung, standby!"

"Alright!"

For Ryu Chungwoo, that was enough.

He had members who naturally blended in with that side of him.

"Did you see the post Ryu Chungwoo uploaded?"

"Of course."

Park Moondae recalled Ryu Chungwoo's letter, which had been on his smartphone screen just moments ago.

The final part.

——————————

I will do well.

So that you can believe these words without any more anxiety, I want to see you on stage again as soon as possible.

On the day we meet in person, I want to show you a performance with skills that surpass everything I've shown until now.

Please look forward to it. Loviewer.

——————————

"......"

It was a sufficient post.

Park Moondae saw Bae Sejin and Seon Ahyeon wanting to comment on the post, only to stop each other. He decided to keep it a secret.

"Right, let's hold back!"

"Yes...! Since Chungwoo wrote his feelings so completely...!"

He also saw Lee Sejin glance at the scene with an expression similar to his own before whistling and walking past.

"......"

Truly, the group's atmosphere couldn't be better.

*It's running smoothly.*

I meant that sincerely, not sarcastically.

Park Moondae let out a small laugh.

Ryu Chungwoo had recovered. The group's activities were going well, and they had crushed their rivals, the festival headliners, in a head-to-head battle for public attention.

However, looking at it objectively, it didn't seem like all of this would directly lead to album sales.

This was because they hadn't performed the original song, and the viral remix felt completely different. Moreover, none of their previous title tracks sounded anything like the performance's old-school hip-hop. There was no connection. The buzz would just float away into thin air.

*The buzz is only focused on the group's image.*

If things continued like this, TeSTAR would likely absorb this buzz well and then promise results with the next album, saying things like 'next time for sure'.

However.

*That's only if you look at the present moment...*

Park Moondae was neither shaken nor disappointed. He hadn't expected the Glastonbury appearance to go this well in the first place. He wasn't enough of an optimist to base his plans on such an uncertain factor.

*Still, I can make more use of this.*

He began to carefully consider how to use this buzz as a stepping stone for an even greater effect.

"Moondae-hyung! Cha Eugene is strongly insisting that he wants to eat rabokki for breakfast tomorrow."

"Tell him no."

"Yes!"

That night, as he got into bed, Park Moondae chuckled and picked up his smartphone.

*Is it about time?*

[Bad feelings (ver.G)]

[The crazy Glastonbury version!!!]

[AAAAAHHH]

[I'll go and share this happy news with the international fans]

Excited fans busily spread the surprise news, delighted by the fan service. Some also enjoyed a calculating pleasure, wondering if this might increase album sales a little.

But some people lingered on the download page for longer...

[Uh, wait a minute]

[D-7 until the next Hidden Code]
