# Chapter 438

*A senior from my club, someone I only knew from getting drinks, just appeared on a video call as my fan, and right now, I'm an idol. This is incredibly bewildering.*

However, years of professional training made my mouth say the right thing.

"...Yes. Seojin."

[Yes.]

*This is crazy.*

Seeing Ryu Seojin's satisfied face on the screen only made me more unsettled. I remembered that it was because of something she said at a drinking party that I successfully got cast in the ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ karaoke episode.

*Did that senior... even like idols?*

I was too busy trying to make a living to care about my club members' preferences, but I thought Ryu Seojin mostly just took photos for her portfolio. She definitely never gave me any work to shoot on her behalf.

And yet...

*Did she... just keep it a secret for her social life?*

She was even a fansite master. The account "Puppy Bear" posted photos of Lee Sejin and Park Moondae. It was famous enough that even I remembered its name from its many well-known shots.

*Judging by the posting schedule and management style, it seemed like TeSTAR definitely wasn't her first idol group.*

I didn't know if I just failed to notice because I wasn't interested, or if that senior was just good at hiding it.

Anyway.

*...No wonder the composition of her photos looked so uniquely natural.*

Was it because we learned similarly in the club? No, let's stop.

*Get to work, work.*

I had to think of her as a grateful fan who participated in the event. I couldn't let any hint of strangeness slip into a live video call that was being recorded.

*If I just think of her as a fan...*

But even then, a question remained. Why did she submit her story to me? And not Lee Sejin.

*Didn't she like Lee Sejin, and was just including me, the first-place winner, to expand her activities?*

Then I realized what must have happened.

*...Because we rushed the event, we must not have set a one-application-per-member limit.*

Someone with a meticulous personality might have created and submitted a custom story for every single member. This gave journalism and broadcasting majors an undeniable advantage.

...And the story that senior, no, that fan submitted to me was...

[As someone who chose a similar career path to my older sister in high school, will I ever be able to accept and love myself?]

She had written a very plausible story. She compared her competition with her sister and her own low self-esteem to my experience of debuting in first place through a survival show.

Of course, the identity of that older sister was the ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ writer, Ryu Seorin.

[First of all, my sister is a very goal-oriented and driven person.]

*Yeah, I agree.*

I almost twitched, thinking of the master-of-aggro writer at the peak of her career. However, I hid my reaction and nodded deliberately.

"She sounds like a wonderful person."

*I'm amazing myself.*

[Yes. That's why I think it was a bit difficult for me to work in the same field. I ended up comparing myself to her.]

Ryu Seojin effortlessly spun her reconstructed story. As expected of a journalism major, she didn't bat an eye while telling a story fabricated like a job application's personal statement. Still, the story's general flow was clear, making it easy for me to respond.

"I think I had similar thoughts when I first joined the idol survival show, looking at the other contestants."

As I calmly went along with the flow, it felt more and more like we were following a script, and things got better. By the time the end was approaching, I had completely regained my composure.

"...Seeing you talk about your worries like this, I think you're just as driven as she is, Seojin."

The call ended with a beautiful, predictable conclusion: it's okay to believe in yourself more and not compare yourself to others.

And the fan on the screen listened with sincere attention, despite receiving typical, broadcast-style idol feedback for a fabricated story.

"......"

*Right. Whether she's my senior or not, I shouldn't forget that she's a hardcore fan who willingly spends her time and money on me...*

[In that case, could I maybe get a puppy version of a 'fighting!' cheer from Moondae?]

The corner of my mouth twitched.

"...Of course."

*Fucking hell, really.*

I didn't know what to call this. Humiliation?

"Fighting!"

However, I willingly pinched one cheek and flapped my hands above my head to cheer for her. My mind went blank. After a few more signature fan sign motions, the first call ended successfully.

[BEEP!]

"......"

*I feel like I've been reduced to ash.*

Completely drained, I lay on the floor for a moment. The staff didn't stop me, but then the door burst open.

"Moondae Moondae, this is fun, isn't it? It's pretty meaningful too~"

The guy from the next room, who must have been waiting right outside, exclaimed excitedly.

"I just did a call with your fan."

"......"

What a shame. If Lee Sejin had come in sooner, she would have gotten more for her money, considering the time she spent writing her submission.

***

The first call freaked me out, but the work itself was reasonable. Sitting in a villa with staff and talking to a fan for a few minutes? I'd do this even without getting paid.

In fact, each member was only assigned seven fans. We had agreed to take that many people to get one or two usable clips for the concert VCR.

*We probably could have picked more.*

The schedule was five to ten-minute calls per person with a ten-minute break, so it would all be over in two hours. It felt like a waste of the effort put into organizing it.

I looked at the screen, smiled, and nodded.

[Our Moondae, have you ever had a hard time living in the dorms?]

*No matter how I answer, it's a minefield, but I know there's no malice behind it.*

"Hmm... Once, a bug appeared, and the members wouldn't let me in the room, so we all had to sleep in the living room."

The answer was a non-sequitur that seemed related.

[Gasp! Who?]

*Nice, she took the bait.*

And so, I finished another video call by sharing an anecdote.

I mostly chose relatable stories with clear cause-and-effect structures, then picked from them randomly. About half the callers were people who, like the one before, had never considered talking to an idol, which was refreshing.

Of course, there were some familiar faces too. Surprisingly, the first fansite master appeared on the fourth or fifth call.

[Oh my gosh, hi, Moondae!]

"Is this the story about how you've started to like an idol other than me?"

[...?! W-What? No...??]

It was a joke. Her story was about her worries over dropping out of high school because of a friend and her future career. Not even three minutes in, the counseling session devolved into a typical fan sign chat, but I didn't cut it short and just went along with it.

[Thank you so much, Moondae! Really, let's see each other again!]

I just wanted to.

And I felt it anew.

*...Has it been a while?*

It had been a long time since I'd done something like this with TeSTAR, well over a year. After all, since we returned from destroying the System, we had only been touring without releasing an album. This novel experience made me feel like I was truly a part of TeSTAR again.

"......"

*Well, let's not hold back and keep working hard.*I quietly got my hair touched up and then stared at the smartphone again.

It was already the last counseling session.

And this person was...

[Ah, hello! Wow, oh my gosh!]

"...!"

The face on the screen was one I had seen just a few days ago. The person I had seen by the light of a lightstick between dark concrete walls.

"...Hello."

She was the fan who was a graduate student.

[This is the first time I've ever won something like this!]

Her bright face had never experienced a collapsing building. I clenched and unclenched my fist under the table.

The excited fan shared some of her personal stories before getting to her worry. I already knew her stories. They were the same words I had heard from beyond the collapsing supply room.

*It seems she's always had such a bright and optimistic personality.*

I spoke without thinking.

"...So you're a graduate student."

[Yes!]

Her worry was about her studies. I had chosen her thinking that talking about it from the perspective of Park Moondae, who took the qualification exam, would offer a new viewpoint.

[But I don't recommend it...]

Ah, she was talking about graduate school.

I smiled faintly.

"I think it's amazing to have research or studies you want to pursue. That means you're an amazing person."

[Uwaaah.]

It was a strange sound, but one I hear at every fan sign, so it didn't matter. Just as I had with the others, I listened and responded with as much sincerity as I could.

When the end of the call was announced, the graduate student, her face now much more relaxed, said with sparkling eyes,

[Thank you. Really...! To be honest, I'm still struggling with it, but I feel like I get the strength to overcome these worries from Moondae and TeSTAR. I really do.]

The fan on the screen smiled brightly.

"No, I'm the one who should be thanking you."

It really wasn't bad.

Only after the call ended did I realize this fan hadn't asked for any special lines or poses.

*Damn.*

She didn't get her money's worth.

*Maybe I'll see her at a fan sign next time.*

In any case, the video calls, which started at 7 PM, finally concluded late at night. After filming a light ending cut outside the room, the day's shoot was over.

"You all worked hard~"

"That was fun!"

Thanks to the work not being too strenuous, the staff's expressions were bright. The members were the same.

"I think it'd be nice to hold fan meetings like this sometimes. Lining up at an in-person event doesn't really feel like a one-on-one conversation."

"So we're diversifying how we meet our fans using modern tools."

"I... liked it...!"

*Looks like they had a pretty good time.*

I chuckled and scrolled through my phone, looking for today's reviews.

[I was asked by Baese to recommend a textbook for my major. I'm not taking questions.]

[Asked Chungwoo for a heart challenge. It was embarrassing to say something so X-ing unrelated to my problem, but it was a truly worthwhile embarrassment (video)]

[Kim Raebin you crazy bastard how dare you have a ponytail? You call me noona? I'm Son Min-su-ing that clavicle knit]

Looks like everyone else had an impressive experience too. I kept scrolling, reading reviews about Lee Sejin being a 'KakaoTalk boyfriend profile pic generator' and Cha Eugene being a 'top-tier instructor for travel Spanish'.

The last guy subtly asked.

"Moondae, are you having fun right now?"

"......"

This was his way of saying I owed him something delicious for sneaking me the smartphone.

And it wasn't just Cha Eugene. The atmosphere already felt like a pre-company dinner.

"Can we get delivery here? If not, I don't mind going out to buy something."

"Ah, we'll go get it. We were about to go to the mart anyway."

The charity concert staff, who had joined the conversation, boisterously offered to go buy pizza. I was grateful but suspected they were just running away from a company dinner with unfamiliar celebrities. A natural move for an office worker.

Anyway, it was after we saw off the hastily departing staff.

"Then before they get back, let's set the table..."

"Ohhhh, the cat is here again! We'll take responsibility for this cat!"

"Eugene, Bae Sejin is allergic to cat fur..."

"Oh."

After a moment of cat-chaos, we had made some progress with the dinner preparations.

DING-DONG

The doorbell rang. The guys setting down cups quickly raised their heads.

"Hm?"

"Are they back already?"

Impossible. The staff left only ten minutes ago. The pizza wouldn't even be in the oven yet.

*They must have left something behind.*

My crime was being closest to the entrance, so I went to get it.

DING-DONG.

*I get it, I'm coming.*

I quickened my pace and immediately grabbed the doorknob.

CLICK.

Light and sound poured from the cracked-open door.

"Hello!"

That voice was...

"Surprise~"

"Wow, applause."

"......"

I froze.

The members of VTIC stood unannounced outside the villa's front door, dressed in casual clothes.

*What the.*

"Moondae, hello!"

*What is this situation.*

Cheongryeo smiled brightly and waved.

*Even as I thought it, I wondered what kind of fucking bullshit description this was, but it was reality.*

And I saw the cause behind them. Bright lights shone above a camera, held by the same staff who had left earlier.

*Oh.*

I understood at once and slowly lowered the hand I had almost clenched into a fist. The truth was, we had agreed to do a small surprise stage at the end of the charity concert VCR...

*...Was this how they planned to do it?*

I didn't know we would be the ones getting surprised like this.

"We're here for a special stage with TeSTAR!"

In front of the lights, Jin Chaeyul smiled sweetly and waved.

*Did going to the military make you lose all fear?*

It was a strange, somewhat irritating reunion. However...

"...Welcome."

"...!"

*There's no reason it can't happen.*

I let out a small laugh and stepped aside.

Let's do it.
