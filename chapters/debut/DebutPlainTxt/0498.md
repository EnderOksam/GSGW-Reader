# Chapter 498

The ❬Nice to Meet You❭ Stage Competition.

[Your weekend afternoon delight! A meeting of stars, a collaboration for the ages, the ❬Nice to Meet You❭ stage is about to begin!]

The MC fluently read the standardized script, honed by years of experience. A fitting response of cheers and applause poured from the audience.

[This competition's theme is 'Millennial 2000'! The stages have been designed with the 2000s as a theme.]

[The performers chose artists from genres they wanted to work with, forming pairs to make these collaborations happen. Please look forward to seeing what kind of stages have been created.]

The MC then quickly summarized the voting rules. It was simple. If you liked the performance, press the button.

It was a cliché speech for every recording, but this time, quite a few audience members heard it for the first time.

*I can't believe I'm at a place like this.*

*Thanks, Jaehyun. I'm finally being a dutiful child.*

They were all people who applied after hearing news of Cheongryeo's appearance. In other words, many were VTIC fans who were not regular viewers of the show.

That fact was also a double-edged sword in this competition. If Cheongryeo won first place through the audience vote without a worthy performance, it would cause controversy.

However, the fans sitting there had almost no such worries.

*It's Jaehyun, though.*

It was a faith that grew stronger the longer one was a fan. VTIC, and especially Cheongryeo, had never done anything disappointing.

[Now, the curtain rises on that special stage.]

Finally, the third themed competition of Season 7 began.

"WOAAAAH!"

With polite cheers, the singers and masters came out to perform. Here, 'singers' mostly meant established artists popular with the middle-aged and older demographic. This meant there were no male idols like VTIC.

"......"

Because the fields were so different, even Cheongryeo's fans watched quite favorably, feeling no rivalry.

*This is pretty good.*

*They're all really skilled.*

They made pure evaluations freely. There were quite a few unconventional stages as well.

A representative musical number from the 2000s, sung by an opera singer and a rapper of yesteryear, had a certain flair that made the old feel hip. The collaboration between a master acrobat and a dance singer, while perhaps a bit disharmonious, was quite impressive.

*Wow!*

It was especially striking to the younger VTIC fans. Since they didn't watch the program much, they were unaware of its characteristic, standardized stage compositions that 'played well with the audience', which made it feel all the more fresh.

They began to imagine Cheongryeo's stage in their own ways.

*Who did Jaehyun team up with?*

*It would've been so cool if he'd worked with an opera singer.*

Singers occasionally paired up due to a lack of masters or mismatched pairs, but no one thought Cheongryeo would be one of them.

*Hmm, to do better than these people...*

One or two stages were so good that they even started to worry that Jaehyun might make a mistake.

As time passed, the sixth stage arrived. The one right before the last. The MC finally opened with the long-awaited introduction.

[The performer for this stage is... a global K-pop artist representing Korea. It's VTIC's Cheongryeo!]

UAAAAH!!

WAAAH!

The fans, who caught on the moment the word 'global' was mentioned, were already cheering enthusiastically.

Even the middle-aged and older audience members who weren't necessarily fans reacted positively. Everyone had heard of VTIC's name, whether on the news or on WeTube. The anticipation for a famous celebrity was palpable. Those gazes tried to gauge just how well he would do.

Amidst the overflowing adrenaline, the MC controlled the tempo and continued.

[And the collaboration partner Cheongryeo has chosen is...]

A short pause built suspense. When the audience's curiosity was at its peak, a sound like a bolt from the blue fell.

[The Nation's Idol! It's none other than TeSTAR's Main Vocalist, Park Moondae.]

"......"

"...?!"

Huh?

Unlike the casual reaction from the older audience members, like 'Oh, the young idols are performing together~', Cheongryeo's fans in the audience were internally screaming, doubting their own ears.

*Why is TeSTAR showing up here...?*

*Cheongryeo?*

*Hey, Shin Jaehyun!*

One fan even screamed that a kindergarten recorder prodigy would have been better.

[Well then, let's see them now! Collaboration, start!]

Without a moment for them to compose themselves, the stage began with the MC's shout.

Lights came on over the dark stage.

"...!"

The fans reflexively held their breath and focused their gazes on the stage. However, there was no singer. A single standing microphone stood alone in the spotlight at the edge of the stage.

WHOOOOSH-

A brief sound of wind echoed. A familiar piano riff filled the void. It was a familiar accompaniment.

Cutting through the quiet background music, a chorus in a minor key began.

[Snow falls in my winter]

Wrapping around both cheeks

A cold yet cozy warmth

With you

It was a hit song by a sentimental hip-hop group popular in the mid-2000s. The song followed the formula of having a famous female solo artist feature on an addictive chorus. That featuring part was recreated, carried by a wonderful male voice.

[Um, umm- Um, umm-]

Um, umm-

A lonely hum. The sentiment was endlessly close to the original, which sang of reminiscing about the bittersweetness of love after a breakup.

"......"

The one singing was not Cheongryeo.

*Is that... Park Moondae?*

Despite the minor key, the voice was so refreshingly clear that the audience momentarily leaned in to listen. The gentle piano accompaniment and humming softly echoed through the audience.

[Yes]

The accompaniment returned to a major key. It was the moment where a fast rap should have come in with a band sound in a brighter atmosphere.

[No]

"...!"

The notes fell even heavier.

THUD.

A fluid and somber piano accompaniment settled heavily. Over the heavy minor key notes, the tempo of the accompaniment dropped, becoming even slower. It was leisurely and dark, to the point of feeling slightly dangerous.

Then, a figure appeared.

[On a night with the lights out]

Our yearning hearts

"......"

Cheongryeo!

The singer, wearing a head mic, walked in from the edge of the stage. His movement seemed to fill the stage all by itself. The ultramarine stage shirt went wonderfully with his black slacks.

*Aah!*

As the audience's suppressed cheers erupted here and there, a few people were puzzled.

*Huh?*

The song Cheongryeo was singing. The melody was indeed familiar. However, it wasn't 'Winter love', the song that had been playing earlier.

[I hear the voice of love]

❬Heat of a Summer Night❭.

Like the previous song, it was indeed from the 2000s. However, it was a song by a female solo artist famous for her sexy concepts.

Moreover, the choreography and song unfolded with a slow tempo and heavy accompaniment, unlike the original's fast-tempo techno dance track.

[Summer night]

A hot and ecstatic moment

Movements hinting at contemporary dance or waacking unfolded along his solid physique. Dancers came out and filled the stage doing the same moves, but that only added to the splendor.

The movement of the lights, the unwavering live vocals, and the dance, where every single detail was precise and the dynamics were so perfect it felt cathartic. With that alone, the stage felt tightly complete. The characteristic naturalness of someone who has stood on stage for a long time captivated the audience's gaze.

[I've been waiting, you know]

While condensing the splendor and sexiness of the original, the wild beauty of the much slower, more static, and heavier tempo added a layer of artistry. The characteristic tackiness of old pop songs, which could have easily sounded frivolous, was suppressed and gone without a trace.

*This is insane.*

*He could have just done this alone!*

*Then why was Park Moondae at the beginning...?*

The fans, lost in their stream of consciousness, immersed themselves in the stage.

[The moment becomes eternity]

Burning the flame of love

Summer night

The seemingly relaxed yet weighty performance gracefully led the stage, and the first verse's chorus ended in the blink of an eye.

Then, the music paused for a moment. Cheongryeo walked toward the standing microphone at the edge of the stage, his shoes clicking.

"......"

The audience held their breath again, watching him and waiting for the next catharsis.

Suddenly, from the opposite side, someone else appeared under a spotlight.

"...!"

Park Moondae.

The singer, his soft blond hair neatly styled, grabbed the standing microphone on the opposite side. The accompaniment flowed again.

[My love is in winter]

Even when spring comes, in my heart

"...?"

The previous accompaniment used for Cheongryeo's ❬Heat of a Summer Night❭ continued seamlessly, without any sense of disharmony. Park Moondae's melody blended in, astonishingly and naturally.

*Huh...?*

It was because they had connected two songs from the same era that used a similar compositional grammar. However, the mood and lyrics were polar opposites. Thanks to this, a very strange atmosphere formed. A narrative point had been created.

A brief love on a summer night and a farewell in an eternal winter.

[A snowdrift, not melting]

The warmth of an eternal winter

Oooh~

A clear and solid strength was added to the lonely voice, and it rose with the accompaniment. The vocal ad-libs, free of excessive technique, rushed toward the climax in a way that was pleasing to the ear.

[Snow falls in my winter]

Wrapping around both cheeks

A cold yet cozy warmth

The sound rushed to a high note, effortlessly stretching into an ultra-high note that was elegant and beautiful.

[Yes, you]

From there, the notes and lyrics continued.

[You and me]

in summer night

Cheongryeo's song cut in. The two of them continued to sing their respective songs alternately, as if they were singing parts of a single song.

[You can't miss this chance]

At the end of the rising notes, two huge spotlights shone on the center of the stage. The two people on stage moved.

"...!"

The two, now in the center, moved in time with the building accompaniment. It was mirror choreography.

[Summer night]

Hot and ecstatic

(I hear a voice)

The movements mixed, and their lyrics intertwined.

[My love is in winter]

(in summer night)

Before long, the two had swapped songs, then naturally picked up and continued singing.

[The moment becomes eternity]

(The warmth of an eternal winter)

And the final verse.

[And you.]

The performers faced each other and stopped the choreography. Then, they harmonized, lifting the song.

[Yes, you!]

You, you, u- uu, umm, umumm,

The string instruments swelled emotionally. The choreography also reached its climax.

[Um, umumm, Um, umumm, um, Umm...]

The humming, a mix of the two songs' melodies, exquisitely created a harmony and then faded. Their two movements fit together like a mirror as they executed a complex pair choreography.

It was a performance where their feet crossed, torsos bent, and they grabbed each other's shoulders, filling the empty spaces like an acrobatic feat without a single error. All those movements, which at a glance seemed soft, could only look elegant with the strength to perfectly control one's limbs.

To perform this choreography while delivering not a high note, but a 'hum' in perfect-pitch live vocals, was a sight that seemed almost surreal.

*Wow.*

"......"

Gazes were fixed, as if being sucked in. It was a composition that only a performer with an extremely high level of understanding and skill in K-pop stages could pull off.

*Ah...*

And so, a few of the fans understood why Cheongryeo had chosen Park Moondae.

*So that's why.*

*If he wanted to do something like this, he would have had to choose an idol.*

However, there was a misunderstanding. In truth, it was impossible for Park Moondae to execute the choreography for this climax part alone with his current stats.

However, through sheer practice, he had managed to cover it, enabling him to copy and dance the short 30-second choreography 'exactly like his partner'. Moreover, this was possible because the person who had spent decades developing methods to effectively drill choreography into his members had personally crammed in each and every move. For a moment, an unbelievable recreation was possible.

At the end of that 'unbelievable' synchronization rate, a short, definite high-range harmony soared over the splendidly built-up accompaniment.

[A hot and ecstatic moment]

I've been waiting, you know

Catharsis!

[BRA-DA-DA-DA-DA!]

In time with the brass accompaniment, they stood back-to-back and faced the audience. The stage that began with winter met its end with a summer night.

THUD!

"......"

And just like that, the performance was over. It was a composition that poured artistry fitting for this program into the framework of an idol stage. That atmosphere! And the exquisite arrangement, the live vocals, and the acrobatic-like choreography.

It was the essence of performance.

*They killed it.*

*Insane.*

WAAAAAAH!!

Scream-like cheers filled the audience. It wasn't cheering born from being a fan, but a reaction to the performance itself.

[That was a truly amazing performance!]

Even the MC's comment sounded sincere.

Soon, the two on stage smiled faintly, bowed politely to the audience a few times, and returned to their seats with composure. It was for the voting, but in truth, the result was as good as decided.

The fans already knew.

*We can definitely vote for this.*

Even when it aired, Cheongryeo would absolutely not be criticized for taking first place. That conviction filled the fans in their seats with overwhelming emotion.

However, confusion and question marks still remained in the minds of most of the fans in the audience.

*How did he end up doing it with Park Moondae??*

*What was with that insane song selection?*

Those questions were also scheduled to be answered soon. The process of the two forming a team had received screen time in the main broadcast that weekend.
