# Chapter 445

People who clung to a celebrity's every move were just a time-honored type of stalker.

*Aren't they?*

Ever since ❬Idol Inc.❭, people had dug up everything from my one-room apartment to my official documents. This was nothing new.

Security was good inside our dorm complex, so we were safe there. The moment we stepped outside, however, this kind of thing still happened from time to time.

The problem was, the person currently inside Park Moondae wasn't me.

*What impeccable timing...*

Damn it.

I suppressed a sigh and looked up. A group of people got out of a taxi, about to swarm the convenience store.

I stood before them, my back against the door.

"......"

I recognized a few of their faces. They were definitely not regular customers.

"Excuse me?"

"What are you doing? Get out of the way."

They first addressed me as if they didn't realize I was an affiliate, but they seemed to figure it out quickly.

"Ah, what the hell."

After that, they stopped talking to me. Instead, they raised their smartphone cameras and shouted questions toward the inside of the store.

*So they're that type.*

"What are you doing? Why'd you come out? Where are you going? Moondae? Is that you, Moondae?"

"Kim Raebin! Raebin, what's that? What did you buy? Is it for a member? Or for your girlfriend?"

*Oh.*

I never thought I'd see this again from a third-person perspective.

It really hit home how intimidating this must be for a newcomer.

Remembering the home invasion incident during ❬Idol Inc.❭, I immediately sent a pop-up to the man inside the store.

[The people outside aren't normal fans.]

[Just give a slight nod and walk past. If you can't, it's fine to pretend you don't see them.]

Keundal flinched for a moment but soon grabbed the bag of ice cream from the counter. He walked toward the door as the other two casually stuck close to him.

I spread my arms to create a space for the three of them, then immediately guided them to the car.

"Hey!"

"Why is Eugene being so mean? A Loviewer is calling out to you, and you won't even answer?"

As expected, their attitude grew more aggressive with time.

*This will be a problem if it turns into a physical altercation.*

This was Ryu Gunwoo's body. If we switched back, Keundal would have to keep using it. I needed to resolve this as quickly as possible.

Let's see...

*No one's sticking to the car.*

Perhaps because there weren't many of them, they all swarmed the members. I didn't see any of the more calculating types who would wait by the car.

*In that case.*

I gauged the distance to the car, then whispered to Cha Eugene.

"When I give the signal, run and get in the car."

One condition.

"Go the opposite way."

"Got it."

A few seconds later, Cha Eugene suddenly bolted and started sprinting around the car.

"Huh."

The moment the crowd reflexively shifted their gazes and turned toward Cha Eugene, I quickly opened the car's back door for the other two.

CLICK.

I pushed them in, and that was it.

"Argh!!"

*Cha Eugene made it in, too.*

Since he had bolted ahead, he had created enough distance to get in easily.

All the members were safely in the car.

The windows were tinted, so no one could see inside. Even if they knocked on the glass and shouted, that would be the end of it.

Situation resolved.

"......"

The crowd, suddenly left like a dog that had been chasing a chicken, seemed furious. They immediately switched targets and focused on the car.

*If they put that much energy into something else, they probably would have landed a decent job by now. I'll never understand why they waste it on illegal activities that don't even make money.*

"Moondae! Moondae, what are you doing today?"

*I'm being a manager.*

I deliberately pushed aside someone who was about to hit the car and made my way to the driver's seat.

In any case, the situation was resolved without escalating.

"Hmm."

I suppose I could call it a victory born from years of experience, first as an observer selling data and later as an idol myself.

*It was my first time playing manager, though.*

THUD.

The car door shut with me in the driver's seat.

The well-soundproofed car was rather quiet.

"......"

"Let's go," I announced, immediately starting the engine and pulling out onto the road.

Keundal finally spoke when we entered the apartment complex where the dorm was located.

"Th-that was brutal..."

Kim Raebin immediately bowed his head low.

"It's not always like this, but it does happen on rare occasions. I'm so sorry for getting you caught up in such an uncomfortable situation..."

"Gasp, no, not at all! I'm the one who should be sorry for insisting on going out for ice cream..."

"Umm? I said I'd buy the ice cream! But nobody got hurt, so it's no problem. Mission complete!"

Cha Eugene exclaimed cheerfully as he tore open an ice cream wrapper. He looked perfectly happy with his melon-flavored ice cream.

*Such composure. As expected from someone who came from the land of paparazzi.*

Keundal dazedly accepted the ice cream Cha Eugene handed him. He swallowed hard, then asked in a small voice, "Uh, um. Doesn't it bother you?"

"Well," Cha Eugene shrugged. "Every job has its good and bad sides. I just need to have a fun job that I can handle well!"

"......"

"And I'm the one who can drive this."

*Well, look at him.*

"I agree with the first part as well."

Now even Kim Raebin, who had been bowing his head in apology, nodded quite seriously.

"I felt it even more after talking with my older sister, who recently changed jobs. Every profession has its own difficulties."

Keundal gave a small nod.

"So, rather than denying the downsides, I want to be thankful for the good parts and aim to make improvements!"

"...! Yes!"

The man in Park Moondae's body nodded, his eyes sparkling.

The tension had already melted away.

*...This is good.*

I watched the two of them finally start eating their ice cream, then looked away from the rearview mirror.

A moment later, when the car arrived in the underground parking garage, Keundal sent a pop-up.

[Hyung.]

*What is it?*

Since he didn't say it out loud, I expected a serious, private conversation.

However...

[TeSTAR... TeSTAR is seriously amazing. I love that it feels like they'll be successful for a thousand, ten thousand years. Really!]

"......"

...Well, I hadn't expected a message quite so full of emotion.

*This is his impression after everything that just happened?*

I had sensed it since the radio show, but this guy was no ordinary fellow. Still...

In the end, I just let out a small chuckle.

If it didn't leave him with a bad memory, then that was fine.

*Yeah. That would be nice.*

[Yes!!]

*Well, those two really are the kind of guys who could do this for a long, long time.*

"Cha Eugene, wait! That's the flavor Moondae prefers, so save it for him!"

"Umm... OK!"

It was a sudden thought, but it seemed I hadn't watched STier in my past life for nothing.

I finished parking the car smoothly.

"Let's get out."

"Yes, sir!"

And so, the ice cream and the three men arrived safely at the dorm.

Officially, all our schedules were finished.

"You worked hard. We're done with everything."

"......"

"Now all we have to do is wait to return."

"...Yes."

As I got out of the driver's seat, I did a final check and then looked back at Keundal. His expression showed he had a lot on his mind.

I had just remembered something myself.

"Come to think of it, there was something I meant to tell you after this was all over."

"...! Yes, sir. What is it?"

I began to speak seriously and saw Keundal swallow.

I didn't know what he expected, but what I had to say was important.

It was...

"As you already know, public officials aren't allowed to hold a second job due to service regulations."

"......"

"Don't report the income you made as a manager."

If a public official gets paid for a side job without permission, it's grounds for disciplinary action.

*I went through all the trouble of using my vacation days for this. I could get screwed.*

"Just process it as unpaid volunteer work. I'll pay you separately later."

"......"

*Why isn't he answering?*

Just then, Cha Eugene raised his hand from the back seat.

"Hyung, five minutes ago you were like the star of an action movie, but now you're a supporting character in a sitcom."

*For a real-life office worker, it's better to be a sitcom side character with good attendance than an action movie protagonist who gets disciplined.*

I patted Keundal on the back.

"You took today off because of food poisoning. Got it?"

"Yes..."

That's right.

And so, the turbulent 24 hours of Keundal's failed mission ended without incident.

...Or so it seemed, right up until the end.

***

I opened my eyes.

Pairs of eyes looked down at me. They were familiar faces.

Then came a slightly tense question.

"Moondae, is that you, Moondae?"

I opened my mouth.

"......"

"......"

"Oh! Welcome home, bro!"

Only then did the men standing around me seem to relax, breaking into a fuss.

I lifted my head and raised my hand. A familiar pale hand came into view. It was Park Moondae's hand.

A brief wave of relief washed over me.

*Hoo.*

I clenched my fist.

Next to me, Keundal, now back in Ryu Gunwoo's body, also seemed to let out a deep sigh of relief.

But that wasn't all.

"......"

Without relaxing my expression, I stared into the empty air.

"...?"

"Moondae?"

Sensing my mood, the others stopped their chatter and slowly fell silent.

Seon Ahyeon's pupils trembled as he asked, "D-don't tell me..."

"......"

I looked up at the Status Window.

[Sudden!]

Status Abnormality Mission Failure: Return to Original State (2)

[Everything returns to how it was, as if it never happened]

: D-?? until next start

That's right. It popped up again.

*I knew it was too easy. Dammit...*

I wondered if they'd bundled us together since we both swapped bodies. Now it was even kind enough to display that guy Keundal's mission failure on my own Status Window.

*You really think I'm going to thank you for this?*

However, there was a hint this time.

More information was below.

[Target 'Park Moondae' lost]

[Removal in progress]

[Remaining target: Ryu Gunwoo]

Exactly... our names.

*The two of us who swapped bodies.*

This... this was definitely information I could work with. I had to find a hint here. It had to be related to Keundal's mission.

"......"

*First, I have to figure out the mission's true nature before I can solve it.*

That meant I had to rack my brain to form and test hypotheses. The problem was, I couldn't dedicate all my brainpower to just this.

I suppressed a sigh and pinched the bridge of my nose.

*Only two weeks left.*

Our comeback was right around the corner. We had to prepare like crazy.

Of course, I had to prevent the hellish disaster of our bodies swapping on stage during promotions.

However, did that mean I should just drop all comeback preparations and pour all my brainpower into this mission?

*Do I look like I want to ruin our promotions?*

No way. If my own brainpower wasn't enough, I'd just have to borrow someone else's.

The problem was that the number of brains I could borrow was limited.

The other guys who knew about the situation were all preparing for the same comeback as me.

The TeSTAR members, immediately briefed for an emergency meeting, were just as pressed for time as I was.

"Our... our bodies might switch again...?"

"You think it might overlap with our promotions? All right. Then we just need to prepare a contingency plan. Or we could consider adjusting our comeback schedule a little..."

"Sejin, hold on. I think we can discuss that last. Let's check on Moondae first. Moondae, how are you feeling?"

"I'm perfectly fine. The problem is our bodies switching during our promotional period."

"Still, it's a relief that you're okay..."

This was the situation. As for Keundal...

[Hyung, there's no way I can perform on stage. Absolutely not.]

Well, that was his state.

"I know."

*I have no intention of making you, so calm down.*

I calmed the panicking pop-up message while bringing the conversation with the gathered members to a close.

We settled on a vague, stopgap measure. If worst came to worst, he could miss a day of promotions with an excuse like the flu.

*It can't be helped.*

This wasn't the time for an all-night debate to test every possible hypothesis.

So I sat alone and started mulling over what felt like a crazy idea.

*Is there anyone not preparing for a comeback right now who thinks in unusual ways...?*

"......"

There was... someone.

The next day, after some deliberation, I picked a suitable time and contacted a certain person.

[Well now, hello there.]

It was none other than Judan of VTIC.

I had contacted that strange guy who was now in the military.
