# Chapter 630

TeSTAR's One-Day Experience as Theme Park Employees.

I had considered a similar project for years, but various issues kept pushing it back. Doing it in Korea was risky. It could cause a commotion or controversy. Doing it abroad was tough due to investment costs, from securing a theme park to adjusting schedules.

*But now, the time is perfect.*
