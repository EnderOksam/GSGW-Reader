# Chapter 359

*Rattle.*

"...The scenery is nice."

"Th-there's a cow over there...!"

An idyllic rural scene passed by outside the windows as the members commented on it.

Yes. We boarded a typical tourist bus and headed for the countryside.

Of course, there were cameras all over the bus. In other words, this too would be broadcast, so we couldn't let it go in vain.

"Wow, there's a karaoke machine here too?"

"Ah, it's been a while since I've seen that."

"I want to sing! Give me the microphone!"

"Okay! Shall we bet on the scores?"

Lee Sejin smiled and lifted the karaoke microphone to liven up the atmosphere. He seemed almost desperate to create some variety-show energy.

I understood him.

I couldn't stand this peaceful atmosphere any longer.

"Come on~ Our main vocalist has to sing just one song here!"

*He was working really hard.*

"Give it to me."

"Oooh!"

I chose a popular song from about ten years ago and sang it with as much excitement as I could muster, trying to get a good score.

I was just as desperate.

*There's a possibility that we'll go on a fishing boat when we arrive...*

*Production Team, please just go back to your old ways.*

"That blue sea~ Cool breeze~"

"Oh, oh! Oh, oh!"

*Please, let's just go with what we've always done before!*

But when we finally arrived, we were greeted by a cozy, warm-looking rural farmhouse.

"......"

The picturesque country house had a blue roof, a veranda, and plenty of sunlight.

The members were excited.

"Wow!"

"It's a wonderful home with a very cozy feel!"

"......"

*No... The appearance may be fake. Yes, what was inside was important.*

But after entering...

"Here... The floor is warm."

"It feels more personal because there's a sense of life."

"Th-that's right...!"

The neatly organized rooms had an old-fashioned charm, with mother-of-pearl cabinets, white porcelain, colorful vintage candy containers, and uniquely shaped stones.

"Have you seen the refrigerator?"

"...Yes, there were a lot of things inside."

The kitchen was stocked with various sauces, rice, and side dishes.

With sunlight shining through the window, even the air was very warm. I swallowed.

*... It's better inside.*

The place seemed like the perfect embodiment of the romance and longing of rural life.

*What on earth did the art director get up to?*

Why did they create a title like "The House's Grandson" and make the accommodation so fancy?

*How dare they not pull any tricks on the cast? This was treachery of the highest degree.*

"Wow~ It's really amazing! How did they do this?"

Lee Sejin read the atmosphere and quickly switched to reacting as if he were attending a housewarming party.

*He was trying hard.*

In the meantime, the director also quietly added a word.

"Haha, everyone, I borrowed this from a resident, so I have to return it when the period is over. Please use it carefully~"

"Yes!"

*Why did he look so proud while saying that?*

I opened my mouth just in case.

"Is there anything we have to pay for this...?"

"Oh, of course not~ We paid the rent for everything! You won't have to pay a single won!"

*Yes, I was very grateful.*

*Things are really messed up.*

After a while, we finished our reactions to being asked to gather on the veranda and left.

"TeSTAR, please sit here~"

"Yes, yes~"

Then a short briefing on the content followed.

It wasn't much different from what we heard at the meeting. Living in the countryside, helping the elderly with their work and cooking meals...

*No fun...*

I would have preferred some intense volunteer work, but instead, we were essentially being paid to live here and help out.

And the production team's explanation did not end there.

"And here's how you'll be compensated~"

Before I knew it, there was a goodie bag on the table on the veranda. Ryu Chungwoo picked it up and opened it.

"What? Wow~"

"...That's interesting. Are these bottle caps?"

Inside, a circle with an engraved pattern sparkled.

"You can only use this currency here, and you can earn more by completing various jobs!"

The director pointed to the goodie bag.

"That's right~ Carrot Coin!"

"Oh, oh!"

The name had some rather dubious cryptocurrency connotations.

However, the members' eyes gleamed at the toy-like coins made from pressed bottle caps.

"Why is it called Carrot?"

"That's because it's like a one-on-one exchange with a real carrot~"

"Hahaha!"

Even the production crew who said nothing were met with generous reactions.

"I understand~ Carrot, carrot!"

"It's fresh, isn't it?"

"We'll have to manage our money together."

And then I nodded.

*Yes.*

There was no way the production team would let us simply enjoy a relaxing time without a catch.

They must have something planned involving these coins.

They would probably put us into debt or create a situation where we'd run out of coins very quickly.

Then, for maximum impact... we needed to start by spending as many coins as possible.

My brain was finally working again. I tried my luck with enthusiasm.

"For now, shall we start by looking at groceries for dinner?"

"Sounds good."

"I'll make a list of things to buy!"

First, I needed to see the prices and figure out what would make the strongest impression.

As we entered the grocery store, I held up a coin I had received from Ryu Chungwoo and meaningfully asked the production crew a question.

"Are you lending us these?"

However, the production team responded firmly.

"No. Those are yours~ They're your starting funds!"

"...Yes."

Perhaps they would make the prices ridiculously high so we'd eventually run out of coins.

So, I started shopping.

"How much is this?"

Towels were a necessity. Usually, items like these would be marked up ridiculously...

"Ah, young people... Anyway, these are worth two Carrot Coins! That's all!"

"Thank you."

"...?"

*Wait a minute.*

"Please buy me that!"

"Eugene, do you want to eat dalgona? Okay. Auntie, how much is this?"

"Wow, you're so handsome. Hm? Very handsome~"

"Aigoo, thank you! Auntie is so beautiful too~"

"Really? Then, just one Carrot Coin is enough for 7 of these!"

"...Wow~ Awesome! Thank you!!"

"...?"

*Everything was ridiculously cheap.*

*They were basically giving everything away for free.*

*What was this?*

After receiving the dalgona from Lee Sejin and almost dropping it, I immediately looked back at the production crew and tried to get my luck going.

"...It's very cheap."

"All the residents are kind-hearted."

*That meant that residents arbitrarily set prices for goods.*

*Of course they'd be generous when the cameras were rolling, since this wasn't even real money!*

*Had they completely lost their minds?*

*What were they thinking, coming up with such a meaningless system?*

"Then, shall we go in and cook quickly?"

"Moo-Moondae. Should I try it this time...? Grilling the meat..."

"...Sounds good."

*The prices were practically free, the work was easy, the house was nice, and there were no problems to solve.*

I made a comprehensive judgment.

*This show was a dud.*

*Who the hell would want to watch already-successful idols simply relax on a farm?*

No, of course, fans might want to see it... No matter what, there had to be some level of stimulation that created interest.

*There were no ups and downs.*

This bland structure couldn't evoke any strong emotions, laughter, or excitement.

In short, it was about as boring as a TV show could get.

It would be better to go somewhere amazing or do something exciting, because the show was going nowhere like this.

*This is driving me crazy.*

I stuffed the dalgona into my mouth and suppressed my salivation.

Of course, I had already tried several times to change the direction of the program before we left.

Anything from a difficult farming boot camp to a horror special would have been fine. If only we could make it even a little more stimulating.

But surprisingly, it didn't work.

– Oh, that's a bit... Right?

– Yes, I don't think that will work.

*They cut everything off.*

The production team smoothly rejected every attempt, saying they had already discussed everything with the agency.

*Why were they so determined to stick with this boring healing content when they'd been so good at pulling tricks before?*

*Why were they doing this to us when their previous show had been a quiz show?*

I felt bitter.

*Are we too popular?*

Maybe they were afraid that, since TeSTAR was so successful, making us do anything unpleasant would anger the fans.

This situation was so likely that it just left me with a bitter taste in my mouth. The dalgona started tasting bitter as well.

"Wow~ Look at this at Moondaemoondae, there's an eel here too!"

"Wait."

I decided to find work first and then do more.

In this situation, the least I could do was build our image as peaceful workaholics.

*If there's nothing to do, I just have to make something to do.*

Thanks to this, the sweet and sour pork I made for dinner, despite all the unnecessary steps, turned out amazingly well.

*...It'd be nice if we could at least do an eating show with this. Please.*

***

Attempts to somehow tie together the content continued, but no matter how much I thought about it, I couldn't come up with an angle.

It was amazingly peaceful.

*I'm tired.*

Still, something happened after dinner on the first day.

The guys who went downstairs brought something with them.

"Voila!"

"Wow..."

A smiling Ryu Chungwoo placed a dog with a black nose and white fur on the veranda.

The dog kicked my feet energetically.

"Woof!"

"......"

I almost reached out my hand without thinking, but then took it back.

It looked like a ball of hair small enough to be picked up and lifted.

"I met the camera director's grandmother, and she said this was one of the pups her dog gave birth to."

"He kept following us, and she said it was okay to bring him back!"

"I-it's name is Mungae."

"Woof!"

The dog got between me and Bae Sejin and stumbled. Bae Sejin's face turned red.

"Do... Do you mind if I hold it?"

"Here, just hold the side."

I looked at the dog's face, which looked stupid and thoughtless. Its tail was wagging wildly.

*This... It might be okay.*

*Because people liked dogs.*

Moreover, since it didn't have a known breed, there was no need for unnecessary controversy. Hmm. Good.

I nodded internally.

"Since this puppy came to TeSTAR, I think it should get a stage name~ How about Moon Puppy?"

"Hey."

And, deliberately overreacting to Lee Sejin's bullshit, I ran through the yard with Lee Sejin and the dog behind us.

"Hahaha!"

I wondered if the threshold for laughter had been set too low, but the members and staff laughed anyway.

*...Maybe it wasn't that bad.* From that day on, the dog slept in the middle of the heated room.

After that, small tasks continued.

We dug potatoes, watered crops, fed ducks, and gave massages to villagers.

And what I was doing right now was delivering groceries.

"Ugh!"

"We're almost done."

We were making deliveries to farms farther away.

Walking through the countryside with a puppy was the perfect mindless activity.

And on the way back...

"Hm, shall we take a rest over there?"

"Good idea!"

There were no restrictions, so we could use our time however we wanted.

So, just before sunset, we parked the bicycle cart and sat down in the pavilion.

"I want to lie down~"

"Me too!"

We could simply lie around and stretch this part out as much as possible.

*We can do all kinds of things.*

We would all just be lying down together on camera, and I wondered how they would even use this footage.

At that time, Seon Ahyeon, who was lying next to me, muttered briefly.

"Th-this isn't so bad."

"......"

"Without having any worries, just like right now... It's been a while since we had time like this."

"...You're right."

This whole time, we had been running hard without stopping. In this line of work, if you slowed down, you'd fall behind.

*We even plan our breaks so that we can rest efficiently.*

*It had been a long time since we'd simply relaxed without having a goal in mind.*

Time passed by aimlessly.

"It's one per person. Don't take any more."

"Boo..."

Bae Sejin had just returned from the supermarket with a bag of popsicles.

They tasted fruity.

*Sururu-sururuk.*

The grass swayed in the wind.

"It smells good."

"Yes."

It was quiet.

I lay down in the pavilion and realized that I was quite relaxed.

"......"

*Maybe this wasn't so bad after all.*

Looking back, the steady supply of healing entertainment shows probably meant they were secretly quite popular.

*Maybe people liked the feeling of getting away from unnecessary problems.*

I shook my head. And I had a thought.

*Maybe I'll just let things be this time.*

Even if I just enjoyed the entertainment show without thinking about anything else, it didn't seem like there would be much of a problem in completing the mission.

Just this time, should I trust the production team's editing skills and delegate the content?

*It could be that since I took my first step into entertainment with ⟨Idol Inc.⟩, I was only looking for things that would gain attention.*

I didn't know what I'd been missing.

I slowly enjoyed the sweetness of the ice cream and placed my head on the floor.

It was nice to simply relax together in the small pavilion.

***

Two peaceful days passed.

Around the time when the members and I were finally getting used to this uneventful life...

"Hello everyone!"

"Hello!"

The production team, who dutifully appeared every morning, called us over again.

*They'll give us small tasks to do again.*

However, today there was something that was conducted first before the daily briefing.

"Everyone, shall we play a mini-game for a moment?"

"A minigame?"

"Yes. There was a brief event that occurred this morning, so let's play a mini-game to find out what happened!"

"Oooh."

The mini-game was a simple game played against the production team.

We played it often before. When we'd won before, we'd often received snacks, fireworks, and other rewards.

So everyone naturally agreed and participated.

"Wow, it seems like our Mungae is always with Moondae."

"Haha, I guess they feel the same way because they are dogs."

"...Hyung."

Even this guy, Ryu Chungwoo, was like this.

I played without much tension, more focused on petting the puppy, and lost.

"Ah."

"Well, it's possible."

"It's okay! Let's do more work!"

No one was very upset. The atmosphere was still very smiley, with encouragement.

"Now then, let's find out what penalty losing will incur!"

"Yes~"

Until now, the penalty notes had words like '1 volunteer activity today' and 'buying corn'.

And all that was left were two notes.

"Let Raebin pick it."

"Alright!"

Kim Raebin eagerly drew one of the two remaining penalty slips.

Then his expression froze.

"...??"

"Raebin?"

"What's the matter?"

"Raebin-ssi, please read it out loud~"
