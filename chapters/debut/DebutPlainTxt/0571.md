# Chapter 571

Holding a meeting whenever a problem arose had become a daily routine for TeSTAR. It meant the members were so seasoned that almost nothing surprised them anymore.

"Huh? Oh, a meeting. Yep."

So, no one panicked or showered us with questions about the summons. They just calmly gathered in the living room as soon as their schedules ended.

Though, Lee Sejin absurdly looked at me as if he were proud.

"So that's why your face was so grim, Moondae Moondae? Still, you're quick to spill the beans now."

*If you know, then let's just get on with it quickly.*

But their calmness didn't mean they failed to grasp the situation's severity. After I explained everything, the members' faces quickly turned serious.

"...Hmm. So... the problem is the image of being on a downward trend, is that it?"

"Right."

No matter what we chose for the next album, it would damage either our fandom or our public appeal. It could signal an uncontrollable downward spiral.

Kim Raebin's face darkened.

"It's because my skills are lacking..."

"No, that's definitely not it."

*I knew he'd be like this as soon as the album was mentioned.*

The dark expression left Kim Raebin's face only after he heard the detailed explanation that this mess happened because things went too well.

Instead, he thought deeply for a moment before finally speaking.

"There's a saying that something extremely well-made transcends taste! In that case, I'll devote myself even more to writing a new song that can satisfy both the public and Loviewer!"

"Aigoo, here he goes again."

"Kim Raebin is an idiot."

"......"

I placed both hands on Kim Raebin's shoulders.

"Raebin, listen carefully."

"Yes!"

"You're a genius."

"......"

"And your songs have always been so good it's absurd."

Except for a few slumps, his talent was insane, with no weaknesses in quality or speed.

*Honestly, if it weren't for this guy, things would have been several times harder.*

This meant the songs Kim Raebin had made so far were not of a lower grade.

"But just because a song is good doesn't mean it'll automatically be a mega-hit. That's a matter of timing and luck."

"That's..."

"Hmm, Moondae is a hundred percent right! That's a universal truth that applies to many things, not just songs~ Right, hyung?"

"Huh?"

Bae Sejin seemed a bit taken aback by Lee Sejin's words, but he soon nodded calmly.

"...That's right. There was a movie I was in when I was young that was the most fun, but it wasn't the one that did the best at the box office."

"......"

"So, you can't artificially create something like that."

Kim Raebin gave a small nod.

*That's right.*

No matter how well you made something or how hard you promoted it, a trend that satisfied everyone couldn't be intentionally manufactured.

*The best you can do is set a target audience and capture them.*

Then, sometimes, luck and timing align, and everyone ends up liking it.

Cha Eugene, who had been listening, shrugged.

"I don't get it. Why make only one song? Why can't we make both?"

"Huh?"

"We can do both a hard one and an easy one. A double title!"

"Ah."

A double title. It was a method of diversifying to target both the public and the fandom instead of making one perfect song to satisfy everyone.

The reason this wouldn't work was simple.

"There's no time."

Doing a double title meant doing exactly twice what we were doing now.

*No, since we'd just be adding it on, it would probably take more than twice the effort when you consider schedule coordination.*

It meant redoing the choreography, outfits, concept, and music video shoot. We would also have to achieve a level of completeness that didn't clash with the other title track.

*So what happens then?*

"If we do a double title, far from releasing the next album sooner, we might have to release it even later than scheduled."

In the end, we'd be leaving the fandom exodus unchecked for longer.

The fundamental premise of acting quickly to stop fans from leaving would be completely undermined.

Of course, we could try to somehow meet the deadline.

"Let's work a lot, and fast! We're all hard-working people."

*By grinding our bodies to dust.*

*To be honest, I'm the type of person who's tempted by those words.*

However...

"If someone collapses from overwork, who's going to take responsibility?"

The workload would be more intense than anything we had experienced so far.

Our current schedule was already tough. If we crammed twice the work into it, someone might really collapse.

*I can't let that happen.*

*...If we're going to be in this for the long haul, I have to take care of this group's health.*

I saw Ryu Chungwoo smiling faintly from across the room.

*Are you proud? Fine. Well... if you're happy, then that's that.*

Cha Eugene also nodded listlessly, as if he had conceded.

"OK. I'll consider the hyungs' ages."

"......"

Bae Sejin opened his mouth.

"You... I'm not bringing you any more candy."

"What?!"

The members ignored Cha Eugene, who was horrified that he could no longer leech homemade candy from Bae Sejin's mother. They all fell into their own thoughts.

"So... we have to leave it to luck?"

Perhaps the idea was too defeatist for Lee Sejin to accept, as his expression was displeased, and the other members' faces were also a bit reluctant. It was because we'd never said something as carefree as 'Let's leave it to luck' before.

In the midst of it all, Seon Ahyeon timidly raised his hand.

"Um, excuse me, what if... we explained this promotion directly to the fans?"

"Hmm?"

"On W-App... or we could post a letter on social media."

In short, Seon Ahyeon's suggestion was this.

[We didn't meticulously plan this! We rushed the promotions too!]

[If you thought we were going to take it easy and be less intense because our passion has cooled, that's all a misunderstanding!]

*Let's unpack this and be honest.*

We would explain the process of releasing this song in two weeks and how we hastily prepared for promotions after it suddenly did well.

The idea was to calmly explain it to the fans and get them to understand.

"I-If we tell them what process we went through to promote like this... I thought they might feel reassured."

"And we just prepare the album step-by-step as scheduled?"

"Yes...!"

Seon Ahyeon's words were the upright, standard answer.

But there was a problem.

I rubbed my chin.

*I don't think they'll believe it.*

Doing that right now would make us look desperate, which would be counterproductive. If people think, 'These guys are trying to make excuses because they're scared,' our credibility will drop and our image will take a hit.

[Stop... it's so obvious you're making excuses, I'm getting second-hand embarrassment..ㅠㅠ]

[Ah, TeSTAR's A-list pride is all dead, for real]

[Is this really something to make a fuss about?;;; It's a bit baffling, they just needed to come back with an intense album..]

*In crude terms, we'd look like we have no sense.*

*That would be a problem.*

This was a time when we needed to choose everything carefully to properly restore our image.

And there was no need to explain this so cruelly.

"Umm, honestly, I want to go with Ahyeon's opinion too..., but to make it sound natural, we'd have to say it in passing, right? Then I don't think it would get much attention."

"Ah...!"

"Of course, we could persuade the people who listen, but right now, we need to make a strong! impression on a lot more people."

Lee Sejin skillfully deflected. Seon Ahyeon nodded firmly with a convinced expression.

"Mhm. Then, it's better to show them with definite action, I see...!"

"That's right."

But... we still didn't know how to release this damn album.

"...We can't just postpone the album either. Hmm. We have to reassure the fans."

HMMMM.

Whether we moved the album up, postponed it, or chose any concept, we couldn't avoid risks and losses.

A brief silence fell among the guys clutching their heads.

Then Bae Sejin clenched his fist.

"Ahem, how about we just ignore everything and prepare diligently? This... isn't about leaving it to luck, it's about trusting the way we've always done things."

"Hyung..."

At Bae Sejin's words, Kim Raebin blinked, looking slightly moved.

And Lee Sejin also looked at him with melancholic eyes.

"Those are cool words, but... isn't this our usual method? Diligently building a strategy."

"...Huh?"

*That's... true.*

"Hyung, this attitude of fiercely analyzing the situation and trying to respond! Isn't this the most TeSTAR-like thing to do?"

"That... hmm, I guess it seems that way."

Bae Sejin looked a bit dazed for a moment but eventually nodded. Persuasion had succeeded between the two roommates who got along so poorly. It was a rare sight.

"Besides, this industry is increasingly stimulating people's interest in more provocative ways. Like Halo Heim this time."

"Halo Heim?"

"They're a group that uses a dating reality show as their own content, and they're number one on the music charts right now."

"......"

Lee Sejin explained the co-ed group's bizarre marketing method. A horrified Bae Sejin exclaimed, "Why are entertainment agencies becoming more and more immoral?" Lee Sejin then said in a firm voice.

"Our competitors are staking their lives on marketing like that."

"......"

"Well, we could just walk the solitary path of being 'well-made' and maybe one day people will recognize it and we'll climb back up the charts... but our current situation is too precious for that."

A few of the guys nodded at his serious words.

"I know what you mean."

Bae Sejin's expression also became serious.

Only for him to shake his head.

"But I don't want to promote our album like that."

*I thought they were in agreement, but I guess not.*

Even Seon Ahyeon opened his mouth with a somewhat firm expression.

"I... me, me too."

"......"

"If we have to, we have no choice, but... it seems a little, strange. It feels like we'd be deceiving the fans."

"...Okay."

Lee Sejin didn't press further and skillfully withdrew his opinion.

"It's not like I was suggesting we use that method in the first place. I was just saying we should consider using a somewhat bizarre and provocative method."

"Mhm."

"...Right. I know."

Bae Sejin now nodded as well.

It might actually be harder now to see those two fight like cats and dogs, shouting, "Why do you think like that?" That was a good thing.

*Right, but...*

The purpose of holding the meeting remained unachieved.

No matter how much we discussed, no brilliant solution emerged.

I rubbed my chin.

*No matter what we did, it was a choice between two evils, and no matter how we released the album, there would be a loss...*

"......"

*Wait a minute.*

*An album?*

I felt a spark go off in my head.

"Why did we only think about an album?"

"Huh?"

"It doesn't have to be an album."

*That's right.*

From the promotions to the next full-length album. Since the cause and process of all this were related to the promotion period, we had naturally only considered an album, the thing that creates the promotion period, as the solution.

But in truth, a comeback promotion period with an album wasn't the only way for TeSTAR to show something.

"How much large-scale content have we made besides albums until now?"

Our own content, concerts, tours, even a collaboration game.

Lee Sejin snapped his fingers.

"Ah, right! We can just show a new and intense performance in the content that the fans watch a lot!"

"...!"

The atmosphere of the meeting changed with that response.

"Th-That's right! If we make it well, the fans will all watch it..."

"Ohhhhhh!"

"But isn't that somewhat more unofficial than an album?"

"That's the best part."

I grinned.

"It's not related to the metrics."

"......"

It meant we could do a concept as intense as we wanted without considering highly watched indicators, like digital chart rankings or album sales.

"It's content that the fans primarily watch in the first place. The general public doesn't pay as much attention to it."

They might not even know TeSTAR released something like that.

There'd be no disappointment that it wasn't the easy-listening song the public currently expects, and no comparison to Epic's chart rankings!

"...And if it does happen to get the public's attention, it just means it was good enough to become a hot topic?"

"Exactly."

*This was the answer.*

My mind raced, hammering out answers to every question. In a surge of adrenaline, I turned my gaze to the group's Leader.

Ryu Chungwoo opened his mouth.

"What's our soonest scheduled content?"

A short time later, a video was uploaded to TeSTAR's official WeTube. It happened around the time the broadcast of the programs TeSTAR had filmed for their promotions was winding down.

However, this time, no one was surprised.

[202X TeSTAR 'HUNT or BE HUNTED' WORLD TOUR TRAILER]

It was because the fans already knew this information.

TeSTAR's tour was resuming.

[The boys are starting their tour again]

[But weren't the tickets all sold out... Did they add more dates? What's with this promo video]

[Huh, the tour has a new subtitle too, Hunt or Be Hunted?]

Puzzled by the details, the fans clicked on the video. They speculated that perhaps the schedule had been pushed back and that's why it was being released now.

[Hello.]

As always, the video looked gorgeous.

An elegant, intense arrangement of their last title song, ❬Hunting❭, played as props from a classic hotel passed by. A golden doorbell, a lavish lobby, a metal key, a vintage telephone.

*Is the concept old-fashioned travel?*

Black-and-white, upper-body close-ups of the members in suits were interspersed between the props. However, shadows obscured their faces, so they weren't clearly visible.

*...*

*It was, how should I put it, lacking in stimulation.*

*There's nothing particularly new.*

The 32-second video was just a formal tour trailer introducing the concept. The fans watched it casually with an 'I see' kind of feeling.

But in the last five seconds.

Just as the final melody of the arranged ❬Hunting❭ played, a magnificent hotel exterior appeared, and the title 'HUNT or BE HUNTED' appeared in large letters.

Suddenly, a subtle color washed over the building, and a classically arranged BGM began to flow.

*...?*

And then.

[-> Enter the hotel.]

[-> Leave.]

*...!*

Choices appeared as related videos.
