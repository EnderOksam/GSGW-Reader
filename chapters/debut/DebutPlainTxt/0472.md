# Chapter 472

Cha Eugene collapsed in the conference room and was rushed to the emergency room.

"Cha Eugene? Eugene!"

"Can you hear me?"

"......"

Cha Eugene remained completely still as they carried him to the car and transported him. His body, usually overflowing with enough vitality to burst from his limbs, gave no response. He was like an object. It was strange.

"How did this happen?!"

"...He just, he just suddenly collapsed... There were no other symptoms. None at all."

*Is this how it is? Do all the shitty things in my life just happen suddenly? What's the point of all that damn analysis and preparation? No matter how hard I try to prepare, when something actually happens, it all turns out to be a useless waste of effort...*

*No.*

I pressed down on my thigh.

*We haven't even gotten him properly examined yet, and I'm already acting like this. Way to go, you idiot. Fuck. Let's do something productive.*

*First, the symptoms.*

I reached for Cha Eugene's face, then checked his breathing and heartbeat.

"......"

"Park Moondae?"

I let out a breath.

"...His breathing, it seems stable."

"Ah...!"

I was no expert, but at least he wasn't gasping for air, and his heartbeat wasn't too erratic or faint.

At that moment, Keun Sejin also started checking, massaging his arms and legs.

"His color doesn't look too bad either. He's a bit pale, but his temperature seems fine, right?"

"......"

His body temperature did not feel noticeably low to me either.

The manager driving seemed to hear us and muttered frantically.

"Then it might be that thing, uh, vaso... what was it, anyway, fainting."

Ah.

"Vasovagal syncope."

"Ah! Yes, that..."

"......"

That condition was mostly caused by extreme stress or tension. I knew this for the same reason the company employee did.

"It's quite common among idols. Since it's a promotion period, it could happen to the members of TeSTAR too. You just moved companies, and your environment has changed..."

"......"

Right. It was true that our situation was complicated in many ways.

However.

*But Cha Eugene? The guy who laughed it off when everyone else looked like death during the 'Idol Inc.' negative vote, the guy who managed his own mental state even when he was being flamed for the camera violence rumor?*

*That Cha Eugene would feel enough 'extreme stress and tension' from something like this to lose consciousness...*

*No, in the first place, is this the face of someone who fainted? He looks... asleep.*

I looked again at Cha Eugene's peaceful, expressionless face as he breathed shallowly. No matter how I thought about it, it was unnatural.

"...! We've arrived at the hospital, so right away..."

"Yes!"

The car sped to a nearby hospital, arriving secretly at the emergency room. Cha Eugene was immediately moved inside.

We rushed into the hospital, following staff from another company car. Keun Sejin muttered lowly, "Let's contact the other guys... in a little bit."

"......"

He meant we should wait for the test results.

"It might be nothing. No, I think it's probably nothing."

"......"

However, I had no idea what the results would be. Cha Eugene had collapsed without any warning, and I still didn't know what was so strange about it.

*...Fuck.*

I leaned against the wall to mull over this fucking unnatural situation. However, something even more unnatural was waiting for us next.

***

"The results are in, and, uh, it seems to be a temporary loss of consciousness..."

"Yes?"

"In cases like this, they said it's usually stress-related, and while they could check his brain for a more accurate diagnosis... for now, the tests they've run show no other abnormalities."

"......"

They were beating around the bush, but it all meant one thing. For now, the cause was unknown.

"Anyway, they did say he'll probably regain consciousness soon!"

"......"

"Yes, thank you."

Still, it was a relief that it did not seem to be the worst-case scenario. Up to this point, fine, we would do more tests. The problem was what came next.

"We should contact the other members now, right?"

"...Yeah."

Keun Sejin and I planned to tell the other members about Cha Eugene's condition ourselves, thinking it was better than them hearing from company staff. However, in that process, we found out.

"...You too?"

Most of the other members we contacted had experienced similar, though milder, symptoms.

[No, I didn't completely collapse, but I got dizzy... We had to stop filming for a moment. B-But more importantly, what about Cha Eugene!?]

[...Just now, I thought I'd dozed off in the car, but maybe that wasn't it. Looking back, I don't think I was sleepy at all.]

[I thought it was a side effect of overdosing on caffeinated drinks recently... I-I don't know. I... I will head to the hospital right away!]

*What is this.*

I had not confirmed with Seon Ahyeon since I did not want to disturb him while he was still on a boat filming a fishing scene, so I just left him a text, but that was already three people.

I looked at Keun Sejin, whose face was grave.

"You?"

"I was fine. What about you, Moondae?"

"Me too."

However, when it gets to this point, it becomes a different problem.

"They're saying we should get tested since it could be related to food poisoning or an infection."

We got a comprehensive check-up before the other members arrived.

"Hyungs!"

"Raebin."

Kim Raebin, who ran over with a face that looked like he was about to cry, also got tested. However, all of our results came back clean.

"...There's no need to call the members who are in the middle of their schedules right away."

"......"

Nothing leaked to the press either. That was because Cha Eugene had not had an accident or gotten sick in the first place. He had literally just 'lost consciousness' all of a sudden.

"......"

"Uh, hyung. Here."

I lifted my head at the sound of his voice. I do not know when he had gone, but Kim Raebin was holding a black plastic bag. He took out a drink from it and offered it to me. It was a golden red ginseng drink, the kind you often see in a convenience store's hot case.

"You might wonder why I got a hot drink in the summer, but since you've been sitting in a strongly air-conditioned room for a long time, I thought this would be better..."

"...Thanks."

I quietly opened and drank the red ginseng honey tea he offered me.

Then, a resolute voice was heard.

"Cha Eugene will be fine. We once talked about his family history back when we were trainees, and he even boasted about having no history of illness..."

"......"

*It seems he's trying to comfort me. Good grief.*

I gave a faint smile.

"Yeah. Like I said earlier, the doctor also said Cha Eugene would probably wake up soon. You shouldn't worry too much either. He's a really healthy guy."

"...! Yes..."

Kim Raebin barely managed a nod, then quietly sat down next to me and started sipping his own drink. I caught a glimpse of Keun Sejin patting his back.

"......"

I leaned my back against the wall and thought.

*Right. Cha Eugene is a healthy guy. He's someone you could say is in the top one percent in terms of both physical and mental health, yet he suddenly collapsed without any warning...*

*And of all things, a majority of our group almost fainted at around the same time.*

But they said none of them have any health problems. Even Cha Eugene, according to the tests so far.

*Then, among the remaining possibilities... what is the most suspicious one? A supernatural intervention.*

I twisted my head to look at the empty air.

['For Business Use' Update in progress]

"......"

*Come to think of it. Didn't this happen right after I absorbed the 'Fragment of ■■■' from Kwon Heeseung? And losing consciousness like this... It seems similar to the symptoms that appeared when I entered the virtual world created by the System.*

I still remembered it clearly. That damn Status Window pop-up that appeared back then.

[Enjoy your daydream :)]

[Enjoy your reality :D]

The Daydream was made with coins, and the other was the System's world. Of course, the Daydream was closer to an opportunity created by Keundal, who was the Status Window, to save me...

*Wait.*

In that moment, I realized I had found someone I could ask about this situation.

*Keundal. The Status Window itself, the one who created the Daydream.*

A moment later, a response came.

[Yes, hyung! What's wrong?]

Fortunately, the response came back without much delay.

*Hoo.*

[H-Hyung, are you at the hospital right now? You're not in trouble, are you?ㅠㅠ]

*Not me, but it happened to the other guys.*

[What?!]

I briefly explained what had happened, skipping over the pop-up's reaction of screams and worries to get to the point.

*So, I'm asking if Cha Eugene's consciousness might be trapped by the System.*

This was it. I did not know the principle behind it, but the timing was just too perfect.

A quick response came back from the pop-up, which had recognized the seriousness of the situation.

[First, I'll check your Status Window right away, hyung!]

"......"

I gladly nodded.

But a moment later, he brought unexpected news.

[Uh, hyung. Right now, this Status Window, and the System itself, aren't working at all...]

*It's not working?*

[Yes! It seems like its functions have completely stopped... It says it's Updating right now, right? I think it's because it's completely reconfiguring itself. You know, just like how we can't use an app when it's updating, this thing is in a state where it can't run.]

"......"

*Then does that mean there's no possibility that Cha Eugene's mind is trapped somewhere due to the System's machinations? Because the System has completely stopped?*

[Yes!]

He added a hypothesis of his own.

[If anything, if it's really related, I think it's possible that some kind of error occurred during the Update process.]

However, that would mean that TeSTAR was being affected by the update of this 'For Business Use' thing. In other words, it could be interpreted in a very negative way.

*...Don't tell me it's treating TeSTAR as a part of this System for Company Use?*

[W-Well, rather than that... I think it's more likely that since the only thing this System has ever handled is TeSTAR's albums, it unilaterally attached excessive meaning to it...]

"......"

*Somehow... that feels a bit pathetic.*

[Anyway, I didn't find any particularly strange tricks, hyung!]

*Right. If that's the case... it would be a relief if it's really nothing, but still.*

*Anyway, thanks. I'll contact you when he wakes up.*

[Yep! Don't worry too muchㅠㅠ]

After finishing my conversation with Keundal, I looked at the Update pop-up that was still floating there. I held back a sigh and asked, just in case I might get an answer.

*How long will it take.*

Surprisingly, something did pop up.

[※Usage is restricted during the Update.]

*Fuck. I already know that.*

*Are you just telling me to wait unconditionally?*

If it was really related and Cha Eugene was going to be out for the entire Update, I was thinking of ripping out this damn System the moment the Update finished.

"Guys. Chungwoo just finished his schedule and is coming right over, and..."

"...Ugh,"

Keun Sejin's words stopped. The sound that stopped him did not come from me or Kim Raebin. It came from the hospital bed.

"......"

"Cha Eugene...!"

We shot up to our feet at once.

"The company, no, the doctor...!"

"......"

Keun Sejin, who was standing near the door, quickly dashed out to get someone. Kim Raebin and I immediately headed for the bed.

There, Cha Eugene was twisting his body to the side, his brow furrowed. He had woken up.

"Cha Eugene!"

As his body stirred, Cha Eugene was mumbling, rubbing his face with his hand as if to crush it.

[This fucking headache...]

"......"

*English?*

"Are you in a lot of pain?"

At that moment, Cha Eugene stopped moving. When he lowered his hand, his bare eyes, without contact lenses, were revealed. Our eyes met, as if he was piercing through me.

[Korean? I don't remember calling 911, who the hell are you and why did you bring me here...]

*...What?*

However, Cha Eugene turned his gaze on his own and recognized someone else.

"Raebin?"

"Ch-Cha Eugene! Are you okay?"

However, he looked just as bewildered this time.

"Kim Raebin, why are you in America?"

"......"

"What are you talking about! This is Korea!"

"Korea? [Do you think I was internationally kidnapped or something...[ ...Wait, Kim Raebin, did someone give you a brownie or something? Or a chocolate chip cookie?"

"What are you talking about?!"

"Ah... whatever. [If it's just once. It'll be fine if I just get the drugs out of my system before going back to Korea. It's not like we're a K-pop boy band anymore anyway.]"

"M-Moondae. Something's wrong with Cha Eugene!"

*I know.*

"You're the one who's acting weird, Kim Raebin."

However, Cha Eugene retorted firmly, as if he were really dealing with someone on drugs. Then he turned to me and ruffled the back of his hair.

[Maybe Moondae... 'Moondae'? Did you bring me and Kim Raebin, who unknowingly ate some weed, to the hospital?]
