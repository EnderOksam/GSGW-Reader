# Chapter 585

A con-artist PD and a spicy writer combined.

I knew it would be hell.

I just didn't know it would be this kind of hell.

What did I expect? A-list idols trapped in an endless cycle of rejections, struggling to produce a commercial jingle for an advertiser on a tight schedule. A chaotic deadline party where we would chug caffeine and taste the spiciness of capitalism.

But in reality...

"...We're out of sweet potatoes."

"......"

"......"

"...Should we run away?"

We worried about food before we even started composing.

*Is this for real?*

Before capitalism, we needed an industrial revolution.

Bae Sejin, who had carefully set down the last roasted sweet potato, squeezed his eyes shut.

"When the hell is the hunting team coming back? No, what are they even doing?!"

I figured it was time to tell Bae Sejin the truth.

"I don't know either."

"......"

"The production team just called them the 'hunting team' and took them away."

Maybe the production team just slapped that name on them because TeSTAR's last title track was ❬Hunt❭.

"...Isn't that kidnapping?"

"I suppose so."

"......"

I said it as if it had nothing to do with me. I had my own problems to deal with.

*They won't starve, I guess.*

Weren't they better off than me, surviving on smuggled roasted sweet potatoes?

Bae Sejin sat in the warmest spot on the floor and groaned.

"Anyway... Seon Ahyeon and Kim Raebin are at the house, right? Why can't I see them? Where are they?"

"Ahyeon is in the kitchen."

"...Is he cooking?"

"Yes."

I glanced at the clock and declared.

"For almost four hours now."

"......"

"He said we can't see him until it's finished."

"...Should I call the police?"

Unfortunately, we didn't have our smartphones.

Bae Sejin and I fell into a meaningful silence.

"Right... Anyway, what about Raebin?"

"He's in the Room of Dreams and Happiness."

"...?"

It would be better to show Bae Sejin than to explain.

"Let's go see him."

I got up, opened the door, and walked down the mountain cabin's hallway. Bae Sejin followed me with a slightly nervous look.

At the very end of the hall, there was a room with a nameplate that read 'Room of Dreams and Happiness'.

I peeked inside and called out.

"Raebin."

"...Hyung!"

The kid sat on the floor like he had lost his entire fortune in stocks. He shot up and turned his head.

His face brightened slightly when he saw the person behind me.

"Sejin! You're back!"

"Uh, yeah..."

Bae Sejin, however, looked bewildered.

It was understandable.

The room Kim Raebin occupied was the very picture of a luxury hotel staycation. It had everything. A large TV, a plush beanbag sofa, a game console, books, a diffuser, and even a sleep mask. The bathroom even had a portable bubble bathtub.

It was truly the space of relaxation that modern people dreamed of.

However.

"Why... are there bars on the door?"

"......"

Kim Raebin stood before the bars and said solemnly.

"I'm forbidden from giving any meaningful help to the other members..."

"......"

Thanks to that rule, Kim Raebin was being mentally tortured instead of enjoying a sweet staycation.

Seeing this, Bae Sejin hurriedly turned his head to me.

"Since when did 'Darling' mean something like that...? The way I know it... it's when you let someone play along but exempt them from elimination, right?"

"That's right."

Kkakdugi. It is a role usually given to young kids or people unfamiliar with a game, letting them participate without the bitter taste of elimination.

"It's up to the production team, I guess."

The production team defined the Darling here as 'a person who gets to slack off, participating in the shoot without doing any hard work'.

"......"

Bae Sejin looked like he would grab the PD by the collar if one appeared right then.

Meanwhile, Kim Raebin lowered his voice, hesitated, and then asked.

"H-How is the composing going, by any chance?"

"We haven't even started."

"What?"

At that moment, Bae Sejin's eyes widened as if he had realized something. He lowered his voice as well.

"...That's right! Can you give us some advice? Like, what should we start with when making a CM song... or things we should be careful about."

"If I can be of help, of course!"

Bae Sejin's face lit up. It seemed Bae Sejin planned to get advice by asking questions like this.

Kim Raebin, whose eyes had been like a dead fish's, regained a sparkle and clasped his hands together.

I just stared at the two of them with my own dead-fish eyes.

Because...

[Warning~~!]

BEEEEEP-!

"......"

A loud alarm blared from inside Kim Raebin's room. Anyone would have thought there was an earthquake.

I opened my mouth, my expression blank.

"This is what happens when Kim Raebin says the word 'help'."

"......"

"Let's go. The first PPL is for a carbonated drink."

And so, Bae Sejin began composing in silence.

"...S-Since it's a carbonated drink, a phrase like 'bubble bubble pop pop' comes to mind..."

"Sounds like detergent."

"I think so too."

They made no progress.

Bae Sejin clutched his head. His dizzy eyes just stared at the children's composition program with the musical staff on the screen.

Bae Sejin wasn't actually this incompetent at composing. He had participated in creating the top notes for a few B-side tracks on our next full-length album. It was just that...

*He has no confidence.*

It would be better if Kim Raebin were here. Bae Sejin would trust Kim Raebin to stop him if he did something completely ridiculous.

Now that the responsibility was entirely his, the pressure seemed to stifle his creative thinking. This was just as you would expect from someone whose immense sense of responsibility led to avoidance.

I should try to guide Bae Sejin toward a lighter approach.

I nudged him.

"For now, how about we try borrowing a melody from a copyright-free children's song or folk song?"

"Ah... that could work..."

It was then.

DING-DONG!

"...!"

The doorbell rang, and a bright voice called out from beyond the door.

"Guys, we're back~!!"

Bae Sejin shot up from his seat.

"That's them!"

"Yes."

The hunting team had returned.

Thinking of Kim Raebin trapped in his room, we all squeezed together and sat on the hallway floor in front of his barred door.

The atmosphere became livelier with more people, and Kim Raebin's complexion improved a little. I shrugged and spoke to Lee Sejin.

"How was it?"

*If the hunting team talks a bit about what they did, the mood should lighten up...*

"It was humiliating..."

"......"

A shocking statement came from someone who would rarely say such a thing.

What on earth did they do?

"Didn't you guys go hunting?"

"It was hunting... I guess."

"...?"

A grim silence passed.

I realized this was the same kind of silence from when I explained the situation to Bae Sejin earlier. The 'I've been through a hell of a lot' kind of silence.

Of course, being the extroverts they were, they started talking before I even had to ask.

"'Today, you're the sheep'... you know that lyric. From our song ❬Hunt❭."

Yeah.

"They did exactly that?"

"...?"

Lee Sejin covered his face with both hands.

"We wore the sheep-wool fleece we got for PPL and had a competition to see who could get hit by the most suction-cup arrows..."

"......"

So.

*These guys weren't the hunters. They were the hunted...*

I looked at the three of them, who couldn't bring themselves to lift their faces.

By throwing their bodies at flying suction-cup arrows while wearing layers of fleece on a snowy mountain in midwinter, they had personally proven that 'the fleece is warm and thick'.

"I mean... to a former national athlete..."

"Hahaha."

Ryu Chungwoo's laugh had never sounded so empty.

"I can catch a wild boar. I'd rather do that."

The shock had unlocked Cha Eugene's advanced conversation skills.

After some time passed in shock and solemnity, Lee Sejin finally took his hands off his face and said in a forced, bright voice.

"Still, we got a lot of stuff, you know? We even won a special prize and got some pocket money to use in town!"

"Won?"

"...They gave us tickets to draw for ingredients based on the number of suction-cup arrows we were hit by."

Ah.

"There were a lot of 'duds'..."

Just as the mood was about to turn solemn again.

"Guys...!!"

Just then, someone ran frantically from outside the back door. The door burst open, revealing a flushed face.

It was Seon Ahyeon, his hair stuck to his face with sweat.

"H-Hey...! I made food!"

"Ooooh!"

Everyone shot to their feet.

Seon Ahyeon's expression became even more delighted when he saw that the other members had joined.

In Seon Ahyeon's hands was the food that the kid had finally created after countless trials and errors for a full four and a half hours. It was...

"H-Here."

Grilled corn.

Five cobs.

"......"

"......"

"...They decided the menu for you too?"

"Y-Yeah..."

Seon Ahyeon blushed and lowered his head.

"There was a menu and a recipe in the kitchen..."

Three meals a day and a snack.

Seon Ahyeon was only allowed to cook from that menu.

[Today's Meal]

Breakfast: Potato Salad Morning Buns, Banana Yogurt, Fresh Tomato Juice.

Lunch: Bulgogi, Radish Soup, Seasoned Spinach, Fried Egg, Stir-fried Shredded Potatoes and Ham.

Snack: Butter-grilled Corn, Strawberry Milk.

"......"

"There were almost no ingredients for that menu..."

"......"

The only basic ingredients provided were root vegetables and canned ham.

*The sweet potatoes... I smuggled those out.*

Seon Ahyeon had attempted the stir-fried shredded potatoes and ham with what little he had, failed miserably, and barely managed to grill the corn.

"I-I'm sorry. All the recipes required fire... I kept burning everything."

"...They knew Ahyeon isn't good with fire and chose this on purpose."

These vicious bastards, seriously.

"Ahyeon is good at slicing raw fish. They should have just sent us near the sea!"

"That's right!"

"The sea is right down the hill from here!"

Even the person who had a hand in choosing this location because it was close to his drama set chimed in, his face red with anger.

It seemed no one could bring themselves to speak the cruel truth that Seon Ahyeon shouldn't have been in charge of cooking in the first place.

*It's understandable.*

What could you possibly say to the kid who had spent four hours sweating buckets, confined to the kitchen and the room next to it?

"Still, you grilled the corn well."

"...!! Y-Yeah...! Thank you!"

I praised Seon Ahyeon while holding up a half-burnt cob of corn, with butter stuck to one side and the other side squashed.

Encouragement doesn't cost anything, does it?

Of course, the team that had become sheep to earn ingredients was already worrying about the future of those ingredients.

"If we give him beef... it'll come out as charcoal."

"......"

"Noooooo."

Cha Eugene rolled on the floor, wailing that they should have won lamb from the draw. It was the only type of meat Seon Ahyeon could grill properly.

"I-I'm sorry..."

"What are you talking about, Ahyeon!"

"This isn't something you should apologize for."

"That's right~ Ahyeon! It's the production team's fault for making you do it!"

However, after saying that, Lee Sejin sneakily whispered to me.

"Moondae, is there any way to sneak into the kitchen..."

"This is my limit."

I showed him the one remaining roasted sweet potato.

Lee Sejin sank down, hugging the radish he had won.

After roughly satisfying our hunger with plain rice and corn, we finally started working on the CM song together. Thankfully, Ryu Chungwoo had won some instant rice.

"Kim Raebin can't do it..."

Let me correct myself. We started the CM song work among ourselves, with the copyright-rich Kim Raebin, who even has the Maestro trait, serving only as a totem.

This is how it's going.

"No! This is a scam! The advertisers must have signed the contract thinking Kim Raebin would be composing!"

"Probably."

There's no way this production team would have honestly told them the expected situation.

"They said that's why the other members would have to work even harder to meet those expectations."

"......"

"Wow."

"Let's sue our production team."

Even an American-style remark came out. Regardless, we worked hard on the CM song while encouraging Bae Sejin.

"Hyung... it's great that you've laid out a melody! Now that we're all working together, we'll come up with something, right!"

"Right."

Bae Sejin muttered as if he had given up on everything.

"But whatever we come up with, I'm the one who has to lead..."

"......"

Lee Sejin subtly turned his head away. Cha Eugene insisted they should tear down the bars on Kim Raebin's door, and his proposal almost passed.

Anyway, since everyone had some producing experience, we managed to create a pretty decent CM song in just a few hours, even though we were just winging it.

Kim Raebin, who couldn't even participate, almost cried.

[Oh, ooh, o-oh, Orange Soda!]

"It has a classic feel, I like it...!"

"Yeah! I like it too!"

"Yeah... I think so too."

Bae Sejin let out a sigh of relief and submitted that version to the production team, his complexion a little brighter.

"The first song is done!"

However.

[The melody seems a bit too complicated.]

[They said they wish it were a bit more flashy. While also making the 'wow' point clear?]

[They said it would have been better if the instruments were simpler.]

[Ayy~ They said the previous version was better!]

An endless hell of feedback was waiting.

"......"

The members endured it quite well. Despite feeling dejected, they would put their heads together, somehow analyze the feedback's meaning, and make revisions...

At 2 AM, the final blow came.

[For some reason, it's less exciting than the previous version?]

"...Which part?"

[Uh... overall? Anyway, that's the feeling I get.]

Click.

"...Be honest. This doesn't feel like feedback from the advertisers, does it? It feels like the production team is just spinning a wheel and giving us random feedback!"

"Calm down!"

"It's true, isn't it!"

"Sejin has lost it! But he's right!"

After all those twists and turns, we pulled an all-nighter. The carbonated drink CM song was finally approved at 3 AM the next day.

[It would have been great if you'd given us this from the start~ Ah, everyone loves it!]

Bae Sejin covered his face.

"It was almost identical to the first draft..."

"......"

This couldn't go on.

I gathered the hunting team guys and had a long talk.

"If we keep going like this, we'll get sick from frustration."

"Yeah."

The guys, who had been trembling in fear that the production team might make them wrestle a PPL elephant doll, participated in the conversation seriously. The condition would probably be that they must lose.

I also succeeded in persuading Seon Ahyeon, who had been agonizing with worry that people would starve because he ruined the cooking.

And it goes without saying that Kim Raebin, who had been enjoying his forced, tear-filled staycation alone inside his cage, eagerly joined in.

And so, that day, in the late afternoon after everyone had woken up from passing out in the early morning.

"We're going into town tonight, right?"

"...And?"

I looked at Bae Sejin and declared.

"Let's go on strike."

Writer Ryu Seorin adjusted her glasses and smirked at the PD, who was also adjusting his glasses.

The PD grinned from ear to ear, as if just the thought of it was exciting.

"Wow, then... the timing would be when they go out to town, right?"

"Exactly."

The harmonious laughter of two people crazy for views spread far and wide.

The dogfight between the two prideful, genius production staff and the idols begins now.
