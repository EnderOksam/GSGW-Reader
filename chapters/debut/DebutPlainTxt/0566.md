# Chapter 566

I made a declaration.

"So, I've called a quick TeSTAR meeting."

Clap, clap, clap, clap...

Kim Raebin, who had been clapping alone, noticed the mood and quickly stopped.

The other members sat at the kitchen table, their faces clearly marked with question marks.

"So suddenly...?"

"Hmm. Do you want to go on a trip or something?"

*No.*

"It's about an album."

The moment I said that, a look of 'Of course it is' flashed across their faces. The fact that they weren't angry just proved this group was full of workaholics. I nodded, inwardly satisfied.

This was their reaction after their reflexes kicked in and they actually started thinking.

"Wait a minute. We already have our album plan all set."

"That's right. The schedule is for early next year."

That was correct. Work on TeSTAR's next album was already underway. Our last title track, 'Hunt', was a mini-album, not a full one, so it was time for us to release a full-length album. We had decided to release it in the early to middle part of next year.

*Of course, it's normal not to have such a tight schedule.*

Around this point in a group's career, many only had a comeback once a year, if that, to minimize image consumption. Well, it wasn't just a matter of image consumption.

*The company doesn't invest.*

There's no need to be as aggressive as when they were still growing. A successful group can leisurely coast on the recognition and fanbase they've already built. The producing staff with proven skills are all pulled away for rookie development. That's how it goes. In fact, didn't we almost suffer the same fate under T1?

*Of course, that won't happen now since we have a share of this company's stocks.*

Money really is the best. It's as reliable as a hearty gukbap.

Anyway, I nodded and spoke. The album would come out next year.

"But there's something I want to do before that."

At that, a few members suddenly gulped.

*...? What is it?*

"Is it because you think some controversy is about to break out!?"

"Gosh... Don't tell me we're not going to get the Grand Prize?"

"......"

Even Seon Ahyeon nodded at me with a serious face.

"M-Moondae. If a problem has come up... I think it would be good to discuss it thoroughly together first, and then respond."

"......"

*Is this what they call karma?*

Every time I've offered an opinion, it's been linked to some crisis-aversion politicking. I get it. This time, however, was different.

I rubbed my cheek.

"Actually... this isn't an opinion I'm giving because of scores or strategic aims like before."

"...?"

"We've always done it that way until now, so I wanted to try something a little different this time."

Lee Sejin, who had been listening quietly, spoke up.

"Different how?"

"I was thinking, what if we just try doing what we want to do?"

"...!"

"Without any other calculations."

I had realized something during the recent incident.

*It's true that the members of this group are an unbelievably good match.*

A team where every member is passionate, unique without crossing the line, and gets along despite being together every day is probably rare enough to be counted on one hand, but honestly, isn't it common for even guys like them to experience burnout at this stage and just say, 'Yeah, I'm not doing it'?

*No matter how good a person is, you can't help but feel a moment of doubt when you keep doing the same thing over and over.*

You've worked the same job for seven years, but you're always passionate and composed? You should probably suspect they're on drugs.

*But TeSTAR is doing just that right now.*

In my opinion, the reason none of them have ever shown any doubt about their work was because there had been another stimulus. It was simple.

*It's because we kept almost dying.*

From car accidents to time travel, virtual worlds, and even being trapped in a collapsing building. At this point, wouldn't our activities in the entertainment industry seem like a sweet drink? Even I, having just come back from the dead, was on the verge of that illusion. It was funny that such bizarre experiences had a good side.

*But that's all over now.*

Since the System is gone, the most infuriating and nerve-wracking situations we'll face from now on will come from our activities. I thought it would be good to take some time to get our minds in order before that.

"I think it would be good if we tried an activity that is purely 'to our own liking' without being too analytical."

"......"

"That's all. Of course, if you oppose it, I'll concede."

Perhaps they never expected me to say something like this, because every single one of them was silent for a moment.

*Right. I knew it wasn't the kind of thing that suited me. I guess I'll have to pull out everything I prepared and really try to persuade them...*

"I see. I think it will be an enjoyable project!"

No, I took that back. Kim Raebin immediately raised his hand and jumped in.

*Just like the clueless guy he is... Wait.*

I looked at his expression again. Kim Raebin had a fierce expression, his ears slightly red. It was a look you often see when someone pushes through embarrassment.

"......"

*Right. There's no way he wouldn't have noticed that everyone else was silent.*

He just answered first to show his support for my opinion.

*...What a simple-hearted guy.*

However, the unexpected events didn't end there.

"Oh, Kim Raebin, you're fast! I like it too. None of us have weird tastes. It's fine."

"That's true. It's not like we'll come up with something unusable."

Following Cha Eugene, Bae Sejin also reacted as if the idea was tempting, and the atmosphere naturally lifted. There were even guys saying cringey things.

"Me too, I want to try following just our hearts for once... all together! I didn't expect you to say something like this, so my answer was a little late, Moondae."

"I know. It's a bit unfamiliar... but the fact that you're the one suggesting we try this is actually really great."

"...Thank you."

Once the answers started coming, a consensus formed in an instant. I was a little flustered.

*...I thought I would have to persuade them.*

Just a little while ago, I was almost screwed on my own. I had assumed that if I suddenly acted so out of character, I would naturally have to persuade the more suspicious members with logical arguments.

Then Lee Sejin also spoke with a smile.

"Me too~ Honestly, I never thought the great Park Moondae would say something like this."

*Hey. Your nuance is different, isn't it?*

However, Lee Sejin just shrugged and smiled.

"Hmm, still, I do believe that Moondae wouldn't do anything to harm the group."

"...!"

"Right?"

I suddenly looked around. Every single one of them had an expression that said they had no reason to refute Lee Sejin's words. Seeing Cha Eugene shout 'Yes' with an indifferent face confirmed it.

*Ah. I see.*

Then I realized. I might not know about other things, but it seemed I've definitely built up one thing well... the belief that I won't make a choice that would negatively affect this group.

"Right, Moondae Moondae?"

"...Of course."

I was, in fact, grateful.

While I was slightly lost in that feeling, Lee Sejin continued with a smile.

"Let's just give it a try with no regrets."

"......"

"Well. You all know that if we don't get the Order of Cultural Merit [文化勳章], Sejin will be the first to go to the military next year, right?"

"......"

"......"

"So let's do it with no regrets before that~"

Bae Sejin nodded with a dazed expression. I put a hand to my forehead.

*Looks like he did want to oppose it.*

Lee Sejin, who had so skillfully paraphrased 'We'll give it a try since everyone's reaction is good and you're good at your job, but you know that an activity that doesn't produce results at this point is meaningless, right?', smiled affably. Still, the surprising thing was that even he didn't actually oppose it in the end. That made me think about a lot of things.

"Alright. Then let's do it if the company says they have the capacity."

"Yep!"

Ryu Chungwoo's words sealed the deal.

I looked around. Familiar, friendly gazes looked at me.

*These guys... have a way of getting a person needlessly excited.*

So I made my declaration more cheerfully than I had expected.

"Let's throw in everything we want to do."
