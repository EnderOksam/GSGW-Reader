# Chapter 406 - The Game Master

The person who'd had the system at the time must have been on the plane with us, but he'd evaporated from my mind.

As if he'd never existed.

And yet the guy in front of me, Cheongryeo, remembered him.

*There's no way this bastard didn't know I'd forgotten.*

We'd spoken almost every day for over half a year. There was no way he hadn't noticed something was off.

He'd intentionally kept it from me. This guy's reaction right now made that much clear.

I spoke through gritted teeth.

"Why didn't you tell me?"

Cheongryeo smiled and responded.

"Because I thought Hubaenim would react like this."

"......"

*What?*

"You probably have a lot going through your mind right now... Well, aside from your anger and irritation toward me, there's probably only one other emotion."

Cheongryeo spoke casually.

"His existence bothers you, doesn't it?"

"...!"

"You're worried about him even though you don't know who he is, where he is, or what his situation is. Isn't that right?"

"Of course I'm fucking worried! The guy just *evaporated* into thin air, so of course—"

*No, wait.*

I remembered this guy's reaction when I'd brought up my suspicions that there were inconsistencies in my memories of my parents.

*He'd said that memories were bound to become distorted over time, and it was fine as long as I remembered the information I needed to proceed...*

– *What you need to watch out for is... the pesky things that come with memories.*

– *Emotions.*

And then I realized.

"...You think whether he disappears or not has anything to do with accomplishing our goal?"

"That's right."

The guy narrowed his eyes.

"Hubaenim, when something bothers you, you tend to justify choosing the less efficient route."

*What?*

"Well, shall I give you an example? Even though our group has already been formed, Hubaenim keeps looking for the other TeSTAR members. I'm sure it's not because doing so will help us win a *daesang*."

"...!"

Cheongryeo watched me closely.

"Today, you even participated in a fairly meaningless congratulatory performance at an awards ceremony that didn't make much of a splash."

"......"

"Even now, you pay so much attention to things that don't matter. Of course Hubaenim would worry about the person you got involved in all this."

I closed my mouth. The guy stood up and smiled.

"This explanation should be sufficient. If you're angry, why don't you—"

"The guy who had the system isn't doing well now, is he?"

"...!"

*This is it.*

I watched the guy's face.

"If it were just that I'd forgotten and you remembered, things wouldn't have had to come to this."

He could've simply offered to look for the person in my place, since I couldn't remember anyway.

But here was the problem. He'd gone so far as to say that knowing the truth would reduce my efficiency.

"The only explanation for your decision to hide this from me is that something happened to that bastard."

*Something... pretty bad. Something bad enough to shake me up.*

I crossed my arms and glared at him.

"......"

Cheongryeo was unperturbed.

"Hubaenim, is there any need to get so worked up trying to find out about someone you don't even know? You were never really close in the first place. The two of you."

"Don't you understand? What matters is that you're lying to me."

"I didn't lie to you."

The guy clicked his tongue. *This bastard.*

"Hubaenim, you also have information that you didn't tell me. Isn't that so? You thought it would be difficult for me to understand."

"......"

I reflexively thought of the list of emoji status messages in the status window that showed my companions' current statuses, then closed my mouth.

The guy laughed.

"It's the same with me. I didn't want Hubaenim to waste time and resources on someone you don't even know."

*Aren't you slick?*

"It sounds like you'd be willing to talk if I knew who it was. I may not remember him, but he's someone I know, right?"

"Hmm?"

The guy cocked his head.

"You don't know him right now, though."

I gritted my teeth.

Getting angry or hitting him would be stupid. This bastard was in my group now, whether I liked it or not. The moment any violence got caught by the media, it'd be a scandal.

*In that case, let's approach it rationally.*

*I have a brain. I just need to use it.*

With the system having run amok, what was the worst thing that could've happened to the missing host?

Messages appeared before me.

*The status window.*

Looking at Keundal's pop-up, I said the answer out loud without meaning to.

"He's been eaten by the system."

"......"

*Just like Keundal.*

"Right?"

Cheongryeo didn't respond. His silence was as good as a yes. I clenched my fists.

*Then...*

*There has to be a clue somewhere.*

I racked my brain.

If he'd really been absorbed into the system, then as its current user, I might have noticed something different from usual.

Something that had felt off.

Just like how Keundal had created a bug in the system and given me a coin when I was in a coma, there might have been new rules that had suddenly appeared.

*Exceptions to the rule. Things that didn't happen naturally within the system.*

"......"

*Oh, I know...*

– *This is useless. Why is it coming out?*

Something round and golden, like coins... Except it existed only as textual data within the system.

I didn't know why it existed, so I'd assumed it was just dummy data left over from when the system adopted the ⟨127 Section⟩ format.

*But what if... it was actually some kind of signal?*

*What if it was a workaround or a bug he'd managed to pull off with great difficulty, trying to leave a sign for me from within the system?*

*When I think of gold, who comes to mind?*

Immediately, something popped up in my head.

– *Well... Maybe I'll just call them Goldie #1 and Goldie #2.*

Nicknames I'd come up with on ⟨Idol Inc.⟩.

Two people who'd been in my life until recently.

*Golden Age's Ha Iljoon, and Spacer's Kwon Heeseung.*

"...!"

I suppressed an exclamation laced with profanity.

*Stay calm.*

*Which of the two seems more likely to know about the future?*

*This is too obvious.*

I opened my mouth.

"Kwon Heeseung."

"......"

He was the guy who'd suddenly won the new season of ⟨Idol Inc.⟩, and since we were from the same agency, he'd had more opportunities to meet with me.

Cheongryeo was still expressionless, but I saw his eyes narrow for a moment.

*I got it right.*

I clenched my fists and walked to the desk where he was standing.

"Now that I know, answer me."

"......"

"What exactly happened to that bastard?"

*And how on earth do you know about it?*

Cheongryeo's face grew waxen and impassive, and he slowly opened his mouth.

"Would you be willing to wait until our goal is achieved?"

"No."

"Hmm."

The guy stared at the floor.

And when he raised his head again...

Suddenly, the world stopped.

*Ah.*

I looked around.

The room had already seemed quiet and motionless, but in fact, some things were still moving.

Things like the sound of the TV coming from the living room outside, the ticking of the clock, and the subtle shifts in light.

But now everything seemed flatter and somehow completely still. A world where all movement had disappeared.

In the meantime...

"Oh, are you surprised? I was told everything has to come to a pause to show you what I'm about to show you."

Only one person was moving.

Cheongryeo.

"Put on... pause?"

"Yes. Hmm, based on what I know, it's a pretty common feature in games. You know, a pause button."

He raised his hand.

"When you bring up the settings, you stop playing, right?"

And then numerous holograms began to appear in the air before my eyes.

*Jiiiing-*

The still world was filled with small and large boxes.

But the words were mirrored, facing away from me.

Therefore... The pop-ups weren't for me to see. They were for the guy standing in the opposite direction to see.
