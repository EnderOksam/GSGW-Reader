# Chapter 461

[A crazy board game;;;;]

Fans' social media timelines exploded with news of TeSTAR's limited edition board game album. The reaction grew even more intense when word spread. After comparing images, fans realized the board game looked identical to the one from the teaser.

[Looks like they actually used it as a prop in the music video]

[Is it structured so you play the game using the photocards? I'm not sure what it'll be like. Is the TeSTAR universe coming out?]

[They really know how to sell things, hahaha]

[Wow, I absolutely have to get this]

Most reactions were excited and anticipatory, but not all of them were positive. The limited quantity was the main problem. Even though the agency said a regular version would be released later, there would surely be differences in content compared to the limited edition.

[Only 1000 copies?]

From a group that sells millions of albums?

[Aren't they thinking about the scalpers? Wow, so many resellers are going to jump on this]

[How are we supposed to buy this, for real ㅠㅠㅠㅠ]

[The agency can't shake off its T1 habits]

[Just as you'd expect from someone who came from a label under them. A chip off the old block, hahaha]

Thanks to this, TeSTAR's new agency, Orbit Entertainment, finally started getting some hate.

The truth was, the company was rushing to meet the schedule. It was simply impossible to produce tens of thousands of units within the production timeline.

However, the agency had intentionally let this negative reaction fester to some extent. It was a way to cool down the atmosphere that had been excessively deifying the label. This was a burden for both the new company and the group.

Besides, the complaints would soon die down if the regular version's contents were almost identical to the limited edition's anyway.

Moreover, since the teasers for TeSTAR's comeback came first, fans couldn't focus solely on cursing the agency. For instance, there was this.

[Oh, the album is a USB type]

The TeSTAR album released this time was not a CD but a USB. That was the only component. The configuration was simple, consisting only of the album USB, a recycled paper box, and a single photocard.

[I think they'll keep making them as USBs in the future because of environmental issues]

[Honestly, with album sales overheating, there is way too much trash being produced, yeah yeah]

However, the fact that fans couldn't receive a physical copy of the photos and lyric book felt strangely disappointing. In an era where online streaming was the norm, the reason for buying an album was more for the significance of physical ownership. The small number of components also felt shabby.

The photos and lyrics would be included as data inside the USB, but the fact that you couldn't hold them in your hand dampened the excitement.

[Hmm... 3 versions as USBs...]

[I wish they'd at least show us what the USB looks like]

[I hope it's a doll or something like that]

[Seriously, they're not telling us anything concrete but are already taking pre-orders. They should at least release some concept photos, haha]

These voices were relatively quiet, thanks to the decent quality of the teaser and the enthusiasm for continuing the high school theme from the previous, slightly disappointing album. However, a strange, deflating atmosphere created by the discontent definitely existed.

*Haa...*

In the end, the fans bothered by this deliberately tried to find more positive aspects. Part of this effort involved finding special content on TeSTAR's album pre-order page that wasn't usually in CD albums and spreading the word about it.

[They say there's an extended version of the MV in this album. Since it's a USB, it seems like they tried something with a large file size. I'm already excited]

[There's a warning related to a QR code! I guess there's something you read with a QR code ㅠㅠㅠ It seems like they prepared a lot]

However, that was it. No more specific information about the album was released, even as the comeback date approached.

*What are they thinking??*

Thanks to this, Moondae's first fansite master was at the point of biting her nails. Concept photos were released once, but they had a comfortable high school concept similar to the last teaser. It was safe and pretty, which meant it lacked distinction. It also felt overly similar to the last album.

Compared to 'Procession' which had a 30-minute teaser, 'Better me' which had a cinematic trailer, and 'Promise' which was like a movie...

*Only one teaser, a USB album, and simple contents...*

The terrifying phrase 'cost-effective' flashed through her mind.

[I never imagined they'd get this kind of treatment at the new company, lol]

[My head is ringing from this betrayal~~~ What has Gloomy-daeng been doing? I guess his gloomy act only works on the empty-headed Syeombyeol.]

[Is Syeombyeol also just going to promote for one week and then get sent on an endless tour grind now?]

[Doesn't it feel like this title track will be similar to the last one, 'Pose'? But like, a worse version]

Undercurrents swirled. As anxiety and anguish mixed with excitement and anticipation, the fateful day finally arrived. The day of TeSTAR's first comeback at their new company. Midnight.

Finally, the MV... was up.

"...Hah."

[TeSTAR 'Roll the Dice' Official MV]

The fansite master took a deep breath. The title was about dice, and the thumbnail also had dice. How far were they planning to take this?

*Is it really a full-on board game concept? At this point, I'm worried it feels like they're overdoing it... No!*

The fansite master shook off the useless thoughts and clicked on the MV with a trembling hand.

And then...

"Huh?"

A wooden floor appeared on the screen. It was the floor of the attic from the previous teaser. However, the members of TeSTAR who had been sitting there were gone. The board game was the focus. Tokens and cards were scattered across it.

The impression it gave was a bit different from when it had briefly appeared in the teaser. She had thought it was an obvious design where you advanced by conquering spaces, but looking at it now, it was more intricate, more realistic, like the blueprint of a building.

*...A prison? A maze?*

In that moment, the camera was sucked into it.

[You have my dice]

A low voice echoed through the black screen. The screen returned.

[Doong...!]

A long corridor stretched out in a straight line. Built with stones of mixed gray shades and varying contrasts, the place resembled a giant underground dungeon. A huge, rusty nameplate on the floor was briefly captured by the camera.

[Keep It Safe]

The camera moved forward.

CLOMP, CLOMP.

Footsteps echoed as iron doors lined up on both sides came into view. It was like seeing through someone's eyes.

*A guard?*

Then, the camera broke away again.

WHOOSH!

The camera passed through an iron door on the left and immediately approached a figure sitting on the floor. The figure wore a white outfit with a harness, a stage costume reminiscent of a straitjacket or prison uniform.

The moment she saw the figure with his head slightly bowed, the fansite master instantly recognized him. It was the black-haired Park Moondae, with his eyes closed.

*Gasp...!*

His slightly long bangs partially covered his eyes. The camera closed in on his pale face.

[Pipipipipipipipipi...]

A small sound started somewhere, like a machine detecting or activating something. Then, Park Moondae raised his hand, his eyes still closed. He struck down somewhere.

WHACK.

A surveillance camera exploded in mid-air. The music erupted.

[Touched by your hand, it rewinds again, My tape]

A refreshingly cool mid-high tone sang, almost cheerful. The screen also shot up cheerfully, breaking through the dark, confined room to an upper level. There, it shone on a single ray of light pouring down from a grated iron cage on the ceiling.

The tempo picked up.

[The moment my heart pounds, all my senses go Slow]

Within that beam of light, someone stealthily and nimbly descended, having hooked a rope to the end of a torn-off iron bar. It was Ryu Chungwoo, wearing a tight-fitting leather suit.

THUD.

He landed with a slightly mischievous smile on his face. A drum beat mixed in, and with a slightly more rocking sound, the bass raced forward heavily.

[An electrifying contact, searching for the clear answer in your silent eyes]

As Ryu Chungwoo touched his ear, the scene cut to someone else making the same gesture. It was Kim Raebin, wearing glasses.

[There's only one choice, yes, take it all (one more), even if you lose this tightly held hand]

Sitting in front of a dark monitor room, Kim Raebin looked like a hacker or an employee. However, he soon tilted his head with a blank expression, as if he had no idea what was going on. He then simply pulled an awl from his pocket and smashed the monitor.

Just before the monitor fizzled out with a crackle of static, Cha Eugene, in a straitjacket, looked at the camera between the distorted angles and smiled, revealing his canines. The monitor went black. Silence.

[Let's Start, begin the melee]

A low-pitched melody began, and the pre-chorus erupted.

[Find me, look at my face, I don't care what's next]

Individual cuts of the TeSTAR members flashed by without a moment to breathe. They ran through the gray corridor, the sealed room, the maze, the monitoring room, and various other unknown conceptual locations.

[Chaser, today too, don't stop your hands]

It looked magnificent. Just as she thought the tremendous number of cuts were repeating to the point where it was impossible to tell how many they had filmed, a hand blocked the view. It was a hand wearing a cotton glove.

[Find out everything, Don't be scared what's next]

The owner of the hand was Lee Sejin. Dressed in an ultramarine uniform and drinking coffee, Lee Sejin smiled faintly. He then turned his head to look at the broken monitor, grabbed a key, and left the monitor room, humming.

[Chaser, tomorrow too, don't end the twist]

Then came the chorus.

[Just roll the dice]

A dance break started. Like a video cross-cutting between different stages, the outfits and backgrounds changed seamlessly. They switched from the white prison uniform to the ultramarine uniform, and then to the leather suit.

"Wow."

*Just how many cuts did they film for this too...*No, it seemed like it would have taken even more tremendous effort than the running scenes, but the thought that it was thrilling came first, making her heart pound apologetically.

The fansite master swallowed and continued to watch the video.

[Trust, take my side]

The second verse began after the chorus. The story and choreography were appropriately mixed, and the video returned to a classic K-pop feel. It was a powerful performance video, though full of symbolism.

Thanks to the beginning, the story could be understood in quite specific detail.

*Let's see... Moondae and Cha Eugene are, hmm, prisoners? Something like that. Raebin and Chungwoo are phantom thieves, and is Keun Sejin the guard?*

However, just as she was tilting her head, unsure of the specific roles of Bae Sejin and Seon Ahyeon, who appeared in various outfits during the performance, the melody of the bridge began to flow.

[Yes, this Final round]

Suddenly, the screen, which had been changing at a fast tempo, switched to a long take.

...CLACK.

It showed the back of someone standing in front of a huge iron door. The camera soon turned halfway around, revealing his identity. It was Bae Sejin.

*Ah, so he was a phantom thief!*

His outfit was similar to Ryu Chungwoo's. Just as the fansite master nodded her head...

[......]

Bae Sejin calmly stared at the iron door, then reached for the lock pad next to it. He was holding a strange mechanical device that looked like a key. The camera seemed as if it was about to close up on his leather-gloved hand.

VMMMM.

Suddenly, that hand flickered and changed several times. It became a hand in a cotton glove, then a scarred hand.

"...!"

However, it soon returned to the gloved hand. The moment he connected the key to the lock pad, a code entry field appeared on the machine.

[_____]

*Oh!*

Bae Sejin seemed to be smiling faintly, but...

WHAM!

With the sound of a blow, the screen went black.

"......"

[Just roll the dice

Trust, take my side]

There was no next part. In an instant, the dazzling performance video resumed. While mesmerized by it, the fansite master still had a question mark lingering in her mind, but in the end, she surrendered to the performance.

*They'll definitely give us a clue at the end...!*

That's right. The hint was given right when the song ended. However, it wasn't in the way she had expected. On the screen that had turned black, something else appeared instead of Bae Sejin's whereabouts. It was a close-up shot of a beautiful face.

"...?"

It was the blond-haired Seon Ahyeon, dressed in an ultramarine uniform. He looked straight into the camera and his lips parted slightly.

[...Choose your side.]

The soft sound of string instruments began to flow. As the screen slowly closed in with black bars from the top and bottom, Seon Ahyeon slowly turned his head and began to walk down the corridor. It was the same gray corridor from the beginning.

A black baton hanging from Seon Ahyeon's waist swayed slightly. It looked good for suppressing someone.

"......"

The screen went black. Only the distinct sound of military boots remained amidst the soft strings.

[CLACK, CLACK, CLACK...]

And then, text appeared.

[Roll the Dice]

~Please enter the code

The last thing to appear was a blinking black box. It was the same one that had appeared when Bae Sejin accessed the lock pad in the music video.

And...

"...Hah!"

The fansite master let out the breath she had been holding.

Crazy!

*I was worried for nothing!*

The quality was like a movie! It was a bit different from their previous music videos, but this would definitely get a reaction.

*The international fan reactions are going to explode...*

She could already picture it.

*The world-building is a bit... very grandiose, though.*

It seemed like a new universe. They had put so much effort into it that it felt like a fan-made music video for a drama.

*Is the board game part of that universe? Will I understand it if I play it?*

While she was racking her brain, her social media was suddenly flooded with private messages.

*What is this?*

She clicked on them, thinking it was just fan chatter.

[The album product page is up and holy sh*t it's the key from the MV]

"......"

[The key from the guys' music video is the album USB!!]

"...Wha-wha-what??"

Her mouth fell open as she screamed.

*AHHHHHH!!*

The next day, she found herself frantically unboxing the album that had been delivered.

*Hurry! Hurry!*

While being as careful as possible with the USB that was an actual prop from the music video, she hurriedly detached it. She connected it to her computer and tried to access its folder.

Because...

*They made it so meaningful!*

There had to be something in the extended version of the MV! She absolutely had to see it!

*If I just click on the video file like this!*

I'll be able to watch the extended MV...

"......"

However, what greeted her was not a video player.

[Allow the app TeSTAR - Roll the Dice MV Extended Version (Playable) to make changes to your device?]

An app? In that moment, she realized.

*Playable.*

A game. The so-called extended version of the MV... was a game.
