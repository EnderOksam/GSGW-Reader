# Chapter 429

An opportunity to speak directly with a fan.

It was actually rare. Most of the time, communication is indirect, through the waving lightsticks above the stage or through posts uploaded on the internet.

Even at a fan sign event, it's just a short conversation in a space shared with many others while standing in line.

So, a moment like this, a one-on-one conversation, is extremely rare.

"......"

Even if I was in a room hanging precariously from a tilted, collapsed stage, and even if I could die at any moment.

"Why are you here? Why are you here?!"

A shout came from beyond the wall. A flustered voice.

There was little fear or panic. Just the appropriate amount for the current situation of a stage collapse.

*...She doesn't know.*

I slowly regained my composure and assessed the situation.

The lightstick that fan was holding was quite bright, but since it didn't shoot light forward, it wasn't very effective at illuminating the area beyond her immediate sight. If anything, it only made it easier for me to see her.

So, that fan could probably only make out my outfit or my general silhouette.

My situation and my opponent were not clearly visible to her.

I swallowed and opened my mouth.

"...I came to get something and got trapped. I'm okay."

Would I be okay?

A wall was blocking the way anyway. In such an unstable situation, it was unlikely that some bizarre, crazy event would happen that would drag her into this...

*You idiot.*

The right thing to do was to send her away. Why was I trying to create another variable?

"Oh my god, wa-wait a minute. I'll go get people..."

"No. It won't be good if it gets too noisy."

Now it would be a problem even if I sent her away. She would just bring other people.

The problem was that this thought was reassuring.

"Ah, um, right... Sorry, I'm sorry! Then... should I try to go to the other side and open the door?"

"It could be dangerous, so how about we just wait here for the rescue team?"

"Uhm..."

The fact that I had to stall for time by talking was a welcome one. My eyes felt hot.

I never thought a day would come when I'd feel this way. It was so ridiculous I was on the verge of laughing.

*Stupid bastard.*

I backed away further, leaning against the door as if I were leaning against a wall, and grabbed the steel shelf again. With more distance secured, I felt calmer.

She wouldn't get dragged into this.

And I opened my mouth without thinking.

"Are the others, are they okay?"

"I don't know, but, but the members probably all got out. There was an announcement and the audience and everyone left..."

That was a relief, at least. I bit my tongue to keep from getting complacent with a useless sense of security and asked.

"...What about you, noona?"

"Me, me? I'm... ah, I'm fine! I'm not hurt, and it feels like the building has mostly stopped shaking."

She said she had been looking for the place where the staff had collected the cell phones to prevent spoilers.

And she had discovered me beyond the collapsed wall while carefully coming up the emergency stairs.

"I was a bit late getting out, so I got trapped. I was sitting in the front row."

I gave a weak laugh. A bitter taste filled my mouth for having dragged her into this.

"Yes. I saw you."

"...You saw me?!"

"Yes. You were sitting in the front left. So, from your perspective, on the right."

"Heok."

I think I'd actually seen her occasionally since my debut, but let's not bring that up.

Let's just... keep talking.

"How did you manage to come? Were you free because it's the weekend?"

"Ah, yeah. I'm not working, I'm... a grad student."

Majoring in biotechnology, raising a dog...

She must have been quite anxious herself, as she started sharing personal stories she probably wouldn't normally tell a stranger.

And also about why she liked me, which of my performances, which broadcast scenes had made her feel fond of me and decide to cheer for me.

There was a warmth in the familiar yet unfamiliar stories that didn't fit the situation.

Maybe that's why I did it more. To forget the situation.

"...I see."

I listened quietly.

This wasn't so bad.

And as she also began to relax, she must have realized again that she was trapped in a building, because she started asking questions.

"Hey... did you stop the performance earlier because you felt something was wrong, too?"

"Something like that."

It seemed she hadn't seen me collapse.

Moreover, thinking about it, she didn't seem surprised that I was alive, which meant she probably didn't see that it was me who had run out.

She was probably not mentioning the person who looked like they had been crushed to death by the stage equipment so as not to upset me.

"...Thank you. For worrying about me."

"...!! What are you talking about, of course... Ah. This is the middle of Seoul, they'll come rescue us soon. Don't worry... let's just wait a little longer!"

Her voice was cheerful, deliberately raised a tone.

I smiled.

"Yes."

My mind was made up.

At the same time.

CREAAAK.

A sound came from the door behind me.

And a premonition.

The cold sweat, the anxiety, the precarious sense of failure as if everything was over, it all came back.

*...This is the end.*

A sharp judgment returned in an instant. What if I leaped forward right now and somehow made it over to the other side of that open wall where the fan was standing?

Just like when I dove into this supply closet.

*If I time it right...*

"......"

"...Moondae-ya? Are you hurt or sick anywhere?"

Then the probability of getting caught up in this with that fan would be extremely high.

"No."

Right.

I let go of the steel shelf.

Simultaneously, an ominous shattering sound echoed from behind me.

The sound of the door being smashed to pieces.

"...!"

"Ah."

GRIIIND-

Concrete scraped and broke against itself.

The small supply closet, with a terrifying sound, dropped and descended.

At the same time, I leaped through the broken doorway.

Thud.

But the hallway was already destroyed. On top of that, because the supply closet had tilted, my hands could barely reach the hallway floor, not my feet.

All that remained was a black void.

"Gasp!"

Thump.

I barely managed to grab the edge of the broken hallway and hang on.

*...No.*

But my damn hands lacked strength.

The limbs I had already overused several times had significantly reduced output, and one hand couldn't support my body.

*I'm going to fall.*

I thought I heard someone calling my name amidst a scream from behind the roaring noise.

"......"

But it was okay.

This was the right choice, not the other side.

If I couldn't avoid it no matter what, I should at least get to choose how I die.

Hanging from a piece of concrete with rebar sticking out, I had what would probably be my last thought.

[Hyung!]

"......"

Since it had come to this, there was one last attempt I could make.

*Status Window.*

Turn on the Status Window and contact Keundal...

[Processing]

At that moment, as if in a lie, my bloody hand slipped.

"...!!"

I fell into the open air.

A brief moment of dizziness.

Soon, in the cool darkness, an immense impact and pain should have enveloped my entire body... but then.

......

[??'s Search Request! (101)]

[??'s Search Request! (102)]

[??'s Search Request! (103)]

[Confirm]

The world stopped.

As if everything had slowed to a crawl, my consciousness accelerated as if I were watching my life flash before my eyes.

And the text on the Status Window in front of me suddenly became opaque. Hundreds of pop-ups overlapped, glowing.

And on top of them, a new pop-up appeared.

[Search Complete!]

[Result Value: 'Title' Confirmed]

What is this?

[Title: The Successful One (Idol)]

[You have succeeded.]

: Permanent removal of status abnormality occurrences

*Ah.*

Come to think of it, I remember.

This was the perk I received after clearing the last status abnormality... after confirming the Truth at Bae Sejin's house.

A rule that Keundal had received from the System and expressed in words.

...The Status Window's guarantee that I would never suffer from a status abnormality again.

If that's the case,

*This shitty situation right now... contradicts the title.*

It was then.

WHOOSH!

Pain.

I stopped in mid-air, grabbing my wrist as an immense pressure and a force that felt like it would snap my arm began.

Time started flowing again.

A voice was heard.

"Moondae...!"

I looked up.

Above the concrete. I could see a deathly pale face and a hand gripping my arm.

It was Seon Ahyeon.

"...!"

I didn't know why he was here or what was happening.

But the pain screamed that this was reality.

"W-wait."

Seon Ahyeon pulled me up with surprising strength, and above his desperate face, beyond the Status Window, words appeared.

[Error!]

[Error Detected: Status Abnormality Occurrence]

[Attempting Restoration]

I gasped for breath.

[Correcting]

['Status Abnormality Failure' -> 'Mission Failure']

[Restoring Penalty...]

The Status Window was changing.

Becoming more stable.

[Sudden!]

Mission Failure: Building Collapse

[The Disaster of the Collapsing Building]

: Until next disaster 00:59:59

One hour.

"Heueup."

"I-It's okay, it's okay..."

My breathing steadied. The tension and irrational pressure that had filled me to the brim receded like a tide, leaving behind fatigue, pain, and a rational sense of anxiety before melting away for a moment.

"It's okay..."

Leaning almost completely against Seon Ahyeon, I slowly accepted it.

"......"

That I was still alive.

***

"H-here's some water."

"...Thanks."

I took the plastic water bottle Seon Ahyeon offered. It was a bit hard to drink because my teeth were chattering, but after drinking as much as I could and wiping my mouth, I saw the brand name.

*This is.*

It wasn't something prepared with the cameras in mind.

*...He couldn't have gotten it from a vending machine, he wouldn't have any coins.*

Basically, we were all just in our stage outfits. It would be ridiculous to go on stage with something in your pocket. This question was answered shortly after.

"...!! Moondae-hyung! Are you alright??"

After moving a little with Seon Ahyeon's support, I ran into Kim Raebin, who had diligently grabbed an emergency flashlight and was carrying a load of water.

It turned out these two, upon deciding I looked cold, had dashed to the waiting room to find a blanket and gotten separated from the other members during the collapse.

*They don't know the stage equipment fell on me.*

No need to bring it up.

"...Your injuries are severe. There should be a first-aid kit in the waiting room, so after disinfecting it first..."

"I get it."

I did my best to calm down the nearly-crying Kim Raebin and asked as calmly as possible.

"So this is the 2nd floor, where the waiting rooms are."

"That is correct..."

The source of the water was the waiting room. The path down to the 1st-floor backstage was blocked by the collapsed stage ceiling.

If that was the case.

"What about the other staff members?"

"We got separated... during the evacuation... I'm not sure where they went."

"......"

I narrowed my eyes and looked at the two of them. Now that I looked, they were also quite disheveled, as if they had rolled around in a pile of dust.

Surely these guys didn't hear some strange rumor during the evacuation and decide to stay here to find me.

But I could talk about that after we escaped, so let's move on.

*Right.*

Escape.

I checked the Status Window pop-up from earlier again.

Actually, I hadn't even put it away.

...Afraid it would disappear.

[Mission Failure: Building Collapse]

[The Deadly Disaster of the Collapse]

: Until next accident 00:55:37

It changed from 'Status Abnormality Failure' to 'Mission Failure', and the scope became narrower and slightly kinder.

*Is it because I have 'Mission Constitution' that it connected to that?*

It seemed to have somehow forced the words to fit.

Still, if I had seen that message on the Status Window before this whole mess started, I would have wanted to go crazy.

But now, it was actually reassuring. An hour was a long time.

*At least I can try something.*

If your favorite idol died right in front of you while you were talking to them, that would be truly traumatic. I wanted to at least leave a trace that I lived a little longer after that.

I went to the waiting room and received the first aid that Kim Raebin insisted on.

"Your back..."

"I can get it treated properly once we get out."

The hand treating my back was trembling.

"F-for this to happen, what on earth were you doing on the upper floor...?"

"......"

I hesitated for a moment, then changed the subject.

To what needed to be done.

"Actually, I was looking for Keundal."

"Keundal...?"

"You saw him in the Gold Chat before."

"Ah!"

The one who had just tried to search countless times on my Status Window must have been him. He was the only one who could access the Status Window.

*And it seems he managed to find a loophole in the interpretation by linking my title and Mission Constitution.*

It was similar in nature to the things I had often made him do.

...Somehow, he had tried to save me.

In that case.

*...I need to check directly with Keundal to see if he knows more, and if this can be handled in a similar way again.*

But communication was impossible right now.

['Mission Failure' Penalty: Access to Status Window's additional functions is restricted.]

Now, this popped up every time I tried to start a pop-up chat with Keundal.

*There's no choice but to meet him in person.*

So, for the sake of cooperation, let's be honest up to this point.

"This situation appeared on the Status Window like a warning."

After explaining the general situation, I swallowed past a dry throat and said.

"So I need to find Keundal somehow and discuss if he knows anything..."

"N-no."

"...?!"

"Moondae is too hurt right now. Until the rescue comes, I-I'll be the one to look."

"I'd like to do that too."

I didn't really want to, but let's just say that.

Anyway.

"If we just stay put... it says this building will collapse again in 49 minutes and 31 seconds."

"...!!"

"We might find a way to stop it, so we have to try everything."

I pushed myself up. I felt like some of my strength had returned.

Let's move.

Given his personality, that guy would have definitely stayed in the building even while watching this disaster unfold.

Cheongryeo pressed the call button on his smartphone.

The customer's phone is currently off, please leave a message at the tone...

Beep.

No response.

But the traces before his eyes did not lie.

He opened the emergency door and looked at the collapsed 3rd-floor hallway.

He must have run here, and...

*He was alive.*

At least, up to this point.

He looked down and turned on his smartphone's flashlight function. Concrete and steel fragments were scattered about chaotically.

There were no bloodstains.

"It would be better to look for the place where they're making the announcements..."

"Quiet."

"......"

Judan shut his mouth.

*Was it a mistake to follow him?*

He had followed his Leader out of the waiting room with a premonition of an incident, and now he was trapped in a collapsed building.

*There was a game like this...*

Judan squinted and followed Cheongryeo, who was heading down the emergency stairs.
