# Chapter 309

The theme song that Kim Raebin arranged alone was commented on by T1 Plays, the company that took over the production team of Section 127.

And less than a day after it was posted, the comment section was flooded with comments from people full of anticipation and making pilgrimages to the "holy site."

– Gaahhhh

– Put it up!

The atmosphere made it seem like this arrangement would be officially adopted for something like a cinematic trailer.

There were even overflowing foreigners posting foreign memes plastered with emoticons.

"Uh, uh..."

Kim Raebin was stunned. He seemed to have overloaded his brain in an unexpected situation.

It was funny, but I felt the same way.

*When did it get to this point?*

I knew the reaction would be good, but becoming such a hot topic was another matter. 

A parody video of a game on a new account suddenly turned into such a big deal in one week.

It was an anonymous account anyway, so we just left it alone, but that didn't work. 

I turned on my smartphone to find the cause of this uproar.

*Is it an algorithmic pick again?*

It wasn't. It was more artificial than that.

It was true that 'Section 127' had gone through its heyday again in Korea, leaving only maniacs behind and losing popularity and sales.

However, perhaps because of the right sensibility, overseas, it had settled down as a steady seller based on a strong fan base.

Thanks to that, there were a lot of people who were still attached to this game series even among overseas gamer WeTubers, and one of them seemed to have seen this video during a livestream.

The subscribers...

★$
@cc@25.92 million subscribers@cc@

Yes, it was a piece of cake. Thanks to that, it came to the attention of the person in charge, who was working hard, and this situation happened.

So what was the conclusion?

"What should we do?"

"Yes."

For a while now, the frequency of "yes" as an answer had increased significantly.

It was a funny situation.

It wasn't like he was a minor character. It was because he was going to build a second career as a professional composer.

That was because the other party had taken the bait on their own. 

At that moment, Cha Eugene raised his hand.

"Let's do it! It's fun!"

I thought so.

"Wait, wait!"

"Let's think about it, Eugene!"

Watching Ryu Chungwoo and Lee Sejin stop Cha Eugene, who was trying to settle it with a majority vote, I opened my mouth.

"First... If you really accept this offer, you will have to put your real name and account number when writing the contract."

"Ah, so we'll get caught right away, right?"

"Yes."

At that moment, it was clear that TeSTAR was being used as a story to promote the game. Since it was the same affiliate, headquarters would push it without a second thought.

It was a fun story, so I thought that TeSTAR would benefit and the game would benefit as well.

*And suspicion of fabrication is everywhere.*

Of course, such a coincidence could look like an obvious marketing deception.

★$
– Using TeSTAR is disgusting.

– Look at them trying to get fame one more time.

– I change my mind. T1 marketing team, why are you doing this?
$★

It was stupid to make such noise in front of the target. The formation of public opinion would be like, 'TeSTAR is not qualified' during the Rookie of the Year award.

It was important to actually receive the grand prize, but the public's acknowledgment of 'deserved' would definitely affect the success of the mission.

*Even if we try to stop it, someone higher up at headquarters could interfere the moment we make up our minds.*

I imagined it.

Then, an unexpected person opened his mouth with a serious face.

"H-how about doing it in the name of family...?"

It was Seon Ahyeon. It seemed that he suggested it because he felt sorry for giving up on Kim Raebin's achievements.

"Oh, that sounds pretty good?"

"I like it!"

I answered with difficulty.

"No. The moment we get caught, they'll accuse us of tax evasion."

No matter what. It was a sensitive area these days, so we had to be careful.

Bae Sejin, who once went through an uproar over his comprehensive income tax return in the first year of his debut, swallowed his saliva.

"Then... Should we ignore it?"

"Some of that too."

Since it had already received attention once, it seemed that contact stories would continue to emerge if we continued to post arrangements on this account.

*If it's Kim Raebin's talent, it's understandable.*

Besides, looking at the comments was amusing right now. On the contrary, if there was no response, that alone could stand out and attract unwanted attention. 

Then those who wanted to use it as viral content would come out.

*...Should we just change the account?*

Well, I thought this account had a symbolic meaning for Kim Raebin overcoming the slump, so I was going to leave it as long as possible.

To avoid that... In the end, we had to do something.

"If you're thinking of refusing, I think it's better to express yourself in appropriate words."

"Yes."

It would be perfect if we had a reasonable excuse that everyone could understand and move on from. 

There would come a time when their interest would fade away, and he could just do his composing activities without difficulty.

"Well, make up something plausible, since you are good at such things!"

"What? Me?"

Bae Sejin flinched when Lee Sejin laughed as if it was ridiculous. They had been behaving politely for a year or two now, so it seemed the medicine was wearing off.

*Still, is it okay to say it openly?*

Anyway, Lee Sejin gave his opinion instead of fighting uselessly with Bae Sejin.

"As expected, the opinion of the first contributor is important for this~ What does Raebin think?"

"What?"

Kim Raebin, who seemed to be deep in thought with his mouth open, suddenly came to his senses at Lee Sejin calling his name.

And he answered carefully.

"I... I don't mind refusing the offer!"

"Wouldn't it be a waste?"

"Yes! It was originally made at a composing camp as a hobby, not for commercial purposes, so even now, the reaction is undeserved and enjoyable!"

"Okay, if Raebin thinks so."

"Okay~"

The conversation ended with them agreeing on an appropriate refusal message. 

"We should say that we're busy studying, right?"

"Yes. Let's tune it up a bit."

So this was the comment we put on the video.

Everyone probably had similar thoughts, but things didn't go that way.

The more he immersed himself in the revised setlist, the more details he took care of.

*He's a good guy.*

If he had something to gain, he was pretty good at designing a realistic path there.

*That's why he's the one who played the biggest role in my plan.*

*Stage 3 of composition camp.*

*As soon as...*

Making him feel a sense of accomplishment and reward even when he was cut off from the opportunity to produce.
