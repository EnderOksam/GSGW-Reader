# Chapter 565

My choreography rehabilitation went smoothly.

My body had only been unconscious for half a day. I just needed to reteach my brain what it had forgotten after being stuck in the past for six months.

*It only took a day to synchronize the event choreography.*

Of course, I felt a bit dizzy when I realized I had spent that half-day unconscious in a JSA military visitation room.

*I thought that bastard Cheongryeo's military life was screwed.*

However, Cheongryeo himself was nonchalant.

[It's nothing to worry about, but hmm... If you feel that sorry, how about you grant me one request to ease your conscience?]

[What is it.]

[I'd like you to let me hear the new TeSTAR album before it's released.]

*Denied. Who does he think he is, trying to get a freebie?*

I almost died retrieving your fragment. Did you leave your conscience in the past?

In any case, just as his attitude suggested, the controversy over Cheongryeo's 'celebrity special treatment' never became an issue and remained quiet.

It meant Cheongryeo had shut it down with an iron fist.

*He really is an impressive bastard.*

Just thinking about facing VTIC when they are discharged next year made me grind my teeth. I hammered it into my brain once more that I needed a solid annual plan before then.

Thinking about our rival groups got my mind racing.

Come to think of it, I remembered another person from a rival group was there, not from TeSTAR.

Even if it was just through a video call.

[Hyung, I'm so relieved... Wow, that was so draining... I'm definitely having a beer with the guys tonight.]

[But I thought I was watching a movie. You know, that space movie. Was it InterXtella... that one.]

Gold 2's Kwon Heeseung.

It seemed Kwon Heeseung had remembered me and stuck around, even joining via video call, wanting to help in any way he could.

[Come to think of it, our group's name is the most space-like. Spacer...]

"......"

Though as Kwon Heeseung relaxed, he started spouting more stream-of-consciousness nonsense.

In any case, Kwon Heeseung left for his schedule, relieved about my condition.

Since we are from the same agency, I plan to do something for him for real.

So, what was I planning for TeSTAR, my own group members who went as far as to bestow a divine protection to save me?

Of course, I had a plan.

"One more time!"

"Alright."

There was no time. These guys were fully cooperating with my choreography rehabilitation.

*This is a bit much.*

Lately, it felt like we were all practicing together at a level comparable to our team training camp during ❬Idol Inc.❭.

This was the best way to synchronize our group choreography, but I could not help but feel I was taking up too much of their time.

"Okay~ It's a perfect match now."

"Yeah. I think I can do it on my own now."

"Oh~ Is that habit of Moondae trying to do everything by himself coming out again?"

"......"

I quietly shut my mouth.

Lee Sejin seemed to nag me every hour, but I could not say anything back. It was true that I had almost died.

*Just think of it as the price for my life and be grateful.*

Recalling the guys who had persistently watched over me and granted their divine protection, my irritation slowly subsided.

Besides, while Lee Sejin ran his mouth like this, he was almost devotedly cooperative during choreography practice, so I just let it be.

He will probably stop once he feels better. I leisurely drank some water.

Of course, this peace of mind likely stemmed from the fact that the System was gone.

I remembered what Keundal, or rather, Ryu Gunwoo, had declared in a trembling voice after checking on me before we parted ways.

[There's nothing at all. Hyung. It's completely... gone!]

[...Yeah.]

It was refreshing.

If there was one small regret, it was that I could no longer chat with him telepathically, but modern civilization could handle that.

*It's not like I'm going to use my smartphone for a barbecue.*

Since then, we have been keeping in touch through calls and messages.

[Hyung, I took a day off and came to Sokcho! The winter sea is so pretty, so I took a picture to send you.]

Ryu Gunwoo seemed to be doing well.

"Good work, everyone~"

"Thank you."

After about three days like that, I had comfortably caught up with practice for the special arrangement version of our new song for the year-end stages.

There were photoshoots in between, but that required no rehabilitation. It was something I had done even while working as an actor.

However, when I explained this fact, a few of the members were taken aback.

"Wait, so you... felt all of that in real-time?"

"That's right."

"You really debuted as an actor?"

"Yes."

Still, Bae Sejin seemed somewhat excited for some reason. He kept subtly bringing up the topic during breaks after that.

Other than that, our schedule was being smoothly organized without any major issues.

The life of TeSTAR's Park Moondae was thus slowly returning to normal.

Although, it might have been faster because this was not the first or second time I had to return to daily life after the System's absurd bullshit.

In any case.

*It's peaceful.*

During break time, I lay sprawled on the dance practice room floor and blinked my eyes.

The sense of stability made me drowsy, but it was perfectly comfortable.

Ryu Chungwoo and Cha Eugene had volunteered to get some fresh air and buy drinks. Once I had an iced coffee, I would be able to practice again like someone sobered up with cold water.

CLICK.

"We're back."

Oh, just then, the two of them opened the door and came in.

In their hands was a drink carrier... no.

*That looks like some kind of box.*

They held a rectangular box wrapped in gift paper.

Just as I was wondering about it, the box was suddenly thrust in front of my face.

"......"

It was large, clearly not the size to hold an Americano.

*What is this.*

I reflexively sat up. Ryu Chungwoo, who offered the box, spoke.

"Moondae. Happy birthday."

"......"

Ah.

I see. Today was the day before Park Moondae's birthday.

*Did they pretend to go out just to bring back the presents?*

Sure enough, Cha Eugene, who followed him in, had no drinks in his hands either. Instead, he held something enormous and wrapped in both hands.

Moreover, the other guys were also sneaking to the back to pull something out. It seemed the two with the bulky items had gone out to bring them in without getting caught.

"...Thank you."

I accepted the gift box Ryu Chungwoo held out.

When I opened it, I found a dartboard inside.

It was a custom version of the very same dartboard we had used to pick our performance song on that variety show.

My signature glinted in the reflected light at the end of the yellow and wood-colored darts.

"To be honest, we thought about giving it to you on your actual birthday, the 8th... but we thought you'd like it more if we all gave it to you during the period when the fans are celebrating."

Ryu Chungwoo grinned and said it was a good thing they waited.

"......"

He was right.

"Let's make good use of this dartboard until I can beat you, hyung."

"Of course."

The guy who was good at any kind of ranged attack readily replied. I let out a small laugh.

"OK. Now receive my present!"

From that moment, the presents the guys had prepared began to pour in.

"A doll?"

"Yeees."

Cha Eugene's gift was just like him.

It was an extra-large Tiger doll wearing sunglasses.

I could already see the future. This would be for common use. It would end up as a cushion under the living room sofa.

"...I heard you like apples. Th-They said it has an apple scent, too."

"Ah, I bought something with an apple, too~"

Bae Sejin gave me a diffuser from a brand he often uses, and Lee Sejin gave me a sweatshirt from some brand. It had a comfortable texture with a large apple picture printed on it.

It was kind of funny how these two always chose a similar theme but went through opposite processes.

"...Wear it well. Moondae Moondae."

"Thanks."

I designated the sweatshirt as my practice wear.

And the next guy was...

"If you would just grant me a little more time...!"

"It's not my birthday yet, Raebin."

For reference, Kim Raebin hesitated in the back, looking like he was about to do a full bow while saying 'it's still in preparation,' so I stopped him.

"H-Here."

Lastly, Seon Ahyeon gave me a muffler and a set of some kind of iced cookies.

The surprising thing was the latter was also handmade.

"Seon Ahyeon made these?"

"...! Ahyeon did?"

"I-I only did the, the decorations...!"

"......"

Why do you guys look so visibly relieved?

*These funny guys.*

And so, the gift-giving ceremony concluded.

"I'll say it again, happy birthday, Moondae."

I looked at the pile of gifts, too big to hold with both hands, and then at the wrapping paper scattered on the floor.

*Why did they insist on doing this in the dance practice room when we'll have to clean all this up later?*

Of course, I knew the reason.

Because it was to celebrate my birthday.

They must have done it to create a special feeling.

They probably tried to make it fun while considering my mental state and stability as much as possible.

"Let's do the cake tomorrow."

"...Yes."

"The flavor is a secret. I won't answer even if you ask, Moondae-hyung."

"Alright."

And so, I returned to the dorm with my arms full of unexpected gifts.

Was it because it had been six months since things like this happened, or was it because I had genuinely thought I might die?

Strangely, the feeling of elation would not fade.

*What is this.*

It is not like it is a concert or anything.

The birthday celebration posts already appearing on my SNS timeline after I got back to the dorm only fueled that elation.

[A star born in winter, as beautiful as snow (Photo)]

[I'm glad Park Moondae exists in this world, the end of the year isn't cold (Meme of a puppy curled up)]

A flood of diverse posts and photos from people with different personalities and ways of speaking.

All those varied expressions ultimately meant they were celebrating my birthday.

"......"

In the end, I sat at my desk and started typing on the keyboard.

For a slightly early birthday letter.

[Hello, Loviewer.]

This is Moondae

If I add a puppy emoticon after this, my usual opening line is complete.

The fact that so many people shared the sentiment of being familiar with this formula gave me an awkward yet not-unpleasant feeling.

[You can probably tell it's me just by reading the first line, right? I've been writing like this since my debut. I'd be proud if you immediately thought of Park Moondae.]

I hesitated, then added the next line.

[And this is a letter from Moondae to Loviewer to celebrate his birthday.]

A letter written by Park Moondae.

What followed was the tone and writing style befitting the image of TeSTAR's Park Moondae that I had built since debut.

Meaning, I tried with all my might to appear naturally gentle.

[How was the time you spent with me this year?]

I enjoyed it very much, and I hope Loviewer did too.

First, I'll leave some proof that I enjoyed it. Ta-da.

That idol-like tone, which now came out comfortably, even wrapped up naturally.

*Now, if I attach a photo after this...*

"......"

I paused my hands for a moment.

I knew that the fans, in fact, would prefer a photo.

Still, this time.

[Normally, I would probably attach a photo taken with the members here and greet Loviewer through a live broadcast, but today I'm going to try something a little different.]

Which is.

[To keep writing.]

I wrote down the thoughts that came to mind, more honestly and at greater length than before.

[Loviewer.]

I am happy to be TeSTAR's Park Moondae.

When I'm on stage, when I'm practicing, when I'm at a concert, every time I go through a difficult and arduous process to show something to Loviewer, it's incredibly fun and enjoyable.

I want to be a part of TeSTAR for a long, long time.

I like the members, and I like the albums we make.

No, I did not intend to write this honestly.

I was a bit flustered by the words that were writing themselves and almost stopped typing, but I pushed past that feeling and kept writing.

Even if I edited it later, I wanted to write it all down as it was for now.

[Sometimes, when we get a vacation and everyone leaves the TeSTAR dorm, I even feel... empty or a little sad.]

(Please keep this a secret.)

After finally spouting such unbelievably embarrassing words, the main point I really wanted to write finally came out.

[And there's something most important.]

More than anything, I'm happy that there's a Loviewer who is reading this long post all the way to this point.

And who might that be?

[You.]

I mean the person who will read this post.

[The fact that there are people today who are happy that I exist in this world and are celebrating it, feels unbelievably good.]

The fact that I was not actually born on this day did not matter anymore.

I just realized that seeing people rejoice and commemorate my birth was a feeling so good it was elating.

So much so that these embarrassing words kept pouring out.

[It's a bit embarrassing, but I really wanted to say this.]

And so, I finished the post, written by pressing down hard on the keyboard.

And I held back a scream.

"...Am I crazy?"

Since my debut, no, since I was born, I had never written something so full of late-night sentimentality.

*I'm not a teenager.*

I covered my eyes and groaned.

However... however.

"......"

*I don't want to take anything away.*

I finally took my hands completely off the keyboard.

And I pressed save.

In the end, a few hours later at midnight.

The post was uploaded without any edits.

[So... for these reasons, I want to continue living as your idol, Moondae.]

Thank you, always, and I love you.

Along with the final phrase.

[From TeSTAR's Park Moondae to Loviewer]

***

"Moondae Moondae."

"......"

"Saying it's a secret from the members but then posting a confession like this on the internet, truly the skill of a Grand Prize-winning idol~."

"......"

"The Loviewers are going crazy saying how cute it is... Ack! Ugh! No, it's a compliment! It's a compliment, Moondae Moondae!"

So noisy.

Before the other guys could join in, I quickly fled to my room. No, I made a strategic retreat.

*Hoo.*

Fortunately, Kim Raebin was so engrossed in composing that he looked like he was about to be sucked into his PC. I successfully locked the door and sat on my bed.

*Should I have left out the part about the members?*

Well, still, it was not bad seeing Lee Sejin being his usual slick self.

Thinking about how the others who saw it would soon come over and talk to me with smirks, I decided to enjoy the brief moment of silence.

In fact, that was not the only thing I sent at midnight.

TAP!

I turned on my smartphone and checked the message I had sent earlier.

[Happy birthday.]

[May Park Moondae, may Ryu Gunwoo, always be happy and live joyfully.]

It was a text sent to Keundal.

To the Park Moondae I knew. The guy who was now Ryu Gunwoo.

*He probably received birthday wishes from his current acquaintances on December 8th, Ryu Gunwoo's birthday...*

Still, receiving congratulations on the actual day from someone who knows your original birthday has a different flavor, does it not?

Just as it had been for me, before.

"......"

And there was a reply to that text.

[Thank you.]

It was short, but it would be fine to start from there.

I smiled and closed the message.

Just as I was briefly lost in sentiment.

"Moondae-hyung!"

You scared me.

I met the eyes of Kim Raebin, who had already thrown off his headset and was looking this way.

Wait.

*Don't tell me this guy is planning to tease me too.*

However, making me feel sorry for doubting him, the reason he called me was this.

"Here... I just finished it with the final touches!"

"...This is."

"It's a song I made with you as the theme, hyung."

I took the headset from the flushed guy with hollow eyes.

A sound that delicately resonated in my ears burst forth from the laptop, riding the musical score.

"......"

"It's your birthday present!"

Wait a minute.

It is a gift I am truly grateful for, one that is almost moving even for someone like me, but don't tell me... is this my solo song?

"Ah, I didn't compose it with any particular limitations... but there are a few parts I'd like you to sing, Moondae-hyung. For instance, here..."

"......"

"...?"

"So... this is a song you made without any restrictions, just the way you wanted to."

"That's right!"

"......"

I rubbed my chin.

"Raebin."

"Yes?"

"This is it."

"...?"

The theme for our next comeback was set.
