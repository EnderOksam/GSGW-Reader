# Chapter 434

A few hours earlier.

KRAKOOOM!

"Aaargh!"

Screams erupted the moment the stage equipment collapsed.

*Quickly, qui-quickly...!*

Keundal tried to access the Status Window. He needed to talk to TeSTAR's Park Moondae, or rather, Ryu Gunwoo, and see if he was okay.

It was impossible.

*...Huh?*

Instead, a bright red warning window appeared. It was a 'Status Abnormality Failure' pop-up.

"......"

Keundal swore he hadn't let his guard down after returning to reality. Park Moondae had even warned him specifically.

[Do you feel like you're resonating with the System like before? You said it felt like you were being absorbed.]

He had meticulously checked, worried the System might still be around. There had been no sign of it.

[No, I don't feel that at all anymore!]

[I see. That's a relief.]

Now, a sudden Status Abnormality Failure had occurred.

*If so, then... doesn't that mean he's going to die?*

*Gunwoo-hyung.*

*Wh-what should I do?*

He still couldn't feel the System's powerful presence, but the incident had already happened.

Even as his mind went blank, Keundal desperately racked his brain.

Miraculously, something flashed in his mind. It was the title he had given Park Moondae as a reward for clearing the game.

"...!"

Permanent Suspension of Status Abnormalities! The fact that a Status Abnormality Failure appeared, completely ignoring that title, was a contradiction.

*If I can just exploit this...*

He knew from experience that such logical flaws could work against the System. He himself had once been the Status Window.

*It might be possible!*

Keundal gritted his teeth and anxiously began to dig into the Status Window.

***

"...So, you tried to change the Status Abnormality into a Mission Failure to make the disaster as light as possible. In the process, you tried so many times that you poured all your focus into the Status Window and lost consciousness."

"Yes, yes..."

We sat on a bench in front of the broadcast station building as the sun set.

Listening to the story without any interruptions, it seemed like a peaceful scene.

Except there wasn't a single soul around.

I sat on the bench and watched Keundal gratefully accept and drink the water Kim Raebin offered him.

We were still in the empty front yard of the broadcast station.

And while it was a positive development that this guy had woken up and confirmed my suspicions, I still had no idea what was going on besides that.

*What's with this reward window?*

I glanced again at the pop-up that appeared the moment Keundal regained consciousness.

[Mission Failure Scenario Complete]

Victory: Player Park Moondae

Reward: Fragment of ■■■ 1 (1/4)

I had a mountain of questions, from the scenario to the victory and the fragment, but I couldn't ask.

"...Umm."

Eight guys were standing around with wide, clueless eyes.

One of them, Keun Sejin, even raised his hand slightly to interject.

"Um, I'm sorry for listening in on such a private conversation, but since we all went through this together, I have to ask."

"Yes, yes!"

"So, does this mean Park Moondae knew this was targeted at him and that he could have died at any moment...?"

"......"

The atmosphere instantly turned cold. I avoided their gazes.

*We escaped because I didn't say anything, dammit.*

This felt a little unfair.

Fortunately, someone came to my defense.

"Moondae-hyung went through a lot. I understand hyung. Hyung wanted people to be safe."

It was surprising that Cha Eugene had made the most mature judgment.

"But understanding and accepting aren't the same thing. What you did was too cruel, hyung!"

"......"

So it was a build-up.

"The situation was unfolding so fast there was no time to think. I couldn't tell anyone because I wasn't sure myself and had no one to ask."

I quietly explained the situation.

"I was just trying my best to survive, too. It wasn't some self-sacrificing act for others."

"......"

The atmosphere calmed down a bit. Keun Sejin wiped his face with his hand and finally conceded.

"Alright. You went through a lot."

"...!"

Good.

Before any more objections could be raised, I quickly looked at Keundal. Just in time, the next questioner appeared.

"I have a question as well."

It was Judan.

Of all the people dragged into this, he seemed the most intrigued.

"Then is this place a mental inner world that you created, like last time?"

*And this is what he's curious about?*

Keundal answered readily.

"No, not exactly. This place is real, but... because hyung successfully escaped, the building collapse penalty was nullified. So this reality will disappear soon!"

"...!"

This was the explanation.

Originally, the 'Mission Failure Penalty' would have only been completed if I had been hit by the final building collapse.

*But I avoided it.*

Thanks to that, the penalty was deleted. Retroactively, the 'building collapse' itself, the penalty for the mission failure, was erased as if it never happened.

*Even I, who roughly know the situation, think it sounds like bullshit. The others must be completely lost.*

"Um, perhaps the System's ability to go back in time is a similar power, erasing reality and starting over from the past..."

"...!"

One other person here besides me could understand those words.

I looked back at Cheongryeo. He listened with a blank expression.

Keundal continued his explanation.

He said they probably ended up staying here instead of returning to the past because they escaped the building at the same time as me.

"...Anyway, so you'll all be leaving here soon and returning to a point in time before the building collapse! Just like everyone else."

"Ooh."

"That's a relief, really."

The disheveled guys, who had clearly just escaped a disaster, nodded with much calmer faces. They seemed to like the conclusion.

The questioner himself was impressed.

"I see, it's a type of... parallel world where one is chosen and the rest are discarded."

"...! You understand?"

"On a theoretical level, yes. I could recommend some related materials if you'd like."

I pretended not to see Cha Eugene, next to Kim Raebin, mouth the word 'Nerd'.

Ryu Chungwoo gently asked Keundal again.

"Then when can we go back?"

"Uh... right now!"

"...!"

The next moment.

——————————

The figures sitting on the bench vanished as if they had evaporated.

I, however, did not disappear.

Instead, I saw Keundal take a deep breath and look at me.

"There's something I think I need to tell you separately, hyung. I sent them back a little early!"

A rather thoughtful idea.

The problem was that not everyone had disappeared.

"Hmm, I can see a countdown."

"Ah."

"Looks like you still have some GM authority left."

Cheongryeo was still sitting on another bench.

I confirmed the countdown he mentioned.

[100 seconds until scenario deletion]

That meant this place would disappear in 100 seconds. Not much time.

"Just ignore him and talk quickly. If there's a countdown, it means we're short on time."

"Yes. Yes."

I decided to save my other questions for later, starting with whether the System was still alive. I needed to hear what this guy wanted to say first.

Keundal began to speak, clearly trying to stay calm.

"Hyung."

Yeah.

"When we go back, the building collapse won't have happened, but... the events before it will have."

"...?"

The events before?

A beat later, I realized.

"I only started interfering after you were first threatened with your life by the Status Abnormality Failure."

The first signal of the Status Abnormality Failure.

Keundal swallowed and said in a low voice.

"When you go back, it'll be the moment the stage equipment falls on you."

"......"

"That was it, right, hyung? You dodged it, but it's still dangerous, so I thought you needed to know this to be able to dodge it again... I felt I had to tell you."

That's when I realized.

*He doesn't know I got hurt then.*

This guy thinks I dodged it safely the first time, so he's just a little nervous.

"Got it. I'll be extra careful and dodge it, just in case."

"...Yes."

I glanced at Cheongryeo, signaling him to keep his mouth shut. I soon realized it was time for him to worry about himself.

"...The stage equipment collapsing will affect you guys too. Shouldn't you start thinking about how to respond now?"

Wasn't this a charity concert hosted by VTIC? The moment the keywords 'accident' and 'event' were linked, it would be chaos.

However, Cheongryeo did not smile.

He opened his mouth, his face expressionless.

"Why don't you worry about yourself?"

"......"

*Seriously, he's a pain even when I'm trying to help... whatever. It's true I have my own problems to deal with.*

"Protect your head first and foremost."

"Yeah. I know."

I took a deep breath and reviewed my next moves. Thankfully, time was running out before Keundal could notice anything strange.

"The countdown's almost over, right?"

"Yes..."

I read the numbers.

3.

2.

1.

When I blinked.

"Park Moondae...!"

I was running on the stage again.

Instead of musty, dust-filled air, I smelled the dry ice on stage and felt the heat of the lights warming the air-conditioned space.

*It's here.*

The equipment was falling from above.

CREAAAK!!

This time, with a perfectly clear mind, I knew exactly what to do.

I knew how far it would fall.

*Faster...!*

Instead of just running blindly, I accelerated and slid.

My thigh stung.

KABOOOM!

Amidst the roar, a chilling sound came from behind me as the equipment turned to debris. Screams erupted.

"Aaargh!"

"Ack!"

And...

"Hoo-wook."

The moment my slide stopped, I identified my location.

I was backstage, completely off the stage.

"......"

I looked back.

I could see the shattered and collapsed stage equipment, but this time... it was a good body-length away from me.

A complete evasion.

*Success.*

Unlike before, my back and ribs didn't hurt.

I waited a little longer.

My thigh was hot from the friction, but that was it.

That was the end of it.

"You!"

The members appeared from backstage with horrified faces. Their expressions quickly turned to relief as they ran over.

The building didn't collapse or shake any further.

"Hoo."

I stretched out on my back to my heart's content.

There were still many unanswered questions and problems to think about, but I couldn't hold back a laugh.

"You're laughing?"

*I'm alive.*

No one died, no one got hurt, and we got out.

***

Things flowed strangely after that.

Naturally, as soon as the audience members got their smartphones back and evacuated, they posted all sorts of things on social media, and articles flooded in.

[SBC Broadcast Station Stage Collapse (Breaking News)]

['Unprecedented Stage Collapse' No Casualties... All Safe]

The headlines were terrifying, but that's how articles are. Fortunately, since no one was hurt, it seemed the public impact wouldn't last that long.

No matter how big the collapse was, there were no photos of the stage as evidence because all the smartphones had been collected.

And it wasn't a fatal accident either? Then its shelf life would be really short.

I was expecting the pre-recording schedule to be moved to another location after things settled down, but...

[TeSTAR Member Park Moondae Narrowly Avoids Stage Accident... "Plans to Recuperate"]

Who was it?

[Seems like every time I see an article about this guy, he's being threatened by a steel frame]

[Shouldn't Park Moondae get some kind of warding ritual done to get rid of bad luck?]

[I heard he ran out onto the stage, why would he do that all of a sudden? It's fucking scary, like he knew the accident would happen and was trying to get hit on purpose;;; Did he want to send someone to their grave? The stage manager?]

└Someone almost died and there's a psycho bastard leaving comments like this

└Why would he gamble his life as a handsome idol who makes a ton of money? You gotta hand it to these loser incels and their delusions lol

I don't know who leaked it, but thanks to them, a brake was put on my activities. How very thoughtful of them.

Moreover, I still hadn't seen who the blame was shifted to for the stage collapse, or how VTIC responded to the situation.

Because...

I was in this state.

"I'm just trying to contact the company..."

"Mmm~ Just lie down. Get plenty of rest."

"......"

"D-do you want an apple, Moondae...?"

That's right.

...I've been hospitalized for a week.

For the record, I had no injuries other than a minor abrasion on my thigh.

Even that healed in two days.

*Why on earth.*

"I want to be discharged..."

"Ah, the doctor said no~"

"Th-that's right."

"......"

*These bastards must have gotten their stories straight somewhere before coming here with such obvious lies...*

*This is driving me crazy.*

And so, my forced rest began.
