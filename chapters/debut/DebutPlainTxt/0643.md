# Chapter 643

TeSTAR's first concert in Seoul was a passionate, resounding success.

It ended successfully.

"......"

WAAAAAAAH!

The lift mechanism closed above me as I descended below the stage. Though blocked and separated, the elation from the countless people above vibrated through my body.

I clenched and unclenched my fist.

The afterimages would not fade. Cheers, laughter, balloons, confetti, lights, immersion, passion. Every image and feeling remained imprinted on my mind in intense fragments. My hands still trembled.

"......"

Naturally, a question like this arose. When a performance concludes so thrillingly, what should the singer do next?

The answer was...

"Alright, let's get inside, wash up, and sleep for tomorrow."

"Yep."

Prepare for tomorrow's show.

It was too early for a post-concert mood. This was only the first day.

*Save the fancy afterparty for your dreams.*

We had to conserve our energy.

However, one more event remained before we could go to bed. The aftermath of the concert hit immediately.

"You all worked so hard!"

"Ah~ It was so good!"

[TeSTAR's first concert was seriously so fun ahㅠㅠ I cried so much my head hurts]

[I have achieved everything...]

[I was so happy, guys, thank you]

[My Star (Photo)]

These were the immediate reactions to today's performance.

"......"

I swiped through my smartphone, which I got back in the waiting room, and quietly turned the page.

This was enough.

Of course, I did not need to search for concert reactions. Messages also came from acquaintances.

[I enjoyed the show!]

T-Holic, Golden Age, Mirynay, Yeongrin, Myudi. They were all idols I had met throughout my career. In this brutal red ocean, even competitors congratulated each other on their performances without ulterior motives. Keun Sejin even said he got a text from one of the Aether members.

A few idols with schedules at this time even called right when our concert ended.

Spacer's Kwon Heeseung was one of them. The moment I answered, he started shouting through the receiver.

[What kind of workout have you guys been doing!]

Hm?

[Honestly, while watching the show, I was thinking, 'Wow, with this setlist, they're going to tell everyone to get lost, forget celebratory visits or anything'...]

Hey.

[But you guys are full of energy. I can hear you all laughing right now!]

I looked around.

"Haha!"

"Ah... this is nice."

It was just as he said.

The members sprawled on the waiting room sofas, drinking water. They had just finished a crazy, four-hour marathon where someone could have easily collapsed, yet their faces showed life.

*It was an energy expenditure that would have justified them crawling on the floor.*

It was as if the concert's heat had converted into energy.

"......"

The old me might have opened the Status Window, wondering if this was some kind of status abnormality.

But I knew now.

I chuckled and spoke into the receiver.

"...It's a concert."

[Ooh.]

They had kept running for years without burning out because they were that kind of people. Kwon Heeseung seemed to agree. As expected of someone in the same profession, he was quick to under...

[Ah, could it be... a recommended herbal medicine from Chungwoo-hyung's national athlete experience?]

"......"

[Did you brew something special for the concert? Please share, hyung!]

As if.

While getting ready to go home, I replied to a few calls and texts. During that time, many people visited TeSTAR's waiting room to offer congratulations and impressions. Company employees, staff, concert officials, and invited guests all stopped by.

The members' families arrived.

"My Ahyeon! When did you grow up so much... to be so composed and cool."

I saw Seon Ahyeon's parents rush in and pat his back. Ryu Chungwoo's father handed him a bouquet.

"Sob..."

"I-It's okay... Hey."

Bae Sejin comforted his own teary-eyed mother.

The concert, at the end of a record-breaking run, was packed to re-experience the activities of the past few years. This seemed to make it especially moving for the families.

"Son! Come here."

"Aigoo, Mom..."

Keun Sejin's parents shared an emotional hug. Nearby, Cha Eugene chatted leisurely in Spanish.

"Grandma!"

"Raebin, look at how thin our puppy's arms are. How does so much explosive energy come from this body..."

"I, I've actually gained weight..."

Kim Raebin's grandmother had also come all the way from Gangwon-do. It seemed her health had recovered a lot.

"......"

The whole scene was quite nice. I smiled faintly and put my smartphone away.

"Moondae, my~ you're looking as handsome and striking as ever today. How did you sing so well on stage today!"

"Thank you."

I joined in, exchanging greetings and thanks with the members' families. This kind of gathering was a bit awkward during our first concert, but after several years, there was no reason to be shy. We were all acquainted. No one was tactless enough to ask if anyone was coming to see me.

"Our master singer should go home and get a good night's sleep too, okay?"

"...Yes."

Just as the conversation flowed smoothly, the waiting room door opened again.

Thump.

A dark figure stood awkwardly in the doorway, holding a gift.

"...Hyung."

Keundal. It was Park Moondae, the one who had become Ryu Gunwoo. He looked around cautiously, about to step inside, then stammered.

"Hyung, ah, no, I mean..."

It must have occurred to him that the title 'hyung' did not fit their apparent ages and relationship.

But I just smiled.

"Welcome."

"...!"

As his eyes wavered, one of the family members asked a question.

"Excuse me, are you perhaps Moondae's relative?"

"Ah? Ah, yes... well."

"We're similar," I spoke up first. "He's a hyung I'm as close to as family."

"Oh, is that so?"

"Aigoo, why are you just standing there. Come on in, family member!"

Welcomed by the other members' families, he finally entered the waiting room with a dumbfounded expression. He then managed to hand me the gift he held. Through the translucent wrapping, it looked like a trophy-shaped mug celebrating the concert.

He carefully opened his mouth.

"Um, I waited until all the audience had left, so I'm a little late..."

"You're not late."

At my words, Keundal let out a small breath, his heart seemingly relaxing. Another expression appeared on his relieved face.

Overwhelmed emotion.

"...The concert. It was really good."

He began to pour out his feelings in an emotional voice. He mixed conscious informal speech with unconscious formal speech but seemed too moved to notice.

"Really, I was truly touched..."

*...Yeah, I know.*

I glanced at the members. Every one of them smiled slightly. It was a pretty good moment.

Although Keundal's words took a strange turn after that.

"Ah, and hyung, no, the other ticket you gave me! I, uh, gave it to an SNS friend..."

Perhaps thinking I would assume he gave it to a weirdo, he hastily added.

"Ah, she's a Loviewer! A TeSTAR all-fan!"

Good job.

"But she was a high schooler."

"...?"

"I received a pack of tissues..."

Whatever happened, it seemed he had a very bewildering time.

"Anyway, I just wanted to say that I think I gave it to a good person!"

"Right."

Honestly, it did not matter who he gave it to.

*...Though it's even better that it went to a fan.*

Her personal details were not important. What mattered was that I knew what kind of feeling she had been sitting there with.

I grinned.

"Thanks."

"Hup, no!"

He waved his hands, insisting he was the one who was thankful, but he could not hide his smile. A couple of members nudged my side.

"It's nice that you enjoyed it."

"It is."

After that concert review, the conversation continued in a relaxed atmosphere.

"Where should we go if we get a vacation in the summer?"

"Ah, then...! The sea! J-Jeju Island?"

"Sounds good."

The members' families stayed for a while before leaving. However, the visits did not end there.

Just as we were about to finish cleaning up, someone knocked.

KNOCK, KNOCK.

"Is TeSTAR here?"

"...!"

The two members of VTIC who had taken my tickets arrived.

"Hello."

They wore masks and hats, but it was obvious they were not ordinary people. It seemed they had waited longer to approach cautiously.

"Wow, we really, really enjoyed the show!"

"...Thank you."

I accepted what was either a handshake or a high five from Chaeyul. I thought they would just watch and go home quietly. I did not expect them to go out of their way to greet us.

And I certainly did not expect what came next.

"Ah, I hope TeSTAR comes to see a VTIC performance next time!"

"...Is that something you've agreed upon with Cheongryeo sunbae?"

"Ah."

Right. I knew it was not.

"Yes."

"......"

I turned my head toward the voice. Cheongryeo had been watching quietly. He spoke calmly.

"Come see it."

"......"

I let out a small laugh.

"If I have time."

Cheongryeo smiled coolly and replied that he understood.

"Wow, has everyone left?"

"Yeah."

The waiting room grew quiet after the whirlwind of people swept through. The guys who had been humming on the sofa slowly got up.

"Should we go home now?"

"Wait, let's take a picture first!"

"OK... Oh, the rain stopped outside."

We soon learned the manager had stepped out for a call, leaving us with some time before we could go home.

*About 30 minutes, I guess.*

This was the longest departure delay we had ever had for a concert, but I did not feel bad about it.

I suddenly recalled what Keundal had said.

[I waited until all the people had left...]

Hmm... He did say the rain had stopped.

***

The cleaned-up concert stage was spotless.

"Hoo."

I walked quietly from backstage onto the stage. The cool floor pressed through my thin sneakers. On a night close to an early autumn dawn, the weather was cool.

"......"

A cool breeze blew as I took a deep breath. I stood silently in the dark, looking around the huge venue that had been filled with people. I had no particular intention. I just wanted to see this sight at least once, and since I had the chance, I came up.

I sat quietly on the stage. I looked out at the silent, cool audience seats, finding it hard to believe they had burned with passion just moments ago.

As I was doing that, I heard a voice.

"Moondae, what are you doing?"

"...!"

I turned my head. A figure walked onto the stage with light steps.

"...Chungwoo-hyung?"

"Mhm."

Ryu Chungwoo grinned and sat down next to me.

He was not the last one.

"Moondae-hyung is here?"

"...I think this is the first time we've come up like this after a show."

"The air... is very nice...!"

One by one, the members appeared on stage. They snickered or cleared their throats.

"Did you follow me?"

"Of course! You just snuck off by yourself."

The TeSTAR guys bounded lightly up the backstage stairs. They walked around the stage and came toward me. No... a few already had me in a headlock or were slapping someone else's back. Did they have energy to spare?

"But to think you tried to see this magnificent view all by yourself... Moondae Moondae, that's too much."

"That's right, we're a team. No solo actions from you, hyung!"

I could not believe Cha Eugene knew the words 'solo action'. I never thought I would hear them from the very epitome of it.

The guys soon stopped fooling around. They lay down or sat casually beside me.

And they looked at the sky.

"Oh~ The view is nice."

Yeah.

I lifted my head again. My eyes adjusted to the darkness and could make out detailed outlines.

"......"

I could see the starlight. Specks of light twinkled in the night sky, cleared by the rain.

The vast audience seats below remained empty, holding no light or heat.

However.

"Wow, look at the stars."

"The sky is very clear, probably because the rain stopped."

"Yeah."

*Right now, I am with my members.*

I remembered the lights that filled those audience seats and looked forward to meeting them again tomorrow.

"Moondae...!"

Seon Ahyeon called to me with a smile.

"This feels good... doesn't it?"

I lifted my head to the sky and smiled.

I was happy.

[The End-]
