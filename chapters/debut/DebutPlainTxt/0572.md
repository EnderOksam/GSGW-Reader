# Chapter 572

[Click Immediately (Link)]

That morning, the graduate student checked a message from the night before. Her friend, Park Moondae's first fansite master, had sent it.

Actually, she wasn't a graduate student anymore.

"Oh!"

She had finally graduated from grad school last December.

Freed from her status as a slave, or rather, a graduate student, the world looked three times more beautiful. She happily clicked the link. It was a new tour promotion video for TeSTAR.

*I even made all the reservations to follow the tour for at least a few days this year!*

She figured her friend knew about this grand plan and had sent the link to get her excited.

At the end of the video, a classic hotel building appeared in the background. When related videos popped up on both sides as choices, she exclaimed, "Wow!"

So this was content, too!

*This is like that game from the last album, isn't it?*

The thought reminded her of their last album's interactive game concept. She smiled, fondly recalling Park Moondae in his phantom thief outfit.

She guessed they had moved the format to WeTube to make it more casual.

"Hmm."

She examined the two choices, which were clearly distinct.

[-> Go inside the hotel.]

[-> Leave.]

Hmm. Normally, the story would only proceed if you went inside. The graduate student had a fun idea.

*Should I check the other one first?*

She had plenty of time to check both. Yes, now that she had graduated, she had all the time in the world. She had landed a job at a corporate research lab and was enjoying a sweet break before her start date.

Feeling relaxed, she clicked the bottom option.

[-> Leave.]

Her heart pounded in anticipation.

*In that album game last time... I remember getting caught by a guard and getting a game over, right?*

She imagined what would happen if she picked the wrong choice. The new title track was called 'Hunt', so she was excited to see how unconventional it would be.

The video played.

The hotel she had just seen slowly reappeared on a black screen.

[Goodbye, Loviewer~]

A subtitle in a pastel, rounded font appeared.

"...?"

She saw the heartwarming, smiling faces of TeSTAR waving excitedly from the sky. The semi-transparent, poorly cut-out images floated by like a soap opera ending.

[We hope you visit again. :)]

[-Happy End-]

"...?"

It was a happy ending. At least, that's what it said.

The graduate student rubbed her eyes, but the video remained the same.

"No way."

She stared blankly at the screen, then burst out laughing. What kidnapping?

*I guess I was overthinking it!*

She reflected that she hadn't played many games like this and had overgeneralized.

*Well, this is fun in its own way.*

She glanced at the many 'ㅋㅋㅋㅋ' in the comments, then pressed the back button to see the choices again.

*Let's do it properly this time!*

[-> Go inside the hotel.]

The video began, pulling her into the hotel to match the choice.

*Oh.*

In the silence, the screen showed a view looking down at walking feet, accompanied by footsteps.

TAP, TAP.

It was midday. The camera watched a pair of men's dress shoes neatly ascend granite stairs, then panned up to reveal their owner.

[Are you feeling sick?]

It was Ryu Chungwoo.

He wore a classic suit with his hair swept back, his handsome face showing a hint of concern.

"......"

The graduate student realized this wasn't like the last album's game. It wasn't from a member's point of view. The camera's perspective was her own, the viewer's.

[Let's go in.]

It felt like she was married to Ryu Chungwoo.

*A honeymoon!*

Well, maybe not married. They could just be companions, but his neat appearance and gentle demeanor naturally suggested it.

"Wow, crazy."

Even the former graduate student, whose opinion of Ryu Chungwoo was only moderately good, gulped. The Ryu Chungwoo on screen was that stunning.

*Is this that dating sim thing? Is that what this is?*

Don't tell me that's what 'Hunt' meant!

She thought of dating reality shows and nodded to herself. It seemed like it would be fun.

Some might have agonized that TeSTAR would create an embarrassing moment with overly calculated comments at this stage of their career. The graduate student, a layman in this area, simply enjoyed the video.

They entered the magnificent hotel. A counter was visible beyond a traditional, chandeliered lobby. Behind it stood a tall employee with a chilly impression.

[...Thank you for visiting the Red Wonder Hotel. How may I help you?]

[Ah, yes. We have a reservation.]

Kim Raebin handled the check-in at the desk, dressed in a hotelier's uniform with red stripes. His neat bangs gave him a strange, boyish charm.

*He's handsome!*

From this point on, however, a strange feeling began to set in.

Thump.

A grandfather clock started ticking. Tick, tock. As meaningful BGM played softly, the employee tapped the register with a pen and looked up.

[......]

*Huh?*

*Did the front desk employee just glance at me, the 'protagonist'?*

But when the employee spoke again, his tone was still polite.

[Yes. I've confirmed it.]

[Do you have any other requests?]

Next to Kim Raebin, who stood with his hands politely clasped, the choices appeared again.

[-> A comfortable high floor, please.]

[-> A quiet room in the outer wing, please.]

Ooh. This was unusual.

It seemed to be a thriller, not a romance. She naturally reached for a choice.

*It has to be the high floor! If I go to a quiet room, won't I get hunted?*

Making a thriller-movie deduction, she clicked the related video without hesitation.

[A day has already passed.]

"...?"

[Did you rest well? Let's go back now.]

A video showed Ryu Chungwoo smiling and looking back at her. They walked out of the hotel door together as sunlight poured in.

"......"

[We hope you visit again. :)]

[-Happy End-]

The protagonist had apparently enjoyed a happy hotel staycation in a comfortable high-floor room.

It was a happy ending.

"......"

The graduate student watched this scene several times.

That was right. If she chose the 'right' option, a happy ending about a 'happy hotel staycation' would pop up, and the choices would end.

"......"

She looked at the comments. A forum of denunciation unfolded in languages from all over the world. 'Are you serious?', 'The endless forcing of happy endings', and 'I'll put up with it because Ryu Chungwoo is handsome'.

It was the same after that.

[-Happy End-]

The structure was designed to skip all intermediate content and link to a happy ending at the slightest misstep. Let your guard down, and it was a happy ending.

"......"

The graduate student suddenly went back to the absurd, soap-opera-style happy ending she saw after choosing 'Leave'. She reread the comments, which were full of 'ㅋㅋㅋㅋ' that she had initially skimmed.

[ㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋ]

[You again]

[Wow, a happy ending no matter what you do, truly amazing!]

[Don't just put the ending where you go back with Chungwoo, ffs, at least let me see Ryu Chungwoo's face]

[This is so tilting, for real ㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋ]

She realized.

*I have to pick the suspicious options...!*

And so began her fierce battle of wits to avoid the happy ending. She was completely immersed.

Events where members talked to her or made cool appearances kept popping up. She found herself clicking through the choices that continued the story, just to see them.

"Ah, seriously!"

She felt the catharsis of an unpredictable reward popping up after finally choosing correctly.

[Hello!]

Cha Eugene suddenly invaded the room from the ceiling on a rope, grinning. Bae Sejin bought her coffee at the hotel cafe. Lee Sejin rang the doorbell, smiling brightly and claiming to be room service.

[Shall I get that?]

And in the elevator, she met the blunt but kind bellboy, Park Moondae.

"UWAAAAAH!!"

[Ah.]

The former graduate student clenched her fists as she watched Park Moondae. He kept glancing at her before reaching out to press her floor button, even giving an awkward wave.

*This is making my heart race a little!*

There was a definite fun in finding which videos the members were in and building rapport with them one by one.

Meanwhile, the story progressed through occasional ten-minute videos.

[Have you ever heard the rumors about this hotel?]

[About the missing people.]

Bae Sejin revealed he was a reporter and casually discussed the rumors surrounding the old boutique hotel.

[That this hotel was the last place several of the missing people stayed... that's the story.]

[Not that it's very credible.]

The rumor took a more concrete shape through Cha Eugene, who had secretly infiltrated the vents to find his missing friend.

[It's this person!]

The photo of the friend Cha Eugene showed was Kim Raebin, the man from the front desk.

"Gasp!"

The former graduate student agonized over whether to tell Cha Eugene this fact. She chose 'Tell him' and nervously watched the video. Thankfully, the story continued.

Cha Eugene expressed his thanks and climbed back into the air vent.

*Phew!*

Her relief didn't last long. The moment she met Bae Sejin again, he gave her an unexpected pointer.

[Are the staff friendly? Ah, that bellboy.]

[...He pressed the button for your floor for you?]

[How would the bellboy know which floor you're staying on?]

"GAAAAASP!"

The story, peppered with twists, hurtled toward its climax.

The former graduate student once again encountered the 'bellboy' in the elevator.

[...Hello.]

"HOOOOO."

Next to the bellboy, Park Moondae, who wore a white cap pulled down low, choices appeared again.

[-> (You had picked up an item for the bellboy.)]

[-> (You had not picked up an item for the bellboy.)]

The answer seemed obvious.

*I definitely have to go with the one where I was kind! The choice of not picking it up is clearly a landmine! Doesn't that happen a lot in dramas?*

However, there was a problem.

*A choice about whether or not to pick up an item for the bellboy... Did I ever choose that?*

She didn't think so. A video with that event might exist, but it wasn't among the related videos she had reached.

It was like a personality test where following the choice arrows leads to a tangled algorithm.

*No... but still, if that had come up, I would have definitely helped him!*

Unable to overcome her desire to see what happened next, the graduate student rationalized her choice.

And she clicked.

[-> (You had picked up an item for the bellboy.)]

The moment she did, someone else turned their head from where the bellboy should have been.

It was a smiling Seon Ahyeon.

"...?"

His beautiful face slowly zoomed in.

[You never picked anything up.]

Thud.

The screen glitched, whooshed, and seemed to fall to the elevator floor. Then it faded to black.

"...!"

It was the first game over.

The former graduate student covered her mouth and stared at the screen for a long time. After the light, annoyingly repetitive happy endings, this was even more shocking.

She marveled at the taste of meta-horror before she finally pressed the back button.

This time, she honestly clicked 'had not picked up'.

*Whoa, damn.*

She was so engrossed that a cold sweat ran down her spine.

Click.

The related video played immediately.

At that moment...

[A day has already passed.]

[Did you rest well? Let's go back now.]

The happy ending Ryu Chungwoo appeared again.

"Ack!"

The graduate student slapped her head.

*Where did I go wrong!*

She was about to accept that both choices were duds and ask her friend for a walkthrough. She stared at the handsome Ryu Chungwoo's face.

[Ah.]

Ryu Chungwoo stopped walking.

"...?"

Normally, this repetitive 'happy ending' ended with her leaving the hotel with him, but the lobby door didn't open. Instead, Ryu Chungwoo took something from his coat.

[Come to think of it... there's something an employee asked me to give you.]

[This.]

Ryu Chungwoo held out a thin, rectangular paper engraved with brilliant gold foil.

It was a ticket.

[He said... 'Thank you for answering honestly'.]

Thump.

Thump.

The ticket zoomed into focus as an instrumental played.

The graduate student realized the ticket's shape was familiar. It was something she had already seen in person.

[]

*Holy shit.*

It was a ticket for TeSTAR's upcoming tour.
