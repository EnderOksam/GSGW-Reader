# Chapter 212

I hung up the phone with the T1 representative and recalled the conversation.

*Please appear as a mentor on a K-pop global survival reality show... they asked.*

Of course, so far, more than a few survival shows have claimed to be 'global.' Because ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ had also claimed to select global K-pop stars.

However, the format for this one... was a bit unique.

*It won't be broadcast on Tnet and is also produced by Netplus.*

Perhaps the production was inspired by the small success of ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ on Netplus in the US and British regions.

The show's composition was quite experimental, but it was more important that the deal was good.

– As soon as you confirm your appearance on the show, we'll discuss forming an exclusive team.

How efficient.

An exclusive team for an appearance on a variety show, the exchange rate was worth considering.

*And TeStar won't be the only mentor appearing.*

And I wasn't the only one who thought so.

All the members' responses when I forwarded it to them were positive, saying, 'It's worth a try.'

"Mm... Seems fine."

"They will establish a dedicated team if we make an appearance? I think this will be very beneficial in the long run!"

However, there were also concerns.

"...Though, I'm a bit reluctant since the producers are those people."

"B-but I don't think I have enough skills to teach anyone... W-will it be all right?"

It was an expected conversation, and most of it was organized reasonably.

"I don't think you have to worry about that. You're good."

"Right, right~ Looking at the nature of the program, I don't think we'll get bashed as 'mentors', right?"

"That's right."

It seemed like the selling point of the program was having rookies as mentors. Besides, there was no reason for the mentor to be evil-edited.

What kind of program was it, you ask?

After we accepted the offer to appear on the show, the words we heard in the meeting with the producers will explain that.

"We will gather K-pop haters and train them for K-pop activities. Of course, it's a one-off that'll end as a humor shot."

"...??"

The members were a bit taken aback.

*They're still the same as always.*

That's putting it bluntly, to say the least.

Ryu Seorin, a writer I hadn't seen in a long time, had glistening eyes.

Even now that TeStar had become this popular, this person's attitude had only become rougher.

*Is it because she doesn't have to gently coax people as she was while casting?*

Perhaps paying mind that Ryu Chungwoo was somewhat uncomfortable with the writer, Keun Sejin quickly replied.

"Hmm~ So, the composition would be that foreigners who didn't know much about K-pop would become attached to it as they get to know it, right?"

"That's one way to look at it."

It means you're painting a bigger, more aggressive picture. Of course, we don't need to know the details.

The producers only asked one thing from TeStar.

"You must do well no matter what."

Which wasn't particularly worrisome.

"I will try to structure the script as well as possible so that your skills will look even more superior."

"...That's right! Our TeStar members are always amazing! We just have to work hard to capture that image well on the screen~"

Perhaps a bit taken aback by writer Ryu Seorin's unfiltered words, the other producers intervened and slightly hyped TeStar up.

At that moment, Ryu Chungwoo, who had been quiet, calmly asked.

"Then we won't look unnecessarily arrogant on the screen, right?"

"...!"

"...Of course."

Writer Ryu Seorin answered calmly. However, she seemed a little agitated.

*Aha.*

Her attitude from just now was to get a head start.

It seems she came out stronger because she feared that TeStar, who didn't have good feelings toward the production team, would make it difficult.

*I should've seen it coming when they offered an appearance in exchange for an exclusive team in the first place.*

Word has already gotten around with the people involved that TeStar would not easily agree to be a part of this production.

However, TeStar has now shown signs of discomfort at the fastball so they couldn't be messed with. It was clear that T1 had put quite a bit of pressure on this.

*Well, I won't have to worry about the editing.*

The production team wasn't stupid, so there was no way they would have a fallout with the parent company.

"Various K-pop stars, including TeStar, have already been recruited as mentors..."

"Yes, yes."

The rest of the meeting went smoothly and ended with no particular issues.

And a few days later, the articles also became a big hit.

★$
[K-pop joins hands with Netplus to produce a large-scale survival variety show.]

['A Guaranteed Extraordinary Cast'... What's the deal with Netplus's new K-pop reality show?]

[Netplus's 'Know KPOP Now,' the biggest investment in K-pop variety show... Are they aiming for the K-pop market?]
$★

It seemed that the company name, T1, was not mentioned on purpose, perhaps because they wanted to independently grow the image of a new production studio.

Instead, and perhaps intentionally, the gossip about the cast on the Internet rotated quickly and loudly.

★$
- Seems like ㅂㅇㅌ appearance is confirmed.

-#[TN: The hangeul is supposed to be initials for someone or a group, but I have no idea what they stand for. Might be VTIC and TeStar.]#-

└ You're delusional. Why would the biggest of male idols do a third-rate variety show on Netplus?

└ You dumbass, how is Netplus third-rate? ㅂㅇㅌ documentary was produced by Netplus ㅋㅋㅋ

- I heard that the line-up was gathered without conscience and the people in the field are buzzing about it. I'm curious.

- The producers of Might Studio? Don't screw with me. It's confirmed that this is the hideout of Idolco producers ㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋ

└ Syeombyeol and even Young1rin, they're all appearing. Looks like all of Idol1co cast was caught.

-#[TN: Syeombyeol = TeStar's search prevention name]#-

└ The whole Internet is about to turn upside down

- No, what the hell kind of program is this? You sure it's an audition? How is this a survival show with that kind of lineup?
$★

If they had known that the mentor's role wasn't that big, it would have cooled down quickly, but people who didn't know were excited about the rumors of their appearance.

*They're enjoying the benefits of not revealing the details.*

With all kinds of idols being mentioned, the first day of filming approached without disclosing an official cast list yet.

And after an 11-hour flight to LA, I could confirm the identities of the cast rumors.

"Hello."

*Fuck.*

It was really VTIC.

Though, not the rest of them, only Chungryeo.

*He wasn't in the cast lineup I heard about beforehand.*

As it turns out, he only gave them the okay sign at the last minute and boarded a private plane to join us.

I'm unsure, but one of the first-class mentors must have been cut off.

"I didn't expect you to participate in the audition program as one of the mentors, seonbae-nim, especially alone."

And in a variety show like this at that.

And Chungryeo gave an unexpected answer.

"Ah, I've got a solo album coming out soon, so I thought I'd do some global promotion?"

"......"

"Like hoobae-nim suggested, I've been trying to redirect some of my activities outside of the group. Haha."

"Ah, I see."

*How did he manage to persuade his company?*

The same agency, that didn't budge even if fans regularly sent trucks demanding individual activities, gave him a solo? Did he threaten to leave?

...Wait a minute.

*Could it be that there have been no solo activities until now due to his influence?*

He could have been artificially blocking the other members to make them cling to the group activities.

*Well, it's none of my business.*

Other than the fact that it was unpleasant, it didn't matter whether this guy came or not.

"...Park Moondae! Let's go sit down!"

"Ah, yes."

At Bae Sejin's loud call, I returned to my designated seat in the booth and sat down.

Bae Sejin covered the microphone and hurriedly whispered.

*Why- why does that crazy bastard keep talking to you?*

*I know, right? Maybe he wants to get his head smashed again.*

*...*

It's convenient to have support like this from people who know the whole story of my vacation injury.

*Let's just go like this.*

And let's be honest, I don't care if this program fails.

It would be the production team's fault if they brought in a lineup like this and still failed. TeStar can't be blamed.

Moreover, the company approved the proposal to form an exclusive team just yesterday. Now, it's just a matter of bringing in some capable people when the promotion for this album ends.

*Then, that's enough.*

We finished carrying out this deal, so as long as TeStar doesn't get cursed at during this program, that's enough.

The flight time was a bit of a waste, but since we were here anyway, we might as well balance the schedule by taking care of the US schedule.

*Well, looking at the script, the contestants are the ones to eat all the aggro.*

I really wondered if the producers of ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ crawled up from the pits of hell.

"Going into standby!"

"Yes~"

The mentors watched as the contestants' faces reflected on the monitor screens.

All the appearances and behaviors were according to the script.

***- I'm ready to be famous!***

***- I can't help but say that I don't really think of this as something cool, but I can obviously lie a bit to get some hearts on social media. Isn't that the same with everyone?***

***- I want people to know me, I want them to be excited, I want them to be crazy about what I'm doing, and I'll do anything for that.***

The friendly Korean subtitles under the English emphasized the identities of these people.

To put it bluntly, they were attention seekers.

It was perfect for the criticism, 'These people would sell their soul to become famous or a celebrity.'

And the program recruited participants under the promise that 'Celebrities will teach you how to become famous.'

And there was one more thing that the production team subtly left out specifying.

It was the music style.

***- K-pop? No! I'm like, um... I don't listen to it! Like ever! (Laughter)***

***- At school, uh, you know? I've seen a lot of kids listening to that.***

As the writer, Ryu Seorin said, these guys disliked K-pop.

No, 'dislike' was too dull of an expression... I think 'ignore' would be a more appropriate description.

That was precisely how Cha Yujin felt when he snorted at the American talk show we appeared on because of our game collaboration.

"Ooh~ That's too much!"

It was kind of funny to say something like that without hesitation while casually looking at the monitor.

Anyway, that's how the story of this program was established.

The kind of story where these contestants appeared on a reality show expecting, 'I'm going to be famous since this is airing all over the world on Netplus,' only to get a taste of K-pop training.

*That's probably why they insisted that we had to do well no matter what.*

It seems like they wanted to capture a scene where the participants had their mouths opened in amazement and declare 'I will never ignore K-pop again.'

*It's nationalistic for sure.*

Besides, this kind of aggro would definitely attract foreign K-pop fans as well. The challenge was how to handle that into universal appeal without being cringy.

"This is interesting."

"There can be people who might not like it!"

The guys were relaxed, looking at the monitors and taking turns talking. Since we knew that scenes like that would come out anyway.

*Honestly, there's a good chance that the participants have already known about everything and are already plotting on their own.*

Well, that was none of my business.

All we had to do was to play our part properly.

Mentors from each team would appear on a stage set, where participants were gathered together in anticipation of who the 'celebrity' would be.

With a very showy, 'K-pop-like effect' that people abroad thought of.

*Puff, puff, pop! Puff!*

**[Please welcome them with a round of applause! The ones who are going to teach you how to become famous... 7 teams of K-pop stars!]**

I could hear the A.I. host, supported by Osung, shouting in English.

Then a cloth fell from the top of the set, revealing the truth on an electronic display.

+=
#><Welcome to ☆K-POP Training Camp☆><#
=+

Needless to say, the response was not favorable.

**[Oh.]**

**[Oh, my god!]**

However, the filming immediately moved on to the next cut showing the attention seekers bewildered, being intentionally over-excited, or forcing their smiles.

*There's no time.*

How much time could you expect seven popular idol groups to devote to this program?

So, while many of the mentors left work and others went back in to change their costumes and prepare, the participants were being walked through the new content.

It was called the 'Intro Stage'.

**[During this stage, you could walk away as the winner of this reality right now. Before even entering the camp!]**

To sum it up, you could say it was, 'You're not acknowledging them as mentors, right? Then, if you can win, we'll give you the prize money'.

And I was the first to take charge here.

**[Our mentors~ Please come in!]**

It was a childish setup, but likely to work.

**[With 100 randomly selected LA residents as your audience, you'll take turns singing the same song as your mentor!]**

**[Of course, it won't be a K-pop song... but a pre-selected pop song you chose! You picked it as your personal expertise, right?]**

**[And your mentor will be checking out that song now!]**

It was time to take off and attach the rank badge.
