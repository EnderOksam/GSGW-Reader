# Chapter 547

Cheongryeo's 100th restart.

I knew Judan hadn't debuted with VTIC in this place. I just never expected to meet him in such a ridiculous situation.

After being Cheongryeo's Status Window and then getting deleted, I opened my eyes outside a VTIC concert venue in the middle of the night.

On top of that...

I pressed my temples and asked again.

"The embassy?"

*He knows the embassy's number? What is he talking about all of a sudden?*

"Yes."

Judan, however, nodded nonchalantly.

"It's midnight, you're not even wearing a coat in this weather... It looks like you got drunk and were robbed."

"......"

"If you think you still have your passport, you should check for it first."

He was basically saying I looked like a tourist who got wasted, stumbled around a foreign country, and got mugged.

...I must look pathetic. I asked, still pressing my temples.

"...Do you have a mirror?"

"You can just use your smartphone's selfie camera mode..."

Judan stopped mid-sentence and gave me a peculiar look. It was an expression that said, 'I knew it'.

"It seems you've lost your smartphone too."

"......"

He was creating a narrative for me.

This was actually good. I didn't have to make any excuses.

"First, I'd like to move somewhere warm."

"Ah, a place nearby we can enter... Hmm, this way."

I quickly followed the guy as he started to lead the way. As I walked behind him, my mind slowly regained its composure, separate from the pain.

*My current state is...*

The feeling that my mind would fly away if I lost focus for a moment was still there, but it was somehow bearable. My physical condition, however, was garbage.

*It felt like my whole body was being shattered.*

It seemed to be an aftereffect of being torn from that bastard Cheongryeo's Status Window. I thought the worst would be over, so if this was it, it was a bit disappointing but average...

"......"

Then I slowly realized.

*Am I in my right mind?*

What the fuck was I so casually accepting? I just escaped the insane experience of being a Status Window for three months. What was there to be disappointed about? I should be happy. What kind of bullshit was 'average'?

It was the same inside the System. I was joking around and even sending emoticons? Me? Instead of racking my brain to find a way back.

*This is strange.*

Furthermore, this deletion incident was the same. Considering that bastard's thought process, it was a problem I should have anticipated. Shouldn't I have gritted my teeth and prepared to backstab him first?

*...No, that's not right.*

I recalled coldly. I definitely thought that way at the beginning, when I first became a Status Window.

*But at some point, I forgot.*

And I got hit without being prepared. I couldn't see the big picture and just appealed to my usefulness, begging him not to delete me... No. I wasn't appealing for 'me', but for the usefulness of the Status Window.

"......"

Son of a bitch.

Did I just try to equate the Status Window with myself? This was driving me crazy. I wiped my face with both hands.

*Is this the kind of influence you're under inside the System?*

So, in conclusion... I had assimilated to the point where I felt comfortable with the resistance and pressure I used to feel when accessing the System. To a dangerous degree.

I wiped away a cold sweat and thought calmly.

*It's actually a relief that I was thrown out.*

My physical condition was the worst right now, but it was a relief to be free from this damn mindset. At least I could feel that something was wrong and get the chills. The more I looked back on my three months as a Status Window, the more absurd it seemed.

...And there was something I finally understood.

*So Keundal went through this process too.*

His personality traits disappeared, and he gradually assimilated into being part of the System. It left a bitter taste in my mouth, especially since I had no idea what was happening in reality right now.

*I just hope the flow of time in reality isn't the same as here.*

If Park Moondae was missing for three months, the internet would be in an uproar. Even if I went back, I couldn't promote like before because I'd be too busy explaining everything.

*Hoo.*

How do I handle this? I pressed my head, troubled by the dizzying possibilities.

Just then, the guy next to me started rambling.

"Just in case you're suspicious that this is another crime, like human trafficking, because I just happened to talk to you at the perfect time..."

*When is he going to get to the point?*

"I'm a trainee at LeTi. I came back for a moment to get something I left behind."

"Ah, yes."

"If you're wondering why a trainee like me came to observe VTIC's concert..."

Oh, it's obvious.

"Are you in the debut group?"

"...Yes."

"The company probably requested it for you to gain experience."

Companies usually brought trainees for media play, creating a 'passionate rookie' image to get them noticed. Since LeTi was a rich company, they probably brought him to the LA concert to show off the symbolic status of being in America.

"...Yes."

Judan finally shut his mouth and just walked. It seemed I was correct.

The place the guy led me to was a cafe and bar. Most cafes overseas would be closed at this hour, so it was an appropriate choice. The heated air was warm, but my body ached so badly I couldn't feel much of a difference.

I replied rationally.

"Thank you for guiding me, but as for the cafe... I don't have any money."

"I'll buy you a cup of coffee."

Ah, I was waiting for that. I suppressed the urge to down a whiskey and ordered a hot latte.

"I'm sorry, but after you call the embassy, I don't think I can accompany you any further. It's already close to midnight, and it's difficult for me to move around after that."

"Yes. That's LeTi for you. A curfew."

"......"

"You have official promotional materials to release, and there's the issue of witness accounts. I understand."

"......"

Why. Judan seemed to be agonizing over something before he asked.

"Are you perhaps an industry insider?"

*You're only figuring that out now?*

I didn't answer, but the guy scanned my face and nodded.

"Your appearance is handsome, but as you know, this industry values age, so you probably aren't an idol trainee. In that case..."

"......"

*Is it real that I'm sitting here in the middle of the night listening to this?*

I had already figured out my appearance from my reflection in the window here. I was in Ryu Gunwoo's body. It was the haggard appearance of my office worker days, right before I died. The only saving grace was that I wore thin outdoor clothes. It would have been spectacular if I were in my pajamas. I silently drank the latte that was served.

"Ah, have you perhaps already debuted?"

I didn't answer. It was an ambiguous situation. I couldn't say I had debuted, nor could I say I hadn't. To return to my debuted state, I had to go back to reality... and right now, there was only one clue I could grasp.

[[Rebooting[]

That phrase. The pop-up I saw in the System, the one I encountered in reality before being dragged here. That was the only clue.

However, to see it again, I had to access the System. I would have to endure that brain-melting symptom while staying sane.

I furrowed my brow.

*Then do I have to make contact with Cheongryeo again?*

I had to somehow make good use of the bastard who was trying to restart to kill me again...

"......"

Wait a minute.

I recalled the moment that guy removed me as a Status Window. Cheongryeo's clothes at that time.

*He was wearing outdoor clothes.*

The reason he suddenly changed clothes when he had no plans to go out. Could it be...

"......"

Has he already restarted?

Come to think of it, it was a logical circuit. He removed the Status Window because it delayed his restart, so of course he would try to restart immediately.

Then... where am I right now? Inside his 101st restarted life? Or is this world operating separately, regardless of whether that bastard Cheongryeo restarted?

*That wouldn't make sense.*

Keundal had already figured out that going back to the past meant the world itself was literally being rewound.

I stared down at my empty coffee cup, my mind racing.

*Either way, it's decided.*

First, I need to figure out Cheongryeo's whereabouts.

...In that sense.

I raised my head. I saw a perfect person to use... no, a pretty decent accomplice.

"Hmm. If you've calmed down, it would be good to start contacting the embassy."

Judan was running his mouth like this while sipping the Einspänner he had ordered.

I answered the guy seriously.

"There's no need for that."

"...! The reason is?"

The guy's eyes flashed, as if I'd come up with some bizarre idea.

This was actually good.

I opened my mouth leisurely. Words that could capture this guy's interest would be...

Alright. I have no choice.

"I came from the future."

"Pfft!"

Judan spat out the Einspänner coffee he was drinking.

***

"...So."

Judan muttered quickly, as if in a trance.

"The current Leader of VTIC has the ability to go back in time, and you're a person from the future who inherited and used it..."

"Yes."

I cut him off, but it wouldn't have mattered if I let him ramble. We were in a foreign country, after all. Besides, even if someone understood, they'd probably think we were just fans playing with some made-up lore. It sounded that much like bullshit.

However, this nonsense worked well on Judan. With a dead-serious face, he asked a few follow-up questions to check for inconsistencies in my story. Even now, he was seriously verifying...

"This is truly fascinating. Have you ever heard of chaos theory? It's similar to the Butterfly Effect..."

"Yes. I've looked into many theories."

I hastily cut the guy off. There was no time. It would be better if I just quickly verified it for him myself.

"If you look up my personal information, you'll see that I should still be a university student right now. It'll show that I'm in Korea."

When I went as far as to log into my university account for him, the guy meticulously checked the information, then nodded his head emphatically.

It seemed to be working...

"By any chance, don't you want to meet the yourself of this timeline? Hmm, no. I wouldn't really recommend it. It's a rather dangerous proposition."

"......"

I'll endure it.

"So, do you believe me?"

"Let's act on the assumption that I do. Honestly, you seem to know me quite well."

"......"

"I suppose we had a connection in the future?"

I gave a slight nod.

"Excellent."

The guy sounded quite reliable. Judan straightened his appearance, then interlaced his fingers and spoke.

"First... you seem to be older than me, so please speak comfortably."

"Alright."

"......"

He fell silent, seemingly flustered that I agreed at once. Whatever.

"...May I also speak comfortably?"

"Go ahead."

"Actually, it's fine. I'm more comfortable with formal speech."

*Then why the hell did you ask?*

Anyway, I returned to the main topic.

"...Understood. Anyway, there's one thing I want to confirm here."

I looked at the guy seriously.

"Whether Cheongryeo has, by any chance, returned to the past."

"...!"

The color drained from Judan's face. A little thrill had been added.

"In that case, Cheongryeo might have already..."

I nodded.

"That's what I need to confirm."

Judan swallowed hard.

"I understand."

Thanks for taking the bait.

***

I stayed the night at Judan's lodging. It was a decent business hotel that he said the company had paid for.

"Don't you have a roommate?"

"It's originally a two-person room, but he went out to stay somewhere else with his family. Thanks to that, I've been using it comfortably."

So there was a golden spoon in the debut group. Anyway, thanks to that, I avoided making a clumsy excuse and sleeping on the floor.

However, I couldn't fall asleep even after lying down in bed that night. I wasn't sure if it was okay to sleep, and the pain was severe. That gave me plenty of time to reflect on my judgment.

Now. First, short-term countermeasures, like 'how to deal with that bastard Cheongryeo depending on the situation'. Things like that could be planned while checking the situation as it unfolded. My physical condition wasn't great, but I could still use my head.

The problem was this. The fundamental threat.

*To check the clue, I have to go back into the System.*

And what kind of influence I'll be under when I go back.

"......"

Damn it. Even if the clue itself was bait, I had to see it.

*Because I have to go back.*

And so, I stayed up all night, lost in intense calculations.

The next day, Judan guided me back to the VTIC concert venue. The concert was actually tomorrow, but most of the staff were already there.

"If you use the identity of my cousin, you won't be questioned at the security check."

He had created some background information on his own, but I just let it be. I had to leave him that much fun so he'd continue to cooperate.

"For your information, there are no particular rumors about Cheongryeo."

...Okay.

So, since morning, I had been following the guy, sneaking toward the staff entrance of the VTIC concert. My head, however, was still cool from the problem for which I hadn't yet found a solution.

*A way for me to keep my sanity inside the System.*

That's what I needed.

In the process, we inevitably passed by the place where I had come to my senses yesterday. Even in my dazed state, I could tell the atmosphere had changed 180 degrees.

[WHOA!]

It was bright and noisy. The excited chatter and laughter of people echoed from all around, and VTIC's hit songs cut through the gaps in between. It was a scene befitting concertgoers.

The interesting thing was that I could also hear songs that weren't VTIC's at all.

[Come on, baby you know it's real!]

[Just like sweet cotton candy!]

"Hmm, it looks like a K-pop random dance challenge. The one to the west seems to be a performance by a professional dance team."

Judan nodded as he looked at the plaza-like area. All over the wide space, small stages abounded, set up for WeTube videos or to enjoy the pre-concert festivities.

"VTIC is a group that represents the genre. So their concert commemorations often become festivals for K-pop itself. You probably already know, but just to add an explanation."

Yeah. I already know. I've seen it a few times at our concerts, too.

"......"

Hmm.

I checked my appearance. A stranger in a hat.

Then I asked.

"Can anyone participate in something like that?"

"Yes. They probably can, but usually, as a matter of etiquette, only local fans participate..."

I didn't listen to the end and started walking.

"Excuse me?"

***

A moment later, in the middle of the noisy, excited festival, a man with a black hat pulled down low stood on the edge of the stairs, holding a portable microphone.

"......"

Judan wondered what this sudden action was. He suspected he might have been fooled by smooth talk and was now guiding a madman. For now, he stood opposite him and watched.

The man checked the microphone with a tap, tap. He was surprisingly skillful, yet something seemed awkward. As he was about to choose a backing track on WeTube... he flinched.

"......"

There were almost no songs that TeSTAR's Park Moondae had sung. They were all songs that would be released in the future, a future that would unfold differently from this one. For now, they were just imaginary songs that existed only in his head.

And of course.

*...Right. They don't exist.*

There are no TeSTAR songs either. Not a single one.

"......"

However, that didn't mean he couldn't sing. The man finally chose a song. Soon, along with a faint backing track, the man raised the microphone.

TAP. TAP.

In the very beginning. The song he had practiced to become an idol, right after he crash-landed into a new life. The song he had practiced so often back then that it still flowed out with the same feeling.

It came out from Ryu Gunwoo's mouth.

[I remember the me I used to know.]

.

It was Park Moondae's first evaluation song from ❬Re-listed ! Idol Inc. ❭. In this place, it was a solo song released just a few weeks ago by the youngest member of the disbanded group T-Holic. Here, that song, which hadn't even started climbing back up the charts yet, was now coming out through Ryu Gunwoo's voice.

"Beyond the shining, dazzling- seasons, to the tired and weak times."

Overlapping with the poor microphone quality, his raw voice rang out.

"The one that holds the broken me, oh..."

[Only me.]

[Yeah, It's me.]

His voice spread.
