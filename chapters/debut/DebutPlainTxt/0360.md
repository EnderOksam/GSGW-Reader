# Chapter 360

An hour had passed since we learned that all our carrot coins had been stolen.

"Aaaahhh."

Inside the comfortable house, everyone was in a state of mental chaos.

Kim Raebin, who had drawn the note, was huddled in a corner, drenched in cold sweat.

"Carrots... Carrots."

It seemed like a big shock.

"Then..."

Ryu Chungwoo calmly opened his mouth.

"That means we don't have any money at all right now."

*Dong!*

When they edited this, an exclamation mark would definitely appear above our heads. These guys were genuinely shocked.

I crossed my arms and spoke quietly.

"In other words, we're beggars."

"Th-that's it..."

"How could something like this happen overnight?"

All we had left was one dog.

A little bundle of fur jumped around the devastated members as if to cheer them up.

*Maaaang! Woof!*

The members' expressions brightened a little.

"Well, I'm glad they didn't steal Mungaemungae, right?"

"Y-yeah!"

"...It's still a pup, so there's a risk. I'm glad they didn't take it away. It could have gotten hurt."

*Was he serious?*

To be blunt, I wondered if this much shock was really necessary just because some entertainment money had disappeared, but then I realized we'd been completely relaxed for the past two days...

*No, it wasn't a side effect.*

"...!"

*We got tricked.*

These guys had planned everything and executed it perfectly.

To make us this shocked while we were completely off guard...

They'd done it brilliantly.

– Huh? What should I do? Why did you pick this? The other message penalty slip probably didn't contain this message.

– Then show us, so we can check!

– Oh, you can't do that! We're going to rewrite it next time!

– Ugh! What?

*Both of the penalty slips probably contained the same contents anyway.*

I refrained from cursing as I remembered the face of the PD who was babbling so shamelessly.

*You called it healing, you fucker!*

*No... Let's calm down.*

This was a section that would be quite fun. The relaxation... Yes, two days of healing had already been more than enough.

I had to think like that. I definitely thought that way. Don't get upset. If I got upset here, I'd lose.

Meanwhile, Seon Ahyeon kept his mouth shut, then raised his hand as if he had made up his mind.

"Yes, Ahyeon."

"I-if we have yarn... We can make something... In fact, let's find out if there's something...?"

"Wow."

"Someone with good dexterity could come up with an idea like this."

It was a good idea. Seeing top-tier idols desperately sitting together and doing handicrafts would make for a unique and funny scene.

However, the production team's intentions were important here.

"...Well, it's true that it's a great idea... But isn't catching the culprit the top priority? The production team also said so."

Yeah, that was probably the direction this program preferred.

– Who on earth?? Who stole it?

– Yes! This unidentified night guest is someone currently living in this town!

Bae Sejin swallowed his saliva.

"They're telling us to catch the culprit ourselves."

"Hmm."

The members fell into thought. After a while, Kim Raebin muttered seriously, his face pale.

"If we get harmed..."

"Oh, that won't work."

"Y-yes. It could be dangerous..."

*No, you guys were taking this way too seriously.* This wasn't that kind of show, and the production team's top priority was making people laugh, so there was no way they'd actually put us in danger.

Lee Sejin nodded his head seriously.

"Yes. It could be dangerous."

He looked excited. Finally, some hardship and adversity had come our way, giving us something to work with... Was that why?

Ryu Chungwoo proposed a compromise.

"But right now, we need food... For now, let's help out and earn some coins."

"Yes!"

"In the meantime, let's look into who the thief might be. There must be some kind of clue."

"Good... That is a wise decision."

It was immersive, and compared to the carefree volunteer work we'd done the day before, this was actually pretty funny.

"......"

*No, I said I wouldn't get upset.*

Anyway, we set off with the cameras to look for residents.

"Father~ Is it okay if we continue cleaning the yard like we did yesterday? If you give us some coins, we'll be really nice to you!"

"Huh? It's okay."

However, surprisingly, people didn't give us any work.

"...?"

"How many houses have we been turned down by now?"

"Five houses."

It was an obviously unnatural situation.

The grandmother at the sixth house even clicked her tongue and said:

"Oh my... Those coins? I don't have any left. Can't I just give you something to eat before you go?"

The production team answered brightly.

"No~"

"Why are you answering for us?!"

"It's a rule! Why are people who know everything about the system like this?"

*These bastards were really...*

It seemed that the rural hospitality the production crew had been boasting about had suddenly disappeared overnight.

At this point, Ryu Chungwoo asked again with a smile. Even the saintly Chungwoo seemed confused.

"Then what should we do?"

"So! We have prepared something just in case."

*Yes, I guessed that they would.*

*They're definitely going to make us do some ridiculous amount of work...*

"It's a carrot farm!"

"...!"

*This wasn't the scale I thought.*

We took a short bus ride and arrived at a green farm spread across a vast flat field.

"Wow..."

"It's so spacious."

*How on earth did they arrange this? I guess they chose the farm first, then decided on the accommodation and coin system.*

The PD opened his mouth with a bright smile on his face.

"Did I tell you that physical carrots are exchanged for carrot coins on a one-to-one basis?"

"Ah, I remember!"

"That's right."

Among the admiring members, Kim Raebin looked relieved.

*Oh my.*

"So, can we exchange the carrots we harvest directly for coins?"

"Uh-huh~ Everyone, these carrots were grown in someone else's field a long time ago, but of course, you can't just give them away just because you harvested them~"

"......"

*I thought so.*

Then the PD made an absurdly unfair suggestion.

"First of all, if you harvest everything from here to there today, it'll be five coins per person!"

"Hey!"

"Don't do that!!"

"I'm leaving."

The production crew stifled their laughter as they watched our horrified reactions.

*Was this funny?*

"Everyone, you need to eat~"

"......"

Having grown accustomed to the show's absurdity, the members stared blankly for a moment before getting to work.

"Shall we start from there?"

"Okay."

"I don't like those carrots."

Even though they complained, they ended up doing their best.

I grabbed a tool and dug into the soil, pulling out rows of bright orange carrots.

*The image will come out well.*

We would come across as sincere young people who hadn't forgotten their roots and who worked hard despite their success.

*Or were we bastards who got fooled again?*

*Fuck you.*

*Either way, it was beneficial... Maybe I'd just been betrayed so many times that I was starting to feel numb.*

*Is this the inland version of the fishing boat I imagined before?*

"Let's put it here!"

"Okay."

We continued our hard labor, filling the huge basket.

The PD grabbed the megaphone.

"Okay, five more! And don't straighten your backs until you've pulled out the carrots."

"Ugh! Will you take responsibility if our backs hurt?"

"Huh? Are your daily wages too high?"

"Sorry."

Honestly, it wasn't that hard on the body. Compared to when I was active and practicing for a performance, it was just tiring.

"Work, work!"

"......"

*Still, the atmosphere was just right.*

*Being treated like medieval servants was infuriating...* Of course, it would become more interesting after editing. This must be it.

*I understand.*

Sometimes I would look at the production crew with resentful eyes and eagerly grab a carrot. It was bearable up to this point.

The problem arose from the following.

"Here's the daily wage!"

"Wow!"

"We won't eat carrots for a while. I won't even eat carrot cake."

"Yes, Moondae."

We went to the supermarket to buy dinner with our hard-earned coins... only to be faced with exorbitant prices.

"That... It'll be ten coins!"

"...!?"

Suddenly, the price of dalgona had increased tenfold.

"Twenty coins per piece?"

"We're dying!"

The price of meat skyrocketed as well.

Of course, some members turned their attention to the production crew and shouted at them.

"You said there was no manipulation!"

"We never said that! We didn't set the prices beforehand~ Absolutely not!"

Kim Raebin asked with an almost delirious expression.

"Then what is this situation..."

"How are we supposed to know what the residents are thinking? They can sell things at whatever prices they want."

"......"

*I didn't know who came up with this idea, but I sincerely wanted to punch them just once.*

In the end, we returned home with a meager haul compared to what we'd bought over the previous two days.

I declared calmly.

"Let's just make some sujebi and eat."

"Ah, ah..."

"Sujebi... It's delicious."

They were still trying to react for the cameras.

"Sorry... I was the starting point for all of this... It was my negligence..."

"What are you talking about! Of course, the PD played a trick!"

Kim Raebin, who had drawn the note, looked completely lost, and Bae Sejin and the dog were there to comfort him.

"......"

*This won't work.*

I tapped Lee Sejin, who was putting his luggage in the kitchen.

"Is there flour and butter left?"

"Hm? Yeah. There's some left behind... Why?"

"Give me a second."

I checked the butter I had overpaid for on the first day and headed to the kitchen.

I could use the free time the production staff gave us however I wanted.

---

Park Moondae created something.

*Mmm.*

As soon as Ryu Chungwoo came out after washing up, he smelled a fragrant smell. And then he saw the back of Park Moondae moving steadily in the kitchen.

*... Is he making Western food? I thought he said he was making sujebi.*

As always, he was conscious of the camera angles and took care to capture the cooking process properly.

*He's so calm.*

It was definitely a situation worth getting upset about, but he was silently coming up with a realistic answer. It was moments like this that made him feel like the real hyung here.

Ryu Chungwoo carefully entered the kitchen.

"Do you need anything?"

"It's almost over, so it's okay. The other guys helped me."

Park Moondae opened the air fryer.

A fragrant smell wafted out, revealing a batch of yellow cookies.

"Would you like to sample this?"

"Ah."

It was a cookie.

At that moment, Cha Eugene came out.

"I want to eat!"

"It's hot so be careful."

"Hehe!"

Ryu Chungwoo realized this when he saw Cha Eugene put a cookie in his mouth and give a thumbs-up with shining eyes.

*I guess he's thinking of selling snacks.*

It was a really good idea. He thought the members who didn't want to return to the carrot field would react well to the idea too.

"...! It's delicious."

"That's a relief."

Ryu Chungwoo was impressed by both the taste and the idea.

*I'll have to keep an eye on him and assist him when he's cooking in large quantities.*

But that idea was quickly shattered.

This was because as soon as the cookies cooled down, Park Moondae, who had put more than half of them into a container, said something else.

"The other guys are going to make sujebi... Hyung, let's go together."

"...Me?"

*Where?*

And the place Park Moondae took him... was the village hall where the residents had just finished dinner.

*...??*

*What on earth was going on?*

"Hello, old man."

"Ah! Aren't you that star who came with the cameras from Seoul?"

"The camera is with me today too. And I made something. It's soft, so it would be perfect to eat now."

"Oh my, what's this!"

The elderly residents, delighted to have something interesting happen for once, enthusiastically welcomed Park Moondae as he handed out the cookies.

Ryu Chungwoo suddenly joined in the action.

Park Moondae went one step further and even used Ryu Chungwoo to promote himself.

"This guy won a gold medal in archery at the Olympics. Do you remember him?"

"Oh my!"

"Yes, I think I remember. Yah~"

"Hahaha."

Ryu Chungwoo smiled, eagerly shook their hands, and gave them massages. However, he still didn't know why Park Moondae was acting like this.

"Let me sing a song."

"Okay, okay~"

Park Moondae even sang his own trot song and even danced to 'POP★CON', showing off all kinds of jokes.

"...??"

Ryu Chungwoo became even more confused, but without saying anything, he sang a verse and eagerly answered the seniors' questions.

*I guess he has an idea.*

Once the atmosphere had warmed up and the elders had lowered their guard...

Park Moondae got straight to the point.

"By any chance, have you heard anything about this bottle cap-looking coin? Did the people from Seoul say anything about this today?"

"Ah, that!"

The camera director looked confused, but Park Moondae finally uncovered the truth.

"That is..."

"I won't ever forget this..."

"Please find the culprit~"
