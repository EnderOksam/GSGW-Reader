# Chapter 599

"Hoo."

She was a solo Kim Raebin fan. At this point, she was a fan who had developed a full-blown love-hate relationship with the group.

The TeSTAR WeTube upload notification popped up, and she moved to her PC. Her nails clicked against the mouse. Anxious anticipation pricked at her heart, as it always did during a comeback.

*Kim Raebin already had a solo for the intro right before this, so it's a stretch for him to get it again... Ah, whatever. Just give it to him!*

The chef knows best how to eat the dish. Wasn't it obvious that the person who made the song would be the best at expressing it?

This was a truly crucial time. Even she, who usually maintained a stance of*I don't care about anything else, just let Kim Raebin succeed,*could feel it in her bones. There were subtle indicators.

*The follower count for a well-known translation account nearly tripled.*

This translation account did not change English to Korean. It translated Korean activities into English. In other words, the number of foreigners wanting to keep up with TeSTAR's news had increased. It was a sign of global demand and an indicator of fan growth.

This was all happening during their tour. They were not in a proper promotional period, yet the fact that they were growing little by little meant there was even more untapped potential.

*Foreign fans who got interested through the tour are going back through all of TeSTAR's activities and creating new accounts.*

Just by scrolling through her SNS timeline, she could feel a certain stirring. It created a strange tension among the fans as well. A feeling that the time had come.

In truth, TeSTAR had steadily built up elements to appeal to an international audience and had achieved results. Starting with ❬127 Section❭, their game collaborations, movie appearances, and variety shows all had global appeal. Thanks to that, TeSTAR's international fandom was by no means small for their level.

However, they had yet to have that one explosive, all-sweeping, hot-as-hell breakthrough.

[Ah... it feels like it's just one step away!]

It would be one thing if there was no possibility at all. Because it seemed so close, it was all the more tantalizing and frustrating. In that sense, the moment fans saw the tour gaining word-of-mouth praise from global critics, they felt it too.

[This... if this goes well, it could blow up?]

This reaction was not without foundation. The very fact that TeSTAR had gained the image of a 'conceptual singer-songwriter group making unique attempts' through their tour would have been impossible without the content they had consistently built up. It also meant their efforts to satisfy both domestic and international audiences had not been in vain.

This was why even she was a bit nervous.

*Seriously... if I'm feeling this way, those all-group fans must be getting their hopes up to the point of bursting.*

Normally, she would have worried that if TeSTAR became more popular overseas, they would neglect domestic activities and just get stuck doing endless concert tours.

*Hmm, unless the company suddenly gets sold, that's unlikely to happen?*

Surprisingly, even a somewhat radical solo stan like her was no longer concerned about that. They were no longer under the money-grubbing, incompetent T1. Since they were the types to stubbornly create content even with T1, the possibility was even lower now that they were independent.

So, she was all for them getting even more successful.

*Thinking about how I almost lost my mind over people saying they lost their touch during the Epic era... just do well.*

TeSTAR had worked nonstop for years. She hoped that this time, the group would not fizzle out or end up a dud, but would truly, madly explode.

"......"

Kim Raebin's fan found herself making a slightly sentimental wish.

"Ah, what am I saying!"

She immediately feigned disgust and snapped out of it.

*Let's just watch the intro!*

She logged onto the internet, ready for battle, but saw something she did not want to see.

[VTIC All Members Discharged Simultaneously... 'Returning to Our Main Profession at This Moment.']

"Aargh! Sh-,"

It was an article about VTIC's military discharge. An idol group that had won a Bill award, all discharged together on a coordinated schedule, was naturally receiving a lot of public attention.

[They've come to bring discipline to a slacking K-Pop scene]

[Coordinating their discharge dates is insane... I can feel their grit from here (that's a compliment)]

[I started an 18-month savings account for this, guys]

VTIC had only mentioned that they would 'be back soon' and had not officially announced a comeback date. However, everyone predicted they would definitely release something within the year.

*Putting on a show.*

She twitched her eyebrow. With all that experience, they should just become company directors.

Anyway, she took another deep breath.

"Hoo."

Just as she was about to watch TeSTAR's intro music video, she came across a short spoiler on SNS.

*Ugh, haa.*

It was about the main figure in the intro.

[Th-th-the intro Seon Ahyeon is in;sane]

"......"

*So it was him.*

Of course, they did not give it to Kim Raebin.

*Putting Kim Raebin front and center with some old-school hip-hop or a money-soaked performance is the shortcut to making the global audience go wild, those tasteless bastards.*

Kim Raebin's solo fan tapped at her PC, slightly annoyed.

"Ugh."

Her annoyance had killed her anticipation. She just wanted to get it over with. However, the moment she saw the thumbnail, she was momentarily at a loss for words.

"......"

Against a background of clear and hazy flowers and grass, a moon-like face shone brightly. Streaks of light filtered through the parasol he held, scattering across his cheek, jaw, and around his eyes. The way his eyelids were slightly lowered was so elegant it was a masterpiece in itself.

It was an aesthetic that could captivate even her, who was not particularly a fan of Seon Ahyeon.

*His face... I'll give him that.*

Her annoyance vanished. She finally pressed the play button firmly. The screen before her filled with a vast landscape.

"...!"

An elegant, light-drenched flower garden on a spring day, or perhaps nature itself, unfolded fantastically like an Impressionist painting. The sounds of butterflies and birdsong filled the air.

[Toutes les couleurs ont ton nom.]

A French narration whispered like a song through the sounds. A slow instrumental began.

Subtitles appeared.

[ALL THE COLORS]

HAVE YOUR NAME.

The white text soon faded, and the beautiful background shimmered like a watercolor painting again. Then, a tall dancer in a white outfit emerged alone, cutting through the static colors.

*Ah.*

It was Seon Ahyeon. Dressed in something between dancewear and slacks, he was crouched down. He rose with steps that drew elegant lines and opened his mouth.

[Morning glory

Dyeing everything in violet

A scent lingers

I can't stop trembling]

His arms and legs moved sleekly and gracefully to their very tips, drawing circles.

"......"

Every movement spread across the screen at a dramatically perfect angle. It was a solo dance in the light.

[My blue is gone

I'm not sad anymore

That bluebird is you]

It was an overwhelming sense of artistry, as if a masterpiece had come to life. Like the lyrics, which delicately traced each color of a prism's rainbow, the screen filled with a feast of colors. The pure white Seon Ahyeon boasted a surprisingly eye-catching presence, yet also blended into the scenery as if he belonged there.

No one could argue about why Seon Ahyeon was in the intro.

[From green to gold

My feelings ripen

With all of your moments]

With a slight tremble of his fingertips, Seon Ahyeon extended his arm to the corner of the screen and gently pulled it back. Then, from beyond his fingertips, someone else's hand entered the screen.

*Huh?*

What appeared beyond Seon Ahyeon's clasped hands was the hand of a black ballerina. Seon Ahyeon gently embraced the ballerina and danced a pas de deux with her.

But...

[Until our cheeks turn red

We breathe in sync

And then I keep meeting you]

"......"

*This is insane.*

The black ballerina moving with Seon Ahyeon was not a person. It was a mannequin with a black torso and delicately connected joints. Seon Ahyeon used only his own strength and technique to create the rebound of the joints, performing it as if it were a pair dance.

From penché to pirouette. Like lovers.

[The last color of yours is]

The screen, which had been like a Monet painting on a sunny day, gradually turned red as the song progressed, becoming more splendid and emotional. Then came the climax. The camera angle slowly moved sideways with the dancer's movements.

Seon Ahyeon, now standing before the water's edge, moved.

[in the Sunset]

Carefully, meticulously, Seon Ahyeon gently placed his foot on the surface of the water. Then he stepped out onto the lake, gliding as if without hesitation.

"......"

[Red is yours

your color, your name]

A dramatic and beautiful harmony burst forth, ringing pleasingly in her ears. The mountains, wildflowers, and grass on the other side of the lake swayed. Red sunlight poured onto the water's surface.

[More dazzling than the morning

Approaching for a kiss]

Wherever his feet touched, parabolas formed, and the sunset shimmered. Seon Ahyeon danced passionately on the water's surface with the torso, which was adorned with a disheveled bouquet of flowers.

[(Red is yours)

The sun sets

And the moonlight descends

With a color that can't be forgotten]

This is where the thumbnail scene appeared.

*Ah.*

Seon Ahyeon briefly dipped his hand into the water and pulled out a stick wrapped in white cloth. A parasol. However, he did not use it for himself, but respectfully shielded the torso. He elegantly flicked water droplets as he opened the parasol, tilting it over the torso in his arms.

He then wrapped his large hand around the torso's waist. It gave off a strange, sexual tension and a poignant feeling, but the song remained clear and pure.

[Red is yours

your color, your name]

To the other side of the water. Seon Ahyeon continued his elegant and passionate dance with the torso, using even the parasol as part of the splendid movements. They reached the end of the lake before she knew it.

[U- Um, u- Umm]

Seon Ahyeon's feet, stepping onto the now-darkening land, were captured on screen. He walked gently, arriving at a hill covered in a thick layer of flower petals. The center of the hill was shown in a close-up.

A white coffin.

"......"

Seon Ahyeon respectfully and delicately placed the parasol on top of the torso. Then, with a smile, he entered the open coffin with it and closed the lid.

Before the sun set.

THUD.

The screen silently turned black. A whispered narration followed.

[Les rêves ne finissent jamais.]

Soon, the black screen cracked open. A piercing light descended from between the cracks. Beyond the opened view, boys in casual clothes looked down at Seon Ahyeon with smiles.

The members. Their faces were relaxed, as if they had been laughing and chatting casually.

[Comme, une chanson d'amour.]

Park Moondae reached out his hand and helped Seon Ahyeon up. He was also in casual clothes now.

[Did you sleep well?]

[Yeah.]

Seon Ahyeon sat up. Their backs as they walked away together were leisurely captured with a soft instrumental, until it suddenly escalated.

[in the Sunset-]

The moment the instrumental climaxed.

THUD.

Something black fell from the side, covering the screen.

"...!"

It was the black torso with the parasol.

"......"

That was how the video ended.

[♪♪♩- ♬♩♪♪-]

Kim Raebin's fan sat still for a moment, listening to the melody of the song's chorus playing with the ending credits. This intro stimulated all five senses.

"...That."

She realized it then. It was not just the fans who were putting their full anticipation, tension, and strength into this album. Their artist knew it too.

*They went all out.*

It was clear they had poured their heart, soul, skill, and time into this. Watching the intro, which came out with a charm and perfection that made it impossible to guess how long they had been preparing it, Kim Raebin's fan clenched her fists.

"...Let's go, damn it!"

Excitement and anticipation surged. Something akin to a sense of duty she had never had before settled in her heart.

*If they brought out an intro like this, what in the world have they done for the title track!*

She laughed, full of spirit, as she watched the reactions flooding SNS and online communities.

But a short while later.

[In-in-insane, this is]

"......"

Something no fan could have ever imagined happened. One night, a certain video was suddenly released.

However, it was not a TeSTAR video.

[VTIC – 'Serpent' Official M/V Teaser 1]

"......"

The thumbnail featured a handsome man with sharp features, sitting bare-backed on a marble chair and looking back. It was Shino.

And the concept was...

[Th-th-this is Danbam, isn't it]

[Ah, crazy, the sweet babies who used to smile in knit sweaters are now like thisㅠㅠ]

The afterlife. It was VTIC's debut concept. They had taken their first concept and reprised it intensely, pushing it to the extreme.

[The grit is legendary...]

"You crazy bastards..."

The storm had begun.
