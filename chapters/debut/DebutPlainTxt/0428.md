# Chapter 428

An image flashed.

A scream.

A dull roar followed, then a flood of afterimages and flickering lights.

A massive impact crashed down from behind.

And I...

"......"

I opened my eyes.

[Junior]

The smell of dust and iron filled the air. A faint light spread at the edge of my vision.

A voice.

[Junior.]

"...Ugh."

Cough! A coughing fit cut me off. I could barely breathe, but I could see.

Flicker.

My smartphone, sent flying by the impact, lay face down on the cracked floor, its screen blinking. A low, muffled voice came through it.

[You're alive.]

*Yeah, I'm alive, you bastard.*

*Wasn't that the whole point of you telling me?*

But I was in too shitty a condition to say that.

*This feels like death.*

My vision swam. My heart beat so erratically it felt like it would burst from my mouth. The feeling was agonizing.

*When did I lose consciousness?*

I could not even tell where my own limbs were.

*...Either way, I survived.*

I wiped my mouth. The back of my hand scraped my teeth, and I tasted blood. I was not dead.

[Did the stage equipment collapse?]

"......"

I suppressed a cough and forced my mouth open.

"I came up onto the stage."

For me, failing a status abnormality check meant death. If the System was trying to kill me now, an 'unbelievable incident' would be a convenient way for it to happen.

*A fatal accident.*

A ridiculous accident. That was why I tried to steer things toward a situation I could at least predict. Since no one was on stage, I reflexively thought that whatever happened would likely be a structural collapse.

Thankfully, I was right.

*...If this had happened in the hallway, everyone would have died.*

I guided it toward a predictable accident in a way that would involve as few people as possible.

*And I somehow managed to avoid dying on the first hit.*

However.

"This isn't the end, is it."

[......]

"Right," I said calmly. "If this was all it took, you wouldn't have needed to restart the mission so many times."

He must have restarted so many times because it was unavoidable.

I held my breath and waited. Soon, a low voice flowed from the smartphone.

[Yes.]

I gritted my teeth.

"...When?"

[Soon.]

Fuck.

I raised my head. I could vaguely see my surroundings were a chaotic mess of precariously stacked steel beams and fragments of lighting equipment. The whole thing looked like it would crash down and finish me off if its balance shifted.

*If I get rescued before that... no, that would be even worse.*

I had no idea what might happen during a rescue. The more variables there were, the harder it would get.

This was the right way.

I caught my breath, cleared my head, and said what needed to be said.

"Don't report it."

[......]

"Don't tell anyone that I'm safe, or that I'm in contact with you, or anything like that."

*You probably already know, but just in case...*

[Don't worry about that.]

"......"

*The situation must be really fucked for him to use that tone.*

The voice continued quickly.

[And until the next thing happens.]

*...Until what.*

But the next words never came.

[......]

Silence.

"Shin Jaehyun?"

I turned my head toward the smartphone and realized the faint light had vanished.

"...!"

Fuck.

I immediately fumbled on the floor, reaching for where I saw the smartphone. Soon, my hand hit an angular, hard piece...

"Hk."

A sharp pain pierced my knuckles. It stung.

*Damn it.*

I wrapped my hand in my costume and managed to flip the smartphone over.

"......"

The smartphone was shattered. The impact from the falling steel beams probably destroyed it. That was why the call had disconnected. It was a miracle it had stayed connected this long in that state.

So...

*Just accept it.*

I did not know what the fuck this situation was, but I had failed to clear the status abnormality, and all communication with the outside world was completely cut off.

I bit my tongue.

*Another way, another way.*

First, my hearing.

*...What happened?*

I strained my ears for any sounds from outside, but there was only a low humming. The sound was so blocked off that the ceiling itself must have collapsed, not just the stage equipment.

In that case... next.

*Move. Quickly.*

Before more shit hit the fan. I immediately pushed my upper body up.

My side ached. It felt like the rib I broke last time had either cracked again or broken completely.

*Or it could just be my imagination.*

I hoped it was the latter. Anyway, I could get up on my own.

*...It's touching my head.*

I felt cold metal brushing precariously against the top of my head. It seemed to be debris from the stage equipment.

"Hoo."

I closed my eyes and waited a moment. I was damn anxious, but letting my eyes adjust was the priority.

When I opened them again, my vision had adjusted to the darkness, revealing the area's general outline.

"...!"

The stage was a complete wreck. The steel beams had all collapsed, leaving nothing that could be called a stage. The surrounding beams and ceiling materials blocked any view of the outside.

I was right in front of the backstage area on the opposite side. I had been pushed back this far, almost like I had slid, which was how I avoided being crushed.

"Hoo."

My assessment was complete. I would not gain anything useful by observing further in this state.

*First, I need to move.*

If I stayed here, I felt like the steel beams would fall and crush me. The good thing was that my arms and legs did not seem seriously injured. My back felt a little strange, but it did not hinder my movement much, so it was fine.

I stumbled toward the opposite backstage area, trying to stay on my feet.

What I needed to find was...

*A place where danger is certain.*

An obviously safe place would not do. The System might pull some unbelievable bullshit like a gas explosion. I needed a space where an accident I could at least roughly predict might occur.

And...

*I have to be alone.*

I had to move in a direction that would take me as far away from others as possible. If they got involved, I would lose what little control I barely had.

"......"

So waiting here was absolutely not an option. Once the shock wore off, people would realize they could approach the collapsed stage from this side. I had to move quickly.

I decided to go around the edge of the backstage area and exit toward the emergency stairs. Thankfully, even though the lights were out, I could see the blue light of an emergency exit sign.

"Hoo-uk."

I opened the emergency exit door with a sweaty hand. After a moment's hesitation, I ran up.

*If I go down and the ceiling collapses from above, burying me alive, there's nothing I can do.*

Up the stairs, as fast as possible...

CREAK.

An ominous sound continued from behind me.

*No way.*

However, an artificial sound cut through the creaking.

[DING- DONG-]

A chime. An announcement echoed from speakers somewhere.

[We would like to inform the audience. A safety incident has occurred due to ground instability. Please follow the staff's instructions and use the emergency stairs to...]

I immediately stopped climbing and turned around.

*The upstairs hallway.*

People might flood into these emergency stairs, so it was better to slip into the hallway.

The moment I went up one more floor, left the emergency stairs, opened the door, and stepped into the hallway... the hair on my neck stood on end.

THUD.

My footsteps echoed unnaturally loudly.

Next... that spine-chilling sense of crisis and irrational fear returned.

*Fuck..!*

A step ahead, I gritted my teeth and ran.

SCREEECH-CRUMBLE-CRUMBLE-SCREEEEE-CRASH!

With a deafening roar, the floor I had just been standing on collapsed without warning.

*I know why.*

*This is the area above the stage.*

Location-wise, it was the most unstable place, and since the broadcasting station used this line for material storage, there were no permanent staff here.

There should not be.

*Because I chose it on purpose...!*

It was fine.

*I can do this. I can do this!*

I pictured the building's structure in my head. I was deliberately avoiding the restrooms and facilities designed with large pillars to better withstand weight. If a collapse big enough to bring those down occurred, everyone inside would get hurt.

*This is hell.*

There were a thousand people in the audience alone.

"Hup!"

Dust, air, and debris of all sizes flew everywhere.

The place I ran to was...

[Supply Room 303]

Here.

*A place that isn't directly above the stage!*

Instead of foolishly pulling on the door, I slammed my whole body into it.

And then.

RUMBLE-CRASH-!

The room shook.

*Fuck.*

*You damn son of a bitch System.*

I shielded myself with my body from the falling supplies, grabbing the heaviest, most solid-looking thing I could find and holding it over my head. The roaring continued, and my body kept shaking. Large and small objects struck my hands and arms.

How much time had passed?

"......"

The roaring and vibrations stopped.

I blinked repeatedly to clear my vision. I was out of breath. I checked the object clutched in my pale hands. It was metal, a shelf made of a solid surface and pipes. It was half-torn from the wall. No, it was torn out along with the wall itself, revealing a dark void beyond.

I turned my head. The door I came through had slammed shut when the space shook and was now precariously wedged in its frame.

"......"

It would not open.

"Heh."

I realized I was trapped in this small supply room.

RATTLE...

A pen, likely one of the supplies, rolled and collected in one corner.

*It's tilted.*

The room itself was tilted and warped. It seemed to be leaning toward the stage, hanging precariously.

"...Hoo-uk."

I was trapped in a smaller, more unstable space than the stage. I could not move.

Still clinging to the metal shelf, I tried to accept reality. An hour ago, no, just a few dozen minutes ago, all that mattered was finishing one performance well. Now I had ended up like this.

I had somehow survived twice, only to be hunted by a supernatural force determined to kill me.

"...Hk."

Pain began to rise from all over my body, but stronger than the pain was a sense of helplessness.

*I don't know.*

*How much longer can I hold on?*

I did not even have any information.

"......"

Actually, it was not like there was no way to contact someone.

I knew one way.

*Keundal.*

*The guy I can call through the user window, even without a smartphone.*

He might even know some clues since he could access the Status Window. However, the problem was whether I should even open the Status Window right now.

*It could be a switch that triggers the next 'unbelievable incident' faster, or it could just be a more certain death sentence.*

So I would not call him. Absolutely not.

"......"

That was what I thought.

But I let the strength leave my hands and lowered my head. The cold metal cooled my forehead.

*Wouldn't it be better to just give up.*

Logically, if this kept up and everyone here died, including me, what was the point of all this bullshit? If the scale of this kept growing, I did not know what would happen to the casualties.

*You idiot.*

It was almost funny, wondering if I was in any state to worry about others' lives when I was about to die myself, but if a person had even a shred of conscience, they could not ignore this. They were not strangers. They were the guys I had spent years with.

And...

*...They're all fans.*

The thousand people in the audience were all fans. This was, fuck. This environment made it almost too easy for me to give up, as if it were designed that way.

I let out a long sigh. The shelf glistened with blood from the cut on my palm, sliced by the broken smartphone.

*...It's heavy.*

I closed my eyes. I shut my mouth and lowered my body.

It was as quiet as a void.

How much time passed like that?

"...Excuse me?"

"......"

A person's voice.

I lifted my head. The first thing I saw was a fluorescent glow. A soft yellow light spilled from beyond the half-torn wall where the metal shelf had collapsed.

A moment later, I realized what that light was.

...It was a light stick.

"M-Moondae?!"

The person who found me from beyond the wall was a fan who had been sitting in the audience earlier.
