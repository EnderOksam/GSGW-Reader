# Chapter 408 - A Life of His Own

Shin Jaehyeon's memories had very deep layers.

On the ocean floor, amid the thousands of layers that had built up over the course of his many failed lives, Shin Jaehyeon had never bothered to dredge up anything unnecessary.

That was why there was no end in sight.

They couldn't be moved.

His first attempt lay submerged far too deep to be reached by this expedient attempt at 'awakening'.

But there was one layer, a place that was reached.

The topmost part.

His most recent memory.

Reality.

*Tuk.*

A shockwave rippled through his mind.

And as it happened, his most recent life had been quite unique, different from what he was used to.

A life he believed was forever out of his reach.

– *Think about how you decided to live on in reality even after experiencing difficulties...*

The way he'd put it was a bit harsh, but Park Moondae was probably right.

Cheongryeo continued to live on, even after failure.

*Tuk.*

Ripples spread through his memories of that time.

It had all started with one person.

– *You can't restart now.*

These were the words spoken by Park Moondae, who had subdued him and then left the villa beaten and bloody.

And perhaps, more importantly, the words that followed.

– *What you have now won't go away anymore.*

Those words floated like a buoy in his head during VTIC's hiatus for self-reflection and apology, something extremely unfamiliar to him because it had been so long since the last one.

He wasn't touched by those words, nor did they resonate with him through any deep understanding of their meaning.

However, they became a sort of signpost.

If restarting wasn't possible, what were his options?

What could he have?

First, he brought home a dog. He no longer had to struggle to remember the name of the dog he was raising.

Then, he wrote new music. This was because he no longer had a backlog of songs he knew would be successful.

He did solo activities. The group's performance was no longer the sole measure of his success.

Still, VTIC did not sink.

Failure did not go away. However, neither did the things he'd built on top of failure.

But throughout that process, one question lingered.

*Why am I doing this?*

It was a question born of genuine curiosity rather than a sense of futility.

It felt as if all his actions had no driving force, no goal to reach.

A life without a mission.

An unfamiliar landscape he had to navigate without any foreknowledge or answers.

It had slowly become part of his daily life, and Shin Jaehyeon took in those memories.

*Tuk.*

Waves overlapped.

Then, the wave inadvertently brought to mind a memory with a very similar impression. It was deep, deep below at the end of the invisible bottomless sea.

His very first regression.

– *What is this...*

He believed he was only given one chance to make things right.

Therefore, in his first restart, he'd lived as though it were his last life. His young and ignorant self.

What exactly did that memory look like?

He didn't know. The details escaped him. Only a hazy outline remained in his mind.

*Okay.*

It wasn't worth reading into, Shin Jaehyeon thought.

It should have ended there originally.

But his subconscious moved again from there.

*...?*

In an instant, the ripples spread like roots, creating microscopic cracks in the layers of his memories.

Very faintly, something resonated throughout the deep sea. The tremors were barely noticeable.

However, similar shapes could be seen between the numerous layers of attempts and repetitions.

Events that happened rarely. Occasionally.

Accidental harmony.

Glimpses of scenes through heavy rain.

Performing while injured.

He couldn't specifically remember what the situations were, what emotions arose, or what kind of pain existed.

Because the depth of his experiences was already far too deep and multi-layered.

It was all just a vague trace.

But sometimes, just a trace was enough.

Because they served as evidence that those moments had once existed.

The feeling that no matter how much he restarted, such unique and brilliant moments would never come again.

– *Please.*

*Tuk.* Something tugged at him.

It wasn't that he believed it was a life that couldn't be restarted. It was just that he didn't want to, Shin Jaehyeon reflected.

He also had moments when he thought restarting was a loss rather than an opportunity.

*I knew that very well.*

Images that suddenly came to mind as he watched Park Moondae struggling not to restart.

– *I think I felt the same way... once.*

However, there was no value in the things that had already been crushed underfoot and pressed deep beneath the surface of his memories.

Such sentiments were useless.

No matter how such moments made him feel, he still had to start over if he failed nonetheless.

Because he thought emotions were just inefficient impulses that slowed down progress to the next step.

– ...

The ripples soon slowed. The surface became calm again.

His past lives remained solid and inaccessible, like the unseen depths of the deep sea.

Shin Jaehyeon thought to himself.

*Nothing had changed.*

Still, there was no absolute benefit to be gained by returning to reality.

VTIC would not be able to maintain its prime time any longer.

An inseparable stain marked the group's once spotless reputation.

The empty position in the group would have to remain empty for the rest of its lifespan.

*And yet...*

*Yet—*

He didn't want to throw it away.

Like a long time ago when he wanted to stay, even though he knew restarting would erase all his problems.

Because he just wanted to do it.

*Were irrational impulses the real driving force behind living?*

Cheongryeo opened his eyes.

He felt someone watching him, perhaps with worry.

"Ah."

He reflexively ran his hand along the bottom of his face.

Of course, there were no tears there. No water and no ocean.

The ripples that lasted for a few brief seconds in his mind had never grown into massive waves or a shuddering earthquake.

But they had left a mark.

"......"

Cheongryeo had to admit it.

His urge to go back to reality.

It didn't matter whether he could start over here. What mattered was that, as Park Moondae had said, his reality was unique and could only happen once.

So, he opened his mouth.

"There was no new information."

"......"

"There's something I want to ask you."

And he asked a question he'd been considering for some time but had never been able to put into words.

"Why do I want to go back to reality?"

Park Moondae did not scold him by saying, 'Have you not been fucking listening to what I've been saying so far?'

He just held back a sigh and opened his mouth.

"Since your reality has always been yours, naturally, you wouldn't want to discard it. Who would want to just throw away what's theirs?"

"...!"

Since life was originally his, it was natural to want to maintain and own it completely.

Shin Jaehyeon was slowly recovering that impulse and desire.

Park Moondae spoke gruffly again.

"Let's go back, you fucking son of a bitch."

"......"

He still couldn't find a rational explanation for why he should. But not knowing may be the essence of impulse.

And now, it was okay for him to act impulsively.

Cheongryeo opened his mouth.

"Alright."

"...!"

"And... I guess we'll have to change our plans."

He brought up the holograms again.

There were numerous windows, but neither of them batted an eye.

"If we're not going to stay here for long, then I think there's one more method we can use."

"......"

"What do you think?"

The person in front of him did not refuse. Instead, he nodded his head.

"Say it."
