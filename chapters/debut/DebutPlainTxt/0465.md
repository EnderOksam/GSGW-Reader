# Chapter 465

It was funny to think about it now, but our game was never supposed to be this big when we first planned it.

[Hmm, so it's a supplement for the USB album.]

[That's right. I think the fans will have fun playing it for a day or so.]

[Sounds good~ It's fresh, so won't it get some buzz on social media? I'm all for it!]

It was literally just an extended version of the music video. The plan was for a one-hour movie that came as a bonus with the album, with some choices added in.

The move cleverly sublimated the weakness of a sparse physical album into a concept of extreme immersion.

*...However.*

The fact that our first meeting was at a ruined factory was the root of all our problems.

[That... seems possible? Yes, I think it'll be fun!]

Things were great up until I first brought up the interactive music video idea.

[Really?]

[Yes! We actually have a tool we were developing before. If you're okay with it, we could adapt this... This could work, right?]

[Oh, it works, it'll work. Uh, you said the playtime is about an hour, right? We should be able to fit it into the schedule somehow!]

At that moment, I even smiled to myself, triumphant. In truth, I had come there knowing everything.

*I heard these guys were originally planning to release an interactive music video-style horror adventure as the follow-up to ❬127 Section❭.*

After that, they went under T1, and ❬127 Section❭ became a huge hit. Well then, wasn't the result obvious?

*T1 must have been singing the same old song, telling them to just release the next installment of ❬127 Section❭.*

So, unable to stand the crazy parachute executives at T1 Plays who wanted to milk ❬127 Section❭ to the bone, they escaped. That was the established theory in academic circles, or rather, among game enthusiasts.

But if someone happened to bring them a genre they had started developing and then abandoned, wouldn't they take the chance? They could recycle it and earn some funds for their next project.

[However, the physics engine and implementation for things like that are a bit of an older version... If you want to add action elements to the game, we'll need more development time.]

[Ah, we're fine with just adding choices!]

It didn't matter. We weren't trying to create an all-time masterpiece with incredible gameplay. As long as the visuals were amazing and players could choose routes, it was enough. As long as the fans could feel like they were interacting with us, that was all that mattered.

[Ah, if that's the case, we just need to polish the UI a bit...]

So, the discussion proceeded quickly and smoothly. We ended up having a pleasant time, signing autographs for the employees in a friendly atmosphere.

And then, this topic came up.

[Oh! In that case, would you like to come see us film our music video?]

[Gasp, is that really okay?]

[Hey, of course~ We're going to be working as one team now, so we'd be grateful if you came!]

[It would be a huge help if experts in game development could watch the content creation process and give us their opinions!]

And that's how the two representatives and a few key production staff were invited to the filming site. It was written as a music video shoot but was really for game content and a movie.

And then, on the day of the shoot.

"......"

"Whew, it's been a while."

"This is like a proper crank-in."

They stared, mouths agape and eyes wide, at the splendid equipment and setup brought in by the crew Bae Sejin had recruited.

*Of course, all the money was my money...*

No matter how well Bae Sejin acted as a child or how outstanding his skills were in his cameos, a capitalist society couldn't function without money. It made me realize just how important capital is.

It felt like I could hear ten billion won burning.

*Still, is it a relief that the filming location was secured in advance...*

It was a relief we found a suitable building in the countryside. We would have to patch up anything that didn't work with post-production CG, but I thought this was better than nothing.

"Moondae, look down~"

"Yes."

Following the makeup artist's instructions, I narrowed my eyes. I saw today's first batter standing in the middle of the set, dressed in a police uniform.

"Hmm, should I go for this kind of vibe?"

It was Keun Sejin, filming the prologue. He played a guard, like something from an urban legend, who pretends to be a kind police officer before kidnapping people. He wrote his own lines so he could deliver them as naturally as possible.

*We're not established actors, after all.*

The characters were usually made to speak in a tone as similar to the members' usual way of talking as possible. If that wasn't feasible, their lines were drastically reduced.

In Lee Sejin's case, the acting lessons he supposedly received back when he was doing GY-ROP in the virtual world had some influence on him landing the role.

[Wow~ So that crappy group life is helpful after all! I guess all hardships and adversities really are stepping stones for growth, haha!]

[That hyung is clenching his fists. He's definitely traumatized.]

Of course, it would have been impossible without Bae Sejin's coaching. Even right now, Bae Sejin stared at Keun Sejin with a hawk's eye after the first cut and fired off feedback.

"No. Speak even more like your usual self. When a beginner tries to consciously act like they're hiding something for the story, it just comes off as more unnatural."

"Ah."

"Once the BGM and the other actor's reaction are added, you'll look suspicious enough, so don't try to be all meaningful on your own."

"Yes, yes. Ah, Hyung. About slinging the camera over my shoulder like this..."

Keun Sejin showed no awkwardness at the reversed situation. He seemed completely focused on cheerfully asking questions and absorbing everything he could.

*Then again, with a 60-day schedule, it's not like he has the luxury to be awkward.*

And the guy finished filming the prologue even more successfully than expected.

"Cut! That's great~"

"...! Thank you!"

Hearing the light exclamations and seeing the staff exchange looks of 'that's pretty good', I let out a small laugh.

"...You did well!"

"It's all thanks to your great teaching~"

The two of them exchanged some rather awkward words of praise.

"Aigoo, if it weren't for the car exploding, it would be great to shoot it one more time... but it's a shame. I guess this is as far as we can go!"

Not missing the chance to appeal to the behind-the-scenes camera at the very end was just so like Keun Sejin.

Then we immediately moved locations and went straight into filming the main story. The time Bae Sejin had booked this crew wasn't long in the first place.

*What a killer schedule.*

For reference, from the main shoot onward, the accomplished actor took the baton first. We dealt with the most important scene right away.

[......]

In other words, Bae Sejin filmed the scene where he attempts to escape through the main gate and got an 'OK' on the first take.

*...It's overwhelming, though.*

His acting was at a level where you could almost feel some kind of aura from him. It was like Cha Eugene on stage. Bae Sejin is now a guy who can pull his own weight on stage, but you could still see the difference compared to his acting.

The director also seemed the most excited he had been all day.

"Hey, our Sejin is still as good as ever~"

"Ah, um... Thank you."

Bae Sejin looked a little embarrassed, but he also seemed quite proud.

After that, the filming preparations went smoothly, and the schedule proceeded tightly.

"Yes, next up is closed room number 3!"

Was it fortunate that the filming order was fixed so that everyone barely had to change outfits to save time? Thanks to that, I was stuck wearing this damn uncomfortable straitjacket, though.

*If this doesn't look good on screen, I'm going to hunt down the bastard who made it.*

I spent the day thinking that.

"M-Moondae. How's this...?"

"It looks very plausible."

I nodded at Seon Ahyeon, who walked down the hallway swinging a riot baton with sharp, controlled movements.

It was around that time.

"Excuse me."

"...? Yes."

The people from the ruined factory, no, the industrial complex, had followed us there. The people who had been quietly enjoying themselves the whole time suddenly started talking to me proactively.

"By any chance, have you thought of anything else regarding the game's progression?"

What about it.

"That ending cut you filmed earlier, you're making the album in the shape of the key device that appeared there... and that photocard? You said you'd put a code on it, right?"

"Yes."

And?

"Seeing it in person, it was just so good. The gate design, the composition, the outfits, and everything else was just perfect..."

The female representative clenched her fists. The male representative hurriedly continued.

"Um, what about a system where the character who enters the code changes depending on the member on the photocard?"

"......"

"Or maybe based on the affinity that builds up according to the choices made so far..."

Wait a minute.

All sorts of ideas that we had brought up but cut due to time and environmental constraints started pouring out of the developers' mouths.

"......"

It seemed that everything from the filming set and the costume concept to Bae Sejin's acting skills had stimulated these people's tastes.

No, but still.

*Do these people not know the meaning of realistic thinking?*

I barely managed to ask.

"That sounds good, but is the implementation... p-possible?"

"...Would it not be?"

How would we know?

"But don't you want to at least try? While you were filming, those ad-libs that caused NGs looked great to use as new choices..."

"......"

"Ah, and Eugene's escape attempt also caught my eye. Maybe we could use it as a sort of gag ending that appears in the middle?"

"Did I film well? Was I good earlier?"

"Wait a minute."

Where did you pop out from?

The two representatives nodded in perfect sync at Cha Eugene, who had rushed over in his straitjacket with sparkling eyes.

"Yes!"

"We want to use it?"

"Then use it! Umm, please use it!"

"Wow!"

The guy who had been dead set against this concept was now just happy to be praised. No, before that.

I barely managed to open my mouth.

"...Is, is that possible with the time you have?"

"Haha, well, it's just a matter of adding existing assets as choices..."

So?

"If we, who are free from labor laws, work overtime!"

"That's right!"

"......"

And so, by reviving ad-lib scenes and NG takes as new choices and gag endings, the volume of the game exploded like crazy.

It didn't end there.

"And you said there are three versions, right?"

"Yes, but only to the extent that the USB album shape and the prologue are different..."

"Wow, wouldn't adding some individual events make that feeling even stronger?"

"......"

"If we revive some of the scenarios you discarded here..."

It was then.

"Oh, oh, there are discarded scenarios? Really?"

"Yes! Take a look at this."

Before I knew it, the director had joined in, and an extended shoot was decided on the spot.

"I'm sorry, but the time to memorize the script..."

"Ah~ It's similar to your personalities anyway, so don't be too conscious of the script and just go with the feeling of reacting to the situation!"

"......"

"You guys wrote it anyway, so why are you thinking so hard about it! If you want to act, you need to have these kinds of experiences."

We're idols who are supposed to be performing on stage.

However, that rebuttal was impossible due to the existence of numerous idols capable of acting, led by Bae Sejin.

And even the Leader...

"Ah, we're fine with it."

"...!"

So, in conclusion, that's how a ridiculous game with a volume of over two and a half hours came to be.

*It became 2.5 times bigger.*

Moreover, since we implemented three versions with bad endings, gag endings, normal endings, a true ending, and even a Hidden Ending, the actual playtime increased enormously. Thanks to that, the amount of buzz also seemed to have increased by about three times more than planned.

Crazy talk about the game would pop up and streams would keep appearing, so the buzz was maintained for several days.

[The hot TeSTAR game these days.. I tried it too.]

[Y'all know this? There's a route where if you never check on Raebin in the lab, he causes an accident. Bottles shatter, Keun-se's mentality shattersㅋㅋㅋㅋ (Video)]

[The Hidden Ending Seon Ahyeon that's driving TeSTAR fans crazy]

Of course, not all of these people had actually played our game. Most of them just watched the short clips or edited videos circulating online, or the fans' reactions, had a laugh, and that was it.

*It's a packaged game, not even a mobile game, so what kind of mainstream appeal could it have in the first place.*

Besides, it was an expanded version of the music video's world, a concept aimed squarely at the fans. It probably provided a meaningful boost to album sales, but it wasn't a massive inflation.

*We also released the streams, so there's the side effect of people who are only moderately interested being satisfied with just that.*

However, we grasped the most important thing. The fact that we created an incentive through the game that brought in a new potential fanbase, and that the existing fans were satisfied, was very good.

[Wow, I can't believe TeSTAR is making a comeback like thisㅋㅋㅋ]

[The title melody is already stuck in my head because I've watched so many reaction videosㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋ]

First of all, we had successfully imprinted the very fact that 'TeSTAR is making a comeback' onto the internet. Moreover, we inserted the title track's chorus melody throughout the game, so anyone who had seen a stream or a reaction video would find it familiar.

*I'm satisfied as long as we can make them hear it once.*

And as expected, TeSTAR's digital music chart rankings were not lacking compared to when we had T1's insane promotions backing us up.

*...! That's right.*

Now that we've announced our comeback like this, all we need to do is deliver the final blow on stage.

*And that day is today.*

We will be performing our first comeback stage live. On a public broadcast network.

"......"

I smiled. It was a rather satisfying result.

However...

[Moondae! By any chance, are the TeSTAR members interested in a more serious game? We were wondering what you'd think about the TeSTAR members' characters making a short cameo in a gag ending for our next project...]

"We're fine. We're really fine."

The people from Ruined Industrial Complex seemed to see us as another one of their colleagues. I'll think about them later.

~~~

"...Yes. Thank you."

Bae Sejin thanked the person on the other end of the phone once again. It was the director, who had gladly taken his call despite it being a long time since they last spoke. The director of the drama in which he had made a cameo as a shaman.

The director, who had also been part of the production team for the ten-million-viewer movie he starred in as a child shaman, was naturally someone who also wrote and directed films. She was the one who had arranged the meeting.

Thanks to her, he was able to meet with the film production staff he knew from his child actor days and attempt to make a difficult request.

*...I'm glad I worked up the courage.*

It was an absurdly forced march, but it felt like the first time he had contributed this much to the team.

*A-And the reaction was good, too...*

Now, all he had to do was work hard on stage. He was once again steeling his resolve for this promotion.

[Remember? When you were just a little kid, you suddenly started shedding huge tears on set, and all the adults were flustered.]

[Y-Yes?]

[You coolly said, 'I put my real feelings into it, so what's the big deal~' You were such a bold kid.]

"Euh... Ahem, yes."

Bae Sejin barely suppressed a cough.

The director, who had been cheerfully reminiscing, suddenly muttered in a low voice.

[As expected... it's a waste. Such a waste.]

"......"

[I really enjoyed it during this shoot, Sejin. Jeongjin also said it was great to see you after so long.]

"Ah."

Bae Sejin recalled the actor who played the driver in the prologue scene. He was a senior from his child actor days.

*...He was good to me.*

He was momentarily lost in the memories of that time.

[I got my hands on a really good script this time.]

From over the phone, a direct offer came.

[You're old enough now, have you thought about giving it a try?]

"...!"

This happened thirty minutes before TeSTAR's first live comeback stage.
