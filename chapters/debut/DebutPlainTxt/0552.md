# Chapter 552

"Energy restored! We did that, right?"

Bae Sejin pointed at the hologram screen.

[Park Moondae : Chatting with an acting coach.]

The pixelated Park Moondae on the screen still looked worn out and exhausted, but he moved around in better condition than before.

The displayed energy gauge was no longer a thin, precarious dot. It had grown a little thicker.

It was still a glaring red that signaled danger, but an achievement was an achievement. Keundal shouted excitedly.

"Yes! Using the trait to transfer energy was a definite success!"

"Alright!"

The members reviewed the formula. When a moment arrived for Park Moondae to use one of their traits, they just had to bestow a 'blessing' on him. Then they could give him energy as a reward.

[Additional energy unlocked!]

Park Moondae's increased energy bar also meant additional connections were now possible.

"The capacity is two people now!"

"......"

"Good, good."

"If we keep taking turns using our traits like this... hmm?"

But the environment around the Park Moondae they thought was an actor changed again.

[Park Moondae : Making donuts.]

Park Moondae was now in a shabby shop, diligently kneading dough.

"...Donuts?"

"Hmm, it looks like a part-time job."

Lee Sejin frowned and peered at the screen. The display seemed to fast-forward as Park Moondae struggled to diligently fry donuts.

"...Can't we help with something like this?"

"I know," Ryu Chungwoo muttered. "His efficiency seems to have dropped a lot because of his stamina..."

Efficiency.

In that moment, everyone's gaze fixed on one person. They looked toward the man who could make perfectly proportioned hotteok with just his fingers, without measuring.

"......"

Kim Raebin looked around, bewildered.

"Rae, Raebin."

"Yeah. That's it."

The members murmured amongst themselves and switched places.

And then.

[❬'Midnight Maestro' has entered the channel.❭]

[Midnight Maestro bestows a blessing upon you!]

[Characteristic: Increased Creation Speed]

On the screen, Park Moondae began churning out donuts at a much faster pace.

"OOH!"

Kim Raebin flushed and looked down at the screen. He showed no disappointment that his extreme artistic ability, which let him create beautiful musical ideas instantly, was now just being used to make donuts.

*That's part of his charm, though!*

Keundal finally lowered his guard, his overwhelmed eyes adjusting the pop-up.

[Midnight Maestro is satisfied.]

[Midnight Maestro has sponsored you! : Energy +]

Kim Raebin lifted his head, his face bright.

"I think it worked!"

"Uh-huh!"

Seon Ahyeon smiled gently. He looked at Park Moondae's energy bar, which had turned vermilion and grown a little longer.

But that was only for a moment.

"But why is Moondae working at a place like this?"

"Huh?"

Ryu Chungwoo muttered in confusion. "The Gunwoo I know... he could be tutoring."

Or he could be selling data. Either option would be easier on his body, so he wondered why Park Moondae was doing this.

"Just a moment."

After some trial and error, Keundal successfully received the backstory information.

[Park Moondae has crash-landed in the United States! With no passport or ID, he is an undocumented immigrant struggling to make a living with a part-time job... What will be his fate?]

"......"

"......"

"C-couldn't we make him win the lottery or something."

"Please."

[Hey! I know you can't see me, but, uh, if this is a 'raising a daughter' game, isn't money duplication like, the standard move?]

"We can't..."

[Sob.]

It was a spectacle. Cheongryeo twisted the corner of his lip.

***

[The Focused Genius Actor recommends a gambling den!]

*What's with these guys.*

For now, I continued to fry donuts without enthusiasm. I was in the middle of my part-time job, and the manual labor was now more bearable.

Surprisingly, my physical condition had improved a bit.

Before, I felt like I would die, as if I had pulled an all-nighter and then run a marathon. Now, it was more like having a bad case of body aches and just being out of it.

From experience, I knew I could endure this with willpower.

*And it's probably thanks to this.*

[The Focused Genius Actor is in anguish...]

[The Focused Genius Actor wants a sponsorship.]

I stared sourly at the chat pop-up. My stamina had increased the moment I received energy through this 'sponsorship'.

*How on earth did they do it?*

I was grateful but wanted to ask where it came from. I suspected Keundal was the source of all this communication.

*I hope they're not doing anything dangerous.*

*If you overdo it, you'll end up like me.*

I had warned them several times, but the pop-up continued to act on its own. It seemed useless.

[Two cherry donuts!]

[Yep.]

I responded immediately to the owner's request and packed the donuts.

*For the record, the working environment here isn't great. They hired me without even checking if I'm an undocumented immigrant... Well, I should be grateful.*

*If they try to stiff me on my pay, I'll just have to threaten to report myself to immigration and take the shop down with me.*

"Hoo."

Packing complete. I returned to the donut production line and started applying a chocolate coating to the fried donuts.

My speed was surprisingly fast for my exhausted body, thanks to some... blessing from the chat pop-up.

*Increased Creation Speed', was it?*

If so, it was obvious.

*The one who gave me this, the guy called 'Midnight Maestro'... it must be Kim Raebin.*

*Why would he waste such an insanely overpowered trait that increases composition speed on frying stupid donuts...*

*...It's too comfortable to say that.*

*Damn it.*

I started churning out donuts as if possessed. Each one fit perfectly, like assembling a puzzle where every piece clicks into place.

[Midnight Maestro feels a sense of accomplishment.]

*Thanks. Damn it.*

I held back a sigh at my recovering stamina.

I couldn't tell if they were listening, and the whole business with stamina blessings was ridiculous enough to make me scoff...

*...But the fact that they're here is what's important.*

Embarrassingly, my spirits were also lifted. I could see a possibility of escaping this damn situation.

I kept my hands moving. After arranging the donuts in the display case, I took a short, self-imposed break.

*Reporting in early would just get me more work.*

"Hoo..."

I was leaning against the counter when my smartphone vibrated in my pocket.

VZZZT!

It was a text from LeTi.

[Gunwoo~ How have you been?*^^*The music video teaser is out!]

"......"

*Already?*

***

Cheongryeo looked at the link, unimpressed. It led to a generic breakup song.

[Now I know]

The small whispers, the careful touches

That they were love

The song was a lonely, heartbreaking mumble about things one only realized after a breakup. Its regretful lyrics, 'How great would it have been if I had looked back then,' stimulated a common sentiment.

This made it a good song for a female soloist aiming for mass appeal instead of a fandom.

*It's always like this.*

Cheongryeo glanced down at his smartphone screen at an angle. This was the kind of single album almost always released around this time, and he received this request with a 50-50 probability.

"She's from an affiliate company, you know? Just share it on social media once for her."

"Yes."

Posting on social media about a married, female solo ballad singer in her 40s carried no risk. This was true even when one of the members had caused a huge dating scandal.

They had restored their social media posting cycle a month ago. Anyone could tell this was an agency request. Besides, this song didn't have enough buzz to create any variables.

*Just enough to save face.*

*It'll hang around the top 30s and then disappear.*

After his cold assessment, Cheongryeo briefly entered a music streaming site to take a screenshot. He raised an eyebrow slightly.

[29. A Bad Night (New!)]

The song's rank had risen by a very small margin.

*Hmm.*

Cheongryeo placed a hand on his chin. The probability that such a small rise was a coincidence was overwhelmingly high.

It was a minor fluctuation, just unrelated factors intertwining like a spiderweb in this reset to create ripples. It was a trace that would likely disappear upon the next reset.

However...

*There's no harm in finding out the cause.*

Music charts were always a factor he paid attention to. He casually flipped through the page and began to trace the spiderweb backward.

It was then.

[What are you doing.]

Help popped up after just one day. Cheongryeo replied, slightly amused.

"Analysis."

Help was silent for a moment, then soon replied in a slightly gentler tone.

[It seems fine. The atmosphere has calmed down a lot too.]

"......"

Cheongryeo thought. Help seemed to have misunderstood his answer of 'analysis' and was now rambling about VTIC's current reputation.

After all, VTIC-related social media posts were scrolling past on his smartphone screen.

[Still, I wish they'd make a comeback without that guy, maybe using health issues as an excuse. The one with the dating scandal.]

*I see.*

Cheongryeo glanced at 'the one with the dating scandal' before replying nonchalantly.

"He'll have to be excluded next time."

*After the reset, that is.*

Help was silent for a moment, perhaps understanding the unspoken part. It no longer tried to stop the reset. It simply asked this.

[Then who among the current members will you keep?]

"We'll see."

Despite the vague answer, Help replied indifferently.

[Jin Chaeyul will have perfect attendance, I bet.]

*Quite the guess.*

Cheongryeo smiled faintly.

"Because he's suitable."

[You mean his stats are good, his personality is decent, his temperament is agreeable, and his mentality is strong, so he pulls his own weight in most situations.]

Cheongryeo didn't deny it and just smiled.

"Yes. The fact that I don't need to create a special environment for him to do his part. That's his most useful quality."

It sounded like he was describing an 'easy-to-grow, sturdy houseplant'.

Park Moondae thought sourly. He realized this Cheongryeo was treating Help with a slightly lower hurdle than before. Unlike at the beginning, he was actually explaining things to Help.

*Anyway, nothing good comes from being consumed by that damn System.*

*Now that I'm in my right mind, the conversation is actually flowing better, isn't it?*

Park Moondae took the opportunity to ask outright.

[Don't tell me, since you're going to reset anyway, are you thinking of trying some high-risk attempts now?]

Cheongryeo, his eyes lowered to the screen, answered simply.

"I'm not."

The time for gaining 'unexpected' information through such actions had already passed during the initial resets. Now, he had to maintain the control variable of 'Cheongryeo acts like Cheongryeo' to obtain reliable data.

This was actually good for Park Moondae.

*He'll release the next album properly.*

It seemed Cheongryeo had no intention of doing anything stupid just because this was a throwaway round.

Satisfied that he had bought time to access the terminal, Park Moondae prepared to enter the System's deeper layers and resume his search. Then he suddenly realized.

*...I see.*

Cheongryeo intended to maintain his stance of 'doing his best to win the grand prize' here as well. In other words, a junior male idol group to VTIC would not be able to debut here either.

Just like always.

[...Right.]

Park Moondae briefly recalled the guy who had become almost certain of his own debut after meeting him.

[I will contact you regularly.]

He was talking about this world's Judan, who had now returned to Korea. Judan probably didn't know that LeTi's next boy group was already finished.

*...*

It left a slightly bitter taste in Park Moondae's mouth.

Meanwhile, Cheongryeo had finally found the reason for the music chart's rise.

"So this is it."

Random comments scrolled past the screen.

[Came back to see my future husband again]

[I think he's a rookie actor, but his vibe is so good^^]

"...??"

Park Moondae almost rubbed his non-existent eyes. What Cheongryeo was looking at... was the music video teaser. The one he had appeared in.

*Crazy.*

[......]

A man stood on a beach, turning his head with a sorrowful expression. The strange atmosphere, exquisite camera angle, and backlighting combined to create an odd charm.

[He's from LeTi!]

It wasn't exactly viral, but it seemed that minor chatter in online communities had a slightly positive effect on the song's ranking.

*This is the cause.*

It was a new anomaly unique to this reset. Nothing major, though. Cheongryeo spoke as an extension of their small talk.

"A talent I've never seen before."

[......]

"Hmm, he's under our company."

Cheongryeo quickly analyzed.

*Seeing as he didn't go the idol route, he's likely in his mid-to-late 20s or older. An irregularly scouted talent, almost no training period.*

*In that case...*

*Assuming this was a few years ago, would he have value as a resource?*

The more options available, the safer it was. The probability was slim, but he still contemplated gathering information.

Help suddenly interjected.

[He looks too old to join your group.]

[Anyway, I'm leaving now.]

Leaving that abrupt message, the pop-up stopped.

"......"

The entity didn't answer, just as it had forewarned it would do as it pleased. However, Cheongryeo knew it hadn't disappeared. The bright red error deletion window was still there.

"Ha."

He let out a short laugh and took his hand off his smartphone. It didn't matter if he couldn't control this kind of behavior from Help.

Unless he was looking at a new person's profile, it made no difference whether Help, which was just text, was there or not. Besides, since it returned even after being deleted, it would probably stick around in the next reset too.

*Then does it become a constant instead of a variable?*

Something that's always there without change.

*Hmm.*

Cheongryeo faintly thought that wouldn't be so bad.

***

I thought.

*Is this the power of a trait from a child actor who drew ten million viewers?*

[The music video teaser's reaction is amazing, Gunwoo!]

The calls from LeTi kept coming. At this stage, the staff tended to exaggerate any speck of public reaction by calling it 'amazing', and this was exactly that. Their attitude became very proactive too.

[You have two weeks left of your US trip, right? We're planning to meet you at the airport, so could you tell us your airplane flight details?]

*I'm screwed.*

I pressed my eyes. I was going to make up a plausible excuse to postpone it.

*Should I say I broke my leg or something?*

*No, they'd probably come to personally escort me then.*

This was all a side effect of the video turning out too well. The thought of an ending where I get arrested for illegal entry because I don't have a passport made my eye twitch.

D-14 until entry.

And the method... there is none!

***

[Park Moondae : Searching for a way to enter the country...]

[Park Moondae : In despair!]

In the hologram pop-up, the pixelated Park Moondae slumped to the ground.

"Oh my god."

"...A way to get to Korea without a passport?"

And surprisingly, a moment later.

"I've got it!"

The ❬Raising Park Moondae❭ operation team had found a solution.
