# Chapter 426

I had not completely lost contact with the VTIC members after returning to reality. It started right from the beginning.

[VTIC Chaeyul: Moondae~~]

[VTIC Chaeyul: You remember the wish, right? Haha, the wish!]

He contacted me the very next day. His tone clearly suggested he was checking in just in case I did not remember, while also reminding me that something had happened between us.

*Even if I hadn't remembered, I would have questioned them about this.*

I had barely stopped the VTIC members from creating a 'Wish group chat' right before we filmed the healing variety show.

[VTIC Chaeyul: Ah, that's right. Since there are people who weren't in Wish, they might feel left out ㅠ]

[VTIC Shino: Then should we just invite everyone to the old group chat]

[Hold on.]

We almost ended up in a situation where all our members had to reply to the senior group's daily greetings.

*After that, it was quiet, so I thought nothing more would come of it.*

I had even seen a news headline that their enlistment date was set, so I figured I would not see them for a while. I would have kept thinking that if one of them had not suddenly called me. Especially at this hour.

*...What game?*

*Who taught you the crazy act of calling a junior at 2 AM just because you had an idea?*

When I questioned him indirectly, this was Judan's answer.

[You just had a live broadcast filming, so I assumed you wouldn't be in bed yet. I have my own sense of propriety.]

"...Yes."

That was not propriety. It was an assumption.

Anyway, the guy started to get to his point.

[I have a plan. The proposal is to release a surprise fan song before enlistment. We want to treat it as a meaningful memento even if we can't promote it.]

*Is that so? We'll have to release ours so it doesn't overlap. I should probably find their schedule.*

"Congratulations. When are you releasing it?"

[It's under discussion.]

"...?"

They did not have much time left. If it was still under discussion...

*Hey, don't tell me.*

"Did Cheongryeo not approve it?"

[......]

"You didn't tell him, did you."

A long silence followed.

[We are, after all, members of the same group, so the word 'approval' is a bit inappropriate.]

*So you didn't get his approval. Got it.*

[And... wait, this call isn't being leaked, is it?]

I turned my head.

"Is that Judan-sunbaenim?"

"Yes."

"Ah, I see. Tell him I said hello."

No one else was very interested. They had realized it was not the album news they awaited.

Once I let him know that it was already being treated as a personal call, Judan lowered his voice and continued.

[The timeline that hyung knew is already over. Wouldn't there come a time when long-held beliefs become stubbornness and cloud one's judgment?]

*Are you serious? That bastard?*

"Then, how about talking to him directly..."

[Of course, that doesn't necessarily mean right now.]

*Breathe before you answer.*

Anyway, he said they planned to persuade their Leader on their own and that there was something he wanted to ask of TeSTAR. From this point on, we all listened on speakerphone.

[So, what I'd like to ask is...]

"Yes, yes, yes."

[It's a charity concert.]

"......"

The topic suddenly jumped from an album to helping the needy.

"What a truly meaningful idea!"

*No, don't be like that already.*

[Yes, the themes of fan songs are universally about gratitude and love, aren't they? So we're planning this because the thematic frequencies seem to match quite well.]

To summarize his explanation, instead of promotions, they were thinking of holding a charity concert hosted by VTIC.

[And we would like TeSTAR to appear as guests.]

This gave me a strange sense of déjà vu.

Sure enough, Ryu Chungwoo carefully interjected.

"Um, is this by any chance an extension of the charity concert Moondae invited you to last time?"

[That's also part of the reason I'm suggesting it, as it would be better if we frame the narrative that way.]

That was right. It was a return favor for them appearing as guests at our charity concert. Since we had held our first charity concert with VTIC's company, they wanted us to appear as a guest.

"Oh."

"That's a good cause! I'm all for it."

He even said they were not just inviting us, but several other teams.

*Then it's just like a big event.*

In truth, it was an awkward situation to refuse after we had so readily invited them as guests before. The atmosphere naturally leaned toward a tentative agreement.

"I agree~ It seems like a very meaningful and wonderful event! But we still need to talk to our company, so could you give us a little time?"

[Of course. I will contact you again tomorrow morning around ten.]

It was a response that did not consider that people sleep, but in any case, the agreement was put on hold.

"Ayy, we should be the ones to call you~ We can't have you calling us again!"

The other guys did not interfere and tacitly approved of the one who had amiably ended the call. The guy who interjected was not the type to do so without a reason.

"Hmm."

After the call ended, the person in question, Keun Sejin, sat in the living room and smiled bitterly.

"You... did you have something else in mind?"

"...Kind of, right? I was worried that it might look like the company is sending us to a charity concert out of guilt because of the recent controversy, like we're doing volunteer work."

"...!"

"If it looks like that, is it... not good?"

It was not good.

I opened my mouth.

"If we go in a direction where we're taking responsibility for the company's mistake, it'll look like we're also at fault."

"Correct~"

"Ahh..."

Our images would get tied together. We barely distanced ourselves from our agency's bullshit with the premise that 'the artists are victims too', and now we were doing a charity concert? The problem was that it could be linked to an image of 'the artists are also reflecting on their actions'.

[T1 is trying so hard, TeSTAR is trying so hard ㅋㅋ]

[See, they knew about it and participated. They feel guilty, so they're piggybacking on their sunbae's charity concert for some media play WW]

Something like that.

*He must have suffered a bit in GY-ROP. He's gotten more agile.*

I looked at Keun Sejin with pleasure and nodded.

"We need to approach this carefully. Well, it's good that we can use this as an excuse to adjust the album schedule a bit."

Compared to when the album was postponed indefinitely, it was a happy problem, but it also meant the head office might try to rush us to release it first and see what happened.

*Then the promotions will be clumsy.*

Using the charity concert as a pretext to secure a few weeks for the promotion staff was a pretty attractive bait.

"I don't think we need to adjust the schedule. We can finish preparing the album within a fortnight!"

"You might be able to, but wouldn't it be hard for the company's promotion managers?"

"Aigoo~ Our Raebin, you must be so frustrated that not everyone in the world is a genius like you, right?"

"...?! That's too much praise, no, I've never thought that..."

"Ahaha!"

*Hey, you're going to make the kid break.*

Anyway, that was why.

"Anyone have a good idea?"

Honestly, we did not have to do this charity concert. It was just a matter of professional courtesy, so I told them to speak freely. Just then, someone waved his hand very freely.

"Yep."

Cha Eugene's eyes met mine, and he grinned.

***

The next day, Cha Eugene confidently declared his idea to Judan.

"We're doing it because we're close. Make sure you tell the fans that first!"

That was right.

[It's okay to do charity as friends. We're friends with those sunbaenims now, so it's not a lie.]

*Let's just frame it as us doing it because of our groups' friendship.*

We would just be one of many guests, so it would not provoke our fans' animosity. We could also build an image of being famous enough to be helpful at a charity event.

[Well, that won't be difficult with a few preliminary preparations.]

"I'm not doing that, Moondae-hyung is."

[I thought so.]

The guys who remembered being in the same group communicated without much awkwardness.

"Right. That aside."

I smiled faintly.

*Shall we try to gain a little more profit?*

"Sunbaenim, I have one more guest I'd like to recommend."

[Hmm?]

I had actually made a call a few minutes earlier, but not to Judan.

[Yes. Hello!!]

It was the second-place winner of Mirynay. Her voice sounded more disciplined for some reason, though I did not know why.

Anyway, I was about to get to the point, but she spoke first.

[First of all, I really want to say thank you!]

[Yes?]

[...Ah.]

However, before I could react properly, she let out a short sigh and her words quickened.

[It's just... yes. It's not thanks for a specific situation. It's just something I'm saying because you always take such good care of your juniors!!]

[Ah, yes.]

[That was all!]

*No, it's fine to talk about the album.*

Anyone watching would have thought I had threatened her to keep her mouth shut.

*You've been so cooperative, why are you acting like this?*

I recalled this junior's actions over the past few weeks. Since our labels were different, the information I could get was biased. Until now, Mirynay's Park Minha had earned her keep as an informant, bringing me every little detail about company happenings and schedule changes from an artist's perspective.

That was why I thought of making this proposal.

[A good offer came in. I thought it would be good to tell you juniors about it, so I contacted you.]

[Wh-what kind of...?]

[A charity concert.]

We planned to first clearly emphasize that we were participating due to our personal friendship with VTIC, but what if, as the next step, Mirynay joined in?

*It becomes something TeSTAR initiated.*

When the stage gets bigger like that, it takes on a slightly new meaning. People will still think of the agency's controversy, but the idea will be distorted because the foundation of personal friendship has already been laid.

[This is... they're cutting ties with their agency]

[Drawing the line lolololololololol]

[The management must have been such assholes for the kids to send a signal like this]

Then, following T1's head office, the artists would also be sending a signal that they were drawing a line with the management's actions. As in,*If we were really in charge, we would choose this kind of positive influence.*

*And anyway, the management that's been kicked out won't be able to touch our schedule now.*

It was not bad.

Mirynay's reaction after hearing the explanation was also positive.

[That... I think all our members would like that.]

And so, it was finalized.

Judan nodded at the guest recommendation.

[That's fine. We were already thinking of casting a wide range of singers by gender and age to broaden the viewer demographic anyway.]

"Yes."

*It's a perfect fit.*

Was the only thing left a final check?

"But how did you persuade Cheongryeo?"

*How did you guys handle the issue of getting your Leader's permission?*

Surprisingly, this guy just said he got the okay with great confidence.

[...I presented various logics and reasons, so I won't hastily specify which one worked.]

But it seemed he did not know the reason himself.

*Anyway, it seems that guy also found some angle to profit from.*

I would have to watch the progress with a bit of caution.

I gladly handed off the call with Judan to the other guys and reviewed the situation in my head.

*Hmm.*

We would earn time to properly prepare the album promotions and cut ties with the management. Killing two birds with one stone. It did not seem likely to fall through midway.

So I called the Status Window.

[Hyung?]

*Hey, are you free on this day by any chance?*

It was in Korea, and a lot of people he knew would be there, so it should be fine to send him a ticket.

*Come see the pre-recording.*
