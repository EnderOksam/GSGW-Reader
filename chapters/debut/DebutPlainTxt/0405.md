# Chapter 405 - A Path of His Own, II

Ballerino Seon Ahyeon had never walked a different path.

His lot in life was to tread carefully along the single path that led to his destination.

The road was steep and rough at times, but his destination was always in sight. The sense of responsibility that told him he couldn't stop kept his feet moving forward.

*Just responsibility.*

*When I first started... It was fun.*

It was fascinating to take on new challenges and discover new ways of expressing himself. Dancing had been such an alluring activity.

However, everything changed after he suddenly won an international competition by a margin greater than anyone could have imagined.

*Ah.*

– *Ahyeon, you were born to do this!*

The luxury of choice disappeared.

He joined an agency, countless articles were written about him, and he became famous, with a formidable reputation and well-known affiliations.

Now that the perfect path had opened up before him, he could do nothing but follow it.

There was no reason or justification to rebel against all expectations and advice. All that remained was a life aimed at greater success through ballet.

...But something seemed wrong.

*I... I haven't experienced enough of this to dedicate my entire life to it.*

Still, it wasn't an unbearable fate. Dancing was fun.

*I can't do everything I want, I suppose.*

He heard similar things during counseling as well.

Because he wasn't at the point of wanting to give up on everything, the counselor assigned to him by the agency didn't recommend a long break or a change of career.

But his sense of resignation didn't go away.

He was no longer confident about the future.

*I... I won't be able to do anything else from now on.*

He had already poured his childhood and half of his teenage years into it.

He had no real friends or social experience. He hadn't been able to study properly, either.

Deviating from this orbit was impossible.

So he couldn't seek out other things now. He couldn't burden his agency, his parents, and everyone involved with uncertainty and worry.

He couldn't ruin this life, which was already a guaranteed success.

*Wouldn't it be nice if I were the sort of person who never had any doubts or worries?*

Seon Ahyeon worked hard to make that happen.

He didn't pursue any other hobbies or imagine other relationships or the lives he could have lived. He deliberately narrowed the radius of his own existence.

He didn't want to have any regrets or curiosity.

That was how he was able to consistently win.

– *The principal dancer this time will be Ahyeon. As expected!*

– *Then, let's film just three more advertisements this way. Is that okay, Ahyeon-ssi?*

His position became increasingly set in stone.

He could never break free of this. He couldn't afford to fail.

*Okay.*

Yet just when Seon Ahyeon's path seemed completely set, he unexpectedly encountered a survival show contestant sitting in the hallway of a broadcast station he'd visited for a scheduled appearance. The contestant looked as if he'd lost his mind.

– *Are... Are you okay?*

The words he poured out in an attempt to encourage that person somehow sounded unfamiliar to him.

– *Good results aren't necessarily the answer.*

– *Choosing not to put yourself through pain is also a valid answer.*

*I...*

He never thought about letting go so easily, so he didn't know why he said it with such confidence.

It was as if someone other than himself was speaking.

*It's strange.*

But those words were comforting to him as well.

– *Sometimes an alternative plan can be what you need.*

Even though he didn't believe he could do so himself.

And surprisingly, the other person soon regained his spirits and achieved great results by taking a different direction.

– *Wow, it was really well done!*

– *It was the best.*

Even though he'd only been a temporary mentor, the whole situation and that person had stirred a strange feeling of excitement in him.

– *...Thank you. Thanks to you, we finished the performance well.*

When Ryu Gunwoo said those words, Ahyeon wanted to tell him sincerely that he was the one who should be grateful.

But he felt that saying so might make Gunwoo uncomfortable.

*...If I ever get the chance...*

Seon Ahyeon wanted to become friends with him and tell him then.

He wanted to apply the advice he'd given this participant, which had felt so unfamiliar to him, to his own life as well.

With that resolution, he exchanged numbers with Gunwoo and made a promise...

And as the participant disappeared down the hallway, Seon Ahyeon suddenly saw something.

*Uh?*

– *...You?*

Someone with blond hair wearing an in-ear monitor was standing in a dark area that looked like backstage, speaking to him.

He was dressed for a performance, his hand outstretched as if to draw Ahyeon toward him.

"...!"

However, before that hand could reach him, the strange afterimage quickly disappeared.

It was far too clear to dismiss as a hallucination.

*Ah.*

However, strangely enough, Seon Ahyeon didn't feel anxious.

It just... felt right. A strange feeling settled in his mind, as if something that had been wrong was being set right.

*This is good.*

Rather, he wanted to see more. Seon Ahyeon began to commit those afterimages to memory so he wouldn't forget them.

Then the afterimages began to appear again here and there.

When he looked out at the audience, got ready in the waiting room, or went to a broadcasting station.

When he cautiously contacted the idol who had seemed so down in the dumps.

– *Wow!*

It was a life that was no easier, noisier and more crowded, with more hardships and ups and downs.

And yet the images of that life were more lovely, more sparkling and heart-pounding.

Those images confused Seon Ahyeon, but strangely enough, he still didn't feel anxious that he was going in the wrong direction.

And gradually, the afterimages grew stronger, forming connections and coming together into a story.

Images connected by dotted lines.

*What is it?*

Seon Ahyeon carefully puzzled over them and stored them away in his mind.

And at this moment...

"We've performed this same song at a concert before."

As if those words had lifted the shackles, sound and movement flooded into the gaps between the images.

And the afterimages he'd kept gained meaning and turned into memories.

Memories of his real life.

"...Back then..."

Eventually, Seon Ahyeon realized.

*Ah.*

He was not a ballerino.

He was Seon Ahyeon, someone who had failed to become a ballerino and run away from classical dance.

*But...*

*I was happy.*

Seon Ahyeon had become an idol.

"We were holding... balloons."

He recalled the fun unit stage at his first concert. Then there was a group performance and an after-party.

The version of himself who stuttered, the version of himself who gave up, the version of himself who refused to listen to reason, the version of himself who even doubted his friend.

"...Moondae."

It was okay to fail.

Quitting ballet didn't mean that he was worthless.

Even the life that had crumbled under the weight of merciless bullying and the school life he'd abandoned had been recovered.

Life didn't have to stay in ruins.

Not as long as he had the will to choose a new direction and move forward.

*That's right.*

On the other hand, living a seemingly perfect life without any terrible failures didn't make his inner anxiety and fear go away.

On the contrary, it was this realization that erased the trauma TeSTAR's Seon Ahyeon had carried.

The shame of thinking he'd been weak and chosen to give up at a crossroads in his life.

*I too... I did my best at every turn. Just like the other members.*

All the questions and doubts that had buried him for so long faded away.

He remembered the words that had first given him the courage to stray from the original path and follow a new one without hesitation.

– *Just think to yourself, "I'm going to do this." Nothing else.*

And now, he was facing the person who'd once said those words to him.

***

"So... You didn't have your memories at first."

"No, I didn't."

I went into the waiting room with Seon Ahyeon.

It was okay for Seon Ahyeon, who'd already received his award in the first part, to return to his seat a little late. Moreover, I hadn't even been nominated in the first place.

That was why I could sit face-to-face with this guy for a little while.

Seon Ahyeon nodded and smiled.

"I... I just remembered. Completely."

Although his appearance was no different, I could tell from his expressions, movements, and tone of voice. The Seon Ahyeon I knew was back.

I was speechless and couldn't talk for a while, but then I finally answered.

"Okay."

And I held out my hand.

"Welcome back."

"Yes...!"

The guy held out both hands and shook my hand. He really was still the same guy.

*...Alright.*

*I need to calm down. We need to figure out the situation first. Catching up can come later.*

First, the main question.

*How did Seon Ahyeon get his memories back?*

I took a deep breath and got to the point.

He said that after several afterimages, hearing me talk about our past together had completely restored his memories, as if he'd been given a wake-up call.

"When I mentioned the concert to you?"

"That's right..."

If that had been the trigger, then the process had been building up for some time.

"I thought I could almost understand what you were saying, Moondae... I wanted to. And then it suddenly came to me."

It seemed Seon Ahyeon had remembered each afterimage as it came to him and connected the dots between the people he'd seen in them.

Still, it didn't seem he'd realized that I was the Park Moondae who appeared in his afterimages...

"How did you know that I was Park Moondae?"

Seon Ahyeon's eyes widened.

It was as if he wondered how I could ask such an obvious question.

"...! Even if your appearance is slightly different... You're still very much Moondae."

"Ah."

"And... You told me before."

The guy blushed, speaking slowly and seriously, sounding out each word.

"You told me your name was originally Ryu Gunwoo."

"...!"

"I remember... Of course, I do."

*Ah.*

I remembered that time.

After doing all those stupid things and revealing who I was all at once, I'd barely regained my composure and managed to convince Seon Ahyeon that I was telling the truth.

*...Not the most pleasant memory.*

But it seemed that it hadn't been entirely useless.

I decided to revise my thoughts slightly.

Maybe the decision to talk to him back then wasn't a bad idea.

"Thank you."

"...! N-no! We're friends. It's only natural that we should do that."

Seon Ahyeon smiled brightly. I smiled back, not sure what else to do.

*I thought he would be very confused when his memories came back.*

Seon Ahyeon did not seem to be suffering from pain or internal conflict at all.

On the contrary, he seemed more comfortable than before.

*Bae Sejin was right.*

– *But just because he looks that way on the outside doesn't mean he's fine on the inside... Who knows?*

I had to admit it. *You win.*

After that, I quickly informed him of the current situation.

I no longer felt the pressure of having to provide more details to back up my reasoning and convince him that all of this was really happening.

"For more details, let's talk over the phone after the awards ceremony."

"Yes!"

We quickly returned to our seats before any more time could slip away.

Thanks to performing with the two nominated artists, I'd been given a temporary seat at the corner of their table.

Bae Sejin, who'd been looking at us as if to ask 'Why are you guys so late?', seemed to realize that something had happened as soon as he saw Seon Ahyeon's expression.

As the VCR played, he quickly whispered in my ear.

"Did he just...?"

I nodded slowly. A big smile began to spread on Bae Sejin's face.

And he immediately poked Seon Ahyeon, who was sitting next to him.

"Ahyeon!" he whispered.

"Hey, Sejin-hyung...!"

I left the excited Bae Sejin and Seon Ahyeon to whisper to one another, gesturing wildly. It looked like articles about their supposed friendship were going to be published. I chuckled and watched the VCR play on the screen.

The awards ceremony was resuming.

And that night...

The TeSTAR group chat finally contained all seven members.

I lay down on my bed and scrolled through the group chat, where people of mixed ages had quickly returned to their usual excited chatter despite nearly falling into chaos over how they should address each other.

Half of the people here were actually in the dorm, but it felt a bit different.

*I guess I'll wait a little while before contacting Seon Ahyeon.*

I'd let them chat and tell him the details a little later.

Before that, I needed to clear up some questions in my head.

*Hmm.*

Well, all things considered, this was a good development... But I still had to figure out why only Seon Ahyeon had regained his memories on his own.

*Seon Ahyeon still isn't in my status window, is he?*

Seon Ahyeon hadn't complained of the headaches or dizziness that were common when the others first regained their memories. His memories had returned naturally.

*...How?*

It was as if he'd been capable of it from the beginning...

*Like it was his own ability.*

Cheongryeo's room.

*By making sure there were no other hosts the system could latch onto.* That meant the system had to have been on the plane with us to begin with.

"If we were going to go through the damn trouble of destroying the system, we had to have the person who had it with us."

"Well, I'm not sure."

"You knew I'd forgotten, too."

"You're still smart, Hubaenim."
