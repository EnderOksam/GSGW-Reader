# Chapter 641

The viewers held their breath, their heads turning as one.

At the massive Jamsil Olympic Stadium, idols holding microphones were lifted high into the air from the concert stage, each enclosed in a transparent device.

"Minha!"

"Ah."

Mirynay's Park Minha hurriedly took off her VR headset. Seong Harin, the member who had shouted in her ear, spoke with relief.

"We have to go to practice."

"Oh, okay."

Park Minha's disappointment showed for a moment before she quickly hid it and turned off the device.

Seong Harin asked, "Uh... were you watching the TeSTAR concert?"

"Huh? Umm... yeah."

It wasn't a surprising question. All the Mirynay members were interested in TeSTAR's concert. It was, after all, a concert by an idol group that had been doing ridiculously well recently.

Since they were from the same company, they might use a similar format. Wasn't it worth monitoring?

Still, she didn't need to show she had been watching it so intently in real-time.

*For idols, friendships with the opposite gender are a risk...*

There was no need to leave any room for that.

The members who wanted to monitor the concert had an unspoken agreement. They would watch the VOD later on their own to avoid useless misunderstandings about dating or crushes.

That was why Park Minha had just stopped her real-time monitoring without a word, but the scene she had just witnessed flickered in her mind.

The transparent devices carrying each TeSTAR member rose from the stage into the air.

*That thing from before...*

The members getting ready at the entrance happened to chime in.

"Come to think of it, I think I heard something about those sunbaenims using some... latest technology again for their concert."

"That's right. I heard they spent over two months just giving feedback on the order to get that machine made."

"Really?"

"Yeah, yeah."

"Wow, it must be something amazing! Two months?"

Jeong Yulgi reacted so strongly that even an uninterested member asked, her ears perking up.

"No, what kind of machine is it exactly? Is it a stage device?"

It was. A special stage device.

Park Minha recalled Park Moondae's voice. She had overheard it through a cracked meeting room door while passing by in the company building.

[I think there might be a demand for this.]

It must have been during a meeting about the concert. Around that time, rumors had already spread quietly within the company that TeSTAR had packed this concert completely full of songs.

*Well... people would like that!*

Park Minha remembered inwardly agreeing. A packed performance was generally considered 'getting your money's worth' at a concert.

However, the audience was made of people, and people's value judgments were bound to differ.

[I wanted to see the kids talk and chat in person more ㅠㅠ]

[It felt like we just watched stages without any real connection]

The stages could be seen plenty online. These audience members wanted to feel a special intimacy and sense of reality when they came to see the artists in person.

That was why TeSTAR had also considered a supplementary measure. It was a device that could provide a special 'connecting' experience for the audience while still being packed with songs.

"They say it's a machine that can go right up to the audience's seats."

~~~

Delayed screams erupted intermittently from all over the concert venue.

*W-wait?*

An all-fan of TeSTAR blinked.

The stage devices carrying the members rose into the air and neared the audience seats. That was great. She had seen videos of similar productions where members on wires came close to the audience.

But... it wasn't stopping.

[Umm, umum, Um-]

A soft, humming instrumental echoed through the venue as TeSTAR got even closer.

The hemispherical stage device looked like a small hot-air balloon, a bubble, or a soap bubble. It was now so close that the person inside looked larger than a finger.

It didn't stop there. It grew to the size of her hand. Soon it came closer, taking up as much of her vision as the light stick in front of her.

Bigger.

Closer.

The detailed contours of their faces became visible.

"......"

Her hand, holding the light stick, trembled.

Soon, the all-fan found herself face-to-face with a member. He seemed to slow to a stop right in front of her.

Seon Ahyeon smiled gently and waved.

"Hk."

The fan held her breath, feeling a hiccup coming on.

Productions that involved approaching the audience were already common in concerts. Protruding stages were a prime example. Various methods had been tried, from riding in moving carts called 'torokko' to using hot-air balloons or even hiding in the audience.

However, most were content with getting moderately close. The larger the venue, the greater the physical limitations and safety issues.

But problems are meant to be overcome.

The floating stage device, fixed to wires for maximum stability, came right up to the front of the audience seats. They could see the members actually lift their microphones.

"......"

The soft intro melody ended.

A member stopped right in front of her.

The all-fan couldn't even breathe as she looked at Park Moondae. Their eyes met.

He had a pale face, with slightly long hair covering the nape of his neck.

His voice rang out.

[Stay here-]

His clear, rich vocals passed through her entire body. She felt unreal goosebumps.

The lyrics echoed in her ears.

[A deep story

Flows between us

I want to dive in and paint a splash]

Park Moondae passed by. Lee Sejin followed, smiling coolly and extending his hand.

Her hand unconsciously reached out, passing right in front of Lee Sejin's as if they were about to high-five. They almost touched.

[Even if we clap our hands

So no sound is made

Let me be held deep inside your heart]

Ryu Chungwoo made eye contact with each person and smiled. Bae Sejin looked into their eyes earnestly, singing with all his heart.

The key changed, and the pre-chorus rose.

[The waves grow

Bigger and clearer]

Cha Eugene plopped down on the stage device to playfully meet their eye level. Kim Raebin carefully observed each person in the audience with a serious face.

She was face-to-face with them.

[Until they cover the whole world-]

The bass and drums dropped softly. The sound washed over her body like a wave.

[Stay

I'll be here

Just like this, with this same heart

An unchanging promise]

The members visited every corner of the audience, not missing a single spot. The meticulously planned path left no one out.

Even on the fast-moving stage devices, their voices were unwavering. The big screens showed their serious faces.

[Stay

On days when you're tired and weary

Come find me, lean on my shoulder

And rest]

The light sticks, under central control, were dyed in TeSTAR's three symbolic colors. The sight looked like a flower garden in full bloom or a festival of lights. The members turned to the big screen for a moment and smiled.

They looked genuinely happy.

Sharing that overwhelming feeling together was a very special experience for everyone there.

*Guys...*

However, some people watched this scene but couldn't share the feeling.

The people who weren't there.

[I'm so jealous..]

[Ah, I wanted to go in person ㅠㅠ]

These were the online viewers, watching the stage with a slightly bitter feeling. They felt a deep sense of alienation and wallowed in sadness, muttering things like, 'This is why you have to see concerts in person'.

"......"

[Always]

In front of the VR camera.

Park Moondae quietly stopped his stage device and looked at the camera, as if looking at the person on the other side.

[I'll cherish it

And hold it tight

The moments we spent together]

He looked as if he could see the audience beyond it.

His gaze was fixed here.

[FML ㅠㅠㅠ]

[I think my heart's gonna drop]

[Wow what is this for real... ah it's a weird feeling]

"......"

The exploding number of comments suddenly dwindled. They seemed truly at a loss for words, as if they had made eye contact for real.

[All as memories]

Soon, his stage device rose again, moving away from the camera.

The instrumental swelled once more.

[Stay

I'll be here

Just like this, with this same heart

An unchanging promise]

Like petals or starlight, like a beautiful natural phenomenon forming a momentary pattern, TeSTAR floated in a semicircle above the stage and sang.

The protective upper part of the devices descended. Their raw voices burst out over the surrounding audience.

And the members lowered their gazes.

They looked directly at the standing section right in front of them.

[Stay

On days when you're tired and weary

Come find me, lean on my shoulder

And rest]

The light sticks swayed.

[Stay]

The lights twinkled all the way to the distant seats in the air. They looked like the Milky Way, their distance hard to gauge.

Even though she had just seen their faces up close, it was hard to believe people were actually there.

[Beyond the time, in your heart-]

Park Moondae thought.

He had created this situation with plausible, rational statements about the concert's balance and meeting demand.

[Stay]

But perhaps he was the one who wanted to know the other person, not the audience.

[We know for sure there are people all the way out there, but what we see is the movement of the light sticks.]

[Yep, I'm curious.]

[...I just want to see it with my own eyes.]

The words that came from the members might not have been so different from his own thoughts.

[Then let's go see them.]

A high harmony exploded like a firecracker.

[Stay-!]

A spectacular fireworks display erupted along with the instrumental.

BANG.

A feast of light embroidered the completely dark sky. Drones flew through it, drawing the song's lyrics like a lyric video.

Fireworks exploded in the sky.

On the ground, the twinkling lights of the light sticks seemed to reflect the sight.

[Stay here-]

The song closed with a beautiful harmony.

Without an instrumental, an a cappella matched to the piano's melody left a long, lingering echo as the members of TeSTAR landed back on the stage.

The big screens filled with the faces they had just seen, including a pleasantly smiling Park Moondae.

"......"

The all-fan's eyes suddenly welled up. She barely managed a sniffle.

"Hk."

She made a sound without realizing it. It was low and hoarse from all her screaming.

"Sniff."

"......"

It was the person in the seat next to her.

She turned her head and was surprised. The man beside her had buried his face in his hands, his shoulders shaking slightly. Anyone could see he was in the same state as her. Even his camera trembled on his lap.

*H-His camera is going to fall!*

She confirmed that TeSTAR wasn't on stage for a moment and quickly rummaged through her bag. She had received bottled water, so this should be fine.

"E-Excuse me."

She carefully held out the tissues she had taken out.

"...!"

The person beside her flinched, then slowly took a few tissues.

She couldn't hear him well. The man, who she guessed was an actor, seemed to say 'thank you so much' while bowing deeply. He then covered his eyes and mouth with the tissues.

*Uh...*

Feeling a sense of kinship, she guessed the man was also a huge TeSTAR fan who had gotten an invitation from an affiliate.

*I should give him a snack when the time is right.*

Her heart warmed, and the tension of being in the affiliates' section eased a bit. She turned her gaze back to the stage with renewed vigor.

...For reference, a similar scene was unfolding right next to them.

"Hyung, this is so moving..."

"......"

Cheongryeo didn't scold his member for getting so moved at a rival's concert. Instead, he crossed his arms and briefly reviewed the stage they had just seen.

*Clever.*

After a series of flashy, performance-focused stages, they had changed the mood in one go. It was clever because this kind of production would emotionally imprint even a new, unfamiliar song on the audience.

It was an experimental attempt, but the budget was used appropriately.

However...

*They might not have calculated that far.*

But that's probably what made it an even better move.

"Let's invite TeSTAR to our concert too...!"

"Mm."

Cheongryeo gave a short laugh at Chaeyul's words, then refocused on the stage.

The concert was nearing its end.
