# Chapter 497

"You okay?"

*I'm gonna kill him... No, I don't even have the strength to kill him.*

*You bastard.*

I sprawled out on the floor of LeTi's practice room, the one reserved for VTIC. I had been practicing choreography with that bastard Cheongryeo for seven hours.

*Isn't he insane?*

He should be swamped with his comeback preparations. Why would he clear out a whole seven hours for a single guest performance? The worst part was that it didn't look like it would end today.

He cleverly pushed a person to their limit, like an extreme personal training session, only to let them barely catch their breath. It was a work of art.

*This guy would have made a fortune as an instructor.*

Wait, saying that to a guy who had already made a fortune was probably meaningless.

*My brain isn't working...*

I caught my breath. TeSTAR was not a group that slacked on practice. We were no strangers to high-volume practice sessions. However, cramming an unfamiliar dance style into someone in such a short period was a different story.

I couldn't help but remember ❬Idol Inc.❭, when I learned to dance seriously for the first time at that age. I silently stared at the ceiling, still spread-eagled on the floor. An unfamiliar fluorescent light shone down.

*Hm?*

I opened my mouth without thinking.

"Did you remodel the lighting?"

"Hm?" Cheongryeo, who was fiddling with the music, answered with a puzzled look. "We did. Three years ago, though."

"Ah."

Then I realized. The LeTi practice room in my memory, the one I practiced in for nearly a year, was not this one.

*That wasn't reality.*

It was the ceiling I saw when I tried to win the Grand Prize as 'Wish', a hybrid group mixing members of VTIC and TeSTAR. It was an experience from when I debuted with the VTIC guys in Ryu Gunwoo's body, inside a virtual world the System created.

*The words just slipped out without thinking.*

It seemed Cheongryeo had noticed that part too.

"Is it similar to when you debuted with me? I suppose it could be. I fixed that one too."

He followed up with the ridiculous explanation that 'when you make something multiple times, a standard specification emerges and it becomes formalized.'

I let out a dry laugh.

"Is there such a thing as efficiency for a practice room?"

"Of course."

If he had figured that out too, I couldn't imagine how much of a veteran he was. It was nothing new, but in terms of experience and information, it would be hard for anyone to compare to that guy.

Hmm, as expected.

*I thought VTIC would see some decline after their military service hiatus.*

I needed to prepare more rigorously. From the rookies who debuted backed by capital and benchmarked us, to VTIC whose form had not faltered, TeSTAR was also caught in the middle.

*I can't let my guard down just because we won the Grand Prize.*

I drank some water and quickly organized my thoughts for the next album. Just then, Cheongryeo sat down next to me.

"Hubaenim, your choreography acquisition is pretty good."

Of course it was.

"If I didn't have good acquisition at this stage of my career, I'd have to quit."

"Haha."

It didn't sound like he was laughing because it was funny. He probably had experience with a member who lacked acquisition at this stage of their career.

*He must have been going crazy with dissatisfaction.*

I could tell just by looking at that guy's status window. He was a guy who had already ground his talents to their absolute limit, to his max stats. He must have confidence in his routine. He probably wouldn't have treated anyone who couldn't keep up with the practice regimen he set as a human being.

*...Hmm.*

But you know. A sudden question came to mind. I called for a certain someone, the only one I could have this kind of conversation with.

*Keundal.*

[Yep?]

"......"

He appeared in an instant. I thought his reply might be delayed since he was on duty, but I never expected it to be this prompt. Anyway, after chatting with him a bit about recent events, I brought it up.

*...About the potential stats that appear in the Status Window.*

[...?? Ah, you mean your Infinite Potential trait? That wasn't given by the System, it's something you originally had...]

No, that's not what I'm talking about.

[Yes?]

*I mean the ones that appear in other people's Status Windows.*

The limit stats displayed in parentheses next to the main stat. An indicator that tells you a person's limit.

Let me give an example.

[Dance : C- (B)]

Here, the 'B' in the parentheses is the limit. It was also the biggest reason why making a living in the arts and entertainment was only for those destined for it. The talent factor.

And what I was curious about was this.

*It's impossible to overcome that, no matter what you do.*

Is it impossible for a person to grow beyond that limit stat?

"......"

After a moment of silence, a timid pop-up appeared.

[Probably...?]

It was followed by the explanation, 'Actually, I just converted the System's judgment so you could see it...' In the end, it meant it was a value the System calculated.

*Hmm.*

I furrowed my brow. At that moment.

[Survey available!]

"...!"

Next to Keundal's chat pop-up, a Status Window pop-up suddenly appeared.

*What the.*

[Help : You can customize the function of through feedback from a Survey.]

"......"

So... you popped up to advertise yourself because we were talking about you? Are you saying you can tell me if it's possible?

I stared at the Status Window pop-up. It glowed like an ad banner, as if begging me to click it.

But you know.

*You think I'd do it?*

If you were me, would you fall for it? I dismissed the pop-up without hesitation.

*As soon as I collect the System fragment that guy has, you're getting scrapped.*

That was the right answer. Just as I was nodding to myself.

"You've rested enough, right? Get up."

"......"

For the record, I had been lying down for exactly five minutes.

*Hoo.*

I swallowed a string of curses and pushed myself up. Right, limit stats or whatever... my current dance stat was still B+. Well, fine. I couldn't whine that I couldn't do it at this point in my career.

*Let's see how far I can push myself.*

Let's do this.

That night, the door to TeSTAR's new dorm opened weakly.

Beep-boop-click.

A subdued voice followed.

"...I'm home."

It was Park Moondae, his hood pulled over his head.

"Oh, Moondae...??"

"......"

*What's with his condition?*

Lee Sejin, who had been taking a face mask out of the fridge, was flustered. He had been about to greet him cheerfully.

However, Park Moondae only replied with this.

"I practiced."

What kind of practice would make that Park Moondae look like that? This was the kid who would doggedly keep up during all-night practices and monitor his performances without sleeping. The sight of such a Park Moondae, limp and soggy like waterlogged laundry, was unfamiliar and even a little sobering...

*Wait a minute.*

For a moment, Lee Sejin remembered what Park Moondae had been doing.

"It was practice for the Cheongryeo feature, right?"

"......"

Who asks for a feature and then treats the person like one of their own company's trainees?

*Does he not know any boundaries?*

Although his opinion of Cheongryeo had softened slightly due to recent events, he was still in another group and a rival. That's why Lee Sejin was even more displeased.

*Should I have opposed it more actively?*

So he deliberately threw an arm around Park Moondae's shoulders and exclaimed cheerfully.

"Moondae Moondae~ Aren't you working too hard for someone else's benefit? Why are you doing it like your life depends on it!"

It was then.

"...I agree."

"...!"

Bae Sejin, who had been enjoying his life in a private room, had stealthily come out to the living room and backed him up. Lee Sejin was almost taken aback.

*What's with that hyung all of a sudden?*

"It's something you're doing even though you don't have to," Bae Sejin continued resolutely.

That's right. He was uncomfortable with VTIC's Cheongryeo. The shocking fact that he had suddenly ambushed Park Moondae early in their debut was a decisive factor, but it wasn't just that.

*...It was strange.*

It was the unique atmosphere of the person named Cheongryeo. In fact, he had rarely even spoken to him directly, but he could feel it just by observing. There were moments when he felt like a person who lacked an 'emotion' context. In other words, someone who lacked empathy.

It was enough to remind Bae Sejin of the psychopaths he himself had played. Being naturally sensitive and alert to others' attitudes, he felt it even more keenly.

It was an impression that didn't easily fade, even with the objective facts that he had received help in the System's virtual world or during the building collapse incident. As long as he didn't know that this was a gap created by an extremely long series of repeated restarts, it was inevitable.

*Hmm.*

Anyway, Lee Sejin looked at the now-serious Bae Sejin, then changed the mood again.

*Aigoo, if we get all serious here, Moondae will just listen even less.*

More lightly.

"That's right. Save your stamina for our album, Moondae~"

"I should."

*That's it!*

Lee Sejin cheered inwardly as he saw Park Moondae nod his head agreeably. However, Park Moondae wasn't finished.

"After I do what I have to do properly."

"......"

*Hey, then what's the difference between you and Kim Raebin, who works all night?*

The words telling him to look at himself before scolding his roommate, Kim Raebin, rose to his throat. Fortunately, next to Lee Sejin was Bae Sejin, who would get angry in his place.

"So, do it in a way that doesn't overexert you!"

"Yes. Well... I did practice within a range that wouldn't affect my health," Park Moondae answered calmly.

Then he muttered softly.

"Though that's what pisses me off more."

"...?"

"It's nothing."

Park Moondae fell silent, reflecting on the state of his exquisitely overworked body, but he soon shrugged.

"And it is helpful. It's my first time with this genre of choreography, and... I have something to get from him when it's over."

"Get from him?"

"Yes. There's something I need to get."

Park Moondae nodded and grinned. It was a surprisingly rewarding-looking smile.

"I think our self-produced content this time turned out to be fun, but I think it'll be a bit less mainstream than before."

"Hm?"

Ryu Seorin would balance it to be stimulating and exciting, but Park Moondae had also concluded that this was for the hardcore fans.

*Mystery requires you to use your head, so it's hard to watch casually.*

However, to connect it to the next album's teasers, it had to be somewhat symbolic and meaningful, which would move it further away from a lighthearted flavor.

*We managed to balance the public appeal by highlighting the horror elements.*

That was also only possible because it was late summer, early autumn. So, what I mean is.

"We need to properly secure mass appeal in the next album."

*I'll secure both national pride and mass appeal!*

Park Moondae grinned.

"I'm going to get something that will help with that."

A big picture was being drawn in his head. Though he did meet an ending where he was dragged into his bedroom by his roommate, who nagged him to 'just get some rest already'...

A few days passed like that, and finally, after TeSTAR's self-produced content, was grandly released. The day of the first filming for Cheongryeo's competition stage, featuring Park Moondae, arrived.
