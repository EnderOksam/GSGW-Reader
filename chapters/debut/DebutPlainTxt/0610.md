# Chapter 610

The TeSTAR members were in their dorm's living room. A languid atmosphere settled over them after they finished monitoring the pre-release clip of their variety show.

"It was a good idea to gather and watch this together, even though we're busy, right?"

"Mhmm..."

"Yes...*yawn*."

"Our office worker variety show is fun."

The sleepy members yawned or dozed off. After the music video release, their already tight schedule had become even more packed. They chose to watch together, no matter how late, rather than monitor individually in their nonexistent free time.

"Let's go to bed now... We have to wake up at 3 a.m. again tomorrow."

"God..."

The TeSTAR members naturally rose from the living room sofa. They moved in groups of two and three to go to bed. There was no particular tension. Everyone had their own expectations and anxieties about the upcoming promotions, but they seemed at ease when they were all together. It seemed nothing weighed on their minds.

On his way to wash his face, Ryu Chungwoo checked on each of them. When he came out of the bathroom with a towel, all the members had already gone to their beds.

All except for one.

Park Moondae sat in the dark kitchen, quietly drinking water.

"......"

His face was serene.

"...Hyung?"

Ryu Chungwoo called out unconsciously. Park Moondae, his cousin Ryu Gunwoo, turned to look at him. Ryu Chungwoo sat across from him and suggested thoughtfully.

"You should go to bed now."

Park Moondae was silent for a moment before replying, "Just a moment. Watching the pre-release clip of the office variety show reminded me of the shoot."

"Ah."

It seemed he was recalling how the situation had turned after the blood-stained calendar fell from the ceiling-mounted outdoor unit.

*I guess it makes sense that he can't sleep?*

Somehow amused, Ryu Chungwoo grinned.

"You mean when you tried to climb into the ceiling unit?"

"......"

Park Moondae avoided Ryu Chungwoo's gaze!

***

A horror atmosphere had suddenly descended upon the company variety show, where we were just bantering in suits.

"...For now, there doesn't seem to be a separate path. There are some bloodstains, though."

"Moondae, your legs are shaking right now."

*It's just my imagination.*

I pulled my upper body out of the ceiling, where I had stuck it to investigate.

*What a waste of a suit.*

It was a pit of dust.

"That's a relief. Moondae Moondae would have fainted if he saw a ghost."

*No, I wouldn't. Don't jinx it. I went this far just to shed my cowardly image.*

Anyway, I avoided the calendar and stepped down from the desk. The guys, who had finally regained their composure, started talking again. Their topic was the current situation.

"Why has the time suddenly jumped to July, and why are we at the office on a Sunday of all days... Um, does anyone know?"

As expected, there was no response.

"Seeing how grimy Moondae got, it doesn't seem like there's a secret passage or anything up in that ceiling."

"Right."

Bae Sejin nodded. "I guess it's not like that time with the zombie variety show."

"...A zombie variety show?"

"Ah!"

As if suddenly remembering this was all an office worker skit, the guys scrambled to cover for Bae Sejin.

"An escape room! He must have meant an escape room!"

"R-Right...!"

"Yeah. We did an escape room after our company dinner. That one!"

"That's right. The section chief led it~ And our intern Ryu was the star!"

Having recovered their senses, the guys successfully immersed themselves in their office worker identities once more. At the same time, the strange tension from the blood-stained calendar made them more cautious.

"Let's look for clues in here for now."

"Yeah. I feel like there must be a hint."

Instead of rushing out, they decided to search the office.

*They seem so immersed that they're genuinely scared. The viewers will probably find this fun.*

"Assistant Manager Moondae, let's do it together!"

"Alright, Joom."

At Cha Eugene's suggestion, I also began to rummage through the office. I found one thing for certain.

"A lot of things are locked."

"......"

Several cabinets and desk drawers were locked. If there was nothing to put inside, they could have just left them empty. The fact that they were locked like this without any padlocks was a bit suspicious.

*Will they be unlocked later?*

Just then, Bae Sejin slapped his own desk and exclaimed as if he had realized something.

"Come to think of it, about us developing an AI."

"...?"

*Why is he suddenly making an AI joke now?*

Amidst the guys who were flustered by the sudden attempt at a skit, Ryu Chungwoo kindly asked, "Section Chief, did you think of an AI you want to develop?"

"No, here...!"

Bae Sejin hastily shook his head and rummaged under his desk. Then he pulled out a file.

"Look at this!"

It was a navy-blue file folder with 'KIS' written in large letters on the front.

[Artificial Intelligence Entity Development]

"......"

So it was not a joke, but the actual setting.

"...Are we really developing an AI?"

"I told you we were!"

The brief misunderstanding was cleared up. We rummaged through the file, which seemed to be part of the company setting. On every document inside, every sentence was crossed out with a black marker. The only words we could make out were things like 'KIS', 'Artificial Intelligence', and 'Delivery'.

I flipped through the pages and muttered to myself, "People usually do this to sensitive data like personal information before putting it through a shredder."

"Ah, is that what this is~?"

"Yes."

It was then.

FLUTTER.

"Hm?"

A single sheet of paper, tucked into the very back of the file, fell onto the desk. The paper had a strangely glossy texture. A title was printed on its front in what looked like 26-point Myeongjo font.

[Summer Special Work Guidelines – Holiday Version]

"What's this?"

Lee Sejin rubbed his chin and began to read the contents. "Welcome to the valuable employees of KIS who have applied for special summer duty to mark this year's summer season..."

"......"

"...Wait a minute."

Which meant...

"What? We don't get a summer vacation?"

"I guess not."

It was just special holiday work.

*Special Sunday duty during the peak vacation season. What a fantastic world this is. Are they trying to show K-overtime to the world?*

"Th-There's more on the back...! I-I can see it through the paper."

"Ah."

At Seon Ahyeon's words, Ryu Chungwoo flipped the paper over. There, just as the title suggested, were neatly printed work regulations. However, they were in a very familiar format.

——————————

1. Please be sure to meet the daily delivery quota.

2. During summer work, only the 7th-floor office space of the company building will be open. Access to other floors is restricted.

3. Deliveries can be registered on the tablet PC at the entrance of each office, and by-products can be registered on the tablet PC in front of the B1 warehouse.

4. The hallway lights turning off is a natural power-saving phenomenon. Please return to your office with caution.

5. On public holidays, the elevator will only operate with an employee ID card to save power.

6. Do not go to the stairs

7. If an employee not listed on the work schedule appears, do not welcome them. Do not react to them.

8. Work will end once all deliveries and by-product processing are complete.

9. There is no rule 6 in these guidelines.

10. This headquarters operates on a flexible work system and has no end-of-work notification. Please recommend the sleeping quarters to any employee who reacts to a notification.

11. Only full-time employees may enter the break room.

Please be advised that KIS Co., Ltd. has no obligation to compensate for any situation that arises from not following the above guidelines.

We hope you have a rewarding work experience.

——————————

For a moment, a chilling silence fell over the office.

"...This is weird."

Ryu Chungwoo also touched the paper with his finger. "It says to stay on the 7th-floor office space, so why does it mention the B1 floor?"

"Only rule 6 is informal and scary... no, wait, there's also an item that says there is no rule 6? What is this?"

"There are a lot of strange phrases..."

The contents were indeed somewhat creepy.

Cha Eugene frowned at the rules but shrugged. "Then let's go out for now! It's better to see for ourselves."

"Mmm."

"Should we? It seems like we've searched the whole office anyway."

After meticulously examining the 7th-floor plan, which they eventually found on Seon Ahyeon's desk, the members quickly reached an agreement.

"There's an elevator and stairs at the opposite end."

"OK."

Cha Eugene proactively took the lead. He loosened his tie completely, hung it to the side, and approached the door.

"Are you mentally prepared?"

"Mhmm."

The office door slid open slightly.

SLIDE.

The space-efficient sliding door moved smoothly. Cha Eugene stuck his head out into the hallway and shouted, "I'm going to go...!!"

He stopped before he could finish.

"......"

"Eugene...?"

In that moment, Cha Eugene grabbed Seon Ahyeon, who was about to peek his head out behind him, and pushed him back.

"Get back inside!!"

"Huh...?!"

"......"

I immediately looked past the open door, over Seon Ahyeon's shoulder.

FLICKER.

The hallway light blinked. At the far end of the hallway, in front of the emergency exit, there was something.

[♪♪♪♬♬♬♪♬♬♬]

It was an old, overturned music box. Its contents were visible, and a distorted, eerie melody flowed out. If that was all, it might have just been a scary prop.

But something was sticking out of that box. The upper torso of what could be a ballerina or a clown, with black, sticky arms, was crawling on the floor.

Slowly.

CREEEAK.

"AAAAAAH!"

"Aaargh!"

*What is that? It's... it's not big enough for a person to fit inside.*

It was the size of an upper torso. It definitely was not a mascot costume. How on earth was something like that moving? The guys around me seemed to realize this instinctively. They could not look away and started shouting in genuine confusion.

[Should I punch it? Damn, it doesn't look like it'll work, but I'll try, so just wait!]

"Eugene forgot his Korean!"

"Just close the door!"

In that moment, among the guys trying to close the door, I saw Ryu Chungwoo's gaze turn toward the opposite end of the hallway.

Another light source glowed there, one that was not the office. It was a black stand next to the office entrance.

DING.

A glowing white rectangle sat on top of it.

"Wait, the left..."

I reached out my hand before Ryu Chungwoo could finish his sentence. As I gripped the glowing rectangle, Cha Eugene swept me and everyone else back with his arm.

"The door!"

Kim Raebin shut the door with perfect timing.

THUD.

I fell back into a sitting position and looked down at what I had grabbed. It was a tablet PC.

"Hoo."

"Wait, hold on... Assistant Manager Moondae, that's the thing from the guidelines, right?"

"Yes."

It seemed so.

[3. Deliveries can be registered on the tablet PC at the entrance of each office, and by-products can be registered on the tablet PC in front of the B1 warehouse.]

Even so, the music box slowly, sluggishly crawled across the hallway, inching toward the office. The sound was audible.

[♪♪♪♬♬♬♪♬♬♬]

The flickering hallway light appeared and disappeared through the crack in the door.

*Damn it. This is creepier than I thought. Let's move quickly.*

I immediately lowered my gaze to the tablet PC. A message was displayed there.

[Delivery Item Transport Plan]

[Would you like to make a delivery?]

"What are we supposed to be delivering?"

"W-Will it be okay... if we just press it?"

"I think it was written in the work guidelines earlier..."

"Press it!"

Words burst out from several people at once.

I clenched my teeth.

*There must be a progression to this. Calm down. This is a variety show, a variety show. So... right. That means even if we press it, nothing too terrible will happen.*

"I'm pressing it."

"Got it!"

After glancing at the other guys, I immediately moved my finger.

[Would you like to make a delivery?]

[Yes]

And so, I pressed the button.

[Delivery in progress]

TICK, TOCK, TICK, TOCK.

"Is it not done yet?"

"Wait."

A few seconds of spine-chilling, anxious waiting passed. A message appeared on the screen.

[Delivery Complete]

In that instant, the hallway light that had been flickering through the door crack returned to normal. The music box sound also disappeared.

[-]

"......"

The situation was over.

"Hoo."

"Hah..."

The members let out long breaths. They all slumped onto the office floor, not caring about their suits.

"Is it over?"

"It seems so..."

As if coming to his senses, Bae Sejin looked at the tablet PC in my hand and muttered, "So then... do we just have to find these pads while playing tag?"

"Good grief."

"Aigoo, this special duty is really something else."

The tension eased a little.

*Hoo. This is intense.*

I was about to put down the tablet PC and take a breather. Just then, an artificial voice flowed out from it.

[Commencing requisition of employee for delivery.]

The office lights went out.

"......"

WIIIIING-!

A loud siren blared, and a red light flashed.

*What is this now?*

"Is everyone okay?!"

Lee Sejin's shout from beside me was swallowed by the siren.

Flicker. Flicker.

A few disastrous seconds passed like an emergency alarm.

"Moondae, be care...!"

Then, once again, it suddenly became quiet.

FLICK.

The office lights came back on.

"Hoo."

I immediately turned my head. The office looked the same as before.

"Is everyone safe?"

"What was that just now?"

The guys checking on each other did not seem any different either. They were still in their suits.

However...

"Raebin...?"

One person was missing. The one who had been standing by the door.

"Where did Kim Raebin go?"

"......"

After looking around, I quietly opened my mouth.

"He's gone."

DING-DONG, DING-DONG!

An announcement began to play from the office speakers.

[Intern Kim Raebin. Your work for today has ended.]

[Please check the by-product.]

CLICK.

One of the locked drawers in the office opened automatically, revealing a black box inside.

"......"

I reflexively reached out and opened the box. Inside was a small music box. Draped over it was Kim Raebin's employee ID card.

[KIS Company]

Intern Kim Raebin / Music Box

[Keep it Safe]

***

"It gets even more interesting from there, doesn't it?"

Park Moondae did not deny it and simply nodded.
