# Chapter 475

We couldn't work with the current Cha Eugene.

This applied to everything. It included personal schedules, short events, and even the group reality show we had barely managed to secure.

"It can't be helped."

"Mmm."

*Is this really it? All based on my ten-minute attempt on the veranda? I'm glad the discussion was quick, but we're planning a whole month's schedule based on this.*

"Ah, if Moondae says it won't work, then that's that."

"......"

"......"

*If that's the case.*

"Then let's announce it's a health issue around next week and pull Eugene from the schedule entirely."

"Yep, yep. If we delay the reality show's broadcast a bit, we can film the real Eugene separately when he comes back and edit his parts in..."

"Right. I'll call the PD first thing tomorrow morning about it."

Now that we had a stake in the company, it felt a little less unfair that we had to handle all these detailed matters ourselves.

After we quickly sorted things out, I spoke to Bae Sejin, who was holding his script. I figured I should bring it up.

"Hyung."

"What? What is it?"

"Should we switch rooms?"

"...What?"

"You seem like the type who's uncomfortable around strangers. Your schedule's been getting busier, so it'd be better if you could at least be comfortable in your room."

*That's half the truth. Honestly, I'm more worried STier Cha Eugene will be uncomfortable with you, and the room situation will turn sour.*

However, Bae Sejin, who I expected to jump at the offer, just scoffed.

"It's fine. I'm not that sensitive."

"......"

*His self-awareness is... well, whatever.*

*He's so busy with filming that he's mostly just sleeping anyway.*

*There's a very high probability they'll both just keep their mouths shut and quietly do their own thing.*This was a better option than creating variables by insisting on it.

*As long as he doesn't develop any stomach problems, it should be fine.*

"Yes."

I decided to wait and see. I also left my door open, just in case.

However, I hadn't considered that Bae Sejin had grown more confident and motivated in his role as the eldest.

So, when a loud shout came from that room in the middle of the night two days later, I reflexively rushed out.

"...!!"

*Fuck.*

"...Moondae, just now,"

"......"

I guessed what had happened.

*The worst-case scenario happened. Damn it.*

*STier Cha Eugene must have finally lost his temper and snapped, or maybe he got confused in his sleep!*

*That bastard said he knew he was a different person and would respect boundaries... Don't tell me it was all just talk...*

But when all the members rushed out and peered into the room, the shout was from Bae Sejin. He was yelling at Cha Eugene.

"Don't talk like that!"

"......"

"Wh-What's going on?"

What in the world was happening?

~~~

A few days ago.

Bae Sejin took a short, deep breath.*Hoo.*He stood in front of his room.

He had confidently told Park Moondae it was 'fine' earlier, but in truth, he was a little nervous. Sharing a space with someone he wasn't close to was still an ordeal for him.

The same was true for this Cha Eugene, who had become like a different person. He was a stranger.

*Still... I've grown, too.*

He was no longer a child who stormed off or blindly avoided uncomfortable situations. He could face the situation!

He had already been roommates with Cha Eugene for over a year. Since it was the same body, he figured he could get by on inertia.

*...Alright.*

He opened the door with determination.

THUD.

"......"

But Cha Eugene didn't look back. He didn't even spare Bae Sejin a glance. He just lay on his bed, tossing a football up and catching it.

*Ugh.*

Bae Sejin nodded once toward Cha Eugene, immediately regretting it, then lay on his own bed.

*Hoo...*

He opened his script, but his mind was restless. He wasn't sure if he was offended, upset, or feeling a sense of duty.

However, he couldn't help but feel a little curious.

*How... was I living?*

He meant the version of himself from this Cha Eugene's world, the one who went through ❬Idol Inc.❭ without 'Park Moondae'.

He could guess. Judging by this Cha Eugene's unfriendly attitude, that Bae Sejin probably hadn't debuted.

Since he had only been uncooperative during ❬Idol Inc.❭, Cha Eugene likely only remembered him by that first impression. Yes, that was highly likely.

*In any case, I must have gained some recognition, so maybe I'm acting...*

But if he was still with the agency 'Dream K', he was unlikely to be doing very well.

"......"

He knew it was a useless thought. It wasn't his current reality.

*It's just speculation in the first place...*

It was the kind of speculation that drained his mental energy, but diving deep into such thoughts was also one of Bae Sejin's characteristics.

*...Stop it!*

But now he had also learned how to challenge himself!

He took another deep breath and stopped the useless thoughts. Instead, he read through his script once and then went to bed.

After managing his anxiety and completing his schedule, he tried again late the next night. He had prepared a secret weapon.

"...Want some?"

Bae Sejin took a paper bag from a box by his bed and offered it to Cha Eugene.

It was homemade candy, the snack Cha Eugene usually loved most from Bae Sejin. His mother bought them from an acquaintance's shop and sent them to him. Cha Eugene used to covet them, saying they had the 'taste of home'...

"Oh. Nope."

"......"

He used to covet them.

[Hyung, buy a lot of these! I like them.]

[...I'll ask my mom.]

[Oh!!]

*...You ate the brownie, though!*

Bae Sejin suddenly missed his loud, talkative roommate immensely. Holding back his emotions, he steadfastly continued.

"Ahem, right. Um, and under your bed are the snacks you used to keep and your hobby stuff. If you're bored... you can look for them and use them."

"I got it."

He agreed, but the current Cha Eugene didn't move from the bed. He didn't even pretend to look. It was a gentle dismissal.

"......"

Until now, Bae Sejin hadn't been sure. They had only interacted as a group in a hectic rush, but now, talking one-on-one, the perceptive Bae Sejin noticed it immediately.

*...What's with this guy?*

Contempt. Hostility. A condescending attitude.

Bae Sejin, who was sensitive to such things, instantly picked up on STier Cha Eugene's subtle non-verbal cues. Even if Cha Eugene wasn't trying to show it, Bae Sejin's sensitive nature allowed him to read it.

"......"

Bae Sejin didn't get angry. He turned back to his script, but his heart pounded.

*What is it?*

*Why... is he acting like that toward me? Could it be that in his timeline, I failed to debut in ❬Idol Inc.❭ and... became some absurdly pathetic person?*

*Come to think of it, he's only like that with me.*

*St-Stop it.*

*This feels like a victim complex. I don't want to be someone who's swayed by one person's attitude anymore!*

Bae Sejin composed himself and shifted his gaze back to the script. It was then that Cha Eugene spoke.

"Are you close with the me from here?"

"......"

Cha Eugene's tone wasn't friendly. He spoke almost to himself, a thoughtless remark that sounded close to disbelief.

Bae Sejin answered earnestly.

"We're in the same group... ahem, and we're also roommates."

"Umm, isn't this a new dorm?"

He implied that since they had just become roommates, they couldn't have shared a room for more than a few days.

However, Bae Sejin shook his head.

"We just moved, but we were roommates for over a year before this. For now, we're keeping the same roommate pairings as the old dorm."

"......"

Cha Eugene fell silent, but something flashed in Bae Sejin's mind.

*Huh?*

The next part of the conversation felt like it was being typed out in his head, fitting together perfectly like a script.

*In that case... Wait, could this be an opportunity?*

"W-Well, they're scheduled to change soon."

Bae Sejin swallowed and spoke again.

"We'll probably switch roommates when we start filming the reality show."

Cha Eugene glanced his way, as if asking, 'So?'

Bae Sejin spoke as gently as possible, mimicking Ryu Chungwoo's speaking style as if he were acting.

"So, I was wondering, if there's a room you'd like to use, would you be willing to participate in that one shoot?"

"......"

"When we film, we'll try our best to make sure you get the room you want..."

"I don't care. Any room, with anyone."

*Why is this bastard so good at Korean...!*

Bae Sejin wanted to squeeze his eyes shut, but the real topic was next.

"...Right, but! It'll be inconvenient, won't it? You'll have to stay somewhere else during filming, and you won't be able to go out much."

Since we would tell the public that Cha Eugene was sick, he would have to stay in a hospital or hotel the whole time.

*It would be incredibly suffocating. It would be better for him to just say he has a leg injury, skip the stage performances, and at least appear in the group reality show...*

*Wouldn't that be better for this Cha Eugene too?*

Bae Sejin was about to calmly explain this when a laugh echoed.

It was a scoff.

"Why should I do that?"

...What?

"Leaving the dorm is fine."

Cha Eugene looked at Bae Sejin with a blank expression, as if he had never laughed.

"But where I live during that time is up to me. It's my body, and I'll do what I want. OK?"

"......"

Bae Sejin couldn't answer for a moment, but not because he had nothing to say.

*This is...*

Until now, Bae Sejin had tried his best to understand this Cha Eugene's perspective. How confusing and difficult must it be to suddenly be dropped into an unfamiliar environment with different memories, with no one else in the same boat?

Considering his own behavior during ❬Idol Inc.❭, this Cha Eugene was a gentleman. He truly thought so.

*But,*

Even so, to casually declare that he would do whatever he wanted, even if it could cause huge trouble for the 'TeSTAR Cha Eugene' who originally used this body...

*That's just violence.*

Just because I'm cold doesn't mean I have the right to take someone else's clothes. That wasn't individualism. It was selfishness.

That's what he thought.

"No."

Bae Sejin's expression hardened.

"What are you talking about."
