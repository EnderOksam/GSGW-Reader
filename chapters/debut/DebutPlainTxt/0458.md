# Chapter 458

*Gold... No, it seems Kwon Heeseung has a fragment of the System.*

[!!]

[Aaaaaah, the System must have reacted to that! Because Heeseung has a fragment!]

Exactly.

*I'm not sure, but I might have to go through with this to get it from him.*

Reacting meant it was showing activity. If I used it, a method might present itself.

It was a dangerous move for someone without a Status Window to handle the penalty for a sudden mission failure. It was like banging his head against the ground.

And as for just letting Gold 2 get screwed over... I did owe him enough to offer him a wish.

*...And one more thing.*

If one of the remaining System fragments was with Kwon Heeseung, the pattern became pretty obvious. There were four fragments in total.

If the ones revealed so far were me, Keundal, and Kwon Heeseung, then the next one was, with a high probability...

*...It must be Cheongryeo.*

The lineup was full of people experienced with this damn System's bullshit.

*If it comes to it, should I ask him to state a wish or something?*

I would have to phrase it carefully. For now, I shook my head and returned to the more pressing topic.

So, what should I do with this System?

If it was just me unilaterally taking on the burden of Gold 2's mission failure, then I was sorry, but that would be too much of a loss. Wish or no wish, I probably would have realistically cut my losses.

"To be honest."

[...To be honest?]

I rubbed my chin.

"I think I can profit from this."

[?!]

Think about it. From drawing traits and leveling up to checking my Status Window, I had used the System well, sucking it dry to the bone.

The problem was that I was suddenly told to become an idol in someone else's body and that I would die if I failed.

*In reality, this is a cheat key.*

[Hyung?]

But look at the situation now. Growing a company aligned perfectly with my goals.

A failure penalty? If I couldn't grow the company properly, it would fail anyway. It was all the same.

Based on my experience, mission penalties were weaker than those for status abnormalities. It probably wouldn't be on the level of 'die if you can't debut'.

And...

"No matter how I think about it, I don't see myself failing."

One little mission was no big deal.

[Huuuh.]

Hmm, good. I decided.

I was already wondering how I would raise this new company's level to match TeSTAR's class, and it seemed like it would be a struggle.

I nodded. Considering everything, there was no need to refuse a shortcut.

Still, I decided to check one last time. I asked Keundal.

*Does this look dangerous to you?*

[...The item itself doesn't feel threatening, but this whole situation is just scary! It's so scary!]

[It feels like you're sacrificing yourself for Heeseung?!]

If that was all, then it was fine. It was just his imagination.

[!?]

*So the System is my item now?*

Good. I went through hell escaping a collapsing building and having my body swapped to collect these fragments. I would make good use of them.

I immediately brought up the pop-up again.

['Item: ■■■ (ver. Beta)'

Would you like to cancel its use?]

*No.*

[Use of 'Item: ■■■ (ver. Beta)' confirmed!]

['■■■' has become For Business Use!]

The translucent hologram began to take shape. A border appeared and colors filled in. What was completed looked quite a bit like a plausible mobile game window.

A pop-up flashed at the top at the end of the evolution-like scene.

[Mission Generated (NEW!)]

Mission: Achieve B-Rank for ❬Stars Orbit Entertainment❭

[Let's raise the target's rank to retrieve the 'Fragment of ■■■'.]

Hmm?

[Help: As the rank of ❬Stars Orbit Entertainment❭ increases, the owner's dominance grows stronger.]

[Try to dominate the 'Fragment of ■■■' with your strong dominance!]

Oh.

So, since I used the System on the company, the more I grew the company, the greater my dominance over the System became. That was how I could acquire the other fragments.

Kwon Heeseung's 'wish' and my objective had mixed together well. Not bad.

However...

[Time Remaining: D-100]

[Upon failure, ownership of ■■■ will be revoked.]

"......"

This time limit was ridiculously shameless. The penalty for failure was also vaguely explained, which I did not like. I did not know exactly what would happen.

[Then!! Try to cancel it now, Hyung!]

Well, I decided to give it a try.

[※Cancellation is not possible during a mission!]

*So it says.*

[*Sob*...]

[Company Name: Stars Orbit]

Public Recognition: C+

['Function: Acting' Unlocked!]

['Function: Variety' Unlocked!]

*It's going wild.*

*Acting and variety can wait.*

After all, there was only one urgent matter right now.

*Official album promotions.*

For a standard group promotion period, I had to push for what everyone would universally expect.

*I have to land the first hit properly.*

We needed to make a quick and precise comeback to powerfully declare our survival.

If I were to prioritize for the sake of growing the company... the group with the most impact had to make its mark first. Who would that be?

*TeSTAR, of course.*

Starting with the A-team.

[Album]

Manpower: (-)

Capital: (-)

Period: (-)

*Start Production ← Click!

[Help: The higher the star-rating you invest, the higher the chance of good effects occurring during production!]

*Does it automatically make an album for you or something?*

That would be a total scam, so it was probably not that, but even if it just provided assistance, it would be a huge advantage.

"...!"

[Kim Najin - AR Team]

[Kim Seowon - AR Team]

[Na Sejung - AR Team]

*Then again, if they were truly outstanding, they'd probably have formed their own team and be working independently.*

They would make more money that way.

[Kim Raebin - TeSTAR]

*After all, even if I make a test album, I can just choose not to release it.*

"...?"

Reason: Company dominance level insufficient

Are you crazy?

It was then.

[Hyung-nim!]

[Aigoo, that wish... should I cancel it?ㅠㅠ Was I being too presumptuous!?]

Too late.

"Is it resolved?"

"More or less."

A moment ago, in the meeting room, I had shouted "Cancel" at thin air, but when the actual cancellation option appeared, I had slumped back into my seat with a deflated expression.

[...?]

*I thought I bought a rapid company growth package, but an even more vicious inducement to spend real money was waiting for me.*That was how I could summarize it.

Yeah.

Don't forget that you and I get paid about the same amount.

"Then... is the company in a situation where it needs that much more money?"

"That's what I was asking."

"Ah! R-Right."

Cha Eugene shot his hand up.

"That's what a scammer says!"

No. That was not it.

"It's not a scammer. I'm saying I would be investing in the company."

*That's right.*

I was not sinking 10 billion won into the System. I was investing it in the company.

*I could make it spit back out as much as I invested.*

If the album did well, and if we could just tour properly, I could even grow it into something more.

My head cleared.

"...Anyway, I was just debating whether or not to invest in our company, so you don't have to worry."

"Because it seems like I could multiply it several times over if we do well."

"OK!"

...But putting in 10 billion won would mean scraping together almost all the money I had left.

*That's a bit much.*

It seemed he was so lost in thought that he did not hear the part about investing. He was probably the richest one here, but for some reason, taking money from him would make me feel like the biggest freeloader.

*Still... it's reassuring.*

Even in the worst-case scenario, I would be able to eat.

In the end, I made my choice.

*Let's do it.*
