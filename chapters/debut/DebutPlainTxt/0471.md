# Chapter 471

The meeting with Spacer happened the next day. It was quick and decisive.

To be precise, it was a meeting in a conference room with Kwon Heeseung, who represented Spacer.

"Ooh, Heeseung~ It's been a while!"

"Aigoo, Hyung!"

I glanced at the fuss Keun Sejin and Kwon Heeseung were making and sat down.

A guy then copied me, pulling out the chair next to mine and sitting down smoothly. It was Cha Eugene.

He crossed his arms and whispered, "Should we get lunch after this?"

*No, why did you even follow me?*

"If you were hungry, you should've just stayed at the dorm."

"I'm bored. I don't have any schedules."

"Hmm."

*I guess that's true.*

Cha Eugene's solo schedule had ended unusually quickly. He had filmed it all at once over four days and three nights.

How was that possible? The show he chose was...

[Camping Diary: Our Story Grows.]

...this one.

It was a public broadcast variety show where they gathered troubled kids with sob stories to go camping. A celebrity would appear as a camp counselor to listen to their problems and grow mentally alongside them.

As you might have guessed, the program got this kind of reaction when it first launched.

[We don't care about your stories]

[Instead of this, give school violence victims more opportunities, you idiots. Are you seriously trying to get us to empathize with and re-evaluate perpetrators?]

[A celebrity isn't an expert, what's with this 'healing counselor' bullshit? Hahaha. I'm just... speechless.]

[They're gonna force out fake tears again. It's already nauseating.]

[A celebrity who caused trouble could come on as a counselor too, sell their story, and clean up their image, right? Wow, double laundry, haha.]

Whoever planned this, show yourself. It was thoroughly criticized as an outdated idea, just as expected from a public broadcast network.

Of course, once it actually aired, it turned out to be just a lukewarm, generic variety show. The hype died down and it settled in.

[They call them 'problem kids', but they don't seem that bad? They're just kids who need counseling, not like bullies or anything.]

[If you dig into it, half their stories are probably fake. A shopping mall owner and an aspiring actor have already been revealed, haha.]

└Sigh

It was that kind of show. The participants were only civilians on the surface, telling exaggerated stories for self-promotion.

But then, word got out that Cha Eugene was going to appear on a show like this.

[What's he trying to do?]

[Just go on Water Bomb instead.]

[Cha Goyoung is nice, but he's a Cali-guy, an eternal optimist. Isn't he just going to get flamed for being clueless?]

Fans and the public alike were confused, wondering what on earth he would say.

However, on the day of the broadcast.

[Participant: Whoa, Cha Eugene!]

[Participant: Is that TeSTAR's Cha Eugene? Is it really you? Whoa, can you see this? Cha Eugene!]

[Cha Eugene: ...]

[Cha Eugene: You will not address this instructor in that manner.]

[????]

Cha Eugene appeared and used military-style speech. No one had expected it.

Of course, one might think using such a speech style awkwardly would only make him seem out of place and easy to look down on...

[Participant: Excuse me, do we really have to do things like rock climbing...?]

[Cha Eugene: No questions until it is over.]

[Participant: (Gasp)]

The guy had already experienced a military variety show in the System's virtual world, so he fluently used even idiomatic phrases.

[W-What is this?]

The viewers and participants didn't know this, so they could only be bewildered.

Seizing that opportunity, Cha Eugene began to masterfully handle the late-teenagers. After the initial impact, at the perfect moment, he would ease up, listen intently to their words, and take care of them, letting them grow attached to him.

[Why is his speech style like a drill instructor's, but his attitude is like an American camp counselor's?]

[Cha Eugene is hilarious and so heartwarming...]

[Is this the flavor of someone with American camp experience?]

In any case, Cha Eugene seized control with his unique speech style. He navigated between strictness and kindness, comedy and seriousness, and even handled any potential image controversies.

He created a meme with the old-fashioned, public-broadcast-style caption 'The Danaka Instructor' and achieved incredible cost-effectiveness.

*Don't tell me he calculated all this.*

The fact that he probably hadn't made him even more amazing. I remembered what he had said when he chose this.

[I have experience now.]

"......"

Now, that same guy sat next to me, nodding with a sullen look on his face.

"And I have the right to hear this conversation directly. I'm a member of this team."

*Alright.*

*He has good instincts. He might offer a good opinion with his quick judgment. I decided to consider Cha Eugene's input.*

Only then did Keun Sejin and Kwon Heeseung finish their greetings and sit down cheerfully in the conference room chairs.

Kwon Heeseung's eyes sparkled.

"Wow, does this mean Moondae is going to produce for our group, Spacer? Whoa!"

"Yes. That's what I called you here to talk about."

"But we need to confirm something first."

"...Yes?"

I clasped my hands together.

"Can you trust me?"

"...!"

"We're in the same agency, but you know we're ultimately competitors. Aren't you suspicious that I wouldn't present a plan for Spacer that only leads to surefire success?"

"......"

Gold 2, Kwon Heeseung, shut his mouth.

*As expected.*

*He must have his doubts, unless he's an idiot.*

The three of us from TeSTAR waited patiently for his answer without rushing him.

After a short silence, he laughed awkwardly.

"Uh, no?"

"...!"

"But isn't everyone like that, Hyung?"

Kwon Heeseung let out a hollow laugh. He seemed somewhat resigned.

"When we were at T1... well, the staff must've planned everything to benefit the company, right? It's not like we ever got a plan that was only good for us in the first place..."

He muttered, then soon gave a thumbs-up.

"Isn't that just how society works? We all do things hoping for mutual benefit!"

"...Is that so."

"That's right!"

*Indeed.*

*A realistic perspective, just as you'd expect from a guy who made money from stocks as soon as he returned to the past.*

"And seeing you say this, Hyung, I actually feel better about it. Are you going to tell me everything? Like what parts of this plan are risky for us and good for you?"

"I have to."

*It's cleaner to disclose everything and have him agree. If I keep my mouth shut, unexpected problems could arise later.*

*I told him everything, and he's the one making the choice. He can't complain later.*

*It's like an insurance policy clause, though.*

I thought this with a hint of bitterness.

"Wow."

"...?"

*Why are you sighing in admiration?*

"Hoo... Hyung, that's a top one percent conscience right there. You should be proud!"

"......"

"Wow, Hyung, are you secretly a saint while pretending you're not?"

"Right? Moondae's a bit like that, isn't he?"

*I'm not.*

"Exactly. You don't even really gain anything from this, Hyung, but you're helping our group and even worrying about things like that..."

*That's because this company is my System... no, let's just stop.*

I pressed my temples and opened my mouth.

"...Anyway, let's start the discussion."

"Keuh, yes!"

Kwon Heeseung nodded. His face was a mixture of anticipation and tension, wondering what kind of strategy would be revealed.

Keun Sejin smiled friendly and spoke.

"Heeseung~ You know that new group from Wonder Hall that just debuted, right? Aether!"

"Yes, yes."

Kwon Heeseung knew about the controversy. Speculations flashed across his face for a moment, but the next words wiped them clean.

"Those guys are going to be a big help to Spacer's promotion!"

"......"

*To be precise, it means I'll make them help.*

***

A few dozen minutes later.

"......"

Kwon Heeseung sat dazedly in the conference room, his eyes unfocused, as if a storm had hit him head-on.

The shock seemed severe.

"It's okay to think it over carefully before giving me an answer."

"No... it's just," Kwon Heeseung swallowed hard.

"Will this even work?!"

"I wouldn't have suggested it if it wouldn't work."

"R-Right... If you say so, Hyung, it will."

*To that extent?*

This time, Kwon Heeseung stared down at the conference table with a serious expression.

I thought he was about to start counting the patterns on the table.

BZZZ...

"Ah, I need to take this call for a moment."

"......"

Kwon Heeseung's smartphone rang. He got up, closed the door, and left the conference room.

Click.

"......"

After a brief silence, I spoke.

"He called himself, didn't he?"

"No. I think it was a real call."

"Hmm."

*I thought he was going to consult with his members right away.*

I had intended to call more Spacer members to this meeting, but Kwon Heeseung surprisingly insisted on coming alone.

"Is that person their Leader?"

"Spacer? They don't have a leader system, but Heeseung was first place and is the center, so he has a big role."

"Ooh."

*Why are you looking at me? You're the center.*

Anyway, I tapped the conference room table for a moment before getting up at the right time.

*He should be about done with his call by now.*

"I'm going to the restroom for a bit, too."

"......"

I slipped out of the conference room to speak privately with Kwon Heeseung.

Click.

The moment I closed the door, I heard Kwon Heeseung's voice echoing softly from the hallway. He was still on the phone.

"Yeah, yeah, hey! Ah, I'm telling you, our new song is really good. Really, trust me! Hyung, you're really talented at composing, why are you worrying!"

"......"

"If we just get good promotions, we'll make it onto the charts. Our company is good now, so we'll do even better. ...Yeah, yeah. Good luck with your work."

He spoke in a placating tone and ended the call.

After a short sigh... his eyes met mine.

"Uh, um. Hyung," he said, looking flustered.

*...Hoo.*

"Sorry. I didn't mean to listen."

"I figured you didn't... ahem."

Kwon Heeseung seemed embarrassed and uncharacteristically dragged out his words.

*Damn it.*

I held back a sigh and spoke again.

"...Your team's atmosphere seems good."

"It is."

Kwon Heeseung leaned against the wall and sighed.

"But honestly... it's not like everyone is as hardworking as you guys, Hyungs. I think our season of ❬Idol Inc.❭ was a really unique one."

"......"

He continued, perhaps deciding to get it all out since I'd overheard him, or maybe it was just unintentional.

"Um, so I've sometimes imagined what it would've been like if I had debuted back then... I made it to the finale, after all."

*Ah.*

I realized what he was remembering.

*It wasn't just the finale as a whole.*

*The announcement of the final member.*

*My first-place privilege.*

[As the first-place winner, contestant Park Moondae has the authority to select one person from the 14 contestants who made it to the final ranking announcement to debut as a team member!]

And I... had passed that choice to the viewers to eliminate as much backlash as possible.

[The contestant I have chosen is... the 7th place contestant chosen by the shareholders.]

In truth, among the remaining contestants not called for TeSTAR, the one I was closest to as a potential teammate was this guy.

Gold 2. Kwon Heeseung.

*There's no way he wasn't hopeful in that moment.*

*It must still come to his mind now, more than five years later.*

*Besides, it's not like TeSTAR was just moderately successful.*

His words, however, didn't drift into lament.

"But the more I imagine it, the more I realize. I think I still like my current team. Sigh... I've grown too attached."

"......"

"The hyung I was just on the phone with lacks a lot of confidence, but he's a really fun person, you know? He's good at making songs too... but our digital music scores haven't been that great lately."

Objectively, Spacer was a group that had settled in quite well. Their public recognition had naturally declined since the survival show ended, and their digital music scores had dropped. However, their album sales had steadily increased, and they had secured a stable international fanbase.

People tend to compare things relatively. Who was the easiest target for Spacer to be compared to?

*TeSTAR, of course.*

Their company wasn't even good at its job, so they must have heard such comparisons more than once or twice.

But the guy in front of me just shrugged his shoulders and said this.

"So please take good care of us, Hyung! I'll have to listen to the other members' opinions and get back to you... but for now, I've decided I definitely want to give it a try!"

"......"

"We have to prove that Spacer is a group with a bright future ahead!"

"...Alright."

"You only live once! No, wait, we... ahem, we got another chance, but anyway."

"......"

I laughed quietly. Then I looked away from the winking Kwon Heeseung.

The pop-up that had been there since we met was still visible.

[Reconfirming holder of 'Fragment of ■■■']

Absorb '■■■ Fragment' into 'For Business Use'?

※This will take some time.

"......"

*Well, I'm using him to get rid of that rookie group anyway.*

*I already planned to extract it to upgrade the company's System function. There's probably no need to tell him about mission failures just to make him feel indebted.*

*So... this isn't a bad thing either.*

I silently responded to the Status Window without a word.

*Yes.*

At that, the pop-up began to glow.

"...!"

"Shall we head back into the conference room then?"

It seemed invisible to Kwon Heeseung, who showed no reaction. A stream of light extended from the pop-up and wrapped around Kwon Heeseung once.

It smoothly returned to the pop-up.

['■■■ Fragment' retrieval complete!]

The content of the pop-up seemed to change. Then all the pop-ups vanished, replaced by a new one made of very simple lines.

['For Business Use' Updating]

*Is that it?*

*I thought I'd feel some reaction in my body, but apparently not.*

Instead, just as I was about to look closer at the pop-up.

BAM!

"...!!"

"W-What was that?"

The conference room door suddenly flew open.

Keun Sejin, his face deathly pale, had opened it.

He shouted.

"Call for help!"

"What?"

"Eugene collapsed. Call for help!"

"......"

I immediately looked inside the conference room. I could see Cha Eugene collapsed on the floor below his chair.

"......"

*Why?*

"Park Moondae!"

"......"

I moved immediately. Calling a company employee to get him to the emergency room was the fastest way.

However, even as I ran, I thought.

*What the fuck is going on?*

Beside me, the System window followed, not disappearing.

['For Business Use' Updating]
