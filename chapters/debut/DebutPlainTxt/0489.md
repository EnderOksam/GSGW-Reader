# Chapter 489

Two men with the same name, Bae Sejin and Keun Sejin, both claimed to be the lawyer. They were locked in a tense war of words.

"...Lee Sejin was quiet this whole time and only spoke up after the person to die was decided. Anyone can see that's strategic. He's doing it on purpose to confuse people!"

"Hey, hyung, it's the opposite. Isn't it more unnatural to go around talking about your job and abilities right from the start? Think about a game of Mafia, everyone~ Usually, an innocent citizen wouldn't do that, if only to be wary of the mafia."

Both of them had a point.

One of them had to be bluffing since there was no way they were both lawyers. Impressive. I was inwardly amazed.

"Oh... Moondae-hyung, who do you suspect?"

"Looking at the situation right now, it seems like both are possibilities."

One was an actor, and the other was good at variety shows.

*They could both naturally pretend to have a different job.*

If they put their minds to it, both of them could play a hardcore game with a trolling job. Even now, neither of them sounded logically awkward.

"...! That's sophistry. I just found someone suspicious early on and shared it with people I could trust!"

"But if you're the mafia, then that reason is also a lie, isn't it? Then it just becomes a baseless story, you see?"

*What a mess.*

I could almost see the other guys' pupils spinning. I inwardly clicked my tongue and raised my hand.

"First, I have a question for both of you."

"Huh?"

"Could you please explain the lawyer job? Liars usually have loopholes in their stories."

"That's right. If they explain in detail, one of them is bound to have a contradiction," Ryu Chungwoo nodded.

*That's right. No matter how prepared they are, if we keep digging, they'll have to improvise answers for things they didn't think of.*

Then their words wouldn't match up, and their logic would awkwardly crumble.

"Ah."

"And lawyer."

"That's right," Bae Sejin nodded seriously. "If your job isn't on this list, raise your hand."

There were no objections.

*No one's expression changed noticeably.*

It must mean their jobs were among those listed.

*In other words, there's a high probability that Bae Sejin's story is the truth.*

As if sensing this, Bae Sejin looked at Keun Sejin challengingly. However, Keun Sejin just gave a bitter smile.

"Hmm, so you know what jobs there are..."

Keun Sejin shrugged.

"But hearing your tone, hyung, it does seem like you really know?"

"......"

"You, are you admitting right now that you're not the lawyer and you lied..."

"No, no."

Keun Sejin shook his head. Then he said quite seriously, "It just seems like Bae Sejin-hyung has a different job with that ability. Only the part about being a lawyer is a lie."

"......"

So, what Keun Sejin was saying was...

*Bae Sejin found out those facts using the ability of a different job, but he's impersonating a lawyer?*

Certainly, that was a possible scenario!

"I mean, just... honestly, at a glance, it doesn't seem like an ability that has much to do with the lawyer profession."

"......"

Kim Raebin was horrified. At the same time, Bae Sejin flared up.

"A lawyer is supposed to know a lot about the defendant. What's so awkward about this!"

"No, an ability like that looks like something the culprit! The mastermind would have. It seems perfect for setting the stage, doesn't it~"

For a moment, a look that said 'That kind of makes sense' passed over the members' faces. Bae Sejin was now narrowing his eyes with a cold expression.

*Is he getting too into it, or is it an act?*

I shrugged, then turned to Keun Sejin and asked, "Then what's the real lawyer's ability?"

Keun Sejin grinned and said without hesitation, "It's the ability to designate one person and find out if they're innocent."

"...!!"

My god.
