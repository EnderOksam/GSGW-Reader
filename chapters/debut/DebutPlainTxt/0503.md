# Chapter 503

System Auto Re-Update?

My mind raced the moment I saw the pop-up's insane message.

Considering the incidents from the last Update, the first thing I had to do was...

*Stop!*

[An error may occur if the auto-Update is forcibly stopped.]

You son of a bitch.

I couldn't just stop it recklessly. I didn't know what kind of fucked-up situation it might cause.

I had to assess the situation before I moved.

*And... during the last Update, STier Cha Eugene woke up.*

In that case.

I shot up from my seat and started checking each room.

"M-Moondae?"

"Moondae Moondae, why are you running around like... huh?"

"Hyung, are you working out?"

Then I found them.

One had collapsed face-first onto his laptop while working. Another had passed out on his bed while reading an e-book, and one more leaned against a bookshelf with his eyes closed, having just looked at a program introduction at his desk.

Kim Raebin, Bae Sejin, Ryu Chungwoo.

Three of them.

I almost bit my tongue.

*Let's think about this logically.*

*Can three people suddenly fall asleep at the same time?*

*And only the ones who said they 'felt a little dizzy' during the last System Update?*

I wasn't the only one with that thought. When I briefed the awake members on the situation, their expressions changed.

"Wait, don't tell me this is..."

"Seriously? Is this happening again?"

I left the others to their frantic discussion about the emergency room and wracked my brain again.

*What's the cause of this mess?*

I replayed the pop-up's message.

*Detected owner dissatisfaction... Re-Update?*

A thought struck me.

*No way. Did it do this because it judged me to be in a 'state of dissatisfaction' for ignoring that damn Survey and not even touching the System?*

*Damn it.*

In other words, it sounded like it would keep adjusting the System until I used it.

*Does it think it's some kind of customer satisfaction service?*

It was even a little creepy.

I kept this company System thing alive because the VTIC Leader's life was on the line. He would have to face a mission failure without a Status Window, but there were far too many variables.

*...As if it has a will of its own.*

I remembered the System's human form, the one I could talk to before I shattered it. A slight chill ran down my spine.

However, we weren't completely unprepared for this.

*Of course, I've considered the possibility of another Update incident.*

Especially with the person who experienced it firsthand.

I met Cha Eugene's eyes. He gave a slight nod.

*There's already someone here with memories from his STier days.*

*Even if all three of them roll back to that time... damn it. As long as we get rid of that one public broadcast survival show, we can hold on more stably than before.*

*No, from their perspective, this might actually be better than being alone.*

When this symptom occurs in multiple people, it prevents one definite problem. A sense of isolation. The kind you get when you're suddenly thrown into an unfamiliar environment and start to doubt your own sanity. It was like that with Cha Eugene, and that's what makes it so hard on a person.

*But the side effect is...*

"...If this happens, almost half our members could forget what they were doing, right?"

"......"

I clenched my jaw, then released it. We couldn't even dream of group activities until they were back to normal.

"I'm sorry."

"...!"

Keun Sejin's expression changed instantly. He clapped my shoulder and smiled faintly.

"Moondae, why are you taking responsibility for something that's not your fault? We're just figuring things out~"

*No, it is my fault.*

But insisting on it to the guy trying to lighten the mood would only make things awkward. I decided not to say it just to feel better.

So instead, we made a plan.

"I'm against the hospital. [Waking up in a hospital bed with amnesia? It's too cliché. They might misunderstand and think we're messing with them.]"

"Still... just in case, I think we need to be ready to call."

"Right. Oh, and our top priority is to be ready to explain everything when the members wake up. Right?"

The conclusion was this. First, we would wait at the dorm. To make a quick explanation possible, we decided to gather evidence from photos, data, WeTube, and portal sites, and organize our story.

"If there's a problem, we can go to the emergency room right away... I've contacted our manager!"

"......"

Finally, we reached a dramatic agreement. If they didn't wake up within thirty minutes, we would try to wake them. If that failed, we'd go to the hospital.

We each took up a post, sitting and watching over one of the rooms. The dorm filled with a tension so thick it felt like we were breaking out in a cold sweat.

A few minutes passed.

Then there was a sign.

"...! H-Here...!"

Seon Ahyeon shouted.

"......"

We immediately ran to his post.

Seon Ahyeon was in Bae Sejin's private room.

Seon Ahyeon, already inside by the bed, turned his head and shouted, "I think... he's waking up...!"

Sure enough, Bae Sejin's facial muscles twitched.

*Hoo.*

A tense moment.

"Ugh..."

"......"

Bae Sejin sat up, frowning and pressing his eyes.

I braced myself.

*STier Bae Sejin?*

That guy was falsely accused of drug use early in his debut, kicked out of the group, and probably even went to prison.

*Idol or not, he's most likely completely fed up with the entertainment industry.*

At worst, he had a high chance of distrusting people. Besides that, his uncooperative attitude from ❬Idol Inc.❭ would have worsened, not improved.

I thought we should approach him more cautiously... but then it happened.

Bae Sejin opened his mouth blankly.

*Wait... blankly?*

"...What are you guys doing?"

"......"

"......"

He was fine.

On the contrary, Bae Sejin seemed freaked out by the lanky guys staring down at him while he slept in his own room.

"Oh!! Hyung, are you in your right mind?!"

"Of course I am! Hey, are you all doing this to me just because I dozed off while reading...?"

He looked around, noticed the guys had even brought photo albums, and was bewildered.

"What's that?"

"...Well."

*I get it. Looks like... it's not him.*

After a quick summary of the situation, Bae Sejin soon joined the flow of things.

He came into the hallway and muttered with a troubled expression, looking between Kim Raebin's and Ryu Chungwoo's rooms.

"...So, you're saying I could have ended up like Cha Eugene did last time?"

"Hyung, that's harsh."

"You're one to talk."

His attack power boosted after the 'are you in your right mind' crack, and Bae Sejin growled at Cha Eugene. Considering I thought things were fucked, it was a remarkably peaceful picture.

However, I took this opportunity to remember what I had to do.

I knew Bae Sejin hadn't regained his STier memories, but I didn't know what would happen with the other two.

*Which means... should I tell him?*

That he suffered quite a bit in his past life.

*It might be better to get the bad news over with so he can prepare himself mentally.*

In the end, I called out to Bae Sejin, who held his e-book reader with a troubled look.

"Hyung, wait a sec..."

"Huh?"

It was then.

"......"

A sound erupted from one of the outer rooms.

It was Kim Raebin's room.

"Raebin!"

Everyone, without exception, sprinted frantically down the hall and into the room.

This time, the situation we faced was...

"It seems to be a side effect of an energy drink! I sampled a new product from Company P that was released this month..."

"Hold on."

*Hey.*

"Raebin. How are you feeling right now?"

Kim Raebin blinked and answered coolly.

"...? I feel refreshed thanks to the nap...?"

"......"

Perfectly fine.

Two safe calls in a row. The members practically collapsed.

"Hoo."

"Ah, maybe it was just a coincidence that everyone fell asleep?"

"...We have all been overworking ourselves lately."

"Y-Yeah, we all had a late meeting yesterday, talking about the, uh, program appearance..."

"Not going to the hospital was the right answer. I'm smart."

Now everyone suspected it was just a coincidence.

*...Really.*

I asked the bewildered Kim Raebin once more.

"You haven't suddenly remembered anything you didn't before, have you?"

"Not particularly... Ah, is there a memory I'm supposed to recall?"

"No."

*This is actually better.*

I patted his head and left the room. The other guys seemed to stick to Kim Raebin and explain the situation.

*Then the only one left is Ryu Chungwoo.*

*He might be fine too, but let's not let our guard down. Even if his consciousness doesn't revert to his STier days, some other weird thing could happen.*

"Let's wait in front of here now."

"Mmm."

So, all the members ended up waiting in Ryu Chungwoo's room.

However...

"...It's too cramped."

"......"

With seven adults crammed into the room, it was quite awkward.

*Hmm.*

*Is it a bit much to just sit here quietly? Since the first two were fine, it's time to try something bolder.*

As a test, I got up and called out to Ryu Chungwoo, who leaned against the bookshelf.

I wondered if this would wake him if he was really just asleep.

"Hyung."

"......"

No response.

*I guess not.*

I let go of my expectations and changed how I called him. I addressed him specifically.

"Ryu Chungwoo."

In that instant.

"...!"

Ryu Chungwoo's eyes snapped open.

"Oh my god!"

"Did he always wake up like this?"

"This is like a cult movie right now."

Amidst the guys' comments, I inwardly held back a sigh of relief. If he opened his eyes just from his name being called, it was obvious.

*Was this guy just sleeping?*

Considering he was leaning against the bookshelf instead of collapsed, it was possible. If I had known, I wouldn't have waited so cautiously and would have just woken them all up sooner.

*I'll have to explain that we overreacted a bit...*

"...Who are you?"

"......"

"......"

"......"

I checked Ryu Chungwoo's face. His usual gentle expression was gone, replaced by faint bewilderment and wariness.

That look deepened the moment he saw who was behind me.

"Cha Eugene? And Kim Raebin..."

"......"

This guy didn't know Park Moondae.

But he specifically recognized and called out only those two...*I see.*

I forced my lips to move.

"By any chance, is your group's name STier?"

"Yes? Yes, it was, but what is this situation...?"

"......"

*We're fucked...*

***

Ryu Chungwoo had reverted to his STier days.

I handed him an unopened bottle of water, worried he might be wary. He just held it without drinking, a smile on his face. At least his expression showed some of his usual gentleness.

"But first, I'd like an explanation of the situation."

"......"

He must be wondering what kind of mess this was.

I quickly replayed my conversation with Cha Eugene in my head. Although Cha Eugene had forgotten many details from his STier days, he hadn't forgotten everything. This meant I was able to hear Cha Eugene's impression of Ryu Chungwoo from that time.

[Chungwoo was a little scary back then. We should all be careful.]

[Scary in what way?]

[[I mean, um... a classic case of burnout, and a particularly twisted kind of toughness?[]

*What is he even talking about.*

Cha Eugene himself couldn't give a good example, but I understood one thing clearly. We had to handle him delicately, like a bomb.

*Maybe even more so than Cha Eugene.*

Didn't I have the memory of messing up our relationship with STier Cha Eugene so badly that he even ran away from home?

Using that as a cautionary tale, we began explaining things to Ryu Chungwoo with a very serious, almost grim, attitude.

"...So, right now, we're a group called TeSTAR."

"Mm."

Our explanation included evidence with visual aids and internet access, followed by a succession of witnesses. I'm confident we didn't lack materials.

But even after the full explanation, STier Ryu Chungwoo was silent for a while. He just kept re-checking the provided materials over and over.

"......"

*Is this one a lost cause too?*

As expected, it would be hard for him to believe it right away. He wasn't the type to be interested in such unrealistic things, after all...

"I see."

"......"

*Hm?*

A clean affirmation came from nowhere.

Even Cha Eugene was flustered and asked again.

"Hyung, you believe us?"

"Mm... It's hard to believe, but it's also a hard situation to disbelieve. You can't just manipulate a portal site."

Ryu Chungwoo touched the back of his head, then gave a wry smile as he looked at the portal site screen several members had pulled up on their smartphones. He then turned his gaze to the albums I held out, looking back at me with an expression that was hard to tell if it was a smile or a frown.

"You couldn't fake all of this, either."

"......"

"So, about that, we have a schedule, right? It would be best if you could share that with me as soon as possible."

*Hm?*

"Yes?"

To Kim Raebin's question, Ryu Chungwoo replied nonchalantly.

"Mm, I think it would be better for me to handle it to some extent, rather than suddenly taking a break from group activities."

"...!"

Ryu Chungwoo looked at the unfamiliar members and smiled a little shyly.

"For now, I'm confident in my skills and perseverance."

Wait a minute.

*We're... not fucked?*

And the situation began to enter an unexpected phase.
