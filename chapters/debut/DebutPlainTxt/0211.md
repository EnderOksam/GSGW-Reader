Oh, my. Although it was within expectations, their side also seemed to have suffered a lot in their own way.

"Oh, no. You don't have to do that. I'm just asking because I'm curious how they're taking care of you."

"I just wanted to know if the management office is briefing you well on schedules or if the health of the members is being checked well... things like that."

"Ah, umm."

The hoobae's expression became contemplative. I couldn't tell if she was trying to recall or choosing her words, but her next words sounded quite sincere.

"I'm not sure, but he seems very busy and overwhelmed... I think it's because we have many members. I also feel sorry since he seems to be having a hard time."

As I thought.

Things weren't running well on their side either.

*Were there nine members?*

That was about 1.5 times TeStar's size, so they definitely needed more managers, but it turned out they were still running with just two people.

*It seems like the production team also had to go back and forth listening to the director's words and listening to this side's words.*

Since the director had a tighter grip on the other group, it seemed that the management's section chief was less likely to respond.

Hmm... I wonder how it could work out.

"Ah, right."

"Ah, you mean a team created exclusively for our own groups?"

"Of course, I won't say I personally heard it... I'm going to say I heard it from someone in the company. What do you think?"

The hoobae swallowed and soon answered with her eyes lit up.

"We would be very grateful."

It was a smart choice.

Since we already talked about the recent circumstances, she must have realized that if I intended to set a trap or scam her, I would not have bothered to ask her first.

"Um, then, I'll include you in this proposal."

"Yes! Thank you!"
