# Chapter 410

The competition program in which Wishze appeared this time,

, did not show a long story of preparing for the stage, although it provided a short amount of talk.

This was to maintain the concept of a battle between talented groups with the idea that 'everything is said on stage'.

So, on the contrary, Ryu Gunwoo was happy.

*If we show too much of the preparation process, the impact will decrease.*

If they showed the entire preparation process they wouldn't show rookie-like images since they were all experienced workers anyway.

He didn't want that kind of character.

*We have to give it a feeling that betrays expectations.*

Thanks to this, Wishze received the editing typical of a 'big rookie with one song', with very little preparation.

Every time I moved to the next page, the story of this group popped up.

This meant that the main focus was not to shock people with an insanely high difficulty level or to have a strong jaw-dropping force that made people shocked.

It was just a stage where people could feel good and we could show off our talent.
