# Chapter 410 - ⟨Battle of Voices⟩, II

The competition program in which Wishze appeared this time, ⟨Battle of Voices⟩, did not show the preparation process at length, although it gave the guests a little time to talk.

This was to maintain the concept of a battle between talented groups with the idea that 'everything is said on stage'.

So, on the contrary, Ryu Gunwoo was happy.

*If we show too much of the preparation process, the impact will decrease.*

If they showed the entire preparation process, it would only reveal how unlike rookies they were, since they were all seasoned professionals anyway.

He didn't want people to see them that way.

*We have to defy their expectations.*

Thanks to this, Wishze received the editing typical of a 'one-hit rookie group', with very little coverage of their preparations.
