# Chapter 551

Bae Sejin trembled while staring straight ahead. An odd, pixelated screen still floated there.

[Park Moondae : Deducing your identity!]

"......"

"I-It worked!"

*What does he mean, it worked?*

Bae Sejin stared blankly at Keundal. Dressed in a suit, Keundal had a bright expression. Bae Sejin's body turned on its own.

"What... do I have to do..."

"Whoa, whoa, whoa, keep contact, just like that, and slowly let go! I'm still connecting!"

*Ugh.*

Bae Sejin kept his hand on Keundal's shoulder. The people who had been haphazardly grabbing Keundal's limbs slowly and orderly backed away. However, everyone's eyes were fixed on the strange window floating before Bae Sejin.

*This is...*

"It's like... one of those 'raising a daughter' games, isn't it? That kind of feeling?"

"But why is Park Moondae in there!"

"Hyung, is there anyone here who isn't wondering the same thing?"

After a sudden, intense round of deductions, they concluded that Park Moondae had fallen into a strange virtual world, just like last time.

"That's... a relief, at least."

"......"

*...But why a Tamagotchi, of all things?*

"I did my best..."

Keundal recalled the situation when he had caught up beyond the passage.

*Suddenly, the information value 'Learning' came in.*

When he tried to exert his influence on Park Moondae as comfortably as possible in that situation, this format was created.

[Anyway, it's a relief we found him. Phew...]

Next to the video call screen showing a sighing Kwon Heeseung, Cha Eugene pointed an accusatory finger.

"Moondae's face is different!"

"......"

Keundal observed the screen. Then, with a dumbfounded look, he pointed at himself.

"...You're right. It's this face, isn't it?"

"It is!"

The atmosphere was worlds apart. Unlike Keundal, who was clearly a good-natured and handsome young man, the low-resolution Park Moondae on the screen looked like he had a terrible personality. No, he looked haggard. The exhaustion was palpable even in the pixels.

"...Wait a minute. Why does he look so shabby?!"

"Is there anything else we can check?"

"O-One moment."

Keundal did his best. Eventually, the group could see a summary of Moondae's status, formatted like a pixelated game. Bae Sejin, at the very front, quickly skimmed the information.

*Park Moondae...*

He had no money and a sick body. While his mental stats were solid...

"His stamina is garbage."

"......"

"This isn't a relief at all."

At Cha Eugene's factual statement, Keundal pressed his hands to his eyes. Still, indirectly confirming that Park Moondae was alive somewhere considerably eased the atmosphere.

"So now we just need to figure out how to bring him back, right?"

"First... let's let him know that we're here and trying to bring him back."

However, overly complex communication was nearly impossible. Furthermore, Keundal judged it was dangerous to try and communicate by injecting any more information into Park Moondae.

"For now... this seems to be the limit! Hyung seems really tired and in a tough state..."

"......"

Worse, they had to take turns 'connecting' like this, one person with a trait at a time. For some reason, Bae Sejin, chosen as the first one up, continued to stare at the screen with a sour expression.

Seon Ahyeon timidly raised his hand.

"Th-Then, first, is there... a way to help Moondae here get healthier?"

"I agree! His health recovering is the priority."

Keundal nodded vigorously.

"Yes! Once he recovers, I think we can try something more! Ummm..."

Bae Sejin listened intently, watching the speech bubble before him change again. He doubted his eyes.

[Park Moondae : Attempting to act.]

"...Acting?"

*Why are you doing that...?*

"...W-Well, looking at his job status and all, it seems like hyung has become something like an aspiring actor."

"......"

"......"

They all wondered what on earth had happened.

Cheongryeo, who had been watching silently, rested his chin on his hand.

*The flow of time is different.*

It was just like how time in a game differs from reality. The Park Moondae in the pop-up had already finished poring over a bundle of papers that looked like a script in just a few dozen seconds.

*About a day for every one or two minutes.*

Cheongryeo quickly finished his calculation. In the meantime, the Park Moondae on the screen had adopted a sour expression.

[Park Moondae : Agonizing over his acting...]

Ryu Chungwoo muttered.

"Moondae... is supposed to be bad at acting."

"......"

Park Moondae was surprisingly good at shamelessly imitating someone. However, the results were disastrous if you asked him to immerse himself in a character to trigger feelings or express complex, conflicted emotions.

"Hmm..."

Reflexively, everyone's attention turned to the person here who was best at acting.

"......"

Bae Sejin furrowed his brow and poked the Tamagotchi-like Park Moondae.

"Is there... anything I can do to help?"

At that moment, the screen changed.

[A talent connected to your trait!]

[Will you bestow a blessing?]

"......"

***

I stared blankly at the pop-up hologram. It looked like the chat window I had used to message Keundal. This was the first message I had seen since coming here. A signal from someone.

[A focused genius actor feels sorry for you.]

*...An actor?*

Keywords like 'focused' and 'genius' were attached as descriptions. Besides, the person standing before me was Bae Sejin from a few years ago.

*Could this be... the real Bae Sejin?*

*...Sejin?*

But there was no response. It seemed this was not a form of direct conversation. I waited for a new message to appear, but nothing showed up for several seconds.

"Excuse me."

"Ah, I'm sorry."

I shifted my focus and looked at 'this' Bae Sejin. He was the Bae Sejin from the past, before ❬Idol Inc.❭, who seemed to be here for rehabilitation near the end of his hiatus. The guy was not appearing himself. It seemed he had been invited literally for coaching.

*That guy's company and its affiliates are different, so how... Ah, it seems the director knows him.*

"If you give it a try... I'll watch and give you my feedback."

"...Mm."

I opened the music video script I received from a staff member. I did not even have any lines. I just had to walk along the beach with a lonely, sorrowful feeling, then throw a bouquet. A few lines instructed me to kick the seawater with my foot.

However, it was the last part.

[C : (Turns back with eyes that want to tell many stories) Backlit, the high-contrast sea sparkles.]

"......"

*What in the world are 'eyes that want to tell many stories' supposed to mean?*

It was dizzying in many ways, but I could not refuse, so I held back a sigh and collected my thoughts.

*Let's recall my experience filming music videos.*

I walked and turned around as naturally as possible.

"......"

"......"

*Say something, this is embarrassing.*

Bae Sejin, with dark circles under his eyes, looked down.

"...You've never learned to act before, have you."

"No."

"Then that's fine."

"......"

"Just do what you did earlier during the actual shoot."

And Bae Sejin, or rather, the guy who was Lee Sejin right now, lowered his gaze back to the script he was reading.

"......"

*Hmm.*

***

[You don't have any acting experience?]

Inside VTIC's moving van, Cheongryeo closed and opened his eyes at the 'Help' that had popped up. This 'Help' popped up 'whenever it wanted' and no longer tried to prove its usefulness at every turn. However, it did want to hear about Cheongryeo's daily life.

[Nothing pissed you off?]

On days when the conversation grew long, it would sometimes offer words of comfort or advice. Sometimes, it would ask questions like this. Now, instead of asking, 'Why are you asking that kind of question?', Cheongryeo readily answered.

"Because it didn't affect the group's success."

He tried but gave up on it. Park Moondae interpreted it at once.

[...Right. Usually, one or two members in an idol group get into acting. I was wondering why VTIC didn't have any.]

Cheongryeo almost laughed.

"Typically... there are positions assigned to specific members from the moment a group is formed."

He continued breezily.

"We were no exception."

It meant that a member in VTIC had indeed taken acting lessons.

[Who was it.]

"Shino."

"......"

*Is this something to be surprised about?*

Cheongryeo shifted his gaze slightly, aware of Shino sitting diagonally behind him. A slightly bewildered pop-up appeared.

[He doesn't seem like an acting major.]

"Well. That's because appearance is a more important criterion than current acting ability."

When an idol agency selects a member for acting, they prefer to choose the right appearance and then develop their skills through training. It was not just about being handsome. A neat impression, good bone structure, and a good presence on camera were the usual selection criteria.

The peculiar thing was that, unlike in idol evaluations, a plainer type was rated better than an overly intense one.

"The baseline is different from the stage."

The 'Help' pop-up disappeared for a moment, as if lost in thought.

[Right. You have to look more natural and casual than when you're performing. It's normal for the standards to be different.]

Cheongryeo watched with considerable interest as 'Help' repeated those words, as if organizing its thoughts.

*What idea is it coming up with?*

But instead of continuing, 'Help' changed the subject as if to clear the air.

[Still, isn't acting ability as important as appearance? You could get flamed for bad acting.]

"That won't happen."

[...?]

Cheongryeo rested his arm on the windowsill.

"Because he won't have any acting schedules until contract renewal anyway."

[......]

It was because it would disperse the hype.

[If you're human, be good to that guy.]

Cheongryeo smiled faintly. Exchanging these kinds of remarks was not such a bad feeling.

*Hmm.*

Now that the conversations were no longer regular, Cheongryeo could maintain a strange distance from 'Help'. If he did not want to delete it right away, this method was better.

"We've arrived."

"Okay~!"

The car stopped, and the members of VTIC began to get up. 'Help' asked a question.

[Going to a recording?]

"......"

But there was no 'have a good time' farewell. Cheongryeo was aware of this but walked on without hesitation.

***

*Naturally,' he said.*

After my part-time job, I sat in my old studio apartment and reviewed the script again. While doing so, I checked the pop-up from time to time.

[A focused genius actor is concerned about your health.]

But there were no other changes. Sometimes the message would change to something like 'A focused genius actor is agonizing,' but there was still no feeling of real-time communication.

*I guess my thoughts and words aren't being transmitted after all.*

...It left a slightly bitter taste in my mouth.

Still, the surprising thing was that the pop-up remained, albeit faintly, even when I accessed the System. It was a good thing I tried it with a bit of an adventurous spirit.

*So... to sum it up. Perhaps.*

*There's a pretty high chance that this message is from the real world, isn't there?*

If that was the case, I should be able to find a way to communicate somehow. Just that thought alone made me feel quite a bit better.

"Mm."

I got up and paced around the small room.

*Actually, I don't need to be that good at acting.*

This is just something I'm doing to gather information about reality. If I were as good as a real actor, I might get dragged back to Korea immediately. However, it would also be a problem if what I did was not even at an acceptable level. In that case, I could also be dragged back to Korea.

*What if they tell me to return quickly and take acting lessons? No way.*

Besides...

*...The pop-up appeared when I started acting.*

It might be because I met Bae Sejin, but since I did not know the cause, I had to hold on to this acting thread.

*So the conclusion is to practice.*

I thought I had figured out a proper route for how to practice.

[It should be a natural and casual movement, but it must look atmospheric.]

In other words, I had to move naturally enough to be casual, but coolly at the same time.

*It's like a designer's request to make something simple yet flashy.*

But hard work does not betray you. If I focused on my body movements and practiced repeatedly, I would eventually get something similar. The obstacle was the fact that my physical condition was garbage.

*Ah, damn it.*

I sighed as I walked. Maintaining naturalness and concentration for a long time was physically almost impossible in this body's condition, even more so without the adrenaline of the stage.

*I could probably grit my teeth and endure it for about three minutes.*

Unlike the stage, the tempo was long, and it was not about explosive, high-density impacts, which made it even more agonizing.

*It's hard to concentrate.*

*Should I bite my tongue?*

"Hoo."

I kicked at the non-existent sea. A few days passed in a flash, and finally, the filming day arrived. I met this place's Bae Sejin again.

"Hello."

"...Yes. Hello."

*His expression is still bad.*

I asked the guy point-blank.

"I practiced, but there's something bothering me. You told me before to 'do it just like that,' coach."

"......"

"Did you say that because you thought there was no point in me practicing more."

"...What? That's not what I meant."

Bae Sejin frowned.

"You said you've never learned to act."

"Yes."

"Then it's better to do it like you did then, without being conscious of it. It's not like you have any lines."

"......"

"So, just do it like that."

It was similar advice to what I had given Keun Sejin when we were making the game for the album. I realized this guy's intention.

*He was trying to give precise advice.*

However, the fact that it sounded like he was picking a fight, contrary to his intention, was also very Bae Sejin-like.

"Yes. Thank you."

"...No. I'm getting paid for this."

*He's really consistent.*

Instead of getting pissed off, I felt a slight sense of nostalgia, shrugged, and walked away. Well, fine. Let's really just do it moderately then.

"Okay, you're on now~"

"Yes."

I stepped onto the sandy beach.

*I need to get the 'OK' quickly.*

I distrusted my own stamina, and then, a message appeared.

[A focused genius actor bestows a blessing upon you!]

[Trait: Concentration]

"......"

At that moment, my whole body suddenly began to feel light.

***

I walked along the beach. My body moved naturally, without me being conscious of anything. A bouquet of flowers was in my hand.

*A bouquet is a somewhat unusual object, so it's more natural not to be too conscious of it. Let's just let it sway in my hand.*

"Hoo."

Beyond my sighing breath, I could see the sea.

SHWAAAA-

The sun was setting.

"......"

I stopped walking. I did not know what story this person had that brought them to the sea.

*It doesn't matter.*

So I just needed to act clearly, exactly as the directions said. Naturally. I lightly tossed the bouquet into the seawater.

Thump.

Just as I had focused, the bouquet fell onto the beach in a pleasing arc. I gently composed myself. After a beat, I moved again.

Thump...

I lightly kicked a wave. There were no directions saying I was angry.

*So, as if just touching it. A little cautiously.*

"......"

After pulling my foot back, I turned, conscious that I was standing facing the sun. I faced the remembered camera position, without wavering.

Swish.

Just once. Only in that single moment when my eyes met the camera did I give it life. Just like I did on stage.

"......"

I realized that I had perfectly executed the entire process, without a hitch, just as planned. I did not throw it all out there expressively, but I focused on every single detail.

*So this is what it was like.*

I stared silently at the camera without blinking. The world of conscious concentration. I felt like I understood, if only superficially, what it was about acting that had captivated Bae Sejin.

And then, a voice called out.

"Cut! We're going with that."

The world outside the camera began to move again. The cut ended with a noisy buzz of voices.

"Thank you."

I bowed my head and stepped away from the camera.

"That was pretty good, wasn't it?"

"Fewer takes than I thought..."

Beyond the chattering staff, Coach Bae Sejin watched me with slightly widened eyes, and probably, the other Bae Sejin too.

[A focused genius actor declares that was more than good enough!]

[A focused genius actor has sponsored you! : Energy +]

Strength filled my body, and the headache slowly subsided.

*Ah.*

*No matter how I think about it, it really seems like the guys from reality are the ones sending these messages.*

Coach Bae Sejin opened his mouth.

"...How."

I smiled faintly.

"Because you're a good coach."

It was the truth, without a shred of falsehood.

***

Meanwhile, at JSA, the group was celebrating.

"Woooah!"

"Let's go with this!"

"Wh-Who should be next...?"

Celebrating the successful result, they quickly established a formal plan.

*We have to help him recover quickly and bring him back!*

It was, in a word, 'Raising Park Moondae'.
