# Chapter 543

After the Status Window Appeared

For several seconds, Cheongryeo was unresponsive, as if lightning had struck him.

Then, he spat, "It says 'Help'."

The process that led to these words was simple. This guy's first thought in response to my 'Accepting questions' pop-up was this.

[What is this.]

I roughly counted it as a question. My answer was this.

[Help.]

It was a simple and clear explanation of my function. There was no way this bastard would believe me if I suddenly said I was his junior idol from the future.

*It would only make him more wary.*

That's why I approached it with a familiar computer concept. I almost spouted nonsense about appearing as an algorithm from his 100th restart, but I stopped, thinking it crossed a line. Instead, I just said I had appeared naturally, without my knowledge.

Now, I was about to kindly answer his follow-up question, 'Are you really the Help'.

[Of course.]

[This is a Help that will assist in resolving your status abnormalities.]

It was bullshit. Even if I helped this guy and VTIC won the Grand Prize this time, that did not happen in reality. The current VTIC had no members who created a secret dating hideout.

The real Cheongryeo, whom I met just before and who is now in the military, said he had never seen a Status Window. This meant the original Cheongryeo had no memory of meeting me, the current Status Window.

*...no matter what I do here, the events that the real Cheongryeo experienced won't change.*

I already confirmed this when Keundal became Ryu Gunwoo and was afflicted with the status abnormality of dying if he could not become a civil servant. The things I did there as Keundal's Status Window, like reporting Bae Sejin's father for drugs or emailing Park Moondae's old classmate, did not change my future.

Instead, I only learned that I had personally resolved incidents I had thought were coincidences. In other words, there was no time paradox. It was impossible to change the past to affect the future.

This place was probably like the System's virtual memory, just like with those STier guys.

*Hmm.*

I smacked my lips, feeling a bit bitter. Still, the reason I was trying so hard to stop this guy from restarting right now was because of the variables.

*I'm currently attached to this guy's mind. Who knows what would happen if he restarted?*

I needed to preserve the current state as much as possible to grasp the situation and gather information.

*So, looking at the big picture, me being a 'Help' is just... a scam.*

I never dreamed I would be doing something like a voice phishing scam. Still, it was true that I was going to help, so it was mutually beneficial.

[As a Help, I will faithfully answer all your questions.]

And the next words were a plain fact.

[If you use my functions, you won't suffer a loss, at the very least.]

Having an instruction manual makes work much easier. That is a truth. From what I knew, Cheongryeo was not the type to refuse something beneficial, though I expected a tremendous number of questions and experiments from the suspicious guy.

*How about it.*

Having made all my preparations, I waited for his answer.

"......"

Cheongryeo stood perfectly still, staring at the pop-up I displayed without any agitation.

Then, he smiled faintly.

"Alright."

I slapped my forehead.

*This bastard is pretending to believe me.*

***

The pop-up spewed out text.

[I can show you the members' statuses.]

[You can check them right now.]

Cheongryeo did not respond. In the dorm's living room, only the coats of the two who went to the practice room and one person who had fallen asleep fiddling with a game console were visible.

Then, another pop-up appeared.

[You can also check their characteristics and potential stats.]

Cheongryeo did not react and casually walked out of the dorm.

[Not just the current members, but anyone you meet]

"That's excessive."

The pop-up's text stopped.

"If you're a Help, you should only appear when I call you."

The hologram was silent for a short moment, as if calculating. Then, a submissive answer appeared.

[Alright.]

Cheongryeo started his car. After arriving at his desired location, he walked until he reached his destination. It was the rooftop of the building he had climbed yesterday.

Just as planned, he leaned his body over the railing.

[Wait.]

[Hey.]

[You bastard]

[I told you this crazy bastard doesn't need to restart]

The pop-ups went wild the moment he leaned on the railing.

[You have enough time]

"Trying to persuade me?"

[]

The text stopped. Cheongryeo remained leaning against the railing and clasped his hands.

"You said you were a Help."

The pop-up froze, as if startled.

Cheongryeo watched the scene, slowly scanning it. He adjusted his gloves and looked down from the rooftop.

"I don't think so."

He calculated with a cool head. Something had suddenly appeared, offering systematic, kind, and verbalized help like a computer operating system. It appeared when he had experienced enough restarts to find this very tempting.

It was as if it were looking for prey. That thing was an intelligent being with a will of its own. Through the process so far, he had proven one thing for certain.

Cheongryeo smiled faintly.

"You'll be in trouble if I restart."

Still, the pop-up displayed text as if it had been prepared.

[Using me increases your chances of success this time, and even if it doesn't work out, restarting will make it as if it never happened.]

[There's nothing to lose.]

Cheongryeo did not respond for a moment. The cold dawn air swirled around the rooftop.

And then, a laugh rang out.

"......"

"Increases the chances, you say."

Cheongryeo laughed, turning his upper body to put the railing behind him. He then spoke as if talking to himself.

"What's the most fatal aspect of a dating scandal controversy?"

[......]

"That it's not a crime."

He continued, answering his own question.

"It's a matter of moral reprehensibility... The problem is that the general public, who aren't fans, can't sympathize with that."

[You gather a bunch of handsome men and beautiful women, of course they'll date. Are there any super handsome guys who don't date at that age? lol]

"So what do the fans do?"

There was no pop-up.

Cheongryeo spoke comfortably to the empty air.

"They'll go looking for other controversies about that member."

From skill controversies to school violence and bullying within the group. They will passionately pour their time into somehow digging up a flaw that the public can sympathize with and creating a persuasive post. As much as they loved and knew idols well, they could create effective and fitting controversy fodder. For the sake of a reason to get revenge, a just cause befitting their sense of betrayal.

[A fan who has turned into an anti-fan.]

"Colloquially, you could put it that way."

Cheongryeo continued with a smile.

"Even if you make the member voluntarily leave, the risk doesn't disappear."

Among the individual fans of the member whose dating scandal broke, a minority will still support him, and they will develop animosity towards the group itself.

[Forcing him to leave just because a dating scandal broke out. Fucking hell, idols really have no human rights.]

[Wow, is this the 80s?]

This was especially dangerous given VTIC's current position with a large proportion of international fans. It is common for some international fans to dislike making an idol's dating life a controversy in the first place. Of course, Cheongryeo did have the ability to plan things out well and minimize the risk.

However, it would unconditionally take time.

"Hmm, it would take half a year for public opinion to settle. Even if we minimize losses by touring then..."

His gloved fingers tapped the railing as if calculating.

Tap, tap.

They stopped.

"The comeback opportunity will be in June at the earliest."

Just once. The only comeback that would be meaningfully reflected in that year's results is that one.

"The probability of winning the Grand Prize with a single promotion after a long hiatus due to a fatal controversy."

Cheongryeo demanded of the pop-up, "Answer me."

*...*

"When I have the option to just restart without that member."

Cheongryeo smiled and adjusted his gloves. He then clasped his hands and asked, "Why should I invest 359 days' worth of time into this slim probability?"

*...*

"Explain."

Park Moondae thought.

*I'm screwed.*

***

Park Moondae gave up on the helper concept. This bastard did not believe it anyway. Instead, he answered Cheongryeo with moderate honesty.

[Actually, I succeeded in a mission thanks to someone who became a Help like this for me, and this time, I've become the Help myself.]

"I see."

Unsurprisingly, Cheongryeo still did not seem to believe him. He did not even seem to have any intention of asking how that was possible.

*Fuck.*

So Park Moondae decided to take the easiest path. Spoilers from the future.

[I know what kind of songs will be popular after this, and what kind of concepts will do well.]

[If you knew that, 359 days should be worth investing.]

Cheongryeo was also clear about that.

"Keep talking."

[If you promise not to press that button.]

"......"

Of course, Park Moondae meant the 'Delete Status Window' button floating right next to him.

Cheongryeo was silent for a moment but soon answered calmly.

"......"

Park Moondae was not fooled.

[Don't just give me lip service. Mean it.]

From his perspective, it seemed Cheongryeo's eyebrow twitched for a moment. However, his answer was still calm.

"Alright."

*On the condition that you continue to give useful answers.*

Park Moondae, who could hear the thought omitted from the end of that sentence, groaned, but there was no one to see it. The same went for Cheongryeo.

Instead, he quickly organized the several genres, singers, and composers the self-proclaimed 'Help' was listing in his head. It was a pretty plausible portfolio.

However.

"Your basis?"

[I don't think you'd believe me even if I told you.]

"Hmm."

Cheongryeo's gaze shifted.

[!Unauthorized Error Occurred]

[Would you like to remove the Status Window?]

*This son of a bitch.*

Park Moondae, having received the message that it would be deleted if he did not answer properly, finally gave a clean answer.

[I'm from the future, happy now?]

"Hmm?"

[I'm the next one after you.]

It was an unrealistic story, but it had plausibility. After all, he himself could already go back to the past and restart. There was also an implied meaning in the self-proclaimed 'Help's' explanation.

"So I succeeded."

That is, that he would succeed someday. The existence of a 'next one' meant that he would eventually end his restarts.

*Hmm.*

However, Cheongryeo felt no great emotion at this fact. Fundamentally, he did not trust the Help's sentences. Besides, if you tried anything repeatedly, you were bound to succeed eventually.

In fact, he had already stabilized the pattern for 'VTIC's success' several dozen restarts ago. The only problems were the timing of several status abnormalities and the member composition. Of course, this too was a problem that could be solved by trying a few different scenarios.

[......]

The pop-up did not answer directly, but it indirectly acknowledged it.

[I can tell you the members you chose then.]

Cheongryeo replied with a smile.

"I didn't ask."

The unproven, self-proclaimed 'Help's' sweet talk was about to cross the line.

The pop-up stopped immediately.

[Right.]

However, Cheongryeo guessed that it seemed quite pissed off. Of course, the pop-up was just text and had no face, so there was almost nothing to read from it.

*It's a bit annoying.*

As if reading that surface thought, a pop-up appeared reluctantly.

[Do you want me to add an emoticon or something?]

"No."

He wanted to scrape out information the other party did not intend to express, not see useless surface expressions.

Having finished the conversation, Cheongryeo stood up. His mind was organized about this self-proclaimed Help.

1. It has a personality.

2. It reads thoughts.

3. It lies.

He had confirmed all three, and the most important thing remained to confirm.

4. It has utility value.

[Where are you going.]

"To try you out."

Now was the start of confirming its 'provable' utility.
