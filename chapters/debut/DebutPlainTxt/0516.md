# Chapter 516

This was a joint effort with Bae Sejin. The plan was to turn Ryu Chungwoo into a producer.

Ryu Chungwoo had fallen for the first step. The next morning, he unwittingly accompanied me to see Kim Raebin's work.

He had agreed himself, so there was no helping it.

"I should go check on it."

"Ah."

I spent the morning with Kim Raebin, who had instantly adapted to the unfamiliar studio. All I asked of Ryu Chungwoo was to chat and react to the song.

In the process, Ryu Chungwoo naturally focused more and more on the music.

"As you advised, I've created three versions with brass samples!"

"They definitely fit well... I like number 2 or 3. What about you?"

"Hmm... Number 2?"

By participating in the arrangement selection, he unknowingly added his own opinions to the song.

Then came the next step.

"Alright, I'll play it with the choreography draft now."

"Yes!"

I played the semi-finished second track over the recently filmed choreography video. The topic naturally shifted from the song to the stage.

"I think it fits well."

"It does."

"While we're organizing the formation, it might be good to add a part where they go back and forth in pairs. In that case, that part would be..."

I looked at Ryu Chungwoo, who inadvertently offered his own thoughts.

"If it's that part... the bridge would probably feel the most natural."

As expected from someone with so much stage experience, STier Ryu Chungwoo's opinions were quite good, even without my intentional guidance.

"I think it sounds good. Raebin?"

"Ah, me too... Yes. I think it'll match the arrangement well!"

STier Kim Raebin no longer seemed to cautiously watch Ryu Chungwoo's reactions. Although the kid had rejected the stage, he seemed emotionally excited about having 'my own studio'.

*...It could just be because he escaped the military.*

No, a normal person might feel that way, but it was probably different for Kim Raebin.

In any case, the atmosphere was quite good, and the work progressed smoothly.

Ryu Chungwoo unwittingly completed several producing tasks, all tangled together like a spiderweb. It was as if he were just sliding down a slide.

He contributed to the formation, part distribution, costume points, and theme. He even gave feedback on choreography details.

"...Since they'll be wearing gloves, it'd be better to avoid gestures that rely on friction."

"Oops. OK."

He even went to the practice room to say things like that.

Before he was fully aware, time flew by, and we reached the end.

"The rehearsal filming is starting now. Let's go."

The stage was complete.

This time, the moment the performance began on the official competition set, Ryu Chungwoo focused on the stage, unlike before.

"It's starting now."

Unlike last time, Ryu Chungwoo began to focus on the stage.

***

The dress rehearsal incorporated everything from costumes to lighting and effects. With all the elements of a real stage, it promised a flawless performance.

And now.

On the lit stage, Lee Sejin, dressed in a magician's suit, grinned and gave a theatrical stage greeting.

[Ladies- and gentlemen!]

A brilliant brass ensemble burst forth.

Seon Ahyeon and Cha Eugene appeared in the light with a turn. Bae Sejin took his place between them and sang the intro.

[It's as if between us

there's a special blank space]

The backdrop was velvet curtains and spotlights. Finger snaps and light-footed steps created a stage reminiscent of a 20th-century black-and-white film, but the song was a recent hit.

It was the latest comeback song from Mirynay, TeSTAR's junior group.

Instead of a modern remake of an old song, common for competition stages, they took a unique approach. They turned a recent hit into a retro-style piece.

[We don't know yet what will become of this gap

That's true-]

The live sound perfectly suited the stage effects and the rhythmic arrangement.

The brass exploded. In time with the building music, Cha Eugene pushed his vocals forward.

[Taking one step

toward you, who won't come closer]

Then, as if everything was refreshingly resolved, the sound suddenly opened for the chorus. In the middle of the choreography, the four completed one big move together. Bae Sejin adjusted his hat, smiled, and nodded.

[Fill in the blanks,

fill this empty space

cross the gap]

From the side, Seon Ahyeon spun an ornate wooden standing microphone that resembled a classic cane. He changed the performance formation into a diagonal line.

[Something special

even if I misstep and fall in

it's a sweet dream]

Even without the main and lead vocalists, the live performance was solid.

Moreover, the members who excelled at dancing took the verses, each delivering a solo performance with strong character.

When the sub-vocalist, who was not a strong dancer, came to the center, the other three members supported him with their performance. They helped him showcase his talent well.

The part that mixed in tap dancing to match the outfits and theme was also a nice touch.

"Wow."

The staff watching the rehearsal with us gasped in admiration, burst into laughter, or were too focused on the stage to react at all.

At the very front of the audience, Ryu Chungwoo was also deeply focused on the stage.

"......"

His sentiment was not that of a complete spectator like the filming staff.

Because he had participated.

The steps taken to complete that performance surfaced in his mind automatically.

[An easy-to-break-down rhythm with a not-too-fast tempo.]

[The ensemble only for the crucial parts.]

[Practice focusing on the steps.]

"......"

It was inevitable.

*They reflected it well.*

His feedback had been thoroughly reflected without distortion.

Not all of his opinions were reflected. However, the elements that were not included had gone through a process of agreement, making the reasons understandable.

That was why he felt more at ease. It gave him confidence that his participation had only helped raise the quality.

[Ye-eeees!]

On stage, lights sparkled like fireworks. The dazzling stage was joyful.

And that was all.

There was no rejection born from learned frustration, no obstacle to overcome.

He had gradually and unconsciously immersed himself in this work in a different way. He was not the performance idol who had been mentally pushed to his limits.

What remained was the thrill of seeing a perfect final product, completed exactly as intended.

[Fill- in the blanks!]

WHOA!

The moment the song ended on a clean, solid mid-to-high note, the watching staff cheered and applauded.

Praise poured out for the unit members, who caught their breath with flushed faces. It even reached the audience members in charge of producing.

"You guys planned it so well... It was really great!"

STier Ryu Chungwoo smiled faintly.

"Thank you."

He felt a small spark. It was the heat of accomplishment.

***

After the rehearsal wrapped up, the TeSTAR members from the stage left for their interviews. Park Moondae excused himself for a moment, saying he needed to talk to the production team.

Only the two STier members remained in the waiting room.

"This was a very enjoyable and meaningful project!"

"It was."

Kim Raebin, in casual clothes with his hair tied back and wearing glasses, looked extremely happy. He was so happy that he did not even flinch when Ryu Chungwoo agreed with him.

So Ryu Chungwoo spoke.

"Raebin."

"......"

"There must have been many times when my attitude toward you felt harsh."

"...?"

He calmly said the words he had wanted to say for a long time.

"I'm sorry."

"......"

"You've been through a lot."

He started to pat Kim Raebin's head but stopped, worried it might seem intimidating. He lightly tapped his shoulder instead. Either way, Kim Raebin was frozen in shock.

"If you have something to say, it's okay to say it comfortably... but even saying that is probably difficult, huh."

Ryu Chungwoo did not let his bitter smile show.

He knew that even if he went back to the past, there probably would not have been another way. He had to suppress everyone equally with hierarchy and fear. Otherwise, it was impossible for him to lead the members and maintain the group in a crumbling team atmosphere.

However, he also knew there was no need to justify it aloud to others. So he simply ended with this.

"It's okay if you want to talk after I get my memories back. Don't worry about it."

"......"

Kim Raebin did not answer, but it was not a refusal. He was debating whether it was okay to say what he was thinking. His long-suppressed inquisitive nature had revived to some extent.

"......"

"Hm?"

In the end, Kim Raebin asked cautiously.

His question.

"Then, do you still plan on getting your memories back...?"

"I'm curious about that too."

Another voice cut in.

"...!"

Thud.

Park Moondae, having finished his conversation with the production team, opened the door and tossed out the words.

In truth, he had made an excuse to leave so the two of them could talk, but that did not matter now.

*Don't tell me none of this worked?*

*Could it be that there was really no change of heart at all?*

Park Moondae seriously began to question his own abilities, but he checked Ryu Chungwoo's expression first.

"That's right."

STier Ryu Chungwoo had a rather comfortable expression.

It was not resignation. There was clearly a change.

"......"

*In that case.*

Park Moondae decided to dig a little deeper.

"But it seems your reason for wanting them back has changed a little."

"...Hmm, it's the opposite."

Ryu Chungwoo rubbed his chin.

"I've come to realize more about why I hated the idea of getting my memories back."

At the unexpected answer, Park Moondae momentarily repeated the words.

*A reason to hate it?*

STier Ryu Chungwoo answered as if he had heard Park Moondae's inner thoughts.

"Because it felt like losing."

"......"

Kim Raebin's mouth fell open at the bombshell statement, but Ryu Chungwoo continued speaking calmly.

"I told you before, right? That after seeing Cha Eugene, it seemed like he changed a lot after getting his memories back."

That was right.

*Was that why he said he hated it? Because it felt like becoming a different person?*

Park Moondae recalled their past conversation.

"......"

"Yeah. He looked very comfortable. Happy and at ease. It was good."

Ryu Chungwoo described Cha Eugene's healthy state in the most positive terms, smiling faintly.

"But I didn't want to become like that."

"What?"

"It felt like giving up and taking the easy way out. Like rationalizing that some other person who succeeded in a way I don't even know was the 'original me'."

It was a rather harsh rejection. It might have been a kind of atrophied pride or self-esteem.

However, STier Ryu Chungwoo had lived for a long time having forgotten his pride, making it hard to realize so vividly.

"So instead, I wanted to show that I could achieve just as much as that person and take his place."

"......"

"This is also just a rationalization, of course."

Because it was just imitation, Ryu Chungwoo added calmly.

Park Moondae asked, "From the beginning?"

"From the beginning."

From the moment he came to his senses in this body and grasped the situation, Ryu Chungwoo had reached this rather extreme conclusion.

This was possible because he had, in fact, repeated a similar worry for several years as an STier.

One's own usefulness.

[What on earth can I do?]

But in truth, Ryu Chungwoo was not the type to yearn for escape after such gloomy self-reflection. He was the type to find a clear answer even to complex problems and execute it immediately.

During his STier days, there were no clear answers to anything, so he never escaped that worry. Perhaps that was why it was all the more painful.

A long, slow fall.

However.

"I don't think that way anymore."

STier Ryu Chungwoo answered Park Moondae with the unique gaze of someone who had already grasped the entire situation.

"Today's stage was probably your way of trying to cheer me up. Thanks."

"......"

At the same time, Park Moondae realized.

*His way of speaking has changed.*

Now, instead of similarly trying to recreate 'TeSTAR Ryu Chungwoo', he used a slightly more assertive and uninflected tone.

Park Moondae guessed this was probably the tone he used during his STier days.

And, the conversation developed.

"Ah, there's something I'm curious about too."

"What is it?"

"I feel like there was something you were going to say at the beginning to persuade me to want my memories back."

"......"

"Can I hear it?"

Park Moondae smiled faintly.

"Of course."

He brought out the words he had prepared.

"You said Cha Eugene changed a lot after getting his memories back, but he just reclaimed what was his. It's the same for you."

"Hmm."

"...You've had a lot of good experiences with this group."

"I have."

"Those are your achievements."

"You think so?"

"Yes."

If this had been the Ryu Chungwoo from a few days ago, those words would not have resonated at all.

Because he had almost forgotten what accomplishment felt like, he would have been completely unable to think of them as his own achievements. They would have still felt like someone else's.

But the stage he saw today, this small experience, opened the possibility for him to guess what that feeling might be like.

So he smiled and answered.

"Alright. I'm curious."

That night, STier Ryu Chungwoo decided to reclaim himself as TeSTAR.
