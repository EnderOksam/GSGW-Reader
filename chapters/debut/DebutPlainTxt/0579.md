# Chapter 579

"Hoo."

Ryu Gunwoo, Keundal, pressed a hand to his pounding heart. He opened his laptop in the hospital room.

This was only possible because his hyung had stopped by his apartment and sent the laptop over via quick delivery.

He felt both sorry and grateful. His hyung must have been busy preparing for the concert, yet he still considered Keundal's hospitalization.

...Even though Keundal had been nothing but a burden.

[Hyung, I'm so sorry for causing so much trouble yesterday...]

[What're you talking about? It's not like you asked me to skip the concert. More importantly, I'm gonna do a great job at the concert today, so just watch that.]

If it had ended there, Keundal's guilt might have deepened, but Park Moondae had lightly added something else.

[Still, it'd be nice if you watched the content and gave me some feedback.]

[...! Yes!]

Keundal had been given a task. He now diligently logged into his account on his laptop.

*It's been a while for this, too.*

To be honest, he had recently been consciously avoiding TeSTAR's content.

After his hyung told him to find his own life, Ryu Gunwoo's life, Keundal had abruptly stopped confiding in him about TeSTAR's problems.

*[It's probably because the pop-ups are gone... He must be being careful.]*

Even knowing that was not the intention, it felt as if Park Moondae had told him they should no longer concern themselves with each other.

Keundal tried his best to follow that advice. He spent money and time trying to do something on his own.

But it was not fun. Life had just become more numb.

"......"

No. If he kept thinking like this, he would just become a burden to his hyung again.

*Let's get it together!*

Keundal consciously took a deep breath, finished logging in, and accessed the concert streaming site. He expected the site to redirect him to the payment page, but a pop-up suddenly blocked the screen.

A newly added feature flashed gaudily.

[4K VR Streaming Support.]

: Experience the heat of the venue, a concert more realistic than a front-row seat!

"Huh?"

VR. It literally meant virtual reality, but it was also used for watching 360-degree videos. This was clearly the latter.

*But I don't have a device like that...*

He was about to mindlessly close the pop-up.

*Ah!*

He remembered. The device his hyung had sent as a birthday present was a VR headset. He felt sorry that he had not even tried using it, due to a strange sense of lethargy.

*I want to try using it now.*

It would have been useless if it was not here, but surprisingly, the item was inside the box his hyung had sent.

"Wow!"

Keundal carefully opened the package. The new, white, goggle-shaped device was sleek and shiny.

Maybe...

*Is Hyung recommending I watch it in VR?*

An idea sparked in his mind.

*My laptop's performance is... Ah! I think it's possible.*

He checked his laptop's specs and the VR streaming requirements. He was relieved to find it barely met the ultra-high-definition standard and connected it.

The message 'Connection Successful' appeared. Pleased, he finished a test run of wearing the headset.

"Wow."

For some reason, his heart started pounding in a different way. It was the thrill of enjoying something he loved in a new way.

Suddenly, he felt a surge of energy.

He moved his hands busily, quickly paying for the stream. He started looking through TeSTAR's back catalog of content until the concert began.

*Ah, there are probably things I should watch first... Whoa, what's this?! Choices?*

Several hours flew by in an instant. Keundal diligently watched everything, right down to the tour promotion videos that tormented viewers with a cycle of endless happy endings.

Finally, the time had come.

[PM 05:45]

Keundal sat tensely, wearing the VR device and twitching his hands. He finally pressed the start button that had just become active.

The internet browser disappeared, and a loading screen appeared.

WHOOSH!

His vision changed. He was at the concert venue, fifteen minutes before the show.

"Wow...!"

Inside the dim, sunset-lit concert hall, light sources of all colors rippled like waves. They were the lights from the light sticks.

The unfiltered murmuring and ambient noise of the venue enhanced the sense of being there. Keundal willingly endured the dizziness and whipped his head around.

*This is amazing...*

It was only sight and sound, but the two overwhelming senses tricked the others. It felt as if he were actually there.

"......"

For a moment, he felt a fulfilling excitement. It was the sense of camaraderie, buried among people waiting for the same thing.

It was excitement and anticipation.

The things he had forgotten due to his lethargy suddenly reared their heads, growing in his heart as if they had never left. They grew from the scenery of this concert hall.

DING!

[Hello Loviewer! Is your light stick connected properly? ...Not yet? Then let's do it together step-by-step!]

The giant screen at the front showed instructions for connecting the light stick via Bluetooth, concert information, and PPLs in rotation.

Every time a member's face appeared, cheers filled with anticipation cut through the air.

Keundal had already realized the box his hyung brought also contained a TeSTAR light stick. He had already connected it. The status was 'Streaming'.

Then, something interesting happened.

"Ooh!"

Somehow, the light from the light stick he was waving flashed within his VR vision like augmented reality. That something like this was possible!

*So they didn't release a renewed version of the light stick for nothing!*

He excitedly waved his light stick. The options said he could see other streaming users waving theirs, but it was too dizzying, so he turned that off.

He endured the next ten minutes with a pounding heart. A medley of instrumentals from various TeSTAR songs echoed through the venue.

And then, the lights suddenly went out without warning.

*...!*

On the screen, which had split into two, huge letters in a black-and-white film style appeared. It was a countdown.

5.

4.

3.

2.

1.

The voices of the audience echoed from all sides.

The numbers ended.

[Piiiiiiooooooo-!]

Along with the cheers, a train horn blared loudly. Then a smooth announcement began to play. It was Cha Eugene's voice.

[This stop is Red Wonder, Red Wonder...]

It finished, low and clear.

[...Hotel.]

THUD.

Without warning, a bright light shone on the stage.

*Ah.*

It was a hotel lobby. It looked familiar. It was the very same place from the TeSTAR tour promotion choice-based video that Keundal had just watched.

The moment the audience widened their gaze, they realized it was not just a lobby.

The massive set utilized the stage all the way to the ceiling. It pieced together all sorts of hotel structures like stairs, an elevator, a cafe, and even a suite. It was designed to bring back fresh memories of every location from the promotion video.

"......"

Every element of the hotel from the video was perfectly realized and set up on the stage.

AAAAAAAAH!!

Just as the cheers began to die down, figures exquisitely appeared from here and there on the set.

They were behind the check-in counter, next to a lobby pillar, on a cafe chair, in the bathtub of the suite, and in the elevator.

It was TeSTAR, acting nonchalantly. They read a newspaper or looked at a ledger on the counter.

An intro played, a clever combination of a train whistle and a circus accordion. They expertly dashed off the set and came to the front of the stage.

The moment they formed their positions, a burst of colors exploded.

[It's now.]

❬Hunting❭, rearranged to the tour promotion version, magnificently opened the concert.

[The tables have turned

Today, you're the sheep]

Keundal almost got so excited that he reached for his smartphone, but then he remembered he was watching in VR. His body swayed.

[(Run, Run!)

Circling below the stage

Come on, back to the start]

Amidst the power and heat, the members' close-ups were captured on the big screen. This included the one member who could not be on stage yesterday.

*Hyung!*

His whole body was perfectly styled. Park Moondae looked exactly the same as he had yesterday.

But the light in his eyes was different. The light in his eyes burned brightly amidst his bursting vitality, as if he could not control himself because his condition was so good.

[Call up, it's hunting season]

THUMP.

The sound from the speakers mixed with the audience's cheers, electrifying the atmosphere. A complete, unburdened excitement filled the venue.

Instead of forming a final formation, the members scattered. They returned to their original starting positions on the stage set and disappeared just as they had appeared.

However, the cheers did not end. One person remained, standing nonchalantly in his spot.

DING.

Kim Raebin casually rang the bell at the counter and gave a deadpan nod.

[Welcome, again.]

The stage rotated.

"......"

The counter jutted forward. Kim Raebin, who was standing behind it, vaulted over it and charged into view.

His solo song, which had received a tremendous response, began to flow with the live sound of a bursting band.

[Jump into the rabbit hole

Into a strange land

manhole]

In a performance that connected seamlessly with the opening, Kim Raebin ran all over the entire stage set with dancers in rabbit masks.

True to its subtitle, 'overture', he went around blowing up all the impressive elements of the set as if introducing them.

The storm of rapping and performance rushed toward the ending without a moment to catch a breath.

CLACK, CLACK.

The rabbit-masked dancers all disappeared. The center of the stage was empty.

The spotlight on Kim Raebin, who was walking backward with the sound of his dress shoes, went out.

AAAAAAAAH!!!

The screams, which sounded like they would make the venue explode, rang in his ears.

"Huuuuh..."

Keundal blinked. Ten minutes had passed without him even realizing he was holding his breath.

*Wh-what's next??*

Having forgotten all his complicated feelings, the concert once again rushed fiercely before his eyes.

While the cheers still echoed, the screen lit up instead of the dark stage. It was a VCR.

Here, Keundal had a different experience from the live audience.

*Whoa, whoa...?!*

His entire field of vision changed. He was no longer sitting in the concert hall.

He was in an elegant hallway, covered in classic and ornate wooden patterns and wallpaper.

He was... inside the VCR!

*Whoa!?*

As he whipped his head around, the hotel hallway came into view, completely split into two sides. Left, and right.

The hallways seemed to be lined with rooms, but each side had a completely different atmosphere.

On the right, beyond the softly glowing lamps, he could see the figures of kind-looking hoteliers.

On the left, amidst the flickering of an ominous, crimson lamp, dark silhouettes stood beneath it.

*Huk.*

He whipped his head back and forth, checking both sides.

Ryu Chungwoo's low, gentle voice recited a narration.

[A rabbit hole. Where shall we go?]

Like augmented reality, text appeared on both sides.

[RED / YELLOW]

[Let us know with your light.]

"Huh?"

The word 'RED' glowed red, and 'YELLOW' glowed yellow.

[Try operating your light stick!]

"Huuuh."

He lifted his light stick with trembling hands and soon realized.

*Ah, I can't do it.*

People who were streaming had no voting rights. No human rights.

"......"

*I should have gotten a ticket.*

He swallowed his tears, yet his heart pounded as he looked at both sides again. Red light and yellow light.

Which side did they choose?

Soon, the result was revealed.

[Loviewer's choice]

The current audience seating was shown before his eyes.

*Wow...*

The ratio of the audience, rippling with red and yellow, was clearly dominated by red at a glance.

It was decided.

[RED]

[HUNT]

With a claw-like mark, the video collapsed.

A shout echoed from the stage.

[That's ma sa---vior!!]

The extended stage was dyed red. A powerful metal-rock version of ❬Drill❭ slammed down on the venue from around Park Moondae, who was holding a head mic.

~~~

"Aaaaaaaah!!"

Park Moondae's first fansite master screamed herself hoarse in the standing section.

The concert was a relentless storm. This concert, structured like an exploration of a hotel, was filled with all sorts of spectacles, as if it were a circus. It had everything from the giant set to all kinds of stage equipment.

Of course, the best part was the choices made with the light sticks.

*This is so damn fun!*

Choosing either side did not change the song itself. However, a slight difference in outfits and arrangement completely changed the atmosphere. Watching that was so much fun.

*Hah...!!*

They secured extra time between stages by making the VCR interactive, then used that time to fill every single moment so perfectly.

The fansite master had come to today's concert with a heart half-full of anticipation and half-full of worry. She now felt a deep joy spreading in her chest.

There was no need to worry.

*Moondae is flying around up there!*

She was even happier because she had watched yesterday's concert in a state of near-unconsciousness.

*The vibe is definitely different from yesterday.*

Of course, the other members performed well yesterday too, but the sudden awareness of the empty space seemed to have prevented people from becoming fully immersed.

*And this is a concert designed for complete immersion.*

The choices were there for immersion. They were designed to let you get completely lost in the concert, without any moments that brought you back to reality. She could feel that was the very intention.

*I heard they gave concert streaming tickets to all of the first-day audience members.*

*Please, I hope they didn't just get a refund and aren't just waiting for the additional concert dates. I hope they're watching today's show too.*The fansite master muttered to herself and looked at the stage again.

[Wish you call me like this-]

On stage, Lee Sejin's solo performance was passing its climax. He used a large, golden trolley with one hand, putting on a spectacular show with turns and movements.

It was the 'YELLOW' choice stage.

*I think this is better than the 'RED' I saw yesterday!?*

It seemed the members had each chosen their solo songs as they wished, without any restrictions. Cha Eugene even remade an old pop song, arranging it hiply to his liking and inserting it as a sample.

*I thought he was crazy.*

She had been flabbergasted when the dance break changed because she chose a different option from yesterday. She now watched the stage with her fists clenched.

It was not far now. Now... the turn was coming.

WAAAAAAAAH!!

The previous stage ended with all sorts of hoarse cheers from the crowd. She cheered along, but swallowed hard with a heart-stopping tension.

The stage lights went out. As the VCR started playing again, an empty elevator appeared.

[RED / YELLOW]

The choice between RED, dashing out of the elevator, and YELLOW, remaining inside, was presented in contrasting colors.

The fansite master already knew what stage would come from this choice.

*Moondae...!*

It was Park Moondae's solo stage.

Although this VCR was shown yesterday, the choice was probably meaningless. It had only shown a video of the rehearsal stage being filmed for a test run.

"......"

Feeling the unusually quiet surroundings, she realized that others felt a similar tension.

In truth, Park Moondae's absence had not faced enormous criticism thanks to the story behind it, but that same story had also made it a subtly unpleasant topic of interest.

[Wow ㅠㅠ How is Park Moondae going to perform today... I bet he's not in his right mind]

[I'm curious about his state of mind]

People were curious about what state Park Moondae was in. They were curious about how this incident of his absence might have affected him, and what his state of mind would be.

*Then buy a stream ticket and see for yourself!*

The fansite master had shouted that in her head, but she thought there were definitely people in this very venue who had similar curiosities. People focused not on Park Moondae's performance, but on Park Moondae's feelings and condition.

She herself was concerned to some extent.

"......"

However, while she was worried, the fansite master also believed in Park Moondae. She believed in all his actions up to now.

*Let's do this.*

In the meantime, on the VCR where the choice had been made, a single word dominated the screen.

[YELLOW]

[BE HUNTED]

And it completely tore apart and vanished.

Her heart pounded.

CLICK.

In the quiet concert hall, a single beam of light descended onto the black stage.

A rectangular stage device, hanging in the air near the ceiling, was caught in the spotlight. An elevator.

It seemed to be slowly descending, then it stopped in the middle of the lobby.

DING.

The doors opened, and a languid voice emerged.

[With me]

Park Moondae's solo song began.
