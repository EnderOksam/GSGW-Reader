# Chapter 548

Kim Raebin lifted his head. The PC screen showed a dizzying array of complex, densely packed dots and lines.

He knew, however, that converting it all to sound would create a surprisingly harmonious and beautiful melody, but something was strange.

*Who is this song for?*

Of course, he sometimes composed for fun, imagining a random singer. After the members created the 'Song of the Stars' account together, he would occasionally create songs overflowing with imagination but without a specific vocalist in mind.

For a song to be completed this quickly and specifically, however, meant he had a very clear singing voice in his head.

"That is..."

Looking at the notes he had input, he reflexively started to hear them as a sound source in his mind. He even heard the voice that would fit this track perfectly, like a puzzle piece.

It was a clear and firm voice. It was not overly heavy, and a soft breathiness mixed slightly with the notes as it moved up and down the octaves.

Then, at the perfect moment, an appealing sound seemed to draw out emotions, with a vibrato that was not overdone.

"Kim Raebin, are you composing again?"

"...!"

Startled, Kim Raebin whipped his head around. Cha Eugene was lying on the spare bed in his room, waving his hand.

"I wanna listen too!"

"...Alright."

A strange regret lingered, as if a profound realization had been snatched away just before he could grasp it. Kim Raebin sighed softly and connected the speakers to his PC instead of his headphones.

The music colored the room as if three-dimensional layers were sparkling and popping in every direction.

"Wow."

Even Cha Eugene, who had been rolling around on the bed, stopped moving for a moment. He whistled softly, immersing himself in the music, and Kim Raebin sank back into the sound.

Beautiful music and then silence dominated the space for several tens of seconds. Cha Eugene suddenly opened his mouth.

"By the way, Kim Raebin. Why is there an extra bed here?"

"......"

Kim Raebin stared blankly at the spare bed Cha Eugene pointed at.

"...I must have bought it just in case."

"......"

"Well... quite a while ago."

"For who?"

Kim Raebin stopped mid-sentence. A blank space he had unconsciously overlooked floated in his mind, like something in a fog. The track he created flowed from the speakers, shaking his thoughts like background music.

Whose bed was it?

And for whom exactly had he composed this song, with a vocal range spanning from C3 in the first octave to D#5 in the third?

***

The outdoor wind buffeted his hat in the plaza next to the concert venue.

Ryu Gunwoo, however, was so immersed in his singing that he barely noticed. The melody shot up like an escalator and lightly stepped on the rhythmic stepping stones, carrying the song forward.

[So I]

I must celebrate myself

I must never forget again

He focused on drawing the listener in, yet he was not anxious. It was a clever vocal style that caught the ear. His attitude while singing in front of an audience had not changed.

However, this song did not come from the vocal cords of an idol main vocalist, honed by years of practice, management, and promotions. It was a sound from the vocal cords of Ryu Gunwoo, who had never even considered singing a hobby.

In other words.

[Now, remember]

Until now

His voice cracked slightly and turned husky. There were parts where the pitch did not come out as he wanted and parts where his voice did not sound as he intended.

However, it was the same when Park Moondae first sang this song on ❬Idol Inc.❭.

[For always living through today]

This amazing miracle

Don't forget, don't erase it

The essence did not change.

[So, congratulations]

Celebrate this moment

That's the party in me

Like every day is a PARTY, Ooh-

He was TeSTAR's Park Moondae. No matter where he was, no matter what he looked like. The identity forged during the most dense and intense years of his life was so solid that it kept him grounded.

*I was me.*

[Let's PARTY]

His clear voice, unwavering even in the high notes, firmly filled the microphone's range. The single song came to a satisfying end, full to the very last note.

The man bowed his head in greeting, just as he would at any other performance.

CLAP, CLAP.

Applause from a few audience members who had stopped to listen filled the space left by the finished instrumental. A strange atmosphere also lingered, perhaps because he had sung a song by T-Holic, VTIC's former rivals, at a VTIC concert venue.

Still, a reaction was a reaction. Applause was applause.

"......"

He descended the stairs and let out a refreshing breath. Judan, finally recovering from his dazed expression, made a confident comment.

"So you were a singer."

TeSTAR's Park Moondae did not answer and just grinned.

***

That guy Judan started talking a lot more. Seeing me shamelessly belt out a song in a crowded plaza must have convinced him that I really did this for a living. Thanks to that, I was now listening to his parade of deductions, but wait, wasn't he originally a strangely secular guy?

*Why is he so excited?*

The problem was that his hit rate was surprisingly high.

"Are you perhaps active as a singer at LeTi in the future?"

"......"

"Your knowledge is something only a LeTi affiliate would know, and you even have the appearance this agency prefers..."

Eventually, it came to this.

"No, wait... I see. Are you going to join our debut team as the final member and debut soon? If we assume about 10 years have passed, the age range seems about right..."

"No."

It was so plausible it was driving me crazy. However, Judan nodded his head slightly with a meaningful look. He looked as if he were seeing a future member he was destined to meet.

"I understand. I'll keep that in mind."

"......"

*...Let's just not mention that I've experienced something similar before.*

I walked on in silence. Hmm, the gazes following me were probably because of my song choice earlier.

*Did I cross a line by singing a T-Holic solo song?*

It was not intentional, though. The truth is, I suddenly grabbed a mic and sang while walking down the street because I wanted to feel it for real.

*That I was Park Moondae.*

I felt like I needed that to properly come to my senses inside the System. To vividly embody the life I had lived for the past few years. To solidify my identity.

Moreover, I currently had no proof or connection to TeSTAR's Park Moondae. In the end, what I personally felt was what mattered.

And after doing it, it was definitely effective. I had convinced myself.

*That idol is me.*

I chuckled and clenched and unclenched my fist.

*Good.*

I was certain now. As long as I do not lose this feeling, I can keep my wits about me anywhere. Even inside the System.

Let's define it clearly right now.

*[Just because a Status Window pops up for me doesn't mean I'm the System.]*

The reason the Status Window appeared was quite human in the first place, was not it? I am talking about Keundal. The Status Window appeared because that guy wanted to help me live as an idol. He had been doing that for years.

*I can't just say my identity collapsed because I couldn't last a few damn months.*

I have to check that terminal inside the System with a clear mind, no matter what. My eyes flashed as I started walking again. Judan, finally calm, pointed to a nearby building.

Okay.

"It's over there. We'll be there soon..."

It was then.

"Excuse me, you're the one who was singing in the plaza earlier, right?"

"......"

Some person cut in with a bright, loud voice.

*That's the voice of a salesperson.*

Besides, it sounded a bit familiar. When I checked his face...

"Director."

"Oh, Woodan!"

"......"

It was a director from LeTi's rookie development team.

*Hey, no way.*

This person, who was making a fuss pretending to know Judan, feigned a question to him while his eyes sparkled at me.

"You know him? Ah, is this the relative you said was coming? Wow, you really can't fool bloodlines. How is your cousin so handsome too? I was so surprised. You sing so well, too..."

"......"

"Ah, it's still early... You haven't eaten yet, right? I was planning on buying Woodan a meal anyway, and there's a great restaurant right here."

"...Well."

"What do you say? It's right next door! To be honest, you sang so wonderfully earlier... You weren't nervous at all. As an audience member, I got way too invested! That's why I wanted to buy you a meal..."

"......"

It seemed my choice to sing a T-Holic song had a more potent aggro effect than I thought. Hmm.

*This could make things more natural, couldn't it?*

To dig up information, that is. After a quick calculation, I opened my mouth.

"Yes. Um... thank you."

"What are you talking about! I'm the one who's thankful."

The rookie development team director's face lit up. He quickly pointed to the so-called 'great restaurant', and our destination naturally changed.

Judan whispered.

"Don't tell me you calculated this far and sang that song?"

As if. I just grinned.

"Let's get going."

Judan looked at me with eyes that said he was re-evaluating me.

*It's not a scam. Not a scam.*

"This place is nice, right?"

"Yes. Thank you."

At that 'great restaurant', the director spent the meal loosening up the atmosphere with small talk while investigating my personal details, just as I expected.

"I see!"

Then, perhaps deciding I was suitable, he started his pitch right around when we were full and dessert was being served. It was the groundwork for a contract persuasion.

It mainly consisted of him pretending to be frank while listing praises about how attractive an agency LeTi realistically was. Naturally, the topic of their flagship artist was bound to come up.

VTIC.

"This LA concert also sold out really fast."

I observed the director's complexion as he spoke.

*He looks fine.*

Moreover, he brought it up without any hesitation. His lack of caution meant that no emergency notice regarding the members had been passed down to him either.

"Woodan's training period has been a bit long, but if he does well, he'll be rewarded for it..."

All sorts of information came from this man's mouth, from the trainee Judan to the disbandment of T-Holic. Seeing the various current realities that matched the information I had collected as a Status Window, a clear conclusion emerged.

*[Cheongryeo has not restarted yet.]*

This is still within his 100th restarted life. That guy is either still preparing to restart, or he might have run into another problem while trying to restart and put it on hold.

I quickly came to a conclusion.

*Then all that's left is to make contact.*

Now, if I just politely decline this director's scouting offer, send him on his way, and wait for the right moment to naturally bump into Cheongryeo...

"So, Gunwoo. Are you perhaps interested in... something other than singing?"

"......"

Hmm...

***

Cheongryeo lay quietly in a first-class seat on the airplane. The LA concert was over. However, he still had not restarted.

If he put his mind to it, it would be simple to break open the airplane window and jump out right now. He did not, however. Because he had eliminated the risk factor, there was no pressure to start at any particular time.

...Or, for no reason at all.

"......"

Lying in the first-class seat, which was almost like a private room, he thought.

*I'm glad I deleted it.*

It was a faint, yet sincere, sense of relief. He had to erase this useless influence before restarting.

However.

"......"

The habit of the past few months had been more excessive than necessary. A conversation partner with nothing to hide, always available to talk, inevitably fosters dependence. It was not good.

*...Help, huh.*

Cheongryeo muttered numbly.

At that moment.

[Why.]

"...!"

His hand gripped the armrest tightly. He immediately sat up and stared into the empty air at the bright blue pop-up.

[You said you were going to restart, so why are you still here?]

"......"

Help. Cheongryeo stared at it silently before slowly muttering to himself.

"When I restart should be up to me."

[I guess wanting to restart so badly that you tried to kill me was also up to you.]

[It's not like my death would be undone if you restarted.]

"Is this a hallucination I'm talking to now?"

[It's not a hallucination, it's real.]

[And if you're being sarcastic, saying I didn't die in the end, then you're a son of a bitch.]

Cheongryeo stared blankly at the pop-up. He read the hidden feeling between the lines.

It was anger.

[You definitely knew. You pressed it knowing I could die.]

[Isn't that why you drank the beer?]

"......"

Cheongryeo let out a long sigh. For some reason, he looked extremely tired. However, the pop-up relentlessly poured out its words.

[If you want to press it again, go ahead. Let's see what happens this time.]

"How did you come back?"

[I don't know either. Somehow...]

The pop-up flickered as if it were struggling slightly. It seemed to have taken some kind of hit, but the sentences were still relentless.

[Anyway, I recovered a bit and sent a message. Because I was pissed off.]

"......"

Cheongryeo looked at the pop-up expressionlessly. However, he did not display the suspicion, the barrage of questions, or the shameless composure that the sender of the pop-up had expected.

[That's all.]

Cheongryeo gave a slight nod at the last message.

"...Alright."

Suppressing a grumble like,*Is being calm after backstabbing someone an inborn trait?*, Park Moondae removed the pop-up. Just like that, the blue pop-up that had appeared before Cheongryeo's eyes vanished.

"......"

Help had returned. Cheongryeo silently deleted the red deletion recommendation window that had reappeared. He leaned back into his seat again.

He had to delete it. The problem was, he did not.

***

And at the same time.

"Gunwoo?"

"...Ah."

"Oh my, you must have dozed off while waiting~"

Park Moondae opened his eyes in Ryu Gunwoo's body. That was right. He had succeeded in accessing the System inside Cheongryeo while maintaining his own body!

*It actually works.*

[Hello!]

Just brushing past him as the trainees and staff were greeting each other with handshakes was enough. The balance was better than I had expected.

Although my body hurts like hell right now, and the burden of accessing the System is doubled...

*It feels like I have some leeway now.*

Including this. He smiled and shook hands with the director in front of him.

"Congratulations on the contract."

"Thank you."

He had even signed an aspiring artist contract with LeTi in his real body. Of course, being in his early 30s was an age that made it difficult to debut as an idol by societal standards. So he used two secret plans.

One was a career change.

"You really won't regret it. Our actor lineup is really solid this time."

He signed on as an aspiring rookie actor. This had been the director's scouting purpose from the beginning. He had been scouted for his composed attitude of singing in front of people without a single tremble and his appearance, rather than for Ryu Gunwoo's singing skills.

*It's not like I plan to actually debut here anyway.*

So this was enough, and the second.

"Being in your mid-20s is the perfect age!"

"...Yes."

You need a resident registration number to sign a contract, don't you? So... well. He had simply written his own number.

"Then, we'll be in your care, Ryu Gunwoo!"

This world's Ryu Gunwoo, that is. He sent a silent apology to the college student version of himself who was living in this reality.

*Sorry about that. Let's just borrow your identity for a bit.*
