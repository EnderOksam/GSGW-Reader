# Chapter 541

Shin Jaehyun lifted his head.

The snow-covered landscape of the DMZ was beautiful, regardless of the unexploded ordnance hidden beneath. He served outdoors against that backdrop less often than he expected. He was assigned to the command and control center.

Cheongryeo guessed they originally intended to assign him to guide visitors at Panmunjom because of his profession. He was likely excluded for the same reason, under the pretext of 'potential for incidents and accidents'.

And so, quiet and orderly days passed for what felt like the first time in decades. It was a novel experience.

People might think the show business industry is dynamic and fresh every day, but humans were creatures of adaptation. This was especially true for someone who repeated the same tasks for so long that everything became a manual.

A docile day with nothing to control or squeeze did not quite fit Cheongryeo's way of thinking. Still, this daily routine was quite interesting, if one excluded the drawback of not being with his dog.

Before long, that desire was also satisfied to some extent.

"Hello, Sunbae."

A visitor had arrived, bearing news of his dog, Kongi, in person.

Cheongryeo watched Park Moondae approach with a reluctant expression and smiled faintly.

***

"It's been a while."

I fell silent for a moment, looking at the person who came out to meet me. I had expected this, but it was the first time I had to go through such a fuss for entry clearance.

*Then again, this is pretty much the first time I've ever visited someone else.*

On top of that, this place only allowed visits on Sundays. I nearly ground my teeth trying to fit it into my schedule.

"You must've had a hard time getting here."

"Not at all."

From his tone, you would think I was the private. The guy even made a kind-sounding suggestion.

"How about a tour?"

"I'm fine."

I had no interest in a JSA museum tour guided by this guy. He did not press the issue, probably having just said it for the sake of it. We simply walked slowly through the quiet space and entered an equally quiet room.

"Have a seat."

It looked like a space often used for visits. However, besides me, there were no other visitors.

"There aren't many people here."

"Yes."

It would be strange if there were many civilians at the Armistice Line. I sat down first and took out what I had prepared.

"Here, take this."

It was a paw stamp, a dog's paw print debossed into clay. That guy's dog... Anyway, it seemed to have been made during an outdoor activity, but at least it was not a prohibited item.

The guy's expression softened slightly.

"Thank you."

He picked up the small, disc-shaped ornament with the hardened paw print and gently stroked its surface.

"......"

*Well, I guess it wasn't a bad thing to do.*

However, I had not come here just to show him the status of Cheongryeo's dog's paws. I could have just sent that in a text. It was time for business talk.

"By the way, I met with the T-Holic sunbaes."

"Yes."

"They said they collaborated with LeTi to establish a company."

"That's right."

Cheongryeo nonchalantly agreed as if it were nothing, then said with a smile, "Now we really won't have to worry about our schedules overlapping when we make a comeback. Haha."

"......"

*Right. This bastard was the one who led it.*

I had a suspicion, and sure enough, he was a relentless guy.*Does he just swallow up even his detestable old rivals if they can be of use?*

*He sure got a lot done before enlisting.*

Moreover, Cheongryeo was not finished.

"Hmm, I also created a small event in the process. You're already well aware of this, aren't you, Hubae?"

"...An event?"

"Yes. You saw it in the news articles."

It hit me like a lightning bolt.

*The article event.*

An incident involving both T-Holic and TeSTAR, one that would make the news...

[Aether Affiliates Allegedly Cursed Out TeSTAR?]

The 'TeSTAR recording' incident with the Aether staff.

"...You're saying the T-Holic sunbaes created that event?"

Cheongryeo did not answer and only smiled, but it was as good as an affirmation. I swallowed a groan.

*I thought a rival rookie group did it.*

So T-Holic was the one who set it off.

"That's right. It must have been a pleasant event for you too, Hubae."

"......"

If you were to ask whether TeSTAR benefited from that incident, we definitely did. It was a massive windfall. It felt like someone had suddenly shoved an ice-cold soda down my throat.

However, TeSTAR's gain was likely a side effect. From T-Holic's perspective, they had deliberately intended to turn Wonder Hall upside down.

*They diverted attention so that Wonder Hall couldn't pull any useless tricks on their independence.*

In other words, our schemes had somehow intertwined, and we both ended up profiting. Good grief.

*I feel like I've become part of the establishment.*

Wonder Hall was the one pulling the real corrupt bullshit, but these guys ganged up, wiped their mouths clean, and now it feels like I have been cast in a role I did not ask for. This guy seemed to be deliberately reminding me of 'TeSTAR's gains' to try and get something out of me.

So I responded rationally. I played dumb.

"I'm not sure what you're talking about."

"Ah, I'm sure you don't."

*Can he not grin like that, as if he's letting me off the hook?*

Surprisingly, Cheongryeo quickly changed his tone.

"But I doubt you came all this way just to talk about this."

"......"

"It seems you came because you have something to ask. Go ahead."

*Right. I have a purpose, and I have a lot to ask.*

First, I was curious how this guy could still so naturally use that infuriating tone of his even after completing basic training. No, he had probably spoken like that for at least a few decades, so it was natural it would not change after just a month or two.

I realized such atmosphere-setting questions were unnecessary and decided to just throw a straight pitch. Let's get straight to it.

"I believe I mentioned the System fragments last time."

"Mm."

I did not hesitate much to bring this up. The story was so absurd that anyone who overheard would just assume we were talking about a game. Still, as a courtesy, I lowered my voice, and he immediately gave feedback.

"Speak comfortably. There are CCTVs, but no recording devices, and no one is listening."

He would not be lying, or we would both be screwed. In that case.

"Alright."

I immediately dropped the formal speech and continued the story related to these System fragments. I explained how having a fragment caused me to fail the building collapse mission, and how I used the remaining fragment to create the company System. I summarized the story I had already explained to fit the flow of the conversation.

And the conclusion is this.

*[You probably have one of those time-bomb-like fragments too.]*

The guy, who had been smirking when I dropped the formalities, became expressionless as the story progressed. Finally, he retorted.

"So?"

"I'm saying I'm going to collect the fragment you have right now."

"......"

"It's possible now that the conditions have been met."

If I did not do it now, there was no telling when the fragment this guy had would also blow up from a mission failure.

*And for this guy, who doesn't have a Status Window, there's a pretty high chance that day would be the last day of his life. This guy wouldn't want to die a sudden, absurd death without even making a comeback, would he? Honestly, this is a proposal where I'm getting the short end of the stick.*

However, Cheongryeo frowned.

"It feels like we're being led."

What?

"Raising the grade, giving missions... This doesn't seem any different from what we've been doing."

"......"

"Think carefully. I must have told you several times."

Don't use the System. Well, I agreed with that.

"But we'll die if we don't."

"That's the same too."

"Right."

I sighed.

"So shouldn't we be avoiding the 'not doing it' option even more?"

"......"

We had already witnessed and experienced just how screwed a person can get. Since we knew, we should use the next best option. Between people who know what happens if you do not deal with the System's status abnormalities or missions, not many more words should be necessary.

And I had prepared a decent countermeasure.

*Logically speaking... STier, Memory whatever, those kinds of singularities won't happen again. As long as the System's operating principle is 'make him use me more', and I'm directly controlling that part through regular use, it won't trouble me with something like a 'System Update'.*

And most importantly, I would turn off the System as soon as I got it.

"So you'll unilaterally bear the risk?"

"Not exactly."

I clasped my hands together.

"I need you to make a few promises."

"Go on."

"First," I said seriously. "Do not refer to ORBIT with words like 'the LeTi family'."

"......"

"Of course, similar words like family, housemates, or little brothers are all forbidden."

The next one was also important.

"Second, do not make a group appearance on a T-Holic variety show."

"I don't know what you mean."

"There's a reason."

No matter how I thought about it, it seemed like something those guys would actually push for, given their personalities. I continued, looking at the guy who seemed a bit dumbfounded.

"Last... if something happens to me after I retrieve this, you will cooperate fully in resolving it."

"......"

This was only natural as a matter of human decency, and, based on past experience, I knew how this guy would react.

"Alright."

That's right. I immediately nodded.

"On the condition that you stop using the System the moment you retrieve this so-called fragment."

"Of course."

Even if he had not said it, I was already prepared to turn it into an inventory item right away. I nodded and called up the Status Window.

"Then I'll do it."

"Go ahead."

Is it because the situation is similar? I was reminded of the time I tried to delete the System on this guy's private jet. However, there was also a clear difference. Back then, this guy was so interested that he provided a private jet to watch.

But now, neither of the two guys here had any interest. We were forced to press this button to prevent the worst-case scenario.

*Hoo.*

"What's your wish?"

"Hmm?"

"It's a necessary procedure."

"Hmm."

The guy seemed to think for a moment, then said, "I'd like it if you took good care of Kongi."

"......"

*Well... it's better than a wish like 'I hope TeSTAR fails'.*

As if it were a matter of course, a pop-up appeared before my eyes.

['■■■ Fragment' Holder Confirmed!]

[❬Wish❭ Registered]

As expected. Cheongryeo had a fragment.

*Should I be glad it reacted to a wish like this?*

Thanks to having already met all the company grade criteria, the result pop-up followed immediately without any complex base process.

['Fragment of ■■■' Holder Reconfirmed]

Absorb '■■■ Fragment' into 'For Business Use'?

※This will take some time.

So far, so good.

"Can you see it?"

"I can."

After confirming there were no issues, I immediately responded to the Status Window.

*Absorb.*

Instantly, a stream of light from the pop-up cheerfully circled Cheongryeo once before returning. Cheongryeo watched the phenomenon with cool eyes.

['■■■ Fragment' Retrieval Complete!]

This too was as expected.

*Hoo.*

I let out a short sigh of relief. However, what appeared next was something that had never appeared before.

['■■■ Fragment' (4 / 4) Entirety Confirmed]

[■■■ Complete]

As if it were being peeled away, the black paint on '■■■' came off, revealing its true name.

[System Complete]

And the explanation that appeared below.

[Reboot Initiated]

***

Cha Eugene lifted his head.

He was the kind of person who did not think much of having rescued a stray cat from a fire in Spain just a few weeks ago. He only used it as a reason to protest when Park Moondae, who had given the contradictory assessment of 'Good job, but it was dangerous', served him a low-sodium meal.

*Well, it wasn't like it was inedible.*

How nice it was to be in an environment where he could whine a little. He hummed as he opened the refrigerator, then spotted Kim Raebin drinking apple juice at the table.

"Did Moondae-hyung make that for you?"

"That's right!"

Cha Eugene felt a little disappointed, but he quickly shook it off.

*I'll just ask him to make me some!*

"Where did hyung go?"

"He went out to meet a friend. He looked busy, so you shouldn't bother him with troublesome requests..."

"OK!"

Before the interrupted Kim Raebin could chase after him in a rage, Cha Eugene slipped into his room, snickering as he flopped onto his bed. Then, he suddenly felt a strange sensation.

*What is this?*

Something unknown was bothering him. He was quite positive and even a bit optimistic, but he was not out of touch with reality. Right now, there should not be anything weighing on his mind.

*Everything is satisfactory, but why do I feel so anxious?*

If he could have observed Park Moondae's mind from a location in the JSA 50km away, he would have known the answer.

[Son of a bitch!]

Park Moondae was currently experiencing his world shattering once again. The System was exploding.

...No, that's not it!

*It's me.*

The one exploding was Park Moondae himself.

He struggled to hold on, but his consciousness began to crumble, as if it were being 'pulled out' in a mental sense. Some kind of non-standard problem had occurred.

Without delay, Park Moondae called for the helper who was on standby for emergencies.

[Keundal!]

However, there was no response.

Park Moondae repeated every curse he knew, trying to maintain his consciousness. However, the situation was already out of control. It felt as if his mind was being stretched out like taffy.

[Reboot Initiated]

Even the Status Window grew faint and distorted.

[...!]

For a moment, it felt like the person sitting across from him grabbed his arm, but that sensation soon slipped away like grains of sand. The area around the Status Window also distorted, the colors changing rapidly with a whoosh, as if being sucked in. It was like looking out the window of an impossibly fast high-speed train.

*Fuck.*

Park Moondae cursed again, struggling to regain control.

*Stop!*

He had the absurd feeling of trying to stop a running train by pulling it with his bare hands, but in that instant.

[-]

As if by a lie, he barely managed to get a grip on the situation. Finally, his vision stabilized, and he was looking at a static world.

[Gasp!]

Amidst the taut tension, where it felt like his consciousness would be sucked away again if he let his guard down for a second, he saw it.

*Where am I?*

It was night. Outdoors. A rooftop.

...A person.

On a cool Winter Night, a figure stood before his eyes on a rooftop, leaning against the railing and gazing blankly down at the buildings below.

"......"

A familiar man in a coat.

Cheongryeo.

"......"

Park Moondae reflexively recalled the scene he had seen several years ago, the memory of Cheongryeo's first restart, which he had received as a reward for clearing the 'status abnormality'.

*It's the same place.*

It felt as if his consciousness was barely clinging to that memory. However, he also realized the difference from the memory with an almost animalistic instinct.

*There's no cigarette.*

The cigarette Cheongryeo had been smoking back then was nowhere to be seen in the hands of the figure before him now, and the electronic billboard that the Cheongryeo in the memory had looked at while recalling, 'VTIC's ad used to be here, but it isn't anymore'.

There.

[ with VTIC]

A VTIC ad was still displayed. Brilliant colors flashed from the billboard. This was a point in time when his group had not yet failed. To be precise, a point within one of the 'repeated restarts'.

[......]

Park Moondae asked himself.

Just which 'number' Cheongryeo is this?

And...

*Why am I here?*

What in the world just happened?
