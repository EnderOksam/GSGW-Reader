# Chapter 540

This was an alley in Barcelona, Spain.

KYAHAAHA!

The foreign scenery was filled with the sounds of children running and adults walking about busily.

We were lost.

"......"

"......"

Confused? I was too.

The reason was simple. Seon Ahyeon and I were suddenly standing blankly in the middle of Spain.

To explain this, I had to go back to when we first arrived.

[Ooooh! Barcelona is a really cool city...]

[We have to get churros!]

[......]

At Cha Eugene's strong insistence, we were about to go to a famous churro place.

[...Where did he go?]

[Cha Eugene!!]

While everyone panicked and ran around after Cha Eugene suddenly vanished from an alley, we ended up separated from the production crew as well.

*How can something like this happen?*

To think we'd vanish too while trying to find Cha Eugene. I resisted the urge to ruffle my hair in frustration.

This was the first time in my decades of life that I had ever been a lost child.

Of course, there was no reason for two grown adults to lose their cool. I calmly said, "First... a taxi."

Then I realized.

*We can't.*

We had no money.

According to the variety show's concept, the production crew held the entire budget. We earned our allowance through busking missions.

Even that allowance was divided. Ryu Chungwoo held the operating funds, and Bae Sejin had the emergency funds.

In other words, we had...

"...Enough to ride the bus once."

"...I-I see."

That was all the cash we had left.

*Smartphone payment services won't work overseas.*

Wait a minute.

I reached into my pocket and realized a fatal fact. I didn't even have my smartphone.

I had left it with our manager to avoid being caught on camera using it during filming.

Seon Ahyeon rarely used his smartphone, so he kept it in his bag.

"......"

So now, two foreigners who couldn't speak the language were stranded in a foreign country without any money.

*This is driving me crazy.*

The moment we had to transfer buses, we'd be penniless and have to contact the embassy. What an absurd situation.

Seon Ahyeon must have realized the situation. His face paled, and he shot to his feet.

"I'll, I'll try talking... to other people...!"

"......"

*What's with his confidence? He only speaks English and French.*

Of course, he might get his point across with English words and gestures, but that would just draw attention.

*It'll be a problem if there's a miscommunication.*

We couldn't forget that we're celebrities. Who knew what kind of controversy could arise if we were led to the wrong place?

I suggested a simpler method, the first rule for lost children.

"Let's sit down and talk."

We should wait for our group so we don't needlessly miss each other.

"O-Okay..."

I sat on a nearby bench in an alley next to the plaza. Seon Ahyeon quickly followed and sat beside me.

My mind calmed down a little.

*It's been a while since I've had time like this.*

It felt like years since I had last sat leisurely in a wide-open outdoor space during the day, just watching people.

Even if it was because we were lost.

*It's not bad.*

I quietly shut my mouth and looked at the plaza.

"It feels like it's been a while... since we've been able to walk around outdoors like this."

Seon Ahyeon's expression softened as well.

However, he soon seemed to realize something and quickly added in a fluster, "Of course, being able to work hard is something I'm grateful for and enjoy...!"

"Right."

It just wasn't bad to enjoy things like this once in a while.

When I nodded, Seon Ahyeon seemed slightly relieved. The atmosphere returned to people-watching.

*Hmm.*

Speaking of activities, something did come to mind.

"Seon Ahyeon."

"......"

"Do you have any desire to go solo?"

Seon Ahyeon looked like he was about to jump off the bench.

"M-Me...?!"

"......"

Even if we postponed enlistment as long as possible with the Order of Cultural Merit to manage our peak, we couldn't avoid military service itself.

*Those who have to go, have to go.*

To maintain public opinion in Korea, there was no other option.

Naturally, it would be more efficient to do solo or unit promotions around that time.

It was a reality that would come within a few years.

However, seeing Seon Ahyeon's reaction, it seemed this guy had never even considered going solo.

He seemed lost in thought for a moment, then shook his head seriously.

"For now, I... prefer promoting with the members."

"Is that so."

"......"

*I wonder if he'd faint if he knew that Kim Raebin already produced a demo version of a solo song for him.*

*To lower the entry barrier, this guy should probably start with a unit.*

I let out a small laugh and nodded.

"I'm more inclined toward team activities too."

"...Yeah!"

Seon Ahyeon nodded, looking quite happy.

Even though our most recent team activity was dressing up as Christmas trees on the street to sell stamps.

It was even a B-grade costume.

The dangling light bulbs singing an a cappella carol felt less sacred and more like a commercial gimmick to sell stamps.

It was disastrous.

*...To think we all had to lose the mini-game together.*

Seven people, no, seven glittering, moving trees. It was such a spectacle that more people gathered to watch than ever before.

The production crew's mission was 'Be cheered on by at least 3 children'. After a desperate argument that all minors are children, TeSTAR was deemed successful.

[If you're not an adult, you're a child! Wait. Surely you're not saying, PD, that a minor is an adult...?]

[Wow, you're really twisting this.]

Because we had earned money for food, the suggestion to buy churros passed without any trouble.

*Cha Eugene...*

My eyebrow twitched.

I didn't know why Cha Eugene ran off, but the moment he was caught and the cameras were off, he would be living on a healthy, low-sodium diet for the rest of the tour.

*You can eat late-night snacks in your dreams.*

I was a little worried about the other members and crew, but considering who was left, I figured they would be fine.

*Only the calm ones are left.*

They were the type who could find us.

I stretched my legs and leaned back against the bench again.

In reality, the December weather was cold, and we were lost.

It was supposed to be a gourmet trip, and we didn't even have a snack in our hands, but I honestly didn't feel too bad.

*Is it because I've worn so many weird costumes?*

This was a walk in the park by comparison.

How long had we been sitting there?

"Hey, guys!"

"......"

A familiar Korean voice called out.

Turning my head, I saw a figure running from the distance, waving his hand.

"Hyung...!"

It was Ryu Chungwoo.

Rarely out of breath, he ran over to us in one go.

"Are you both okay?"

"Yes."

"Yes!"

Staying put was the right answer. The situation was over.

I gladly got up from the bench to greet him.

However...

"But Hyung, where are the others?"

"Ah."

"......"

That ominous sound made me look at Ryu Chungwoo's face. He glanced at the empty space behind him and laughed awkwardly.

"While looking for you guys... I think I got separated."

"......"

"Th-Then..."

Seon Ahyeon looked like he was about to say, 'Isn't that just one more lost child?'

I held back a sigh.

*Still, three is better than two.*

Besides, this guy had the most important thing.

Money.

"...Still, thanks to you, Hyung, we can take a taxi or something. If the production crew can't find us."

"Yeah, that should work. Before that..."

Ryu Chungwoo smiled brightly.

"You guys should eat something first. You've been out in the cold for a while."

"......"

Seon Ahyeon was touched, but an idea flashed through my mind.

*Something to eat?*

Wait a minute.

Could Cha Eugene's destination be...

***

Next to the plaza.

"Gracias!"

The famous churro place.

Standing before the shop, which reeked of cinnamon, I nodded.

"I think he'll come here."

Unlike us, Cha Eugene was a native Spanish speaker and would have no trouble finding his way.

So, whatever his reason for running off, he would most likely head to the original destination after achieving his goal.

"That's a good guess, and it's perfect for you guys to eat, too."

"Mm."

It was better to be on the street so the people looking for us could spot us. This was better than food you had to eat inside somewhere.

I gave a slight nod, and Ryu Chungwoo smiled as he approached the shop.

Come to think of it, his coat seemed a bit thin for this weather.

"Aren't you cold?"

"I'm fine. I did a bit of running."

Replying lightly, he took a banknote from a lucky pouch-like bag labeled ❬Allowance❭ that the production crew had given him.

Then he placed the order.

"Esto, por favor."

"......"

To my surprise, Ryu Chungwoo spoke Spanish.

*What the.*

Perhaps feeling my gaze, he spoke awkwardly.

"I'm not good at it, but I remembered some that I learned before."

"......"

He must have been referring to his STier days.

"It comes in handy like this sometimes."

As he said that, he gave a wry smile, as if the thought was new to him.

Ryu Chungwoo seemed to be reminiscing. The strange rejection he once felt toward his 'STier self' had clearly subsided.

While waiting for the churros, he even spoke again.

"...Come to think of it, Eugene did sometimes leave without a word during breaks."

The current situation must have reminded him of it.

"...I guess he did it because he was struggling."

At that moment, Seon Ahyeon replied.

"But, the Eugene now... I think if he's struggling, he'd tell us directly."

"...!"

Ryu Chungwoo was surprised for a moment, but then he grinned.

"You're right. That's why I don't think he'd suddenly leave his spot now."

It was a pleasantly reassuring response.

Seon Ahyeon also smiled quickly and nodded.

The trust in the group and its members, which had been running smoothly for several years, could be felt in their conversation.

It made me feel a bit embarrassed for just planning Cha Eugene's low-sodium diet.

"Moondae, you think so too, right...?"

"......"

I leaned lightly against the wall.

Well... if I were to be a bit more generous, I could assume that Cha Eugene must have had a good reason.

And going a step further, I could even think about his future.

"In that sense. Since this happened during filming, the production crew must be recording it..."

*Let's see, if this gets filmed and broadcast...*

[Cha Eugene is the type of person I hate to see in a group setting]

[Ah, I'm already pissed off]

[Honestly, they'll edit it to be funny, but isn't this a serious issue?]

[Yeah, if Cha Eugene is going to keep acting out, cancel his contract renewal 🙏]

It would be a fiery hell.

"Let's just pretend it never happened."

"......"

Both Ryu Chungwoo and Seon Ahyeon quickly nodded without any objection.

So, assuming the footage would be scrapped, we each held a warm churro and enjoyed a snack in the alley.

Around the time we were halfway through our churros.

"Ohhhh!!"

"Hey!"

Amazingly, Cha Eugene appeared from around the corner of the alley as if he had smelled them.

My prediction was spot on.

*Finally.*

*First, I'll tell him to forget about eating any churros.*

However, the moment I focused my gaze, I realized Cha Eugene's appearance was different.

His hair and face were grimy, and he was holding a bundle of dark red fur.

*What is that.*

Cha Eugene adjusted his hold on what was in his arms.

It was... a cat.

"There was a fire over there! I rescued this cat."

"......"

And behind him in the alley.

Among the panting production crew and members who had chased after Cha Eugene, Bae Sejin pointed a finger at him.

"...He saw a building on fire and ran toward it..."

"......"

[My baby! Thank you so much!]

...And so, Cha Eugene received the cat owner's thanks and got to eat a free churro.

Cha Eugene achieved the astonishing feat of being featured in the local newspaper. The headline read 'The Christmas Tree Who Saved a Cat'. The entire incident was successfully edited into an episode for the variety show.

[TeSTAR's Tigercat Rescues a Cat on His Way to a Famous Restaurant... | ❬TeStar's Not-So-Lonely Gourmet Trip ❭ EP. 4]

Less than an hour after the video was uploaded, funny parts were screenshotted, and summaries circulated on social media and online communities.

Naturally, since it was a dramatic episode that would be difficult to stage intentionally, it garnered a huge amount of attention.

Using that attention, an article about the tour selling out was also released at the perfect time.

[Company Rating (B+) → Company Rating (A-)]

Upgrade successful!

The company's rating had finally gone up.

What this meant was also simple.

[Mission Accomplished!]

Mission: Achieve A- Rating for ❬Stars Orbit Entertainment❭

['Fragment of ■■■' can be retrieved]

It was time to go meet Cheongryeo.

To retrieve the System fragment.

However, there was one peculiar thing...

[VTIC's Cheongryeo, assignment to JSA unit complete... "Can we see him if we go to Panmunjom?"]

JSA. The Joint Security Area.

That guy Cheongryeo was assigned to Panmunjom, the northernmost point of our country.

Right in front of the Armistice Line.

"......"

*First of all... I guess I can't bring the dog.*
