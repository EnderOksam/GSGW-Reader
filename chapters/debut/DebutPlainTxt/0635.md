# Chapter 635

The summer was scorching.

[TeSTAR #1]

[Whoa]

[Go look at the charts right now]

[They're #1 on the Hot 100, this is insane]

[The 200 is climbing back up too, this is crazy]

News of their number-one spot spread like wildfire.

As the fourth week of their remix promotions began, TeSTAR naturally reduced their broadcast appearances. However, the prestige of the shows they did attend soared to new heights.

Even when they weren't on screen, TeSTAR and their remix track were mentioned everywhere.

[Car companies suddenly started promoting on social media, saying a secret agent needs a cool carㅋㅋㅋ (This is about TeSTAR)]

[Lately, every viral marketing campaign seems to be sneakily using TeSTAR's outfits]

[A piercing company copied their in-ear concept and had to post an apology LMAO hahahahahahaha]

TeSTAR itself had become a tool for going viral. They were no longer just gaining buzz. They had become the buzz itself. The internet was more excited about this situation than the members were.

In crude terms, it was a high from their success.

TeSTAR hit number one with scores that didn't fall behind in any category, from album sales and streaming to radio airplay and reviews.

[There have been cases where a single song suddenly became a meme and blew up, but isn't this a first?]

[These are the kind of standard results you'd expect from a local artist;;]

[All the English articles are incredibly favorable]

Amidst all the reactions, stories, soaring rankings, and recognition, Ryu Seojin, the twin fansite master for Park Moondae and Lee Sejin, finally had to admit it.

"They have no weaknesses."

TeSTAR had ultimately broken through their peak.

They satisfied the heavenly condition of achieving great results both domestically and internationally. They also achieved a perfect balance between their fandom and public appeal.

"The promotions themselves went so well..."

The song, the performance, and the artist. All three elements had fallen perfectly into place. A group that seemed to have no higher to climb had broken through the final ceiling.

Such an unconventional achievement was bound to attract attention, even from those who weren't interested. It was even more so for someone who had watched from the very beginning.

[It'll be hard to film variety shows with them now.]

She recalled what her older sister had said. That was the common sentiment among people who had worked with TeSTAR until now. They would express disappointment while subtly showing off their connection. There was also a hint of pride, boasting about their own foresight in making a successful investment.

And...

[Well, they were the kind of kids who would have made it no matter what they did.]

A strange sense of support.

The twin fansite master twisted her lip.

*Even though they tried to kill off one of the members with malicious editing before their debut.*

But funnily enough, she understood that sentiment to some extent.

The twin fansite master watched the TeSTAR video playing before her. TeSTAR still diligently uploaded behind-the-scenes and personal videos to their channel and social media.

HAHAHA!

[Hyung, you can't draw!]

The shaky screen, seemingly filmed by a member on a smartphone, showed a drawing made with a chocolate pen. On the border of a whipped cream cake, the animals symbolizing TeSTAR were drawn with what seemed to be great care.

[Wow, honestly, it was a good thing we decorated this anniversary cake ourselves.]

[That's right. The cakes Moondae makes are delicious, but his drawings are weird.]

[Eugene is brave. Saying something like that to the one in charge of snacks...]

[(!!!!!)]

[Noooo!! I take it back! I take it back! Moondae, you're a great artist!]

[This is the first time I've seen Cha Eugene look so desperate.]

HAHAHA!

Amidst the casual jokes and laughter, large hands moved busily, shakily squeezing out letters with a small chocolate pen. Words began to form on the whipped cream.

The phrase was 'We love you this year too'.

[Ta-da!]

[Ah, ah, don't throw it at my face! Protect our TeSTAR animals!]

[Careful, careful!]

The video was filmed on June 18th. It showed them chuckling as they held their own small celebration for their debut anniversary this year. Ryu Chungwoo, who was on hiatus at the time, was occasionally captured on screen, sitting quietly in a corner with a smile. They had clearly gone out of their way to meet up with Ryu Chungwoo.

[Should we... all make a wish together?]

[Sounds good!]

They were always the same. However, the comments on this video overflowed with foreign languages, more so than during any other promotion period. It was a characteristic of TeSTAR's recently uploaded content.

"......"

The twin fansite master thought to herself. Objectively, if you wanted to see them often in Korea, this wasn't exactly a welcome situation.

*They've become too big to see often.*

It wasn't just that their reputation had grown overseas. Their entire status had changed. It was obvious that once they blew up this big, they would stop appearing on most programs to reduce image consumption and further increase their value. Even if they wanted to, their company would persuade them to do so.

*Their annual plan must have changed.*

It might be good for them, but in the long run, wasn't it a disappointing direction for their domestic fans? Even before, TeSTAR was an undisputed top-tier group in Korea. They were the type of guys who would try to do something on their own even after their military service.

*They wouldn't have had to suffer because of their results anyway...*

In that case, it would have been better for existing fans if they hadn't released this album at the same time as VTIC. They could have just maintained their reputation as a "top-tier group."

[Hmm... a wish.]

On the screen, Ryu Chungwoo looked at the cake and said quietly, [We have a big concert coming up soon. I hope everyone stays safe and uninjured, and that we can all put on a performance as good as we want it to be.]

"......"

[Let's do everything we want to do, without regrets.]

Didn't she already know the result of that wish? She knew the final outcome, achieved through the consistent sincerity, effort, and skill they had built up until now. Watching TeSTAR's daily life with that knowledge made one's heart swell.

[Happy birthday, TeSTAR!]

Confetti poppers went off. Laughter and cake were passed around.

[Ack!]

[It's all over me!]

[Ah, if this (cream) gets on your face, you have to scrub it off hard.]

[Ppak... Ppak-Ppak Moondae.]

"......"

BWAHAHAHA!

Seeing TeSTAR, who were still the same as ever. Seeing Lee Sejin in the video where they reacted in real-time to the news of their Billboard #1 spot.

[Thank you. Really, thank you so much...]

That scene just purely made one feel good. Even herself.

*This is ridiculous.*

The twin fansite master scoffed and pressed her forehead. To think that someone else's life succeeding on the other side of a screen could affect one's mood like this. This was why being an idol fan seemed so irrational and funny. The fact that she had no intention of quitting made it even more so.

"Time to edit some photos."

[2X0723 Performance #Lee Sejin #Park Moondae]

The twin fansite master neatly divided the cuts of the fancam data she had bought in advance. She then uploaded the two people she supported. As she repeated the task, her needlessly fluttering heart gradually calmed down.

Then, a quoted post caught her eye.

Ding.

[The person who always takes good pictures of Moondae.. 🥲]

It was the private, personal account of a well-known fansite master who had been taking pictures of Park Moondae since his debut. Her posts often appeared on her timeline. Originally, she was someone she couldn't understand because their principles as fansite masters were so different, so she felt neither favorable nor unfavorable towards her.

"......"

She silently liked the post with her personal account. It felt okay to do so now.

*The timing just feels right.*

Of course, with many new foreign fans joining the fandom, new incidents and troubles were bound to erupt. They would take responsibility for her future stomachaches and frustrations. However, until then, she decided to enjoy this atmosphere of harmony and success.

The time of harvest.

She leisurely browsed social media. She observed the surprisingly strong camaraderie among the domestic fandom and the old fans kindly providing information to the newly joined international fans.

Then, uncharacteristically, she decided to contact a junior of hers. The data seller who was famous for taking good photos.

[Ryu Gunwoo, are you still taking pictures of TeSTAR these days? If you have any leftover from before, let me know.]

[I'll give you the best price.]

The guy had always had a keen eye for idols. It wouldn't be strange if he had seen its potential to skyrocket and held onto the data.

Although the message she sent was clearly marked as read, the reply came quite a while later.

[Sorry. I've been busy lately, so I haven't been able to take many pictures.]

"......"

She had clearly seen him at an awards ceremony after work, wearing a suit and holding a telephoto lens.

However, before she could probe further to see who had offered him a better price, the next message arrived.

[I'm still cheering for TeSTAR.]

"......"

To think she would see the day this cold, heartless price demon would say he was cheering for an idol.

*Is it because time has passed, or is this really TeSTAR's time to succeed no matter what?*

Or maybe, she had misunderstood this junior of hers since college. In any case, she sent a reply.

[Alright. Take care.]

It was truly a strange season.

Feeling much more relaxed, she tapped her mouse a few times and looked at social media again.

[I love TeSTAR. Please give me many pictures. ❤️]

Clumsy Korean.

*The influx of foreigners...*

She wanted to check this in a quantifiable way.

*Wait a minute.*

A thought suddenly occurred to her, and she stopped browsing social media. Instead, she went to a site that aggregated album sales. Additional album sales during this period were the most reliable indicator of a significant influx of casual fans who were on the verge of becoming part of the core fandom.

*In the age of streaming, buying an album shows the potential to spend money.*

So, looking at TeSTAR's current sales figures...

[Sales: 79,627]

"...!"

An astonishing number was displayed. Almost 80,000 copies. That was close to a single day's sales during the first week for most groups. Since the first-week sales period was already over, not many people seemed to be paying attention.

*No way.*

The twin fansite master's hands flew across the keyboard, using shortcuts to quickly switch screens. She downloaded the data for the two groups, whose release dates were only a few days apart, into Excel. She applied a date correction and sorted it.

The first thing she looked at was the first-week sales. The first week after release.

[VTIC | Non-Cryptid (Full Album) | 2,931,713]

[TeSTAR | Badtime (Full Album) | 2,083,375]

A damning difference.

*Fuck.*

The curse escaped her lips again just looking at it. Although they didn't know at the time, it was later revealed that TeSTAR had actually lost out because two or three overseas group orders were delayed due to shipping and system issues. They had been so focused on surpassing two million copies that they were late in raising suspicions.

*I wondered what the point was.*

The first-week sales were over, and even including those, the difference was nearly 700,000 copies. The twin fansite master's eyebrows twitched as she turned the page.

Swish.

After that gap of several hundred thousand in the first week, their daily sales seemed similar, but VTIC actually had a slight edge. However, at some point, TeSTAR's sales began to spike.

[VTIC | 12,721]

[TeSTAR | 20,274]

It was when TeSTAR's Glastonbury performance blew up.

*This is where the meaningful influx began...*

Still, it wasn't a huge difference. It was just a degree to which the buzz was indirectly reflected.

*There was some reaction when Attraction came out, too.*

However, in July, TeSTAR's momentum, which had been fluctuating, suddenly began to sprint forward. It was right as summer began.

[TeSTAR | 45,261]

[TeSTAR | 81,563]

[TeSTAR | 50,171]

[TeSTAR | 62,872]

After starting their remix promotions, TeSTAR's album sales began to draw an upward curve again.

*The graph shot right through.*

Because they continuously updated the existing album USB with remix promotion songs and concept photos, people who came in through the remix concept all ended up buying the original album.

*They even sold it as a set with decorative goods to customize the USB with the remix concept.*

Even existing fans with some spare cash in their bank accounts were tempted to buy one or two more out of pure purchasing impulse. However, no one paid attention to the album sales figures because of that. The first-week sales period was over, after all. It was meaningless.

But now.

When you looked at the cumulative album sales with your own eyes...

[TeSTAR | Badtime (Full Album) | 3,428,611]

[VTIC | Non-Cryptid (Full Album) | 3,387,942]

"......"

The result was reversed.

The twin fansite master quietly released the mouse with a trembling hand.

The long, long season of the simultaneous release.

[VTIC took the album sales, and TeSTAR took the digital music sales.]

Contrary to the thoughts of people who had reached that conclusion in just two weeks, TeSTAR had fiercely run and changed the field over a long summer. They had finally refuted that sentence.

TeSTAR had ultimately won.
