# Chapter 564

After calling for Help, Cheongryeo faced the Incarnation of an Idol and waited for a response in the deep silence.

A 'response' finally appeared in his vision, but it was unexpected.

[■■■■■■■■■■■]

The hologram window, which looked like Help's chat pop-up, flickered. It glitched erratically before disappearing, as if it had just passed him by.

"Hel-"

Cheongryeo called for Help again.

[Wait.]"

The Incarnation of an Idol declared.

Cheongryeo had no reason to listen. He ignored the command and tried to call again.

THUMP.

An unfamiliar sound made both figures turn their heads. The front door had opened.

A person emerged from the inner door beyond the hallway, into the officetel where the floor had become a black surface of water.

"...!"

A black-haired figure appeared. He was a tall, thin man with handsome features. It was a familiar face.

He was the person living in this officetel, the same one who had just collapsed on the table before Cheongryeo. He was the debut actor, Ryu Gunwoo.

He was the person Cheongryeo had just called.

"......"

The man turned his head and looked at Cheongryeo.

Cheongryeo realized his impression was different. His eyes were gentle.

The man no longer looked like he could suffocate from fatigue, his glimpse of cold reason gone. Instead, a man with a gentle and wholesome impression walked briskly across the entryway and down the hall.

He seemed like a completely different person.

"......"

Just then, a firm, pleasant voice cut in.

[Not that one, this one.]

Behind the black-haired man, another person opened the door and entered. Brightly dyed hair swayed as he crossed the threshold.

It was a blond man with a boyish impression.

Park Moondae smiled.

[You called?]

A cool smile layered over his familiar, dry expression.

From his movements and way of speaking, Cheongryeo realized instinctively.

*This one is Help.*

[Your appearance has returned.]

[Yeah.]

Park Moondae omitted the thought.*Isn't this just a mental world you created with your GM ability? So a form you're familiar with was just implemented?*

Instead, he beckoned to the Incarnation of an Idol with his finger.

[It's all over.]

[......]

The System would soon go further into the past, and the GM ability would disappear. Above all, Park Moondae himself had desperately gritted his teeth and endured for this moment.

He couldn't delay any longer.

[Let's go back.]

The Incarnation of an Idol, Shin Jaehyun, smirked.

[You took the words right out of my mouth.]

He walked away without any attachment. Parabolas spread across the water's surface with his every step, reaching the tips of Cheongryeo's feet.

"......"

Cheongryeo watched the scene blankly, then opened his mouth. He asked the Help who had appeared in an unfamiliar form a fundamental question.

"Who are you?"

Park Moondae looked at Cheongryeo.

[Hyung. Our time is...]

[I know.]

There was no time to explain the situation, but he could leave him with these words.

He grinned.

[A member of your rival group.]

*You'll be meeting soon.*

Then he turned and walked toward the front door.

Watching him move, Cheongryeo noticed his attire for the first time. He wore flashy accessories, long boots, and perfectly styled blond hair. His outfit, a white shirt with glossy black leather pants, was also familiar to Cheongryeo.

Stage outfits.

*Ah.*

Cheongryeo suddenly realized.

It was an idol's attire.

[See you again.]

And so, the three figures opened the front door side by side and left the past.

"Hoo."

Park Moondae let out a breath. He had returned through the black front door to the officetel in the mental world, where the fantastical sunset of ❬Magical Boy❭ spread out.

He heard Shin Jaehyun murmur.

"Too bad, but he won't remember your rival declaration, junior. I erased the information."

"...From where to where."

"Junior, well... everything related to 'Help'."

He had used his GM authority one last time to erase his own past memories.

Shin Jaehyun's choice was reasonable. Too many insane situations had occurred to just leave things as they were.

*Still, this is probably just a virtual world or data within the System anyway.*

Park Moondae inadvertently said it out loud.

Surprisingly, he received a reply.

"Hm? No. It was the past."

"......"

"So, it's better that I erased my own memories rather than letting unnecessary variables arise, right?"

Park Moondae stopped in his tracks, shocked.

*It was the real past? Meeting Cheongryeo, meeting Judan, and even debuting as an actor with a ridiculous stage name, all of that really happened?*

*Good grief.*

His head was spinning.

He soon started walking again calmly. Whatever he had done, it was all erased from Cheongryeo's memory anyway.

*Besides, if he restarts, everything I did will be undone.*

It felt a little futile, but he was also relieved.

But Shin Jaehyun wasn't finished.

"But... even if he doesn't remember, it will remain in his subconscious. It must have been a very special round."

"...!"

As he repeated the restarts, that subconscious memory would naturally become a hint for new development regarding T-Holic and Judan.

"Hmm, it should have a slight influence, don't you think?"

It was like lightly tapping a speeding airplane's control lever, changing its entire flight path.

"......"

*Is that so.*

Park Moondae felt he now understood why Cheongryeo's 'Appraisal' characteristic had dropped from S to its current A.

Over time, Cheongryeo would come to accept that he sometimes needed to reconsider his own judgment, like with the recruitment of Judan.

"He'll become wiser."

"That's right."

Shin Jaehyun smiled faintly.

"He'll eventually develop a habit of restarting even when he's about to succeed, all to create a perfect group without a single flaw."

"......"

"But he stops when he meets you, junior, doesn't he?"

*Figures.*

Park Moondae shrugged.

"So... what are your thoughts on seeing your past self?"

"Seeing it with my own eyes made me realize something new."

"That you're better off now that you've stopped, compared to when you could spam restarts?"

Shin Jaehyun stared at Park Moondae expressionlessly, but he soon let out a small laugh.

"That's right."

"......"

Park Moondae grinned back.

It didn't feel bad.

From behind him, Keundal, who had been watching the scenery outside the living room with a relaxed expression, shouted.

"We're almost there!"

"......"

Park Moondae took a breath. He felt like he could see familiar, welcome faces beyond.
