# Chapter 609

Turning back time a little, to a sunny April.

TAP, TAP.

I sat in a company office with the blinds drawn, tapping on my keyboard.

[This Quarter's Deadline

[____________[]

I had a random Excel file open in front of me. I had never created it, but it was installed on this PC anyway.

The quiet office was filled with people at their partitioned desks. The person next to me casually started a conversation.

"Assistant Manager Moondae, no, Assistant Manager Park!"

Keun Sejin, dressed in a navy suit, grinned and offered me the quintessential office worker's drink.

"How about an americano? I just brewed it."

"Sounds good."

Anyone watching this on video would probably ask one question.

[Is that coffee a PPL?]

The answer was no. It was just coffee from the break room.

*It's been a while since we've done something like this during a shoot.*

It was rare to see such a clean, unbranded tumbler in this age of WeTube content and endless advertising.

I downed the iced americano and returned the tumbler to him.

"Thank you, Assistant Manager Lee."

"Don't mention it~"

Of course, we were in the middle of filming our own content.

*We became office workers to match the suit concept for our new album.*

I looked down at my gray suit with its faint pinstripes. The custom-tailored suit fit me perfectly, something I'd never worn even as a real office worker.

How we managed to film our own content in these clothes without any PPL was a story for later. In any case, we excitedly aimed to show a realistic office life free of indirect advertising.

"By the way, Section Chief Bae Sejin, you said you were going to show us something at the office today during yesterday's company dinner~"

"...I did?"

"Yes! Come on, you're not going to say you don't remember, are you?"

As Keun Sejin grinned, daring him to respond, Bae Sejin answered through gritted teeth. He sat at the head of the table with his bangs neatly styled.

"...Right. I remember now. Assistant Manager Lee Sejin was drunk and yelling about quitting, so I said I'd show you a resignation interview."

"...Ah, I did?"

"Yes."

"I think you must have misheard."

"No, it's true."

"No, it's not."

The section chief and assistant manager, who shared a last name, thus began a battle of instigation and fabrication.

"Good morning! I'm not late! It's still 9 o'clock!"

Supervisor Cha Eugene burst in right on the dot, his blond hair striking.

"Supervisor Cha is from a prestigious Moondae foreign company, you know. In that culture, they say it's customary to arrive exactly on time."

"I-Is that so?"

"Then it can't be helped."

"Where did he graduate from? Harvard?"

[Harvard? That kind of Ivy League school is for nerds. The real universities are on the West Coast.]

[Please just shut your mouth, Eugene.]

He had a knack for saying fiery things that would give the production team an editing headache.

And to top it all off, the most competent one among them was the intern.

"Yeeees! Kim Raebin is an intern, so he works for me!"

"......"

"Section Chief, I'm a supervisor!"

Bae Sejin hesitated, then played along for the sake of the concept.

"...Right! Intern Raebin, follow Supervisor Cha Eugene's example and work hard."

"Yes...?"

Kim Raebin, who became the intern because he was the youngest, suddenly looked at Bae Sejin with a shocked expression.

Bae Sejin struggled to ignore his expression and continue the skit...

"No! Just work with me. Follow my example!"

"...! Understood!"

He failed.

Lee Sejin slammed his head on his desk, trying to stifle a laugh.

"......"

*It's just... a skit, that's right.*

*Realism in the midst of this?*

It seemed hopeless.

Besides, there were a few unpredictable exceptions.

"I've brought the printouts."

"Ah. Thank you. You've worked hard."

"Haha. It's okay to speak casually, Assistant Manager Park."

"......"

An intern Ryu Chungwoo, of all things.

"Department Head, you've arrived!"

"Department Head!"

And even Department Head Seon Ahyeon, a position decided by a lottery.

*This is chaotic.*

The guys broke out in a cold sweat at the sight of the completely shattered hierarchy.

I silently watched Ryu Chungwoo, dressed in a perfectly neat and formal black suit befitting an 'intern', continue his antics.

"Raebin, let's work hard."

"Yes, Chungwoo!"

"Ah, we're fellow interns, so we agreed to speak casually."

"...I-I will try to do th- no, I'll try!"

He broke Kim Raebin.

"Department Head, I look forward to working with you today."

"Th-Thank you..."

And he broke Seon Ahyeon, too.

I watched Seon Ahyeon's face turn bright red as he gaped like a fish and inwardly shook my head.

For reference, the reaction when he was the last member to enter the office was anything but ordinary. An enthusiastic cheer erupted, as if the chairman himself had entered.

"The best Department Head!"

"So cool! They said he just came from an executive meeting!"

"Amazing! WOW!"

I thought his face was going to explode.

"Assistant Manager Moondae, Assistant Manager Moondae, did you know? Department Head Seon Ahyeon is actually the chairman's secret grandson..."

"......"

"......"

I cut that plotline short before Seon Ahyeon could faint.

After that, I inevitably realized one thing.

*Am I... the only one here who can lead the proceedings?*

It was a mess, with everyone getting dragged around by the premise. It was a situation where even I had to step in and cut things off.

It went like this.

"Hmm, in my opinion, Intern Chungwoo is good at his job!"

"That's right~ He's the pillar of our company."

"...The intern?"

At Bae Sejin's skeptical question, the two of them went wild.

"Section Chief Bae Sejin just said something discriminatory against interns!"

"They said it's an intern-to-hire position!*Sob sob*! Poor Intern Ryu, what are we going to do!"

"Ah, no...!"

Though I could imagine the comment section flooding with 'lololol', I rescued Bae Sejin before he could sink.

"Speaking of pillars, what is our department's goal for this quarter?"

"Ah...!"

Thinking we were finally getting into the proper office worker setting, Bae Sejin's face brightened, and he quickly moved to the whiteboard.

Of course, he was mistaken.

I saw Lee Sejin whispering to none other than Department Head Seon Ahyeon.

"Such a smart question. Assistant Manager Moondae really is the pillar of our company. Don't you agree, Department Head?"

"Y-Yes!"

"......"

The job titles were just... fluid. A bizarre scene unfolded, one that would be unthinkable in a real office.

*These guys are even subtly calling me Assistant Manager Moondae instead of Assistant Manager Park.*

*I guess this is a family company in the truest sense...*

I wouldn't know what a true superior acts like, since I've only ever been an assistant manager.

"We're... right. We're a company that develops, uh, AI."

What was certain was that Bae Sejin was being funny by making an absurd suggestion. I nodded as I watched him draw a strange circle on the whiteboard.

"But what is that?"

"...It's an AI assistant device."

"Ah."

"I-I see...!"

I ignored Lee Sejin, who had once again slammed his head on his desk, his shoulders shaking.

And the price for looking away was harsh.

After the ridiculous 'All-Purpose AI Development Idea Discussion'.

"Assistant Manager Moondae says he'll take responsibility and lead the project."

"Wow!"

The arrow was pointed at me.

"......"

"The pillar of our company!"

"......"

So this is how it ends up.

"Ah, that's right. Assistant Manager Moondae was working on Excel at his desk earlier."

"......"

I decided to accept reality.

*It's funny because it's so unrealistic...*

Not hiding my reluctance, I sighed and sat down lightly in my seat.

*Is it my turn to make up a story now?*

"Is that the Excel file?"

I don't know.

"That's right. Let's set a goal first."

Feeling like a con artist, I opened the Excel file from before and started typing.

*That's just how skits are.*

When I moved the cursor, a date input field appeared.

[This Quarter's Deadline

[____Year___Month___Day[]

"How about June 18th~?"

"I see. A debut... no, it feels like a special anniversary date for some reason!"

Alright.

*The fans will find this fun.*

I started typing nonchalantly, just as they said.

[This Quarter's Deadline

[20XX, 0June 18[]

The moment I entered it, the result was calculated.

FLICKER.

The monitor flickered for a moment.

"Ah."

DRIP.

A liquid dripped onto the back of my hand. Startled by the cold, I looked up. A droplet seeped from what looked like a leak in the ceiling and fell...

It was blood.

"......"

"Moondae, your hand!!"

...I knew, but I was still surprised.

*So this is how it appears.*

I pulled my arm away from the freaked-out members who were trying to grab it and wiped it with a tissue.

"Maybe it's tomato juice."

"Ooooh... Should I taste it?"

"Are you crazy?"

In reality, it was probably edible coloring made with corn syrup. Still, everyone was terrified by the production, which was clearly meant to look like blood.

Ryu Chungwoo looked at the ceiling and gave a quick command.

"Wait a minute. Supervisor Cha Eugene. You see that pole on the left? Let's use it."

"......"

Cha Eugene momentarily forgot his skit-like complaint of 'Hey, an intern is ordering a supervisor around!' He immediately picked up the pole in the left corner as Ryu Chungwoo said.

CLUNK.

The blood-stained ceiling panel popped open. Something precariously caught there fell with a thud, dangling along with red droplets...

"...!"

The object, covered in a sticky red crust, was,

"A calendar?"

That's right. It was an old-fashioned, tear-off wall calendar.

The members swarmed around it, not daring to get too close, and surrounded the calendar. They all exclaimed in unison.

"......"

[July (July)]

The calendar, with all the previous months torn off, was showing July...

"But, it's April right now..."

For a moment, the creepiness made the skit disappear.

"...Was our setting for this supposed to be July?"

"But the clothes we're wearing aren't for summer..."

"Wait a minute."

I looked at the PC. I could now see the time displayed in the bottom left corner.

10:07 AM

20XX-07-07

"July 7th."

"...!"

At that moment, the other members also rushed to their PCs to check the time.

"Mine is July 7th, too."

"Me too...!"

A moment of silence fell.

"Then... is today July 7th?"

"I guess, so...?"

Instead of joining the conversation, I looked back at the calendar hanging from the ceiling. At those dates.

"...Moondae?"

"It's still strange."

"Huh?"

I pointed to July 7th on the calendar. The number on the far left was marked in red.

"Today is a Sunday."

"......"

A holiday. A day you don't go to the office.

"......"

Bae Sejin's eyes trembled as he opened his mouth.

"Then why are we at the office...?"

Where are we?

And so, TeSTAR's creepy and strange office exploration began.
