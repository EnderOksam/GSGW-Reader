Group potential, market distribution, individual achievement, image, performance...

Literally, we debated over everything possible.

*Damn it.*

I didn't know how many hours we'd been going in circles. My head was pounding.

*...Why are we doing this?*

When comparing entertainment careers, everything was just in the realm of possibility. If anything, this world... had a slight edge over reality.
