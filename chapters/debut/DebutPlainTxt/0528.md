# Chapter 528

"......"

I gripped my smartphone and read the article again.

[VTIC's Cheongryeo, D-4 Until Enlistment (Breaking News)]

A story I had zero interest in was now trending as breaking news.

The timing made it obvious. That bastard had deliberately texted me about his enlistment to coincide with the public announcement.

I pressed the bridge of my nose and fought back a scowl.

*We're still on set. I can't give the staff any juicy rumors to spread, like 'I saw TeSTAR's Park Moondae get super competitive after seeing the news about VTIC's enlistment'.*

However, the guy who had been with me for years seemed to read my face.

Lee Sejin deliberately made a fuss and slapped my shoulder.*Stop it...*

"What is it? What's going on?"

"It says that VTIC's Cheongryeo... is also enlisting."

"Oh~"

Lee Sejin let out a meaningless exclamation and smiled amiably.

"I hope he has a safe trip~"

His tone dripped with social grace, but his face screamed,*I hope he gets lost quickly.*

Then I realized this wasn't exactly surprising news.

*Right. It's November.*

I had already heard VTIC planned to be discharged together, so it was ridiculous to be surprised by an obvious enlistment date. I had calculated it last year but briefly forgot with all the recent incidents.

"Then, with VTIC... having no activities at all, I think... their fans will be very sad..."

Seon Ahyeon must have spoken from genuine concern, not just for show.

However, there was no need to worry about that either.

*That guy has probably already paved his own path, complete with a lawn.*

Cheongryeo is the type who has likely already decided on their full-group comeback song. A guy like Jin Chaeyul might have even received choreography videos while in the military.

*Before that, let's just focus on using their group hiatus to attract more fans. All that matters is that TeSTAR succeeds.*

Besides, the VTIC fans seemed to have expected it, so their reactions weren't that hostile.

Though there were some like this.

[Jaehyun-ah, why are you telling us you're enlisting just four days beforehand?]

[Does this mean we can see our boys' full group performance in the military? ㅋㅋ I'm so excited (Actually not excited, please move along)]

[Why is baby 17-year-old Shin Jaehyun going to the military?]

"......"

I wondered if the last one was serious, but anyway.

"Still, being discharged at the same time sounds really cool, don't you think?"

"I know. I hope they have a safe service."

"Eung...!"

We ended the conversation on that heartwarming note.

On the set, that is.

"Moondae Moondae! About what we were talking about earlier."

The moment the car door closed, Lee Sejin immediately brought up the topic again.

"Discharging after six months and then an immediate comeback... no, there's the hair issue, so maybe about seven months. A comeback next June, then?"

"Probably."

The guy's expression hardened slightly.

"We'll have to plan for next year while avoiding that time, then."

I agreed. Although, we would need to secure the Order of Cultural Merit to postpone our own enlistment before we could make any proper plans.

"Phew, it's not easy for us to plan this stuff ourselves, is it?"

"Yeah, but it's better than not being able to plan at all."

The guy grinned as if I had stated the obvious.

Compared to our T1 days, when the company stubbornly pushed our debut album to release alongside a VTIC comeback, this was a luxury.

It was around then that Seon Ahyeon cautiously chimed in.

"Um... are, are you talking about VTIC?"

"Hm? Ah~ yeah, that's right! We're thinking about the timing since it looks like they'll have a comeback next year. It's almost the end of the year, so we need to make our own plans for next year too."

Seon Ahyeon nodded vigorously, his face serious.

"I think... spring would be good, too. What if we made an album that could capture the feeling of the season...?"

"Oh~ That's a good idea, too."

They excitedly started chattering about ideas for the next album. Having a lot of ideas and being proactive was a good thing. More heads meant more options.

A peaceful moment passed before Lee Sejin naturally arrived at a question.

"Wait a minute, did he contact you directly?"

I must have looked too dumbfounded for someone who had just learned it from the news.

"......"

"What, why on earth?"

"I don't know."

Lee Sejin looked bewildered, but I really didn't know.

If he had called me two weeks ago, I might have understood, but why call a busy person just four days before his enlistment?

*Is this a scheme to interfere with our comeback?*

Honestly, it wouldn't be easy for me to find time within the next three days. My schedules were lined up past midnight.

*Besides, it'll be a headache the moment we're spotted together.*

[Hanging out right before Gommeo's comebackㅋㅋㅋㅋ Ha, I'm so pissed off]

[Gotta go drinking with the hyung-nim who's off to the army, right?ㅋㅋㅋ What an amazing bastard, his own social life is more important even if it's with a rival group that made his fans suffer like dogsㅋㅋ]

Running through the expected responses gave me my answer. A phone call would definitely be enough.

I was contemplating how to politely text,*Are you sane? Let's just talk on the phone.*

[Hyung! I just had a great idea!]

"...?"

Keundal chimed in.

He had insisted on watching our variety show filming in real-time, so I had shared my vision with him. He must have seen the text message from before.

However...

*A great idea?*

What on earth was he thinking?

[Ah, but only if you promise you absolutely won't access the System!]

Then I understood Keundal's 'great idea'.

***

"...So you came here looking like that?"

We were in a Korean restaurant in Yeouido.

I lifted the glass cup and saw a hand with more calluses than Park Moondae's. It was Ryu Gunwoo's hand.

That's right. Keundal had suggested a 'one-hour body swap'.

He had realized from last time that swapping bodies was risk-free, so he willingly switched with me.

[I don't have any schedules in the evening this week, so you can talk comfortably, Hyung!]

[Yeah. Thanks, but won't you have to do TeSTAR's photoshoot during that time?]

[Gasp!]

Of course, I was joking.

It was two evenings after Cheongryeo's call. I had freed up an hour by combining travel and massage time, so I used it. A massage wouldn't be an unpleasant experience for him. In any case, I couldn't deny that Keundal had been considerate.

*I owe him one.*

I should make sure to compensate him properly this time too. I roughly calculated the amount and placed the cup back on the table.

This wasn't the outdoor cafe where I'd met Cheongryeo before. It was a Korean course-meal restaurant with private rooms.

However, it had a unique feature.

"......"

The place was pet-friendly.

*He just had to bring the dog again...*

I confirmed the large dog, lying blissfully on its back, was secured by a harness, then looked at the man holding the leash.

Cheongryeo.

The guy, dressed in monochrome casual clothes, didn't laugh even after I roughly explained why I was in Ryu Gunwoo's body.

Instead, he reacted like this.

"If you keep swapping bodies, you'll start taking it for granted."

"......"

"Considering everything that's happened so far, it doesn't seem like a very good idea, does it?"

I shrugged.

"I'm not just using it recklessly."

"Hmm?"

*Worried I'll get dragged into the System's bullshit again? Of course I've thought about that.*

I wouldn't have done it unless I was a complete idiot.

"I'm using it according to a frequency pattern."

This wasn't me 'specially' picking out the System for this purpose today. I have been using the System regularly, once every three days.

"This System thing seems to want to create situations where I use it often, so I'm using it at the exact same interval every time to prevent it from ranking 'how often' I use certain functions."

I have been deliberately using useless functions, so there's no chance of me getting swayed by its performance.

*Then, when I really need it like this... I just swap it in once in a while.*

This way, it can't figure out which use was out of genuine need. Only the regular pattern of using it once every three days remains in the System's records.

In short, it's data obfuscation.

The guy sitting across from me raised the corner of his mouth.

"I see. That's a method I'd expect from you."

*Was that an insult?*

My eyebrow twitched as I opened my mouth.

"What method were you thinking of?"

"You should throw it away. Don't use it."

"......"

Well... that's the standard answer.

To be honest, it was such a moderate option that I couldn't believe it came from his mouth.

*But if I get rid of the company's System right now, you'd be the one to lose the most.*

*If I don't absorb the System fragment you have, who knows what will happen from the mission failure.*

Thinking about it, this didn't seem very profitable for me. After dealing with the fragment, I should use it as leverage to squeeze some information out of this guy.

*Since he's benefited from it, he'll have no choice but to cough something up.*

Of course, explaining it all in detail right now would be ridiculous, so I just said this.

"I have my reasons. Anyway... why did you ask to meet?"

Let's get to the point, I thought.

I'm crazy busy right now. He wouldn't spout some bullshit like,*Sob, I just wanted to see your face one last time before I go to the training center.*

Cheongryeo opened his mouth, looking slightly hurt.

"I just wanted to see your face one last time before I enlist..."

"Cut the bullshit."

"......"

Yep, I knew it.

"Hmm, it wasn't a lie. Of course, there's another reason too."

Cheongryeo smiled brightly and said.

"It's about Kongi."

"......"

I looked down at the furry creature still lying on its back on the floor.

*Hey, don't tell me...*

"Are you asking me to look after him while you're away?"

"Yes."

*Is he serious?*

*We couldn't even take care of Mungge in our dorm, so Bae Sejin's mother ended up adopting him. How could I possibly raise someone else's dog?*

*Wait.*

I was about to retort immediately, but then I realized this pattern was quite familiar.

*This is the pattern he uses when he wants to piss someone off.*

"Of course, I plan to entrust him to a professional service. You didn't think I meant for you to look after him in your dorm, did you?"

As expected.

*That guy and his long tongue.*

I sighed and took a spoonful of porridge.

"What about your parents?"

"My mother... hmm, let's just say she's not in a situation to take care of a dog. She's a very busy person, and she doesn't particularly like dogs."

*I'm busy too, you bastard.*

But I remembered his mother was a national merit honoree, so I kept my mouth shut. Cheongryeo grinned.

*Did he aim for that on purpose?*

"Besides, things like this are best left to someone who likes them."

Me?

"Your dog?"

"Yes."

Cheongryeo released the harness leash he was holding.

WOOF!

The yellow furball charged at me.

*What the.*

*This one seems to love people too much.*I answered while casually stroking its head.

"Your dog must just like people."

"You can think of it that way."

What a ridiculous guy.

Cheongryeo fell silent for a moment. Then he turned his gaze to his dog. His expression didn't change, but he seemed to be contemplating something, which was unlike him.

His face was quite serious.

"I don't have anyone I can entrust this to. I don't trust the character of people I hire."

"......"

*Come to think of it, didn't he get hospitalized because the dorm's housekeeper didn't clean up some grape peels?*

*Well, the probability of problems arising does decrease if multiple people cross-check.*

And since all the guys in his network who knew his situation had already enlisted, I understood why the offer had come to me.

"You just need to visit once or twice a week to check on his condition. Simple, right?"

"Hmm..."

"If you have time... it would be even better if you could spend it with Kongi."

"......"

Ah, damn it.

I sighed.

"I can't make any promises."

"...!"

"If you're okay with that..."

"......"

No.

"I said I can't make any promises."

"Haha, with your personality?"

"......"

"I mean that you're the type of person who responsibly sees through any job you take on. Hmm... thank you."

Surprisingly, Cheongryeo seemed quite sincere.

*...Hoo.*

*I'd probably have to visit him during his service anyway to retrieve the System fragment. Using this as a reason would make it seem more natural.*

In the end, I agreed to check on this guy's dog from time to time.

Not for free, of course.

I should at least get this much before I go.

"When exactly is your comeback next year?"

"Hmm, you should be able to guess without me saying it."

Well, I expected him not to give an exact date, considering the risk. However, just hearing this much gave me a rough idea.

*So it's a meaningful date for VTIC in June.*

I instantly pinpointed a few possible dates and nodded.

"If you want to have a comeback at the same time, I won't stop you."

*Is he crazy?*

"Yeah. You too."

"......"

I sent the dog back to him and was about to pick up my chopsticks again.

Just then, a challenging word of blessing came from across the table.

"Do well on your next comeback."

"I will."

*I'll do that even if you don't tell me to.*

I shook the hand Cheongryeo extended.

"By the way, are you joining the military band?"

"No. I'm an interpretation soldier."

"......"

"Consistency is the most important thing in acquiring a foreign language, and it was an environment that allowed for great consistency."

*He's a really tenacious bastard.*

A few days later.

[ㅠㅠ Jaehyun left quietly.. I'll hold on for 6 months]

[Why is Shin Jaehyun an interpretation soldier and not in the military band? Just how good is he at foreign languages?]

└They say it's the experience of a global K-Pop Leader

Cheongryeo enlisted as scheduled. That left half a year until VTIC's group comeback.

Half a year.

*I have to change the game and get that Order of Cultural Merit within that time.*

The goal for this comeback was clear. I turned off my smartphone and stood up.

And TeSTAR began their aggressive comeback promotions.
