# Chapter 632

I looked down at my smartphone.

TeSTAR was promoting again.

[Oh my god]

After the news broke, opinions flooded the internet.

Idol groups commonly released repackaged albums with one new song right after their promotions ended. In the past, some even chose a B-side from the same album for follow-up promotions.

*But I doubt any group has suddenly returned with a remix of the same song.*

Especially not like this, where it seemed promotions were over, only for a new schedule to suddenly drop.

[This is awesome but I have no idea what's going on]

At first, everyone was baffled.

As expected, the first issue people raised was our chart performance. They questioned why we were doing a fan-service promotion where the means outweighed the end, especially after we had maintained a good position on the music charts.

Thankfully, they aimed their arrows at the company, not us.

[I think TeSTAR's company made a bad call]

Criticisms like "This is why you can't trust a new company," "They're too emotional," and "What an amateurish decision" appeared here and there.

However, after a little time passed, the tone of the discussion shifted.

[TeSTAR's remix MV is on the US trending videos list...]

└Huh??

└It just came out, how is it already there?

The international response came pouring in.

The foundation for that was accessibility.

[Hey, Limestone is screaming from the rooftops that TeSTAR is promoting]

└??

└Why...?

Limestone was running promotions for us. The global content conglomerate that runs Dreamland had openly stepped in.

It was not some annoying viral marketing campaign, but a classic, orthodox promotion. They immediately rolled out standard news exposure, from Times Square billboards to radio shows, ensuring that anyone with even a slight interest would hear about our return.

The result.

[This is that TeSTAR?]

[Hey where did your hip-hop go]

[I'm so confused what the hell are these guys?]

└A K-Pop band that lives as if hearing that question is their life's goal

└oh my god lol

[I'm calling it now: I like this sexy agent version more]

└you're just horny

The remix music video's comment section began flooding with English comments from people who had discovered us through the Glastonbury performance.

In Korea, the confusion only grew.

[Did TeSTAR sign some kind of artist label contract with Limestone?]

People said it was the kind of treatment you would only give an artist on your own label. It took only half a day for the "TeSTAR's US label is Limestone" theory to emerge.

The truth was soon revealed.

[Ah, they're making it the official theme song for the attraction! So that's why they released a remix, holy crap]

In other words, Limestone was simply running a standard promotion for its own attraction.

[Ah lol, you can't miss out on a big corporation]

[TeSTAR rode the wave well]

As if they finally understood the full picture, people nodded in agreement. They said things like, "So TeSTAR didn't miss the chance to elevate their global recognition to a celestial level."

If they understood it that way, that was fine with me.

[I believed in you, Eumseup-daeng, fuck]

You bet.

*Thank you.*

Since we were even doing a week of domestic music shows, there was no unnecessary noise or unpleasant feeling of distance. It would be treated like an official promotion.

And above all...

*The reaction to the remix itself is pretty good in Korea, too.*

Honestly, people could have found it boring or random, thinking we were overdoing it with the same song, but thanks to the Glastonbury performance blowing up, the anticipation was even greater.

An anticipation of, "What are they trying to do?"

[It would've been a waste to just close up shop for real lol]

[Yeah, let's go]

[Don't miss out on the sexy concept, become a TeSTAR fan now. If you join now, we'll throw in super fun original content and legendary performance videos]

Being seen as "striking while the iron is hot" was an unexpectedly good outcome.

*To be honest, I was prepared to take a risk with the domestic public opinion on this.*

We caught this wave by chance.

Of course, whether by chance or by fate, an opportunity is only an opportunity if you know how to seize it. I knew we could seize it because this group had members who chose their profession very well.

I smiled quietly.

"Hm? Moondae, what is it?"

I turned my head to Ryu Chungwoo, who was stretching beside me. Ryu Chungwoo looked at me quizzically before smiling slightly.

This guy seemed to have completely regained his composure since that insane performance. No, he actually seemed even bolder than before.

"Let's proceed with the music show's pre-recording as planned. There's no need for it to be private."

"Yes."

He had started to act more selectively and freely. He moved away from his habit of always asking for opinions one more time and adding a safe, consensus-building step for group matters.

*He used to try to take all the responsibility like the eldest, just because he was the Leader.*

Some of the tension had left his shoulders.

Before, whenever he looked at the more avoidant members, he would show a troubled and cautious expression, as if to say, "I want to help you. I'll try, even if I don't fully understand." That was gone now.

Should I say the mental distance has shrunk?

*Does it mean he's also experienced a time when he was so lost in thought he couldn't do anything?*

He concluded that it was not like him, but it seemed he definitely gained something from it. His instructions and work-related talk were now more casual and uninhibited.

A few days after the performance, I still remembered what Ryu Chungwoo had said so nonchalantly.

[Hyung, I like that you're able to be considerate of others. But I hope you'll think it's okay even if you're not considerate.]

Right. It was much better than him embracing everything and worrying by himself.

I let out a small laugh.

Behind me, Lee Sejin grinned while adjusting his in-ear monitor.

"It's been a while since we've been on this talk show, hasn't it? We've been to the US a couple of times, but it feels strangely new."

"Maybe it's because we didn't appear on it during our last comeback."

"Ah, that's true."

This was the backstage. We were preparing to film for an American talk show.

We had postponed our full-scale overseas promotions for this remix activity, so we crammed all our international appearances into this period. Thanks to that, our schedule became tight. We were set to fly around on an airplane with only a day between locations.

The one among us with a personal schedule had a bit of a hard time again.

"Um, Sejin... you have to prepare for your next project, too."

"That's right. That romantic comedy!"

However, the person in question, Bae Sejin, was nonchalant.

"It's fine. Acting isn't something you can only do in a practice room."

"Whoa."

"Ooh~"

"Hyung, that's so cool!"

Despite the exaggerated reaction, Bae Sejin did not yell this time. Instead, he gave a small, slightly awkward laugh.

"...! Oh?"

"Hyung. You're not getting angry?"

"I'm not. It's a compliment."

Even Kim Raebin looked at Bae Sejin with slightly surprised eyes, but soon everyone started chuckling.

"I think these clothes really suit us."

"You think so?"

Even though they were like infiltration suits, a suit was a suit. Everyone was acting a bit more mature.

"I have something to admit, too."

Cha Eugene, who had been muttering something like a prayer in Spanish, suddenly chimed in.

"What is it?"

"I can do something fun here."

"Here?"

"On this show."

Cha Eugene said with a sullen face.

"."

"Oh my god."

A few years ago, when he first appeared on this talk show to appeal to gamers with ❬Section 127❭, he was horrified, thinking,*I can't believe I'm on a nerdy show like this.*Now, that same guy was nodding seriously.

"If the fans like something, I'll try to like it too. If the members want to do something, I'll do it too."

"Oh~"

The members clapped as if they were proud of him.

"...You've changed a bit, too."

Bae Sejin muttered, then hastily added when he realized the nuance was off.

"I meant in a good way! That, uh, your teamwork has gotten better."

"Haha, yeah. I'm sure Eugene understood."

"Yep, I know it's a compliment!"

I looked at Bae Sejin.

"You've changed a lot too, Hyung. In a good way."

"...! I... guess I have."

Bae Sejin's face flushed a little. It soon subsided, and he grew quiet as if lost in thought. It seemed he was looking back on everything he had been through, starting from the debut survival show.

Well, a lot of things did happen.

I looked around at the familiar members, huddled together amidst the unfamiliar staff. I could see the faces of the guys standing here, who had gone through all sorts of ups and downs since their debut, no, even before their debut.

The guy who would have avoided eye contact a few years ago, the guy who would only start chatting actively when the cameras were rolling, the individualistic guy who would not have bothered to step up...

These guys, who each had their own circumstances, were now gathered together, having an honest and calm conversation with passion in their hearts.

Of course, there was also a guy who was consistent in a good way.

I patted Kim Raebin's shoulder.

"...? Hyung?"

"Let's do well today, too."

"Of course!"

Yeah.

Just then, Seon Ahyeon turned around. He listened intently to a staff member before nodding with a bright face.

"They said filming is about to start...!"

"No delays? Nice."

Out there, there was an audience, a show host, a band... and beyond the cameras, countless people would be watching us.

But right now, it was just us here.

"Alright, gather 'round."

The guys, slightly damp with sweat, stood in a circle. Heat radiated from their heads pressed together.

Still, it was okay.

"TeSTAR is gonna show them something today."

"We have to."

"Let's go."

We walked out onto the stage.

[Let's welcome today's guests. They're the KPOP stars who recently put on an incredible performance in the UK. The hottest 7-member male band in the world right now...]

The curtain was rising.

Korea.

[Andy: It's been a long time, everyone! But... did you change professions?]

[Cha Eugene: Oh, how did you know? We got jobs at the CIA.]

[Hahahaha!]

"Uwaaah..."

A fan melted as she watched the pre-released clip from TeSTAR's American talk show.

She was an all-fan, a type of fan said to be rarer than wild ginseng in the early days of their debut. A Loviewer of TeSTAR.

Now no longer small in number, the all-fan happily went online after finishing the video.

TeSTAR was there, too.

[Our Moon-daeng is all grown up ㅠㅠ So he grew his hair out to sweep it to one side and show off the hologram tattoo on his neck, haa (Photo)]

"Thank you..."

Saving the precious edited photo that Park Moondae's first fansite master had uploaded, the fan scrolled down excitedly.

The world of TeSTAR unfolded with the movement of her hand. A GIF of a paired choreography, clips from variety shows, concept photos, past stages, and photos of each member's airport departure all formed a story.

And the reactions.

Screenshots of WeTube comments, article rankings, music chart ratings, and concert reviews adorned the timeline with numbers and descriptions.

Soon, after scrolling past posts from solo stans engaged in keyboard battles, she skimmed the pleasant posts from her social media friends and refreshed her timeline.

The sea of TeSTAR.

Just then, a new post popped up for the fan, who was completely immersed in it.

[Guys, the Bill Hot 100 prediction is out. Go look, quick, please, someone talk to me about this (Link)]

Huh?

Urged by the post's frantic tone, she clicked the link. It led to a post on an online community.

[❬Bill Hot 100 Prediction❭]

At the top of the chart, which had been translated into Korean, a single line colored in red was clearly visible.

[Bad feelings? / TeSTAR 26th]

"...Huh?"

[???]

[TeSTAR?]

[Why is TeSTAR here]

All sorts of people expressed their confusion, joy, and disbelief. TeSTAR's title track had suddenly re-entered the predictions long after its promotion period had ended.

And in the top 20s, no less! Not on the album chart, but the singles chart.

What is this?

"Uh, uhh?"

Before joy could even set in, a comment posted very early on caught the fan's eye. It was clearly written in a tone meant to rain on the parade.

[Remixes lol. A classic Bill chart manipulation tactic.]

"......"

What did that mean?

The fan frantically searched the term online and soon understood.

*Ah...!*

The remix loophole.

[Bill counts a remix's performance as part of the original song's score upon release. In other words, by taking advantage of the fact that releasing many remixes is advantageous for charting...]

On the Billboard charts, different remix versions of a song were not counted as separate tracks.

*Since they're all bundled with the original song, there's a loophole where people deliberately release dozens of remixes...*

In truth, because the competition for chart positions was so fierce, it seemed less like a loophole and more like a way for large fandoms to show their purchasing power, but labels tend to stick. Those who wanted to tear them down were bound to use derogatory terms.

Moreover, it seemed there were quite a few cases where people would insist it was the remix loophole to ruin another artist's reputation, even when the circumstances did not fit.

*...Don't tell me TeSTAR is now, too?*

TeSTAR had just released the Glastonbury performance version and the secret agent version as remixes. Their scores were probably a huge part of the total...

[I've seen a lot of cases where people jump on you just for releasing a remix tsk tsk]

For a moment, it was a chilling thought.

The fan bit her lip and returned to the previous page. She forced her eyes down to the comments to see the reactions.

However...

[What are you talking about, it's only two versions]

[The 'remix loophole' parrots are out again]

[Hey, one is a live performance track and the other is a collaboration track. Anyone would think we mindlessly churned out remixes to force our way onto the charts lol]

[They're actively promoting with the remix, so stop with the bullshit]

"Huh?"

The public opinion was very good.

The fan blinked, then realized why. In TeSTAR's case, there was a clear justification for this remix.

A track released spontaneously because the performance reaction was good, a theme park attraction collaboration track, and even the fact that they resumed promotions with it... There was no part of it that could not be defended against the claim that "they meaninglessly increased the number of versions just to aim for a chart position."

Instead, it was more accurate to say, "They released the remix late, but due to the nature of the US charts, they were able to build up momentum without taking a loss."

If that was the case...

[TeSTAR just started promotions, right? I think this will go higher]

[The music shows and the US stage haven't even been revealed yet... could it?]

[Anyone see the Bill Hot 100 prediction?]

Her heart pounded.

Just what is going to happen from now on?
