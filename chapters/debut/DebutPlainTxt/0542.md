# Chapter 542

Let's be honest.

This wasn't the first time I had closed my eyes and woken up in a different body, in a different world. It wouldn't be strange if I were getting used to it.

*Is this... the fifth time?*

I'm going crazy. No, even a ridiculous situation like this becomes familiar with repetition. Now, it only took a few seconds to regain my rational thinking.

So, I calmly and rationally organized what had happened so far.

[I retrieved the System fragment.]

I successfully took the fragment from Cheongryeo, who was in the middle of his military service.

Everything went according to plan up to that point. The problem was what happened next.

[Suddenly, my vision distorted.]

Actually, it wasn't just my vision. My surroundings suddenly twisted and swirled like a fast-forwarded video.

Now that the spinning had finally stopped, what I saw before me was...

[VTIC's Cheongryeo is standing on a rooftop.]

That's right.

However, this didn't seem to be the same Cheongryeo I was just talking to, the one serving in the JSA.

I looked at the man in front of me again.

This Cheongryeo, looking down from the rooftop, wore a black coat. His face, hair, and body were well-maintained. He looked to be in his early to mid-twenties.

So, this was the past, at the very least.

A certain Winter Night before ❬Re-listed ! Idol Inc.❭ even aired.

And... a rooftop on a building in the outskirts, with a view of the Seoul nightscape.

"......"

This was the place of Cheongryeo's first 'restart' that I had seen through the System.

*But there are a few differences in the details.*

Combining all of this led to a rather convincing hypothesis.

[A past Cheongryeo who is repeating his 'restarts'.]

In other words, he was a man I had no connection to and didn't know.

*Damn it.*

I felt like shoving a bottle of soju down my throat. I sighed.

*Things are getting complicated.*

If I were to objectively point out a silver lining, it was that this wasn't the worst-case scenario I had anticipated.

The System had gone haywire more than once or twice. I had naturally considered all sorts of side effects that could occur when collecting the fragment. Keundal was practically foaming at the mouth over it.

But a situation where I meet some old version of Cheongryeo?

This wasn't something to panic over. I mean, I had already met the STier members once.

Let's just decide on a course of action for now.

If we were to call this guy... Restart Cheongryeo, for now.

[Confirm if communication with this Restart Cheongryeo is possible.]

First, I needed to grasp the situation.

Besides, look at him. He went out of his way to find the place of his first 'restart'. Seeing him assign meaning to a place like this... isn't it obvious?

This guy couldn't have restarted that many times.

*I can probably handle him with words.*

There would be a difference in the value of information compared to someone who's been through it all. Doesn't it seem like he'd be easier to win over than the one in the army?

Still, the feeling that I was going through all sorts of crazy things remained... but well, this should be a bit better.

I calmly tried to open my mouth.

*You...*

Then I realized.

I had no voice.

This wasn't some nonsense about being too nervous to speak. My voice itself was literally 'gone'.

I tried to raise my hand into my field of vision, but I couldn't see it.

"......"

Come to think of it...

*This guy can't see me either.*

If someone were this close, even a man about to jump off a rooftop would surely notice them at some point.

However, Cheongryeo kept looking down from the rooftop. He hadn't noticed me.

I scanned the man as if crossing my non-existent arms.

*So I'm just watching him one-sidedly, huh.*

It was just like the flashbacks I saw whenever I cleared a status abnormality. I wondered if I was accidentally watching this man's memories again.

It was at that moment.

"Who's there?"

The man in the coat, who had been leaning on the railing, suddenly spun around.

Towards me.

"......"

*Can he see me?*

But that was it.

The man scanned the empty air around me as if sensing invisible dust, but he didn't seem to find anything.

He turned his gaze back down to the rooftop below.

"......"

*Why the hell am I clenching my teeth like I'm hiding?*

I wanted to smack my own non-existent head. I sighed in bewilderment.

As expected, there was no sound.

However, there was a problem.

As if that movement had been a trigger, the crushing pressure that shook my body returned.

[...!]

It felt like I was being sucked into a typhoon again.

If this continued, I felt like I would be swept away again and experience my surroundings swirling madly once more.

*This could go to shit if I'm not careful.*

Looking on the bright side, I might return to normal after this, but...

*Somehow... I have a bad feeling about this!*

I gritted my teeth and tried to grab onto something nearby. The air conditioner unit, electrical wires, the wall, the railing...

*It's no use.*

I failed to grab anything. The moment I had a gut feeling that I was about to be violently sucked away somewhere.

As I tried to grab the edge of the railing, I brushed against Restart Cheongryeo's clothes.

——————————

Then suddenly, a force began to act in two directions.

*Wait.*

One was still pulling me outward.

The other... was towards Cheongryeo.

"......"

*I don't know what the hell is going on, but...*

*I have a choice now!*

And usually, the new option is the better one.

Amidst the pressure, I started cursing and struggling to grab onto the force pulling me towards Cheongryeo.

And when the situation ended.

*Hoo.*

I realized I was looking down from the rooftop.

And this wasn't my vision.

...It was the spot where Restart Cheongryeo had been standing.

"......"

It sounded completely insane, but I think I had latched onto this Cheongryeo's mind... or his vision.

There was only one possible reason.

I realized it just now, in this state...

*There's a System.*

This bastard has a System right now.

Because I came into contact with it, my consciousness was able to stick to it like a magnet.

*What is this?*

*Why did I latch onto the System?*

However, contemplating the cause was a lower priority. What I needed right now was...

*To grasp the situation again, I guess.*

To find out why I ended up in this situation of all things.

Talk to him, dig for information.

The problem was, to dig for anything, I first needed to be able to express myself...

"......"

I remembered.

A way to communicate without a body.

I remembered how I found STier Lee Sejin A inside the System and made him display a chat window.

*...Right.*

I accessed the System directly.

Very directly and crudely.

*And I can try that again now.*

After a short, deep breath.

I dove into Cheongryeo's System.

***

The cool night air moved across the rooftop.

For a moment, Cheongryeo felt a sharp pain in his head, accompanied by dizziness.

"Hmph."

However, there was no tremor in the hand gripping the railing. His gaze was also calm.

Migraines were an occasional problem.

Physical pain was supposed to disappear upon restarting, but that wasn't always the case. Chronic pain, attributed to the vague cause of 'mental fatigue', sometimes appeared, and it was as annoying as a penalty.

Of course, as he got used to it, he began to treat it less like a penalty and more like a condition.

Besides, this headache was fleeting. There was no need to even place a hand on his forehead. He dismissed the symptom without any particular feeling.

He was going to restart anyway.

*I'll have to check again with a new body.*

To see if the symptom remained.

He shifted his gaze back to the road below the building he had been staring at.

A quiet back alley, with no crowds or commotion.

It was a suitable height for his head to be smashed.

He had tried a few other places, but none were as clean a 'restart spot' as this.

*To think the first attempt was the most appropriate.*

If he was lucky, it was a good thing.

And while he had originally planned to execute that 'appropriate method' right now... he twisted the hand gripping the railing.

"......"

For some reason, he didn't feel like it.

For the first time in a very long, rare while.

*Is it because of the headache?*

In truth, the cause didn't matter, whatever it was.

There was no problem in staying for a few more hours without restarting to continue reviewing his new plan.

Just like with cooking or exercise, you could mess up and practice as much as you wanted before the real competition began.

This was time he was going to throw away anyway.

*If I use it well, it'll be efficient.*

He felt a faint interest in this idea. It was a thin, crude feeling, hardly enough to be called pleasure.

This was because it was an idea he was already sick of.

It was just one of the many methods he had tried to squeeze out efficiency.

"......"

Cheongryeo pulled himself away from the railing and casually turned around, walking down from the building.

He didn't have a cigarette, but his white breath remained.

The dorm wasn't far.

"Are you okay?"

"Of course."

He replied nonchalantly to the member who asked with worried eyes, then returned to his room.

Of course, that member didn't usually worry about Cheongryeo's late returns.

That attitude was because of an incident that happened to the group three days ago.

]"Bill-board Male Idol's Seocho-dong Pink Love Nest" Eyewitness Account Causes a Stir]

[VTIC's Heewoon Releases Apology... "Sorry for Causing Concern to the Fans"]

A dating scandal.

A noisy romance involving one of VTIC's members was exposed and took over the internet.

Since they were strictly managed at the dorm, a sasaeng had exposed that he had rented a separate space and set up a home there, as if building a nest.

And during the first week of their comeback, no less.

[The crazy bastard really set up a whole house lol]

[Leave the group]

[Thinking about how he grinned after getting the Grand Prize makes me want to kill him lol]

Thanks to that, VTIC had to quietly halt the promotional activities they had ambitiously started with an awards ceremony after just three days.

And Cheongryeo thought.

It would have been easier to handle if it had been a crime.

*If it were a crime, we could erase him from the group.*

Meaning, make him leave.

However, a dating scandal is ambiguous.

The blow is more immense than any other incident, but it's difficult to kick him out on moral grounds. It's just dating, after all.

And a dating scandal at this stage of their career, one that involved setting up a separate home, was a death sentence for the individual idol and a fatal blow to the group.

It was nothing short of a signal for their downfall.

*Even if we rebound... we'll have no activities or fame for at least a few months.*

Right now, all of VTIC's buzz was being sucked into that member's dating scandal.

And today was December 27th.

What this meant was obvious.

Even if VTIC were to receive the Grand Prize at the current awards ceremony, they wouldn't be recognized by the public as a 'top-tier idol' to that extent.

With a very high probability.

*So...*

This year's Grand Prize is finished.

[Failure.]

It's time for a clean restart.

And this time, too, a new opportunity will be given.

Perhaps forever.

"......"

Cheongryeo felt his head become cold and clear.

The reluctance he felt on the rooftop earlier had vanished without a trace.

*Then, 5 AM tomorrow.*

He made the plan numbly.

He had nothing to lose.

At one point, he had done some rather destructive things to relieve stress, but now even that was unnecessary.

He was relaxed and calm.

Everything becomes more numb as the attempts pile up.

Even if it meant repeating an endless challenge without any hints or help.

He no longer expected new variables, unexpected help, or unforeseen miracles.

Everything becomes standardized, flattened, and bland, turning into just a pattern.

He simply sat quietly and reviewed his new VTIC member composition once more.

It was in that peaceful moment.

[Can you see this?]

"......"

Cheongryeo's eyes froze.

As if it had seen him, the translucent text in front of him updated.

[Hold on.]

The color stabilized. It became clearer and clearer.

The text in the empty air said.

[Don't restart.]

Cheongryeo held his breath.

Once upon a time, he too had reasoned like a human.

About what kind of situation he was in.

Aliens, a spiritual experience, or even some kind of computer simulation. There were many hypotheses.

However, none had ever been proven. Because no matter what he tried, no response ever came back.

But now.

[There's still some left.]

[Time! There's still time left.]

A pop-up appeared before his eyes.

['Death if not the Grand Prize']

: Death if you fail to receive the Grand Prize from the most prestigious domestic awards ceremony within the set period.

Time remaining: D-359

"......"

A goal clearly stated in sentences and dates.

And...

[The dates overlap. Since it's December 28th now... you have until December 28th of next year. Then you could probably aim for a few starting with ToneA.]

Someone's advice.

Cheongryeo blinked.

Breathing slowly, he turned his head.

Another window was appearing next to it.

[!Unauthorized error occurred]

Do you want to remove the Status Window?

And there was only one option.

[Yes]

After a moment of dizzying silence.

Cheongryeo looked away from the window.

He did not delete this error.

*Hoo.*

Park Moondae, who was rummaging through and connected to the System, couldn't help but let out a sigh of relief.

Anyone could see that was an option to eliminate him.

And with a guy like that, wouldn't it be plausible for him to delete it immediately just because it was annoying?

*Of course, it's easy to guide him not to, though.*

It was so obvious what this era's Cheongryeo cared about.

Only efficiency!

Park Moondae's reasoning was that if it was an element that helped him reach his goal, he would definitely keep it.

*Proving my worth, huh.*

And that was his specialty.

[I'll take questions for now.]

Park Moondae grinned as he displayed a pop-up in front of Cheongryeo, one he had forcibly created by randomly generating and stitching together terminals.

A moment later, however, he started cursing.

It was because he realized just what kind of person this 'Restart Cheongryeo' was.

[!Status Abnormality: Correction]

: Let's try again

[Upon failure, returns to the beginning.]

This was the familiar description of Cheongryeo's status abnormality.

However, there was no deactivation mark.

*Meaning it's in an activated state.*

And as if to support that, another line of explanation was attached.

[Number of returns: 100]

"......"

*Not 10 times, not 11 times, not 20 times.*

*This is the 101st time.*

*Fuck!*

Park Moondae had become the Status Window for a Cheongryeo who had restarted 100 times.
