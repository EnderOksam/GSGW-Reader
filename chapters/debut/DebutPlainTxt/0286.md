"What Raebin means is that we don't have to take such a hard road. We're uncomfortable with each other."

I didn't need to open my mouth for this.

"Boo..."

*Then, except for Cha Eugene... Does everyone want to change?*

I looked around and organized my thoughts.

A way that was possible.

"First of all, it is difficult to hasten a comeback. There is also a music video schedule."

"That's right, we'll also release our teasers separately. No matter how much we pull it, a week seems to be the limit."

And if we tried to pull it, if we got a week of music broadcasting, VTIC would push in, and it'd be over.

"Okay. So the main thing to consider is to postpone it..."

Ryu Chungwoo carefully added his words.

"The company said August or September."

"Mmm."

We'd be losing more money by the end of the year.

*It's still difficult to beat VTIC, so I think it's dangerous.*

Still, it was a stable choice in terms of avoiding a greater risk. At least we could get 1st place on a music show for about 2-3 weeks.

But I'd be in trouble.

*I should avoid making choices that damage the route to the destination.*

Here, I wanted to choose a date that would benefit winning a grand prize more than winning first place on a music show.

But this... I could speak only when there was a clear alternative.

*Usually, I'd just choose August and end it here.*

I shook my head to get my words out before the trend hardened in that direction.

It was then.

"But I don't want to put it off that long."

Ryu Chungwoo spoke first.

"Really?"

"Why, why?"

"It would be nice to easily win first place on a music show right now, but in the long run, I thought it would be better if we went the way we planned."

The guy smiled coolly.

"We have to try for the grand prize this year."

No way.

*That...*

"Ohhh, hyung!"

"It is a wonderful aspiration."

"I like that too!"

The response was good because it was said by a guy who didn't usually say things like that.

And I was convinced after eye contact with him.

*He's doing it properly.*

He took my words that we had to win the grand prize seriously.

*Good.*

Then it was definitely easier for me to speak out.

"Then, if we think in the direction of preserving our existing schedule as much as possible... Let's analyze the expected performance."

I started organizing with a pen on paper instead of the whiteboard occupied by Keun Sejin.

"First of all, VTIC sunbaenim has a shorter term than us, and since it's a repackage album, it won't rise dramatically."

Even if I didn't add the word annual leave, they would have known.

*Of course, I can't deny that the virtue of starting with Idol Co' is huge.*

*However, all of our music and album scores are top-notch now, so they won't be able to say anything else.*

It was a gamble, but it was worth a try.

"But if we do this, VTIC fans will really hate it, you know?"

"I guess so? They'll feel like they're being used."

That was the part I had to accept.

The topicality of this two-way structure was always accompanied by resistance and fatigue.

"Hmm."
