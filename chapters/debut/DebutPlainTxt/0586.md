# Chapter 586

That evening.

"The weather is great~"

"What a nice neighborhood."

After finishing a commercial song, TeSTAR got free time to hang out in a nearby seaside village at the foot of the mountain.

Honestly, we had a good time. The village was charmingly pretty and well-equipped with amenities.

The production team had clearly chosen it to contrast with the hellish commercial song camp.

*They must have scouted several villages before picking this one.*

We ate cockle bibimbap for dinner, walked along the beach, and drank some coffee.

For the record, Kim Raebin bought this coffee. He had run to a cafe a little ways away.

The guy was tearfully happy. He was finally out of the 'Room of Dreams and Happiness', the 'Room of Forced Luxury Staycation', and could finally work.

"Thanks. Are you feeling okay?"

"Yes! My heart is perfectly peaceful and joyful!"

"......"

Poor guy.

I let Kim Raebin enjoy running errands for the members.

While leisurely sipping coffee on a beach bench, I quietly got up and started walking. Seon Ahyeon quickly followed.

A staff member then mouthed a question.

[Where are you going?]

"We have some allowance, so we're going to buy a few things."

Of course, no one stopped us. The PD himself had said it.

[As long as it's not a crime, you can spend your allowance however you want~]

So we took a leisurely walk to a stationery store. We bought some sketchbooks, markers, and writing tools.

"How about this?"

"This color looks good, too...!"

We had some writing tools for composing, but not such a wide variety. The staff seemed to understand, assuming we were just buying them for supplementary use.

*Good.*

Without rushing, I paid and returned to the beach to rejoin the others.

"Did you get everything okay~?"

"......"

"Uh-huh!"

"The hyungs were shopping over there, I heard. Let's go!"

We naturally joined Ryu Chungwoo and Bae Sejin, who stood far down the beach, and started walking again.

Around that time, the youngest members returned. They had volunteered to throw away the cups.

"......"

"Eugene is here too."

When our outing time ended, TeSTAR casually returned to the mountain cabin.

However, as the staff tried to send Kim Raebin back into the iron cage of his luxury staycation, Ryu Chungwoo raised a hand.

"Just a moment."

"...?"

Ryu Chungwoo unwrapped one of the sketchbooks I had bought and handed it to Kim Raebin.

Then, TeSTAR naturally distributed the remaining sketchbooks among themselves and picked up their writing tools.

SCRIBBLING.

The phrases they wrote without hesitation were... these.

[Give us back Kim Raebin!]

[A kitchen for Park Moondae!]

[Give us lamb]

[I'm not the lamb today]

"......"

As soon as the slogans were complete, the staff's expressions changed.

"What, what are you doing?"

"Haha."

"...Members of TeSTAR?"

"Hahaha."

"......"

We plopped down on the hallway floor and held up our sketchbooks.

"We'll just be like this here during our break."

"......"

Before long, we successfully got on a video call with the root of all evil... no, the head PD of the program.

"Hello, PD."

Have a taste of a labor union.

[Wait a minute! Everyone from TeSTAR, are you guys on strike right now? On a variety show?]

Of course, we couldn't just admit we were on strike.

"A strike?" Ryu Chungwoo replied with a smile. "We're just resting to recover our energy."

[......]

But now, nobody knew how long that rest would last.

[No.]

Through the call, the PD's hollow silence and the sound of the writers suppressing their laughter echoed pleasantly.

[I, I see.]

"Yes."

[But if you rest for too long and the commercial song isn't ready on time... hmm, don't you think that might cause a problem for TeSTAR's credibility?]

Ryu Chungwoo answered smoothly. "PD, would that be our credibility?"

"......"

"Or would it be the credibility of the program that locked up its best composer in a luxury staycation room?"

[......]

"Ohhh, that's right! Think carefully, PD!"

The shouts of TeSTAR, holding their sketchbooks, echoed through the hallway.

Of course, we couldn't ruin the variety show we'd worked so hard to get on. However, wasn't that guy thinking the same thing?

*The one who gets more scared loses. Let's play a game of chicken!*

TeSTAR nonchalantly sat down and waved their placards at the smartphone camera. I held mine up high too.

[It's so spicy I want to run away]

When cornered, even TeSTAR would bite the production team.

[Ah, no.]

The flustered PD let out a dying groan. In the background, the faint sound of the writers bursting into laughter could be heard again.

BWAHAHAHA!

[No, how can you laugh in this serious situation? You traitors!]

They would.

*We're doing this to make them laugh.*

If we had seriously demanded better treatment on a variety show, it would have just made the atmosphere solemn. The moment we became self-conscious, the production team would have started walking on eggshells, trying to moderate the tone, and it could have become incredibly boring.

So, through this method, we were subtly sending the production team a message.*You guys seem to have crossed a line.*This way, they could handle it themselves.

*Then they'll either release Kim Raebin... or at least let us all cook together.*

Drawing this obvious conclusion, I waited for a compromise to flow from the phone like a declaration of surrender.

And then, the PD finally spoke...

[What should I do about this? The Loviewers are going to be disappointed, aren't they?]

"......"

Words I had never imagined began to come out.

[Actually, we were planning to remotely invite the Loviewers and have them evaluate a pre-release of TeSTAR's commercial song.]

What?

[Hoo... We already! Took all the applications online and even finished the lottery.]

"Wait a minute."

These guys... no way.

[However, if the song isn't ready by then, I guess we have no choice. We'll just have to announce the cancellation now. To the Loviewers.]

"......"

[Because TeSTAR is resting! They say there's no song to play for the Loviewers!]

They're bringing out an unblockable move?

Looking around, I saw the members' pupils trembling in unison.

The production team bastards... had succeeded in scaring the hell out of TeSTAR.

[Ha... Our Loviewers who won the lottery must have been really looking forward to it, right?]

"......"

[They must have been so happy, thinking, 'I wonder what kind of song TeSTAR made, it'll be so fun to see it first like this.']

"......"

[If they can't see it, their disappointment will be truly immense...]

"Stop it."

[Excuse me?]

"I said stop it, we'll go compose."

The strike was suppressed in just one hour.

BWAHAHAHA!

This time, the writers' burst of laughter didn't sound so refreshing...

***

RRRIP.

We tore the pages with the strike slogans from our sketchbooks. We placed them in front of Kim Raebin's bars as if to console him, then moved on.

Even then, the video call didn't end, and the PD kept taunting us.

[Everyone, you... you can't win, I tell you.]

Shut up.

*Just how far did they see through us?*

This meant they had anticipated the strike and prepared for it. A trick that would only be possible if they knew us extremely well...

*No, of course they know us well.*

It's not like we've only done one or two variety shows with them.

Worse, with that PD and that writer crossing paths, their experience points must have doubled.

"Hoo..."

Sighs echoed from all around.

Of course, resignation came quickly.

"Ah~ To be suppressed so quickly!"

"This is so sad, really..."

"...It can't be helped. They brought up Loviewer..."

The production staff filming this situation from the side weren't surprised by this change in attitude.

They knew full well that the responsible guys of TeSTAR wouldn't just abandon a job they received as PPL. Nor would they throw away a variety show filming they'd worked hard to secure.

"So, shall we work hard on composing?"

"Let's."

After entering the room, TeSTAR began composing the commercial song for the wool fleece, the next one after the carbonated drink.

It was the very product the hunting team had endured a barrage of suction-cup arrows for.

"...Hmm, it really was warm."

"Agh."

The sad memory almost caused a delay, but soon, the hunting team members all calmly proceeded with their work.

"Hmm, since it's an outerwear... how about something a little emotional?"

"A winter vibe~ Sounds good. Ah, Chungwoo, you look like you have something to say?"

"Ah, I was thinking, what if we went with something completely cute?"

"Ahem, that sounds good too."

This time, for some reason, no one was rolling on the floor demanding Kim Raebin be released.

"I'm going to the bathroom!"

"Yeah, yeah."

TeSTAR just harmoniously patted each other on the back, clicking away on the program to input the notes.

It was a scene in stark contrast to the previous day.

"...?"

The smoothness of it all was enough to slightly fluster even the staff.

And then.

"Ta-da!"

The time it took to complete... was just 100 minutes!

"......"

It was truly lightning fast.

We immediately submitted the commercial song. Not long after, an urgent video call came from the PD.

The PD clasped his hands and was silent for a moment before asking.

[Everyone.]

"Yes."

[...What did you do?]

"Ah, when you mentioned Loviewer, inspiration suddenly struck and we finished it quickly."

[......]

He clearly wanted to scream, 'TeSTAR, where the hell did you cheat?'

After listening to the song from beginning to end, speechless at its quality, the sound of Ryu Seorin running could be heard from behind the PD.

She must be going to check the recorded footage.

Meanwhile, the PD was targeting the person in charge of producing.

[Bae Sejin?]

Bae Sejin turned his head.

[Bae Sejin, your skills are truly outstanding.]

"...Thank you."

[As expected of TeSTAR's main producer! Isn't your producing ability surpassing Raebin's? A genius actor and now a producer too?]

"Yes. That's right."

"......"

When Bae Sejin answered so brazenly, the PD was the one who became flustered.

"I am the producer of this generation."

[......]

"Is Sejin imitating Kim Raebin?"

"Looks like it."

"He's a great actor, as expected."

[No, these guys, really.]

That's why you shouldn't test an actor.

The PD, flustered by Bae Sejin's sharp answer, kept asking again. When no one spoke, he tore at his hair.

It was a great sight to see.

[No, are you really going with this?]

"Yes."

I asked kindly. "You seem very curious about what happened."

[...I am, right?!]

"Should I tell you?"

[...That would be great, right?]

I nodded. The PD leaned forward, concentrating. I also leaned in as if to whisper and opened my mouth...

"I don't want to."

"Pfft, khh!"

The members doubled over, barely suppressing their laughter.

Even the camera director following the scene struggled not to shake his hands from laughing.

For everyone to be so happy at the expense of one PD, it was truly a cost-effective affair.

Unfortunately, it didn't last long. Ryu Seorin, who had gone to review the recorded footage, returned.

[Check Cha Eugene's belongings!]

"......"

At that shout, the staff zoomed in on Cha Eugene.

"Ah, I'm caught."

"You don't have to check! I'll show you."

While the staff tensed up, Cha Eugene laughed cheerfully and pulled something the size of a baby's palm from his back pocket.

It was... a colorful toy.

A walkie-talkie.

"......"

HUUUH?

Sounds of surprise rippled through the on-site staff. It was an item that shouldn't have been there!

[Where on earth did you get...?]

Cha Eugene answered confidently. "I bought it in the village earlier!"

"......"

That's right.

From planning to preparation.

This strike... was purely for drawing aggro!

***

The town we visited.

This operation had been in effect from the moment I got up to buy the sketchbooks for the strike.

[First, let's all split up.]

*If our members split up, the staff will also have to split up, and their numbers will decrease.*

*The number of eyes meticulously checking what we're doing will be reduced.*

This makes it relatively easier to do something secretly.

Moreover, the staff's attention was drawn to me and Ryu Chungwoo, who went shopping. Kim Raebin's group, who went to return coffee cups and throw away trash, had even more freedom of movement.

Then, something like this becomes freely possible.

[Oh, let's go look at this!]

Using the smartphones we got back for the outing, we quickly found a nearby franchise store in the car. Then we pretended to stumble upon it, browsing for items.

And so, Cha Eugene, who had followed Kim Raebin, successfully dodged the camera for a moment. He pretended to be an unruly kid window shopping and succeeded in his purchase.

It was...

"A toy walkie-talkie."

The rest was simple.

When we returned to the cabin, they were about to lock Kim Raebin in his luxury staycation room.

[Just a moment.]

While Ryu Chungwoo spoke to the staff to draw their attention and the sketchbooks created a distraction, Kim Raebin secretly hid one of the walkie-talkies under the beanbag sofa.

[Kim Raebin, tell us!]

And when the members took turns pretending to go to the bathroom to contact Kim Raebin, everything connected.

[Finally, some help...]

Kim Raebin, who had been suffering in his solo luxury staycation, excitedly recited the melodies he had been composing in his head for hours. He gave feedback on the members' ideas.

And when the returning member input that melody...

[Complete!]

"...That's what happened."

[...Wow.]

The PD seemed to be at a loss for words for a moment.

*Right.*

With this level of determination, wasn't this a level of execution where you had to wonder if you should just let them win?

However, as befitting a PD who had been through thick and thin, even the counterfeiting of Carrot Coins, he immediately came up with a countermeasure.

[First, those walkie-talkies are confiscated!]

We obediently nodded.

"OK."

"Go ahead."

"......"

[Why are you all so calm?]

"I wonder," Ryu Chungwoo smiled faintly. "Could it be because we've already discussed with Raebin how to make the next song?"

"......"

We used that one hour to the fullest.

[Right now! That! The song you received after consulting with Raebin is forbidden!]

"Yep!" Lee Sejin grinned. "Ah, but you wouldn't know even if we used it as is."

[......]

That's right.

He hadn't heard it beforehand, so how could he possibly tell?

[If I look at the composing style...]

"You have no proof."

[......]

The PD finally played his last card.

[At the very least, you have to redo the one you just submitted...]

"Huh? Wait a minute."

I've got you now.

"You said you'd send it to the advertiser right after we submitted it. To get feedback."

"......"

"Changing a song you've already sent to a completely different one... wouldn't that be a bit... impolite?"

The PD fell silent.

"Perhaps... you haven't been sending them and have just been giving us arbitrary feedback all this time, right?"

"......"

I knew it.

[Of course not.]

"Right?"

[Of course.]

"As expected!"

"We believe in you, PD!"

A heartwarming conversation took place, even though everyone knew no one believed each other.

"Then since you've already sent this one too, we'll just keep going with it, right?"

[...That's right!]

The sound of the PD sinking could be heard from the other side.

"YEEEEEES!"

After hanging up, we silently threw our fists in the air in celebration and high-fived.

We won!

...However, it seems there was someone for whom this wasn't enough.

That night.

"Our actual treatment hasn't improved, has it? We don't know how many hours Ahyeon will have to suffer cooking again tomorrow. Our meals are on the line."

"......"

"It's the same with the producing feedback. Giving strange feedback to a singer as a joke isn't something to be used as material for laughs, I think."

Before lying down to sleep, Bae Sejin gathered the members with his mic turned off and brought up a serious topic.

"What we did earlier might have made the show more entertaining... but I don't think that's everything."

He explained as if repeating it to himself.

And he finally reached this conclusion.

"In my opinion... the program looks unnecessarily sadistic right now."

"...!"

"I'm going to meet them and talk about it directly."

Someone had gotten serious.
