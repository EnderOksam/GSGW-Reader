# Chapter 401 - The Variety Show Takeover, I

The music washed over the stage. The singer's voice flowed from beneath the dog mask.

The college student listened intently.

$p

*I give you my love* ♫

*Ah.*

But here, it didn't sound like 'that kind' of love.

The college student wasn't sure if it was the dog costume or something in his voice.

$p
♪ *When you hear of love*\
*Please think of me* ♫
p$

A dog looking for a family.

What was written on the signboard he held was clearly a joke, but somehow, when it was brought together with the lyrics... It was no longer funny.

$p
♪ *Today and tomorrow*\
*I will keep you in my heart* ♫
p$

It was a duet meant for a man and a woman, but the singer inside the dog costume sounded neat and polished singing it alone.

He chose this song even though he could have gone for something more shocking or powerful.

Oddly enough, it was that choice that made the performance all the more moving.

$p
♪ *I love you* ♫
p$

The Welsh Corgi finished his final song with a very deep and rich tone.

The applause from the audience lasted for a long time.

"......"

The college student sat frozen in front of the TV, lost in thought.

A strange longing, a sense of nostalgia, welled up from the depths of her soul. For a moment, she stopped thinking and just sat there.

+=
[An impressive performance from 'The Welsh Corgi at Your House'!]
=+

"...! Hoo."

The MC's loud voice came through the speakers, accompanied by captions. The college student shook her head, trying to snap out of her daze.

*Anyway... It was good.*

Unless the audio had been heavily processed for broadcast, the winner was already obvious.

Without showing off his incredibly rich voice or high notes, he'd hit every note just right, making the song a beautiful sensory experience.

+=
[The winner is...]

#><['The Welsh Corgi at Your House'!]><#
=+

Sure enough, he won by a considerable margin.

+=
[Our Bespoke Singer is 'The Welsh Corgi at Your House'!]
=+

The Welsh Corgi pressed both front paws to his face as if he was touched.

It was quite cute, and she was happy he'd won, but what she really cared about was what came next.

*His identity!*

After the runner-up, who'd come so close to winning, gave a short, disappointed speech and left the stage, the focus finally returned to the Welsh Corgi.

+=
[Now it's finally time to meet our winner!]
=+

Confetti exploded all over the stage.

The Welsh Corgi made a show of hugging his signboard, drawing laughter and cheers. The MC finally opened his mouth.

+=
[The creator of 'The Welsh Corgi at Your House' is...]
=+

At the signal, the Welsh Corgi lowered his signboard, raised both front paws again, and took off his mask.

And what was revealed underneath it... Sweat-damp black hair and a coldly handsome face.

"...!!"

+=
[This is Ryu Gunwoo-ssi, the main vocalist of the big rookie group Wishze!]
=+

...He really *was* an idol.

The college student barely had time to feel thrilled that she'd guessed correctly before astonishment overtook her.

He wasn't a complete unknown, either... He was a familiar face!

*Ryu Gunwoo??*

The main vocalist from the in-house survival show, whom she'd briefly considered taking as her bias, was smiling faintly.

*No, I also thought he sang really well when I watched the survival show...!*

She'd had no idea he was this good. She'd moved on because his looks weren't quite her type.

She'd never imagined the Welsh Corgi would turn out to be a rookie idol.

The way he'd walked the tightrope between attention-seeking and wit, his cute antics and the range of songs he'd sung... She'd been sure he was an idol with at least 5 years of experience. But he was only a rookie?

*He was only a trainee for 3 months...!*

*Hey, with that face, he was acting all cute in a Welsh Corgi mask?*

As she watched TV with her mouth open, Ryu Gunwoo smiled and tucked the doll mask under his arm.

And then he picked up the microphone again.

+=
[Hello. I am Ryu Gunwoo from Wishze. Thank you very much for voting for 'The Welsh Corgi at Your House'.]
=+

A strange mixture of groans and screams came from the audience, followed by laughter.

+=
[Can I hear your thoughts on winning?]

[Actually, I didn't dare think about winning. My goal was to give it my all doing something I like and show it to the world, but really... I can't believe it.]

[What part can't you believe?]

[That everyone actually found my humor entertaining.]
=+

Applause erupted. The MC asked another question, seemingly holding back a smirk.

+=
[Do you like Welsh Corgis??]

[I like dogs with yellow fur.]
=+

Saying that, Ryu Gunwoo hugged the yellow dog head mask and smiled.

His cold, severe features suddenly softened, and somehow he looked cute...

+=
[Please give a big round of applause once again to our winning singer!]

['The Welsh Corgi at Your House' created by Ryu Gunwoo will serve as the broadcasting station's mascot for the next month!]
=+

*Ta-da-dan!*

Accompanied by magnificent BGM, the Welsh Corgi, wearing his mask again, raised one paw and cutely waved the signboard. The college student almost reflexively waved her hand at the screen.

That was how the broadcast ended.

But the turmoil in her heart was far from over.

"......"

The college student eventually picked up her smartphone.

This was the situation 30 seconds before a new Ryu Gunwoo fan account was created.
