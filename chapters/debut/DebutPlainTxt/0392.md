*What did they do in a previous timeline to get written off like that?*

I looked at the two guys who seemed perfectly normal, then gathered my things and went out into the hallway. The others would be waiting in the lobby.

"Ahyeon-ssi, please wait a moment, just a moment!"

*Hmm.*

"I was going to say goodbye before I left, but here you are!"

I couldn't have asked for more from a mentor.

"I rarely get to perform on stage using pop songs like this... It was refreshing. Thank you."

However, Seon Ahyeon spoke again.

"And... I really wanted to tell you this, but I liked the revised version better than the original."

"......"

"I think you found a better answer."

"And if you ever want to talk to my counselor... the offer still stands."

*Ah.*

*I should ask him about this.*

Why on earth was this guy seeing a counselor here?

"May I ask how you started seeing your counselor?"

That much would sound like reasonable interest and an attempt to check whether the counselor was trustworthy.

I'd already thought of something to say to take back the question if he hesitated.

However, Seon Ahyeon answered calmly.

"Seeing her was a requirement in my agency contract."

*Hmm?*

"After all, working in the performing arts is always a battle with yourself. Counseling is part of the basic curriculum."

"...Ah."

So... It was part of the welfare benefits for their artists.

*Then why did he say that he'd been in pain too?*

I looked at him to see if he was lying. In real life, it was always obvious when he lied, but maybe he could hide it better here.

But there was no sign of it.

*He's not lying.*

And, as if he'd noticed me deliberating, Seon Ahyeon added a few words.

"Performers must take care of not only their physical condition but also their mental condition."

"Uh, yes."

"So, if you want, I can contact the counselor for you. You can probably... get at least five sessions for free."

"......"

It wasn't until I saw Seon Ahyeon's smiling face that I realized what was going on.

*This... I see.*

*He mentioned being in pain on purpose.*

He'd been lowering the hurdle and building rapport with me so I'd talk about what was troubling me and feel more at ease around him.

*I can't believe I didn't notice this.*

I looked at him uncertainly.

*Wait, then... Doesn't this just mean that this guy is in perfectly healthy condition?*

This meant that he was building his career by receiving as much care as he could.
