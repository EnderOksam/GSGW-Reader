# Chapter 510

Entering the System felt similar to being in a hypnagogic state. It was the disgusting feeling of waking from sleep paralysis for a moment, only to be dragged back under by overwhelming sleepiness, fully conscious.

*The expression 'getting sucked in' is a perfect fit.*

It was just as Keundal had explained.

[Hyung! Maybe you should stop...]

[Hyung? My...]

[......]

Keundal seemed to be sending messages, but that was all. The words became a dull hum, like hearing sounds from outside while underwater, and soon faded completely.

It was like falling into a deep sleep, or off a cliff. The sensation was like sinking into the distant, deep sea.

*...Hoo.*

A brief, dizzying sense of being overwhelmed washed over me, but I quickly shook it off.

*I already expected this.*

There was no need to be flustered by something I already knew. I stilled my consciousness again and tried to grasp the structure of this System.

A moment later.

"......"

As if slowly opening my eyes, I began to perceive where I was.

And I was horrified.

*My god.*

Masses of different sizes shone, endlessly and randomly packed in all directions. They were intricately connected, flashing irregularly. The inside of the System was a mess, like a circuit board patched over and over again.

*What is this?*

It almost felt like a System coded by a beginner. Calling it a System seemed generous. The connections, clinging together like a spiderweb or synapses, also resembled a bizarre and chaotically spliced architectural blueprint.

In a word, it was a massive clusterfuck.

He first implemented this into a neat Status Window? Keundal must have ground his own brain into it.

*Do I have to check every single one?*

I approached a nearby mass and focused my consciousness. It felt like accessing a database.

This is...

[Company Grade Evaluation]

I see.

*So it's divided and connected by function like this?*

I guessed this messy structure was the result of adding necessary things as they came up. From then on, I began to explore in earnest.

*There's still plenty of time until morning.*

I planned to be stuck in here for at least a few hours.

After what felt like a considerable amount of time, I had checked the names of countless masses. Perhaps because this was the periphery, there were many recently created company-related functions.

*There are so damn many.*

It would have been easy to get annoyed, but I was not pissed off. I was satisfied digging into the guts of this damn System. I patiently continued checking my surroundings, and it was not long before I found what I was looking for.

[☆Search☆ ]

This mass had a flashy expression attached to it, unlike the others. This was not made by the System. It was a trace Keundal left when he implemented it last time.

*He implemented it during the failed building collapse mission.*

This thing, intricately connected to various masses, looked more like a terminal than a mass itself.

*Good.*

Through this, I could directly access the functions I wanted. He also said I could connect them to create applied functions if I wished.

[It's not really an application, I just changed the way it's displayed...]

No, the search is a truly amazing function.

Recalling Keundal's words, I accessed the 'search' terminal.

*So this is how you make it.*

After thoroughly checking the implementation method, I concluded I could also create an application function in a similar way. Then, as I originally intended, I used the search function. My purpose was to understand the operating principle of this System's Update.

[Search.]

[...All information related to the Update.]

Give it all to me.

At that moment, my consciousness began to be rapidly pulled toward destinations in all directions. I gritted my teeth and began to accept the flood of information.

And I realized.

"...!!"

The purpose of this damn bastard was...

~~~

*Hyung! Are you okay?*

Keundal, now in Park Moondae's body, could not shout out loud, but he was practically screaming internally.

*I should have gone myself!*

TeSTAR's Park Moondae, who was originally Ryu Gunwoo, was extremely skilled at subtle logical distortion and persuasion. In the end, Keundal had been flustered and succumbed.

The argument that 'it's more satisfying to do it myself' was a matter of subjective feeling. Keundal, who still had a soft side, could not rationally refute those words. Besides, Keundal already had a record of failure.

[Hyung, the pressure was too strong, so it was impossible to find out specific information. Even if you go in there...]

[...? If someone failed once, doesn't it seem rational to switch people and try again?]

[Huh? Yes, that's true, but...]

The moment he uttered a vague affirmation, he was bewitched. When he came to his senses, he was in the crazy situation of having his body swapped.

*Ugh...*

Thus, Keundal could not bring himself to get a good night's sleep. He came out to the living room sofa to wait for news from Ryu Gunwoo with his eyes wide open.

However, he knew. Controlling the System was easier said than done. In reality, it was an incredibly tricky and difficult task.

*He probably won't be able to come out for a long time...*

[Keundal.]

THUD.

"......"

Keundal rolled off the sofa and had to clamp a hand over his mouth. A flickering, static-filled message from TeSTAR's Park Moondae had arrived in the air.

*Did... did he come out?*

[Not exactly.]

The message replied nonchalantly.

[I found your search function. I tried to revive the message function in a similar way. It seems a bit... unstable, though.]

"......"

Does that mean he is sending a message from inside the System right now? Why did he even think of that? No, more importantly!

*Hyung! Come out quickly! I think I was wrong!*

[No, it's going smoothly here. I don't think that's necessary.]

Keundal wanted to wail, asking how he could be so calm inside that monstrous thing, but the messages kept arriving.

[I have something to report.]

[I found the reason why the System Updates in this way.]

"......"

Keundal forgot to even wail.

"Wh-what is it?"

A sharp reply came back to the question he had muttered aloud.

[To make me use the System frequently.]

"??"

Huh?

[Think about it.]

[Last time, during the Update when Cha Eugene's memories were wiped. I tried many times to use the System somehow to get information.]

That was true. In that absurd state of emergency, TeSTAR's Park Moondae had clung to the System, which had suspended its operations for the Update, and repeatedly tried to activate even some of its functions. Of course, it was impossible.

*Hyung...*

Just as his heart was about to ache for a moment.

[It seems to have learned from that.]

"......"

A long message arrived at a terrifying speed.

[This bastard decided that the most effective way to lure me in was to wipe my members' memories, just like last time.]

[That's why it's pulling this shit. It expects me to use the System frequently if it does this.]

Gasp.

[It saw me frantically trying to use the System last time, is what it is.]

"......"

His choice of words was extraordinary.

*H-Hyung.*

Keundal realized why this hyung had bothered to revive the inefficient messaging function to talk to him.

This hyung... he was so pissed off that he just had to tell someone about it.

[Remember? Last time, during the Update, it shoved in all sorts of extra features.]

[As if telling me to use them.]

*Yes...*

Keundal knew, too. After the Update was finished, he had seen it all through the shared vision. He saw the pop-ups flashing with things like Surveys and new functions.

However.

*But it didn't work...*

That hyung had ignored it all, sharp as a knife.

[Right.]

[Since making itself look useful didn't work, it tried the opposite, to make me desperate.]

"......"

[That's the current situation.]

*My god.*

The System had judged that cornering its opponent was far more efficient than making itself useful. It was a chilling conclusion.

*The fact that Hyung became the owner of the System, rather than being subordinate to it, didn't mean he was safe... it wasn't.*

Even a rattlesnake will die if it bites its own tongue and is poisoned by its own venom. Possessing the venom does not mean you are immune to its harm.

After a moment of reflection, Keundal realized this and quickly exclaimed.

*Hyung, you know now, so you're coming out, right? Hurry up and come out!*

He thought that as soon as Hyung came out, he would beg him to please just throw this System away.

However.

[Wait.]

"...?"

A surprisingly calm message came back, as if he had calmed down in that short time, but the content did not calm Keundal down.

[I just found something else.]

*What on earth?*

What could he have possibly found in that short time? Keundal now felt like crying.

Inside that System, what in the world was Hyung doing right now?

~~~

Inside the System.

*Crazy bastard.*

I am barely suppressing my anger. The result of my search was infuriating. Beta version or not, this System bastard was no different from before it was shattered.

*Still, getting a result at all is a harvest in itself.*

The path to get here was not easy. I will not pretend otherwise. First, using the 'application function' Keundal made, the search, consumed an immense amount of mental energy. I thought my head was going to explode from the enormous pressure.

*Damn it.*

I had been thinking it for a while, but how on earth did that Keundal guy endure this while working? It was no wonder he had not been able to find out the specifics of the Update until now.

As for me, I judged that coming all this way only to return empty-handed would be foolish, so I was able to endure it somehow.

[I'll do it anyway, fuck!]

...while thinking things like that.

Besides, now that Keundal knew the whole situation, if I came out without succeeding, that guy would definitely access this place and try to do this again.

*That would be inefficient.*

A waste of both time and pain.

So I just did it. I did not much like the conclusion I found.

*This son of a bitch, really.*

I suppressed my irritation and looked at a part of the Update information floating before me.

[ Usage Frequency : 0]

[Re-Update Implemented]

: Apply the most optimal situation based on 'Usage Frequency' (Period : 365)

Its goal was to increase the usage frequency.

*So that's why it became such a clusterfuck.*

However, the first Update that became the standard for this mess, the case of STier Cha Eugene, was slightly different.

: 'Company' Affiliated Entity's Ability Enhancement Command Applied

In STier Cha Eugene's case, it seemed it was an attempt to return the STier activity experience that Cha Eugene had forgotten, in order to promote the 'Company Affiliated Artist's Ability Enhancement'.

But the process included his original memories being wiped during the Update.

*But since the usage frequency suddenly increased during the Update, it achieved its goal by stumbling backward, is what it is.*

It was absurd.

[Get lost.]

My will echoed through the void, and the information scattered. I collected my consciousness and calmed my anger again. However, there was also a strange sense of pleasure, like untying a knot.

In any case, just by knowing this bastard's purpose, a path forward became visible.

*Once you know, handling it isn't difficult.*

The 'View Failed Mission' function I am using now to enter Ryu Gunwoo's body probably was not just given to me by the System either. It probably analyzed that my dependence on the System would be high in the desperate situation of a mission failure. Did it want to make me recall that experience?

Sorry, but you failed.

*I'll make you regret giving me this function.*

I smiled fiercely and cleared the damn information from before my eyes. My mind raced as a plan formed. It seemed my business here was done.

*Alright, time to get out... wait.*

But then.

I noticed one last item, the most recently added one, dangling at the bottom of the information I had scattered.

This very thing.

[Ver.2.2 Update]

[Implementing Data]

*Is this... the next one in line?*

In modern terms, it felt like content that was 'scheduled' or 'reserved'.

*Let's check.*

It would be best to find out what this bastard had originally prepared. I tried to analyze that information one more time. As I focused my consciousness and tried to grasp the specific details...

A message rang in my head.

[Who are you?]

"......"

I lost my concentration.

*Just now.*

Something had forced its way into the application function I implemented called 'Message'.

*What is it?*

I tried to implement the 'Message' function in more detail to make it more visible.

*Damn it.*

For a moment, it felt like my non-existent teeth were clenching against a strange pressure, as if the System was resisting. A name appeared in front of the message.

[Lee Sejin A : Who are you?]

"...!"

I realized.

Lee Sejin A. The name tag Bae Sejin received during ❬Idol Inc.❭. In other words... the Bae Sejin who had not changed his name.

And a scheduled Update.

*There's only one possibility.*

[Lee Sejin A : Where is this?]

Right now, I have connected to the memories of 'STier Bae Sejin'.
