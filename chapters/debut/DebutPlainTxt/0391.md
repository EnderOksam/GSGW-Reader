# Chapter 391 - The Performances We Share

It was the day of the ambitious art-themed performances on the LeTi survival show.

Some of the contestants were gathered backstage...

"Shall we do our chant first?"

"Yes!"

The teammates responded loudly and held out their hands. Ryu Chungwoo did the same.

"Team D, we're good at everything! Let's do well!"

It was participant Shin Jaehyeon who smiled and asked for a chant. Also known to Chungwoo as Cheongryeo.

Although he was the leader of VTIC, they were all caught up in this strange situation together.

*I never thought I'd be on stage together with him like this.*

It was an unexpected thing for Ryu Chungwoo. The same went for returning to the past with Ryu Gunwoo and attending college with him.

*It's like a fantasy.*

He'd never dreamed he would face such a surreal situation. But he'd more or less come to accept it as a new experience in another life.

The stage preparation under this VTIC leader had gone extremely smoothly.

*He's very... resourceful. He knows exactly where everything needs to go.*

From participants to trainers, he knew how to put everyone to use.

It was surprising. The way he'd already planned everything out in his head and put it into action was so skillful and resolute that it gave Chungwoo goosebumps.

It was a bit mechanical, but... He had to admit that Cheongryeo seemed more capable of leading on stage than he was.

*I suppose I should work on what I'm good at.*

Ryu Chungwoo chose to think that way instead of smiling bitterly over his perceived defeat.

"Please stand by now."

"Yes!"

The cameraman who'd recorded the chant left in search of another target, leaving only the mounted cameras filming backstage.

Because their team was performing last, Ryu Chungwoo had watched the other performances from the waiting room below.

*They all did well.*

Cha Eugene and Kim Raebin had been split up into different teams, and each had contributed a great deal with his talents.

-Yeah!

He recalled Cha Eugene, who was the center of the live Action Paint performance, and Kim Raebin, who led the arrangement almost alone after meeting the orchestra conductor and chatting excitedly with him.

*Especially Raebin, he's really great.*

His progress was palpable.

Chungwoo had thought as much before, but Raebin was definitely a genius, especially considering he hadn't even gotten his memories back.

"Hmm."

And now the third performance was about to begin, after which it would be their team's turn.

The stage of Park Moondae and two VTIC members.

It was a combination that would have made people faint in the real world, but that wasn't the case here.

*...I hope it'll be okay.*

He remembered what Cha Eugene had whispered to him as soon as he saw Moondae's team practicing in the practice room.

– *That's that team performance! From 'October 31', you know?*

The first team performance on ⟨Idol Inc.⟩ was so impressive that Cha Eugene still remembered the team name.

*They crushed our team, if I remember right.*

That team had competed with Ryu Chungwoo's team. With a bit of luck, he could have been on the same team as them, but a single button press had decided otherwise.

*I pretended otherwise at the time, but I was actually quite disappointed.*

Ryu Chungwoo almost smiled as he looked back on those days, now fond memories, but he froze.

He'd remembered Park Moondae's state of mind while practicing this almost identical performance.

To put it bluntly, Moondae had seemed like he was spiraling.

– *Are you okay?*

– *Yes. I'm okay.*

– *Gunwoo, are you sick?? What's wrong?* Eugene had asked.

– *No. I'm not sick.*

He'd been almost gray in the face.

It had been so obvious that he and the other TeSTAR members had hovered around Moondae a few times, even though they were on different teams.

However, it was difficult to talk about it when everyone (except Kim Raebin) could guess why he was struggling.

It was Moondae's performance, and they had no right to tell him what to do or what not to do.

This was especially true since Moondae's goal at the moment was to eventually win the grand prize and get out of here.

*But if he'd been like that for a few more days, I would've figured out a way to talk to him.*

The situation had changed after the interim evaluation.

Park Moondae had completely changed the stage composition.

– *You don't have enough time for that, do you?*

– *Still, we think it's worth trying.*

As Park Moondae said that, the light returned to his eyes and his tone was as resolute as ever.

It was no longer shaky.

Cha Eugene had neatly summed it up.

– *Moondae-hyung thinks too much, so he needs to practice doing less of that. We just need to watch.*

Although Cha Eugene was one of the youngest members, he knew how to hit the nail on the head in unexpected ways.

So Ryu Chungwoo decided not to interfere with Park Moondae this time.

And the results were about to be revealed on that stage.

He planned to lean forward to get a closer look at the stage where all the sets were installed, but...

"Hyung is always incredible on stage."

"...! Yes. That's right," Chungwoo agreed.

The leader of VTIC started speaking next to him.

The other person was younger than him now, but Chungwoo couldn't bring himself to speak casually to him. Cheongryeo was looking at the stage with quite joyful eyes.

"So this is what he prepared," Cheongryeo said.

"......"

Ryu Chungwoo turned away.

The performance had begun, the stage already projecting a birch forest with snow drifting down from above.

-...

A bell tolled as the lights slowly dimmed, and a yellow moon rose.

A female chorus sang.

$p
♪ *Ah- ha ha. Ah- ha ha.* ♫
p$

It was a beautiful minor-key harmony.

And in time with that unresolved chord, figures crawled out from between the trees.

Three slim silhouettes wearing black hoods appeared.

They spread their limbs, white arms and legs draped in linen. It was a short, intense intro performance.

$p
♪ *Woo! Oooh, ooh, ooh* ♫
p$

As the chorus ended, the three stood tall again, pushing back their hoods one after the other.

Their pale faces came into view, and they sang in harmony, their voices washing over the stage like a stream of water.

$p
♪ *It's cold outside*\
*Come here, come inside!*\
*Drink with us tonight* ♫
p$

"...!"

It was a Christmas carol.

An English track from the joint winter special album LeTi had released last year.

The retro carol evoked the nineteenth century. It hadn't been a massive public success, but it had earned itself a cult following on the Internet.

The classical, heartwarming melody, combined with the grand orchestral accompaniment, created an elegant, almost secretive feeling.

*And that was only possible because...*

*The accompaniment includes To a New World.*

Yes.

There was no restriction on how many songs they could use.

Instead of completely rearranging To a New World as he had on ⟨Idol Inc.⟩, Park Moondae had simply added the original song's accompaniment.

The simplified, darker melody created a rich atmosphere with the graceful orchestral notes meant to evoke flower fairies.

The song had been shifted to a rhythm more suitable for classical dance.

$p
♪ *Tonight*\
*Hear the bells outside my window*\
*Ding-dong ding-dong* ♫
p$

The singing in the first verse sounded like a ballad.

The two members held Chaeyul by the waist and lifted him into the air as he lay prone. It was a dramatic piece of choreography.

Ryu Gunwoo joined in with a low voice, supporting Chaeyul's vocals, and the resulting sound was firm and rounded like water droplets.

$p
♪ *Ding-dong ding-dong* ♫
p$

The first verse started with storytelling and idol choreography.

The participants didn't smile, since they were acting like vampires, but they used a range of other facial expressions along with the right gestures.

Instead of screaming 'I'm a vampire' with fangs or fake blood, they used indirect methods like costumes, choreography, and gestures to lead the audience to that conclusion.

The hooded robes fluttered behind them like cloaks, revealing outfits adorned with elaborate chains.

$p
♪ *The beautiful sound of winter*\
*Ding-dong ding-dong*\
*The voice of winter* ♫
p$

The red lining of the robes fluttered through the air.

During the pre-chorus, they grabbed their own necks with both hands and spun before moving into the chorus, a group dance with exquisitely controlled dynamics.

The dark, elaborate orchestra grew magnificent again after the chorus.

$p
♪ *Tonight!*\
*In the middle of winter*\
*La lalalalala!* ♫
p$

At the end of the first verse, the three participants threw their bodies backwards in an impressive pose and scattered in all directions, and the accompaniment died away.

The piano melody slid into the center of the empty stage.

♬♩ ♪♪♩- ♬♬♪- ♩

And what immediately began was... The verse melody from To a New World.

Then a new figure appeared.

White hands appeared from behind the trees in time with the music.

Soon, the arms followed, and the whole figure was revealed on stage.

♩♬

It was their black-haired ballerino mentor, wearing a white outfit cut close to his figure.

He performed a light turn, then a grand jeté.

As he leapt through the air, his movements were finely controlled without being excessive, and he blended with the background like a figure in a snow globe.

And a voice began again, lilting like a music box.

$p
♪ *Can't you feel me?* ♫
p$

One of the participants, wearing his hood again on the left side of the stage, turned to face the audience.

"...!"

Ryu Gunwoo.

He held the head microphone close to his mouth with both hands and sang gently and quietly.

$p
♪ *Your warm hands pull me out*\
*It's beautiful*\
*Like a garden in spring* ♫
p$

The lights warmed to beige, and for a moment, the atmosphere of winter disappeared.

The ballerino's perfect movements, beautiful and powerful, continued.

He did an arabesque.

$p
♪ *Umuum, umuum...* ♫
p$

It only lasted a moment. At the end of the short bridge, the orchestra returned...

The ballerino leapt offstage as if he were running away.

And in a moment, the three figures returned to take his place.

The performers threw back their hoods and tossed off their robes, revealing elaborate outfits.

Cold lights flooded the stage with explosive force again.

$p
♪ *Woo! Oooh ooh ooh* ♫
p$

And the choreography of the chorus started again. This time, however, some new moves had been incorporated.

The same turns and jumps the ballerino had performed moments before.

They'd been incorporated cleverly.

*They feel different.*

It was as if they were imitating him, but their movements were closer to dance-pop, completely different from ballet.

Instead of directly comparing the quality of their dancing to their mentor's, the audience would focus on the overall completeness of the performance.

$p
♪ *In the snowy garden*\
*In a teacup, in a sugar bowl* ♫
p$

An exciting idol group dance began, with gestures meant for close-up shots.

And there was a powerful ending.

$p
♪ *The bell rings*\
*Ring ri-ring*\
*Ring-ring-ring* ♫
p$

As the music grew quiet, they turned their faces away from the cameras.

The arrangement then grew cacophonous, and all the sounds fused together with the high-pitched ad-lib vocals.

Finally, the participants raised their arms, looked forward, and smiled.

$p
♪ *The winter is... in!!* ♫
p$

The music ended.

And on the stage where all movements stopped...

Wow!!!

Applause and cheer poured out. Even screams.

"Good job!"

The participants readjusted their robes, gathered themselves, bowed to the audience, and smiled.

It was a dizzying, intense, and dark stage.

It was rather convoluted, perhaps on purpose.

If the theme had been anything else, there would have been a risk of being criticized for being too experimental and ignoring public taste.

But this time, the keyword was 'Art'.

The audience and judges would be expecting something slightly different.

"Hmm."

Ryu Chungwoo turned his head slightly and looked toward the front rows of the audience.

Not surprisingly, the president was trying his best to hide a smile as he gave a slight thumbs-up.

He seemed to approve.

*Wow.*

*That was fun!*

Ryu Chungwoo knew his team's turn was next, but he smiled and clapped eagerly nonetheless.

***

The stage was over.

"Hoo."

I took off the hooded robe and held it in my hand.

It was refreshing to finally be free of it.

*What is this feeling?*

The feeling of liberation was enormous. Anyone who saw it would've thought I'd just won the grand prize.

I folded the robe and gave it to the staff.

*That worked.*

I didn't even need to see the score. Our changes had worked. My sluggish, muddled brain was finally working again as if I'd never struggled at all.

*Perfect.*

There was nothing quite like the adrenaline of being onstage once it kicked in.

I pushed my hair back from my forehead.

"Hyung!"

At that time, the guy who'd taken off his paint-covered outfit ran over and threw his arm around my neck.

Cha Eugene.

"You were great. It was so cool! "

"Ack."

*No, wait... Was this bastard trying to choke me?*

*This American football-crazy guy used to play, didn't he? Was he trying to break my neck?* I managed to slip free of his grasp.

Then he spoke quietly.

"Hyung, let me tell you this in advance."

*Tell me what?*

"You can use any of the stages we did together however you want if you need to. Don't worry about it!"

"...!"

And he pointed to his head and heart in turn with the arm that had almost broken my neck earlier.

"Our stages are here and here. And they're also in reality. Even if you use them in the multiverse, you won't use them all up!"

"......"

I stared at him.

"We must not forget that. Okay?"

If I had heard this a few days ago, I would've interpreted it as 'so do it properly' and would've been gritting my teeth and pounding my head against the wall.

But now that it was over and my head was clear, I listened to him... and I could see he was right.

*Was it me who forgot? I guess I'd been struggling.*

*Who knew I'd be the first to lose it in this strange situation?* I grinned and patted the guy on the shoulder.

"I got it."

"Good!"

The guy grinned.

"And I'll do the same! You wait and see!"

"Yes, okay."

I watched him being taken away by the staff for the interview, then finished changing my clothes.

And a little later, another guy who'd finished his own performance spoke to me as soon as he came into the waiting room.

Ryu Chungwoo.
