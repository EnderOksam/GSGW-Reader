# Chapter 518

Bae Sejin hadn't intentionally kept his role from the members. The timing was just never right.

*We were talking about having memories or not. I couldn't just suddenly bring up my drama!*

Besides, Bae Sejin had already finished filming without any issues. No matter how he thought about it, the topic was a low priority.

So, Bae Sejin made up his mind.

*I have to tell them before I go to sleep tonight.*

That night, however, a surprise birthday party was thrown for Ryu Chungwoo.

"Hyung, happy birthday~!"

That's right. Of all days, it was October 28th, Ryu Chungwoo's birthday.

"......"

*Why didn't I know...*

"Hey, you've been busy too, Hyung."

The surprise party had been secretly prepared by the three members of TeSTAR's middle line, the so-called 'Same-Agers'. They decided to let the members, who had struggled with memory and identity, just have fun. The three gladly put their heads together, divided the roles, and procured the party supplies.

"Just enjoy it~"

Lee Sejin, his former roommate with whom he was now on much better terms, spoke. At Lee Sejin's words, Bae Sejin screamed internally.

*I don't need this kind of consideration...!*

However, Bae Sejin wasn't so socially inept as to have a meaningless outburst.

*...Still, it is a considerate gesture.*

After all, the atmosphere was warm and pleasant. Ryu Chungwoo seemed so unexpectedly moved that he was beyond joy and slightly dazed. In turn, the faces of those who had prepared the party lit up.

Seon Ahyeon, blushing, presented the cake. A cheerful phrase like 'TeSTAR's Eldest Hyung is Handsome' was written on the giant chocolate cake.

"You can, blow out the candles...!"

"...Haha, alright. Should I make a wish too?"

"Ooooh~"

"......"

Of course, Bae Sejin couldn't suddenly bring up a psychopath in this atmosphere.

*Especially when he's been through so much emotional turmoil!*

Ryu Chungwoo deserved to be the star of his own birthday with a peaceful mind.

So, Bae Sejin made a choice like a social butterfly, something he had never done in his life. He decided to 'keep his mouth shut and go with the flow'. The result was a heartwarming scene where only Bae Sejin suffered.

*Ugh.*

"Hyung, what did you wish for?"

"Hmm... For our team to be healthy and promote for a long time?"

"That's a really, great wish...!"

"...It is."

Bae Sejin agreed, accepting a slice of cake that Park Moondae had cut with knife-like precision.

*Hoo.*

Still, the cake was delicious.

After having a glass of non-alcoholic champagne, Bae Sejin tried to subtly bring up the drama again.

"I, I didn't expect this kind of atmosphere, but... I feel that this is a very fun and harmonious team."

"......"

"It makes me want to regain my memories quickly so I can contribute more to this team..."

*How did this day become so meaningful?*

At the words carefully offered by STier Kim Raebin, Bae Sejin almost squeezed his eyes shut.

"...Right."

*Let's give up.*

Bae Sejin decided to aim for the next day.

The very next day was a new filming day for ❬Genuine Match❭. It was the last shoot before a break. They would announce the competition's winner, film some lighthearted content, and adjust the tempo for the middle of the season.

TeSTAR did well, placing second.

"Thank you!"

With a result they had more or less expected, everyone finished filming in a good mood.

For reference, Aether from Wonder Hall, who had newly appeared, received a low rank of fifth place. Their performance, befitting rookies, was full of power and had flashy effects. Since it was a stage with outstanding planning, meticulously constructed by experts, fifth place must have been a disappointing rank.

However, Bae Sejin managed his expression with his years of experience but thought bluntly.

*Honestly, I don't feel bad about it.*

Bae Sejin felt it was a bit like karmic justice.

Of course, next to him, Park Moondae was thinking,*It would have been better for us if those guys had taken first place and faced the backlash.*

In any case, Aether's naive yet ignorant attitude left a strange and uncomfortable impression on TeSTAR, but the filming itself wrapped up well.

"Then we'll see you in two weeks, Sunbaenim!"

"Yes, you guys from TeSTAR, get home safe!"

After bidding farewell to SoftSweet, who had tearfully taken first place, they scattered in the waiting room to pack their things. The atmosphere was relaxed. It was the kind of mood where he could start a conversation with small talk and they could all laugh together.

*Finally... is this the time to say it?*

"Sejin."

"Uh, yeah."

However, Ryu Chungwoo spoke to him first.

"It's late for me to be saying this, isn't it? ...Thank you. I heard you took great care of me so I could get a grip, Sejin."

"...It wasn't anything amazing."

"No, it was amazing."

Ryu Chungwoo's eyes were warm.

"You must have been very flustered by my attitude. It must have been even harder to take care of someone else when you were in a difficult situation yourself."

"......"

"But you took care of me."

Bae Sejin hesitated for a moment but soon tossed out a remark.

"...Because we're friends, of course."

For Bae Sejin, Ryu Chungwoo was pretty much the first friend he had ever made of the same age. Besides, Bae Sejin had received so much one-sided help that being thanked to this extent was actually embarrassing.

Ryu Chungwoo grinned.

"Still, thank you."

"...Alright."

The sincere, almost clumsy words were exchanged gently before ending on a heartwarming note.

A pleasant silence settled.

Bae Sejin thought.

"......"

*In this atmosphere, to suddenly talk about a psychopath role... As if that would work...*

In the end, Bae Sejin gave up on speaking, feeling like he wanted to burst into tears.

...And just like that, the broadcast day arrived.

At midnight on October 31st, Halloween, the members of TeSTAR gathered on the sofa, giddy with joy, thanks to the bold decision of not scheduling anything for the next morning.

"Hyung! It's starting! Sit down, quick! Hurry!"

"......"

Bae Sejin plopped down on the sofa. To hide his anxious heart, he grabbed a random handful of Cha Eugene's popcorn, stuffed it into his mouth, and chewed aggressively.

"...?? What the..."

"It's starting."

Cha Eugene was momentarily taken aback, but he soon heard Park Moondae's voice and fixed his gaze on the TV.

[A dream.]

[This must be a dream.]

[It had to be.]

Episode 1 of the new Netplus series, ❬Doll Hunter❭, which had been released just a few seconds ago, began to play.

***

The plot of ❬Doll Hunter❭ was close to a modern mystery survival story.

One day, the protagonist wakes up to an alarm and realizes that not a single speck of light is coming through the window, even though it's morning. As he gets closer to the window, he notices a chilling fact. The outside of the window was completely filled with damp earth, as if the entire building had been buried alive.

[Aaargh!]

This mystery drama was about escaping from within the bizarrely twisted, dark building. The protagonist had to climb continuously to the upper floors while encountering neighbors who had turned into monsters.

Of course, not all the neighbors had turned into monsters. The majority of the residents were simply buried along with the building.

The protagonist continued to climb, meeting a diverse cast of characters, allies, and enemies. He would even team up and travel with those he formed a connection with. This included a retired firefighter, a student taking the college entrance exam for the third time, a high school baseball player, a colleague from work, and...

[...Hello.]

"Oh, Sejin-hyung!"

The role Bae Sejin played, the medical student 'Jeong Isol', was also a companion who joined in the early to mid-part of the story. He was a man in his mid-twenties with neatly swept-back black hair and rimless glasses.

He was intelligent, had good judgment, and was even equipped with first-aid skills. If this were a game, he would be the number one recruitment priority.

[Stop.]

[...!!]

The scene where he first appeared was particularly impressive. He held a fire hose in front of the protagonist's group, who were in a crisis, and chased away a monster. The engine BGM combined with the action to provide a sense of catharsis.

Even Ryu Chungwoo, who had little interest in surreal media, was lightly impressed.

"Sejin, you look really cool."

"...Uh, yeah. Thanks."

A capable supporter who not only prevents frustrating plot developments but also maintains the team's morality. That coveted role shone even brighter with Bae Sejin, who excelled in both looks and acting skills.

[This way.]

[Ah...!]

Even his reserved nature felt charming.

Moreover, this medical student invited the protagonist's group to his hideout and even treated the injured.

That hideout was a convenience store full of supplies.

[A convenience store!]

[Shh.]

Watching the protagonist's group hastily devour greasy, salty canned ham and hot instant rice made one crave ham and rice, even knowing it was PPL.

"Wow~ Hyung is even taking care of the PPL? You really have an important role in many ways~"

"You're totally cool. Awesome!"

"...Yeah."

*An important role in many ways... that's right.*

Bae Sejin couldn't stop the members from excitedly watching nonstop through the second episode. As is often the case with works based on long and solid original stories, the drama progressed at a tremendous pace. Thanks to that, the members were on the edge of their seats, continuously immersed in the story.

There wasn't a single dull moment. They even started analyzing the protagonists' strategies and giving advice.

"Ah, they're going to use that convenience store as a base now."

"I guess so."

"Hmm~ I think it would be better to go up a bit more. Aish, but then again, it'd be hard to think like that in that situation, right?"

The 31-story mixed-use building had its structure twisted upside down, so the residential officetels on the top floors had become the bottom floors. Around the 28th floor, there was a convenience store aimed at the residents.

Opinions were divided among the members on whether it was better to leave the officetel floor and go up a little further to the commercial area.

Even Seon Ahyeon asked Park Moondae.

"Um, Moondae, what do you think?"

Park Moondae answered nonchalantly.

"Well... either way, I think they'll leave the convenience store."

"...?"

"Because it's a prime target for an attack."

The moment he finished speaking, the convenience store appeared on the screen again. Its exterior was dark, the lights turned off.

"......"

[...Huh? Uh oh?]

The protagonist had gone to a lower floor to check a distress signal from middle school students trapped in a small officetel room. In doing so, the group had split in two. The uninjured people moved while the injured people remained at the convenience store.

Now, they had returned to the convenience store where only the injured had remained. All the LED lights were off, creating an eerie atmosphere.

"Ooooh."

"I see. It's obvious there's food in a convenience store, so anyone could target it."

"Mmm~ They really should have gone to the floor with the shops. That would've dispersed the risk a bit..."

Even as they said this, both Ryu Chungwoo and Lee Sejin couldn't take their eyes off the screen. From their own words, they realized something.

*A convenience store is easy to attack.*

*Then how did the medical student manage to use it as a hideout without being attacked until now...?*

That question crossed the minds of a few members.

[......]

On the screen, the protagonist and a few members of his group finally entered the convenience store. Inside the dark convenience store, cast in the protagonist's shadow, the shelves and counter, touched by a faint light, still looked fine.

[Hello?]

However, no answer came. Only a suffocating white noise filled the screen.

*Did they evacuate beforehand?*

Just as that hopeful guess passed by, the beam of the protagonist's flashlight turned toward the convenience store's stockroom door.

Through the crack of the open door, shoes and legs began to appear. They were an unnatural sight, lying horizontally instead of standing vertically.

THUMP. THUMP.

A sound effect like a beating heart dominated the screen. The camera, from the protagonist's point of view, slowly panned up to capture the only figure standing inside the stockroom door.

[You're back?]

And everyone realized. The medical student hadn't been attacked in the convenience store. He was the one who did the attacking.

Behind the medical student, who calmly wiped blood from his hands with a wet wipe, wide-eyed corpses were briefly captured by the shaking camera. The things piled on top were shown in a close-up. They were the protagonist's companions who had remained in the convenience store due to their injuries.

Especially the first members the protagonist had met, who had significant weight in the first episode. Among them was the heroine, the woman forming a love line with the protagonist. There was also a beloved supporting character, a colleague from the protagonist's company, who everyone thought would make it to the end.

And yet... that's right. In just two episodes, the starting members were all dead.

By the hands of the medical student.

"......"

"......"

Bae Sejin desperately began to avoid his members' gazes.
