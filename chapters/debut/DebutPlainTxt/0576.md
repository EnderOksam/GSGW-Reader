# Chapter 576

The search proceeded quickly.

[Confirmed! Moving two pitches down from the current location.]

[Southeast slab face... possibility of a fall along the victim's route...]

But even with a narrowed search area, the mountain at night limited their speed. It was impossible to predict the victim's current condition.

[Team 1, Team 1 moving to the traverse section.]

Radio chatter, running feet, and breathless alerts assaulted my ears through the phone. I had just endured a two-hour taxi ride from hell.

"......"

I got out of the taxi and boarded a mobile rescue vehicle parked at the mountain's base. I was not allowed to enter the mountain myself. There was no reason to double the number of victims at this time of night.

All I could do was answer their questions and wait.

Just wait endlessly.

Continuously.

If someone said I was lucky to even be able to wait for his return, I could not argue.

"Sir, aren't you cold?"

"I'm fine."

I could feel furtive glances on me. Of course, it was not because I looked cold.

*They must have recognized me.*

I did not underestimate the public recognition of TeSTAR's Park Moondae. Besides, they would have noticed even if they did not recognize Park Moondae. I wore a stage outfit with a padded jacket thrown carelessly over it. Seeing my current state, they would wonder what on earth this person was doing before rushing over.

MURMUR, MURMUR.

The prickling sensation of people's interest occasionally pierced my skin.

I thought.

*It's understandable.*

For these people, seeing the family of a lost person must be routine, but a celebrity showing up in the middle of it all is an event.

*Just not acknowledging it shows their professionalism.*

So, it was only natural to hear such a question.

"Um, but... you said you're Ryu Gunwoo's older brother?"

"......"

"We jokingly call each other brother. He's a close hyung, like family."

"......"

They nodded at this flimsy explanation. They probably figured a famous idol would not have intentionally impersonated a family member.

"......"

I never imagined being famous would be beneficial in this way. Just because they did not ask, 'So you're not his real family, right? Why are you even here?' did not mean I felt any relief.

*Whatever, it's fine.*

However, my brain would not hold up if I did not have these kinds of thoughts every now and then.

Because...

*There's still no news!*

Two hours. It had already been two hours without a single trace of Keundal.

I clenched my fists. My head spun dizzily.

*Did I narrow the search area for nothing?*

What if Keundal was stuck somewhere else, injured, and I had ruined his chance of rescue by spouting baseless nonsense? What guarantee was there that I was not wrong? What guarantee was there that I was being helpful?

*Shut up.*

I was on the verge of panicking at the slightest thing. It was useless. I took a deep breath. Cold sweat ran down my face, but I did not bother wiping it away.

Calmly, I asked a rescue worker on standby in the vehicle.

"Excuse me, how much of the area I mentioned earlier has been searched?"

"By now... you can consider it about half done."

It was not an exact figure, but his attitude suggested he was not lying.

*And they would naturally search the places with the highest probability of finding a missing person first...*

This meant Keundal was not on the path the rescue experts predicted, the one where they thought 'The missing Ryu Gunwoo would have moved this way'. He had not moved in a conventional way. His path had diverged from the predictions of these people, who had considered the high possibility that Ryu Gunwoo was suicidal.

That was actually not a bad sign.

*Good.*

I pressed my knuckles. The area searched in two hours was half, or maybe even less. Even at a minimum, if it took another two hours to search the rest, it would be well past 9 PM. The temperature had long since dropped below minus five degrees Celsius. If he had been on the mountain since yesterday, this would be his second brush with the danger of freezing to death.

If he was within the search area I had narrowed down, that would be a relief. If not, it would be the worst-case scenario. Then I would have to beg or bow my head and ask them to search other places, admitting my mistake.

"......"

I bit the inside of my cheek. If only he had his smartphone.

*I desperately want to ask what the hell he's thinking.*

No, this situation would not have happened in the first place. If the System still existed and we could just talk through pop-ups...

*No.*

Nothing would get better by rambling about how I missed that shitty situation. I can still do it now. There must be clues in what he has said and done so far.

Think.

Among all the conversations I have had with him, or the facts I knew about him, what was the most vivid thing? What would best reveal his true intentions?

"......"

*There is.*

I raised my head. I have directly experienced Keundal's thoughts before, back when Keundal was the original Park Moondae. He showed me how he came to want to die while watching the videos on my fancam account, the situation leading to him entering that old motel, and how he felt.

*The reward for the first status abnormality.*

It still comes to mind vividly.

[I'm tired of living.]

His thoughts.

"......"

I slowly opened my mouth.

"May I ask one question?"

"Yes?"

"You're currently searching the spots on Mount Mani with a good view of the sea, right?"

The rescue worker nodded slightly.

"Among those spots, are there any you've prioritized searching?"

The worker seemed conflicted, but he soon spoke.

"...Below the rocky ridge. Usually, um, there are areas where we find a lot of stranded people. It's originally a restricted area, but some people recklessly climb up to get a good view of the sea. If they misstep and fall there, there are spots where it's difficult to get out from if they get injured."

"...Yes."

"We've thoroughly scanned those areas."

Isolated after a misstep, or a place one cannot leave of their own will. It was a rational deduction and search.

However.

"Could you also check a location from which he could come down on his own?"

I knew the depths of his depression.

[It doesn't matter if I live or stop living, right?]

[But I didn't particularly want to die either.]

It was the kind of depression where you do not want to die, but you do not particularly want to move either. If he had really wanted to die, he would have chosen a different method.

Because.

[The weather was too cold, so I couldn't bring myself to jump into the water or climb a mountain. I felt like I would give up halfway.]

Climbing a mountain was a method he himself had ruled out. That was why he chose the motel.

*So, he probably didn't want to die.*

He will not die on purpose, but he will not move because he is exhausted and lethargic.

If that is the case.

"A location with a good view of the sea, one that he can leave on his own. ...Please, just once."

"......"

He could go somewhere else, but he is just staying put.

*Because it's a place where he can see his parents, alone and undisturbed.*

If this did not work, I would have to admit I knew nothing about Keundal and retract all my statements, and I would have to quickly bow my head and beg them to check the other mountains.

But before that, these people had to listen to me.

*Please.*

It was a suggestion that was difficult to justify with rational words. I braced myself for rejection, gritting my teeth to come up with an excuse.

Then, the rescue worker seemed to hesitate, but in the end, he picked up the radio.

"One moment."

"...!"

It worked. I almost tore at my own hands but stopped myself.

I was glad I was a celebrity. Truly.

Then, the agonizing time began to flow again.

*Fuck... please.*

My arms and legs trembled with impatience, anxiety, hope, and fear. Suppressing the thoughts that spiraled into negative logic, I sat still in my seat.

How much time passed during that long, long wait?

Suddenly, I noticed the radios crackling with unusual speed.

[This is...]

[Backup! Requesting backup.]

[Confirming location...]

Amidst the mix of alert tones, footsteps, and various voices, I found one phrase that was different from before. The words I had been waiting for.

[We've found him!]

A moment later, I saw a helicopter descending from the mountain.

"Be careful!"

The long stretcher suspended below the helicopter touched the ground gently, and the straps were securely detached. A person staggered as he was helped to his feet from within it.

Ryu Gunwoo.

It was Keundal. He was moving.

[Preparing for hospital transport...]

"......"

In the midst of the radio chatter, he looked at the ground with a pale, dazed face, then lifted his head.

His eyes met mine.

"...Hyung."

Without a word, I ran and hugged him tightly.

Then I realized.

I was not too late.

***

Park Moondae, no, Keundal stared at the food in front of him. The steaming soup dish was haejangguk.

Just like that one time.

The twenty-year-old who had tried to die in a motel, failed, and woken up again, sitting dazedly in front of that building. The person who had bought him a meal without a word. Back then, at the gukbap restaurant, that person had sat across the table with a tired expression, silently eating.

Just like now.

"......"

Although this was a hospital and their appearances were swapped, it felt as if nothing had changed. The warm indifference that asked no questions, and the hot food, all of it.

"The basic check-up is done. They said there's nothing wrong except for some minor frostbite. You barely had any dehydration symptoms either. Did you bring water?"

"...Yes."

"Good job."

There were no other words. Keundal quietly dipped his spoon into the haejangguk and lowered his head. A sob rose in his dry heart, but he swallowed it.

*I can't.*

He knew he had caused his hyung too much trouble. He opened his rough, dry mouth to explain the situation, even just a little, to the person who must have been shocked.

"I didn't... I didn't mean to get stranded."

"......"

"I went to see my parents, found a quiet place where I could see them alone... and I guess I just stayed there, and too much time passed."

"I see."

Park Moondae did not point out that Ryu Gunwoo had been absent from work for five days, nor did he question how he could 'just' sit on that mountain for a whole day in the cold. He did not yell, demanding to know what he was thinking. He just nodded.

Perhaps because of that, Keundal found himself saying things he had not realized he was thinking.

"When I went to Sokcho... I actually just sat by the sea the whole time."

"Mm."

"And then, since it was the same sea, I started wanting to see my parents... but I just felt so drained."

*Did I really think that?*

Stories poured out that made him question himself. The emptiness he had not spoken of, not wanting to cause worry, pushed its way out between his sentences.

"...So, I tried to find a hobby, but I couldn't think of anything... so I just existed. Then, I thought maybe I'd go to the mountain."

His hyung listened quietly, only checking to see that Keundal was eating. He replied, pouring him a glass of warm water.

"You went through a lot."

Keundal suddenly gripped his spoon tightly as something surged up inside him.

"You, um, you told me to live as Ryu Gunwoo, right?"

"......"

His hyung nodded slightly.

So he finally said it.

"But can't I just live as Keundal...?"

"......"

"I, I don't know what I should do, and I don't really... know what I want to do. I'll just cheer for TeSTAR, and even though the Status Window is gone, there are other things I can do to help with your activities,"

"......"

"...!"

Keundal looked up. His hyung swallowed a spoonful of rice and answered nonchalantly.

"But this time, let's try doing it the other way around."

"Huh?"

"This time, let's try focusing on you instead of me."

"......"

"If you don't know what you should do, or what you want to do... we can figure it out together. You helped me find a way to debut, didn't you?"

"But,"

"But?"

"You're busy, Hyung..."

"We improved TeSTAR's work-life balance, so we agreed to take one or two days off a week."

"R-Really?"

"Yeah. Ah, your hand stopped."

"Ah!"

Keundal hurriedly started moving his spoon again. The conversation flowed so smoothly that he felt strength returning to his body. The soup was still hot and spicy.

"Of course, it's fine to talk about TeSTAR too. Ask me anytime. I have a few things to ask you about TeSTAR as well."

"Yes!"

He had something to do. At those words, he felt more energized. Keundal moved his hands more aggressively. As his stomach filled, he started talking about more sensible things.

"Hyung, you must have been... really shocked this time. I'm so sorry. For making you worry and for being such a bother..."

"I told you it's fine. Oh, and I've already compensated the rescue team members separately, so don't worry about that either."

"......"

Keundal almost let his jaw drop. He had not thought that far.

"I-I'm sorry! I caused so much trouble!"

"They weren't that angry. Since the rescue was successful, after all."

"......"

From his tone, he could tell that the other person had been very worried. To think he had waited for hours until he was rescued.

*...*

Keundal studied the other's face. A sharp pang of guilt, apology, and gratitude pierced his chest. He looked haggard and tired, but thanks to the makeup, it was not too noticeable. As expected of an idol.

Then he realized.

*...Huh?*

The perfectly styled hair and face, and peeking out from under the padded jacket, a uniform with flashy accessories.

It was a stage outfit.

"......"

TeSTAR's stage.

A chill ran down his spine as Keundal opened his mouth.

"Hyung, today... what's the date?"

There was no answer, but he had already done the math. It started with him sitting dazedly in the entryway on Tuesday morning, and he had been missing for five days. If he added up the days, then today was Saturday.

And this Saturday was.

"The, the TeSTAR concert... isn't it?"

He hurriedly raised his head. The current time came into focus, his vision as clear as if he had been doused with cold water.

10 PM.

The time when the concert would be over.

"......"

"Hyung?"

The hyung across from him said nothing.
