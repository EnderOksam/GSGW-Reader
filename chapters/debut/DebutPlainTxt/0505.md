# Chapter 505

It was the day of the first group recording for ❬Genuine Match❭.

Greetings were exchanged among the cast, which was packed with big names and established figures. There were no rookies in sight.

TeSTAR was no exception.

"Ahyeon is still a Flower Deer, and Moondae, you can stop sending me red ginseng during the holidays! I received it every time and even talked about it on the radio!"

"Hahaha!"

Myudi happily offered words of blessing and jokes to each member, one by one. She finally turned her gaze to TeSTAR's Leader.

More precisely, she turned to the person she thought was the Leader.

"It's been a while, Chungwoo. It must be tough being the Leader, right?"

"Haha, I'm doing well since the members are so great."

However, this STier Chungwoo had also participated in ❬Idol Inc.❭ and met the vocal trainer Myudi, so his response was no problem.

I watched him, checking to see that he chatted with Myudi with a smile.

*He's fine.*

Anyway, Myudi's casting was probably not a coincidence.

*I'm sure that in the beginning, casting offers were sent out to big names who had some connection to TeSTAR.*

For the other party to calculate that 'they can benefit from the editing', these kinds of publicly known relationships are important.

*As soon as TeSTAR signed the appearance contract, they probably sent out the first round of casting offers emphasizing that connection.*

Then, once a few cast members were confirmed, they would broaden the horizon for casting contacts from there.

Perhaps that was why the cast members connected to us did not end with Myudi.

"Moondae! Oh my goodness!"

As soon as we visited the next waiting room, the members of SoftSweet waved their hands enthusiastically.

That's right. The girl group from LeTi, VTIC's agency, who left behind the legendary, nonsensical concept song 'POP☆CON'.

SoftSweet was also part of the cast. They even managed to recruit one of the members who had already succeeded as an actress.

"Hello, Sohyeon."

"Oh my~ Wow, we meet again like this!"

It was a very unique case, with the original members fully reuniting to appear on a survival show. However, it was by no means a foolish choice.

*They're quite clever.*

Since their heyday was already over, they had little to lose even if their results were not good here, where many big names were among the cast, and since the one member who was currently doing well had succeeded as an actress, her field was different, so there would be no negative impact on her image.

They just had to avoid any personality controversies, but it was not like they were idols who had only been on broadcast for a day or two. They would know how to avoid it. Weren't they the ones who survived even during their nonsensical concept era?

I was inwardly nodding in agreement when it happened.

"You know, part of the reason we decided to come on the show was because we heard you were appearing, Moondae! Please take care of us!"

"...?"

"Ahahat, look at Moondae's expression!"

*What are they talking about?*

Just then, Sohyeon patted the shoulder of a heartily laughing SoftSweet member and said with a pleased smile, "Moondae is our fan, isn't he! It gives us courage to know there's at least one person who will watch us seriously."

Hmm. It had been several years since I danced to 'POP☆CON' on ❬Idol Inc.❭, so it was too late to clarify that I was not a fan. In the end, I shamelessly nodded.

*I'll just mention it in an interview or something.*

"It's an honor."

"Heol, we're the ones who are honored!"

Just as the friendly, concluding conversation continued, a playful question popped up.

"As the Leader, what do you think? Is it true that Moondae is still our fan?"

*...!*

The question was for Ryu Chungwoo. Given its nature, it seemed to have been naturally directed to the Leader.

*No.*

This STier Ryu Chungwoo would have no way of knowing whether I did the 'POP☆CON' dance or popping on ❬Idol Inc.❭. Naturally, he would not know about the meme that I was their fan.

I clenched my fists.

*I'm going in for the cover.*

Lee Sejin, who had been acting as the substitute Leader for the day, also subtly flicked his eyes, about to jump in.

"Of course."

STier Ryu Chungwoo answered cleanly before he could.

"Moondae even plays your songs during our live broadcasts."

"......"

"Heol! Really? That's daebak..."

"Thank you, Moondae!"

The conversation flowed naturally. STier Ryu Chungwoo smoothly wrapped up the greeting, mentioning TeSTAR's past activities without any issues.

Lee Sejin observed the atmosphere, then smiled without jumping in and kept his mouth shut. The other guys and I also rode the flow and ended the conversation well, but my head was in chaos.

"......"

*No way.*

"See you in a bit~"

"Yep!"

Click.

As we left SoftSweet's waiting room, I looked back at Ryu Chungwoo.

"Just now, that was..."

*How did you know how to answer that?*

The guy scratched his face as if slightly embarrassed, then grinned. "Um, I was watching some performances, and videos popped up in my algorithm... The fans have organized TeSTAR's activities really well."

"......"

"To think you watched all of that... Thank you. You're amazing, really...!"

"Haha, it's not that big of a deal... but thanks."

No. This was not something that could be brushed off with an 'amazing'.

*It wasn't just performance videos, he watched all of TeSTAR's activities?*

From the way he spoke, it sounded like he just stumbled upon them through the algorithm, but judging by his answer earlier, it was as if he had memorized the entire fan wiki.

Moreover, Ryu Chungwoo was not the type to do that kind of research. I doubted STier Ryu Chungwoo would be any different. He must have deliberately looked it up.

*When on earth did he do that?*

*Did this guy even sleep?*

It was astounding that he voluntarily studied all of that when no one had even asked him to.

Amidst all that, a calm question came back. "Did I make any mistakes earlier?"

"No. It was perfect."

"Haha, thanks."

*No, I'm the one who wants to ask the questions, not answer them. What kind of mental fortitude allows you to produce this kind of output after being dropped into a strange world without a Status Window?*

*It's not like he's the protagonist of a novel.*

However, STier Ryu Chungwoo just continued speaking calmly. "Still, if I'm misunderstanding anything, let me know. It seems things are often presented more gently to the fans than what actually happened."

"...Yes."

Fine. I don't know anymore, fuck.

*Is this mental strength why Cha Eugene said he was scary?*

It was quite convincing.
