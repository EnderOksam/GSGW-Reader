# Chapter 473

The medical team diagnosed Cha Eugene's condition as 'stress-induced confusion'. This was a known issue in the celebrity industry, where people saw all sorts of strange things. There had been several similar cases in the past.

"They can't sleep, and when they suddenly come to their senses, they find themselves throwing things..." a staff member explained.

It was an ambiguous diagnosis. Cha Eugene was awake and his tests showed he was healthy. The doctors believed he would likely be fine after some mental and physical rest.

"I understand. Thank you...!" Kim Raebin said, his expression easing. He seemed relieved just to hear an expert mention 'previous cases'.

Lee Sejin then started a separate conversation with the medical staff. He asked what measures to take if Cha Eugene did not recover on his own within a few hours.

Staff members ran around them, making calls and working frantically. Half were likely scheduling meetings, while the other half urgently adjusted schedules.

*What a mess.*

By then, this 'STier' Cha Eugene seemed to have realized the situation was strange. He must have noticed he was not in the United States the moment the medical staff and employees rushed in speaking Korean.

"Moondae, can you stay here with Eugene for now?" Lee Sejin asked. "I think it'd be better to divide the work that way."

"...Alright."

From Cha Eugene's perspective, a group of strangers was acting like they knew him well. However, he did not cause a scene during the examination.

[You want me to explain TeSTAR? Well.]

[Oh... no. I was sleeping.]

He just gave vague, uncooperative answers to their questions.

*Is he sizing up the situation?*

Just then, the door flew open.

BAM!

"Guys!"

"...!"

Another member, who had been in the middle of a schedule, urgently entered. It was not Ryu Chungwoo, who Lee Sejin said would arrive soon.

"Sejin!" Bae Sejin called out, panting heavily.

He must have run with all his might. He clutched the doorknob to catch his breath, his face flushed red.

"Cha... The shoot ended early, hoo, so I came right away... Is Cha Eugene awake?!"

"Yes!"

He seemed so anxious that he had rushed over the moment his schedule ended, without even calling. He greeted Kim Raebin and strode toward Cha Eugene.

*I thought Ryu Chungwoo would be the first to arrive.*

It seemed Bae Sejin's filming location was closer.

Wait a minute.

*...Bae Sejin?*

I suddenly realized something about my previous life, the one where I had not participated in ❬Re-listed! Idol Inc.❭ or entered Park Moondae's body.

Back then, Bae Sejin had also debuted and was active as a member of STier.

Briefly.

['Idol Inc.' Alumnus Lee Sejin, Arrested on Suspicion of Drug Distribution.]

"......"

...that is, until he appeared on the nine o'clock news as a drug supplier.

STier's activities had not been smooth from the start due to agency and fandom issues. However, it was obvious to anyone that the drug news was the fatal blow that sent STier's career downhill.

"......"

And if the person sitting in this hospital room was...

"Oh."

...that 'STier' Cha Eugene.

*Fuck.*

I immediately moved closer to the bed. I could hear Bae Sejin asking worriedly from a short distance away.

"Are, are you okay?"

Cha Eugene did not answer. He just stared at Bae Sejin, as if he had seen something completely out of place.

Then he opened his mouth.

"Lee Sejin?"

"...!? Why, why is he calling me..."

[I think you guys picked the wrong person. Seriously, this junkie...]

"...!"

Damn it.

[Shut up.]

I hurriedly whispered to 'Cha Eugene', then approached Bae Sejin. "He just woke up, so he's in a weird state. He's saying he's in America."

"Th-that's right!" Kim Raebin agreed vehemently. "Ah! He also talked about brownies and chocolate chip cookies! Could the stress have been caused by holding back on snacks during our promotion period?"

*That's not it.*

Unfortunately, Bae Sejin's eyes grew serious.

"R-really? ...Ah! There's a bakery right out front! I'll go buy some, just wait!"

"...! Indeed! I shall go with you!"

The two of them scrambled out of the hospital room, looking ready to buy out every brownie and cookie in the store.

SLAM!

"......"

*Was it better this way?*

*Hoo.*

I stared at the closed door and held back a sigh. The situation was completely overwhelming.

*Still... I should start with a conversation.*

Now that it was quiet, it seemed like a good time. However, I was not the only one waiting for the right moment.

With everyone outside busy and the room now quiet, the guy who had been observing the situation made his move.

"Excuse me. Umm, 'Moondae'?"

"......"

I turned my head.

The 'STier' Cha Eugene sat on the hospital bed. He remembered the name Kim Raebin had called me and was now putting it to good use.

[You know what kind of crazy shit is going on here, don't you?]

"......"

[I might have eaten a special brownie and be having some kind of ridiculous K-POP hallucination.]

He pointed a finger at his own blonde hair.

[I don't dye my hair anymore.]

In that moment, he reached out. He grabbed my shoulder and leaned in, his posture intimidating.

"...!"

[And you recognized me. When I said 'STier', when I said 'junkie', you recognized me.]

"......"

Cha Eugene did not remove his hand from my shoulder. He maintained eye contact and slowly tapped the side of my arm with his other hand.

[I don't know what the hell "TeSTAR" is, but if this is some kind of prank show, you'd better be ready for a lawsuit. I don't remember signing anything.]

"......"

Hoo. I took a deep breath. It was complicated.

*I can't just... start by decking him.*

Given the situation, I was not even angry.

*This just sucks...*

I suppressed a headache and opened my mouth.

"First of all."

[Hm?]

"Who in America would be crazy enough to do something like this with a member of a disbanded K-POP group?"

"......"

"And you're not on drugs. Neither is Kim Raebin."

*I have no idea why they'd put drugs in perfectly good brownies in America, though.*

'Cha Eugene's' eyes widened slightly. The moment his grip on my shoulder loosened, I roughly pulled his hand away.

"But there is one thing you said that's right."

"What?"

"Something crazy is happening."

"...huh?"

I tossed the dazed guy's hand back onto the bed. It was the most kindness I could offer.

***

New members.

New activities.

New developments.

Before the two returned from the bakery, I briefly summarized the activities of 'TeSTAR', not STier, for Cha Eugene.

Of course, his reaction was as expected.

[In my opinion, there are three possibilities. Want to hear them?]

"What."

[Either you're on drugs, or I'm on drugs.]

"That's two."

[Or we both are.]

Damn it.

*Why has this bastard become a broken record about drugs?*

I barely stopped myself from grabbing my head.

"Why is it always about drugs? Because it's a hallucination? Is that a symptom of drugs?"

[That's what they say.]

"......"

*So he's never tried them himself.*

Thankfully, it seemed he did not go that far off the rails.

[Then am I crazy? Hey, do you have any idea how much an emergency room visit costs in the States? Why would you carelessly bring a crazy person to the hospital?]

"I told you, you're not," I said quietly, pressing my temples. "Just look out the window."

"What?"

"You just have to turn your head. Look."

I opened the blinds slightly.

Cha Eugene shifted his gaze to look outside.

"...!"

"That's right, it's Korea."

The Hangul signs and familiar cityscape were visible outside the window. It was Seoul. He might claim to be in America, but he had spent years based in Seoul. There was no way he would not recognize it.

"Holy..."

RATTLE!

Only after opening the window to confirm it for himself did Cha Eugene's attitude change slightly.

[Are you serious?]

"Yeah."

This meant he was starting to consider that this was all true.

"So we don't have to worry about the hospital bill. We're a K-POP boy group. The company will cover the costs."

"......"

"You're in a group called 'TeSTAR' right now, and we're your members."

[Bullshit.]

[It's not bullshit.]

Hearing footsteps running toward the room, likely from a successful brownie purchase, I muttered nonchalantly.

"In just half a day, you'll have no choice but to accept it too."

***

"We're leaving."

"Yep!"

Since he was physically healthy and showed no signs of violent outbursts or seizures, Cha Eugene was discharged. Of course, all his schedules for the next three days were canceled.

"Eugene, are you uncomfortable anywhere?" Ryu Chungwoo asked gently from the front seat.

"No. I'm fine," Cha Eugene answered politely.

He soon turned his head with an uncomfortable expression and muttered.

[This fucking weird world...]

"Language."

Cha Eugene gave me a look that said, 'What right do you have?' but he answered obediently.

"Sorry. It's been a few months since I became free."

"......"

I remembered. The three who did unit activities as STier until the very end were Ryu Chungwoo, Kim Raebin, and Cha Eugene. I remembered that even those guys, who had been particularly dedicated, had gone silent after the disbandment.

This 'STier' Cha Eugene seemed to have returned to his home country, the United States, as soon as their activities ended.

But... 'free'.

*Did he hate being an idol that much?*

Then again, doing the job you were given properly and liking the job you were given were not the same thing.

*Then again, the situation was pretty fucked up.*

Even knowing that, it left a bitter aftertaste.

"This is the dorm we moved into. If it doesn't help you feel settled because we haven't been here long, please tell us!"

"OK."

Cha Eugene, who had silently looked out the window the whole car ride, did not become any more talkative after arriving at the dorm. He just seemed to be checking out the dorm's condition.

The incident happened next.

"This is a playlist of our music videos. I brought it thinking that maybe watching them would help Cha Eugene come to his senses faster!"

"Good idea!"

"We can watch them too, it's been a while."

They began a marathon viewing, starting with TeSTAR's debut music video, 'Magical Boy'.

"......"

To be honest, I wondered if showing him this was the right move.

*If I don't show him, he's not going to be convinced.*

Cha Eugene might not seem like it, but he was a hardcore realist. In this situation where we had no prior relationship, he would only pretend to cooperate if presented with convincing evidence.

And sure enough.

"...This one."

"Cha Eugene! Do you have something to say about this song?"

"How did you do it?"

"...?"

[Ah, I'm dizzy~]

He pointed to the music video for 'High Five'. It filled the screen with TeSTAR dancing and smiling in baseball uniforms on a school field under a brilliant blue sky.

'High Five'.

It was the song the company had originally prepared as the first title track for the ❬Idol Inc.❭ debut group. The song was scheduled to be an ambiguous disco-style track that did not fit the concept.

However.

"The company originally wanted to go with a disco style, but we persuaded them, even using a PowerPoint presentation," I explained. "We argued to go with a refreshing rock style."

"......"

"And Raebin arranged it."

Cha Eugene silently watched the 'High Five' music video to the end. He then watched the next music video, and the one after that.

Finally, when they reached 'Better Me', he spoke.

"OK, I'll admit it."

"...?"

"TeSTAR."

STier Cha Eugene lifted his head. As he pronounced 'TeSTAR' with a Korean accent, his face showed neither realization nor bewilderment, but something heavy. It was like a shadow.

"......"

For some reason, it did not feel very refreshing.

*Still, we're at the starting line.*

From now on, I had to control him so he did not do anything rash.

"...?? What is Cha Eugene saying right now?"

Of course, that was after I explained things to the others.

***

A few minutes later, Ryu Chungwoo and Bae Sejin conversed in confusion.

"So... this Eugene is the Eugene from when Moondae was Gunwoo, before he became Moondae?" Lee Sejin asked. "Hmm? Is that the right way to put it?"

"I think that's right... Yeah, it is," Ryu Chungwoo replied.

Even though they had been through all sorts of things, this was a first. Everyone seemed flustered, but at the same time, they also seemed relieved.

"...Anyway, since Cha Eugene isn't sick... is this a relief?" Bae Sejin asked.

"Figuring out the cause is, I suppose," Kim Raebin muttered blankly.

"Still, to think he debuted from an ❬Idol Inc.❭ that Moondae didn't participate in... I can't really imagine it," Lee Sejin said. "Right? I feel like I wouldn't have debuted. Ah, is it okay to ask who did debut?"

It seemed to be a mix of pure curiosity and an attempt to figure this Cha Eugene out.

At that, Cha Eugene grinned and rested his chin on the table.

"It's not okay."

"...!"

"It's meaningless for you guys to hear it. So I'm not telling you. Got it?"

The atmosphere instantly froze.
