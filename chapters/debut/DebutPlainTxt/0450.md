# Chapter 450

Seeing Cheongryeo at an awards ceremony was nothing new. After all, neither VTIC nor TeSTAR had ever produced results that would get them uninvited from an awards show.

However, seeing him from this position was something that had not happened in a very long time. I was in the audience, holding a camera and taking pictures of the guy.

*...Hmm.*

Cheongryeo wore a suit. He still looked like he would fetch a high price. It might have been a natural aura for someone who had been in that spot for decades.

*Not that I'm going to sell him anymore, though.*

I habitually snapped a few shots.

It was then.

"......"

Cheongryeo turned his gaze. Then he slowly raised his hand.

"......"

It was a light wave. However, the direction was coincidentally toward the section of the audience where I was. It was enough to make me feel like our eyes met through the viewfinder.

*...No way.*

...Did he notice? No. Even for that bastard, it would not make sense for him to know without any hints.

*Could he be seeing something with whatever GM functions he has left...*

At that moment, a pop-up appeared before my eyes.

[Hyung! You're here?]

It was Keundal.

...! Yeah.

I ignored the gaze I felt from the person next to me as I answered aloud, then replied again internally.

*Did the VTIC members sitting next to you say anything?*

[Uh... no? They haven't said anything to me. ...Sh-should I try talking to them?]

*No.*

Just avoid them.

[Phew.]

*Why did he even ask when he was going to let out a sigh of relief like that?*

I shook my head and replied.

*You don't need to worry about that. It'll be fine as long as you just sit there quietly.*

Unlike the radio show, he had no lines to say, so he only had one role. To sit there looking cool.

[Right?]

*Of course. Ah, right.*

*Make sure you focus when other artists are performing.*

The moment you yawn or frown, WeTube could be flooded with clips of it.

*It can't be helped if you let your guard down once or twice, but...*

[...I will never let my guard down!]

I wanted to tell him he did not have to go that far, but it was actually the best method. I decided to just cheer him on.

I lifted the camera I had lowered and pointed it back toward the artists' seating area. Cheongryeo had already turned his gaze back to the stage.

*Was I just imagining it?*

I clicked my tongue briefly and took a few more pictures of TeSTAR. The same went for Park Moondae, who was currently Keundal. The guy, looking all tense and focused, seemed quite plausible.

To think that was him imitating me.

*...So this is what it feels like.*

I silently kept pressing the shutter. Taking pictures of TeSTAR's Park Moondae myself felt a little strange.

"Excuse me, sorry, your arm."

"......"

In the process, my arm bumped into the person next to me a few times. This person was quite an aggressive photographer.

I turned my shoulder and glanced at the person who had spoken to me. Seeing that our camera angles were similar, this person must also be taking pictures of TeSTAR...

...!!

...That's right.

It was a familiar face. The problem was that it was a face familiar to Ryu Gunwoo as well.

*...Ryu Seojin.*

The person sitting next to me was a senior from my university photography club. The younger sister of ❬Idol Inc.❭'s author, Ryu Seorin. In other words... she was the fansite master who took pictures of me and Lee Sejin.

***

*The lighting is trash.*

Ryu Seojin grumbled as she set up her camera. The area was packed with people holding cameras similar to her own. It was probably because this spot was perfect for getting good pictures.

*Besides, this awards show doesn't go out of its way to film audience members with cameras, so it's easier to shoot here.*

The photographers had blatantly flocked here.

The seat next to her was no exception. A well-built man wearing a black mask held a camera. However, it was a bit strange that he was dressed in a black suit. It was a respectable and neat 'working' attire that anyone would recognize.

*Is he a staff member?*

Then again, he did not have a press pass around his neck, but he could have tucked it into his pocket. She could probably get a better idea if she saw his face and guessed his age, but...

*Who cares.*

She was busy enough trying to capture idols' faces. She could not care less what the man next to her looked like. Ryu Seojin fixed her gaze on her camera again.

*Did these guys have a fight?*

There seemed to be a strange distance between them, so she figured it would be difficult to get a decent two-shot of Lee Sejin and Park Moondae.

Soon, the performances began.

[The Hot Performance of the Year award goes to Spacer.]

Several singers and groups went up on stage one after another. Out of the hundreds, even thousands of groups that were active last year, only about twenty teams made it here, and even among them, there was a clear hierarchy and ranking.

*I have to get enough to trade.*

Having agreed to exchange data with other artists' fansite masters in different seats, she put in considerable effort to photograph a few teams. However, she had neither the loyalty nor the energy to shoot teams she was not interested in. She was an office worker who had just gotten off work, after all.

*Should I stop here?*

Besides, TeSTAR had also briefly left their seats in the artists' section, so she lowered her camera for a moment while massaging her shoulder.

In that moment, she saw it.

CLICK.

The man in the suit next to her displayed dazzling camera work.

*...That's.*

*He's... really good.*

She could tell just by the way he moved the camera. He had a stable hold, fluid movements, and masterful controls.

Besides, judging by his camera angle...

*...He's only shooting the ones who'll sell the best, isn't he?*

He was not just shooting the popular members. He was seeking out and shooting only those idols at the perfect moment when demand was skyrocketing and supply was short in the data market. For instance, a member like Park Minha of Mirynay. She had recently appeared on a variety show alone, appealing more to the light-fan layer than the general public, causing a momentary surge in her fandom popularity. Or members whose demand was constantly overflowing because they were exceptional performers. He was only targeting idols like that.

*What the.*

It was an act impossible for someone who did not have this scene completely figured out.

*Is he a staff member, or a data seller?*

He was a current A-list idol who was a former data seller. The twin fansite master, who had no way of guessing this fact, simply clicked her tongue inwardly in disappointment.

*It would've been great if he were sitting on the opposite side.*

The angle was almost identical to her own shots, so there was no need to buy his data.

*Well, with that method, he probably would've only shot Cha Eugene from TeSTAR anyway.*

She thought idly and was about to check her own data again when she suddenly realized something.

*...?*

This man in the suit next to her... his photography habits were strangely familiar. They resembled her own.

*Hmm.*

She took another careful look. To be precise, it was not something unique to her. She could catch glimpses of tips and postures shared among her club members.

*Come to think of it, his build looks a bit familiar too...*

It was the moment she finally decided to take a close look at Ryu Gunwoo's face.

"Whoa, Yeongrin!"

A voice shouted from behind. At the name of such a famous singer, she reflexively turned her head toward the stage.

Youngrin was indeed coming up on stage.

*Already?*

Judging by her seniority and achievements, she should have appeared later. Wasn't she after TeSTAR on the leaked cue sheet? However, looking closely, Youngrin had appeared in the middle of her juniors' performance. It was a surprise feature performance of sorts.

*Oh.*

Yeongrin cleanly slipped into the performance formation. Although she was incredibly successful as a soloist, the performance was a reminder that Youngrin was originally part of a group.

*The juniors are getting overshadowed.*

If the agency's intention was to promote the juniors, it was a failure. That was Ryu Seojin's cold assessment.

Youngrin looked this way and made a gesture.

"AHHHH!"

"Yeongrin-ah!!"

The surroundings erupted in a deafening roar, as if in surround sound. It seemed everyone thought they had made eye contact with her.

Usually, this was the 'Yeongrin saw me!' type of delusion, but...

*Hm?*

Ryu Seojin frowned slightly. She did not think Youngrin had looked at her.

*Didn't Youngrin... just look at the data seller next to me?*

***

[Thank you!]

I took a few more shots of TeSTAR accepting their award on stage. I was doing it out of habit, but it was also to check on Keundal's attitude one more time.

*He's fine.*

While giving him a passing grade, I deliberately kept the camera in front of my face. In fact, there was another reason I was continuing to do this.

*...To hide my face.*

It was a precaution in case Ryu Seojin recognized me. Well, I wore a mask and it had been years, so I thought the chances were close to zero.

I cautiously decided to take pictures of all sorts of idols with my camera. My old data-selling habits kicked in, and my brain automatically started updating the price rankings. One of the top-ranking female idols appeared a little early.

*Yeongrin.*

The star of the first viral fancam that earned me a semester's worth of tuition. I think that is what made the then-unknown Yeongrin famous, too.

*It was a fancam I shot on a rainy day.*

I continued to stare through the viewfinder without much thought.

It was then.

Youngrin pointed her hand at my camera.

"......"

I froze for a moment, still holding the camera.

If I was not mistaken...

*Does she... remember?*

No way. That was almost ten years ago. Not only was it unlikely she would still remember, but even if she did, how on earth could she have recognized me right at this moment?

*However...*

Just before the final ending pose, when the broadcast camera was not on her, Youngrin turned her gaze again.

[Remember me]

Eye contact, and a hand gesture. It was a familiar hand gesture. The signature gesture from the song in that rainy-day fancam I had filmed.

This... could not be a misunderstanding.

"......"

[WAAAAAH!!]

The performance ended with a roar of cheers. Youngrin's gaze vanished along with the lights.

However... I felt strange.

*How does she remember?*

I was not even a fan back then. I had shot Youngrin a few more times at events after that, but she had never done anything like this. I thought the reason my fancams had such amazing eye contact was just a matter of picking good spots.

Perhaps...

*...Did I leave an impression?*

The audience member with the camera who filmed the fancam that launched her career. Before, I would have thought it was nonsense, but now I knew. That it was a possibility.

*Because I'm doing the same thing now.*

That feeling of looking out at the audience from the stage, of searching for your own fancams, of observing people's reactions, and what it might feel like to see the camera from that fancam return after so many years.

"......"

I lowered the camera. I looked at the time displayed at the top of the screen.

[PM 11:21]

*Ah.*

It was time.

I breathed slowly and counted down.

3

2

1

In that instant.

BLINK.

"......"

I closed and opened my eyes. My vision changed. So did the sound.

[WAAAAAH!]

The cheers were no longer right next to me, but ringing out from a little farther away. I was sitting in the artists' section, next to a speaker.

And...

"You came right on time."

A hand tapped my shoulder. It was Ryu Chungwoo. When I looked up, the other guys seemed to have noticed immediately, as if they had been checking the time too.

"...! Moondae-hyung,"

"You okay?"

*Of course.*

I nodded, looking around just enough not to seem awkward. The members greeted me as discreetly as possible, with a grin or by offering me water. If we were not in the artists' section, they would have made a bigger fuss.

*And they would have first asked if the body-swapping was over for good.*

To be honest, I was curious too...

"......"

I closed the pop-up without looking at it first. I had to block out anything that might affect the performance.

*Same for that side.*

If I turned my head, VTIC's seats, where Cheongryeo had been sitting just a moment ago, were right next to us. However, the seat was already empty. That guy had also gotten up to prepare for his performance.

*Right.*

This was better. Right now, there was only one urgent matter. Because it was almost time.

[Artist of the Year Award / Album – 2nd Quarter]

[Congratulations.]

[TeSTAR / Savior(Guardian)]

A few minutes later, I headed backstage to go up on stage. The comeback stage I had been waiting for so long was right before my eyes.

***

"Excuse me, just a moment."

"Y-Yes?"

"Ah, it is you. Ryu Gunwoo."

"......"

"It's been a while. You're still doing this, huh."

*...Hyung!!*

...For the record, I had no idea that Keundal was going through this in the audience.
