# Chapter 496

Two weeks ago.

It was around the time Park Moondae had just finished filming TeSTAR's original content.

Cheongryeo was finalizing his comeback preparations.

"Next up is the interview, right?"

"Yes!"

The preparations went smoothly. The LeTi staff moved with a singular focus, treating every one of Cheongryeo's activities as their highest priority.

"They've been waiting for a while now. If you could be as quick as possible..."

"Ah! Yes."

This was because Cheongryeo was a celebrity who meticulously managed himself. He was gentle yet business-like and never had capricious fits of irritation, but he was also difficult to be around.

*Hmm.*

Cheongryeo knew this but didn't bother changing his attitude just to put people at ease.

*People listen better to someone they find difficult than someone they find comfortable.*

It was a principle he had perfected through countless trials and inductive statistics. In any case, Cheongryeo wasn't the type who needed to share his daily life to feel comfort and belonging.

He sometimes felt the urge to talk about his dog, Kongi, but that didn't matter since he had someone for that.

Cheongryeo checked the time, then called that 'someone'. This time, however, it wasn't to text a picture of Kongi as he occasionally did.

"......"

The other person picked up after a few short rings.

"Did the original content filming go well?"

[Yes, Sunbae.]

It was none other than Park Moondae.

Interestingly, his junior always used formal speech when they talked on the phone or texted, even though they had spoken informally before. It was a habitual meticulousness.

*That part isn't bad, either.*

An attitude of never letting one's guard down would never be a disadvantage. Cheongryeo found the thought slightly amusing. It was a pleasure to work with such a proven asset.

Soon, Park Moondae's impassive congratulations came through the phone.

[Congratulations on your solo album comeback, Sunbae.]

"Thank you."

Cheongryeo, who had heard the same thing from countless people recently, responded lightly and got straight to the point.

"The time has come to start preparing for what we agreed on back then."

[......]

"As I said last time, it's not that you'll be featuring on a B-side track of my album."

[Yes.]

Cheongryeo almost smiled, recalling how flustered Park Moondae had been at the time. He kept a straight face and continued, revealing the featuring method he had chosen.

"There's a show I'll be on soon. Let's go on it together."

[...!]

"I'm sure you'll like it too."

Cheongryeo had finished his calculations.

"The name of the show is..."

***

❬Nice to Meet You !❭

It was an MBS variety show that aired on Saturdays at 5:40 PM. The program was one of the few long-running competition shows on a public broadcast network that singers could appear on.

The title might suggest a typical public broadcast program that introduces talented unknown singers or masters from genres other than pop music.

*Correct.*

It was exactly that style favored by public broadcasters. However, that was only half the truth.

*The ratings wouldn't come in if that's all they did.*

What did they have to do to make it sell? The answer was simple.

*Bundle selling.*

It wasn't just artists from non-mainstream fields. Famous established singers aiming for album or concert promotion also appeared as cast members. By making two cast members form a pair to perform, they could get a variety of interesting scenes.

This program had been running for a long time, now in its seventh season. The casting lineup was still quite glamorous, but its main audience had settled into the middle-aged and older demographic, giving it a slightly old-fashioned feel.

*But this guy is appearing on this show?*

I glanced at Cheongryeo. The program had too many cast members and too much going on for VTIC to appear on. A big name like him could have his own special feature, yet he was just appearing on this?

*Hmm.*

Of course, he must have a motive. I recalled his answer to my blunt question, 'Why did you choose this program?'

[Because there's something I want to show.]

Well, that was why I was here today for the show's pre-meeting.

For reference, the main PD had pushed his way in during season three. Even though it was now past season seven, he was still holding onto it instead of passing it to a junior PD or producing a new show. After talking to him, I thought I knew why.

"Aigoo, nice to meet you, Moondae. My niece is a fan, so could you sign an album for her? A new one, not one you have left over."

"Of course."

*What a boomer.*

I bet he wasn't continuing out of some sense of duty. He probably saw it was a cushy, cost-effective gig and decided to squat on it.

Still, it was amusing to be reminded that even after I raised my idol brand value to the very top, a broadcast station PD was still the one in power. I guess I should be glad he wasn't treating me dismissively.

His attitude was different toward VTIC, which had an insane level of international recognition.

"And it's such an honor to have our Hallyu Star VTIC! appearing on our show. We're counting on you."

"Haha, the pleasure is all mine. I'm counting on you as well."

*So patriotism is the answer, after all.*

For this type of classic, old-fashioned elder, that was the best approach. I inwardly nodded as I watched the PD.

*Just wait until my next album.*

My goal was a lethal dose of patriotism to hunt for a Cultural Merit Medal anyway.

In any case, the fan who wanted an autograph was innocent. It was a relief that they weren't using their family connection to the PD to come into the waiting room. I diligently signed a spare album I had brought just in case. The PD quickly took it before finally getting to the topic of the show.

"So, um, the two of you will be performing together... but how did you two become so close?"

"Ah, we were on a variety show together when Moondae debuted. That show was also on MBS."

"...Ah, I remember! That's right."

*You clearly don't remember.*

He was quite good at navigating social situations. I guess you had to be like that to succeed enough to plant your butt on a hit show on a public broadcast network.

"Anyway, our show is truly, truly happy to have both of you."

As expected, the PD didn't mention the variety show again. He trailed off and slyly brought up his real concern.

"But since you're both in the same profession and have similar images, uh? I'm a bit worried it might be tricky to create a climax for your screen time, you know? Is that going to be okay?"

Uh. It's fine. The fact that two top-tier idols from different groups were teaming up was already explosive in terms of buzz. What the hell did he mean, climax?

*He's scheming.*

He meant that since he was getting two famous idols anyway, he wanted to split them into different episodes to extend the ratings boost.

*Nope, not happening.*

Without me needing to say a word, Cheongryeo answered immediately.

"Yes. You don't have to worry about that part."

"...Ah, okay."

*He seems confident.*

The fact that I didn't need to open my mouth was convenient. Working with a veteran had its perks. This was nice.

"And I heard our director already relayed this to you. This is..."

"Ah, yes, yes."

After that, there was no more useless baiting. The meeting flowed smoothly as we talked with the show's writer and sub-PD. I nodded, pretending to listen intently while I thought.

*So what kind of stage does this guy want to do using me?*

This question would soon be answered. It happened right after the meeting ended, in a conference room at the LeTi building twenty minutes from the broadcast station.

"First, I'll show you the music video."

Cheongryeo turned on the projector and immediately began to discuss the stage composition. Normally, the process of planning a concept like this would be included in the show's filming, but those scenes were often just reenactments due to practice time constraints.

*It's all scripted.*

So I could understand that part.

"It's a rough cut, but it's basically just missing the intro and credits. There shouldn't be any problem for you to understand the album concept..."

"Wait a minute."

The flow of this was a bit strange.

"Why are we suddenly watching your music video instead of going over the stage outline?"

"Hmm?"

I was asking why he was showing off his soon-to-be-released MV when he said our stage wasn't even for a song on the album.

However, Cheongryeo answered nonchalantly with an expression that said, 'Why are you asking such an obvious question?'

"We're appearing on this show to promote my album. Of course, the stage has to be made so that people immediately think of my new title track the moment they see it."

"......"

"This title track itself will be the base concept for our stage, so watch it carefully and familiarize yourself with it now. I can't send you the pre-release music video file."

Alright.

*He's got a good head on his shoulders.*

I held back a sigh and nodded. I wondered if I had made a mistake agreeing to cooperate with this guy's stage without any complaints. I had already told him that I would unilaterally follow the stage composition he created, as long as it didn't damage my image.

But if his vision was this clear...

*I just hope I'm not being used as a high-note shuttle.*

No, that might even be better than doing some ridiculously unfitting concept. I clicked my tongue and stared at the MV screen he was playing.

A marble floor turned to ash. In the middle of the ruined, elegant art museum floor, a spot of chaotically mixed, exploded paints lay. Lying alone on top of it was Cheongryeo.

[......]

The contrasting image slowly panned out from an overhead view. The sound of wind whispered.

And then.

[The devil is in the details]

A meaningful phrase appeared, and the song began.

[Ash]

It was the title of Cheongryeo's new solo album.

[Oh maybe we're just in pain]

A wistful, minor-key medium tempo started with a jazzy accompaniment.

[Ashes to ashes, dust to dust.]

In the embers of what burned down

The same old Story

And love is dead

It threw out a very addictive chorus right from the intro. Then, a slow hum before the verse, and off-beat choreography.

He danced barefoot in a turtleneck and black slacks on the ashes. The dance had its own power, pulling and pushing the camera to force new angles. Because he was a guy with a top-tier physique and proportions who had managed his body to the extreme, the minimalist style was incredibly impressive.

*Damn it.*

He hadn't lost his touch. I almost bit my tongue. He had properly executed the difficult task of mixing impressive choreography with an easy-listening song that was good on its own.

And what's more, this choreography...

*The movements themselves are rather easy to follow, but expressing the feeling is difficult.*

It meant that many people would try the challenge, and it would be easy for viewers to rank them. I could already see it.

[This person is so similar to Cheongryeo, it's insaneㄷㄷ]

└Sir, please stop hiring part-timers to write comments.

└ㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋSick burn

[This person dances well too, but there's really no one who can capture the vibe of the original songㅋㅋ]

[Why are there so many hate comments? It looks like they tried hardㅠ]

Challenge videos would be flooded with comments and reactions like these.

*It's the optimal choice for a solo artist confident in their dancing.*

If you could pull it off, it was the most ideal result. Even if TeSTAR postponed their military service as much as possible, some of them would have to enlist in a few years. Cha Eugene and Ryu Chungwoo would definitely have to do solo or unit promotions.

I even felt competitive, wondering if we could plan a result as refined as this.

"......"

I kept my mouth shut and watched the music video to the end. The background and outfits grew progressively more extravagant, then returned to the ashes at the end, finishing with a minimalist touch. The tempo control was incredible.

[And love is the end.]

The screen abruptly ended with Cheongryeo standing on black water in a dark space.

And the man himself, Cheongryeo, finally spoke with a smile still on his face after the rough cut finished.

"Do you like it?"

He didn't even ask for an objective opinion, jumping straight to a subjective question.

*He's confident, is what he's saying.*

Is that so? I readily nodded.

"Yeah."

"......"

"It's well-made."

Why did he look so surprised?

*You have to give credit where credit is due.*

There was no point in a pride battle when we had to work together right now anyway.

*And it's not like our comebacks overlap.*

However.

"It'll be hard for me to capture the feeling of this choreography."

"Hmm?"

"I'm saying this just in case, but you'd better not think of asking me to replicate a similar choreographic feel in just two weeks."

It was advice for the sake of the stage quality.

*This is a level of perfection that only someone with an A-rank in dance or higher could even attempt to match.*

Besides, this was a show where established singers appeared. A narrative where people would understand as long as you tried hard and showed growth, like in a debut survival show, would just be laughable here. If I were to do a similar choreography, I would have to be good enough not to break his concept.

But realistically, the cost-effectiveness of that was poor.

*Just use me as a high-note shuttle, you bastard.*

However, Cheongryeo burst out laughing.

"Haha, I thought we agreed that I get to do what I want for this stage, no?"

"......"

*Is he serious?*

"I'm kidding."

The guy grinned and tapped the table with his fingers as if making a calculation.

"We each have to do what we're good at... for our agreement to have value, right, junior?"

Right.

"Still, you have to do the basics."

"...?"

Cheongryeo asked lightly.

"What's the most hours you've ever practiced in a day?"

Then, a training regimen on the level of ❬Idol Inc.❭ dropped on my head.
