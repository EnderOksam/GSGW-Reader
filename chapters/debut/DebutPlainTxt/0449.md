# Chapter 449

Unfortunately for Park Moondae, the 7th-grade civil servant was remembered by the title 'Supervisor'.

This man remembered when Ryu Gunwoo was first assigned.

[Hello!]

Most new hires were sent to the city hall to get ground down. This guy must have had incredible luck or connections to get placed in the district office.

Well, the fact that he didn't get a single call from someone asking to 'take good care of him' quickly revealed it wasn't connections.

*I need to put a guy like that in his place from the very beginning.*

For starters, he seemed like an easy target.

He couldn't do any work without knowing something, and what could a rookie who just passed the exam possibly know?

[Supervisor Ryu, do your parents live here? Where's your hometown?]

[Uh... No, sir. I moved here recently!]

The guy had no particular connections in this neighborhood. He wouldn't be any help with the kind of projects a 7th-grade civil servant handled at the district office.

*Then he'd better be prepared to suffer a bit.*

He had planned to show him the bitter taste of office life.

With a face so smooth it looked like he'd never known a day of hardship, the way he pretended to stand firm was even more infuriating.

The fact that this greenhorn seemed to be holding on with nothing but the pride of having passed an exam made him even more of an eyesore.

He had expected him to be crying and begging for a transfer to city hall by the time the next assignments came around.

*Hah... Well, it doesn't matter now.*

The work was already done.

He had dumped all the troublesome, high-responsibility tasks with poor handovers onto Ryu Gunwoo. Meanwhile, he snatched up all the projects that would help with his connections and promotion.

He considered his actions a kind of resourcefulness, and even a rather generous gesture toward Ryu Gunwoo.

*If he'd gone to another district office, he'd already be screaming about resigning.*

Compared to that, wasn't he working more comfortably here than even at the city hall?

In any case, it would be impossible for that guy to hold his head high here from now on. Crushing him now was the only way to avoid seeing someone like him get promoted quickly.

He truly believed that.

But on Monday.

"Good morning."

When he saw Ryu Gunwoo arrive at work, he was momentarily flustered.

It was when Ryu Gunwoo swept his gaze across the office as if he were scouting it.

"......"

*Did he always have such a cold expression?*

His original expression was soft, almost gentle-looking, but somehow his impression seemed to have changed.

For a moment, he looked like a completely different person.

"Hello, Supervisor!"

However, as Ryu Gunwoo greeted the other staff, he had already returned to his usual meek expression.

It was the same when he deliberately went over to give him a subtle earful.

"Let's get it right this time! Please~"

"...Yes."

See? He couldn't even talk back.

He shrugged and moved on.

*It must have been my imagination.*

However, he should have noticed the signs right then and there.

The way Ryu Gunwoo smiled as he turned his gaze to his computer.

That afternoon.

When he returned from a meal disguised as a meeting, the office atmosphere had strangely shifted.

"...?"

In one corner of the office, a figure was visible through a half-open door.

The team leader was talking with Ryu Gunwoo at a table.

*What are they doing?*

It was a time when they should have been clattering away at their keyboards. What on earth had gotten into them?

It didn't seem like he was getting scolded for some trouble. The atmosphere looked good.

HAHA!

Just then, Ryu Gunwoo, who had been speaking politely with a constant smile, spotted him.

"...!"

When the team leader said something to Ryu Gunwoo, he stood up, approached the door, and called over someone from his group.

Only, it wasn't him.

"Supervisor Lee."

"Yes."

He called the 9th-grade civil servant, the nephew of the former chairman of the Seoul City council and the current city council member for this district.

"The team leader is calling for you."

"......"

"Oh, right. Come over here and have a seat for a moment."

"Ah, yes, sir!"

As the team leader gestured, Supervisor Lee hurriedly walked out from behind him and entered through the door.

"...??"

As the 'Supervisor' failed to react to the sudden situation, Ryu Gunwoo, who had been wearing a placid smile, turned his head.

Their eyes met.

"......"

Ryu Gunwoo seemed to stare at him for a moment...

CLICK.

He closed the door.

*...That son of a bitch.*

*What the hell is his problem, acting so damn unpleasant.*

Feeling inexplicably uneasy, the 'Supervisor' cursed a few times under his breath and headed to the break room.

This was the first strange phenomenon.

And the second strange phenomenon was witnessed two hours before the end of the workday.

On his way up to the rooftop for a smoke, he saw Ryu Gunwoo and Supervisor Lee next to the emergency staircase.

The two were talking with quite serious faces.

*Oh, for fuck's sake.*

*What kind of important business could they possibly have to be out here during work hours?*

This time, he couldn't hold back. After enjoying his cigarette, he came down and went straight to Supervisor Lee to ask him directly.

"Supervisor Lee, what were you two talking about so interestingly?"

Supervisor Lee flinched, then trailed off.

"Ah... It was nothing, he was just helping me with a civil complaint."

"Wow, Supervisor Ryu is helping people now? That's amazing, isn't it?"

It was, of course, not a compliment but a sneer. It was his way of asking what help he could possibly offer.

However, the submissive agreement he always got didn't come back this time.

"Yes. Supervisor Ryu is kind and a good person, you know."

"......"

*Huh? This brat... is he showing that he's uncomfortable?*

This was the same guy who usually just laughed or obediently agreed.

For a moment, he was about to get offended, but he forced a hearty smile.

"Well, if you take away his personality, Supervisor Ryu is nothing. He's a good person."

"Yes..."

The response wasn't great this time either, but he decided to be satisfied that his junior had at least yielded.

However, around the time to leave work, the final anomaly occurred.

"Supervisor Lee~ Shall we go for a drink?"

This was an invitation he made at least once a week to be a good superior who buys food and drinks.

However, Supervisor Lee shook his head.

"Um... I have plans today."

"...!"

It was a polite refusal, but a refusal nonetheless.

This had never happened since he was first assigned.

"...Really?"

"Yes. I'm very sorry, but I'll see you tomorrow. Thank you, Supervisor."

And with that, he briskly walked out of the office.

On his way out, he even glanced at Ryu Gunwoo and gave a nod of his head!

"......"

He was dumbfounded, but a chill ran down his spine.

*Something's weird.*

He distractedly left the office and got on the elevator.

But inside, two female employees who had been in the office just moments ago were already on the open elevator.

They were whispering about something.

"OMG, you're right."

"Ah, then that's completely..."

But the moment their eyes met his, they flinched in surprise and looked at each other. Then they smiled awkwardly.

"...?"

DING-.

At that moment, the elevator arrived. The two of them quickly stepped out the door.

As if they didn't even want to hear a question.

"Have a good evening."

"We'll be heading out now~"

In the end, the 'Supervisor' was left alone in the desolate lobby.

Without knowing anything.

Without a clue, completely alienated.

"......"

What the.

*What's wrong with all of them.*

Just this morning, the day had been no different from last week.

What in the world... is this situation?!

What do you mean?

[How is this my way at all?!]

The pop-up screamed. It seemed there was a misunderstanding.

Hmm, to explain this properly... I suppose I should start here.

I asked bluntly.

*Why do you think that guy, Supervisor Lee, followed him to external meetings?*

[Uh...? Because it was his job, I guess?]

Right.

You think he enjoyed it? Absolutely not.

*He did it unconditionally because he was scared.*

[...!]

He didn't want to get on his superior's bad side in his first real job.

People are the same everywhere. Why would a guy who likely became a civil servant for the work-life balance enjoy being dragged around to off-site meetings and client entertainment?

And there was no way he wouldn't have complaints while tagging along with a condescending fossil like that.

*He was probably annoyed even when the guy thought he was doing him a favor.*

Things like holding him back in the evening by saying he'd buy him dinner.

*So.*

[...So?]

I just... created an opening for him to cut ties.

*All I had to do was make him unafraid.*

So I first targeted the most powerful speaker. The team leader.

[Ah, Team Leader. That was actually done by Supervisor Lee...!]

I deliberately praised 'Supervisor Lee' during lunchtime with the team leader, attaching a plausible reason.

Then, I gently pried into the team leader's personal life to draw out the conversation. When the timing was right, I brought Supervisor Lee into it... and the stage was set.

[What stage...]

The stage to show Supervisor Lee his own position.

All I had to do was borrow the voice of the most authoritative person, the 'team leader'.

*By gently steering the conversation in that direction...*

I made him realize anew how advantageous it was for his work that he was related to a local bigwig.

Then this thought occurs to him.

[Huh? So I could have stood up to that supervisor bastard?]

It was a realization that would have normally taken another year of work to dawn on him, but I just brought it forward.

Of course, if I had stopped there, it would have taken some time for him to actually act on this thought. So the final push to give him a nudge was...

*Giving him a justification.*

[...A justification?]

Yeah.

*I just reminded him that that guy had been deliberately tormenting you.*

[Supervisor Ryu. Thank you. For speaking well of me...]

[Hey, no. It's the truth! And... we're both struggling, so it's nothing.]

[......]

[Let's hang in there. It'll be okay once this period passes.]

The conviction that he is doing a good thing.

That's surprisingly important to people. Add to that the refreshing taste of serving justice, and now there's nothing holding him back.

I nodded.

Besides, look at the reactions of the people in the office when it all went down.

The fact that so many people found this overturned situation amusing meant that the original Ryu Gunwoo had a pretty good reputation.

*This was only possible because you lived a good life.*

[......]

Hmm, I complimented his character. Shouldn't he be a little happier?

*Anyway, from now on, even if he takes that 'Supervisor Lee' guy to meetings, he won't be as completely cooperative as before.*

Because he now knows how useful he is.

He already has some resentment. He'll show it when he doesn't like something, and then... things will get rocky.

Until that 'Supervisor' gets royally pissed off.

*But it's too late to change his mind and say he'll take Ryu Gunwoo now...*

Before long, that guy will have no choice but to accept the reality that his days of networking in this district office are over.

[Then...]

I shrugged.

"Well... he'll probably aim for the next transfer."

*Then he'll leave, and you can keep leaving work on time here.*

A happy ending.

[Uhuhuhu...]

Hmm, thinking about it again, it was a very gentle solution.

To think I could handle it so meekly just by complimenting others. Anyone could see it was similar to Keundal's usual behavior.

[What?]

And yet, no one lost anything, and everyone was happy. Indeed.

I was briefly impressed.

*Living like you seems like a pretty good way to go.*

[...Yes. Thank you...]

Don't mention it.

Feeling refreshed, I shut down the internal network system.

I think this is enough of a civil servant life experience.

*Are your preparations for the evening schedule going well?*

Because my turn to control Park Moondae is coming up again.

[Ah... Yes! I'm all ready.]

*Good.*

I'm heading out now too.

I took out the luggage I had put in my personal cabinet as soon as I got to work.

The weight of the heavy bag pressed down on my hand.

*It's been a really long time for this too.*

Straight to... Jamsil Indoor Gymnasium.

This guy's after-work plan.

To try and take pictures of TeSTAR with a camera at the music chart awards show.
