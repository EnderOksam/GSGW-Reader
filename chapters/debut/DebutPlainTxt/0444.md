# Chapter 444

That morning, one of TeSTAR's managers welcomed an unexpected freeloader.

[Moondae isn't feeling too well, so it seems Chungwoo called a relative over. They say he's practically family to Moondae.]

*Handling a schedule with a member's family...*

The manager, a stickler for rules, found the task daunting but had no choice. TeSTAR, with a brand value in the tens or even hundreds of billions of won, rarely made unreasonable demands. The company decided to accommodate this rare, willful request. As a subordinate, he had to obey.

"Hoo..."

He went to meet the members with a sense of resignation. Though his title was 'daily manager', he braced himself for a complicated day looking after a layman he couldn't treat casually.

Then, he finally met the freeloader.

"Hello. I am Ryu Gunwoo."

"Ah, yes."

The man was handsome. He looked like Ryu Chungwoo after being locked in a library for five years on a diet of only vegetables. It was obvious he was a relative. The manager was a bit surprised, but it wasn't strange.

"Headcount is complete, and there are no members with any particular issues. Ah, Moondae's situation has already been reported to the company."

"Ah, yes."

From that point, things got strange. The man was too sharp.

"Wow~ Please take good care of us, hyung~"

"......"

"Th-this! How about we drink this on the way..."

"Thanks."

The members were also completely at ease with him. Anyone watching would think they had lived and worked together for about seven years. Question marks popped into the manager's head, but his body was already moving diligently for the schedule.

When they reached the car, Ryu Gunwoo raised his hand and suggested something.

"Should I drive?"

The steering wheel? It was right for the person with the least seniority to do the hard work, but this case felt different. The manager hesitated for a moment, then nodded obediently.

"Ah, hmm... Yes. Thank you."

"It's only natural. Thank you for allowing me."

It seemed better to just hand it over to save time, rather than risk an argument. The company was nearby, so he could just set the GPS. Later, he could use their busy schedule as an excuse to get to the driver's seat first.

*After a few trials and errors, he'll give up the driver's seat on his own...*

However, Ryu Gunwoo got into the driver's seat and skillfully set the GPS without even asking.

"...?"

"Then we'll head to the company first."

"Ah, yes."

He started driving, naturally checking the upcoming schedule as he went.

"The morning schedule will end around 1 PM after the meeting, a salon stop, and a consultation. For lunch... tell me if there's anything you want to eat."

"I want chicken!"

"Let's have a show of hands. Who's okay with that? ...Good, but since it's right before promotions, we're having it boiled."

The manager, sitting in the passenger seat, received an unearned break.

"...?"

Ryu Gunwoo even checked the back seat through the rearview mirror and gave advice.

"Raebin, your hair."

"Oh, thank you!"

His demeanor, a mix of kindergarten teacher and veteran manager, projected years of experience. The manager opened his mouth without realizing it.

"Excuse me, what do you usually do for work?"

"Ah, I'm a public official in an administrative position."

"......"

An unimaginable identity was revealed. Meanwhile, the car arrived at the company. Ryu Gunwoo smoothly handled entry with the ID card in his pocket. As soon as he parked, he confirmed the members were going inside. He didn't follow them.

"Excuse me... aren't you going in?"

"...? I have to go buy coffee. I'm the rookie, after all."

*How in the world do you know that.*

"Ah, I'll buy it from a place with an affiliate discount. Four iced Americanos, no decaf. Two chamomiles, and one hot chocolate."

"......"

"I overheard it when we were getting out of the car earlier. What would you like to have, Chief?"

The manager gave up thinking. A few hours later, Keundal would also give up thinking in a similar way.

***

"I think I'm going to die."

"Hey, you're not gonna die, you're not gonna die~"

Lee Sejin laughed and handed him a water bottle. Keundal barely took it and gulped it down, trembling.

*Aaargh!*

It was an insane forced march. Park Moondae hadn't lied. There really were no nerve-wracking public schedules like a live radio show. TeSTAR had already finished their album recording and pre-release content filming, so there were no other burdens.

However, the training schedule... the management schedule!

*I don't even know where I am...*

Meeting room, salon, vocal training room, bone-setting clinic. They moved so busily it made his head spin. Even during travel, they were handed pre-interview questionnaires.

[You don't have to read it. We're not doing it today.]

The pop-up came from Ryu Gunwoo, who held the steering wheel. Or rather, from Park Moondae inside him, but not having to read the questionnaire didn't mean he got any rest. Five minutes later, the car stopped at the company again. The destination was the choreography practice room on the first basement floor.

"The bridge part you guys mentioned last time has been revised. It's a little tricky, you know? You guys okay with that?"

"Yep~"

"Aigoo, you're always so reliable."

The label's exclusive choreographer was scheduled to teach the finalized choreography, a combination of six or seven drafts, for forty minutes. Before they began, Ryu Chungwoo smoothly relayed a special note.

"Ah, Moondae is excluded from choreography practice today only, due to an injury."

This had been agreed upon beforehand. There was no way Keundal could dance choreography he had never learned.

"Oh, really? Then we'll start with him out."

'Park Moondae' was immediately excluded without any time for questions or concern. The revised choreography was demonstrated.

*Wow...*

Keundal sank into a chair, unsure if he should be happy he'd hit the jackpot or happy he'd gotten a breather. He soon forgot all about it.

*Woah!*

[Again!]

Even though they only practiced the bridge part repeatedly, watching it up close was incredibly powerful. The fact that he was watching an unreleased new song made his hands tremble.

Moreover, when he turned his head, he saw something.

"...!"

Ryu Gunwoo leaned against the wall by the door, his eyes quickly scanning the choreography. To be precise, it was TeSTAR's Park Moondae in Ryu Gunwoo's body.

*He's memorizing it in his head.*

It hit him again how the impression of the same body could change depending on who was inside, and... that this was the time that hyung should have been practicing in this body.

"......"

*I can't let this happen.*

As the Status Window, Keundal had a strange pride in his own achievements. He had only been a great help to Moondae-hyung on his path as an idol. For him to be the one causing a setback!

*This isn't right.*

So when the next schedule came up, he had an idea.

"Now it's PT in the training room until 9:10."

*This is it!*

"Excuse me, hyung!"

"Hm?"

At Keundal's call, Ryu Gunwoo, who had been observing from the wall, turned his head.

"Shouldn't it be okay to at least do the workout? The body is the same!"

"Hmm..."

"Ah, you want to try it? It's pretty fun, though," Ryu Chungwoo readily responded from the side.

Encouraged by that reaction, Keundal again resolved to willingly participate in the PT session.

"Yes!"

He shouldn't have done that. He should have checked Ryu Gunwoo's expression, but Keundal didn't have an eagle's 340-degree vision. He was now sprawled limply on the training room floor.

The advanced course was a den of demons that a beginner shouldn't challenge with resolve alone.

*So this is how you've been living...*

It felt like a rest after long torture. He only endured because the body was used to it.

*Khhhp...*

He sucked down the water Lee Sejin handed him as if it were the water of life. That rest also ended immediately.

"It's time for us to move!"

*Aaargh!*

That's right. An idol's pre-promotion schedule meant even breaks were measured in minutes. Just then, a hand reached out to help Keundal up.

"...!"

It was Bae Sejin.

"Oh, oh, thank you."

"...It's nothing," Bae Sejin muttered. He hesitated slightly, then added, "It's normal to find it hard the first time, so you don't have to think any strange thoughts."

"...! Yes, thank you!"

Bae Sejin just nodded bluntly and walked away. Keundal realized again that he had a rather kind personality. In fact, most of TeSTAR was like that. Despite their busy schedules, they were simply kind to him, a stranger, without any hazing. No one was wary or sarcastic. The more extroverted members even started conversations, checking if he was uncomfortable.

"Hey."

"Oh, y-yes."

For instance, in a situation just like this one. The car arrived at the dorm after sunset, when almost all the schedules were finished. Cha Eugene had struck up a conversation.

"Speak informally! I'm younger. It's okay!"

"Uh, um, okay."

Exhausted, Keundal succumbed to the comfort and obediently dropped the formal speech. As he got out of the driver's seat, Ryu Gunwoo noticed the two of them chatting.

***

*Hmm, that went over well.*

I nodded. Keundal had suffered a bit, but it was a relief that everything was wrapping up cleanly without any major issues.

"Ahem, you worked hard today."

"Not at all. I'm the one who should be thankful."

I exchanged conventional greetings with the manager. As soon as Ryu Chungwoo and Lee Sejin acted as guarantors, Ryu Gunwoo's interview for the one-day road manager position passed at high speed.

*Blood ties really are the best.*

Park Moondae wasn't feeling well and had no immediate family to care for him. The excuse that Ryu Chungwoo had called his own cousin, who was close to Park Moondae, worked. The Keundal version of Park Moondae, who was a bit dazed and cheerful, seemed to be in a slightly bad state to the company staff. Thanks to that, Ryu Gunwoo smoothly wedged himself in under the pretext of mental care.

As for driving, I got the hang of it after a few tries last night.

*I got some help from Ryu Chungwoo, who has his own car.*

He was a pretty good instructor from the passenger seat.

[Hyung, how about taking a driver's license test when you're back as Moondae? It's a bit of a waste otherwise...]

That suggestion was worth remembering.

*Anyway, it's been a while since I've done this.*

Fortunately, my body seemed to remember. I drove through downtown Seoul all day without any problems. Perhaps because I was a disposable worker hired through connections, and also doing unpaid labor by taking turns at the wheel, the existing manager didn't try any useless power-tripping.

I reviewed the day's events and nodded.

*I don't think I did anything that could get me in trouble.*

I planned to go straight into the dorm and collapse for the day.

"Moondae-hyung!"

"......"

Hey.

Fortunately, Cha Eugene immediately corrected the name.

"Oh! Gunwoo-hyung! Let's go get ice cream! Me, Kim Raebin, and BM!"

"BM?"

[He said that "Keundal" is a special nickname? I gave it a hip twist.]

*Don't tell me it's Big Moon.*

It was a sensibility I couldn't understand. In any case, Keundal himself seemed quite tempted by the suggestion, judging by his bright expression.

However, Kim Raebin whispered seriously.

"Hyung, given the special circumstances, I think it would be safer to just go home..."

"Nooo! Kim Raebin betrayed us! The Kim Raebin who said he wanted ice cream is gone!"

"...! Betrayal! Saying I want to eat it doesn't imply I'll buy it myself! Cha Eugene is making a leap in logic right now!"

"Alright. I get it."

Right. As Kim Raebin said, it would be best to just return to the dorm, but...

*I should give him a carrot.*

Let's be honest. Keundal must have felt like he was being tortured throughout today's entire schedule. I gave the manager a nod, then switched cars and got behind the wheel again. The manager would take care of the members going back to the dorm, so I could stick with these guys for a bit.

"Let's go. If the convenience store right ahead is okay."

"......"

"Ah, yes!"

"Wear your masks."

The car started off. The drive was over in an instant. I parked in front of the convenience store and got out. I stood outside and waited instead of going in with the others.

"Hyung! Aren't you coming in?"

"I'm tired. You guys go pick something out."

"OK~"

I watched the convenience store door close and turned my gaze to the road.

That taxi.

"......"

The truth was, I had just seen that tail following our car.

*Of all times, did I have to get caught now?*

Hmm, in industry terms, a sasaeng. Stalkers, that is.
