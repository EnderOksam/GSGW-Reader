# Chapter 403 - A Familiar Memory

The national TV variety show, ⟨Only the Winner Gets Chicken⟩, was in the middle of filming a new episode.

*More idols?*

One of the regular cast members wasn't particularly impressed by the guest lineup he'd received in advance.

The show was a typical weekend variety program aimed at families, where guests were invited to play physical games and talk together, so appearing on it took little effort.

Moreover, because it was widely exported overseas, all sorts of people appeared on it to promote all kinds of things.

*Still, this week's guests are actually famous.*

They were kids from a large agency with one of the hottest songs these days. They probably hadn't asked to appear; the production team had likely invited them.

However, what was really surprising was the special guest that was suddenly announced.

*Wow, awesome.*

Ballerino Seon Ahyeon!

He'd suddenly returned to the country, perhaps to film a commercial during his off-season, and the production team had somehow managed to get him.

Given his reputation, it wouldn't have been strange for him to have his own special, but apparently there hadn't been enough time to plan one, so he was appearing as a special guest instead.

*Should I take advantage of this opportunity?*

After nodding to the Wishze members who'd come to the waiting room to greet him like proper rookies, the cast member slowly stood up.

"Hyung, what is it?" a staff member asked.

"Hey, follow me."

Since there was no way Seon Ahyeon would come to his waiting room to say hello, he planned to seek him out and introduce himself.

*If I'm lucky, I might be able to talk about our meeting on air sometime...*

But when the cast member arrived at Seon Ahyeon's waiting room...

The place was empty.

"...??"

The staff suddenly started speaking to the cast member.

"I... I heard he went to see *them*."

"Who?"

"Wishze. Wishze!"

*He went to see those rookies instead of one of the MCs? Even if they're popular right now, they've only just debuted...!*

"Well, they were on some kind of survival show and he appeared as a mentor there."

"Ah."

That was understandable.

*So they already know each other, huh?*

Of course, he didn't expect filming to go smoothly.

The dancer and the idol guests were around the same age and worked in the same industry, with similar selling points.

*It'll be a battle of wills, all right.*

The cast member nodded.

He'd probably have to laugh for the cameras and brace himself for the awful mood once they stopped rolling.

Sure enough, after the intro had been filmed and the special guest appeared, everyone busied themselves praising him.

Use the existing guests to flatter the special guest!

"You're so handsome, really~"

"Ahyeon, you look just like an idol, right?"

"When you're standing together like this... Hey, you could all be in the same group!"

It was standard praise, but it was also true. Even the writers liked the sight of three handsome men standing side by side.

*Women will really like it.*

The cast member watched the predictable situation unfold and readied a quick series of reactions for the cameras.

It was obvious to him that the special guest was meant to benefit at the expense of the existing guests.

But surprisingly, the existing guests accepted the situation smoothly and cheerfully, without any sign of awkwardness or annoyance.

They even moved apart to give Seon Ahyeon a place between them.

"If you stand like this... Ta-da, center."

"Hahaha!"

*Well, would you look at that?*

They were pretty gutsy for rookies. Moreover, the longer filming went on, the more one thing became clear.

What a blessing these guys were. They had an endless supply of fun stories to talk about.

"Don't you remember, hyung? They have other members who escaped from an exploding building with just one newspaper! I guess they learned this method from those members. How unique!"

"Jaehyeon-ssi, you know a thing or two about climbing ropes~ Are you planning to go to the military after Eugene-ssi?"

"Wow! Can you do an impression of Raebin-ssi?"

During the games, almost every member was mentioned in turn, and the MCs knew all about them and could join in the conversation.

Moreover, the members on the show had their own personalities and stories, so the conversation naturally centered on them.

Only then did the regular cast member sense it keenly.

*These guys... They've set the stage perfectly, haven't they?*

He got the feeling that they couldn't possibly be a one-hit wonder.

This was the first group he'd seen since T-Holic where each member had a reputation of their own. Now their song had made it big, too.

This group had broken through during T-Holic's several-month hiatus with perfect timing, and it had a distinctive smell.

The smell of great success.

*Hmmm.*

Looking at it that way, the guys who were appearing on the show now looked different to him.

First of all, they worked hard, were willing to play along, had enough guts, and had good sense.

*It seems like they get along well.*

When it was time for a break, the younger member smiled and kept talking to the older member, and the older one accepted it kindly. It didn't seem like he was ignoring the younger member or that it was just a facade for the show.

*They're neither hypocritical nor showy.*

The cast member nodded decisively and began thinking about how to befriend them, only to realize he wasn't the only one looking at them that way.

He took a quick glance... and saw Seon Ahyeon.

He was quietly watching the rookies.

"...?"

*Huh?*

*Is it just me, or does Seon Ahyeon watch them every time they take a break...?*

Ahyeon eventually looked away, but the cast member got the feeling he was right.

*Hm?*

*Does he really want to become friends with them?*

It would be natural for them to want to talk to someone so famous, but the two rookies seemed to be very cautious and reluctant to approach Seon Ahyeon first.

And the cast member had his own guess as to why that was so.

Because he also heard rumors about Seon Ahyeon.

*Still, they have no sense, they have no sense. They'll owe me for this.*

The cast member clicked his tongue cheerfully and took advantage of the break while the crew replaced the cameras' memory cards to approach the rookies.

To be exact, he'd purposely waited until the member who was one year older was alone.

"Oh... how is it? Has filming been okay?"

"I've been doing my best. I really appreciate you checking in on us. Thank you."

The cast member set a friendly mood by chatting with the polite guy about various things.

Then, bringing up Seon Ahyeon out of the blue...

He got right to the point!

"He's a little chilly, right?"

"...?"

Rookie Ryu Gunwoo's eyes widened slightly and then returned to normal.

The regular cast member suppressed a laugh. The way the rookie pretended to be surprised, obviously trying to say something nice, was so typical of a newcomer.

"People who do art are kind of like that. I guess it's even more so because he's so handsome. It feels a little different from entertainers like us."

Yes.

Seon Ahyeon was well known in broadcasting circles for his reputation for 'drawing a line'.

He was polite, but that was it. Some people said he acted as though he lived in a different world and was better than everyone else.

*Some even mock him for acting holier than thou, as if he's devoted his life to the noble art of ballet.*

It was an attitude that younger people in particular might find petty or unpleasant. The cast member fully understood that.

Of course, the person listening to him didn't agree:

*What kind of bullshit is this guy saying?*

Ryu Gunwoo barely answered.

"...Hmm. I think he's perfectly kind."

"Right. But you got the feeling that he doesn't want to have anything more to do with you, didn't you?"

"......"

"But actually, I think he wants to get to know you two better. He kept looking at you... Well, in the direction of Wishze."

"Ah..."

Ryu Gunwoo trailed off for a moment. With a subtle smile, the cast member intended to continue giving advice, but...

"Hello."

Shin Jaehyeon, the member who was one year younger, returned and greeted the cast member.

"Hey," the cast member said.

"I was just checking with Gunwoo-ssi to see if everything was all right."

"Ah, I heard that sunbaenim looks after the rookies at every awards ceremony..." Shin Jaehyeon said.

"Ah, hmm, you know about that?"

"Yes. It's a really famous story."

At this point in time, there wasn't a variety show entertainer Shin Jaehyeon didn't have detailed information on.

As Jaehyeon took control of the conversation to send the cast member away, Ryu Gunwoo was deep in thought.

*Seon Ahyeon discriminates against people...?*

It was an unimaginable and strange thing to say.

*What on earth happened?*

Ryu Gunwoo almost looked back at Seon Ahyeon for a moment, but stopped.

*There was no need to do that, right?*

"Do you want to use him?" Shin Jaehyeon asked after sending the cast member away.

Ryu Gunwoo exhaled.

"No."

There was no time to get lost in unnecessary sentimentality, and the *daesang* they'd been aiming for was within reach.

*Just focus on the goal.*

So he continued filming without wavering.

After the break came a blatant promotional segment for the special guest.

A section where they learned simple ballet movements.

Of course, the idol guests were also given a brief opportunity to promote themselves at the end of the segment.

"Since we've tried ballet, shall we try idol dancing now?"

"Oh~"

Shin Jaehyeon and Ryu Gunwoo came to the front according to the set script.

"You can dance like this. Wriggle your shoulders like this."

"Ohh!"

When Shin Jaehyeon demonstrated a dance move, deliberately exaggerating its kitschy hand movements, the cast members joined in and showed off their body gags.

The idols even turned their attention to the special guest.

"Ah~ By any chance, does Ahyeon want to try?"

The special guest's turn.

However, the cast members were surprised when Seon Ahyeon, whom they'd expected to refuse, came forward immediately.

As if he'd been waiting.

"Like this...?"

"Yes."

And he executed it perfectly, though he clearly hadn't expected to.

"...!"

It didn't seem to be simply because he was a dancer. There was something about the movement unique to someone who'd practiced it repeatedly.

Shin Jaehyeon narrowed his eyes.

*Is some of it still in there?*

Of course, that expression vanished in an instant, and all the cast members were impressed.

"You're good~ You could really be an idol, right?"

"That's right. What do you two think?"

*Did they really think he couldn't do it?*

Even though Ryu Gunwoo thought the answer was obvious, he nodded and played along.

"I think so too."

"Oh, of course! Ahyeon, would you consider trying a proper idol dance?"

Seon Ahyeon seemed to hesitate, but only for a moment.

"I really appreciate the compliment, but... But I'm a ballerino."

"Ah, you are firm."

"That's only to be expected from a real master of his craft!"

Seon Ahyeon just smiled and nodded. Ryu Gunwoo found himself asking without thinking.

"You seem to really like ballet. Has it always been your only focus?"

"......"

Seon Ahyeon slightly lowered his gaze and spoke calmly.

"Because it's my everything. I've never thought about anything else."

"......"

Park Moondae understood what that answer implied.

*Maybe it's because he talks like this that such strange rumors have spread about him.*

The cast member, who'd been watching them, shrugged to himself, thinking his advice had worked.

*This is how you make connections. Hoo.*

And the shoot moved on to its final segment: the main event.
