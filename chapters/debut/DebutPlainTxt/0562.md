# Chapter 562

The pop-up formed another word.

[Junior.]

That was the trigger.

A torrent of thoughts flooded my mind, like ripping tape from a box. Another word connected.

Shin Jaehyun.

The leader of VTIC. He called me Junior because I was a competitor in the same industry who debuted after him. That industry was idols.

The group's name was...

*TeSTAR.*

Right. I was Park Moondae of TeSTAR.

I remembered. I nodded crisply.

Then I froze.*Wait. I remembered? That means...*

*...I had forgotten.*

The realization sent a chill down my spine, and I snapped to my senses.

[Gasp!]

Everything around me sharpened into focus. I took a deep breath and looked around. My awakened mind felt cold and sharp.

*What the hell is this.*

This was still inside the System.

Only one thing had changed. The terminal I had just been analyzing was gone. Instead, a single, simple pop-up floated in the air. It was the very thing that had snapped me out of my daze.

I immediately opened my mouth.

"Cheongryeo."

[Finally.]

"...Good grief."

He cut me off and answered however he pleased. It really was him. Funnily enough, even that felt somewhat welcome.

*How on earth did he appear here?*

Unlike the others who appeared as pop-ups with bizarre descriptions, he was speaking normally. I wanted to know why, but that wasn't the urgent matter right now.

I tried to compose myself, then frowned when I realized I had no body.

The pop-up changed its text.

[What is your current situation, Junior?]

*Let's see.*

I recalled what I had just seen. The terminal had informed me that I'd diligently learned the 'How Does the System Behave' textbook.

Then my sense of self vanished the moment the 'Restarting with the new version of the System' notification appeared.

*Hmm.*

"I'm screwed."

I wiped the smile from my face.

"It looks like this bastard is trying to make me the next System."

~~~

[Park Moondae : In the process of reclaiming Park Moondae.]

"AAAAH!"

"Hah..."

In front of Raising Dot Park Moondae, people sighed in relief or groaned as they stared at the barely legible screen.

However, Park Moondae's form was still a dark, lumpy circle. On the pixelated screen, with its smeared and blackened background, only Park Moondae's will held its shape and glittered.

Lee Sejin immediately grabbed Keundal's shoulder.

"Can you bring Moondae back right now?"

"I-I'll check right away."

However, someone was already using the passage.

It was Cheongryeo. He had practically snatched the connection away and now used the passage motionlessly, his concentration incredible.

Keundal guessed it was possible because Cheongryeo, as a GM, could exert greater influence.

*Still, the perceived time must be different...*

Time flowed incredibly fast on the other side, didn't it? Thanks to that, Park Moondae's indicator had almost certainly returned. Keundal opened his mouth sincerely.

"You've worked hard...!"

But he stopped speaking.

Cheongryeo stared at a pop-up with wide eyes, as if he had received a shock.

~~~

[You...]

I was the one about to die, but the pop-up looked dazed. I held back a sigh.

"Snap out of it. I'm going to try something before that happens."

If I were the type to meekly become the System, it would've happened long ago. I endured for so long and desperately tried to find a way back. You want me to just die now?

*Not a chance.*

I would find a way out somehow.

I took a deep breath. First, I would verify my assumptions.

"First... it seemed like you guys were preparing something to return me to reality. Is that right?"

The pop-up answered after a brief pause.

[That's right. The plan was to bring you back after restoring your stamina, Junior.]

He had become much calmer.

I understood. So that was why he kept trying to pour energy into me with blessings and other things. I still didn't know the principle behind it, but this wasn't the time to ask. I fired off my prepared questions.

"This is inside the System. The other guys' pop-ups don't appear when I'm in here. Is it correct that you can't try to bring me back to reality from here?"

[Yes.]

[So get out of here.]

It was rational advice. I had escaped while being consumed by the System last time and was fine, so I wanted to escape right away.

The problem was that it wasn't up to me.

"I think I'm trapped. I don't see a way out."

I also just realized I was now speaking freely inside the System, as if it were the most natural thing to do.

*This is bad.*

I had gotten used to it. This place no longer felt pressuring or rejecting. It was starting to feel as comfortable as my own limbs. The 'waiting time' was ending.

Soon, something good would come...

[Junior.]

*Hoo.*

I snapped back to my senses.

*If I'm not careful, I'm in the perfect position to be dragged away and get screwed.*

I unconsciously tried to wipe away cold sweat, only to realize I had no body and groan. Terrifyingly, I still felt fine.

[So you're saying you can't get out of here right now.]

"...Yeah."

I wanted to squeeze my eyes shut, but I had no eyes. It was probably for the best. It was better not to let him know I was shaken.

I asked calmly, "How are you talking to me inside the System right now?"

[The title of GM was still there.]

[So I used it.]

So that was it. The authority could even stop time in the System's virtual world. It was obvious it could display a pop-up within the System.

*But what if he overdoes it and gets sucked into the System too?*

I pictured the complete disaster of us all getting screwed together.

I added, just in case, "Don't do anything stupid. Just wait. I need to... think for a moment."

Fortunately, the pop-up didn't disappear. I panted as I sank into thought.*A way to escape... a way to escape.*

*Damn it.*

Everything was conceptual and abstract. The only concrete method I could imagine was someone like Judan beating me up from the outside to wake me, but that would never work.

I tried to think logically.

*Logically certain...*

In that moment, a crazy idea came to me.

"......"

"If I become the System, won't I naturally return to reality?"

[Junior.]

*No, listen.*This was a past where a System already existed. If a reboot was the goal, there was no need for another System in this timeline.

*No, come to think of it, it's impossible for two Systems to exist at the same time. The System's ability is to go back to the past.*

Two of them couldn't possibly turn back time to different pasts. It was like sending a new employee on assignment for field experience. It might have sent me to the era of Reset Syndrome Cheongryeo, the System's prime.

"Then if I become the System, it will naturally summon me back to reality to find a host."

If it was a reboot, that was the answer. Of course, the one returning to reality would be the System, not Park Moondae...

*Can't I use this to my advantage?*

[You can't be serious.]

"......"

I hurriedly stopped him before he thought I was completely consumed and acted on his own. After talking with him and running a few verifications, I grinned, breaking out in a cold sweat.

"Alright. I have a plan."

First.

"Leave me here and get out."

~~~

In a space of nothingness, the new System was completed.

*Good.*

The waiting was over. Now, it just had to return to where it came from and do what it must. Grant wishes and survive.

*Not bad.*

The System thought so and nodded, then realized it had no head.

*Hmm.*

That felt strange, but the System immediately surrendered to its instincts.

Everything was comfortable. The way back was clear. The pressure that had sucked it into this place would now reverse, pulling it toward reality. It naturally rode the current and let itself go. Soon, the interior of the existing System began to distort. The entire surrounding environment swirled rapidly.

*Ah.*

Space-time flowed forward rapidly. It had already escaped the interior of the existing System. It watched the surroundings flash by, curling up to avoid being crushed by the pressure.

*Let's go back.*

To reality.

At those words, its non-existent heart seemed to pound strangely. The pressure intensified, and the surroundings swirled even faster.

Just then.

TIRING!

[Keundal is calling you.]

Silence.

The System stared at the pop-up. The entire environment tightened, distorted, and moved at a suffocating, lightning-fast speed, yet only that one thing remained still.

Affectionately.

[Keundal recognizes you.]

*It recognizes me?*

For a moment, the System stopped surrendering to the sweeping pressure. It stopped in place, just like the pop-up, and looked again.

*It recognized me...*

In that fleeting moment.

[Keundal is moving you.]

[Target: Park Moondae]

Light burst from the pop-up.

"...!!"

In the light, it saw a phantom white hand appear and grab it. The hand didn't grab all of it, only the part that reached its grasp. It was the part of itself that had protruded, the part that had reacted to the pop-up.

RRRIP!

Park Moondae entered the clenching hand.

"Hyung!"

Park Moondae lifted his head. He was in a familiar officetel. It was the living room, with the fantastic sunset of ❬Magical Boy❭ setting outside the window.

"Hyung!"

This was his own mental space. An artificial figure flickered before his eyes with static and glitches. It looked like neither Park Moondae nor Ryu Gunwoo. It was a form he had seen several times before, Keundal's appearance in the mental world.

"Ah."

It worked.

Park Moondae finally pressed his hands against his own face.

"......"

The prediction was brilliant.

*To return to reality, I had to get out of that bastard Cheongryeo's System.*

The plan was to summon myself right at the moment before I returned to reality. Of course, it was dangerous. Park Moondae swallowed hard, recalling that his ego had been on the verge of merging with the System.

His expression soon softened.

*Still, I succeeded.*

*Look at me, sitting here.*

Across from him, Keundal, the creator of this mental world, looked like he was about to cry. Park Moondae met his gaze and grinned. He finally faced the welcome person who had called for him.

"Hyung, are you crazy?! No, no! How could you do something like this!"

Keundal grabbed his collar.

"You said there was no time! You just told me to call you when the timing was right, but that you'd die if it was off! The dots kept getting smudged! The screen said you were losing Park Moondae! And you did something so dangerous..."

"It worked, didn't it?"

Anyway, it was a success.

"Ugh!"

Keundal looked furious enough to breathe fire. He shook Park Moondae's collar back and forth, but his face became tearful. It was from irrepressible relief, reassurance, and fear.

"I-I only managed to grab you, Hyung, but the System is still trying to return to reality! At this rate, you and the System will merge again as soon as you get there!"

But Park Moondae didn't get anxious. He looked out the living room window at the gentle mix of red, yellow, pink, and purple, as if he had missed them.

He calmly opened his mouth.

"It's fine."

"Huh?"

"The System will be pulled in the opposite direction."

Park Moondae grinned.

"The guy who has a System is calling for it!"

Cheongryeo mouthed the word as he looked ahead at the one who had demanded this.

"......"

A figure that looked exactly like him, but without a smile, stood there.
