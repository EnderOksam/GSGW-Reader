# Chapter 590

TeSTAR's variety show retreat ended that day.

We had quickly submitted the Doll Hunter OST to meet the deadline. We then spent the last day burning with a passion befitting a variety show.

"And, 'If the current TeSTAR wins, filming will end immediately'... Aaaah! If we win, we get to go home right away!"

"OHHHH!"

We spent the day playing various games for the prize of getting off work on time.

The segment was called 'Beat TeSTAR!'

The rule was simple. If we surpassed our best records from games on past variety shows, we could go home.

We also played PPL games for food, clothing, and shelter in between. Even those were changed into wholesome team games, like jumping into a hot spring to find balls with ingredient names written on them.

Well... in a way, it was less wholesome.

"This... the fans will probably like this."

"......"

*Hmm, there will be demand for it, in a different sense.*

*Anyway, the overall structure is pretty good.*

The show started by lowering the barrier for the general public, showing TeSTAR suffering in a traditional variety show format. The latter half appealed to viewers by showing our professionalism as we seriously dived into composing. It then wrapped up at the end so people could watch and laugh without much thought.

"That was fun!"

"No regrets~"

Just like that, our haggard 'we won't let the production team get away with this' attitude from the beginning vanished. TeSTAR happily succeeded in getting off work before sunset.

[No!]

The feeling of leaving work while the PD screamed that it ended too early was excellent. The day ended on a refreshing note.

*The final quality should be decent.*

I roughly pictured it in my head. I estimated the profits we would gain from the variety show, which was currently airing to high praise.

However, after we returned home.

Kim Raebin went online thinking,*I want to look up reviews of the existing Doll Hunter OSTs.*Instead, he was the first to accidentally stumble upon reactions to our new variety show.

He found them through a stream of popular posts about TeSTAR's variety show.

However, the guy was reportedly startled after clicking on those posts.

"......"

*To think that our variety show's staff are getting slammed with criticism!*

"...So you're saying the production team and the person in charge of the CM song must be in a tough spot."

"That's right!"

Kim Raebin nodded vigorously.

I nodded back.

"I see."

*I said I get it.*

"Ah... aren't you surprised?"

That was a lie.

*Why would I be surprised by something so obvious?*

In the first place, writer Ryu Seorin's specialty was creating a villain for viewers to become overly invested in hating.

She probably framed it subtly, intending for them to take some heat and convert it all into buzz. This meant they brought it on themselves intentionally.

"They could've edited it all out, so the fact that they didn't must mean they have a plan."

"But..."

Still, Kim Raebin's expression did not brighten much. The issue seemed to weigh on his mind.

If that was the case.

"Shall we take a look?"

"...! Yes!"

I took his smartphone.

I had to at least pretend to check so he would feel relieved.

*While I'm at it, I'll check the buzz for this show.*

I leisurely scrolled through the screen.

*This is the kind of public opinion that will resolve as the show continues to air. Since the production team themselves are getting cursed out, they must have drawn a line and kept it moderate...*

"......"

Wait.

*What is this.*

It was a total mess.

Only then did I realize Kim Raebin was not just soft-heartedly surprised to see people he knew getting cursed out.

It was just... a fiery hell.

[Wow, but they're even blaming TeSTAR?ㅋㅋㅋ Right, so when Eugene said creativity comes from suffering, he meant he wanted to be confined and threatened while filming a variety show... is that it?!]

[In my opinion, variety shows come from the suffering of the production team. So you guys should edit while eating only raw sweet potatoes.]

So cold!

The fandom mobbed the production team and the PPL manager, as if burning them at the stake.

*To this extent?*

Slightly flustered, I rewatched the first two episodes of the aired show.

However...

*The level of intensity was managed well, though.*

It was a known fact that being boring is a crime in variety shows. If a show was not fun and the cast suffered, it was painful to watch, but if it was fun*and*the cast suffered, did that not just become super entertaining content?

Even parts that could have been frowned upon, like TeSTAR not eating properly, were moderately cut. They were matched with TeSTAR's interviews or a countryside dog, making the scene just look funny and pitiful.

*We were the ones involved and pissed off because there was no agreement...*

Honestly, from a viewer's perspective, it was fun. At this level, it seemed likely to get plenty of defense from the viewership. The production team must have thought that far ahead when they made it.

*What is it?*

I returned to the viewer reactions and looked through the related posts more broadly. Eventually, I found the reason.

[Idol who composed until 3 AM while receiving weird feedback.jpg]

*In the first place, that person didn't even know TeSTAR composed it.*

They were probably just... thoroughly reflecting on their life up to this point.

A few days later, Bae Sejin showed me a text he received from the PD.

"Park Moondae. Um... I got a text."

"Yes?"

[I wanted to express my gratitude separately one more time, so I'm sending this text.]

The long text that followed expressed thanks that TeSTAR took the show seriously and did not just gloss over things.

Instead of the slyness he had shown during the previous meeting, his tone was now that of a production team representative trying to be somewhat sincere. That was probably why Bae Sejin was even more flustered.

"...I don't know why he sent this to me. What... what do you think?"

*What is there to think.*

I shrugged and handed the smartphone back.

"He's realized that thanks to you requesting that meeting, they got a chance to handle the current public opinion."

"...!"

Without that catalyst, the production team probably would not have bothered to pay so much attention to TeSTAR's environment and try to improve it.

*They probably would have stopped at making things just a little better while testing the waters.*

That would have been apparent in the footage. Naturally, it would have been much harder to manage public opinion, with people saying, 'The treatment isn't actually good, they're just using editing tricks because they got backlash'.

So now, they had realized it firsthand. The meeting was not simply the cast demanding an improvement in their environment, but an opinion brought up from a more macroscopic perspective.

"It seems the production team is now acknowledging that you really helped them."

"...I see."

Bae Sejin nodded and took the smartphone.

A sense of pride clearly flashed in his eyes.

He did not delete the text and seemed to have replied well in his own way later.

*Well, that's good then.*

We had properly achieved the variety show's goals, after all. We secured both the sense of familiarity from 'They suffer like that too,' and the professional charm of 'But they're working hard and producing well'.

I returned to my room, stretched, and lay on the bed.

*All that's left depends on how well ❬Doll Hunter❭ Season 2 turns out...*

That was something we would only know once it aired.

We even pushed the OST composition part of our variety show to Part 2 to align with the broadcast. We would have to wait a few more weeks to find out.

"I acted with no regrets."

"Oh... then that's enough."

Now, TeSTAR just had to focus on the tour while waiting for the latter half of our variety show and ❬Doll Hunter❭ Season 2.

"Since we couldn't rest a single day during the holidays, there was talk of taking a break now."
