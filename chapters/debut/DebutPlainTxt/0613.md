[Contact with other employees who are not fellow permanent employees requires mutual consent!]

The premise was confidentiality, but that was all bullshit. They were obviously aiming for this picture of mutual misunderstanding.

*Well, I guess it would be funny...*

*So Lee Sejin was being clever.*

I realized that when Lee Sejin had spoken quietly from behind me, his intention was not just to scare me. He wanted to make me turn around voluntarily. I decided to try something similar, using reverse psychology.

"Section Chief."

"Aaaaaargh!"

"Noooooo!"

"......"

No.

*Are you that scared?*

Oh.

"......"

*You... you're not even scared of ghosts.*

*...Maybe next time I should try waiting on the 4th floor by the elevator again.*

However, this action only made the guys panic even more.

"The music box over there...!"

"Uwaaah!"

In the end, those guys, who were (voluntarily) being chased by the disappeared employees, ended up stepping on all sorts of traps and falling into an even more urgent crisis (voluntarily).

*Was it 'a comedy in long-shot, but a tragedy in close-up'?*

"...I'm pressing 'Deliver'."

"Aaaah, really!"

*They're being pretty desperate among themselves.*

That was why it was even more so. At this point, every guy who met the music box monster and got 'delivered' could not accept reality and looked like they were about to die of embarrassment.

"Gosh... please edit this out."

"Kwahahaha!"

"......"

The final survivor, who with such tragic eyes delivered even himself at the end, was...

"Euhahahaha! Ahahak!"

[Permanent Employee Appointment]

Bae Sejin A 14:35:26

[Congratulations!]

He received a grand celebration. Bae Sejin's mouth trembled as he looked at the holographic panel with his name on it. He briefly denied reality upon seeing the guys clapping. However, his face soon turned red and blue.

"Wait a minute, then you guys!!"

"We did our best to persuade you."

"......"

It seemed he could not bring himself to deny it. The guy gaped for a moment, then finally muttered in a deflated tone.

"It would've been better to disappear early on."

"That's right."

Seeing as Kim Raebin was the first to be eliminated, it seems the production team was considerate enough to eliminate the most timid members first.

*Of course, I was an exception since I wrote my own name.*

As I was nodding, Lee Sejin poked me in the side and snickered.

"Honestly~ Assistant Manager Moondae, it looked like you were enjoying it a bit."

"I was not."

"It was fun seeing people get scared of you, wasn't it! After always being the one who gets scared, you were probably thinking, 'What's all this?'"

"I was not."

*It's slander.*

Anyway, after that, everything proceeded swiftly and smoothly. Once we were all together, it was as if everyone's courage returned, and their spirits soared.

*Is this the permanent employee effect?*

Even after seeing the bizarre, giant altar attached to the basement storage wall, the guys just made a fuss a few times before simply nodding.

"So we just have to find all these music boxes and place them all up there, right?"

"......"

"There's one left. This is easy!"

After screaming and running away together from the last remaining monster before 'delivering' it, we placed the final music box on the avant-garde altar. There were eight of them.

"An octagon. Hmm... so we place one on each point of the octagon."

"It has a unique design."

"Maybe it's... based on a historical artifact?"

One guy even offered this opinion after observing it carefully.

"Looking at it like this, don't the music boxes we delivered kind of resemble our members... I mean, our fellow employees?"

"Haha. Do they?"

*He's already thinking about merchandise.*

In any case, once the altar consumed the eight music boxes, the entire wall actually opened up...

"......"

"Woah...!"

It led to a new, modern space.

[Laboratory]

It was a strange biology lab, a classic staple of mysteries.

"Is it something like human experimentation?"

"Ugh, that's creepy."

Inside, large experimental containers filled with unknown liquids and mannequin parts, or torsos, were scattered about as if on display. It was a magnificent sight, but one that made me resolve to put a 15+ age rating on it when we released it on WeTube.

"Alright, alright, let's find our way home from work."

We unraveled the story, which had now become more of an exciting adventure than a horror, as we retraced the full account of the incident.

"So, at this corporation..."

"At*our*company!"

"R-Right, our company... Anyway, it seems like they manage and research these music box ghosts... or something here."

"Looks like we got jobs at a place that would pay a lot of hazard pay."

Looking at the research records in the censored documents scattered everywhere, it seemed they had also conducted AI research using the music box monsters.

*They're really getting their money's worth out of this setting.*

In the midst of this, we also discovered a key element.

"Look at this!"

[For Temporary Hires / Summer Special Duty Manual]

"Ooh."

"So they made it... here...!"

It was the item with the conspicuously different, jarring tone.

"But why did it say not to go to the stairs? If we had gone to the stairs, we would've figured it out right away."

The point was that if we had gone to the stairs, seen the space inside, and met a 'permanent employee', persuading them would have been immediately easier. If it were a matter of 'adult circumstances', the reason would be, 'Guys, if you do that, the shoot will end too quickly and it'll be no fun'.

*Still, there must be a plausible reason.*

After thinking for a moment, I added.

"Maybe it wasn't there originally, and someone added it in a hurry."

[Don't go to the stairs]

"To stop us from becoming permanent employees?"

"Maybe a rival?"

Lightly tossing around various speculations, the guys continued forward, and so, at the end of the lab, the members solved the final puzzle.

"Oh. Good."

Kim Raebin.

[Permanent Employee Appointment]

Park Moondae A 12:33:52

"Ryu Chungwoo."

*He's still good at his job.*

His health.

[Section Chief Cha Eugene. See that pole on the left? Let's use it.]

[Wait, the left...]

"I haven't just seen it once or twice."

*Even when he faced me in hide-and-seek, he aimed for a psychological weakness.*

"...I became certain of it after monitoring this time."

Why?

"Why are you uncomfortable using your left arm?"
