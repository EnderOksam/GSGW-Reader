# Chapter 485

Cheongryeo looked at Keundal's message pop-up. Ironically, the person most dumbfounded by this situation was not even here. It was the one who sent the pop-up in the first place.

[H-Huuuh, what the he—]

"He's even making typos."

Cheongryeo propped his chin on his hand, observing it like a fascinating phenomenon. Keundal let out another shriek through the pop-up.

[Uwaaah!!]

*What a mess.*

I resisted the urge to press my brow and asked, "How long have you been able to see it?"

"Hmm, this is the first time."

Cheongryeo did not even blink. He watched the pop-up pour out whimpering noises for a few more seconds, then spoke nonchalantly.

"Is that... the thing using your old body, junior?"

"......"

"Ryu Gunwoo."

Cheongryeo smiled faintly.

"It seems you two switched bodies a while ago. I'd like to hear an explanation."

"......"

He was talking about when Keundal and I swapped bodies.

*He knows about that too? Wait.*

"...Did you notice at the awards ceremony?"

No wonder it felt strange when our eyes met as he zoomed in with that cannon-like camera.

"That's right," Cheongryeo readily affirmed. He then tapped his chin with his finger.

"Hmm, let's just say that your... judgment seemed a bit clouded, junior. Not like your usual self."

"......"

*Is he saying Keundal looked stupid?*

*Look at him choosing his words, even though he knows the situation.*

A guy who is not even in the industry ended up in an idol's body, and he was here assessing his damn judgment?

"......"

Anyway, the mood was right for explaining why he was in that state.

"Something did happen."

I gave him a summary of the events with the System fragment that possessed Keundal.

Cheongryeo's reaction was not particularly dramatic as he listened. It was probably because the problem had been resolved at a moderate level, instead of a crazy incident like a building collapse.

...Or perhaps he had already guessed.

"I see. Ah, you posted the photos you took back then online, right? I saw them. They were good."

"......"

*How did he know about that... Never mind.*

I was speechless when he smiled and thanked me for the good pictures.

*Forget it.*

*The guy's like a ghost.*

In any case, I had a guess why Cheongryeo could suddenly see the Status Window.

"I think it's because you were a GM in the virtual world the System created."

"Hmm. That's convincing."

That was it. Cheongryeo had a history of being a GM, a Game Master, in a game the System created.

*He could manipulate the settings window and pause the game.*

He was backstabbed because of it, but it was also an incredible ability that let him land a blow on the System.

*If the power from that role returned to some extent when I reactivated this System... it would make sense.*

*Hmm, in that case. There's something I should test.*

"......"

Without a word, I summoned a Status Window pop-up in the air.

[New! Update Completion Reward]

[New! New Function Unlocked]

[New! Survey]

"......"

It was in a different location from Keundal's pop-up.

[Hyung...?]

I deliberately did not respond. Then, Cheongryeo, who was in front of me, opened his mouth.

"I think it's calling for you, junior."

"Yeah. I'm sending a message telling him not to panic."

With that, I confirmed it.

*Cheongryeo had reacted exclusively to Keundal's pop-up.*

*If that's the case... does that mean he can't see the Status Window next to it?*

I once again pretended not to notice while checking his reaction. Pupils, expression, gestures...

*...It's natural.*

Of course, this was a guy who had successfully worked for decades in a profession that required extreme control over his facial expressions. The chance of it being a fake-out was not zero, but...

I unfolded my arms.

*I'll let this much slide.*

If he really could see it and was determined to hide it just to mess with me? No, our relationship had not soured to that point.

*I have at least that much trust in him now.*

I came to that conclusion. The only problem was that Cheongryeo's analysis was just beginning.

His fingers skimmed the condensation on his glass.

"Hmm, so this thing you call the 'System', junior... it didn't disappear completely but entered multiple bodies."

"......"

"Even if it's a spoil of war, to absorb and use it like that. Don't you think you're taking an excessively large risk?"

He lifted his gaze from the glass and met my eyes.

*Applying the System to the company?*

[What will you do if you feel the urge to 'Restart'?]

"......"

His eyes seemed to say just that. It was the kind of conviction only someone who had internalized countless repetitions could possess. The certainty that if you use it, you will definitely get addicted.

*It's almost like fanaticism.*

A strange chill crept up my spine, despite the summer heat. It was enough to make me doubt my own judgment, my decision to use the System that was now my bound item.

*...Fuck.*

I swallowed a sigh.

"I only used it to break away from T1 and go independent. I don't plan on using it anymore."

"I see."

Cheongryeo shrugged as if to say 'do as you please', but then casually tossed out his next words.

"I'd like to check."

"......"

"The Status Window?"

"Yes."

At that, another pop-up quickly appeared, as if its ears had perked up.

[Designate 'GM' as a System Administrator?]

*Are you crazy?*

*This bastard doesn't know when to butt in and when to stay out.*

*Get lost.*

What kind of nonsense was this, suggesting I reveal my internal information to a competitor. I immediately dismissed the Status Window.

Then I barely managed to speak.

"I just summoned the Status Window."

"Oh? As expected, I can't see it."

"......"

That concluded the conversation. However, I felt even more uneasy. Meeting him made me realize something new.

*To be honest, I did say I would get rid of the company's System...*

*Damn it, it's not like there's nothing bothering me.*

I looked at Cheongryeo and recalled the pop-up from when I met Kwon Heeseung.

['Fragment of ■■■' Holder Confirmed!]

"......"

*A 'Fragment of ■■■' must be a piece of the System.*

Since Kwon Heeseung had one, it was highly probable that this guy, who also experienced the System, had a fragment inside him too.

That was the problem.

*I could only absorb more of these fragments by raising the company's grade.*

To put it another way, it meant this.

[If I terminate this System, then naturally, there will be no company grade operated by the System.]

[Then I won't be able to retrieve the 'Fragment of ■■■' that Cheongryeo might have.]

*...It means this guy will be hit with a mission failure without a Status Window.*

I could not just say he would be fine since he was experienced... I guess not.

*I don't even know how many times this bastard restarted while going through that experience.*

Besides, if that were to trigger his Reset Syndrome again, there would be nothing more horrifying.

*No, to be blunt, couldn't the fallout hit me and get me killed?*

Besides... did not that guy give me crucial help to escape when I was hit with that status abnormality? During the building collapse.

"......"

*Alright.*

*Yes, I should draw a line out of conscience.*

I made my decision.

*I'll leave the company System as it is.*

However, I could not just be caught off guard like last time.

*At the very least, I need to figure out the reason and principle behind the Updates caused by the fragments.*

[Why did my ego from the timeline before I became Park Moondae suddenly appear?]

[And why, of all people, was it Cha Eugene?]

The effect and the target. To that extent,

*I need to be fully prepared and minimize the variables.*

After that, I would have to create a stable way to retrieve his fragment without triggering a mission failure. However, from now on, I would refrain from using the System itself carelessly. For now, I would just watch without touching it.

*At least until Keundal finishes his analysis.*

The previously quiet pop-up flickered again.

[I will do my best...!]

*Thanks.*

*I'll make sure you're well paid.*

[Hyung... but don't I have more money than you right now...]

"......"

[I-I'm sorry. I mean, this is all thanks to you, Hyung, so please feel free to use it...]

*It's fine.*

We reached a dramatic agreement for VIP concert seats through the pop-up.

For the record, Cheongryeo watched the pop-up with interest and asked a few questions before belatedly asking how I had been. Though it was just about work.

"Ah, I heard your group is filming a reality variety show, junior."

*That leaked too?*

"Hmm, but the director you used to work with... he should be under a T1-affiliated studio."

"......"

The variety show production team that made everything from that travel show where we sold hotteok to That House's Grandson Carrot Coins was all tied up with T1. So it was a huge pain.

Cheongryeo continued smoothly with a sly smile.

"Shall I introduce you to someone good?"

*Oh.*

*This bastard is determined to fleece me.*

"We've already finished casting."

*Just be satisfied with the paid platform for behind-the-scenes content, you punk.*

Because the person we cast was none other than...

~~~

"...Hello."

"Wow, hello, Writer!"

The main writer for TeSTAR's new reality show sat across from the energetically greeting members.

Beaming, Keun Sejin offered a handshake.

"It's been a while!"

"Ah, yes."

Ryu Seorin.

She was the writer from ❬Idol Inc.❭.

She was also related to my university senior and twin fansite master, Ryu Seojin, but I would think about that later.

In any case, she was one of the most important staff members scouted for TeSTAR's new reality show. There were two very simple reasons.

First.

"It's great to be working with you again, Writer."

"...Ah, that's right."

It was because she had also been cast aside by T1.

Why was this person, a key contributor to the ❬Idol Inc.❭ series, cast aside?

*It's because we, who debuted from that show, all left to become independent.*

Of course, that was not the only reason. A complicated, politically-tied power struggle in the broadcasting industry was apparently involved. The freelance sub-writer got caught in that fierce office politics and lost half her livelihood.

*Of course, knowing her personality, it probably would have been possible for her to cling on tenaciously.*

It seemed she chose not to. Because there was no need to.

After a few hits collaborating with Netplus, she built connections and seemed to shift her career entirely in that direction. For the record, we also participated in her first work.

That... hellish KPOP Camp.

"How many years has it been since then?"

"It's been quite a while. I was grateful back then."

Ryu Seorin nodded. She even offered some lip service, saying things went well thanks to the members of TeSTAR.

Of course, that was not the reason we were working with her.

The second reason was...

"So... about this reality show."

*She's good.*

Whether sacrificing contestants or amplifying a single point of emotion into ten, she was a writer who knew how to make a good variety show. She also had a good eye for people.

In other words, there was no one more suitable for TeSTAR to partner with right now.

"You're not looking for the cute, charming reality programs that idols usually produce, are you?"

It meant we should look elsewhere if that was what we wanted.

Ryu Chungwoo immediately nodded.

"Yes. Even with our previous reality shows, we've always leaned toward making them fun, even if it meant more hardship and time."

"That's right."

Ryu Seorin answered without hesitation.

"Then shall we go with a style similar to that PD? I'm acquainted with the writers there, too."

Confidence laced Ryu Seorin's blunt voice.

*As expected.*

*She would properly create that atmosphere, nothing half-baked. The kind of atmosphere where they joyfully tease and trick the cast.*

Besides, we had already made several variety shows with that PD's team, so the public would likely be generous and think, 'Ah, this is TeSTAR's style'.

However.

"No."

"Hmm?"

It felt like we would not be able to avoid comments about it being a copycat.

*This isn't Ryu Seorin's specialty.*

Naturally, the originality would be lower than that of the crazy production army, and there was no need to accept that.

That was why.

"Please do what you originally do."

"...!"

We should each do what we do best.

Ryu Seorin narrowed her eyes, her expression asking, 'No way'.

"Well, if you mean what I originally do..."

"Yes."

"......"

There was a prime example we all knew. TeSTAR's new reality show would go that way.

"Like ❬Idol Inc.❭."

Ryu Seorin's expression immediately screamed, 'What kind of crazy talk is this?'.

However, the investor always has a strong say.

A few days later, filming for TeSTAR's reality show began as scheduled.

I opened my eyes.

I saw a familiar ceiling. The one in my room... at the dorm I had seen for years.

"......"

At first glance, it sounded natural. Was it not perfectly normal to see a familiar ceiling when you open your eyes?

And there was one more premise to this.

*TeSTAR has moved.*

In other words, this was not TeSTAR's current dorm. It was a space that looked like our dorm before we moved.

"What is this."

I sat up. Something rested on my stomach in the dim room.

"...?"

It was a yellow file.

I narrowed my eyes, adjusting to the dark, and read the file's cover aloud for the camera.

"Your Profile File."

Then I opened it.

[Your occupation is a doctor.]

A description and characteristics were written below that.

*Hmm.*

*So it's this kind of thing.*

In truth, TeSTAR had deliberately not listened to a detailed briefing on the program's internal settings. It was for the sake of reality. We only knew the format and the objective.

"What is this for?" I dutifully said aloud as I got out of bed.

The lines from our meeting to scout Ryu Seorin replayed in my head.

[We're experienced, so we already know.]

The concept for TeSTAR's new reality program.

[That writer's survival shows are fun.]

It was a death survival.
