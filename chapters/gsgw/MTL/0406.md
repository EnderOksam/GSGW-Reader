# Chapter 405

I opened my eyes.

<br>

—Good morning! All workers, please prepare for today’s assignment~

<br>

The familiar roll-call announcement. The same familiar surroundings.

And familiar work uniforms worn by people lying unconscious in the waiting area, slowly stirring one by one.

But their responses were nothing like what I was used to.

“Uh... huh?”

“What’s going on? Excuse me, where are we?”

“No, I mean... I was told I’d get paid by the day, so I took down the number and called...”

The first day of the Workers at the Namhae Branch.

Around a thousand people murmured among themselves, bewildered by the vast waiting area and the sheer number of people.

They were confused about why they had suddenly woken up here wearing these clothes, too, when a voice came through the speakers.

<br>

*Bzzzt—*

<br>

[Ahem, microphone test.]

<br>

The roll-call announcement cut off, and someone else began speaking.

<br>

[Yes. You’re all the people who applied after seeing the recruitment notice for workers at the Daydream Namhae Branch, correct?]

<br>

“...!”

“Yes, yes, that’s right!”

“Who are you?”

<br>

[I’m the person who’ll be assigning you work from now on.]

<br>

A researcher newly assigned to the branch had arrived.

<br>

#f#—You’ve already sent them? ...Damn it.#f#

<br>

Baek Saheon, who had been put on hold, was naturally given special treatment once the researcher arrived and was ‘comfortably’ ushered into a private office.

Which meant he had quietly been pushed out of the branch's affairs.

…I had a reasonable suspicion that the Branch Manager had probably timed this arrangement to coincide with the schedule for processing and producing Workers.

<br>

#f#—It’ll be difficult for me to come visit you separately on a regular basis now, and I probably won’t be able to give you information in person whenever something comes up… So don’t do anything dangerous. Just keep your head down and stay out of trouble.#f#

<br>

All kinds of shouting were erupting around me as I recalled that conversation.

“Hey! Who gave you permission to just take people like this?”

“When did you change our clothes? Is this even legal? And what’s this sticker supposed to be?”

“No, I said I wanted to work! How is this kind of treatment acceptable? Fine, then at least let me out!”

<br>

[Yes, yes. You agreed to this over the phone beforehand, so this was carried out under the confidentiality agreement. First, let me explain the working conditions. If you don’t like them, you’re free to leave.]

[First of all, the daily wage is one million won. It’ll be paid in cash.]

<br>

“...!!”

<br>

[Occasionally, you may be assigned two jobs in a single day. If that happens, you’ll be paid twice.]

<br>

Those were the magic words that silenced every complaint.

But they were deceptive, too.

“One... one million won?”

“Do we actually get paid?”

<br>

[Yes. You’ll be paid in cash every day.]

<br>

At the promise of daily cash payments that could never actually be taken outside, the people filling the waiting area gradually became more docile.

Then they began to grow excited, their hopes rising at the thought of how much they could make.

<br>

[All of our workers stay here together for confidentiality purposes, and you can work for up to 100 days.]

<br>

That was all the information he gave them before the employment documents began to be issued.

“Wow.”

“Then... what, it’s seriously one hundred million won?”

“I heard they were making a fortune from that hair-loss medicine... Guess it’s true.”

Hearing the Workers murmuring hopefully, I felt my own unease settle even further.

“Exactly what kind of work are we doing? It’s not like you’re going to make us take some cancer drug or send us into a radioactive area with nothing on, right?”

<br>

[Yes. Nothing like that. It’s basically a combination of drug trials and odd jobs, but security is important, so the pay is high. You won’t be able to leave the facility during your stay, after all.]

<br>

“Ah~”

<br>

[Then we’ll have you fill out the simple employment contract.]

<br>

Finally, I received the employment documents with a remarkably cold heart.

<br>

And I signed it.

Under an alias.

<br>

When I handed the documents back, I was given a temporary number.

No. 99.

“…….”

“Ah, you surprised me. Sh*t.”

As I was looking at the number, someone bumped hard into my shoulder as they passed, then turned around and started getting angry at me instead.

“Hey, why the f*ck are you just standing around in the middle of the way? Huh?!”

I was used to seeing that cursing face. He was one of the two Workers from the X group who used to harass Z999.

For some reason, I felt like this was a turning point.

“…….”

I said nothing. I simply stared at him in silence.

“W-What?”

I didn’t look away.

The guy, who had been muttering curses under his breath, quietly turned away and disappeared into the crowd of Workers as though nothing had happened.

And then he picked a fight with someone else.

“Hey, your handwriting is f*cking ugly. Is it because you’re a f*cking scrawny little shrimp?”

*‘Just as I thought.’*

He only acted like that toward people who looked easy to push around.

*‘He’s exactly the kind of person I can’t stand….’*

But at the same time, remembering how the Workers who had bullied me in the previous cycle had ended up left me with a strange feeling.

…The people who had been heavily contaminated and mutated into instruments, some of whom were still alive—would they have also been ‘disposed of’ once their expiration dates ran out?

I couldn’t tell whether that would have been the lesser horror, or something even more terrible.

*‘Phew.’*

Either way.

It almost felt unreal.

Being here, in a place where everything from two weeks before I woke up in this body was being recreated exactly as it had happened, felt almost like I had gone back in time.

Back to a point in the past, when I wasn't here.

And I already knew what to expect from everything that would happen in the future.

<br>

[Yes. We’ve confirmed that all of you are willing to work.]

[Now we’ll assign you to your work groups. The group and number you’re assigned will determine the benefits and accommodations available to you.]

[Those with the lower numbers might even get to spend all 100 days living like they're ordering room service at a luxury hotel in Seoul.]

<br>

And on the other hand, those with the higher numbers would probably be given a miserable room, with nothing but earthen walls.

<br>

[And these arrangements...]

[Will be determined according to the results of today's work.]

<br>

…Here it comes.

The first assignment.

The very first Darkness exploration.

<br>

#f#—You’re saying it doesn’t matter which Darkness you’re sent into? Well, at least try to find an opening to somehow send a note to me.#f#

#f#—No. It's fine.#f#

<br>

The situation wouldn’t allow it.

The CCTV cameras had just been reinstalled, and the facility is only now starting a new operating cycle.

*‘This is when the researcher will be watching over the Worker’s Zone the most, keeping a close eye on everything.’*

I couldn't let even the slightest hint slip that Baek Saheon was looking out for me.

Not if I wanted my performance to be properly recognized.

<br>

#f#—The Darkness they use for the Workers' first deployment probably won't have that high of a mortality rate.#f#

<br>

After all, if everyone got wiped out on the first job, that would be a huge loss.

<br>

#f#—But they'll still need to make use of having such a large number of people, so there'll probably be a reasonable amount of danger—enough to obtain a decent amount of Dream Solution.#f#

<br>

In other words.

A Darkness likely to be listed in the Darkness Exploration Records, with plenty of exploration data already accumulated.

<br>

#f#—All I need is the right keywords.#f#

<br>

I can figure out the rest from there.

<br>

#f#—...Well, you were a rookie hire who rose through the ranks faster than anyone else in Headquarters. Do whatever you want.#f#

<br>

And I truly intended to do exactly that.

Soon, once the Workers’ headcount was completed, we were loaded onto mine carts and transported away. I quietly slipped into a seat, keeping myself out of sight so I wouldn’t draw any attention….

Before long, the mine cart came to a stop at a new containment zone.

<br>

[Yes. Then I’ll explain today’s work.]

<br>

I listened carefully….

<br>

[The job you’ll be doing from this point onward is cleaning.]

<br>

“…….”

<br>

[Once you go inside, you’ll find a room connected to another building—your job is simply to clean that building thoroughly.]

[If you take care of a large number of areas properly, and make sure to organize the cleaning tools neatly before bringing them back, that will be reflected in your performance evaluation as well.]

<br>


“That’s it?”

“What is this, a kindergarten? They want us to organize the cleaning tools properly before bringing them back? Heh.”

The people around me began murmuring. I could hear them joking that this sounded like some kind of prank straight out of a movie.

<br>

[We’ll start by letting those with the lower numbers in first.]

<br>

“…….”

I turned my head.

Far in the distance behind me, I could see Worker No. 401.

Assistant Manager Jin Nasol.

Her hair had returned to its original length, and she was looking at me, too.

<br>

#f#—I think it would be better if we acted like we didn’t know each other.#f#

#f#—Because people will get suspicious? Well, I suppose there’s nothing wrong with being cautious.#f#

<br>

The fact that we had gone around together during the previous cycle would probably be recorded in the dead former researcher's records.

*‘They might find it strange if we were already moving as a pair from the very beginning.’*

We needed to join up naturally at some point.

Which meant—

<br>

#f#—For the first job, it would probably be better if we handled the Darkness separately.#f#

#f#—Well, sure. Any objections?#f#

#f#—No.#f#

<br>

I think I smiled.

<br>

#f#—Not at all.#f#

<br>

Our eyes crossed briefly, but I turned my head without even exchanging a greeting with the former elite field exploration team member.

Then I put on the familiar armband-style Worker equipment and, following the instructions, entered the Darkness in turn.

My temporary number was 99.

Assigned to Group 4, I entered fourth alongside twenty-five other Workers, passing through the containment door into the darkness beyond.

“…….”

<br>

What greeted me was…

A shabby, run-down space that looked almost like a closet.

Somewhere outsiders never went, where cleaners might have been able to take a brief break.

“Hmm?”

“Jeez, it’s cramped.”

“What kind of room is this?”

While everyone around me grumbled, I quickly spotted a cabinet along the wall and opened it.

Inside were all sorts of cleaning tools, along with a laminated sheet attached to the door.

“Oh?”

“Did you find something?”

“…Looks like instructions for the cleaning job.”

I quickly skimmed through the contents.

+$

So you’re ■■, the cleaner who came here to work today, right? Thank you so much!

A whopping twenty-five people volunteered for cleaning duty. That means you’re one of them, ■■! Isn’t that great? Since we have so many people, we were able to take on a bigger job!

The building you’ll be cleaning today is a wonderful resort facility. Make sure to give it a good, thorough clean!

“Whoa….”

The people around me let out awkward little laughs as they looked over the random assortment of cleaning tools inside the closet.

“Wait, isn’t this like one of those cleaning games? There was a trend for a while where you had to mop the floors, clean the ceilings, stuff like that.”

“I think I’ve seen something like that on Shorts!”

“But they’re really going to pay us a million won for this? What is this, some kind of hidden-camera show?”

“Is this a set?”

“No, it doesn’t make sense in the first place. How are they even going to judge whether we cleaned properly? This is way too half-assed.”

“Even if they’re a big corporation and all, you can’t expect much from a company this young. They haven’t even been listed on the stock market for that long.”

The people's casual chatter as they commented on the situation reached my ears, but I wasn't particularly shaken. I had already figured out what was going on.

As expected.

*‘Of course a ghost story like this would show up.’*

One of the possibilities that had crossed my mind the moment I heard them mention ‘cleaning’ had turned out to be exactly right.

+-

A fairly ordinary ghost story—one of the ones registered in the early days.

It was classified as a C-grade because things could occasionally get completely insane, but I had a full set of exploration methods in my head that could more or less be called a ‘strategy guide.’

It was the type of ghost story that reminded me of a game—the kind that made me wonder what I would have done in their shoes as I read.

*‘The problem is, these things usually get more terrifying the longer they go on….’*

Haah….

Well… I’d just have to endure it. What else could I do?

*‘This reminds me of when I cleaned the underground security area.’*

If I’d made up my mind, there was no point in standing around.

I immediately searched through the closet again.

Then I decided to bring a mop.

*‘I already knew this was the best thing to start with.’*

Among them, I also found several sheets of advertising stickers for the cleaning company, with ‘Washing Combo’ printed across them in an exaggerated font. I made sure to take every one.

*‘Putting them up would count toward additional points.’*

Right.

That was my plan.

I knew exactly how to clear this ghost story in the most efficient way possible—and earn the highest score while I was at it.

*‘And I’m going to do it without holding anything back.’*

And then….

I’d hand all of it over to Headquarters.

In what form?

*‘A manual.’*

I’d give them a perfect manual—something they would never have expected from a Worker at the Namhae Branch.

And I wouldn’t stop there.

Every day.

All week long.

Every time I entered a ghost story, I would compile a complete Darkness Exploration manual and send it up to Headquarters.

*‘Let’s see if they can still refuse to promote me after I give them six of these in a single week.’*

I was going to make it impossible for them to keep overlooking me.

And that was only the first step.

“Heh. What are we supposed to clean with this?”

“It’s all so random.”

“Oh?”

I passed by the Workers, who were still chatting among themselves and picking up and putting down the cleaning tools, and checked the door opposite us.

<br>

Washing Combo!

Today's Cleaning Area

<br>

“Wait, we're starting already?”

“Hey, take it easy.”

The longer we wait, the more likely we are to run into some seriously terrifying phenomena….

*‘They’ll understand soon enough.’*

I paid no attention to their remarks and opened the door.

<br>

*Click.*

<br>

With that short sound, I saw the inside of the ‘building that the Group 4 Workers were supposed to clean today’….

“……!”

“Whoa… it’s fancy.”

“They weren’t kidding when they called it a resort. This place is the real deal.”

“Wouldn’t somewhere like this normally use a professional cleaning company?”

<br>

Small chandeliers with elegant lights swayed gently from the ceiling.

<br>

A grand golden staircase stood majestically in the vast, beautiful lobby, its steps draped in magenta velvet like flowing curtains. Warm light shimmered off crystal ornaments adorning the walls.

A luxurious and magnificent, dream-like golden…

<br>

Resort.

<br>

The building you’ll be cleaning today is a wonderful resort facility. Make sure to give it a good, thorough clean!

*‘…Good grief.’*

<br>

The lobby of the Golden Flower Resort unfolded before my eyes.
