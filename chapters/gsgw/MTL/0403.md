# Chapter 402

...The Purgatory Choir's ensemble had visited every area in Namhae Branch where signs of life still remained.

Unsurprisingly, only a handful of Workers had survived. No... perhaps the surprising thing was that anyone had survived at all.

And even those who had...

<br>

%%“Uhh? Uhhhhh? Uhh, uhh."%%

%%“Heeh-heehheeh-heeh-heehheeh.”%%

<br>

I swallowed hard.

Inside the storage room before me, eleven figures shifted and writhed in the darkness.

Most appeared catatonic, while a few let out strange murmurs that made it impossible to tell whether they were awake or merely muttering in their sleep.

But they weren't words.

They were sounds.

Voices that never quite became language, as though they were trying to imitate musical instruments.

<br>

%%“Hooo, hooooo-hoo hoo-hoo-hooo.”%%

<br>

As my eyes adjusted to the darkness, the grotesque silhouettes inside the storage room slowly came into focus.

Long, stringed-like bones had grown where one person's arms should’ve been. Another's skull looked as though it had caved in on itself. One figure's facial features had shifted into a single horizontal line.

Contaminated beings that only barely still resembled distorted people.

Existences that had failed to become complete instruments, yet could no longer truly be called human.

“......”

And among them...

“Ugh... hhk....”

Scattered here and there were a few silhouettes that still appeared normal.

There couldn't have been more than three.

Most of them stared blankly into space or had likewise fallen into a catatonic state, as though something else was wrong with them...

But one...

“Ugh... hhk... u-ugh...?”

One person who was crying, their face already contorted with fear, noticed me standing beyond the door.

“A... a person!”

“......!”

“P-please... save me....”

The moment the Worker saw my security team uniform, they desperately tried to crawl toward the door. But with both their hands and feet bound, they could do nothing but writhe helplessly on the floor.

Tears streamed down their face.

“Just let me out. Please... I-I don't even need the money!”

......

“I-I swear, I won't tell anyone. Really! I'll keep my mouth shut for the rest of my life. I have to get out of here. My grandmother... she's got no one but me....”

......

“Please.”

I stepped back.

“H-Hey!”

I grabbed a notebook and pen from the desk beside the filing cabinets, quickly wrote something down, and held it up for the Worker to see.

<br>

$$– I’ll see what I can do.$$

<br>

“...!!”

A faint glimmer of hope spread across the Worker's face.

“Th-thank you! Thank you so much....”

I turned away and hurried out of the storage room, as if escaping.

Their words of gratitude continued from behind me, but I wasn't in any state to accept them.

Once they calmed down, they would probably realize what I had actually said.

It wasn’t that I had promised to get them out of here. I had only meant that I would look into it.

Maybe... it had just been something I said to make the situation easier to get through.

“......”

Of course, I hadn't said it just to brush them off...

But honestly, I didn't know what else I was supposed to do.

*‘If I can save them, I want to.’*

Otherwise... what kind of person would I be?

But if I secretly helped them escape, the temporary manager would end up taking the blame. Or worse, all of us could get caught up in it.

*‘...What if I made them unconscious and secretly moved them out?’*

A few possibilities came to mind, but... I wasn't sure.

Knowing there was a chance it could put Baek Saheon or Assistant Manager Jin Nasol in danger, every option felt far too reckless.

*‘...Huu.’*

For now, I needed to head back.

Holding back a sigh, I stepped into the hallway and began making my way out of the personnel-only area—

......

“...!!”

<br>

A member of the security team was standing there.

<br>

I knew it instantly.

It was the same one that had been following me.

*‘...!’*

I immediately began backing away, slowly putting distance between us. If things had come to this, it would be better to find Baek Saheon and tell him what was happening.

I took one step back.

<br>

*Tuk.*

*...Tuk.*

<br>

They were getting closer.

<br>

*Tuk.*

*...Tuk.*

<br>

They moved forward by the same amount that I moved backward.

Strangely, they seemed determined to maintain the exact distance between us.

*‘No.’*

Something felt wrong. Cold sweat ran down my back.

*‘Turn around and run.’*

I shifted my body at an angle, gathering strength throughout my body to sprint—

<br>

“Just as I thought.”

<br>

Right now...

<br>

Was the security team member the one who had spoken?

<br>

“To think we’d meet again like this.”

The security member raised a hand to their head.

With a small sound, the headgear came off into their hand, revealing the face hidden beneath it....

<br>

“I’m really, really happy to see you again.”

<br>

It was a familiar face.

Sweat-dampened, messy white curls. Black eyes, with pupils that shimmered green. A complexion that had grown slightly pale.

“...Dolphin?”

I questioned my own eyes.

But the cheerful voice coming from those small, neatly arranged features hadn’t changed at all.

“Though I’m not an Assistant Manager anymore!”

It was Assistant Manager Lee Seonghae.

...Why was she at Namhae Branch, wearing a security team uniform...?

*‘What... is this?’*

It felt like a ringing had started inside my head.

“Roe, you really are still such a kind person. It’s nice to see you trying to save that worker!”

“......”

“You know the person who spoke to Roe earlier? I think they’re actually a pretty decent person! Among the Workers here, there are plenty of people where letting them die would probably be better for the environment and society, but that person isn’t like that. They’re actually pretty decent, don’t you think, Roe?”

I thought back to the security team member who had followed me.

How they had kept me moving deeper inside, toward this specific area.

Could it be...

*‘Did she deliberately steer me here?’*

So that I would save someone.

Or...

*‘...Was she trying to see if I was still someone who would try to save others?’*

......

Something about it sent a chill down my spine.

But Assistant Manager Lee Seonghae only smiled with her usual bright, innocent expression.

And somehow, that made the sense of wrongness even stronger.

What... exactly was happening here?

“Why are you here?”

“Hmm... I can’t tell you that!”

“Then why... are you part of the security team?”

“I can’t tell you that either!”

......

......

“More importantly, what if you sent that Worker from earlier to the resort? You could just charge the rescue cost as labor, right?”

I started to speak, but no words came out. Slowly, I closed my mouth again.

Then I answered carefully.

“...I’d rather do that too, but under the current circumstances, it’s difficult.”

“Ah. That’s a shame.”

“Yes.”

I swallowed hard before continuing.

“And if possible, I’d like to send you there first, Dolphin. ...What do you think?”

“......”

“......”

<br>

“Ah, it’s time.”

Assistant Manager Lee Seonghae’s head turned.

“I need to go on patrol.”

Her face lit up with a smile as she waved.

“Take care. Keep going!”

She spoke as though she had simply run into an old coworker she used to be close with while walking down the street.

Then that familiar face disappeared beneath the headgear once more, leaving behind an unnamed, featureless security team member from the branch’s mass-produced security team.

And then she walked away down the hallway... and vanished.

I chased after her, but I was a step too late.

“......”

There was no one beyond the corner.

<br>

<br>

@c@* * *@c@

<br>

<br>

And so, that evening at dinner,

“You went around wearing the security team uniform.”

As Baek Saheon ladled seaweed soup into his bowl, he asked a question. This time, I had something definite to tell him.

“Did you find anything while exploring?”

“...Yes.”

“...! What was it?”

“Workers who are still alive.”

“......”

The interest that had briefly lit up Baek Saheon’s eye immediately faded.

“Why did you go out of your way to look for them?”

“I didn’t find them myself. Someone led me there.”

“What?”

I spoke quietly.

“Assistant Manager Dolphin. She’s here.”

“...What did you say?”

“I saw it with my own eyes. She was wearing a security team uniform.”

Something about her had felt off, but after thinking about it more, I wondered if it was simply the effects of contamination... After all, she was part of the security team.

Like Assistant Manager Jin Nasol, there was a high chance she had been targeted because of her connection to Director Ho’s project.

But thinking about it that way only made the strange sense of wrongness I felt weigh heavier on me.

“Of course, this might not seem like an important incident to you... but since she’s here—”

I was about to say that I wanted to figure out a way to get both the Workers and the surviving security members out.

But Baek Saheon cut me off.

“Don’t say something ridiculous.”

“Can you just listen for a moment? I’ll think of a way to minimize your risk while still finding a way to benefit us—“

“No, that’s not what I mean!”

Baek Saheon clenched his fist.

<br>

"How can someone who was processed as dead after going missing inside a Darkness possibly be here?”

<br>

......

......

“What?”

“Exactly as I said. Assistant Manager Dolphin was reported as missing inside a Darkness.”

“What do you mean...?”

“It’s already been more than a year. She’s been officially declared dead.”

“......”

I turned to Assistant Manager Jin Nasol.

She calmly nodded.

“That’s right. I think I heard she went missing back then.”

......

“Then maybe she only pretended to go missing and someone secretly got her out—”

“That’s impossible.”

Baek Saheon cut me off.

“I saw her disappear myself.”

“......”

“I remember exactly what kind of Darkness it was, too. It was one where you entered an old house and had to find somewhere to sleep. Then, all of a sudden, she started talking to herself, walked out of the house, and disappeared.”

My blood ran cold.

“Then... what I saw was...”

“I don’t know. What the hell did you see?”

“......”

“...The security team members at this branch only have numbers. There are no personal records for them. Actually, they never even take off their uniforms. Have you ever seen one of them remove it? No, right?”

That was true.

But Assistant Manager Lee Seonghae had removed her headgear without hesitation, looking strangely happy to see me, and spoke to me as if nothing was wrong....

My head began to spin.

Even Baek Saheon’s words sounded distant and warped.

“This branch was strange from the beginning. Do you think I wore my mask for no reason when I first got here?”

And there had to be a reason behind that strange feeling.

Without realizing it, I understood the words he hadn’t said.

“...Could it be that Darknesses occur randomly at this branch? Like it’s some kind of ghost story?”

“It’s not impossible. Just look at how they’ve been running this branch. How many containment failures have you already experienced here?”

Assistant Manager Jin Nasol frowned.

“What are you two even going on about? You can just check the CCTV footage.”

“...!”

“Can you access it?”

“Give me a moment.”

And after Baek Saheon viewed the footage from that area...

<br>

“...That’s almost too convenient.”

There was definitely someone wearing a security team uniform standing in the B-9 corridor, right in front of me.

But the camera had perfectly avoided capturing their head.

Almost as if they didn’t want anyone to see them remove their headgear.

“......”

“Doesn’t it look like they did that on purpose?”

It almost felt intentional.

*‘...Was she trying not to leave any records behind?’*

Or... had I really been deceived by a Darkness?

I didn’t know.

*‘Even if I gathered the entire security team together, there probably wouldn’t be any distinguishing features that would let me identify Assistant Manager Dolphin.’*

...That is, if they were all wearing their uniforms.

“Could we check the faces of every member on the security team?”

“What makes you think they’ll allow that? It’s against operating policy. Even if I haven’t received the proper handover yet, it was written down back when I was an auditor.”

<br>

—Security team members do not remove their uniforms at their own discretion until disposal.

<br>

“After everything you went through outside because of that sticker, why are you trying to test things again? Just chalk it up to something strange and leave it at that.”

“......”

“What did you even talk about with that security team member who looked like Assistant Manager Dolphin? Did she threaten you or something? Or did she show some kind of strange signs of contamination?”

“It wasn’t anything like that. We just exchanged greetings...”

A quiet voice escaped me.

“She just seemed like she wanted me to save one of the Workers in the storage room.”

“......”

“Someone who wasn’t contaminated.”

......

“Hah.”

Baek Saheon stood up.

“Don’t tell me you made this up just to get that Worker released?”

“Baek Saheon.”

“I knew something was off when you suddenly started going on and on. Why don't you just release every Worker and get yourself a nice big disciplinary action while you're at it?”

“That’s not it.”

“Then if you’re going to make things up, why not say you saw someone you’re closer to? What, Assistant Manager Eun Haje or something?”

“Hey.”

I gritted my teeth.

“Do I really look like the kind of person who'd lie about seeing an acquaintance in the security team when I don't even know whether they're alive or dead?"

“......”

“If it’s impossible, then just say it’s impossible. Why do you always have to say things like that? I’m just saying...”

I steadied my breathing.

“I’m just saying that if there’s a way, I’ll try to think of a solution that works out for everyone.”

“Why are you trying to do that now?! Don’t!”

“You’re the one who told me to come up with clever solutions before!”

“That’s completely different! That was about how we survive! This is...”

“Enough!”

<br>

*Bang!*

<br>

Assistant Manager Jin Nasol rose to her feet, looking as though she wanted to smack us with her tray.

“I've had enough of listening to this.... Hey. Instead of wasting time picking fights, eat your damn food and save your strength. Don't stand in front of me arguing over something that isn't going anywhere!”

“......”

Baek Saheon's face flushed red with anger, but instead of shouting back, he eventually dropped into his seat.

By then, I'd cooled off as well.

*Huu.*

“I'm sorry.”

“......”

“I know you're already helping me. I wasn't trying to put more of a burden on you.”

“......Yeah.”

Baek Saheon didn't apologize in return, but the edge had gone out of his voice.

“Just... stay put for a while, alright? It's not like you'll be here much longer....”

Just then—

<br>

*Bzzzzz!*

<br>

“...!”

Baek Saheon's phone vibrated.

The moment he glanced at the screen, his expression changed.

“...Well. That settles it.”

He turned his phone toward me.

“The approval came through. I've been granted access to the operations system.”

“...!”

“There’s no reason or excuse to wait any longer. We’ll proceed tomorrow... with the Worker disposal, that is.”

“......”

“Congratulations. By tomorrow, that sticker will be off, and you'll be free.”

Silence settled over the room.

And so, amid the unease, the lingering tension, and unexplained mysteries... the day finally arrived.

The day the Workers would be disposed of.
