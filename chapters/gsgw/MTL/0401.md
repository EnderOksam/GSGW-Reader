# Chapter 400

My neck reflected in the mirror.

My eyes were immediately drawn to the sticker. Its message had changed.

<br>

![Official Illustration translated by Beiruu](ch400_1.webp)

<br>

Capture requi—.

“...!”

My thoughts cut off. I couldn't breathe! Air...!

“Kim Soleum!”

“Hah—”

The pressure vanished.

I dragged air into my lungs, barely managing to speak between desperate breaths.

“I think... it comes in waves....”

It hit again.

“...!!”

“Wait!”

Was this... was I actually going to die?

*'After making it all the way here... I was really going to die because of this...?'*

More than fear, I felt a crushing sense of disbelief and frustration.

*‘No. Calm down....’*

What kind of function had they built into this sticker? Capture required...? Worker....

......

The worker sticker's message.

<br>

Assigned post abandoned.

<br>

...!!

“I have to go back... to the branch... Kh—”

“What the hell are you talking about...?”

Bang! The guesthouse door flew open, and someone burst into the room.

“Assistant Manager....”

“Don't talk. Keep your airway open.”

Cold sweat streamed down Assistant Manager Jin Nasol's face as she tilted her head back, one hand pressed against the sticker on her neck.

Her collarbone.

An angry rash had spread around her collarbone—horrific enough to make my skin crawl...!

Just like mine!

“If you panic... it'll only get worse. Turn your head... away from the sticker... and force sharp breaths... into your airway...!”

“Hk—!”

My throat tightened again. It was spreading—creeping over my entire neck—

“Hah—”

“...!!”

I felt like I was going to die, but I forced myself to follow Assistant Manager Jin Nasol’s words as best I could... exactly as she’d told me...!

*‘Turn my head the other way... breathe... sharply....’*

.........

Somehow, I managed to force a thin stream of air through.

*'B-Breathe.’*

Just hold on until it passed.

“Hah—”

“F*ck... this....”

“Baek Saheon.”

The moment I found even the smallest bit of relief, I grabbed his shoulder.

“We... have to get back... to the branch...!”

“......!”

“A taxi... I'll pay....”

Looking straight at Baek Saheon, I squeezed the words out.

“I’m counting on you.”

“......”

<br>

<br>

Fortunately, Baek Saheon didn't make the perfectly Daydream-like, ruthlessly practical decision to abandon both of us, take only the Dream Solution, and leave.

Instead, he called a taxi, shoved the two of us inside, and told the driver to head for Yongmeori Coast.

“Uh... shouldn’t you be going to the emergency room?”

“Just drive!”

The driver, clearly overwhelmed by the situation, silently did as he was told.

I could hear Baek Saheon, crammed into the middle seat in the back, muttering curses under his breath. I leaned against the window, focusing on nothing but taking proper breaths.

Hah... hah... haaah....

Then, as we drew close enough for the silhouette of Yongmeori Coast to come into view even in the darkness—

“Hah!”

The pressure around my throat finally disappeared.

I reached up and touched my neck. The suffocating force from the sticker was gone....

When I looked to the side, I met Assistant Manager Jin Nasol’s gaze. The colour had returned to her face as well.

...!

“Hey. Stop the car.”

“W-wait, ma’am?”

“Driver. We’ll get out here!”

<br>

*Tap.*

<br>

The three of us stepped out of the taxi.

Assistant Manager Jin Nasol and I had both reached the same quick conclusion: we couldn’t risk leaving the area.

“......”

“......”

We caught our breath.

Standing by the roadside along the coast, where the branch’s escape route had been, I looked at my neck reflected in a convex mirror....

<br>

![Official Illustration translated by Beiruu](ch400_2.webp)

<br>

It had gone back to normal.

*‘I’m alive.’*

A primal sense of relief washed over me.

But what followed was a more rational kind of fear.

This.

“It seems like... the branch is trying to stop us from moving beyond a certain range.”

“......”

If it had been on another part of the body, the initial effect probably would have been nothing more than unbearable pain.

Then, as the rash gradually spread, the entire body would become incapacitated, leaving the person only able to writhe in agony.

An efficient way to render someone completely helpless on the spot, preventing them from doing anything else.

But.

“Of all places... my neck.”

Because it was on my neck, the entire process had been skipped. I had nearly died right from the start.
Ha...

Judging by the word ‘Capture’ written there, if the branch had been operating normally, an alarm would have gone off the moment I left, and they would already be after me....

I shouldn’t have underestimated it just because it was a sticker.

I had assumed they wouldn’t use an item on hundreds, maybe thousands, of temporary workers. It wouldn’t have been worth the cost.

But Namhae Branch... Somehow, they had made the cost worth it.

*‘Damn it.’*

What was I supposed to do?

Even if I reported this to the Management Bureau, it would be meaningless if I couldn’t leave the branch...!

This wasn’t even a Darkness. Expecting them to immediately provide a precise solution perfectly suited to such a complicated situation was nothing more than wishful thinking.

I didn’t even know whether this was an item or a curse. Was I supposed to ask Lee Gyeol to send rescue workers, dedicated exorcism personnel, and every other kind of manpower available just to help two temporary Daydream workers? It was ridiculous no matter how I phrased it!

Damn it. Damn it!

I anxiously rubbed the sticker with my hand, then suddenly thought of a very straightforward solution.

“...Baek Saheon. How long will it take until we can convert the Dream Solution into points?”

“......Why?”

“I’m going to buy a regeneration potion with those points.”

“...!”

Judging by his expression, he understood immediately.

“Exactly. I think we can just cut out the sticker itself.”

“Are you insane? That’s where the carotid artery is. You don’t even have any alcohol to disinfect it right now, so don’t spout nonsense.”

“There’s no other option.”

I said calmly,

“The Namhae Branch may be empty right now, but the record of a worker leaving their assigned post without authorization will still remain.”

“...!”

“And once things settle down, Daydream Headquarters will look into what happened.”

So if they found a surviving worker...

“Even if we wait here until then, don’t you think Daydream would choose the method that leaves no loose ends?”

Meaning they would kill me.

“......”

“So cutting it out probably gives us a better chance of surviving.”

“Your reasoning makes sense. If that’s the plan, then go ahead.”

Assistant Manager Jin Nasol, the other person whose neck was at stake, accepted it without much reaction.

But Baek Saheon stayed silent for a moment before speaking again.

“To receive points, I have to provide the source of the Dream Solution. Which means, at minimum, my inspection report has to be submitted. That’ll take a week.”

“......”

“Even if we skip all that and use my points to buy one right now, what are you going to do if the branch system catches that purchase before I can get a regeneration potion through headquarters?!”

“Right. It’s going to come down to a race.”

“A race? What race? It doesn’t matter how fast either side moves—it all depends on how the company handles it. It’s luck! Just pure luck!”

Baek Saheon grabbed me by the collar.

“Anyone could think of something like that.”

“...!”

“I’m telling you to come up with something that’ll actually work. You’re good at that, aren’t you? You’re always talking about how there’s a solution that works for everyone, coming up with all those weird ideas...!”

I stared at Baek Saheon, feeling as though I'd just been punched.

I never expected to hear those words from him.

In an instant, my head cleared.

*‘Right.’*

I'd settled for the first solution that seemed remotely reasonable.

*‘So much for telling myself to stay calm.’*

After being driven into a corner over and over again today—both by my own body and by the situation around me—I’d simply grabbed at the first obvious option I could see.

*‘Then... what else?’*

If I stopped limiting myself to what seemed realistic, and considered every option and every resource available to me...

......!

“I’ve got one.”

“...!”

“But you might not like it.”

“What is it?”

I took a slow breath.

“Let me add a few lines to your inspection report.”

“...?!”

“Of course,”

I looked at Baek Saheon, who still had a fistful of my collar.

“I’ll write it in a way that benefits you too.”

“......”

“......”

“Now that sounds more like you.”

He finally let go of me.

Dropping his hand, he jerked his chin toward me.

“So? What exactly do you want to add to the report?”

"Ha..."

I let out a long sigh of relief and pressed both hands over my eyes.

"Thank goodness."

“Thank goodness? After a report like this, of course they’d grant it.”

Baek Saheon let out a scoff as he locked his phone.

“As expected. Making them fight among themselves really is the fastest and easiest way.”

"......"

He had a point.

There were exactly two things I asked Baek Saheon to add to his audit report.

<br>

The first was to submit a shortened account of the incident as quickly as possible.

<br>

The idea was to get ahead of the branch before they had a chance to present their own version of events.

They were probably already preparing a carefully polished report of their own, one filled with excuses and justifications. But before they could even present it, we had already handed Headquarters a justification to act.

<br>

*‘You really can’t trust those bastards at Namhae Branch, can you? Why don’t we take this opportunity to replace the whole lot of them?’*

<br>

...That was the kind of thing any executive competing with the current Namhae Branch manager could say internally without it seeming out of place.

There was nothing particularly unnatural about it.

*‘I mean, anyone looking at the situation would be horrified.’*

It was an easy conclusion to draw. Baek Saheon himself had nearly died, and after witnessing the situation firsthand, it was only natural to assume he had included it in the report out of alarm.

It had enough justification behind it that the branch wouldn’t be able to see it as some kind of retaliation or claim they had been wronged.

After all, they’d basically screwed themselves over. It was just the natural result of their own mistakes.

Well, anyway. At that point, Headquarters would naturally end up summoning Namhae Branch’s manager.

*‘They’ll be too busy fighting over internal politics to properly deal with the aftermath.’*

The typical, and rather sad, reality of a company.

Which led to the next thing I asked for....

<br>

The second was to have the auditor remain at the abandoned Namhae Branch for the time being.

<br>

That made the whole arrangement seem perfectly natural.

Of course, none of it would have worked without Baek Saheon's cooperation.

I thanked him sincerely.

“Are you sure you'll be okay with this? You'd earn more points staying at Headquarters.”

“What are you talking about? I've earned more points in the four days since coming down here than I did during the entire first quarter of this year.”

“......”

“As long as we don't get another containment failure on that scale, the expected returns are still better here.”

...But without another containment failure, would there even be any high-grade Dream Solution to collect...?

“Wasn't that why you told me to stay here in the first place?”

That wasn't what I meant... I meant staying behind would make Headquarters see you as someone willing to take responsibility.

But I caught myself and quickly changed my answer.

“Well... you'll have full authority here for the next few days. So I suppose you'll be able to choose whichever Darkness you want, explore safely, and collect Dream Solution without too much trouble.”
That was only meant to be an added bonus...

“Exactly.”

...Figures.

Baek Saheon let out a smug little scoff.

“Well, don't forget that you owe me another one.”

That much was true.

With this arrangement, Baek Saheon would even gain the authority to dispose of Workers.

Once the scheduled date arrived, he would be able to safely remove both my sticker and Assistant Manager Jin Nasol’s.

All he’d have to do was report us as disposed of, then secretly smuggle us out.

So I nodded without hesitation. 

“Of course.”

“...Good. Then catch your breath while I get a grasp of how this branch operates.”

For some reason, Baek Saheon headed into the personnel-only area looking strangely motivated.

“Don’t follow me.”

I wasn’t planning to...

“There are still a few working CCTV cameras in there, so just wait.”

My head was still spinning.

Even so, the relief was unmistakable.

“Phew...”

*‘Now then... where did Assistant Manager disappear to...?’*


For some reason, a very likely thought crossed my mind—that she was probably already relaxing in a massage chair with a Happy Maker beside her.

*‘Huu.’*

Either way, I never thought I’d find myself back here so soon.

*‘Looks like I’ll be staying here a little longer.’*

I walked down the corridor in the Workers' Zone, taking in my surroundings.

*‘...It’s quiet.’*

Being able to wander through this place so freely felt almost unnatural.

The traces left behind by the parade and the contamination had already been cleaned up by the branch security team and the Headquarters personnel who arrived later. But somehow, I couldn’t look at the Workers’ Zone the same way anymore.

Because I had experienced firsthand, far too many times, just how many people had died here.

“......”

The silence felt suffocating.

The few Workers who had escaped to the Off-duty Zone and ultimately survived had been placed in isolation.

When they were scheduled for disposal at the end of the month... would it be possible to get these people out too?

They were probably already heavily contaminated.

*‘...I don’t know.’*

Once again, I was reminded that this was a world where even keeping myself alive was a challenge.
If not for a series of absurd coincidences, I never would have made it out alive.

*‘Of all things... an Intermission.’*

It was ridiculously good timing.

“......”

And yet...

A thought had been faintly lingering in my mind... What if it wasn’t luck?

I looked through the notes Baek Saheon had referenced while writing his report—records made while documenting the facility after the Parade ended.

Since the anomaly had already ended by then, most of the notes were little more than scattered traces and hints.

But among them, one entry in particular caught my attention...

Why had something like that been there?

Of course, it was possible that it had been something worn by the Purgatory Choir.

But given their worn, tattered appearance and the grime covering their bodies... would something they wore really have been described as a ‘white cotton glove’?

Perhaps it belonged to another Darkness—one that always maintained an elegant and refined appearance.

In other words... the distinguished figure who had suddenly been introduced during the Intermission.

No. Enough.

It’s not like I’m going to find an answer anyway.

*‘I should just deal with the hole I had dug in the dirt wall of Room Z999.’*

Now was the perfect opportunity, with the CCTV still offline.

Maybe the cleanup personnel had already noticed it. But since nobody had been in that room, there was likely no significant damage or reason to investigate. And given how filthy the place was, they probably just glanced over it and moved on.

*‘As expected. They didn’t notice it.’*

Strangely, it had a certain weight to it.
