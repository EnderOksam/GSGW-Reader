# Chapter 387

An ordinary voice echoed through the corridor, where white hoses sprawled eerily across the floor.

The source was the fermentation jar in my arms.

“So, where are we headed?”

“…The Off-Duty Zone.”

*Slosh.*

“And the situation’s dangerous, so we’re trying to stay as quiet as possible.”

“Oh dear, how thoughtless of me. I’ll keep my mouth shut!”

But for the sake of my own sanity, it might have been better if he had kept talking.

Otherwise, my thoughts kept drifting back to what I was carrying.

The thing inside the jar…

*‘Stop thinking about it.’*

I gritted my teeth and pressed on.

My condition was already poor, and carrying a fermentation jar that weighed more than a person made it feel like my shoulders were about to give out, but I was too on edge to care.

All that mattered was reaching the Off-Duty Zone.

I focused on that alone. And then…

*’…We’re here.’*

I watched anxiously as Assistant Manager Jin Nasol pulled the pass from her pocket.

*‘Surely being here at dawn won’t stop it from working…’*

……

……

<br>

Biiip.

—Pass verified.

—Opening Off-Duty Zone. Best of luck this week! Hope you get rich!

<br>

I hurried inside, my eyes immediately dropping to the floor.

*‘Nothing.’*

No hoses. Good. Now all we had to do was find the door…

<br>

**Squelch.**

<br>

“……”

I looked back.

Beyond the open quarantine door to the Off-Duty Zone, a mass of white hoses writhed.

And then—

<br>

**Fwikk!**

<br>

It shot toward us.

“…!”

It was like watching a river split and spread across parched earth from a drone’s perspective. The hoses branched off, surging into the newly opened zone.

And they were heading straight for us… Damn it!

“You—that!”

Assistant Manager Jin Nasol was pointing at me—No, at the jar I was carrying.

<br>

**Squelch.**

<br>

It was after this, too.

“Throw it!”

“…Wait!”

At the same time, I whipped my head around and looked at the store right beside the door.

<br>

[Namhae Tavern]

<br>

A bottle of liqueur caught my eye.

I snatched it up without hesitation. The weight of the fermentation jar shifted onto one arm, making my shoulder scream in protest, but I gritted my teeth and pushed through the pain…

Then I hurled the bottle.

Thunk!

It slammed into one of the hoses and ricocheted away, the neck shattering on impact. Shards of glass flew through the air.

The hose writhed. Then, from within itself, it extruded a new white cord, plunging it into the thick liquid spilling from the shattered bottle…

*‘What the hell is that…?’*

<br>

*Biiip.*

<br>

Thankfully, the door began to close.

Keeping myself ready to bolt at a moment’s notice, I watched through the narrowing gap.

…The white cord lodged inside the bottle had gone still.

*‘It was drawn to the liqueur… but it isn’t swallowing it…?’*

It only consumed the liquefied remains of people…?

*‘What the hell…’*

In horror, I watched.

<br>

From the thick main hose, another white cord extended outward, creeping toward the narrow gap beneath the door…

*’……!!’*

The instant it touched—

<br>

*Thud.*

<br>

The quarantine door slammed shut.

But there was no time to relax.

“It’s wedged in the gap beneath the door.” 

I turned to Assistant Manager Jin Nasol.

“It’s definitely going to force its way through.”

“Move. Now.”

Without a second’s hesitation, I took off.

The first place we headed was the entrance inside the Religious Activity Room.

<br>

<p align="center">AUTHORIZED PERSONNEL ONLY<p align="left">

<br>

But—

“It won’t open.”

The handle wouldn’t budge, as though it had been welded in place.

Rattle. Rattle. Crack! Even though the jar blocked my view of Assistant Manager Jin Nasol’s hands, I could easily imagine the veins standing out as she strained with enough force to nearly break the handle. But it still wouldn’t give.

I set the fermentation jar down and tried myself, but the result was the same.

There wasn’t even a keyhole, let alone some kind of authentication device.

How were the authorized personnel supposed to get in?

“If brute force is the plan, we’d need your old Manager Lee here.”

“……”

Meaning physical force wasn’t an option. Human strength alone wouldn’t be enough. Damn it!

What do I do? I’ve got nothing on me right now—no supernatural gimmicks, no tricks, nothing I can use!

*‘Then…’*

Ah.

I remembered the door handle part sitting in my pocket.

…What if I found a door that was missing a handle—one where I could attach it myself?

“Oh dear… oh dear…”

“…!”

Right then, the voice came from inside the jar I was carrying again.

I took a deep breath before speaking to him.

“Excuse me, could I ask you for a favor?”

“Uh, what was it I was supposed to do again, young man?”

“Do you remember any doors in the Off-Duty Zone marked ‘Authorised Personnel Only’?”

“Uh… yeah! There was one in the Religious Activity Room! Are we there now?”

“Yes. We’re there now. Do you remember any others? Especially ones that didn’t have a doorknob?”

“Uh……”

And somehow—

“I dunno. Uh, uh… the lottery store? Was it next to the lottery store? Yeah, I think there was one there. Yeah.”

The words coming from the ‘worker inside the jar’ were becoming increasingly slurred…

“…Yes.”

I swallowed hard and lifted the fermentation jar, leaving the Religious Activity Room.

…My shoulders felt like they were about to come apart.

<br>

“Disgusting. Seriously.”

Back in the main hallway of the Off-Duty Zone—

From the gap beneath the quarantine door at the end of the corridor, white strands were slowly slipping out.

The way they crawled out in thick clumps reminded me of those parasite horror stories about horsehair worms and the like.

“……”

*‘The quarantine door… it's not going to hold for much longer.’*
Anxiety tightened around my throat.

“The lottery store is that way!”

“Got it.”

We moved even faster.

Even while running beside me, Assistant Manager Jin Nasol suddenly darted into the butcher shop as we passed it, grabbing whatever sharp tools she could find—kitchen knives, scissors, and anything else with a blade.

*‘Ah.’*

I quickly searched through the shop as well and found a roll of yellow tape. I used it to make a makeshift shoulder strap, like the kind used to carry watermelons, spreading out the weight so I could hang the fermentation jar in front of me.

*‘This should free up my hands a little.’*

Earlier, when I had tried holding it with one arm while grabbing the liqueur bottle, I’d nearly dislocated my shoulder.

But Assistant Manager Jin Nasol narrowed her eyes as she looked at me, seeing that both my hands were still effectively occupied by its weight.

“Think again about whether this is actually worth the cost to you.”

“……”

I understood perfectly what she meant—that my chances of survival would be higher if I simply abandoned it and took something else instead—but…

*‘…We don’t know how the situation might change.’*

As long as I could still manage it, I would keep carrying it.

I pretended not to know and simply nodded before moving forward again.

But then we ran into a problem.

“Hey. None of them are missing.”

…

No matter how thoroughly we searched, every door marked ‘Authorised Personnel Only’ still had its handle attached.

*‘Damn.’*

I kept glancing back at the quarantine door, trying to judge how much time we had left.

By now, the area around it was almost completely covered in white, as if someone had painted over it. Countless white tendrils stretched out like writhing tentacles, slowly swaying.

*‘Damn it!’*

“Excuse me, sir! Is there anywhere else?”

“I don’t know… Uh, but I can’t see very well. Why can’t I see anything? Ah, right… I’m inside the jar. That’s why I can’t see. That must be it.”

“……”

“Young man, can you see me?”

I was losing my mind.

“Hey. Are you sure this is the right call? Even now, we could change course and look for a way to open the door, or find a vent big enough for someone to get through.”

“Just… wait a moment.”

“I’ve put up with you carrying that thing around and listening to its bullshit long enough.”

“……”

Jin Nasol spat the words out irritably, then grabbed the handle of another ‘Authorised Personnel Only’ door without even looking back at me. She already knew it wouldn’t open, but it seemed like she was yanking at it anyway just to vent her frustration.

But…

“Assistant Manager.”

“Stop talking and choose.”

“No, it’s not that.”

I swallowed hard.

“Look at your hand.”

“……”

Assistant Manager Jin Nasol lowered her gaze to the hand wrapped around the doorknob.

To the fingers covered by two layers of black latex gloves taken from the medical room.

<br>

One of them dangled uselessly, failing to grip the handle like the others.

<br>

“……”

Assistant Manager Jin Nasol removed her hand from the doorknob.

Then she raised her left hand and pressed against the ring finger of her right glove.

<br>

*Squish…*

<br>

The harder she pressed, the more the finger collapsed inward.

“……”

“……”

<br>

She was infected.

<br>

“Wait…”

My blood ran cold.

*‘Since when…?’*

How did she get infected?

Her hand had been covered up, and she’d taken the same precautions as me. So why was it only her?

Wait… what if I just hadn’t noticed it either?

*‘…!!’*

I immediately pressed down on my own hands and feet. They were fine, but I felt no relief. Because right now, Jin Nasol was…

<br>

**Squelch.**

<br>

“……”

“Was that sound… from the quarantine door?”

For once, Assistant Manager Jin Nasol didn’t respond with her usual answer along the lines of ‘if you already know, don’t ask.’

Instead—

“Hey.”

I looked up.

A butcher’s knife was being held out toward me.

My hand instinctively closed around the handle, and at the same moment, Jin Nasol pressed her gloved right hand against the wall.

“Only the ring finger… cut it off.”

“Wait—”

“Don’t waste time saying ‘wait.’ Do it now. Why are you acting so stupid?”

“You should at least use painkillers first!”

I might pass out from shock!

<br>

**Squelch.**

<br>

“Can’t you see what’s happening? Can’t you hear it? Do it!”

Shit.

“Do it!”

In the end, I brought the butcher’s knife down, dragging it across.

<br>

*Thunk.*

*Thunk, thunk!*

<br>

With those short, heavy sounds, Assistant Manager Jin Nasol’s limp ring finger fell away, still inside the glove.

“…!!”

Jin Nasol clutched her hand, her body trembling as she gritted her teeth.

I steadied her shaking body and, with the remaining glove, somehow managed to do something resembling first aid to stop the bleeding.

*‘Hurry. Hurry!’*

And then I saw it.

Jin Nasol’s finger lying on the floor.

At first glance, it looked completely normal. But…

<br>

**Squelch.**

<br>

A moment later, it began to sag and melt away.

Thick, viscous liquid began seeping from the severed finger, until even the skin split apart and the whole thing dissolved into the ooze…

Slosh. A yellowish muscus tinged with red.

Just like the contents of the fermentation jar I was carrying against my chest…

“Young man.”

“…!”

I looked down at the sealed jar.

A human voice came from inside.

“What was that sound just now?”

“…Ah. I dropped something by accident.”

*Slosh. Slosh.*

“It was nothing. More importantly—”

“No, it wasn’t. She… she told you to cut off her finger, didn’t she? What… What was that sound? Why…”

<br>

“…You could hear it from in there?”

<br>

“……”

“……”

“Open the lid.”

“Wait.”

“Open it! Open it! I want to see! Something’s wrong with me!Somethingswroooong!Somethingswrooong!”

The jar began to shake. The liquid sloshed violently inside, and the thick slime-like-mucus struck the sides with wet, heavy impacts that reverberated through my arms.

Revulsion, horror, and sheer terror overwhelmed me.

*Slosh! Slosh!*

**“AAAAAH!AAH!AAAAAAH!”**

I froze.

**“AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAH?”**

“Shut up! Shut up!”

“Assistant Manager!”

**“AAAAAAAUUGHUUGHAAAARGH.”**

The voice inside the jar became more and more garbled until it was no longer recognizable as belonging to a human.

Wet sloshing. Liquid splashing. Something striking the inside of the jar.

And then…

The sounds stopped completely.

“……”

I thought I was going to die from fear.

More than the nausea or grief, what overwhelmed me most was the crushing realization that I was holding it against my body.

“Finally. Quiet.”

“……”

“Hey.”

I looked over to see Jin Nasol glaring at me irritably, cold sweat running down her face as she chewed on a painkiller.

But the look in her eyes…

It was almost one of resignation.

“It’s too late.”

<br>

**Creak. Creak. Creak…**

<br>

I turned around.

From the quarantine door came a series of sharp cracking sounds…

It shook under the strain.

And then—

<br>

**Bang.**

<br>

The quarantine door gave way.

From the widening gap, countless white cords spilled through, writhing as they reached toward us.

“…!!”

“The vent in the ceiling isn’t even big enough for one person to crawl through. Planning to hide inside one of the shops with the door shut? You’ll just die a little later.”

<br>

**Bang.**

<br>

“Maybe if you scoop some of that stuff out of the jar you brought and scatter it around as bait, you’ll buy yourself a little more time. So, are you going to shut up and do at least that?”

“Run! Now!”

Damn it! She’s just about given up—and honestly, I couldn’t blame her!

This situation looked completely hopeless.

The sharper your mind was, the more experienced you were with ghost stories… the easier it probably became to think that dying now would be a mercy.

*‘In a situation like this, how the hell are we supposed to search the whole place for an ‘Authorized Personnel Only’ door without a handle?!’*

We hadn’t found one up to now!

<br>

**Squelch.**

<br>

I shoved Jin Nasol ahead of me as we ran deeper inside, biting down on the inside of my cheek.

Behind us came the sound of the hoses slurping up what had once been Assistant Manager Jin Nasol’s finger.

Think. Think…!

The silver rosary had led us here because this was supposed to be the right answer!

*‘A door without a handle…’*

What if it was concealed in the wall so it didn’t even look like a door? Then there’d be no reason to put up an ‘Authorised Personnel Only’ sign. The workers couldn’t get in anyway!

*‘Right… if the workers can’t get inside in the first place…’*

…

There’d be no reason to hang a sign.

<br>

>—Oh, right. There's a bathhouse here too. But apparently it's been broken for ages or something—they've never actually opened it.

<br>

If the facility was already out of order…

…!!

“Assistant Manager! I found it! The escape route!”

“…!”

“This way!”

The bathhouse…

*‘I definitely passed something that looked like a sign for it earlier!’*

I gritted my teeth and ran.

For a moment, a spark returned to Assistant Manager Jin Nasol’s eyes.

“Where exactly?”

“The bathhouse!”

“Then why are you going that way?! This way is faster, idiot!”

Jin Nasol grabbed me and pulled me sharply to the side.

After weaving through several cramped, winding corridors, a sign finally came into view at the far end of the area.

<br>

<p align="center">Namhae Hot Spring<p align="left">

<br>

“Keep wasting time and I’ll kill you myself!”

As if I wasn’t already going to die horribly anyway!

*‘Please! Please!’*

I hurriedly ran toward the door. It slowly came into view…

<br>

<p align="center">OUT OF ORDER<p align="left">

<br>

An A4 sheet with those words printed on it was stuck to the door, which had been crudely sealed off with yellow tape.

And there…

<br>

There was no handle.

<br>

“…!”

Found it.

A rush of adrenaline surged through me, and without hesitation, I pulled the doorknob piece from my pocket and pressed it against the door.

<br>

*…Click.*

<br>

It fit perfectly.

As if it had been waiting for this moment.

“It’s done—”

“Move!”

I threw myself to the side on instinct.

A white cord shot out from the ground where I had been standing just moments before…!

This is crazy.

“It’s insanely fast!”

Tell me about it!

*‘If that thing gets inside the ‘Authorized Personnel Only’ area, it’s over.’*

Cold sweat dripped down my face.

Meanwhile, Jin Nasol had moved around to the other side, reaching for the doorknob.

“Go in first!”

Even as I shouted it, the situation felt hopeless.

*‘Do I have to… do I have to fight those things off as I go?’*

Could I even do that?

No, actually… parts of my body might already have been melted into something easier to consume. If it connected with me, wouldn’t it just suck out what’s inside me?

Just as I was desperately weighing my options…

<br>

“Leave me behind.”

<br>

“…what?”

I hurriedly leaned down toward the sealed jar.

But as if to prove I had been mistaken, no other words came from inside.

Instead…

<br>

**Crack.**

<br>

The bottom of the jar split.

“…!!”

From a corner of the broken base, a golden, reddish, sticky liquid seeped out, spilling onto the floor.

The writhing hoses lunged toward it as if they had been waiting, splitting into strands that reached out like a straw about to drink.

<br>

“Hey!”

I slipped out of the strap holding the jar and stumbled backward, barely keeping my balance as I ran.

*‘…I’m sorry!’*

<br>

**Squelch.**

<br>

“Now!”

I heard the hoses forcing their way into the broken jar, the sound of them devouring what remained inside. I gritted my teeth and forced my now lighter body forward…

And jumped through the door Jin Nasol was holding open.

<br>

*Bang.*

<br>

“……Hah.”

The door closed, and Jin Nasol and I disappeared beyond it.

<br>

Into the hidden personnel-only area.
