# Chapter 381

Deep in the mountains at night, where a courage test was taking place.

An old-fashioned telephone was attached to the wall of the lodge.

“A phone…!”

“Don’t grab it!”

It was a case I hadn't seen specifically in the Exploration Records, but just because it wasn't written didn't mean there wasn't a chance it existed.

“Hold on.”

It was a classic, ordinary telephone dial with about six speed-dial buttons above the number pad.

‘Turning around and running away is a very proactive and conspicuous action.’

It wasn't a suitable behavior for every ghost story. It was the kind of instruction that would only be given when absolutely necessary.
