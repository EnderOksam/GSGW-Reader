# Chapter 403

I blinked my heavy eyes.

As my consciousness slowly returned, I began to piece together my situation, my mind feeling as though it were being weighed down.

*‘So...’*

I’m being transported.

<br>

*Vrrrrrrrr—*

<br>

The sound of the mine cart moving reached my ears.

It was the same vibration I felt every time I was sent into a Darkness—whenever I was taken out to work. It seemed like it used the same rails.

But the vehicle itself was different.
The enclosed space, surrounded by iron bars, felt more like a prisoner transport. 

Movement was restricted, and the Workers—including me—were being ‘carried’ away with blindfolds over our eyes and restraints around our bodies.

Even though I couldn’t see, I already knew.
I had been briefed about it last night.

<br>

#f#—Tomorrow at dawn, you’ll be given a temporary dose of sedatives and muscle relaxants before being transported. ...I’ll make sure you regain consciousness slightly during the trip, so don’t worry too much.#f#

<br>

Just as Baek Saheon had said, the dosage of the medication had been adjusted beforehand. Slowly, the haze faded, and my awareness returned.

...The atmosphere had become a little awkward after our conversation yesterday, but that didn’t mean he was going to leave me to die.

I could also hear Assistant Manager Jin Nasol moving beside me, almost as if she was testing her restraints. It seemed she had woken up around the same time.

But I didn’t try to sense anything beyond that.

...Because I already knew the state of the Workers being transported with us.

Even now, it felt like those voices were still echoing in my ears—the ones that sounded as though they were trying to imitate musical instruments.

*‘Huu.’*

Trying not to tense up from the unease creeping through my body, I waited.

<br>

*Vrrrrrrrr—*

<br>

Unlike usual, my body suddenly felt like it was being pulled backward.

*‘...Are we going uphill?’*

I had never experienced anything like this while riding the mine cart before. It seemed we weren’t heading toward the containment rooms, but somewhere completely unfamiliar.

<br>

#f#—There’s almost nothing I can do during the disposal process. Everything is automated. Once the Workers are loaded onto the mine cart, it proceeds on its own.#f#

<br>

Only then did I truly understand what Baek Saheon meant by, ‘It proceeds on its own.’

I was just wondering how much time had passed, when the mine cart came to an abrupt halt.

<br>

*Screeeech.*

<br>

The entire space tilted, and my body immediately rolled uncontrollably to one side.

“...!”

Thud. My body hit the floor.

And then…

<br>

*Whiiiiir—*

<br>

...the floor started moving.

“......”

With my head pressed against the surface beneath me, I turned it slightly, trying to look at my surroundings through the small gap beneath my loosened blindfold.

What I was lying on was… like a conveyor belt.

“...!!”

I quickly turned my head to look behind me.

Farther away, I could see one side of another mine cart open up. The ‘Workers’ inside were poured out onto the conveyor belt.

They were… being handled like products on a factory assembly line.

*‘Crazy.’*

So this was what automation meant?

<br>

*Whiiiiiiiiir—*

<br>

The sound of machinery filled the area.

The Workers were bound and moved along a black track, like livestock being transported toward slaughter.

I hurriedly turned my head in the opposite direction.

Toward the area we were heading.

Above something that looked like the entrance to an automatic car wash at a gas station, a red light blinked.

Then words appeared above it.

<br>

@c@$EbSCAN / CONFIRMEb$@c@

<br>

*Beep.*

The central red light turned green, and one by one, the restrained Workers were swallowed beyond it and carried inside.

<br>

#f#—The number of people entering the processing area is recorded. So once you’re inside, untie the ropes and escape.#f#

<br>

It was like an automated slaughterhouse.

*‘...So that was it.’*

The realization hit me.

*‘...They never intended to let us out, even after 30 days.’*

No. We couldn’t even complete the full 100 days of employment.

Because every month, they killed off and replaced the Workers!

The reason they promised such generous pay was because they never intended for us to actually leave with it in the first place.

*‘Are these people seriously insane?’*

Even in a death game, if you survived and won, they gave you the money, you bastards....

If someone completed the terms of a work contract, wasn’t it basic decency to pay them? I never imagined they would make sure no one could leave alive.

No, wouldn’t that be a waste of money too?

And if you thought about it that way, finding new people every month was also a cost!

“Ha.”

...Of course, there was no need to think about any of this until after I escaped. I swallowed hard and lay still on the conveyor belt, trying not to move.

It seemed Assistant Manager Jin Nasol was in a similar position beside me.

<br>

*Whiiiiir—*

<br>

Quietly, I passed through the entrance.

I felt something like a laser sweep over the sticker on my neck....

<br>

@c@$EbSCAN / CONFIRMEb$@c@

<br>

...I made it through safely.

The moment I was on the other side, I immediately began undoing the restraints around my hands. They had been tied loosely and carelessly, so they came undone quickly. Next, the restraints around my feet—

<br>

**Bang!**

<br>

“......”

The conveyor belt shook. Right now…

<br>

**Bang!**

<br>

Up ahead, there was something.

<br>

**Bang!**

<br>

The sound of something being crushed grew closer.

The conveyor belt was carrying me toward it!

<br>

#*Bang! Bang!*#

<br>

I twisted my body, trying to get free somehow… No. This would be faster! Cold sweat streamed down my face as I hurriedly untied the restraints around my feet. Once I got the rope off and loosened the knot...

That was it.

“Get down!”

Without a second's hesitation, I leapt off the conveyor belt, escaping the current carrying me toward death.

<br>

**Bang!**

<br>

As I hit the ground beside the line, I tore off my blindfold. The first thing I saw was Assistant Manager Jin Nasol, who had caught me rather unceremoniously.

Then, almost instinctively, I turned toward the source of the sound.

“...!!”

<br>

**Bang!**

<br>

@c@$EbSTANDARDISATIONEb$@c@

<br>

The pounding noise that had been drawing closer was the sound of the Workers being... ‘fastened in place’

*‘Crazy.’*

It was like watching someone staple ants to the ground.

I stared at the endless line of Workers being carried deeper into the facility, their clothes—and sometimes even their flesh—pinned in place with heavy fastening spikes. Then they were lowered into boxes, sealed shut, and carried farther inside.

Cold sweat ran down my back.

*‘Huu.’*

Only then did I finally have a moment to look around.

*‘So this is the Worker processing facility.’*

I slowly surveyed my surroundings. The dimly lit place was unmistakably a factory...

*‘...Why does it feel like I’m inside a toy workshop?’*

Everything—the conveyor belts, the lights, the boxes, even the bolts—looked exaggerated, rounded, and oddly stylized.

It was almost like an abandoned factory-themed amusement park built for children.

“......”

I had a bad feeling about this.

*‘And... it’s bigger than I expected.’*

I turned my head.

The conveyor belt carrying the secured Workers had already moved into the next section of the facility, taking them on to the next stage of processing....

“Hey. We need to find the door.”

“...Right.”

<br>

#f#—There’s an entrance for authorized personnel, but it only opens once the entire process is finished. Find it and wait there for me.#f#

<br>

If that was the case, then the door was probably somewhere near the end of the processing line.

“I can't see it from here. Let's head farther inside!”

We passed the section where the Workers were pinned down and boxed, then climbed back onto the conveyor belt to move on to the next area.

Naturally, those already sealed inside boxes were all around us.

*‘That person...’*

<br>

#f#—P-please... save me....#f#

<br>

My eyes searched instinctively.

Since we couldn't leave until the entire process was over anyway, surely it wouldn't hurt to at least find them.

*‘Besides, we'd already got off the conveyor belt, and the facility hadn't reacted at all.’*

I stepped over the Workers sealed inside transparent, plastic-like boxes, checking their faces one by one.

“What are you doing?”

“Just a second.”

It wasn't long before I stopped.

*‘Found them.’*

A Worker who still looked human.

I lowered my head, wondering if I could wake them...

......

<br>

“They're not breathing.”

<br>

The Worker was dead.

“......”

Whether it had been suicide or whether someone had killed them… I couldn't tell.

Inside the transparent box, their face had turned a bluish color, as though they had died from suffocation. But that was the only clue.

“Hey. Get off.”

“...Right.”

I answered immediately, but one question continued to linger in my mind.

If someone had killed them...

*‘...Surely not.’*

I shouldn't jump to conclusions.

I quickly pushed the face that had flashed through my mind away and stepped off the conveyor belt, leaving the dead behind.

Then I looked around the next section.

“...There seem to be more lights here than before.”

“Yeah.”

It felt... more like a place that was actually operating.

Beyond the curve ahead, another stage of the process was already underway.

<br>

@c@$EbSTICKER REMOVALEb$@c@

<br>

*Hissssss—!*

A device above the conveyor belt fired a laser, which seemed to heat the sticker inside the plastic box. Then, something resembling a giant syringe was pushed into a narrow gap in its side.

The plastic turned cloudy from the laser, but I could still see some kind of liquid being sprayed inside.

“...It looks like that’s how they remove the stickers.”

“Yeah.”

<br>

#f#—There should be a sticker removal procedure somewhere in the middle. Use that to peel them off.#f#

<br>

But...

*‘How am I supposed to use that?’*

Even Baek Saheon probably never imagined it would work like this.

Still, I quickly thought of a method.

“Should we open one of the plastic boxes slightly and take some of the remaining solution inside?”

“Try it.”

Since the facility hadn’t stopped us during our escape attempt, it didn’t seem like it would prevent us from interfering with the Workers either.

We stepped off the conveyor belt again and quickly made our way over to carefully open one of the sealed plastic boxes.

Just in case, we chose one containing an uncontaminated... corpse.

*‘...I’m sorry.’*

I opened the box containing the Worker who had called out for help only slightly and collected the liquid inside, careful not to let it touch the body.

The box could be easily separated by hand, almost like a snap-lock container. But when I closed it again, the joints sealed shut with a click.

“If we apply this, I think it should come off.”

“Good.”

Assistant Manager Jin Nasol and I rubbed the liquid over our respective stickers until they were thoroughly soaked.

However, the sticker didn’t come off immediately.

*‘It seems like it needs time to work.’*

Maybe that was why they left them sealed inside those boxes....

Without thinking, I brought the liquid closer and smelled it. A strangely artificial scent drifted from it, almost like bubble gum.

“......”

“Now we just need to find the door.”

“Yes.”

The conveyor belt carried us into the next stage of processing.

At the far end of the new section, a new process title came into view.

<br>

@c@$EbMATERIAL INSPECTIONEb$@c@

<br>

*‘...Material Inspection?’*

What material were they supposed to be inspecting?

The farther we went, the more this place resembled an actual factory.

But even then… Wasn't this all far too elaborate for something that was supposed to be just for disposal? I had assumed they would simply kill the Workers and make sure no one could identify the bodies… But instead, everything was being carried out with unsettling care.

It was almost as though this whole thing existed in an entirely separate space...

......

Maybe the entire facility itself…

was a ghost story.

“...Assistant Manager. Doesn't this place feel like a Darkness?”

“You think so too?”

So I wasn't the only one.

Every nerve in my body went on high alert.

So this was what the Namhae Branch had done after going on and on about cutting costs. They'd actually woven a Darkness into the operating system.

*‘Is that why they were so determined not to give Baek Saheon access?’*

This really was the worst-case scenario.

“...If that's true, then there could be dangerous phenomena here that have nothing to do with the disposal process. Let's be careful while we look for the door.”

“Yeah. Search separately for thirty seconds.”

Assistant Manager Jin Nasol quickly disappeared past the conveyor belt.

I let out a quiet sigh and started looking around this section myself, but then… I abruptly stopped.

Something here hadn't existed in the previous area.

*‘...A mirror.’*

Mounted halfway along the wall was a mirror of moderate size, reflecting both me and Assistant Manager Jin Nasol.

And I'd seen a mirror exactly like this before.

*‘The escape route.’*

The surveillance corridor connected to Daydream's old central system.

The windows there had looked directly into the authorized personnel offices...

Without thinking, I walked over and peered into the mirror. All I saw was my own reflection—a tired, thin Worker, with the edges of the sticker around my neck beginning to peel away.

“...Huu.”

Unless I shone a flashlight through it, there was no way to tell whether this was actually a surveillance window.

*‘I should remember where it is... just in case.’*

Just as I was about to turn away without another thought...

Something appeared in the mirror behind me.

<br>

A member of the security team was standing there, waving at me.

<br>

“......!!”

But when I quickly came to my senses and looked again, the mirror only showed my own face.

*‘...Assistant Manager Lee Seonghae?’*

No, it can’t be.

I let out a breath.

*‘...Even if the other side really is a surveillance corridor, and this really is a two-way mirror, there’s no way I should be able to see through it.’*

And there was no way a security team employee could have entered that area either. Daydream no longer used it.
I backed away from the mirror.

And then—

“Hey.”

“...Yes!”

“Look at this.”

Assistant Manager Jin Nasol called out to me.

When I turned around, she was already standing on the conveyor belt farther ahead.

*‘Wait... when did she even...?’*

I hurried over to catch up, and as I did, my eyes fell on the ‘process’ taking place in this section.

Like the previous area, syringe-like devices were being inserted into the boxes, as if injecting something inside or carrying out some kind of inspection.

Assistant Manager Jin Nasol was already waiting beside the first box that had already passed through it.

“You’re here.”

The moment I jumped onto the conveyor belt...

I finally understood what she wanted me to see.

Assistant Manager Jin Nasol had opened the plastic box halfway and was peering in.

And—

“Ugh!”

There was no longer a person inside.

Only a thick, dark orange liquid sloshed around within.

Floating on top of it… was the sticker.

“You saw it, right? I’m closing it.”

“......”

This was insane.

*‘If they melt everything down like this, there'd really be no way to figure out who anyone was.’*

I held my breath.

“...We’ll need to be careful not to come into contact with any similar equipment.”

“You catch on quick.”

<br>

Whiiiiir—

<br>

The conveyor belt carrying us continued forward.

This section felt unusually long, and now I understood why. It was probably to give the contents inside the boxes enough time to melt down.

*‘This is probably the last stage.’*

Maybe after this, they would stack the boxes up and ship them out as if they were products… only to dispose of them exactly as they were. Just thinking about it made my stomach churn.

*‘Still... if this is the end, then that's a relief.’*

<br>

*Whiiiiir—*

<br>

Once again, the conveyor belt passed through a device, one that looked like the entrance to an automatic car wash. A curtain of hanging brushes installed across the entrance brushed against my face and body as we passed through, and finally, my vision cleared....

A flash of light stabbed at my eyes.

Right in front of the conveyor belt...

A panel flashed brightly before me, like a toy that had just been switched on.

The name of the final process was displayed.

+.
#*#yRECYCLINGy#*#
.+

“......”

What?

What did that... mean?

“Get down.”

Before her words had even fully registered, I had already stepped off the conveyor belt.

However, my eyes unconsciously continued following its movement. The conveyor belt immediately disappeared into the machine beneath the panel, carrying the boxes through whatever process was inside...

...and came back out on the other side.

<br>

*Whiiiiir—*

<br>

A packaged Worker entered the machine.

+.
#*#rRECYCLINGr#*#
.+

A red glow appeared from inside.

Tuk, Tuk, The box and sticker separated, ejecting outward and dropping into a storage compartment halfway through the machine. Each new box fed into it triggered the same process.

<br>

*Beep!*

<br>

From the conveyor belt on the opposite side of the machine, something emerged again.

A transparent box.

“......”

As it approached, I could see what it contained....

<br>

A single, perfectly normal person was sealed inside.

<br>

It was the Worker who had begged me to save him.

He was inside the box, breathing heavily but peacefully. A mark had been stamped onto the surface of the transparent plastic, as though burned there by heat....

<br>

@c@$EbExpiration date: 100 daysEb$@c@

<br>

Beside it was a smiling thumbs-up emoji and a message.

<br>

@c@$EbEven after the expiration date has passed, it can still be recycled!Eb$@c@

<br>

My brain began piecing things together.

The descriptions of this branch.

The words I could finally understand now.

<br>

#f#—I’ve heard that, despite its poor conditions, it’s highly efficient. They’re good at recycling resources, and they don’t use up many supplies…#f#

#f#—Save resources and contribute to the company.#f#

#f#—No, Headquarters Security! Not those recycled, mass-produced nobodies at the branch! Manager...!#f#

<br>

Resource conservation.

Recycling.

Those words weren’t just slogans about exploiting day workers lured in by money and using them in terrible conditions.

They meant exactly what they said.

*‘They’re recycling... the Workers.’*

It wasn’t even as simple as the cruelty of erasing their memories and forcing them to continue working.
That would reduce the efficiency of recycling.

Because even if their memories were erased, some contamination would remain.

So, so…

<br>

They were grinding them down… and making them anew.

<br>

*Whiiiiir—*

<br>

And the corpses of Workers whose expiration dates had ended were fed back into this facility, only to be recycled again!

Back into the state they were in when they were first hired by this branch!

I moved my eyes.

From the clean conveyor belt on the opposite side, people continued to come out, still asleep inside their boxes. While this batch contained only a little over ten bodies, the facility itself seemed to have no shortage of ‘raw material.’

Among them, another familiar face passed by beside me, sealed away inside one of the boxes.

The middle-aged man who had helped me.

Maybe the two members of X group who had tormented me were here too.

No, they definitely had to be!

“Ugh.”

I covered my mouth. I barely managed to suppress the biological revulsion rising from my stomach. And...

“You...”

“......”

And.

The Worker standing beside me now.

Assistant Manager Jin Nasol.

<br>

#f#—Doesn't that strike you as strange? She came here six months ago, yet she says she's only been here for two weeks?#f#

<br>

I understood now.

Assistant Manager Jin Nasol probably hadn’t lied....

She had simply been recycled every month.

And.

And....

“......”

I reached up and touched my neck.

<br>

*Riiip.*

<br>

The sticker came away easily.

With my other hand, I traced the area where the sticker had been attached....

“......”

<br>

There was a small hole.

<br>

The edges of the hole had been neatly reinforced with a slightly hardened material. Like a place where air was pumped into a tube, or perhaps… a place where something was injected.

Then...

<br>

From the hole where the seal had disappeared,

the reddish-orange contents of the recycled material began to leak out.
