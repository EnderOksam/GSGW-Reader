# Chapter 391

#f#—Morning has broken. Workers, please prepare for today’s duties~#f#

<br>

I opened my eyes.

Rubbing at my throbbing head, I pushed myself upright. The empty three-person dorm room assigned to Team X, Unit 910 came into view.

Only the morning broadcast broke the silence.

“……”

Even after stepping out into the shared lounge that served as the workers’ common area, there wasn’t a soul in sight.

Because I was the only one left.

Instead, only the trails left by the white cords and hoses remained, the stains of human bodily fluids lingering ominously behind them.

*‘Ha.’*

That’s right.

I had made it back alive to workers’ quarters that Daydream had only ‘hastily tidied up.’

The company had started ‘cleaning,’ after all.

Though they only moved in once it had been confirmed that the uncontained Darkness incident had truly been brought under control.

<br>

#f#—Unit Z999 confirmed! We’ve found Unit Z999!#f#

<br>

I wasn’t discovered until much later, tucked away inside one of the more secluded shops in the Off-Duty Zone.

I’d shut myself inside a shop hidden in a corner, making it look as though I’d barely managed to escape the white cords before losing consciousness.

Well…actually, I really had passed out.

Because I was just that exhausted!

*‘I seriously thought I was going to die…’*

Carrying Assistant Manager Jin Nasol on my back while moving through the ‘Authorized Personnel Only’ area—after shutting down the central system, with bizarre bodily fluids and the trails left by the hoses strewn everywhere—had pushed me to my physical limit.

At least I didn’t have to smash the Off-Duty Zone’s CCTV cameras. Whether it was the white hoses or something else, they’d already all been destroyed.

*‘After everything, I was so out of breath I couldn’t believe I’d actually planned to clean up even that…’*

If the cameras had recorded me opening the bathhouse door and going inside, I figured there would’ve been nothing I could do about it. Fortunately, though, that area happened to be a CCTV blind spot.

Judging by the fact that no one had pressed me for answers even now, it seemed I hadn’t been found out.

That’s right.

In the end, Assistant Manager Jin Nasol and I had both survived safely.

No one had discovered I’d entered the central control room, and the company had found us under perfectly believable circumstances.

*‘Everything went according to plan.’*

But…

Do you know what this company did for the two of us after we barely survived?

<br>

#f#—Hope you get rich~#f#

<br>

“……”

Absolutely nothing.

In other words, they simply left us to rot.

<br>

#f#—Disinfection complete. You may now enter.#f#

<br>

*‘You insane bastards.’*

They’re seriously telling me to sleep in this lounge again after just one day? There are still traces of corpses everywhere!

*‘How is this any different from being back in my old room…?’*

So what, just because they’d ‘cleared’ the workers’ quarters, everything was supposed to be fine now? Or was it that, even if something was wrong, it didn’t matter because the workers were only temporary hires anyway?

Either way, it was beyond frustrating.

Sleeping alone in the lounge where the security team had watched people be reduced to nothing but shells in real time was genuinely terrifying…

*‘Ha…’*

For the record, the people who had survived the entire incident without ever leaving their rooms had been told it was ‘a quarantine incident caused by parasites.’

Though, honestly, that wasn’t exactly a reassuring explanation either.

<br>

#f#—No, they said the people infected with those parasites got fevers and started going completely crazy!#f#

#f#—What if we catch it too? Didn’t they say everyone’s been quarantined and is getting treatment? Are we really safe?#f#

<br>

Instead of directing their anxiety toward the company, they turned it on the one worker who, among everyone staying in the parasite-infected lounge, had somehow been the only one left unquarantined.

That worker was me.

<br>

#f#—I heard he suddenly started having a fit, saying he thought he was infected too.#f#

#f#—What if he actually was infected and they just missed him?#f#

#f#—No, I heard he kept saying weird things, like he’d seen people melting. He’s clearly out of his mind.#f#

#f#—Out of his mind? Please. He’s just trying to get everyone’s attention! Ignore him!#f#

<br>

Thanks to that, even while standing in the corridor during this morning’s roll call, I was being treated like a mosquito carrying some contagious disease.

…Honestly, after everything I’d experienced firsthand during the containment failure, I’d expected Daydream would at least try to erase my memories.

But now I understood why they hadn’t.

*‘There was no need.’*

The only ones who survived after opening the door were Assistant Manager Jin Nasol and me. We were the only two people who had witnessed that insane incident.

So they must have decided it was more ‘cost-effective’ in every sense to make it look like we simply lost our minds because we were mentally weak.

Memory erasure costs money, after all.

Even if I just told the plain truth-that ‘the missing people didn't just get infected by parasites, they were eaten by them and died! They melted into liquid!’—well… it would still sound like something a madman would say.

My head was pounding.

So, looking back on everything I had experienced during my week at the Namhae Branch…

<br>

“I’m…just so exhausted and scared…”

“What do you want me to do about it? Get lost.”

“Still, I’m so relieved you allowed me to visit you in the hospital… I’m really glad I got to see you…”

“Ha.”

Assistant Manager Jin Nasol sat upright on the shabby hospital bed, pressing her fingers against her forehead as she clenched her teeth.

Right. Assistant Manager Jin Nasol was in the hastily repaired medical room, and I had come to see her as soon as my morning duties were over.

Apparently, they considered losing a finger a serious enough incident that they were keeping her hospitalized for a day to treat it.

Though I suspected they were actually trying to collect samples from the bodily fluids left behind where her finger had melted away… I figured they would probably send her back to work within a day or two.

From the perspective of someone who had to be left alone in the lounge where people had melted away, I honestly wanted to beg them to hurry up and bring her back.

I looked at Assistant Manager Jin Nasol’s right hand, carelessly resting on the bed.

At the bandaged spot where her ring finger used to be.

*‘…She’s missing a finger, and they still expect her to keep going on expeditions.’*

Having a regeneration potion available and choosing not to use it was completely different from being in a situation where you couldn’t obtain one at all.

I clicked my tongue bitterly, but I didn’t let it show.

Regardless, my way of speaking was only like this because of the wiretapping, and being granted permission to visit someone in the infirmary of this godforsaken Namhae Branch was genuinely something to be grateful for.

Even if the patient herself didn’t seem very happy about it.

“I thought I could finally get some rest since no people were coming into the medical room… and now it’s noisy again because of you.”

“I-I hope you can keep your spirits up…. If we can just endure one more week, we’ll have saved enough money to leave. So… hang in there…”

“You…”

“I mean it sincerely.”

Assistant Manager Jin Nasol gritted her teeth, then stopped.

She looked at me as though she wanted to punch me, but in the end, she only closed her eyes and lowered her head back onto the pillow.

She must have taken some painkillers….

“One week left, right.”

“……Yes.”

“But are you really planning to leave?”

“Pardon?”

“I’m asking if you’ve thought about the possibility of not leaving.”

And then I realized that, because of the surveillance, it was a roundabout way of asking:

<br>

#f#—Do you really think you’ll be able to leave in a week?#f#

<br>

…Hmm.

Actually, I was starting to feel a little…uneasy about it too.

Even if I managed to spend the next week without incident, handling ordinary ghost stories and getting through it peacefully…

*‘Will they really just let me go after a week?’*

Even I had to admit that I’d done a lot to stand out.

This time, I had even earned the achievement of ‘came out of the room and survived,’ further cementing my image as the lucky worker.

<br>

#f#—You were alive after all! Just like I thought! Hahahahaha!! Hahaha!#f#

<br>

The researcher’s reaction had honestly been a little frightening. He looked like a sports betting addict whose ridiculous underdog pick had actually paid off.

Somehow… I had a strong feeling that if I said I was leaving in a week, they’d try to tempt me by offering me more money, and if that failed, they’d do something shady.

But I didn’t panic.

“I-I have thought about it…. If I say I want to leave, wouldn’t I be able to? The company said it was possible…”

“…Really?”

“Y-yes…”

Of course, by ‘company,’ I wasn’t referring to the Daydream researcher.

I meant something else entirely.

The central system I had discovered beneath the bathhouse.

<br>

★$
[Device Management]
-Entry/Exit Control
-Ventilation System
-Emergency Alarm Deactivation
-Escape Route Maintenance
$★

<br>

Before heading for the Off-Duty Zone… There was one last thing I checked.

Escape Route Maintenance.

<br>

★$
Escape Route Maintenance

7 escape routes have been detected as currently inaccessible.
$★

<br>

Which meant that, aside from those seven, the rest were still usable.

I’d committed every escape route displayed on the monitor to memory before I left.

What did that mean?

*‘It meant I now had several routes out of this facility mapped out in my head.’*

The situation had changed.

<br>

If things went south, I could run.

<br>

Of course, the map had only been a simplified display, so I didn’t know exactly where everything was.

On top of that, I’d have to make my way into the ‘Authorized Personnel Only’ staff areas without being caught.

*‘But if I really was on the verge of death… it wasn’t a risk I couldn’t take.’*

Besides, if I wanted to, I could always get back into the Central Control Room.

The moment that thought crossed my mind, something else surfaced almost reflexively.

The alert I’d seen on the monitor just before I left.

<br>

★$
A new Darkness has been registered in the Containment Zone!
Location: Underground Containment Zone 1 (Closed)
Would you like to issue a registration number?
$★

<br>

What on earth had that been?

If the Lover of Paradise really had been registered as a new Darkness within the branch… then what state was it being contained in now…?

“……”

I slipped a hand into my pocket and felt the cold metal inside.

The doorknob from the bathhouse area.

Wrapped entirely in black tape in case someone discovered it, it was still usable.

So…

<br>

#f#—It’s lunchtime! Have a hearty meal, and make lots of money this afternoon too~#f#

<br>

For now… I’ll see how things play out.

Assistant Manager Jin Nasol needed time to recover anyway.

“Um… then I’ll just keep working hard for the next week…. Ah, I-I can bring you some food…! I’ll bring yours first before I eat!”

“Forget it.”

“Just a moment…!”

“Hey!”

As the lunch announcement sounded, I got to my feet.

Then, after giving Assistant Manager Jin Nasol a small bow and turning to leave—

“Hey.”

Once again, her firm voice came from behind me.

“…Yes?”

“If it hadn’t been for you, I’d be dead.”

“……!!”

I looked back.

Without meeting my eyes, Assistant Manager Jin Nasol pressed a hand against her temple and spoke firmly.

“…I’m saying that i’m well aware. Do you understand?”

“…Yes!”

I nodded quickly.

“Thank you, too.”

“What are you talking about?”

Pretending not to hear, I faced forward again.

And I left the infirmary feeling a little better than before.

It felt… nice, in a way, to know that even here at the Namhae Branch, there were still things I could find rewarding.

<br>

* * *

<br>

After my brief visit, I dropped off the packaged meal for Assistant Manager Jin Nasol before heading back to the cafeteria for my own lunch.

Although I was eating by myself here, too.

*‘Sigh…’*

It was fine.

*‘At least no one’s picking a fight with me.’*

The food in Team X’s 900-unit block was good, though. I decided to make up for the horrible sleeping conditions by enjoying the freshly boiled pork and bossam kimchi….

…or so I tried to comfort myself.

<br>

*Payment Required*

<br>

As I came out after eating, a security employee walked over and handed me something.

“What’s this…?”

It was some kind of document.

With a bad feeling, I turned it over.

And there… listed in detail were the medical room supplies Assistant Manager Jin Nasol and I had used, along with the items from the Off-Duty Zone.

…Wait.

<br>

*Expense Collection Due to Unauthorized Occupation and Use*

<br>

It was a bill.

“……”

Hey, you bastards…!

What made it even more infuriating was the line saying it had been ‘calculated at the regular price without fines, taking the emergency situation into consideration.’ I felt like my vision was turning red.

*‘They’re even pretending they’re doing us a favor…?’*

They really had a talent for making people cry tears of blood out of sheer frustration, no glasses item necessary.

*‘Wow.’*

But today’s anger-inducing events didn’t end there.

<br>

[Due to parasite damage, the lounge will undergo repairs. Worker X915, step forward.]

<br>

After my afternoon duties were over, during a sudden roll call in the common area, the announcement that called me forward said:

<br>

[Until repairs on the current lounge are complete, you will stay in your previous quarters for two days.]

<br>

What?

“Um, when you say ‘previous quarters’…”

<br>

[Where else would it be? The room matching the number tag on your neck, obviously.]

<br>

…!

Was today some kind of unlucky day?

“But I’m from Team X, so why am I being sent to Room Z990…?”

<br>

[Seriously, are you not understanding what I’m saying? Do you think this place is some kind of hotel? You’re here to work, so stop making pointless complaints and just switch rooms. Understood?]

<br>

The researcher… his attitude had suddenly changed.

He’d spent all this time trying to gently coax me along, dangling rewards in front of me and doing everything he could to raise my motivation to work. So why had he suddenly gone back to how he treated me during Z999?

“Yes…”

For now, I didn’t object.

Besides, it didn’t fit the persona I’d built. And if the researcher had already taken this attitude toward me, arguing back would only damage my already poor reputation even further.

I simply lowered my head and nodded.

<br>

[Good. Then move as instructed.]

<br>

Slowly, I could feel the workers watching me with strangely satisfied expressions.

Seeing the idiot who had suddenly risen through the ranks fall from grace must have been a huge dopamine rush for them.

*‘Well, this is something.’*

Could this have been what the researcher was aiming for?

‘Isolate him and make him focus only on work.’ Was that the plan? Had he switched from carrots to a stick?

But the moment I heard his next words, I realized what was really going on.

<br>

[After dinner, during roll call, someone from headquarters will be coming. Don’t bother them or try to talk to them for no reason. Just mind your own business.]

<br>

Headquarters?

<br>

[Since someone from headquarters will be visiting, we’re postponing this week’s unit number reassignment for three days to avoid unnecessary confusion. Listen carefully. Don’t come complaining later that you weren’t told.]

<br>

Ah.

I understood.

This was because the containment failure had triggered an inspection.

*‘So they’re trying to keep me out of sight on purpose…!’*

To keep their high-grade Dream Essence source from being taken away!

Rather than reveal why I had suddenly risen through the ranks and risk having me taken from them, they were trying to quietly make it seem like the promotion had never happened.

They were just going to treat me according to the sticker around my neck and ride out the inspection period, right?

*‘So this is just a scheme to keep their results from being taken.’*

This was… actually a good thing. No, it might have been exactly what I needed.

It was much better than accidentally drawing the attention of headquarters and having word reach Director Cheong!

*‘Let’s keep my head down.’*

Of course, I really didn’t want to go back to that place that was so bad it felt less like a dorm and more like a medieval prison….

But the truth was, there was something I needed to investigate there.

*‘The doorknob piece from the area leading to the Central Control Room… of all places, it turned up in the floor of that room.’*

I wanted to take a closer look.

*‘Let’s use this as an opportunity.’*

My stamina was still better than when I’d first come to my senses as Z999, so I probably wouldn’t suddenly drop dead.

…Maybe I could endure it for a while.

*‘At least the food hasn’t changed.’*

I came to that conclusion surprisingly quickly.

<br>

And after finishing dinner…

I went into the bathroom near the cafeteria and filled a container at the sink.

Since the room at the very end of the Z990 block didn’t even have running water, I figured this would have to serve as my supply for everyday needs…

But then I suddenly heard someone behind me.

“Oh~ You’re back to being Pigeon again?”

“Just like I thought, you’re a pigeon. You know your own habits well, don’t you? Come on, let’s hear a gugu-gugu~”

“…!”

The final remark came at the same moment they grabbed my head and tried to slam it into the sink.

I reflexively dodged, but they still managed to hit me on the head.

*Thud!*

Wow.

*‘These bastards came for me immediately.’*

Right.

With Assistant Manager Jin Nasol gone, and after somehow getting on the researcher’s bad side again and being thrown straight back down to the lowest accommodation tier… I’d become a deliciously easy target.

They already had a grudge against me from last time, and this was probably their way of taking out their frustration over their failed attempt in the Off-Duty Zone.

“Hey.”

One of them grabbed the back of my head and held me in place.

“The CCTV here is broken, you little shit.”

“……Kgh.”

“Don’t remember? You got the hell beaten out of you here. Our Pigeon did.”

“Hey, don’t say it like that. He didn’t get beaten up. It was education. Education.”

“Ah, right. Of course, bro!”

Squeak. The other guy turned on the water and grabbed me again.

“Come on, Pigeon. Time for a drink.”

He tapped my cheek a few times before slamming my face back into the sink.

“Hey, you need to drink lots of clean water for the parasite to come out, right? Huh? This is basically treatment.”

“Between the water you owe us and the treatment bill, our Pigeon’s got a lot to pay back, huh?”

“……Cough.”

No CCTV?

*‘Wait. This is my chance.’*

Should I?

“Kuh…!”

But the moment I saw myself reflected in the sink mirror, I realized.

*‘No.’*

I had to endure this.

*‘Right now, I can’t afford to stand out.’*

If those guys suddenly started acting afraid of me instead, the entire dynamic would shift. It would look unnatural.

*‘I have to let this happen.’*

Actually… maybe I should even make it look a little worse than it really is!

*‘If anything, I need to make the researcher realize he should be taking care of me a bit. I need to push him into fixing the balance himself.’*

Honestly, Z999’s treatment was far too harsh. I should at least plant the idea that they need to know where to draw the line!

“Ugh.”

In the end, I let these bastards do what they wanted for a while.

Of course, I wasn’t going to let them extort any money out of me.

“I-I don’t have any money…”

“Fucking hell!”

*‘So this was how they planned to make me ‘pay.’’*

My little act of ‘I don’t have a single cent on me’ eventually left the two of them with no choice but to say something like, ‘We’ll come collect your wages tomorrow, Pigeon.’

*‘Is it over?’*

But apparently still irritated, they shoved my face back into the sink.

“Kgh!”

No… this was starting to go too far.

*‘Should I resist?’*

How long had this been going on now?

If this continued, I’d end up standing out because of some unnecessary incident..

“Bro, it’s roll call time now…”

“Hey, hey, it’s fine. Just say you were late because you went to the bathroom. It’s true, isn’t it~? Worst case, you’ll just get a penalty warning.”

The guy who had been snickering forced my face down again.

“You thought this was over, didn’t you? Nope~”

“……”

“Let’s see if we can scare you enough to piss yourself today, huh?”

<br>

This wasn’t going to work.

<br>

“Ghk…!”

I drove my elbow into his solar plexus.

The moment the strength in the hand pressing down on me loosened.

I twisted around, grabbed him by the neck, and used my body weight to take him down.

“……!”

Bam! He hit the bathroom floor and thrashed around, but I kept him pinned, pressing down until the veins in both my hands bulged.

Then I spoke in a calm voice.

“Hey.”

“Kh—khk!”

“You need to know when to stop.”

“Wh—what?!”

The other guy had clearly never expected me to fight back. He just stood there behind me, frozen and unsure what to do.

Before he could even react, I snapped my head around and locked eyes with him.

“Being late for roll call is against the rules, isn’t it?”

“……!”
I looked down at the guy beneath me, whose face was starting to turn pale, then slowly released my grip.

I got to my feet—and immediately slammed my own face into the sink.

“W-what are you doing?!”

I shouted:

“P-please, help me! Please save me!”

“You crazy bastard…”

Good. Even I had to admit I seemed insane. Nice to have confirmation.

I straightened up again.

“Y-you’re done now, right? You got what you wanted….”

“……”

“……”

“Ugh…”

The one I’d choked and the one I’d locked eyes with both recoiled in horror. They slowly backed away before suddenly bolting out of the bathroom.

“Aaaaaah!”

Yeah. That scream was pretty satisfying.

*‘Phew…’*

That brief struggle had completely drained me. My legs were trembling, and I sank down beneath the sink for a moment to catch my breath.

*‘I look like hell….’*

My face was covered in bruises, and my work uniform was soaked through. This ought to be enough to make even the researcher realize how serious things had become.

Just as I lifted my head to leave—

“S-Section Chief! It’s just some workers causing a disturbance! It has nothing to do with the emergency!”

Hurried voices and the sound of approaching footsteps rushed toward the bathroom.

A moment later, someone strode through the doorway, the sharp click of polished dress shoes echoing across the floor.

“Section Chief!”

The brown-haired man in the immaculate black suit—

was wearing a black goat mask.

“……”

“……”

<br>

…Baek Saheon?
