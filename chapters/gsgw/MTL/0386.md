# Chapter 385

First, something to be happy about.

The accommodations assigned to the newly promoted X800-range workers were... surprisingly, pretty decent!

I can finally get a proper night’s sleep.

*‘The bedding is clean... and it’s actually comfortable!’*

No more bunk beds—everyone had a bed of their own. Yeah...This is what a working adult wants. I nodded to myself with quiet satisfaction.

"Looks like it’s three people to a room, but you’ve got a room to yourself."

"Really?"

How did I get this lucky? I was just about to celebrate. Then—

"Yeah. Every worker assigned to your room disappeared."

"......"

"Lucky you, right? More space."

I'm terrified.

Did it really have to be that two of the people who ‘vanished’ during the courage test had been assigned as my roommates?

*‘No, wait. Did all three of them disappear, and I was simply assigned to one of the now-empty rooms?’*

That explanation actually made more sense. I guess all three roommates had originally been assigned to the same group.

*‘Ha...’*

Apparently, the other workers had been told that the missing people had simply been transferred elsewhere because they were no longer fit to carry out their duties.

The fact that it wasn’t a complete lie made it even creepier.

Between pretending nothing had happened and doing everything they could to stop workers from quitting within thirty days, the Namhae Branch really had perfected the art of deceiving its workers.

So my excitement over having such a comfortable room vanished almost instantly.

*‘...It’s scary being in here by myself.’*

After roughly tossing my belongings onto the bed near the door, I found myself thinking back to the wrap-up interview I’d been called into that afternoon. The researcher had finished with the usual ‘Be grateful for everything I’ve done for you’ speech.

Then they started talking about something else.

*‘An Off-Duty Zone pass...’*

$p
#f#—Normally, it’s only open one day a week—Sundays. But X Team workers receive one pass per week, so they’re allowed to go there on weekdays as well.#f#
p$


In other words, they were saying they’d given me that opportunity.

*‘The way they talked about it... is there something good over there?’*

So I asked Assistant Manager Jin Nasol first.

Her answer was simple.

"I’ve never been there either."

"Then let’s go togeth—"

"Not interested."

"......"

Right.

And so, I decided to use my spare time today to cash in my Off-Duty Zone pass!

*‘Strike while the iron’s hot. There might be a chance to find some useful items, so I’d better have a look around first.’*

The earlier I found something, the more cards I’d have up my sleeve when things went wrong.

Even aside from that, getting to explore a brand-new area of the Namhae Branch—one I’d never even seen mentioned on the wiki—was genuinely exciting.

It feels like unlocking a new section of the map.

<br>

—Pass verified.

—Opening Off-Duty Zone. Best of luck this week! Hope you get rich!

<br>

With that, the heavy fire door that had always remained shut slowly swung open, revealing the Off-Duty Zone...

"...!"

It looked like...the run-down entertainment district of an old rural town at night.

<br>

#><[Namhae Tavern]><#

#><[Happy Lottery]><#

#><[Rhythm Karaoke]><#

#><[Sleep Management Center]><#

<br>

An arcade. A comic café... A shabby collection of barely passable attractions, all charging utterly ridiculous service fees.

Even so, they were probably the workers' only source of entertainment—and the only place to unwind—so people would end up paying anyway. After all, they actually had cash.

The doors lining both sides of the corridor stood wide open, revealing strings of exposed bulbs and the warm glow of old-fashioned shop signs.

*‘I guess they were trying to create an atmosphere.’*

When I’d first woken up here, I’d been half-dead. And it had probably been closing time too, with most of the shops already shut, so I hadn’t noticed any of this.

*‘...There aren’t any staff around.’*

Maybe it was because it was a weekday. Nothing here looked especially impressive, but for now...

"Hey."

"......"

"Hey. What’s with this asshole? Pretending he can’t hear me?"

Wow.

I hadn’t even been here five minutes, and someone was already looking for a fight.

*‘Right... this is exactly why I didn’t want to walk around by myself.’*

My eyes went completely blank as I turned my head. Some X guy was pointing straight at me.

"W-What is it...?"

"Oh? ‘What is it?’ You seriously have to ask? Because some bastard walked in here wearing a Z999 tag!"

He jabbed a finger against my neck as he shouted,

"What the hell’s a Z bastard doing in here?!"

Ha...

Should I just shut him down?

It might be easier if I settle things now.

Just as I was about to open my mouth—

"Hey, hey, you idiot. Our Pigeon isn’t in Z class anymore. He’s X class now."

"Huh? Oh... right?"

"Man, this moron never reads the notices or pays attention to the rumors. All he does is complain about not having internet."

"Kang-hoonie, why don’t you go lie down in one of the massage chairs and get some sleep, huh?"

The two of them bantered as they casually slung an arm around the guy who’d been yelling, dragging him away.

After sending him off toward the Sleep Management Center, they turned back to me.

"Well, well~ Fancy seeing you here, Pigeon."

To my surprise...

...it was the two hopeless X Team guys I’d run into the moment I first woke up here.

*’What...?’*

Why were these guys suddenly acting friendly?

And then, the next moment, I realised why.

"Hey, hey, I heard you’ve been getting carried by the person you hang around with."

"......"

"Why don’t you introduce us? Huh? Looks like you’ve really hit the jackpot."

"How much’ve you made over the past two days? Come on, don’t be scared. Since you’re here, why don’t you have a drink with us, and we can chat for a bit?"

So that was it.

I was worth squeezing a little more out of now.

After all, I was earning money far more consistently than I had as Z999.

Now that my status had gone up a little, it seemed they were planning to build a bit of rapport first, then use that familiarity to pressure me into handing over my money more easily.

*'Oh.'*

Well, this saves me some trouble.

I was already planning to handle these guys anyway.

"W-Well..."

"Come on."

I was just beginning to think that maybe I’d play along, let them drag me off for a drink, then settle things with as little hassle as possible—

"Hey, young man!"

"Over here! What’re you doing over there?"

*‘...Huh?’*

When I turned my head, I saw a group of older workers waving me over.

"Hurry up! You said you’d help us with this!"

...What?

But the moment I met their eyes, I understood.

They were signalling for me to get away.

"Y-Yes...?"

Alright.

For now, I backed off. There were witnesses around, so I’d have to put my plan on hold.

"Hey! You bastard! Where the hell do you think you’re running off to—"

"Bro, bro."

The X Team guys looked ready to come after me like a pair of belligerent troublemakers, but after seeing that the people calling me over numbered four or five, they thought better of rushing in.

That didn’t stop them from running their mouths.

"Wow... seriously? You old folks are still here at your age? You really screwed up your lives, huh?"

"Hey, young man, come over here~ Yeah~ We’re just 40 and 50-year-olds nobody would hire for a part-time job, but here? We get to act like we’re in charge~"

...Okay, that’s crossing the line.

*’If I don’t do anything here, won’t I just make the people who helped me look foolish?’*

Just as I was debating what to do, someone put a hand on my shoulder.

"Honestly..."

"Good grief. Don’t they realize everyone’s here because they’re in the same boat?"

"Just ignore them. Ignore them."

"Little punks like that should be kicked out and never allowed to work here again."

While the two X Team guys stood there laughing to themselves, the others quickly ushered me over to the opposite side.

"You alright? Don’t get mixed up with those guys. Even if they call you over, don’t go."

"Exactly. No matter where you go, there are always people like that. We came all this way, so we need to be careful, earn our money, and get out safely."

Once again, I was reminded of something.

*‘...There are good people here too.’*

It was a relief to know they were fortunate enough to have held onto who they were.

I looked at the few people wearing X tags and felt a strange sense of relief.

Of course, this kindness was probably only possible because I’d moved up a class and looked like a ‘sane young man.’ But still, I wasn’t going to complain.

"Goodness. You were always stumbling around looking half-dead, but turns out you’re a perfectly decent young man after all."

"Life’s been hard lately, hasn’t it?"

"Still, you’re young. You’ve got better chances than we do. Once you get out of here, don’t waste your money. Save it, learn a trade, alright?"

"...Thank you."

...I hope these people are able to survive in X-class, get their money, and make it out safely.

"There are a few decent things here, at least. They’re all ridiculously expensive, so we mostly just window-shop... but every now and then, you do find something worth buying."

After that, I wandered around the rest area with the workers, who were about old enough to be my aunts and uncles.

Most places seemed designed to squeeze as much money out of workers as possible, charging absurd prices. Even the occasional item that looked like a bargain was something you could buy outside for the regular price.

The workers with me would sometimes buy things too, though they always seemed hesitant about it. Most of the time, they held back and tried to save their money.

"Fifty thousand won for two hours at a comic café?! What a bunch of thieves."

"Didn’t you say there was a comic you wanted to read, Mr. Kim?"

"Ah, no. Good grief, it’s not like I’m a kid who’s obsessed with comics or anything! Come on, let’s go."

After thinking it over for a while, I quietly bought a bottle of very strong vodka from the bar.
The alcohol content was practically on the level of hand sanitizer.

*‘Something this strong could definitely have a meaningful use in a ghost story.’*

After what happened with the beer and the wooden doll, it was hard to convince myself not to buy it.

The price was outrageous—it looked like someone had multiplied the liquor tax by ten—but I swallowed my tears and paid anyway.

*‘Maybe I’m just not destined to save money...’*

Everything else was mostly for entertainment.

"Every weekend, there’s always one or two idiots who come in here and blow their money like it’s nothing. Sigh..."

There were expensive bottles of liquor on display, along with premium side dishes that weren’t sold in the commissary and even fresh domestic meat meant for grilling.

Even though it was an unmanned shop, nobody seemed willing to steal anything. Apparently, they’d all seen firsthand what happened to people who got caught.

*’I don’t think I’ll ever have a reason to spend money here...’*

But there was one place.

My steps came to a stop there.

<br>

#><[Sleep Management Center]><#

<br>

Beside the massage chairs there was a shelf stocked with vitamins and medicine.

*‘They even have Daydream Hair Loss Treatment.’*

Surprisingly, those were being sold at their normal retail price.

But what caught my attention even more was when I looked through the vitamins and found the pills.

*‘...These are prescription medications.’*

Drugs that were illegal to obtain without a prescription were sitting out in plain sight.

Most of them were sleeping pills, diabetes medication, or products related to the immune system.
But among them was this.

<br>

***Painkillers — 300,000 won (SUPER STRONG!)***

<br>

I checked the active ingredient and nearly recoiled.

*‘Isn't this a narcotic painkiller?’*

It looked like the kind of extremely powerful painkiller hospitals only used when ordinary ones no longer worked...

*‘...Should I buy it?’*

It seemed that Assistant Manager Jin Nasol had been spending every bit of the cash she'd received on painkillers.

I wrestled with the decision until the very end.

"......"

"Young man?"

"Ah, yes."

When I finally stepped out of the Sleep Management Center, I was carrying a box of sleeping pills in one hand and a box of painkillers in the other.

*‘...I'll just keep them on me.’*

<br>

The final stop on the tour...was the place where I'd first opened my eyes.

The room with the ritual table.

<br>

#><[Religious Activity Room]><#

<br>

Now that I looked more closely, the entire corridor had been turned into a religious area, with each room dedicated to a different faith and containing its own religious symbol.

...Maybe it was because of the ghost story I'd just gone through, but it made my stomach turn.

"Considering the kind of work we do here, I guess there are always people who end up wanting to pray to God, Buddha, or the Heavenly Spirits. That's probably why they've got this place."

"On Sundays, there's even a shaman who'll read your fortune for a small fee."

"'Religious Activity Room'... At my age, it almost feels like I'm back in the military."

I could understand.

As we talked, I made sure to look around every room, even if I had to force myself.

Naturally, one of them was the room where I'd first woken up.

The ritual table was exactly as I’d remembered it.

"......"

Of course it had to be the room all the way at the end.

The dried offerings still gave it an unsettling, ominous feel.

*‘...If I woke up here, that means I must've been doing something in this room.’*

I spent a little time searching the shabby room, but nothing in particular stood out.

*‘Hmm.’*

There’s really nothing here. I decided to give up for now and was on my way out when something else caught my eye.

Huh?

There was another room right beside this one.

It was just...

...closed, so I hadn’t noticed it before.

It was clearly a restricted area that workers weren’t allowed to enter.

But...

"......"

It reminded me of something I'd hidden beneath my bed.

One located here in the rest area?

"Young man? We're about to head out. Unless you're planning to leave an offering for the ancestors, you should come with us."

*‘...If I start wandering around looking for it by myself now, I'll stand out too much.’*

The CCTV cameras were watching, so were the people walking with me.

Besides, I didn't need to uncover the secret behind that doorknob...but I couldn't help being curious.

*‘I'll remember it for later.’*

"Oh, right. There's a bathhouse here too. But apparently it's been broken for ages or something—they've never actually opened it."

"Ah... I see. That's a shame."
