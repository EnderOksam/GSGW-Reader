# Chapter 392

In front of the bathroom sinks.

Before I knew it, I found myself face-to-face with the headquarters employee who had come to inspect the Namhae Branch—without anyone else around.

Except...

It was Baek Saheon.

*‘...?!’*

What was he doing here?

*‘Wait... don’t tell me he was the one headquarters sent...?’*

If I hadn’t already been sitting down, I probably would’ve slipped.

I was completely caught off guard.

And judging by the look on his face, so was he.

Behind the black goat mask, one of his eyes stared at me in utter disbelief.

It was the kind of expression you’d have if the pig you slaughtered last year suddenly wandered back into its pen alive.

*‘Then again... I’m surprised he recognized me.’*

With my face covered in bruises and my clothes soaked through, I hadn’t expected anyone to connect this miserable day laborer with Supervisor Kim Soleum.

Especially not someone who, from his perspective, hadn’t seen me in a whole three years.

Hmm.

I suddenly got to my feet.

“...!!”

Then I stared straight at Baek Saheon.

I couldn’t read his expression through the goat mask, but somehow... it looked like he’d flinched.

Oh?

“Did you notice?”

“...What?”

“So you did notice.”

“......!”

At that moment—

“Section Chief!”

Someone came rushing into the bathroom.

A figure covered head to toe in a makeshift protective suit, with a white lab coat draped over it, appeared in the doorway. They were on the shorter side. 

*‘The person in charge of the announcements, maybe?’*

With headquarters conducting an inspection, they must have been forced to accompany them into the workers’ area as a guide.

“Section Chief, the contamination in here has already been completely clea—?!”

The branch employee froze the moment they saw me.

Even through the full-body protective suit, their shock was obvious. My face was bruised all over, my clothes were drenched, and I looked like I’d just been subjected to water torture.

“U-um...”

“I told you not to open your mouth during roll call! That’s a penalty point. This isn’t a warning—I’m actually assigning it!”

The moment I spoke, the researcher practically had a fit.

“Sorry...”

Instead of saying, ‘Roll call hasn’t even started yet, and we’re in the bathroom. What are you talking about?’ I hurriedly mumbled an apology and stepped back.

“...!”

The staff member quickly moved in front of ‘Section Chief Baek Saheon’, trying to smooth things over.

“Ah! The workers in the lowest-number assignments are usually in this kind of state. You know how they are, haha...”

“I wouldn’t know.”

“...Excuse me?”

“I said I wouldn’t know.”

“...!”

The single visible eye behind the black goat mask fixed itself on the person standing in his way.

“So you’re able to tell whether someone’s contaminated or not at a glance?”

“......Th-that...”

The headquarters employee in the black suit looked the branch employee up and down, taking in his sloppy protective gear.

Then, sounding genuinely curious, he asked,

“Then why are you still working at a branch office?”

“......”

Baek Saheon uncrossed his arms.

“I’ll write you a recommendation letter. With eyesight like that, you’d be perfect for the Field Exploration Team.”

“Y-Yes...?”

“Then I’ll have you transferred to my team.”

His gloved hand shot out, grabbed the branch employee by the shoulder, and squeezed hard.

“As it happens, one of my supervisors died three days ago, so there’s an opening.”

“That...”

“He was officially recorded as missing, but he’s definitely dead. I was there when it happened.”

“......”

“I only mention it in case you had any doubts.”

“N-Not at all! O-Of course not! I wouldn’t dream of doubting you! It’s just... research work at the branch really suits me...”

“Ah, I see. So you’ve grown so comfortable with life here that your judgment’s started slipping. Is that it?”

“......”

“Isn’t it?”

“...Y-Yes.”

*...Tuk.*

Only then did Baek Saheon release the employee’s shoulder.

He dusted off his gloved hand and folded his arms again.

“Well, don’t worry. That’s exactly why I'm here—to conduct an inspection.”

“......”

“Roll call won’t be necessary. I’ll gather statements directly from the workers.”

Then he tilted his chin toward the door.

“Wait outside.”

“...Pardon?”

“I said, wait outside.”

“N-No, I...”

“If you have a problem with the audit procedures, you’re welcome to take it up with headquarters directly. Otherwise, I’ll speak to the Branch Manager myself and let them know that a Supervisor seems to have a problem with the audit.”

“N-No, not at all!”

Clenching his teeth, the branch employee tore his gaze away from me. Then, forcing an awkward smile, he hurried out of the bathroom.

“Haha... I-I’ll wait outside!”

<br>

*Bang!*

<br>

The bathroom door slammed shut, the sound echoing through the room. Silence settled over the space in front of the sinks once more.

Baek Saheon’s lips slowly parted.

“You...”

“You?”

“...!”

This guy flinches like it’s a spinal reflex.

Hmm. Still works, I see.

And judging by the look in his eye, he’d come to the same conclusion.

“...What?”

“......”

“It really is you.”

I watched the single eye visible through Baek Saheon’s goat mask tremble as it scanned me from head to toe.

Then he stepped closer and lowered his voice.

“You took the points I gave you and left... So why the hell are you stuck in a place like this...! And what happened to you...?”

“Y-You’re scaring me...”

“...!?”

“I-I don’t know what you’re talking about...”

As I said that, I tilted my chin toward the bathroom.

Then I mouthed,

*‘They’re listening.’*

“......”

Baek Saheon let out a quiet sigh.

He straightened, pulled what looked like a tiny earplug from his breast pocket, then walked over to the bathroom door and wedged it into the keyhole.

“It’s a soundproofing item.”

“......”

“There. Better?”

“Yeah.”

A smile slowly spread across my face.

“...!”

“It’s been a while.”

Baek Saheon froze for the briefest moment. Then, as though forcing himself back under control, his expression settled.

I continued.

“So... it’s been three years now, hasn’t it?”

“......”

“How have you been?”

Baek Saheon took one look at the sorry state I was in.

“Doesn’t look like you’ve been doing too well.”

“......”

“Did another worker do this to you?”

“Yeah.”

I admitted it without hesitation.

The corner of Baek Saheon’s mouth twitched. Whether he was sneering or irritated, I couldn’t tell.

“So you’ve been keeping your head down to avoid drawing attention? Looks like I showed up before they could beat you any worse.”

Not exactly.

Still, saying it out loud seemed to help him regain his footing. By the time he spoke again, he was back to acting like the composed Section Chief conducting an inspection.

Then he turned to me and asked,

“You’re a day laborer now, and I’m the Section Chief here to conduct an inspection. Do you really think that attitude of yours is appropriate?

Huh?

“Of course?”

“~!”

“I’m going to quit in a week anyway.”

Baek Saheon’s expression shifted.

“If you were going to quit, then why are you even here...? You disappeared for three years. What, did you lose a bet or something?”

“I have no idea. One moment I came to my senses, and I was here.”

“......”

“Anyway, I really do plan on quitting. I’m just not sure they’ll let me leave that easily.”

“...What, do they kill people who resign here?”

“I don’t know about that. I just have a feeling they’re not going to let me go.”

I smiled faintly.

“Last week, I obtained a B-grade Dream Solution with nothing but my bare hands.”

“...?!”

“Then I survived direct contact with the Darkness during the containment failure.”

“Wait—”

“So... I might’ve attracted a little more attention than I meant to.”

I shook the water from my hair and smoothed it back. 

“They might decide they can’t let me leave after all.”

“......Ha.”

Baek Saheon folded his arms, the corner of his mouth curling into a knowing smirk.

“So that’s it—you need my help.”

“......”

I didn’t deny it.

“Wow... Three years is a long time, isn’t it? While I was getting promoted to Section Chief, you ended up stuck here as a day laborer. After everything I did to help you, you’re not even a little embarrassed?”

He tilted his head.

“And yet... this is the attitude you’re going with when asking me for a favor?”

“......”

“So how exactly are you supposed to intimidate me now? From what I can see, you don’t seem to have anything.”

“Oh, I do have an equipment item.”

“...?”

“It’s a really good one. First prize. I got it on my first trip into the Darkness.”

“That’s a lie.”

“It’s true.”

I shrugged as I adjusted my work jacket.

“And I’m not trying to scare you anymore. There’s no need for that now.”

“......”

“I’m just glad to see you again.”

He flinched.

But I only looked at him calmly.

“More importantly, why are you still working for Daydream?”

“......”

“Did something happen in your hometown—”

“It’s nothing like that.”

The answer came before I could even finish speaking.

Baek Saheon ran a hand over his slicked-back hair and sighed.

“Forget it. Anyway... just wait and see. Finding out whether you can leave shouldn’t be too difficult.”

...!

“Thanks.”

“You say that way too easily, like I’m doing this for free.”

He frowned.

“You said you had an item. You can pay me back with that. If we start counting interest too, the number of things you owe me would be more than just one or two...”

His voice trailed off.

“Whatever. Anyway.”

“......”

This guy... He seemed to have already figured out that I was the agent from Jisan Village.

After what he’d just said, I was certain of it.

*‘He seems to have realized the truth, yet he still doesn’t know how to act around me.’*

Even now, I couldn’t tell whether he wanted to bring it up, make it obvious he knew, or pretend he didn’t.

So, for the moment, I changed the subject.

“I’m not the only one here. Deputy Butterfly is here too. We’re working together.”

“Deputy Butterfly? Ah, the one from A-squad.”

Baek Saheon spoke indifferently, but he avoided my gaze.

“So that’s where she ended up after going to the branch to repay her debt.”

“......”

I paused for a moment before asking,

“Then what happened to the others?”

“Who?”

“The people who were part of Director Ho’s project team.”

“......”

After a moment of silence—

“I don’t really know. After the project fell apart, I stopped keeping in touch with them.”

“Don’t lie. You got promoted. You expect me to believe you went on without hearing any company rumors?”

“You’ve gotten worse at reading people. I guess life got comfortable for you.”

“What?”

“I’m avoiding the subject because there’s nothing good that’ll come from telling you.”

...A bad feeling crawled down my spine.

Baek Saheon abruptly looked away.

“But if you really want to know... well. Most of them aren’t with the company anymore. They either quit, or they went missing...”

What?

“Who quit, and who went missing?”

“I told you I don’t know because I didn’t want to get involved!”

“...!”

“Why? You look disappointed. Were you about to say something like, ‘I should’ve helped them too’?”

I didn’t know why he was being so defensive. If anything, he seemed almost desperate to change the topic.

“That’s not it. I was trying to find out what happened because I was worried about the others.”

“......Ha.”

Baek Saheon raised a hand and rubbed at the area around his eyes.

“Of course you’re still like this...”

......

“More people simply quit than went missing. For now, Section Chief Lizard is still with the company. Though he’s not a Chief anymore.”

“Then—”

“It’s getting late, so I’m leaving. Just wait here, alright? Don’t go doing anything stupid. Besides... you’re not exactly in any condition to save anyone right now.”

Before I could answer, he turned away as if ending the conversation and started to leave. But then he stopped and asked:

“The paper boat I gave you. Do you still have it?”

......

“No reason. It would just make it easier to contact you if you did.”

“I don’t have it anymore.”

“......Well, alright.”

<br>

*Bang.*

<br>

The bathroom door closed, and Section Chief Baek Saheon disappeared.

Just like that, I was left alone again.

“Huu.”

I still couldn’t wrap my head around what had just happened.

*‘No, seriously... Why did it have to be Baek Saheon who showed up here?’*

It all felt so unreal that, even after being waterboarded, I almost washed my face again just to make sure I wasn’t still out of it.

To think I’d meet Baek Saheon—the ‘Section Chief’ who had climbed the ranks so quickly in the <Darkness Exploration Records>—like this.

And in this state, no less.

*‘Still... I don’t think he’s going to report my whereabouts to the higher-ups.’*

I had to admit, seeing him again was a little reassuring.

He was definitely more friendly than he used to be.

Of course, there was a chance he was only avoiding trouble for his own sake. Reporting me would only drag him into the mess as well. But even setting that aside, he was still more willing to help than I expected.

“Hmm.”

Either way, this was a good thing.

If I had help from a Section Chief from headquarters, getting out of the branch would be much easier.
Of course, my worries about the others from Director Ho’s project team still came rushing back...

But I took a deep breath.

*‘Baek Saheon wasn’t wrong.’*

Finding out what happened to them was something I could do after I got out.

Right now, what I needed to do was...

*‘Focus on getting out of here safely.’*

Alright.

I made up my mind to do that.

<br>

...But seriously, I still had no idea how I was supposed to treat Section Chief Baek Saheon.

What exactly did he want?

<br>

Meanwhile, as Kim Soleum remained alone in the bathroom, lost in thought—

*‘What’s going on?’*

The branch’s research supervisor was practically losing his mind, muttering the same question to himself—but for a completely different reason.

No, seriously. The headquarters employee who came for the audit had seemed like a total pushover!

<br>

#f#—I’m the one who should be asking for your cooperation.#f#

<br>

The leader of Team H from the Field Exploration Team had even seemed gentle and approachable when he greeted him with a smile.

Even with an eyepatch, he looked so young, and his expression was so soft that he seemed almost pitiful. Like he was just someone who’d lost a body part in a Darkness and barely managed to make it back alive.

*‘He probably only became a Section Chief so quickly because people in the Field Exploration Teams die so often.’*

It wasn’t like he was from some elite squad either. He had just taken over an empty position in an ordinary team.

There were rumors that everyone who entered Darknesses with him had died one after another, and he was the only one who made it out. But that was just survivor bias, wasn’t it? If anything, he’d probably only risen to Section Chief because he was the only one who kept coming back.

...With that in mind, the supervisor had gone along with him without much hesitation, thinking he’d be easy to deal with.

<br>

#f#—I heard screaming coming from the bathroom. Could there still be contamination left from the containment failure? I think I need to investigate this.#f#

#f#—Y-Yes?#f#

<br>

The instant he found a chance to press the issue, the man completely changed.

<br>

#f#—I said it’s suspicious, didn’t I? Supervisor, are you not understanding what I’m saying?#f#

<br>

Wasn’t it obvious that he was desperately looking for any excuse to pin the failure on the branch...?!

What infuriated him even more was that it had actually worked: he’d managed to get a private meeting with Z999 in the bathroom.

And so, the Supervisor had returned alone to the personnel only area and was now sitting in his office, biting his nails.

*‘No way...’*

We weren’t found out, were we?

*‘No. That worker bastard is completely stupid.’*

Without his luck, he was nothing but dead weight.

Even today, with the protection of the researcher gone, he had clearly been beaten up again on his way back to the dorm assigned to his number.

There was no way he would say anything useful during an interview.

“I don’t think they’ll know...”

“Know what?”

“...!!”

The Supervisor whipped his head around.

Section Chief Baek Saheon from Team H had entered the office without making a sound, watching him with a faint smirk.

“Who doesn’t know?”

“...No, I just meant... the workers probably don’t even fully understand what happened during the containment failure. And they probably don’t realize how important an audit from headquarters like this really is...”

“Really? It didn’t seem that way to me.”

“......”

“Well, it doesn’t matter.”

Then, without another word, he quietly began flipping through the documents he had printed out.

*Rustle, rustle.*

“Um.”

The only sound in the office was the quiet turning of pages.

“Excuse me!”

“What is it?”

“I was just wondering if the interviews went well.”

“Yeah. More or less.”

*Tap.*

Baek Saheon closed the documents and answered in an indifferent tone.

“The worker who spoke to me earlier did seem a little strange, though.”

Damn it!

“Haha, like I said, that wasn’t contamination...”

“No. I’m saying he seemed mentally unstable to begin with.” 

“...Pardon?”

“The two workers who came running out of the bathroom earlier said that Z999 is insane. What do you make of that?”

What do I make of it? They were just trying to cover up what they’d done after squeezing him for money and tormenting him!

But for some reason, every time Section Chief Baek Saheon asked a question, the corner of his mouth twitched. It almost looked like he was enjoying himself. Is he actually having fun messing with me?

He already knows everything, and he’s still asking!

“Hahaha... Well, you know how workers can be...”

“Where exactly did you find people like that? Aren’t you worried you’ll drag headquarters into unnecessary trouble?”

“Oh, no. That won’t happen.”

“I also heard there are workers planning to quit after only a month. Are you actually letting them leave?”

“Th-That... yes.”

Like hell I’m going to explain every detail of how this branch operates to you. The Supervisor had nearly been cornered into answering questions that weren’t even part of the audit. He clenched his teeth.

Then he forced out a slick, evasive smile and let his words trail off.

“The details of how we manage our workers are part of Namhae Branch’s unique management methods. So, yes... for specifics, you’ll really have to ask the ‘Branch Manager’ directly.”

“......”

The Section Chief’s single visible eye remained fixed on him for a long moment.

Just as cold sweat began to bead on the Supervisor’s skin...

“Well, alright then.”

Phew...

A quiet chuckle reached the Supervisor’s ears.

“Well, it’s not like you have the authority to decide that anyway, do you, Supervisor?”

“...Hahaha...”

I want to kill him.

“In that case, let’s take a look at next week’s area management plan. Include the worker deployment schedule—who’s entering which area and when.” 

“Ah... R-Right!”

In the end, the Supervisor had no choice but to hand over the requested documents to the Section Chief from Headquarters Team H. 

“That’s more like it.”

Completely unaware of who actually intended to obtain that information through Baek Saheon.
