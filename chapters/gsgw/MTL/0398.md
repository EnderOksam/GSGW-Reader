# Chapter 397

#f#Have you... by any chance, taken the pill?#f#

<br>

The question hung in the air.

For a fleeting moment, a silence as heavy as death seemed to settle over us.

All around, the corridor buzzed with confused voices. Workers in shabby uniforms milled about, their faces unnaturally healthy. Among them stood Assistant Manager Jin Nasol, the former elite-team employee, looking no different.

The dark circles beneath her eyes—circles that should have been etched there by exhaustion, pain, and constant irritation—had vanished completely.

And considering everything she'd been through, it shouldn't have been possible.

“Assistant Manager.”

“......”

She finally parted her lips.

“Not sure.”

“...What?”

A voice cut in from behind me.

“That means she took them.”

…!

When I turned my head, Baek Saheon was already beside me. His face twisted into a frown as he urged me on.

“Leave her. Quickly!”

“She never actually said she took it.”

“Are you an idiot? If she hadn't, she'd have just said so!”

“…!”

“‘Not sure’ means she's hiding something! She can't tell us the truth, so she's stalling. Why are you entertaining this?”

“She's not the kind of person to make excuses like that. There has to be something else—”

“And what are you so sure of? Even if there is, what are you going to do? It won't change the fact that she took it!”

“I'd rather ask her and settle it than waste time arguing with you!”

During the brief moment Baek Saheon fell silent, I immediately turned back to Assistant Manager Jin Nasol.

“Why aren’t you sure?”

“I don't remember taking it, but I feel better.”

“……”

“My condition is unusually good.”

That meant...

“You don't know exactly when it happened, but you think it's possible you took one…?”

“Yeah.”

Assistant Manager Jin Nasol's gaze drifted over the chaotic corridor before settling on Baek Saheon and me, who had suddenly appeared in front of her.

With weary eyes, she said,

“I told you already. I’ve become a complete idiot. Maybe I accidentally swallowed one while I was taking my painkillers.”

“…!”

“Or maybe I shoved it into my mouth myself while I wasn’t in my right mind.”

“You haven’t acted like that once since you tried to take all those painkillers. What are you talking about?”

“Then what other explanation is there? Why would the symptoms appear all of a sudden? You’re the one who needs to get things straight!”

Irritation briefly surfaced beneath Jin Nasol’s otherwise calm voice before settling back down.

“Judging by the way you’re both acting, something must’ve happened. If someone who’s taken the pills can’t come with you, then just leave me behind.”

“......”

No.

Something wasn't right. Ever since the painkiller incident, Jin Nasol had never once slipped back into that delirious state.

And yet now she was saying she might have taken one of the pills...

*‘Then that meant…’*

Without even realizing it...

“......”

“That settles it, then. Let’s go.”

“Wait.”

“What now? Don’t tell me you’re making a special exception just because you know her. Aren’t you ashamed of yourself—”

“No, that’s not it.”

I raised a hand.

Cold sweat trickled down from my chin.

“I might have taken it too.”

“……!”

“Assistant Manager and I ate almost the exact same meal. But if she took it without realizing…”

“Wait.”

“Then that means I did too.”

I remembered.

Back when I heard the head singing atop the silver platter during the original Tuesday Talk Show.

Back then… I don’t think I instinctively felt that the melody was that beautiful.

But now, when I heard the instrument’s demonstration, I felt a strange pull toward it. I was even able to form a precise image of the performance in my mind.

The reason I could do that was…

*‘…Don’t tell me it was because I took the medicine too?’*

Even the bruises and wounds on my face that I thought were simply healing naturally…

They might have actually been the effects of the capsule.

Cold sweat trickled down to my chin again.

……And then another thing that had been bothering me came back to mind.

*‘This… the dosage instructions said you were supposed to take three pills consecutively.’*

Take one capsule a day for three days.

If alcohol was set as the manipulated variable, then from an experimental standpoint, wouldn’t it make sense for the other variables to be properly controlled according to the dosage instructions?

But we had only been given two capsules so far, and yet they opened the Off-Duty Zone and had allowed us to consume alcohol.

Then that means, maybe…

“Could it be… that the day before breakfast was distributed, they had already crushed one capsule and hidden it in our meal?”

Hadn’t there already been three capsules from the beginning?

“…….”

“…….”

“Baek— no, Section Chief Goat.”

I took a breath.

“Like I said earlier, thank you.”

“What are you…”

“Maybe you’d be better off returning to the authorised personnel area you’re familiar with.”

“…….”

“Right now… I can’t even be sure that I didn’t take it.”

If we really had taken the medicine, then from Baek Saheon’s perspective, his chances of survival would probably be higher there than moving around with people who might become instruments.

I quickly spoke to the unmoving Baek Saheon.

“I’m sorry for making you waste your time coming all the way here for nothing. We’ll still try to escape as planned, so if it’s possible, you should leave and we can meet—”

“Shut up.”

“…!”

Baek Saheon looked up.

For a brief moment, countless conflicting thoughts seemed to flicker across his face, his expression and gaze shifting with each one.

Then, after a moment, he gritted his teeth and forced out each word.

“Convince me.”

“What?”

“Convince me there's something for me to gain by going with you.”

The words ‘just go already’ rose to the tip of my tongue, but in the next instant, I understood.

He was asking me to give him a reason to stay.

“…I know how to reactivate this branch's old central system.”

“…….”

“That’s how I learned about the route we’re taking.”

Silence.

“So… if we follow the path behind the dirt wall, we might find something we can use to negotiate with headquarters.”

“Understood.”

Baek Saheon spoke through gritted teeth.

“I’m coming with you, so shut up and move.”

“But are you sur—”

“I said stop asking and get moving. I'm telling you I'll go with you.”

“…Okay.”

It would definitely be better to have someone familiar with the personnel-only area accompany us.

But seeing how pale he looked, I swallowed the sigh that threatened to escape.

“Assistant Manager, let's go.”

The workers had apparently noticed the oddly dressed Section Chief from headquarters and were beginning to steal glances our way. Aware of their attention, I quickened my pace.

Assistant Manager Jin Nasol watched the whole exchange with an expression that said it was already giving her a headache, but she followed us for the time being.

“You don’t really look like someone who took it.”

“...Maybe the effects kicked in before the changes showed up on my face.”

This was driving me crazy.

“Either way, we can't be sure of anything. That capsule gave rise to an absurdly high-grade Darkness. We don't have time, so I'll explain everything while we move. It—”

"Forget it. Just do what needs to be done."

…Right.

When we returned to Room Z999, I made sure the two of them were inside before shutting the door and leading them toward the dirt wall.

“Behind this is...”

Just then—

The workers’ zone, despite its worn and aged appearance, still had the feeling of a place where people lived and worked. But this place was different. Something about it felt wrong.

A hint of skepticism entered Baek Saheon’s voice.

“…You’re saying we can find an escape route from here? Are you serious?”

“Yes. We just need to find even one part of the layout that I recognise.”

I steadied my breathing and tried to recall the escape route maps I had seen on the central system’s screens as calmly as possible.

I had memorised more than a dozen of them, each showing the routes from the entrances to the designated exits.

a fork in the path.

“We just keep going straight from here!”

“How much farther?”

“Probably... about three times the distance we've already covered.”

“Twenty-five minutes.”

“If it's six times the distance, that's about how long it'll take.”

“Thank you!”

“…Your gratitude is pretty cheap. I saved your life, and she gets the same thanks just for calculating how long it'll take!”

Oh, come on.

Thankfully, Assistant Manager Jin Nasol merely looked exasperated instead of reacting any further.

Even while saying that, Baek Saheon never once slowed his pace.

If there was one thing all three of us excelled at, it was staying alive.

“No wonder I thought it was unnecessarily large. I figured it was some kind of setup for dealing with Darkness, but there was actually a place like this hidden behind it? Ha!”

If we continued straight ahead, there was a good chance we would actually find the exit.

Baek Saheon seemed to have reached the same conclusion, because his voice sounded noticeably lighter.

“Let’s move for now. Oh, and I’m going to make use of this information later, so keep that in mind.”

Because all three of us were staring at the same place.
