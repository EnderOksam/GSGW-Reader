# Chapter 404

The sticker was gone from my neck.

Something was flowing out through the small hole beneath it.

“......”

I stared down at the liquid clinging to my hand.

A thick, reddish-orange fluid.

It was exactly the same as what the Workers had become inside those boxes after going through the process.

And the name of that process had been…

<br>

@c@#f#$EbMaterial InspectionEb$#f#@c@

<br>

So...

This was the raw material my body was made from.

And right now, it was leaking out...!

“...!!”

I immediately tried to stick the label back on, but It wouldn't stay. In desperation, I pressed the sticker against my neck with one hand, trying to seal the hole shut.

And, and...

...the words I'd just absentmindedly read on the crumpled sticker flashed through my mind.

![Official Illustration Translated by Beiruu](ch404_1.webp)

My eyes met Assistant Manager Jin Nasol's. Cold sweat ran down my back.

“The sticker… Don't take it off.”

“......”

Assistant Manager Jin Nasol was pressing a hand against the base of her neck as well. Her sticker had already lost most of its adhesive.

And the expression she wore... was probably the same one I had.

Empty and shaken.

“So,”

“......”

“That means we’re one of those too.”

<br>

*Whiiiiir—*

<br>

Boxes placed neatly one after another on the moving conveyor belt passed beside us.

The Workers inside.

Packaged bodies.

Recycled products with a 100-day expiration date.

“...If I was going to be made again anyway, it would’ve been more efficient to remove my pain too.”

Assistant Manager Jin Nasol spoke without even furrowing her brow.

“Well, if this kind of convenient wishful thinking worked, it wouldn’t be a Darkness.”

“......”

“Anyway.... Yeah.”

Her voice was terrifyingly calm.

“Jin Nasol is already dead, and I’m something newly produced.”

<br>

*Whiiiiir—*

<br>

“If I take my hand away from the sticker now, the contents inside will just start leaking out and I’ll probably collapse.”

“......”

“But the machine will just produce a brand new Jin Nasol, right?”

The corner of her mouth twisted.

“...No. That wouldn’t be Jin Nasol. And the same goes for me. ...I was something imitating someone who was already dead.”

“......”

“No.”

I raised my head.

My stomach churned from the shock, and my ears rang, but even so, my words came out clearly.

“What?”

“It’s true that we’re not in a normal state right now, but that isn’t the case. Assistant Manager, you are Jin Nasol.”

“Stop running away from reality. It’s irritating.”

“I’m not running away from reality. I’m speaking realistically.”

I pointed toward the machine.

“Because if that were true, they wouldn’t have used that word.”

The panel in front of the machine.

<br>

@c@$EbRECYCLINGEb$@c@

<br>

“It means they’re reusing us. Not taking our original bodies and producing copies from them.”

“......!”

“In Darknesses, these kinds of conceptual details are important, aren’t they? If this facility is a Darkness, then the difference between words like this matters even more. So...”

I spoke slowly.

“I think the fact that they specifically called it ‘recycling’ means they’re taking something that has already outlived its usefulness and putting it to use again.”

A factory that kept recycling a toy into something new, even after it could no longer move.

If that was how this place could be defined...

“Then we’re ourselves, recycled. You don’t need to question your own identity.”

“......”

Slowly, understanding and acceptance began to settle into the eyes of the woman who had once been an Assistant Manager of the elite team most skilled at exploring Darknesses.

As I spoke, I felt my own thoughts slowly clearing as well.

I instinctively knew this reasoning was correct. But—

“...And even if we assume that, as you said, the original is already dead and this body is a copy made by the machine...”

I was able to say it without hesitation.

“That still wouldn’t mean we’re fake. Assistant Manager, you definitely have a sense of self. And so do I.”

“......”

“We’re simply trapped in a cycle of thirty days, losing our memories over and over. ...Of course, that would still be the worst possible situation. But there’s no need to deny who you are because of it.”

“......”

Assistant Manager Jin Nasol stared at me as though I had just struck her.

“...How can you be so sure so quickly?”

It was simple.

“I’ve thought about this problem many times before.”

Even as cold sweat ran down my face, I gave her a faint smile.

“So I already know the answer.”

And as I said that, I realised it again myself.

Yeah.

Even in this bizarre state, with an expiration date attached to my body and a hole for injecting ‘material’ into it...

I was scared, but I wasn’t panicking.

My mind was calm and steady.

No. At the very least, I could keep pulling myself back to that state.

*‘Honestly, I’ve been in situations even worse than this.’*

And after everything I had been through, I was still Kim Soleum in the end.

*‘It’s the same.’*

The person standing here...

was definitely me.

I already knew that my sense of self was intact. I had no reason to doubt it.

So at the very least, I wouldn’t panic over that.

I would do what I could.

“Now... getting back to the point,”

I wiped the cold sweat from my face and spoke with a clear mind.

“Let’s not give up. Let’s find a way to get out of here alive. At the very least, we have a machine right in front of us that we can examine, and it might have a clue.”

“......”

“......”

“Then hurry up and move.”

“Yes.”

I took a deep breath, turned around, and sprinted back.

“This may sound a little strange, but... I’m glad we’re going through this together rather than having to face it separately.”

“......”

Assistant Manager Jin Nasol didn't reject what I said with a “It’s the same damned thing either way.”

But that wasn't a bad sign, either.

*‘...Stay calm.’*

Let's find a way out of this.

Don't panic!

<br>

*Whiiiiir—*

<br>

We crossed over the conveyor belt and made our way around to the back of the machine.

There was definitely something new here.

“...! There's a panel.”

We had found a machine control panel that seemed to be intended for employees.

<br>

@c@$EbRECYCLING IN PROGRESSEb$@c@

@c@$Eb82% COMPLETEEb$@c@

@c@$Eb824 / 999Eb$@c@

<br>

*‘That’s a lot.’*

Seeing that it could produce 999 people in a single run made my stomach churn again.

No wonder it had always felt like they treated the Workers so carelessly, as though they only cared about moving on to the next thing as quickly as possible.

Including me.

How is it that, in this place, I don't think I've ever had the chance to use a normal body? I swallowed back a groan and began examining the machine more closely.

The employee panel was sealed behind a transparent plastic cover, with colourful buttons underneath. But when I tried to lift it, it wouldn't budge. 

“Check the lock first.”

“...!”

She was right.

There was a separate lock attached beside the cover, as though it had been added afterward.

*‘...That’s strange.’*

It felt out of place in this factory.

Unlike the exaggerated, toy-like machines, facilities, and illustrations around it, this device had a realistic, modern appearance.

And there was a small label attached to the bottom....

<br>

@c@$EbNO UNAUTHORIZED MODIFICATIONSEb$@c@

<br>

It seemed likely that the branch had attached it themselves afterward.

“There’s no keypad either. Should I just break it open?”

“Wait a moment.”

Doing so might cause it to stop working completely... or there might be some other trap-like mechanism hidden inside.

And if it wasn’t a regular lock and there was no keypad either, then usually...

*‘A keycard!’*

The thought clicked into place. I reached into my pocket and pulled out the item I had been carrying....

<br>

@c@*Ba-da-ba-da-bam!*@c@

<br>

“...!”

A notification sound came from the machine.

I stopped moving and reflexively looked back at the control panel.

<br>

@c@$Eb■■■■ VehicleEb$@c@

@c@$EbProcessing CompleteEb$@c@

<br>

“So it finished producing all the Workers.”

“Yes.”

Now, once the conveyor belt carrying the Workers completed the final dispatch process, Baek Saheon would open the door for us. It might be better to just regroup and continue from there…

<br>

@c@$EbEntering Final Cleaning StageEb$@c@

@c@$EbLine MaintenanceEb$@c@

<br>

“......”

*‘Cleaning?’*

<br>

*Whiiiiir—*

<br>

Another sound of movement, separate from the conveyor belt, came from above us.

I looked up. Something long and hose-like had descended near the conveyor belt.... But—

<br>

It wasn’t moving toward the belt.

It was fixed directly above our heads.

“......”

<br>

@c@$EbPrevious Production Batch IdentifiedEb$@c@

@c@$EbAutomatic Disposal InitiatedEb$@c@

<br>

Liquid shot out from the nozzle.

<br>

“……!!”

I instinctively turned to avoid it, but the liquid still splashed across my back. The clear substance had no strange smell, nor did it cause any pain. It only carried a faint lemon scent. But....

I looked at Assistant Manager Jin Nasol in front of me.

The outer layer of skin on her shoulder where the liquid had hit was melting away.

......

......!!

Jin Nasol and I rolled to the side at the same time.

<br>

*Whiiiiir!*

<br>

The nozzle moved again, tracking us as we shifted positions.

*‘No.’*

No! Now that we’d already been hit, there was no point in just dodging it anymore! Hurry!

*‘Somehow...!’*

I clenched my teeth and jammed my neck tightly against my shoulder, pressing down hard as I moved both hands. More slime fell, but I endured it.

I pulled the charged chip from the ring case in my hand—no, from the storage case for the administrator card....

<br>

The liquid shot out again.

<br>

“……!!”

“Open the lock!”

Assistant Manager Jin Nasol grabbed onto the nozzle with both hands and twisted it with all her strength. Crimson slime dripped down beneath it.

“Hurry, I—”

Assistant Manager Jin Nasol was melting.

No.

Splatter. Splatter. Crimson slime seeped out and fell onto the floor. My mind seemed to go completely blank. Her face, the arm she was holding onto it with....

*‘Don’t look!’*

Hurry! Just move your hands!

I forced myself upright and shoved the keycard in my hand against the locking device.

<br>

*Beep-beep-beep!*

<br>

With a cheerful sound, the plastic cover opened. I frantically looked over the buttons.

*‘...This is insane.’*

My mind went blank again.

The buttons, all different sizes and shapes and covered in bright colours, had no labels whatsoever. Out of these dozens of buttons, which one was I supposed to press now....

*‘No!’*

Don’t panic. After all, this is a UI too. There has to be a pattern!

I had to think rationally! In the most urgent situation, the button that would catch an employee’s eye the fastest would be—

*‘The biggest red one.’*

I slammed my hand down on it.

“…….”

<br>

*Beeeeep!*

<br>

@c@$EbEMERGENCY STOPEb$@c@

@c@$EbFINAL CLEANING STAGE CANCELLEDEb$@c@

<br>

Air hissed out of the module as it stopped operating. The conveyor belt screeched to a halt as well, and silence fell over the facility.

...It worked.

“......”

I turned my head in the silence.

Assistant Manager Jin Nasol was half melted away, slumped on the floor....

And—

Though I couldn’t see it, my back was probably in the same state.

“......”

I looked at the control panel again. Dozens of colourful buttons, each marked with different shapes, pictures, and patterns.

*‘Leaving things like this won’t change the outcome anyway.’*

If this UI was designed to be understood at a glance... I needed to choose something quickly. I reached out with trembling hands.

Then I began looking through the colours and shapes, searching for ones that seemed to have some meaning.

*‘This.’*

Four white arrow-shaped buttons were arranged around a central point, pointing up, down, left, and right.

And at the very centre was a heart-shaped button....

Direction and selection.

“......”

I pressed the heart-shaped button in the centre of the arrows.

<br>

@c@**$EbPROCESS SELECTIONEb$**@c@

@c@$EbCUSTOM 26Eb$@c@

@c@$EbSTANDARD RECYCLINGEb$@c@

@c@$EbSPECIAL RECYCLINGEb$@c@

@c@$EbCUSTOM RECYCLINGEb$@c@

<br>

“...!”

I realized it instinctively.

*‘Custom 26 must be the process that just handled the Workers.’*

It had to be the twenty-sixth custom process developed through repeated testing at this branch. And since it was listed at the very top, hadn’t they probably arranged it that way so it could be selected immediately?

*‘That means this branch’s ‘Worker processing’ is unusual enough to warrant its own custom process.’*

If so, then the option that most faithfully followed the most basic meaning of recycling was obviously....

*‘...Standard Recycling.’*

...I didn't know which option would restore my body to its original state. So I chose the one that seemed most likely to do so.

I pressed it.

<br>

@c@**$EbSTANDARD RECYCLINGEb$**@c@

@c@$EbRepairs damaged items and turns them into new products.Eb$@c@

@c@$Eb(Expiration date: 100 days)Eb$@c@

@c@$EbPress the **Start** button.Eb$@c@


<br>

I slammed my hand down on the largest green button.

<br>

@c@$EbNEW PROCESS **START**Eb$@c@

<br>

*Whiiiiir!*

<br>

The conveyor belt began moving again.

*‘I’m sorry.’*

I crawled forward to the two unconscious Workers who had just emerged from the machine, pushed them onto the conveyor belt, and pulled free the boxes they’d been inside.

Then, somehow, I managed to gather up what remained of Assistant Manager Jin Nasol’s melted body from the floor and put it into one of the boxes I had just taken.

I sent it down the conveyor belt into the machine.

And then.... me, too.

I moved the other box onto the conveyor belt and threw myself into it.

*Splatter.*

I wanted to gather as much of my body that had spilled onto the floor as I could, but I was already reaching the point where I could barely move.

In the end, I could only get the remaining parts within reach into the box.

‘It’s melting....’

My vision blurred, then brightened again.

The trembling vibration of the conveyor belt. Then....

<br>

My body was swallowed into the machine.

<br>

Complete darkness.

“…….”

<br>

*Whiiish.*

<br>

I heard the sound of some kind of mechanical device operating.

Through my faintly returning vision, I could see blue, yellow, and red lights moving around, sparkling like tiny incandescent bulbs....

A sensation of being filled and replenished.

*‘...Material injection.’*

Something was being sprayed over my body.

Like air being pumped into a balloon or material being injected into a stress ball, something was being poured into me, filling out my body as my exterior was coated over.

Sparkles danced before my eyes.

Sparkle, sparkle, sparkle, sparkle, sparkle, sparkle, sparkle, sparkle, sparkle, sparkle, sparkle, sparkle, sparkle, sparkle, sparkle, sparkle, sparkle, sparkle, sparkle, sparkle, sparkle, sparkle, sparkle, sparkle, sparkle, sparkle, sparkle, sparkle, sparkle, sparkle, sparkle, sparkle, sparkle, sparkle, sparkle, sparkle, sparkle

<br>

*Whiiiiir—*

<br>

My vision brightened.

<br>

I realized that, at some point, I had been neatly packaged inside a box, dressed in a clean work uniform, and sent out from the conveyor belt.

“…….”

My mind was clear.

I looked at my reflection in the transparent plastic covering.

Thin, but clean and pale.

And around my neck was a newly printed, spotless sticker....

![Official Illustration Translated by Beiruu](ch404_2.webp)

“…….”

I opened the top and climbed out.

I pulled the box that had come out before mine down from the belt. The jolt was enough to wake Assistant Manager Jin Nasol.

“...Assistant Manager.”

“…….”

She touched her collarbone, then slowly turned to me, her eyes quiet and unreadable.

“Did we get recycled again?”

“Yes.”

“I see.”

......

In the silence that followed, we found a small emergency door for employees at the back of the machine.

It was just after the conveyor belt started moving again, finally carrying all the remaining Workers out of the facility.

<br>

*Click.*

<br>

The door opened.

“You’re here? Good. You were waiting right where you were supposed to.”

It was Baek Saheon.

The moment he saw us, he spoke quickly, caught somewhere between relief and excitement.

“Come on, get out. I’ll send the security team ahead—”

And then—

He stopped.

His eyes fixed on my neck.

……

“Why do you have a sticker—“

“Baek Saheon.”

I spoke softly.

“Have you read the manual for the Worker processing procedure?”

“...Why are you asking?”

“Do you want to read it now?”

At my calm question, the colour gradually drained from Baek Saheon’s face as he pulled out his electronic pad.

He had probably spent the time waiting for us looking over the facility's overall operating system, but there was no reason for him to rush through the procedures following Worker processing or the origins of the facility.

It wasn't his responsibility, after all. What mattered was finding the right time and method to get us out.

And so....

As Baek Saheon began checking the ‘Detailed Information’ on his pad, all expression disappeared from his face.

……

……

“A Darkness?”

“Baek Saheon.”

I grabbed him by the shoulders.

“We can't get out of here.”

“…….”

“If we remove the sticker, we die....”

Baek Saheon’s eyes shifted toward the sticker on my neck.

“And if we aren't recycled, we'll die even after a hundred days.”

……

……

“What are you talking about?”

His pupils trembled violently before his eyes soon turned cold and still.

“What is this... What the hell is this....”

“I’m sorry.”

“Sorry? And what the hell am I supposed to do with that? If this was going to happen, you shouldn't have brought me into this in the first place! You already dragged me into it, so why are you saying that now...?!”

Thump. Baek Saheon grabbed me by the collar, panic erupting into a burst of incoherent anger in his eyes.

“Don’t give up! You’re still alive, and you can still move! It’s not too late yet—“

“I’m not giving up.”

“...!!”

“Why would I give up?”

I looked straight into Baek Saheon’s enlarged pupils and spoke quietly.

“We can still try something.”

“Try what...?”

......

“You said it yourself. This branch has an experimental nature.”

<br>

#f#—I’ve heard that, despite its poor conditions, it’s highly efficient. They’re good at recycling resources, and they don’t use up many supplies… There were even rumors that it was a sort of experimental branch.#f#

They adopted unconventional operating methods that differed from Headquarters, while maintaining the same straightforward efficiency of simply throwing people in, like Headquarters used to do in the past.

And that wasn't all.

“It also means this is a place where people can receive unusually rapid promotions if they produce exceptional results. Ho Yuwon, who was a Supervisor here, was promoted all the way to a director at Headquarters because of his achievements. Right?”

“…….”

So.

“I’m going to produce completely ridiculous results over the next week.”

“...!”

There was no point in trying to stay unnoticed anymore.

*‘I would just die after thirty days, anyway.’*

If that was all that awaited me....

I might as well bet everything.

<br>

If I can't escape,

then I’ll take control instead.

<br>

“Can you not leave out my achievements? Just report them to the higher-ups exactly as they are... so I can get promoted.”

“…….”

I spoke each word with absolute conviction.

<br>

“Instead of a Worker, I’m going to become a regular employee.”
