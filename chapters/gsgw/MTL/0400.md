# Chapter 399

I blinked.

The breath that had been lodged in my throat, so tight it felt like it would burst from my lungs, slowly began to settle.

*'Seagulls....'*

At daybreak, a vast blue sky greeted me with an overwhelming sense of openness.

Outside.

The moment we escaped the Namhae Branch.

“…….”

“…….”

“…You're in your right mind, aren't you?”

“…Yeah.”

I slowly propped myself up.

Only now that the adrenaline was fading did my whole body begin screaming in protest. My overworked muscles had finally gone on strike, leaving me unsteady. 

*'Holy shit.'*

I really almost died.

I lifted a hand to run it through my hair… That damn vodka!

I frantically wiped the alcohol and blood off my hand and onto the ground. Beside me, Assistant Manager Jin Nasol watched me like I'd completely lost my mind.

“Hey. Pull the glass out first.”

“……Yes.”

I took a pack of wet wipes from my backpack, cleaned my palm, and picked out the larger shards of glass.
Only then did the stinging begin.

*'Never thought the vodka I bought in the Offy-Duty Zone would almost end up being used to kill myself.'*

Watching me obsessively wipe away the stains on my hand, Jin Nasol, looking utterly exhausted, closed her eyes and lay flat on the ground.

Baek Saheon remained seated nearby, quietly watching us.

Before I knew it, I laughed.

Maybe it was because we'd actually made it out.

“Looks like your gamble paid off, Baek Saheon.”

“…What?”

“Taking the more dangerous route paid off. We got something extra out of it, and we escaped.”

“……!”

I meant every word.

“And thanks to you, we made it through a few moments where we could’ve died.”

“…….”

“Thanks for saving us.”

Baek Saheon simply stared at me, momentarily at a loss.

“How do you feel?”

…….

“Not bad.”

<br>

*Ssshhh.*

<br>

The waves rolled gently onto the shore, breaking along the coastline.

“Then that's good enough.”

Baek Saheon, who I expected would usually say something like, 'Good enough? Get the items ready,' simply stood there in silence, his back to us as he looked out at the sea.

*'...It's peaceful.'*

It was a brief moment of rest.

We sprawled out on the ground, taking a short break until some strength returned to our bodies.

“Should we figure out where we are?”

“We should.”

With a grunt, I pushed myself upright.

Only then did I realise I'd been lying on damp, black soil.

No... if we're by the sea, is this sand?

*'Black sand....'*

It felt strangely familiar.

Well, they did call it the Namhae Branch. Turns out it really was near the ocean.

I brushed the dirt from my clothes and stood. As my unsteady vision cleared, the shoreline and the stretch of coast before me came into view.

*'Wow.'*

Black rocks covered the beach....

Wait.

Black sand. Black rocks?

*'Basalt.'*

And when it came to basalt, there was one place in Korea that immediately came to mind.

I looked around until I spotted a natural rock formation further along the coastline.

Ah.

“Isn't that...?”

“What is it?”

“Yongmeori Coast.”[1]{Yongmeori Coast (용머리 해안) is a famous volcanic coastal formation on Jeju Island. Its name means 'Dragon Head Coast' because the cliffs resemble a dragon’s head.}

“……!”

Wow.

“We're on Jeju Island.”

The branch itself had been located on Jeju.

*‘Well… I suppose Namhae really did mean South Sea, huh.’*

Still, when someone says Namhae, wouldn’t you usually think of the region in Gyeongsang Province?[2]{Namhae (남해) literally means 'southern sea,' but it is also the name of a coastal county in South Gyeongsang Province, South Korea.}

I never imagined we weren't even on the mainland. Even Baek Saheon looked a little taken aback.

“No, we only travelled by car.... You're telling me we're on an island?”

“Could the car itself have been a Darkness?”

“…Can’t rule that out.”

Otherwise, that would mean Daydream did something insane like drilling an underwater tunnel, and I really didn’t think they’d go that far.

“Assistant Manager, what was it like when you were transported as a worker?”

“Don’t know. I don’t remember.”

It felt like some dream you’d have after taking melatonin.

Either way, the three of us ended up getting to experience something that had never once been part of our lives: standing on a Jeju beach.

…….

Baek Saheon, who had been silently letting the sea breeze wash over him, suddenly spoke.

“I’ve never seen Yongmeori Coast before.”

“You never went on a school trip?”

Baek Saheon scoffed.

“I went once, and that was to Gyeongju.[3]{Gyeongju is a city in North Gyeongsang Province, South Korea. Many historical sites are preserved there, making it one of the most common destinations for student field trips.} …I mean I’ve never even been to Jeju Island before.”

Considering the circumstances of the village where he grew up, I figured he probably wouldn’t have been allowed to go on school trips, even if he’d attended high school somewhere else.

“Either way, you’re seeing it now.”

“…That’s true.”

We walked along the coastline.

No matter how I looked at it, this really did seem like the kind of place that should have a sign reading, ‘Danger. Do Not Enter.’ But even so, seeing the houses in the distance, along with the sandy beach and the clear blue sea, was incredibly....

Reassuring.

*'…The outside world.'*

It felt like we were connected to society again.

Of course, judging by our clothes and current state, the first place we'd probably be taken after going anywhere was a police station.

“What are you going to do now?”

“I’ll have to inform them that the Namhae Branch has been destroyed. Isn’t that a completely honest report? Of course, once things are under control, I’ll have to return to the branch and check things for myself.”

“Wait.”

Assistant Manager Jin Nasol cut in.

“Distrube things first.”

Ah.

<br>

…And so, we sat down together by the shore, eating a few pieces of candy and chocolate from my backpack while immediately taking out the items and the Dream Solution.

*'This is brutal....'*

Part of me wondered if we really had to go this far, but the two Daydream employees showed no hesitation.

First, we decided to put the Dream Solution aside for now, since Baek Saheon would convert it into points (after deducting his fee) and divide it up.

Next came the items.

Baek Saheon said with a smug smile,

“Seven items. Well… since you participated too, we’ll split them. Two should be enough for you, wouldn’t you say?”

“Yeah.”

“…?!”

Honestly, if it weren’t for this guy in the vertical shaft, I would’ve already become an instrument by now. No matter how we divided things, I was prepared to accept it.

*‘No, seriously. He’s the one who suggested it first. Why is he acting surprised now that I agreed?’*

I even made a counteroffer.

“I said I’d give you the glasses if you helped me get out of the branch safely. If you're okay with it, how about I just take one item instead?”

“…Fine.”

Okay.

And so, I chose a single one.

“What are you taking?”

Of course, like most unidentified items, it was impossible to tell what they did just from appearance alone.

A carbonated drink character keychain, a golf ball marked with a red X, a mechanical wristwatch, a pure gold dessert spoon, a checkered handkerchief, a ring case….

I’d probably need to do some additional work to figure out their abilities, but there was one item I had already used before.

“Happy Maker....”

For the record, the moment she saw it, Assistant Manager Jin Nasol snatched it away.

“The rewards include my share too, don’t they? Any complaints?”

“No….”

Please make good use of it….

*‘There are only three left in the container, so she probably won’t get addicted….’*

Phew.

“What are you choosing? If you can’t decide, just pay me a little. I have a way of figuring out how items work.”

Hmm.

“No. I’ll just take this.”

I picked up one of the remaining items.

“The ring case?”

Baek Saheon frowned.

“There wasn’t even a ring inside.”

“I know.”

Maybe whatever had been inside wasn’t a ring in the first place.

But there was one thing I knew for certain.

The marking engraved in the center of the inside of the lid had caught my eye.

*‘…It was the same marking that had been on the doorknob of the Namhae Branch’s Personnel-Only room.’*

More accurately, it looked as though only part of the original design had been borrowed and used as a logo.

If this had been an item found inside the branch, and that was how it ended up in that researcher's hands….

*'Then it could be an item connected to the branch's old central system.'*

Maybe it was because I had already escaped, but after uncovering so many clues about the branch’s secrets, I found myself wanting to come back and investigate again someday.

*‘After all, this is where I first opened my eyes.’*

Well, that could wait until I came back.

“I’ll take this, then.”

“Alright.”

Baek Saheon reconsidered his words.

“But honestly, you know this isn’t exactly a good deal for me, right? The fact that you couldn’t give up the glasses and still insisted on taking one of the items means you must’ve really needed it. You really are completely broke, aren’t you?”

“Ah. I was actually going to give you something else instead of the item.”

“? What…?”

“This.”

I removed the ‘equipment’ I was still wearing on my arm over my work uniform and used the knife Assistant Manager Jin Nasol handed me to cut it open.

Just as expected.

The cylindrical object I could feel inside was a Dream Collector.

So I finally got to see the real thing.

“Here.”

“…!!”

Its core design was incredibly simple, even compared to the one I had used at headquarters.

*‘Looks like it doesn’t have any recording or transmission functions.’*

If anything, that was a good thing.

And best of all, inside was a golden liquid shimmering with a holographic glow, gently sloshing around.

High-grade Dream Solution.

I now had two bottles.

“I’ll give you half of this.”

“…….”

Baek Saheon’s eyes trembled.

This should be a pretty decent deal….

“…If the company had given me a collector too, it would’ve been twice as much. Damn it!”

“…….”

Of course, that was the first thing he thought of….

“Anyway… I got it. Give it here.”

In the end, Baek Saheon eagerly took my Dream Solution. Judging by the enthusiasm in his hands, his dopamine seemed to be doing just fine.

I figured my share would probably come out to around thirty or forty thousand points… I’d have to think carefully about how to use it.

Anyway, after sorting out the distribution, we stood back up.

I looked at the completely sealed escape hatch, still visible in the distance.

“The branch… do you think they’ll be able to deal with this?”

Those entities had appeared there  for what was supposedly a performance, so I assumed they would leave once it was over… but even so, I couldn’t shake the uneasiness.

Every time I thought about the terrible situation unfolding right below us.

Every time I imagined what the people still inside must be going through….

“Well, your concern doesn’t really change anything for me. The branch manager here will have to handle it. As for me, I’ll just monitor the situation for a day or two until things are settled and submit my report.”

“…….”

I wasn't worried about that... but if he was fine, I supposed that was a good thing too.

*‘Phew.’*

Either way, the way back in was sealed shut, and there was realistically nothing more I could do.

“For now, let’s get out of here.”

“You two should do something about those clothes first.”

With that, we headed toward the nearby houses.

Thanks to Baek Saheon buying us some clothes from a nearby shop with the cash he had, we were finally able to walk into a small home-style restaurant looking reasonably presentable.

*‘Huu.’*

No longer wearing the worker uniforms and blending in among ordinary people, the sense of freedom I felt was greater than I expected.

Over hot braised cutlassfish and sea urchin seaweed soup, we finally had a chance to catch our breath.
Assistant Manager Jin Nasol absentmindedly took a few bites of her now-cooled rice before asking me,

“…Does your face hurt?”

“Ah.”

Come to think of it, Baek Saheon had kicked the same spot where I’d been hit in the bathroom. 

If he hadn’t done that, I probably would have died, so I had no complaints. Besides, it was already swollen, so it didn’t make much of a difference.

“I’m fine.”

“Then that’s enough.”

“…….”

Gathering courage through the power of carbohydrates, I asked,

“Assistant Manager… what are your plans going forward?”

“You told me to look into items or Darknesses with pain-relieving effects.”

“You’re actually going to do that?”

“Don’t know.”

Tap. Assistant Manager Jin Nasol placed her chopsticks across the top of her water cup.

“I was only at that branch for two weeks in the first place. If you were in my situation, would you really have made plans for the future?”

“You could have. Um… If you don’t mind, could I ask what you did before joining Daydream?”

“Similar work.”

“Pardon?”

“…Something like a mercenary. Taking requests and retrieving things isn't all that different.”

“Ah. I see.”

No wonder she was so physically capable and so skilled at exploration. She'd already been in that line of work before.

“That's impressive.”

“Shut up.”

She said that, but Assistant Manager Jin Nasol didn’t look embarrassed or annoyed at all.

I simply smiled and gave a small nod.

“Yeah.”

It was strange. Before, I never would have imagined having conversations like this with Assistant Manager Jin Nasol.

*'I think we've gotten a little closer... in our own way.'*

Surprisingly, I found myself feeling the same way about the person sitting across from me.

Baek Saheon.

Somehow, I didn’t know when it happened, but this time around, it really felt like something between us had changed.

He'd even started showing me an oddly generous amount of goodwill.

As if on cue, he spoke.

“Hey, what are you going to do now?”

Hmm.

“I know someone at the Disaster Management Bureau, so I was thinking about getting in touch with them.”
“…You're not planning to go back up to Seoul?”

“I don't really know.”

I answered honestly.

“I'd rather stay out of Director Cheong's sight, so I'm planning to avoid headquarters for now.”

“Ah.”

Baek Saheon seemed to think it over for a moment before giving an unexpected answer.

“That... might actually work out.”

Huh?

“Really? Why?”

“Well... Director Cheong hasn't been around lately.”

…!

“After you disappeared, Director Cheong stayed quiet for about a year. Then, starting the year before last, she started showing her face at the company again nonstop. Well, that was around the time she became the sole director of the Development Department.”

But this year...

“She hasn't shown up at the company for about six months.”

Wait.

“You mean she quit?”

“There wasn't any official announcement saying she'd been removed as a director. But it's so obvious that... the general assumption is that she's gone into hiding.”

“……I see.”

That in itself was suspicious enough to make me uneasy, but Baek Saheon's point was clear.

With Director Cheong no longer actively controlling the company, I would have a little more room to breathe where Daydream was concerned.

“Then... I guess I'd have a bit more freedom to move around.”

“Right?”

Baek Saheon took a drink of water before the corner of his mouth curled upward.

“Must be nice. Sitting here eating a meal while getting to hear all about the company's internal affairs.”

“…I guess.”

I rubbed the back of my neck.

Then, honestly, I said,

“If there's any information you need, just let me know. I'll do my best to find it.”

“…Hah. At least you've finally developed a bit of a conscience.”

Baek Saheon turned his head away.

“Well, I don't need anything right now. But when I do, you'd better hand it over the moment I ask.”

“Alright.”

He's really difficult.

I absentmindedly rubbed the back of my neck again, my fingers brushing against the Namhae Branch sticker that was still stuck there.

Baek Saheon then added, looking slightly pleased with himself,

“That sticker probably won’t come off on its own. Apparently, the branch had it custom-made.”

Ah.

“They said it’s because it went through some kind of special processing. Just leave it for now. Once things settle down, we can sneak some chemicals out of the branch and deal with it.”

“…Thanks?”

“Why did you put a question mark on that? Seriously, that’s ridiculous.”

“Thanks.”

“…….”

Baek Saheon downed another glass of water.

Since I was genuinely grateful, I decided not to question him any further.

<br>

“Let’s get some rest now.”

That day, exhausted to the point of collapse, we found a reasonably clean guesthouse in town.

Then we went straight to sleep in broad daylight.

The location had been chosen because Baek Saheon needed to see how the situation at the branch would be handled, and honestly, Assistant Manager Jin Nasol and I weren’t exactly in any condition to travel far either.

But my heart already felt free.

I had some cash, some points secured, and items of my own.

*‘First, if I want to get back to the mainland… do I have to take a boat?’*

I wasn’t sure if I could use my ID. If I could, I’d just take a plane.

And while we stayed here for the next few days, I should casually ask Baek Saheon one more time and get an answer.

About how the people here were doing—especially my acquaintances who used to work at Daydream.

*‘Let’s hang in there.’*

Thinking over my plans, I slowly drifted off to sleep.

…….

…….

<br>

“Hk—!”

My eyes snapped open.

Breath—

I couldn’t breathe!

“Kh, k-kh!”

“What now…?”

Baek Saheon, who had been sleeping in the bed beside mine, woke up with an irritated groan. But when he saw me, he froze.

I heard footsteps rushing toward me. I couldn’t even lift my head.

*‘Ggkk, a-air…!’*

This was insane.

I tried to tear away whatever was gripping my neck with both hands, but Baek Saheon knocked them away and checked what was there.

“What is this?”

“Wh—”

I couldn’t even finish the words. A sharp pain surged through my throat.

“The sticker!”

“…!!”

I tried to tear it off again, but the sticker wouldn’t even budge, as if it had fused with my skin.

“Kh—”

I choked out a breath and pushed myself up. Then I immediately looked into the bedroom mirror.

“…!!”

My neck in the reflection.

The sticker had turned completely black, and the area around my neck was covered in bulging red veins and a rash.

And…

The words written on the sticker had changed.
