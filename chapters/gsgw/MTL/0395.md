# Chapter 394

The next morning.

Breakfast was handed out again under the guise of being a ‘special meal.’ This time, it was a salad bun and banana milk.

The workers were happy with the unexpectedly good food, but their attention was focused more on the pink capsules that came with it.

I picked up the pink, translucent soft gel that had been distributed once again.

“So It’s a Darkness.”

“Yes.”

Like me, Assistant Manager Jin Nasol only pretended to swallow it, letting it slip away instead. Before doing so, she turned it over in her hand, examining it with clear interest.

Nearby, the workers’ voices could be heard.

“I’m telling you, look at this. The inflammation is completely gone.”

“Whoa, did you really recover after getting infected by a parasite? Why does your skin look so much better?”

“My bruises are gone! Oh my!”

Of course, there were probably many among them who were simply caught up in the atmosphere, mistaking their own feelings for actual changes, or exaggerating what they noticed.

Even so, the pattern was becoming clear.

“These capsules are actually improving people’s condition?”

“I-I’m not sure….”

As the voices around us grew louder, I slipped back into my usual foolish act.

“But I do kind of feel like I’m a little better too…. Like my face might be a bit less swollen…?”

“You’re still a complete mess.”

“……”

I know that….

But could you at least play along a little?

Just hearing the way I spoke seemed to give Assistant Manager Jin Nasol a headache, and she furrowed her brow.

“Why do you keep letting yourself get beaten up in the first place? What do you think you’re carrying that knife for?”

Because i’m scared.

“Hahaha….”

I gave an awkward laugh and looked away.

For reference, Assistant Manager Jin Nasol had been ‘temporarily reassigned’ to the original Y quarters.

*‘Seriously… I’m the only one stuck with the worst room…’*

Because our quarters were different, Jin Nasol and I couldn’t talk as freely as before. Still, we were able to exchange information quickly whenever we found places without CCTV or wiretapping.

And since we were together more often, the workers had also stopped throwing out as many hostile remarks just to vent their frustrations, or openly cursing and avoiding me.

*‘As expected, even a lackey needs a thug around to have any meaning.’*

“Still… I’m glad the food is good.”

“…Ha.”

I threw out meaningless remarks while keeping an eye on the workers around me.

They all seemed to be in better spirits, looking happier not only because their health had improved, but also because of the tasty breakfast they had been given.

There was no one who had quietly disappeared, nor anyone showing strange reactions that suggested they had become contaminated.

“……”

Maybe it wasn't a particularly powerful Darkness after all. Maybe it was just some kind of clinical trial for an item.

*‘Come to think of it, with a headquarters employee here for an inspection, they probably wouldn’t do something as reckless as using their entire workforce for a dangerous experiment.’*

I decided to stop watching for the time being.

<br>

[Proceeding to the workplace.]

<br>

The day off was delayed another day due to the ‘renovation of the Off-Duty Zone,’ but in exchange, only relatively easy work had been scheduled for the morning.

This time as well, I had entered the same harmless coin-purse ghost story as before—the kind that was practically a free pass.

Once again, nothing unusual happened during it. After receiving my daily wage, I quietly returned to my assigned quarters and continued digging through the dirt wall.

All while silently waiting to see how things developed.

<br>

And then, the next day—

“Look at this. Wow, my skin feels like a baby’s bottom.”

“Wait, didn’t you say you had arthritis? The pain is gone?”

“Huh? The swelling’s cleared up completely.”

The effects of the pills continued.

In fact, among those who had taken them for another day, there were even people whose old scars or long-standing ailments had completely disappeared.

Of course, the effects clearly varied from person to person. It was almost like how some people’s bodies responded well to certain medicines while others didn’t.

But for those it worked on especially well, their complexion brightened to the point where, as people casually put it, they looked like they had a completely different ‘glow.’

*‘A medicine that improves a person’s condition…’*

Since that was the very nature of medicine itself, it brought far too many possibilities to mind.

For example, what if it wasn’t actually medicine at all, but some kind of parasitic ghost story where the ‘egg’ attached itself to the stomach lining, grew there, and eventually replaced the person?

*‘No way… They didn’t get inspired by the parasite incident and use it as a pretext to do something like this, did they?’*

Nothing had even happened, yet I was getting worked up for no reason.

I steadied myself despite the uneasy feeling in my stomach and held back a sigh.

*‘Good thing I fixed up my appearance just in case.’*

To avoid drawing suspicion, I had deliberately made an effort to look cleaner and more presentable than I had yesterday.

Even just that alone made me look a little healthier.

After finishing my meal—

<br>

[Opening the Off-Duty Zone.]

<br>

“Woooo!”

At last, the day off had arrived.

We had it easy for the past two days, but now that we finally had a proper break, the workers flooded toward the Off-Duty Zone in excitement.

*‘If I don’t want to stand out, I should go too.’*

I kept glancing over at Assistant Manager Jin Nasol.

“Um… if it’s okay…”

“I’m not going.”

“……”

“……”

“I-I’d really like to see it with you… Would that be okay?”

In the end, Assistant Manager Jin Nasol came with me.

She looked absolutely furious about it.

*‘I’m sorry.’*

But don’t we need to check whether we left any traces in the Off-Duty Zone…?

*‘Besides, if I go in by myself, people are going to pick a fight with me!’*

I broke into a cold sweat and quietly looked away as Assistant Manager Jin Nasol swallowed a painkiller. I was already grateful enough that she’d agreed to come with me—I really shouldn’t push my luck.

And so, once again, we entered the Off-Duty Zone.

<br>

*Step.*

<br>

*‘Wow.’*

With people filling every corner, the Off-Duty Zone almost felt like a night market.

Even the shabby alley-like corridors had a certain charm now that they were bustling with people.

I also noticed that some of the places were no longer unmanned.

“Come on, let’s grab a drink!”

“Of course, of course.”

There were employees carrying plates of meat around the tavern, and someone selling lottery tickets at the lottery stand.

There were even staff assigned to work on the workers’ day off. For a place with security this tight, it was a surprisingly strange sight.

One line, in particular, stood out for how absurdly long it was.

*‘Hm?’*

I looked toward the end of the queue.

…It led to the Religious Activity Room.

“That’s the line for fortunes!”

“Man, it’s so long.”

Fortunes.

*‘…I did want to take another look at the Religious Activity Room.’*

I was also curious whether the staff working in the Off-Duty Zone could open the ‘Authorized Personnel Only’ doors.

“Um… I-I’d like to have my fortune read too…”

I said it casually enough for Assistant Manager Jin Nasol to hear, while subtly edging closer to the corridor beside the queue.

Then—

A hand shot out from the secluded hallway, grabbed my arm, and yanked me inside.

“…!!”

Instinctively, I tried to pull away.

“Wh—”

“Quiet!”

It was a familiar voice.

I immediately stopped resisting. Still gripping my arm, they pulled me straight into an abandoned construction area where no one else was around.

When I looked up, someone was standing in front of an ‘Authorized Personnel Only’ door.

“Even the Off-Duty Zone looks like a dump. I suppose it’s cost-effective when you call a place like this a recreational facility.”

It was Baek Saheon. Again.

“Section Chief.”

“You can speak normally. There aren’t any CCTV cameras here. They said the system’s still under repairs… Honestly, for their own company to have this many security holes—”

He had been speaking rather smugly, but the moment he spotted Assistant Manager Jin Nasol storming after me, he abruptly fell silent.

“……”

Then he jerked his chin toward her.

“I don’t have anything to say to you, so you can head back. I’m collecting witness statements about what happened in the Off-Duty Zone.”

“……You.”

Assistant Manager Jin Nasol’s eyes suddenly sharpened.

It seemed she had recognized Baek Saheon.

*‘Damn it.’*

I quickly stepped between them.

“There’s no need for that. We’ll be sharing information later anyway.”

“……”

Baek Saheon looked back and forth between me and Jin Nasol before twisting his mouth into a grimace.

“You told her?”

“Told her what?”

“About the pills.”

“…Yeah. You said not to take them.”

“Exactly. So why would you tell her too?”

What?

“It’s bad enough that you have to keep quiet about it. Why double the risk of getting caught!”

“……”

“And you’re passing along information I gave you? The least you could’ve done was ask for my permission first.”

“Your permission?”

Baek Saheon flinched.

…I hadn't meant to throw his own words back at him.

I let out a sigh.

“Like you said, I’m a worker right now. I don’t exactly have the opportunity—or the freedom—to walk up to someone from headquarters and ask for permission in the middle of all this.”

“……”

“And you don’t have to worry that much about us getting caught. If she were the kind of person who couldn’t keep it under control and would end up exposing us to the company, I wouldn’t have told her anything in the first place, no matter how much we were cooperating.”

“…Ah.”

Baek Saheon’s single visible eye shifted between me and Assistant Manager Jin Nasol.

“So that’s how you think of me too?”

What?

“Fine. Do whatever you want. It’s not like I’m the one who’ll end up dead.”

“Baek Saheon.”

“Forget it.”

Taking a slow breath, Baek Saheon suddenly tossed something he had been holding.

I caught it on instinct.

…It was a clipboard. Several sheets of A4 paper fluttered on top.

“Read it. It’s this week’s worker schedule.”

…!

“Thank you.”

“……”

Leaning against the wall with his arms crossed, Baek Saheon turned his head away.

*‘Was that why he came over? To give me this?’*

I’m grateful for it, but… I honestly have no idea how I’m supposed to deal with him without us butting heads.

It was just as I was quietly sighing to myself—

“Hey.”

“Yes?”

Assistant Manager Jin Nasol’s flat voice broke the silence.

“Looks like I’m not needed here.”

“……”

“I’ll wait outside. Do what you need to do.”

…Come to think of it, Assistant Manager Jin Nasol had entered ghost stories several times before as Baek Saheon’s superior.

But now, the junior from the Regular Team she had once supervised had become a Section Chief at headquarters, while she was standing before him as a day laborer.

Baek Saheon hadn’t gone out of his way to mock her to her face, but the situation itself… was more than enough to make things uncomfortable.

I figured being around him couldn’t have been easy for her.

“The way he talks is irritating.”

“……”

Well.

Let’s just say I was right about it being uncomfortable.

I stared blankly at Jin Nasol, who had moved a short distance away and leaned against the wall, keeping watch like a guard.

Somehow, I felt a little better.

*‘Heh…’*

I quickly lowered my gaze to the documents Baek Saheon had given me.

*‘Hmm.’*

<br>
 
<p align="center"> Worker Schedule <p align="left">

<br>

It wasn’t exactly the kind of schedule you’d expect from a major corporation. The format lacked both consistency and professionalism, making it feel oddly amateurish.

It simply listed the days of the week, followed by a rough outline of which workers would be assigned to which tasks on each date.

I noticed that I had been listed as Z999 again.

Well, I suppose they're trying pretty hard to keep my Dream Essence productivity under wraps....

*‘Ah. Too late for that.’*

Especially since the headquarters Section Chief and I happened to be from the same hiring batch.

I skimmed through the pages, estimating the danger level based on the descriptions of the Darknesses I would be entering.

Thankfully, they were all fairly easy assignments.

*‘Phew.’*

Up to this point, everything was fine.
The problem was the very last page.

<br>

<p align="center"> Worker Experiment Proposal <p align="left">

<br>

“…!”

+$
To improve efficiency, a trial work experiment will be conducted next week.
$+

My attention immediately sharpened.

Moving past the dull, formal introduction explaining the purpose of the experiment, I reached the section describing how it would actually be carried out.

+$
One Qterw-■-■■■ capsule was distributed with breakfast.

To ensure successful administration into the human body, censored information regarding the 'parasitic infection' from the recent containment breach incident is used to encourage consumption through heightened vigilance.
$+

I already knew that much.

“If you’re finished reading, hand it over.”

“……”

“Hello?”

The next line was the problem.
+$
Qterw-■-■■■ takes the form of a pink liquid-filled soft gel capsule, and as an additional security measure, the surface markings on the capsule (the letters ‘BB’) were ordered to be removed.

The security team is responsible for carrying out this procedure.
$+

My eyes snapped away from the paper as if I’d been struck by lightning.

“What’s wrong?”

“I know this item.”

“…!”

Seeing my pale expression, Baek Saheon asked in a tone that suggested he didn’t understand.

“What is it? Is it one of those cases where it makes you healthier first, only for you to die later?”

“No.”

Still, he wasn’t entirely wrong about one thing. This capsule did appear to improve the condition of those who took it.

I had thought the same thing.

That was why I’d taken the time to tidy up my appearance.

But I’d gotten it the wrong way around.

“It doesn’t improve your health.”

“Huh?”

“It improves your appearance.”

“…!”

A medicine that makes you beautiful.
