# Chapter 393

The morning after I ran into Baek Saheon in the bathroom—

“S-Section Chief!”

Once again, I watched him stride through the workers’ corridor during roll call as though he owned the place.

It looked like he was finishing the inspection that had been interrupted yesterday.

Naturally, he gave no indication that he knew me.

“Th-That’s the end of the corridor! There’s nothing over there!”

“Yes, I know.”

The Section Chief from headquarters walked the length of the corridor, his gaze sweeping over the workers lined up outside their rooms before briefly checking the workers’ quarters. When he reached me, he passed by without so much as a glance.

Then, at the very last room—mine—he casually turned to leave…

…only to stop for a moment.

“……”

“……”

Baek Saheon’s gaze shifted from me to the room behind me and back again. For the briefest moment, I could’ve sworn one of his eyebrows twitched beneath the mask.

I couldn’t exactly blame him. Seriously… this was no place for a person to live.

On top of that, my face had swollen up even more overnight, making me practically unrecognizable.

*‘And thanks to those bills, I don’t even have any cash…’*

I’m practically worse off than a beggar.

Just as that thought crossed my mind—

Baek Saheon, standing with the CCTV behind him, moved only his lips.

’…!’

So that only I could read them.

<br>

*‘Don’t eat.’*

<br>

“…….”

What was that supposed to mean?

“Y-Yes! This is the end of the Workers’ Corridor, and… well, that’s about it. Haha… Thankfully, the work areas weren’t affected by the parasite outbreak.”

As I watched the supervisor hurriedly escort Baek Saheon away, the thought came to me almost reflexively.

Did my face swell up so much that he thought I was chewing something?

Of course, that wasn’t what he meant.

What he actually meant… I figured it out soon enough.

<br>

“Time for lunch~”

It was when breakfast was handed out after roll call.

Under the pretense of ‘a special meal to comfort everyone after the parasite incident,’ every worker received the exact same meal.

And honestly… it looked surprisingly good.

*‘The rice ball actually has a nice shine to it…’*

I was given a white rice ball filled with tuna mayo and stir-fried kimchi, a packet of roasted seaweed, and a plum drink.

There was one more thing sitting beside the foil-wrapped rice ball, though.

*’…Medicine?’*

The pink capsule was transparent enough to see through, looking like a liquid-filled soft gel.

I held it up to the light. There was a faint scratch running across the middle…

<br>

[This is parasite medication. Please make sure to take one every day for the next few days. This is being distributed as a precaution, just in case.]

<br>

One pill had been issued to everyone.

“At last. They’re finally doing something sensible.”

“Do we just take it with water?”

The workers grumbled among themselves, but they all seemed to think it was a reasonable measure, and one after another they popped the capsules into their mouths.

<br>

[Yes, yes. Just don’t chew it. The medicine loses its effectiveness if it comes into contact with saliva.]

<br>

“……”

If the medicine really lost its effectiveness the moment it came into contact with saliva, then there was no way it would’ve been developed as something taken orally in the first place.

I realized it immediately.

*‘So this is what Baek Saheon meant when he told me not to eat.’*

“……”

Without showing any reaction, I pretended to swallow the capsule along with the drink, letting it slip into my palm instead.

Then I tucked it away in the deepest part of my pocket.

<br>

* * *

<br>

After finishing my morning duties, I watched the workers closely on my way back.

…Nothing seemed out of the ordinary, but I still couldn’t shake the uneasy feeling.

“……”

<br>

[Worker Z999, collect your daily wage.]

<br>

However, when I went to receive my pay, an unexpected bit of satisfaction came with it.

“Y-You’re… giving it back?”

To my surprise, the branch security team employee who handed me my daily wage also returned all the money they had taken from me through yesterday’s charge.

They had me sign the bill, which had the following statement written on it: ‘Due to deficiencies found in the grounds for the charge, this bill is hereby deemed invalid.’

*‘Oh.’*

I deliberately scribbled a sloppy signature that looked like initials but didn’t actually mean anything, then took the stack of cash back.

Somehow, it felt like free money, which put me in a slightly better mood….

*‘After the ridiculous stunt I pulled on those X guys yesterday, they probably won’t come trying to squeeze money out of me again today.’*

I should put it somewhere safe. I'll need money to spend once I get out.

And since there was no additional work scheduled for the afternoon, I was able to return to my room early.

Not that there was anything in there worth calling a place to rest.

“Ha.”

I held back a sigh as I looked around the room.

The mattress had started to smell rotten, and the poor conditions of the cramped space hadn’t improved in the slightest.

Looking at the unfinished dirt wall, the pit they used as a substitute for a bathroom, and the dead bugs and cobwebs scattered around, I swallowed a groan.

*‘The Off-Duty Zone opens tomorrow, right?’*

Wasn’t it supposed to open today? Did they delay it by a day because of maintenance? I’d rather go there and rest instead….

But I did have something to do, so having the afternoon free actually worked out.

Because I had already decided to thoroughly search this room.

*‘Let’s do it now.’*

I’d even deliberately waited a day so it wouldn't look suspicious.

From the backpack sitting in the corner, I took out the miscellaneous little items one by one—like a child pulling out treasures to comfort himself while alone.

It was all a performance very deliberately done with the CCTV in mind.

Finally, I grabbed the glasses case.

*‘Good.’*

Just as I was about to take it out—

<br>

*Thud.*

<br>

Someone opened the door behind me.

“…!!”

Who was it?

I pretended to jump up in surprise and hurriedly shoved the glasses case back into my backpack.

Then I quickly turned around…

Baek Saheon was standing in the open doorway.

“……”

“……”

The door closed again.

“Wh-Who… Ah, the person from headquarters… W-What brings you here…?”

“I turned off all the CCTV cameras.”

“What can I do for you?”

“Hah.”

Baek Saheon let out a scoff, looking utterly incredulous, then walked straight into the room without hesitation.

“You really do know how to put on a convincing act.”

“Yeah. Thanks.”

“……”

“Still, being able to turn off the CCTV and all… A Section Chief from headquarters really is on another level. But are you sure this is okay?”

“Are you really putting me in the same category as the workers? Isn’t that a little arrogant?”

“If the excuse seems suspicious, people might start questioning it.”

“I told you, my situation is different from yours. I’m not a worker.”

A crooked smile spread across Section Chief Baek Saheon’s face.

“I’m conducting random spot checks on the workers’ quarters in the branch. Did you think I was wasting time here because of you? Your self-importance is impressive.”

Sure.

Well… at least he was confident.

“Either way, I can stay in this room for five minutes… no, even ten minutes, and no one will find it suspicious.”

Baek Saheon shrugged.

“Forget those unnecessary worries. …You got the money back, didn’t you?”

Huh?

“Do you mean the invalidated bill?”

“Of course. Who do you think made that happen?”

Wow.

This time, I was genuinely impressed.

*‘Thank you.’*

I really needed that money…!

But then—

“Hand it over.”

“Hm?”

“Half of that money is mine, obviously. I hope you set it aside.”

“It’s all gone already…”

“~!”

“I’m kidding.”

“……”

“But I really don’t want to give it to you.”

Baek Saheon clenched his fist so tightly that the tendons stood out, then slowly forced it open again.

“Look at you, making such a fuss over a little money. And joking about it, too? Even if a Civil Servant embezzled—”

“Well, I’m not a Civil Servant anymore, so I suppose it’s fine.”

“……”

Baek Saheon said nothing for a while. I quietly took the opportunity to finish organizing my backpack.

Then he suddenly asked,

“So… were you originally an agent who infiltrated Daydream?”

“No.”

I answered calmly.

“Not originally. But you’re right—I was a spy.”

“……”

For some reason, the silence that followed lasted even longer.

*‘…This is awkward.’*

Only after putting the last of my things away did I continue.

“The thing you said this morning… you meant I shouldn’t take the supplements, right?”

“…I’m glad you still have enough sense to pick up on that. Yes. I was telling you not to take them.”

“……”

“Because they’re related to a Darkness.”

Of course.

While the workers remained completely unaware, they had already been sent into a new Darkness.

The Darkness we were scheduled to enter this week was probably one that used those pills as its medium.

I took the capsule still sitting in my pocket and held it up.

Baek Saheon’s gaze followed it.

“Why are you touching it with your bare hands? Throw it away.”

“What kind of Darkness is it?”

“That part was censored from the documents. They went out of their way to keep it hidden, but what does that matter? A researcher from a branch like this won’t be able to hide it from me for long.”

“…Just don’t do anything too noticeable.”

Between the bill incident and this, I wondered if he was going a little too far. But Baek Saheon only scoffed.

“You asked me to make sure you could get out of here safely in a week, didn’t you?”

I don’t remember asking for that much…?

“Now you’re acting like you’re worried about me? Forget it. Do you still think I’m just some junior employee?”

“Still, I’m just saying you should be careful. I’m allowed to worry about you, aren’t I?”

“……”

Baek Saheon spoke in a heavy, frustrated voice.

“…I really don’t understand what you’re thinking.”

That’s exactly what I wanted to say.

“……Did you infiltrate the Namhae Branch to investigate it?”

“Not exactly. But since I ended up trapped here anyway, I’ve been looking into things a little. There’s too much that feels suspicious.”

After thinking for a moment, I took the glasses case completely out of my backpack and opened it again.

It was a good opportunity to use it while the CCTV was turned off.

“For one thing, this room itself is suspicious.”

“This room?”

“Yeah.”

I glanced around the cramped space.

“So I wanted to see what it looked like before it became a workers’ quarter.”

Then I lifted the item from the open case.

A flicker of interest passed through Baek Saheon’s eye.

“…‘Before it became a workers’ quarter?’ Don’t tell me those glasses show the past?”

I nodded.

“That’s the item you mentioned yesterday, right? The first-place prize.”

“That’s right.”

A trace of greed flashed in Baek Saheon’s single visible eye.

But unlike before, it disappeared almost instantly, replaced by his usual calm expression.

His words, however, remained unchanged.

“Well, enjoy it while you can. The day you get out of here, you’ll have to politely hand it over as your escape fee.”

Dream big.

Either way, I put the glasses back on and looked through them.

Where the filthy, miserable room assigned to Worker Z999 had been…

“……!”

<br>

There was a clean, well-kept office.

<br>

*‘No way…’*

A tastefully aged yet surprisingly comfortable-looking two-seater sofa, chairs, and a solid wooden desk filled the room, while the floor itself had been kept perfectly clean.

On the desk sat a steaming drink resting on a coaster.

Most of the furnishings—including the cabinets and shelves—were made of clean metal and arranged neatly, without any unnecessary clutter. But there was one thing that stood out.

A large window.

Although it was completely covered by blinds, it was still there — an element that had no reason to exist in a sealed-off space like this, sitting there as naturally as anything else.

Almost like… a certain counseling room I knew.

“……”

I stepped closer and looked at the nameplate on the wooden desk.

<br>

Supervisor: Ho Yuwon

<br>

This was Ho Yuwon’s branch office.

*‘My god…’*

But when they reorganized this area into the workers’ corridor, they turned this office into the very last room — the worst one of all?

I hurriedly looked around more.

“What is it? What do you see?”

“Director Ho’s office. Before his promotion.”

“…?!”

“Apparently, he worked here before becoming a Director.”

“Give it to me. Let me see too.”

“Shouldn’t you know what the side effects are before you start asking for it?”

“……”

And then I realized one disappointing thing about these glasses.

*‘I can’t touch anything.’*

It was difficult to check inside drawers or cabinets.

I wondered if I could just stick my head inside and look, but it was too dark to see properly.

*‘Damn it.’*

But on the other hand, there were also spaces that could only be seen within the ‘past shown through the glasses.’

Like this place.

“……”

I lowered the glasses from my eyes.

In reality, an unfinished, filthy dirt wall was right in front of my face.

But when I looked through the lenses again, the office stretched out beyond the obstruction.

*‘It’s been covered up.’*

<br>

There was another area here.

They had simply sealed it off with a dirt barrier.

<br>

And in this current space was Director Ho’s desk, along with…

“There’s another door further in.”

“……”

A new passageway.

The wall in reality kept me from going any further, but the doorknob of the half-open door had a symbol engraved on it that I recognized.

An ‘Authorized Personnel Only’ door.

I reached into my pocket and rubbed the taped piece of doorknob I had kept.

It was the same.

*‘Found it.’*

A way to sneak into the Personnel Only area.

Candidate number one.

“Phew.”

I took the glasses off. A sharp, stabbing pain shot through me, and a little bit of blood welled up around my eyes.

“…!”

“Ah. This is the side effect. I’m fine.”

“……”

After roughly wiping away the blood, I put the glasses back into their case and tucked it away in my backpack.

“There’s nothing important left to see. Everything that looked significant was behind that dirt wall anyway. This branch has a lot hidden away… you must be enjoying yourself.”

“…Ha.”

I’m the one who should be making that kind of pained noise. Why are you the one doing it?

Come to think of it, Baek Saheon might know something.

Additional information!

“Did you happen to hear anything about the Namhae Branch on your way here?”

“Well… you do hear that each branch has its own characteristics. The Namhae Branch…”

Baek Saheon pressed his gloved hand against his forehead, his eyes narrowing.

“I’ve heard that, despite its poor conditions, it’s highly efficient. They’re good at recycling resources, and they don’t use up many supplies… There were even rumors that it was a sort of experimental branch.”

Hmm.

“What about its location?”

“They were desperately trying to keep where this place was a secret. I mean, they transport people around in tinted vehicles and all.”

So that meant he didn’t really know.

But instead of pushing further and deliberately creating an awkward atmosphere, I simply nodded.

“Makes sense. It would be hard to find out.”

“……”

“……”

An awkward silence passed between us.

“…Anyway, if I want to investigate further, it would be useful if I could secretly dig through this wall. Can you keep the CCTV off here all the time, or get rid of it somehow?”

“It’s not impossible.”

Baek Saheon crossed his arms.

“But do you really think I’m going to listen to you when you ask like that?”

There he goes again.

“If you had any common sense, shouldn’t you start by saying, ‘Please. I’m asking for your help’?”

“Yes. Please. I’m asking for your help.”

“……”

Baek Saheon’s expression became somewhat hollow…

“…Forget it. Investigation or whatever — just don’t do anything pointless. I’m not turning off the CCTV.”

<br>

*Beep beep beep.*

<br>

His wristwatch alarm went off right then, and Baek Saheon immediately silenced it.

“I’ll come back again, so don’t do anything else until then. And don’t waste the item either. I don’t like secondhand things.”

“What…?”

“I’m telling you not to throw your life away when there’s nothing you can do.”

With that, he turned and walked out the door.

“……”

Seriously… What am I supposed to make of this?

Was he trying to get under my skin, or was he actually trying to help me?

*‘I don’t even know why he’s helping me in the first place.’*

When I was communicating with him as an agent, he was somewhat friendly toward me. But I thought that would end the moment he found out I was Kim Soleum.

Well… one thing was certain: he was interested in the item. Maybe he was only acting this way because he wanted a reason to get his hands on it.

*‘Either way, it’s nice seeing someone I know again after so long.’*


I stared at the dirt wall and shrugged.

*‘Let’s just dig at it little by little while pretending to do other work, as long as my backpack covers it.’*

After making a bit of progress, I lay down on the blanket I had bought from the store and tried to sleep.

But the moment I settled in, something else naturally came to mind.

The ghost story.

*‘Tomorrow morning… Assistant Manager Jin Nasol should be discharged too. I need to tell her right away not to take the pill.’*

I had already taken it out of my pocket and hidden it as far away as possible, tucked between my other belongings.

…Because it was an unknown substance.

*‘What on earth is going to happen?’*


<br>

And then, the next day.

A change really did happen.

Just not the one I was expecting.

“Hey, hey, look at this. I’m telling you, even my old scars are completely gone.”

“Oh, damn. It’s clean as hell.”

The workers began to get healthier.
