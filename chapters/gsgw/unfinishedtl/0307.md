# Chapter 306

Without warning, I had encountered dozens of Daydream Inc. researchers inside the Segwang Special City subway station.

And now our eyes had met in this insane reality.

*'Damn it.'*

Reflexively, my hand flew to my face.

Hanging there, as always, was the mask crowned with antlers branching like tree limbs.

Which meant one thing. I was dressed exactly the same as when I had worked as Roe Deer.

*'Damn...!'*

There would definitely be researchers who recognized me.

*'I avoided entering Segwang Special City in the alien lizard body in case it caused problems.'*

Even though that monstrous strength might have been useful, I had given it up for safety's sake. After all, it might affect my sleeping consciousness.

Yet instead, I ended up facing a sudden situation far worse for my mental health.

"What?"

"The Field Exploration Team?"

The moment researchers beyond the fence began poking their heads out at the voice of the man pointing at me—

*'...Wait.'*

I turned around and switched masks.

From Roe Deer to the feline one.

The mask belonging to the identity I had newly received.

"...Huh?"

The researcher who had pointed at me stared blankly before shouting.

"W-Wait! That field employee... his mask changed!"

"What?"

"No, just now, there were definitely antlers or something! Hey! You changed your mask just now, didn't you?!"

"I'm not sure what you're talking about..."

"N-No, I definitely saw it!"

"Could it have been a hallucination?"

"A hallucination?"

"So this really is darkness after all, damn it!"

The researchers' attention quickly shifted away from the supposed Field Exploration Team employee and back toward the immediate danger surrounding them.

The researcher who had pointed at me still stared as though unable to understand what he'd seen, but I ignored him for now.

"Excuse me, but what kind of darkness is this place exactly? And who are you?"

Hoo.

"Honestly, I don't know either."

"…!"

"More accurately, I've been trapped here so long that I can't even properly answer who I am anymore."

Still, if I wanted to keep them alive, this was probably the right approach.

I spoke seriously.

"But one thing I remember clearly. This place is dangerous, so go down there."

"Huh?"

I pointed toward the stairs leading back down to the platform.

"...The subway?"

"Yes. Go down there and board the train. At the very least, you'll secure a minimum level of safety—"

"Ah, yes, yes."

Thunk. One of the researchers slowly looked me up and down before cutting me off.

"No, I heard what you said, but..."

"Tch. You don't trust the words of a contaminated person who's been missing inside a place like this."

"Ah~"

"Let's keep our distance. Don't provoke him either. That's the best protocol."

Telling everyone not to speak to me, they pulled each other away and resumed talking among themselves.

But soon, from the back of the group, the voices of those realizing the seriousness of the situation began growing increasingly frantic.

"We can't contact security!"

"W-Wait, what do you mean we can't?"

"That shouldn't be possible. By design, the communicator should work in any darkness…"

"There's no way something that expensive isn't functioning!"

Eventually, one of the researchers pacing anxiously through the group began demanding accountability.

I glanced at the glossy employee badge hanging from his chest.

Even a team leader was here.

It was also a name I vaguely remembered from Dark Exploration Records. True to the cliché of a corrupt pharmaceutical executive, he had been the type to cause accidents through cost-cutting measures.

But apparently that wasn't his role today.

"Section Chief Kwak. This wasn't your doing, was it?"

"Excuse me?"

"You're the only person crazy enough to pull something like this!"

"Hahaha, what are you talking about, Team Leader Choi?"

Kwak Jaekwang smirked.

"The only thing this research group has in common is being inside the annex laboratory building. Yet somehow you're accusing me... Honestly, you sound more like a politician than a researcher."

"W-What?"

"Now that I think about it, I'm curious. Doesn't anyone recognize this darkness? It must be one of the darknesses our company possesses, after all. Wouldn't identifying it solve the problem?"

"Ah!"

And then ghost stories centered around residential neighborhoods began pouring out from the researchers.

The Woman at the Corner, Marble Alleyway, The Playground Beyond, Streetlight Shadow Games…

Gradually… not only did the panic fade, but even the tension itself began disappearing.

**[Oh, they're becoming careless.]**

Yeah.

'As long as we identify which ghost story this is, we can just follow the manual and get out. Easy.'

That seemed to be their mindset.

Arrogance.

*'Because all they've ever done is shove Field Exploration Team members and civilians into darknesses.'*

Even amateurs managed it, so naturally they believed that educated experts like themselves—people who understood ghost stories properly—could simply follow protocol and walk out safely. An unconscious sort of white-collar superiority complex.

*'...Hoo.'*

Let's try warning them one more time.

"Everyone, at the very least, you should continue this conversation back on the platform—"

"Yes, yes. We understand. Let's stop talking about it already. Since we've heard you out... Well, I suppose we won't stop you from escaping by copying what we do."

Then he grinned.

"Since nobody here seems to know you, you must be from some branch office or another... But don't think a field employee should be lecturing researchers."

"......"

[My friend, do you wish to teach them a lesson?]

No... forget it.

I simply followed the researchers as they moved off together.

Because I had noticed something strange within the group.

*'Those two.'*

Among the loudly chatting researchers, two figures stood slightly apart from everyone else.

Oddly passive and faces pale.

*'......Those.'*

And I recognized their clothing.

*'The project dinner.'*

I had seen them at Director Ho's project celebration dinner.

Most people there had worn masks, but some tables had seated people whose masks lacked the cognitive interference effect used by the Field Exploration Team.

*'Was that the researchers' table...?'*

Right. Thinking about it now, it was obvious.

There was no way Ho Yuwon—who had even involved Disaster Management Bureau agents to make the project succeed—would have built the project using only the Field Exploration Team.

*'Of course he recruited researchers too, to study ways of entering the sealed Catastrophe-Level disaster.'*

And now those people had appeared in the middle of Segwang Special City.

*'Hoo.'*

With a bad feeling creeping over me, my eyes finally met theirs.

"...!"

As expected, they recognized me. They had seen this mask at the company dinner, after all.

So slowly, I mouthed words at them.

Like this.

'Director Ho project.'

"...!!"

'Report.'

"......"

The two pale-faced researchers glanced at one another, apparently misunderstanding me for something else entirely. Then one of them hurriedly moved his lips.

I read the words from the motion…

Ac...cidentduringentrymethoddevelopment....

*'Accident during entry method development.'*

*'…!'*

Wait.

It felt like being struck in the head. So then...

*'...Director Ho really was continuing to develop other ways of entering Segwang Special City!'*

Right. In hindsight, that made perfect sense.

The Daydream Inc. director presumed to be Ho Yuwon had desperately tried every possible method to enter Segwang Special City in the wiki as well.

He had succeeded in entering through the well for now, but that method limited what could actually be brought back out. So it wasn't strange at all if he was still experimenting with other approaches.

Then this... was it a success?

*'No.'*

If it had succeeded, those researchers wouldn't look this pale. Which meant this situation was the result of failure.

They had arrived together with nearby researchers. And judging from their expressions...

*'It's a complete disaster.'*

They had been transported one-way into Segwang Special City.

Without any exit.

*'......'*

Meanwhile, the other researcher nervously checked whether anyone was watching before mouthing more words.

Please tell us how to get out

...I could read it without even trying.

Those people had already realized they were inside a Catastrophe-Level supernatural disaster.

A chill ran down my spine.

*'Wait, so that means...'*

All of those researchers...

Had been dragged in here with their real bodies?

Dozens of Daydream Inc. researchers were trapped beneath Segwang Special City...?

And specifically at this station?

"Hoo. At the very least, we've narrowed down the possibilities."

Meanwhile, the other researchers had already finished their discussion and were now looking over a list of darknesses they themselves had created.

"Then let's assume it's one of these three..."

Myeongjin!

Research Team 2's Team Leader Choi Myeongjin instinctively jerked his head toward the source of the voice, only to stop when the people around him restrained him.

"Team Leader?"

"Did you hear that?"

"Yes! I think this darkness contains entities that imitate relatives."

"That's right! It must be trying to lure us over!"

"Ah... Ah."

Team Leader Choi nodded.

"Right. Actually, this might help us identify which darkness it is. Narrow it down. Who knows, we might even get some valuable data out of this opportunity too. Just think of it as experience..."

But—

 

"Myeongjin, get out now!"

 

Creeeak.

The door of one of the houses swung open.

A middle-aged woman appeared inside, stomping her feet frantically with panic on her face.

"Hurry and go to work! Why are you home?! Go somewhere else right now!"

Not luring them at all—but desperately trying to reject them.

"......"

"...Everyone ignore it and quietly move somewhere else."

"Y-Yes."

But several of the researchers apparently couldn't suppress their curiosity and began observing the 'entity' that had appeared in the neighborhood.

She, who looked utterly ordinary, flailed her arms helplessly while hopping anxiously in place.

"Faster, faster!! If you stay like this, it'll be too late! Don't rest! Don't stop!"

It had begun.

"You should at least go back down the stairs and return to the platform—"

"You're really getting annoying!"

I swallowed my irritation and moved forward, tension tightening my body.

The two people from Ho Yuwon's project who had been looking at me desperately subtly tried walking closer.

 

Clunk, clunk.

 

"Yeonhwa!"



The researchers walked through the housing district.

The door of another house opened, and the figure of an ordinary person appeared, shouting.

Their eyes and nose were red, as if they had been crying.

“Dad… go to work quickly…”

“Unnie, you can’t stay here—it’s daylight! Get out, Run!”

“Honey, don’t come home! You have to work!”

As the group moved, doors creaked open all around them. From inside, voices shouted their names with familiarity, wailing for them not to come home.

On the researchers’ faces, confusion, fear, and curiosity appeared all at once.

For some, the latter was stronger.

“Something seems slightly off. Fascinating! Could it be that this ghost story is about hiding something valuable in their home? Maybe that’s why they’re trying to drive us away…”

“Jekang-ah.”

“…….”

“You shouldn’t walk—run. Come on.”

“Hyung! I’m telling you to hurry to work!”

“Eunha! Don’t you still have things to do? You do, right?”

Kwak Jekang smiled.

“Hmm, how about someone tries entering a house from a different route, via the station? We should test it! As far as I know, this kind of darkness hasn’t been registered with the company…”

“For heaven’s sake! Be quiet!”

Team Leader Choi spun around in anger, spitting as he shouted at Kwak Jekang.

“Then you go in yourself! I have no intention of doing that kind of field exploratgrrrrkkkrrrkkkrrrrr…”

“…….”

“…….”

“Grrrleuhhheuhhrrleuhhheuhhhrrleuhhh…”

“Team Leader?”

The leader of Research Team 2 grabbed his own throat.

And then he began to twist.

With a sickening crack—crrrrack, his neck turned, and kept turning, until it broke and tangled in on itself. Blood surged up and spilled from the opening of his mouth, gushing past his ruined bones.

Thud.

The researcher standing behind him was suddenly drenched in the blood pouring from the team leader’s head, now twisted to face backward.

“…Huh?”

……

“Rrrr… rrghrrr… rrrrk.”

The team leader seized the researcher’s head and twisted it.

“Urrrgh—rrrk—ghhrrrk—!”

The man thrashed, screaming, his whole body shaking.

And then he grabbed his own neck and wrenched it around.

“Aaahhh!!”

“What the, what is that!?!”

When I lifted my head, the neighborhood around us was still bright and warm under the midday sun—so normal it felt wrong.

But right in front of us, blood was splattering, shouts were echoing, and chaos was erupting.


'Damn it!'

The twisted-neck team leader and researcher kept moving, chasing the still-living researchers.

“AAAH!”

Reflexively, I lunged forward, yanked one researcher away, and threw him backward—but I realized almost immediately.

It was hopeless.

“AAAAAH!!”

“There's more over here!”

The figures of relatives who had screamed at them not to stop moving had vanished before anyone noticed.

Instead, figures with twisted necks were now emerging from every alleyway, their bodies bent grotesquely.

Their arms stretched outward.

Their peaceful closed eyes faced forward—while the backs of their heads pointed where their faces should have been.

“Wh-what... hiiiiik!”

This nightmare-like situation, like some deranged bad dream during a nap, was the true ghost story of Noon Station.

 

Survivors who visited this station after hearing its nickname, 'Nap Shelter,' had initially imagined an actual refuge. But they soon realized that at Noon Station, a nap meant eternal rest.

They ran through the alleys searching for food and necessities while fleeing the drowsiness pursuing them. Yet the abyssal obsession with rest and home never released them so easily.

Not until every last person finally fell asleep.

Not until peace mercifully came for them.

 

Do you know what that 'drowsiness' is hyperlinked to?

'Zombies.'

Only now did I fully understand the meaning of that expression.

'I thought because I was alone, I'd at least be able to kill myself and end it if things got bad.'

But seeing this hellscape unfold before me made it hard to breathe.


[Oh, good heavens. Just look at those filthy things! My friend, they'll ruin your elegance. Let's avoid them and head elsewhere.]


...Maybe Braun was right.

While these people drew all the aggro, maybe I could search the station for useful clues.

I could probably comfort myself by thinking most of these researchers were sociopaths anyway…

“AAAAH!”

'Damn it.'

Let's do it.

I pressed the tattoo near my heart and reflexively summoned the only method capable of surviving this situation.

'Simply becoming stronger is inefficient.'

If the descriptions of 'Nap Shelter' were accurate, then these things would keep coming endlessly.

So instead, I needed something suited for evasion. Something easy to hide with. Something with a broad field of vision.

The only contamination among my tattoos with a truly small form.

And…

Something capable of making others follow me.

 

Tap.

 

My body compressed inward as I transformed into a black cat, leaping atop the fence with light footsteps.

“W-What?!”

“A c-cat...?!”

In the end, I stopped in front of the two project researchers who had continued following me and began guiding them.

 

[This way]

 

“Ah...!!”

At least save these two.

'I need information.'

I guided them toward areas where fewer twisted-neck entities appeared.

The two researchers stared at me in disbelief, unable to comprehend the fact that I had become a cat—but ultimately began following me anyway.

“S-save me... ghrrrghhh.”

“AAAAAH!”

“Hhk, hhk, I don't want to die—”

I continued sprinting across fences while checking the safest possible routes. Over corners, past intersections without traffic lights, and toward the path leading beside the main road into a run-down house.

'We need to get back to the platform!'

 

[This way]

 

The two chased after me desperately.

But their stamina and coordination were poor. One stumbled awkwardly and collided with someone around a corner.

“Ah!”

“Miss Lee!”

I tried immediately shoving the fallen researcher upright with my body, but it was too late.

“Ghhrrrgh.”

Before my eyes, the sleepy person's neck twisted around as blood sprayed outward.

'Fuck!'

Suppressing a scream, I immediately redirected our route again.

Now, only one researcher remained, following me with a deathly pale face.

Then, as we rounded another corner, two other blood-covered researchers burst from a nearby alley and froze when they saw me.

“Section Chief Kwak! Move!”

The researcher following me shouted.

But instead of listening, Kwak Jaekwang's eyes lit up the moment he spotted me, and he immediately began following behind me.

“Eek!”

And the researcher accompanying Kwak Jaekwang gritted their teeth before eventually following me too, despite my black cat appearance.

“Huff.”

 

[This way]

 

At intersections, I tried directing as many other researchers as possible toward safer paths whenever I spotted them—but very few actually listened.

And moving forward became harder and harder.

I understood now. The closer we got to the platform stairs, the more numerous the twisted-neck entities became.

I could barely breathe.

'Should we hide inside a house for now?'

But wouldn't that just trap us instead?

I narrowly slipped between two twisted-neck figures, climbed another fence, and searched for a route…

Then spotted something beyond the other side of the wall.

A bright silver rectangular box.

 

A fire hydrant.

 

'……!'

That, maybe…

 

[This way]

 

I guided the others toward the hydrant.

Then struck it with my paw.

“H-Here?”

 

[This way]

 

“No, that's…”

“Quit getting in the way!”

Kwak Jaekwang boldly opened the hydrant and threw himself inside first.

After the others entered too, I slipped through the opening myself.

 

Bang!

 

The door shut.

Inside, the hydrant was strangely much larger than it had appeared from outside.

'Just as I thought.'

...Just like Yeongeun had told me, the fire hydrants inside Segwang Special City's subway system truly were special places.

“Huff...”

The researchers tried to steady their breathing.

 

[Quiet]

 

They immediately clamped their mouths shut.

 

Step. Step…

 

...Outside the hydrant, barefoot footsteps passed by.

Until the sound finally faded away, the three researchers aside from Kwak Jaekwang desperately kept their mouths covered shut......

 

[Clear]

 

“Hoo.”

Only after my signal did the researchers finally exhale in relief.

And only then did I properly look at the face of the researcher accompanying Kwak Jaekwang.

A woman wearing glasses with deep dark circles under her eyes.

'...Assistant Manager Lee Yeonhwa.'

One of the very few researchers in the company who actually possessed ethical concerns.

Even in this nightmare situation, a slight sense of relief and gladness began surfacing until suddenly—

“...Black cat.”

Assistant Manager Lee Yeonhwa abruptly stared at me and muttered.

'......?'

“Section Chief Kwak, th-that entity—it's Identification Code Qterw-A-1845, the Black Cat!”

Excuse me?

“An A-rank darkness!”

Me?

“The entity from Mermaid's Grave that supposedly saved people—the one that contaminated Employee Pony with symbiotic contamination...!”

 

Ah.
