# Chapter 365

I looked into the drawer with trembling eyes.

But the item inside had not changed.

An employment contract.

It was unmistakably the very contract I had signed.

After drinking the Wish Ticket, in the moment I realized the truth and my identity collapsed, Director Cheong appeared and held it out to me—a lifeline for survival.

Or a lethal noose.

—It’s just a contract. All you need to do is sign. I promise very humane treatment.

In the end, I signed.

I signed to live, and I worked in the Security Team as 130666…

But why was that contract here, inside the abandoned resort control room of my mental world?

…Wait.

Is this the binding force of the contract itself?

Had it taken shape deep within me, manifested inside this drawer—the drawer symbolically used to collect “resort employment contracts”?

If so—

If so…!

With shaking hands, I grabbed the employment contract.

It felt real.

And then—

Riiip.

I tore it apart.

The middle split cleanly, the paper that bound me ripped in two.

A sharp thrill of release surged up through me. I lifted my eyes—

And lowered them again.

The contract was there.

Whole.

Untorn.

In my hands.

……

I tore it again.

Riiip.

Blink.

The contract was there.

I tore it again.

Riiip.

Blink.

The contract was there.

I tore it again—

Stop!

Panting, I froze my hands.

It seemed simple tearing wouldn’t damage this contract.

Of course it wouldn’t be that easy…

Good friend. Where are you hiding?

My body went rigid.

…From beyond the corridor, I sensed movement approaching.

The corrupted Daydream character was coming this way.

“……”

This time, I didn’t touch the door.

Instead, without making a sound, I crawled behind the control room desk and curled up.

Thud.

Please.

Thud.

Go past.

Thud.

The sound came closer.

Thud.

And then—

Thud…

……

……It moved away.

Ha.

Drenched in cold sweat, I stayed frozen, straining to listen as the sound receded.

It hadn’t noticed me.

I could feel the vibration of it moving toward the far end of the corridor.

Am I… a little freer now?

It seemed the mascot was having more difficulty tracking me than when I had been inside the old Daydream office.

Is it because I broke away once?

Of course, if this dragged on, it would likely devise another way to hunt “Good Friends.”

So focus.

Quickly!

What do I do?

I glared at the contract still in my hand.

If tearing didn’t work—

Right.

…What about revising it?

A pen.

Without making noise, I reached onto the desk and quickly found one.

A fountain pen.

Hurriedly, I inked it and drew a line across the contract.

I revised the resort employment contracts like this.

Made them safer. More suitable for human employees.

So maybe I could do the same to my contract—

“……”

The ink vanished.

As if it had never existed, the fountain pen’s trace slid off the page and dripped onto the floor.

Plink.

It didn’t work.

As if to say: You have no authority to revise this.

Ah.

Right. I could revise resort employment contracts because I was the the employer.

And besides, you can’t unilaterally alter a contract already signed.

Not without mutual agreement from Director Cheong.

Or maybe not even then.

Only one thing was certain.

I had no authority.

【 Looks like a dead end, my friend. 】

【 Shall we search for another path? 】

Right.

At a dead end, you find a new road.

A method I have authority over.

An action that can affect this contract.

…There is one.

I held the contract.

And I ‘read’ it.

With the wiki.

Not even a real human body, just a Good Friend doll form, and I still melt.

Maybe because this is the mental world.

Of course, the severed arm hasn’t regenerated.

Editing doesn’t work.

Using the wiki doesn’t work.

Stay calm.

There’s always a hint in information.

Because this drawer existed.

Yellow Dragon.

Like being an ant watching a shoe descend from above.

Cosmic horror.

My mouth dries.

Yeouiju cores?

Collected one by one from dragon-like beings in urban legends?

Insane.

Like a creature from a grotesque horror film.

So what can I do?
