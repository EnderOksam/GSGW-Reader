# Chapter 344

*I looked at Researcher Lee Jaheon—no… Manager Lee Jaheon.*

*He no longer looked like a lizard, but even so, I found myself naturally imagining those reptilian, vertical pupils.*

*“…Since when have you remembered?”*

*“? I have always remembered.”*

*In that instant, an overwhelming wave of relief washed through me, and I nearly let out a hollow laugh.*

*“I see….”*

*The tension drained from my shoulders.*

*‘Phew….’*

*It seemed I had been under far more pressure than I’d realized.*

*Being dropped into an unfamiliar time with not a single familiar person around, trying on my own to save acquaintances who had no memories of me…*

*…It had been exhausting.*

*【 Mr. Roe Deer…. 】*

*【 But strictly speaking, you were not alone. Your friend was always with you, offering witty remarks to comfort and guide you. 】*

*Y-yeah, that’s true.*

*Thanks.*

*【 Oh, think nothing of it! 】*

Let’s avoid saying something like, “I meant acquaintances with physical bodies,” and triggering the host to pop out with an “Oh, I understand perfectly!” *….*

*I decided to enjoy this sense of relief, however briefly.*

*It wasn’t just me doing the rescuing anymore—there was someone who could work with me to save others.*

*And besides—*

*‘Come to think of it, it’s more natural that Manager Lee Jaheon still has his memories too.’*

*He’d fallen into this place under conditions not so different from mine.*

*After all, both of us escaped the loop and then… well, died.*

*‘Did I really need to put on that whole act with the ID card back at the door….’*

*Feeling a bit embarrassed, I was about to mentally rewind that moment when—*

*“Then I will reiterate.”*

*“Pardon?”*

*“I do not recommend attempting to reach the rooftop of Segwang City Hall, or any aboveground floors here.”*

*…!*

*It was like cold water splashing over my head, snapping me back to reality.*

*The counselor had suggested going to the rooftop…*

*‘And the manager shut it down.’*

*When I turned, the spirit was smiling as it looked at Researcher Lee Jaheon.*

*“Do you even have the right to say that?”*

*“Advice does not require qualification.”*

Yet the counselor didn’t ask a perfunctory question like, “Were you two acquainted?”

*Instead, he quietly ignored Lee Jaheon’s words, focused on me, and stepped closer. With his one remaining hand, he took both of mine, like a familiar older relative.*

*Then he spoke softly.*

*“Agent.”*

*…While looking at the agent jacket I was wearing.*

*“In fact, my jacket wasn’t at the Bureau branch—it was in the counseling office.”*

*……!!*

*‘I know you lied.’*

*That was the only possible meaning.*

*Yet there was no hostility in the counselor’s eyes.*

*If anything, there was an inexplicable warmth.*

*“But I can sense my consciousness within you….”*

*“…….”*

*“Have you ever received counseling from me?”*

*Ah.*

*I slowly nodded.*

*He smiled faintly.*

*“I thought so. I remember every client I’ve counseled, yet strangely, I don’t remember you.”*

*“…….”*

*“You helped me from the very beginning. So perhaps… I wondered if you might be someone my past self arranged, for my own sake….”*

*…….*

*“If that’s the case, would you be willing to help me?”*

*I lifted my head and looked at the counselor.*

*At Ho Yuwon’s face.*

*“…You’re planning to go to City Hall’s rooftop to search for your fox marble? Betting on the chance it was used in an experiment there?”*

*“Yes.”*

*“I can’t guarantee what state that place is in right now.”*

*“Yes.”*

*I took a deep breath.*

*“…If Segwang Special City’s surface becomes a place where supernatural phenomena cannot exist, you might not be able to exist either.”*

*“…….”*

*After a brief silence, the counselor spoke.*

*“Even so, I don’t want to turn back while there’s still a method I can try.”*

*“…….”*

*“Someone out there must be desperately trying to save citizens, to bring this disaster to an end. I should at least try as well.”*

*Ha.*

*Seriously….*

*I pressed my hands over my eyes and said,*

*“I’ll go with you.”*

*“…!”*

*A smile spread across the counselor’s face.*

*“Thank you.”*

*“Mr. Roe Deer.”*

*Hold on.*

*“Manager.”*

*As if trying to calm a researcher from the Cheerful Research Institute, I took hold of him and spoke ambiguously to Manager Lee Jaheon.*

*“We should probably find out how Director Ho ended up like this.”*

*“That means…”*

*“Yes. Exactly what you’re thinking.”*

*Please don’t make subject-guessing statements in front of the counselor that spell everything out.*

*Instead, I spoke vaguely again.*

*“Perhaps we might be able to prevent the tragedy that’s yet to come.”*

*“…….”*

*If we really succeeded in retrieving the marble, it might even be possible to stop this day of disaster and bring the urban legend to an end.*

*‘Even if not, we might gain information or clues.’*

*“And I have one request for you, Manager.”*

*Though I couldn’t see his face, I swallowed.*

*“I want you to help the other two members of our exploration team escape.”*

*Beep-beep-beep-beeeep!*

*The fire alarm rang intermittently again.*

*I hurriedly turned to the two companions.*

*“Agent…!”*

*“Student Jaegwan, listen carefully.”*

*Grabbing his shoulders, I said,*

*“When you get out, take the train—go to the farthest station from here. Exit there and leave with that researcher. Understood?”*

*“…What?”*

*“He’s someone you can trust. He’ll protect both of you.”*

*“…….”*

*Questions filled the high schooler’s eyes, but in the end he quietly asked,*

*“Then… does that mean you’re not coming with us, Agent?”*

*“It’s fine. I’ll follow later.”*

*“How do you plan to escape later…?”*

*“That’s not something you need to worry about. You should focus on your own safety before that of adults. Got it?”*

*“…….”*

*But Ryu Jaegwan didn’t answer—he just stared at me like stone.*

*My anxiety rose.*

*“Jaegwan….”*

*“Is that really necessary?”*

*Reporter Eun Haje cut in.*

*Taking Jaegwan’s side, she said,*

*“From what the researcher said earlier, the subway station seems safe. Then we can just wait there.”*

*Ah.*

*“Well, we can take a quick look around here if there’s anything else, then head out. I’d like to get a few interviews from the people at the station too.”*

*After some thought, I nodded.*

*At least until those who’d taken refuge in Segwang Station despaired over the lack of rescue, they’d been alive.*

*‘Most crowded places in Segwang City are probably already contaminated anyway.’*

*No matter which station, the surrounding area would be chaos—so it might actually be safer for me to join them later.*

*Especially with the counselor involved.*

*‘We need to go and come back before this corridor is engulfed in flames.’*

*My mind grew urgent.*

*“…All right. But if I don’t return before this place burns down, you need to move on first. I’ll take another route to the subway.”*

*“…….”*

*Reporter Eun Haje seemed to notice my attempt at self-justification, but simply nodded.*

*He didn’t miss this, though.*

*“By the way, how do you know that handsome guy? You didn’t seem to recognize him earlier.”*

*“…He looked different when I met him.”*

*Ugh.*

*I brushed off Eun Haje’s confusion and went over to Manager Lee Jaheon.*

*Then, firmly, I said again,*

*“I’m going up.”*

*“I see.”*

*Manager Lee Jaheon paused as if thinking, then took something out of his pocket and held it out to me.*

*“Take this.”*

*It was a button-shaped item—wait.*

Isn’t this ‘We Can Help’ *?!*

*‘Why does he have this…?’*

*Wait.*

*‘…Why is Manager Lee Jaheon even here as a Cheerful Research Institute researcher to begin with?’*

*I woke up in Segwang Special City as someone from the profession I once worked in. Which means…*

*Lowering my voice so no one else could hear, I asked,*

*“Manager. In the past… were you ever a researcher at the Cheerful Research Institute?”*

*“Yes.”*

*…!!*

*“I’ll correct that. I am not Manager Lee Jaheon. However, ‘we’ were once that.”*

*What does that even mean?*

But seeing the ‘We Can Help’ *button in his hand, a memory suddenly surfaced.*

*—Is your reason for purchase to escape from your current coordinates to a safe location? This is not recommended.

—There is a record of failed escape.*

*“…Did someone from the Cheerful Research Institute try to use this button to escape and fail?”*

*“Yes.”*

*…….*

*“And are you currently fulfilling that role?”*

*“Yes.”*

*…I see.*

*Looking at the faceless Manager Lee Jaheon, I couldn’t help but guess.*

*Could the entity that answered the researcher’s call back then have been Manager Lee Jaheon as well?*

*“At the time, the rescue request was activated three days after the disaster began. Considering environmental differences, its use is recommended under current conditions.”*

*Right. Since it’s the very day the incident broke out, conditions might be different.*

*“…Thank you.”*

*I bowed and accepted the item. Manager Lee Jaheon nodded.*

*Without thinking, I asked,*

*“Then… how does ‘we’ feel about this situation?”*

*“I do not know.”*

*…?*

*Beep-beep-beep-beeeep!*

*“…!”*

*The fire alarm rang again.*

*“If you have decided, you must move quickly. There is a high probability that less than forty minutes remain before the fire spreads to this floor.”*

*Swallowing, I nodded.*

*“Let’s go.”*

*I looked at the counselor as he held out his hand.*

*In his palm lay a small red fruit.*

*“…….”*

*I took it and swallowed one.*

*“If you feel dizzy or experience strange impulses, take another immediately.”*

*“…Yes.”*

*And so the group split in two.*

*Lee Jaheon, Eun Haje, and Ryu Jaegwan headed for the subway entrance.*

*And we…*

*“I see the elevator.”*

*The emergency stairs.*

*“…….”*

*The elevator still looked intact. It gleamed brilliantly, almost magically—but riding it would only take us straight into an inferno.*

*So we opened the emergency stairwell door beside it.*

*Creeeeak.*

*The door opened with a strained sound, as if rarely used, revealing the stairs.*

*They looked like ordinary, old emergency stairs.*

*And in the center of the landing was written—*

*[B3]*

*“I’ve heard that the emergency stairs from the institute’s core floors connect directly to City Hall, just in case.”*

*“…….”*

*“So from here on, it’s a real space—without the institute’s flashy supernatural phenomena.”*

*In other words, we were already standing on Basement Level 3 of Segwang City Hall.*

*I took a deep breath.*

*“…Confirmed.”*

*I turned off my smartphone.*

*Indoors, the range was too limited for disaster alerts to matter anyway.*

*“In places like this, it’s best to rely on sight and sound to avoid danger. If we’re heading straight to the rooftop via the emergency stairs, there’s also a chance we won’t encounter any entities at all.”*

*“I see. You’re experienced.”*

*“…Thank you.”*

*I took the lead and began climbing the stairs.*

*With a slightly hurried pace.*

*Step. Step.*

*Until we left the underground levels, there was no sign of anything.*

*Only the sound of my footsteps and the counselor’s.*

*“…….”*

*[1F]*

*Soon, we reached the ground floor.*

*Step. Step.*

*The stairwell was eerily quiet, and now sunlight was even pouring in from outside.*

*It almost felt like we’d come in together after lunch, saying we’d go for a walk.*

*Step. Step.*

*…Squelch.*

*I stopped.*

*“That just—”*

*“I’m fine.”*

*…Behind me.*

*Something melted and fell to the floor.*

*I’d heard that sound before.*

*…When Ho Yuwon’s hand was severed.*

*That wet mass hitting the floor with a similar rupture….*

*“Just now, you—”*

*“Don’t look back.”*

*“…….”*

*I froze my head in place.*

*As long as I didn’t turn around, all I could perceive was the counselor’s calm voice behind me.*

*It continued.*

*“If you don’t mind, could you keep talking with me as we walk?”*

*“…Yes.”*

*I took a deep breath and started walking again.*

*Step. Step.*

*“Was there anything you were curious about?”*

*I swallowed.*

*If I had to pick a topic…*

*“…What exactly is Nine-Tailed Fox Syndrome?”*

*“You know about that too? That’s… one of the methods I use to treat clients.”*

*His voice behind me brightened slightly.*

*“Are you familiar with the role-reversal technique used in counseling?”*

*“…You mean when people in group counseling take on each other’s roles to better understand one another?”*

*He chuckled warmly.*

*“You know it well. That’s correct. It helps facilitate the process.”*

*“I see.”*

*“Of course, in the past it truly was an illness. Something closer to a scheme for devouring human livers….”*

*Squelch.*

*The sound of a massive mass falling.*

*“…!”*

*Gripping the handrail, I barely resisted turning around.*

*The sounds of things pouring down, of stumbling and vibrations, traveled up the railing.*

*This is—*

*“Don’t look back.”*

*“…….”*

*“Keep walking.”*

*This won’t work.*

*‘It really does seem like a supernatural phenomenon that’s dissolving.’*

*Then—then—*

*‘…!’*

*As I slowly continued walking, I spoke in as ordinary a tone as I could.*

*Cold sweat dripped from my chin.*

*“You use a wide variety of counseling techniques. You’re truly an impressive counselor.”*

*What about the opposite?*

*I focused on a realistic identity, not a supernatural one.*

*“You were popular at school too, weren’t you?”*

*“Ha ha….”*

*“It’s hard to find a good counselor. If you open a practice later, please give me your card.”*

*Step. Step.*

*“That’s right…. I’m a counselor. A counselor. Counsel…or.”*

*Squelch.*

*Something fell again. Tension surged through me—but this time…*

*That was the end of it.*

*‘Hurry.’*

*Next.*

*Something even more grounded in reality…*

*Ah.*

*“That’s right. You are a counselor.”*

*“…….”*

*“A very capable counselor. Your name is…”*

*So.*

*“Your name is Ho Yuwon.”*

*“Is that so?”*

*“Yes.”*

*“Nice. Ho (護), as in protect; Yu (瀏), as in clear; Won (援), as in help? ‘Protecting the heart, reflecting it clearly like a mirror, and helping people.’ A very fitting name for a counselor.”*

*“…Yes.”*

*Step. Step.*

*“That’s exactly right, Counselor Ho Yuwon. A young counselor living in Segwang Special City, with such a good name.”*

*“Ha ha ha… thank you.”*

*I kept walking.*

*…Now, there was no longer any sound of something melting and falling behind us.*

*Only our footsteps remained.*

*“…….”*

*I let out a small but deep sigh of relief.*

*And then—*

*[12F]*

*“The top floor.”*

*The end was in sight.*

*I climbed one more flight of the bright emergency stairs, toward a landing with no floor number.*

*[Rooftop Access Door]*

*Grasping the door—*

*“Let’s go.”*

I opened the door to the rooftop.
