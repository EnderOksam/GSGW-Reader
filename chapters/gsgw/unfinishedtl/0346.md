# Chapter 345

*The moment the rooftop access door opened, piercing sunlight poured straight into my eyes.*

*And as my vision adjusted, beneath a chillingly clear blue sky, the rooftop of Segwang City Hall came into view.*

*...The floor was covered in a membrane-like layer of some strange grayish-white substance.*

*Like the shell of a cocoon.*

*Yet it shimmered in iridescent colors.*

*‘...Eggs.’*

*I recalled the footage taken by Reporter Eun Haje’s group.*

*That grotesque, swollen mass bulging at the very top of the City Hall building.*

*It had “swollen” all the way down to cover the rooftop floor.*

*“......”*

*Struggling to keep my hands from shaking, I slipped one into my pocket and carefully pulled out a bundle of convenience-store receipts.*

*Then I tossed them.*

*Tap.*

*The receipts rolled across the cocoon-like surface of the rooftop floor, hit the railing, and fell down below.*

*Naturally, beyond them, the city sprawled out—*

*“Don’t look.”*

*“......”*

*Suppressing the tension prickling up my spine, I turned my head back toward the rooftop.*

*...At the center of the roof stood a single machine.*

*The dream incubator.*

*Around the base of that massive machine lay toy fragments, lab coats, glasses, and clothes—strewn about like trash.*

*And standing in front of it was something upright.*

*A silhouette about the size of a human.*

*Covered in cloth.*

*“......”*

*I stepped forward cautiously.*

*There was a strangely soft, slightly cushioned sensation underfoot as I moved ahead.*

*【 So bold, my friend! 】*

*Once I reached the front of the dream incubator,*

*I stretched out my hand and—whipped the cloth off the figure.*

*“......!”*

*I found myself staring into retro-style illustrated eyes.*

*It wasn’t a person.*

*A Daydream Corporation character.*

*A life-sized standee of a yellow dragon character.*

*The flat panel, illustrated with a retro cartoon-style yellow dragon, stood incongruously beside the dream incubator. In the empty space next to it was a blue button with an explanation.*

CLICK

*→ A congratulatory message from Yongyongi is recorded here.

(Each press plays a different message!)*

*“......”*

*A sense of unease swelled into dread.*

*“Shall I press it?”*

*“...No.”*

*I swallowed, then raised my hand and pressed the button myself.*

*A cheerful voice played through a low-quality recording.*

*Hello, Cheerful Research Institute researchers!*

*I’m a commemorative panel created by Director Cheong Dallae of Daydream to celebrate this experiment at the Cheerful Research Institute.*

*It’s a great honor for Yongyongi to attend the key experimental site meant to achieve your research goals!*

*You must be waiting for the results, so I hope I can help ease your boredom just a little!*

*...Though to be honest, I’m also here for company promotion. Daydream is launching a new product line, so please take good care of us!*

*The distorted, crackling childlike voice echoed across the empty rooftop.*

*If a researcher had pressed this while waiting for results, they would have burst out laughing...*

*Now only this lonely panel remained.*

*“......”*

*I pressed the button once more.*

*Daydream and the Cheerful Research Institute have maintained an active cooperative relationship from the very beginning.*

*Under Director Cheong Dallae’s leadership, Daydream has enthusiastically supplied the dream-fluid required for the Institute’s experiments.*

*This is because Director Cheong was deeply moved by the Cheerful Research Institute’s meaningful research goal.*

*“A safe world without supernatural phenomena”! What a wonderful catchphrase!*

*Did you know?*

*If that catchphrase really came true, Daydream would go bankrupt.*

*“...!”*

*The recording ended.*

*With trembling hands, I pressed the button again.*

*Hahaha! Just kidding!*

*Daydream has respected the Cheerful Research Institute’s catchphrase and cooperated up until now.*

*And this cooperation will likely continue in the future...*

*Though the direction might change a little!*

*That’s why today, for this commemorative and decisive experiment, Director Cheong prepared a very special surprise gift...*

*A gift meant to ease your hard work.*

*Curious?*

*......*

*Take a look at the dream incubator.*

*Reflexively, I turned my head. My eyes quickly scanned every inch of the dream incubator on the rooftop.*

*Here and there on the incubator... were bloodstains, as if clawed by fingernails.*

*Director Cheong placed a small music box inside the incubator tank.*

*As a commemoration.*

*The moment your summoning succeeds, the arriving entity will hear the music box.*

*The problem is that if you hear its melody three times, you go insane.*

*“...!”*

*Ah, it should be about time for the summoning to have succeeded.*

*The music box is set to autoplay, so in five minutes, it’ll all be over!*

*The timer is running!*

*In five minutes, your grand plan to cultivate residents inside the tank will end in a tragic accident—oh? You’re trying to open the incubator?*

*Oh dear, I changed the screw-lock mechanism yesterday. You can’t open it using your usual methods anymore.*

*If you don’t plan on giving up... well, maybe you could still force it open?*

*The countless desperate marks left on the incubator.*

*They were grotesque and chilling, like evidence of someone trying desperately to pry it open until the very end.*

*You must be trying everything right now. Fighting! Running down the stairs to fetch stronger toys, right?*

*But...*

*Huh?*

*Doesn’t something feel... off?*

*“......”*

*Huh? Wait a second, something strange is happening at the rooftop experiment site, isn’t it?*

*The researchers’ bodies are suddenly turning into toy parts, right? The mysterious toys you brought to open the tank aren’t working, right?*

*Curious why? Oops, the recording time is up! Try pressing the button again.*

*Thump!*

*I practically slammed the button.*

*You’re pressing it again?*

*Well then, congratulations!*

*You’ve achieved your goal.*

*A “world without supernatural phenomena” is now arriving, centered on this City Hall!*

*“...!!”*

*How is that possible?*

*Oh, I forgot to tell you the name of the music box!*

*It’s called...*

*The “Angel’s Sigh” music box.*

*“......”*

*Wait.*

*I’d heard that name somewhere before.*

*That was— that was—*

*‘...Mermaid’s Grave!’*

*On the glittering underside of the Dragon Palace, a place destroyed by a biological disaster, there was a conch-shell item you could find there.*

*That item was called Angel’s Sigh.*

*And I had heard its melody before—right here in Segwang Special City’s subway.*

*— “Angel’s Sigh” cassette recording. “Listen to the auspicious melody of heaven at a reasonable price.”*

*A slot machine prize at Body Casino Station.*

*“......”*

*Something that was neither shivers nor shock crawled chillingly up my spine.*

*‘Don’t tell me the people who received disaster alerts were humming...’*

*That melody...?*

*Don’t worry. The function of that music box’s melody has already been thoroughly verified in one deep-sea city!*

*This melody parasitizes humans, granting vitality and regenerative power—but if overused, the parasite eventually devours the host.*

*It wasn’t originally meant for humans.*

*And it spreads mentally and physically in all directions!*

*A perfect function for creating a world, don’t you think?*

*I clenched my fists.*

*How wonderful!*

*You’ve achieved your research goal, haven’t you? “The advent of a world without supernatural phenomena”!*

*Of course, one urban legend did spread widely in the process... but still, with no other supernatural phenomena left, it’s quite a remarkable achievement.*

*This kind of decisive push is exactly what you needed.*

*Compared to your grand ambitions, this experiment was far too passive and sluggish!*

*Summoning just one “resident of a world without supernatural phenomena.”*

*Studying the worldbuilding through that resident.*

*Too slow.*

*Too vague.*

*Director Cheong dislikes both approaches.*

*So Director Cheong personally took other measures.*

*You can thank them for the success later!*

*They’ll probably say something like this—*

*I can hear Director Cheong’s voice...*

*“Be grateful. I achieved your slogan for you.”*

*“Of course, since Daydream is a profit-driven company, it’s only right that we own a stake in that slogan.”*

*And that’s exactly what will happen.*

*To the catchphrase “a safe world without supernatural phenomena,” we’ll efficiently add just one line.*

*......*

*“Except for me.”*

*“......!”*

*“A safe world without supernatural phenomena—except for me!”*

*Eliminating competitors is the most favored route to monopoly in the corporate world.*

*Insane.*

*This is insane...*

*Unfortunately, the Cheerful Research Institute may disappear from this world now.*

*But our cooperation will continue. Your dream incubator will be faithfully repurposed to produce Daydream’s new product line, generating enormous profits.*

*Think of it as Daydream cleaning up after your unrealistic, spineless efforts!*

*And...*

*Do you know why I’m explaining all this to you?*

*“...!”*

*I stood up.*

*Creak.*

*...A sound came from the opposite rooftop door.*

*If someone heard everything and made it all the way here, it meant it was already too late.*

*You’re either already useless toys—or inside me now. In the flames.*

*Ahahahahaha...*

*I struck the panel.*

*Hahahahahahaha!*

*Hahahahahahahahahahahaha!*

*Hahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahaha...*

*With several loud bangs, the panel crumpled. The laughter warped, crackled, emitted a twisted, eerie sound—and then cut off.*

*“...Huff.”*

*As I gasped for breath—*

*Creeeeak.*

*“......”*

*The opposite rooftop door twisted open.*

*‘Ah.’*

*The swollen cocoon on the floor forced the door open through the gap...*

*Beyond it, I couldn’t see a person. But—*

*I heard the clear sound of a music box.*

*“......!!”*

*Eerily pure, yet somehow warm...*

*‘Damn it.’*

*I covered my ears and stumbled backward.*

*But it didn’t block it. The music box melody flowing through the open rooftop door was cheerful—growing louder, pulling me closer—*

*Someone grabbed my shoulder.*

*“Agent.”*

*“...!”*

*“Let’s go back.”*

*I turned and ran like mad.*

*The moment I left the rooftop, I heard the silhouette behind me slam the emergency stairwell door shut.*

*Bang!*

*The music box stopped.*

*I sprinted, shoving the fruit Ho Yuwon had given me into my mouth and chewing.*

*‘It’s okay.’*

*I wasn’t enthralled yet. Barely keeping myself from biting my tongue, I spoke.*

*“The fox marble you were looking for—huff—”*

*“It wasn’t... there.”*

*There wasn’t even time to feel despair. No—after that, there wasn’t time to speak at all.*

*We simply ran, like mad, to escape the surface.*

*Downward.*

*Lower,*

*Lower still!*

*【 Almost there. We’ll reach the Institute soon, Mr. Roe Deer! 】*

*Right. First, get out of this insane building where a warped version of a “world without supernatural phenomena” had arrived—*

*......*

*‘Braun.’*

*【 My friend? 】*

*My feet wouldn’t stop.*

*But neither would the question rising from within me.*

*So I thought as I ran.*

*This was a place where urban legends vanished.*

*So how could Braun—*

*‘How are you... still talking?’*

*【 Why, of course I can remain by your side and offer advice, my friend. A one-sided environmental trial can no longer separate us. 】*

*【 Isn’t it touching? 】*

*An exaggerated sound played, like wiping tears from a cathode-ray screen...*

*That cheerful tone made my heart sink instead.*

*Crunch. Crunch.*

*Th-this sound...*

*How am I even hearing it?*

*In a place where supernatural phenomena cannot exist...?*

*【 Oh, but Braun here happened to have a similar question. Shall we ask it? Affirmative! Excellent. Then—one question. 】*

*【 Mr. Roe Deer, how are you still sane? 】*

*......*

*What?*

*【 Of course, “sane” by your standards, not mine. Shall we dig a little deeper into the same idea? 】*

*【 You’ve heard the music box melody quite a bit already, haven’t you? Yet you insist you’re not contaminated! 】*

*That’s... that’s because I had countermeasures.*

*‘I kept eating the fruit the counselor—Ho Yuwon—gave me.’*

*【 Hmm. I see. Then let me ask you this. 】*

*【 How exactly does that fruit work? 】*

*......*

*......!!*

*【 Isn’t that fruit itself a “supernatural phenomenon,” as defined by your own group? 】*

*【 Its creator is barely holding together by clinging to the identity of “counselor” you gave them—do you really think it would work so well? 】*

*It was a precise critique.*

*......*

*What is this?*

*‘Why am I...’*

*Able to resist?*

*“Agent!”*

*“...!”*

*I snapped to my senses and stopped short.*

*[B3]*

*We’d arrived. The door to the Cheerful Research Institute was right in front of us.*

*I grabbed the handle immediately.*

*No time for brooding. We had to get to the subway—*

*Ah.*

*The handle was hot.*

*“...Counselor.”*

*I said quietly.*

*“The fire has spread inside.”*

*“......”*

*“Get on my back.”*

*“I’m fine—”*

*“Hurry!”*

*We had to move.*

*I took off my jacket, splashed nearly all the remaining water from my backpack onto it—and onto my sleeves—then tossed it back.*

*“Cover your head with it. Use the wet cloth to protect your airway. I won’t look back.”*

*“......”*

*Thankfully, I heard the fabric being pulled over. I let out a breath of relief and hoisted them onto my back.*

*The strangely weightless feeling was gone, but they were still light—for an adult man.*

*As if several parts were missing.*

*“...I’m opening it.”*

*Forcing myself not to dwell on that sensation, I opened the door.*

*A wave of heat slammed into my nose.*

*“......”*

*Flames were raging throughout the Cheerful Research Institute’s core floor.*

*But I could see a path.*

*‘We can make it.’*

*Covering my mouth with my wet sleeve, I charged into the area where smoke hadn’t yet thickened.*

*Huff—short, ragged breaths echoed from my back and my own mouth. I ran as fast as I could, carefully avoiding smoke, watching the ceiling and walls.*

*‘I can do this.’*

*We can get out.*

*Without its mystery, the office wasn’t very large. I ran the distance.*

*Four meters,*

*Three meters,*

*Two meters,*

*One meter—*

*I grabbed the door handle.*

*I yanked it open with all my strength.*

*Whoosh—the musty air of the subway stung my nose. The stench of corpses and everything else didn’t matter.*

*What mattered was that we’d escaped the inferno together...*

*Something poured down over my head.*

*I rolled sideways with the person on my back and fell. Steel beams and concrete rained down above us. The ceiling and wall structures of the platform—completely collapsed by something that had flown in and slammed down from the concourse—came crashing down like deadly weapons.*

“Agent—”

I heard someone shouting from outside. Thud. *My head was struck by a sharp chunk of concrete; my ears rang, the sound blurring.*

*And then, suddenly, I remembered.*

*— It’s all collapsed.*

*When I had first woken up at Segwang Station. This platform had already been completely destroyed.*

*“Ah.”

So that was the first day......*

*The brief thought vanished, and steel beams and chunks of concrete continued to pour down onto my body.*

*I was buried beneath the concrete.*

*“......”*

*Blood. A sticky sensation. Pain. And then... faintly... I heard voices.*

*“Th-there’s a person trapped!”

“Agent!”

“......Didn’t someone just say ‘agent’ over there?”

“Sounded like it. Check the headcount for Team Two that entered the station.”*

*Voices I recognized by context.*

*“Anyone missing?”

“No, sir!”

“Then it’s not one of our agents?”*

They were the voices of Segwang City Disaster Management Bureau agents.
