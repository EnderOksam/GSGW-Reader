# Chapter 357

*High school student Ryu Jaekwan held his breath.*

*It had already been several hours since they began moving through Sekwang Special City, following a researcher named Lee Jaheon—someone whose features were chillingly perfect.*

*...And it had also been several hours since the agent who had saved him vanished beneath a pile of concrete.*

—Agent!

*The shock still lingered, but something equally shocking happened again just now.*

*“......”*

*Ryu Jaekwan looked at the underground maintenance tracks in front of him.*

*Just moments ago, he had communicated with the agent who had suddenly appeared from that passage—and then vanished into paper.*

*He had no idea how it was possible, but paper had definitely burst out of empty air...*

*Relief and confusion crossed over each other.*

*“This is ghostly stuff.”*

*“......”*

*“Researcher, are you really sure we just wait like this?”*

*“Yes.”*

*Originally, following the agent’s instructions, they planned to take the subway as far away from the city center as possible, then exit and head toward a nearby mountain path.*

*But after countless twists and turns, when they finally approached the edge of Sekwang Special City and crossed beyond the mountain trail...*

*Something strange happened.*

—Do not stare.

*...When they came to their senses, the two of them were already quite far from the mountain path, being carried by the researcher.*

The only thing they remembered was a powerful signal saying, “There is nothing.”

*Ontological terror.*

*The mind that had nearly been consumed there had made his heart pound like crazy...*

—We will change the destination.

*In the end, following the researcher’s guidance, they reorganized their route and forced their way here.*

*The entrance to a maintenance track.*

*It was relatively close to Sekwang Station, but since it was a staff-only track for an unmanned station, the place was desolate.*

*They had already turned off their smartphones, and since the strange “procession” was circling the downtown area rather than here, they could finally catch their breath.*

*The researcher said this place might regain its function as an exit to the outside...*

*That hard-to-understand statement hadn’t come true yet—but a note had arrived from the agent, nonetheless.*

*However, now, quite some time had passed since the last note was sent, and still no reply had come.*

*“Hm.”*

*Ryu Jaekwan watched the reporter Eun Haje—whose sharp gaze still hadn’t dulled—enter and exit the underground tracks, searching around.*

*Soon, though, the reporter came back, shrugging.*

*Eyes trying to stay calm, yet clearly confused.*

*“Doesn’t seem like there’s anything unusual.”*

*Anxiety crept up from his feet.*

*And then, at that moment—*

Thunk.

*Something abruptly appeared in midair at the entrance to the underground tracks.*

*A shoe sole, then a long leg in a suit stepped out.*

*Supernaturally.*

*“...!!”*

*The lower body emerged, then the torso above the waist. And then revealed—*

*A face.*

*“Hello.”*

*“......”*

*“I’m glad you’re all safe.”*

*It was the agent.*

*...the person they thought might have died, crushed beneath concrete and rebar, now stood neatly dressed, face clean, smiling as he looked over the three of them.*

*Completely unharmed.*

*For a moment, joy and relief surged up all at once—*

*“We don’t have much time.”*

*The agent urgently grabbed their shoulders and pushed them into the tracks.*

*“You need to stay somewhere as safe as possible. Go inside the tracks for now. That’s subway territory.”*

*“O-okay.”*

*As Ryu Jaekwan stepped into the tracks, he glanced back.*

*He could hear the researcher and the agent talking beside him.*

*The conversation, full of implications, stirred more questions.*

*“Mr. Roe Deer.”*

*“It’s fine. ...I also received help from ‘us.’ I’m ready.”*

*Ready for what?*

*What exactly had happened?*

*If the researcher really went outside, like they said, was it okay to come back in?*

*How could he be this clean after only a few hours?*

*But before any of those questions could be answered, the agent was already moving.*

*As if without a shred of hesitation.*

*As if all mental preparation had already been completed.*

*“I’ll be going.”*

*After patting the researcher’s shoulder one last time, the agent was already far away.*

*The suited figure ran beyond the tracks.*

*At some point, he was wearing a mask he’d never worn before—a horned mask.*

*The words he left behind lingered in their ears.*

*“Before today ends, you’ll be able to get out safely.”*

*Only the sound of people marching echoed.*

*It was probably the moment when the “mass” I’d seen falling from the train was being formed...*

Could it be that the mass is born from the cocoon at Sekwang City Hall?

*...The cocoon was now swelling absurdly—what-is-that-abnormal-abnormal-strange-weird-shape—bloated into a biological disaster! A biological catastrophe spreading—*

*【 Action! 】*

*...!*

*“...Thanks.”*

*【 My pleasure! 】*

*I immediately pulled a piece of candy from my pocket, unwrapped it, and put it in my mouth.*

*Nostalgia Candy.*

*My body and mind returned to a clean, lucid state.*

*It melted quickly, but that was fine.*

I’ll just keep putting more in.

*I grabbed a handful from my pocket.*

*This was what it meant to have the full cooperation of Director Ho Yuwon of Daydream Inc.*

—Mr. Roe Deer. If you need anything, I’ll try to prepare it.

*From the moment I succeeded in persuading Ho Yuwon, I used Daydream Inc.’s capital to buy every item I needed.*

*Items usable within Sekwang Special City—without borrowing power from external beings.*

*Verified items.*

*That didn’t mean I’d merely read about other people’s use cases in the Dark Exploration Records.*

*It meant items I had personally used over time.*

*Everything I had carefully chosen in this strange and terrifying world, experiencing both side effects and benefits firsthand.*

*I now knew what worked, and what could be used in this situation with minimal risk.*

*I also had plenty of people around to ask.*

—Uh... the Nostalgia series should... work...

—Yeah. Artifacts from the same origin tend to influence each other easily. Even if one side is overwhelmingly stronger. Exploiting that gap should be fine.

*The prepared items clinked in my pockets. Instead of a tattooed inventory filled with a Potion Machine, my real pockets were full.*

*And from my waist, a bell rang.*

*...Agent Choi’s equipment.*

*After the signing, as if he’d never interfered with me at all, Agent Choi crammed countless “pro tips” into me—painfully thorough and precise attribute guidance.*

*And at the end, he handed me this.*

*An agent-issued item of his own.*

—This bell was kept inside a goblin cap. I didn’t dedicate it to anyone, but its nature changed on its own.

—If you wear this, you’ll run into fewer impure things. Like slightly avoiding people on the street shouting “Believe in the Way.”

—Put it on from the very start. Save it and you’ll get wiped out. Got it, Grapes?

*I moved straight ahead.*

*Whether thanks to the bell or not, crowds and strange gazes brushed past me.*

*But this bell alone wouldn’t let me reach the core of a world-ending disaster.*

*So I took out the next measure.*

*The most decisive guide.*

—For the final attempt, I need Ms. Yeongeun’s cooperation.

—Say anything. No, this time, I actually want to go in myself.

*But since everyone went mad when they heard the music box, that wasn’t possible.*

*While desperately persuading Ms. Yeongeun, I asked her colleague to help create an item.*

*An object that had illuminated my path and protected my group when we fell into a large-scale darkness.*

—Once again, please.

*A mystical candle kit.*

*A product from the Cheerful Research Institute—sharing the same origin as this incident.*

*Honor, healing, meditation, wounds, lies, rage, interference, protection, strike, gaze, confusion, dream.*

*You draw three of these word cards and perform a ritual to create a candle reflecting those attributes.*

*The personalities and desires of the three creators are strongly reflected.*

*Which made Ms. Go Yeongeun the perfect candidate.*

*Someone with a strong sense of purpose toward the conclusion, a stakeholder of Sekwang Special City.*

*Above all, someone proven.*

—Shall we do it now?

*Previously, this colleague had drawn “Honor” at the Mansion of the Blind, and “Confusion” at Lookie Mart.*

*And now, at the end of the journey, the card drawn was—*

*【 Gaze 】*

*A firm sense of purpose, and a will that pierces a single point.*

*I accepted it gratefully.*

*But it wasn’t over. These word cards were meant to be drawn by three people.*

*And I already knew the next suitable person.*

*To safely reach the rooftop, a motive prioritizing one’s own safety was also necessary.*

—W-what is it?

—I need a favor. Please.

*Baek Saheon.*

*That Daydream colleague with an intense obsession with survival eventually came to the counseling room and drew a card with us.*

*【 Protection 】*

*I remembered that the first card he’d drawn back then was “Interference.”*

*It gave me a strange feeling.*

—......

*Baek Sahyeon finished unusually quietly. He seemed to be observing me, somehow—but that was all.*

*And at this point, the final participant was already decided.*

*The person who actually needed this candle.*

*Me.*

*“......”*

*I drew a card.*

*In my hand—which had previously drawn “Lie” and “Protection”—was—*

*【 Meditation 】*

*...A mindset seeking to escape prejudice and pain, and to reflect upon the world.*

*“......”*

*The three of us, former Daydream colleagues, were the same people who had first created a candle together.*

*But this time, with completely different keywords.*

*Thus it was completed.*

*【 Gaze, Protection, Meditation. 】*

*A powerful sense of purpose toward the end.*

*The desire for self-preservation.*

*And the user’s reflection.*

*As the three elements merged, the well-hardened candle now hung from my hand as I ran toward City Hall.*

*“......”*

*...Because from the beginning, I didn’t even have a human body.*

And I didn’t even understand that properly.

*Since I didn’t know who I was or how I came here, I couldn’t even discuss qualifications.*

Creeeak.

*I didn’t let go of the candle.*

*I’d practiced for hours to do it quickly with one hand.*

I can do this.

Hurry, hurry.

I can’t just rip them out...!
