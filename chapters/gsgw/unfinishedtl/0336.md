# Chapter 335

My studio apartment in Sekwang Special City.

I stare blankly as a human hand suddenly bursts out from within a blanket patterned with white lizards.

As I witness something that is realistically impossible, I feel a deep shock—and relief.

Without knowing why I feel either of those things.

“Mr. Roe Deer.”

Someone inside the blanket speaks to me.

I don’t know why I’m being called Roe Deer.

But my mouth opens anyway.

“Do you... know me?”

“Yes.”

“Then... what kind of manager are you? Why do I call you ‘Manager’?”

“That is not important.”

A black-gloved hand, emerging from the blanket, speaks.

I swallow.

“...Why am I trapped on May 4th? What on earth... is this situation?”

“It is the situation you wished for.”

What?

“No, that can’t be... That can’t be true!”

Just thinking about how many times—how endlessly—I’ve repeated May 4th makes me feel like I’m going insane!

It’s a miracle I haven’t lost my mind already. The only reason I’ve endured is because May 4th is genuinely such a calm, pleasant day...

......

A good day.

A good routine.

“Does May 4th hold some special, positive meaning for me?”

“I do not know.”

“Am I obsessed with May 4th, unable to escape it?”

“No.”

“Then what is this, exactly?”

“Do you want a hypothesis?”

“...Yes.”

From within the blanket comes a dry, even-toned male voice.

“This place is a projection of your wish to return home.”

......!!

“I don’t understand what you mean—”

Why am I so shocked?

“Because Mr. Roe Deer’s wish is to return home, it is presumed that this desire has been projected here.”

......

What does that mean?

I want to go home? Then shouldn’t that be my parents’ house in Bundang?

Yes—going back to weekends helping out at my sibling’s dessert café, shouldn’t that be it? Why would I wish to return to this day, alone in a studio apartment in another city?

That’s... that’s...

......

“...In reality.”

I swallow.

“What state am I actually in?”

There is no answer.

“What kind of state am I in, that I would wish for this—this single day, May 4th, to repeat forever?”

“Mr. Roe Deer.”

“Why am I Roe Deer?”

That unfamiliar name fills me with fear for the first time.

What state am I in?

I...

“Please calm yourself.”

“......”

“Would you like my hypothesis?”

“...Yes.”

“The desire for May 4th to repeat is a separate factor.”

The calmness of the voice dampens my fear.

“This is a day recreated in a special way, after your wish was affected by the disaster in Sekwang Special City.”

Disaster.

At that word, an inexplicable anxiety grips me, cold sweat forming at my temples.

“It is presumed that your wish interacted with it, resulting in this phenomenon.”

Wish.

At that moment, a phrase flashes through my mind.

Children’s Paradise Syrup.

The prototype of wish-granting tokens—“medicine that grants wishes.”

The Cheerful Research Institute that tried to use it for Sekwang Special City’s project, and the horrific disaster that seemed to result from it.

......

What is this?

Where is this knowledge coming from?

“Urk.”

I suppress the bile rising in my throat.

My mind, soaked in 612 days of peace, begins to grind with a strange noise.

And then—

“Manager.”

Gasping, I look at the blanket.

“...It seems your arm has come further out.”

“Yes.”

It keeps extending.

Now nearly the shoulder is visible beyond the blanket.

It had been stretched out motionless, yet somehow—

There shouldn’t be space between the nightstand and the blanket, but it’s as if someone had been hiding between its folds and was emerging.

Or as if the blanket itself were mutating.

“Is that... good?”

“No.”

The flat voice echoes from inside.

“It means the time you have to leave this place, using the blanket as an alien source of dissonance, is decreasing.”

...!!

“Then—then I should leave immediately! It’s dangerous—”

“But no.”

“...No?”

“Yes.”

I stare blankly at the right arm that’s almost fully emerged.

“What do you mean?”

“Considering the traumatic circumstances you are about to experience in reality, remaining here may statistically be safer.”

“......”

“If you choose to stay, leave the blanket as it is. You have 10 minutes and 32 seconds.”

I stare at the hand emerging from the blanket.

“You’re saying it might be safer here.”

“Yes.”

I turn around.

Beyond the wide living-room window stretches the view of Sekwang Special City on May 4th—a pleasant spring day, a holiday both yesterday and tomorrow...

Forever.

“...Can you tell me exactly what’s happening in reality?”

“That is impossible.”

“...Can you at least tell me what situation you and I are in right now?”

“That is impossible.”

......Then.

My mouth opens slowly.

“Even so, I’m going out.”

Not only because staying here any longer would drive me insane.

“I want to know what kind of situation this is.”

Frustration.

A drive to uncover the truth and solution.

That unusually powerful motivation solidifies into a clear direction within me.

And finally—

Sweating, I grin crookedly.

“Because staying here without knowing anything feels even more terrifying.”

......

“Confirmed.”

The reply is just as calm as before.

But somehow, it feels like the mouth of the white lizard character on the blanket has curved into a smile.

“Then prepare yourself.”

Prepare?

“What should I do?”

“Gather what you will take with you.”

I spring to my feet. Scanning my familiar apartment, my eyes land on the rabbit plush on the bed.

The one that had sat in my pocket hundreds of times.

“Um... can I take this with me?”

“I do not recommend it.”

Reflexively, I glance at the rabbit plush.

“You have 7 minutes and 24 seconds remaining.”

Damn it. After a brief hesitation, I grab the plush anyway.

Even if it’s not recommended, there must be a reason it’s stayed with me all this time.

And—

“By the way... does this rabbit plush normally talk?”

“No.”

......Whew.

What was I expecting? Of course, talking to a plush is ridiculous—

“Stuffed toys cannot speak.”

“No, I mean—not plush toys in general. I mean this rabbit plush specifically... can it talk?”

“I see.”

Then comes the simple answer.

“Yes.”

......!

I look at the rabbit plush.

Its pebble-like black eyes.

“Is it... talking right now?”

“Yes.”

“Then why can’t I hear it?”

“It is presumed to be a necessary condition for realizing your wish, Mr. Roe Deer.”

“......”

I don’t understand.

But with a trembling voice, I ask:

“What... is it saying? If you can hear it, can you tell me?”

“Yes.”

“‘Friend, this Braun is fine.’”

Braun.

“...Braun.”

I carefully call the rabbit plush.

There is no reply. Still, I tuck it carefully—not into my pocket this time, but into the front pouch.

I feel more at ease.

“Then—does this coin have any meaning? It keeps turning black every time May 4th repeats. It also tingles.”

“I do not know. Do you want a hypothesis?”

“...Yes.”

“Discoloration is commonly observed when a talisman absorbs corruption in place of its bearer.”

A talisman?

“The tingling is presumed to be a stimulus meant to cause you dissonance. It is possible that another entity is sending you signals through the coin.”

“......”

Another entity?

But I’m alone here, aren’t I? I’ve experienced today dozens—no, hundreds of times alone...

......

Anxiety wells up. I feel like I need to confirm something—fast.

“You have 5 minutes and 14 seconds remaining.”

“I’m ready!”

I step straight toward the blanket.

The arm I sensed as unnatural now extends past the shoulder, nearly revealing the entire upper body.

Even the vague outline of a head is visible beneath the blanket.

......

A human jawline.

Was it... always shaped like that? It feels like it should have been more grotesque.

“Hold on.”

After a deep breath, I grab the arm without hesitation.

Immediately, an overwhelming force yanks me into the blanket.

“......!”

Thump—my head slams into the blanket, the fabric rippling as it covers my face.

Like sleep paralysis.

‘Hrk!’

The familiar softness wraps around my head—but only for a moment before it seals every opening on my face.

‘Wait—’

I can’t breathe. I reach for the blanket in panic, but there’s nothing to grab. Just as I begin to struggle—

All sensation disappears.

“......”

No smell. No sight. No sound.

Inside the blanket.

The beautiful May 4th I experienced—no matter how I reached or struggled—vanishes from my senses.

It’s gone.

Instead of relief, a strange surge of emotion wells up.

But I’m still there—and soon I realize.

My consciousness. My emotions.

And then, like a flood, my sense of self returns.

‘Ah.’

I understand why I’m called Roe Deer.

It was a nickname I got while working at Daydream Corporation.

And Daydream Corporation was the urban-legend company from **Records of Darkness Exploration** .

One day, I was pulled into this urban-legend world. I met many people—those listed on the wiki, and those who weren’t.

All the pain and happiness.

All the purpose.

And in the end, my wish... was still to go home.

Right.

‘...I told that story to Manager Lizard.’

I now know the identity of the hand from the blanket.

Leader of Field Investigation D-squad.

Manager Lee Jaheon.

“......”

I open my eyes.

Pain. Agony. And the familiar head of a white lizard.

The reptilian alien is gripping me, as if pulling me out of somewhere.

‘What the hell.’

Even in pain, I almost laugh.

Thanks to him, I survived again. Whew. Looks like I’d been trapped in a loop-type urban legend where a single day repeats.

How many times? ...612? 613? Just thinking about it makes me dizzy. I desperately try to treat it as a dream—I can’t let myself get pulled back into it.

‘Focus.’

I shake my head. Now I need to synchronize with Manager Lee Jaheon and get out, then assess the situation.

I look at his movement—

......

He isn’t moving.

“...Manager?”

I look down.

There’s nothing.

Below his waist—nothing remains.

“Huh.”

I twist my body.

The hand holding me falls away.

Lee Jaheon’s torso—only the upper half—drops down.

Thud.

A short, brutal sound.

......

Huh?

Huh—??

‘Just now—’

What just happened?

This is wrong. Manager Lee Jaheon—Employee D—wouldn’t die like this. He even becomes a Team Leader later—

Huh?

What? What just happened— I reach out to retrieve his body—

But I realize it’s impossible, because I barely have limbs either. My body is attached to a massive sphere, screaming, along with other mouths making the same sounds.

The end has come!

There is a paradise!

We have failed!

What

Whatwhatwhatisthiswhatisthis

What state am I in right now?

I look at the empty air.

From afar, a train approaches. A train! It rushes past us, skimming overhead, heading far away—outside.

Why?

The end has come!

There is a paradise!

We have failed!

My body is being sucked into the sphere. Pulled in further.

No! No!

I twist. Beneath the rolling mass, my remaining lower body is crushed and torn away in an instant. Splat—my body hits the ground.

I begin to crawl through the ruins.

The bleeding worsens. My consciousness fades. The pain dulls. Something is behind me—I have to avoid it—I can’t be attached again—I don’t want to—I still—

Still—

[Mr. Roe Deer.]

[This is as far as you go.]

Dead.

“Ghk—huff!”

I lift my head.

The familiar department store lobby comes into view—no, no! No!

I’m back! Back to May 4th! I wish it were just a dream— No, I stagger up from the chair, trying to run back to my apartment—

[Oh my, friend! Are you all right?]

......

Ah.

[Here I am. Quickly—check your front pocket!]

I look down.

I search my pockets. Reflexively, my hand leaves my pants pocket and moves to the front pouch. Then—

“Braun...”

The soft rabbit plush is there.

Along with a pleasant host-like voice.

[Your friend Braun is right here!]

[Your kind friend—who endured a dull, predictable, endlessly repeating scene for 613 days without skipping a single moment—the entertainer of this era, is with you!]

“......”

Right.

Right......

I steady my breathing.

I need to get my head straight.

‘My situation.’

I painfully drag memories from what feels like hundreds of days ago.

I fell from a train.

From a subway crossing Sekwang Special City, headed outward—the cab door suddenly opened, and I fell into the city below.

Exposed directly to an extinction-class disaster, and now, inside it...

“Did I... return to May 4th again?”

[First, it would be wise to confirm what is different and what is the same, Mr. Roe Deer!]

I immediately start checking my condition.

‘Clothes...’

Not a suit.

The outfit I wore on the train, pretending to be Agent Grapes. Glasses on.

‘Different.’

My mind calms.

But one difference chills me.

...the silver coin is gone.

The coin imbued with the counselor’s ego.

‘...It had turned black.’

A bad feeling.

But I don’t have time to dwell on it.

Bzzzzzz!!

My smartphone begins to vibrate.

No—every smartphone of everyone in the department store lobby, all at once.

A chill runs through me.

With a sense of foreboding, I open my phone.

[Sekwang Special City] Emergency Alert Issued.  A suspected terrorist explosion has occurred near City Hall. Presumed to involve lethal biochemical weapons.

Multiple explosions suspected. Citizens are instructed to prepare for evacuation and to remain indoors under all circumstances.

“......”

That’s when I realize.

This is May 4th.

But not the calm, beautiful May 4th I experienced.

—It was May. The day before Children’s Day.

—First period had just started when the disaster alert came. Terror at City Hall.

The day of disaster.

This is the day an extinction-class disaster struck Sekwang Special City.

But how?

I definitely fell from the train and died. And this should be a day from years ago—so how...?

......!

—The desire for May 4th to repeat is a separate factor.

—A day recreated in a special way, after your wish was affected by the disaster.

My wish was “a peaceful home with no urban legends.” If the repeated May 4th I experienced was influenced by the disaster in Sekwang Special City, then—

‘...A daily loop.’

A new conclusion forms.

“Aaaah!”

“What is that?!”

Screams begin faintly from somewhere outside the department store.

Frozen, I put my realization into words.

Sekwang Special City.

Inside this sealed city, today has been repeating endlessly.

The day of disaster.

Hundreds, thousands of times.

Forever.

And now, I am trapped inside it.

“......”

A deep chill runs through me.

‘No.’

What should I do in this situation?

Wait—wait—

‘The tattoo!’

Right. When I returned to my original body, I got a tattoo. I need to pull out two people first. I urgently reach into it.

There’s no way they endured 612 days inside this place, right? No way...

......

Nothing.

Neither Agent Bronze, nor Assistant Manager Eun Haje.

Nothing.

The space inside the tattoo—meant for two people—is completely empty.

“......”

My mind starts to blur—

‘No!’

Focus. Get a grip.

‘I just died and woke up again.’

Here, in the department store, in the morning.

Let’s assume others experienced something similar. Maybe the people in the tattoo were also placed somewhere in this disaster day.

Agent Bronze. Assistant Manager Eun Haje.

Manager Lee Jaheon too.

Then what I need to do now is—

‘Find them.’

“Aaaah!”

I look outside the department store doors, where chaos is starting to erupt.

And then I run out.

Into May 4th of Sekwang Special City—

The very moment the extinction-class disaster begins.
