# Chapter 354

*Re-entering the Segwang Special City subway.*

*“This way.”*

*“Yep!”*

*I was walking along the tracks with Assistant Manager Lee Seonghae, flashlights in hand.*

*There was enough space to step aside if a train approached, and even if we were struck and died, we’d wake up outside, so our pace was calm.*

*Still, wary of any sudden situation, I kept my ears pricked as I talked with Assistant Manager Lee.*

*Thanks to having slept, my thoughts were clearer than before.*

*Even when replaying my conversation with Agent Choi.*

*I realized it clearly.*

...He never believed me from the start.

*—So you’re saying you got caught up in a supernatural disaster and fell into this place. The world suddenly changed, and you got equipment.*

*Before.*

*When I told Agent Choi that I’d come from another world and wanted to go back, I’d felt relieved by his calm reaction.*

*I thought he’d accepted my situation.*

*But it wasn’t acceptance......*

It was just... caution.

*Had he already decided back then that I couldn’t be trusted?*

...Why?

*Back at the Mermaid’s Grave... and the Glittering Dragon Palace, hadn’t we seen children from other worlds?*

*Parallel worlds weren’t such an unfamiliar concept in this urban-legend world.*

*That much... couldn’t he at least believe?*

*“......”*

*My mind felt strangely foggy.*

*Through it, Assistant Manager Lee’s clear voice rang out cleanly.*

*“Have you been doing well all this time?”*

*Ah.*

*“I heard you fell from above the city at the train shelter and couldn’t wake up, but then you went and saved people again!”*

*I tugged faintly at the corner of my mouth.*

*“That was... honestly more thanks to Ms. Goral and Agent Bronze than me.”*

*“Huh? The three of you saved them together?!”*

*Up to there, it was heartwarming.*

*“But those shelter people didn’t really seem that nice, you know... I kinda wish a few of the nasty ones had died instead.”*

*“...Excuse me?”*

*“I heard Agent Bronze still hasn’t woken up. It feels unfair! If we could swap it even now, that’d be nice.”*

*Whoa. The shock snapped my hazy mind fully awake.*

*Right—Assistant Manager Lee had always been this kind of person...*

*I barely managed to respond.*

*“...There was no such way. And the shelter people... most of them weren’t especially bad.”*

*I added, “People become irrational in extreme situations.”*

*As I said it, a thought crossed my mind.*

*Had I looked like that, too?*

*Always saying I wanted to go home......*

*“That’s true!”*

*As if answering the thought, Assistant Manager Lee said, “But even when people make ‘oh no, I messed up!’ choices under pressure... there are definitely kind people among those wrong choices.”*

*“......”*

*“Like you, Mr. Roe Deer.”*

*“Me? ...I don’t think so.”*

*“Nope. You were a really good person even back when you were Mascot-nim. Even when you didn’t have to be.”*

*“......”*

*“That’s really amazing.”*

*“...Thank you.”*

*That oddly subjective and firm statement was strangely comforting.*

*“Anyway, Mascot-nim’s shop is doing fine, so I honestly wasn’t that worried while you were missing!”*

*“That’s... a relief.”*

*Somehow, I felt at ease.*

*There was a strange comfort in that extremeness.*

*I’d been breaking into cold sweats thinking that if things went south, I might be stabbed for being ‘a bad person,’ but now I felt this person’s clarity was nice.*

*Because they answered any question without hesitation.*

*So I asked it.*

*A question I hadn’t dared to ask before, because it was sensitive and related to me.*

*“Um, may I ask what your wish is, Dolphin-nim?”*

*“My wish?”*

*“Yes. The wish you’d make if you got a Wish Ticket.”*

*A mysterious smile spread across Assistant Manager Lee’s lips.*

*“Should I be honest?”*

*“...I’d appreciate that.”*

*“Alright...”*

*They leaned in and whispered in my ear.*

*“I actually already saved up all the points for a Wish Ticket.”*

......!!

*“Five hundred thousand points. All of them.”*

*I turned to Assistant Manager Lee with wide, shocked eyes.*

*She was still smiling.*

*“Other people contracted to get a Wish Ticket if this project succeeds, right? I just asked them to count the points based on the grade of dream-fluid we produce.”*

*Plus, since they entered lots of high-grade urban legends as part of normal duties, the points piled up faster.*

*So just a week ago, she passed 500,000 points...*

*“......”*

*“Oh, Assistant Manager Butterfly’s probably almost there, too! She might’ve earned even more points than me, but she spends a lot on the company’s special painkillers!”*

*“Then...”*

*I swallowed.*

*“Why... haven’t you quit?”*

*“......”*

*Assistant Manager Lee stopped her flowing chatter and looked straight at me.*

*“I haven’t decided on my wish yet.”*

*What did that mean?*

*“...Didn’t you join because you had a wish?”*

*“Mm, well, I do have a wish in my heart, but I’m still thinking about how to make it come true.”*

*Ah.*

*“Because you have to phrase a Wish Ticket in one sentence?”*

*“Yep! And I really want the world itself to change. Not just me going to a world where my wish came true.”*

*“That would be... difficult.”*

*“Right? That’s what I’m struggling with!”*

*Walking boldly along the tracks, Assistant Manager Lee spoke just as boldly.*

*Her wish was...*

*“I want only good people to remain in the world.”*

*“......!”*

*“So I thought about it, and it felt unfair for bad people to just turn good without paying any price... so at first, I thought about wishing for all bad people to disappear.”*

*Cold sweat ran down my back.*

*“Um, may I ask what your standard for ‘bad people’ is...?”*

*“People who are immoral and unethical! Like those who shove others aside in the dark just to survive? Feels like about half the population!”*

*“...If you make that wish, would all those people disappear?”*

*“Yep!”*

*It felt almost like madness.*

*Had my saying that I wanted to go home sounded like this?*

*Completely sincere to the speaker, but crazy to anyone listening?*

*That crazy?*

*No—this might be rude to Assistant Manager Lee—though is it rude to say that about someone who was ready to wipe out half of humanity without hesitation...?*

*“...What about wishing to clean up the environment? Like developing sustainable energy resources...?”*

*“Oh, that’s nice! That’d be good for Earth in the long run!”*

*Yes......*

*But Assistant Manager Lee didn’t say, “I’ll make that my wish.”*

*I don’t think I’ll ever forget that...*

Ha...

【 *Oh, my friend really does have talent as an interviewer! What will you ask next? 】*

*Now... I wasn’t going to talk about that anymore...*

*Quietly swallowing my breath, I kept walking along the tracks.*

*“A train’s coming.”*

*After letting several trains rush past us from behind.*

*“......”*

*“Is it there?”*

*“Yes.”*

*At last, I found the subway depot connected to the line leading to the “maintenance track” the train shelter had escaped through.*

*Unlike other depots, there was something familiar here.*

*When I shone the flashlight beyond that stretch of connected track, at the far end, I could see:*

Safety First During Maintenance

Two Workers Required

*Through that passage, the train shelter had escaped to the surface of Segwang Special City...*

*“......”*

*I glanced at Assistant Manager Lee.*

*It seemed she’d only heard a condensed version of what the outside was like...*

*“Shall we go in, just to check the route before actually going for real?”*

*I shook my head.*

*“I’ll go alone. We don’t know where exactly counts as ‘outside,’ so I’ll be very careful...”*

*“Huh? We’ll be careful anyway, let’s go together!”*

*And Assistant Manager Lee started striding toward the maintenance track.*

*No—*

*I tried to say something, then gave up and hurried after her.*

*I had no confidence I could persuade her... I’d just have to watch carefully for any signs.*

*So we walked along the straight track, up a sloped path leading upward.*

*Thanks to the grooves in the track, it was actually easier to step and climb.*

*“It’s actually kind of comfy. And we don’t have to worry about trains coming from behind anymore.”*

*“...Yes.”*

Tap, tap.

*Our footsteps echoed in the dark tracks.*

*Upward, higher.*

*Toward the surface.*

*“......”*

*To shake off the pressure, I asked slowly,*

*“Um, how have things been on the new Project Team?”*

*“I’ve been doing well! Lots of familiar faces, so it wasn’t awkward. But I miss the many good people who are gone.”*

*Hmm.*

*“Oh! I eat lots of snacks with the Security Chief! Mr. Badger mostly talks to me only in the subway, though.”*

*Assistant Manager Lee cheerfully shared news about the Security Team and Team A.*

*“People from Team A could probably be reassigned to you this time if you want? Director Ho said they absolutely have to cooperate.”*

*“...I see.”*

*Most of the people I knew seemed to be doing fine.*

*Not all the stories were bright, of course...*

*“Oh, Chief Pony’s doing okay, too, but she keeps hanging around the reopened body casino. I don’t think she’ll live long!”*

*“...I see.”*

*You don’t mean you’ll personally make that happen, right?*

*Anyway, hearing everyday updates helped bring my sunken state of mind back onto its proper track.*

*We kept talking as we walked through the dark tunnel.*

*And then...*

*After walking upward for quite a while.*

*“......”*

*“......”*

*“I don’t think we need the flashlight anymore.”*

*Sunlight poured down onto my head.*

*At the far end of the straight track, I could see the exit.*

*The point where the underground ended.*

*The natural light stabbing into the underground tracks made goosebumps rise as it reached my eyes...*

...There.

*I knew instinctively.*

*That exit marked the boundary between the surface and underground of Segwang Special City.*

*But—*

*“Why don’t we need the flashlight?”*

*“...Pardon?”*

*Assistant Manager Lee’s voice reached me.*

*I raised my hand slightly to point at the sunlit exit.*

*“...Can you see it?”*

*“Hmm...”*

*They waved the flashlight and shrugged.*

*“I can see, like, a pitch-black exit or something.”*

*“......”*

*It seemed the sunlight was visible only to me.*

Is it... because I’ve been to the surface once?

*That felt right.*

*Because I’d belonged to the ‘outside’ before, my senses were open to recognizing it...*

*“Shall we go?”*

*“Wait!”*

*I panicked and managed to grab Assistant Manager Lee’s shoulder before she rushed ahead—no, why are you so strong?!*

*“Let’s go together. Slowly. Carefully.”*

*“Yep!”*

*I stepped forward.*

*I kept the flashlight on, just in case.*

*“......”*

*The closer I got to the exit, the stronger a strange pull and impulse became.*

...It feels like I’m being sucked in.

*Was it calling to me, someone who’d slipped out of the seal?*

*“Hmm, doesn’t look like there’s anything there... Do you see something beyond?”*

*“To me, yes.”*

*It seemed that to Assistant Manager Lee, the outside surface felt like ‘nothing’ due to the seal’s influence.*

*Cognitively unreachable.*

Hoo.

*Sweat slicked the hand gripping the flashlight.*

*“...Dolphin-nim. If something happens, can you pull me back?”*

*“Of course!”*

*I moved forward one step at a time, carefully checking for anything unusual.*

Tap. Tap.

*When only my height’s distance remained between me and the exit—*

*The exit, which had shown only sunlight, began to ripple...*

...Ah.

*A strangely distorted, obscured view of the outside.*

*Like when I’d escaped to the surface before and looked out from the train shelter window, scenery appeared beyond the track exit.*

*But fainter than before.*

*As if it might show itself—*

*Somewhere... wavering...*

...Is it because the seal is being lifted?

*I resisted the urge to leap into it and finished my check.*

If I go out, it’ll definitely be the surface of Segwang Special City...

*That was enough.*

*Just as I was about to step back—*

*Something began to appear in the wavering scene outside the exit.*

*“...!”*

*I froze.*

*My eyes, now accustomed to seeing the ‘outside,’ picked out a silhouette within the distorted view.*

*A person.*

*A large build, a familiar silhouette.*

*“...Manager?”*

*Manager Lee Jaheon.*

*The lizard leader of Team D was standing blankly at the front of the passage.*

*“...!!”*

*Wait—why was he here...?*

*I wondered if it was a hallucination, but the longer I stared, the clearer the silhouette became.*

*And behind him, I could make out two more figures.*

*Wait—two people would mean...*

...The assistant manager and the agent!

*I almost gave in to the urge to rush outside.*

*No!*

*“Yah!”*

*...I turned to Assistant Manager Lee, who had pulled me back, and barely spoke.*

*“Thank you.”*

*“Yep! Oh, is there something outside?”*

*“...Yes.”*

*Clenching my fist, I stepped back and took a deep breath, thinking.*

*Was there some way to communicate? I couldn’t hear them, and I couldn’t read their lips...... Ah!*

*I pulled out pen and paper and wrote:*

*—Roe Deer is here.*

*“......”*

I crumpled the paper and threw it beyond the sunlight.
