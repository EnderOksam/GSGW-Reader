# Chapter 233

The one waiting for me in front of the elevator.

The new hire, Kim Heowoon.

“Mr. Employee?”

I cannot distinguish human faces.

They look like melted mosaics, and their voices are as indistinct as an old radio recording.

Therefore, I cannot tell how much this ‘new hire Kim Heowoon’, standing before the elevator now, resembles the Jang Heowoon I once knew… but one thing I can be certain of.

‘…A strange situation.’

This is strange.

And the one who knows this better than I do is standing beside me.

One of the directors of this company.

“Hello. You must be the new hire with the Field Exploration Team.”

“Ah. Yes, sir…! My name is Kim Heowoon.”

“I see. …But how did you come in here?”

“……Pardon?”

“You’re with the Field Exploration Team, aren’t you? Then you wouldn’t have been able to enter this Security Team sector on your own… so how are you here?”

Silence.

“And how did you find this elevator? And to that this Mr. Employee would be here,”

Ho Yoowon pointed at me.

“How did you know that?”

“……”

The mouth opened.

<br>

“I imitated.”

<br>

…What?

“I imitated a Security Team member to get in, and I imitated the Research Team to learn the employee’s location.”

‘Imitated…?’

The moment a sense of foreboding rose.

A smile spread across Kim Heowoon’s indistinguishable face.

“But with this, my purpose for joining has already been fulfilled, so I don’t mind being fired over it.”

And he looked at me.

“Mr. Employee.”

The purpose of joining the company.

<br>

—It’s because there was something I had to find.

<br>

It was me.

“You don’t need to worry. Nothing troublesome has happened. I only came here to seize the opportunity to introduce myself properly… and to express my gratitude.”

Then Kim Heowoon bowed deeply before me.

“Thank you for saving us. Thanks to you, all the people at the facility survived.”

……

Wait.

“Because of you, Mr. Employee, Jang Heowoon’s wish was fulfilled.”

That means…

“Aha. How unfortunate. Looks like my guess was wrong, Roe Deer-nim.”

<br>

I realized.

<br>

The two Wish Tickets delivered to Go Yeongeun-ssi being used did not mean…

‘They weren’t used to save Jang Heowoon himself…!’

From the start, the chance that such a smart and composed person would suddenly make a sloppy wish on a Wish Ticket was low. If anything…

‘She would have felt more unsettled to keep both Wish Tickets in her possession.’

Because someone might find out and steal them.

And she may also have heard that, while the Disaster Management Bureau was doing its best to improve Jang Heowoon’s condition, there was no telling when he would recover fully.

In that case, before some mishap happened while holding onto the Wish Tickets indefinitely… it would have been far more reasonable for her to use one on Jang Heowoon’s behalf.

Recovery from contamination had precedents, like with Security Team members, so she could entrust that much to the Disaster Management Bureau.

Instead, she would have made the true wish.

Like this.

<br>

<br>

#><**Please grant Jang Heowoon-ssi’s wish.**><#

<br>

<br>

<br>

And Jang Heowoon’s wish was…

<br>

<br>

#f#*– I wanted to save my precious family.*#f#

<br>

<br>

……

Before my eyes, I saw the result of Jang Heowoon’s wish being granted.

A family member, bearing the name Kim Heowoon.

One of the people from a countryside religious welfare facility where the orphan Jang Heowoon had grown up. A place that had burned down in a fire.

<br>

[What an interesting situation, *Friend*.]

[But moreover. How exactly does this person know that it was you, *Friend*, who was the one who handed over the Wish Ticket?]

[And doesn’t the explanation of ‘I imitated’ strike you as rather vague?]

<br>

…That.

‘I have a… suspicion.’

The chill crawling up my spine told me. A single hypothesis that struck me like a shock in my mind.

…I’ll narrow it down through Q&A.

<br>

*Question : Relationship with Jang Heowoon*

<br>

“Ah. We are family who grew up in the same facility. Out of eight, seven died in the fire.”

<br>

*Question : Reason for sharing the same name*

<br>

“In the facility, those who lived together were given the same name. The surnames differed, but we shared the given name. Because we’re family.”

<br>

*Question : Rules shared by the family*

<br>

“There weren’t really rules, but… we received the same education, ate the same food, and slept in the same place in order to have a lifestyle fitting the name ‘Heowoon’. So that we could each have the same experiences, fairly.”

<br>

*Question : Example of a ‘lifestyle fitting the name Heowoon’*

<br>

“Ah. It wasn’t difficult. For example… going to bed late and waking up late.”

……

“Reading poetry collections more than twice a week, becoming afraid of blood, starting middle school one year early, and dropping out of high school.”

And.

“When meeting shamans or other religious figures, not introducing one’s own name.”

……

The final question.

<br>

*Question : Name of the facility*

<br>

“Ah, that place is…”

And I already knew the answer.

In this world, there are countless bizarre groups, but only one insane group that operates a childcare facility in this way…

“…It is a facility called Paradise of Love shelter.”

……

<br>

<br>

**Paradise of Love**

<br>

<br>

“Do you know it?”

I know it.

A fairly well-known cult within this setting’s Korea.

I had even seen its name once before at the Disaster Management Bureau.

<br>

<br>

#f#*– Human life is filled with fear. The lover of paradise, who will protect and love you so that you no longer have to be afraid, is waiting for you. Come to the Paradise of Love.*#f#

<br>

<br>

Back when the White-Tiger Unit, at my suggestion, dealt with the ghost story of ‘Red Clothes at Exit 4’, the agents had pretended to be missionaries of this very cult while writing their recruitment lines.

But to me, there is something beyond even that.

This group was registered on the \<Dark Exploration Records\> wiki as having its own page.

<br>

**Paradise of Love**

<br>

**One of the**

#r**emerging religions**r#

**that spread in Korea in the early 2000s within the setting.**

<br>

**Promoting slogans like ‘#rYou can meet your ideal partnerr#’ or ‘#rWe will make you into the ideal type you imaginer#’, they proselytized in ways appealing to the younger generation, becoming popular on the early internet.**

**But in truth, it was an unethical, ritualistic cult that #rforcibly reshaped the lives of its followersr# to become suitable for a specific being called the ‘#rLover of Paradiser#’, through bizarre ceremonies.**

<br>

<br>

And.

<br>

**This process gained interest for the hypothesis that it was influenced by the**

#r**Mimicry Sect of the Church of the Luminous Unknown**r#

**, and eventually it was established as one of the church’s affiliated organizations.**

<br>

<br>

That facility belonged to the Church of the Luminous Unknown.

<br>

Jang Heowoon grew up in a facility of the Church of the Luminous Unknown.

And he joined Daydream Inc. in order to save the fellow sect members he grew up with… the orphans of the facility whom he called ‘family’, who had become subjects of those same rituals.

‘……’

By this point, it became clear how Lee Heowoon of the Cheerful Research Institute was connected.

‘Heowoon was the target of the ritual.’

<br>

**Under the circumstance that the ‘Lover of Paradise’ is a real, existing person, an attempt may be made to**

#r**usurp the narrative**r#

**of that individual through the ritual process of imitating that person.**

<br>

**Thus, it is an act of following the doctrine of the Church of the Luminous Unknown, which seeks to become a meaningful existence in this world.**

<br>

<br>

‘Is it that they raised someone in order to imitate Lee Heowoon of the Cheerful Research Institute, to steal that researcher’s role?’

Stories not written in the \<Dark Exploration Records\>, details I did not know, reached me through the words of those around me.

The sensation of cold sweat running down my back felt like a hallucination…

<br>

[Ah, to imitate every twist of someone’s life, to fashion a role that would produce a story resembling theirs…]

[As if inheriting the spirit of industrial capitalism, they treat stories like machine parts, carving and processing them.]

[As though running them through the same process could yield identical products!]

<br>

And one of those products now stood before us.

A replica fashioned after ‘Lee Heowoon’.

Kim Heowoon lowered his head.

“Pardon, um. Actually, it was a lie when I said I only came here to introduce myself, sir.”

……

“I came to make an earnest plea of you, Mr. Employee.”

He spoke with extreme politeness.

An absurd request.

“Just as you took in Jang Heowoon, could you take all of us in as well?”

…!!

“We can tell. Because we are the same ‘Heowoon’. …Isn’t Jang Heowoon happy now under your care, Mr. Employee?”

My head began to ache.

<br>

*Reason for the request*

<br>

“…There is nowhere else we can return to.”

His voice lowered.

“The facility we were in burned down, and as for the other Paradise of Love shelters… we cannot go there. ……We cannot live as we did before.”

There was no need to ask why.

‘The fire.’

That fire had also been part of the ritual imitating the narrative of the Cheerful Research Institute researcher….

And from that fire, surviving with the story of ‘having lived through a great fire at one’s facility’, Jang Heowoon was the finished product.

But this one… was among those culled and used as material in the process.

“Please, I implore you, grant us the same name that Jang Heowoon received.”

……

“Then, we too can exist as the same person. Because that is how we were raised!”

His voice grew desperate.

“…Even the fact that Jang Heowoon entered first feels meaningful, doesn’t it? As if, being the eldest, the firstborn, he received the surname ‘Jang’. Then we could continue…”

I raised my hand.

Kim Heowoon hastily bowed his head, adopting a posture as if expecting my hand to reach him… but.

From my hand, smoke rose.

<br>

*Rejected*

“…Pardon?”

*► Live your own life*

“I’ve already received the Mimicry Sect’s authority, that’s why, well, what I did to enter this place… If it continues like this, I won’t be able to escape—”

Because in dying, he was no longer human.

The dead face of a man in his twenties, bearing a somewhat dark and gentle impression, one quite resembling Jang Heowoon-ssi, was frozen, stained with expectation and despair….

“Now you’ll be happy forever. Congratulations.”

And then I realized.

Wish Tickets are personally omnipotent.

That is why, in the process of Jang Heowoon-ssi’s wish being fulfilled, instead of Go Yeongeun-ssi or Jang Heowoon-ssi themselves being transferred to another world, the fact that as many as seven people had simply come back alive made it clear.

This wish had been judged as one incapable of greatly affecting the world in the first place.

Because.

This kid was even wearing a ragged, old-fashioned school uniform.

But with the emergency broadcast of the company beginning to blare, her true identity began to dawn on me…

*[Evacuation alert. High-risk intruder containment failed. Evacuation alert. High-risk intruder containment failed. Evacuation alert. High-risk intruder containment failed…]*

Which meant only one thing.

He’d had help from someone.

A follower of Ireum-nim.
