# Chapter 321

I gasped for breath and looked down.

My body, hidden beneath an old hospital bed that was pitch-black and reeked of disinfectant.

My hand gripping the camcorder.

Another hand shooting out and grabbing it from above.

“...!!”

I reflexively twisted my body, trying to shake the hand off. But the hand gripping the camcorder roughly yanked it, trying to drag it out from under the bed.

**No!**

I writhed, trying to pull the camcorder back. But strangely, as if it were predetermined, my body was dragged out from beneath the bed.

Outside the bed, what awaited the camcorder’s owner was already prepared. The first cameraman would be discovered atop a hospital bed, the lower half of the body grotesquely dissected. The second recording would begin with the horrific scream of the one who discovered it.

**Thud!**

Something long pierced the strange hand that had been pulling at my camcorder.

“...!”

A ballpoint pen.

When I lifted my head, I saw Baek Saheon, his face deathly pale, twisting the pen as he stabbed down into the back of the hand.

“Ah.”

Blood splattered from the patient’s hand clutching the camcorder.

The moment the back of that hand twitched with involuntary muscle tension—

Baek Saheon yanked the camcorder hard.

“......!”

I kicked the bed leg with all my strength.

The hospital bed above our heads lurched violently forward.

Screeech!

The wheeled frame squealed as it rolled, then rattled forward, slamming the “patient” into the wall.

**Bang!**

The hand gripping the camcorder fell away.

“Go!”

I grabbed Baek Saheon by the nape of the neck and bolted backward like a madman.

At some point, the camcorder had naturally ended up in Baek Saheon’s hand, and on its screen flickered the image of a grotesque figure in a patient gown, twitching and trembling against a desk as we passed by.

And the corridor we burst into—

“Hng.”

A dark corridor of a sealed ward.

I steadied the camcorder in Baek Saheon’s hands again and looked down the hallway through the screen.

What appeared wasn’t a hospital, but a dark corporate hallway.

“Eleva—”

“No!” I shouted urgently. “Take the stairs. This is a disaster where you must not use the elevator!”

Every expedition ended with a corpse found in the elevator, always censored out so you never even got to see it—one of those infamous horror descriptions!

“—!!”

Baek Saheon seemed to swallow a curse, then finally sprinted toward the emergency exit.

As we ran down the corridor, I heard the sound of someone pushing a bed and charging after us, doors of patient rooms flying open, and the sounds of figures that felt eerily familiar appearing again and again.

“Look through the cam—”

But there was no need to say it. Baek Saheon kept running, regardless of whether someone tried to speak to him or suddenly lunged out to grab him.

Not a shred of hesitation. Not the slightest intention to help.

“Huff.”

I opened the emergency door first, threw myself through with Baek Saheon, and slammed it shut immediately.

**Bang!**

I locked the door.

Thump, thump  —pounding on the door and voices calling out followed, and disturbingly, the rusted metal door bore a pentagram-like shape that looked as though it had been drawn with blood-smeared hands, sending chills down my spine.

Still, even as I shivered, I let out a sigh filled with certainty.

A sigh of relief.

“...We should be fine for a while.”

Trouble on the emergency stairwell only occurred in the latter half of the “Last Day Footage of the Nursing Hospital.”

**Before that, the Security Team will subdue them.**

Or maybe the “real Security Chief,” who never returned, had been mobilized for the job and was already working from the annex’s first floor upward.

Or starting from the tenth floor, where there would be more people.

“Huff.”

Only then did I let go of Baek Saheon.

Still pale and gasping, he glanced at the camcorder in his hand.

He had already realized it. “This thing...”

“I’d like you to return it now.”

I held out my hand.

Greed flickered in Baek Saheon’s eyes.

“What does it do?”

Damn it.

“...With what’s shown on the screen, you can distinguish what’s real and what’s fake inside this building.”

“Hm.”

“It’s something I need in order to investigate this disaster.”

I spoke seriously, like an agent should.

“But if you’re not planning to investigate this disaster, it’s an item you don’t need. I’ll guide you to a safe place, so please return the camcorder to me. I have work to do.”

“......”

Baek Saheon lowered the camcorder.

But he didn’t let go of it. In a quiet voice, he asked, “Where’s the safe place?”

“Upstairs.”

More precisely, there was a safe zone above the fifth floor.

“This disaster is based on an incident that occurred in a five-story hospital building, so above the fifth floor, the phenomena of the first floor repeat again.”

I looked at him.

“And that’s where distortion occurs.”

“...!”

“That distortion point is not recognized within this disaster.”

In other words, it truly was a safe zone.

“I’ll guide you.”

I started moving upward.

Fortunately, Baek Saheon followed. Good.

“How can you be so sure?”

“...From experience.”

Not experience as an agent, but experience from thoroughly reading Darkness Exploration Records  .

“That’s also why I’m telling you this.”

I glanced at the camcorder.

“While hiding, that camcorder could suddenly turn on or emit light, putting your life in danger.”

“......”

It was both the truth and an attempt at persuasion.

**Baek Saheon’s the type who won’t listen even if you beg with justification and sincerity.**

But trying to take it by force worried me, too—he might troll me—and more than anything...

**Honestly, I don’t want to be alone in a hospital horror scenario...**

I’m scared...

Let’s stick together as far as we can. In a real-life horror game situation like this, even a troll companion is better than no companion at all...

【 Oh, friend... 】

**Khhk...**

“You saw it earlier, too. Because of the camcorder, we were almost exposed and got into serious trouble.”

“......”

“It’s a double-edged sword.”

Baek Saheon fell silent.

“We don’t know why this disaster happened, but for now, go as high as possible and hide. The Security Team will come.”

“......”

If worst comes to worst, even if I have to knock him out and take it, let’s at least stay together for now.

The emergency stairwell was deathly quiet and smelled of stagnant water. As I moved again, I deliberately changed the subject, asking in a placating tone, “By the way, why were you in the annex?”

I thought he said he’d be waiting at the company.

Baek Saheon flinched, then answered curtly, “...Obviously, because there was Darkness to look for. One managed by our team.”

“Then you came from the first floor?”

“Of course. Otherwise, how do you think I saved you on the second floor?”

I had wondered how he’d been on the second floor, since Field Investigation Teams usually didn’t get clearance above the annex’s first floor.

**So this hospital urban-legend phenomenon must have started on the first floor.**

According to Baek Saheon, when the power went out and the twisted image of a sealed hospital suddenly overlaid reality, the emergency exit to the ground floor opened, and he ran up.

**Then the first floor must be absolute chaos right now...**

**Phew.**

“I see. Thank you for saving me. It was a huge help.”

“......”

Still holding the camcorder, Baek Saheon glanced at me and muttered, “You knew everything and still won’t hand over the item.”

Ha.

“Sir.”

I spoke in a slightly tired voice.

“Do you... want me to die?”

“......!”

“Without that camcorder, carrying out my mission will be extremely difficult, and there’s a high chance I’ll meet a bad end halfway through.”

“Then...!”

Baek Saheon’s face flushed red and pale in turns.

“Then you should hide, too! Why are you trying to move around in a situation like this, looking for something...!”

He shook the camcorder.

“What are you going to do with this camcorder? Where are you planning to investigate?”

“...There’s an item I must find on the ground floors of this company’s annex.”

I took a deep breath.

“I need it. There are civilians who can be rescued only if I have that item.”

“......”

Baek Saheon lowered his head so I couldn’t see his expression.

“So you’re breaking in and stealing just to save strangers? That’s ridiculous.”

“If it meant saving someone, wouldn’t you want to steal, too?”

“......”

“I won’t cause harm to the company.”

“How can you not cause harm if you’re stealing something?”

Then Baek Saheon tossed out casually, “What you’re trying to steal—it’s like a potion-making machine, right?”

...!

“Right? They shut down the annex and caused a huge fuss, saying it was missing. ...Yeah, figures.”

His voice sounded oddly excited.

“Did you steal it?”

“No.”

I shook my head.

Then, stopping my steps, I smiled faintly.

“What I’m looking for isn’t on that floor.”

I lifted my head and checked the floor we had arrived at.

[5F]

“It’s on this floor.”

Baek Saheon’s lips parted.

“...The potion machines should be on the tenth floor.”

“Normally, yes. But I have something to find here.”

Coming here had been the plan from the start.

What mattered was having an excuse to roam the annex’s ground floors without drawing attention.

**In that sense, things have actually tilted in my favor.**

With a hospital urban legend swallowing the building, whatever I do won’t draw much notice now.

**Act before this urban legend is suppressed.**

That was right.

Yeah, I know all that, but...

The problem is—

**Ha.**

I wanted to squeeze my eyes shut as I stared at the fifth-floor emergency door.

So rusted it looked black.

And smeared across it, as if dripping downward, were dark red letters.

HAPPY

        D-DAY

╚════════════════════╝

This was the very heart of the hospital urban legend...

In other words, the main event area—the place where you’d see every horrific thing imaginable.

**This is insane.**

At the base of the fifth-floor emergency door lay a shattered pile of syringes, and on the door, “HAPPY D-DAY” was written in sticky blood.

Something clotted from the needles was dripping down. On closer look, it was bundles of nerves and blood vessels...

I really, really wanted to run away.

But forcing myself to act brave, I grabbed the emergency door handle.

It was sticky, **aaaagh...**

“What I’m trying to take here is something which its disappearance truly won’t harm this company.”

I finished explaining to Baek Saheon.

“So please give me the camcorder and go to the tenth floor. I’ll designate a hiding place for you.”

“......”

“I’ll get this done quickly before the Security Team handles this disaster.”

**Phew.**

**We’re running out of time.**

Anxious, I continued. Hey, this is the final offer.

“If you’re that worried, I can guide you up to the tenth—”

“Ah, seriously!!”

“...?!”

“Ha, damn it, phew......”

Baek Saheon scowled deeply, then said to me, “Let’s do the opposite.”

“What?”

“I’ll take you to the fifth floor. That works.”

He stood beside me with the camcorder, wearing a look like he was doing this against his will.

“How am I supposed to trust you and hand over the camcorder completely? I’ll be alone—something could happen that you don’t even know about!”

I was the one who told him about the camcorder in the first place.

“If I die, it’s not like you can take responsibility.”

“......”

But I got the gist of it.

**You can’t just trade a useful item for information alone.**

**Phew.**

Still, it was unexpected.

**He’s coming with me?**

And honestly, having one more person was an enormous advantage in a hospital urban legend scenario.

**Well... if it comes to it, I can always take it by force.**

Strangely enough, this agent’s persuasion was actually working on Baek Saheon, within the bounds of common sense.

“But whatever we get, I get a say, too. Half of it. Got it?”

“Understood.”

Yeah. Ho Yuwon will pay.

And I added deliberately, with a smile, “Thank you, sir. ...It’s a little less scary thanks to you.”

Doesn’t seem to have worked. Damn it.
