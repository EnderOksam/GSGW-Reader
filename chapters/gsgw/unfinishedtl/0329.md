# Chapter 328

I trembled my eyelids and looked at the scene before me.

“Refit the train!”

More than several dozen people were crowding into Car No. 1, holding placards and shouting.

But listen.

“Rabbit dolls for everyone!”

Everything was pink.

Each placard bore bizarre slogans like “The Perfect Rabbit Doll,” “The Fluffy One’s Grace,” “Blessed Are the Cushy Front Paws,” and “Lead Us to a Wider Place.”

People had their arms around one another’s shoulders, and here and there I saw people wearing rabbit-ear headbands made by cutting up cardboard boxes.

It was shocking—but given the way the past few days had been going, I’d expected something shocking to happen in one direction or another, so I managed to endure it.

But then…

“Guarantee the rabbits~”

Deputy.

What on earth are you doing…?

I fell into confusion when I spotted Deputy Eun Haje at the very front, subtly wedging herself in, a necktie wrapped around her head as she waved her hands.

Before launching Operation Rabbit Doll Cult, I had already given Ms. Go Yeongeun, Agent Bronze, and Deputy Eun Haje a rough explanation of what would happen.

– Rabbit doll enthusiasts will suddenly multiply.

– Excuse me?

So I definitely told them there would be a disturbance on the train, but…

‘Why would you become the ringleader of that disturbance…?’

What’s more, Deputy Eun Haje had slyly slipped in among the people, picking up bits and pieces by eavesdropping and grasping the situation.

“Ah~ it seemed like you were doing something good, so I thought I’d lend a hand, hahaha! Ahh, so this… person is the one who leads you all to a better place?”

While smoothly extracting generous bits of information from these newly initiated “Rabbit Doll Cult” members, Deputy Eun Haje eventually got to see their “rabbit doll goods” up close.

“Oh….”

A long note of admiration escaped her as she looked at the embossed rabbit doll head engraved on a jingling metal keychain…

“Our goal is to bestow this amazing miracle upon everyone on the train, so that no one is discriminated against and everyone receives the Fluffy One’s grace.”

“Oh….”

“Isn’t that right, Distributor!”

Don’t look at me.

But people had already turned sharply toward me, their eyes sparkling.

That’s right.

In fact, I was standing right in the middle of this crowd with placards….

That title, “Distributor,” was something people had deliberately attached to me, calling me “the one who began distributing rabbit dolls.”

‘I took it because it felt like the perfect hat for becoming a sycophant.’

Trying to look as if I wanted to placate the loudest voices, I deliberately glanced around and smiled, and in the end my eyes met Deputy Eun Haje’s.

“…….”

“…….”

This is so humiliating.

Yet my mouth was smoothly spouting the most shamelessly sycophantic words.

“Of course…! I’m just a fool who sells snacks, after all. I trust completely in the words of you who so often meet the Fluffy One…!”

“Distributor!”

Phew.

I quickly averted my gaze when I saw Deputy Eun Haje, who had been alternating her attention between the rabbit doll merchandise people were holding and me, narrow her eyes as if she’d noticed something.

Please go away, Deputy….

But the “good friend” inside the massive velvet box I was holding seemed quite excited by this spectacle.

[Friend, for an entertainer who knows how to respond to the audience’s support, this is a rather delightful situation. I almost feel like giving them some merchandise by lottery!]

[Right, how about selling special products at your beautiful, modest resort?]

…Talk-show merchandise?

[Indeed. Oh, you could consider this a formal proposal. A collaboration with “Braun’s  Late-Night Talk Show.”]

In my mind, I pictured a classy, retro-style section in a theme park gift shop, with phrases like “Featured on a Popular TV Program.”

Adding a ghost story—an expert at captivating viewers—into an already bewitching urban legend…

Ah, no.

‘…These people can’t actually go to a real fun theme park anyway. Even if we collaborate, they won’t be able to buy that “rabbit doll.”’

I chose my words carefully.

[Hmm.]

‘For that to happen, you’d first have to resolve this Segwang Special City urban legend and become free!’

Even if it isn’t the right path, let’s cooperate….

[Very well! Then let us continue the development, Mr. Roe Deer.]

Phew.

But the relief of having cleared one hurdle was brief.

I met Deputy Eun Haje’s strangely probing gaze again.

“Hm. Distributor… are you the representative of this group?”

“No way!”

I almost answered honestly.

I quickly hunched my shoulders and bowed my head deeply.

“I’m just one of many people with roles. The real representative is… ah, here they come now!”

He’s here!

“Representative!”

…The newborn cult leader, under a fake title, parted the crowd from the back with a dignified air.

People naturally stepped aside. Some devotees even politely removed the rabbit-ear headbands they were wearing.

I felt like laughing and crying at the same time.

“Did you all dream of rabbits?”

“We dreamed of fluff!”

Where did that greeting even come from? I didn’t teach that….

[Oh, this really is a textbook cult!]

The “Rabbit Doll Association Representative” looked the same as before.

Except now there was a single gold badge with a pink rabbit on it pinned to his padded jacket. It was a badge I’d made by cutting up the tape merchandise I brought.

He gazed over the people with sorrowful eyes, then turned to me and raised a hand.

“Now, bring Him forth.”

“Yes…!”

Anxious as I was, work still had to be done.

After putting on a flattering smile, I quickly lifted the velvet case with both hands and opened it respectfully.

Dozens of people holding placards gazed into the velvet case with eyes full of longing, admiration, and happiness…

At the fluffy Braun.

[Greetings, everyone!]

But their view was blocked by the representative.

With a solemn expression, he brought his face right up to the doll, nodded, and—

[Good heavens, sticking your head within one foot in a private situation—what an unpleasant distance. Are you sane?]

“You are radiant and perfect in form today as well.”

[Not sane, but your eyesight seems intact!]

“I’d like to carry you myself, but….”

[Oh? You dream of a damages lawsuit! Understood….]

Cold sweat poured down my back.

I forced a smile and subtly pulled B
raun back, as if daring to make such a demand of the representative would be unthinkable—polite, as always.

“I’ll risk my life and transport Him with utmost care. From here on, Representative, you will lead us….”

“…Indeed.”

With a swish, the representative neatly adjusted his padded jacket and stepped forward.

In the process, though, the jacket slapped Braun across the face. I stifled a scream.

[Mr. Roe Deer. Aren’t you curious how Braun deals with someone who barges onto the stage and blocks the camera during a live broadcast?]

‘…Incineration?’

[Correct! Oh, to celebrate, let’s run a reenactment now—]

‘I-it’s fine! I already enjoyed it in my head!’

[Excellent imagination, my friend.]

I almost choked on my saliva.

Once more, I carefully lifted Braun’s case with both hands.

“Let’s march to the very front of Car No. 1!”

“Yes!”

Feeling my eye twitch, I slowly followed the placard-holding crowd as they once again advanced with grim expressions toward the engine room.

Those who hadn’t joined the “Fluffy Body Sect” yet stared blankly, as if witnessing some bizarre short-form video shoot.

‘Ha….’

How did things come to this…?

‘I was aiming for a mysterious small secret religion.’

A dream of comforting the weary—this cult spreading quietly through the train until it became the majority, causing unrest that dispersed the power of the engine room….

…But what we had now was a “Good job! We’re cheering you on!”-type protest.

‘Why?’

[How about retracing the path we took, my friend? Perhaps you chose a different direction at a crossroads!]

That’s true.

‘So….’

I looked back over the past few days.

First of all… the point where things began to spiral unexpectedly was…

‘Ah.’

It was when I started inserting messages into the amusement-park dreams.

That “Be kind to everyone” choreography.

‘I made it because of the cult-leader candidate’s personality.’

But the people who joined the mascot’s vague, cheerful dance while holding a rabbit doll began adding their own interpretations.

– The target is everyone! Doesn’t “kindness” here mean handing over the rabbit doll’s symbol?

– Yes, it seems He wants to share His softness and warmth with more people!

– Perhaps He appeared because He saw how exhausted we are, trapped on this train. He comforts those who are confined and struggling!

– Then we shouldn’t fight among ourselves, but live harmoniously and not look down on others.

– That’s right!

– That way, just as the Fluffy One wishes, won’t everyone on this train get to meet Him?

Thus, doctrines I’d never considered or created formed on their own.

‘That last leap was a stretch.’

Still, as they say, all roads lead to Seoul.

In any case, unrest did occur….

“Rabbit dolls for everyone!”

The protesters marched.

I was following at the very back, carrying the velvet case with my good friend inside for the sake of the Fluffy One’s safety, when Deputy Eun Haje cautiously slipped in behind me.

“Roe Deer.”

“…….”

“You… haven’t you already had more than enough of the pseudo-religion flavor?”

“…….”

“Or was it not enough?”

If there were a mouse hole, I’d crawl into it.

But after reading slogans like “The Warmth of the Rabbit Doll Will Save Us,” the deputy’s eyes grew strange.

“Well, still… this feels less like a cult and more like… just a bunch of over-the-line fanatics.”

Yes…….

So much so that even people from the front cars, instead of feeling threatened or fleeing in fear, were merely watching in confusion or a daze.

It seemed to be triggering quite the dopamine rush.

Or maybe they were thinking, what kind of crazy dream is this?

“Guarantee it!”

The march of dozens was hard to miss, and when they finally reached the very front of the engine room, they shouted their chant again.

Of course, I was included.

“Hey, you should shout, too.”

“…Guarantee it!”

…I’ll just be grateful I can hide my face behind the case with Braun inside.

I quietly blended into the back of the crowd and avoided eye contact.

And then, a little while later.

“W-what is all this nonsense!”

The conductor came rushing out of the engine room.

Wiping cold sweat and brushing snack crumbs off his waistband, he jammed his conductor’s cap down as if it were a symbol of authority.

But he looked flustered at the sight of dozens of people.

Rolling his eyes frantically, he scanned the faces and seemed to find a point of leverage, his expression finally brightening.

“Well, I mean, if the people in the front cars have complaints, of course we should, uh, listen. As public servants, you know. But….”

He shrugged as he noticed faces from the back cars mixed in.

“There are also some parasite-like people mixed in who only want things.”

Divide and conquer.

“Let’s send those people back and have a calm discussion among ourselves. I know the people from our car are all reasonable.”

A middle-aged man in a padded jacket who had once tried to shove me was standing in this group. The conductor spoke pointedly while looking at him.

But….

“So what if we’re from the back cars!”

“Yeah!”

“We’re all people having the same experience!”

It didn’t work on the crowd at all.

Their sense of group identity had already changed.

“On a hard day like today, everyone deserves comfort!”

It would’ve been moving under different circumstances.

“And the Fluffy One is the best friend for comforting people!”

That description feels oddly close to the truth.

“Someone like you—who hoards good things instead of sharing them, excludes others, and struts around—wouldn’t understand!”

“That’s right!”

The representative pointed at the conductor.

“But!”

Then he pointed at himself.

“Even someone like you can change if you’re with a rabbit doll. Just like me.”

Deputy Eun Haje muttered, “This is kind of having a socially positive impact….”

“…….”

“Isn’t this religion’s good side? Why is this happening here?”

Exactly.

[Interesting, my friend. Didn’t that ordinary and unpleasant fellow puff up with pride when he was specially chosen by you as the representative and eagerly took the position?]

‘He did.’

[But now he opposes anyone else receiving special treatment. Oh, that illogic is almost enough to evoke the theater of the absurd!]

Well… when I do it, it’s romance; when others do it, it’s an affair.

‘And once the numbers grow, the leader becomes even more special.’

Still, compared to that stagnant, rotten conductor, the new flow led by the representative—at least in this context—seemed better.

“Let us experience His softness and His warmth together.”

And yes, that is a phrase I spread….

It sounds oddly like a mattress ad? Just your imagination.

Anyway, the conductor finally started to lose his mind.

“Are you all completely insane!”

“That’s right.”

Now was the time to press the momentum.

Putting on a thick skin, I stepped forward and replied with a serious “I’m scared but I’ll say it anyway” expression.

“We are all people who are crazy about rabbit dolls…!”

“Hear, hear!”

“The representative will lead us to the perfect rabbit doll!”

“…….”

The conductor was speechless.

You can’t reason with crazy people.

Meanwhile, the representative spoke on, his face growing ever more confident and courageous.

People have a strange tendency.

‘When people keep agreeing with you, you start believing your own claims more strongly.’

Even if the belief doesn’t match your original disposition.

“So please, grant permission to supply rabbit dolls to everyone on the train!”

“Please!”

“Refit the train so everyone can enjoy the same space equally!”

“Please!”

At the chants, the conductor’s face twisted into, “What the hell do these lunatics even want?”

And finally he shouted, “I’ve been entrusted with the grave responsibility of operating this train! Is it right to pester someone like me over such trivial matters! Huh? You should all ask the railway staff first—”

“It’s already been approved.”

“…….”

The conductor fled back into the engine room.

“He’s running!”

“Wait!”

People tried to grab him, but he’d already slipped into the engine room and locked the door.

“Let’s break the lock!”

“Should we smash it?”

Wait!

I stopped the people trying to break down the door.

“If the shelter gets damaged, that’d be bad, right? Maybe we should wait until he comes out…? He probably won’t hold out long….”

“Hm…. True.”

“Let’s wait until the conductor comes out.”

The situation, which had been on the verge of escalation, settled again.

As bedtime approached, the people of the “Rabbit Doll Cult” growing more relaxed and happy also helped.

Statistically, they’d be enjoying amusement-park ads in their dreams.

“May you all dream of rabbits!”

“May the Fluffy Hand be with you.”

The “Rabbit Doll Cult” settled down in Car No. 1, handing out various snacks to that car and curious onlookers, stoking the mood.

When something looks pleasant, it’s easier to lure people in.

Residents of other shelters were also getting hit full-on by the aggro advertising effect and being drawn in.

“Would you like one?”

“Uh… oh, popcorn.”

To the point that people from other cars were constantly coming and going from Car No. 1, and instead of picking fights or getting angry, everyone was just having a dopamine feast with snacks.

Those who hadn’t come to Car No. 7 before were especially dazzled by the new snacks.

‘Numbers and atmosphere are scary.’

The Rabbit Doll Cult members then took turns sleeping.

“You must be tired….”

“It’s fine. I’ll guard the Fluffy One tonight…!”

Smiling, I declined people’s offers and kept watch over Car No. 1 like a sentry.

‘This is nothing like I expected, but it’s going smoothly….’

Anyway, we’re heading to Seoul.

Now all I need is to use this unrest as cover to approach the engine room.

I kept chanting slogans appropriately and handing out snacks with the practiced skill of a flyer part-timer, plotting my chance.

“Guarantee rabbit dolls! Here, take some snacks….”

“Yes.”

“…….”

“…….”

Wait.

“Manager?”

“Yes.”

I turned my head.

Section Chief Lee Jaheon, wearing a mask, stood there blankly looking at me, holding the snack I’d offered.

“…….”

“…….”

Why are you here?

“Mr. Roe Deer.”

“It’s Grapes.”

“Mr. Grapes.”

I could see the blank eyes of a lizard.

The lizard’s crimson eyes glanced at the various Braun goods people were holding, then returned to me.

“Do you believe in rabbit dolls?”

“Well, k-kind of…?”

“…….”

“…….”

I hurriedly looked around, lowered my voice, and whispered.

“We’re not a cult.”

“I see.”

The lizard nodded.

He must have caught on that it was all an operation….

“All cults say that.”

“…….”

I want to pass out.
