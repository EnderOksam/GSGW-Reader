# Chapter 320

Daydream Corporation’s Interrogation Room.

In the unprecedented incident where the potion-manufacturing machine disappeared, my bombshell statement at the scene left the interrogators gaping.

“You’re saying you s-saw a bomb in the elevator?”

“Yes.”

I nodded expressionlessly, then stated flatly,

“I told you, it was there.”

And chaos erupted.

However, the staff who dragged me along and checked the elevator found nothing—its interior was perfectly clean.

“Huh, it’s gone. It was definitely there.”

“......”

“Anyway, I saw it.”

A truth detector was brought in.

At Daydream, “haunted items that reveal the truth” are a recurring staple. This time, a Twilight-grade hand-knitted doll appeared.

The big-eyed knitted doll asked through ventriloquism, 『Was there really a bomb?』

“There really was.”

......

『It’s true.』

The interrogation room fell into turmoil!

And from that moment on, I began spouting every kind of nonsense imaginable.

“Ah, I also saw a cult-background employee in the elevator.”

“I saw researchers sprinting through an alley.”

“I saw the dragon and fox from the new-hire training video getting beaten up by a rabbit.”

“I saw the Security Team beating up a director.”

『It’s true.』

Every single one was judged to be true!

“Confession potion! We need a confession potion!”

“Why waste a confession potion on someone who’s clearly insane? None of that makes any sense!”

Yeah.

“It’s nonsense!”

It’s real.

But the realistic, seasoned interrogators arrived at a very reasonable hypothesis.

“Even if it’s not objectively true, if the person believes it’s true, it registers as true.”

“Ah...!”

The verdict: I’m insane.

In the end, after whispering among themselves in utter exasperation, the interrogators declared, “Let’s check whether that researcher is contaminated.”

“......!”

“In my opinion...”

More whispers.

“It seems someone released Darkness in here.”

“Then does that mean the machine disappearance, too...?”

“Exactly.”

Someone swallowed hard.

“It looks like the work of a Darkness that escaped containment. People may be getting contaminated—this researcher could be the first symptomatic case.”

【 ...That’s what they’re saying, my friend! 】

Mm.

Inside the comfortable interrogation room, I listened—via Braun—to the company employees outside passionately constructing theories about my interrogation.

Now then, what comes next is...

“We need to test that researcher for contamination. And also formally request another full inspection of the annex for any containment failures.”

And so, thirty minutes later—

“Please take him away!”

“Yes......”

I was transferred into the custody of the Security Team.

To verify my “contamination status,” I was to be moved to an isolation room.

Thus, after drawing massive aggro in the interrogation room, I exited quite naturally.

The new researchers, still isolated on the 10th floor, stared at me like they were looking at a lunatic.

Goodbye, Park Kyunghwan’s brilliant career.

【 Seeing everyone panic like that was hilarious—oh, it was a full-on farce! 】

*Whoever stole it should be thanking me.*

Thanks to me, they probably bought themselves at least an hour or two. On top of that, there’s now an extra hypothesis to verify, which will only complicate the pursuit.

Of course, something was paid in exchange.

*This researcher’s bright corporate life...*

To waste this much administrative power over the unprecedented loss of the Potion Machine—contamination or not, I’ve definitely made a bad impression.

With my peers, too.

*You... are really not cut out to work at this company...*

Let’s just resign.

I’ll think of it as having done something beneficial for the countless Field Investigation Team members and civilians who died or got screwed because of you. Yeah.

I shrugged inwardly and followed where the thin, hunched Security Team member led me.

With annex access completely blocked due to the theft incident, I rode the elevator with the Security Guard to the temporary isolation area on the annex’s second floor.

“......”

“......”

*Thud.*

The thin Security Guard closed the isolation room door behind me, and the moment we were alone, he pressed a device on the wall to stop the recording.

“...Um.”

I saw the other person lower their mask in a CCTV blind spot.

The Sergeant, J3, blinked at me.

“Roe Deer-ssi... right...?”

Ah.

“...What makes you think that?”

“Well... the smell’s the same...?”

Body scent.

I’d deodorized, even sprayed the researcher’s usual perfume—but it still got caught.

*Must be because of the Sergeant’s contamination trait.*

I finally sighed inwardly and gave a small nod.

“Yes. That’s right.”

I was planning to talk to the Sergeant anyway, so this worked out.

But his blank stare didn’t change.

“You’re... talking.”

...Ah.

“Yes.”

I smiled faintly.

“I’ve recovered.”

“...!”

“It’s not a disguise—I really can do it now.”

I waved my hand.

His eyes followed the movement of my bare hand.

No gloves, no black smoke, no dripping flesh.

A clean... human hand.

“I can eat, too. When we were on the same team, I couldn’t do that. Next time, let’s try eating together.”

“...Okay.”

The Sergeant slowly nodded.

And just as slowly, a faint smile spread across his face as well.

“That’s good...”

“...Thank you.”

【 Still, this washed-up one knows his place. Oh, there are countless people in the entertainment market who, having hit rock bottom, vent pointless jealousy over their miserable circumstances... 】

...Probably because he has no expectation of recovery himself.

Isn’t that why?

“Um... should I bring you a snack...?”

“Ah, I’m fine for now. I’ll buy one later.”

“Mm, thanks...”

Watching the Sergeant—who seemed oddly less bothered by everything than before—I suddenly had a thought.

*If you drink a Wish Ticket... couldn’t you wish for full recovery from contamination?*

Has no one ever tried?

*...Ah, only full-time employees can use them.*

And full-time Field Investigators would use any Wish Ticket they had on the “desperate wish” that made them join in the first place, not on curing their contamination.

Meaning recovery via Wish Ticket almost never happens.

But still—

*...What if someone else makes the wish for them?*

What would happen?

Like how Ms. Go Yeongeun once made a wish on Jang Heoun’s behalf.

*...Has absolutely no one ever done that?*

【  An intriguing question, Mr. Roe Deer. Hard to resolve immediately, though! 】

I decided to shelve that thought for now.

There was a more urgent topic at hand.

In a noticeably lighter atmosphere, we continued talking.

The disappearance of Daydream’s Potion Machine!

“Um... the machine—did you steal it...?”

“Absolutely not.”

Though I had planned to.

“Did you steal it, Sergeant?”

“No...”

“......”

“......”

An awkward silence fell.

“Um, about the bomb... in the elevator...”

“Ah.”

I nodded solemnly.

“I meant that *I* am the bomb.”

“......”

I mean, seriously, I do have a similar item in my inventory.

The other statements were similar.

*It’s a prank where you omit the subject or object.*

I’ve actually witnessed or experienced those things—just not *right now*.

*But when everything comes out as true, it drives people insane.*

If it had gone as far as a confession potion, I had other plans—but anyway.

“So neither of us did it.”

“Yes......”

Two would-be thieves who got preempted blinked at each other.

“...Do you think someone else stole it?”

“I don’t know...”

Y-yeah, I suppose...

“Then, did you notice anything strange?”

“Something strange...?”

“Yes. Anyone suspicious, or anything that felt off.”

The Sergeant seemed to think for a moment, then answered slowly.

“Mm... somehow... right now... feels a bit strange...”

...Right now?

“In what way?”

“Uh......”

Looking too lazy to think, the Sergeant slowly turned his gaze toward the isolation room door.

“Feels like... someone died...”

“...Pardon?”

At that moment—

*Flicker.*

“...!”

The lights blinked.

As if all illumination vanished at once, plunging everything into darkness—

Then the lights came back on.

“......”

“......”

*Ding-dong.*

[  Attention, please! Everyone in the annex, please remain calm during this sudden power outage and stay where you are...]

The voice came from the wall speaker.

The orientation facilitator’s voice.

[Until the dream-processing machine is recovered, the annex lockdown will not be lifted-krrrshzzzk—]

[......]

[......]

[Lockdown remains in effect.]

*Click.*

The announcement cut off.

“......”

“Shh.”

*Creeeak.*

The Sergeant, now masked again, reopened the isolation room door and looked back at me.

“Stay here... quietly... I’ll go check...”

“......Yes.”

“If it’s dangerous... lock it.”

*Thud.*

Leaving the door slightly ajar, the Sergeant went out.

Soon, I heard his footsteps walking down the hallway...

“......”

I waited for a moment.

*Stay calm.*

I’m infiltrating under someone else’s identity. Don’t waste that advantage.

Information is important, so I planned to wait until the Sergeant came back with a report.

But the ominous atmosphere made my hands cold.

And a few dozen seconds later—

*Flicker.*

“...!”

My vision vanished.

A blackout.

Every light disappeared completely, leaving nothing but cold darkness.

“......”

Frozen in place, I sat and let my eyes adjust...

This time, the lights did not return.

And then—

“□□□...”

From outside the narrow gap in the door, I thought I heard a voice.

“...Sergeant?”

No answer.

Carefully standing up, taking a deep breath to calm myself, I brought my eyes to the door gap...

My hands trembled at doing something straight out of a horror movie.

And as my eyes finished adjusting, I saw a very faint silhouette.

...A dark hallway.

[2F]

“......”

No one was there.

*Is this... a ghost story?*

Could the Potion Machine really have disappeared because of an urban legend?

If so, I might’ve accidentally helped the deduction.

【 So, what will you do, my friend? 】

I looked out again.

Then I realized—the hallway wasn’t completely empty.

A black, rectangular device lay on the floor.

*...That is?*

Too dark to see clearly.

After a brief hesitation, instead of drawing out contamination, I quietly stepped outside.

If someone found me, they’d just think the crazy researcher was acting up again.

Carefully, I walked down the dark corridor toward the object...

Stop.

Crouching close, I recognized it.

*...A camcorder.*

An old camcorder—nothing about it felt modern.

A handheld camcorder with a side-opening screen.

【 Oh, quite a charming filming device. Though rather shabby. 】

*......*

Camcorder urban legend.

Several possibilities flashed through my mind. After deliberating, I chose not to touch it and stood—

“There...”

“...! Sergeant.”

It was J.

I barely managed not to mess up the honorific as I saw Sergeant J3 walking toward me from the other end of the corridor.

“Are you alright?”

“Yes... nothing happened...”

He walked closer.

I sighed in relief, glanced at him, then down at the camcorder.

“That...?”

“Oh, it was on the flo—”

No shadow.

“......”

Even though the Sergeant was standing right in front of me, there was no shadow beneath him.

In the darkness, I hadn’t noticed it from afar.

“Um...”

“......”

Cold sweat ran down my back.

My mind frantically searched the Darkness Exploration Records for a matching legend. This—this is—

“......!!”

I grabbed the camcorder.

Flipping the screen open in a panic, it powered on, the display lighting up.

I pointed it at the “Sergeant.”

“What... are you doing?”

On the camcorder screen—

Not the Sergeant.

A face stared back at me.

Grinning.

Eyes rolling, filling the screen, grinning grotesquely—messy hair, protruding cheekbones.

An old hospital gown.

...Below the baggy pants, instead of legs, only nerves and blood vessels dangled in midair.

But when I lifted my head, the Sergeant stood there, looking puzzled.

“What’s wrong...?”

“......”

Fuck.

I turned and ran.

The sound of frantic pursuit echoed behind me.

*Damn it!*

Damn it, damn it, damn it!!

This is a hospital ghost story...!

Get a grip!

I almost added another contamination type.

...The danger’s still there, though.

*Tatadadadadadadadadadadadadadadadadadadadadadadadadadadada—*

Damn it, damn it!

I could feel the “thing that looked like the Sergeant” relentlessly chasing me.

The sound of it pursuing me to reenact what it had suffered.

No—not footsteps...

The sound of something whipping against the walls and floor!

*Ha.*

Chills ran from head to toe.

Still, using the camcorder’s screen to see through the darkness, I hastily formed a plan.

Lose it at the next corner—

Something shoved me.

“...!!”

No—pulled me at the same time.

With my mouth covered, I was dragged, rolling into one of the offices. Someone shoved me under a desk.

I almost counterattacked reflexively, then stopped—its behavior felt off for a ghost story.

*Who?*

I looked up to see who it was...

Baek Saheon.

“...!”

I reflexively pointed the camcorder at him—but nothing appeared...

It was the real Baek Saheon.

With an irritated expression, he grabbed my camcorder.

*Off!*

I snapped it shut and ducked.

He glared at me to keep quiet, then crouched down as well.

*...No.*

How is this guy even in the annex?

And—

*Did he just save me?*

Why? I focused on his expression, trying to read it. He glared back as if to say, “ *What are you looking at?”* But—

*Creeeak.*

“......”

“......”

We both froze.

“...Hello?”

The door opened.

“Where is it... I’m sure it came this way......”

......A presence.

The shadowless “Sergeant,” walking.

Not normal footsteps, but thin strands sticking to the floor and peeling away...

*Screeek, screeeek—*

Swearing rose to my throat; sweat poured down.

Baek Saheon and I stayed silent, crouched under the desk, holding our breath in the darkness...

“Is it in this room...”

*...Room?*

And slowly, I realized.

The air had changed.

Amid the paper and diffuser scent of an office was a strange smell.

Cold. Uncomfortable.

......The smell of disinfectant.

I realized then.

We were no longer under a desk—

We were under a hospital bed.

“......”

I lowered my gaze.

The camcorder had turned on by itself, its red light glowing—

“There you are.”

A patient’s hand suddenly grabbed my camcorder.
