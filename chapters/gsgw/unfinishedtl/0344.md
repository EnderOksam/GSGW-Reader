# Chapter 343

*“......”*

*From the beginning.*

*Why did I keep exploring this dangerous Sekwang Special City?*

*It was because of a vague sense of expectation.*

*An expectation that, if I dug into this enormous, secret-ridden urban legend, I might somehow reach the fundamental truth of this world of rumors.*

*And then...*

*An expectation that I might learn why this was happening to me.*

That I might discover the cause or reason behind being suddenly thrown into the world of Darkness Exploration Records *and forced to suffer through incomprehensible hardship...*

*I had just realized that those expectations had been answered.*

*In a direction I had never anticipated.*

*‘So this place was trying to invite me...’*

*The Cheerful Research Institute had tried to summon me—a person from that world—as proof of a “safe world without urban legends”...*

*And because of that, the disaster in Sekwang Special City occurred.*

*My heart went cold.*

*‘Then... the hundreds of thousands who died in Sekwang Special City, the city being sealed off—does that ultimately have something to do with me...?’*

*...No! Don’t get confused.*

*‘Did I ever ask to be summoned?’*

*Absolutely not.*

*Like the other citizens of Sekwang City, I was dragged into this. Caught up in the insane experiments of the institutions of this damned urban-legend world...*

*Dragged in—and it just happened to be me.*

*“......”*

*In an instant, something like searing pain surged from my chest up to my head.*

*It was anger.*

*Barely managing to exhale softly, I looked at the researcher from the Cheerful Research Institute.*

*My words came out strangely dry.*

*In an agent’s voice.*

*“So in the end, you’re saying you tried to summon a person from a world without supernatural phenomena—a completely absurd notion. And... you failed.”*

*“......”*

*“An entire metropolis turned into an urban legend. Why did you attempt something like this without even having the ability to take responsibility? What confidence did you have?”*

*“We didn’t do this. It wasn’t supposed to fail. This attempt—”*

*“‘Wasn’t supposed to fail’? What kind of irresponsible statement is that? If you’re a research institute, it’s only natural to prepare for the possibility of failure.”*

*Researcher Lee Heoun’s face grew even paler.*

*Good.*

*“How are you going to clean this up? Where are the other researchers right now?”*

“They evacuated due to the fire. And there was *a contingency plan. But it’s not working now. We were deceived too. We—”*

*“What kind of irresponsible excuse is that?”*

*“That’s exactly what I think.”*

*...!*

*I snapped my head around.*

*The person I had forgotten was quietly speaking.*

*The counseling teacher, his expression gone.*

*“I think so too. This is unbelievably irresponsible.”*

*“......”*

*“I was very clearly promised something by them.”*

*The air twisted strangely.*

*“That on the day their efforts succeeded, this city would become a place safer for people than anywhere else on the surface.”*

*Lee Heoun gave no answer.*

*No—he couldn’t.*

*I saw him clutch his own throat.*

*‘...!’*

*In that fantastical space filled with colorful toys, he rolled on the floor with choking sounds.*

*Yet the counseling teacher smiled at me and explained calmly, as if kindly going over pre-session instructions.*

*“I’ve lived in Sekwang Special City—formerly called Gwangjin—for a very long time. Once, I used to harm people here and there, but after growing attached and gaining reason, I’ve watched over and protected this place ever since. So hearing that promise made me quite pleased.”*

*His previously composed, rational tone grew softer and gentler.*

*As if he were returning to his true form.*

*“Because even the officials who followed them assured me, I offered them the underground of this land and listened to what they asked. Then one day, I received a particularly earnest request.”*

*“...What kind of request?”*

*“They asked to borrow a mysterious orb for a short while.”*

*At the counseling teacher’s—or rather, the spirit’s—gesture, a bright, perfectly round sphere floated in midair above his finger.*

*It glowed with dazzling, multicolored brilliance tinged with red.*

*I was reminded of a similar orb that Deputy Eun Haje had once used to infiltrate the Myeongchanlan Sect disguised as an old man...*

*But that one had been only fingertip-sized, and nowhere near this sacred-looking.*

*“Have you ever heard the folk tales about a fox orb? Not a fruit, but an orb possessed by a fox spirit.”*

*It sparkled.*

*“Some say it originated from a dragon’s wish-granting jewel, but it’s probably just a term born from similar stories being passed down... Namely, that it grants wishes.”*

*“...!”*

*Soon, the fantastical image vanished.*

*But I realized that it resembled the egg-like object floating at the center of the dream incubator’s tank...*

*‘...A wish.’*

*Don’t tell me.*

*“The thing you lent them—was it used in this experiment?”*

*“That seems likely, doesn’t it?”*

*A laugh followed.*

*“Without knowing that, I hurried to retrieve my orb and take action. Guarding my territory and repelling other impurities was something the Management Bureau had repeatedly asked of me through careful rituals as well. But...”*

*His voice hardened.*

*“They say the orb is gone.”*

*“......”*

*“There’s no way now. No way to protect this city from the profane intrusion outside, from the infection spreading like a plague.”*

*The researcher’s face turned blue. His hands trembled thinly, as if he might lose consciousness at any moment.*

*‘...There’s no point in letting Lee Heoun die like this.’*

*It wasn’t solely his personal responsibility, and we’d lose information.*

*I finally spoke quickly.*

*“Can’t you at least try to trace how the orb disappeared?”*

*“......”*

*“And if that doesn’t work, we should see whether there’s another way to save the city. ...By interrogating the researcher.”*

*......*

*......*

*“That’s true.”*

*“Ghk!”*

*Lee Heoun jerked his hands away from his throat as if shocked by electricity.*

*After gasping on the floor a few times, he spoke with eyes still focused.*

*“I—I’m sorry... I’m sorry...”*

*“Apologies can wait. First, we need to find a way to deal with this. Explain how the experiment proceeded, from the beginning.”*

*“Yes, cough—so, we...”*

*But at that moment—*

Researcher Lee Heoun’s right ear fell to the floor with a soft thud *.*

*“...!”*

*“Ah.”*

*The researcher groped around and picked up the ear.*

*What had fallen was now unmistakably a plastic toy...*

*“......”*

*I turned my head toward the corridor.*

*Beyond Lee Jaheon and the others, the gaudy hallway looked strangely more static than before.*

*Some of the colorful toys had stopped working, standing still or losing their vitality...*

*They looked like things that had lost their mystery.*

*“...Please escape quickly. Th-this office won’t hold much longer either. You need to get out...”*

*Lee Heoun spoke frantically.*

*“We’re currently protected from the fire and the disaster outside, but the flames will reach here soon. There isn’t much time left!”*

*Wait.*

*“Is the supernatural phenomenon that’s preventing the fire from spreading to this floor... disappearing?”*

*“Yes...”*

*I recalled how the counseling teacher’s hand, severed when placed into the monster, had seemed to dissolve and vanish.*

*...Then.*

*Could it be.*

*“Is Sekwang Special City right now... losing supernatural phenomena altogether?”*

*“......”*

*A faint smile appeared on the researcher’s face.*

*“That’s correct.”*

*...!!*

“So strictly speaking, we did *achieve the objective of our experiment. The invitation must have succeeded...!”*

*A faint thrill could be heard in the researcher’s voice.*

*“Someone... has arrived.”*

*My head went cold with emotions I couldn’t tell apart—anger or fear.*

*But my reason caught onto a sense of wrongness.*

*“That’s strange. If supernatural phenomena are disappearing, shouldn’t things be getting safer? Why is the outside in that state?”*

*“That’s why this isn’t our intention. It’s a result that’s impossible under our goals...”*

*“Impossible?”*

*“Yes. Today, all we intended to do was establish and prove the existence of a ‘place without urban legends’...”*

*His tired voice sank lower.*

*“Someone interfered and subtly altered the ritual.”*

*“......”*

— That’s not what we research. Someone tampered with it!

*“And they must have set the fire to prevent us from responding. That would be...”*

*“Usually, the one who benefits the most is the culprit.”*

*Everyone’s gaze turned toward the door.*

*Reporter Eun Haje was scratching her head.*

*“Honestly, half of what you’re saying sounds like nonsense to me, but the overall context is right in my field. That’s how the world usually works. So—who benefits the most from this mess?”*

*The researcher’s eyes sank.*

*It made sense, but guessing it seemed difficult for him by nature.*

*Then.*

*‘We can make it more concrete.’*

*“Good point, citizen.”*

*I added casually,*

*“There seem to be a few people that come to mind. Among them, was there anyone who suddenly disappeared from the scene right after the experiment?”*

*“......!”*

*The dazed researcher turned toward me, then spoke in shock.*

*“There is.”*

*“Wow.”*

*An admiring exclamation came from Eun Haje.*

*【 Excellent interrogation, my friend! 】*

*“Who?”*

*“A sponsor who insisted on attending this experiment. A spirit affiliated with the company that cooperated with us to develop the Dreamwake Solution. That is...”*

*“Can you tell us their name or appearance?”*

*The researcher staggered up and rummaged through what looked like his desk.*

From the bottom drawer, he pulled out a teal hardbound book labeled Cheerful Research Institute Founding Commemorative Publication *, flipping through it rapidly.*

*He stopped at one page.*

*A group photo labeled “Corporate Cooperation.”*

*The researcher pointed at one person.*

*“It’s this one.”*

*Director Cheong.*

*The same director, identical in appearance to when I had seen her in the annex basement last time, was in the photo wearing a lab coat...*

*“......”*

*A numbing shock spread through my head.*

*“She’s a being classified as a dragon-type supernatural entity. She was definitely present until the experiment was conducted on City Hall’s rooftop today, but at some point she disappeared without contact...”*

*“Hmm...”*

*Eun Haje spoke uneasily.*

*“This feels really obvious. The partner company stabbed you in the back and ran off with the results, didn’t they? Or they tweaked the environment to get the outcome they wanted and it all blew up.”*

*“...!”*

*Shock and rejection crossed Lee Heoun’s face, then shifted into resignation and bitterness.*

*“...That may be the case. We’re already...”*

*The next moment, Lee Heoun’s thumb fell to the floor.*

*It too was plastic.*

*“...!”*

*“There really isn’t much time left now.”*

*Urgency filled his face.*

*“Please escape to the subway. This experiment’s range was limited to ‘aboveground,’ so the subway—being outside the origin point—should be relatively safe. At least you should survive...”*

*Then he bowed deeply toward the counseling teacher.*

*“I’m sorry. Given the circumstances, there’s a high probability that your treasure was used in this experiment.”*

*“......”*

*“So it might still be on the rooftop... but I can’t be certain. And this institute no longer has any powerful mystical toys capable of reaching there.”*

*The counseling teacher didn’t reply.*

*Lee Heoun couldn’t raise his head, and the movements of the corridor and office’s flashy toys began stopping intermittently.*

*Then Lee Heoun’s foot fell off with a dull sound.*

*A toy shoe like that of a nutcracker soldier rolled across the floor. The researcher staggered.*

*High school student Ryu Jaekwan, who had been near the door, approached cautiously.*

*“I—I can help support you...”*

*“Thank you, but...”*

*A bitter smile spread across Lee Heoun’s face.*

*“I can only live inside this institute. I’ve already lost most of my body.”*

*“......”*

*“But that researcher... he’ll be able to live even if he leaves.”*

*He pointed at Researcher Lee Jaheon near the door.*

*“He’s from another department. He barely participated in this research. He bears no responsibility, so please take him with you if possible...”*

*Despite those thoughtful words, Lee Jaheon simply stared at him, seemingly unmoved.*

*“You should evacuate too... don’t rely on that strange button item anymore. Leave the office and escape now. Go.”*

*...Button item?*

*I couldn’t see Lee Jaheon’s face, but I did see him step into the corridor instead of nodding.*

*“I’ll guide you to the subway entrance.”*

*That seemed to reassure Lee Heoun.*

*“I’ll look for another method, so it’s best if everyone leaves.”*

*“Phew.”*

*Starting with Reporter Eun Haje, everyone exited the office.*

*And when even the counseling teacher stepped into the corridor—*

*“......”*

*“Agent?”*

*I quietly stood in front of Lee Heoun.*

*“Researcher. Are you almost certain that a person from a ‘world without urban legends’ has come here?”*

*“That’s... yes.”*

*“Does that outcome make you happy?”*

*Beyond regret and despair, a flicker of achievement as a researcher passed briefly across Lee Heoun’s face.*

*“At least that much, yes.”*

*“I see.”*

*I lowered my head and whispered into his ear.*

*“Then does it not matter what happens to the being you summoned?”*

*“...What?”*

*“What you summoned wasn’t a spirit. It was a person. Just a person.”*

*My voice spoke slowly, deliberately.*

*“Have you ever imagined what would happen to an ordinary person—someone who had lived a normal life without ever encountering supernatural phenomena—if they were suddenly dragged into an unfamiliar environment like this?”*

*“What are you—”*

*Lee Heoun’s puzzled gaze changed the moment our eyes met.*

*“......”*

*“W-wait.”*

*His eyes trembled.*

*“Could it be...”*

*I silently looked down at him.*

*His eyes hurriedly scanned my appearance.*

*They stopped at my clothes, and his pupils shook.*

*As if he had realized they weren’t an official agent’s uniform, but the attire of a cooperative spirit—the counseling teacher.*

*“...Agent, what is your name?”*

*“Who knows.”*

*“Are you... a citizen of Sekwang Special City?”*

*“Who knows.”*

*Lee Heoun looked me over desperately, as if trying to find a clue—or something to remember.*

*Instead of wavering, I leaned closer and whispered.*

*“But there’s one thing I want to tell you.”*

*“Yes—yes! What is it...”*

*“In my opinion, even if your invitation had succeeded... the one you summoned would never cooperate with you.”*

*“......”*

*“Never.”*

*Lee Heoun froze.*

*Beeeep—beep—beep!!!*

*The fire alarm began blaring through the corridor.*

*Leaving the dazed Cheerful Research Institute researcher behind, I stepped out into the hallway.*

*There was no way to save Lee Heoun.*

*And even if there had been, I couldn’t be sure I would have used it—an idea that filled me with a quiet sense of desolation.*

*“......”*

*‘Let’s go.’*

*There was something I had to do.*

*‘We need to get two people... no, three people out safely.’*

*And just as I started walking down the corridor—*

*“Agent.”*

*The counseling teacher spoke.*

*“Did you know? According to what that researcher just told us, this place is beneath Sekwang City Hall.”*

*“...What?!”*

*I heard Lee Jaheon’s blank voice.*

*“This is beneath Sekwang City Hall...”*

*“I heard that part! Then doesn’t that mean we’re directly under the incident site right now?”*

*“Yes.”*

*The moment I felt something beyond unease—a chill—*

*A gentle voice followed.*

*“Then if we just go up, that would be the rooftop, wouldn’t it?”*

*“Yes.”*

*Damn it.*

*“However, the probability of extreme danger is exceedingly high.”*

*“I know. But sometimes, even if it’s dangerous, it still has to be done.”*

*...I could feel the counseling teacher’s gaze on me.*

*“Agent.”*

*“......”*

*“Shall we go together? To City Hall’s rooftop.”*

*“......”*

*“We can use the emergency stairs. I’ll provide plenty of fruit.”*

*Just as I was about to turn to him with a stiff head—*

*“I do not recommend that course of action, Mr. Roe.”*

*“......”*

*...?!*

*Wait. Hold on.*

*“...Manager?”*

*“Yes.”*

I spun around toward Researcher Lee Jaheon—no, toward Lee Jaheon *.*

*My heart pounded.*

*Though his face was absent, instead of the usual unsettling unease, I felt sheer shock.*

*No way.*

*“Do you remember—”*

*No! That was too broad a question.*

*After a moment of hesitation, I chose one.*

*A question trivial on the surface, yet fundamental at its core.*

*......*

*“Do you remember which team we were in at the company?”*

*“Yes.”*

*And for no clear reason, I knew.*

*“Team D.”*

If a face had been visible, the lizard’s snout would have been curved into a smile.
