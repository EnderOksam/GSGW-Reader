# Chapter 312

【  Congratulations, friend! You’ve got a new goal. 】

I put the Dream Incubator back inside my tattoo storage.

Now that I knew this incubator could create the potion that would send me home, there was no way I could leave it in this burnt-down office.

My heart kept pounding.

*If I end the Sekwang Special City urban legend, I can go home.*

That sentence wouldn’t leave my mind.

However—

“Grapes.”

“......”

“You’re planning to use that to make your wish to go home?”

I turned to face him.

Agent Choi was watching me.

*He noticed.*

He had already read the documents in the dream cultivation room, and he had seen the button I assembled.

He must already be convinced that I found a way to make the potion that becomes the original Wish Ticket.

He had been unusually quiet for a while before he finally spoke.

“Isn’t there another way?”

“There might be.”

There probably wasn’t.

More importantly, when the most certain method is right in front of us, suggesting we should search for another option was basically his indirect attempt to stop me.

So I also needed to speak gently.

Openly.

【 Excellent! 】

“For now, I’m thinking of carrying out Director Ho’s project while learning more about Sekwang Special City.”

“...Really?”

“Yes. As I said, if I want to obtain a Daydream Potion Machine, going through Director Ho is the most accurate way.”

Ho Yuwon would absolutely try to find out why I suddenly asked for a Daydream Potion Machine.

It had nothing to do with exploration.

*And once he knows my situation, he’ll definitely try to use it.*

I couldn’t know whether Director Ho wanted the Sekwang Special City urban legend resolved.

But if I found what Ho Yuwon wanted inside Sekwang Special City and offered it in exchange for the Daydream Potion Machine—

*It’ll definitely work.*

And—

If installing the Daydream Potion Machine here ends the Sekwang Special City legend, just as Lee Kangheon’s note said...

“......”

My brain buzzed. Just imagining the possibility made me want to start immediately.

But we would only know after installing it.

“And since installing the incubator itself had no issues, it should be fine to try installing it.”

Even if that weren’t the case, I couldn’t fully trust Lee Kangheon’s note, so I still needed to scout Sekwang Special City.

To gather information for its closure.

Also, very few places had such good solution-extraction efficiency.

*Just the escape clear from the subway station gave almost an A-grade extract.*

Which meant simply resolving subway stations one by one would give a considerable profit.

Wait. Thinking about it—if burning down the Midday Station and resolving it produced extract, then...

I frantically searched through my tattoo space.

*The collector...*

...Was there!

I saw the radiant, golden collector.

*Is this really A-grade?*

That meant between 100,000 and 200,000...

If I added the extract I already had and what I gained from resolving the Sekwang Technical High School legend, I might actually have 400,000–500,000.

*That’s good.*

It fills up nicely. Right. If I just repeat what I’ve been doing nine more times...

......

Wait.

Couldn’t I just keep killing myself here?

Some of the Sekwang Special City subway stations were extremely dangerous. If I kept dying in those and exiting immediately, the dream-essence collector would fill up.

That still counted as clearing, didn’t it?

Then that’s fine. I could keep entering.

Then...

【 Efficient, friend! But theory and reality often differ. Please consider carefully... 】

Ah.

...Right. The dream-essence density would probably drop.

*If you don’t fully experience the darkness, the essence will only be low-grade.*

There’s a reason Daydream doesn’t throw the same person into the same legend every day without breaks.

*Thanks, Braun.*

【  Oh, it’s only natural for a good friend! 】

I looked at the A-grade extract shimmering inside the collector and steadied myself again.

So first, I need to finish filling the extracts I have...

“You’re going to use that?”

“Pardon? Not now—later...”

“Then until then, can I hold onto the incubator?”

“No.”

“......”

Damn.

I answered too fast.

I forced a baffled expression and looked at him.

“I only took it because I have storage space. Agent, how exactly are you planning to carry it...?”

“Hey, I’ve got strength and guts~”

Agent Choi joked lightly, but his eyes were cold.

“Why don’t you just put the incubator back where it was?”

“......”

“Well, anyway, Grapes, you’re planning to keep exploring the subway here.”

“Yes.”

“I see. Alright.”

Agent Choi stood up and gave me a small smile.

“Let’s go.”

“......”

“I want to meet the remaining people here, too.”

That—

“Let’s see... Jaekwan said he’s in the train shelter, right?”

“...Yes.”

A cold wave of guilt spread through my chest, cooling my head.

“I’ll guide you.”

We left the charred corridor of the Research Institute and headed toward the platform.

***

Some time later.

Train G1572 emerged onto the platform. When I waved my hand, the door of car 7 opened slightly late.

“Roe Deer—ah.”

“It’s been a while, Agent Mint.”

When Ms. Go Yeongeun saw Agent Choi, her eyes wavered, and she greeted him with an expression that was somewhere between awkward and relieved.

And then—

“Agent.”

“Bronze.”

The dead walked into car 7.

The senior agent of Hyeonmu Team 1 stared at his junior, who had died and been trapped inside a catastrophe-class disaster. After a long silence, he smiled faintly.

“You look good, Bronze.”

“Yes.”

Agent Bronze gave a small smile.

Because he was inside the train, his dead complexion didn’t show.

Just like when he was alive.

“I’m doing well.”

“...Good.”

The look in Agent Choi’s eyes resembled the feeling I had earlier.

Guilt.

But he forced that emotion down and resumed speaking with a neutral tone.

He patted Bronze’s back and continued a conversation that sounded ordinary but carried meaning beneath it.

“About your future work—”

“Why worry about that? Your ace senior is here—”

He was clearly talking about how they’d cover Bronze’s absence.

And then—

“...Is there anyone you want me to deliver a message to?”

He was basically asking whether Bronze had any last words he wanted relayed, just in case.

*Ah.*

My toes curled.

Restlessness.

*I need to act fast.*

I should have guided Agent Choi to the shelter, then immediately taken any train to the next station...

“You seem restless.”

“...!”

Assistant Manager Eun Haje, who had been quietly observing the two agents like I was, turned to me.

“Roe Deer. You’re faster than I thought. You ran off like an actual deer.”

“Assistant Manager...”

“What were you in such a hurry for that you only left a note...?”

She paused when she saw my face.

“You look pale.”

“...Pardon?”

“You’re so white. Anyone would think you’re the corpse, not me.”

A terrifying joke, said casually.

“I’m fine. I just... discovered a few things, that’s all.”

“......”

She lowered her voice and pulled me aside.

“That Dream Incubator or whatever—was it because of that?”

I slowly nodded.

“More precisely, I need our company’s potion-manufacturing machine.”

“...That’s really out of nowhere.”

I didn’t explain further.

She was still trying to accept that she died and was trapped here. Adding more burden because of the note I left felt wrong.

Just then, the two agents finished talking and approached.

As soon as I saw Agent Bronze, I blurted out, “I’m sorry, Agent. I should have asked if you had anything helpful when I escaped earlier, but I was in a rush...”

“It’s fine.”

He shook his head.

“I already told Agent Choi everything you’d need. ...Please don’t feel pressured.”

“...Yes.”

Standing still inside this train was becoming harder and harder.

Thankfully, it didn’t last long.

*This stop is Morning Station...*

The train had completed a full loop and returned to the destination.

I rushed toward the door.

“I’ll get off here.”

“Already?”

“Yes. If you need anything, please tell me. I’ll find it.”

“Hmm...”

Assistant Manager Eun Haje stared at me, then returned to Ms. Go Yeongeun and Agent Bronze without a word.

I made eye contact with Ms. Go Yeongeun, but I quietly bowed my head and waited.

[The doors are opening.]

And I stepped onto Morning Station.

The modern, clean platform was just as before.

I hurried toward the stairs—

“Let’s go together, Grapes!”

“......”

I felt Agent Choi’s arm sling around my shoulders.

【 Hmph. Still an overfriendly guy, I see. 】

Great. He got off, too.

Well, if Agent Choi decided to accompany me, stopping him would be difficult.

And honestly, for this station, having him with me was something I wanted.

In that case—

“Um, Agent.”

“Yeah?”

I carefully spoke.

“If the collector fills up during exploration, could you... sell the extract to me?”

He had helped me extract essence when resolving the Sekwang Technical High School legend.

So this time too—

“Nope.”

“......”

“Grapes. You said we’d split the last one fifty-fifty. Didn’t you?”

Ah.

“...Yes. I did.”

【 Unbelievable! After all the help my friend has given, such rude and stingy words! 】

No.

A wave of resentment suddenly surged—but I forced it down.

Don’t be shameless.

*I’ll negotiate properly later.*

A good offer would work.

I could do this—

“Oh, right. I forgot. Civil servant.”

......

......!!

“Take my collector first, Roe Deer.”

I turned around.

Assistant Manager Eun Haje was standing there.

And Agent Bronze behind her.

They had stepped onto the platform—and behind them, the train doors closed.

The train left the station.

“......”

I stared at them, stunned.

“Why did you get off?”

“Agent Grapes.”

“Please get back on! Hurry!”

“No.”

“...!!”

Their bodies had already lost their vitality—the pale forms of the dead, exposed.

Yet both were calm.

Even Agent Choi.

... *Did they already talk about this?*

I clenched my teeth.

“We gathered information about this station from the shelter.”

“......”

“This is a good place for an agent and a journalist to explore.”

“......”

“Agent Grapes.”

Damn it.

*I didn’t share the wiki information so they wouldn’t know.*

But they had inferred it from a survivor’s testimony.

“Do you know what used to be here?”

[Morning Station (Scale Court)]

The station where Sekwang Special City’s district court had been located.

“...A court, right.”

“Yes. But that’s not all.”

......

Agent Bronze’s gaze moved toward the stairs.

╔════════════════════╗

Scale Court

╚════════════════════╝

The wooden plaque above the door had a silver scale emblem next to it.

And the small engraved pattern inside that scale—

Anyone familiar would recognize it.

It was the same one inside the Silver Heart.

Disaster Management Bureau equipment.

The underground floor of this district court was also the location of a secret government agency.

“Given the circumstances, the local branch of the Disaster Management Bureau seems to have been here.”

Yes.

This was the missing Disaster Management Bureau branch of Sekwang Special City.

On Disaster Day, this agency evacuated the greatest number of survivors into the underground.

Afterward, the scattered agents attempted both rescue missions and distress calls—but success was extremely rare.

However, the agonizing debate over *whom* to rescue remained in this place, even after their deaths.

Do you know what happens in a subway station that mutated into a legend because of that?

Here, in Morning Station, that endless debate repeats forever.

Scale Court will determine whether you are worthy of being rescued.

Using tried-and-tested methods.

*Creak—*

Above the stairs.

The door under the plaque opened, revealing the silver courtroom.

“Do you remember? Everyone at the Horizon Lodge turned out to be criminals with terrible pasts.”

“......”

“It seems that same equipment remained in this branch.”

The *Scale of Evil* will weigh you.

The Scale of Evil—

A device originally used by the Bureau to requisition civilian victims.

It now dominates this station.

“......”

But—

Not in the way Agent Bronze thinks.

It was twisted and contaminated.

Originally, the scale weighed guilt as the standard measure.

But on Disaster Day, it changed.

I sucked in a breath.

What you love most will be placed on the opposite scale.
