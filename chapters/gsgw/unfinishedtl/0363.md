# Chapter 362

Warm, gentle sunlight dyes the rooftop in gold.

One of the intrinsic elements of the Fox Counseling Room.

Even though there should be nothing beyond that window, soft sunshine from a clear day always filters through the curtains.

A space prepared for the client.

Click.

The door to the counseling room beyond the waiting area stands wide open.

Unlike the waiting room, the counseling room inside was usually closed.

It only opened when a session was in progress, or when the counselor called someone in—so even I had only entered it a few times.

But it is unchanged.

Fwoooo—

Beyond the large window stretches a green garden, and inside is a cozy space with soft sofas.

The green leaves of the potted plant bearing fox-bead fruit sway in the wind.

The counselor, who had been watching that sight with me, smiles.

In a refreshed voice, he says, “Since many of them have difficulty moving, the counseling room will go out to greet them directly.”

“……”

“Roe Deer-nim. May the clients come in now?”

…….

“Yes.”

The full view of the Fox Counseling Room spreads across the rooftop.

Inside the torn cocoon, peaceful sunlight touches the face of the transformed Kim Soleum.

Before long, I am fully revealed from within the cocoon.

Drawn out.

The clumped mass of Segwang Special City’s citizens fused beneath his upper body—the distorted forms of countless human figures—all gently enter the Fox Counseling Room as though embraced.

A hidden place.

A small space belonging to a counselor for the contaminated—one that must be visited by knocking.

That space willingly receives everything distorted and hidden beneath the rooftop as visitors.

No.

As clients who will remain together for a long time.

…….

“Welcome.”

Beside me, the counselor’s form completely melts down.

Like wrapping paper stuffed with unbearable contents, like a cloth bundle stretched tight and bulging so that the shapes inside show through—his form distorts and swells.

It fades.

The counselor’s ghost story—having swallowed the influence of Kim Soleum within the cocoon, who rejected all ghost stories but mine, and the mass of contaminated Segwang citizens—was collapsing.

Unable to withstand that influence.

It becomes completely washed out, like watercolor diluted in water…

“Don’t worry.”

“…….”

“Mm, though now… I may have difficulty accepting my former clients. I suppose there will come a time when I’m needed…”

His voice grows faint.

As if it can no longer affect reality.

“…Don’t worry.”

I say with effort.

And I listen desperately.

“Based on their counseling experiences so far, they’ll find a way….”

“You’re returning my own words to me.”

A laugh is heard.

“That’s right. And since I’ve made my choice, I’ll go meet the moment I’ve been waiting for.”

“…….”

“Roe Deer-nim.”

The warm aura of the counseling room filling the rooftop begins to fade as the last citizen of Segwang Special City enters the room…

Ho Yuwon looks at me.

“Farewell.”

“…….”

“No, perhaps that expression isn’t appropriate. We’ll continue our counseling together.”

“…….”

“It’s just that in your current form, you won’t be able to perceive it.”

I finally speak.

“If we pull you out of the counseling room even now—”

“Hmm? Abandon the client who needs help the most? Surely you didn’t see me as that kind of counselor? That would be truly sad….”

I take a deep breath.

His gentle voice faintly follows.

“I’m joking, actually.”

…….

“I’m not sad.”

Ho Yuwon smiles and extends his hand.

“Ho Yuwon’s first client… and my last client.”

“…….”

I extend my hand as well.

We shake.

“Thank you.”

Ho Yuwon’s form completely collapses.

The counselor’s shape disappears beneath my grasp, drawn into the counseling room and scattering…

Now, the door of the Fox Counseling Room closes.

As it slides shut, perhaps—just perhaps—I thought I saw the small tail of a fox moving inside…

Click.

The rooftop emergency door locks softly.

Silence settles everywhere.

The nameplate I hung falls to the ground like an ordinary wooden ornament when the emergency door shuts.

“…….”

I catch it with both hands.

The nameplate that holds no trace of mystery.

“…….”

I look up.

The sunset shines.

A red and warm yellow light emerges between the clouds and spreads across the rooftop—like the sunlight of the Fox Counseling Room.

Over the broken Dream Incubator, over the stopped and dark potion-making machine…

Inside the empty cocoon.

“…….”

Segwang Special City.

The day of May 4th was ending.

Ah.

Below the rooftop, shattered roads and sidewalks, a city devoid of people, come into view.

Everything is rapidly aging.

As though the past several years are pouring in at once, the city catches up to the time outside.

But today, the contaminated citizens of this city will remain safe within the flow of time.

They will no longer be trapped in May 4th, repeating death—instead, they are safely receiving counseling.

By the counselor who had long waited for this moment.

By one fox.

“…….”

“Roe Deer-ssi.”

I turn my head.

A figure with a lizard’s head emerges through the rooftop door.

“Section Chief.”

The figure that had been a faceless researcher now bears the familiar head of Section Chief Lee Jaheon.

“You’ve returned.”

“Yes. I moved immediately upon waking outside the Special City.”

Ah.

“…The blockade is completely lifting, then.”

“Yes. Cracks and fractures are forming; more than twenty entry points currently exist.”

Section Chief Lee Jaheon’s gaze shifts below.

“The blockade will soon vanish entirely.”

…….

Segwang Special City will thus be reabsorbed into reality.

And…

It’s over.

At last.

The city’s liberation.

I inhale deeply and exhale.

“…You arrived very quickly. Did you receive our assistance?”

“I am our assistance.”

That returned, too.

“But.”

I turn my head.

The ends of the lizard’s snout curl slightly upward.

“Existing as a separate entity from us was a unique experience.”

“It was. …Truly.”

I feel my state, once swallowed by the resort, slowly stabilizing.

My melted body regains its shape.

And I reclaim my identity as the person once completely covered by the Flower Golden mascot.

Despite the bloated mascot identity and pressure, my own identity maintains a precarious balance.

As if someone is observing my condition and constantly calming it.

Even if the consciousness of the Good Friend—untouched by madness—cannot know it.

It feels as though someone is still counseling me, even now.

“It was… a unique experience.”

I carefully tuck the Fox Counseling Room nameplate into my coat.

I know the tattoo’s inventory function has returned—but I do not use it.

……

No.

Don’t get too immersed.

I survived.

I am recovering thanks to someone.

It is enough, for now, to feel that deeply.

I take a breath and speak more brightly.

“…Are the other team members all right? The deputy and the agent.”

“Yes. All personnel showed signs of waking.”

“…! That’s a relief.”

Truly.

Relief spreads deeply.

Now we’ll have to let the people out from Resort Branch No. 2.

Of course, as the mascot, I already knew they were fine…

I clench the empty hand that had been holding the nameplate.

Section Chief Lee Jaheon, who had been watching closely, asks, “Did Director Ho reach this place?”

“……Yes.”

I answer quietly.

“The extinction-class supernatural disaster of Segwang Special City was concluded by Ho Yuwon.”

“…….”

“I see.”

The lizard replies.

“However, Director Ho’s arrival here was influenced by you, Roe Deer-ssi.”

“…….”

“This attempt is an extremely exceptional case of dark termination—brought about through your choice and effort.”

I barely manage a quiet reply.

“Yes.”

I swallow the surge rising in my throat.

And try to steady my voice.

“I… and the Fox Counseling Room will likely be difficult to visit now. It has accepted an enormous number of clients. It says it’s closing. Even if we use the old method of entry, it won’t respond anymore.”

No matter how the nameplate is hung or the door opened, the waiting room bathed in warm sunlight will not appear again.

“I understand. That explains why I awoke in an external space rather than the long-term ward.”

“…Yes.”

In the meantime, he must have released all the clients he had ‘stored’ in the original ward.

Really.

“Well, Director Ho wasn’t the most comfortable direct superior as a project executive.”

I speak lightly.

“But to his clients, he was truly a good counselor, wasn’t he?”

…….

“Yes.”

The wind brushes past.

The empty shell of the cocoon on the rooftop continues to crumble and disappear.

I know.

Once I leave this city, this ghost story will vanish entirely from this place.

And…

“Director Ho’s project is over.”

I turn to Section Chief Lee Jaheon.

“The Daydream executive is gone, and the darkness to explore is gone as well.”

“Yes.”

Or…

I don’t know.

A strange sadness—somewhere between relief and loneliness—spreads faintly through my heart.

I try to think of good things.

Things worth feeling relieved about.

Are Ms. Yeongeun’s parents safe?

I hope so. No—now that I think of it, Ms. Yeongeun ran out of the resort the moment I opened the gate.

She’s probably searching for them already.

I see people who had been hiding emerging from buildings.

They will all return home.

Home.

…….

Will I be able to return home now?

If I leave here and safely extract the dream essence, and make the “Children’s Paradise Syrup” from it—will I be able to go home?

I still have a usable Dream Incubator.

And I have the grip-tok that will act as a button.

A small hope rises.
