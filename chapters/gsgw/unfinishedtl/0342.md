# Chapter 341

*I swallowed.*

*The fierce, ominous face of the lion mask was staring straight at me.*

*Its two yellow, sharp eyes moved inside the mask as if they were alive on their own.*

*“Did you know? There are many regional legends about lion masks... and one of them is that they drive away evil spirits.”*

*“......”*

*The mane of the lion mask spilled over my shoulder.*

*It looked as if, at any moment, the mask might open its snout, lined with rows of sharp teeth.*

*“Wouldn’t harming civilians after being bewitched by Kkwangcheol count as an evil spirit too~?”*

*I saw that chilling thing draw closer.*

*Watching it...*

*“...No.”*

*I answered.*

*“I have no intention of impersonating an agent and stirring up chaos in a disaster of this scale.”*

*“Oh ho.”*

*“And you know I’m innocent too, Agent.”*

*“Why do you think that?”*

*The lion mask tilted its head, the mane sliding down my back.*

*It felt cold and strange.*

*A bead of cold sweat ran down the back of my neck.*

*‘I need to sort this out properly.’*

*Suppressing my anxiety as much as I could, I spoke calmly.*

*“You wouldn’t have lent me agent equipment if you thought I was guilty.”*

*“...!”*

*“If you were suspicious, you wouldn’t have given me gear that helps with escaping. Isn’t that right?”*

*I barely turned my eyes to check my companions.*

*Thinking it was just ‘a conversation between agents,’ the two of them were looking at the surrounding devastation rather than at us, without much suspicion.*

*“If you really can distinguish corruption by scent, Agent, then you’d know I have no malice or guilt.”*

*Maybe it was because you saw the people I was trying to rescue—and how I treated them—that you judged me sincere.*

*‘Please.’*

*Trying to release the tension that felt like my hands were curling in on themselves, I continued.*

*“It’s true that I signed a contract with Daydream. But that has nothing to do with what I’m about to do in this situation.”*

*“......”*

*The lion mask swayed, moving as if to peer into me.*

*“What are you going to do?”*

*“As I said, I’ll rescue those two people. There are people I have to save. And...”*

*After hesitating, I said it.*

*“If possible, I want to end this disaster.”*

*The lion mask fell silent.*

*Then the mask shook—*

*“Your words are sincere!”*

*The mane swept back.*

*I gasped for breath.*

*In the meantime, the lion mask—having regained its playful movements—grabbed my arm.*

*“Then I’ll leave the rescue to you, fellow agent!”*

*“...Yes.”*

*Phew.*

*Watching the lion mask return to its friendly-agent demeanor, I let out a sigh of relief inwardly.*

*But tension still clung to my body.*

*...The lion mask still hadn’t let go of my arm.*

*‘...Strangely enough, it feels like I’m facing an entity straight out of an urban legend.’*

*Was it because of that lion mask? I could barely feel any humanity from that agent.*

*The lion-mask agent twisted its head amiably.*

*Yet it stayed glued to me, never releasing my arm.*

*As if ready to drag me away at any moment.*

*“By the way, how did that vicious Kkwangcheol end up coiling itself around your body, Agent?”*

*“......”*

*I don’t even know who Mr. Kkwangcheol is, but...*

*‘One of the Daydream directors?’*

*But here, I can’t say things like—*

*— I signed contracts with two development directors, one of whom went insane after being eaten by a local cooperative spirit. Oh, and before that I also signed an employment contract, so I might be contracted with another director from a different department too. Like octopus legs, you know?*

*...I swallowed those words and summarized vaguely instead.*

*“You mean how I came to contract with Daydream?”*

*“That’s right!”*

*In that case, there was a simple, concise answer.*

*“I was deceived.”*

*“Ah.”*

*“And I’m looking for a way out.”*

*The lion-mask agent seemed lost in thought, and soon the white mane floated lightly.*

*“Hm. So you’re on leave so as not to inconvenience the Administration?”*

*“Not only that... honestly, it’s also for my own safety.”*

*Because I was suspected of being a spy.*

*But I really wasn’t, so it felt unfair...*

*“Of course, that makes sense!”*

*Fortunately, they responded cheerfully. Phew.*

*Letting out a relieved breath, I looked at the remaining stations.*

*...About eight minutes left.*

*‘So we’ll reach Sekwang Station soon.’*

*If I get detained by agents at a time like this and get delayed any further, I might not be able to find the counseling teacher at Sekwang Station.*

*Then I’d lose everything.*

*‘I need to ease the atmosphere quickly...’*

*But suddenly, a cold voice reached my ear.*

*“That’s strange too.”*

*“...Yes?”*

*“Then how did a fellow agent on leave learn about the disaster and enter the city to rescue someone?”*

*“...!”*

*“The disaster hasn’t even been half a day old—barely a few hours, really. Yet you’ve already found civilians to rescue and are here!”*

*“......”*

*“Even if news spread outside, it’s still far too soon. Quite a coincidence!”*

*It was a sharp question.*

*And a precise one.*

*‘The timeline doesn’t match.’*

*I was already in this city the moment the disaster alert arrived.*

*And it takes quite a while before agents from other cities can enter Sekwang.*

*So saying ‘I entered because there was a rescue request’ doesn’t work. The timing doesn’t add up.*

*...I need time to think of an answer. But there’s no time. Damn it!*

*‘I need to counterattack...’*

*As I turned my thoughts over, I noticed something.*

*The agents visible in the car ahead.*

*...!*

*“...By that logic, you’re suspicious too, Agent.”*

*“Oh? Me?”*

*“Yes.”*

*I spoke calmly.*

*“You’re not from the Sekwang City branch either, are you?”*

*“...Why do you think that?”*

*“Look over there.”*

*Following my gaze, the lion-mask agent looked ahead.*

*To the agents in the next car, handling the accident and explaining the ‘supernatural disaster’ to civilians.*

*“The agents over there and you—the agent wearing the lion mask in front of me—have slightly different jacket details.”*

*“......”*

*The agents in the front car had patches with the Sekwang City logo on their jacket pockets.*

*The lion-mask agent checked their own clean, empty pocket, then raised their head.*

*“...Fellow agent. You have remarkably sharp eyes!”*

*“Thank you.”*

*But the lion mask didn’t waver.*

*“I’m just filling in temporarily, supporting this branch! So naturally, I had to deploy with them!”*

*Phew.*

*“But your clothes... didn’t you say they were lent by a cooperative spirit?”*

*Get it together. Choose your words carefully.*

*Cleverly.*

*“They belong to a cooperative spirit. And... that person is fully aware that I’m wearing these clothes.”*

*I didn’t say they were lent.*

*Even so, the lion mask tilted its head.*

*“How odd. That cooperative spirit must have known you were suspicious!”*

*“...!”*

*“And yet they just gave you a jacket—what a ghostly mystery. Did you perhaps turn the tables and bewitch a fox instead?”*

*I shook my head.*

*“I don’t know about that either. But I have never lied.”*

*“Hmmm. Very well.”*

*The lion mask swayed as if turning upside down, then returned.*

*“Then let’s go back to the main point and find out your true intentions!”*

*“......”*

*Damn it.*

*The lion mask stared at me.*

*That strangely swaying mane poured over my shoulder once more.*

*“How exactly did you know a disaster would happen today, and how did you promptly choose someone to rescue and end up here?”*

*I thought this lion mask felt almost monstrous...*

*‘Lacking humanity...’*

*......*

*Which, put another way, might mean they could bear truths too heavy for a human to handle.*

*‘If that’s the case.’*

*This might be the move that changes the situation.*

*...There wasn’t much time left until we reached Sekwang Station.*

*“Agent.”*

*I lowered my voice even further.*

*“What would you think if this kept repeating?”*

*“Hm?”*

*“If this disaster day has been repeating dozens, hundreds of times...”*

*I locked eyes with them.*

*“And if I were aware of it—what would you think then?”*

*“...!”*

*The lion-mask agent froze.*

*I rapidly relayed what I knew about ‘what happens on the Day of the Sekwang Special City Disaster.’*

*“So you’re saying we’re endlessly repeating May 4th...?”*

*“Yes.”*

*“That’s... something.”*

*The lion mask swayed uneasily.*

*— I don’t know the details myself. My memories are vague; I just know it’s been repeated hundreds of times.*

*In reality, I’d experienced the peaceful May 4th of Sekwang City over 600 times, so it was easy for me to give specific place names.*

*They understood quickly.*

*And once again sensing that I wasn’t lying, the lion mask’s yellow eyes darkened.*

*“A time loop... I’ve seen a few supernatural disasters like that. These days they call it that... route stories, was it?”*

*“...Loop stories?”*

*“Ah, right. That.”*

*The lion-mask agent chuckled, saying they had a friend who liked that sort of thing, then soon let out a deep sigh.*

*“But even that doesn’t add up! From what I can see, this mess isn’t the kind of disaster where you’re trapped inside some haunted drama videotape!”*

*“......”*

*They seemed to be doubting my sanity.*

*But that was fine. Better than being suspected of scheming.*

*So I changed the subject.*

*“Were you originally from headquarters?”*

*“That’s right. Yes. I’m from the Azure Dragon Team at headquarters.”*

*The lion-mask agent, now speaking more casually, revealed their affiliation in a peculiar tone.*

*...A team that had already disappeared.*

*“......”*

*“Usually I handle other duties rather than rescue work, but I come out like this from time to time to support. They say if you stay too long in the workshop, you start to rub off.”*

*Wait, workshop?*

*I looked at the lion-mask agent again.*

*Their speech.*

*Their sense of smell.*

*Their way of summoning will-o’-the-wisps...*

*“By any chance, is your main assignment... infiltrating goblin workshops and living while pretending to be a goblin?”*

*“...!”*

*I recalled a memory that felt distant enough to fade—back when I was a new employee at Daydream.*

*The agent from the goblin workshop who had put adhesive on my grip tok.*

*— Kim-seobang. Looks like you’ve got something good there!*

*‘But after joining, I never saw them again.’*

*I never even heard them mentioned.*

*If they were an agent, you’d expect them to return to their post—or at least be talked about.*

*Unless they’d lost their original self, no longer able to return from ‘pretending to be a goblin.’*

*Like Team Leader Hong, who left half of herself at that high school, or the elder from Black Tortoise Team 1.*

*Right here.*

*An echo-like existence trapped in an extinction-level disaster.*

*“...How did you know?”*

*“...You may not remember, but we’ve met before.”*

*“Hmmm.”*

*The lion mask sighed.*

*And then—*

*“I understand.”*

*The pressure the lion mask had been giving off eased slightly.*

*“No wonder I couldn’t take this lion mask off. I must’ve been tainted by the disaster!”*

*The agent said something serious in a serious tone while tapping the lion mask, but hidden behind the comical face, it looked almost exaggerated.*

*That seemed to be how they accepted it.*

*The possibility that they themselves might be trapped on May 4th.*

*‘Phew.’*

*I held back a sigh of relief.*

*...Instead of panicking, this agent seemed to sink into deep thought.*

*“So... you were heading to Sekwang Station to properly find out what’s really happening on your end.”*

*“That’s correct.”*

*“I’d like to go too, but I’ve got my own assignment.”*

*The lion-mask agent turned around.*

*At the civilians panicking inside the train.*

*“Even if your story about the day repeating is true, and even if the disaster ends—if no civilians are alive by the end of the day, it means nothing.”*

*“......”*

*“Someone has to protect the civilians and carry out rescues.”*

*I slowly nodded.*

*“You’re absolutely right.”*

This station is Sekwang. Sekwang Station... *.*

*“...We’ve arrived.”*

*“Yes.”*

*The lion mask listened quietly to the announcement, then finally held out a hand to me and said:*

*“All right. Then let’s each do our best where we are!”*

*Then they hastily added, exaggerating their tone:*

*“And don’t think that being under Daydream Kkwangcheol is where you belong!”*

*No.*

*Without thinking, I said:*

*“...Well, not everyone who works at Daydream is a bad person.”*

*“Then at least name one!”*

*I barely stopped my eyes from turning toward Reporter Eun Haje.*

*Instead, something else came to mind.*

*If these agents were the ones who entered the subway and organized the civilians...*

*They might be connected to the train shelter.*

*“Do you know a Daydream employee named Lee Kangheon?”*

*“Lee Kangheon? Never heard that name in my life!”*

*“If you do meet him, I hope you won’t be hostile right away.”*

*“Someone you know?”*

*“...Sort of.”*

*Recalling the note he left behind, I nodded with a faint smile.*

*“Hm. I’ll keep that in mind!”*

*Only then did the lion mask finally lift its head and speak in a voice that sounded like laughter.*

*Their speech had returned to normal.*

*“Agent! If you go near Sekwang Station, you’ll definitely meet the local Black Tortoise Team agents. Try cooperating with them! I’m counting on you.”*

*It meant the conversation was over.*

*“Yes.”*

*As the train began to stop, I bowed my head.*

*“Thank you.”*

*The lion mask’s yellow eyes looked at me strangely.*

The doors will open on the left. Left side...

*And just before the doors opened, the agent held out a hand and said:*

*“Fellow agent, if you’re okay with it, shall we at least exchange names? Agent names, that is!”*

*The lion mask smiled playfully.*

*“I’m Agent Chogae.”*

*...!!*

*“Agent?”*

*I barely managed to speak.*

*“...I don’t think I can tell you mine.”*

*“That happens!”*

*The lion mask tilted its head.*

*“Then if things get urgent later, feel free to use my agent name. Hahaha—free pass just for today!”*

*“...!”*

*Beep beep beep beep...*

*With the alert sound, the doors opened.*

*“Thank you.”*

*“Oh, don’t mention it! Let’s both hang in there.”*

*“...Yes.”*

*I gathered my companions and barely managed to get off safely at Sekwang Station, avoiding the surging crowd.*

*Through the closing screen doors and train doors, I saw the lion-mask agent waving at me while trying to control the chaotic crowd.*

*The train departed.*

*“...Phew.”*

*“You had a really long and detailed conversation. Did you figure things out?”*

*“Yes. It was a very informative conversation.”*

*I wanted to at least smile faintly at Reporter Eun Haje, who looked like she’d strip me for information the moment she got the chance—but I couldn’t.*

*The environment made it impossible.*

*“Agent...”*

*Sekwang Station was already filled with countless people.*

*People wailing, people staring blankly, people crushed to death under the tracks while trying to board trains.*

*As if various disturbances had already swept through once, a desperate, flattened sense of despair clung to the air.*

*“......”*

*I know.*

*I know that all of these people will remain trapped in the station, never rescued, and eventually all hang themselves.*

*And that in the resulting urban legend, I myself had died with a noose tightening around my neck.*

*“......”*

*“This way.”*

*We moved carefully.*

*The high schooler, Ryu Jaegwan, kept trying to push ahead and clear a path, but I moved in front to stop him as much as possible.*

*Reporter Eun Haje, like Assistant Manager Eun Haje, was the type to charge ahead too, so in the end we surged forward in fits and starts, practically sprinting through the crowd of refugees.*

*“...The exits are all blocked already.”*

*As I ran, I looked with a chilling feeling at the exits where shutters were down and objects piled up in front.*

*People who had left friends or family outside were wailing there.*

*We crossed the concourse and headed to the opposite platform...*

*“There aren’t even station staff here.”*

*“......”*

*There were many people on the opposite platform too—but even more bodies.*

*With no trains coming, it looked like they’d placed the dead here temporarily, like stacked belongings.*

*And then—*

*“This is the place you were looking for?”*

*The machine room.*

*The entrance to the Cheerful Research Institute.*

*“...Yes.”*

*A place I’d never once entered before, and probably the ‘place I must go’ the counseling teacher mentioned on the day of the disaster.*

*Right now, who knows what might be happening inside.*

*Holding my breath, I grabbed the door handle—then realized I’d need to unlock it first.*

*‘Ah.’*

*I hurriedly searched my tattoos, trying to see if I had the ID of the researcher disguised as a ‘subway employee.’*

*But at that moment—*

*Footsteps approached from behind.*

*Clear, fast steps of someone walking with purpose.*

*They were coming straight toward us.*

*“Agent!”*

*“...!”*

*At Ryu Jaegwan’s urgent call, I turned my head.*

*And froze.*

*Someone in a white lab coat was striding unhesitatingly toward us through the bodies on the platform.*

*But—*

*They had no face.*

*Where their face should have been was empty—an empty black void that you could see and not see—*

*“Wow.”*

*Wow?*

*Is that the kind of exclamation that fits? That thing... that thing has no face.*

*What is that?*

*I instinctively tried to step back, then realized there were people depending on me and planted my feet firmly. Hold it together!*

*And then—*

*“Man, what kind of face is that?”*

*Exactly!*

*“Seriously handsome.”*

*......*

*......!!*

*Wait.*

*With trembling eyes, I looked again at the researcher’s coat.*

*...They were wearing an ID badge.*

*Cheerful Researcher

Lee Jaheon*

*“Do you have authorization to enter the machine room?”*

This is insane.
