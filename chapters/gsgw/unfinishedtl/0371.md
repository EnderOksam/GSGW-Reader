# Chapter 370

The mass watches the glittering Dream Incubator.

The liquid inside it.

DE 05002160

The mass keeps watching.

Though it feels no meaning in it.

“Soleum. Just wait a little longer, okay?”

Even when people took turns standing guard over the space.

Even when the mascots outside occasionally froze as if glitched.

Even when, at some point, it was left alone.

It remained there—in the dark research facility, together with the prismatic Dream Essence solution.

Together with the astronomical Dream Essence gathered as the result of what Kim Soleum had done.

The map the mascot gave her.

Kim Soleum’s possessions.

But she looks back.

“It told me it wanted to give me a hint.”

The host had said, “This time, you won’t need a specific sentence to make a wish. The wish will simply be granted.”

“It said that was the truth you confirmed.”

The truth Kim Soleum uncovered.

The traces left at the Cheerful Research Institute.

The confirmation that among the pharmaceutical company’s products was a children’s Paradise Syrup analogue.

Its name: Wish Ticket.

The mass even perceives the word that had once been deleted and unreadable.

Because that is what it is.

She had checked.

The agents had indeed preserved such documents.

The incubator’s cultivation function removed; the required concentration for production deliberately lowered—estimated to be a degraded version.

Degradation effects:

– Requires verbalized sentence.

– Deteriorated worldline-altering function.

– Compromised fulfillment instead of true paradise.

Daydream’s “Wish Ticket” is a degraded version.

It requires verbalization, the original does not.

It grants the wish as conceived—without distortion.

And the reason the late-night talk show host gave Go Yeongeun that hint…

“…Because you can’t make the wish yourself right now… I’ll make it for you.”

Someone who would not steal the opportunity.

Someone close enough.

And among those Kim Soleum knows, the one with the most rational and wise experience using a Wish Ticket looks at the mass.

“Soleum.”

Her hand grips the tank.

The mass sees her pale fingers trembling.

“Honestly… I’m not as rational as you think.”

A desperate voice.

“I’m not a genius either.”

……

“I don’t even know if I can wish exactly what you would want. Because…”

Her trembling voice speaks truth.

“I only looked for my mom.”

“My dad was out… and he never made it home.”

……

“I’m not sure I can stop myself from wishing for him to come back alive.”

Her voice shakes.

“But Agent Choi checked with a divination ritual. And he said…”

The trembling eases.

“My father didn’t die.”

“He’s just… receiving a very earnest counseling session from someone.”

“So maybe I can do it.”
