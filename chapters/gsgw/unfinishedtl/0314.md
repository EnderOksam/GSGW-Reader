# Chapter 313

Calm... I need to stay calm.

At this station, I’m going to thoroughly investigate this courthouse ghost story and collect information and Dream Essence.

Just focus on that.

I felt anger rise at how the group was recklessly following me around in their dead bodies, but I held it back.

If I can’t persuade them, getting angry will only hurt our feelings.

It was the same with Agent Choi refusing to sell me Dream Essence.

*I just need to do well.*

I just have to do my best while keeping everyone alive.

That’s how I’ve always done it up to now.

That’s enough...

【 The sight of my friend doing their best so they won’t have regrets is beautiful. Oh, though it pains me to see those heavy shoulders... 】

I’m fine.

【 Very well. Then let us advance. This is a step toward judgment—no, trial! 】

I climbed the stairs from the platform toward the Scales Courthouse.

And before I knew it, I was staring into the wide-open doorway.

I thought the interior of the Scales Courthouse would be revealed, but—

“......”

There was nothing inside.

A pure white void.

No color, no sound, no form. In fact, it felt more like a pitch-black basement devoid of even a speck of light.

“...Are you scared, Grapes?”

“No.”

Truthfully, I was scared.

But being scared wouldn’t change anything, so I stepped inside.

**Disaster Day in Sekwang Special City.

Verifying rescue request #370614. Registering the requester as a** ***selected candidate*** **.**

**202■ Administration Case 370614—Kim Soleum. Candidate, please undergo value assessment via the Scales of Malice.**

The next moment, I snapped back to consciousness and found myself standing at the very end of a line inside a massive courtroom.

“......!”

A modern, almost grand space constructed in shades of brown, navy, and neutral colors.

In the center of the courtroom, people stood in a long line, as though waiting for a summary judgment. Murmurs filled the air.

Dressed like ordinary citizens, they almost looked as if they were here for routine administrative paperwork. The place was so calm it nearly felt *safe*.

But if you listened closely—

"...the thing is, if we don't hurry... that day... again..."

"...is it true... that they... all...?"

A chill ran down my spine.

I instinctively turned around.

The companions who had come with me were already lined up behind me.

A flash of tension crossed Agent Bronze’s face.

“Agent Grapes, I know this place looks like a courtroom. But what they’re doing over there is not an actual trial—”

**202■ Administration Case 370601—Selected Candidate Jung Hojong, please step forward.**

“...!”

A voice rang out from far ahead.

But because of the long line of people and a strangely dense haze beyond, I couldn’t make anything out clearly.

Only that a human *shape* walked out from the very front of the line—

*Thud.*

Something heavy hit the floor.

Silence.

“......”

“...What they’re doing here is probably the Disaster Management Bureau’s emergency selection procedure.”

He stopped there, but I already knew the rest.

*Contaminated.*

**202■ Administration Case 370603—Selected Candidate Kim Jahoon, please step forward.**

The people ahead of us moved forward, one by one.

We were pulled along helplessly.

At some point, ordinary-looking citizens had filled the line behind my group, and only four or five people remained in front of us.

And then—

*Thud.*

I finally saw the source of the sound.

**202■ Administration Case 370611—Selected Candidate Lee Hyemi, please step forward.**

At the highest center of the courtroom stood a massive ashen scale.

Coated in dust and grime, the scale was shaped like a balance—its two concave plates perfectly level.

A human walked toward the structure placed on the judge’s bench.

Or... *was* it human?

“...Ple... ease... don’t... take me... my daughter...”

A middle-aged figure in a light padded vest staggered forward in a daze.

**We will now administer the 8,312th reassessment regarding the candidate’s rescue eligibility.**

**Candidate, submit the evidence.**

A voice boomed from the speakers beyond the bench.

The person standing at the scale shook their head as if trying to regain consciousness.

“Y-yes...”

With trembling hands, they picked up the long object lying beside the scale.

A gray knife.

Like a pen at a public office chained down to prevent theft, the knife was tied to the scale.

The middle-aged person lifted the knife... and stabbed it into their own chest.

“......!”

Then they pulled something out.

【 Oh! A heart, I see! 】

A beating organ, still pulsing, was lifted out on the blade, grotesquely clear.

Assistant Manager Eun Haje let out a suppressed gasp.

“Public officials actually used that as a civilian procedure...?”

“No! The Bureau uses the candidate’s birth chart and legal name—”

Agent Bronze hurriedly denied it, but I already knew.

This was *also* a correct use of the Scales of Malice.

“Bronze... they used it like this, too, didn’t they?”

“......”

“The Scales of Malice were originally a supernatural disaster that people ‘repurposed’ for supposedly good use.”

Agent Choi’s eyes darkened.

“If we’re being strict, that over there is how it was used *before* people processed it.”

The symbols that can be weighed on the Scales of Malice vary—birth chart, heart, even ■■  —  but all represent the candidate’s conscience.

When formed as a heart, a person’s sense of ethics and good deeds pulse on the blade.

**Place it on the scale.**

Amazingly, despite having torn out his own heart, the middle-aged man staggered forward and placed the crimson organ—still impaled on the knife—onto the tray.

**Confirmed.**

Then the opposite tray of the scale began to tremble...

Originally, that side should hold the weight that represents sin.

The Disaster Management Bureau used the Scales of Malice chiefly to punish evil people who would be used as sacrificial victims.

But in this contaminated courthouse of Sekwang Special City, the scales instead selected people who possessed *rescue value*.

Thus—

**What you love most appears on the opposite scale.**

Instead of sin, something else materialized.

A small figure, looking like a college student in a tracksuit, stood on the right tray, fidgeting and nervously looking around.

The middle-aged man, seeing the blurry, wavering figure, staggered closer to see better.

“Haemin!”

The scale shook.

The perfectly balanced beam began to quiver.

**Checking the scale reading now.**

“I can’t wait anymore! No! No more! Someone—someone please die in my place!! Someone die for me! I have to be rescued so I can see Haemin!”

His scream tore through the courtroom.

“Wait—if he says something like that...”

The candidate’s crimson heart on the left tray darkened into a murky black and shriveled, drying and twisting unnaturally.

“—!”

The right tray dipped downward.

**The result of the 8,312th assessment for Selected Candidate #370611... is rejection from rescue selection.**

The balance broke.

The speaker’s voice remained emotionless:

**The Scales of Malice have determined that the candidate’s heart contains psychological values that supersede ethical and moral judgment.**

**Given the circumstances, guidance is impossible. The candidate is classified as an irredeemable evil.**

**Reassessment is hereby permanently terminated.**

**A humanitarian execution is ordered.**

*Thud.*

The right tray crashed to the floor.

The beam collapsed completely.

The heart on the left tray bounced into the air from the recoil.

“ACKAAAAAAAAAA—!”

With a slick sound, blood spurted from the chest of the middle-aged man, screaming without his heart, and his body tumbled down below the bench.

“......”

*Creeeeeak—*

The scale began to move again.

The beam returned to balance as blood dripped down from beneath it.

**202■ Administration Case 370612—Selected Candidate Park Kyungju, please step forward.**

“...We’re screwed,” Assistant Manager Eun Haje muttered urgently.

“What is that supposed to be? Something precious shows up on the opposite tray? And what—if it’s precious enough that you’d commit a serious crime for it, you fail?”

Close, but probably not as simple as whether someone *could* commit a major crime.

Rather, whether there’s something they prioritize so twistedly that they push aside ethics, convictions, and conscience.

“This is different from what Goral said—”

“Wait. The two of you... Did you hear this from Goral—Agent Mint?”

“Yes.”

Tension flickered over Agent Bronze’s face.

“You heard rumors that they use the Scales of Malice here to select candidates worth rescuing...”

But it seemed no one knew that failure resulted in immediate execution.

Agent Bronze clenched his teeth and muttered, “...Do not worry. The fact that all these people are undergoing reassessment means that the criteria—while strict—should not be excessively distorted.”

【 Oh? And what do you think of that statement, friend? 】

I simply kept my mouth shut.

Meanwhile—

**202■ Administration Case 370613—Selected Candidate Sun Yuseok, please step forward.**

The person ahead of me stepped onto the bench...

*Thud.*

Once again, a heart was torn out, and the body tumbled beneath the bench.

“......”

Now, no one remained in front of me.

And at last, I could see the courtroom properly...

The floor was glass.

“......!”

Beneath it, corpses were piled high.

A mountain of bodies. Hearts and corpses, discarded like trash, projected grotesquely beneath the floor.

From the top, I caught a glimpse of the middle-aged man’s light padded jacket that had just fallen from the bench...

A dizzying, spine-chilling fear surged through me.

And then—

**202■ Administration Case 370614—Selected Candidate Kim Soleum, please step forward.**

It was my turn.

*Haah.*

I took a deep breath and, stiffly, moved my foot forward—

“Wait!”

“...!”

Someone leapt in front of me.

The person who had been standing behind me.

*Agent Choi!*

“May I go first?”

Was he insane?

“Agent... you’re... crazy...”

“No, this person is too shaken... It looks like they’re proceeding in a simplified manner. Since you’re an agent, don’t you have the capacity to accommodate this? Look—this person is really—”

Agent Choi grabbed me firmly, pressing tightly on the vein in my forearm.

My hands trembled reflexively.

“This... yeah, their state isn’t good.”

“...!”

The other two companions pulled me close, almost supporting me.

*What the—*

“Grapes, you know if you start shouting and causing a scene here, it’ll only be more dangerous, right?”

Agent Choi whispered.

“Watch what happens to me first, then think and act. You’re not in your right mind right now. Got it?”

“......”

Who went first didn’t really matter.

But *not in my right mind*—what was that supposed to mean?

“Please, Judge—no, Selection Officer!”

After a piercing silence and stares...

**Confirmed.**

**202■ Administration Case 370615—Selected Candidate ■■■, please step forward.**

Hearing the speaker, Agent Choi stepped forward with a small smile.

I clenched my teeth to stop myself from swearing.

The only reason I could stay calm was that I was confident Choi wouldn’t fail this assessment.

**We will now conduct the assessment regarding the candidate’s suitability for rescue.**

**Submit the evidence.**

“...Of course.”

Choi lifted the knife connected to the scale and pressed it against his chest.

Through the slit of pierced flesh, the organ inside gleamed... silver.

“...!”

Right. This is what the Silver Heart originated from...

The symbol of the candidate being extracted for the Scales of Malice can vary according to the person’s life history.

Most agents likely take a similar form, which is probably what inspired the creation of the Silver Heart.

**Place it on the scale.**

Choi did as instructed.

And on the opposite side appeared...

*Flap.*

The curtain swayed. Sunlight spilled in through a warm window.

A worn sofa, a table.

A familiar scene... the Hyunmu 1 Team’s waiting room.

**Checking the scale reading.**

The beam tilted.

But in a different direction than previously observed.

Choi’s heart sank heavily downward...

*Thunk.*

It finally touched the floor.

“...!”

The scale vibrated.

**202■ Administration Case 370615—Selected Candidate ■■■ assessment result** ... **Rescue selection: PASSED.**

“...!”

**The requested candidate has been selected by the Scales of Malice and is eligible for rescue. An agent will now be dispatched to respond to this request.**

“Oh.”

The scale trembled as if it had finally received a true verdict. Somehow, the courtroom seemed slightly brighter than before, and the atmosphere felt breathable.

A sense of hope that comes with passing.

【 Hmm, a candidate has passed! Let’s see if they jumped ahead to steal Mr. Roe Deer’s glory. 】

...No. That wouldn’t happen.

【 Hm? 】

Agent Choi picked up the heart from the scale, managing a faint smile.

“Wait, about that—”

At that moment, the static-spewing speaker suddenly blurted out a flat, rapid voice:

“ **RESCUE IMPOSSIBLE.”**

“...What?”

**RESCUE IMPOSSIBLE RESCUE IMPOSSIBLE RESCUE IMPOSSIBLE...** (repeated frenetically)

**Currently, there are no additional rescue slots available at the ■■■■ branch of Sekwang Special City. Due to a lack of capacity, the assessment result is nullified.**

The scale lowered and returned to its original balanced position. A huge ash-colored scale, dusted with grime and debris.

**Re-assessment. The evaluation result for 202■ Administration Case 370615 is: PENDING. Please follow the procedure again for the next assessment.**

“Wait a minute.”

“......”

Then...

Agent Choi’s eyes widened as if he’d realized something while looking at the line.

“Agent.”

I finally found my frozen voice.

“This is a test that can never be passed.”

“...!”

“...You heard it earlier.”

The voice given to the middle-aged person who was previously on trial:

**We will now conduct the 8,312th reassessment regarding the candidate’s rescue suitability.**

“That person... has undergone reassessment 8,312 times.”

“...!”

“They continue to be postponed under the premise that ‘rescue is currently impossible’ and that ‘there are no slots for a rescuer...’ ”

And so, the trials continue endlessly.

Caught in this infinite cycle of the Scales of Malice.

Tormented with the hope that someday a ‘slot for a rescuer’ might appear.

“...And if the corruption deepens, or if they cannot endure, so that their sense of right and wrong becomes blurred...”

When they show ‘unethical obsession’ with what they love most, according to the standards of the Scales of Malice—

“They are executed.”

“......”

“They can never escape here... until they are sentenced to death.”

I looked around at the people in line.

“These people have been undergoing trials endlessly since the day Sekwang Special City became like this.”

But—

“Still, from now on, it will be fine.”

“What?”

“I’ll find a way to solve it.”

I’ve done it before.

This time, too, I’ve already thought of a way to clear this tribunal.

And in fact, any method would do, wouldn’t it?

Desperately, do everything possible, find gaps, cling to them.

Even if it’s terrifying like death—it’s all part of the process.

Use corruption, die if necessary—whatever the result, that’s all that matters.

“Don’t worry.”

**202■ Administration Case 370614—Selected Candidate Kim Soleum, please step forward.**

“Wait...”

I stepped forward.

This time, before anyone could grab me.

**We will now assess the candidate’s suitability for rescue. Candidate, submit your evidence.**

I cut open my chest and pulled out my heart...

Amazingly, silver light seeped out.

*Still counted as an agent, huh?*

Good. If I were in the state of 130666 or some other corrupted condition, I probably wouldn’t have passed this assessment.

But I hadn’t intentionally killed or harmed anyone for pleasure... so it was okay.

*Good. Let it be postponed.*

Then it would make things easier.

I placed the heart on the scale.

**Confirmed.**

The scale trembled, and on the opposite tray, something should appear...

...but nothing appeared.

... *Huh?*

There was no shape. No person, no scene.

*Does that mean I have nothing I love?*

... *That’s beyond awkward.*

I instinctively bent down to look at the opposite tray.

The scale was shaking.

There seemed to be something inside. Something like a stain. A black circle...

*Click.*

**Checking the scale reading.**

The beam collapsed.

**202■ Administration Case 370614—Selected Candidate assessment result:

Rescue selection: FAILED.**

The scale plummeted to the floor.
