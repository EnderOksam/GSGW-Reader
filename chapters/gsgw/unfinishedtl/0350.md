# Chapter 349

*I took a steadying breath.*

*Meeting Director Ho itself wasn’t difficult, method-wise.*

*I belonged to Director Ho’s project team, and since I often worked independently, the director had granted me free access to his domain.*

*Unlike the agents who had smuggled out sleeping members of the project team, that “authorization” was still active for me.*

*In a tangible form, at that.*

Fox Counseling Office

*“…….”*

*I took out the warmly colored nameplate from within my tattoo.*

*“…Are you going in?”*

*“Yes.”*

*The two agents decided to stay behind instead of accompanying me, to avoid provoking Ho Yuwon.*

*…The persuasion process had been a bit pressuring, though.*

*【 He really was a persistent one. Whew! 】*

*As I walked the rough path out of Agent Choi’s hideout, I recalled Braun showering me with joking complaints, and felt a faint smile touch my lips.*

*My tension eased a little.*

*Though thinking about what lay ahead still made my hands stiff.*

*“…….”*

*A rural road in Gangwon Province.*

*I hung the nameplate on the front door of one of the scattered empty houses and pulled on the old door.*

Creeeak.

*Sunlight poured in.*

*A calm, soft, carefully prepared interior revealed itself.*

*The Fox Counseling Office.*

*A warm counseling waiting room, neatly arranged to receive clients.*

*The first time I stepped into this place, I’d suspected even this comforting atmosphere to be part of some urban-legend gimmick.*

*Even afterward, every time I entered, the coziness put me on guard first.*

*But now, it didn’t.*

*That was…*

*Perhaps because I’d come to truly feel how sincerely this being devoted itself to counseling, to its clients.*

*Even if only through a single day—May 4th—repeated endlessly.*

*“…There’s no presence. Shall we go in?”*

*“Yes.”*

*I carefully stepped inside.*

*“Ho Yuwon.”*

*There was no reply.*

*But I kept moving deeper in.*

*Past the table prepared with tea and snacks, toward the inner door.*

Long-Term Inpatient Room

A sign on the door read: ‘Inpatient rest area.’

*It was the very place where we’d entered the Segwang Special City subway.*

*I cautiously grasped the doorknob.*

*At that moment—*

*“Roe deer…?”*

*…!*

*“Chief Badger.”*

*I turned around to see a large man with a warm impression standing there.*

*Someone I hadn’t seen in a very long time…*

*Chief Park Minseong.*

*That familiar face trembled as he looked at me, then hurried over.*

*“You’re awake—safe! Thank goodness…!”*

*Had he recovered somewhat from the contamination? Seeing Chief Minseong intact, able to talk normally, filled me with relief.*

*…In this moment, the 293-day gap didn’t feel entirely bad.*

*“This place is… ah, Miss Goral. You came together?”*

*“Yes… hello, Chief.”*

*Go Yeongeun greeted him softly.*

*But when I glanced at her face, I caught a fleetingly strange expression.*

*…Why?*

*A vague sense of unease rose, but Yeongeun soon composed herself, wearing only a polite smile.*

*Instead, the joy faded from Chief Park Minseong’s face, replaced by tension.*

*“This place… no, both of you should leave first. While Director Ho is gone—”*

*“It’s fine. I have something to say to him.”*

*“That—that’s really not a good idea either… If you just woke up, you might not have heard, but… Director Ho’s condition seems to be getting worse.”*

*Chief Minseong lowered his voice.*

*“Nearly half the people who went in for counseling have gone missing. Not long ago… someone high up from HR came, got transferred to a secure zone, and never came back out.”*

*“…….”*

*“Real conversation has become harder and harder, and now… he barely feels human.”*

*“Even so, I have to talk to him.”*

*Chief Minseong’s face stiffened—then strangely melted into an overly gentle expression.*

*“Why are you being so stubborn… You should listen to the counselor’s words—no, no…!”*

*…!*

*Covering his face and gasping for breath, Chief Minseong suddenly lifted his head and spoke.*

*In a slightly dazed tone.*

*“I’ll… go take a look.”*

*“…Pardon?”*

*“The Segwang City subway.”*

*…?!*

*“The project, yeah—I do exploration, too. The station… it’s my off-duty time, so I was just here for a bit, but it feels like someone woke up inside. Yeah? Ah—here they come.”*

*The door to the long-term inpatient room opened.*

*“…!”*

*What emerged was…*

*A gaunt, faded-looking man with shaggy hair.*

*Still wearing an old security jacket.*

*J3.*

*“…Mr. J.”*

*“I’m going.”*

*Chief Park Minseong fled almost as if escaping, entering the inpatient room through the door J3 had come out of, then shut it.*

Thud.

*…….*

*The security captain stared blankly at the scene, then turned toward me and spoke.*

*“Um… want some tea?”*

*“…Pardon?”*

*He fumbled awkwardly to set out the snacks on the desk, and I hurriedly stopped him.*

*“It’s fine. I think Mr. J should have some—looks like you just finished work.”*

*“Mm….”*

*He glanced at me a few times, then quietly offered a few snacks in my direction.*

So that was his way of showing happiness…

*Strangely enough, he accepted my awakening as something natural.*

*And perhaps because of that, the words slipped out.*

*“What Chief Badger did just now…”*

*“Ah.”*

*The security captain replied slowly.*

*“Badger… um… keeps wanting to stay in the subway, you see…?”*

*……!*

*“I knew it…”*

*I heard Go Yeongeun mutter.*

*I turned and met her eyes.*

*“I actually secretly observed Director Ho’s project team inside the subway once. And back then… everyone was trying to leave, but he alone wanted to stay.”*

*“…….”*

*Suddenly, I remembered exploring the Hanbit Library with Chief Park Minseong.*

—Um, should I just stay here?

*…That anxious voice.*

*Now I understood why contaminated people regained relative clarity in that subway.*

*Those who had been sane on May 4th—the day in question—may have regained their former mental framework by being affected by the distortions happening on the surface.*

*Perhaps it was even strengthened by the influence of a “world without urban legends.”*

*What mattered was that Chief Park Minseong had become fixated on that effect.*

*“Mm… in the subway… you’re not swayed by contamination… It’s quiet, so it’s nice….”*

*“Even so… leaving him like that…”*

*“Well, in the end, it’s something he has to handle himself…?”*

*“…….”*

*“He has to regulate it himself. Accept it….”*

*In that calm, almost cold reflection, I faintly saw his former self.*

*Though he’d been contaminated long before that May 4th and remained the same even in the subway, the image of the wolf squad leader I saw at Hanbit Library flickered briefly.*

*“…You’ve kept working on the project.”*

*“Mm….”*

*“Are your work and condition alright?”*

*The security captain adjusted his cap.*

*“Well… I mean… it can’t really get any worse….”*

*“…….”*

*Somehow, it felt bitter.*

*Still, the fact that he hadn’t been under Director Cheong was enough to make me sigh in relief.*

*Hiding my bitterness, I asked what mattered.*

*“I heard that the project team is meeting researchers in the subway and… protecting them. Do you know who that is?”*

*“That’s me….”*

*…?!*

*“Y-you’re the one protecting them, Mr. J?”*

*“Protection is more like… discovery and capture…?”*

*“…….”*

*“I monitored them….”*

*So that’s how it turned out.*

Honestly… that’s a relief.

*At least the security captain wouldn’t intentionally harm them or seek revenge.*

*If anything, Kwak Jekang probably picked a fight—but with that temperament, he likely ignored it, so it was fine.*

*Either way, my tension eased.*

*“Are they doing well?”*

*“In their own way…? But I’m on shift change now… Dolphin—um, that person’s probably watching them….”*

*So Seonghae was still involved….*

*I was about to ask about the daydream potion apparatus the researchers had been repairing—*

*But the security captain was staring at me.*

*“Is this why you told me to come out…?”*

*“…Excuse me?”*

*“You made me come out… Guess someone noticed you woke up….”*

*Who…?*

*Before I could answer myself, a sound echoed.*

Click.

*“…!”*

*I turned my head reflexively.*

*…Inside—*

*The counseling room door had opened automatically.*

Next client, please come in.

*“…….”*

Client Kim Soleum, please come in.

*“Roe Deer—”*

*Go Yeongeun grabbed my back, sweating, as if to stop my impulsive move.*

*I gently removed her hand.*

*“…I’ll go have a ‘session.’ ”*

*“…….”*

*“Mr. J. Miss Goral’s safety—”*

*The security captain’s yellow pupils looked down at Go Yeongeun.*

*“It’s fine… I’m here….”*

*I slowly nodded.*

*Then, carefully—but without hesitation—I stepped forward.*

*Into the counseling room.*

*…….*

*…….*

*A warm space.*

*A counseling room expertly designed for psychological effect.*

*But beyond the large window—where the counselor once appeared in the exact form of the client—there was only pitch-black darkness.*

*The modest, beautiful green backyard was gone.*

*A dark night.*

*“…….”*

*I walked forward and sat on the sofa by the window.*

*Footsteps approached behind me, and someone placed both hands on my shoulders and spoke.*

*“Soleum-nim.”*

*…….*

*“You’re awake. Very good. Now, it’s time to tell me what you saw and heard. Before that, raise your hand—”*

*“Hello, sir.”*

*The other party stopped.*

*“Have you been well? Waiting nearly 300 days without any news must have been very difficult.”*

*“…….”*

*The hands left my shoulders.*

*“What is your intention here?”*

*“I heard you’ve been protecting the citizens of Segwang Special City you recently counseled. When you met them directly—seeing clients who had forgotten everything—you must have felt both relief and anger.”*

*“…….”*

*“Did the Administration Bureau tell you to say that?”*

*“No.”*

*I spoke slowly.*

*“I haven’t met anyone from the Disaster Management Bureau on my way here. Aside from former project members.”*

*“Ah, those agents who smuggled out the sleeping project members….”*

*“But something felt strange.”*

*“What do you mean?”*

*“This Fox Counseling Office isn’t just a space—it’s you yourself, isn’t it?”*

*The presence froze.*

*“You’re maintaining this place under a powerful obsession with preserving your identity as a counselor.”*

*“That—”*

*“And yet, smuggling something out of here would be difficult without your tacit permission, wouldn’t it, sir?”*

*“……!”*

*“At that moment, you must have been startled by your own impulse to overlook the agent. …But unable to intervene, you let them go in that instant, didn’t you?”*

*“I’m not sure.”*

*“Weren’t you confused about where that impulse came from? Why you feel such pity for your clients, and why—when regular counseling deepens that feeling—you want to protect them by hiding them from the outside world… Have you ever thought about that?”*

*“Right now.”*

*A low, chilling voice spoke.*

*“Are you trying to counsel me instead?”*

*“Perhaps.”*

*I smiled with difficulty.*

*“I’m imitating you, sir.”*

*“…What?”*

*“Yes. I’m imitating the you I experienced—a counselor who stayed in one region for a very long time, grew attached to its people, and tried to protect those clients.”*

*“What you’re describing is—”*

*“Do you remember when you first counseled me?”*

*I looked at the window.*

*“I entered this room contaminated by the Hungry Hangman’s darkness, carrying an instruction manual. You… counseled me from outside the window, wearing my form.”*

*And then—*

*“When the counseling ended, I saw a small fox run into the garden.”*

*“…….”*

*“Was that also you?”*

*“Darkness has many facets. But I don’t see what this conversation is meant to accomplish. Are you trying to buy time?”*

*“No. There’s something I want to tell you.”*

—Soleum-nim.

*Why does Ho Yuwon use extreme honorifics only with me?*

*That was…*

*As the puzzle clicked into place, a sudden surge of emotion rose in my chest, and I spoke.*

*“Sir… weren’t you also in the form of a small fox when you came to your senses outside Segwang Special City?”*

*“…….”*

*“And when you came out, didn’t it feel like you came out with someone?”*

*“You—”*

*“You pushed someone larger than yourself and came out—but when you regained awareness, you weren’t alone, were you?”*

*“…….”*

*“That’s only natural.”*

*Because—*

*“We each went to our own timelines.”*

*“…….”*

*“You regained consciousness years ago, on the afternoon of May 4th—the day of the disaster. I woke up yesterday.”*

*I turned my head.*

*And met the eyes of Ho Yuwon, whose expression had vanished.*

—I’ll push you out… I’ll attach the small fox, too, so it can slip out through the counseling room’s back door.

*The counseling teacher.*

*The small fragment he’d sent with me to see me off—the being I met in Segwang Special City—stood before me.*

*“Thank you, sir.”*

*I held out my hand.*

*“Thanks to you… I escaped safely.”*

*The Fox Counseling Office’s counselor looked at my hand with trembling eyes.*

*Then slowly—*

He shook it.
