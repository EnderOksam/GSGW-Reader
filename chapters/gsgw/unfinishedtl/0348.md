# Chapter 347

*I swallowed my breath.*

*Agent Choi didn’t look well.*

*His smiling face clearly showed hope and joy at having a team member return…

and yet beneath it, deep exhaustion was unmistakable.*

*“How’s your body?”*

*“…….”*

*“When you get up after lying down for a long time, your muscles are totally gone, so it’s really tough, right? Hah, this senior’s been there too. Take it easy. Really, take it easy.”*

*Following his bantering tone, I carefully pushed myself upright.*

*My arms and legs trembled, but considering I’d been asleep for 293 days, it wasn’t that bad. Maybe I… no, let’s stop thinking about that.*

*What mattered was that, for now, I had escaped both Segwang Special City and Director Cheong’s grasp.*

*Probably thanks to this person.*

*I turned my head to thank him while hiding my still-shaking hands—and froze when I saw the surroundings.*

*“…Where exactly is this?”*

*“A place with good air and good water.”*

*It looked like the inside of a natural cave.*

*A small sheltered space nestled among jagged, carved-out rocks.*

*In one part of the rock wall, fabrics in the five traditional colors were draped like a ritual site, concealing something…*

*Beside me lay the sleeping body of Agent Bronze.*

*“…….”*

*I looked between the carefully prepared bedding, Agent Bronze’s body, and my own spot—then turned back to Agent Choi.*

*“…We definitely entered Segwang Special City through the Fox Counseling Center.”*

*“I diverted you.”*

*“…!!”*

*“That place was going to—no, never mind.”*

*Agent Choi suddenly brightened again.*

*“Anyway, that timing just now was perfect, right? This is what the top ace of Hyeonmu Team 1 can do. Grape, see? I told you I’d save you.”*

*“…….”*

*A bad feeling crept over me.*

*I glanced to the side.*

*Agent Choi was casually trying to cover it, but the ritual bell was still there.*

*And the bloodstains that had splashed from it.*

*“…Why is there blood on the bell?”*

*“It’s nothing.”*

*“Did you ask the Tiger General of Daecheong Peak on Mount Seorak to save me?”*

*“Something like that.”*

*“How?”*

*“A good method.”*

*“…Then why is there blood on the bell?”*

*“…….”*

*“You said the Tiger General is… vegan.”*

*“…….”*

*“Agent.”*

*I asked in a trembling voice.*

*“Who did you offer what to?”*

*“…….”*

*Agent Choi stared at me for a moment—*

*Then burst out laughing.*

*“What are you talking about! Hey, look.”*

*He rolled up his sleeves, showing arms full of scars. He lifted his pant legs, shifted his body.*

*…He looked fine.*

*“I’m perfectly okay, see?”*

*Then he shrugged.*

*“Don’t say things that sound so misleading~ Someone might think a stylish agent like me, who works day and night for citizens, is some kind of gambling addict betting his body at a casino.”*

*“…….”*

*Then what did he—*

*“Get some rest for now. Eat… then we’ll talk.”*

*“…….”*

*“Hm?”*

*I saw something desperate flicker across Agent Choi’s smiling face.*

*I thought of Agent Bronze, still asleep.*

*And wondered how this man had lived alone for the past 293 days.*

*“…….”*

*In the end, I slowly nodded.*

*Agent Choi broke into a wide smile.*

*“Alright, eat plenty.”*

*The food laid out was egg porridge with side dishes.*

*The side dishes looked like frozen or store-bought food, but the egg porridge tasted freshly made, as if he’d cooked it himself somewhere…*

*【 For such limited means, this is quite the effort. 】*

*I ate as diligently as I could. Agent Choi’s face relaxed a little, becoming brighter and calmer.*

*So I was able to ask.*

*“Agent. While I was asleep… what happened?”*

*“…….”*

*Agent Choi opened his mouth.*

*“There’s one piece of good news and one piece of bad news. Which do you want to hear first?”*

*“…Th—”*

*“No, never mind. I’ll just tell you.”*

*He waved a hand and looked at me.*

*A calm warmth settled in his eyes.*

*“Grape. Do you remember the train shelter?”*

*“Of course… I remember.”*

*That’s where I fell.*

*As I fell—and even while going through something awful—I remembered the train slicing through the air, heading beyond the city.*

Maybe it was better that it was empty space.

*Because the train was running through the ‘sky’ rather than the ground, it may have safely continued using supernatural phenomena as fuel instead of crashing or stopping.*

*Just like how the subway remained relatively intact because it was separated from the surface.*

But the fuel… both agents disappeared from the train.

*Agent Bronze and me.*

*My blood ran cold as I jerked my head up.*

*…No way.*

*The people on that train—Yeongeun—*

*“Grape.”*

*“…….”*

*“The train made it outside. And…”*

*“The people inside all survived.”*

*I stared at him as if I’d been punched.*

*“You saved them.”*

*“…Really?”*

*“Really.”*

*“All of them… got out safely?”*

*Agent Choi nodded.*

*“Yeah.”*

*“…!”*

*“Congratulations. Agent Grape of Hyeonmu Team 1. You successfully rescued 104 citizens from an extinction-class supernatural disaster.”*

*Ah.*

*A surge of emotion I couldn’t put into words slammed into my chest for the first time in ages.*

*A sense of achievement.*

…Thank goodness.

*I was so relieved.*

*【 Congratulations, my friend! 】*

*Deep relief made it hard to breathe.*

*How did it happen? How did they make the train run safely with an engine that used agents as fuel…*

*“Was there someone in the engine room?”*

*“Oh, that’s a pretty specific question.”*

*But Agent Choi answered easily.*

*“According to the report, there were two people. A middle-aged man with bruises all over his body.”*

*…The engineer.*

*“And beside him, a young woman watching over the controls.”*

*……No way.*

*“Agent Mint.”*

*Ah.*

*Ah…!*

It was Ms. Go Yeongeun.

*If I could be recognized as an agent, then she could, too. Right…!*

*Since there wasn’t much distance left before exiting the city, it seemed she had safely stayed in the engine room.*

*“Is Ms. Go Yeongeun healthy?”*

*“She was hospitalized briefly, but she recovered and was discharged.”*

*“…….”*

*Thank goodness.*

Yeongeun made it out of Segwang Special City…

*It didn’t feel real. I wanted to see her in person.*

*And even if the conditions aligned like this, I still needed to understand how the train escaped the blockade.*

*Then realization struck like lightning.*

Because it was the subway…!

*The subway passengers weren’t offerings. They weren’t included in May 4th!*

*They were exceptions to the components of the blockade!*

So that’s why they could get out.

*Everything snapped into place in my head, and that same thrilling relief surged again. Then—*

*“The rescued citizens were well taken care of by the Bureau after Agent Mint’s report.”*

*“…….”*

*“Grape?”*

*“Did you… confirm that the citizens were safe afterward? I mean… the Bureau returned them to society without any other… issues?”*

*“Huh? Yeah. After all, when you’re rescued from a supernatural disaster, you just get basic memory erasure and return. Even if you escaped a cognitive blockade, the process doesn’t really change.”*

*…Phew.*

*“Contamination checks, making sure nothing contagious remained. Identity restoration—everyone was released.”*

*“…I see.”*

*Agent Choi answered without hesitation. He didn’t seem to notice what I was worried about.*

*That made it unsettling.*

*…What if the ‘someone’ who planned the blockade ritual had interfered somehow?*

*But Agent Choi’s tone turned slightly playful.*

*“Oh, there was one thing nobody expected that sped up the process.”*

*“Something nobody expected…?”*

*He looked at me.*

*Or rather, at the well-behaved stuffed animal placed neatly beside my bedding.*

*“Hmm~ The rescued citizens showed cult-like devotion to a pink rabbit plushie.”*

*Ah.*

*“Further tests showed it was a supernatural phenomenon, so that became the conclusion.”*

*He lightly slapped his knee.*

*“ ‘The citizens were contaminated by a cult-related supernatural phenomenon involving a stuffed doll, and are believed to have exited the blockade indirectly by entering that phenomenon!’ ”*

*“…….”*

*“That’s how it was classified. Treated as a special case.”*

*Good grief.*

*【 Haha, for viewers who trust and wait for this Braun, surprises like gifts always follow! 】*

*“Well, their memories related to supernatural phenomena were erased before returning to society, but apparently their fondness for rabbit dolls remained.”*

*Ahhh…*

*I didn’t know whether to laugh or cry.*

*Still, I was so insanely relieved that I just nodded.*

*But a bitter smile crossed Agent Choi’s face.*

*“The problem is… some of those citizens went missing again shortly after returning.”*

*“…What?”*

*“To a certain counseling center.”*

*……!*

*“Yeah. The one you call Director Ho. He heard that someone made it out of the Special City.”*

*Ah.*

*“After that… those citizens never returned to society. And that Director Ho project started making increasingly extreme and bizarre choices… you could put it that way.”*

*“…….”*

*Agent Choi’s blue eyes sank calmly.*

*“So I secretly pulled you out. I knew what he’d do if he got his hands on you again.”*

*His voice trembled for a moment, then steadied.*

*“I wanted to pull out your other sleeping teammates too, but they’d completely separated the rooms.”*

*“…….”*

*“…Grape. I’m sorry.”*

*“No. Not at all…”*

*How could I possibly ask him why he made that choice?*

*And he probably already knew I wouldn’t.*

*“Right. Better not see that dangerous director again, huh? If you ever have to go underground again, just use a well.”*

*“…….”*

*I couldn’t give an affirmative answer.*

*I knew too much now.*

*Even though he must have noticed, Agent Choi deliberately brightened his tone again.*

“Oh, and there’s that *, too. Agent Mint seems to be returning to duty.”*

*“…! Really?”*

*“Yeah.”*

*A soft, low voice joined in from behind.*

*“And you’ll see her soon. You can hear the detailed explanation about the train from her.”*

*…!*

*I sprang up and turned around.*

*My head spun for a moment, but more importantly, a familiar face appeared from beyond the rocks.*

*“Agent Haegum!”*

*“Agent Grape.”*

*Haegum’s face lit up with joy when she saw me—then twisted when she saw Agent Choi grinning beside me.*

*“You—!”*

*“Hahaha, hey, noona, it worked out! Choi pulled off another one!”*

*Haegum strode forward as if to punch him, then sighed and lowered her hand.*

*“…I got the message and came. Agent Grape, I’m glad you woke up safely.”*

*“Thank you.”*

*Watching them, a question suddenly occurred to me.*

*“Are you both… on leave?”*

*No—more importantly—*

Isn’t Agent Choi the only one left in Hyeonmu Team 1…?

*Don’t tell me he operated alone?*

*Sensing my gaze, Agent Choi smiled and looked away slightly.*

*“Hyeonmu Team 1 is… officially all on leave right now.”*

*“…!”*

*“I’ve never taken leave that wasn’t medical before. But honestly, it was about time to rest. Hey, what’s that look?”*

*He smiled and shook his head.*

*“It’s fine. The agents who were previously on leave returned as Hyeonmu Team 5. They took over our schedule completely.”*

*Listening to him joke about how “four sounds unlucky so they deliberately picked five,” my heart felt heavy.*

*Only then did I realize this place wasn’t just somewhere to store us while we slept—it was a base where Agent Choi had been living for months.*

Did he take leave just to keep searching for a way to wake me and Agent Bronze?

*Otherwise, there was no way he could’ve pulled me out of Director Cheong’s grasp at such perfect timing.*

*With that thought, my resolve hardened.*

*I took a deep breath.*

*“Aren’t you going to ask what I went through?”*

*“…Are you ready to talk about it?”*

*I think I was.*

*…About the truth of what I experienced in Segwang Special City.*

*I was about to speak when I realized both agents were unusually tense.*

*And then I noticed another sense of wrongness.*

*I’d felt it since the moment I woke up, but hadn’t dared bring it to the surface.*

Why aren’t they asking about the others…?

*They hadn’t asked why the others hadn’t woken up.*

*Even knowing I’d speak up if something was urgent, waiting outside for nearly a year should’ve been agonizing.*

They should be impatient.

*At the very least, they should have urgently asked whether Agent Bronze was okay, alive, safe—if they’d even seen him.*

*Instead, they were avoiding the topic.*

*…As if trying to protect me just a little longer.*

*“…….”*

*Protect me from what?*

—There’s one good piece of news and one bad piece of news.

*The rescue of the train survivors was clearly the good news.*

*Then…*

*“Agent.”*

*“…….”*

*“What exactly was the bad news?”*

*“…….”*

*“Agent?”*

*Pain flickered across Agent Choi’s face, then was swiftly suppressed, replaced by a calm, professional expression.*

*“Calm down.”*

*Agent Haegum spoke.*

*“Agent Grape. Good and bad always come as two sides of the same coin.”*

*“…What do you mean?”*

*She looked straight into my eyes.*

*“Don’t be too shocked. Listen slowly.”*

*…….*

*“The Special City’s blockade is breaking.”*

*Information poured into my blank mind.*

*“It seems the train became the trigger. The moment over a hundred people escaped from inside the city, something in the conditions of the blockade ritual… went wrong. Cracks have formed.”*

*Wait.*

*“They’re keeping it quiet inside, but that damn Choi guy pulled some dangerous—ugh, anyway, he dug up the info.”*

*She gestured toward the ritual site hidden behind the five-colored cloth.*

*“He’s been watching the blockade from here. How it’s twisted, and when it’ll lose effect.”*

*…….*

*“How much time is left?”*

*“300 days.”*

*Damn.*

*Do we have to find a solution within that time? My mouth went dry at the sudden time limit.*

*Then I realized she was still talking.*

*“The collapse began with a 300-day grace period starting the day the train escaped…”*

*Wait—wait.*

*“Today marks day 293.”*

*…….*

*…….*

*“Then the days remaining are…”*

*“Seven.”*

*I stopped breathing.*

*“In one week, the Special City’s blockade ritual will collapse.”*

*I stared at her blankly.*

“Then an extinction-class disaster will be unleashed upon this land.”
