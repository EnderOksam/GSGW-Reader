# Chapter 368

Riiip.

Amid the rooftop filled with the clash of small gongs, large gongs, and bells, one more alien tearing sound rang out.

The sound of me ripping paper.

“What foolish—”

Director Cheong stopped mid-sentence.

Dozens of eyes simultaneously fixed on the torn paper in my hand.

It would be familiar.

Because it had read:

Contract

The subject enters into the following agreement:

The subject (hereafter “Eul”) transfers exclusive and complete ownership of the __Core to Cheong Dalrae (hereafter “Gap”) and permanently forfeits all rights to use the __Core.

Gap assigns Eul a new role, and Eul shall comply with the employment regulations of that role and faithfully carry out assigned duties.

(Gap): Cheong Dalrae

(Eul): Yellow Dragon

Clauses bordering on tyranny, bearing no resemblance to legitimate legal form.

It was the standard contract Director Cheong used to seize a dragon’s core.

And the designated subject was already specified.

“Yellow Dragon.”

The core of the Yellow Dragon, which held the authority of contracts.

This contract was the vault of all contracts Director Cheong used—the source of her absolute authority.

And as if proving that even this contract derived from the Yellow Dragon’s authority, a faint circular pattern shimmered across it.

Just like my contract had.

Thus came the syllogism.

The logic held.

And at this very instant, Director Cheong’s eyes in the sky were watching.

……

……

A roar of fury crashed down upon the rooftop.

Within transparent crystalline lenses, the pupils bulged and writhed like inflamed growths, thrashing wildly.

The dragon cores that had lost the foundation of their authority.

Return! Return to me, you ■ with nowhere ■ to go! Return and guard ■ your place!

A sound no human could withstand reverberated.

The ruined Azure Dragon wrapped itself around the rooftop people, trying to shield them.

It had not died.

I felt Agent Choi, blood streaming from his face, gripping me, trying desperately to return to the rooftop.

But my lower body was already gone. My organs and chest had collapsed.

And what he needed to do now was not this.

With the arm that still retained the shape of bone, I struck the pole he was holding.

Jingle.

“……”

For a split second, Agent Choi hesitated, then lifted his head.

At Gwangcheoli.

And at the tool in his hand that must complete the ritual.

“……”

Refusing to look at the massive eye from which dozens of pupils bulged in all directions, he barely stepped forward—

Tap.

At the end of the pole, he shook the bell.

A droplet of water flew and touched the crystalline lens.

(An untranslatable sound)■■■■■■■■■■■■■■■■■■■■■■■■■■■■■■■■

The sky closed.

The colossal calamity departed.

The dozens of rolling pupils gradually blurred, losing resolution.

Agent Choi coughed blood and collapsed.

At that moment—

■■■■■!

The Azure Dragon opened its jaws and shot upward.

Its claws curved around the vanishing massive eye.

!!

The surface of the crystalline lens cracked slightly, and under pressure, one or two pupils burst free.

A golden something—a dragon’s core, a yeouiju—vanished into the Azure Dragon’s mouth as if drawn by gravity.

And then—

■■■…

The sky closed.

“……”

“……”

In the silence, everyone on the rooftop understood.

The ritual of “Driving out Gwangcheoli” had succeeded.

Woooo—

The Azure Dragon descended.

From its body settling lightly onto the rooftop, Agent Choi and I slid down.

Something sacred flashed.

Its streamlined body, shimmering gold and blue, coiled tightly around the rooftop, then brought its massive face close to the people.

It dropped something before me.

Thunk.

…A cracked golden orb.

Yellow Dragon’s Core Cheong Dalrae’s 33rd pupil.

Symbol of contractual authority.

Through this pupil, Cheong Dalrae stored collected cores and powers in file binders.

But now, for some reason, it had been flung outside the crystalline lens and drawn before its original owner’s body.

It flickered briefly—

Then burrowed into my crushed body.

And disappeared somewhere unknown.

“Agent Grapes.”

Agent Haegeum tried to support me, but I slipped through.

I saw the Azure Dragon’s eye looking down at my ruined form.

As if wanting to help further. As if wanting to tell me something more.

But eventually it had to lift its head.

Because the time had come.

The Azure Dragon left the rooftop.

But its decayed form was no longer the same.

Light filled its flesh and scales, restoring them.

Its silhouette grew whole.

Woooo—

Rain fell.

The Azure Dragon, having reclaimed its core, carved its sacred form through the rain.

The brilliant golden-blue current eventually vanished back into the pool covering Segwang Special City’s roads.

Without a splash.

Woooo—

The Blue Dream Water Park pool receded like ebbing tides, sucked back into fire hydrants.

Only the sound of rain remained.

“……”

This should have been a sacred, tear-inducing, moving scene.

But I felt nothing.

“Grapes…!”

I knew.

My final attempt had succeeded.

Executive Director Cheong Dalrae would no longer have the luxury of tormenting those involved in the ritual.

The authority of contracts—the pupils neatly organized into binders—would require time to fully regain control.

All labor contracts had lost their enforceability and power.

Time would be needed to restore them.

A year.

Perhaps more.

Director Cheong would surely find another method.

But until then, a reprieve would exist.

And in exchange—

Now, I no longer exist.

“We did it.”

“……”

“We did it, Kim Soleum? You—”

“Step back.”

I have no form now.

I am disappearing.

Cognitive ability remains, but refined into something increasingly inhuman.

The protrusions of personality and emotion smooth away, leaving only traces.

I do not fear the end of the self I fought to preserve.

What remains is pattern recognition. Situational analysis. A vague awareness.

Even facing something incomprehensibly great or horrific, I do not go mad or suffer.

Because there is nothing left to wound.

Or perhaps—

I have become such a thing.

“Mr. Roe Deer.”

Section Chief Lee Jaheon—“We”—look at me.

I look back.

From that alien plural entity, I read the intent to trade for the Yellow Dragon’s core.

But I know that because of the core, I remain alive in collapsed form.

I cannot trade it away to survive.

Yet the entity within Lee Jaheon does not leave.

“Can you hear us?”

Everything but my right arm has melted.

I try to nod—

But realize I have no spine to hold up my head.

“Agent Grapes!”

My head falls to the floor.

Clink.

The jade bell rings.

……

……

It has no effect.

There is no path back.

I erased it all so I could edit the wiki.

Clink.

“Stay conscious, Kim Soleum! Stay—”

Hands grasp the collapsed flesh; bone fragments, corrupted golden blood, and organ tissue slip through fingers.

Smoke rises.

“Agent Choi!”

“Wait, just—”

I push him away with smoke to prevent contamination.

From within the black smoke, a voice speaks.

“You said you wanted to go home.”

……

“Hey? You remember?”

Home.

……Ah.

I find the last fragment of will, a grain of sand of emotion left after everything drained away.

With that strength, I flick something from the pocket of the suit jacket on the ground.

A cylinder.

Dream Essence Collector.

My last hope rolled across the rooftop floor.

Bang.

Rattle—

“……”

It was empty.

We ended the supernatural disaster of Segwang Special City.

We drove out Director Cheong Dalrae.

Everything succeeded.

Perhaps an immense concentration of dream essence had been produced—

Enough to create Children’s Paradise Syrup in one go.

But that possibility—

Remained only a possibility.

Only people can collect
dream essence .

“…Soleum.”

My body fully collapsed.

A heap on the rooftop floor.

No form remains.

After countless trials and realizations, the humanity I rebuilt collapses, revealing the true nature of this body:

The recycled corpse of the Yellow Dragon.

Within it, the consciousness of one who read Dark Exploration Record  .

A resident of a world “where ghost stories do not exist.”

I am no longer human.

I feel no fear. No anger. No sorrow.

No joy or relief at the safety of those on the rooftop.

Nothing.

I cannot move.

Only a formless mass.

Therefore, Kim Soleum does not exist.

Agent Haegeum looked at the melted mass of flesh before her.

What had been Agent Grapes.

The one who charged toward Gwangcheoli had lost all form.

Only a mass remained.

Corrupted golden-red flesh and bone, pulsing with black smoke.

A strange, grotesque pool.

Nowhere within it was there any trace of the new employee of Daydream, the youngest member of Team Hyeonmu-1.

Only the suit and mask tangled beneath suggested it had once been human.

Agent Haegeum felt her breath catch—but suppressed it.

“Agent Choi.”

“……”

“From this moment, redefine Agent Grapes… as fully contaminated. A ghost-story entity.”

Her cracked voice echoed in the rain.

“Using dokkaebi fire… isolate ‘Agent Grapes’ and move to a secure location.”

“……”

“Right now—wait, there!”

Splash.

Section Chief Lee Jaheon detached a tank from the broken potion machine and began scooping Kim Soleum’s crushed flesh into it.

Agent Haegeum opened her mouth to protest, then closed it.

She understood the heart of someone from the same team.

Splash.

Once the mass was gathered into the tank, Lee Jaheon quietly asked it—

As if believing conversation was possible.

“Do you require assistance? Indicate with smoke.”

The mass only pulsed.

Still, he moved.

He searched Kim Soleum’s jacket and found Nostalgia Candy.

Then he searched the mass, located the digestive tract, and pushed the candy inside.

It melted instantly.

Nothing happened.

“……”

“……”

“…Agent Grapes.”

These veterans, who had explored countless ghost stories, understood.

It cannot recover.

This is an irreversible transformation.

All possibilities exhausted.

Roll… tap.

The empty Dream Essence Collector rolled across the rooftop in wind and rain, brushing the mass, stopping at someone’s feet.

Agent Choi picked it up.

The Dream Essence Collector Kim Soleum had cherished.

“……”

He gripped it tightly.

“You were going to gather the solution here, right? Make a wish-granting medicine. Go home.”

……

“Soleum, I’ve been thinking.”

Blood streamed from his face as he stared at the empty cylinder.

“You said, ‘This situation itself—this place—is my ghost story. My supernatural disaster.’”

Rain fell into the black smoke in the tank.

“Why did you say that?”

Lightning flashed.

“In disasters, sometimes rescue calls are tricks by ghost stories.”

“……”

“Actually, that’s more likely. Citizens rarely request rescue in their right mind.”

Water dripped from his hair.

“Sometimes it’s a trap. Or it’s already too late.”

……

“But sometimes… sometimes you really can save them.”

Head lowered.

“When they’re truly civilians—and that was the moment you had to believe them.”

……

“You have to try to tell them apart. Or even if you can’t, try to send them back when you have the chance.”

……

“Yeah. I think that’s right.”

He looked at Kim Soleum.

Tried to.

“So.”

The man in the blue jacket reached into his pocket.

And pulled out something.

Something resembling what Kim Soleum had carried.

A
Dream Essence Collector.

Collected by someone who passed through Flower Golden Resort, a city-destroying ghost story, and Driving out Gwangcheoli.
