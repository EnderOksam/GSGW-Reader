# Chapter 351

*I no longer heard people trying to stop me with things like, “You escaped Sekwang Special City’s surface after 293 days, and now you’re saying you’re going back underground?”*

*No—

I didn’t even have the room to listen.*

*“Is there something wrong with this music box?”*

*“...I’ll go for now.”*

*With that, I immediately hung the Fox Counseling Office sign back up and crossed into the counseling room, preparing to enter Sekwang Special City’s underground subway via the long-term inpatient ward.*

*“You’re going in? ...Grapes, wait!”*

*Agent Choi, who had been wary of Director Ho the whole time, ended up accompanying me—but I didn’t have the bandwidth to observe or mediate the conflict between Ho Yuwon and Agent Choi that I encountered in the counseling room.*

*All I felt was icy, blood-draining anxiety.*

What’s going on?

*Why... was the music box that caused the Day of Disaster also inside the daydream potion manufacturing machine?*

*“I’ll guide you for now, Sir Roe Deer.”*

*Even while entering through the long-term ward and going through the ‘entry’ process, my questions didn’t stop.*

*The one saving grace was that the moment I opened my eyes, I realized I had successfully entered the Sekwang Special City subway.*

*A familiar, modern platform came into view.*

*...Sekwang Station.*

—Just as your breath was about to stop and you were about to be offered up in the sacrificial rite, before being trapped in this May 4th... you would return to where you originally belonged.

*The counselor had been right.*

*Because I didn’t die on the Day of Disaster, May 4th, my intuition that I wouldn’t be dragged there was correct.*

*But that relief didn’t last long.*

*Because I hadn’t forgotten why I’d come here.*

*“Um, this way...”*

*Following the instructions I’d been given, I took the train to Twilight Station. The Security Captain guided me to the platform of the station where Delusion Home Shopping had once been.*

Creak.

*When he opened a fire hydrant in one corner—*

*An astonishingly wide space appeared.*

*“......!”*

*Originally, the inside of the hydrant had already been distorted enough for Yeongeun and me to enter together, but now it was almost the size of a living space.*

*Seven adults could easily lie down here.*

*However, one side was occupied by a massive machine...*

*The potion manufacturing machine.*

*“They say it was expanded... with an item...”*

*So this was the idea they came up with after the train shelter disappeared.*

*The passage of several months was palpable.*

*The shabby space, heavy with signs of living, was furnished with items that looked like they’d been scavenged from the subway. Judging by that, it seemed they hadn’t been able to procure many daily necessities from outside.*

*Then, the moment the two people sitting on the mattress in the corner saw me, they jumped to their feet.*

*“Sir!”*

*Two members of the research team.*

*Deputy Manager Lee Yeonhwa and Section Chief Kwak Jekang.*

*“Wow, we heard you’d returned! You came straight here. I knew you would!”*

*Both of them looked worn and haggard.*

*All the more so when compared to how impeccably neat they’d been back at the company.*

*In Kwak Jekang’s case, especially, the fact that one joint of his pinky finger was missing made him look even more pitiful.*

*But now, his eyes were shining like a glow.*

*The excitement of finally being able to savor new knowledge.*

*“Have you heard? About that music box!”*

*He rushed to the machine and immediately yanked open a drawer beside it. Deputy Manager Lee Yeonhwa ran after him in horror.*

*“Section Chief, the safety protocols—!”*

Thud.

*Before Kwak Jekang could grab the drawer, the Security Captain struck it as if to block him.*

*“Step back. No... sudden actions...”*

*“......”*

*Kwak Jekang’s gaze twitched, but soon he twisted his lips into a smile and exaggeratedly stepped back.*

*“Of course! I wouldn’t want to ruin your fun—bossing researchers around for the first time in over a decade, right?”*

*The Security Captain ignored him completely, took something out of the drawer, and came over.*

*Deputy Manager Lee Yeonhwa spoke in a low voice.*

*“Section Chief Kwak... he’s like that every time. I told him it’d be better not to provoke the project members.”*

*“...You’ve been through a lot.”*

*“We have—but compared to everyone who died, disappearing into the darkness isn’t the worst fate. Taking apart the potion machine has been kind of fun, too...”*

*It was a resigned tone, very different from her earlier taut tension.*

*But I was far too on edge to offer comfort.*

*That.*

*The silver box the Security Captain was carrying.*

*Beside him, Kwak Jekang followed, muttering.*

*“So you let us research freely all this time, and now you pull this? You’re really doing something useless!”*

*Deputy Manager Lee Yeonhwa glanced at me and spoke in a deliberately vague tone.*

*“We never told the newly arrived staff why we were fixing the machine...”*

*“...Thank you.”*

*It seemed she’d kept her promise to me.*

*Soon, the silver box arrived in front of my eyes.*

*“......”*

*“Shall I... open it...?”*

*“It’s fine.”*

*I opened the box.*

*...Inside was the exact same black music box from the photos Yeongeun had sent me.*

*The rectangular music box was pitch-black, as if silver had rusted. Intricate embossed patterns covered it, and golden letters were engraved where the top and bottom met.*

Angel’s Sigh.

*It really was—truly—that thing.*

*“...Have you tried opening this music box?”*

*“Well, the lock wouldn’t come undone!”*

*Whew.*

*“So we forced it open.”*

*...!!*

*“But it didn’t make any sound. I wanted to take it apart, but the mainspring wouldn’t turn at all. Thought maybe it needed lubricant. I was thinking up various other approaches!”*

*“......”*

*Lubricant.*

*Liquid.*

*“Did you ever let this music box come into contact with dream-state solution?”*

*“...!”*

*The researchers’ expressions changed.*

*“No, but—wait!”*

*Kwak Jekang hurried to the potion machine and began checking something.*

*“Apparently, that music box was additionally hidden here—in the storage section where the dream solution flows down and meets the apparatus to produce potions.”*

*“......”*

*“So if this machine was actually supplied with power and operated, then the music box would’ve come into contact with the dream solution.”*

*Oh no.*

*“Then you think this music box would start playing... huh?”*

*I placed the music box back into the silver case.*

*Cold sweat ran down my temples.*

It was a trap.

*The moment I installed this potion machine on the rooftop of Sekwang City Hall, another disaster would have occurred.*

*If the researchers hadn’t spent 293 days drifting within the disaster, dismantling this machine—*

*If they’d simply done standard operational repairs without discovering the music box...*

Total destruction.

*My heart went ice-cold with dizziness.*

*And that meant...*

I was led here.

*The machine had been deliberately left so I could steal it.*

*When I infiltrated Daydream, a disturbance was intentionally caused so the potion machine would go missing—making it “easier to steal the discarded machine.”*

*With the music box already installed inside it.*

*Who?*

*There was really only one answer.*

...Director Cheong.

*A chill ran down my spine.*

Wait.

*How much does she know?*

*Is she reading my psychology? How far does the power of the labor contract extend?*

*Could she even be watching right now...?*

No.

*I had to stay calm.*

The fact that she laid a trap means this attempt must not succeed for her.

*If anything, this strengthened the credibility of the method written in Lee Kangheon’s note.*

*It meant Director Cheong also believed this was the correct answer.*

Yes. That’s right.

*My mind cleared.*

*After a moment’s thought, I put the silver box—music box and all—into my inventory tattoo.*

*“Hey, that—!”*

*I heard Kwak Jekang cry out in shock behind me, but I didn’t hesitate.*

*I already knew how to placate him.*

*“Within a week, the two of you may escape this darkness.”*

*“......!”*

*“I came today because there are things we need to prepare for that attempt. ...Please teach me how to install that potion machine.”*

*And so, I learned from the two researchers how to connect the potion machine to a power source.*

With this, I can go to the rooftop and install this instead of the Dream Incubator.

*But even as I prepared thoroughly, the ominous feeling didn’t fade.*

*It lingered even after I finished learning the installation process, stored the potion machine itself in my inventory tattoo, and exited the hydrant.*

*“Ha ha! Then we’ll count the days and wai—”*

Bang.

*I stood silently, staring at the closed hydrant before Kwak Jekang could finish his sentence.*

*“So... are you heading back now...?”*

*“...Just a moment.”*

*There was still something to check.*

*I turned my head toward the stairs on the platform.*

*A familiar sign came into view.*

*[ Flower Golden Resort ]

Souvenir Pop-Up Store*

*“Mascot-nim...!”*

*When I went inside, the two “Heoun” sprang to their feet.*

*“We heard from the staff that you couldn’t come because something came up—are you finished now?”*

*“That’s a relief, Mascot-nim!”*

*They were full of joy and relief, and I wanted to chat more—but something else was urgent.*

*“Is the rent okay?”*

*The rent for this pop-up space—paid in blood.*

*Payment item: Fresh blood (human)

Security deposit: 499 L

Monthly payment: 3 L*

*Was that really—*

*“Well, we got reminder letters sometimes... so we covered it ourselves. Don’t worry...!”*

*“......What?”*

*“There are two of us, after all. If we take turns giving a little each week, it’s manageable...”*

*“......”*

*I was speechless.*

*“You’re afraid of blood...”*

*“It’s fine. We put it in the mailbox so we didn’t have to see it, and if we get hurt, the resort treats us right away.”*

*A bandage with a yellow mascot’s face was stuck to Jang Heoun’s arm.*

*“......”*

*My chest felt heavy.*

*“I’m sorry. If I’m ever absent like this again, please deduct the blood from the deposit.”*

*“Huh? Yes. Of course...!”*

*But even as I said it, I knew.*

*Would there even be a “next time”?*

If we succeed in ending a cataclysm-class disaster... what happens to this subway station?

*Once the station is freed from lockdown and disaster influence, it will lose the property of being “a space where contaminated beings can exist sanely.”*

*Then these people would...*

*......*

*Thinking of Chief Park Minseong’s case made the feeling even harder to place.*

*Was I missing something?*

*Was there something I hadn’t checked because I was anxious about the time limit?*

*The music box inside the machine had been a trap discovered only because enough time had passed. Could there be other traps elsewhere?*

*Or, even if not, perhaps taking more time would reveal other clues...*

*......*

*“Um, J-ssi.”*

*As we left the pop-up store, I asked, “Do you know Director Cheong... well?”*

*“Well, I know as much as other employees do...”*

*That much I already knew.*

*While waiting to hear back from the researchers, I’d looked into Director Cheong as much as I could externally.*

She almost never appears publicly.

*No official events. No press conferences.*

*Yet her name had been listed as a director ever since the Daydream website first launched.*

*Meaning she’d been here since long before the IPO.*

*The disaster-management agents I’d asked said similar things.*

—A director who’s been with Daydream for a long time. Almost like Daydream itself.

*That’s what it always came back to.*

*A very typical Daydream director.*

*To learn more concretely...*

...I’d need intranet access.

*Using the intranet of someone on Director Ho’s Project Team would be too obvious.*

*“Has anyone ever quit the Project Team?”*

*“Quit...?”*

*“More specifically—someone who stopped working directly under Director Ho and returned to regular company duties.”*

*“Hmm...”*

*The Security Captain seemed to think, then spoke.*

*“There is... that... Black Goat.”*

*...!*

*Baek Saheon.*

*****

*That evening.*

*The Daydream company housing.*

*“Good evening, citizen.”*

*“WAAAH!”*

*Baek Saheon slammed the front door shut and turned—only to scream when he saw me climbing in through the window.*

*Because I was wearing a mask.*

*Was it called the Chorengi mask?*

*Anyway, before he could open the door and bolt, I rushed forward and held out my hand.*

*“Are you all right? ...Please don’t be startled. It’s me.”*

*It sounded like an “It’s me, trust me” scam, but I had solid proof.*

*A paper boat.*

*“......”*

*The moment Baek Saheon saw the paper in my hand, his expression flipped.*

*“...Agent?”*

*“Yes.”*

*This angle felt easier to talk from—but honestly, the moment he lifted his eye patch to ‘confirm’ me, I planned to switch approaches.*

*I held out my hand.*

*“Nice to see you again, citizen. It’s been a while.”*

*“...So you do know.”*

*But instead of lifting his eye patch again, Baek Saheon slapped my hand away, glaring.*

*“Why were you out of contact for so long?! Were you planning to run off after stealing my item?”*

*“I was trapped in a supernatural disaster.”*

*“......”*

*“I’m sorry. Thank you again for helping me last time. I still owe you for that.”*

*I expected him to snap again.*

*But—*

*“Hey.”*

*A calm voice.*

*“Why do you keep doing that job?”*

*“......”*

*“You could quit even now. Honestly, it’s low pay and only puts your life at risk.”*

*“True.”*

*I gave a bitter smile.*

*“I think I missed the timing to quit comfortably.”*

*“......”*

*Baek Saheon pressed his lips together, sighed, and jerked his chin.*

*“Sit down.”*

*“Thank you.”*

*I sat at the dining table of the company housing where I once lived, feeling oddly nostalgic.*

*Then I asked casually, “I have something I’m curious about too.”*

*“What?”*

*“Why do you keep working at Daydream?”*

*Baek Saheon froze mid-movement.*

*“I heard most people who work here have a desperate wish. Do you... have one, too?”*

*“......”*

*“I don’t know.”*

*“There was a reason at first, but now it doesn’t matter.”*

*“......”*

*“If I keep collecting points... I guess I’ll find something I want to use a Wish Ticket on.”*

*The way he spoke felt like he was dodging his own inner truth.*

*Maybe Baek Saheon actually knew very well why he wanted a Wish Ticket...*

*Perhaps that’s why, as someone with experience, the words slipped out.*

*“Be careful with Wish Tickets.”*

*“Oh. Yeah.”*

*...His reaction was just like mine had been when Agent Bronze once told me something similar. No—wait.*

*“They’re not useless—but they can be granted in completely unexpected ways.”*

*“...What?”*

*“Daydream’s Wish Tickets... aren’t kind tools that fulfill the user’s deepest, truest desire.”*

*I said seriously.*

*“They’re closer to ‘technically granting the wish’ within the limits of what they can do—based strictly on the wording.”*

*“How do you know that?”*

*“I had the chance to hear testimony from a user. That potion grants wishes in a self-centered way, not user-center—”*

*...Wait.*

*Self-centered.*

...Isn’t that like Director Cheong?

—You should be grateful. I fulfilled your wish for you.

*In that instant, as I recalled why I’d come here, a strange feeling crept over me.*

*The music box in the potion machine.*

*Director Cheong’s manipulation.*

*The mutated form of the Wish Tickets produced from it...*

*...Something felt off.*

*“Anyway... got it.”*

*“Citizen.”*

*“What now?”*

*“Do you know Director Cheong Dallae?”*

*“...Does anyone working here not know their own division’s director? Of course I do.”*

*......*

*“Could you let me access the intranet? There’s something I want to investigate about that director.”*

*“What?”*

*“I heard you can access the intranet from the company housing PCs.”*

*“No way—”*

*“I won’t leave any records that could inconvenience you.”*

*That was why I’d come here in the first place—to gather internal information in a way Director Cheong wouldn’t suspect.*

*“I really need this information... Please.”*

*I was about to offer some kind of incentive.*

*Something Baek Saheon might find tempting.*

*But—*

*“...If you try anything weird with my account, I’m taking it back.”*

*...!*

*“Thank you.”*

*“Think of it as payment for telling me about Wish Tickets.”*

*Baek Saheon led me to his PC.*

*As I followed him, I felt strangely unsettled.*

Did this guy always know how to show small kindnesses...?

*He seemed far removed from the venomous viper of the Dark Exploration Records...*

*Even from how he’d been during onboarding.*

*...Could I have influenced him?*

*The thought stirred something in me—but I couldn’t dwell on it.*

*“Here.”*

*I quickly took control of the PC and logged into the intranet.*

*“From there, the logo at the top—”*

*Baek Saheon’s smug explanatory voice cut off.*

*Because I was already navigating the intranet’s personal page with ease.*

*I entered the internal notices tab and began reviewing entries from the oldest onward.*

*Instead of directly searching “Cheong Dallae,” I searched by the executive director position, then narrowed down related projects.*

*“......!”*

*And then I realized something.*

There’s an unusually large number of dragon-related dark phenomena.

*Dragon-Head Coast urban legend.*

*Dragon-Hunting Game urban legend.*

*Hwangnyongsa Stone Pagoda legend.*

*Han River Dragon legend...*

*All kinds of dragon-related darkness had been developed or researched under Director Cheong’s jurisdiction.*

*Of course, as head of development, that alone might not be strange.*

*She managed a wide variety of darkness beyond that as well.*

*But—*

Even after Director Ho joined... dragon-related darkness stayed under Director Cheong.

*It felt like her people were deliberately assigned there.*

*Suddenly, I recalled something an agent from the Blue Dragon Team had said in Sekwang Special City.*

—I smelled that nasty fake dragon stench here.

—A Daydream director. That cultist!

*A “fake dragon” is a failed dragon—a serpent that never became one.*

*Could it be...*

Is Director Cheong that entity?

*And yet, the agents now never spoke of that fake dragon.*

*“......”*

*It felt like I’d caught hold of a hint.*

*And then another realization followed.*

*Among those “dragon-related dark entities under Director Cheong,” there was one I could meet right away.*

*One friendly to me.*

*One I could talk to.*

The one who called me a Good Child *.*

*“......”*

*Early that morning.*

*Through my inventory tattoo, I entered the Cheerful Theme Park.*

*To meet the blue dragon mascot of Blue Dream Water Park.*

D-3 until the Day of Disaster.
