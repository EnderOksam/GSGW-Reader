# Chapter 367

“Oh.”

Lee Seonghae lifted her head.

Raindrops were falling onto her nose.

Before she realized it, rain was pouring down from a sky thick with dark clouds.

“Are you okay?”

“Yes, yes.”

And beside her was someone running toward their destination.

Go Yeongeun.

—Oh, I’ll follow you, Ms. Goral.

Lee Seonghae had decided to help this employee and had left the resort for Segwang Special City.

In Go Yeongeun’s anxious hands, the map given by the mascot was tightly gripped and spread open.

“......”

She had already come quite a long way.

She had nearly crossed the entire city at a run, entered a residential area, and kept moving forward.

Without pause, she walked while staring down at the map—

Then suddenly stopped.

“......”

Before them stood a house.

A slightly old detached house in a district slated for redevelopment.

A navy roof. Red brick walls.

Go Yeongeun stood frozen, staring at it with trembling eyes.

With a familiarity her body remembered.

With a sense of closeness that remained even after knowledge of it had vanished.

She recognized it.

The house.

Someone ran out from inside.

Someone she knew. Family.

“Yeongeun-ah!!”

“...Mom.”

Go Yeongeun tightly embraced her family member, who kept repeating, “Oh my goodness, what is this, oh my goodness,” and she felt relief and a rising swell of emotion flood through her entire body.

Oh.

Lee Seonghae watched without agitation, pleased.

She couldn’t understand that trembling sensation, but it seemed like something good.

Then she suddenly looked up.

“......”

The rain had stopped.

And a strange vibration began faintly resonating through the walls and ground.

“...!”

From her family’s embrace, Go Yeongeun snapped her head around.

Someone accustomed to supernatural phenomena, to darkness, quickly sensed the abnormal sign.

“Assistant Manager, just now...”

“Oh.”

“...Was it near City Hall?”

The direction the others had gone. Where Kim Soleum was.

“Hmm. Just a moment.”

Lee Seonghae pulled out a small item shaped like binoculars and looked ahead.

Toward the Segwang City Hall building in the distance, barely visible beneath the heavy clouds.

“It looks like there’s something on the rooftop. Hmm...”

“......”

Under the dark sky, Go Yeongeun drew a sharp breath.

Would they be safe?

...Though she had already set her priorities, moral judgment, anguish, and inner conflict stabbed upward inside her. Clenching her teeth, she finally asked, “What does it look like?”

“Ummm...”

Lee Seonghae didn’t notice her turmoil at all, but she could describe what she saw clearly.

So she answered confidently, “It looks like a festival!”
