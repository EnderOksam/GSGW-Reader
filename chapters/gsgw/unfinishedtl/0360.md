# Chapter 359

*A fantastical, uncanny moment.*

*The subway tracks of Segwang Special City mutate as if being swallowed whole.*

*“Ah…!”*

*As though several frozen years suddenly come crashing down at once, the subway tracks—rapidly aging in fast-forward—flip over, glitter gold in midair, then settle back onto the ground.*

*Their shape resembles an exquisitely crafted toy.*

*Elegant lines shimmer across a black-and-white floor, while the once-grimy, weathered arched walls of the tunnel take on a smooth, ivory glow—dazzling, almost intoxicating.*

*From the far end of the visible rails, that wave-like, dreamlike transformation surges forward in an instant…*

*And soon crashes all the way to where the exploration team stands.*

*“…!”*

*People who had instinctively stepped back or raised their equipment feel it brush past them from all sides.*

*“Oh.”*

*Lee Seonghae sniffed the air.*

*Even the smell had changed.*

*Instead of the damp, stale underground air, a sweet, pleasant scent filled the space.*

*The scent of the Cheerful Theme Park—Golden Zone.*

*Two figures in Flower Golden Resort uniforms, who had watched the entire spectacle without moving a muscle, spoke with smiling mouths.*

*“Construction is complete.”*

*“Confirmed.”*

*Those rumor-shrouded employees checked that even the tracks had been perfectly transformed, then turned around.*

*They walked away along the ornate golden rails.*

*“W–wait…!”*

*Go Yeongeun dashed after them in a panic.*

*“Hm.”*

*Lee Seonghae followed close behind.*

*The twin-like employees, striding unnaturally fast along the tracks, eventually headed toward the nearest station.*

*…Segwang Station.*

*“…!!”*

*Go Yeongeun stopped in stunned disbelief.*

*What stood there was not a modern platform.*

*╔════════════════════╗*

*MONORAIL CITY

FLOWER GOLDEN*

*╚════════════════════╝*

*An elegant, old-fashioned sign gleamed above the station.*

*The station looked as if one had stepped straight into a theme park illustration—dazzling golden lighting, ivory pillars, and plush purple velvet carpeting that seemed to melt into sweetness beneath one’s feet.*

*All of it—*

*“Oh! It really is Flower Golden Resort.”*

*“…Are you talking about that pop-up store that was at Twilight Station?”*

*“Yes!”*

*Lee Seonghae casually approached the strange resort employees and asked cheerfully, “So, what are you doing?”*

*“We’re performing operational maintenance, Dolphin-nim!”*

*“We’ll be checking train operations now. Would you like to watch together?”*

*And at that moment—*

♩♪♬♬~
♩♬♬
~ *♩♪♪*

*A clear, bell-like melody rang out.*

*It was instantly recognizable.*

*The signal of an arriving train.*

*From the far end of the opposite track, a train riding the golden rails began to glide into the station.*

*“…!”*

*Go Yeongeun swallowed hard.*

Chiiiiiiik—!

*The subway had become an intricate monorail.*

*Its glossy white body shimmered warmly with gold embellishments and crystal accents, and mounted on the very front was a chibi-style feline mascot face with flower trees jutting out like horns.*

*The Flower Golden mascot.*

*The fantastical train came to a halt at Segwang Station—no, at “Monorail City” Station.*

*Then, as the screen doors—now transformed into antique glass doors—opened, someone stepped down from the first car…*

*A small, round yellow body, soft fur.*

*The conductor mascot.*

*The mascot, identical to the one on the front of the train, carefully stepped onto the platform.*

*“Mr. Mascot!”*

*“This place is incredible! We’re so happy to be working here!”*

*The two employees ran over joyfully.*

*“Oh!”*

*When Lee Seonghae ran over to greet it as well, Go Yeongeun felt an even stranger sensation.*

*“….”*

*When she looked up, the conductor-uniformed “mascot” met her gaze, then pulled something from its chest and held it out to her.*

*As if calling her.*

*“…!”*

*After hesitating briefly, Go Yeongeun forced her stiff legs forward.*

*What the mascot offered her was a flyer.*

*╔════════════════════╗*

*Cheerful Theme Park

Flower Golden Resort No. 2

Monorail City Branch*

*Ride the adorable monorail and enjoy attractions at every station.

Stay in a special room themed after your favorite attraction.*

*Present this coupon to receive complimentary resort lodging.*

*Eligible room:

Suite (Attraction Garden View)*

*╚════════════════════╝*

*“Oh, you’ve received a coupon. Congratulations, Goral-nim.”*

*“An accommodation guide for Monorail City guests is currently in production.”*

*The employees’ voices echoed dully in her head.*

*Holding the coupon-shaped flyer in a daze, Go Yeongeun slowly lifted her head.*

*An unsettling premonition stirred.*

*…A horned mascot.*

*Someone who knew an abnormal amount about the pop-up store that had appeared at Twilight Station.*

*Someone who had shown strange guilt over Jang Heoun’s contamination.*

*……*

*“…Mr. Roe Deer?”*

*The mascot didn’t answer.*

*Its oversized, expressionless eyes stared at her for a moment, then tapped the back of its hand gently.*

*Like a pat.*

*Then it boarded the train again.*

*The mascot sat in the driver’s seat, tipped its hat in farewell, and the train began to depart.*

*“There are people on the tracks…!”*

*“It’s fine! The Flower Golden Monorail automatically avoids obstacles.”*

*She heard Jang Heoun’s gentle voice.*

*Go Yeongeun reflexively turned her head.*

*She saw the soft smile at the corner of an employee’s mouth.*

*“Do you have any other questions, honored guest?”*

*“….”*

*It was a familiar face—one she had seen just days ago.*

*Over the past few months, Go Yeongeun had occasionally visited the pop-up store, chatting with the two of them, even bringing books for Jang Heoun in case he was bored…*

*But—*

*“…Mr. Bison?”*

*“Yes! Goral Guest! I’m Bison, a receptionist at the front desk!”*

*Something was different.*

*It felt like staring at something deeply contaminated.*

*The body and memories were the same, yet it felt like a different being entirely—as if the qualities that made someone human had been stripped away.*

*Was this how Jang Heoun had been when he was isolated for contamination by the Disaster Management Bureau?*

*Why had he suddenly returned like this?*

*“Because the city lockdown was lifted…?”*

*Or because the pop-up store had expanded…?*

*A chill crept in.*

*“Oh! You’re here, too!”*

*“…!”*

*“Isn’t this place fascinating?”*

*New figures appeared on the platform.*

*Researchers Kwak Jekang and Lee Yeonhwa.*

*“This is incredibly interesting—darkness devouring other darkness! It’s not even artificially grafted, yet we can observe such a phenomenon directly! Haha! Truly fascinating—”*

*Go Yeongeun quickly turned away from Kwak Jekang and exchanged a glance with Lee Yeonhwa.*

*Now that she looked closely, it seemed they had disembarked from the back cars, guided by a mascot holding a Flower Golden Resort flag.*

*Trailing behind them, trudging with lifeless eyes, was the slumped Security Chief.*

*In his hand was a flyer coupon similar to hers—and a handful of strange coin-like objects.*

*Coins?*

*“Uh, Chief… what are those…?”*

*“…Hmm.”*

*Though they hadn’t spoken much before, the Chief answered readily.*

*“Mr. Roe Deer… gave them to me……”*

*“…When?”*

*“Before he left. I asked if there was anything I could help with, and he told me to hand these out later… hmm.”*

*He held them out. Go Yeongeun took one without thinking and read the words engraved on it.*

*It wasn’t a coin—it was a small badge.*

*╔════════════════════╗*

*EARLY

ACCESS*

*╚════════════════════╝*

*Early entry.*

*“What is this…?”*

*As if they’d been waiting for the question, the two employees answered brightly.*

*“An early access pass! Holders may enjoy the Flower Golden Resort Monorail City Branch for today only, prior to its official opening!”*

*…!*

*“It’s an honor to host you! If you enjoy your stay, we’d appreciate a short review upon checkout—”*

*“W–wait.”*

*Go Yeongeun finally spoke, unable to endure any longer.*

*“I’m fine. I’m going to leave now.”*

*If the lockdown was lifting, she couldn’t afford to wait.*

*Whether she exited through the maintenance tracks Mr. Roe Deer used or returned to the surface and came back later—either way, she had to get above ground.*

*…She wouldn’t be able to endure it if she didn’t find her parents.*

*“Our house…”*

*Before her aunt lost her memories, she had already memorized the address. She still repeated it to herself.*

*She wanted to go there immediately, to find her mom and dad.*

*And… if possible, she wanted to confirm Mr. Roe Deer’s safety, too.*

*“What are you even doing…?”*

*Was the termination attempt proceeding safely?*

*Was this resort his doing?*

*If so, what was it all for?*

*Guilt, anxiety, anger, and worry twisted together as Go Yeongeun looked desperately at the two employees.*

*But—*

*“……”

“……”*

*“Excuse me?”*

*The two employees—long brown hair tied back, bowties neatly fastened—smiled in perfect unison, like figures in a painting.*

*“There is currently no way to exit Flower Golden Resort Monorail City Branch.”*

*“…What?”*

*“The guest exit gate has not yet been constructed.”*

*“What do you mean—?!”*

*A rough voice cut in. “That’s exactly what it sounds like.”*

*“…!”*

*“The maintenance tracks are completely gone. And the exit at the end is sealed shut. Whew.”*

*Agent Choi sounded casual, almost joking, but his movements were swift.*

*He flung open the glass doors and strode forward as if to grab someone by the collar, speaking lightly in contrast.*

*“Hey, staff. What’s going on here? Why did the tracks that were here just disappear?”*

*“We do not think! This decision was made by the Mascot-nim!”*

*Agent Choi’s eyes narrowed.*

*“…Is that so? Then what about the door that appeared where the maintenance exit used to be—why doesn’t it open?”*

*…!*

*“Was that also the mascot’s decision?”*

*“Yes. Honored guest.”*

*“…….”*

*After a pause, Agent Choi spoke again.*

*“…And what if someone isn’t a guest?”*

*“Huh?”*

*“What if I have no intention of staying at this resort at all?”*

*“…….”*

*The employees glanced at each other.*

*“Then unfortunately, for security reasons, you’ll need to remain within the restricted access area until opening.”*

*They meant to confine them.*

*“Do you perhaps have no intention of staying?”*

*Go Yeongeun noticed it then.*

*She could feel eyes on her.*

*…The yellow mascot that had guided the researchers was watching them.*

*No—now that she looked properly…*

*All around the platform—now fully transformed into a dazzling theme park resort station—*

*Identical mascots had appeared.*

*Mascots holding balloons. Mascots wearing monocles. Mascots waving from the stairs.*

*All of them watching.*

*“…….”*

*Lee Seonghae’s bright voice rang out.*

*“Nope! We’ll all stay for now, please. Um, once the exit opens, we’re free to come and go, right?”*

*“Of course! Dolphin Guest!”*

*A chill ran down Go Yeongeun’s spine.*

*Dread and a terrible premonition settled in.*

*“Do you think… Mr. Roe Deer did this?”*

*“Yep. Seems like it.”*

*“Of course he did. He’s the owner here, after all!”*

*Agent Haegum joined them on the platform.*

*“…Noona.”*

*“I checked the other stations. Looks like the entire subway system has changed. And…”*

*Haegum’s eyes darkened as he looked at Agent Choi.*

*“You tried opening the door to the surface, didn’t you?”*

*“Yes, ma’am.”*

*Agent Choi admitted calmly.*

*“Well, with the lockdown lifting, it might not seem like a big deal. The subway would disappear anyway, leaving behind the May 4th version—”*

*“…….”*

*“Agent Choi?”*

*“So if the lockdown lifts, we’d naturally be spat out somewhere… and yet he’s deliberately keeping us here—”*

*His voice hardened.*

*“He swallowed up a supernatural phenomenon that would’ve vanished once the lockdown ended.”*

*“…….”*

*Agent Choi stepped toward one of the mascots.*

*“What is this?”*

*…….*

*“Why are you trying to keep people here?”*

*The mascot didn’t answer.*

*The generic mascot simply drooped its ears and tried to hand him a flyer, as if urging him to enjoy himself.*

*“You—”*

*“Wait.”*

*Go Yeongeun interrupted urgently.*

*She was sorry to disrupt the questioning, but this was personal—and the most effective question she could ask was…*

*Becoming an insider.*

*“I’m interested in working at the resort. May I ask something?”*

*“Oh!”*

*The employees perked up instantly.*

*“You’ve come at the perfect time! We’re recruiting new staff right now!”*

*“Yes.”*

*Go Yeongeun spoke calmly.*

*“How did you turn this place into a resort so quickly? And… are you planning to transform the surface as well?”*

*…!*

*She felt everyone’s attention shift toward her.*

*Including the researchers’.*

*“Yes! I’ll explain.”*

*Go Yeongeun swallowed.*

*“As far as I’m allowed to say, the Mascot-nim had already completed all the surveying of this area beforehand. That’s what made such rapid progress possible.”*

*“…!”*

*“He visited all key zones, inspected every track, and expelled any organizations or entities illegally occupying or leasing this property.”*

*Ah.*

*Go Yeongeun realized.*

*“They’re talking about Kim Soleum’s explorations…!”*

*Kim Soleum had visited nearly every station in Segwang Special City, clearing or temporarily terminating more than half of them.*

*Those actions had… come back to support this outcome.*

*“However, the library entrance is a public facility, so it wasn’t acquired—it was left as is through negotiation!”*

*“And there are currently no plans to expand to the surface.”*

*…….*

*“I see. Thank you.”*

*Go Yeongeun staggered back.*

*Her head was spinning.*

*The results of exploration.*

*Or perhaps rewards…*

*A gorgeous, terrifying resort expansion.*

*Her jaw trembled.*

*No matter how she thought about it, this wasn’t a reward meant for a human being.*

*“…Is it really okay to do something like this?”*

*“Huh?”*

*“I mean… isn’t the Mascot-nim pushing himself too far?”*

*“Absolutely not!”*

*The two employees replied in chorus.*

*“The Flower Golden Mascot-nim will become even more powerful and magnificent. His domain is expanding!”*

*“Guests will come from countless places. Reservations are already pouring in! Even a famous talk show host is arriving today!”*

*“WehopehemakesitsoonwehopehecomesquicklysoallofustogetherinthebeautifulFlowerGoldenResort—”*

*Ah.*

*“I understand.”*

*Agent Choi yanked Go Yeongeun sharply away from the employees.*

*“So growing the resort makes the mascot stronger. Got it! Crystal clear!”*

*He followed it up with a light, warding gesture—something ritualistic.*

*“Hup!”*

*Suddenly, Go Yeongeun could breathe again.*

*And—*

*“Noona.”*

*A low voice spoke beside her.*

*“I just had a thought.”*

*“…….”*

*“When a will-o’-the-wisp grows strong enough, even replacing just a few body parts can turn a person into an Elder, right?”*

*“…….”*

*“This situation—wait, don’t tell me this bastard—”*

*“Yes.”*

*Agent Haegum’s voice trembled with clenched teeth.*

*“He’s deliberately amplifying his own contamination…!”*

*****

*I knew it.*

*“Humans go mad when they hear the music box.”*

*So if a moment came when I was about to lose my mind because of it… I just had to escape the premise.*

*—Become something that isn’t human.*

*Then I’d be free from the music box’s harmful effects.*

*But I couldn’t abandon the identity I’d only just reclaimed.*

*I endured until now because I believed I was human.*

*“I want to stay human.”*

*So—*

*Instead of abandoning myself, what if I were naturally swallowed by contamination?*

*Just to the brink.*

*To a level that could still be recovered with time.*

*But on the surface of Segwang Special City—where other ghost stories are rejected—it wasn’t easy to deepen contamination.*

*“And if I go too far, I might just vanish as a ghost story.”*

*Like the counselor did.*

*So I needed just one moment.*

*—A single moment where I’d be overwhelmed by an even stronger contamination.*

*Even if I was already mad, already contaminated, I needed that state to be forcibly suppressed, just for an instant.*

*How could that happen?*

*Simple.*

*One of my existing ghost-story identities would have to grow immensely powerful—shattering the balance and overwhelming me.*

*And so I did it.*

*By expanding Flower Golden Resort.*

*“Ah.”*

*My mind and body twisted.*

*A domain twice as wide.*

*A role grown massive.*

*Below me, nearby—the distorted Segwang subway, the Flower Golden Resort built within it, and my role there—all became clear.*

*The golden resort.*

*The Flower Golden Mascot.*

*The moment all those sensations overwhelmed the human me—*

*My body warped and twisted into the mascot’s form.*

*Melting at the same time.*

*This was the moment.*

*I had to seize it.*

*Because in truth—*

*I hurled myself into the torn cocoon.*

*And—*

Inside the cocoon, from Kim Soleum’s arms, I tore the music box free with both hands.
