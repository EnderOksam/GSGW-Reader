# Chapter 356

*From below the tracks, Agent Choi’s voice reaches me—flat, explanatory, devoid of any rise or fall.*

*A shocking truth.*

*“The resealing ritual for Segwang Special City is already underway.”*

*……!*

*“For something this massive, it takes at least three days. It’s already started.”*

*My head cools as I understand.*

*Erasing an entire metropolis could never happen rapidly in a short span of time….*

*Especially not when a countdown of 300 days was given.*

*If it’s already begun and in full swing, there’s nothing strange about that.*

“Of course, it’s different from ordinary shamanistic rites or devotional ceremonies, but the underlying principle— putting in sincerity *—is similar. Anything works better when you invest real effort, right?”*

*“……”*

*“When the seal lifts two days from now, everything has to align perfectly with that moment. There’s been a seven-day Anbangut, and once the purification was complete, professional agents were already holding ritual blades.”*

*Agent Choi describes it as if he can see it.*

“The ritual implement is a myeongdo *—a bronze mirror, basically. A sun-and-moon myeongdo engraved with incantations symbolizing Segwang Special City. They’ll cover it with fresh blood representing the offering, then flip it upside down onto the altar floor…”*

*Somewhere, I almost hear the clang of gongs, shouted chants, drums pounding, bells ringing madly…*

*And Agent Choi looks at me.*

*“I was planning to swap out the blood offered to the myeongdo.”*

*Every nerve in my body stands on end.*

*“…You don’t mean—with your own blood?”*

*“That’s right.”*

*“Are you out of your mi—”*

*“Listen to the rest.”*

*He cuts me off and continues calmly.*

*“Then the direction of the offering changes. Originally, they’d have prepared the blood of a spiritually attuned animal—matched by birth data—to symbolize the citizens of Segwang Special City. If I replace it… the weight of that sacrifice comes to me instead.”*

*“……”*

*“Of course, that alone wouldn’t be enough. So from the moment the seal lifts, I’d perform a separate ritual. I guess in modern terms, you’d call it ‘drawing aggro.’ ”*

*“How were you planning to replace an entire city’s population on your own?”*

*“Simple. Match the numbers.”*

*“…….”*

*A sense of dread coils in the air.*

*“What matters is the moment life is severed—this offering is about cutting fate itself. So I would…”*

*“Die that many times?”*

*“…….”*

*“As many times as there are people?”*

*“That’s right.”*

*He says it casually.*

Wait.

*What kind of number are we talking about?*

Hundreds of thousands die every day… so that means repeating it alone, every day…

*The scale is so astronomical it borders on cosmic horror.*

*The hair on my scalp bristles.*

*“Of course, not just anyone could complete a ritual like that. But I do have… some pull. Enough to try something like this.”*

*“Y-you’re insane.”*

Is insane *even the right word?*

*“No. It’s actually bearable. Since I lose my memory every time I die, I don’t go mad.”*

*He exhales.*

*“Of course, I’m not planning to endure this forever—becoming some kind of living totem of suffering. I planned to leave hints. Like Team Leader Hong did back at Segwang High.”*

*“…!”*

*“Then someday you’d come save me, right? You and Bronze. Grapes.”*

*“……”*

*“Not with reckless attempts like now, but after more research. With Bronze, it’d be different.”*

*A faint smile flickers across Agent Choi’s face, then fades back into weary calm.*

*His gaze shifts—to the body lying between us.*

*Specifically, Agent Bronze’s corpse.*

*“…Agent.”*

*“Don’t worry. Do you know how many times I’ve seen the bodies of acquaintances on missions? I know he’s sleeping peacefully outside.”*

*Yet he looks at Bronze as if at a fallen younger brother or son, reaches out, gently strokes his hair, and lowers his hand.*

*“…Since the body hasn’t disappeared, he probably hasn’t woken up outside. The ritual must still be anchoring him to the surface of Segwang Special City.”*

*“……”*

*“When the seal holding the citizens breaks, Bronze will come out, too.”*

*He says it like a vow.*

*Then he glances briefly toward the track exit—where nothing would seem to be there to him—and looks back at me.*

*“So, shall we keep talking? You were curious about what I did to pull you out of the Daydream Basement.”*

*“…….”*

*“It’s simple. Think of it like a collateral loan.”*

*He rubs the back of his neck.*

“If I don’t present an appropriate gift within a few years to the… well, someone *who helped locate you, I’ll lose a few parts of myself.”*

*This is insane…*

*“Finding a gift they’d be satisfied with is tough. If that fails, I’ll have to redirect those parts into organs. Then maybe I’ll live on as something like will-o’-the-wisps—or end up like the Elder.”*

*“……”*

*“That’s it.”*

*I stare at him, feeling like I’ve just been doused with ice water.*

*“Anything else you want to know? If so, say it now.”*

*After dumping something this brain-numbing, Agent Choi looks at me without a change in expression.*

*“The reason I never told you any of this before is simple. Hearing it would only worry you, and there wasn’t much to learn from it.”*

*“……”*

*“Right? It’s not like you’d suddenly go learn shamanism just because I told you.”*

*A trace of his usual banter sneaks into his voice.*

*I stare at him.*

*“Please don’t lie.”*

*“…….”*

*“You just didn’t want to deal with my concern or resistance. Because you never intended to regret or change your decision anyway.”*

*“…If you want to put it that way, sure.”*

*“Does Agent Haegum know this? Did she agree?”*

*“Whether she agrees or not doesn’t matter. I’m the only one among us who can interfere with the sealing ritual.”*

*His voice roughens.*

*“This is a method I’m fairly confident will work.”*

*“…….”*

*“And yet you decided everything on your own—trusted a note from Lee Kangheon, lugged a Potion Machine, and headed for city hall.”*

*“Because persuasion doesn’t work.”*

*“You’re the same.”*

*“No. It’s different.”*

*I grit my teeth.*

“You don’t believe a word I say, so there’s no way persuasion can *work.”*

*“Yeah. Blindly trusting someone contaminated by a supernatural disaster is basically suicide!”*

*“……”*

*“That could get both of us killed. Do you know how many times people followed a missing agent and vanished too? You—”*

*He pauses to breathe.*

*“…Right. You said you came from another world.”*

*“…….”*

*“You know what? I didn’t just dismiss that out of hand.”*

*His voice drops.*

*“You were gone for 293 days. That’s long enough to build a highway. You think I wouldn’t try to verify whether you were telling the truth?”*

*…!!*

*“…You verified it?”*

*“Of course.”*

*He gives a bitter smile, then sobers.*

*“The ritual site I set up doesn’t just let me peek at the sealing ritual. I chose an auspicious time and consulted divination. To check if what you said was true.”*

*I inhale.*

*Tension clamps down on my body.*

*“I chose the wording carefully. Packed in everything that could be packed, gave it proper form…”*

*“ ‘Is this being’s bones and flesh not born of this place, but a lost child wandering between worlds?’ ”*

*He looks at me.*

*“The answer came instantly.”*

*“…….”*

*“When I interpreted it, it was shockingly short.”*

*“No.”*

*“…….”*

“Put into words, it sounds like that—but really, it was a negation of every *description I gave.”*

*He raises his hand as if craving a cigarette, then lowers it, steadying himself.*

*“Grape.”*

*“…….”*

*“A few days ago… I chose my words badly out of impatience. I went too far.”*

*I see his blue eyes, faded and glinting in the sunlight.*

*He looks exhausted.*

*“But I don’t have time or leeway right now. And I want you to know—I’m not lying.”*

*“…….”*

*“You belong here. You were born here. You didn’t lose your way home.”*

*A bitter smile tugs at his mouth again.*

*“This probably won’t convince you.”*

*“…….”*

*A strange feeling rises.*

So that’s why.

*That’s why he was so certain.*

*Relief, emptiness, frustration, simmering anger—all mix together and scatter through my head.*

*Then clarity settles in.*

*“I understand.”*

*My voice is calm.*

*A faint spark of hope flickers in his eyes—quickly overshadowed by wariness.*

*Of course.*

*That’s expected.*

*“You’re right. I’ve been influenced by too many urban legends in too short a time, and I only recently managed to steady myself. My fixation on going home is excessive.”*

*I glance at the handcuffs.*

*“Maybe I’m just a long-term missing person from a supernatural disaster—someone so soaked in fear and panic that they’ve lost all judgment.”*

*But.*

*“But even those people still have a home to return to.”*

*“…….”*

*“And so do I, Agent.”*

*I look at him again and smile.*

*“You chose the wrong wording for your divination.”*

*“…!”*

“I don’t fall under that category. If we’re being precise, this body was invited *. I’m not a lost child—I’m a guest.”*

*“…A guest?”*

*“That probably sounds just as cultish. Either way, one thing is certain.”*

*His pupils tremble.*

*I speak gently.*

*“Believe it or not, you can’t stop my choice. It’s my decision, and I’ll do what I believe is right.”*

*“Th—”*

*“Just like you already did.”*

*Right?*

*“You only told me all this now because everything for the resealing ritual is already prepared, and I can’t interfere anymore.”*

*“…!”*

*“I’m not angry.”*

*I really am not.*

*After hearing so much—so honestly—from an exhausted Agent Choi…*

*Even without the cuffs, I don’t feel like screaming or grabbing him by the collar.*

*A calm judgment settles in.*

*“From your perspective, that’s the most reliable answer. People could die by mistake—you can’t choose another method just because it feels right or interesting.”*

*“…….”*

*“My approach probably looks like an untested gamble. Wanting to go home probably looks distorted, too.”*

*“…Still.”*

*His voice cracks slightly.*

*“I’m always willing to hear you out. About home—or anything.”*

*“…….”*

*For a moment, I’m speechless.*

*“…Thank you.”*

*I know.*

*Passionate. Decisive. Choosing self-sacrifice over fear. Curious. Hard to persuade unless something is rational.*

*…A good person.*

*All traits of the Bureau’s early agents.*

*He’s always been that way.*

*“Even so, your judgment probably won’t change.”*

*Saying it only reinforces my certainty.*

*“Yes. We can’t persuade each other.”*

*Not right now.*

*“Grapes.”*

*“But…”*

*I inhale and give a wry smile.*

*“I still think we can cooperate.”*

*“…….”*

“I think you’re an incredible person. And until now… well. I can’t say I never lied. I was *a spy, after all.”*

*“…….”*

*“I know I’ve said a lot of things that can’t be verified. So I won’t demand your trust.”*

*His eyes tremble slightly.*

*“I just thought—what if we made a promise?”*

*With my free hand, I gesture as if making one.*

*“For this Segwang Special City incident, let’s cooperate so each of us can attempt what we want. No interference. Genuine help.”*

*“…….”*

*“Even if only one of us succeeds, it’s good for the citizens. And if we don’t cooperate, the chance of both attempts failing only goes up.”*

*I deliberately hold his gaze.*

*“Even if trusting me looks like suicide, you can still raise the survival rate.”*

*“You—”*

*“Just kidding. But I am asking for cooperation.”*

*“…….”*

*Looking at Agent Choi with his head bowed, I add bitterly,*

*“If not, then there’s no choice. We try without any cooperation at all.”*

*…….*

*“You sly bastard.”*

*“Sounds like your own introduction.”*

*【 Hahaha! 】*

*Braun’s laughter rings out, while Agent Choi only exhales softly.*

*Then, from below, his subdued voice emerges.*

*“What do you want?”*

*“We write a contract. Then you swear an oath to a supernatural being using your equipment.”*

*“And you?”*

*“I’ll stake my supernatural status.”*

*So I write on the paper:*

The two who swear below shall cooperate to achieve each other’s objectives and shall not interfere with one another.

This agreement may be dissolved by mutual consent.

*Below that, I write first:*

GOLDEN

*“Sign.”*

*“…….”*

*Agent Choi meets my eyes, calculates something… then grits his teeth and strikes the handle of his ritual blade down.*

*Instead of a name, a strange stain remains.*

*As the mascot of Flower Golden Resort, I sense that the signature is valid.*

*【 Excellent, my friend! 】*

…Thanks.

*I feel like some sea-witch octopus getting a signature from the Little Mermaid.*

*But it worked.*

Phew.

*Mentally exhausted, I try not to dwell on Agent Choi, who has grown regretfully silent after signing…*

*Then—*

*“Do you really… have to?”*

*…….*

*“…Yes.”*

*I steady my breath, looking at the silhouettes still beyond the exit, then at the bodies on the tracks.*

*“Then I’ll explain now.”*

*“…….”*

*“How I’m going to install the Potion Machine.”*

*And so, the next day—*

*I stepped alone through the track exit onto the surface of Segwang Special City.*

It was the day before the seal was due to lift.
