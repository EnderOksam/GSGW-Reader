# Chapter 371

“......”

Holding my breath, I stared at the staircase leading down below.

It was the way back.

Stretching long beneath the box, at the end of those dark stairs...

...was the place I would return to.

【 Oh, you’ve finally achieved your long-standing goal. Congratulations, my friend! 】

...!

“Braun!”

I picked up the rabbit doll resting beside the tank, atop the rumpled suit.

【 Indeed, Mr. Roe. You’ve found the path. And fortunately, it seems our reception has improved as well! 】

In my once-quiet mind, I heard applause and the faint scratch of an antenna being tapped atop a TV screen.

So I really couldn’t hear that voice again...

Just like when I became 130666 right after signing the contract with Director Cheong.

When I was reduced to a mere mass of thought, I couldn’t perceive my “good friend.” Maybe... it was because I was in no state to make friends.

The memory of that crushed, formless state made me queasy—but the relief of hearing him again was stronger.

Not being alone always brings comfort.

Even if the one beside me is a TV-headed grand host who wants to turn me into a talk show staff member... we’ve been together a long time.

The words came naturally.

The first thought that flashed through my mind was about the membrane surrounding me.

“Did I... enter the egg that was inside the Dream Incubator’s tank?”

【 Hmm. An intriguing and clever guess! Whether that’s true or not, in any case, isn’t this space something created by that bottle of syrup from the incubator to grant your wish? 】

...My wish.

Reflexively, I looked back at the staircase inside the box.

Even at the paper sign that read:

【 Way Back, Dark Exploration Record Pop-up Store. 】

That was when—

—Agent Grape?

...!

A faint voice came from beyond the membrane.

“Agent Haegeum!”

I shouted instinctively toward it.

“I’m safe. I’m okay!”

—Good!

It sounded slightly chaotic outside, but completely blocked off. Only faint voices reached my ears, frustratingly muffled.

—Has anything changed?

“Pardon? I... there’s a path here.”

—A path?

“Yes. ...A path home.”

......

After a brief silence, the voice returned.

—Does it look safe?

“I can’t be certain. But judging from the circumstances...”

The original Wish Ticket. A complete ‘wish-granting syrup’ infused with five million Dream Essences had activated properly.

“I think... I can make it home safely.”

—......

A tightened voice replied.

—And you want to take that path.

I steadied my breath.

“...Yes.”

—Alright. Understood.

The surroundings grew faintly noisy again.

Maybe Ms. Yeongeun or the Security Chief was trying to say something. I couldn’t hear clearly, and it was frustrating.

I want to talk more.

Pressing against the membrane, I called out,

“Um, are the others there? At least let me say goodbye—”

But—

—No. It would be better not to.

“...Pardon?”

—The egg is getting smaller.

Ah.

I realized it then.

The membrane around me was shrinking.

As if it would collapse... or as if I could break through it.

—It’s been happening since we started talking.

—It seems interaction with the outside is affecting it. It would be safest to minimize it.

“......”

Naturally, I understood.

The “Children’s Paradise Syrup” had given me time to choose.

Break through the shell—

Or go home.

“......”

I looked at the stairs.

—Agent Grape. Have you decided?

“...Yes.”

I said the goal I had repeated countless times.

The wish these people had tried to grant for me.

“I want... to go home.”

—...Alright. Understood.

Agent Haegeum’s voice came through.

—Then don’t look back. Just keep walking.

—If you look back, you’ll lose your way.

“...Yes.”

I nodded, taking a deep breath.

The membrane shrank further.

Ah.

There wasn’t much time.

Hurriedly, I said, “Thank you!”

Yes.

“Could you... tell them I was truly grateful?”

I could have met my end at that resort.

Updating the count of missing persons in a ghost story, leaving behind a chilling exploration-record ending on the wiki.

Thank you for not letting that happen.

“I’ve received so much help until now.”

To the team leaders who helped a rookie employee who appeared and disappeared without warning.

To my colleagues.

To the Security Chief.

To Agent 007 I met through secondhand trading.

And... to a certain agent who doubted me until the very end, yet still answered my call for rescue.

“Thanks to you, I’m going home safely from a supernatural disaster.”

—......

—I will deliver your message. Without fail.

“...Yes.”

Something hot welled up in my throat.

And then Agent Haegeum spoke slowly, almost as if relaying someone’s words—someone who had been in the room all along but hadn’t announced their presence.

—Please return safely, citizen.

And that was the end.

No more sound came from beyond the membrane.

“......”

I stood and examined myself.

...A black suit.

Just like—

【 The same appearance as when you began, Mr. Roe Deer! 】

“......”

【 Oh, are you pondering how to say farewell to this Braun as well? 】

“...!”

【 Hmm. A splendid closing line is every entertainer’s dilemma! But since we are friends, there’s no need to fret over something so trivial— 】

The words slipped out.

“...Are you really okay with it ending like this?”

【 Hmm? 】

They said a good friend follows you anywhere.

Commentating on my every move.

Lending me a huge sum of money.

Dropping advice at crucial moments...

I knew there was no need to poke at it—but I said it anyway.

And—

【 Ah, Mr. Roe Deer. 】

The entertainer replied.

【 All broadcasts are more delightful and eagerly awaited precisely because they eventually end. 】

【 As for when the guest might return to the studio with another wonderful story—that depends entirely on the guest. 】

“......”

【 Did you enjoy it? 】

Objectively, it was horrific.

But somehow, I couldn’t answer that way here.

“...To some extent.”

【 An answer brimming with ambition. Not satisfied, yet enjoyed it—excellent! 】

【 In that sense, it seems this recording has reached its conclusion. 】

The cheerful host’s voice came from the rabbit doll.

【 It has been a tremendous honor to accompany you on this program as your good friend and spirited MC. 】

“...Me too.”

I swallowed.

“I’m grateful, too.”

【 My pleasure. 】

【 Well then, today’s broadcast ends here... 】

......

【 Cut! 】

I carefully tucked the rabbit doll into my arms.

And then—

I stepped beneath the black box.

Thud.

The dark staircase supported my steps.

My body entered the box.

It was dark—nothing visible.

Narrow and stifling.

But faint light glimmered below.

I kept walking.

Thud.

With every step down the dark stairs, new thoughts surfaced.

The things I was leaving behind.

Stories still in progress.

Thud.

What would happen to Sekwang Special City now?

Could the Disaster Management Bureau solve their problems?

Shouldn’t I have stayed to help?

Maybe I could’ve contributed a good idea.

Thud.

Director Cheong’s contract had been voided—what would happen to the Security Team?

Would J-ssi be okay?

Chief Minseong would need a way to recover from contamination.

I might have been able to help...

Thud.

What about Agent Chogae?

Did the lion mask disappear, leaving only the goblin workshop agent? Or did they merge?

Were the students and teachers at Sekwang High safe?

...Was Ho Yuwon still counseling?

Was some part of me still there?

Thud.

With every step, the urge to turn back grew stronger.

I was moving farther and farther from the story that once included me.

Faces I wanted to talk to more.

Futures I wanted to know.

Thud.

I wanted to know the next chapter.

It felt like so much remained to be done.

I was curious.

I wanted to see.

Even now, I imagined turning around, running back up the stairs, tearing through the membrane to see what would happen...

......

But I didn’t look back.

Thud.

I moved forward.

Thud.

Watching the faint light grow closer.

Walking straight down the narrow stairs.

Thud.

At some point, all other sounds faded.

And then—

“......”

I stopped before the light.

It was a door.

The faint glow had been seeping from the crack.

I had reached the end of the stairs.

Click.

I opened it.

A department store interior spread before me.

A lively pop-up space filled with children and guardians.

[Apocalypse Prophecy: Dark Exploration Record  ]

Creak, thud.

Behind me, the door shut completely.

On the marble floor in front of me, the faint outline of the door shimmered.

An emergency exit.

“......”

I looked down at my hands.

Somehow, I was holding the “Dark Exploration Record Real Goods Box.”

I opened it.

...On top lay the “Good Friend” rabbit keyring I had won as a prize, still packaged.

Bzzzz.

My smartphone vibrated.

[Younger Sister: Hey Kim Soleum, Mom says come down this weekend]

I opened my phone and checked my contacts.

Dad. Mom. Sister. Friends.

The people I’d missed were all there.

Opening the gallery, I saw photos.

Records of who I am.

Traces of the life Kim Soleum had lived—familiar and dearly missed.

But none of the people I’d grown used to contacting over the past few years.

My returned world.

[Younger Brother: Reply if you saw this]

“......”

[Yeah.]

After replying, I put the clean smartphone—no grip-tok attached—back into my pocket.

And I walked on.

Past the Dark Exploration Record pop-up store.

Through the crowd.

“That’s a must-buy.”

“Ah, it’s sold out...”

Among the chatter of young visitors, I glanced at the roulette line.

Employees were explaining at the front—but the one who had let me spin it was nowhere to be seen.

“......”

I kept walking and left the department store.

Without looking back.

Without stopping.

I took the familiar bus and retraced my route.

Back to where I had been before visiting the pop-up store.

I arrived.

Beep beep beep.

I entered the familiar passcode and opened the door.

“......”

Where I originally belonged.

Home.

“...Haa.”

I let out a long, slow sigh of relief.

As if setting everything down.

I slipped off my shoes, set the massive black box in the entryway, and hurried inside.

The comforting scent.

The familiar, cozy space.

Surrounded by things I knew well, I set down my phone.

I took off my suit and changed into pajamas.

As the tension drained from my shoulders, an exquisitely comfortable fatigue washed over me.

A safe, gentle drowsiness.

There was... a client dinner tonight.

Right.

I had to go back to work.

But before that—just a little while.

“......”

I set an alarm and lay down in my bed.

And fell asleep.

A sweet, peaceful sleep.

Rest.
