# Chapter 346

*Pinned beneath the collapsed concrete, I listened to the voices coming from outside.*

*I tried to move my mouth, but no sound came out.*

*Only dull, foggy fragments of voices flowed into my head…*

*The voices of Segwang City Disaster Management Bureau agents, worn down by deep fatigue and confusion.*

*“Additional collapse has been prevented for now. …By radio, we’re shifting from termination to rescue priority….”*

*“There are civilians in the concourse too… …so many… dead….”*

*“What do we do, Team Leader? The triage criteria—”*

*The voices drift farther away…*

*“Ah.”*

*They won’t have time to save the person trapped here. I understood the situation very slowly.*

*The sound of agents’ footsteps moving away, leaving the pile of collapsed concrete behind, traveled as vibrations through the cold rebar pressed against my cheek…*

*“Agents.”*

*Then, a familiar voice rang out.*

*The counselor’s voice.*

*Hoarse, cracked, it sounded low and muffled, as if trapped inside the concrete just like me…*

*“There is… an agent… in here……”*

*“…!!”*

*“Down… below….”*

*A brief stir seemed to pass through the agents, then they sounded closer.*

*“Who are you?”*

*“I’m… I’m a counselor at Segwang High School.”*

*“Ah!”*

*As if realizing who it was, hurried footsteps approached. The sound of concrete and rebar being cleared echoed.*

*With the movement, a piece of debris lodged in my back collapsed further to the side. I felt the pain spreading through my body in a dull haze…*

*I couldn’t move.*

*“Wait.”*

*A calm voice spoke.*

*“…How did someone like you end up buried under there?”*

*“Yes…? I’m just… a counselor….”*

*“…….”*

*A heavy silence fell. The sounds of clearing debris continued, but—*

*“Something’s off. Fall back.”*

*“Wait, just one more piece— ngh!”*

*“…!!”*

*“H-hold on.”*

*The sound of heavy debris being dropped, of someone sucking in breath.*

*As if they had seen something completely unexpected.*

*“The appearance is—”*

*“It’s contamination. Step away.”*

*Footsteps, equipment clashing, friction noises echoed…*

“No— cough  *— it’s not… contamination.”*

*The counselor’s voice, now exposed to the open air, was no longer muffled.*

*“Don’t worry….”*

*But it sagged weakly as he spoke.*

*“…‘Counselor.’ Are you aware of your own condition?”*

*“Yes. I’m… fine. But, down there…”*

*His voice sounded desperate.*

*“There’s an agent who carried me out….”*

*“…….”*

*“He has… special abilities. He will definitely be of help. So—”*

*Silence followed.*

*None of the agents answered.*

*“…Agents?”*

*“What is that agent’s call sign?”*

*“I… haven’t heard it yet.”*

*“…Then what about affiliation? We should be able to tell from his jacket.”*

*“…….”*

*Slowly, an answer came.*

*“The jacket is… here. He gave it to me…. He’s not wearing it.”*

*“…….”*

*“…….”*

*“This jacket— isn’t it yours?”*

*“It may… look that way. But in reality… it isn’t….”*

*“…….”*

*“…….”*

*“Understood.”*

*“Agent… sir?”*

*The sound of agents retreating followed.*

*Their low conversation reached my ears through the collapsed concrete…*

*“We can’t waste any more time. …Move.”*

*“But—!”*

*“The circumstances don’t line up. That entity is already unstable. Even if we rescue it, it won’t be useful. It’s already—”*

*“……E-even so, even if he’s not an agent, there’s a person down there… he’ll die soon…!”*

*“Agent!”*

*The sound of a hand grabbing a shoulder.*

*“Right now, there are tens—hundreds of thousands of people dying in this city.”*

*“…….”*

*“And you want to spend more time for one person with uncertain survival? If we make exceptions, what about the other civilians we fail to save?”*

*“…That—”*

*“Go. Before more civilians arrive!”*

*And then—*

*“That line…”*

*The counselor’s slow voice spoke.*

*“It sounds… very familiar.”*

*“…….”*

*“I feel like… I’ve heard it before.”*

*I swallowed.*

*“What?”*

*“Don’t answer.”*

*“So… yesterday…?”*

*…….*

*“No… the day before, and before that, and before that before that, and before that before that before that……”*

*“Wait.”*

*“Fall back!”*

*Even as the outside grew chaotic, even as my mind sank into haze, I was caught by the counselor’s voice and listened.*

*“That’s….”*

*Because I realized how eerily similar it was.*

*…To myself, not long ago.*

*“He realized it.”*

*The sensation I felt when I regained consciousness inside the 613th May 4th—*

*He was speaking it aloud.*

*“But it was different then, so… yes. Burned to death.”*

*“Move back!”*

*“I think I burned to death while holding the doorknob… yes. Burned to death. I was trapped in the machine room, burning, calling for help. The collapsed concrete blocked the door, so I couldn’t get out……”*

*“And you just left.”*

*“I thought it couldn’t be helped. I didn’t resent you. Saving civilians comes first… but then.”*

*“…….”*

*“How many times… are you saving civilians? Why does the same thing keep happening?”*

*“What is he talking about?”*

*“Don’t listen.”*

“Why does it keep… repeating… this is… this is strange— cough  *— huh? Trapped—”*

*“Damn it.”*

*Urgent footsteps rang out.*

*“Evacuate! Seal the platform. Seolpia, talisman!”*

*“That’s—”*

*“Seal it?”*

*In that instant—*

*The atmosphere sank into a chilling stillness.*

*“That’s right. That was it… sealing.”*

*…!*

*A voice murmured as if entranced.*

*“After I burned to death in there, I passed through the door and remained on the platform. Because that’s what I became. And for four days, trapped, I listened to civilians killing themselves, desperately trying to reach the surface, the outside—begging anyone, anyone at all, to help this city… and then.”*

*…….*

*“They began performing a strange ritual.”*

*My heart tightened.*

*“When I looked closely, it was a sacrificial rite. I was desperate and elated, wondering what sacrifice they were offering to stop this, what price they were paying to save my clients… waiting for its completion with hope—”*

*“The sacrifices… were my clients.”*

*“They piled countless people living in this city onto the ritual altar. No, not just people—every living thing, even blades of grass on the surface. And then, then…”*

*…….*

*“May 4th began again.”*

*A flash of realization pierced my dull mind.*

*“Ah.”*

*The day repeating wasn’t… part of a calamity-class supernatural disaster.*

*“It was part of the sealing ritual….”*

*Then there were probably two reasons the day kept repeating…*

*To prevent spread.*

*And…*

*To continuously revive and supply living sacrifices to maintain the seal.*

*Segwang Special City was offering itself as a sacrifice every day, like a self-generating facility sustaining its own containment…*

*Trapped in May 4th.*

*“Ah.”*

*A chilling cold settled into my body.*

*Not from blood loss.*

*“So…”*

*…….*

*“All my clients… everyone living in Gwangjin…”*

*The counselor’s voice echoed hollowly across the platform.*

*…the agents’ voices were gone now.*

*As if the already-collapsed platform had been sealed with talismans, all screams, noise, and voices vanished—as if a switch had been flipped.*

*Silence.*

*But—*

*A strange, bubbling presence settled in.*

*“No….”*

*I struggled to lift my head.*

*“Every day I’ll die miserably. Every day I’ll burn to death watching it happen. I threw tens—hundreds of thousands of lives in as bait instead of saving them. You deceived me with empty words, sweet lies, and made me—”*

Thud.

*I slammed my head.*

*“…!”*

Thud, thud….

*“Agent….”*

*I let my head drop against the concrete.*

*I had no strength left to move.*

*But—*

*“Wait… just a moment….”*

*Soon, I felt movement—unsteady, solitary vibrations.*

*Hands clearing the debris above me.*

*It took an agonizingly long time.*

*The heavy weight shifted little by little, nearly slipping, yet never stopping—*

*And then—*

Crash.

*The slab pressing down on my head disappeared.*

*Light poured in—*

*But I couldn’t see.*

*“…….”*

*Had I injured my head badly?*

*All I felt was someone gently patting my head, as if soothing me.*

*“It’s okay. It’ll be okay….”*

*With his remaining hand, he stroked my head like one would comfort the injured.*

Drip. Drip.

*The sensation of hot liquid—like blood—falling onto my forehead rang through my skull…*

*“…Did you come from outside… to help me?”*

*I barely twitched my fingers.*

*“Thank you….”*

*“…….”*

*“This city has been abandoned. I couldn’t protect it… I’m just a fox being offered up alongside it.”*

*A whisper trembling with rage and despair followed.*

Splat.

*Something collapsed.*

*No.*

*“As a counselor… I have to speak.”*

*I barely raised my arm and tapped him.*

*“Ah—ah….”*

*“…….”*

*“Don’t worry.”*

*He tried to prop my upper body onto his leg.*

*“Even so, you will go outside.”*

*…….*

*…What?*

*“Those from outside have a way back. It’s fine if you can’t move. I’ll push you…. I’ll attach a small fox, just like helping someone slip out through the counseling office’s back door.”*

Splat.

*“A counselor can always see off a client whose session has ended.”*

*A laugh.*

*“Of course, an ordinary counselor couldn’t do that. Haha….”*

Splat.

*The sensation of his hand on my head vanished.*

*Something melted, slid down my cheek, and disappeared onto the floor.*

*Now only a voice remained in the air.*

*The faint voice of the counselor.*

*“Before your breath stops and you’re placed on the sacrificial altar—before you’re trapped in this May 4th—I’ll send you back to where you belong. Your workplace.”*

Click.

*The sound of a doorknob turning.*

*Then, suddenly, the sensation of the floor supporting my back vanished—as if a door had opened behind me.*

*My body fell into it.*

*Through the door the counselor had opened.*

*“Goodbye.”*

*I felt the paws of a small furry animal wrap around my waist—*

*And we fell together into the void……*

*…….*

*“Return in peace, my client.”*

*That was the last thing.*

*****

*“…Ah.”*

*I slowly regained consciousness.*

*The pain throughout my body was gone as if it had never existed, but an unnaturally heavy stillness weighed on my heart.*

*“So… that’s how it was.”*

*New understanding filled my mind.*

*The Cheerful Research Institute, the true nature of the disaster, the sealing ritual……*

*Ho Yuwon.*

*“…….”*

*At the final moment, Ho Yuwon had nearly lost all form.*

*Perhaps because he abandoned the safe identity of “counselor” and used his true, uncanny power to send me out…*

*I thought of the small fox that felt as if it should still be clinging to my waist, but the sensation was already gone.*

*“…Get up.”*

*There was no time to dwell.*

*I got out—but what about the other three… Section Chief Lee Jaheon, Assistant Manager Eun Haje, Agent Bronze?*

*“Please… let the director have led them out safely.”*

*Please let us all have woken up outside together.*

*“First—open my eyes.”*

*Then the fake sunlight of the Fox Counseling Office would greet me.*

*I exhaled and looked ahead—*

*“Ah. You’re conscious.”*

*…….*

*I looked straight ahead.*

*A concrete wall.*

*The wall of Daydream’s underground security isolation chamber.*

*And leaning against it, smiling cheerfully at me—*

*The director.*

*“130666.”*

*Director Cheong Dallae.*

*“…!!”*

*What—what is this—*

—I’ll send you back to your workplace.

*Wait.*

*Wait—!*

*No, no—*

*“You’ve finally returned. Your absence was rather long.”*

*As if all the blood drained from my body, I staggered back—*

*No, no—*

*“And you look terribly frail. You even lost your body.”*

*The director waved her hand.*

*My contract was in it—wait—*

*“Hm. It would be better to return you to the form you had when we signed the contract. For work.”*

*My form began to melt—*

*No, no—*

*It’s not melting, it’s not melting! I’m human! Hold on—*

*“You shouldn’t hold back. Returning to your original form makes it easier to inhabit the security suit. Without a body, you can use the suit as one.”*

*No.*

*No—*

*“Now.”*

*I had to stand and find the security suit—*

*No—refuse—*

*“Refuse? That’s not in line with the contract.”*

*130666 is assigned to Director Ho’s project! I’m still on that project—*

*“Oh dear. There’s no longer a place for you on that project.”*

*…….*

*What?*

*“Director Ho started running that project with a different team last year.”*

*…Last year?*

*Then how much time has passed—*

*“You and several others were deployed on a long-term mission, so you were removed from active use….”*

*Director Cheong looked at me.*

*The one who casually killed hundreds of thousands, burned the Cheerful Research Institute, took the Dream Incubator—*

*Clear, overwhelming violence.*

*A being who thinks only of its own profit.*

*An evil made more terrible because it is understandable gazed at me in delight.*

*“Seems there were… other circumstances?”*

*…….*

*“I can feel your fear.”*

*A cheerful voice.*

*“So? Did you find something interesting in the Special City? About me?”*

*…!!*

*“Then you must know I don’t do things that lose me money. Including with you.”*

*…….*

*“Now. Get up. Put on the security suit.”*

*I tried to rise.*

*Then realized—*

*I had no body.*

*I was… a bodiless consciousness that infiltrated Segwang Special City through dreams and escaped….*

*“You’ll have to take it out yourself.”*

*I moved forward, toward the cabinet set into the concrete wall—*

*And tried to shoot through the wall into the corridor.*

*“Oh dear. You thought you could do that without a body?”*

*Ah.*

*“Security walls are conceptually isolated. Otherwise, how could we contain supernatural phenomena?”*

*…….*

*“Unless someone lets you out, there’s no other way.”*

*I turned to the director.*

*“Clever, but unfortunate. I’ll have to discipline you once you suit up. How about killing a child?”*

*Please—*

*…I approached the cabinet, unable to do anything else.*

*My trembling hand opened it.*

*The bizarre security employee 130666, exerting physical force without a body, shoved his head inside—*

*…….*

*I heard a chime.*

*—Found you.*

*I was dragged into the cabinet.*

*A crushing pressure pulled me upward, tore me free from Daydream’s security zone.*

*Alarms blared behind me.*

*But in an instant, I was seized by something enormous, caught like prey by the nape of the neck—*

*Transported.*

*Somewhere.*

*“Grape.”*

*“…….”*

*“Grape.”*

*I opened my eyes.*

*I turned my head and looked at my arm.*

*It was there.*

*I looked at my hand.*

*It was there.*

*It moved.*

*I had… a human body….*

*“…….”*

*But just now—just now…!*

*“Huff—huff…!”*

*“It’s okay.”*

*Matching the rhythm of the hand patting my back, I struggled to calm my breathing.*

*“It’s over. You’re safe….”*

*I steadied myself.*

*“…….”*

*“Grape.”*

*I looked up.*

*…Agent Choi.*

*Pale-faced, the Hyunmu Team One agent smiled faintly and set the ritual bell in his hand aside.*

*Blood splattered from the bell.*

*A trembling voice spoke.*

“Good to see you. …It’s been 293 days.”
