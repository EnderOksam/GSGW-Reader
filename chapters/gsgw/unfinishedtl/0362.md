# Chapter 361

The agent with the scar on his neck silently stared at the school counselor inside the monorail train.

“…You have the qualification.”

“Yes.”

The smile that had seemed about to twist into ecstasy on the counselor’s face straightened again.

“Did you perhaps forget? I’m from Segwang Special City.”

“……”

“And since I’ve signed a contract with Mascot-nim, there could hardly be anyone more of a stakeholder here than me. Ah, thank you.”

The last words were directed at the mascot, who was offering him a souvenir sticker.

…A deer? A cat?

In any case, the mascot costume—something you’d obviously see at an amusement park—waddled forward to the front of the train and waved as if welcoming the four of them, escorting them along.

Looking closer, it was dressed like a conductor.

“D-do we just get on?”

“Yes.”

Researcher Lee Jaheon boarded without hesitation.

The mascot circled him as if strangely delighted, then seemed to remember its role as conductor and cleared its throat, stopping.

As Reporter Eun Haje muttered something like, “Brat’s kind of cute,” and stepped aboard, Ryu Jaekwan hurried onto the monorail after her.

♩♪♬♬~ #r♩♬♬r# ~ #r♩♪♪r#

The golden monorail set off cheerfully.

Ivory-colored beautiful track walls and a map of the theme park painted on them slid past the window.

…Cheerful Theme Park?

Ryu Jaekwan was turning his head to look when he met the counselor’s eyes and snapped back to his senses.

“Um… sir.”

“……”

The counselor gazed at the high school student before speaking.

“Rather than ‘sir,’ Ho Yuwon would be better.”

“Huh? I—I can’t just call a teacher by name…”

Ho Yuwon’s gaze drifted over Ryu Jaekwan’s uniform.

“…Are you a Segwang High student?”

“Huh? Y-Yes…”

Silence.

Why ask something so obvious…?

Just as Ryu Jaekwan stiffened at the strange sense of incongruity—

“…I see.”

Ho Yuwon smiled quietly at him.

“Even when we know something isn’t true, having our hearts deceived by what’s immediately before our eyes is a very human response, isn’t it?”

“…?”

“It’s fascinating… and strange.”

The scarred agent watched with wary eyes, but Ho Yuwon simply looked at the high schooler in the Segwang High uniform.

…As though looking at something dearly missed.

And smiling, he said, “I hope you have a happy resort trip—and that you return safely. …Student Jaekwan.”

The last words sounded slightly awkward.

As if imitating something unfamiliar, or saying it for the first time in a very long while.

♩♪♬♬~ #r♩♬♬r# ~ #r♩♪♪r#

“Oh, looks like we’ve arrived.”

The monorail stopped, revealing a scene like a train station from a classic fairy tale.

The conductor mascot stepped outside and once again escorted the passengers.

When Ryu Jaekwan looked back at Ho Yuwon, he was still smiling.

“Please go ahead. I’ll get off last.”

“…Thank you.”

Ryu Jaekwan bowed reflexively and stepped out.

Behind him, the agent and Ho Yuwon exchanged a look.

Unaware of the strange atmosphere at his back, Ryu Jaekwan stepped off the train.

A young woman’s bright voice rang out as she spoke with Researcher Lee.

“I’m so glad you’re safe!”

“Yes.”

“And also…”

A small figure in a business suit wearing a dolphin mask suddenly turned toward him.

“Oh! Agent-nim.”

Agent-nim?

Ryu Jaekwan was startled, but quickly reasoned they must be referring to the agent behind him.

And yet, he couldn’t shake the feeling that the gaze behind that dolphin mask had been directed at him.

Why?

“You rescued those two?”

“Yes.”

“It only counts as rescue if they get home safely! So I hope these two civilians can find a safe way out.”

The dolphin mask smoothly turned away and continued speaking to someone else, and the strange feeling faded.

Everyone seemed to know one another.

And the conversation was clearly about him.

Can we really escape Segwang Special City through this resort?

“Really? You could even stay and enjoy a day! This resort seems extremely safe for kind people as long as they follow the rules, hmm…”

But after giving Reporter Eun Haje and Ryu Jaekwan another once-over, the dolphin mask nodded.

“Yep. It’d be best if you return to where you originally were.”

“Yes. I’ll speak with the assigned mascot.”

“Yesss! Oh, I think it’s that one!”

Researcher Lee strode toward the mascot indicated.

At the foot of the stairs stood a brown podium decorated with a dazzling crystal flower tree, like a reception desk.

The mascot, dressed like a manager with a vest and pocket watch, even tried to shake Ryu Jaekwan’s hand like someone at an amusement park.

“I-It’s okay.”

The mascot’s ears drooped slightly, as if deflated. Ryu Jaekwan felt an inexplicable guilt.

“Mm. According to Mascot-nim, the way out for you two is already prepared!”

“Really? Then let’s confirm it~”

The adults discussed matters Ryu Jaekwan couldn’t quite follow, tension hidden beneath their casual tone.

Then he noticed Ho Yuwon quietly approaching the desk mascot.

“You’re not Roe Deer-nim either.”

……

“He isn’t at this resort, is he? You’re only a part of him.”

The mascot nodded.

“Just like me.”

Ho Yuwon still wore a calm smile.

“Jaekwan! They’ll guide us!”

“Ah! Yes!”

Ryu Jaekwan hurried after the others.

Ho Yuwon, however, did not follow.

Ryu Jaekwan had no idea their arrival had conveniently diverted attention away from Ho Yuwon.

Still—

He felt something.

After hesitating, he bowed his head slightly.

Ho Yuwon’s eyes flickered faintly before he raised a hand in greeting.

Like a teacher would.

……

“Do we just go up these stairs?”

“Yes, yes.”

The scarred agent called out, “From here on, I’ll accompany them alone. The rest of you handle your tasks!”

Thus the group was reduced again.

They climbed ivory stairs lined with carpet.

Instead of the original waiting hall, at the top—

“Whoa.”

An exquisitely crafted indoor garden.

Glass flowers, golden branches, yellow blossoms glimmering among green leaves.

“How big is this place…?”

The hedges formed a maze, impossible to measure.

The mascot led them confidently to the outer path and pointed at a modest but charming brick house.

A plaque hung on the back door beneath a crystal lamp:

╔════════════════════╗

For Staff Leaving Work

Authorized Personnel Only

No Entry

╚════════════════════╝

“Um, it says no entry…”

“…Mm. It says it’s fine.”

The mascot thumped its chest as if to say “Follow me,” and opened the door.

Inside was a neat brown wooden corridor.

On a chalkboard:

╔════════════════════╗

See you next time!

– GOLDEN

╚════════════════════╝

The mascot entered first, holding the door.

But as the agent tried to step in last, the mascot stopped him.

“Ah.”

The agent halted.

“…Seems I can’t go any further. But the mascot will go with you.”

A faint, bitter smile crossed his face.

“You see that door at the end? Once you exit the resort through there, you’ll return to where you originally were.”

……

“Stay healthy, okay?”

Ryu Jaekwan suddenly felt anxious.

“Um, after we return… how can we thank you agents?”

“……”

“…Ah, just call!”

The agent pulled out a piece of paper and scribbled a number.

“I’ll give you this. It’s rare—direct line to headquarters.”

He grinned.

“If you say you escaped a supernatural disaster with an agent’s help, they’ll guide you kindly.”

His voice was steady.

“They’ll check for aftereffects or other issues, too. Even if it’s not just to say thanks, make sure you call. Got it?”

“Yes. Thank you…!”

“Hey, it’s nothing.”

Beyond the smiling agent’s face, Ryu Jaekwan remembered the other agent who saved him—the one who arrived on a bicycle.

The relief then.

The warmth now.

“…Um. Is there any way I could work there, too?”

“…!”

“I want to rescue people from disasters, too… like you.”

“….”

“Of course.”

His voice sounded tight for a moment.

Then he chuckled.

“But you need to grow a bit first.”

“Huh…!?”

“Not height—age! You have to be an adult to take the exam. Got it? So grow up fast!”

“Ah, yes.”

“I’ll be waiting. Future junior agent!”

“…Yes!”

“Then go on! They’re waiting.”

“Thank you… thank you!”

Ryu Jaekwan bowed once more and followed the mascot.

He wasn’t afraid.

There were three of them, and the mascot was kind.

To experience something like this.

It would be unforgettable.

Not entirely in a bad way.

He reached the final door and turned the handle.

Click.

Through a doorway that revealed nothing, the three who had been trapped in Segwang Special City stepped out.

And disappeared.

Agent Choi watched his past junior return to his own time.

“……”

“They’re gone?”

“Ma’am.”

Agent Haegum appeared.

“…He won’t remember.”

……

“If only his mind was summoned here, he’ll likely treat it as a nightmare. He has a sensitive temperament, so he might think it was real—but the details will fade.”

The name of the place.

The agents’ appearances.

Identifiable specifics—all forgotten.

“But he’ll remember being caught in a supernatural disaster.”

“……”

“And that an agent saved him. He’ll call that number.”

Agent Choi let out a damp laugh.

“In the end, I’ll get a random call, laugh at the kid who can’t even remember the disaster properly, and assume some future agent saved him. And Team Leader Hong will probably introduce him to Leejeong Bookstore.”

And later—

“That high schooler will become a fine agent and meet you at the mountain lodge.”

“……”

“Grapes.”

Choi’s voice cracked as he looked at the returning mascot.

“The seal is lifting in real time. There’s really not much time left.”

……

“If you want to stop me from interfering in the sealing ritual, at least let me help you. Make me help you with everything I’ve got. That was the promise.”

He looked at the mascot.

“If you can hear me, let me out.”

……

“I’ll… do everything I can to help you.”

“How unfortunate.”

Ho Yuwon, who had been faintly listening from below the stairs, spoke to the mascot.

“When conditions aren’t met, that’s how one loses their role.”

The mascot looked at him.

“Let me tell you a secret.”

Ho Yuwon whispered, “I know where you are.”

……

“It doesn’t require any mystical ability. It’s simple fact. You’re on the City Hall rooftop, aren’t you? Installing the machine.”

His gentle counselor’s tone gained resolve.

“And I can reach Segwang Special City through the staff-only surface entrance…”

……

“So I’m coming to find you.”

“And in this moment, I’ll do what is most like myself.”

***

I turned around.

Ho Yuwon.

The being wearing the counselor’s face stood there.

But his form was trembling.

Like me—melting rapidly.

Because this is a ghost story.

“Go back—”

No. It’s faster if I destroy the being in the cocoon now.

Then everything will be fine.

This whole mess—I tried to steady my tangled body somewhere between the yellow mascot and the masked Roe Deer and tear Kim Soleum free—

“I asked whether you’d considered another method.”

There’s no time. I’m going insane. There’s no time, and why are you saying this now—

“I’m sorry. I keep speaking this way. Occupational hazard of a counselor, I suppose.”

He approaches.

Why—

“Client-nim. I’m finally standing here. In Segwang Special City.”

His face flickered with ecstasy, then returned to calm.

“Do you remember what I said in our first session?”

His gentle voice spoke, “When you’re seized by powerful impulses or ideas you never wanted, it’s unbearably painful.”

……

“And it’s the counselor’s role to help you escape that in the proper way.”

His gaze turned to Kim Soleum in the cocoon.

More precisely—

To everything hanging beneath.

The citizens of Segwang Special City.

Scared.

“I’ve always recommended regular counseling and long-term hospitalization to those who can’t endure themselves or their circumstances.”

…Wait.

“Indefinite hospitalization. Until recovery.”

Wait—don’t tell me.

“And now.”

He crouched.

“I think I’ve met the clients I truly wished to recommend that to. People who need my recommendation more than ever.”

That—

“It’ll be a very long group session. Perhaps it will require my entire life as a counselor.”

If you do that—

“But it feels worthwhile. As though I were prepared for this very moment.”

“……”

“Roe Deer-nim.”

The nearly melted beings looked at each other.

Counselor and Good Friend.

“Will you consent to counseling?”

Ho Yuwon dragged his half-melted body closer and placed a nameplate in my hand.

“Unless the client—or their guardian—opens the door themselves, counseling cannot begin.”

……

“Because counseling requires consent. It cannot be forced.”

I realized.

Kim Soleum in the cocoon, and the citizens clinging to him—

I qualify as both client and guardian.

“I’m asking you.”

……

“You may have noticed, but while I’ve been talking, you’ve melted quite a bit, too. You don’t look like you have the strength to tear even one person from that cocoon anymore. Giving up might be easier.”

Ha.

“Don’t look at me so frighteningly. I’m rather timid…”

He wore the same annoyingly timid expression I knew.

Director Ho.

…My eyes stung.

“……”

“Quickly. Before we melt further and die together.”

He smiled.

“Let’s try the best method. Roe Deer-nim. That’s why you came this far, isn’t it?”

He grasped me weakly.

“Instead of turning away, try recovery—just as you once wished.”

“……”

“And…”

Ho Yuwon said, “Please introduce to the surviving counselor Ho Yuwon the clients he truly wanted to counsel.”

……

I accepted the nameplate.

Overwhelmed by unbearable emotion, I staggered to the rooftop emergency door.

And hung the nameplate on it.

Knock. Knock.

Grinding my teeth, I pulled the handle.

“Well done. …Thank you.”

The view of the Fox Counseling Room poured out onto the rooftop.
