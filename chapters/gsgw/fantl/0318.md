# Chapter 317 - One-Day Researcher, I

I took a shower for the first time in a long while.

I mean, it had been a long time since I’d showered *in my original body.*

My body stayed in one piece, not melting or spilling apart.

The feeling of hot water running across my skin.

I was myself.

“......”

I changed my clothes.

It felt a little awkward to wear something other than the Security Team uniform for the first time in a while.

A clean sense of reality—neither a temporary body nor a dream sparked by sleep.

The sensation of breathing.

*Hoo.*

...It was refreshing.

Feeling lighter, I opened the door of the hospital room and stepped out.

In the waiting area of the Fox Counseling Room, people who had already prepared themselves were waiting.

“Sorry I’m late. I was washing up.”

Now that I thought about it, it was surprising that this counseling room even had a shower room... No, actually, it’d be more of a horror story if a hospital room didn’t have one.

The sensation of sunlight warming my face.

And I really was.

Then he hugged me, as if to congratulate me.

And I saw Agent Choi secretly dowsing me to check for contamination, confirming I was clear before putting it away.

This guy is seriously consistent...

*I barely used it... maybe I could get a refund—*

Besides, I was already grateful... Even for a few days, I got to talk to people and eat meat.

Anyway, I shook my head with a smile.

“I’ll be fine now. From here on.”

As long as I keep accepting myself, I’ll be able to sit here as Kim Soleum.

'If you don’t like it, fire me.'
