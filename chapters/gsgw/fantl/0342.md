# Chapter 341 - Sekwang Special City Disaster, VII

I swallowed hard.

The fierce, ominous face of the Lion Mask was staring straight at me.

Its two yellow, sharp eyes moved inside the mask as if they were alive on their own.

“Did you know? There are many regional legends about Lion Masks... and one of them is that they drive away evil spirits.”

“......”

The mane of the Lion Mask spilled over my shoulder.

It looked as if, at any moment, the mask might open its snout, lined with rows of sharp teeth.

“Wouldn’t harming civilians after being bewitched by Kkwangcheol count as the work of an evil spirit too?”

I saw that chilling thing draw closer.

Watching it...

“...No.”

I answered.

“I have no intention of impersonating an agent to cause trouble during a disaster of this scale.”

“Oh ho?”

“And you know I’m innocent too, Agent.”

“Why do you think that?”

The Lion Mask tilted its head, the mane sliding down my back.

It felt cold and strange.

A bead of cold sweat ran down the back of my neck.

‘I need to sort this out properly.’

Suppressing my anxiety as much as I could, I spoke calmly.

“You wouldn’t have lent me agent equipment if you thought I was guilty.”

“...!”

“If you were suspicious, you wouldn’t have given me gear that helps with escaping. Isn’t that right?”

I barely turned my eyes to check my companions.

Thinking it was just ‘a conversation between agents,’ the two of them were looking at the surrounding devastation rather than at us, without much suspicion.

“If you really can distinguish evil by scent, Agent, then you’d know I have no malice or guilt.”

Perhaps after seeing the people I was trying to rescue and how I treated them, the agent had judged me to be sincere.

‘Please.’

Trying to release the tension that made my hands feel like they were curling in on themselves, I continued.

“It’s true that I signed a contract with Daydream. But that has nothing to do with what I’m about to do in this situation.”

“......”

The Lion Mask swayed, as if peering deep into my soul.

“What are you going to do?”

“As I said, I’ll rescue those two people. There are people I have to save. And...”

After hesitating, I said it.

“If possible, I want to end this disaster.”

They fell silent.

Then the mask shook—

“Your words are sincere!”

I gasped for breath.

In the meantime, the agent—having regained its playful movements—grabbed my arm.

“Then I’ll leave the rescue to you, fellow agent!”

“...Yes.”

Phew.

Watching them return to their friendly-agent demeanor, I let out a sigh of relief inwardly.

But tension still clung to my body.

...They still hadn’t let go of my arm.

‘...Strangely enough, it feels like I’m facing an entity straight out of a ghost story.’

Was it because of that Lion Mask? I could barely feel any humanity from that agent.

The lion-masked agent tilted their head amiably.

Yet they stayed close, never releasing my arm.

As if ready to drag me away at any moment.

“By the way, how did that vicious Kkwangcheol end up coiling itself around your body, Agent?”

“......”

I don’t even know who Mr. Kkwangcheol is, but...

‘One of the Daydream directors?’

But here, I can’t say things like—

$p
*— I signed contracts with two development directors, one of whom went insane after being eaten by a local cooperative spirit. Oh, and before that I also signed an employment contract, so I might be contracted with another director from a different department too. Like octopus legs, you know?*

...I swallowed those words and summarized vaguely instead.

“You mean how I came to contract with Daydream?”

“That’s right!”

In that case, there was a simple, concise answer.

“I was deceived.”

“Ah.”

“And I’m looking for a way out.”

The agent seemed lost in thought, and soon the mask’s white mane fluttered upward.

“Hm. So you’re on leave so as not to burden the Bureau?”

“Not only that... honestly, it’s also for my own safety.”

Because I was suspected of being a spy.

But I really wasn’t, so it felt unfair...

“Of course, that makes sense!”

Fortunately, they responded cheerfully. Phew.

Letting out a relieved breath, I looked at the remaining stations.

...About eight minutes left.

‘So we’ll reach Sekwang Station soon.’

If agents detained me now and delayed me any further, I might not be able to find the Counselor at Sekwang Station.

Then I’d lose everything.

‘I need to ease the atmosphere quickly...’

But suddenly, a cold voice reached my ear.

“That’s strange too.”

“...Yes?”

“Then how did a fellow agent on leave learn about the disaster and enter the city to rescue someone?”

“...!”

“The disaster hasn’t even been half a day old—barely a few hours, really. Yet you’ve already found civilians to rescue and are here!”

“......”

“Even if news spread outside, it’s still far too soon. Quite a coincidence!”

It was a sharp question.

And a precise one.

‘The timeline doesn’t match.’

I was already in this city the moment the disaster alert arrived.

It would take quite a while for agents from other cities to enter Sekwang.

So saying ‘I entered because there was a rescue request’ wouldn’t hold up. Chronologically, it was impossible.

...I need time to think of an answer. But there’s no time. Damn it!

‘I need to counterattack...’

As I turned my thoughts over, I noticed something.

The agents visible in the car ahead.

...!

“Yes.”

I spoke calmly.

“You’re not from the Sekwang City branch either, are you?”

“...Fellow agent. You have remarkably sharp eyes!”

“Thank you.”

But they didn’t waver.

“I’m just here on temporary support duty for this branch! Of course I was deployed with them!”

Phew.

Cleverly.

I didn’t say they were lent.

“How odd. That cooperative spirit must have known you were suspicious!”

“I don’t know about that either. But I have never lied.”

“Then let’s go back to the main point and find out your true intentions!”

That strangely swaying mane poured over my shoulder once more.

“How exactly did you know a disaster would happen today, and how did you promptly choose someone to rescue and end up here?”

I thought this Lion Mask felt almost monstrous...

‘Lacking humanity...’
