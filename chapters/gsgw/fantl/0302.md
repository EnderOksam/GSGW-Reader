Within the bizarre home shopping channel that was playing, the host spoke.

With the face of her colleague, Kim Soleum.

$p

'This is... home shopping?'

No, she wasn't even sure it could still be called home shopping anymore.

'Isn't this just a variety show?'

If she had to classify it... yeah.

A talk show.

Listening to all sorts of people's stories. Laughing with them and crying with them. And giving gifts to those who participated in the interview...

The format was almost stereotypically familiar.

But clichés became clichés because they worked.

And this show had its own twist.

$p
#f# *— Hellooooo? Hellooooohellohellohello... kikikikikikikik* #f#

The people connecting through the phone were not always human.

Sometimes the eerie creaking of wooden floorboards echoed through the call. Sometimes the screams of dying people could be heard in the background.

The callers spoke in ways that made no sense. Studio lights would flicker or suddenly turn red.

Kim Soleum always made sure to hide the caller ID. Yet every now and then, impossibly long strings of numbers or bizarre phrases flashed across the screen for an instant.

Whenever that happened, Go Yeongeun's heart turned cold.

Even now.

Kim Soleum had won.
