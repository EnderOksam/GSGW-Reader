# Chapter 371 - The Homecoming

“.......”

Holding my breath, I stared at the staircase leading down below.

It was the way back.

Those dark stairs stretched deep beneath the box, and at their end....

Was the place I would return to.

$Brd
[Oh, you’ve finally achieved your long-standing goal. Congratulations, my *friend*!]
Brd$

...!

“Braun!”

$Brd
[Indeed, Mr. Roe Deer. You’ve found the path. And fortunately, it seems our reception has improved as well!]
Brd$

My mind, which had been quiet, now heard applause and the scraping sound of someone tapping the antenna above the TV screen.

‘Once again, I had become unable to hear that voice...’

Just like when I was 130666, right after I made the contract with Director Cheong.

A mass reduced to nothing but the capacity for thought couldn’t perceive the Good Friend. Maybe... it was because I was in a state where I couldn’t make friends.

I felt nauseous when I remembered my previously crushed state, but more than that, my happiness at hearing him again came first.

Not being alone is always reassuring.

Even if the one beside me is a TV-headed grand host who wants to turn me into a talk show staff member... we’ve been together a long time.

The words came naturally.

The first thought that flashed through my mind was about the membrane surrounding me.

“Did I... enter the egg that was inside the Dream Incubator’s tank?”

$Brd
[Hmm. An intriguing and clever guess! Whether it’s true or not, isn’t this the place that the bottle of syrup from the incubator created to grant your wish?]
Brd$

...My wish.

Reflexively, I looked back at the staircase inside the box.

Even at the paper sign that read, ‘The Way Back, \<Darkness Exploration Records\> Pop-up Store.’

It was then.

$p
– Agent Grapes?
p$

...!

A faint voice came from beyond the membrane.

“Agent Haegeum!”

I called out instinctively toward it.

“I’m safe. I’m okay!”

$p
– Good!

The sounds outside were slightly chaotic, but completely muffled. Only faint voices reached my ears, frustratingly blocked off.

$p
– Has anything changed?
p$

“Pardon? I... there’s a path here.”

$p
– A path?
p$

“Yes. ...It’s the way back home.”

.......

$p
After a brief silence, the voice returned.
p$

$p
– Does it look safe?
p$

“I can’t be certain. But judging from the circumstances....”

The original Wish Ticket. A complete ‘wish-granting syrup’ containing five million Dream Essence points had activated properly.

“I think... I might be able to return home safely.”

$p
– .......
p$

A hoarse voice replied.

$p
– And I suppose you want to take that path.
p$

I steadied my breath.

“...Yes.”

$p
– Alright. Understood.
p$

And then, the surroundings seemed to grow a little noisy again.

Was it possible that Yeongeun-ssi or the Sergeant was trying to say something? It was frustrating not being able to hear them clearly.

‘I want to talk to them.’

I spoke while pushing against the membrane.

“Excuse me, are the others here? I’d like to at least say hello....”

But.

$p
– No. It would be better not to.

p$
“...Pardon?”

$p
– The egg is getting smaller.
p$

Ah.

I realized it.

The membrane surrounding me was gradually shrinking.

As if it would get smaller and smaller until it collapsed, or until I broke through it....

$p
– It’s been happening since we started talking.

– It seems interaction with the outside is affecting it. It would be safest to minimize it.
p$

“.......”

Naturally, I understood.

The “Children’s Paradise Syrup” had given me time to choose.

Either break through that shell and get out.

Or return home.

“.......”

I looked at the stairs.

$p
– Agent Grapes. Have you decided on your path?
p$

“...Yes.”

I said the goal I had repeated countless times.

The wish these people had tried to grant for me.

“I want... to go home.”

$p
– ...Alright. Understood.
p$

Agent Haegeum’s voice came through.

$p
– In that case, don’t look back for no reason. Just keep walking straight ahead.

– You’re bound to get lost if you look back.
p$

“...Yes.”

I nodded, taking a deep breath.

The membrane shrank even further.

*'Ah.'*

There wasn’t much time.

Hurriedly, I said, “Thank you!”

Yes.

“Could you please tell everyone... that I was truly, deeply grateful?”

I could have met my end in this resort.

Adding one more to the list of missing persons in a ghost story, leaving behind a creepy exploration log ending on the wiki.

I was grateful that they prevented that from happening.

“I’ve received so much help until now.”

To my team superiors, who helped a new employee who appeared and disappeared unpredictably.

To my batchmates.

To the Security Team Sergeant.

To Agent 007, whom I met through a secondhand transaction.

To... a certain agent who, despite doubting me until the very end, answered my call for rescue.

“Thanks to you all, I’m returning home safely from a Supernatural Disaster.”

$p
– .......

– I will definitely tell them.
p$

“......Yes.”

A lump of heat rose in my throat.

In that moment, Agent Haegeum spoke slowly, as if reciting.

As if she were relaying the words of someone standing beside her, someone who had been in the room for a while but whose presence she hadn’t announced to me.

$p
– Please return safely, Mr. Citizen.
p$

And that was the end.

No more sound came from beyond the membrane.

“.......”

I stood and examined myself.

...I’m wearing a black suit.

Just like....

$Brd
[The same appearance as when you began, Mr. Roe Deer!]
Brd$

“.......”

$Brd
[Oh, are you pondering how to say farewell to this Braun as well?]

“...!”

$Brd
[Hmm. A splendid closing line is every entertainer’s dilemma! But since we are *friends*, there’s no need to fret over something so trivial....]

“...Are you really okay with it ending like this?”

$Brd
[Hmm?]
Brd$

The Good Friend said he’d follow me anywhere.

He’d watched and narrated my every move as if broadcasting, lent me a ridiculous sum of money, and then tossed out advice only at the most critical moments....

I said it even though I knew there was no need to invite trouble by saying such things.

And....

[Ah, Mr. Roe Deer.]
Brd$

$Brd

“...To some extent.”

[What an answer! Your drive for improvement truly shines through. Not satisfied, but still enjoyed yourself. Excellent!]

[In that sense, it seems it’s about time to wrap up this shoot.]

I kept walking.

The things I was leaving behind.

Stories still in progress.

*Step.*

I was getting farther and farther away from the story that had once unfolded with me in it.

People I wanted to talk to more, whose futures I wanted to know, came to mind....

It felt like there was still so much left to do.

I wanted to see.

p$

*Step.*
