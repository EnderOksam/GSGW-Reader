# Chapter 343 - Sekwang Special City Disaster, IX

“......”

In the first place.

What was the reason I had continued to explore this dangerous Sekwang Special City?

It was because of a vague sense of expectation.

The hope that if I dug into this massive, secret-filled ghost story, I might reach the fundamental truth of this world.

And...

The hope that then, I might understand why I was going through this.

That I might discover the cause or reason behind being suddenly thrown into the world of Darkness Exploration Records and forced to suffer through incomprehensible hardship...

I just realized that those expectations had been rewarded.

In a direction I had never anticipated.

‘They were trying to invite me here...’

The Cheerful Research Institute had tried to summon me, a person from a “safe world without ghost stories,” simply to prove its existence...

And because of that, the disaster in Sekwang Special City occurred.

My heart turned ice-cold.

‘Then... the deaths of hundreds of thousands in Sekwang Special City and the sealing of the city are eventually related to me...’

...No! I shouldn’t delude myself.

‘Did I ask them to summon me?’

Absolutely not.

I was caught up in this just like any other citizen of Sekwang City. Caught up in the insane experiments of the organizations in this damn ghost story world...

Caught up, and of all people, it was me.

“......”

A pain like a sharp pang surged from my chest to my head.

It was fury.

Barely managing to exhale softly, I looked at the researcher from the Cheerful Research Institute.

My words came out strangely dry.

In a tone befitting an agent.

“So, in the end, you’re saying you tried to summon someone from a world without supernatural phenomena? A preposterous claim. And... you failed, didn’t you?”

“......”

“An entire metropolis turned into a ghost story. Why did you do something like this when you had no ability to take responsibility for it? Where did that confidence come from?”

“We didn’t do this. It wasn’t supposed to fail. This attempt—”

“‘Wasn’t supposed to fail’? What kind of irresponsible statement is that? If you’re a research institute, it’s only natural to prepare for the possibility of failure.”

Researcher Lee Heoun’s face grew even paler.

Fine, then.

“How are you going to clean this up? Where are the other researchers right now?”

“They evacuated... because of the fire. And there was definitely a contingency plan. But it doesn’t work now. We were deceived too. We—”

“What kind of irresponsible nonsense is that?”

“Indeed it is.”

...!

I snapped my head around.

A member of the group I had forgotten was quietly speaking.

The Counselor, his face devoid of expression.

“I think so too. This is unbelievably irresponsible.”

“......”

“I certainly received a promise from them.”

The air twisted strangely.

“That on the day their efforts succeeded, this city would become a place safer for people than anywhere else on earth.”

Lee Heoun gave no answer.

No—he couldn’t.

I saw him clutch at his own throat.

‘...!’

In that fantastical space filled with colorful toys, he rolled on the floor with choking sounds.

However, the Counselor smiled at me and explained, as if kindly providing instructions before a session.

“I have lived in this region, once called Gwangjin and now Sekwang Special City, for a very long time. I used to harm people here and there, but ever since I grew fond of them and gained reason, I have watched over and protected them. That’s why those words sounded quite pleasing to me.”

His previously composed, rational tone grew softer, gentler, and more melodic.

As if he were returning to his true form.

“Because the people from the Bureau who followed those researchers also gave their word, I gave up the ground beneath this land and listened to their requests. Then, one day, I heard a desperate plea.”

“...What kind of request?”

“A request to borrow my mysterious bead for just a moment.”

At the gesture of the Counselor, or rather the spirit-being, a bright, round sphere floated in the air above his finger.

It glowed with a red, five-colored brilliance.

I recalled Assistant Manager Eun Haje using a similar bead once to infiltrate the Church of the Luminous Unknown disguised as an elderly woman...

But it had been only the size of a finger and had not seemed nearly as powerful.

“Have you ever heard the folk tales about the Fox Bead? Not a fruit, but a bead possessed by a fox spirit.”

It sparkled.

“Some say it originated from a dragon’s Yeouiju[1]{Editor’s Note: The Yeouiju (여의주) is a mythical Korean dragon pearl that grants wish-fulfillment and omnipotence. Its primary importance in folklore is acting as the catalyst for transformation, as lesser serpents (Imugis) must possess it to become true dragons and ascend to heaven.}, but it’s likely a story born from similar tales.... It is said to grant wishes.”

...!

Soon, the fantastical image vanished.

But I realized that it reminded me of the egg-like shape that had been in the center of the Dream Incubator’s tank...

‘...A wish.’

Don’t tell me.

“Was that thing you lent them used in this experiment?”

“That seems likely, doesn’t it?”

A laugh followed.

“Unaware of that, I intended to quickly retrieve my bead and take action. Protecting my territory and repelling other evil essence was something the Bureau had requested of me many times, performing rituals with great care. But....”

His voice became solemn.

“I’m told the bead has disappeared.”

“......”

“Now there is no way. No way to protect this city from the evil infiltration and the infection spreading like a plague outside.”

The researcher’s face turned blue. His hands trembled as though he might lose consciousness at any moment.

‘...It’s pointless to let Lee Heoun go like this.’

It’s not just his personal responsibility. I’d lose the information.

I finally spoke quickly.

“Can’t we track how the bead disappeared?”

“......”

“And if that’s not possible, we need to find out if there’s any other way to save the city. ...By interrogating the researcher.”

“That’s true.”

“Ghk!”

Lee Heoun jerked his hands away from his throat as if shocked by electricity.

After gasping on the floor a few times, he spoke with eyes still focused.

“I—I’m sorry... I’m sorry...”

“Keep your apologies. We need to find a way to fix this. Explain exactly how the experiment was conducted.”

“Yes, cough, so we...”

But at that moment—

Researcher Lee Heoun’s right ear fell to the floor with a soft thud.

“...!”

“Ah.”

The researcher fumbled on the floor and picked up his ear.

The object that had fallen now looked completely like a plastic toy...

“......”

I turned my head toward the corridor.

The colorful corridor visible beyond Lee Jaheon and the two others felt somehow more static than before.

Some of the multicolored toys stood still, stopped, or lost their vitality...

They looked like things that had lost their mystery.

“...Run quickly. This, this office won’t hold out for long. You must get out....”

Researcher Lee Heoun spoke frantically.

“Currently, we are protected from the fire and the disaster outside... but the flames will soon break through here. There’s not much time left!”

Wait.

“Are you saying that the supernatural power preventing the fire from spreading to this floor... is disappearing?”

“Yes...”

I recalled how the Counselor’s hand, when placed into the monster, had turned mushy before vanishing as if it had dissolved.

...Then.

Could it be...?

“Is the supernatural power in Sekwang Special City… disappearing entirely?”

“......”

A faint smile appeared on the researcher’s face.

“That’s correct.”

...!!

“So, strictly speaking, the goal of our experiment has been achieved. The invitation must have been a success...!”

A faint ecstasy could be heard in the researcher’s voice.

“Someone... has arrived.”

My head went cold with a feeling that was either anger or fear.

But my sense of reason caught on to the fact that something was not right.

“That’s strange. If the supernatural phenomena vanished, it should have become safer. Why is it like that outside?”

“That’s why this isn’t our intention. It’s a result that’s impossible under our goals...”

“An impossible result?”

“Yes. Today, we simply wanted to establish and prove the existence of a ‘place without ghost stories’...”

His exhausted voice grew lower.

“Someone interfered and subtly altered the ritual.”

“......”

“Escape to the subway quickly. Since the range of this experiment was limited to the ‘surface,’ the subway, which isn’t the source, should be relatively safe. At least you all should be safe...”

“I—I can help support you...”

“I appreciate it, but....”

“I can only stay alive inside this institute. I’ve already lost most of my body.”

“But that researcher... he should be able to live even if he goes outside.”

“He’s from a different department. He hardly participated in this research. He’s not responsible, so I hope you’ll escape with him if possible....”

“You should evacuate too... Don’t just trust that strange button item, leave the office now and escape. Hurry.”

“I’ll guide you to the subway entrance.”

“I’ll look into other methods, so it’s best if you all leave.”
