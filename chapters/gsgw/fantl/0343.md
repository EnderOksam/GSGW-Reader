Had Section Chief Lee Jaheon really been swept into this Day of Disaster as just another civilian, like me?

I had encountered the two people I’d stored inside my tattoo in different ways, but Section Chief Lee Jaheon... had definitely died before I did.

That memory always brought a wave of anxious uncertainty whenever I recalled it, and I had forced myself to assume, “He must still be here,” just to keep going.

And he really was.

I just never expected to meet him like this.

“...?”
