# Chapter 348 - Sekwang Sealing Ritual, II

I wanted to blurt out, “What do you mean?”

But in truth, my mind had already understood Agent Haegeum’s words.

It felt like a part of my brain had burst from the shock.

‘...The sealing ritual is collapsing?’

So the disaster of Sekwang Special City... will be completely unleashed?

But the thing is—

‘In just one week...’

An overwhelming sense of terror surged up reflexively.

Having just experienced the reality of that “disaster” first-hand, a panic so tangible it was almost electric rose through my mind, prickling across my skin like goosebumps.

‘Then...’

It’s over.

‘What’s going to happen now?’

Even though I knew the horrific truth behind the seal, even though I had seen the suffering of Sekwang’s citizens with my own eyes, my face must have gone deathly pale.

Because in a few days, that scene would befall the entire country!

Helplessly... no, wait a minute.

I turned to Agent Haegeum.

There was no way I was the only one feeling this fear.

“Does the Management Bureau... not know?”

If they had known, there was no way they would’ve wasted the past 300 days. They surely would have come up with some kind of countermeasure.

The problem was that, if they had a countermeasure, these two agents wouldn’t be talking about an Annihilation-Sanctioned Disaster breaking out in one week in this kind of tone.

“...Are they keeping it secret?”

Agent Haegeum frowned slightly.

“You know how it is. There are barely any agents or personnel in the Bureau who even know about that Special City.”

...!

That’s right. They had erased the memories of all the agents and made the city cognitively inaccessible.

In the process, the Azure Dragon Team’s very existence had been erased, and the organization had even been reorganized as if it had always been that way.

But—

“But surely there’s someone who knows.”

“......”

Looking into Agent Haegeum’s eyes, I realized who she meant.

‘The ones who spearheaded the Sealing Ritual.’

Someone who pushed the horrifying idea of making May 4th, the day of the disaster, repeat endlessly, using the people who died over and over as fuel to maintain the Sealing Ritual.

Someone higher up in the hierarchy would definitely know.

“...But from what Choi-ssi and I have been able to find out, there hasn’t been any particular movement within the Bureau over the past few months. If they were taking action, it should’ve affected the sealing ritual—but there’s been nothing.”

“That’s...”

Wait.

A horrifying possibility flashed through my mind.

If they aren’t making a move now, then...

“...Are they waiting until the seal completely disappears?”

“......”

“And immediately after it disappears... are they planning to perform the ritual again?”

“Yes.”

“...!”

“If they have to seal it anyway, they’ll likely choose a method that’s already been proven. I think they’re more likely to choose that over an uncertain reinforcement.”

Ha.

“The problem is... where they’ll get the ‘sacrifices’ from.”

Agent Haegeum seemed tense just from the uncertainty of that question.

But I already knew the answer.

‘...All they have to do is offer up the citizens of Sekwang Special City who were released.’

This is insane.

The hair on my scalp stood on end.

I spoke urgently.

I needed to talk about this...

“Agent, have you... already talked with the other agent about the Sealing Ritual?”

I was referring to the human sacrifices.

I tried to keep it vague, but the moment I met Agent Choi’s eyes, I knew.

“Why beat around the bush, Grapes-ie? I already heard.”

...!

His bitter smile was accompanied by a dry, somewhat weary voice.

But there was little anger or confusion.

...He seemed to have already thought it over many times.

“Oh. Worried about being tracked? You can talk freely here. This place is right below the Tiger General’s Sanctuary—they can’t come after us.”

Then he added quietly,

“...And this is a place even the Bureau doesn’t know exists.”

“......”

“We tried to somehow get the living citizens out while maintaining the existing sealing ritual—but we just couldn’t interfere with the ritual itself.”

His blue eyes looked at me.

“Right up to this point, with seven days left until the seal ends.”

“......”

He must have been at the point of feeling like his head would explode.

His direct junior and a former spy still hadn’t woken up since disappearing in an Annihilation-Sanctioned Disaster.

The agency he had devoted his life to was hiding a ghastly secret.

There was no telling what impulsive thing Director Ho might do.

Looking into those tired eyes, which clearly hadn’t gotten any rest during his leave, I felt a chill in my chest.

The crisis was already right before us.

I’m losing it.

All sorts of things that needed dealing with swirled through my head. Director Ho, the Security Team’s well-being, the state of the researchers at Twilight Station, Blood Broadcasting Station’s rent, the lizard body stored at Section Chief Lee Jaheon’s residence...

The suffocating gaps of those 293 days.

But if we couldn’t stop the Annihilation-Sanctioned Disaster coming in one week, there’d be no point worrying about any of it.

And yet... re-sealing Sekwang Special City—

I couldn’t bring myself to think positively about doing that again.

This way, it’s hell for someone, and that way, it’s hell for someone else.

...How much does Director Ho know by now?

No. Focus.

If we couldn’t change the situation now that it had already come upon us, we had to move faster.

I had to somehow resolve this situation.

“Agent Grapes, be careful—”

“I’m fine.”

I stood up without support. My legs shook.

My mind cleared.

The first thing I needed...

“I’d like to meet Yeongeun-ssi, I mean... Agent Mint.”

The incident where the seal began to unravel.

We needed details from the person who had been at the center of the Train Shelter escape.

Fast.

***

“Soleum-ssi!”

She looked completely fine.

Instead of looking haggard in the padded jacket from the Sekwang Special City subway, she was dressed neatly and properly in ordinary clothes, just as when I first saw her at the company.

...Which also meant all her visible body parts were present.

Then I forced myself to speak.

“Yeongeun-ssi. I should’ve come to see you myself, but given the situation, I asked the agents to invite you here.”

“Of course that’s fine! I mean, you just woke up. You must be exhausted and have lost all your muscle and strength...”

She noticed that I was in better shape than expected, and a strange glint passed through her eyes. But instead of asking outright, she stayed quiet.

...Even there, I could feel the strange passage of time.

“Yeongeun-ssi, how have you been? I heard you’ve been going back into the Special City subway.”

“I’m... better than before. I’m living with my aunt now. My aunt... has forgotten everything that happened.”

Her voice seemed to darken for a moment, then returned to normal.

“Honestly, part of why I want to return as an agent is to avoid having my memories erased.”

Ah.

“You might call it calculating, but... losing my memories feels like losing myself.”

She added that, immediately after her rescue, she had deliberately hinted at wanting to return as an agent, implicitly getting the memory erasure postponed.

“Afterward, I also brushed off getting my body parts back by saying it was thanks to an item. ...Sounds excessive, right? It’s the Disaster Management Bureau, not some Daydream.”

“No. I understand completely.”

Especially from Yeongeun-ssi’s perspective, having lost her memories of her family in Sekwang Special City and tried to get a Wish Ticket without even knowing why she had forgotten them.

And then—

Thinking that far, I felt even more reluctant to open my mouth.

I couldn’t bring myself to casually say it again after telling the two agents, so I’d planned to tell them when all three of them were together.

Damn it.

But there was no time, and destruction was approaching.

“Yeongeun-ssi.”

“Yes?”

“There’s... good news, and bad news.”

A bitter smile appeared on Agent Choi’s lips, then disappeared as he heard what I said next.

“The good news is that the others may also wake up safely.”

“That’s—that’s wonderful!”

Joy sparkled in her voice, then quickly settled.

Agent Choi was watching me too.

“Assistant Manager Haje, Ryu Jaekwan-ssi... and Section Chief Lee Jaheon—are they alive? They disappeared from the train, too. We had assumed they... fell together.”

I nodded slowly. A flash of cold fury crossed her face.

“...That chief conductor pushed them, didn’t he?”

“......”

“I knew it. So that’s what happened. That crazy bastard...”

She said she didn’t regret hitting him at all, then steadied her breath.

“Still... I’m glad they’re alive. No matter how much we searched the subway, we couldn’t find any trace of the four of you.”

“......”

“Then you were on the surface?”

“Yes. ...Unexpectedly, people were living on the surface of Sekwang Special City too.”

I met her eyes.

“And they were dying every day.”

“...What?”

The air turned cold.

“...What does that mean?”

I told her.

About the tragedy of Sekwang Special City, where May 4th repeats.

“...!!”

“And... that’s the method of sealing the disaster?”

“......”

“By repeating a single day over and over, so that no matter how much time passes, the disaster never leaves the city’s radius—people are dying every day?”

Emotion slowly began to boil up from Yeongeun-ssi’s dazed words.

“Then what about my mom and dad?”

“Yeongeun-ssi...”

“It’s been years now... so...”

She gasped. I hurried over to pat her back, but she pushed my hand away.

...!

Her bowed head gave no answer for a while.

Then a whisper.

“...Is this real? Is this reality? Did it really... happen?”

“It might only be my deduction...”

“You’ve always been right! Every deduction you made was right! Why say something like that now...!”

She took another breath.

A stabbing silence sank into the cave.

Her fists were pale and shaking.

Agent Choi rose quietly and moved beside her. Both he and Agent Haegeum had cold, pale faces. However, in front of the family member directly involved, they did not show their shock.

I barely caught my breath and quietly asked him an indirect question.

“...Do you know?”

That Go Yeongeun-ssi...

“Whether I know that the seal will break in seven days?”

...!

“I know. My ability to hear ‘rumors about the Special City’ didn’t disappear after I got out. In fact, those rumors were part of the reason I kept trying to enter the subway.”

Her voice cracked.

“When I thought about it...”

“...Yes.”

“If we just let the seal break... maybe it could break while my parents are still alive?”

...!

“May 4th keeps repeating. If the timing’s right—if it breaks at a point when many people are still alive—wouldn’t they be fixed in place while still alive?”

“Why should I care about that?”

“...!”

“Let them deal with it themselves, whether they’re ruined or not. I don’t care... ha, sorry...”

“No. I’m not sorry at all. ...I only apologize for getting angry at you, Agent.”

Agent Haegeum set down her sword and gently patted Go Yeongeun’s back.

“Honestly, you don’t even need to apologize. I feel the same way...”

“......”

I knew she was gritting her teeth and silently shedding tears—not from sadness, but from pain and rage.

A suppressed indignation, much like the Counselor’s.

I suppressed the emotions rising in me.

...Is there really no way?

Was what Yeongeun-ssi had suggested really nothing more than a personal plan or wish?

Or—

Wait.

“What if we end it before then?”

“...What?”

“If we end the disaster before the sealing is released... it might be possible.”

“Agent Grapes!”

“Wait.”

“So if the disaster ends right before the sealing ends... the citizens might be able to make it out of the disaster alive.”

“Yes.”

It felt like a spark flashed through my mind.

I knew how to leave the subway now.

‘I just have to walk along the maintenance tracks.’

If I set foot on the surface of Sekwang Special City on the morning when the day starts over.

At some point very early on, before the city itself was completely contaminated.

We might actually be able to try to end it...

And I already had a “method” I’d been preparing after getting a hint.
