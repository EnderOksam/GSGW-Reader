# Chapter 336 - Sekwang Special City Disaster, II

“Was that a disaster alert just now?”

“They say it’s a terror attack! A terror attack!”

May 4th—the day of the disaster in Sekwang Special City.

A moment in the past.

The bustling street near Hanbit Department Store, where I had rushed out that morning, had not yet descended into chaos.

But something ominous hung in the air.

The scent of fear at the very place where something enormous was about to erupt. An unsettling feeling.

Tension.

An atmosphere created by the disaster alert that read, ‘Biochemical terror incident near City Hall.’

“Look up a nearby shelter, just in case.”

“Junseong-ah, stop looking at that. We’re going home. Hurry!”

“Are we just going to take the subway? What are we supposed to do?”

“Don’t you hear that noise coming from over there? It hasn’t stopped. Someone’s shouting. There, over there...”

At that last remark, I instinctively turned my head.

Far beyond the street stood a rectangular glass building, only its upper portion visible from where I stood.

After 613 days spent reliving the same peaceful May 4th in Sekwang Special City, I recognized it at once.

Sekwang City Hall.

“…This is weird.”

“Hey, hey, look at that.”

Firefighters.

They walked toward us in their orange emergency uniforms, their expressions perfectly serene.

Even police officers were following behind them.

Paramedics came after them.

All of them wore faint smiles.

And behind them came the injured.

People who looked as though they should be taken away in ambulances were walking among ordinary passersby...

Everyone who had responded to or gathered at the attack site was marching along, still looking just as they had amid the emergency.

“……”

“Hey, this is strange.”

Even the mere sense that something is wrong can inspire fear.

Only then did the people crowding the busy streets near the department store begin to shrink back, while others hastily raised their phones to record the scene.

‘Damn it.’

I began edging backward.

My heart was pounding.
