# Chapter 336 - Sekwang Special City Disaster, II

“Was that a disaster notice just now?”

“They said it’s a terrorist incident! A terrorist incident!”

May 4th—the day Sekwang Special City was destroyed.

A moment in the past.

Around Hanbit Department Store, where I had emerged that morning, Sekwang’s busy streets had not yet tipped into chaos.

But it was ominous.

Something enormous was about to break loose. At the point where it would begin, I sensed fear in the air, along with a feeling I could not name.

Tension.

An atmosphere created by the disaster alert that read, ‘Biochemical terror incident near City Hall.’

“Search for a nearby shelter.”

“Junseong-ah, put that away and come on. We’re going home!”

“Should we take the subway? What are we supposed to do?”

“Can you hear that noise? Is somebody yelling? It’s coming from over there...”

I reflexively turned my head at those words.

Beyond the street stood a rectangular building of glass, with only the upper floors visible from here.

Six hundred and thirteen repetitions of a peaceful May 4th had taught me to recognize it immediately.

Sekwang City Hall.

“…This is weird.”

More of them spilled onto the sidewalk, and the people behind them wore entirely different uniforms...

“……”

Firefighters.

Their orange emergency uniforms were unmistakable, yet their expressions remained serene.

Police officers followed in the same orderly line.

Paramedics came after them.

Every one of them wore a small, peaceful smile.

Behind them, injured people could be seen.

Even the wounded were on their feet, mixed among ordinary civilians despite looking fit for an ambulance...

Everyone who had been drawn to the attack site was now advancing as though frozen at the moment they arrived.

“……”

“Hey, this is strange.”

Humans feel fear even from a simple sense of wrongness.

The people in the bustling area near the department store finally began to flinch and back away, or quickly raised their phones to record videos.

‘Damn it.’

I began edging backward.

My heart was pounding.
