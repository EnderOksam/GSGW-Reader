# Chapter 336 - Sekwang Special City Disaster, II

“Was that a disaster notice just now?”

“They said it’s a terrorist incident! A terrorist incident!”

May 4th—the day of the disaster in Sekwang Special City.

A moment in the past.

Around Hanbit Department Store, where I had emerged that morning, Sekwang’s busy streets had not yet tipped into chaos.

But it was ominous.

Something enormous was about to break loose. At the point where it would begin, I sensed fear in the air, along with a feeling I could not name.

Tension.

An atmosphere created by the disaster alert that read, ‘Biochemical terror incident near City Hall.’

“Look up a nearby shelter, just in case.”

“Junseong-ah, put that away and come on. We’re going home!”

“Should we take the subway? What are we supposed to do?”

“Can you hear that noise coming from over there? Someone’s shouting. There, over there...”

I reflexively turned my head at those words.

Beyond the street stood a rectangular building of glass, with only the upper floors visible from here.

After 613 days spent reliving the same peaceful May 4th in Sekwang Special City, I recognized it at once.

Sekwang City Hall.

They weren’t running from something or screaming.

Still, a crowd that size was bound to draw attention, and the passersby watched them, murmuring and speculating.

“What is it?”

“Maybe they’re shutting down the road.”

All along the street, people speculated that the police must have come because of the terror attack. Others said they’d seen posts about fire trucks arriving, too.

I swallowed hard and watched the approaching crowd.

And then…

“Huh?”

Something about it struck me as wrong.

Every face was calm. They moved in silence, never once speaking to the person beside them.

“…This is weird.”

Their clothing varied wildly: restaurant uniforms, business attire, tracksuits, café uniforms... Yet they all walked shoulder to shoulder without a trace of anxiety, agitation, or hesitation.

“Hey, hey, look at that.”

“Why is everyone acting so strange?”

But the unease didn’t end there.

More people kept spilling onto the sidewalk, revealing an endless succession of different outfits behind them...

“……”

“…Are those firefighters?”

Firefighters.

Their orange emergency uniforms were unmistakable, yet their expressions remained serene.

Police officers followed in the same orderly line.

Paramedics came after them.

All of them wore faint smiles.

Behind them, injured people could be seen.

People who looked as though they needed ambulances were walking among ordinary passersby...

Everyone who had responded to or gathered at the attack site was marching along, still looking just as they had amid the emergency.

“……”

“Hey, this is strange.”

Humans feel fear even from a simple sense of wrongness.

The people in the bustling area near the department store finally began to flinch and back away, or quickly raised their phones to record videos.

‘Damn it.’

I began edging backward.

My heart was pounding.

And in the next moment…
