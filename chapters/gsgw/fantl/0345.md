# Chapter 344 - Sekwang Special City Disaster, X

He no longer looked like a lizard, but even so, I still found myself naturally imagining those vertical, reptilian pupils.

“I see...”

The tension finally drained from my shoulders.

‘Phew...’

It seemed I had been under far more pressure than I realized.

Being dropped into an unfamiliar time with no one I knew around, trying on my own to save acquaintances who had no memories of me...
