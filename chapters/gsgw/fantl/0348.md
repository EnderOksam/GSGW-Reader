# Chapter 347 - Sekwang Sealing Ritual, I

I held my breath.

Agent Choi’s complexion didn’t look good.

His smiling face clearly showed how hopeful and happy he was to have a team member return...

Despite this, deep fatigue showed through underneath it.

“How’s your body feeling?”

“......”

“Getting up after lying down for so long is brutal, right? Your muscles feel like they’re gone. Kuu, this senior has been through it all, too. Just relax. Get comfortable.”

Following his lighthearted rambling, I carefully pushed myself up.

My limbs trembled, but for someone who had been asleep for 293 days, I wasn’t in terrible shape. Perhaps I... no, let’s stop thinking about that.

What mattered was that, for now, I had escaped both Sekwang Special City and Director Cheong’s grasp.

Probably thanks to this man.

Still hiding my trembling hands, I turned to thank him, only to freeze when I saw my surroundings.

“...Where exactly is this?”

“A place with good air and good water.”

It looked like the inside of a natural cave.

A small space tucked between jagged, cliff-like rocks, seemingly prepared as a hideout.

In one corner of the rock face, Five-Direction Colors Cloths were wrapped around something, hiding it behind an arrangement that looked like a ritual site...

Beside me, I could see Agent Bronze’s sleeping body.

“......”

I looked back and forth between Agent Bronze’s body, lying on a well-made bed, and my own spot, then turned to Agent Choi.

“...I’m certain we entered Sekwang Special City through the Fox Counseling Office.”

“I stole you away.”

“...!!”

“That place was going to... well, never mind that.”

Agent Choi suddenly brightened again.

“Anyway, that was some nice timing just now, huh? That’s what you get from Black Tortoise Team 1’s top ace agent. Grapes-ie, see? Didn’t I tell you I’d rescue you?”

“......”

An ominous premonition came creeping over me, as though it were slowly consuming me.

I glanced to the side.

Agent Choi was trying to nonchalantly hide it, as if clearing it away, but the ritual bell was still there.

And the blood that had splattered and run from it.

“...Why did blood splatter from that bell?”

“It’s nothing.”

“You said, the Tiger General... was a vegan...”

“Just what did you offer up, and to whom?”

“......”

Agent Choi stared at me silently...

Then he burst out laughing.

“What are you talking about! Hey, look.”

He rolled up his sleeves to show me his scar-covered arms. He also rolled up his pant leg to show me, and pressed on his body.

...He looked fine.

“I’m okay, see?”

Then he shrugged.

“Let’s not say things that could give people the wrong idea~ They might think a cool agent like me, who thinks about civilians day and night, is some kind of gambling addict who stakes his own body at a casino.”

Then, what on earth...

“For now, just get some rest. Eat something... and then let’s talk.”

“......”

“Hm?”

Beneath Agent Choi’s gaunt smile, I could see how desperate he was.

I thought of Agent Bronze, still asleep.

And I wondered how this person had managed, alone, for those 293 days.

“......”
