# Chapter 347 - Sekwang Sealing Ritual, I

I caught my breath.

Agent Choi didn’t look well.

His smiling face clearly showed hope and joy at the return of a team member...

Despite this, deep fatigue was evident beneath it.

“How are you feeling?”

“......”

“Getting up after lying down for so long is brutal, right? Your muscles feel like they’re gone. Kuu, this senior has been through it all, too. Just relax. Get comfortable.”

Following his bantering words, I carefully pushed myself up.

My limbs trembled, but for someone who had been asleep for 293 days, I wasn’t in terrible shape. Perhaps I... no, let’s stop thinking about that.

What mattered was that, for now, I had escaped both Sekwang Special City and Director Cheong’s grasp.

Probably thanks to this man.

Hiding my still-trembling hands, I turned my head to thank him, but froze at the sight of my surroundings.

“...Where exactly is this?”

“A place with good air and good water.”

It looked like the inside of a natural cave.

A small space between jagged, cliff-like rocks, a hideout that seemed to have been prepared within.

In one corner of the rock face, Five-Direction Colors Cloths were draped around something, as if it were a ritual site...

Beside me lay the sleeping body of Agent Bronze.

“......”
