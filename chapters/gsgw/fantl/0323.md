‘It’s surprising, though—he did seem like he was trying to help me just now.’

‘...Hmm.’

Was it because I’m an agent?

Thinking back to what happened in Jisan Village, I chose my words carefully.

The sheer terror made me let out a sigh.

“Sir.”

I spoke as calmly as I could.

“I’m not doing this because I’m an agent. ...It’s just simple logic.”

I looked at the rescued person, who was still crouched in the corner, trembling as he fumbled with his flayed skin.

“If they can live, there’s no reason to deliberately kill them.”

“......”

Instead of waiting for Baek Saheon’s reply, I lowered myself toward the person I’d brought along.

And asked quietly,

“Do you think it would be better for you to be in a safe place?”

“Hik—hng...”

He nodded frantically.

Then he glanced fearfully at Baek Saheon. Baek Saheon seemed to be gauging my reaction with a cold, calculating face, as if assessing a potential threat to his own survival...

But I didn’t react to either of them. I just calmly declared, “Alright. Then we’ll move to a safe place.”

I supported the rescued person and went up one more floor via the emergency stairs.

I didn’t call for Baek Saheon.

He seemed to stand there blankly for a moment. Then, instead of running off with the camcorders, he followed after me.

I entered the sixth floor.

‘Definitely less brutal than the fifth floor.’

The sealed ward’s corridor was dark, but the faint stench of antiseptic covered any faint hint of something fishy.

It still felt ominous and uncanny, like something out of a horror attraction—but at least it looked more normal.
