# Chapter 318 - One-Day Researcher, II

A casually dressed man in his early thirties was talking excitedly on the phone in his apartment.

“Yeah, yeah, seriously. I got into Daydream, I’m telling you. Yeah, I just threw in an application as a test.”

He’d applied when Daydream suddenly posted an additional round of recruitment—and made it through the final selection.

Now he was calling everyone he could, pretending not to care while boasting.

Especially to a friend with less impressive academic credentials who still hadn’t found a job—he made sure to rub it in subtly.

‘What? You said the look in my eyes wasn’t great and that I should prepare better for interviews?’

But look at the result!

“Well, I mean, I kind of got hired by chance, but it’s also a bit disappointing, you know? Huh? I didn’t get the chance to apply to lots of different places. Unlike you.”

The thrilling pleasure of tearing down a competitor he considered inferior to himself.

Just imagining this conversation replaying in the other person’s mind later and lowering their self-esteem was enough to make his heart flutter.

The new researcher at Daydream felt a thrill of pleasure deep inside as he kept talking.

“You should’ve applied, too. Huh, you did? I didn’t know. Sorry about that. Next time we meet, I’ll treat you to—AAARGH!!”

Suddenly.
