# Chapter 318 - One-Day Researcher, II

A man in his early thirties, dressed ordinarily, was energetically talking on the phone in his small studio apartment.

“Yeah, yeah, seriously. I got into Daydream, I’m telling you. Yeah, I just threw in an application as a test.”

He had applied to an unexpected additional recruitment notice released by Daydream, and in the end, he had made it all the way through and passed.

Now he was calling everyone he could, pretending not to care while boasting.

Especially to a friend whose academic background was worse than his and who still hadn’t found a job—subtly rubbing it in.

“What? You said I should work harder on interview prep because my grades weren’t great?”

But look at the result!

“Well, I mean, I kind of got hired by chance, but it’s also a bit disappointing, you know? Huh? I didn’t get the chance to apply to lots of different places. Unlike you.”

The thrilling pleasure of tearing down a competitor he considered inferior to himself.

Just imagining this conversation replaying in the other person’s mind later and lowering their self-esteem was enough to make his heart flutter.

The new Daydream researcher felt a shiver of pleasure inside as he kept talking.

“You should’ve applied, too. Huh, you did? I didn’t know. Sorry about that. Next time we meet, I’ll treat you to—”
