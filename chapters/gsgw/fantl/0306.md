# Chapter 305 - The Note in the Wall

Inside Car No. 8 of the train shelter.

After unscrewing the Daydream Inc. potion cap disguised as a wall screw, I froze while staring at the note hidden inside.

More specifically, at the name of the 'writer' written on it.

'...Lee Kangheon?'

$p
#f# *— K. LEE: This is Assistant Manager Lee Kangheon from the C-Squad haha*#f#
p$

That had been Agent Choi's false identity.

More precisely, the employee identity he had stolen in order to infiltrate Daydream Inc. and gather information.

Then...

Did that mean this note had been left behind by the actual employee whose identity had been stolen?

'Wait.'

I hurriedly continued reading.

!pb
*You're probably reading this in the middle of the absolute circus that happened after the Cheerful Research Institute's experiment failed and high-grade darkness started exploding everywhere, right?*
pb!

This was talking about Sekwang Special City!

I inhaled sharply.

!pb

pb!

What?

!pb
*At the root of it all, Daydream stabbed the research institute in the back and caused this entire disaster. Seriously, what an unbelievable company. Anyway...*

*You want to clean up this mess?*
pb!

Without realizing it, I lowered my gaze even faster.

!pb
*Then somehow smuggle back the dream incubator that Daydream Inc. copied and stole.*

"...!!"

!pb

*Sorry, but I can't do it myself.*

*Good luck!*

*—  Lee Kangheon*

That was the end of the note.

'What kind of insane nonsense is this now?'

The information was absurd and direct enough to make the back of my head throb.

'Daydream Inc.?'

Reflexively, I recalled it.

The Dream Incubation Chamber I had discovered outside Sekwang Special City.

And the desperate sentence scrawled across the research notes beside the prototype Dreamcore device, as though someone had crushed the pages while writing it.

$p
#f# *—&nbsp;IwasdeceivedIwasdeceivedIwasdeceivedIwasdeceivedIwasdeceivedIwasdeceivedIwasdeceivedIwasdeceived*#f#
p$

Honestly, the circumstances fit together almost perfectly like puzzle pieces.

Daydream Inc. had not only copied and used the Cheerful Research Institute's research and potions wholesale, but even in the basement of the main building, there had existed a ghost story where the institute's past appeared.

...And in the first place, wasn't there one classic reason ghost stories came into existence?

'Resentment.'

The Cheerful Research Institute had experienced something severe enough to leave behind resentment carved into the bone against Daydream Inc.

'...Besides.'

The research note beside the broken dream incubator where I had awakened after drinking the Wish Ticket.

Daydream found out they came it was another deception before they take Ireum-nim away smuggle it out hid it where the most people are

'It was another deception.'

That expression implied they had already been tricked once before.

'Just what the hell has this insane pharmaceutical company been doing all this time?'

Though honestly, it wasn't surprising that the company built on grinding employees to pieces inside a ghost story world was itself stained by horrifying ghost stories.

And more importantly...

'...Smuggle a Daydream Dreamcore device back into a Cheerful Research Institute facility?'

Given the context, and given where this note had been found... Wasn't it practically saying that doing so would end the Catastrophe-Level disaster consuming Sekwang Special City?

"......"

I quickly looked around.

I was alone inside Car No. 8, but countless survivors from Sekwang Special City must have used this car before.

'If he anticipated even that and installed this screw...'

Then it was clearly intended for any Daydream Inc. employee among the survivors to find.

And I had been the one to pull it out.

'......'

$Brd
[You seem troubled, my friend. This Braun is always prepared to listen. A witty piece of advice comes free of charge.]
Brd$

Troubled... wasn't quite the right word.

My thoughts had drifted in another direction entirely, leaving me even more unsettled.

Because if the contents of this note were true, then there was another conclusion that could be inferred from it.

If reinstalling the dream incubator could resolve this disaster...

'...Then ultimately, this means Sekwang Special City ended up like this because something went wrong with the dream incubator.'

$Brd
[Hooh!]
Brd$

'And meanwhile, Daydream Inc. is using those dream incubators everywhere while expanding the company.'

It was an absolutely horrifying red flag.

And I knew something else.

The subtitle of this wiki setting.

No... actually, its true title.

$p
#><[Prophecy of the Apocalypse : Darkness Exploration Records]><#
p$

'......'

This was driving me insane.

Suppressing a sigh, I folded the note again.

My head pounded. I felt uneasy.

'I don't even know how far I'm supposed to think this through.'

This suspicious note, the future ahead, and what I was supposed to do from here.

I rolled over onto my side.

'Let's sleep first.'

And just before falling asleep, one more thought crossed my mind.

...Separate from the contents of the note, something else bothered me.

'It's similar.'

The tone of this self-proclaimed 'Lee Kangheon' felt strangely similar to Agent Choi's way of speaking.

Reflexively, I almost tried comparing the handwriting too. But the handwriting remaining in my memory from Agent Choi's notes felt distinctly different.

'Though this note's handwriting is way messier.'

Besides, the whole setup stopped making sense the more I thought about it.

'If Agent Choi had actually worked at Daydream Inc. too, that'd basically make him a complete Mary Sue character.'

One of the Disaster Management Bureau's iconic early named characters secretly having a backstory where he had also worked at Daydream Inc. in the past?

That kind of character setup rarely survived in a collaborative wiki. And even if it did appear, people usually tried to kill off or erase the character eventually.

At least that had been true during the era of Darkness Exploration Records when named characters like Agent Choi still existed.

In other words, before the Church of the Luminous Unknown had truly begun expanding its influence throughout the wiki.

This had nothing to do with whether or not there were parts of the wiki lore I no longer remembered.

It was a matter of narrative plausibility.

'...Hah.'

Maybe it really had just been some enraged Daydream Inc. employee, trapped inside Sekwang Special City and moments away from dying a meaningless death, scattering fake bait information out of spite.

But those expressions that sounded as though the writer knew every detail of the incident bothered me too much.

Maybe that was why even the writing style kept catching my attention.

'...Sleep.'

Once I got out, I'd show this note to Agent Choi.

I closed my eyes again and pulled the old blanket all the way over my head.

And amid the countless worries, anxieties, and chaotic thoughts rampaging through my mind, exhaustion finally dragged me into sleep.

***

"You're awake?"

"...Good morning."

I opened my eyes five hours later.

It was nearly noon, yet the inside of the train remained brightly lit, to the point where it was impossible to tell whether it was day or night.

"That's why some of the cars cover their lights during sleeping hours."

Go Yeongeun's aunt—the woman called the station worker—raised a hand in greeting when she saw me coming out into Car No. 7, and we spoke briefly.

Including about the condition of Assistant Manager Eun Haje and Agent Bronze.

"They were accepted safely."

Thank goodness.

I let out a sigh of relief.

'...It was a good thing I set up that store at Afternoon Station.'

It seemed that being able to confidently promise that the two of them could regularly obtain food supplies from the store had become an excellent bargaining chip during negotiations.

The Happy Maker we brought as a bribe also seemed to have helped somewhat.

'Strong painkillers are welcomed anywhere.'

Aside from that, I recalled the Delusion Home Shopping items I had carefully scraped together while preserving at least a portion of the 'home shopping survival prize money.'

Of course, no matter how good the bribe items were, their effect would only last temporarily, and I was still worried about the possibility of territorial bullying later on.

Still, it was reassuring that Assistant Manager Eun Haje—a former reporter skilled at navigating small social circles—was there.

"From now on, the two of them will stay beside the area Yeongeun was assigned. In Car No. 6. It may be a little cramped, though."

"...Thank you."

"If people can survive by helping each other, then they should help each other. That's what this place was originally made for, anyway."

The station worker spoke calmly, though her face looked tired.

For reference, I had also asked whether any survivor staying in Car No. 8 had been a man wearing a suit and an animal mask.

"People like that... You all were the first."

"I-I see..."

That was the end of it.

As expected, it seemed impossible to identify the 'Lee Kangheon' who left the note through something like this.

The station worker sighed.

"The people in the front cars weren't suspicious of you for no reason. Though lately... they've started showing some rather strange elitist attitudes."

"...Um, are the people in the front and rear cars really that different from each other?"

"Well, when a disaster like this happens, people usually choose one of two paths."

She shrugged.

"They either become extremely exclusionary and selfish toward outsiders, or they obsessively gather supplies like mad."

"......"

"You could say the people in this train shelter split into those two groups. Why not stay in Car No. 8 a little longer and try talking to people once they get used to you?"

"...It's a generous offer, but I don't think I can right now."

I lifted my head at the station announcement.

"I'm getting off here."

I still didn't know how one could sleep again inside a dream, but after resting, my head definitely felt clearer.

'Now I can move again.'

And whether the note was true or not, whether Daydream Inc. had caused some kind of catastrophe or not, what I needed to do hadn't changed.

Explore the stations.

Gather information and traces.

'...And this station is one I can handle alone.'

But when the station worker heard that I intended to get off here, her expression stiffened.

"...It's dangerous. Noon Station might sound pleasant from the name alone, but it isn't that kind of place. A lot of people fear it even more than Afternoon Station."

"It's alright. I've heard roughly what kind of place it is. Even so, I think someone has to go."

"Hah..."

The station worker looked bitter, but didn't stop me any further. In the middle of a disaster like this, she was probably already used to people making these choices.

"How about greeting Yeongeun and your companions before getting off at the next stop?"

"...No. It's alright."

They had only just been accepted into the train shelter. It wouldn't do them any favors if outsiders immediately saw them maintaining close contact with me again.

I shook my head with a faint smile.

"We already said our goodbyes yesterday."

"...I see."

The station worker nodded.

Recalling the contents of the wiki entry that had long since been updated.

And the ghost story here possessed a fitting gimmick for such a place.

"*Hoo.*"

Alone, I climbed the stairs leading from the platform toward the waiting hall...

And sunlight poured down from above.

"....!"

...An open space spread before me.

This was a sight utterly impossible to believe was indoors.

Under a brilliantly clear sky bathed in sunlight stood fences and small homes lining a residential neighborhood.

Lush garden trees. The smell of lunch drifting over from houses beyond the fences. It was warm and peaceful.

This wasn't some newly developed apartment district in a modern city. It looked more like an old neighborhood left untouched by redevelopment.

...But that wasn't what shocked me.

"What is this place now?!"

"Assistant Manager Lee, did you touch something? Wasn't it whatever you were handling earlier?"

"I didn't! I never touched any darkness-related item!"

"This isn't actually inside the darkness, is it? It's way too normal for that."

"Call security first!"

People.

Dozens of people were talking amongst themselves while standing beyond the fence near the platform stairs, looking around at their surroundings.

Every one of them wore white lab coats. More than half had employee IDs hanging from their necks...

Some of them were familiar faces.

Especially one person.

"Section Chief Kwak, were you perhaps doing something just moments ago—"

"Hm? I was on my way to an interview. What, did you think I tossed all the researchers into the darkness as test subjects? Hahaha!"

"O-Of course not?! Haha, we're not some kind of cleanup team."

...It was Kwak Jekang.

Those people were all members of Daydream Inc.'s research division...!

'What kind of insane situation is this?'

My eyes nearly bulged out of my head.

Why were Daydream people here—no, not even the Field Exploration Team, but the research division—in the middle of underground Sekwang Special City?

Dozens of them had entered a highest-grade darkness sealed by the Disaster Management Bureau with nothing but their own bodies...!

At that moment—

"...!!"

One of the researchers peering around beyond the fence spotted me.

Reflexively, I tried to hide myself, but immediately realized it was already too late.

The researcher pointed at me.

More specifically, at my suit... and my animal mask.

"T-The Field Exploration Team??"
