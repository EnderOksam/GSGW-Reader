What are they doing?

Even if I died by ‘humane execution,’ there was a good chance I’d simply end up outside Sekwang Special City. I can prepare and enter again. Shouldn’t they worry more about their own position?

Are they concerned that I might die *permanently* in this ghost story? Like the piles of corpses under glass.

‘I don’t think so.’

They seem to be attempting a method to overcome this ghost story, but it’d be safer if they tried *after* my trial.

I spoke very quietly, so only the agents could hear.

“It’s really okay.”

“Really. My original plan was just to destroy the scales.”

“......!”

The same approach I’d taken at Noon Station—simply setting the residential district on fire.

‘If there’s no way to judge, they have no choice but to end it.’

And since it wouldn’t break easily, I planned to unleash all sorts of contamination and wreak havoc.

I’d figured there was a good chance I’d die in the attempt.

“I went into this expecting to die anyway. So it’s fi—”
