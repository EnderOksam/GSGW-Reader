# Chapter 310 - A Perfect Fit

“Falcon-ssi.”

“A note came into Car 7... they asked me to pass it along to you.”

Eun Haje took the note the station employee discreetly held out to her, away from the others.

‘That brat Soleum.’

‘Feels like he’s becoming a little more flexible.’
