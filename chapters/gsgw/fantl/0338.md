# Chapter 337 - Sekwang Special City Disaster, III

Why was Agent Bronze... wearing a Sekwang High uniform and looking so young?

“.......”

The sheer absurdity of the situation nearly stunned me, but I quickly collected my thoughts and began assessing what was happening.

He looked slightly younger than he had as a high school student in the ‘Within the Shadow of Darkness’ ghost story.

And it wasn’t just his uniform that had changed.

He looked unmistakably young, both in expression and demeanor.

“Um....”

And he didn’t recognize me.

‘...Has he been assimilated into the Sekwang Special City ghost story?’

Like me, he seemed to have been ‘assigned’ the role of a citizen of Sekwang Special City on May 4th.

...Wait a minute.

If everyone who’d fallen here with me was in the same state, how on earth was I supposed to get them out?

How was I supposed to convince them?

The relief of finding one of my companions lasted only a moment before I was faced with another urgent decision.

I desperately racked my brain. Meanwhile, young Ryu Jaekwan, who had come running after me, caught his breath and chose his words carefully.

“About what happened with the bicycle earlier... are you all right?”

“I’m fine.”

I studied his remarkably youthful face.

His expression showed a mixture of confusion and concern. Still, for someone his age who had just witnessed something straight out of a ghost story happen in real life, he was remarkably composed.

‘...Agent Bronze said he was still a student when he first became caught up in a ghost story and was rescued by the Disaster Management Bureau.’

Could this be a version of him from even earlier in his life? No. What mattered right now was figuring out how to get him to come with me.

Pretending to examine his uniform, which had no name tag, I asked,

“By the way... you’re a student?”

“Yes.”

“Are you heading to Sekwang High? That looks like their uniform.”

“Yes, but....”

“Good. I’m going in the same direction.”

I approached the shared-bicycle rack and began smashing one of the locks with a stone.

“...!”

“Renting a shared bicycle through your phone will probably be difficult now. This is quicker than wasting time trying.”

“.......”

“Did you happen to see anyone swelling up on the way here? Or receive a disaster alert?”

“...Yes.”

“From now on, only listen for the alert tone. Never check the messages themselves. And if you see people walking in large groups along the street, absolutely do not approach them.”

By then, I had completely broken the first bicycle’s lock, but I kept going until I had broken the second one as well.

I took one of the bicycles out and offered it to the high school student.

“Do you know how to ride a bike?”

Ryu Jaekwan gave me an uncertain look, then nodded firmly.

I took the lead on my bicycle, pedaling hard.

The young Ryu Jaekwan followed close behind on the shared bicycle I had given him.

Seeing him grit his teeth and keep up, I figured Agent Bronze had always had a knack for cycling....

I rode skillfully toward Sekwang Technical High School, taking detour after detour to avoid the sounds of disaster alerts and screams.

But even those signs of danger would soon disappear.

...Once there were no survivors left in the vicinity, the disaster alerts would naturally stop ringing.

‘I have to move fast.’

My mind raced as I planned our route ahead. Then... I spotted something.

A convenience store.

“Hold on.”

“What is it?”

Making sure Ryu Jaekwan wasn’t wobbling on his bicycle, I stopped briefly outside the convenience store.

I quickly opened the door, stepped inside, and began grabbing supplies.

‘Mostly food.’

I made sure not to take too much, though. Mobility was essential.

Instead, after some thought, I bought the two sturdiest-looking long umbrellas I could find, along with a toolkit.

The part-timer at the register had kept glancing at a book, earphones in, as he rang up my purchases. I said to him,

“You need to get out of here. Now.”

“Huh?”

“There’s been a biochemical attack near City Hall, and it’s spreading. Get to the subway station as quickly as you can.”

“!!”

Only then did the part-timer turn on his phone and check his messages. Looking flustered, he began frantically scanning his screen...

As I stepped outside, I caught a glimpse of the part-timer gathering supplies, just as I had....

Young Ryu Jaekwan stood awkwardly holding his bicycle and watching me. I said to him,

“Open your bag, please.”

“Huh?”

I also packed two small bottles of water and some energy bars into Ryu Jaekwan’s schoolbag. Then I handed him an umbrella and made sure it was secured to the side of his bicycle.

“We don’t know what will happen next, so you should take these with you. Use the umbrella to push things away in an emergency.”

“Th-thank you.”

Ryu Jaekwan took the bag, hesitating before speaking.

“Um, let me pay you for this.”

“I’m not about to take money from a student in an emergency like this. Get on your bike. We’ll make for Sekwang High as quickly as possible.”

“But... okay.”

Ryu Jaekwan looked as though he had plenty to say and ask, but he seemed to grasp the seriousness of the situation. He wiped away his sweat and took hold of his bicycle again.

Eventually, he couldn’t help but ask.

“How is it that... you know all this so well?”

“......I’m just used to it.”

“Excuse me?”

I quickly changed the subject.

“By the way, it’s already past the time you should be at school. Why are you heading there only now?”

“I... I had to stop by the bank, so I’m a bit late.”

“Have you contacted your family?”

“...It’s fine.”

From that evasive answer, I realized that the background Ryu Jaekwan had been assigned here was probably much the same as in reality.
