# Chapter 359 - Flower Golden Resort [Monorail City], I

A fantastical and bizarre moment.

The subway tracks of Sekwang Special City mutated as if they were being devoured.

“Ah...!”

As if the past few years of frozen time were collapsing all at once, the rapidly aging subway tracks flipped over, glittered gold in midair, and settled back onto the ground.

They looked like an exquisite toy.

Elegant lines sparkled across the black-and-white floor, while the stained, shabby arched walls of the tunnel took on a smooth ivory hue, dazzling and almost intoxicating.

That fantastical wave of change surged forward in an instant from the farthest visible end of the rails...

Finally, it swept over the exploration team.

“...!”

It swept past them on all sides as they reflexively stepped back or raised their equipment.

“Oh.”

Lee Seonghae sniffed the air.

Even the smell had changed.

Instead of the damp, stale underground air, a sweet, pleasant scent filled the space.

The scent of Cheerful Theme Park’s Golden Zone.

The two figures in Flower Golden Resort uniforms, who had watched the entire spectacle without moving a muscle, spoke with smiles on their lips.

“Construction is complete.”

“Confirmed.”

The employees from the Ghost Story turned around after confirming that even the tracks had been perfectly transformed.

They walked back along the ornate golden rails.

“Wait...!”

Go Yeongeun dashed after them as if in urgent pursuit.

‘Hmm.’

Lee Seonghae followed close behind.

The twin-like employees, striding along the tracks at an unnaturally fast pace, eventually headed toward the nearest station.

...Sekwang Station.

“...!!”

Go Yeongeun stopped in stunned disbelief.

What stood there was no longer a modern platform.
