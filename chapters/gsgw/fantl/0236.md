# Chapter 235 - Happy Ending Sect, II

The days inside the Happy Ending Sect’s underground bunker were monotonous and strange.

Those who took turns praying.

The corpses brought in fresh every day.

And the people who gave impassioned speeches about how those corpses had been good people in life, and thus worthy of meeting a happy ending.

The people who wept at those words, smiling and applauding.

The bodies crammed into the black boxes.

The sight of a small, deranged society was grotesque, and yet among themselves they were at peace.

‘Truly cult-like.’

Cruel and insane practices.

And, in contrast, a closed sense of comfort.

The believers were strangely kind, treating each other warmly, intimately, calling one another family.

All in this survival bunker, cut off underground.

[Ah, that too is a technology of rule. A sense of solidarity, belonging, and the confinement of daily routine are what keep a group from ever leaving.]

Yes. Maybe it’s only natural. But still…

‘…It feels like something’s missing in the details.’

[Hm?]

The little details that should make this unreasonable situation possible.

For example… ordinarily in cults, rituals like these involve some sort of shared act.

‘Like drinking a certain beverage, carrying a specific branch, imitating an animal…’

Some small symbol that only they share.

A practice that is a little magical, unique, symbolic, easy enough to do, and helps foster religious belonging.

‘The same goes for clothing.’

I had expected them to share at least one unifying color or ornament.

But here in this underground bunker, everyone was just wearing ordinary clothes. No real sign of initiation.

Even the school uniform the sect official wore… well, as a ghost story it’s symbolic, but it wouldn’t feel out of place even if you just called it normal clothing.

‘In some eerie way, it would have been more striking if everyone was wearing school uniforms.’

But since only the official wears one, it feels neither here nor there.

‘I mean, how are they even forming unity?’

That this inhumane group is sustained on nothing but the ideas of ‘[Name]-nim’ and ‘happiness’ is… baffling.

Is it really just, brainwash with [Name]-nim and kill people, the end?

[Oh, what a witty analysis! My *friend,* you could even make a splendid cult group of your own. The prophet role you played on the train was deeply impressive!]

[Why not create a fine organization even now? Something intriguing, uncanny, cheerful… And this is a truly radical proposal, but I’ll allow my cotton-stuffed body to be taken as its symbol!]

T-Thank you, but I’ll pass for now.

Anyway.

To be very honest, hm… those gaps feel almost like blanks.

From the perspective of a wiki.

‘As though the worldbuilding parameters were never filled in.’

Because the focus is only on visceral stimuli and disgust, constructing events and ghost-story horror, it feels like the realistic details are missing. Yet it’s precisely those details that provide the foundation that supports the story.

‘…The Happy Ending Sect was one of the most recent sects added to the Church of the Luminous Unknown on the wiki.’

So maybe that’s why. I even wondered if it would have been different had I ended up in a sect added in the early days of registration.

‘Something like the Quota Worship Sect?’

But I erased that thought.

It’s probably better like this—being able to feel nothing but disgust, without great disturbance.

‘…Better than a visceral, crawling sense of dread, anyway.’

Let’s be grateful it isn’t that.

I simply sighed at the clumsy balance, trying to grow used to the pain in my stomach as I watched cruel and revolting sights.

[Ah, certainly a sharp analysis, but… Tell me, Mr. Roe Deer.]

[Haven’t you ever thought that some cult groups only reveal their true nature once you’re inside the in-group itself?]

So he’s saying you’d have to be initiated to experience the real thing…

‘I’d rather decline.’

At least over the past two days, I hadn’t been pressured to swallow blank scripture fragments and be reborn into the authority of Church of the Luminous Unknown.

There had been no veiled suggestions or pressure. If it were another cult, that kind of thing would have been all the more chilling.

Here, it seemed more like intentional calm.

‘Maybe because they see me not as a contaminated human but as a ghost story itself, and so they’re taking the long view.’

I asked Ho Yoowon too, but he only shook his head.

“Ah, no, nothing of the sort.”

…So that’s how it is.

“It’s hard to say there’s anything worth calling information to look into. Right? Still, it’s convenient to have an easy chance to talk with you separately, Roe Deer-nim.”

That was true.

Within this underground bunker, the Happy Ending Sect had assigned us to what could be called… living spaces set on an equal footing.

Hemispherical chambers, connected like an ant nest.

<br>

**[Authority Zone]**

<br>

Unlike the clustered, antlike dormitories where new initiates were packed close together, given only the bare minimum for survival, this place was slightly apart. The area was wider, emptier… independent. As though a personal territory had been granted.

Strange sealed rooms with no CCTV at all.

[Like a lion’s den. If these were the living quarters of those who hid underground at the world’s end, they might look just like this.]

For reference, aside from the spaces assigned to each of us, the nearby ‘Authority Zone’ seemed to be empty.

Except for those two in school uniforms.

“Authority Zone… In the Church of the Luminous Unknown, I think they call Darknesses ‘Authority’. Isn’t that right?”

Correct.

Now that I thought about it.

$s*Question :*s$

$s*Knowledge about the Church of the Luminous Unknown*s$

How much did Ho Yoowon know about them?

“I know as much as needed. I’ve read the experimentation logs mentioned in Cheerful Research Institute.”

Ah.

#f#Derived from earlier research, the name of a god worshiped by a certain cult currently in rapid growth was adopted and used as the subtitle for this research.#f#

…The very experimentation logs that were beside the incubation chamber I woke up in.

Apparently, Ho Yoowon had tracked down other traces mentioned a few times in the underground Cheerful Research Institute as well.

“A radical religious group that worships Darkness and seeks to become Darkness, expanding rapidly. It seemed like just the place to find information I’d missed before. And…”

Ho Yoowon met my gaze.

“In the experimentation log that created you, Roe Deer-nim… it said the name was taken from this religious group’s god, didn’t it?”

…!

“I’m a little curious about what connection you might have to the group. Ah, that’s not a question, of course.”

……

“Then I’ll ask a question this time, Roe Deer-nim.”

The topic that had been cut off back in the elevator.

A single question, almost obsessive.

“How do you know about Sekwang Special Metropolitan City?”

……Huu.

‘I can’t exactly say I read it on the wiki.’

There was no helping it.

$s*Answer to answer*s$

$s*: Substitute with another question*s$

“……”

Ho Yoowon’s laughter stopped.

For a brief moment, expressionless eyes looked straight at me. And then, as if flipping a switch, the smile returned.

“Can’t answer… I see.”

……

“I’ll remember that, Roe Deer-nim.”

There were no follow-up questions.

An uneasy end to an uneasy Q&A.

***

And, that evening.

Just one ordinary routine event returned, detailed enough to feel believable.

The Happy Ending Sect’s meal time.

“Let us share happiness.”

“The gaze of [Name]-nim.”

Here, every morning and evening meal required full attendance. Everyone sat and ate together. No exceptions.

Even those contaminated by ghost stories sat side by side with the new initiates.

Incidentally, across from me sat the school uniformed officials… ugh.

‘At this rate, they’d probably kill an initiate and say they made them happy.’

I swallowed the retort and kept quiet.

Soon the meal began.

I sat still, glancing at the exposed cement walls and ceilings of the underground bunker, then looked down at the table.

Canned rations.

“Can’t eat?”

I turned my head.

One of the uniformed officials was leaning on a hand, looking at me.

Then she tapped the boy next to her, the one who looked exactly like her.

“Hey, hey, what do dragons usually eat?”

The boy just shrugged. She smacked his back lightly, then smiled brightly at me.

“Ah. Should introduce ourselves! Introductions are important… Right, I’m Lee Seoyu, and this is Lee Seobin. We’re twins. I’m the older sister!”

She beamed.

“Some people call me ‘Isseoyu~’ in the Chungcheong dialect!” 

So cheerful.

Which only made it more awkward.

This normal, sunny demeanor from someone who had killed without hesitation, brainwashed through hallucinations and whispers, and tried to abduct others.

That unsettling casualness of giving real names inside a ghost story. And…

‘They really are twins.’

Now that I was here, I recognized them…… They’re the Happy Ending Sect twins who had appeared in several Church of the Luminous Unknown tales.

I’d even seen their faces on a few WeTube thumbnails.

Though this was my first time hearing their actual names.

“Do you maybe eat, like, something unusual? Human brains or something? Or livers?”

But looking at them like this, they really just seemed like high school students.

So after some hesitation, I asked at last.

$s*Reason for outfit choice*s$

“Ah, my school uniform?”

Her head fell off.

“Curious?”

And it rolled across the table.

Long hair tied with a red ribbon wriggled on the tablecloth.

“Are you reallyreallyreallyreallyreally curious?AreyoucuriouswhyImwearingthis?Reallyreally?Really?”

No.

I think I’m good…

$s*Polite remark for table manners.*s$

“I see! I see!”

‘Just drop the subject, please…’

Maybe because they’re insane, there’s never any warning.

I’d wear out mentally before I even found a way to return to a human shape.

Everything was just tiring and… overwhelming…

Then.

<br>

*Thud.*

<br>

“Aigoo…”

On my right.

The old woman sitting next to Ho Yoowon bumped his arm and spilled her water cup.

His clothes got wet.

“Oh no, are you alright?”

But instead of checking the water on his sleeve, Ho Yoowon immediately moved to help the old woman up. He cleared the table in front of her and wiped her clothes.

…He’d go that far?

“What a good person.”

“A good person.”

…I felt eyes on us.

From every table, whispers spread as they watched Ho Yoowon’s actions.

‘……’

That evening, Ho Yoowon seemed to chat softly with the old woman, and before long, he went so far as to invite her into his ‘quarters’.

“I’ll tell you an interesting story.”

No one stopped him.

[Oh. I can feel the audience’s anticipation.]

Yes.

Anticipation.

One of the Happy Ending Sect’s officials, who had introduced herself as Lee Seoyu, whispered as we walked away.

“If you’re going to make Grandma Kim Bokja happy, be sure to tell me in the morning. We have to hold the ceremony for her!”

……

My stomach lurched.

But I didn’t stop Ho Yoowon.

‘It must be for information.’

If anything went wrong, I would step in myself.

So I slowly followed after him as he supported the old woman toward his quarters…

And we entered.

<br>

*Bam—*

<br>

But the moment I shut the door and leaned against it.

Ho Yoowon released the old woman and stepped back.

And the old woman straightened up without difficulty, standing tall.

‘…!’

She spat out a round glass-like bead she’d been holding in her mouth. Immediately, her form shifted and hardened…

A cold, tired look.

Eyes as sharp as a hawk.

Pale face, short blue hair.

Someone I knew.

‘…!!’

Assistant Manager Eun Haje.

‘Wait a second.’

What’s happening?

My brain froze, unable to process the situation.

And then, like a bolt of lightning, realization struck.

‘No wonder he helped the old woman.’

Assistant Manager Eun Haje had infiltrated this Happy Ending Sect facility disguised as an old woman…!

And there was only one person who could’ve arranged that.

Ho Yoowon!

“So you sent someone in, and then you came in yourself too?”

“No. It’s coincidence. I didn’t expect to meet you like this either.”

“Sure, sure. Of course.”

Assistant Manager Eun Haje collapsed into a seat with a wet splat.

Then, after briefly showing an expression that looked like she wanted to sock Ho Yoowon, her eyes lit up as she noticed the bruise on his face.

“Director, you got hurt?”

“Ah. This one’s hand is a bit heavy…”

[How shameless.]

Exactly.

The finger Ho Yoowon used to point at me naturally drew Assistant Manager Eun Haje’s gaze in my direction.

She nearly went pale, but steadied her breath and asked calmly.

“…Isn’t this a Security Team member?”

“That’s right. And in fact, Falcon-nim also knows this person.”

“……”

Assistant Manager Eun Haje’s eyes sank.

As if recalling the names of all the Field Exploration Team staff she had worked with… and their fates.

But before she could finish that thought, Ho Yoowon spoke first.

“Been a while, hasn’t it? This is Roe Deer-nim.”

Assistant Manager Eun Haje froze.

Her gaze left Ho Yoowon entirely and fixed on me.

Her mouth opened.

“Roe Deer?”

……

$s*Yes, Assistant Manager.*s$

I shaped the letters with smoke.

‘It’s fine.’

In fact, I was in a better state than when I had met with the Security Team members.

I was in a state where I could think clearly again, and I was freer than when I’d been in the isolation cell.

To meet Assistant Manager Eun Haje at eye level, I cautiously sat down at the doorway as well.

I forced myself into the tone I’d used before.

$s*I made a mistake and it caused problems, which is why I ended up like this, but it’ll be fine.*s$

$s*I’m looking for a way to recover.*s$

<br>

“……”

Assistant Manager Eun Haje stared at the floor. Her face, hidden by hair, was momentarily out of sight. Then.

“Right. Roe.”

She met my eyes again. Her face had all agitation wiped clean. Only composure remained.

“Of course you can recover. Hey, having ridiculous side effects from Darkness is part of becoming a true Daydream employee.”

She tapped my arm that lay inside the black smoke.

“You’re clever, so there’ll be another way. Think about what you want to do once you recover.”

……

$s*Thank you.*s$

“Thanks for what? Hey. I’ll just stand next to you whenever you want a cigarette.”

I don’t think that’s possible, but…

Anyway, I was able to talk a bit more about the events that had happened and about Assistant Manager Eun Haje grumbling that she couldn’t smoke while disguised like that.

It was a short but fairly pleasant time.

Hearing that I had worked with Supervisor Park Minseong even brought a faint smile to her face. Of course, it vanished when we returned to the main topic.

“Ms. Falcon, I didn’t call you here just for idle chatter with Roe Deer-nim.”

“Sorry.”

Assistant Manager Eun Haje apologized immediately, though there was still an expression like, *‘What are you going to do about it, prick?’* left on her face.

Hm. Unexpectedly, we seemed to have grown fairly used to one another…

‘I was worried last time when this scumbag even managed to control Assistant Manager Eun Haje’s body.’

It was a relief to see Assistant Manager Eun Haje behaving in character.

‘Still, I should check on her later.’

For now, this first.

$s*Assistant Manager, were you sent here under Director Ho’s orders?*s$

“Yep. It’s been hell. Getting corpses and all…”

She grumbled that she’d only infiltrated cults twice as a reporter. 

“Worshipping darkness in an underground bunker and killing people? Happy ending? If they want a happy ending, they should get one themselves instead of messing with others.” 

Every word is spot-on…

$s*And since this place seems to gather initiates, won’t it be hard to get specific information?*s$

“Nah, initiations are exactly where you dig up the good stuff.” 

Huh?

“Cults usually show their true colors once they’ve pulled you in.” Assistant Manager Eunhaje grinned. “And to do that, they’d have prepared something, right? There’s gotta be something in this facility. So…”

“That’s all fine, Peregrine Falcon, but don’t forget why you’re here.” Director Ho cut in.

“Have you looked into Segwang Special City?”

“You’re talking about that in front of him… Guess it’s fine. Anyway, to find that info here, we need to figure out what these guys are up to first, as a baseline… ugh,” Assistant Manager Eunhaje spoke with exasperation.

“Just wait. You sent me here because you saw a future where I’d find out about Segwang Special City, didn’t you?”

…!

Future?

Something flashed in my mind.

Right before I boarded the Tamra-bound train, what Assistant Manager Eunhaje urgently told me at my dorm.

You’ll be marked as deceased on January 2nd.

I confirmed it… through the darkness that sees the future.

If that was learned during Director Ho’s project…

That explains Director Ho’s behavior.

He’d already confirmed it.

Assistant Manager Eunhaje will find out about Segwang Special City in the Church of the Luminous Unknown… that future.

Another realization hit.

Wait, then did he plan to infiltrate as a convert too?

As a director, it wouldn’t be strange if he noticed something the day of or before and took measures.

No wonder the Happy Ending Sect leaders infiltrated the company basement without causing more casualties.

Maybe he pulled some strings to minimize conflict and clear out security that day.

To enter here without suspicion.

And of course, I got caught up in his planned scheme, which doesn’t feel great, but…more than that.

…What darkness did he use to see the future?

This accuracy.

Darkness is a supernatural phenomenon, and the stronger it is, the more dangerous and incomprehensible.

What is Director Ho using?

$s*Question: The darkness that confirms the future*s$ 

“…Are you asking me? That’s odd. I’m pretty sure it’s my turn to ask.”

Damn it.

“You don’t have the right to ask until I’m done, Roe Deer.”

The gas-masked me and the smiling Director Ho stared at each other for a moment.

Assistant Manager Eunhaje watched this scene with a “what’s going on?” look, then shrugged and spoke.

“Anyway, with more people, it’ll be easier. If you and the director help search.”

$s*Search?*s$

“Yeah. I was planning to investigate this facility itself… Hmm.”

Assistant Manager Eunhaje shrugged.

“Even if this bunker itself is darkness, there’s gotta be an entrance somewhere, right? To the surface.”

That’s… probably true.

Most spatial ghost stories have an escape route to reality.

If there’s a way in, there’s a way out.

No matter how much this is a Church of the Luminous Unknown ghost story, it wouldn’t break that basic rule.

Even if this bunker isn’t in a fixed location, there’s probably a connected place each time.

“Initiates lose consciousness and wake up here, so they wouldn’t know, but deeper insiders probably do.”

Like those twins.

“And the ones to watch, the uniformed twins, seem like the deepest insiders here, but they’re never in the cafeteria at lunch.”

They’re doing something other than “managing initiates,” Assistant Manager Eunhaje added.

Anyway, finding hints about the entrance would be good.

It could lead us to the core.

“In cults, the most valuable stuff is usually kept farthest from the entrance, deep inside. Of course, so they can escape if needed.”

“Aha.”

Director Ho didn’t object.

After some thought, I answered.

$s*Then there might be a device nearby to check the outside*s$

“Oh, that could be.”

Actually, I’m certain.

Some wiki entries about this final ritual site mentioned descriptions of checking the outside from within.

“It probably wouldn’t be where the clueless initiates are. Showing the outside would ruin the indoctrination effect.”

Somewhere away from the initiates’ living area.

Somewhere elevated.

Somewhere convenient for insiders to check.

That’s…

Here.

<br>

#*[Power Zone]*#

<br>

With its ant-colony structure and high ceilings, it’s closer to the surface. In the end, we decided to explore this zone, avoiding the twins carefully. 

“Alright. With you two, it’ll be easier for this grandma to move around.” Assistant Manager Eunhaje swallowed the glass bead again.

And shortly after.

“Here it is.”

We found it.

At the very end of the Power Zone. An empty area.

There, near the ceiling, was a small, round cover.

When opened…

“Glass.”

A small, telescope-like fixture designed to peek outside. 

“Roe Deer, wanna check?”

Gladly.

Covered in light smoke, I floated up and put my eye to the round opening… The view through the one-foot opaque glass telescope.

…

…!!

“Roe Deer?”

I looked again.

But the scenery didn’t change. Old houses, mountains, utility poles, unpaved roads, hanok buildings.

That’s…

Jisan Village.

This Church of Luminous Unknown ritual site was rooted beneath the hometown village of Baek Saheon!
