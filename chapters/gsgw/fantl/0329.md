# Chapter 328 - The Rabbit Doll Cult, II

My eyelids fluttered as I stared at the scene before me.

“Reform the train!”

Dozens of people were crowding into Car No. 1, holding placards and shouting.

But here’s the thing.

“Rabbit dolls for everyone!”

Everything was pink.

Each placard bore bizarre slogans like ‘The Perfect Rabbit Doll,’ ‘The Fluffy One’s Grace,’ ‘Blessed Are the Cushy Front Paws,’ and ‘Lead Us to a Wider Place.’

People had their arms around one another’s shoulders, and here and there I saw people wearing rabbit-ear headbands made by cutting up cardboard boxes.

It was a shock, but given how the past few days had gone, I’d expected some kind of upheaval. I could handle this much.

But....

“Guarantee the rabbits~”

Assistant Manager.

What on earth are you doing....?

I stared in bewilderment at Assistant Manager Eun Haje, who’d quietly slipped into the front rank with a necktie wrapped around her head, thrusting her hand forward.

Before launching Operation Rabbit Doll Cult, I had already given Ms. Go Yeongeun, Agent Bronze, and Assistant Manager Eun Haje a rough explanation of what would happen.

‘Why would you go and spearhead that very disturbance....?’

“Ah~ it seemed like you were doing something good, so I thought I’d lend a hand, hahaha! Ahh, so this... person is the one who leads you all to a better place?”

After coaxing a steady stream of freely offered information out of the newly initiated ‘Rabbit Doll Cult’ members, Assistant Manager Eun Haje finally got to see their ‘rabbit doll goods’ up close.

“Oh....”

Her ‘Oh’ stretched into a long note of admiration as she examined the jingling metal keychain embossed with the cute head of a rabbit doll....

“Our goal is to bestow this amazing miracle upon everyone on the train, so that no one is discriminated against and everyone receives the Being of Stuffed Cotton’s grace.”

Don’t look at me.

But people had already turned sharply toward me, their eyes sparkling.

That’s right.

In fact, I was standing right in the middle of this crowd with placards....

People had gone out of their way to saddle me with the title ‘Distributor,’ calling me ‘the one who started handing out rabbit dolls.’

‘I accepted it because it seemed like the perfect position for playing a sycophant.’

I smiled and cast deliberate glances around, pretending to be eager to flatter whoever was shouting the loudest—until my eyes met Assistant Manager Eun Haje’s.

“.......”

“.......”

This is so humiliating.

“Distributor!”

Phew.

Please go away, Assistant Manager....

But the ‘Good Friend’ inside the massive velvet box I was holding seemed quite excited by this spectacle.

People naturally stepped aside. Some devotees even politely removed the rabbit-ear headbands they were wearing.

I felt like laughing and crying at the same time.
